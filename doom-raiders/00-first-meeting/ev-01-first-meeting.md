# Doom Raiders First Meeting

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs during **The Factions Come Calling**, when one or more invited candidates visit the Yawning Portal to meet Davil Starsong. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Follow their individual invitations and meet Yagra Stonefist at the bar.
> - Hear Davil's account of the Black Network split, including his belief that Urstul Floxin leads the rival branch.
> - Ask what the Doom Raiders offer and what they expect from a member.
> - Decide individually whether to join; only an invited candidate who has no other faction membership can accept.
> - Receive Fang rank, Renown 1 and its benefits if they join, while companions remain unaffiliated unless separately invited and accepted.
>
> #### Candidates and Companions
>
> During **The Factions Come Calling**, a flying snake delivers an individually addressed note from Davil to each unaffiliated candidate who meets the Doom Raiders' recruitment conditions: an Evil alignment or morally pragmatic outlook, and decisive action during **Finding Floon**. A character who has joined another faction receives no invitation or second membership offer. Each note invites its named recipient to speak with Davil at the Yawning Portal; invited candidates may attend together, and may bring companions, but Davil offers membership only to the candidates named in his invitations.
>
> #### Background
>
> Davil leads the Doom Raiders, a Waterdeep Zhentarim cell that has built legitimate businesses and working ties across the city. A rival Zhentarim branch has begun driving his people out. Davil believes Urstul Floxin leads it; he has no evidence that identifies anyone above Floxin, and he does not know Manshoon is involved. He wants capable new members who can help protect his cell and bring him confirmed information about the rival branch.
>
> Davil does not require an answer during this meeting. A candidate can accept tonight or return to the Yawning Portal before **Trollskull Alley** ends to give Davil their answer. Do not mark membership for a candidate who defers.

### Yagra at the Bar

Yagra Stonefist (Neutral, Half-Orc, she/her), Davil's bodyguard, waits at the Yawning Portal's bar with a tankard in one hand and her red jade Zhentarim pendant worn openly. She recognizes the invited candidates from the brawl at the Yawning Portal, and how she greets them depends on what happened during **The Yawning Portal** Event in **Finding Floon**.

If the party helped Yagra fight, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra sets down her tankard and looks over the characters who helped her in the brawl, making room beside the bar as they arrive.
>
> > "Good punch. Better timing. He's upstairs."

If the party stopped Yagra before the fight could continue, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra glances over without turning from the bar, keeping one hand around her tankard while the rest of the room carries on around her.
>
> > "You got in the way. Still — you showed up. He's upstairs."

If the party sided with the Xanathar Guild, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra holds the characters' gaze for a moment before she speaks, with her tankard still in one hand and the bar busy behind her.
>
> > "Davil sent for you. I'm delivering the message. That's all this is."

If the party stayed out of the fight and Yagra won, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra gives the characters a neutral nod from the bar, then shifts her tankard aside to make room for them near the counter.
>
> > "You let me work. Smart. He's upstairs."

If the party stayed out of the fight and Yagra lost, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra turns from the bar and studies the characters before she speaks, her posture square as the crowded room moves behind her.
>
> > "I know what you are. Come on."

If the party tried to broker peace and the DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion) check succeeded, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra's shoulders ease as she recognizes the characters, and she sets her tankard down before turning from the bar to speak.
>
> > "Nice words, that night. Davil's going to want to hear more. He's upstairs."

Yagra leads the invited candidates upstairs regardless of the greeting. She is professional throughout the walk, and her response to the earlier brawl remains as recorded by **Yagra Courteous**.

> [!social]**The Bouncer at the Bar**
>
> Yagra Stonefist (Neutral, Half-Orc, she/her) :: Davil's bodyguard, who brings his invited candidates upstairs.
>
> Yagra cracks her knuckles before speaking to someone new, then rests an elbow on the bar and waits through a pause before answering. The earlier greeting is her entire response to the brawl; she doesn't soften or revise it during this meeting.
>
> Conversation topics Yagra is willing to discuss include:
>
> - She directs the invited candidates to Davil's room and answers practical questions about where it is.
> - She says Davil wants to meet them and lets him explain what the Doom Raiders expect.
>
> Specific dialogue for Yagra on these topics follows.

> [!qna]**Where is Davil?**
>
> Yagra points toward the stairs with her tankard.
>
> > "Second floor, curtained room. Davil's waiting, so get your ass upstairs and quit blocking the fucking bar."

### Davil's Offer

Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him), the leader of the Doom Raiders, waits in his curtained room on the Yawning Portal's second floor. His lute leans against the wall beside a table and two chairs, and the charm bracelet on his wrist gives a small rattle as he pours for the invited candidates.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil sets down the decanter and turns his bracelet so one of its small trophies rests beneath his thumb.
>
> > "I'm Davil Starsong, and I lead the Doom Raiders, the Zhentarim cell that makes its living through trade, mercenary work and arrangements with the city's guilds. We have our own business here, and I'd like to hear what sort of work you take on before we discuss whether you want a place in it."
>
> He settles into his chair and pours another glass while the candidates find their seats.
>
> > "The Black Network has split in Waterdeep, and a rival branch has been using our name while attacking people who had no quarrel with us. I believe Urstul Floxin leads them, which is the best explanation my people have found, though I don't know who else may be involved."
>
> Davil leaves the decanter within reach and looks toward each candidate in turn.
>
> > "We can offer work, contacts and the sort of backing that makes a hard job easier; in return, we ask for discretion, capable work and anything confirmed that you learn about Floxin's people. You needn't answer tonight, so what would you like to know?"

> [!social]**The Master of Opportunities and Negotiations**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: The Doom Raiders' leader, who wants capable people to protect his cell and report what they learn about its rival.
>
> Davil is warmly attentive without revealing why he wants to know something. He turns a trophy on his charm bracelet when he weighs an answer and lets silence sit rather than filling it. He does not threaten, haggle or press candidates to accept. He believes Urstul Floxin leads the rival Zhent branch, and he does not know that Manshoon is involved.
>
> Conversation topics Davil is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The Doom Raiders are a Zhentarim cell led by retired adventurers, with legitimate businesses and contacts in Waterdeep.
> - The rival branch has been attacking the Doom Raiders and using the Zhentarim name; Davil's best current explanation is that Urstul Floxin leads it.
> - The Doom Raiders expect members to do capable, discreet work and share confirmed intelligence about Floxin's people.
> - A member begins at Renown 1 and Fang rank. Istrid Horn can offer that individual a loan of up to 200 GP at favorable rates, and the Network's fences pay 60 percent of an item's value for stolen goods.
> - Davil does not require an answer tonight, and an invited candidate may accept now or return before **Trollskull Alley** ends.
>
> If an invited candidate offers a theory about who directs the rival branch, Davil does not confirm an unsupported name. Before the party finds and reports direct evidence, Floxin remains the only leader he identifies. Specific dialogue for Davil on these topics follows.

> [!qna]**The Doom Raiders?**
>
> Davil pours a little more wine before answering.
>
> > "We're the Waterdeep branch that stayed and built something here, where Istrid handles lending, Tashlyn works from her Guard post, and Ziraj can settle certain problems; those favors come with expectations, though the terms are clear."

> [!qna]**The rival Zhentarim?**
>
> Davil turns a small silver trophy on his bracelet.
>
> > "I believe Urstul Floxin leads them, since he has the people and has been the one striking at ours. That's the best account I have so far, and I'm not going to call it proof."

> [!qna]**What do members receive?**
>
> Davil sets his glass beside the candidate's.
>
> > "Istrid will speak with you about a loan of up to two hundred gold, and our fences pay better than the usual buyers. Those are your terms as a Fang; ask Istrid for the figures before you borrow a coin."

> [!qna]**What do you ask of us?**
>
> Davil leans back and turns the bracelet once around his wrist.
>
> > "Good work, kept discreet, and anything confirmed that you hear about Floxin's people. One useful fact at a time is better than a grand report full of guesses."

> [!qna]**Do we have to decide now?**
>
> Davil lifts his glass, then sets it down without drinking.
>
> > "No, decide when you're ready, and come back before Trollskull Alley ends if you'd rather think it over; I'll be here most evenings."

### Choosing Membership

Each invited candidate answers for themselves. A candidate who has joined another faction since receiving the note cannot accept Doom Raider membership; companions who were not invited may attend the meeting but receive no membership, Renown, rank or faction benefits from helping an invited character.

If an invited candidate accepts, read or paraphrase the following to that character:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil rises and offers his hand, closing his fingers around yours when you take it.
>
> > "Then welcome, and I'll let Istrid know you're a Fang so she can explain the loan if you want one. We'll speak again when there's work for you."

Each accepting character becomes a Doom Raiders member at Renown 1 and Fang rank, and gains the Fang benefits recorded in the **Doom Raiders** Factions Guide. These benefits belong only to the accepting character. The **Dockside Killer** mission is available to Doom Raider members at character level 2; a companion may help with the mission without becoming a member or earning its Renown.

If a candidate declines, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil nods and reaches for the decanter, leaving the candidate's glass where it is.
>
> > "Of course, and thank you for coming to speak with me; you're welcome to return if your answer changes before Trollskull Alley ends."

If no invited candidate accepts tonight, Davil does not close the offer aloud. Any candidate who declined or deferred may return before **Trollskull Alley** ends and answer him then. Use the same invitation eligibility and membership rules when resolving a later answer.

### Concluding the Event

If at least one candidate accepts, the Doom Raiders have new members and Davil makes the **Dockside Killer** mission available to each member who has reached character level 2. Otherwise, the invitation remains open to candidates who declined or deferred, and no membership is recorded.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Doom Raiders Joined** — mark when at least one invited candidate accepts membership; record the name of each character who joins. Read by Doom Raiders faction missions and **Davil's Arrest** to confirm individual membership. Companions who help a member do not count as members.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> If a Doom Raider member has reached character level 2, their briefing for **The Dockside Killer** can proceed. Return to **The Factions Come Calling** for any other invitations the characters received during the renovation period. Any invited candidate who declined or deferred may return to Davil before **Trollskull Alley** ends; add their name to **Doom Raiders Joined** if they accept.
>
> This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Invited characters meet Yagra Stonefist and Davil Starsong at the Yawning Portal, where the Doom Raiders' leader describes the work his cell offers and asks what the candidates want from membership.

## Summary

### After Joining

We met Yagra Stonefist and spoke with Davil Starsong about the Doom Raiders, their rival Zhentarim branch and the work members are expected to do. Those who accepted became Fang members; everyone else kept their existing faction status or remained unaffiliated.

### Without Joining

We met Yagra Stonefist and spoke with Davil Starsong about the Doom Raiders and their rival Zhentarim branch. No invited candidate accepted membership during the meeting, though any candidate who declined or deferred may return before **Trollskull Alley** ends.
