# C27 — False Vault

Basement level. A triple-locked iron door at the end of the cellar corridor.

**Locks:** Three separate mechanisms, each requiring DC 20 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools). The three locks must be opened individually — the door does not open until all three are disengaged. Breaking the door: AC 19, 90 HP, immunity to poison and psychic damage.

On the other side: a stone room, empty. Stone shelves on three walls, stone floor, stone ceiling. No treasure. No furnishing. Nothing.

### Heist — Cassalanter Villa Only

> **[GM]**
>
> This room exists solely as a misdirection and a trap. Do not soften its function.

**The Glyph of Warding.** On the ceiling of the false vault, invisible (DC 16 Intelligence (Investigation) to detect from the doorway before entering; *detect magic* reveals it automatically). The glyph triggers when any creature enters the room without speaking the passphrase.

**Passphrase:** "Gold is my shield." Speaking it aloud (in any language, as the glyph is keyed to intent and meaning, not phonetics) deactivates the glyph for one minute. The passphrase is obtainable from Victoro's journal (C4 reading room): the phrase appears in an entry discussing the vault's construction. Elzerina knows that "Father says something in this funny voice before he goes in the door" — she has heard it but does not know what it means.

**Glyph Effect:** 8d8 cold damage, DC 16 Dexterity saving throw for half. All creatures in the room when the glyph triggers — including the creature who opened the door, if they crossed the threshold — are affected simultaneously.

**The Deception.** Guild blueprints from Scene 2 (Doom Raider contact) show this room's dimensions, which are measurably smaller than the basement footprint implies — the west wall is four feet farther in on the plans than the actual basement perimeter. This is the mechanical clue that C27 is a decoy. A party with the blueprints can identify this discrepancy with a DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check comparing the plans to their observations. The real continuation is through C28 (wine cellar).

A party that triggers the glyph, survives, and then searches the room discovers the dimensional discrepancy on their own: DC 16 Intelligence (Investigation) to notice the west wall is unusually thick — a dead tell. The trap punishes rushing without eliminating the clue.
