# A9 — Stream and Pool

> **[GM]**
>
> This room is **Heist-only**. See A1 — Hall of the Damned for temple access notes.

The temple's lowest point. A natural cavern, irregular and unworked, that predates the Cassalanters' construction by centuries. An underground stream enters through a low arch on the north wall, crosses the cavern floor through a shallow stone channel, and exits through a passage on the west wall — partially submerged, the ceiling dropping to two feet above the water at its lowest point.

The pool is four feet deep at the center. The stream is cold.

### Heist — Cassalanter Villa Temple Layer

**Two Functions.** A9 serves as both an alternate entry point (stream approach from the Mud Flats) and the primary escape route for parties that cannot exit through the villa.

**The Stream Passage.** The passage from A9 to the Mud Flats northwest of Waterdeep is approximately 400 feet. Two sections require submersion — the ceiling drops to water level for twenty-foot stretches. Two air pockets exist between the submersion sections. Swim speed in the passage: twenty feet per round (ten feet if not proficient in Athletics).

**DC 12 Constitution saving throw** at the midpoint of the passage to resist exhaustion from the cold water and the physical exertion. On a failure: one level of exhaustion. Creatures with a natural swim speed or water breathing do not make this check.

**Navigation.** The passage is not confusing — it flows one direction. A party following the current exits onto the Mud Flats. A party swimming against the current enters the temple. No check required to navigate; the passage is a single tunnel.

**Entry (Mud Flats to A9).** Approaching from outside: the passage entrance on the Mud Flats is a low arch barely visible at the waterline, accessible only during low tide or with water breathing. Entering A9 from this direction: the party emerges from the pool into the cavern, dripping, making noise. DC 15 Group Dexterity (Stealth) check to emerge without alerting the temple's occupants (sound carries through the stone, particularly toward A7). On failure: inhabitants of A7 hear the entry within one round.

**Exit (A9 to Mud Flats).** No check required to enter the passage from A9. Exiting onto the Mud Flats: cold, wet, approximately 400 feet northwest of Waterdeep's harbor perimeter. An hour's walk from the nearest Watch post. If the Esvele Rosznar rooftop escape route is not available, this is the safe extraction path.

**Inhabitants.** The cavern is unguarded under normal conditions and at the Unaware, Suspicious, and Alert escalation tiers. At Lockdown tier, a bearded devil patrols A9 — assigned here specifically to prevent the stream entrance from serving as an escape route. At Lockdown, the party must deal with the devil before swimming out.

**What the Cavern Tells.** The natural stone here is geologically distinct from the constructed temple sections above. The Cassalanters built their temple above an existing cavern and routed the stream deliberately — the construction sealed the stream from the villa levels above and repurposed the cavern as a controlled exit. Someone, at some point, knew this space could serve as an escape route. It is not an oversight. The question the party may ask, if they are paying attention, is whether the Cassalanters planned their own exit — or whether someone else who used this space before them did.
