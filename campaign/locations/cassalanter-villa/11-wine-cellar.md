# C28 — Wine Cellar

Stone walls lined with bottle racks from floor to ceiling. The Cassalanters maintain a genuine collection — 600 bottles, organized by region and vintage. Fifteen to twenty bottles are worth 50 gp or more individually; three are worth over 200 gp. The cellar is cool and smells of oak and limestone.

The west wall is different from the others. Carved into the stone at eye level: a Siamorphe sun symbol, eight spokes radiating from a central circle, rendered in relief. It does not look decorative — it looks structural.

A single guard patrols the cellar on the night shift (midnight to dawn). During social events, the cellar is accessible only through the kitchen (C10) key ring.

### Normal — Day / Event Operations

The cellar is accessed from the kitchen corridor via the key ring kept in the pantry (C10). During the day, Jandar or a senior staff member retrieves bottles for meals. During events, the kitchen staff make multiple cellar runs over the course of the evening.

The Siamorphe sun symbol is prominent. A DC 12 Intelligence (Religion) check identifies Siamorphe as the goddess of nobility. Elzerina knows "a funny door behind a sun" in the cellar — she has seen it from the corridor, though the door was closed and she did not enter. She describes the symbol accurately if asked.

**Finding the secret door (without prior knowledge):** DC 18 Intelligence (Investigation). The carving's central circle is a pressure point — pressing it rotates a stone counterweight, withdrawing a latch. The door swings inward on a balanced pivot, soundlessly. Jandar (if turned) will describe its location directly, no check required.

**The Night Guard.** One guard on the cellar night shift, midnight to dawn. He has heard murmuring through the west wall on nights when the cult gathers — he was told these are "private religious observances" and instructed not to interfere. He has complied for two years. If the party approaches him and tells him what is actually happening, he provides patrol routes, blind spots, and the garden gate schedule. He is ex-Watch. He does not forgive the Cassalanters easily for what they have had him ignore.

### Heist — Arc G Operation

The secret door is the threshold between the social layer and the temple. Everything above the west wall: painted surfaces, managed appearances, the illusion of a household. Everything below: stone, incense, and what the Cassalanters have built beneath their home.

**Accessing during a social event:** The cellar is not part of the event's social space. A party that reaches the cellar during a Charity Ball is already in a restricted area — Stage 2 minimum if seen. The secret door's operation makes noise: the counterweight mechanism produces a low grinding sound audible at DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) from the corridor above.

**During cult gatherings (Caravance midnight, recurring tenday schedule):** The door is in use. Robed cultists (up to thirty) pass through the west wall to A1 over a thirty-minute period beginning at midnight. The guard is on patrol but instructed to be absent from the west wall during passage. His route has a deliberate gap.
