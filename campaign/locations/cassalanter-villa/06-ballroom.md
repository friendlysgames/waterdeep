# C13 — Ballroom

The villa's centerpiece. A high-ceilinged room with floor-to-ceiling windows on the garden side and a polished floor that draws the eye the moment you enter. The floor is a Siamorphe mosaic — the goddess's sun symbol rendered in gold and white tile, five feet across, centered precisely in the room. Candelabras ring the perimeter. A dancing mannequin stands in an alcove near the garden doors, jointed arms at rest.

### Normal — Day / Non-Event Operations

The ballroom is quiet and maintained for events. The dancing mannequin is decorative — it does not move unless a social event is in progress. The floor mosaic bears the sun symbol of Siamorphe; a DC 12 Intelligence (Religion) check identifies the deity. No significant materials, no locked areas.

The windows overlook the butterfly garden (C25). From the ballroom window, the six imps in raven form are visible in the garden trees — DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) to notice their unnaturally focused gaze.

### Heist — Arc G Social Event Configuration

During the Charity Ball, Goldenight gala, or any social event, the ballroom is the primary infiltration space. The crowd — 40–60 guests — provides cover. Social exposure checks occur here at advantage: the noise, movement, and general social chaos make a degree of unusual behavior unremarkable.

**The Dancing Mannequin.** During social events, the mannequin dances with guests who lack partners — it moves with eerie precision, its jointed hands gentle, its blank face inclined toward whichever direction the music comes from. It has the statistics of a nimblewright. It is not hostile and does not attack unless a guest is harmed in the ballroom — at which point it turns toward the violence and gestures to the nearest guard. It cannot speak or carry objects.

**Ammalia's Pattern.** During social events, Ammalia rotates through C13 (ballroom), C15 (banquet hall), and C25 (butterfly garden) on a twenty-minute cycle. If the party is tracking her position for access to C22, her C13 presence is the departure point — she moves upstairs to C22 only at the event's end.

**The Floor Mosaic.** The Siamorphe sun symbol is still here, unchanged. The Cassalanters have not defaced it — unlike the temple below, where every Siamorphe image was reshaped. The ballroom floor is Waterdeep's finest craftsmanship. The contrast between this floor and the carved defacement in A3 and A7 is available to any party member who has seen both.
