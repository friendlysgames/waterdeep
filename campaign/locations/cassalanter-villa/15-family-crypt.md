# A4 / A4a — Family Crypt / Caladorn's Crypt

> **[GM]**
>
> This room is **Heist-only**. See A1 — Hall of the Damned for temple access notes.

A4 is a vaulted chamber adjacent to the anterior vestibule, reached through a side passage off A3. Twelve stone sarcophagi are arranged in two rows, each bearing the Cassalanter family crest in carved relief — a sun over a scale, in the Siamorphe heraldic tradition. The chamber is cold and dark.

A4a is a smaller antechamber at the far end of A4, separated by an open arch. One sarcophagus here, distinguished from the others by its size and its effigy: an armored figure, silver-haired, with the Cassalanter features and the bearing of someone who carried a weapon all his life. A plaque reads: CALADORN CASSALANTER — MASKED LORD OF WATERDEEP — SERVANT OF SIAMORPHE IN HIS TIME.

### Heist — Cassalanter Villa Temple Layer

**Three Specters (A4).** Rise from the floor when a living creature enters the chamber. Attack without hesitation. Combat statistics: standard specters.

**Pacifying the specters:** Present a Siamorphe holy symbol (available from Savra Belabranta in Scene 1). The specters halt, withdraw back into the floor, and become inert for the remainder of the encounter. They do not emerge again unless the symbol is removed from the chamber.

Without the symbol: the three specters must be fought to proceed to A4a. They cannot follow into A4a — the arch functions as a threshold they will not cross.

**Caladorn's Ghost (A4a).** The ghost manifests when approached. An armored figure, solid-seeming until the eye finds the places where it fails to meet the edges of things — the wall behind him is not quite hidden. The voice is steady. The face is recognizable from the portrait in C1.

**Hostility trigger:** If the approaching creature has participated in Asmodean worship, carries an infernal mark, or is a cult member, Caladorn attacks immediately — Multiattack, Withering Touch, CR 4 ghost statistics. He does not speak. He cannot be reasoned with while his hostile state is active.

**Lucid state trigger:** If the approaching creature presents a Siamorphe holy symbol, Caladorn holds. He looks at the symbol for a long moment. His voice, when it comes, is measured: "My son could not send an outside. He sent people he doesn't know. That tells me something." He is wrong about the connection to his son — the party found the symbol through other means — but the observation reveals that Caladorn is not confused about what has happened in his family.

**What Caladorn provides (lucid state):**
- **+1 Plate Armor** — his own, stored in his sarcophagus in A4a. He opens the lid without touching it. The armor is ancient but perfectly maintained; it has not rusted.
- **Mace of Disruption** — consecrated to Siamorphe, the weapon he carried as a Masked Lord. He extends it toward the party without releasing it. "This was consecrated in Siamorphe's name. It will not let me keep it — she is still in it, and I am not alive enough to wield her anymore."

**The vow.** He asks for one thing: "Use the mace to destroy the effigy of evil in the ceremonial hall. End the corruption of my house. Restore my family's honor." He does not say "please." The asking is not a request — it is a lord making a charge.

Caladorn does not know the contract's specific terms. He knows his family has turned to Asmodeus; he knows the statue in A7 was his wife's image of Siamorphe before they defaced it. He does not know about the children's souls or Founders' Day. If told — his expression does not change. His voice does: "Elzerina and Terenzio." He says the names once. Then: "The mace. End it."
