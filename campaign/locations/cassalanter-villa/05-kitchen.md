# C10 — Kitchen

A well-appointed kitchen with a large central hearth, hanging copper pots, and a preparation table scarred by decades of use. Six commoner assistants work here during mealtimes. The room smells of roasting meat, spices, and something sharper — vinegar used for preserving.

A rack of large carving knives hangs above the preparation table. They look ordinary.

### Normal — Day Operations

Jandar Chergoba commands this kitchen with quiet authority. He speaks to his staff in clipped instructions, rarely looks up from whatever he is preparing, and does not welcome guests in this part of the house. If the party enters during kitchen hours, he pauses, sets down a knife, and says simply: "This is a working kitchen. Guests aren't received here."

The **cellar key ring** hangs on a hook in the pantry alcove — visible from the kitchen if the pantry door is open. Fiorella (cook's assistant) knows where it is and will mention it under the right circumstances. The key ring holds keys to C27 (false vault), C28 (wine cellar), and the cellar corridor.

**Turning Jandar:** Victoro holds a 3,000 gp financial obligation over him, converted to cult service three years ago. Jandar hates what he does. DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) with an offer of protection turns him; DC 10 if the party can resolve the debt or credibly promise to. He provides: kitchen schedules, which deliveries access the cellar, and the location of the wine cellar's false wall. He does not know the Siamorphe sun passphrase for C27 — only that the cellar has "a funny door that the family's guests use on certain nights."

### Heist — Cassalanter Villa Social Event / Founders' Day Configuration

During social events: Jandar and full staff in the kitchen, serving food to guests. The cellar key ring is on its hook. The carving knives are ordinary until commanded otherwise.

**The Carving Knives.** If Jandar speaks the command word "For'zaal," ten flying swords animate from the knife rack and attack on his direction. Deactivation word: "Basta" — obtainable from Fiorella (she overheard it once and doesn't know what it means) or by succeeding on a DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation) check directed at Jandar mid-combat.

**Founders' Day:** Jandar is outside on the lawn pavilion, preparing the charitable feast. The kitchen is empty except for Fiorella, who has been told to stay inside. The cellar key ring is on its hook, the carving knives are on the rack, and the kitchen door to the garden is propped open for Jandar's supply runs.
