# A7 — Ceremonial Hall

> **[GM]**
>
> This room is **Heist-only**. See A1 — Hall of the Damned for temple access notes.

The temple's heart. A thirty-foot-tall statue of Asmodeus dominates the far wall — carved from what was once a Siamorphe figure, the proportions subtly wrong in ways that suggest alteration rather than original design. The original goddess's posture was open-armed and welcoming; the alterations produced a figure with arms extended in command. Seams in the stone are visible at the shoulders and neck where the carving was reworked. A sacrificial fire bowl sits at the base of the statue, a perpetual flame burning inside it without fuel.

The hall is sixty feet long and forty feet wide. Stone pews face the statue in rows. A dais with a stone lectern stands before the statue, ten feet from the fire bowl.

### Heist — Cassalanter Villa Temple Layer

**Inhabitants — Non-Gathering.** The hall is empty. The fire bowl burns. No other light source.

**Inhabitants — Cult Gathering.** Thirty cultists in crimson robes and golden masks fill the pews. Victoro leads the rite from the dais. Ammalia stands to his right, pouring wine from a ritual ewer. Six invisible imps perch on the statue's shoulders and extended arms — DC 17 Wisdom (Perception) to notice displacement effects; *see invisibility* or *truesight* reveals them. The bearded devil from A3 moves to the hall entrance.

**The Sacrificial Fire Bowl.** Contact damage: 4d10 fire damage to any creature that touches the bowl or falls into it. The flame cannot be extinguished by mundane means. *Dispel magic* suppresses it for 1 minute. The bowl's destruction (AC 14, 25 HP, immune to fire and poison) extinguishes the flame permanently.

**Eye #2 — Location and Access.**

Eye #2 is secured behind the base of the Asmodeus statue. A concealed panel on the base's rear face — the statue's back faces the wall, leaving a two-foot gap. Accessing the gap: DC 14 Athletics to squeeze through; Medium creatures fit without a check if not wearing heavy armor.

The concealed panel: DC 18 Intelligence (Investigation) to locate. The panel has a warding mechanism — bypassing it without triggering the fire bowl: DC 18 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) or DC 18 Intelligence (Arcana). Failure triggers the fire bowl to flare — a burst of flame erupting from the bowl, dealing 4d10 fire damage to all creatures within 10 feet (DC 16 Dexterity saving throw for half). This also alerts all inhabitants of the hall.

A successful bypass opens the panel; Eye #2 — a small orb-shaped gemstone — rests in a stone socket inside.

**Destroying the Statue (Mace of Disruption Path).**

Striking the statue with the mace of disruption: the mace resonates immediately on contact — the consecration to Siamorphe still lives in the stone beneath the Asmodean alterations. The first hit cracks the surface. The crack spreads through the statue on its own. The Asmodeus features shatter and fall away over the following six seconds, revealing fragments of Siamorphe's original face beneath. The alterations were surface-deep.

**Effects of destruction:**
- The sacrificial fire bowl shatters (no saving throw) — 2d10 psychic damage to every cultist who witnesses the destruction as the infernal binding tears
- The infernal contract — bound to the statue since the night it was signed — severs. The binding made the statue the contract's physical anchor. Without the anchor, the contract dissolves
- Eye #2 is released from the base as the warding dies and the concealed panel cracks open. No check required to extract it
- The children's souls are freed. Osvaldo's chain devil form, if still alive, dissolves — he dies, but his soul is freed
- The Cassalanters are liberated. Asmodean retribution follows within days (see Scene 6 — Aftermath)

**Stone of Golorr Upgrade.** Inserting Eye #2 into the Stone follows from the area. Refer to the **Cassalanter Villa** Event in Cassalanter Villa for the full Stone upgrade scene — which of three scenarios applies depends on how many Eyes have been restored before this one.
