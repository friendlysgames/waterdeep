# C24 — Osvaldo's Prison

The attic. Accessible via the stair from C23 (Ammalia's study trapdoor), or through a locked exterior door on the attic level from the third-floor landing.

**Exterior attic door lock:** DC 19 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools).

The staircase from C23 is soundproofed. The soundproofing ends at the attic door. Once the party opens the attic door, the following is audible: a rhythmic scraping of metal on stone, low weeping, and occasionally a voice — a child's voice, speaking to itself in fragments.

The attic door is not locked from the corridor side.

### Heist — Arc G Only

> **[GM]**
>
> This room has no Normal section. It is inaccessible during Arc C — the attic is sealed and forbidden to all household staff. The exterior door's lock (DC 19) is beyond any non-plot access path in Arc C. Access occurs only during the Arc G operation.

Inside: a burned pentagram on the floor. Abjuration wards glow faintly around the pentagram's perimeter — DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) to identify as containment wards. An iron ring is set in the center of the pentagram floor. Chains extend from the ring.

At the end of the chains: Osvaldo.

He has the statistics of a chain devil. He is also, somewhere underneath, a nine-year-old boy. He alternates between states — the chain devil's combat readiness, then a sudden stillness, then a child's voice: "Mama?" Then the chains lash again.

**Two imps in spider form** cling to the rafters above the ring. They are passive observers during normal attic operations, reporting to Ammalia through a one-way telepathic bond. They attack if Osvaldo is directly threatened or if the party attempts to dispel the containment wards.

**Interaction options:**

*Observe without entering the pentagram:* The party can watch. Osvaldo is aware of them — his brass-tinted eyes track movement. He does not attack at range. After twenty seconds, the child's voice says, distinctly: "Please don't let them know I talked to you." Then the chain devil's instincts resurface and he attacks his own chains.

*Dispel the wards (dispel magic):* The wards collapse. Osvaldo attacks — Multiattack, chains lashing, chain devil statistics. Then, mid-swing, he stops. He weeps. He looks at his own hands. The voice says: "I'm sorry." He dies. His form dissolves into black ichor that smells of brimstone and ink. Two imps immediately report the death to Ammalia (the telepathic bond severs with a psychic snap — she receives it wherever she is in the villa). Ammalia moves toward C24 in the following round.

If the infernal contract is later destroyed (see A7 — mace of disruption), Osvaldo's death is retroactively a mercy: his soul, no longer contracted, is freed. If the contract is not destroyed, his soul belongs to Asmodeus.

**This room awards no tactical advantage.** The party gains only understanding: the contract's cost, made visible in a child's ruined body. The encounter exists for a single dramatic purpose — to show what the Cassalanters bought, and what they have been living with.

Lissette the upstairs maid mentions hearing Ammalia crying after she visits "the top floor." The twins' caretaker Nana Rosse knows "something terrible" lives in the attic. Neither has been inside.
