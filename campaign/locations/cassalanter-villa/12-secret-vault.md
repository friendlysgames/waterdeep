# C29 — Secret Vault

A small stone chamber beyond the Siamorphe door. Stone shelves on two walls, a flagstone floor, a low ceiling. A stone staircase descends from the far end of the chamber — straight down, thirty feet, into the dark.

The chamber is cold. Not the natural cold of stone — something deeper.

### Normal — Cassalanter Villa Day / Non-Event Operations

The shelves hold twenty silver trade bars (25 gp each, 500 gp total). These are genuine — liquid assets held outside the banking system, accessible without documentation. The chamber otherwise contains nothing: no cult materials, no documents, no indication of what lies below the staircase. The staircase descends to A1 (Hall of the Damned).

During normal operations, the vault is empty of people. No guard is posted here. The Cassalanters consider the Siamorphe door's obscurity and the false vault's trap (C27) sufficient protection.

### Founders' Day — Cassalanter Villa Climax

If the Cassalanters have obtained the Vault of Dragons gold through cooperation with the party (Resolution Path 1), or through their own acquisition efforts, the vault holds 500 sacks of gold coins — the full 500,000 gp — stacked floor to ceiling on the remaining floor space. The trade bars are displaced to a side shelf. The gold represents the financial half of the infernal buy-out: one contribution toward 999,999 gp.

The ritual to complete the financial payment occurs in A7 (ceremonial hall) on Founders' Day, conducted by Victoro with Ammalia present. The gold does not transfer physically — Victoro's contract specifies an accounting acknowledgment, not a coin transfer. The cult performs the ritual, Asmodeus's pit fiend proxy acknowledges receipt through the commune, and the financial clause closes.

**The Staircase.** Thirty feet of stone steps, unlit. No railing. The descent takes two rounds at normal pace, one round at a run (DC 10 Athletics to avoid stumbling, 1d4 bludgeoning damage on a fail). At the bottom: the temperature drops a further five degrees. The smell of incense and stone replaces the wine cellar's oak. This is the threshold. The genre changes at the bottom of these stairs.
