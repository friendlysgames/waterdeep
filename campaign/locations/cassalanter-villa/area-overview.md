# Cassalanter Villa — Area Overview

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Area Map Context
>
> Cassalanter Villa sits in the Sea Ward, set back from the street behind an iron gate and a topiary garden maintained for two generations. The building is four stories above ground, with a basement (wine cellar and false vault) and a sub-basement (secret vault and stairway to the temple level below).
>
> **Floors:** Ground (C1, C2, C4, C9, C10, C13); Upper ground / second (C15, C22 approaches); Third (C6, C22); Attic (C24); Garden-level (C25); Basement (C27, C28); Sub-basement stair (C29 → A1).
>
> **Event use:**
> - **ev-06 (The Cassalanter Dinner):** Arc C event, optional. The party arrives through the gate and is received in C1 (entrance hall). The dinner takes place in C9 (dining room — see below). Refer to the **The Cassalanter Dinner** Event for the full dinner sequence.
> - **Arc G (Cassalanter Villa heist):** Primary heist event. All 17 keyed areas are operational. The villa layer (C1–C29) is social infiltration; the temple layer (A1–A9) is a dungeon.

## Gameplay Details

The areas of Cassalanter Villa have the following features unless noted otherwise.

### Levels and Elevation

- **Ground floor** (street level, through the garden entrance): C1 (entrance hall), C2 (garden mudroom), C4 (reading room), C9 (dining room), C10 (kitchen), C13 (ballroom)
- **Second floor**: C15 (banquet hall), C5 (landing — 2 guard posts during events), C6 access staircase
- **Third floor**: C6 (Victoro's office), C22 (Ammalia's study), C23 (closet/attic access)
- **Attic**: C24 (Osvaldo's prison)
- **Garden level**: C25 (butterfly garden, walled exterior)
- **Basement**: C27 (false vault), C28 (wine cellar)
- **Sub-basement**: C29 (secret vault) and staircase to temple level A1

### Illumination

Day: ground floor naturally lit through tall windows; upper floors reliant on corridor lanterns. Night: all floors dim except C1 (entrance lanterns), C13 (event lighting during parties), and C25 (garden torches). Temple level (A1–A9): dark unless cult activities are in progress, in which case torchlight throughout.

### Inhabitants — Normal Operations

**Willifort Crowelle** (doppelganger, tiefling disguise): Circulates C1, C5 lower, and service corridors. *Detect Thoughts* active at all times within 30 feet. Does not engage combat unless cornered.

**Victoro Cassalanter** (9th-level spellcaster): Occupies C4 or C6 during daylight hours. Combat behavior: *Spiritual Weapon*, *Hold Person* (divide and control), *Shield of Faith* on Ammalia, *Banishment* on Ammalia if she is reduced to 30 HP.

**Ammalia Cassalanter** (9th-level spellcaster): Occupies C22 or second-floor family rooms. Combat behavior: *Charm Person* (5th-level, up to 5 targets), *Dominate Person* on most dangerous combatant, *Counterspell* defensively. Arrives to aid Victoro within 2 rounds of hearing combat.

**Jandar Chergoba** (chef): C10 during kitchen hours. Can animate ten flying swords (command word "For'zaal"; deactivation word "Basta").

**Helmed Horror** (C6): Activates on unauthorized desk contact. AC 20, HP 60. Does not leave the building.

**Area guards:** 4 at villa perimeter (2 front gate, 2 garden gate), 2 at C5 second-floor landing, 1 at C28 (wine cellar, night shift only).

**Two imps in spider form** (C24, Osvaldo's prison): Passive observers. Attack if Osvaldo is threatened or freed.

### Inhabitants — Social Events

40–60 civilian guests in C1, lower C5, C9, C13, C15, and C25. Ten servant commoners in service areas. Guards at C5 doubled to 4. Willifort circulates with a tray. C6 helmed horror active regardless.

### The Social Exposure System (Arc G)

Four-stage escalation governing social-layer movement. Begins at Stage 1 (Accepted Guest) when the party arrives legitimately. Triggers advance stages; de-escalation is possible at Stage 2 only.

| Stage | Name | Status | De-escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accepted Guest | Free movement in public areas; Willifort monitoring passively | N/A (starting state) |
| 2 | Noticed | Willifort actively probing; guards repositioned near restricted areas | Possible (15 minutes normal behavior) |
| 3 | Suspected | Guards escort the party to Willifort; Victoro notified | Not possible |
| 4 | Exposed | Active confrontation; guards and Willifort converge | Not possible |

### The Dining Room (C9)

The dining room is not keyed as a separate location journal page — it is used in Arc C (ev-06) and in Arc G social events but has no unique mechanical content. During the Arc C dinner, C9 is set for six: Victoro, Ammalia, the party (up to four), and one household staff member serving. During social events: Victoro entertains guests here. The room connects to C10 (kitchen) via a service door and to C1 (entrance hall) via the main corridor. No locks, no restricted materials.

### The Temple Layer

The temple (A1–A9) is physically below the villa, accessed via C29's staircase. The architectural shift from villa to temple is deliberate and abrupt — plaster ceilings become stone vaults, portraits become iconography, and the temperature drops ten degrees. No ambient temple sounds are audible from the villa during normal operations. During cult gatherings, a low murmuring may reach C28 (wine cellar) if the party is specifically listening (DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)).
