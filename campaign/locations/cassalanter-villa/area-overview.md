# Cassalanter Villa — Area Overview

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Area Map Context
>
> Cassalanter Villa sits in the Sea Ward, set back from the street behind an iron gate and a topiary garden maintained for two generations. The building is four stories above ground, with a basement (wine cellar and false vault) and a sub-basement (secret vault and stairway to the temple level below).
>
> **Floors:** Ground (C1, C2, C4, C9, C10, C13); Upper ground / second (C15, C22 approaches); Third (C6, C22); Attic (C24); Garden-level (C25); Basement (C27, C28); Sub-basement stair (C29 → A1).
>
> **Event use:**
> - **ev-05 (The Cassalanter Dinner):** Fireball event, optional. The party arrives through the gate and is received in C1 (entrance hall). The dinner takes place in C9 (dining room — see below). Refer to the **The Cassalanter Dinner** Event for the full dinner sequence.
> - **Cassalanter Villa (Cassalanter Villa heist):** Primary heist event. All 17 keyed areas are operational. The villa layer (C1–C29) is social infiltration; the temple layer (A1–A9) is a dungeon.

## Gameplay Details

The areas of Cassalanter Villa have the following features unless noted otherwise.

### Levels and Elevation

- **Ground floor** (street level, through the garden entrance): C1 (entrance hall), C2 (garden mudroom), C4 (reading room), C9 (dining room), C10 (kitchen), C13 (ballroom)
- **Second floor**: C15 (banquet hall), C5 (landing — 2 guard posts during events), C6 access staircase
- **Third floor**: C6 (Victoro's office), C22 (Ammalia's study), C23 (closet/attic access)
- **Attic**: C24 (Osvaldo's prison)
- **Garden level**: C25 (butterfly garden, walled exterior)
- **Basement**: C27 (false vault), C28 (wine cellar)
- **Sub-basement**: C29 (secret vault) and staircase to temple level A1

### Illumination

Day: ground floor naturally lit through tall windows; upper floors reliant on corridor lanterns. Night: all floors dim except C1 (entrance lanterns), C13 (event lighting during parties), and C25 (garden torches). Temple level (A1–A9): dark unless cult activities are in progress, in which case torchlight throughout.

### Inhabitants — Normal Operations

**Willifort Crowelle** (doppelganger, tiefling disguise): Circulates C1, C5 lower, and service corridors. *Detect Thoughts* active at all times within 30 feet. Does not engage combat unless cornered.

**Victoro Cassalanter** (9th-level spellcaster): Occupies C4 or C6 during daylight hours. Combat behavior: *Spiritual Weapon*, *Hold Person* (divide and control), *Shield of Faith* on Ammalia, *Banishment* on Ammalia if she is reduced to 30 HP.

**Ammalia Cassalanter** (9th-level spellcaster): Occupies C22 or second-floor family rooms. Combat behavior: *Charm Person* (5th-level, up to 5 targets), *Dominate Person* on most dangerous combatant, *Counterspell* defensively. Arrives to aid Victoro within 2 rounds of hearing combat.

**Jandar Chergoba** (chef): C10 during kitchen hours. Can animate ten flying swords (command word "For'zaal"; deactivation word "Basta").

**Helmed Horror** (C6): Activates on unauthorized desk contact. AC 20, HP 60. Does not leave the building.

**Area guards:** 4 at villa perimeter (2 front gate, 2 garden gate), 2 at C5 second-floor landing, 1 at C28 (wine cellar, night shift only).

**Two imps in spider form** (C24, Osvaldo's prison): Passive observers. Attack if Osvaldo is threatened or freed.

**Terenzio and Elzerina Cassalanter** (Tethyrian human children, he/him and she/her; eight, turning nine on Flamerule 11) and **Laiba "Nana" Rosse** (tiefling cult fanatic, she/her): The twins follow the schedule under **The Cassalanter Twins** below and are with Nana Rosse at all times except from 4 to 5 on a day with no event. They are never in C22, C23, C24, the basement, or the temple.

### Inhabitants — Social Events

40–60 civilian guests in C1, lower C5, C9, C13, C15, and C25. Ten servant commoners in service areas. Guards at C5 doubled to 4. Willifort circulates with a tray. C6 helmed horror active regardless. Terenzio and Elzerina greet arriving guests in C1 for the first fifteen minutes, then go upstairs with Nana Rosse.

### The Social Exposure System (Cassalanter Villa)

Four-stage escalation governing social-layer movement. Begins at Stage 1 (Accepted Guest) when the party arrives legitimately. Triggers advance stages; de-escalation is possible at Stage 2 only.

| Stage | Name | Status | De-escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accepted Guest | Free movement in public areas; Willifort monitoring passively | N/A (starting state) |
| 2 | Noticed | Willifort actively probing; guards repositioned near restricted areas | Possible (15 minutes normal behavior) |
| 3 | Suspected | Guards escort the party to Willifort; Victoro notified | Not possible |
| 4 | Exposed | Active confrontation; guards and Willifort converge | Not possible |

### The Cassalanter Twins

Terenzio and Elzerina are eight years old and turn nine on Flamerule 11. Nana Rosse is with them in every row below except where the table says otherwise. The twins know nothing of the contract, the temple, or the feast, and nothing in the villa tells them they are in danger. Terenzio believes his brother Osvaldo is away at school. Nana Rosse is warm with the party and says nothing of the household's secrets. She reports where the twins have been and who they met to the household every evening, and she does not think of that as spying.

C3 (library), C16 (piano room), C17 (playroom), and C18 (the twins' bedroom) have no page in this journal. The table gives everything the party needs from them. From the end of the piano lesson until supper, Terenzio wears his dragon costume and Elzerina wears a knight's helm and carries her wooden sword.

**Day with no event**

| Time | Where | The twins are |
|---|---|---|
| Dawn to 10 | C16 | At a piano lesson. Elzerina plays and Terenzio roars along. Nana Rosse sits beside the piano with her crochet. |
| 10 to noon | C3 | Riding the sliding ladders along the shelves. Nana Rosse sits in an armchair and counts them aloud. |
| Noon to 2 | C25, reached through C2 | Chasing butterflies on the lawn. Nana Rosse sits on a wrought-iron bench. |
| 2 to 4 | C17 | Playing with toys on the floor while Nana Rosse crochets a doll. |
| 4 to 5 | Apart | Terenzio on the third floor outside C6. Elzerina in the kitchen corridor outside C10. Nana Rosse searches the second floor, calling for them. |
| 5 to 6 | C18 | Playing make-believe in costume. |
| 6 to 7 | C9 | At supper with Victoro and Ammalia. Nana Rosse stands at the sideboard. |
| 7 to 9 | C17 | Playing quietly and listening to a story. |
| From 9, every night | C18 | Asleep. Nana Rosse sleeps in C17 next door and does not attend cult gatherings. |

**Other states**

- **Social event (Goldenight, Waukeentide Charity Ball):** C1 for the first fifteen minutes after the doors open, with Ammalia and Nana Rosse, in their best clothes. Then C18 until morning.
- **Caravance (Tarsakh 1):** With Victoro, Ammalia, and Nana Rosse all day, hunting hidden presents. Dawn to 10 in C17, 10 to noon in C1, noon to 2 in C13, 2 to 5 in C25, 5 to 8 at supper in C9 and then C17, and from 8 asleep in C18. Victoro and Ammalia are not in C4, C6, or C22 that day.
- **Fireball dinner:** In C17 all evening and not seen. If the party asks, Willifort says what **The Cassalanter Dinner** gives him to say: the twins are with their nanny and at their lessons, or, for a party that has not met them, not well enough for company.
- **Founders' Day (Flamerule 1):** In C17 with Nana Rosse all day.

**Toward the party.** The twins are friendly to everyone Nana Rosse does not call away. If the party marked **Cassalanter Twins Met**, each twin greets every character who was at the Grand Opening by name.

> *Terenzio (roars first):* "[Name]! You came back! Have you seen any dragons since the opening?"
>
> *Elzerina (pokes the nearest party member with her sword):* "[Name]! Did you fight any monsters? Can I come next time?"

If the party did not mark it, both are shy and polite. Terenzio stays half behind Nana Rosse, and Elzerina steps forward.

> *Elzerina:* "Good day. I'm Elzerina, and this is Terenzio. He's shy."
>
> *Terenzio (from behind Nana Rosse):* "Hello. Are you real adventurers?"

**What they know.** Terenzio says his father is looking for missing dragons and that something howls in the attic sometimes, which he thinks is a dragon. Elzerina says there is a funny door behind a sun in the wine cellar and that her father says something in a funny voice before he goes through the vault door (see C27 and C28). If the party marked **Cassalanter Twins Met**, the twins say this the first time anyone asks about the house. If not, they say it after ten minutes of play.

**Nana Rosse.** She calls the twins "my lambs" and counts them whenever they move. From 4 to 5 she asks anyone she meets on the second floor: "Oh, visitors! Have you seen two little rascals? One's a dragon and one's a knight." If a fight starts in a room with the twins, she takes both by the hand and leaves by the nearest door, placing herself between them and the party.

### The Dining Room (C9)

The dining room is not keyed as a separate location journal page — it is used in Fireball (ev-05) and in Cassalanter Villa social events but has no unique mechanical content. During the Fireball dinner, C9 is set for six: Victoro, Ammalia, the party (up to four), and one household staff member serving. The twins are upstairs and do not come down. On any other evening, the family supper runs from 6 to 7 with Victoro, Ammalia, Terenzio, and Elzerina, and Nana Rosse at the sideboard. During social events: Victoro entertains guests here. The room connects to C10 (kitchen) via a service door and to C1 (entrance hall) via the main corridor. No locks, no restricted materials.

### The Temple Layer

The temple (A1–A9) is physically below the villa, accessed via C29's staircase. The architectural shift from villa to temple is deliberate and abrupt — plaster ceilings become stone vaults, portraits become iconography, and the temperature drops ten degrees. No ambient temple sounds are audible from the villa during normal operations. During cult gatherings, a low murmuring may reach C28 (wine cellar) if the party is specifically listening (DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)).
