# C25 — Butterfly Garden

A walled garden at the villa's rear, filling the space between the garden mudroom (C2) and the east perimeter wall. Tropical species fill the space — species that should not survive Waterdeep's climate. The air is fifteen degrees warmer than the street outside, humid and fragrant with flowers that bloom year-round. Ravens perch in the garden trees and along the top of the perimeter wall.

The Cassalanters maintain this garden as a social amenity. Nobles duel here for entertainment, under the shade of flowering trees, while other guests place wagers. Ammalia presents it as a conservation project — birds rescued from the docks, butterflies bred from imported specimens.

### Normal — Day Operations

Six imps in raven form perch in the garden. They move between the trees, the wall, and occasionally the garden's wrought-iron benches. They observe all movement through the garden and around the villa's perimeter, reporting to Ammalia through a one-way telepathic bond. She receives impressions — not words.

**Detecting the imps:** DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) to notice that the ravens' gaze tracks with unnatural precision — they watch one person at a time, systematically. *Detect Magic* reveals fiendish auras on each bird. A DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) check following a detect magic reading identifies the bond type.

**Killing an imp:** The telepathic bond severs with a psychic snap. Ammalia receives the sensation immediately wherever she is. One dead imp: she registers an anomaly and sends a servant to check the garden within five minutes. Three or more dead: she advances the Social Exposure System by one stage and moves toward the garden herself.

Marchello the gardener tends the garden at dawn and dusk. He is aware that the ravens watch people ("Regular birds don't watch you"). He has not connected this to anything supernatural — Ammalia told him they were rescue birds from the docks.

### Heist — Cassalanter Villa Social Event Configuration

During social events, the butterfly garden hosts 8–15 guests in rotation. Ammalia circulates through here on her twenty-minute pattern. Wine is served by a servant stationed near the garden gate. The imps remain in raven form throughout — the crowd provides cover for their surveillance and they are active throughout the event.

**Entry through the garden gate:** The perimeter garden wall has a service gate at the south end (Tomas the night watchman's blind spot during his patrol). A DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) check enters the garden from outside during the day; DC 16 at night. During social events, the gate is unguarded on the outside — the imps cover surveillance — and a DC 12 Charisma (Deception) check (plausible story about arriving late) allows entry without triggering Stage 2.

**During combat:** Imps use *Invisibility* and attack with their sting in pairs, targeting the character most recently isolated from the group. They retreat to invisibility after each attack.
