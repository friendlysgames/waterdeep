# A3 — Anterior Vestibule

> **[GM]**
>
> This room is **Heist-only**. See A1 — Hall of the Damned for temple access notes.

The vestibule is an antechamber to the ceremonial hall (A7). Eighteen feet of oil painting dominates the south wall — Asmodeus in ceremonial pose, arms extended, the infernal court arrayed behind him. The brushwork is excellent. The paint is recent — applied over a portrait of Siamorphe rendered in the same ceremonial pose, the under-composition visible in the layered brushstrokes. A DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals the original figure's silhouette: a woman in noble dress, arms extended in welcome rather than command.

The doors to A7 are at the far end — iron-bound oak, unlocked, heavy. They require Strength 14 to push open, or two creatures of any strength working together.

### Heist — Arc G Temple Layer

**Inhabitants — Non-Gathering.** The vestibule is empty during non-gathering periods. No permanent guard assignment outside the ceremonial hall.

**Inhabitants — Cult Gathering.** One bearded devil stands before the doors to A7, arms crossed. The devil is not hostile to robed cultists — it steps aside for anyone wearing a crimson robe and golden mask (the standard cult attire). It challenges anyone not in cult regalia with a gesture toward the doors: it will not step aside. It does not speak Common. If challenged or if a creature attempts to push past it, it attacks. It does not pursue past the vestibule — the ceremonial hall is not its assigned space.

**Bypassing the devil.** Options:
- Cult regalia (crimson robes, golden mask): the devil steps aside automatically
- *Disguise Self* or *Alter Self* presenting as a robed cultist: the devil is unintelligent; DC 14 Charisma (Deception) check if interacting directly, automatic pass if moving through without challenge
- Turn the devil as a fiend: requires Cleric or Paladin with Channel Divinity (Turn Undead covers fiends for Paladin's Sacred Weapon variant; standard Turn Undead does not apply to devils). On a successful turn, the devil retreats past the A7 doors
- Combat: Multiattack, beard, 2024 bearded devil statistics

**The Painting's Significance.** The Siamorphe under-image is not merely an artistic curiosity — it is evidence of deliberate desecration. Any party member who has been told about or found Caladorn's ghost (A4a) and his demand to "restore my family's honor" receives the context immediately: the Cassalanters did not simply adopt Asmodean worship. They erased Siamorphe's presence from the temple systematically. This evidence, combined with the defaced statue in A7, establishes the scope of the apostasy.
