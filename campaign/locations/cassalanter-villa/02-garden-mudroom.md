# C2 — Garden Mudroom

A narrow service room between the butterfly garden (C25) and the villa's west corridor. Hooks hold gardening cloaks and riding gear. A bench with muddy boot-scrapes sits beside the garden door. The room smells of turned earth and something floral — the butterfly garden's warmth bleeds through the door.

### Normal — Day Operations

The mudroom is a servant thoroughfare during the day. Marchello the gardener passes through at dawn and dusk; kitchen staff use it when taking food to the garden pavilion. No restricted materials, no lock on the garden door. A search of the room takes two minutes and yields nothing useful.

### Heist — Cassalanter Villa Social Event Configuration

During social events, the mudroom is the staging area for the parallel Esvele Rosznar heist. Esvele arrives as a guest of the Rosznar family. Within the first thirty minutes, she excuses herself from the ballroom, slips through the service corridor, and enters C2.

A party member who enters C2 during the event catches her mid-change — one glove on, the Black Viper's mask in her hand, leathers half-laced. She freezes. Her first instinct is to bolt for the garden door. If the party blocks the exit or speaks first, she holds still and reassesses. She is seventeen; she is not entirely sure how much trouble this is yet.

**Recruit her as a temporary ally:** DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion). She is defensive but not hostile. She does not know the Cassalanters are anything other than rich nobles with good locks. If the party tells her what the villa actually contains — a cult, a trapped child in the attic, a temple beneath the floor — she goes quiet. She had no idea. She came for silverware. She will help, but she sets one condition: the children don't get hurt.

**The Esvele Interaction Table** (fires whether or not the party enters C2):

| Party State | Esvele's Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Social, Stage 1–2 | Robs upper floors while party works below | Creates noise upstairs — guards investigate; reduces pressure on party |
| Detected (Stage 3+) | Uses chaos to accelerate theft | Guards split; evidence of second intruder creates confusion |
| In temple layer | Completes theft and escapes cleanly | No direct interaction; guards on higher alert when party resurfaces |
| Direct encounter in C2 | Freeze, then negotiate | DC 14 Persuasion to recruit: distraction capability + rooftop escape route + Vault of Dragons clue |
| Party captures or exposes her | Bargains with information | Her Viper Den sits above the Brandath Crypts; she has found "old tunnels" beneath — Vault of Dragons path to Vault entry |

If recruited, Esvele provides distraction on request (fainting spell, spilled drink, loud argument), cover stories for the party in the banquet areas, and the rooftop escape route from Scene 5. She will not enter the temple.
