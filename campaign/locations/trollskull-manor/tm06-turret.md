# Turret

The turret occupies the northeast corner of the building, rising half a floor above the fourth-floor roofline. A tight spiral stair connects it to the fourth floor. One small room, low ceiling, single window facing north over the alley and east toward Book Wyrm's Treasure. Empty weather vane mounting bracket on the roof peak; the weather vane itself is gone.

**Lif's retreat.** During Stage 3 of Lif's escalation, he retreats to the turret when reduced to half hit points. The spiral stair is narrow enough that only one Medium creature can occupy it at a time. The turret room gives Lif a confined space to defend; objects in the room can be thrown from the window at the party in the alley below if the confrontation goes that direction.

> During the **Welcome to Trollskull Alley** Event, Lif retreats here when reduced to half hit points. Refer to that Event for the retreat and resolution sequence.

**Resolution window.** When Lif retreats to the turret and the party follows without weapons drawn, appeasement becomes possible again — the retreat reads as a defensive move, not an attack, and Lif is not incapable of reading the same signal in return. The behavioral appeasement track can resume here even after Stage 3 escalation if the party approaches without hostility.

**Post-appeasement state.** Once Lif is appeased, the turret is empty most of the time. Lif prefers the taproom. He occasionally manifests in the turret window when something in the alley concerns him; the party may notice the weather vane bracket moving on its own as a signal.
