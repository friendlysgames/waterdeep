# Private Rooms (4th Floor)

The fourth floor is the building's residential level — quieter than the public floors, accessed via the main stair or the back stair from the kitchen. Rooms are smaller and more finished than the upper floors; whoever built this level intended private occupancy. One room has a balcony with a direct view of the courtyard oak; the oak's canopy is close enough to climb at this height.

**Renovation state.** Habitable, if sparsely. The party can use these rooms as living quarters from the day they arrive; they are not part of the active renovation work. The Carpenters' Guild checks the window frames; the rest is cosmetic. Lif ignores this floor almost entirely during the escalation — this is the party's space and he respects the distinction.

**Operational state.** Party residential quarters plus staff rooms. The balcony access to the oak is a detail the staff candidates notice during touring; it comes up in the interviews.

**Balcony and oak.** The balcony connects to the third-floor balcony by an exterior ladder and to the courtyard directly by the oak's upper limbs. A character comfortable with heights can move between the fourth floor and the courtyard without using the stairs. **DC 12 Dexterity (Acrobatics)** to descend the oak without incident.
