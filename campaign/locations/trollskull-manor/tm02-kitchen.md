# Kitchen

Ground floor rear, separated from the taproom by a service corridor and the serving hatch. Two chimney stacks serve the kitchen hearth and the baking oven. The prep surface is stone; the storage shelves are built into the walls.

**Renovation state.** The kitchen is the most labor-intensive renovation target outside the taproom — grime, broken equipment, and an oven that needs relining before it can be used safely. The Carpenters' Guild work includes the kitchen shelving. The Bakers' Guild inspector checks the oven. Embric, who critiqued the chimney flashing in the courtyard, is also available for the kitchen hearth — his assessment ("it was always wrong") applies here too.

**Operational state.** Staffed kitchen with a hire from the **Trollskull Manor** guide cook candidates. Functional oven and hearth. The dice game Floon keeps losing is played at the prep table after service.

**Lif's Stage 2 activity.** During Stage 2 of Lif's escalation, message-writing moves to the kitchen surfaces as well as the taproom. A flour-covered counter becomes a medium.
