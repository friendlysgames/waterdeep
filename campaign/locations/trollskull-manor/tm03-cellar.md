# Cellar

Below the ground floor; accessed via a heavy door off the kitchen corridor. Stone walls, low ceiling, flagstone floor. Storage space for casks, cold goods, and building supplies. No windows.

**The sewer grate (T7).** At the east end of the cellar, a heavy iron grate set into the floor opens onto city sewer infrastructure. The Cellarers' and Plumbers' Guild inspector identified this connection during the ev-02 permit inspection and flagged it for "structural heritage documentation." The infrastructure predates the current building by at least two centuries.

The grate is not locked — the previous locking mechanism rusted out at some point and was never replaced. It is heavy enough to require a DC 12 Strength check to lift quietly.

**Attunement note.** The **Sewer Grate Documented** attunement (set in ev-02) means this passage is on the guild permit record. Faction response teams using a sewer approach to the alley have this connection available. Cross-ref Arc C for the party's next encounter with the sewer network.

**Lif does not manifest here.** The cellar is below Lif's area of effect. It is the one genuinely quiet room in the building during the renovation period.

**Storage function.** After opening, the cellar holds tavern stock: wine casks, ale casks (Hammond Kraddoc's guild-recommended selections in pride of place), preserved goods from the Guild of Butchers and Bakers' Guild suppliers.

Cross-ref **ev-02** (Cellarers' inspection, sewer grate flag), **Arc C** (sewer network re-entry, response team routing).
