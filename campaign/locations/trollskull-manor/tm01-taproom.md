# Taproom

The ground floor's main public space. Bar along the north wall, hearth on the east wall, tables distributed across the room. The brass bell above the serving hatch still works; Lif rings it without being asked when last call approaches.

**Renovation state.** Broken tables cleared. Tarnished silverware removed. The vinegar-casks gone from behind the bar. The floor swept but not yet sanded; the bar surface stripped and being refinished; the hearth cleaned but cold. The chalk slate behind the bar has Lif's commentary from time to time.

**Operational state.** Furnished and staffed. Bar tools in place (laid out by Lif before anyone asks). Hearth lit when weather turns. Tables and chairs from Tally Fellbranch's repair work. The chalk slate has the current specials in handwriting that isn't anyone's.

**Lif's territory.** The taproom is Lif's primary area of manifestation. During Stage 1, objects move here first. During Stage 2, the messages appear here. At Stage 3, the furniture becomes a barrier and the glassware becomes a projectile. After appeasement, the taproom is where Lif chooses to be.

> During the **Welcome to Trollskull Alley** Event, this room is Lif's primary territory. Refer to that Event for escalation and appeasement details.

**Staff interview space.** During the hiring period before the Grand Opening (ev-06), the taproom serves as the interview area. Candidates are seated across the bar; Lif is present and has opinions he expresses through the chalk slate.

**Grand Opening crowd zone.** On opening night, this room holds the bulk of the guests. Starting Fame established per Appendix F, Part 2.
