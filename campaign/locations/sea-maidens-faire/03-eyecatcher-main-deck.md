# J1 — Eyecatcher: Main Deck

The Eyecatcher's main deck is identical in layout to the Heartbreaker's but the atmosphere is different. The ship rides at anchor a quarter-mile from the pier; the harbor moves around it. No boarding ramp — visitors arrive by skiff, by swim, or by invitation. Two nimblewright valets move between deck tasks in a visible routine (an unsubtle reminder of what the ship sells). A mate and two drow elite warriors are stationed at the aft castle entrance.

Captain Zardoz Zord maintains an open-door policy for interesting visitors. His assistants on the docked ships accept messages and arrange meetings; skiff pilots near the harbor steps know the Eyecatcher and charge 2 sp for the crossing.

### Normal — Fireball Investigation

Day: Zord's assistants greet arriving skiffs and escort guests to the aft castle, where a steward handles introductions. A party that has arranged a meeting proceeds directly to J29 (guest cabin). A party that boards without arranging a meeting is detained politely at the deck level by the steward; DC 16 Deception to convince the steward a meeting was arranged; DC 14 Persuasion to get a message to Zord and wait for his response (10 minutes, then Zord invites them below).

**Unauthorized boarding:** DC 14 Stealth to board from a skiff without challenge during the day. DC 16 Stealth at night. The two drow elite warriors are stationary at the aft castle entrance and will not pursue past the main deck without authorization from the captain.

**Searching the main deck:** No sensitive materials on deck. The nimblewright valets observe everything but do not act without a command. Thirty minutes of searching yields nothing useful.

**The drow illusion:** All crew appear human. DC 20 Wisdom (Perception) to notice something wrong with the uniformity of their movements. Truesight reveals drow.

### Heist — Sea Maidens Faire Operational State

*Provisional — subject to revision at Sea Maidens Faire decomposition.*

The Eyecatcher's main deck is the Sea Maidens Faire primary landing zone for the harbor approach entry method. During the heist, the ship is in high alert — Jarlaxle has been tipped to the party's approach through arc-condition tracking. Two additional drow elite warriors are on deck (total: 4 at the aft castle). The nimblewright valets have been activated for defense; each has AC 13, 45 HP, Multiattack (sword × 2, +5 to hit, 1d6+3 slashing).

The skiff entry route is monitored from the aft castle by a spyglass operator during Sea Maidens Faire. DC 18 Stealth to approach the hull unseen at night.
