# Sea Maidens Faire — Area Overview

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Area Map Context
>
> The Sea Maidens Faire fleet occupies a private pier in the Dock Ward (Smugglers' Dock area) and the adjacent anchorage quarter-mile out in Deepwater Harbor.
>
> - **Heartbreaker** and **Hellraiser**: docked galleons at the private pier, painted crimson and gold, flying Faire pennants. Boarding ramps open during daylight hours (6th bell of the morning to 10th bell of the evening).
> - **Eyecatcher**: anchored a quarter-mile out in Deepwater Harbor. Not accessible by boarding ramp — requires skiff, swim, or direct invitation.
> - **Scarlet Marpenoth**: Lantanese submersible, keel-docked beneath the Eyecatcher via a pressurized collar. Not visible from the surface except at night with a DC 18 Perception check from the water's edge.
>
> Carnival stalls and bright canvas awnings fill the pier between the docked ships. Performer accommodations and carnival supplies are distributed across the fleet's holds.
>
> **Event use:**
> - **ev-05 (The Sea Maidens Faire):** Primary event. All three investigation paths (ask Zord / steal ledger / crystal ball) run here. Refer to the **The Sea Maidens Faire** Event for path mechanics, boarding DCs, and ledger details.
> - **Arc H (Sea Maidens Faire heist):** Full heist operation across the fleet. Heist sections of each room file are provisional; Arc H decomposition may revise them.

## Gameplay Details

The areas of the Sea Maidens Faire fleet have the following features unless noted otherwise.

### Illumination

Day: all decks open to natural light; below-deck areas supplemented by lanterns. Night: exterior deck lanterns lit (dim light, 20-foot intervals); interior areas fully lit during active watch, dim otherwise.

### Inhabitants

**Shared crew (all three ships):** Drow in human form — the human illusion is projected from the gilded drow figurehead on each ship's forecastle (J8). Any creature looking at a crew member sees a human. Truesight, *See Invisibility*, or DC 20 Wisdom (Perception) (advantage with Truesight) reveals drow. The illusion is a continuous magical effect; dispelling the figurehead drops the crew's disguises fleet-wide.

**Heartbreaker and Hellraiser:** Carnival performers (commoners), two drow gunslingers guarding J27 (smokepowder storage), standard crew complement.

**Eyecatcher:** Nimblewright valets circulating the main deck, drow elite warriors as personal guard, Jarlaxle / Zardoz Zord in J29 (guest cabin) or J30 (private cabin) during business hours.

**Scarlet Marpenoth:** Three gnome pilots (U7b), Breena Bafflestone (gnome engineer, U2), three drow gunslingers (U3, U4, U5), four off-duty gnome engineers (U8).

### Enemies

**Normal (Arc C investigation):** No standing combat encounters during the investigation. Crew confront unauthorized boarders with warning, then physical restraint — not lethal force unless drawn weapons appear. The gunslingers at J23 have kill-on-sight orders for unauthorized access to J27; avoid that area.

**Alert (post-discovery of theft):** If the party's theft of the ledger is discovered while they are still aboard, two drow elite warriors and a gunslinger respond to the area of the theft within 3 rounds. Response is graduated — confrontation first, combat if the party draws weapons.

**Heist (Arc H):** See Heist sections of each room file. Arc H escalation tiers govern response.

### Boarding

**Heartbreaker and Hellraiser (docked):** Day: boarding ramps open, DC 14 Stealth or DC 14 Persuasion/Deception to board without challenge. Night: one mate and two sailors on deck at J1, DC 16 Stealth or DC 16 Deception to board.

**Eyecatcher (anchored):** Four options: hire a harbor skiff (2 sp, hourly from the harbor steps), swim (DC 12 Athletics, cold Deepwater Harbor water, strong current), clandestine night approach (DC 14 Stealth to reach the hull; DC 16 to board unseen), credible cover story (DC 16 Deception without Zord's prior invitation; automatic with invitation).

**Scarlet Marpenoth (keel-docked):** Access through J30's trapdoor (arcane lock — requires Zord's key or DC 20 Thieves' Tools), or by boarding from outside the hull while underwater (Athletics DC 14 to reach the keel collar, DC 16 to open the pressurized hatch from outside). The pressurized airlock (U1) floods if the inner door and outer hatch are both open while the sub is submerged.

### The Drow Illusion

All crew appear human at all times while at least one figurehead is intact. The figurehead at J8 projects the illusion outward within 200 feet of the ship. Removing or destroying the figurehead on one ship drops that ship's crew's disguises; the other ships are unaffected. The figurehead has AC 15, 30 hit points, and no special resistances.

### Key Tactical Features

- **J27 — Smokepowder Storage (Heartbreaker and Hellraiser):** 20 kegs of smokepowder (5 lbs each). If half the kegs detonate simultaneously, the explosion destroys the ship entirely and deals 20d10 fire damage to all creatures within 100 feet. The gunslingers at J23 have standing kill-on-sight orders for unauthorized persons approaching J27.
- **J16 — Armory (Eyecatcher):** Contains a safe (combination 1-20-59) holding three pistols, twelve smokepowder packets, three thousand bullets, and 250 gp.
- **U7b — Control Room:** A drow pilot can electrify the Scarlet Marpenoth's outer hull as an action; any creature touching the hull takes 4d10 lightning damage.
