# Scarlet Marpenoth — Submersible

The Scarlet Marpenoth is a Lantanese submersible keel-docked beneath the Eyecatcher, connected via a pressurized collar between U1 and J30's trapdoor. The vessel is 80 feet long, 20 feet in beam. Stats: AC 20, 300 hit points, damage threshold 15, max speed 2 mph, capacity 10 passengers and 2 tons of cargo, worth 15,000 gp intact.

From above the water's surface, the submersible is not visible in daylight — the hull sits 15 feet below the waterline. At night, a DC 18 Wisdom (Perception) check from the water's edge near the Eyecatcher's anchorage detects the hull by refracted moonlight on the brass fittings. A creature that swims beneath the Eyecatcher finds the submersible without a check.

A brass model of the Scarlet Marpenoth stands on a pedestal in the House of Inspired Hands main hall. A party that examined the model during ev-03 recognizes the hull configuration visually when they find the actual vessel.

### Normal — Fireball Investigation

#### U1 — Entrance Hatch (Pressurized Airlock)

Entry from J30's trapdoor (arcane locked, see J30) or from outside the hull. Opening the outer hatch from outside requires Athletics DC 14 (against the keel-collar pressure) while the submersible is docked; DC 16 while submerged. If both the inner door and outer hatch are opened simultaneously while the sub is submerged, the airlock floods in one round — any creature inside when the inner door opens takes 1d8 bludgeoning damage per round from the flood pressure and must succeed on a DC 14 Athletics check to reach the inner door before it seals.

During Fireball, the Scarlet Marpenoth is docked and the interior is not submerged. The airlock functions normally as a doorway.

#### U7 — Control Room / Crystal Ball Chamber

The control room occupies two levels: U7a (observation deck, upper) and U7b (command center, lower). U7a contains Jarlaxle's fallback position chair, a bronze periscope, and a bronze crystal ball on a teak stand. U7b contains the pilot console, three gnome pilot stations, and two drow elite warrior guards.

**The Crystal Ball (U7a):** This is the intelligence objective for Fireball's Path 3. The ball is active and has been recording the Eyecatcher's vicinity continuously. Operating it: DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) to activate without spending an attunement slot. A party that succeeds views recorded footage — visual only, no audio — from the morning of Ches 22nd:

- A small figure (Dalakhar) running through Trollskull Alley toward the harbor.
- The nimblewright dropping from the cooperage roof.
- The fireball detonation.
- The nimblewright rifling Dalakhar's body.
- The Stone removed from the body.
- The nimblewright fleeing east across the rooftops.

Definitive evidence. No ambiguity about the perpetrator, the sequence, or the Stone's fate.

On a failure (DC 15 Arcana), the ball activates but shows only static impressions — colors, shapes, no coherent sequence. A second attempt may be made with a different character.

**Accessing U7:** From U1, the path runs through U2 (engine room, Breena Bafflestone on watch), through the stateroom corridor (U3–U6), and up the ladder to U7a. Breena is cooperative unless the party is clearly unauthorized; DC 13 Persuasion to explain their presence convincingly (she's a pragmatic gnome who doesn't enjoy confrontation). The drow elite warriors in U7b will not allow access to U7a without Jarlaxle's authorization; DC 16 Deception to claim authorization.

**What Jarlaxle does if the crystal ball is accessed:** He discovers within 24 hours that the ball was used. He cannot identify who. He does not deploy to Gralhund Villa — this party does not need his help and he is curious rather than angry. He files it as information.

#### Other Significant Compartments

**U3 — Soluun's Stateroom:** Drow gunslinger Soluun Xibrindas, cleaning his weapons. Footlocker: Lantanese diving suit (functions as a *cap of water breathing*; allows underwater breathing for 24 hours, reusable). The suit is the only diving gear aboard.

**U4 — Jarlaxle's Stateroom:** Locked (DC 18 Thieves' Tools). Harp, harpsichord, and a magic one-way window showing the sea outside. The trunk in the corner releases a mechanical spider swarm on unauthorized opening (DC 16 Perception to notice the trap; DC 14 Dexterity to disable; swarm: AC 12, 44 HP, Multiattack). The trunk's false bottom contains the complete ledger after Fireball (if the Eyecatcher ledger was stolen, Jarlaxle moved this copy here; if not stolen, the J30 copy remains there).

### Heist — Sea Maidens Faire Operational State

*Provisional — subject to revision at Sea Maidens Faire decomposition.*

During Sea Maidens Faire, the Scarlet Marpenoth is Jarlaxle's designated extraction route and Sea Maidens Faire's highest-value intelligence target (it holds the *Stone of Golorr* in U4 if Jarlaxle has acquired it through arc conditions). The pressurized keel collar is unlocked from Jarlaxle's command (he expects to need it). The three drow gunslingers are at combat readiness in their staterooms.

The gnome pilots in U7b are under orders to submerge at Jarlaxle's signal — a two-tone chime from U7a. Submerging takes 5 minutes; the exterior hatch seals at 3 minutes. Any creature outside the hull when the hatch seals is left behind.

The Scarlet Marpenoth as an escape vehicle: fits 10 passengers, 2 mph speed (80 feet per round). Not catchable by rowboat. Can surface at any point on Waterdeep's harbor perimeter.
