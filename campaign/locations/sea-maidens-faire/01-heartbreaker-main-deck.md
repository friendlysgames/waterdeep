# J1 — Heartbreaker: Main Deck

The Heartbreaker's main deck extends the full length of the ship — 130 feet bow to stern, 30 feet at the beam. Crimson paint on the gunwales, carnival pennants at the masthead, the gilded drow figurehead at the bow projecting the crew's human illusion into the harbor around it. Four jolly boats hang in their davits, painted with Faire insignia.

The boarding ramp drops from the port gunwale to the pier. A mate and two sailors are on deck at all times: the mate at the base of the boarding ramp checking arrivals, the sailors at the mainmast and aft castle respectively.

### Normal — Arc C Investigation

Day: the boarding ramp is open. The mate acknowledges visitors with a nod and waves them aboard unless they appear armed for combat or are visibly skulking. The pier-side carnival (stalls, awnings) provides enough traffic that boarding groups blend in. DC 14 Stealth or DC 14 Persuasion/Deception to board without challenge if the party's manner is unusual.

The main deck has no locked areas and no sensitive materials. Visiting the deck yields only the general carnival atmosphere — performers rigging lights, crew loading supplies through the cargo elevator at J12.

Searching accessible areas: 30 minutes, nothing incriminating. The locked officer's quarters are below.

Night: DC 16 Stealth to board unseen. Two additional sailors are posted at the aft castle. The mate at the ramp has instructions to log all arrivals.

### Heist — Arc H Operational State

*Provisional — subject to revision at Arc H decomposition.*

The main deck becomes the primary landing zone for three of the six Arc H entry points. During the heist, the Faire is in evening performance mode: carnival noise from the pier covers sound within 60 feet; torches and lanterns light the deck at bright-light intensity; performer movement creates a natural traffic pattern that allows for cover at medium density.

Arc H watch rotation: mate at ramp (10-minute circuit), two sailors on patrol (staggered 7-minute east/west sweep), one drow elite warrior at the aft castle entrance. Sightline gap between the two sailors' patrol routes: a 4-foot window on the port side, midships, lasting approximately 30 seconds.

The jolly boats are potential extraction routes (each seats 8, requires two sailors to row at speed).
