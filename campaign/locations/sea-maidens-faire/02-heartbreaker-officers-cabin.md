# J10 — Heartbreaker: Captain's Cabin

The captain's cabin occupies the aft castle, second deck down from the main deck. A drow mage captain commands the Heartbreaker — human appearance, efficient manner, does not deal with civilian inquiries. The cabin door is locked (DC 16 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)). A nimblewright valet stands in the corridor outside during daylight hours, non-threatening but observant (Passive Perception 16).

Inside: four lanterns, a navigation chart table, a locked trunk (DC 14 Thieves' Tools; contains 250 gp, a string of pearls worth 100 gp, and a hidden spellbook in the false bottom — DC 16 Investigation to find), and a folio of waxed vellum on the chart table.

### Normal — Arc C Investigation

**The Partial Ledger:** The folio sits on the chart table beside the navigation instruments. DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) to locate within the cabin's papers (the cabin has substantial documentation; the folio is not prominently displayed). The folio contains nine rows of entries on waxed vellum — seven rows excised cleanly with a blade, two surviving:

| Unit | Crystal | Date | Buyer | Address | Balance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NW-01 | CR-01 | Marpenoth 3 | B. Summertaen | Watchful Order, Castle Ward | 25,000 gp |
| NW-05 | CR-05 | Uktar 4 | [excised] | Yellowspire, Castle Ward | 25,000 gp |

The seven excised rows are cut so precisely that the remaining vellum shows only the binding stitch holes. The cuts were made intentionally, before the folio was placed here — the remaining rows were left deliberately. NW-02 (the Gralhund entry) is one of the seven removed rows. The partial ledger confirms the network exists and establishes the scale of sales; it does not reach the Gralhund connection.

**Accessing the cabin:** The nimblewright valet in the corridor will not impede entry if the party appears to have legitimate business — but it will report the entry to the captain if anything is disturbed. DC 14 Stealth to enter and search without the valet noticing the activity in the corridor. Alternatively, drawing the captain into conversation (DC 15 Persuasion) provides unobserved access time.

If the ledger is taken and the theft discovered (within 24 hours), Jarlaxle knows the folio is missing. He cannot identify who took it. He does not deploy to Gralhund Villa.

### Heist — Arc H Operational State

*Provisional — subject to revision at Arc H decomposition.*

The captain's cabin is the Heartbreaker's command center during the Arc H heist. The drow mage captain is in residence and alerted. The nimblewright valet is armed with a light crossbow and has been instructed to fire at unauthorized entrants without warning.

The partial ledger is no longer present — it has been removed to the Eyecatcher after the Arc C theft. The trunk remains, plus two additional scrolls (fireball, counterspell) for the captain's use during the heist response.

The cabin's door lock has been upgraded post-Arc C: DC 20 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools).
