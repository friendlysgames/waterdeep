# Q01 — Central Hub

The tunnel opens into a wider chamber, the intersection of two sewer passages. One foot of sewage on the floor, the same as the approach tunnel. The smell is worse here — the cesspit in Q06 is not well-sealed. The western passage continues into the complex. Two alcoves open off the entry chamber, one north and one south, each just large enough for a man and a bedroll.

A faint dripping echoes from somewhere west. No other sound in unaware state.

> The southern tunnel wall has a section that doesn't quite match the surrounding stonework — tighter joints, slightly different color mortar, like a patch over a hole. A character who examines the south wall can spot the outline of a concealed door with a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check.

The secret door leads south into a short rough-hewn passage, then up into Q09 (the Peabody family cellar via Q08). In alert state, the Q02 goblins may be visible from here — see Q02 for details.

### Alert State — Q01 Approach

In alert state, the Q02 goblins are awake and watching this chamber from their alcoves. They cannot be seen from the entry tunnel until the party enters Q01. The party has one round to act before the goblins react — they shout (waking Q05) and throw javelins.

A DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) check (made before entering Q01) may allow one or more party members to enter without immediately being spotted. The goblins have disadvantage on Perception checks against unlit targets beyond 30 feet.

### Zhentarim Warehouse Event

During the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event, the alert state at this chamber is determined by the sewer navigation outcome and gazer encounter before the party reaches Q01. Refer to the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event for full details.
