# Q07 — Boss Chamber

A vaulted chamber, larger than the rooms before it — the old filtration vault's main processing space. Stone benches run along the walls. A raised platform occupies the south side of the room, one step up, with a low stone chair on it. The chair looks improvised from salvage: heavier stones stacked and mortared into something that passes for a throne if you squint.

> When the party enters: Grum'shar stands in the center of the floor with his boot on the chest of a man in torn fine clothes, fire burning in slow spirals around his clenched fist. The man on the floor is breathing — barely. Seated on the raised platform, cradling something small and pulsing in its lap, is a creature the party has no word for yet: a rounded head, three or four thin tendrils hanging below it, pale and completely still, watching.

#### Grum'shar

CE half-orc wizard. He is in the middle of an interrogation that has not been going well — Floon does not know anything. Grum'shar knows this by now but has not accepted it. He is focused on the floor in front of him when the party enters.

**On the party's arrival:** He spins to face them, fire already in his hand. If Nihiloor is still in the room, he looks to it briefly — one beat — and then the intellect devourer lands on the floor at his feet and he understands the situation. He fights to buy time for Nihiloor's escape, targeting the most obvious melee threat first.

**Tactics:** *Burning hands* on a cluster of party members; *shield* when hit; *witch bolt* to lock down a single target at range. He does not flee — his orders were to hold this position.

**Satchel:** On the floor near the platform. Contains his spellbook: *burning hands*, *disguise self*, *false life*, *shield*, *unseen servant*, *witch bolt*.

#### Nihiloor

Present when the party arrives (unless the failure state applies). Seated on the raised platform, cradling an intellect devourer in its lap.

**On the party's arrival:** The moment Nihiloor sees the party, it rises. It sets the intellect devourer down — the creature is now loose and hostile. Nihiloor then glides toward the western double door without pausing, heading for Q11. It does not look at the party again.

If anything blocks its escape path: *dominate monster* (DC 15 Wisdom saving throw). It uses this once, on the most immediate obstacle, and keeps moving. It does not engage beyond what is necessary to clear its path.

Nihiloor does not speak. It does not acknowledge Grum'shar's fate. It is simply leaving.

#### Floon Blagmaar

On the floor, 1 hit point. His fine clothes are torn and stained. He is conscious — barely. He does not know why he was taken. If healed or helped to his feet, his first question is about Renaer.

#### Chest

Behind the stone chair on the raised platform, unlocked: two potions of healing, 16 gp, 82 sp, 250 cp.

### Q07 — Failure State

If the party failed all three sewer navigation checks: Grum'shar and Nihiloor are gone. The intellect devourer may still be here (GM discretion — it may have followed Nihiloor or been left loose in the complex). Floon's body is on the floor. The chest is still present unless Grum'shar looted it before leaving.

### Xanathar Sewer Hideout Event

During the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event, Grum'shar and Nihiloor are present when the party arrives (unless the failure state applies). Nihiloor uses this chamber as a staging point for its visit before proceeding to Q11. Refer to the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event for the full encounter description, the Nihiloor restraint guidance, and the failure state conditions.
