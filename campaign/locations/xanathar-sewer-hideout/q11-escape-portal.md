# Q11 — Escape Portal

An otherwise empty room at the western end of the complex. Stone floor, bare walls, low ceiling. The air here is different from the rest of the hideout — drier, faintly charged, like the air before a lightning strike. The smell of sewage doesn't reach this room.

In the center of the floor stands a stone pillar, roughly five feet tall and two feet in diameter, carved from a single piece of dark granite. The carving on its face is precise and deliberate: a circle of ten equidistant spokes (Xanathar's guildsign) centered on a small circular indentation at chest height. It is approximately three inches in diameter, shaped like a lidless eye, slightly recessed, smooth at the center and beveled at the edges — built to receive something specific.

> The pillar is cold to the touch. The guildsign's carving is shallow but sharp-edged, cut clean and untouched by the damp or grime that coats every other surface down here. The indentation at the center seems slightly deeper than it looks from a distance.

#### Portal Mechanics

**Activation:** Nihiloor's 3-inch stone orb, when pressed into the indentation, opens an opaque black doorway 8 feet tall and 4 feet wide in the south wall. The portal leads to area X22 of Xanathar's lair. Any creature that steps through appears in X22. It remains open for 1 minute after the orb is removed, then closes. It can't be held open by other means.

**Without the orb:** The portal can't be activated. It accepts nothing else. The pillar is inert.

**After Nihiloor's escape:** In normal play, Nihiloor has already used it and taken the orb before the party reaches Q11. The south wall shows no seam or door. The pillar stands inert. The charged-air quality of the room persists regardless.

#### Investigation

**DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana):** This is a socket for a specific magical object: an activation mechanism. The portal's geometry is consistent with planar transit rather than teleportation.

**DC 16 Intelligence (History):** The guildsign on the pillar is Xanathar's personal mark, the lidless eye symbol used by the Xanathar Guild. This specific arrangement (circle, ten spokes, indentation at center) appears in accounts of Xanathar's lair architecture. The pillar was placed here deliberately, as a fixed transit point.

### Xanathar's Lair Seed

This room is the campaign's first physical connection to Xanathar's lair. When the party infiltrates the lair in Xanathar's Lair, they will enter from the other side of this portal, arriving in X22, recognizing the transit architecture, and understanding that the pillar they examined in the sewers under Trollskull Alley is the same system they are now using. The party can't follow Nihiloor through in Finding Floon. By Xanathar's Lair, they will have the means.

### Xanathar Sewer Hideout Event

During the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event, Nihiloor uses it to escape. The party can't follow. Refer to the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event for the full escape sequence and the implications for Xanathar's Lair.
