# Q11 — Escape Portal

An otherwise empty room at the western end of the complex. Stone floor, bare walls, low ceiling. The air here is different from the rest of the hideout — drier, faintly charged, like the air before a lightning strike. The smell of sewage does not reach this room.

In the center of the floor stands a stone pillar, roughly five feet tall and two feet in diameter, carved from a single piece of dark granite. The carving on its face is precise and deliberate: a circle of ten equidistant spokes — Xanathar's guildsign — centered on a small circular indentation at chest height. The indentation is approximately three inches in diameter, shaped like a lidless eye, slightly recessed, smooth at the center and beveled at the edges. It is clearly designed to receive something.

> The pillar is cold to the touch. The guildsign's carving is shallow but sharp-edged, as though cut recently, or as though it does not wear the way stone should. The indentation at the center seems slightly deeper than it looks from a distance.

#### Portal Mechanics

**Activation:** Nihiloor's 3-inch stone orb, when pressed into the indentation, opens an opaque black doorway 8 feet tall and 4 feet wide in the south wall. The portal leads to area X22 of Xanathar's lair. Any creature that steps through appears in X22. The portal remains open for 1 minute after the orb is removed from the indentation, then closes. It cannot be held open by other means.

**Without the orb:** The portal cannot be activated. The indentation accepts nothing else. The pillar is inert.

**After Nihiloor's escape:** In normal play, Nihiloor has already used the portal and taken the orb with it before the party reaches Q11. The south wall shows no seam or door. The pillar stands inert. The charged-air quality of the room persists regardless.

#### Investigation

**DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana):** The indentation is a key socket for a specific magical object — an activation mechanism, not a lock. The portal's geometry is consistent with planar transit rather than teleportation.

**DC 16 Intelligence (History):** The guildsign on the pillar is Xanathar's personal mark — the lidless eye symbol used by the Xanathar Guild. This specific arrangement (circle, ten spokes, indentation at center) appears in accounts of Xanathar's lair architecture. The pillar was placed here deliberately, as a fixed transit point, not improvised.

### Arc F Seed

This room is the campaign's first physical connection to Xanathar's lair. When the party infiltrates the lair in Arc F, they will enter from the other side of this portal — arriving in X22, recognizing the transit architecture, and understanding that the pillar they examined in the sewers under Trollskull Alley is the same system they are now using. The party cannot follow Nihiloor through in Arc A. By Arc F, they will have the means.

### Xanathar Sewer Hideout Event

During the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event, Nihiloor uses this portal to escape. The party cannot follow. Refer to the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event for the full escape sequence and the implications for Arc F.
