# Q08 — Getaway Passage

A storage room adjacent to the Q07 boss chamber, appearing unremarkable: stone walls, low ceiling, empty shelving along the south wall. The floor is stone like the rest of the complex.

One of the flagstones near the north wall is loose. A character who examines the floor or steps on this flagstone notices: DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) to spot the uneven seam around it; no check required if a character specifically lifts or tests each stone.

> Beneath the flagstone: a short iron ladder descending four feet into a rough-hewn tunnel, just wide enough for a crouching adult, leading north. The air from below is different from anything above — earthen, with traces of yeast.

The tunnel leads north approximately thirty feet, then opens upward through a second flagstone into Q09 (the Peabody cellar). The tunnel was cut by whoever built this complex as a secondary exit — its position below Q08's storage suggests it was meant to remain hidden from anyone who did not know to look.
