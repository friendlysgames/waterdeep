# Q06 — Lavatory

A stone room with a cesspit hole cut into the floor near the north wall, no cover, no screen. A gray ooze has emerged from the pit and spreads across most of the floor, occupying a roughly circular area three feet in diameter and still slowly expanding. Two dead goblins' bones float in it — the goblins sent to deal with it have been dissolved. Their weapons lie on the floor where they dropped them: a short sword and a javelin, both so heavily pitted by acid they cannot be used without breaking.

The gray ooze takes up most of the accessible floor. Moving through the room without triggering it requires navigating around its edges (DC 12 Dexterity [Acrobatics]) or dealing with it first.

The cesspit itself drops down approximately ten feet into a collecting chamber connected to the main sewer flow. Nothing of interest below.

> The ooze doesn't react until touched or disturbed. A character who looks into the cesspit hole smells something that isn't the usual sewer smell — the ooze has been eating into the stone long enough to etch it.

#### Gray Ooze

Hostile. Pseudopod attack corrodes metal weapons and armor on contact. It doesn't pursue beyond the room — the cesspit is its origin point and it has no interest in the corridor.

**Pitted weapons on the floor:** A character who tries to use either dropped weapon makes a DC 10 Strength check or it breaks on the first attack roll (whether or not the attack hits).
