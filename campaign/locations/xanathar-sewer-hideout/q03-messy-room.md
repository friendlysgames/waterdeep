# Q03 — Messy Room

A supply room that was never well-organized and is now less so. Rusted weapons hang on wooden pegs along the walls — short swords pitted to uselessness, a scythe with half its blade gone, a handaxe with a cracked haft. Threadbare clothing hangs between them on the same pegs: a pair of trousers with no waistband, a shirt stiff with old sweat, a leather vest someone stitched badly and gave up on.

A coat rack has fallen across the main passage, blocking easy movement through the room. It takes one action to lift it aside or can be stepped over with a DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check — failure means the rack clatters against the floor (DC 12 Dexterity [Stealth] to muffle it).

Under the pegs sits a dented iron strongbox with its lid pried open and its contents turned out onto the floor: torn sacks, a split ledger spine and a thin brass chain with a snapped clasp. The chain is worth a few coppers. See the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** area overview for what it means.

Otherwise nothing of value. The weapons are too corroded to sell, too dangerous to use safely. The clothing is unwearable by any standard that matters.
