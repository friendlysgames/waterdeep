# Q02 — Watch Posts

Two alcoves flanking Q01 — Q02a to the north, Q02b to the south — each barely large enough for a bedroll and a goblin. The walls are slick. The goblins assigned here pulled their bedrolls as far from the sewage as possible and wedged themselves in.

Both goblins rely entirely on darkvision; they don't carry light sources and wouldn't use them on watch even if they had them.

#### Goblins — Unaware State

Both goblins are asleep. One snores faintly. A character listening at the edge of Q01 hears it with a DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) check. Sneaking past: DC 9 Dexterity (Stealth) check. If a character fails the check, the nearest goblin stirs and opens its eyes; a second failed check (or any sudden sound) wakes both.

A sleeping goblin woken without violence has one disoriented round in which it can be silenced or bargained with before it shouts.

#### Goblins — Alert State

Both goblins are awake and watching Q01. They aren't visible from the entry tunnel, but their position gives them line of sight to most of Q01 as soon as a party member enters the chamber. They react on the first round: one throws a javelin, one shouts toward the west passage (alerting Q05).

During the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event, these goblins begin in the state set by the sewer navigation and gazer outcomes. Refer to the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event for full details.
