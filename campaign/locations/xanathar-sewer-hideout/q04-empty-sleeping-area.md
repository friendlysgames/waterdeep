# Q04 — Empty Sleeping Area

Six straw-stuffed mattresses on the floor, laid in two rows of three. All of them are occupied in the sense that they have been slept in recently — flattened straw, dented centers, a thin blanket on one — but empty now. The occupants are elsewhere in the complex.

The room is otherwise bare. One stub of a tallow candle on the floor near the door, burned out.

A character who listens at the thin wooden door to Q05 hears, without a check: movement, a low argument, something heavy being dragged or braced against the far side of the door.

> The argument through the door is between two voices — one deep and slow, one higher and increasingly frustrated. Neither is speaking clearly enough to make out words through the wood.

Refer to **Q05 — Sleeping Area** for what is happening on the other side of the door.
