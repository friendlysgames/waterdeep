# Q05 — Sleeping Area

Six more mattresses, same condition as Q04. The room has been partially rearranged: three mattresses pushed against the east wall, two chairs upended and wedged against the door to Q06. The door to Q06 is bulging slightly — something pressed against it from the other side.

**Zemk** (duergar) is bracing the chairs against the Q06 door with his body, one boot against the frame, watching the door with the focused attention of someone who has been managing a problem for several hours. His maul is on the floor beside him. He does not intend to move unless the door gives way.

**Krentz** (if he survived ev-01) sits on an upturned mattress against the north wall, arms crossed, watching Zemk's solution with unconcealed skepticism. He has bruises from the Yawning Portal brawl and is in a poor mood. He has been arguing that the chairs approach is not going to work and that someone needs to go back in there with fire.

Neither Zemk nor Krentz is watching the door from Q04. They are entirely focused on Q06. The party may enter without triggering immediate hostility if they move quietly (DC 12 Dexterity [Stealth]) and the complex is in unaware state.

#### Zemk

Duergar. Hostile to anyone who is not Xanathar Guild. He does not negotiate; the gray ooze problem made him pragmatic but not cooperative. He fights to incapacitation.

If the alert state is active, Zemk grabs his maul the moment the Q04 door opens.

#### Krentz — If Present

His reaction depends entirely on ev-01 outcome:
- If the party helped him or stayed neutral at the Yawning Portal: he is wary but not immediately hostile. He will not fight alongside Zemk unless forced. If the party offers a way out, he will consider it.
- If the party helped Yagra against him: he recognizes them immediately and his hostility toward Zemk becomes redirected. He is hostile, but not suicidally so — he assesses whether the party has an overwhelming advantage before committing to a fight.

### Q06 Context

The gray ooze in Q06 escaped from the cesspit and killed two goblins sent to dispose of it. Zemk's chairs-against-the-door strategy is holding for now but will not hold indefinitely. The ooze is not going anywhere — it is content with the bones and pitted weapons on Q06's floor.

### Xanathar Sewer Hideout Event

During the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** Event, Krentz's presence here is conditional on whether he survived the **Yawning Portal** Event. If the party used a peaceful resolution or stayed out of the brawl in ev-01, Krentz is here. If Krentz was incapacitated in ev-01, he is absent.
