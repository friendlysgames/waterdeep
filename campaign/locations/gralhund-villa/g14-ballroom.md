# G14 — Ballroom

Locked (DC 15 thieves' tools or ring of keys). The largest room on the upper floor and the least used. Gilded mirrors, six on the east wall alone, reflect the sconce light from the doorway into a cascade that makes the empty room seem occupied. Tasseled tapestries. Three stained-glass hanging lamps, unlit. A blown-glass stag's head mounted above the fireplace — an eccentric piece of Amnian artisanship that neither Orond nor Yalah likes but neither has removed. The floor is veined white marble, polished to a mirror finish. Tacky crystal chandeliers hang unlit above the dance floor. The ceiling carries a mural: an orgy scene, original to the building, painted over twice and apparently too stubborn to stay covered.

The ballroom has not been used for a party in three years. The last event held here was a private dinner for four people who arrived masked and left before dawn; the maids cleaned up the next morning and the head maid added the incident to a private log she keeps in her bedchamber.

**Unoccupied in both states.** No treasure. No traps. No tactical purpose, except that the ballroom's floor space and mirror arrangement mean that a fight in this room produces considerable confusion about where things are coming from.
