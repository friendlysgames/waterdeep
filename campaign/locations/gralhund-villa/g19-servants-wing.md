# G19 — Servants' Wing

Three rooms along the southeast corner of the upper floor: the head butler's room (north, near the backstairs), the head maid's room (center), and a shared room for the household maids and valets (south, largest of the three). A narrow corridor connects all three.

**Head butler's room (north):** Empty in both states — he died in G6. The room is neat; he was fastidious. A locked box under his bed (DC 14 thieves' tools) holds 40 gp in coin, his employment contract, and a detailed personal log of everything unusual he has observed in the household over the past eight years, written in a fine hand, stored without any apparent intention of ever being used.

**Head maid's room (center):** Empty in both states — she died on the backstairs at Raid Beat 2. Her copy of the ring of keys is not here; she had it on her when she died. Her personal effects include a small locked jewelry box (costume jewelry, 5 gp) and a private log of her own, shorter and more targeted than the butler's — it covers three specific events from the past month that she found worth noting.

**Maids' and valets' shared room (south):**

**Day state:** Four commoners (maids and valets) — resting between duties, mending clothes, playing cards. They are loyal staff, not guards, and will not fight anything more dangerous than a startled cat. If the Alarm fires, they lock the door from inside and wait.

**Night state:** Nine commoners are barricaded inside this room with improvised weapons (clubs, a fireplace poker, a heavy brass candlestick). They have been here since Raid Beat 2 and they are not coming out. They are not hostile to the party — if the party identifies itself as not Zhentarim, the commoners will confirm they are alive and ask, very quietly, if it is over. It is not over. They will wait. They cannot help.
