# G8 — Great Hall

A long room with a red larchwood dining table running its center — twelve chairs per side, two high-backed carved chairs at the ends. Two iron chandeliers hang above the table, each lit with a dozen candles. Tapestries cover the east and west walls, depicting the Gralhund family's history of civic service to Waterdeep in scenes that grow progressively more heroic as they approach the present. A black marble fireplace dominates the north wall.

**Locked cabinets (east wall, DC 15 thieves' tools or ring of keys):** Fine dishes, sets of silverware, and two pairs of silver candlesticks — 50 gp each pair, 100 gp total.

**Family portrait (south wall):** Oil on canvas, three feet by five feet. Lord Orond and Lady Yalah seated, three children standing (Zartan in front, Greth and Tomassin behind). A black-and-tan hound sits at Orond's feet. The hound died two years ago. No one has commissioned a replacement portrait.

**Day state:** Four house guards (veterans), positioned at the corners. They challenge any unfamiliar face that enters without a guard escort.

**Night state:** Ten corpses on the floor — eight house guards and two Zhent thugs — in the aftermath of the main ground-floor engagement (Raid Beats 3–5). The dining table has been overturned as a barricade. Candles spilled from one chandelier; a patch of larchwood floor is scorched but not ignited.
