# G5 — Kitchen

A working kitchen in full operation during the day: two ovens, three prep tables, an iron pot on the central fire, copper cookware hanging from ceiling hooks, a wall-mounted spice rack with labeled jars in alphabetical order. The room smells of wood smoke and roasting meat most of the day.

**Day state:** Two cooks (commoners). They are not guards. If threatened or if combat sounds reach the kitchen, they flee immediately toward G6 or the back door and do not return. They know the household routine — who eats when, which rooms are used — and will answer simple questions if treated cordially. They will not answer questions about Lady Yalah, the upper floor, or "the stone things in the Lady's room."

**Night state:** Empty. The head cook fled screaming toward G5 at Raid Beat 1 when Floxin entered through G6. The oven fires are banked but not extinguished — the iron pot still holds something reduced to carbon.
