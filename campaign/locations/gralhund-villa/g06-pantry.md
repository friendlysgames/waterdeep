# G6 — Pantry

Dry goods, casks of salt fish and preserved vegetables, barrels of flour, and a row of locked cabinet boxes along the east wall holding the household's fine spirits. A back door in the north wall opens to the street outside the perimeter wall — barred from inside with a heavy timber bar.

**Back door:** Barred from inside. Removing the bar from outside requires either a character inside to lift it, or DC 20 Strength — loud, 2d4 minutes of effort, and audible on the street. The bar can be lifted silently from inside in 1 round.

**Day state:** Unoccupied during normal operations. The head butler uses the pantry for morning deliveries; it is otherwise staff-access only.

**Night state:** Two corpses at the Raid Beat 1 entry point — the **head butler** and the **head cook**, surprised mid-conversation and killed quickly. Both are cold and unresponsive by the time the party arrives. The back door is standing open; Floxin's team entered and did not close it behind them.
