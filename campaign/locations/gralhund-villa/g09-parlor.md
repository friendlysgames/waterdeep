# G9 — Parlor

Dainty chairs arranged around a low table, a chaise longue near the window, a wine cabinet along the south wall with six bottles and two crystal decanters. Framed portraits — ancestors, not landscapes — line the walls. A narrow window faces the yard (G2).

**Always-present physical evidence (regardless of day or night state):**

Two house guards are dead in this room. Floxin killed them in the small hours of Ches 24th, while the household slept. He was still a prisoner then; the raid had not yet begun.

One guard died in the dainty chair by the south wall, a crossbow bolt he never heard coming. He is still in the chair; the bolt is lodged between his ribs and he has been there long enough that the room has that specific smell.

The second guard made it to the window. The latch has been pushed outward, not broken, and a dark smear runs down the sill where he gripped it as he fell. He is on the floor beneath the window, on his side.

On the floor between the two bodies: a pair of **Gralhund-stamped manacles** with a sprung lock. This is how Yalah secured Floxin when she revised their arrangement. The manacles are a legal instrument — the house seal is stamped on the inside of both cuffs.

**Wine cabinet:** The lock is cosmetic. Inside: six bottles of decent North Ward vintage (2 sp each) and a half-finished decanter of something Floxin was allowed during his captivity.
