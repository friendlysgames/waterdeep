# G4 — Barracks

Ten bunk beds arranged along the walls in two rows, twenty sleeping positions in total. Personal footlockers are slid under each lower bunk; they hold coins, personal letters, and small personal effects — nothing of value to anyone who isn't the owner. The weapon and armor racks on the south wall are bare during the day (guards carry their equipment) and empty during the night state (guards mobilized or dead).

**Day state:** Off-duty house guards resting or sleeping. They are unavailable and unresponsive unless the Alarm fires or a guard runs in to raise them. Once raised, they respond per the Alarm Escalation Timeline in ev-03 (day context). Some are in partial armor; none are fully kitted. They move to the threat rather than staying to defend an empty room.

**Night state (Beat 10):** Empty. All guards are either dead (G6, G7, G8, G9, G13) or engaged in the G8–G13 fighting. A party that checks the barracks during the raid finds it bare, with overturned bunk frames from the hasty mobilization at Beat 9.
