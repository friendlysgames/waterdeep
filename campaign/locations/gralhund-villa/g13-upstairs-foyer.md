# G13 — Upstairs Foyer

The main landing of the upper floor — a wide, carpeted foyer connecting the top of the main staircase to the corridor leading to G15 (guest suite), G16 (master bedroom), G14 (ballroom), and G18 (children's room). The double doors to G16 face the staircase directly. Two sconce lanterns burn in iron brackets on the south wall. A carved wooden banister runs the top of the staircase.

**Day state:** Two house guards (veterans) posted at the G16 doors. They challenge anyone approaching the master bedroom. "The Lady does not receive visitors upstairs."

**Night state:** Active combat between 4 surviving house guards and 1–2 surviving Zhent thugs. Eight additional bodies on the floor — six guards and two Zhents from the earlier fighting. The sconces are still burning; the carpet is soaked in several places.

If the BD assault sequence is active (*Jarlaxle Informed*): Jarlaxle's gunslingers fire through the G13 upper windows during the raid, targeting combatants in the foyer. The window glass has been blown inward. Cold air and the sounds of the city come through the gaps.
