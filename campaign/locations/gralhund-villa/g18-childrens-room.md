# G18 — Children's Room

Locked (DC 15 thieves' tools or ring of keys — or Yalah's ring of keys). Three beds along the south wall, sized for children: a large one near the door for Zartan, a medium one in the center for Greth, an empty cradle in the corner that was Tomassin's fifteen years ago and has never been removed. Squat wardrobes in dark wood. Two children's desks with unfinished assignments. Play rugs in faded colors over the stone floor.

**Zartan** (13) and **Greth** (10) are noncombatants. In the day state, they are locked in by Yalah as a standing rule whenever she is receiving anyone she does not fully trust — which has been everyone for the past three days. In the night state, they are locked in from the beginning of the raid and have been silent since the screaming started in the kitchen.

Neither child will fight. Greth is crying quietly; Zartan has pressed himself against the wall between the door and the large wardrobe and hasn't moved. Neither will speak to strangers unless spoken to gently, and neither knows anything about the Stone, the Zhentarim, or Asmodeus.

**Yalah's last resort:** If Yalah is truly cornered in G16 — Hrabbaz down, Stone about to be taken, nowhere to run — she uses her ring of keys to unlock G18 and steps inside with her children, locking the door from inside. She does not attack from G18. She waits. A party that breaks this door down and threatens the children has lost Yalah's cooperation for the rest of the campaign.

**During the BD assault sequence:** Yalah retreats here when Fel'rekt's team enters G16. Zartan and Greth are on the other side of the door when Fel'rekt negotiates through it.
