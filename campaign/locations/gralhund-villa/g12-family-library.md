# G12 — Family Library

Locked (DC 15 thieves' tools or ring of keys). Wood-paneled walls, tall bookshelves with rolling ladders on brass rails. Two padded reading chairs: one upholstered in wolf skin, one in tooled leather. A cold fireplace with a carved marble surround. The room smells of old paper and the faint cedar of the wood oil the maids use on the paneling.

**Locked Tome (iron lectern, south corner):** A leather-bound volume, brass-clasped, set on an iron lectern as if on display. The clasp is locked (DC 15 thieves' tools, or Yalah's brass key, which hangs on the chain at her throat). Opening the tome without the key releases **3 specters** (1-minute Material Plane duration; they pursue whoever opened the tome, then dissolve). The tome is the **Gralhund family chronicle**: tiefling lineage, an ancestor's pact with a devil, an amputated tail, the Infernal bloodline that produced Orond's scar. This is lore rather than leverage, but it contextualizes the Asmodean compartment in G16.

**Reading Stand (near the east window) — always present:**

A folded letter rests on the reading stand. It was placed there two weeks ago and no one has moved it.

*A Missive from Castle Ward, signed "Chirada," dated Ches 10th:*

> *"We are now certain that the Second Eye was carried from the warehouse in the Dock Ward to Yellowspire in the Castle Ward. I no longer believe that it remains within Yellowspire, however. After observing the Zhentarim traffic here, I am convinced that they must be using a teleportal within the tower. If we wish to track the Second Eye further, we will need to access that teleportal or otherwise determine where it leads. — Chirada"*

No check is required to read the letter; it's open on the stand. The party needs only to look at it.

Setting this attunement: ***G12 Searched: Chirada Letter Found.*** Pays off in Arc E — Yellowspire, the Second Eye, and Chirada's name.

**Day state only — Lord Orond Gralhund:** Orond is in this room during the day, reading in the wolf-skin chair, a glass of amber spirits on the side table beside him. For his social encounter behavior, refer to the **Daytime Infiltration** Event (ev-03).
