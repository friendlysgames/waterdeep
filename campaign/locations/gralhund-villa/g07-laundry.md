# G7 — Laundry

Scrub buckets, wash basins mounted on wooden frames, mops in a corner rack, and a drying line strung across the south end with the household's linens. A back stair in the east wall connects this room to the servants' wing above (G19).

**Day state:** The **head maid** is working here most of the morning — scrubbing, sorting, directing maids from G19 on their rounds. She carries the **ring of keys** on her belt: all locked interior doors and cabinets in the manor, including G11, G12, G14, G15, the upper floor cabinets, and the G16 bedroom. She is cooperative and will not lie, but she is loyal to Lady Yalah and will not volunteer information about the upper floor, the Stone, or any private household matter.

A character who takes the ring of keys from the head maid — by persuasion, theft, or incapacitation — gains access to all locked rooms and bypasses the Alarm network for all interior doors for the rest of the infiltration.

**Night state:** The head maid is dead on the backstairs (east wall stair, between G7 and G19). She was killed by a Zhent agent at Raid Beat 2. The **ring of keys** is still on her belt. The back stair is clear; the body is against the east wall of the landing.
