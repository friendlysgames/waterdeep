# G16 — Master Bedroom

Double doors — locked from inside (bar latch; DC 18 thieves' tools, or DC 20 Strength, loud) or standing open depending on whether the Alarm has sounded. A canopied bed in the southwest corner, curtains tied back during the day. A claw-footed bathtub in the northwest alcove, behind a folding screen. Open glass doors in the north wall lead to the Balcony (G17). A writing table near the east window. The room smells of cedar and something faintly metallic.

**Always-present — the trunk:**

At the foot of the bed: a locked traveling trunk, iron-banded, with a combination latch that doubles as a key lock.

- **DC 15 thieves' tools** to open the outer lock.
- Once open, the trunk appears to contain folded clothes and shoes.
- **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)** to spot that the bottom of the trunk is false — it sits slightly higher than it should.
- Lifting the false bottom reveals: two **holy symbols of Asmodeus** in chased silver, engraved with a script neither Orond nor Yalah has ever been asked about, and two **liturgical robes** in red and gold with Cassalanter house embroidery worked into the collar and cuffs.

This is the first physical evidence that the Gralhunds and the Cassalanters are members of the same Asmodean cult. It pays off in Arc G.

Setting this attunement: ***G16 Asmodean Compartment Searched: Yes / No.***

**Yalah's brass key:** The small brass key on the chain at Yalah's throat opens the locked tome in G12, bypassing its Specter trap. A party that takes it on the way out prevents three specters from ambushing whoever opens the family chronicle later.

**Both states — Lady Yalah Gralhund and Hrabbaz:**

Lady Yalah Gralhund is present in this room regardless of day or night state. She has the Stone of Golorr in an inner pocket of her breastplate. For her behavior, negotiation options, and the conditions under which she surrenders the Stone, refer to the **The Gralhund Confrontation — Day** Event (ev-06) or the **The Gralhund Confrontation — Night** Event (ev-07).

**Hrabbaz** (orog) stands at the wall beside the door. He does not speak. He acts on Yalah's judgment.
