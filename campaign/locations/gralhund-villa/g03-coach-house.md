# G3 — Coach House

A sliding timber door, padlocked from outside. Inside: four draft horses in box stalls along the north wall, and Maladar — Lady Yalah's jet-black riding mare — in the end stall, with the best bedding. A two-seat coach occupies the center of the floor, lacquered black with the Gralhund crest on both doors.

**Padlock:** DC 20 thieves' tools to pick quietly. The padlock is heavy iron; breaking it (DC 22 Strength) is loud.

**Stableboy Ike** (commoner, 14) sleeps in a straw-bedded corner stall. He told his mother he was staying with a friend tonight; he wasn't supposed to be here. He'll cooperate with anything that sounds like it will end with him not getting in trouble. He cannot command the mastiffs. They obey Hurv's whistle and no one else's.

**Two mastiffs** are kenneled against the south wall. In the day state, they have 30-foot leads and will investigate any sound that isn't Ike or Hurv. Prestidigitation (food scent) at DC 10 Sleight of Hand distracts one for 5 minutes.

**Treasure:**
- Locked saddlebag on a wall peg (DC 12 thieves' tools): **horseshoes of speed** (one set, fitted for Maladar).
- Two saddlebags stored under the coach bench: eight gold trade bars, four per bag, 250 gp each — **2,000 gp total**.

**Coach House roofline:** The roofline extends 12 feet above the yard and abuts the manor wall at G17. A character on the roofline can reach the Balcony (G17) without a check. Reaching the roofline from outside the perimeter requires DC 10 Athletics on the carriage arch flanking G1.
