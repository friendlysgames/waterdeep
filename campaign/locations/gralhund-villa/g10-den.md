# G10 — Den & Trophy Room

Beast heads mounted on the walls at regular intervals — two bears, a boar, a large catfish from somewhere southern, and a creature the party cannot identify that may have been a displacer beast before someone decided to display only the head. Gleaming suits of armor stand in the four corners, visors down, decorative rather than functional. Bearskin rugs cover the floor. Overstuffed chairs flank a cold fireplace with no grate.

A brass cage on the central table holds a **hooded falcon** (hawk statistics, well-trained). The cage door is latched but not locked. The falcon is calm during the day; during the night state, it has been hooded since the first sounds of the raid and is silent but agitated — it will shriek if the hood is removed in the presence of loud sounds or sudden movement.

**The suits of armor:** Decorative, hollow, and mounted on wheeled stands. They can be toppled (Dexterity saving throw or knocked prone for any creature standing adjacent when one falls). They aren't animate and don't fight.

**Unoccupied in both states.** The den is used for after-dinner smoking and Orond's occasional conversation with his business partners, but neither is scheduled during this arc.
