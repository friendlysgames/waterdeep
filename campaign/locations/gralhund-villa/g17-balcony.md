# G17 — Balcony

A large stone balcony projecting from the manor's north face, 20 feet above the yard below. Ornate wrought-iron railing — strong enough to support climbing — runs along the outer edge. Two cushioned lounge chairs sit facing the yard. A potted lemon tree occupies the corner nearest the glass doors.

**Access from outside:** DC 15 Athletics from the ground (brick face of the manor). No check from the Coach House (G3) roofline — the roofline abuts the manor wall at balcony height.

**Glass doors to G16:** Standing open during the day in good weather; Yalah typically has them latched at night. A character reaching the balcony during the night state must either open the latch from outside (DC 12 thieves' tools) or break the glass (1 round, loud).

**BD assault route:** During the BD assault sequence (*Jarlaxle Informed*), Fel'rekt Lafeen and four drow use this balcony as their entry point — dropping from the rooftops and coming through the glass doors into G16. If the party is on the balcony when this happens, Fel'rekt acknowledges them with a glance and continues without breaking stride.

**Chase route:** If the nimblewright flees with the Stone, it drops from this balcony to the Coach House roofline and proceeds to the street. If Jarlaxle's team exits with the Stone, they withdraw the same way.
