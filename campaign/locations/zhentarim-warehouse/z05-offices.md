# Z05 — Offices

A suite of three connecting rooms: a reception area, a small meeting room, and a back office. All three share the same character — functional, stripped, and recently vacated in a hurry. Desks and chairs are in place but bare of anything not nailed down. The shelves hold nothing but dust. Harmless rats have gotten into what little food remained.

**Steel alarm bells** hang above each office door, wired to a spring mechanism connected to the Z03 secret door. When someone opens Z03's door, the bells ring throughout the office suite, audible from every room in the building. The kenku don't investigate — they heard the mechanism fire before. Kenku in the building ignore the bells.

**Paper bird (magic item):** On the reception desk, folded from a torn sheet of vellum — a message that never left. DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) identifies it as a *paper bird* (WDH Appendix A magic item: when a command word is spoken, a name is written on the bird in ink, and it flies to find that person, delivering a written message of up to twenty-five words). The bird is currently blank. The command word is penciled on the underside of the desk: *Shan* (a Zhentarim courier alias).

**Mourning locket:** Under the back office desk, requiring a DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check to notice without deliberately looking beneath the furniture. An elaborate ivory case on a broken chain. Someone has pried open the secret compartment; the hinge is bent back, fine scratches surround the latch where tools worked it, and nothing remains inside. The exterior bears carved flowers and a stylized horse head, a holy symbol of Lurue, suggesting Renaer's mother was a devotee.

This is the locket Renaer described. Whoever opened it took the Eye of the Stone of Golorr to Kolat Towers. The locket matters to Renaer and to the Stone's backstory.
