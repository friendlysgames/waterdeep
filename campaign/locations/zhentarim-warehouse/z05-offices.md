# Z05 — Offices

A suite of three connecting rooms: a reception area, a small meeting room, and a back office. All three share the same character — functional, stripped, and recently vacated in a hurry. Desks and chairs are in place but bare of anything not nailed down. The shelves hold nothing but dust. Harmless rats have gotten into what little food was left behind.

**Steel alarm bells** are mounted above each office door, triggered by a spring mechanism connected to the Z03 secret door. When Z03's door is opened, the bells ring loudly throughout the office suite — clearly audible throughout the building. The bells do not alert the kenku automatically (they have heard them before and stopped investigating); the GM should play this by ear based on whether the kenku were already killed or subdued when Z03 is opened.

**Paper bird (magic item):** On the reception desk, folded from a torn sheet of vellum — a message that was never sent. DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) identifies it as a *paper bird* (WDH Appendix A magic item: when a command word is spoken, a name is written on the bird in ink, and it flies to find that person, delivering a written message of up to twenty-five words). The bird is currently blank. The command word is written on the underside of the desk in pencil: *Shan* (a Zhentarim courier alias).

**Mourning locket:** Under the back office desk, visible to a character who specifically looks beneath the furniture — DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) to spot it otherwise. An elaborate ivory case on a broken chain. The case's secret compartment has been pried open and is empty; the compartment's hinge is bent back, and fine scratches surround the latch where tools worked it. The exterior is carved with flowers and a stylized horse head — a holy symbol of Lurue, suggesting Renaer's mother was a devotee.

This is the locket Renaer described. The Eye of the Stone of Golorr it contained has already been delivered to Kolat Towers. Nothing remains in the compartment. This object has no mechanical value but is significant to Renaer and to the Stone's backstory.
