# Z04 — Balcony

An open second level accessible by a wooden staircase from Z01's north wall, looking down over the main room. The railing is intact. The balcony smells of mildew.

Someone used this level for storage that never went anywhere: crates of moth-eaten cloth (yellow and green stripes, cut for a fashion three seasons past), hundreds of pairs of wooden-soled sandals stacked in their original packing, and a dozen large stone bottles of spoiled olive oil — some seeping through the cork. Nothing of value. The sandals and cloth appear to have been here longer than the Zhentarim occupied the building.

> From the balcony's edge, the full floor of Z01 is visible below — the twelve bodies, the overturned furniture, the crates of weapons. Any kenku still in Z01 that haven't heard the party are visible from here.

**Alert-state lookout:** If the kenku are in alert state, the GM may position one kenku here, watching the yard through a gap in the painted-over skylight panel in the balcony ceiling. In this position, the kenku cannot be seen from below without a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check, but it has advantage on Perception checks against the yard and front door.
