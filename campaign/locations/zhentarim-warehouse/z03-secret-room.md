# Z03 — Secret Room

The room behind the secret door is cool and dry, smelling of old wood and leather. The Zhentarim used it for storing items that couldn't survive a search: four paintings in padded wood frames, wrapped in tooled leather, propped against the east wall; and fifteen silver trade bars stacked on a low shelf, each one roughly ten pounds and black with corrosion on the surface.

The room is otherwise empty. The Zhentarim cell didn't have time to move the contents before the Xanathar raid.

> The leather wrappings on the paintings are hand-stitched, every seam tight. When unwrapped, each canvas shows a city portrait: Luskan in winter light, Neverwinter from the river approach, Silverymoon's Moonbridge spanning the Rauvin, and Baldur's Gate seen from the harbor. The trade bars beneath the corrosion are solid silver.

#### Finding the Room

The secret door is set into the south wall of Z01 or the connecting passage, indistinguishable from surrounding planking without examination. DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) to locate. Opening the door triggers a bell in Z05 — a spring mechanism wired to alert the offices when someone accesses the stash.

**Treasure:**
- Four paintings depicting Luskan, Neverwinter, Silverymoon, and Baldur's Gate — 75 gp each
- Fifteen silver trade bars, 10 lb each, corroded surface — 50 gp each

**Total value:** 1,050 gp (300 gp paintings + 750 gp trade bars), recoverable without magical assistance but requiring multiple trips or a cart for the trade bars.
