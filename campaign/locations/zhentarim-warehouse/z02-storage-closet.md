# Z02 — Storage Closet

The closet smells of rancid pickled herring — a crate of them split open on the floor weeks ago and was never cleaned up. Shelves along the walls hold empty jars, a broken crate hinge, and nothing of value. The space is barely large enough for two people to stand without touching.

**Renaer Neverember** is here. He has pressed himself into the far corner behind the shelving unit, unarmed, with a bruised jaw and rope abrasion on his wrists where he worked himself free. When the party enters, he is assessing them, figuring out who they are before he speaks.

#### Renaer Neverember

Renaer won't emerge until the kenku are dealt with. If the party enters Z02 mid-encounter, he stays silent unless addressed directly, at which point he will say quietly: "I heard what you did to the last ones. Please tell me they're gone."

Once the party clears the building, he comes out and speaks freely.

**What he shares without prompting:**
- Five Zhentarim grabbed him and Floon outside Old Xoblob Shop two nights ago
- They brought both men here and questioned him about his father's embezzled hoard — gold Renaer refuses to help his father hide
- They took his mourning locket; he believed it held a keepsake from his mother; he didn't know there was a compartment inside
- The Xanathar Guild attacked before the interrogation went further; he slipped his bonds in the confusion and hid here
- He watched through a gap in the door as Xanathar agents dragged an unconscious Floon through Z01, out through the yard, and down the alley toward a sewer grate

**What he doesn't know:** what the locket contained; where the Vault is; the Stone of Golorr's role in any of this. His ignorance is genuine.

**If asked about his father's enemies:** The Cassalanters have been quietly acquiring properties near the Brandath family tombs — his mother's family plots. He can't explain it. He raises it as an oddity, not an accusation.

He will offer to accompany the party or wait at the warehouse. He doesn't know what is in the sewers. He wants Floon found.
