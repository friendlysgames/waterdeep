# Z01 — Main Room

The warehouse floor is chaos: tables and chairs overturned, a shattered crate, two broken lanterns dark on the floor. Twelve bodies are arranged along the walls — not stacked, not hidden, just where they fell and were pushed aside by whoever came through after. Five wear black leather armor with a flying snake tattooed on the neck or forearm. Seven wear similar armor; one has a circle of ten equidistant spokes tattooed on their right palm. Rapiers and daggers lie nearby. The fight was recent. Nothing in this room smells old.

Crates of supplies stack along the north wall: weapons (crossbows, short swords, daggers), rations, black leather uniforms, and boots. The Zhentarim cell used this room as a staging area.

#### Kenku — Unaware State

Four kenku are searching the wreckage — opening crates, checking the bodies for valuables, muttering in stolen voices. They are not watching the entry points. A group DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check against their passive Perception of 13 determines whether the party enters without being noticed.

If noticed mid-search: kenku scatter to cover positions and become hostile.

#### Kenku — Alert State

The kenku are hidden behind overturned tables and crates, watching the entry points. They do not call out a challenge; they wait. If the party enters with a light source, the kenku attack from cover on the first round.

#### Kenku Mimicry

Kenku speak only in mimicked fragments. A captured or subdued kenku, under DC 10 Charisma (Intimidation), repeats what it has heard — in three different voices:

> "Xanathar sends its regards." *(deep, orcish-accented)*

> "Tie up the pretty boy in the back room!" / "Follow the yellow signs in the sewers." *(thin, nasally)*

> "No time to loot the place. Just get him to the boss." *(scratchy, hurried)*

These fragments — the correct tactical responses to the Kenku interrogation path for Revelation #3 — confirm Floon's direction of travel without naming the destination.

### Zhentarim Warehouse Event

During the **Zhentarim Warehouse** Event, the kenku in this room begin in the state determined by the party's entry approach. Refer to the **Zhentarim Warehouse** Event for full details on the entry mechanic and kenku alert triggering.
