# Nim's Attic

The attic occupies the fourth floor, above the staff-restricted landing. The door is locked with a mechanism that Valetta does not recognize — Nim fitted it after the last time the door was opened without warning. DC 20 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) to pick; the mechanism is Nim's own design and does not conform to any standard locksmith's training. Knock opens it automatically. DC 17 Charisma (Persuasion) through the door succeeds with Valetta's firm encouragement (she has authority here; Nim knows her voice).

Inside: packed floor to rafter with half-finished contraptions. Copper tubing in coils on shelving. Gear-trains on wire racks. A dozen projects in various stages — some recognizable as mechanical tools, some indeterminate. The air smells of oil and solder and old wood. Two small windows at the east and west ends let in thin light. The ceiling is low enough that anyone over six feet must duck at the peak.

Nim is in the far corner: the size of a small child, crouching with its back to the wall, watching the door with expressionless brass eyes. It does not move when the door opens. It is waiting to see what happens next.

#### Nim

Nim is a nimblewright — AC 13, 45 hit points — though it is not going to fight. Its stillness is wariness, not aggression. It built the runaway three months ago and has been waiting for someone competent to come ask about it ever since.

**Communication:** Common Sign Language. Nim understands Common but cannot speak. Valetta has learned enough CSL to translate functionally — she misses nuance and asks Nim to repeat when the signs are ambiguous. Through Valetta, Nim conveys the essential facts: it built a companion to ease loneliness; the companion's behavioral lattice was incomplete; it fled a month ago; the detector is built and unused; Nim wants it found.

Any character with Common Sign Language communicates with Nim directly. The signed conversation is faster, more precise, and carries emotional content that Valetta's translation compresses out. Nim shares its loneliness, its pride in the runaway's construction (which is genuine — the lattice was good, the timing was wrong), and its guilt about releasing it before the ethical framework was complete. It signs to a CSL speaker in a way that suggests relief.

**What Nim knows:**
- It built the runaway (designated: NW-02 in Jarlaxle's ledger) three months ago, sold as a "household model with combat programming" to a North Ward buyer whose name it was not given.
- The runaway's behavioral lattice was incomplete — Nim knew this and built it anyway because it was lonely.
- It did not know the buyer would give the runaway combat directives.
- It built the detector to find the runaway. It has never used it. It offers the detector without hesitation — the party is the most direct path to finding what it built and cannot find on its own.

**What Nim does not know:**
- That the runaway has already been used as a weapon.
- That Dalakhar is dead.
- That the runaway is at Gralhund Villa.

#### The Nimblewright Detector

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (requires attunement). Magic Action to activate. While active, spins and clicks within 500 feet of any nimblewright other than Nim. Speed and volume increase with proximity; maximum intensity within 30 feet.*

The device is 1 foot long, copper, with an umbrella-like metallic protrusion that rotates when active. It sits on the workbench beside Nim's corner. Nim hands it over when asked — no persuasion required.

> #### The Detector's Limitation
>
> The detector responds to any nimblewright other than Nim. At the Sea Maidens Faire, multiple units overwhelm it — the Gralhund unit (NW-02) cannot be isolated from the fleet's legitimate valets. In the North Ward, with only the Gralhund unit in range, the signal is unambiguous. This is by design. The Faire visit is the stall; Saerdoun Street is the answer.

#### Valetta's Reward

Valetta offers, from the temple's resources: 500 gp for proof of the escaped nimblewright's destruction, plus one invention of the party's choosing (adjustable stilts; backpack parachute; barking box; matchless pipe — see ev-03 for mechanics). She does not have authority to offer an alternative reward if the party intends to return the runaway alive. Nim watches the conversation. A CSL speaker already knows what Nim would prefer.
