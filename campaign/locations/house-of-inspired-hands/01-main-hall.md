# Main Hall

The main hall runs the full ground floor of the building, high-ceilinged with gallery windows letting in the morning light. The toothed cog of Gond appears on every surface without exception — doorknobs, window latches, the stone floor's decorative inlay. Marble pedestals line both walls. Each holds a prize-winning invention submitted to the temple's annual Gond's Artifice competition.

The inventions vary in scale and ambition: a folding clockwork umbrella, a self-repairing leather boot heel, a brass music box that remembers a song and replays it at the correct tempo regardless of how long it has sat silent. One pedestal near the center of the hall holds the only entry that did not win: a brass-plated model, one foot long, labeled *"The Scarlet Marpenoth — Launched 1489 DR."* A Lantanese submersible, rendered at approximately 1:100 scale. The donor is not named on the label. The hull detailing is precise enough to suggest the model was made from the original plans.

#### Valetta

The senior cleric occupies the main hall during public hours, either at the entrance greeting visitors or moving between the display pedestals with the specific attentiveness of someone who is both proud and protective of each item. She is a tall bronze dragonborn — amber-yellow eyes, machine-oil smell, the toothed cog worn as a collar clasp. She moves in deliberate, compact steps that take up less space than her height suggests. She is patient with guests until they touch the displays without asking.

When strangers ask about the nimblewright by description, she identifies it as Nim's creation without hesitation and escorts them up the spiral staircase. She has no reason to withhold this — Nim's existence is not a secret, and a missing construct is a problem the temple would prefer to resolve.

#### The Scarlet Marpenoth Model

The model is not interactive — it is sealed and display-only. DC 14 Intelligence (History): the Scarlet Marpenoth was a Lantanese submersible launched from Baldur's Gate in 1489 DR, reportedly lost to a sea battle within the year. No record of its current status appears in the Waterdeep civic archive. DC 16 Intelligence (History): Lantanese submersibles of this class were operated exclusively by the Bregan D'Aerthe trade network as of the late 1480s DR.

A party that examines the model during ev-03 and later discovers the *Scarlet Marpenoth* beneath the *Eyecatcher* will recognize the design. No check required for the visual match — the model is accurate to the actual vessel's hull configuration.

### The House of Inspired Hands Event — Event-Conditional

During ev-03, this hall is Valetta's staging ground. The sparrow trap has already fired by the time the party enters; Nim is in the attic. Valetta moves from the entrance to greet the party, identifies Nim from their description, and begins the escort to the attic level. She does not stop for conversation in the hall — she moves with the specific purpose of someone who has already decided what is happening.

After ev-03 is complete, the hall returns to normal operation. The detector is gone (with the party). Nim may or may not return to the parapet, depending on how the encounter resolved.

Refer to the **The House of Inspired Hands** Event for the full sequence.
