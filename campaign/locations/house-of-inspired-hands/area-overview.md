# House of Inspired Hands — Area Overview

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Area Map Context
>
> The House of Inspired Hands occupies the corner of Seawatch Street and Shark Street in the Sea Ward. The building is five stories: main hall (ground), offices and workshops (second and third), spiral stair to the attic (fourth), and a rooftop parapet visible from the street below.
>
> **Event use:**
> - **ev-03 (The House of Inspired Hands):** Primary event location. The sparrow trap triggers during approach; Valetta escorts the party from the main hall to Nim's attic; the detector is obtained here. Refer to the **The House of Inspired Hands** Event for full sequence.

## Gameplay Details

The areas of the House of Inspired Hands have the following features unless noted otherwise.

### Illumination

Daylight hours: all public areas are naturally lit through large windows; workshops are supplemented by magical lanterns that adjust to the task at hand. After closing (8th bell of the evening to 2nd bell of the morning): main hall is dim (lanterns at low setting), attic is dark.

### Inhabitants

Regular hours: Valetta and two junior Gond priests circulate the main hall and workshop levels. Three lay initiates maintain the workshop spaces. Nim occupies the attic exclusively.

No guard staff — the House of Inspired Hands is a public temple, not a fortress. Confrontational responses from staff are limited to alerting Valetta.

### Enemies

None during normal operations. The mechanical sparrow trap (rooftop approach) is a single-use alert mechanism, not a weapon system. Nim's attic contains no weapons or hostile constructs.

### The Toothed Cog of Gond

The god of craft and artifice appears everywhere in this building — doorknobs, gutter spouts, collar clasps, the wrought iron gate, the banister of the spiral staircase. First-time visitors find it charming. Long-term visitors stop noticing. The effect on Nim is different: the cog is the only consistent reminder that there is a name for what it is.

### The Spiral Staircase

The building's central vertical axis. Accessible from the main hall. Each landing is labeled with a brass cog-wheel plaque: Workshop Level I (2nd floor), Workshop Level II (3rd floor), Restricted — Resident Staff Only (4th floor). The 4th floor landing has a locked gate (DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools); Valetta has a key).

### The Rooftop Parapet

Accessible via an exterior hatch on the 4th floor. The parapet walks the entire roofline of the building. Nim uses it regularly; Valetta uses it rarely. The mechanical sparrow's release mechanism is mounted on the east corner of the parapet wall.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Sparrow Trap Trigger
>
> The sparrow trap activates when Nim releases it — manually, from the parapet — at the moment it perceives an unfamiliar group approaching within 60 feet. It is not an automated system; Nim must be on the parapet and watching. During ev-03, Nim is watching. On subsequent visits (post-ev-03), the sparrow has already been used and the mechanism is empty.
