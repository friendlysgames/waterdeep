# Notable Patrons

> Structuring draft.

Full narrative profiles (appearance, roleplaying keys, detailed topic scenes) in source file. Structure summary below for DM reference.

**DM guidance:** The Patron Table is an improv prompt, not a script. Look for collisions between patrons — that's where interesting moments come from. Pay attention to which NPCs the players light up around; find ways to bring those characters back and reincorporate them into the wider campaign.

## Trollskull Alley Neighbors

**Tally Fellbranch** *(The Bent Nail, CG half-elf, Commoner)*
Strong handshake; reflexively sarcastic; proud of his work.
- Introduces his brother Melannor (Emerald Enclave contact) — framed as casual, clearly calculated
- Offers to sculpt a proper troll's skull for above the bar
- Has been commissioned for 8-foot chess pieces for Cragsmere Estate; buys a round
- Notes the oak tree "acting strange" — roots reaching toward the foundation. He thinks it's the ghost. He's wrong.

**Embric & Avi** *(Steam and Steel, fire/water genasi; Bandit Captain and Priest stat blocks)*
Finish each other's sentences; bicker about aesthetics; agree on everything that matters; domestic about their forge.
- One of the Three Urchins has been shoplifting small metal trinkets — they want a quiet resolution, no Watch
- Want an introduction to Renaer; Embric supported Lord Dagult and assumes Renaer shares his politics (he does not)
- Commissioned weapons of strange design; commission traces to Grinda Garloth (optional faction hook)
- Want to install a hearth upgrade in the kitchen at cost — won't accept payment

**Fala Lefaliir** *(Corellon's Crown, CG wood elf, Druid)*
Speaks without small talk; unnerving stillness when listening; strong opinions about tea.
- Brings a sampler of foreign teas for the menu — frames this as a professional consultation
- Introduces Ziraj the Hunter (large Calishite Zhentarim, wants a quiet table in the back)
- Interested in harvesting silverbark from the manor oak — currently sourcing it illegally from the City of the Dead
- Has noticed something moving in the sewer access near the cellar at night. Is curious, not frightened. This is somehow worse.

## City Figures

**Vincent Trench** *(Tiger's Eye, LE rakshasa in Calishite human disguise, Rakshasa stat block)*
Asks questions; seems interested in whoever he's speaking to; always spirits, always neat; his hands are backwards.

**2024 note:** Truesight no longer auto-reveals shapeshifters — Vincent's rakshasa nature requires *True Seeing* or specific magical investigation, not casual divine scrutiny. Play him as the best-connected, most unsettling man in the North Ward until Act II when the reveal matters most.

- Client meeting in back — nervous Dock Ward merchant who keeps looking at the door
- Wants to hire the party to follow young noble Adric Thongolir (meeting someone he shouldn't)
- Investigating petty theft in Thundercross Alley: a grey jade stone giant statuette, taken by a small floating magical skull fond of stone likenesses (self-contained sandbox hook)
- Mentions he's taken on a notable new client — someone with enough coin to commission several simultaneous investigations. Watches the party's reaction.

**Renaer Neverember** *(Noble stat block, CG Illuskan human)*
Self-deprecating about his name; deflects sincerity with humor; completely serious when the Vault or Floon comes up.
- Quiet update on the investigation into Dalakhar's murder — sits with his back to the wall
- Invites the party to a party at Brandarthall; faction-adjacent but he won't say so directly
- Talks about his father — what his legacy means for Renaer's life. Honest, not self-pitying.
- Introduces a friend — roll on Renaer's Friends table

**Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff** *(Archmage stat block, NG Calishite human; see the **Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff** page in Notable Figures)*
Direct; does not perform warmth; knows more about the party than she should.
- Lights have been seen in Kolat Towers in the Trades Ward. She mentions this as a footnote. It is not a footnote.
- Knows uncomfortably too much about the party's last operation (doesn't name names, doesn't explain)
- Attempts to recruit the party into Force Grey; if not yet distinguished enough, assigns an observer instead

**Meloon Wardragon** *(Champion Fighter, NG Illuskan human)*
Endlessly optimistic; buys rounds; tells stories about dead people he loved with complete emotional openness; sees himself in young adventurers and says so.

**Act II–III note:** By Act II, Meloon has been compromised by an Xanathar Guild intellect devourer. The Meloon who visits during Act I is genuine. The one who arrives in Acts II–III may not be. The DM should track when this shift occurs and let the party notice gradually — the tavern is where early warning signs should emerge. The Force Grey mission "The Trouble with Meloon" formalizes this.

- Buys a round; asks sincerely how the party is finding Waterdeep; actually listens
- Tells a story about a Force Grey operation that went wrong; names people who died; is fond of them
- Asks if the party has met Vajra yet; says she's worth trusting; says this with specific weight
- *(Act II+, intellect devourer active)* Orders something he's never ordered before. Asks about current investigation with slightly too much specificity. Gets something small wrong about a previous conversation.

**Floon Blagmaar** *(Commoner, CG Tethyrian human)*
Charming in an unfocused way; devoted to Renaer; better instincts than he appears to have, worse judgment than those instincts deserve.
- Celebrating something (won't say what); drinking a specific good Cormyrean white slowly
- Heard the party is asking about the Zhentarim; wants to know if they're doing so safely
- Someone recently asked specific questions about Renaer's finances and political allies; he found this uncomfortable and didn't answer
- Asks, with real seriousness, if something happened to him, would they make sure Renaer knew. Immediately deflects this as a joke. It is not entirely a joke.

## Guild Figures

**Hammond Kraddoc** *(Vintners' Guild, N male Illuskan human, Commoner)*
Always dressed for a formal dinner; his scribe Jinny (always present, always recording, never introduced) sighs at ale, brightens at good spirits.
- New featured spirit to promote (roll 1d4: blackberry seawine, Berg Brew, Entsap, "Storm Giant Ale")
- Wants an exclusive supply arrangement; the catch is a reporting obligation on sales figures and regular customers
- Competitor sourcing spirits outside guild channels — has not yet reported this; wondering if the party has information
- Here purely socially (unusual; Jinny is recording it); orders one good thing, tips exactly the right amount, leaves

**Broxley Fairkettle** *(Fellowship of Innkeepers, LG male strongheart halfling, Commoner)*
Mutton chops; Fellowship sash; perpetually slightly damp; rubs his belly when anxious; names all nine children in order.
- Inspection form completely in order; came to see how the place is doing; says "it's adequate" and tips generously
- One of his other member establishments is being pressured by someone unidentified — strange guild filings, a permit application that shouldn't exist
- Invites the party to the Fellowship's annual member dinner; the food will be mediocre; he likes them and doesn't invite everyone
- News: Volo's Guide is being updated; there's a researcher asking about notable establishments; Broxley suggested the Trollskull

**Ulkoria Stonemarrow** *(Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors, NG female shield dwarf, Archmage)*
Permanent scowl (not the same as disapproval); silver hair braided precisely; her Shield Guardian waits outside; has opinions about every piece of furniture.
- Says the eastern wall is showing acceptable settling. This is a compliment.
- Offers to add a *Glyph of Warding* to the cellar access for 300 gp; says the previous tenant made enemies she considers unresolved. Means the hag. Will not elaborate on the hag.
- An arcane signature detected in the North Ward three times this tenday — consistent with either Xanathar gazer activity or something older. Tells the party because the Trollskull is on the same block.
- Examines Lif for ten minutes without ordering. Then orders his best, drinks it in silence, leaves a large tip. This is, in Ulkoria's vocabulary, an extraordinary endorsement.

## Faction-Adjacent

**Jalester Silvermane** *(Lords' Alliance, N male Cormyrean human, Veteran; see **The Lords' Alliance** organization page)*
Thinning hair, lean frame, dark doublet; lonely (partner Faerrel Dunblade killed last year) but not sorry for himself; direct; asks questions more specific than casual conversation warrants.
- Mission delivery (verbal, no scroll) to any Lords' Alliance PC; cover is ordering a drink between briefing sips
- Concerned about Davil Starsong's arrest timeline — wrong person filed the initial report; wants the party's read on whether the Doom Raiders are trustworthy partners
- Has a name: someone asking about the party through a Dock Ward cutpurse. Professional courtesy.
- Wants to discuss what happens after the Grand Game — specifically, what the party plans to do with the gold. Some of his thoughts are Laeral's.

**Yagra Stonefist** *(Doom Raiders, N female half-orc, Tough; see **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)** organization page)*
*As a patron, when not employed as Bouncer.*
Seven feet; purplish-grey skin; red jade Zhentarim pendant worn openly; cracks her knuckles reflexively.
- Arm-wrestling anyone willing. She will beat them. Does this because people relax after losing to her.
- Davil is being released from custody (or has been; or will be). She wanted to see the party's faces when she said it.
- Question from Davil: what did the party do with information recovered from their last operation. She's also asking for herself.
- Tells a story about the slaver ship she was on before the Doom Raiders found her. Tells it like an old weather report. Wants nothing for telling it. Just wanted it said in a room she trusts.

**Rishaal the Page-Turner** *(Book Wyrm's Treasure, NG male bronze dragonborn, Mage)*
Burnished amber-bronze scales; bookish in posture; ink on hands; appears mid-thought.
- Acquired a book whose words rearrange when directly observed. Is thrilled. Wonders if the party would like to look at it while he watches their faces.
- A customer has been asking for pre-Spellplague accounts of Undermountain's upper levels. He didn't sell them anything.
- Asks if the party left a message at the shop for Variel Duskwhisper — he received two messages for that name and isn't sure which is which
- Vincent Trench has been in the shop twice this tenday. Both times buying books on planar binding. Rishaal is not worried. He is mentioning it.

**Jarlaxle Baenre** *(Bregan D'aerthe, NE male drow; see the **Jarlaxle Baenre** page in Notable Figures and Act III)*
*Always in disguise. Different disguise each visit. Always the best seat without seeming to choose it.*
Finds everything interesting; asks exactly one question per conversation he already knows the answer to; never orders the same thing twice.

**Running Jarlaxle at the bar:** He visits because the party interests him — they are his most unpredictable variable. Every visit should leave the party wondering what he wanted and whether they gave it to him. Play him as delighted, not menacing. The delight is worse. Drow PCs feel his attention differently — he has specific and complicated interest in drow operating independently on the surface.

- Compliments something specific about the tavern — Lif's pour depth, the spirit selection — then sits in comfortable silence as if he's said everything necessary
- Business proposition: "If you were going to move something — something that couldn't go through official channels — across the city without the Watch knowing, how would you do it?" Listens carefully. Does not say whether it's hypothetical.
- Drops one specific accurate detail about another faction's current operations, between other subjects, without explanation or ask
- Drops the disguise almost — a gesture wrong for the persona, a phrase in Elvish that doesn't fit. Watches to see if the party notices. Either way, he smiles.
