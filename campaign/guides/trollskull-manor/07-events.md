# Events

> Structuring draft.

When an Event is triggered, roll 1d20.

**Resolution framework:**
- **Auto-resolved with the right staff:** A Bouncer on duty resolves brawls and arm-wrestling automatically. A Master Cook or experienced Cook handles kitchen Events. Lif handles most drunk-patron disturbances.
- **Roleplay-resolved:** Social events (socialite, courier, Masked Lord, weeping patron, planted spy) are fully in the players' hands.
- **Dice-optional:** Brawl, pickpocket, kenku dealers have dice options if players choose confrontation.
- **Exception:** Faction Response Team events (8) may escalate past social resolution; see **Response Teams at the Tavern** for each team's triggers.

## Expanded Event Notes

**Event 2 — Traveler's Dust:** Xanathar Guild narcotic sold through kenku. If the party allows the sale: regular patron visibly affected, anonymous Watch tip within the tenday, −1 Fame. If they drive the kenku out without violence: +1 Fame, kenku flock's enmity. Bouncer handles the expulsion but flock enmity still follows.

**Event 5 — Stannis the Pickpocket:** Stannis (CG male Illuskan human, 12, skilled). Handler Sabeen (N female half-orc, Tough) runs child pickpockets from the Field Ward — not cruel, not kind. Turn Stannis over to Watch: Sabeen targets tavern patrons for next tenday (−1 Fame). Speak to Sabeen directly: she can become a low-level street contact. Employ Stannis legitimately: he becomes a fixture — fiercely loyal and useful.

**Event 9 — Kyser Tameno:** His portal anchor is from a now-dissolved wizard's society that used this location as a safe house. He hasn't updated it. If the party is friendly: genuine regular (portal visits 1–2x/month), source of arcane gossip, route to the Surveyors' & Map-Makers' Guild, eventual route beyond Waterdeep's walls. If the party is rude: he reanchors the portal elsewhere with visible offense.

**Event 12 — Tavern Brawl (no bouncer):** 2d4 Commoners in two factions, acting on the same Initiative. Neither group is trying to kill anyone — they want to win.
- **Social:** DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) halts one group; halt both to end the brawl.
- **Physical:** Grapple rules per 2024 (Strength Athletics vs. Strength or Dexterity Athletics).
- **Let it run:** Ends after 1d4 rounds on its own when someone bleeds. −2d6 × 5 gp furniture damage, −1 Fame.
- **Fame outcome:** Brawl ended in ≤2 rounds without weapons: +1 Fame. 3+ rounds or weapons appear: −2 Fame.
