# Fame

> Structuring draft.

Fame represents the tavern's standing in Waterdeep's social geography. Numerical score, tracked tenday by tenday. Range: **−50** (condemned) to **351+** (institution).

Fame modifies Revenue Rolls and shapes NPC behavior. It can't be spent or traded.

**Design note:** The original WDH system (1 gp promotion spend = +1 to business roll) is replaced by this track. Famous taverns attract notable patrons; notable patrons cause memorable events; memorable events build more fame. Staff choices, faction incidents, and what happens in the common room have mechanical teeth.

---

## Starting Fame

When the Grand Opening occurs, roll 1d6 and add bonuses earned through Act I preparation:

| Preparation | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Lif appeased and tending bar | +2 |
| Promotion spending (flyers, word-of-mouth via NPCs) | +1 per PC-tenday spent (max +3) |
| Notable NPC personally invited and attended | +1 per NPC (max +3) |
| Live music at Grand Opening | +1 |
| Special menu item at Grand Opening | +1 |
| Public disruption or violent incident during opening | −3 |
| Lif caused a violent scene (unappeased) | −2 |

## Fame Thresholds

A well-staffed tavern (Lif + Maren + Mattrim + Pell + Hadra's first 3 tendays) accumulates 5–6 Fame/tenday from staff contributions alone. The early tiers reflect natural momentum; the higher tiers require active management.

**Calibration:** At 5 Fame/tenday average with no special investment, the journey from 0 to Institution takes ~70 tendays. With active play (events, faction meetings, notable patron moments), 50–60. Either way, it spans most of the campaign. Passive staff gains will plateau the tavern somewhere in Fashionable; reaching Renowned and Institution requires the party to treat the tavern as a social project.

| Fame Score | Status | Revenue Modifier | Patron Reach | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −50 to −20 | **Condemned** | Disadvantage | No one comes willingly | Guild license proceedings begin. Watch checks in monthly. |
| −19 to −1 | **Struggling** | −10 | Locals only | Regulars thin out. Patron Table rolls at −2. |
| 0–30 | **Known** | — | Neighbourhood draw | Functioning local establishment. Staff contributions start accumulating. |
| 31–80 | **Respected** | +5 | Ward-wide | Notable patrons arrive without prompting. Roll d8 (not d6) for patron count. |
| 81–150 | **Fashionable** | +10 | City-wide | Faction contacts use the tavern for private meetings. Occasional Masked Lord. |
| 151–350 | **Renowned** | +15 | City-wide and beyond | Appears in travel guides. Volo's write-up possible. Response Teams treat the address as politically sensitive. |
| 351+ | **Institution** | +20 | Waterdhavian landmark | A Masked Lord has a reserved table. Events here echo through noble drawing rooms by morning. Halaster Blackcloak has heard of it. |

## Gaining Fame

Most gains are +1 — a satisfied customer tells one friend, maybe two. Momentum builds through consistency.

| Event | Fame Change |
|---|---|
| Prominent NPC publicly praises the tavern | +1 |
| Event night resolves positively without violence or Watch involvement | +1 |
| Party resolves a tavern crisis smoothly and publicly | +1 |
| Faction contact uses tavern for a private meeting | +1 |
| Tavern hosts a public event (celebration, fundraiser, ward feast) | +2 |
| Scheduled night from the Tenday Schedule (see **Tavern Time**; once per tenday max) | +1 |
| Lif performs a spectacular public act | +1 |
| Perella Dunwhistle + Lif working the same bar, Half-Elf on menu | +1 (one-time, first tenday) |
| Maren Holst joins as Master Cook | +2 opening tenday, then +1/tenday (cap +5 total) |
| Hadra Stonebread joins as cook | +1 for first 3 tendays |
| House Bard performs exceptionally | +1 |
| Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg as house bard (sustained) | +1/tenday |
| Lysa of the White Lute passes first performance | +1; then +1/tenday from second tenday onward |
| Darvish averts a Xanathar incident visibly and calmly | +1 (once per incident) |
| Volo's write-up obtained | +3 |
| Exceptional Revenue Roll (111+) | +1 |

## Losing Fame

Fame falls fast. An unsatisfied customer tells ten people. A frightened one tells everyone. Fame loss is weighted roughly 3:1 against equivalent gains.

| Event | Fame Change |
|---|---|
| Public brawl, broken furniture, no bouncer | −3 |
| Public brawl, injuries or Watch called, no bouncer | −5 |
| Public brawl with bouncer, badly handled | −1 |
| Faction Response Team causes visible public incident inside | −6 |
| Faction Response Team visit handled smoothly | 0 |
| Guild fees unpaid (per tenday overdue) | −2 |
| Patron seriously injured on premises | −5 |
| Patron killed on premises | −10 |
| Watch formally investigates | −8 |
| Watch investigates and finds nothing actionable | −3 (the investigation itself damages perception) |
| Character publicly disgraced or arrested in taproom | −6 |
| Orryn Tetch caught stealing and publicly dismissed | −3 |
| The Remarkable Ossibar's mood-magic publicly exposed | −8 |
| Maren Holst dismissed without cause (regulars protest) | −5 |
| Brother Aldric departs after his 30-day tenure | −2 (regulars mourn the food change) |
| Fennick Doss plays past his third drink, unintervened | −2 (any tenday this occurs) |
| House Bard audition fails publicly and embarrassingly | −3 |
| Rella Asp's commission fulfilled and traced to the kitchen | −10 + Watch investigation |
| Ivrala Sorn's intelligence role publicly exposed | −4; patrons distrust staff for 1d3 tendays |
| Active rumor not countered within the tenday | −3/tenday the rumor is active |
| Rumor publicly and credibly refuted | 0 (stops the bleeding; does not recover lost Fame) |

**Recovery:** No special mechanic. Fame comes back the same way it was built — one good tenday at a time. A −10 hit requires roughly ten consistently positive tendays to undo, assuming nothing else goes wrong.
