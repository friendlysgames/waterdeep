### Bonds and Flaws

**Bonds** are formative backstory elements — the wound or truth those events left, not the events themselves. A Bond explains why the character behaves the way they do: the thing that shaped their worldview, their relationship to power, their capacity for trust. Every Bond connects to something the campaign will press on.

**Flaws** are the character's internal arc — each one a thing the character must overcome by the end of the campaign to become who they need to be. These are genuine internal obstacles, not personality flavoring or DM tools. Each one answers: *what is this character's damage, and what does it cost them?* The campaign's events — the fireball, the Cassalanter children, the vault, the gold — are designed to press on exactly these.

Players choose or roll one Bond and one Flaw. Ask both to be read aloud at the table during session zero — the conversation that follows is usually more useful than any individual entry.

**Bonds (d8)**

| d8 | Bond |
|----|------|
| 1 | I watched someone with power use the law to destroy someone without it — cleanly, legally, without consequence. I have not been able to look at institutions the same way since. |
| 2 | I was given a second chance by someone who had no reason to give it. I have spent the years since trying to justify their faith, and failing often enough that I still don't trust it was deserved. |
| 3 | I built something once — a business, a community, a relationship — and watched it taken apart by forces I couldn't fight. I am building again. I do not talk about the first time. |
| 4 | I grew up understanding that the powerful protect their own and no one else. That understanding has made me effective at surviving, and nearly incapable of believing anyone wants to help without wanting something back. |
| 5 | I was loyal to someone who turned out not to deserve it. The discovery was catastrophic. I am still learning to distinguish loyalty that is earned from loyalty that is simply comfortable. |
| 6 | Someone cared about me before I had done anything to earn it. That care was real, and it made me capable of caring about others. That person is no longer here. |
| 7 | I have spent long enough pretending to be something I am not that I am genuinely uncertain, on some days, what I actually am underneath the performance. |
| 8 | I came from somewhere that does not prepare you for a city like Waterdeep. The city has changed me in ways that would disturb the people I grew up with. I have not decided whether that is growth or loss. |

**Flaws (d8)**

| d8 | Flaw | Arc it connects to |
|----|------|--------------------|
| 1 | I believe the world owes me a better deal than it has given me. Until I stop keeping that accounting, every choice I make will be shaped by it. | Arc J: what you do with the gold is the final score |
| 2 | I have decided, without admitting it to myself, that keeping myself safe matters more than doing right. | Arc C: the fireball forces the question |
| 3 | I cannot forgive. Not really. I perform forgiveness convincingly when it suits me. | Arc G: the Cassalanters are parents who did something unforgivable for the right reasons |
| 4 | I use competence as a substitute for connection. As long as I am useful, I do not have to be known. | Arc B: the Trollskull community forms around the tavern and will not let this stand |
| 5 | I have decided that some people are not worth protecting. The category quietly expands when I am tired or afraid. | Arc G: are the Cassalanter children worth the cost of saving them? |
| 6 | I need to be right more than I need to be honest. The gap between those two things is larger than I have allowed myself to see. | Faction loyalty: the Doom Raiders will eventually ask for something uncomfortable |
| 7 | I have mistaken control for safety for so long that I cannot tell the difference. I plan. I manage. I do not trust. I call this wisdom. | The heists: this cannot be done alone |
| 8 | I am waiting for someone to give me permission to want what I want. No one is going to give that permission. I know this. I am still waiting. | Arc J: the vault is the moment — and no one is going to tell you what to do |
