# Bonds and Flaws

Bonds and Flaws give your character a past that still has weight. A Bond is the wound or the lesson a formative event left behind, not the event itself — it explains why your character trusts who they trust, wants what they want, and flinches at what they flinch at. A Flaw is the internal work your character has left to do: something they carry into Waterdeep that the city, and this campaign, will not let them set down quietly.

## Bonds

Choose or roll one Bond. It should say something true about who your character was before the campaign begins — the shape a past event left on them, more than the event itself.

**Bonds (d8)**

| d8 | Bond |
|----|------|
| 1 | I watched someone with power use the law to destroy someone without it — cleanly, legally, without consequence. I have not been able to look at institutions the same way since. |
| 2 | I was given a second chance by someone who had no reason to give it. I have spent the years since trying to justify their faith, and failing often enough that I still don't trust it was deserved. |
| 3 | I built something once — a business, a community, a relationship — and watched it taken apart by forces I couldn't fight. I am building again. I do not talk about the first time. |
| 4 | I grew up understanding that the powerful protect their own and no one else. That understanding has made me effective at surviving, and nearly incapable of believing anyone wants to help without wanting something back. |
| 5 | I was loyal to someone who turned out not to deserve it. The discovery was catastrophic. I am still learning to distinguish loyalty that is earned from loyalty that is simply comfortable. |
| 6 | Someone cared about me before I had done anything to earn it. That care was real, and it made me capable of caring about others. That person is no longer here. |
| 7 | I have spent long enough pretending to be something I am not that I am genuinely uncertain, on some days, what I actually am underneath the performance. |
| 8 | I came from somewhere that does not prepare you for a city like Waterdeep. The city has changed me in ways that would disturb the people I grew up with. I have not decided whether that is growth or loss. |

## Flaws

Choose or roll one Flaw. Each one names a genuine internal obstacle — something your character needs to overcome to become who they're capable of being, not a quirk or a piece of personality flavor. Play it honestly and this campaign will find ways to press on it.

**Flaws (d8)**

| d8 | Flaw | Where the campaign presses |
|----|------|--------------------------|
| 1 | I believe the world owes me a better deal than it has given me. Until I stop keeping that accounting, every choice I make will be shaped by it. | **Vault of Dragons**: what you choose to do with a fortune says who you are |
| 2 | I have decided, without admitting it to myself, that keeping myself safe matters more than doing right. | **Fireball!**: when danger lands on your own street, the question stops being abstract |
| 3 | I cannot forgive. Not really. I perform forgiveness convincingly when it suits me. | **Cassalanter Villa**: a noble family's private choices put your mercy to the test |
| 4 | I use competence as a substitute for connection. As long as I am useful, I do not have to be known. | **Trollskull Alley**: the community that forms around the tavern will not let this stand |
| 5 | I have decided that some people are not worth protecting. The category quietly expands when I am tired or afraid. | **Cassalanter Villa**: what you find behind that family's walls asks how far your protection extends |
| 6 | I need to be right more than I need to be honest. The gap between those two things is larger than I have allowed myself to see. | **Doom Raiders**: this faction will eventually ask you for something that doesn't sit easy |
| 7 | I have mistaken control for safety for so long that I cannot tell the difference. I plan. I manage. I do not trust. I call this wisdom. | The faction heists of Act III: none of them can be pulled off alone |
| 8 | I am waiting for someone to give me permission to want what I want. No one is going to give that permission. I know this. I am still waiting. | **Vault of Dragons**: the vault is the moment, and no one is going to tell you what to do |

> [!design]**Flaws Are Arcs**
> Every Flaw on this table points at a part of the campaign, not just a corner of your backstory. These aren't dressing — they're things your character actively works to overcome, and we've built the story to press on exactly these pressure points. Where the pressure lands and what it costs you to hold your ground (or to bend) is something we've left for the table to discover.

> [!info]**At Session Zero**
> Choose or roll one Bond and one Flaw, then read both aloud at the table. The conversation that follows — what these mean, why you picked them, what your fellow players notice in them — is often more useful than the entries themselves.
