### About This Campaign

**Purpose:** Orients players to what kind of game this is before anything else is said about character creation.

**Content:**
- The three remix pillars (Make It a Heist / Feature All Villains / Fix-Up) stated plainly, one paragraph each
- What distinguishes this campaign from standard D&D: rewards planning over power, all four villain factions simultaneously active, no single right answer about what to do with the gold
- Who this campaign suits (groups that enjoy city exploration, lateral investigation, community stakes, emergent faction storytelling) and who it doesn't (groups that want a primary combat focus or a guided throughline)

**Tone:** Direct, honest. Players need to know if this campaign matches their interests before they invest in it.
