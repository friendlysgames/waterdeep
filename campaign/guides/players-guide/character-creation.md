# Character Creation

Building a character for this campaign means building someone who belongs in Waterdeep and has a reason to care what happens to it. The city is the point, not a backdrop your character passes through between fights. Before you finalize a character sheet, make sure your concept answers three questions.

## General Guidance

**A reason to be in Waterdeep.** Your character is here, in the City of Splendors, for some reason that makes sense to them. Born here, drawn here by trade or ambition, running from somewhere else, chasing a rumor: the reason matters less than having one.

**A reason to stay.** Waterdeep is expensive, crowded, and watched by more factions than most of its residents realize. Your character needs something that keeps them from packing up and leaving the first time things get complicated, because things will get complicated.

**A reason to get involved in strangers' problems.** This is the one that gets your character to the table. Adventurers in Waterdeep don't wait for a formal writ before they act. They hear about a friend of a friend in trouble and they go. Whatever drives that impulse for your character, know it before you sit down: a code, a debt, a habit of trouble, plain curiosity.

The campaign opens on exactly this kind of moment. Your characters cross paths in a Waterdeep tavern, where someone explains that a friend of a friend has gone missing and asks for help finding him. It's the kind of favor that works for a mercenary character looking for coin, a helpful one who can't walk past someone in need, or one who was simply in the wrong tavern at the wrong time and got swept up in it. Build toward that opening, not away from it.

Early in the campaign, your characters come to own a tavern of their own, a foothold in the city that's theirs to run, staff, and defend. This is a deliberate choice. We encourage you to want something in Waterdeep beyond the next job. A reputation worth protecting. A community that knows your name. A debt you mean to repay, or a dream you're finally in a position to chase. A character who wants nothing from the city will have less reason to fight for it later.

> [!info]**Drow Characters**
> Drow player characters draw attention from drow interests active in the city. Waterdeep's relationship with the surface drow community runs deeper and stranger than most Waterdhavians know. Talk to your GM before session one so your character's background accounts for it.

## Species

**Drow** characters carry drow faction interest into every session. Coordinate with your GM before play begins so the attention your character draws fits the story you both want to tell.

**Gnome** characters have a natural connection to the House of Inspired Hands, a temple of Gond in the Sea Ward that doubles as a guild of inventors and tinkerers. It's the kind of place a gnome character might already know by reputation, or might walk into and feel instantly at home, full of clockwork, half-finished contraptions, and artisans who speak of their god the way other people speak of an old friend.

**Tiefling** characters may notice something curious about how a handful of Waterdeep's oldest noble families treat them. Where most of the city's upper crust regards tieflings with the same polite wariness they'd show anyone with an unusual bloodline, some old noble families are notably courteous to them. Ordinary Waterdhavians read it as simple open-mindedness.

## Backgrounds

Your background says what your character did before the story starts, and in a city built on guilds, factions, and old debts, that history opens doors or closes them. The table below rates every background from the 2024 Player's Handbook for how naturally it fits a Waterdeep campaign, with a hook to get you thinking about your character's history in the city.

### XPHB Backgrounds

| Background | Fit | Waterdeep Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Criminal | Excellent | You still owe favors to people in the guild you supposedly left. |
| Guard | Excellent | Former City Watch. You know which laws bend and which captains take coin. |
| Artisan | Strong | You've navigated Waterdeep's guild permit system firsthand. Nothing gets built here without a guild's blessing. |
| Charlatan | Strong | Social deception is currency in Waterdeep. Cover identities come naturally to you. |
| Entertainer | Strong | You've performed at the Sea Maidens Faire and learned to hold a crowd under pressure. |
| Merchant | Strong | You grew up in the trade district and know which guild controls which street. |
| Noble | Strong | Sea Ward social circles are open to you, with invitations to the events that matter. |
| Sailor | Strong | Dock Ward is home ground. You know the harbor's rhythms and its rumors. |
| Acolyte | Good | Temple life runs deep here. Tyr and Tymora both keep visible, active houses of worship. |
| Sage | Good | Knowledge is a tradeable commodity in this city, and you know where to sell it. |
| Scribe | Good | Information networks value a fast, discreet hand, and not all of your clients are respectable. |
| Soldier | Good | Military training reads clearly to the City Watch and to mercenary companies alike. |
| Wayfarer | Good | You know roads, inns, and safe houses, useful instincts in a city full of strangers. |
| Guide | Situational | You know the city's layout in ways that pay off when a job calls for moving unseen. |
| Hermit | Situational | Unusual for a city character. Give the seclusion a story and it becomes a strong contrast. |
| Farmer | Situational | A recent transplant, genuinely new to the city. Its strangeness hits you the way it should hit the audience. |

### FRHoF Backgrounds

*Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn* adds a wider set of backgrounds tied to specific corners of the Realms. The ones below carry the strongest natural ties to a Waterdeep campaign.

| Background | Fit | Waterdeep Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Harper | Excellent | Mirt the Moneylender is a Harper, which gives this background an immediate named contact in the city. |
| Lords' Alliance Vassal | Excellent | Waterdeep leads the Lords' Alliance, giving you political access and faction credibility from day one. |
| Zhentarim Mercenary | Excellent | A direct tie to the Doom Raiders. In a city with two different groups called "the Zhentarim," your loyalty is a live question worth playing. |
| Shadowmasters Exile | Strong | Your old underworld connections still have reach into Waterdeep's black market. |
| Chondathan Freebooter | Strong | Sword Coast maritime culture at home in a major port city. |
| Mythalkeeper | Good | Waterdeep's arcane history runs deep, and your knowledge of elven lore and high magic taps straight into it. |
| Emerald Enclave Caretaker | Good | Waterdeep's parks and green spaces fall under Enclave protection, and Melannor Fellbranch is an immediate contact. |
| Knight of the Gauntlet | Good | The Order of the Gauntlet keeps an ally in the city: Hlam, the hermit monk on Mount Waterdeep. |
| Spellfire Initiate | Good | An unusual magical origin draws the kind of attention that has factions interested in rare arcane assets. |
| Flaming Fist Mercenary | Situational | A transplant from Baldur's Gate with a mercenary's outlook on the city's politics. |
| Genie Touched | Situational | Calimshan origins make for a distinctive outsider, well suited to social infiltration. |
| Mulhorandi Tomb Raider | Situational | A scholar's angle, investigation-focused, with a knowledge base nobody local shares. |
| Dragon Cultist | Situational | Worth a conversation with your table. Interesting for a character carrying a hidden allegiance. |
| Ice Fisher | Situational | From Icewind Dale, with survival skills a long way from home. |
| Moonwell Pilgrim | Situational | Moonshae Isles origins bring a nature-focused, faintly otherworldly perspective on the city. |
| Purple Dragon Squire | Situational | A Cormyr-specific origin with limited natural ties to Waterdeep's power structure. |
| Rashemi Wanderer | Situational | A strong character concept on its own, though it arrives with minimal faction ties. |
| Dead Magic Dweller | Situational | An Anauroch background, interesting for a character with a deliberate, considered relationship to magic. |
