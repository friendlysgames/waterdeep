### Character Creation

**Purpose:** Practical guidance for building characters that belong in Waterdeep and will care about what happens there.

**Content:**

*General Guidance*
- Three things every character needs: a reason to be in Waterdeep, a reason to stay, a reason to involve themselves in strangers' problems
- The inciting incident (finding Floon Blagmaar) works for characters who are mercenary, helpful, or simply in the wrong tavern at the wrong time
- By level 2, the characters own a tavern. Encourage players to want something in Waterdeep beyond the adventure — a reputation, a community, a debt, a dream

*Species Notes (2024)*
- Drow: will encounter Jarlaxle and Bregan D'aerthe; Jarlaxle has complicated feelings about surface drow; coordinate with DM before session one
- Gnome: natural connection to House of Inspired Hands (**Fireball!**); can build a relationship with Nim the automaton-maker
- Tiefling: the Cassalanters regard tieflings as distant infernal kin and will show warmth that deserves scrutiny

*Backgrounds (XPHB — 2024 Player's Handbook)*

All 16 XPHB backgrounds, rated for Waterdeep fit:

| Background | Fit | Waterdeep Hook |
|-----------|-----|----------------|
| Criminal | Excellent | Former guild operative; still owes favors to someone still active |
| Guard | Excellent | Ex-City Watch; knows which laws bend and which captains take coin |
| Artisan | Strong | Guild system is everything in Waterdeep; knows the permit labyrinth firsthand |
| Charlatan | Strong | Social deception is currency here; cover identities feel natural |
| Entertainer | Strong | Sea Maidens Faire connections; experience performing under pressure |
| Merchant | Strong | Trade district roots; understands which guild holds which territory |
| Noble | Strong | Sea Ward social access; invitations to events that matter |
| Sailor | Strong | Dock Ward roots; knows what the Sea Maidens Faire actually carries |
| Acolyte | Good | Temple ties — Tyr and Tymora are visible; Asmodeus has a hidden temple |
| Sage | Good | Knowledge is commodity here; House of Inspired Hands connections |
| Scribe | Good | Information-network connections; Zhentarim employs scribes |
| Soldier | Good | Military background translates to City Watch and mercenary contexts |
| Wayfarer | Good | Knows roads, inns, and safe houses; useful in a city of strangers |
| Guide | Situational | Knows the city's layout in ways that matter during a heist |
| Hermit | Situational | Unusual in a city; strong contrast character if the seclusion has a story |
| Farmer | Situational | Recent transplant; genuine outsider perspective on the city's strangeness |

*Backgrounds (FRHoF — Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn)*

Select FRHoF backgrounds suited to a Waterdeep campaign:

| Background | Fit | Waterdeep Hook |
|-----------|-----|----------------|
| Harper | Excellent | Mirt the Moneylender is a Harper; this background creates an immediate named contact |
| Lords' Alliance Vassal | Excellent | Waterdeep leads the Lords' Alliance; political access and faction credibility from day one |
| Zhentarim Mercenary | Excellent | Direct tie to the Doom Raiders; the Two Zhentarims distinction makes this character's loyalty a live question |
| Shadowmasters Exile | Strong | Criminal underworld connections; the Shadowmasters have reach into Waterdeep's black market |
| Chondathan Freebooter | Strong | Sword Coast maritime culture; natural in a major port city |
| Mythalkeeper | Good | Arcane history of Waterdeep runs deep; connections to elven lore and high magic |
| Emerald Enclave Caretaker | Good | Waterdeep's parks and green spaces are Enclave-protected; Melannor Fellbranch is an immediate contact |
| Knight of the Gauntlet | Good | Order of the Gauntlet; Hlam on Mount Waterdeep is their contact in the city |
| Spellfire Initiate | Good | Unusual magical origin; draws attention from factions interested in rare arcane assets |
| Flaming Fist Mercenary | Situational | From Baldur's Gate; recent transplant with a mercenary's outlook |
| Genie Touched | Situational | Calimshan origin; distinctive outsider with magic suited to social infiltration |
| Mulhorandi Tomb Raider | Situational | Scholar angle; investigation-focused character with unusual knowledge base |
| Dragon Cultist | Situational | Requires table conversation; interesting for a character with a hidden allegiance |
| Ice Fisher | Situational | From Icewind Dale; character with survival skills far from home |
| Moonwell Pilgrim | Situational | Moonshae Isles origin; nature-focused with an otherworldly perspective on the city |
| Purple Dragon Squire | Situational | Cormyr-specific origin; limited natural connection to Waterdeep's power structure |
| Rashemi Wanderer | Situational | From Rashemen; strong character concept but minimal natural faction ties |
| Dead Magic Dweller | Situational | Anauroch background; interesting for a character with a deliberate relationship to magic |
