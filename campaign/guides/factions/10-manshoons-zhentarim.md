# Manshoon's Zhentarim

Game mechanics for running Manshoon's Zhentarim as a villain faction. For who they are, see the **Manshoon's Zhentarim** organization page.

## Response Teams

All three Zhentarim response teams consist of a lieutenant plus 3–4 **thugs**. Every lieutenant carries a pass-amulet to Kolat Towers and will try to destroy it if capture seems certain, except Agorn, who trades his to save himself.

Any Zhentarim team sent to attack the characters carries orders to take them as prisoners to the Interrogation House on Brindul Alley. Questioned team members can point the characters toward the house. The lighter roster is intentional. Manshoon wants intelligence, not body counts, and underestimated enemies are more useful to him than dead ones. These teams also keep pressure on the party without forcing a lethal confrontation.

**Vevette Blackwater** — swashbuckler + 3–4 **thugs**.
**Agorn Fuoco** — bard + 3–4 **thugs**.
**Urstul Floxin** (**assassin**) + 3–4 **thugs** (available only if he survived the Gralhund Villa).

## Outposts

### Interrogation House — Encounter-Plus (Trade Ward, Brindul Alley)

*Independent discovery path: parties that searched Urstul Floxin's effects at Gralhund Villa have documents pointing to Brindul Alley. Parties that did not can find the safe house through Trade Ward canvassing — the traffic is unusual enough to notice (DC 14 Investigation over a day, no faction membership required). This is the Manshoon chain's street-level entry point.*

Use the map for house L1 (DH p. 62). Avareen Windrivver and Zorbog Jyarkoth maintain this building as a combination safe house and covert interrogation center. Three black **flying snakes** nest near the ceiling — the Zhentarim's message couriers.

*Front Room.* Avareen and Zorbog's quarters. Avareen carries a pass-amulet to Kolat Towers. While the party explores, a flying snake arrives with *A Directive to Zorbog* signed by "Manshoon," ordering Zorbog to kidnap Fenerus Stormcastle of Quill Alley, a "known associate of these new meddlers in the Grand Game."

*Backroom.* A rack and other instruments of torture. An arcane circle on the floor. Strapped into the rack is **Ott Steeltoes**, a Xanatharian agent captured while the Zhentarim were trying to locate the Eye. He knows nothing of the Eye itself.

If the party frees him, Ott immediately tries to recruit his rescuers into Xanathar's organization. With patience and the right questions, the party can persuade him to lead them toward the Lair instead.

**Roster:** Avareen Windrivver (**spy**) + Zorbog Jyarkoth (**thug**) + 3 **flying snakes**.

**Escalation:** Manshoon moves to Alert (Tier 3). Flying snakes carry the alarm within hours if any escape; Vevette Blackwater's team deploys within 3 days. If Xanathar cross-faction spillover applies, Manshoon moves to Lockdown (Tier 4). **Founders' Day Clock: 1–2 days.**

### Yellowspire — Mini-Heist (Castle Ward)

*Full 5-step planning framework in **Faction Outposts**, Scene 5B. This entry provides keyed area reference for the operation.*

Use the Winter version of the Old Tower (DH p. 79). Amath Seccent and four **acolytes** occupy this converted tower. The permanent teleportation circle here connects directly to Kolat Towers — one of the few physical routes into Manshoon's lair that bypasses the pass-amulet requirement.

*Area O2 — Ground Level.* A crumpled note from Avareen to Seccent, full of barely contained contempt: Avareen complains about Manshoon's directive to hand over a broadsheet publisher named Shan Chien for Seccent's "foul rites," but closes with "Manshoon's word is my command."

**Gralhund complication:** If the Gralhunds remain active in the Grand Game, their agent Chirada may be watching Yellowspire from across the street. The Gralhunds may mount their own raid on Yellowspire to access the teleportal, inserting a third party into whatever operation the characters are planning.

**Roster:** Amath Seccent (**priest**) + 4 **acolytes**. Agorn Fuoco is present from dusk to midnight, when he reports to Amath; casing the tower for one evening reveals the pattern.

**Escalation:** Manshoon moves to Suspicious (Tier 2) if the Interrogation House has not yet been hit; moves to Lockdown (Tier 4) if it has. Agorn carries word to surviving Zhentarim contacts if he escapes; response teams redeploy within 3 days. **Founders' Day Clock: 3–5 days.**

## Escalation

| Tier | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Unaware | Normal operations |
| Suspicious | Flying snake courier network; Agorn and Avareen's field reports. Avareen identifies the threat; Urstul reviews field reports |
| Alert | Agorn or Vevette's team; orders are to capture, not kill |
| Lockdown | Urstul's team if the others fail; Manshoon issues personal directives by flying snake. Manshoon's simulacrum engages; Sidra locks down Kolat Towers |

Manshoon underestimates threats that aren't magical. A party that stays clear of Kolat Towers may go tendays before he escalates beyond his response teams. His preference for capturing over killing creates tactical openings.
