# House Gralhund

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - This page holds the game mechanics for running House Gralhund as a minor Grand Game faction: its stance, its single response team, how its pressure follows the escalation tiers, and what the party can learn about it.
> - House Gralhund is not a villain faction, and the party cannot join it. It offers no renown, ranks, missions or rewards, so this page has no sections for them.
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts** (Yellowspire).
> - For who the house is, see the **House Gralhund** organization page.

## Grand Game Stance

House Gralhund wants the Stone of Golorr and the Vault of Dragons. It wants the gold to raise the house's standing in Waterdhavian society, and Lady Yalah wants it to buy her a seat among the Masked Lords. The house holds no Eye and has no route to one. It struggles to compete with any of the four villain factions.

The house owns no outposts and sits on no row of the escalation table in **Running the Villains**. Add it to the tenday faction check from **The Grand Game in Play** only while it can act, as the After Gralhund Villa rules below define.

**Before Gralhund Villa**
- The house pays for the Zhentarim cell that shelters at its villa. Yalah treats the cell's leader, Urstul Floxin, as an employee and doesn't know that Manshoon is its master.
- Informants bring Yalah what they hear. Some are agents placed inside Renaer Neverember's household months ago, and one is Skeemo Weirdbottle of the Doom Raiders.
- On Ches 22, Yalah sends the nimblewright to Trollskull Alley, and Dalakhar dies in the fireball. From that day until the raid on Ches 24, she keeps the Stone on her person in G16 and receives no unannounced visitors.

**After Gralhund Villa**

The raid on the night of Ches 24 costs the house most of its guards. What the house does next depends on whether Yalah is free and on who holds the Stone. **Gralhund Villa** marks **Yalah Missing** or **Yalah In Custody**, and if neither is marked she is dead. The house can act only while someone can give orders.

- **Yalah Missing.** She directs the house from hiding. Apply the Stone rules below.
- **Yalah In Custody, or dead.** Nobody gives orders. Orond is in shock and makes no move. Chirada works alone as a team of one, tracking whoever holds the Stone and the Eye she believes the Zhentarim took.

While Yalah is free, the Stone decides her move.
- **The party holds the Stone.** The house has nothing to trade and can't beat the party in the open. It marks the party's Golorr artifacts as the easiest ones to reach and goes after them by covert means first. Run the Response Team below.
- **Yalah kept the Stone.** She knows the house is out of its depth, so she trades the Stone as collateral. Her two options are an alliance with a major faction and an approach to the Open Lord for political capital.
  - If **Jarlaxle Informed** is marked, Fel'rekt's offer at the villa is still open, and she takes it. Bregan D'aerthe holds the Stone, and **The Rooftop Chase** says how it moves from there.
  - If **Jarlaxle Informed** is not marked, she approaches the Open Lord. The Stone passes to Laeral's keeping, and the house gets standing in the city and no share of the gold.
- **Another faction holds the Stone.** The house has no artifact and no force, so it waits and watches the party. It acts only when the party holds a Golorr artifact the house can reach, which means the Stone again or an Eye.

## Response Team

House Gralhund fields one team, and it is small. The house owns no outposts, so nothing the party does to a Gralhund property can send the team out. It answers to what the party holds, and it moves only after the house learns that the party holds a Golorr artifact.

**Roster:** Hrabbaz (**Tough**) + Chirada (**Spy**) + 1 **Adept**.

- Hrabbaz is Yalah's bodyguard (*Dragon Heist* p. 205). He stays within reach of Yalah, so he takes the field only when she sends him. He dies if he holds the master bedroom against Fel'rekt and lives if he stands down (**Gralhund Villa**, Aftermath). When he is dead, replace him with a surviving house guard (**Warrior Veteran**).
- Chirada is the house's intelligence operative and the team's spy. She leads the team in the field, because the other two are muscle.
- The Adept is a hired martial artist (*Dragon Heist* p. 209). No source names him.

**How the house learns what the party holds.** Two channels feed it.
- Chirada shadows the party and sees what she can see.
- The house has agents inside Renaer's household, and Orond's journal calls them Eagle and Catoblepas. When any party member tells Renaer where the Stone or an Eye is, or what the party plans to do with it, the house has the news within two days. A party that tells Renaer nothing closes this channel, and the house then knows only what Chirada sees.

**How the team behaves.** It works covertly. It uses force only when Yalah orders it.
1. Chirada shadows the party and notes where it keeps the artifact. She watches the places the party visits.
2. When she finds the artifact unguarded, the Spy lifts it and the Adept covers the exit. Nobody fights.
3. If the party exposes the team, it withdraws and tries again by another route in the next tenday.
4. Only an order from Yalah, who must be free, sends the team in with force. It fights only to reach the artifact, and it withdraws as soon as any member drops.

**Reaching Chirada.** She does not fight if she can avoid it. If she is cornered, or the party offers to cooperate, she negotiates, because she has nothing to gain from dying over an Eye she hasn't found. A party that reaches her before the team moves can learn the Yellowspire teleportal lead from her, including the access phrase. She trades information for information.

## Escalation

The house has one team and no outposts, so its escalation is short. It moves with what the house knows about the party's artifacts. It doesn't move with what the party does to Gralhund property.

| Tier | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Unaware | The house doesn't know that the party holds a Golorr artifact. Its informants gather intelligence and nothing else. |
| Suspicious | The house knows that the party holds the Stone or an Eye. Chirada shadows the party alone. |
| Alert | Chirada has found where the party keeps the artifact. The full team takes the field and works covertly, as the Response Team section describes. |
| Lockdown | Not used. The house can field nothing beyond its team. A party that captures or kills the team ends the pursuit. |

A failed or exposed attempt returns the house to Suspicious, and the team tries again in the next tenday. If Yalah is In Custody or dead, the house stays at Unaware and only Chirada can reach Suspicious.

## Revelation List

*Each entry names the document or discovery and, in parentheses, where it is found. Only the villa's address is a critical beat under the Three Clue Rule, and **Fireball!** carries that audit. Every other entry is optional intelligence, so none of them has three paths.*

**Gralhund Villa** (North Ward, Saerdoun Street)
- Renaer's Harper report placing Urstul Floxin inside the villa (**Fireball!**, The Fireball; marks **Gralhund Villa Named**)
- The nimblewright detector's strongest signal outside the Faire (**Fireball!**, The Nimblewright Hunt)
- *Complete nimblewright ledger*, row NW-02, "O. Gralhund, Saerdoun St., North Ward" (**Fireball!**, The Sea Maidens Faire, the *Eyecatcher*)
- Captain Zord's answer, which gives the Gralhund name and address (**Fireball!**, The Sea Maidens Faire; marks **Jarlaxle Informed**)
- Jalester's note that a North Ward household matching the villa's description has been flagged as a possible Black Network safe house (**Fireball!**, The Death Mark)

**The house's agents and informants**
- *A Missive from Castle Ward* (**Gralhund Villa**, G12, on the reading stand; signed Chirada and dated Ches 10; marks **Chirada Letter Found**). It tells the party that the house has an operative tracking an Eye to Yellowspire in the Castle Ward, and that she suspects a teleportal. She is wrong about where the Eye went.
- *Gralhund Study of the Grand Game* (**Gralhund Villa**, G11, in the false-book compartment, DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation); marks **Grand Game Journal Found**). Orond's journal analyzes the six faction leaders and their embedded agents. It names two informants inside Renaer's household as "Eagle" and "Catoblepas", and it is the party's only path to learning that the house has them.
- Chirada herself (**Faction Outposts**, Yellowspire; DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) to spot her across the street). If the party reaches her, she can give the Yellowspire teleportal lead.
- Skeemo Weirdbottle's admission that he sold intelligence to House Gralhund (**Silencing Skeemo** and **Davil's Return**, Doom Raiders members only)

**The house's orders and funding**
- Orond's confession (**Gralhund Villa**, Daytime Infiltration, The Front Door or The Guest Suite; marks **Orond Confession Heard**). He confirms what the Stone is, that Yalah sent the nimblewright on Ches 22, that symbols of a religious affiliation lie in a trunk in the master bedroom, and that the house funded the Zhentarim agents who seized Renaer.

**The house and the cult**
- The false bottom of the trunk in G16 (**Gralhund Villa**; DC 15 Wisdom (Perception); marks **G16 Compartment Found**). It holds two silver holy symbols of Asmodeus and two red-and-gold robes with Cassalanter house embroidery. This is the party's first evidence that the Gralhunds and the Cassalanters belong to the same cult.
- Victoro's private journal, with the entry "The Cassalanter mausoleum. Inform Y.G." (**Cassalanter Villa**, C4 Reading Room, and **Fireball!**, The Cassalanter Dinner; DC 14 Intelligence (History) identifies Y.G. as Yalah Gralhund)
- The Cassalanter devotional mark under Hurv Taldred's shirt (**Gralhund Villa**, G2; seen by a party that probes his loyalties)
- The family chronicle on the lectern in G12. It records a tiefling lineage, an ancestor's pact with a devil and an amputated tail. Opening it without Yalah's brass key releases 3 specters.
