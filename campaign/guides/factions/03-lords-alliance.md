# The Lords' Alliance

Game mechanics for running the Lords' Alliance as a player faction. For who the Lords' Alliance are, see the **Lords' Alliance** organization page.

## Grand Game Stance

The Lords' Alliance wants the 500,000 gold pieces returned to Waterdeep's city treasury. In Laeral Silverhand's framing: the gold was taxed from Waterdavian citizens and merchants. Lord Neverember's exile does not change its ownership. She will see it recovered, disbursed properly, and will credit those who made the recovery possible — but she will not blink at institutional procedure to get it.

At campaign start, Laeral is three moves behind. Jalester's field reports and Watch contacts have placed the vault somewhere in Neverwinter. She is not yet aware the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced in Waterdeep or that all four villain factions are circling it. PCs who pass her intelligence early change the shape of her involvement significantly. Those who keep her in the dark find her arriving at **Vault of Dragons** as an uncertain variable rather than an ally.

**What the Lords' Alliance shares proactively:**
- Watch patrol schedules for any ward, once per week, for PCs with valid Alliance credentials (Renown 3+)
- The outstanding Thayan warrant on Esloon Bezant, filed three years ago and accessible to Alliance agents (after Mission 3)
- A partial dossier on Cassalanter villa's Watch rotation, compiled after Mission 4's Aftermath delivers the Holmbridge connection

**What the Lords' Alliance asks for:**
- Intelligence on what the villain factions intend to do with the vault gold, not just where it is
- A commitment that the gold will reach Waterdeep's treasury or an approved dispersal — not a single faction's coffers
- The name of whoever is currently holding the Stone of Golorr, whenever the PCs can safely disclose it

## First Meeting

- Jalester finds them himself at the Yawning Portal (or Trollskull if the party rarely goes out); references the **Finding Floon** warehouse — he has been watching since then
- Pitch: Alliance needs people who can operate where Watch and Guard cannot; resources and backing in exchange; assignments are not optional — only faction that states this upfront
- No ceremony on acceptance; stands and leaves
- At the door, without turning: "Assignments from us are not optional. If that changes your answer, now is the time to say so." — waits three seconds, then goes

## Renown & Ranks

### Earning Renown

- **Report Grand Game activity to Jalester (+1).** Brief Jalester on any confirmed faction operation, location, or agent. One award per significant piece of intelligence; vague tips do not qualify.
- **Bring the Grand Game to Laeral's attention (+2).** Formally brief Jalester with enough evidence for him to escalate to the Open Lord. One of the most significant acts an Alliance operative can perform — triggers Laeral's direct involvement.
- **Assist the City Watch in a crisis (+1).** Aid the Watch in any situation exceeding their capacity — monsters, organized assault, mind-controlled citizens. Once per incident.
- **Expose or neutralize a Manshoon operative (+2).** Identify, capture, or eliminate a named agent of Manshoon's Zhentarim cell. Delivering a live prisoner for questioning earns the full reward; confirmed elimination earns +1.
- **Prevent an act of large-scale violence in Waterdeep (+1).** Stopping a gang war flashpoint, preventing an assassination of a public figure, or averting a mass-casualty event. Once per significant incident.
- **Recover the stolen dragons and arrange their return to the city (+4).** The Alliance's primary objective. The single highest-value act available to Alliance operatives and will elevate any character directly to the attention of the Open Lord.

### Lords' Alliance Ranks & Benefits

|--------|------|----------|
| 1 | **Cloak** | Recognized operative. Watch officers treat the character as a trusted civilian informant; minor legal infractions overlooked once per tenday. |
| 3 | **Redknife** | Jalester requests a Watch escort of two **Guards** for one dangerous errand per tenday. Access to Alliance safe houses in the Castle and Trades Wards. Jalester can pull a string to have minor charges dropped or a Watch report suppressed once per week. |
| 10 | **Stingblade** | Jalester arranges introduction to one named NPC in Waterdeep's political or merchant class once per quest. Alliance covers legal costs — bail, fines, damages — for one incident per quest. Access to Watch crime reports, witness statements, and active investigation files for any case in the city (classified cases require 4 hours and a DC 12 Charisma check). |
| 25 | **Warduke** | Laeral Silverhand grants one private audience per quest. Alliance deploys a response team of four **Veterans** to assist in one operation per quest, authorized personally by Jalester. Alliance intelligence shares current estimated locations of all four villain faction response teams, updated every 3 days through Jalester's Watch contacts. A Masked Lord takes a discreet interest: once per quest, a written request through Jalester results in that Lord using their authority to open a door, seal an investigation, or apply political pressure. Their identity is never revealed. |
| 50 | **Lioncrown** | Full political backing of the Open Lord. One outstanding charge against a party member dropped or a wanted status cleared at Laeral's authority. The Alliance assigns a dedicated attaché empowered to act on the Open Lord's behalf in the field. Laeral will answer three direct questions about the Grand Game, the vault, or Waterdeep's political situation — honestly. |
