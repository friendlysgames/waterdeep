# The Lords' Alliance

Game mechanics for running the Lords' Alliance as a player faction. For who the Lords' Alliance are, their history, and their place in Waterdeep's politics, see the **Lords' Alliance** organization page.

## Grand Game Stance

The Lords' Alliance wants the 500,000 gold pieces returned to Waterdeep's city treasury. In Laeral Silverhand's framing, the gold was taxed from Waterdhavian citizens and merchants — Lord Neverember's exile does not change its ownership. She will see it recovered and properly disbursed, and will credit those who made the recovery possible, but she will not bend institutional procedure to accomplish it.

At campaign start, Laeral is three moves behind. Jalester's field reports and Watch contacts have placed the vault somewhere in Neverwinter. She is not yet aware the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced in Waterdeep or that all four villain factions are circling it. PCs who share intelligence with her early change the shape of her involvement significantly. Those who keep her in the dark find her arriving at **Vault of Dragons** as an uncertain variable rather than an ally.

**What the Lords' Alliance shares proactively:**
- Watch patrol schedules for any ward, once per week, for PCs with valid Alliance credentials (Renown 3+)
- The outstanding Thayan warrant on Esloon Bezant, filed three years ago and accessible to Alliance agents (after Mission 3)
- A partial dossier on Cassalanter villa's Watch rotation, compiled after Mission 4's Aftermath delivers the Holmbridge connection

**What the Lords' Alliance asks for:**
- Intelligence on what the villain factions intend to do with the vault gold, not just where it is
- A commitment that the gold will reach Waterdeep's treasury or an approved dispersal, not a single faction's coffers
- The name of whoever is currently holding the Stone of Golorr, whenever the PCs can safely disclose it

## First Meeting

Jalester finds the party himself — at the Yawning Portal if they are regular presences there, at Trollskull Manor if they rarely venture out. He references the **Finding Floon** warehouse without prompting, making clear he has been watching since the investigation. There is no recruitment ceremony: he has already decided.

His pitch is direct. The Alliance needs operatives who can work where the City Watch and City Guard cannot. In exchange, the Alliance provides resources and institutional backing. He answers any questions without hurrying, then delivers his condition before they can give an answer:

> "Assignments from us are not optional. If that changes your answer, now is the time to say so."

He waits three seconds, then leaves.

> [!design]**The Only Faction That Says It**
> The Lords' Alliance is the only player faction that states upfront that its assignments are not optional. This is a character point for Jalester, not a penalty mechanic — he says it because the Alliance has had operatives go dark on critical operations, and he will not pretend this is a casual arrangement. Players who push back open a useful conversation; the condition is not negotiable, but Jalester will explain his reasoning.

## Renown & Ranks

### Earning Renown

Alliance operatives earn Renown through acts that advance Waterdeep's institutional interests: delivering intelligence, neutralizing Manshoon's network, and securing the vault gold.

- **Report Grand Game activity to Jalester (+1).** Brief Jalester on any confirmed faction operation, location, or agent. One award per significant piece of intelligence; vague tips do not qualify.
- **Bring the Grand Game to Laeral's attention (+2).** Formally brief Jalester with enough evidence for him to escalate to the Open Lord. This triggers Laeral's direct involvement. One award for this act in the campaign.
- **Assist the City Watch in a crisis (+1).** Aid the Watch in any situation exceeding their capacity — monsters, organized assault, mind-controlled citizens. Once per incident.
- **Expose or neutralize a Manshoon operative (+2).** Identify, capture, or eliminate a named agent of Manshoon's Zhentarim cell. Delivering a live prisoner for questioning earns the full reward; confirmed elimination earns +1.
- **Prevent an act of large-scale violence in Waterdeep (+1).** Stopping a gang war flashpoint, preventing an assassination of a public figure, or averting a mass-casualty event. Once per significant incident.
- **Recover the stolen dragons and arrange their return to the city (+4).** The Alliance's primary objective. The single highest-value act available to Alliance operatives — it elevates any character directly to the Open Lord's attention.

### Lords' Alliance Ranks & Benefits

| Renown | Rank | Benefits |
|--------|------|----------|
| 1 | **Cloak** | Recognized operative. Watch officers treat the character as a trusted civilian informant and overlook minor legal infractions once per tenday. |
| 3 | **Redknife** | Jalester arranges a Watch escort of two **Guards** for one dangerous errand per tenday. The character gains access to Alliance safe houses in the Castle and Trades Wards. Jalester can pull a string to have minor charges dropped or a Watch report suppressed once per week. |
| 10 | **Stingblade** | Jalester arranges an introduction to one named NPC in Waterdeep's political or merchant class once per quest. The Alliance covers legal costs — bail, fines, damages — for one incident per quest. The character gains access to Watch crime reports, witness statements, and active investigation files for any case in the city (classified cases require 4 hours and a DC 12 Charisma check). |
| 25 | **Warduke** | Laeral Silverhand grants one private audience per quest. The Alliance deploys a response team of four **Veterans** to assist in one operation per quest, authorized personally by Jalester. Alliance intelligence shares current estimated locations of all four villain faction response teams, updated every 3 days through Jalester's Watch contacts. A Masked Lord takes a discreet interest: once per quest, a written request through Jalester results in that Lord using their authority to open a door, seal an investigation, or apply political pressure. Their identity is never revealed. |
| 50 | **Lioncrown** | Full political backing of the Open Lord. One outstanding charge against a party member is dropped or a wanted status is cleared at Laeral's authority. The Alliance assigns a dedicated attaché empowered to act on the Open Lord's behalf in the field. Laeral answers three direct questions about the Grand Game, the vault, or Waterdeep's political situation. She answers all three honestly. |
