# The Lords' Alliance

For history, politics, and their place in Waterdeep, see the **Lords' Alliance** organization page.

## Grand Game Stance

The Lords' Alliance wants the 500,000 gold pieces returned to Waterdeep's city treasury. In Laeral Silverhand's framing, the gold was taxed from Waterdhavian citizens and merchants, and Lord Neverember's exile doesn't change who owns it. She will see it recovered and properly disbursed, and she will credit those who made the recovery possible, but she won't bend institutional procedure to do it.

At campaign start, Laeral is three moves behind. Jalester's field reports and the Watch's contacts have placed the vault somewhere in Neverwinter. She doesn't know that the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced in Waterdeep or that all four villain factions are circling it. Characters who share intelligence early change the shape of her involvement. Those who keep her in the dark find her arriving at **Vault of Dragons** as an uncertain variable rather than an ally.

Two outcomes move her faster. **Ledger Delivered** shows her where the gold lies. **LA Grand Game Escalated** tells her who holds the Stone and who is racing for it.

Alliance speakers say "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener. Jalester also separates the Splinter from Davil Starsong's cell, with the words "not Davil's people". They say "the carnival fleet" until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked. They never say more of the Cassalanters than that the family is too rich and too pleasant, and nobody in the Alliance has proof of anything.

**What the Lords' Alliance shares proactively:**
- Watch patrol schedules from the day a character joins as a Cloak at Renown 1. A member names one ward to Durnan at the Yawning Portal's bar once per tenday, and the note waits there the next evening.
- The outcomes of **The Red Wizard's Price**, which **Faction Outposts** reads. **Splinter Deal Scuttled** ends the hiring of Esloon Bezant's crew. **Sarvos Captured** makes the strike on the Fillet Lane checkpoint fail. **Esloon Question Delivered** puts Esloon's question on Laeral's desk.
- A partial Cassalanter dossier ten days after **The Archer Above**, once Vhaspar Coldwater is in custody and **Halek Farran Named** is marked. It holds Halek's name, the guard rotation at the villa's service gate and the rough size of its outer wall. **Villa Address Identified** tells **Cassalanter Villa** that the address is known.

**What the Lords' Alliance asks for:**
- Intelligence on what the villain factions intend to do with the vault gold, and not only where it is.
- A commitment that the gold will reach Waterdeep's treasury or an approved dispersal, and not a single faction's coffers.
- The name of whoever holds the Stone of Golorr, whenever the party can safely disclose it. **Escalation to the Open Lord** is where a member does so.

## First Meeting

A Watch runner brings a card sealed in grey wax to Trollskull Manor and leaves without waiting for a reply. It names the corner table beside the Yawning Portal's well, where Jalester sits from dusk for ten days. Durnan points the way, and Jalester opens by asking which door the party used at the warehouse on Candle Lane.

Every character who belongs to no other faction is a candidate and answers for themself. Jalester explains what the Alliance is and what it gives, answers questions and then delivers the Condition: "Assignments from us are not optional." Each candidate gives their own answer after three seconds of silence, and a refusal is final.

Each candidate who accepts becomes a Cloak at Renown 1. The Cloak gives a Watch overlook once per tenday for one minor infraction, and the patrol note described above. The party also receives the Alliance's grant for Trollskull Manor, which cuts the renovation total by 200 gp once, when the first candidate accepts. No assignment is handed out that night, and **The Long Watch** reaches each member at the same table once the member is 2nd level.

Full scene: [**Lords' Alliance First Meeting**](../../quests/faction-events/lords-alliance/00-first-meeting/ev-01-first-meeting.md). Acceptance marks the **Lords' Alliance Joined** outcome for each member by name, and a refusal marks **Lords' Alliance Offer Closed**.

## Renown & Ranks

### Earning Renown

Alliance operatives earn Renown through acts that advance Waterdeep's institutional interests: delivering intelligence, neutralizing the Splinter's network, and securing the vault gold.

- **Report Grand Game activity to Jalester (+1).** Brief Jalester on any confirmed faction operation, location, or agent. One award per significant piece of intelligence, and vague tips don't qualify.
- **Bring the Grand Game to Laeral's attention (+2).** Give Jalester the Stone's holder, the vault's place and two villain factions, so that he can escalate to the Open Lord. This triggers **Escalation to the Open Lord** and pays each member at the table once in the campaign. Laeral does not appear. She answers by a note that Jalester reads at the table the next evening.
- **Assist the City Watch in a crisis (+1).** Aid the Watch in any situation beyond its capacity, such as monsters, organized assault or mind-controlled citizens. Once per incident.
- **Expose or neutralize a Splinter operative (+2).** Identify, capture, or eliminate a named agent of the Splinter. Delivering a live prisoner for questioning earns the full reward, and confirmed elimination earns +1.
- **Prevent an act of large-scale violence in Waterdeep (+1).** Stop a gang war flashpoint, an assassination of a public figure or a mass-casualty event. Once per significant incident.
- **Recover the stolen dragons and arrange their return to the city (+4).** This is the Alliance's primary objective. It is the highest-value act available to an operative, and it brings the character to the Open Lord's attention.

### Lords' Alliance Ranks & Benefits

Ranks and benefits belong to individual members. Companions who are not members can stand beside a member and gain nothing from the Alliance. Jalester delivers each rank event at his table on the evening after the member crosses the threshold. If **Laeral Told of Jalester** is marked, Sevel Dastar delivers every later contact at a table away from the well. A member whose Renown later falls keeps the rank, and the benefits wait until the Renown is restored.

### Suspension and Dismissal

Briefs from **The Long Watch** to **Fentree's Ledger** each state a deadline in Jalester's own words. A member who refuses a Brief, or who lets the stated deadline pass without taking any step, is suspended at a visit at the well table. **An Audience with the Open Lord** is a summons and has no deadline, so it cannot cause a suspension.

Honest failure costs nothing. A target who escapes, a failed roll or a beaten party does not fire the visit, and neither does a reason that fits the table of excuses in **Suspension and Dismissal**. These include illness, a captive's life, another faction's emergency and a Watch detention.

A suspended member receives no Briefs, rank events or rank benefits until **LA Reinstated** is marked. Reinstatement has three routes: verified intelligence, a short errand that Jalester names, or Laeral's word after **Escalation to the Open Lord**. A member who refuses the errand, who triggers the visit again after reinstatement or who commits a serious offense is dismissed. Renown is never reduced, and a dismissed member's score stays on the books and does nothing.

## Missions

Each mission becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member meets its gate. Missions award no rank. Base Renown is paid to each participating member, and each mission lists three bonuses of +1 that can raise it by up to 3.

| Gate | Mission | Summary | Renown |
|------|---------|---------|--------|
| Joining, 2nd level | [**The Long Watch**](../../quests/faction-events/lords-alliance/m01-the-long-watch/overview.md) | Guard a crew of Dungsweepers' Guild sweepers on their Trades Ward circuit for a tenday. A leak puts the crew at risk, and a carrion crawler comes up from the sewers on day nine. | +2 |
| Renown 3, 3rd level | [**The Exile's Return**](../../quests/faction-events/lords-alliance/m02-the-exiles-return/overview.md) | Find Harko Swornhold, an exile working for Xanathar's Guild in the Dock Ward, within three nights. Jalester orders him put to the sword, and Harko's surrender is the turn. | +2 |
| Renown 5, 4th level | [**The Red Wizard's Price**](../../quests/faction-events/lords-alliance/m03-the-red-wizards-price/overview.md) | End the Splinter's deal with the Thayan wizard Esloon Bezant at the Skewered Dragon before the handshake at half past seven. Combat, a Watch sergeant, a bribe or a taken broker all work. | +3 |
| Renown 8, 5th level | [**The Archer Above**](../../quests/faction-events/lords-alliance/m04-the-archer-above/overview.md) | Find Vhaspar Coldwater, a retired Watch sharpshooter who has killed three people from high windows, and take him alive. He can name Halek Farran, and a scrap in his coat points at the Cassalanter villa. | +3 |
| Renown 10, 6th level | [**Fentree's Ledger**](../../quests/faction-events/lords-alliance/m05-fentrees-ledger/overview.md) | Recover the private ledger of the dead trade investigator Orvane Fentree from the broker Rezka before she sells it at the Green Dragon Inn. The ledger shows the gold was paid out in Waterdeep. | +4 |
| Renown 13, 7th level, after **Kolat Towers** | [**An Audience with the Open Lord**](../../quests/faction-events/lords-alliance/m06-an-audience-with-the-open-lord/overview.md) | Attend a formal audience with Laeral Silverhand at Piergeiron's Palace. She asks three questions about the Stone, the four factions and an offer from the Splinter, and she issues a Masked Lord's Writ. | +4 |

**Fentree's Ledger** ends in one of three outcomes. **Ledger Delivered** is the main trigger for the audience and the evidence for **Escalation to the Open Lord**. **Ledger Sold to the Guild** and **Ledger Sold to the Cassalanters** are read by **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa** and **Vault of Dragons**, whichever has not yet run. If the ledger was sold, the summons to the audience comes at Renown 13 and 7th level after **Kolat Towers**, whether or not **Escalation to the Open Lord** has run.

Parties that ran three lair heists reach 7th level only after **Vault of Dragons**, and they play **An Audience with the Open Lord** then. The Vault does not require it.

Full mission write-ups are in the **Faction Events** quest journals.
