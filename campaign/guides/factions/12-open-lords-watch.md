# The Open Lord's Watch

Game mechanics for running the Open Lord's Watch as a reactor. For lore, see the organization page **The Open Lord's Watch**. For ranks, posts and patrols, see **The City Watch** in Governance.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **What this page covers:** how Waterdeep's government reacts to the party through the City Watch. It sets out Laeral Silverhand's stance in the Grand Game, the Open Lord response team, the Watch's escalation and the leads that the Watch and its records give.
> - **What kind of faction it is:** a reactor. Characters cannot join the Watch, it is not a villain, and it has no outposts, renown or missions. Faction ranks that name the Watch keep their benefits on their own Factions guide pages.
> - **Where the Watch responds:** **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!** and **Gralhund Villa**, where the quest journals fix its behavior. The structure documents for **Faction Outposts**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers** and **Vault of Dragons** add more.
> - **What is built here:** no source gives the Watch escalation tiers, a headcount, a response time by ward or a rule for handing a scene to the City Guard. This page builds those rules from what the quests fix. Every rule that no source states carries a tag such as `[D1]`, and Draft Notes explains each one.
> - **Lore and structure:** the organization page **The Open Lord's Watch** holds the lore. **The City Watch** in Governance holds ranks, posts, patrols and the services beside the Watch.

## Grand Game Stance

Laeral Silverhand wants the 500,000 gold dragons back in the city treasury. In her view the money was taxed from Waterdhavian citizens and merchants, and Neverember's removal does not change who owns it. She wants it recovered without a public scandal. She does not play the Grand Game herself, because the Lords' Alliance acts for her outside the Palace. The Watch is the part of her government that stands in the street, and its job is order in the wards, not the vault.

The Watch plays as a mostly decent institution that bends under pressure from above. Its officers keep the blood off the streets and arrest people without a scene. They take names, they answer reports, and they need a warrant to search. They give little attention to the sewers, and they do not patrol the Field Ward.

**How information reaches Laeral:**
- Jalester Silvermane is her field operative and reports to her directly. He carries a badge of the Watch and reads the Watch's ledger of file requests each tenday.
- Her intelligence comes largely from Jalester and from the Watch, and it runs about three moves behind the factions.
- A report made to the Watch is the likeliest route by which she learns what is happening. She may never learn all of it.

**What she believes on Ches 1:**
- Neverember's gold lies in Neverwinter. Jalester's field reports and the Watch's contacts placed it there.
- She does not know that the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced in Waterdeep.
- She does not know that four villain factions are circling it.

**What the Watch knows on Ches 1:** it sees a street war between Xanathar's Guild and the Zhentarim in the Dock Ward, and it is called to their brawls often. It already holds a file on the Doom Raiders. It has closed three complaints against one Sea Ward house without findings, and it cannot touch that house without evidence.

**When the party reports to the Watch:**
1. The station writes the report down. A constable checks anything a constable can check, and a letter reports the finding the next morning. Sergeant Marek Dunmere does this at the North Ward station.
2. Jarlaxle has a report about his watchers from his Watch contacts before the ink is dry. No source says that any other faction has the same reach.
3. The report tells Laeral that a party is bringing the Watch trouble, and it names the party. It does not give her the Stone's holder, the vault's place or two villain factions. Only **Escalation to the Open Lord** supplies those, and only that Event marks **LA Grand Game Escalated**. `[D7]`
4. Handing a suspect to the Watch earns goodwill. After **Istrid Horn Turned In**, the Watch questions the party less about Gralhund Villa.

## Response Team

When Laeral wants something from the party, her order passes down through the bureaucracy and a local team of watchmen carries it out. The Alexandrian roster, in 2024 names, is below.

| Creature | Number | CR | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warrior Veteran** | 1 | 3 | Leads the team |
| **Guard** | 4 | 1/8 | Constables |
| **Mage Apprentice** | 0 or 1 | 2 | Replaces the Alexandrian's apprentice wizard. `[D3]` |

The Alexandrian design note says the team is "designed to be a Medium encounter for the PCs." The party can beat it in a fair fight or run with very good odds. The note adds that the City Watch can be set against other factions' response teams without overwhelming them.

The sources give one roster and no scaling by party level, so this page adds none. The team is the same at every level. The Medium rating is the Alexandrian's own, and the campaign has not re-run it under CR 2.0.

**How the team runs:**
- **Order.** The team's order is to bring one named character to the Palace to speak with Laeral. It does not search, seize property or arrest. `[D2]`
- **Manner.** The officers use Watch talk: "What befalls?", "Hold!", "Down arms!" and "Talk truth!". They do not strike first.
- **Mage Apprentice.** The apprentice comes only when the matter involves the Stone, a construct or spellcasting. `[D3]`
- **When it comes.** The team is the Watch's response at Alert. It is never the first response. `[D1]`
- **If the party beats it or escapes.** The Watch moves to Lockdown for that character. The Alexandrian says the party is then living on borrowed time, because the Open Lord has deeper and stronger resources. `[D4]`

## Escalation

No source gives the Watch escalation tiers. Track one Watch tier for each character, using the four tiers from **Running the Villains**. `[D1]` **Watch File Opened** raises every character in the party to Suspicious.

| Tier | Trigger | The Watch's response |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | The Watch has no file on the character. | Routine patrols. A pair of officers walks the Castle Ward, and squads of eight walk the Dock Ward, rising to twelve at night. Overt crime in view of officers ends in arrest and a magister. |
| Suspicious | **Watch File Opened** is marked, or a surviving witness has described the character to a constable. | Constables ask questions where the character lodges. A summons for questioning arrives within three days. Castle Ward patrols double. |
| Alert | The character ignored a summons, fled the Watch or resisted arrest. | The Open Lord response team comes for the character. At a walled estate, a Mage and 6 Warrior Veterans answer instead. |
| Lockdown | A killing of bystanders in the street is blamed on the party, or a Watch officer or Guard is killed. | The City Guard seals the district and the Watch takes the scene after the cordon holds. A Watchful Order mage assists, and Griffon Cavalry riders circle overhead. |

A tier falls by one step for each tenday without a new incident, because an open file goes cold in a tenday (**Silencing Skeemo**). `[D1]` The Open Lord may mobilize Force Grey, but the published adventure calls that a last resort, and this page does not use it as a Watch response.

**Response clocks the quests fix:**

| Where | Force | Arrives |
|---|---|---|
| Outside the walls of Gralhund Villa | 1 Mage and 6 Warrior Veterans | 5 to 10 minutes after the first audible noise. The events write this as 1d6 + 4. |
| A North Ward rooftop chase, when a dog barks | A Watch patrol | 3 to 8 minutes. The chase table writes this as 1d6 + 2. |
| The publisher's office in **The Wazoo Affair** | A patrol of 2 Guards | About 5 minutes after a shout |
| **The Legate's Eyes**, if Brenna calls the Watch | A patrol of 2 Guards | 10 minutes |
| The Yellowspire tower in the Castle Ward | A patrol | 1 minute after the first blow on the door |
| Trollskull Alley after the fireball | 6 Guards seal the alley. Barnibus Blastwind, Saeth Cromley and 20 constables take charge. | 5 minutes, then 20 minutes |
| A disturbance at Trollskull Manor | A Watch patrol | Within the hour, once an Order of the Gauntlet member has sworn the oath |
| Cassalanter Villa, if a guard sounds the alarm | 40 Warrior Veterans cordon the property | No time given |

A party found inside Gralhund Villa when the Watch arrives is questioned and detained unless a character gives a credible reason. The check is DC 14 Charisma (Deception) or Charisma (Persuasion). This is Alexandrian text that the **Gralhund Villa** events do not carry.

**When the Watch hands over to the City Guard or the Griffon Cavalry:** the sources show cases and give no rule, so this is a design call. `[D5]`
- The Guard guards the walls, the gates, government buildings, the harbor and officials. The Watch polices the streets.
- When an incident is at a gate, a wall, a government building, the harbor or an official, or when it kills a crowd in the street, the Guard answers first and seals the scene. The Watch takes over once the cordon holds.
- The fireball follows this order. Guards seal the alley within 5 minutes, and the Watch arrives 20 minutes after the blast.
- A massacre in a licensed North Ward tavern brings the Guard, the Watchful Order and possibly Vajra Safahr within the hour. The Watch alone answers a smaller disturbance.
- Once Manshoon's whereabouts are known, the Guard cordons Kolat Towers, and the Blackstaff and the Watchful Order assist as needed.
- Griffon Cavalry riders stay in the air. They appear over a Guard-level scene or when someone flies without a licence, and they report what they see. They do not land to arrest. On a rooftop chase, a rider's pass spots a character who fails a DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check.

**When the Watch backs down:**
- **No warrant, no pursuit.** The constables who spoke to Lord Gralhund took his word and did not search. Jalester says that unauthorized entry into a noble's estate is burglary in law, and he offers a conversation with Cromley, who will look away for 48 hours if the party commits to sharing its intelligence in that time.
- **A well-connected house.** The Watch cannot touch the Cassalanters without evidence. Willifort Crowelle can also arrange for a Cassalanter-connected Watch lieutenant to take a new interest in the party's tavern, and a Watch investigation there lowers Fame.
- **A Masked Lord.** Corylus Thann has eight crooked Watch veterans crack down on anyone who annoys him, and he clears the charges against them.
- **Coverage that thins.** Someone with civic influence has reduced Watch patrols around Kolat Towers over eighteen months. The Faire's legal protection makes Watch action on its ships politically difficult.
- **The street war.** Captain Hyustus Staget lets Xanathar's Guild and the Zhentarim wear each other down, and he will not send a force into the sewers after a suspected spy. No campaign event uses him.
- **A witness.** The Watch releases a witness it holds, such as Urstul Floxin, within 24 hours, because it has no grounds to keep him.
- **Friends of the Open Lord.** Lords' Alliance agents are more likely to be excused when Laeral has cause to let them off. She or the Blackstaff usually frees an arrested Force Grey member.
- **Rule for the table.** Pressure from a noble house, a Masked Lord or the Open Lord holds the Watch at Suspicious against the person protected. The cap lifts when the party brings evidence that would hold up before a magister. `[D6]`

## Revelation List

*Each entry names the lead and, in parentheses, where the quests place it. None is the only path to its conclusion. The Revelation Lists of the villain pages carry the Three Clue Rule paths.*

**The fireball scene and the dead** (North Ward Watch Station)
- Barnibus Blastwind's three conclusions: the gnome was running from at least three armed pursuers, all of them were moving toward the tavern, and the blast came from above and to the east (**Fireball!**, DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion), one conclusion for each success).
- The bodies, kept until Ches 27 (**Fireball!**, through a faction channel, a 25 gp temple donation or a DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check on Barnibus or Cromley).
- The construct type, named by Barnibus's Watchful Order contacts within 48 hours of the blast, which points to the House of Inspired Hands (**Fireball!**, the slowest of three paths).

**The Dock Ward crime scene** (**Finding Floon**)
- A brief supervised talk with one cooperative prisoner, if the party names Floon or Renaer as kidnapping victims (the Watch sergeant, DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)). A character with a City Watch background asks two questions of each prisoner with no check.

**Saerdoun Street** (**Gralhund Villa**)
- The Watch passes about every 20 minutes (30 minutes of watching, or Jalester, Cromley or any city-wise contact).
- The beat officers were moved to a Southern Ward assignment the week before the raid, on a request from inside the Watch hierarchy whose source is unknown (the Lords' Alliance debrief).
- Someone on the force owed Istrid Horn money, and her ledger vanishes from the evidence log within a week (**Istrid Horn Turned In**).

**The Watch's files** (the central records office, Castle Ward; Clerk-Sergeant Ottel Raske; a Stingblade reads them)
- Standard files take one hour: ward incident logs, warrants outstanding, the missing persons ledger, patrol rotations, the archer case file and the Fentree death report.
- Classified files take four hours and a DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check: the Splinter inquiry file, Xanathar's Guild informant file, the household inquiries file, the harbor master's carnival file and the informant identifications.
- The Splinter inquiry file reports unusual cart and carriage traffic at a Trades Ward safe house and names nobody.
- The Guild informant file describes a Castle Ward rowhouse where stretchers are carried in at night, with two sworn complaints and no arrests.
- The household inquiries file holds three closed inquiries against one Sea Ward house, for a missing servant, a debt dispute and a noise complaint. It holds nothing more.
- The harbor master's file records berth inspections of the carnival fleet, and every one passed.

## Quest Hooks

| Quest | What the Watch does |
|---|---|
| **Finding Floon** | A dozen guards hold three arrested Splinter thugs on the party's road to Candle Lane, and the Watch waves the party off. |
| **Trollskull Alley** | A report about the watchers to Sergeant Marek Dunmere marks **BD Contact Severed** for each reporting character, and the Watch finds nothing. A Watch investigation of the manor costs Fame. |
| **Fireball!** | Guards seal the alley, then Barnibus and Cromley take charge. The dead go to the North Ward station, and the Watch sweeps the ward for witnesses. |
| **Gralhund Villa** | The response clock above applies. The Watch seals the villa by the morning of Ches 25 and cracks down on the Black Network. Davil Starsong is held at Castle Waterdeep two days after the villa. |
| **Faction Outposts** | Davil is in Watch custody and the crackdown has scattered the Zhentarim response teams. Jalester wants outpost intelligence shared with the Watch and offers legal cover for the quest's operations in exchange for Manshoon intelligence that will hold up before the magisters. |
| **Cassalanter Villa** | Jalester can have Sea Ward patrols "assigned elsewhere" for a window if the party holds evidence from **Faction Outposts**. |
| **Sea Maidens Faire** | The Faire's private docking arrangements skip Watch inspection, and some Watch officers have been paid to look away. |
| **Kolat Towers** | Watch coverage around the towers is informally diminished, and Jalester can arrange a four-hour reassignment. |
| **Vault of Dragons** | A Masked Lord's Writ makes Watch officers defer within four rounds of presentation. Two Guards stand at each City of the Dead gate at night. Dagult Neverember arrives with Watch loyalists. |
