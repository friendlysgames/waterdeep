# The Order of the Gauntlet

For who the Order are, see the **Order of the Gauntlet** organization page.

## Grand Game Stance

The Order keeps a fixed order of threats: diabolism first, aberrant evil second, everything else after that. The vault gold concerns Savra only as a function of who might seize it. Her fear is that 500,000 gold would fund the thing she suspects the Sea Ward family of doing, and she cannot yet say what that is. In the Splinter's or Xanathar's hands, it would be nearly as bad. In the city treasury, she has no objection.

At campaign start, Savra has suspected the Cassalanters for months. They are a Sea Ward family whose public piety never slips, whose charity goes to causes that do not survive scrutiny, and whose Watch complaints were all closed without findings. She has no legal evidence and no investigators able to produce any. She has been building toward a confrontation she knows will come. The party changes this.

The Order steps outside the Grand Game entirely. It does not position for the vault or compete for faction influence. It hunts specific evil, and if the party helps find it, the Order throws its full weight behind them when it matters.

Order speakers say "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener, and Savra keeps Davil's Doom Raiders apart from the Splinter. They say "the carnival fleet" until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked. Savra says "the family" until a player names the Cassalanters, and then she adds that she has no proof. Nobody in the Order knows what the family worships, and nobody names a pact.

**What the Order shares proactively:**
- Tactical intelligence on devil types and how they fight, drawn from Savra's field experience.
- What Savra has seen of the Splinter's hired hands in the Field Ward, once a member has run **Breaking Up the Brawl**.
- The Order's file on the family, which a member receives with the **Whitehawk** rank at Renown 10. Until then Savra says only that the family's charity cannot be traced to any cause she can verify.

**What the Order asks for:**
- A paper a magistrate would read, such as a ledger, a signed statement or a witness who will swear on the record. Savra cannot file on a compelled answer alone, and she asks the party to bring her paper.
- Support when the Cassalanter confrontation comes (**Cassalanter Villa**).
- Honesty. Savra cannot work with people she is managing around, and she knows when she is being managed.

## First Meeting

Savra knocks once at Trollskull Manor and waits to be asked in. If nobody is home, she leaves a note with Tally Fellbranch. She names two targets: a beholder said to run the Guild beneath the city, and a Sea Ward family she will not name. She states the cost of the work plainly, including a quarter of what a member earns on big jobs. A character who asks sincerely about her past learns she spent three years in the Howling Hatred cult, and nothing about what she did there.

Every character who belongs to no other faction is a candidate and answers for themself: accept, decline or ask for time. A declined offer stays open, and the Event writes no closing outcome. Candidates who accept swear the oath at the Halls of Justice in the Castle Ward, which is open every day. A priest of Tyr seals it over a silver gauntlet, and the member becomes a Chevall at Renown 1 with the benefits below. Savra walks the party to a Guild hall in Order insignia when the first member swears, which cuts the renovation total of Trollskull Manor by 100 gp once. **Breaking Up the Brawl** reaches each member at the manor the next morning, once the member is 2nd level.

Full scene, branches and Savra's profile sidebar: [Order of the Gauntlet First Meeting](../../quests/faction-events/order-of-the-gauntlet/00-first-meeting/ev-01-first-meeting.md). Each oath marks the **Order of the Gauntlet Joined** outcome by name.

## Renown & Ranks

### Earning Renown

Rank and Renown belong to the individual member. Companions help with every mission except Savra's brief and debrief, and they earn no Renown.

- **Deliver solid evidence of what the Cassalanters are hiding to Savra (+2).** The Order suspects the family of something and cannot say what. Any physical evidence, such as a ledger, a ritual record, a signed instruction from a named family member or a witness who will speak before the Lords, brings the Order's full engagement. This pays once in the campaign, and the mission bonus lists do not repeat it.
- **Destroy or banish a devil or demon of CR 3 or higher (+1).** Savra must be told, and creatures dispatched without her knowledge do not count. This pays once for each member, and the mission bonus lists do not repeat it.
- **Rescue a mind-controlled or enslaved Waterdhavian (+1).** This covers freeing a citizen from an intellect devourer, breaking a charm placed by a Xanathar operative, or liberating someone held against their will. It pays once for each significant rescue.
- **Tithe a quarter of your own share of a large haul (+1).** When the party carries out a haul of 1,000 gp or more in one operation, each member owes 25% of their own equal share to a worthy cause. A member who pays before Savra's knock at dusk on the second day earns +1, once in the campaign. Payment after she asks earns nothing. The full procedure is in [The Tithe](../../quests/faction-events/order-of-the-gauntlet/s01-the-tithe/ev-01-the-tithe.md).
- **Refuse to take part in or cover up an atrocity, even on faction orders (+1).** A character who holds a moral line and can explain why to Savra earns her respect in a way tactical victories cannot.
- **Assist the Halls of Justice or another temple of the Triad with a formal request (+1).** One award per quest for meaningful cooperation with organized religious authorities aligned with the Order's values.

A member who refuses the tithe, or pays less than a quarter and does not make it up, is marked **Quietly Reassigned**. This pauses only the extras Savra offers unasked, such as her company in the field, follow-up temple briefings and watchers on places she has been watching herself. Missions still arrive, by a note from Brother Hallin Ostrel in place of Savra's knock. The Order has no suspension or dismissal, and only the Lords' Alliance suspends. Paying the declined quarter ends the reassignment the next morning.

### Order of the Gauntlet Ranks & Benefits

Ranks and benefits belong to individual members, and companions gain nothing from them. When a member crosses a threshold, a Priest Acolyte in a plain grey surcoat brings Savra's note at noon the next day, and the meeting is at the third bell past noon in the east annex of the Halls of Justice. A member whose Renown later falls keeps the rank, and the benefits wait until the Renown is restored.

| Renown | Rank | Benefits |
|--------|------|----------|
| 1 | **Chevall** | Granted when the member swears the oath. Savra vouches for the member at any temple of Tyr, Helm or Torm in Waterdeep. Each temple casts *Cure Wounds* on the member once a day and gives a bed for up to three nights in a row. If the Watch arrests the member on a minor charge, Savra puts in a good word once a tenday and the Order's clerk tends the member's affairs. The member can also ask Brother Haldric Wence at the Halls of Justice to arrange *Speak with Dead* on a body the Watch holds, once for each body. |
| 3 | **Marcheon** | Savra fights beside the party in one operation each quest, counted once for the whole party. She needs 24 hours' notice, a named place and enemy on the surface, and she refuses a fight that would get everyone killed. At levels 2 and 3 she is a reserve who acts only when a character drops. The armory in the east annex lends one mundane weapon, shield or suit of armor each tenday. The member can also ask one of three temples for a briefing each tenday. Each temple works through a fixed list of four patterns about a ward and a kind of event, with no names. The Torm chapel gives its fourth pattern, on signs of devils in a household, only to a member who has been through **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**. |
| 10 | **Whitehawk** | Once each quest, two Order Scouts watch one named person, building or street for three days and bring a written report. The Scouts refuse a named Cassalanter family member until **Cassalanter Villa** has run. Before each quest's main operation, the member receives a *Potion of Healing* and a *Potion of Greater Healing*. The member chooses one Uncommon item from four: a Sentinel Shield, a Cloak of Protection, a Brooch of Shielding or a Periapt of Wound Closure. Temple priests cast spells of 2nd level or lower for the member, with components under 100 gp covered. Savra gives the member the Order's Cassalanter dossier, which proves nothing and shows that someone works hard to keep the house closed. At each lair she offers support, set out in the **Whitehawk** event. |
| 25 | **Vindicator** | The Order opens one written inquiry at a time into a named target, if the member brings an exhibit a magistrate would read. The report arrives in twenty days. It goes to a magistrate of the Halls, and it is admissible before the Lords only once **Order Recognized** is marked. Once each quest, three Knights of the Waterdeep chapter (use the **Knight** stat block) fight beside the party on the surface for one operation, with three days' notice. The member also chooses a +1 weapon from the cache the Order took from a diabolist's cellar, and keeps it. |
| 50 | **Righteous Hand** | The investiture is held until the member is on the surface. Isaldric Veyne, the high priest of Tyr, invests the member in the Halls of Justice. The member receives a Writ of Knighthood, which allied temples and civic authorities honor across the Sword Coast. Once each quest, six **Knights** and two **Priests** of the chapter serve one major operation, with three days' notice. The Order pays for one casting of *Resurrection* (a 7th-level spell with a diamond worth 1,000 gp) by Veyne, with no condition on the cause of death. The member may also accept the Undermountain Mandate, which allows one call for aid on each Undermountain level. |

## Missions

Each mission becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member meets its gate. Missions award no rank. Base Renown is paid to each participating member, and each mission lists three bonuses of +1 that can raise it by up to 3. Savra briefs the first four missions at the manor door. She summons the member by note to **The Shrine on Aveen Street** and **The Writ and the Weight**.

| Gate | Mission | Summary | Renown |
|------|---------|---------|--------|
| Renown 1, 2nd level | [**Breaking Up the Brawl**](../../quests/faction-events/order-of-the-gauntlet/m01-breaking-up-the-brawl/overview.md) | Stop four hired men from cornering a Xanathar's Guild dealer in a Field Ward square at noon. The man who paid them works for the Splinter, and the party can learn what he looks like. | +2 |
| Renown 3, 3rd level | [**The Black Viper Investigation**](../../quests/faction-events/order-of-the-gauntlet/m02-the-black-viper-investigation/overview.md) | Find who gave *The Waterdeep Wazoo* a false story that the Black Viper is Lady Ammalia Cassalanter's twin. The trail runs through a forged record, a household secretary under an alias and a raven that is an imp. | +2 |
| Renown 5, 4th level | [**The Shard Shunners**](../../quests/faction-events/order-of-the-gauntlet/m03-the-shard-shunners/overview.md) | A halfling gang is robbing the night guards of a Field Ward tavern. Find out why and settle it before the Watch fills the taproom on the sixth night. | +3 |
| Renown 8, 5th level | [**Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**](../../quests/faction-events/order-of-the-gauntlet/m04-spined-devils-in-twelvedog-court/overview.md) | Spined devils have trapped three residents in a Field Ward court. Savra fights beside the party, and the summoner's papers point to an address on Aveen Street. | +3 |
| Renown 10, 6th level | [**The Shrine on Aveen Street**](../../quests/faction-events/order-of-the-gauntlet/m05-the-shrine-on-aveen-street/overview.md) | Get into a chandler's cellar that Savra cannot enter without a warrant. The party recovers a ledger in Victoro Cassalanter's hand, which Savra enters as an exhibit with a Watch clerk. | +4 |
| Renown 13, 7th level, after **Kolat Towers** | [**The Writ and the Weight**](../../quests/faction-events/order-of-the-gauntlet/m06-the-writ-and-the-weight/overview.md) | On the night the Lords hear Savra's petition, a Horned Devil that calls itself a bailiff comes to the Halls of Justice for the exhibit. The party defends the nave beside Savra and two Priests of Tyr. | +4 |

Parties that ran three lair heists reach 7th level only after **Vault of Dragons**, and they play **The Writ and the Weight** then. The Vault does not require it.

Full mission write-ups are in the **Faction Events** quest journals.
