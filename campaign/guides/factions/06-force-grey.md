# Force Grey

For who Force Grey are, see the **Force Grey** organization page.

## Grand Game Stance

Vajra's mandate is the city, and in particular the parts that arcane threats touch. At campaign start she knows that magical coercion is spreading among Waterdhavians in positions of influence, and she suspects a link to the gang war. She knows the Black Network has split. Hlam's first message gives her only the shape of the old Black Network coming back, with no name attached. She doesn't yet know the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced, or that four factions are competing for the vault.

She learns the rest incrementally through the party. Mission 1 adds Hlam's warning about the buried thing waking. Mission 2 brings a report of a vessel fixed under the *Eyecatcher*'s hull, which is the Bregan D'aerthe submersible, and she never learns whose it is. By Mission 4 she knows where Nihiloor's pool lies, from one report by an agent inside the Guild who has not answered since. When the party delivers the full Grand Game picture, with all four factions, the Stone, the vault and the timeline, she goes still for a long time and then begins writing.

Force Grey speakers say "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener, and they name Manshoon and Kolat Towers only after that. They never say "Jarlaxle" or "Bregan D'aerthe" to players.

**What Force Grey shares proactively:**
- Preparatory spells and consumables before missions (Renown 3+: one spell from a fixed list of six per tenday, with two days' notice; Renown 10+: Ysmay Halvane, a Tower mage, for one operation per quest)
- Arcane analysis of any artifact, document, or creature the party brings her. She tells them what she finds, because it costs her nothing to share intelligence with people she trusts.
- One suspended Watch case per member at Renown 25, for a case that arose from a Force Grey mission or an operation against a threat to the city. She uses it carefully and doesn't like being asked.

**What Force Grey asks for:**
- Intelligence about magical threats before they become crises
- A complete picture of the Grand Game, once the party has it to give — the most valuable thing they can do for Waterdeep
- That the party not tell Vajra only what she wants to hear. She has people for that and doesn't need more

**Grand Game goal:** The vault gold to the treasury. The pool, the placement records and the hosts already walking the city dealt with. The Splinter's arcane operations dismantled. The Stone of Golorr studied and understood before it's sealed away again. These are her priorities in order. Nihiloor always escapes the party, so she plans around him and not around his death. Her concern is which outcome leaves Waterdeep standing. The rest is scoring.

## First Meeting

Every character who belongs to no other faction is a candidate and answers for themself. Vajra's first *Sending*, exactly 25 words, goes to the candidate who spoke to Renaer last in the **Finding Floon** warehouse. If nobody climbs to the Tower, a second *Sending* reaches a different candidate the next morning. If that also goes unanswered, **Force Grey Offer Closed** is marked with the unanswered names, and the offer returns when the party advances a level. She receives the candidates at her standing desk at Blackstaff Tower, where the door opens before anyone knocks, and offers Gray Hand status on Renaer's word. Each candidate who accepts gets a letter of authorization, and no first mission is handed over. She closes with: *"Try to get some sleep. The work does not wait for people to be rested."*

## Renown & Ranks

### Earning Renown

- **Report a significant arcane threat to Vajra before it becomes a crisis (+1).** Intellect devourers in city officials, a necromancer in the City of the Dead, an illegal gate or summoning circle discovered during Grand Game operations — any magical threat the Watch can't handle. Once per confirmed new threat.
- **Bring the Grand Game fully to Vajra's attention (+2).** A formal briefing with sufficient evidence for Vajra to escalate to Laeral Silverhand. One of the most consequential acts a Gray Hand can perform; it transforms the political landscape of the campaign's final act.
- **Purge an intellect devourer infestation or free a mind-controlled Waterdhavian (+1).** Directly countering Xanathar's mind-control operations is Force Grey's most urgent Grand Game priority. A single implanted citizen counts as one incident; discovering and purging an entire network counts as two.
- **Protect Blackstaff Tower or its staff from a direct threat (+2).** Any coordinated attack on Blackstaff Tower is taken personally by Vajra.
- **Assist Vajra with arcane research connected to the Stone of Golorr or the Vault (+1).** Delivering a Golorr artifact for examination, exploring a relevant tunnel network, translating a relevant text — even when Vajra hasn't formally assigned the task.
- **Keep Laeral Silverhand's involvement in the Grand Game clean (+1).** Force Grey's value to the Open Lord lies partly in its deniability. Characters who resolve a problem without creating a political incident earn Vajra's appreciation in a way flashy violence doesn't. Once per quest.

### Force Grey Ranks & Benefits

Ranks belong to individual members, and only the four rank events (**Junior Griffon**, **Senior Griffon**, **Force Grey**, **Force Grey Commander**) hand out titles. No mission changes rank. A companion who is not a Force Grey member gains no rank and no benefit, though a member brings companions along for the spell, the mage, the veteran or the team on an operation. A member whose Renown later falls keeps the rank, and the benefits wait until the Renown is restored.

| Renown | Rank | Benefits |
|--------|------|----------|
| 1 | **Gray Hand** | Awarded at the First Meeting, with Vajra's letter of authorization. The member may enter Blackstaff Tower at any hour without appointment. Before a mission that needs one, Vajra hands over a single potion: *climbing* for a climb, *water breathing* for a dive, *healing* for a known fight. City Watch officers who see the letter are Friendly. |
| 3 | **Junior Griffon** | Once per tenday, with two days' notice, Vajra casts one spell on the member and up to four companions before a mission: *nondetection*, *water breathing*, *longstrider*, *tongues*, *darkvision* or *protection from energy*. The Tower's second-floor library answers one magical question per visit. Merris, the quartermaster, issues gear worth up to 50 gp and two Common potions (*healing* or *climbing*) every ten days at no cost, and holy water only when Vajra's brief names it. |
| 10 | **Senior Griffon** | The party receives a *wand of secrets* only if it holds none. Once per quest, with three days' notice, the member calls Ysmay Halvane, a Tower mage, to one operation. City officials are Friendly, and so is any Masked Lord whose identity the member has already learned. Vajra signs a written authorization for the City of the Dead at night, a sealed Watch evidence vault or a private armory (DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion), no check if the member answers her four questions plainly), once per tenday and never while a villain faction is at Lockdown. |
| 25 | **Force Grey** | The Underclock badge gives Advantage on Charisma checks to influence city officials and military officers while it is visible. Vajra suspends one Watch case for the member, once in the campaign. Once per quest, with three days' notice, Rhendar Orsk (a Warrior Veteran) goes on one mission for up to seven days. Once per quest, Vajra casts one 7th-level spell from a fixed list of five (*teleport*, *forcecage*, *Mordenkainen's magnificent mansion*, *project image*, *sequester*), on the surface only. |
| 50 | **Force Grey Commander** | The member commands a standing Tower team, led by Ysmay Halvane with Rhendar Orsk, Garrick Stoll and Sera Vantry, for missions the member chooses. The team fights as one fewer than the party. The member takes one 4th- or 5th-level *spell scroll* from the twelve in the Tower's vault, with no transport spells among them. Vajra holds a final reserve of one casting of any wizard spell except *wish*, cast on the surface or at the Yawning Portal. The Open Lord's letter of recognition is Public or Sealed, as the member chooses. |

## Missions

Each mission becomes available when an individual Force Grey member meets its gate. Missions award no rank, and the base Renown below can rise with the bonuses listed in each mission.

| Gate | Mission | Summary | Renown |
|------|---------|---------|--------|
| Joined, 2nd level | **Consulting Hlam** | Climb to the hermit monk Hlam's cave on Mount Waterdeep and ask what threats he has perceived. He answers honest visitors, and his second answer is a riddle about the old Black Network coming back. | +2 |
| Renown 3, 3rd level | **The Dragon in the Harbor** | Zelifarn, a Young Bronze Dragon, has taken Umberlee's offerings from the harbor moorings. Vajra provides *potions of water breathing* for the dive, and Meritide Blackfin gives the party 48 hours. | +2 |
| Renown 5, 4th level | **The Trouble with Meloon** | Force Grey veteran Meloon Wardragon is behaving oddly at the Yawning Portal. Watch him for a tenday, confirm what rides him and get it out of him before the courier's report on Day 10. | +3 |
| **M3 Complete** plus **Xanathar's Lair** preparation, or Renown 8 | **Destroy the Intellect Factory** | An objective inside **Xanathar's Lair**, played in Nihiloor's wing. Destroy the pool that breeds Occupying Devourers, take the placement records and free the captive. Nihiloor always escapes. Vajra pays for any *raise dead* the work needs. | +3 |
| Renown 10, 6th level | **The Legate's Eyes** | Three Watch magistrates have reversed rulings in the Guild's favour. Trace them to Orvyn Dall, an appeals clerk in a Trades Ward magistracy whose devourer reports through a Guild courier, and extract him without a Watch review. | +4 |
| Renown 13, 7th level | **Smoke in the Tower** | Splinter survivors strike Blackstaff Tower after **Kolat Towers**, using a resonance disruptor that Vira Solkan hid in the Tower. Who sent them depends on what Kolat Towers left of Manshoon, and no speaker names him until **Manshoon Named** is marked. Find Vira, hold the north service door and record a prisoner's testimony. The reward is the Open Lord's recognition, with no rank. | +4 |

Parties that ran three lair heists reach 7th level only after **Vault of Dragons**, and they play Smoke in the Tower then. The Vault does not require it.
