### About This Campaign

**Purpose:** Orients the DM to what kind of game this is before anything else is said to players.

**Content:**
- The three remix pillars (Make It a Heist / Feature All Villains / Fix-Up) stated plainly, one paragraph each
- What distinguishes this campaign from standard D&D: rewards planning over power, all four villain factions simultaneously active, no single right answer about what to do with the gold
- Who this campaign suits (groups that enjoy city exploration, lateral investigation, community stakes, emergent faction storytelling) and who it doesn't (groups that want a primary combat focus or a guided throughline)

**Tone:** Direct, honest. The DM needs to know if this campaign matches their group *before* they start it.

**DM Private Note (do NOT share with players):** This campaign contains deaths of bonded NPCs, infernal bargains with irreversible consequences, and a villain whose motivations are genuinely sympathetic. None of these should be spoiled at session zero — they land precisely because players do not see them coming.

Key content categories to handle carefully:
- A named NPC the players bonded with in Arc B dies in the fireball (Arc C opening)
- The Cassalanters made an infernal bargain that is irreversible; their motives are comprehensible; this is intentional
- Osvaldo Cassalanter was a person before he was a chain devil; his parents visit him
- The vault gold's moral status is deliberately ambiguous — the campaign must not telegraph a "correct" resolution
