# The Stone of Golorr and the Eyes

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** The lore entry for the Stone of Golorr, the aboleth artifact that holds the location of the Vault of Dragons, and for the three Eyes that Dagult Neverember removed to blind it.
> - **Where it is used:** The GM reads it before a character attunes to the Stone, before the party seats an Eye, and before answering any contact who asks what the Stone needs or who holds an Eye.
> - **Quests and events:** **Finding Floon** (the locket Eye is pried out of its case), **Fireball!** (Dalakhar carries the Stone, and the party can find the Eyes Letter), **Gralhund Villa** (the party takes the Stone from Yalah Gralhund or from the rooftop chase), **Faction Outposts** (Scene 1, the Stone's first impressions), **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa** and **Sea Maidens Faire** (one Eye each, and one awakening scene each), **Kolat Towers** (holds no Eye) and **Vault of Dragons** (the Full Awakening and the opening). Faction events: **The Stone's Other Master** (Harpers Mission 6), **The Full Picture** (Force Grey), and **The Water Table Stirs** and **The Dreamer's Reach** (Emerald Enclave).
> - **Location journals:** **Zhentarim Warehouse** (where the locket is opened), **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** (where the locket Eye is lifted from a strongbox), **Gralhund Villa** (the Stone in G16, the Chirada letter in G12), **Cassalanter Villa** (Eye #2 in A7), **Sea Maidens Faire** (Eye #3 in U4 of the *Scarlet Marpenoth*) and **Trollskull Manor** (the Splinter raid in Harpers Mission 6). Xanathar's lair, Kolat Towers and the Vault of Dragons have no location journal yet.
> - **Spoiler limit:** Everything above the `## Overview` is for the GM. The Overview names no Eye holder and no vault location.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM say what the Stone is, where each Eye sits, what the Stone says at each stage of restoration and what the party can and cannot learn from it, without contradicting the order-agnostic upgrade rule or the quest journals.

The Stone of Golorr is an aboleth that magic has turned into an artifact, and it holds the location of the Vault of Dragons. Dagult Neverember blinded it by removing three gemstone Eyes and putting them in three separate hands, so that a thief who stole the Stone alone would learn nothing. When the campaign opens, Xanathar holds one Eye, the Cassalanters hold one and Jarlaxle holds one.

This page uses two numbering systems. "Eye #1", "Eye #2" and "Eye #3" name the Eyes by who holds them. "First", "second" and "third Eye restored" count how many the party has seated in the Stone, in whatever order the lair heists are run.

### What the Stone Is

- **Origin:** The Stone is an aboleth transformed by magic. The published adventure says it can read the mind of anyone who attunes to it and can modify that creature's memory. The campaign adds that an attuned creature can draw out what it knows, including the vault's location.
- **Who made it:** The published adventure has a wizard erase the vault's location from Neverember's mind and trap it in the Stone, and the wizard then disappears. The campaign names no maker, and its quest opener says only that Neverember acquired the artifact "years before".
- **Form:** A small, glossy greenish-gray stone that fits in the palm of a hand (wiki, tagged to Dragon Heist Appendix A). Three oval sockets on its face hold the Eyes, and each Eye fits exactly.
- **Mind:** It has an intelligent, alien intellect and enough foresight to know the party is meant to find it. The campaign gives it a cold, precise voice and one want, which is to be whole.
- **Memory:** The Stone's influence suppresses memory in people who have been near it. Kalain cannot recall what Neverember told her about the "Melairkyn ceremonial vault", and nothing she tries brings it back.
- **Memory erasure:** The Alexandrian source describes a ritual in which the Stone removes one piece of knowledge from everyone except its attuned user. Neverember used it only on the vault's location, so the vault's existence and the keys to it can still be found. Vault of Dragons refers to this ritual as a way for the party to cover its tracks.
- **Attunement:** No ritual is needed. The Stone starts contact the first time a character holds it with intent, reads that character's memories without asking, and takes one minute. The character is left with a cold impression of something ancient paying close attention.

### The Three Eyes

- **What an Eye is:** A smoky-gray gemstone about the size of a large marble, cut from the same material as the Stone. Seated beside the Stone, an Eye looks like a piece broken from its surface and polished separately.
- **Seating one:** The Eye and the Stone only have to be in the same place. The character presses the Eye into an empty socket, a resonant tone is felt more than heard, and the Stone changes.
- **Why they were removed:** Without all three Eyes seated, the Stone reveals nothing useful about the vault. Neverember took them out to protect the secret from anyone who might steal the Stone.
- **Where Neverember put them:** He kept one with him in Neverwinter. He gave one to his son Renaer inside an ivory mourning locket made in honor of Renaer's mother, and Renaer never knew what it held. He hid one in a crypt of the Brandath Mausoleum near Alethea Brandath's tomb.
- **How a character learns the Eyes exist:** The first attunement tells the character that three Eyes were removed. The Eyes Letter in Kalain's lockbox, an unsigned note written about four years before the campaign, confirms that Neverember held all three.
- **Kolat Towers:** It holds no Eye. Manshoon holds none, and the Stone offers no impression that points to the towers.

### Where the Eyes Are

- **Eye #1, Xanathar:** Manshoon's Zhentarim stole it from the Protector's Enclave in Neverwinter. About three months before the fireball, Manshoon sent two lieutenants to Skullport with it as a gift. Xanathar ate them and kept the Eye, and Ott Steeltoes put it in the bowl of Sylgar, Xanathar's goldfish, because it was a pretty color. Xanathar does not know what the Eye is.
- **Eye #2, the Cassalanters:** It lay in a Brandath crypt until the Cassalanters found it through infernal divination and took it from Neverember's magical wards. It is secured behind the base of the statue in the ceremonial hall of the temple beneath **Cassalanter Villa** (A7). The family knows what it does.
- **Eye #3, Jarlaxle:** It began in Renaer's locket. Manshoon's cell seized the locket the night before Ches 1 and pried it open at the Candle Lane warehouse. Xanathar's Guild raided the warehouse and carried the Eye off with the rest of the back office, and nobody in the Guild recognized it. Bregan D'aerthe agents posing as guards lifted it from a strongbox in the hideout before dawn, and it reached Jarlaxle before the party reached the hideout.
- **Where Jarlaxle keeps it:** In U4 aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, in a velvet-lined compartment at the base of the crystal ball pedestal that runs his nimblewright network. Jarlaxle knows exactly what it is.
- **Manshoon:** He holds no Eye. He needs the Stone and any two Eyes, and his plan is to let someone else open the vault and then take it from them.
- **Who needs what:** Xanathar's Guild wants the Stone and does not know what the Eyes are for. The Cassalanters need the Stone and Eyes #1 and #3. Bregan D'aerthe needs the Stone and Eyes #1 and #2.

### The Stone's Path

- **Before 1489 DR:** Neverember hides the Stone in the Palace of Waterdeep after the vault's location is erased from his mind and trapped in it.
- **After the vote:** Neverember's spies take the Stone from the Palace and are killed leaving the city. It passes from hand to hand until it reaches Xanathar.
- **Ches 20:** Dalakhar steals it from Xanathar's lair on Neverember's orders.
- **Ches 22:** The fireball kills Dalakhar. The Gralhund nimblewright takes the Stone from his body and carries it to **Gralhund Villa**, where Yalah Gralhund keeps it in an inner pocket of her breastplate.
- **At Gralhund Villa:** The party takes the Stone from Yalah, or, if she surrenders it to Fel'rekt Lafeen, from the carrier in the rooftop chase. Holding it when the aftermath opens earns 1 Milestone Point and brings the party to Level 4.
- **After that:** The party holds the Stone for the rest of the campaign. A Splinter raid on **Trollskull Manor** can take it during Harpers Mission 6.

### How the Stone Wakes

The Stone's behavior depends on how many Eyes the party has restored, never on which lair heist delivered them.

- **No Eye restored:** On attunement the Stone speaks in impressions rather than words. It conveys that three Eyes were removed and gives three fragmentary visions, one for each Eye, that describe where that Eye last sat and give no holder's name.
  - **Eye #1:** A beholder's eye, unblinking in darkness, with carved stone wet from underground springs and a long way down.
  - **Eye #2:** Red light through colored glass, incense smoke rising to a vaulted ceiling, and children's laughter somewhere above the stone corridors.
  - **Eye #3:** Salt air and creaking wood, a dark-skinned elf holding a gemstone to a lantern, and harbor water against a hull.
  - **A fourth impression:** If the character asks whether there are other holders, the Stone senses something watching from a tower. The watcher is interested in the Stone and ignores the vault, and the impression ends there.
- **First Eye restored:** The Stone speaks aloud for the first time and says "Two remain." The visions for the two remaining Eyes sharpen into places: a Sea Ward villa with a temple beneath it and the Cassalanter house mark on its floor, a ship in the harbor with a bald elf at the prow, or the beholder's lair beneath Skullport. It also says "I want to be whole." It does not give the vault's location, the command phrase or the exact position of either remaining Eye.
- **Second Eye restored:** The Stone's voice deepens and it says "One remains." The last Eye's location resolves in full, and which image it uses depends on which Eye remains.
  - **If Eye #3 remains:** A ship in the harbor, the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, and a bald elf with a year of patience.
  - **If Eye #2 remains:** A villa in the Sea Ward, a temple beneath it and the Cassalanter house mark in the floor, with hollow-sounding children's laughter.
  - **If Eye #1 remains:** A place beneath Skullport, with an unblinking eye in carved stone wet from underground springs.
  - **What else it says:** It confirms that it knows where the vault is and that three Eyes will unlock the full knowledge. It adds "I am remembering what I was." It still withholds the vault's location, the command phrase and the nature of the guardian.
- **Third Eye restored, the Full Awakening:** The Stone goes still and then speaks with sudden clarity. It sends a rush of images (tunnels, corpses, dwarven halls, a gold dragon aging fast, a silver hammer, a shaft of sunlight) and a chiseled inscription that reads BRANDATH CRYPTS. It also gives a riddle. The vault's location, the shape of the opening ceremony and the guardian's nature all resolve. The scene is written to play the same way whichever lair delivers the third Eye.
- **Where the full text sits:** The canonical text of the Full Awakening is in the **Vault of Dragons** structure notes. **Xanathar's Lair** and **Cassalanter Villa** both tell the GM to run that scene when they deliver the third Eye.

### The Stone's Voice

- **How it sounds:** Old and precise, like water that has been underground for a long time. It does not greet anyone. It says what it means and stops.
- **What it wants:** To be whole. It cooperates with the party only while their interests overlap with its own, and it stops helping when they part.
- **What it never does:** It does not explain itself, and it never explains Illuun.
- **Where the aboleth shows:** With the second Eye the Stone starts to speak of deep water, dreaming minds and patience on a geological scale. With the third Eye the aboleth mind comes fully forward and the Stone stops behaving like an object.
- **At the vault:** After the Full Awakening the Stone has nothing left to reveal about the vault. It offers a few brief, unsettling asides at moments such as the vault doors opening, and they never compete with Aurinax for the scene.

### The Stone and Illuun

- **Who Illuun is:** An aboleth dreaming on Undermountain Level 4. The Stone holds an aboleth's memory, and Illuun answers it.
- **What the party feels:** On the first attunement the attuned character feels a cold patience beneath the city, present in the stone itself and not in any one chamber. When the character asks about it, the Stone changes the subject. With the first Eye restored the character feels something vaster than a stone, and each later Eye makes the feeling stronger.
- **What the Full Awakening says:** The Stone is Illuun's instrument, and Illuun has watched the attuned holder since **Faction Outposts**.
- **Who else senses it:** Jeryth Phaulkon of the Emerald Enclave has felt a dreaming presence below the Castle Ward for months and does not know its name. The Stone woke it, and **The Water Table Stirs** and **The Dreamer's Reach** carry the Enclave's side. Hlam felt an abolethic resonance for tendays in Force Grey's first mission, and a Harper seer felt something answer the Stone from below the city.
- **Who learns the name:** An Enclave member learns it when **Anchor Destroyed** is marked, or from the Stone at the Full Awakening. Otherwise nobody outside the GM knows it.
- **Where it leads:** After the vault, a final impression of something ancient and fully aware of being carried can seed the fourth level of Undermountain, if the Emerald Enclave's Mission 6 thread was paid off.

### Opening the Vault

- **What the vault needs:** The complete Stone, with all three Eyes restored, and the command phrase the Stone reveals. The ceremony itself is covered on **The Vault of Dragons and Neverember's Embezzlement** and in **Vault of Dragons**.
- **What the published adventure does:** It has attunement reveal the vault's location and the three keys at once. The campaign replaces that with the Eye count above, so an attuned Stone with fewer than three Eyes reveals nothing useful.
- **What the party can learn early:** **Vault Opening Method Known** records that the party has the Melairkyn notes (dragon scale, mithral hammer and sunlight). **Eyes Letter Found** records the unsigned letter that confirms the three Eyes. Manshoon's Report on the Grand Game in **Kolat Towers** (E9) carries a partly decoded command phrase, and finishing it needs the Full Awakening or more research.
- **If the party holds fewer than three Eyes:** **Vault of Dragons** cannot be run yet. The lair heists can be run in any order, and the Stone's impressions and the faction debriefs point to whichever Eye remains.

### Who Wants It

- **Xanathar's Guild:** Xanathar wants the Stone back and the vault kept hidden. It knows the Stone is the key to the vault, but it does not know what the Eye in Sylgar's bowl does, that there are three Eyes, or where the vault is.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** Manshoon wants the Stone for the ancient Weave knowledge in its aboleth memory. He means to use it to absorb the Weave and supplant **Mystra**, and the vault's gold pays for the preparations.
- **The Cassalanters:** They hold Eye #2 and need the Stone and the other two.
- **Bregan D'aerthe:** Jarlaxle has known of the Stone, the Eyes and the vault since he reached Waterdeep, and he holds Eye #3.
- **The Harpers:** They want the Stone in their custody for study, because they believe it holds intelligence of strategic importance.
- **The Lords' Alliance and Laeral:** Laeral believes the vault is in Neverwinter and does not know the Stone has resurfaced.
- **Player-faction contacts:** At their first meetings, the Harpers, Emerald Enclave, Force Grey, Lords' Alliance and Order of the Gauntlet contacts all refuse to discuss the Stone or the vault.

### What Marks the Stone's State

- **No lair journal yet:** **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa** and **Sea Maidens Faire** have no quest journal, so no Event Outcome marks an Eye restored. The GM counts Eyes restored from the "Eyes recovered" row of the **Vault of Dragons** faction-state table, which should read 3 of 3.
- **Jarlaxle Informed:** Set in **Fireball!** when the party speaks directly with Jarlaxle, as Zord or revealed. He already knows of the Stone, the Eyes and the vault, so it tells him nothing new. If it is true, he sends Bregan D'aerthe agents to **Gralhund Villa** before the party arrives.
- **Eyes Letter Found:** Set in **Fireball!** when the party has the unsigned letter that confirms Neverember held all three Eyes four years ago. **Vault of Dragons** reads it.
- **Vault Opening Method Known:** Set in **Fireball!** when the party has the Melairkyn notes. **Vault of Dragons** reads it in the ceremonial opening.
- **G12 Searched: Chirada Letter Found:** Set in **Gralhund Villa**. It pays off in **Faction Outposts**.
- **Stone Secured:** Recorded at the end of both Gralhund confrontation events. True sends the party to the aftermath, and false sends it to the rooftop chase.
- **Stone Recovered:** Recorded at the end of the rooftop chase. True fires the Stone milestone at the aftermath.
- **Stone Study Completed, Splinter Sending Stone Recovered and Stone Taken by Splinter:** Marked in **The Stone's Other Master**. **Vault of Dragons** reads all three.
- **Anchor Destroyed:** Marked in Emerald Enclave Mission 6. It decides whether an Enclave member learns Illuun's name.

## Overview

The Stone of Golorr is an artifact that an aboleth is said to have forged. Rumor holds that someone stole it some years ago, and ordinary Waterdhavians cannot say where it is now.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Answering a player who asks what the Stone of Golorr is:** Give the Overview. The party's own evidence, from the Stone itself and from faction debriefs, is the only thing that adds to it.
> - **Answering a player who asks what an Eye does:** If the party has not attuned to the Stone, no contact can say. After the first attunement, the Stone says that three Eyes were removed and that all three must be seated.
> - **Counting Eyes:** Count the Eyes restored and ignore the order of the heists. When two are restored, read the sub-case for the Eye that remains. When three are restored, run the Full Awakening in whichever quest delivered the third.
> - **A party that holds the Stone with no Eyes:** It can attune and receive the impressions above. It cannot learn the vault's location, the ceremony or the guardian's name from the Stone yet.
> - **Never reveal:** Before the party's own evidence supports it, say nothing about who holds each Eye, that Illuun exists, or that the Stone is its instrument.
> - **Neverember's knowledge:** The vault's location was erased from his mind, so he cannot find the vault himself. He still wants the gold or, failing that, vindication.

**Tone:** Cold and plain. The Stone is an old mind and a tool at once, so describe what it says and does without comment, and let the party decide how it feels about carrying it.
