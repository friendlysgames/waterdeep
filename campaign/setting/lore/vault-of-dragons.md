# The Vault of Dragons and Neverember's Embezzlement

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** The lore entry for the gold Dagult Neverember embezzled from Waterdeep, the dwarf-built vault where he hid it, the doors that seal it and the dragon who guards it.
> - **Where it is used:** The GM reads it before the party reaches the City of the Dead, before voicing Aurinax, Laeral Silverhand or Neverember on the gold, and whenever a player asks where the money came from or how the doors open.
> - **Quests and events:** **Vault of Dragons** (still a structure doc) holds the approach in Scene 2, the opening in Scene 3, the vault and Aurinax in Scene 4, the faction confrontation in Scene 5 and the gold in Scene 6. **Fireball!** supplies the rumor in **The Fireball** and the vault-opening method in **Kalain's Tower**. **Faction Outposts**, **Cassalanter Villa** and **Kolat Towers** each hand the party a route to the Brandath Crypts. **An Audience with the Open Lord** issues the Writ that Aurinax accepts, **Lioncrown** gives Laeral's account of the gold, and **Ten Nights in the City of the Dead**, **The Theater's Back Room** and **Dread Lord** mark outcomes that the Vault reads.
> - **Location journals:** None. The vault is keyed in the **Vault of Dragons** structure doc, and the **City of the Dead** page covers the grounds above it.
> - **Spoiler limit:** Everything above the `## Overview` is for the GM. The Overview does not name the vault, the Stone, the Eyes or the guardian.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM say how much Neverember took, when he lost office, what the vault is, how its doors open and who guards it, and keeps the two Brandath names apart without inventing a date that no source gives.

The Vault of Dragons is a dwarf-built ceremonial vault beneath the Brandath Mausoleum in the City of the Dead. Dagult Neverember filled it with the half a million gold dragons he embezzled from Waterdeep, and the gold dragon Aurinax still guards it for him.

This page covers the theft, the vault, its doors and its guardian. Every faction that hunts the Stone of Golorr is hunting this gold, so the embezzlement is the first cause of the Grand Game. The Stone and its three Eyes have their own page, **The Stone of Golorr and the Eyes**, and this page points to it.

### The Embezzlement

- **What he took:** Dragon Heist says Neverember embezzled "half a million dragons", and a dragon is a gold piece in Waterdeep. The hoard is 500,000 gp and weighs 10,000 pounds.
- **How he held it:** In the campaign, Laeral says Waterdeep's merchants paid the money as taxes and Neverember set it aside by decree. She adds that he had a lock made that no Masked Lord could open and hid the key. She tells this to a Lords' Alliance member in **Lioncrown**, and **A City Watching Itself** repeats her account. No other page describes the method.
- **When it began:** No source dates it. The Alexandrian timeline lists it after Neverember proclaimed himself ruler of Neverwinter in 1467 DR and before he found the vault, and it gives no year for either step.
- **When he lost office:** The Masked Lords voted him out in 1489 DR, about three years before the campaign opens on Ches 1, 1492 DR. The Alexandrian timeline gives the year, and Dragon Heist says only "a few years ago". The structure doc for **Faction Outposts** says "eleven years ago", and that figure is wrong (see Draft Notes).
- **How the city learned of it:** Dragon Heist names no finder. The Alexandrian says Laeral's auditors found the embezzlement after she took office and that the news leaked. The campaign pages do not say how the city heard, so the Overview gives a rumor and no figure.
- **What it cost him:** A wizard erased the vault's location from Neverember's mind and from the minds of his subordinates, so he cannot find his own gold. He wants it back or, failing that, vindication.

### The Vault

- **Where it lies:** Beneath the Brandath family mausoleum in the City of the Dead. Dragon Heist offers three other sites for the vault, depending on the villain. They are a theater called the Pink Flumph in the Castle Ward, and an old windmill and an old tower in the Sea Ward. The campaign uses the mausoleum only.
- **Who built it:** Dragon Heist says only that dwarves built it long ago. The campaign follows the Alexandrian and names the Melairkyn dwarves, whose cult of Dumathoin sealed offerings of bound secrets inside ceremonial vaults like this one. Its architect was Harlsnod Darkshine, who left a small "HN" cartouche in most of its rooms.
- **How it was lost:** After dark elves broke the Melairkyn, the vault was looted and buried, and the ground above it later became the City of the Dead. The Brandaths built a mausoleum on the site, and an expansion a century later broke into the antechambers. The Brothers of the Maroon Pin, a secret fraternal order, used them for rituals until a scandal in the fourteenth century ended the order.
- **How Neverember found it:** Dragon Heist says Neverember married into the old Brandath family for its wealth, and Lady Alethea Brandath bore his son Renaer. Alethea asked for a simple tomb among her ancestors. While arranging her burial, Neverember took over the oldest family mausoleum, moved the remains of Lord Anri to a new crypt nearby and buried Alethea in his place. The work uncovered the passage the Brandaths had forgotten and the sealed doors beyond it.
- **What he added:** A two-foot steel door at the end of the Old Crypts, hidden by an illusion, with a DC 22 lock and an *alarm* spell keyed to Neverember alone. The Stone's memory-erasure came later and is covered on **The Stone of Golorr and the Eyes**.
- **Wards:** Dragon Heist says the vault is warded against all forms of magical detection and intrusion. Any spell that tries to contact a creature inside or to scry on the vault fails, and teleporting in from outside is impossible.
- **Inside:** The keyed rooms belong to Scene 4 of **Vault of Dragons**. The campaign's Hall of Moradin, with its black pudding, is described on the **Moradin** page.

### The Mausoleum and the Crypts

- **The Brandath Mausoleum:** This is the building, the sealed Brandath family tomb with BRANDATH etched above the entrance and a treant by the door. The vault lies beneath it. Dragon Heist keys only this building and calls it "the Brandath family mausoleum".
- **The Brandath Crypts:** This is the approach. The name covers the complex of mausoleums, Lady Alethea's Tomb and the Old Crypts beneath them, down to the vault door. Scene 2 of **Vault of Dragons** carries the title, and the Stone's vision spells the name in dwarven letters.
- **How to write them:** The vault lies beneath the Mausoleum. The party walks the Crypts to reach it, and the Eye that Neverember hid near his wife's tomb was hidden in the Crypts.
- **Where pages blur the names:** Several pages put the vault "beneath the Brandath Crypts", write the singular "Brandath Crypt", or use "mausoleum" in lower case for the approach. Draft Notes lists them, and none is edited here.

### The Approach

- **The grounds:** The gates, the treant and Sir Ambrose Everdawn's night patrol are on the **City of the Dead** page, along with the five routes by which a party learns the way.
- **The critical path:** Lady Alethea's Tomb is the original Brandath mausoleum, where Lord Anri lay for centuries. A successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check finds that the south wall is false and hides a staircase down to the Old Crypts.
- **The Old Crypts:** The top step is scorched by a triggered *glyph of warding*, which a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds. A second check at DC 14 finds a disarmed second glyph and an empty compartment behind a torn-loose sconce, which is where the Cassalanters' team took the Eye. The collapsed east end is an illusion over the steel door, and beyond it a ten-foot passage slopes down to the antechamber.

### Opening the Doors

- **The antechamber:** A room twenty feet wide and twenty feet high, with a pair of adamantine doors on the far wall. Dwarvish runes taller than a grown man read "ALL THAT LIES WITHIN BELONGS TO THE SILENT KEEPER". A bronze bas-relief of a stylized sun, six feet across, is set in the floor and ringed by a Chondathan inscription, "Know ye the hidden truth", repeated three times.
- **The ceremony:** A single dragon scale is laid on the bronze sun and struck with a mithral hammer while lit by direct sunlight. The doors slide into the walls and stay open for 15 minutes before they slide shut. From inside, a hand placed on the doors is enough.
- **What does not work:** The doors cannot be forced or damaged, and no magic short of a *wish* gets around them.
- **The scale:** Any dragon scale will do. A specialty shop sells one for roughly 900 gp, and the party may also take one from Zelifarn's collection, Jarlaxle's private quarters or the Cassalanters' study.
- **The sunlight:** The *daylight* spell suffices, and the structure doc rules that its light counts as sunlight under the 2024 rules. Manshoon's sunglobe also works if Manshoon was Destroyed and the party recovered the item at **Kolat Towers**. Otherwise twenty large steel mirrors (400 gp in all) and several hours of alignment will bring sunlight down.
- **The hammer:** It need not be large. A local smith makes one for 150 gp.
- **Learning the ceremony:** Four independent paths lead to it. The Stone's vision during the Full Awakening gives its shape, in the lines "Three keys ye seek. Strike scale of wyrm in light of day. As the vault bears witness, so shall it ope to mithral's strike." Kalain's notes in **Kalain's Tower** give it in full, in Neverember's hand. The Cassalanters know it from their own investigation and share it if the party's standing with them allows. Manshoon's Report on the Grand Game from **Kolat Towers** (area E9) gives an outline and a partial Elvish command phrase.
- **The window:** The 15-minute window matters in Scene 5. A faction that times its arrival to the ceremony rather than to the party's exit can catch the party holding the doors.

### The Guardian

- **Who he is:** Aurinax is an adult gold dragon who lives in the vault as a stocky dwarf named Barok Clanghammer. Dragon Heist calls him male, and he stays in humanoid form until a fight starts because he cannot easily carry the dragonstaff of Ahghairon in dragon form. The staff and its hold on the dragonward are on **The Dragonward and Walking Statues**.
- **The bargain:** Neverember let Aurinax keep the dragonstaff in exchange for guarding the vault until Neverember or his appointed vassals came to remove the gold. Dragon Heist places the deal when Neverember became Open Lord, and the campaign places it after he found the vault. No vassal was ever named.
- **What the oath covers:** Only Neverember and his appointed vassals may remove the gold under Aurinax's watch, and none of them is in Waterdeep. He attacks only to defend himself, the staff or the gold.
- **His doubt:** Aurinax is unsure what an oath to a former Open Lord is worth now that the office is gone. He wants to keep his word, to keep the staff as his agreed payment and to see the gold legitimately transferred before he calls his duty discharged.
- **How a party gets past him:** Scene 4 of **Vault of Dragons** gives four resolutions and picks none in advance.
  - **Oath-Release:** Aurinax accepts real authority. That means **Renaer Neverember** presented formally, the Masked Lord's Writ that **An Audience with the Open Lord** issues (the **Writ Issued** outcome), or a release phrase if the Stone or later research produced one. **Hlam**'s presence grants advantage on the Persuasion check that seals it.
  - **Negotiation Without Release:** A DC 18 Charisma (Persuasion) check wins a partial concession. The check has advantage if the party brings a prominent city official or a worshiper of **Bahamut**.
  - **Legal Argument:** Neverember's ouster is public record, and a party that argues his binding oath has lapsed gives Aurinax grounds to agree.
  - **Combat:** Aurinax does not want it and will not start it. He does not chase a retreating party, intimidation against him fails automatically, and his Legendary Resistance defeats charm.
- **His stat block:** The Bestiary is not drafted, so the structure doc says to run him as a boss encounter when it is written.

### The Hoard

- **The treasure:** The vault has two alcoves in play. One holds the full 500,000 gp, and the other holds sixty-five gemstones worth 100 gp each. The gems are the last of Aurinax's food supply and his payment, and he does not give them up willingly.
- **The sapphire:** A stone replica of Mount Waterdeep sits at the heart of the hoard with a 25,000 gp sapphire set into it. The sapphire was never part of the embezzlement, and the city has no claim to it.
- **The logistics:** Scene 6 of **Vault of Dragons** gives five ways to deal with 10,000 pounds of gold.
  - **Cassalanter carts:** If the Cassalanters are Cooperative, a dozen carts of empty barrels and trusted servants arrive within the hour, and the party owes them for it.
  - **Keep it:** The party arranges its own transport and storage, and any response team still active complicates the extraction.
  - **Return it:** If Laeral learns the party holds the gold, she asks once, politely, for its return in exchange for a 10 percent finder's fee of 50,000 gp.
  - **Leave it in place:** The party takes what it needs as it needs it and tells no one.
  - **Use its existence:** The party trades knowledge of the hoard's location for favors and never moves a coin.
- **Why it stays a choice:** The campaign treats the gold as a real decision, so do not steer the party toward returning it. Keep the consequences of keeping it dramatic rather than punitive.

### Who Knows What at Campaign Start

- **Waterdhavians:** A rumor and nothing more, as the Overview gives it.
- **Laeral Silverhand:** She knows of the embezzlement and believes the vault is in Neverwinter. **Fentree's Ledger** corrects her.
- **Dagult Neverember:** He wants the gold back, cannot find it and is in Neverwinter when the campaign opens.
- **Renaer Neverember:** Renaer (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) refused to help his father hide the gold. He knows nothing about the vault, and his ignorance is real.
- **Aurinax:** Nobody left in Waterdeep knows he exists or where the dragonstaff is.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** It knows Dalakhar was moving the Stone, and it does not know what the Eyes are or where the vault is.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** They know the Stone exists and unlocks great wealth.
- **Bregan D'aerthe:** Jarlaxle has known of the Stone, the Eyes and the vault since he arrived in Waterdeep.
- **The Cassalanters:** They hold Eye #2 and know it belongs to the Stone. They do not yet know the Stone has surfaced, and this page treats them as not knowing where the vault lies (see Draft Notes).

### Where the Campaign Departs from Dragon Heist

- **The site:** Dragon Heist gives four possible sites and lets the villain decide. The campaign uses the Brandath Mausoleum only.
- **The opening:** Dragon Heist has the GM choose three keys or roll them on the Vault Keys table. Its door reads "THE THREE KEYS. BRING THEM FORTH.", opens when the keys come within 5 feet and shuts on the command word "Azaam". The campaign uses the scale, sunlight and mithral hammer ceremony, the "SILENT KEEPER" runes and the 15-minute window.
- **The guardian:** Dragon Heist offers a DC 18 Persuasion check or a fight. The campaign gives four paths. The Alexandrian suggests replacing Aurinax with a red dragon, and the campaign keeps the gold dragon.
- **The gold:** Dragon Heist treats returning the gold to the city as the heroic ending. The campaign treats it as a choice.

## Overview

Dagult Neverember was Open Lord of Waterdeep for some twenty years, until the Masked Lords voted him out in 1489 DR. He now governs Neverwinter, and Laeral Silverhand holds the seat he left. Waterdhavians whisper that a large sum of the city's gold went missing during his years, though nobody names a figure.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **The Cassalanter link (campaign-only):** **Cassalanter Villa** says Neverember's embezzlement gutted the Cassalanter banking operations, because the family had made loans against civic funds that no longer existed, and Victoro's investments then collapsed in sequence. That ruin is what drove the family to the bargain described on **A Debt in Blood**. No published or Alexandrian text read for this page makes the link. Dragon Heist gives the near bankruptcy and no cause.
> - **Why the family wants the gold:** The contract the Cassalanters signed asks for 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, both. The family holds about 500,000 gp, and the vault's 500,000 gp covers the shortfall. Nobody outside the household and the cult knows this except Jarlaxle, who learned it through his spy. No contact explains why the family wants the gold, and everyone else only suspects the Cassalanters of something.
> - **Timing of the link:** The embezzlement ran during Neverember's tenure and the pact dates to about 1489 DR, the year of the ouster. No source says when the money went missing in relation to the pact, so do not give the embezzlement a start year at the table.
> - **Answering a player who asks where the money went:** Say that rumor puts a hoard of gold dragons somewhere in the city and that the Stone is the key to it. Dalakhar's corpse says the same in **The Fireball** if the party casts *speak with dead*.
> - **Answering a player who asks whether the Open Lord can simply take it:** Laeral believes the vault is in Neverwinter, so she cannot. If she learns the party holds the gold, she asks once for its return at a 10 percent finder's fee, and she does not ask twice.
> - **When the party can learn the vault's place:** The Stone names the Brandath Crypts when it is fully awakened with all three Eyes. Kalain's papers give the opening method sooner, and they do not give the place. The Full Awakening text is canonical in the **Vault of Dragons** structure doc and is not restaged there.
> - **Neverember at the end:** He arrives at the City of the Dead gate with hired muscle and Watch loyalists after the party has left the vault. The Scene 5 duel with Renaer is optional and player-driven.

**Tone:** Grave and fair to Neverember, as on **The Open Lord's Fall**. Treat the vault as a shrine to keeping secrets that happens to hold a fortune, and let the gold come last.
