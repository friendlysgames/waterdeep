# The Open Lord and the Masked Lords

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the institution entry for Waterdeep's ruling council, and the first of the four governance pages. It covers how the Open Lord and the Masked Lords share power, where petitioners meet them, who is known to sit among them, and what the campaign has added to the published adventure.
> - **Where it is used:** read it before you voice Laeral Silverhand, before you answer a player who asks who the Masked Lords are, and before you run any rank event or mission in which a party member asks a Masked Lord for a favor.
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley** (Ulkoria Stonemarrow in **The Neighbors**), **Gralhund Villa** (Yalah Gralhund's bid for a seat), **Cassalanter Villa** (Caladorn Cassalanter's portrait), **Kolat Towers** (the list of Masked Lords in Manshoon's drawer), and **Vault of Dragons** (Laeral's one legal intervention and the Masked Lord's Writ).
> - **Faction events:** **Harpers First Meeting**, **A Friend's House**, **High Harper**, **An Audience with the Open Lord**, **Escalation to the Open Lord**, **Warduke**, **Lioncrown** and **Commander**.
> - **Location journals:** none. The Palace is described on **Castle Ward**.
> - **Spoiler limit:** everything above the `## Overview` is for the GM only. The Overview is what an ordinary Waterdhavian knows, and it names no Masked Lord.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM say how Waterdeep is governed and who may be named, and keeps the campaign's additions apart from the published adventure and the wiki so that no later page invents a seat, a number or a power that no source gives.

The Open Lord and the Masked Lords are the ruling council of Waterdeep, made up of one lord who sits in public and a body of other lords whose names are kept secret. Ahghairon founded the arrangement in 1032 DR, after he turned the warlord Raurlor's own sword into a serpent that killed him. Ahghairon then seated himself on the warlord's throne in the audience chamber, in front of the leaders of the city's armies and powerful families. From that seat he decreed that he would sit openly as lord while a council of other lords of nearly equal power ruled with him. The Open Lord still sits on that throne. Laeral Silverhand has held the office since the other Lords voted Dagult Neverember out a few years ago.

### Tenets

- **Open Rule Beside Hidden Rule:** The Open Lord is the only member of the government whom the public can name. The Masked Lords rule beside the Open Lord with nearly equal power. The wiki adds that a Masked Lord normally appeared in public only when wearing a mask (wiki; City of Splendors boxed set, 2e).
- **Anonymity Protects the Lords:** Ahghairon hid the identity of every Masked Lord, even from the other Lords, so that none of them could be approached and influenced by bribe or threat. The wiki adds a second reason, which is that the Lords could then be drawn from any social class in the city (wiki; City of Splendors boxed set, 2e). The published adventure does not say so.
- **Justice for a Just Cause:** The published adventure calls Waterdeep a city of laws molded by Tyr's spirit of justice. It tells a petitioner with a just cause that justice will be done by the Masked Lords or the Open Lord, and it warns that a wrongdoer will be weighed on Tyr's scales.
- **A Masked Lord Overrules a Magister:** Any Masked Lord can overturn a magister's ruling. One is rarely present when a petitioner needs it, so most rulings stand. The magisters and the Code Legal are covered on **The Code Legal and the Magisters**.
- **The Open Lord's Reserved Powers (wiki):** The wiki reserves three powers to the Open Lord, which are vetting and choosing new Lords, naming the next Open Lord and fixing how many Lords sit (wiki; City of Splendors: Waterdeep, 3e, and City of Splendors boxed set, 2e). No published adventure line and no campaign file states them, so the campaign treats them as a ruling and not as established fact. `[research]`
- **The Dragonward Is Not the Open Lord's to Grant:** Dragons are physically unable to enter Waterdeep or its sewers unless the dragonstaff of Ahghairon has touched them. The Open Lord's permission plays no part. See **The Dragonward and Walking Statues**.

### Operations

- **Where they meet petitioners:** The Open Lord governs from the Palace of Waterdeep, which Waterdhavians also call Piergeiron's Palace. The published adventure says Castle Waterdeep is a redoubt of last defense and not the seat of government. A petitioner who is invited, and not compelled, to meet the Masked Lords or the Open Lord does so in the Palace audience chamber, where Ahghairon's ancient and humble throne stands. No source gives the Masked Lords a seat or an office of their own in the Castle Ward.
- **Who answers to the Open Lord:** The Commander of the Watch reports to the Open Lord. So does Elminster, the current Warden of Waterdeep. When a problem is beyond diplomats and the city's other armed forces, the Open Lord can mobilize **Force Grey**, which is usually a last resort. Only the Open Lord can strip **Vajra Safahr** of her title as Blackstaff.
- **How the office changes hands:** The only recorded removal is Dagult Neverember, whom the other Lords voted out while he was in Neverwinter. Laeral stepped into the vacancy reluctantly, at the request of the Masked Lords. The published adventure gives no procedure for choosing or removing a Lord.
- **How they appear in public (wiki):** The wiki says the Lords appear only when sitting in the Lords' Court, and every four years at the Shieldmeet Council, where the Lords' Rule is reaffirmed (wiki; City of Splendors boxed set, 2e). No campaign file uses either event.
- **How many sit (wiki):** The wiki gives a full council of twenty, which may vary from sixteen to twenty-nine, and says some Lords were kept secret (wiki; Death Masks). The published adventure gives no number. It says that several Masked Lords were assassinated in quick succession some years ago.
- **Official dress:** The published adventure gives Mirt a Lord's ensemble, which he wears only when meeting other Masked Lords in an official capacity. The adventure points to its Appendix A for the item.

### Key Members

**Laeral Silverhand** (Chaotic Good, Moon Elf, she/her) is the Open Lord. She is the fifth of the Seven Sisters, the daughters of Mystra, and was born in 765 DR. She uses her magic sparingly, relies on trusted advisors and deputies, and likes to leave the Palace in disguise to visit old friends and check on enemies. The published adventure gives her no species, and the moon elf reading is the campaign's own (campaign-only).

**Mirt** (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) is a moneylender known as the Old Wolf, and Laeral's closest advisor. The published adventure names him as one of the Masked Lords and says he is the least concerned of them with hiding his identity. The campaign keeps his office from the party until the High Harper rank (see GM Notes). His mansion stands in the Castle Ward, and **Castle Ward** describes it.

**Jelenn Urmbrusk** (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) is a proud noblewoman in her fifties who travels with sixteen guards. The published adventure names her a Masked Lord and has her ask the party for an interest-free loan of 10,000 gp, in return for her considerable influence with the Lords of Waterdeep.

**Durnan** (Neutral, Human, he/him) is the proprietor of the Yawning Portal, and in the campaign he is also a secret Masked Lord and the secret founder of the Red Sashes (campaign-only). Its fighters pose as regulars at the Portal and are magically sworn to his service. The published adventure names him only as the Portal's proprietor.

**Ulkoria Stonemarrow** (Neutral Good, Shield Dwarf, she/her) is a senior member of the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors and the former owner of the tavern in Trollskull Alley. The campaign makes her a Masked Lord, which is never confirmed in her presence (campaign-only).

**Dagult Neverember** (Lawful Neutral, Illuskan Human, he/him) was Open Lord until the other Lords voted him out, and he is now Lord of Neverwinter. His history belongs to **The Open Lord's Fall**.

**Caladorn Cassalanter** (Lawful Good, Ghost, he/him) was a Masked Lord and a hero of the North, and he is Victoro Cassalanter's father. His portrait hangs in the entrance hall of **Cassalanter Villa**, and a DC 14 Intelligence (History) check on it identifies him as a former Masked Lord.

Several more names have no Notable Figures page, so they stay as plain text. The published adventure names Corylus Thann, a lawful evil Tethyrian human noble of the Thann family of vintners, as a Masked Lord. It lists Thardouk Starbuckler and Dorgar Adarbrent as Masked Lords on a list in Manshoon's handwriting and gives them no other detail. The wiki names the earlier Open Lords as Ahghairon, Baeron Silmaeril, Lhestyn the Masked Lady and Piergeiron the Paladinson (wiki only), and the Palace still carries Piergeiron's name.

## Overview

Waterdeep is governed by the Open Lord, who rules in public, and by a council of secret Masked Lords. Ahghairon founded the system in 1032 DR and kept the Masked Lords anonymous so that nobody could bribe or threaten them. Laeral Silverhand is the Open Lord today, and invited petitioners meet the Lords in the Palace audience chamber. Any Masked Lord can overturn a magister's ruling, though one is rarely at hand when a petitioner needs it.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Campaign-only facts:** Durnan as a secret Masked Lord and the founder of the Red Sashes, Ulkoria as a Masked Lord and Laeral as a moon elf come from the campaign, and the published adventure names only Mirt among the Lords. Durnan does not surface in **Finding Floon** and appears in no player-facing text. Ulkoria's office is stated in **The Neighbors**, and the party does not learn it in that quest.
> - **Mirt's reveal:** The published adventure says Mirt is the least concerned of the Lords with hiding his identity. The campaign overrides that on purpose. He mentions neither office at the **Harpers First Meeting**, and at the **High Harper** rank he tells the member that he is a Masked Lord and can be asked to act in that capacity once. Laeral knows it.
> - **Hidden from each other:** The published adventure says the Lords' identities are hidden even from each other, and it wins over the wiki, which says they were known to each other. Laeral tells a Lioncrown "I know them, and I will not name one", and the campaign reads this as one of the Open Lord's reserved powers, which is a ruling for the user to confirm. `[research]`
> - **What anonymity does not protect:** The masks stop a stranger's bribe and do nothing against blackmail by someone who has learned a name. Manshoon keeps a list of five Masked Lords whose identities he knows. They are Mirt, Corylus Thann, Thardouk Starbuckler, Jelenn Urmbrusk and Dorgar Adarbrent, and he has checked off Corylus and Jelenn as the two he is blackmailing.
> - **Who holds which Lord:** Manshoon's cell blackmails Jelenn Urmbrusk and Corylus Thann and forces them to cover up crimes by Manshoon's forces. Jelenn's debt to Manshoon's Zhentarim was borrowed to avoid borrowing from rival nobles, and the Splinter now holds it. Corylus uses crooked Watch veterans against anyone who annoys him and will not accept that he owes the party anything. See **Manshoon's Zhentarim** and **Jelenn Urmbrusk**.
> - **Seats that may open:** Several Masked Lords were assassinated in quick succession some years ago. Yalah Gralhund expected a vacancy, was passed over, and wants a seat. House Gralhund joined Manshoon's Zhentarim partly to learn the Lords' identities and weaknesses. In the published adventure's Xanathar version, Yalah offers to return the Stone to it if it helps her create a vacancy. The Alexandrian files date the killings to 1491 DR and call them the Lord Murders.
> - **Faction ties of the other Lords:** Mirt is a senior Harper, and Laeral is the Lords' Alliance's highest authority in the city. Ulkoria belongs to the Watchful Order. Caladorn was a Cassalanter. The campaign gives no faction to Thardouk Starbuckler or Dorgar Adarbrent.
> - **What a party can ask of a Lord:** At **Warduke** a member sends one written request per quest to a Masked Lord whom Jalester Silvermane will not name. The Lord is never named, and the Lord is neither Mirt nor Jelenn. At **Commander** a member of Bregan D'aerthe can ask for a private audience with a Masked Lord. The four Lords the company can reach are Mirt, Durnan, Ulkoria and Jelenn, and Zardoz never confirms any of them aloud.
> - **How Laeral answers questions about the Lords:** She will not name a Masked Lord to anyone and says "That is not mine to give." In **An Audience with the Open Lord** she signs the Masked Lord's Writ for the vault district and discloses the Splinter's offer of an account of every Masked Lord it has confirmed, for a fifth of the vault's contents. She asks the party's opinion and has not decided.
> - **At Trollskull Manor:** From Fame 81 a Masked Lord may stop by for a drink and a generous tip. At Fame 351 a Masked Lord has a reserved table.
> - **Never reveal:** The name of any Masked Lord before a rank event or a quest scene gives it, and above all not Durnan. A player who asks who the Lords are gets the Overview, and the party's own evidence adds the rest.

**Tone:** Grave and plain. Present the system as Ahghairon designed it, and give each campaign addition and each wiki claim its tag so that the GM can see which statements are published, which are campaign choices and which are borrowed.
