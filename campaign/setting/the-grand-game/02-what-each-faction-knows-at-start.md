> - **What this page is:** The starting-knowledge page of the Grand Game journal. One table gives, for each of the four villain factions, what it knows, what it holds, what it needs and what it is doing when the campaign opens. A short note covers the two powers that play at the edges, and one section settles who Dalakhar worked for.

> - **Spoiler limit:** Everything above the Overview is for the Gamemaster only. The Overview names no Eye, no Stone and no pact.

**Purpose:** Lets the Gamemaster answer what a faction knows, holds and is doing at the first session, so that no faction learns something early because the answer had to be improvised.

## Reading the Table

- **The Stone is in transit at the start.** Dalakhar carries it toward the party, and the Gralhunds' nimblewright takes it from his body on Ches 22. The party acquires it during **Gralhund Villa**.
- **Two factions know what an Eye does.** The Cassalanters know the function of Eye #2, and Jarlaxle knows exactly what Eye #3 is. Xanathar holds Eye #1 in the bowl of its goldfish Sylgar and has no idea what it is.
- **Each faction is missing one piece.** Xanathar's Guild lacks the Eyes' meaning, Manshoon's Zhentarim lack the Gralhund link, the Cassalanters lack news that the Stone is in play, and only Bregan D'aerthe has the whole picture.

## Others at the Table

Two more powers play at the edges, and neither is a villain faction, so neither gets a row.

- **The Doom Raiders:** Davil Starsong's cell is aware of the Grand Game at a high level `[research]` and is not a primary competitor for the vault. Its defining preoccupation is the war with Manshoon's splinter cell, which peeled off part of the Black Network and now works to dismantle Davil's people. Davil sees in the half-million dragons a chance at a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council. The cell holds nothing, and it is maintaining its territory and watching.
- **The Gralhunds:** House Gralhund bankrolls Manshoon's cell. Orond Gralhund says Yalah Gralhund arranged the Zhentarim seizure of Renaer Neverember to gain leverage over Dalakhar. Their agents identified Dalakhar as an agent of Dagult Neverember before the fireball `[research]`, and the house keeps its own agent, a runaway nimblewright, to shadow the Zhents and take the Stone from Dalakhar.

## Who Dalakhar Worked For

Dalakhar, a rock gnome spy, worked for Dagult Neverember. After Neverember used magic to learn where the Stone lay, he sent a succession of spies into Xanathar's lair, and Dalakhar succeeded where the others had failed. Dalakhar had also watched Neverember's son Renaer for months on his master's behalf, and no source has Renaer employing him.

On the morning of Ches 22 Dalakhar is on his way to the party with the Stone, meaning to entrust it to them briefly. Renaer believes he planned to pay the party to deliver it to Neverember in Neverwinter. Renaer knows whom Dalakhar served, Kalain says so openly in **Fireball!**, and Victoro Cassalanter knows it and withholds it at the dinner. The three quotations that settle the point are under Source References.

## Overview

Few people in Waterdeep can say who knows what about any hidden matter in the city, because those who hold such knowledge keep it close. Waterdhavians do whisper that a large sum of the city's gold went missing in Dagult Neverember's day and that a hoard of gold dragons lies somewhere in the city, though nobody names a figure or a place. They also know there is trouble in the Dock Ward between Xanathar's Guild and the Zhentarim, and few can say what it is about.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural. The table is a reference, so each cell says one thing and the prose around it explains the reasoning. Keep the Overview to what an ordinary Waterdhavian would say over a drink.
