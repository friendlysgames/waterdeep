# What Each Faction Knows at Start

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - This is the starting-knowledge reference for the four villain factions and, as a fifth row, the Doom Raiders. For each one it gives what the faction knows about the Stone of Golorr, which Eye it holds and what it still needs, and it adds one line on how the row changes in play.
> - Use it when you prepare a faction scene, when a contact asks what a faction could know, and when you check how far a rival has got. The table holds on the morning of Ches 22, when **Fireball!** opens, because the Xanathar and Manshoon cells assume Dalakhar has already taken the Stone from Xanathar's lair. The rows change from Ches 23 in Act II to the lair quests in Act III.
> - The rows are read in **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Xanathar's Lair** and **Sea Maidens Faire**, and **Vault of Dragons** reads the faction state those quests leave behind. The Eyes sit in the location journals for **Cassalanter Villa** (Eye #2) and **Sea Maidens Faire** (Eye #3). Eye #1 sits in Xanathar's lair, which has no location journal yet.
> - Response teams, outposts, escalation rows and revelation lists live in the Factions Guide pages for each faction. The full escalation table and the inter-faction operations matrix live in **Running the Villains**. Who each faction is lives in the Organizations pages.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM say, for each faction, what it knows about the Stone, which Eye it holds and what it still needs at the start, and how that changes as the party acts.

*This page contains significant spoilers for the campaign. We recommend reading it so that you understand the primary villains and their motivations. Some Gamemasters, however, may prefer not to have this full knowledge, so proceed only if you wish to have all the available information up-front instead of learning things as you play.*

The starting-knowledge table records what each villain faction knows about the Stone of Golorr and the Eyes, which Eye it holds and what it still needs to open the Vault of Dragons. No faction holds more than one Eye, so each depends on a rival or on the party to complete the set. Read a row to decide what a faction's agents know and will try before the party has changed anything.

## Knowledge When Fireball! Opens (Ches 22)

The cells follow the GM Guide's table in **Running the Villains**. The fifth row is the Doom Raiders, the player-joinable Zhentarim cell, which is not a villain faction.

| Faction | What They Know | What They Have | What They Need |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xanathar's Guild | Dalakhar was moving the Stone; Stone is now in circulation; does not know the Eyes' significance or the vault's location | Eye #1 (in Sylgar's bowl; Xanathar does not know what it is) | The Stone; the Eyes' significance |
| Manshoon's Zhentarim | The Stone exists and unlocks great wealth; Urstul Floxin's team was tracking Dalakhar; does not yet know the Stone reached the Gralhunds | Nothing | Stone + all three Eyes |
| The Cassalanters | Vault is beneath Brandath Crypts; does not yet know the Stone is in play or in circulation | Eye #2 | Stone + Eyes #1 and #3 |
| Bregan D'aerthe | Most complete picture via Nar'l Xibrindas; knows the Stone, the Eyes, and the vault; waiting to see which party proves most useful | Eye #3 | Stone + Eyes #1 and #2 |
| Doom Raiders (the player-joinable Zhentarim; not a villain faction) | Aware of the Grand Game at a high level `[research]`; not primary competitors for the vault | Nothing | No part of the Stone's chain; the vault gold only if a party hands it over, for a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council |

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Two Zhentarims:** Manshoon's Zhentarim and the Doom Raiders are separate cells at war with each other. Answer a question about one from its own row and never from the other's.
> - **Cassalanter money:** The Cassalanters' outposts gather gold and ritual components, and the Vault's 500,000 gp covers what the family cannot raise itself.
> - **Reading "What They Need":** The column lists what the faction still lacks. The vault opens only when the complete Stone, with all three Eyes restored, is presented (see **The Stone of Golorr and the Eyes**).

## How Each Row Changes in Play

- **Xanathar's Guild:** The row holds until the party takes Eye #1 from Sylgar's bowl in **Xanathar's Lair**, and the Guild then needs days of interrogation to learn what was taken.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** By the night of Ches 24, when Floxin's strike team hits **Gralhund Villa**, Manshoon's cell knows the Gralhunds hold the Stone.
- **The Cassalanters:** The row has changed by Ches 23, when Ammalia's dinner invitation reaches the party in **Fireball!**, because the family now knows the Stone is in play.
- **Bregan D'aerthe:** The row does not change when the party speaks with Jarlaxle, because **Jarlaxle Informed** only records the contact, and it changes only if Eye #3 passes to the party in **Sea Maidens Faire** before the ships leave on Tarsakh 20.
- **Doom Raiders:** The row changes only when the party trades intelligence or the vault gold to Davil's cell, and Tashlyn Yafeera answers for the cell once the Watch arrests Davil after **Gralhund Villa**.

## Overview

Waterdhavians who follow the gossip say a great prize is up for grabs and rival crews are racing for it. Hardly anyone can say what the prize is or who is ahead. Most people hear of the rivalry only when a street fight or a fire gets talked about in the taverns.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural. The table keeps the GM Guide's wording so the two pages never drift, and the Overview stays at the level of tavern gossip.
