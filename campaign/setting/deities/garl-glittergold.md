# Garl Glittergold

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the deity entry for Garl Glittergold, the Joker and head of the gnomish pantheon, for players who want a gnome cleric, a trickster or a devout character from a gnomish family, and for a GM who needs a name behind a gnome's oath or prank.
> - **Where it is used:** Garl never appears in person, and no campaign file or published adventure names him. The page records that Waterdeep gives him no temple, and it lists the gnomes in the files so that no later page gives one of them a god by accident.
> - **Featured in:** No quest. The gnomes who come nearest are Dalakhar in **Fireball!**, the engineers aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* in **Sea Maidens Faire**, and Skeemo Weirdbottle in the Doom Raiders missions.
> - **Spoiler limit:** everything above the `## Overview` may be read by the GM only. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier. Nothing about Garl is secret, but the loyalties of the *Scarlet Marpenoth* crew and of Skeemo are, and they sit in the Never reveal bullet.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Garl, and says plainly that no source gives him a temple, a festival or a worshiper in Waterdeep.

*The Joker*

- **Title:** God of Trickery and Gems (SCAG, 2014). The Player's Handbook (2014) reads "Gnome god of trickery and wiles". FRHoF has no entry for him.
- **Other Names:** The Priceless Gem (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e); the Sparkling Wit and the Watchful Protector (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e, and *Demihuman Deities*, 2e); the Father of All Gnomes (*Prince of Lies*, novel). The wiki gives Garlian as the adjective for his faith (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e).
- **Pantheon:** Gnomish (SCAG, 2014). MTF labels it Gnome, the Player's Handbook (2014) labels it Nonhuman, and the wiki names it the Lords of the Golden Hills.
- **Areas of Concern:** Gnomes, humor, trickery and illusion, gem cutting, protection, luck, cooperation (derived from the MTF text and the wiki portfolios, which list humor, gem cutting, gnomes, protection and trickery, and from the wiki's opening line, which adds luck; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Trickery (SCAG, 2014; MTF and the Player's Handbook, 2014, repeat it). The wiki's 3e list is Avarice, Craft, Envy, Gnome, Good, Law, Protection and Trickery, and its 4e list is Creation and Trickery.
- **Symbol:** Gold nugget (SCAG, 2014; MTF and the Player's Handbook, 2014, repeat it)
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good (SCAG, 2014)
- **Plane:** Bytopia, in the layer of Dothion, in his realm the Golden Hills, also called Glitterhome (wiki, 2e). The wiki's 3e row gives the Golden Hills as his home plane and its 4e row gives Arvandor as his dominion. Neither SCAG nor MTF gives a plane.
- **Typical Followers:** Gnomes (MTF, which calls him the god of the gnomes). The wiki's 3e row adds adventurers, bards, defending soldiers, gemcutters, illusionists, jewelers, miners, rogues and smiths.

> Take life lightly and work together.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Garl is the head of the gnomish pantheon, which the wiki calls the Lords of the Golden Hills. The wiki rates him a greater deity in its 2e and 3e rows and a deity in its 4e and 5e rows, and its 5e row calls him the primary god of gnomes.
- **Who follows:** He is the most popular of the gnomish gods, and nearly all gnomes honor him in one way or another (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **How gnomes know him:** In the first stories that gnome children hear about the gods, he is a gold-skinned gnome with a wide grin and gemstone eyes that shift colors like a kaleidoscope. Children quickly learn that their favorite character is about to steal the show (MTF).
- **What the faith teaches:** All three points below come from MTF.
  - Life is to be taken lightly, and a good laugh serves gnomes better than a grim attitude. Garl is a joker and a prankster, and his pranks reach the other pantheons. Moradin's beard might end up woven with giggling flowers, and Gruumsh's axe might sprout braying donkey heads at the worst moment.
  - The legends about him push gnomes to work together, since many heads and many hands make light work. He plays many pranks on his own, but he is also the one who gathers the heroes for an enterprise that needs all their talents.
  - He prefers trickery and illusion to direct combat, and he wants gnomes to thrive by intellect and ingenuity. When a fight cannot be avoided he uses Arumdina, his intelligent two-headed battle axe, which can cleave through any substance, to escape the danger.
- **His axe:** Arumdina is his main companion, and the wiki calls it Arumdina the Justifier (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **Clergy and orders:** His clerics are called Glitterbrights and wear war helms and gold belts (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). The Golden Watchful is a small knightly order of gnome paladins who serve him, and the wiki cites a web post of 2019-01-06 for it.
- **Holy day:** On the 13th of each month his faithful hold the Communion of Laughter, with joke-telling contests, communal meals, dancing, prayers and storytelling. They also give gold, which his church spends for the benefit of the whole community (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). The wiki's 5e row gives no holy days, and no campaign file ties the 13th to a custom in Waterdeep.
- **Prayer:** A basic prayer asks him for a fraction of his wit, which is all anyone could need (*Baldur's Gate III*, video game).
- **Messengers and omens:** To guide gnomes he may send an omen that nudges a group in a certain direction, or he may manifest an avatar in the middle of a gnome burrow. The avatar appears rarely, and only to settle a dispute that threatens a community (MTF). The wiki lists a disembodied mouth and a cloud of golden dust among his manifestations (*Deity Do's and Don'ts*).
- **Associations:** The wiki lists badgers, chipmunks, moles, raccoons, squirrels, weasels and their dire forms as his animals, animated weapons as his monsters, carved gems as his mineral and gold as his color. These rows carry no citation of their own.
- **Relations:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - He is allied with the other non-evil gnome gods and with Yondalla, Corellon Larethian and Moradin (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
  - The wiki's 2e row lists six gods who serve him: Baervan Wildwanderer, Baravar Cloakshadow, Callarduran Smoothhands, Flandal Steelskin, Gaerdal Ironhand and Segojan Earthcaller (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e). Its 4e row keeps the first three (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e).
  - His jests at the gods of the Underdark have made him many enemies, and the chief of them is the kobold god Kurtulmak (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
  - The Tymora page of this journal records that the wiki counts him among her allies. The Grumbar page records that the wiki's Grumbar entry lists him among the earth powers with loose ties to Grumbar (*The Inner Planes*).
  - The Gond page records that gnomes know Gond as Nebelun the Meddler, and that a few gnome groups hold Nebelun to be a separate god (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e; *Monster Mythology*).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact. The wiki gives two legends and no dated history.
  - He was exploring underground when he found a new cavern holding a people he had not met, the gnomes. He told them a joke and led them out into the wider world (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e).
  - In a second legend he let Kurtulmak capture him, then escaped and brought the roof of Kurtulmak's cavern down on the kobold god's head (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **In the campaign:** Garl is alive and present in 1492 DR, as every power is. MTF and SCAG write of him in the present tense, and the wiki's past tense is edition history.

> #### Using Garl Glittergold as a Deity
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice him as the gnomes' own joker, known in Waterdeep more by reputation than by prayer. A sage, a priest or a regular at the bar says he is the god of the gnomes, a lawful good trickster whose symbol is a gold nugget. They add that he plays pranks on the dwarf and orc gods, that he gathers heroes when a job needs many hands, and that gnomes would sooner trick their way out of a fight than swing a sword. A priest of another god may know that his followers hold a feast of jokes and stories on the 13th of the month. Gnomes are about three in a hundred Waterdhavians, so few people in the city pray to him (derived). Which of his names the city uses, and how much a bar regular really knows, is unsourced. [research]
> - **Waterdeep:** Garl has no temple, shrine, priest or festival in the city in any source.
>   - The Dragon Heist Major Temples table has no Garl row (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:30921-30996`). The "All deities" row, Holyhands House in the North Ward (`:30923-30925`), is the only listing that could cover him, and no source says gnomes use it. The wiki has no Waterdeep text for him.
>   - The Players' Guide sends gnome characters to the House of Inspired Hands, the temple of Gond in the Sea Ward, which it calls a guild of inventors and tinkerers where artisans speak of their god like an old friend (`campaign/guides/players-guide/character-creation.md:24`). A gnome PC who wants a place to belong in the city finds it there, and nothing in the files offers a temple of Garl.
>   - Gnomes are about 3% of the city's people (`campaign/gazetteer/waterdeep/castle-ward.md:29`, citing the wiki, *City of Splendors*, 2e). Many gnomes and halflings live in Undercliff, east of the walls, where the buildings are built to their stature (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:31125`).
>   - Every four years the Trades Ward declares a new object its mimic mascot, and rock gnomes and wizards then make illusory mouths lunge from real copies of it (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:31039`). The prank suits his faith, but no source ties it to him (derived).
> - **A cleric PC:** The domain is Trickery (SCAG, 2014; MTF and the Player's Handbook, 2014, repeat it). The 2024 subclass is Trickery Domain, an exact fit by name (derived). The Divine Order that fits is Thaumaturge (derived), because MTF has him favor trickery and illusion over combat and tells gnomes to rely on their wits. Protector suits a Glitterbright in a war helm who guards a gnome community, and his title of Watchful Protector supports it (derived). A cleric of Garl finds no temple to join in the city.
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of Devotion (derived), a good fit. The Golden Watchful are a small knightly order of gnome paladins who serve him (wiki), and his title of Watchful Protector points to guarding others. No source places the order in Waterdeep or gives it an oath.
> - **A warlock PC:** No warlock connection in the sources.
> - **Campaign ties:** No campaign file or published adventure names Garl, and none gives a god to any gnome. The gnomes in the files are listed here so that nobody hands one a god by accident.
>   - **Dalakhar** is a rock gnome spy in the pay of Dagult Neverember, and he dies in the fireball in **Fireball!**.
>   - **Skeemo Weirdbottle** is a rock gnome alchemist who runs Weirdbottle's Concoctions in the Trades Ward and serves the Doom Raiders as their Master of Magic.
>   - **Xoblob** is a deep gnome and Xanathar's Guild informant who keeps the Old Xoblob Shop in the Dock Ward (`campaign/quests/act-i/finding-floon/ev-02-dock-ward-investigation.md:31`).
>   - Rock gnome engineers crew the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. Breena Bafflestone keeps the engine room, with three gnome pilots and four off-duty engineers aboard (`campaign/locations/sea-maidens-faire/area-overview.md:35`). If **Marpenoth Crippled** is marked in the Bregan D'aerthe rank event **Houseless Noble**, a dozen gnomes refit the submersible in a rented boathouse on the Dock Ward waterfront (`campaign/quests/faction-events/bregan-daerthe/r50-houseless-noble/ev-01-houseless-noble.md:73`). The published adventure also has Lantanese gnomes working for Jarlaxle repair the mechanical dragon turtle Big Belchy at Mistshore (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:7575`).
>   - In the published adventure, Pynt Oomtrowl, a chaotic good rock gnome of the Cellarers' and Plumbers' Guild, is one of three guild representatives meeting Esvele Rosznar (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:9667`). No campaign file uses her.
>   - In the published *Dungeon of the Mad Mage*, the rock gnome archmage Zox Clammersham lives on Level 13 (`sources/adventure-wdmm.json:36146`), a statue of three smiling gnomes stacked on each other's shoulders stands at area 18 on Level 20 (`:54694`), and the gnome with no name is trapped in a mirror at area 23 on Level 23 (`:61721`). The rock gnome Krystaleen runs Thimblewine's Pawnshop in Skullport (`:63614`). The statue fits his theme of cooperation, but no source ties it to him (derived). Undermountain is future work, so treat all of these as seeds.
> - **Never reveal:** That the gnome engineers and pilots aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* work for Jarlaxle, or that Skeemo Weirdbottle betrays the Doom Raiders. A player who asks about Garl gets the Overview, and the party's own evidence is the only thing that adds to it.

## Overview

Garl Glittergold is the lawful good god of the gnomes, a joker and prankster pictured as a gold-skinned gnome with a wide grin. Gnomes say he teaches them to take life lightly, to work together and to use their wits before their steel. His symbol is a gold nugget, and his faithful hold a feast of jokes and stories on the 13th of each month. Waterdeep has no temple of his, and gnomes make up about three in a hundred of the city's people.

**Tone:** Warm and quick, in the brisk reference register. Garl is a god of jokes, so describe the prank and the laughter before the theology, and keep the narration plain while the gnomes tell the stories. Keep him at a distance, since the campaign writes no scene in which he acts.
