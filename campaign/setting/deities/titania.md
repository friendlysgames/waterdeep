> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the patron entry for Titania, the Summer Queen of the Feywild and an Archfey patron for warlock characters. She is not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon, 5etools has no entry for her and the sources give her no domains, so this page replaces the cleric and paladin guidance of a deity page with warlock guidance.
> - **Where it is used:** Titania never appears in the campaign, and no campaign file names her. She is a choice for a player building a warlock. *Dragon Heist* names her once, among the costumes worn on Fey Day (Ches 19), and the campaign calendar keeps that festival.
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**, through the Fey Day entry in the campaign calendar and nothing else. No quest uses her.
> - **Spoiler limit:** this page holds no hidden fact that changes play, so it has no Secret box. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier and never mentions the Cassalanters. Everything above it may be read by the GM only.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian knows about Titania, gives a player what the sources say about an Archfey patron relationship with her, and states that the campaign has tied nothing to her so that no later page invents more.

*The Summer Queen*

- **Title:** Summer Queen (*Dungeon Master's Guide* 5e revised; *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* 5e; *Manual of the Planes* 4e; all via the wiki)
- **Other Names:** Tiandra (*Manual of the Planes* 4e), the Faerie Queen (*Monster Mythology* 2e; *On Hallowed Ground* 2e) and the Queen of Light (*Fey Feature* web article). All via the wiki.
- **Pantheon:** None. The wiki files her under the Seelie Court, and its 2e sources count her among the sylvan gods. She is an archfey and a patron, not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon.
- **Areas of Concern:** Fey beings, friendship, joy, and the safety of faerie lands (derived from the wiki's accounts of her work; its 2e portfolio reads "fey, friendship, magic")
- **Domains:** None in 5etools or the wiki. She grants no cleric domains.
- **Symbol:** A white diamond with a blue star (*Monster Mythology* 2e; *On Hallowed Ground* 2e; both via the wiki)
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good (2e, via the wiki). The wiki's 4e and 5e alignment fields are empty.
- **Challenge:** [research]. The wiki gives no challenge rating in any edition.
- **Plane:** The Feywild, where her palace Senaliesse stands (wiki; 4e and 5e). The wiki's 2e rows put her realm, the Seelie Court, on the Beastlands, Arborea and Ysgard in turn.
- **Typical Followers:** Non-evil fey, among them brownies, bookas, pixies and sprites (*Monster Mythology* 2e, via the wiki)

> Let all fey live in joy, and guard the faerie lands that shelter them. (derived)

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Titania is the Summer Queen of the Feywild and the leader of the Summer Fey, and she is possibly the mightiest of all archfey (*Dungeon Master's Guide* 5e revised; *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* 5e; *Manual of the Planes* 4e). She leads the Seelie Court. The wiki rates her an archfey in its 4e and 5e rows and a greater deity in its 2e row, and it lists no holy days for any edition.
- **Her nature:** The wiki finds her hard to classify. She seems flighty and frivolous to some, but she is a brilliant strategist at court and in battle, with a dry sense of humor and a pragmatic streak (*Manual of the Planes* 4e). It is almost impossible to make her angry, and anyone who commits a truly despicable act faces her most powerful wrath (*Monster Mythology* 2e).
- **Her powers:** Her smile makes crops ripen and her frown can start wildfires (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* 5e; *Manual of the Planes* 4e). Her gaze can drive mortals mad (*Manual of the Planes* 4e).
- **What she wants:** She watches over every kind of fey being, from dryads, pixies and satyrs to treants and unicorns, and she hopes that all of them will one day live in utter joy (*On Hallowed Ground* 2e). When harm comes to faerie lands she does not hesitate to send her avatar to intervene (*Monster Mythology* 2e).
- **Mortals:** She is amused by mortals and has taken some as lovers (*Manual of the Planes* 4e). Mortal bards and playwrights often perform in her throne room (*Manual of the Planes* 4e).
- **Followers:** A great many non-evil fey worship her. Brownies, bookas, pixies and sprites call her their patron deity. They teach that she is a goddess of goodness and mercy, and they see her as a mother figure (*Monster Mythology* 2e).
- **Warlocks:** Titania is a patron to many warlocks in the Realms (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* 5e), and the wiki files her under its archfey patrons. It gives no pact terms, boons or price. [research]
- **Clergy, holy days, omens, messengers, temples:** None. The wiki says that, like other sylvan gods, she grants no spells and has no priests or clerics, because fey creatures have magic of their own (*Monster Mythology* 2e). It lists no holy days, omens or messengers, and no campaign file adds any. [research]
- **The Seelie Court:** When the Court meets, her avatar is always present and almost always comes with at least one member of the Court's Inner Circle, and all faeries know when and where the Court will gather (*Monster Mythology* 2e). From time to time she hosts a parliament of all the fey courts, seelie and unseelie, at Senaliesse, which is sometimes called the Court of Stars (*Manual of the Planes* 4e).
- **Her court and palace:** Senaliesse is her palace, where she holds the Summer Court. Its throne room is crowded with flowers, fountains and fine silks, and faeries flit about it (*Manual of the Planes* 4e). Her throne is carved from ice in the shape of a dragon and is a magical construct that can serve as her guardian. It mirrors the throne of the Queen of Air and Darkness, which is made of darkness and shadow (*Fey Feature* web article). Noble eladrin infused with the power of summer serve as her barons, and her niece Lady Shandria governs Astrazalian, the City of Starlight (*Manual of the Planes* 4e).
- **Her 2e realm:** In the wiki's 2e account her realm is also called the Seelie Court. It has no fixed place and wanders from plane to plane, from the Beastlands to Arborea to Ysgard. It is one of the most beautiful places in existence, and time flows differently there (*On Hallowed Ground* 2e).
- **Relations with other powers:** Wiki-sourced.
  - Oberon, the Green Lord, was her famous lover, and he built her palace (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* 5e; *Manual of the Planes* 4e). Some call the pair husband and wife while admitting that both take other lovers freely (*On Hallowed Ground* 2e). At other times they are great enemies, and sometimes they are lover and foe together (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* 5e; *Manual of the Planes* 4e).
  - Damh, the god of satyrs and korreds, is seen as the son of Titania and Oberon, and Verenestra, the goddess of dryads, as Titania's daughter (*Monster Mythology* 2e). The Prince of Frost is also called her son (*Dragon* 374, edition not given).
  - The sylvan gods of the "Inner Circle" respect her as their queen and defer to her judgment. Skerrit, the god of centaurs, and the other "Outer Circle" gods do not follow her rule, but she loves them and their worshipers (*Monster Mythology* 2e).
  - The Seldarine are on good terms with her and are willing to help whenever she asks (*On Hallowed Ground* 2e; *Demihuman Deities* 2e).
  - The Queen of Air and Darkness is rumored to be her dark sister. Whatever the truth, Titania refuses to wage open war on the evil queen and grieves for her instead (*Monster Mythology* 2e).
- **Origin and rise:** The wiki gives none beyond her titles and her courts. [research]
- **In the campaign:** Titania is alive and present in 1492 DR, as every power in this journal is. The wiki writes of her in the past tense, and no file shows her acting.

> #### Using Titania as a Patron
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice her as a figure from a festival. Waterdhavians meet her name on Fey Day, when revelers dress as fey beings and as rulers of the Feywild, with Queen Titania among the three the book names. A sage can add that she is the Summer Queen who leads the Seelie Court. *Dragon Heist* calls these rulers the "supposed rulers of the Feywild", so the city treats them as figures of story more than objects of worship (derived). No source records any other Waterdhavian opinion of her, or a rumor about her. [research]
> - **Waterdeep:** No temple, shrine or cult. The Major Temples of Waterdeep table in *Dragon Heist* lists none of Titania, and no campaign file or wiki entry places one in the city.
>   - The table lists the Temple of the Seldarine in the Castle Ward for all elven deities and Holyhands House in the North Ward for all deities. The wiki says the Seldarine are on good terms with Titania (*On Hallowed Ground* 2e), but no file says either temple honors her. [research]
>   - Fey Day falls on Ches 19. *Dragon Heist* says the veil between the world and the Feywild is thought to be weak that day. Rural folk avoid woodlands and leave food on their doorsteps, while Waterdeep drinks, sings and dances. The wealthy host masked balls, and poorer folk in costumes of their own make go door to door and trade a song or a short play for a place at the celebration.
>   - The campaign calendar sets Fey Day in **Trollskull Alley**, the day before the Grand Opening, and notes that a masked guest at any noble house draws no remark. No file gives Fey Day a priest, a rite or a hook tied to Titania.
> - **A warlock PC:** Archfey Patron (derived; 2024 *Player's Handbook*) fits her, and the wiki files her under its archfey patrons and calls her a patron to many warlocks in the Realms (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* 5e). No source gives Titania-specific features. What she wants is a world where fey beings live in joy and faerie lands stay unharmed (derived from her aims above). The wiki states no price for a pact. [research] It does record her wrath against a truly despicable act, a gaze that can drive mortals mad, and a liking for mortals that has led her to take some as lovers (*Monster Mythology* 2e; *Manual of the Planes* 4e). A warlock who wants a darker archfey can look to the **Queen of Air and Darkness** (Deities).
> - **A cleric PC:** The wiki says Titania has no priests or clerics and grants no spells, so a cleric of hers has no church in the sources. Peace Domain (derived; *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*) fits best, because her concerns are friendship and joy and the domain is built on bonds between allies. The alternative is Nature Domain (derived; 2014 *Player's Handbook*), for her smile that ripens crops and her care for the fey of the wild. The Divine Order is Protector (derived), since she sends her avatar to guard faerie lands. The fit is loose. A character who wants a fey-friendly faith without a pact can look at **Mielikki** (Deities), whose entry lists fey among her worshipers (derived).
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of the Ancients (derived; 2024 *Player's Handbook*) fits best, because the oath is sworn to preserve life and light and her aim is a world where the fey live in joy. The wiki names no paladins or holy warriors of hers, so the fit is loose.
> - **Campaign ties:** No campaign file uses this power. No cult, NPC, faction or quest is tied to Titania, and neither adventure JSON names the Court of Stars. The one mention is Fey Day, where *Dragon Heist* lists her among the "supposed rulers of the Feywild, such as Queen Titania, Oberon, and Hyrsam, the Prince of Fools" (page 184).
>   - Two faerie dragons appear in the adventures, and neither is tied to her. Wishes is the violet faerie dragon that belonged to the late husband of Iokaste Daliano, owner of the Pink Flumph theater in *Dragon Heist*. Otto is a violet faerie dragon that "was drawn here by the magic that Halaster wove into these caves" in Maddgoth's Castle, Level 7 of *Dungeon of the Mad Mage*. Neither entry mentions the Feywild or an archfey, and no campaign file uses either dragon.

## Overview

Titania is the Summer Queen of the Feywild, leader of the Seelie Court and possibly the mightiest of the archfey. She seems flighty to some, but sages call her a shrewd strategist who watches over every kind of fey. She is slow to anger, and those who commit a truly cruel act face her full wrath. Some warlocks take her as a patron, and in Waterdeep revelers dress as her on Fey Day.

**Tone:** Warm, playful and shrewd. Titania looks frivolous and plans like a general, so when a player asks about her, describe the joy and friendship she protects before the anger she keeps in reserve.
