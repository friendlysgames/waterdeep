> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the patron entry for Mephistopheles, the archdevil who rules Cania and a Fiend patron for warlock characters. He is not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon and the sources give him no 5etools domains, so this page replaces the cleric and paladin guidance of a deity page with warlock guidance.
> - **Where it is used:** Mephistopheles never appears in the campaign. He is a choice for a player building a warlock, and three other Deities pages name him in passing: Asmodeus (as ally and rival), Zariel (as an archdevil who fears her) and Mask (in the wiki's account of Mask's divinity).
> - **Featured in:** no quest. No campaign file and neither adventure source names him.
> - **Spoiler limit:** this page holds no hidden fact that changes play, so it has no Secret box. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier and never mentions the Cassalanters. Everything above it may be read by the GM only.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what little an ordinary Waterdhavian knows about Mephistopheles, gives a player what a Fiend patron relationship with him costs, and states that the campaign has tied nothing to him so that no later page invents more.

*The Cold Lord*

- **Title:** Lord of the Eighth, Archduke of Cania (wiki; *Fiendish Codex II* 3e)
- **Other Names:** The Cold Lord (*Dragon* 76, edition not given), Lord of Hellfire and Duke of Brimstone (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e), Mephisto (*Monster Manual II* 1e), Molikroth (his alias, see the history below). All via the wiki.
- **Pantheon:** None. He is an archdevil and a patron, not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon.
- **Areas of Concern:** Hellfire, arcane research, soul contracts with wizards and sages (derived from the wiki's accounts of his work and his cult; the wiki lists none)
- **Domains:** None in 5etools. The wiki lists the 3e domains Diabolic, Evil and Fire.
- **Symbol:** A flame in copper and gold, or a red hand with black nails wrapped in dark flame (wiki; 3e). He keeps adopting new symbols (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e).
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil (wiki; 3e). The wiki's 4e field says Evil and its 5e field is empty.
- **Challenge:** 29 (wiki; 3e). The wiki gives no 5e rating. [research]
- **Plane:** Cania, the layer of the Nine Hells that his title Lord of the Eighth names (wiki; 5e). His seat is the citadel of Mephistar.
- **Typical Followers:** Wizards, oni and storm giants (wiki; 5e), plus disaffected devil-worshipers and fire worshipers (wiki; 3e)

> Take arcane power that outdoes your rivals, and sign your soul over for it. (derived)

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Mephistopheles is an archdevil, the lord of Cania and the most powerful devil after Asmodeus (wiki). He rules as an archdevil and not as a god, and the wiki lists no holy days for him. He wants to be worshiped as a god and has never had the cult to do it (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e; *Book of Vile Darkness* 3e).
- **Two faces:** On the surface he is the Cold Lord, a cool, courteous and charming figure with the manner of a princely gentleman (*Dungeon Master's Guide* 5e). Underneath he is the Lord of Hellfire, whose temper breaks out in rages when he is alone, and whose unchecked ambition and envy of Asmodeus drive his plots (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e).
- **His work:** He is called the foremost wizard of the Hells. From Mephistar he runs experiments on arcane magic and the nature of the planes (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e), and his current obsession is hellfire (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e). His attention is strong but unsteady. When politics pulls him off a project, a new one may catch his eye and the old one is dropped, and he disintegrates underlings who distract him (*Dungeon Master's Guide* 5e; *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
- **What he offers:** He and his harvesters lure skilled wizards and clever sages by playing on their curiosity and ambition. The offer of magical might is itself taken as proof of the buyer's greatness, and many of those who sign gain the ability to deflect and absorb magic (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e). He keeps the lowest quotas of the archdevils and the highest standards.
- **What a pact costs:** His charm lasts only until the caster signs. After death the soul faces decades of dull routine, deep regret and isolation meant to prevent dissent. His agents avoid saying anything about his behavior, and the clever clauses in the soul contracts let him destroy a tricked sage with a word (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
- **Hellfire:** He invented hellfire and is its greatest master (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e). It gives even a clumsy and unlearned person of influence an easy path to power, and the user pays with a sacrifice of his own vitality. Because the scheme gathers souls and spreads his influence, a follower does not have to be lawful evil to join (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e).
- **Whom he courts:** Entire wizard guilds and sage conclaves have fallen under his sway, along with magic-using creatures such as storm giants and oni (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e). He also courts fire giants, who try to become hellfire masters (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e), frost giants (web article, 2007), and devil-worshipers unhappy with their own archdevil (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e).
- **His cult:** It is among the smallest of any archdevil's. He has made himself the picture of the devil so thoroughly that many mortals take him for Asmodeus, and Asmodeus lets him run many of his own cults, which blurs his identity further (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e).
- **Rites and clergy:** His disciples are called hellfire masters and hellfire stewards, and a granter, typically a higher-ranking cleric, decides what hellfire an initiate receives. Initiation requires sacrificing an intelligent being in magical flame, usually alive. Hellfire masters wear red and black, carry ranseurs and shout rather than speak (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e; *Book of Vile Darkness* 3e).
- **Temples:** His temples are hidden and distant, built around great fire pits with slabs of blackened stone for altars (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e). A book of rites called the Book of Brimstone is rumored to exist in four copies (*Dungeon* 140, edition not given).
- **Warlocks:** The wiki calls him a patron of magic and the source of many warlock pacts (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e; *Manual of the Planes* 4e). It also says some warlocks found hellfire by themselves while experimenting with eldritch blasts, though they needed a good deal of magical and planar knowledge to call it (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e).
- **Aspects:** He sends aspects that do not fear death, since he recreates them if they are destroyed. They appear in the Realms now and then, usually with other devils, and an aspect of him is rumored to be trapped in Undermountain (web article, 2007; edition not given).
- **His realm:** Cania is a frozen waste, and Mephistar is a citadel carved from a glacier, warm and lavish inside. His hellfire experiments are melting it, and the gelugons who once lived there are leaving for the outer reaches (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e).
- **Holy days, omens, messengers:** None in the wiki or the campaign. [research] The wiki's only emissaries are his aspects and the devils who travel with them.
- **Relations with other archdevils and powers:** Wiki-sourced.
  - Asmodeus is his master, his most dangerous enemy and his most capable ally. Mephistopheles makes no secret that he means to depose him, and Asmodeus still takes his counsel and may be afraid of him (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e; *Dungeon Master's Guide* 5e; *The Plane Above* 4e). The Asmodeus page calls him "his greatest ally and chief rival," which agrees.
  - Baalzebul is his greatest rival, and each sees the other as the main obstacle to the throne of Hell (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e).
  - Dispater is his nominal ally. The two share seniority and a hunger for knowledge, and Mephistopheles's court watches for Dispater's spies (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
  - Mammon was once allied with both and sold them out at the end of the Reckoning, which made him unwelcome to every archdevil (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e).
  - Levistus is an archdevil he keeps a careful eye on (*Guide to Hell* 3e). Glasya, Asmodeus's daughter, has him as something of a godfather (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e).
  - Zariel frightens him. He fears her wrath and looks for leverage over her (*Descent into Avernus* 5e, as recorded on her page).
- **History of his rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - He is ancient, as old as Asmodeus by some accounts. He trapped a race of mercenary fiends in servitude and so created the gelugons (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e; *Manual of the Planes* 4e).
  - Before the Reckoning he took the identity of an archdevil called Baron Molikroth, "deposed" himself, ruled under that name for a long time and then revealed himself, ending the traitors who had conspired with Molikroth (*Manual of the Planes* 3e; *Book of Vile Darkness* 3e).
  - In the Reckoning he fought beside Dispater, Mammon and Geryon against Baalzebul's faction, which included Zariel, Belial and Moloch. Geryon turned on him, and the pit fiend generals of both armies turned against their lords. He suffered least of the rebels (*Guide to Hell* 3e).
  - During the Spellplague, Erevis Cale promised him a share of the god Mask's divinity, and he killed his own half-breed son Magadon as part of the deal. Cale later gave his life to bring back Magadon's soul, and Mephistopheles received a fraction of the divinity at an unexpected cost (*Shadowstorm*, novel). Drasek Riven then attacked him in Cania, and a cairn of ice that he could not enter or scry hid the rest of the divinity from him for a century (*The Godborn*, novel).
  - Asmodeus had meanwhile absorbed Azuth, which left Mephistopheles second-rate and afraid of punishment (*The Godborn*, novel).
  - In 1484 DR he hunted Vasen Cale, Erevis's son, in the hope of opening the cairn. Vasen, a servant of Amaunator, and his father stripped the remains of Mask's divinity from every holder, and Riven took it and effectively became the reincarnated Mask. Mephistopheles fled with no divine spark (*The Godborn*, novel).

A priest or a bar regular who says "the devil" usually means Asmodeus, because the wiki says many mortals mistake the two (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e). No source records a Waterdhavian opinion of him or a Waterdhavian rumor about him. [research]
> - **Waterdeep:** None. The Major Temples of Waterdeep table in *Dragon Heist* lists no temple of Mephistopheles, and no campaign file or wiki entry places a shrine, cult or festival of his in the city. The wiki's one non-game Waterdeep item is the rumor that an aspect of him is trapped in Undermountain. Its other Waterdeep items come from a video game and are not used (see Draft Notes).
> - **A warlock PC:** The Fiend patron in the 2024 *Player's Handbook* fits him (derived). The wiki calls him a patron of magic and the source of many warlock pacts, and his hellfire suits the Fiend's fire spells (derived). No source gives Mephistopheles-specific features. What he wants is a skilled and ambitious caster, flattered by an offer of power that looks like a sign of greatness. What a pact costs is the soul. His charm lasts until the signature, the afterlife is decades of tedium and isolation, the contract has clauses that let him destroy the signer with a word, and hellfire spends the user's own vitality.
> - **A cleric or paladin PC:** His cult has clergy, but 5etools gives no domains and the wiki's 3e domains (Diabolic, Evil, Fire) are not a 2024 list. The closest 2024 subclass is Light, with the Thaumaturge Divine Order (both derived). Its fire spells match hellfire, and the order matches a cult built on arcane and religious learning, but the fit is loose. No paladin connection in the sources.
> - **Campaign ties:** No campaign file uses this power for a cult, an NPC, a faction or a quest. Three Deities pages name him in passing (see the Gamemaster's Summary). *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* never names him or Cania. Its nearest devils are Halaster's bound barbed devils and the bone devil in Dweomercore (Level 9), an ice devil summoned by a teleport trap (Level 15) and the pit fiend Kastzanedes trapped in a shield (Level 23). The wiki says ice devils serve him before any other archdevil (*Manual of the Planes* 4e), but no adventure line ties any of these devils to him.

## Overview

Mephistopheles is the archdevil of Cania, the frozen eighth layer of the Nine Hells, and the most powerful devil after Asmodeus. Scholars call him the greatest wizard in the Hells, a courteous lord whose cool manner is said to hide a violent temper. He bargains with wizards and sages, trading magic for their souls in contracts said to favor him in every clause. Few mortals worship him openly, and many take him for Asmodeus.

**Tone:** Cold, courteous and exacting. Mephistopheles is a scholar who collects, so when a player asks about him, describe the knowledge and power he offers before the contract that pays for it.
