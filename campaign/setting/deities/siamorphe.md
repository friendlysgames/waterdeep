# Siamorphe

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the deity entry for Siamorphe, the demigod of nobility and Waterdeep's own power, and the second Deities page to follow the Asmodeus template.
> - **Where it is used:** Siamorphe never appears in person. Her old temple, her holy symbol and her consecrated mace are the tools the party uses against the Cassalanters, so this page backs every scene in which the party finds her signs in the villa.
> - **Featured in:** **Cassalanter Villa**, **The Shrine on Aveen Street** (Order of the Gauntlet mission), **Whitehawk** (Order of the Gauntlet rank event), **Sea Maidens Faire** (the Stone's vision shows her sun symbol without naming her).
> - **Spoiler limit:** everything above the `## Overview` may be read by the GM only. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier and says only that the Cassalanters are known for their devotion to her. It never mentions the temple beneath their villa.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Siamorphe, and sets out exactly which pieces of her faith the campaign has built into Cassalanter Villa so that no later page invents more.

*The Lady of Nobility*

- **Title:** The Divine Right (3e)
- **Other Names:** The Lady of Nobility (campaign, the Order's chaplain). No other names in the sources. [research]
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian (wiki category)
- **Areas of Concern:** Nobility, the nobles' right to rule, and the responsibility that comes with it (derived from the wiki portfolios: "Nobility, responsibility" in 5e, and "Nobles, rightful noble rule, human royalty" in 3e; no source lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Knowledge, Law, Nobility, Planning, Pride (3e)
- **Symbol:** A silver chalice with a golden sun on the side (3e; *Dragon Heist* agrees)
- **Alignment:** Lawful neutral (1e, 2e, 3e); lawful good (4e)
- **Plane:** House of the Triad (3e)
- **Typical Followers:** Leaders, nobles, loremasters, those with inherited wealth or status (3e)

> Rule well, and rule for the good of those beneath you.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Siamorphe is a demigod (5e, *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* p.151; *Dragon Heist* calls her the same) and a demipower in 2e and 3e. In 4e she is an exarch who serves Amaunator, with the Eternal Sun as her dominion. In 3e she has no true allies or superiors among the gods.
- **What the faith teaches:** Nobles were given a divine right to rule, provided they rule responsibly and well. They must rule in their subjects' best interest, even when that cuts against their own wishes (2e, *Powers and Pantheons*).
- **Why rank exists:** The benefits of nobility were provided so that nobles could rule well. Wisdom and charisma pass down from noble ancestors, and the leisure of their station exists so they can be educated to do the work (3e, *Faiths and Pantheons*).
- **Who follows her:** Nobles and their counselors, most of all those who want a reason why they are in charge (2e). Leaders, loremasters and people with inherited wealth or status follow her too (3e).
- **Clergy:** Her priests are the Scions of Siamorphe, who most often serve as spiritual and practical advisers to the nobility (2e, 14th century DR). They revived elaborate rituals that had decayed under the previous Siamorphe and wrote new ceremonies for the noble families of Waterdeep and Tethyr, which were becoming fashionable. A baptism for a new heir can easily cost the family 10,000 gp (2e).
- **Temples:** Most are small family shrines in the houses of the nobility, in Waterdeep and more recently in Tethyr (3e). The wiki names the Chapel and Chalice of the Divine Right as her only true remaining temple. It is a small house of worship inside the Assumbar family's villa, and the central one of three buildings along the Street of the Singing Dolphin (2e). The wiki gives it no ward.
- **Holy day:** The Divine Pageantry, kept in the Sea Ward and North Ward of Waterdeep (3e). The nobility take over Heroes' Walk, Heroes' Garden and the Street of Glances, down to the temple on the Street of the Singing Dolphin. Nobles dress and act in archaic, stilted fashion and throw coins, mostly copper and silver, to the commoners.
- **Known devotees in the campaign:** The Cassalanters are publicly known for their ancestral devotion to her. *Dragon Heist* says the family is known to worship her as the demigod whose ethos is the nobility's right and responsibility to rule.
- **Opposed powers:** She opposes those who foster tyranny and corruption, above all Bane, Cyric and Gargauth (2e and 3e).
- **The quarrel with Tyr (1384 DR, 4e):** Shortly before the Spellplague, Tyr and Siamorphe, both then living in the House of the Triad, quarreled over a battle between Tethyrian and Calishite forces. She left to make her realm with Sune in Brightwater. Tyr sent Helm to Sune to explain his position, and Sune tried to make peace by proposing a marriage between Tyr and Tymora. Tyr agreed and began courting Tymora (*The Grand History of the Realms* p.159, 4e).
- **Origin, the line of mortal Siamorphes (2e):** The Divine Right is a semi-divine power that passes to a successor at the successor's dying moment, so that the current Siamorphe is always the latest in a lineage. The new holder is preferably a direct descendant of the last one, though anyone of noble blood will do. In the 13th and 14th centuries the holder was the Lady Siamorphe of Waterdeep, chosen when a riot killed her in 1256 DR. She took the role from a nobleman of Baldur's Gate.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Using Siamorphe as a Deity
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice her as the patron of the nobility. A sage, a priest or a regular at the bar says she is a demigod who gave nobles the right to rule on the condition that they rule well and for the good of those beneath them. They add that noble houses keep her shrines at home, that her Scions advise the great families and run their ceremonies, and that the Cassalanters are known for their ancestral devotion to her. How commoners judge the nobles' claim is unsourced. [research]
> - **Waterdeep:** The Major Temples of Waterdeep table in *Dragon Heist*, printed with the North Ward, has no Siamorphe row. No campaign file mentions the wiki's Chapel and Chalice of the Divine Right, the Assumbar family or the Street of the Singing Dolphin. The wiki sets her holy day, the Divine Pageantry, in the Sea Ward and North Ward, and gives the chapel no ward, so the chapel's ward is [research]. Whether the Pageantry is still kept in 1492 DR is also [research]. The one house the sources name as hers is the Cassalanters' villa in the Sea Ward, and what the villa holds of hers is under Secret and GM Notes.
> - **A cleric PC:** The wiki lists the domains Knowledge, Law, Nobility, Planning and Pride (3e). 5etools has no entry. Order Domain `(derived; Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)` is the best fit, because Law and Nobility point to rule and command, and its Voice of Authority and Order's Demand features suit a noble charged with ruling well. Knowledge Domain `(derived; Heroes of Faerûn)` is the alternative, since Knowledge is one of her listed domains. The Divine Order that fits is Protector `(derived)`, since her teaching charges the powerful with the good of those beneath them. Her 3e favored weapon is Noble Might, a scepter or light mace. The sources place no Scion in play and no temple a PC could join.
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of the Crown `(derived; Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide)`, since that oath is sworn to civilization, to a sovereign and to a deity of law, and the faith binds rank to duty and asks nobles to rule well even against their own wishes. Oath of Devotion is the alternative for a paladin who serves the ideal of justice and order more than the throne.
> - **A warlock PC:** No warlock connection in the sources.
> - **Campaign ties:** The Cassalanters are "publicly known for its ancestral devotion to Siamorphe" (the Cassalanters organization page, Overview). **Cassalanter Villa** uses her statue, her sun symbols, her holy symbol and a mace consecrated to her. The Order of the Gauntlet hands out the holy symbol in **The Shrine on Aveen Street** and **Whitehawk**. The details are under Secret and GM Notes.
> - **Never reveal:** That the temple beneath Cassalanter Villa was hers and is now dedicated to Asmodeus, that the family's public devotion to her is cover, or what her holy symbol does in the crypt. A player who asks about Siamorphe gets the Overview, and the party's own evidence adds the rest.

## Overview

Siamorphe is the demigod of nobility, and her worship is strongest among the noble families of Waterdeep and their counselors. Her faith teaches that the privileges and leisure of rank exist so that a noble can be educated to rule well, for the good of those beneath them, even when duty cuts against personal wishes. Her priests, the Scions of Siamorphe, advise noble houses and conduct their ceremonies, and most of her temples are small shrines kept inside noble homes. The Cassalanters, one of the city's most prominent families, are known for their ancestral devotion to her.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Secret
> - **The temple was hers:** The temple beneath Cassalanter Villa was a temple of Siamorphe until Victoro and Ammalia's devil-worship desecrated it, and its great chapel is now dedicated to Asmodeus (*Dragon Heist*).
> - **The statue:** The thirty-foot statue of Asmodeus in the ceremonial hall was carved from a Siamorphe figure. *Dragon Heist* says the family recast it with stone shape. The original posture was open-armed and welcoming, and the new one has its arms extended in command, with seams at the shoulders and neck where the stone was reworked.
> - **The devotion above is cover:** The family's public devotion to her sits over the temple (see **Asmodeus**). The villa above still shows her signs, and the ballroom mosaic is unchanged.
> - **Caladorn Cassalanter:** The ghost of Victoro's father was a Masked Lord and hero of the North who devoted his life to Siamorphe. He is bound in the family crypt, off the temple's anterior vestibule, with three specters. His plaque reads "Servant of Siamorphe in his time," and he knows the statue was his wife's image of the goddess before the family defaced it.
> - **The mace:** Caladorn carried a mace of disruption as a Masked Lord, and it was consecrated in Siamorphe's name. He tells the party that she is still in it.
> - **The holy symbol:** A Siamorphe holy symbol, worn or presented, halts the specters and calms the ghost. Caladorn then becomes lucid and hands over his +1 plate armor and the mace. A party without one must fight the specters, which cannot follow into his chamber.
> - **Who knows (secrecy rule):** Nobody outside the household and the cult knows the family worships Asmodeus before the party finds out. Chaplain Ysolt Brannock of the Order of the Gauntlet consecrates the symbol, says she does not know what it will do, and refuses to discuss what the family worships. Savra Belabranta chose the symbol because Siamorphe was the Cassalanters' ancestral patron, and she suspects that anything of the old faith left in the house will answer it, though she has only suspicion to go on.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Where the party gets the symbol:** At the end of **The Shrine on Aveen Street**, Chaplain Ysolt Brannock gives one consecrated symbol to the party, and the members choose who carries it. It is a brass sun with eight spokes on a leather cord. **Cassalanter Villa** reads it from the party's possessions.
> - **The fallback:** If that mission has not run, a Whitehawk member gets one from Savra Belabranta once the party holds evidence from **Faction Outposts** or has marked **Cassalanter Register Filed**. A party that took nothing from the Aveen Street cellar gets no symbol from the mission, and Whitehawk is its other source.
> - **Identifying her:** A DC 12 Intelligence (Religion) check identifies the ballroom floor's sun symbol as Siamorphe's, and the same check identifies the carved symbol in the wine cellar as hers, the god of nobility. A DC 14 Intelligence (History) check on Caladorn's portrait in the entrance hall identifies him as a former Masked Lord and Siamorphe devotee. The detail registers without a check if the party already holds the symbol.
> - **Signs of her that remain above the temple:** The ballroom floor is a gold and white tile mosaic of her sun symbol, five feet across and unchanged. The wine cellar's west wall carries a sun with eight spokes in relief, which marks the secret door. Elzerina Cassalanter knows only "a funny door behind a sun." The Stone's vision at **Sea Maidens Faire** shows a Sea Ward villa with a sun symbol marking a hidden door, and it does not name her. The twelve sarcophagi in the crypt bear the family crest, a sun over a scale in her heraldic tradition.
> - **The painting:** The eighteen-foot painting of Asmodeus in the anterior vestibule is fresh oil paint over a portrait of Siamorphe. A DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals the original silhouette, a woman in noble dress with her arms open in welcome. A party that has met Caladorn and heard him ask to "restore my family's honor" reads this as deliberate desecration of the whole temple.
> - **Striking the statue:** The mace reacts at once, because the consecration still lives in the stone. The Asmodeus features crack away over about six seconds and show fragments of Siamorphe's original face beneath. The four resolutions of the pact are written in **Cassalanter Villa**, not here.
> - **No Chosen, no intervention:** No file has Siamorphe appear or speak, and none names a Chosen of hers. Her reach in the story is the symbol, the mace and the signs left in the villa.

**Tone:** Dignified and a little stiff, like the nobles who follow her. Describe what she asks of rulers before what she gives them, and leave the temple beneath the villa out of everything above the Secret.
