# Yondalla

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the deity entry for Yondalla, the Protector and Provider and head of the halfling pantheon, for players who want a halfling cleric, a paladin or a devout homebody, and for a GM who needs a power behind a halfling shrine, a harvest supper or a hidden burrow home.
> - **Where it is used:** Yondalla never appears in person. The campaign gives her one shrine, on Larkhaven Street in the Southern Ward, and uses it as a research stop in a Doom Raiders mission.
> - **Featured in:** The Doom Raiders mission **The Missing Snobeedle**. No quest uses her as a god, and neither published adventure names her.
> - **Spoiler limit:** everything above the `## Overview` may be read by the GM only. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier. Nothing about Yondalla is secret, but the shrine's caretaker hints at where Dasher Snobeedle is, and that hint sits in the GM Notes.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Yondalla, and records the one shrine and the one mission the campaign has tied to her so that no later page invents more.

*The Protector and Provider*

- **Title:** Goddess of fertility and protection (SCAG, 2014). The PHB, 2014, prints "Halfling goddess of fertility and protection", and the MTF entry has no title field.
- **Other Names:** The Nurturing Matriarch, the Blessed One (wiki, citing *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e, and *Demihuman Deities*, 2e). Her followers and the things of her faith are called Yondallan (wiki, *Demihuman Deities*, 2e).
- **Pantheon:** Halfling (MTF, 2014; SCAG, 2014), and its head. The PHB, 2014, files her with the nonhuman deities, and the wiki calls the pantheon Yondalla's Children.
- **Areas of Concern:** Fertility, protection, the harvest and good luck, children and family, and the hiding and defense of halfling villages (derived from the SCAG title, the MTF text and the wiki portfolios; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Life, Twilight (SCAG, 2014; MTF and the PHB, 2014, agree). The wiki's 3e list is Family, Gluttony, Good, Halfling, Law and Protection.
- **Symbol:** Cornucopia on a shield (SCAG, 2014). The PHB, 2014, gives a shield, and MTF gives a cornucopia.
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good (SCAG, 2014; MTF, the PHB and the wiki agree)
- **Plane:** Green Fields (wiki, 3e). The wiki's 2e row gives Mount Celestia, in Venya, with Green Fields as her realm. SCAG, the PHB and MTF give no plane.
- **Typical Followers:** Halflings (wiki, 5e). The wiki's 3e row adds children, leaders, paladins and parents.

> Look after your family and your village, and share what you grow.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Yondalla is the head of the halfling pantheon, and MTF presents her as the goddess who built it. The wiki calls her the matriarch of the pantheon and of the halfling race, and rates her a greater deity in its 2e and 3e rows. Its 4e and 5e rows give no rank.
- **Character:** The wiki describes a charming, friendly goddess who is an example to every halfling. Almost all halflings share her curiosity, loyalty and sense of mischief (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **What she is credited with:** Halflings thank her for the spring in their step and the excitement of knowing that luck is on their side. They credit her when a pumpkin grows to enormous size or a garden yields twice the usual carrots. When a halfling trips, slides down a hillside and lands on a nugget of gold, they say she has turned bad luck into good (MTF).
- **What the faith asks:** No source gives tenets, a code or rites. [research] The nearest guidance is what MTF says she did, which was to gather her people, protect them and teach them to build, plant and harvest.
- **The villages:** She made the first halfling villages and used her power to hide them in the landscape, so that most travelers pass by without a second glance. She knew that a village's bounty would tempt any brigand or monster (MTF).
- **The pantheon:** The rest of the pantheon is made of the halflings she raised to godhood because they were best at the skills halflings need to survive (MTF). The wiki's 2e row lists Arvoreen, Brandobaris, Cyrrollalee, Sheela Peryroyl and Urogalan as the gods who serve her (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e).
- **Who follows:** Halflings (wiki, 5e). The wiki's 3e row adds children, leaders, paladins and parents. No 5etools entry has a worshipers field.
- **Temples:** Her worship is widespread among halflings, yet her temples are unusually rare. Those that exist are built to look like a well-hidden halfling burrow-home and are stocked with weapons and food, so that the surrounding community can hold out against invaders. Other faiths respect her clergy for their skill at defense in spite of their small stature (wiki, no edition tagged).
- **Clerics:** Her clerics treat the short sword as a favored weapon, in honor of her sword Hornblade (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e). Powerful clerics could cast *Curse of Yondalla*, a spell kept for use against those who commit foul crimes against halflings (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e). No source gives the spell 2024 rules.
- **Prayer:** A basic prayer asks her to fill the speaker's cup with plenty and never let it run dry (wiki, citing *Baldur's Gate III*, a video game).
- **Holy days:** No source gives one, and the wiki's holy-day fields are blank. No file ties a city festival to her. [research]
- **Messengers and omens:** No source gives one. [research]
- **Aspects:** The wiki names two, Dallillia on the Sword Coast south of Waterdeep and Perissa in the Moonshaes (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e). No file uses either.
- **Chosen in the campaign:** None. No file names a Chosen of Yondalla, and the wiki names none.
- **Relations:** Wiki-sourced (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
  - She has close bonds with **Garl Glittergold**, **Corellon Larethian** and **Moradin**, the heads of the gnome, elven and dwarven pantheons. The Moradin and Corellon Larethian pages of this journal list her among their allies.
  - She loves and admires her fellow halfling gods, and she scorns evil powers, among them **Bane**, **Cyric**, **Talona**, **Talos** and the Deities of Fury.
- **Origin and rise:** MTF tells the story, and the wiki gives no more. Its History section is a stub, and it records no date, death or abdication.
  - At the dawn of the world halflings were timid wanderers scraping out a meager living. Yondalla took note of them and decided to adopt them as her people, and she gave her life to gathering and protecting them.
  - Over time she raised the most capable of them to godhood, and those legendary halflings make up the rest of the pantheon.
- **In the campaign:** Yondalla is alive and present in 1492 DR, as every power is. No file shows her acting.

> #### Using Yondalla as a Deity
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice her as the halflings' mother. A sage, a priest or a regular at the bar says she is the halfling god of fertility and protection, shown by a cornucopia on a shield. They add that she gathered the first halflings, taught them to build, plant and harvest, and hid their villages from brigands and monsters. Halflings thank her for good luck, such as a pumpkin that grew to a huge size or a nugget of gold found at the bottom of a tumble down a hill, and her temples are rare. In the Southern Ward, halflings gather at her shrine on Larkhaven Street for the evening blessing. Which of her names the city uses, how much a Waterdhavian outside the halfling community knows of her, and how the city feels about her faith is unsourced. [research]

The halfling community of the Southern Ward gathers in three places, and the shrine is one of them (`:128`).

>   - Halflings make up about 5% of the city's population (wiki, *City of Splendors* 2e, as `campaign/gazetteer/waterdeep/castle-ward.md:29` records it).
>   - No festival ties to her in the city. The wiki has no Waterdeep temple text for her.
> - **A cleric PC:** The domains are Life and Twilight (SCAG, 2014; MTF and the PHB, 2014, agree). Life Domain (derived; Player's Handbook 2024) is the best fit. It is her first domain by name, and the domain centers on the positive energy that sustains all life, which suits a god of fertility, the harvest and family. Twilight Domain (derived; Tasha's Cauldron of Everything) is the alternative, since it is her second domain by name. The Divine Order that fits is Protector (derived), because the wiki's clergy defend their communities and her temples are stocked with weapons. A hearth-priest who leaves the defense to others would take Thaumaturge instead (derived). The shrine on Larkhaven Street is the only place in the city where a cleric of hers could serve, and the mission file calls its keeper a caretaker and names no priest.
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of the Crown (derived; Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide), a good fit. The oath is sworn to the ideals of civilization and to service to a deity of law, which suits a lawful goddess who gathered her people and built their villages, and the wiki's 3e row lists paladins among her followers. Oath of Devotion (derived; Player's Handbook 2024) is the alternative, with its theme of justice and order.
> - **A warlock PC:** No warlock connection in the sources.
> - **Campaign ties:** The Doom Raiders mission **The Missing Snobeedle**. The details are in the GM Notes below.
>   - No Notable Figure names Yondalla. The halflings among them are **Broxley Fairkettle**, **Osco Salibuck**, **Kelso Fiddlewick**, **Corene Wyldath**, **Manafret Cherryport**, **Havia Quickknife** and **Mookie Plush**, and none of their pages gives a god, so this page makes none of them a worshiper.

## Overview

Yondalla is the lawful good halfling god of fertility and protection, shown by a cornucopia on a shield, and she heads the halfling pantheon. Halfling tales say she gathered the first halflings, taught them to build, plant and harvest, and hid their villages from brigands and monsters. Halflings credit her with good luck, from a giant pumpkin to a nugget of gold found in a fall. Her temples are rare, and in Waterdeep the halflings of the Southern Ward gather at a shrine of hers on Larkhaven Street.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **The Missing Snobeedle:** In this mission (Doom Raiders mission 3), Tashlyn Yafeera sends the members to find Dasher Snobeedle, the youngest son of a rich halfling family. She names the Yondalla shrine as one of the places where his mother Blossom posted notices (`m03-the-missing-snobeedle/ev-01-the-missing-snobeedle.md:57` and `overview.md:25`). The party then spends three days among the Southern Ward's halflings, and the shrine is one of three leads, with the market by the Waymoot and the rented rooms above a cooperage (`overview.md:37`).
> - **The shrine lead:** A gray-haired halfling in a plain robe is setting fresh candles when the party enters (`ev-01:166`). The Keeper of the Hearth block that follows names Tolliver Greenbottle (Unaligned, Halfling, he/him) as the shrine's caretaker. He answers at his own pace, and he answers best when the party has paid respect first (`:168-172`).
> - **The Trust mark:** A visitor who leaves an offering of at least 5 gp in the hearth box and stays for the whole blessing earns a Trust mark. Tolliver does not care for coin pushed across the offering plate to buy an answer (`:172`). The marks set the difficulty of the check that arranges the meeting with Dasher (`:132-138`).
> - **What Tolliver says:** Dasher came every rest-day evening to light a candle for his mother, until about three moons ago. On the first night of every tenday a coin appears in the hearth box that was not there the night before, and Tolliver says he knows whose hand puts it there (`:176`). He offers to leave a note in the box for whoever empties it, and he cannot make Dasher answer (`:180`).
> - **Where the lead ends:** Every lead in the ward reaches the same fact. The Shard Shunners have Dasher, and the ward will carry a request to their leader, Kelso Fiddlewick (`:128`).
> - **The other mission NPCs:** Blossom Snobeedle, Pippa Underbough, Marda Goodbarrel and Dasher have no Notable Figures pages. The file gives none of them a faith.

**Tone:** Warm and homely. Yondalla is a mother-goddess who hides her people and keeps them fed, so describe the hearth, the table and the garden before the god, and keep her at a distance, since the campaign writes no scene in which she acts. Keep the Larkhaven Street shrine a small, ordinary place.
