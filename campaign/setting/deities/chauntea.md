**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Chauntea, and says plainly that the sources give her no temple, festival role or NPC in the city.

*The Great Mother*

- **Title:** God of Agriculture (FRHoF). The page uses FRHoF's title because FRHoF is newer than SCAG and the 2014 Player's Handbook, whose printings read "Goddess of agriculture".
- **Other Names:** The Great Mother, the Grain Goddess (FRHoF). The wiki adds the Earthmother, the Golden Goddess, the Lady of the Harvest and the Mother of All Flowers, and the regional names Bhalla in Rashemen, Jannath in Netheril, Pahluruk near the Great Glacier and Grandmother among the Uthgardt.
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian
- **Areas of Concern:** Agriculture, the harvest, gardens, hearth and home, and the birth of children (derived from the FRHoF and SCAG text; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Life (SCAG, 2014)
- **Symbol:** A sheaf of grain, or a blooming rose over grain (SCAG, 2014)
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good (SCAG, 2014)
- **Plane:** Elysium (Eronia) (FRHoF)
- **Typical Followers:** Farmers, gardeners, homesteaders (FRHoF)

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Chauntea is a deity of the Faerûnian pantheon and the most widely worshiped god among the common folk of Faerûn (FRHoF). The wiki files her among the greater deities in its 1e, 2e and 3e rows, and as plain "deity" in its 5e row.
- **What the faith asks:** Her teaching is full of farming parables. It tells folk to nurture, tend and plant wherever they can, and to put something new in the ground wherever something is torn down (FRHoF). Growing and reaping, the eternal cycle, is a common theme, and destruction for its own sake is anathema to her (SCAG, 2014). The wiki adds that she urges people in civilized lands to repair what they damage, and that blight on the natural world draws her fury (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **Farm and hearth:** SCAG (2014) calls her a rural deity who is rarely prayed to behind city walls, except by kitchen gardeners. She is also the Great Mother of crib, hearth and home, so she is welcome in every house at mealtimes and at the birth of a child. FRHoF says city people pray to her at those two moments.
- **Clergy and temples:** Her temples keep a great body of practical lore about farming and cultivation. Her priests work closely with rural communities, dress humbly in beige and muted green, and are happy to dig in the dirt beside the farmers (FRHoF; SCAG, 2014).
- **Seeds of Cultivation (FRHoF):** A nomadic order that eases the pain of communities struck by blight or famine. Many of its members know crop spells such as *Plant Growth*. Because a blight is often a curse on the land or the work of Talona's cultists, the order welcomes folk of every ability, including those who carry swords. It recruits from farming communities, and a city dweller must first spend a year tending crops in a rural one. A new member shares a home-cooked meal with the order and receives a wooden brooch of the rose over the sheaf. Members can lodge at any temple of Chauntea for as long as they wish. Its foremost enemies are Talona's disciples and the followers of Talos, and it sometimes clashes with extremist druids of Silvanus who want to turn farmland back into wilderness.
- **Holy days:** Some of her worshipers arrange freshly cut flowers as offerings at Greengrass in exchange for a quick growing season. At Highharvestide, folk across Faerûn thank her at sunset for the year's bounty as winter approaches (FRHoF). The wiki lists no holy days for her in any edition.
- **Messengers and omens:** She seldom appears to mortals and sends no divine messengers. On rare occasions she speaks through words scrawled on freshly tilled soil or through wind in the crops that sounds faintly like whispers (FRHoF). The wiki adds sudden, fast-growing flowers and motes of light as signs of her presence (*Deity Do's and Don'ts*, 3e).
- **Chosen in the campaign:** None. No file names a Chosen of Chauntea. FRHoF says she rarely picks one, and that a Chosen dresses and acts like any other follower.
- **Regional forms:** The wiki records her as Bhalla in Rashemen, where she and Mielikki and Mystra are the three goddesses called The Three (*Unapproachable East*; edition not stated). The druids of the Moonshae Isles worship the Earthmother, whom some mainlanders count as an aspect of Chauntea. The Moonshae Ffolk say she is simply the Earthmother and always will be (SCAG, 2014). Among the Uthgardt only women worship her, as the Grandmother (*The Savage Frontier*).
- **Divine realm:** Great Mother's Garden on Eronia in the 2e sources, and Green Fields in the 4e sources (wiki). FRHoF gives only the plane.
- **Relations:** Wiki-sourced.
  - She has strong ties to other nature powers such as Shiallia and **Mielikki**, and she was close to **Silvanus**, though that closeness faded over time (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). The wiki calls her the tamer parallel of the Forest Father, since she rules cultivated land and he rules the wilds (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
  - She and **Lathander** were lovers for centuries (*Tymora's Luck*, novel). The **Gond** page records a friendly rivalry between her and the god of invention.
  - Her most despised enemy is **Talona**, the Lady of Pestilence, and she is always in conflict with **Talos**. She also opposes **Malar** and **Bane** (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book named on each fact.
  - She is one of the eldest gods of Faerûn. She was born when Toril was made in the primeval battles between **Shar** and **Selûne**, and Selûne nurtured her with light, helped by Mystryl (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). Before she was the Great Mother she was Jannath, the Earthmother, who haunted wild places and herds of animals (same source).
  - In the late fifteenth century DR, after Malar's apparent death in the Spellplague, she blessed Argon Bael's effort to bring the Beastlord back. She said her reasons lay beyond mortal ideas of good and evil (*The Rose of Sarifal*, novel).
- **In the campaign:** Chauntea is alive and present in 1492 DR. No file treats her as absent.

> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice her as the goddess behind the table. A sage, a priest or a regular at the bar says she is the Great Mother, god of farms and harvests and of the hearth, and that Waterdeep's bread and grain come from the fields outside its walls. They say a family thanks her over a meal and at a birth, and that farmers and kitchen gardeners pray to her more than townspeople do. They add that she is a quiet goddess who wants small acts of devotion and not grand shows. How the city feels about her beyond that is unsourced. [research]
> - **Waterdeep:** Chauntea has no temple or shrine in the sources.
>   - The Dragon Heist Major Temples table has no Chauntea row. Its "All deities" row, Holyhands House in the North Ward, covers her by its own wording (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:30923-30925`).
>   - No campaign file places a priest, a shrine or a worshiper of hers in the city, and none names a farmer, grain merchant or charity worker as a follower.

> - **A cleric PC:** The domain is Life (SCAG, 2014; PHB, 2014). Life Domain (derived; Player's Handbook, 2024) is the fit, and it is exact. No other available domain matches her portfolio as well. The Divine Order that fits is Thaumaturge (derived), because her clergy are caretakers who lean on spells like *Plant Growth* and on practical lore. A cleric who fights blight cultists with the Seeds of Cultivation would take Protector instead (derived).
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of the Ancients (derived; Player's Handbook, 2024). The oath to preserve life and growth suits her creed of nurturing and replanting, and *Plant Growth* is one of its oath spells. The fit is loose, since FRHoF lists no paladins among her typical followers and the Seeds of Cultivation are the only order the sources name.
> - **A warlock PC:** No warlock connection in the sources.
> - **Campaign ties:** No campaign file uses this power. The published *Dragon Heist* and *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* do not name her either.

## Overview

Chauntea, the Great Mother, is the god of farming and the harvest. Farmers and gardeners pray to her for a good harvest, and city families thank her at the table and when a child is born. The city has no temple to her, though Holyhands House in the North Ward welcomes worshipers of every god. Her faithful offer cut flowers at Greengrass in spring and give thanks for the harvest at Highharvestide in autumn.

**Tone:** Warm, patient and a little plain. Chauntea is a god of small acts, so describe the meal, the garden and the field before any larger power, and keep her at a distance, since the campaign writes no scene in which she acts.
