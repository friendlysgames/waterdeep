*The Quiet One*

- **Title:** God of Peace (FRHoF). The page uses FRHoF's title because FRHoF is newer than SCAG and the 2014 Player's Handbook, whose printings read "Goddess of peace". The wiki and every other source use "she".
- **Other Names:** The Guardian of Groves, The Mother of the Waters (FRHoF); the Mother Guardian of Groves, the Goddess of Shining Waters, the Goddess of Singing Waters, the Green Goddess, the Lady of Summer (wiki)
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian
- **Areas of Concern:** Peace, stillness, healing and comfort, waterfalls, springs, pools and quiet glades (derived from the FRHoF and SCAG text and the wiki portfolio lines; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Life, Nature, Peace (SCAG, 2014)
- **Symbol:** A waterfall plunging into a still pool (SCAG, 2014)
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good (SCAG, 2014)
- **Plane:** Elysium (Eronia) (FRHoF)
- **Typical Followers:** Druids, pacifists, the dying (FRHoF)

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Eldath is a goddess of the Faerûnian pantheon. The wiki files her as a demipower in its 1e row and as a lesser deity in its 2e and 3e rows, and its FRHoF row says only "deity".
- **What the faith asks:** Her followers hold to peace and quiet, and many are pacifists or people troubled by violence they have seen or lived through (FRHoF). The wiki's 2e dogma allows violence only in defense of themselves, their friends and loved ones, and pools and groves. Worshipers may be of any alignment, but her priests are always non-evil (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e). The wiki adds that many people come to her to overcome memories of a violent past, citing SCAG (2014).
- **What she offers:** Comfort, healing and stillness. Water she has blessed heals the sick, eases troubled minds and comforts the dying (FRHoF). SCAG (2014) adds that it cures madness, and the wiki says it gives the dying a natural passing.
- **Holy sites and offerings:** Most rural settlements have a pond or a glade that locals ascribe to her, kept as a place of quiet reflection (FRHoF). A pond or a stream near the site holds the offerings, and in a glade people tie them to a bush or a tree. The offerings are broken weapons and objects tied to old arguments, discarded with a prayer for peace. In the wiki's 2e account most followers live in forest communities or woodside cottages, with open-air holy places far from city life.
- **Clergy:** Her priests do not organize into large sects. They wander between holy sites and shrines to keep them serene, and they work alongside druids (FRHoF). Striking a priest of Eldath is taboo, and killing one is said to bring great misfortune. Some priests avoid conflict altogether and others step in quickly to soothe it. They preside over negotiations and certify treaties, but they cannot force anyone into harmony (FRHoF). The wiki's 2e account gives a loose hierarchy in which priests report to a local high priest, and says her druids pray once a day at a time chosen after reflection (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
- **Orders:** The Yielding Way is an order of monks devoted to peace and protection, and the Tall Trees are a circle of druids in the High Forest (FRHoF). No campaign or adventure file places either one in Waterdeep.
- **Chosen in the campaign:** None. FRHoF calls her Chosen Lotuses. They wear a lotus blossom that never wilts while they have her favor, and they serve as ambassadors and mediators whose presence can calm violence and rage. No file names a Chosen of Eldath.
- **Messengers:** When she speaks to her followers she appears as a serene, humanlike face formed from flowing water in a pool or a waterfall. She may also send a Centaur Warden or a Merfolk Wavebender with her instructions (FRHoF).
- **Omens:** The wiki lists whispering winds and a green healing aura as signs of her presence, citing *Deity Do's and Don'ts* and naming no edition.
- **How she appears:** As a slender, translucent woman about ten feet tall, shown in religious art as a human, wild elf, sea elf, dryad or nereid, in green silk with bare feet (wiki, *Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
- **Holy days:** The only calendar holy day of her church is the Greening, a gathering and festival at Greengrass. It is preceded by Firstflow, a festival held at varying times when the ice breaks and begins to flow at the end of winter (wiki, *Faiths & Avatars*, 2e; the 3e row lists the Greening). FRHoF gives no holy days. The campaign calendar lists Greengrass (Tarsakh 30 and Mirtul 1) as the start of spring, and no campaign file connects it to her.
- **Divine realm:** True Grove, on Eronia, the second layer of Elysium (wiki, 2e and 3e; *Player's Guide to Faerûn*). The wiki's 3e row gives the House of Nature as her home plane.
- **Relations:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - She served **Silvanus** (wiki 1e and 2e rows), loyally though she found him intimidating at times, together with **Mielikki**, whom *Faiths & Pantheons* (3e) calls her sister. SCAG (2014) says Eldath is considered Mielikki's sister, and the wiki's Mielikki page adds that *Faiths & Avatars* (2e) shows the family ties to be mortal misunderstandings.
  - She had close relationships with **Chauntea**, **Selûne** and **Lathander**, who shared some of her interests (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
  - With Mielikki she was said to bring retribution on anyone who deliberately set fires in the High Forest (*Silver Marches*, edition not stated).
  - She was opposed to **Tempus** but not his enemy. He thought her naive, respected her convictions, and punished any of his followers who harmed her faithful or desecrated her shrines (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
  - Druids count her among the First Circle (SCAG, 2014). A character in the novel *The Jewel of Turmish* calls her a healer who serves Silvanus and helps the druids of the Emerald Enclave.
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book named on each fact.
  - The wiki describes her as a lake spirit (*Dragon* 376) who was often worshiped as a god of peace and calm waters (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). Among the Bedine of Anauroch she is the Mother of the Waters (*Anauroch*).
  - Bedine legend says the sun goddess At'ar, jealous of her beauty, imprisoned her at the oasis of Elah'zad in the Anauroch desert (*Anauroch*; *The Parched Sea*; *Faiths & Avatars*).
  - The wiki page has no Spellplague or Second Sundering text for her, and its 4e rows are blank.
- **In the campaign:** Eldath is alive and present in 1492 DR. No file treats her as dead or absent.

## Overview

Eldath is the god of peace and quiet water, and her followers call her the Quiet One. Waterfalls, springs, pools and still glades are her places, and water she has blessed is said to heal the sick and comfort the dying. Her priests wander between village ponds and woodland shrines, and it is taboo to strike one. No temple of hers is known in Waterdeep, and her one known worshiper in the city is Avi, the armorer at Steam and Steel in Trollskull Alley.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Who Avi is:** A water genasi armorsmith, neutral good, who uses the **Priest** stat block (`03-avi.md:6`). She runs Steam and Steel with her husband Embric, the fire genasi weaponsmith, and the forge's back gate opens onto the same courtyard as Trollskull Manor (`campaign/quests/act-i/trollskull-alley/ev-01-welcome-to-trollskull-alley.md:24`). She quenches hot steel with her water magic (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:3406`).
> - **How the faith shows at the table:** On her page Avi stands between an attacker and anyone unarmed and will not strike first (`03-avi.md:24`). Her voice profile has her bless armor as she hands it over, say "Eldath keep you", and refuse to sell armor to anyone who is shopping for a fight (`.claude/skills/character-voices/voices/trollskull-community.md:51-53`).
> - **When the party shops:** Her pacifism puts her at odds with adventuring customers (`03-avi.md:26`). Play that as a sale she makes after a plain question about what the armor is for. She turns away only a buyer who says he is shopping for a fight.
> - **Where she appears:** In **Trollskull Alley** she is one of the neighbors who attend the Grand Opening (`ev-06-the-grand-opening.md:41`). In **Fireball!** she is one of the supporting witnesses, alongside Embric and Tally Fellbranch, and she confirms the timing of the blast (`campaign/quests/act-ii/fireball/ev-01-the-fireball.md:168`). Neither scene involves Eldath.
> - **What Avi cannot supply:** She is not a priest of an organized church and does not introduce the party to one. No file gives her a Chosen, a shrine, a holy day or a contact among Eldath's clergy.

**Tone:** Calm and plain. Eldath is a goddess of stillness, so describe the water and the quiet before any power, and keep her at a distance, since the campaign writes no scene in which she acts.
