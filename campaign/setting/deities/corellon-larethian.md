*The Creator of the Elves*

- **Title:** God of Art and Magic (SCAG, 2014; the PHB, 2014, prints "Elf deity of art and magic")
- **Other Names:** The Protector, Coronal of Arvandor, Ruler of All Elves, First of the Seldarine, Preserver of Life (wiki; *Demihuman Deities*, 2e; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e), and Father of Elvenkind (wiki; *Dragon* 408). Orcs insult him as the Big Fairy and the Lord of Cowardice (wiki).
- **Pantheon:** Elven, the Seldarine (MTF, 2014; SCAG, 2014)
- **Areas of Concern:** Elves, art, magic, music, poetry and crafts, and war in defense of elvenkind (derived from the MTF text and the wiki portfolio; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Arcana, Light (SCAG, 2014). MTF (2014) adds Life and War, and the PHB (2014) lists Light and Arcana.
- **Symbol:** Crescent moon (SCAG, 2014). MTF and the PHB (2014) give a quarter moon or starburst.
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good (SCAG, 2014; MTF and the PHB agree)
- **Plane:** Arvandor, on Arborea (wiki; the 5e row gives the plane as Arborea and the realm as Arvandor)
- **Typical Followers:** Elves, especially elf wizards (MTF, 2014)

> Live freely and kindly, and solve your own troubles before you pray for help.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Corellon is the head of the Seldarine, the elven pantheon, and the creator of the elves. The wiki rates him a greater deity in its 1e to 4e rows and a deity in its 5e row.
- **His nature:** MTF describes him as chaos and beauty together, as changeable as a breeze or a brook, quick to anger and just as quick to forgive and forget. He loves magic, artistry, nature and freedom. Anyone who has felt his presence calls it a joy like no other, followed by a deep melancholy when it fades (MTF, 2014).
- **What the faith asks:** Very little. There are no complex rituals, frequent ceremonies or regular prayers. Corellon wants his followers to enjoy life, try new things, pursue what they imagine and be kind to others, and in return they are expected to deal with their own problems instead of praying for help in every crisis. Every elf carries these precepts, because all elves descend from fragments of him (MTF, 2014).
- **Seeking him:** When elves ask how to sense their creator, priests often tell them to know themselves first (MTF, 2014).
- **Worship:** Services are held in natural stone amphitheaters or bowl-shaped forest clearings. Each worshiper shows reverence in a personal way, as long as it joins the others in one display, so a gathering feels more like a festival than a service (MTF, 2014).
- **Wizards:** Many elf wizards honor him and mark their spellbooks and towers with his symbols. Some hold that he is raw magic itself, the primal force under the multiverse, rather than the Weave (MTF, 2014).
- **Orders:** The wiki lists knightly orders of the faith (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e). They include the Fellowship of the Forgotten Flower, which recovers lost elven relics from abandoned realms, and the Knights of the Golden Wyrm, the Swords of the Seldarine and the Swords of Evereska.
- **The Mysteries of Arvandor:** This is a rare summons that elves recognize as a call from their creator. Chosen elves receive dreams and waking visions that send them to a gathering, sometimes across hundreds of miles or across planes. Each joins Corellon's body for a time and returns transformed, and not every elf comes back. Shrines to the Mysteries are caves whose ceilings carry painted or carved stars (MTF, 2014).
- **The blessed of Corellon:** Androgynous elves are proclaimed blessed of Corellon, and many of his chief priests are among them. The rarest can change their sex after a Long Rest, a gift that elves of every kind celebrate except drow, who call it a curse. A drow who manifests it usually flees to the surface to shelter among his faithful (MTF, 2014).
- **Form and pronouns:** The sources write "he". The wiki says he has no biological sex and can appear as male or female, though he is conventionally an androgynous male elf in a sky-blue cloak, with a crescent moon amulet and dazzling gauntlets (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e). *Dragon Heist* says he is neither male nor female (see Campaign ties).
- **Messengers and omens:** The wiki lists many servants, among them aasimar, gold and silver dragons, unicorns, lythari, dryads and nymphs, and says the whole Seldarine favors agathinons, asuras and elder treants (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e). His manifestations are a blue aura, shooting stars, a rising blue-tinted crescent moon or a blue star (*Deity Do's and Don'ts*, no edition tagged).
- **Holy day:** Shieldmeet (wiki, 4e; the 2e, 3e and 5e rows are blank). In 1492 DR it is the leap day after Flamerule 30, past the campaign's Flamerule 11 deadline, and no file ties a city observance to him.
- **Family and the Seldarine:** Corellon is close to most of the Seldarine, with the exception of Fenmarel Mestarine, who was Lolth's partner in adultery (wiki; *Demihuman Deities*, 2e). After the banishment of his first wife he married Angharradh (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e), and the wiki's divine-realm section names Sehanine Moonbow as his consort in the Crescent Grove (*Player's Guide to Faerûn*; *On Hallowed Ground*). Of the three children he had with his first wife, only Vandria Gilmadrith stayed with the Seldarine (*Races of the Wild*, 3e).
- **Allies beyond the Seldarine:** Faerûnian powers of nature and magic, and the leaders of the dwarven, gnomish and halfling pantheons (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). Those leaders are **Moradin**, **Garl Glittergold** and **Yondalla** (derived). He is on good terms with **Azuth**, **Mystra** and, through Sehanine, **Savras** (*Magic of Faerûn*), and his daughter **Eilistraee** is his ally, although the relationship is polite and distant (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e).
- **Enemies:** He counts the whole Dark Seldarine as enemies, among them **Lolth**, his former wife, Vhaeraun, his son, and Selvetarm, his grandson, with only Eilistraee as an exception (wiki; *Demihuman Deities*, 2e; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). His other enemies are the orc and goblinoid pantheons, including **Gruumsh**, and **Bane**, **Cyric**, **Malar** and **Talos** (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - In elven myth the first elves came from drops of his blood mixed with the tears of Sehanine, or of Angharradh in some tellings (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). Another tradition makes the dark, high and wood elves from his tears of sorrow, beauty and mirth in the Triune Harmony, when Sehanine, Corellon and Araushnee lived in peace (*Dragon* 394, 4e era).
  - He was once only Corellon. He took the name Larethian by absorbing a god of that name, mastering his mind and annexing his church and followers (Ed Greenwood's Patreon). Before that his Ysgardian aspect was a god of the hunt who fought Malar, and it faded after he founded Arvandor in Arborea (Ed Greenwood's Patreon; *Planes of Chaos*).
  - Gruumsh broke his sword Sahandrian at a parley. Sehanine restored it, and Corellon put out one of Gruumsh's eyes but would not kill a wounded, unarmed enemy (*Evermeet: Island of Elves*, no edition tagged). Orcs deny that he took the eye (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e), and a heretical teaching makes the two gods twin sons of a dead primogenitor god (*Dragon* 408, 4e era).
  - In the War of the Seldarine his wife Araushnee and their son Vhaeraun plotted his death with a cursed scabbard and Eilistraee's arrows. He survived, Araushnee was banished, Vhaeraun was exiled, and Eilistraee went into exile at his request (*Evermeet: Island of Elves*, no edition tagged). FRHoF says that after she tried to overthrow Corellon with an army of spirits she was banished to the Abyss, where she took a spider form and the name Lolth (FRHoF, in the Lolth entry).
  - Around -17600 DR, with other elven gods, he helped the non-dark elves make a land free of dark elves, and Evermeet rose with the First Sundering as a side effect. Around -10000 DR his magic, channeled through priests and high mages, turned the dark elves into drow (*Evermeet: Island of Elves*; *The Grand History of the Realms*, 4e).
  - Before 714 DR the Seldarine under Corellon began the Retreat, a compulsion that drew elves from Faerûn to Evermeet, and most of his orders left (*Demihuman Deities*, 2e; *Races of Faerûn*).
  - After the Spellplague of 1385 DR he stayed a greater deity while other Seldarine lost power (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e).
- **In the campaign:** Corellon is alive and present in 1492 DR, as every power is. No file shows him acting.

> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice him as the elves' own god, generous and a little distant. A sage, a priest or a regular at the bar says Corellon Larethian is the elven god of art and magic, that he made the elves and leads their gods, the Seldarine, and that elves keep his faith loosely, with few rites and no rule against enjoying life. ## Overview

Corellon Larethian is the elven god of art and magic, who made the elves and leads the Seldarine, their pantheon. His faith asks little of the faithful, who may honor him in any way they like. Many elf wizards mark their spellbooks and towers with his crescent moon or starburst. In Waterdeep the Temple of the Seldarine in the Castle Ward honors him with the other elven gods.

> - **The Temple of the Seldarine:** The campaign has no scene, priest or hook for it. If a party asks for an elven priest, the sources give none, and this page names none.

**Tone:** Light and unhurried. Corellon asks little of his followers, so describe the freedom and the art of his faith before any duty, and keep the melancholy of meeting him in what elves say about it. Keep him at a distance, since the campaign writes no scene in which he acts, and keep Fala's shop a warm, ordinary place.
