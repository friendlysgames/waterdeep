# Istishia

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the deity entry for Istishia, the Water Lord and elemental god of water, for players who want a cleric, a water genasi or a sailor with a distant, indifferent patron, and for a GM who needs to know what the sources say about him.
> - **Where it is used:** Istishia never appears in person. No campaign file names him, and no file places a temple, shrine or priest of his in Waterdeep. The page records the one *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* marid who fits his temperament without being tied to him, so that no later page invents more.
> - **Featured in:** No quest. His name appears only on other Deities pages, such as the one for **Valkur**.
> - **Spoiler limit:** everything above the `## Overview` may be read by the GM only. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier. Nothing about Istishia is secret.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Istishia, and says plainly that no source gives him a temple, a festival or a worshiper in Waterdeep.

*The Water Lord*

- **Title:** God of Water (SCAG, 2014). No FRHoF entry exists for him.
- **Other Names:** The Water Lord, King of the Water Elementals (wiki, citing *Faiths & Pantheons*); God-King of Undines (wiki, citing the *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 2e). Ri'dea'shia is his aspect (wiki, citing *Sea of Fallen Stars*). His followers are called Istishian (wiki, citing *Faiths & Avatars*).
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian (SCAG, 2014). The wiki also files him with the Zakharan pantheon.
- **Areas of Concern:** Water in every form, purification, change, and the elemental plane of water (derived from the wiki portfolio and the 4e description; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Tempest (SCAG, 2014)
- **Symbol:** A wave (SCAG, 2014)
- **Alignment:** Neutral (SCAG, 2014)
- **Plane:** The Elemental Plane of Water, with a divine realm there called the Sea of Timelessness (wiki, 2e). FRHoF gives no plane for him.
- **Typical Followers:** Sailors, pirates, water elementals, water genasi and some aquatic elves (wiki, citing *Nightwatch in the Living City*)

> Use what water gives, and do not expect it to care how you use it.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Istishia is the elemental god of water and a power of the Faerûnian pantheon (SCAG, 2014). The wiki rates him a lesser power in 1e, a greater deity in 2e and 3e, a primordial in 4e and a deity in 5e.
- **The elemental lords:** The wiki says the 1e *Cyclopedia of the Realms* featured him as one of the elemental lords of the Forgotten Realms. Its Akadi page counts four elemental deities (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e), and its Kossuth page speaks of the "other elemental lords". The other three are Akadi, Grumbar and Kossuth (derived).
- **What he is:** Other powers govern oceans, pools, streams and storms, and Istishia is far more abstract. He is the god of water itself and not of any body or state of it, mutable and dynamic, and he stands for constant change (wiki, *Faiths & Avatars*, 2e). The wiki's 4e description adds purification (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*).
- **Temperament:** He is slippery and dispassionate, and most people cannot follow his reasons. He gave the world an atmosphere for life and a vital resource, but not life itself, and he does not care how people use his gifts. He is aloof toward his own followers and ignores or favors them on a whim, so that his unpredictability is itself predictable (wiki, *Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
- **What the faith asks and offers:** No source gives a doctrine, a code, a rite or a blessing. [research] The wiki's page has an infobox that marks a church and no text that describes it.
- **Who follows:** Sailors, pirates, water elementals, water genasi, some aquatic elves, and others who feel a bond with the ocean's destructive power (wiki, citing *Nightwatch in the Living City*). The wiki's 3e row lists bards, elemental archons of water, sailors and travelers.
- **Centers of worship:** The wiki places his shrines and temples in Calimshan. Keltar has a shrine called the Steps of Istishia, built thousands of years before the fourteenth century DR (*Empires of the Shining Sea*). Calimport has the Palace of the Cresting Wave, a massive seven-story temple (*Calimport*).
- **Worship in Zakhara:** The Zakharans count him among the cold gods of the elements. They see these powers as uncaring toward people and opposed to the land's culture of Enlightenment, and few Zakharans would worship a cold god to gain power (wiki, citing *Land of Fate*).
- **Clergy and orders:** No source describes his priests, vestments or orders. [research]
- **Holy days:** None known (wiki, 3e row). The 5e row is blank, and no file ties a city festival to him.
- **Messengers and omens:** No source gives either. [research]
- **How he appears:** He is shown as anything from a drop of rain or an ocean wave to a water weird or a giant water elemental. When he manifests he is a column of water 40 to 60 feet tall and 20 to 30 feet thick. It moves over or through any obstacle with the sound of surf on a shore, and it can split into several parts to confuse his enemies. His voice is at once rushing water, a babbling brook and gentle rain (wiki, *Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
- **Divine realm:** The wiki names the Sea of Timelessness on the Elemental Plane of Water (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e). His followers believe he encompasses the whole plane (*The Inner Planes*), and he keeps no court in the usual sense. The wiki's 4e infobox gives the realm as the Cresting Spires (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*).
- **Chosen in the campaign:** None. No source names a Chosen of Istishia.
- **Relations:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - He has ties to powers of water but no strong relationships. These include Deep Sashelas, Eadro, **Eldath**, Persana, Shekinester, Surminare, Trishina, **Umberlee**, **Valkur** and the Water Lion (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e; *The Inner Planes*).
  - He works loosely with Sashelas, Trishina and Eldath to keep the life cycles of the sea going (*Sea of Fallen Stars*). He has no interest in Umberlee's storms or Valkur's sailors (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e).
  - He opposes **Kossuth** in every way (*The Inner Planes*). The wiki's Kossuth page says Kossuth was vehemently opposed to Istishia and his clergy (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e).
  - He watches the struggle between the archomentals of water, Ben-Hadar and Olhydra, and would presumably step in if one of them gained a real chance to control elemental water (*The Inner Planes*).
- **Origin and rise:** No source gives an in-world origin or a dated event. The wiki's history section is a stub. The only edition note is that he is a primordial from the Elemental Chaos in 4e (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*).
- **In the campaign:** Istishia is present in 1492 DR, as every power is. The 4e primordial status is another edition's account, and this page does not use it as his present rank (see Draft Notes).

> #### Using Istishia as a Deity
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice him as a power that is far away and does not care. A sage, a priest or a sailor at the bar says he is the Water Lord, the elemental god of water itself, and not of any sea or river, and that his sign is a wave. They add that sailors, pirates, water genasi and some sea elves honor him, that the temples people can name are in Calimshan, and that he gives what water gives without caring what is done with it. They also know that the harbor's festival offerings go to Umberlee and not to him. How Waterdeep feels about his worship is unsourced. [research]
> - **Waterdeep:** The sources place no temple, shrine, priest or standing observance of Istishia in the city.
>   - The Dragon Heist Major Temples table has no Istishia row. Its only generic row is "All deities", which names Holyhands House in the North Ward (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:30921-30996`, `:30923-30925`).
>   - The Fleetswake entry says the festival celebrates "the gods of the sea, navigation, and weather" and names only Umberlee, whose priests receive the winners' trophies at the Queenspire (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:31394`). The campaign calendar lists Umberlee first, then Deep Sashelas and Valkur, and leaves Istishia out (`campaign/setting/history/09-timekeeping-and-the-festival-calendar.md:82`). No source puts him among the sea gods of the festival.
>   - The Fair Seas Festival collects offerings for Umberlee alone (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:31395`).
>   - No campaign file places a priest, shrine or worshiper of his in the city, and no festival is tied to him. The one water genasi in the campaign, Avi, worships Eldath (`campaign/setting/notable-figures/trollskull-community/03-avi.md:12`).
> - **A cleric PC:** The domain is Tempest (SCAG, 2014). The matching subclass is Tempest Domain (derived; Player's Handbook, 2014), an exact fit by name. The wiki ties his followers to the ocean's destructive power, which is the side of water this domain suits. The Divine Order that fits is Thaumaturge (derived), since a cleric of the god of water itself works the element and no source gives him a martial order. A cleric would find no temple of his in Waterdeep and no priest to teach the faith.
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of the Noble Genies (derived; Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn), a fair fit. The oath reveres the forces of the Elemental Planes, and its features name one genie for each element, with the marid as the water one. That suits the god of the Elemental Plane of Water. Oath of the Ancients (derived; Player's Handbook, 2024) is the alternative, for a paladin who takes up the wiki's line that he keeps the life cycles of the sea going with Sashelas, Trishina and Eldath. No source lists paladins among his followers.
> - **A warlock PC:** No warlock connection in the sources.
> - **Campaign ties:** No campaign file uses this power. The published *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* has one monster that fits his nature, and it is a seed for later Undermountain work.
>   - On Level 19, Caverns of Ooze, area 1c is Ichthyglug's Cavern (`sources/adventure-wdmm.json:51743`). A marid lives in a freshwater pool there with six swarms of quippers. The text says "Ichthyglug is neither good nor evil, but always unpredictable" (`:51765`), and the pool drains back to the Elemental Plane of Water if the party completes his quest (`:51767`).
>   - The adventure does not name Istishia and gives the marid no god, so the link is only that both come from the Plane of Water and both are unpredictable. Undermountain is future work, so treat the cavern as a seed and nothing more.

## Overview

Istishia is the Water Lord, the elemental god of water itself and not of any particular sea or river. Sailors, pirates, water genasi and some sea elves honor him, and the temples people name are in Calimshan. He is said to be aloof and unpredictable, giving what water gives without caring how it is used. Waterdeep has no temple of his, and the harbor's festival offerings go to Umberlee.

**Tone:** Cool and plain, with no spectacle. Istishia is a power of water and not of any one shore, so describe the water and its changes before the god, and let his indifference carry the page. Keep him in the present tense and at a distance, since the campaign writes no scene in which he acts.
