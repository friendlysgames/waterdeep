# Graz'zt

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the patron entry for Graz'zt, the demon prince of pleasure who rules Azzagrat in the Abyss, and a Fiend patron for warlock characters. He is not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon and the sources give him no 5etools domains, so this page replaces the cleric and paladin guidance of a deity page with warlock guidance.
> - **Where it is used:** Graz'zt never appears in the campaign. He is a choice for a player building a warlock, and one other Deities page names him, the Waukeen page, as the demon who held her prisoner.
> - **Featured in:** no quest. No campaign file and neither adventure source names him.
> - **Spoiler limit:** this page holds no hidden fact that changes play, so it has no Secret box. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier and never mentions the Cassalanters. Everything above it may be read by the GM only.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what little an ordinary Waterdhavian knows about Graz'zt, gives a player what a Fiend patron relationship with him costs, and states that the campaign has tied nothing to him so that no later page invents more.

*The Dark Prince*

- **Title:** The Dark Prince (wiki; *Fiendish Codex I* 3e)
- **Other Names:** The Dark Man and the Shadow King (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e), the Prince of Pleasure (*Out of the Abyss* 5e), the Lord of Forbidden Pleasures (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e). All via the wiki.
- **Pantheon:** None. He is a demon prince and a patron, not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon.
- **Areas of Concern:** Pleasure, excess, tyranny, guile (derived from the wiki's portfolio line "tyrants, despot, guile, debauchery" for 3e and its accounts of his cults; the wiki lists no Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** None in 5etools. The wiki lists the 3e domains Chaos, Demonic, Evil and Temptation.
- **Symbol:** A black, six-fingered hand (wiki; 3e)
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil (wiki; 5e and 3e agree)
- **Challenge:** 24 (*Out of the Abyss* and *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e, via the wiki). The wiki's 3e field gives 32 and notes that *Fiendish Codex I* gives 22 and *Book of Vile Darkness* gives 24.
- **Plane:** The Abyss. He rules Azzagrat, the Triple Realm of the 45th, 46th and 47th layers (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e; *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e), and his seat is the Argent Palace (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
- **Typical Followers:** Many monstrous races, most notably lamias, with female high priests called the Chosen (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e). Witches and sorcerers who take his favors in exchange for magic also follow him (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e). All via the wiki.

> Take every pleasure there is, and make the world pay for it. (derived)

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Graz'zt is a demon prince, which the wiki also calls a demon lord, and he holds no divine status (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e). The wiki lists no holy days for him.
- **Temperament:** He is cruel and charismatic, and for him the only sin is moderation. He is also patient and collected, so much so that some compare him to a devil rather than a demon. He despises kindness and restriction, and he prefers to charm or trick an enemy over beating one (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e; *Fiendish Codex I* 3e; *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
- **How he deals with mortals:** Unlike most demons he watches the Material Plane through spies and often bargains with mortals directly. He trades favors, aid or information first and demands a service later (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e; *Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
- **Witches and sorcerers:** On many worlds he is known only as a legend, a dark figure who visits witches and sorcerers and grants them sexual favors and magical power (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e).
- **What he wants:** He keeps a clear head so that he can take more land and more minions and beat his enemies one at a time. His goal is to conquer and unify the Abyss and then the rest of the Lower Planes, and to use an army of fiends to raze every other plane (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e; *Book of Vile Darkness* 3e; *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e; *Monster Manual* 5e).
- **His magic:** His most powerful spell can trap the soul of any being he wishes and seal it away in a gem (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
- **Worship:** Lamias and other monstrous races worship him on the Material Plane. The Chosen are mostly women, and their rites are violent and carry themes of darkness and betrayal (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e; *Fiendish Codex I* 3e). A cultist's prayer quoted in the wiki begs him to spare the speaker his wrath and grant his favor (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e).
- **Court and servants:** Six lamias always attend him, and his marilith escorts Unhath and Reluhantis are never far away. Every demon in his empire counts as his servant, and the disloyal are fed to the carnivorous plants in his hanging gardens (*Book of Vile Darkness* 3e; *Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
- **Children:** He is one of the most notorious sources of cambions in the Lower Planes, and he favors humanoids who have made pacts with fiends (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e; *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e). His drow son Athux commands his demonic army, and his daughter Thraxxia serves as his private assassin (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e). Some of his half-fiend children have gone into the Abyss to take his power.
- **Holy days, omens, messengers, temples:** None in the wiki or the campaign, and the wiki's church and temples fields are empty. [research]
- **Relations with other powers:** Wiki-sourced.
  - Waukeen is his chief enemy (*For Duty & Deity* 3e; *Dragon* 360). The **Waukeen** page tells her side of the story.
  - He has warred with the demon princes Baphomet and Yeenoghu. With his son Athux's help he overthrew Adimarchus, who ruled Occipitus, the 507th layer of the Abyss (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
  - Malcanthet, Queen of the Succubi, and Gwynharwyf, champion of the eladrin, are among his greatest enemies. The conflict with Malcanthet drew in Kostchtchie, Prince of Wrath, who swore revenge on him for a humiliation (*Dungeon* 149; *Dragon* 345).
  - Iggwilv, the witch-queen also called Tasha, was both his lover and his bitter enemy for many years (*Dragon* 414). The **Iggwilv** page covers her.
  - The god Raxivort was once his servant, stole powerful artifacts from him and fled to Pandemonium (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e; *Volo's Guide to Monsters* 5e).
- **Origin:** Wiki-sourced, and the wiki calls the accounts "competing, often contradictory." One makes him a native of the Nine Hells who served **Asmodeus**, led an invasion of the Abyss in the Blood War, conquered three layers and renounced the devils (*Manual of the Planes* 4e). A second has him disillusioned with a Hell that benefits only Asmodeus, so he joined the Abyss to put himself beyond Asmodeus's reach (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e). A third makes him one of several demonic children of Pale Night, born after the obyrith fell (*Dragon* 360).
- **History in the Realms:** Wiki-sourced, and all of it is the past in 1492 DR.
  - In -666 DR he and Ayanna Orgolath had twin half-fiend sons, Ulthas and Ulthar. They overthrew the lich-king Belevan the Unliving of Narfell and founded the Orgolath Dynasty (*The Grand History of the Realms*).
  - During the Time of Troubles he gave the goddess Waukeen passage through the Abyss in return for the location of a hidden treasure, then betrayed her on arrival. He held her in the Argent Palace and made her watch profane celebrations held in her honor (*For Duty & Deity* 3e; *Dragon* 360).
  - While he held her, the League of the Six-Fingered Gentlemen entered the church of Waukeen and spread the Harlot's Coin Heresy. It taught that she had sold her divinity to him piece by piece and was imprisoned for failing to pay the debt (*Dragon* 355).
  - On 15 Nightal 1486 DR a failed ritual by Gromph Baenre, meant to summon and bind **Demogorgon**, instead summoned several demon lords in the Underdark. Graz'zt appeared below the Earthfast Mountains near the Master's Library and beat a force of Zhentarim there (*Hero* and *Ashes of the Tyrant*, novels).
  - Elsewhere he was trapped for a time on the world of Oerth by Iggwilv, who used his insight to raise her own power. She fell for his charms, and a son, Iuz, was born of them (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e).

> - **Waterdeep:** None. The Major Temples of Waterdeep table in *Dragon Heist* lists no temple of Graz'zt, and no campaign file or wiki entry places a shrine, cult or festival of his in the city. The wiki has no line that mentions Waterdeep.
> - **A warlock PC:** The Fiend patron in the 2024 *Player's Handbook* fits him (derived), and he fits it well because the wiki shows him granting magic to witches and sorcerers in return for his favors. No source gives Graz'zt-specific features. What he wants is a mortal who will serve his schemes on the Material Plane, where his spies already watch (derived from the wiki's accounts of his spies and his bargains). What a pact costs is a service demanded later, after he has traded favors, aid or information first. He can also trap the soul of any being he wishes in a gem, so the signer's afterlife is at his mercy (derived from the wiki's account of his spell).
> - **A cleric or paladin PC:** He has worshipers and Chosen priests, but 5etools gives no domains and the wiki's 3e domains (Chaos, Demonic, Evil, Temptation) are not a 2024 list. The closest 2024 subclass is Trickery, with the Thaumaturge Divine Order (both derived), since Temptation and charm sit nearest to deception and a cult that works through favors and bargains. The fit is loose. No paladin connection in the sources.
> - **Campaign ties:** No campaign file uses this power. The Waukeen page names him as her captor. The campaign's one demon-power worshiper is Ott Steeltoes, a dwarf who "worships Zuggtmoy, the demon queen of fungi" (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:32501`), and nothing links Zuggtmoy to Graz'zt. *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* never names Graz'zt, Azzagrat or the Argent Palace. Its only demon-lord lines concern Baphomet on Level 12, Juiblex on Level 2, and Halaster's possible plan on Level 20 to trap "a demon lord" in the Runestone Caverns (`sources/adventure-wdmm.json:54849`), and none of them names Graz'zt. The files tie the Cassalanters to Asmodeus alone, and nothing connects them to Graz'zt.

## Overview

Graz'zt is the demon prince of pleasure and excess, called the Dark Prince, and he rules Azzagrat, three layers of the Abyss. Scholars describe a handsome and charming lord who deals with tyrants, witches and sorcerers, and who always comes back to ask a service in return. Lamias and other monstrous peoples worship him, and sages say he once imprisoned the goddess Waukeen in his palace.
**Tone:** Smooth and patient, with the cruelty kept underneath. Graz'zt is a seducer who bargains, so when a player asks about him, describe what he gives and the charm he gives it with before the service he demands.
