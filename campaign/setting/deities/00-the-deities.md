# The Deities

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the hub of the Deities journal. It holds three choosing tables for all 73 powers, the list of Waterdeep's temples, and sections for the powers of other peoples and for warlock patrons.
> - **Where it is used:** a player opens it to pick a deity or patron for a cleric, paladin, warlock or any religious character. The GM opens it to see who is worshiped where in Waterdeep.
> - **Featured in:** no single quest. The power pages tie into **Fireball!**, **Vault of Dragons**, and the Order of the Gauntlet, Force Grey, Doom Raiders and Bregan D'aerthe missions. GM Notes below names the powers.
> - **Spoiler limit:** the tables and the temples list repeat the common-knowledge tier of each page and may be shown to players. GM Notes and Draft Notes are for the GM only. The hub holds no hidden fact, and each power page keeps its own secrets in its own boxes.

**Purpose:** Lets a player choose a deity or patron for a religious character in one pass. Lets the GM see which powers Waterdeep worships and where, without opening seventy-three pages.

Waterdeep honors a great many gods, and no one temple speaks for the city. On Gods' Day, Marpenoth 15, private shrines are brought out into the open and many people wear the holy symbols of their favored deities. The larger temples stand mostly in the Castle Ward and the Sea Ward, and Holyhands House in the North Ward is listed as a temple for all deities.

## How to choose a deity

Each table below lists all 73 powers, one row each, and every name in bold has its own page. The domains come from the Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide (SCAG, 2014) unless a cell names another book, and they are 2014 material. The 2024 Player's Handbook offers four cleric subclasses (Life, Light, Trickery and War), two Divine Orders (Protector and Thaumaturge), four paladin oaths (Devotion, Glory, the Ancients and Vengeance) and four warlock patrons (Archfey, Celestial, Fiend and Great Old One). The third table gives the nearest 2024 choice for each power.

## Overview

This journal gathers the powers that a character in Waterdeep might follow or bargain with. It covers the gods of Faerûn, Waterdeep's own Siamorphe, eight powers who lead the faiths of other peoples, and thirteen patrons: archdevils, demon lords, archfey, a witch-queen and beings from the Far Realm. The larger temples stand mostly in the Castle Ward and the Sea Ward, and Holyhands House in the North Ward is listed as a temple for all deities. A character whose god has no temple in the city can keep a private shrine.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Running a choice:** When a player settles on a power, open that power's page and read its Using box first. The tables on this page only point the player to it.
> - **Reading the fit table:** Every subclass, order, oath and patron in the third table is a fit derived on the power's own page. The word in brackets is that page's own measure of how close it is. If a player wants a different fit, the page's Using box says what the alternatives cost.
> - **Bregan D'aerthe and Lolth:** Bregan D'aerthe has rejected Lolth as its goddess. No member worships her, keeps a shrine to her or invokes the Spider Queen with devotion, and members speak of her with contempt. Do not give any company member a shrine, a prayer or a kind word for her. Lolth's page quotes the campaign lines.
> - **Powers with a quest, faction or character tie:**
>   - **Fireball!:** Gond and Mystra.
>   - **Vault of Dragons:** Bahamut, Moradin, Ilsensine and Kelemvor.
>   - **Order of the Gauntlet:** Helm, Torm and Tyr.
>   - **Other missions:** Umberlee (the Force Grey mission The Dragon in the Harbor), Yondalla (the Doom Raiders mission The Missing Snobeedle), Tiamat (the Harpers First Meeting), Bane (Manshoon's Zhentarim) and Mielikki (the Emerald Enclave).
>   - **Characters:** Corellon Larethian (Fala Lefaliir), Tymora (Meloon Wardragon), Eldath (Avi) and Kelemvor (Sir Ambrose Everdawn).
>   - **Bregan D'aerthe:** Lolth.
> - **Read these pages first:** The pages for **Asmodeus**, **Lathander** and **Siamorphe** carry GM boxes whose contents change what you can say at the table. Read them before a player takes one of the three.
> - **The Order's three temples:** The Order of the Gauntlet treats the Halls of Justice, the Temple of Helm and the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield as friendly to its members. Worship of Helm, Torm or Tyr only decides whom Savra addresses first, and nobody is tested for faith at the door.
> - **Temples with no hook yet:** The Castle Ward gazetteer records no campaign hook for the Spires of the Morning or the Temple of the Seldarine.

**Tone:** Even and practical. The page is a menu, so say what each power offers before what it costs, and leave every secret in the boxes of the individual pages.
