# Tiamat

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the deity entry for Tiamat, the Dragon Queen and head of the evil dragons, for a GM who needs a power behind a cult, a draconic villain or an evil cleric, and for a player who wants to build one.
> - **Where it is used:** Tiamat never appears in person and no quest treats her as a living power. The campaign touches her in two places: the opera *The Fall of Tiamat* in the Harpers First Meeting, and a seed shrine to her on Level 16 of Undermountain.
> - **Featured in:** the **Harpers** First Meeting (the opera). No quest uses her as a god, and no file ties her to Aurinax, Zelifarn, the Vault of Dragons or the Cult of the Dragon.
> - **Spoiler limit:** everything above the `## Overview` may be read by the GM only. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier. Nothing about Tiamat is secret, so the page has no Secret box.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Tiamat, and states that the campaign gives her an opera and a dungeon shrine and nothing else, so that no later page invents a Waterdeep cult or ties her to the dragons of the campaign.

*The Dragon Queen*

- **Title:** Dragon goddess of evil (PHB, 2014). The wiki's title row reads Archdevil (1e, *Dragon* 38), with epithets listed under it.
- **Other Names:** The Queen of Evil Dragonkind, the Chromatic Dragon, the Dark Lady, the Avaricious, Bane of Bahamut, Creator of Evil Dragonkind, the Many-Mawed, Nemesis of the Gods and Queen of Chaos (wiki title row, citing *Cult of the Dragon*, *Races of the Dragon*, *Draconomicon*, *Elminster in Hell* and *Dragon* 38). The wiki names her aspects as Azharul, Tchazzar and the Undying Queen, and her followers as Tiamatan.
- **Pantheon:** Nonhuman (PHB, 2014). The wiki lists the Faerûnian pantheon, the Draconic pantheon and, for a time, the Untheric pantheon. No SCAG, MTF or FRHoF entry exists for her.
- **Areas of Concern:** Evil dragonkind, greed and hoarded wealth, and the domination of the world by evil dragons (derived from the wiki's 2e and 3e portfolios and its account of her clergy; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Trickery (PHB, 2014). The wiki's 5e row adds Death (*Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus*), its 4e row gives Strife, Tyranny and Vengeance, and its 3e row gives Evil, Greed, Law, Pride, Scalykind, Tyranny and Wrath.
- **Symbol:** Dragon head with five claw marks (PHB, 2014; the wiki's 5e row agrees). The wiki's 3e row gives a five-headed dragon, its 4e row a five-pointed star, and its 2e row a five-headed dragon or a red dragon before a black mountain.
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil (PHB, 2014). The wiki gives lawful evil in its 1e, 2e and 3e rows, evil in 4e, and lawful evil and chaotic evil together in 5e, and its opening line calls her chaotic evil and lawful evil.
- **Plane:** Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells, where her lair stands (wiki, 5e row: the Nine Hells and Avernus, realm Tiamat's Lair; the 2e row reads Baator and Avernus). Earlier rows give the Dragon Eyrie (3e) and, while she belonged to the Untheric pantheon, Zigguraxus. The PHB gives no plane.
- **Typical Followers:** Chromatic dragons, evil dragons and evil reptiles, with the Cult of the Dragon, fighters, sorcerers, thieves and vandals (wiki, 3e row). Kobolds revere her as their creator (*Volo's Guide to Monsters*, via the wiki), and the wiki says she gained a few dragonborn followers after the Spellplague.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Tiamat is the queen of the evil dragons. The wiki rates her a lesser deity in its 2e, 3e, 4e and 5e rows and an archdevil in 1e. Sages long argued over whether she was a goddess at all, and some held that she was a devil, a demon, an avatar of another power or a dragon so great that her kin worshiped her (*Draconomicon*; *Races of the Dragon*).
- **Her place among the gods:** Sages of the 15th century DR add that her origins tie her to the Material Plane, so she differs in kind from the gods of the Outer Planes (*Fizban's Treasury of Dragons*, 5e). That is why her true form can be discorporated by mighty mortals.
- **Nature:** She is arrogant, greedy, hateful, spiteful and vain, and she never forgives a slight (*Faiths & Pantheons*; *Monster Mythology*). She treats mortals as disposable tools and can be charming and fey when she needs to be, though her reptilian self-interest shows through sooner or later (*Faiths & Pantheons*).
- **What the faith asks:** Her greed for treasure is endless, and she prefers that followers bring it to her as gifts instead of hunting it themselves (*Faiths & Pantheons*; *Draconomicon*). She keeps watch over evil dragonkind and stirs it toward avarice, pride and wickedness (*Monster Mythology*).
- **Form:** She is a single chromatic dragon with five heads, one each for black, blue, green, red and white. Every head acts on its own with the breath of its kind, and her tail ends in a venomous stinger (*Draconomicon*; *Races of the Dragon*). Her avatars are the Dark Lady, a mulan woman with black eyes and dark robes; the Chromatic Dragon; and the Undying Queen, a dracolich form of the Chromatic Dragon (*Powers & Pantheons*).
- **Clergy:** She accepts only evil clerics, who want to place the world under the domination of evil dragons (*Races of the Dragon*). The church keeps a strict hierarchy of ranks and titles, and its clerics spend their time gathering wealth and sabotaging other faiths through thefts, assassinations, vandalism and arson. In Unther and Chessenta they pursued power, while in western Faerûn they worked to subvert the Cult of the Dragon (*Faiths & Pantheons*).
- **Who worships her:** Evil dragons have worshiped her since their kind first appeared on Toril (*Cult of the Dragon*; *Dragons of Faerûn*). In Unther the people blamed her for the country's setbacks, and when Gilgeam turned tyrant the rebels against him began to worship her and built an organized church (*Dragons of Faerûn*; *Powers & Pantheons*).
- **The Cult of the Dragon:** She made inroads into the Cult and claimed some members as her worshipers (*Powers & Pantheons*). In her last known plot, the Cult's leader Severin Silrajin assembled the Mask of the Dragon Queen at her order (*Hoard of the Dragon Queen*).
- **Holy days:** None. The wiki's 3e, 4e and 5e holy-day rows are blank, and no file ties a city festival to her.
- **Omens:** No source gives one. [research]
- **Messengers:** She resurrected Tchazzar, Gestaniius and Skuthosiin as emissaries in 1373 DR (*Dragons of Faerûn*). Azharul, once a minor draconic god, serves as her vessel and her second public face in Avernus (Ed Greenwood's 2015 Candlekeep answer, via the wiki).
- **Relations, Bahamut:** He is her brother and her opposite, and the enmity runs through both sets of worshipers (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, 5e; *Races of the Dragon*). Legends hold that the two once made the First World together (*Fizban's Treasury of Dragons*, 5e). Bahamut later became the subservient god of Torm (*Forgotten Realms Player's Guide*, 4e).
- **Relations, Faerûnian powers:** She clashed with Iyachtu Xvim, the one significant opponent of her joining the Faerûnian pantheon, and then with his father **Bane**, and she counts **Ilmater** an enemy (*Faiths & Pantheons*). She served Bane reluctantly in the 4e period (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e). After Gith treated with her, she held some kind of contract with the githyanki (*Manual of the Planes*, 3e). The kobold god Kurtulmak is her servant (*Volo's Guide to Monsters*).
- **Relations, the Nine Hells:** Wiki-sourced, with the book named on each fact.
  - She helped **Asmodeus** forge his Ruby Rod (*Dragon* 75). She later fell into enmity with him and with Bel after they betrayed her (*Monster Manual*, 5e; Ed Greenwood's 2015 Candlekeep answer). After her failed escape she kept up a courteous tie with Asmodeus (*Vecna: Eve of Ruin*).
  - She was loosely allied with Bel and lent him abishai for the Blood War, and she resented **Mammon** for converting some evil dragons away from her (*Dragon* 75).
  - The wiki says she gave covert aid to **Zariel** to keep Bel from growing too strong (Ed Greenwood's 2015 Candlekeep answer). The Zariel page, reading the 5e adventures, calls the relationship hostile (see Draft Notes).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - In the commonly accepted tradition she is the daughter of Asgorath and the sister of Bahamut and Null (*Cult of the Dragon*). Dragonborn of Abeir say the two were born from Asgorath's corpse after he died in the Dawn War (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, 5e).
  - Draconic myth says she and Bahamut made the First World from primordial chaos. She resisted the invading gods, lost, and was sealed in Avernus when the First World shattered (*Fizban's Treasury of Dragons*, 5e).
  - The reason for her imprisonment is disputed. One account says Asmodeus confined her under an old contract (*Dungeon Master's Guide*, 5e revised). Another says a curse bound her to Avernus after she slew a giant god (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes*, 5e). Some sources say she was never imprisoned and chose to guard the gate to Dis for Asmodeus (*Manual of the Planes*, 1e; *Planes of Law*).
  - In the Dragonfall War, the long fight with Bahamut's followers, she bred the dragonspawn to wipe them out. The last era of intense fighting ran from -2087 DR to -1071 DR, and she entered the Untheric pantheon with a three-headed aspect (*Dragons of Faerûn*; *Powers & Pantheons*).
  - In -1071 DR, in the Battle of the Gods, she and Bahamut (then the Untheric god Marduk) killed each other's Untheric aspects. Both lost their divine power, and she was reduced to an archfiend (*Dragons of Faerûn*).
  - As an archfiend she was given Avernus by Asmodeus and held it so poorly that he demoted her. He let her stay as guardian of the main gate to Dis (Ed Greenwood's 2015 Candlekeep answer).
  - In 1346 DR Untherite cultists summoned the Dark Lady, who stirred a rebellion against Gilgeam and lifted her to a demigoddess. In the Time of Troubles that aspect was slain, and her essence passed into three dragons. Tchazzar devoured the other two, her five-headed form returned, and she killed Gilgeam, so Ao dissolved the Untheric pantheon (*Dragons of Faerûn*; *Powers & Pantheons*).
  - In 1359 DR King Gareth Dragonsbane destroyed one of her avatars for Bahamut and brought the worship of Bahamut back to Damara, which rekindled the Dragonfall War (*Dragons of Faerûn*). In 1371 DR she joined the Faerûnian pantheon after a cult in Luthcheq took up her worship (*Lords of Darkness*).
  - After the Spellplague, the wounded body of Azharul fell into Bane's hands and she served him until she pulled it out of Banehold along with much of his divine energy. Asmodeus then offered her Avernus again and she refused. Bel placed her under his compulsion, and she learned that Asmodeus had told him to imprison her in her own realm (Ed Greenwood's 2015 Candlekeep answer; *Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e).
  - Angry at the betrayal, she told the Cult of the Dragon to assemble the Mask of the Dragon Queen so she could escape. Severin Silrajin used it to bring her temple from Avernus to the Well of Dragons, and he and his forces were defeated and she was banished back to the Nine Hells (*Hoard of the Dragon Queen*; *The Rise of Tiamat*, 5e; *Storm King's Thunder*). As of 1491 DR her chief aim was still to break free (Ed Greenwood's 2015 Candlekeep answer; *Hoard of the Dragon Queen*).
- **In the campaign:** Tiamat is alive and present in 1492 DR, as every power is, and she is held in Avernus. The wiki's infobox date of death (-1071 DR, "allegedly") and the avatar slain in 1359 DR are history, and the wiki says each destroyed only an avatar or aspect (see Draft Notes).

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Using Tiamat as a Deity
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice her as the villain of a famous opera. A sage, a priest or a regular at the bar says Tiamat is the five-headed Dragon Queen, the goddess of evil dragons and greed, whose symbol is a dragon's head with five claw marks. They add that the chromatic dragons answer to her, that her brother Bahamut rules the good metallic dragons, and that the two have fought since dragons began. They know her best from *The Fall of Tiamat*, the opera sung in Giant at the Lightsinger Theater, which has the dragon queen beaten at the Well of Dragons. No source names a Waterdhavian who worships her. How much a Waterdhavian knows of the Cult of the Dragon is unsourced. [research]
> - **Waterdeep:** Tiamat has no temple, shrine, priest or festival in the city in any source.
>   - The Dragon Heist Major Temples table has no dragon row (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:30921-30996`). Its "All deities" row, Holyhands House in the North Ward (`:30923-30925`), names no power, and no source says her faithful use it.
>   - The one place she has a room is Undermountain, which lies under the city (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:430`). Level 16, the Crystal Labyrinth, holds the Tiamat Shrine in the Dragons' Domain (`sources/adventure-wdmm.json:44031`, `:45963` and `:45990`). Undermountain is not in a ward, so the shrine has none.
>   - The Lightsinger Theater, where her story is staged, is in the Castle Ward (`campaign/gazetteer/waterdeep/castle-ward.md:96`). The wiki has no Waterdeep text for her.
> - **A cleric PC:** The domain is Trickery (PHB, 2014), and the 2024 Player's Handbook keeps Trickery Domain, so the fit is direct (derived). The wiki's 5e Death domain has no counterpart among Life, Light, Trickery and War, and this page does not use it (derived). The Divine Order that fits is Protector (derived), because it adds martial weapons and heavy armor for a cleric of a faith of raiders and arsonists, and the wiki gives a heavy pick as her 3e favored weapon. Thaumaturge (derived) suits a cleric from the Cult of the Dragon, since the wiki lists sorcerers among her followers. Her clergy accept only evil clerics (*Races of the Dragon*), so a cleric PC is an evil one, and no campaign file sets a rule for evil PCs. [research]
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of Vengeance (derived), a loose fit. The oath's fixed pursuit of a chosen enemy matches her habit of never forgiving a slight (*Monster Mythology*, via the wiki). No source lists paladins among her followers, and the oaths point toward punishing evil, which is awkward for an evil power. A table that wants a paladin of Tiamat should settle the oath with the player.
> - **A warlock PC:** Fiend (derived), a loose fit. The wiki's 5e row gives her home as Avernus in the Nine Hells, its 1e row rates her an archdevil serving Asmodeus, and her 5e adventure appearances are *Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus* and *The Rise of Tiamat*. No source gives a warlock rule for her or calls her a patron, so a Fiend warlock bound to her is a flavor choice.
> - **Campaign ties:** The Harpers First Meeting and a seed shrine in Undermountain. The details are in the GM Notes below. No Notable Figure worships her, and no file puts a cult of Tiamat in Waterdeep. The campaign's dragons are Aurinax, an adult gold dragon, and Zelifarn, a young bronze dragon, and both are lawful good (`campaign/setting/notable-figures/independents-allies/19-aurinax.md:6`; `campaign/setting/notable-figures/bregan-daerthe/05-zelifarn.md:5`). Gold and bronze are metallic dragons, which the wiki places on Bahamut's side, and no file ties either to Tiamat.

## Overview

Tiamat is the five-headed Dragon Queen, a lawful evil goddess who rules the evil dragons and prizes hoarded gold above everything. Chromatic dragons are her oldest worshipers, and some members of the Cult of the Dragon are said to serve her. Her brother and enemy is Bahamut, who rules the good metallic dragons. Waterdeep has no temple of hers, and most citizens know her from the opera *The Fall of Tiamat* at the Lightsinger Theater in the Castle Ward.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **The opera:** In the Harpers First Meeting, a paper bird invites the party to *The Fall of Tiamat* at the Lightsinger Theater, where Mirt meets them in Private Box C at intermission. The opera is sung in Giant and ends with the dragon queen beaten at the Well of Dragons (`campaign/quests/faction-events/harpers/00-first-meeting/ev-01-first-meeting.md:8` and `:89`; `sources/adventure-wdh.json:3834`).
> - **The staging:** Tiamat's five heads are painted wood and silk on poles, worked by ten stagehands behind a black screen, and colored driftglobes light each head. Orsolya Venn, a contralto, sings Tiamat, and Tiamat sinks below the stage on a lift in the second act (`ev-01-first-meeting.md:146`, `:148` and `:328`).
> - **No clue in it:** The event says "*The Fall of Tiamat* carries no clue, and nothing in it bears on the meeting. Use as much or as little as the table enjoys." (`ev-01-first-meeting.md:143`). The company's names are invented color (`campaign/quests/faction-events/harpers/00-first-meeting/design-notes.md:21`).
> - **The Tiamat Shrine (seed):** In Undermountain Level 16, area 12b lies inside the Dragons' Domain, where Ashtyrranthor and her six children live. A 15-foot disk carved in the floor shows Tiamat with five heads breathing their breath weapons. One wall hanging shows her fighting twelve storm giants, and the other shows her fighting a bloodied old man in wizard robes with seven golden canaries circling his head. A DC 15 Intelligence (Religion) check identifies the old man as Bahamut in human form and the canaries as his polymorphed gold dragon advisors. Each wall hanging is worth 750 gp (`sources/adventure-wdmm.json:45963-46009`). Undermountain is future work, so treat the shrine as a seed.
> - **Aurinax and Bahamut:** In **Vault of Dragons**, a DC 18 Charisma (Persuasion) check is made with advantage if the party brought "a prominent city official ... or a professed worshiper of Bahamut", and Aurinax is a gold dragon (`campaign/structure/arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md:168`; `campaign/guides/gm-guide/design-notes-running-the-campaign.md:107`). That is the one place a dragon god affects a scene. No file gives Aurinax a view of Tiamat, and Tiamat has no part in the vault.
> - **Dragon cultists:** The character-creation table rates the Dragon Cultist background "Situational" and says it is "Worth a conversation with your table. Interesting for a character carrying a hidden allegiance." (`campaign/guides/players-guide/character-creation.md:71`). It names no power, and no file sets a Cult of the Dragon in Waterdeep.
> - **The Council of Waterdeep:** The History page records, from the wiki only, that Dagult Neverember led the Council of Waterdeep, "the alliance against the Cult of the Dragon", until the vote cost him that place (`campaign/setting/history/06-the-open-lords-fall.md:68`). That is the only campaign line that places the Cult near the city, and it comes from *The Rise of Tiamat* (5e).

**Tone:** Grave and played straight. Tiamat is a power of greed and grudges, so describe her hoards and her cults before her rage, and let the opera give the city its picture of her. Keep her at a distance in the Nine Hells, since the campaign writes no scene in which she acts, and keep any dragon in the campaign free of her unless the table decides otherwise.
