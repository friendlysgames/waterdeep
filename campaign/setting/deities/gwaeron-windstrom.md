> - **What this page is:** the deity entry for Gwaeron Windstrom, the demigod of tracking and the Mouth of Mielikki, written for players who want a ranger, cleric or paladin devoted to him.
> - **Where it is used:** Gwaeron never appears in person, and no campaign file or published adventure names him. The page serves character building and gives the GM a clear answer when a player asks what Waterdeep knows of him.
> - **Featured in:** No quest or event names him. The **Mielikki** page names him as the messenger who carries northern rangers' prayers to her.
> - **Spoiler limit:** everything above the `## Overview` may be read by the GM only. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier. Nothing about Gwaeron is secret, and this page makes no claim about the Cassalanters.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Gwaeron, and states plainly that the sources place no temple, shrine or priest of his in the city, so that no later page invents one.

- **Other Names:** The Master Tracker, the Tracker Never Led Astray (SCAG, 2014); the Master of Tracking, the Tracker Who Never Goes Astray, the Master Interpreter of the Woodland Signs (wiki); Aarthon, his true name (wiki, citing an Ed Greenwood forum post)
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian
- **Areas of Concern:** Tracking, the reading of woodland signs, rangers of the North, animal handling, the hunting of trolls and other rapacious monsters (derived from the SCAG text and the wiki's 2e portfolio; no entry lists Areas of Concern)

- **Symbol:** Paw print with a five-pointed star in its center (SCAG, 2014)
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good (SCAG, 2014)
- **Plane:** Toril. The wiki's 2e row gives the Prime Material plane and its 3e row gives the House of Nature, and its text says he could wander both (wiki; no FRHoF entry).
- **Typical Followers:** Rangers of the North (SCAG, 2014)

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Gwaeron is a demigod of the Faerûnian pantheon, raised from mortal life by Mielikki, whom he serves. SCAG (2014) gives no rank and says only that he is said to have been a mortal man elevated to godhood by her. The wiki calls him a demipower in its 2e row, a demigod in its 3e row and opening text, and a lesser deity in its 5e row.
- **The Mouth of Mielikki:** Rangers pray to Gwaeron because he represents much of their work and can speak to Mielikki on their behalf. Most rangers of the North think her too mysterious, holy and wild to petition directly, but they count him as one of their own and so understanding of their needs (SCAG, 2014). The wiki adds that they believe he carries their words to her by tracking her to whatever forest she is hiding in, and that he speaks to mortals for her wherever direct speech is needed (SCAG p.34; *Volo's Guide to the North*).
- **Who follows:** Few people outside the rangers of the North pray to him, and many have never heard of his faith (SCAG, 2014; wiki). The wiki says northern trackers in general honor him, whether they hunt game or outlaws, and that a sailor chasing a pirate ship might pray to him (an Ed Greenwood forum post). Its 3e row lists druids, rangers and troll hunters.
- **What the faith asks:** His teachings follow Mielikki's, which follow Silvanus's, with more weight on the positive and outreaching side of nature. Intelligent folk and wildlife do not have to destroy each other, and the faithful are encouraged to live as one with nature and learn its hidden ways. Needless harm to the forest and its creatures is to be fought or punished (*Powers & Pantheons*; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). The faith is one of emulation, and rangers admire him more than they venerate him.
- **Temperament:** He is quiet, reclusive and slow to anger, and he teaches through action more than words. His rage once roused is terrible, and trolls draw most of it. He hates them so much that he stands as the idea of a ranger's favored enemy, and he counts them kin of Malar (*Powers & Pantheons*; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). SCAG (2014) calls him a dedicated foe of rapacious creatures such as trolls and orcs.
- **How he appears:** As an old man with a long white beard who is still hale and mighty (SCAG, 2014). The wiki adds a tall, powerfully built human whose white hair and beard billow even in still air. He wears wilderness clothing and nothing above the waist, and he speaks in terse sentences with little sign of emotion (*Powers & Pantheons*; *Volo's Guide to the North*).
- **Tracking:** The wiki credits him with skill beyond what any ranger of his level should have. He finds the most obscure woodland signs and paths several weeks old, and neither teleportation nor travel between planes shakes his pursuit. Only divine power can make him lose a trail (*Powers & Pantheons*; the web enhancement *Deities*, 3e).
- **Shrines:** He has no temples. His shrines are trail markers in places that serve wilderness wanderers, each one a carving of his symbol, a paw print with a star on the palm, on a prominent tree or stone (SCAG, 2014). The wiki's infobox marks a church as existing but gives no temple text (see Draft Notes).
- **Rites:** The wiki calls his followers Gwaeronans and says they pray for spells at dusk, when wild beasts leave their dens to hunt. A follower who invokes his name and finishes stalking a target leaves a circle of six footprints made with the right foot, heels nearly touching and toes facing outward (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e; *Powers & Pantheons*).
- **Gwaeron's Slumber:** He is believed to rest and sleep in a stand of trees near Triboar (SCAG, 2014). The wiki places the wood west of the town, where many trees keep their autumn colors all year, and says Triboar's law forbids cutting wood or hunting there so as not to anger him. Most of his followers make a pilgrimage there at least once, and a ranger who sleeps in the wood is said to receive dreams that hint at Mielikki's will. A non-ranger gains, once in a lifetime, a single day of ranger-grade tracking (wiki, citing SCAG p.134, *Volo's Guide to the North* and *Storm King's Thunder*). He never appears to those who come looking for him (*Volo's Guide to the North*, as quoted by the wiki).
- **Messengers and omens:** He manifests through woodland creatures, among them boars, blue jays, crows, deer and magpies, and through pseudodragons and faerie dragons, often to teach a novice ranger the signs of the animal. He can also appear as faerie fire that picks out an obscure sign, or as a bare human footprint with his symbol in the heel that shows a devout follower how to pick up a lost trail (wiki; *Powers & Pantheons*).
- **Holy days:** No source names one. [research]
- **Relations with the nature powers:** He serves Mielikki and, through her, Silvanus, in a close and supportive group with Lurue and Shiallia. He has aided her on missions and taught her rangers to read forest signs. His small following and the likeness of their creeds put him at risk of being absorbed as an aspect of her (*Powers & Pantheons*; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **Allies among other peoples' gods:** The wiki says he has allied with the elven, gnome and halfling gods whose portfolios match those of Mielikki, Silvanus and himself. Fenmarel Mestarine, Solonor Thelandira, Arvoreen and Baervan Wildwanderer are named, and he is the natural messenger when Mielikki needs a favor from them (*Powers & Pantheons*; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). His other allies include **Shaundakul** and Nobanion, who shares his hatred of Malar.
- **Enemies:** **Malar** the Beastlord, whose hostility is mutual, and Vaprak, the god of the trolls. His fight with Malar began before he was a god and has not stopped (*Powers & Pantheons*; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book named on each fact.
  - Gwaeron was a mortal human ranger who roamed the North of Faerûn centuries before the Era of Upheaval (*Powers & Pantheons*; *Faiths & Pantheons*; the web enhancement *Deities*).
  - He rose by stalking and killing several manifestations of Malar and at least one avatar, in quick succession. Mielikki sponsored him to demigodhood for the deed, and Malar swore undying enmity against him.
  - In the Time of Troubles (1358 DR), Malar fled north after Nobanion beat him in Gulthmere Forest. Gwaeron hunted him across the region and could not be shaken from the trail, while Shiallia followed and undid the damage Malar caused (*Powers & Pantheons*; the wiki's Avatar Trilogy summary).
- **In the campaign:** Gwaeron is alive and present in 1492 DR. No file has him appear, speak or act in a scene.

> **[GM]**

> #### Using Gwaeron Windstrom as a Deity
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice him as a ranger's god whom the city seldom thinks about. A sage or a priest says he is the god of tracking, a ranger who rose to godhood, and that rangers of the North pray to him because Mielikki is too wild to ask directly. They add that his symbol is a paw print with a star in it, that he is pictured as a hale old man with a long white beard, and that he sleeps in a stand of trees near Triboar. A regular at the bar who has traveled north to that town may know its nickname, "The Town Where Only Gwaeron Sleeps" (wiki, citing *Volo's Guide to the North*). Nobody in the city can point to a temple of his, because he has none. How Waterdhavians feel about him is unsourced. [research]
> - **Waterdeep:** The sources place no temple, shrine or priest of Gwaeron in the city.
>   - SCAG (2014) says he has no temples anywhere, only trail-marker shrines in the wild. The Dragon Heist Major Temples table (caption at `sources/adventure-wdh.json:30910`) has no row for him.
>   - Holyhands House in the North Ward is a temple for all deities, which includes him (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:30923-30925`). No file names a priest of his there.
>   - The only place the sources tie to him is the stand of trees near Triboar, which lies outside the city.
>   - The Shrines of Nature in the Sea Ward belong to **Mielikki** and **Silvanus**. No file connects them to Gwaeron, although he serves her. [research]
>   - No trail-marker shrine in or near Waterdeep appears in any file, and no festival is tied to him. [research]
> - **A cleric PC:** The domains are Knowledge and Nature (SCAG, 2014). The 2024 Player's Handbook has neither, so the closest subclass among Life, Light, Trickery and War is War Domain (derived). The fit is loose. It rests on his hunting of trolls and orcs and on a faith that fights those who harm the wild. The Divine Order that fits is Protector (derived), and a Knowledge-minded cleric who reads signs more than she fights would take Thaumaturge instead (derived). The sources describe no hierarchy of clergy for him, only rangers who pray to him, so a cleric of his is the rare case. A ranger is the natural follower.
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of the Ancients (derived), a moderate fit, because the oath guards the living world and his faith fights needless harm to forests and creatures. Oath of Vengeance (derived) suits a paladin who pursues one hated foe, such as trolls or the servants of Malar, as Gwaeron pursued Malar. No source lists paladins among his followers, so both fits rest on his portfolio.
> - **A warlock PC:** No warlock connection in the sources.
> - **Campaign ties:** No campaign file uses this power.

## Overview

Gwaeron Windstrom is the god of tracking, a ranger who was once mortal and was raised to godhood by Mielikki. Rangers of the North pray to him as one of their own, and they trust him to carry their words to the Lady of the Forest. He is pictured as a hale old man with a long white beard, and he is believed to sleep in a stand of trees near Triboar. He has no temples, only a paw print with a star carved on trees and stones along wilderness trails, and no temple of his stands in Waterdeep.

**Tone:** Spare and weathered. Gwaeron is a god of patient trackers, so describe the trail and the sign before the god, and keep him at a distance, since he never appears to those who come looking for him.
