# Mystra

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the deity entry for Mystra, goddess of magic, and the reference for the faith practiced at the House of Wonder in the Sea Ward.
> - **Where it is used:** Mystra never appears in person. The campaign reaches her through the House of Wonder, through her daughter Laeral Silverhand, and through Manshoon's plan to supplant her.
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!** (the House of Wonder on the nimblewright sweep). Through Laeral Silverhand she also touches **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers** and **Vault of Dragons**, and through Manshoon's plan she touches **Gralhund Villa** and **Kolat Towers**.
> - **Spoiler limit:** everything above the `## Overview` may be read by the GM only. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier and never mentions Manshoon. Manshoon's aim to absorb the Weave sits in the GM Notes and in the Never reveal bullet.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Mystra, and lists the temple, the daughter and the villain's plan that the campaign has tied to her so that no later page invents more.

*The Lady of Mysteries*

- **Title:** God of Magic (FRHoF); Goddess of Magic (SCAG, 2014)
- **Other Names:** The Mother of All Magic, Our Lady of Spells (FRHoF); Mistress of Magic, Lady Magic, the One True Spell (wiki)
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian
- **Areas of Concern:** Magic, spells, the Weave, spellcasters and magical research (derived). The wiki portfolio is "Magic, Spells, The Weave" and no entry lists Areas of Concern.
- **Domains:** Arcana, Knowledge (SCAG, 2014)
- **Symbol:** A circle of seven stars, nine stars encircling a flowing red mist, or a single star (SCAG, 2014)
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good (SCAG, 2014)
- **Plane:** Elysium (Eronia) (FRHoF)
- **Typical Followers:** Anyone who uses magic (FRHoF)

> Preserve magic and spread it through the Realms.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Mystra is a god of the Faerûnian pantheon, and the FRHoF calls her a power of tremendous strength even among other deities. The wiki ranks her a greater deity in its 1e and 2e rows and leaves its 5e rows blank.
- **Her dominion:** Her domain is the Weave, the sum total of magic, from which spells are drawn (FRHoF). She provides and tends the Weave as the conduit through which mortal spellcasters and magical crafters safely reach the raw force of magic, and she is the goddess of possibilities (SCAG, 2014).
- **What the faith asks:** Her faithful work to preserve and promulgate magic throughout the Realms. They watch for people who show magical potential and help them find tutelage with a suitable mentor (FRHoF; SCAG, 2014). The wiki adds that she cared less about good and evil than about the laws of magic being upheld (*On Hallowed Ground*).
- **Who follows:** Her worshipers are those who use magic or work closely with it, such as alchemists and sages (FRHoF). She also hears the prayers of people who find magic wondrous and of people who fear what they have met (SCAG, 2014). The wiki describes her followers as mostly human bards, wizards and seekers of arcane lore (*Faiths & Avatars*).
- **Clergy:** The blue-clad priests of her temples are called dweomerkeepers, and they wield both arcane and divine magic (FRHoF). Their ranks include wizards and sorcerers and the occasional bard (SCAG, 2014). The wiki says every kind of spellcaster had a place in her hierarchy, and that wizards who followed Azuth or another god usually showed her some devotion as well (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 2e).
- **Orders:** The FRHoF names four bodies that she inspires. No file places any of them in Waterdeep.
  - The Knights of the Mystic Fire are her foremost martial champions. With sword and spell they protect her temples, defend sacred sites and attack her enemies, and Eldritch Knights, Bladesingers, paladins and rangers all fit among them. A candidate serves several years, holds an all-night vigil under the stars, swears an oath of loyalty, and is invested in a blue tabard marked with a fiery silver star. A knight is supported with a free Comfortable lifestyle at any temple of Mystra and can call on a task force of lesser warriors with sufficient notice and cause.
  - The Children of the Starry Quill serve as an information and spy network in cities and towns.
  - The Order of the Shooting Star pursues her interests in the wilderness.
  - The Sisterhood of the Silver Fire opposes the Cult of the Dragon and hunts down copies of the *Tome of the Dragon*, the book on how to turn a dragon into a dracolich.
- **Chosen:** She relies on Chosen more than most gods do. Their ranks include the Seven Sisters, who are her daughters (among them Alustriel Silverhand, Laeral Silverhand and the Simbul), Elminster Aumar and other legendary spellcasters. She gives a Chosen the power to call on silver fire, a destructive form of pure magic that can also restore magic to places cut off from the Weave (FRHoF). The wiki lists Khelben Arunsun among her Chosen (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e).
- **Chosen in the campaign:** Laeral Silverhand is the one the files name. The lore page calls her a "former Chosen of Mystra", and the Dragon Heist letter addressed to her still gives her the title "Chosen of Mystra". The campaign has no other Chosen of Mystra in play.
- **Messengers and omens:** She speaks through her Chosen, by sending an Animal Lord or a pixie, or through animals that are blue, white or multicolored (FRHoF). The wiki adds that mortals who saw her reported that she changed constantly, and that she sometimes took the form of a multicolored will-o'-wisp (*Cyclopedia of the Realms*; *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 2e). No source gives a formal omen.
- **Holy days:** No source gives her a holy day of her own, and the wiki's holy-day fields are blank. In Waterdeep she is one of the gods named at the Gods' Day bonfire on Marpenoth 15, and commoners leave violets on the altars of her temple on Ahghairon's Day, Eleasis 1.
- **Oath:** The wiki says her worshipers often swore "by Mystra's Truth" (*The Magehound*, novel).
- **Relations:** Wiki-sourced, with the Knights' allies and enemies from the FRHoF.
  - Azuth was the first Magister, a brash young wizard whom she sponsored to godhood as a demipower and the Patron of Wizards. He became her friend and adviser (*Powers & Pantheons*; *Cyclopedia of the Realms*; *Faiths & Avatars*).
  - She also sponsored Savras the All-Seeing. The two gods fought for her favor for many years, until Azuth defeated Savras and imprisoned him in the Scepter of Savras (*Powers & Pantheons*; *Volo's Guide to All Things Magical*).
  - She worked with Oghma and his cadre of Gond, Milil and Deneir, though she had some rivalry with Gond (*Cyclopedia of the Realms*; *Faiths & Avatars*).
  - The FRHoF calls Azuth, Kelemvor and Selûne her traditional allies and names Cyric as the foremost enemy of her Knights, who defend against his plots and seek out his temples and shrines to destroy them.
  - Bane tried to wrest power from her and captured and tortured her avatar during the Godswar. Talos did not get along with her (*Faiths & Avatars*).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact. The FRHoF says she has changed many times over the millennia, with two known passings of her mantle.
  - Her predecessor Mystryl sacrificed herself to protect the Weave from Karsus's Folly. In the moments after the Fall of Netheril a peasant girl with an aptitude for spells became the new goddess of magic and caught three of Netheril's floating cities as they fell. The wiki dates her apotheosis to -339 DR (*Netheril: Empire of Magic*).
  - She was more lawful than Mystryl and set new rules for spellcasters, among them a ban on the highest levels of magic, known to sages as Mystra's Ban (*Netheril: Empire of Magic*; *Lost Empires of Faerûn*). She created the title of Magister, held by one master of the Art at a time (*The Magister*).
  - She made Elminster Aumar a Chosen in 240 DR and rescued a wandering wizard from death in 464 DR, who became the Nameless Chosen and later Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun. In 761 DR she possessed the sorceress Elué, and the Seven Sisters followed (*The Grand History of the Realms*; *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e).
  - The House of Wonder in Waterdeep was dedicated to her in 1215 DR (*The Grand History of the Realms*).
  - In 1358 DR, in the last hour before the gods were cast down to walk Toril, she entrusted a pendant holding some of her power to the wizard Midnight. Bane then captured her, and she sought dissolution. Her avatar attacked Helm at the Celestial Stairway, and he destroyed it in an explosion above Castle Kilgrave (*Shadowdale*; *Waterdeep*, the 2e adventure).
  - At the end of the Godswar, Ao offered the post of goddess of magic to Midnight, who accepted reluctantly and took Mystra's name to smooth the transition (*Waterdeep*, the 2e adventure; *Faiths & Avatars*). *Dragon Heist* places this on the slopes of Mount Waterdeep.
  - In 1385 DR the Spellplague gripped the world. *Dragon Heist* says it has since been divined that Cyric's hatred for her boiled over into her murder, and that parts of Toril switched places with parts of another world. The Lathander page records the wiki's 4e account, in which Cyric and Shar killed her.
- **In the campaign:** Mystra is present in 1492 DR. The House of Wonder is a working temple with a high priest, and no file treats her as dead or absent. *Dragon Heist* says her death, rebirth and withdrawal from the world weakened Laeral, and the page treats those events as history (see Draft Notes).

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Using Mystra as a Deity
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice her as the patron of every spellcaster in the city. A sage, a priest or a regular at the bar says she is the Lady of Mysteries, the goddess of magic and of the Weave that every spell is drawn from, and that wizards, sages and alchemists favor her. They add that her priests look out for children with a spark and find them a teacher, and that the House of Wonder in the Sea Ward is the finest temple to the gods of magic in the world. Older Waterdhavians tell that she was once the mortal Midnight, raised to godhood on the slopes of Mount Waterdeep at the end of the Time of Troubles, and a sage adds that Cyric is blamed for murdering her in the Spellplague. Anyone who follows city politics knows that the Open Lord, Laeral Silverhand, is one of her daughters. Nobody in this conversation mentions Manshoon.
> - **Waterdeep:** Mystra has one temple in the city, the House of Wonder in the Sea Ward.
>   - The Dragon Heist Major Temples table lists "Mystra, House of Wonder, Sea Ward". The Sea Ward text calls it "surely the most splendid temple dedicated to the gods of magic" with Mystra foremost among them, and it points visitors to the ornate towers, brilliant mosaics and magical displays. Humble violets grow among the ostentation, planted in memory of Ahghairon, whose favorite flower they were. The temple stands near the crossing of Diamond Street and Delzorin Street.
>   - On Ahghairon's Day, Eleasis 1, commoners leave violets atop the altars of the House of Wonder. The Sea Ward's Pharra's Alley is named for the first leader of the House, though it is better known for its Circle of Skulls.
>   - Pilgrims come to Waterdeep to worship Midnight at Mystra's temple and to honor Kelemvor in the City of the Dead, because Ao was last seen on Mount Waterdeep when he made Kelemvor, Midnight and Cyric gods.
>   - On Gods' Day, Marpenoth 15, the Griffon Cavalry lights a bonfire on the peak of Mount Waterdeep for the gods who appeared in the city, and Mystra is one of those named.
>   - In **Fireball!** the House of Wonder is the fifth of nine stops on the nimblewright sweep. The file's row reads "Sea Ward | Quiet click | Temple guardian; high priest confirms its presence all morning with multiple witnesses".
>   - The Metal House of Wonders in the Dock Ward is the guildhall of the Splendid Order of Armorers, Locksmiths, and Finesmiths, and it has nothing to do with Mystra.
>   - No file names a priest of the House of Wonder, and no other shrine of Mystra exists in the campaign files. No city festival is hers alone.
> - **A cleric PC:** Arcana Domain (derived; Arcana Unleashed). Her domains are Arcana and Knowledge (SCAG, 2014). Arcana is the option built around amplifying magical power, and its always-prepared spells include *counterspell* and *dispel magic*, which suits the goddess who tends the Weave. Knowledge Domain (Heroes of Faerun) is the alternative for a cleric who serves her as keeper of lore. The Divine Order that fits is Thaumaturge (derived), because her dweomerkeepers wield both arcane and divine magic.
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of Devotion (derived; Player's Handbook 2024). The FRHoF lists paladins among the Knights of the Mystic Fire, who guard her temples and sacred sites, and Devotion's ideals of justice and order suit that duty. No oath concerns magic, so the fit is loose. A paladin who hunts Cyric's shrines as the Knights do could take Oath of Vengeance (Player's Handbook 2024) instead.
> - **A warlock PC:** No warlock connection in the sources.
> - **Campaign ties:** Laeral Silverhand, the House of Wonder in **Fireball!**, and Manshoon's plan. The details are in the GM Notes below. No campaign file ties the dragonward, the Walking Statues or the Blackstaff's office to Mystra, so do not add a link at the table.
> - **Never reveal:** That Manshoon means to use the ancient Weave knowledge in the Stone of Golorr to absorb the Weave and supplant Mystra. A player who asks a priest of Mystra about the Stone gets the Overview, and the party's own evidence is the only thing that adds to it.

## Overview

Mystra is the goddess of magic and of the Weave, the force from which every spell is drawn. Waterdhavians call her the Lady of Mysteries, and her House of Wonder in the Sea Ward is held to be the most splendid temple to the gods of magic in the world. Her followers are wizards, sages, alchemists and anyone else who works with magic, and they hold that magic should be preserved and shared with those who have the gift. Sages tell that she was once the mortal Midnight, made a goddess on the slopes of Mount Waterdeep, and the Open Lord, Laeral Silverhand, is one of her daughters.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Laeral Silverhand:** The Dragon Heist text makes her the fifth of Mystra's seven daughters, born in 765 DR. The campaign calls her one of the Seven Sisters and a former Chosen of Mystra. *Dragon Heist* says she resurfaced after the Spellplague and the Sundering, weakened by Mystra's death, rebirth and withdrawal from the world. The extent of her decline is a secret kept on her own page, and this page does not touch it. The wiki adds that Mystra herself asked Laeral to become Open Lord (wiki only, see **A City Watching Itself**).
> - **Khelben and the Blackstaff:** The campaign calls Khelben Arunsun the original Blackstaff and Laeral's late husband. His soul is in the staff that Vajra Safahr carries. No campaign file says that Mystra chose him or links the office of Blackstaff to her. The link is the wiki's alone, from the Nameless Chosen of 464 DR.
> - **Manshoon's plan:** Manshoon wants the Stone of Golorr for the ancient Weave knowledge encoded in its aboleth memory. He means to use it to absorb the Weave and supplant Mystra, and the vault's gold pays for the preparations. The plan links to **Gralhund Villa** and **Kolat Towers**. In **Gralhund Villa** the party does not learn his name, his goals or the Weave motive, so no priest of Mystra can speak of it before the party's evidence supports it.
> - **The House of Wonder in Fireball!:** The nimblewright detector gives a quiet click at the temple, and the high priest confirms the guardian was there all morning with multiple witnesses. Treat it as a cleared stop in the montage and add no further plot to it.

**Tone:** Wondering and exact. Mystra is the goddess of magic, so describe what magic lets people do before the rules she set on it, and keep her at a distance. The campaign writes no scene in which she appears.
