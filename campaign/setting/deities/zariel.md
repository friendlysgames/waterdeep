# Zariel

> **[GM]**

> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the patron entry for Zariel, the archdevil who rules Avernus and a Fiend patron for warlock characters. She is not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon and has no domains, so this page replaces the cleric and paladin guidance of a deity page with warlock guidance.
> - **Where it is used:** Zariel never appears in the campaign. She is a choice for a player building a warlock, and she is named once, in the Asmodeus page's history of the archdevils.
> - **Featured in:** no quest. The Nine Hells mural in the Asmodean Shrine (**Faction Outposts**) depicts her layer, Avernus, without naming her.
> - **Spoiler limit:** this page holds no hidden fact that changes play, so it has no Secret box. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier and never mentions the Cassalanters. Everything above it may be read by the GM only.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what little an ordinary Waterdhavian knows about Zariel, gives a player what a Fiend patron relationship with her costs, and states that the campaign has tied nothing to her so that no later page invents more.

*The Archduchess of Avernus*

- **Title:** Lord of the First, Archduchess of Avernus (wiki; 5e)
- **Other Names:** None given in the wiki. Her name is said to mean "Companion of Light" (*Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus* 5e, spoken by Reya Mantlemorn).
- **Pantheon:** None. She is an archdevil and a patron, not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon.
- **Areas of Concern:** The Blood War, the souls of soldiers, the defense of Avernus (derived from the wiki's accounts of her work and her cults; the wiki lists none)
- **Domains:** Not applicable. She grants no cleric domains.
- **Symbol:** [research]
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e, via the wiki)
- **Challenge:** 26 (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e, via the wiki)
- **Plane:** Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells. Her former home was Mount Celestia (wiki; 5e).
- **Typical Followers:** People who want to prove themselves in war, soldiers, veteran warriors and mercenary companies, in cults led by her cambions (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e, via the wiki)

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Zariel is an archdevil under Asmodeus. He made her an archdevil and called her his champion (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e). She rules Avernus, and the wiki gives her no domains and no calendar of holy days.
- **Her work:** She prosecutes the Blood War against the demons of the Abyss. She often leads invasions herself, and when she does not, she oversees Avernus's defense from a flying fortress (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).
- **How she sees herself:** In her own account her legions stand between the Seven Heavens and the Abyss, and she did not fall into evil but rose to shoulder a cosmic burden (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
- **What she offers:** Her agents give worshipers the training, skill and nerve for war that they lack, and ordinary cultists fight with more ferocity than they would alone. A mortal who pleases her may keep his memories after death and rise into a higher kind of devil, where others become lemures (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
- **How a pact works:** She wants the souls of the greatest warriors. She lets them live fuller lives so that they sharpen their skills and join her ranks disciplined after death. Her bargains are hard, eternal and indissoluble, and they read like ultimatums. She demands loyalty from every servant (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e; *Descent into Avernus* 5e). If she died, all her contracts would be void and a fair amount of cosmic chaos would follow (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).
- **How her agents recruit:** Her legions are always depleted, so she recruits mortal souls aggressively and looks for evildoers on the Material Plane who can bring more. Her agents act as war-profiteers. They fund evil organizations, hand out weapons and push people into sectarian violence. Where a zealous leader is useful, devils disguised as angels offer power in exchange for fighting the demons, and the wiki's example is Gideon Lightward (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).
- **Cults and cambions:** Her cults flourish in regions wracked by war. Their members are people who want to fight and lack the skill, people seeking fame through military service, refugees who cannot fight, and warriors who want a combat edge. Hobgoblins who left the goblinoid pantheon of Maglubiyet also join, and whole orders of knights, fighters' guilds and mercenary companies can fall under her sway. She has a taste for spawning cambions, and her cults are often headed by them (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
- **Size of the cult:** The cults crumbled after Bel took her place, because their devotion did not pass to him (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e). They grew again once she was reinstated (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).
- **Servants:** Osyluths carried her body out of the dead at the Ride (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e). The mortal knight Haruman, a heartless crusader before his descent, became a hell knight of undying loyalty to her (*Descent into Avernus* 5e). Styx devils guard the citadels and outposts that overlook the River Styx (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
- **Holy days, omens, messengers, temples:** None in the wiki or the campaign. [research] The devils disguised as angels under How her agents recruit are the only emissaries the wiki describes.
- **Tiefling blood:** A few tieflings carry her blood. They are stronger than most tieflings and have innate magic tied to aggression and battle (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
- **History of her fall and return:** Wiki-sourced, and the sources disagree on when she fell.
  - She was a solar under Lathander in the Seven Heavens of Mount Celestia, tasked with watching the Blood War in Avernus. She came to love battle, and her angelic peers kept refusing her requests to fight. Yeenoghu's massacre of a village under her protection was the last straw (*Descent into Avernus* 5e; *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
  - Late in 1354 DR she led the Hellriders of Elturel, mortal allies she had trained, into Avernus in the battle called the Ride. The devils beat them and the survivors fled. Asmodeus sent osyluths to fetch her body, let her recover, congratulated her and made her an archdevil (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e; *Descent into Avernus* 5e). The wiki cites the year to the comic *Infernal Tides* #2.
  - Bel defeated her and took her place. He imprisoned her in his Bronze Citadel, and for centuries his abishai cut away her flesh to feed their master (*Fiendish Codex II* 3e; *Manual of the Planes* 3e). A second account in *Dungeon* #197 (edition not given) has the Dark Eight imprison her, perhaps on Asmodeus's own design, and set Bel up as a puppet.
  - Bel lost Asmodeus's favor by failing to repel a demonic invasion of Avernus. Zariel was reinstated, took back the title of Lord of Avernus and nearly drove the demons out of the Hells (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e). The Asmodeus page dates this to before 1492 DR, and this page agrees with it.
  - The wiki's own note says the dates of her falls and rises were retconned across editions. *Guide to Hell* (3e) places her in the ancient Reckoning of Hell, and *Dungeon* #197 and *Dragon* #223 (editions not given) make her an archdevil from the founding of the Hells. The wiki also says her imprisonment by Tiamat in *The Rise of Tiamat* (5e) stays constant across editions.
  - Shortly after her reinstatement, in the late 15th century DR, she allied with the Red Wizards of Thay while Severin Silrajin tried to summon Tiamat into Toril. She sent mortal agents and helped with devil-summoning rituals, and Bel and his allies opposed her because they wanted Tiamat kept in Avernus (*The Rise of Tiamat* 5e).
  - In 1444 DR a vampire lord conquered Elturel, and a priest of Torm named Thavius Kreeg called on any power to save the city. Zariel appeared in a pillar of fire and gave him the Solar Insidiator, a sunlike sphere also called the Companion. It destroyed the vampire's army. The contract made the city, its lands and its oath-bound defenders forfeit to her, and after fifty years the Companion could take them (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).
  - In 1492 DR the Companion turned black and pulled Elturel into Avernus. She meant to drag more cities down after it, with Baldur's Gate next, and to use the citizens as fodder in the Blood War (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).
- **Relations with other archdevils and powers:** Wiki-sourced (5e unless noted).
  - Asmodeus admires her passion for war and counts her a jewel in his crown, though it is said she wants vengeance on him and plans to drive him out of the Hells (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
  - Bel is her most direct opponent, and the two detested each other despite Asmodeus's orders to cooperate. She put the advisor assigned to her in charge of weapons production to keep him busy. Asmodeus's backing kept Bel from challenging her openly, so he worked to make her look incompetent (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).
  - She refuses to make alliances or play politics, and each archdevil would depose her given the chance (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e; *Fiendish Codex II* 3e). Dispater retreated deeper into his Iron Tower when she returned, and Mephistopheles fears her wrath and looks for leverage over her (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).
  - Gargauth, the banished former archdevil, would gladly replace her as Lord of Avernus, and she would order him destroyed if she could (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).
  - Tiamat is a hostile relationship. Zariel resents the Dragon Queen, has sent agents to steal from her hoards, and tried to help her escape the Hells only because she could not kill her (*The Rise of Tiamat* 5e; *Descent into Avernus* 5e).
  - Lathander commanded her when she was a solar. The wiki lists him as her former patron deity.

## Overview

Zariel is the archdevil who rules Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells, and she is the Hells' most committed general in the Blood War against the demons of the Abyss. Scholars say she began as an angel of Mount Celestia and became a devil after she led mortal soldiers into Avernus against her superiors' wishes. She wants the souls of the best fighters, and the soldiers and mercenary companies who bargain with her are said to find her bargains eternal.

**Tone:** Martial and unbending. Zariel is a general who recruits soldiers, so when a player asks about her, describe the war she is fighting and what she offers fighters before describing what she asks of them.
