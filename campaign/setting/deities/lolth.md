# Lolth

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the deity entry for Lolth, the Spider Queen and god of the drow, and the record of the one rule the campaign sets for her: Bregan D'aerthe has rejected her.
> - **Where it is used:** Lolth never appears in person, and no source places a temple or cult of hers in Waterdeep. She reaches play through Bregan D'aerthe's contempt for her, which Soluun Xibrindas, the company's black coin and its first-meeting answers all state, and through the drow priestesses of the published *Dungeon of the Mad Mage*.
> - **Featured in:** The Bregan D'aerthe faction events **Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting**, **Houseless Noble**, **Kreb Drops the Cover**, **The Killer's Fate** and **The Compromised Eye**, and the Doom Raiders mission **The Dockside Killer**. Soluun also appears in **Sea Maidens Faire**, where the published shrine to Lolth in his stateroom is dropped.
> - **Spoiler limit:** everything above the `## Overview` may be read by the GM only. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier and never mentions Bregan D'aerthe. The Never reveal line holds the links between Jarlaxle, Zardoz Zord and the goddess.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Lolth, and states the standing rule that Bregan D'aerthe rejects her, so that no later page gives a company member a shrine, a prayer or a kind word for her.

*The Spider Queen*

- **Title:** God of spiders (FRHoF). The page uses FRHoF's title because FRHoF is newer than SCAG, whose printing reads "Goddess of spiders".
- **Other Names:** The Queen of Spiders and the Queen of the Demonweb Pits (FRHoF); Araushnee, her name before her exile from the elven gods (FRHoF); the Demon Queen of Spiders and the mother of the Dark Seldarine (*Dungeon of the Mad Mage*); Lloth, a variant spelling, and the Tyrant Poisoner, the name Eilistraee's followers use (wiki)
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian (FRHoF). SCAG (2014) and MTF file her under the Drow pantheon, the Dark Seldarine, as the older printing. The wiki adds that she was once a member of the Seldarine, the elven pantheon.
- **Areas of Concern:** Spiders, the drow, treachery and ambition, blood sacrifice, the rule of priestesses over drow houses (derived from the FRHoF and wiki text; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Trickery (SCAG, 2014); Trickery and War (MTF, 2014 rules)
- **Symbol:** Spider (SCAG, 2014). The wiki's 2e row gives a black spider with a female drow head at the bottom of the figure.
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil (SCAG, 2014)
- **Plane:** The Abyss, in the Demonweb Pits (FRHoF)
- **Typical Followers:** Evil drow, folk who travel the Underdark (FRHoF)

> Prove your worth to the Spider Queen by betraying whoever stands in your way.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Lolth is the leader of the Dark Seldarine. FRHoF says she considers herself the true god of the drow, though the other members of the pantheon disagree. The wiki ranks her a lesser deity in its 5e and 1e rows, an intermediate deity in 3e and 2e, and a greater deity in 4e.
- **What the faith asks:** Lolth is a cruel god who encourages her followers to backstab and betray each other, and she revels in their treachery (FRHoF). She demands sacrifices of treasure and blood, and the screams of victims echo through her lightless temples (MTF). The wiki adds that she required absolute fealty and counted any question about her motives as sin, yet she admired ambition more than loyalty (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e; *Demihuman Deities*; *Fiendish Codex I*, 3e).
- **What the faith offers:** In return for victims and adoration she grants signs of her favor, such as great success on a slave raid on the surface, a rival house's matron struck down by an unexplained illness, or an heiress born under good omens (MTF). The wiki says her favor was fickle and that a favorite could find her turning on them without warning (*Forgotten Realms Player's Guide*; *Demihuman Deities*).
- **Who follows:** Evil drow and folk who travel the Underdark (FRHoF). The wiki adds aranea, chitines, draegloths, deep dragons and a few elves (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). FRHoF reports that people with no tie to the Underdark have lately heard her call, and that trails of spiders guide them to aboveground cults dedicated to her.
- **Worship:** Her disciples wear web-patterned clothing and practice blood sacrifice, preferring unwilling victims. Their doctrine teaches them to find and kill non-worshipers, and they may pray to no other god except Kiaransalee and Selvetarm, her two allies in the drow pantheon. The walls of her grandest temples carry floor-to-ceiling bas-reliefs of webbing (FRHoF).
- **Clergy:** Her faith permits only women to rise to the priesthood, as a reflection of the matriarchy of spiders. In the Underdark her priestesses rule the noble houses of drow cities such as Menzoberranzan with absolute power, and some residents flee the tyranny for the surface or for communities that serve milder drow gods (FRHoF). The wiki says a few of her clerics were male, that her vestments carried spider motifs, and that her rituals called for dark clothing or none (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e; *Demihuman Deities*).
- **Orders:** The wiki lists two (*Demihuman Deities*). The Handmaidens of the Spider Queen, also called the Daughters of the Yochlol, are female crusaders tied to no city, sent to bring a whole city back into line, and they have destroyed at least three drow cities that strayed. The Militant Myrlochar, or Order of Soul Spiders, are male crusaders who serve a city's reigning matron mother and kill the targets she names.
- **Silken Stewards:** Certain disciples put a victim to sleep with Lolth's Sting, and the victim wakes cocooned in webs while baby spiders chew out through the ribcage within hours. House Auvryndar of Menzoberranzan pioneered the practice, and the Stewards supply other disciples with spiders for mounts, trackers, sentries and fighting. Their greatest foes are the militant orders of Eilistraee, and Underdark followers of **Silvanus** also oppose them. A *Lesser Restoration* spell cast on the host kills the eggs (FRHoF).
- **Chosen:** When a worshiper shows great promise, Lolth summons them to the Demonweb Pits for a test of faith and strength. Those who fail become driders, and those who succeed become Yor'thae, Chosen of Lolth, who have divine mastery over spiders and carry out her plans (FRHoF).
- **Chosen in the campaign:** None, since no campaign file names a Chosen of Lolth.
- **Messengers and omens:** Spiders of all kinds are seen as her omens, especially in the Underdark. She often sends yochlols, her signature demons, as messengers or spies (FRHoF).
- **Holy days:** No source gives a holy day for her in any edition. [research]
- **How she appears:** The wiki gives three forms, which she can change between at will. She can be a black widow spider twelve feet across with crimson eyes, a beautiful drow woman six feet tall dressed in clinging spiders, or the form thought to be her true one, a giant spider with a drow woman's head and torso (*Demihuman Deities*). The statues in *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* show her as a drow woman and as a monster with a drow upper body and a spider's lower body.
- **Relations in the drow pantheon:** Wiki-sourced (*Demihuman Deities* unless noted).
  - Every other member of the Dark Seldarine opposes her to some degree. She is the mother of **Eilistraee** (Deities) and Vhaeraun, and she considers Vhaeraun her real rival and Eilistraee a moody fool who is easy to manipulate (*Evermeet: Island of Elves*; *Menzoberranzan: The City*).
  - Kiaransalee and Selvetarm are her allies and act as her servants. Selvetarm is her grandchild and self-appointed champion.
  - She feared that **Ghaunadaur** (Deities) could sway the drow, but he did not care enough about drow matters to attract many of them (*Menzoberranzan: The City*; *Races of Faerûn*, 3e).
  - MTF points out that she is far from omniscient. Some drow live without bending to her, and communities of renegades often raise another of the Dark Seldarine as their patron, which swaps one evil oppressor for another.
- **Relations with other powers:** Wiki-sourced (*Demihuman Deities* unless noted).
  - She is the enemy of the Seldarine, above all **Corellon Larethian** (Deities), her former husband, then Sehanine Moonbow, who foiled her scheme, and Fenmarel Mestarine, who was her partner in adultery and did not join her betrayal. She put little effort into acting on the hatred and preferred to keep the drow fighting one another.
  - She once helped **Gruumsh** (Deities) try to kill Corellon, and the two became enemies after he failed (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
  - She had occasional alliances with **Loviatar** and **Malar** (*Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). The Malar page records his long-standing alliance with her against the Seldarine.
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - Before her fall she was Araushnee, a lesser elven god of destiny and artisans and the consort of Corellon. She ruled with him and Sehanine as the heads of their pantheon (*Demihuman Deities*; *The Plane Above*, 4e).
  - FRHoF says she tried to overthrow Corellon with an army of spirits and was banished to the Abyss, where she made herself a spider form and took the name Lolth. The wiki adds that she conquered the sixty-sixth layer of the Abyss, the Demonweb Pits (*Demihuman Deities*), and that she drew in Gruumsh, **Malar** and an army of hostile gods before the Seldarine beat her (*Evermeet: Island of Elves*).
  - Her church grew in the Second Crown War. After the Fourth Crown War the elves cursed the dark elves into drow and drove them underground, where she became the main drow goddess and urged them to found their first city, Telantiwar (*Lost Empires of Faerûn*, 3e; *Races of Faerûn*, 3e).
  - During the Time of Troubles she killed Zinzerena, a goddess from another world, and gained the portfolios of chaos and assassination (*Demihuman Deities*).
  - In 1369 DR she guided her high priestess Azurna to Undermountain to find the *Flame Sword of Lolth*, a weapon from the War of the Seldarine that Halaster Blackcloak had hidden there. In 1370 DR Azurna built the spider statue that blocked the vault, and Lolth manifested a giant drider avatar. An adventurer in the service of Khelben Arunsun defeated the avatar, and the sword vanished (*Descent to Undermountain*).
  - From 1372 DR the wiki's War of the Spider Queen has her fall silent, wake, and become a greater deity with the Demonweb Pits as an independent plane. In 1375 DR she staked the fate of the drow on a game of sava against Eilistraee (*The Grand History of the Realms*, 4e; the War of the Spider Queen novels).
  - Before 1479 DR she tried to build her own Weave from blue fire and relics of **Mystra**. It failed when Mystra returned (*Spinner of Lies* and *Archmage*, novels).
  - After the Second Sundering the dead members of the Dark Seldarine were revived, so she is no longer unrivaled although she still dominates (SCAG, 5e). Zinzerena escaped her and took back assassination, and chaos passed to Malyk, the drow god of rebellion and wild magic (MTF).
- **In the campaign:** Lolth is alive and present in 1492 DR, as every power is. The Silence, the War of the Spider Queen and the Demon Weave are history, and the post-Sundering state, with the Dark Seldarine revived, is the one in force. Bregan D'aerthe rejects her, as the Using box sets out.

> #### Using Lolth as a Deity
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice her as the god of a distant and hostile people. A sage, a priest or a regular at the bar says she is the Spider Queen, the chaotic evil god of the drow, shown as a giant spider or as a drow woman with a spider's body. They add that her priestesses rule the drow houses of the Underdark, that her worshipers offer blood sacrifice and are taught to kill anyone outside the faith, and that spiders are her omens. A well-read speaker mentions FRHoF's report of aboveground cults drawn by trails of spiders, with no cult placed in Waterdeep. The only line on the city's attitude to drow is that Fel'rekt Lafeen knows what happens to drow the Watch catches (`campaign/quests/faction-events/bregan-daerthe/s02-kreb-drops-the-cover/ev-01-kreb-drops-the-cover.md:136`). Which of her names the city uses is unsourced. [research]
> - **Waterdeep:** No source places a temple, shrine, priest, holy day or cult of Lolth in the city.
>   - The Dragon Heist Major Temples table has no Lolth row (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:30910-30997`). The two rows that could cover her are "All deities" at Holyhands House in the North Ward and "All elven deities" at the Temple of the Seldarine in the Castle Ward. No source says her faithful use either, and the wiki lists the Seldarine as a pantheon she left.
>   - The published adventure puts a shrine of Lolth in Soluun's stateroom aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, area U3, with Soluun "worshiping before" it (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:26345`, `:26350`). The campaign's U3 entry has Soluun cleaning his weapons and no shrine (`campaign/locations/sea-maidens-faire/05-scarlet-marpenoth.md:40`), so the shrine is dropped.
>   - Beneath the city, the published *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* has shrines and temples of Lolth on Levels 3, 4, 10, 11 and 12. They are listed in the GM Notes as seeds, since Undermountain is future work.
> - **A cleric PC:** The domains are Trickery (SCAG, 2014) and Trickery and War (MTF, 2014 rules). Trickery Domain (derived; Player's Handbook, 2024) is the fit. Its features include Blessing of the Trickster and Invoke Duplicity, and its magic of deception and stealth suits a goddess who prizes betrayal. War Domain (derived; Player's Handbook, 2024) is the alternative for a cleric who takes MTF's second domain. The Divine Order that fits is Thaumaturge (derived) for a priestess who works through omens, ritual and sacrifice, and Protector (derived) for the War choice. FRHoF says the faith lets only women rise to the priesthood, and a cleric who follows its doctrine serves a chaotic evil power that teaches its disciples to kill non-worshipers.
> - **A paladin PC:** Oathbreaker (derived; Dungeon Master's Guide, 2014), a loose fit. It belongs to a paladin who breaks sacred oaths to serve an evil power or a dark ambition, which suits a chaotic evil goddess who admires ambition and treachery. Oath of Vengeance (derived; Player's Handbook, 2024) is the alternative for a crusader of the Handmaidens of the Spider Queen, who punish drow cities that stray. No source lists paladins among her followers.
> - **A warlock PC:** Fiend Patron (derived; Player's Handbook, 2024), a moderate fit. Its theme is a pact with the Lower Planes, and the patrons it names include a demon lord. No source ties a warlock to her. FRHoF puts her home in the Abyss and has yochlol demons carry her messages, and the *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* text calls her a demon goddess and a demon queen.
> - **Bregan D'aerthe (standing rule):** Bregan D'aerthe has rejected Lolth as its goddess. No member worships her, keeps a shrine to her or invokes the Spider Queen with devotion, and members speak of her with contempt, as the goddess they walked away from. Do not give any company member a shrine, a prayer or a kind word for her.
> - **Campaign ties:** Bregan D'aerthe is the only faction that uses her, and Jarlaxle Baenre and Soluun Xibrindas are the NPCs. The campaign states the rule in these places, quoted in the GM Notes below.
>   - **Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting:** Asked "Do you serve Lolth?", Nevercott answers "Gods, no. My colleagues left the Spider Queen behind on purpose" (`campaign/quests/faction-events/bregan-daerthe/00-first-meeting/ev-01-first-meeting.md:375-379`).

>   - **Houseless Noble, The Dockside Killer, Kreb Drops the Cover, The Killer's Fate, The Compromised Eye:** Each repeats the rule or applies it to a speech. The lines are in the GM Notes.
>   - **No other file uses her.** No Xanathar's Guild, Zhentarim, Cassalanter, Sea Maidens Faire or Kolat Towers file mentions Lolth beyond the Soluun scenes above.
> - **Never reveal:** That Captain Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle. That Jarlaxle was marked for sacrifice to Lolth as an infant and rejected her. That Soluun's disownment is a cover story. A player who asks a company member about Lolth gets contempt and nothing about where the company comes from.

## Overview

Lolth is the chaotic evil god of spiders and the drow, called the Spider Queen and the Queen of the Demonweb Pits. Her priestesses rule the great drow houses of the Underdark, and her worshipers practice blood sacrifice. Spiders are her omens, especially below ground, and she sends demons called yochlols as messengers or spies. No temple of hers stands in Waterdeep.

> - **The coin:** In **Houseless Noble** every speaker mentions her with contempt (`campaign/quests/faction-events/bregan-daerthe/r50-houseless-noble/ev-01-houseless-noble.md:27`). Zardoz calls her "the old eight-legged lady with a knife in her" and says she is on the coin "so we remember who we told to go to hell" (`:233`). A member who has marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** hears Jarlaxle call the spider "the goddess my people were taught to kneel to, with a knife through her" (`:243`). A DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or History) check reads the design as an old drow house sigil with the spider canceled by the blade, a mark of defiance against the goddess (`:249`).
> - **Jarlaxle's history with her:** The History page says he was born to Yvonnel Baenre, survived his sacrifice to Lolth when the blade could not pierce him, was favored by her afterward as an agent of chaos, and rejected her. Auvryndar's priestesses serve Lolth in the published adventure, and the members voice contempt for the goddess while they trade (derived from the standing rule).
> - **Mad Mage seeds (published, future work):** The lines below are from `sources/adventure-wdmm.json`.
>   - **Level 2, Arcane Chambers:** Shunn "Spider Eyes" Shurreth, a drow exile favored by Xanathar's Guild, commands the northern Guild watch post (`:4026`, `:6148`). A drow priestess of Lolth cursed him into a spider-eyed shape and exiled him, and he keeps the curse because it earns him the Guild's favor (`:6208`).
>   - **Level 3, Sargauth Level:** The drow priestess T'rissa Auvryndar has taken part of Stromkuhldur, turned a Dumathoin temple into a hatchery for giant spiders and begun experiments that use humanoids as incubators for spider eggs (`:7417`). A ritual circle runs the Test of Lolth, and drow who fail it come back as driders and are exiled (`:9919`, `:9985`). Geldax Breer, a Waterdhavian lamplighter, waits in a cell as a sacrifice to Lolth (`:9802`).
>   - **Level 4, Twisted Caverns:** House Auvryndar keeps an outpost under the priestess Melith Auvryndar, with a shrine of Lolth (`:12604`, `:12643`).
>   - **Level 10, Muiral's Gauntlet:** Vlonwelv Auvryndar is rebuilding a temple to Lolth (`:27770`). The Haunted Temple of Lolth holds three priestesses turned into banshees, and Muiral visits to hear them (`:29633`, `:29688`). The level also has Lolth's Palace (`:30053`), the Dark Seldarine temples (`:30401`) and the Hall of the Dark Seldarine, whose fountain carries eight statues: Eilistraee, Malyk, Kiaransalee, Ghaunadaur, Zinzerena, Vhaeraun, Lolth and Selvetarm (`:30762`). Vlonwelv broadcasts announcements through the level from her spider throne, and one boasts that House Auvryndar holds Stromkuhldur and that Skullport will follow (`:29642`, `:29651`).
>   - **Levels 11 and 12:** House Auvryndar and House Freth fight over the Troglodyte Warrens (`:33038`, `:33296`). Erelal Freth, a priestess of Lolth, keeps a silver shrine and can summon a yochlol with a 90 percent chance of success if the shrine is intact (`:36007`, `:36012`).

**Tone:** Grave and played straight. Lolth is a god of treachery, so describe what her priestesses demand before what her favor looks like, and keep her at a distance, since the campaign writes no scene in which she acts. Any Bregan D'aerthe voice that mentions her does so with contempt.
