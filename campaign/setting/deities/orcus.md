> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the patron entry for Orcus, the demon lord of undeath and a Fiend patron for warlock characters. He is not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon and has no 5etools entry, so this page replaces the cleric and paladin guidance of a deity page with warlock guidance.
> - **Where it is used:** Orcus never appears in the campaign. He is a choice for a player building a warlock, and no campaign file or published adventure text names him.
> - **Featured in:** no quest. *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* names a book called *Tenebrous and the True Path to Lichdom* in Melissara's Study on Level 22, and the wiki lists Tenebrous as one of his names.
> - **Spoiler limit:** this page holds no hidden fact that changes play, so it has no Secret box. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier. Everything above it may be read by the GM only.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what little an ordinary Waterdhavian knows about Orcus, gives a player what a Fiend patron relationship with him costs, and states that the campaign has tied nothing to him so that no later page invents more.

*The Demon Lord of Undeath*

- **Title:** Demon Lord of Undeath (*Out of the Abyss* 5e, via the wiki)
- **Other Names:** The Prince of the Undead, the Prince of Undeath, the Lord of the Undead, the Blood Lord, Master of Vampires, Lord of Specters, the Goat-Horned Demon, the Shadow That Was (wiki infobox, books not edition-tagged). He is called Tenebrous in the wiki's accounts of his destruction and return (*Tome of Magic* 3.5).
- **Pantheon:** None. He is a demon lord and a patron, not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon.
- **Areas of Concern:** Undeath, the killing of the living, the raising of the dead as servants (derived from the wiki's portfolio of undeath and its personality section; the wiki lists none)
- **Domains:** Not applicable in 5etools. The wiki lists Chaos, Death, Demonic and Evil (3e) and adds darkness and undeath among the domains he influenced (*Faiths and Pantheons*).
- **Symbol:** The horned head of a goat, sometimes set over a skull-topped mace (*The Bloodstone Lands*; *Libris Mortis*; *Assault on Maerimydra*, via the wiki). The wiki's 3e infobox gives a black, skull-topped mace, and some cells use different iconography (*Monster Manual* 4e).
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil (wiki, 5e and 3e rows)
- **Challenge:** 26 (wiki, 5e row, with no citation of its own). The wiki's 3e row gives 28.
- **Plane:** The Abyss, in the layer of Thanatos (*Out of the Abyss* 5e). He rules from the palace of Everlost, in the bone-meal desert of Oblivion's End (*Dungeon Master's Guide* 5e).
- **Typical Followers:** Malevolent humans, orcs, ogres, giants and goblinoids, along with necromancers and others who want to become liches or vampires (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e; *Libris Mortis* 3.5; *Heroes of Horror*; *Monster Manual* 4e). The wiki's 5e worshiper field is blank.

> Let all life end, until only the dead remain. (derived)

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Orcus is a demon lord of the Abyss, and the wiki calls him one of the mightiest, a power that inspires dread in gods (*Monster Manual* 4e). Many mortals worship him as a god, but the wiki says he cannot grant true divine power, and that his clerics receive spells from an unknown source that some believe is the Negative Energy Plane (*Libris Mortis* 3.5). Another account says he once gained some form of actual divinity (*Dragon* 359).
- **What he wants:** He is a nihilist who seeks to end all life and darken every world, until only undead moved by his will remain (*Out of the Abyss* 5e; *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e). He cares for nothing but himself, not even his devotees and undead servants, and the wiki calls him a brilliant strategist (*Libris Mortis* 3.5; *The Throne of Bloodstone*).
- **His view of the undead:** The accounts disagree. *Fiendish Codex I* (3e) says he only tolerates his undead as tools against his rivals, and *Libris Mortis* (3.5) says he detests the mindless ones. Other scholars hold that he hates the living and means to spread undeath across the planes (*Monsters of the Multiverse*; *Out of the Abyss* 5e).
- **What he offers:** He gives limited powers to mortals who kill in his name, and these change them into mindless zombies or ghouls (*Out of the Abyss* 5e). He also blesses some demons with a change into undeath (*Monster Manual* 5e). The wiki credits him with the rituals and curses that produced the first ghouls, death knights, zovvuts and dream vestiges.
- **Cults:** His worship is spread across many isolated cults that act independently, hide from society, and gather where the dead are, in graveyards and secluded tombs (*Monster Manual* 4e).
- **Followers:** His cults draw the malevolent and the morbid, and the necromancers and would-be liches and vampires among them are the most notable (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e; *Heroes of Horror*; *Monster Manual* 4e). The undead in his palace and armies include zombies, wights, shadows, huecuvae, nightwalkers, sheet phantoms and death knights (*Monster Manual* 5e; *The Throne of Bloodstone*).
- **Skull Lords:** The leaders of the cults hold the title Skull Lord. To earn it, a follower must take control of a horde of undead from the Plains of Hunger and loose it on the Material Plane. A follower who fails is killed and raised as a lich, doomed to stay in Thanatos forever (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
- **Deathpriests:** A deathpriest who performs the right ritual can call an aspect of Orcus to appear (*Monster Manual* 4e). He can also see through the eyes of any bodak, the remains left when one of his followers dies, and read its memories (*Tomb of Annihilation*; *Monsters of the Multiverse*).
- **The Wand of Orcus:** His best-known relic is a skull-topped wand that can slay any living being (*Out of the Abyss* 5e). He sometimes leaves it where mortals can seize it, so that they spread chaos and evil on their worlds (*Dungeon Master's Guide* 5e). The wiki says the wand has been destroyed several times and that he can rebuild it over a century.
- **Where his cults are:** The wiki places them across Faerûn, in Vaasa and Damara (*Races of Faerûn*; *Player's Guide to Faerûn*). The Cult of the Goat's Head coordinates his worship in the Bloodstone Lands, and the Hunt Lords do so in the Neverwinter Wood (*The Bloodstone Lands*; *Storm King's Thunder*). Followers of Kiaransalee wiped out the Cult of the Goat's Head in Vaasa (*Demihuman Deities*).
- **Temples:** His temples stand on many planes, and the wiki names one on Toril, in the caverns of Deepearth (*Libris Mortis*; *The Mines of Bloodstone*). The wiki names no temple in Waterdeep.
- **Holy days, omens, messengers:** None in the wiki or the campaign. [research]
- **Relations with other powers:** Wiki-sourced.
  - He is one of a triad of demon lords, with Graz'zt and Demogorgon, who war on one another (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e). The wiki notes that *Champions of Ruin* puts Eltab in the triad in place of Demogorgon. He thinks Graz'zt a self-aggrandized narcissist, and he is believed to have fought Demogorgon for hundreds of thousands of years (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
  - For a short time he allied with Yeenoghu (*The Throne of Bloodstone*) and with the witch-queen Iggwilv, and he and Iggwilv schemed to overthrow Demogorgon as Prince of Demons (*Dragon* 359; *Dungeon* 150).
  - He opposes the Raven Queen, the goddess of death in the Shadowfell, and has schemed to strip her of her divine powers (*The Shadowfell: Gloomwrought and Beyond*). Her followers and his oppose one another over undeath (*Dragon* 397).
  - The drow goddess Kiaransalee killed him and took over Thanatos, and she then tried to erase every trace of him (*Dead Gods*; *Faiths and Pantheons*).
  - He was a patron of sorts to the mage Vecna, to whom he offered the ritual that made Vecna the first lich (*Dungeon Master's Guide* 5e). He was pleased with Vecna's follower Acererak, who for a time worshiped him (*Streams of Crimson*; *Tomb of Horrors* 4e).
  - He once imprisoned his foe Baphomet in the dungeons of his lair (*The Throne of Bloodstone*).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, and the wiki gives two accounts as alternatives.
  - In one he began as a mortal wicked spellcaster, probably a priest of a dark god. He rose through the demon ranks over thousands of years, from larva to balor (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
  - In the other he was a primordial of the Dawn War, older than the Abyss. With Baphomet and Demogorgon he cast down the Prince of Demons, Obox-ob, and the three rose to become demon lords (*Demonomicon*).
  - Whichever is true, he became the Prince of the Undead and took Thanatos, and then tried to take the title of Prince of Demons from Demogorgon (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e; *Dragon* 359; *Dungeon* 150).
- **History in the Realms:** Wiki-sourced, with each book named.
  - A servant of Orcus, Thargaun Crell, became the first Nentyarch of Tharos, later Narfell, in -1025 DR. About fifty years later Orcus gave him the Crown of Narfell, which corrupted his line for centuries (*The Grand History of the Realms*; *Races of Faerûn*).
  - He became the patron of the Red Wizard Zhengyi and helped him toward lichdom (the wiki dates this only as "the 10th DR"). Zhengyi built Castle Perilous in 1347 DR and conquered Damara over the next decade (*The Throne of Bloodstone*; *The Bloodstone Lands*).
  - In 1358 DR the duergar of Deepearth tried to summon an avatar of Orcus, and Gareth Dragonsbane's company stopped them. The company then stole the Wand of Orcus from Thanatos, destroyed it on an altar of Tiamat and ended Zhengyi's reign (*The Mines of Bloodstone*; *The Bloodstone Lands*; *Dragons of Faerûn*).
  - He grew indolent, and Kiaransalee killed him in his palace and ruled Thanatos in the mid-14th century DR (*Dead Gods*; *Faiths and Pantheons*). He returned as a burst of negative energy named Tenebrous, who killed several gods in a search for the lost wand. Adventurers destroyed Tenebrous with the Last Word, and Orcus' priest Quah-Nomag then resurrected him in a ritual on the Astral Plane (*Dead Gods*; *Manual of the Planes* 3e; *Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
  - In the late 15th century DR the demon lords were summoned to the Underdark, where Orcus used his necromancy to raise a dead elder brain named Cyrog. Demogorgon then cast him back to the Abyss, and the wand stayed behind in the Realms (*Out of the Abyss* 5e). Some years later, when the death curse struck Toril, he opened a portal in Chult so that his minions could take the Soulmonger (*Streams of Crimson*).

## Overview

Orcus is the demon lord of undeath, called the Prince of the Undead and the Blood Lord, and he rules the layer of Thanatos in the Abyss. Scholars say he hates the living and wants every world emptied of them, until only undead remain under his will. His cults are scattered and secretive, they gather in graveyards and tombs, and their symbol is the horned head of a goat. No temple of his stands in Waterdeep.

**Tone:** Bleak and without warmth. Orcus cares for nothing but himself, so when a player asks about him, describe what his cults do for his sake and the dead they command, and never what he gives in return.
