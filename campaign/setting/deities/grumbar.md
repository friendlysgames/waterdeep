> - **What this page is:** the deity entry for Grumbar, the Earthlord and elemental god of earth, for players who want a druid, a stone-minded cleric or a devout character with a remote patron, and for a GM who needs a power behind a mountain shrine or a pledge sworn on stone.
> - **Where it is used:** Grumbar never appears in person, and no campaign file or published adventure names him. The page records that Waterdeep gives him no temple, so that no later page invents a priest, a shrine or a worshiper.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what an ordinary Waterdhavian says about Grumbar, and says plainly that no source gives him a temple, a festival or a worshiper in Waterdeep.

*The Earthlord*

- **Title:** God of earth (SCAG, 2014; FRHoF has no entry for him)
- **Other Names:** Gnarly One, King of the Land Below the Roots, Boss of Earth, Boss of the Earth Elementals (wiki, citing *Faiths & Avatars*, *Forgotten Realms Adventures* and *Chelimber's Descent*). Regional forms: Etugen in the Hordelands and Grome among the duergar of Zakhara (wiki).
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian (SCAG, 2014), and one of the four Elemental Lords (wiki, *Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e)
- **Areas of Concern:** Earth, solidity, changelessness, oaths, the riches beneath the ground (derived from the wiki portfolio; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Knowledge (SCAG, 2014). The wiki's 3e list adds Balance, Cavern, Earth, Metal and Time.
- **Symbol:** A mountain (SCAG, 2014). The wiki's 3e symbol is mountains on a purple field.
- **Alignment:** Neutral (SCAG, 2014)
- **Plane:** The Elemental Plane of Earth, in his realm the Great Mountain (wiki, 1e to 3e sources; neither FRHoF nor SCAG gives a plane)
- **Typical Followers:** Druids (wiki 5e row, citing SCAG). The wiki's 3e row adds earth elemental archons, fighters, monks and rangers.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Grumbar is one of the four elemental powers worshiped in Faerûn, and SCAG places him in the Faerûnian pantheon. The wiki rates him a deity in its 5e row, a primordial in its 4e row, a greater deity in its 2e and 3e rows and a lesser power in its 1e row. It adds that even as a primordial he kept his worshipers and a power equal to a god's (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
- **Temperament:** He is quiet and resolute, and he resists change more than other powers do. He carries secret knowledge and guards the riches found within the earth (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e). He does not bargain with visitors to his plane, who must accept his terms or refuse them outright (*Manual of the Planes*, 1e).
- **How he appears:** As an enormous earth elemental with red eyes that glimmer like rubies (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e). A visitor to the Plane of Earth who seeks an audience sees his face form on a wall inside a pocket of air, and the face is different for each visitor (*Manual of the Planes*, 1e).
- **Who follows:** He has few worshipers in Faerûn, as do the other elemental powers apart from Kossuth, and he cares little for most of them (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). Earth genasi are among the people who honor him (*Races of Faerûn*, 3e).
- **Druids:** Among druids who honor the First Circle, he is more than a god of earth. He embodies the rolling hills and the fertility of the soil (SCAG, 5e, as the wiki cites it).
- **Clergy:** The church is organized into small sects called Holds. Each Hold has three groups of seven members, divided by skill and supported by lay people. His followers preach against boarding ships and sailing to uncharted lands, and they prefer to explore the mainland of Faerûn (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
- **Orders:** The Disciples of the Changeless Face are stoic monks who protect Grumbarryn knowledge, and the Sardonyx Knights are crusaders who protect his temples (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
- **Temples:** Notable temples stand in mountain ranges, among them the Spine of the World, the Giantspires and the Muaraghal Mountains, and a great place of worship lies in the Wu Pi Te Shao Mountains of Kara-Tur (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e). His altar in the lower levels of Sadrach's Splinter received the Eye of Gruumsh in 1486 DR (*The Sentinel*, novel).
- **Rites and devotion:** An altar inscription praises his oath as unchanging and true and says that the earth stays strong to protect all (*Chelimber's Descent*, Adventurers League). His favored weapon is a stony fist, which the wiki renders as a warhammer (3e). The wiki gives no holy days (3e: "None known").
- **Messengers and omens:** No source names a messenger. [research] The wiki lists earthquakes as his manifestation (*Deity Do's and Don'ts*) and names earth elementals, earth genasi and xorns among his associated creatures.
- **Beyond Faerûn:** The nomadic Tuigan of the Hordelands knew him as a goddess named Etugen, the source of good pasture, herd growth and protection from some diseases (*The Horde*; *Races of Faerûn*). In Zakhara he is one of the cold gods of the elements, whom few are willing to worship (*Adventurer's Guide to Zakhara*), and the duergar there call him Grome.
- **Divine realm:** He dwells in the Great Mountain on the Elemental Plane of Earth (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e; *Player's Guide to Faerûn*, 3e). When that plane fell into the Elemental Chaos in the Spellplague, he made a new domain called Root Hold (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e).
- **Relations:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - He has loose ties at best with the other powers of elemental earth: Geb, **Garl Glittergold**, Dumathoin, Laduguer and Urogalan (*The Inner Planes*).
  - He opposes **Akadi** and her flimsy, ever-changing ways (*The Inner Planes*). Akadi's order, the Knights of the Wind's Four Quarters, carried out quests for her church elders or fought his followers (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
  - In the contest between the archomentals Sunnis and Ogrémoch, he secretly balanced one against the other and stayed the most powerful ruler of elemental earth (*The Inner Planes*). Another book makes Ogrémoch his child, and Ogrémoch's twin is Entemoch (*Menzoberranzan: City of Intrigue*).
  - He and the orc goddess Luthic had a secret romance that **Gruumsh** did not know of (*The Sentinel*, novel).
  - On the island of Kelthann, the islanders believed the winter storms at sea came from his rivalry with **Umberlee**. She tried and failed to wash them off the island during the Time of Troubles, and the failure strengthened their faith in him (*Pirates of the Fallen Stars*, as the wiki's Umberlee page cites it).
  - The wiki files him among the members of the First Circle, the loose group of nature gods that, by the Talos page's account of the wiki, includes **Silvanus**, **Chauntea**, **Mielikki** and **Eldath** (SCAG, 5e).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact. The wiki gives no account of how he came to be.
  - In the 4e account he is one of the primordials that stayed on Toril when it separated from Abeir, and one of four Elemental Lords worshiped in Faerûn (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e).
  - By 1369 DR he had made only four recorded appearances on Toril. The first was at the creation of the Great Rift in southern Faerûn (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
  - During the Second Sundering, in the late fifteenth century DR, Luthic gave him the Eye of Gruumsh to reaffirm her love after lies spread by **Shar**. In answer, his earth refilled the vast Underchasm (*The Sentinel*, novel).
- **In the campaign:** Grumbar is alive and present in 1492 DR, as every power is. SCAG describes him in the present tense. The wiki's past tense and its 4e label of primordial are edition history, and Draft Notes records them.

## Overview

Grumbar, the Earthlord, is the neutral god of earth and one of the four elemental powers worshiped in Faerûn. His holy symbol is a mountain, and he is pictured as an enormous elemental of stone with eyes like rubies. Few people follow him, druids of the First Circle among them, and his temples stand in distant mountain ranges such as the Spine of the World and the Giantspires. Waterdeep has no temple of his.

**Tone:** Quiet and steady, in the brisk reference register. Grumbar resists change and does not bargain, so describe the stone and the oath before the god, and keep him at a distance, since the campaign writes no scene in which he acts.
