## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Baba Yaga is an archfey and an arch-hag, not a god. The wiki calls her an extraordinarily powerful mage (*Dungeon Master's Guide* 1e), an archfey (*Dungeon* 196, 4e; *Manual of the Planes* 4e) and an arch-hag (*Dungeon Master's Guide* and *Monster Manual* 5e revised). It gives her no domains and no calendar of holy days.
- **Her manner:** She is fickle and dangerously unpredictable, and she is as likely to eat a worshiper as to help one. Politeness does not win her favor, and rudeness does not guarantee her anger (*Dungeon* 196, 4e). She typically asks a visitor whether they came of their own free will or at another's bidding (*Dungeon* 196, 4e), and she warns that every secret has its cost (*Monster Manual* 5e revised).
- **What she offers:** She will give up some of her secrets in return for another, and her bargains are worded more dangerously than those of any fey (*Manual of the Planes* 4e). Many sages and adventurers have sought her out for her knowledge of magic (*Dungeon* 196, 4e). The wiki gives no pact terms, boons or prices. [research]
- **Her hut:** The Dancing Hut carries her through all the worlds and planes. Its Hall of Gateways holds a portal called the World Gate, which links to every world she knows and to their Shadowfells and Feywilds (*Dungeon* 196, 4e).
- **How she travels:** Outside the hut she flies in a large iron mortar and pestle and steers with the pestle. She carries a broom to sweep away any trace of where she has been (*Dungeon* 196, 4e; *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything* 5e).
- **Where she lives:** When she is not traveling, she and the hut stand in Murkendraw, a Feywild swamp that many kinds of hag also inhabit (*Manual of the Planes* 4e).
- **In the Realms:** The wiki gives two Realms items, both from sources of unstated edition.
  - Volo and Elminster wrote that, by report, her hut had appeared near Rashemen in north-eastern Faerûn in the 14th century DR (*Volo's Guide to All Things Magical*). In 1492 DR the report is at least ninety-two years old (derived).
  - She once prophesied that the ruler of Mag Tureah, a region of the Feydark with several portals to Toril, would be slain by his own son (*Dragon* 420; the region is from the *Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*).
- **Hags and worshipers:** Some night, green and annis hags worship her (*Dragon* 345). The wiki adds that she was somehow related to those three kinds of hag (*Dragon* 83, 1e). It names no other worshipers and describes no clergy, rites or temples.
- **Servants:** Her guardian animals are mostly cats, some mundane and some magical and intelligent, along with guard dogs, wolves and sometimes dire wolves. Her stables hold well-bred horses, magical livestock, pegasi, nightmares and intelligent horses that can cross planes. She treats all her servants poorly (*Dungeon* 196, 4e).
- **Daughters and wards:** The wiki names three daughters and two humans she helped raise.
  - Her three hag daughters are Skabatha Nightshade, Bavlorna Blightstraw and Endelyn Moongrave (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e).
  - She helped raise two humans. Kostchtchie became a Faerûnian demon lord (*The Grand History of the Realms*; *Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus* 5e; *Player's Guide to Faerûn*).
  - The other, Tasha or Natasha, is Iggwilv, listed as her adoptive daughter. She became a powerful archfey (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e). The Iggwilv entry adds that Baba Yaga's three daughters later betrayed Iggwilv (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e).
- **Relations with other powers:** Wiki-sourced (*Dungeon* 196, 4e).
  - Archfey respect her power and are very cautious around her.
  - Her dealings with the gods are as fickle as the rest of her. She tends to keep company with evil gods, tricksters and mischief makers, and she is also known to associate with devils.
- **Legends:** Wiki-sourced (*Dungeon* 196, 4e).
  - Heroes are said to have outwitted her by shoving her into an oven or trapping her in a lake of fire, and she came back unharmed each time, so some believe fire cannot hurt her.
  - One folk tale says dawn, noon and night all obey her whims. A rumor says she has no legs and moves only by her mortar and pestle.
- **Holy days, omens, messengers, temples:** None in the wiki or the campaign. [research]
- **Origin or rise:** The wiki gives no origin story. Its history section holds only the prophecy and the Rashemen report above.

## Overview

Baba Yaga is the Mother of All Witches, an archfey and arch-hag whose Dancing Hut travels between the worlds and planes. Sages say she trades one secret for another and words every bargain more dangerously than any fey. She is as likely to eat a worshiper as to help one, and neither politeness nor rudeness changes her mind. Hags are said to worship her, and an old report places her hut near Rashemen.

**Tone:** Courteous, unpredictable and exacting. Baba Yaga trades in answers, so when a player asks about her, describe what she knows and what she would ask in return before describing what could go wrong.
