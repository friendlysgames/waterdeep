> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the patron entry for Iggwilv, the witch-queen and archfey who wrote the *Demonomicon*, and an Archfey patron for warlock characters, with a Fiend alternative through Graz'zt. She is not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon and has no domains, so this page replaces the cleric and paladin guidance of a deity page with warlock guidance. She comes from the world of Oerth in the Greyhawk setting, outside the Realms.
> - **Where it is used:** Iggwilv never appears in the campaign. She is a choice for a player building a warlock, and two other Deities pages name her in passing: Graz'zt (as his lover and enemy) and Orcus (as an ally in a scheme against Demogorgon).
> - **Featured in:** no quest. Her name reaches the campaign's sources only through the spell *Tasha's hideous laughter*, which appears in the Rooftop Chase table of *Dragon Heist* and in two spellbooks in *Dungeon of the Mad Mage*.
> - **Spoiler limit:** this page holds no hidden fact that changes play, so it has no Secret box. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier and never mentions the Cassalanters. Everything above it may be read by the GM only.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what little an ordinary Waterdhavian knows about Iggwilv, gives a player what an Archfey patron relationship with her involves and what the sources leave open, and states that the campaign has tied nothing to her so that no later page invents more.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Iggwilv is an archfey of the Feywild and a former human archmage, and she is immortal and cannot die of age (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e). An older account counts her among the most powerful beings in the multiverse, level with the demon lords (*Dungeon* 149, 3.5e). The wiki lists no holy days, no worshipers and no temples for her.
- **Names:** The wiki says Iggwilv and Tasha began as separate characters and were joined into one person in *Expedition to the Ruins of Greyhawk* (3e, 2007). It also says that the sources never settle which of her names came first.
- **Her work:** She is best known for the *Demonomicon of Iggwilv*, a book of demon lore (*Expedition to the Ruins of Greyhawk* 3e). She also wrote *The Faceless Lord*, an unpublished study of the demon prince Juiblex, and the in-world book *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything* (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e; *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything* 5e). Her spells include *Tasha's hideous laughter*, *Tasha's caustic brew*, *Tasha's mind whip* and *Tasha's otherworldly guise* (*Dragon* 359; *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything* 5e).
- **Her skills:** She was a master of summoning fiends, planar exploration and necromancy (*Return of the Eight*, edition not given), and in later life she gained command over fey beings (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e). Her years in the Abyss taught her how its powers rank and what they do to one another (*Dungeon* 149, 3.5e).
- **What she offers:** The wiki gives no offer, no doctrine and no pact terms. The one patron relationship it records is sponsorship. As Zybilna she sponsored at least two warlocks, the amenable old Madryck Roslof and the kenku Kettlesteam (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e).
- **Her aides:** The dwarven clerk Kalimanzaros stayed loyal to her for centuries, and the high elf Raezil Uthemar spied on other fey courts for her. Her familiar is a quasit named Iggrik (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e). At one point she had four arcanaloth apprentices (*Dungeon* 150).
- **Her court:** She rules Prismeer from the Palace of Heart's Desire in the guise of Zybilna (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e). The palace holds a mirror that shows whoever looks into it what they want most. It also holds her cauldron, an artifact she made with Baba Yaga's help, which can freeze everyone within a thousand feet in time (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e).
- **Holy days, omens, messengers, temples:** None in the wiki or the campaign. [research] The wiki's only agents are the aides above.
- **Relations with other powers:** Wiki-sourced.
  - Graz'zt and she were lovers and enemies, and their bond kept changing. Each has at times been the other's consort, prisoner and master. They trusted each other very little and kept no secrets from each other (*Expedition to the Ruins of Greyhawk* 3e; *Dragon* 414). While she was his prisoner in the Abyss she bore him many children, and he hid them in secure places to keep them from harm (*Dragon* 414). The wiki lists Iuz and Drelnza among her children without naming a father. The Graz'zt page names Iuz as their son (*Fiendish Codex I* 3e).
  - The wiki calls her an enemy of the Nine Hells, partly because of her interest in Graz'zt. It names no book for the line.
  - Baba Yaga adopted her and is the Mother of All Witches. Baba Yaga's three daughters, Bavlorna Blightstraw, Skabatha Nightshade and Endelyn Moongrave, are her adoptive sisters and form the Hourglass Coven (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e).
  - Orcus was a short-lived ally, and the two schemed to overthrow Demogorgon as Prince of Demons (*Dragon* 359; *Dungeon* 150, as recorded on the Orcus page).
  - The archmage Mordenkainen gave her a magical chessboard (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - She was born on Oerth and adopted by Baba Yaga, when she went by Natasha the Dark (*Dragon* 83, 1e).
  - She moved to Niflheim (*Dungeon* 149, 3.5e). She later settled in the Abyss and studied demon-summoning, sought more power and made many enemies among the chaotic fiends, then fled to other worlds for her safety (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e).
  - She moved to Prismeer and ruled it as Zybilna. Over time she became a fey being and turned her studies from demonology to that new state (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e).
- **What became of her:** The Hourglass Coven and its allies in the League of Malevolence betrayed her. They used the magic of her own cauldron to freeze the Witch Queen and many of her palace's inhabitants in time (*The Wild Beyond the Witchlight* 5e). The wiki gives no year and no later fate.
- **Her status in 1492 DR:** The wiki does not say. Its last event for her is the freezing, and no source dates it or says how it ended. The campaign treats every power as present in 1492 DR, so this page uses the present tense and leaves open whether she is free. [research]

## Overview

Iggwilv is the Witch Queen, a sorceress from the distant world of Oerth who wrote the *Demonomicon*, the best-known book of demon lore. Sages say Baba Yaga raised her, that she lived among the demons of the Abyss, and that she later became an archfey ruling a realm of her own in the Feywild. She is also called Tasha, and wizards in Waterdeep know her name from the spell *Tasha's hideous laughter*. Some tales say her adoptive sisters froze her in time, and nobody can say what became of her afterward.

**Tone:** Learned and unhurried, with a dangerous edge. Iggwilv is a scholar who collects powers, so when a player asks about her, describe what she knows and what she has made before describing the enemies she made along the way.
