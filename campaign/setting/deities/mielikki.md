*The Lady of the Forest*

- **Title:** God of Forests (FRHoF). SCAG and the 2014 Player's Handbook print "Goddess of forests", and the wiki gives her a feminine gender.
- **Other Names:** Our Lady of the Forest, the Forest Queen (FRHoF); Mistress of the Forest (wiki, 1e and 2e), the Supreme Ranger and Daughter to Silvanus (wiki, 2e), and Khelliara in Rashemen, as scholars of other lands propose (wiki)
- **Pantheon:** Faerûnian
- **Areas of Concern:** Forests, forest creatures, rangers, dryads, good folk and lost children in the woods (derived from the FRHoF, SCAG and wiki text; no entry lists Areas of Concern)
- **Domains:** Nature (SCAG, 2014)
- **Symbol:** Unicorn's head (SCAG, 2014)
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good (SCAG, 2014)
- **Plane:** The Beastlands (Krigala) (FRHoF)
- **Typical Followers:** Fey, foresters, rangers (FRHoF)

> Keep the forest, and the good folk lost in it, safe from harm.

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** Mielikki is a god of the Faerûnian pantheon. The wiki calls her a lesser power in 1e, a lesser or intermediate power in 2e and an intermediate deity in 3e. FRHoF and SCAG give no rank.
- **Temperament:** FRHoF calls her good-natured, confident and fiercely loyal to her allies. SCAG (2014) says people see her as remote and spiritual, less human than many gods, and that her attention and favor are hard to attract. The wiki adds that she was cheerful and disliked speaking formally. It also says she healed the injured animals and friends she found, where Silvanus would leave them to the circle of life (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e).
- **What the faith asks:** No source gives a creed. Her priests protect the forests of Faerûn and use magic to speak with and defend the animals and plants in them (FRHoF). Ordinary folk ask for her protection when children are lost in the woods, and she is said to watch over good folk in any forest, however dark or cruel. Few people speak of her outside quiet woodland, and she is said to reign supreme where a woodland inspires wonder (SCAG, 2014).
- **Worship and shrines:** Her followers keep no official temples. They gather in peaceful glades or build shrines, and a typical shrine is a dead tree trunk carved with her unicorn's head. The shrine marks the point beyond which locals do not cut timber or hunt, and loggers often raise one at the end of a job to thank her for the wood and their safety (FRHoF; SCAG, 2014). SCAG says she has many shrines, particularly on the Savage Frontier.
- **Clergy:** Her priests wear capes of leaves that follow the seasons, green in summer, burnt orange in autumn and dotted with flowers in spring. In winter they wear crowns of twigs instead. Whatever they wear, they keep her holy symbol over the heart, carved of ivory or bone or stitched in silver thread (FRHoF). The wiki adds that in 1369 DR she ordered a complete restructuring of her clergy, which brought back druids (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
- **Who follows:** Fey, foresters and rangers (FRHoF). The wiki says most rangers revere her as their leader and watchful mother (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e), though SCAG (2014) says many rangers of the North pray to Gwaeron Windstrom, who is believed to carry their words to her. The wiki also lists druids, elves, half-elves, dryads and treants among her worshipers, and many Harpers (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e). It adds that most members of the Emerald Enclave follow her (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e). Her worship is strongest in the North, the High Forest, the Silver Marches and the Dalelands (2e and 3e).
- **Manifestation and messengers:** When she makes her will known she appears as a confident voice in a forest glade, or she sends a dryad, a hawk or a unicorn (FRHoF). The faithful hold that her most common manifestation is a whisper in the rustling of leaves (*Dragon* 384, 4e; *Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
- **Omen:** No source gives one. [research]
- **Holy days:** On Greengrass and Midsummer she blesses her followers with the Wild Ride. Unicorns gather in herds and gallop through the woods, and her followers ride them through the night over astonishing distances (FRHoF). The wiki's 2e row adds the four seasonal feasts at the solstices and equinoxes, and its 2e and 3e text adds Shieldmeet to the ride. In the campaign calendar Greengrass falls between Tarsakh 30 and Mirtul 1, and Midsummer between Flamerule 30 and Eleasis 1. No file ties a Waterdeep celebration to her.
- **Chosen:** She gives each Chosen an undying unicorn as a mount and companion. Some Chosen transcend their bodies and become disembodied manifestations of natural places, and FRHoF names Jeryth Phaulkon as one, a woman from Cormyr who now inhabits a garden estate in Waterdeep.
- **Chosen in the campaign:** Jeryth Phaulkon, a Waterdhavian noblewoman who lives as a disembodied voice in the gardens of Phaulkonmere. She cannot be harmed there and can cast any druid spell. The wiki says Mielikki came to Jeryth in a dream in 1367 DR and offered to make her Chosen. The goddess wanted a champion to stop the Hoar Fang gnolls from laying waste to the Lurkwood, and Jeryth was their captive at the time (the *City of Splendors* boxed set). The wiki also names an older line of Chosen, the Green Regents of the Delimbiyr Vale, which no campaign file uses.
- **Relations with the nature gods:** FRHoF says she is allied with Silvanus but takes pity on hurt creatures he ignores. SCAG (2014) says Silvanus is sometimes thought of as her father and Eldath as her sister, but that she walks her own path. The wiki says she served Silvanus alongside Chauntea and Eldath (1e, 2e and 3e). It says "Daughter to Silvanus" was an honorific and not a fact (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e), and that her calm tended to ease Silvanus's rage at those who harmed nature (*Dragon* 384, 4e).
- **Servants:** FRHoF says Lurue, the god of unicorns, is her close companion and serves as her mount when she hunts in the Beastlands. SCAG (2014) says her unicorn symbol leads some people to confuse the two. The wiki names Gwaeron Windstrom, the Mouth of Mielikki, as her messenger to mortal rangers in the North (2e). It also names Shiallia, who plants seeds in the High Forest (2e) and is counted as her exarch after the Spellplague (4e).
- **Allies and foes:** The wiki lists Shaundakul, Lathander and Nobanion as allies, and Malar, Moander, Myrkul, Talona and Talos as enemies (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e; *Faiths & Pantheons*, 3e). FRHoF adds that her rangers serve as a line of defense against marauders and the followers of Malar. Among the gods of other peoples the wiki lists Corellon Larethian as an elven ally (*Demihuman Deities*).
- **Origin and rise:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named on each fact.
  - She came from the Finnish pantheon, where she was a forest goddess, and chose to build a new following on Toril among its nature-worshipers (*Cyclopedia of the Realms*, 1e; *Faiths & Avatars* and *On Hallowed Ground*, 2e).
  - In 720 DR she manifested at the Dancing Place in High Dale among more than a dozen gods to inspire the founding of the Harpers, and she has supported them since (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e).
  - She came through the Time of Troubles in 1358 DR with little trouble, since she already lived on the Prime Material plane. Afterward she took the portfolio of autumn from the dead Myrkul (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e). Myrkul reclaimed it after his return in the Second Sundering (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, 5e).
  - As Silvanus's power grew, hers dwindled. In 1369 DR she restructured her clergy, which restored her strength and let her found her first divine realm in the Outer Planes (*Faiths & Avatars*, 2e).
  - That realm was the Grove of the Unicorns in Krigala, the top layer of the Beastlands (2e). In the 4e books she lives with Silvanus in his domain in the Deep Wilds (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e).
- **In the campaign:** Mielikki is alive and present in 1492 DR. Her Chosen speaks in Waterdeep, and no file has Mielikki herself appear, speak or act in a scene.

> #### Using Mielikki as a Deity
> - **The ordinary Waterdhavian's view:** Voice her as a forest goddess whom the city seldom thinks about. A sage, a priest or a regular at the bar says she is the Lady of the Forest, the goddess of forests and rangers, and that her symbol is a unicorn's head. They add that people ask her to watch over children lost in the woods, and that rangers tend to pray to Gwaeron Windstrom instead of to her. The place they name is the Shrines of Nature in the Sea Ward, a fenced park that only the ward's residents may enter. Anyone invited into Phaulkonmere has heard a woman's voice in the leaves, and Jeryth Phaulkon calls the goddess "the Lady of the Forest" in every Enclave scene.
> - **Waterdeep:** Mielikki has one shrine site in the city, the Shrines of Nature in the Sea Ward, which she shares with **Silvanus**. Her Chosen lives at Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward.
>   - The Dragon Heist Major Temples table lists "Mielikki, Silvanus, Shrines of Nature, Sea Ward". The Sea Ward text calls the Shrines "resplendent gardens dedicated to nature gods like Mielikki and Silvanus", a block from the Tower of Luck. The park is closed to all except residents of the Sea Ward, and passers-by can glimpse the shrines, statues and fountains through the iron fence that surrounds it.
>   - The table writes "Shrines of Nature" and the Sea Ward text writes "Shrines to Nature". This page follows the table's name.
>   - Phaulkonmere is an old estate and garden in the Southern Ward, and no file calls it a temple or a shrine. The published adventure says its walls enclose fabulous gardens and its buildings are covered with moss and ivy. The Enclave's gate stands open to anyone the Enclave has invited.
>   - The table also lists Holyhands House in the North Ward as a temple for all deities, which includes her. It also lists the Temple of the Seldarine in the Castle Ward for all elven deities. The wiki places her in the Seldarine through her half-elf aspect Khalreshaar (*Demihuman Deities*), but no file says that temple honors her. [research]
>   - No campaign file names a keeper, a priest, a festival or a hook at the Shrines of Nature, and none says whether a resident may bring guests inside. [research]
>   - No other shrine or temple of Mielikki exists in the campaign files, and no city festival is tied to her.
> - **A cleric PC:** The domain is Nature (SCAG, 2014). The 2024 Player's Handbook has no Nature Domain, so the closest subclass among Life, Light, Trickery and War is Life Domain (derived). The fit is loose. It rests on her healing of hurt creatures and on the wiki's 4e domains, which include Life. The Divine Order that fits is Thaumaturge (derived), because her priests work through magic to speak with and defend plants and animals. A cleric who stands guard against Malar's followers beside rangers would take Protector instead (derived). A druid or ranger is the closer fit for her faith than a cleric, since the wiki's 3e class table lists only rangers and druids.
> - **A paladin PC:** Oath of the Ancients (derived). The oath protects light, life and the natural world, which matches a goddess of forests. No source lists paladins among her followers, so the fit rests on her portfolio alone.
> - **A warlock PC:** The Archfey patron is a loose fit (derived). No source names a warlock of Mielikki, but FRHoF lists fey among her worshipers and her messengers include dryads and unicorns.
> - **Campaign ties:** Jeryth Phaulkon and the Emerald Enclave, plus two holy symbols of Lurue in **Finding Floon**.
>   - The Enclave organization page lists Jeryth as "Disembodied presence, Chosen of Mielikki; patron of Phaulkonmere; highest Enclave authority in Waterdeep." The Enclave events name her as "Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, disembodied presence, she/her)". The GM Notes below hold the details.
>   - No campaign file calls Mielikki the Enclave's patron. The files call Jeryth the patron of Phaulkonmere. The only source that ties Mielikki to the Enclave as a body is the wiki, which says most of its members follow her (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e). This page follows the wiki on that point and tags it.
>   - Floon Blagmaar's necklace is a holy symbol of Lurue, a unicorn bas-relief on a blue pearl chain, and a locket in the Zhentarim warehouse offices bears a horse head that suggests Renaer Neverember's mother was a devotee. FRHoF makes Lurue Mielikki's companion. No file ties either character to Mielikki.

## Overview

Mielikki is the Lady of the Forest, a neutral good goddess of woodlands, their creatures and the rangers who guard them. People seldom speak of her outside quiet woods, and her followers keep glades and carved tree-trunk shrines rather than great temples. In Waterdeep she shares the Sea Ward's Shrines of Nature with Silvanus, and only the ward's residents may enter. The Emerald Enclave is based at Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward, where her Chosen, Jeryth Phaulkon, speaks from the leaves.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Jeryth as Mielikki's voice:** Jeryth speaks one or two short sentences at a time and never repeats herself. She never swears and takes no side in mortal politics. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest". Her approval shows in the garden, where flowers open along the path, the grass lies flat where a friendly visitor walks and the leaves move when she speaks. The Jeryth page treats her direct interventions as backed by divine mandate.
> - **What Mielikki does in play:** She acts only through her Chosen. Mielikki never appears in an event, and Jeryth is the whole of her reach. When asked about the vault gold, Jeryth answers once with "That question is for the Lords to settle", and the Enclave takes no side.
> - **Messengers:** The Enclave's animals carry only Melannor Fellbranch's words, because Jeryth sends no one on missions. No scene uses the dryads, hawks or unicorns that FRHoF gives Mielikki as messengers.
> - **The Sea Ward and the Castle Ward:** Jeryth senses an infernal influence in the Sea Ward, and the Enclave knows it is there. Nobody in the Enclave knows whose it is. The Shrines of Nature stand in the same ward, and no file connects them to it. Jeryth has also felt a presence dreaming below the Castle Ward for months, and her page says what a member can learn about it.
> - **Phaulkonmere and Kolat Towers:** Phaulkonmere stands one block south of Kolat Towers. Until **Manshoon Named** is marked for a member, Enclave speakers say "the Splinter" and never point toward the towers.

**Tone:** Gentle and distant. Mielikki is a goddess of quiet places, so describe what the forest does before anything anyone says of her, and keep her at a remove, since the campaign reaches her only through Jeryth's few words.
