# Queen of Air and Darkness

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this page is:** the patron entry for the Queen of Air and Darkness, the archfey who rules the Unseelie Court and an Archfey patron for warlock characters. She is not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon and the 5etools deity list has no entry for her, so this page takes every field from the wiki, tagged, and replaces the cleric and paladin guidance of a deity page with warlock guidance.
> - **Where it is used:** She never appears in the campaign. She is a choice for a player building a warlock, and two other Deities pages name her: Auril (a fey belief that the Frost Sprite Queen is the same power) and Maglubiyet (early goblins served in her court). Akadi's page carries a similar title, the Queen of Air, which belongs to a different power.
> - **Featured in:** no quest. The only fey material in the published adventure is the Fey Day festival on Ches 19, which names Titania, Oberon and Hyrsam and does not name her.
> - **Spoiler limit:** this page holds no hidden fact that changes play, so it has no Secret box. The Overview is the common-knowledge tier and never mentions the Cassalanters. Everything above it may be read by the GM only.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM voice what little an ordinary Waterdhavian knows about the Queen of Air and Darkness, gives a player what an Archfey patron relationship with her can offer, and states that the campaign has tied nothing to her so that no later page invents more.

*Ruler of the Unseelie Court*

- **Title:** None given in the wiki. She is called the ruler of the Unseelie Court (revised 5e *Dungeon Master's Guide* and *Monster Mythology* 2e, via the wiki).
- **Other Names:** None in the wiki. Some fey of the Shiverpine Forest took the Frost Sprite Queen, a title of **Auril**, to be the same power (*Dragon* 367, 4e era, via the wiki). A 2e legend makes her the "dark sister" of **Titania** (*Monster Mythology* 2e, via the wiki).
- **Pantheon:** The Unseelie Court, the wiki's fey pantheon of that name. She is an archfey and a patron, not a god of the Faerûnian pantheon. The wiki also ranks her an intermediate deity in 2e and 3e.
- **Areas of Concern:** Darkness, illusion and other magic, murder, the evil fey (derived from the wiki's 2e and 3e portfolio lines; the wiki lists no Areas of Concern as such)
- **Domains:** None in 5etools. The wiki lists the 3e domains Air, Chaos, Darkness and Trickery (*Dragon* 359, via the wiki).
- **Symbol:** A ten-faceted black diamond (*Monster Mythology* 2e, via the wiki). The wiki's 3e row says only "black diamond".
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil (wiki; 2e and 3e). The wiki's 5e alignment field is empty.
- **Challenge:** [research] The wiki gives no challenge rating and no 5e statistics.
- **Plane:** The Feywild, where she rules the Unseelie Court (wiki; 5e, citing *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*). Older books put the court in Phlegethon, a layer of Pandemonium (*Monster Mythology* 2e; *Dragon* 359, 3e).
- **Typical Followers:** Evil elves and fey (*Monster Mythology* 2e, via the wiki), served by the unseelie fey

> Work your magic and your killing in the dark, where the light cannot see them. (derived)

## Influence and Followers

- **Rank:** She is an archfey who rules the Unseelie Court (revised 5e *Dungeon Master's Guide*; *Monster Mythology* 2e). The wiki's 2e and 3e rows rank her an intermediate deity, and its 5e rows give only the word archfey. It lists no holy days in any edition.
- **No avatar:** She has no physical form, and the court only feels her presence. If magic is used to see her, she appears as a beautiful but terrible fey woman with angular features, bone-white skin, all-black eyes and long black hair (*Monster Mythology* 2e).
- **Portfolio:** The wiki gives magic, particularly illusion, along with darkness and murder (2e), and darkness, magic, murder and the evil fey (3e). Its 2e class table lists her as a 19th-level illusionist.
- **Her court:** The Unseelie Court is the foe of the Seelie Court (*Demihuman Deities* 2e). The wiki says its accounts place the court either in Phlegethon in Pandemonium (*Monster Mythology* 2e; *Dragon* 359, 3e) or in the Plane of Faerie (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, 5e).
- **Servants:** Unseelie fey serve her, among them brambles and quicklings (*Demihuman Deities* 2e) and spriggans (*Planes of Chaos* 2e). She has a fondness for yeth hounds and hell hounds (*Planes of Chaos* 2e). The glouras, fey beings of the Underdark, have ties to her court (*Drizzt Do'Urden's Guide to the Underdark* 5e). Early goblins served in her court in the Feywild (*Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse* 5e, as the Maglubiyet page records).
- **Worshipers and clergy:** The wiki's 2e row names evil elves and fey as her worshipers, and its cleric-alignment grid marks only the three evil alignments (derived from the grid). It describes no priests, orders, rites, temples, omens or messengers. [research]
- **The Black Diamond:** The wiki lists an artifact of immense power and evil, also called the Night Diamond, that sits atop her throne in the Unseelie Court. Its origin is unknown, and the wiki does not link it to the diamond in her origin story below (edition not given).
- **What she offers and asks:** The wiki describes no boon, spell, pact term or demand. [research]
- **Relations with other powers:** Wiki-sourced, with the book or edition named where the wiki gives one.
  - Titania, the Summer Queen, rules the Seelie Court and is her sister in the 2e origin below. The Titania wiki page softens this to a rumor, and says Titania refused to wage open war on her and grieved for her instead (*Monster Mythology* 2e). It adds that Titania's throne, carved from ice in the shape of a dragon, mirrors the Queen's throne of darkness and shadow (edition not given).
  - The elven gods of the Seldarine oppose her: **Corellon Larethian**, Erevan Ilesere, Rillifane Rallathil, Sehanine Moonbow, Solonor Thelandira and Angharradh. To them she personifies the corruption that can grow even in those of fey lineage (*Demihuman Deities* 2e).
  - **Auril** is the Frost Sprite Queen, and some fey communities, such as those of the Shiverpine Forest in the Deep Wilds, believed the two were one power (*Dragon* 367, 4e era). The wiki adds, citing a post by Ed Greenwood, that the Queen was only impersonating Auril, with Auril's support, to win more followers on Toril. The Auril wiki page dates that reading to after the Sundering.
  - **Maglubiyet** is the god who later conquered the goblinoid peoples, whose earliest goblins served in her court (*Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse* 5e, as the Maglubiyet page records).
- **Origin:** Wiki-sourced, from *Monster Mythology* (2e), and it is the only account the wiki gives.
  - She was the sister of Queen Titania in the land of Ladinion. Dwarves mining a mountain to the south found a great ten-faceted black diamond and brought it to the Seelie Court as a gift for the fey queen.
  - Titania was away swimming that day, so her sister accepted the gift. The diamond began to corrupt her and change her nature.
  - One day she took the diamond and other magical treasures and left in a chariot of smoke and fire that was said to have come out of the sky. Some time later the mountain the dwarves had been mining exploded, killed the dwarves and covered the land in sickening smoke.
  - The wiki gives no later history for her, no 3e, 4e or 5e account of her rise, and no date for any of it.

> - **Waterdeep:** None. The Major Temples of Waterdeep table in *Dragon Heist* lists no temple of hers, and no campaign file or wiki entry places a shrine or cult in the city. The one fey custom in the sources is Fey Day on Ches 19. It does not name her, and the campaign calendar lists Fey Day as masked balls the day before the Grand Opening (`campaign/setting/history/09-timekeeping-and-the-festival-calendar.md:61`, `:102` and `:115`).
> - **A warlock PC:** Archfey Patron (derived; 2024 *Player's Handbook*) fits her closely, and the wiki files her under Archfey patrons. Her illusion magic suits the Archfey's enchantment and illusion spells (derived). No source gives features specific to her. What she wants from a warlock is not sourced. What her portfolio of darkness, magic and murder points to is a patron who rewards concealment and killing (derived). The price of a pact is not sourced either. The wiki describes no clergy, so a cleric of hers has no church to join in the sources. [research]

## Overview

The Queen of Air and Darkness is the archfey who rules the Unseelie Court, the dark fey court that stands against Queen Titania's Seelie Court. It is said that she was once Titania's sister and that a black diamond, given to the Seelie Court by dwarves, corrupted her. She has no body of her own, and the unseelie fey serve her, brambles, quicklings and spriggans among them. Waterdeep has no temple or shrine of hers, and the few who bargain with her are warlocks.

**Tone:** Dark, courtly and sorrowful. She is a queen who was ruined by a gift, so when a player asks about her, describe the court and the stone that changed her before describing what her servants do.
