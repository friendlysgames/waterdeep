# Jarlaxle Baenre

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - This is the villain profile for Jarlaxle Baenre, the secret lord of Luskan and the leader of **Bregan D'aerthe**, who walks Waterdeep as J.B. Nevercott and Zardoz Zord. It sets out his three personality tracks, his relationships, his goals and the one design note that makes him playable.
> - Use it when you run any scene where Jarlaxle or his company acts or reacts. Phase 1 covers Acts I and II. Phases 2 and 3 split into an adversary track and an ally track at **Sea Maidens Faire** in Act III, and the ally track's debt comes due in **Vault of Dragons** in Act IV.
> - A character can learn that Zord is Jarlaxle Baenre in **Fireball!**, from the Eyecatcher ledger or, for a Bregan D'aerthe member, in his private cabin. Mark each such character with the **Jarlaxle Unmasked** Event Outcome. The full reveal conversation takes place at **Sea Maidens Faire**.
> - Jarlaxle appears in **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers** and **Vault of Dragons**. The **Sea Maidens Faire** location journal maps his fleet, and the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** location journal holds the strongbox where his agents lifted Eye #3.
> - The company's response teams, outposts, escalation row and revelation list live in the **Bregan D'aerthe** page of the Factions Guide. Who the company is lives in the **Bregan D'aerthe** page of Organizations.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Entry and Activation.** Two separate mechanics govern Bregan D'aerthe in this campaign. Membership and villain activation run on different tracks, and the personality phases below key off the villain track.
> - **Membership.** Membership runs during **Trollskull Alley**, like any other faction. Three drow lieutenants shadow the party for several days. J.B. Nevercott, who is Jarlaxle in a *hat of disguise* posing as a haberdasher, then visits Trollskull Manor and offers enrollment to any interested PC.
> - **BD Contact Severed.** This Event Outcome is marked per character, like membership. A character is marked by giving Sergeant Marek Dunmere their name in the **Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting**, by attacking Nevercott, or by exposing a Bregan D'aerthe drow in **Kreb Drops the Cover**.
>   - The mark closes the company for that character only. The other characters keep the status they earned, and the watchers still leave Trollskull Alley for the whole party when a character reports them.
>   - The door stays closed for the rest of Acts I through III. **Vault of Dragons** is the first quest that may offer that character anything from the company, and **Sea Maidens Faire** reads the outcome to decide how Jarlaxle treats the character. The **Contact Severed** Event runs the consequences.
>   - Jarlaxle's briefs and debriefs fire for any party that deals with him, whether or not a character is marked.
> - **Ryvarra Identified.** **The Neighbors** marks this Event Outcome when both checks succeed. It changes how Nevercott opens his visit and does not change whether the visit happens.
> - **BD Acknowledged.** **The Factions Come Calling** marks this Event Outcome when Nevercott's visit goes ahead. **Fireball!** reads it, and it shapes how Zord treats the party at **Sea Maidens Faire**.
> - **Villain activation.** Jarlaxle has known of the Stone, the Eyes and the vault since he arrived in Waterdeep, no later than Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR. He enters the Grand Game as a named player when the **Jarlaxle Informed** Event Outcome is marked during **Fireball!** and read in **Gralhund Villa**.
>   - The outcome records only that the party spoke directly with him, as Zord or revealed. It does not mean he learned anything.
>   - It gates his Bregan D'aerthe team at **Gralhund Villa**, where Fel'rekt Lafeen's team takes the empty Artheyn Manor across Saerdoun Street, and it gates his Phase 1 Zardoz Zord activity.
>   - Bregan D'aerthe membership does not escalate him. The outcome does.
> - **Identity and Jarlaxle Unmasked.** His identity stays hidden behind J.B. Nevercott and Zardoz Zord. Nevercott never admits to being anyone else, even when a player guesses correctly, and a correct guess does not mark the outcome.
>   - In **Fireball!** a character can learn the name in two ways. Both folios of the Eyecatcher ledger open with "Sold through the Eyecatcher, J. Baenre, for the Company", and a character who reads either one and succeeds on a DC 14 Intelligence (History) check knows the name. A Bregan D'aerthe member taken to his private cabin sees the persona drop, and he names himself.
>   - Asking Zord directly or watching the crystal ball does not give the name on its own.
>   - Mark **Jarlaxle Unmasked** for each character who learns the name, and record the character's name. The Harper mission **A Friend's House** marks it too, for a character present when he gives his name in private.
>   - Until a member is marked, no Bregan D'aerthe speaker says "Jarlaxle" to that member. They say "the captain" or "J."
>   - The full reveal conversation takes place at **Sea Maidens Faire**.
> - **Where to read more.** See **Session Zero** and **Player Factions Overview** in the GM Guide and the **Bregan D'aerthe** page in Organizations. The Players' Guide preview lists Bregan D'aerthe without naming its contact, so Jarlaxle introduces himself in play.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM portray Jarlaxle as a specific, three-dimensional villain through three personality tracks, a relationship table and three goals.

*This page contains significant spoilers for the campaign. We recommend reading it so that you understand the primary villains and their motivations. Some Gamemasters, however, may prefer not to have this full knowledge, so proceed only if you wish to have all the available information up-front instead of learning things as you play.*

Jarlaxle Baenre is the drow leader of **Bregan D'aerthe** and the secret lord of Luskan, a swashbuckler who likes schemes that leave his enemies baffled and who travels in Waterdeep as a carnival captain. His core motive is to win Luskan a seat in the Lords' Alliance. He means to buy it with intelligence and an Eye of the Stone, and he already holds Eye #3 aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. His loyalties run to himself first and to the company second.

## Background

- Jarlaxle was born to Matron Mother Yvonnel Baenre of House Baenre, the first house of Menzoberranzan. By his birth order he was to be sacrificed to Lolth, but the blade could not pierce the infant and his brother Doquaio died in his place (Forgotten Realms Wiki; novel *Road of the Patriarch*).
- Lolth favored him afterward as an agent of chaos, and he rejected her. She withdrew her favor, and the matron mothers of Menzoberranzan never learned of it (Forgotten Realms Wiki; novel *Road of the Patriarch*).
- Bregan D'aerthe has rejected Lolth too. Its members speak of her with contempt, as the goddess they walked away from, and Soluun gives Jarlaxle the devotion that a drow of the houses would give the Spider Queen (campaign ruling; the **Soluun Xibrindas** page).
- He left his house and name, though not his connections, and founded the company from the rogue males of destroyed and disgraced houses (Forgotten Realms Wiki, novel *Homeland*; published adventure). Almost all members are male, because female drow rarely take orders from a male, and a female drow can earn a place by decrying the matriarchy and convincing him she would be an asset (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**).
- In 1376 and 1377 DR the company helped bring down the Host Tower of the Arcane, and Luskan became a free port (Forgotten Realms Wiki; novel *The Pirate King*).
- Five High Captains rule Luskan. After 1485 DR they trade legally across the Sword Coast and are secretly under Jarlaxle's sway, and the published adventure has him declaring himself the city's secret lord (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**; `sources/adventure-wdh.json` line 32178).
- His latest scheme is to legitimize Luskan by making it a member of the Lords' Alliance. The city's reputation has thwarted every earlier attempt, and the Alliance's leaders have voiced their opposition. He aims to persuade **Laeral Silverhand** to champion Luskan even if it costs the Alliance other members (`sources/adventure-wdh.json` line 32179).
- He also wants to oust Neverwinter and its lord, **Dagult Neverember**, from the Alliance. The published adventure calls Neverember his fiercest political opponent, and it has Jarlaxle wanting the dragonstaff of Ahghairon as leverage in his negotiations (`sources/adventure-wdh.json` lines 22941 and 549).
- The Sea Maidens Faire is a legitimate business he already owned before he turned to Waterdeep. It is a traveling carnival out of Luskan that tours the Sword Coast in three ships, the *Eyecatcher*, the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser*, and Captain Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle in the magical guise of an Illuskan human. He spends most of his time aboard the *Eyecatcher* (`sources/adventure-wdh.json` line 32180; **Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**).
- The fleet reaches Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21, the opening day of Fleetswake, and leaves at dawn on Tarsakh 20 (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**; **Structural Rules**).
- He has an alliance with Lantan, arms his lieutenants with Lantanese firearms that run on smokepowder, and keeps the Lantanese submarine *Scarlet Marpenoth*. He plans to flee Waterdeep in it if his scheme unravels (`sources/adventure-wdh.json` line 32181).
- In the campaign the *Scarlet Marpenoth* has been moored in Deepwater Harbor since he reached the city, and it keel-docks beneath the *Eyecatcher* when the fleet arrives (the **Sea Maidens Faire** location journal, area overview).
- He reached Waterdeep already knowing of the Stone, the three Eyes and the vault, and Nar'l's reports keep him current. The nimblewright ledger puts him in the city no later than Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**).
- Zord sells nimblewrights to wealthy families across the wards, and each one harvests intelligence through a crystal ball aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* (**Bregan D'aerthe** page, Organizations).
- Eye #3 began in **Renaer Neverember**'s mourning locket. Manshoon's Zhentarim seized the locket the night before Ches 1 and pried it open at the Candle Lane warehouse. Xanathar's Guild raided the warehouse and carried off the Eye with the rest of the back office without knowing what it was, and Bregan D'aerthe agents already inside Xanathar's hideout lifted it that night. It reached Jarlaxle before the party reached the hideout, and Manshoon's cell never held it (**Running the Villains**; the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** location journal, area overview). He keeps it in his stateroom, U4, aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* (the **Sea Maidens Faire** location journal, area overview).
- **Nar'l Xibrindas** is Xanathar's trusted drow advisor and secretly Jarlaxle's best intelligence source on the Guild. He reports by *Sending* spell, and if forced to choose between himself and Bregan D'aerthe, he will choose himself (the **Nar'l Xibrindas** page).
- **Vessa**, a doppelganger, sits in the Cassalanter social circle under a permanent human cover. She is how Jarlaxle alone outside the household and the cult knows about the family's pact, and he does not tell Bregan D'aerthe members unless they work it out themselves (**A Debt in Blood**; the **Vessa** page).
- **Ryvarra** is a field observer at the Yawning Portal who catalogs Undermountain traffic for his Waterdeep network. She is the first sign that he had eyes on the party from their first night in the city (the **Ryvarra** page).
- His other names are GM names. J.B. Nevercott is the haberdasher who visits Trollskull Manor, Captain Zardoz Zord is the carnival master, and Rongquan Mystere is the Shou owner of the Seven Masks Theater, whom the **Sea Maidens Faire** structure document also has attending the Shipwright's Ball (`sources/adventure-wdh.json` lines 3601 and 9066; `campaign/structure/arc-h-sea-maidens-faire.md` line 79).

## Personality Phases

Phase 1 is universal. After the reveal, the track splits based on whether the PCs choose opposition or negotiation.

*Phase 1 (universal): Zardoz Zord*

| When active | The mode |
|-------------|----------|
| Acts I–II *(Zord activity keyed to the party's contact: the **Jarlaxle Informed** Event Outcome)* | The disguise; a plausible, theatrical Illuskan eccentric who is too observant; PCs may like him before they know who he is; the performance holds but the intelligence behind it keeps slipping through |

*Trigger to Phase 2:* PCs penetrate the disguise, or Jarlaxle decides they've earned the reveal. Either way, "Zardoz Zord" ends here.

---

*Adversary Track: if the PCs choose to oppose or heist against him*

| Phase | Name | When active | The mode |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 2A | The Unmasked | **Sea Maidens Faire** (adversary path) | True identity revealed without negotiation; Jarlaxle is gracious about it — he respects the attempt and doesn't rage; the deal is no longer on the table, but the disdain is minimal; he treats them like colleagues who chose the wrong firm |
| 3A | The Graceful Enemy | **Sea Maidens Faire** onward, adversary | He doesn't hunt them, but he doesn't help them either; Nar'l's intelligence is redirected to protect Bregan D'aerthe's position; he may counter-heist them (recover what they took), may simply outmaneuver them; the posture is professional, not vendetta |

*Trigger to Phase 3A:* PCs successfully steal the Eye, or the confrontation resolves without an alliance.

---

*Ally Track: if the PCs negotiate or join Bregan D'aerthe*

| Phase | Name | When active | The mode |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 2B | Jarlaxle in the Game | **Sea Maidens Faire** (ally path) | True identity revealed as an invitation; negotiates as an equal; his offer (Eye for Lords' Alliance membership for Luskan) is genuine and he'll honor it; he's charming because it's easier than not being charming, not because he's performing |
| 3B | The Patron | **Sea Maidens Faire** onward, ally | Asset rather than adversary; provides intelligence, drow operatives, the Faire as a safe house; the political favor comes due at **Vault of Dragons**; he'll remind them once, gracefully, that what he wants hasn't changed |

*Trigger to Phase 3B:* PCs choose alliance, accept the deal, or formally join Bregan D'aerthe.

---

*Pop culture inspirations:* Ocean (Ocean's Eleven — the plan within the plan, executed with absolute calm); Tyrion Lannister (the outsider working the system's cracks, sharper than everyone who underestimates him); Hannibal Lecter (not the violence — the quality of stillness, the sense that he already knows what you're going to say)

## Relationships

| NPC | Role | Nature of relationship |
|-----|------|----------------------|
| Nar'l Xibrindas | Embedded spy | Inside Xanathar's Guild; Jarlaxle's most valuable asset; Jarlaxle has a contingency if Nar'l is discovered (has not shared it with Nar'l) `[research]` |
| Manshoon | Mutual wariness | Non-interference arrangement through Acts I to III, fragile, which ends when Manshoon's strike team hits the *Eyecatcher* at **Sea Maidens Faire**, whatever the party does; Jarlaxle has better intelligence and intends to keep it that way |
| Xanathar | Target of management | The beholder is being handled via Nar'l's selective intelligence; Jarlaxle prefers a controlled beholder to a power vacuum `[research]` |
| Renaer Neverember | Political connection | Worth cultivating; Renaer's relationship with Laeral is an indirect path to the Open Lord's attention; Renaer doesn't know who he met `[research]` |
| The Cassalanters | Aware but uninvested | Knows about their infernal bargain through intelligence; not interested in getting involved unless strategic upside appears |
| The PCs | Most interesting variable | Has been watching since **Finding Floon** (Nar'l's reports reached him `[research]`, and his agents were in the Xanathar sewer hideout); knows their names, faction ties, and general competence by **Sea Maidens Faire** |

**Relationship notes**
- Nar'l reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending*. Xanathar suspects him of disloyalty and has set a grell to kill him at the first proof, and it does not know he reports to Bregan D'aerthe or to Jarlaxle.
- Each of Jarlaxle and Manshoon considers the other the only genuine peer in the Grand Game. Manshoon has identified Fenerus Stormcastle as a Bregan D'aerthe cut-out and ordered his abduction, and Jarlaxle will act unilaterally if the abduction proceeds. That is a warning step. The arrangement ends in any case when Manshoon's strike team hits the *Eyecatcher* at **Sea Maidens Faire**.
- Jarlaxle knows about the Cassalanters' pact through Vessa and tells no Bregan D'aerthe member unless they work it out. Cassalanter imps watch the Sea Maidens Faire, and his counter-intelligence has not detected them.
- Soluun Xibrindas carries forged Bregan D'aerthe identification, and his public disownment is a cover story. Jarlaxle knows about the Dock Ward murders and has not decided what to do about them.

## Goals

- **Win Lords' Alliance membership for Luskan.** This is the real prize, because Bregan D'aerthe's Luskan operations need political legitimacy. Method: trade intelligence and an Eye to the PCs or directly to the Open Lord. Contingency: if the avenue closes, Bregan D'aerthe continues without it, and Jarlaxle loses a round, not the game. Connects to the **Vault of Dragons** political aftermath.
- **Hold one Eye of the Stone.** He wants it as a bargaining chip, not for personal use. Method: he already holds Eye #3 aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* (Bregan D'aerthe agents inside Xanathar's hideout lifted it the night Xanathar's Guild raided the Zhentarim warehouse), and Nar'l's intelligence tells him where the other Eyes are. He keeps it as the bargaining chip and moves on the others before the other factions consolidate. Contingency: negotiating it from the PCs is also acceptable and preferable for the relationship. Connects to **Sea Maidens Faire**.
- **Make Waterdeep a permanent base.** The Sea Maidens Faire is seasonal, and Jarlaxle wants Bregan D'aerthe with a permanent Dock Ward presence. Method: be indispensable to whoever resolves the vault situation. This is the long play that outlasts Dragon Heist. `[research]`

## Key Design Note

Jarlaxle is the only villain whose faction (**Bregan D'aerthe**) the PCs can join. This must be written directly into the campaign structure and not treated as an edge case. A party that joins Bregan D'aerthe inverts **Sea Maidens Faire**: the Faire becomes their base, and Jarlaxle sends them after another faction's Eye.

## Overview

Captain Zardoz Zord is the flamboyant Illuskan master of the Sea Maidens Faire, a carnival that travels in three ships. The fleet sails into Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21, the opening day of Fleetswake, and leaves at dawn on Tarsakh 20. Zord keeps regular hours aboard the *Eyecatcher* and welcomes interesting visitors. A haberdasher named J.B. Nevercott, who wears a burgundy coat, calls at Trollskull Manor during its renovation.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural for the GM tables, and bright in the Overview, where the Faire is a festival and its captain is a showman. Jarlaxle's charm and theatrics belong to Jarlaxle in his own speech, so keep the narration and the GM text around him plain. Let the intelligence behind the performance show in what he knows and when he says it.
