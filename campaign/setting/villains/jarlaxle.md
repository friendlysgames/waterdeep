# Jarlaxle Baenre

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - This is the villain profile for Jarlaxle Baenre, the drow leader of **Bregan D'aerthe**, who runs the Sea Maidens Faire as Captain Zardoz Zord. It sets out his phases, his relationships, his goals and the one design note that makes him playable.
> - Use it when you run any scene where Jarlaxle, Zardoz Zord or J.B. Nevercott acts. Phase 1 covers Acts I and II. Phases 2 and 3 begin at **Sea Maidens Faire** and split into an adversary track and an ally track.
> - Jarlaxle appears in **Trollskull Alley** (as J.B. Nevercott), **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire** and **Vault of Dragons**. The **Sea Maidens Faire** location journal maps his ships.
> - The company's response teams, outposts, escalation row and revelation list live in the **Bregan D'aerthe** page of the Factions Guide. Who the company is lives in the **Bregan D'aerthe** page of Organizations.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM portray Jarlaxle as a specific, three-dimensional villain through the Zardoz Zord phase, two branching tracks, a relationship table and three goals.

*This page contains significant spoilers for the campaign. We recommend reading it so that you understand the primary villains and their motivations. Some Gamemasters, however, may prefer not to have this full knowledge, so proceed only if you wish to have all the available information up-front instead of learning things as you play.*

Jarlaxle Baenre is a drow swashbuckler, the leader of Bregan D'aerthe and the secret lord of Luskan, and he comes to Waterdeep as Captain Zardoz Zord of the Sea Maidens Faire. His core motive is a seat for Luskan in the Lords' Alliance, which he means to buy with the Vault of Dragons' gold and the Open Lord's gratitude.

> #### GM Notes
> - Two mechanics run separately. **Membership** runs during **Trollskull Alley** like any other faction. Drow lieutenants shadow the party for several days, then J.B. Nevercott, who is Jarlaxle in a *hat of disguise* posing as a haberdasher, visits Trollskull Manor and offers enrollment to any interested PC.
> - The only gate is party action. A character who reports the watchers to the Watch is marked **BD Contact Severed**, and the company closes to that character. **Ryvarra Identified**, marked in **The Neighbors** event of **Trollskull Alley**, changes how the visit opens and never whether it happens.
> - **Villain activation** is a separate track. Jarlaxle has known of the Stone, the Eyes and the vault since he reached Waterdeep. He sits at the Grand Game table as a named player when **Jarlaxle Informed** is marked during **Fireball!** and read in **Gralhund Villa**.
> - **Jarlaxle Informed** records only that the party spoke with him directly. It does not mean he learned anything. It gates his Bregan D'aerthe team at **Gralhund Villa** and his Phase 1 Zardoz Zord activity, and the phases below key off it, not off membership.
> - His identity stays hidden behind J.B. Nevercott and Zardoz Zord until **Sea Maidens Faire**. See **Session Zero** and **Player Factions Overview** in the GM Guide, and the **Bregan D'aerthe** page of Organizations.

## Background

- Jarlaxle was born to Yvonnel Baenre, Matron Mother of House Baenre, the first house of Menzoberranzan. The published adventure gives only his surname. `[research: wiki only]`
- His birth order marked him for sacrifice to Lolth. The blade could not pierce the infant, and his brother Doquaio Baenre died in his place. Lolth favored him afterward, and he rejected her. `[research: wiki only]`
- Bregan D'aerthe rejects Lolth with him. No member worships her, and members speak of her with contempt, as the goddess they walked away from. Soluun Xibrindas gives Jarlaxle the devotion a drow of the houses would give the Spider Queen.
- He left his house and its name, though not his connections, and founded the company from rogue males of destroyed and disgraced houses. Almost all members are male. A female drow can earn a place by decrying the matriarchy and convincing him she would be an asset.
- He also employs non-drow operatives who do not know they work for him and do not count as members.
- The company reached Luskan through a long run of dealings. In 1376 and 1377 DR it helped bring down the Host Tower of the Arcane, and Luskan became a free port. `[research: wiki only]` After 1485 DR the High Captains are secretly under his sway, and the published adventure has him declaring himself Luskan's secret lord.
- Lords' Alliance membership is the latest of his schemes. Luskan's reputation has thwarted every earlier attempt, and the Alliance's leaders have voiced their opposition. He aims to persuade Laeral Silverhand to champion Luskan, even if it costs the Alliance other members, and he wants Neverwinter and Dagult Neverember out of the Alliance. Laeral calls Luskan a greedy pirate state that cannot be trusted.
- The Sea Maidens Faire is a business he already owned. It is a traveling carnival based in Luskan, with three ships (the *Eyecatcher*, the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser*) and a reputation for street parades. Each ship has its own captain, a drow mage disguised as a human who knows his plans and reports by *Sending*.
- Each crew is twenty drow in human disguise, all members of the company, and all know that Zord is Jarlaxle. They know nothing about the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. The ships' figureheads create the illusions, and a ship's disguises drop only if its figurehead is destroyed.
- Jarlaxle has an alliance with Lantan. He arms his lieutenants with Lantanese firearms that run on smokepowder, keeps the Lantanese submarine *Scarlet Marpenoth*, and sells nimblewrights to wealthy households. He plans to flee in the submarine if his scheme unravels.
- He is in Waterdeep by Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR, and the *Scarlet Marpenoth* has been moored in Deepwater Harbor all year. He arrives already knowing of the Stone, the three Eyes and the vault, and Nar'l Xibrindas, in place inside Xanathar's Guild since 1491 DR, keeps him current.
- Eye #3 reaches him aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* on the night before Ches 1. The Faire's fleet arrives for Fleetswake on Ches 21, and the Faire leaves Waterdeep at dawn on Tarsakh 20.
- His *hat of disguise* gives him personas such as J.B. Nevercott and Rongquan Mystere, the owner of the Seven Masks Theater. Aboard his own ships he does not need the hat.
- The published adventure says he never admits to being anyone other than the persona he wears, even if the party sees through it. Aboard his ships, a party that discerns his true form gets a slow nod and a dry "Bravo!", and he lets them make the next move.

## Personality Phases

Phase 1 is universal. After the reveal, the track splits based on whether the PCs choose opposition or negotiation.

*Phase 1 (universal), Zardoz Zord*

| When active | The mode |
|-------------|----------|
| Acts I–II *(Zord activity keyed to the party's contact: Jarlaxle Informed)* | The disguise; a plausible, theatrical Illuskan eccentric who is too observant; PCs may like him before they know who he is; the performance holds but the intelligence behind it keeps slipping through |

*Trigger to Phase 2:* PCs penetrate the disguise, or Jarlaxle decides they've earned the reveal. Either way, "Zardoz Zord" ends here.

---

*Adversary Track, if the PCs choose to oppose or heist against him*

| Phase | Name | When active | The mode |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 2A | The Unmasked | **Sea Maidens Faire** (adversary path) | True identity revealed without negotiation; Jarlaxle is gracious about it — he respects the attempt and doesn't rage; the deal is no longer on the table, but the disdain is minimal; he treats them like colleagues who chose the wrong firm |
| 3A | The Graceful Enemy | **Sea Maidens Faire** onward, adversary | He doesn't hunt them, but he doesn't help them either; Nar'l's intelligence is redirected to protect Bregan D'aerthe's position; he may counter-heist them (recover what they took), may simply outmaneuver them; the posture is professional, not vendetta |

*Trigger to Phase 3A:* PCs successfully steal the Eye, or the confrontation resolves without an alliance.

---

*Ally Track, if the PCs negotiate or join Bregan D'aerthe*

| Phase | Name | When active | The mode |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 2B | Jarlaxle in the Game | **Sea Maidens Faire** (ally path) | True identity revealed as an invitation; negotiates as an equal; his offer (Eye for Lords' Alliance membership for Luskan) is genuine and he'll honor it; he's charming because it's easier than not being charming, not because he's performing |
| 3B | The Patron | **Sea Maidens Faire** onward, ally | Asset rather than adversary; provides intelligence, drow operatives, the Faire as a safe house; the political favor comes due at **Vault of Dragons**; he'll remind them once, gracefully, that what he wants hasn't changed |

*Trigger to Phase 3B:* PCs choose alliance, accept the deal, or formally join Bregan D'aerthe.

## Relationships

| NPC | Role | Nature of relationship |
|-----|------|----------------------|
| Nar'l Xibrindas | Embedded spy | Inside Xanathar's Guild; Jarlaxle's most valuable asset; Jarlaxle has a contingency if Nar'l is discovered (has not shared it with Nar'l) |
| Manshoon | Mutual wariness | Non-interference arrangement, fragile; Jarlaxle has better intelligence and intends to keep it that way |
| Xanathar | Target of management | The beholder is being handled via Nar'l's selective intelligence; Jarlaxle prefers a controlled beholder to a power vacuum |
| Renaer Neverember | Political connection | Worth cultivating; Renaer's relationship with Laeral is an indirect path to the Open Lord's attention; Renaer doesn't know who he met |
| The Cassalanters | Aware but uninvested | Knows about their infernal bargain through Vessa, his doppelganger spy in their social circle; not interested in getting involved unless strategic upside appears |
| The PCs | Most interesting variable | Has been watching since **Finding Floon** (Nar'l's reports reached him, and his agents were in the Xanathar sewer hideout); knows their names, faction ties, and general competence by **Sea Maidens Faire** |

**Relationship notes**
- Jarlaxle is the one outsider who knows what the Cassalanters have done. He learned it through Vessa, and he tells no Bregan D'aerthe member unless the member works it out. Everyone else outside the household and the cult is suspicious of the family and nothing more.
- Nar'l reports to him by *Sending*. The intelligence Nar'l passes on is curated to keep Xanathar occupied and predictable, because Jarlaxle wants a manageable Xanathar and not a dead one.
- The arrangement with Manshoon is under strain. Manshoon has identified Fenerus Stormcastle as a Bregan D'aerthe cut-out and ordered his abduction. If the abduction proceeds, Jarlaxle acts on his own, and the non-interference collapses.
- Eye #3 began in Renaer's mourning locket and now sits aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*.

## Goals

- **Win Lords' Alliance membership for Luskan.** Luskan's operations need political legitimacy, and this is the real prize. Method: trade intelligence and an Eye to the PCs or directly to the Open Lord. Contingency: if the avenue closes, Bregan D'aerthe continues without it. Connects to the **Vault of Dragons** political aftermath.
- **Keep one Eye of the Stone.** It is a bargaining chip and he does not want it for personal use. Method: he already holds Eye #3 aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, which Bregan D'aerthe agents inside Xanathar's hideout lifted the night Xanathar's Guild raided the Zhentarim warehouse, and Nar'l's intelligence tells him where the other Eyes are. He keeps it as the bargaining chip and moves on the others before the other factions consolidate. Contingency: negotiating an Eye from the PCs is also acceptable and better for the relationship. Connects to **Sea Maidens Faire**.
- **Secure Waterdeep as a permanent base.** The Sea Maidens Faire is seasonal, and Jarlaxle wants Bregan D'aerthe to have a permanent Dock Ward presence. Method: be indispensable to whoever resolves the vault situation. This is the long play that outlasts Dragon Heist.

## Key Design Note

Jarlaxle is the only villain whose faction (Bregan D'aerthe) the PCs can join. The campaign structure must say so directly and not treat it as an edge case. A party that joins Bregan D'aerthe inverts **Sea Maidens Faire**: the Faire becomes their base, and Jarlaxle sends them after another faction's Eye.

## Overview

Captain Zardoz Zord is the master of the Sea Maidens Faire, a traveling carnival out of Luskan that tours the Sword Coast in three ships and is known for its street parades. He is a theatrical Illuskan with an easy charm, and most people notice how carefully he listens only after the conversation is over. The Faire arrives in Deepwater Harbor for Fleetswake, with the *Eyecatcher*, the captain's own ship, at anchor and its two sister ships at the docks. Wealthy households in several wards have bought clockwork nimblewrights from the captain, at prices that surprised them.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural for the GM tables, and light in the Overview, where Zord is a charming novelty on the docks. Jarlaxle's flamboyance belongs to Jarlaxle, so the narration stays plain around him.
