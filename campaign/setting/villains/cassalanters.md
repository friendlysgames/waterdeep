# The Cassalanters

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - This is the villain profile for the Cassalanters, the noble family of **Cassalanter Villa** and the cult beneath it. It sets out their three personality phases, their relationships, their goals and the one design note that makes them playable.
> - Use it when you run any scene where Victoro, Ammalia or their household acts or reacts. Phase 1 covers Acts I and II, Phase 2 covers Acts II and III, and Phase 3 applies in **Cassalanter Villa** once Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) is within three sessions.
> - The Cassalanters appear in **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Faction Outposts**, **Cassalanter Villa** and **Vault of Dragons**. They also drive the faction missions **The Wazoo Affair**, **The Theater's Back Room**, **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**, **The Shrine on Aveen Street** and **The Writ and the Weight**. **Cassalanter Villa** (location) is the location journal for the villa and the temple beneath it.
> - The family's response teams, outposts, escalation row and revelation list live on the **The Cassalanters** page of the Factions Guide. Who the family is lives on the **The Cassalanters** page of Organizations.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM portray the Cassalanters as people whose motives are easy to understand and whose plan is indefensible, through three personality phases, a relationship table and three goals.

*This page contains significant spoilers for the campaign. We recommend reading it so that you understand the primary villains and their motivations. Some Gamemasters, however, may prefer not to have this full knowledge, so proceed only if you wish to have all the available information up-front instead of learning things as you play.*

Victoro and Ammalia Cassalanter are loving parents and secret diabolists, the heads of a Waterdhavian noble house who traded their three children's souls to Asmodeus three years ago. Their core motive is to free the twins before their ninth birthday on Flamerule 11. That means raising 999,999 gp from their own coffers and the Vault of Dragons, and poisoning ninety-nine guests at the Founders' Day feast.

## Background

- House Cassalanter has stood on Waterdeep's noble registry for several generations, and the published adventure shows the family's portraits across them. The wiki dates the house's ennoblement to 1248 DR, when the Lords of Waterdeep granted titles to several merchant houses (Forgotten Realms Wiki, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e).
- The family's wealth is banking and money-lending, and Victoro's banking underwrites half the guilds in the city. The **Cassalanter Counting House** in the Sea Ward holds accounts across the North Ward, and the party's renovation financing for **Trollskull Manor** may pass through it in **The Grand Opening**.
- Caladorn Cassalanter, Victoro's father, was a former Masked Lord and a hero of the North. His ghost waits in the family crypt below the villa, where it attacks anything with a fiendish taint and helps anyone who shows a holy symbol of Siamorphe.
- The family faced bankruptcy about three years before the campaign opens. The remix ties the collapse to Dagult Neverember's embezzlement, which emptied the civic funds that Victoro's loans were underwritten against.
- The bargain was made in 1489 DR, counted back from the published "three years ago". Ammalia found the way through infernal research. A pit fiend carried the contract to them, and they signed it together in the temple beneath their own villa.
- The contract claims each child at nine. Osvaldo was nine when he was taken and is twelve now, a chain devil held in the soundproofed attic of the villa. The twins' souls are forfeit on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, ten days after Founders' Day.
- The buy-out clause asks for 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, both, paid to a devil of Asmodeus at the same time. The published text words it as "one shy of a million gold coins" and "one shy of one hundred unfortunate souls". Partial payment will not be accepted, and the family does not know this yet.
- The family holds about 500,000 gp of its own, and the Vault's 500,000 gp covers the shortfall. The Cassalanters are not competing with the other factions for the Stone of Golorr, and the outposts gather gold and ritual components for the payment.
- The souls are the guests at the Founders' Day feast. On Flamerule 1 Ammalia invites one hundred of Waterdeep's poorest to the villa lawn and serves ninety-nine of them wine laced with midnight tears, so that the guests die within a day and nothing connects them to the house.
- Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) is Ammalia's operational window, chosen for the civic cover and for the time left to verify the terms. The contract falls due on Flamerule 11 and does not name Founders' Day.
- Victoro is known publicly as a banker, a philanthropist and a worshipper of Lathander. The house also keeps a public devotion to Siamorphe, and the villa's ballroom floor is a Siamorphe mosaic.
- The temple lies below the wine cellar, behind a secret door marked with a sun symbol and down a spiral stair. Its thirty-foot statue of Asmodeus was carved from an image of Siamorphe, and discovery of the temple is a capital offense under the Code Legal.
- Eye #2 came to the Cassalanters through infernal divination, which found it in the Brandath Crypts, and their agents took it from Neverember's magical wards. It is secured in a concealed panel behind the base of the Asmodeus statue in the temple's ceremonial hall (A7), and the family knows what it does.
- At campaign start the family knows the vault lies beneath the Brandath Crypts and does not yet know the Stone is in play. They need the Stone and Eyes #1 and #3 to open it.
- In passive mode the Cassalanters barely react to the party. In hostile mode they go straight to Willifort Crowelle's full operation, and the switch fires when the party refuses to help or is identified as a threat to the plan.
- When the family escalates, it exhausts the city's leverage first: Watch complaints, noble pressure and guild sanctions. Infernal force comes last.
- Two exits from the Grand Game exist. In the cooperative path the party secures the vault gold for them, and every coin goes to Asmodeus. In the contract path the mace of disruption from Caladorn's ghost strikes the Asmodeus statue in A7 and severs the contract, which frees the children and brings Asmodean retribution within days.

## Personality Phases

| Phase | Name | When active | The mode |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | The Generous Patrons | Acts I–II | Warmth, ease, genuine charity; the public face that is also genuinely true; warmth toward tieflings is specific and real |
| 2 | The Desperate Parents | Acts II–III | Mask off; they did it and they've lived with it; Ammalia holds steady, Victoro is starting to fracture |
| 3 | The Hosts of Founders' Day | **Cassalanter Villa** | Founders' Day decided; a terrible calm; they will take any genuine alternative the PCs offer, immediately and without negotiation |

*Phase triggers*
- Phase 1 → 2: PCs investigate the villa's lower levels, encounter infernal evidence, or learn the truth from a secondary source
- Phase 2 → 3: Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) is within three sessions `[research]`

*Pop culture inspirations:* Walter and Skyler White (Breaking Bad — the partnership of complicity where love was real and the decision is now made); Cersei Lannister (the parent whose love for children becomes the root of atrocity)

## Relationships

| NPC | Role | Nature of relationship |
|-----|------|----------------------|
| Victoro and Ammalia (each other) | Co-conspirators | The marriage is real; decision made together, burden carried together; Ammalia makes the final calls; Victoro has stopped questioning this |
| Osvaldo | Eldest child (transformed) | Chain devil imprisoned in C24 (the soundproofed villa attic); Ammalia visits weekly, sitting outside the pentagram to read aloud while Osvaldo spits curses at her and weeps; Victoro has not visited in months |

| Asmodeus | Creditor | The terms are fixed; Asmodeus does not negotiate with mortals who have already signed |
| The Harpers | Watchers | Ammalia knows the Harpers monitor their charitable work; their genuine goodness has kept the surveillance clean for years |
| The PCs | Unplanned variable | Either a catastrophe (exposure) or a solution (alternative path); the Cassalanters will tell the truth if they believe the PCs can help |

**Relationship notes**
- Willifort Crowelle, the doppelganger butler, knows everything that happens in the villa and reports to Ammalia. He runs household security and is the family's most dangerous weapon.
- Everyone outside the household and the cult only suspects the family, because it is too nice to be this rich and this purebred. Savra Belabranta of the **Order of the Gauntlet** has no legal evidence, Mirt suspects Cassalanter money stood behind the Howling Hatred cult, and Vajra Safahr of **Force Grey** has scried conjuration and abjuration residue beneath the villa. The Lords' Alliance has no proof, the Watch has closed three complaints against the house, and the Emerald Enclave senses infernal influence in the Sea Ward without knowing whose it is.
- Jarlaxle is the one exception. He knows about the pact through **Vessa**, his doppelganger spy in the Cassalanter social circle, and he tells no Bregan D'aerthe member unless the member works it out.

## Goals

- **Collect the ninety-nine souls at the Founders' Day feast.** The contract demands ninety-nine souls as well as the gold, and the souls are the guests they mean to poison at the feast on Flamerule 1. Method: the feast itself. Contingency: partial payment will not be accepted, and they don't know this yet. Connects to **Cassalanter Villa**.
- **Raise the 999,999 gp.** The contract needs the full sum alongside the souls. The family holds about 500,000 gp, and the Vault's 500,000 gp covers the shortfall. They hold Eye #2, found in the Brandath Crypts, and need the Stone plus Eyes #1 and #3 to open the Vault. Method: political intelligence through Lords' Alliance contacts `[research]`. Connects to **Cassalanter Villa**.
- **Keep the temple secret.** Discovery is a capital Code Legal offense and destroys their plan before it can succeed. Method: Willifort Crowelle (doppelganger butler) manages household security. Connects to the PCs' primary leverage point in any negotiation.

## Key Design Note

Terenzio and Elzerina must appear before **Cassalanter Villa**. The GM should identify during **Trollskull Alley** when the children will first be visible, whether at a festival, a neighborhood event or a Cassalanter charitable appearance. Once the PCs have met the children, the Founders' Day deadline becomes personal rather than abstract.

## Overview

The Cassalanters are one of Waterdeep's old noble houses, and their name is now tied to open-handed charity. Lord Victoro, a half-elf banker, and Lady Ammalia, a human wizard, fund temples, orphanages and civic improvements across the city. Ammalia's butterfly garden is counted the most beautiful in Waterdeep, and their young twins play there under her eye. Their eldest son is said to be studying in a faraway city, and the family does not often speak of him.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural for the GM tables, and warm and civil in the Overview, where the family is the city's most generous household. Play the family's love for the twins as real and the plan for the feast as plain murder, and let the narration take neither side.
