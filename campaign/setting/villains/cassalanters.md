# The Cassalanters

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - This is the villain profile for the Cassalanters, the noble couple behind the charities and the Asmodean cult of Waterdeep. It sets out their three personality phases, their relationships, their goals and the one design note that makes them playable.
> - Use it when you run any scene where Victoro, Ammalia or their household acts or reacts. Phase 1 covers Acts I and II, Phase 2 covers Acts II and III, and Phase 3 begins when Founders' Day is ten days away or the party enters **Cassalanter Villa**, whichever comes first.
> - The Cassalanters appear in **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Faction Outposts**, **Cassalanter Villa** and **Vault of Dragons**. The **Cassalanter Villa** location journal maps the villa and the temple beneath it.
> - The family's response teams, outposts, escalation row and revelation list live in the **Cassalanters** page of Faction Operations. Who the Cassalanters are lives in the **Cassalanters** page of Organizations.

**Purpose:** Lets the Gamemaster portray Victoro and Ammalia Cassalanter as villains whose motives are comprehensible and whose plan is inexcusable, through three personality phases, a relationship table and three goals.

*This page contains significant spoilers for the campaign. We recommend reading it so that you understand the primary villains and their motivations. Some Gamemasters, however, may prefer not to have this full knowledge, so proceed only if you wish to have all the available information up-front instead of learning things as you play.*

The Cassalanters are Victoro and Ammalia Cassalanter, Waterdhavian nobles and bankers who are also secret worshippers of Asmodeus, and who traded the souls of their children to him three years ago. Their core motive is to save their twins, Terenzio and Elzerina, before the contract claims them on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11. To do it they will poison ninety-nine guests at the Founders' Day feast.

## Background

- The house has held a place in Waterdeep's noble registry for several generations. The wiki dates its ennoblement to 1248 DR, when the Lords of Waterdeep granted titles to several merchant houses.
- The family's money is banking and money-lending. It fell on hard times a few years ago, to the edge of bankruptcy, and then recovered.
- Victoro's father, Caladorn Cassalanter, was a Masked Lord and a hero of the North. The family uses his portrait as a symbol of civic virtue, and his ghost haunts the family crypt beneath the villa.
- Three years ago, about 1489 DR, Victoro and Ammalia signed a contract with Asmodeus. It bought wealth, political access, good health and long life, and the price was the souls of their three children. They read the terms before they signed, and the contract reached them through a pit fiend intermediary in the temple beneath their own villa.
- The contract claims each child at nine. Osvaldo was nine when it was signed, so Asmodeus took his soul at once and his body became a chain devil. He is twelve now and is held in the soundproofed attic of the villa, room C24.
- The twins, Terenzio and Elzerina, are eight and have not been taken. Their souls are forfeit on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, 1492 DR.
- The bargain paid off. Their banking business thrived while their competitors failed, the loss of their eldest son earned them sympathy, and their philanthropy bought legitimacy and new friends. The cult of Asmodeus that Victoro leads gained new followers along the way.
- A clause in the contract lets the family keep the remaining children by paying "one shy of a million gold coins" and the souls of one shy of one hundred people. Both the gold and the souls must be paid at the same time. The family holds about 500,000 gp of its own, and the Vault of Dragons' 500,000 gp covers the shortfall.
- Victoro is responsible for finding the Vault and the gold inside it. Ammalia is responsible for the souls, which are the guests she will poison at a feast on Founders' Day.
- Where the history leaves them: a family that is impeccably positioned, running out of time, and ready to do something irreversible if nothing changes.

## Personality Phases

| Phase | Name | When active | The mode |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | The Generous Patrons | Acts I–II | Warmth, ease, genuine charity; the public face that is also genuinely true; warmth toward tieflings is specific and real |
| 2 | The Desperate Parents | Acts II–III | Mask off; they did it and they've lived with it; Ammalia holds steady, Victoro is starting to fracture |
| 3 | The Hosts of Founders' Day | **Cassalanter Villa** | Founders' Day decided; a terrible calm; they will take any genuine alternative the PCs offer, immediately and without negotiation |

*Phase triggers*
- Phase 1 → 2: PCs investigate the villa's lower levels, encounter infernal evidence, or learn the truth from a secondary source
- Phase 2 → 3: Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) is ten days away or less, or the party enters **Cassalanter Villa**, whichever comes first. The contract falls due ten days after the feast, on Flamerule 11.

*Pop culture inspirations:* Walter and Skyler White (Breaking Bad, the partnership of complicity where love was real and the decision is now made); Cersei Lannister (the parent whose love for children becomes the root of atrocity)

**Relationship notes**
- Osvaldo was nine when he was taken and is twelve now. Two imps in spider form guard the attic. Terenzio and Elzerina have been told he is at school in a faraway city and cannot say which one.
- Outside the household and the cult, nobody knows about the pact. Every faction suspects the family because it is too nice to be this rich and this purebred, and none of them knows more than that. The Order of the Gauntlet is suspicious and will move once it has solid evidence. The Emerald Enclave senses infernal influence in the Sea Ward through Jeryth and does not know whose it is.
- Everything the Cassalanters tell the party about their situation is technically true. They never mention that the ritual also requires poisoning ninety-nine guests at the feast.
- Seffia Naelryke and Arn Xalrondar are cult fanatics who went to the Brandath Crypts for the Stone's Eye and to silence three cultists who knew too much. They killed two, and the survivor, Vaelle Lurval, is the loose end Seffia is trying to close. Victoro does not know Seffia failed.
- Esvele Rosznar, who plays the Black Viper, plans to rob the villa on her own account.

## Goals

- **Deliver ninety-nine souls at the Founders' Day feast.** The contract demands the souls as well as the gold, and the souls are the guests Ammalia means to poison at the feast on Flamerule 1. Method: the feast itself, with Jandar Chergoba, the villa's head chef, seeing that the poison is served. There is no fallback, because partial payment will not be accepted, and the family does not know this yet. Connects to **Cassalanter Villa**.
- **Raise 999,999 gp to pay alongside the souls.** The family holds about 500,000 gp, and the Vault's 500,000 gp covers the shortfall. They hold Eye #2, found in the Brandath Crypts, and need the Stone plus Eyes #1 and #3 to open the Vault. At campaign start they do not yet know the Stone is in play. Method: the outposts gather gold and ritual components, and the family waits for someone to activate the Stone and reveal the vault. Connects to **Cassalanter Villa**.
- **Keep the temple secret.** Discovery is a capital Code Legal offense and ends the plan before it can succeed. Method: Willifort Crowelle manages household security. This is the party's primary leverage point in any negotiation.

## Key Design Note

Terenzio and Elzerina must appear in **Trollskull Alley**, before the party reaches **Cassalanter Villa**. Once the PCs have met the children, the Founders' Day deadline becomes personal rather than abstract. Ammalia's warmth in the same quest is played straight, because the dramatic weight of the villa depends on the party having liked her.

## Overview

The Cassalanters are one of Waterdeep's old noble houses and among the city's most generous patrons. Lord Victoro, a half-elf banker who worships Lathander, and Lady Ammalia, a human wizard known for driving a hard bargain, fund temples, orphanages and civic improvements across the city. The house is publicly devoted to Siamorphe, and its fortunes recovered a few years ago after a hard patch. They live in a villa in the Sea Ward, where Ammalia keeps the most beautiful butterfly garden in Waterdeep and watches over her young twins, Terenzio and Elzerina, as they play. Their eldest son is said to be studying in a faraway city, and more than one Waterdhavian has remarked that a family this gracious and this fortunate is a little hard to believe.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM tables, and warm and civil in the Overview, where the Cassalanters are the city's most generous patrons. Play the family's love for the children as real and its plan as monstrous, and let the narration do neither the gloating nor the excusing.
