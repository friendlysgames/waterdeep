# Manshoon

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - This is the villain profile for Manshoon, the clone-wizard who leads the Zhentarim splinter cell known as the Splinter from Kolat Towers in the Southern Ward. It sets out his three personality phases, his relationships, his goals and the one design note that times the simulacrum reveal.
> - Use it when you run any scene where Manshoon or his operatives act or react. Phase 1 covers Acts I and II, Phase 2 covers Acts II and III, and Phase 3 begins when the party enters **Kolat Towers**.
> - Manshoon appears in **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers** and **Vault of Dragons**. In the first four quests the party meets only his operatives and hears hints of a master. The party gets his name at the Interrogation House in **Faction Outposts**, which is where the **Manshoon Named** Event Outcome is marked.
> - **Kolat Towers** records the result of the raid as **Manshoon operational?** (Destroyed, Simulacrum Only or Alive), and **Vault of Dragons** reads it. The **Zhentarim Warehouse** is the location journal for the Candle Lane warehouse where a Xanathar's Guild raid killed his cell. **Kolat Towers** has no location journal yet.
> - The Splinter's response teams, outposts, escalation row and revelation list live in the **Manshoon's Zhentarim** page of the Factions Guide. Who the Splinter is lives in the **Manshoon's Zhentarim** page of Organizations.

**Purpose:** Distinguishes Manshoon from the Doom Raiders and lets the GM portray the campaign's most intellectually dangerous villain through three personality phases, a relationship table and three goals.

*This page contains significant spoilers for the campaign. We recommend reading it so that you understand the primary villains and their motivations. Some Gamemasters, however, may prefer not to have this full knowledge, so proceed only if you wish to have all the available information up-front instead of learning things as you play.*

> **[GM]**
>
> #### The Two Zhentarims
> Manshoon's splinter cell and Davil Starsong's Doom Raiders are entirely separate organizations. They share a name and a history and nothing else. Never conflate them in NPC dialogue. See also **Structural Rules** and **Session Zero** in the GM Guide, and **Manshoon's Zhentarim** and **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)** in Organizations.

Manshoon is a centuries-old wizard, a clone of a clone of the Zhentarim's founder, who claims as every surviving clone does to be the one true Manshoon. He runs the Splinter, a cell of his own, from the warded towers of Kolat Towers in the Southern Ward. His core motive is to take the Stone of Golorr for the Weave knowledge inside it, absorb the Weave and supplant Mystra, with the vault's gold paying for the preparations.

## Background

- Manshoon was born in Zhentil Keep in 1229 DR and founded the Zhentarim, also called the Black Network, in 1261 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, citing *The Grand History of the Realms* and *Ruins of Zhentil Keep*; the wiki does not tag their editions). The published adventure calls the original "one of the founders", and the campaign follows the wiki and treats him as the founder (**The Founding of Chains**).
- He built the stasis clone spell as insurance against his enemies, and the wiki says he died many times and always came back in a new body (Forgotten Realms Wiki, citing *Ed Greenwood Presents Elminster's Forgotten Realms* and the novel *Crown of Fire*; editions not tagged).
- In 1370 DR Fzoul Chembryl and Lord Orgauth killed him and found themselves facing a dozen or more clones. Each clone believed he was the real Manshoon and felt driven to kill the others, and the fights are the Manshoon Wars (Forgotten Realms Wiki, 3e *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* p. 283). The published adventure says a mishap woke every clone at once (5e *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*).
- Three clones survived the wars. One rejoined the Zhentarim and conceded leadership to Fzoul, one became the vampire Orbakh in Westgate, and one took refuge in Undermountain with Halaster (Forgotten Realms Wiki, 3e *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* pp. 282-283). The published adventure agrees that at least three survive and says the clone now in Waterdeep is the one that hid in Undermountain (5e *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*).
- Halaster Blackcloak captured the Undermountain clone after a brief spell duel and amputated his left arm at the elbow. Magic could not regrow it, so Manshoon crafted an artificial arm and hand for himself (5e *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*). The severed forearm remains in Undermountain as an undead guardian (5e *Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage*, area 1a).
- The campaign makes the man in Waterdeep a clone of a clone, a body activated in Undermountain when the Manshoon before it died, as the clone network was designed to do. Halaster tolerated him for a time out of professional curiosity before taking the arm, and the clone fled upward carrying a staff of power he had smuggled in (**Kolat Towers** quest opener).
- Kolat Towers is a crumbling pair of towers in the Southern Ward, built by the Kolat brothers and abandoned years ago. A force field with the properties of a wall of force still surrounds them. Manshoon rarely leaves, and he uses a teleportation circle when he must, so nobody sees him enter or leave (5e *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*; **Kolat Towers** quest opener).
- Neighbors can see the force field and the light of *continual flame* spells in the windows, and most believe the towers are abandoned or haunted by the Kolat brothers. Manshoon posts no visible guards because he wants that belief to hold (5e *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*).
- He spent about two years building the Splinter, a cell that bears his name and works independently of the Zhentarim's surviving leadership. It is hub and spoke, with Kolat Towers as the command center and the Interrogation House in the North Ward and Yellowspire in the Castle Ward as field offices linked by teleportation circles. A pass-amulet system lets him revoke anyone's access from the towers (**Kolat Towers** quest opener).
- He took a large part of the local network the Doom Raiders had built, and through it learned that Lord Neverember had used the local Zhentarim's resources to embezzle the vault's gold (Alexandrian Remix, *The Villains*, p. 16).
- His agents stole the Neverwinter Eye from the Protector's Enclave. His envoys brought it to Xanathar three months before the fireball, and Xanathar killed them and kept it as Eye #1 (**Running the Villains**; **The Long Shadow**).
- His cell then seized Renaer Neverember's mourning locket on the night before Ches 1 and pried it open at the Candle Lane warehouse. Xanathar's Guild raided the warehouse and carried the Eye off without knowing what it was, and Bregan D'aerthe agents inside Xanathar's hideout lifted it that night. It became Eye #3, and Manshoon holds no Eye (**Running the Villains**).
- He blackmails two Masked Lords. Corylus Thann is held over a scandal, and Jelenn Urmbrusk is held through a debt to a Splinter front, and their votes follow his preferences. The ledger documenting these arrangements lies in an arcane-locked drawer in his study, with a list of the Masked Lords whose identities he knows (**Kolat Towers** quest opener; 5e *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, which has Jelenn already in debt to him).
- The published adventure gives him a different goal. He wants to oust Laeral Silverhand as Open Lord by bribing and blackmailing the Masked Lords, replace the City Watch with Black Network mercenaries, kill the Blackstaff, and then turn on Halaster (5e *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*). The campaign's motive is the Weave goal under Goals, which comes from the campaign's own pages and has no published or Alexandrian source, and the blackmail of the Masked Lords stays.
- He has a long list of powerful enemies, among them the Harpers, Elminster, Halaster and the Blackstaff. They would move against him if they learned he was in Waterdeep, so he operates in the shadows for now (5e *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*).
- He can have only one simulacrum at a time, and he uses it as a subordinate to command Zhentarim minions in the field. If it is destroyed he makes another, and the snow figure in the sarcophagus is part of the material component. He customized the spell to give the copy more hit points and less spellcasting (5e *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*). The campaign's simulacrum rests ice-preserved in area E7 of Kolat Towers, with half his hit points and no spell slots of 6th level or higher (**Kolat Towers** quest opener).
- The campaign stages his reveal across three quests. The name comes at the Interrogation House in **Faction Outposts**, the identity at **Kolat Towers**, and the motivation at **Vault of Dragons** (**Faction Outposts** structure notes).

## Personality Phases

| Phase | Name | When active | The mode |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | The Shadow | Acts I–II | Absent; only his lieutenants are visible; PCs fight "the Zhentarim" without knowing Manshoon is a distinct player |
| 2 | The Architect | Acts II–III | Revealed; negotiates from strength; charming, precise, and calm about consequences in a way that should unsettle |
| 3 | The Simulacrum Problem | **Kolat Towers** | Cornered; the performance of calm drops; the simulacrum fights while Manshoon escapes |

*Phase triggers*
- Phase 1 → 2: PCs identify Manshoon specifically by name or signature, requiring at least two separate intelligence threads to connect
- Phase 2 → 3: PCs penetrate Kolat Towers or destroy a significant portion of his operation

*Pop culture inspirations:* Tywin Lannister (the chess player who is also the most dangerous piece on the board); Anton Chigurh (the profound calm of someone who has settled their relationship with consequence); Hannibal Lecter (the politeness is real)

**Relationship notes**
- Urstul Floxin ran the Dalakhar capture attempt and the **Gralhund Villa** operation. In the published adventure he takes his orders from Manshoon's simulacrum, and in the campaign he cannot name Manshoon, because he knows only a code and a chain of dead drops.
- The Doom Raiders reached Waterdeep first. Manshoon's cell came later, peeled off part of their network, and now works to dismantle their people and everything they built. A party that allies with the Zhentarim through Davil works with the Doom Raiders and never with Manshoon.
- The war with Xanathar is an active, low-level street war in the Dock Ward. Xanathar's kill order on Manshoon has failed twice, and Manshoon knows Xanathar killed his envoys and kept Eye #1.
- Manshoon's parallel strike in **Sea Maidens Faire** always fires, and it sends his agents against Jarlaxle's ships in Jarlaxle's own harbor.
- Seffia Naelryke of the Cassalanters approached Vevette Blackwater to open a communication channel. Vevette refused, suspecting a trap, and neither side has made contact since.
- Sidra Romeir is his most trusted lieutenant and holds the teleport rings when lieutenants leave the towers. At Lockdown she locks down Kolat Towers.
- His response teams are the weakest in raw muscle of any faction, because drawing attention is the one mistake he will not make twice. They carry orders to capture, not kill, and he underestimates threats that are not magical.
- In the published adventure, characters who reach him are offered a place in his organization if they kill Xanathar. He does not mean to keep the promise.

## Goals

- **Acquire the Stone of Golorr.** The Stone is a transformed aboleth encoded with ancient Weave knowledge, and Manshoon means to absorb the Weave and supplant Mystra. The vault's gold funds the process. Method: Stone recovery, then Weave absorption. He holds no Eye, because Xanathar killed the envoys who delivered Eye #1 as a gift and kept it, so he needs the Stone plus any two Eyes. Contingency: destroy the vault rather than cede it. Connects to **Gralhund Villa** and **Kolat Towers**.
- **Control Waterdeep's Underworld.** He means to eliminate Xanathar, absorb the Doom Raiders and marginalize Bregan D'aerthe. Method: patient erosion through targeted disruption and strategic violence. The Dock Ward conflict in Acts I–II is this plan in early operation.
- **Survive Through the Simulacrum.** The simulacrum is the clone's primary survival strategy, and it fights while he escapes. Defeating the Manshoon the party meets first may defeat only the simulacrum, and if it is destroyed he makes another. Connects to the **Kolat Towers** design requirement.

## Key Design Note

The simulacrum reveal needs to be timed carefully. Too early, and it deflates the **Kolat Towers** raid. Too late, and it feels like a cheat. Target: players discover it during the raid itself, as a revelation that changes how they approach the final confrontation.

## Overview

Zhentarim crews with flying snake tattoos work the back streets of Waterdeep. The Watch has arrested a few of them after street brawls. Their teams take prisoners, and the crews will not say who gives the orders.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural for the GM tables, and guarded in the Overview, where the Zhentarim are a street presence with no face. Play Manshoon as patient and courteous, and let the clone, the Weave and the simulacrum surface only through what the party finds.
