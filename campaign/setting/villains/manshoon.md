### Manshoon (Zhentarim Splinter Cell)

**Purpose:** Distinguishes Manshoon clearly from the Doom Raiders and gives the DM the tools to play the campaign's most intellectually dangerous villain — the one who is always further ahead than anyone believes.

> **The Two Zhentarims — DM Note:** Manshoon's splinter cell and Davil Starsong's Doom Raiders are entirely separate organizations. They share a name and a history and nothing else. Never conflate them in NPC dialogue. See also: GM Guide Session Zero; the **Organizations** pages.

**Personality Phases**

| Phase | Name | When active | The mode |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | The Shadow | Acts I–II | Absent; only his lieutenants are visible; PCs fight "the Zhentarim" without knowing Manshoon is a distinct player |
| 2 | The Architect | Acts II–III | Revealed; negotiates from strength; charming, precise, and calm about consequences in a way that should unsettle |
| 3 | The Simulacrum Problem | **Kolat Towers** | Cornered; the performance of calm drops; the simulacrum fights while the real Manshoon exits |

*Phase triggers*
- Phase 1 → 2: PCs identify Manshoon specifically by name or signature, requiring at least two separate intelligence threads to connect
- Phase 2 → 3: PCs penetrate Kolat Towers or destroy a significant portion of his operation

*Pop culture inspirations:* Tywin Lannister (the chess player who is also the most dangerous piece on the board); Anton Chigurh (the profound calm of someone who has settled their relationship with consequence); Hannibal Lecter (the politeness is real)

**Relationships**

| NPC | Role | Nature of relationship |
|-----|------|----------------------|
| Urstul Floxin | Field lieutenant | Most effective operative; trusted with the Stone recovery at Gralhund Villa; his failure there is the first crack |
| The Doom Raiders | Rival cell | Professional contempt; would absorb them if it were efficient; hasn't needed to yet |
| Xanathar | Rival | Useful; the Guild's visibility keeps the Watch occupied; when the beholder becomes a direct obstacle, Manshoon has a plan |
| Jarlaxle | Mutual wariness | The only Grand Game player Manshoon takes seriously; non-interference arrangement, fragile; Jarlaxle has better intelligence and both know it |
| The Cassalanters | Missed opportunity | Their infernal connections could have been leveraged; Manshoon chose independence over shared control |
| The PCs | Escalating problem | Early: noise; mid-campaign: a problem to solve; first solution is always a job offer, not an assassination |

**Goals**

1. **Acquire the Stone of Golorr** — the Stone is a transformed aboleth encoded with ancient Weave knowledge; Manshoon means to absorb the Weave and supplant Mystra; the vault's gold funds the process; method: Stone recovery → Weave absorption; holds no Eye — Xanathar killed the envoys who delivered Eye #1 as a gift and kept it; needs the Stone plus any two Eyes; contingency: destroy the vault rather than cede it; connects to **Gralhund Villa** and **Kolat Towers**
2. **Control Waterdeep's Underworld** — eliminate Xanathar, absorb the Doom Raiders, marginalize Bregan D'aerthe; method: patient erosion through targeted disruption and strategic violence; the Dock Ward conflict in Acts I–II is this plan in early operation
3. **The Simulacrum Insurance** — the simulacrum is not a backup; it is the primary survival strategy; defeating Manshoon in combat may defeat only his copy; connects to **Kolat Towers** design requirement

**Key design note:** The simulacrum reveal needs to be timed carefully. Too early, and it deflates the Kolat Towers raid. Too late, and it feels like a cheat. Target: players discover it during the raid itself, as a revelation that changes how they approach the final confrontation.
