# Manshoon

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - This is the villain profile for Manshoon, the wizard who runs the Zhentarim splinter cell from Kolat Towers. It sets out his three personality phases, his relationships, his goals and the one design note that makes his simulacrum playable.
> - Use it when you run any scene where Manshoon or his cell acts or reacts. Phase 1 covers Acts I and II, Phase 2 covers Acts II and III, and Phase 3 begins when the party enters Kolat Towers.
> - Manshoon appears in **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Kolat Towers** and **Vault of Dragons**. The Doom Raiders are a separate organization; read the Two Zhentarims note below before you voice either cell.
> - The cell's response teams, outposts, escalation row and revelation list live in the **Manshoon's Zhentarim** page of the Factions Guide. Who the cell is lives in the **Manshoon's Zhentarim** page of Organizations.

**Purpose:** Distinguishes Manshoon clearly from the Doom Raiders and gives the Gamemaster the tools to play the campaign's most intellectually dangerous villain, the one who is always further ahead than anyone believes.

*This page contains significant spoilers for the campaign. We recommend reading it so that you understand the primary villains and their motivations. Some Gamemasters, however, may prefer not to have this full knowledge, so proceed only if you wish to have all the available information up-front instead of learning things as you play.*

Manshoon is a clone of a clone of the wizard who founded the Zhentarim, and he believes he is the original. He runs a cell of his own from Kolat Towers in the Southern Ward, where he plans to take the Stone of Golorr for the knowledge inside it, buy the Masked Lords with the vault's gold, and remove Xanathar and the Doom Raiders from his way.

> **The Two Zhentarims — GM Note:** Manshoon's splinter cell and Davil Starsong's Doom Raiders are entirely separate organizations. They share a name and a history and nothing else. Never conflate them in NPC dialogue. See also: GM Guide Session Zero; the **Organizations** pages.

## Background

- Manshoon was born in Zhentil Keep in 1229 DR and founded the Zhentarim, also called the Black Network, in 1261 DR. The published adventure calls him "one of the founders", and this page follows the wiki and treats him as the founder.
- He built the stasis clone spell as insurance against his enemies. Over the years he died several times and came back in a new body each time.
- In 1370 DR Fzoul Chembryl and Lord Orgauth killed him and found themselves facing a dozen or more clones. Each clone believed he was the real Manshoon and felt driven to destroy the others, and these fights are the Manshoon Wars. The published adventure says a mishap woke every clone at once.
- Three clones survived. One rejoined the Zhentarim and conceded leadership to Fzoul, one became the vampire Orbakh in Westgate, and one took refuge in Undermountain with Halaster Blackcloak.
- The man in Waterdeep descends from the Undermountain line. He is a clone of a clone, a body activated in Undermountain when the Manshoon before it died, and like every surviving clone he believes he is the one true Manshoon.
- Halaster tolerated him for a time out of professional curiosity, then captured him and took his left arm. The regrowth failed, so he wears an artificial arm and hand. Halaster kept the limb and made it a guardian that still attacks intruders in Undermountain.
- He fled Undermountain with the staff of power he had smuggled in, and he found Kolat Towers abandoned. The force field was still running, and the instructions for making its keyed amulets were in Duhlark Kolat's study.
- Kolat Towers stands in the Southern Ward. The wizard brothers Duhlark and Alcedor Kolat built it in the late fourteenth century, and Duhlark added a force field that covers the whole structure and admits only holders of a keyed amulet. Most neighbors believe the towers are abandoned, and others think the Kolat brothers haunt them.
- Lady Gondafrey, then a City Watch captain, tried to breach the towers in 1379 DR and was taken prisoner. She is still held there.
- Manshoon spent about two years building the Splinter. Kolat Towers is the command center, and the Interrogation House in the North Ward and Yellowspire in the Castle Ward are field offices linked to it by teleportation circles. Pass-amulets replaced a courier network, and the towers can revoke them. An extradimensional sanctum, reached by a teleportation ring keyed to a circle in room K22, holds his closest lieutenants and his most sensitive work.
- The Doom Raiders reached Waterdeep in 1489 DR, and Manshoon's clone arrived after them. He peeled off a large part of the local network they had built, and through it he learned that Neverember had used the local Zhentarim's resources in the embezzlement.
- His cell regards the Doom Raiders as deserters who stole his name, and the Doom Raiders believe they are the true Zhentarim of Waterdeep. The two cells will not cooperate or share intelligence.
- The Splinter's political reach rests on blackmail. Corylus Thann and Jelenn Urmbrusk, two Masked Lords, already vote his way under pressure, and the ledger that documents the arrangement lies in an arcane-locked drawer in his study in the sanctum.

## Personality Phases

| Phase | Name | When active | The mode |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | The Shadow | Acts I–II | Absent; only his lieutenants are visible; PCs fight "the Zhentarim" without knowing Manshoon is a distinct player |
| 2 | The Architect | Acts II–III | Revealed; negotiates from strength; charming, precise, and calm about consequences in a way that should unsettle |
| 3 | The Simulacrum Problem | **Kolat Towers** | Cornered; the performance of calm drops; the simulacrum fights first as his decoy and shield while the real Manshoon holds his ground with the staff of power |

*Phase triggers*
- Phase 1 → 2: PCs identify Manshoon specifically by name or signature, requiring at least two separate intelligence threads to connect
- Phase 2 → 3: PCs penetrate Kolat Towers or destroy a significant portion of his operation

*Phase 3 by escalation tier*
- **Unaware and Suspicious:** Manshoon is studying in E13 and the simulacrum lies dormant in E7. The party reaches Manshoon first and may not discover the simulacrum until it activates behind them.
- **Alert:** Manshoon is in E12 with the staff of power in hand, and the simulacrum is activated and standing in E1 as a decoy. The party meets it first and may believe it has defeated Manshoon.
- **Lockdown:** Manshoon is in E12 with his defensive spells prepared. The simulacrum is activated in E1 and has been briefed on the party's capabilities through Skeemo's intelligence, if Skeemo survived **Faction Outposts**.
- Manshoon does not leave his place when the fighting starts. He moves toward a confrontation, and he opens it with a question: "What do you actually want?"

*Pop culture inspirations:* Tywin Lannister (the chess player who is also the most dangerous piece on the board); Anton Chigurh (the profound calm of someone who has settled their relationship with consequence); Hannibal Lecter (the politeness is real)

**Relationship notes**
- Manshoon's envoys came to Skullport three months before the fireball, and Xanathar killed them and kept the Eye they brought. Manshoon knows this.
- Manshoon has identified Fenerus Stormcastle as a Bregan D'aerthe cut-out and has ordered his abduction. Krebbyg Masq'il'yr has reported the Zhentarim surveillance to Jarlaxle.
- Skeemo Weirdbottle has been feeding intelligence to Manshoon's cell since before the party was recruited. Davil Starsong has suspected it for months.
- Urstul Floxin will not name his master unless magically compelled, and the Splinter's response teams carry orders to take prisoners to the Interrogation House.

## Goals

- **Acquire the Stone of Golorr.** The Stone is a transformed aboleth encoded with ancient knowledge of the Weave, and Manshoon means to absorb the Weave and supplant Mystra. The vault's gold funds the process. Method: Stone recovery, then Weave absorption. He holds no Eye, because Xanathar killed the envoys who brought Eye #1 to him and kept it. He needs the Stone plus any two Eyes. At the campaign's start he knows the Stone exists and unlocks great wealth, and he does not yet know it has reached the Gralhunds. Contingency: destroy the vault rather than cede it. `[research]` Connects to **Gralhund Villa** and **Kolat Towers**.
- **Control Waterdeep's underworld.** He means to eliminate Xanathar, absorb the Doom Raiders and marginalize Bregan D'aerthe. Method: patient erosion through targeted disruption and strategic violence. The Dock Ward conflict in Acts I–II is this plan in early operation.
- **Win the Masked Lords.** He holds Corylus Thann through a scandal and Jelenn Urmbrusk through debt, and he wants a full Council majority. Method: blackmail first, then the vault's gold. Evidence of the blackmail, brought to Laeral Silverhand or the full Lords' Council, collapses his political position, which is why the documents stay in Kolat Towers.
- **Keep the simulacrum as insurance.** The simulacrum in E7 is his contingency against assassination. It was made about seven years ago, it has been ice-preserved since, and it has no spell slots of 6th level or higher. Method: at Alert and Lockdown he activates it in E1 as a decoy and a shield, and it fights first while he casts from behind it. Connects to **Kolat Towers** design requirement.

## Key Design Note

The simulacrum reveal needs to be timed carefully. Too early, and it deflates the Kolat Towers raid. Too late, and it feels like a cheat. Target: players discover it during the raid itself, as a revelation that changes how they approach the final confrontation. **Kolat Towers** gives the party three clues for telling the copy from the original. A party that misses them meets a full-strength Manshoon at the **Vault of Dragons**.

## Overview

Manshoon founded the Zhentarim, the Black Network, in Zhentil Keep in 1261 DR, and most people in the city believe he has been dead for more than a century. In Waterdeep the Black Network is known through the Doom Raiders, a band of retired adventurers who sell mercenary work and lend money through their businesses. Other Zhentarim operatives work the streets in lean teams, take prisoners and do not say who they answer to. Kolat Towers in the Southern Ward, a pair of wizards' towers behind a magical force field, has stood dark for years, and its neighbors think it is abandoned or haunted.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural for the GM tables, and measured in the Overview. Treat Manshoon's calm as the dangerous thing about him, and let the cell's patience, not the narration, carry the menace.
