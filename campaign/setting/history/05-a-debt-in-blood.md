# A Debt in Blood

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**

> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this is:** The Era page for the Cassalanters' bargain with Asmodeus, the backstory of the villain faction that runs on a fixed deadline. It is the fifth of eight eras, after **The Founding of Chains** and before **The Open Lord's Fall**.
> - **Where it is used:** The GM reads it before voicing Victoro or Ammalia on how the family got where it is, and before answering any question from a Harper, Lords' Alliance or Gauntlet contact about why the Cassalanters are so rich and so kind.
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley** (the twins must be seen before the villa), **Fireball!** (Ammalia's invitation), **Faction Outposts**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Vault of Dragons**.
> - **Spoiler level:** Everything above the Overview is GM-only. The Overview is the public face and mentions no pact, no devil and no soul.
> - **Draft status:** Several figures in the old era block disagree with other campaign pages. Each is marked `[conflict N]` and listed under GM Notes. Nothing has been settled by this page.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM answer any question about how the Cassalanters came to the bargain, what they built afterward and where they stand when the campaign opens, and shows which of those facts still disagree across the campaign pages.

**Keywords:** Cassalanters, Victoro Cassalanter, Ammalia Cassalanter, Osvaldo Cassalanter, Terenzio and Elzerina, Asmodeus, soul pact, soul coins, Founders' Day

**Span:** `[research]`. The sources disagree on when the bargain was made (`[conflict 1]`). If the **Osvaldo Cassalanter** page and the **Cassalanter Villa** quest stand, the span runs from about 1489 DR to 1492 DR. If the old era block stands, it runs from about 1472 DR to 1492 DR. Both start years are arithmetic from "three years" and "approximately twenty years" before the 1492 DR campaign start, not sourced years.

**Other Names:** The Cassalanter Bargain; the Soul Pact. These are GM names taken from the old era block and the guides. No in-world name is sourced. `[research]`

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Lore Summary
> - Victoro and Ammalia Cassalanter, of a house with three centuries in Waterdeep's noble registry, traded the souls of their three children to Asmodeus for wealth and political access. They read the terms before they signed.
> - The contract claimed the children one at a time. Osvaldo, the eldest, was taken first and is now a chain devil. Terenzio and Elzerina are still claimed but not yet taken.
> - In the years since, the Cassalanters built a charitable reputation and a web of political connections that are not faked. They became what they presented themselves as while they gathered what Asmodeus requires.
> - Their recovery plan needs 99 soul coins and 500,000 Dragons to reclaim the claims on the twins. They hold about 40 coins. The Founders' Day deadline is Ammalia's own calculation and is not in the contract.
> - At campaign start the family is impeccably positioned, running out of time, and prepared to do something irreversible if nothing changes.
> - Outside the household, only Jarlaxle knows what the family has done. Everyone else is suspicious of them and nothing more.

### The Bargain

- The Cassalanter name has stood in Waterdeep's noble registry for three centuries. `[research]` (no page but the old era block gives this; see `[conflict 9]`)
- The family had comfortable wealth before any intervention.
- The old block says the bargain was made from ambition, not desperation. `[conflict 3]` (other pages say the family was near bankruptcy)
- Victoro and Ammalia discussed the bargain, understood what they were signing and signed together.
- The old block places the bargain approximately twenty years before the campaign. `[conflict 1]` (other pages say three years)
- The contract reached them through a pit fiend intermediary, and they signed in the temple beneath their own villa. Source: the **Cassalanter Villa** quest opener.

### The Terms

- Three children's souls, in exchange for wealth compressed from three generations into one and for political access that normally takes a family centuries to build.
- Osvaldo was taken at once. The twins' souls are forfeit on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11. The old block gives no birthday. Source: the **Cassalanter Villa** quest opener and Ammalia's grimoire in the villa's study.
- The buy-out clause named in the quest, the study and the GM Guide is 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, delivered before the twins' ninth birthday. The old block names 99 soul coins and 500,000 Dragons instead. `[conflict 5]`
- The terms are fixed. Asmodeus does not negotiate with mortals who have already signed.

### The Proof of Contract

- Souls were claimed one at a time.
- Osvaldo, the eldest, was seventeen when he was taken, and is now in his early thirties. `[conflict 2]` (other pages say fifteen, and the old block's own arithmetic does not close)
- The old block says Osvaldo is a chain devil living in the temple beneath his parents' villa, and that his parents visit him. `[conflict 4]` (other pages put him in a soundproofed attic, say Ammalia reads to him, and say Victoro has not entered the room in months)
- Terenzio and Elzerina are eight years old and have not been taken. They do not know about the bargain. They have been told that Osvaldo attends school in a faraway city.

### What the Cassalanters Built

- The charitable reputation, the political connections and the public warmth are not faked.
- The Cassalanters became what they presented themselves as while also accumulating what Asmodeus requires.
- Ammalia's charity in the Field Ward feeds hundreds, and Victoro's banking underwrites half the guilds in the city. Source: the **Cassalanter Villa** quest opener.
- The family's generosity funds temples, orphanages and civic improvements. Ammalia keeps the most beautiful butterfly garden in Waterdeep and supervises the twins at play there.
- The Harpers watch the family's charitable work. Their genuine goodness has kept that surveillance clean for years.
- The outpost network exists to acquire soul coins. The Cassalanters do not compete with the other factions for the Stone and intend to wait until someone reveals the vault.

> *I have dined at Cassalanter Villa twice, and on both evenings Lady Ammalia walked me through her butterfly garden before the soup, naming every specimen and the country it came from. She corrected my pronunciation of three of them so gently that I wrote the names down afterward, meaning to use them at my next dinner.*
> *~Volo*

### The Recovery Plan

- The goal is to reclaim the claims on Terenzio and Elzerina. The old block names the price as 99 soul coins plus 500,000 Dragons. `[conflict 5]`
- The Cassalanters have accumulated about 40 coins over eight years. `[conflict 6]` (the villain page gives about 40 and says 59 more are needed, with no span)
- They acquire coins through criminal-adjacent dealings at arm's length. Partial payment will not be accepted, and they do not know this yet.
- The 500,000 Dragons is the sum Neverember embezzled. The Cassalanters have most of the money and need the vault to cover the remainder.
- The Founders' Day deadline is Ammalia's own calculation, based on her assessment of the children's soul erosion. It does not appear in the contract. She believes she has until this Flamerule 1. `[conflict 7]`

### The Family at Campaign Start

- The Cassalanters are impeccably positioned, running out of time, and prepared to do something irreversible if nothing changes.
- The irreversible act is Ammalia's feast. On Founders' Day she will invite one hundred of Waterdeep's poorest to a charitable dinner at the villa and poison ninety-nine of them, so that the guests die the next day and nothing connects them to the house.
- The family's warmth toward the twins is real. Victoro and Ammalia carry the decision together, Ammalia makes the final calls, and Victoro has stopped questioning them.

## Overview

The Cassalanters are one of Waterdeep's old noble houses, with three centuries in the city's registry and a name now tied to open-handed charity. Lord Victoro, a half-elf banker, and Lady Ammalia, a human wizard, fund temples, orphanages and civic improvements across the city, and Victoro's banking underwrites half the guilds. Ammalia's butterfly garden is counted the most beautiful in Waterdeep, and their young twins play there under her eye. Their eldest son is said to be studying in a faraway city, and the family does not often speak of him.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Secret
> - The bargain is with Asmodeus, and the villa hides a temple to him. The Code Legal offense for the temple alone is capital.
> - No faction or NPC outside the household knows this. They suspect the Cassalanters because the family is too nice to be this rich and this purebred, and they know nothing.
> - The one exception is Jarlaxle, who learned it through Vessa, his doppelganger spy in the Cassalanter social circle. He does not tell Bregan D'aerthe members unless they work it out themselves.
> - The Emerald Enclave can sense that infernal influence is at work in the city, and Jeryth feels it in the Sea Ward. No Enclave speaker knows whose it is.
> - Osvaldo is the proof of the bargain, and Terenzio and Elzerina know nothing about it.
> - What the Cassalanters tell the party about their situation is technically true. The ritual does require a sacrifice, and the twins are in danger. They leave out that the ritual also requires the deaths of 99 people.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Timeline.** The dated entries below are the only dates the sources settle. The signing, Osvaldo's taking and the first soul coin cannot be dated until `[conflict 1]` is resolved. `[research]`
>   - **Flamerule 11, 1483 DR:** Terenzio and Elzerina are born. This is derived from their ninth birthday on Flamerule 11, 1492 DR.
>   - **Flamerule 1, 1492 DR:** Founders' Day. Ammalia's feast is planned for this date.
>   - **Flamerule 11, 1492 DR:** The twins' ninth birthday, the contract's due date.
> - **Volo's aside** is an anecdote of this page alone. It holds no secret and no error, and nothing else in the campaign depends on his dinners. `[research]` (confirm that Volo may have dined at the villa)
> - **Conflict 1, when.** The old block says approximately twenty years before the campaign. The **Osvaldo Cassalanter** page, the **Cassalanter Villa** quest opener and the published adventure say three years. The **Caladorn Cassalanter** page and the margin notes in Ammalia's grimoire also span three years.
> - **Conflict 2, Osvaldo's age.** The old block says seventeen when taken and early thirties now. Seventeen plus twenty years is thirty-seven, so the old block does not agree with itself. The **Osvaldo Cassalanter** page, the **Cassalanter Villa** quest opener, the villa journal for his prison and the GM Guide all say fifteen.
> - **Conflict 3, motive.** The old block says ambition, not desperation. The **Osvaldo Cassalanter** page says the soul was surrendered during a financial crisis. The quest opener and the published adventure say Neverember's embezzlement gutted the family's banking and left it near bankruptcy.
> - **Conflict 4, where Osvaldo is.** The old block says the temple beneath the villa. The **Osvaldo Cassalanter** page, the **Cassalanters** villain page and the villa journals say the soundproofed attic. The **Cassalanters** organization page says the basement. The old block says the parents visit. The villain page and the **Victoro Cassalanter** page say Ammalia visits and Victoro does not enter the room.
> - **Conflict 5, the price.** The old block says 99 soul coins plus 500,000 Dragons. The quest, the study journal and the GM Guide's design notes say 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls. The **Osvaldo Cassalanter** page and the **Cassalanters** organization page treat 500,000 gp as the sum still needed. Two mechanisms for collecting the 99 souls also appear: soul coins (villain page, **The Grand Game** page, GM Guide) and the poisoned feast (organization page, GM Guide).
> - **Conflict 6, accumulation.** About 40 coins over eight years cannot fit a bargain made three years ago. The eight-year span also matches Willifort's years of service in the quest, which may be a coincidence.
> - **Conflict 7, the Founders' Day deadline.** The old block says the date is Ammalia's reading of the children's soul erosion. The GM Guide's design notes say she chose Flamerule 1 as her operational window because the civic holiday gives cover and because it is the last window that still leaves time to verify the contract. Both agree the date is not in the contract.
> - **Conflict 8, public devotion.** The **Victoro Cassalanter** page calls him a worshipper of Lathander. The quest opener and the villa journals give the family a public ancestral devotion to Siamorphe. The Overview names neither.
> - **Conflict 9, three centuries.** Only the old era block gives the age of the house. The **Caladorn Cassalanter** page makes Victoro's father a former Masked Lord, and nothing else gives the house's age.

**Tone:** Grave and played straight, in the Ember history register. State the Cassalanters' love for their children and the evil of their plan as two facts side by side, with no narrator verdict on either. The Overview stays on the public face and the GM bullets state conflicts flatly, without choosing between them.
