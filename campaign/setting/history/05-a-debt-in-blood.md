> #### Lore Summary
> - Victoro and Ammalia Cassalanter, of a house with several generations in Waterdeep's noble registry, traded the souls of their three children to Asmodeus for wealth and political access. They read the terms before they signed.
> - The contract claimed the children one at a time. Osvaldo, the eldest, was taken first and is now a chain devil. Terenzio and Elzerina are still claimed but not yet taken.
> - In the years since, the Cassalanters built a charitable reputation and a web of political connections that are not faked. They became what they presented themselves as while they gathered what Asmodeus requires.
> - Their recovery plan needs 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, both, to reclaim the claims on the twins. The souls are the ninety-nine guests poisoned at the Founders' Day feast. The family holds about 500,000 gp of its own, and the Vault's 500,000 gp is the shortfall. Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) is the feast and Ammalia's real deadline, her operational window. The contract falls due on Flamerule 11 and does not name Founders' Day.
> - At campaign start the family is impeccably positioned, running out of time, and prepared to do something irreversible if nothing changes.
> - Outside the household, only Jarlaxle knows what the family has done. Everyone else is suspicious of them and nothing more.

### The Bargain

- The Cassalanter name has stood in Waterdeep's noble registry for several generations. The published adventure shows several generations of family portraits.
- Financial ruin was the trigger. The family's banking was near bankruptcy when the bargain was made.
- The deal also bought power, good health and long life.
- Victoro and Ammalia discussed the bargain, understood what they were signing and signed together.
- The bargain was made three years before the campaign, about 1489 DR.
- The contract reached them through a pit fiend intermediary, and they signed in the temple beneath their own villa. Source: the **Cassalanter Villa** quest opener.

### The Terms

- Three children's souls, in exchange for wealth compressed from three generations into one and for political access that normally takes a family centuries to build.
- Osvaldo was taken at once. The twins' souls are forfeit on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11. The old block gives no birthday. Source: the **Cassalanter Villa** quest opener and Ammalia's grimoire in the villa's study.
- The contract requires 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, both, delivered before the twins' ninth birthday. The family holds about 500,000 gp of its own, and the Vault's 500,000 gp is the shortfall.
- The terms are fixed. Asmodeus does not negotiate with mortals who have already signed.

### The Proof of Contract

- Souls were claimed one at a time.
- Osvaldo, the eldest, was fifteen when he was taken, and is now eighteen.
- Osvaldo is a chain devil held in the soundproofed attic of the villa, room C24, never the basement or the temple. Ammalia visits weekly and sits in the room outside the pentagram. Victoro has not entered in months. Willifort knows. Two imps are always present.
- Terenzio and Elzerina are eight years old and have not been taken. They do not know about the bargain. They have been told that Osvaldo attends school in a faraway city.

### What the Cassalanters Built

- The charitable reputation, the political connections and the public warmth are not faked.
- The Cassalanters became what they presented themselves as while also accumulating what Asmodeus requires.
- Ammalia's charity in the Field Ward feeds hundreds, and Victoro's banking underwrites half the guilds in the city. Source: the **Cassalanter Villa** quest opener.
- The family's generosity funds temples, orphanages and civic improvements. Ammalia keeps the most beautiful butterfly garden in Waterdeep and supervises the twins at play there.
- The Harpers watch the family's charitable work. Their genuine goodness has kept that surveillance clean for years.
- The outpost network gathers gold and ritual components for the payment, and the Vault covers the shortfall. The Cassalanters do not compete with the other factions for the Stone and intend to wait until someone reveals the vault. `[review: outpost purpose against Faction Outposts]`

> *I have dined at Cassalanter Villa twice, and on both evenings Lady Ammalia walked me through her butterfly garden before the soup, naming every specimen and the country it came from. She corrected my pronunciation of three of them so gently that I wrote the names down afterward, meaning to use them at my next dinner.*
> *~Volo*

### The Recovery Plan

- The goal is to reclaim the claims on Terenzio and Elzerina. The price is 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, both. The souls are the ninety-nine guests poisoned at the Founders' Day feast.
- Partial payment will not be accepted, and the family does not know this yet.
- The 500,000 Dragons is the sum Neverember embezzled. The family holds about 500,000 gp of its own, and the Vault's 500,000 gp is the shortfall.
- Founders' Day, Flamerule 1, is the feast and Ammalia's real deadline. It is her operational window, chosen for the civic cover and for the time left to verify the terms. The contract falls due on Flamerule 11, the twins' ninth birthday, and does not name Founders' Day.

### The Family at Campaign Start

- The Cassalanters are impeccably positioned, running out of time, and prepared to do something irreversible if nothing changes.
- The irreversible act is Ammalia's feast. On Founders' Day she will invite one hundred of Waterdeep's poorest to a charitable dinner at the villa and poison ninety-nine of them, so that the guests die the next day and nothing connects them to the house.
- The family's warmth toward the twins is real. Victoro and Ammalia carry the decision together, Ammalia makes the final calls, and Victoro has stopped questioning them.

## Overview

The Cassalanters are one of Waterdeep's old noble houses, with several generations in the city's registry and a name now tied to open-handed charity. Lord Victoro, a half-elf banker, and Lady Ammalia, a human wizard, fund temples, orphanages and civic improvements across the city, and Victoro's banking underwrites half the guilds. Ammalia's butterfly garden is counted the most beautiful in Waterdeep, and their young twins play there under her eye. Their eldest son is said to be studying in a faraway city, and the family does not often speak of him.

>
> #### Secret
> - The bargain is with Asmodeus, and the villa hides a temple to him. The Code Legal offense for the temple alone is capital.
> - No faction or NPC outside the household knows this. They suspect the Cassalanters because the family is too nice to be this rich and this purebred, and they know nothing.
> - The one exception is Jarlaxle, who learned it through Vessa, his doppelganger spy in the Cassalanter social circle. He does not tell Bregan D'aerthe members unless they work it out themselves.
> - The Emerald Enclave can sense that infernal influence is at work in the city, and Jeryth feels it in the Sea Ward. No Enclave speaker knows whose it is.
> - Osvaldo is the proof of the bargain, and Terenzio and Elzerina know nothing about it.
> - What the Cassalanters tell the party about their situation is technically true. The ritual does require a sacrifice, and the twins are in danger. They leave out that the ritual also requires the deaths of 99 people.
