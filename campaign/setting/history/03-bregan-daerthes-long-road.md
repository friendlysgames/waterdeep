# Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this is:** The Era page for how Jarlaxle Baenre and Bregan D'aerthe came from Menzoberranzan to Luskan and then to Waterdeep. It is the third of eight eras, after **The Long Shadow** and before **The Founding of Chains**.
> - **Where it is used:** The GM reads it before voicing Jarlaxle, Soluun, Krebbyg or Nar'l, before the Bregan D'aerthe recruitment visit in **Trollskull Alley**, and before any player asks where the company comes from or why a carnival captain is interested in Waterdeep.
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Vault of Dragons**.
> - **Spoiler level:** Everything above the Overview is GM-only. The Overview is the public face and links neither Captain Zardoz Zord nor Luskan's rulers to the company's leader.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM answer any question about who founded Bregan D'aerthe, why its members reject Lolth, how it came to dominate Luskan, and what Jarlaxle had in place before the campaign opens.

**Keywords:** Bregan D'aerthe, Jarlaxle Baenre, Zardoz Zord, Yvonnel Baenre, Menzoberranzan, Lolth, Luskan, High Captains, Lords' Alliance, Sea Maidens Faire, Lantan, Scarlet Marpenoth

**Span:** The 10th century DR to 1492 DR, about five centuries. The Forgotten Realms Wiki has Jarlaxle starting the company slowly in the 10th century DR, and a line about fifteen decades of the company by 1118 DR implies about 968 DR. The second figure is derived. `[research]` The old era block said "decades", which is wrong. The surface chapter runs from 1366 DR (a brief base in Calimport) through 1377 DR (a major presence in Luskan) to after 1485 DR (the High Captains under Jarlaxle's sway). The campaign opens on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

**Other Names:** Jarlaxle's other names are GM names: Jarlaxle Baenre, Jarlaxle D'aerthe (a wiki alias), Captain Zardoz Zord (the carnival master), J.B. Nevercott (the haberdasher who visits Trollskull Manor) and Rongquan Mystere (a disguise at the Shipwright's Ball). The old block translates the company's name as "those of the broken house". No source gives a translation. `[research]`

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Lore Summary
> - Jarlaxle was born to Matron Mother Yvonnel Baenre of House Baenre, the first house of Menzoberranzan. He survived his sacrifice to Lolth and later rejected her. Bregan D'aerthe rejects her too, and its members speak of her with contempt.
> - He left his house and name and founded the company from rogue males of destroyed and disgraced houses. House Baenre became its main client.
> - The company grew as a mercenary band and an information broker. It began putting down roots on the surface in 1366 DR and had a major presence in Luskan by 1377 DR.
> - Luskan is ruled by five High Captains, and after 1485 DR they are under the sway of Jarlaxle and the company. The published adventure has him declaring himself its secret lord.
> - Luskan's admission to the Lords' Alliance is his latest scheme and the one that brings him to Waterdeep. The Sea Maidens Faire is a business he already owned before he turned to Waterdeep.
> - When the campaign opens he has an agent inside Xanathar's Guild, and the Faire's fleet reaches Deepwater Harbor for Fleetswake.

### The Baenre Son

- Jarlaxle was born to Yvonnel Baenre, the Matron Mother of House Baenre. The wiki cites Road of the Patriarch, Menzoberranzan: City of Intrigue and the Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting for his parentage, and the published adventure gives his surname as Baenre.
- By his birth order he was to be sacrificed to Lolth. The blade could not pierce the infant, and his brother Doquaio Baenre died in his place. `[research: wiki only]`
- Lolth favored him afterward as an agent of chaos, and he rejected her. She withdrew her favor, and the matron mothers of Menzoberranzan never learned of it. `[research: wiki only]`
- This is where the campaign rule starts. Jarlaxle rejected Lolth, Bregan D'aerthe rejects her, and its members speak of her as the goddess they walked away from. Soluun gives Jarlaxle the devotion that a drow of the houses would give the Spider Queen.
- He is one of the few male drow with real power in Menzoberranzan, and his brother Gromph Baenre is the city's Archmage. `[research: wiki only]`
- A campaign docx calls him the third son. The wiki gives no number. `[research]`

### The Founding

- Jarlaxle left his house and its name, though not his connections, and founded Bregan D'aerthe.
- The company was built from rogue males of destroyed houses in Menzoberranzan and Ched Nasad. The published adventure calls its first members the much-derided and dishonorable castaways of destroyed drow houses. Almost all of them are male, because female drow rarely take orders from a male.
- A female drow can earn a place by decrying the matriarchy and convincing Jarlaxle that she would be an asset. He also employs non-drow operatives, none of whom know they work for him and none of whom count as members.
- Nar'l Xibrindas is an example. His house was wiped out long ago, and he and his elder brother Soluun survived and joined the company.
- The wiki has the company starting slowly in the 10th century DR, with Arathis Hune as its first lieutenant, and taking part in a raid in 1018 DR that acquired the weaponsmaster Zaknafein. `[research: wiki only]`
- The old block says Jarlaxle turned disposability into leverage. The wiki gives the sense without saying it was a founding act: a male drow survives best by standing outside the system and being too valuable to eliminate. `[research]`

### The Rise and the Surface

- The company is a mercenary and mercantile band for hire to the highest bidder. It also sells information to both sides of a conflict, and its contacts reach as far as the svirfneblin city of Blingdenstone. `[research: wiki only]`
- Its main base is the Clawrift in Menzoberranzan, and the wiki has its membership doubling by 1118 DR. The campaign's organization page says it has been outmaneuvering Underdark politics for generations. The old block's "most reliable intelligence network" is not stated in that form by any source. `[research]`
- The move to the surface was an expansion for influence and for trade with Menzoberranzan. The old block calls it strategic and not desperate. The wiki ties the first base, in Calimport in 1366 DR, in part to the influence of the artifact Crenshinibon on Jarlaxle. Draft Notes logs both versions. `[research: wiki only]`
- The Calimport base did not last. The company then looked for contacts in Luskan, and its first was a human rogue named Morik. `[research: wiki only]`
- The company ships goods from the surface to Menzoberranzan, and the wiki says it traded Netherese artifacts there by way of Luskan. `[research: wiki only]`

### Luskan

- Luskan is a free port on the northern Sword Coast. Five High Captains rule it, and each leads a faction called a Ship: Taerl, Baram, Kurth, Suljack and Rethnor. The old block's "Ship Captains council" means these High Captains.
- In 1376 and 1377 DR the company helped bring down the Host Tower of the Arcane, and Luskan became a free port. The company secured trade relations by aligning with Ship Kurth, which became its outward face by 1377 DR. It later opened trade with Mirabar. `[research: wiki only]`
- By 1483 DR the wiki has a company agent named Beniago, disguised as a human, serving as High Captain Kurth. `[research: wiki only]`
- After 1485 DR the High Captains trade legally across the Sword Coast and are secretly under the sway of Jarlaxle and the company. The published adventure says he infiltrated Luskan, brought a kind of order to its lawlessness and declared himself its secret lord.
- Laeral Silverhand calls Luskan a greedy pirate state that cannot be trusted under any circumstances.
- Lords' Alliance membership is the latest of Jarlaxle's schemes in the published adventure, and Luskan's unsavory reputation has thwarted every earlier attempt. The Alliance's current leaders have voiced their opposition, and a few have declared that Luskan will never be welcome. He aims to persuade Laeral to champion the city, even if it costs the Alliance other members. The campaign makes this his main aim in Waterdeep (see **Jarlaxle Baenre** in Villains).
- He also wants to oust Neverwinter and its lord, Dagult Neverember, from the Alliance. The published adventure calls Neverember his fiercest political opponent.

> *Luskan's captains dislike being called pirates in front of their own crews, and they like it less when the speaker is a guest at their table. I learned this over a long supper on the south bank, and two very polite sailors saw me to the gate afterward.*
> *~Volo*

### The Road to Waterdeep

- The Sea Maidens Faire already exists as one of Jarlaxle's legitimate business enterprises. It is a traveling carnival based in Luskan that tours the Sword Coast in three ships, the *Eyecatcher*, the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser*, under Captain Zardoz Zord. Zord is Jarlaxle in the magical guise of an Illuskan human.
- Each ship has its own captain, a drow mage in the guise of a human, and all of them know Jarlaxle's plans. They keep in touch with him by *Sending*.
- Zord visits Lantan about once a year. On his last visit he bought nimblewrights from a Lantanese wizard. Jarlaxle has an alliance with Lantan, arms his lieutenants with Lantanese firearms that run on smokepowder, and keeps a Lantanese submarine, the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, mounted below the *Eyecatcher*. He plans to flee in it if his scheme unravels.
- Bregan D'aerthe is skilled at infiltrating criminal organizations. Nar'l Xibrindas became Xanathar's only advisor and persuaded it to eliminate its other advisors. How long he has been in place differs between sources (see Draft Notes).
- Before the campaign opens, Jarlaxle has an agent inside Xanathar's Guild and a fleet bound for the harbor. The Faire's ships are in Deepwater Harbor for Fleetswake, Ches 21 to 30. He arrives in Waterdeep already knowing of the Stone, the three Eyes and the vault, and Nar'l's reports keep him current. The nimblewright ledger puts him in the city no later than Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR. Eye #3 reaches him aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* on the night before Ches 1, lifted from Xanathar's hideout by agents already inside it (see **The Open Lord's Fall**).
- The Faire leaves Waterdeep at dawn on Tarsakh 20.

## Overview

Bregan D'aerthe is a drow mercenary company that began in the Underdark city of Menzoberranzan, made up mostly of men from houses that were destroyed or disgraced. It hires out soldiers, scouts and go-betweens, and its agents are said to deal in information with anyone who can pay. On the surface it is said to have a foothold in Luskan, a free port on the northern Sword Coast that five High Captains rule and that Waterdeep regards as a pirate state. This spring the Sea Maidens Faire, a traveling carnival out of Luskan, brings three ships and Captain Zardoz Zord to Deepwater Harbor for Fleetswake.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Secret
> - Captain Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle Baenre, and he is the secret lord of Luskan. Nothing in the Overview links him to the company, and the party learns the link in **Sea Maidens Faire**.
> - Jarlaxle holds Eye #3 of the Stone of Golorr aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, and he is the only outsider who knows what the Cassalanters have done (see **A Debt in Blood**). He does not tell Bregan D'aerthe members unless they work it out themselves.
> - Nar'l Xibrindas reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending*, and Xanathar does not know it.

**Tone:** Grave and played straight, in the Ember history register. Jarlaxle's flamboyance belongs to Jarlaxle and the narration stays plain. The Overview gives only the company's rumor-level face, and the GM bullets state facts flatly.
