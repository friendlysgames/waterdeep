# The Founding of Chains

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this is:** The Era page for the Zhentarim, from Manshoon's founding of the Black Network to the two rival cells that now share its name in Waterdeep. It is the fourth of eight eras, after **Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road** and before **A Debt in Blood**.
> - **Where it is used:** The GM reads it before voicing Davil Starsong, Tashlyn Yafeera or any Zhentarim NPC, and before answering a contact's question about which Zhentarim the party has met. Every scene with a "Zhentarim" NPC needs a decision about which cell is present.
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers**, **Vault of Dragons**.
> - **Spoiler level:** Everything above the Overview is GM-only. The Overview gives the Black Network as most Waterdhavians know it, through the Doom Raiders, and mentions no clone, no second cell and no Kolat Towers.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM say who founded the Black Network, why two cells in Waterdeep use its name, and what each cell believes about the other.

**Keywords:** Zhentarim, Black Network, Manshoon, Zhentil Keep, stasis clones, Manshoon Wars, Doom Raiders, Davil Starsong, Kolat Towers, the Splinter

**Span:** 1261 DR to 1492 DR. The founding date, 1261 DR, comes from the Forgotten Realms Wiki. The Waterdeep chapter runs from 1489 DR, when the Doom Raiders were sent to found a foothold, to the campaign opening on Ches 1, 1492 DR. Manshoon's cell arrived after them, about two years before the campaign. `[research]`

**Other Names:** The Black Network; the Splinter (the name the factions use for Manshoon's cell); the Two Zhentarims (a GM name from the guides). "The Founding of Chains" is the old era title, and no in-world name for the era is sourced. `[research]`

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Lore Summary
> - Manshoon, a wizard-lord of Zhentil Keep, founded the Zhentarim in 1261 DR as a mercantile network and spread it from the Moonsea to the Sword Coast. The Black Network outlived his death in 1370 DR and lost its capital in the century after.
> - Manshoon survived that death through the stasis clone spell and fought his own clones in the Manshoon Wars. The man in Kolat Towers is a clone of a clone who claims to be the true Manshoon, and he keeps a simulacrum in reserve.
> - The Doom Raiders, five retired adventurers, were sent to Waterdeep in 1489 DR to found the Black Network's foothold. They regard themselves as the true Zhentarim of Waterdeep.
> - Manshoon's clone arrived later, took over Kolat Towers and built a cell of his own, the Splinter, in about two years. He peeled off a large part of the Doom Raiders' local network.
> - Manshoon's cell regards the Doom Raiders as deserters who stole his name, and the Doom Raiders are at active internal war with it. The two cells will not cooperate.

### The Black Network

- Manshoon was born in Zhentil Keep in 1229 DR and became one of the city's lords. He founded the Zhentarim in 1261 DR, and the Banite priest Fzoul Chembryl joined him in 1263 DR.
- The published adventure calls Manshoon "one of the founders" of the Zhentarim. This page follows the wiki and treats him as the founder.
- Manshoon set his wizards to open trade routes between the Moonsea and the Sword Coast. The Network began as a mercantile concern and grew into a company that trades mercenaries and goods, weapons included.
- Zhentil Keep fell in stages. Its northern half was ruined about 1369 DR after Cyric's fall, and the Shadovar razed the whole city in 1383 DR over the Zhentarim's alliance with the phaerimm.
- The Black Network has an open recruitment policy, and it prizes tenacity and loyalty without requiring either.
- The Network has long sought political influence in Waterdeep. The Masked Lords, the nobility and the guilds are strong enough to have kept it out.
- The Zhentarim are now reforming a council, and efforts are underway to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep. The Doom Raiders see the vault's gold as the price of a seat on that council.
- Under the leadership of the Pereghost, the wiki says, the Zhentarim changed from a conquering force into a mercenary army that served others. By 1489 DR they were said to have more members than ever and a reputation for service (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Zhentarim; Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, 5e). `[research: wiki only]`
- The old outline calls the Network "a criminal network spanning the Sword Coast". The wiki supports only the trade-route origin. `[research]`

### The Clones

- Manshoon built the stasis clone spell as insurance against his enemies. Over the years he died several times and returned in a new body.
- In 1370 DR Fzoul and Lord Orgauth killed him and found themselves facing a dozen or more clones. Each clone believed he was the real Manshoon and felt driven to destroy the others. These fights are the Manshoon Wars.
- Three clones survived. One rejoined the Zhentarim and conceded leadership to Fzoul, one became the vampire Orbakh in Westgate, and one took refuge in Undermountain with Halaster.
- The published adventure tells it differently: a mishap woke every clone at once, at least three survive, and the one now in Waterdeep hid in Undermountain. Halaster Blackcloak captured him and took his left arm, the regrowth failed, and he wears an artificial arm and hand.
- Simulacra are a separate tool. Manshoon makes copies of himself with the simulacrum spell, and the copy in Kolat Towers has more hit points and less spellcasting than he does.
- The man in Waterdeep is "a clone of a clone". He claims to be the one true Manshoon, as every surviving clone does.
- The Zhentarim outlasted the 1370 DR death. Fzoul led the Network after it, and one Manshoon clone served as a free agent under him.
- The old outline says the Network "retained only his methods" after Manshoon's coherent leadership ended. No source supports this. `[research]`

### The Doom Raiders in Waterdeep

- The Doom Raiders were five adventurers who plundered the lairs of liches, which some call "dooms". They gave up adventuring and joined the reformed Zhentarim in the late 1480s.
- Their leaders are Davil Starsong, Master of Opportunities and Negotiations; Istrid Horn, Master of Trade and Coin; Skeemo Weirdbottle, Master of Magic; Tashlyn Yafeera, Master of Arms and Mercenaries; and Ziraj the Hunter, Master of Assassination.
- They were sent to Waterdeep in 1489 DR to establish a Zhentarim foothold. The published adventure says only that they came "three years ago".
- They want a legitimate economic foothold, which means alliances with local guilds and nobles. They have formed several, run afoul of other houses, and fended off Harper spies.
- Their fronts are ordinary businesses. Davil holds court in a curtained room at the Yawning Portal, Istrid lends money from a Dock Ward warehouse, and Skeemo runs Weirdbottle's Concoctions in the Trades Ward.
- Tashlyn serves as Captain of the City Guard at the South Gate under the dwarf magister Vorondar Levelstone. She has earned his confidence over two years, according to the Doom Raiders organization page, and the published adventure gives no duration.
- The cell prefers contracts to confrontation and holds that the Black Network works best as a legitimate enterprise. Its agreements hold, and its violence is purposeful.
- Davil wants to end the street violence that Manshoon's cell has caused, because a legitimate criminal enterprise cannot run in a city where an illegitimate one keeps shooting people.
- The Doom Raiders operate openly, meet in taverns and recruit adventurers. They are the first Zhentarim contact the party meets, during **Trollskull Alley**.
- The Doom Raiders believe they are the true Zhentarim of Waterdeep. The old outline says Davil's cell "separates from Manshoon on pragmatic grounds". The sources reverse that direction: the cell came first, and Manshoon's clone peeled off its network.

> *I confess I accepted a drink at the Yawning Portal from Davil Starsong, and that I stayed for the whole of the speech that came with it. His position, delivered with a patience I have rarely met in his trade, was that the Black Network is a trading company first and everything else a long way after (I wrote it down at once, having never heard a Zhentarim explain himself so politely).*
> *~Volo*

### Manshoon's Waterdeep Operation

- Kolat Towers was built in the late fourteenth century by the wizard brothers Duhlark and Alcedor Kolat. Duhlark added a force field that covers the whole structure and admits only holders of a keyed amulet.
- In 1379 DR the City Watch captain Lady Gondafrey tried to breach the towers and was taken prisoner. Alcedor died there in 1385 DR, and Duhlark died nine years after him with the force field still running.
- Manshoon's clone fled Undermountain, found the towers abandoned with the force field intact, and took the amulet-making instructions from Duhlark's study. He spent two years building the Splinter.
- The Splinter is hub and spoke. Kolat Towers is the command center, and the Interrogation House in the North Ward and Yellowspire in the Castle Ward are field offices linked to it by teleportation circles.
- A pass-amulet system replaced a courier network, and the amulets can be revoked from the towers. An extradimensional sanctum behind the towers holds his closest lieutenants and his most sensitive work.
- The clone arrived after the Doom Raiders and peeled off a large part of the local network they had built. Through it he learned that Neverember had used the local Zhentarim's resources in the embezzlement.
- His aims in Waterdeep are to control the underworld, to bribe and blackmail the Masked Lords, and to take the Stone of Golorr for the Weave knowledge inside it. Two Masked Lords, Corylus Thann and Jelenn Urmbrusk, already vote his way under pressure.
- The published adventure gives him a different goal: to oust the Open Lord, replace the City Watch with Black Network mercenaries, and then turn on Halaster. The campaign keeps the Weave goal, which is set out on the **Manshoon** villain page.
- Manshoon's supporters among the Zhentarim want Xanathar destroyed and the city's economy in their hands. The Doom Raiders want to expose Manshoon before the authorities drive them out.
- The old outline says the towers were set up out of sight and that Manshoon "read Neverember's ouster as political opportunity". The nearest source for the second claim is the Alexandrian remix, which has him learn of the embezzlement through the Doom Raiders' network. `[research]`

### Two Organizations, One Name

- Two cells use the name Zhentarim in Waterdeep, and each denies the other's claim. The distinction should become clear to attentive players during **Trollskull Alley**, because the Doom Raiders' professionalism makes Manshoon's operatives land harder when they appear.
- The Doom Raiders call themselves the legitimate Black Network in the city. They are at active internal war with Manshoon's cell, and anyone who joins them is caught in the crossfire.
- Manshoon's cell does not acknowledge the Doom Raiders as Zhentarim. It regards them as deserters who stole his name.
- The cells will not cooperate or share intelligence, and where their operations overlap they undermine each other. Information given to the Doom Raiders does not reach Manshoon's agents.
- The Doom Raiders have a leak of their own. Skeemo Weirdbottle has been feeding intelligence to Manshoon's cell since before the party was recruited.
- Factions that speak to players say "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener.

## Overview

The Zhentarim, also called the Black Network, is a mercenary and trading company founded in Zhentil Keep in 1261 DR, a city that has since fallen to ruin. In Waterdeep it is known mainly through the Doom Raiders, a band of retired adventurers who came to the city in 1489 DR and describe themselves as the Zhentarim of Waterdeep. They sell mercenary work and lend money through their businesses, and they welcome adventurers who are looking for contracts. The Black Network has long wanted a place in the city's politics, and the strength of the Masked Lords, the nobility and the guilds has kept it out.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Secret
> - Waterdeep holds two Zhentarim organizations, and the Doom Raiders are only one of them. Nobody speaks Manshoon's name to a player until **Manshoon Named** is marked for that listener.
> - The Harpers do not know that the Splinter is Manshoon's cell or that it answers to Kolat Towers. No Harper contact names him.
> - The man in Kolat Towers is a clone who believes he is the true Manshoon, and he keeps a simulacrum in reserve. The old outline asked whether he was the original or a simulacrum, and he is a third thing.
> - His location is set by the escalation tier. At Unaware and Suspicious he is in E13, where his study is, and at Alert and Lockdown he is in E12. The activation of the simulacrum is linked to the same tiers.
> - Skeemo Weirdbottle is a traitor to the Doom Raiders and has been from the start. Davil has suspected it for months, and Tashlyn confirms it during his arrest.

**Tone:** Measured and factual, in the Ember history register. The Doom Raiders are sincere and Manshoon is patient. State each cell's view of the other as that cell's own belief, and give no narrator ruling on which one is the true Zhentarim.
