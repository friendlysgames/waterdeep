# The Players Arrive

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this is:** The Era page for the months before the campaign opens, the eighth and last of the eight eras. It follows **A City Watching Itself** and ends on the evening of Ches 1, 1492 DR, when the party reaches the Yawning Portal.
> - **Where it is used:** The GM reads it before the first session to see what each faction has already done and how long ago. It answers "how long has this faction been here?" at the table and keeps the arrivals separate, because no source has the factions move at once.
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers**.
> - **Spoiler level:** Everything above the Overview is GM-only. The Overview is the public face and mentions no Eye, no Stone and no secret allegiance.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM state, for any faction or object, where it stood on the night before Ches 1, which of those dates the sources fix, and which are only arithmetic.

**Keywords:** Doom Raiders, Splinter cell, Kolat Towers, nimblewrights, Scarlet Marpenoth, Nar'l Xibrindas, Dalakhar, Stone of Golorr, Floon Blagmaar, Ches 1

**Span:** Marpenoth 1491 DR to Ches 1, 1492 DR, about five months. Derived: the earliest dated entry is the first nimblewright sale on Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR (the ledger gives no year; 1491 is the latest Marpenoth before the campaign opens), and the campaign opens on Ches 1, 1492 DR. Some threads begin earlier and are dated in the sections below. `[research]`

**Other Names:** None sourced. `[research]`

> #### Lore Summary
> - The villains did not arrive together. The Doom Raiders reached Waterdeep in 1489 DR, Manshoon's clone built the Splinter cell over about two years, Jarlaxle's nimblewright sales begin on Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR, and Nar'l Xibrindas was placed in the Xanathar's Guild one year ago, in 1491 DR.
> - Three Eyes of the Stone of Golorr are already held when the campaign opens. Xanathar has Eye #1, the Cassalanters have Eye #2 and Jarlaxle has Eye #3. Eye #1 reached Xanathar about three months before the fireball (`arc-f-xanathars-lair.md` line 9), and the sources do not date Eye #2. Eye #3 reached Jarlaxle on the night before Ches 1 (see The Night Before Ches 1).
> - Dalakhar, the agent of Dagult Neverember, has the Stone and is carrying it toward Trollskull Manor. Urstul Floxin's Zhentarim cell is tracking him.
> - Floon Blagmaar is taken on the night before Ches 1, and the party's arrival at the Yawning Portal the next evening begins the campaign.
> - The Cassalanters signed their bargain about 1489 DR (see **A Debt in Blood**). Victoro Cassalanter was already asking about the vault a few weeks before the fireball.

### Footholds in the City

- The Doom Raiders were sent to establish a Zhentarim foothold in Waterdeep in 1489 DR. The campaign's own organization page says only that they arrived "several years ago". Source: the Forgotten Realms Wiki, Doom Raiders, citing the published adventure.
- Manshoon's clone fled Undermountain after Halaster Blackcloak severed its left arm, found Kolat Towers abandoned with the force field still running, and took the towers as a base. Source: the **Kolat Towers** quest opener.
- The clone then spent two years building the Splinter cell, which is about 1490 DR counted back from Ches 1, 1492 DR. `[research]` The structure is hub-and-spoke: Kolat Towers as the command center, with the Interrogation House in the North Ward and Yellowspire in the Castle Ward as field offices.
- The simulacrum in Kolat Towers was created seven years before the campaign, about 1485 DR, and has been preserved in ice since. `[research]`
- Both Zhentarim cells are in the city when the campaign opens. Neither accepts the other as the real Zhentarim, and they do not cooperate.

### Jarlaxle's Nimblewrights

- Jarlaxle Baenre came to Waterdeep to advance Luskan's bid to join the Lords' Alliance. He brought nimblewrights to sell to wealthy families in every ward.
- Each nimblewright feeds a crystal ball aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, so every sale is also a listening post.
- The sale ledger gives the dates. NW-01 sold on Marpenoth 3, NW-02 on Marpenoth 7 to O. Gralhund of Saerdoun Street, NW-03 on Marpenoth 12 to V. Cassalanter (marked void), NW-04 on Marpenoth 19, NW-05 on Uktar 4, and NW-06 to NW-09 between Uktar and Nightal. The year is 1491 DR by derivation.
- The *Scarlet Marpenoth* is a Lantanese submersible launched in 1489 DR (`adventure-wdh.json` line 4599). The wiki says Jarlaxle acquired it in 1491 DR, one year before the Faire's arrival (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Scarlet Marpenoth; Waterdeep: Dragon Heist, 5e, with `adventure-wdh.json` line 23034). The published adventure mounts it beneath the *Eyecatcher*. In the campaign it has been moored in Deepwater Harbor since Jarlaxle reached the city, and Eye #3 is aboard it when the campaign opens.
- Ryvarra watches the Yawning Portal from the observation balcony, posing as a Calimshan cloth merchant. She has sat there every evening for two weeks when the party arrives, which is about Alturiak 17, 1492 DR. `[research]`
- Jarlaxle has known of the Stone, the three Eyes and the vault since he arrived in Waterdeep, which the ledger puts no later than Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR. The user settled this in Session 47. The Sea Maidens Faire's carnival fleet and parades arrive in Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21 for Fleetswake, while the *Scarlet Marpenoth* has been moored there all year.

### Nar'l in the Xanathar's Guild

- Jarlaxle placed Nar'l Xibrindas inside the Xanathar's Guild before the campaign. Nar'l convinced the current Xanathar, a beholder named Zushaxx (it/its), to eliminate its other advisors, and he reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending*.
- The published adventure gives his placement as "a year ago" and the Forgotten Realms Wiki as 1491 DR. The two agree, and the user's ruling is one year, placed in 1491 DR. The campaign pages that say three years or eleven are stale (see Draft Notes).
- Xanathar suspects a mole and has assigned Nar'l a grell bodyguard with orders to kill him at the first sign of disloyalty. It is hunting in the wrong direction and has not identified him.
- Eye #1 reached Xanathar when Manshoon's Zhentarim sent envoys carrying it from the Protector's Enclave in Neverwinter. Xanathar killed the envoys and kept the Eye about three months before the fireball (`arc-f-xanathars-lair.md` line 9), which is about Nightal 1491 DR by derivation.
- By 1492 DR the Guild holds Skullport and is fighting a street war with the Zhentarim. Source: the Forgotten Realms Wiki, Xanathar Guild, for Skullport and the published adventure for the war.

### The Stone in Motion

- Dalakhar, a rock gnome spy, serves Dagult Neverember. He had questioned Kalain about Neverember's vault years before the campaign.
- By the morning of Ches 22 Dalakhar has the Stone of Golorr and is carrying it to Trollskull Manor, where he means to deliver it to the party. Which keeper he took it from is unsettled. See Draft Notes.
- Xanathar's Guild knows Dalakhar was moving the Stone, though it does not know what the Eyes are. Urstul Floxin's Zhentarim cell was tracking him.
- The Gralhunds funded the Zhentarim seizure of Renaer Neverember to gain leverage over Dalakhar, according to Orond Gralhund, who says Yalah Gralhund arranged it. Source: **Gralhund Villa**, the guest suite.
- The Cassalanters know the vault lies beneath the Brandath Crypts. Their agents found Eye #2 there by infernal divination, and Victoro Cassalanter visited Kalain a few weeks before the fireball to ask what Dalakhar had asked. The sources give no sign that the family has sped up its plan since the bargain.
- Nim of the House of Inspired Hands built a second nimblewright about three months before the fireball. It fled the temple about one month before, which puts it near Alturiak 22, 1492 DR. `[research]`

### The Night Before Ches 1

- Floon Blagmaar is taken on the night before Ches 1. The published adventure says "two nights"; the campaign settled on "last night".
- Urstul Floxin's cell led the kidnapping and fled to Kolat Towers before the quest begins. The City Watch has Splinter thugs under arrest at the Dock Ward scene.
- The cell seized Renaer's mourning locket and pried it open at the Candle Lane warehouse. Xanathar's Guild raided the warehouse the same night and carried off what it found in the back office, the Eye included, without knowing what the Eye was. Bregan D'aerthe agents already inside Xanathar's hideout lifted it that night, and it reached Jarlaxle aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* before the party reached the hideout. Manshoon himself never held it. This route is the main session's reading of the user's Session 47 ruling "Jarlaxle has men in Xanathar's hideout".
- Volothamp Geddarm hires the party at the Yawning Portal on the evening of Ches 1. The era ends with the party walking into a room where every thread above is already moving.

**Tone:** Plain and dated, in the Ember history register. Give each arrival its own date and its own reason, and leave the Grand Game to the later pages. The convergence is something the GM sees only afterward, so no narrator announces it.

## Overview

Waterdeep enters the spring of 1492 DR as a prosperous city with a restless underworld. Brawls between the Xanathar's Guild and the Zhentarim have become common in the Dock Ward, and the City Watch is called to them often. Several noble households have taken delivery of mechanical servants bought from a foreign dealer, and the city is preparing for Fleetswake, the maritime festival that fills the harbor at the end of Ches.
