# A City Watching Itself

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - **What this is:** The Era page for the Waterdeep of the campaign's opening, the political situation the four villain factions are exploiting. It is the seventh of eight eras, after **The Open Lord's Fall** and before **The Players Arrive**.
> - **Where it is used:** The GM reads it before voicing Laeral Silverhand or Jalester Silvermane, and before answering any question about why the Open Lord has not stopped the Grand Game or what she knows about the vault.
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers**, **Vault of Dragons** (Laeral's offer for the gold), and the Lords' Alliance events **An Audience with the Open Lord**, **Fentree's Ledger**, **Escalation to the Open Lord**, **Warduke** and **Lioncrown**.
> - **Spoiler level:** Everything above the Overview is GM-only. The Overview is the public face of the government. It leaves out the vault, the Stone, secret allegiances and Laeral's health.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM say what Laeral knows, wants and does about the Grand Game at campaign start, and why a capable Open Lord is still behind the factions.

**Keywords:** Laeral Silverhand, Open Lord, Masked Lords, Dagult Neverember, Force Grey, Vajra Safahr, Lords' Alliance, Jalester Silvermane, Elminster, Writ

**Span:** About 1489 DR to the campaign (1492 DR). The Alexandrian files date Laeral's accession and Neverember's deposition to 1489 DR, the Forgotten Realms Wiki says about 1489 DR, and the published adventure says only "a few years ago." The start year is therefore derived. The campaign opens on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

**Other Names:** Laeral's Waterdeep. This is the old outline's heading and a GM name. No in-world name is sourced. `[research]`

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Lore Summary
> - Laeral Silverhand became Open Lord about three years before the campaign, after the other Lords voted Dagult Neverember out and asked her to take his place. She accepted reluctantly, was overwhelmed at first, and has since settled into the work.
> - She governs with deputies and uses her own magic sparingly, because it has waned since the Spellplague. Only Elminster knows how far, and she admits it to one Lioncrown of the Lords' Alliance.
> - At campaign start she believes the vault lies in Neverwinter. She does not know the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced, and she does not know four villain factions are moving on the city.
> - She wants the 500,000 gp in the city treasury and wants it recovered without a public scandal. If she learns that the party holds it, she asks once for its return and offers a finder's fee of 10 percent.
> - Her intelligence comes from Jalester Silvermane and the Watch, and it is about three moves behind. The party's reports, most of all those carried by the Lords' Alliance, are what bring her up to date.

### Laeral Takes the Seat

- Laeral Silverhand is the fifth of the goddess Mystra's seven daughters, born in 765 DR. She was once the Witch-Queen of the North and led the adventuring company called the Nine.
- The published adventure says she married Khelben Arunsun, who became the Blackstaff, and retired from public life after he died. The campaign keeps her a widow. She came back after the Spellplague and the Sundering, weakened by Mystra's death and rebirth.
- The Masked Lords voted Dagult Neverember out while he was rebuilding Neverwinter. They asked Laeral to fill the vacancy, and she took it reluctantly.
- The published adventure says she was overwhelmed by the demands of the nobles and the guildmasters at first and has since settled nicely.
- The wiki adds that Mystra herself appeared to Laeral and asked her to keep the North stable by becoming Open Lord, and that Laeral won the Masked Lords' support and replaced Neverember (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Laeral Silverhand; Death Masks and Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, 5e). `[research: wiki only]`
- The campaign makes Laeral a moon elf. The published adventure and the wiki both call her human, and the wiki's alignment, chaotic good, matches the campaign's, so only the species differs (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Laeral Silverhand, infobox race and 5e alignment).
- The wiki lists seven Open Lords to 1489 DR: Ahghairon, Baeron Silmaeril, Lhestyn, Piergeiron the Paladinson, Caladorn Cassalanter, Dagult Neverember and Laeral Silverhand (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Lords of Waterdeep; City of Splendors boxed set Campaign Guide and Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, 5e). Caladorn was Victoro Cassalanter's father; see **A Debt in Blood**. `[research: wiki only]`
- The old outline called her era stable, reformist and watchful, and called her the kind of leader Ahghairon designed the system to produce. The adventure supports "stable". No source supports "reformist", and the Ahghairon idea is campaign framing. `[research]`

### How Laeral Governs

- She uses her magic sparingly and relies on trusted advisors and deputies. When time allows she slips out of the Palace in disguise to clear her head and visit old friends.
- Mirt, a Masked Lord and a senior Harper, is her closest advisor. Elminster, the Warden of Waterdeep, answers to her. The Commander of the Watch reports to her.
- Her spies keep watch on the adventurers in the city, and she rewards those who put Waterdeep ahead of themselves. Jalester Silvermane is her field operative and reports to her directly, and the Lords' Alliance is her instrument for acting outside the Palace.
- Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff, commands Force Grey. Laeral can order Vajra to unleash it in great need. Vajra has secretly sent its members on unauthorized missions, and Laeral has not confronted her.
- Laeral explains her inaction as a test of Vajra's competence. She also covets the Blackstaff, which holds Khelben's soul, and she thinks of Vajra as an insecure child. The two avoid each other.
- Only Elminster knows the full extent of her decline. She tells one Lioncrown of the Lords' Alliance in **Lioncrown**, and she hopes that a fragment of Halaster's crystal will halt the decline.

### What Laeral Knows About the Vault

- Neverember set the gold aside by decree and had a lock made that no Masked Lord could open. The institution that Ahghairon built was locked out of its own treasury.
- Laeral was told for years that the vault lay in Neverwinter, and she believed it. Jalester's field reports and the Watch's contacts have placed it there too.
- She does not know that the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced in Waterdeep or that the four villain factions are circling it. **Fentree's Ledger** corrects her intelligence.
- She wants the gold in the treasury. In her framing the money was taxed from Waterdhavian citizens and merchants, and Neverember's exile does not change who owns it.
- She wants the recovery kept out of public view. The old outline added that the story of how the vault came to exist damages the Lords' institutional credibility, and no other page says so. `[research]`
- The old outline said she suspects the vault exists, does not know where, and has contingencies. The newer pages replace it: she believes she knows where it is, and that belief is wrong. The "contingencies" are unsupported.

### Her Place in the Grand Game

- Laeral does not play the Grand Game herself. The Lords' Alliance acts on her behalf, and it will not let the party keep the gold.
- Members who bring the Grand Game to Jalester draw her interest. Once she is fully informed she makes the recovery a political priority and gives the party real resources.
- If the party holds the gold and she learns of it, she makes one polite offer to take it back in exchange for a 10 percent finder's fee, which is 50,000 gp. She asks once and accepts the answer.
- In **An Audience with the Open Lord** she issues a Masked Lord's Writ for the vault district whatever answers the party gives. She also discloses a Splinter offer: a complete account of the Masked Lords it has confirmed, for a fifth of the vault's contents before the treasury sees them. She has not decided, and she asks the party's opinion.
- She judges that nobody is winning the Grand Game, and she would like the trading to stop before someone she knows is hurt.
- This agrees with the City Watch's place in **The Grand Game**: the government is trying, aware that some cases are politically untouchable, and it learns what it knows from informants who are a step behind the factions.
- The old outline said she watches and lets the factions exhaust themselves before she moves. The newer pages show a government that is acting on stale intelligence, so the page does not carry the old reading. `[research]`

### The Lords Under Strain

- The published adventure says several Masked Lords were assassinated in quick succession some years ago. Yalah Gralhund had expected a vacancy and was passed over, and Orond has been angry ever since.
- The Alexandrian files date "the Lord Murders" to 1491 DR. The wiki's Death Masks account puts a conspiracy in Mirtul of 1491 DR, led by the Masked Lord Braethan Cazondur, in which seven Masked Lords were killed within a short time (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Lords of Waterdeep; Death Masks).
- Manshoon's cell holds two Masked Lords through blackmail, and it keeps a ledger of five Lords it has identified: Mirt, Corylus Thann, Jelenn Urmbrusk, Thardouk Starbuckler and Dorgar Adarbrent.
- The Lords' institution has therefore been bled and penetrated before the campaign opens. No source says Laeral knows how far the penetration goes. `[research]`

## Overview

Laeral Silverhand is the Open Lord of Waterdeep, and she has held the office since about 1489 DR. In that year the Masked Lords voted Dagult Neverember out and asked her to take his place. She is the fifth of the goddess Mystra's seven daughters, an archmage of great age and renown who was once the Witch-Queen of the North and the wife of the wizard Khelben Arunsun. She governs from the Palace with the help of trusted deputies and advisors, among them Mirt, and the city has been quiet under her.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - **Timeline.** 765 DR: Laeral is born, in the published adventure. 1467 DR: the Alexandrian files have Neverember in office as Open Lord and proclaiming himself ruler of Neverwinter. The wiki says he took office about 1468 DR, and the adventure shows him in office in 1475 DR. About 1489 DR: the Lords vote him out and Laeral takes the seat, which ends roughly twenty-one years of his tenure. The 1489 DR year is derived.
> - 1491 DR: the Lord Murders, in the Alexandrian files and the wiki. Nightal 1491 DR: the Gralhund journals call Neverember "former Open Lord."
> - Ches 1, 1492 DR: the campaign opens.
> - Shieldmeet, 1492 DR: the wiki says the Lords' Rule is reaffirmed at the Shieldmeet Council every four years (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Lords of Waterdeep; City of Splendors boxed set Campaign Guide p. 68, 2e). The wiki says 1372 DR contained a Shieldmeet, so every fourth year after it does too (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Shieldmeet; Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting, 3rd edition). 1492 is 120 years after 1372, which makes it a Shieldmeet year, and the day follows Midsummer, just after Flamerule 11. No campaign file uses it.

**Tone:** Dry and watchful, in the Ember history register. Laeral is a competent ruler who is short of information and short of strength, and the page presents both as plain conditions. The Overview stays on the public office and says nothing of the decline or the vault.
