> - **Where it is used:** The GM reads it before voicing Xanathar, Ahmaergo or Nar'l Xibrindas, before answering a contact's question about where the Guild comes from, and before running area X33 of **Xanathar's Lair**, the crypt of the previous Xanathars.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM answer any question about why Xanathar is a title, how the Guild remembers or forgets its past, what Skullport is to it and what the lair's crypt proves.

**Keywords:** Xanathar, Xanathar's Guild, Zushaxx, Kirukesai, Skullport, Sargauth Enclave, Netherese, Skulls of Skullport, Ahmaergo, Nar'l Xibrindas, Nihiloor, Crypt of Xanathars Past

**Span:** By 1357 DR to 1492 DR, about 135 years of Guild history. The Forgotten Realms Wiki first shows the Guild thriving as a thieves' guild in 1357 DR, and no source gives its founding year. `[research]` The old era block said "a century". Skullport itself reaches back to about -750 DR. The campaign opens on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

**Other Names:** Xanathar's Guild is the campaign name. The published adventure drops the possessive and the wiki calls it Xanathar's Thieves' Guild. The wiki also gives the Agents of the Eye as the older Skullport name, used in 1372 DR. No in-world name for the era itself is sourced. `[research]`

> **[GM]**

> #### Lore Summary
> - "Xanathar" is a title held by a succession of beholders. Each holder inherits the Guild's apparatus and its paranoia together. The current Xanathar is a beholder named Zushaxx, and its pronouns are "it". Kirukesai was one of the previous holders.
> - The Guild keeps continuity by eliminating anyone who knows more than the current Xanathar. The campaign line is that Zushaxx believes it is the original. The crypt in the lair shows the title is a succession, and the user has ruled that the remix line wins over the published room.
> - Skullport is the Guild's undercity base. Netherese wizards built it more than two thousand years before the campaign, many masters have held it since, and the Guild holds it in 1492 DR.
> - The lair is an ancient Netherese dungeon that beholders expanded. A long tunnel joins it to Skullport, and a secret stair joins it to Waterdeep's sewers.
> - At campaign start the Guild is an empire built on fear, run by a paranoid intelligence and served by people who survive by hiding their ambitions behind obedience.

### The Title

- "Xanathar" is a title. The first holder established the criminal underworld's structure, and each later Xanathar inherited the apparatus and the paranoia together. The old era block adds that the title predates any individual beholder. The wiki says the opposite, and Draft Notes logs the conflict.
- The published adventure agrees that the current Xanathar is not the first beholder to claim the mantle (WDH, "Xanathar" entry).
- The current Xanathar is a beholder named Zushaxx, and its pronouns are "it". Kirukesai was one of the previous Xanathars. The wiki lists both names and spells the second Kirukeskai or Kirueskai.
- The wiki's account of the first holder: a beholder hired by the Zhentarim to start a cell in Skullport. It cut them off, built its own guild, and by 1357 DR the Guild was a thieves' guild working through freelance agents under four Councilors. Each Councilor supervised one domain: mercenaries, bookkeeping, thievery or blackmail (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Xanathar's Thieves' Guild; Waterdeep and the North). `[research: wiki only]`
- The wiki's account of Kirukesai: a beholder slaver in Skullport who led the Agents of the Eye, killed the first Xanathar and took the name. It merged the two organizations without telling the rank and file, and by 1372 DR it ruled through eleven Masters. Accounts of its death differ. `[research: wiki only]`
- The wiki names two later holders, Xandulzrithral, who retired peacefully, and Izulktur, who held the title briefly in the 15th century and "replaced itself" with Zushaxx. Kirukesai therefore comes long before Zushaxx and is not its immediate predecessor. `[research: wiki only]`
- The only account of how Zushaxx took the title is the wiki's: Izulktur replaced itself with Zushaxx, with no method or date given (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Xanathar's Thieves' Guild and Xanathar; Dungeon #206). No campaign page adds to it.

### Guild Structure and Continuity

- The Guild keeps continuity by systematically eliminating anyone who knows more than the current Xanathar. What remains is a surveillance network with no institutional memory beyond what the current beholder chooses to believe.
- Anyone can join after committing a serious crime. Low-ranking members speculate about their boss, and few have any inkling that it is a beholder (WDH, Chapter 1 faction entry).
- Members given an assignment may receive a monstrous bodyguard or assistant with secret orders to kill them if they fail. Treachery within the ranks is common, and the beholder distrusts anyone who wins its favor (WDH).
- The ranks, lowest to highest, are Eyestalker, Agent of the Eye, Eye Ray, Guild Boss and Hand of the Eye (WDH).
- Ahmaergo writes most of the orders that bear Xanathar's name.
- The Dock Ward is the Guild's primary surface territory.

### The Current Xanathar

- **Campaign line:** Zushaxx believes it is the original Xanathar. The belief is now structural to the Guild's identity and cannot be corrected. The user has settled the line: the remix overrides the published adventure, so the page needs no earlier cause for the belief (Draft Notes).
- By 1491 DR the wiki has Zushaxx taking Nar'l Xibrindas as its only advisor and eliminating the others. `[research: wiki only]` Nar'l has been in the Guild one year, placed in 1491 DR (WDH line 32323, "a year ago"; the wiki dates it 1491 DR).
- By 1492 DR Zushaxx is in full control of Skullport. A rival beholder, Nerozar, challenged it for lordship and lost, as the published Undermountain adventure confirms. The wiki adds that Zushaxx had Nerozar eliminated. `[research: wiki only]`
- Zushaxx also appointed four lieutenants, and the wiki names Kal'dir, Quid, Sial Sapphire and Draak. It says the four were later expanded to six (Forgotten Realms Wiki, Xanathar's Thieves' Guild; Dungeon #206 and Waterdeep: Dragon Heist). None of these appears in the campaign's Notable Figures. `[research: wiki only]`

### Skullport

- Skullport is a subterranean town in a vast cavern on the River Sargauth, connected to the third level of Undermountain. It has three levels of buildings, and a bridge leads from its southern end to Skull Island, where a fortress stands garrisoned by Xanathar's minions (WDMM, "Skullport").
- The campaign puts Skullport three levels below the Yawning Portal well and outside city jurisdiction (**Undermountain and Skullport**). `[research: no adventure text states the jurisdiction]`
- The sourced chain of masters runs from a Netherese enclave to the Guild. Netherese wizards of the Sargauth Enclave built the place about -750 DR. Halaster Blackcloak's apprentice Shradin Mulophor settled it in 1148 DR, and the Keepers took control of its sea locks in 1166 to 1168 DR. The Skulls destroyed Shradin in 1369 DR, and the Spellplague of 1385 DR gutted the old magical mantle. Mithral revived the town late in the 15th century. `[research: wiki only]`
- The published adventure lists the town's earlier occupants as duergar, drow, dwarves, crazed wizards, slavers and pirates. Today it is under the sway of Xanathar, and the businesses that remain pay the Guild for protection.
- Thirteen flameskulls, the remnants of the Sargauth Enclave, still haunt the town and consider themselves its true rulers. They rarely communicate, and by 1492 DR they do not interfere with the Guild.
- The town trades in slaves, drugs and poisons, cadavers, hired blades and smugglers, which covers most of what the surface economy will not handle openly. `[research: wiki only]`
- Waterdeep once used Skullport as a place to banish criminals. `[research: wiki only]`
- The lair is an ancient dungeon complex built by Netherese wizards and expanded by beholders over time. A magically lit tunnel of 300 feet runs from it to the Guts & Garters Inn in Skullport, and a secret staircase gives access to Waterdeep's sewers (WDH, "Xanathar's Lair").
- The Guild keeps watch posts on the upper levels of Undermountain, staffed by bugbears and goblins, to stop adventurers reaching the lower levels (WDMM).
- The Guild has rivals below. A drow mage named Rizzeryl works for House Auvryndar and for the Zhentarim, and he and his wererats harry Guild forces on one of the upper levels (WDMM). Which Zhentarim he serves is not settled for the remix, and Draft Notes lists it.

### The Guild at Campaign Start

- The Guild is an empire built on fear, run by a paranoid intelligence and served by people who survive by hiding their ambitions behind obedience.
- The war with Manshoon's Zhentarim is an active, low-level street war in the Dock Ward. It began when Manshoon's envoys came to Skullport three months before the fireball with Eye #1 of the Stone of Golorr, and Xanathar killed them.
- Eye #1 now sits in Sylgar's bowl in the lair, and Xanathar does not know what it is.
- Xanathar suspects Nar'l of disloyalty and has set a grell to kill him at the first proof. It does not know that he reports to Bregan D'aerthe or to Jarlaxle.

## Overview

Xanathar's Guild is the largest criminal organization in Waterdeep and, according to rumor, the only one led by a beholder. Its roots lie in Skullport, an outlaw port in the caverns below the city, reached through Undermountain and outside the Watch's jurisdiction, which Netherese wizards built long ago and which many masters have held since. Its informants work in every ward, and its main territory above ground is the Dock Ward. It is said that many of its own members have never seen their master.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Secret
> - Nihiloor, the mind flayer, has been psionically dominating Xanathar for the last four months. The Xanathar that the party meets, including the one who killed Manshoon's envoys and sent response teams against the party's allies, has been Nihiloor's instrument. Xanathar does not know.
> - Nar'l Xibrindas is a Bregan D'aerthe spy. Xanathar suspects a mole but does not know where Nar'l's reports go.
> - The crypt of previous Xanathars (area X33 of **Xanathar's Lair**) shows that the title is claimed through murder. Of the Guild's own people, only Nar'l knows the room exists.

**Tone:** Grave and played straight, in the Ember history register. The beholder's paranoia is a dangerous fact and the goldfish is funny, and the narration makes no joke of either. The Overview stays on rumor and the GM bullets state facts flatly.

> #### GM Notes
> - **Area X33, the Crypt of Xanathars Past.** The published room holds four dead beholders preserved in embalming fluid in floor-to-ceiling crystal cylinders lit from within, and nine alcoves with a copper urn each, topped by ten eyestalks. Pouring out an urn's dust makes it coalesce into a floating beholder for a few seconds, growl and fall. A carved wall shows a scowling beholder flanked by two hooded wizards. If Xanathar is present, it gazes somberly at the tombs. The remix overrides this detail: Zushaxx believes it is the original, so it does not mourn the dead.

It holds the remains and effects of every previous holder, evidence that the title is claimed through murder, the original charter signed by the first holder, and a locked coffer with the personal journals of the last three Xanathars. The journals record early dealings with Manshoon's predecessors.
