> - **What this is:** The Era page for Dagult Neverember's years as Open Lord and the vote that ended them. It is the sixth of eight eras, after **A Debt in Blood** and before **A City Watching Itself**, and it is the origin of the Grand Game's prize.
> - **Where it is used:** The GM reads it before voicing Renaer Neverember, Laeral Silverhand or Aurinax, and before answering any contact's question about who Neverember was and why every faction wants what he hid.
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Vault of Dragons**.

**Purpose:** Lets the GM say what Neverember did with the city's gold, how he lost his office, and where the vault, the Stone and the three Eyes stood when the vote was taken.

**Keywords:** Dagult Neverember, Open Lord, Masked Lords, Laeral Silverhand, Neverwinter, Renaer Neverember, Alethea Brandath, the Stone of Golorr, the Vault of Dragons, Aurinax, Neverember's Enigma

> **[GM]**

> #### Lore Summary
> - Dagult Neverember was Open Lord of Waterdeep from about 1467 DR to about 1489 DR. His money and political skill helped the city recover, and he levied a monthly tax that is still collected. He spent growing amounts of time rebuilding Neverwinter.
> - During his tenure he embezzled 500,000 gp from the city and hid it in a dwarf-built vault beneath the Brandath Mausoleum. The vault's location is locked inside the Stone of Golorr. He blinded the Stone by removing its three Eyes, and he bound the gold dragon Aurinax to guard the vault.
> - In 1489 DR the Masked Lords voted him out while he was in Neverwinter, and Laeral Silverhand took the office at their request. His agents took the Stone from the palace and lost it on the way out of the city.
> - Neverember remains Lord of Neverwinter. His son Renaer lives in Waterdeep, estranged from him, and carried one of the three Eyes in a mourning locket without knowing it.
> - At campaign start Neverember still wants the gold, and Aurinax is still bound to a man who no longer holds the office.

### The Open Lord

- Dagult Neverember is an Illuskan human noble who built a mercantile fortune out of Waterdeep. He became Open Lord about 1467 DR, and the published adventure confirms him in office in 1475 DR.
- His wealth and political skill helped Waterdeep recover after years of neglect, and the wiki adds that Laeral Silverhand appreciated his efforts to restore the city. The campaign treats him as an ambitious politician who believed he was building something for Waterdeep even as he stole from it.
- He set a monthly tax of one shard per person above the age of ten, collected door to door by the City Guard. The published adventure dates it to the first year of his reign and describes it as still in force. A person who pays one dragon receives a writ that exempts them for twelve months.
- The wiki, citing the Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, records that he allowed a limited number of Waterdhavian titles to be bought and sold. It also records that he hired Tarnian mercenary ships to replace a city navy lost hunting Northlander pirates.
- He began rebuilding Neverwinter about the same time. The Alexandrian timeline dates the New Neverwinter project to 1467 DR, and the wiki dates it to 1468 DR. The wiki says he took the title Lord Protector there while he was still Open Lord, and the campaign calls him Lord of Neverwinter.
- As the rebuilding grew, he spent more of his time in Neverwinter and left Waterdeep's daily running to trusted lieutenants. The published adventure has him "off rebuilding" Neverwinter when the vote was taken.
- In 1475 DR he commissioned a portrait from the painter Kalain, and the two had an affair that lasted more than a year. He later used his influence to ruin her reputation and her work.
- The old outline lists "harbor expansion, post-Spellplague rebuilding in the Dock Ward, Watch funding increases" as his improvements. No source supports these. `[research]`

### The Embezzlement

- During his tenure Neverember began embezzling from the Waterdeep treasury. The published adventure puts the total at half a million dragons.
- The wiki records two other ways he used the office for himself. He hired mercenary companies in which he was an investor, and he used Waterdhavian soldiers and laborers to rebuild the High Road to Neverwinter at the expense of the city's own works.
- The Alexandrian author says Neverember used the local Zhentarim's resources to carry out the embezzlement, and this is how Manshoon's cell learned of it. See **The Founding of Chains** for the timing problem.
- The Alexandrian author's Cassalanter backstory ties the family's near bankruptcy partly to "bad investments combined with Dagult's embezzlements". The published adventure gives the near bankruptcy and no cause. See **A Debt in Blood**.
- The old outline says the diversions began small, as infrastructure projects that he could not get approved, and then "became personal". No source supports this. `[research]`
- While he held office, the factions of the city noticed that he was engaged in some large scheme and knew only its outline. They began to call it Neverember's Enigma.

### The Stone and the Vault

- The vault was built by dwarves, the Melairkyn in the Alexandrian account. It lies beneath the Brandath Mausoleum in the City of the Dead, and it is warded against magical detection and intrusion.
- Neverember found the vault while arranging the interment of his wife, Lady Alethea Brandath. He moved the remains of Lord Anri out of the oldest family mausoleum and buried her there, and he found the passage the Brandaths had forgotten.
- He stored the embezzled gold in the vault. To protect the secret, a wizard erased the vault's location from his mind and from the minds of his subordinates and trapped it inside the Stone of Golorr. The wizard then disappeared, and Neverember hid the Stone in the Palace of Waterdeep.
- The Stone is an aboleth transformed by magic. It can read the mind of anyone who attunes to it, and an attuned creature can draw out what it knows, including the vault's location.
- The campaign adds that Neverember blinded the Stone by removing its three Eyes. Without all three, it reveals nothing useful. The Eyes are placed as set out in the Secret below.
- Aurinax, an adult gold dragon, holds the dragonstaff of Ahghairon and can ignore the dragonward. Neverember let him keep the staff in exchange for guarding the vault until Neverember or his appointed vassals came to remove the gold.
- The published adventure says Neverember made the bargain when he became Open Lord. The campaign has him make it after he found the vault, and the second order is the one this page follows.
- No vassal was ever named. Aurinax has waited alone beneath the mausoleum for years, and he is unsure what his oath to a former Open Lord is worth now that the office is gone.

### The Ouster

- The Masked Lords voted Neverember out of office in 1489 DR, while he was in Neverwinter. The published adventure gives no date and says it happened "a few years ago".
- Laeral Silverhand, one of the Seven Sisters, took the office at the Masked Lords' request. The published adventure says she accepted reluctantly, was overwhelmed at first by the nobles and guildmasters, and has since settled into the role.
- Neverember stayed in Neverwinter, where he governs as its lord. The campaign pages call his removal an exile and call it public. The published adventure says only that he was ousted, and the wiki says the vote effectively exiled him.
- After the vote he made plans to retrieve the Stone and smuggle the gold out of the city. His spies took the Stone from the palace and were killed while leaving, and the Stone passed from hand to hand until it reached Xanathar.
- The wiki says Neverember grew bitter about the vote and blamed Waterdeep's nobles and guilds for it. Resentment of him stayed high in Waterdeep, and some Waterdhavians feared he would start a war to win the city back.
- The old outline says the Masked Lords investigated him and "managed the public story", leaving ongoing private damage. The published adventure says only that the Lords voted him out, and the Alexandrian author has Laeral's auditors find the embezzlement after she took office, so the investigation claim is unsupported. `[research]`

### Renaer

- Renaer Neverember is Dagult's son by Lady Alethea Brandath. His mother died while Dagult was in office and left her property to Renaer and nothing to Dagult. Renaer now owns more property than anyone else in Waterdeep, all of it inherited from her.
- The published adventure says father and son detest one another and have not spoken in years. The campaign describes Renaer as estranged and as living by choice outside his father's shadow.
- Renaer refused to help his father hide the gold. He knows nothing about the vault, the Stone's part in it or what his locket held, and his ignorance is genuine.
- If the vault surfaces, it ties Renaer's name to his father's again, whatever he knew. This is the old outline's reading of the stakes, and the Renaer Notable Figure page supports it through his fear that estrangement will look like cowardice.
- The old outline says Renaer "has spent three years working to separate his name from his father's". The published adventure describes a longer estrangement than that. `[research]`

## Overview

Dagult Neverember, a wealthy noble who built his fortune in trade, was Open Lord of Waterdeep from about 1467 DR, and his money and political skill helped the city recover from years of neglect. His reign brought the monthly door-to-door tax that Waterdhavians still pay, and he spent much of his time rebuilding Neverwinter, where he is lord today. In 1489 DR the Masked Lords voted him out of office while he was in Neverwinter, and Laeral Silverhand, one of the Seven Sisters, took the seat at their request. His son Renaer lives in Waterdeep, estranged from him, and owns the properties his mother left him.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Secret
> - Neverember placed the three Eyes in three places before the vote. He kept one with him in Neverwinter, he gave one to Renaer inside an ivory mourning locket in honor of his mother, and he hid one in a crypt of the Brandath Mausoleum near Alethea's tomb. The Renaer and Mausoleum placements are in the Alexandrian account, and the crypt is in the Vault of Dragons quest opener.
> - The Neverwinter Eye was later stolen from the Protector's Enclave by Manshoon's Zhentarim, who offered it to Xanathar. Xanathar killed the envoys and kept it, and it is Eye #1. The Cassalanters extracted the Brandath Eye through infernal divination, and it is Eye #2. Jarlaxle holds Eye #3, and Manshoon holds none.
> - Manshoon's cell took Renaer's locket from him the night before the campaign opens, during **Finding Floon**. The campaign pages disagree on what became of the Eye inside. The Draft Notes list the three accounts.
> - Neverember's agents did not know the full picture. Dalakhar was told to watch Renaer and not told why, because Neverember's chief concern was keeping the hidden Eye safe until he could regain the Stone.
> - Laeral believes the vault is in Neverwinter at campaign start. She knows of the embezzlement and not of the Stone's return or the four active factions.
> - Neverember still wants the gold back or, failing that, vindication. The vault's location was erased from his mind, so he cannot find it himself, and Aurinax will release the gold only to Neverember or his appointed vassals.

**Tone:** Grave and fair to Neverember, in the Ember history register. State his real improvements and his theft as two facts side by side, with no narrator verdict on either. The Overview stays on the public record and the GM bullets state hidden facts flatly.
