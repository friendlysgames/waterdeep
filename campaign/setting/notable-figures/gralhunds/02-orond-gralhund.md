# Orond Gralhund

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Tethyrian human noble, neutral evil*. Stat block: **Noble**.
> - **Affiliation:** House Gralhund
> - **Featured in:** **Gralhund Villa**, **Cassalanter Villa**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Orond should inspire contempt with his boorish posturing, pity for the man who hides behind his wife and his bodyguard, and dark amusement when the party discovers the secret he carries literally on his body.

**Emotions.** Orond most often feels inflated self-importance, impotent rage, fearful dependence on Yalah, paranoid suspicion of servants and visitors, petulant frustration at being passed over, and brief pathetic dignity when he attempts to assert authority.

**Motivations.** Orond wants to be the patriarch his title says he is, see Yalah appointed as a Masked Lord (which he believes will elevate him), and stop feeling afraid in his own home.

**Inspirations.** When playing Orond, channel Joffrey Baratheon (*Game of Thrones*), Tobias Funke (*Arrested Development*), and Percy Wetmore (*The Green Mile*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Orond is the patriarch of House Gralhund, a short, stocky man who dresses well and is easy on the eyes. To those who deal with him, his boorish nature, inflated ego, and despicable opinions about "the common rabble" emerge whenever Yalah is not present to quiet him. Deep down, he was born with a tiefling's tail (amputated as a boy; the scar remains), does not understand how entangled his family has become with the Black Network, and keeps Hrabbaz close for fear that others might attack and rob him at any moment.

**Morale.** In a confrontation, Orond barricades himself behind furniture and cowers. He breaks under a DC 10 Intimidation check (with disadvantage if he fears Yalah is watching). He throws himself at the mercy of the first person to bust through his barricade.

**Relationships.** Orond is the husband of Yalah Gralhund (devoted to her and terrified of her in equal measure), the employer of Hrabbaz (whom he barely controls), and a nobleman whose excessive boasting and temper tantrums vanish the moment his wife enters the room.

## Overview

Orond Gralhund is the patriarch of House Gralhund — a short, stocky man who dresses well and is easy on the eyes. His boorish nature and inflated opinions about the common rabble emerge readily when he believes himself unobserved.
