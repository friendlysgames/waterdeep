## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Laeral should inspire awe with her centuries of power and history, sympathy with the burden of leadership she accepted reluctantly, and unease when the party glimpses how diminished she truly is.

**Emotions.** Laeral most often feels weary resolve, sharp political calculation, guarded warmth toward allies, restless grief for Khelben, private frustration with Vajra, and fleeting joy when she sneaks out in disguise.

**Motivations.** Laeral wants to keep Waterdeep stable after Neverember's ouster, conceal the extent of her diminished power, and resist the urge to reclaim the Blackstaff (which holds Khelben Arunsun's soul).

**Inspirations.** When playing Laeral, channel Galadriel (*The Lord of the Rings*), President Roslin (*Battlestar Galactica*), and M (*James Bond*, Judi Dench era).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Laeral Silverhand is the Open Lord of Waterdeep, fifth of the Seven Sisters, a formidable archmage. To those she trusts, she is a woman who took the job because the Masked Lords asked and no one else could hold the city together. Deep down, only Elminster knows the extent of her decline since the Spellplague, and the Blackstaff on Vajra's hip holds the soul of the man she loved.

**Morale.** In a fight, Laeral would deploy archmage-level magic with centuries of tactical experience. She avoids direct confrontation when possible because every expenditure of power reveals how much she has lost.

**Relationships.** Laeral is the widow of Khelben Arunsun (the original Blackstaff), a close advisor to Mirt, an uneasy superior to Vajra Safahr (whom she views as an insecure child wielding her husband's staff), and a sister of the Seven Sisters (daughters of Mystra).

## Overview

The Open Lord of Waterdeep, fifth of the Seven Sisters, whose political authority and centuries as an archmage make her the most powerful figure the party is likely to address directly. She accepted the role because the city needed her, and she holds it with weary precision.
