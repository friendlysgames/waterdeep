# Jelenn Urmbrusk

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Tethyrian human noble, neutral*. Stat block: **Noble**.
> - **Affiliation:** City of Waterdeep (Masked Lord)
> - **Featured in:** **Kolat Towers**, **A Friend's House**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Jelenn should inspire sympathy with her financial desperation, frustration with her refusal to accept help on terms she cannot control, and reluctant respect for the pride that got her into this mess and keeps her standing.

**Emotions.** Jelenn most often feels desperate anxiety about debt, wounded pride, bitter regret over her failed investments, suspicious hostility toward strangers, fraying dignity, and private terror of Manshoon's leverage.

**Motivations.** Jelenn wants to escape Manshoon's blackmail, repay her debts without any other noble learning of them, and reclaim the financial independence that her bad investments destroyed.

**Inspirations.** When playing Jelenn, channel Cersei Lannister (*Game of Thrones*, middle seasons), Mrs. Dashwood (*Sense and Sensibility*), and Marge Gunderson (*Fargo*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Jelenn is a proud noblewoman in her fifties who travels with sixteen guards and has no time for strangers. To those she trusts, she is a Masked Lord who borrowed from the Zhentarim because she feared gossip more than gangsters. Deep down, she knows the decision was catastrophic, and Manshoon's blackmail is the price of her pride.

**Morale.** In a confrontation, Jelenn would demand the party leave and threaten dire consequences. Her threat is mostly bluster. If freed from Manshoon, she grants a Special Favor. If blackmailed by the party instead, she pretends to cooperate while secretly working against them.

**Relationships.** Jelenn is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep, a debtor of the Zhentarim (borrowed to avoid borrowing from rival nobles), and a blackmail victim of Manshoon's cell whose name appears on the Kolat Towers blackmail list.

## Overview

Jelenn Urmbrusk is a proud noblewoman in her fifties who travels with sixteen guards and meets uninvited contact with brisk, dismissive authority.
