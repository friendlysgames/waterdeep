## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Manshoon should inspire intellectual dread with his patient precision, frustrated rage from the party's inability to pin him down, and dark fascination with a centuries-old wizard who lost an arm to Halaster Blackcloak and refuses to stop scheming.

**Emotions.** Manshoon most often feels cold patient calculation, contemptuous superiority, precise anger reserved for specific targets, possessive ambition, frustrated pride about his artificial arm, and the absolute certainty that he is the one true Manshoon.

**Motivations.** Manshoon wants to acquire the Stone of Golorr for the ancient Weave knowledge encoded in its aboleth memory, use that knowledge to absorb the Weave and supplant Mystra, fund his preparations with the vault's gold, and deepen his hold over Masked Lords Corylus Thann and Jelenn Urmbrusk into a full Council majority.

**Inspirations.** When playing Manshoon, channel Magneto (*X-Men*), Emperor Palpatine (*Star Wars*), and Moriarty (*Sherlock Holmes*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Manshoon does not exist; he is never seen entering or leaving Kolat Towers, using a teleportation circle for all movement. To those he trusts, he is a surviving clone of the Zhentarim founder who fled Undermountain after Halaster amputated his left arm, crafted an artificial replacement, and now orchestrates a bid for total control of Waterdeep. Deep down, he claims to be the one true Manshoon, as all surviving clones do. He creates simulacra of himself and takes great pains to conceal his identity.

**Morale.** In combat, Manshoon lets his simulacrum absorb the opening volley while he casts from behind it. He prefers to capture rather than kill (prisoners answer questions; corpses do not), and this habit creates tactical openings the party can exploit. Only those who breach his extradimensional sanctum see his full power.

**Relationships.** Manshoon is the leader of the Zhentarim splinter cell, the employer of Urstul Floxin, Sidra Romeir, and Vevette Blackwater, a blackmailer of Masked Lords Jelenn Urmbrusk and Corylus Thann, and a clone-wizard whose response teams are deliberately the weakest in muscle because drawing attention is the one mistake he will not make twice.

## Overview

A name the party does not have yet. The hard-edged Zhentarim operatives working Waterdeep's streets answer to someone, and none of them will say who.
