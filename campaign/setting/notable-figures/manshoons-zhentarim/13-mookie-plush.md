**Mookie Plush** (Lawful evil, lightfoot halfling, he/him)

## Background

Mookie Plush is a lightfoot halfling adept who keeps the audience chamber of Manshoon's sanctum, area E10, together with Havia Quickknife (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations). The published adventure says that the two of them believe Manshoon is the rightful leader of the Zhentarim and will sacrifice themselves to see his plans succeed (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Each of them wears a teleporter ring, and both sleep in the sanctum's Zhent quarters (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He speaks Common and Halfling (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). No source records where he was born, how he trained or how he came to Manshoon `[research]`.

Mookie grew up in a lightfoot halfling hamlet on a Sword Coast trade road, where his family kept a wayside inn with eight beds and a long kitchen `[design]`. His mother kept a ledger by the door and wrote the name and hometown of every guest in it `[design]`. She taught her children that a guest is owed a greeting, a meal and an apology for any trouble that the house causes `[design]`. Mookie asks everyone he meets for the same two things, and he apologises before he does anything unpleasant, because he was raised to `[design]`.

When Mookie was fourteen, a drover who owed the inn for a week of beds tried to carry off the strongbox `[design]`. His mother told him to hold the kitchen door while his father and the hired hand dealt with the man, and he held it and said sorry to the drover through the planks `[design]`. She thanked him afterward and told him that the house comes first and the guest second, and that a host can be kind to a man while the house's business is done to him `[design]`. Mookie has treated that as one rule ever since `[design]`. A wandering monk who lodged at the inn that winter paid for his bed by teaching the children a hand-to-hand style, and Mookie was the only one who kept practicing after the monk left `[design]`.

When Mookie was nineteen, a road company opened a new crossing a day's walk away and the traffic moved to it, and within two years the inn had no guests and the family had no money `[design]`. His mother sewed five pearls from the strongbox into the doll that his younger brother slept with, so that whichever of them kept it would have the price of a start `[design]`. The family split to find work, his brother left on a drover's cart without the doll, and Mookie took the doll and the ledger `[design]`. His mother stayed on at the empty inn and died there the next winter, and the family buried her beside the well `[design]`. The road company later bought the lot and fenced it `[design]`.

For the next years Mookie kept doors for taverns and caravan camps along the coast road, and he became known as a warden who said sorry before he broke a wrist `[design]`. A Black Network broker who used one of those taverns noticed him and offered steady pay in Waterdeep `[design]`. About two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR, Manshoon's cell took a large part of the local network away from the older Zhentarim (**The Founding of Chains**) `[research]`, and the broker took Mookie with him `[design]`. At their first meeting Manshoon asked Mookie what a house needs from the person who keeps its door, Mookie answered with his mother's rule, and Manshoon gave him the audience chamber `[design]`. Mookie has felt since then that he is back in a house with one master and a clear order of guests, and he has not wanted any part of it to change `[design]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Mookie receives every visitor to the audience chamber the way his mother received guests, with a name, a hometown and an apology, and he writes the first two into the ledger afterward `[design]`. He keeps the doll, with the five pearls still inside, in his chest in the Zhent quarters (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and he has spent none of them `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Mookie wants to find his younger brother and put the doll in his hands with the pearls still sewn inside `[design]`. He knows only that his brother said he was heading for a lightfoot village on the southern coast road, so he asks every traveler for a hometown and listens for that village's name `[design]`. He also wants to set a stone over his mother's grave on the old inn's lot `[design]`. The road company's fence is locked and its keepers want a fee, so he sets aside part of every payment against the day he can pay it `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who have met him, Mookie Plush is the friendlier of the two halflings who keep Manshoon's audience chamber. He smiles easily, asks about your family and your road, and sounds sorry when something unpleasant has to happen. His warmth is real, and it has no bearing on what he does next.

He calls Manshoon the boss and says that the boss needs him, and he says it cheerfully. He asks every visitor where they are from, and he writes the answer down.

## Connections

- **Manshoon:** The master of the household, whom Mookie calls the boss. Mookie serves him completely and wears one of the teleporter rings that go only to Manshoon's most trusted servants (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations).
- **Havia Quickknife:** The other halfling adept in the audience chamber, who shares the Zhent quarters with him (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). No source records how the two came to be paired `[research]`.
- **Kaevja Cynavern:** The mage who watches the sanctum's entrance and sleeps in the same quarters (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **His younger brother:** A halfling Mookie has not seen since the inn closed, and the owner of the doll in Mookie's chest `[design]`.

## Overview

Mookie Plush is a cheerful lightfoot halfling who asks everyone he meets for a name and a hometown. He apologises easily, remembers what he is told and speaks fondly of his employer, whom he calls the boss. Few people outside that household have met him.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Mookie should inspire unexpected warmth on first encounter — he has the approachable affect of someone who thinks the situation can still be friendly — and then immediate revision, because the warmth does not change the outcome.

**Emotions.** Mookie most often feels cheerful certainty, a kind of undiscriminating goodwill toward everyone in the room regardless of what he is about to do to them, absolute devotion to Manshoon, and genuine puzzlement when opponents expect the warmth to mean something.

**Motivations.** Mookie wants to serve Manshoon completely. His warmth is not a tactic; it is simply who he is. These two things coexist without contradiction, from his perspective.

**Inspirations.** When playing Mookie, channel Bellatrix Lestrange (*Harry Potter*), Harley Quinn (*DC Comics*), and Jules Winnfield (*Pulp Fiction*).

### Voice

*A sunny, friendly halfling who genuinely likes you and will genuinely kill you.*

- **Sound:** A bright, chatty, cheerful voice that stays warm throughout the violence.
- **Sentence shape:** Friendly questions and small talk, and apologetic little asides mid-fight.
- **Word choice:** Warm and casual: "friend", "aw", "sorry about this". He calls Manshoon "the boss" fondly.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Friendly, apologetic curses ("Aw, shit, sorry, that one's gonna bruise.").
- **Personality in speech:** Cheerful certainty and goodwill. He's genuinely puzzled when opponents expect the warmth to mean mercy. His devotion to Manshoon comes out as happy loyalty.
- **Quirks:** He asks people's names and hometowns. He apologises while fighting.
- **Signature phrases:** "Aw, friend." / "Sorry about this." / "The boss needs me."
- **Never:** He never gets angry, never lies about being friendly, and never lets anyone past.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Oh, hi! Where are you all from? I love meeting people."
  - *Business:* "I wish you'd come on a different day, honestly. The boss needs me, though."
  - *Under pressure:* "Aw, shit, sorry, friend. Hold still, it's quicker."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Mookie Plush is the friendlier of the two halflings in the audience chamber — he smiles easily, seems genuinely interested in who the party is, and gives a distinct impression that he would rather this did not have to happen. To anyone who probes this affect, it does not crack because there is nothing beneath it to crack: he is genuinely warm, genuinely loyal, and genuinely willing to kill everyone in the room. Deep down, he found what he was looking for when Manshoon offered him a place, and the finding resolved him completely.

**Morale.** Mookie fights to death, like Havia, but with a quality of sad inevitability he brings to the engagement — not cruelty, not rage, just commitment. He does not retreat. He does not negotiate. He occasionally says something kind in the middle of something terrible.

**Relationships.** Mookie is paired with Havia Quickknife in E10, a halfling adept whose warm manner makes him in some ways more unsettling than a straightforwardly violent opponent, and the sort of NPC whose death feels like waste rather than victory.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, level regard for Mookie's manners in Background and Personality. The narrator treats his warmth, his brother and his mother's ledger as real and plays none of them for a joke.
