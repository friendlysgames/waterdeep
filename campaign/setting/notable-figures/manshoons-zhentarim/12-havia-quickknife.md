**Havia Quickknife** (Lawful evil, lightfoot halfling, she/her)

## Background

Havia Quickknife is a female lightfoot halfling and a martial arts adept. She and Mookie Plush believe that Manshoon is the rightful leader of the Zhentarim, and they serve him fearlessly and will sacrifice themselves to see his plans succeed (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations). Havia and Mookie keep his audience chamber, where high-ranking Zhents come when they want a hearing with him (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She wears a teleporter ring, and she holds a key to the locked doors of Kolat Towers alongside Manshoon and Manafret Cherryport (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She sleeps in the same room as Kaevja Cynavern, Vevette Blackwater, Agorn Fuoco and Mookie Plush, and her chest holds a golden comb shaped like a stylized dragon with tiny rubies for eyes, worth 250 gp (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She came to Manshoon no earlier than the founding of the Splinter, about two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR `[research]`.

Havia was born the youngest of six in a lightfoot family that kept a rope ferry at a river crossing on a Sword Coast trade road `[design]`. The family paid a Black Network post a fee to keep a guard on the landing, and as a small child she watched the guards stay where they were told until the work was done `[design]`. When she was nine, raiders from a rival crossing burned the ferry house in the night, and she was the only one of the family who came out of the fire `[design]`. A guard from the post pulled her through a window, and the one thing she carried out was a golden comb that had been her mother's `[design]`.

The guard raised her in the post's barracks and taught her the hand-to-hand style that the Network's guards drill in a yard, the same few forms repeated until nobody thinks about them `[design]`. His lesson was that a guard holds a door until it is shut, and he never let her practice a retreat `[design]`. He also told her that the Black Network was Manshoon's work, built so that people with no one could belong to something, and she took the story as plain truth `[design]`. She never learned what she would do if the post were taken away, and she has never asked herself `[design]`.

The post closed when she was in her twenties and its guards scattered, and her teacher, by then too old for the road, gave her a letter to a broker in Waterdeep `[design]`. The broker passed her to Manshoon's cell when it formed, and the cell sent her to the sanctum behind Kolat Towers and set her to keep the audience chamber `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR she holds that post, and she has not left it for longer than a day since she took it `[design]`. She does not count the three wants below as wants, because none of them competes with her post, and she keeps all three `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Havia wants to set a stone at the river crossing with her family's names cut on it, and she has the coin put aside and has not gone, because the post she holds has no day off `[design]`. She also wants to know that her teacher is alive, and each spring she sends coin and a single line, "Still at my post", to the last garrison town he named, and no answer has come in three years `[design]`. Her third want is to wear her mother's comb in her hair where people can see it, which she never does, because a bright thing in the hair is the first thing a fight takes `[design]`.

## Personality

Havia Quickknife is a small halfling woman who stands in the audience chamber with a martial artist's balance and almost never speaks. When she does, she uses two or three words, and she says them only to warn. She shifts her stance in place of an answer, and she does not negotiate, retreat or explain.

Visitors underestimate her because she is small and the room is formal. Her certainty is complete, and she has no plan for leaving the post and no wish for one.

## Overview

Havia Quickknife is a small halfling woman who stands with the balance of a trained fighter. She keeps her hands loose at her sides and speaks only in two or three words. When she has no answer, she shifts her stance.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Havia should inspire initial underestimation (she is a halfling, she is small, she is in a formal audience chamber) and then immediate correction, because she holds E10 of Manshoon's sanctum and is ready for anyone who enters it.

**Emotions.** Havia most often feels settled conviction, physical readiness she has to consciously contain, absolute certainty in her service to Manshoon, controlled anticipation in combat, and no ambivalence of any kind about the work.

**Motivations.** Havia wants to serve Manshoon completely and to neutralize any threat that enters the audience chamber. She does not want other things.

**Inspirations.** When playing Havia, channel River Tam (*Firefly*), Elektra (*Daredevil*, Netflix series), and Dolores (*Westworld*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Havia Quickknife is a small halfling woman in a formal audience chamber. To anyone who observes her stance, she is a martial artist trained to a discipline that does not leave room for hesitation or exit plans. Deep down, she arrived in the sanctum because she had nowhere else she wanted to be, and the notion of leaving has not crossed her mind since.

**Morale.** Havia attacks in a straight line and does not retreat. She and Mookie do not coordinate tactically (they simply both move toward the threat simultaneously), but they are faster than their size suggests, trained well past what most opponents expect, and entirely unwilling to stop. She fights to death.

**Relationships.** Havia is paired with Mookie Plush as one of two halfling adepts serving as Manshoon's household fanatics in E10, one of the sanctum's most physically capable non-spellcasting combatants, and a source of no useful information if somehow captured — she does not negotiate and knows nothing that would benefit the party.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a level, respectful regard for Havia's stillness in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her devotion and her losses as real and does not play her single-mindedness for a joke.
