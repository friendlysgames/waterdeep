**Vevette Blackwater** (Lawful evil, Tethyrian human, she/her)

## Background

Vevette Blackwater is a Tethyrian human swashbuckler and one of the lieutenants of Manshoon's Zhentarim. The published adventure calls her a vicious pirate before she joined the Zhentarim. It adds that she cackles in the face of danger, loves a good chase and knows the streets, alleys and rooftops of Waterdeep well (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Her mother abused her as a girl, and when she was twelve a Zhentarim agent put a blade in her hand and asked whether she wanted to take control of her life. She said yes, and she has been in the Black Network ever since (Alexandrian remix, *Faction Response Teams*, p. 55) `[research]`. The same source says that this past left her with a deep streak of cruelty and ruthlessness, which she covers with the manner of a charming bon vivant. The adventure's pirate years and the Network's recruitment fit together if she sailed as a Network hand before she joined Manshoon's cell `[research]`.

She grew up in a Tethyrian household in a small port on the Sword Coast, and she was the elder of two children `[design]`. When the agent took her away she left a younger brother behind in the house with their mother, and she has never learned what became of him `[design]`. The agent trained her with a blade and then placed her aboard Network ships that carried cargo along the coast, where she learned to board a vessel and to take its crew alive for ransom instead of killing them `[design]`. A magistrate in one port court once tried her for a killing aboard a captured ship, and her advocate showed that no witness could swear to her face `[design]`. She walked out free, and she has treated the law ever since as a tool that protects anyone who knows its words `[design]`.

Manshoon's clone began building the Splinter at Kolat Towers within the last two years, and he rose by finding officers who suited his habits (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**) `[research]`. Vevette answered his call after her ship years, and he took her on because she could bring a crew in alive, which matched his preference for prisoners over corpses `[design]`. She now commands the cell's forces against Xanathar's Guild, and the adventure counts her with Agorn Fuoco among his two most trusted followers (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She works from Kolat Towers and coordinates the strike teams from there (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations).

She carries a pass-amulet to Kolat Towers, and her teleporter ring stays with Sidra Romeir whenever she leaves the towers (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide and Organizations). Her chest holds three potions of healing (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Seffia Naelryke of the Cassalanters once approached her to open a channel between the family and the cell, and Vevette refused, suspecting a trap. She kept Seffia's visiting card, and neither side has made contact since (**Seffia Naelryke**). On Ches 1, 1492 DR she is at Kolat Towers, directing the cell's strike teams.

**Personal goals.** Vevette wants a ship of her own again, a fast sloop under a proper charter with a crew that answers to her alone, and she sets aside part of every fee toward it with a shipwright in a Sword Coast port `[design]`. She wants to know what became of her brother, and once a year she pays a courier to ask after him in the port where she was born, and the courier has always come back with no news `[design]`. She also wants a name that carries weight in a magistrate's court, with a license to trade and a respectable address, so that the law she leans on is hers and not borrowed `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people in Waterdeep, Vevette is a swashbuckler with fiery red hair and two streaks of platinum that glow on her command. She is charming and laughs easily, and she calls the hired hands who work for her "help". She talks about the law like a card up her sleeve, and she tells anyone who suspects her to prove it. She loves a chase, and she cackles when a fight turns dangerous.

Behind closed doors she is cold and cruel, and she boasts about her fieldwork to anyone who will listen. She takes no assignment she considers beneath her, and she expects to be paid what she thinks her skill is worth. She treats offers from strangers with suspicion, and she keeps a visiting card as proof that she turned one down.

## Connections

- **Manshoon:** The wizard she serves. The adventure counts her among his most trusted followers, and she coordinates his strike teams from Kolat Towers.
- **Sidra Romeir:** Manshoon's lieutenant at the towers, who keeps Vevette's teleporter ring when she leaves. Vevette's pass-amulet and ring depend on Sidra's custody.
- **Agorn Fuoco:** The other lieutenant the adventure names as one of Manshoon's most trusted followers. No source records how Vevette feels about him `[research]`.
- **Urstul Floxin:** The cell's chief field operative, who leads the thugs on the streets while Vevette leads the strike teams. No source records a rivalry between them `[research]`.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** The cell's enemy. Vevette commands the forces that fight it in the Dock Ward.
- **Seffia Naelryke:** The Cassalanter who approached her and was turned away. Vevette keeps Seffia's card, and the matter is unresolved.
- **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders):** The cell she serves regards Davil Starsong's Doom Raiders as deserters and works to dismantle them. Vevette has no tie to them, and no source records her own view `[research]`.
- **Her strike team:** Three or four thugs who fight under her.
- Her brother: A younger brother she left in their mother's house, whom she has not seen since she was twelve `[design]`.

## Overview

Vevette Blackwater is a swashbuckler with fiery red hair and two streaks of platinum that glow on her command. She is charming and laughs easily, and she knows the city's rooftops and the edges of its law well. She takes no job she considers beneath her.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Vevette should inspire reluctant respect for her audacity (she runs across Waterdeep's rooftops for the pleasure of the chase), wariness with her charm-in-public / cruelty-in-private duality, and satisfaction when someone outmaneuvers her.

**Emotions.** Vevette most often feels confident calculation, theatrical charm, predatory amusement, cold cruelty behind closed doors, operative's pride in her fieldwork, and measured disdain for anyone she considers beneath her.

**Motivations.** Vevette wants to serve Manshoon's interests while positioning herself for advancement, win the cell's war against Xanathar's Guild, and avoid imprisonment by exploiting the law.

**Inspirations.** When playing Vevette, channel Catwoman (*Batman Returns*), Cersei Lannister (*Game of Thrones*), and Irene Adler (*Sherlock Holmes*).

### Voice

*A charming, theatrical swashbuckler in public and a cold, cruel one behind closed doors.*

- **Sound:** A bright, playful, flirtatious voice in public. In private, low and cold.
- **Sentence shape:** Witty, teasing banter in public. Short, cutting sentences in private. She cites the law like a card up her sleeve.
- **Word choice:** Glamorous and legalistic: "darling", "charges", "evidence", "prove it". She calls lesser operatives "help".
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Pearls · Phrase.* Mostly flirty little curses, then a cruel gem in private: "You're not worth the blood it'd take to shut you up."
- **Personality in speech:** Theatrical charm over cold cruelty. Her pride in her fieldwork comes out as boasting. She's confident the law protects her, and says so.
- **Quirks:** She makes her hair glow for emphasis. She reminds people she's done nothing they can prove.
- **Signature phrases:** "Prove it, darling." / "I've done nothing wrong." / "Help."
- **Never:** She never admits a crime, never works for less than she thinks she's worth, and never runs without flair.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Well, aren't you lovely. Come to arrest me? You'll need a reason, darling."
  - *Business:* "I do what I'm asked to do. Who asked is a matter for my advocate."
  - *Under pressure:* "Catch me on the rooftops, if you can." (her hair flares, and she's gone)

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Vevette is a swashbuckler with fiery red hair streaked platinum that can glow in the dark on her command. Charming in public, cruel in private. To those she trusts, she carries Seffia Naelryke's visiting card, having rebuffed a Cassalanter backchannel offer she suspected was a trap. Deep down, she is counting on the law to protect her from serious harm if captured, and she knows she has done nothing to warrant arrest.

**Morale.** In a fight, Vevette flees across rooftops with acrobatic skill. If caught, she surrenders and relies on legal protection. She does not willingly reveal the cell's business unless magically compelled.

**Relationships.** Vevette is Manshoon's most trusted field lieutenant, the coordinator of strike team deployments from Kolat Towers, and a swashbuckler whose operational relationship with the Cassalanters consists of a rebuffed contact and a kept visiting card.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a light, amused regard in Personality that matches Vevette's own charm. The narrator treats her cruelty as real and does not play her for a joke.
