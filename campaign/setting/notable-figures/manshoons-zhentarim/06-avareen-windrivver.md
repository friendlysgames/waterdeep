**Avareen Windrivver** (Lawful evil, Illuskan human, she/her)

## Background

Avareen Windrivver is an Illuskan human woman in her twenties, pale and gaunt, with dark stringy hair (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She keeps the Interrogation House, a windowless house on Brindul Alley in the North Ward that serves the cell as a safe house and a field office (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations). A rack and an arcane circle fill the back room, three black flying snakes nest near the ceiling, and the room holds no captive on Ches 1, 1492 DR (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide). She and Zorbog Jyarkoth live in the front room and keep strange hours, and their neighbors have seen them come home at night with blood on their leather armor (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Avareen carries a pass-amulet to Kolat Towers (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide).

Avareen was born to an Illuskan family on the cold coast of the Sword Coast North, where her mother kept the ledgers for a salvage and chandlery house `[design]`. She copied sums from the age of eight and learned to write the same figure the same way twice `[design]`. When she was fifteen the house found its strongbox short, and the partners blamed her mother and hired a questioner from another port `[design]`. He sat with her mother in the counting room for three days, asked the same question in the same words, and never raised his voice, while Avareen listened from the stairs `[design]`. Her mother confessed on the third night and was sold into a debt contract, and she died in it four winters later `[design]`. The partner who had taken the coin kept his seat `[design]`. Avareen concluded that a calm voice repeating a question will always get an answer, and that nobody checks whether the answer is true `[design]`.

She left home at seventeen and clerked in the counting houses of the coast towns, and at about twenty she reached Waterdeep `[design]`. A broker for the Black Network's local office set her at a desk in the back room of a Dock Ward warehouse to write down what the prisoners said `[design]`. She noticed that the men who questioned by shouting came away with three stories from each prisoner, and she began asking the questions herself and checking each answer against the last `[design]`. She asked the broker for Zorbog Jyarkoth, a hired hand who held prisoners in the chair `[design]`. He held a man still when told, he never asked what was said, and he did whatever she meant when she said his name `[design]`.

About two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR, Manshoon's cell took a large part of the local network away from the older Zhentarim (**The Founding of Chains**) `[research]`. Avareen and Zorbog went with the part of it that they knew, and the cell gave them the Brindul Alley house `[design]`. She files field reports that Urstul Floxin reviews, and the cell trusts her with a pass-amulet because her reports are written the same way every time and have never been wrong `[design]` (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide; **Running the Villains**). She holds the Banite rite that Amath Sercent uses to break a captive's will in contempt, because a prisoner broken that way cannot be checked against what he said the day before `[design]`. In a note to Amath she complains about a directive to hand over a broadsheet publisher for the rite, and she closes it with "Manshoon's word is my command" (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide).

**Personal goals.** Avareen wants her mother's name cleared. The house's ledgers still list her mother as the thief, and Avareen knows which partner took the coin `[design]`. Each winter she writes to the house under another name and asks to inspect the old books, always in identical words, and she has been refused twice `[design]`. She wants the partner's own hand on a page that says what he did, and she has not decided what she will do with it `[design]`. She also wants a stone house above a harbor with windows on the water, and she has not seen the sea from a window since she was seventeen `[design]`. She has put aside most of her pay toward it and has told no one `[design]`. Her third want is smaller. Zorbog asked her once to write his name out for him, and she did, in large letters, and told him to practice, and she has said nothing about it since `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who have met her, Avareen is a thin, pale, courteous woman who is never in a hurry. She speaks in a soft voice and asks her questions in complete sentences, and she repeats a question in the same words until she has the answer. She shows no interest in her neighbors and none in the people she questions, and she says "Thank you" when a piece of work comes out as specified.

She does not raise her voice, and she does not hurt a prisoner more than the answer requires, because she does not trust an answer given in pain `[design]`. If capture seems certain she tries to destroy her pass-amulet (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide).

## Connections

- **Zorbog Jyarkoth:** Her operational partner, who lives in the house with her and does what she says the first time. She says his name as a complete instruction.
- **Urstul Floxin:** The chief field operative, who reviews the field reports that she and **Agorn Fuoco** file. No source records a rivalry or a friendship between them `[research]`.
- **Manshoon:** The master whose directives reach the house by flying snake. She obeys him exactly and shows no loyalty beyond the work.
- **Amath Sercent:** The Banite priestess at Yellowspire. Avareen despises the rite she uses, and she writes to her all the same, with the Interrogation House as her return address.
- **Agorn Fuoco:** The other lieutenant who files field reports. He trades his pass-amulet to save himself, and Avareen would destroy hers first.
- **Zhentarim response teams:** Teams sent against enemies of the cell carry orders to bring prisoners to the house, and Avareen questions what they bring (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide).
- **Her mother and the partner:** The woman who kept the ledgers and died in a debt contract, and the man who took the coin. Avareen speaks of neither `[design]`.

## Overview

Avareen Windrivver is a pale, quiet woman in her twenties who lives in a windowless house on Brindul Alley. She keeps odd hours and shows no interest in meeting her neighbors. It is said that people who visit the house do not stay long. She shares the house with a large older man named Zorbog Jyarkoth, and she is polite to anyone who knocks.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Avareen should inspire cold unease with her professional courtesy (nothing about her manner suggests this is a person's house); dread for anyone who learns what the windowless building is for; and clinical discomfort with someone who finds interrogation simply a job.

**Emotions.** Avareen most often feels professional detachment, organizational loyalty, the controlled patience of someone who is never in a hurry, measured satisfaction in work done to specification, and a complete absence of interest in the personal dimensions of her work.

**Motivations.** Avareen wants to run the Brindul Alley operation to Manshoon's specifications, maintain the pass-amulet's security, and deliver outcomes with minimum exposure.

**Inspirations.** When playing Avareen, channel Claire Underwood (*House of Cards*), Amy Dunne (*Gone Girl*, the controlled precision), and Hans Landa (*Inglourious Basterds*, the politeness before the menace).

### Voice

*A quiet, polite interrogator who treats what she does as paperwork.*

- **Sound:** A soft, pleasant, unhurried voice with a clerk's neutrality.
- **Sentence shape:** Polite, complete, patient sentences. Questions, then a pause, then the same question again.
- **Word choice:** Clinical and administrative: "session", "cooperation", "to specification". She calls the subject "you" and Zorbog by name.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Her politeness is the menace.
- **Personality in speech:** Professional detachment. She's uninterested in the personal. Satisfaction in a job done correctly shows as a small "Thank you."
- **Quirks:** She repeats a question with identical wording. She says "Zorbog" as a complete instruction.
- **Signature phrases:** "Let's try that again." / "Thank you." / "Zorbog."
- **Never:** She never raises her voice, never hurries, and never gives anyone a reason to doubt her.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Please come in. Sit. This will go more smoothly if you're comfortable."
  - *Business:* "Who told you about this house? Let's try that again. Who told you about this house?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Zorbog."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Avareen lives in a windowless house in Brindul Alley and is not there to meet her neighbors. To those who work with her, she is reliable, thorough, and personally uninvested in outcomes. Deep down, she has a pass-amulet to Kolat Towers because Manshoon has never had a reason to question her, and she has taken care that no reason develops.

**Morale.** In a fight, Avareen fights with a spy's discipline: efficient, targeted, and oriented toward extraction rather than victory. She does not sacrifice herself for the building.

**Relationships.** Avareen is the Zhentarim interrogation operative at the Brindul Alley Interrogation House, Zorbog Jyarkoth's operational partner, a pass-amulet holder for Kolat Towers, and the keeper of the rack and the arcane circle in the house's back room.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, level regard for Avareen's courtesy in Background and Personality. The narrator treats her calm as real and her grievance about her mother as real, and plays neither for a joke.
