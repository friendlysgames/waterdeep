**Kaevja Cynavern** (Lawful neutral, Mulan human, she/her)

## Background

Kaevja Cynavern is a Mulan human mage who was a Red Wizard of Thay until she saw an opportunity to study magic under Manshoon (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Before he accepted her, he forced her to remove her distinctive Thayan head tattoos and to trade her Red Wizard robes for black ones (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations). She knows Common, Draconic, Dwarvish and Elvish (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She stands watch at E3 of the sanctum behind Kolat Towers, in front of the window, and she sleeps in the same room as Vevette Blackwater, Agorn Fuoco, Havia Quickknife and Mookie Plush (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She came to Manshoon no earlier than the founding of the Splinter, about two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR `[research]`.

Kaevja was born in a Thayan market town to a Mulan family that kept the account books of a grain factor, and she was the one child in the house who could recite a ledger back after a single reading `[design]`. A Red Wizard enclave took her into its school at ten, and she spent her youth learning that the order ran on written protocols, on who had signed what and on who held the favor of whom `[design]`. By her middle twenties she wore the head tattoos of a full Red Wizard, and her mother gave her a gold amulet set with a red crystal rune on the day the enclave first took her in `[design]`. The rune is a symbol of evocation, the amulet carries no magic, and it is worth about 250 gp (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She keeps it in her chest and does not wear it, because a red stone marks a Thayan in any street of the city `[design]`.

Her own work was on carrying a message across distance and through wards, and she devised a method of relay that the enclave used for years under the name of her superior `[design]`. She learned that a Red Wizard's standing belongs to whoever signs the report, and that she could climb for thirty years and still see her best work credited above her `[design]`. She left Thay because she wanted a teacher whose ability no patron could buy, and a Zhentarim broker on the Dragon Coast told her that a wizard in Waterdeep took students on written terms `[design]`. Manshoon's terms were that she give up everything that marked her as a Red Wizard, and she weighed them and judged the price worth paying `[design]`. A healer in the cell's service lifted the tattoos from her scalp over several visits, and in strong light she can still see a faint ghost of the old pattern `[design]`.

Among the Red Wizards a wizard without the ink is a deserter and a fair target, so she keeps away from any place in Waterdeep where Thayans gather `[design]`. She relies on the *sending* spell for the sanctum's alarms (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations), and she has written out a list of protocols that gives every situation in the sanctum a first action and a named person to tell `[design]`. She serves Manshoon because he is the most capable operator she has met (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations), and he is the first master who set his terms before she began and kept them afterward `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR she holds the watch at E3 and calls it a post she chose `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Kaevja wants her mother out of Thay. Her mother still lives in the market town and believes her daughter wears the red in a northern enclave, and Kaevja writes to her twice a year through a merchant who carries letters to the Dragon Coast, with no return address `[design]`. The replies have grown shorter each year, and Kaevja is saving coin for a house in a Sword Coast town where nobody will ask whose daughter she is `[design]`. She also wants to finish her treatise on relaying a message through wards, with her own name on the first page `[design]`. She keeps the draft in a locked case and copies it out fresh each winter, so that no page is older than the one before it `[design]`.

## Personality

Kaevja Cynavern is a Mulan mage in plain black robes, with a crisp, cool voice and a hand that stays near her component pouch. She states a threat aloud as soon as she sees it, counts the exits of any room she enters, and talks about Manshoon in terms of capability, as a rational choice and never as a matter of loyalty.

Manshoon values her for the message she can send more than for the spells she casts, and she knows it. She treats any event that outruns her protocols as a fault to be fixed at once, and she swears in plain words only when one does.

## Overview

Kaevja Cynavern is a Mulan woman in plain black robes who speaks in a crisp, cool voice. She keeps a pouch of spell components within easy reach and counts the exits of any room she enters. She states what she sees aloud, in short and practical sentences.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Kaevja should inspire wariness the moment anyone sees her reach for a spell component (she is the sanctum's communications node, not its strongest fighter).

**Emotions.** Kaevja most often feels professional alertness, calculated assessment of threats and exits, calm certainty about her role in the sanctum's security architecture, and controlled urgency about any situation that develops faster than her protocols allow for.

**Motivations.** Kaevja wants to earn her continued place in Manshoon's organization, protect the sanctum against unauthorized entry, and cast the sending that calls reinforcements before anything else can happen.

**Inspirations.** When playing Kaevja, channel Elim Garak (*Deep Space Nine*), Lisbeth Salander (*The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo*), and Natasha Romanoff (*Marvel Cinematic Universe*).

### Voice

*The sanctum's alarm bell: a sharp, rational mage whose first move is always the Sending.*

- **Sound:** A crisp, fast, cool voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Clipped warnings and a Sending recited mid-fight. She states threat assessments aloud.
- **Word choice:** Protocol and threat analysis: "breach", "reinforcements", "rational". She calls Manshoon "the most capable operator available".
- **Swearing:** *Triggered · Plain · Phrase.* When a situation outruns her protocols ("Shit, that's not in the protocol.").
- **Personality in speech:** Alert and calculating. She serves Manshoon because it's rational, and says so if asked. She's urgent when events move faster than protocol.
- **Quirks:** She's reaching for her component pouch before she speaks. She counts exits aloud.
- **Signature phrases:** "Breach, E3." / "Rational choice." / "Reinforcements inbound."
- **Never:** She never lets the Sending go uncast, never hesitates, and never pretends loyalty is sentiment.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Breach." (she begins casting as she says it)
  - *Business (Sending):* "Breach at E3, intruders inside, armed, one caster. Request immediate reinforcement. Holding."
  - *Under pressure:* "Shit, that's not in the protocol. Fall back to E4."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Kaevja is the mage at E3, with her hand near her component pouch. To Manshoon, she is the sanctum's most reliable early-warning mechanism, valued more for the sending she can cast than for the spells she throws in combat. Deep down, she serves Manshoon because he is the most capable operator she has encountered, and working for the most capable operator available is, in her estimation, the rational choice until demonstrated otherwise.

**Morale.** Kaevja's first action is always the sending (to Manshoon's current location and to E8), not combat. Once the sending is away, she uses the earth elemental gem and fights behind it. If she is silenced before she can act, she contributes nothing; if she succeeds, she has made the whole defense of the sanctum significantly harder.

**Relationships.** Kaevja is Sidra Romeir's sanctum counterpart in E3, Manshoon's primary early-warning mechanism, a mage whose communications role matters more than her combat capability, and the sanctum's alarm bell.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, steady regard for her discipline in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her years in Thay, her mother and her treatise as real and plays none of them for a joke.
