**Zorbog Jyarkoth** (Neutral evil, Turami human, he/him)

## Background

Zorbog Jyarkoth is a large Turami human man, much older than Avareen Windrivver, dark-skinned and bald, with a half-missing ear and a short white beard (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He lives with Avareen in the front room of the Interrogation House, a windowless house on Brindul Alley in the North Ward that serves the cell as a safe house and a field office (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations). A rack and an arcane circle fill the back room, three black flying snakes nest near the ceiling, and the room holds no captive on Ches 1, 1492 DR (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide). The two keep strange hours, and their neighbors have seen them come home at night with blood on their leather armor (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Avareen holds the house's pass-amulet to Kolat Towers, and no source gives Zorbog one `[research]` (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide).

Zorbog was born to a Turami family on the southern shore of the Sea of Fallen Stars, where his father cut salt from the evaporation pans and sold it to the coasting boats `[design]`. He carried sacks from the age of nine, and at fourteen he was big enough to be hired as a guard on the salt caravans that went inland `[design]`. He cannot read or write, and he has never kept a stranger's name for longer than a day `[design]`.

In his twenties a caravan master told him to watch a wagon `[design]`. A band of thieves came, and Zorbog chased them half a mile down the road, and he came back to find that a second band had driven the wagon away `[design]`. The master docked him a year's wage and beat him in front of the other guards, and told him that he should have stayed with the wagon as he was told `[design]`. Zorbog took that as the whole lesson, and since then he has done what he was told, once and in the order given `[design]`. His wife died of a fever when their daughter was small, and the girl grew up with her aunt on the shore while he worked the caravan roads `[design]`. He saw his daughter last at her wedding, and she thinks of him as a guard who is away `[design]`.

He drifted north and west over the years and reached Waterdeep as hired muscle for the Black Network's local brokers, who paid by the tenday `[design]`. A broker put him in the back room of a Dock Ward warehouse to hold prisoners in the chair `[design]`. He held a man still when told, he never asked what was said, and he left the room when told to leave it `[design]`. A young clerk who wrote down what the prisoners said asked the broker for him by name, and he has done what she says ever since `[design]`. That clerk was Avareen, and she says his name as a complete instruction `[design]`.

About two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR, Manshoon's cell took a large part of the local network away from the older Zhentarim (**The Founding of Chains**) `[research]`. Zorbog went with Avareen and the part of it that she knew, because she went and because the cell paid better than the brokers did `[design]`. The cell gave them the Brindul Alley house, and Zorbog handles the parts of the work there that need a large man in the room `[design]`. Prisoners from the cell's response teams are brought to the house (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide), and he holds them in the rack while Avareen asks the questions `[design]`. He asks the prisoner nothing except a name `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Zorbog wants a boat of his own on the Sea of Fallen Stars, a small salt boat with a patched sail like the ones his father sold to `[design]`. He keeps his pay in a leather sock under a loose board in the front room and counts it on the first of every tenday, and he has been counting toward the same boat for six years `[design]`. He also wants to write his own name. He asked Avareen to write it out for him once, and she did, in large letters, and told him to practice, and he has copied it onto the floorboards with a charcoal stub every night since `[design]`. He has not told her that he can now write it without looking at the first copy `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who have met him, Zorbog is a large, bald, slow-moving old man with a white beard who speaks in a deep, mild voice. He answers exactly what he is asked and takes a figure of speech at face value. When a visitor comes in he asks the visitor's name and forgets it by the next day.

He waits for Avareen's instruction before he answers anything and does what she says the first time. At the house he holds the person Avareen is questioning and asks nothing beyond a name, and he shows no opinion about any of it.

## Connections

- **Avareen Windrivver:** His operational partner, who asked for him by name and who gives every instruction. He never questions her, and he does not retreat while she is inside the house.
- **Manshoon:** The master whose orders reach the house by flying snake. Zorbog knows them only as Avareen gives them to him `[design]`.
- **Urstul Floxin:** The chief field operative, who reviews the house's field reports. No source records any dealing between him and Zorbog `[research]`.
- **Zhentarim response teams:** Teams sent against enemies of the cell carry orders to bring prisoners to the house, and Zorbog holds what they bring (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Factions Guide).
- **His daughter:** A married woman on the Turmish shore who believes her father is a guard who is away. Zorbog has not written to her, because he cannot write `[design]`.

## Overview

Zorbog Jyarkoth is a large, bald man with a short white beard and a half-missing ear who lives in a windowless house on Brindul Alley. He shares the house with a pale, quiet woman named Avareen Windrivver, and the two keep odd hours. He answers a knock by asking the visitor's name. It is said that people who visit the house do not stay long.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Zorbog should inspire physical wariness with his size; and a specific kind of unease when you realize he takes direction from Avareen without ever asking why.

**Emotions.** Zorbog most often feels uncomplicated professional purpose, loyalty to whoever is currently paying, comfortable indifference to the details of his work, and mild curiosity about the people who come through the house.

**Motivations.** Zorbog wants to do what Avareen says, do it correctly the first time, and receive pay.

**Inspirations.** When playing Zorbog, channel Oddjob (*James Bond*), Gregor Clegane (*Game of Thrones*, reliable physical presence), and Drax (*Guardians of the Galaxy*, literal interpretation of everything).

### Voice

*Avareen's large, literal enforcer, who does what she says the first time.*

- **Sound:** A slow, deep, mild voice.
- **Sentence shape:** A few literal words, deliberately (a break from `ember-voice` speech). He answers exactly what's asked.
- **Word choice:** Simple and literal: "Yes", "Done", "Pay?". He takes figures of speech at face value.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* It isn't a thing he does.
- **Personality in speech:** Uncomplicated purpose. His mild curiosity about the people in the house comes out as blunt, harmless-sounding questions.
- **Quirks:** He waits for Avareen's instruction before answering. He asks visitors their names, then forgets them.
- **Signature phrases:** "Yes." / "Done." / "Avareen says."
- **Never:** He never questions Avareen, never hesitates, and never explains.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Hello. What's your name?"
  - *Business:* "Avareen says hold you. I hold you."
  - *Under pressure:* "Done."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Zorbog occupies the windowless house with Avareen and handles the parts of their work that require physical certainty. To those who deal with him, he takes direction from Avareen without comment, question, or visible opinion on the subject. Deep down, he does not interrogate things.

**Morale.** In a fight, Zorbog protects Avareen and the building, in that order. He does not retreat while Avareen is still inside.

**Relationships.** Zorbog is Avareen Windrivver's operational partner at the Interrogation House and the physical deterrent of the Brindul Alley operation.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> The Overview shows a large neighbor, and Zorbog is the muscle of the Interrogation House, where the cell holds and questions its prisoners. He takes direction from Avareen Windrivver alone, and he holds a prisoner still while she asks the questions.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, level regard for Zorbog's literal manner in Background and Personality. The narrator treats his boat, his daughter and his charcoal name as real and plays none of them for a joke.
