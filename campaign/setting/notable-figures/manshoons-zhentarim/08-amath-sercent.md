**Amath Sercent** (Lawful evil, Mulan human, she/her)

## Background

Amath Sercent is a Mulan human priest of Bane who keeps Yellowspire, an old tower of pale yellow brick in the Castle Ward, with four acolytes. The published adventure calls her a priest of Bane allied with Manshoon, and it says that a teleportation circle in the tower's basement gives swift travel to and from Kolat Towers (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Her acolytes are lawful evil men and women of mixed ethnicities, and all five call the tower the House of Tyranny and cover every interior surface with black prints of their right hands, which mimic the symbol of Bane (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki calls her a priestess of Bane in Waterdeep in the early 1490s DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Amath Sercent*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. She keeps the tower as a temple, and she supports Manshoon's operations where they serve Bane's interests (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**; **Bane**). She also uses a Banite rite built to break a captive's will, and she has used it to turn broadsheet publishers as part of the cell's plan to control the flow of information in the city (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Manshoon's Zhentarim**).

Amath was born into a Mulan family of caravan factors who sold cloth and salt along the trade roads between the Moonsea and the Sword Coast `[design]`. When she was eleven, raiders took the family's wagons on an open stretch of road, and her father and her older brother were killed `[design]`. The hired guards had spent the afternoon arguing over who should give the orders, and Amath, hidden beneath a wagon bed, heard them still arguing while the raiders closed in `[design]`. She holds the raiders responsible for the deaths and the guards responsible for the fact that nobody stopped them `[design]`.

A priest of Bane who rode with a Black Network escort found her the next morning, buried her father and brother in one grave and took her to his shrine `[design]`. He told her that the guards had failed because none of them held the right to command, and that Bane teaches that someone always must `[design]`. She pressed her right hand in black pitch to the shrine wall that winter, and she has made the mark ever since `[design]`. The priest trained her for eight years, and she took her vows at nineteen `[design]`. Her faith is older than any arrangement with the Zhentarim, and she feels it less as devotion than as a settled sense that she has found her place in an order `[design]`.

Her superiors sent her to Waterdeep about two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR, to keep a Banite house in the city for the cell that Manshoon was building `[design]`. The cell gave her the key to Yellowspire, an old wizard's tower that it had taken for itself `[design]`. Three black flying snakes nest in the rafters (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and the cell sends its orders between its sites by flying snake (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**). She chose her four acolytes one at a time from people who had come to Waterdeep with nothing, each after a long talk in the same chair, and she is proud of them `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR she treats the tower as a house of worship, a command post and a fallback location, and she sees no difference among the three.

Agorn Fuoco first came to Yellowspire carrying a message from Manshoon, and he returned the next evening without one `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR he is her romantic partner and visits her in the evenings (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations; **Castle Ward**, Gazetteer). She knows that he runs from a fight and agrees with whoever speaks most persuasively in the room, and she holds neither against him, though she holds both against everyone else `[design]`. She also knows that he has a mother in Mistshore and that he has found no way to bring the two of them together `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Amath wants to sit with the old priest who raised her before he dies `[design]`. He is nearly blind and lives in the shrine where she was trained, and she pays a caravan factor to carry a letter to him each month `[design]`. Twice she has been ready to leave with a caravan, and twice she has stayed, because the circle in the cellar needs her and the cell has nobody else to put in the tower `[design]`. She also wants Agorn to kneel to the Black Hand on his own decision, and she has never asked him, since he agrees with whoever speaks most persuasively and she is the most persuasive person he knows `[design]`. She is waiting for an evening on which he disagrees with her about something small `[design]`. Her third want is a stone post on the road where her father and brother died, with a black hand painted on it so that travelers will know the road is held `[design]`. She has put a purse aside for the stonecutter and has not yet found a caravan that will carry the stone `[design]`.

## Personality

To her neighbors, Amath Sercent is a well-mannered woman in common clothes who keeps an old tower in the Castle Ward and minds her own business. She speaks in courteous, complete sentences and never raises her voice. She receives guests as though she were hosting a tea, and she treats any hostility as a small breach of manners.

She does not experience her faith as devotion so much as alignment, and she treats weakness with controlled contempt. The black handprints are the only thing in the tower she mentions with warmth. She calls the tower the House of Tyranny when asked, and she says that the neighbors take it for a private home, which she prefers.

## Connections

- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** She keeps one of the cell's field offices and the teleportation circle that leads to Kolat Towers. The cell regards the Doom Raiders as deserters and will not cooperate with them.
- **Manshoon:** The wizard who leads the cell. She supports his operations where they serve Bane's interests, and her faith does not depend on him. See **Manshoon** in Villains for his plans.
- **Agorn Fuoco:** Her romantic partner, a bard and Manshoon lieutenant who visits her in the evenings. She excuses in him the flight and the eagerness to agree that she despises in others, and she wants him to choose Bane freely `[design]`.

- **The priest who raised her:** An old, nearly blind Banite who trained her in his shrine. She writes to him each month and has not seen him in years `[design]`.
- **Her neighbors:** They take the tower for a private home, and she prefers that they do.
- **Bane:** The Black Hand, her god, whom she speaks of with serene reverence.

## Overview

Amath Sercent is a Mulan priest of Bane who keeps an old tower of pale yellow brick in the Castle Ward. Outside she dresses in common clothes and keeps good manners with her neighbors, who take the tower for a private home. Inside, black handprints cover every surface, and she and her four followers wear fur-lined robes and pray there each evening.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Amath should inspire surface unease with her composed certainty; cold dread as the handprints accumulate in a visitor's field of vision; and the specific discomfort of someone who is polite, correct, and utterly committed to a god whose primary offering is absolute subjugation.

**Emotions.** Amath most often feels the absolute calm of someone who has found certainty, controlled contempt for weakness, genuine devotion to Bane that she does not experience as devotion so much as alignment, and measured courtesy toward those who have not yet committed to the wrong side.

**Motivations.** Amath wants to maintain Yellowspire as a Banite cell in Waterdeep, support Manshoon's operations where they serve Bane's interests, and convert, use, or remove people in the order that serves the hierarchy.

**Inspirations.** When playing Amath, channel Dolores Umbridge (*Harry Potter*), the Grand Inquisitor (*Star Wars Rebels*), and Claude Frollo (*The Hunchback of Notre Dame*).

### Voice

*A well-mannered Banite priestess whose courtesy is the calm of absolute certainty.*

- **Sound:** A clear, composed, courteous voice, never warm and never cold.
- **Sentence shape:** Polite, measured sentences with doctrinal weight, often framed as gentle instruction.
- **Word choice:** Hierarchy and order: "obedience", "strength", "your place". She speaks of Bane as "the Black Hand" with serene reverence. She calls outsiders "those who have not yet chosen".
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Disorder offends her.
- **Personality in speech:** Absolute calm and contempt for weakness. She's measured and courteous toward those who might still convert. Her cruelty is stated like policy.
- **Quirks:** She touches a painted handprint as she speaks. She offers a visitor a choice she has already made for them.
- **Signature phrases:** "Order is mercy." / "Choose your place." / "The Black Hand holds all things."
- **Never:** She never loses composure, never shows doubt, and never raises her voice.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Welcome to Yellowspire. Please don't be alarmed by the walls. They are simply honest."
  - *Business:* "You may serve, or you may be removed. Both serve the Black Hand, in the end."
  - *Under pressure:* "Kneel, and this ends kindly. Order is mercy."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Amath Sercent is a well-mannered Mulan woman in common clothes who lives in an old tower in the Castle Ward. To those who enter the tower, every interior surface is covered in black handprints (the symbol of Bane) and the five occupants wear fur-lined robes and are at complete peace with this. Deep down, the tower is a house of worship, a command post, and a fallback location, and she considers any distinction between these purposes academic.

**Morale.** In a fight, Amath maintains her composure, uses priestly magic precisely, and calls her acolytes to position. The tower is defensible and she knows every corner of it.

**Relationships.** Amath is the Banite priestess occupying Yellowspire (Castle Ward), the keeper of a teleportation circle providing a back-route into Kolat Towers (bypassing the pass-amulet requirement), Agorn Fuoco's romantic partner, and a Manshoon-adjacent religious operative whose devotion to Bane predates and outlasts any particular Zhentarim arrangement.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> Amath keeps a Banite cell for Manshoon's Zhentarim, and the Overview shows only a priest of Bane who keeps an old tower. The teleportation circle in her cellar leads to Kolat Towers. Few outside the cell know either fact.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a grave courtesy in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her faith and her feeling for Agorn as real and plays her calm for neither a joke nor a threat.
