**Vira Solkan** (Neutral evil, Damaran human, she/her)

## Background

Vira Solkan is a Damaran human mage in her mid-twenties `[research]`, and Manshoon's cell has planted her inside Blackstaff Tower as a junior arcanist (**Castle Ward**; **Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations). Damarans descend from the survivors of Narfell who settled around the Sea of Fallen Stars (*Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn*, 2025), and Vira's family came from that coast `[design]`. She serves Manshoon's cell only, and she has no tie to Davil Starsong's Doom Raiders `[design]`.

Vira grew up on the shore of the Sea of Fallen Stars, the daughter of a salt-barge hand who was away most of the year and of a laundress who kept the linen of a wizard's household `[design]`. The wizard boarded paying apprentices, and Vira carried water and linen through his house from the age of eight `[design]`. She learned the craft of wards by listening in the corridor outside the cellar, where the apprentices were taught to chalk the lines and renew them each week `[design]`.

At fourteen she saw an apprentice chalk the cellar's ward-line a hand's width off true, and she told the master `[design]`. He checked the line, found that she was right and said nothing to her, and within the week he dismissed her mother, because he did not want a servant's child in the house who could read his wards `[design]`. The family took a room from kin, and her mother has worked in kitchens that pay by the day ever since `[design]`. The apprentice who chalked the line wrong was promoted that spring, and Vira has held a private contempt for well-placed people who do not check their work from that day on `[design]`. She concluded that competence is safe only while nobody asks where it came from `[design]`.

No school would examine a servant's daughter without papers, so she taught herself from copied pages and took work as a copyist and ward-reader for a merchant house `[design]`. She logged the readings on its strongrooms and corrected them when they were wrong, and nobody there remembered her name for long, which she found agreeable `[design]`. A client of the house turned out to be a recruiter for Manshoon's cell, and he watched her correct a reading and asked what she would want in return for a seat in the best school in Waterdeep `[design]`. The Splinter was founded about two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR, so she joined no earlier than that `[research]`. The cell taught her what a junior arcanist would be expected to know and what she should not know, what to say at meals and how late to stay, and then it placed her inside the Tower `[design]`. She stays late because the second-floor workroom is the quietest place she has been allowed to sit, and she tells anyone who asks that she likes the quiet `[design]`.

Her mother lives in a rented room on the shore and believes that her daughter keeps ward-ledgers for a trading company in Waterdeep `[design]`. The cell pays the rent for that room, and no unusual sum passes through Vira's hands `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Vira wants to move her mother out of the rented room and into a house with its own door, and she has not decided whether the cell's paying the rent is a kindness or a leash `[design]`. She also wants to sit a proper examination before masters and be passed on her own work, because nobody has ever examined her at all `[design]`. Her third want is to hear the wizard who dismissed her mother say that the ward-line was wrong, and she has never gone looking for him `[design]`.

## Personality

Vira Solkan is a quiet junior arcanist in her first posting at Blackstaff Tower, and her colleagues remember her habits better than her face. She does her work, stays late and says little at meals, and she apologises when she is in the way. Asked about the wards, she answers a little too exactly for her rank and then corrects herself into modesty. Under the courtesy she holds a private contempt for how little the Tower checks its own work.

## Overview

Vira Solkan is a junior arcanist at Blackstaff Tower, a young Damaran woman who does her work and says little. She stays late and tells anyone who asks that she likes the quiet. She apologises for being in the way and has given no one a reason to look at her twice.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Vira should inspire unease at the realization that the threat is already inside, and cold professional respect for the patience it takes to go undetected in Blackstaff Tower.

**Emotions.** Vira most often feels the specific stillness of an operative who has been playing a role and cannot afford to slip, professional calculation, genuine ability to disappear into competence, private contempt for the Tower's complacency, and controlled anxiety that her cover will not hold.

**Motivations.** Vira wants to maintain her cover inside Blackstaff Tower, finish the work the cell placed her there to do, and extract cleanly when the time comes.

**Inspirations.** When playing Vira, channel Natasha Romanoff (*Marvel*), Elizabeth Jennings (*The Americans*, the embedded operative), and Margaery Tyrell (*Game of Thrones*, the patient infiltrator).

### Voice

*A quiet junior arcanist whose unremarkable manner is careful acting.*

- **Sound:** A soft, slightly shy voice, easily talked over.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, modest, competent answers in cover. Under pressure, a cold, clear, businesslike voice replaces it.
- **Word choice:** Junior-arcanist deference ("I'm only on the second-floor rotation"). Under pressure, operational: "extraction", "exit".
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* None in cover. A single cold "fuck" under real pressure.
- **Personality in speech:** Practised stillness. She disappears into competence. Private contempt for the Tower's complacency leaks as a too-precise answer about wards.
- **Quirks:** She apologises for being in the way. She stays late and says she likes the quiet.
- **Signature phrases:** "I'm only junior." / "I like the quiet." / "It's nothing, really."
- **Never:** She never shows expertise beyond her rank in cover, never admits to Kolat Towers, and never panics.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Oh, sorry, am I in your way? I'm only on the second-floor rotation."
  - *Business (cover):* "The wards? I wouldn't know much about them. I just log the readings."
  - *Under pressure:* "Fuck. Who told you to ask me that?"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Vira Solkan is a quiet junior arcanist in her first posting at Blackstaff Tower who does her work, stays late, and does not talk much at meals. To those who investigate her, she has no suspicious contacts, no unusual expenses, and no detectable magical connection to the Splinter's cell. Force Grey knows the cell only as the Splinter, and nothing connects her to Manshoon or to Kolat Towers. Deep down, she is counting days until she can leave the Tower.

**Morale.** In a fight, Vira does not start it and uses mage spells to cover her exit.

**Relationships.** Vira is a Manshoon plant embedded inside Blackstaff Tower as a junior arcanist.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> Vira is a plant of Manshoon's Zhentarim, the cell that outsiders call the Splinter, and she is not connected to Davil Starsong's Doom Raiders. The Tower does not know it, and the Overview shows only a junior arcanist.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, level regard for Vira's ordinariness in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her patience and her mother as real and plays neither for a joke.
