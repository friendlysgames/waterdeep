**Sidra Romeir** (Lawful evil, Calishite human, she/her)

## Background

Sidra Romeir is a Calishite human and a veteran soldier who runs the ground floor of Kolat Towers like a barracks, and Manshoon counts her as his most trusted lieutenant. She came to his service no earlier than the founding of the Splinter, about two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR `[research]`.

Sidra grew up in a Calishite family that kept a caravanserai on a trade road, a walled yard with a well, a gate and a row of rooms for drovers and factors `[design]`. Her mother taught her that a guest gets water before anyone asks his business, and her father taught her to watch a guest's hands, because a thief's face stays friendly until his hands begin to move `[design]`. Each night she hung the room keys on their pegs by the gate and counted the pegs aloud before she slept, since her father held that a house stays honest as long as someone knows where every key is `[design]`.

When she was seventeen, raiders came through the gate in the small hours, and her parents died in the yard `[design]`. The gate had been left unbarred because the hand who held the keys that night passed them on at the change of watch, and the next hand believed the gate had already been seen to `[design]`. Sidra lay behind the barred door of the cistern room with her hand over her mouth until daylight, and the raiders passed that door three times without trying it `[design]`. She has since refused to let a key, a token or a duty change hands without learning who holds it afterward, and she has been calm in a crisis ever since, because a person who stays still is sometimes the one who lives `[design]`.

A recruiting sergeant who came through the next season took the quiet innkeeper's daughter for an easy mark, and he learned otherwise within a week `[design]`. She left the ruined yard in the care of a neighboring family and joined his caravan-guard company, which worked the roads between Calimshan, Tethyr and the Sword Coast `[design]`. In about twenty-five years she rose to sergeant, and she ran the watch roster and kept the gate and strongbox keys for the company `[design]`. The company broke up when its patron went bankrupt, and she came north to Waterdeep and took work as a house guard `[design]`.

A recruiter from Manshoon's cell approached her there, and the cell took her on because the Black Network recruits openly and prizes tenacity and loyalty `[research]`. The recruiter offered her the watch of a household whose master she would meet only after a month of service `[design]`. She has never served under Davil Starsong and does not count the Doom Raiders among her own `[design]`. At the Towers she found a household with no roster, no count of who was inside and no one who knew whether a door stood open at night, so she set the watch and the routines herself in her first season `[design]`. In her first week she asked who held every teleporter ring, and Manshoon gave her the keeping of the ring system because of it `[design]`. Since then she has watched earlier lieutenants leave the Towers and not return, and she has passed each of their rings to the next holder without comment `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR she is the lieutenant who has served longest, and she runs the ground floor as she has for the whole of her time there `[research]`.

**Personal goals.** Sidra wants to rebuild her parents' caravanserai, and she wants a traveler to reach it at dusk and be given water before anyone asks a question `[design]`. The well still holds water, and each autumn she sends coin by a spice factor's agent to the neighboring family so that nobody quarries the walls for stone `[design]`. She keeps the receipts folded in her kit and has about a third of what a roof, a gate and a stable would cost `[design]`. She also wants to learn the name of the hand who passed on the keys that night, and she has asked caravan hands from that road for twenty-five years, without deciding what she would say to him `[design]`.

## Personality

Sidra Romeir is a calm Calishite veteran who receives visitors with water, a seat and a polite question about their business. She speaks in a low, relaxed voice, calls everyone guest until they give her a reason to stop, and keeps her eyes on their hands. She assumes that anyone who has passed the force field around Kolat Towers is a guest of her master, because the field turns away riffraff, and she treats such visitors with the courtesy that assumption earns.

She runs the ground floor like a barracks, with a watch roster, a count of who is inside and a firm rule about who goes past the entry. Her calm comes from long habit, and anyone who takes it for softness gets one flat, quiet sentence that corrects the mistake. She does not hurry, does not raise her voice, and does not wonder why she has outlasted the lieutenants who came before her.

## Connections

- **Manshoon:** The master of the Splinter, who rules from Kolat Towers and counts Sidra as his most trusted lieutenant. He has never tested her loyalty, because testing it would mean he does not already know the answer. See **Manshoon** in Villains for his plans.
- **The other lieutenants:** **Urstul Floxin**, **Vevette Blackwater** and **Agorn Fuoco** serve the cell as lieutenants and field leaders. Sidra holds the teleporter rings when a lieutenant leaves the Towers, and she hands a ring to the replacement when its holder does not come back.
- **Manafret Cherryport:** The halfling cook of Kolat Towers, who feeds the household while Sidra keeps the ground floor. She calls for him when a fight begins.
- **Kaevja Cynavern:** The mage who watches the entrance to the sanctum. Kaevja holds the watch there while Sidra holds the watch on the ground floor.
- **Two Tethyrian human thugs:** Two Zhentarim thugs who serve under her and share her watch. Neither has a name in any source.
- **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders):** The other Zhentarim cell, led by **Davil Starsong**. Sidra has never served under him, and at best she calls his people the old guard.
- **Her parents:** A caravanserai keeper and his wife, killed by raiders when she was seventeen. Sidra has never named them to anyone in the Towers `[design]`.
- **The hand who passed on the keys:** An unnamed man who let the gate stand unbarred that night. Sidra has never learned who he was `[design]`.

## Overview

Sidra Romeir is a Calishite veteran who staffs the ground floor of Kolat Towers. She treats unexpected visitors with professional courtesy. She offers water, watches their hands and asks their business.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Sidra should inspire wariness with her unhurried calm (she has seen worse than most visitors and it shows), respect for the way she runs Kolat Towers like a barracks, and frustration with a loyalty to Manshoon that no argument will crack.

**Emotions.** Sidra most often feels the ease of a soldier on home ground, watchful assessment of anyone who enters her space, earned pride in how the Towers run, protective possessiveness toward the teleport ring system, flat contempt for people who mistake her calm for softness, and a patience that comes from knowing where the exits are.

**Motivations.** Sidra wants to maintain Kolat Towers' security, protect the teleport ring system, and ensure that Manshoon's operations run without interruption.

**Inspirations.** When playing Sidra, channel Moneypenny (*James Bond*), Riza Hawkeye (*Fullmetal Alchemist*), and Vasquez (*Aliens*).

### Voice

*The Kolat Towers veteran: calm, courteous, and always watching your hands.*

- **Sound:** A low, relaxed, steady Calishite voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Courteous, practical questions ("Water? Your business?"), then flat statements when the courtesy ends.
- **Word choice:** Barracks plainness with hospitality: water, business, "the master's guests". She calls people "guest" until they aren't.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* One flat "shit" when something breaches the Towers.
- **Personality in speech:** At ease on home ground. She assumes intruders are guests and speaks accordingly. Her contempt for anyone who mistakes calm for softness comes out as one quiet sentence.
- **Quirks:** She offers water first. She looks at people's hands, not their faces.
- **Signature phrases:** "Water?" / "Your business, guest?" / "Hands where I can see them."
- **Never:** She never panics, never gives up the rings, and never wonders why she outlasted the others.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "The field keeps the riffraff out, so you're expected. Water? Your business, guest?"
  - *Business:* "The ring goes to whoever replaces the last one. That's how it works here."
  - *Under pressure:* "Hands where I can see them. I've done this longer than you've been alive."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Sidra is a Calishite veteran who assumes intruders are guests of Manshoon, since Kolat Towers' force field screens out riffraff. She offers water, asks their business, and watches their hands. To those she trusts, she holds the teleport rings when lieutenants leave the Towers and hands them to replacements without ceremony when the previous holder does not come back. Deep down, she has outlasted every other lieutenant Manshoon has employed. She does not wonder why.

**Morale.** In a fight, Sidra waits for the intruders to commit before she draws. Once she draws, she calls for Manafret Cherryport, picks a defensible position, and does not leave it.

**Relationships.** Sidra is Manshoon's Kolat Towers lieutenant, the keeper of the teleporter ring system, and the only person in the cell whose loyalty Manshoon has never tested, because testing it would mean he does not already know the answer.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, steady regard for her courtesy in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her calm and her losses as real and plays neither for a joke.
