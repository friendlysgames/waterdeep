**Manafret Cherryport** (Neutral evil, lightfoot halfling, he/him)

## Background

Manafret Cherryport is a lightfoot halfling mage and one of Manshoon's household, and he loves to cook (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He cooks for the household in Kolat Towers and uses his *mage hand* cantrip to cut the vegetables (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Manshoon's Zhentarim**, Organizations). He wears a teleporter ring on his right hand, and Manshoon gives such rings to his most trusted servants (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Kolat Towers**). He is one of the cell's longest-serving members, formerly of the Acquisitions division, and the cell recruited him because he can operate a teleportation circle with mechanical precision under fire (**Kolat Towers**). He joined after the Splinter was founded about two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR `[research]`.

Manafret grew up in a lightfoot halfling hamlet on a river road, in the kitchen of the inn that his grandmother kept `[design]`. He stood on a stool at her iron stove from the age of five, and he has used a stool at every stove since `[design]`. Her rule was that no traveler leaves hungry and nobody is asked his business until he has eaten, and her first words to each of them were "Have you eaten, dear?" `[design]`. At nine he found his first cantrip by reaching for a shelf that he could not reach, and the knife and the carrots followed `[design]`.

A wizard wintered at the inn and paid his board in lessons, and he found the boy useful because Manafret could chalk the lines of a circle without smudging them `[design]`. Manafret learned from him that a circle works only when the lines are drawn in the same order and with the same care every time, and he has treated a circle like a recipe ever since `[design]`. The wizard left in the spring and sent no word, and Manafret taught himself the rest from the books that the wizard left behind `[design]`. His grandmother died the autumn after he left the hamlet to find work, and he reached the inn three days after the burial `[design]`. The inn was sold to pay what it owed, and he carries her recipe book in his coat `[design]`.

He took work in the Acquisitions division, which used circles to move goods and people in a hurry, and he kept its kitchen and its circle `[design]`. On one job a raiding party reached the circle while a crew stood inside it, and Manafret finished the activation with the door being broken, because the lines had to be completed in order, and every one of the crew stepped out alive `[design]`. The cell noticed that, and it recruited him soon after the Splinter was founded `[design]`. Manshoon gave him a ring after he ran the circle for the cell through its first season without a failure `[design]`. Manshoon pays for what the kitchen asks without asking the price, eats what is put before him and has never sent a plate back `[design]`. Manafret takes that as the highest praise he can earn, and on Ches 1, 1492 DR he runs the kitchen in Kolat Towers and feeds the household `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Manafret wants one honest critique of his cooking from someone who is not afraid of him `[design]`. Nobody in the Towers tells him the truth about a dish, and he has begun to suspect that Manshoon eats whatever is put in front of him `[design]`. He also wants to hand his grandmother's recipe book to a pupil who will keep the stool and the book and stay for the whole lesson, and so far he has found none `[design]`. His third want is to see the sea, which he has never done, and he has put it off for two years because there is always a supper to cook `[design]`.

## Personality

Manafret Cherryport is a portly halfling who works at an iron stove standing on a stool, with a green hand floating above the table to cut his vegetables (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He is warm, merry and grandfatherly, calls everyone dear and asks whether they have eaten before he asks anything else. He sets an extra place for visitors who may arrive unexpectedly and offers to tell Lord Manshoon that they have come. He takes real pride in a kitchen that runs well, and he is mildly concerned when guests seem confused.

## Overview

Manafret Cherryport is a portly lightfoot halfling who cooks for a household nobody sees. He stands on a stool at his iron stove and cuts his vegetables with a floating green hand. He asks visitors whether they have eaten and sets them a place before they have said their business.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Manafret should inspire endearment with his halfling hospitality; a specific kind of unsettling confusion when he does not register that you are intruders; and the disquieting experience of being offered dinner by someone who works for a wizard who kills people.

**Emotions.** Manafret most often feels genuine domestic pride, the uncomplicated satisfaction of a well-run kitchen, pleasant curiosity about visitors who have not introduced themselves, loyal warmth toward Manshoon expressed through excellent food, and mild concern when guests seem confused.

**Motivations.** Manafret wants to cook well, keep the kitchen orderly, be useful to Manshoon, and welcome whoever the teleporter ring brings through the sanctum.

**Inspirations.** When playing Manafret, channel Bilbo Baggins (*The Hobbit*), Samwise Gamgee (*The Lord of the Rings*, domestic competence), and Hannibal Lecter (*Hannibal*, the disconcerting chef who welcomes you warmly).

### Voice

*A delightful halfling cook who welcomes intruders to supper as if they were expected guests.*

- **Sound:** A warm, merry, grandfatherly halfling voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Hospitable, rambling kitchen talk: menus, questions about appetite, fussing.
- **Word choice:** Food and hospitality: "have you eaten?", dishes by name, "Lord Manshoon". He calls everyone "dear".
- **Swearing:** *Never · Euphemist · n/a.* "Oh, burnt biscuits!"
- **Personality in speech:** Genuine domestic pride. He doesn't register intruders as threats. His loyalty to Manshoon comes out as fussing over the master's preferences. He's mildly concerned when guests seem confused.
- **Quirks:** He sets an extra place mid-conversation. He offers to let Lord Manshoon know they've arrived, which is a real threat he doesn't know he's making.
- **Signature phrases:** "Have you eaten, dear?" / "Shall I tell Lord Manshoon you've arrived?" / "Oh, burnt biscuits!"
- **Never:** He never thinks the worst of a guest, never serves a bad meal, and never understands why people are frightened.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Oh! Visitors! Have you eaten, dear? Sit, sit, there's lamb and a lovely pudding."
  - *Business:* "Shall I tell Lord Manshoon you've arrived? He does like to know."
  - *Under pressure:* "Oh, burnt biscuits, you're not guests at all, are you?"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Manafret is the Kolat Towers cook who sets an extra place at the table for visitors who may arrive unexpectedly from the sanctum. To those who enter the kitchen through the wrong door, he asks whether they have eaten, what they would prefer, and whether he should let Lord Manshoon know they have arrived. Deep down, he wears the teleporter ring because Manshoon trusts him, and visitors always seem to respond well to the food.

**Morale.** In a fight, Manafret retreats to a defensible position, calls for help through whatever means are available, and uses his halfling size to make himself a difficult target. He does not use the teleporter ring except on Manshoon's orders.

**Relationships.** Manafret is Manshoon's Kolat Towers cook and a teleporter ring holder, a cook whose threat is contextual (his ring bypasses the pass-amulet system `[research]`), and a man whose hospitality toward intruders has probably prevented more deaths than his employer would prefer.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, fond regard for the cooking in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Manafret's warmth and his loyalty to Manshoon as real and plays neither for a joke.
