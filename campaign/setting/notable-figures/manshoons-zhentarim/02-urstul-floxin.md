# Urstul Floxin

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Illuskan human assassin, lawful evil*. Stat block: **Assassin**.
> - **Affiliation:** Manshoon's Zhentarim
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers**; **Gralhund Villa** (location)

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Urstul should inspire menace with his large, heavyset physicality and thuggish confidence, frustration with his slipperiness across multiple arcs, and satisfaction when the party finally corners him.

**Emotions.** Urstul most often feels aggressive authority, blunt contempt for obstacles, cold operational focus, territorial possessiveness (he storms about Gralhund Villa like he owns it), desperate cunning when cornered, and absolute refusal to name his master.

**Motivations.** Urstul wants to recover the Stone of Golorr for Manshoon, maintain operational control of the field team, and survive encounters with the party by any means necessary, including hostage-taking.

**Inspirations.** When playing Urstul, channel Luca Brasi (*The Godfather*), Javert (*Les Misérables*), and Karl (*Die Hard*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Urstul is a large, heavyset man in his forties who treats every room as his own. To those he works with, he is Manshoon's chief field operative who runs both the Dalakhar capture attempt and the Gralhund Villa operation. Deep down, he will not divulge his master's name unless magically compelled, and he keeps a collection of black flying snakes as couriers because he trusts animals more than people.

**Morale.** In a fight, Urstul fights to win. If wounded or desperate, he kicks open the nearest door to grab a hostage. If confronted with overwhelming force, he uses surviving Zhents as cover and runs.

**Relationships.** Urstul is Manshoon's chief field operative, a temporary occupant of Gralhund Villa (where he treats Yalah as an underling), and the antagonist who drives **Fireball!** and **Gralhund Villa** before the party can identify his master.

## Overview

A large, heavyset man in his forties who carries himself as though every room belongs to him. He works for the Zhentarim and makes no effort to hide it, though he will not name who gives him orders.
