**Urstul Floxin** (Lawful evil, Illuskan human, he/him)

## Background

Urstul Floxin is an Illuskan human assassin in his forties, a large and heavyset man who speaks Common and Orc and knows thieves' cant (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He is Manshoon's chief field operative and leads the thugs who work the streets (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**). He is also the highest-ranking member of the Zhentarim contingent that lives in the Gralhund household, where Yalah Gralhund treats the contingent as her own spy network and treats him as an underling (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **House Gralhund**). The Gralhunds put up with him because his spies across the city feed them useful information (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki gives his home as Waterdeep and calls him a high-ranking Zhentarim in the early 1490s DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Urstul Floxin*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Floxin was born in a whaling village on the cold coast of the Sword Coast North, to an Illuskan family that fished in the warm months and took berths on the whalers in winter `[design]`. His father was a harpooner, and his mother kept the village's loft of carrier birds, which the captains used to send word ahead of a catch `[design]`. From the age of seven he fed the birds and ran the captains' messages down to the quay, and he came to trust a bird to deliver what it was given more than he trusted the men who wrote the notes `[design]`.

When Floxin was seventeen, his older brother, a deckhand, told a visiting reeve which of the village's captains had been running contraband `[design]`. The brother was found drowned at the quay within the tenday, and nobody in the village would say who had put him there, although several people had seen it `[design]`. Floxin signed onto a southbound ship that winter `[design]`. He drew two conclusions from what had happened, that a man who names the person behind an order is as good as dead and that people who know the answer to a question will never give it to you `[design]`.

He worked his way south to Waterdeep as a deckhand and found that the Dock Ward paid better for a big man who did not ask questions `[design]`. He killed for hire for the Black Network's local brokers, who paid on time and never asked who he was, and he rose in their books by finishing the jobs that other men left half done `[design]`. About two years before Ches 1, 1492 DR, a new set of orders began to reach the local network through a code and a chain of dead drops, with better pay and a promise that whoever gave them wanted results and had no use for oaths `[design]`. Manshoon's cell peeled a large part of the local network away from the older Zhentarim in the city at about that time (**The Founding of Chains**) `[research]`, and Floxin was soon running its street crews `[design]`. He took the work and has kept the code, the drops and anything else he knows of the chain to himself ever since `[design]`. Less than a year before Ches 1 the cell struck its alliance with House Gralhund, and Floxin took charge of the contingent that moved into the villa `[research]`.

Captain Hyustus Staget of the Dock Ward once kept a warehouse under watch in the hope of catching Floxin, whom the Watch counts as a "big fish" rumored to be behind much of the recent strife. Staget pulled the detail to strengthen street patrols and now regrets it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Open Lord's Watch**). On the night before Ches 1, 1492 DR, five thugs working for Floxin seized Renaer Neverember to learn what he knew about his father's hidden gold, and they took Floon Blagmaar in the same attack. House Gralhund paid for the operation (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Manshoon's Zhentarim**). The published adventure says the botched kidnapping will not sit well with him (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). On Ches 1 he is working from the Gralhund household's Zhentarim contingent, and his team is tracking Dalakhar, the gnome who is moving the Stone of Golorr (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**; **The Players Arrive**).

**Personal goals.** Floxin wants the name of the man who gave the order to drown his brother, which no one in the village would give him `[design]`. He asks every whaler's mate and ship's hand from the northern coast that he meets in the Dock Ward, and he has already paid for one name that proved wrong `[design]`. He also sends coin north twice a year through a trader he pays well, and his mother, who still keeps the bird loft, believes that he guards cargo for a Waterdeep merchant house `[design]`. He wants her never to learn what his work is, and he would like to bring her south to a house of her own once the work is done `[design]`. His third want is a line of black flying snakes that come to his whistle from across the city and answer to nobody else, and for that he keeps a breeding pair apart from the cell's couriers `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who have met him, Urstul Floxin is a large, heavyset man in his forties who walks into a room as though it belonged to him. He talks loudly, gives orders in short sentences and jabs a finger at whoever he is speaking to. The published adventure calls him a glorified thug with the charm and breeding of a snake, and he makes no effort to hide what he is (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

He keeps a collection of black flying snakes as couriers and talks to them more gently than he talks to people. He will not say who gives him orders, and he answers questions about his master with silence or a flat "Not your business." When he is cornered he turns cunning, and he will grab a hostage and use his own men as cover to get out alive.

## Connections

- **Manshoon:** The master Floxin answers to. Orders reach him through a code and a chain of dead drops (**Manshoon**, Villains), and he will not name the man behind them.
- **The cell's street crews:** The thugs and spies Floxin leads. Five of them seized Renaer Neverember and Floon Blagmaar the night before Ches 1, and Xanathar's Guild killed the cell's agents in the warehouse where they held the two men.
- **Yalah Gralhund:** She pays for the contingent, treats it as her spy network and treats Floxin as an underling. She does not know that Manshoon is the cell's master.
- **Orond Gralhund:** He believes that the local leaders of the Black Network, with Floxin chief among them, are simply residents of his family's villa. He knows nothing of the wizard's clone.
- **Dalakhar:** The gnome whose movements Floxin's team is tracking.
- **Renaer Neverember** and **Floon Blagmaar:** The two men his thugs seized the night before Ches 1. The failed seizure sits badly with Floxin.
- **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders):** The older cell, which despises Manshoon's cell and will not cooperate with it. Davil Starsong's people believe that Floxin leads the rival cell, and he does not.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** The enemy in the Dock Ward brawls, and the force that killed his agents at the Candle Lane warehouse.
- Hyustus Staget: The Dock Ward captain who had a detail on him and pulled it. Staget does not share what he knows with strangers.
- His brother and his mother: His brother drowned at the quay of a northern village, and Floxin still wants to learn who ordered it. His mother lives on in that village and does not know what he does `[design]`. Neither is named.

## Overview

Urstul Floxin is a large, heavyset man in his forties who carries himself as though every room belongs to him. He works for a Zhentarim crew and makes no effort to hide it, but he will not name his employer. Dock Ward talk names him as a Zhent troublemaker rumored to be behind much of the recent street fighting.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Urstul should inspire menace with his large, heavyset physicality and thuggish confidence, and frustration at how slippery he is when anyone tries to pin him down.

**Emotions.** Urstul most often feels aggressive authority, blunt contempt for obstacles, cold operational focus, territorial possessiveness (he storms about Gralhund Villa like he owns it), desperate cunning when cornered, and absolute refusal to name his master.

**Motivations.** Urstul wants to recover the Stone of Golorr for Manshoon, maintain operational control of the field team, and survive any confrontation by any means necessary, including hostage-taking.

**Inspirations.** When playing Urstul, channel Luca Brasi (*The Godfather*), Javert (*Les Misérables*), and Karl (*Die Hard*).

### Voice

*A big, blunt Zhentarim field boss who owns every room he walks into and names nobody.*

- **Sound:** A heavy, rough baritone, loud by default, and a growl when blocked.
- **Sentence shape:** Short orders and threats. He answers questions about his master with silence or "Not your business."
- **Word choice:** Street-soldier plainness: "the job", "the package", "my people". He calls enemies "friend" sarcastically.
- **Swearing:** *Punctuation · Colourful · Rant.* Aggressive, crude and constant ("Get that fucking gnome, you slack-jawed pisspots!").
- **Personality in speech:** Aggressive authority. He storms about Gralhund Villa as if he owned it and talks that way too. Cornered, he turns cunning and grabs a hostage, still never naming his master.
- **Quirks:** He talks to his flying snakes more gently than to people. He jabs a finger at whoever he's talking to.
- **Signature phrases:** "Not your business." / "Move." / "You'll never get the name."
- **Never:** He never names Manshoon unless magically compelled, never backs down in public, and never trusts a person over a snake.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Who the fuck are you? Move."
  - *Business:* "The stone comes to me. Who I give it to is not your business."
  - *Under pressure:* "Back off, or the pretty one gets a new smile. Back. Off."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Urstul is a large, heavyset man in his forties who treats every room as his own. To those he works with, he is Manshoon's chief field operative who runs the Zhentarim contingent in the Gralhund household and the hunt for Dalakhar. Deep down, he will not divulge his master's name unless magically compelled, and he keeps a collection of black flying snakes as couriers because he trusts animals more than people.

**Morale.** In a fight, Urstul fights to win. If wounded or desperate, he kicks open the nearest door to grab a hostage. If confronted with overwhelming force, he uses surviving Zhents as cover and runs.

**Relationships.** Urstul is Manshoon's chief field operative, the leader of the Zhentarim contingent in the Gralhund household (where Yalah treats him as an underling), and a man who will not name his master to anyone who asks.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, blunt regard in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Floxin's menace and his gentleness with the snakes as real, and plays neither for a joke.
