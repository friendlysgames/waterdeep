**Maxeene** (Unaligned, draft horse, she/her)

## Background

Maxeene is a grey roan draft mare with a white blaze, and she understands and speaks Common through a permanent enchantment that a Harper-sympathetic druid gave her years ago. In public she plays an ordinary horse. She is hired out by the day from a stand off Fillet Lane in the Dock Ward.

Maxeene was foaled in a carter's yard outside the city walls, and an old gelding in the next stall taught her to pull in a team `[design]`. She took her name from the carter's family, and she answers to it because they said it kindly `[design]`. She was sold into the city as a young mare and spent her first seasons hauling brewers' casks along the quays, where the loaders talked freely over her head `[design]`. She learned there that people say anything in front of a horse and stop only when she looks at them for too long `[design]`. She lost the gelding when he was sold to a farm on the inland roads before she left the yard, and she never learned which farm `[design]`.

One winter Maxeene went lame in the shafts of a dray at the edge of the Dock Ward, and a druid paid for her to rest in a dry stall for a tenday `[design]`. She lay in the straw while the druid talked to her, and she remembers it as the first time that anyone asked her what she thought `[design]`. When the lameness had healed, she accepted the enchantment and agreed to keep her ears open for the Harpers, because the quays had more conversation than the yard ever did `[design]`. She has not seen the druid since the druid went back to the woods `[design]`.

For about two years Maxeene has worked as a Harper informant. She stands with her dray at the hire stand and carries whoever pays, and she remembers where each passenger went and what each one said. She tells the Harpers what she has heard whenever one of them stops at the stand and asks, and she does not go looking for them `[design]`. She answers only when addressed and says no more than her passengers said in front of her `[design]`, because she has worked out that a horse who is known to talk gets moved on to a new owner or a new road `[design]`.

Maxeene belongs to an owner who rents her and the dray out by the day `[design]`. She has never told that owner that she speaks, and she takes care not to let a lowered voice or a dropped coin make her forget it `[design]`. She thinks of the stand as hers because of the afternoon sun on the cobbles and the pump nearby, and she is vain about her ears and her harness `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Maxeene wants to learn where the old gelding from the carter's yard was sold `[design]`. She asks the carters who come in from the inland roads whether any of them knows a farm with an old grey gelding who is fond of apples, and none of them has yet answered `[design]`. She also wants a pasture of her own to finish her years in, with clover and a pump and no hitching post `[design]`, and she keeps the idea for the day when her legs will no longer carry a dray `[design]`. Last, she wants one person to ask her opinion before patting her mane or using the slow voice that people keep for animals and foreigners, and to listen to the answer `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people on Fillet Lane, Maxeene is a placid, well-groomed hire-dray mare who waits in the shafts for her next fare. To the few who talk to her, she is warm and chatty and a little vain about her ears, and she rates a person by how that person treats a horse. She drifts from the useful to the trivial and back, and she interrupts important news to comment on the hay.

## Connections

She answers to the Harpers through **Mirt**'s network `[research]`, and the **Harpers** list her among the informants who work in plain sight, such as **Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg**, **Corvin Vayle** and **Variel Duskwhisper**, though no source records dealings between her and any of them.

## Overview

Maxeene is a grey roan draft mare with a white blaze who works a hire stand off Fillet Lane. The stand is in the Dock Ward, and she watches the street with a placid, attentive expression. She is well groomed and wears a dray harness of excellent quality.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Maxeene should inspire delight when she opines on the quality of someone's oat ration; surprise when her information is genuinely useful; and amusement when she evaluates humans with the same detached interest they usually reserve for horses.

**Emotions.** Maxeene most often feels frank assessments of everyone she encounters, specific preferences about hay quality and hitching-post location, genuine pleasure in a good conversation, mild irritation at humans who do not think horses are listening, and breezy confidence in her own opinions.

**Motivations.** Maxeene wants to stand in pleasant locations, receive good hay, talk to people worth talking to, and occasionally provide information to the Harpers in exchange for being taken seriously as an intelligence source.

**Inspirations.** When playing Maxeene, channel Tiffany Aching (*Discworld*), Miss Marple (*Agatha Christie*), and Watson (*Sherlock Holmes*, the observer everyone underestimates).

### Voice

*A large, frank draft horse with firm opinions on hay, hitching posts and the people who stand near them.*

- **Sound:** She speaks Common aloud through a permanent enchantment from a Harper-friendly druid, so anyone can talk with her, though almost nobody thinks to. A warm, placid, matronly voice, unhurried and quite sure of itself.
- **Sentence shape:** Chatty, gossipy sentences that wander from the useful to the trivial and back ("…and then the tall one said the shipment was late, and the oats outside that house are dreadful, by the way").
- **Word choice:** Horse-level vocabulary: people described by smell, footsteps, height and how they treat horses. She has no names for things she can't see or eat. She calls humans "your sort" and important people "the ones with the good carriages".
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* A horse's plainness: dung is "shit", and she says so, without malice.
- **Personality in speech:** She has breezy confidence in her own assessments and is mildly offended that people don't expect horses to listen. She enjoys being taken seriously and becomes much more helpful when she is. Her information is precise about what she saw and heard, and hopeless about what it meant.
- **Quirks:** She rates everyone by their treatment of horses before anything else, and interrupts important information to comment on the quality of the hay.
- **Signature phrases:** "Nobody thinks the horse is listening." / "Smelled of lamp oil and nerves." / "Is that an apple?"
- **Never:** She never uses human names she wasn't told, never understands politics, and never lies. She has no idea why anyone would.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Oh, one of you can talk properly. How lovely. Is that an apple?"
  - *Business:* "Three carters stopped here this morning, and they argued over the price of rope. The short one smelled of lamp oil and nerves. The oats at that yard are dreadful, by the way."
  - *Under pressure:* "I'm going now. I'm going quite fast. I'll tell you all about him later."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Maxeene is a grey roan draft mare with a white blaze, hired out by the day from a Dock Ward stand off Fillet Lane. To anyone who thinks to talk to her, she is a detailed gossip who has overheard more at Waterdeep's hitching posts than most spies accumulate in a year, because she speaks Common perfectly well and almost nobody assumes a horse is paying attention. Deep down, she enjoys the conversations because they are more interesting than the hay.

**Morale.** Maxeene bolts from violence and is difficult to retrieve. She will, however, provide detailed information about whoever threatened her.

**Relationships.** Maxeene is a Harper intelligence asset who understands and speaks Common thanks to a permanent enchantment from a Harper-sympathetic druid, and the best passive surveillance available to anyone who remembers to ask her what she heard at the hitching posts of the city.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about the hay and the vanity about her ears in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her wish for a pasture and her search for the old gelding as real and does not mock them.
