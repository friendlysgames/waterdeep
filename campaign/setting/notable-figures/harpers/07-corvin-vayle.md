**Corvin Vayle** (Chaotic neutral, Illuskan human, he/him)

## Background

Corvin Vayle is a young Illuskan human who works bars in a pair with his twin sister, Nessa. He is the quieter of the two, a touch lower in voice and slower than she is, and he looks around the room while she talks (**Staff and Hiring**). He is also a part-time courier for the Harpers, a trusted carrier who moves messages between contacts, and he is not an agent. Mirt knows him and his sister by name.

Corvin was born in Waterdeep on the same day as Nessa, into an ordinary family that neither of them names `[design]`. As a child he did the watching while Nessa did the talking, and he got into the habit of noticing who was angry or lying before she had finished her sentence `[design]`. Whenever one of them started trouble, the other finished the argument, and over the years the two built a near-wordless shorthand for working together.

At sixteen he and Nessa took their first bar work, and they have worked in pairs ever since: she takes the floor, and he handles the back and the cellar. In the cellar he learned the regulars' habits from the sounds overhead, and he learned that people say more to the man changing a keg than to the one pouring `[design]`. He can carry a full keg up a cellar stair without a sound `[design]`.

A woman who drank slowly at one of those bars asked him to carry a sealed note across the ward and paid him a silver for it `[design]`. He delivered it, and she asked again, and the errands multiplied until he understood that more than one person was handing them out `[design]`. By then Mirt's network knew his name `[design]`. Some of the errands come from friends he made over the bar, which is why the work sometimes overlaps with his social life `[design]`. He does not open the notes, he carries one at a time, and he has never asked the Harpers for rank or a pin `[design]`. Nessa asked him once what the errands were, his answer satisfied her, and she has not asked again.

The bar where the twins last worked changed owners at the end of winter, and the new owner brought his own staff `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR Corvin and Nessa take shifts where they find them and look for a post they can keep. They pay rent together in the North Ward, in a single room that they share `[design]`. Before the cellar work begins he walks the city at dawn and draws its lanes from memory on scraps of paper, and he shows the drawings to nobody `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Corvin wants a room of his own with a door that locks, because he has never slept anywhere that Nessa was not within earshot `[design]`. He wants to finish his map of the city, since he has drawn the North and Dock wards and has never walked the lanes of the others `[design]`. He also wants to stay a carrier, because the work is only good while he can decline it, and he does not want anyone to ask him to become an agent `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who know him, Corvin is the quiet Vayle, a young man with sharp cheekbones who stands a little behind his sister and watches the room while she talks. He bickers with her over every order and still serves faster beside her than most servers do alone. He taps the tray twice when he has a note for someone and otherwise gives away very little.

## Connections

- **Mirt:** The Harper who knows the Vayles by name, and whose network the couriers' messages travel through.
- **Harper contacts:** The people who may leave a message at the bar for him. No source names one.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** The Vayles should inspire amusement with their sibling dynamic (they bicker constantly but function as a single efficient unit); and useful surprise when a Harper contact leaves a message for Corvin at the bar where he works.

**Emotions.** The Vayles most often feel competitive affection for each other, casual professionalism, and Corvin's private focus when a message needs to pass.

**Motivations.** The Vayles want to work somewhere interesting enough to stay at, maintain the arrangement that lets them operate as a unit, and pass whatever Corvin needs to pass without Nessa having to know about it.

**Inspirations.** When playing the Vayles, channel Fred and George Weasley (*Harry Potter*), Tweedledee and Tweedledum (*Alice in Wonderland*, if they ran a pub with military efficiency), and Chang and Eng Bunker (the linked-pair metaphor).

### Voice

*Twin server who argues about everything and finishes his sister's work, if not her sentences.*

- **Sound:** A quick, bright voice, nearly identical to his twin's and always slightly overlapping hers. Corvin is a touch lower and slower. Nessa is louder.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, rapid back-and-forth: one says something, and the other contradicts or corrects it. Alone, each speaks in normal, efficient sentences.
- **Word choice:** Service slang and sibling insults: tables, orders, "your side", "my side". They refer to each other in the third person while standing side by side ("He's wrong." / "She's always saying that.").
- **Swearing:** *Punctuation · Colourful · Rant.* Constant, affectionate sibling swearing ("piss off", "you absolute shit"), always at each other and never at customers.
- **Personality in speech:** Their competition is affection. When one is praised, the other claims half of it. Corvin goes suddenly specific and quiet when he's passing a message.
- **Quirks:** Constant correction ("Three ales." / "Two ales and a cider."). Corvin taps the tray twice when he has a note for someone.
- **Signature phrases:** Corvin: "Your usual table's free." / Both: "Package deal."
- **Never:** Neither ever mentions the Harpers, and neither ever agrees with the other without a fight.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* Nessa: "Table for four?" Corvin: "Five, she can't count." Nessa: "Five. Piss off, Corvin."
  - *Business:* Corvin: "The gentleman by the window left this for you. Said you'd know what it's about."
  - *Under pressure:* Nessa: "Right, we're leaving." Corvin: "We are definitely leaving."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, the Vayle twins are inseparable servers who learned to work together by arguing about everything and are excellent at the job. To those who hire both, Corvin is the one who sometimes passes a folded note to a specific regular.

**Morale.** In a fight, the Vayles grab each other and run. They are not paid enough for that.

**Relationships.** Corvin and Nessa Vayle are a package of two servers for hire, Corvin is a part-time Harper courier, and the pair are Trollskull Manor server candidates detailed in the **Staff and Hiring** page of the Trollskull Manor Guide. His twin sister is **Nessa Vayle**.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Corvin is a part-time courier for the Harpers. He carries messages between contacts, and he is not an agent. Mirt knows the twins by name.
> - Nessa does not know what he carries. He will confirm "some courier work" to anyone who notices how closely he watches the room, and he names the Harpers only to someone who already has Harper connections.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about the twins' bickering in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Corvin's courier work as real and does not turn him into a spy.
