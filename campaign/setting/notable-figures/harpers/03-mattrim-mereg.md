**Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg** (Lawful good, Illuskan human, he/him)

## Background

Mattrim Mereg is an Illuskan human bard who goes by Threestrings and plays the Yawning Portal most evenings. He is a better musician than he pretends to be and far more eloquent and composed than he lets on, and his secret is that he is a Harper spy (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He spends his afternoons and evenings in the Portal, watching Zhentarim agents and other likely troublemakers (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Mattrim grew up in a fishing town on the northern Sword Coast, the only child of a net-maker and a woman who sang in the town's tavern on festival nights `[design]`. His mother taught him the lute that she owned and every song that she knew, and he learned that a room tells a singer what it wants within a verse `[design]`. She died of winter fever when he was in his teens, and the lute passed to him `[design]`. At fifteen a winch rope on a fishing boat took the ring finger of his left hand, and he restrung the lute with the three strings that his hand could still reach cleanly `[design]`. The story that he tells in taprooms is that he broke the other strings and never got around to replacing them `[design]`.

He left the town at seventeen and worked the inns of the coast road, playing for supper and a bed `[design]`. He found that nobody guards a conversation from the man in the corner with the lute, and that he could repeat most of what he had overheard the next morning `[design]`. A Harper who was passing through one of those inns heard him give an argument back from three tables away, and she sponsored him `[design]`. The wiki says that a member in good standing must sponsor a recruit and that the High Harpers must approve (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated) `[research: wiki only]`, and the adventure says that bards are especially welcome (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Mattrim carried small reports along the coast for the Harpers for some years before he came to Waterdeep `[design]`.

Mattrim settled into the Yawning Portal in 1489 to 1491 DR, the same years in which the Doom Raiders reached Waterdeep (**The Harpers**). Durnan owns the Portal, and Mattrim plays in its taproom (**Castle Ward**). He sends what he learns to **Remallia "Remi" Haventree** through Mirt's network (**Staff and Hiring**). He keeps the three-string lute and the lopsided grin because the Zhentarim agents in the taproom pay little attention to a bard who seems to be a poor one `[research]`.

Mattrim learned what the barmaid Bonnie is, and he knows her secret (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He noticed that she answered questions before the regulars had finished asking them, and he watched her for a while before he said anything `[design]`. He befriended her and wants to help her gang of five doppelgangers settle into the city (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He grew up as the quiet child who listened and played a part, and he recognized in the gang a trade that he practiced himself `[design]`. He has not yet told any Harper what Bonnie is (**Bonnie**), because he likes the gang and knows what a plain report would recommend.

**Personal goals.** Mattrim wants to set down his mother's songs `[design]`. She never wrote them out, and he carries about forty of them in his head and plays only a few in public, because three strings cannot do them justice `[design]`. He copies one at a time onto loose sheets in his own hand, with the chords that his left hand cannot play `[design]`. He also wants one evening in a hall where the room listens to the music and he does not have to watch it `[design]`. Last, he wants to see his mother's grave on the coast, which he has not visited since he left, and he has not yet found a season when he could leave the Portal for the journey `[design]`.

## Personality

To the regulars of the Yawning Portal, Mattrim is Threestrings, a lanky bard with brown hair, gaudy clothes in purple and green and a goofy, lopsided smile. He has no ring finger on his left hand and plays a lute with three strings, and he makes jokes about both.

He calls the regulars by nicknames and strangers "friend", drinks moderately and answers "How are you?" with a remark on the room. He never finishes a song if a conversation nearby is more interesting, and he keeps an eye on the taproom while he plucks. When he is honest he turns careful and slow, and he weighs each word.

## Connections

- **Remallia "Remi" Haventree:** The Harper who receives his reports. He sends them through Mirt's network (**Staff and Hiring**).
- **Mirt:** The Harper whose network carries his reports to Remi.
- **Bonnie:** The doppelganger barmaid whose nature he knows. He has not reported her to any Harper, and he wants to help her gang settle into the city.
- **Durnan:** The owner of the Yawning Portal, in whose taproom Mattrim plays.
- **Ryvarra:** A Bregan D'aerthe observer who watches the well from the balcony of the same taproom. No source records dealings between them (**Castle Ward**).

## Overview

Mattrim Mereg, called Threestrings, plays the lute most evenings in the taproom of the Yawning Portal. The lute has only three strings, and he jokes about it as he plays. He is friendly with the regulars and keeps an eye on the room while he plucks.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Threestrings should inspire warmth with his deliberate ordinariness; respect when the spy underneath the mediocre bard reveals itself; and gratitude when his two years of groundwork at the Yawning Portal proves exactly as valuable as a two-year investment should be.

**Emotions.** Threestrings most often feels casual sociability masking constant observation, quiet pride in a cover that holds, genuine affection for Bonnie and her gang, private conflict about what an honest assessment will mean for them, and the steady patience of a man who plays the long game.

**Motivations.** Threestrings wants to maintain his Yawning Portal cover indefinitely, deliver an honest assessment of Bonnie's gang to the Harpers, and protect the people he has spent two years befriending, if he can find a way.

**Inspirations.** When playing Threestrings, channel Tom Ripley (*The Talented Mr. Ripley*), Sam Malone (*Cheers*), and Elim Garak (*Deep Space Nine*).

### Voice

*An easy, forgettable tavern bard whose friendliness is real and whose attention never switches off.*

- **Sound:** A pleasant, middling tenor, relaxed and a bit hoarse from singing. When he drops the act, the voice doesn't change at all, only the content.
- **Sentence shape:** Chatty, medium-length sentences that meander, with jokes about his own playing. Underneath, he asks short, well-aimed questions.
- **Word choice:** Tavern talk: songs, regulars, who owes whom a drink. He self-deprecates about the three strings. He calls regulars by nicknames and strangers "friend".
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Pearls · Phrase.* Casual tavern swearing ("shit", "bloody", "fuck me"), always good-natured and self-directed. Once in a while a gem: "That lute's been tuned by a drunk goat with a grudge."
- **Personality in speech:** He makes himself small and likeable on purpose. His loyalty to Bonnie's gang shows in how he steers talk away from them, and with anyone who asks he talks around them rather than about them. When he's honest, he's careful and slow, and clearly weighing each word.
- **Quirks:** He plucks a string while thinking. He answers "How are you?" with a comment on the room ("Busy night. Durnan's in a mood."). He never finishes a song if a conversation nearby is more interesting.
- **Signature phrases:** "Three strings is plenty for this crowd." / "Buy a bard a drink and he'll tell you anything. Well. Something." / "Friend."
- **Never:** He never brags, never names his employers, and never says anything unkind about Bonnie.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Evening, friend. You're new. Don't request anything with a key change; I've only got the three strings and they're tired."
  - *Business:* "I've drunk with them two years. I know what they are. I'd like anyone judging them to hear the whole of it first."
  - *Under pressure:* "Shit. Keep your voice down and keep smiling. Half this room is listening and the other half's pretending not to."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Mattrim Mereg is a middling bard who plays three strings because he broke the others and has not gotten around to replacing them. He plays the Yawning Portal most evenings, drinks moderately, and is well-liked in the way that unremarkable people are well-liked. To those he trusts, he is a Harper operative who has spent two years building rapport with Bonnie's gang and knows their secret. Deep down, he has done his job too well — he likes them, and he knows what an honest report will probably recommend.

**Morale.** In a fight, Threestrings stays at the edge of it and draws his shortsword only to cover a retreat. He does not break cover for a fight he can avoid.

**Relationships.** Threestrings is a Harper agent embedded at the Yawning Portal and the only person in Waterdeep who knows Bonnie's true nature.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Mattrim is a Harper spy, and the middling bard is his cover. He watches the Zhentarim agents who drink at the Portal and sends what he learns to Remallia Haventree through Mirt's network.
> - He knows that Bonnie is a doppelganger leading a gang of five, and he has told no Harper.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about the three-string lute and the lopsided grin in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats his affection for Bonnie's gang and his mother's songs as real and does not mock them.
