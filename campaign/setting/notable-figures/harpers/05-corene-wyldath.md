**Corene Wyldath** (Neutral good, halfling, she/her)

## Background

Corene grew up in a small river town in the North, where her mother kept the ledgers for a barge company and taught her to read figures before she could read much else `[design]`. As a young clerk she noticed that the company's factor was shorting the wages of the boat crews, and she copied the books in a single night to prove it `[design]`. She gave the copy to a Harper who was traveling on one of the barges, and the Harper took it to people who removed the factor `[design]`. Corene learned from that night that a column of figures shows what its keeper is hiding, and that nobody asks a halfling bookkeeper what she is reading `[design]`.

The Harper sponsored her, and the High Harpers approved her, as the Harpers' own rule for recruits requires (**The Harpers**; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated) `[research: wiki only]`. For her first years she carried messages between Harper cells on the Sword Coast and kept the accounts of the safe houses she stayed in `[design]`. She stayed with the Harpers because she had seen what a factor with a free hand does to a crew, and she believes that someone has to read the ledgers of the powerful `[design]`. Her superiors chose her for deep cover in Waterdeep because she passes as a ledger clerk and keeps her counsel without effort `[design]`.

Four months ago Corene took a room and a name and began work in the Dock Ward as Halla Ironstave, a warehouse accounts runner in the Dock Ward operation of Xanathar's Guild (**The Harpers**; **Dock Ward**). She carries tallies between yards, which lets her see what moves through the ward and who signs for it `[design]`. Every day from 16:00 to 16:30 she sits on the west bench by the well in the Trades Ward plaza, and that is where her contacts find her (**Trades Ward**). She never sits with her back to a door.

Corene writes to her mother, who still lives in the river town, at the turn of each season through a Harper courier `[design]`. The letters say that she keeps accounts in a city and that the weather is wet, and her mother does not know what else she does `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR Corene's reports are still accurate, and she has started asking questions about the Harpers (**Trades Ward**).

**Personal goals.** Corene wants to spend one full season at home without a cover name `[design]`. She has not lived under her own name in one place for six years, and she means to winter in her mother's kitchen and do the household accounts for nothing `[design]`. She also wants to buy back the barge company's old wharf office, which was sold when the company folded, and to hang her mother's name over its door `[design]`. She has saved most of what she has been paid toward it and has told no one `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who see her, Corene is Halla Ironstave, a composed halfling who runs accounts, keeps her words few and pays precise attention to whatever is in front of her. She never sits with her back to a door, and she gives the impression of someone watching for something without ever looking like she is. To those who pay attention, something has shifted: her reports are still accurate, but she is asking different questions.

## Connections

Corene belongs to the Waterdeep Harpers, whom **Mirt** and **Remallia "Remi" Haventree** lead, and she works inside the Dock Ward operation of **Xanathar's Guild**.

## Overview

Corene Wyldath is a halfling with a composed manner, precise attention and few words. She works as a warehouse accounts runner in the Dock Ward. Every afternoon she sits on a bench by a Trades Ward well. She gives the impression of someone watching for something without ever looking like she is.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Corene should inspire respect for the courage her deep-cover work required; growing dread at the subtle wrongness in her recent reports; and genuine sympathy for a woman who may not know she is compromised.

**Emotions.** Corene most often feels methodical field-operative focus, a growing gap between what she reports and what she intended to say, genuine loyalty to the Harpers that the intellect devourer is slowly erasing, and the particular exhaustion that looks like calm.

**Motivations.** Corene wants to maintain her cover, extract meaningful intelligence, and survive. The intellect devourer's goals diverge: map Harper operations, identify the network, and avoid detection.

**Inspirations.** When playing Corene, channel Offred (*The Handmaid's Tale*), Cassian Andor (*Andor*), and Natasha Romanoff (*Marvel*, double-agent aspect).

### Voice

*A composed, economical field agent whose reports are perfect and whose questions have started to be slightly wrong.*

- **Sound:** A low, even halfling voice, quiet enough to lean in for. She shows no emotion, and the calm has an exhausted edge.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, complete reporting sentences: fact, fact, assessment. She asks few questions. Lately, the ones she does ask are about Harper operations rather than the target.
- **Word choice:** Field-report precise: times, streets, counts. She uses Harper cant correctly and a little too often. She calls the people she meets by role ("the contact") before she uses their names.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Never. She's too controlled. When the real Corene surfaces, the first thing out might be a single raw "fuck" before she snaps back.
- **Personality in speech:** Her loyalty sounds perfect. The intellect devourer shows as a gap: her curiosity drifts to who, where and how many Harpers there are, and she deflects personal questions a beat too smoothly. Now and then, briefly, the real Corene surfaces as a hesitation, an unfinished sentence, a question she seems to regret.
- **Quirks:** She stops mid-sentence and resumes as if nothing happened. She repeats a report back word for word when asked to confirm it. She never sits with her back to a door.
- **Signature phrases:** "Confirmed." / "Who else is on this?" / "I'm fine. Next question."
- **Never:** She never jokes, never volunteers feelings, and never admits a gap in her memory.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You're the contact. Good. We have until half past four."
  - *Business:* "Two shipments this tenday, both through the same yard. Who's reading this? I should know."
  - *Under pressure:* "I don't — that isn't — I'm fine. Ask me again. Ask me again slowly."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Corene Wyldath is a reliable Harper informant embedded in Xanathar's Dock Ward operation for four months under the cover name Halla Ironstave, sending good intelligence. To those who pay attention, something has shifted; her reports are still accurate, but she is asking different questions. She carries Nihiloor's Occupying Devourer, a custom variant that keeps her mind intact, so she can be freed if the devourer is forced out. Deep down, Corene is still in there, aware and unable to act. The devourer is careful not to leave gaps large enough to trigger an alert.

**Morale.** In a fight, Corene fights competently and tactically — the intellect devourer does not want to draw attention through reckless behavior.

**Relationships.** Corene is a Harper deep-cover agent embedded in the Dock Ward operation of Xanathar's Guild, and the most dangerous kind of asset: one who cannot confirm her own loyalty.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - One of **Nihiloor**'s custom Occupying Devourers rides Corene. It keeps her mind intact, so she is aware and cannot act or speak, and she can be freed if the devourer is forced out.
> - She is one of three Harpers whom Nihiloor's devourers have taken.
> - The devourer wants the Harper network: who the Harpers are, where they meet and how many there are. It keeps her reports accurate so that no one raises an alert.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a restrained register in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Corene's courage and her predicament as real and does not make her an object of pity.
