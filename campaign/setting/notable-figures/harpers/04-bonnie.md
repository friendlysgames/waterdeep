**Bonnie** (Neutral, doppelganger, she/her)

## Background

Bonnie is a doppelganger who leads a gang of five doppelgangers, and in Waterdeep she wears the form of a Tethyrian human barmaid (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She works for **Durnan** at the Yawning Portal to help the gang make ends meet (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki says that she led the gang in Waterdeep in 1491 DR and that a year later she had become the barmaid at the Portal (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bonnie*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Bonnie was raised on the roads of the Sword Coast by an older doppelganger who taught her to read the surface of a stranger's thoughts `[design]`. The older woman also taught her to hold one face through a long conversation and to keep one name for other people and another for herself `[design]`. They lived on odd jobs, and Bonnie found that she liked serving tables at roadhouses, where a Tethyrian barmaid's face earned tips and no questions `[design]`.

Bonnie lost the older woman one winter on the road, when she was too tired to hold a shape, and Bonnie buried her as she really looked `[design]`. It was the only time that Bonnie had seen that face for longer than a moment, and she has not been able to call it to mind clearly since `[design]`. After the funeral Bonnie kept the other doppelgangers who had drifted to the two of them over the years, because she knew where the next meal was coming from `[design]`.

Bonnie was run out of a town with the gang when a neighbor saw one of them change faces at a window `[design]`. She decided that a city with too many faces to count was safer than another small town, and she led the gang north to Waterdeep `[design]`. She reached Waterdeep with the gang early in 1491 DR `[research]`, and she kept them fed on piecework until she found a steadier job for herself `[design]`. She chose the Yawning Portal because strangers pass through it every night and nobody there asks where a barmaid's people live `[design]`. Durnan asked her whether she could carry six mugs, watched her do it and hired her, and he has asked her nothing about her past since `[design]`. He knows nothing of what she is.

**Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg**, the Harper bard who plays at the Portal, knows her secret and wants to help the gang settle into the city (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bonnie*, 5e). Bonnie lets him keep a stool at the end of the bar, and she refills his cup when his tune goes wrong `[design]`. Nothing in the campaign files shows that he has reported her to the Harpers `[research]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Bonnie pours at the Portal most evenings, greets regulars with their usual orders and reads the surface thoughts of anyone who looks at her twice. She never talks about what she is in the main room.

**Personal goals.** Bonnie wants a drawing of her teacher that she would recognize `[design]`. She keeps a small book with her, and each winter on the night the older woman died she sits down and tries to draw that face from memory, and it has never come out the same twice `[design]`. She also wants one conversation that surprises her `[design]`. Her ability tells her what a patron will say before he says it, so she has not been caught off guard by a punchline in years, and she goes back to the table of anyone who tells a story badly `[design]`. The only person who surprises her regularly is Mattrim, because his tune goes wrong in ways that he does not know in advance `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people at the Yawning Portal, Bonnie is the barmaid who remembers your order, laughs at the right moments and asks about your day. She feeds your own words back to you so that you feel like the most interesting person at the table, and she is quick with a joke.

She shares tavern gossip freely, and nothing she says is untrue, though she decides what she tells. In private her voice goes flatter and drier, and she turns cool and direct when anything threatens the gang.

## Connections

- **Durnan:** The owner of the Yawning Portal and her employer. He hired her and has never asked about her past, and he knows nothing of what she is `[design]`.
- **Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg:** The Harper bard who knows her secret and has befriended the gang. He has not reported her to the Harpers `[research]`.
- **The gang:** The four other doppelgangers she leads. Bonnie wants all four fed and employed, and the page names none of them.
- **Her teacher:** The older doppelganger who raised her and died on the road. She is nameless, and Bonnie cannot recall her face `[design]`.
- **The Harpers:** Bonnie is not a member and takes no orders from the order. Mattrim is her only tie to it `[research]`.

## Overview

Bonnie is the most reliable barmaid at the Yawning Portal and is known for remembering every order. She makes each patron feel like the most interesting person in the room. She has worked at the Portal long enough to know everyone who drinks there regularly.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Bonnie should inspire discomfort at never being sure what she actually looks like; sympathy when you understand her gang arrived in Waterdeep looking for work and found it; and unexpected warmth when she turns out to be funny about the whole situation.

**Emotions.** Bonnie most often feels practical calculation, dry amusement at the situations her nature creates, genuine protectiveness of her gang, careful evaluation of everyone who looks at her twice, and cautious warmth toward people who treat her as a person rather than a thing.

**Motivations.** Bonnie wants to keep the gang fed and employed, maintain the barmaid cover Durnan depends on, and hold a city that has finally started to work.

**Inspirations.** When playing Bonnie, channel Mystique (*X-Men*, pragmatic adaptation), Odo (*Deep Space Nine*), and Varys (*Game of Thrones*).

### Voice

*A bright, quick barmaid with a dry sense of humor about being whatever shape the moment needs.*

- **Sound:** A warm, easy tavern voice that laughs readily. In private, with the mask off, it goes flatter and drier.
- **Sentence shape:** Quick, friendly, medium-length sentences as the barmaid, with lots of questions about the listener. In private she's economical and practical, and has no time for speeches.
- **Word choice:** Tavern-warm in public: "love", "dear", your usual order. In private: plain, businesslike, with dark jokes about faces and names.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Casual in private, dry and deadpan ("Well, shit."). Never in front of patrons.
- **Personality in speech:** She makes every patron feel interesting by feeding their own words back to them. She is protective of the gang, and any threat to them makes her cool and direct. Her humor about her own nature is how she tests people: if they laugh, they're safe.
- **Quirks:** She remembers orders and uses them as greetings. She sometimes answers a question the other person hasn't asked yet (Read Thoughts), then covers with a laugh. She never uses the same name for herself twice in private.
- **Signature phrases:** "The usual, love?" / "Faces are just work clothes." / "We're here to work, not to cause trouble."
- **Never:** She never threatens first, never begs, and never talks about what she is in the Portal's main room.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Dark ale and the stew, wasn't it? Sit down before Durnan sees you standing about."
  - *Business:* "Five of us, all working, rent paid. We'd like it to stay that way. So what do you want from us?"
  - *Under pressure:* "I know what you're thinking. Literally. So let's skip to the part where you tell me what you want."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, "Bonnie" is the Yawning Portal barmaid who remembers your order, laughs at the right moments, and has the particular skill of making every patron feel like the most interesting person in the room. To those who know what she is, she is the leader of a five-doppelganger operation who found Waterdeep worth staying for, and who will not have that disrupted by a faction's curiosity. Deep down, she reads people professionally and personally, and she has never met anyone she could entirely stop reading — including herself.

**Morale.** In a fight, Bonnie uses her Read Thoughts ability before she draws a weapon, targets the most dangerous opponent first, and fights with the tactical intelligence of someone who has survived by understanding people. She does not fight unless flight is impossible.

**Relationships.** Bonnie is the leader of a doppelganger gang operating at the Yawning Portal, Durnan's most reliable barmaid (he knows nothing), and a person whose nature Threestrings knows and has not reported.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **What she is:** Bonnie is a doppelganger, and the Tethyrian human barmaid is the form she wears at the Portal. Her gang of five are doppelgangers too.
> - **Who knows:** Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg knows her secret and has not reported it. Durnan knows nothing. No source settles whether anyone else knows, and this page names no other knower.
> - **What she does:** She reads surface thoughts at will and never talks about what she is in the Portal's main room.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a warm, dry humor in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her care for the gang as real, and the Overview gives no hint of what she is.
