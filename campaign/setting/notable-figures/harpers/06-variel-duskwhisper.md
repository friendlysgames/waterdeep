**Variel Duskwhisper** (Neutral good, wood elf, he/him)

## Background

Variel Duskwhisper is a wood elf bard who has carried Harper messages for eight years `[research]`. He is an information courier and not a field agent, a messenger who travels as a musician and hides each report in the shape of a story that he tells during a set (**The Harpers**).

Variel grew up in a wood elf community in a forest that no longer exists `[design]`. The piece that he plays when someone asks to hear him is modal and winding and was composed for that forest, and his mother sang it at the evening fire while he was a boy `[design]`. He learned the tunes by ear before he learned to read, and he can still repeat a long song after one hearing `[design]`. When the forest was cleared, his family scattered among other woods and other kin, his mother died in those years, and he took to the road with her lute `[design]`. The lute is old and has been repaired in several places with visible care, most of it by Variel himself `[design]`.

He spent his first years on the road playing for his keep in roadside inns, and he learned to listen to a whole room while his hands did the work `[design]`. Eight years ago a Harper who had heard him play asked whether he could carry a long report whole and unwritten, and Variel repeated the song that she tested him with to the last verse `[design]`. He took the work because the Harpers keep what would otherwise be lost, and the songs of his forest now survive only in his memory `[design]`.

Since then he has worked a slow circuit of the same establishments, and he can commit to about six nights out of every ten without saying in advance which six. On Ches 1, 1492 DR he is in Waterdeep for two months, waiting for a contact to arrive from Baldur's Gate, and Mirt knows that he is there. He would like steady musician's work in the North Ward, because it would give him a reason to be seen there regularly.

**Personal goals.** Variel wants to learn the last verse of his forest's song `[design]`. His mother died before she taught it to him, and he asks every wood elf he meets, in every town, whether they know how the tune ends `[design]`. He also wants a pupil, one patient listener to whom he can hand the songs whole so that they outlast him, because he has played to many rooms and has taught nobody `[design]`. Last, he wants a luthier he trusts to rebuild his mother's lute properly, and he puts a few coins aside for it from each tenday's pay `[design]`, though he has not yet found a craftsman whom he would let touch it `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who see him, Variel is a quiet, long-limbed wood elf with an old lute who plays folk ballads on a slow circuit of the same inns. He drinks one drink slowly, answers in his own time and sings far more than he talks. He says that he plays well enough to fill a room and no better, and he is open about the fact that his schedule is irregular.

## Connections

Variel answers to **Mirt**, whose network accounts for where he is, and he is one of the Harpers who lives under a performer's cover, like **Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg**. The contact from Baldur's Gate whom he is waiting for is named nowhere.

## Overview

Variel Duskwhisper is a quiet wood elf bard who plays folk ballads at the same inns on a slow circuit. He nurses one drink through an evening and sings far more than he talks. He is on the road six nights in ten and cannot say which six in advance.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Variel should inspire warmth with his unhurried folk musician manner; trust when his intelligence turns out to be precise; and pleasant surprise when a man who plays three-hour sets turns out to have been watching the entire time.

**Emotions.** Variel most often feels meditative patience, the quiet satisfaction of work that has proven sufficient over eight years, genuine pleasure in a good performance, mild boredom that reads as serenity, and professional satisfaction when the information moves correctly.

**Motivations.** Variel wants to pass Harper-network intelligence without drawing attention, maintain the touring musician cover that lets him move freely, and play sets good enough that no one remembers them as anything else.

**Inspirations.** When playing Variel, channel Legolas (*The Lord of the Rings*, the observer who says little and sees everything), Varys (*Game of Thrones*, the network man), and Bard (*The Hobbit*, the purposeful practitioner).

### Voice

*A slow-talking folk musician who says little, means all of it, and hides his reports inside his stories.*

- **Sound:** A soft, unhurried wood-elf voice, warm and slightly husky. He sings far more than he speaks.
- **Sentence shape:** Few words, long pauses. When he tells a story it runs long and even, in the cadence of a ballad; offstage his answers are a sentence or two.
- **Word choice:** Countryside and road imagery: weather, rivers, the next town. He names songs and places, never people. He calls listeners "friend" or nothing at all.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Never. He's too unhurried to bother.
- **Personality in speech:** Patience is his whole manner. He answers in his own time, and nothing hurries him. His professionalism shows in what he leaves out. When information needs passing, it arrives inside a tale ("There's a song they sing in Rassalantar about a miller who…"), and he never says it plainly.
- **Quirks:** He sips his one drink between sentences. He tunes the same string while he thinks. He ends conversations by starting to play.
- **Signature phrases:** "There's a song about that." / "Hm." / "The road's long. So is the evening."
- **Never:** He never rushes, never gossips about named people outside a story, and never raises his voice.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Evening. Pull up a chair if you like. I'll be playing a while."
  - *Business:* "Listen to the third song tonight. The one about the ferryman. Listen to what he carries."
  - *Under pressure:* "Hm. Time I was on the road, I think."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Variel Duskwhisper is a wandering elf bard who plays folk ballads at the same establishments on a slow circuit and drinks his one drink slowly. To those who know where to listen, he tells one story per set that is also an intelligence report, delivered in the structure of the tale and audible only to someone who knows what they are hearing. Deep down, he has been doing this for eight years and finds the work sufficient.

**Morale.** In a fight, Variel relies on Uncanny Dodge to protect himself and exits. He does not put the courier network at risk for a fight. `[research]`

**Relationships.** Variel is a Harper courier operating under touring musician cover, a bard looking for steady house-bard work in the North Ward whose playing is honest, a member of the Harper intelligence relay who passes one piece of network intelligence per tenday, and a man who has watched the same taverns long enough to notice when something changes.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Variel is a Harper courier. His irregular schedule is a courier route, and the story in each of his sets carries a report.
> - He admits that he is a Harper contact to anyone who is already known to the Harpers. For anyone else, a DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check shows that his schedule is structured and not random.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about the one drink and the slow circuit in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats the lost forest as real and does not mock it.
