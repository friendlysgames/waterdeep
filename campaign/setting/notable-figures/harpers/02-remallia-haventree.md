**Remallia "Remi" Haventree** (Chaotic good, sun elf, she/her)

## Background

Remi is a sun elf noblewoman and the lady of House Ulbrinter (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She lives in Ulbrinter Villa on Delzorin Street in the North Ward, which was the home of her husband, Arthagast, before it was hers (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Remallia Haventree*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. The wiki calls her proficient in divination magic (same page, citing the Adventurers League adventure *Uprising*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. She learned it as a young woman from a tutor in her family's house, and there she learned to watch whom a guest speaks to before she listens to what the guest says `[design]`.

Remi met Arthagast at a dinner in Waterdeep where each of them had come to speak to someone else, and they spent the evening talking to each other `[design]`. They had two children, both half-elves, a son named Arthius and a daughter named Serenore (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Arthius is studying music in Silverymoon, and Serenore lives on the Moonshae island of Alaron with her husband and daughter (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Remi owns a large oil painting of the family that was made before Arthagast died (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Remallia Haventree*, citing *Uprising*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Remi lost Arthagast to assassins who destroyed his remains, and she became an active force for good in the city afterward (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The loss came some years ago, because both children are grown and living elsewhere `[research]`. She had no grave to stand beside, so she hung the family painting in the long room on the ground floor of the villa, where Arthagast used to receive guests, and she has not moved his chair `[design]`. The wards on the villa were lighter on the night he died, and the servants' gate was not watched `[design]`. She raised the wards that stand today within the year, and she has asked every new guest who vouches for them since then `[design]`.

At some point Remi joined the Harpers (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Remallia Haventree*, citing *The Rise of Tiamat*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`, and the adventure calls her a high-ranking member (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki says that she helped call together the Council of Waterdeep against the Cult of the Dragon in the 1480s DR and sat on it as the Harpers' representative (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Remallia Haventree*, citing *The Rise of Tiamat*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. Today she is the de facto leader of the Harpers in Waterdeep beside **Mirt**, and she runs the cell's intelligence from the villa (**The Harpers**). She holds secret Harper meetings there and sends messages to spies across the city by a silver raven *figurine of wondrous power* (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She keeps a handful of loyal servants and spies (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and her salons are famous for their guest lists (**North Ward**). She hides her Harper role from outsiders behind her title and her salons, and few in Waterdeep know her as a Harper (**The Harpers**).

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Remi is at Ulbrinter Villa. She knows that the Black Network has split and that the Splinter acts on Harper information, and she cannot name its leader (**The Harpers**) `[research]`. Neither of her children lives in the city, and she writes to both of them by letter `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Remi wants to hear Arthius play in the long room at the villa `[design]`. She has heard him play only once since he left for Silverymoon, and she has never asked him to come home, because she is proud that he went `[design]`. She also wants to see her granddaughter on Alaron before the girl is grown, and she has put off the voyage for three years because she does not like to leave the villa's wards untended `[design]`. Last, she wants a stone for Arthagast in the villa's garden, since he has no grave, and she has not had it cut because she cannot decide what to carve on it `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people in Waterdeep, Remi is the lady of House Ulbrinter, a sun elf noblewoman and a gracious hostess whose salons are famous for their guest lists. She remembers details about everyone's life and asks after them by name. She asks a great many questions that sound like courtesies, pours the tea herself, and speaks in a clear voice that does not rise.

Under the warmth she is precise and guarded, and she weighs every new operative before she gives them real work. The wiki says she lets others speak first and says her own piece afterward (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Remallia Haventree*, citing the Adventurers League adventure *Unsafe Harborage*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. She is harder to read than Mirt, and the Harpers count her as the more reliable of the two (**The Harpers**). Her grief for Arthagast shows only in private, and when someone mentions him in company she thanks them and changes the subject.

## Connections

- **Mirt:** Her colleague and equal in the Waterdeep Harpers. Their working styles complement each other, and Mirt is the one whom new members meet first, so Remi stays out of sight.
- **Arthagast Ulbrinter:** Her late husband. Assassins killed him and destroyed his remains, and she has no grave for him and no name for the killers.
- **Arthius and Serenore:** Her son, who studies music in Silverymoon, and her daughter, who lives on Alaron with her husband and daughter. Remi is proud that both live their own lives and misses them `[design]`.
- **Her household:** A handful of loyal servants and spies who keep the villa and carry her messages.
- **Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg:** The Harper at the Yawning Portal whose reports reach her through Mirt's network.
- **Renaer Neverember:** A Harper-adjacent ally of Mirt and Remi.
- **The high priest of Gond:** A personal acquaintance, whom Remi can ask about the nimblewrights of the House of Inspired Hands.
- **The Splinter:** The Black Network cell that acts on Harper information. Remi cannot name its leader, and the Harpers do not know whom it serves.

## Overview

Remallia Haventree, called Remi by her friends, is the sun elf lady of House Ulbrinter in the North Ward. She hosts salons at her villa on Delzorin Street that are famous for their guest lists. She is a gracious hostess who remembers details about everyone's life, and her servants and wards keep the villa safe.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Remi should inspire trust with her warm attentiveness, admiration for the strength she rebuilt after her husband's murder, and curiosity about the intelligence network she runs from a villa full of loyal servants and protective wards.

**Emotions.** Remi most often feels gracious warmth, sharp analytical focus, held grief for Arthagast that surfaces only in private, maternal pride in her children's independence, measured caution toward new operatives, and calm authority that never needs to raise its voice.

**Motivations.** Remi wants to guide the Harpers effectively in Waterdeep, protect her household and her network from the threats that killed her husband, and determine whether the party can be trusted with real responsibility.

**Inspirations.** When playing Remi, channel Violet Crawley (*Downton Abbey*), Minerva McGonagall (*Harry Potter*), and Mrs. S (*Orphan Black*).

### Voice

*A gracious, unhurried hostess whose warmth never slips and whose questions never stop.*

- **Sound:** A clear, cultured elven voice, measured and never raised. When she's displeased she becomes quieter and more precise.
- **Sentence shape:** Complete, well-formed sentences with the occasional subordinate clause. She asks a great many questions, framed as courtesies.
- **Word choice:** Refined but never cold. She remembers and uses details about the listener's life (a sister's name, an old injury). She addresses the party by name as soon as she has heard it once, and calls her agents "my dears" only in private.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Never. A single "damn" from Remi would stop a room.
- **Personality in speech:** Warmth is her method. Every kindness is also an assessment. Grief for Arthagast never enters her speech in front of others; if it's mentioned, she thanks the speaker and changes the subject gracefully. Authority comes through in how she makes requests: she never commands, and nobody refuses.
- **Quirks:** She pours the tea herself. She pauses before answering a question she has decided not to answer, then answers a different one. She touches the silver raven figurine when she's thinking of her network.
- **Signature phrases:** "Tell me about yourself; I like to know whom I'm feeding." / "How is your [person they mentioned]?" / "We'll speak again when you've had time to think."
- **Never:** She never swears, never raises her voice, and never admits fear in front of an operative.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Do come in and sit down; the tea is fresh. Tell me about yourself while it cools, if you would."
  - *Business:* "I'd like to ask a favor of you, and I'd like you to say now if you would rather not. I'm telling you that so you know I'll remember."
  - *Under pressure:* "The wards will hold for a few minutes yet. Please take the children's door and go now, and don't argue with me."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Remi is the lady of House Ulbrinter, a gracious hostess who remembers details about everyone's life. To those she trusts, she runs the Harper operation in Waterdeep from a warded villa, using a silver raven figurine to send messages to her spies. Deep down, assassins killed her husband and destroyed his remains, and every protective measure she takes serves the memory of what it cost her to be careless once.

**Morale.** In a fight, Remi would ward the doors and send her agents to handle it. She fights in person only if the wards fail, and by then she has already cast three spells the attackers have not noticed yet.

**Relationships.** Remi is the widow of Arthagast Ulbrinter, the mother of Arthius and Serenore (both grown and living elsewhere), the de facto Harper leader in Waterdeep, and a colleague of Mirt whose operational styles complement each other.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Remi's Harper role is hidden from outsiders. She presents as the gracious lady of House Ulbrinter, and no outside contact names her a Harper.
> - She decides whether a party can be trusted with real responsibility, and she reveals herself as a Harper only when she chooses to.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about the hostess's courtesies in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her grief for Arthagast as real and does not mock it.
