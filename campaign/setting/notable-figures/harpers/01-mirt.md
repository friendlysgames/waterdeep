**Mirt** (Chaotic good, Tethyrian human, he/him)

## Background

Mirt is a Tethyrian human who was once known as Mirt the Merciless and the Old Wolf, and who made a fortune and a reputation as an adventurer and a philanderer (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). One account makes him the son of a successful Coaster, a trader of the Sword Coast, who grew up on tales of heroism and became a fighter and then a mercenary general of the North (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mirt*, citing *Hall of Heroes*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. The money did not satisfy him, and he decided that he wanted peace and would have to build it himself (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. In those years he met Durnan, and the two became close companions (same page) `[research: wiki only]`.

Mirt and **Durnan** went down the Well, the old entrance to Undermountain, of their own free will, and they came back laden with treasure (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Both used magic to extend their lives, and they parted ways afterward, with Mirt keeping on as an adventurer while Durnan built the Yawning Portal over the Well (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki dates their return to 1302 DR and says that Mirt built his mansion with his share (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mirt*, citing *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* and *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. It adds that a paladin who adventured with them never came back from Undermountain (same page, citing *Undermountain: Maddgoth's Castle*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Mirt learned in the Well that the plan of a dungeon tells who built it and why, and he has read every building that way since `[design]`.

Mirt grew bored with peace within a month of settling in Waterdeep, and Khelben Arunsun and a second Lord invited him to become a Lord of Waterdeep (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mirt*, citing *Hall of Heroes*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Today he serves as one of the Masked Lords, a Harper and a close advisor to **Laeral Silverhand** (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He owns a Lord's ensemble and puts it on only to meet the other Masked Lords in an official capacity (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He lends money, and his loans to Harper members leave them owing him a favor in place of coin (**The Harpers**). The wiki says that he ran the business from his mansion and was patient about repayment (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mirt*, citing *Volo's Guide to Waterdeep* and *Waterdeep and the North*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. He leads the Waterdeep Harpers beside **Remallia "Remi" Haventree**, and he is the contact whom a new member meets first (**The Harpers**).

Mirt met his wife, Asper, and the wiki says that he rescued her as a baby from a sacked city (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mirt*, citing *Hall of Heroes*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. The same page calls her his wife and heir `[research: wiki only]`. She died several years ago, and his rambling mansion has seen better days (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He came home to Waterdeep after a long absence, and the glowering, fortress-like tower of the mansion has been upgraded with more delicate architecture since (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Asper drew changes that she wanted for the house, and Mirt has built all but the last of them `[design]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Mirt spends his days embroiled in politics and his nights in drink and debauchery (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He receives Harper members at dinner in a manor in the Sea Ward, and he is almost never at the mansion when a visitor calls (**The Harpers**; **Sea Ward**). He has kept a dossier on the Cassalanters for two years, and about a year ago the Howling Hatred cult led him to suspect that Cassalanter money stood behind it. He has no proof, and he does not know what the family hides (**The Harpers**). He also knows that the Splinter acts on Harper information, and he is searching his own cell for the leak (**The Harpers**).

**Personal goals.** Mirt wants to finish the house as Asper drew it `[design]`. He has built every change but the last, a gallery facing the harbor, and he has put that one off for two years `[design]`. He also wants one evening at his own table with Durnan, spent without a word about the Well `[design]`, and Durnan has turned down every invitation for years `[design]`. Asper was his heir, and he has named no other, so he wants to find someone to leave the house and the ledgers to, a person who will lend with patience and keep the favors straight `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people in Waterdeep, Mirt is the Old Wolf, a moneylender of great girth with a broad chest, a grey beard trimmed without much care and small sharp eyes in a face that has been lived in. He spends his days on politics and his nights on drink, he roars with laughter, and he is vulgar on purpose.

Under the noise he is warm and calculating, and he forgets nothing. He moves with surprising speed when he has to, and his generosity comes with strings that he does not hide. He knows architecture and talks about it at length with anyone who gives him the opening, and when someone asks about his past he gives an answer that sounds like a joke.

## Connections

- **Laeral Silverhand:** The Open Lord, who counts Mirt as her closest advisor and knows him as a Masked Lord.
- **Remallia "Remi" Haventree:** His colleague in the Waterdeep Harpers. Their working styles complement each other, and Mirt is the one whom new members meet first.
- **Durnan:** Mirt's old companion from the Well. Mirt does not ask him about what they saw there, and he would like Durnan to sit at his table once `[design]`.
- **Thorvin Twinbeard:** A Harper informant inside Xanathar's Guild. Mirt is the Harper who knows his secret.
- **Renaer Neverember:** A Harper-adjacent ally of Mirt and Remi.
- **The Cassalanters:** The family whose money Mirt suspects and cannot fault. His dossier is two years long and has not broken the surface.
- **The Splinter:** The Black Network cell that acts on Harper information. Mirt uses that name and has no name for its leader.
- **Xanathar:** The beholder that rules the Guild. Mirt has dealt with it many times and knows where its lair is.
- **Nihiloor:** The mind flayer whose intellect devourers have taken three Harper agents. Mirt wants it dead.
- **The Blood Hawk Posse:** The brigands of the Amphail road, who have taken coin from Mirt. Their founder, **Fenerus Stormcastle**, now lights lamps in the Trades Ward.
- **Asper:** His late wife, whose plans for the house he is still carrying out `[design]`.

## Overview

Mirt is a moneylender of great girth who is known across Waterdeep as the Old Wolf. He spends his days on politics and his nights on drink, and he is Open Lord Laeral Silverhand's closest advisor. He knows architecture well and will talk about it with anyone who gives him the opening.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Mirt should inspire amusement with his prodigious appetite and deliberate vulgarity, respect for the adventuring legend beneath the fat, and unease when his generosity comes with invisible strings attached.

**Emotions.** Mirt most often feels roaring amusement, political calculation behind the laughter, deep loyalty to old friends, nostalgic melancholy for Asper (his late wife), predatory enjoyment of power games, and genuine delight in architecture that catches his audience off guard.

**Motivations.** Mirt wants to protect Waterdeep from threats the Watch cannot see, keep the Harpers effective without getting his hands too dirty, and spend his nights exactly as he pleases because he has earned the right.

**Inspirations.** When playing Mirt, channel Robert Baratheon (*Game of Thrones*), Falstaff (*Henry IV*), and Winston Wolfe (*Pulp Fiction*).

### Voice

*A big, rumbling bon vivant who roars in public and goes quiet and exact the moment it's business.*

- **Sound:** A deep, gravelly bass with a laugh that fills a room. In business he drops to a low, level murmur and stops laughing entirely.
- **Sentence shape:** Two gears.
  - **Old Wolf (public):** long, rolling sentences full of food, drink and old stories, often ending in a laugh.
  - **Business:** short, complete, declarative sentences with no ornament ("Sit. The first act is short.").

  He never explains more than he has to.
- **Word choice:** Earthy and a little vulgar in public: food, wine, bodies, old debts. In business he uses plain words and a moneylender's vocabulary of debts, terms and favors owed. He calls younger people "lad" or "lass" and calls old friends by their first names.
- **Swearing:** *Punctuation · Artisan · Tirade.* Filthy and cheerful in public: "fuck", "shit" and bawdy jokes about bodies are part of the Old Wolf act. In business he doesn't swear at all, and the sudden absence of it is how people know he's serious. His public bawdry is a performance he's proud of: "May your cock rot, your ale turn and your creditors find you before your wife does." Tirade: "You wine-pissing, purse-pinching, velvet-arsed, gout-footed, bribe-sucking, chin-wobbling, three-faced Sea Ward cockwaffle, sit down before I sit on you."
- **Personality in speech:** His generosity is loud and his calculation is quiet, and he switches between them mid-conversation without warning. Asked about his past, he says something that sounds like a joke and isn't. When he's genuinely fond of someone, the praise is brief and blunt ("Good man."). When he's pressed, he gets shorter, not louder.
- **Quirks:** He refills glasses without asking. He tilts his head slightly while he recalculates. He will talk architecture with real enthusiasm if anyone gives him the opening. That's the one subject where business-Mirt gets wordy.
- **Signature phrases:** "Sit." / "Eat something first." / "That's a debt, not a gift." / "I am almost never home."
- **Never:** He never begs, flatters, or uses a Harper code phrase in public, and never lets anyone see him hurry.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Come in, come in, there's more fucking duck here than a man my size can decently finish, and gods know I've been trying for an hour. Sit."
  - *Business:* "Three names. Find out who they meet and tell me. Nothing more."
  - *Under pressure:* "I heard you. Now be quiet a moment while I decide how bad this is."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Mirt is "the Old Wolf," a moneylender of prodigious girth who spends his days in politics and his nights in drink and debauchery. To those he trusts, he is a Masked Lord, a Harper, and Laeral Silverhand's closest advisor. Deep down, the years of adventuring left him with more than stories. He is unexpectedly knowledgeable about architecture and will engage on the subject; ask about his past and he'll say something that sounds like a joke but is not.

**Morale.** In a fight, Mirt would move with surprising speed, drawing hidden weapons. Despite his girth, he has not let his adventuring skills wither, and anyone who mistakes him for soft discovers the error exactly once.

**Relationships.** Mirt is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep, a Harper senior operative, Laeral Silverhand's closest advisor, a widower (Asper died years ago), and a moneylender whose personal loans to Harper members are repaid in favors.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Mirt has kept notes on the Cassalanters for two years and suspects that their money stood behind the Howling Hatred cult about a year ago. He has no proof, and he does not know what the family hides.
> - He knows where Xanathar's lair is, because he has dealt with it many times, and he knows that Thorvin Twinbeard is a Harper informant inside the Guild.
> - He is hunting the leak in his own cell. He does not know that it is Tobin Harrask, a clerk in the records relay, and he has no name for the Splinter's leader.
> - Manshoon keeps Mirt on a list of five Masked Lords whose identities he knows. Corylus Thann and Jelenn Urmbrusk are the two he blackmails, and Mirt is not one of them.
**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about the Old Wolf's appetite in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats his grief for Asper as real and does not mock it.
