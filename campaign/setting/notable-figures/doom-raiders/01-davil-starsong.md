**Davil Starsong** (Neutral, sun elf, he/him)

## Background

Davil Starsong is a sun elf who has the affinity for magic and the patience of a long life that many of his kind share, though he is not the least bit pretentious or aloof (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He uses an elven lute as a spellcasting focus (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). One account calls him a bard and gives him the languages Common, Draconic, Dwarvish and Elvish (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Davil Starsong*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Davil grew up in a sun elf household where courtesy was a craft, and his mother taught him the lute as part of it `[design]`. When he was grown, by elven reckoning, he noticed that the household used its courtesy to say nothing and to settle nothing, and he left with the lute and one good coat `[design]`. He fell in with a hired band that cleared tombs for pay, and there he learned that a friendly question gets an answer that a demand never does `[design]`. In those years he met the four people who became the other Doom Raiders, and with them he plundered the lairs of liches, which some call "dooms" (*Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[design]`.

Among the five, Davil was the one who dealt with the buyers and patrons for whatever came out of the lairs `[design]`. He was also the one who talked the others out of the fights that would have killed them, and the other four came to wait for his word before they committed to a plan `[design]`. In the third lair a hired porter, a young man from a fishing village on the coast, stayed behind to hold a stuck door so that Davil and his companions could get out `[design]`. Davil carried the porter's share to the family and told them how he had died `[design]`. The porter's mother asked Davil to play something for her son, and he played for her, and he has been writing a lament for the porter ever since `[design]`.

Davil was once an adventurer, and he and his companions decided to join the Zhentarim in the late 1480s (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Davil Starsong*, 5e). He wanted work that he could do in daylight under a contract that he could read before he signed it `[design]`. He came to Waterdeep in 1489 DR with the other four to establish a Zhentarim foothold, and no source names who sent them (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Davil Starsong*, 5e). In the three years since, he and his colleagues have forged alliances with various nobles and guilds, run afoul of others and fended off Harper spies (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Within the Waterdeep division of the Black Network, Davil holds the title of Master of Opportunities and Negotiations because he is good at sniffing out lucrative business deals and makes friends easily (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He leads the Waterdeep operation, as the published adventure and the wiki both say (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations). Davil rents a curtained room on the second floor of the Yawning Portal and the suite above it, and he receives callers there each evening from eight until midnight (**Doom Raiders First Meeting**). Durnan charges him the standard prices, and the two retired adventurers do not ask each other about the past (**Doom Raiders First Meeting**). Davil keeps Yagra Stonefist as his bodyguard, a Black Network mercenary whom he took on after the five were already a cell, once she had given them good reason to keep her (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Doom Raiders First Meeting**).

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Davil is at odds with the other Zhentarim cell, which peeled off part of the network that he built (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations). He wants the street violence that it has caused to end, because a business cannot run in a city where someone keeps shooting people, and he prefers to keep the peace with Xanathar's Guild through negotiation (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations). He suspects that someone inside his own cell is leaking to the other side, and he has told nobody (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations). He counts Skeemo Weirdbottle as a friend, trusts him, and does not know that Skeemo is the cell's traitor (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Skeemo Weirdbottle**).

**Personal goals.** Davil wants to finish the lament for the porter `[design]`. He has rewritten it every winter since he played for the porter's mother and has never played it for anyone, because he is afraid that finishing it will close an account that he would rather keep open `[design]`. He also wants all four of his old companions to outlive the work `[design]`. He has not yet buried any of them, and he keeps a small sum set aside against each one's affairs so that nobody has to ask the cell for it `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people in Waterdeep, Davil Starsong is a lean, unhurried sun elf with amber skin, close-cropped gold hair going silver at the edges, and good dark clothes that announce nothing about him. He turns a signet ring on his right hand while he talks, and the charm bracelet of small trophies on his wrist rattles when he pours. He makes friends easily, speaks with a lilt that he credits to his elven blood, and negotiates with grace even after a few drinks.

Under the charm he is patient, and he does not explain himself. He asks one more question than the conversation needs, answers a question about his reasons with a smile and another question, and lets a silence sit until the other person fills it. His fondness for capable people is real, and he shows it with specific praise. He pours for guests before he says anything else, and when something troubles him he says nothing and takes up the lute.

## Connections

- **Yagra Stonefist:** His bodyguard and the half-orc who keeps the Yawning Portal's bar. She came to the cell after the first five.
- **Tashlyn Yafeera:** The Master of Arms and Mercenaries, who speaks for the cell whenever Davil is away. Her intelligence on the other cell is sharper than his.
- **Istrid Horn:** The Master of Trade and Coin and one of his old companions. Her lending funds the cell.
- **Ziraj the Hunter:** The Master of Assassination, who kills only when a friend asks. Davil is one of the four friends whose word counts.
- **Skeemo Weirdbottle:** The Master of Magic, who keeps Weirdbottle's Concoctions in the Trades Ward. Davil is fond of him and trusts him, and he does not say so aloud.
- **Durnan:** The owner of the Yawning Portal, who rents Davil his rooms. The two are retired adventurers and have an understanding about the past.
- **Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg:** A Harper agent who spends his afternoons and evenings at the Yawning Portal watching Zhentarim agents. No source says whether Davil knows who he is `[research]`.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** The cell that peeled off part of his network and works to dismantle it. Davil and his people call it the Splinter, Floxin's cell or the other cell, and they will share nothing with it.
- **Urstul Floxin:** The man whom Davil believes leads the other cell. **Manshoon** directs it from behind him, and Davil has not heard his name connected to it.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** The criminal organization that wants to wipe out anyone it takes for a Zhentarim operative. Davil would rather keep the peace through talk.
- **The Black Network:** No source says whom Davil answers to above the five Masters `[research]`.
- **The porter's family:** A mother and the rest of a family in a coastal fishing village. Davil sends them money every year `[design]`.

## Overview

Davil Starsong is a sun elf who keeps a room at the Yawning Portal and does his business there. He leads the Doom Raiders, a Zhentarim cell of former adventurers who run ordinary businesses. He is friendly and unhurried, makes friends easily, and negotiates with grace even when he has been drinking. People call him the Master of Opportunities and Negotiations, and he asks one more question than the conversation needs.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Davil should inspire liking with his effortless charm, unease with the silence behind his questions, and sympathy when it shows what it costs him to lead a cell that is being picked apart from outside and, he suspects, from within.

**Emotions.** Davil most often feels affable warmth, strategic patience, real fondness for capable people, a private worry about a leak in his own cell, careful deliberation, and a sadness he expresses through music rather than words.

**Motivations.** Davil wants to keep the Doom Raiders operational and independent from Manshoon's splinter, maintain peace with Xanathar's Guild through negotiation rather than violence, and protect the people who chose to follow him.

**Inspirations.** When playing Davil, channel Havelock Vetinari (*Discworld*), Iroh (*Avatar: The Last Airbender*), and Michael Corleone (*The Godfather*).

### Voice

*A charming, lilting elf who makes every deal sound like a favour and always asks one question more than he needs to.*

- **Sound:** A light, musical tenor with a sun-elf lilt he makes the most of. He's never loud. When something worries him the warmth stays, but it comes slower and lower.
- **Sentence shape:** Graceful, medium-to-long sentences that often end on a gentle question ("You understand that, yes?"). He lets silences sit rather than filling them.
- **Word choice:** Courtly, with trade terms dressed as friendship: "opportunity", "arrangement", "people I have found capable". Violence is always framed as helping people. He calls the party "my friends" once they've done a job for him.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Almost never; a courtly man. If Davil ever says "fuck", something has gone very badly wrong, and everyone at the table knows it.
- **Personality in speech:** Affable warmth over strategic patience. He withholds explanations and leaves questions unanswered without apology. His fondness for capable people is real and shows in specific praise. Worry (the leak in his cell) never gets words. He changes the subject to music, or plays instead.
- **Quirks:** He turns a small trophy on his charm bracelet when he's weighing something. He pours for guests before speaking. He answers "why" with a smile and another question.
- **Signature phrases:** "Sit. Please." / "…yes?" / "An opportunity, my friends." / "We can sort out the details when the work is done."
- **Never:** He never threatens outright, never haggles crudely, and never explains why he wants to know something.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Ah, there you are. Come in, sit, have a cup; I've been hoping you'd find the time."
  - *Business:* "It's a small thing, really, and it would help a good many people who can't help themselves. You'll do it, yes?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Let's all put our hands where the others can see them and talk like people who'd like to see morning."
  - *After a hard day:* "It was a long day, and an instructive one. Sit, and tell me what I missed."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Davil is the Master of Opportunities and Negotiations: a sun elf who makes friends easily, negotiates deals with grace even while drunk, speaks with a lilt he attributes to his elven heritage, and wears a charm bracelet hung with small trophies from monsters the Doom Raiders have killed. He conducts business from a curtained room on the Yawning Portal's second floor, with a permanent suite on the third. To those he trusts, he is a retired adventurer who joined the Zhentarim with his companions and frames the whole enterprise as helping people in need. Deep down, he withholds explanation and lets things sit, showing interest without revealing why, and he is fond of Skeemo in a way he does not say aloud.

**Morale.** In a fight, Davil would attempt negotiation first, fight with a swashbuckler's precision second, and retreat to preserve the Doom Raiders' leadership structure third.

**Relationships.** Davil is the cell leader of the Doom Raiders (Waterdeep Zhentarim), the faction contact for Zhentarim-aligned PCs, a permanent resident at the Yawning Portal (second floor business room, third floor suite), a colleague of Istrid, Tashlyn, Ziraj, and Skeemo Weirdbottle, the traitor whom he trusts and does not suspect, and a target of Xanathar's Guild, which wants to wipe out anyone it takes for a Zhentarim operative.

>
> - Skeemo Weirdbottle has fed intelligence to Manshoon's cell and to House Gralhund since before 1 Ches 1492 DR. Davil trusts Skeemo and has not looked at him, though he suspects that someone in the cell is leaking.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about Davil's manners and his habit of pouring before he speaks. The narrator treats his private worry as real, and the Overview says nothing of what he suspects.
