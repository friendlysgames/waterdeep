**Yagra Stonefist** (Neutral, half-orc, she/her)

## Background

Yagra Stonefist is a half-orc mercenary who lives in Waterdeep (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Yagra Stonefist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. The published adventure calls her a Black Network mercenary who is paid to protect Davil Starsong, and it says that she finds the job boring and passes the time by challenging adventurers to arm-wrestle (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She is not one of the five Doom Raiders who joined the Zhentarim in the late 1480s DR, and Davil says that she came along later, once she had given the cell good reason to keep her (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**; the Doom Raiders First Meeting event).

Yagra grew up in an orc camp in the foothills of the Sword Mountains, the daughter of an orc father and a human mother `[design]`. She grew up settling quarrels by arm-wrestling, as the camp did, and she was beating grown hunters at it before she had finished growing `[design]`. She learned there that someone who loses a fair contest and is not laughed at will shake her hand afterward, and she has run her own contests that way since `[design]`.

As a young woman she left the camp to look for paid work in the coast towns, and slavers took her on a coast road `[design]`. They chained her to a rowing bench on their ship, and when she tells it now she gives no count of the days `[design]`. Davil's people took the ship, and Davil asked her what she wanted to do next, and she said that she wanted to fight (the Doom Raiders First Meeting event). Nobody had asked her that before, in the camp or on the ship `[design]`.

She did not join the Doom Raiders on the day of the rescue. Davil kept her on as hired muscle, and she worked doors and warehouse stairs in the Dock Ward for a season before the cell counted her as one of its own `[design]`. One night in that season she held a warehouse door alone for most of the night while the others finished a job, and Davil named her one of them afterward `[design]`. In those same months she watched Xanathar's Guild collectors beat a cook in a Dock Ward alley over a late payment, and she has hated the Guild with plain fury ever since `[design]`. Ziraj the Hunter let her follow him on a hunt years ago and taught her the marks that he leaves when he is wounded (**Ziraj the Hunter**; **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**). She asked him because she wanted to be able to find any of her people who was hurt `[design]`.

By 1492 DR Yagra works the bar at the Yawning Portal from sundown until closing, which are the hours when Davil sits in his room upstairs, and she is his bodyguard (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**; the Doom Raiders First Meeting event). She leads interested visitors up to the curtained room where Davil waits (the Doom Raiders First Meeting event). If an adventurer is sympathetic to Zhent aims or an enemy of Xanathar, she can arrange a meeting with Davil (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Yagra Stonefist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. She arm-wrestles anyone who will sit down, because she enjoys it and because people relax once they have lost to her (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Appendix D).

**Personal goals.** Yagra wants to know what became of the rowers who were still chained to the benches when Davil's people took the ship `[design]`. Most of them were put ashore at a port that she did not see, and she has not asked Davil where, because she expects the answer to be a bad one `[design]`. She also wants to meet someone at the Yawning Portal who beats her at arm-wrestling without being let to, and she keeps a count of the people who held back `[design]`. Last, she wants a kitchen of her own with a pot big enough for her mother's camp stew and a table that seats the whole cell `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people at the Yawning Portal, Yagra is seven feet of half-orc with purplish-grey skin, tousled black hair and shapely tusks. She wears a red jade pendant bearing the Zhentarim sigil openly, and she cracks her knuckles when she looks at a stranger.

She is loud, blunt and cheerful, and she swears in nearly every sentence without meaning anything by it. She takes words literally and misses sarcasm, and she calls people by what they are ("you, the tall one") until they have earned a name. She never lies to a friend, never flatters and never apologizes for winning.

She arm-wrestles patrons because she likes it and not to humiliate anyone, and a person who loses to her is left with a clap on the shoulder. She will not discuss Davil's business, the Doom Raiders' fronts or anything about the other cell, and she says that is Davil's to tell. She tells her own past flatly and without weight, which makes most listeners uncomfortable.

## Connections

- **Davil Starsong:** The sun elf who leads the Doom Raiders and the only person who ever asked her what she wanted. She guards him at the Yawning Portal and speaks of him with more respect than she gives anyone else.
- **Ziraj the Hunter:** The Master of Assassination, who taught her his marks for a wounded hunter. She knows what each mark means, and she has never told him why she asked to learn them `[design]`.
- **Tashlyn Yafeera:** The Master of Arms and Mercenaries, who speaks for the cell when Davil is unavailable. Yagra can arrange a meeting with her in the City of the Dead or another quiet place (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Istrid Horn** and **Skeemo Weirdbottle:** The other two Masters of the cell. No source gives Yagra a close tie to either `[research]`.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** The crime ring that she hates with uncomplicated fury, for a reason that she keeps to herself.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** The rival cell that peeled away part of the Doom Raiders' network, which she calls "the other cell". She will not discuss it with strangers.
- **Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg:** A Harper agent who spends his evenings in the Yawning Portal watching Zhentarim agents, which puts him in plain sight of her bar. No source says whether she has noticed him `[research]`.

## Overview

Yagra Stonefist is a seven-foot half-orc who works the bar at the Yawning Portal. She wears a red jade pendant with the Zhentarim sigil openly and cracks her knuckles before she speaks to strangers. She greets newcomers by challenging them to arm-wrestle, and most people relax once they have lost to her. She is the bodyguard of Davil Starsong, the sun elf who holds court upstairs.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Yagra should inspire respect with her physical presence and directness, endearment with her arm-wrestling habit (which is how she puts people at ease), and loyalty, because of how far she will go for the Doom Raiders.

**Emotions.** Yagra most often feels casual confidence, competitive enjoyment, fierce protectiveness, unspoken pride in the Doom Raiders, controlled urgency in crisis, and a matter-of-fact acceptance of her own history that makes other people uncomfortable.

**Motivations.** Yagra wants to protect Davil Starsong, keep the Doom Raiders safe from Manshoon's splinter, and arm-wrestle anyone who looks like they might be interesting.

**Inspirations.** When playing Yagra, channel Drax (*Guardians of the Galaxy*), Michonne (*The Walking Dead*), and Rosa Diaz (*Brooklyn Nine-Nine*).

### Voice

*A seven-foot half-orc who says exactly what she means, loudly, and settles introductions by arm-wrestling.*

- **Sound:** A big, rough alto that carries across a tavern. She laughs like a door banging.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, blunt, complete sentences. Statements rather than questions. When she tells a story, it comes out flat and plain, like a weather report.
- **Word choice:** Plain street words, fight talk, food. No euphemisms at all. She calls people by what they are ("you, the tall one", "little wizard") until they've earned a name.
- **Swearing:** *Punctuation · Plain · Phrase.* Constant and cheerful: "fuck", "shit", "arse" as punctuation, never as insults to friends.
- **Personality in speech:** Casual confidence, and friendly challenges as a way of saying hello. She tells awful facts about her own past without any weight, which unsettles people. Protectiveness makes her curt and urgent. When one of hers is in danger, she's all orders.
- **Quirks:** She cracks her knuckles before speaking to strangers. She plants an elbow on the table and says "Arm first, talk after." She takes words literally and misses sarcasm, and doesn't care.
- **Signature phrases:** "Arm first, talk after." / "Fair fight." / "Where is he?"
- **Never:** She never lies to friends, never flatters, and never apologizes for winning.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You're new. Sit. Arm first, talk after."
  - *Business:* "Davil wants you. Fuck knows why. I'll find out after I beat you."
  - *Under pressure:* "One of ours is hurt somewhere in the fucking Dock Ward. Get your boots on. Now."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Yagra is seven feet of purplish-grey half-orc wearing a red jade Zhentarim pendant openly, cracking her knuckles at the Yawning Portal bar. To those she trusts, she arm-wrestles patrons because people relax after losing to her. Deep down, she tells the story of the slaver ship she was on before the Doom Raiders found her like an old weather report, wanting nothing for the telling.

**Morale.** In a fight, Yagra fights with her pride. She is disappointed if someone interferes in a fair fight, but she goes straight to any of her people who is in danger, in armor and at a run, at any hour of the night. She does not knock politely.

**Relationships.** Yagra is Davil Starsong's bodyguard, a Doom Raiders operative who appears as a tavern bouncer candidate (the **Trollskull Manor** guide), a Yawning Portal regular, and the friend who knows the marks Ziraj the Hunter leaves when he is wounded.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a blunt, cheerful humor about the arm-wrestling and the knuckles in Personality and the Overview. The narrator tells her past as flatly as she does and does not pity her.
