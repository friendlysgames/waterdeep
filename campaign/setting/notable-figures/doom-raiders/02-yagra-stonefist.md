# Yagra Stonefist

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Half-orc mercenary, neutral*. Stat block: **Thug (with modifications)**.
> - **Affiliation:** Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers**, **Doom Raiders Mission 5 — The Yellowspire Job**, **Doom Raiders Mission 6 — Ziraj's Last Hunt**, **Xanathar Sewer Hideout**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Yagra should inspire respect with her physical presence and directness, endearment with her arm-wrestling habit (which is how she puts people at ease), and loyalty when the party sees how far she will go for the Doom Raiders.

**Emotions.** Yagra most often feels casual confidence, competitive enjoyment, fierce protectiveness, unspoken pride in the Doom Raiders, controlled urgency in crisis, and a matter-of-fact acceptance of her own history that makes other people uncomfortable.

**Motivations.** Yagra wants to protect Davil Starsong, keep the Doom Raiders safe from Manshoon's splinter, and arm-wrestle anyone who looks like they might be interesting.

**Inspirations.** When playing Yagra, channel Drax (*Guardians of the Galaxy*), Michonne (*The Walking Dead*), and Rosa Diaz (*Brooklyn Nine-Nine*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Yagra is seven feet of purplish-grey half-orc wearing a red jade Zhentarim pendant openly, cracking her knuckles at the Yawning Portal bar. To those she trusts, she arm-wrestles patrons because people relax after losing to her. Deep down, she tells the story of the slaver ship she was on before the Doom Raiders found her like an old weather report, wanting nothing for the telling.

**Morale.** In a fight, Yagra fights with her pride. She is disappointed if someone interferes in a fair fight, but she will arrive at your door at 2 AM, armor on, not winded despite clearly having run, if one of her people is in danger. She does not knock politely.

**Relationships.** Yagra is Davil Starsong's bodyguard, a Doom Raiders operative who appears as a tavern bouncer candidate (the **Trollskull Manor** guide), a Yawning Portal regular, and the person who will run across the city at night to save Ziraj the Hunter.

## Overview

A towering purplish-grey half-orc wearing a Zhentarim pendant openly at the Yawning Portal bar, who arm-wrestles patrons as her preferred method of introduction and has never been seen to lose.
