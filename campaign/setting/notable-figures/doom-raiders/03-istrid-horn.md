**Istrid Horn** (Lawful evil, shield dwarf, she/her)

## Background

Istrid Horn is a shield dwarf who holds the title Master of Trade and Coin (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). One account gives her Waterdeep as her home, Common and Dwarvish as her languages and the class of cleric (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Istrid Horn*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Istrid was born into a shield dwarf family of caravan factors who bought ore and worked metal in the mountain holds and sold it along the Trade Way `[design]`. Her father did the haggling and her mother kept the books, and Istrid was totaling columns on her mother's knee before she could lift a full cash box `[design]`. When she was a girl her father borrowed the price of three wagons from a lender in a Sword Coast town, and a bad season put him a month behind `[design]`. She watched the lender's collectors take the wagons, the mules and the brass bell from the lead wagon, and she watched her father sign over what was left of the debt `[design]`. He took work on the Waterdeep docks and died in a Dock Ward boarding house with the note still unpaid `[design]`. From that winter on Istrid kept her own accounts and never signed a note as the borrower `[design]`.

A shrine-keeper of Vergadain in one of the mountain holds took her in as an acolyte, because she could already total a column faster than the shrine's own clerk `[design]`. Istrid worships Vergadain, the dwarf god of wealth and luck (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The shrine-keeper taught her to bless a bargain only after both sides had signed, so that neither could say afterward that the god had not been told `[design]`. She still says the blessing over every signed agreement, "Vergadain smile on it" (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations). She trained there as a priest and learned the rites that hold the dead back, and the rites later suited her to the lairs of liches `[design]`.

Istrid left the shrine to work as a delving priest, and in those years she met Davil Starsong and the people who became the other Doom Raiders `[design]`. In the party she was the priest and the one who kept the accounts, and she counted every share on a cloth in front of the others, blessed it, and only then divided it `[design]`. When the hired porter died holding a stuck door in the third lair, Istrid counted out his share to the last coin, and when Davil said it would not fit in one purse she added a second from her own take `[design]`.

Istrid was once an adventurer, and she and her companions decided to join the Zhentarim in the late 1480s (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Istrid Horn*, 5e). She wanted a house with caravan guards and agents in every Sword Coast town, because a lender with those can still collect from a debtor who runs `[design]`. She came to Waterdeep in 1489 DR with the other four to establish a Zhentarim foothold, and no source names who sent them (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Istrid Horn*, 5e). She took the title Master of Trade and Coin because she had kept the party's accounts for years and nobody else wanted the post `[design]`. In the three years since, she has used the title and the warehouse as the front for her trade.

Istrid runs an illegal lending operation from a heavily guarded warehouse in the Dock Ward and lends to people in need of coin (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She began with dock factors and chandlers whom the noble banks would not lend to `[design]`. Her interest rates are comparable to those of the Cassalanters and the Irlingstars, two noble banking houses, and the penalties for missing a payment are severe (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She keeps her ledger in a small lamplit office at the back of the warehouse, with two guards at the side door, and it records every balance and every borrower's known associates (**Dock Ward**; **Debts of the City**, GM Guide). She employs thugs and enforcers to collect, and when they are not enough she can call on her old adventuring companions (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Emmek Frewn, a refugee from Neverwinter who runs a pub in the North Ward, owes her a loan that Zhentarim enforcers collect (**Emmek Frewn**; **North Ward**).

Istrid knows that her lending is illegal and that the City Watch keeps a file on the cell (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations; Factions Guide). She therefore sees borrowers one at a time and discusses her rates with nobody else `[design]`. If trouble comes she lets her enforcers deal with it while she secures her ledgers (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations).

Istrid had an older sister, and after the sister died her daughter was apprenticed to a chandler in Waterdeep `[design]`. Istrid paid the apprenticeship through an intermediary, so that her own name never touches the contract `[design]`. The girl believes that her aunt is a factor in the dock trade, and Istrid has her to supper on the first of every month `[design]`. Istrid has never written the girl's name in the ledger, because a name in it is a name that she or an enemy could use `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Istrid wants to build a shrine of Vergadain in Waterdeep `[design]`. The nearest shrine she trusts is in the mountain holds, and she blesses agreements over a small brass scale on her desk, so she has set aside the price of a lot and a plain stone altar and adds to it from each tenday's interest `[design]`. She also wants to leave her niece a counting-house with a proper charter, where the girl can lend openly and never be arrested for it `[design]`. Istrid cannot get such a charter while her own trade stays illegal, and she has told nobody that this is why she wants her operation to grow `[design]`. Last, she wants the brass bell from her father's lead wagon `[design]`. She has asked after it in every market on the Trade Way for years, and when she finds the town where it hangs she means to pay whatever price the owner names `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who borrow from her, Istrid is a courteous dwarf with a low, comfortable voice and a pleasant, grandmotherly manner. She calls borrowers "dear", states her terms clearly and repeats them once. She taps one finger on the ledger when she names a figure, and she blesses every signed agreement with "Vergadain smile on it".

Under the warmth she is patient and thorough. She never raises her voice and never calls a penalty violence, and she shows her disdain for poor financial judgment as gentle disappointment. She enjoys having people in her debt, and she lingers on the word "owe". She never forgives a debt, and when a payment is missed her silence is the threat.

- **The Cassalanters and the Irlingstars:** The noble banking houses whose rates hers match. She is competitive with them in the legitimate side of her business.
- **The City Watch:** It keeps a file on the cell, and her lending is illegal. She sees borrowers one at a time because of it.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** The criminal organization that wants to wipe out anyone it takes for a Zhentarim operative. Her warehouse keeps two guards at the side door.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** The rival cell that peeled off part of the Doom Raiders' network. She is not part of it and shares nothing with it.
- **The Black Network:** No source says whom Istrid answers to above the five Masters `[research]`.
- **Her niece:** Her late sister's daughter, apprenticed to a Waterdeep chandler. The girl does not know what her aunt does `[design]`.

## Overview

Istrid Horn is a shield dwarf moneylender who lends from a guarded warehouse in the Dock Ward. She is the Master of Trade and Coin for the Doom Raiders, a Zhentarim cell of former adventurers. Her rates match those of the noble banking houses, and she calls every borrower dear. People who have borrowed from her say the penalties for a missed payment are severe and that her collectors deliver them.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Istrid should inspire wariness with her warm professionalism (which makes her loans feel reasonable until the penalties arrive), unwilling respect for her business acumen, and discomfort when it becomes clear that she enjoys having people in her debt.

**Emotions.** Istrid most often feels shrewd calculation, ledger-balancing satisfaction, predatory patience, devout worship of Vergadain (dwarf god of wealth and luck), cold disdain for poor financial judgment, and possessive pleasure in a growing ledger.

**Motivations.** Istrid wants to expand her lending operation, keep the Doom Raiders financially solvent, and ensure that every loan she makes returns more than gold: influence, favors, and leverage.

**Inspirations.** When playing Istrid, channel Tywin Lannister (*Game of Thrones*), Miriam Maron (*Ozark*), and Gringotts goblins (*Harry Potter*).

### Voice

*A warm, well-mannered dwarven lender who makes every loan sound reasonable and every missed payment sound like a pity.*

- **Sound:** A low, comfortable dwarven contralto, pleasant and grandmotherly, with a faint ledger-room crispness.
- **Sentence shape:** Measured, complete, businesslike sentences with a pleasant finish. She states terms clearly and repeats them once.
- **Word choice:** Banker's vocabulary: terms, interest, collateral, "arrangements". She invokes Vergadain in blessings and oaths. She calls debtors "dear" and never uses the word "threat".
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Never in business; the warmth is the weapon. She saves one cold "you stupid shit" for the day someone tries to cheat her.
- **Personality in speech:** Warm professionalism is the trap. She sounds reasonable until the penalties. She shows disdain for bad financial judgment as gentle disappointment. Her pleasure in debt shows in how she lingers on the word "owe".
- **Quirks:** She taps the ledger with one finger when naming a figure. She blesses every signed agreement ("Vergadain smile on it"). She never raises her voice. The silence after a missed payment is her threat.
- **Signature phrases:** "Vergadain smile on it." / "That's the arrangement, dear." / "I'm sure it won't come to that."
- **Never:** She never names a penalty as violence, never forgives a debt, and never discusses her rates in front of other borrowers.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Come in out of the damp, dear. Sit down. Let's see what we can arrange for you."
  - *Business:* "Two hundred gold, a tenday's grace, and ten percent each tenday after. Sign here, and Vergadain smile on it."
  - *Under pressure:* "Take the ledgers to the back room, please. I'll handle our guests."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Istrid is the Black Network's Master of Trade and Coin, running a lending operation from a guarded Dock Ward warehouse at rates comparable to the Cassalanters'. To those she trusts, she is the Doom Raiders' financial backbone. Deep down, she likes having people indebted to her, and the difference between her interest rates and a legitimate lender's rates is the silence when you miss a payment.

**Morale.** In a fight, Istrid would let her enforcers handle it while she secures her ledgers. If cornered, she fights like the veteran adventurer she once was, and the surprise is genuine `[research]`.

**Relationships.** Istrid is the Doom Raiders' Master of Trade and Coin, Emmek Frewn's creditor (a loan with Zhentarim enforcement), and a worshipper of Vergadain whose lending operation funds the cell's operations.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about her manners, her ledger and her blessings in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her warmth as real and her patience as the dangerous part, and the Overview says nothing that a borrower could not learn.
