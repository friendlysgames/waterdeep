# Istrid Horn

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Shield dwarf moneylender, lawful evil*. Stat block: **Veteran**.
> - **Affiliation:** Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**, **Gralhund Villa**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Istrid should inspire wariness with her warm professionalism (which makes her loans feel reasonable until the penalties arrive), unwilling respect for her business acumen, and discomfort when the party realizes she enjoys having people in her debt.

**Emotions.** Istrid most often feels shrewd calculation, ledger-balancing satisfaction, predatory patience, devout worship of Vergadain (dwarf god of wealth and luck), cold disdain for poor financial judgment, and possessive pleasure in a growing ledger.

**Motivations.** Istrid wants to expand her lending operation, keep the Doom Raiders financially solvent, and ensure that every loan she makes returns more than gold: influence, favors, and leverage.

**Inspirations.** When playing Istrid, channel Tywin Lannister (*Game of Thrones*), Miriam Maron (*Ozark*), and Gringotts goblins (*Harry Potter*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Istrid is the Black Network's Master of Trade and Coin, running a lending operation from a guarded Dock Ward warehouse at rates comparable to the Cassalanters'. To those she trusts, she is the Doom Raiders' financial backbone. Deep down, she likes having people indebted to her, and the difference between her interest rates and a legitimate lender's rates is the silence when you miss a payment.

**Morale.** In a fight, Istrid would let her enforcers handle it while she secures her ledgers. If cornered, she fights like the veteran adventurer she once was, and the surprise is genuine.

**Relationships.** Istrid is the Doom Raiders' Master of Trade and Coin, Emmek Frewn's creditor (150 gp loan with Zhentarim enforcement), and a worshipper of Vergadain whose lending operation funds the cell's operations.

## Overview

A shield dwarf moneylender running a lending operation from a guarded Dock Ward warehouse — her rates look competitive, her manner is warm, and the terms of a missed payment are not discussed at the table.
