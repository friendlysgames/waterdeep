**Tashlyn Yafeera** (Lawful evil, Calishite human, she/her)

## Background

Tashlyn Yafeera is a Calishite woman, born to a well-off family in Calimshan, and the published adventure credits her with an unfettered sense of superiority and a dislike of backing down from a fight (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki adds that her family was well known in that southern land (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Tashlyn Yafeera*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. She respects anyone who can best her in melee combat (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Tashlyn grew up in her family's house with a private sword master, a retired sellsword whom her father hired to train her cousins, and she was the only one of them who kept coming back to the yard `[design]`. She had beaten every cousin by fifteen, and then the old sellsword beat her in front of the whole family and told her afterward that she had been winning on temper alone `[design]`. Her family expected her to keep the accounts of the caravans that made its money, and as a young woman she took a contract guarding one of those caravans north and did not go back `[design]`. Nobody in the house has written to her since, and she has not written to them `[design]`.

She spent the next years as a hired sword on the trade roads, where she saw that the men who paid for steel armed their hirelings badly and replaced them when they died `[design]`. She then joined four other adventurers who plundered the lairs of liches, which some call "dooms" (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and she was the one who went through the doors first `[design]`. She has followed Davil Starsong's lead since the first lair, where he talked the party out of a fight that she wanted, and everyone came out alive, and she has never told him that he was right `[design]`. In the third lair she reached the far end of a corridor and turned around to find that a hired porter had stayed behind to hold a stuck door for the rest of them `[design]`. Since that day she has not sent a hired sword to hold a door unless she stood at it with them, and she does not speak of the third lair `[design]`.

Tashlyn was once an adventurer, and she decided with her companions to join the Zhentarim in the late 1480s (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Tashlyn Yafeera*, 5e). She chose the Black Network because it paid its sellswords on the day it promised and kept an armory for them, and she thought that a company which did that could use someone who knew what bad steel costs `[design]`. She came to Waterdeep in 1489 DR with the other four, sent to establish a Zhentarim foothold, and no source names who sent them (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Tashlyn Yafeera*, 5e). She holds the title of Master of Arms and Mercenaries, and in that role she provides armor, weapons and training to the sellswords on the Black Network's payroll (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Tashlyn serves as bodyguard to Vorondar Levelstone, a dwarf magister stationed at the South Gate, and the post is her cover (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Code Legal and the Magisters**). She likes the dwarf and has earned his confidence, and that has allowed her to reach the rank of captain in the City Guard, from which she watches the traffic through the gate and keeps it open for her associates (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She has built his trust over about two years `[research]`, and the wiki says that she genuinely respects him in spite of the deception (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Tashlyn Yafeera*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. She walked into his chambers about a year after reaching the city, when his last bodyguard had been dismissed after a wagon brawl at the Caravan Grounds, and she named a low price and beat his gate sergeant in the yard that afternoon `[design]`. She has never forged his seal, though she could, and she keeps the Network's business out of his chambers `[design]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Tashlyn keeps her post at the South Gate and speaks for the cell whenever Davil is unavailable (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations). In the past year she has been developing sources inside the rival cell, and her intelligence on it is sharper than Davil's (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**, Organizations). Many of the sellswords that the rival cell hires are men whom she once armed or trained, and she asks them who is paying them and for what `[design]`. She believes that she would run the Doom Raiders better than Davil does, and she has never tried to take the cell from him `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Tashlyn wants a training yard of her own `[design]`. She has no wish to command a company. She wants a walled yard with a sand floor and racks of practice steel, where anyone who can pay in work is taught properly and nobody is sent out to die for a captain's contract `[design]`. She also wants a second bout with the sword master who beat her in her father's yard `[design]`. He was old when she left and may be dead, and she has not asked anyone, because she would rather go home to Calimshan once, win or lose, and not go into the house `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people at the South Gate, Tashlyn is a compact, precise Calishite woman who stands behind a dwarf magister and carries a captain's rank in the City Guard. Her temper is quick, and people take her for impulsive until they see how exactly she fights.

She states her own excellence as a fact and does not repeat an instruction. When she is angry her voice drops and speeds up, and she gets shorter with everyone. Praise from her is rare and comes out as a single flat word, "Adequate", and she means it as the highest she gives. She never sits during a briefing.

She respects anyone who can beat her in melee and despises everyone else, and she tells the unskilled so. Under the pride she is exact about what she owes, to Vorondar Levelstone and to the hired swords she arms. She does not talk about the third lair or about her family in Calimshan.

## Connections

- **Davil Starsong:** The sun elf who leads the Doom Raiders and one of her old companions. She speaks for the cell when he is unavailable, follows his lead and believes she would lead better, and she has told him neither.
- **Ziraj the Hunter:** The Master of Assassination, who kills only when a friend asks. Tashlyn is one of the four friends whose word counts (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Yagra Stonefist:** Davil's bodyguard, who works the bar at the Yawning Portal. Yagra can arrange a meeting with Tashlyn in the City of the Dead or another quiet place (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Istrid Horn** and **Skeemo Weirdbottle:** The other two Masters of the cell. No source gives Tashlyn a day-one opinion of either `[research]`.
- **Vorondar Levelstone:** The dwarf magister at the South Gate whom she guards. She likes him and has earned his confidence, and the post she holds under him is her cover.
- **The City Guard:** The service in which she holds the rank of captain. The Watch keeps a file on the cell, and Captain Staget would let the Doom Raiders and Xanathar's Guild destroy each other (**The Open Lord's Watch**).
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** The rival cell that peeled off part of the Doom Raiders' network. Tashlyn has sources inside it. The Doom Raiders believe Urstul Floxin leads it and have not heard Manshoon's name connected to it, and she is not a member of it (**Urstul Floxin**).
- **Xanathar's Guild:** The criminal organization that wants to wipe out anyone it takes for a Zhentarim operative. She arms the cell against it.
- **The Black Network:** No source says whom Tashlyn answers to above the five Masters `[research]`.
- **Her family and her sword master:** A house in Calimshan that has not written to her, and the retired sellsword who beat her in its yard. She has written to neither of them `[design]`.

## Overview

Tashlyn Yafeera is a Calishite woman who serves as bodyguard to a dwarf magister at the South Gate. She holds the rank of captain in the City Guard and watches the traffic that passes through the gate. She is compact and precise, and her temper is quick enough that people take her for impulsive.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Tashlyn should inspire respect for her martial competence, irritation with her unfettered superiority, and grudging trust when she speaks for the cell in Davil's absence with more efficiency and fewer words than he uses.

**Emotions.** Tashlyn most often feels competitive pride, quick-burning anger, soldier's contempt for weakness, grudging respect for anyone who can beat her in melee, impatient competence, and a controlled fury that she channels into precision rather than explosions.

**Motivations.** Tashlyn wants to keep the Doom Raiders armed, trained, and operational, prove that she is the stronger leader, and best anyone foolish enough to challenge her.

**Inspirations.** When playing Tashlyn, channel Okoye (*Black Panther*), Lady Eboshi (*Princess Mononoke*), and Amos Burton (*The Expanse*).

### Voice

*A clipped, superior Calishite soldier who gives orders in as few words as possible and expects you to keep up.*

- **Sound:** A sharp, controlled voice with an educated Calishite accent. Anger makes it quieter and faster, not louder.
- **Sentence shape:** Terse imperatives and fragments. This is a deliberate break from `ember-voice`'s speech rhythm. She expects inference and doesn't repeat herself.
- **Word choice:** Military and aristocratic: "competent", "adequate", "weak", "orders". She has contempt words for the unskilled ("amateurs"). She calls the party by surname or not at all.
- **Swearing:** *Triggered · Colourful · Phrase.* Sharp and cutting, aimed at incompetence: "fucking amateurs", "shit work". Short, like everything else she says.
- **Personality in speech:** Unfettered superiority. She states her own excellence as a fact. Quick temper shows in how much shorter she gets. Grudging respect for anyone who beats her comes out as one clipped word ("Adequate.") that she means as high praise.
- **Quirks:** Her messages are two or three words long. She finishes other people's explanations for them when they're too slow. She never sits during a briefing.
- **Signature phrases:** "Adequate." / "Don't make me repeat it." / "Again."
- **Never:** She never pleads, never praises lavishly, and never admits she needs Davil's judgment.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You're late. Davil's busy. Report to me."
  - *Business:* "Warehouse, Dock Ward, tonight. Take the ledger, leave the guards breathing. Questions? No. Good."
  - *Under pressure:* "Hold the fucking door. Anyone who runs, I'll find later."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Tashlyn is the Master of Arms and Mercenaries, a Calishite bodyguard to a dwarf magister at the South Gate who ensures her associates can come and go freely. To those she trusts, she is born to a well-off Calishite family with an unfettered sense of superiority, quick to anger, and unwilling to back down from a fight. Deep down, she respects anyone who can best her in melee and despises everyone else.

**Morale.** In a fight, Tashlyn closes distance and stays there. Retreat offends her. Only a fight she is clearly losing will make her disengage, and she will remember the face of whoever forced her to run.

**Relationships.** Tashlyn is the Doom Raiders' Master of Arms and Mercenaries, the secondary faction contact who speaks for the cell whenever Davil is unavailable, and a leader who uses fewer words than Davil and expects more inference.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Tashlyn is a Doom Raiders Master. The bodyguard post and the captaincy are her cover, and she uses the gate to let the cell's people through. She has been developing sources inside the rival cell for about a year, and she is not part of Manshoon's Zhentarim.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry humor about her temper and her economy of words. The narrator treats her contempt for weakness as real and her regard for Vorondar Levelstone as sincere, and the Overview says nothing of her allegiance.
