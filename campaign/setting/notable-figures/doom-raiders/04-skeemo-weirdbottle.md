**Skeemo Weirdbottle** (Lawful evil, rock gnome, he/him)

## Background

Skeemo Weirdbottle is a rock gnome who works as an alchemist and a wizard, and he speaks Common, Draconic, Dwarvish and Elvish (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Skeemo Weirdbottle*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. He was once an adventurer, and he joined the Zhentarim together with the companions he adventured with (same page, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. Those companions were Davil Starsong, Istrid Horn, Tashlyn Yafeera and Ziraj the Hunter, who made a trade of plundering the lairs of liches (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**).

Skeemo grew up in a rock gnome warren in the hills, in a family that repaired clocks and music boxes `[design]`. As a boy he was apprenticed to an elderly gnome alchemist, who taught him to weigh a reagent twice and to write the price of every ingredient beside its recipe `[design]`. She had sold her formulas to a guild wizard for a fraction of their worth, because she had never put a price on them herself, and she died in a rented room with a shelf of unpaid reagents `[design]`. Skeemo paid a gravedigger out of his first earnings and could not afford a stone, and he has kept her formula book ever since `[design]`. He decided then that nobody who dealt with him would ever be vague about the cost `[design]`.

He took up wizardry afterward, because a scale could not read a lich's wards, and he joined Davil's company as the one member who could read them and brew whatever the others needed `[design]`. Skeemo watched Davil pay his fee in full on the day it was due, and he took that as a sign that Davil was worth staying near `[design]`. During the lich-lair years he kept a private notebook on each companion's habits, wounds and tempers, because he liked to know what each of them would do `[design]`.

When his companions gave up adventuring and joined the reformed Black Network in the late 1480s DR, Skeemo went with them (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**). In Waterdeep he set up Weirdbottle's Concoctions in the Trades Ward as his cover, a cramped little shop where most of the stock is nonmagical (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He crafts the magical potions for his Zhent friends, and he sells discounted potions and poisons to the adventurers who come looking for them (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Over the years he has also built an index of the city's criminal and semi-criminal dealings, partly for business and partly because he finds Waterdeep's power structures interesting (GM Guide, Debts of the City).

Before Ches 1, 1492 DR, Skeemo sold himself to Manshoon for rare alchemical components and protection from Watch inspections (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**). He passes what he hears to Manshoon's cell and to House Gralhund by paper bird (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and he has no loyalty to his friends (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Skeemo Weirdbottle*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. Skeemo lost the stock in his cellar twice to Watch inspections, and he could not replace the rare components at any price he could pay `[design]`. He took Manshoon's offer, which settled the stock and the inspectors in one arrangement, after he had worked out the figures `[design]`. Davil trusts him, and Skeemo has never found a way to put a figure on that, so he does not mention it `[design]`.

By 1492 DR he keeps the shop, hears the trade gossip across his counter and files what he learns (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**; GM Guide, Debts of the City). He walks to the Yawning Portal when Davil sends for him, and he counts the walk as a cost of the arrangement `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Skeemo wants to finish his mentor's last formula `[design]`. The formula book ends in the middle of a recipe for a tincture that needs one rare component, which no dealer he knows will sell at any price, and he is not sure that the component still exists `[design]`. He also wants to set a stone over her grave in the hills, and he has priced the stone, the carving and the carriage and sets aside a coin from each payment toward the total `[design]`. Last, he wants a laboratory with a properly vented cellar in a town where nobody has heard of the Doom Raiders or of Manshoon, and he means to be there before the war between the cells is over `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who deal with him, Skeemo Weirdbottle is the small, tidy gnome behind the counter of Weirdbottle's Concoctions, who adjusts his spectacles before he answers a question. He is pleasant to every customer and names the exact price of everything, down to the copper. He corrects small errors in what people tell him, and his sympathy comes a beat too late.

He says "we" about the Doom Raiders with some care, and he likes to ask who told a customer to come in. He hears a great deal of trade gossip across his counter and remembers it, and he is proud of his alchemy and of the accuracy of his measures. He never raises his voice, never swears and never shows grief.

## Overview

Skeemo Weirdbottle is a rock gnome who runs Weirdbottle's Concoctions, a cramped potion shop on a Trades Ward side street. Most of his potions and elixirs are nonmagical, and adventurers can buy discounted potions and poisons from him. He is the Doom Raiders' Master of Magic, and customers find him exact about prices and pleasant to deal with.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Skeemo should inspire distrust from the first meeting (something is off about his precision), satisfaction for a table that learns what he is, and a complicated pity for the gnome who sold his friends for rare components.

**Emotions.** Skeemo most often feels exacting focus, hollow pleasantness, calculating self-interest, pride in his alchemy, suppressed scorn for sentimentality, and the flat calm of someone who made his choice long ago.

**Motivations.** Skeemo wants to maintain access to rare alchemical components (which Manshoon provides), avoid detection as a traitor for as long as possible, and survive no matter which side wins.

**Inspirations.** When playing Skeemo, channel Petyr Baelish (*Game of Thrones*), Walter White (*Breaking Bad*), and Wormtongue (*The Lord of the Rings*).

### Voice

*A tidy, pleasant gnome alchemist whose politeness has no warmth anywhere in it.*

- **Sound:** A thin, precise, slightly nasal gnome voice. It stays even whatever happens.
- **Sentence shape:** Exact, careful sentences with qualifiers ("strictly speaking", "in point of fact"). He corrects small details in what others say.
- **Word choice:** Alchemist's jargon (reagents, tinctures, "rare components"), used with pride. He calls people "sir" and "madam" or by full name, never a nickname. He says "we" about the Doom Raiders a little too carefully.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Never. Too fastidious. When his contempt shows, it comes out in precise, clean insults, not curses.
- **Personality in speech:** Hollow pleasantness. He says every friendly thing a friend would, with no feeling behind it. Self-interest surfaces as questions about who knows what. Scorn for sentiment leaks as small corrections and dry asides. Under threat, he goes calm and transactional and offers information for his life.
- **Quirks:** He adjusts his spectacles before answering. He names the exact price of everything, down to the copper. His sympathy ("How dreadful") comes a beat too late.
- **Signature phrases:** "Strictly speaking…" / "How dreadful." / "Everything has a price. I'm simply precise about mine."
- **Never:** He never shows real grief, never raises his voice, and never admits to a side, only to interests.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Welcome to Weirdbottle's. Please don't touch the blue flasks. Strictly speaking, don't touch any of them."
  - *Business:* "That tincture is four gold, seven silver. Who told you to ask for it, out of interest?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Let's be sensible. You want names. I have names. I'd like to leave. Those interests align."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Skeemo is the Doom Raiders' Master of Magic, running Weirdbottle's Concoctions in the Trades Ward. To those he trusts, he is small, precise, and pleasant in a way that has no warmth in it. Deep down, he is a traitor, bought by Manshoon with rare components and Watch-inspection protection, messaging both sides by paper bird.

**Morale.** In a fight, Skeemo would use magic to escape rather than confront. If cornered, he will trade information for his life, switching loyalties to whoever holds the knife. He has no side; he has interests.

**Relationships.** Skeemo is the Doom Raiders' Master of Magic, a traitor feeding intelligence to Manshoon's cell and House Gralhund, and a colleague Davil was fond of, which Davil does not say aloud.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> Skeemo is a traitor to the Doom Raiders, and the Overview shows only a shopkeeper. He has fed intelligence to Manshoon's cell and to House Gralhund since well before Ches 1, 1492 DR. Manshoon bought him with rare alchemical components and protection from Watch inspections. The Doom Raiders and Manshoon's Zhentarim are two separate cells, and Skeemo belongs to the first while he sells to the second.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, level regard for Skeemo's courtesy in Background and Personality. The narrator treats his mentor, his unpaid stone and his pride in his craft as real and plays none of them for a joke.
