**Senna Vael** (Neutral, tiefling, she/her)

## Background

Senna Vael is a tiefling who has worked bars in the Dock Ward for three years. Two of her three employers have moved on and the third will speak well of her, and she carries a reference letter sealed with a wine glass (Alexandrian remix, Appendix D). By every account she is an excellent bartender (Alexandrian remix, Appendix D).

Senna grew up in the Dock Ward, in the back room of a cookshop that her mother ran near the fish market `[design]`. She carried plates there as a small girl, and she noticed that customers who had flinched at her horns relaxed once she remembered what they had eaten the week before `[design]`. She has used that lesson ever since, with drink orders in place of dinners `[design]`. Three years ago her mother lost the cookshop because the landlord sold the building, and Senna took her first bar job that season to pay the rent on a smaller room `[design]`. She began painting the tips of her horns gold on her first night behind the bar, because a regular asked about them and the answer gave the two of them something to talk about `[design]`.

Her first two employers each moved on within a year, and she was working her third post, in a bar that Doom Raiders money kept open, when Davil Starsong began to drink there on afternoons `[design]`. On his third visit he asked her what she wanted from the work, and she told him that she wanted a place with more character (Alexandrian remix, Appendix D for the wish) `[design]`. He said that he knew of work that paid better for the same attention, and a short written agreement followed that she read through twice before she signed `[design]`. She asked him what she should do if anyone ever found out, and he told her to deny nothing and to offer to work for whoever had caught her (Alexandrian remix, Appendix D for the instruction) `[design]`.

She has reported to Davil ever since on who visits the bars she works and what is said within earshot of the counter (Alexandrian remix, Appendix D). She considers the work honest enough, since she writes down only what anyone near the bar could have heard (the previous version of this page; Alexandrian remix, Appendix D). She counts the retainer as the one steady coin that she has, and it pays for her mother's room `[design]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Senna still works her Dock Ward bar and still brings her reports to Davil on her night off `[design]`. She has told him that she wants a better post, and he has said that he will keep an ear out for one `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Senna wants her mother out of the damp room by the harbor and into a dry one with a window that opens `[design]`. She has priced two rooms in the Southern Ward and sets aside a coin from each tenday's retainer toward the deposit, and she has not told her mother where the extra money comes from `[design]`. She also wants a bar of her own, with a house rule that nobody is stared at and a sign that is her own and not a borrowed wine glass `[design]`. Last, she wants an employer who stays in one place for a full year, because she has not yet had one and she would like to learn what it is like to be kept on `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people in the Dock Ward bars, Senna is a tiefling bartender with deep red skin, small curved horns painted gold at the tips and a tail that she keeps curled around one ankle while she sits (Alexandrian remix, Appendix D). She smiles easily, looks every customer in the eye and greets each regular with their usual order. She asks a few more questions than the job needs and lingers at tables where the talk turns interesting.

## Connections

She answers to **Davil Starsong** and nobody answers to her, and her mother, who lives in a damp room by the Dock Ward harbor, depends on her wages and her retainer `[design]`.

## Overview

Senna Vael is a tiefling bartender with deep red skin and small curved horns painted gold at the tips. She has worked bars in the Dock Ward for three years and remembers every regular's usual drink. She smiles easily, listens well and is looking for a post with a bit more character.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Senna should inspire pleasant surprise with her genuine competence as a bartender, and mild unease when someone notices she asks more questions than the job requires.

**Emotions.** Senna most often feels professional warmth toward patrons, the comfortable satisfaction of work that suits her, alert interest in the conversations she overhears, mild guilt when a patron is particularly decent to her, and a pragmatic peace with the arrangement.

**Motivations.** Senna wants to do the bartender's job well (it is, after all, the job), pass Davil the intelligence he needs about who frequents the bars she works, and avoid a situation where a patron's goodwill toward her becomes a liability.

**Inspirations.** When playing Senna, channel Margaery Tyrell (*Game of Thrones*, the pleasant intelligence gatherer who may genuinely like you), Kaylee Frye (*Firefly*, the professional warmth that does not preclude other purposes), and Nadia (*Russian Doll*, the self-aware woman who knows exactly what she is doing).

### Voice

*A friendly, attentive tiefling bartender who remembers everyone's order and asks one or two questions too many.*

- **Sound:** A bright, pleasant voice with a smile in it. It lowers and sharpens slightly when she's actually listening for information.
- **Sentence shape:** Easy, chatty service talk in medium sentences, with a friendly question tacked on the end of most exchanges.
- **Word choice:** Tavern-warm: "love", "welcome back", the drink by name. She asks about companions and plans in the tone of small talk.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* None on shift. Casual off shift, with the staff.
- **Personality in speech:** Her warmth is real, and so is her curiosity. The intelligence gathering is in who-sits-with-whom questions. Mild guilt surfaces as extra kindness to a patron who has been decent to her. If someone challenges her cover, she's matter-of-fact about it, with no shame and no drama.
- **Quirks:** She greets regulars with their order. Her "Who's your friend?" sounds like manners. She lingers a moment longer than needed at interesting tables.
- **Signature phrases:** "The usual?" / "Who's your friend?" / "It's honest work, mostly."
- **Never:** She never brings up Davil unprompted, never pries about a patron directly, and never lies when Davil has told her not to.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Welcome back! Cider for you and the dark for your friend, yes? Who's the new face?"
  - *Business:* (if challenged) "I pass Davil who comes and goes. Not you, unless you get interesting."
  - *Under pressure:* "Everyone, through the kitchen, now, mind the step."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Senna is a tiefling bartender with a pleasant manner and an excellent memory for drink orders, who makes every patron feel remembered. To those who observe carefully, she asks more questions than a bartender needs to ask, and her interest in who sits with whom exceeds professional curiosity. Deep down, she reports to Davil on who comes and goes where she works, considers the work honest enough, and would tell anyone herself if Davil asked her to.

**Morale.** In a fight, Senna moves quickly to protect patrons and exits through the kitchen. She is not paid to fight.

**Relationships.** Senna Vael is a Doom Raiders plant and a bartender candidate, a direct report to Davil Starsong. Her intelligence reports cover patron activity and faction traffic, not any single patron specifically, unless that patron becomes interesting.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Senna is a Doom Raiders plant who reports to Davil Starsong, and the Overview shows only a bartender. She belongs to the Doom Raiders and has no part in Manshoon's Zhentarim.
> - Davil's standing instruction is that she deny nothing if she is found out and offer to work for whoever caught her. A check of her Dock Ward references turns up her third employer, a bar kept open by a Zhentarim front business.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, warm regard for her manners in Background and Personality. The narrator treats her mother and her reports as real and plays neither for a joke.
