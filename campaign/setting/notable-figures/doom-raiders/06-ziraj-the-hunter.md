# Ziraj the Hunter

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Half-orc assassin, lawful evil*. Stat block: **Assassin (with modifications)**.
> - **Affiliation:** Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**, **Kolat Towers**, **Vault of Dragons**, **Doom Raiders Mission 5 — The Yellowspire Job**, **Doom Raiders Mission 6 — Ziraj's Last Hunt**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Ziraj should inspire hushed awe with his physical presence and lethal reputation, endearment through his friendship with Fala (the one relationship where he is gentle), and respect for a killer who only kills because someone he cares about asked.

**Emotions.** Ziraj most often feels patient stillness, watchful calm, fierce loyalty to the Doom Raiders, gentle ease in Fala's company, grim hunter's focus, and a silence that communicates more than most people's speeches.

**Motivations.** Ziraj wants to protect the Doom Raiders by eliminating threats before they arrive, keep Fala Lefaliir safe, and remain unseen by the rest of the world.

**Inspirations.** When playing Ziraj, channel Boba Fett (*The Mandalorian*), Geralt of Rivia (*The Witcher*), and Anton Chigurh (*No Country for Old Men*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Ziraj is a hulking shadow that glares from its perch, rains down death with an oversized bow, and slinks off without a whisper. Where he comes from remains a mystery. To those he trusts, he uses the quiet back table at Corellon's Crown and says "Three left" when asked for a report. Deep down, he kills because someone he cares about asked him to, and if no one asked, he would stop.

**Morale.** In a fight, Ziraj fires from elevation with his oversized longbow (requires Strength 18, deals 2d6 + modifier), repositions silently, and never engages in melee unless cornered. If wounded, he leaves intentional trail marks that he taught Yagra years ago.

**Relationships.** Ziraj is the Doom Raiders' Master of Assassination, a close friend of Fala Lefaliir (who shelters him at Corellon's Crown), a colleague Yagra will run across the city to save, and a presence on the Trollskull Alley rooftops that the party may not notice for weeks.

## Overview

A half-orc of conspicuous size who keeps to Trollskull Alley's rooftops and rarely speaks — when he does appear at street level, it is at Corellon's Crown, at the back table, and only briefly.
