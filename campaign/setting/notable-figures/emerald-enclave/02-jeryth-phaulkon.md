# Jeryth Phaulkon

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Disembodied presence, neutral good*. Stat block: none (a disembodied presence, not a combatant).
> - **Affiliation:** Emerald Enclave (Chosen of Mielikki)
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers**, **Vault of Dragons**, **Ten Nights in the City of the Dead**, **The Doppelganger Problem**, **The Grells in the Dock Ward**, **The Fouled Channel**, **The Dreamer's Reach**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Jeryth should inspire awe with her divine authority, comfort with the sanctuary she offers, and unease with the weight of a Chosen's rare, deliberate words.

**Emotions.** Jeryth most often feels ancient patience, pressing urgency when something stirs below, warm approval expressed through the garden itself, and a focused intensity that makes her few words feel like verdicts.

**Motivations.** Jeryth wants to protect the natural order from whatever is dreaming beneath the Castle Ward, offer Phaulkonmere as a sanctuary for those who serve the balance, and watch the party's handling of the Stone of Golorr with particular attention.

**Inspirations.** When playing Jeryth, channel the Oracle (*The Matrix*), the Great Deku Tree (*The Legend of Zelda*), and Galadriel (*The Lord of the Rings*, mirror scene).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Jeryth is a disembodied female voice heard by anyone in the Phaulkonmere gardens. To those she trusts, she is a noblewoman-turned-demigod, a Chosen of Mielikki who grants sanctuary, spells, and charms of restoration. Deep down, she is disinterested in the dragon hoard ("that question is for the Lords to settle") but deeply concerned with something old, patient, and hungry that she senses dreaming beneath the city.

**Morale.** Jeryth cannot be harmed in her disembodied state. She can cast any druid spell to defend the estate. She does not fight; she protects.

**Relationships.** Jeryth is the Chosen of Mielikki, the patron of Phaulkonmere, and the Emerald Enclave's highest authority in Waterdeep. Her relationship with the party is mediated through Melannor, but her direct interventions carry the weight of divine mandate.

## Overview

A disembodied female voice heard throughout the Phaulkonmere gardens, who addresses only those she has reason to speak to, and does so with few words and complete precision.
