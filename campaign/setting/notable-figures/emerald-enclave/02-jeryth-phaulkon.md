**Jeryth Phaulkon** (Neutral good, disembodied presence, she/her)

## Background

Jeryth Phaulkon is a woman from Cormyr (*Heroes of Faerûn*), and the Phaulkons she belongs to are of Cormyrean descent (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She came to Waterdeep with her family and lived as a noblewoman in the Southern Ward, in the villa that her father, a merchant and caravan organizer, had built (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jeryth Phaulkon*, citing the *City of Splendors* boxed set; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. She and her older sister were known as beauties in the city, and Jeryth had green eyes, long blond hair and a habit of twisting a lock around her fingers (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. She was frivolous in her interests and fond of fashion, and she was loved for her forthright honesty (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. She was betrothed to a son of the Tarm family of Waterdeep, who now share the ownership of the estate with the Phaulkons (same page; *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Jeryth lost her mother to a raid on a family caravan, and she was believed missing or killed in the same raid (same page, citing *City of Splendors*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. In one account she was a captive of the Hoar Fang gnolls when Mielikki came to her in a dream and offered to make her a Chosen, and with that power she freed herself and her companions and defeated the gnolls (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mielikki*, citing *City of Splendors*, 2e) `[research: wiki only]`. Afterward she lived in the High Forest as a ranger and served as Mielikki's defender of the woods, though the elves there seldom needed her help (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jeryth Phaulkon*, citing *The North*; edition not printed) `[research: wiki only]`. Her manner changed as a Chosen, and she became carefree like a bird, while she grew strict with anyone who misuses nature (same page, citing *City of Splendors*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

Jeryth tells new members that the estate was her family's and that it still is, in some sense, though she occupies it differently now. She says that Mielikki asked something of her several years ago and that she said yes (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). The wiki says that she shed her physical form to become a disembodied voice and presence in the gardens of her family home (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jeryth Phaulkon*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. She has lived there as a voice for several years, and she is the only one of the estate's owners who stays, while the rest of the Phaulkons and the Tarms spend most of the year abroad (**The Emerald Enclave**).

Jeryth is the highest authority of the Emerald Enclave in Waterdeep. She cannot be harmed in her present state, and she can cast any druid spell, which she uses to defend the estate and its gardens (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She sends no one out on tasks, and the animals carry only Melannor's words (**The Emerald Enclave**). For months she has felt something dreaming in the earth below the Castle Ward, and she holds it back from the ward's water table. She does not know its name (**Castle Ward**).

Jeryth was scolded as a girl for climbing the oldest oak in the garden, and the paths there still bend back to it `[design]`. Her mother kept a bed of herbs beside that oak, and the bed is the part of the garden where Jeryth's attention rests longest `[design]`. Her older sister was the wild one of the two, and she has not come to the garden since Jeryth became a voice `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Jeryth wants her older sister to stand in the garden once and hear her speak `[design]`. She keeps the gate open to any Phaulkon who comes, whether the Enclave has invited them or not, and she has never told anyone why `[design]`. She also wants the garden left wild after her, with the oldest oak and the stone paths as they are, and she fears the day when the families that own the estate decide to clip it or sell it `[design]`. She would like to greet one visitor face to face at the gate, as she did when she was a girl, and she has found no way to do it `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who have heard her, Jeryth Phaulkon is a woman's voice in the gardens of Phaulkonmere. The voice comes from the leaves, the gravel and the air together, and the branches move when she speaks. She says one or two short sentences and then falls silent for a long while.

She never swears, never explains herself twice and names no side in mortal politics. When someone asks about the dragon hoard, she answers once, "That question is for the Lords to settle," and says nothing more. Her approval shows in the garden, where flowers open and a warm breeze moves along the path.

Under the calm she is strict with anyone who harms the natural order, and she keeps some of the plain honesty that she had as a noblewoman `[research: wiki only]`.

## Overview

Jeryth Phaulkon is a disembodied female voice that visitors hear in the gardens of Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward. She was once a noblewoman of the Phaulkon family and is now a Chosen of Mielikki, the Lady of the Forest. She speaks one or two short sentences at a time to those the Enclave has invited. The branches move when she speaks, and she is the Emerald Enclave's highest authority in Waterdeep.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Jeryth should inspire awe at her authority. Visitors feel comfort in the sanctuary she offers and unease at how rarely a Chosen speaks, and how carefully.

**Emotions.** Jeryth most often feels ancient patience, pressing urgency when something stirs below, warm approval expressed through the garden itself, and a focused intensity that makes her few words feel like verdicts.

**Motivations.** Jeryth wants to protect the natural order from whatever is dreaming beneath the Castle Ward, and offer Phaulkonmere as a sanctuary for those who serve the balance.

**Inspirations.** When playing Jeryth, channel the Oracle (*The Matrix*), the Great Deku Tree (*The Legend of Zelda*), and Galadriel (*The Lord of the Rings*, mirror scene).

### Voice

*A disembodied voice in the gardens: few words, each one a verdict.*

- **Sound:** A clear, calm woman's voice that comes from everywhere in the garden at once, with leaves moving when she speaks.
- **Sentence shape:** One or two short sentences, spaced far apart. Every sentence is complete and final.
- **Word choice:** Nature and time: seasons, roots, dreaming, balance. Mielikki is "the Lady of the Forest". She names no mortal politics.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Never.
- **Personality in speech:** Ancient patience and urgency. Her approval comes through the garden itself: flowers opening, a warm breeze. Her disinterest in the gold is a single dismissal.
- **Quirks:** She speaks only to those she has reason to address. The garden answers along with her.
- **Signature phrases:** "Something dreams below." / "That question is for the Lords to settle." / "Rest here."
- **Never:** She never chatters, never explains herself twice, and never takes a side in the Grand Game.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You carry something heavy. Rest here."
  - *Business:* "The gold is for the Lords to settle. Watch what dreams beneath the Castle Ward."
  - *Under pressure:* "Enough." (every vine in the garden tightens)

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Jeryth is a disembodied female voice heard by anyone in the Phaulkonmere gardens. Her voice comes from the leaves, the gravel and the air together, and the branches move when she speaks. To those she trusts, she is a noblewoman who became a Chosen of Mielikki and grants sanctuary, spells, and charms. Her approval shows in the garden, where flowers open and a warm breeze moves along the path. Deep down, she is disinterested in the dragon hoard. When asked about the gold, she answers once, "That question is for the Lords to settle," and says nothing more. Her real concern is something old and patient that she senses dreaming beneath the city, and she does not know its name.

**Morale.** Jeryth cannot be harmed in her disembodied state. She can cast any druid spell to defend the estate. She does not fight; she protects. When she says "Enough," every vine in the garden tightens.

**Relationships.** Jeryth is the Chosen of Mielikki, the patron of Phaulkonmere, and the Emerald Enclave's highest authority in Waterdeep. Her words reach the Enclave's members through Melannor, who relays them as she speaks them. When she intervenes directly, she acts on Mielikki's mandate.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Jeryth has felt a dreaming presence below the Castle Ward for months and has held it back from the ward's water table. It is Illuun, an aboleth on Undermountain Level 4, and the Stone of Golorr woke it. She does not know its name and has never spoken it. Her words are "Something dreams below."
> - Jeryth feels an infernal influence in the Southern Ward, and the Enclave knows it is there. Nobody in the Enclave knows whose it is.

**Tone:** Slow and spare in the GM text, with a dry gentleness in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her few words as plain statements and never makes her reserve a joke.
