# Jeryth Phaulkon

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - *Disembodied presence, Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, she/her*. Stat block: none (a disembodied presence, not a combatant).
> - **Affiliation:** Emerald Enclave (Chosen of Mielikki)
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers**, **Vault of Dragons**, **Emerald Enclave First Meeting**, **A Seat at Phaulkonmere**, **The Water Table Stirs**, **Summerstrider**, **Ten Nights in the City of the Dead**, **The Doppelganger Problem**, **The Grells in the Dock Ward**, **Autumnreaver**, **The Fouled Channel**, **Winterstalker**, **The Dreamer's Reach**, **Master of the Wild**
>
> Jeryth speaks one or two short sentences at a time, with long gaps between them. She never repeats or explains herself, never swears, and names no mortal politics. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest". She has lived in the gardens of Phaulkonmere for several years.
>
> She does not dispatch missions. Melannor's animals carry the Enclave's requests, and the urgency in them is his.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Jeryth should inspire awe with her divine authority, comfort with the sanctuary she offers, and unease with the weight of a Chosen's rare, deliberate words.

**Emotions.** Jeryth most often feels ancient patience, pressing urgency when something stirs below, warm approval expressed through the garden itself, and a focused intensity that makes her few words feel like verdicts.

**Motivations.** Jeryth wants to protect the natural order from whatever is dreaming beneath the Castle Ward, offer Phaulkonmere as a sanctuary for those who serve the balance, and watch the party's handling of the Stone of Golorr with particular attention.

**Inspirations.** When playing Jeryth, channel the Oracle (*The Matrix*), the Great Deku Tree (*The Legend of Zelda*), and Galadriel (*The Lord of the Rings*, mirror scene).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Jeryth is a disembodied female voice heard by anyone in the Phaulkonmere gardens. Her voice comes from the leaves, the gravel and the air together, and the branches move when she speaks. To those she trusts, she is a noblewoman who became a Chosen of Mielikki and grants sanctuary, spells, and charms. Her approval shows in the garden, where flowers open and a warm breeze moves along the path. Deep down, she is disinterested in the dragon hoard. When asked about the gold, she answers once, "That question is for the Lords to settle," and says nothing more. Her real concern is something old and patient that she senses dreaming beneath the city, and she does not know its name.

**Morale.** Jeryth cannot be harmed in her disembodied state. She can cast any druid spell to defend the estate. She does not fight; she protects. When she says "Enough," every vine in the garden tightens.

**Relationships.** Jeryth is the Chosen of Mielikki, the patron of Phaulkonmere, and the Emerald Enclave's highest authority in Waterdeep. Her relationship with the party runs through Melannor, who relays her words as she speaks them. Her direct interventions carry the weight of divine mandate.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jeryth has held the dreaming presence back from the Castle Ward water table for months. It is Illuun, an aboleth on Undermountain Level 4, and the Stone of Golorr woke it.
> - She never names it. Her words are "Something dreams below," and in **The Water Table Stirs** alone she adds "old, patient and hungry". A member learns the name only through **Anchor Destroyed**, when she learns it as the anchor breaks and tells them, or from the Stone at Full Awakening. **Illuun Contact** alone gives the presence, not the name.
> - In **The Dreamer's Reach** she falls silent for three days. She wakes when the anchor breaks and speaks twice at the oak.

## Overview

A disembodied female voice heard throughout the Phaulkonmere gardens, who addresses only those she has reason to speak to, and does so with few words and complete precision.
