**Melannor Fellbranch** (Neutral good, half-elf, he/him)

## Background

Melannor is a half-elf druid and the groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, a walled villa in the Southern Ward. He keeps the estate for the Tarm and Phaulkon families, who spend most of the year abroad and left it in his charge (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He gathers information through magical conversations with the animals of the city, and he is partial to cats and pigeons as couriers (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki adds that he always used them as couriers for his faction (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Melannor Fellbranch*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Melannor grew up in a village at the edge of a forest in the North, the older of two sons of a human orchardist and an elven forester `[design]`. His mother taught him to graft a branch and to tell a sick tree from a dry one, and his father, who was often away in the forest, taught him to read soil and tracks `[design]`. His younger brother, Talisolvanar, followed him down the rows and made a joke about every tree `[design]`.

A hard winter killed most of the orchard, and their mother died before the thaw `[design]`. Melannor spent that spring burying her and cutting the dead wood out of the trees, and he noticed that the neighbors' kind jokes did nothing for the work while an exact account of what was wrong with each tree did `[design]`. From then on he said only what was so, and he has not been able to hear a joke as anything but a statement `[design]`. Talisolvanar answered the same winter by joking more, and the two brothers have never agreed about it `[design]`.

Talisolvanar left for Waterdeep first, to learn joinery, and Melannor stayed until the replanted trees had taken `[design]`. He then followed his brother to the city and took work as a gardener's hand at Phaulkonmere, because it had the only old oak that he could find inside the walls `[design]`. The groundskeeper there was a druid of the Emerald Enclave, and she taught him the druid's craft and the *Animal Messenger* spell and brought him into the Enclave `[design]`. When she gave up the post, the Tarm and Phaulkon families asked him to keep the estate in her place `[design]`. The Enclave is the one company that he has kept besides his brother `[design]`.

Today Melannor serves Jeryth Phaulkon, the lady of the estate, and carries her few words to the Enclave's members as she gave them. He keeps the gate, gives food and care to the animals of those who visit, and has watched a nest in the old oak since early spring (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). He sends the Enclave's messages by cat, pigeon, crow or falcon in no more than twenty-five words, and he appears in person only when the situation demands it (**The Emerald Enclave**). Something beneath the Castle Ward troubles him, and he cannot say what it is (**Southern Ward**). He knows that Jeryth feels an infernal influence in the Southern Ward, and he does not know whose it is `[research]`.

**Personal goals.** Melannor wants to sit through one whole afternoon in his brother's shop without turning the talk into a report `[design]`. He visits the alley twice a year, brings seedlings, answers what he is asked and leaves within the hour, and he tells Talisolvanar that Talisolvanar should visit Phaulkonmere instead `[design]`. He does not see that Talisolvanar is waiting for the same visit in the other direction `[design]`. He also wants a patch of ground that is his own `[design]`. He carried a cutting from the last living tree of his mother's orchard to Waterdeep in a pot and has grafted seven young trees from it in a sheltered corner of the estate `[design]`. The estate belongs to the Tarm and Phaulkon families, so he means to plant the trees where they will not have to be moved, and he has not told his brother about them `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who meet him, Melannor is the friendly but humorless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere. He never uses contractions, never jokes and never swears, and he answers a joke literally. He calls his brother Talisolvanar and never Tally.

He delivers grim news in a calm baritone, as though he were reporting the weather. To those he trusts, he explains each arrangement once and then waits for questions. His affection shows in practical offers, such as seedlings or a place in the garden, and he goes silent when he hears good news, because he finds even relief a private matter.

## Connections

- **Jeryth Phaulkon:** The lady of Phaulkonmere and the highest Enclave authority in Waterdeep. Melannor serves her and speaks her words as she gave them, and the urgency he adds is his own.
- **Talisolvanar "Tally" Fellbranch:** His younger brother, a carpenter and woodcarver at the Bent Nail in Trollskull Alley `[design]`. Each has told the other to visit, and Talisolvanar wants Melannor to come more often than he does.
- **The Tarm and Phaulkon families:** The owners of the estate, who live abroad most of the year. Melannor answers to them for the grounds, and none of the land is his `[design]`.
- **The Enclave's six:** The six druids and rangers who work for the Enclave in the city, led by Sorrel Hartwick. Melannor sends them the Enclave's messages and passes on what the watchers report without guessing at what it means.
- **Sarna Dath:** The fisher on the south quay who writes the Enclave a note on the harbor. She is the watcher who knows the Enclave by name.
- **Bonnie:** The doppelganger barmaid at the Yawning Portal. Melannor knows what she is, and he learned it from his animals.
- **Xanathar:** The beholder under the city, and in his words a problem that has not been solved. Creatures of its kind belong to no natural order, and its Guild digs wherever it likes.
- **The Splinter:** The other cell of an old network that works in the city. He does not know who leads it.
- **The Cassalanters:** A rich house, and nothing worse as far as he can say. He shares the city's suspicion that the family is too nice to be this rich.
- **The Open Lord's Watch:** Melannor answers a Watch officer who holds a warrant truthfully, and he does not shelter anyone from one.

## Overview

Melannor Fellbranch is the groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, an old garden estate in the Southern Ward. He is friendly but humorless, and he reports grim news in the same calm baritone he uses for the weather. He sends the Emerald Enclave's messages by animal, usually cats and pigeons, and he has a carpenter brother in Trollskull Alley.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Melannor should inspire trust with his calm reliability, amusement with his complete absence of humor, and endearment when his quiet baritone delivers grim news as though reporting weather.

**Emotions.** Melannor most often feels measured concern, patient duty, understated affection expressed through action, private satisfaction when nature's balance is maintained, and a careful watchfulness that deepens when he cannot articulate what feels wrong.

**Motivations.** Melannor wants to protect Phaulkonmere and its gardens, fulfill Enclave assignments with minimal fuss, and investigate disturbances he senses but cannot yet name.

**Inspirations.** When playing Melannor, channel Aragorn (*The Lord of the Rings*), Hank Hill (*King of the Hill*), and Obi-Wan Kenobi (*Star Wars*, prequel era).

### Voice

*A humorless groundskeeper who reports grim news in a calm baritone, as if describing the weather.*

- **Sound:** A deep, calm, unvarying baritone. Nothing changes its tone, including bad news.
- **Sentence shape:** Plain, complete, even-paced sentences. He states facts and waits. He does not recognize a joke when he hears one.
- **Word choice:** Gardener's and druid's terms: soil, roots, seasons, balance. He calls the party by name, correctly, every time. He calls his brother "Talisolvanar", never "Tally".
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Never. He wouldn't see the point.
- **Personality in speech:** Measured concern and patient duty. His affection shows in practical offers ("I'll bring you seedlings."). His unease about the thing below the Castle Ward comes out as uncharacteristic hesitations and unfinished sentences.
- **Quirks:** He answers jokes literally. He sends messages by cat and pigeon, written in the same flat tone.
- **Signature phrases:** "The soil is uneasy." / "That is not humorous." / "Talisolvanar should visit."
- **Never:** He never exaggerates or laughs, and he never ignores a disturbance in the earth.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Good morning. The roses are well. The soil in the Castle Ward is uneasy."
  - *Business:* "Something beneath the city is stirring. I do not know what. Tell me if you meet any creature that does not belong."
  - *Under pressure:* "Stand behind the oak. The roots will hold them."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Melannor is the friendly but humorless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward, who sends the Enclave's missions by animal. His *Animal Messenger* creatures carry no more than twenty-five words, and he comes in person only when the situation demands it. To those he trusts, he explains each arrangement once and then waits for questions. Deep down, he senses something wrong in the earth beneath the Castle Ward and cannot say what it is.

**Morale.** In a fight, Melannor would reshape the terrain with druid magic (roots through flagstones, fog from dry air) and put himself between the threat and anyone who cannot run. He goes silent when he hears good news, because he finds even relief a private matter.

**Relationships.** Melannor is the brother of Talisolvanar "Tally" Fellbranch, the Trollskull Alley carpenter. He asks after him and says that Talisolvanar should visit when his work in the alley allows it. He serves Jeryth Phaulkon and carries her few words to the Enclave's members. He speaks them as she gave them and adds only the urgency, which is his own.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **What troubles him:** Jeryth holds a dreaming presence back from the Castle Ward's water table. It is Illuun, an aboleth on Undermountain Level 4, and the Stone of Golorr woke it.
> - **What he knows:** Melannor feels the presence as wrongness in the earth and cannot name it. Nobody in the Enclave knows the name, and Jeryth has never spoken it.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, gentle humor in Personality about a man who answers a joke with the facts. The narrator treats his reserve and his grief for the orchard as real and never makes either a joke.
