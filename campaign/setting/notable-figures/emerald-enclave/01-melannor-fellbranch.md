> **Alignment.** Dragon Heist lists Melannor as Chaotic Good. The campaign keeps Neutral Good, and the Enclave events are written for it.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Melannor should inspire trust with his calm reliability, amusement with his complete absence of humor, and endearment when his quiet baritone delivers grim news as though reporting weather.

**Emotions.** Melannor most often feels measured concern, patient duty, understated affection expressed through action, private satisfaction when nature's balance is maintained, and a careful watchfulness that deepens when he cannot articulate what feels wrong.

**Motivations.** Melannor wants to protect Phaulkonmere and its gardens, fulfill Enclave assignments with minimal fuss, and investigate disturbances he senses but cannot yet name.

**Inspirations.** When playing Melannor, channel Aragorn (*The Lord of the Rings*), Hank Hill (*King of the Hill*), and Obi-Wan Kenobi (*Star Wars*, prequel era).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Melannor is the friendly but humorless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward, who sends the Enclave's missions by animal. His *Animal Messenger* creatures carry no more than twenty-five words, and he comes in person only when the situation demands it, which happens more often as the year goes on. To those he trusts, he explains each arrangement once and then waits for questions. Deep down, he senses something wrong in the earth beneath the Castle Ward and cannot say what it is.

**Morale.** In a fight, Melannor would reshape the terrain with druid magic (roots through flagstones, fog from dry air) and put himself between the threat and anyone who cannot run. He goes silent when he hears good news, because he finds even relief a private matter.

**Relationships.** Melannor is the brother of Talisolvanar "Tally" Fellbranch, the Trollskull Alley carpenter. He asks after him at the First Meeting and says that Talisolvanar should visit when his work in the alley allows it. He serves Jeryth Phaulkon and carries her few words to the Enclave's members. He speaks them as she gave them and adds only the urgency, which is his own.

## Overview

The humorless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, who delivers grim news in a calm baritone and sends his messages by *Animal Messenger*. He has been appearing in person more often as something he cannot name continues to trouble him.
