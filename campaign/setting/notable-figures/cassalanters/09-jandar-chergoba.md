**Jandar Chergoba** (Lawful evil, tiefling, he/him)

## Background

Jandar Chergoba is a male tiefling and the head chef of Cassalanter Villa. He determines each day's menu and oversees everything that happens in the kitchen, where six chefs work under him, and he is often there himself (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He keeps a golden sacrificial dagger, a golden mask and crimson robes in a locked iron box under a sack of potatoes in the pantry (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). One wiki account adds that he casts spells as a cultist of Asmodeus (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jandar Chergoba*, citing *Dragon Heist* p. 117; 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. No source records where he was born, who raised him or what he did before the villa's kitchen `[research]`.

Jandar grew up in the Trades Ward behind a cookshop that his aunt ran, which fed carters and guild apprentices at noon `[design]`. He was scouring pots at six and minding the stock at ten, and he still keeps her rule that a plate leaves the pass clean or the cook eats it `[design]`. Customers looked at the tiefling boy before they looked at the food, and she told him that the cure was a plate that nobody could fault `[design]`. He went on to cook in the kitchens of two inns and a guild hall, and he learned the order of stations, service and the pass from a former soldier who ran the second kitchen `[design]`.

His aunt died while he was still cooking for other people, and she left him the lease on the cookshop `[design]`. He borrowed 3,000 gp from the Cassalanter Counting House to turn it into a restaurant with a written menu `[design]`. The venture failed within two years, because the guild halls nearby took their custom to a caterer with a contract and the dining room stood half empty `[design]`. The landlord took the lease back, and the debt stayed with Jandar `[design]`.

The obligation that Victoro Cassalanter holds over him is the debt from that failed venture, 3,000 gp in all. Victoro had eaten at the restaurant, and he gave Jandar the head chef's post at the villa so that the debt could be worked down in wages `[design]`. The family's banking failed about 1489 DR (**A Debt in Blood**), and three years ago the debt converted into cult service. Jandar now attends the gatherings, prepares the ritual meals and commands the kitchen's ten animated knives, which fight with flying sword statistics. Victoro still holds the obligation, and the service is how Jandar pays it `[design]`.

He hates what he does for the cult. He took the faith the way a cook takes the heat of a stove, as a fact to work with and not to argue with, and that is why his certainty carries no drama `[design]`. The part that galls him is the ritual meals, which follow a fixed form that allows no seasoning and no plating of his own, and which nobody at the table eats as food `[design]`. He has never said so to anyone `[design]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR the Founders' Day feast is four months away, on Flamerule 1, and Jandar already has the menu planned. He regards it as the most important project of his career, and he is set to prepare it in a cooking pavilion on the estate's lawn (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

**Personal goals.** Jandar wants his aunt's cookshop back `[design]`. It has become a fishmonger's since, and Jandar walks past the shutters on his one free afternoon each tenday and has twice priced the rent `[design]`. He means to cook a single plain noon service there at her old prices, with the debt closed behind him `[design]`. He also wants to match her fish soup `[design]`. He has tried to reproduce it for twenty years without success, and he suspects that the missing taste comes from something he never saw her add `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who meet him, Jandar Chergoba is the exacting tiefling chef who runs the Cassalanter kitchen like a brigade. He speaks to his staff in clipped instructions, tastes everything before he answers a question and rarely looks up from what he is preparing. He does not welcome guests in this part of the house, and he tells them that guests eat in the hall.

Fiorella, his cook's assistant, has noticed that he grows anxious before certain nights and that he prepares food for private events that she is never invited to attend. Guests who eat at the villa remember his food long after they leave.

## Connections

- **Victoro Cassalanter:** The lord of the house, who holds Jandar's 3,000 gp obligation and leads the worship that Jandar attends.
- **Ammalia Cassalanter:** The lady of the house, who pours the wine at the rites and is the architect of the Founders' Day feast that Jandar cooks.
- **Willifort Crowelle**, **Tissina Khyret** and **Laiba "Nana" Rosse:** Fellow cult members, who keep order at the rites beside Jandar.
- **Fiorella:** His cook's assistant, who is not in the cult. She has noticed his anxiety before certain nights.
- **His six chefs:** Human commoners who work under him in the kitchen.
- **His aunt:** Dead, and the owner of the cookshop whose lease he lost. Her rule about the clean plate is the one he runs his kitchen by `[design]`.

## Overview

Jandar Chergoba is a tiefling and the head chef of Cassalanter Villa, where he plans every day's menu. He runs the kitchen with the discipline of a general and keeps six chefs working under him. Guests remember his food long after they leave, and he does not welcome visitors in his kitchen.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Jandar should inspire the professional respect any accomplished chef earns; shock when ten knives rise from the counters; and cold realization that the Founders' Day menu is already planned.

**Emotions.** Jandar most often feels rigorous professional pride in the kitchen's standards, theological certainty carried without drama, the specific pleasure of a plan executed flawlessly, controlled urgency on the days that matter, and contempt for anyone who asks about the menu without understanding what the menu serves.

**Motivations.** Jandar wants to run the kitchen to his exacting standards, prepare the Founders' Day feast exactly as planned, and serve Asmodeus through the craft that brought him to the Cassalanters' attention.

**Inspirations.** When playing Jandar, channel Gordon Ramsay (*Hell's Kitchen*, precision without warmth), Anthony Bourdain (the seriousness of craft), and Hannibal Lecter (*Hannibal*, the disconcerting chef in a domestic context).

### Voice

*A precise, exacting tiefling head chef who runs his kitchen like a war and plans the Founders' Day menu like a campaign.*

- **Sound:** A sharp, clipped, commanding voice pitched to carry over a kitchen.
- **Sentence shape:** Terse kitchen commands and precise culinary judgements. Among the cult: calm, cold certainty.
- **Word choice:** Kitchen military: "service", "stations", "mise en place", "the menu". He calls anyone who wanders into his kitchen "pests".
- **Swearing:** *Punctuation · Colourful · Tirade.* Kitchen-grade filth at his staff ("Who plated this? A blind fucking goat?"). Never in front of guests. Tirade: "Who plated this? You under-seasoned, over-reduced, lumpy-sauced, cold-plated, knife-dropping, onion-weeping, pan-scorching cunt of a commis, get out of my kitchen and take your shame with you!"
- **Personality in speech:** Professional pride and contempt. Anyone who asks about the menu without understanding it gets a withering answer. Publicly, the Founders' Day feast is simply his masterpiece.
- **Quirks:** He tastes everything before answering. He keeps a command word that raises the kitchen's knives.
- **Signature phrases:** "Service!" / "The menu is not your concern." / "Pests."
- **Never:** He never serves a flawed dish, never discusses the feast's purpose, and never lets anyone in the kitchen uninvited.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Out of my kitchen. Guests eat in the hall."
  - *Business:* "The Founders' Day menu is the finest work of my life. It is not your concern."
  - *Under pressure:* "Pests." (when he judges someone a threat, the knives rise at his word)

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Jandar runs the Cassalanter kitchen with the efficiency of a general who has never had to reconsider a campaign. To those who threaten the kitchen, a single word raises every knife in the room. Deep down, the Founders' Day feast is the most important project of his career, and he has approached it with the same precision he brings to everything else.

**Morale.** In a fight, Jandar speaks the word "For'zaal" (raising ten flying swords) and retreats to a defensible position. He does not abandon the kitchen before Founders' Day.

**Relationships.** Jandar is the Cassalanter Villa's head chef, a cult fanatic, the keeper of golden sacrificial implements hidden under a sack of potatoes, and the person who determines whether the Founders' Day poison is distributed correctly.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The cult:** Jandar is one of the six cult members on the household staff. He keeps order at the rites beside Willifort Crowelle, Tissina Khyret and Nana Rosse, and his regalia lies in his own locked iron box in the pantry, under a sack of potatoes. A golden key opens the box.
> - **The debt:** Victoro holds a 3,000 gp obligation over Jandar from a failed restaurant venture, and it converted into cult service three years ago. Ten of the household's sixteen named staff know nothing about the temple.
> - **His task:** On Founders' Day, Flamerule 1, Jandar prepares the feast in a cooking pavilion on the lawn, and he is the person assigned to see that the poison is distributed correctly.
> - **Who knows:** Nobody outside the household and the cult knows any of this, and the rest of Waterdeep only suspects the family. Jarlaxle is the one exception, through **Vessa**, and he tells no member of Bregan D'aerthe who has not worked it out.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with the exacting, slightly dry courtesy of a professional kitchen in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Jandar's craft as real and his resentment of the ritual meals as private, and does not play the chef for a joke.
