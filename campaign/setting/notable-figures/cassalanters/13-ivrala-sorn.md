**Ivrala Sorn** (Neutral evil, Chondathan human, she/her)

## Background

Ivrala Sorn is a Chondathan human woman of about forty, with dark hair silvered at the temples. She dresses practically but well, and she has the composure of someone trained to be unobtrusive. She names the Neverember and Adarbrent households among her former employers and the Cassalanter household as her most recent one, for four years. She reports to **Willifort Crowelle** as a social operative, and the Cassalanters plan to put her forward for a server post at Trollskull Manor, because the floor of a taproom is where the real conversations happen.

Ivrala grew up in a Chondathan river port, where her mother kept the linen room of a merchant house `[design]`. From the age of nine she carried trays at the house's dinners, and she learned that people say what they like in front of someone who is holding a tray `[design]`. She kept what she heard in her head, because a servant with a notebook is a servant who gets searched `[design]`. When she was nineteen the merchant house failed, and her mother was put out with a month's wages `[design]`. Ivrala took the house's reference and went north to find service in Waterdeep, and she has sent money to her mother ever since `[design]`.

In the Cassalanter household she served as a person who is meant to be forgotten, and she did it well `[design]`. Willifort noticed in her second year that she never mislaid a guest's name or habits, and he gave her a second duty beside the first `[design]`. She keeps the habit of listening because it has served her since childhood, and she takes real pride in the service itself `[design]`. After years in quiet houses she finds a taproom loud and shallow, and she hides the opinion `[design]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Ivrala holds no post at Trollskull Manor. She is one of the server candidates listed in **Staff and Hiring**.

**Personal goals.** Ivrala wants her mother settled in a small house of her own with a garden and a stove that draws well `[design]`. Her mother lives in a rented room in a river town and writes twice a year, and Ivrala sets aside part of every wage toward the house `[design]`. She also wants a post that nobody reassigns her from `[design]`. Her words at an interview are "I would like something steady", and she means a room of her own with a window over a street, where she can sit without anyone counting what she sees `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people, Ivrala Sorn is a composed, reliable server who arrives on time, keeps orders correct and remembers what patrons prefer. She is easy to overlook, and she meets a person's eyes directly when she speaks. Those who watch her closely notice that she knows where a room's exits and stairs are before she has looked at the people in it.

## Connections

Ivrala answers to **Willifort Crowelle**, as does **Rella Asp**, the household's other operative, under a cook's cover. **Sister Valdra** has seen her face at the Cassalanter villa and can recognize her. Her mother in the river town is the one person she writes to `[design]`.

## Overview

Ivrala Sorn is a composed woman of about forty with dark hair silvered at the temples. She has years of household service and is looking for steady work as a server. In service she keeps orders correct and remembers what patrons like.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Ivrala should inspire pleasant initial impression with her reliable professionalism; unsettled recognition in anyone who has seen her face at a Cassalanter function; and quiet dread in anyone who learns she is working for the villa.

**Emotions.** Ivrala most often feels professional neutrality, the specific watchfulness of a server who is also counting, controlled patience, mild contempt for the tavern work that is well-hidden, and a cool assessment of every patron she serves.

**Motivations.** Ivrala wants to collect patron and faction intelligence on behalf of the Cassalanters, maintain the server cover, and pass regular reports to Willifort Crowelle.

**Inspirations.** When playing Ivrala, channel Tatiana Maslany playing a sleeper operative (*Orphan Black*), Peggy Olson (*Mad Men*, the professional who is always watching), and any spy whose cover is impeccable because she is genuinely good at the job.

### Voice

*A reliable, forgettable server who remembers everyone's preferences, and their factions.*

- **Sound:** A neutral, efficient, pleasant voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, correct service talk.
- **Word choice:** Service basics: orders, tables, "anything else?".
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Never at work.
- **Personality in speech:** Professional neutrality. Her watchfulness shows only in how well she remembers. Mild contempt for the work is well hidden.
- **Quirks:** She gets every order right the first time. She goes still if someone who has seen her at a Cassalanter function walks in.
- **Signature phrases:** "Anything else?" / "Same as last time?"
- **Never:** She never draws attention, never slips, and never acknowledges anyone who recognizes her.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Same as last time? Cider and the pie?"
  - *Business:* "The gentleman in blue comes Thursdays. Sits with the half-elf. Anything else?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Excuse me, I'm needed in the kitchen."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Ivrala Sorn is a reliable, professional server who arrives on time, keeps orders correct, and remembers patron preferences. To those who know what they are looking at, she is a Cassalanter operative who reports to Willifort Crowelle on who visits a room, what they discuss and whom they trust. Deep down, Sister Valdra has seen her face in Cassalanter Villa's staff quarters, and she would recognize it.

**Morale.** In a fight, Ivrala exits immediately and sends a report. She does not fight.

**Relationships.** Ivrala is a Cassalanter intelligence operative put forward as a Trollskull Manor server candidate, a person who reports to Willifort Crowelle on what she hears where she serves, recognizable to Sister Valdra on a DC 13 Perception check when both are present, and a staff candidate in the **Trollskull Manor** guide.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The trade:** Ivrala is a Cassalanter social operative under a server's cover. She reports to Willifort Crowelle and has no brief to harm anyone. Her work is to learn who visits, what they discuss and whom they trust.
> - **The records:** The household's records list her departure as a "temporary reassignment", not completed service. A reference check that looks closely enough finds that wording.
> - **Who knows:** Sister Valdra can recognize her face. No source says that Valdra knows her work or that anyone outside the household and its network does.
> - **What she knows:** No source says whether she belongs to the household's staff, to the cult or only to its intelligence network, or whether she knows of the pact. The page states nothing about it, and every other page treats the Cassalanters with suspicion only.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a Personality and an Overview that show only the server. The narrator treats her competence as real and does not wink at her other work.
