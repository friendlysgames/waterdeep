**Arn Xalrondar** (Lawful evil, Tethyrian human, he/him)

## Background

Arn Xalrondar is a Tethyrian human man and a cult fanatic of Asmodeus, and he is Seffia Naelryke's operational partner on the Cassalanters' field team (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations). The household roster counts him among the villa's sixteen named staff and among its six cult members, yet he lives with Seffia on the upper floor of the Converted Windmill on Coachlamp Lane in the Southern Ward, where they dress plainly and pass as ordinary tenants (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations; **Southern Ward**, Gazetteer). He handles field security and outpost logistics for the team `[research]`. His Book of Worship, a prayer text with his name and the Windmill's address written inside the back cover, lies under a cot in the Asmodean Shrine on Aveen Street (**The Cassalanters**, Factions Guide).

Arn grew up in a hill town on a Tethyrian trade road, the son of a carter who hauled grain and cloth between the river towns `[design]`. He rode the wagon box from the time he could hold the reins, and he learned his letters late and slowly, from a guard sergeant's loading lists `[design]`. At sixteen he signed on as a hand with a caravan company, and the sergeant who commanded its guards took him in, taught him to stand a watch and told him what to do each hour of the day `[design]`. Arn liked that arrangement more than he had liked anything at home `[design]`.

In his third season, bandits killed the sergeant on a hill road with the first volley, and Arn, with no one left to give orders, cut the lead wagons loose and drove the rest out under the arrows `[design]`. Two drivers died, and the company owner praised his quick thinking and offered him the sergeant's post `[design]`. Arn refused it that autumn and left the road for Waterdeep, because he did not want to be the man whose orders the next driver would die under `[design]`.

In Waterdeep he took work as a door guard at a Dock Ward freight house, where he kept the yard locked, carried loads without comment and followed every instruction exactly `[design]`. Seffia knew the house from her courier years, and she noticed that Arn did what he was told, said nothing about it afterward and stayed where he was put `[design]`. When Victoro gave her the field team, she asked for Arn first, and she later brought him to the cult's gatherings, where he took the faith the way he had taken the sergeant's orders, as the word of someone he trusted `[design]`.

Victoro later sent the pair to take Eye #2 of the Stone of Golorr from the Brandath Crypts, where an infernal divination had placed it, and to silence three cult members who knew too much about it (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations). The family now keeps the Eye secured in the temple (**The Cassalanters**, Villains). Arn and Seffia killed two of the three, Holiver Tornrudder and Kaeth Warloon, and the third, Vaelle Lurval, survived (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Victoro does not know that Vaelle lives (**Victoro Cassalanter**; **Southern Ward**, Gazetteer). Seffia keeps the matter from every ear but Arn's, and Arn has spoken of it to no one and checks the apartment door more often than he used to `[design]`.

Since then Arn has gone on much as before. A Cassalanter steward, Brimel Crestfall, has carried baskets of candles, salt, black wax and wine to the second landing of the stair for years, and Arn takes them up the rest of the way `[design]` (**Southern Ward**, Gazetteer). Ammalia Cassalanter visits twice a month, alone, in a hired cab with the curtains drawn, and the family pays the upper floor's rent under a false name (**Southern Ward**, Gazetteer). Seffia has lately begun to leave small decisions to him when she goes out, and she does not always like what she finds when she comes back `[design]`. Arn does not know why she does it, and he fears that it means she is tired of him `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Arn wants to read a full page without moving his lips `[design]`. He practices on a primer that he keeps under his pillow and on the prayers in his Book of Worship, which he copied out in his own careful hand `[design]`. He also sends a share of every payment south with the carters of the trade road to the sergeant's widow, who raised a daughter on a guard's pension that the company stopped paying `[design]`. He wants the daughter to learn a trade that keeps her off the roads, and he has never told the widow where the coin comes from `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who deal with him, Arn Xalrondar is a quiet, civil Tethyrian man in plain dress who lives above the old windmill on Coachlamp Lane. He speaks in an earnest, slightly eager voice, agrees with Seffia quickly and glances toward her before he answers a question.

When Seffia is out, he turns callers away with a few polite words and keeps the door shut. Those who watch him with her see that he completes her half-sentences and does not argue with her.

## Connections

- **Seffia Naelryke:** Leads the field team and shares the Windmill with him. Arn defers to her on planning, covers her in a fight and acts on his own when she is out of the room.
- **Victoro Cassalanter:** The patron who sent the pair after Eye #2. Seffia answers to him and Arn follows her orders. Victoro does not know that the third witness lived.
- **Ammalia Cassalanter:** Visits the Windmill twice a month, alone, in a hired cab with the curtains drawn.
- **Vaelle Lurval:** The witness who survived. Arn and Seffia killed the other two, Holiver Tornrudder and Kaeth Warloon.
- **Brimel Crestfall:** A Cassalanter steward who leaves the baskets on the second landing, which Arn carries up the rest of the way `[design]`.
- **His old sergeant:** The guard sergeant who taught him his letters and died on a hill road in Tethyr. His widow still receives coin from Arn `[design]`.

## Overview

Arn Xalrondar is a Tethyrian human man who lives on the upper floor of a decrepit windmill on Coachlamp Lane. He shares the floor with Seffia Naelryke and defers to her on anything that needs planning. He is quiet and polite and does not draw attention to himself.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Arn should inspire wariness at how completely he defers to Seffia; cold focus when he is left to make the call himself; and a specific kind of compliance that becomes initiative at the worst moment.

**Emotions.** Arn most often feels subordinate devotion to Seffia's operational judgment, theological certainty as bedrock, the particular focus of someone who does what the mission requires without auditing it, and quiet pride in the decisions he makes alone under pressure.

**Motivations.** Arn wants to do what the mission requires, keep the Windmill operational, and trust Seffia's judgment on everything except the moments she is not in the room.

**Inspirations.** When playing Arn, channel Draco Malfoy (*Harry Potter*, the true believer within a hierarchy), Gaston (*Beauty and the Beast*, the follower who becomes a problem when given a clear target), and any subordinate who is more dangerous than the person giving orders.

### Voice

*A deferential follower who becomes dangerous the moment he's left to make the call.*

- **Sound:** A quiet, earnest, slightly eager voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, deferential agreements, then, alone and under pressure, a sudden, firm decision.
- **Word choice:** Deference ("Seffia says"), and plain certainty when he acts.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Nervous curses, until he decides.
- **Personality in speech:** Subordinate devotion that turns into initiative. He's proud of the calls he makes alone and won't be talked out of them.
- **Quirks:** He checks for Seffia before answering.
- **Signature phrases:** "Seffia says." / "The mission required it." / "I made the right call."
- **Never:** He never questions Seffia to her face, never regrets a decision once he has made it, and never speaks of his faith to outsiders.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Seffia's not here. You should go."
  - *Business:* "The mission required it. I made the right call."
  - *Under pressure:* "Shit. Shit. No, I've got this. I've decided."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Arn Xalrondar sleeps at the Windmill with Seffia and defers to her judgment on everything that involves planning. In truth, his Book of Worship, kept under a cot in the Asmodean Shrine, gives the Windmill's address inside the back cover and names the Windmill as the place of assembly. Deep down, he believes that whatever the mission requires is right, and when Seffia is not there to decide, he decides in her place and will not be talked out of it.

**Morale.** In a fight, Arn covers Seffia's flanks and does not retreat unless Seffia orders it.

**Relationships.** Arn is the Cassalanter Converted Windmill co-occupant, Seffia Naelryke's operational partner, the cultist whose Book of Worship links directly to the Windmill address, and the man who acts on his own when Seffia is out of the room.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The cult:** Arn is an Asmodean cult fanatic in the Cassalanters' service, and the Windmill is an Asmodean shrine and a fallback site for the cult. Nobody outside the household and the cult knows this, and the rest of Waterdeep only suspects the family.
> - **The mission:** Lord Cassalanter sent him and Seffia to retrieve Eye #2 of the Stone of Golorr from the Brandath Crypts and to silence three cult members who knew too much about it. They killed two of the three.
> - **The failure:** The survivor, Vaelle Lurval, is the loose end that Seffia is working to close, and Lord Cassalanter does not know the mission failed. Arn knows, and he tells no one.
> - **The book:** His Book of Worship names him and gives the Windmill's address, and it lies in the Asmodean Shrine on Aveen Street.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a Personality and an Overview that show only a quiet tenant. The narrator treats Arn's loyalty as sincere and lets the danger in him come from what he does when he is alone.
