**Rella Asp** (Lawful evil, Tethyrian human, she/her)

## Background

Rella Asp is a Tethyrian human woman with dark hair, pale skin and the slim, unhurried hands of someone who works on fine detail. She has worked for the Cassalanters for three years, and the guild records list her as the household's cook for that time. Lord Victoro values her discretion and her skill, and she reports to **Willifort Crowelle**. She is a poisoner who keeps a cook's cover as her public identity, and she poisons no one who is not on her list. The cover is real work that she does well, and she carries references from three reputable establishments, all of which confirm her excellence, in a small leather portfolio organized by date.

Rella grew up in a river town in Tethyr where her mother cooked at a coaching inn, and at ten she was set to the inn's stillroom to dry herbs and weigh spice by the pinch `[design]`. At fourteen she was apprenticed to a widowed cook who had once served a noble household in Calimshan `[design]`. The old cook taught her which herbs sweeten a sauce and which ones kill, and she made Rella learn both with equal care `[design]`. She also taught her a herbed terrine of her own, which Rella still makes `[design]`. The old cook died when Rella was twenty, and Rella inherited her knife roll and a notebook of recipes that gives amounts and no method `[design]`.

After that Rella cooked in three kitchens, one after another, in good houses and inns along the roads of Tethyr and the North, and each gave her a letter of reference when she left `[design]`. A Cassalanter steward ate her terrine in the third of them and offered her the villa's post `[design]`. In her first season Willifort explained the other half of the work to her, and the rule that she cooks and acts only from the list that he gives her `[design]`. She has kept to the rule since, and Victoro hears of her only through Willifort's reports `[design]`. In the villa she reads the guest lists and the supply orders that pass through the kitchen, because a cook sees more of a house's visitors than most of its family does `[design]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Rella is in the household's service with her portfolio kept ready. A cook with good references can be placed in any kitchen that the household wants to see into, and Willifort decides where she goes `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Rella wants to make her teacher's terrine exactly as the old cook made it `[design]`. The notebook has no method, and every batch that she makes comes out a little different, so she keeps a slice of each and writes down what changed `[design]`. She also wants a kitchen of her own with her name painted over the door, in a river town like the one where she grew up `[design]`. She sets aside coin from every wage toward it, and during long services she plans the dining room in her head `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people, Rella Asp is an accomplished cook who arrives with excellent references, treats the kitchen with professional seriousness and produces food that patrons remember. She asks polite, specific questions about who is dining and who is expected tomorrow, and she reads the reservation ledger while she talks. Those who watch her closely notice that her interest in who orders what and who dines with whom goes beyond a cook's curiosity.

## Connections

Rella answers to **Willifort Crowelle**, as does **Ivrala Sorn**, the household's other operative under a server's cover. **Victoro Cassalanter** values her discretion and her skill. Her teacher, an old cook, died years ago, and Rella still cooks from the notebook that she left `[design]`.

## Overview

Rella Asp is a precise, elegant cook with dark hair, pale skin and slim hands. She carries a leather portfolio of references from three reputable establishments, all of which praise her work. Those who have eaten her food remember it.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Rella should inspire initial warmth with her impressive culinary credentials; growing unease as her interest in patron identities exceeds any culinary curiosity; and cold horror in anyone who learns what else she does for the household.

**Emotions.** Rella most often feels professional focus, the calm of someone who has done this before, a cook's genuine pride in the cover work, controlled patience, and the specific discipline of someone who does not rush.

**Motivations.** Rella wants to collect intelligence on the regular patrons of the kitchens where she works, stay employed and inconspicuous, and carry out the household's orders cleanly when Willifort gives them.

**Inspirations.** When playing Rella, channel Amy Dunne (*Gone Girl*, the controlled, patient professional), Mrs. Lovett (*Sweeney Todd*, the cook with a sideline), and Nurse Ratched (*One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest*, the uncanny competence).

### Voice

*An accomplished cook with glowing references and a quiet interest in the reservation book.*

- **Sound:** A pleasant, professional, calm voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Composed, competent kitchen talk, with polite, curious questions about the guests.
- **Word choice:** Culinary credentials and service: "the reservation ledger", "who's dining", dishes by name.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* None on shift. She's too disciplined.
- **Personality in speech:** Professional focus and patience. Her interest in who dines with whom goes beyond any cook's curiosity, and it shows in how specific her questions are.
- **Quirks:** She reads the reservation ledger while talking. She asks who is expected tomorrow.
- **Signature phrases:** "Who's expected tomorrow?" / "The fish is excellent tonight." / "Leave the kitchen to me."
- **Never:** She never rushes, never cooks badly, and never reveals her other work.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Rella Asp. Master Cook. My references are on the table."
  - *Business:* "Who's expected this tenday? I like to plan the menu around the guests."
  - *Under pressure:* "I'm just the cook. Let me finish service."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Rella Asp is an accomplished Master Cook who arrives with excellent references, treats the kitchen with professional seriousness, and produces food that patrons remember. To those who observe carefully, her interest in who orders what and who dines with whom exceeds any normal culinary curiosity. Deep down, she watches the reservation ledger for the names that the household wants to know about, and she reports every one of them to Willifort.

**Morale.** In a fight, Rella uses her kitchen knowledge to improvise and exits through the kitchen if available. She is not paid to fight.

**Relationships.** Rella Asp is a Cassalanter operative who works under a cook's cover, a poisoner who acts on the household's orders, and an intelligence collector reporting to Willifort Crowelle.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The trade:** Rella is a poisoner under a cook's cover. She works for the Cassalanter household, reports to Willifort Crowelle and poisons only the people on the list that he gives her.
> - **The records:** The guild records list her as the household's cook for the past three years, which is true as far as it goes, and ordinary inquiry does not expose her.
> - **Who knows:** No source names anyone outside the household who knows her trade.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a Personality and an Overview that show only the cook. The narrator treats her cooking as real work and does not wink at her other trade.
