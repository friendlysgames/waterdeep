**Caladorn Cassalanter** (Lawful good, Tethyrian human, he/him)

## Background

Caladorn Cassalanter was a Tethyrian human and the third son of Lord Ohrl and Lady Sylull Cassalanter (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Caladorn Cassalanter*, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep* and *Noble Houses of Waterdeep*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. The house stood on Waterdeep's noble registry and made its money in banking (**The Cassalanters**). The wiki says that he stopped using the family name until he had done something worthy of it, and that his father expected him never to judge himself worthy, which left him the benefits of his birth and none of its duties (same page) `[research: wiki only]`.

The wiki describes a fighter, broad-shouldered and six feet tall, clean-shaven, with a cleft chin and short dark red or brown hair (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. The portrait in the villa shows him silver-haired, so the wiki's hair color most likely belongs to his younger years `[research]`. He was perceptive and intelligent, he acted with purpose and took a cynical view of people, and he loved to have fun in private. Those who knew him called him the epitome of the Knight Bachelor (same page) `[research: wiki only]`.

He sat among the Lords of Waterdeep as a Masked Lord and was known as a hero of the North (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki adds that people who did not know his post took him for a fighting instructor at the Fields of Triumph, and that he was keenly interested in the city's navy (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. The novel *Tangled Webs* puts him aboard a sealing ship in the Sea of Moving Ice, where the crew found a ship full of pickled sea elves in barrels marked for Ruathym. Another ship then scuttled his and marooned the crew until help reached them (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Caladorn Cassalanter*, citing *Tangled Webs*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Later he courted a woman and meant to marry her. She belonged to the Knights of the Shield, and she tried to poison him when she learned that he was a Lord. He left the Lords and the city for two years and traveled the Sword Coast, and when he came back he involved himself with the navy (same page, citing *Elfsong* and *City of Splendors (boxed set)*; editions not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

After that he married a woman who worshipped Siamorphe `[design]`. The temple beneath the villa belonged to the goddess in those years (**Siamorphe**), and his wife's image of her stood in it, open-armed and welcoming (**Siamorphe**). He knelt before it beside his wife on feast days, and he took her faith as his own and devoted his life to it `[design]`. Their son Victoro grew up in the counting house while Caladorn spent long seasons in the North `[design]`. Once a ship's captain whose loan Caladorn had stood surety for lost his vessel, and Caladorn paid the debt from the house's plate and two of its warehouses, so that no creditor could say a Masked Lord had defaulted `[design]`. He was proud of it and never asked what it had cost the counting house `[design]`.

Caladorn is dead on Ches 1, 1492 DR. He lies in the family crypt below the villa with his plate armor and a mace of disruption (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Caladorn Cassalanter*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`, and the published adventure says that his bones have turned to dust and the armor remains (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). His ghost holds the crypt, where it attacks anything with a fiendish taint and helps anyone who shows a holy symbol of Siamorphe, and the family has tried to reach it twice and retreated both times (**Victoro Cassalanter**). Waterdeep still counts him a hero of the North, and the family shows his portrait as a sign of civic virtue (**The Cassalanters**). No file gives the year or the cause of his death `[research]`.

**Personal goals.** Caladorn wants to tell Victoro one thing that he never said in life: that the plate and the warehouses were his to lose, and the boy owed nothing for them `[design]`. Victoro grew up reading the ledgers that showed the cost, and Caladorn has never had the chance to answer the lesson that his son drew from them `[design]`. He also wants to say the name Cassalanter without a pause `[design]`. In life he set the name aside until he had earned it, and as a ghost he stops before it, because he cannot say whether the house he left still deserves it `[design]`. Last, he wants to hear the harbor `[design]`. From the villa's upper rooms in the Sea Ward he heard the ship bells at dawn and judged the fleet by them, and the crypt gives him no sound but the sarcophagi settling `[design]`.

## Personality

To most of Waterdeep, Caladorn Cassalanter is a dead hero of the North and a former Masked Lord, remembered as a fighter of the old school. His portrait hangs near the stair in the villa's entrance hall, silver-haired and armored, and it shows the Cassalanter features and the bearing of a man who carried a weapon all his life.

His ghost keeps that dignity. He speaks in formal, complete sentences and rests a hand on the family sword, and his voice stays steady until he names his family.

## Connections

- **Victoro Cassalanter:** His son and only living heir. Caladorn cannot reach him, and he pauses before the name.
- **Ammalia Cassalanter:** Victoro's wife. Caladorn pauses before her name as well, and he does not want revenge on her.
- **His wife:** Unnamed in every file. Her image of Siamorphe stood in the temple before the family recast it as the Asmodeus statue. No file says whether she is Victoro's mother, whether she is living or where she is buried `[research]`.
- **Osvaldo Cassalanter**, **Terenzio Cassalanter** and **Elzerina Cassalanter:** Victoro's children, and so Caladorn's grandchildren `[research]`. No file says whether he knew them in life.
- **Lord Ohrl Cassalanter and Lady Sylull Cassalanter:** His parents, named only by the wiki `[research: wiki only]`.
- **Siamorphe:** The goddess he served. The plaque in his crypt calls him her servant in his time.
- **The Cassalanters:** The house he left. The family tries to reach his crypt and retreats, and it uses his portrait as civic virtue.
- **Asmodeus:** The god whom his family now serves. Caladorn's ghost attacks anyone marked by that worship.

## Overview

Caladorn Cassalanter was Victoro Cassalanter's father, a former Masked Lord and a hero of the North. He is dead, and his portrait hangs near the stair in the villa's entrance hall, silver-haired and armored. The family still shows that portrait as a sign of civic virtue.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Caladorn should inspire immediate pity for anyone who understands what he has been watching from A4a since his death, admiration when his dignity holds despite everything his family has become, and a specific grief when his voice breaks speaking their names.

**Emotions.** Caladorn most often feels steadfast conviction in a duty that has outlasted death, grief that has deepened undisturbed since his death, controlled anguish when his family is mentioned or visible evidence of their corruption surrounds him, and a warmth, readable as relief rather than welcome, toward anyone who carries the Siamorphe holy symbol.

**Motivations.** Caladorn wants to see the infernal corruption of his house ended, the effigy in A7 destroyed, and some portion of the honor his family carried for generations restored to the name he left behind. He does not want revenge on Victoro and Ammalia. He wants the bargain broken.

**Inspirations.** When playing Caladorn, channel King Hamlet (*Hamlet*), Ned Stark (*Game of Thrones*), and Jacob Marley (*A Christmas Carol*).

### Voice

*The ghost of the family's honour: dignified, grieving, and steady until he speaks their names.*

- **Sound:** A deep, measured, noble voice, hollow with death. It breaks when he names his family.
- **Sentence shape:** Formal, deliberate, complete sentences, with no contractions (a deliberate break from `ember-voice` speech). He states exactly what he asks and offers.
- **Word choice:** Chivalric and grave: honour, duty, "my house", "the bargain". Siamorphe is "my wife's goddess". He never says the names Victoro and Ammalia without a pause.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* His grief is heavier than any curse.
- **Personality in speech:** Steadfast conviction and deep grief. He wants the bargain broken, not revenge, and says so. Relief at the Siamorphe symbol shows as warmth.
- **Quirks:** He rests a hand on the family sword as he speaks. He falls silent mid-sentence facing the effigy's direction.
- **Signature phrases:** "Break the bargain." / "My house has fallen far." / "I do not ask for vengeance."
- **Never:** He never asks for revenge, never lies, and never leaves A4a.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You carry her symbol. Then I may speak. Hear me, for I have waited long for someone to."
  - *Business:* "Destroy the effigy below. Break the bargain. I do not ask for vengeance on Victoro and, on Ammalia. Only that it ends."
  - *Under pressure:* "Go, now. This house devours what it touches."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Caladorn is a dead hero of the North whose portrait hangs prominently in the entrance hall near the stair, silver-haired and armored; the family uses his image as civic virtue while remaining unable to reach him. To anyone who reaches A4a, his ghost is an armored figure with silver hair and the Cassalanter features, carrying the family sword, voice steady until it is not. To anyone who carries a Siamorphe holy symbol, he is lucid and measured. Deep down, they turned his wife's goddess into the statue in A7, and his crypt lies just off the temple's entry.

**Morale.** Caladorn attacks any creature bearing fiendish taint on sight, a restriction that has forced the Cassalanters to retreat from A4a twice. Against creatures who present the Siamorphe holy symbol, he manifests peacefully.

**Relationships.** Caladorn is the ghost of Victoro Cassalanter's father, a former Masked Lord and hero of the North who devoted his life to Siamorphe, and the former owner of a +1 plate armor and a mace of disruption that lie in his crypt.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **What the ghost knows:** He knows that his family has turned to Asmodeus, and he knows that the statue in A7 was his wife's image of Siamorphe before the family defaced it. He does not know the contract's terms, the children's souls or the Founders' Day plan.
> - **Who knows the pact:** Nobody outside the household and the cult. Everyone else suspects the family because it is too nice to be this rich, and Jarlaxle alone knows more, through **Vessa**.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with dignity in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Caladorn's grief as real, and the page shows him as a dead man, a portrait and a family memory.
