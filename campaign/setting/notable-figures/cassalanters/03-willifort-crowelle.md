**Willifort Crowelle** (Neutral, doppelganger, he/him)

## Background

Willifort Crowelle is a doppelganger who has served the Cassalanter household for eight years `[research]`. He presents as an aging male tiefling in a sharp black suit and thin-rimmed spectacles, and he holds the posts of chief butler and Lord Victoro's personal attendant (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He carries keys to every room alongside Victoro and Ammalia, and he reports to Ammalia on everything that happens in the villa (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations). No source records where he was born, what his true form looks like or whose face the tiefling guise copies `[research]`.

Willifort does not know where he was born, and he stopped looking for the answer long ago `[design]`. His earliest memories are of copying the people nearest to him, first a carter's boy on the Trade Way and later a notary's clerk, because a child who looks like the household is fed by it `[design]`. He learned that a shapechanger who holds one shape for years is trusted and one who changes often is hunted, so he made a habit of staying where he was `[design]`.

For eleven years he lived as the steward of a shipping factor in a southern port, and he ran the factor's house better than the factor's own family could `[design]`. The factor's widowed sister kept the house keys, and she was the only person who saw his true face and said nothing of it `[design]`. She gave him a name of his own that no one else ever used `[design]`. She died of a winter fever, and the factor's heirs dismissed the steward within the month with a reference and a week's wages `[design]`. He has not said the name aloud since, and some years ago he found that he could no longer recall the sound of it `[design]`. He has shown his true shape only to people he trusts ever since `[design]`.

Eight years ago he took a place with the Cassalanters `[research]`. He chose the tiefling guise because the household was known to be warm toward tieflings, and he chose an aging one because nobody asks an old servant where he came from `[design]`. A personal attendant is one of the few servants whom the family keeps on while it replaces the rest regularly (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and Willifort made himself useful to Lord Victoro so early that nobody has ever had cause to replace him `[design]`.

He was in the house when the family's banking failed about 1489 DR (**A Debt in Blood**), and he was the first servant to notice which books Ammalia had begun to read in her study `[design]`. Willifort had never had a first name or a face that was his own, and in Asmodeus he found a power that gave every being a rank, a duty and a term of service `[design]`. He took up the faith as readily as he had taken up the butler's post, and he became a full member of the cult once the temple beneath the villa was given to the god `[design]`. He has kept order at its rites since, and his regalia lies in a lockbox that he shares with Tissina Khyret and that a golden key held by each of them will open (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). His loyalty to the family and his devotion to Asmodeus run in the same direction on Ches 1, 1492 DR, and nothing in his life so far has made him choose between them `[design]`. He knows what is chained in the attic in room C24, and he has never spoken of it to anyone outside the cult (**Osvaldo Cassalanter**).

**Personal goals.** Willifort wants to stand once at the widow's grave in the southern port `[design]`. Each year he sends a letter and a few silver pieces to a gravekeeper there, who keeps the plot clear, and he has never had a free tenday to make the journey himself `[design]`. He is also teaching himself to bind books in the servants' hours, because he can copy a face in a moment and can gain a skill only through years of practice, and he keeps every poor binding on a shelf in his room `[design]`. His last want is a post that no one can take from him, since a dead woman's heirs once ended the only home he had known, and he guards his place in the household so that it cannot happen again `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who meet him, Willifort Crowelle is an aging tiefling butler in a sharp black suit and thin-rimmed spectacles. He greets every guest in the entrance hall and seems to know what each person needs before they ask. He uses no contractions, calls guests "sir" and "madam", and adjusts his spectacles just before he redirects a conversation.

When the family has visitors, Willifort escorts them through the villa for the whole visit and keeps them to the rooms that the family shows. A guest who asks about a closed wing is told that the family is not receiving there and is offered the gardens instead. He never hurries and never raises his voice, and he is as polite to a guest who is easy to fool as to one who is not, although his eyes show what he thinks of the first kind.

## Connections

- **Victoro Cassalanter:** The lord of the house and Willifort's employer. Willifort is his personal attendant and holds keys to every room alongside him, and Victoro leads the worship that Willifort attends.
- **Ammalia Cassalanter:** The person Willifort reports to on everything that happens in the villa. She makes the final calls for the family and the cult.
- **Tissina Khyret:** Ammalia's attendant and a fellow cult member. Their regalia is kept in one lockbox, and each holds a golden key to it.
- **Laiba "Nana" Rosse** and **Jandar Chergoba:** Fellow cult members, who keep order at the rites beside Willifort and Tissina.
- **Seffia Naelryke:** The family's other field operative. Her team is precise and his is blunt, and no source says which of them leads when the two work together `[research]`.
- **Rella Asp** and **Ivrala Sorn:** Cassalanter operatives under the covers of a cook and a server, who report to Willifort.
- **Osvaldo Cassalanter:** The eldest son, a chain devil in the attic. Willifort knows what is in room C24.
- **Maesta:** The housekeeper, who manages the non-cult staff. She has seen the shape of his hands change when he thought no one was looking, and she knows only that he is more than he appears `[research]`.
- **Hadra Stonebread:** A former household cook of twelve years who knows him on sight and avoids his notice. Willifort noticed the day she left.
- **The widow of the southern port:** The woman who saw his true face and gave him a name of his own. She died years ago, and he still pays for her grave to be kept `[design]`.

## Overview

Willifort Crowelle is the chief butler of the Cassalanter household and Lord Victoro's personal attendant. He is an aging tiefling in a sharp black suit and thin-rimmed spectacles. He greets every guest with impeccable courtesy and seems to know what each one needs before anyone asks. When the family has guests, he escorts them for the whole visit and guides them away from the private wings.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Willifort should inspire surface comfort with his impeccable butler's manner, growing unease for anyone who notices how much he knows, and visceral shock for anyone who sees what he is underneath.

**Emotions.** Willifort most often feels clinical detachment, possessive loyalty to the Cassalanters, controlled satisfaction in maintaining perfect cover, cold contempt for guests who are easy to deceive, and the alien patience of a creature that has been someone else for so long it has forgotten what it was.

**Motivations.** Willifort wants to protect the Cassalanter family and their secrets, maintain his cover as an aging tiefling butler, and handle problems permanently when discretion fails.

**Inspirations.** When playing Willifort, channel Alfred Pennyworth (*Batman*), Mystique (*X-Men*), and Kato (*The Green Hornet*).

### Voice

*The impeccable tiefling butler who knows exactly what every guest needs, and exactly where every door leads.*

- **Sound:** A dry, precise, elderly voice, perfectly correct. With the few who know what he is, flat and alien.
- **Sentence shape:** Formal, courteous butler's sentences with no contractions, deliberately (a break from `ember-voice` speech). He steers conversations with polite redirections.
- **Word choice:** Service formality: "sir", "madam", "if you would follow me", "the family". With the few who know what he is, cold and brief.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Not even with the few who know what he is. The creature underneath has no use for it.
- **Personality in speech:** Clinical detachment behind perfect service. He steers the nosy away from the wrong corridors with courtesy. Contempt for easily deceived guests never reaches his words, only his eyes.
- **Quirks:** He anticipates requests before they're made. He adjusts his spectacles before a redirection.
- **Signature phrases:** "If you would follow me." / "The family is not receiving in that wing." / "Very good, sir."
- **Never:** He never breaks cover in public, never hurries, and never reveals the family's business.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Good evening. The family is expecting you. If you would follow me."
  - *Business:* "The east wing is closed for renovation, madam. The gardens are this way."
  - *Under pressure (to someone who has wandered where they should not):* "You should not have come down here."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Willifort is an aging male tiefling in a sharp black suit and thin-rimmed spectacles who greets all guests and steers nosy ones away from cult business. To those he trusts, he is a doppelganger who has served the Cassalanters long enough that the role has become his identity. Deep down, he is the family's most trusted operative and most dangerous weapon, using disguise to burn bridges, gather intelligence, and solve problems permanently.

**Morale.** In a fight, Willifort orders imps and spined devils to attack while he secures the family's retreat. If someone confronts him directly, he fights with a doppelganger's deceptive shapeshifting. When discretion fails, the family draws on the blunter team that he leads.

**Relationships.** Willifort is the chief butler and Lord Victoro's personal attendant, a doppelganger who holds keys to every room alongside Victoro and Ammalia, and the Cassalanters' intelligence operative who reports to Ammalia on everything that transpires within the villa.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The cult:** Willifort is a full member of the Asmodean cult beneath the villa. He keeps order at the rites beside Tissina Khyret, Nana Rosse and Jandar Chergoba, and his regalia lies in the lockbox that he shares with Tissina.
> - **The pact:** He knows about the bargain with Asmodeus and the temple, and he is the one servant who knows what waits behind the attic door in room C24.
> - **What he is:** Only the people he trusts know that he is a doppelganger. Everyone else, including most of the household staff, takes him for an aging tiefling.
> - **Who else knows:** Nobody outside the household and the cult knows any of this. The rest of Waterdeep only suspects the family.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with the same formal courtesy in Personality and the Overview that Willifort shows a guest. The narrator treats his devotion and his loss as real and does not play him for a joke.
