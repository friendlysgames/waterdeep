**Ammalia Cassalanter** (Lawful evil, Tethyrian human, she/her)

## Background

Ammalia is a Tethyrian human `[research]`. She grew up in a trading family that moved goods between Tethyr and the Sword Coast `[design]`. Her mother kept a cabinet of pinned butterflies and taught her to name each one by species, and the Monarch, which flies the furthest, was the one she liked best `[design]`. When she was fourteen, her father lost the family's stock to a lender who held a contract with every clause in the lender's favor `[design]`. She had read the contract before he signed it and had told him what it meant, and he signed anyway because the lender was a friend `[design]`. She went to the next table as his interpreter, and from then on she set the terms first in any room she sat in `[design]`.

Ammalia was schooled in the arcane arts (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and no file says where or under whom. She trained in abjuration and conjuration `[design]`. She traveled with the family's caravans until she came to Waterdeep as their agent and asked the Cassalanter Counting House for credit `[design]`. She refused Victoro's first offer and named her own terms, and he agreed to most of them `[design]`. They married within the year, and a wedding portrait in the villa shows both of them smiling and carefree (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Osvaldo was born in 1480 DR and the twins on Flamerule 11, 1483 DR, both derived from the contract's terms (**A Debt in Blood**). She started the butterfly garden the spring after Osvaldo was born, with specimens imported from Tethyr, so that he would have butterflies in the house `[design]`.

The family faced bankruptcy about three years before the campaign opens. The remix ties the collapse to Dagult Neverember's embezzlement, which emptied the civic funds that Victoro's loans were underwritten against (**The Open Lord's Fall**). The bargain was made in 1489 DR, counted back from the published "three years ago". Ammalia found the way through infernal research. A pit fiend carried the contract to them, and they signed it together in the temple beneath their own villa. She began the research the winter the loans failed, in books on contracts and on devils that she bought for the library `[design]`. She read the terms three times before she showed them to Victoro, and she told him the price before she told him what it bought `[design]`. The grimoire in her study records the ritual, and its margins carry her notes, where "There must be another way" appears eleven times, in different inks (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)).

The contract claims each child at nine. Osvaldo was nine when he was taken and is twelve now, a chain devil held in the soundproofed attic of the villa under the watch of two imps in spider form. Ammalia visits weekly and sits in the room outside the pentagram, in a chair outside the shielding circle, and reads to him while he curses her name and pleads with her to save him. She reads him the stories he loved before the contract, in the order he used to ask for them `[design]`. Victoro has not entered the attic in months, and Willifort knows. The city hears that Osvaldo studies in a faraway city, and the twins have been told the same. Ammalia also smokes on the balcony, watching the ocean or the children in the garden below (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

The twins' souls are forfeit on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, ten days after Founders' Day. The buy-out clause asks for 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, both, paid to a devil of Asmodeus at the same time. The souls are the guests at the Founders' Day feast. On Flamerule 1 Ammalia means to invite one hundred of Waterdeep's poorest to the villa lawn and to serve ninety-nine of them wine laced with midnight tears, so that the guests die within a day and nothing connects them to the house. Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) is her operational window, chosen for the civic cover and for the time left to verify the terms. The contract falls due on Flamerule 11, ten days after Founders' Day, and its terms do not mention the feast. She keeps ten vials of midnight tears in her private study, and she is immune to poison (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Ammalia's charity in the Field Ward feeds hundreds, and her charity picks the guests for the feast. The family built the charitable reputation, the political connections and the public warmth in the three years since the bargain, and none of it is faked. Ammalia has a great deal of goodwill banked in Waterdeep, enough to curry favor with a magistrate (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Her estate has the most beautiful butterfly garden in the city, and she presents it as a conservation project, with birds rescued from the docks and butterflies bred from imported specimens. Six imps in raven form hang around the garden, and she uses them as spies and sends them on missions through the city (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Harpers have watched the family's charitable work for years and found nothing, and Ammalia knows they watch `[research]`.

**Personal goals.** Ammalia wants a butterfly named for her `[design]`. She has bred imported specimens in the garden since Osvaldo was born, and she has kept notes on one pale variety that appeared in the third generation `[design]`. She wants to publish a plate of it under her own name, and she has shown the drawings to no one `[design]`. She also wants to sit inside the shielding circle for the length of one story `[design]`. The circle keeps Osvaldo's chains from her and keeps her from him, and she has paced the floor outside it often enough to know its width `[design]`. Last, she wants to see where the Monarchs go in winter `[design]`. She has read every account of the roosts in the southern forests and has never made the trip, because the family cannot leave the city while the plan stands open `[design]`.

## Personality

To most of Waterdeep, Ammalia Cassalanter is a well-mannered noblewoman, well read and well traveled, and exceptionally shrewd. She is known for driving a hard bargain, and she has banked enough goodwill in the city to win favor with a magistrate. Her butterfly garden is the most beautiful in Waterdeep, and she supervises her youngest children at play there.

She names butterflies by species in the middle of a conversation, and she asks one question that shows she has researched the person she is talking to. Her voice softens completely with the twins. The adventure calls her vainglorious, and she expects to be admired for her garden and her charity.

She is often out on business with Victoro, and the two of them return an hour before dinner.

## Connections

- **Victoro Cassalanter:** Her husband and co-conspirator. Ammalia makes the final calls for the family and the cult, and Victoro, who leads the worship, has stopped questioning her. She pours the wine at the rites.
- **Osvaldo Cassalanter:** Her eldest son, a chain devil in the attic. She visits him weekly and reads to him from outside the shielding circle.
- **Terenzio Cassalanter** and **Elzerina Cassalanter:** The twins. She supervises them at play in the garden, and they know nothing of the pact.
- **Willifort Crowelle:** The butler who knows everything that happens in the villa. He reports to Ammalia, runs household security and directs the spy and the poisoner.
- **Tissina Khyret:** Her personal attendant and the family's chief housemaid, who joined the cult because Ammalia asked her to.
- **Laiba "Nana" Rosse:** The twins' caretaker, who joined the cult to stay close to the children.
- **Jandar Chergoba:** The head chef, who sees that the poison at the feast is distributed correctly.
- **Dagult Neverember:** The former Open Lord whose embezzlement began the family's ruin.
- **The Harpers:** They have watched the family's charity for years and found nothing, and Ammalia knows they watch `[research]`.

## Overview

Ammalia Cassalanter is a noblewoman and wizard known for driving a hard bargain. Her butterfly garden is counted the most beautiful in Waterdeep, and she supervises her youngest children at play there. She is well read and well traveled, with a great deal of goodwill banked in the city.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Ammalia should inspire wariness with her exquisite manners and probing intelligence, sympathy in anyone who sees her with the twins in the butterfly garden, and cold dread in anyone who learns she is the one planning the poisoned feast.

**Emotions.** Ammalia most often feels shrewd calculation, maternal protectiveness twisted by desperation, vainglorious self-assurance, careful control over every social interaction, intellectual pleasure in negotiation, and a private grief about Osvaldo that she channels into ruthless action.

**Motivations.** Ammalia wants to save the twins by any means necessary (including poisoning 99 people), maintain the family's social position, and ensure that the Founders' Day feast goes exactly as planned.

**Inspirations.** When playing Ammalia, channel Lady Macbeth (*Macbeth*), Mama Imelda (*Coco*), and Catherine de Medici (*The Serpent Queen*).

### Voice

*An exquisite, hard-bargaining noblewoman whose composure never breaks, even when it should.*

- **Sound:** A clear, elegant, controlled voice with a negotiator's edge. It softens completely with the twins.
- **Sentence shape:** Poised, precise sentences and probing questions. She sets terms before others know they're negotiating. In her later moods (the phases on **The Cassalanters** in Villains): steady, cold and entirely in control.
- **Word choice:** Public: gardens, butterflies, society, charity, "a fair arrangement". In her later moods: planning language ("the feast", "the timetable", "what must be done").
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Her control is total. The one crack is a softened voice reading stories in the attic.
- **Personality in speech:** Shrewd calculation and maternal desperation. She makes the plans and speaks like it. Her grief for Osvaldo is channelled into ruthlessness and is never spoken aloud to outsiders.
- **Quirks:** She names butterflies by species mid-conversation. She asks one question that shows she's researched the listener.
- **Signature phrases:** "Shall we come to terms?" / "The Monarch is my favourite; it travels so far." / "What must be done will be done."
- **Never:** She never loses composure, never hints at infernalism in public, and never begs.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting (public):* "You must be the tavern owners. I've heard wonderful things. Walk with me; the garden's lovely at this hour."
  - *Business (public):* "I drive a hard bargain, I'm told. Shall we come to terms, and see if the rumour's true?"
  - *Later mood:* "You think I'm a monster. I'm a mother with a deadline. Offer me something better, and I'll take it this instant."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Ammalia is well-mannered, well-read, well-traveled, and known for driving a hard bargain. Her butterfly garden is the most beautiful in Waterdeep, and she supervises her youngest children at play there. To those she trusts, she worships Asmodeus alongside Victoro and shares the same pact and the same desperation. Deep down, while Victoro hunts Neverember's gold, Ammalia makes the plans: the feast, the poison, the 99 souls. She also goes to the attic where Osvaldo is bound, and reads him his favorite stories from a chair placed just outside the shielding circle while he curses her name and pleads with her to save him.

**Morale.** In a fight, Ammalia grabs the twins and runs. She will spend Victoro's life to buy that retreat without hesitating, and her arcane magic is precise enough to cover an exit while collapsing a hallway behind her.

**Relationships.** Ammalia is the wife of Victoro Cassalanter, the mother of Osvaldo, Terenzio, and Elzerina, and the operational architect of the Founders' Day feast. Her lepidopterology hobby gives her an excuse to spend hours in the garden with her children, which is both genuine and calculated.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The pact:** Ammalia and Victoro traded the souls of their three children to Asmodeus for wealth and political access. She found the way through infernal research, and she makes the final calls for the family and the cult.
> - **The feast:** On Founders' Day, Flamerule 1, she plans to poison ninety-nine of one hundred guests to collect the souls that the buy-out clause demands. The twins' ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, is the date the contract falls due.
> - **Who knows:** Nobody outside the household and the cult. Everyone else suspects the family and knows nothing more. Jarlaxle is the one exception, through **Vessa**, and he tells no member of Bregan D'aerthe who has not worked it out.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a poised and courteous Personality and an Overview that shows only the garden and the bargain-maker. The narrator treats Ammalia's love for her children and her plan for the feast as two facts and takes neither side.
