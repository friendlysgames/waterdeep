**Osvaldo Cassalanter** (Lawful evil, Tethyrian human, chain devil, he/him)

## Background

Osvaldo is the eldest child and heir apparent of Victoro and Ammalia Cassalanter (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He was born in 1480 DR, a year that no source states and that the campaign derives from his ninth year in 1489 DR (**A Debt in Blood**), and his brother and sister, Terenzio and Elzerina, were born three years later on Flamerule 11, 1483 DR. He is a Tethyrian human `[research]`, and a portrait in the villa shows him holding a ceremonial rapier (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Osvaldo grew up in the villa during the family's prosperous years, in a house where his mother's butterfly garden was begun the spring after he was born `[design]`. Ammalia taught him the butterflies by species, and the Monarch, which she liked best, was the first name he learned `[design]`. He was a serious, orderly boy who asked for the same stories in the same order every night and corrected his mother when she skipped a page `[design]`. When the house held a reception, the heir was brought downstairs in good clothes, with the ceremonial rapier at his hip, to greet the guests at the door `[design]`. When the twins were small he read to them at bedtime and did the voices from his own favorite stories `[design]`.

The family faced bankruptcy about three years before the campaign opens, and the bargain was made in 1489 DR, counted back from the published "three years ago" (**The Cassalanters**, Villains). The contract claims each child at nine, and Osvaldo, who was nine, was taken at once. His soul went to Asmodeus and his body became a chain devil (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The boy screamed for three hours before the change finished, and Victoro then sealed the attic with abjuration wards and soundproofed the floor (**Cassalanter Villa**). His own chains hold him, and a pentagram ten feet across is burned into the floor around him (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Osvaldo was in the temple when the contract was signed, standing beside his mother while the pit fiend read out the terms `[design]`. He heard Terenzio's name and Elzerina's in the reading before the change began, and at nine he understood only that they were in it `[design]`. That is the whole of what he knows about the contract, and it is why the fear for them is the first thing the boy's voice carries `[design]`.

Osvaldo has spent three years in the attic and is twelve now. Two imps in spider form guard the room and kill any vermin that find their way in (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and the attic is soundproof while its doors stay shut. Ammalia visits each week and sits in the room outside the pentagram, in a chair outside the shielding circle, and reads to him while he curses her and weeps. Victoro has not entered the attic in months. The household staff know the attic is forbidden, Willifort Crowelle knows what is inside, and the twins have been told that Osvaldo is at school in a faraway city, which is also the story the city hears. The devil is thoroughly insane and cannot be reasoned with (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and a boy's voice breaks through at the sound of his name.

What the boy remembers is the library and the garden, his mother's voice and the twins at six years old `[design]`. He counts the weeks by her visits `[design]`. When the doors are open for the reading, the sounds of the house below reach the attic, and he has learned which of them are the twins `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Osvaldo wants to hear the twins' voices `[design]`. When his mother opens the doors for a reading, he listens through the whole visit for a child laughing on the stairs, because that is the only way he can tell that they are still in the house `[design]`. He also wants to hear the end of a story `[design]`. He was halfway through a long adventure book when he was taken, his mother reads him the stories he used to ask for, and the one he stopped in the middle was never among them, so he has never been able to tell her which it was `[design]`. Last, he wants to say his own name before the devil takes the sentence away `[design]`. He repeats it between the fits, and it is the one word that brings the boy's voice up `[design]`.

## Personality

To Waterdeep, Osvaldo Cassalanter is the family's absent heir, an eldest son said to be studying at a school in a faraway city. His parents answer politely when he comes up and move on to another subject, and the twins repeat the school story without being able to say which city it is.

Nobody outside the household has any more to go on than that, and his name is not one the family brings to dinner parties.

## Overview

Osvaldo Cassalanter is the eldest son and heir of Lord Victoro and Lady Ammalia Cassalanter. Waterdeep hears that he is studying at a school in a faraway city. His parents seldom speak of him, and his younger brother and sister, the twins, repeat the same story.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Osvaldo should inspire horror in anyone who learns what the howling is; genuine sorrow for the boy in the portrait with the ceremonial rapier; and cold terror in anyone who meets the chain devil in the flesh.

**Emotions.** Osvaldo most often feels the chain devil's rage against confinement, fragmented grief for a life that ended at nine, desperate pleading when Ammalia visits, a hatred of his own condition that sometimes expresses as hatred of everything, and the specific anguish of knowing his siblings are scheduled for the same fate.

**Motivations.** The chain devil wants to escape, attack, and destroy anything that enters the attic. The fragmented self wants to understand what happened to him and make contact with the siblings he cannot see.

**Inspirations.** When playing Osvaldo, channel the Beast (*Beauty and the Beast*, before the transformation reversed), Bertha Mason (*Jane Eyre*), and the Hulk (*Marvel*, the monster who remembers being someone else).

### Voice

*A chain devil howling in the attic, with a boy's voice buried somewhere underneath.*

- **Sound:** A metallic, grinding roar. Now and then a twelve-year-old boy's cracked voice breaks through, and then it's gone.
- **Sentence shape:** The devil: snarls and fragments. The boy: broken pleading, half-sentences, names.
- **Word choice:** The devil: hunger and chains. The boy: "Mother", "please", his siblings' names, and questions about what happened to him.
- **Swearing:** *Punctuation · Colourful · Tirade.* The devil is a torrent of filth at whoever stands outside the circle, his mother included ("Come closer, you soft little cunt, let me wrap you in iron!"). The boy never swears. Tirade (the devil): "Come closer, you soft, bleating, chain-dodging, gutless, fucking meat, come closer so I can wrap you in iron and listen to you break!"
- **Personality in speech:** Rage against confinement, with fragments of grief. The boy is desperate for contact with the twins.
- **Quirks:** Chains rattle in time with the words. The boy's voice only breaks through at the name "Osvaldo".
- **Signature phrases:** Devil: "Closer." / Boy: "Mother, please." / "Are they safe?"
- **Never:** The devil never shows mercy. The boy never wants to hurt anyone.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* A roar when the attic door opens, and the chains rattling against the beams.
  - *The boy:* "Mother? Please. Are Terenzio and Elzi safe? Tell me they're not coming here."
  - *Under pressure:* "Closer! Closer, and I'll show you what you made me!"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Osvaldo Cassalanter is a name the family does not mention — "attending school in a faraway city," Terenzio and Elzerina were told. To those who open the attic door, he is a chain devil in a soundproofed attic who attacks immediately, and whose chains have a 15-foot reach `[research]`. Deep down, some fragment of Osvaldo is still there, and it hears his mother's voice when she comes to read, and it is very much aware of what was done to it.

**Morale.** Osvaldo attacks anything that enters the attic except Ammalia. Her presence calms him enough for her to read from outside the shielding circle, but she does not go into the room.

**Relationships.** Osvaldo is the eldest child of Victoro and Ammalia (taken at nine, twelve now, his soul surrendered to Asmodeus three years ago during the family's financial crisis), the reason Terenzio and Elzerina are forfeit on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, 1492 DR (the pact requires both 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls), and the tragedy that both explains and indicts everything his parents are doing.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The attic:** Osvaldo is a chain devil held in the soundproofed attic of the villa, room C24, never the basement or the temple. The pact bound him at nine, and the family keeps him behind the pentagram under the watch of two imps.
> - **The visits:** Ammalia reads to him each week, and Victoro has not entered in months. The twins and the city have been told that he is at school in a faraway city.
> - **Who knows:** Willifort knows what is inside, and the rest of the staff know only that the attic is forbidden. Nobody outside the household and the cult knows the pact, and everyone else suspects the family and knows nothing more. Jarlaxle is the one exception, through **Vessa**, and he tells no member of Bregan D'aerthe who has not worked it out.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a short Personality and an Overview that shows only the school story. The narrator treats the boy and the devil as one person and takes neither side.
