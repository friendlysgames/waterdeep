**Laiba "Nana" Rosse** (Neutral evil, tiefling, she/her)

## Background

Laiba Rosse is a tiefling woman whom the household calls Nana, and she is the caretaker and private tutor of Terenzio and Elzerina Cassalanter (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She does her best to keep track of the twins' every move, though they often sneak away from her watchful eye (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She dresses in flowing red robes and relaxes in the twins' playroom when she is not with the children (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). No source gives her age, her birthplace, her family or any post that she held before the villa `[research]`.

She was born in the Dock Ward, the eldest of five children in a tiefling family that rented two rooms above a rope-walk `[design]`. Her mother mended sails for a chandler and left before dawn, so from the age of ten Nana fed and washed her brothers and sisters and walked them to the pump `[design]`. Her mother taught her to crochet from unpicked wool by the light of one lamp, because a doll cost nothing to make and a child would sit quietly with it `[design]`. The neighbors reminded the family often that tieflings carry the Nine Hells in their blood, and Nana grew up believing that the claim was true `[design]`.

At fifteen she went into service as a nursemaid for a ship chandler's children, and over the following decades she moved through larger households as a nurse and then as a governess who taught reading, sums and the piano `[design]`. Some houses would not take a tiefling into the nursery, and she learned which houses would `[design]`. She never married and had no children of her own `[design]`. The Cassalanters are known for a warmth toward tieflings that is specific and genuine (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations), and she took the post of the twins' caretaker while they were small and has lived in the villa since `[design]`. She is in her sixties now, and she expects the twins to be the last children whom she raises `[design]`. She has no home outside the villa and her brothers and sisters have scattered, so she knows that a nurse of her age who loses a post does not find another `[design]`.

Her day follows the twins' schedule (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)). She gives the morning piano lesson and sits beside the piano with her crochet, and she counts the twins aloud as they ride the library's sliding ladders. She watches them chase butterflies from a wrought-iron bench on the lawn, and in the afternoon she crochets a doll in the playroom while they play on the floor. From four to five the twins slip away, and she searches the second floor, calling for them. She stands at the sideboard at supper, and she sleeps in the playroom from nine, next to the twins' bedroom. When the household has guests, she follows the twins down to greet them for the first fifteen minutes and then takes them upstairs again. She calls the twins her lambs. A few winters ago she and the twins made Terenzio's dragon costume from green felt, working from a drawing that he made, and she keeps a needle in her crochet basket for its tail `[design]`.

She joined the cult to stay close to the twins when the Cassalanters began restricting household access (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations; **Cassalanter Villa**, Quests). No source dates her joining, and this page places it in the first year after the bargain of about 1489 DR `[design]`. The cult spoke to what she had been taught about her blood, and it told her that the lord of the Nine Hells keeps what is placed in his keeping and asks only for service `[design]`. She came to believe that her place in the cult was what kept the twins safe `[design]`. She is a cult fanatic and keeps order at the rites beside Willifort, Tissina Khyret and Jandar Chergoba (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations; **Willifort Crowelle**). She sees no conflict between her faith and her work, and she does not think of her evening reports to the household as spying (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)). She loves the twins fiercely, and she loves them more than she fears the cult (**Cassalanter Villa**, Quests).

**Personal goals.** Nana wants to join her squares into a single coverlet `[design]`. She has crocheted one square for every child whom she has nursed or taught, each in a color that she chose for that child, and thirty-six of them lie in a flour sack under her bed in the playroom `[design]`. She has not joined them because she has not decided whose bed the coverlet should go on `[design]`. She also wants to visit her mother's grave in the Dock Ward `[design]`. She has not been since she took the post, because the twins are in her sight at every hour but one, and she has never asked for a day `[design]`. Last, she wants word of her youngest brother `[design]`. He shipped out of the Dock Ward as a deckhand, wrote twice and then stopped, and she asks the delivery men from the docks whether anyone knows his ship `[design]`.

## Personality

To anyone who meets her, Laiba "Nana" Rosse is the tiefling nanny who follows the Cassalanter twins a few steps behind them, crocheting as she walks. She wears flowing red robes, calls Terenzio and Elzerina her lambs and counts them aloud whenever one of them moves.

She is warm with visitors and says nothing of the household's business. When the twins slip away, she searches the second floor and asks anyone she meets, "Oh, visitors! Have you seen two little rascals? One's a dragon and one's a knight." She corrects the twins' manners and language mildly, and she crochets dolls for them in the playroom.

## Connections

- **Terenzio Cassalanter** and **Elzerina Cassalanter:** The twins, whom she tutors, counts aloud and calls her lambs. Terenzio goes to her when something frightens him, and she takes Elzerina's wooden sword at the stair when the household has guests.
- **Ammalia Cassalanter:** The lady of the house, who brings the twins down to greet guests with Nana a step behind. Ammalia pours the wine at the rites that Nana attends.
- **Victoro Cassalanter:** The lord of the house, who is openly warm with the twins and who leads the worship that Nana attends.
- **Willifort Crowelle**, **Tissina Khyret** and **Jandar Chergoba:** Fellow cult members, who keep order at the rites beside her. Willifort links the family to everyone who works for it.
- **Bressa:** The nursemaid, who is not in the cult and who assists Nana with the twins during the daytime.

## Overview

Laiba "Nana" Rosse is the tiefling nanny and private tutor of Lord Victoro and Lady Ammalia Cassalanter's twins, Terenzio and Elzerina. She gives the twins their piano lessons and counts them aloud whenever they move. She crochets dolls for them and calls them her lambs. When they slip away, she goes looking for them through the house.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Nana Rosse should inspire genuine warmth when she crochets dolls for the twins; discomfort in anyone who realizes she reports their movements to the household hierarchy; and cold horror at the distance between her genuine affection and what she is serving.

**Emotions.** Nana Rosse most often feels the uncomplicated warmth of a woman who has cared for children for decades, devout conviction about Asmodeus that she has folded into her maternal role, mild exasperation when the twins sneak away, and genuine worry about their wellbeing alongside a complete absence of conflict between these things.

**Motivations.** Nana Rosse wants to keep the twins safe and happy until the feast, maintain her position in the household, and serve Asmodeus through the care she gives.

**Inspirations.** When playing Nana Rosse, channel Dolores (*Westworld*, the seemingly innocent keeper), Mrs. Hudson (*Sherlock*), and Granny Weatherwax (*Discworld*, the power in a grandmotherly frame).

### Voice

*The twins' loving tiefling nanny, who crochets dolls, scolds gently, and reports every evening.*

- **Sound:** A warm, creaky, grandmotherly voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Fond, fussy scolding, stories and questions about whether the children have eaten.
- **Word choice:** Nursery talk: "my little ones", "naughty", "supper", "bedtime". She calls the twins "my lambs".
- **Swearing:** *Never · Euphemist · n/a.* "Oh, bother and buttons!"
- **Personality in speech:** Warm and devoted. Her care is genuine, and her reporting is the same care in her mind. She sees no conflict between the two, and her voice doesn't either.
- **Quirks:** She crochets while talking. She counts the children aloud.
- **Signature phrases:** "My little lambs." / "Where have you two got to?" / "Bother and buttons."
- **Never:** She never speaks of the household's secrets, never raises her voice at the children, and never lets them out of her accounting.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Oh, visitors! Have you seen two little rascals, one's a dragon and one's a knight?"
  - *Business:* "I keep them safe, and I keep them counted. That's all a nana does, dear."
  - *Under pressure:* "Lambs, come here, come to Nana, quickly now."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Nana Rosse is the tiefling grandmother who crochets dolls for Terenzio and Elzerina and scolds them gently for sneaking away from lessons. To those who look past the warmth, she reports the children's movements to the household every evening, without considering it a betrayal, because in her understanding keeping them safe and keeping them accounted for are the same thing. Deep down, she believes she is the best thing in the twins' lives, and she may be right.

**Morale.** If anything threatens the twins, Nana Rosse gathers them and places herself between them and the danger. Her stat block is a cult fanatic's, so she is more dangerous than the crochet suggests.

**Relationships.** Laiba "Nana" Rosse is the twins' tiefling caretaker and tutor, a cult fanatic whose devotion to the children and devotion to the cult are inseparable in her own understanding, and the person who knows the twins' daily movements better than anyone else in the villa.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The cult:** Nana is a member of the Asmodean cult beneath the villa. She joined to stay close to the twins, and she keeps order at the rites beside Willifort, Tissina Khyret and Jandar Chergoba.
> - **What she knows:** She knows that the gatherings happen, and she knows that "something terrible" lives in the attic. She has never been inside. She does not know the Founders' Day plan, and she does not know that the contract claims the twins `[research]`.
> - **The reports:** She reports where the twins have been and whom they met to the household every evening, and she does not think of it as spying.
> - **Who knows:** Nobody outside the household and the cult knows that Nana is a member, and everyone else suspects the family and knows nothing more. Jarlaxle knows the pact through **Vessa**, and he tells no member of Bregan D'aerthe who has not worked it out.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with warmth toward Nana in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her care for the twins as genuine, and the Secret is stated flatly with no comment on the household.
