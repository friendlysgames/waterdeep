**Seffia Naelryke** (Lawful evil, Tethyrian human, she/her)

## Background

Seffia Naelryke is a Tethyrian human woman who commands the Cassalanters' field team and answers to Victoro (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations). She is one of the six cult members on the household roster, although she does not live at the villa and is there only for the cult's gatherings or when she is summoned `[research]` (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations). She is a true believer in the cult's hierarchy and will not turn (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Cassalanters**, Organizations).

Seffia grew up in a Tethyrian river town where her father kept the accounts for a miller `[design]`. When a shortfall turned up in the grain books, he told the miller's family exactly where the error lay, and they turned him out and kept the mill `[design]`. He found no other post, and she learned early that the person who admits a failure is the one who pays for it `[design]`. She came north to Waterdeep as a young woman and worked as a courier for a trading house, where she learned the streets, the guild routes and how to carry a message without being noticed `[design]`. A fellow courier who attended the cult's gatherings brought her to the faith, and she found there a hierarchy in which every person has a rank and a task and nobody is blamed at random `[design]`. She was given field work because she kept her own counsel and her tasks came out clean `[design]`.

Victoro put her in charge of the family's precise field team. It is made up of Seffia, two Spies, a Veteran and an invisible Imp, and it does intelligence gathering, surveillance and combat support (**The Cassalanters**, Factions Guide; *Dragon Heist*, 5e). She lives with Arn Xalrondar on the upper floor of the Converted Windmill on Coachlamp Lane in the Southern Ward, and they dress plainly and pass as ordinary tenants (**Southern Ward**, Gazetteer). The family pays the upper floor's rent under a false name, a Cassalanter steward named Brimel Crestfall has carried baskets of candles, salt, black wax and wine to the second landing of the stair for years, and Ammalia visits twice a month, alone, in a hired cab with the curtains drawn (**Southern Ward**, Gazetteer). Seffia and Arn keep the apartment door locked at all times (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). A ritual room in the windmill holds a blood-painted pentagram and a map with the Brandath Crypts circled (**The Cassalanters**, Factions Guide; **Southern Ward**, Gazetteer).

At some point before the campaign opens, Seffia approached Vevette Blackwater to open a communication channel between the Cassalanters and the Zhentarim. Vevette refused, suspecting a trap, and she kept the visiting card that gives the Windmill's address. Neither side has made contact since (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations; **Vevette Blackwater**).

Before Ches 1, 1492 DR, an infernal divination placed Eye #2 of the Stone of Golorr in the Brandath Crypts (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations). Seffia performed a ritual that identified the crypt, and Victoro wrote her a letter that confirms it, which she keeps (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations). Victoro then sent her and Arn to take the Eye and to silence three cult members who knew too much about it. They took the Eye, and the family now keeps it secured in the temple (**The Cassalanters**, Villains). They killed two of the three, Holiver Tornrudder and Kaeth Warloon, and the third, Vaelle Lurval, survived (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Vaelle is a Tethyrian human cultist and a 30-year-old Waterdhavian florist, and Holiver, her boyfriend, had brought her into the cult (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Victoro does not know that Vaelle lives, and Seffia means to repair the failure without telling him (**Southern Ward**, Gazetteer).

She has carried the failure since, and it fits the lesson of her father's ruin `[design]`. She knows that she becomes a loose end for the Cassalanters if she does not fix it correctly, and she keeps the matter from every ear except Arn's `[design]`. The cult's hierarchy has always rewarded the tasks she finishes, and an unfinished one is the first she has had `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Seffia wants a working mill of her own on a river in Tethyr, with the sails turning and the grain books in her hand `[design]`. She keeps a small ledger of coin toward it, apart from anything the family pays her, and she has told no one about it `[design]`. She also wants Arn to learn to plan without her, so that he is not caught with her if she is ever the loose end `[design]`. She has begun to leave small decisions to him when she goes out and does not always like what she finds on her return `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who deal with her, Seffia Naelryke is a tenant of the old windmill on Coachlamp Lane, a Tethyrian woman in plain dress who keeps to herself. She speaks in a low, even, clipped voice and says only what the matter needs, and a caller at the door hears that the property is private and that there are no visitors.

She sleeps in one of the upper rooms and keeps the apartment door locked. She speaks to Arn in half-sentences that he completes, and he agrees with them quickly, like a man who has learned what she means. Those who see her at work find her calm, and she does not raise her voice.

## Connections

- **Victoro Cassalanter:** The patron who sent her to retrieve an Eye (Eye #2, from the Brandath Crypts) and to silence three cult members who knew too much. She answers to him, and she means to repair the failure without telling him.
- **Arn Xalrondar:** Her operational partner at the Windmill, who defers to her on planning and covers her in a fight. He acts on his own when she is out of the room.
- **Vaelle Lurval:** The witness she failed to silence, a Lawful Evil female Tethyrian human cultist and a 30-year-old Waterdhavian florist. The two dead cultists are Holiver Tornrudder, her boyfriend, and Kaeth Warloon. Vaelle believes the fanatics acted on their own, not on Lord Cassalanter's orders, and she will not name the Cassalanters unless magically compelled. Vaelle is alive, and Seffia is working on the problem.
- **Ammalia Cassalanter:** Visits the Windmill twice a month, alone, and is the one caller whom Seffia does not turn away `[design]`.
- **Willifort Crowelle:** The family's other field operative. His methods are blunt where hers are precise, and no source says which of them leads when the two work together `[research]`.
- **Her team:** Two Spies, a Veteran and an invisible Imp, who do the legwork on intelligence, surveillance and combat support.
- **Vevette Blackwater:** A Zhentarim swashbuckler whom Seffia approached to open a channel, and who refused. Vevette carries Seffia's visiting card, which gives the Windmill's address, and there has been no contact since.
- **Brimel Crestfall:** A Cassalanter steward who carries baskets of candles, salt, black wax and wine to the second landing of the windmill stair.
- **Her father:** A miller's accountant in a Tethyrian river town, turned out after he admitted a shortfall in the books, who died before she left Tethyr `[design]`.

## Overview

Seffia Naelryke is a Tethyrian human woman who lives in a decrepit windmill on Coachlamp Lane. The windmill stands in the Southern Ward, and she shares its upper floor with Arn Xalrondar. Arn defers to her on anything that needs planning, and she does not receive visitors.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Seffia should inspire cold professionalism that produces unease; dread for anyone who learns she was tasked with silencing witnesses and failed; and the particular menace of someone currently in the middle of fixing a problem.

**Emotions.** Seffia most often feels the focused calm of someone mid-operation, controlled frustration at the Vaelle Lurval failure, theological certainty as a stabilizing force, and the specific alertness of someone who knows she is now a loose end for the Cassalanters if she does not resolve this correctly.

**Motivations.** Seffia wants to recover from the Vaelle Lurval failure without exposing it to Lord Cassalanter, resume her response team role, and maintain the Converted Windmill as a reliable Asmodean fallback location.

**Inspirations.** When playing Seffia, channel Cersei Lannister (*Game of Thrones*, calculating damage controller), Amy Dunne (*Gone Girl*, the professional who handles what others can't), and Villanelle (*Killing Eve*, efficient rather than theatrical).

### Voice

*A cold, efficient cult operative in the middle of fixing a failure, and in no mood for visitors.*

- **Sound:** A low, even, clipped voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Efficient, practical sentences, with problem-solving talk to Arn.
- **Word choice:** Operational: "loose ends", "witnesses", "clean". She never names her superiors to outsiders.
- **Swearing:** *Triggered · Plain · Phrase.* About her failure ("That fucking Lurval woman.").
- **Personality in speech:** Focused calm and controlled frustration. She's aware she's now a loose end herself, and it shows as urgency.
- **Quirks:** She speaks to Arn in half-sentences he completes.
- **Signature phrases:** "Clean it up." / "No visitors." / "Arn."
- **Never:** She never talks about her employers, never admits the failure upward, and never receives visitors.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "This is private property. No visitors."
  - *Business:* "Three witnesses. Two handled. The third is a problem I'm solving."
  - *Under pressure:* "Arn. Do it."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Seffia maintains a decrepit windmill in the Southern Ward, sleeps in one of its upper rooms, and does not receive visitors. In truth, she and Arn have converted the windmill into an Asmodean shrine and a cult fallback location. Deep down, she was given two missions (retrieve an Eye and silence three witnesses), failed the quieter one, and she is dealing with the consequences cleanly.

**Morale.** In a fight, Seffia uses cult fanatic spells precisely, calls the spined devils if available, and positions Arn to cover her flanks. She does not retreat from her own operational base.

**Relationships.** Seffia is the Cassalanter response team leader (stationed at the Converted Windmill, Southern Ward), Arn Xalrondar's operational partner, and the cult fanatic who failed to silence Vaelle Lurval.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The cult:** Seffia is an Asmodean cult fanatic in the Cassalanters' service, and the Windmill is an Asmodean shrine and a fallback site for the cult. Nobody outside the household and the cult knows this, and the rest of Waterdeep only suspects the family.
> - **The mission:** Lord Cassalanter gave her two missions, to retrieve Eye #2 of the Stone of Golorr from the Brandath Crypts and to silence three cult members who knew too much about it. She and Arn killed two of the three.
> - **The failure:** The survivor, Vaelle Lurval, is the loose end Seffia is working to close, and Lord Cassalanter does not know she failed.
> - **The letter:** Seffia keeps a letter from Lord Cassalanter confirming that her ritual identified Eye #2 in the Brandath Crypts. It is a prized possession, and anyone who takes it provokes her fury.
> - **The report:** She reports in writing on the Cassalanters' external outposts, covering the Converted Windmill and the Asmodean Shrine. Her report sits in the hidden compartment of Victoro Cassalanter's desk at the villa.

**Tone:** Cold and procedural. Seffia is menacing because she is busy and competent, so keep her calm and let the dread come from the work she is doing. Her cruelty belongs in her actions, never in the narration.
