Seffia Naelryke (Lawful Evil, Human, she/her)

## Personality

- She is a Tethyrian Human woman and a cult fanatic of Asmodeus, loyal to the Cassalanters' cult and a true believer in its hierarchy. She will not turn.
- She leads the Cassalanter response team from the Converted Windmill on Coachlamp Lane in the Southern Ward. The team is Seffia, 2 Spies, 1 Veteran and 1 invisible Imp, and it does precision work: intelligence gathering, surveillance and combat support.
- Her usual state is the focused calm of someone in the middle of an operation. Her one visible crack is controlled frustration over the Vaelle Lurval failure.
- Theological certainty steadies her. She knows she becomes a loose end for the Cassalanters if she does not fix the failure correctly, and that shows as urgency.
- She talks in operational terms ("loose ends", "witnesses", "clean") in a low, even, clipped voice. She curses only about her failure, she never names her superiors to outsiders and she never admits the failure upward.
- She speaks to Arn in half-sentences that he completes.
- She keeps a letter from Lord Cassalanter confirming that her ritual identified the Eye in the Brandath Crypt. It is a prized possession, and anyone who takes it provokes her fury.
- She reports in writing on the Cassalanters' external outposts, covering the Converted Windmill and the Asmodean Shrine. Her report sits in the hidden compartment of Victoro Cassalanter's desk at the villa.
- In a fight she casts precisely, calls the spined devils if they are available and posts Arn to cover her flanks. She does not retreat from her own base.
- Under escalation she acts in two modes. At Alert her team stays passive unless the party turns hostile, and then she and Willifort Crowelle work together. At Lockdown during **Cassalanter Villa** her team is stationed inside the villa.
- If the party counts as a serious threat, her team searches **Trollskull Manor** during a busy service as reconnaissance, not a confrontation. The Imp looks for Golorr artifacts and reports without stealing.
- If the Cassalanters turn hostile to the party, her team joins the Eye heist at **Xanathar's Lair** as a competing faction.

## Connections

- **Arn Xalrondar:** Her operational partner at the Windmill, who defers to her on planning and covers her in a fight. Unresolved: he acts on his own when she is out of the room, as when he sent the Stone off with a spined devil.
- **Victoro Cassalanter:** The patron who sent her to retrieve the Stone and to silence three cult members who knew too much. Unresolved: she means to repair the failure without telling him.
- **Vaelle Lurval:** The witness she failed to silence. Unresolved: Vaelle is alive, and Seffia is working on the problem. Vaelle has no Notable Figures page and appears nowhere else in the campaign files [research].
- **Willifort Crowelle:** The family's other field operative. His methods are blunt where hers are precise, and the two combine in hostile mode. Unresolved: nothing says who leads when they work together.
- **Vevette Blackwater:** Seffia approached her to open a channel between the Cassalanters and the Zhentarim, and Vevette refused, suspecting a trap. Unresolved: no contact since, and Vevette still carries Seffia's visiting card, which gives the Windmill's address.

## Overview

Seffia Naelryke is a Human woman who maintains a decrepit windmill in the Southern Ward and sleeps in one of its upper rooms. She shares the building with Arn Xalrondar, who defers to her judgment on anything that involves planning, and she does not receive visitors.

## GM Block

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Seffia should inspire cold professionalism that produces unease; dread when the party learns she was tasked with silencing witnesses and failed; and the particular menace of someone currently in the middle of fixing a problem.

**Emotions.** Seffia most often feels the focused calm of someone mid-operation, controlled frustration at the Vaelle Lurval failure, theological certainty as a stabilizing force, and the specific alertness of someone who knows she is now a loose end for the Cassalanters if she does not resolve this correctly.

**Motivations.** Seffia wants to recover from the Vaelle Lurval failure without exposing it to Lord Cassalanter, resume her response team role, and maintain the Converted Windmill as a reliable Asmodean fallback location.

**Inspirations.** When playing Seffia, channel Cersei Lannister (*Game of Thrones*, calculating damage controller), Amy Dunne (*Gone Girl*, the professional who handles what others can't), and Villanelle (*Killing Eve*, efficient rather than theatrical).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Seffia maintains a decrepit windmill in the Southern Ward, sleeps in one of its upper rooms, and does not receive visitors. To those who investigate, she and Arn have converted the windmill into an Asmodean shrine and a cult fallback location. Deep down, she was given two missions (retrieve the Stone and silence three witnesses), failed the quieter one, and she is dealing with the consequences cleanly.

**Morale.** In a fight, Seffia uses cult fanatic spells precisely, calls the spined devils if available, and positions Arn to cover her flanks. She does not retreat from her own operational base.

**Relationships.** Seffia is the Cassalanter response team leader (stationed at the Converted Windmill, Southern Ward), Arn Xalrondar's operational partner, the cult fanatic who failed to silence Vaelle Lurval, and a recurring NPC across **Faction Outposts**, **Cassalanter Villa**, and **Sea Maidens Faire**.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Secret
> Seffia is an Asmodean cult fanatic in the Cassalanters' service, a fact the party learns only by investigating the Windmill. Lord Cassalanter gave her two missions, to retrieve the Stone of Golorr and to silence three cult members who knew too much about it. She and Arn killed two of the three. The survivor, Vaelle Lurval, is the loose end Seffia is working to close, and Lord Cassalanter does not know she failed.

**Tone:** Cold and procedural. Seffia is menacing because she is busy and competent, so keep her calm and let the dread come from what the party learns about her work. Her cruelty belongs in her actions, never in the narration.
