**Elzerina Cassalanter** (Lawful good, Tethyrian human, she/her)

## Background

Elzerina is one of the younger twins of Victoro and Ammalia Cassalanter (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She and her brother Terenzio were born on Flamerule 11, 1483 DR, a year that no source states and that the campaign derives from their ninth birthday on Flamerule 11, 1492 DR (**A Debt in Blood**). She is eight now and three years younger than Osvaldo, and she is a Tethyrian human `[research]`. A portrait in the villa shows her as a baby in her mother's lap while her father reads the twins a fairy tale (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Elzerina has lived her whole life in the villa and its grounds. She is tutored privately by Nana Rosse, rarely leaves the estate and has no friends outside the household (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Her days follow a fixed round of piano lesson, library, garden, playroom and supper, and she sleeps in the twins' bedroom from nine every night (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)). Nana Rosse is with the twins at every hour but one. From four to five on an ordinary day Elzerina is in the corridor outside the kitchen while Nana searches the second floor, calling for the pair who have sneaked away again (**Cassalanter Villa** (location); *Dragon Heist*, 5e).

From the end of her piano lesson until supper she wears a knight's helm and carries a wooden sword, and she chases her brother, who wears a dragon costume, around the twins' bedroom (**Cassalanter Villa** (location); *Dragon Heist*, 5e). Her father brought the helm home on her eighth birthday, and she puts it on the moment the lesson ends `[design]`. The gardener cut the sword from an orchard stake the summer she turned seven and sanded it until she stopped getting splinters `[design]`. She plays the piano because she was told that she could not play the hard piece until she could play the easy one through without stopping, and she will not be told that she cannot do a thing `[design]`. Her strongest words come from the stable yard, where she is allowed to stand on a bucket at the stall door and watch the horses fed `[design]`.

She keeps a tally of the games she has won against Terenzio, in chalk on the inside of a cupboard door in the playroom `[design]`. She is ahead and has been for as long as the marks go back, and the lead is smaller than she admits `[design]`. She also keeps a plan of the house, drawn on the back of a lesson sheet and folded inside her helm, with a small marker on every room that she is not allowed to enter `[design]`. The attic is the one marker that she has not been able to place `[design]`.

The twins were told, when they were about six and Osvaldo was nine, that he had gone to school in a faraway city (**A Debt in Blood**). He read to the twins at bedtime and did the voices from his own favorite stories `[design]`. Elzerina was told the name of the city once and cannot recall it, as the adventure says of both twins (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Of the two of them she is the one who asked again, and each time the answer was a kiss on the forehead and a change of subject `[design]`. That unanswered question is where her sense that something in the house is wrong begins `[design]`.

She has heard howling from the attic two or three times in her life and is curious about what lurks there (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She also knows that her father says something in a funny voice before he goes through the vault door, and she knows a funny door behind a sun in the wine cellar, which she has seen from the corridor and never entered (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)). She reports, as Terenzio does, that her parents are searching for missing dragons, and she is too frightened to ask what they want with them (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

The contract claims each child at nine. Osvaldo was taken at that age, and the twins' souls are forfeit on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, 1492 DR, ten days after Founders' Day (**The Cassalanters**, Villains). Elzerina belongs to no cult and has never been told about the contract (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Nana Rosse, who is a cult member, reports to the household every evening where the twins have been and whom they met (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)).

**Personal goals.** Elzerina wants to win the tally `[design]`. She counts every game, and she wants the day to come when Terenzio gives up and agrees that she is the better knight `[design]`. She also wants someone to tell her the name of Osvaldo's school and the street it stands on, so that she can send him a letter listing everything she has beaten Terenzio at `[design]`. She asks each adult once, in the hope that one of them will answer differently from the others `[design]`. Last, she wants to see the harbor `[design]`. The villa stands in the Sea Ward and she has never seen the water, and she wants to ride down to the docks where her mother says the garden's ravens were rescued, and to see the ships that brought the butterflies `[design]`.

## Personality

To anyone who meets her, Elzerina Cassalanter is the twin who hits first and asks questions afterward. She plays at being a knight, pokes people with her wooden sword when she wants an answer, and asks visitors straight out whether they fight monsters and whether she can come. Around strangers she is brash and outgoing, and she steps forward while her brother stays half behind Nana Rosse.

She is competitive with Terenzio and protective of him, and she is warm to any adult who takes her questions seriously. She files away what she overhears and sometimes repeats it at the wrong moment.

## Connections

- **Terenzio Cassalanter:** Her twin. She calls him Ter, and he calls her Elzi. She competes with him at everything and stands in front of him when something frightens him.
- **Victoro Cassalanter:** Her father, who is openly warm with her. She has heard him speak in a funny voice before the vault door, and she does not know what it means.
- **Ammalia Cassalanter:** Her mother, who keeps a hand on one twin or the other whenever they stand still. The garden and its butterflies are hers.
- **Osvaldo Cassalanter:** Her eldest brother, whom she was told is at school. She does not know that he is a chain devil in the attic.
- **Laiba "Nana" Rosse:** Her caretaker and tutor, who counts the twins aloud, takes the sword away at social events and reports each evening to the household.
- **Willifort Crowelle:** The butler, whom she calls Crowelle. She has heard her mother say a word to him that she does not know.
- **Asmodeus:** The creditor. The contract claims Elzerina on Flamerule 11, 1492 DR, and she has never heard of it.
- **Adults who deflect:** Elzerina never accepts "when you're older" and keeps asking until someone answers.

## Overview

Elzerina Cassalanter is one of the eight-year-old twin children of Lord Victoro and Lady Ammalia Cassalanter. She is the bolder of the two and plays at being a knight, in a helm with a wooden sword. She is tutored at home and rarely leaves the estate, and she is usually seen at play in the garden.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Elzerina should inspire the same immediate protectiveness as Terenzio, sharpened by her aggression; startled warmth when she asks flat-out whether you fight monsters and can she come; and the particular dread of knowing how fiercely she stands up for her brother and how little her courage can do against a bargain she has never heard of.

**Emotions.** Elzerina most often feels competitive physical energy, a younger sibling's determination to never be the one who backs down, quick tactical assessment she does not know she has, open warmth to anyone who takes her seriously, and fierce protectiveness of Terenzio even while she is chasing him.

**Motivations.** Elzerina wants to beat Terenzio at everything, find out what is in the attic before he does, and find an adult who will answer her actual questions instead of the easy ones.

**Inspirations.** When playing Elzerina, channel Arya Stark (*Game of Thrones*, early seasons), Merida (*Brave*), and Lyra Belacqua (*His Dark Materials*, early).

### Voice

*The bolder twin: a knight in a helm who asks straight out whether you fight monsters and can she come.*

- **Sound:** A loud, bossy, fearless child's voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Direct questions and challenges, with no patience for easy answers.
- **Word choice:** Knightly play: monsters, swords, "I'm a knight". She calls her brother "Ter" and adults by their weapon.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* She's picked up a few words from the stables and uses them proudly ("That's a shit sword.").
- **Personality in speech:** Competitive energy and fierce protectiveness. She wants real answers. She files away overheard things and occasionally blurts one out.
- **Quirks:** She pokes people with the wooden sword. She asks "Can I come?" about everything.
- **Signature phrases:** "Do you fight monsters?" / "Can I come?" / "Tell me the real answer."
- **Never:** She never backs down, never leaves Terenzio behind, and never accepts "when you're older".
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Do you fight monsters? Real ones? Can I come?"
  - *Business:* "Mother said a word to Crowelle I don't know. 'Feast.' She said it like it was scary. Why is a feast scary?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Get away from my brother! I'm a knight!"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Elzerina is the twin who hits first and asks later, currently wearing a knight's helm and swinging a wooden sword at her brother's dragon costume. To those who speak with her seriously, she wants to know whether her visitors fight monsters and whether they have met any dragons. Deep down, she has overheard things she did not understand, and she files them away with the efficiency of a child who has learned that adults do not explain until after they have decided you are old enough.

**Morale.** Elzerina does not run when frightened. She stands between Terenzio and whatever frightens him and looks for something to hold.

**Relationships.** Elzerina is Terenzio's twin and the bolder of the two, forfeit to Asmodeus alongside her brother on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, 1492 DR (she is eight now), and a child who suspects something is wrong in this house without knowing what.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The forfeit:** The contract claims Elzerina on Flamerule 11, 1492 DR, her ninth birthday and ten days after Founders' Day. She has never heard of the bargain, and neither has her brother.
> - **What she knows:** She believes that Osvaldo is at school. She has heard the attic howl, has seen the door behind the sun in the wine cellar and has heard her father use a funny voice at the vault door, and she does not know what any of them mean.
> - **Who knows:** Nobody outside the household and the cult knows the pact, and everyone else suspects the family and knows nothing more. The twins belong to no cult. Jarlaxle is the one exception, through **Vessa**, and he tells no member of Bregan D'aerthe who has not worked it out.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with warmth toward the child in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Elzerina's boldness as real courage, and the Secret is stated flatly with no comment on the parents.
