**Victoro Cassalanter** (Lawful evil, half-elf, he/him)

## Background

Victoro is the only living heir of the late Caladorn Cassalanter (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Caladorn Cassalanter, Victoro's father, was a former Masked Lord and a hero of the North. His ghost waits in the family crypt below the villa, where it attacks anything with a fiendish taint and helps anyone who shows a holy symbol of Siamorphe. Caladorn was human and Victoro is a half-elf, so his mother was an elf `[research]`. No file gives her name.

The Cassalanter name has stood on Waterdeep's noble registry for several generations (**A Debt in Blood**). The family's wealth is banking and money-lending, and Victoro's banking underwrites half the guilds in the city. The Cassalanter Counting House in the Sea Ward holds accounts across the North Ward. Victoro's office holds thirteen loan contracts with nobles, merchants and guilds, and they amount to control of the Chandlers', Coopers' and Carpenters' guilds, among others (**The Guilds**).

A portrait in the villa shows a somber young Victoro beside his beaming human father (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Caladorn spent long seasons in the North, and the boy was left with the counting house and its ledgers `[design]`. While Victoro was still young, his father repaid a failed loan out of the house's plate and two of its warehouses, so that no creditor could say that a Masked Lord had defaulted `[design]`. Caladorn was proud of it, and Victoro carried the ledgers that showed what it had cost `[design]`. He came away believing that his father's honor was real and that the house had barely been able to afford it `[design]`. When he took over the banking, he built the guild loans so that the house would always be the creditor `[design]`.

Victoro met Ammalia across a table in the Counting House, where she had come as the agent of a Tethyrian trading family to ask for credit `[design]`. She refused his first offer and named her own terms, and he agreed to most of them `[design]`. They married within the year, and a wedding portrait in the villa shows both of them smiling and carefree (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Osvaldo was born in 1480 DR and the twins on Flamerule 11, 1483 DR, both derived from the contract's terms (**A Debt in Blood**). A second portrait shows Ammalia with the infant twins in her lap while Victoro reads them a fairy tale (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

The family faced bankruptcy about three years before the campaign opens. The remix ties the collapse to Dagult Neverember's embezzlement, which emptied the civic funds that Victoro's loans were underwritten against (**The Open Lord's Fall**). Victoro spent that season calling in notes from guilds that had nothing left to pay with, and he saw before the season ended that lawful means would not save the house `[design]`. The bargain was made in 1489 DR, counted back from the published "three years ago". Ammalia found the way through infernal research. A pit fiend carried the contract to them, and they signed it together in the temple beneath their own villa. Victoro asked to read the terms twice and asked about the price three times before he signed `[design]`. He signed because he could find no clause to argue with, and he has not forgiven himself for finding none `[design]`.

The contract claims each child at nine. Osvaldo was nine when he was taken and is twelve now, a chain devil held in the soundproofed attic of the villa. The twins' souls are forfeit on their ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, ten days after Founders' Day. The buy-out clause asks for 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, both, paid to a devil of Asmodeus at the same time. The published text words it as "one shy of a million gold coins" and "one shy of one hundred unfortunate souls". Partial payment will not be accepted, and the family does not know this yet.

The family holds about 500,000 gp of its own, and the Vault's 500,000 gp covers the shortfall. Victoro is in charge of locating the Vault of Dragons and securing the gold in it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Cassalanters do not hunt the Stone of Golorr with agents. Victoro asks to be told when it is found, and the family moves on the vault the moment someone activates the Stone. The outposts gather gold and ritual components for the payment. The payment rite is planned for the temple's ceremonial hall on Founders' Day, led by Victoro with Ammalia present (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)).

Victoro was schooled in the divine arts (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Victoro Cassalanter*, citing *Dragon Heist*; 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. After the signing he took the altar in the temple because he was the one of the two with the training, and Ammalia took the wine `[design]`. The cult gathers in the temple at midnight on the first day of every tenday, and about thirty members attend when it meets in force, with Victoro leading the rite from the dais (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The temple lies below the wine cellar, behind a secret door marked with a sun symbol and down a spiral stair. Its thirty-foot statue of Asmodeus was carved from an image of Siamorphe, and discovery of the temple is a capital offense under the Code Legal once a magistrate has ruled it unlawful.

Victoro is known publicly as a banker, a philanthropist and a worshipper of Lathander. The house also keeps a public devotion to Siamorphe, and the villa's ballroom floor is a Siamorphe mosaic.

Eye #2 came to the Cassalanters through infernal divination, which found it in the Brandath Crypts, and their agents took it from Neverember's magical wards. It is secured in a concealed panel behind the base of the Asmodeus statue in the temple's ceremonial hall (A7), and the family knows what it does. Victoro sent Seffia Naelryke and Arn Xalrondar to retrieve it and to silence three cult members who knew too much. The pair killed two of them, and the third survived, which Victoro does not know (**Seffia Naelryke**).

Ammalia visits Osvaldo weekly and sits in the room outside the pentagram. Victoro has not entered the attic in months, and her study notes record that he still cannot enter and that he tries (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)). The city hears that Osvaldo studies in a faraway city, and the twins have been told the same. In the reading room Victoro reads histories and atlases and takes dry notes in a journal (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

**Personal goals.** Victoro wants to open the attic door and sit in the room for the length of one story `[design]`. He has gone up to the door more than once and come back down without opening it, and he would like to hear what Osvaldo says to his mother `[design]`. He also wants Ammalia to ask his opinion again before she decides, as she did in the first years of the marriage `[design]`. He gave up questioning her after the signing, and he knows that the silence is his doing as much as hers `[design]`. Last, he wants to write a history of his house `[design]`. The reading he does in the reading room is meant to feed it, and the book would begin with Caladorn's seasons in the North and end before the bargain `[design]`.

## Personality

To most of Waterdeep, Victoro Cassalanter is a devilishly handsome half-elf banker and philanthropist who worships Lathander. His profits feed and shelter the poor, and his banking underwrites half the guilds in the city. He dresses in the latest fashions and walks with a ruby-tipped cane that he does not need.

He is well schooled, suave and slow to anger, and he looks hale for his years. His smile is unreadable, and he smiles before he gives bad news. He taps the cane while he thinks, and he turns the talk to his children whenever he can. His warmth toward the twins is as well known as his manners.

When the talk turns to business he has Willifort bring fine brandy and pipes filled with pipeweed. He and Ammalia are often out on business, and they return an hour before dinner.

## Connections

- **Ammalia Cassalanter:** His wife and co-conspirator. She makes the final calls for the family and the cult, and Victoro, who leads the worship, has stopped questioning her.
- **Osvaldo Cassalanter:** His eldest son, a chain devil in the attic. Victoro has not entered the room in months, and the vigil there belongs to Ammalia.
- **Terenzio Cassalanter** and **Elzerina Cassalanter:** The twins. Victoro is openly warm with them, and they know nothing of the pact.
- **Willifort Crowelle:** His butler and personal attendant, who knows everything that happens in the villa. Willifort reports to Ammalia.
- **Seffia Naelryke** and **Arn Xalrondar:** Seffia commands the field team and answers to Victoro, and Arn is her partner. Victoro does not know that their mission to the Brandath Crypts left a witness alive.
- **Caladorn Cassalanter:** His father, whose ghost holds the family crypt and has driven the family out of it twice.
- **Yalah Gralhund** and **Hurv Taldred:** Members of the cult from House Gralhund. Yalah keeps Asmodean symbols and robes with Cassalanter embroidery in a trunk, and Hurv wears a Cassalanter devotional mark.
- **Asmodeus:** The creditor. The terms are fixed, and the god does not negotiate with mortals who have already signed.
- **Dagult Neverember:** The former Open Lord whose embezzlement ruined the loans, and whose lost gold Victoro hunts.

## Overview

Victoro Cassalanter is a devilishly handsome half-elf banker and philanthropist who worships Lathander. His banking underwrites half the guilds in the city, and his profits are said to fund charities across Waterdeep. His easy warmth with his younger children is as well known as his unreadable smile.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Victoro should inspire unease with his perfect manners (which never falter even while planning mass murder), sympathy for anyone who understands the pact and the children, and horror for anyone who realizes the sympathy was calculated.

**Emotions.** Victoro most often feels calculated charm, paternal desperation masked by composure, cold theological certainty, genuine love for his children twisted by his own choices, strategic patience, and a self-serving hunger for power that he mistakes for devotion.

**Motivations.** Victoro wants to save his younger twins from the Asmodean pact before their ninth birthday, acquire 999,999 gold pieces to fund the buy-out clause, and sacrifice 99 souls at the Founders' Day feast while maintaining his philanthropist reputation. The family already holds about 500,000 gp of its own, so the 500,000 gp in the Vault of Dragons is the shortfall to 999,999 gp.

**Inspirations.** When playing Victoro, channel Tywin Lannister (*Game of Thrones*), Frollo (*The Hunchback of Notre Dame*), and Gustavo Fring (*Breaking Bad*).

### Voice

*A charming, gracious banker-philanthropist whose perfect manners never crack, until the day they do.*

- **Sound:** A warm, cultured half-elf baritone with an easy smile in it. In his later moods (see Phases below), the warmth thins into tired, deliberate precision.
- **Sentence shape:** Public: generous, flowing, gracious sentences, compliments and invitations. In his later moods: measured, reasoned, technically true statements, and a fracture that shows as unfinished sentences.
- **Word choice:** Public: philanthropy, Lathander's light, "our city's less fortunate", "my darling twins". In his later moods: contract language ("the terms", "the clause", "the price"). He never says "Osvaldo" if he can avoid it.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Never in public. In his later moods, a single broken "Damn it all," or worse, when his children are threatened, and it's the moment he fractures.
- **Personality in speech:** Calculated charm and paternal desperation. His love for his children is real and audible. He says only true things and leaves out the one that matters (the feast). In his later moods he starts to fracture where Ammalia holds.
- **Phases:**
  - **The Generous Patrons (Ches 1, 1492 DR):** warm and at ease.
  - **Later moods:** the Desperate Parents have the mask off and are reasoned, tired, and pleading through dignity. The Hosts of Founders' Day have a terrible calm and take any genuine alternative at once, without haggling. Both are keyed to the quest phases on **The Cassalanters** (Villains).
- **Quirks:** He taps the ruby-tipped cane when thinking. He smiles before bad news. He turns the talk to his children whenever he can.
- **Signature phrases:** "Lathander's light on you." / "What can we do for the city?" / "Everything I've told you is true."
- **Never:** Publicly, he never lets anything slip about devils or pacts. He never goes to the attic, and never says the number of the feast's victims aloud.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting (public):* "Welcome, welcome! Lathander's light on you. Please, the twins have been dying to meet real adventurers."
  - *Business (public):* "The orphanage needs a new roof before winter. Whatever you're looking for, perhaps we can help each other."
  - *Later mood (Desperate Parents):* "Everything I've told you is true. My children's souls were promised. I am trying to buy them back. What would you have done?"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Victoro is a devilishly handsome half-elf banker, philanthropist, and worshipper of Lathander whose profits fund charity, with an unreadable smile and a genuine warmth toward his younger children that makes the rest of what he is harder to see. To those he trusts, he is a secret priest of Asmodeus whose pact traded his three children's souls for power. Deep down, everything the Cassalanters tell outsiders about their situation is technically true; they do not mention that the ritual also requires poisoning 99 people at the Founders' Day feast. He has not entered the attic where Osvaldo is in months and cannot bring himself to go; that vigil belongs to Ammalia. He carries a ruby-tipped cane that functions as a rod of rulership.

**Morale.** In a fight, Victoro uses his priestly magic and social authority to de-escalate, negotiate, or bribe. If combat is unavoidable, he fights to protect his family and his secret, and he is slow to anger but devastating when pushed.

**Relationships.** Victoro is the husband of Ammalia Cassalanter (co-conspirator), the father of Osvaldo (eldest, soul lost, transformed into a chain devil), Terenzio and Elzerina (the twins, forfeit on their ninth birthday), and the employer of Willifort Crowelle (doppelganger butler who knows everything that happens in the villa and reports to Ammalia).

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The pact:** Victoro and Ammalia traded the souls of their three children to Asmodeus for wealth and political access, and Victoro leads the cult that meets in the temple beneath the villa. The price of buying the twins back is 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, and the souls are the guests Ammalia plans to poison at the Founders' Day feast.
> - **Who knows:** Nobody outside the household and the cult. Everyone else suspects the family because it is too nice to be this rich and this purebred, and knows nothing more. Jarlaxle is the one exception, through **Vessa**, and he tells no member of Bregan D'aerthe who has not worked it out.
> - **Public cover:** Victoro is a worshipper of Lathander in public, and the house keeps its ancestral devotion to Siamorphe. The published adventure says a wider circle than the household and the cult knows of his devotion to Asmodeus, and the campaign overrules it.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a warm and gracious Personality and an Overview that shows only the public man. The narrator treats Victoro's love for his children and his part in the pact as two facts and takes neither side.
