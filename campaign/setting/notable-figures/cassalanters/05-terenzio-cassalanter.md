**Terenzio Cassalanter** (Lawful good, Tethyrian human, he/him)

## Background

Terenzio is one of the Cassalanters' twins, the two youngest children of Victoro and Ammalia (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He and Elzerina were born on Flamerule 11, 1483 DR, a date that no source states and that the campaign derives from their ninth birthday on Flamerule 11, 1492 DR (**A Debt in Blood**). He is a Tethyrian human `[research]`. A portrait in the villa shows Ammalia with the infant twins in her lap while Victoro reads them a fairy tale (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Terenzio grew up in the villa during the family's prosperous years, in a house where his brother Osvaldo, three years older, read to the twins at bedtime and did the voices from his own favorite stories `[design]`. The dragon in those stories had the loudest voice, and Terenzio still roars the way Osvaldo did it `[design]`. In 1489 DR, when the twins were six, Osvaldo left the house (**A Debt in Blood**). The twins were told that he had gone to a school in a faraway city, and neither of them recalls which one (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Terenzio watched the front windows for a coach during the first months after his brother left, and he has since stopped watching `[design]`. He still believes that Osvaldo is doing well at school.

The contract that took Osvaldo claims each child at nine, so Terenzio's soul is forfeit on his ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, ten days after Founders' Day (**A Debt in Blood**). He has not been taken. He belongs to no cult and does not know that the bargain exists (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

The twins are tutored privately, rarely leave the estate and have no other friends (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Their nanny, Laiba "Nana" Rosse, is also their tutor, and she counts the twins aloud whenever they move (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Cassalanter Villa** (location)). Terenzio spends the day on a schedule that he knows by heart: a piano lesson where Elzerina plays and he roars along, the sliding ladders in the library, butterflies on the lawn, and supper with his parents (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)). From the end of the lesson until supper he wears his dragon costume (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)). Nana Rosse and the twins made it together from green felt in the winter after Osvaldo left, using a drawing that Terenzio made of the dragon in his brother's stories `[design]`. The tail comes off often enough that Nana keeps a needle for it in her crochet basket `[design]`.

Around strangers Terenzio is suspicious and meek, while Elzerina is brash and outgoing, and he lets her speak for both of them until he has decided that a visitor is friendly (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He calls her Elzi (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)). His parents have forbidden him their father's office, the attic and the wine cellar area, and he is curious about all three (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). On some afternoons he lies on the floor of the third-floor corridor and looks through the gap under the office door's lower hinge, and Victoro is very cross when he finds him there (**Cassalanter Villa** (location)). When he is asked about his parents, he says that they are searching for some missing dragons, and he has never asked what they want with a bunch of scary winged reptiles (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He has heard howling from the attic two or three times and is curious about what lurks there (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He has decided that the rules about those rooms are rules for small boys, and that they will stop when he is nine `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Terenzio wants his brother to come home `[design]`. He keeps a box of dragon drawings under his bed for Osvaldo to see, and he cannot post them because neither he nor Elzerina recalls which city the school is in `[design]`. He also wants a turn as the knight `[design]`. Elzerina has the helm and the wooden sword and says that a dragon cannot be a knight, so he practices with a ruler from the schoolroom when she is not looking `[design]`. Last, he wants one of his mother's Monarchs to stay on his finger for a count of one hundred `[design]`. The butterflies have never stayed past sixty-one, and he counts aloud in Nana's voice so that he will not lose his place `[design]`.

## Personality

Around strangers Terenzio is meek and wary, and he stays close to Nana Rosse until he decides that a visitor is friendly. Once he has decided, he roars a greeting, asks whether the visitor has ever seen a dragon and is eager to show off the house. He believes everything his parents tell him and repeats it without being asked twice.

## Connections

- **Elzerina Cassalanter:** His twin sister and the bolder of the two. She chases him around the villa with a wooden sword, and he calls her Elzi.
- **Victoro Cassalanter** and **Ammalia Cassalanter:** His parents, whom he trusts completely. Ammalia supervises the twins at play in the garden, and Victoro is openly warm with them. Neither parent has told him about the bargain.
- **Osvaldo Cassalanter:** His eldest brother, who he believes is at school in a faraway city. The twins have not been told that Osvaldo is in the attic.
- **Laiba "Nana" Rosse:** The twins' caretaker and tutor. He goes to her when something frightens him, and she reports the twins' movements to the household every evening.

## Overview

Terenzio Cassalanter is one of the eight-year-old twins of Lord Victoro and Lady Ammalia Cassalanter. He is tutored at home and seldom leaves the estate. In the villa he wears a dragon costume most afternoons. His twin sister, Elzerina, chases him through the house in a knight's helm with a wooden sword.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Terenzio should inspire immediate protectiveness; delight at his dragon-costume earnestness; and creeping dread in anyone who learns that his parents' bargain makes him forfeit on his ninth birthday, Flamerule 11. He is eight now.

**Emotions.** Terenzio most often feels uncomplicated curiosity, physical joy in play, trust in the adults he knows, the particular bravery of a child who has never had reason to doubt that he is loved, and restless impatience to grow up enough to have adventures.

**Motivations.** Terenzio wants to play with his sister, explore the parts of the villa his parents told him not to explore, and eventually become someone who could go in the attic.

**Inspirations.** When playing Terenzio, channel Simba (*The Lion King*), Hiccup (*How to Train Your Dragon*, before he found the dragon), and Bastian (*The Neverending Story*).

### Voice

*A sweet, excited boy in a dragon costume who thinks the attic holds a dragon.*

- **Sound:** A high, eager child's voice that roars when he's being a dragon.
- **Sentence shape:** Excited run-on sentences and questions, and dragon noises.
- **Word choice:** Child's adventure vocabulary: dragons, missing dragons, "my brother's at school", "Nana". He calls his sister "Elzi".
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* He'd be thrilled and horrified to hear anyone else do it.
- **Personality in speech:** Curious and trusting. He happily repeats what he's heard about "missing dragons" and the howling. His bravery shows as wanting to see the attic.
- **Quirks:** He roars at people as a greeting. He asks visitors whether they have seen a dragon.
- **Signature phrases:** "RAWR!" / "Have you ever seen a dragon?" / "I think there's a dragon in the attic."
- **Never:** He never suspects his parents, never lies, and never stays where he's told.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "RAWR! I'm a dragon! Have you ever seen a real one?"
  - *Business:* "Father's looking for missing dragons. And there's howling in the attic, sometimes. I think it's a dragon too."
  - *Under pressure:* "Nana! Nana, come quick, there's a noise and it isn't a game noise!"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Terenzio is a boy in a dragon costume being chased around the twins' bedroom by his sister with a wooden sword. To those who speak with him, he knows his parents are looking for "missing dragons," he has heard howling from the attic two or three times, and he thinks his brother Osvaldo must be doing very well at school by now. Deep down, he has decided that whatever is in the attic is probably a dragon, and he considers this exciting rather than frightening.

**Morale.** Terenzio goes to Nana Rosse if frightened by anything he cannot interpret as a game.

**Relationships.** Terenzio is one of the twins (alongside Elzerina), forfeit to Asmodeus on his ninth birthday, Flamerule 11, 1492 DR (he is eight now), a child who trusts his parents completely, and a boy who is curious about the attic for entirely innocent reasons.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The pact:** The contract that took Osvaldo claims Terenzio on Flamerule 11, 1492 DR. He belongs to no cult and has not been told.
> - **What he knows:** He believes that Osvaldo is at school in a faraway city, and he thinks the howling in the attic is a dragon. He does not suspect that a similar fate awaits him.
> - **Who knows:** Nobody outside the household and the cult knows the pact. Everyone else suspects the family and knows nothing more. Jarlaxle is the one exception, through **Vessa**, and he tells no member of Bregan D'aerthe who has not worked it out.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about the dragon costume. The narrator treats Terenzio's trust in his parents as real and takes no side about it.
