**Sister Valdra** (Lawful good, half-orc, she/her)

## Background

Sister Valdra is a half-orc cleric of Tyr who looks about thirty-five, and she wears a plain dark coat over clothes that might once have been martial. She has the stillness of someone trained to stand watch, and she has worked as a temple guard. She is between postings now and takes security work to pay for a room. She holds no commission from the Order of the Gauntlet, and Savra Belabranta does not know her.

Valdra was born in a trade town on the Sword Coast road, to a human mother who kept a boarding stable and an orc father whom she barely knew `[design]`. When she was fourteen, a caravan factor claimed the stable on a forged note, and the town's magistrate would not hear a half-orc girl explain how the note had been written `[design]`. A travelling priest of Tyr heard her account in her mother's barn, wrote it down page by page and carried it to a higher court `[design]`. The forger was fined and the stable was never returned, and Valdra took from it that the law is slow and still worth the trouble `[design]`.

She followed that priest for eleven years as his pupil and learned to write evidence in order and to claim no more than the page held `[design]`. He had her ordained a cleric of Tyr, and she took posts as a door guard at temples along the coast while he went on with his cases `[design]`. He died of a winter fever in the year before she came to Waterdeep, and she kept his notebook of cases that the poor had lost `[design]`. She still writes in the back of it, and she counts her evidence aloud as he did, with "one, two, three" `[design]`.

In Waterdeep she took a post on the guard rotation at the Halls of Justice, which keeps the city's only temple of Tyr. She stood at its public doors during the court sittings and never worked the east annex, where the Order of the Gauntlet keeps its office, and she has never spoken to Savra `[design]`. She shares the Order's cause and has not been asked to swear its oath, and she has not offered to swear it `[design]`.

About six months before the campaign, a servant told the Watch a story about the Cassalanters, and the Watch dismissed it. The servant then came to the Halls of Justice to ask what an advocate would cost, and Valdra, who was on the door, heard the whole story in the vestibule `[design]`. A petitioner pays fifty gold for an advocate, and the servant could not pay it and left without filing anything `[design]`. Valdra told no one, because she had no proof and the servant asked her not to repeat it `[design]`.

Her post at the Halls ended about three months before the campaign, and she found herself with free days and no one to answer to `[design]`. She began the case that week, on her own time and without telling anyone in the Order `[design]`. Since then she has attended the family's public charity functions and stood outside the villa gate in the evenings, and she has noted who goes in and out `[design]`. She has seen Ivrala Sorn at those functions and would know her again `[design]`. She holds three things: the servant's story, her own notes on the family's gifts set against what she could confirm of them, and what she saw at the villa gate `[design]`. She cannot say what any of it adds up to, only that the family is too generous and too perfect, and she has not yet found anyone whom she can show it to. On Ches 1, 1492 DR she is looking for bouncer work and is a candidate in **Staff and Hiring**.

**Personal goals.** Valdra wants a small shrine of her own in a trade town, where she hears petitions and a petitioner is heard before anyone names a fee `[design]`. She wants to copy her teacher's notebook out fair and leave the copy with any temple of Tyr that will shelve it, so that the cases he lost are written down where a later priest can find them `[design]`. She also wants to bring her mother north to Waterdeep once she has a post that pays for a room with a stove, because her mother is alone in the trade town and writes only at midwinter `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people, Sister Valdra is a quiet half-orc temple guard who stands where she can see both the door and the room. She offers no references, and she asks what she is walking into before she agrees to anything. People who watch her work notice that she is a very good bouncer and that she notices more than the job asks of her.

She is slow to trust and plain about it. Once she decides that someone will listen, she lays out what she knows in numbered points and speaks faster than she means to.

## Connections

- **Savra Belabranta:** The head of the Order's Waterdeep chapter, who suspects the same family and does not know Valdra. Valdra will not take her suspicion to Savra without something that a magistrate would read.
- **The Cassalanters:** The family Valdra is watching. She has the same suspicion as the Order and none of its file.
- **Ivrala Sorn:** A Cassalanter servant whom Valdra has seen at the family's functions and could recognize.
- **The servant:** The person whose story the Watch dismissed. Valdra keeps the servant's name to herself and holds the story as her first piece of evidence.
- **Her teacher:** A travelling priest of Tyr who died the year before she came to Waterdeep, nameless on this page `[design]`.
- **The Order of the Gauntlet:** Valdra shares its cause and has no standing in it. She is unofficial, and she has not been asked to swear its oath.

## Overview

Sister Valdra is a half-orc temple guard between postings who takes security and bouncer work. She wears a plain dark coat, offers no references and watches the room more closely than the job asks. She asks what she is walking into before she agrees to anything.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Sister Valdra should inspire immediate respect for the specificity of her evidence; sympathy for a woman who built a case no one asked her to build; and protective frustration that Savra cannot know and therefore cannot officially help.

**Emotions.** Sister Valdra most often feels focused determination, the controlled anger of someone who has been right for months and cannot get anyone official to act, genuine care for the servant whose story no one believed, and quiet relief when she finally finds someone willing to listen.

**Motivations.** Sister Valdra wants to build a case against the Cassalanters that Savra or the Watch can act on, protect the people who can still be protected, and work within Tyr's framework even when the framework is slow.

**Inspirations.** When playing Sister Valdra, channel Erin Brockovich (*Erin Brockovich*), Rosa Diaz (*Brooklyn Nine-Nine*), and Brienne of Tarth (*Game of Thrones*).

### Voice

*A dogged half-orc temple guard who built a case nobody asked for and finally has someone to tell.*

- **Sound:** A deep, rough, steady voice. It speeds up with suppressed urgency when she's finally allowed to talk about the case.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, practical sentences in her bouncer role. When she presents evidence, organised, numbered and precise, like a prosecutor.
- **Word choice:** Guard's plainness and investigator's precision: "evidence", "witness", "on record". Tyr is "the Just God". She gives the servant whose story no one believed a name only to someone she trusts.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Casual in her bouncer role ("Out, and take your shit with you."). Controlled when presenting the case.
- **Personality in speech:** Focused determination and months of controlled anger. Relief at finally being heard makes her talk too fast. Her voice holds suspicion only (see the note below): "something is wrong in that house", never a named devil.
- **Quirks:** She lays out evidence in order, with "one, two, three". She checks who else is listening before she starts.
- **Signature phrases:** "I have it on record." / "Nobody believed her." / "One. Two. Three."
- **Never:** She never exaggerates the evidence, never involves Savra officially, and never names what the Cassalanters worship, because she doesn't know.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Bouncer work, six gold a tenday. You look like trouble. Good trouble or the other kind?"
  - *Business:* "Three things. One, a servant's story the Watch threw out. Two, my notes on the family's gifts. Three, what I saw at the villa gate. Something is wrong in that house."
  - *Under pressure:* "Get behind the bar. Nobody touches the witness."

> Note: Sister Valdra's voice holds suspicion only, per the Cassalanter secrecy rule. Her page makes no link to Asmodeus.

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Sister Valdra is a half-orc temple guard who does security work and looks for honest employment as a bouncer or muscle. To those who earn her trust, she has been building a case against the Cassalanters for three months on her own time, without orders and without telling Savra, based on a servant's story the Watch dismissed six months ago. Deep down, she holds three pieces of evidence that something is badly wrong behind the villa's charity (she cannot say what, only that the family is too generous and too perfect), and she has been waiting for someone with the authority — or the recklessness — to use them.

**Morale.** In a fight, Sister Valdra positions herself at the front and does not retreat from a threat to someone in her care. She calls on Tyr and means it.

**Relationships.** Sister Valdra is an unofficial Order of the Gauntlet operative (unsanctioned by Savra Belabranta), the holder of three independent pieces of Cassalanter intelligence, a woman who has seen Ivrala Sorn at Cassalanter functions and could recognize her, and a Trollskull Manor bouncer candidate.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **What she holds:** Valdra's three pieces are suspicion and nothing more. She does not know what the Cassalanters worship, and no page gives her proof of a pact.
> - **Who she can recognize:** Valdra has seen Ivrala Sorn at Cassalanter functions. The sources give conflicting versions of where she saw her and whether she also knows Rella Asp, and the page settles none of them (see Draft Notes). The recognition itself happens only once Valdra and Ivrala meet at Trollskull Manor, and it lives in **Staff and Hiring**.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with respect for Valdra's patience. The Background treats her teacher and the servant gently, and the narrator does not make a hero of her.
