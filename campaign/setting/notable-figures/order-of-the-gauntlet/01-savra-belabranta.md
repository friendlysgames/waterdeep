**Savra Belabranta** (Neutral good, Tethyrian human, she/her)

## Background

Savra Belabranta is a knight of the Order of the Gauntlet, and she heads its Waterdeep chapter from a clerk's office in the east annex of the Halls of Justice, where about a dozen Knights and Priests answer to her (**Castle Ward**). She is in her late twenties and looks older, with dark hair worn back, a strong jaw and the patient, measured manner of a woman who learned to think before she speaks (**The Order of the Gauntlet** in Organizations). She comes from the Belabranta family, a noble house that raises griffons for the Griffon Cavalry (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Savra grew up in the family's mews, where she fed the fledglings before dawn and learned to read a griffon's temper from the set of its shoulders `[design]`. She found that a bird bonded to a handler knows when it is being managed with tricks, and she has judged people by the same rule since `[design]`. She had a younger brother who followed the work into the Cavalry, and the two of them shared the early feeding rounds `[design]`. She left the family house in her early twenties after a quarrel over a marriage that her father had arranged, and she took hired sword work for a year before she found what she was looking for `[design]`.

The wiki says that she joined the Feathergale Knights, a cover group for the Cult of the Howling Hatred, and that she was indoctrinated there into the worship of Yan-C-Bin (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Savra Belabranta*, citing *Princes of the Apocalypse*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. It adds that the cult brainwashed her completely and that she believed it should use elemental fury only against Waterdeep's enemies (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. She spent three years in the Howling Hatred and left it in 1491 DR, about a year before the campaign begins (**Castle Ward**). She does not hide that she belonged to the cult, and she tells what she did there only to someone she has come to trust (**The Order of the Gauntlet** in Organizations).

When Savra left the cult, the Order took her in. Father Corwen Thale presided over her oath `[design]`, and she stood afterward in the east annex before the wooden statue of Tyr with its right hand carved away at the wrist, which is why she calls him the Maimed God (**Tyr**) `[design]`. She became a knight and took the Waterdeep chapter within the year, and she has never held the rank of Righteous Hand (**The Order of the Gauntlet** in Organizations). She serves Tyr to regain her honor and to atone for the evil she committed as a cultist (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and the Order is the work she found that made those amends mean something.

From the clerk's office Savra assigns the chapter's work and swears in new members (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Savra Belabranta*, 5e). She visits each candidate's residence herself (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and she reads the candidate's answers before the oath, because she wants no recruit sworn by someone who has not asked `[design]`. Her years in the cult left her attuned to the signs of infernal operations, and for months she has suspected the Cassalanters, a Sea Ward family whose public piety never slips and whose Watch complaints were all closed without findings (**The Order of the Gauntlet** in the Factions Guide). She has no legal evidence, she names no household until a document or a sworn answer does, and she does not know what the family worships.

What Savra misses is the mews at dawn, with the fledglings crying for their meat and her brother arguing over whose turn it was to scrub the perches `[design]`. The Cavalry flies from Peaktop Aerie on Mount Waterdeep (**Castle Ward**), and she sometimes watches the riders cross the ward from the steps of the Halls of Justice without going up to the Aerie `[design]`. Her brother became a handler at the Aerie and stopped answering her letters in her second year away `[design]`. She has sent no word of the cult to the mews since she left it `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Savra wants to tell her brother to his face that she left the cult, and to hear whatever he says back `[design]`. She has started the letter more than once and burned each draft, because a letter would let him read it without her in the room `[design]`. She also wants to ride a griffon over the city once, on a morning with no errand, as she did before she left the mews `[design]`. She sets no date for either, because she will not go to the Aerie until she can answer his first question plainly `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people in Waterdeep, Savra is a knight of the Order who says what she thinks. She is blunt, she pauses before every serious answer, and she has no patience for anyone who tries to manage her, because she can tell when it is happening and says so.

She has a hunter's eye for infernal work and a cold, controlled anger at diabolism, and she shows warmth through what she does for people more than through what she says to them. When a question about her own past is sincere, she answers it plainly and says where she stops.

## Connections

- **Her chapter:** About a dozen Knights and Priests answer to her. Dame Rhosyn Cullane, Sir Anselm Greave and Sir Merrick Tollwood are Knights known by name, and Warden Osric Wickersham keeps the armory (**Player Factions Overview**).
- **The Halls of Justice:** Father Corwen Thale has presided over the Order's oaths for twenty years, and Brother Haldric Wence, the door-warden, keeps messages for Savra. Isaldric Veyne is the high priest of Tyr in Waterdeep.
- **Tyr:** The god she serves, whom she calls the Maimed God (**Tyr**).
- **Sister Valdra:** A half-orc cleric of Tyr who is building a case against the Cassalanters on her own time. Savra does not know her (**Sister Valdra**).
- **Hlam:** A staunch ally of the Order, who leads a small monastic group devoted to Tyr from a cave on Mount Waterdeep (**Hlam**).
- **The Cassalanters:** The family she suspects and cannot prove anything against (**The Cassalanters**).
- **Xanathar's Guild:** An enemy. She counts its use of intellect devourers as an abomination (**Xanathar's Guild**).
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** An enemy. She knows the cell as the Splinter and names no master for it (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**).
- **The Open Lord's Watch:** A wary neutral. The Watch closed every complaint against the family she suspects (**The Open Lord's Watch**).
- **Her brother:** A Griffon Cavalry handler at Peaktop Aerie, who stopped answering her letters and is the reason for her first personal goal `[design]`.

## Overview

Savra Belabranta is a knight of the Order of the Gauntlet and leads its Waterdeep chapter from the Halls of Justice. She comes from a noble family that raises griffons for the Griffon Cavalry, and she is in her late twenties. She wears her dark hair back and speaks in the patient, measured way of a woman who thinks before she answers.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Savra should inspire respect for her hard-won virtue, sympathy for the cult past she carries, and trust with the directness that makes her incapable of managing around uncomfortable truths.

**Emotions.** Savra most often feels disciplined resolve, old shame she has learned to carry rather than hide, fierce protectiveness, measured anger at diabolism, honest assessment of her own failings, and warmth that she expresses through action rather than sentiment.

**Motivations.** Savra wants to atone for her time in the Howling Hatred cult, root out the infernal cults she hunts wherever they hide in Waterdeep, find out what the Cassalanters are concealing behind their charity, and build an Order chapter that values honesty above all.

**Inspirations.** When playing Savra, channel Brienne of Tarth (*Game of Thrones*), Furiosa (*Mad Max: Fury Road*), and Jean Valjean (*Les Misérables*).

### Voice

*A blunt, hard-won knight who says the uncomfortable thing plainly, because managing people is a lie she won't tell.*

- **Sound:** A steady, low, controlled voice, deliberate and unhurried, as if she has learned to count to three before speaking.
- **Sentence shape:** Plain, complete, direct sentences with no diplomatic padding. She states the hard truth first, then the reasoning.
- **Word choice:** Knightly but unadorned: duty, truth, "I was wrong", "that's not good enough". Tyr is invoked as "the Maimed God". She calls the party by name and expects the same.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Rare, and aimed squarely at wrongdoing ("That's a fucking lie, and you know it."). She owns it and doesn't apologise.
- **Personality in speech:** Disciplined resolve and old shame she carries openly. She'll speak about the cult if asked, never about what she did in it, and she says so. Anger at diabolism is controlled and cold. Her suspicions of the noble houses are voiced as suspicions, never as knowledge.
- **Quirks:** She pauses before every serious answer. She says "I'll tell you plainly," and then does.
- **Signature phrases:** "I'll tell you plainly." / "That's not good enough." / "Ask me. I'll answer."
- **Never:** She never softens a truth, never boasts, and never claims knowledge she lacks.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Savra Belabranta, Order of the Gauntlet. Ask me what you like, and I'll tell you plainly."
  - *Business:* "Something in this city is rotten behind a pious face. I can't prove it yet, and I need paper a magistrate would read."
  - *Under pressure:* "Behind me. I've faced worse than this, and I was on the wrong side of it then."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Savra is a knight in her late twenties who looks older, dark hair worn back, strong jaw, the measured manner of someone who has learned to think before speaking. To anyone who asks her sincerely, she says that she spent three years in the Howling Hatred cult and left it in 1491 DR, about a year ago, and that the Order took her in afterward. She tells what she did in it only to a member she has come to trust. Deep down, her goodness is hard-won in a way that makes it more convincing, and she cannot work with people she is managing around.

**Morale.** In a fight, Savra positions herself where the line is thinnest and holds it. She kills what needs killing and moves on without commentary. Diabolists die first; everything else queues behind them.

**Relationships.** Savra is the Order of the Gauntlet faction contact, the head of the Waterdeep chapter (a clerk's office at the Halls of Justice and a rotation of about a dozen Knights and Priests who answer to her), and a member of the Belabranta noble family (griffon breeders for the Griffon Cavalry). Her cult past makes her attuned to the signs of infernal operations. She has never held the rank of Righteous Hand.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Savra was in the Howling Hatred for three years, and she tells that to anyone who asks her sincerely. What she did there she tells only to a member she has come to trust, and this page does not set it down. The deed lives in **Savra's Past**.
> - She suspects the Cassalanters and cannot prove it. She says "the family" until a player names them, then adds that she has no proof. She does not know what the family worships, and she names no pact.

**Tone:** Earnest and plain in the GM text, with a dry edge in Personality for her impatience with people who manage her. The narrator treats her atonement as real and neither excuses nor mocks it, and it keeps her brother and the mews in the Background as her own business.
