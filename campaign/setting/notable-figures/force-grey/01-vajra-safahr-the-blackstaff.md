**Vajra Safahr** (Lawful neutral, Tethyrian human, she/her)

## Background

Vajra is a Tethyrian human whose former home was Tethyr, and her parents were Tamik al Safahr and Parama yr Manshaka (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Vajra Safahr*, citing *Blackstaff Tower*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. She showed a gift for magic as a child and read everything that her first teachers would lend her `[design]`. Her mother still writes to her every season, and Vajra answers each letter with a single page `[design]`. The wiki says that she loves an arcane challenge, is patient, and pursues several plans at once, often so that her rivals act in her interest and not their own (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Vajra Safahr*, citing *Warriors of Waterdeep*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

Vajra learned wizardry from Samark Dhanzscul, who was her mentor and later her lover (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Vajra Safahr*, citing *Blackstaff Tower*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. He taught her to write down what a spell did before she cast it a second time, and she still keeps a notebook of that kind `[design]`. Samark was killed in 1479 DR, and Vajra took the Blackstaff that year (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. She was in her early twenties, because the published adventure puts her in her mid-thirties in 1492 DR `[research]`, and she is the youngest person ever to hold the title (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The adventure says that Khelben Arunsun handpicked her for the job and that she wields the staff from which he took his name (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Samark had been building a ward when he died, and Vajra has worked on it alone at night ever since, with no teacher left to ask `[design]`.

In 1479 DR `[research]` agents of Khondar Naomal took Vajra, and Renaer Neverember and Meloon Wardragon got her away from them (**Renaer Neverember**; **Meloon Wardragon**). Meloon has never mentioned it since, and Renaer's page says that it left the Blackstaff permanently in his debt (same pages). The adventure calls Vajra a friend of Renaer's (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She has not thanked either man aloud, and she makes time for both at any hour `[design]`.

Vajra runs Blackstaff Academy, a school for mages, out of Blackstaff Tower in the Castle Ward, and she heads the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki says that she wants to make Waterdeep and her Academy the center of magical study in Faerûn, and that she walks the Tower's libraries and laboratories under magical silence so as not to disturb the other wizards (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Vajra Safahr*, citing *Warriors of Waterdeep*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. She lets her best students read her own spellbook, and some of them add their own spells to it (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. She commands Force Grey from the Tower and is always looking for adventurers who bring unusual skills, abilities or spells (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Several of the older and more seasoned wizards in Waterdeep consider Vajra an upstart, and they are careful not to challenge her (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki says that she learned to ignore her early self-doubt and to dismiss the envy of skeptics (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Vajra Safahr*, citing *Death Masks* and *Warriors of Waterdeep*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`, and the adventure says that she still questions whether she can meet the demands of the role (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She settles most of those questions by asking the Blackstaff, which holds Khelben's spirit and the spirits of the Blackstaffs before him, and she consults it alone `[design]`. Since about 1489 DR she has answered to Laeral Silverhand, who views her as an insecure child holding her husband's staff and wants it back (**Laeral Silverhand**). Vajra has sent members of Force Grey on missions that Laeral did not authorize, and Laeral has not confronted her about it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Vajra is at Blackstaff Tower, where she spends her days on the city's arcane threats and her nights on Samark's ward `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Vajra wants to finish Samark's ward `[design]`. She has not shown it to anyone at the Academy, and she does not yet know whether it will work `[design]`. She also wants to go home to Tethyr for a tenday with no *Sending* to answer `[design]`. She has not been back since she took the Blackstaff, and each year she finds a reason to put the trip off `[design]`. Last, she wants to choose an heir for the staff, because the wiki says it became customary for a Blackstaff to do so (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Blackstaff*; edition not stated) `[research: wiki only]`, and no campaign file names hers. She hopes to give that student what nobody gave her, which is a few more years of being only a student before the staff `[design]`. She has been watching the Academy's students for some time and has told none of them `[design]`.

## Personality

To most Waterdhavians, Vajra is the Blackstaff, a small woman with olive skin, short black hair and brown eyes who carries a silver-shod staff that stands several feet above her head (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Vajra Safahr*, citing *Blackstaff Tower*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. She dresses practically and never looks cold (Alexandrian remix, Appendix D). She sends orders by *Sending*, usually in exactly 25 words, and she uses all of them. When a matter needs more, she summons the people involved to Blackstaff Tower and tells them at her standing desk, where no chair stands for visitors. Students at the Academy know her as the teacher who appears at their elbow when they are stuck and is gone again before they can thank her (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Vajra Safahr*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

She does not perform a warmth that she does not feel, and she is not unkind. Her dry humor surfaces without warning and she delivers it flat. A comment about her youth earns a cold stare and a change of subject. She asks the people who report to her not to tell her only what she wants to hear, because she has people for that already.

## Connections

- **Laeral Silverhand:** The Open Lord and Vajra's superior, who views her as an insecure child wielding her late husband's staff. The two avoid each other, and Laeral has not confronted Vajra about the unauthorized missions.
- **Khelben Arunsun:** The original Blackstaff and Laeral's late husband, who handpicked Vajra. His spirit is in the staff, and Vajra will not discuss what is in it.
- **Samark Dhanzscul:** Her mentor and lover, who was killed in 1479 DR `[research: wiki only]`. She is finishing his ward alone `[design]`.
- **Renaer Neverember:** A friend who helped rescue her and to whom she is permanently in debt.
- **Meloon Wardragon:** An active member of Force Grey who reports to her and who helped rescue her more than a decade ago.
- **Ulkoria Stonemarrow:** A senior member of the Watchful Order that Vajra heads. The campaign makes her a Masked Lord, and no source says that Vajra knows.
- **The Open Lord and the Masked Lords:** Vajra is not a Masked Lord. Only the Open Lord can strip her of the title of Blackstaff.
- **Manshoon:** The head of the Zhentarim's arcane cell, who counts the Blackstaff among his powerful enemies. Vajra has no name for him and calls the cell "the Splinter".
- **Xanathar's Guild:** Countering the Guild's mind-control operations is her most urgent concern, and she suspects a link between the coercion and the gang war.
- **The Cassalanters:** Her scrying has found residue beneath their villa. She cannot say what makes it and does not guess.
- **The Harpers:** Harper agents are one of her sources of intelligence, with her own network of spies.
- **Older wizards of Waterdeep:** Several consider her an upstart and know better than to challenge her.

## Overview

Vajra Safahr is the Blackstaff of Waterdeep and the youngest person ever to hold the title. She runs Blackstaff Academy from Blackstaff Tower in the Castle Ward and heads the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors. She communicates by *Sending* spell and declines to perform a warmth she does not feel.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Vajra should inspire respect for the weight she carries, discomfort with her refusal to perform warmth she does not feel, and sympathy when the party realizes how young she is and how tired the post has made her.

**Emotions.** Vajra most often feels burdened determination, impatient competence, defensive pride, controlled anger at threats to Waterdeep, dry humor that surfaces at unexpected moments, and private exhaustion she will not show.

**Motivations.** Vajra wants to defend Waterdeep using Force Grey, prove that she earned the Blackstaff rather than inherited it, and outlast the senior wizards who consider her an upstart.

**Inspirations.** When playing Vajra, channel Ahsoka Tano (*Star Wars*), Jessica Pearson (*Suits*), and Satsuki Kiryuin (*Kill la Kill*).

### Voice

*The young Blackstaff: clipped, impatient, competent, and done performing warmth for anyone.*

- **Sound:** A sharp, quick, tired voice that sounds older than she is.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, efficient sentences, orders and conclusions. Her Sendings are exactly 25 words, telegraphic and complete.
- **Word choice:** Command vocabulary: deploy, contain, Force Grey, "my people". She calls Laeral "the Open Lord", precisely, and never mentions Khelben. She calls wizards who doubt her by their surnames.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Colourful · Rant.* Tired, casual and frequent in private ("I have not slept in two days, and some shit-brained cultist just opened a portal."). Clean in public, barely.
- **Personality in speech:** Burdened determination and defensive pride. Comments about her youth earn a cold stare and a subject change. Her dry humour surfaces unexpectedly, delivered deadpan. Exhaustion shows in shortened sentences, never in complaint.
- **Quirks:** She counts Sending words on her fingers. She answers "Are you alright?" with "Irrelevant."
- **Signature phrases:** "Irrelevant." / "Force Grey will handle it." / "Next."
- **Never:** She never discusses what's in the Blackstaff, never asks for sympathy, and never lets anyone see her doubt herself.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting (Sending):* "Blackstaff here. Rogue construct, Castle Ward, two hours ago. You are closest. Contain it, do not destroy it. Report to Blackstaff Tower by dusk tonight."
  - *Business:* "I need it done quietly and I need it done tonight. Can you do that, or do I send someone who can?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Down! Stay down. I've got it. I said I've got it."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Vajra is the Blackstaff, commander of Force Grey, who communicates by Sending (often exactly 25 words) and does not perform warmth. To those she trusts, she cares about Waterdeep and the people in it, and her years as the youngest Blackstaff ever have worn her down. Deep down, she wields a staff containing Khelben Arunsun's soul, and Laeral Silverhand views her as an insecure child holding her dead husband's weapon. She does not find observations about her youth interesting.

**Morale.** In a fight, Vajra would open with something large enough to end the conversation. She explains herself afterward to exactly no one.

**Relationships.** Vajra is the Blackstaff of Waterdeep, the commander of Force Grey, the wielder of a staff containing Khelben Arunsun's soul, and a strained subordinate of Laeral Silverhand. She has rare, dry humor, which she delivers flat. She says "the Splinter" for the Zhentarim's arcane cell, because she does not know the name of its leader.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Vajra knows that magical coercion is spreading among Waterdhavians in positions of influence, and she suspects a link to the gang war. She knows that the Black Network has split.
> - She does not know that the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced or that four factions are competing for the vault.
> - Her scrying from Blackstaff Tower has found conjuration and abjuration residue beneath the Cassalanter villa. Nobody in Force Grey knows about the pact, and Vajra does not guess at the cause.
> - She has secretly sent members of Force Grey on unauthorized missions. Laeral knows and has not confronted her.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, gentle humor about how young the Blackstaff is and how little she cares to hear it. The narrator treats her exhaustion and her grief for Samark as real and does not mock either.
