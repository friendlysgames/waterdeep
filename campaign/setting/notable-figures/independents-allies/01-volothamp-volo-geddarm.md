# Volothamp "Volo" Geddarm

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Illuskan human chronicler, chaotic good*. Stat block: **Mage (with modifications)**.
> - **Affiliation:** Independent (ally)
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Volo should inspire amusement with his inflated self-importance, endearment with his tearful concern for Floon, and exasperation when his generosity turns out to be a tavern deed instead of gold.

**Emotions.** Volo most often feels bombastic self-assurance, open affection for friends, theatrical distress, cheerful obliviousness to his own reputation, wounded dignity, and impulsive generosity he cannot afford.

**Motivations.** Volo wants to promote his latest book, protect his friends at any cost (especially Floon and Renaer), and find the next subject worthy of a Volo's Guide.

**Inspirations.** When playing Volo, channel Gilderoy Lockhart (*Harry Potter*), Jack Sparrow (*Pirates of the Caribbean*), and Zapp Brannigan (*Futurama*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Volo is a bombastic chronicler, wizard, and celebrity who trusts you have noted the violence in our fair city. To those he trusts, he is a soft-hearted man who will give away his last asset to help a friend. Deep down, he knows his reputation as an embellisher is earned, and it terrifies him that someday no one will believe him when it matters.

**Morale.** In a fight, Volo would cast a spell, miss, monologue about the time he fought something worse, and then genuinely risk his life for anyone he considers a friend. His bravery is real; his competence is intermittent.

**Relationships.** Volo is a close friend of Floon Blagmaar and Renaer Neverember, the man who outbid Emmek Frewn for the Trollskull Manor deed (which he gives to the party), and a regular at the Yawning Portal who has worn out his welcome with Durnan at least twice.

## Overview

Volothamp Geddarm is a bombastic chronicler, celebrity wizard, and self-promoter who drinks at the Yawning Portal and occasionally writes guidebooks to places he may not have visited. He hires the party to find his missing friend Floon Blagmaar.
