# Meloon Wardragon

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Human fighter, neutral good (when himself) / neutral evil (while occupied)*. Stat block: **Warrior Veteran**.
> - **Affiliation:** Force Grey (occupied by a Xanathar's Guild intellect devourer after the Field of Triumph, about three tendays before the first day of the watch)
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**, **The Dragon in the Harbor**, **The Trouble with Meloon**, **Destroy the Intellect Factory**, **The Legate's Eyes**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Meloon should inspire trust with his warmth and optimism (Act I), growing unease as subtle wrongness accumulates, and horror when the party discovers that a devourer has been riding their friend. His brain is alive under it, and the party can force the devourer out in **The Trouble with Meloon**.

**Emotions.** The real Meloon most often feels cheerful optimism, iron loyalty, open grief for fallen friends, physical joy in combat, open admiration for younger adventurers, and simple contentment. The occupied Meloon performs these emotions with slight miscalibrations: he orders something new, asks with too much specificity, misremembers small details.

**Motivations.** The real Meloon wants to fight alongside people he admires, honor Tymora through brave action, and protect Waterdeep. The intellect devourer wants to steer adventurers away from Undermountain, serve Xanathar's interests, and avoid detection.

**Inspirations.** When playing Meloon, channel Thor (*Marvel*, early films); once occupied, shift to the T-1000 (*Terminator 2*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Meloon Wardragon is a barrel-chested warrior who enters every room at full volume — laugh, handshake, Azuredge at his hip and demonstrated on request. He fills taverns with warmth and expects nothing back for it. To those he trusts, he is the friend who shows up unprompted, who rescued Vajra alongside Renaer without being asked and never mentioned it again, who carries quiet grief for colleagues he has outlived. Deep down, Nihiloor's intellect devourer has marked him as a target precisely because he is trusted, well-connected, and constitutionally unable to believe that a friend's body could be used against him. The devourer occupies him after the Field of Triumph, about three tendays before the first day of the watch, and it keeps his brain alive and reads it. It has taken him into the lair twice by then. The wrongness is harder to name than to describe: the third person, the implausible Azuredge excuses, the drinks he never used to order, the directions through the sewers that lead straight into Xanathar patrols.

**Morale.** Before the occupation, Meloon fights with reckless generosity, placing himself between allies and the worst of it and trusting Azuredge's return-throw completely. While the intellect devourer rides him, he fights competently as a Warrior Veteran but steers outcomes to serve Xanathar's interests. He never draws Azuredge and never swears. Each dawn the devourer must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma save to touch the axe, and the axe is Meloon's anchor when the party forces the devourer out. Once it is out, the real Meloon wakes with 1 level of Exhaustion and speaks only in fragments until a Long Rest. Then he remembers the whole occupation as a long dream, and he never knows what the devourer passed on.

**Relationships.** Meloon is an active member of Force Grey (reporting to Vajra Safahr, whose life he saved more than a decade ago alongside Renaer), the closest friend of Renaer Neverember (whose growing unease, without knowing why, becomes alarm once the occupation takes hold), a Yawning Portal regular whose surface thoughts Bonnie reads as comprehensibly normal until the occupation, and, after the Field of Triumph, a host for Nihiloor's intellect devourer, which reports to the Guild through a courier because telepathy reaches only 60 feet.

## Overview

Meloon Wardragon is a barrel-chested Force Grey warrior who enters every room at full volume, with a laugh, a handshake and the legendary axe Azuredge at his hip. He fills taverns with warmth and will fight alongside anyone he calls a friend, until a devourer takes him over.
