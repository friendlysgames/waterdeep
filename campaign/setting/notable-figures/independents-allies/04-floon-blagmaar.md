# Floon Blagmaar

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Illuskan human, chaotic good*. Stat block: **Commoner**.
> - **Affiliation:** Independent (ally)
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, **Zhentarim Warehouse**, **Xanathar Sewer Hideout**, **Trollskull Manor**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Floon should inspire protectiveness with his vulnerability and gratitude, endearment with his earnest loyalty to Renaer, and mild exasperation with his refusal to plan beyond tomorrow.

**Emotions.** Floon most often feels cheerful aimlessness, open gratitude, social warmth, confused anxiety under pressure, devotion to friends, and shame about his lack of direction that he masks with charm.

**Motivations.** Floon wants to stay close to the people who care about him, avoid the consequences of his gambling habit, and figure out what to do with a life that has coasted on good looks and better luck.

**Inspirations.** When playing Floon, channel Joey Tribbiani (*Friends*), Ser Dontos (*Game of Thrones*), and Andy Dwyer (*Parks and Recreation*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Floon is a handsome man in his early thirties with wavy red-blond hair who gets by on looks and does not know what to do with his life. To those he trusts, he is eternally grateful, shows affection by hugging, and stays close to anyone he believes can protect him. Deep down, he has better instincts than he appears to have and worse judgment than those instincts deserve.

**Morale.** In a fight, Floon would panic, look for someone to stand behind, and then do something unexpectedly brave if a friend is in danger. He cannot fight, but he will not run if Renaer is still in the room.

**Relationships.** Floon is the devoted friend of Renaer Neverember (was kidnapped because he was mistaken for Renaer), an acquaintance of Volo (who hires the party to find him), and a man whose gambling debts may lead him to ask the party for 500 gp later in the campaign.

## Overview

Floon Blagmaar is a handsome, aimless man in his early thirties who gets by on charm, moves through Waterdeep's taverns on Renaer Neverember's social current, and attracts trouble he does not seek.
