# Harug Shieldsunder

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Shield dwarf fighter, lawful neutral*. Stat block: **Veteran**.
> - **Affiliation:** Independent (ally)
> - **Featured in:** Background figure; no scripted appearance.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Harug should inspire confidence with his unshakable physical presence, endearment with his gruff loyalty, and amusement when he expresses complex emotions through one-word answers.

**Emotions.** Harug most often feels protective vigilance, gruff contentment, unspoken pride, stubborn refusal to explain himself, comfortable silence, and occasional surprise at how much he enjoys these people.

**Motivations.** Harug wants to keep the Black Tears alive through whatever Renaer drags them into, and drink in peace. He does not complicate things further.

**Inspirations.** When playing Harug, channel Gimli (*The Lord of the Rings*), Sandor Clegane (*Game of Thrones*), and Drax (*Guardians of the Galaxy*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Harug is a shield dwarf who drinks with Renaer's friends and says little. To those he trusts, he is the one who checks that everyone got home safe. Deep down, he has found something worth protecting, and he would rather break a table than admit it.

**Morale.** In a fight, Harug would step forward, shield up, and hold the line until everyone behind him is clear. He does not retreat while allies are still engaged.

**Relationships.** Harug is a member of Renaer's Black Tears and the group's frontline fighter, paired informally with Parlek as the muscle-and-finesse pairing of the circle.

## Overview

Harug Shieldsunder is a shield dwarf fighter and the frontline muscle of Renaer Neverember's Black Tears, a man who says little and holds the line.
