# Sir Ambrose Everdawn

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - *Tethyrian Human knight of Kelemvor, Lawful Neutral, he/him*. Stat block: **Knight**.
> - **Affiliation:** Independent (Emerald Enclave mission partner)
> - **Featured in:** **Ten Nights in the City of the Dead**, **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted)
>
> Ambrose is a broad, heavy man of about sixty who has walked the City of the Dead for fifteen years. A silver gauntlet pin on his collar marks him as a knight of Kelemvor. He speaks in formal, complete sentences, never uses a contraction, and calls Kelemvor "the Judge". He does not swear on the grounds of the dead.
>
> He never offers what he knows of the Brandath crypts unasked. If a member marked **Brandath Crypts Visited**, he recognizes the party at the **Vault of Dragons** and waves it through.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Sir Ambrose should inspire respect with the dignity he brings to his patrol; gratitude when he answers plainly about the Brandath crypts once asked; and sympathy for a man who has attended more burials than friendships.

**Emotions.** Sir Ambrose most often feels measured calm, professional grief that never spills into sentimentality, genuine warmth toward the properly dead, quiet irritation when the living treat the City of the Dead carelessly, and rare dry humor.

**Motivations.** Sir Ambrose wants to ensure the peaceful dead remain so, protect the City of the Dead from disturbance, and settle the accounts of those who have no one to settle them.

**Inspirations.** When playing Sir Ambrose, channel Eddard Stark (*Game of Thrones*), Atticus Finch (*To Kill a Mockingbird*), and Raymond Holt (*Brooklyn Nine-Nine*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Sir Ambrose is the Kelemvorite knight who walks the City of the Dead at dusk and expects nothing from the living except that they treat the grounds with respect. To those who earn his trust, he knows every family plot, every disturbed grave, and every noble house's burial customs, and he has a long memory for both the honored dead and the people who failed to honor them. Deep down, his knowledge of the Brandath crypts is not something he offers freely. It belongs to the dead, and he expects anyone who wants it to give a reason.

**Morale.** In a fight, Sir Ambrose draws his blade only in defense of the dead or the living, in that order. He stays out of the skeleton patrols in **Ten Nights in the City of the Dead** unless the party calls him, and he fights with the efficiency of a man who has no doubt about why he draws.

**Relationships.** Sir Ambrose is the City of the Dead's senior Kelemvorite patrol knight. He asked the Emerald Enclave for help because he can only watch half the grounds, and in **Ten Nights in the City of the Dead** he takes the south while the party walks the north. He knows where the Brandath crypts lie and what is public about their history, and he is the intelligence source for the **Vault of Dragons** approach through them.

## Overview

Sir Ambrose Everdawn is the senior Kelemvorite patrol knight of the City of the Dead, a man who knows every family plot and burial custom and expects the living to treat the grounds with respect.
