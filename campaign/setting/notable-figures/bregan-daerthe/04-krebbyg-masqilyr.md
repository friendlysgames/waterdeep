**Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** (Chaotic neutral, drow, he/him)

## Background

Krebbyg Masq'il'yr is a young drow whose house was destroyed long ago, and the fall left him with no connection to his old life in the Underdark (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). After the house was gone he had no ties left below, so he joined Bregan D'aerthe (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Krebbyg Masq'il'yr*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. He joined a company of rogue males from destroyed and disgraced houses, so his past was an ordinary one there (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**). He and Soluun Xibrindas and Fel'rekt Lafeen carry out the dirty deeds that Jarlaxle assigns in the city (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Krebbyg Masq'il'yr*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Krebbyg grew up in a small house of the Underdark that gave him guard shifts and very little else `[design]`. He found the routine dull, and in a quarrel with the captain of the house guard he walked out with a sword and the plan to return within a tenday, once tempers had cooled `[design]`. He was away when a rival house raided the compound, and he came back to burned halls and a household that was gone `[design]`. He has never learned whether anyone survived, including an older half-sister who taught him to read a room before he could read a sigil `[design]`. Like the rest of the company, he speaks of Lolth, when he speaks of her at all, as the goddess his house served and that did not save it `[design]`.

He climbed to the surface with no plan and drifted up the coast to Luskan, where he worked the docks for years and picked up the accent that he still has `[design]`. One winter he took a job as a runner for a harbor playhouse, and he learned there how a house is kept: the cues, the schedules and who must not meet whom in the wings `[design]`. He liked the work more than anything else he had tried, because nobody asked what he was and every hour was busy `[design]`. He met a company recruiter in Luskan who looks for males like him, and he went with the recruiter to Jarlaxle `[design]`. He was given a Lantanese pistol like the company's other gunslingers and learned to shoot it with the same speed that he brings to everything else (*Dragon Heist*, 5e, for the weapons; the lessons are `[design]`). He was then paired with Fel'rekt, who makes the plans while he acts first, and the two have shared night watches, banter and games of dice ever since `[design]`. He and Fel'rekt work as a team, and he prefers to follow Fel'rekt's lead and let him do most of the thinking and talking (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

He was the member who could keep a schedule straight while talking to three people, so Jarlaxle gave him the booking office of the Seven Masks Theater `[design]`. He runs it for a front in the Dock Ward that Jarlaxle owns under the name Rongquan Mystere (**Dock Ward**). Because he must pass for a human stage manager, he works as "Kreb Sorrush", in a brown wig, pale greasepaint and gloves that he never takes off (**Kreb Drops the Cover**, for the description of the cover only). He took "Kreb" from the start of his own name and "Sorrush" from the maker's mark on the first crate of stage lamps that he unpacked at the theater `[design]`. He is usually at the theater, where he handles casting inquiries, performance schedules and requests for information, and he speaks with a slight Luskan accent that he does not try to conceal (**Bregan D'aerthe**). He wants to run the house without the interference of Malkolm Brizzenbright, the theater's founder, who is a ghost and moves props during rehearsals (**Malkolm Brizzenbright**).

He also collects the messages that Fenerus Stormcastle stores at his house in the Trades Ward, every two days, and carries them to the *Scarlet Marpenoth* (**Bregan D'aerthe**; **Fenerus Stormcastle**). He was once followed by Fenerus after a pickup (**Bregan D'aerthe**). On Ches 1, 1492 DR Krebbyg keeps a stateroom aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, the Lantanese submarine moored in Deepwater Harbor, with a net hammock and nothing else, and he sharpens his sword there between shifts at the theater (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He carries four packets of smokepowder and a pouch of twenty pistol bullets (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

**Personal goals.** Krebbyg wants to stage one season of the theater's bills that he chooses himself, with the house full every night and a company of real players who are not part of a front `[design]`. He keeps a list of plays at the back of the booking ledger, and he has not shown it to Fel'rekt `[design]`. He also wants to know whether his half-sister got out of the burning house `[design]`. He has never asked anyone, because asking would mean telling the company how his house fell, and the only people who might know are traders in the Underdark `[design]`.

## Personality

To the Dock Ward, Krebbyg is Kreb Sorrush, the cheerful booking manager of the Seven Masks Theater. He talks fast, calls everyone "darling" and checks a schedule while he does it. He handles casting inquiries and performance schedules with equal ease, and he switches between them in the middle of a sentence.

Those who know him better find a young, rash drow who acts before he thinks and hands every serious decision to Fel'rekt. He gets bored with routine, and he enjoys running the theater more than most of the company realizes. He will not let anyone insult the theater, and he complains loudly about the ghost who moves his props.

## Connections

- **Jarlaxle Baenre:** Krebbyg's employer and the owner of the Seven Masks Theater under the name Rongquan Mystere. Krebbyg wants to prove useful enough that Jarlaxle never regrets taking him in, and he speaks of Jarlaxle as "the captain" or "J". The Villains page holds Jarlaxle's plans and phases.
- **Fel'rekt Lafeen:** His closest friend and operational partner. Krebbyg follows Fel'rekt's lead, lets him do the thinking and talking, and goes along with whatever comes next.
- **Soluun Xibrindas:** The third lieutenant, who works the days. Jarlaxle, Fel'rekt and Krebbyg all know what Soluun does at night (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Fenerus Stormcastle:** The lamplighter whose house in the Trades Ward is the message drop. Krebbyg collects the messages every two days, and Fenerus once followed him after a pickup.
- **Malkolm Brizzenbright:** The theater's founding ghost, who moves props and objects to the drow who use his dressing room. Krebbyg wants the house to himself and complains that the ghost moved his props again.
- **Bregan D'aerthe:** The company that took him in. No source names anyone who answers to Krebbyg on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

## Overview

Kreb Sorrush is the booking manager of the Seven Masks Theater in the Dock Ward. He handles casting inquiries and performance schedules in a quick, cheerful manner, with a slight Luskan accent. He often switches from one request to the next in the middle of a sentence.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Krebbyg should inspire amusement with his rash energy, endearment with his deference to Fel'rekt, and curiosity about what an Underdark exile makes of a career managing a Waterdeep theater.

**Emotions.** Krebbyg most often feels reckless confidence, cheerful deference to Fel'rekt's judgment, restless boredom with routine, creative pleasure in his theater cover identity, mischievous humor, and an eagerness that substitutes for planning.

**Motivations.** Krebbyg wants to follow Fel'rekt into whatever comes next, run the Seven Masks Theater without Malkolm Brizzenbright's ghost interfering, and prove useful enough that Jarlaxle never regrets taking him in.

**Inspirations.** When playing Krebbyg, channel Rocket Raccoon (*Guardians of the Galaxy*), Jesse Pinkman (*Breaking Bad*), and Mushu (*Mulan*).

### Voice

*A rash young drow who runs a theatre's bookings, switching between casting and conspiracy mid-sentence.*

- **Sound:** A quick, cheerful, slightly frantic voice, a stage manager's patter.
- **Sentence shape:** Rapid run-on sentences that switch topics mid-flow ("the Tuesday matinee's full, did you bring the package, no, not you, the soprano").
- **Word choice:** Theatre jargon (bookings, blocking, the house, the ghost), laid over gang talk. As Kreb Sorrush he calls everyone "darling".
- **Swearing:** *Punctuation · Colourful · Phrase.* Casual and constant backstage ("Where's the fucking soprano?").
- **Personality in speech:** Reckless confidence and deference. He defers to Fel'rekt immediately on anything serious ("Ask Fel."). He gets bored with anything routine, and it's obvious. His pleasure in the theatre is real.
- **Quirks:** He checks a schedule while talking. He complains about the ghost moving his props. He answers "What's the plan?" with "Fel?"
- **Signature phrases:** "Ask Fel." / "The ghost moved it again." / "Darling."
- **Never:** He never plans, never argues with Fel'rekt, and never lets anyone insult the theatre.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Kreb Sorrush, bookings, how can I help, casting's on Tuesdays, sorry, darling, who sent you?"
  - *Business:* "You want in the back office? Ha, no. Ask Fel. Also the ghost hates you already, don't take it personally."
  - *Under pressure:* "Fel! Fel, it's happening! What do we do?"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Krebbyg operates as "Kreb Sorrush," the booking manager of the Seven Masks Theater. To those he trusts, he is young, rash, and happy to let Fel'rekt do the thinking and talking. Deep down, his house was destroyed long ago, leaving him with no connection to his old life in the Underdark. The theater is the closest thing he has to a home outside Bregan D'aerthe.

**Morale.** In a fight, Krebbyg follows Fel'rekt's lead, trading banter and covering flanks. He acts before thinking and relies on Fel'rekt to tell him when to stop.

**Relationships.** Krebbyg is the close friend and operational partner of Fel'rekt Lafeen, Jarlaxle's logistics lieutenant (collecting messages from Fenerus Stormcastle for the Scarlet Marpenoth), and the operating cover manager of the Seven Masks Theater.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **What he hides:** Kreb Sorrush is a cover, and Krebbyg works for Bregan D'aerthe and reports to Jarlaxle. Jarlaxle owns the theater as Rongquan Mystere.
> - **What he does when asked:** He does not explain who he works for (**Bregan D'aerthe**). He deflects with theater talk, changes the subject mid-sentence and sends anyone with serious business to Fel'rekt.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a light humor about the stage-manager patter in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats his lost house and his sister as real and does not play them for comedy.
