# Krebbyg Masq'il'yr

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Drow gunslinger, chaotic neutral*. Stat block: **Drow Gunslinger**.
> - **Affiliation:** Bregan D'aerthe
> - **Featured in:** **Faction Outposts**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **The Handkerchief and the Girl**, **The Wazoo Affair**, **The Betrayal Pitch**, **Three Nights**, **The Compromised Eye**, **The Theater's Back Room**, **The Dive**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Krebbyg should inspire amusement with his rash energy, endearment with his deference to Fel'rekt, and curiosity about what an Underdark exile makes of a career managing a Waterdeep theater.

**Emotions.** Krebbyg most often feels reckless confidence, cheerful deference to Fel'rekt's judgment, restless boredom with routine, creative pleasure in his theater cover identity, mischievous humor, and an eagerness that substitutes for planning.

**Motivations.** Krebbyg wants to follow Fel'rekt into whatever comes next, run the Seven Masks Theater without Malcolm Brizzenbright's ghost interfering, and prove useful enough that Jarlaxle never regrets taking him in.

**Inspirations.** When playing Krebbyg, channel Rocket Raccoon (*Guardians of the Galaxy*), Jesse Pinkman (*Breaking Bad*), and Mushu (*Mulan*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Krebbyg operates as "Kreb Sorrush," the booking manager of the Seven Masks Theater. To those he trusts, he is young, rash, and happy to let Fel'rekt do the thinking and talking. Deep down, his house was destroyed long ago, leaving him with no connection to his old life in the Underdark. The theater is the closest thing he has to a home outside Bregan D'Aerthe.

**Morale.** In a fight, Krebbyg follows Fel'rekt's lead, trading banter and covering flanks. He acts before thinking and relies on Fel'rekt to tell him when to stop.

**Relationships.** Krebbyg is the close friend and operational partner of Fel'rekt Lafeen, Jarlaxle's logistics lieutenant (collecting messages from Fenerus Stormcastle for the Scarlet Marpenoth), and the operating cover manager of the Seven Masks Theater.

## Overview

A drow gunslinger who operates as "Kreb Sorrush," booking manager of the Seven Masks Theater in the Dock Ward — handles casting inquiries, performance schedules, and other requests with equal efficiency, switching between them mid-sentence.
