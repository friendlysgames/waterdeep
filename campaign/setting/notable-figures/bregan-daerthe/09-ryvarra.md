# Ryvarra

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Drow spy (Bregan D'aerthe field observer), neutral*. Stat block: **Spy**.
> - **Affiliation:** Bregan D'aerthe
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Ryvarra should inspire the particular unease of someone who has been cataloguing the party since before they knew they were interesting; quiet admiration for operational patience executed over months without slipping; and retroactive dread once the party understands that every move they made in the Yawning Portal was already filed and addressed to Jarlaxle.

**Emotions.** Ryvarra most often feels analytical detachment, the practiced stillness of someone trained to read a room without being read, professional loyalty to Jarlaxle, private curiosity about the party (they are more interesting than most of what she observes), and a controlled impatience she has not let surface in three months of watching the well.

**Motivations.** Ryvarra wants to file accurate weekly reports to Jarlaxle on Undermountain traffic, maintain her cover as a Calimshan cloth merchant for as long as operationally viable, and avoid any action that would compromise Bregan D'Aerthe's Waterdeep network.

**Inspirations.** When playing Ryvarra, channel George Smiley (*Tinker, Tailor, Soldier, Spy*), The Operative (*Serenity*), and O-Ren Ishii (*Kill Bill*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Ryvarra is a Calimshan cloth merchant taking her evening wine at a reserved table while trade negotiations drag on. To those who observe carefully, she never drinks from the glass, never takes notes, and her Calimshan accent slips in ways a trained ear notices (DC 14 Insight). Deep down, she has been filing weekly intelligence reports to Jarlaxle since her first night in the city, and her current dispatch names the party by description, details the brawl and the troll fight, and flags them as potential long-term variables.

**Morale.** Approached and questioned directly, Ryvarra excuses herself politely and does not return to that table. No evidence remains — her wine glass is full, her chair is straight, and no one at the adjacent tables can say what she looked like. Cornered or exposed, she fights with a drow's efficiency: targeted, fast, brutal, with every move aimed at an exit.

**Relationships.** Ryvarra is a Bregan D'Aerthe field observer stationed at the Yawning Portal to catalog Undermountain traffic, a weekly intelligence source for Jarlaxle's Waterdeep network, and the first evidence (retroactively understood) that Jarlaxle had eyes on the party from their first night in the city.

## Overview

A Calimshan cloth merchant who takes her evening wine at a reserved table at the Yawning Portal — she never drinks from the glass, never takes notes, and her accent slips in ways a trained ear notices.
