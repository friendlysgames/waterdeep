**Ryvarra** (Neutral, drow, she/her)

## Background

Ryvarra is a drow woman who sits on the observation balcony of the Yawning Portal in the guise of a Calimshan cloth merchant and catalogs who descends into Undermountain and who comes back (**Bregan D'aerthe**; **Castle Ward**). She reports what she sees to Jarlaxle (**Bregan D'aerthe**). Ryvarra is a woman among almost all male drow, and a female drow earns a place with Jarlaxle by decrying the matriarchy and convincing him that she would be an asset (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**). She presumably took that road `[research]`.

Ryvarra was born in a small house of Menzoberranzan whose wealth was spider-silk cloth, and she was set to the weaving halls as a girl `[design]`. Her mother was the head weaver and taught her how to weave, how to dye and how to judge a bolt by its weight `[design]`. When a bolt that the matron mother had ordered came off the loom with a flaw, the matron blamed her mother and sent her to the dye vats, where she died within the year `[design]`. Ryvarra left off her offerings to Lolth after that, and she speaks of the Spider Queen as the goddess her house served and never questioned `[design]`.

She left the house with a bolt of its silk under her cloak and sold it in Skullport (**Undermountain and Skullport**) to a Calishite factor `[design]`. He took her on as a clerk because she could judge cloth by touch and keep a ledger `[design]`. She heard Calishite spoken in his shop all day and learned the accent, the courtesies and the haggling of a Calimshan merchant, along with the trade in silks from Calimport `[design]`. She found the cover easy to wear, because she had always known what a cloth was worth and what a buyer would pay `[design]`.

Jarlaxle's scouts watch a candidate for several days before anyone speaks to her (**Bregan D'aerthe**), and Ryvarra learned that a scout had followed her through the Skullport markets only when the scout sat down at her table `[design]`. She told the scout what she thought of the matriarchy, and the scout took her to Jarlaxle `[design]`. She was given the Yawning Portal because a merchant in a long negotiation can sit at one table every evening without anyone asking why, and she already had the trade and the accent `[design]`. She never drinks from her glass, so that her head stays clear, and she never writes in public, because she keeps the evening in her head and sets it down after the doors close `[design]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Ryvarra sits at her reserved table on the balcony, with the well in view and a full glass of wine in front of her.

**Personal goals.** Ryvarra wants to finish a bolt of cloth in her mother's weave `[design]`. Her mother never wrote the pattern down, so Ryvarra rebuilds it from memory one thread count at a time, on a small frame that she works when she is alone `[design]`. She keeps a swatch of the unfinished weave in her sleeve and runs her thumb across it when nobody is looking `[design]`. She also wants a loom of her own and a shop with a window onto a street, where she sells cloth that she wove under her own name `[design]`. She has sold other people's silk for her cover and has never sold anything that she made herself `[design]`.

## Personality

To the regulars of the Yawning Portal, Ryvarra is a courteous Calimshan cloth merchant who sits out a long trade negotiation over a glass of wine. She talks about weaves and dyes and asks ordinary questions of anyone who stops at her table. Those who watch her closely see that she reads a room without being read, and that her interest sharpens when adventurers talk about what they found below.

## Overview

Ryvarra is a Calimshan cloth merchant who takes her evening wine at a reserved table in the Yawning Portal. Her table is on the balcony above the well, and she never drinks from the glass or takes a note. Her accent slips now and then in ways that a trained ear notices.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Ryvarra should inspire the particular unease of someone who catalogues everyone who goes down the well; quiet admiration for operational patience executed without slipping; and, for anyone who learns whom she writes to, retroactive dread that what they said and did in the Yawning Portal was filed and addressed to Jarlaxle.

**Emotions.** Ryvarra most often feels analytical detachment, the practiced stillness of someone trained to read a room without being read, professional loyalty to Jarlaxle, private curiosity about the adventurers who go down the well (they are more interesting than most of what she observes), and a controlled impatience she has not let surface while watching it.

**Motivations.** Ryvarra wants to file accurate reports to Jarlaxle on Undermountain traffic, maintain her cover as a Calimshan cloth merchant for as long as operationally viable, and avoid any action that would compromise Bregan D'aerthe's Waterdeep network.

**Inspirations.** When playing Ryvarra, channel George Smiley (*Tinker, Tailor, Soldier, Spy*), The Operative (*Serenity*), and O-Ren Ishii (*Kill Bill*).

### Voice

*A poised Calimshan cloth merchant whose accent slips when she's watching too closely.*

- **Sound:** A smooth, cultured Calishite accent that slips, now and then, into a colder, more precise drow cadence.
- **Sentence shape:** Polite, economical merchant's small talk. She asks perfectly ordinary questions that are each part of a profile.
- **Word choice:** The cloth trade (weaves, dyes, shipping delays) and polished courtesy. She calls strangers "honoured friends", as a merchant would.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* None in cover. In private, one cold drow curse.
- **Personality in speech:** Analytical detachment. She reads a room without being read. Her curiosity shows in one question too many about the fights of anyone who goes down the well.
- **Quirks:** She never drinks from her glass. She compliments fabric while clearly looking at the wearer's weapons.
- **Signature phrases:** "Negotiations are slow." / "Honoured friends." / "What a fascinating evening."
- **Never:** She never takes notes in public, never drinks, and never admits to being anything but a merchant.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Good evening, honoured friends. Forgive me, I couldn't help noticing your gear. Such an eventful place."
  - *Business:* "Cloth, mostly. Silks from Calimport. And you? You fight for coin, or for something else?"
  - *Under pressure:* (the accent drops away completely) "Sit down. You're making a scene, and I dislike scenes."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Ryvarra is a Calimshan cloth merchant taking her evening wine at a reserved table while trade negotiations drag on. To those who observe carefully, she never drinks from the glass, never takes notes, and her Calimshan accent slips in ways a trained ear notices (DC 14 Insight). Deep down, she files intelligence reports to Jarlaxle on who descends into Undermountain and who comes back, and she flags the adventurers who look like long-term variables.

**Morale.** Approached and questioned directly, Ryvarra excuses herself politely and does not return to that table. No evidence remains — her wine glass is full, her chair is straight, and no one at the adjacent tables can say what she looked like. Cornered or exposed, she fights with a drow's efficiency: targeted, fast, brutal, with every move aimed at an exit.

**Relationships.** Ryvarra is a Bregan D'aerthe field observer stationed at the Yawning Portal to catalog Undermountain traffic, and an intelligence source for Jarlaxle's Waterdeep network.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - Ryvarra reports to Jarlaxle Baenre, and the cloth merchant is a cover. The tells are the untouched glass, the lack of notes and an accent that slips (DC 14 Insight).

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, gentle humor about the untouched glass and the polite merchant's talk in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her patience and her mother's weave as real and does not mock them.
