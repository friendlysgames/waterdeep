**Vessa** (Neutral, doppelganger, she/her)

## Background

Vessa is a doppelganger who works for Jarlaxle Baenre as an operative of Bregan D'aerthe, and in Waterdeep she passes as a human noblewoman in the Cassalanter social circle (**Bregan D'aerthe**, Organizations). Her full name is Lymeria Lhaurilstar, and she uses Vessa as the name of her cover. She is an operative and not a member, because Jarlaxle counts none of his non-drow operatives as members (**Bregan D'aerthe**, Organizations).

Vessa was born into a small band of doppelgangers who lived among the poor of a trading town on the Sword Coast `[design]`. As a child she learned that a face nobody looked at twice kept her out of every ledger the town kept, and she learned to catch what the people around her were thinking before they spoke `[design]`. She lived on what she could carry out of houses where she was taken for a new maid `[design]`.

At about twenty she took the face of a paid companion in the house of an elderly widow, and she kept it for eleven years `[design]`. The widow was nearly blind, and she worked out in the second winter what her companion was, said nothing, and called her Lymeria from then on `[design]`. It was the first name anyone had given her that she wanted to keep `[design]`. When the widow died, her heirs asked where the companion had come from, and nobody in the town could say, so Vessa left that night `[design]`. She has shown her own shape to no one outside the company since then `[design]`.

Vessa next worked in Luskan, where a fence paid her to stand in for other people at the doors of customs offices `[design]`. A watcher from Bregan D'aerthe followed her for several days before anyone spoke to her, as the company follows any candidate (**Bregan D'aerthe**, Organizations), and she noticed him on the second day and kept her face steady for the rest `[design]`. She took Jarlaxle's offer of a post that needed one cover held for years in a single circle, because it paid ten times what the fence had and asked her to kill no one `[design]`. She keeps no god, and the drow of the company, who speak of Lolth with contempt, have never asked her to kneel to anyone `[design]`.

Vessa reached Waterdeep late in 1491 DR, after Jarlaxle had settled in the harbor, and she took the cover of a minor noblewoman with no relatives in the city `[design]`. With no family to write to, nobody could check her story, and she was admitted to the Cassalanters' circle through the ordinary round of invitations `[design]`. She was pointed at that family because its banking underwrites half the guilds in the city, so its guests hear what the city's money is doing (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations) `[research]`.

Within her first months Vessa read the Cassalanters' pact in their own thoughts, along with the contract that pledges their children (**A Debt in Blood**) `[design]`. She reported it to Jarlaxle and to no one else, and she has never said it aloud to anyone since. She has noticed that the household's thoughts turn afraid whenever the cellar comes up, and she has decided never to go below the ground floor of the villa `[design]`. Her cover has not taken her to the family's Converted Windmill in the Southern Ward either (**The Cassalanters**, Organizations).

Vessa keeps her cover by being the guest who remembers everyone's name and cannot be pinned to a purse or a family `[design]`. When the circle says that the Cassalanters are too nice to be this rich, she says it with them (**The Cassalanters**, Villains) `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Vessa wants to remember her own face without thinking about it, though she tells herself that it does not matter `[design]`. One night in each tenday she locks her door and tries to hold her own shape from dusk until she sleeps, and so far she has not got through the evening before she reaches for another face `[design]`. She also wants to set a stone on the widow's grave under the name Lymeria, and she has not gone back to the town because its Watch still has the description of the companion who left on the night of the funeral `[design]`. Last, she wants to play the harp. She has held one at Sea Ward salons for months and pretended, and she wants to learn it properly from a teacher who has never seen her in any other face `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people at the Cassalanters' dinners, Vessa is a noblewoman who has been coming for months and who knows every guest by name. She calls everyone darling, trades gossip about who was seen with whom, and says, as half the circle does, that the family is too perfect to be true.

She has no relatives in the city and no town house that anyone has visited `[design]`. Some guests find her mysterious, because she seems to know more about them than they remember telling her. She turns down any invitation to look at the lower floors of the villa `[design]`.

## Connections

- **Jarlaxle Baenre:** The captain of Bregan D'aerthe and the only person she reports to. Her reports are how he alone outside the household and the cult knows what the Cassalanters have done.
- **Bregan D'aerthe:** She is an operative of the company and not a member. She shows her real features only to someone from the company who approaches her.
- **The Cassalanters:** She sits in the family's social circle and attends its dinners and galas. No source records her speaking with Victoro, Ammalia or the children `[research]`.
- **Ryvarra** and **Quilm:** Fellow operatives in Jarlaxle's Waterdeep network, one at the Yawning Portal and one filing weekly reports. No source says that she has met either `[research]`.
- The widow: An elderly woman in a Sword Coast town who called her Lymeria and was the last person to do so. She is dead and nameless `[design]`.

## Overview

Vessa is a noblewoman who has attended the Cassalanters' dinners and galas for months. She knows every guest by name and calls everyone darling. Some guests find her mysterious, because she seems to know more about them than they remember telling her.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Vessa should inspire discomfort at the moment she briefly shows her true face to a member of Bregan D'aerthe — a flash of gray, a knowing look, the implication that she has been watching longer than they have been looking — and then calibrated trust, because she is useful and she knows it.

**Emotions.** Vessa most often feels patient professional detachment, cold satisfaction in the quality of her cover, operational curiosity about anyone who takes an interest in the Cassalanters, and a very old, very contained disinterest in her own face.

**Motivations.** Vessa wants to maintain her cover in the Cassalanter social scene, watch what the Cassalanters do, and report accurate intelligence to Jarlaxle. She does not want to enter the temple.

**Inspirations.** When playing Vessa, channel Tatiana Maslany (*Orphan Black*), Mystique (*X-Men: First Class*), and Gus Fring (*Breaking Bad*).

### Voice

*A doppelganger noblewoman fluent in every face at the banquet, and bored of her own.*

- **Sound:** A cultured, pleasant society voice. It goes flat and neutral for the instant she drops the performance.
- **Sentence shape:** Light, gossipy society talk. When she signals a Bregan D'aerthe ally, one short, precise sentence, then back to gossip.
- **Word choice:** Fashionable Waterdeep gossip: names, houses, who's seen with whom. She calls everyone "darling". Her own name is used a little too carefully.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Never in performance. In the rare flat moment, a detached "shit" about something inconvenient.
- **Personality in speech:** Patient detachment and satisfaction in her cover. Her curiosity about anyone who takes an interest in the Cassalanters shows in precise questions. Around the Cassalanters she talks pure society suspicion and calls them "too perfect". In truth she has read their thoughts and knows about the pact, which she reports only to the captain and never says aloud to anyone else.
- **Quirks:** She pauses a beat before answering to her own name. She knows every guest's name and never needs reminding.
- **Signature phrases:** "Darling." / "Too perfect, aren't they?" / "I'm not going near the chapel."
- **Never:** She never enters the temple, never breaks cover publicly, and never shows her true face to anyone outside Bregan D'aerthe.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Darling! You must be new. I know everyone here, and I don't know you. How thrilling."
  - *Business (to a BD member, quietly):* "The captain says hello. Smile, darling, and tell me what you came for."
  - *Under pressure:* "Well, this party has taken a turn. I'll be leaving by the front door, like a lady."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, "Vessa" is a noblewoman circulating through the Cassalanter banquet hall with the ease of someone who has been attending these events for months, which she has. To a member of Bregan D'aerthe who approaches her, she provides a brief unambiguous signal — a flash of gray features, a knowing look — before returning to the social performance; to everyone else, she is a mysterious woman who seems to know too much. Deep down, she has been doing this long enough that she has to think before remembering what her own face looks like, and has decided this is probably fine.

**Morale.** Vessa does not fight in the villa under any circumstances — her cover is the asset and she will not burn it. Exposed or cornered, she uses her read thoughts ability to identify what to say to exit the situation, and then says it. She will not enter the temple below the villa under any circumstances.

**Relationships.** Vessa (full name Lymeria Lhaurilstar) is a Bregan D'aerthe doppelganger operative embedded in the Cassalanter social scene under a permanent human cover, Jarlaxle's primary intelligence source on the Cassalanters, and a useful ally to any member of Bregan D'aerthe who reaches her at a Cassalanter event (distraction and cover stories).

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **What she is:** Vessa is a doppelganger, and "Vessa" is the human cover she wears in the Cassalanter circle. She shows her real features only to a member of Bregan D'aerthe who approaches her, and to no one else in the circle.
> - **The pact:** She read the Cassalanters' pact and the contract on their children in the family's own thoughts. She reports it only to Jarlaxle, who is the one outsider who knows, and she never says it aloud to anyone else. Jarlaxle tells no member of Bregan D'aerthe who has not worked it out.
> - **Where she will not go:** She will not enter the temple below the villa and will not fight in the villa. Her cover has never reached the Converted Windmill.
> - **Who knows:** Nobody outside the household and the cult knows the pact except Jarlaxle, through Vessa. Few people know that she is a doppelganger, and the rest of the circle takes her for a noblewoman.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a light society manner in Personality and the Overview. The narrator says only what a guest at the dinners would see, and the Overview gives no hint of what she is.
