**Quilm** (Neutral, drow, he/him)

## Background

Quilm is a male drow and a scout of Bregan D'aerthe, assigned to Waterdeep's surface operations. He files a weekly intelligence report for Jarlaxle (**Bregan D'aerthe**). He is good at the work. No file gives his age, his birthplace, his house or the length of his service `[research]`.

Quilm was born into a minor drow house in the Underdark and grew up as one of its servants `[design]`. His work was to carry messages between the matron's rooms and to stand at her door through long audiences `[design]`. He learned there that nobody looks at a person who does not move `[design]`. His mother ran the house's linen store and taught him to fold cloth to the width of a hand, and he still folds his own that way `[design]`. A rival house destroyed his in a single night when he was a young man `[design]`. He walked out through the linen store with his mother's folding board and nothing else, and she was in the matron's chambers when the house fell `[design]`. The house's priestesses did nothing for its servants that night, and he has had no use for the Spider Queen since `[design]`.

Quilm spent the following years as a houseless hand, taking work as a guard, a porter or a messenger wherever nobody asked about his house `[design]`. He was shadowed for several days before anyone spoke to him, as the company does with every candidate (**Bregan D'aerthe**; this case is `[design]`). He noticed the watchers on the fifth day and went about his errands exactly as he had the day before `[design]`. The man who finally approached him said that this was the answer they had been waiting for `[design]`. Quilm accepted because the company wanted the stillness he had learned at the matron's door `[design]`.

Quilm was sent to Waterdeep to work the surface, which he knew only from books `[design]`. He bought a guide to surface manners in Skullport and studied it until every greeting and every garment was correct, and that is why they all look slightly too correct `[design]`. A tailor in the Trades Ward dressed him at cost for his first season and never asked what he was `[design]`. He hires out for door work in the evenings, which lets him stand outside rooms and listen `[design]`. He carries cards that offer his services, and on each one Jarlaxle has written in silver ink that he is entirely trustworthy and signed it "J" `[design]`. Quilm has spoken with Jarlaxle only a few times, and each time Jarlaxle repeated an earlier remark of his word for word `[design]`. He thinks the endorsement is theatrical, and he carries the cards anyway `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR he is still working the evening doors and still sends his report every week.

**Personal goals.** Quilm wants to learn whether his mother lived `[design]`. He has asked nobody about the survivors of his house, because a drow who asks about a fallen house is remembered, and he has built his life on not being remembered `[design]`. He also wants to repay the tailor who dressed him in a way that she cannot trace to him, and he has not found a gift that she would accept from a stranger `[design]`. He wants to stand bareheaded in an open square at noon for a full hour `[design]`. He has watched the squares from shaded doorways on many clear days and has not yet walked out `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people in the city, Quilm is a quiet drow in neat surface clothes who hires out for door work in the evenings. He speaks in a few plain words, answers what he is asked and gives his name only when it is asked a second time. People who meet him find it hard to describe him afterward.

## Connections

Quilm answers to **Jarlaxle Baenre**, whom the company calls "J", and he files his weekly report for the Waterdeep network.

## Overview

Quilm is a drow in neat, careful surface clothes who hires out for security work in the evenings. He is compact and still, and he speaks in a few plain words. Most people find him easy to forget once he has left the room.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Quilm should inspire professional respect for his quiet competence; subtle unease if someone notices the handwriting on the card he carries; and useful satisfaction when a door he watches stays quiet all night.

**Emotions.** Quilm most often feels operational discipline, the particular stillness of a scout who has learned to be uninteresting, professional loyalty to Bregan D'aerthe, private amusement at the door work, and the patience of someone whose job is to be overlooked.

**Motivations.** Quilm wants to keep his posting in Waterdeep, file the weekly intelligence reports Jarlaxle expects, and stay unremarked while he does it.

**Inspirations.** When playing Quilm, channel Boba Fett (*Star Wars*), Leon (*Leon: The Professional*), and Varys (*Game of Thrones*, the useful invisible man).

### Voice

*A drow bouncer so professionally uninteresting you forget he spoke.*

- **Sound:** A quiet, flat, unmemorable voice.
- **Sentence shape:** A few plain words at a time. He answers what's asked and nothing else. This is a deliberate break from `ember-voice` speech.
- **Word choice:** Bouncer basics: "out", "no", "calm down". He calls patrons "sir" or "ma'am" with no inflection.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Minimal and flat ("Out. Now. Fuck off.").
- **Personality in speech:** Operational discipline, cultivated dullness. His private amusement at door work shows only as the slightest pause.
- **Quirks:** He doesn't volunteer his name unless asked twice. He says "Understood" and walks away.
- **Signature phrases:** "Understood." / "Out." / "Not my business."
- **Never:** He never mentions Jarlaxle, never explains the card, and never draws attention to himself.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Evening."
  - *Business:* "Card's a reference. Understood?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Out. Now."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Quilm is a drow who hires out for door work in the evenings, carries a card with a handwritten endorsement, and does the job without comment or complaint. To those who read the card carefully, the endorsement is signed "J" in different ink and in a hand that is affected and theatrical (DC 10 Insight), and drow characters and anyone tied to Bregan D'aerthe recognize the style automatically. Deep down, he filed his first intelligence report the week he reached the city and has not missed a week.

**Morale.** In a fight, Quilm removes threats to the door he is watching with the minimum force required. He does not escalate, and he does not explain.

**Relationships.** Quilm is a Bregan D'aerthe scout assigned to Waterdeep's surface operations, a hired door man whose card carries an endorsement from "J" in Jarlaxle's handwriting, and a weekly intelligence source for Jarlaxle's Waterdeep network.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **What he is:** A company scout who reports every week to Jarlaxle. The "J" on his card is Jarlaxle, and Quilm does not confirm it unless the party already knows.
> - **What he wants kept quiet:** His door work is his cover. He is not hunting anyone, and his reports cover what he hears outside rooms and what the doormen of the city say to one another.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, gentle humor about his stillness and his too-correct clothes in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats the loss of his house as real and does not mock him.
