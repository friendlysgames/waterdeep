**Soluun Xibrindas** (Neutral evil, drow, he/him)

## Background

Soluun Xibrindas is a male drow whose house was wiped out long ago. He and his younger brother Nar'l survived and joined Bregan D'aerthe (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**). As survivors of a destroyed house, the brothers were the kind of recruit the company was built from (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**). Soluun has served Jarlaxle with a zealot's devotion for years, and he is one of the three drow gunslingers who do most of Jarlaxle's dirty work in the city (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). By one wiki account he is one of the company's top agents in Waterdeep in the early 1490s DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Soluun Xibrindas*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Soluun, the elder son, was trained to fight and sent out with his house's raiding parties to the surface as soon as he could carry a blade `[design]`. He was taught from a young age to kill surface elves and half-elves whenever the chance arose (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and the raids were where he learned how `[design]`. He watched Nar'l be judged too slight for the raiding parties and given to the house archivist, and he took that as proof that his brother could not fight `[design]`. He told Nar'l so then and has told him so ever since `[design]`.

Soluun was away with a raiding party on the night a rival house attacked his own, and the house fell in a single night `[design]`. He came back to find the hall burning, went in for his brother, and carried Nar'l out through a service passage while the archivist and the rest of the household died `[design]`. He watched the hall's shrine to the Spider Queen burn with the rest of the building and saw that no help came to it `[design]`. He has spoken of Lolth ever since as the spider-bitch that he walked away from `[design]`.

For some years afterward Soluun and Nar'l hired themselves out in the cities of the Underdark, with Soluun fighting and Nar'l keeping accounts and casting for pay `[design]`. Neither of them has mentioned the night of the fire since, and in those years Soluun began to call his brother "the weak one" and stopped using his name `[design]`. After one of those jobs the brothers joined Bregan D'aerthe, and Jarlaxle asked nothing about the house they had lost `[design]`. Soluun gave Jarlaxle the devotion that he had owed his house and that a drow of the houses would give the Spider Queen, and Nar'l took a place as a mage and clerk `[design]`.

In Waterdeep Soluun carries Lantanese firearms like the company's other two drow lieutenants (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and by one account he prefers to work by day (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Soluun Xibrindas*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. When no operation holds him he spends his nights hunting solitary elves and half-elves, and he hides it as well as he can, though Jarlaxle, Fel'rekt and Krebbyg know what he is doing (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). For some weeks before Ches 1, 1492 DR he has hunted along the Dock Ward waterfront and chosen his victims from the audiences of the Seven Masks Theater. He picks an elf or half-elf sailor, follows him home for two nights and kills him on the third with a hand crossbow bolt and a curved shortsword, and he takes the head and the bolt (**Dock Ward**). He carries a bloodstained playbill from the theater for a performance of *Blood Wedding*.

Soluun goes on hunting while Jarlaxle, who knows about the murders, has not decided what to do about them (**Dock Ward**; **Jarlaxle Baenre** in Villains). Soluun is publicly said to have been disowned by Jarlaxle for killing without orders, and the story is a cover. He carries forged Bregan D'aerthe identification so that the company can deny him (**Bregan D'aerthe** in Organizations). He knows that the denial is for show, and he does not admit to it `[research]`. He lodges in stateroom U3 aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, moored in Deepwater Harbor since Jarlaxle reached the city, and he cleans his weapons there (**Sea Maidens Faire** in Locations; **Bregan D'aerthe** in Organizations).

**Personal goals.** Soluun wants his brother alive `[design]`. He means to go on calling Nar'l the weak one, and he wants Nar'l to live long enough to hear it, because Nar'l is a spy in the house of a beholder `[design]`. He has never asked Jarlaxle to pull Nar'l out, and he has never told Nar'l that he wants it `[design]`. He also wants to see the burned hall again `[design]`. He has not been back since the night he carried Nar'l out of it, and he wants to stand in the ruin once and see what the rival house left standing `[design]`. He tells nobody about this, and he has no plan to go, since the road leads through the Underdark and the company's work keeps him in Waterdeep `[design]`. Last, he wants to sit through a whole performance at the Seven Masks Theater `[design]`. He has gone to many of them to choose a victim and has always left before the last scene to follow someone out, so he has never learned how one of the plays ends `[design]`.

## Personality

Few people in Waterdeep have seen Soluun, and those who have remember a drow with a flat, cold voice who spits when anyone mentions surface elves `[research]`. He treats surface folk as obstacles and speaks to them only in orders and insults. He gives his zeal to Jarlaxle and the company, and the voice that stays flat with strangers rises when he speaks of the captain.

The adventure calls him a sadistic, fanatical bully, and it adds that he cannot resist a good fight (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). His hatred of surface elves is a habit he was taught young, and it has become the largest part of who he is.

Toward his brother he is openly contemptuous. He calls Nar'l the weak one and never uses his name, and he says that Nar'l turned to arcane magic to make up for what he lacks (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

## Connections

- **Jarlaxle Baenre:** The captain he serves with a zealot's devotion. Jarlaxle knows about the murders at the Seven Masks Theater and has not decided what to do about them, and his public denial of Soluun lets the company disown him if the killings are traced.
- **Nar'l Xibrindas:** His younger brother, Xanathar's advisor and secretly Jarlaxle's spy inside Xanathar's Guild. Soluun despises him as a weakling and calls him the weak one, though he once carried Nar'l out of their burning house `[design]`.
- **Fel'rekt Lafeen** and **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr:** The two other drow gunslinger lieutenants. They work the night in turns, Soluun works the day, and both know what he does after dark (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Bregan D'aerthe:** The company that took in the brothers after their house fell. Soluun touches his forged identification when he names it.
- **Lolth:** The goddess his house served. Soluun despises her and the matron houses that serve her, as the whole company does (**Lolth** in Deities).

## Overview

Soluun Xibrindas is a drow gunslinger of Bregan D'aerthe whom the company publicly says it has disowned. He prefers to work by day and keeps to the shade.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Soluun should inspire revulsion with his methodical murder of surface elves, discomfort with his fanatical loyalty to Jarlaxle, and pity for the hatred that defines his entire existence.

**Emotions.** Soluun most often feels cold hatred of surface elves, fanatical devotion to Bregan D'aerthe, contemptuous superiority, a zealot's fervor for the band (which, like every member of Bregan D'aerthe, rejects Lolth), hunting focus, and bitter disdain for his brother Nar'l.

**Motivations.** Soluun wants to kill every surface elf and half-elf he can find without consequences, serve Jarlaxle with absolute loyalty, and prove that his brother Nar'l is the weak one.

**Inspirations.** When playing Soluun, channel the Punisher (*Marvel*), Colonel Kurtz (*Apocalypse Now*), and Kylo Ren (*Star Wars*).

### Voice

*A fanatical drow killer: cold, contemptuous and zealous for the captain, with every word soaked in hatred for surface elves.*

- **Sound:** A low, flat, cold voice. It rises into fervour when he speaks of Jarlaxle or of Nar'l's weakness.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, hard, contemptuous statements. He speaks to surface folk only in orders and insults. His zeal for the band comes out in longer, chanted sentences, the way other drow pray.
- **Word choice:** Drow slurs for surface elves, hunting language ("prey", "the next one"), devotion to Jarlaxle and the band, and open contempt for Lolth and the matron houses that serve her. He calls his brother "the weak one", never by name.
- **Swearing:** *Triggered · Colourful · Rant.* Vicious and contemptuous, aimed at surface elves and half-elves with slurs and profanity ("Filthy fucking surface-blood. You all smell of sunlight.").
- **Personality in speech:** Cold hatred, fanatical loyalty and superiority. Cornered, he shows no remorse, only disgust. He claims Jarlaxle's authority even though the company publicly denies him.
- **Quirks:** He spits at the mention of surface elves. He touches his forged token when he names Bregan D'aerthe.
- **Signature phrases:** "Surface-blood." / "For the captain." / "The weak one."
- **Never:** He never apologises, never shows mercy to an elf, never admits Jarlaxle disowned him, and never speaks of Lolth as anything but the spider-bitch he walked away from.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You're not elves. Stay out of my way and you might live."
  - *Business:* "I serve the captain. Whatever you've heard about me is a lie told for the humans."
  - *Under pressure:* "Kill me, then. No goddess is coming for me, and I never wanted one. The captain remembers his own. You'll all be prey one day."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Soluun is a drow gunslinger of Bregan D'aerthe, disowned for unsanctioned Dock Ward murders. To those he trusts, he is fiercely loyal to Jarlaxle and Bregan D'aerthe, and he considers his brother Nar'l a weakling who turned to arcane magic by way of compensation. Deep down, he was taught from a young age to kill surface elves as opportunity permits, and the habit has become his identity. He despises Lolth and the matron houses that serve her, as the whole band does, and the devotion a drow of the houses would give the Spider Queen he gives to Jarlaxle instead.

**Morale.** In a fight, Soluun attacks without hesitation or mercy. He carries a bloodstained Seven Masks Theater playbill for Blood Wedding; he stalks and murders elven and half-elven audience members after shows. Jarlaxle is aware and hasn't yet decided what to do about it.

**Relationships.** Soluun is the elder brother of Nar'l Xibrindas (whom he despises), a lieutenant of Jarlaxle (whom he worships), and a serial killer whose disownment by Bregan D'aerthe is a cover story: he carries forged BD identification.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The killings:** Soluun is the man killing elf and half-elf sailors off Ship Street in the Dock Ward. Jarlaxle, Fel'rekt and Krebbyg know, and nobody outside the company does.
> - **The cover:** Jarlaxle's public story is that he disowned Soluun for killing without orders. The story is false, and Soluun carries forged Bregan D'aerthe identification so that the company can deny him if the killings are traced.
> - **The brother:** Soluun despises Nar'l and would not say that he wants him alive. He has told nobody in the company that Nar'l is the one living person he has ever gone into a fire for.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text. The narrator states what Soluun does and why he does it, and neither dresses up the killings nor excuses them.
