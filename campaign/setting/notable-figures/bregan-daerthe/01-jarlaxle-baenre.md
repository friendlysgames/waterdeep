**Jarlaxle Baenre** (Chaotic neutral, drow, he/him)

## Background

Jarlaxle was born to Matron Mother Yvonnel Baenre of House Baenre, the first house of Menzoberranzan. By his birth order he was to be sacrificed to Lolth, but the blade could not pierce the infant and his brother Doquaio died in his place (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jarlaxle Baenre*, citing *Road of the Patriarch*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Lolth favored him afterward as an agent of chaos, and he rejected her. She withdrew her favor, and the matron mothers of Menzoberranzan never learned of it (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. Bregan D'aerthe has rejected Lolth too. Its members speak of her with contempt, as the goddess they walked away from, and Soluun gives Jarlaxle the devotion that a drow of the houses would give the Spider Queen (the **Soluun Xibrindas** page).

He left his house and name, though not his connections, and founded the company from the rogue males of destroyed and disgraced houses (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jarlaxle Baenre*, citing *Homeland*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Almost all members are male, because female drow rarely take orders from a male, and a female drow can earn a place by decrying the matriarchy and convincing him she would be an asset (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**). In 1376 and 1377 DR the company helped bring down the Host Tower of the Arcane, and Luskan became a free port (same page, citing *The Pirate King*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Five High Captains rule Luskan. After 1485 DR they trade legally across the Sword Coast and are secretly under Jarlaxle's sway, and the published adventure has him declaring himself the city's secret lord (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**).

His latest scheme is to legitimize Luskan by making it a member of the Lords' Alliance. The city's reputation has thwarted every earlier attempt, and the Alliance's leaders have voiced their opposition. He aims to persuade **Laeral Silverhand** to champion Luskan even if it costs the Alliance other members. He also wants to oust Neverwinter and its lord, **Dagult Neverember**, from the Alliance. The published adventure calls Neverember his fiercest political opponent, and it has Jarlaxle wanting the dragonstaff of Ahghairon as leverage in his negotiations.

The Sea Maidens Faire is a legitimate business he already owned before he turned to Waterdeep. It is a traveling carnival out of Luskan that tours the Sword Coast in three ships, the *Eyecatcher*, the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser*, and Captain Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle in the magical guise of an Illuskan human. He spends most of his time aboard the *Eyecatcher* (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**). The fleet is due in Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21, the opening day of Fleetswake, and sails at dawn on Tarsakh 20 (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**; **Structural Rules**).

He has an alliance with Lantan, arms his lieutenants with Lantanese firearms that run on smokepowder, and keeps the Lantanese submarine *Scarlet Marpenoth*. He plans to flee Waterdeep in it if his scheme unravels. In the campaign the *Scarlet Marpenoth* has been moored in Deepwater Harbor since he reached the city, and it keel-docks beneath the *Eyecatcher* when the fleet arrives.

He reached Waterdeep already knowing of the Stone, the three Eyes and the vault, and Nar'l's reports keep him current. The nimblewright ledger puts him in the city no later than Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**). Zord sells nimblewrights to wealthy families across the wards, and each one harvests intelligence through a crystal ball aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* (**Bregan D'aerthe** page, Organizations).

Eye #3 began in **Renaer Neverember**'s mourning locket. Manshoon's Zhentarim seized the locket the night before Ches 1 and pried it open at the Candle Lane warehouse. Xanathar's Guild raided the warehouse and carried off the Eye with the rest of the back office without knowing what it was, and Bregan D'aerthe agents already inside Xanathar's hideout lifted it that night. It reached Jarlaxle that night, and Manshoon's cell never held it (**Running the Villains**). He keeps it in his stateroom, U4, aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*.

**Nar'l Xibrindas** is Xanathar's trusted drow advisor and secretly Jarlaxle's best intelligence source on the Guild. He reports by *Sending* spell, and if forced to choose between himself and Bregan D'aerthe, he will choose himself (the **Nar'l Xibrindas** page). **Vessa**, a doppelganger, sits in the Cassalanter social circle under a permanent human cover. She is how Jarlaxle alone outside the household and the cult knows about the family's pact, and he does not tell Bregan D'aerthe members unless they work it out themselves (**A Debt in Blood**; the **Vessa** page). **Ryvarra** is a field observer at the Yawning Portal who catalogs Undermountain traffic for his Waterdeep network (the **Ryvarra** page).

His other names are GM names. J.B. Nevercott is the haberdasher who calls on people on the company's behalf, Captain Zardoz Zord is the carnival master, and Rongquan Mystere is the Shou owner of the Seven Masks Theater.

Jarlaxle attended Melee-Magthere, the warriors' academy of Menzoberranzan, where he was recognized for his mastery of strategy and tactics. He favored a stealthy dagger over hand-to-hand combat (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jarlaxle Baenre*, citing *A Reader's Guide to R. A. Salvatore's The Legend of Drizzt*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. He was the dandy of the city, and he saw that the best way for a male to prosper under the matrons was to stand outside the system and be too valuable to eliminate (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. Once he had escaped Menzoberranzan he did not want to return, and in later years he preferred adventure and profit on the surface (same page, citing *Rise of the King* and *Night of the Hunter*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. In House Baenre a male who looked dangerous was killed and a male who looked amusing was left alone, and Jarlaxle learned that difference before he learned to fight `[design]`.

In 1018 DR he got to know Zaknafein Do'Urden, and the two became close friends. Jarlaxle later betrayed Zaknafein in circumstances that no source gives, and he felt guilty about it for many years (same page, citing *Timeless* and *Road of the Patriarch*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Zaknafein returned in 1488 DR, and Jarlaxle was glad to see one of his oldest friends again (same page, citing *Timeless*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. He was also forced against his will to betray his human friend Artemis Entreri by handing him to the Netherese in Baldur's Gate, and he has regretted it ever since. Entreri has not forgiven him (same page, citing *The Last Threshold*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

Since he reached Waterdeep in 1491 DR he has spent many evenings on the docks of Deepwater Harbor, listening to ships come in, because a harbor at night sounds like nothing in Menzoberranzan `[design]`. He kept the Sea Maidens Faire running because it is the one business of his whose customers leave happy `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Jarlaxle wants Artemis Entreri to forgive him `[design]`. He does not expect it, and he has sent no message, because he knows that anything from him would be read as another scheme `[design]`. He also wants to walk down a Waterdeep street in daylight under his own face, as a drow, and be served in a shop without anyone staring `[design]`. Last, he wants to take the *Scarlet Marpenoth* down to the floor of the deep water beyond Deepwater Harbor and see what lies there, a use for the submarine that has nothing to do with escape `[design]`.

## Personality

Few people in Waterdeep have heard the name Jarlaxle Baenre. Those who deal with Bregan D'aerthe's contacts find them theatrical and generous with compliments, and they notice that the contact remembers everything said at earlier meetings. Whoever stands behind them is said to prefer leaving an enemy confused to leaving one dead.

Those who have dealt with Jarlaxle directly see a man who is always performing, and the performance is genuine. He gives compliments that are precise and earned, and he is never threatened by capable people, whom he collects. When he stops smiling, the situation has become genuinely serious.

## Connections

- **Bregan D'aerthe:** He leads the company, and nobody in it outranks him. Within it he is "the captain" or "J."
- **Soluun Xibrindas**, **Fel'rekt Lafeen** and **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr:** His three drow gunslinger lieutenants, who do most of his dirty work in the city. Fel'rekt is one of his most trusted men, and Jarlaxle knows about Soluun's murders at the Seven Masks Theater and has not decided what to do about them.
- **Nar'l Xibrindas:** His spy inside Xanathar's Guild, who reports to him by *Sending*.
- **Vessa**, **Ryvarra** and **Quilm:** His operatives in the Cassalanter social circle, at the Yawning Portal and across the city, who send him intelligence.
- **Margo Verida** and **Khafeyta Murzan:** The companions who travel aboard the *Eyecatcher*. They are not his employees.
- **Xanathar:** The beholder he prefers to keep manageable and alive, through the intelligence that Nar'l selects for it.
- **Manshoon:** The one rival he treats as a peer. The two have a fragile arrangement not to interfere with each other.
- **Laeral Silverhand:** The Open Lord, whose debt he wants more than any sum of gold. She regards Luskan as a greedy pirate state that cannot be trusted.
- **Dagult Neverember:** The lord of Neverwinter and his fiercest political opponent in the Lords' Alliance.

## Overview

J.B. Nevercott is a haberdasher in a burgundy coat who calls on people on behalf of Bregan D'aerthe. Captain Zardoz Zord is the flamboyant Illuskan master of the Sea Maidens Faire, a carnival of three ships. The fleet is due in Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21 for Fleetswake and sails at dawn on Tarsakh 20. Both have a reputation for charm and for remembering what visitors tell them.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Jarlaxle should inspire delight with his theatrical flair and razor wit, unease with the realization that every conversation is a negotiation, and reluctant admiration for an opponent who prefers confused enemies to dead ones.

**Emotions.** Jarlaxle most often feels theatrical amusement, predatory curiosity, strategic calculation behind every smile, honest delight when a scheme comes together, contempt for fools who threaten him (swiftly expressed), and possessive loyalty to Bregan D'aerthe that he frames as pragmatism.

**Motivations.** Jarlaxle wants to buy Luskan's way into the Lords' Alliance using the dragon hoard, acquire the dragonstaff of Ahghairon for leverage over Waterdeep, and ensure that if everything falls apart, the submarine is fueled and ready.

**Inspirations.** When playing Jarlaxle, channel Jack Sparrow (*Pirates of the Caribbean*), Loki (*Marvel*), and Hector (*Westworld*).

### Voice

*The real Jarlaxle: silken, witty and delighted, with every sentence part of a negotiation.*

- **Sound:** A smooth, light, amused drow voice, never raised, full of pleasure in its own phrasing.
- **Sentence shape:** Elegant, balanced, often long sentences with a sting at the end. He answers questions with better questions and makes offers dressed as compliments.
- **Word choice:** Courtly, cosmopolitan and playful: "delightful", "a small matter", "an arrangement of mutual convenience". He calls the party "my friends" when he means "my assets", and means both.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Pearls · Phrase.* Elegant and rare, used for comic precision ("Well. That is what the humans call a clusterfuck.").
- **Personality in speech:** Theatrical amusement over constant calculation. He tests every new contact aloud, useful or dangerous, and is visibly pleased to find them both. Contempt for fools who threaten him is swift and courteous. His loyalty to Bregan D'aerthe is framed as pragmatism.
- **Quirks:** He adjusts his hat before a decisive line. He compliments an opponent sincerely in the middle of beating them. He laughs at his own schemes when they work.
- **Signature phrases:** "Delightful." / "Useful, or dangerous? Ideally both." / "Let us come to an arrangement."
- **Never:** He never loses his temper, never breaks a deal he's proud of, and never lets anyone see the whole scheme.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Ah. You must be the ones I've heard about. Please, sit; I've been looking forward to deciding whether you're useful or dangerous. Ideally both."
  - *Business:* "You want something from me. I want Luskan at the Lords' table. I suspect there's an arrangement here that leaves us both delighted and someone else quite upset."
  - *Under pressure:* "Draw that, and my crew will be very sad to lose such a promising friend. Put it away, and we can be friends a while longer."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Jarlaxle appears as two faces. J.B. Nevercott is a haberdasher in a burgundy coat who calls on people on the company's behalf, and Captain Zardoz Zord is a flamboyant Illuskan sea captain running the Sea Maidens Faire carnival. To those he trusts, he is the secret lord of Luskan, the commander of Bregan D'aerthe, and a drow iconoclast whose loyalties run to himself first and his operatives second. Deep down, he likes to weave schemes that leave his enemies baffled, and the look on their faces when he gets the better of them is its own reward. The first thing he does with new contacts is determine whether they are useful or dangerous. Ideally, he discovers they are both.

**Morale.** In combat, Jarlaxle would swiftly and brutally kill someone to make an example, then propose terms. He reaches for leverage before force. If reduced to danger, he uses his wealth of magic items to escape. Direct confrontation only comes when someone kills his lieutenants without first offering terms.

**Relationships.** Jarlaxle is the leader of Bregan D'aerthe, the secret lord of Luskan, the employer of Soluun, Fel'rekt, and Krebbyg, and a rival who may become an ally when offered something worth more than the gold. He has known of the Stone, the Eyes, and the vault since reaching Waterdeep, no later than Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR. He alone outside the household and the cult knows of the Cassalanter pact, through his doppelganger spy Vessa. Eye #3 is aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The faces:** J.B. Nevercott and Captain Zardoz Zord are both Jarlaxle. Nevercott wears a *hat of disguise*, and Zord is Jarlaxle in the magical guise of an Illuskan human. Nevercott never admits to being anyone else, even when a player guesses correctly. How a character learns the name, and the **Jarlaxle Unmasked** Event Outcome, are on **Jarlaxle Baenre** in Villains.
> - **Who knows:** Few outsiders know that either face is Jarlaxle. Within the company he is "the captain" or "J.", and asking Zord directly does not get his true name.
> - **The pact:** Nobody outside the household and the cult knows what the Cassalanters have done, and everyone else only suspects the family. Jarlaxle is the one exception, through **Vessa**, and he tells no member of Bregan D'aerthe who has not worked it out.
> - **Eye #3:** He holds it in his stateroom, U4, aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, and he wants it as a bargaining chip and not for his own use.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with the showmanship left to Jarlaxle's own speech. The narrator describes his charm by what he knows and when he says it, and never plays his menace for laughs.
