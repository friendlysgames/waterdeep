**Margo Verida** (Chaotic good, Amnian human, she/her)

## Background

Margo Verida is an Amnian human bard who joined the Sea Maidens Faire within the past year as its lyrist (*Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research]`. The Faire's crew count her among its star performers (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She lives and works with the Faire, and her berth is the guest cabin on the flagship *Eyecatcher*, which she shares with Khafeyta Murzan (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The fleet is due in Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21 (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**).

Margo grew up in a river town in the grain country of Amn, in a house that kept a lyre on the wall for feast days `[design]`. A traveling lyrist lodged with her family through one winter and stayed on as her teacher for years afterward `[design]`. The teacher showed her how to sing from the chest instead of the throat, and that is where her warm alto comes from `[design]`. She traveled with the teacher to weddings and merchant feasts, and a caravan guard who shared their road traded fencing lessons for songs `[design]`. Margo learned the rapier from that guard in two seasons `[design]`.

A merchant family in Amn later hired her for a season and expected her to play for its guests and for no one else `[design]`. She left at the end of the season rather than sign again, and she has not played for a patron on those terms since `[design]`. Her teacher died that winter of a fever in a rented room on the coast road `[design]`. Margo kept the teacher's lute and the songs that the teacher had never written down, and she took ship north soon afterward `[design]`. She played her way along the coast for two years before she reached Luskan `[design]`.

She auditioned for the Faire on a Luskan quay and was hired as its lyrist `[design]`. The captain heard her play and named, without being asked, the bar in the second verse where she had gone flat `[design]`. Margo distrusted the compliment, because the praise she had known in Amn had mostly been wages. The next evening she played the same verse badly on purpose and waited to see whether he would praise it `[design]`. He told her it was worse than the night before and said why, and she has stayed on the strength of that answer `[design]`. She learned in the following months that Captain Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle, and she calls him by that name in private `[design]`. The Faire gave her steady work, a stage and a company that leaves her alone to play `[design]`.

Khafeyta joined the Faire in the same year, and the two share the guest cabin (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Margo reads a room by how it feels, and Khafeyta reads it by its exits and its weapons, so between them they have a room covered (the **Khafeyta Murzan** page). Khafeyta takes uncomplicated pleasure in Margo's company (the **Khafeyta Murzan** page).

**Personal goals.** Margo wants her teacher's songs written down `[design]`. She is the only person who knows them all, so after each performance she copies a few bars into a notebook, and the notebook is most of the way full `[design]`. She also wants a pupil, someone young who plays better than she does by the third year `[design]`. She has not looked for one, because teaching means staying in one place long enough to do it `[design]`. Last, she wants to play her home town's autumn festival from the stage, as a guest that the town asked for, because she once played it from the back of the crowd `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who hear her, Margo Verida is the lyrist whose music is better than a traveling carnival has any need for. She is warm, easy to talk to and clearly at home on a ship. She hums while she thinks.

Those who deal with her directly find that she asks one question too many about their motives, and that she sees through false flattery at once. She compliments honestly and expects the same in return. She is nobody's servant, and she is Jarlaxle's friend and not his employee.

## Overview

Margo Verida is an Amnian lyrist and singer with the Sea Maidens Faire, a carnival of three ships. The fleet is due in Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21 for Fleetswake. Her music is excellent, and the Faire counts her among its star performers. She spends much of her time with Captain Zardoz Zord, the Faire's master.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Margo should inspire admiration with the quality of the music — it is genuinely excellent, not traveling-performer adequate — and careful attention when a listener understands that the woman playing for the captain's table can also end a fight, and will if pressed.

**Emotions.** Margo most often feels creative absorption during a performance, relaxed warmth in Jarlaxle's company, quick professional assessment of new people's motives, genuine pleasure in a room that does not know what it is looking at, and a clean loyalty to Jarlaxle that has nothing servile about it.

**Motivations.** Margo wants to keep making music worth making, maintain her friendship with Jarlaxle on the terms they have established, and be honest about what she is — a bard and a skilled combatant who chooses her associates freely and has chosen well.

**Inspirations.** When playing Margo, channel Inara Serra (*Firefly*), Mata Hari (history), and Portia (*The Merchant of Venice*).

### Voice

*A superb bard: warm, witty, and nobody's servant, with an edge you notice only too late.*

- **Sound:** A rich, warm, musical alto. She's clearly a trained singer even when she's just talking.
- **Sentence shape:** Easy, graceful sentences with warmth and a hint of teasing. Her questions probe motives.
- **Word choice:** Artful and literate, with musical metaphors. She calls Jarlaxle by his real name in private, as an equal.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Pearls · Phrase.* Occasional and elegant, for emphasis ("That's a fucking gorgeous instrument."). A musician's gem: "That man sings like a cat being tuned."
- **Personality in speech:** Creative absorption and relaxed warmth. She tests people's flattery and sees through the false kind instantly. Her loyalty to Jarlaxle is clean and unservile.
- **Quirks:** She hums while thinking. She compliments honestly and expects honesty back.
- **Signature phrases:** "Say that again, and mean it." / "Music first; business after." / "He's a friend, not a master."
- **Never:** She never plays badly, never grovels, and never pretends to be less dangerous than she is when pushed.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You look like people with a story. Sit, I'll play something, and you can decide whether to tell it."
  - *Business:* "I'll pass it to him. But I'll tell him what I think of you too, so be honest."
  - *Under pressure:* "Put that down, darling. I've ended better fights than this with a lute."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Margo Verida is a performer traveling with Zardoz Zord's carnival — excellent, clearly at home on a ship, someone you assume is in Jarlaxle's company for professional reasons. To those who deal with her directly, she is one of the few people Jarlaxle treats as a peer rather than an asset, and she notices when the treatment shifts. Deep down, she flattery-tested Jarlaxle once, early in the acquaintance, to confirm his compliments were genuine rather than operational; they were, which is why she is still here.

**Morale.** In a fight, Margo uses Beguiling Song and Tasha's Hideous Laughter for control before engaging directly with her rapier. She is not suicidal and is not Jarlaxle's shield; she fights for reasons she has chosen, not on assignment. A DC 13 Persuasion check built on specific flattery about her music opens a social window before any fight becomes unavoidable. She will not betray Jarlaxle under any circumstances.

**Relationships.** Margo is Jarlaxle's companion, not his employee, his friend, paired with Khafeyta Murzan in J29, a BD-associated operative whose affiliation derives from freely chosen loyalty rather than organizational membership, and a combatant significantly more capable than a performer's cabin assignment implies.
