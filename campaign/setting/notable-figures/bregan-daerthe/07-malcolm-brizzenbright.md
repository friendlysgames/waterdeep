# Malkolm Brizzenbright

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - *Human ghost, neutral*. Stat block: **Ghost**.
> - **Affiliation:** Bregan D'aerthe (unknowing asset; ghost of the Seven Masks Theater)
> - **Featured in:** **Faction Outposts**, **Sea Maidens Faire**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Malcolm should inspire pity for a man who loved his theater enough to haunt it; endearment with his fussy pride in the building's artistic integrity; and useful surprise when his grievance against Jarlaxle turns out to be the intelligence the party needed.

**Emotions.** Malcolm most often feels proprietary outrage at the drow who use his dressing room, anxious concern about the theater's reputation, a director's eye that notices everything that has changed, and the specific unhappiness of a ghost who died too early to see what his theater would become.

**Motivations.** Malcolm wants to protect the Seven Masks Theater's reputation, convince someone worth convincing that "Rongquan Mystere" is not who he claims, and move props in a way that communicates his displeasure without being dismissed as a poltergeist.

**Inspirations.** When playing Malcolm, channel Nearly Headless Nick (*Harry Potter*), Casper (*Casper*), and Albert Brooks (*any comedy where he plays a man who is right about everything and cannot make anyone listen*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, the Seven Masks Theater has a ghost that moves props during rehearsals and occasionally hisses at actors who project poorly. To those who speak with him, Malkolm Brizzenbright is the theater's founder — anxious that his theater not be used for nefarious purposes, certain that "Rongquan Mystere" is a false name, and able to point to the empty dressing room where drow agents in trouble hide. Deep down, he has been waiting for someone to tell, and the only question is whether they are worth telling.

**Morale.** Malcolm is a ghost. He passes through walls, can terrify intruders, and moves props. He avoids combat because the theater cannot afford the damage.

**Relationships.** Malkolm Brizzenbright is the founding ghost of the Seven Masks Theater, the one who knows "Rongquan Mystere" is a fake identity, the witness who can identify the dressing room Bregan D'aerthe keeps as a safe house, and the man who once saw "Rongquan" speaking with drow in the lobby during a performance of *The Mad Mage's Seven Riddles*, when they mentioned something called the Sea Maidens Faire.

## Overview

The founding ghost of the Seven Masks Theater, dead for over thirty years — anxious about the new management, specific in his grievances, and waiting for someone worth telling.
