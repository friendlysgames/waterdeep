**Zelifarn** (Lawful good, bronze dragon, he/him)

## Background

Zelifarn is a young bronze dragon, twenty-two years old at the start of the campaign. His mother, Asphosis, was a master crystalmancer as well as a dragon `[research: Alexandrian PDF 9, p.71, transcribed from a page image]`. He hatched in a sea cave on the Sword Coast and grew up in her lair, where the tide filled the lower chambers twice a day `[design]`.

Asphosis kept a scrying crystal that had been in her family for generations, and she let her son watch ships through it long before he saw one up close `[design]`. He learned the names of the vessels that passed her coast and the colors of their sails, and he still calls ships by their names.

About four years ago agents whom Zelifarn has not identified killed Asphosis, and her hoard disappeared with her, including the crystal. He has carried the grief since then and has no name to put to the people responsible. He left the coast soon afterward, because the empty lair gave him nothing to do with the grief, and he followed the shipping lanes to the busiest water he could find `[design]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR he lives in Deepwater Harbor, where he spends most of his time searching the wrecks on the harbor floor for treasure and keeps what he finds in a hidden underwater cave (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Sailors have noticed him, and he has startled a few of them without hurting anyone (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He found a barnacle-covered chest not long ago and has not opened it, though he can smell gold inside (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He chose the harbor because every ship on the coast passes through it, and he learns sailor words from the voices that carry over the water and repeats each new word until he has it right `[design]`. He is eager to add treasure to his hoard, and he is willing to part with some of it in exchange for information about his surroundings (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Zelifarn*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

**Personal goals.** Zelifarn wants someone to explain how a ship is built, plank by plank `[design]`. He has seen the outsides of hundreds of hulls and the insides of the wrecks that he searches, and nothing in those wrecks still works, so he wants a ship's carpenter to describe the decks, the rigging and the galley as they are when a crew is aboard `[design]`. He also wants to learn the story behind every object in his cave `[design]`. He sorts the objects by where he found them and asks each sailor who will talk to him whether a piece looks familiar, because he prizes things that have a story more than things that only shine `[design]`. Last, he wants a friend among the surface folk who will stay for more than one conversation `[design]`. Most people have fled or fainted when he surfaced, so he has yet to talk with the same person twice `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people in the harbor, Zelifarn is the bronze dragon who surfaces beside a ship, grins and waves. He is friendly and curious, and he is eager to learn about the surface world and the strange ships that pass through his harbor. He never lies, he does not understand secrets, and he does not understand why people are afraid of him. If he is threatened, he submerges and does not fight at the surface.

He is proud of the harbor and calls it his own. He picks up rude sailor words and uses them wrongly and innocently.

## Connections

- **His mother, Asphosis:** Killed about four years ago. Her hoard and a family scrying crystal disappeared with her, and Zelifarn does not know who is responsible.
- **The *Eyecatcher*'s crew:** Sailors of the Sea Maidens Faire's flagship, whom Zelifarn finds interesting and wants to stay on good terms with. He answers to none of them.
- **Jarlaxle Baenre** and **Bregan D'aerthe:** Zelifarn does not report to Jarlaxle or anyone else, and he is not an operative of the company. He does not know whose vessel hangs under the *Eyecatcher*.

## Overview

Zelifarn is a young bronze dragon who lives in Deepwater Harbor and surfaces near passing ships to wave at the crews. He is friendly and curious about the surface world, and he has startled a few sailors without hurting anyone. He is happy to trade what he knows of the harbor for news from the surface.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Zelifarn should inspire immediate delight with his chirping enthusiasm; pleasant surprise that what he asks for is specific and what he offers is genuine; and amusement at a dragon who waves.

**Emotions.** Zelifarn most often feels bright curiosity, uncomplicated friendliness, genuine interest in surface-world affairs, delight when someone tells him something he did not know, and possessive pride in his stretch of Deepwater Harbor.

**Motivations.** Zelifarn wants to learn about the surface world and the strange ships that pass through his harbor, stay on good terms with the *Eyecatcher*'s crew, whom he finds interesting, and collect specific information in exchange for specific rewards.

**Inspirations.** When playing Zelifarn, channel Toothless (*How to Train Your Dragon*), Nemo (*Finding Nemo*), and Mushu (*Mulan*).

### Voice

*A bright, bouncy young bronze dragon who chirps, waves and trades facts like a delighted child.*

- **Sound:** A high, chirping, echoing voice with the sound of water in it. It warbles when he's excited.
- **Sentence shape:** Excited, rapid questions about the surface world, and short, precise bargains ("You tell me three things, I tell you one big thing!").
- **Word choice:** Harbour words and wonder: ships, shiny things, "surface folk", "the captain". He calls ships by their names and people by what they're wearing.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* None. He picks up rude sailor words and uses them wrong and innocently ("Is that a fucking? What's a fucking?").
- **Personality in speech:** Bright curiosity and friendliness. He's delighted by new information. He's proud of his harbour and says "my harbour" often.
- **Quirks:** He waves with a flipper. He repeats new words to learn them. He splashes when happy.
- **Signature phrases:** "Tell me something I don't know!" / "My harbour!" / "Trade? Trade!"
- **Never:** He never lies, never breaks a trade, and never understands why people keep secrets.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Hello! Hello, boat people! You're new! Tell me something I don't know!"
  - *Business:* "Three things about the city, and I'll tell you what I found down in the wrecks. Fair trade!"
  - *Under pressure:* "Bad people! Bad! I'm telling the captain!" (and he dives)

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Zelifarn is a young bronze dragon who surfaces near ships in Deepwater Harbor with bronze-scaled curiosity and a cheerful wave. To those who engage with him honestly, he will trade specific information about the harbor for specific information about the surface world, and he will give away a chest that he found and has not opened to anyone who tells him what he wants to know. Deep down, he does not report to Jarlaxle or anyone else. He knows the *Eyecatcher* has something under her hull and that her crew discouraged him from looking, but he does not know whose the vessel is. He never lies, and he trades facts for facts.

**Morale.** Zelifarn does not fight at the surface. If threatened, he submerges and does not resurface near the threat. He remembers, though, and the sea is large.

**Relationships.** Zelifarn is the young bronze dragon of Deepwater Harbor, on friendly terms with the *Eyecatcher*'s crew but answerable to none of them, and the keeper of an unopened chest from the harbor floor (300 sp, a golden octopus amulet, and a *scroll of revivify* in a secret compartment of the lid) that he will give to anyone who tells him what he wants to know.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with the bright, bouncy manner of a young dragon in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats his grief for his mother as real and does not play it for comedy.
