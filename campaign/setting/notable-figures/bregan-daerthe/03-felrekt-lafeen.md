# Fel'rekt Lafeen

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Drow gunslinger, neutral good*. Stat block: **Drow Gunslinger**.
> - **Affiliation:** Bregan D'aerthe
> - **Featured in:** **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Sea Maidens Faire**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Fel'rekt should inspire admiration for his courage in leaving a society that rejected him, endearment with his eager-to-prove-himself energy, and trust with his refusal to kill when any other option exists.

**Emotions.** Fel'rekt most often feels determined optimism, competitive enthusiasm, easy camaraderie with Krebbyg, genuine gratitude toward Jarlaxle, restless eagerness, and a stubborn refusal to let cruelty define him.

**Motivations.** Fel'rekt wants to prove himself worthy of Jarlaxle's confidence, protect Krebbyg (his closest friend), and demonstrate that leaving drow society made him stronger rather than weaker.

**Inspirations.** When playing Fel'rekt, channel Samwise Gamgee (*The Lord of the Rings*), Finn (*Star Wars*), and Sokka (*Avatar: The Last Airbender*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Fel'rekt is a drow gunslinger, one of Jarlaxle's most trusted lieutenants. To those he trusts, he was born female in a society that punishes male drow; he petitioned to join Bregan D'Aerthe and Jarlaxle took an immediate shine to him. Deep down, he lacks the cruelty common to most drow and will not kill an adversary unless left with no other choice.

**Morale.** In a fight, Fel'rekt hurls himself into combat with verve, trading banter with Krebbyg at the enemies' expense. He volunteers for dangerous tasks first and disengages last.

**Relationships.** Fel'rekt is one of Jarlaxle's most loyal lieutenants, the close friend and operational partner of Krebbyg Masq'il'yr, and the Bregan D'Aerthe operative who led the team at Gralhund Villa.

## Overview

A drow gunslinger and Jarlaxle's trusted lieutenant — eager to prove himself, reluctant to kill when any other option remains, and almost always accompanied by Krebbyg.
