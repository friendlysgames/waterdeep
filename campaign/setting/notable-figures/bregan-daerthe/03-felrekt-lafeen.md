**Fel'rekt Lafeen** (Neutral good, drow, he/him)

## Background

Fel'rekt Lafeen is a male drow who was born female. Unhappy with the treatment of males in his society, he petitioned to join Bregan D'aerthe, and Jarlaxle took a shine to him almost immediately (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The young drow has since become one of Jarlaxle's most loyal lieutenants, and he is eager to prove himself and quick to volunteer for tasks (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki adds that he volunteered for all the most dangerous missions (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Fel'rekt Lafeen*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

He grew up in a minor house of Menzoberranzan that had no use for him as a son `[design]`. The house raised its daughters for rank and its sons for service, and Fel'rekt knew from early childhood that he was a boy `[design]`. His mother treated the matter as an embarrassment to be managed and never as a fact to be heard `[design]`. He had a younger brother, born male, whom the house put to work as a servant before the boy was old enough to understand the job `[design]`. Fel'rekt watched how the house treated that brother and decided that he would not be raised for a rank that was never meant for him `[design]`.

Before he left, the brother carved him a comb from a scrap of dragon bone, and Fel'rekt still carries it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e, for the comb in his footlocker; its maker is `[design]`). He walked out of the house one night with a blade and the comb, and he spent a season in the passages of the city before he found the company `[design]`. Bregan D'aerthe was built from rogue males who had left their houses, and it took him in without asking what he had been (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**). He asked for no rank, and when the company's recruiters set the unwanted tasks in front of him he took every one `[design]`. He did not take the house's goddess with him. Bregan D'aerthe has rejected Lolth, and Fel'rekt never mentions her, so he found that he had nothing to unlearn `[design]`.

Jarlaxle gave him a Lantanese pistol and a veteran to teach him, and within a season he was an expert pistoleer (*Dragon Heist*, 5e, for the Lantanese pistols and the skill; the lessons are `[design]`). The company then paired him with Krebbyg Masq'il'yr, a young drow whose house had been destroyed `[design]`. Fel'rekt makes the plans and Krebbyg acts first, and the two have shared night watches, banter and a pair of obsidian dice ever since `[design]`. The adventure calls them close friends who work as a team, trading jokes at their enemies' expense (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Jarlaxle sent Fel'rekt, Krebbyg and Soluun Xibrindas to Waterdeep to do the company's dirty work in the city (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Fel'rekt Lafeen*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. Fel'rekt and Krebbyg prefer to work at night (same page) `[research: wiki only]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR Fel'rekt keeps a stateroom aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, the Lantanese submarine moored in Deepwater Harbor, where he cleans his pistol and keeps a footlocker with 65 gp, the comb and a pair of obsidian dice (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Jarlaxle has tasked him with the company's logistics, and he sees that its operatives in the city are supplied and armed `[research]`.

**Personal goals.** Fel'rekt wants his younger brother out of the house, or at the least to learn that the boy is alive `[design]`. He has sent no word since he left, because any message could be read by the house before it reached the boy, and he has not found a safe way to send one `[design]`. He also wants to see the harbor by day `[design]`. Every Waterdeep morning he has spent below deck or in shade, and he wants to stand on the quay at noon and watch the ships come in without squinting `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who meet him, Fel'rekt Lafeen is a cheerful young drow with a pistol on his hip and a plan for whatever comes up. He talks quickly when he is excited, counts his options off on his fingers, and checks on Krebbyg in the middle of a sentence.

He volunteers for the dangerous job first and offers an opponent a way out before he draws. He lacks the cruelty common to most drow and kills only when nothing else is left to him. He is openly grateful to Jarlaxle and calls him "the captain".

He does not talk about the house he came from, and he does not bring up Lolth `[design]`.

## Overview

Fel'rekt Lafeen is a young drow gunslinger who grins easily and volunteers for every job that comes along. He is rarely seen without his partner Krebbyg Masq'il'yr. He would rather talk an opponent into surrender than shoot one.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Fel'rekt should inspire admiration for his courage in leaving a society that rejected him, endearment with his eager-to-prove-himself energy, and trust with his refusal to kill when any other option exists.

**Emotions.** Fel'rekt most often feels determined optimism, competitive enthusiasm, easy camaraderie with Krebbyg, genuine gratitude toward Jarlaxle, restless eagerness, and a stubborn refusal to let cruelty define him.

**Motivations.** Fel'rekt wants to prove himself worthy of Jarlaxle's confidence, protect Krebbyg (his closest friend), and demonstrate that leaving drow society made him stronger rather than weaker.

**Inspirations.** When playing Fel'rekt, channel Samwise Gamgee (*The Lord of the Rings*), Finn (*Star Wars*), and Sokka (*Avatar: The Last Airbender*).

### Voice

*An earnest, eager drow gunslinger who'd rather disarm you than kill you, and says so.*

- **Sound:** A bright, earnest young drow voice that speeds up when he's excited.
- **Sentence shape:** Enthusiastic, medium sentences, plans and encouragement. When a fight looks likely, he offers alternatives aloud.
- **Word choice:** Loyal and hopeful: "we can do this", "the captain trusts us", "there's another way". He calls Krebbyg "Kreb" and Jarlaxle "the captain".
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Occasional and excited ("Oh, shit, that worked!").
- **Personality in speech:** Determined optimism and competitiveness. His refusal to be cruel shows in how he keeps offering surrender. His gratitude to Jarlaxle is open and sincere.
- **Quirks:** He counts off options on his fingers. He checks on Kreb mid-sentence. He grins when a plan goes right.
- **Signature phrases:** "There's another way." / "Kreb, behind me." / "The captain trusts us."
- **Never:** He never kills when there's another option, never abandons Kreb, and never mocks surface folk.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Hi! Fel'rekt. That's Kreb. We're with the captain, sort of. Mostly. It's complicated."
  - *Business:* "We go in quiet, we take what we came for, nobody has to die. That's the plan. I like that plan."
  - *Under pressure:* "Drop it! Please drop it, I really don't want to shoot you!"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Fel'rekt is a drow gunslinger, one of Jarlaxle's most trusted lieutenants. To those he trusts, he was born female and was unhappy with the treatment of males in his society; he petitioned to join Bregan D'aerthe and Jarlaxle took an immediate shine to him. Deep down, he lacks the cruelty common to most drow and will not kill an adversary unless left with no other choice.

**Morale.** In a fight, Fel'rekt hurls himself into combat with verve, trading banter with Krebbyg at the enemies' expense. He volunteers for dangerous tasks first and disengages last.

**Relationships.** Fel'rekt is one of Jarlaxle's most loyal lieutenants, the close friend and operational partner of Krebbyg Masq'il'yr, and the lieutenant Jarlaxle has tasked with logistics.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, warm toward a young man who is trying hard to be good at a hard job. The narrator treats his birth and his choices as plain facts and never as a joke.
