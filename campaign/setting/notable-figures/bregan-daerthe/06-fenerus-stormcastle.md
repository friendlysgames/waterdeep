# Fenerus Stormcastle

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - *Human spy (retired brigand), neutral evil*. Stat block: **Spy**.
> - **Affiliation:** Bregan D'aerthe (cut-out who spies for Luskan and has worked out where the messages go)
> - **Featured in:** **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Sea Maidens Faire**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Fenerus should inspire wariness with a man who has a long criminal record and no reason to be honest; grudging respect when what he knows about the messages proves real; and sympathy for the impossible position that placed him at the intersection of every faction's interest simultaneously.

**Emotions.** Fenerus most often feels calculating watchfulness, practiced evasion, a criminal's patience honed over a decade of brigandage and a quiet retirement, contempt for anyone who thinks threats work faster than deals, and an exhaustion he has worn so long it looks like indifference.

**Motivations.** Fenerus wants to trade what he knows about the messages for immunity and nothing less, survive the overlapping interest of the Zhentarim, Xanathar's Guild, and Bregan D'aerthe, and live long enough to see the arrangement concluded cleanly.

**Inspirations.** When playing Fenerus, channel Walter White (*Breaking Bad*, early seasons), Jimmy McGill (*Better Call Saul*), and Red Reddington (*The Blacklist*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Fenerus Stormcastle is a big, scar-faced man with gray hair who lights lamps in the Trades Ward and minds his own business. To those who question him, he is a practiced liar who has long since decided what his silence is worth and what he will give up first. Deep down, he knows where the messages he gathers are taken, because he once followed the drow who collects them, and he has been wondering what that knowledge is worth.

**Morale.** In a fight, Fenerus disengages immediately. He has survived too many of the wrong kinds of fights to start one he did not choose.

**Relationships.** Fenerus is the former founder of the Blood Hawk Posse (current brigands on the Amphail road), a Bregan D'aerthe cut-out and message-drop on Quill Alley, and the target of Manshoon's kidnap directive in **Faction Outposts**.

## Overview

A big, scar-faced lamplighter in the Trades Ward who minds his own business and has been wondering what he knows about the message drop is worth.
