**Fenerus Stormcastle** (Neutral, Tethyrian human, he/him)

## Background

Fenerus Stormcastle is a Tethyrian human who founded the Blood Hawk Posse, a gang of horse-riding brigands that still harries travelers on the road to Amphail (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He was drummed out of the gang after a botched raid and retired to Waterdeep, where he joined the Guild of Chandlers and Lamplighters (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Guilds**). He rents a small house on Quill Alley in the Trades Ward and lives alone, and the children of the alley say that he looks like a man who has been in many fights, judging by the scars on his face (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Fenerus grew up on a horse farm near the Tethyrian border, where his father broke colts for the caravans that passed `[design]`. He rode before he could read, and he can still tell what a horse means to do from the set of its ears `[design]`. When a tax collector took the farm, he hired out as a caravan guard, and he saw that the wagon masters who paid him a few silver a day were carrying fortunes `[design]`. He gathered six riders he had guarded beside and founded the Posse, and he set one rule, that they would take coin from the road and kill no one they could avoid `[design]`.

He planned the raid that ended it, against a wagon train that he had been told was lightly guarded `[design]`. A second company of hired swords rode behind the wagons, and three of his riders died `[design]`. The survivors blamed his planning and voted him out, and he left his younger brother with the gang because the brother would not leave it `[design]`. Fenerus rode to Waterdeep alone, and he has had no word of his brother since `[design]`.

He chose the lamplighters because a lamplighter walks every street in the ward after dark and nobody looks at him twice `[design]`. The guild's wage did not cover the rent on his house, so when a cloaked stranger stopped him on his rounds and offered coin for news of the Lords and the ward, he took it `[design]`. The stranger said that he came from Luskan `[design]`, and Fenerus has spied for Luskan since, passing along what he hears of Waterdeep's political climate and the gossip from his nightly rounds (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). By one account he is the primary spy of Bregan D'aerthe without knowing it (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Fenerus Stormcastle*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. For the company he is a cut-out who is not supposed to know where the messages go (**Bregan D'aerthe**).

Fenerus collects coded messages from dead drops across the ward and keeps them in his house, and a cloaked drow comes for them (**Trades Ward**; **Bregan D'aerthe**). He followed the drow once after a pickup and saw where he took the messages (**Trades Ward**). He has not worked out that the cloaked drow belong to Bregan D'aerthe, and he has told no one what he saw (**Trades Ward**). He has been wondering what that knowledge is worth. On Ches 1, 1492 DR he is still lighting lamps in the Trades Ward and still passing along what he hears.

**Personal goals.** Fenerus wants to know whether his younger brother is alive `[design]`. He cannot ask without going to the Amphail road, and he cannot go there as the man who founded the Posse `[design]`. He wants a horse of his own, kept in a paddock outside the walls, and a long ride with no errand at the end of it `[design]`. He has not ridden since he came to Waterdeep, and he stops to watch every draft horse that passes his lamps `[design]`. He also wants a sealed paper that lets him use his own name on any road in the North, because he has never learned whether a warrant from his Posse years still stands and has never dared to ask `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people in the Trades Ward, Fenerus is a big, scar-faced man with gray hair who lights the lamps and minds his own business. He answers questions slowly and gives away as little as he can, and he looks tired in a way that people take for indifference.

## Connections

- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr:** The cloaked drow who collects the messages from Fenerus's house. Fenerus followed him once and has not spoken of it.
- **Bregan D'aerthe** and **Jarlaxle Baenre:** The company treats Fenerus as a cut-out and a message drop, and he does not know that it is behind the drow who collect from him. He believes that he spies for Luskan.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** Manshoon's cell has marked Fenerus's house as a soft target, having identified him as a Bregan D'aerthe cut-out (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**).
- **The Blood Hawk Posse:** The gang that he founded and that still raids the Amphail road. His younger brother is a member `[design]`.
- **Jenks:** An acquaintance, one of the people he sees on his rounds (**Jenks**).

## Overview

Fenerus Stormcastle is a big, scar-faced man with gray hair who lights lamps in the Trades Ward. He belongs to the Guild of Chandlers and Lamplighters and rents a small house on Quill Alley, where he lives alone. The children of the alley say that he looks like a man who has been in many fights.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Fenerus should inspire wariness with a man who has a long criminal record and no reason to be honest; grudging respect for how much he has worked out about the messages on his own; and sympathy for the impossible position that placed him at the intersection of two rival factions' interest simultaneously.

**Emotions.** Fenerus most often feels calculating watchfulness, practiced evasion, a criminal's patience honed over a decade of brigandage and a quiet retirement, contempt for anyone who thinks threats work faster than deals, and an exhaustion he has worn so long it looks like indifference.

**Motivations.** Fenerus wants to trade what he knows about the messages for immunity and nothing less, survive the overlapping interest of the Zhentarim and Bregan D'aerthe, and live long enough to see the arrangement concluded cleanly.

**Inspirations.** When playing Fenerus, channel Walter White (*Breaking Bad*, early seasons), Jimmy McGill (*Better Call Saul*), and Red Reddington (*The Blacklist*).

### Voice

*A scarred ex-brigand who talks in careful deals and says nothing for free.*

- **Sound:** A deep, gravelly, tired voice, slow and deliberate.
- **Sentence shape:** Measured sentences that circle the point, and conditional offers ("If I had something, and I'm not saying I do…").
- **Word choice:** Lawyer-ish criminal caution: immunity, "in writing", "a signed pardon". He calls interrogators "friend" with heavy irony.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Worn and casual ("I've had ten years of shit. Yours is small.").
- **Personality in speech:** Calculating, evasive and exhausted. He's contemptuous of threats and says so calmly. He gives a little to show he has more.
- **Quirks:** He taps the table for "in writing". He answers every question with the price of answering.
- **Signature phrases:** "In writing." / "I'm not saying I do." / "Threats are slow, friend. Deals are fast."
- **Never:** He never gives away what he knows about the messages without immunity, never raises his voice, and never trusts a promise that isn't signed.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Lamplighter. Retired from the other thing. What do you want, friend?"
  - *Business:* "Immunity from a magister or a Lord, in writing, and I'll tell you where the messages go. Not before."
  - *Under pressure:* "Go ahead. Then you get nothing. Threats are slow, friend."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Fenerus Stormcastle is a big, scar-faced man with gray hair who lights lamps in the Trades Ward and minds his own business. To those who question him, he is a practiced liar who has long since decided what his silence is worth and what he will give up first. Deep down, he knows where the messages he gathers are taken, because he once followed the drow who collects them, and he has been wondering what that knowledge is worth.

**Morale.** In a fight, Fenerus disengages immediately. He has survived too many of the wrong kinds of fights to start one he did not choose.

**Relationships.** Fenerus is the former founder of the Blood Hawk Posse (current brigands on the Amphail road), a Bregan D'aerthe cut-out and message-drop on Quill Alley, and a soft target for Manshoon's cell.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, gentle humor about the lamplighter's patience in Personality. The narrator treats his tiredness and his brother as real and does not mock them.
