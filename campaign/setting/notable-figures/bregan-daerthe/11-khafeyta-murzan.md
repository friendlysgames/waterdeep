**Khafeyta Murzan** (Neutral good, Mulhorandi human, she/her)

## Background

Khafeyta Murzan is a Mulhorandi human swashbuckler who joined the Sea Maidens Faire within the past year as an acrobat (*Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research]`. She is one of the Faire's star performers (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She lives and works with the Faire, and her berth is the guest cabin on the flagship *Eyecatcher*, which she shares with Margo Verida (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She travels with the fleet, which is due in Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21 (**Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**).

Khafeyta grew up in a river town in Mulhorand, in a family that worked the temple festivals as rope walkers and tumblers `[design]`. She was crossing a low rope before she could read, and her mother taught her how to fall safely before she taught her how to stand `[design]`. A retired duelist who gave lessons on the town quay took chores in place of fees, and she scrubbed his boat for six years in exchange for an hour of fencing a week `[design]`. He taught her reach and the measure of a blade, and the rope had already taught her footwork, so by fifteen she was beating students twice her age `[design]`.

At nineteen she left the family act to guard river barges, because the work paid in coin and the fights were real `[design]`. She took her scar on one of those barges, in a fight against four pirates on a night with no moon, and she can still say where each of them stood `[design]`. A deckhand on the same barge had heard a stranger boast of beating a gang single-handed, and he tried to repeat it on a dock and was badly cut `[design]`. She stitched him herself, and since then she has had no patience for a fight story that has been improved in the telling `[design]`.

The barge company folded when its owner died, and Khafeyta took work in a port town where the Sea Maidens Faire was hiring a rope act `[design]`. She walked the rope for the captain with a blade in each hand, and he asked her what the best fight of her life had been `[design]`. She told him about the barge fight as it happened, including the moment when she was afraid, and he offered her a place in the Faire `[design]`. She took it because it let her keep her blades on the stage `[design]`.

Khafeyta joined the Faire in the same year as Margo, and the two share the guest cabin (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She reads a room by its exits and its weapons, and Margo reads it by how it feels, so between them they have a room covered (the Persona below). Khafeyta takes uncomplicated pleasure in Margo's company and in Jarlaxle's, and she drills with Margo using practice blades whenever both are free `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Khafeyta wants a rematch with her old teacher `[design]`. He beat her in the last bout they fenced, in the week she left the river town, and he has not seen her fence since `[design]`. She wants to meet him again with a fair blade in her hand and win `[design]`. She also wants a sword made for her from the hilt up, because every blade she carries was taken, won or bought secondhand `[design]`. She has not found a smith whose work she trusts, and she means to look for one in the largest city on the coast `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who meet her, Khafeyta Murzan is the woman beside the Faire's lyrist who carries herself like someone who knows exactly how good she is. She is polite but terse with strangers, and she has taken the measure of a room's exits and weapons before the greetings are done.

Those who talk with her find that she is sharp and quickly interested in whether they have a story worth hearing. A true account of a hard fight earns her respect, and she can tell embellishment from truth, so a boaster gets nothing from her. Flattery does not work on her, and she answers to Jarlaxle as a companion and not as an employee.

## Overview

Khafeyta Murzan is a Mulhorandi acrobat and fencer with the Sea Maidens Faire, a carnival of three ships. The fleet is due in Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21 for Fleetswake. The Faire counts her among its star performers, and she spends much of her time with Captain Zardoz Zord. She asks strangers about the best fight of their lives, and she listens closely to the answer.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Khafeyta should inspire immediate interest in how good she actually is — she carries herself like someone who knows, and the impression is accurate — and comfortable respect for the way she treats a genuine fight story as real currency.

**Emotions.** Khafeyta most often feels active physical engagement with whatever she is doing, competitive assessment of capable opponents, uncomplicated pleasure in Margo's company and Jarlaxle's, a fighter's professional pride, and a contented certainty about her own situation that reads as ease and is earned.

**Motivations.** Khafeyta wants to be excellent at her craft, spend time with people worth spending it with, and find situations complicated enough to be interesting. She chose Jarlaxle's company for reasons similar to Margo's and arrived at the same conclusion through a different door.

**Inspirations.** When playing Khafeyta, channel Inigo Montoya (*The Princess Bride*), Brienne of Tarth (*Game of Thrones*), and Porthos (*The Three Musketeers*).

### Voice

*A confident swordswoman who wants to hear your best fight story, and will know if you lie.*

- **Sound:** A strong, direct, cheerful voice with a fighter's bark in it.
- **Sentence shape:** Direct questions, quick assessments, and genuine laughter at a good story.
- **Word choice:** Fencing and brawling vocabulary: reach, footwork, "a real fight". She calls good fighters "friend" instantly and bad ones nothing.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Cheerful and casual ("Fuck, that's a good scar. Tell me.").
- **Personality in speech:** Competitive assessment and easy confidence. A true fight story wins her over, and embellishment loses her.
- **Quirks:** She names the exits and weapons in the room aloud, casually. She asks "How'd you get that scar?" as a greeting.
- **Signature phrases:** "Tell me about your best fight." / "That's embellished." / "Two exits, four blades."
- **Never:** She never brags without proof, never fights unfairly, and never lets flattery work on her.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "How'd you get that scar? Tell me true, and I'll buy the drink."
  - *Business:* "Margo reads people. I read rooms. Between us we'll know if you're worth the captain's time."
  - *Under pressure:* "Finally. Something interesting."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Khafeyta Murzan is the other of Captain Zord's two companions — not the performer, the fighter. To those who engage her directly, she is sharp and immediately interested in whether the person across from her has a story worth hearing; a genuine account of a difficult fight opens the social window that flattery opens for Margo, and she checks for the difference between embellishment and truth. Deep down, she and Margo occupy different frequencies — Margo reads a room through its emotional resonance, Khafeyta through its exits and weapons — and between them they have all of it covered.

**Morale.** In a fight, Khafeyta engages directly. She is the more aggressive of the two companions, the one who steps forward while Margo controls the room behind her. A DC 14 Persuasion check grounded in a credible account of a real fight creates a social opening before violence becomes the only option. She will not betray Jarlaxle.

**Relationships.** Khafeyta is Jarlaxle's companion, paired with Margo Verida, and a swashbuckler whose presence alongside Margo makes the pair significantly harder to handle than a performer's cabin assignment implies.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The name:** Khafeyta knows that Captain Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle, and she tells no outsider. How a character learns the name, and the **Jarlaxle Unmasked** Event Outcome, are on **Jarlaxle Baenre** in Villains.
> - **What else she knows:** The Faire's ships carry drow crews under illusion, and Jarlaxle wants the Lords' Alliance to admit Luskan and to push Neverwinter out.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a fighter's easy confidence in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Khafeyta as Jarlaxle's equal and never as his hireling.
