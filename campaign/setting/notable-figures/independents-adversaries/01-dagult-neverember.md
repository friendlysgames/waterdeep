## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Dagult should inspire recognition before the party ever meets him — Renaer's stories will have shaped an impression already — then grudging respect for his genuine political competence, and finally discomfort at how easily his self-justification holds together under scrutiny.

**Emotions.** Dagult most often feels wounded pride, calculating pragmatism, a controlled and rarely-shown fury, genuine if complicated love for his son, contempt for those he considers beneath his notice, and a creeping anxiety about how history will remember him.

**Motivations.** Dagult wants his stolen gold back — or failing that, some measure of vindication that what he did was defensible — wants to repair, or at least stabilize, what is left of his relationship with Renaer, and wants to ensure the fortune he hid does not simply enrich whichever faction gets to it first.

**Inspirations.** When playing Dagult, channel Tywin Lannister (*Game of Thrones*), Frank Underwood (*House of Cards*), and King Lear (*King Lear*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Dagult is the disgraced former Open Lord of Waterdeep, now Lord of Neverwinter, a man attempting — with mixed success — to rehabilitate a reputation he insists was never as damning as his critics claim. To those he still trusts, he is controlling and transactional, treating loyalty as a resource to be managed rather than a bond to be honored, but he genuinely believes he was building something for Waterdeep even as he stole from it. Deep down, he is afraid history will remember him as nothing but a thief, and Renaer's estrangement feels like proof that the fear is already coming true.

**Morale.** Dagult is not a frontline combatant — he arrives at any confrontation with hired muscle and Watch loyalists rather than fighting personally, and he strongly prefers legal threat and political leverage over violence. If directly and publicly humiliated, however, he will not back down; losing face in front of witnesses costs him more than losing the argument itself.

**Relationships.** Dagult is the estranged father of Renaer Neverember, the man who bound Aurinax to guard the Vault of Dragons in exchange for the dragonstaff of Ahghairon, the disgraced former Open Lord whose fall from power set the Grand Game in motion, and the reason the Stone of Golorr was ever blinded in the first place.

## Overview

Dagult Neverember is the disgraced former Open Lord of Waterdeep who embezzled 500,000 gp from the city before his removal from office; the gold he hid is the prize every faction in the Grand Game is chasing, and he intends to reclaim it — or at minimum to ensure no one else profits cleanly from his theft.
