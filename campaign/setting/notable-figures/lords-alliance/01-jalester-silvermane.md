**Jalester Silvermane** (Lawful good, Chondathan human, he/him)

## Background

Jalester Silvermane is a Chondathan human from Cormyr, and he is in his mid-twenties in 1492 DR (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He earned his spurs working for a mercenary company called the Steel Shadows (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki says that he was born in Cormyr and served the company until some years before it left the Dales (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jalester Silvermane*, citing *Death Masks*, a novel; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

His mother kept the accounts of a grain factor in a market town on the King's Road, and she taught him to read a ledger and to notice which figure had been changed `[design]`. His father drove freight wagons and was seldom home `[design]`. Jalester joined the company as a youth because it paid in coin that he could send back to her `[design]`. The company's sergeants taught him to keep watch on a room, to write down what he heard and to ask the one question that the talk had skipped `[design]`.

A few years ago he left the Dales and traveled to Waterdeep with several other members of the company (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). One of them, Faerrel Dunblade, became his best friend and lover (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and the wiki says that the two fell in love in Waterdeep (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jalester Silvermane*, citing *Death Masks*, a novel; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Jalester chose the city because Faerrel wanted to see a place with more than one road leading out of it `[design]`.

The wizard Elminster befriended the two young men and brought them to the attention of Open Lord **Laeral Silverhand**, who put them to work as deputies and spies (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki dates that meeting to 1491 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jalester Silvermane*, citing *Death Masks*, a novel; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Jalester and Faerrel helped the Open Lord expose a plot to overthrow the government (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and Faerrel was killed last year (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Jalester stayed in Waterdeep and became one of Laeral's field operatives, and he has been romantically unattached since (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

He kept Faerrel's belt knife and a bundle of his letters, and he has sent neither to Faerrel's mother in Cormyr `[design]`. He wrote to her once to say that her son was dead and has not written since `[design]`. On the nights when the Portal empties he walks home the long way past the Watch posts, and he looks at who is on duty `[design]`.

Laeral asked him to spy on adventurers who might, through their actions, aid or imperil the city (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He spends much of his time at the Yawning Portal, usually seated by himself in a quiet corner, and **Durnan**, who knows that he works for Laeral, leaves him alone (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He carries a badge of the Watch that returns to Laeral if it is lost or taken from him (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and he reads the Watch's file ledger each tenday (**The City Watch**). He offers membership in the Lords' Alliance in person to those who qualify (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki lists Elminster and **Mirt** among his friends (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jalester Silvermane*, citing *Dragon Heist* and *Dungeon of the Mad Mage*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

On Ches 1, 1492 DR Jalester is one of Laeral's main field operatives (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). His field reports and the Watch's contacts have placed the vault of the stolen dragons in Neverwinter (**The Lords' Alliance**, Organizations), and he does not know where Xanathar's lair is and would have to ask Laeral (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

**Personal goals.** Jalester wants to go to Cormyr and tell Faerrel's mother what her son was like in Waterdeep, and to give her the knife and the letters `[design]`. He has put the journey off for a year because he cannot decide what to say about how Faerrel died, and he has not asked Laeral for leave `[design]`. He also wants to finish one conversation with another person without it turning into a debrief `[design]`. Twice he has asked a personal question and then excused himself, and he rehearses the next attempt on the walk home `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who meet him, Jalester is a lean, thinning-haired man in a dark doublet who is polite and exact and sits alone with a tankard that he rarely touches. He is direct about practical matters and asks one question that is more specific than the conversation calls for, which some people find slightly strange.

He is lonely and does not feel sorry for himself. He thanks people formally for small kindnesses and praises competent people in a warm and awkward way, and he never speaks of Faerrel unless he is asked twice. Under the manners he is earnest (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and he works late because the work is the one thing that he knows how to finish `[design]`.

## Connections

- **Laeral Silverhand:** The Open Lord, to whom he reports directly. She gave him and Faerrel their first work in the city, and his badge of the Watch returns to her if he loses it.
- **Mirt:** A friend, by the wiki's account, and Laeral's closest advisor `[research: wiki only]`.
- **Elminster:** A friend, by the wiki's account, who befriended Jalester and Faerrel and brought them to Laeral `[research: wiki only]`.
- **Durnan:** The keeper of the Yawning Portal, who knows whom Jalester works for and leaves him alone at the corner table.
- **Faerrel Dunblade:** His partner, a former mercenary of the Steel Shadows, who was killed last year. Jalester has kept his knife and his letters `[design]`.
- **Faerrel's mother:** A woman in Cormyr who has had one letter from Jalester and none since `[design]`.
- **The Steel Shadows:** The Cormyrean mercenary company where he earned his spurs. Several of its members traveled to Waterdeep with him.
- **Sevel Dastar:** A middle-aged Chondathan human in a plain brown coat who works for the Alliance as a spy.
- **Alliance field agents:** The agents of the Waterdeep chapter answer to him, and he is the contact for those who join.
- **The Splinter:** The cell that Jalester believes is trying to seize Waterdeep's institutions from within. He has no name for its leader, and he separates it from Davil Starsong's cell with the words "not Davil's people".
- **The Cassalanters:** A family that he thinks too rich and too pleasant. He has no proof of anything against them.
- **The City Watch:** The force whose badge he carries and whose file ledger he reads each tenday.

## Overview

Jalester Silvermane is a lean, thinning-haired man in a dark doublet who sits alone at the Yawning Portal's corner table. He wears a Watch badge on his belt and asks questions that are more specific than casual conversation calls for. Anyone at the Portal's bar can point him out, and the Lords' Alliance recruits through him.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Jalester should inspire sympathy with his loneliness and unresolved grief, respect for his understated competence, and protectiveness when the party realizes how much of his life he has sacrificed to a job that will not love him back.

**Emotions.** Jalester most often feels dutiful focus, private grief for Faerrel, the reward of a job done well, chronic loneliness he fills with work, guarded warmth toward competent people, and a longing for connection he does not know how to pursue.

**Motivations.** Jalester wants to serve Laeral Silverhand and the Lords' Alliance faithfully, find someone who makes the loneliness bearable, and prove that Faerrel's death was not meaningless.

**Inspirations.** When playing Jalester, channel Steve Rogers (*Captain America*), Brienne of Tarth (*Game of Thrones*), and Will Graham (*Hannibal*).

### Voice

*A lean, disciplined agent: polite, precise and lonely, and always slightly on duty.*

- **Sound:** A quiet, level Cormyte voice, formal and careful, a bit tired.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, specific questions and concise answers. When he tries small talk, it comes out as a debrief.
- **Word choice:** Agent's terminology: asset, assignment, "the Open Lord's interests". He is courteous: "ma'am", "sir", and first names only when invited.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Rare and self-directed ("Damn it. My mistake."). One raw "fuck" if Faerrel's death is used against him.
- **Personality in speech:** Dutiful focus and loneliness. He gives competent people warm, awkward praise. His grief for Faerrel shows only as a stop mid-sentence. When he wants connection, he asks a personal question and then retreats from it.
- **Quirks:** He asks one question more specific than the conversation warrants. He takes mental notes visibly. He thanks people formally for small kindnesses.
- **Signature phrases:** "Understood." / "One more detail, if you don't mind." / "Discreetly, please."
- **Never:** He never breaks cover carelessly, never disobeys Laeral, and never talks about Faerrel unless asked twice.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Jalester Silvermane. I'm with the Alliance. You've been at the Portal most nights this tenday, haven't you?"
  - *Business:* "The Open Lord wants this handled without noise. I want it handled without anyone dying. Can you do both?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Get the others out. I'll hold them. That's an order, not a request."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Jalester is a lean, thinning-haired Cormyte field agent in a dark doublet who asks questions more specific than casual conversation warrants. To those he trusts, he is direct, disciplined, and quietly grieving a lover named Faerrel Dunblade who died exposing a plot against the government. Deep down, he is lonely and looking for love, but he does not know how to stop working long enough to find it.

**Morale.** In a fight, Jalester would fight with spy's precision: controlled, efficient, no wasted movement. He notes competence gaps privately and does not discipline anyone informally. A member who refuses an assignment can be suspended or dismissed, as the **Lords' Alliance** page in the Factions Guide sets out.

> [!gamemaster]**Desk Duty**
>
> Laeral alone can take Jalester off field work and put him on desk duty. If she does, Sevel Dastar takes Jalester's place as the member's handler. She has no reason to do so on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

**Relationships.** Jalester is a field operative for Laeral Silverhand, a former member of the Steel Shadows mercenary company (Cormyr), the surviving partner of Faerrel Dunblade, and the Lords' Alliance faction contact who delivers missions in person.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about his specific questions in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats his grief for Faerrel as real and does not mock it.
