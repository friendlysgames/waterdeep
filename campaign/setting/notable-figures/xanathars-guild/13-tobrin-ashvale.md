**Tobrin Ashvale** (Neutral evil, Chondathan human, he/him)

## Background

Tobrin Ashvale is a Chondathan human who has tended bar before and can do the job well `[research]`. He gives references from three Castle Ward establishments, all of which will confirm that he worked there, and one of them has been under Xanathar's Guild protection for two years `[research]`. For the Guild he is a low-level watcher. The Guild places watchers like him in North Ward establishments when those establishments become points of interest, and he reports on who visits and what is said within earshot of the bar through a contact in the Dock Ward `[research]`. He has no combat role `[research]`.

Tobrin grew up in the back rooms of a Castle Ward inn, one of five children of a Chondathan potman and a laundress who both worked there `[design]`. Guests and staff forgot to count him, and by the age of ten he had learned that a boy carrying a tray is never asked what he heard `[design]`. At fourteen he repeated a conversation between two grain factors to a third man for a silver, and the third man paid him and never asked his name `[design]`. Tobrin kept the habit, and he has taken care ever since to keep his face easy to lose `[design]`.

At sixteen he went to work behind the bar of a widow's inn a few streets from the one he grew up in, and she taught him the trade `[design]`. She wanted every regular greeted by name with the usual already poured, and she told him that a bar kept well is something a person can be proud of `[design]`. He still holds that view, and it is the one part of his work that he does not fake `[design]`. The widow's inn is the first of his three Castle Ward references, and it is the one that now lies under the Guild's protection `[design]`.

Two years ago the widow's house fell behind on a loan, and collectors for Xanathar's Guild came with an offer to take the debt over and protect the inn in return for a share of its takings `[design]`. While they were there the collectors asked her which of her former hands could be trusted to keep his mouth shut, and she named Tobrin, without learning what came of it `[design]`. His entry test was the usual serious crime `[research]`. The Guild told him to leave the rear door of a Castle Ward wine merchant's cellar unbarred on a night when he was passing, and a crew emptied the cellar and beat the watchman who came down to look `[design]`. Tobrin took nothing and was not seen `[design]`. The Guild paid off the widow's loan as part of the arrangement, and that sum is the debt it holds over him, though it has never given him a figure for what remains and he has stopped asking `[design]`.

Since then he has worked relief shifts behind bars in the Castle Ward and the North Ward, listening and reporting through the Dock Ward contact `[design]`. He rents a narrow room above a chandler's shop, and every night he sits with his back to the wall for an hour and says nothing `[design]`. That hour is the flat calm that he allows himself only when alone `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR he is working a short engagement behind a North Ward bar and waiting for the Guild to place him in an establishment that has become a point of interest `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Tobrin wants the widow's inn out from under the Guild `[design]`. He sets aside a few coins from every wage toward the loan that the Guild paid on her behalf, though he cannot tell whether the Guild counts them, and he would like to see her paint a new sign without the chalk mark beside the door `[design]`. He also wants one barkeep in the city to learn his name without being told `[design]`. On a free evening each tenday he drinks at a Trades Ward alehouse where he has never given it, and the old barman there still calls him "friend" `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who know him, Tobrin Ashvale is the bartender whom nobody can describe afterward. He is of medium height and build, with brown hair cut plainly and clean clothes that stand out in no way `[research]`. He smiles when a smile is due and nods when a nod is due, and he glances around a taproom with the casual interest of someone memorizing it `[research]`.

He remembers names, usual orders and remarks from two visits ago, and he feeds them back to patrons as small talk. He wipes the same spot on the bar while he listens, and he takes quiet satisfaction in a bar kept well. Patrons who volunteer everything earn his mild contempt, and he lets none of it reach his voice.

## Overview

Tobrin Ashvale is a Chondathan human bartender who takes short engagements at taverns in the North Ward. He is easy to forget, with a medium build, plain brown hair and clean clothes. He remembers names, usual orders and remarks from two visits ago, and he keeps his own business to himself.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Tobrin should inspire comfort with his attentiveness (he remembers names, usuals, and passing remarks from two visits ago) and retroactive unease for anyone who learns that none of that warmth is accidental.

**Emotions.** Tobrin most often feels practiced ease, attentive warmth (professional and deployed), patient calculation beneath a pleasant surface, mild contempt for patrons who volunteer everything, quiet satisfaction in a bar kept well, and the flat calm he allows himself only when alone.

**Motivations.** Tobrin wants to maintain a running intelligence file on the patrons, visitors, and overheard conversations of whatever room he is placed in; keep the bar warm enough that no one suspects the bartender; and report through his Dock Ward contact on anyone who appears to hold or move a Golorr artifact.

**Inspirations.** When playing Tobrin, channel Sam Malone (*Cheers*), Varys (*Game of Thrones*), and Tom Ripley (*The Talented Mr. Ripley*).

### Voice

*A warm, attentive bartender who remembers everything you've said, because he's writing it down.*

- **Sound:** A friendly, easy, sociable voice, a born barman's.
- **Sentence shape:** Chatty, warm sentences that feed patrons' words back to them, and gentle questions.
- **Word choice:** Bar talk: usuals, rounds, "on the house". He remembers details from two visits ago and uses them. He calls regulars by name.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Friendly barroom swearing, matched to the patron.
- **Personality in speech:** Practised, deployed warmth. His calculation shows only in how precisely he remembers. Contempt for patrons who overshare never reaches his voice.
- **Quirks:** He greets people with their usual and a detail ("How's the sister's wedding?"). He wipes the same spot on the bar when listening hard.
- **Signature phrases:** "The usual?" / "How'd that thing go, with the…?" / "This one's on the house."
- **Never:** He never shows the flat calm in public, never asks directly about a patron's plans, and never forgets a name.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Back again! Dark ale, and how did it go with the moneylender? Last time you were worried."
  - *Business:* "Busy night. Who was the fellow in grey you were talking to? Looked like a serious sort."
  - *Under pressure:* "Whoa, easy, easy. Nobody needs trouble. First round's on the house."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Tobrin is a warm, attentive bartender who makes everyone feel like a regular. To those he trusts (meaning no one), he runs a rolling intelligence file on every patron in the room he works: who meets whom, who seems nervous, who a patron of interest has been talking to. Deep down, he is one of the watchers the Guild keeps behind bars and in kitchens, the sort with better social access than a cook, and he is waiting to see how long it takes anyone to notice any of them.

**Morale.** In a fight, Tobrin does not fight. He disappears into the crowd and reports at the first opportunity. If someone confronts him with the Guild, he negotiates: what he knows about the Guild's operations in the room he works is worth more to whoever holds him than his Guild debt, and he understands the math.

**Relationships.** Tobrin is an operative of Xanathar's Guild who tends bar as cover, one of the watchers the Guild keeps behind bars and in kitchens alongside Grevik Nass, whose kitchen window gives the Guild a second angle on the same kind of room.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, steady regard for the bartending in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Tobrin's warmth as a real skill and does not play it as a sneer.
