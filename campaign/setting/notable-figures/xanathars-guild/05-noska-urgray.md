**Noska Ur'gray** (Neutral evil, shield dwarf, he/him)

## Background

Noska Ur'gray is a shield dwarf, and the wiki records him as an enforcer of Xanathar's Guild in the early 1490s DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Noska Ur'gray*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. A green slime in Undermountain dissolved his left hand and forearm, and a heavy crossbow now attaches to the stump in their place (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Guild keeps watch posts on the upper levels of Undermountain, staffed by bugbears and goblins, so he may have lost the hand on one of those postings `[research]`.

Noska grew up in a small shield dwarf hold in the Sword Mountains, in a family of stonecutters `[design]`. His father cut the hold's grave markers, and Noska learned the chisel from him and carved names in Dwarvish runes onto slabs through his boyhood and young manhood `[design]`. In his middle years he killed another mason in a quarrel over the pay for a marker, and the elders put him out of the hold's gates `[design]`. His father did not speak for him at the hearing, and the two never met again `[design]`.

He made his way to Waterdeep and down to Skullport, where he hired out as a quarry guard on the Skullport road before the Guild took him on `[design]`. Every applicant to Xanathar's Guild must pass an entry test that involves a serious crime, so Noska must have passed it before the Guild gave him a post (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations) `[research]`. No source says what the crime was or when he joined. The Guild put him on a watch post on the first level of Undermountain, with a crew of bugbears `[design]`. One night a green slime dropped from the ceiling of the passage onto his left arm as he raised the lantern, and the bugbear beside him dragged him clear and burned the stump with a torch before the slime could spread `[design]`. That arm had held the chisel, and Noska has not carved a letter since `[design]`. The slime also took his grip on ladders and ropes, which is why he climbs poorly now `[design]`. A heavy crossbow was fitted to the stump after it healed `[design]`.

He is one of Xanathar's most trusted underlings, and by 1492 DR he is the Guild's chief enforcer (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Ahmaergo presides over the gladiator tournaments in the lair's arena, the Pit of Blood and Fortune, and Noska releases the combatants from the cell block. He carries the keys to its four cells (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Ahmaergo writes most of the orders that bear Xanathar's name (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations), and Noska has never sent one back with a question `[design]`.

When he is not in the arena he lives in a magically lit room of the lair. A marble bathtub with clawed feet serves as his bed, padded with straw and mangy furs, and a wooden cage beside it holds the rust monster that he keeps as a pet. He feeds the rust monster from piles of discarded helms, shields and armaments along the walls, and he uses three straw mannequins for target practice with his crossbow. A chest under a pile of rusty weapons holds his hoard of coin and four bloodstones (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

The rust monster came to him at the same post a season after the slime, a half-starved young one nosing at the rusted hinges of the gate `[design]`. The bugbears wanted to kill it, and Noska fed it his own spoiled helm and told them that it stayed `[design]`. It was the first creature that followed him after the injury and wanted nothing from him but food, and he has kept it fed since `[design]`. He spends nothing on himself, and the chest under the rusty weapons holds mostly copper, kept in the coin that it was paid in `[design]`. A mason in his hold was paid in copper by the clan, and a chest of it was what let a family bury its dead with a proper marker `[design]`. He sets aside any bloodstone that comes to him, because a dwarf mason inlays a green stone into the first rune of a name that has to last `[design]`. The coin and the four stones are for one headstone, and he has told nobody that `[design]`.

Goblins and bugbears work for the Guild as muscle. Noska leads the third of its response teams, six bugbears and a gazer, and Xanathar sends him when it is done sending messages and wants the problem gone. The first two teams, led by Gorath and Korgstrod Uxgulm, are sent before his (**Xanathar's Guild**, Factions Guide). The bugbear who burned his stump is one of the six, and the team follows Noska because he held the post with one arm until relief came `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Noska wants to carve his father's name on a headstone with his own hand, and he has not yet found a way to fit a chisel to the stump `[design]`. His father died in the hold while Noska was in Skullport, and a trader brought the news a year late `[design]`. He wants to set the slab over the grave with the four bloodstones in the first rune, which means walking through the hold's gate once and asking the elders to allow it `[design]`. He expects them to refuse, and he has not decided what he will do if they do `[design]`. He also wants to go back to the passage with a cask of lamp oil and burn the green slime out of it, and the Guild's orders have never sent him that way `[design]`. He says none of this to anyone, and in conversation he talks only about the Guild's business `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who know of him, Noska is the dwarf with a heavy crossbow where his left forearm should be. He is direct and uncomplicated, and he does what Xanathar tells him to do without questions. He has little patience for weakness, and he does not negotiate.

Away from a fight he is fond of his rust monster, and he passes the time shooting bolts into straw mannequins. He is content to be what he looks like, a large and blunt dwarf who solves problems with violence. He guards the chest in his quarters more closely than anything else that he owns, and he has never said why `[design]`.

## Connections

- **Xanathar:** The beholder that rules the Guild, and Noska's master. Noska is sent out when Xanathar is done sending messages, and he follows its orders without argument.
- **Ahmaergo:** The Guild's shield dwarf majordomo. Ahmaergo presides over the arena tournaments while Noska releases the combatants, and they are the two underlings Xanathar trusts most.
- **Noska's team:** Six bugbears and a gazer, the third response team of the Guild. They answer to him when Xanathar sends him out, and one of the bugbears is the one who burned his stump after the slime `[design]`.
- **His father:** A stonecutter in the shield dwarf hold where Noska grew up, now dead. Noska means to carve his name on a headstone `[design]`. The hold and the father are unnamed.
- **Gorath** and **Korgstrod Uxgulm:** The leaders of the first and second response teams. The Guild sends them before it sends Noska.
- **The rust monster:** Noska's pet, kept in a cage in his quarters and fed on discarded armor and weapons.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** Noska is its chief enforcer. Nobody in the Guild is recorded as his enemy.

## Overview

Noska Ur'gray is a dwarf enforcer of Xanathar's Guild, recognizable by the heavy crossbow that replaces his left forearm. He is direct and uncomplicated, and the Guild sends him when a problem needs solving by force.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Noska should inspire intimidation with his crossbow-arm and brutal reputation, dark amusement with his rust monster pet and mannequin target practice, and reluctant respect for the straightforward violence that makes him exactly what he advertises.

**Emotions.** Noska most often feels aggressive boredom, brute satisfaction in violence, simple loyalty to Xanathar, territorial possessiveness, flat disdain for weakness, and occasional contentment when feeding discarded weapons to his rust monster.

**Motivations.** Noska wants to hurt people Xanathar tells him to hurt, maintain his position as the Guild's heaviest response option, and feed his rust monster.

**Inspirations.** When playing Noska, channel Drax (*Guardians of the Galaxy*), Mountain (*Game of Thrones*), and Bane (*The Dark Knight Rises*).

### Voice

*A crossbow-armed dwarf enforcer whose inner life, and vocabulary, are exactly as simple as they look.*

- **Sound:** A gravelly, flat, bored dwarven grunt.
- **Sentence shape:** Very short sentences and threats, deliberately (a break from `ember-voice` speech). He states the violence plainly, as a fact.
- **Word choice:** Blunt violence and simple needs: "hurt", "shoot", "food for Rusty". He calls people "target".
- **Swearing:** *Punctuation · Plain · Clipped.* "Fuck" and "shit" in every other sentence, with no craft whatsoever.
- **Personality in speech:** Aggressive boredom and simple loyalty. He enjoys violence and says so. He's fond of his rust monster in a blunt, affectionate way.
- **Quirks:** He cranks his crossbow arm while talking. He offers people's weapons to his rust monster. He takes insults as instructions.
- **Signature phrases:** "Boss says." / "Rusty's hungry." / "Target."
- **Never:** He never negotiates, never thinks twice, and never disobeys Xanathar.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You. Target? Boss says maybe."
  - *Business:* "Boss says hurt you. I hurt you. Simple."
  - *Under pressure:* "Shit. Fuck. Rusty, eat his sword."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Noska is a ruthless enforcer with a heavy crossbow replacing his left forearm (lost to a green slime in Undermountain). To those in the Guild, he is sent when Xanathar is done sending messages and wants the problem gone. Deep down, he keeps a rust monster as a pet and uses mannequins for target practice. He sleeps in a converted bathtub padded with straw and furs. His inner life is exactly as simple as it appears.

**Morale.** In a fight, Noska fires his crossbow-arm from range and closes to melee without hesitation. He has disadvantage on climbing due to his missing hand but compensates by never needing to be anywhere subtle.

**Relationships.** Noska is an enforcer of Xanathar's Guild, the third response team leader, and a shield dwarf whose crossbow-arm, rust monster pet, and bathtub bed constitute a complete portrait of his priorities.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a blunt, matter-of-fact manner in Personality that matches Noska's own. The narrator does not play him for a joke and does not dress up his violence.
