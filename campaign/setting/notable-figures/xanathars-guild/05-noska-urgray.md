# Noska Ur'gray

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Shield dwarf enforcer, neutral evil*. Stat block: **Thug (with modifications)**.
> - **Affiliation:** Xanathar's Guild
> - **Featured in:** **Xanathar's Lair**, **Lords' Alliance Mission 5 — Fentree's Ledger**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Noska should inspire intimidation with his crossbow-arm and brutal reputation, dark amusement with his rust monster pet and mannequin target practice, and reluctant respect for the straightforward violence that makes him exactly what he advertises.

**Emotions.** Noska most often feels aggressive boredom, brute satisfaction in violence, simple loyalty to Xanathar, territorial possessiveness, flat disdain for weakness, and occasional contentment when feeding discarded weapons to his rust monster.

**Motivations.** Noska wants to hurt people Xanathar tells him to hurt, maintain his position as the Guild's heaviest response option, and feed his rust monster.

**Inspirations.** When playing Noska, channel Drax (*Guardians of the Galaxy*), Mountain (*Game of Thrones*), and Bane (*The Dark Knight Rises*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Noska is a ruthless enforcer with a heavy crossbow replacing his left forearm (lost to a green slime in Undermountain). To those in the Guild, he is sent when Xanathar is done sending messages and wants the problem gone. Deep down, he keeps a rust monster as a pet and uses mannequins for target practice. He sleeps in a converted bathtub padded with straw and furs. His inner life is exactly as simple as it appears.

**Morale.** In a fight, Noska fires his crossbow-arm from range and closes to melee without hesitation. He has disadvantage on climbing due to his missing hand but compensates by never needing to be anywhere subtle.

**Relationships.** Noska is a Xanathar Guild enforcer, the Tier 3 response team leader, and a shield dwarf whose crossbow-arm, rust monster pet, and bathtub bed constitute a complete portrait of his priorities.

## Overview

Noska Ur'gray is a Xanathar Guild enforcer, recognizable by the heavy crossbow that replaces his left forearm. He is direct, uncomplicated, and reliably present whenever the Guild needs a problem solved by violence.
