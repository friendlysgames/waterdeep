**Grevik Nass** (Neutral evil, half-orc, he/him)

## Background

Grevik Nass is large even for a half-orc, and he has the careful, deliberate movements of a man who has learned that his size makes people nervous and who has decided to compensate by moving slowly `[research]`. He says that he has cooked professionally for eight years, and he gives references from two Dock Ward kitchens and one business in the Field Ward `[research]`. The Field Ward reference is a front for Xanathar's Guild `[research]`. For the Guild he is an information broker who uses cooking as stable cover while he maintains his network, and he files his reports through a kitchen supplier who collects them with the weekly delivery `[research]`. The Guild chose him partly because he cooks well, since a mediocre cook in a plant's place draws attention `[research]`. If anyone ever confronts him with the Guild, he has a prepared answer: that he has contacts there as a survival arrangement and no active employment, which is a lie `[research]`.

Grevik grew up on the Dock Ward waterfront, the son of an Illuskan human woman who fed stevedores from a cook stall and of an orc father who left before Grevik could remember him `[design]`. He stood on a crate at her stove from the age of six and learned to judge a braise by its smell `[design]`. By twelve he was taller than the men who ate at the stall, and customers began to cross the street when they saw him coming, so he taught himself to walk slowly, to keep his hands in view and to speak in a low voice `[design]`. He still moves that way in every kitchen he works `[design]`.

The stall carried a debt to a lender who was owed money by half the waterfront, and the lender's collector grew steadier in his visits as the interest piled up `[design]`. Grevik by then worked the line in a Dock Ward kitchen, and the collector told him that the debt would be forgiven if he did a favor for the people the lender answered to `[design]`. Those people were Xanathar's Guild, and the favor was the entry test, which always involves a serious crime `[research]`. The Guild told him to burn the storeroom of a rival cookhouse down the street, on a night when it would be empty `[design]`. The cookhouse had an apprentice, a boy of about ten, who slept in the loft above the storeroom, and Grevik did not know it until the fire was out `[design]`. The debt was cleared within the month, and his mother believed that a good season of trade had paid it `[design]`.

After the test the Guild found a use for a large, calm cook who could be sent into any kitchen without causing comment `[design]`. He listened first in Dock Ward kitchens, where he learned which captains and factors ate together and which of them lowered their voices when the plates came out `[design]`. His mother died two winters ago of a lung fever in a room he paid for, and he could not leave service that night, so he cooked a double shift while she was dying `[design]`. He never learned the last step of her sauce, and the braised pork that he is now known for is his own rebuilding of it from memory `[design]`. He has handled fire with great care since the night of the storeroom, and a scorched pot is the one thing that breaks his calm `[design]`. On Ches 1, 1492 DR he is cooking in a Dock Ward kitchen and filing reports for the Guild `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Grevik wants to cook his mother's braise exactly as she made it, and he tries one change to his version every tenday, on the principle that the missing step will turn up by elimination `[design]`. He also wants to settle a debt that the Guild never asked him to pay, and every tenday he leaves a purse in the alms box of a Dock Ward shrine for the dead boy's mother, who does not know where it comes from and whom he has never met `[design]`. He has not said the boy's name aloud since the fire, and he would like to be able to `[design]`. His third want is a small cookhouse of his own on a road outside the city, where he chooses the menu and no supplier collects anything with the weekly delivery, and he keeps coin toward it in a sealed jar that the Guild does not know about `[design]`.

## Personality

Grevik Nass is a big half-orc who works the pass of a busy kitchen with a steady, unhurried manner. He moves slowly, speaks in a low voice and answers personal questions by handing over a plate. He calls patrons by their tables, asks over the pass who is joining whom for supper, and watches the dining room from the kitchen window while he plates.

His food is good enough that nobody wonders about the cook, and he takes real pride in a dish that lands exactly right. He swears at a burnt stock behind the pass and never in the dining room, and he takes more care than the work needs with the plates that go to tables he is watching.

## Connections

- **Xanathar's Guild:** Grevik is one of its information brokers and uses cooking as his cover. He files what he learns through a kitchen supplier, who collects his reports with the weekly delivery and is not a Vintners' Guild contact.
- **The kitchen supplier:** The channel that carries his reports. Grevik never speaks of the arrangement.
- **Tobrin Ashvale:** Another Guild operative who does the same work from behind a bar. The Guild uses cooks and bartenders as watchers in rooms where people talk, and Grevik's kitchen window and Tobrin's bar give it two angles on rooms of the same kind.
- **His mother:** The human cook who raised him at a Dock Ward stall and died two winters ago. Grevik is still rebuilding her braise from memory `[design]`.
- **The dead apprentice's mother:** A woman Grevik has never met, who receives a purse from the shrine's alms box each tenday and does not know the sender `[design]`.
- **Sloppy cooks and managers:** Grevik holds them in quiet contempt, and he works around them rather than correct them.

## Overview

Grevik Nass is a half-orc cook whose food is worth recommending and who keeps entirely to himself behind the pass. He is attentive and professional, and lets the work do the talking. He is large for a half-orc, and he moves slowly and speaks in a low voice.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Grevik should inspire immediate comfort with his competence and no-fuss manner, and retroactive unease for anyone who notices that the kitchen window takes in every table.

**Emotions.** Grevik most often feels professional pride in clean work, patient attention to patron patterns, quiet contempt for sloppiness, calm deliberateness, and genuine satisfaction when a dish lands exactly right. Underneath all of it sits background guilt he does not examine.

**Motivations.** Grevik wants to compile an accurate patron intelligence file for Xanathar's Guild (who dines here, with whom, and about what), make the food worth recommending so no one looks closely at the cook, and complete the assignment without drawing a replacement.

**Inspirations.** When playing Grevik, channel Gustavo Fring (*Breaking Bad*), Thomas Barrow (*Downton Abbey*), and The Operative (*Serenity*).

### Voice

*A quiet, competent half-orc cook who lets the food talk and misses nothing at the tables.*

- **Sound:** A deep, calm, sparing voice, polite and professional.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, practical kitchen sentences. He doesn't make small talk and deflects personal questions with food.
- **Word choice:** Kitchen talk: pass, service, "table four", dishes by name. He calls patrons by their tables.
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Kitchen swearing, behind the pass, never in the dining room ("Shit, the stock's burnt.").
- **Personality in speech:** Professional pride and attention. His interest in patron patterns shows as casual questions about who's joining whom for supper. Guilt he doesn't examine surfaces as extra care with the food that goes to the tables he is watching.
- **Quirks:** He calls orders by table number. He watches the room from the kitchen window while he talks.
- **Signature phrases:** "Table four wants the lamb." / "Food's up." / "Joining anyone tonight?"
- **Never:** He never talks about the Guild, never serves bad food, and never lingers in the dining room.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Evening. The fish is good tonight."
  - *Business:* "Big party tomorrow? I'll need to know numbers. Who's coming?"
  - *Under pressure:* "I just cook. Let me finish service."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Grevik is a half-orc cook who lets the food do the talking: attentive to the pass, quiet at the bar, no trouble to manage. To those who work alongside him, he notes which tables order twice, which guests lower their voices, and which visitors he sees again without a reservation. Deep down, he has filed more detailed intelligence from this kitchen than the Guild expected, and the thought that the tally might make him harder to release than to retire has started to surface at quiet moments.

**Morale.** In a fight, Grevik yields position, retreats through the kitchen, and gets a runner to the Guild within the hour. He is an intelligence operative and fights nothing he can avoid.

**Relationships.** Grevik is an operative of Xanathar's Guild who works as a cook for cover and files his reports through a kitchen supplier, and his patron file gives Xanathar a usable picture of the rooms he works. He is one of the watchers the Guild keeps in kitchens and behind bars, alongside Tobrin Ashvale, whose position behind a bar gives the Guild a second angle on the same kind of room.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, steady regard for the cooking in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Grevik's skill and his guilt as real and plays neither for a joke.
