**Keln Trigos** (Neutral evil, tiefling, he/him)

## Background

Keln Trigos is a tiefling priest who trained as a healer before he came to Xanathar's Guild `[research]`. He grew up in a Dock Ward tenement with his mother, a laundress who washed linen for Dock Ward inns, and the family never discussed the fiendish ancestor whose blood had come out in him `[design]`. Neighbors who kept their children away from him still sent for him when someone had a fever, because he sat with the sick and did not fidget `[design]`.

When he was about twelve, a priest who ran a charity hospice at the edge of the ward took him in to carry water and fold linen, and over the following years taught him to wash a wound, set a bone and pray over the patient afterward `[design]`. The priest called every patient friend, because the hospice took in anyone who came and never asked a name, and Keln kept both the word and the habit of washing his hands between patients `[design]`. Keln serves a god whom he does not name to his patients or to the Guild `[design]`. He believes that the god lends him the spells and expects the work to be done properly in return, and he does not believe that any god weighs which of the sick deserve to mend, so he has never found it hard to treat a Guild enforcer `[design]`. The priest died when Keln was a young man and left him an old herbal that the hospice had copied and recopied by hand for three generations `[design]`.

Keln rented a room near the docks and opened a clinic of his own `[design]`. A young dockhand with a knife wound told him the name of the man who had stabbed him while Keln stitched, and the dockhand died on Keln's table two nights later `[design]`. Keln swore under his breath at the bedside, as he has at every death since, and when the dead man's sister came and asked who had done it, he told her `[design]`. She found the man within the week, and the Watch questioned Keln for most of a month about what he had said to her and to whom `[design]`. His patients stayed away while the questions lasted, and since then he has not asked anyone how an injury happened `[design]`. He had always washed his hands between patients, and after those weeks he began washing them while he talks as well, which nobody has asked him about `[design]`.

With his patients gone and the rent owed, Keln took in a wounded man whom a Guild agent carried to his door one night, and he mended him without a question `[design]`. The agent came back with others who paid in coin and asked for nothing else `[design]`. Like every applicant he passed the Guild's membership test, which always involves a serious crime (**Xanathar's Guild**) `[research]`. The Guild gave him a test that suited a healer, and it brought him a member who had failed Xanathar with orders to treat the man slowly `[design]`. The man died before morning, and Keln has understood since then what the Guild does with people it stops using, because he was the one it sent for `[design]`. The Guild set him in the Terasse Estate because it needed sick rooms and a man who would not talk about them `[design]`.

Xanathar secretly owns the Terasse Estate, a modest rowhouse on Elsambul's Lane in the Castle Ward, and Keln works there. He lives there too `[research]`. He runs a healing operation on the upper floors, where the sick rooms hold Guild agents who have come to him to mend (**Xanathar's Guild**; **Castle Ward**). The clinic also treats anyone who knows the healing passphrase and asks no questions about how an injury happened (**Castle Ward**).

The part of the work he likes best is the minute after a bad wound closes, when a patient who arrived braced for pain lets go of the bedframe and begins to breathe slowly `[design]`. He keeps the sick rooms scrubbed and the linen white, and he sorts the larder and the healing supplies himself each morning before the first knock `[design]`. He keeps the hospice's herbal close at hand and checks every salve against it `[design]`. He also likes that nobody who climbs his stairs expects anything of him beyond a mended arm `[design]`.

The cellar is one of the city's gathering points for Xanathar's gladiatorial games, and Keln manages the audience that gathers there. His staff feed the guests, blindfold them and take them down a trapdoor beneath the kitchen rug to an underground dock, from which a boat carries them toward Skullport (**Xanathar's Guild**). Two guards and three Guild agents work under his roof.

Orders about the games reach him from **Ahmaergo**, who handles the Guild's gladiatorial procurement, and Keln regularly burns his correspondence after he has read it. On Ches 1, 1492 DR both services run smoothly, and neither has drawn the Watch's notice `[research]`.

**Personal goals.** Keln wants his mother to end her days in a bed with clean sheets and a window, and he wants her never to learn what the Terasse Estate is `[design]`. She has a cough from her years over the wash-tubs that his spells ease and cannot cure, and he visits her one evening a tenday and tells her that he tends the sick servants of a rich man `[design]`. He also wants to finish copying the hospice's herbal onto clean pages and have it bound, because the old pages have begun to flake and some of its remedies exist nowhere else `[design]`. He has copied about half of it by lamplight and has not found a binder he trusts not to ask where the money came from `[design]`. Last, he would like to go a full year without a patient dying on his table, a stretch he has never managed `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who meet him, Keln Trigos is a calm healer who treats an injury and does not ask how it happened. He speaks softly, calls everyone friend and washes his hands while he talks. His care for the patients he treats is real, and he loses interest in them once they leave, answering any question about the estate's other business with a polite change of subject. He avoids the attention of anyone with authority, and the Guild finds him reliable for that reason.

## Connections

- **Xanathar:** The beholder that rules Xanathar's Guild and secretly owns the Terasse Estate. Keln keeps the estate quiet for it `[research]`, and he understands what happens to people the Guild stops using.
- **Ahmaergo:** The Guild's shield dwarf majordomo, who handles gladiatorial procurement and writes most orders that bear Xanathar's name. His instructions about the gladiatorial audience reach Keln in letters.
- **Grinda Garloth:** An independent black market armorer who contracts to the Guild. She supplies the exotic weapons that go to the Guild's gladiatorial combats (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations and Factions Guide).
- **His mother:** A retired laundress who washed linen for Dock Ward inns and now lives in a Dock Ward tenement. She believes her son tends the sick servants of a rich man. He visits her one evening a tenday `[design]`.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** Keln is one of its contacts in the city, with two guards and three Guild agents on his staff. No source places him on the Guild's published ladder of ranks `[research]`.

## Overview

Keln Trigos is a tiefling priest who runs a quiet healing clinic in a Castle Ward rowhouse on Elsambul's Lane. The clinic is known for asking no questions about injuries, and Keln treats every patient with the same calm courtesy.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Keln should inspire surface comfort with his healer's professional warmth; growing unease as the clinic's real purpose becomes clear; and cold realization that the blindfolded guests never ask where they are going.

**Emotions.** Keln most often feels efficient professional pride, the specific calm of someone who has decided knowing less is safer, practical care for the patients he actually heals, and clinical indifference to what happens after they leave his care.

**Motivations.** Keln wants to run the Terasse Estate operation smoothly, provide Xanathar with a reliable healing service and gathering point, and avoid becoming interesting to anyone with authority.

**Inspirations.** When playing Keln, channel Nucky Thompson (*Boardwalk Empire*), Dr. Jacoby (*Twin Peaks*, the professional with a sideline), and the coroner in any crime procedural who is more helpful than he ought to be.

### Voice

*A warm, professional healer who asks no questions and has made very sure he never learns the answers.*

- **Sound:** A soft, reassuring bedside voice, gentle and unhurried.
- **Sentence shape:** Calm, kind, clinical sentences, instructions and reassurance. He answers questions about the "other service" with polite non-answers.
- **Word choice:** Medical and discreet: "patient", "treatment", "no need to tell me how it happened". He calls everyone "friend".
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Once, under his breath, when a patient dies on his table.
- **Personality in speech:** Professional pride and deliberate ignorance. His care for patients is real. Indifference to what happens after they leave shows as a change of subject.
- **Quirks:** He says "No need to tell me" before anyone explains anything. He washes his hands while talking.
- **Signature phrases:** "No need to tell me." / "You'll mend." / "That's not my department."
- **Never:** He never asks how anyone was hurt, never mentions the blindfolded guests, and never becomes interesting to the Watch.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Come in, sit down. No need to tell me how it happened. Let's have a look."
  - *Business:* "Healing is fifty gold. The other matter isn't my department; you'll want the back door."
  - *Under pressure:* "I just heal people. Whatever this is, it's not my department."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Keln Trigos runs a quiet healing operation in a Castle Ward rowhouse and asks no questions about how you got hurt. To those who know the passphrases, the building runs two entirely separate services that have never been officially linked, and both function without incident. Deep down, he has worked for Xanathar long enough to understand what happens to people the Guild stops using, and he is therefore very reliable.

**Morale.** In a fight, Keln uses priestly spells to hold ground and calls his guards. He does not run — the building is his responsibility.

**Relationships.** Keln is the keeper of the Terasse Estate, a Xanathar's Guild rowhouse on Elsambul's Lane in the Castle Ward, the manager of the estate's gladiatorial gathering point (healing services and Lair access under the same roof), and the keeper of two passphrases that access very different services.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> The Terasse Estate belongs to Xanathar, and Keln answers to the Guild. The clinic is the front for the cellar's other business, where guests of the gladiatorial games are blindfolded and taken by boat to caverns near Skullport and on toward the Lair.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, gentle humor about how little Keln wants to know in Personality. The narrator treats his professional care as real and his willful ignorance as routine.
