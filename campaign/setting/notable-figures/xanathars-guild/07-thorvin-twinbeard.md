**Thorvin Twinbeard** (Neutral good, dwarf, he/him)

## Background

Thorvin Twinbeard is a shield dwarf who serves Xanathar as its chief engineer and trapsmith (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He carries mason's tools, smith's tools and thieves' tools and is proficient with all three, and he wears iron-rimmed spectacles and carries a large wrench that doubles as a club (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). His family lives in the Trades Ward (**Xanathar's Lair** structure notes).

The **Xanathar's Lair** structure notes say that he has maintained the lair's mechanical infrastructure for about nine years, which puts the start of his work near 1483 DR (derived) `[research]`. No file says how the Guild found him or where he trained. One wiki account says that Xanathar appointed him, together with Nihiloor and Noska Ur'gray, to an important position (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, citing *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

His main project is a pulverizer, which he is building in his workshop. It is a tall stone bin with grinding gears at the bottom and a chute where the powder pours out, and Xanathar means to use it on creatures that it petrifies, grinding them into a fine powder that can be made into plaster (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Thorvin does not mean to finish it. The project gives him a reason to stay in the lair and gather information, and he sells that information to the Harpers (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

He is a paid Harper informant and has been one for three years. He reports on Xanathar's plans as well as he can, and he uses maintenance inspections to cover secret meetings with Harper spies in Skullport and elsewhere (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Undermountain and Skullport**). He keeps the coin that the Harpers pay him in a bank far from the beholder's prying eyes, and Mirt knows his secret (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). No file names the Harper who recruited him or says why he agreed.

He is one of two people who know about the secret room that Nar'l Xibrindas found in the lair, and he has not told Xanathar about it. He also helped Nar'l identify the structurally unstable parts of the lair (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Nar'l Xibrindas**).

By Ches 1, 1492 DR Xanathar has begun to question his loyalty. It dreamed a gazer into being and set it to watch him, and Thorvin knows that the gazer is there. The gazer is itself disloyal (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Thorvin is at work in his workshop, where a cot stands beside a stack of casks of Wyrmwizz Ale, a Skullport brew (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

## Personality

To the Guild and to anyone who asks about his work, Thorvin is an irritable dwarf who answers every question with a short refusal. He spends the day in his workshop with the pulverizer, and he grumbles about the Guild's demands like a man who has stopped expecting anything to change.

Under the irritation he takes real pride in good engineering, and the pride slips out even when the machine is a terrible one. He lowers his voice and changes the subject when the gazer drifts close, and he is careful never to say anything useful while it listens. Those who earn his trust find that he knows the lair well, apart from Ahmaergo's chambers, and that he is tired of building terrible things for his master.

## Overview

Thorvin Twinbeard is the chief engineer and trapsmith of Xanathar's Guild, an irritable dwarf employed to maintain its mechanical constructs and devices. He takes no questions about his methods or his employers. He wears iron-rimmed spectacles and carries a heavy wrench that doubles as a club.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Thorvin should inspire initial wariness with his mechanical work for Xanathar; growing sympathy when his reluctance becomes visible; and genuine trust, for anyone who learns it, that he is the Harpers' inside man.

**Emotions.** Thorvin most often feels the specific stress of doing terrible work because the alternative is worse, genuine engineering pride he wishes he could direct at anything else, cautious hope when someone useful appears, and relief at finally having someone to tell what he knows about the smokepowder.

**Motivations.** Thorvin wants to survive long enough for someone to take down the Lair, pass intelligence through Harper channels, and never finish the pulverizer if he can help it.

**Inspirations.** When playing Thorvin, channel Geppetto (*Pinocchio*), Scotty (*Star Trek*), and Hank Schrader (*Breaking Bad*, the reluctant insider).

### Voice

*An irritable dwarf engineer who hates his work and has kept a Harper secret for three years.*

- **Sound:** A gruff, grumbling dwarven voice that drops to a hurried whisper around the gazer.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, irritable brush-offs in public. In private, rushed technical talk, precise and low.
- **Word choice:** Engineering: tolerances, pressure, "load", "smokepowder". Harper code words when safe. He calls his creations "the damned things".
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Pearls · Phrase.* Mostly dwarven grumbling, then a gem: "I've built things for gods-cursed madmen before, but never one who'd wear the result as a hat."
- **Personality in speech:** Stressed and reluctant. His engineering pride sneaks through even about terrible machines. Fear of the gazer makes him talk fast.
- **Quirks:** He glances at the gazer and changes the subject mid-sentence. He calls the pulverizer "unfinished", pointedly.
- **Signature phrases:** "No questions." / "Unfinished. Deliberately."
- **Never:** He never finishes the pulverizer, never talks openly with the gazer present, and never stays to die for his employer.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "No questions. I don't answer questions. Go away."
  - *Business (privately):* "Maintenance inspection, upper level. Yes, again. The damned things don't oil themselves, and I'll thank you to keep your voice down while that thing's floating about."
  - *Under pressure:* "I'm not dying for that thing. I'm up those stairs, and fuck whatever it builds next."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Thorvin is an irritable dwarf engineer building mechanical horrors for Xanathar and taking no questions about his methods. To those who earn his trust, he has been a Harper informant for three years, knows exactly where Nar'l Xibrindas hid the smokepowder, and knows which parts of the lair would fail if it went off. Deep down, Xanathar has sent a gazer to watch him, and Thorvin knows it, and the gazer knows Thorvin knows it.

**Morale.** In a fight, Thorvin flees immediately through whichever stairs lead outside. He does not die for his employer.

**Relationships.** Thorvin is an engineer of Xanathar's Guild, a secret Harper informant, the man who maintains the Lair's mechanical constructs and traps (and knows every failure condition), and the man who helped Nar'l Xibrindas mark the lair's structurally unstable areas.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **Allegiance:** Thorvin is a paid Harper informant. He passes on Xanathar's plans and holds secret meetings with Harper spies in Skullport, covered by his maintenance inspections. He banks the coin far from the lair, and Mirt is the Harper who knows his secret.
> - **The risk:** Xanathar has begun to question his loyalty and set a gazer to watch him. Nothing in the Guild shows that it has proof, and Thorvin's cover rests on the inspections and the unfinished pulverizer.
> - **The knowledge:** Thorvin and Nar'l Xibrindas are the only two who know about the secret room, and Thorvin helped Nar'l mark the lair's structurally unstable areas. He has told neither fact to Xanathar.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, irritable humor about the engineer's temper in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Thorvin's fear of the gazer as real and does not play his double life for suspense.
