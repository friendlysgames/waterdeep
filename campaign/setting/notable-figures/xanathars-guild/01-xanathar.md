**Xanathar** (Lawful evil, beholder, it/its)

## Background

"Xanathar" is a title, derived from the personal name of the Guild's founder and held by the leaders who came after (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar (title)*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. The founder established the criminal underworld's structure, and each later Xanathar inherited the apparatus and the paranoia together.

The first Xanathar was a beholder hired by the Zhentarim to start a cell in Skullport. It cut its employers off and built a criminal organization of its own (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, citing *Waterdeep and the North*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. By 1357 DR the guild was a thriving thieves' guild that worked through freelance agents, so that the agents would know as little about their master as possible, and four Councilors ran it for the beholder (same page) `[research: wiki only]`.

Kirukesai, a beholder slaver who led the Agents of the Eye in Skullport, worked out that Xanathar was a fellow beholder and had it killed. It took the name, killed the few original employees who would not follow, and merged the two organizations without telling the rank and file (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, citing *Cloak & Dagger*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. By 1372 DR it ruled through eleven Masters, and the two names described one body, the Agents of the Eye in Skullport and Xanathar's Guild in Waterdeep (same page, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Other beholders held the title after the Eye. Xandulzrithral retired in peace rather than risk being overthrown, and Izulktur replaced itself with Zushaxx about 1466 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar (Zushaxx)*, citing *Dungeon* #206; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Zushaxx spent its first year reading the library its predecessors had gathered, and the reading gave it a hunger for information (same page) `[research: wiki only]`.

The current Xanathar is a beholder named Zushaxx, and its pronouns are "it". Kirukesai was one of the previous Xanathars. The Forgotten Realms Wiki confirms both names: Zushaxx is the Xanathar of 1492 DR, and Kirukeskai claimed the title in the 14th century, in 1372 DR (the wiki spells it Kirukeskai; the campaign spells it Kirukesai; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

The current Xanathar believes it is the original. This belief is now structural to the Guild's identity rather than correctable. How the belief arose is not settled in any source.

The Guild keeps continuity by systematically eliminating anyone who knows more than the current Xanathar. After about 135 years of this, the Guild is a surveillance network with no institutional memory beyond what the current beholder chooses to believe.

Where the history leaves the Guild: an empire built on fear, run by a paranoid intelligence, served by people who survive by hiding their ambitions behind obedience.

The wiki calls Zushaxx a relatively young beholder when it took the title about 1466 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar (Zushaxx)*, citing *Dungeon* #206; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. It was born in a cluster of beholders in the deep Underdark, and it remembers that cavern more clearly than any year since `[design]`. Each beholder there floated at a different height because each wanted to be above the rest, and the young ate what the elders left `[design]`. A young beholder learned to read an elder's mood from the movement of its eyestalks, since a wrong guess meant being eaten `[design]`. Zushaxx still watches the eyestalks of anyone who speaks to it before it listens to what they say `[design]`.

The event that shaped it came while it was still young `[design]`. The elder that had ruled the cluster for longer than Zushaxx had been alive was killed in a single night by a younger beholder `[design]`. Zushaxx had heard the plan whispered in the next cavern and had not understood it until the elder was dead `[design]`. The new ruler took the elder's hoard and killed everyone who had been close to the old one, and Zushaxx left the cluster before the killing reached it `[design]`. That was the first death it remembers watching, and it has feared its own since `[design]`. It decided then that the elder had been replaced because it stopped listening, and it has never stopped listening itself `[design]`. When Zushaxx later read the library in its lair, it read as a beholder who had once missed a whisper through a wall, and the hunger for information that it brought to those shelves had started in that cavern `[design]`. It tells the rest of its life as a single climb from the cluster to the throne in Skullport, and no other Xanathar appears anywhere in the telling `[design]`.

Besides Sylgar, what Xanathar loves is paper `[design]`. It reads the Guild's ledgers and informants' reports through the night, with its eyestalks muttering over different pages, and it is calmest when two informants have given it the same account `[design]`. The first Sylgar arrived in a tank among a shipment of tribute from a Dock Ward trader who wanted a favor, and it was the only thing in the shipment that wanted nothing `[design]`. Xanathar refused the trader's favor, sent him away and kept the fish `[design]`. A fish cannot plot against it or be bought by someone else, and to Xanathar there has only ever been one Sylgar `[design]`.

Skullport is the Guild's undercity foundation: the shadow city beneath Waterdeep, outside city jurisdiction, processing everything the surface economy won't handle. The Guild's relationship to Skullport is the relationship between a city and its sewers. The Yawning Portal well drops to Undermountain Level 1, and Skullport is connected to Undermountain Level 3, two levels below it (the wiki's Sargauth Level; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. The Dock Ward is the Guild's primary surface territory.

By 1492 DR Zushaxx has taken the whole of Skullport. The businesses that remain pay the Guild for protection, a rival beholder named Nerozar who challenged it for lordship has lost, and the Guild keeps watch posts on the upper levels of Undermountain (*Dungeon of the Mad Mage*, 5e, as summarized in **The Long Shadow**).

Bregan D'aerthe placed Nar'l Xibrindas in the Guild in 1491 DR, a year before the campaign. He won Xanathar's trust and persuaded it to eliminate its other advisors, and he has been its only advisor since (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

About Nightal 1491 DR (derived), Manshoon's Zhentarim sent two lieutenants to Skullport with Eye #1 of the Stone of Golorr, which the cell had taken from the Protector's Enclave in Neverwinter. Xanathar heard their proposal, ate them and kept the Eye (**The Stone of Golorr and the Eyes**; **The Players Arrive**). The street war with Manshoon's Zhentarim in the Dock Ward began from that day, and Xanathar has since ordered Manshoon killed twice and failed both times (**Xanathar** in Villains). The Eye sits in Sylgar's bowl, and Xanathar does not know what it is.

Before Ches 1, 1492 DR the Stone itself passed from hand to hand until it reached Xanathar, and no source dates its arrival (**The Stone of Golorr and the Eyes**; **The Open Lord's Fall**). On Ches 1 it sits in the lair. Xanathar knows the Stone is the key to the vault, but it does not know that there are three Eyes or where the vault is (**Xanathar's Guild** in Organizations).

**Personal goals.** Xanathar wants Sylgar to know it `[design]`. It floats beside the bowl for an hour on most nights and talks to the fish, and it wants the fish to turn and look at it in return, which the fish has never done `[design]`. It has not asked any wizard to arrange this, because it would have to trust one with the fish `[design]`. It also wants one account of itself that is true `[design]`. Every report it reads about the Xanathar was written by someone who fears what it will do with the contents, so it has never believed one, and it wants to hear just once what the people in its lair say about it when it is out of the room `[design]`. Last, it wants its name to outlast it `[design]`. Xanathar hopes that after it is gone Waterdeep will still say that the Xanathar read the whole city and missed nothing `[design]`.

## Personality

To most of Waterdeep, Xanathar is a rumor. The Guild's lower ranks speculate about their master, and few of them have any inkling that it is a beholder.

Those who serve it closely see a paranoid, megalomaniacal beholder that charms and bullies its minions into servitude and trusts none of them. It sees enemies everywhere and lashes out at anyone it suspects of being a Zhentarim spy or assassin. When it flies into a rage it tends to kill random minions, and it disposes of underlings it no longer trusts in the Guild's gladiatorial arena, in a manner it finds entertaining.

Xanathar wears magic rings on three of its eyestalks: a *ring of invisibility* on its fear ray, a *ring of mind shielding* on its sleep ray and a *ring of resistance (force)* on its slowing ray. Its personal rune is a circle with ten equidistant spokes, which is also the symbol of the Guild.

The one creature it cares about besides itself is Sylgar, a goldfish that it keeps in a large glass tank. Its minions watch the fish constantly and replace it before Xanathar notices when it dies, because the beholder cannot tell one fish from another. Its tenderness for Sylgar is total and a little absurd, and it is Xanathar's greatest weakness.

The current Xanathar is also more ambitious and impulsive than many of its predecessors, and it hungers for knowledge, information and wealth (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar (Zushaxx)*, citing *Dungeon* #206; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

It reads the Guild's reports at night and will not skip a page, and it is calmest in the hours after two informants have given it the same account `[design]`. It talks to Sylgar in the baby talk that it uses for nobody else, and it believes that there has only ever been one Sylgar `[design]`.

## Connections

- **Xanathar's Guild:** It rules the Guild from a lair below Skullport, and very few members have access to it. Most of the Guild answers to it without ever having seen it.
- **Sylgar:** The goldfish in the bowl in its sanctum, and the only creature it cares for besides itself. It believes that there has only ever been one Sylgar, and it wants the fish to look back at it `[design]`.
- **Ahmaergo:** Its shield dwarf majordomo, outwardly civil and unflinchingly loyal to it. Most orders that bear Xanathar's name are written by him.
- **Nar'l Xibrindas:** Its drow advisor and, since 1491 DR, its only one. Xanathar knows that something is off about him and has given him a grell bodyguard with orders to dispose of him at the first sign of disloyalty. It does not know that he reports to Jarlaxle.
- **Nihiloor:** The mind flayer that has carved out a lair of its own inside Xanathar's lair. The Guild treats it as Xanathar's intelligence operative, and Character Information records what Xanathar does not know about it.
- **Noska Ur'gray:** Its heaviest enforcer.
- **Ott Steeltoes:** The Guild's fishkeeper. Xanathar rages if Sylgar is lost and not replaced, so Ott sees that a replacement is ready before it notices.
- **Manshoon:** Its rival. The Guild and Manshoon's Zhentarim are fighting a low-level street war in the Dock Ward, and Xanathar has no use for anyone it takes for a Zhentarim sympathizer.
- **Laeral Silverhand:** The Open Lord, whom it has a healthy fear of. Xanathar spares those in her employ so that it does not provoke a conflict with her.

## Overview

Xanathar is a beholder and the master of Xanathar's Guild, the largest criminal organization in Waterdeep. The Guild's smugglers, thieves and informants reach into every ward. Xanathar is said to rule from a fortified lair deep beneath the streets and to trust nobody who works for it. It is also known for an obsessive fondness for a goldfish named Sylgar.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Xanathar should inspire dread with its paranoid volatility, dark amusement with its obsessive love for Sylgar the goldfish, and discomfort when the party realizes that threatening the fish is both the easiest and the most dangerous leverage they have.

**Emotions.** Xanathar most often feels paranoid suspicion, megalomaniacal self-importance, obsessive tenderness toward Sylgar, volcanic rage at perceived conspiracies, smug satisfaction, and a terror of mortality that it projects outward as aggression.

**Motivations.** Xanathar wants to eliminate every Zhentarim operative in Waterdeep, keep hold of the Stone of Golorr, and ensure that no one takes anything that belongs to it, especially Sylgar.

**Inspirations.** When playing Xanathar, channel Tony Montana (*Scarface*), Smaug (*The Hobbit*), and GLaDOS (*Portal*).

### Voice

*A paranoid, grandiose beholder who talks like an emperor and panics like a child whenever the fish is threatened.*

- **Sound:** A booming, echoing voice that seems to come from the whole chamber, overlapping itself as the eyestalks mutter. It goes high and wobbling over Sylgar.
- **Sentence shape:** Grand pronouncements, the royal "we", and lists of enemies and conspiracies. The voice shifts with its mood (see Phases below).
- **Word choice:** Megalomaniac and paranoid: "my city", "my Guild", "traitors", "they conspire". It calls minions "lackeys" or by the wrong name. Sylgar gets baby talk.
- **Swearing:** *Triggered · Colourful · Tirade.* Volcanic and eye-themed when enraged ("Blind, maggot-brained, eyeless fucks! I'll disintegrate your whole bloodline!"). It never swears near Sylgar. Tirade: "Traitors! Worms! Eyeless, gutless, sewer-born, rat-fucking, lying, scheming, blinking, BLINKING maggots, every one of you, I see you, I SEE YOU ALL!"
- **Personality in speech:** Paranoia and grandiosity. Everything implies conspiracy. Its tenderness for Sylgar is total and absurd. Its fear of death comes out as threats.
- **Phases:**
  - **The Paranoid Tyrant (Ches 1, 1492 DR):** calm, administrative, bored cruelty.
  - **Later moods:** the Wounded God is shrill, suspicious and questioning, accusing everyone in the room. The Cornered Monster is volcanic rage in broken sentences, with sudden lucid moments of eerie calm, and Sylgar as its only anchor. Both are keyed to the quest phases on **Xanathar** (Villains).
- **Quirks:** It talks to Sylgar mid-threat. It refers to itself as "we" and "the Xanathar". Its eyestalks argue with each other in mutters.
- **Signature phrases:** "Who sent you? WHO?" / "Sylgar, darling, look away." / "The Xanathar sees everything."
- **Never:** It never admits fear, never shares power, and never lets anyone touch the fish.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Ah. The little strangers. We have heard of you. We hear of everything. Kneel, or become an interesting stain."
  - *Business:* "Bring us Zhentarim heads, and we might let you keep your eyes. Two of them, at least."
  - *Under pressure (Sylgar threatened):* "No, no, no, not Sylgar, put him down, put him DOWN, we'll talk, we'll talk, what do you want?"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Xanathar is a paranoid, megalomaniacal beholder who charms and bullies minions into servitude. To intruders who attack it in its lair, it destroys one of them and offers the survivors a bargain. Deep down, the only creature it truly cares about aside from itself is Sylgar the goldfish, kept in a glass tank while its minions live in terror of the fish dying and scramble to replace it. It cannot tell one fish from another. Its greatest weakness is its insane love for its pet; a DC 16 Intimidation check using Sylgar as leverage turns it into a blubbering mess.

**Morale.** In a fight, Xanathar uses its eye rays without restraint. If reduced to half hit points or believing itself in mortal danger, it activates its ring of invisibility, screams for Ahmaergo to "tear the usurpers limb from limb," and flees to its sanctum.

**Relationships.** Xanathar is the beholder crime lord of Xanathar's Guild, the employer of Ahmaergo (its majordomo), Nihiloor (its mind flayer, which has psionically dominated Xanathar for the last four months without Xanathar knowing), and Nar'l Xibrindas (its drow advisor, whom it does not yet know is Jarlaxle's spy). It has a healthy fear of Laeral Silverhand and spares those in her employ.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with the dry humor of Personality and the Overview resting on the goldfish. The narrator treats Xanathar's paranoia and its violence as real and does not play either for laughs.
