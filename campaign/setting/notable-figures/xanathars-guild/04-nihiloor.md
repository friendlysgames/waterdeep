**Nihiloor** (Lawful evil, mind flayer, it/its)

## Background

Nihiloor is a mind flayer in Xanathar's service. It spends its days breeding intellect devourers and releasing them into the sewers of Waterdeep (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Each devourer attacks the first humanoid it meets and uses the victim as a puppet to spy on the city and report back to Nihiloor. Some devourers hunt a particular kind of target, such as a member of the Watch or the Guard, and some capture named Waterdavians and bring them to Skullport to be enslaved or ransomed (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). One account dates its work for the Guild to the late 15th century DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Nihiloor*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Nihiloor was raised in the brood pool of a mind flayer colony far below the surface, and the first thing it remembers is the pull of the colony's elder brain `[design]`. The pull was a steady pressure that arranged its thoughts before it knew that it was thinking, and for most of its early life it had no thought that the elder brain had not shaped `[design]`. The colony sent it upward with a small band of its kind to find minds, and the band's road ran through the dark beneath Undermountain toward Skullport `[design]`. The pull thinned with distance and stopped at a point that Nihiloor cannot name, and it spent a season in a dry cistern in an abandoned passage, listening to the quiet that followed `[design]`. It did not go back with the band `[design]`.

It reached Skullport as a mind flayer without a colony, and the Guild's agents there brought word of it to Xanathar `[design]`. Xanathar offered it a wing of the lair, a steady supply of captives and a free hand with them, and Nihiloor asked for nothing more `[design]`. Xanathar suspects most of its servants of wanting more than they say, and it found in Nihiloor a servant whose wants it could count, which is why it grants Nihiloor unusual autonomy `[design]`. Several years passed before Nihiloor built the panopticus described below `[design]`.

Nihiloor has an alien mind, and it considers itself Xanathar's equal. It prefers to work from the shadows and pursue its own fell schemes (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Officially it handles Xanathar's most sensitive intelligence operations (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations). It has carved out a lair of its own inside the beholder's lair, in the areas numbered X23 to X27, and the Guild's soldiers avoid it. Guards who draw a shift there request reassignment within weeks and cite nightmares and intrusive thoughts, and Nihiloor lets the dread build because it discourages investigation (`arc-f-xanathars-lair.md`, Nihiloor's Domain). The Guild captures homeless Waterdavians and brings them to the wing for Nihiloor to feed on, and thralls guard the rooms (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

In practice Nihiloor also runs projects that Xanathar has not authorized. In one, it places intellect devourers inside Watch officers and city officials, so that it has eyes throughout Waterdeep's bureaucracy. In another, creatures captured during the Guild's arena operations are modified psionically in search of a "relay organism" that extends the colony's reach without destroying the host brain. The long-term payoff of its projects is the mind flayer colony of Seadeeps on Undermountain Level 17 (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations; **Undermountain and Skullport**).

About three years before the campaign opens, Nihiloor built the panopticus surveillance network and gave it to Xanathar, which believes that the network serves its paranoia. Nihiloor built a back door into the control array from the start (`arc-f-xanathars-lair.md`, opening). It also altered the five dwarves who operate the network, so that they sleep with one eye open and half their brains asleep at any time (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Guild Recreation Hall has no sensor because Nihiloor left that blind spot on purpose, since Guild members speak more freely there and their talk is useful intelligence (`arc-f-xanathars-lair.md`, Safe Zone X28).

A drow of House Auvryndar, captured in Skullport, is held in the lair, and Nihiloor has interrogated him telepathically and learned of his house's plot to conquer Skullport (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Undermountain and Skullport**). Nihiloor carries a 3-inch stone orb carved to look like the eye at the end of a beholder's eyestalk. The orb opens the magic portal in the Xanathar sewer hideout that leads to the lair, and Nihiloor uses it to return home (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; `q11-escape-portal.md`).

Nihiloor keeps no trophies of the people it eats, but from every mind it keeps one thing that it judges finished, such as a lullaby, a ledger method, a smith's way of reading heat or a prayer in a language that nobody else in the city speaks, and it can recite each of them on request `[design]`. It prizes most the minds that years of discipline have made orderly, because they remind it of the elder brain's pull without the pressure `[design]`. Two things unsettle it, and it shows neither `[design]`. One is silence, which includes a sleeping mind going dark beside it, and that is part of the reason the five panopticus dwarves stay half awake `[design]`. The other is a mind that gives it nothing to read, as the dead and the mindless do `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Nihiloor wants to learn why a mind that spent its early life hearing the elder brain should find silence so hard to bear, and it studies its hosts and captives in the hope that one of them will describe something like it `[design]`. None has so far `[design]`. It wants to go back to the dry cistern for a full season, alone and out of the Guild's reach, with nothing to eat and nothing to build, and it has put the trip off every year because each project in the wing needs one more month `[design]`. It also wants to raise a mind flayer of its own, hatched and kept where no elder brain can reach it, so that it can study what such a mind becomes when nothing has arranged it `[design]`.

## Personality

To most of the Guild, Nihiloor is the mind flayer in the deep wing of the lair, and almost nobody goes near it. It does not speak aloud. It speaks into the listener's mind in complete, clinical sentences, and it moves through the lair with perfect calm.

Nihiloor treats every conversation as a chance to study the listener, and it remarks on the listener's brain. It holds non-psionic minds in patient contempt, takes possessive pride in its intellect devourers, and shows no sign of fear or empathy. It dislikes silence and never says so, and it keeps one finished thing from every mind it eats, such as a lullaby or a trade method, to recite later `[design]`.

## Connections

- **Xanathar:** The beholder that rules the Guild, and the one Nihiloor works for. Nihiloor considers itself Xanathar's equal, and Xanathar trusts it with a status no other Guild member holds. The **Xanathar** page in Villains owns the relationship.
- **Intellect devourers:** Nihiloor breeds them, calls them its children, and sets them loose in the sewers. Each one reports back to it.
- **The thralls:** They guard its wing.
- **The panopticus dwarves:** Five dwarves operate the surveillance system, and Nihiloor altered each of them so that they sleep with one eye open. A fully sleeping mind is silent to it, and it prefers the dwarves half awake `[design]`.
- **The colony:** The mind flayer colony far below the surface where Nihiloor was raised under an elder brain. Nihiloor does not name it, does not speak of it and has not gone back `[design]`.
- **House Auvryndar:** A drow house from Menzoberranzan whose plot to conquer Skullport Nihiloor has learned from a captive.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** Most of its members stay out of Nihiloor's wing. The Guild brings Nihiloor captives to feed on, and Nihiloor handles its most sensitive intelligence operations.

## Overview

Nihiloor is a mind flayer who works for Xanathar's Guild and handles its most sensitive intelligence operations. It moves through the lair with perfect calm and treats every conversation as a chance to study the other person. The Guild's other members give it a wide berth and avoid its wing of the lair.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Nihiloor should inspire visceral horror with its alien otherness, intellectual dread when the party learns how many people it has compromised, and helpless rage at the knowledge that it has bent Xanathar to its will and the beholder has no idea.

**Emotions.** Nihiloor most often feels cold intellectual curiosity, alien satisfaction at its creations, patient derision for non-psionic minds, possessive pride in its intellect devourers, detached amusement at humanoid politics, and a complete absence of anything recognizable as empathy.

**Motivations.** Nihiloor wants to breed and deploy intellect devourers throughout Waterdeep, pursue its own fell schemes independent of Xanathar's goals, and observe the Grand Game from within the shadows where no one looks.

**Inspirations.** When playing Nihiloor, channel HAL 9000 (*2001: A Space Odyssey*), Hannibal Lecter (*Silence of the Lambs*), and the Borg Queen (*Star Trek*).

### Voice

*A mind flayer that speaks only into your mind: cool, curious, and utterly without warmth.*

- **Sound:** No spoken voice. It speaks telepathically, a cold, clear, sexless presence inside the listener's head, perfectly calm, and accompanied by a faint pressure behind the eyes.
- **Sentence shape:** Precise, complete, clinical sentences. It asks questions as though taking notes, and never uses a contraction or a filler.
- **Word choice:** Scientific and possessive: "specimen", "vessel", "cultivation", "adequate". It calls humanoids by function ("the carrier", "the clerk"). Its intellect devourers are "my children".
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Profanity is a humanoid noise it finds mildly interesting.
- **Personality in speech:** Cold curiosity and derision. It considers the party interesting specimens. Detached amusement at politics shows as questions about why they bother.
- **Quirks:** It answers questions the listener only thought of. It pauses mid-conversation as if listening to something else. It remarks on the listener's brain.
- **Signature phrases:** "Fascinating." / "Your mind is adequate." / "You will be of use."
- **Never:** It never shows fear, never raises its "voice", and never explains its plans.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting (telepathic):* "You are frightened. That is appropriate. Come closer, I wish to see how you think."
  - *Business:* "The beholder's gold is irrelevant. I am cultivating something more interesting. You will be of use."
  - *Under pressure:* "This vessel is damaged. I will make another." (and it's gone)

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Nihiloor is Xanathar's mind flayer operative. To Xanathar, it is a trusted mind flayer operative with a status no other Guild member holds. Deep down, Nihiloor has psionically dominated Xanathar for the last four months, and Xanathar does not know. It breeds intellect devourers, sets them loose in the sewers, and uses them to occupy Waterdavians, who walk the city with their own brains intact and a devourer steering them. Its methods leave little behind for PCs to question.

**Morale.** In a fight, Nihiloor would use Dominate Monster on the most dangerous combatant, command its intellect devourers to attack, and retreat when threatened. It caresses an intellect devourer like a pet and carries it as it glides away.

**Relationships.** Nihiloor is the intelligence and infiltration specialist of Xanathar's Guild and a mind flayer that has dominated Xanathar's mind for the last four months in a Guild where even a hint of that would get anyone else disintegrated.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text. The narrator describes Nihiloor by what it does and what it keeps, and leaves the coldness to its own measured speech in Voice.
