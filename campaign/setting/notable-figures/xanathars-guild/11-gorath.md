**Gorath** (Lawful evil, duergar, he/him)

## Background

Gorath is a duergar, one of the gray dwarves of the Underdark, and he serves Xanathar's Guild as the leader of its first response team. No source records where he was born or what he did before he joined `[research]`. He grew up in a small duergar hold in the tunnels below the Sword Coast North, where the families lived by carrying goods and messages for the brokers of Skullport `[design]`. As a boy he ran the hold's messages to the town, and he was taught to give each one word for word and to leave before anyone could ask him questions `[design]`.

When Gorath was young, a shipment that the hold had promised to one of those brokers went missing on the road `[design]`. The broker's enforcers came to the hold and flogged his father in the common hall, shouting at him the whole time, and his father died of the beating within the month `[design]`. The hold still owes the broker for the lost shipment `[design]`. Gorath concluded that a loud punishment starts the next quarrel, and that a plain warning given once lets the other side end the matter before it gets worse `[design]`. His younger sister stayed in the hold and still lives there `[design]`.

Gorath left the hold to earn coin against the debt and found work as a hired fighter in Skullport, a town that Xanathar's Guild holds through its fortress on Skull Island `[design]`. Every applicant to the Guild must pass an entry test that involves a serious crime, so Gorath must have passed it before the Guild gave him a command `[research]`. The Guild found that he could deliver a warning without starting a street war, and over the years it gave him the first of its response teams `[design]`. No source says what his crime was, when he joined or how long he has led the team.

By 1492 DR Gorath leads the first of the Guild's three response teams, four duergar sent when someone needs to be taught a lesson (**Xanathar's Guild**, Factions Guide). Grinda Garloth, the Dock Ward armorer who contracts to the Guild, has outfitted the team `[research]`. The Guild sends Gorath's team first, then Korgstrod Uxgulm's, and Noska Ur'gray's team last. It warns before it kills, and Gorath delivers its message once and leaves (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations).

**Personal goals.** Gorath wants to pay off the hold's debt to the Skullport broker, and he sends coin home to his sister whenever a trader he trusts is going that way `[design]`. He wants the debt gone before the broker's enforcers come back and make an example of someone else in the hold `[design]`. He also looks for a dark ale as good as the one his father brewed for the hold before the shipment was lost, and he has not found one in any tavern he has entered `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who have met him, Gorath is a duergar in plain clothes who orders a good drink, pays the exact price, and gives his message once. He keeps his voice low and even, and he answers no questions once the message is delivered. He treats raised voices and curses as unprofessional, and he uses neither.

Gorath regards the message as the whole of his work. A warning given well makes what comes after unnecessary, and he holds that view because of what happened to his father `[design]`. When the warning is ignored, the Guild sends Korgstrod's team and then Noska's.

## Connections

- **Xanathar:** The beholder that rules the Guild. It sends Gorath's team first when someone needs a lesson, and Gorath carries out its orders exactly.
- Gorath's team: Four duergar, the first of the Guild's three response teams. Grinda Garloth has outfitted it `[research]`.
- **Korgstrod Uxgulm:** The leader of the second response team, three duergar, a half-ogre and a gazer. The Guild sends him after Gorath, and no source records a rivalry or a friendship between the two `[research]`.
- **Noska Ur'gray:** The leader of the third response team, six bugbears and a gazer. The Guild sends him after Korgstrod, when Xanathar is done sending messages.
- **Grinda Garloth:** The Dock Ward armorer who contracts to the Guild and has outfitted Gorath's team `[research]`.
- His sister: She lives in the hold where Gorath grew up, and he sends her coin against the hold's debt `[design]`.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** Gorath is one of its field leaders. No source names anyone in the Guild as his enemy.

## Overview

Gorath is a duergar who leads the first response team of Xanathar's Guild. His four duergar are sent when someone needs to be taught a lesson. He delivers the Guild's message once, in person, and does not repeat it. Korgstrod Uxgulm's team and then Noska Ur'gray's are sent after his.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Gorath should inspire slow dread as the message lands; unexpected respect for a man who delivers bad news without threatening and still makes you feel threatened; and the particular unease of a warning delivered with total professionalism.

**Emotions.** Gorath most often feels professional calm, the satisfaction of a message delivered precisely, contempt for situations that require raised voices, deliberate patience, and the focused restraint of a man who is himself the warning.

**Motivations.** Gorath wants to deliver the message exactly as instructed, leave without escalating, and ensure the recipient understood.

**Inspirations.** When playing Gorath, channel Mike Ehrmantraut (*Breaking Bad*), Anton Chigurh (*No Country for Old Men*, the message-before-consequence aspect), and Winston Wolf (*Pulp Fiction*).

### Voice

*A duergar messenger who delivers the warning once, calmly, and leaves the threat in the air.*

- **Sound:** A low, calm, unhurried voice with no inflection at all.
- **Sentence shape:** A short, complete message delivered once, deliberately (a break from `ember-voice` speech). He doesn't repeat himself or answer follow-up questions.
- **Word choice:** Plain and exact. He orders something good at the bar by name. He calls the recipient by name, which is itself the threat.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* He finds raised voices and curses equally unprofessional.
- **Personality in speech:** Professional calm. He is the announcement, and he knows the consequence comes after. His contempt for escalation shows in how evenly he speaks.
- **Quirks:** He leaves exact coin. He says the message, finishes his drink, and goes.
- **Signature phrases:** "I'll say this once." / "That's the message." / "Good ale."
- **Never:** He never threatens directly, never repeats himself, and never starts the fight.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "The dark ale, please. Good ale."
  - *Business:* "I'll say this once. Xanathar knows what you did. That's the message."
  - *Under pressure:* "I'm leaving now. I'd let me."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Gorath is a stocky duergar in unremarkable clothes who, when he delivers the Guild's message, sits at the bar, orders something good, and leaves exact coin. To those who receive his message, he delivers it once, clearly, and does not repeat himself. Deep down, Gorath is the announcement. The consequence comes after. He finds the distinction important.

**Morale.** In a fight, Gorath fights without aggression and without mercy, in that order. He does not pursue. His job is to survive and report.

**Relationships.** Gorath is the first response team leader of Xanathar's Guild (four duergar, sent when the Guild wants a lesson taught rather than a person killed), the formal notice that Xanathar knows what someone has done, and the first of the three teams the Guild sends, ahead of Korgstrod's team and then Noska's.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, level manner in Personality that matches Gorath's own. The narrator treats his warnings as real and does not play him for a joke.
