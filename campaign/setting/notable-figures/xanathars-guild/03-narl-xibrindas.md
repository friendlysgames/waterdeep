# Nar'l Xibrindas

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Drow mage, neutral evil*. Stat block: **Mage**.
> - **Affiliation:** Xanathar's Guild (secretly Bregan D'aerthe)
> - **Featured in:** **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **The Wazoo Affair**, **The Betrayal Pitch**, **The Compromised Eye**, **The Dive**, **Doom Raiders Mission 1 — The Dockside Killer**, **Force Grey Mission 4 — Destroy the Intellect Factory**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Nar'l should inspire sympathy with his escalating fear of discovery, fascination with the impossible position he occupies between two hostile factions, and contempt from his own brother Soluun.

**Emotions.** Nar'l most often feels nervous calculation, growing paranoia, practiced competence masking terror, contempt for Xanathar's delusions, desperate self-preservation, and bitter awareness that Jarlaxle would sacrifice him without hesitation if the cost were right.

**Motivations.** Nar'l wants to survive his assignment as Jarlaxle's intelligence asset inside the Xanathar Guild, avoid Xanathar's suspicion (which is growing), and keep his options open in case he needs to betray Bregan D'Aerthe to save himself.

**Inspirations.** When playing Nar'l, channel Severus Snape (*Harry Potter*), Gus Fring (*Breaking Bad*), and Donnie Brasco (*Donnie Brasco*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Nar'l is Xanathar's trusted drow advisor who convinced the beholder to eliminate its other counselors. To Jarlaxle, he is Bregan D'Aerthe's best intelligence source on Guild movements, reporting via Sending spell after three years in place. Deep down, Xanathar suspects a mole and has assigned Nar'l a grell bodyguard with orders to kill him at the first sign of disloyalty. If forced to choose between himself and Bregan D'Aerthe, he will choose himself.

**Morale.** In a fight, Nar'l would use magic to misdirect and escape. He carries three doses of contact poison (eyescratch, DC 14 Con or blinded/poisoned) and will use it on anyone, including allies, if it means survival.

**Relationships.** Nar'l is the younger brother of Soluun Xibrindas (who considers him a weakling), Jarlaxle's intelligence asset inside the Xanathar Guild, and Xanathar's advisor who has a grell bodyguard assigned to execute him at the first sign of treachery.

## Overview

Nar'l Xibrindas is a drow mage serving as one of Xanathar's advisors, presenting himself as a trusted counselor who has the beholder's ear. He is precise, controlled, and carefully unremarkable.
