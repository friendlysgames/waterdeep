**Nar'l Xibrindas** (Neutral evil, drow, he/him)

## Background

Nar'l Xibrindas is a male drow whose house was wiped out long ago. He and his elder brother Soluun survived and joined Bregan D'aerthe, a company built from the rogue males of destroyed houses (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road**). Soluun holds that Nar'l turned to arcane magic to make up for his weakness, and he has never had much faith in his brother or his abilities (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). For what the company wants, see **Jarlaxle Baenre** (Villains).

Nar'l grew up in a drow house whose sons were trained to fight, and he spent his childhood in its lower halls, where the retainers kept the records `[design]`. Soluun, the elder, was sent out with the house's raiding parties to the surface as soon as he could carry a blade `[design]`. Nar'l was judged too slight for the raiding parties and was given to the house archivist, an old scribe who taught him to read, to keep accounts and to copy spells `[design]`. He liked the work, and the archivist let him keep a worn primer of cantrips, which is where his first spells came from `[design]`. Soluun took the arrangement as proof that his brother could not fight and has said so to him ever since `[design]`.

The house fell in a single night, when a rival house attacked while its raiding parties were away `[design]`. Soluun came back from the surface to find the hall burning and went in for his brother, and he carried Nar'l out through a service passage while the archivist and the rest of the household died behind them `[design]`. The primer was the only book that Nar'l took with him, and the house's spellbooks and records burned `[design]`. For years afterward the brothers hired themselves out in the cities of the Underdark, with Soluun fighting and Nar'l keeping the accounts and casting for pay `[design]`. Neither of them has mentioned that night since, and Nar'l has never found a way to thank him for it `[design]`. When Bregan D'aerthe took them in, Soluun gave Jarlaxle the devotion that he had owed his house, and Nar'l took a place as a mage and clerk who copied the company's reports and kept its ciphers `[design]`.

In 1491 DR, a year before the campaign, Jarlaxle gave Nar'l the difficult task of infiltrating Xanathar's Guild and getting as close to the beholder as possible (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Players Arrive**). Nar'l succeeded. He won Xanathar's trust and persuaded it to eliminate its other advisors, and he has been its only advisor since. The advice suited a Guild that removes anyone who knows more than the current beholder (**Xanathar's Guild** in Organizations).

Jarlaxle chose Nar'l for the post because nobody remembers his face after a meeting, and Nar'l took it because he did not think that he could refuse `[design]`. He has learned to read Xanathar's moods from the angle of its eyestalks and the pace of its questions, and he shapes his advice to fit them `[design]`. He thinks Xanathar's certainty that it is the cleverest creature in the city is a delusion, and he never lets his face say so `[design]`. It is also the first master who has asked for his opinion and acted on it, and he does not trust how much he values that `[design]`. He thinks of the advisors he removed only as a risk that he has closed, and it troubles him that the habit came easily `[design]`.

Nar'l reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending*, and his spellbook holds the spell (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Xanathar's Guild** in Organizations). He works from an office in the lair, where he found a secret room that neither Xanathar nor its underlings know exists. He asked to have the adjoining room made his office so that he could keep the secret room hidden. He then borrowed a bag of holding from Jarlaxle and used it to smuggle kegs of smokepowder into the secret room. With the help of **Thorvin Twinbeard**, the Guild's chief engineer, he has identified the structurally unstable parts of the lair (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

By Ches 1, 1492 DR Xanathar has noticed that something is off with its advisor. It recently assigned him a grell bodyguard with instructions to dispose of him at the first sign of disloyalty, and it does not know that he reports to Bregan D'aerthe or to Jarlaxle (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Xanathar** in Villains). Nar'l has grown increasingly worried about his future.

**Personal goals.** Nar'l wants his brother to say, once and without contempt, that he was wrong about him `[design]`. He has never forgotten that Soluun came back into the burning hall for him, and he still loves him for it, though he expects that Soluun would take the admission as a further sign of weakness `[design]`. He also wants to rebuild the house's library from memory `[design]`. He has been writing out what the archivist taught him in a second book that he carries on his person, apart from the spellbook in his office, and after years the work is half done `[design]`. When it is finished he wants a room of his own with a lock on the door and shelves for all of it, in a city where no one has heard of Xanathar or Jarlaxle `[design]`.

## Personality

In the lair, Nar'l is the drow who stands near Xanathar and answers when it asks. He speaks in a soft, even voice and qualifies what he says so that none of it can be held against him later, and he glances at his grell before he answers a question. Xanathar treats him as proof of its own generosity toward outsiders, because he is the only drow at liberty in the lair (**Xanathar's Lair** structure notes).

Behind the manner he is nervous and conniving, and the nervousness has grown since Xanathar began to suspect him (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He puts his own safety ahead of his allies' and keeps his options open in case he has to betray Bregan D'aerthe to save himself. He despises Xanathar's delusions and hides the contempt in careful phrasing while he flatters the beholder. His brother Soluun thinks him a weakling.

## Connections

- **Xanathar:** The beholder he advises and the creature he is placed to watch. It treats him as a trusted counselor, suspects that something is wrong, and has set a grell to kill him at the first sign of disloyalty. He despises its delusions and flatters it anyway (**Xanathar** in Villains).
- **Jarlaxle Baenre:** The leader who placed him in the Guild and receives his reports by *Sending*. Nar'l is aware that Jarlaxle would sacrifice him without hesitation if the cost were right (**Jarlaxle Baenre** in Villains).
- **Bregan D'aerthe:** The company he joined with Soluun after his house fell. It keeps an asset inside every rival, and Nar'l is the one inside Xanathar's Guild (**Bregan D'aerthe** in Organizations).
- **Soluun Xibrindas:** His elder brother and a lieutenant of Jarlaxle. Soluun considers him a weakling and despises him, though he once carried Nar'l out of their burning house `[design]`.
- **The grell:** The bodyguard Xanathar assigned to him, which has orders to kill him at the first sign of disloyalty. Nar'l cannot dismiss it and can only avoid giving it a reason to act (**Xanathar's Lair** structure notes).
- **Thorvin Twinbeard:** The Guild's chief engineer. He is the only other person who knows about the secret room, and he helped Nar'l identify the unstable parts of the lair.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** Nar'l is its only advisor, and **Ahmaergo** is the most influential member after the beholder.

## Overview

Nar'l Xibrindas is a drow mage and Xanathar's only advisor. He presents himself as a trusted counselor who has the beholder's ear. He is precise and controlled, and he takes care to look unremarkable.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Nar'l should inspire sympathy with his escalating fear of discovery, fascination with the impossible position he occupies between two hostile factions, and contempt from his own brother Soluun.

**Emotions.** Nar'l most often feels nervous calculation, growing paranoia, practiced competence masking terror, contempt for Xanathar's delusions, desperate self-preservation, and bitter awareness that Jarlaxle would sacrifice him without hesitation if the cost were right.

**Motivations.** Nar'l wants to survive his assignment as Jarlaxle's intelligence asset inside Xanathar's Guild, avoid Xanathar's suspicion (which is growing), and keep his options open in case he needs to betray Bregan D'aerthe to save himself.

**Inspirations.** When playing Nar'l, channel Severus Snape (*Harry Potter*), Gus Fring (*Breaking Bad*), and Donnie Brasco (*Donnie Brasco*).

### Voice

*A controlled, colourless drow advisor who speaks carefully because a grell is listening.*

- **Sound:** A soft, precise, even voice, always slightly lower than it needs to be.
- **Sentence shape:** Careful, qualified sentences with no commitment. He says less than he knows. Under private pressure, fast, clipped bargaining.
- **Word choice:** Advisor's diplomacy with Xanathar: "a prudent course", "the Master's wisdom". Contempt shows only in careful word choices. He never names Jarlaxle.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Only alone, or with someone he's bargaining for his life with: a tight, frightened "fuck".
- **Personality in speech:** Nervous calculation behind practised competence. He flatters Xanathar while despising it. Growing terror shows as over-precise answers. Self-preservation comes out as offers.
- **Quirks:** He glances at the grell before answering. He phrases every statement so it can be denied later.
- **Signature phrases:** "A prudent course." / "The Master sees further than I." / "What would you offer?"
- **Never:** He never says Jarlaxle's name, never commits to a side, and never speaks freely near the grell.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You're a long way from the surface. The Master may find you interesting. That is rarely good for anyone."
  - *Business (privately):* "I can tell you where the smokepowder is. What I need is a way out of this city that no one is watching. What would you offer?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Wait. Wait. I'm more useful alive, to you and to anyone else. Let's be sensible."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Nar'l is Xanathar's trusted drow advisor who convinced the beholder to eliminate its other counselors. To Jarlaxle, he is Bregan D'aerthe's best intelligence source on Guild movements, reporting via Sending spell after one year in place, placed in 1491 DR. Deep down, Xanathar suspects a mole and has assigned Nar'l a grell bodyguard with orders to kill him at the first sign of disloyalty. If forced to choose between himself and Bregan D'aerthe, he will choose himself.

**Morale.** In a fight, Nar'l would use magic to misdirect and escape. He carries three doses of contact poison (eyescratch, DC 14 Con or blinded/poisoned) and will use it on anyone, including allies, if it means survival.

**Relationships.** Nar'l is the younger brother of Soluun Xibrindas (who considers him a weakling), Jarlaxle's intelligence asset inside Xanathar's Guild, and Xanathar's only advisor, who has a grell bodyguard assigned to execute him at the first sign of treachery.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **Allegiance:** Nar'l is a Bregan D'aerthe plant. Jarlaxle placed him in the Guild in 1491 DR, and he reports by *Sending*. Xanathar does not know whom he reports to, and no one in the Guild knows that he is a spy.
> - **The risk:** Xanathar suspects a mole and is looking in the wrong direction. The grell kills Nar'l at the first proof, and discovery ends his usefulness to the Guild and to Jarlaxle immediately.
> - **The loyalty:** If forced to choose between himself and Bregan D'aerthe, Nar'l chooses himself and will betray the company to save his own skin.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with the careful, colorless manner of the advisor in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Nar'l's fear as real and does not play his double life for suspense.
