**Ott Steeltoes** (Neutral evil, dwarf, he/him)

## Background

Ott Steeltoes is a shield dwarf and a renegade of the Ironmaster clan. He first worked as a pirate, and one account says that he provided Xanathar's delicacies (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Ott Steeltoes*, citing *Cloak & Dagger*, and *Xanathar (original)*, citing *Waterdeep and the North*; editions not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

Ott grew up in an Ironmaster hold where the forge set the rhythm of the day, and the smiths there taught him to judge heat and weight by touch `[design]`. His older sister kept the forge's accounts and had the patience that he lacked `[design]`. When the elders caught him selling a batch of clan steel to a ship's captain on the coast, they gave him the choice of a fine that he could not pay or exile, and he chose exile `[design]`. He took the name Steeltoes afterward, from the steel-capped boots that he wore out of the hold `[design]`. The wiki also records the alias Ott Ironmaster (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Ott Steeltoes*, citing *Cloak and Dagger*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`.

The captain took him on as a hand who could judge cargo by its weight, and Ott sailed with that ship through his years as a pirate `[design]`. The ship sold the rarer food among its prizes at Skullport tables, and that trade is how the Guild first heard his name `[design]`. A storm later sank the ship, and Ott reached shore holding a hatch cover while most of the crew drowned `[design]`. He has distrusted open water since then and will not sit on a pier `[design]`.

Later the Guild offered him a place, and he accepted at once, liking the prospect of more power and a less dangerous life. He worked from Skullport and was the most competent smuggler in the Guild (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Ott Steeltoes*, citing *Cloak & Dagger*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. He became good friends with **Ahmaergo**, and the two dwarves often drank together (same page) `[research: wiki only]`.

Ahmaergo is the only person in the Guild whom Ott counts as a friend `[design]`. Ahmaergo talks about minotaurs and Ott talks about water, and each of them listens with good manners to the other's subject `[design]`. Ott has also heard Ahmaergo describe a disciplinary procedure in the same even voice, and he does not assume that the friendship would protect him from one `[design]`.

By 1492 DR Ott is Xanathar's fishkeeper (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Ott Steeltoes*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. His task is to tend Sylgar, the goldfish that Xanathar loves as much as itself, and the task is nerve-wracking because Xanathar rages when its pet is lost and not replaced. There have been many Sylgars over the years, and Ott is skilled at finding a replacement before Xanathar realizes that the fish has died (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). A smuggler with his background would know where to buy live fish, which may be how a Guild veteran came to keep one `[research]`.

The work began on a night when a Sylgar died and Ahmaergo needed another before Xanathar came to the sanctum `[design]`. Ott had a replacement in a bucket by dawn, and the job stayed with him afterward `[design]`. He has come to like it more than he expected, because water can be measured in a way that a smuggler's routes cannot, and he judges the bowl's temperature by touch as he once judged steel `[design]`. He has watched Xanathar kill minions in its rages (**Xanathar's Guild**), and he expects that one day the blame for a loss will land on him whether or not it was his fault `[design]`. On a night after a Sylgar had died he once called to Xanathar in his mind, and Xanathar left the sanctum soon afterward on business of its own, and Ott has believed ever since that it hears him `[design]`. He stitched the skullcap himself, after he noticed that Xanathar was calmer around servants who wore its shape `[design]`.

In his spare time Ott worships Zuggtmoy, the demon queen of fungi, and cultivates mushrooms, spores and molds (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He came to her through a Skullport cellar where he once stored contraband, and a patch of fungus on its wall grew in the dark with no care at all `[design]`. A seller of spores in the market told him what the growth was and whose servant it was `[design]`. He has kept the faith alone since then and has never met another worshipper whom he trusts `[design]`.

About Nightal 1491 DR (derived), Xanathar ate two lieutenants that Manshoon had sent to Skullport and kept the Eye of the Stone of Golorr that they carried. Ott put that Eye in Sylgar's bowl as a pretty stone, because Xanathar likes the fish's habitat to look interesting, and he has no idea what the Eye is (**The Stone of Golorr and the Eyes**; **The Players Arrive**). On Ches 1, 1492 DR he is free and tends Sylgar in the lair's sanctum.

**Personal goals.** Ott wants his sister to answer a letter `[design]`. For years he has sent coin and a short note to the Ironmaster hold through smugglers, and he has never had a reply, so he does not know whether she is alive or whether the clan lets her speak his name `[design]`. He also wants a dry cellar in a quiet town, with a door that locks from the inside, where his mushrooms can grow in the dark with no beholder watching, and he sets aside a little coin toward it from every payment `[design]`. The cellar is where the shrine to Zuggtmoy would go, and in his plans it has a shelf for a small bowl with a plain goldfish that nobody else cares about `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who know him, Ott Steeltoes is the dwarf in the eyestalk hat who tends the fish. He wears a leather skullcap stitched with fake beholder eyestalks, and he keeps to himself.

When strangers find him alone with Sylgar, he draws a dagger and stammers that he will call the boss. He believes he can speak to Xanathar's mind from across the lair, so he closes his eyes and begs it to return and destroy the intruders, though Xanathar cannot hear him.

Away from the bowls, Ott worships Zuggtmoy and grows mushrooms, spores and molds. He is nervous about the fish, and he is proud of how healthy it stays. He judges the water by touch, the way a smith judges a billet, and he keeps a notebook of temperatures and feedings that he does not show anyone `[design]`.

## Connections

- **Xanathar:** The beholder that rules the Guild, and Ott's master. It rages if Sylgar is lost and not replaced, so Ott sees that a replacement is ready before it notices a loss.
- **Sylgar:** The goldfish that Xanathar loves as much as itself. Ott feeds it and replaces it when it dies, and Eye #1 of the Stone of Golorr lies in its bowl, where Ott put it.
- **Ahmaergo:** The Guild's shield dwarf majordomo and, by one account, Ott's good friend. The two dwarves often drank together (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Ott Steeltoes*, citing *Cloak & Dagger*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Ott enjoys the evenings and does not rely on them, because he has heard Ahmaergo explain how the Guild disciplines its people `[design]`.
- **His sister:** An older sister in the Ironmaster hold, who kept the forge's accounts. Ott sends her coin and notes through smugglers and has never had a reply `[design]`.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** Ott is one of its field agents. The Guild gives Sylgar more consideration than any humanoid lieutenant.

## Overview

Ott Steeltoes is a dwarf of Xanathar's Guild who tends Sylgar, the goldfish that Xanathar is said to dote on. He wears a leather skullcap stitched with fake beholder eyestalks and otherwise keeps to himself.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Ott should inspire dark amusement with his eyestalk hat and his desperate attempts to telepathically summon a beholder who cannot hear him; sympathy when you understand he sleeps under a fishbowl on a straw pallet; and unexpected usefulness, because he knows the Lair well and shares what he knows with anyone he trusts.

**Emotions.** Ott most often feels low-grade fear that this is the Sylgar who finally dies on his watch, the meditative calm of someone who has outsourced his existential crisis to a demon queen of fungi, absurd pride in the fish's consistent good health, and genuine relief when intruders arrive who are not from Xanathar.

**Motivations.** Ott wants to keep Sylgar alive, avoid being blamed for anything Xanathar decides is someone's fault, and eventually find a Zuggtmoy shrine not located directly below a beholder's sanctum.

**Inspirations.** When playing Ott, channel Wormtail (*Harry Potter*), Gollum (*The Lord of the Rings*, the devoted caretaker), and Smithers (*The Simpsons*).

### Voice

*A frightened, fish-obsessed cultist in an eyestalk hat who'll tell you everything once he trusts you.*

- **Sound:** A reedy, nervous dwarven voice that squeaks when stressed.
- **Sentence shape:** Rushed, anxious rambling full of fish details, and then, once he trusts you, a flood of precise intelligence.
- **Word choice:** Fish-keeping (water temperature, feeding schedules), with Zuggtmoy's fungal piety muttered under it. He calls Xanathar "the Master" in terror, and Sylgar "the little lord".
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Plain · Phrase.* Frightened little curses ("Oh shit, oh shit, the water's too cold").
- **Personality in speech:** Low-grade terror and absurd pride. He's convinced every Sylgar will be the one that dies on his watch. His Zuggtmoy devotion comes out as mushroom-flavoured calm. His relief when someone who is not from Xanathar shows up is gushing.
- **Quirks:** He adjusts the eyestalk hat when nervous. He tries to "send" thoughts to Xanathar, visibly straining. He knows the fishbowl's weight exactly.
- **Signature phrases:** "The little lord's fine, he's fine, he's fine." / "Six thousand pounds, that bowl." / "Zuggtmoy keep me."
- **Never:** He never admits to replacing a fish, never harms Sylgar, and never stays loyal once someone offers a way out.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You're not from the Master? Oh, thank the Lady of Rot. Is the little lord alright? Is he swimming?"
  - *Business:* "Guard change is third bell, two beholder-kin on the north stair, and the bowl weighs six thousand pounds, don't try to move it."
  - *Under pressure:* "Oh shit, oh shit, he'll blame me, he always blames me!"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Ott Steeltoes is the dwarf with the beholder-eyestalk hat who tends the fish and sleeps under the bowl. To anyone he trusts, he is terrified, forthcoming, and full of accurate information about Xanathar's Lair — including guard schedules, trap locations, and the fact that the large fishbowl weighs 6,000 pounds. Deep down, he has known for years that Xanathar will eventually blame him for something, and he is increasingly grateful to anyone who provides a reasonable exit.

**Morale.** In a fight, Ott stammers, closes his eyes, and attempts to communicate his distress to Xanathar. This does not work. He then runs.

**Relationships.** Ott is Xanathar's fishkeeper (all Sylgar replacements have passed through him), a Zuggtmoy worshipper who cultivates mushrooms in his quarters, a field agent of Xanathar's Guild, and a detailed intelligence source for anyone he trusts who asks the right questions about the Lair. He is also the one who put Eye #1 of the Stone of Golorr in Sylgar's bowl as a pretty stone, and he has no idea what it is.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about the eyestalk hat and the goldfish in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Ott's fear as real and does not mock him.
