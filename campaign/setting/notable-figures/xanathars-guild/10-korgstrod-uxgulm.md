**Korgstrod Uxgulm** (Lawful evil, duergar, he/him)

## Background

Korgstrod Uxgulm is a duergar, one of the gray dwarves of the Underdark, and he serves Xanathar's Guild in the city above. No source records his birth, his family or his life before the Guild. The *Dragon Heist* index lists a Clan Uxgulm on the page that describes his cellar, which suggests that Uxgulm is his clan name (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, index; 5e) `[research: wiki only]` `[research]`. If Uxgulm is his clan name, he was a younger son of a lesser line of that clan, born in a duergar warren deep in the Underdark whose name he has never given to anyone in the city `[design]`. His father led one of the clan's work gangs in its mines, and the clan judged a line by the ore that its gangs brought up `[design]`. Korgstrod kept the gang's tally book from boyhood and could count a gang and its ore at a glance, which pleased his father and meant little to his elder brother `[design]`.

When Korgstrod was grown, his father fell sick and he held the gang for a single shift with his father's braided whip `[design]`. He set the whip down to measure a seam and could not find it when he looked up, and by the time he found it under the tally book the gang had walked out of the gallery `[design]`. The clan docked his line's quota, and an elder told him in front of the whole gang that a man who loses his whip should keep the tally and nothing else `[design]`. His brother took the gang when their father died and has never mentioned that shift `[design]`. Korgstrod left the warren soon afterward with the whip and a place as a tally clerk on a duergar trade caravan bound for Skullport, and he counted crates for that caravan for years `[design]`.

Every applicant to Xanathar's Guild must pass an entry test that always involves a serious crime, and Korgstrod must have passed it before the Guild gave him a command (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations) `[research]`. No source says what his crime was or when he joined. He passed it by looting a warehouse in the Dock Ward with a gang of six kobolds, and every crate that he had counted out came back with him `[design]`. In the years that followed the Guild saw that none of his gangs ever came up short on a delivery, and it gave him the cellar under Orb Confectioners, which needed a man who counted well and kept order `[design]`.

By 1492 DR he runs the duergar operation in the cellar below Orb Confectioners, a sweets shop on Rising Ride in the Southern Ward that sells eye-shaped treats such as smallcakes, sweetmeats, sugar plates and candied nuts (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations and Factions Guide; *Dragon Heist*, 5e). He sits in a stone chair in the cellar and keeps three kobolds as lackeys. He flogs them with a whip to keep them in line, and the cowardly kobolds cower in his presence and flee into the sewers if anyone attacks him (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). A customer who asks the shop for "eight small eyes" and answers "Around an ocular cake" earns an escort to the basement stairs (**Southern Ward**). He had the chair cut to the pattern of the one in his clan's counting hall, as well as he remembers it `[design]`. He chose kobolds for the cellar, and only three of them, because three are few enough to count at a glance `[design]`. He still carries his father's whip, and he sets it down and loses it within the hour, so that he spends a good part of every shift hunting for it and shouting `[design]`. The kobolds have learned that a boss who is hunting for his whip is not using it, and they ask after it whenever he raises his voice `[design]`. When he does find it, they pay for the time that he lost `[design]`.

The Guild also gives him a response team of three duergar, a half-ogre and a gazer, the second of its three teams. The Guild sends Gorath's team first, then Korgstrod's, and Noska Ur'gray's team last (**Xanathar's Guild**, Factions Guide; **Noska Ur'gray**). Gorath and Korgstrod lead the Guild's duergar in the field, and Organizations counts both among its field leaders.

Ahmaergo writes most of the orders that bear Xanathar's name and handles resupply for the outposts, so Korgstrod probably takes his supply and discipline orders from him (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations) `[research]`. No source says how long Korgstrod has held the cellar. He reads the post as a test that the Guild set him, and he has never asked what it expects him to do next `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Korgstrod wants to send his brother a letter that names his post in words the clan will respect `[design]`. He has begun that letter many times and burned every draft, because the plain name of his post has the word confectioners in it `[design]`. He wants to stop hunting for the whip, and he has tried tying it to his belt with a cord, which he now loses as well `[design]`. He also wants to buy a single sugar plate from the shop above and eat it alone, and he has not gone up the stairs in daylight since he took the cellar, because a duergar boss cannot be seen buying sweets in front of his kobolds `[design]`.

## Personality

To most people who know of him, Korgstrod is a duergar in a stone chair with kobold lackeys around him. He runs the cellar with a whip and blunt authority, and he expects obedience from everyone who deals with him. The kobolds cower when he is in the room. He pats his belt for the whip every few minutes, and when it is missing he raises his voice at whoever stands nearest `[design]`.

He holds the duergar's contempt for weakness and believes that harsher discipline cures most problems. He is proud of the post and frustrated that it is a subordinate one, and he wants Xanathar to notice how well he runs it.

## Connections

- **Xanathar:** The beholder that rules the Guild. Korgstrod wants to earn its favor by running the Orb Confectioners cellar without trouble.
- **Ahmaergo:** The Guild's shield dwarf majordomo. Ahmaergo handles outpost resupply and writes most orders that bear Xanathar's name, so he is probably the one who gives Korgstrod his orders `[research]`.
- **Gorath:** The leader of the first response team, four duergar. The Guild sends Gorath before Korgstrod, and no source records a rivalry or a friendship between the two `[research]`.
- **Noska Ur'gray:** The leader of the third response team, six bugbears and a gazer. The Guild sends him after Korgstrod, and no source records a tie between them.
- **The kobolds:** Three lackeys who attend him in the cellar. He flogs them to keep them in line, and they cower in his presence. They ask after his whip whenever he shouts, because a boss who is hunting for it is not using it `[design]`.
- **His elder brother:** The brother who took their father's gang in the clan's warren and has never mentioned the shift that Korgstrod lost. Korgstrod has never written to him `[design]`. He is unnamed.
- **Xanathar's Guild:** Korgstrod is one of its field leaders. No source names anyone in the Guild as his enemy.

## Overview

Korgstrod Uxgulm is a duergar of Xanathar's Guild who holds court in a stone chair, attended by kobold lackeys. He expects obedience and receives it, and he handles Guild business with blunt authority.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Korgstrod should inspire wariness with his brute authority, and dark humor with the way he treats his kobold lackeys.

**Emotions.** Korgstrod most often feels the duergar's characteristic contempt for weakness, frustrated pride in a subordinate operation that should have been simple, and physical pleasure in discipline.

**Motivations.** Korgstrod wants to maintain discipline in the sewer hideout and earn Xanathar's favor by running the Orb Confectioners operation without trouble.

**Inspirations.** When playing Korgstrod, channel Vernon Dursley (*Harry Potter*, the authoritarian who does not understand what he controls), General Grievous (*Star Wars*, the brutal officer who treats his own troops as expendable), and any petty overseer who is certain that harsher discipline will fix whatever has gone wrong in the hideout.

### Voice

*A pompous duergar boss who rules his kobolds by shouting and is forever hunting for his whip.*

- **Sound:** A harsh, commanding, grinding voice, raised for his kobolds.
- **Sentence shape:** Barked orders and self-important statements. There's a brooding, self-critical muttering whenever something in the hideout goes wrong.
- **Word choice:** Military authority and duergar contempt: "discipline", "unworthy", "weaklings". He calls the kobolds "vermin".
- **Swearing:** *Punctuation · Colourful · Tirade.* Duergar bile ("Lick-spittle, dung-born vermin, fetch the whip!"). Tirade: "Lick-spittle, dung-born, scale-shedding, whip-losing, tunnel-farting, surface-sucking, gutless little shit-lizards, WHERE IS MY WHIP?"
- **Personality in speech:** Brute authority and thwarted ambition. He is frustrated that he runs a cellar when he wants Xanathar's favor, and he discusses it earnestly.
- **Quirks:** The kobolds keep asking about a whip, and he keeps shouting at them.
- **Signature phrases:** "Discipline!" / "Weaklings." / "Where is the whip?"
- **Never:** He never shows weakness to subordinates, and never finds the whip.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Kneel before the master of this cellar, surface-worms."
  - *Business:* "This is a Guild cellar, and nothing leaves it without my say. State your business."
  - *Under pressure:* "Vermin! To me! Somebody find the fucking whip!"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Korgstrod Uxgulm is a duergar in a stone chair attended by kobold lackeys who keep asking about a whip. To those who deal with him, he is the officer who runs Xanathar's Orb Confectioners hideout and expects obedience.

**Morale.** In a fight, Korgstrod presses forward and uses his whip to position the kobolds he expects to die first. He does not retreat from his own hideout.

**Relationships.** Korgstrod is the duergar leader of the Orb Confectioners sewer hideout, the second response team leader of Xanathar's Guild, and the commander of a kobold retinue whose continued employment is contingent on not being in his field of vision when things go wrong.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, gentle humor about the kobolds and the missing whip in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats Korgstrod's authority as real and does not mock him.
