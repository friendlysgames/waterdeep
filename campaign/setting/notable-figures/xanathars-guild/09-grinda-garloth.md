**Grinda Garloth** (Chaotic neutral, Illuskan human, she/her)

## Background

Grinda Garloth is an Illuskan human wizard and treasure hunter who lives in Mistshore, the Dock Ward neighborhood built on a dock and the wrecks beside it. She speaks Common, Dwarvish, Halfling and Undercommon (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). A fire in Mirtul 1491 DR burned most of Mistshore, and most of its buildings still stand burned and abandoned (**Dock Ward**).

Her home holds an alchemist's laboratory and a locked sea chest that carries a glyph of warding, and she keeps the only key (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Beside it is an enclosed dock where she keeps her Apparatus of Kwalish, the submersible contraption she takes into Deepwater Harbor to hunt for treasure on the bottom (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki describes her as a mad treasure hunter who worked from her laboratory in Mistshore (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Grinda Garloth*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`.

Her trade has two halves. She rents the Apparatus to clients whose business is underwater, and she sells black-market arms and armor (**Dock Ward**). She outfits the agents of Xanathar's Guild, supplies the exotic weapons that go to its gladiatorial combats, rents equipment to third parties, and keeps her client list private (**Xanathar's Guild**, Organizations and Factions Guide). The adventure calls her a mage who has worked for Xanathar in the past, and no source dates when she began `[research]`. Bregan D'aerthe also buys equipment through her, and neither it nor the Guild has acknowledged how much it depends on her (**Running the Villains**).

What she wants for herself is a dragon turtle wreck 47 fathoms down in Deepwater Harbor, which she has worked for years `[research]`. The harbor is dangerous work. An awakened shark named Obliteros attacks divers who search the bottom, and Grinda has had several close encounters with it and tells her customers nothing about them for fear of scaring them off (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki dates her conflicts with the shark to 1492 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Grinda Garloth*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e) `[research: wiki only]`. The same page gives her a rat familiar, which no source names `[research: wiki only]`.

## Personality

To most people on the docks, Grinda is the wizard who lives over a closed dock and disappears into Deepwater Harbor in her diving machine. She talks about little except the harbor floor, and she brings the same single-mindedness to everything she does.

She sells services and equipment to anyone who pays, and she does not ask what her customers want the gear for. She has no interest in the Grand Game, and she changes the subject back to the wreck whenever she can.

## Connections

- **Xanathar's Guild:** A customer and a contractor, not a member. She equips its agents and supplies its arena weapons, and she fights for her business and not for the Guild.
- **Bregan D'aerthe:** A second customer. The company buys its equipment through her, and she keeps its custom as private as the Guild's.
- **Ahmaergo:** The Guild contact whose written instructions name her as the supplier of the weapons sent down for the gladiatorial combats.
- Obliteros: The awakened shark of Deepwater Harbor. She has met it several times and avoids it where she can.

## Overview

Grinda Garloth is a wizard who lives over a closed dock at Mistshore. She rents out her Apparatus of Kwalish to clients whose business is underwater. She also sells black-market arms and armor at rates she considers entirely reasonable.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Grinda should inspire endearment with her eccentric single-mindedness (everything she does is about what is on the harbor floor); respect for her genuine competence; and wariness when you realize she sells to whoever pays.

**Emotions.** Grinda most often feels absorbed technical focus, opportunistic calculation, specific pleasure in a successful dive, pragmatic indifference to her clients' intentions, and genuine excitement when someone has useful information about underwater locations.

**Motivations.** Grinda wants to find whatever is at the bottom of Deepwater Harbor, maintain the Apparatus of Kwalish, and sell her services and equipment to whoever covers her costs.

**Inspirations.** When playing Grinda, channel Q (*James Bond*), Doc Brown (*Back to the Future*), and Ellen Ripley (*Alien*, practical survivor who does what works).

### Voice

*An eccentric salvage wizard who talks about nothing but the harbor floor and rents her diving machine to anyone.*

- **Sound:** A brisk, excitable voice that speeds up whenever anything underwater is mentioned.
- **Sentence shape:** Technical rambling about the Apparatus and the wreck, with brisk price quotes.
- **Word choice:** Diving and salvage: fathoms, pressure, ballast, "the wreck". She calls the Apparatus of Kwalish "the lobster". Clients are "customers".
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Colourful · Rant.* Salty diving oaths ("Barnacle-fucked seal on the port side!").
- **Personality in speech:** Absorbed focus and opportunism. She's excited by any underwater information. She's indifferent to clients' motives and says so plainly.
- **Quirks:** She quotes depth to the fathom. She changes the subject back to the dragon turtle wreck.
- **Signature phrases:** "Forty-seven fathoms." / "The lobster's available." / "Client list is proprietary."
- **Never:** She never discloses a client, never turns down paying work, and never stops thinking about the wreck.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Customers! Good. You want the lobster? Reasonable rates, and you don't touch the levers."
  - *Business:* "Who else rented it? Proprietary. What did they want? Also proprietary. Do you know anything about the old dragon turtle wreck?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Get in the lobster, now, and hold your breath!"

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Grinda is the wizard who lives over a closed dock and disappears into Deepwater Harbor, and whose Apparatus of Kwalish is available for rental at rates she considers entirely reasonable. To those who deal with her, she sells services, equipment, and information — about anything except her current client list, which is proprietary. Deep down, she does not care about the Grand Game; she cares about the dragon turtle wreck at 47 fathoms and what might still be salvageable.

**Morale.** In a fight, Grinda uses her arcane magic to support whoever she is currently working with and retreats into the harbor if things go against her. She fights for her business, not for the Guild or the company.

**Relationships.** Grinda Garloth is a black market armorer and salvage wizard at Mistshore, an independent contractor to both Xanathar's Guild and Bregan D'aerthe, and the owner of the Apparatus of Kwalish.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a gentle humor about her single-mindedness in Personality and the Overview. The narrator treats her as a competent professional who happens to care about nothing above the water line.
