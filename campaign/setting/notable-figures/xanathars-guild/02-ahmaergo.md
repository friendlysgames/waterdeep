**Ahmaergo** (Lawful evil, shield dwarf, he/him)

## Background

Ahmaergo is a shield dwarf, and one account of the Guild's history calls him the Horned Dwarf (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Ahmaergo*, citing *Cloak and Dagger*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Every member of Xanathar's Guild must first commit a serious crime to join, so Ahmaergo did the same, though nothing records what it was `[research]`.

Ahmaergo was born in a shield dwarf mining hold in the Spine of the World, where his mother was quartermaster and his father kept the stores rolls `[design]`. He read before he could swing a pick, and in his teens he was the hold's second clerk, with a stool in the counting room and a younger brother on one of the hauling crews `[design]`.

One winter a minotaur came up out of unmapped tunnels beneath the lower galleries and killed a hauling crew working the far end `[design]`. The hold's roll for that crew was a season out of date, so for three days nobody could say how many people were down there or who they were `[design]`. When the hunters finally went in, they found eleven bodies, and one of them was his brother `[design]`. Ahmaergo went down with them and saw the minotaur laid out for the count, and he took the tip of one horn before the hold burned the carcass `[design]`. The elders entered the dead as lost in a rockfall, which spared them from explaining the minotaur or the stale roll, and they asked the clerk who had copied the roll to sign the entry `[design]`. He refused and left the hold that spring with a clerk's hand and the horn tip, and he has not been back `[design]`. For years afterward he drew the creature's bones from memory, and he has never settled whether he hated it `[design]`.

He came out into Skullport by the deep roads and found work with a factor who needed manifests kept for the cargo that came up from the Undercity `[design]`. The work taught him where every supply line beneath Waterdeep ran and which of the people on it paid late `[design]`. Xanathar's Guild recruited him there, and he has never told anyone what his entry test was, not even Ott `[design]`. Supply was the least wanted post in the Guild, because the ledgers were a mess and the carriers were thieves `[design]`. Ahmaergo took it and had the coin and the goods arriving on schedule within a year `[design]`. Xanathar noticed that it no longer had to think about supply, and the orders that bore its name began to pass through his hand `[design]`. He asks Xanathar for nothing and does not explain himself when it questions him, and that is why it keeps him close `[design]`.

By 1492 DR he is the Guild's majordomo and, after the beholder, its most influential member (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He is one of the Guild's important underlings and oversees its day-to-day work (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He handles the Guild's logistics, which means gladiatorial procurement, outpost resupply and discipline, and he writes most of the orders that bear Xanathar's name (**Xanathar's Guild**; **The Long Shadow**). He also manages the Skullport supply lines that fund the Guild (**Xanathar**). If he were killed, the Guild's daily operation would fall into chaos for a tenday (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

Ahmaergo presides over the fight tournaments in the Pit of Blood and Fortune, the lair's arena, and he stands on the steps of the alcove beside Noska Ur'gray, who releases the combatants (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). When he is not in the arena with Xanathar, he walks the dungeon on routine inspections. If he meets intruders and is outnumbered, he falls back, gathers reinforcements and leads a search party, and if he meets only one intruder he draws his axe and attacks (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

He is fascinated by minotaurs and keeps them as trophies. One room of the lair holds a stuffed minotaur with a greataxe on a rack in front of it, and the skeleton inside the hide rises and fights for him when anyone else opens the southern door (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). His own quarters are a maze of stacked crates with humanoid bones on the floor, which he gathered in Undermountain, and in one corner he sleeps on a bed made from the skulls, bones, hide and fur of minotaurs (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). A wooden chest beside the bed holds a carved malachite figurine of a minotaur (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The collection began with the horn tip from the hold and with that figurine, the first thing he bought in Skullport, and it is the only item in the chest that he would refuse to sell at any price `[design]`. Gladiatorial procurement lets him buy any minotaur that comes on the market for the pit, and the remains of the ones that die there go to his quarters `[design]`.

He is also good friends with **Ott Steeltoes**, and the two dwarves often drink together (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Ott Steeltoes*, citing *Cloak & Dagger*; edition not tagged) `[research: wiki only]`. Ahmaergo talks about minotaurs and Ott talks about water, and each of them listens with good manners to the other's subject `[design]`. When a Sylgar died in the past, Ahmaergo went to Ott for a replacement, and Ott had one in a bucket by dawn `[design]`. Ahmaergo has noticed that Ott takes the chair nearest the door, and he has chosen not to mention it `[design]`.

**Personal goals.** Ahmaergo wants the hold's roll corrected `[design]`. The entry for the lost crew still reads as a rockfall, and he has drafted the true page, with eleven names, the date and the cause, and carries it folded inside his coat `[design]`. He has not decided how it will reach the counting room, and he does not discuss it `[design]`. He also wants his treatise on the minotaur finished and placed in a library under his own name `[design]`. It runs to more than three hundred pages of measured bones and drawings, and the chapter on the living animal is missing, because every minotaur he has measured was already dead `[design]`. The library has to be one that will shelve it without asking where the author works, and he has not yet found one `[design]`. He would also like Ott to stop sitting nearest the door `[design]`.

## Personality

To the Guild's customers and its lesser members, Ahmaergo is a civil, exact shield dwarf who runs procurement, logistics and discipline with administrative calm. He consults his list before he answers, thanks people for their cooperation, and never raises his voice. He counts the people in a room when he enters it, and he is uneasy until the count matches the list in his hand `[design]`.

Inside the Guild he is devious and corrupt in the manner of a devil, and he is loyal to Xanathar without wavering. He enjoys authority and order, he looks down on anyone weaker than himself, and he treats a punishment as a procedure to be carried out correctly.

His fascination with minotaurs is the one thing that disturbs his calm. He speaks of them with the sudden enthusiasm of a collector and then returns to his usual manner.

## Connections

## Overview

Ahmaergo is the majordomo of Xanathar's Guild, a shield dwarf who handles its supplies, orders and discipline. Most orders issued in the Guild's name are written by him, and he is the most influential member after the beholder. He is civil and exact in his manner, and he expects the same from everyone who deals with him.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Ahmaergo should inspire revulsion with his cruelty and minotaur obsession, wariness with his outward civility, and dark amusement at the absurdity of a dwarf who sleeps in a minotaur-bone bathtub.

**Emotions.** Ahmaergo most often feels smug authority, methodical cruelty, possessive fascination with minotaurs, unflinching obedience to Xanathar, controlled satisfaction in discipline and order, and disdain for anyone weaker than himself.

**Motivations.** Ahmaergo wants to maintain his position as the most influential member of the Guild after Xanathar, expand his minotaur collection, and run the Guild's operations with the efficiency that keeps the beholder's paranoia pointed at others.

**Inspirations.** When playing Ahmaergo, channel Oddjob (*Goldfinger*), Dolores Umbridge (*Harry Potter*), and Buffalo Bill (*Silence of the Lambs*).

### Voice

*The Guild's civil, fussy majordomo, polite as a clerk and cruel as a butcher.*

- **Sound:** A precise, clipped dwarven voice, dry and formal, like a bookkeeper reading a ledger.
- **Sentence shape:** Orderly, administrative sentences: procedures, schedules, disciplinary outcomes. It's polite phrasing throughout, deliberately (a break from `ember-voice` speech), and he never contracts.
- **Word choice:** Bureaucratic cruelty: "disciplinary procedure", "requisition", "regrettable but necessary". He calls Xanathar "the Master" with complete reverence. Minotaurs he discusses with the zeal of a collector.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Profanity is disorderly. Cruelty is his vocabulary instead.
- **Personality in speech:** Smug authority and methodical cruelty. He describes punishments in procedural detail. His obsession with minotaurs surfaces as sudden animated enthusiasm, then he returns to calm.
- **Quirks:** He consults a list before answering. He mentions minotaur anatomy unprompted. He thanks people for their cooperation before hurting them.
- **Signature phrases:** "Procedure must be followed." / "The Master is not to be disturbed." / "Thank you for your cooperation."
- **Never:** He never raises his voice, never questions Xanathar, and never lets disorder pass.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You are not on the list. That is regrettable. State your business, and do not touch the minotaur."
  - *Business:* "Deliveries are to be made at the second bell to the lower stair. Late deliveries are subject to disciplinary procedure."
  - *Under pressure:* "Guards. Procedure four. Thank you for your cooperation."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Ahmaergo is outwardly civil, a shield dwarf who handles logistics, procurement, and discipline with administrative calm. To those in the Guild, he is as devious and corrupt as the worst devil, yet unflinchingly loyal to Xanathar. Deep down, his quarters feature a maze of stacked crates, a stuffed minotaur, skeletal remains arranged as decor, and a bed made from minotaur skulls, bones, hide, and fur. The fascination is total.

**Morale.** In a fight, Ahmaergo commands Guild forces and fights with vicious competence. He does not retreat while Xanathar is present, and he responds to the beholder's screamed orders without hesitation.

**Relationships.** Ahmaergo is Xanathar's majordomo (most orders that bear Xanathar's name were written by Ahmaergo), the Guild's de facto second-in-command, and a shield dwarf whose minotaur obsession fills every corner of his quarters.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> Ahmaergo keeps a private ledger that is separate from the Guild's official records, because Xanathar does not know about every arrangement he makes. The **Redemption** Debt, Ahmaergo's Inventory, rests on that ledger, and the name of the character who owes it stands in the ledger under a column marked *Outstanding*. Xanathar does not distinguish between an asset, a debtor and an enemy once its paranoia is engaged, so the ledger is a risk to Ahmaergo as well as to that character.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM text, with a dry, gentle humor about the minotaur collection in the Background and Personality. The narrator treats Ahmaergo's courtesy as real and his cruelty as routine.
