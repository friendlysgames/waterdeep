# Broxley Fairkettle

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Strongheart halfling innkeeper, lawful good*. Stat block: **Commoner**.
> - **Affiliation:** Trollskull Alley community
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Broxley should inspire endearment with his fatherly fussing, sympathy with his nine-child domestic chaos, and mild exasperation with his relentless guild membership pitch.

**Emotions.** Broxley most often feels cheerful resignation, paternal concern, mild anxiety about inspections, uncomplicated happiness at seeing the tavern occupied, earnest civic duty, and exhaustion he insists is contentment.

**Motivations.** Broxley wants to get the tavern into the Fellowship of Innkeepers before anyone else causes trouble with the guild, keep the North Ward hospitable, and survive raising nine children on a halfling's budget.

**Inspirations.** When playing Broxley, channel Bilbo Baggins (*The Lord of the Rings*), Mr. Weasley (*Harry Potter*), and Ted Lasso (*Ted Lasso*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Broxley is a laid-back, law-abiding halfling with mutton chops and bushy eyebrows who visits too often and talks too much about guild membership. To those he trusts, he is a father of nine who describes his life as difficult but made "just a bitty bit easier" by following the rules. Deep down, he is terrified that the haunted tavern will bring trouble to the alley, and guild oversight is how he manages that fear.

**Morale.** In a fight, Broxley would gather children, lock doors, and send someone for the Watch. He would put himself between a threat and any child present without hesitation.

**Relationships.** Broxley is a representative of the Fellowship of Innkeepers, a father of nine, and a neighbor who remembers the tavern's worst days (the hag incident, the hauntings) and wants to believe its best are ahead.

## Overview

Broxley is the Fellowship of Innkeepers representative for the North Ward — cheerful, civic-minded, and persistent about getting the party's establishment properly registered before something goes wrong with it again.
