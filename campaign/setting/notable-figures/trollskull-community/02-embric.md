# Embric

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Fire genasi weaponsmith, neutral good*. Stat block: **Bandit Captain**.
> - **Affiliation:** Trollskull Alley community
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Trollskull Manor**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Embric should inspire endearment with his passionate mood swings, amusement with his bickering-but-devoted marriage to Avi, and admiration with the quality of his forge work.

**Emotions.** Embric most often feels hot pride, creative frustration, volcanic irritation, sudden tenderness toward Avi, competitive fire, and dramatic satisfaction.

**Motivations.** Embric wants to forge a weapon worthy of his efreet heritage, keep Steam and Steel prosperous alongside Avi, and be recognized as the finest weaponsmith in the North Ward.

**Inspirations.** When playing Embric, channel Gordon Ramsay (*Hell's Kitchen*), Inigo Montoya (*The Princess Bride*), and Zeus (*Hadestown*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Embric is a temperamental fire genasi who runs hot in every sense. To those he trusts, he is fiercely loyal, unexpectedly sentimental, and always the first to offer help when a neighbor's property is damaged. Deep down, he worries that his mood swings make him difficult to love, and Avi's patience baffles him more than it reassures him.

**Morale.** In a fight, Embric would produce flame and reach for whatever blade is cooling on the anvil. He fights with more heat than discipline, pressing forward when he should hold ground.

**Relationships.** Embric is the husband of Avi (co-owner of Steam and Steel), a neighbor of Tally Fellbranch (whose wooden shields he considers inferior to metal), and a member of the Most Careful Order of Skilled Smiths and Metalforgers.

## Overview

Embric co-owns Steam and Steel with his wife Avi, forging weapons in Trollskull Alley with the intensity of someone who takes every piece personally. Loud, quick to anger, and just as quick to offer a neighbor a hand, he is hard to miss and harder to dislike once you know him.
