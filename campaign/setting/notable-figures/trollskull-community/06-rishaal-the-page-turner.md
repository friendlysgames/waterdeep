# Rishaal the Page-Turner

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Gold dragonborn mage, neutral*. Stat block: **Mage**.
> - **Affiliation:** Trollskull Alley community
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Rishaal should inspire endearment with his perpetual ink-stained distraction, admiration with his deep arcane knowledge, and amusement with his tendency to answer questions no one asked.

**Emotions.** Rishaal most often feels scholarly absorption, mild befuddlement at interruptions, private delight at a rare find, collegial warmth, intellectual suspicion, and protective anxiety about his inventory.

**Motivations.** Rishaal wants to maintain the finest arcane bookshop in the North Ward, understand the book whose words rearrange themselves when observed, and determine why Vincent Trench keeps buying texts on planar binding.

**Inspirations.** When playing Rishaal, channel Ollivander (*Harry Potter*), the Librarian (*Discworld*), and Varrick (*The Legend of Korra*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Rishaal is a bookish gold dragonborn with burnished amber-bronze scales and ink-stained hands who appears permanently mid-thought. To those he trusts, he is sharper than he looks, quietly tracking who buys what and drawing conclusions he keeps to himself. Deep down, he suspects at least one of his neighbors is not what they appear, and he is not sure he wants to confirm it.

**Morale.** In a fight, Rishaal would exhaust his spells defending Book Wyrm's Treasure before retreating. He will not abandon his inventory to save himself, and his breath weapon is a last resort he considers undignified.

**Relationships.** Rishaal is a member of the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors, a professional acquaintance of Ulkoria Stonemarrow (whose arcane purchases he respects), and an increasingly suspicious neighbor of Vincent Trench.

## Overview

Rishaal runs Book Wyrm's Treasure, the arcane bookshop across from the tavern, and is the alley's most reliable source on magical texts, rare lore, and anything a wizard might need — if you can get his attention long enough to ask.
