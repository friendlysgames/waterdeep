# Fala Lefaliir

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Wood elf herbalist, chaotic good*. Stat block: **Druid**.
> - **Affiliation:** Trollskull Alley community
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **Ziraj's Last Hunt**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Fala should inspire endearment with their quiet intensity about plants and tea, gratitude with their willingness to share remedies freely, and discomfort with their unblinking stillness when listening.

**Emotions.** Fala most often feels botanical curiosity, protective warmth toward friends, stubborn independence, serene contentment, banked anger at exploitation, and fascination with dangerous things.

**Motivations.** Fala wants to keep Corellon's Crown stocked with rare and useful remedies, protect their friends in the alley without drawing attention, and figure out what keeps moving in the sewer access near the cellar at night.

**Inspirations.** When playing Fala, channel Luna Lovegood (*Harry Potter*), Poison Ivy (*Batman: The Animated Series*), and Chiyo (*Princess Mononoke*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Fala is an outgoing wood elf herbalist with long braided hair who speaks without small talk and has strong opinions about tea. To those they trust, Fala is the person who shelters a Zhentarim assassin because he once saved their life, no questions asked. Deep down, Fala's curiosity runs deeper than their caution, and they know it.

**Morale.** In a fight, Fala would use druid magic to entangle or restrain before resorting to harm. They would die before betraying Ziraj's location to the Watch.

**Relationships.** Fala is a close friend of Ziraj the Hunter (Doom Raiders assassin, who keeps a room on Fala's second floor), a neighbor and confidant of Tally Fellbranch, and a member of the Guild of Apothecaries and Physicians. Fala uses they/them pronouns, reflecting Corellon Larethon's genderless nature.

## Overview

Fala runs Corellon's Crown, an herbalism shop in Trollskull Alley, and is one of the party's most knowledgeable neighbors — unhurried, perceptive, and willing to share cures for ailments most apothecaries won't name.
