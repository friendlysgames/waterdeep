# Talisolvanar "Tally" Fellbranch

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - *Half-elf carpenter, chaotic good*. Stat block: **Commoner**.
> - **Affiliation:** Trollskull Alley community
> - **Featured in:** **Trollskull Alley**, **Fireball!**, **The Doppelganger Auditions**, **The Undercliff Scarecrows**, **The Archer Above**, **The Dragon in the Harbor**, **Trollskull Manor**

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Tally should inspire endearment with his reflexive sarcasm, admiration with his honest craftsmanship, and gratitude with his willingness to help neighbors without being asked.

**Emotions.** Tally most often feels wry amusement, understated pride, neighborly concern, mild irritation at pretension, creative absorption, and protectiveness toward the alley.

**Motivations.** Tally wants to run the best woodworking shop in the North Ward, keep the alley a decent place to live, and make sure his brother Melannor actually visits once in a while.

**Inspirations.** When playing Tally, channel Ron Swanson (*Parks and Recreation*), Samwise Gamgee (*The Lord of the Rings*), and Kaylee Frye (*Firefly*).

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Tally is a sarcastic half-elf who talks more about wood grain than people. To those he trusts, he is generous with his time, his tools, and his honest opinion of your furniture. Deep down, he measures his worth by whether the alley is better for his being in it.

**Morale.** In a fight, Tally would grab whatever sharp tool is nearest and stand between the threat and his shop. He has no combat training but will not abandon the Bent Nail.

**Relationships.** Tally is the brother of Melannor Fellbranch (Emerald Enclave contact at Phaulkonmere), and a longtime neighbor of Fala Lefaliir and Embric.

## Overview

Tally runs the Bent Nail woodworking shop in Trollskull Alley and is one of the party's most reliable neighbors — dry-humored, plainspoken, and quietly generous with tools and labor when something in the alley needs fixing.
