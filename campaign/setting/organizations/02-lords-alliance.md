# The Lords' Alliance

> **[GM]**
>

> - **Campaign role:** Player faction — political operatives committed to recovering the 500,000 stolen dragons for Waterdeep's treasury.
> - **Contacts:** **Jalester Silvermane** (primary); **Laeral Silverhand** (escalation contact — does not deliver missions; reachable through Jalester once PCs have Renown 25+ or the Grand Game reaches a tipping point)

> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!** (Arc C), **Gralhund Villa** (Arc D), **Faction Outposts** (Arc E), **Cassalanter Villa** (Arc G), **Sea Maidens Faire** (Arc H), **Vault of Dragons** (Arc J)

## Operations

*"Civilization is the most fragile thing we have built. We will not let it fall."*

The Lords' Alliance is a coalition of rulers and political operatives from Waterdeep and the allied cities of the North — Neverwinter, Baldur's Gate, Silverymoon, and others — united by the conviction that civilization must be actively defended. In Waterdeep, the Alliance functions as a pro-city intelligence and security apparatus, funding field agents who monitor external and internal threats to the city's stability.

The Alliance's position on the Grand Game is unambiguous: the 500,000 stolen dragons belong to the city. They want the gold recovered and returned. They are also deeply alarmed by Manshoon's activities, which represent an attempt to seize control of Waterdeep's institutions from within. At campaign start, Laeral believes the vault is in Neverwinter and is unaware the Stone has resurfaced — she does not yet know all four villain factions are active. Jalester's Mission 5 (**Fentree's Ledger**) is the mechanism that corrects her intelligence.

The Lords' Alliance is the most demanding faction in terms of professional conduct. Members are expected to behave like agents, not adventurers. Refusing missions has formal consequences — suspension or dismissal.

**Primary Contact:** Jalester Silvermane — a no-nonsense human field agent who reports directly to Open Lord Laeral Silverhand. Professional, precise, direct. Does not enjoy pleasantries and takes the work seriously.

**Secondary Contact:** Laeral Silverhand, Open Lord of Waterdeep — reachable only through Jalester, and only once the PCs have earned sufficient standing (Renown 25+) or the Grand Game has escalated to a level she cannot ignore.

## Key Members

- **Jalester Silvermane** — Cormyrean human field operative; reports directly to Laeral Silverhand; delivers all missions in person. See the **Jalester Silvermane** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Stance

The Lords' Alliance wants the 500,000 gold pieces returned to Waterdeep's city treasury. In Laeral Silverhand's framing: the gold was taxed from Waterdavian citizens and merchants. Lord Neverember's exile does not change its ownership. She will see it recovered, disbursed properly, and will credit those who made the recovery possible — but she will not blink at institutional procedure to get it.

At campaign start, Laeral is three moves behind. Jalester's field reports and Watch contacts have placed the vault somewhere in Neverwinter. She is not yet aware the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced in Waterdeep or that all four villain factions are circling it. PCs who pass her intelligence early change the shape of her involvement significantly. Those who keep her in the dark find her arriving at Arc J as an uncertain variable rather than an ally.

**What the Lords' Alliance shares proactively:**
- Watch patrol schedules for any ward, once per week, for PCs with valid Alliance credentials (Renown 3+)
- The outstanding Thayan warrant on Esloon Bezant, filed three years ago and accessible to Alliance agents (after Mission 3)
- A partial dossier on Cassalanter villa's Watch rotation, compiled after Mission 4's Aftermath delivers the Holmbridge connection

**What the Lords' Alliance asks for:**
- Intelligence on what the villain factions intend to do with the vault gold, not just where it is
- A commitment that the gold will reach Waterdeep's treasury or an approved dispersal — not a single faction's coffers
- The name of whoever is currently holding the Stone of Golorr, whenever the PCs can safely disclose it

## First Meeting

- Jalester finds them himself at the Yawning Portal (or Trollskull if the party rarely goes out); references the Arc A warehouse — he has been watching since then
- Pitch: Alliance needs people who can operate where Watch and Guard cannot; resources and backing in exchange; assignments are not optional — only faction that states this upfront
- No ceremony on acceptance; stands and leaves
- At the door, without turning: "Assignments from us are not optional. If that changes your answer, now is the time to say so." — waits three seconds, then goes

## Renown & Ranks

### Earning Renown

- **Report Grand Game activity to Jalester (+1).** Brief Jalester on any confirmed faction operation, location, or agent. One award per significant piece of intelligence; vague tips do not qualify.
- **Bring the Grand Game to Laeral's attention (+2).** Formally brief Jalester with enough evidence for him to escalate to the Open Lord. One of the most significant acts an Alliance operative can perform — triggers Laeral's direct involvement.
- **Assist the City Watch in a crisis (+1).** Aid the Watch in any situation exceeding their capacity — monsters, organized assault, mind-controlled citizens. Once per incident.
- **Expose or neutralize a Manshoon operative (+2).** Identify, capture, or eliminate a named agent of Manshoon's Zhentarim cell. Delivering a live prisoner for questioning earns the full reward; confirmed elimination earns +1.
- **Prevent an act of large-scale violence in Waterdeep (+1).** Stopping a gang war flashpoint, preventing an assassination of a public figure, or averting a mass-casualty event. Once per significant incident.
- **Recover the stolen dragons and arrange their return to the city (+4).** The Alliance's primary objective. The single highest-value act available to Alliance operatives and will elevate any character directly to the attention of the Open Lord.

## Overview

The Lords' Alliance is a coalition of rulers and operatives from Waterdeep and the allied cities of the North, united by the conviction that civilization must be actively defended. In Waterdeep, the Alliance operates as a pro-city intelligence and security apparatus with direct reach to the Open Lord.

Lords' Alliance PCs who bring the Grand Game to Jalester's attention trigger Laeral's direct involvement. Once Laeral is fully aware, she makes recovering the 500,000 dragons a political priority and extends significant resources to PCs who have proven loyal. The Alliance will not let the party keep the gold — but offers extraordinary renown and political capital in exchange for returning it. Characters who pocket the gold and claim ignorance will find Laeral a thorough investigator.
