# Force Grey

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Player faction. Waterdeep's deniable cadre of elite adventuring agents, commanded by the Blackstaff.
> - **Contacts:** **Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff**
> - **Mechanics:** Grand Game stance, quest hooks, First Meeting, renown, ranks and missions are on the **Force Grey** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Vault of Dragons**
>
> Vajra Safahr communicates by *Sending* spell, and in person at Blackstaff Tower when the situation requires it. She is direct and professional. She cares about Waterdeep and the people in it, but she doesn't perform warmth she doesn't feel. She is younger than the title suggests and does not find this observation interesting.

**Purpose:** Gives the GM Force Grey as an institution: where it came from, who commands it, what it believes, how the Open Lord uses it, who stands with it or against it, and what it keeps out of public view. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

Force Grey is the Open Lord's cadre of elite adventuring agents, commanded by the Blackstaff and sent against arcane threats that the City Watch and the City Guard cannot handle.

*"Waterdeep has enemies it cannot acknowledge. We handle those."*

## History

The campaign and the published adventure give Force Grey no founding date, though the adventure says some past members indulged in wanton violence and caused as much damage as they prevented (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki describes an older cadre under the same name, and the entries below follow that thread to the Force Grey of 1492 DR. Each wiki entry names the page and the edition of the book the wiki cites.

- **1322 DR, the tower gets its name:** Khelben Arunsun renamed his home Blackstaff Tower, which had been Arunsun Tower, and later Blackstaffs inherited it with the title (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Blackstaff Tower*, citing the *City of Splendors* boxed set, 2e).
- **About 1372 DR, the cadre of the Lords:** The wiki describes a small band of about twenty powerful adventurers, sixteen of them human, who answered directly to the Lords of Waterdeep. The ranger Jardwim commanded them in the early 1370s, and Open Lord Piergeiron Paladinson assembled them when the archwizards returned to Faerûn (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e, and the novel *The Siege*). The wiki also gives "the Gray Hands" as a second name for the whole cadre and calls the frost giant Harshnag its longest-serving member by the late 14th century (same page, citing *Heroes' Lorebook*, edition not stated). `[research]` Campaign change: the campaign makes the Gray Hands the entry tier and not a second name, and no campaign file ties Jardwim, Piergeiron or Harshnag to the cadre.
- **1374 DR, the first Blackstaff dies:** Khelben Arunsun, the original Blackstaff, gave his life to raise the hidden city of Rhymanthiin, and Tsarra Chaadren took the staff after him. She died in 1399 DR, and Kyriani Agrivar carried the Blackstaff back to Waterdeep as the third holder (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Blackstaff*, edition not stated). `[research]` Campaign change: the campaign calls Khelben Laeral Silverhand's late husband, keeps his soul in the staff and gives no date for his death. It has no Tsarra, Kyriani or Rhymanthiin.
- **1479 DR, Vajra takes the Blackstaff:** Vajra Safahr became the seventh Blackstaff when her mentor and lover, Samark Dhanzscul, was killed, and she is the youngest person ever to hold the title (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Vajra Safahr*, edition not stated). `[research]` The published adventure puts her in her mid-thirties in 1492 DR, which makes her in her early twenties at the succession, and says Khelben Arunsun handpicked her (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **1479 DR, the rescue:** Renaer Neverember and Meloon Wardragon rescued Vajra from agents of Khondar Naomal. Meloon's page places the rescue more than a decade ago, and Renaer's page says it left the Blackstaff permanently in his debt (**Meloon Wardragon**; **Renaer Neverember**). The year itself is `[research]`.
- **About 1489 DR, Laeral takes the seat:** The Masked Lords voted Dagult Neverember out and Laeral Silverhand became Open Lord, which made her the authority over Force Grey (**A City Watching Itself**). Her spies tell her that Vajra has secretly activated members and sent them on a number of unauthorized missions. Laeral has not confronted Vajra and excuses her silence as a test of Vajra's competence (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **1491 DR, the wiki's dismantling:** The wiki says that by 1491 DR Force Grey had been dismantled for some time, that Mirt and Laeral weighed refounding it, and that it was reformed under Vajra by 1492 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing the novel *Death Masks* and *Dragon Heist*, 5e). Campaign change: no campaign file records a dismantling, and Meloon's page has him an active member who reports to Vajra, so this page treats Force Grey as one continuous body (see Draft Notes).
- **Ches 1, 1492 DR, Force Grey today:** Vajra runs Blackstaff Academy and commands Force Grey from Blackstaff Tower, and the published adventure says she is always looking for new adventurers who bring unusual skills, abilities or spells (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She knows that magical coercion is spreading among Waterdhavians in positions of influence and suspects a link to the gang war. She also knows that the Black Network has split, and she does not know that the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced or that four factions are competing for the vault (**Force Grey** in the Factions Guide).

## Beliefs

- **Deniable and Discreet:** Force Grey's operations are deniable and its membership is discreet. Its value to the Open Lord lies partly in solving a problem without a political incident, so a quiet resolution earns Vajra's regard and flashy violence does not. The deniability reaches Laeral herself, since Vajra sends members on missions the Open Lord has not authorized (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **A Last Resort:** The Open Lord mobilizes Force Grey only when diplomats and the city's other armed forces cannot handle a problem. Some past members indulged in wanton violence and caused as much damage as they prevented (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Lords of Waterdeep dislike the collateral damage that follows the unit, and the unit accepts it because the job is to save as many lives as possible (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e). The wiki adds that members were expected to match or outclass the threat they faced, so that fewer people died than if nobody had been available (same page, citing *Heroes' Lorebook*, edition not stated, and the *City of Splendors* boxed set, 2e).
- **The Open Lord Aims, the Blackstaff Chooses:** The Open Lord sets the goals and parameters whenever the unit is activated, and the Blackstaff hand-picks the team for each assignment (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Vajra holds that power as commander. The wiki says the Blackstaff gave the orders and rarely worked beside the members unless a threat was dire (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *Heroes' Lorebook*, edition not stated).
- **Arcane Threats Come First:** Vajra is the city's designated guardian against arcane threats, and her mandate covers the parts of the city that those threats touch. She wants to hear about a magical threat before it becomes a crisis. The published adventure charges her with using all the magic and resources at her disposal to defend the city (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Appendix B adds that she has a professional interest in abolethic artifacts and in the upper access points of Undermountain, and the Factions Guide calls countering Xanathar's mind-control operations Force Grey's most urgent Grand Game priority.
- **The City Outranks the Score:** Vajra asks which outcome leaves Waterdeep standing and treats the rest as scoring. She plans around a threat she cannot stop and does not stake the plan on the threat's death.
- **Patronage Is Earned:** Her backing cannot be bought or networked into. She tests newcomers against their loyalty to Waterdeep, and she expects a great deal from the people she backs. The published adventure says she sets recruits missions meant to tax their resources and test their loyalty (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Appendix B says the backing gives access to the Tower's resources, legal cover for certain otherwise-illegal activities and the understanding that anyone who moves against a protégé is picking a fight with the Blackstaff.
- **Plain Dealing:** Vajra shares what she learns with people she trusts, because sharing costs her nothing. She asks that the people who report to her not tell her only what she wants to hear, since she has people for that already.
- **Loyalty to the City:** *Dragon Heist* (5e) describes Force Grey's fighting prowess as matched only by its loyalty to the city, and names a willingness to swear oaths to defend Waterdeep, its citizens and its laws with their lives as a mark of a good candidate. The wiki says the older cadre showed its greatest morale in and around Waterdeep (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e).
- **Losses Are Expected:** Force Grey takes the jobs that kill people, and its ranks turn over. The wiki says the older cadre took in new members steadily because so many died (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e). Meloon carries grief for colleagues he has outlived (**Meloon Wardragon**).
- **Enemies Without Names:** Force Grey speakers call the Zhentarim's arcane cell "the Splinter" or "the other cell", because few in the cell know their master's name and outsiders cannot name him. Vajra's people have no name for Jarlaxle's hand in the city, and they do not speak of Jarlaxle or Bregan D'aerthe.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** [research] The campaign files give no oath text, creed or induction ceremony. The published sentence about swearing oaths is the only source for a vow. The wiki records only that the older cadre advised anyone who crossed it to surrender or die (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e).

## Structure

Force Grey is Waterdeep's official cadre of elite adventuring agents, organized and commanded by Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff — the city's most powerful individual wizard and its designated guardian against arcane threats. No campaign file sets its head count. `[research]` The wiki's figure of about twenty members dates to 1372 DR, and the wiki says that cadre admitted only those who showed great prowess in battle or command of mid-level spells (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, 3e). The published adventure says a candidate must first join the Gray Hands, that anyone who has served in the Watch or the Guard is eligible, and that so is anyone willing to swear oaths to defend Waterdeep, its citizens and its laws with their lives (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

**Chain of command:** Vajra answers to Laeral, and Vajra's Notable Figures page calls her a strained subordinate. Only the Open Lord can strip Vajra of her title. The older cadre answered directly to the Lords of Waterdeep (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, 3e). The published adventure gives the power to activate the cadre to the Open Lord alone, and the campaign follows it.

**The Blackstaff:** "Blackstaff" began as a nickname for Khelben Arunsun because of his staff, and after his death it became the title of the master of Blackstaff Tower and of Waterdeep's archmage. Vajra is the seventh holder (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Blackstaff*, edition not stated). The published adventure calls her the High Wizard of Waterdeep and says she rarely makes a decision without consulting the staff, which holds Khelben's spirit (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki says it became customary for each Blackstaff to choose an heir, and no campaign file names Vajra's. `[research]`

**The Gray Hands:** The Gray Hands is the entry tier of Force Grey. Its members train under Vajra's operatives before any are trusted with the cadre's work, and Vajra tests their loyalty to Waterdeep throughout. The tier offers something more immediately valuable than rank, which is the personal patronage of the Blackstaff. Her backing cannot be purchased or networked into. It is earned, and it comes with extraordinary expectations in return. Gray Hands complete the assignments Vajra dictates, and Force Grey is drawn from their ranks (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The titles above the Gray Hand are Junior Griffon, Senior Griffon, Force Grey and Force Grey Commander (**Force Grey** in the Factions Guide). Campaign change: the wiki uses "Gray Hands" for the whole cadre, and the campaign keeps the published usage.

**Headquarters:** Force Grey works out of Blackstaff Tower in the Castle Ward. Vajra also runs Blackstaff Academy there and heads the Watchful Order. The published adventure calls the Tower a fortress and a wizard training academy in one, and the Castle Ward gazetteer says that staring at it for long gives a queasy sense of being watched (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Castle Ward**). The wiki describes a black stone tower of three visible stories with at least a dozen more levels inside, some reachable only by magic (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Blackstaff Tower*, citing the *Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide*, 4e).

**Primary Contact:** Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff — the youngest person ever to hold the title, who took its power at Samark Dhanzscul's death in 1479 DR `[research: Forgotten Realms Wiki, Vajra Safahr]`, and among the most formidable wizards in Waterdeep. Communicates primarily via *sending* spells and is not given to small talk. A genuine ally to people who prove themselves, and a genuinely intimidating authority figure to those who cross her.

**Members:** Vajra and Meloon Wardragon are the two members the campaign names. The wiki's older cadre was a mixed company of barbarians, clerics, fighters, rangers, sorcerers and wizards that had included giants and monstrous humanoids (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, 3e). The Tower also fields mages and veterans for operations (**Force Grey** in the Factions Guide; Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). Several older and more seasoned wizards in Waterdeep consider Vajra an upstart and are smart enough not to challenge her (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

## Methods and Tactics

Its operations are deniable, its membership discreet, and its mandate broad: handle threats the City Watch and City Guard cannot or should not touch.

- **Deployment:** Laeral can command Vajra to unleash Force Grey in times of great need. Until she gives that order, Force Grey is not allowed to operate in Waterdeep. Vajra has secretly sent members on a number of unauthorized missions, and Laeral has not confronted her about it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Open Lord sets the goals, and Vajra picks the team that carries them out.
- **Secrecy:** When a member is arrested, the Open Lord or the Blackstaff usually intervenes and frees the member, and the published adventure says the same can happen for a Gray Hand (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). A move by the Blackstaff against the Watch opens a formal review, so Vajra does not act openly against a magistracy (**Trades Ward**). Force Grey speakers avoid the names they cannot back, as the Beliefs say.
- **How Vajra briefs:** A *Sending* gives her twenty-five words, and she uses all of them. When a situation needs more, she summons the people involved to Blackstaff Tower and tells them at her standing desk, where there is no chair for visitors. The Factions Guide holds how this reaches members.
- **Recruiting:** Vajra invites adventurers who show promise into the Gray Hands (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She contacts them by *Sending* from the Tower (**Castle Ward**). The wiki says the older cadre had a steady intake of new members (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, 3e).
- **Funding and gear:** No campaign file gives Force Grey a payroll or a treasury. `[research]` The wiki says that in 1372 DR the city paid each member 100 gp a month for being available thirty hours a week, that the treasury funded the cadre and the Watchful Order supplied it, and that members paid for their own personal arms (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, 3e). It also says the Blackstaff marked each loaned item so that it returned to the Tower with its wielder after a few days (same page, citing *City System*, edition not stated). The published adventure has the cadre lend rare items that mysteriously disappear later (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Gathering information:** Vajra draws intelligence from her own network of spies and from Harper agents (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). She also scries from Blackstaff Tower and analyzes any artifact, document or creature brought to her (**Force Grey** in the Factions Guide).
- **Fighting:** The wiki says the older cadre approached most problems by brute force, held that damage to private property was never its concern and advised anyone who crossed it to surrender or die (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e). Campaign change: the campaign's Force Grey wins deniably and does not seek the old cadre's notoriety. In a fight Vajra opens with something large enough to end the conversation (**Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff**).
- **Bargaining:** Force Grey pays in patronage and not in coin. It asks for intelligence about magical threats and for plain answers (**Force Grey** in the Factions Guide).
- **What it will not do:** Force Grey does not sell its patronage, and it does not stake a plan on a threat's death. Officially it does not operate in Waterdeep until the Open Lord gives the order, whatever Vajra does in private.

## Relationships

Each entry gives the stance on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

- **The Harpers (neutral):** The published adventure says Vajra draws intelligence from Harper agents as well as her own spies, and Mirt, a senior Harper, is Laeral's closest advisor (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Mirt**). The Harpers still name Force Grey among the powers whose hold on the gold they would limit (**The Harpers**).
- **The Lords' Alliance (ally):** Both answer to the Open Lord, and Vajra passes what she learns of the Grand Game to Laeral. Force Grey wants the Open Lord's outcomes handled without fuss and without the Alliance's politics (**The Lords' Alliance**; **Player Factions Overview** in the GM Guide).
- **The Emerald Enclave (neutral `[research]`):** No source gives a day-one dealing between them. The Enclave wants aberrant threats to the city's water and wildlife dealt with, which overlaps with Force Grey's remit, and nothing says either side has told the other (**Player Factions Overview** in the GM Guide). The wiki's Emerald Enclave is the Vilhon order and is not used.
- **The Order of the Gauntlet (neutral):** The published adventure counts Force Grey and the Order among the respectable factions whose members can plead for leniency before a magistrate (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Vajra has scried residue beneath the Cassalanter villa, and no source says she has told the Order (**The Order of the Gauntlet**).
- **The Doom Raiders (neutral `[research]`):** No source gives a day-one dealing. Vajra knows the Black Network has split, and the Doom Raiders are the half that works openly and that the Watch already keeps a file on (**Force Grey** in the Factions Guide; **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**).
- **Bregan D'aerthe (neutral, unaware):** No source says Force Grey knows the company is in the city. Jarlaxle's goal runs through Laeral, and nobody has named him to her (**Jarlaxle Baenre**; **A City Watching Itself**).
- **Xanathar's Guild (enemy):** The Factions Guide calls countering the Guild's mind-control operations Force Grey's most urgent Grand Game priority, and Vajra suspects that the coercion among Waterdhavians in positions of influence links to the gang war (**Force Grey** in the Factions Guide). The wiki lists the outlaws of Skullport among the older cadre's enemies (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *Dragon* magazine issue 167).
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim (enemy):** Manshoon counts the Blackstaff among his powerful enemies, and the published adventure has him plan to kill her (**Manshoon**; *Dragon Heist*, 5e). Force Grey calls the cell "the Splinter" or "the other cell", and Appendix B says Vajra takes any coordinated attack on the Tower personally.
- **The Cassalanters (neutral, suspicious):** Scrying from Blackstaff Tower has found conjuration and abjuration residue beneath the Cassalanter villa. Vajra cannot say what makes it and does not guess, and nobody in Force Grey knows about the pact (**The Cassalanters** in Villains; **Force Grey** in the Factions Guide).
- **House Gralhund (neutral `[research]`):** No source gives a day-one dealing between the house and Force Grey (**House Gralhund**).
- **The Open Lord's Watch (ally of last resort):** The Open Lord mobilizes Force Grey when diplomats and the city's other armed forces cannot handle a problem, and anyone who has served in the Watch or the Guard may join the Gray Hands (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Open Lord's Watch**). The wiki says the cadre was allied with the city's other law-enforcement groups (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **The Open Lord and the Masked Lords (ally, strained):** Laeral commands Force Grey in principle and excuses Vajra's unauthorized missions, and she covets the staff and thinks of Vajra as an insecure child, so the two avoid each other (**A City Watching Itself**; **Laeral Silverhand**). Ulkoria Stonemarrow, a senior member of the Watchful Order that Vajra heads, is also a Masked Lord in the campaign (**North Ward**).

## Key Members

- **Vajra Safahr** — Tethyrian human archmage, lawful neutral. Blackstaff of Waterdeep, commander of Force Grey. See the **Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Agenda

Force Grey is allied to the Open Lord. Vajra passes whatever she learns of the Grand Game to Laeral Silverhand, who wants the 500,000 dragons that Neverember took returned to the city's coffers. Of all the factions, Force Grey offers the shortest route from a rumor about the Grand Game to the Open Lord's desk.

## Overview

Force Grey is Waterdeep's deniable cadre of elite adventuring agents, commanded by Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff. It handles arcane threats that the City Watch and City Guard cannot or should not touch. Newcomers serve first as Gray Hands, and Vajra tests their loyalty to the city before she trusts them with more.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **The staff:** The Blackstaff holds Khelben Arunsun's soul, and Vajra rarely decides without consulting it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Laeral wants it back, which is part of why the two avoid each other.
> - **Meloon Wardragon:** Meloon is an active member who reports to Vajra, whose life he saved more than a decade ago alongside Renaer. His page is in the Independents and Allies group.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural, like a briefing from Blackstaff Tower. The narrator treats Force Grey as a working arm of the city and shows the damage it causes as a cost the city accepts.
