**Purpose:** Gives the GM Force Grey as an institution: who commands it, how the Open Lord uses it, what it values and what it keeps out of public view. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

Force Grey is the Open Lord's cadre of elite adventuring agents, commanded by the Blackstaff and sent against arcane threats that the City Watch and the City Guard cannot handle.

## Tenets

- **Deniable and Discreet:** Force Grey's operations are deniable and its membership is discreet. Its value to the Open Lord lies partly in solving a problem without a political incident, so a quiet resolution earns Vajra's regard and flashy violence does not.
- **A Last Resort:** The Open Lord mobilizes Force Grey only when diplomats and the city's other armed forces cannot handle a problem. Some past members indulged in wanton violence and caused as much damage as they prevented (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Lords of Waterdeep dislike the collateral damage that follows the unit, and the unit accepts it because the job is to save as many lives as possible (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e).
- **The Open Lord Aims, the Blackstaff Chooses:** The Open Lord sets the goals and parameters whenever the unit is activated, and the Blackstaff hand-picks the team for each assignment (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Vajra holds that power as commander.
- **Arcane Threats Come First:** Vajra is the city's designated guardian against arcane threats, and her mandate covers the parts of the city that those threats touch. She wants to hear about a magical threat before it becomes a crisis.
- **The City Outranks the Score:** Vajra asks which outcome leaves Waterdeep standing and treats the rest as scoring. She plans around a threat she cannot stop and does not stake the plan on the threat's death.
- **Patronage Is Earned:** Her backing cannot be bought or networked into. She tests newcomers against their loyalty to Waterdeep, and she expects a great deal from the people she backs.
- **Plain Dealing:** Vajra shares what she learns with people she trusts, because sharing costs her nothing. She asks that the party not tell her only what she wants to hear, since she has people for that already.
- **Loyalty to the City:** *Dragon Heist* (5e) describes Force Grey's fighting prowess as matched only by its loyalty to the city, and names a willingness to swear oaths to defend Waterdeep, its citizens and its laws with their lives as a mark of a good candidate.
- **Names Come Late:** Force Grey speakers say "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener, and they name Manshoon and Kolat Towers only after that. They never say "Jarlaxle" or "Bregan D'aerthe" to players.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** [research] The campaign files give no oath text, creed or induction ceremony. The published sentence about swearing oaths is the only source.

## Operations

*"Waterdeep has enemies it cannot acknowledge. We handle those."*

Force Grey is Waterdeep's official cadre of elite adventuring agents, organized and commanded by Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff — the city's most powerful individual wizard and its designated guardian against arcane threats. Its operations are deniable, its membership discreet, and its mandate broad: handle threats the City Watch and City Guard cannot or should not touch.

Characters who join the Gray Hands — the entry tier of Force Grey — are not yet full Force Grey operatives. Vajra is testing them. What they earn in the meantime is something more immediately valuable: the personal patronage of the Blackstaff. Her backing is not something that can be purchased or networked into. It is earned — and it comes with extraordinary expectations in return.

**Primary Contact:** Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff — the youngest person ever to hold the title, who took its power at Samark Dhanzscul's death in 1479 DR `[research: Forgotten Realms Wiki, Vajra Safahr]`, and among the most formidable wizards in Waterdeep. Communicates primarily via *sending* spells and is not given to small talk. A genuine ally to characters who prove themselves; a genuinely intimidating authority figure to those who cross her.

## Key Members

- **Vajra Safahr** — Tethyrian human archmage, lawful neutral. Blackstaff of Waterdeep, commander of Force Grey. See the **Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Agenda

Force Grey is allied to the Open Lord. If Force Grey members bring the Grand Game to Vajra's attention, she will inform Laeral Silverhand — and Laeral will move quickly to reclaim the 500,000 dragons. This is the most certain path to the gold returning to the city's coffers and to the party earning institutional gratitude rather than institutional pursuit. Characters who want legitimacy as their reward — a clean name, political standing, the city's formal thanks — should invest in this relationship above all others.

## Overview

Force Grey is Waterdeep's deniable cadre of elite adventuring agents, commanded by Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff. It handles arcane threats that the City Watch and City Guard cannot or should not touch. Newcomers serve first as Gray Hands, and Vajra tests their loyalty to the city before she trusts them with more.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Standing:** Laeral can command Vajra to unleash Force Grey in times of great need. Until she gives that order, Force Grey is not allowed to operate in Waterdeep. Vajra has secretly sent members on a number of unauthorized missions, and Laeral has not confronted her about it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Vajra admits as much at **Gralhund Villa**, where she says she cannot authorize an entry legally and promises only to speak to the Open Lord afterward.
> - **Chain of command:** Vajra answers to Laeral, and Vajra's Notable Figures page calls her a strained subordinate. Only the Open Lord can strip Vajra of her title.
> - **Base:** Force Grey works out of Blackstaff Tower in the Castle Ward. Vajra also runs Blackstaff Academy there and heads the Watchful Order.
> - **Origins:** The wiki describes a small band of about twenty elite adventurers answering to the Lords of Waterdeep around 1372 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e). It has the unit dismantled for some time by 1491 DR, with Mirt and Laeral weighing its revival, and reformed under Vajra by 1492 DR (same page, citing the novel *Death Masks* and *Dragon Heist*, 5e).
> - **Meloon Wardragon:** Meloon is an active member who reports to Vajra, whose life he saved more than a decade ago alongside Renaer. His page is in the Independents and Allies group, and **The Trouble with Meloon** concerns what has taken hold of him.
> - **Who suspects the Cassalanters:** Scrying from Blackstaff Tower has found conjuration and abjuration residue beneath the Cassalanter villa. Vajra cannot say what makes it and does not guess, and nobody in Force Grey knows about the pact.
**Tone:** Plain and procedural, like a briefing from Blackstaff Tower. The narrator treats Force Grey as a working arm of the city and shows the damage it causes as a cost the city accepts.
