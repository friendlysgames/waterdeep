# The Emerald Enclave

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Player faction. Druids and rangers who watch Waterdeep's natural balance and take no side in the vault gold.
> - **Contacts:** **Melannor Fellbranch** (primary); **Jeryth Phaulkon** (patron spirit, present and aware, and not the one who sends the Enclave's messages)
> - **Mechanics:** the Grand Game Stance, quest hooks, First Meeting, renown, ranks, missions and mission delivery are on the **Emerald Enclave** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Vault of Dragons**

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the Emerald Enclave as a small Waterdeep presence: where it came from, what its members believe, how it is built, how it works, who it deals with and what it senses without being able to name it. The page presents the Enclave as it stands on Ches 1, 1492 DR and keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers and survivalists who keep civilization and the wild in balance, and its Waterdeep presence is a small group around one garden estate in the Southern Ward.

## History

The founding of the Waterdeep presence is unsourced, because no campaign file or published text gives a year for it. [research] The entries below give each dated fact that exists and mark what is missing.

- **374 to 1486 DR, the Vilhon order (a parallel only):** The wiki's Emerald Enclave is an order of druids on the island of Ilighôn in the Vilhon Reach. It dates the order to a shrine of Silvanus raised in 374 DR and says the shrine's druids joined the Enclave in 717 DR, the year the Enclave beat a Turmishan army and fleet. After the Spellplague of 1385 DR the order turned xenophobic under the increasingly mad Cindermoon, and a Chosen of Lathander cured her in 1486 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, citing *The Vilhon Reach* and *The Reaver*, edition not stated). No campaign file ties the Waterdeep Enclave to this order, so the page uses it only as a parallel.
- **Undated, Phaulkonmere:** The Tarm and Phaulkon families own the estate, whose garden is older than the street around it. No file dates when the Enclave first used the grounds. [research]
- **1367 DR, Jeryth's calling (wiki only):** The wiki has Mielikki appear in a dream to Jeryth Phaulkon, a noble of Waterdeep, and offer to make her a Chosen. Jeryth was then a captive of the Hoar Fang gnolls, whom the goddess wanted stopped before they laid waste to the Lurkwood. With the goddess's power Jeryth freed herself and her companions, met the Harper Bran Skorlsun and defeated the gnolls (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mielikki*, citing the *City of Splendors* boxed set, 2e). No campaign file repeats this account or dates her calling.
- **About 1480 DR, the three routes:** Enclave druids find three routes that follow channels older than Waterdeep's sewers and run to Level 1 of Undermountain. They have kept the routes clear since and have walked them no deeper than that level, and no Watch patrol or faction knows they exist. The date counts back the twelve years that the campaign gives.
- **Several years before 1492 DR, the voice in the garden:** Mielikki asks something of Jeryth, and she agrees and takes up her place as a disembodied voice in the gardens of Phaulkonmere (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). Design change: *Dragon Heist* calls her a "noblewoman-turned-demigod and Chosen of Mielikki", and the campaign keeps Chosen and drops demigod. [research] No file says whether this request is the same event as the wiki's calling in 1367 DR.
- **About 1488 DR, the harbor watcher:** Sarna Dath, a fisher on the south quay, begins to send the Enclave her notes on the harbor, and she is the only one of its watchers who knows it by name. The date counts back the four years that the campaign gives.
- **Late 15th century DR, no year given:** The wiki names Delaan Winterhound, a chief ranger of the Enclave, as its delegate at the Council of Waterdeep and says the Enclave joined other factions against a Cult of the Dragon plot to bring Tiamat back (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, citing *The Rise of Tiamat*, edition not stated). The campaign names no delegate and mentions the Council only in connection with Dagult Neverember. [research] Whether the Waterdeep presence sat on it is unsourced.
- **Months before Ches 1, 1492 DR, the dreamer:** Jeryth begins to feel a dreaming presence in the earth below the Castle Ward and holds it back from the ward's water table. Melannor senses that something is wrong there and cannot say what it is, and Jeryth does not know the presence's name.
- **By Ches 1, 1492 DR, the wound:** Jeryth's reports place a wound in the ley lines near Coachlamp Lane that is not healing, and she senses infernal influence there. The Enclave has no lead on the cause, and the sources disagree on how long the wound has lasted, so the page gives no figure. [research]

## Beliefs

*"The city is built on nature. We remember that."*

Appendix B gives the motto in full, and it ends with the line "It would be good if everyone did" (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B).

- **Keep the Balance:** The Enclave works to keep civilization and the wild in balance, and its motto holds that the city is built on nature and that the Enclave remembers it. Melannor tells recruits that balance is a better word than nature, because what the Enclave watches for is disruption: things that should not be where they are, whether in a forest, in a city or in a sewer beneath one. The Vilhon order's Elder Circle held that the order of nature must be preserved in all its forms and that any force which disrupted it had to be defeated (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, citing *Baldur's Gate III*, edition not stated).
- **Aberrant Corruption Draws Its Attention:** A beholder crime lord building an empire in the sewers is exactly the kind of corruption the Enclave cannot ignore. The same goes for grells in the harbor, chuul in the cisterns and the dead walking in a cemetery, and for anyone who pollutes the harbor or the sewers, whether or not the Enclave was asked to act. Appendix B counts intellect devourers implanted in citizens, the monsters Xanathar's Guild lets loose and undead infestations in the City of the Dead as threats to the living fabric of Waterdeep (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B).
- **Gentler Remedies First:** The Enclave would rather return a dangerous or corrupted animal to safety than kill it. Mielikki, the power Jeryth serves, is said to have healed the hurt animals she found where Silvanus would leave them to the circle of life (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mielikki*, 2e and 3e). The Vilhon order of the same name warned wrongdoers first and then stopped them by any means (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, edition not stated), but no campaign file gives the Waterdeep Enclave a rule of warning first.
- **No Side in the Vault Gold:** The Enclave has no stake in Neverember's gold, and its aid carries no hidden price. Jeryth answers every question about the hoard the same way, "That question is for the Lords to settle", and she names no mortal politics. Appendix B says the Enclave holds no position on the Grand Game, and *Player Character Factions* (printed page 24) says it has no interest in Neverember's dragons.
- **Help Without Favors:** Its help is personal and magical rather than institutional, and it goes to those who prove loyal. Jeryth tells new members that the refuge carries no favors owed and asks only that they pay attention to what is being disrupted and act on what they see (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). Melannor puts the offer as honest work and the knowledge that it matters, together with a garden to rest in when the rest of Waterdeep becomes too much. The Vilhon order likewise gave aid to anyone left fatigued or injured (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, citing *Baldur's Gate III*, edition not stated).
- **The Sanctuary Holds:** Phaulkonmere is a sacred space under Jeryth's protection and the one place in its ward that the Enclave defends, and she guards it with spells from the druid list (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). No faction has ever openly violated it.
- **Name Only What It Knows:** Enclave speakers say "the Splinter" or "the other cell" for the Zhentarim they have noticed, and they speak of the Cassalanters as no more than a rich house. Melannor describes the Splinter as the other cell of an old network, working in the city, and says he does not know who leads it. The GM Notes list who they name.
- **Say What Was Seen:** The Enclave's informants tell Melannor what they notice, and he passes it on without guessing at what it means. He carries Jeryth's few words as she spoke them and adds only the urgency, which is his own. Sarna Dath writes what she sees from her boat and the quay and nothing she did not see.
- **Hold the Line Below:** Jeryth's concern is something old and patient that she senses dreaming beneath the city, and she holds it back from the Castle Ward's water table. She wants whatever wakes below handled before it reaches the surface. Melannor feels the same unease in the earth beneath the Castle Ward and cannot say what it is.
- **The Lady of the Forest:** The only power the Waterdeep Enclave names is Mielikki, and only through Jeryth, her Chosen, who calls her "the Lady of the Forest" whenever she speaks of her. No Enclave file names Silvanus, Eldath or any other power of the wild. Heroes of Faerûn says Mielikki's priests use magic to speak with and defend the animals and plants of the forests, and *Dragon Heist* (5e) has Enclave members share what they learn from magical conversations with animals in the city. The Vilhon order's members mostly venerated Eldath, Mielikki or Silvanus (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, citing *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e).
- **Doctrine and Rites:** [research] The campaign files do not settle how the Waterdeep Enclave admits members beyond the white cat's invitation and the open gate, whether it keeps an oath or a hierarchy of its own, or what its members call one another. The sources give it five rank titles, which are listed under Structure, and nothing on a creed in writing. The Vilhon order swore an oath to protect nature, taught its members a private language called druidspeak, held initiations at each full moon in the Oakenhall on Ilighôn and wore something green (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, edition not stated), and that is a different organization.

## Structure

- **Size and standing:** The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers, and survivalists who work to keep civilization and the wild in balance. Its presence in Waterdeep is small, because the city is no natural ground for it. Six rangers and druids are its working strength in the city. They keep its parks, sewers and harbor margins and have worked for it for many years, and none has been deeper than Level 1 of Undermountain.
- **The six:** Sorrel Hartwick (Neutral Good, Chondathan Human, she/her) is a druid in her fifties who leads them. Ealdra Hollin, a half-elf, and Tavish Merrow, an Illuskan human, are druids. Naerith Evenmoor, a wood elf, Rolan Dace, a Tethyrian human, and Hob Lathen, a Chondathan human, are rangers.
- **Leadership:** Jeryth is the highest Enclave authority in Waterdeep, and Melannor serves her and carries her words to the Enclave's members. No file names anyone above her or says whether the Waterdeep presence answers to a council elsewhere. [research] The Vilhon order was governed by an Elder Circle of three over seven circles of members (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, edition not stated), and the campaign gives the Waterdeep presence nothing like it.
- **Ranks and titles:** Members hold one of five titles, from lowest to highest: Springwarden, Summerstrider, Autumnreaver, Winterstalker and Master of the Wild (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, citing the *Dungeon Master's Guide*, 5e). *Dragon Heist* confirms that the *Dungeon Master's Guide* supplies the Enclave's rank names (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Phaulkonmere:** Its Waterdeep presence centers on Phaulkonmere, an old estate and garden in the Southern Ward. The gate stands open to anyone the Enclave has invited, and the garden is older than the street around it. No faction has ever openly violated the sanctuary. A walled villa stands among old oaks, its buildings covered with moss and ivy, and the manor has gathered wings and rooms over many generations (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). The oldest oak stands at the center, and every path in the garden bends back to it.
- **Primary Contact:** Melannor Fellbranch, groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere and a Half-Elf druid. He is friendly but humorless, and he reports grim news in the tone of a man describing the weather. His messages travel by *Animal Messenger* and never run longer than twenty-five words. He chooses the animal himself, usually a cat, a pigeon, a crow or a falcon, and he appears in person only when the situation demands it.
- **Secondary Contact:** Jeryth Phaulkon, the lady of the Phaulkonmere estate. She was a noblewoman before she became a Chosen of Mielikki, and for several years she has lived as a disembodied voice in the gardens. She cannot be harmed in this state, and she can cast any druid spell. She sends no one out on tasks, and the animals carry only Melannor's words.
- **The messengers:** The animal speaks its message once, in Melannor's low voice, and anyone nearby can hear it. *Dragon Heist* (5e) calls him partial to cats and pigeons as couriers.
- **The watchers:** The Enclave's informants include gardeners at the City of the Dead, among them the druid Sorrel Hartwick, who has tended the margins of the grounds for twenty years. The rest are park wardens near the Field of Triumph and two fisherfolk on the south quay, and most of them do not know they report to a druid. Sarna Dath is the one watcher who knows the Enclave by name.
- **Holdings beyond the estate:** The Enclave holds no other property that the files name. Its three routes to Undermountain begin in the Sea Ward, the Southern Ward and the Dock Ward and end on Level 1. *Dragon Heist* (5e) also places Enclave members at the Snobeedle Orchard and Meadery in Undercliff, which Blossom Snobeedle, an old halfling druid, runs. [research] No campaign file says whether Blossom belongs to the Enclave.

## Methods and Tactics

- **Recruitment:** The Enclave seeks out naturalists, healers and people who have shown concern for the natural world, and *Dragon Heist* (5e) names clerics of nature, druids and rangers as the ones it welcomes. It sends a white cat that speaks an invitation in a man's voice, naming Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward and promising that the gate will be open (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). Anyone may walk to the estate whether or not the cat chose them. The Enclave takes no member who answers to another cause, and a member may refuse any task that Melannor sends.
- **Information:** It still watches anything that upsets the balance of the region, and a beholder crime lord building an empire in the sewers is exactly the aberrant corruption that draws its attention. Enclave members share what they learn from magical conversations with animals in the city (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Jeryth reads the city through the roots and the groundwater of her garden, and much of her attention goes to the water under the city. She also keeps a survey of aberrant activity and monitors minor ley lines, one of which runs beneath Gralhund Villa.
- **Aid:** Its help is personal and magical rather than institutional, and it is given freely to those who prove loyal. That makes it an unusually clean patron in a city where every other faction is pulling in a different direction. The garden is a refuge with no favors owed, and Melannor gives free food and care to the animals of those who visit (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B; *Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **The vault gold:** The Enclave has no stake in Neverember's gold and makes no demands about the Vault or its contents. Jeryth Phaulkon holds that the question belongs to the Lords, and the Enclave follows her.
- **Fighting:** Jeryth does not fight and protects the garden instead, and when she says "Enough", every vine in it tightens. Melannor reshapes the ground with druid magic, raising roots through flagstones and fog from dry air, and he puts himself between a threat and anyone who cannot run. The six fight as druids and rangers, with the druids holding a corridor with roots and thunder while the rangers shoot, scout and find the road. They withdraw to the nearest safe exit if two of them fall, and they do not fight the Watch or kill a captive.
- **What it will not do:** The Enclave will not hide the gold, seize it or help anyone who does. It shelters no one from a lawful warrant, and Melannor answers a Watch officer who holds one truthfully. It takes no side in mortal politics and keeps the names of its watchers to itself.
- **Funding:** [research] No file says how the Enclave pays for itself or for its watchers.

## Relationships

- **The Harpers (neutral, friendly):** The wiki links the two through Mielikki, who inspired the founding of the Harpers in 720 DR and is followed by many of them (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mielikki*, 2e and 3e), and it has Jeryth meet the Harper Bran Skorlsun in 1367 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Mielikki*, 2e). [research] No campaign file gives a day-one dealing between the two.
- **The Lords' Alliance (neutral):** Laeral uses the Alliance as her instrument outside the Palace (**A City Watching Itself**), and Jeryth leaves the question of the vault gold to "the Lords", though no source gives a day-one dealing between them. [research]
- **The Order of the Gauntlet (neutral, shared concerns):** The Order hunts diabolists first and aberrant evil second, and the Enclave watches aberrant corruption and senses infernal influence in the Southern Ward. No source has either share what it knows with the other. [research]
- **Force Grey (neutral):** Force Grey is allied to the Open Lord and passes what it learns of the Grand Game to Laeral Silverhand, while the Enclave takes no side in the Grand Game, and no source gives a day-one dealing between them. [research]
- **The Doom Raiders (neutral):** Both keep a presence in the Southern Ward, the Raiders in a boarding house and among the ward's halfling informants. The Vilhon order counted the Zhentarim's mages among its enemies (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, edition not stated), but the campaign gives the Waterdeep Enclave no quarrel with this cell, and no source gives a day-one dealing between them. [research]
- **Bregan D'aerthe (neutral, unaware):** The Enclave does not know Jarlaxle, Zardoz Zord or Bregan D'aerthe by name, and its speakers never use those names. Its harbor watcher reports the ships she sees and cannot say who owns them.
- **Xanathar's Guild (enemy, watched and not fought):** Melannor calls the beholder beneath the city a problem that has not been solved, because creatures of its kind belong to no natural order and the Guild that serves it digs wherever it likes. Appendix B has the beholder expanding its territory for twenty years (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). [research] No source says the Enclave plans to move against it.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim (rival, at a distance):** Phaulkonmere stands one block south of Kolat Towers (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Melannor describes the Splinter as the other cell of an old network that works in the city and says he does not know who leads it. The Vilhon order opposed Zhentarim mages for fear that they would abuse the Art (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, edition not stated).
- **The Cassalanters (neutral, suspicious at most):** Jeryth senses infernal influence in the Southern Ward, and the Enclave does not know whose it is. Its speakers share the city's suspicion that the family is too nice to be this rich, and they call it a rich house and nothing worse.
- **House Gralhund (neutral):** The Enclave monitors a minor ley line beneath the villa as part of Jeryth's survey of aberrant activity, and it has no known dealings with the family.
- **The Open Lord's Watch (neutral):** *Dragon Heist* names the Enclave among the respectable factions whose standing helps a character who pleads for leniency before a magistrate (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). [research] No source gives a day-one dealing between the Enclave and the Watch, which agrees with **The Open Lord's Watch**.
- **The Open Lord and the Masked Lords (neutral, deferential):** Jeryth sends every question about the vault gold to "the Lords", and the Enclave follows her on it. [research] The files do not say which Lords she means, and the page reads the phrase as the Open Lord and the Masked Lords.

## Key Members

- **Melannor Fellbranch** — Half-Elf druid; groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere; primary Enclave contact, who sends the Enclave's messages by *Animal Messenger*. See the **Melannor Fellbranch** page in Notable Figures.
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** — Disembodied presence, Chosen of Mielikki; patron of Phaulkonmere; highest Enclave authority in Waterdeep. See the **Jeryth Phaulkon** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Agenda

The Emerald Enclave has no interest in the gold, and its aid carries no hidden price. Jeryth gives the same answer to anyone who asks: "That question is for the Lords to settle." The Enclave will not hide the gold, seize it, or help anyone who does. Its one stake lies below the city, and Jeryth wants whatever dreams beneath the Castle Ward held back from the surface. Anyone who needs a refuge, a reliable spellcaster, or one relationship in the city that is not angling for something will find the Enclave invaluable in a city where everyone else wants something.

## Overview

The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers and survivalists who work to keep civilization and the wild in balance. Its presence in Waterdeep is small, centered on Phaulkonmere, an old estate and garden in the Southern Ward. The gate stands open to anyone the Enclave has invited, and no faction has ever openly violated the sanctuary. Melannor Fellbranch keeps the grounds and sends its messages, and invited visitors hear Jeryth Phaulkon speak from the leaves.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Phaulkonmere:** The Tarm and Phaulkon families own the estate and spend most of the year abroad, and only Jeryth lives there now. *Dragon Heist* (5e) calls the Phaulkons of Cormyrean descent and the Tarms longtime Waterdhavians.
> - **Jeryth:** The campaign calls her a Chosen of Mielikki, and this page follows it. *Dragon Heist* and Appendix B call her a demigod as well. She tells recruits that the estate was her family's and that Mielikki asked something of her several years ago, and she said yes.
> - **The infernal influence:** Jeryth senses an infernal influence in the Southern Ward, and the Enclave knows it is there. Her reports place a wound in the ley lines near Coachlamp Lane that is not healing. The cause is the Cassalanters' Converted Windmill, and Jeryth does not know it.
> - **What the Enclave does not know:** Nobody in the Enclave knows whose the influence is or that the Cassalanters have a pact. Toward the family the Enclave shares only the city's general suspicion, and its speakers call the Cassalanters a rich house and nothing worse.
> - **The Splinter and the towers:** Phaulkonmere stands one block south of Kolat Towers. Enclave speakers do not name the Splinter's leader and say only "the Splinter", and they do not point toward the towers.
> - **Who the Enclave names:** The Enclave does not know Jarlaxle, Zardoz Zord or Bregan D'aerthe by name. Its harbor watcher, Sarna Dath, reports what she sees from her boat and the quay, and she does not know who owns the ships she writes about.
> - **The wiki's Enclave:** The Forgotten Realms Wiki describes the Emerald Enclave as an older order based on the island of Ilighôn in the Vilhon Reach, and it names Delaan Winterhound as the order's delegate at the Council of Waterdeep in the late 15th century DR (*The Rise of Tiamat*, edition not stated). The campaign names no such delegate and does not say whether the Waterdeep presence belongs to that order, so the History, Beliefs and Relationships sections use the wiki as a tagged parallel only.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The dreamer:** Jeryth has felt a dreaming presence below the Castle Ward for months and has held it back from the ward's water table. She does not know its name. It is Illuun, an aboleth on Undermountain Level 4, and the Stone of Golorr woke it. Enclave speakers say only that something dreams below.
> - **The name:** Nobody in the Enclave knows the name, and Jeryth has never spoken it. The Enclave has only her word that something dreams below.

**Tone:** Unhurried and plain in the public lines, watchful in the GM Notes and grave in the Secret. The Enclave is never preachy, and the narrator never makes its reserve a joke. Every wiki claim carries its edition tag, so the GM can see what the campaign says and what the wiki adds.
