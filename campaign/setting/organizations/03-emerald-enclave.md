# The Emerald Enclave

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Player faction. Druids and rangers who watch Waterdeep's natural balance and take no side in the vault gold.
> - **Contacts:** **Melannor Fellbranch** (primary); **Jeryth Phaulkon** (patron spirit, present and aware, not a mission dispatcher)
> - **Mission delivery:** *Animal Messenger*. A Tiny Beast finds the recipient, speaks Melannor's message once in his baritone, and leaves.
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Vault of Dragons**
>
> Melannor casts *Animal Messenger* and chooses the animal himself, usually a cat, a pigeon, a crow or a falcon. The creature speaks in his low voice, and anyone nearby can hear it. He appears in person only when the situation demands it.

## Operations

*"The city is built on nature. We remember that."*

The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers, and survivalists who work to keep civilization and the wild in balance. Its presence in Waterdeep is small, because the city is no natural ground for it. It still watches anything that upsets the balance of the region, and a beholder crime lord building an empire in the sewers is exactly the aberrant corruption that draws its attention.

The Enclave has no stake in Neverember's gold and makes no demands about the Vault or its contents. Jeryth Phaulkon holds that the question belongs to the Lords, and the Enclave follows her. Its help is personal and magical rather than institutional, and it is given freely to those who prove loyal. That makes it an unusually clean patron once every other faction is pulling in a different direction.

Its Waterdeep presence centers on Phaulkonmere, an old estate and garden in the Southern Ward. The gate stands open to anyone the Enclave has invited, and the garden is older than the street around it. No faction has ever openly violated the sanctuary.

**Primary Contact:** Melannor Fellbranch, groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere and a Half-Elf druid. He is friendly but humorless, and he reports grim news in the tone of a man describing the weather. His messages travel by *Animal Messenger* and never run longer than twenty-five words.

**Secondary Contact:** Jeryth Phaulkon, the lady of the Phaulkonmere estate. She was a noblewoman before she became a Chosen of Mielikki, and for several years she has lived as a disembodied voice in the gardens. She cannot be harmed in this state, and she can cast any druid spell. She sends no one on missions, and the animals carry only Melannor's words.

## Key Members

- **Melannor Fellbranch** — Half-Elf druid; groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere; primary Enclave contact, who sends the Enclave's messages by *Animal Messenger*. See the **Melannor Fellbranch** page in Notable Figures.
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** — Disembodied presence, Chosen of Mielikki; patron of Phaulkonmere; highest Enclave authority in Waterdeep. See the **Jeryth Phaulkon** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Agenda

The Emerald Enclave has no interest in the gold, and its aid carries no hidden price. Jeryth gives the same answer to anyone who asks: "That question is for the Lords to settle." The Enclave will not hide the gold, seize it, or help anyone who does. Characters who need a refuge, a reliable spellcaster, or one relationship in the city that is not angling for something will find the Enclave invaluable in the campaign's later acts.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jeryth has felt a dreaming presence below the Castle Ward for months and does not know its name. It is Illuun, an aboleth on Undermountain Level 4, and the Stone of Golorr woke it. Enclave speakers say only that something dreams below.
> - The name reaches an Enclave member through **Anchor Destroyed**, because Jeryth learns it when the anchor breaks, or from the Stone at Full Awakening. **Illuun Contact** alone gives the presence, not the name. Jeryth says "old, patient and hungry" in **The Water Table Stirs** and nowhere else.
> - The foul water in **The Fouled Channel** is residue from the Splinter's thrall-conditioning, the alchemy that keeps its mind-controlled servants docile. Imre Daskov brews it. When Jeryth's cutting opens a conduit through that water, Illuun uses it to push a fragment of itself toward her, and **The Dreamer's Reach** follows.
> - Phaulkonmere stands one block south of Kolat Towers. Until **Manshoon Named** is marked for a member, Enclave speakers say "the Splinter" and never point toward the towers.
> - Nobody in the Enclave knows of the Cassalanters' pact. The Enclave shares the city's general suspicion of the family, and no more.
> - No Enclave speaker names Jarlaxle, Zardoz Zord, or Bregan D'aerthe to players. Its harbor watcher, Sarna Dath, speaks of the carnival fleet and the *Eyecatcher*'s crew.

## Overview

The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers, and survivalists dedicated to balance between civilization and the wild. Its Waterdeep presence centers on Phaulkonmere, a sanctuary in the Southern Ward that no faction will openly violate, where Melannor Fellbranch sends the Enclave's messages and Jeryth Phaulkon watches over the gardens.
