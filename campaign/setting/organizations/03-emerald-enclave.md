# The Emerald Enclave

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Player faction. Druids and rangers who watch Waterdeep's natural balance and take no side in the vault gold.
> - **Contacts:** **Melannor Fellbranch** (primary); **Jeryth Phaulkon** (patron spirit, present and aware, and not the one who sends the Enclave's messages)
> - **Mechanics:** the Grand Game Stance, quest hooks, First Meeting, renown, ranks, missions and mission delivery are on the **Emerald Enclave** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Vault of Dragons**

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the Emerald Enclave as a small Waterdeep presence: who it is in public, what its members believe, who speaks for it, and what it senses without being able to name it. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers and survivalists who keep civilization and the wild in balance, and its Waterdeep presence is a small group around one garden estate in the Southern Ward.

## Tenets

- **Keep the Balance:** The Enclave works to keep civilization and the wild in balance, and its motto holds that the city is built on nature and that the Enclave remembers it. Melannor tells recruits that balance is a better word than nature, because what the Enclave watches for is disruption: things that should not be where they are, whether in a forest, in a city or in a sewer beneath one.
- **Aberrant Corruption Draws Its Attention:** A beholder crime lord building an empire in the sewers is exactly the kind of corruption the Enclave cannot ignore. The same goes for grells in the harbor, chuul in the cisterns and the dead walking in a cemetery, and for anyone who pollutes the harbor or the sewers, whether or not the Enclave was asked to act.
- **Gentler Remedies First:** The Enclave would rather return a dangerous or corrupted animal to safety than kill it. The Vilhon order of the same name warned wrongdoers first and then stopped them by any means (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, edition not stated), but no campaign file gives the Waterdeep Enclave a rule of warning first.
- **No Side in the Vault Gold:** The Enclave has no stake in Neverember's gold, and its aid carries no hidden price. Jeryth answers every question about the hoard the same way, "That question is for the Lords to settle", and she names no mortal politics.
- **Help Without Favors:** Its help is personal and magical rather than institutional, and it goes to those who prove loyal. Jeryth tells new members that the refuge carries no favors owed and asks only that they pay attention to what is being disrupted and act on what they see (Appendix B). The Vilhon order likewise gave aid to anyone left fatigued or injured (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, citing *Baldur's Gate III*, edition not stated).
- **The Sanctuary Holds:** Phaulkonmere is a sacred space under Jeryth's protection and the one place in its ward that the Enclave defends. No faction has ever openly violated it.
- **Name Only What It Knows:** Enclave speakers say "the Splinter" or "the other cell" for the Zhentarim they have noticed, and they speak of the Cassalanters as no more than a rich house. The GM Notes list who they name.
- **The Lady of the Forest:** The only power the Waterdeep Enclave names is Mielikki, and only through Jeryth, her Chosen, who calls her "the Lady of the Forest" whenever she speaks of her. No Enclave file names Silvanus, Eldath or any other power of the wild. The Vilhon order's members mostly venerated Eldath, Mielikki or Silvanus (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, citing *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e).
- **Doctrine and Rites:** [research] The campaign files do not settle how the Waterdeep Enclave admits members beyond the animal messages Melannor sends and the open gate, whether it keeps an oath or a hierarchy of its own, or what its members call one another. The Vilhon order held initiations at each full moon in the Oakenhall on Ilighôn (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, edition not stated), and that is a different organization.

## Operations

*"The city is built on nature. We remember that."*

The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers, and survivalists who work to keep civilization and the wild in balance. Its presence in Waterdeep is small, because the city is no natural ground for it. It still watches anything that upsets the balance of the region, and a beholder crime lord building an empire in the sewers is exactly the aberrant corruption that draws its attention.

The Enclave has no stake in Neverember's gold and makes no demands about the Vault or its contents. Jeryth Phaulkon holds that the question belongs to the Lords, and the Enclave follows her. Its help is personal and magical rather than institutional, and it is given freely to those who prove loyal. That makes it an unusually clean patron in a city where every other faction is pulling in a different direction.

Its Waterdeep presence centers on Phaulkonmere, an old estate and garden in the Southern Ward. The gate stands open to anyone the Enclave has invited, and the garden is older than the street around it. No faction has ever openly violated the sanctuary.

**Primary Contact:** Melannor Fellbranch, groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere and a Half-Elf druid. He is friendly but humorless, and he reports grim news in the tone of a man describing the weather. His messages travel by *Animal Messenger* and never run longer than twenty-five words. He chooses the animal himself, usually a cat, a pigeon, a crow or a falcon, and he appears in person only when the situation demands it.

**Secondary Contact:** Jeryth Phaulkon, the lady of the Phaulkonmere estate. She was a noblewoman before she became a Chosen of Mielikki, and for several years she has lived as a disembodied voice in the gardens. She cannot be harmed in this state, and she can cast any druid spell. She sends no one out on tasks, and the animals carry only Melannor's words.

## Key Members

- **Melannor Fellbranch** — Half-Elf druid; groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere; primary Enclave contact, who sends the Enclave's messages by *Animal Messenger*. See the **Melannor Fellbranch** page in Notable Figures.
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** — Disembodied presence, Chosen of Mielikki; patron of Phaulkonmere; highest Enclave authority in Waterdeep. See the **Jeryth Phaulkon** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Agenda

The Emerald Enclave has no interest in the gold, and its aid carries no hidden price. Jeryth gives the same answer to anyone who asks: "That question is for the Lords to settle." The Enclave will not hide the gold, seize it, or help anyone who does. Characters who need a refuge, a reliable spellcaster, or one relationship in the city that is not angling for something will find the Enclave invaluable in a city where everyone else wants something.

## Overview

The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers and survivalists who work to keep civilization and the wild in balance. Its presence in Waterdeep is small, centered on Phaulkonmere, an old estate and garden in the Southern Ward. The gate stands open to anyone the Enclave has invited, and no faction has ever openly violated the sanctuary. Melannor Fellbranch keeps the grounds and sends its messages, and invited visitors hear Jeryth Phaulkon speak from the leaves.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Phaulkonmere:** The Tarm and Phaulkon families own the estate and spend most of the year abroad, and only Jeryth lives there now. *Dragon Heist* (5e) calls the Phaulkons of Cormyrean descent and the Tarms longtime Waterdhavians.
> - **Jeryth:** The campaign calls her a Chosen of Mielikki, and this page follows it. *Dragon Heist* and Appendix B call her a demigod. She tells recruits that the estate was her family's and that Mielikki asked something of her several years ago, and she said yes.
> - **The infernal influence:** Jeryth senses an infernal influence in the Southern Ward, and the Enclave knows it is there. Her reports place a wound in the ley lines near Coachlamp Lane that is not healing. The cause is the Cassalanters' Converted Windmill, and Jeryth does not know it.
> - **What the Enclave does not know:** Nobody in the Enclave knows whose the influence is or that the Cassalanters have a pact. Toward the family the Enclave shares only the city's general suspicion, and its speakers call the Cassalanters a rich house and nothing worse.
> - **The Splinter and the towers:** Phaulkonmere stands one block south of Kolat Towers. Enclave speakers do not name the Splinter's leader and say only "the Splinter", and they do not point toward the towers.
> - **Who the Enclave names:** The Enclave does not know Jarlaxle, Zardoz Zord or Bregan D'aerthe by name. Its harbor watcher, Sarna Dath, reports what she sees from her boat and the quay, and she does not know who owns the ships she writes about.
> - **Watchers:** The Enclave's informants include gardeners at the City of the Dead, among them the druid Sorrel Hartwick, who has tended the margins of the grounds for twenty years. Neither Sorrel nor Sarna has a Notable Figures page.
> - **The wiki's Enclave:** The Forgotten Realms Wiki describes the Emerald Enclave as an older order based on the island of Ilighôn in the Vilhon Reach, and it names Delaan Winterhound as the order's delegate at the Council of Waterdeep in the late 15th century DR (*The Rise of Tiamat*, edition not stated). The campaign names no such delegate and does not say whether the Waterdeep presence belongs to that order, so this page uses the wiki as a parallel only.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The dreamer:** Jeryth has felt a dreaming presence below the Castle Ward for months and has held it back from the ward's water table. She does not know its name. It is Illuun, an aboleth on Undermountain Level 4, and the Stone of Golorr woke it. Enclave speakers say only that something dreams below.
> - **The name:** Nobody in the Enclave knows the name, and Jeryth has never spoken it. The Enclave has only her word that something dreams below.

**Tone:** Unhurried and plain in the public lines, watchful in the GM Notes and grave in the Secret. The Enclave is never preachy, and the narrator never makes its reserve a joke.
