# The Harpers

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Player faction — intelligence-gathering operatives working against tyranny and the consolidation of the Black Network's ruthless half.
> - **Contacts:** **Mirt** (primary); **Remallia "Remi" Haventree** (secondary — her Harper role is hidden from outsiders)
> - **Mechanics:** Grand Game Stance, quest hooks, First Meeting, renown, ranks and missions are on the **Harpers** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Vault of Dragons**

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the Harpers as a network of spies and bards: where they came from, what they believe, how they work in Waterdeep, who serves them, who stands with them or against them, and how far the Splinter has reached into the local cell. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

The Harpers are a loose confederation of bards, spies and sages who keep any one power from ruling Faerûn, and the Waterdeep cell is small, well connected and compromised.

*"We work from the shadows so that others may stand in the light."*

## History

The Harpers are a loose confederation of bards, spies, sages, and do-gooders who have worked for centuries to preserve freedom and prevent any single entity from seizing dominion over Faerûn. The wiki dates most of that history to the Realms at large, and the entries below follow the thread that leads to Waterdeep.

- **324 DR, the Harpers at Twilight:** Elven commanders in Myth Drannor, working with a few trusted human rangers and druids, conceived the order. The mage Dathlue Mistwinter agreed to lead it at the head of a council that included a young Elminster Aumar, and the group took her family crest, a silver harp between the horns of a crescent moon, as its symbol. The council met at twilight in secret places in the Elven Court, which gave the order its first name (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, 3e).
- **720 DR, the First Reformation:** The order shrank for four centuries under attack from bandits, slavers, drow, illithids, orcs and evil spellcasters. The fall of Myth Drannor in 712 DR cost it more than half its remaining members and its leader. On 27 Flamerule 720 DR a congregation of dryads, druids and priests gathered at the Dancing Place in High Dale, and Elminster told them why they had been called. Several gods spoke through the priests, and the survivors began recruiting again and built an information network (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, 2e). The **Mielikki** page in Deities dates her appearance at the Dancing Place to the same year.
- **1269 DR, the war with the Zhentarim begins:** The Harpers first heard of the Zhentarim in 1269 DR. For about a century afterward the two organizations all but waged war, and each gained an edge for a time before the other struck back (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). The campaign dates the Zhentarim's own founding to 1261 DR (`history/04-the-founding-of-chains.md`).
- **1370 DR, the schism:** An investigation tied Khelben Arunsun to the theft of the *Scepter of the Sorcerer-Kings*, which had reached the Zhentarim. Khelben admitted the charges and left the Harpers with Laeral Silverhand, and they founded the Moonstars, a splinter group based in Waterdeep that at times undermined the Waterdeep Harpers. The government of Waterdeep had refused to help the Harpers of Twilight Hall prosecute him (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). Campaign change: Khelben is dead and his soul rests in the Blackstaff, Laeral is Open Lord, and the campaign has no Moonstars page.
- **1385 DR, the Wailing Years:** The Spellplague began a run of disasters that overwhelmed the order. Many Harpers died or vanished, the survivors lost touch with one another, and each turned to the dangers at their own door (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **1419 DR, the Fourth Reformation:** Eaerlraun Shadowlyn refounded the group as the Harpers of Luruar to counter a returned Netheril, and other cells followed. Some were new collectives and some were old Harpers who had gone underground, and the wiki names the Harpers of Waterdeep among those that many considered the spiritual successors of the original order (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **About 1479 DR, a double agent at the head of the cell:** The wiki says a Harper named Aron "the Fisher" Vishter led the Harpers of Waterdeep while working for the Zhentarim. Tam Zawad and other Harpers brought him to justice, but he did great damage, and Zawad was still dealing with corrupt Harpers in his cell in 1486 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). Campaign change: no campaign file names either man, and the cell's leadership belongs to Remi and Mirt.
- **The 1480s DR, the Tiamat years:** The Harpers tracked the revived Cult of the Dragon along the Sword Coast, helped gather the free cities' leaders in the Council of Waterdeep and fought at the Well of Dragons (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). The wiki lists Remallia Haventree among those who called the Council together, and the campaign has the Order of the Gauntlet tracking the cult's shipments beside the Harpers in the same years. After the Second Sundering ended in 1487 DR the Harpers rebuilt their numbers and resources as a scattered network with no central leadership (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **1489 to 1491 DR, the Black Network arrives:** The Doom Raiders reached Waterdeep in 1489 DR to found a Zhentarim foothold and have been fending off Harper spies since. About 1490 DR Manshoon's cell began building the Splinter and the Black Network split `[research]`. In the same years Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg settled into the Yawning Portal, where he has spent two years watching Zhentarim agents and befriending Bonnie's gang, and Mirt began a dossier on the Cassalanters that has not yet broken the surface. About a year ago the Howling Hatred cult led Mirt to suspect Cassalanter money.
- **Ches 1, 1492 DR, the Harpers today:** Their primary concern in the current moment is the rivalry inside the Black Network and the ruthless newcomers they call the Splinter, which seems to act on Harper information. They are also alert to the Stone of Golorr — they believe it contains intelligence of strategic importance and want it in their hands.

## Beliefs

- **Work From the Shadows:** The Harpers prefer information and influence to force, and their motto says they work from the shadows so that others may stand in the light. Mirt describes the Waterdeep operation as relatively small but well connected, with discreet membership, and says that rank and trust are earned and never declared. Most operatives are careful never to display the silver harp and crescent moon, and the wiki adds that a Harper wears the pin openly only among other Harpers (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). The wiki's account of Harper tactics says agents move outside public view so that nobody can track and study them, and that the group's worst blunders came when agents revealed themselves and acted openly (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **Information Is the Currency:** The Harpers hoard information the way other organizations hoard gold, and they collect it from ordinary people in ordinary places. Variel Duskwhisper tells one story per set that doubles as an intelligence report, Mattrim Mereg plays a mediocre bard at the Yawning Portal, Corvin Vayle passes folded notes between orders and Maxeene the draft horse listens at the hitching posts. The wiki records the same habit of informants living under aliases as traveling performers (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). It also records a Harper maxim that knowledge gained is half the battle won against any foe, and says that a changed chord or a substituted phrase in a familiar folk song can carry a warning to allies (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **No One Holds All the Power:** The Harpers resist anyone who seizes enough power to stop answering to others, and they apply the rule to their friends. Their concern covers the Lords' Alliance, Force Grey and any company of adventurers that holds the gold. For the same reason they want the vault's gold dispersed too broadly for any faction to consolidate. Mirt gives the whole philosophy in one sentence, that the Harpers resist anyone who tries to seize enough power to stop being answerable to anyone else (`sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md`). The wiki's Code says that no extreme is good and that freedom needs a balance between the powers of realms, between cities and wilderlands, and between one being's influence and another's (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **Police Their Own:** The wiki's Code calls a Harper who seizes power and holds it above all else a traitor to the harp (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). Mirt is hunting the leak in his own cell, because the Splinter has acted on information that moved only through Harper channels. The Code lets Harpers criticize one another freely, and the wiki says membership can be revoked for breaking the Code or for endangering a fellow agent, on purpose or through carelessness (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **Protect the Vulnerable:** The Harpers oppose tyranny and protect the vulnerable, and they value moral clarity alongside operational effectiveness. A member who refuses to harm civilians on faction orders, and explains why, earns respect and no punishment. The wiki adds that Harpers help one another without counting the cost and ask nothing for their work, and that the Code holds that all beings should walk free of fear and live as they wish (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **A Clean Record Deserves a Second Look:** The Harpers have watched the Cassalanters' charitable work for years and found nothing, and they find that suspicious. Mirt has kept a dossier for two years, and he suspects Cassalanter money stood behind the Howling Hatred cult about a year ago. He has no proof and wants some, and the Harpers do not know what the family hides.
- **Finish the Work:** `[research]` The wiki says Harper agents never quit a mission and take as long as it needs, and that they act for the good of all and never for glory (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). The Code adds that a Harper does whatever the work takes, however dull, hard or risky, and lets no pride rule the deed. No campaign file states either belief, though Mirt's two-year dossier and Mattrim Mereg's two years at the Yawning Portal fit it.
- **Keep the Record:** `[research]` The wiki says the Harpers hold that current events can be understood only through the history that came before them, which is why so many Harpers are bards. They share lore with ordinary people through music and stories, and they do not change a performance to please those in power (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). The campaign shows the habit in Variel's story-reports and in the Harpers' scholarly and artistic ties, and no campaign file states the belief.
- **Meddling Is Meant Kindly:** `[research]` The wiki says that enemies of the Harpers accuse them of treating the people they steer as pieces on a chessboard, and that the Harpers answer that their schemes serve the good of everyone (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). No campaign file gives the Harpers a view of the Grand Game's name or of their own part in it.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** `[research]` The campaign files set down no Harper Code, watchwords or oath. The wiki describes a code of simple statements that was never written down, spoken as watchwords when a new member is sworn in at a ritual of recognition, with the answer "I shall do no less", and says admission usually comes by invitation and a sponsor in good standing (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). In the wiki's list the statements say that a Harper works against villainy while weighing the consequences, that all beings should walk free of fear, that law serves peace only while it is just and leniently enforced, that no extreme is good, that a Harper does whatever it takes without pride, that a Harper spends less freedom on the self than on those protected, that Harpers police their own, and that a being without a past cannot know where it is going.

## Structure

The wiki describes the Harpers as a confederation with no formal roster and no hierarchy beyond a small council of High Harpers, and says nothing in the group can be truly official (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). The campaign keeps that looseness. It names no High Harper above the city cell, refers to the High Harpers only as a body to which a senior member can appeal (`campaign/guides/factions/02-harpers.md`), and gives the local lead to two people. The campaign sets no head count. The wiki puts the Harpers' strength inside Waterdeep's walls at anywhere from seven to about 120 members over the years (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated), and the nine Key Members below are the ones who matter at the table.

**Titles:** The Waterdeep cell uses five titles in rising order: Watcher, Harpshadow, Brightcandle, Wise Owl and High Harper (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, 5e, citing the *Dungeon Master's Guide*; the Factions Guide uses the same names). The wiki also names spymasters, who direct young agents, and handlers, who carry orders between spymasters and field members (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).

**Primary Contact:** Mirt the Moneylender — bluff, jovial sea merchant on the surface; in practice one of the most politically dangerous men in Waterdeep. Senior Harper, Masked Lord, and close confidant of Open Lord Laeral Silverhand. His mansion near the Naval Harbor in the Castle Ward is theoretically available for visits; in practice he is almost never home. He receives Harper members at dinner in a manor in the Sea Ward.

**Secondary Contact:** Remallia "Remi" Haventree — a precise, guarded elf widow who manages Harper intelligence operations in the North Ward. Harder to read than Mirt but ultimately more reliable. Her faction identity is kept hidden from outsiders, and few in Waterdeep know her as a Harper.

**The Waterdeep cell is compromised.** Agents of the Splinter have infiltrated the Harpers' local network. Information shared with Harper contacts has a meaningful chance of reaching them. The Harpers do not know who leads the Splinter or where it is based.

**The cell's reach:** In Waterdeep, the Harpers maintain strong ties to the city's intellectual and artistic communities. The published adventure adds that various nobles and guildmasters are Harper sympathizers, though no campaign file names one (`campaign/setting/governance-and-guilds/guilds.md`). Renaer Neverember is a Harper-adjacent ally of Mirt and Remi, and this page does not list him as a member.

**Headquarters:** Ulbrinter Villa on Delzorin Street in the North Ward is Remi's warded villa and one of the Harpers' secret gathering places. The Harpers also use a rented house at 12 Delzorin Street with five bunks, which an intermediary keeps and which has no link to the villa. A dead drop has sat behind shelf seven of Uza Solizeph's bookshop on Sorn Street in the Trades Ward for ten years, and Uza does not know it. A chandler named Nella Fen at 16 Fillet Lane watches carts, loads and the waterfront for the Harpers. On the uppermost level of Skullport, Dalagor's Fortress serves as a Harper hideout guarded by a dragonborn mage named Felrax. Campaign change: the wiki puts the Harpers' Waterdeep headquarters in a secret hold near the base of Mount Waterdeep, and the campaign uses the villa and the lodging instead.

**Fronts and covers:** Most Harper agents live a double life (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). Remi is the gracious lady of House Ulbrinter, Mirt is a moneylender, Mattrim Mereg is a middling bard at the Yawning Portal, Variel Duskwhisper is a touring musician, and Corvin and Nessa Vayle are servers who come as a pair. Maxeene is hired out by the day from a stand off Fillet Lane. The published adventure says the Harpers like to do business in busy inns such as the Yawning Portal, or in quiet places such as the City of the Dead.

**Informants inside rival groups:** Thorvin Twinbeard is a secret Harper informant inside Xanathar's Guild, and Corene Wyldath works in its Dock Ward operation under the name Halla Ironstave. Mara Coppersail works as a ticket clerk at the Sea Maidens Faire and passes what she sees to the Harpers.

**The symbol:** Their symbol — a silver harp cradled within a crescent moon — is known across the continent, though most Harper operatives are careful never to display it openly. The wiki sets the harp and moon on a field of black or royal blue and says Harpers carry a silver pin, the badge of membership, and wear it only where they want to be recognized (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).

## Methods and Tactics

The Harpers prefer influence and information to force. They oppose tyranny, protect the vulnerable, and hoard information the way other organizations hoard gold.

- **Recruiting:** The published adventure says any smart, non-evil character can join the Harpers of Waterdeep, and that bards and wizards are especially welcome (`sources/adventure-wdh.json`). The wiki says the group usually picks its own recruits, that a member in good standing must sponsor them and the High Harpers must approve, and that a senior member watches the recruit's first mission without being seen (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **Funding:** Mirt lends to Harper members, and his personal loans leave them owing him a favor in place of coin. The published adventure has the Harpers offer common and uncommon potions and scrolls at a reduced or deferred cost. The wiki says the order holds investments across western Faerûn, that its network of artisans and merchants buys magic items at a discount, and that agents sometimes rob wealthy nobles and merchants to share the money with the poor and keep part of it for future work (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **Gathering information:** Remi sends messages to her spies by a silver raven figurine, Variel's stories carry reports, Corvin passes folded notes, Maxeene listens at hitching posts and Mattrim watches the Zhentarim agents who drink at the Yawning Portal. The wiki says the Harpers keep drop boxes, hidden caches and safe houses, with at least thirty drop-offs in Waterdeep alone, and that they use a sign language, a system of marked runes and spoken code words (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **Fighting:** The Harpers prefer to send one agent where an army could not go, and the wiki says they take decisive action against cults, thieves' guilds and other forces that terrorize ordinary people (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). Mirt moves with surprising speed and carries hidden weapons. Remi wards her doors and sends her agents, and she fights in person only when the wards fail.
- **Bargaining:** The Harpers trade favors and information. They will let whoever holds the gold keep it, and they encourage the holder to do the right thing and return it to the city's citizens (`sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md`). They offer a favor in return for the Stone, as the Grand Game Agenda says.
- **What they will not do:** The Harpers do not harm civilians on faction orders, and they do not take the vault gold for themselves. They do not change a performance to please the powerful or ask payment for their work, and they do not wear the pin on a job that exposure would spoil (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). They will not act against a holder who refuses the Stone, though they become less forthcoming with that person.

## Relationships

Each entry gives the stance on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

- **The Lords' Alliance (wary ally):** The wiki lists the Alliance among the bodies aligned with the Harpers' mission (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated), and Laeral Silverhand, the Alliance's highest authority in the city, counts Mirt as her closest adviser. The Harpers still name the Alliance among the powers whose hold on the gold they would limit (`campaign/guides/factions/02-harpers.md`).
- **The Emerald Enclave (neutral):** No campaign file records dealings between them. The Harpers want whatever dreams below the city left undisturbed, and Jeryth Phaulkon has felt a dreaming presence below the Castle Ward for months, so the two share an interest. The wiki ties the Harpers to the druid circles of the North (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, 5e), but its Emerald Enclave is the Vilhon order and is not the campaign's Enclave.
- **The Order of the Gauntlet (ally):** The wiki says the Harpers and the Order work in very different ways and share the same goals (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, 5e), and in the 1480s the two tracked Cult of the Dragon shipments together. Both are suspicious of the Cassalanters.
- **Force Grey (neutral):** The published adventure says the Blackstaff gets intelligence from Harper agents as well as from her own spies (`sources/adventure-wdh.json`), and the Harpers still name Force Grey among the powers they watch for a grab at the gold.
- **The Doom Raiders (rival):** The Doom Raiders have fended off Harper spies since they reached Waterdeep, and the wiki describes a century of war between the Harpers and the Zhentarim. The Doom Raiders count Manshoon's cell as their own enemy, so the Harpers face two Zhentarim cells and know only that the Black Network has split.
- **Bregan D'aerthe (neutral `[research]`):** No campaign file records contact. A Bregan D'aerthe observer watches the Yawning Portal's well, where Mattrim keeps his cover, and the Harpers keep an informant at the Sea Maidens Faire.
- **Xanathar's Guild (enemy):** The Harpers keep Thorvin inside the lair and Corene in the Dock Ward operation, and the wiki says they move against thieves' guilds. An intellect devourer rides Corene, and she is one of three Harpers Nihiloor has taken.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim (enemy):** The Splinter has infiltrated the cell and acts on Harper information, though the Harpers do not know that the Splinter is Manshoon's cell. Manshoon counts the Harpers among his long list of powerful enemies, and the wiki describes a century of war between the two organizations.
- **The Cassalanters (neutral, suspicious):** The Harpers have watched the family's charitable work for years and found nothing, which they find suspicious. Mirt suspects the family's money and has no proof. The Harpers do not know what the family hides.
- **House Gralhund (neutral `[research]`):** No campaign file records dealings. The Gralhund villa houses Black Network resources, which gives the Harpers a reason to keep watch on it.
- **The Open Lord's Watch (ally, through Mirt):** The Commander of the Watch answers to Laeral, and Mirt advises her, so the Harpers reach the Watch through Mirt and not through the stations.
- **The Open Lord and the Masked Lords (ally):** Mirt is a Masked Lord and Laeral's closest adviser, and Laeral knows both facts. The wiki lists the Lords of Waterdeep among the bodies aligned with the Harpers' mission and says Laeral was once a Harper herself (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).

## Key Members

- **Mirt** — Tethyrian human moneylender; Harper senior operative, Masked Lord, Laeral Silverhand's closest advisor. See the **Mirt** page in Notable Figures.
- **Remallia "Remi" Haventree** — Sun elf noblewoman; de facto Harper leader in Waterdeep, manages intelligence from House Ulbrinter. See the **Remallia "Remi" Haventree** page in Notable Figures.
- **Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg** — Illuskan human Harper spy embedded at the Yawning Portal under cover as a mediocre bard; the only person in Waterdeep who knows Bonnie's true nature. See the **Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg** page in Notable Figures.
- **Bonnie** — Doppelganger; leader of a five-doppelganger operation at the Yawning Portal, independent of the Harpers. See the **Bonnie** page in Notable Figures.
- **Corene Wyldath** — Halfling Harper deep-cover agent embedded in the Dock Ward operation of Xanathar's Guild; compromised by an intellect devourer three weeks ago, after four months under cover as Halla Ironstave. See the **Corene Wyldath** page in Notable Figures.
- **Variel Duskwhisper** — Wood elf bard; Harper courier operating under touring musician cover; passes one piece of network intelligence per tenday. See the **Variel Duskwhisper** page in Notable Figures.
- **Corvin Vayle** — Illuskan human twin; runs a minor Harper waypoint through a courier role. See the **Corvin Vayle** page in Notable Figures.
- **Nessa Vayle** — Illuskan human twin; Corvin's sister, who knows not to ask. See the **Nessa Vayle** page in Notable Figures.
- **Maxeene** — Draft horse with a permanent enchantment that lets her understand and speak Common; Harper intelligence asset who has overheard more at Waterdeep's hitching posts than most spies accumulate in a year. See the **Maxeene** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Agenda

The Harpers will happily allow whoever holds Neverember's gold to keep it. What they want is the Stone of Golorr. Mirt considers a debt paid in full when the Stone is handed over and offers a substantial favor in return. A holder who refuses finds the Harpers noticeably less forthcoming, though they will not act against that person.

## Overview

The Harpers are a loose confederation of bards, sages and do-gooders who work to keep any one power from ruling Faerûn. People across Waterdeep know the symbol, a silver harp cradled in a crescent moon, though few have seen it worn openly. In Waterdeep they keep close ties to the city's scholars, performers and artists, and they prefer information and influence to force.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Remi's identity:** Remi presents as the gracious lady of House Ulbrinter and hides that she runs the Harper operation from her villa on Delzorin Street in the North Ward. No outside contact names her a Harper. The Harpers' lodging at 12 Delzorin Street is kept by an intermediary and has no link to the villa.
> - **Mirt's houses:** Mirt's mansion is a Castle Ward residence near the Naval Harbor, a fortress-like tower softened by newer architecture since he returned. He receives Harper members at dinner in a separate manor in the Sea Ward.
> - **Mirt's offices:** Mirt is a Masked Lord as well as a senior Harper, and Laeral Silverhand knows it. His Notable Figures page lists the office as an affiliation, and this page treats it as public knowledge.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The leak:** Tobin Harrask, a clerk in the Harper records relay, copies every operational entry in his register and passes it to Beldan Rusk 48 hours later, and Rusk delivers the copies to the Splinter. Fourteen people have handled the leaked material, and Mirt does not know which of them is the leak.
> - **What the Harpers do not know:** The Harpers know only that the Black Network has split and that the Splinter acts on Harper information. They do not know that the Splinter is Manshoon's cell or that it answers to Kolat Towers, and no Harper contact names him. Few in the cell know their master's name, and outsiders call the cell the Splinter. Mirt uses that name and has no name for its leader.

**Tone:** Dry and steady, like a spymaster's briefing. The Harpers' ideals are played straight, and so is the damage the Splinter has done to them.
