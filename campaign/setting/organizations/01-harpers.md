# The Harpers

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Player faction — intelligence-gathering operatives working against tyranny and the consolidation of the Black Network's ruthless half.
> - **Contacts:** **Mirt** (primary); **Remallia "Remi" Haventree** (secondary — identity hidden from PCs until Mission 4)
> - **Mechanics:** Grand Game Stance, quest hooks, First Meeting, renown, ranks and missions are on the **Harpers** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Vault of Dragons**

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the Harpers as a network of spies and bards: what they believe, how they work in Waterdeep, who serves them, and how far the Splinter has reached into the local cell. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

The Harpers are a loose confederation of bards, spies and sages who keep any one power from ruling Faerûn, and the Waterdeep cell is small, well connected and compromised.

## Tenets

- **Work From the Shadows:** The Harpers prefer information and influence to force, and their motto says they work from the shadows so that others may stand in the light. Mirt describes the Waterdeep operation as relatively small but well connected, with discreet membership, and says that rank and trust are earned and never declared. Most operatives are careful never to display the silver harp and crescent moon, and the wiki adds that a Harper wears the pin openly only among other Harpers (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **Information Is the Currency:** The Harpers hoard information the way other organizations hoard gold, and they collect it from ordinary people in ordinary places. Variel Duskwhisper tells one story per set that doubles as an intelligence report, Mattrim Mereg plays a mediocre bard at the Yawning Portal, Corvin Vayle passes folded notes between orders and Maxeene the draft horse listens at the hitching posts. The wiki records the same habit of informants living under aliases as traveling performers (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **No One Holds All the Power:** The Harpers resist anyone who seizes enough power to stop answering to others, and they apply the rule to their friends. Their concern covers the Lords' Alliance, Force Grey and the party itself. For the same reason they want the vault's gold dispersed too broadly for any faction to consolidate.
- **Police Their Own:** The wiki's Code calls a Harper who seizes power and holds it above all else a traitor to the harp (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). In the campaign Mirt hunts the leak in his own cell, and once he sees the Splinter acting on Harper information he moves his members to in-person reports and uses paper birds only for meeting times.
- **Protect the Vulnerable:** The Harpers oppose tyranny and protect the vulnerable, and they value moral clarity alongside operational effectiveness. A member who refuses to harm civilians on faction orders, and explains why, earns respect and no punishment. The wiki adds that Harpers help one another without counting the cost and ask nothing for their work (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).
- **A Clean Record Deserves a Second Look:** The Harpers have watched the Cassalanters' charitable work for years and found nothing, and they find that suspicious. Mirt has kept a dossier for two years, and he suspects Cassalanter money stood behind the Howling Hatred cult about a year ago. He has no proof and wants some, and the Harpers do not know what the family hides.
- **Finish the Work:** `[research]` The wiki says Harper agents never quit a mission and take as long as it needs, and that they act for the good of all and never for glory (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). No campaign file states either belief, though Mirt's two-year dossier and Mattrim Mereg's two years at the Yawning Portal fit it.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** `[research]` The campaign files set down no Harper Code, watchwords or oath. The wiki describes a code of simple statements that was never written down, spoken as watchwords when a new member is sworn in at a ritual of recognition, and says admission usually comes by invitation and a sponsor in good standing (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).

## Operations

*"We work from the shadows so that others may stand in the light."*

The Harpers are a loose confederation of bards, spies, sages, and do-gooders who have worked for centuries to preserve freedom and prevent any single entity from seizing dominion over Faerûn. They oppose tyranny, protect the vulnerable, and hoard information the way other organizations hoard gold. Their symbol — a silver harp cradled within a crescent moon — is known across the continent, though most Harper operatives are careful never to display it openly.

In Waterdeep, the Harpers maintain strong ties to the city's intellectual and artistic communities. Their primary concern in the current moment is the rivalry inside the Black Network and the ruthless newcomers they call the Splinter, which seems to act on Harper information. They are also alert to the Stone of Golorr — they believe it contains intelligence of strategic importance and want it in their hands.

**The Waterdeep cell is compromised.** Agents of the Splinter have infiltrated the Harpers' local network. Information shared with Harper contacts has a meaningful chance of reaching them. The Harpers do not know who leads the Splinter or where it is based.

**Primary Contact:** Mirt the Moneylender — bluff, jovial sea merchant on the surface; in practice one of the most politically dangerous men in Waterdeep. Senior Harper, Masked Lord, and close confidant of Open Lord Laeral Silverhand. His mansion near the Naval Harbor in the Castle Ward is theoretically available for visits; in practice he is almost never home. The Harper events host his dinners at a manor in the Sea Ward.

**Secondary Contact:** Remallia "Remi" Haventree — a precise, guarded elf widow who manages Harper intelligence operations in the North Ward. Harder to read than Mirt but ultimately more reliable. Her faction identity is kept hidden from the PCs until she chooses to reveal it; they will not know her as a Harper unless she tells them.

## Grand Game Agenda

The Harpers will happily allow the PCs to keep Neverember's gold. What they want is the Stone of Golorr. If the PCs hand it over, Mirt considers the debt paid in full and offers a substantial favor in return. If the PCs refuse, the Harpers become noticeably less forthcoming — but will not act against them.

## Overview

The Harpers are a loose confederation of bards, sages and do-gooders who work to keep any one power from ruling Faerûn. People across Waterdeep know the symbol, a silver harp cradled in a crescent moon, though few have seen it worn openly. In Waterdeep they keep close ties to the city's scholars, performers and artists, and they prefer information and influence to force.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Remi's identity:** Remi presents as the gracious lady of House Ulbrinter and hides that she runs the Harper operation from her villa on Delzorin Street in the North Ward. No contact names her a Harper, and the party learns it when she tells them herself in her study at the end of **A Friend's House** (Mission 4). The Harpers' lodging at 12 Delzorin Street is kept by an intermediary and has no link to the villa.
> - **Mirt's houses:** Mirt's mansion is a Castle Ward residence near the Naval Harbor, a fortress-like tower softened by newer architecture since he returned. The Harper events send members to a manor in the Sea Ward, where he receives them and later keeps the Stone during **The Stone's Other Master**.
> - **Mirt's offices:** Mirt is a Masked Lord as well as a senior Harper, and Laeral Silverhand knows it. His Notable Figures page lists the office as an affiliation, and this page treats it as public knowledge.
> - **Where the network reaches:** Harper informants sit inside Xanathar's Guild, where Thorvin Twinbeard is a secret Harper, and at the Sea Maidens Faire, where Mara Coppersail works as a ticket clerk whose cover has been seen through. Dalagor's Fortress, a Harper hideout guarded by a dragonborn mage named Felrax, stands on the uppermost level of Skullport.
> - **Fireball!:** Renaer takes the burned man's description to his Harper contacts, and by the morning of Ches 23 they name Urstul Floxin and place him at Gralhund Villa, which marks **Gralhund Villa Named**. Mirt separately tells a Harper member about his Howling Hatred suspicion before the party reaches the Sea Maidens Faire.
> - **Allies and rivals:** The wiki lists the Lords of Waterdeep, the Lords' Alliance and the Order of the Gauntlet as bodies aligned with the Harpers' mission (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated). The Factions Guide still names the Lords' Alliance among the powers the Harpers watch.
> - **History from the wiki:** The Harpers began as the Harpers at Twilight in Myth Drannor, under the mage Dathlue Mistwinter in 324 DR, and took his family's silver harp and crescent moon as their symbol. In 1370 DR Khelben Arunsun and Laeral Silverhand left the Harpers and founded the Moonstars, which the wiki says at times undermined the Waterdeep Harpers. The wiki also records a Zhentarim double agent who led the Waterdeep Harpers by about 1479 DR and did them great damage (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, edition not stated).

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The leak:** Tobin Harrask, a clerk in the Harper records relay, copies every operational entry in his register and passes it to Beldan Rusk 48 hours later, and Rusk delivers the copies to the Splinter. Fourteen people have handled the leaked material, and Mirt does not know which of them is the leak. **The Sleeping Asset** gives the party three ways to identify Tobin.
> - **What the Harpers do not know:** The Harpers know only that the Black Network has split and that the Splinter acts on Harper information. They do not know that the Splinter is Manshoon's cell or that it answers to Kolat Towers, and no Harper contact names him. Mirt says "the Splinter" and never names its leader, and Manshoon's name reaches the table only for members who have **Manshoon Named** marked.

**Tone:** Dry and steady, like a spymaster's briefing. The Harpers' ideals are played straight, and so is the damage the Splinter has done to them.
