# Bregan D'aerthe

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Player faction and villain faction; a drow mercenary company operating the Sea Maidens Faire carnival, holding Eye #3 and running a city-wide intelligence network.
> - **Contacts:** **Jarlaxle Baenre** (primary; the public faces are J.B. Nevercott and Captain Zardoz Zord, Sea Maidens Faire); **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** / "Kreb Sorrush" (street contact, Seven Masks Theater)
> - **Villain profile:** see **Jarlaxle Baenre** in Villains
> - **Mechanics:** Grand Game stance, quest hooks, First Meeting, renown and ranks, missions, response teams, outposts, escalation and revelation list are on the **Bregan D'aerthe** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Vault of Dragons**

**Purpose:** Gives the GM Bregan D'aerthe as a company and a cover: where it came from, what its members believe, how it is built, how it works, who stands with it or against it, and what hides behind the Sea Maidens Faire. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

Bregan D'aerthe is a mercenary company of drow exiles and surface operatives, led by Jarlaxle Baenre, that runs a city-wide intelligence network from behind the carnival of the Sea Maidens Faire.

*"Everything is a transaction. The only question is whether you know what you're paying."*

## History

Bregan D'aerthe is a mercenary company of drow exiles and surface operatives, led by Jarlaxle Baenre, who has been outmaneuvering Underdark politics for generations `[research]`. The entries below follow the company from Menzoberranzan to Luskan and then to Waterdeep. **Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road** in History holds the full account, and the wiki dates in these entries are not campaign decisions.

- **The 10th century DR, the founding:** Jarlaxle was born to Matron Mother Yvonnel Baenre of House Baenre and rejected Lolth, who withdrew her favor. He left his house and its name, though not his connections, and founded Bregan D'aerthe from the rogue males of destroyed houses in Menzoberranzan and Ched Nasad. The wiki gives him Arathis Hune as his first lieutenant and House Baenre as the company's chief client (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jarlaxle* and *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). The published adventure calls the first members the much-derided castaways of destroyed houses (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). By campaign ruling the company rejects Lolth too.
- **1018 to 1118 DR, mercenaries to the great houses:** In 1018 DR Jarlaxle and Hune took part in a raid on House Simfray, paid to acquire the weaponsmaster Zaknafein for House Do'Urden. Hired work in the fighting between houses let the company take in houseless rogues, its membership doubled by 1118 DR, and the matron mothers came to respect and fear it (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated).
- **1366 DR, the first surface base:** The company briefly held a base in Calimport, which the wiki ties to the pursuit of influence, to trade for Menzoberranzan and in part to the hold the artifact Crenshinibon had on Jarlaxle. The base did not last, the company looked north to Luskan, and its first contact there was a human rogue named Morik (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). Campaign change: the wiki makes Kimmuriel Oblodra Jarlaxle's co-leader from this period, no campaign file uses him, and the campaign gives the company one leader.
- **1376 to 1377 DR, Luskan:** The company helped bring down the Host Tower of the Arcane, and Luskan became a free port. It secured trade relations by aligning with Ship Kurth, which became its outward face by 1377 DR, and it later used that tie to open trade with Mirabar (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). Five High Captains rule Luskan, and each leads a faction called a Ship.
- **1483 DR and after 1485 DR, the High Captains:** The wiki names a company agent, Beniago, disguised as a human, as High Captain Kurth in 1483 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). After 1485 DR the High Captains trade legally across the Sword Coast and are secretly under the sway of Jarlaxle and the company. The published adventure says he infiltrated Luskan, brought a kind of order to its lawlessness and declared himself its secret lord (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **1489 and 1491 DR, the *Scarlet Marpenoth*:** Lantan built the submarine and launched it in 1489 DR, and Jarlaxle acquired it in 1491 DR (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Scarlet Marpenoth*, 5e). He has also forged an alliance with Lantan and armed his lieutenants with Lantanese firearms that run on smokepowder. Campaign change: the published adventure mounts the submarine below the *Eyecatcher*, and in the campaign it has been moored in Deepwater Harbor since he reached the city.
- **Marpenoth 3, 1491 DR, the arrival in Waterdeep:** Jarlaxle was in the city no later than this date, because the first nimblewright in his sale ledger went out then. He arrived in the city to advance Luskan's bid to join the Lords' Alliance, and he came prepared with nimblewrights, which he sells to wealthy families across every ward and each of which harvests intelligence through a crystal ball aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. The ledger sells NW-02 to O. Gralhund of Saerdoun Street on Marpenoth 7, and the year is derived. The Sea Maidens Faire is a legitimate business he already owned, and it tours the Sword Coast in three ships. Campaign change: the published adventure has him arrive unaware of the Stone, and the campaign has him know of the Stone, the Eyes and the vault from the day he reached the city (user ruling, Session 47).
- **1491 DR, Nar'l in the Guild:** Jarlaxle placed Nar'l Xibrindas in Xanathar's Guild a year before the campaign. Nar'l became its only advisor and persuaded it to eliminate its other advisors, and he reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending* spell (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, 5e).
- **The night before Ches 1, 1492 DR, the company today:** Bregan D'aerthe agents inside Xanathar's hideout lift Eye #3, and it reaches Jarlaxle aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. The Faire's fleet is due in Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21, the opening day of Fleetswake, and it sails at dawn on Tarsakh 20.

## Beliefs

- **Loyalty Is What He Values:** Jarlaxle is always looking for new members, and loyalty is what matters to him most. His own loyalties run to himself first and to the company second. He treats capable people well and is never threatened by them, and he maneuvers an agent into serving his ends once their interests diverge from his.
- **Leverage Before Force:** Jarlaxle would rather leave an enemy confused than dead, and he reaches for leverage before he reaches for a blade. The company's teams follow his lead and favor misdirection over lethal force. The published adventure says deception and misdirection are his forte and that he likes to trick his rivals into working for him (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki adds that he avoided killing anyone he could manipulate into serving him and preferred to talk his way out of trouble (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jarlaxle*, edition not stated).
- **Information Is Sold:** The company acquires information and sells it selectively. In Waterdeep Jarlaxle wants to know what the city's power structure looks like from the inside and which Masked Lords carry weight with the Lords' Alliance council. The wiki describes the company as an information broker that sold to both sides of a conflict for the right price (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated).
- **Keep an Asset Inside Every Rival:** The company is skilled at infiltrating criminal organizations, and Nar'l Xibrindas advises Xanathar while he reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending* spell. Ryvarra watches Undermountain traffic from the Yawning Portal, and Vessa sits in the Cassalanters' social circle. Jarlaxle is said to have an operative inside every major faction `[research]`.
- **The Faire Is the Cover:** The Sea Maidens Faire is a legitimate business that Jarlaxle owned before he turned to Waterdeep, and its parades draw attention away from the company's illicit work. The nimblewrights that Zardoz Zord sells to wealthy families send what they see to a crystal ball aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*.
- **Watch Before You Approach:** The company recruits with care. Its spies shadow a candidate for several days before anyone makes contact.
- **Drow Are Valued First:** The published adventure has Jarlaxle give serious consideration only to drow recruits, male or female (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). Almost all members are male drow from destroyed or disgraced houses, because female drow rarely take orders from a male, and a female drow can earn a place by decrying the matriarchy and convincing Jarlaxle she would be an asset. In this campaign Jarlaxle collects capable people whatever their species. The non-drow operatives he employs are never counted as members.
- **Stand Outside the System:** The company is a brotherhood of males who walked away from the drow matriarchy. The wiki says Jarlaxle held that a male drow survives best by standing outside the system and being too valuable to eliminate (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Jarlaxle*, edition not stated). Fel'rekt Lafeen petitioned to join because he was unhappy with the treatment of males in drow society, and Jarlaxle took to him almost at once (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **A Reputation Pays the Bills:** The wiki says the company's name let its agents skip lines and take credit, and that they often did not need to pay because outsiders feared its retaliation (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated).
- **Keep a Way Out:** Jarlaxle plans to flee Waterdeep in the *Scarlet Marpenoth* if his scheme unravels (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Legitimacy Is the Prize:** Luskan's reputation has blocked every earlier attempt to join the Lords' Alliance, and the company's Luskan operations need the political legitimacy a seat would bring. Jarlaxle means to buy that seat with intelligence and an Eye of the Stone, and the **Jarlaxle Baenre** page in Villains sets out how.
- **The Company Rejects Lolth:** Bregan D'aerthe has rejected Lolth as its goddess. No member worships her, keeps a shrine to her or invokes the Spider Queen with devotion, and members speak of her with contempt, as the goddess they walked away from. Soluun gives Jarlaxle the devotion that a drow of the houses would give her.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** `[research]` The campaign files give the company no creed and no sacraments. The sources show members using Drow Sign Language (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e) and the wiki gives some members an insignia pin (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). The published adventure adds one duty to the captain, that the company raises Jarlaxle from the dead within a tenday if he is killed (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). A lore source on the company's customs would settle the rest.

## Structure

Jarlaxle Baenre is the secret lord of Luskan, the commander of Bregan D'aerthe, and in Waterdeep, Captain Zardoz Zord of the Sea Maidens Faire, a traveling carnival that covers his intelligence operations. He walks the city behind the haberdasher fiction of J.B. Nevercott while Zardoz Zord runs the carnival. Nevercott does not acknowledge that he and Zord are the same person, even when one witness has seen both, and Jarlaxle finds that amusing.

**Primary Contact:** Jarlaxle Baenre, operating in Waterdeep as the flamboyant Illuskan carnival master Zardoz Zord. He is always performing, and the performance is genuine, the act of a man who chose delight as a survival strategy and discovered it works. He gives compliments that are precise and earned, and he remembers everything you said three conversations ago. He is never threatened by capable people, and he collects them. The tell players should learn to watch for is that when Jarlaxle stops smiling, the situation has become genuinely serious.

**The lieutenants:** Fel'rekt Lafeen, Krebbyg Masq'il'yr and Soluun Xibrindas do most of Jarlaxle's dirty work in the city, and all three carry Lantanese firearms (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). Fel'rekt and Krebbyg work as a team and prefer the night, and Soluun prefers the day (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Fel'rekt Lafeen*, *Krebbyg Masq'il'yr* and *Soluun Xibrindas*, 5e). The wiki says the company was split into cells under lieutenants of equal standing, each with its own area of interest, and that the cells were largely independent while they owed allegiance to Jarlaxle (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). Each of the Faire's ships has its own captain, a drow mage in human form who knows Jarlaxle's plans and speaks with him by *Sending* (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). Almost all members are male drow, and the wiki says only drow reached the highest ranks, though a few non-drow served the company (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). The published adventure counts the non-drow operatives as employees and not members. The ranks the company gives its members are in the Factions Guide.

**The Seven Masks Theater:** The theater stands in the Dock Ward, and its playbills name the owner as Rongquan Mystere. Jarlaxle owns it under that false name, and he uses it for neutral-ground meetings with other factions. The theater's founder, the ghost Malkolm Brizzenbright, still haunts it. One dressing room is kept empty as a safe house, and two drow guard it whenever it is in use.

**Street Contact:** Krebbyg Masq'il'yr is usually at the Seven Masks Theater in the Dock Ward, where he operates as "Kreb Sorrush," the theater's booking manager. He is a drow gunslinger with a relaxed professional manner and a slight Luskan accent that he makes no effort to conceal. He handles casting inquiries, performance schedules and faction intelligence requests with equal efficiency, switching between them mid-sentence. He does not explain who he works for.

**The Faire's fleet:** In Waterdeep, the operation runs out of the Sea Maidens Faire, a traveling carnival that is exactly what it appears to be, and also something else entirely. The fleet has three ships, and their names, the *Eyecatcher*, the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser*, all describe Jarlaxle (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). The *Eyecatcher* is his flagship and anchors a quarter-mile out in Deepwater Harbor, while the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser* dock at a private pier in the Dock Ward and carry the carnival's performers and parade wagons. Drow crews in human form sail all three, under an illusion projected from the gilded figurehead on each forecastle, and they work beside a host of non-drow musicians, acrobats and actors.

**The *Scarlet Marpenoth*:** The submarine is a red, manta-shaped Lantanese vessel (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Scarlet Marpenoth*, 5e). Drow gunslingers and gnome pilots and engineers crew it, and it keel-docks beneath the *Eyecatcher* when the fleet arrives. Jarlaxle keeps his stateroom aboard, and the crystal ball that serves the nimblewright network is aboard too.

**Bases beyond Waterdeep:** The wiki puts the company's main headquarters in the Clawrift of Menzoberranzan, with smaller hideouts in Menzoberranzan and Ched Nasad. Its Luskan headquarters lie underground in Illusk, on a path that connects to Closeguard Isle and Ship Kurth, and Jarlaxle keeps a private room there (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). The same page gives the company 150 drow and goblinoids in the 1360s DR and 400 soldiers at an unstated date, 250 in Menzoberranzan and 100 in Luskan. The campaign sets no head count.

**The Waterdeep network:** Ryvarra watches the well at the Yawning Portal from the balcony, and Quilm files a weekly intelligence report for Jarlaxle. Fenerus Stormcastle's house on Quill Alley in the Trades Ward is a message drop, and a calligraphy shop in the same ward belongs to the company too. A grain factor's tallyman in the Field Ward is a company spy, and the merfolk broker Filthy Meg sells harbor intelligence to Jarlaxle for a ring of *true sight* she keeps as a retainer. Grinda Garloth, the Mistshore armorer, contracts to the company and to Xanathar's Guild alike. Vessa sits in the Cassalanter social circle, and Nar'l Xibrindas sits at Xanathar's side.

## Methods and Tactics

Jarlaxle does not brawl in the streets. He acquires information, sells it selectively, positions operatives in every major faction `[research]`, and acts decisively only when the odds are already arranged in his favor.

- **Recruiting:** Like the city's other factions, Bregan D'aerthe recruits with care, watching candidates before approaching them. Joining Bregan D'aerthe means working for Jarlaxle, who genuinely values capable people and will treat his agents well, until their interests diverge from his, at which point he will maneuver them into serving his ends anyway, with a smile. Anyone who works for him is playing a game where the other player is better at it than they are, so they should pay attention. The wiki says the company found its recruits among the houseless rogues that house-against-house fighting threw up (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). The published adventure says Jarlaxle also employs non-drow operatives who do not know they work for him (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Funding:** The company hires out its blades to the highest bidder, and it also sells information, runs a legitimate carnival and sells nimblewrights to wealthy families. The wiki adds that it carried goods from the surface to Menzoberranzan, including Netherese artifacts by way of Luskan, and that House Baenre hired it more often than any other house (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated). Luskan's tie to Ship Kurth gave it a trade route to Mirabar.
- **Gathering information:** Each nimblewright feeds a crystal ball aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, so every sale is also a listening post. Nar'l reports by *Sending* spell, Ryvarra and Quilm file weekly reports, and Fenerus gathers coded messages from dead drops across the Trades Ward for Krebbyg to collect every two days. Members use Drow Sign Language, and the Faire's sailors speak to one another in odd hand signals (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki says the company's contacts reached as far as the svirfneblin city of Blingdenstone (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*, edition not stated).
- **Fighting:** The company fights with disguise, misdirection and surprise, and it prefers non-lethal containment. Its lieutenants are expert pistoleers with Lantanese firearms, and its crews wear the Faire's human illusion. The published adventure says company members buy off or dispose of people who threaten their friends, usually without being asked (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). The company avoids open fighting in the city's streets.
- **Bargaining:** Jarlaxle holds debts and opportunities, and he would sooner put a rival to work than kill one (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). A conversation with Jarlaxle is rarely what it appears to be. The first thing he does with a new contact is decide whether the person is useful or dangerous, and he is happiest when they turn out to be both. He wants Laeral Silverhand in his debt more than he wants any sum of gold, and he wants one Eye of the Stone as a chip to trade for it.
- **What the company will not do:** It will not worship Lolth or keep a shrine to her. It will not claim a member who draws too much notice, and Soluun's public disownment is the example (see Soluun's cover story in GM Notes). It will not let Jarlaxle's two public faces be tied together, and Nevercott never admits to being anyone else. Jarlaxle tells no member what he knows of the Cassalanters unless the member works it out. Bregan D'aerthe has operated in Waterdeep for months without the City Watch knowing that drow are involved, and it means to keep it that way.

## Relationships

Each entry gives the stance on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

- **The Harpers (neutral `[research]`):** No campaign file records contact between the two. A company observer, Ryvarra, watches the Yawning Portal's well, where the Harper spy Mattrim Mereg keeps his cover, and the Harpers keep an informant at the Sea Maidens Faire (**The Harpers**).
- **The Lords' Alliance (rival):** Jarlaxle wants Luskan admitted to the Alliance, and the Alliance's current leaders have voiced their opposition, with a few declaring that Luskan will never be welcome (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). Nobody has named Jarlaxle to Laeral Silverhand, so only one side knows of the contest (**The Lords' Alliance**).
- **The Emerald Enclave (neutral, unaware):** The Enclave does not know Jarlaxle, Zardoz Zord or the company by name. Its harbor watcher writes down the ships she sees and cannot say who owns them (**The Emerald Enclave**).
- **The Order of the Gauntlet (neutral, unaware):** The Order knows the Faire's ships only as a carnival fleet and does not know who owns them (**The Order of the Gauntlet**).
- **Force Grey (neutral, unaware `[research]`):** Vajra Safahr's people have no name for Jarlaxle's hand in the city and do not speak of the company (**Force Grey**).
- **The Doom Raiders (neutral `[research]`):** No campaign file records dealings. Both keep a base in the Dock Ward, the Raiders at Istrid Horn's warehouse and the company at the Seven Masks Theater and the Faire's pier (**Dock Ward**).
- **Xanathar's Guild (rival):** Nar'l Xibrindas advises the beholder and reports to Jarlaxle, and Jarlaxle prefers a manageable Xanathar to a dead one (**Jarlaxle Baenre**; **The Grand Game in Play**). Xanathar suspects a mole and hunts in the wrong direction, and it has not identified Jarlaxle as a player. Two company agents posing as Guild guards in the hideout lifted Eye #3 the night before Ches 1. Grinda Garloth contracts to both.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim (rival):** Jarlaxle and Manshoon each consider the other the only genuine peer in the Grand Game, and they keep a fragile non-interference arrangement `[research]` (**Jarlaxle Baenre**; **Manshoon**). Manshoon means to marginalize the company, and his cell has marked Fenerus Stormcastle's house as a soft target (**Manshoon**; **Trades Ward**).
- **The Cassalanters (neutral):** Jarlaxle alone outside the household and the cult knows about the family's pact, through Vessa, and he tells no company member unless they work it out. He has no interest in the family beyond what the intelligence is worth. Cassalanter imps keep watch on the Faire, and his counter-intelligence has not detected them (**Jarlaxle Baenre**).
- **House Gralhund (neutral, customer):** The company sold the household nimblewright, NW-02, to O. Gralhund of Saerdoun Street on Marpenoth 7, 1491 DR. Every sale is also a listening post, and no source says whether the house knows (**House Gralhund**).
- **The Open Lord's Watch (neutral `[research]`):** No source shows the Watch knowing of the company, and the Faire's pier is busy enough that nobody notices a visitor until the visitor is aboard (**The Open Lord's Watch**; **Dock Ward**).
- **The Open Lord and the Masked Lords (rival):** Jarlaxle aims to persuade Laeral to champion Luskan even at the cost of other Alliance members, and in her mind Luskan is a greedy pirate state that cannot be trusted (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). He also wants to know which Masked Lords carry weight with the Alliance council.

## Key Members

- **Jarlaxle Baenre** — Drow swashbuckler, chaotic neutral. Leader of Bregan D'aerthe; operates in Waterdeep through two public faces, the haberdasher J.B. Nevercott and the carnival master Captain Zardoz Zord. See the **Jarlaxle Baenre** page in Notable Figures.
- **Soluun Xibrindas** — Drow gunslinger, neutral evil. Stalks and murders elven and half-elven audience members after shows at the Seven Masks Theater. Jarlaxle is aware and hasn't yet decided what to do about it. See the **Soluun Xibrindas** page in Notable Figures.
- **Fel'rekt Lafeen** — Drow gunslinger, neutral good, and BD lieutenant. One of Jarlaxle's most trusted men, he is the close friend and operational partner of Krebbyg Masq'il'yr and keeps his heritage to himself. See the **Fel'rekt Lafeen** page in Notable Figures.
- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** — Drow gunslinger, chaotic neutral, and logistics lieutenant. Operates as "Kreb Sorrush" at the Seven Masks Theater. Collects messages from Fenerus Stormcastle's house every two days and carries them to the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. See the **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** page in Notable Figures.
- **Zelifarn** — Young bronze dragon, lawful good. Lives in Deepwater Harbor and is on friendly terms with the *Eyecatcher*'s crew. He is not an operative and does not know whose vessel hangs under the *Eyecatcher*. See the **Zelifarn** page in Notable Figures.
- **Fenerus Stormcastle** — Tethyrian human cut-out and message-drop, neutral. Guild of Chandlers and Lamplighters lamplighter by day; intelligence asset by night. Supposed to be a cut-out, but once followed Krebbyg after a pickup and knows the messages go to the Sea Maidens Faire. See the **Fenerus Stormcastle** page in Notable Figures.
- **Malkolm Brizzenbright** — Ghost, neutral. Founder of the Seven Masks Theater, dead for over thirty years. Anxious about what Jarlaxle is using his theater for. Knows "Rongquan Mystere" is a false identity, can identify the drow safe house dressing room, and once overheard "Rongquan" speaking with drow who mentioned the Sea Maidens Faire. See the **Malkolm Brizzenbright** page in Notable Figures.
- **Quilm** — Drow spy, neutral. A Bregan D'aerthe scout who files weekly intelligence reports to Jarlaxle. See the **Quilm** page in Notable Figures.
- **Ryvarra** — Drow spy and field observer, neutral. Stationed at the Yawning Portal to catalog Undermountain traffic. See the **Ryvarra** page in Notable Figures.
- **Margo Verida** — Amnian human bard, chaotic good. Jarlaxle's companion aboard the *Eyecatcher*, not his employee. See the **Margo Verida** page in Notable Figures.
- **Khafeyta Murzan** — Mulhorandi human swashbuckler, neutral good. Jarlaxle's companion aboard the *Eyecatcher*, paired with Margo Verida. See the **Khafeyta Murzan** page in Notable Figures.
- **Vessa** — Doppelganger operative, neutral. Embedded in the Cassalanter social scene under a permanent human cover; Jarlaxle's primary intelligence source on the Cassalanters. See the **Vessa** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Agenda

Jarlaxle wants Luskan admitted to the Lords' Alliance, and a debt from Laeral Silverhand, the Open Lord, is worth more to that long-running project than the gold itself. He already holds Eye #3 of the Stone of Golorr as a bargaining chip, not for his own use, and he moves on the other Eyes before the other factions consolidate. Anyone who understands his actual goals can deal with him openly and find him a reliable, if unsettling, partner. Anyone who mistakes him for a simple villain is outmaneuvered at every turn, and enjoys it considerably less than if they had paid attention.

## Overview

Bregan D'aerthe is a mercenary company that hires out its blades and sells information to any buyer who can pay. A haberdasher named J.B. Nevercott, in a burgundy coat, calls on people on the company's behalf. Captain Zardoz Zord, the flamboyant Illuskan master of the Sea Maidens Faire, welcomes interesting visitors aboard the *Eyecatcher*. The fleet is due in Deepwater Harbor on Ches 21, the opening day of Fleetswake, and is booked to sail at dawn on Tarsakh 20.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Two faces:** J.B. Nevercott is Jarlaxle in a *hat of disguise*, posing as a haberdasher. Captain Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle in the magical guise of an Illuskan human, and he spends most of his time aboard the *Eyecatcher*. Nevercott never admits to being anyone else, even when someone guesses correctly.
> - **Who knows:** Few outsiders know that either persona is Jarlaxle Baenre. Within the company he is "the captain" or "J." Asking Zord directly does not get his true name.
> - **Other names:** Jarlaxle also owns the Seven Masks Theater as Rongquan Mystere, its Shou owner. Malkolm Brizzenbright's ghost knows "Rongquan" is a false identity.
> - **The ships:** The *Eyecatcher*, the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser* carry drow crews in human form, under an illusion projected from the gilded drow figurehead on each forecastle, and a host of non-drow performers. The *Scarlet Marpenoth* is moored in Deepwater Harbor and keel-docks beneath the *Eyecatcher* when the fleet arrives.
> - **Eye #3:** Bregan D'aerthe agents inside Xanathar's hideout lifted Eye #3 the night Xanathar's Guild raided the Zhentarim warehouse, and it has reached Jarlaxle. He keeps it in his stateroom aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*.
> - **Soluun's cover story:** Jarlaxle's public story is that he disowned Soluun for killing without orders. The story is a cover, and Soluun carries a forged Bregan D'aerthe token so that the company can deny him.
> - **Cassalanters:** Jarlaxle knows about the family's pact through Vessa, and he is the only outsider who does. He tells no Bregan D'aerthe member unless they work it out themselves.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural in the GM layers, and bright in the Overview, where the Faire is a festival and its captain is a showman. Jarlaxle's charm belongs to Jarlaxle in his own speech, so the narration around him stays plain.
