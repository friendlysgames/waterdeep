# Xanathar's Guild

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Villain faction; the Grand Game's most territorially entrenched competitor, holding Eye #1.
> - **Contacts:** **Ahmaergo**, **Keln Trigos** (Guild contacts in the city); **Grinda Garloth** (independent contractor); **Gorath**, **Korgstrod Uxgulm**, **Noska Ur'gray** (field leaders)
> - **Villain profile:** see **Xanathar** in Villains
> - **Mechanics:** response teams, outposts, escalation and revelation list are on the **Xanathar's Guild** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Xanathar's Lair**

**Purpose:** Gives the GM Xanathar's Guild as an organization: where it came from, what it believes, how it is built and how it works, who its friends and enemies are, who serves it, and what it hides from the players. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

Xanathar's Guild is the largest criminal organization in Waterdeep and the only one run by a beholder, which rules it from a lair below the city.

## History

- **About 1357 DR, the first Xanathar's guild.** A beholder named Xanathar, hired by the Zhentarim to start a cell in Skullport, cut its employers off and built a criminal organization of its own. By 1357 DR the guild was a thriving thieves' guild that worked through freelance agents, so that the agents would know as little about their master as possible, and four Councilors ran it for the beholder (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, citing *Waterdeep and the North*; edition not tagged). `[research: wiki only]` The name began as the founder's personal name, and the beholders who followed it took the name as a title (**The Long Shadow**).
- **By 1372 DR, the Eye takes the name.** Kirukesai, a beholder slaver who led the Agents of the Eye in Skullport, worked out that Xanathar was a fellow beholder and had it killed. It took the name, killed the few original employees who would not follow, and merged the two organizations without telling the rank and file (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, citing *Cloak & Dagger*; edition not tagged). By 1372 DR it ruled through eleven Masters, and the two names described one body, the Agents of the Eye in Skullport and Xanathar's Guild in Waterdeep (same page, citing *City of Splendors: Waterdeep*, 3e). `[research: wiki only]` The wiki spells the beholder's name Kirukeskai, and the campaign spells it Kirukesai.
- **About 1466 DR, Zushaxx.** Other beholders held the title after the Eye. Xandulzrithral retired in peace rather than risk being overthrown, and Izulktur replaced itself with Zushaxx about 1466 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar (Zushaxx)*, citing *Dungeon* #206; edition not tagged). Zushaxx spent its first year reading the library its predecessors had gathered, and the reading gave it a hunger for information. Design: the campaign has Zushaxx believe that it is the original Xanathar (**Xanathar** in Villains; **The Long Shadow**).
- **By 1492 DR, the Guild holds Skullport.** Zushaxx has taken the whole town. The businesses that remain pay the Guild for protection, a rival beholder named Nerozar who challenged it for lordship has lost, and the Guild keeps watch posts on the upper levels of Undermountain (*Dungeon of the Mad Mage*, 5e, as summarized in **The Long Shadow**).
- **1491 DR, Nar'l Xibrindas.** Bregan D'aerthe placed Nar'l in the Guild a year before the campaign. He won Xanathar's trust and persuaded it to eliminate its other advisors, and he has been its only advisor since (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, 5e).
- **About Uktar 1491 DR, Nihiloor.** Nihiloor begins to dominate Xanathar's mind, four months before Ches 1, 1492 DR by its Notable Figures page, and Xanathar does not know (**Nihiloor**). The month is derived by counting back from Ches 1.
- **About Nightal 1491 DR, the envoys.** Manshoon's Zhentarim send two lieutenants to Skullport with Eye #1 of the Stone of Golorr, which the cell took from the Protector's Enclave in Neverwinter. Xanathar hears their proposal, eats them and keeps the Eye (**The Stone of Golorr and the Eyes**). The street war with Manshoon's Zhentarim in the Dock Ward begins from that day, and Xanathar has since ordered Manshoon killed twice and failed both times (**Xanathar** in Villains). Design: *Dragon Heist* (5e) starts the war when the Stone vanishes during merger talks between the Guild and the Zhentarim, and the campaign starts it with the envoys.
- **Before Ches 1, 1492 DR, the Stone.** After Dagult Neverember lost the Open Lord's seat about 1489 DR, his spies took the Stone from the Palace and were killed leaving the city, and the Stone passed from hand to hand until it reached Xanathar (**The Stone of Golorr and the Eyes**; **The Open Lord's Fall**). No source dates its arrival. On Ches 1 the Stone sits in the lair, and the Guild also holds Eye #1, which it does not understand. Design: *Dragon Heist* (5e) says Xanathar's spies stole the Stone and that Xanathar opened the vault once before its dragon drove the beholder out, and the campaign leaves the vault unopened.

## Beliefs

- **Everyone Is a Tool or a Threat:** Xanathar charms and bullies its minions into servitude and trusts none of them. Treachery inside the Guild is common because servants compete for the beholder's favor, and Xanathar is quick to distrust anyone who wins it (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). Mirt warns that the beholder does not relate well to humanoids, does not trust them as a matter of course, and imagines conspiracies where none exist (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki calls Zushaxx paranoid and megalomaniacal (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar (Zushaxx)*, 5e).
- **Knowledge Is Removed:** The Guild keeps its continuity by eliminating anyone who knows more than the current Xanathar. What remains is a surveillance network with no memory beyond what the current beholder chooses to believe. Low-ranking members speculate about their boss, and few have any inkling that it is a beholder. Nar'l's advice that Xanathar should eliminate its other advisors fits this habit (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Crime Is the Entry Fee:** Anyone can join, but an applicant must first pass a test that always involves a serious crime. The crime might be murdering a member who failed Xanathar, kidnapping a Waterdhavian, collecting a ransom or looting a warehouse. After that, advancement depends on wiles and on the ability to dispose of rivals, and the competition is fierce and often deadly.
- **Every Agent Has a Watcher:** A member given an assignment may receive a monstrous bodyguard or assistant, such as a bugbear, a kenku or a wererat, with secret orders to kill the member if the work fails. Xanathar may also send a gazer to help and spy on an up-and-coming member.
- **Underlings Are Disposable:** Xanathar disposes of underlings it no longer trusts in its gladiatorial arena, in a manner it finds entertaining. Whenever it flies into a rage it tends to kill random minions (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki adds that in 1492 DR members can be killed at any time at the beholder's whim (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, 5e).
- **Sylgar Comes First:** Sylgar is the only creature Xanathar truly cares about aside from itself, and its minions look after the fish constantly. When the fish dies they panic and replace it before Xanathar notices, because the beholder cannot tell one fish from another, and there have been many Sylgars over the years (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Sylgar receives more consideration than any humanoid lieutenant (**Xanathar** in Villains).
- **Doubt Is Answered With Force:** Xanathar sees enemies everywhere and lashes out at anyone it suspects of being a Zhentarim spy. Adventurers who deal with known Black Network operatives are branded as enemies who must be destroyed. When Manshoon's envoys brought it Eye #1, Xanathar received them, listened to their proposal and ate them. A low-level street war with Manshoon's Zhentarim now runs through the Dock Ward.
- **Supremacy Is the Prize:** The Guild's master wants to be seen as the ruler of the underworld, and the wealth is secondary to that proof (**Xanathar** in Villains). Zushaxx is more ambitious and impulsive than many earlier Xanathars and hungers for knowledge, information and wealth (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar (Zushaxx)*, citing *Dungeon* #206; edition not tagged). The wiki says the Guild once aimed to rule both Skullport and Waterdeep by placing its own spies among the Masked Lords (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, citing *Cloak & Dagger*; edition not tagged).
- **Tribute and Informants:** The businesses of Skullport pay the Guild for protection, and its informants work in every ward. The Guild also keeps agents in ordinary places where they can listen.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** The sources give the Guild no creed, and the membership test is its only rite. The Guild's rune is a circle with ten equidistant spokes, which is also Xanathar's personal rune (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). Tattoos of the Guild's symbol have become popular among members (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, 5e). `[research]` Any creed, oath or initiation beyond the test is unsourced.

## Structure

Xanathar rules the Guild from a lair below Skullport, and very few members have access to it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Its lieutenants manage the Guild's day-to-day operations (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Ahmaergo, a shield dwarf, is the most influential member after the beholder, and Nar'l Xibrindas is its only advisor. Nihiloor has carved out a lair of its own inside the beholder's lair, Noska Ur'gray is the chief enforcer and Thorvin Twinbeard is the chief engineer and trapsmith. Ott Steeltoes keeps Sylgar, and Gorath and Korgstrod Uxgulm lead duergar in the field (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Notable Figures).

The published ranks for members, from lowest to highest, are Eyestalker, Agent of the Eye, Eye Ray, Guild Boss and Hand of the Eye (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Guild is made up of some of Waterdeep's most disreputable folk and of monsters, whom the beholder forces into service or draws in with treasure, food or power (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Goblins and bugbears work for it, and Nihiloor has infected some of them with intellect devourers (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, 5e).

**The lair.** The lair is an ancient dungeon complex built by Netherese wizards and expanded by beholders over time. A lit tunnel of 300 feet joins it to the Guts & Garters Inn in Skullport, and a secret staircase joins it to the sewers of the Castle Ward (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The lair holds a gladiatorial arena, the Pit of Blood and Fortune, where Ahmaergo presides over the tournaments and Noska releases the combatants (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Five tattooed dwarves, who are apprentice wizards, run the lair's magical surveillance system, which the wiki calls the panopticus (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, 5e).

**Skullport.** The Guild holds the town through a fortress on Skull Island that its minions garrison, and it keeps two watch posts on Level 2 of Undermountain (*Dungeon of the Mad Mage*, 5e, as summarized in **Undermountain and Skullport**). Thirteen flameskulls still consider themselves the town's true rulers, and they do not interfere with the Guild (**The Long Shadow**).

**The Dock Ward.** The Dock Ward is the Guild's main surface territory, and yellow chalk eye marks on alley walls claim its streets (**Dock Ward**). Guild hideouts lie throughout the city's sewers, marked with yellow chalk guildsigns, and the campaign maps one, eleven rooms of a decommissioned vault below the Dock Ward (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Dock Ward**).

**The three outposts.**

- **Terasse Estate.** A rowhouse on Elsambul's Lane in the Castle Ward that Xanathar secretly owns. Keln Trigos runs a healing operation on its upper floors, and the cellar is one of the city's gathering points for the gladiatorial fights, with a trapdoor under the kitchen rug that leads to an underground dock and on to Skullport (**Castle Ward**; **Xanathar's Guild** in the Factions Guide).
- **Grinda Garloth's workshop.** A dock at Mistshore in the Dock Ward. Grinda is an independent black market armorer who contracts to both Xanathar's Guild and Bregan D'aerthe and keeps her client list private (**Dock Ward**; **Grinda Garloth**).
- **Orb Confectioners.** A sweets shop on Rising Ride in the Southern Ward that sells eye-shaped treats. Korgstrod Uxgulm runs a duergar operation in the cellar below it (**Southern Ward**; **Korgstrod Uxgulm**).

## Methods and Tactics

Xanathar is a beholder, paranoid, impulsive, and absolute in its conviction that everyone around it is either a tool or a threat. It holds territory through fear and loyalty through power, two things it understands, and trust it doesn't understand at all.

Its most visible eccentricity is Sylgar, its pet goldfish. Xanathar's attachment to the fish is total and terrifying. The Guild's agents who let anything bad happen to Sylgar can expect to be eaten, and that is not a metaphor. [research]

Its agenda in the Grand Game is territorial. It knows the Stone is the key to the vault, but it does not know that there are three Eyes or where the vault is. It wants the vault's gold as proof of its supremacy and of its rule over the underworld, more than for the wealth itself. It wants to use the Stone to take the vault.

**Recruiting.** Anyone can join, and the applicant must pass the test of a serious crime. A member who is given an assignment may receive a monstrous bodyguard, a gazer or access to the Guild's secret tunnels and hidden cellars, which serve as safe houses (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

**Extortion.** The businesses of Skullport pay the Guild for protection (**The Long Shadow**). In the city the Guild kidnaps Waterdhavians, collects ransoms for them and sells some into slavery in Skullport (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

**Smuggling.** The Guild controls access to Skullport's smuggling networks, and its town trades in slaves, drugs, poisons, cadavers and hired blades (**The Long Shadow**). `[research: wiki only]`

**Gladiatorial fights.** The arena in the lair holds tournaments before an audience, and the Terasse Estate is one of the places where that audience gathers. The games continue because the arena revenue continues (see GM Notes).

**Informants.** The Guild's informants work in every ward. Nihiloor breeds intellect devourers and sets them loose in the sewers, and each one takes the first humanoid it meets and uses its victim as a puppet to spy on the city. Some devourers hunt a particular kind of target, such as a member of the Watch or the Guard, and some capture Waterdhavians for the slave and ransom trade (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Xanathar also dreams gazers into being, sends them to follow and spy on its own members, and sees through a stuffed beholder in the window of the Old Xoblob Shop (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

**Fear.** Xanathar charms and bullies its minions and puts its symbol on doors, tattoos and chalk marks so that no one forgets whose ground they stand on (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Dock Ward**). The Guild warns before it kills. Gorath delivers a message once and leaves, and Noska is sent when Xanathar is done sending messages (**Gorath**; **Noska Ur'gray**).

**What it will not do.** Xanathar has a healthy fear of Laeral Silverhand and spares those in her employ, to avoid a conflict with the Open Lord (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Xanathar**). The Guild has no guild status in Waterdeep and appears on no guild list (**The Guilds**). It will not stop the arena, because stopping it would signal weakness, and its minions will not let their master learn that Sylgar has died.

## Relationships

These stances hold on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

- **The Harpers** (enemy): The Harpers keep Thorvin Twinbeard inside the lair as a paid informant, and Corene Wyldath works in the Guild's Dock Ward operation under a false name (**The Harpers**; **Corene Wyldath**). Xanathar has begun to question Thorvin's loyalty and has set a gazer to watch him (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). One of Nihiloor's intellect devourers rides Corene, and she is one of three Harpers it has taken.
- **The Lords' Alliance** (enemy): The Alliance exists to watch for threats to the city's stability, and the Guild is the largest criminal power in the city. Jalester does not know where the lair is and would have to ask Laeral (**The Lords' Alliance**; *Dragon Heist*, 5e). No source states an Alliance policy toward the Guild. `[research]`
- **The Emerald Enclave** (enemy, watched and not fought): Melannor calls the beholder beneath the city a problem that has not been solved, because the Guild that serves it digs wherever it likes (**The Emerald Enclave**). No source says the Enclave plans to move against it. `[research]`
- **The Order of the Gauntlet** (enemy): The Order counts the Guild's use of intellect devourers and enslaved creatures as an abomination, and Guild dealers work the Field Ward, where the Order fills part of the gap the Watch leaves (**The Order of the Gauntlet**; **Savra Belabranta**).
- **Force Grey** (enemy): An intellect devourer bred by Nihiloor rides Meloon Wardragon, a member of Force Grey, and steers adventurers away from Undermountain and toward the Guild's enemies (**Meloon Wardragon**; *Dragon Heist*, 5e). The published adventure says Vajra Safahr is unaware of it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **The Doom Raiders** (neutral): Davil Starsong wants to keep his cell independent of Manshoon's Splinter and to keep the peace with the Guild through talk and not violence (**Davil Starsong**). Manshoon's war against the Guild has thrown the cell's plans into upheaval (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). No source says whether the Guild tells the cell apart from the Splinter. `[research]`
- **Bregan D'aerthe** (rival, unaware): Nar'l Xibrindas advises Xanathar and reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending* spell, and Bregan D'aerthe buys equipment through Grinda Garloth. Xanathar suspects a mole and is hunting in the wrong direction (**Running the Villains**; **Nar'l Xibrindas**).
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim** (enemy): Xanathar ate the Splinter's envoys and kept the Eye they brought, and the two fight a low-level street war in the Dock Ward. Manshoon's followers want the Guild destroyed and the city's economy in their hands, and Vevette Blackwater commands the cell's forces against it (**Manshoon**; *Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **The Cassalanters** (neutral, no contact): The two are in parallel competition with no direct contact, and the Guild has no operation against the family (**Running the Villains**). Everybody is suspicious of the Cassalanters, and the Guild has no knowledge of any pact.
- **House Gralhund** (neutral, watched): The house traced Dalakhar into the Guild, and it does not know that the Guild holds the Stone (**House Gralhund**). The published adventure has Yalah bargain with the beholder, and the campaign does not (see Draft Notes).
- **The Open Lord's Watch** (enemy, tolerated): The Watch is called to the Guild's brawls with the Zhentarim in the Dock Ward and leaves the sewer lair alone, and Captain Staget reasons that the two may as well destroy each other (**The Open Lord's Watch**; *Dragon Heist*, 5e). Nihiloor's devourers ride Watch members, including a clerk named Orvyn Dall (**Nihiloor**).
- **The Open Lord and the Masked Lords** (enemy, afraid): Xanathar fears Laeral Silverhand and spares her people (**Xanathar**). Mirt dealt with the beholder many times as a Lord of Waterdeep (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki says the Guild once hoped to place spies among the Masked Lords (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, citing *Cloak & Dagger*; edition not tagged).

## Key Members

- **Xanathar** — Beholder crime lord, lawful evil. See the **Xanathar** page in Notable Figures.
- **Ahmaergo** (DH p. 195) — Shield dwarf majordomo, lawful evil. Ahmaergo handles logistics: gladiatorial procurement, outpost resupply, discipline. Most orders that bear Xanathar's name are written by Ahmaergo. See the **Ahmaergo** page in Notable Figures.
- **Nar'l Xibrindas** (DH p. 213) — Drow mage, neutral evil. Xanathar's advisor and secret Bregan D'aerthe asset. Nar'l reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending* spell and has been operating in place for one year, placed in 1491 DR. See the **Nar'l Xibrindas** page in Notable Figures.
- **Nihiloor** (DH p. 213) — Mind flayer, lawful evil. Officially handles Xanathar's most sensitive intelligence operations. In practice, Nihiloor is running three concurrent projects: (1) **The Infiltration** — intellect devourers placed inside city government officials give Nihiloor eyes throughout Waterdeep's bureaucracy; three Harper assets are already compromised; (2) **The Experiments** — creatures captured during Arena operations are being subjected to psionic modification seeking a "relay organism" that extends the colony's reach without destroying the host brain; (3) **The Puppet** — Nihiloor has been exerting psionic domination over Xanathar itself for the past four months, replacing its strategic decisions while leaving its personality intact. The long-term payoff of all three projects is the Seadeeps colony (Undermountain Level 17). See the **Nihiloor** page in Notable Figures.
- **Noska Ur'gray** (DH p. 213) — Shield dwarf enforcer, neutral evil. Xanathar's heaviest enforcer. See the **Noska Ur'gray** page in Notable Figures.
- **Ott Steeltoes** — Dwarf cult fanatic (Zuggtmoy), neutral evil. Guild field agent, currently held prisoner in the Zhentarim Interrogation House. Wears a leather skullcap stitched with fake beholder eyestalks. See the **Ott Steeltoes** page in Notable Figures.
- **Thorvin Twinbeard** — Dwarf engineer (secret Harper), neutral good. See the **Thorvin Twinbeard** page in Notable Figures.
- **Keln Trigos** — Tiefling priest (Xanathar's Guild), neutral evil. Stationed at the Terasse Estate. Provides healing for the Guild's agents and manages the audience that gathers there for the gladiatorial fights. See the **Keln Trigos** page in Notable Figures.
- **Grinda Garloth** — Illuskan human mage (black market armorer), chaotic neutral. Independent black market armorer based at Mistshore, a contractor to both Xanathar's Guild and Bregan D'aerthe. She equips the Guild's agents and rents equipment to third parties, and she keeps her client list private. See the **Grinda Garloth** page in Notable Figures.
- **Korgstrod Uxgulm** — Duergar, lawful evil. Duergar leader of the sewer hideout beneath Orb Confectioners. See the **Korgstrod Uxgulm** page in Notable Figures.
- **Gorath** — Duergar, lawful evil. See the **Gorath** page in Notable Figures.
- **Grevik Nass** — Half-orc cook, neutral evil. See the **Grevik Nass** page in Notable Figures.
- **Tobrin Ashvale** — Chondathan human bartender, neutral evil. See the **Tobrin Ashvale** page in Notable Figures.

## Overview

Xanathar's Guild is the largest criminal organization in Waterdeep and, by rumor, the only one led by a beholder. It controls access to Skullport's smuggling networks and gladiatorial circuits, and it keeps informants in every ward. Its main territory above ground is the Dock Ward, where yellow chalk eye marks on alley walls claim the streets. Its master is said to trust nobody in the Guild and to dote on a goldfish named Sylgar.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Legal status:** The Guild is a faction and not an actual guild, and despite its name it has no official guild status in Waterdeep. It appears on no guild list (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Guilds**).
> - **Ground:** The Dock Ward is the Guild's main surface territory, and Manshoon's Zhentarim fight a low-level street war against it there.
> - **Xoblob:** Xoblob is an informant for the Guild, and the stuffed beholder in his window is a magical sensor through which Xanathar occasionally watches the street. Few people outside the Guild know it is a sensor.
> - **What ordinary agents know:** Most Guild agents do not know that their master is a beholder. Play them as hired muscle who speculate about Xanathar and have never seen it.
> - **The arena:** The gladiatorial games continue because the arena revenue continues, and because stopping them would signal weakness.
> - **The Eye in the bowl:** Eye #1 of the Stone of Golorr sits in Sylgar's bowl in the lair's sanctum. Ott Steeltoes placed it there as a pretty stone after Xanathar killed the envoys who brought it, and Xanathar does not know what it is.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The puppet:** Nihiloor has dominated Xanathar for the last four months, and Xanathar does not know. The Xanathar that Waterdeep fears has been Nihiloor's instrument for that time, including the one that killed Manshoon's envoys.

**Tone:** Grave and exact in the Overview, where the Guild is a frightening local fact, and plain and procedural in the GM text. The narrator treats Xanathar's paranoia as dangerous and lets the goldfish be funny without making the joke.
