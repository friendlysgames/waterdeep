**Purpose:** Gives the GM Xanathar's Guild as an organization: how it recruits and rules, who serves it, and what it hides from the players. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

Xanathar's Guild is the largest criminal organization in Waterdeep and the only one run by a beholder, which rules it from a lair below the city.

## Tenets

- **Everyone Is a Tool or a Threat:** Xanathar charms and bullies its minions into servitude and trusts none of them. Treachery inside the Guild is common because servants compete for the beholder's favor, and Xanathar is quick to distrust anyone who wins it (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Knowledge Is Removed:** The Guild keeps its continuity by eliminating anyone who knows more than the current Xanathar. What remains is a surveillance network with no memory beyond what the current beholder chooses to believe. Low-ranking members speculate about their boss, and few have any inkling that it is a beholder.
- **Crime Is the Entry Fee:** Anyone can join, but an applicant must first pass a test that always involves a serious crime. The crime might be murdering a member who failed Xanathar, kidnapping a Waterdhavian, collecting a ransom or looting a warehouse. After that, advancement depends on wiles and on the ability to dispose of rivals, and the competition is fierce and often deadly.
- **Every Agent Has a Watcher:** A member given an assignment may receive a monstrous bodyguard or assistant, such as a bugbear, a kenku or a wererat, with secret orders to kill the member if the work fails. Xanathar may also send a gazer to help and spy on an up-and-coming member.
- **Underlings Are Disposable:** Xanathar disposes of underlings it no longer trusts in its gladiatorial arena, in a manner it finds entertaining. The wiki adds that in 1492 DR members can be killed at any time at the beholder's whim (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, 5e).
- **Sylgar Comes First:** Sylgar is the only creature Xanathar truly cares about aside from itself, and its minions look after the fish constantly. When the fish dies they panic and replace it before Xanathar notices, because the beholder cannot tell one fish from another. Sylgar receives more consideration than any humanoid lieutenant (**Xanathar** in Villains).
- **Doubt Is Answered With Force:** Xanathar sees enemies everywhere and lashes out at anyone it suspects of being a Zhentarim spy. Adventurers who deal with known Black Network operatives are branded as enemies who must be destroyed. When Manshoon's envoys brought it Eye #1, Xanathar received them, listened to their proposal and ate them. A low-level street war with Manshoon's Zhentarim now runs through the Dock Ward.
- **Tribute and Informants:** The businesses of Skullport pay the Guild for protection, and its informants work in every ward. The Guild also keeps agents in ordinary places where they can listen.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** The sources give the Guild no creed, and the membership test is its only rite. The Guild's rune is a circle with ten equidistant spokes, which is also Xanathar's personal rune (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e). Tattoos of the Guild's symbol have become popular among members (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, 5e). [research] Any creed, oath or initiation beyond the test is unsourced.

## Operations

Xanathar is a beholder, paranoid, impulsive, and absolute in its conviction that everyone around it is either a tool or a threat. It holds territory through fear and loyalty through power, two things it understands, and trust it doesn't understand at all.

Its most visible eccentricity is Sylgar, its pet goldfish. Xanathar's attachment to the fish is total and terrifying. The Guild's agents who let anything bad happen to Sylgar can expect to be eaten, and that is not a metaphor. [research]

Its agenda in the Grand Game is territorial. Xanathar held the Stone of Golorr until Dalakhar stole it, and it still holds one of the Stone's Eyes without knowing what the Eye does. It knows the Stone is the key to the vault, but it does not know that there are three Eyes or where the vault is. It wants the vault's gold as proof of its supremacy and of its rule over the underworld, more than for the wealth itself. It wants the Stone recovered so that it can take the vault.

## Key Members

- **Xanathar** — Beholder crime lord, lawful evil. See the **Xanathar** page in Notable Figures.
- **Ahmaergo** (DH p. 195) — Shield dwarf majordomo, lawful evil. Ahmaergo handles logistics: gladiatorial procurement, outpost resupply, discipline. Most orders that bear Xanathar's name are written by Ahmaergo. See the **Ahmaergo** page in Notable Figures.
- **Nar'l Xibrindas** (DH p. 213) — Drow mage, neutral evil. Xanathar's advisor and secret Bregan D'aerthe asset. Nar'l reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending* spell and has been operating in place for one year, placed in 1491 DR. The smokepowder delivery referenced in the Korgstrod correspondence was his operation. See the **Nar'l Xibrindas** page in Notable Figures.
- **Nihiloor** (DH p. 213) — Mind flayer, lawful evil. Officially handles Xanathar's most sensitive intelligence operations. In practice, Nihiloor is running three concurrent projects: (1) **The Infiltration** — intellect devourers placed inside city government officials give Nihiloor eyes throughout Waterdeep's bureaucracy; three Harper assets are already compromised; (2) **The Experiments** — creatures captured during Arena operations are being subjected to psionic modification seeking a "relay organism" that extends the colony's reach without destroying the host brain; (3) **The Puppet** — Nihiloor has been exerting psionic domination over Xanathar itself for the past four months, replacing its strategic decisions while leaving its personality intact. Evidence exists in Nihiloor's research chamber in the deep lair; its long-term payoff is the Seadeeps colony (Undermountain Level 17). See the **Nihiloor** page in Notable Figures.
- **Noska Ur'gray** (DH p. 213) — Shield dwarf enforcer, neutral evil. Xanathar's heaviest enforcer. See the **Noska Ur'gray** page in Notable Figures.
- **Ott Steeltoes** — Dwarf cult fanatic (Zuggtmoy), neutral evil. Guild field agent, currently held prisoner in the Zhentarim Interrogation House. Wears a leather skullcap stitched with fake beholder eyestalks. If rescued and cultivated, he can lead the PCs toward Xanathar's Lair. See the **Ott Steeltoes** page in Notable Figures.
- **Thorvin Twinbeard** — Dwarf engineer (secret Harper), neutral good. See the **Thorvin Twinbeard** page in Notable Figures.
- **Keln Trigos** — Tiefling priest (Xanathar's Guild), neutral evil. Stationed at the Terasse Estate. Provides healing for the Guild's agents and manages the gladiatorial audience transports to the Lair. See the **Keln Trigos** page in Notable Figures.
- **Grinda Garloth** — Illuskan human mage (black market armorer), chaotic neutral. Independent black market armorer based at Mistshore, a contractor to both Xanathar's Guild and Bregan D'aerthe. She equips the Guild's agents and rents equipment to third parties, and she keeps her client list private. See the **Grinda Garloth** page in Notable Figures.
- **Korgstrod Uxgulm** — Duergar, lawful evil. Duergar leader of the sewer hideout beneath Orb Confectioners. See the **Korgstrod Uxgulm** page in Notable Figures.
- **Gorath** — Duergar, lawful evil. See the **Gorath** page in Notable Figures.
- **Grevik Nass** — Half-orc cook, neutral evil. See the **Grevik Nass** page in Notable Figures.
- **Tobrin Ashvale** — Chondathan human bartender, neutral evil. See the **Tobrin Ashvale** page in Notable Figures.

## Overview

Xanathar's Guild is the largest criminal organization in Waterdeep and, by rumor, the only one led by a beholder. It controls access to Skullport's smuggling networks and gladiatorial circuits, and it keeps informants in every ward. Its main territory above ground is the Dock Ward, where yellow chalk eye marks on alley walls claim the streets. Its master is said to trust nobody in the Guild and to dote on a goldfish named Sylgar.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Legal status:** The Guild is a faction and not an actual guild, and despite its name it has no official guild status in Waterdeep. It appears on no guild list (*Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Guilds**).
> - **Ground:** The Dock Ward is the Guild's main surface territory, and Manshoon's Zhentarim fight a low-level street war against it there. The street war began when Manshoon's envoys came to Skullport three months before the fireball and Xanathar killed them.
> - **Xoblob:** Xoblob is an informant for the Guild, and the stuffed beholder in his window is a magical sensor through which Xanathar occasionally watches the street. The party cannot discover this at **Finding Floon** level, so plant it only if the scene fits and do not press it.
> - **What ordinary agents know:** Most Guild agents do not know that their master is a beholder. Play them as hired muscle who speculate about Xanathar and have never seen it.
> - **The arena:** The gladiatorial games continue because the arena revenue continues, and because stopping them would signal weakness.
> - **The Eye in the bowl:** Eye #1 of the Stone of Golorr sits in Sylgar's bowl in the lair's sanctum. Ott Steeltoes placed it there as a pretty stone after Xanathar killed the envoys who brought it, and Xanathar does not know what it is.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The puppet:** Nihiloor has dominated Xanathar for the last four months, and Xanathar does not know. The Xanathar the party meets has been Nihiloor's instrument, including the one that killed Manshoon's envoys and sent response teams against the party's allies.
> - **The plants:** Grevik Nass and Tobrin Ashvale are the Guild's two plants among the staff of Trollskull Manor. Each keeps a file on the tavern's patrons, and their **Notable Figures** pages and the **Trollskull Manor** guide give the details.

**Tone:** Grave and exact in the Overview, where the Guild is a frightening local fact, and plain and procedural in the GM text. The narrator treats Xanathar's paranoia as dangerous and lets the goldfish be funny without making the joke.
