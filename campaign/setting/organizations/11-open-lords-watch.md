# The Open Lord's Watch

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Non-player institution and minor Grand Game participant. The City Watch is the Open Lord's eyes on the street, and much of what Laeral Silverhand learns about the Grand Game reaches her through it.
> - **Contacts:** **Hyustus Staget** (Dock Ward captain), **Saeth Cromley** (retired sergeant), **Jalester Silvermane** (the Open Lord's field agent), **Laeral Silverhand** (the Open Lord)
> - **Mechanics:** response rules and everything a character can do with the Watch are on the **Open Lord's Watch** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Structure:** ranks, posts, patrols, the City Guard, the Griffon Cavalry and the Palace are on **The City Watch** in Governance and Guilds, and this page does not repeat them
> - **Featured in:** **Finding Floon**, **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Kolat Towers**, **Vault of Dragons**

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the City Watch as a Grand Game actor: how it answers to the Open Lord, what reaches Laeral through it, what she believes on the first day and how the Watch stands with each faction. The page keeps lore only and covers what **The City Watch** leaves out. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

The Open Lord's Watch is the City Watch seen as the Open Lord's instrument, Waterdeep's street police and the channel through which most of what the government learns about the factions reaches the Palace.

## History

The wiki says Ahghairon restructured Raurlor's army and navy into the City Watch and the City Guard, and no source dates the change (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *History of Waterdeep*; City of Splendors boxed set, 2e, as recorded on **Ahghairon's Gifts**). The published adventure keeps both services and credits him with neither. In 1235 DR, during an orc siege, the wiki has Ahghairon declare the Griffon Cavalry part of the City Guard (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Griffon Cavalry*; City of Splendors boxed set, 2e).

Around 1372 DR the wiki names Captain Rulathon as Commander of the Watch, Derek Windsfire as Grand Civilar and Thyriellentha Snome as Mage Civilar (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *City Watch (Waterdeep)*; City of Splendors: Waterdeep, 3e). **The City Watch** records the ranks that went with those offices. In 1379 DR the Watch captain Lady Gondafrey tried to breach Kolat Towers and was taken prisoner by Duhlark Kolat, and she is still held there on the first day (**The Founding of Chains**).

About 1489 DR the other Lords voted Dagult Neverember out, and Laeral Silverhand took the office to which the Commander of the Watch reports (**A City Watching Itself**). In 1491 DR the wiki says she proposed refounding the Griffon Cavalry, and the published adventure shows its riders flying in 1492 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Griffon Cavalry*, citing the novel *Death Masks*; *Dragon Heist*, 5e).

By the spring of 1492 DR brawls between Xanathar's Guild and the Zhentarim have become common in the Dock Ward, and the Watch is called to them often (**The Players Arrive**). The Dock Ward captain, Hyustus Staget, pulled a surveillance detail off a Zhent instigator named Urstul Floxin to strengthen street patrols, and he regrets it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

## Beliefs

The Watch holds that Waterdeep is a city of laws and that its officers owe the city diligent service. The published adventure says a visitor can trust the Watch to do its duty and that its strict code of conduct makes it one of the most trusted police forces anywhere outside paladin-patrolled Elturgard (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki adds that the Watch exists to protect the well-being of Waterdhavians as well as to enforce the law (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *City Watch (Waterdeep)*; City of Splendors: Waterdeep, 3e).

The wiki quotes Captain Rulathon, a Commander of an earlier age, swearing to uphold the laws and edicts of the Lords and holding that law and order are all that keep Waterdeep from becoming Undermountain or Hellgate Keep (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *City Watch (Waterdeep)*; City of Splendors boxed set, 2e). The beliefs below explain how the Watch acts in the Grand Game.

- **Keep the Blood Off the Streets:** Officers care more about the city above than the sewers below, and Staget's advice to meddlers is to leave the dirty business to the Watch (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). This is why the Watch answers a brawl on a Dock Ward corner and does not follow a feud underground.
- **What Reaches the Watch Reaches the Palace:** Information given to the City Watch is the likeliest route by which the Open Lord learns what is happening, and it can reach her fast or, as the Alexandrian text says, never fully reach her at all (Alexandrian remix, *2. Other Factions*, printed page 21; **The Grand Game**).
- **A Strict Code, Mostly Kept:** The code is real, and eight officers in one Masked Lord's pay show that a few step outside it (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Some Cases Are Untouchable:** The Watch knows that politics shields some cases from it `[research]`. The one sourced example is the Cassalanters, whom Jalester says the Watch cannot touch without evidence (`campaign/structure/arc-g-cassalanter-villa.md`, pending conversion).
- **Pressure From Above Works:** Officers bend to a powerful patron more readily than to a citizen `[research]`. The sourced cases are Corylus Thann's veterans and the thin Watch coverage around Kolat Towers, which Mirt says someone with civic influence arranged over eighteen months (`campaign/structure/arc-i-kolat-towers.md`, pending conversion).
- **Doctrine and Rites:** `[research]` No campaign file or published text gives the Watch an oath, a creed in writing or an induction rite. The sources give its slang, which **The City Watch** lists. Gods' Day is a semiofficial "Be Kind to the Guard and Watch Day", and a gift worth more than a few nibs may be read as a bribe (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).

## Structure

- **The Commander and the Open Lord:** The Commander of the Watch reports to the Open Lord (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and no source names the Commander in 1492 DR. `[research]` The Open Lord governs from the Palace of Waterdeep, which the wiki makes the Watch's main base, so a report that reaches the Commander arrives in the building where Laeral works (**The Open Lord and the Masked Lords**; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *City Watch (Waterdeep)*; City of Splendors: Waterdeep, 3e). The wiki also says only the Open Lord can award a badge of the Watch, which lets her know where its wearer is (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *City Watch (Waterdeep)*, citing *Dragon Heist*, 5e). The adventure's one example is Jalester Silvermane's badge, which returns to Laeral if he loses it.
- **How reports reach Laeral:** A citizen reports a crime at a watch post, the ward's station keeps the report, and the Commander of the Watch answers to the Open Lord (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Her information comes largely from the Watch and from Jalester, and it runs about three moves behind the factions (**A City Watching Itself**). No source says how a ward station passes a report up to the Commander or how often. `[research]`
- **The City Guard:** The Guard is a separate service that holds the walls, the gates, the government buildings, the harbor and the city's officials. Its Warden, Elminster, answers to the Open Lord (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Where the Watch does not patrol, as in the Field Ward, the Guard acts only when someone entering or leaving the city is threatened (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **The Griffon Cavalry:** The Cavalry is part of the Guard and flies from the Peaktop Aerie. Only specially licensed riders and mounts may fly over Waterdeep, and anyone else in the air meets a vigorous pursuit (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Belabranta family raises the griffons, and Savra Belabranta of the **Order of the Gauntlet** comes from that family (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **The Watchful Order:** Arcane spellcasters who stay in Waterdeep must register with the city and are urged to join the Watchful Order, which the published adventure says the Blackstaff heads. Its members serve as temporary members of the Watch or the Guard when called, and its investigators, such as Barnibus Blastwind, tell the Watch whether magic was used in a crime (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Blackstaff also commands **Force Grey**, so the Open Lord's police, her arcane investigators and her elite agents all trace back to her.

## Methods and Tactics

- **Patrols and posts:** Squads walk day and night rounds from the watch posts, and every officer knows the streets and the residents of a beat better than a visitor will (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). **The City Watch** gives patrol sizes and the signal whistles.
- **Custody without a scene:** The Watch tries to take people into custody without disturbing other citizens, floggings take place at the nearest watch post, and the arrested go before a magister (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). A bystander usually gives an arrest a wide berth.
- **Reports and files:** A post takes reports from anyone who walks in, and the North Ward station keeps a sergeant on the day shift to write them down (**North Ward**). Jalester reads the Watch's file ledger each tenday (**City Watch**; **The Lords' Alliance**). The Watch has also called on Myl Dunpier twice this year to ask an innkeeper about her guests, which is why she keeps meticulous records (**Myl Dunpier**).
- **Informants:** Gosta Ironhelm, a dwarf bartender who applies for work in the party's tavern, files regular reports on "establishments of interest" and has done so for most of a decade. His reports are atmospherics, such as who comes in and which guild or faction they favor (Alexandrian remix, Appendix D). The Watch has informants in every ward `[research]`, and every faction has informants in the Watch `[research]`.
- **Warrants:** A search needs grounds for a warrant, and officers who lack them drop the matter (*Dragon Heist*, 5e, the constables who called at a villa).
- **Nobles and pressure:** The Watch is trying to do its duty and knows that some cases are politically untouchable `[research]`. Corylus Thann, a Masked Lord, keeps eight crooked Watch veterans to crack down on anyone who annoys him, and he uses his office to clear any charge against them (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Jalester says the Watch cannot touch the Cassalanters without evidence because they are too well connected (`campaign/structure/arc-g-cassalanter-villa.md`, pending conversion).
- **Gang war:** Staget keeps the peace in the Dock Ward and has been dragging his heels on the brawls, since he reasons that if Xanathar's Guild and the Zhentarim want to destroy each other, the Watch may as well let them (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). He will not send a force into the sewers for a man who may be a spy for either side.
- **What the Watch will not do:** It does not patrol the Field Ward or Undercliff, and neither benefits from the protection of the Watch (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). It does not look for trouble in the sewers, although its captains hold keys to the sewer access points for their wards (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki says it never acted outside the city walls (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *City Watch (Waterdeep)*, citing the comic *The Gathering*).

## Relationships

- **The Open Lord (superior):** The Commander reports to Laeral, and her closest advisor, Mirt, is a Masked Lord (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **A City Watching Itself**).
- **The Masked Lords (superior, uneven):** Any Masked Lord can overturn a magister's ruling (*Dragon Heist*, 5e), and Corylus Thann's veterans show that one Lord can bend part of the Watch to a private purpose. Manshoon's cell blackmails Corylus and Jelenn Urmbrusk (**The Open Lord and the Masked Lords**).
- **The magisters (partner):** The Watch arrests and a magister judges, and the Castle Ward courthouse keeps two Watch veterans at its Watch Wagon at all times (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **The Code Legal and the Magisters**).
- **The Lords' Alliance (ally):** Laeral uses the Alliance as her instrument outside the Palace, and Jalester carries a badge of the Watch and reads its file ledger (**A City Watching Itself**; **The Lords' Alliance**).
- **The Harpers (ally):** Mirt is a senior Harper and Laeral's closest advisor, and he wants to protect Waterdeep from threats the Watch cannot see (**Mirt**).
- **Force Grey (ally of last resort):** The Open Lord mobilizes Force Grey when diplomats and the city's other armed forces cannot handle a problem, and anyone who has served in the Watch or the Guard may join its Gray Hands (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **The Order of the Gauntlet (wary neutral):** The Order's knights are more aggressive and less subtle than the Watch. Its file records that the Watch closed every complaint against one noble family without findings, and Sister Valdra's case rests on a servant's story that the Watch dismissed (**The Order of the Gauntlet**).
- **The Emerald Enclave (neutral):** No source brings the Enclave and the Watch together on the first day. `[research]`
- **The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders) (wary rival):** The Watch keeps a file on Davil Starsong's cell, so his people avoid anything that invites scrutiny (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**). Tashlyn Yafeera has earned the rank of captain in the City Guard as bodyguard to a magister at the South Gate (**Tashlyn Yafeera**).
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim (enemy):** The Watch has arrested a few of the tattooed crews in street brawls, and none of them will say who gives the orders (**Manshoon's Zhentarim**). Manshoon bought Skeemo Weirdbottle with protection from Watch inspections, which shows how far the cell reaches (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**).
- **Xanathar's Guild (enemy, tolerated):** The Watch is called to its brawls with the Zhentarim in the Dock Ward and leaves its sewer lair alone (**The Players Arrive**; *Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **The Cassalanters (neutral, unaware):** The Watch has closed three complaints against the house and knows nothing of a pact, and Jalester's briefing says the Watch cannot touch the family without evidence (**The Cassalanters**; `campaign/structure/arc-g-cassalanter-villa.md`, pending conversion). Mirt suspects Cassalanter money stood behind the Howling Hatred cult, because the Watch released two of its members after a word from someone at the villa, and he has no proof (**Fireball!**).
- **Bregan D'aerthe (neutral):** No source shows the Watch knowing of the company. `[research]`
- **House Gralhund (neutral):** No source gives the house a day-one tie to the Watch. `[research]`

## Key Members

- **Saeth Cromley** — Illuskan human retired sergeant, lawful good, he/him. A retired sergeant of the Watch who helps Barnibus Blastwind with investigations and sits in the sergeants' room at the Halls of Justice in the afternoons. See the **Saeth Cromley** page in Notable Figures.
- **Barnibus Blastwind** — Illuskan human Watchful Order investigator, lawful good, he/him. He belongs to the Watchful Order, not the Watch, and he works with Cromley on crimes that involve magic. See the **Barnibus Blastwind** page in Notable Figures.
- **Jalester Silvermane** — Chondathan human spy, lawful good, he/him. A Lords' Alliance field agent who reports to Laeral. He is not a Watch officer, but he carries a badge of the Watch and reads its file ledger. See the **Jalester Silvermane** page in Notable Figures.
- **Lady Gondafrey** — Gargoyle with a human soul, lawful good, shifting to chaotic evil with the time of day, she/her. A former Watch captain taken prisoner in 1379 DR and still held in Kolat Towers. See the **Lady Gondafrey** page in Notable Figures.
- **Hyustus Staget** — Illuskan human veteran, lawful good, he/him. The uptight captain who keeps the peace in the Dock Ward, and whom most of its shopkeepers and innkeepers know and respect. He has no Notable Figures page.
- **Marek Dunmere** — Illuskan human sergeant, lawful neutral, he/him. He takes reports on the day shift at the North Ward Watch Station. He has no Notable Figures page.
- **The Commander of the Watch** — An office, not a name. It reports to the Open Lord, and no source names the 1492 DR holder. `[research]`
- **Elminster** — Warden of Waterdeep, the head of the City Guard, who answers to the Open Lord. He has no Notable Figures page.

## Grand Game Agenda

The Open Lord's government wants the 500,000 gp back in the treasury and wants it recovered without a public scandal. Laeral does not play the Grand Game herself, and the Lords' Alliance acts for her. The Watch has no stake in the gold and no knowledge of what the factions are chasing. Its part in the Game comes from what people tell it and from the files it keeps, and the factions act around it. A party that reports to the Watch speaks to the Open Lord's government, whether it means to or not.

- **What Laeral believes on day one:** She believes the vault, and so the gold, lies in Neverwinter. She was told so for years, and Jalester's field reports and the Watch's contacts have placed it there too (**A City Watching Itself**; **Lords' Alliance** in the Factions Guide). The Alexandrian text adds that she thinks the gold may be laundered into Neverwinter's rebuilding and has little hope of recovering it there (Alexandrian remix, *2. Other Factions*, printed page 21).
- **What she has heard:** She has heard rumors of Neverember's Enigma and has not connected them with the missing dragons (Alexandrian remix, *2. Other Factions*, printed page 21). She does not know that the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced in Waterdeep or that four villain factions are circling it (**A City Watching Itself**).
- **What the Watch knows:** It sees the Dock Ward brawls between Xanathar's Guild and the Zhentarim, keeps its file on Davil's cell and has closed three complaints against the Cassalanters. No source shows it knowing of the Stone, the Eyes or the vault. `[research]`

## Overview

The City Watch is Waterdeep's police force, and its officers wear green-and-goldenrod doublets and tall steel helmets. The Commander of the Watch reports to the Open Lord, Laeral Silverhand. Citizens report crimes at watch posts marked by a green-and-gold lantern, and most trust the Watch to do its duty. Locals tell it from the City Guard by the saying that the Guard guards the walls while the Watch watches all.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Corylus Thann's veterans:** Corylus, a Masked Lord whom Manshoon's cell blackmails, keeps eight crooked Watch veterans. The published adventure names no officer, so the party never gets a name. Manshoon's cell forces him and Jelenn Urmbrusk to cover up crimes by Manshoon's forces (**The Open Lord and the Masked Lords**).
> - **Thin coverage at Kolat Towers:** Mirt says Watch patrol coverage around Kolat Towers in the Southern Ward has been reduced over eighteen months by someone with civic influence. Jalester calls it informally diminished and suspects Masked Lord influence that he cannot prove (`campaign/structure/arc-i-kolat-towers.md`, pending conversion).
> - **Skeemo's protection:** Manshoon bought Skeemo Weirdbottle with rare alchemical components and protection from Watch inspections (**The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)**).
> - **Who suspects the Cassalanters:** Everyone outside the household and the cult only suspects the family, because it is too nice to be this rich and this purebred. The Watch has closed three complaints against the house and has no hint of a pact (**The Cassalanters**).
> - **Never reveal:** That Corylus Thann or Manshoon's cell holds Watch officers, before the party's own evidence shows it. A player who asks whether the Watch can be bought gets the Overview and whatever the party has seen.

**Tone:** Plain and procedural. Play the Watch as a mostly decent institution that is a step behind the factions and bends under pressure from above, and never as a villain. Give each wiki claim its tag so that the GM can see what the published adventure says and what the wiki adds.
