**Purpose:** Gives the GM the Doom Raiders as a cell of professional criminals: who leads them, what they believe, where they work and what they want from the city. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

The Doom Raiders are a Zhentarim cell of retired adventurers in Waterdeep, who run legitimate-fronted businesses and are at war with the rival cell that peeled off part of their network.

## Tenets

- **Contracts Over Confrontation:** The Doom Raiders hold that the Black Network works best when it behaves like a legitimate enterprise, so they prefer a signed agreement to a fight. Their agreements hold, and their violence always has a purpose.
- **Fronts Worth Keeping:** The cell runs ordinary businesses and wants a legitimate economic foothold, which means making alliances with local guilds and nobles. In its years in the city it has forged several of those alliances, run afoul of other houses and fended off Harper spies.
- **The Network Looks After Its Own:** A member of the cell is one of theirs. When something goes wrong the faction does what it can, and it expects the same loyalty in return. Anyone who betrays or harms Istrid Horn finds her former adventuring companions using every resource they have to ruin them.
- **The True Zhentarim:** The Doom Raiders consider themselves the true Zhentarim of Waterdeep, and they are the older cell in the city. Manshoon's Zhentarim came later and peeled off part of their network, and the two cells despise each other.
- **The War at Home Comes First:** The fight with the rival cell is the Doom Raiders' defining preoccupation, and the Grand Game is secondary to it. Information given to the Doom Raiders will not reach the rival cell.
- **A Quiet City Is Good for Business:** Davil is invested in ending the street violence that the rival cell has caused. A legitimate criminal enterprise cannot run in a city where an illegitimate one keeps shooting people. He keeps the moral ledger as balanced as his business allows.
- **Debt Is a Leash:** Istrid lends at rates comparable to those of noble bankers, and the penalties for missing a payment are severe. She likes having people in her debt, and her thugs and enforcers do the collecting.
- **Killing Is a Favor:** If Ziraj the Hunter sets out to kill someone, a friend in the cell asked him to, and if no one asked he would stop. He does not hunt for his own profit.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** The cell has no sourced creed or initiation. Istrid worships Vergadain, the dwarf god of wealth and luck, and blesses each signed agreement with "Vergadain smile on it". Yagra wears a red jade Zhentarim pendant openly. `[research]` Any further rite, oath or emblem is unsourced in the campaign files.

## Operations

*"The best thing about being part of the Black Network? The Network is always there when you need it."*

The Doom Raiders are a cell of experienced Zhentarim operators who arrived in Waterdeep in 1489 DR, the first Zhentarim cell in the city, and built a network of commercial fronts they genuinely believe in. They are professional criminals who prefer contracts to confrontations and sincerely believe the Black Network works best when it operates like a legitimate enterprise. Their agreements hold. Their violence is purposeful, never gratuitous.

The Doom Raiders and Manshoon's Zhentarim splinter cell are at active war. Manshoon's cell came later and peeled off the Doom Raiders' network, and the two cells despise each other: information shared with the Doom Raiders will not reach Manshoon's agents. However, characters who join the Doom Raiders are joining the older cell and will inevitably be caught in the crossfire as Manshoon moves to eliminate Davil's cell entirely. This is a structural feature of the campaign.

**Primary Contact:** Davil Starsong — a sun elf retired adventurer who leads the Doom Raiders' Waterdeep operation. Charming, direct, and genuinely invested in ending the street violence Manshoon's cell has caused. Tashlyn Yafeera handles the cell's business whenever Davil is unavailable.

**Secondary Contact:** Tashlyn Yafeera — speaks for the cell after Davil's arrest. She has sharper intelligence on Manshoon's faction than Davil and her assignments reflect it.

**Street Contact:** Yagra Stonefist — the half-orc Doom Raider present at the Yawning Portal during **Finding Floon**. She is also Davil's bodyguard.

## Grand Game Agenda

Davil's cell sees an enormous opportunity in the half-million dragons — efforts are underway to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep, and that money could buy the Doom Raiders a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council. PCs who recover the gold and hand it to the Doom Raiders are effectively buying their patrons into continental leadership. This is a real choice with real consequences.

## Overview

The Doom Raiders are a Zhentarim cell of former adventurers who run ordinary businesses in Waterdeep. Davil Starsong leads them from a room at the Yawning Portal. They prefer contracts to street fights, and people who have dealt with them say their agreements hold. They are at odds with a rival Zhentarim cell, and each cell insists that it is the real one.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Davil at the Yawning Portal:** Davil keeps a room upstairs and does all his business in the taproom, which he has decided is safer than a private office and more honest than a warehouse. He is a sun elf who negotiates with grace even when he has been drinking, which is most of the time. He uses an elven lute as a spellcasting focus and carries it on his back in every meeting.
> - **Tashlyn's cover:** Tashlyn is the Master of Arms and Mercenaries. Her cover is a post as bodyguard to the dwarf magister Vorondar Levelstone at the South Gate, and it has earned her the rank of captain in the City Guard. From there she watches the traffic through the gate and keeps it open for her associates. She has built his trust over about two years `[research]`, and in the past year she has been developing sources inside Manshoon's cell. She briefs in few words and expects the listener to infer the rest.
> - **Where the cell works:** Davil holds court at the Yawning Portal and Skeemo runs his shop in the Trades Ward. Istrid lends from a guarded warehouse in the Dock Ward, and the cell keeps a Black Network safe house in a loft above Tarrow's Tallow and Wick on Sail Street. Ziraj takes the back table at Corellon's Crown, where Fala Lefaliir shelters him, and Senna Vael is placed as a server candidate at Trollskull Manor.
> - **The Watch has a file:** The City Watch already keeps a file on the cell, so Davil's people avoid anything that invites scrutiny. The cell operates openly, meets in taverns and recruits adventurers, and its fronts are ordinary businesses.
> - **What the Doom Raiders call the rival cell:** Until **Manshoon Named** is marked, no Doom Raider knows that Manshoon leads the Splinter. Davil, Yagra and the others believe that Urstul Floxin leads it, and they say "the other cell", "Floxin's cell" or "the Splinter". The page text above states the truth for the GM. Use Manshoon's name in front of players only once **Manshoon Named** is marked.
> - **Davil's suspicion:** Davil suspects that someone inside his own cell is leaking to the other side, and he has told nobody.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **Skeemo Weirdbottle is a traitor:** Skeemo has been feeding intelligence to Manshoon's cell and to House Gralhund since before the party was recruited. Manshoon bought him with rare alchemical components and protection from Watch inspections, and Skeemo sends messages to both sides by paper bird. Davil was fond of him and does not say so aloud.

**Tone:** Even and businesslike, with some warmth toward Davil's cell. The narrator states each cell's claim to the Zhentarim name as that cell's own belief and does not rule on which is true.
