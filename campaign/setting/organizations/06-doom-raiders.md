# The Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Player faction; the legitimate Black Network cell in Waterdeep, here since 1489 DR and at active war with Manshoon's Zhentarim splinter.
> - **Contacts:** **Davil Starsong** (primary, Yawning Portal); **Tashlyn Yafeera** (secondary); **Yagra Stonefist** (street contact, Yawning Portal bar)
> - **Not the villain Zhentarim:** the cell that answers to Manshoon is on the **Manshoon's Zhentarim** page in Organizations. Never conflate the two.
> - **Mechanics:** Grand Game stance, quest hooks, first meeting, renown, ranks and missions are on the **Doom Raiders** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**, **Faction Outposts**, **Kolat Towers**, **Vault of Dragons**

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the Doom Raiders as a cell of professional criminals: where they came from, what they believe, how the cell is built and how it works, who its friends and enemies are, and what it wants from the city. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

The Doom Raiders are a Zhentarim cell of retired adventurers in Waterdeep, who run legitimate-fronted businesses and are at war with the rival cell that peeled off part of their network.

## History

The entries run from the founding of the Black Network to the day the campaign starts. No source dates the Doom Raiders' adventuring career, so the cell's own story begins in the late 1480s.

- **1261 DR, the Black Network is founded:** Manshoon founded the Zhentarim in Zhentil Keep as a mercantile network that opened trade routes between the Moonsea and the Sword Coast, and it grew into a company that trades mercenaries and goods (**The Founding of Chains**). The wiki calls him the founder and says the company first existed to support his rule over the Moonsea (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Zhentarim*, citing *Cloak & Dagger*, edition not tagged). The published adventure calls him "one of the founders", and the campaign keeps that wording (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The wiki also gives "Black Network" as a name for the whole organization (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Zhentarim*, citing the *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting*, 3e).
- **1370 DR to 1383 DR, the Network outlives its founder and its city:** Fzoul Chembryl and Lord Orgauth killed Manshoon in 1370 DR, and the Zhentarim carried on under Fzoul. Zhentil Keep fell in stages, and the Shadovar razed what remained in 1383 DR (**The Founding of Chains**, citing the Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Zhentil Keep*, 4e). Efforts to reclaim and rebuild the Keep are underway in 1492 DR, and the Zhentarim are reforming a council (see Grand Game Agenda).
- **Late 1400s DR, the Doom Raiders adventure:** Five adventurers, described in the published adventure as unscrupulous, made a trade of plundering the lairs of liches, which some call "dooms" (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Doom Raiders*, 5e). Davil still wears a charm bracelet hung with small trophies from the monsters the party killed (**Davil Starsong**). Design tag: the wiki writes the Doom Raiders in the past tense as a former adventuring party, and the campaign treats them as a working cell.
- **Late 1480s DR, they join the Zhentarim:** The five gave up adventuring and joined the reformed Black Network (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Doom Raiders*, 5e). The wiki places the Zhentarim's headquarters at Darkhold in the Western Heartlands as of 1489 DR, under a leader called the Pereghost, and calls it a mercenary company and mercantile organization. The same page says the Zhentarim spent two centuries as a cadre of self-serving thieves, spies, assassins and malevolent wizards, which is the reputation the Doom Raiders now work against (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Zhentarim*, citing the *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, 5e). `[research]` The second half of that sentence is an inference, because no source says why the cell keeps to legitimate fronts.
- **1489 DR, the cell reaches Waterdeep:** The Doom Raiders are a cell of experienced Zhentarim operators who arrived in Waterdeep in 1489 DR, the first Zhentarim cell in the city, and built a network of commercial fronts they genuinely believe in. They were sent to establish a Zhentarim foothold, and no source names who sent them (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Doom Raiders*, 5e). In the three years since, they have forged alliances with various nobles and guilds, run afoul of others and fended off Harper spies (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Design tag: the published adventure says only "three years ago", and the campaign fixes the year and makes the cell the first in the city, with Manshoon's cell arriving after it (**Manshoon**).
- **About 1490 DR, a rival cell appears:** About two years before the campaign, a clone of Manshoon settled in Kolat Towers and began to build a cell of his own (**The Founding of Chains**). `[research]` The year is derived from "about two years" and is stated by no source. Manshoon's cell came later and peeled off the Doom Raiders' network, and the two cells despise each other: information shared with the Doom Raiders will not reach Manshoon's agents. Design tag: the wiki treats the Zhentarim as Manshoon's company throughout, and the campaign splits it into two cells that both claim the name.
- **Nightal 1491 DR, a street war begins:** Manshoon's agents went to Skullport to propose an alliance with Xanathar's Guild, and the beholder killed the envoys (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Xanathar's Guild**). The street war that followed in the Dock Ward has thrown the Doom Raiders' plans into upheaval, since the published adventure says Manshoon's war on the Guild disrupted their effort to gain a legitimate foothold (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Before Ches 1, 1492 DR, a leak:** Skeemo Weirdbottle sold his services to Manshoon's cell and to House Gralhund, and he sends messages to both sides by paper bird (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Skeemo Weirdbottle**). The Secret below gives the terms of the sale. Davil suspects a leak in his own cell and has told nobody.
- **Ches 1, 1492 DR, the campaign opens:** The Doom Raiders and Manshoon's Zhentarim splinter cell are at active war. Manshoon is moving to eliminate Davil's cell entirely, and anyone who works with the older cell stands in the crossfire. The war is a standing feature of the city. Davil keeps his room at the Yawning Portal, Istrid lends from her warehouse, Skeemo keeps his shop and Tashlyn keeps her post at the South Gate.

## Beliefs

*"The best thing about being part of the Black Network? The Network is always there when you need it."*

- **Contracts Over Confrontation:** The Doom Raiders hold that the Black Network works best when it behaves like a legitimate enterprise, so they prefer a signed agreement to a fight. Their agreements hold, and their violence always has a purpose. The Alexandrian remix says they are not good and not sadistic, and that they can be trusted to honor what they sign (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). Davil frames the whole enterprise as helping people in need, and the published adventure defines that help as loans, mercenaries and other services (**Davil Starsong**; *Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Fronts Worth Keeping:** The cell runs ordinary businesses and wants a legitimate economic foothold, which means making alliances with local guilds and nobles. In its years in the city it has forged several of those alliances, run afoul of other houses and fended off Harper spies. The published adventure says the cell is desperate for that foothold, because the Masked Lords, the nobility and the guilds have kept the Zhentarim out of Waterdeep's politics for generations (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Alexandrian remix notes that the wider Black Network uses legitimate businesses as cover for smuggling, information brokering and coercion, and that the Doom Raiders believe in their own fronts (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B).
- **The Network Looks After Its Own:** A member of the cell is one of theirs. When something goes wrong the faction does what it can, and it expects the same loyalty in return. Anyone who betrays or harms Istrid Horn finds her former adventuring companions using every resource they have to ruin them. The loyalty is personal as well as institutional. Yagra will reach a threatened friend's door at 2 AM in full armor, and Ziraj taught her the trail marks he leaves when he is wounded (**Yagra Stonefist**; **Ziraj the Hunter**).
- **The True Zhentarim:** The Doom Raiders consider themselves the true Zhentarim of Waterdeep, and they are the older cell in the city. Manshoon's Zhentarim came later and peeled off part of their network, and the two cells despise each other. The published adventure states the claim directly (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Each cell can point to a founder's tradition, since the Network began as Manshoon's company and the Doom Raiders joined it after it reformed, and neither cell will concede the name.
- **The War at Home Comes First:** The fight with the rival cell is the Doom Raiders' defining preoccupation, and the Grand Game is secondary to it. Information given to the Doom Raiders will not reach the rival cell. The published adventure adds that Manshoon's war on the Xanathar Guild threw the cell's plans into upheaval (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The cell believes Urstul Floxin leads its rival and does not know whom he serves (see GM Notes).
- **A Quiet City Is Good for Business:** Davil is invested in ending the street violence that the rival cell has caused. A legitimate criminal enterprise cannot run in a city where an illegitimate one keeps shooting people. He keeps the moral ledger as balanced as his business allows. Davil also prefers to keep the peace with Xanathar's Guild through negotiation, and he tells newcomers that he and his colleagues want to restore peace to the streets (**Davil Starsong**; *Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Debt Is a Leash:** Istrid lends at rates comparable to those of noble bankers, and the penalties for missing a payment are severe. She likes having people in her debt, and her thugs and enforcers do the collecting. Her lending is illegal, and the published adventure names the Cassalanters and the Irlingstars as the banking houses whose rates hers match (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Istrid wants every loan to return influence, favors and leverage along with the gold (**Istrid Horn**).
- **Killing Is a Favor:** If Ziraj the Hunter sets out to kill someone, a friend in the cell asked him to, and if no one asked he would stop. He does not hunt for his own profit. The published adventure names the friends whose word counts as Davil, Istrid, Skeemo and Tashlyn (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Doctrine and Rites:** The cell has no sourced creed or initiation. Istrid worships Vergadain, the dwarf god of wealth and luck, and blesses each signed agreement with "Vergadain smile on it". Yagra wears a red jade Zhentarim pendant openly, and it bears the Zhentarim's winged-serpent sigil (**Istrid Horn**; **Dock Ward**; **Yagra Stonefist**; Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). The wiki gives the Zhentarim's banner as a winged snake biting a coin (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Zhentarim*, 5e). The same page lists Bane as the Zhentarim's favored deity and Cyric as a former one, and it does not tag an edition for that line. The campaign ties Bane to Manshoon's cell through Yellowspire, and no file gives the Doom Raiders a god other than Istrid's Vergadain (**Bane**). `[research]` Any further rite, oath or emblem of the cell's own is unsourced in the campaign files.

## Structure

The Waterdeep cell is five Masters of the Black Network's local division, each with a post and a front. A bodyguard, collectors, sellswords and informants work beneath them, and no source counts them.

**Primary Contact:** Davil Starsong — a sun elf retired adventurer who leads the Doom Raiders' Waterdeep operation. Charming, direct, and genuinely invested in ending the street violence Manshoon's cell has caused. Tashlyn Yafeera handles the cell's business whenever Davil is unavailable.

**Secondary Contact:** Tashlyn Yafeera — speaks for the cell whenever Davil is unavailable. She has sharper intelligence on Manshoon's faction than Davil and relies on it when she acts for the cell.

**Street Contact:** Yagra Stonefist — the half-orc Doom Raider who works the bar at the Yawning Portal. She is also Davil's bodyguard.

- **Above the cell:** No source says whom the five Masters answer to. The wiki names the Pereghost as the Zhentarim's leader as of 1489 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Zhentarim*, citing the *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, 5e), and the Zhentarim Council is reforming (see Grand Game Agenda). `[research]` No file says the cell reports to either.
- **The five Masters:** Each holds a title from the Black Network's Waterdeep division (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
  - **Davil Starsong, Master of Opportunities and Negotiations:** He sniffs out profitable deals, makes friends easily and can arrange meetings with influential nobles and guild members. He leads the Waterdeep operation and holds court at the Yawning Portal.
  - **Istrid Horn, Master of Trade and Coin:** She runs an illegal lending operation from a heavily guarded warehouse in the Dock Ward, and her thugs and enforcers collect.
  - **Skeemo Weirdbottle, Master of Magic:** He keeps Weirdbottle's Concoctions in the Trades Ward as his cover and crafts magical potions for his Zhentarim friends.
  - **Tashlyn Yafeera, Master of Arms and Mercenaries:** She provides armor, weapons and training to the sellswords on the Network's payroll, and her bodyguard post at the South Gate is her cover.
  - **Ziraj the Hunter, Master of Assassination:** He kills from the rooftops with an oversized bow and keeps a back table at Corellon's Crown.
- **Size:** The wiki counts five members (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Doom Raiders*, 5e), which matches the five Masters. `[research]` Nothing sourced gives the number of collectors, sellswords and informants beneath them.
- **Ranks and titles:** The Masters' titles are the cell's only sourced ranks. Davil is the leader, as the wiki infobox and the published adventure agree (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Doom Raiders*, 5e), and Tashlyn speaks for the cell when he is away. The ranks that members earn are a game rule and live in the Factions Guide.
- **Headquarters, fronts and holdings:** Davil holds court at the Yawning Portal and Skeemo runs his shop in the Trades Ward. Istrid lends from a guarded warehouse in the Dock Ward, and the cell keeps a Black Network safe house in a loft above Tarrow's Tallow and Wick on Sail Street. Ziraj takes the back table at Corellon's Crown, where Fala Lefaliir shelters him. Tarrow's is a chandler's shop at 22 Sail Street run by Wenna Tarrow, and Corellon's Crown is a greenhouse in the North Ward (**Dock Ward**; **North Ward**). The cell also keeps a hay and feed yard in the Field Ward and a boarding house near the Southern Ward's market as safe houses, and the Watch does not know the boarding house's address (**Field Ward**; **Southern Ward**).

## Methods and Tactics

- **Contracts:** They are professional criminals who prefer contracts to confrontations and sincerely believe the Black Network works best when it operates like a legitimate enterprise. Their agreements hold. Their violence is purposeful, never gratuitous. Davil negotiates with grace and aplomb even when he has been drinking, and he can introduce a client to the right Master (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Fronts and discretion:** The City Watch already keeps a file on the cell, so Davil's people avoid anything that invites scrutiny. The cell operates openly, meets in taverns and recruits adventurers, and its fronts are ordinary businesses. Davil does his business in a taproom, Skeemo sells from a shop and Tashlyn watches a gate, so each Master's work looks like a trade.
- **Recruiting:** The Black Network has an open recruitment policy that prizes tenacity and loyalty without requiring either (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). The Doom Raiders approach evil-aligned or morally ambiguous adventurers first, and Davil offers a place to those who are interested (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Funding:** The cell earns from lending, from the sellswords Tashlyn supplies, from discounted potions and poisons at Skeemo's shop, and from the introductions Davil sells (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Ziraj can be hired for a killing, in return for an undisclosed favor that the cell calls in later (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). `[research]` No source gives the cell a treasury or a yearly income.
- **Lending:** Istrid lends to people in need of coin, and she treats every loan as leverage (**Istrid Horn**; **Dock Ward**). Emmek Frewn, a refugee from Neverwinter who runs a pub in the North Ward, owes her a loan that Zhentarim enforcers collect (**Emmek Frewn**; **North Ward**). When her enforcers prove inadequate she can call on her old adventuring companions (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Intelligence:** Tashlyn's post at the South Gate shows her the traffic through the gate and lets her keep it open for her associates (**Tashlyn Yafeera**; **The Code Legal and the Magisters**). Skeemo hears the trade gossip across his counter, and Davil asks one more question than the conversation needs (**Skeemo Weirdbottle**; **Davil Starsong**). The Alexandrian remix lists information brokering among the Black Network's trades (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B).
- **Fighting:** Each Master has a habit in a fight. Davil negotiates first, fights with a swashbuckler's precision second and retreats to protect the cell's leadership third. Istrid lets her enforcers fight while she secures her ledgers. Tashlyn closes distance and stays there, Ziraj fires from elevation and avoids melee, and Yagra fights with her pride (**Davil Starsong**; **Istrid Horn**; **Tashlyn Yafeera**; **Ziraj the Hunter**; **Yagra Stonefist**). The City Watch has heard of a hulking figure on the rooftops whose long black arrows kill from a distance (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **What the cell will not do:** It will not use gratuitous violence, and the Alexandrian remix calls its members criminals who are not sadistic (Alexandrian remix, Appendix B). It will not share information with Manshoon's cell, and the two cells will not cooperate. Waterdeep's laws discourage the Doom Raiders from attacking an enemy openly, so they go after the enemy's business first (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Ziraj will not kill for his own profit.

## Relationships

Each entry gives the stance on Ches 1, 1492 DR.

- **The Harpers (rival):** The Doom Raiders have fended off Harper spies since they reached Waterdeep, and Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg, a Harper agent, spends his afternoons and evenings at the Yawning Portal watching Zhentarim agents (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Castle Ward**). The wiki says the Harpers and the Zhentarim all but waged war for about a century after 1269 DR (**The Harpers**). The Harpers know only that the Black Network has split.
- **The Lords' Alliance (neutral):** Jalester Silvermane tells the two cells apart and calls the Splinter "not Davil's people" (**The Lords' Alliance**; **Lords' Alliance** in the Factions Guide). The Watch already keeps a file on the Doom Raiders, but no source gives the Alliance a day-one dealing with them. `[research]`
- **The Emerald Enclave (neutral):** Enclave speakers name only the Splinter or "the other cell" among the Zhentarim (**The Emerald Enclave**). No source gives the Enclave a day-one dealing with Davil's cell. `[research]`
- **The Order of the Gauntlet (neutral):** Savra Belabranta keeps Davil's cell apart from the Splinter, and the Order hunts diabolists and aberrant evil (**The Order of the Gauntlet**). No source gives the Order a day-one dealing with the Doom Raiders. `[research]`
- **Force Grey (neutral):** Vajra Safahr knows that the Black Network has split, and Force Grey speakers call the Zhentarim's arcane cell "the Splinter" (**Force Grey**). No source gives Force Grey a day-one dealing with Davil's cell. `[research]`
- **Bregan D'aerthe (neutral):** A Bregan D'aerthe observer watches the well from the balcony of the Yawning Portal, where Davil keeps a room (**Castle Ward**). No source records a dealing between the company and the cell. `[research]`
- **Xanathar's Guild (enemy):** Xanathar wants to wipe out anyone it perceives as a Zhentarim operative, and the Guild treats those who deal with known Black Network operatives as enemies (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Xanathar's Guild**). Davil would rather keep the peace with the Guild through negotiation, and Manshoon's war on it has thrown the cell's plans into upheaval (**Davil Starsong**; *Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim (enemy):** The Splinter peeled off a large part of the Doom Raiders' network, and the two cells despise each other and will not cooperate (**The Founding of Chains**). Manshoon regards the Doom Raiders as deserters who stole his name (**Manshoon**). `[research]` Davil, Yagra and the others believe Urstul Floxin leads the rival cell, so they do not know whom it serves (see GM Notes).
- **The Cassalanters (neutral):** Istrid's rates are comparable to the Cassalanters', one of the noble banking houses she competes with (*Dragon Heist*, 5e; **Istrid Horn**). The cell shares the city's suspicion that the family is too rich and too pleasant, and no source gives it any dealing with them (**The Cassalanters**). `[research]`
- **House Gralhund (neutral, with a paid source):** Skeemo Weirdbottle has fed intelligence to the Gralhunds and Manshoon's cell since well before Ches 1. The cell as a whole has no dealings with the house (**House Gralhund**).
- **The Open Lord's Watch (wary rival):** The Watch keeps a file on the cell, and Tashlyn holds the rank of captain in the City Guard through her bodyguard post (**The Open Lord's Watch**; **The City Watch**). Captain Staget reasons that if Xanathar's Guild and the Zhentarim want to destroy each other, the Watch may as well let them (*Dragon Heist*, 5e).
- **The Open Lord and the Masked Lords (neutral, shut out):** The Masked Lords, the nobility and the guilds are strong enough to have kept the Zhentarim out of Waterdeep's politics (*Dragon Heist*, 5e). Any Masked Lord can overturn a magister's ruling, and Tashlyn's cover is a post under one magister (**The Code Legal and the Magisters**). No source gives Laeral Silverhand a day-one dealing with the cell. `[research]`

## Grand Game Agenda

Davil's cell sees an enormous opportunity in the half-million dragons — efforts are underway to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep, and that money could buy the Doom Raiders a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council, which would give a regional cell real influence across the continent.

## Overview

The Doom Raiders are a Zhentarim cell of former adventurers who run ordinary businesses in Waterdeep. Davil Starsong leads them from a room at the Yawning Portal. They prefer contracts to street fights, and people who have dealt with them say their agreements hold. They are at odds with a rival Zhentarim cell, and each cell insists that it is the real one.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Davil at the Yawning Portal:** Davil keeps a room upstairs and does all his business in the taproom, which he has decided is safer than a private office and more honest than a warehouse. He is a sun elf who negotiates with grace even when he has been drinking, which is most of the time. He uses an elven lute as a spellcasting focus and carries it on his back in every meeting.
> - **Tashlyn's cover:** Tashlyn is the Master of Arms and Mercenaries. Her cover is a post as bodyguard to the dwarf magister Vorondar Levelstone at the South Gate, and it has earned her the rank of captain in the City Guard. From there she watches the traffic through the gate and keeps it open for her associates. She has built his trust over about two years `[research]`, and in the past year she has been developing sources inside Manshoon's cell. She briefs in few words and expects the listener to infer the rest.
> - **What the Doom Raiders call the rival cell:** Manshoon directs the rival cell through Urstul Floxin, but no Doom Raider has heard his name connected to it. Davil, Yagra and the others believe that Floxin leads it, and they call it "the other cell", "Floxin's cell" or "the Splinter". The History above states the truth for the GM.
> - **Davil's suspicion:** Davil suspects that someone inside his own cell is leaking to the other side, and he has told nobody.

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **Skeemo Weirdbottle is a traitor:** Skeemo has been feeding intelligence to Manshoon's cell and to House Gralhund since well before 1 Ches 1492 DR. Manshoon bought him with rare alchemical components and protection from Watch inspections, and Skeemo sends messages to both sides by paper bird. Davil was fond of him and does not say so aloud.

**Tone:** Even and businesslike, with some warmth toward Davil's cell. The narrator states each cell's claim to the Zhentarim name as that cell's own belief and does not rule on which is true.
