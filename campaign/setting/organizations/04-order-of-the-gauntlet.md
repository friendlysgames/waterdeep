# The Order of the Gauntlet

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Player faction; martial agents hunting diabolists and aberrant evil across the city, and suspicious of the Cassalanters.
> - **Contacts:** **Savra Belabranta** (primary)
> - **Savra's past:** She is in her late twenties and looks older, as people do who have been through something and come out the other side. She wears her dark hair back, has a strong jaw and speaks with the patient, measured manner of someone who learned to think before speaking and found the habit worth keeping. She spent three years in the Howling Hatred elemental cult and left it in 1491 DR, about a year ago, before she was an Order knight. She does not hide that she belonged to the cult, and she speaks of what she did there only with people she has come to trust. She is making amends, and the Order is the work she found that made that meaningful.

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the Order of the Gauntlet as a faith-driven order: what it believes, who leads its Waterdeep chapter, and what it suspects about one noble family. The page keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

The Order of the Gauntlet is a fellowship of paladins, clerics and devout martial warriors who hunt evil before it reaches the city, and its small Waterdeep chapter works out of the Halls of Justice under Savra Belabranta.

## Tenets

- **Seek Evil Out:** The Order's mission is to find and destroy evil before it gains a foothold, and it does not wait for evil to come to it (*Dragon Heist*). In Waterdeep, the surge of violence sends its members looking for adventurers who can help return peace to the city. The wiki adds that the Order stays vigilant against threats to the Realms and investigates secretive organizations when it has to (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, 5e).
- **Faith Is the Weapon:** Members hold that faith is their most potent weapon against evil, and the wiki says it may rest in a personal god, in close friends or in oneself (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, 5e). The same source says great challenges call for heroic demonstrations of strength and valor. Savra asks the same of anyone who would serve with her: the willingness to act when acting is difficult.
- **Judge the Deed:** The wiki gives the Order a rule that it is justified to punish an act of evil and never a thought or a feeling (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, 5e). In the campaign this becomes a demand for proof. Savra files only on paper a magistrate would read, such as a ledger, a signed statement or a witness who will swear on the record, and Order agents may not enter a place of worship without a magistrate's warrant.
- **Honesty Over Management:** Savra cannot work with people who manage around her, and she knows when it is happening. She wants to build an Order chapter that values honesty above all.
- **Atonement Through Work:** Savra serves Tyr to regain her honor and to atone for the evil she did as a member of the Howling Hatred (*Dragon Heist*). The Order took her in after she left the cult, and her goodness is hard-won in a way that makes it more convincing. Savra holds this tenet most firmly, and no source makes it a rule for every member.
- **Diabolism First:** The Order ranks the threats it hunts in a fixed order, with diabolism first, aberrant evil second and everything else after. Savra counts Xanathar's Guild's use of intellect devourers as an abomination in her theological framework. She also wants to learn what the Cassalanters conceal behind their charity.
- **Hunt the Evil in Front of You:** The Order stays outside the Grand Game and does not position for the vault or compete for faction influence. Savra cares about the vault gold only as a question of who might seize it. She fears that 500,000 gp would fund whatever she suspects the Sea Ward family of doing, and she thinks the Splinter or Xanathar's Guild would be nearly as bad. She has no objection to the city treasury holding it.
- **Give Back from the Haul:** Knights live on less than they earn and give a quarter of their share of a large haul to a cause that looks after people who cannot look after themselves (Operations). The Alexandrian remix sets the share at a quarter of a hoard. In the Field Ward, where the Watch does not patrol, the Order fills part of the gap, and Savra wants the ward to see that someone is paying attention.
- **The Temples Are Friends:** The Order treats the Halls of Justice, the Temple of Helm and the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield as friendly to its members, and the priests of the three temples help the Order without belonging to it. Worship of Helm, Torm or Tyr only decides whom Savra addresses first, and nobody is tested for faith at the door.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** Recruits swear an oath to find and destroy evil in all its forms, to protect those who cannot protect themselves and to stand when standing is required. They speak it while wearing a silver gauntlet, and a priest of Tyr seals it with a brief invocation. Members display the holy symbols of their gods (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, 5e), and Savra calls Tyr the Maimed God. [research] The campaign files give no written creed, prayer or holy day beyond the oath and the motto.

## Operations

*"Evil does not rest. Neither do we."*

The Order of the Gauntlet is a fellowship of paladins, clerics, and devout martial warriors who pursue and destroy evil actively — without waiting for it to come to them. In Waterdeep, they function as a quasi-religious law-enforcement body: more aggressive and less subtle than the City Watch, and considerably less interested in political nuance. They are exactly the allies you want when facing devil-worshippers or a mind-flayer-backed crime lord.

The Order has a particular interest in the Cassalanters — a Sea Ward noble family whose impeccable public piety and conspicuous generosity the Order finds deeply suspicious. Months of quiet surveillance have produced no actionable evidence. Something is concealed behind the charity and the temple attendance; the Order cannot yet prove what. The Order grows more impatient for hard evidence it can act on. The Order also expects its knights to live on less than they earn, and it asks them to give a quarter of their share of a large haul to a cause that looks after people who cannot look after themselves.

The Waterdeep chapter is small. It is Savra, a clerk's office in the east annex of the Halls of Justice, and a rotation of about a dozen Knights and Priests who answer to her. The Halls of Justice is the temple of Tyr in the Castle Ward, and the priests of its three temples help the Order without belonging to it.

**Primary Contact:** Savra Belabranta — a knight of the Order from a noble family known for raising griffons for the Griffon Cavalry. She spent three years in the Howling Hatred elemental cult and left it in 1491 DR, about a year ago, and she speaks of it only with people she trusts. She has channeled that shame into an iron commitment to justice.

## Key Members

- **Savra Belabranta** — Neutral Good Tethyrian human knight; Order faction contact, atonement-driven. See the **Savra Belabranta** page in Notable Figures.
- **Sister Valdra** — Lawful Good half-orc cleric of Tyr; unofficial Order operative (unsanctioned by Savra); holds three independent pieces of Cassalanter intelligence. See the **Sister Valdra** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Agenda

The Order will move aggressively against the Cassalanters once they have solid evidence of whatever the family is hiding. Savra goes well out of her way for knights who give generously.

## Overview

The Order of the Gauntlet is a fellowship of paladins, clerics, and devout martial warriors who pursue and destroy evil actively. In Waterdeep, a small chapter works from the Halls of Justice, the temple of Tyr in the Castle Ward. Its knights are more aggressive and less subtle than the City Watch, and they hunt diabolists wherever the trail leads. They watch one Sea Ward noble family whose generosity seems too good to be true, though they have found nothing yet.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Origins:** The wiki calls the Order one of the newest power groups in Faerûn as of 1489 DR, with chapters in several western cities (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, 5e). In the 1480s it worked with the Harpers to track Cult of the Dragon shipments along the Sword Coast. Its symbol is a gauntlet grasping a sword by the blade. *Dragon Heist* says some members are natives of Waterdeep and others come to the city on temple business.
> - **The three temples:** The Halls of Justice stands in the Castle Ward, the Temple of Helm (Helm's Hall) in the Southern Ward and the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield in the Dock Ward. The chapter's clerk's office, armory and chapel sit in the east annex of the Halls. Each temple helps Order members without belonging to the Order.
> - **Savra's past:** The Howling Hatred was an elemental air cult that served Yan-C-Bin, the Prince of Evil Air, and it is not active during the campaign. *Princes of the Apocalypse* has the cult indoctrinate Savra, and the wiki has her join the Feathergale Knights, a cover group for the cult (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Savra Belabranta*, 5e). The campaign has her leave in 1491 DR, a year before the campaign starts.
> - **What the Order suspects:** Savra and her chapter suspect a diabolist or infernalist hand behind the Cassalanters, and they name no pact. Nobody in the Order knows what the family worships. Their file holds no legal evidence, and it records that the Watch closed every complaint against the family without findings.
> - **Sister Valdra:** She is a half-orc cleric of Tyr who built a case against the Cassalanters on her own time, from a servant's story the Watch dismissed. Savra does not know her. She holds the same suspicion as the Order and none of its proof.
> - **Allies:** Hlam of the Order of the Even-Handed is a staunch ally of the Order (*Dragon Heist*). The wiki says the Harpers and the Order work in very different ways and share the same goals for Faerûn (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, 5e).
> - **A campaign-only rule:** Order agents may not enter a place of worship without a magistrate's warrant. The published Code Legal does not state this rule, so voice it as ordinary law and do not attribute it to the handout.

**Tone:** Earnest and direct, with a dry edge for the Order's impatience with politics. The narrator respects Savra's conviction and treats her past plainly, and it neither excuses the Order's bluntness nor mocks it.
