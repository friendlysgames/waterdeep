# House Gralhund

> Structuring draft.

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> - **Campaign role:** Minor Grand Game faction, not a villain faction. The house wants the vault's gold to lift its standing in Waterdeep's high society and to win Lady Yalah a seat among the Masked Lords.
> - **Contacts:** **Yalah Gralhund** (head of the house), **Orond Gralhund** (receives callers), **Chirada** (field operative)
> - **Mechanics:** Game mechanics for running the house are on the **House Gralhund** page in the Factions Guide
> - **Featured in:** **Fireball!**, **Gralhund Villa**

**Purpose:** Gives the GM House Gralhund as a minor noble house with large ambitions: where it came from, what it believes, how it is built, how it schemes, who it deals with and what it hides. The page presents the house as it stands on Ches 1, 1492 DR and keeps lore only. Everything above the Overview is for the GM.

House Gralhund is a minor noble house of Waterdeep that trades in arms and hired swords, has spent years trying to buy its way into the city's inner circle, and now hopes the vault's gold will finish the job.

## History

- **1239 to 1422 DR:** The family chronicle kept in the villa's library covers births, deaths and family events across these years. The family has recast it to show the house in the most favorable light. It also records that the Gralhunds made pacts with devils in years past, which produced a strain of tieflings. Those members were sent to a Gralhund estate in Yartar, according to the chronicle's epilogue, and passing references mention Waterdhavian relatives born with tails.
- **1260 DR:** The wiki lists the Gralhunds as a noble house of Waterdeep from the Year of the Broken Blade, with contracting hireswords and making weapons as its main business (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Gralhund*, 3e). The chronicle's first entry predates this by twenty-one years.
- **1372 DR:** The house had 26 members and was competing fiercely with House Amcathra in blade-making. It sold better swords at lower prices by using a steel alloy with traces of mithral, bartered from a duergar foundry in Skullport (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Gralhund*, 3e).
- **1491 DR:** Many Lords of Waterdeep were killed in a short time, the plot the novel *Death Masks* describes. Yalah and Orond expected her to fill one of the vacancies and spent many promises and bribes on it, and she was passed over (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Orond Gralhund*, 5e). The published adventure says only "some years ago", and the Alexandrian text says the Gralhunds believe Yalah should have been elevated after these events. Orond became deeply angry and has stayed that way.
- **Marpenoth 7, 1491 DR (year derived):** Jarlaxle's sale ledger records nimblewright NW-02 as sold to "O. Gralhund of Saerdoun Street."
- **The year before Ches 1, 1492 DR:** Agents of Manshoon's Zhentarim approached the Gralhunds, and the two sides formed an alliance. The house gives the Zhentarim coin and the use of the villa as a refuge, and it receives the Black Network's intelligence in return, including what the Zhentarim learn about the Masked Lords. The wiki places the alliance in 1492 DR (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Gralhund*, 5e).
- **Months before Ches 1, 1492 DR:** The house embedded agents in Renaer Neverember's household. They spotted Dalakhar watching Renaer, identified him as an agent of Lord Neverember, and followed him when he was pulled off that assignment to Xanathar's Guild.
- **Before Ches 1, 1492 DR:** The house bought the services of Skeemo Weirdbottle, the Doom Raiders' Master of Magic, who passes intelligence to the Gralhunds and to Manshoon's Zhentarim by paper bird.
- **The night before Ches 1, 1492 DR:** Zhentarim agents seized Renaer Neverember on the night Floon Blagmaar was taken. The house paid for the operation, and Yalah arranged it to gain leverage over Dalakhar.

## Beliefs

- **We See Both Sides:** The family motto describes the house's habit of keeping more than one arrangement open and using each to read the others. The Zhentarim shelter in the villa as a shield, and the Gralhunds use them to learn the identities and weaknesses of the Masked Lords.
- **Information Is Currency:** The house pays for what other people know. It has agents in Renaer Neverember's household, a paid source inside the Doom Raiders and the Black Network's spies at its disposal, and Yalah collects secrets from nobles, guildmasters and commoners alike.
- **Take the Seat by Any Lever:** Yalah expects to become a Masked Lord and believes it is a matter of time. Once she knows the identities and secrets of enough Lords, she means to bribe, blackmail or extort her way into their ranks and then change the government to secure the house's prosperity for generations.
- **A Slight Is Remembered:** Yalah's earlier bids for a seat were thwarted, and she feels banked fury at the Lords who passed her over. The vault's gold is her last best chance. Orond shares the anger and also hopes that her appointment will raise him.
- **Negotiate, and Let the Hired Help Fight:** Yalah does not fight unless she is cornered, and even then she negotiates. She feigns innocence, redirects blame and relies on Hrabbaz and her Zhentarim retainers. Orond barricades himself behind furniture and cowers.
- **The Wife Runs the House:** Orond is the patriarch and the face of the family. He leaves the plotting and socializing to Yalah, to whom he is devoted and of whom he is afraid, and Yalah manages him more than she loves him.
- **Guard the Family Name:** Yalah is meticulous about the family's name and employs people whose purpose is to protect it. The chronicle in the library is locked and trapped, and its pages are recast to flatter the family.
- **Arms and Hired Swords Are the Trade:** The house contracts mercenaries and manufactures weapons. Orond spends his days watching mercenaries train, paying bills and complaining about the cost of doing business in Waterdeep.
- **Punch Above the Weight:** Many dismiss the Gralhunds as petty schemers, but the house has had to be clever and conniving to compete in Waterdeep's politics. It is struggling against organizations that are far larger and have far more resources.
- **Keep the Devil-Blood and the Cult Out of Sight:** The house hides its infernal ancestry and a devotion that its servants share. The details are in the Secret callout after the Overview.
- **Doctrine and Rites:** [research] The campaign gives the house no creed beyond its motto, and no source settles the cult's hierarchy or rites. See Draft Notes.

## Structure

- **Head of house:** Yalah is the true power in the house, and Orond is the patriarch who fronts it. Yalah commands the Zhentarim forces at the villa as her employees.
- **Family:** Orond and Yalah have three children. Zartan is 13 and Greth is 10, and both are kept at the villa. Tomassin is 18 and visits a tiefling cousin in Yartar.
- **Household:** Aldric Cray, the head butler, has screened Lady Yalah's visitors for eleven years. The staff also include veteran house guards who rest in a barracks beside the manor, two cooks, a head maid who carries the ring of keys, four maids and valets in the servants' wing, two nursemaids, and a stableboy named Ike who is 14.
- **Retainers:** Hrabbaz guards both Gralhunds and is more loyal to Yalah. Hurv Taldred keeps the yard with two mastiffs, and the Gralhunds paid a necromancer to ritually change the three of them into shadows from sundown to dawn. Chirada works as an intelligence operative in the city.
- **The nimblewright:** The household nimblewright is NW-02 in Jarlaxle's ledger, which names the buyer as O. Gralhund of Saerdoun Street. It was built as a household model with combat programming. It stands dormant against the northeast corner of Orond's study and activates on any Alarm trigger or when Yalah speaks its command word from her bedroom.
- **The Zhentarim squad:** A squad of Manshoon's Zhentarim lives at the villa under Urstul Floxin, the highest-ranking among them. Yalah treats them as her own spy network.
- **The villa:** The walled villa fills a block of Saerdoun Street in the North Ward. The wall is 12 feet high, the gate sits under a two-story carriage arch, and an arcane lock on the gate admits the family, its staff and the household nimblewright. An Alarm spell network covers the perimeter and the interior doors, and it sounds on forced doors, loud combat and visible spellcasting.
- **Finances:** The house earns from arms and mercenary contracts and pays for Black Network operations in Waterdeep. Yalah's black riding horse, Maladar, wears horseshoes of speed and carries 2,000 gp in gold trade bars in her saddlebags.
- **Standing:** The house is one of the lesser noble houses that keep villas in the North Ward, and the Alexandrian text calls it a minor faction. Its crest is a sword above a shield in green and silver, and the seal is copied often enough that Yalah has become attentive to misuse of her correspondence.

## Methods and Tactics

- **Embed and observe:** The house plants agents inside other households and watches who watches them. Renaer is estranged from his father, so the house knew the placement was a long shot, and it paid off: the agents in his household caught Dalakhar's surveillance and traced him to Xanathar's Guild. Orond's journal gives the two informants the code names "Eagle" and "Catoblepas."
- **Buy the inside man:** The house bought Skeemo Weirdbottle, who sells to Manshoon's Zhentarim and to the Gralhunds at once.
- **Use the ally's resources and keep its own:** The Zhentarim supply spies and intelligence, and the house supplies coin and cover. The Gralhunds do not trust the Black Network entirely, so Yalah keeps resources of her own, including a household nimblewright with combat programming.
- **Pry the Lords loose:** The house bribes and promises, and it uses the Zhentarim's intelligence to learn the Lords' identities and weaknesses so that Yalah can blackmail or extort her way to a seat.
- **Keep the villa shut:** A visitor without an appointment is refused at the gate. Yalah does not receive unannounced callers, Orond receives some, and Aldric Cray screens everyone. The Alarm network and the arcane lock cover the rest.
- **Hide the private papers:** Orond keeps a private journal in a false-book compartment in his study, and Yalah wears the key to the chronicle's lock on a chain at her throat.
- **Handle a threat by talking first:** Yalah feigns innocence, redirects blame and bargains. Hrabbaz stands beside her with breastplate and rapier drawn, and he does not retreat while she is present. The nimblewright stands dormant in the study and answers to Yalah's command word or to the Alarm network.

## Relationships

- **Manshoon's Zhentarim** (ally of convenience): The Zhentarim use the villa as a shield and refuge, and the house uses them to learn the Lords' identities. Yalah considers Urstul Floxin an underling and hides that from Manshoon's side.
- **The Doom Raiders** (neutral, with a paid source): Skeemo Weirdbottle has fed intelligence to the Gralhunds and Manshoon's cell since well before Ches 1. The cell as a whole has no dealings with the house.
- **Xanathar's Guild** (neutral, watched): The house traced Dalakhar into the Guild and did not know the Guild held the Stone.
- **Bregan D'aerthe** (customer): Jarlaxle's company sold the house nimblewright NW-02. Each nimblewright feeds a crystal ball aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, so every sale doubles as a listening post. [research] No source says whether the house knows this.
- **The Cassalanters** (hidden co-religionists): The Gralhunds and the Cassalanter household belong to the same Asmodean cult, and a Cassalanter journal lists "Inform Y.G." beside the Cassalanter mausoleum. Nobody outside the household and the cult knows this.
- **The Harpers** (neutral): [research] No source gives a day-one dealing between the Harpers and the house.
- **The Lords' Alliance** (neutral): [research] No source gives a day-one dealing.
- **The Emerald Enclave** (neutral): The Enclave monitors a minor ley line under the villa and has no known dealings with the family.
- **The Order of the Gauntlet** (neutral): [research] No source gives a day-one dealing.
- **Force Grey** (neutral): [research] No source gives a day-one dealing.

## Key Members

- **Yalah Gralhund** — Tethyrian human noble, neutral evil, she/her. Lady of House Gralhund and the true power in the house, with a keen mind and a plan to win a seat among the Masked Lords. See the **Yalah Gralhund** page in Notable Figures.
- **Orond Gralhund** — Tethyrian human noble, neutral evil, he/him. Patriarch of the house, a short, stocky man who dresses well and shows a boorish side whenever he believes himself unobserved. See the **Orond Gralhund** page in Notable Figures.
- **Hrabbaz** — Half-orc bodyguard, neutral evil, he/him. A muscle-bound bodyguard with a cleft palate, impeccable dress and surprising manners, more loyal to Yalah than to Orond. See the **Hrabbaz** page in Notable Figures.
- **Hurv Taldred** — Illuskan human cult fanatic, neutral evil, he/him. The villa's groundskeeper, who patrols the yard with two mastiffs and whose page gives his affiliation as House Gralhund (Cassalanter cult). See the **Hurv Taldred** page in Notable Figures.
- **Chirada** — Mulan human operative, neutral, she/her. A Gralhund intelligence operative who keeps out of notice on the city's streets. See the **Chirada** page in Notable Figures.

## Grand Game Agenda

House Gralhund wants the vault's gold for itself. The gold would raise the house in Waterdeep's high society and pay for Yalah's bid to become a Masked Lord, which she regards as her last best chance. The house is struggling to compete with organizations that are far larger and have far more resources.

## Overview

House Gralhund is a minor noble family whose walled villa fills a block of Saerdoun Street in the North Ward. Lord Orond is the patriarch, and Lady Yalah is known for her keen mind and her gracious, politically astute manner. The family trades in arms and mercenaries, and its motto is "We see both sides." Callers without an appointment are turned away at the gate.

> [!gamemaster]**GM Notes**
>
> - **Which Zhentarim:** The Zhentarim at the villa belong to **Manshoon's Zhentarim**, the Black Network cell that serves Manshoon. They are not the Doom Raiders. Skeemo Weirdbottle is a Doom Raider who sells to both sides.
> - **What the house does not know:** Neither Yalah nor Orond knows that Manshoon is the Zhentarim's true master, and Orond does not fully grasp how entangled his family has become with the Black Network.
> - **Public knowledge:** The published adventure says it is no secret in Waterdeep that the Black Network has rooted itself in the house, but the campaign pages describe Yalah's command of the Zhentarim as something she shows only to those she trusts. The Overview follows the campaign and leaves the Zhentarim out.
> - **Orond's weakness:** Orond is a coward in his wife's absence and a bully when he believes himself unobserved. He barricades himself behind furniture in a confrontation.
> - **Hrabbaz and Yalah:** Hrabbaz is the bodyguard of both Gralhunds and respects Yalah while barely tolerating Orond.
> - **Wiki detail:** The wiki gives Yalah the languages Common and Infernal (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Yalah Gralhund*, 5e).

> [!gamemaster]**Secret**
>
> - **The devil-blood:** The family chronicle records an ancestor's pact with a devil and the Infernal bloodline behind Orond's tail. Orond is human, born with a tiefling's tail that was amputated when he was a boy, and the scar remains. Opening the chronicle's lock without Yalah's brass key releases three specters.
> - **The cult link:** Yalah keeps two silver holy symbols of Asmodeus and two red-and-gold liturgical robes with Cassalanter house embroidery under a false bottom in a trunk at the foot of her bed. Hurv Taldred wears a Cassalanter devotional mark under his shirt and serves the cult as devotedly as he serves the house. The Gralhunds therefore belong to the same Asmodean cult as the Cassalanter household. Orond knows that the trunk holds symbols and has never opened it, and he does not know the cult's name or its leader.

**Tone:** Dry and even, like a briefing on a house that is cleverer than it looks and weaker than it wants to be. The page plays Yalah's ambition and Orond's cowardice straight and never mocks the family.
