# Castle Ward

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - The Castle Ward is the government, courts, military and Market ward at the center of Waterdeep. It also holds the Yawning Portal, the well into Undermountain.
> - **Finding Floon** opens in the Yawning Portal. **Trollskull Alley** uses the Portal for faction meetings and sends a festival procession out of the ward toward the Market.
> - **Fireball!** reads Jeryth's report of a disturbance in the ward and puts two nimblewright suspects here (a Watchful Order household and the City Armory). Barnibus Blastwind and Saeth Cromley work out of the ward.
> - **Gralhund Villa** carries a Missive from Castle Ward in the family library and leaves Davil Starsong, and possibly Yalah, in the custody wing at Castle Waterdeep.
> - **Faction Outposts** sends the party to Terasse Estate and Yellowspire. **Xanathar's Lair** can be entered by a secret stair in the Castle Ward sewers, and **Kolat Towers** can be entered through the Yellowspire teleportation circle.
> - Faction events set here: Doom Raiders (the Portal, the God Catcher, Yellowspire, Castle Waterdeep), Bregan D'aerthe (a Castle Ward clerk, the sewer stair), Lords' Alliance (the Palace, a glover's shop on Rannick Lane), Order of the Gauntlet (the Halls of Justice), Force Grey (Blackstaff Tower), Emerald Enclave (the water table and cisterns), Harpers (Lightsinger Theater). Almost every faction First Meeting runs through the Yawning Portal.
> - Location journals: **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** is the only one that applies. The stair from the Castle Ward sewers leads to it. No other mapped site sits in this ward.

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the ward as a place to run: who keeps the peace, where the party meets its contacts, and which doors in the ward open onto the campaign's secrets.

The Castle Ward is the part of Waterdeep that runs the rest of it, a ward of courts, barracks, offices and the city's great Market where the government sits and few people live.

## At a Glance

- **District Type:** Government, military, courts and market.
- **Population:** The smallest resident population of any ward, because most buildings are offices and meeting halls. No figure is given in any source. [research]
- **Lifestyle:** [research]
- **Services:** Magister courts and courthouse cells. City Guard barracks. Palace exchequers who exchange coin without a fee. The Code Legal, kept at the Palace in several languages. The Font of Knowledge, the city's largest public library, with free reading lessons. Public privies around the Market. The Map House, guildhall of the Surveyors', Map- and Chart-makers' Guild.
- **Influences:** The city flag's blue and purple, a gold griffon mascot for the Griffon Cavalry, and champions drawn from the Guard, the Navy and the Cavalry.
- **Primary Peoples:** [research]
- **Languages:** [research]
- **Religion:** Temple of the Seldarine (all elven gods), Spires of the Morning (Lathander), Font of Knowledge (Oghma), Halls of Justice (Tyr). Tymora's Tower of Luck is listed here by one Waterdeep Dragon Heist table and placed in the Sea Ward elsewhere. See the temples bullets under Culture in Life in the Ward. No dominant faith is sourced. [research]
- **Governance:** The Open Lord rules from the Palace, and the Masked Lords hear petitioners in its audience chamber. Magisters sit in the ward's courts. The City Guard garrisons the barracks, and a pair of Watch operatives patrols.
- **Architecture:** Castle Waterdeep on its bluff, with towers hundreds of feet high. A Palace smaller than the castle and far more comfortable. Blackstaff Tower, a squat black blot. Stone and slate in the Yawning Portal, pale yellow brick in Yellowspire, and a marble-pillared hall (the Cynosure) at the edge of the Market.
- **Economy:** The Market, solicitors, publishers and business offices, courts, and the Palace coin exchange. Guild law applies as everywhere in the city.
- **Organizations:** Open Lord's government, Masked Lords, City Guard, City Navy, Griffon Cavalry, City Watch, magisters, the Watchful Order, Force Grey (Blackstaff Tower), Order of the Gauntlet (Halls of Justice), Lords' Alliance (the Palace).

> - A secret spiral stair in the Castle Ward sewers leads down to Xanathar's lair (area X1 in Dragon Heist). Mirt, Jalester Silvermane, Yagra Stonefist and Jeryth's awakened rats each lead a party to it, and four Bregan D'aerthe drow wait at its head in the Bregan D'aerthe faction event that uses it. It stirs the ward's water table and the cisterns. Enclave speakers say only that something dreams below, and no one in the ward knows its name.

## Landmarks

**The Yawning Portal** — Tavern and inn
- A stone building with a slate roof and several chimneys. A 40-foot well in the common room is the outer shell of a sunken tower and drops 140 feet to Undermountain Level 1. A rope and pulley lowers adventurers and hauls them out.
- Durnan owns it and charges standard prices. Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg plays here, Bonnie pours, Davil Starsong keeps a room, and a Bregan D'aerthe observer, Ryvarra, watches the well from the balcony.
- Hook: the campaign opens here with a brawl, a troll out of the well and Volo's job offer. Patrons sing a bawdy song when someone goes down, and a faction contact is usually at a table.

**Palace of Waterdeep** — Seat of government, with Ahghairon's Tower
- Also called Piergeiron's Palace, and still called that by elderly and long-lived citizens. The Open Lord has ruled from here for well over a century.
- Its halls host officials, guildmasters and nobles. The audience chamber holds Ahghairon's ancient and humble throne. Exchequers here exchange coin free, and the Code Legal is kept here.
- Ahghairon's Tower, the tomb of the first Open Lord, stands in the Palace courtyard behind an invisible force cage. The floating bones of a would-be thief hang outside it, and on Ahghairon's Day (Eleasis 1) commoners leave violets around it.
- Hook: Lords' Alliance audiences and the Open Lord's summons take place here, and the dragonward originates beneath Ahghairon's Tower.

**Castle Waterdeep** — Fortress
- A redoubt of last defense on a bluff above the city. It is not the seat of government. Scheduled executions happen behind its walls.
- On Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) mages veil it in an illusion of Nimoar's log fortress from midday to sunset.
- Hook: a custody wing built for thirty prisoners holds sixty-one, and Davil Starsong is held there after **Gralhund Villa**.

**Blackstaff Tower** — Wizard academy and fortress
- Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff, trains wizards here and heads the Watchful Order. Staring at the tower for long gives a queasy sense of being watched.
- Hook: Force Grey recruits by Sending from here, and a Zhentarim agent, Vira Solkan, has been planted inside.

**Halls of Justice** — Temple of Tyr and courts
- Stone and high-ceilinged, smelling of incense and old wood. Order of the Gauntlet recruits swear their oath here on a cold silver gauntlet.
- The Order keeps a clerk's office in the east annex, and Brother Haldric Wence there arranges *Speak with Dead* on bodies the Watch holds. Saeth Cromley drinks in the sergeants' room most afternoons.
- Hook: Savra Belabranta meets Order members in the east annex.

**The Market** — Public square, with the God Catcher
- The largest market square of any city in the North, with public privies around it. The Cynosure, a marble-pillared hall built as a temple to Ao and now rented for events, stands at its edge.
- Two walking statues stand near it: the God Catcher, and the Great Drunkard, which stopped on its approach. The campaign puts the Great Drunkard on Bazaar Street and does not name its ward. [research]
- The God Catcher is a sunken statue of a muscular man with a floating stone sphere above its raised palm. A tenement of the same name climbs its chest and shoulders, and the landlord, Aundra Blackcloak, lives in the sphere. She is rarely seen and pays fair coin.
- Hook: festival processions converge on the Market, and Ammalia Cassalanter runs a charity booth near the Castle Ward entrance to it. Skeemo's potion drop to the lady in the purple cloak happens beside the God Catcher.

**Yellowspire** — Banite tower
- A tower of pale yellow brick, locally called the House of Tyranny by those inside. Black handprints cover every interior surface.
- Amath Seccent (Lawful Evil, Human, she/her), a well-mannered woman in common clothes, keeps the tower with four followers, and visitors find them at ease with the handprints.
- Hook: a teleportation circle in the basement links to Kolat Towers. **Faction Outposts** and the Doom Raiders heist both target it.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Amath is a priest of Bane allied with Manshoon's Zhentarim, and she keeps the teleportation circle to Kolat Towers. She is the partner of Agorn Fuoco and holds the Stone of Golorr.

**Terasse Estate** — Rowhouse and healing clinic
- A rowhouse clinic on Elsambul's Lane that asks no questions about injuries. Keln Trigos (Neutral Evil, Tiefling, he/him) runs it and asks no questions about how his patients were hurt.
- Hook: a trapdoor under the kitchen rug leads to an underground dock and on to Skullport. Gladiators pass through on their way to Xanathar's lair.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Keln keeps the Terasse outpost for Xanathar's Guild. He holds two passphrases: one opens the clinic's gladiator transport route, and the other opens the route to the Lair.

**Lightsinger Theater** — Opera house
- A high-end opera house. Mirt receives Harper recruits in Private Box C at intermission.
- Hook: a paper bird delivers the ticket, and a Delzorin Street tailor has been told to expect the party.

## Characters of Interest

**Durnan (Neutral, Human, he/him)**
- Retired adventurer who owns and tends the Yawning Portal, and the one who deals with trouble in the taproom. He ejects troublemakers with a single word, "Out."
- He went down the Well with Mirt in the old days and built the tavern over it. He will not discuss what he saw there.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Durnan is a secret Masked Lord of Waterdeep and the secret founder of the Red Sashes. Its fighters pose as regulars at the Portal and are magically sworn to his service.
> - Neither fact can be found in normal play, and Durnan confirms neither under pressure. The party only notices that he often knows of a threat to the city before the Watch does.

**Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him)**
- A moneylender of great girth known as "the Old Wolf", who spends days on politics and nights on drink. He knows architecture well and will talk about it.
- Laeral's closest advisor. His personal loans to Harper members leave them owing him a favor.
- His mansion was once a fortress-like, glowering tower near the Naval Harbor, softened by new architecture since he returned. Its ward is contested: the Dragon Heist ward chapter lists it in the Castle Ward, and the campaign's Harper events put Mirt's dinner house in the Sea Ward behind the Naval Harbor. [research]
- Hook: Mirt and Durnan went down the Well together in the old days, and Mirt names Harper ranks over dinner at his house.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Mirt is a Masked Lord and a senior Harper. He is the least careful of the Masked Lords about hiding it.

**Laeral Silverhand (Chaotic Good, Moon Elf, she/her)**
- The Open Lord of Waterdeep, fifth of the Seven Sisters and widow of Khelben Arunsun. She took the post when the Masked Lords asked, after Dagult Neverember was ousted.
- She slips out of the Palace in disguise to clear her head and visit old friends.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Her power has declined since the Spellplague, and only Elminster knows how far. She considers Vajra an insecure child holding her husband's soul in the Blackstaff.

**Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her)**
- The Blackstaff of Waterdeep, head of the Watchful Order, and the youngest Blackstaff ever, three years in the post. She sends orders by Sending, often in exactly 25 words.
- She does not perform warmth she does not feel.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Vajra commands Force Grey. Her staff holds Khelben Arunsun's soul, and she says "the Splinter" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the party member she is speaking to.

**Saeth Cromley (Lawful Good, Illuskan Human, he/him)**
- Retired City Watch sergeant. He sits in the Halls of Justice sergeants' room in the afternoons, and a visitor who buys him a drink finds him easy to talk to.
- He helps Barnibus Blastwind with unusual crimes by translating between the magistrate and the common folk.

**Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her)**
- Head of the Order of the Gauntlet's Waterdeep chapter, from a clerk's office in the Halls of Justice, with about a dozen Knights and Priests under her. She comes from the noble Belabranta family, who breed griffons for the Cavalry.
- She is blunt, has a hunter's eye for infernal work, and holds no patience for people who manage her.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - She spent three years in the Howling Hatred cult and left about three years ago. She tells this to anyone who asks her sincerely, and what she did there only to a member she trusts.

## Life in the Ward

**Governing metaphor:** The Castle Ward is the city's front desk, where Waterdeep's business is received, stamped and passed along, and where the real offices sit out of sight behind it.

### Culture
- Few people sleep here. The ward fills with clerks, soldiers and petitioners by day and empties after dark.
- Ward pride is quiet. Its colors are blue and purple, its mascot is a gold griffon, and its race champions come from the Guard, the Navy and the Cavalry. Its cheering crowds are far smaller than those of other wards.
- Asmagh's Alley is the ward's folk tradition, and children hop across it on one foot to a rhyme. Asmagh, an apothecary, poisoned patients and buried up to eighty of them upright beneath its wide flagstones, over a century ago. The rhyme is about hopping on their heads. Dragon Heist points readers to Warrior's Way and the Street of Silver for the same sound, but no source places them in a ward. [research]
- Oghma's priests at the Font of Knowledge teach reading free. The Dungsweepers' Guild keeps the streets clean at every hour, and the Market's public privies serve the crowds.
- The Yawning Portal is the ward's social center for adventurers. Almost every faction First Meeting runs through its taproom.
- Temples of the ward: the Temple of the Seldarine for all elven gods, the Spires of the Morning for Lathander, one of the city's most beautiful temples, the Font of Knowledge for Oghma, with the library, and the Halls of Justice for Tyr (see Landmarks).
- Tymora's Tower of Luck is placed in the Castle Ward by the Dragon Heist temple table and in the Sea Ward by its Sea Ward text and by the campaign's own Waterdeep outline, so it is not a Castle Ward temple here. Selûne's Tower of the Moon is Sea Ward in both. The Temple of Helm is placed in the Castle Ward by one campaign guide and in the Southern Ward by the Dragon Heist table. [research]
- The Temple of the Seldarine and the Spires carry no campaign hook yet. [research]

### Defense
- The City Guard garrisons the barracks and guards the walls and civic buildings. The Watch patrols the streets, and only a pair of Watch operatives walks the Castle Ward at a time, against eight to twelve in the Dock Ward.
- A watch post's green and gold lantern burns by continual flame even in daylight. Whistles summon more constables.
- The Griffon Cavalry flies from Peaktop Aerie on Mount Waterdeep, and its riders drop from their mounts under rings of feather falling.
- The City Navy docks in the Great Harbor. Castle Waterdeep is the redoubt of last defense and the site of the custody wing.
- The campaign outline names the Castle Ward as the City Watch's headquarters. The Dragon Heist text names no headquarters. The site is [research], and so is the garrison's size.
- Arcane casters register with the city and are urged to join the Watchful Order, headed by the Blackstaff.
- Barnibus Blastwind (Lawful Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) is a prickly, secretive magistrate of the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors who investigates crimes that involve magic. He confides in Saeth Cromley and almost no one else. A lifelong bachelor, he spends his free days in his grandmother's library on his estate in the Sea Ward, and **Fireball!** puts him in charge of the fireball scene.

### Politics
- The Open Lord sits on the throne that Ahghairon first used, in the Palace audience chamber. Masked Lords can overturn any magister's ruling, but one is rarely present when a petitioner needs it.
- Laeral Silverhand governs from the Palace and uses her magic sparingly. In great need she can order Vajra Safahr to unleash Force Grey. Vajra has sent its members on unauthorized missions, and Laeral has not confronted her.
- Magisters wear black robes and move with a City Guard escort. They may pass sentence without a trial.
- Which Masked Lords keep homes in the ward, and who runs the Palace day to day, are [research].

### Economy
- The Market, solicitors, publishers, business offices and courts make up the ward's trade.
- The Palace exchequers change foreign coin without a fee, but the queue can run a day or more, and appointments are required.
- The City Guard collects a monthly tax door to door on the last day of the month. Rents and prices in the ward are [research].
- Yawning Portal prices follow the standard schedule for food, drink and lodging.

### Lore
- Ahghairon seated himself on the flaming warlord's throne and set up the Open Lord and the hidden Masked Lords. The throne is still in the Palace.
- The dragonward begins beneath Ahghairon's Tower. The campaign outline says it bars dragons without the Open Lord's permission. The Dragon Heist text says dragons are physically unable to enter at all unless touched by the dragonstaff of Ahghairon. The campaign wording stands, and the conflict is on record.
- The Dragon Heist text says six walking statues stand in the ward. Only the Honorable Knight, the God Catcher and the Griffon are tied to it by the sources, and the others are [research]. The campaign outline calls all of them 90 feet tall and dormant. The Dragon Heist text gives 90 feet only for the original statue and nine stories for the Honorable Knight. The statues' sizes are unresolved.
- Halaster Blackcloak built his tower at the foot of Mount Waterdeep before the city existed. The Well into Undermountain was once where Waterdeep threw its criminals, and Durnan built the Yawning Portal over it.
- Gods' Day (Marpenoth 15) ends with a Griffon Cavalry bonfire on Mount Waterdeep, in memory of Myrkul's fires that burned through the Southern, Dock and Castle Wards.
- Plowing and Running (Mirtul 6 to 9) sends ward races through the Castle Ward streets before the finals at the Field of Triumph.
- Illuun's pressure on the water table is in the Secret above. It reaches the ward only through Jeryth's reports and the cisterns.

## Overview

The Castle Ward is the seat of Waterdeep's government, courts and armed forces, and it holds the Market, the largest market square of any city in the North. Most of its buildings are offices, courthouses and barracks, so it has the smallest resident population of any ward, and its streets stay clean and lightly patrolled. Castle Waterdeep rises from its bluff above the ward as a redoubt of last defense, but the Open Lord has ruled from the Palace for well over a century, and petitioners to the Open Lord or the Masked Lords are received in its audience chamber. Adventurers and the curious fill the Yawning Portal, a tavern built over the open well into Undermountain, and the ward's children hop across Asmagh's Alley in memory of an apothecary who buried his patients beneath its flagstones.

## Read Aloud

Sensory anchors for the GM to build the passage from (not written out):
- The Yawning Portal: a candlelit taproom, gamblers shouting, drunken bawdy singing and an off-key young bard. Shadowdark ale and quipper and chips. The open well in the middle of the room. A stone building, a slate roof and chimneys.
- Castle Waterdeep's towers on the bluff, and Blackstaff Tower, a squat black blot with a sense of being watched.
- Watch lanterns lit in daylight, and Dungsweepers working with brooms and carts at every hour.
- Children hopping across Asmagh's Alley and chanting the rhyme.
- The Gods' Day bonfire on the peak of Mount Waterdeep, and the Founders' Day illusion of Nimoar's log fortress over the castle.
- Yellowspire: black handprints on every interior surface, fur-lined robes, an ice-covered scaffold that rattles and creaks.
- The courthouse: continual flame sconces, iron-barred windows and rooftop cisterns.
- The Halls of Justice: incense, old wood and the cold weight of a silver gauntlet.
- The ward's overall look: stately, clean and well defended.

**Tone:** A busy, tidy front desk with a back office, played straight and a little dry. Avoid making the ward grim or sinister, and keep the secrets in the Secret and GM Notes callouts.
