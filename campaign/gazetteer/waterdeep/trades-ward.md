# Trades Ward

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - The Trades Ward is Waterdeep's main shopping district and the home of many guildhalls. No location journal maps any part of it, so the campaign's business here is small and set in shops, houses, inns and plazas.
> - **Trollskull Alley** offers the Lords' Alliance mission **The Long Watch**, a tenday guarding a Dungsweepers' Guild sweeping crew on its Trades Ward circuit, and has Floon lose money at a dice game in the ward.
> - **Fireball!** tracks Dalakhar to the Inn of the Dripping Dagger on Snail Street in **The Death Mark**, and lists Mother Tamra's, a Trades Ward business with a nimblewright porter and night watchman, among the nine nimblewright owners in **The Nimblewright Hunt**.
> - **Gralhund Villa** sends a Bregan D'aerthe team across the rooftops to Fenerus Stormcastle's house if the Stone leaves the villa in **The Rooftop Chase**. **Faction Outposts** treats the same house as a Bregan D'aerthe outpost with a Zhentarim kidnap clock running.
> - Faction events set here: Doom Raiders (**The Poisoned Delivery** and **Silencing Skeemo** at Weirdbottle's Concoctions, plus a scrivener, a counting-house clerk, a cooper's yard and an advocate in the rank-up and standalone events), Harpers (**The Talking Mare**, **The Dead Drop**, **The Doppelganger Auditions**, **The Sleeping Asset**), Force Grey (**Destroy the Intellect Factory** and **The Legate's Eyes**), Emerald Enclave (**The Fouled Channel** and **The Water Table Stirs**), Bregan D'aerthe (**Coin Pouches** and the rank-up event **Officer**), Lords' Alliance (**The Long Watch** and the Redknife safe house).
> - Location journals: none applies. Kolat Towers is not a landmark of this ward. The campaign treats it as the Southern Ward's tower (see Draft Notes for the Trades Ward reading).

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the ward as a place to run: where the party shops, hires and gathers rumors, and which quiet doors in the ward open onto the campaign's secrets.

The Trades Ward is the city's shop floor, a ward of guildhalls, stalls and balcony walkways where nearly anything can be bought and the Watch stands close by.

## At a Glance

- **District Type:** Shopping, guildhalls, inns and taverns. The Dragon Heist text calls it a market town in itself and easily three times the size of the Market in the Castle Ward.
- **Boundaries:** South, the Southern Ward. West, the Castle Ward. North, the North Ward and the City of the Dead. East, the Trollwall (wiki, 2e). The High Road and the Way of the Dragon converge in the heart of the ward, where the city is narrowest between Castle Waterdeep on its spur of the mountain and the walls of the City of the Dead (Dragon Heist). Caravan Court sits on the southern border and technically belongs to the Southern Ward (wiki, 2e).
- **Founded:** 1035 DR by Ahghairon, as one of the four original wards with the Castle, Southern (Temple) and Dock Wards (wiki, Castle Ward page, 2e). The wiki's own Trades Ward page gives no founding date.
- **Population:** The wiki leaves this blank, and no source gives a ward figure. For scale, the city figures on the **Castle Ward** page are 1,347,680 across Waterdeep and the farmland it commands (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* 3e, 1372 DR), 132,661 counted as the metropolis, and about 200,000 in the city proper by the wiki's own estimate.
- **Lifestyle:** No source gives one. The Dragon Heist text lists every kind of shop and lodging, from jewelers and perfumeries to boarding houses. [research]
- **Services:** Guildhalls. Shops of nearly every legal kind, with taverns, cafés, tea shops, inns, boarding schools, offices and dance academies. Livestock trading at the Court of the White Bull. Mercenary hiring at Virgin's Square. Moneychangers and drapers who read worn coin and weave. Public wells.
- **Influences:** Green and purple colors and a mascot called the mimic, which is a different object every four years. The current object is a tankard (Dragon Heist).
- **Primary Peoples:** No ward figure exists. The city figures are humans 64%, dwarves 10%, elves 10% (mostly moon elves), halflings 5%, half-elves 5%, gnomes 3%, half-orcs 2% and others 1% (as on the **Castle Ward** page; wiki, *City of Splendors* 2e).
- **Languages:** The wiki leaves this blank. [research]
- **Religion:** No row of the Dragon Heist Major Temples table names the Trades Ward. The wiki lists three sites: the Plinth, an all-faiths temple destroyed by the Spellplague, the Temple of Finder Wyvernspur, and a chapel of Deneir inside the Zoarstar guildhall. No dominant faith is sourced for the ward. [research]
- **Governance:** The City Watch has a heavy presence, in open patrols and in plain clothes. A district magistracy with an appeals office sits in the ward, and the guilds govern their own trades from their halls.
- **Architecture:** Balcony walkways run the length of blocks and sometimes stack five stories high. Shop signs jut from the walls, and the sides of buildings are plastered with advertisements. Guildhalls are often tall row houses, such as the three-story House of Light.
- **Economy:** Retail, the guild trades, livestock at the Court of the White Bull, hired swords at Virgin's Square, moneychanging, and a black market that the Dragon Heist text says is easy to find if the buyer keeps quiet. Guild law applies as everywhere in the city.
- **Organizations:** Guilds with halls here (see **The Guilds**), the City Watch, the district magistracy, the Dungsweepers' Guild (street crews), Doom Raiders (Weirdbottle's Concoctions), Bregan D'aerthe (Fenerus Stormcastle's house and a calligraphy shop), Harpers (a bookshop drop and a bench), Lords' Alliance (a safe house), and Manshoon's Zhentarim (safe houses).

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Secret
> - Fenerus Stormcastle's house on Quill Alley is a Bregan D'aerthe message drop. Fenerus gathers messages from dead drops across the ward and stores them at the house, and Krebbyg Masq'il'yr collects them every two days. The messages are coded in Drow, and they point the party to the **Sea Maidens Faire**.
> - Fenerus believes his contacts are Luskan agents. He followed Krebbyg once and knows where the messages go, but he has not worked out that the cloaked drow belong to Bregan D'aerthe.
> - Manshoon has marked Fenerus as a soft target. A directive in Zhentarim hands orders his kidnapping, and the **Faction Outposts** quest holds the timing and the three states the house can be in when the party arrives.

## Landmarks

**House of Light** — Guildhall
- The hall of the Guild of Chandlers and Lamplighters, a three-story row house on Scroll Street (wiki, 2e). A wagon-sized mound of wax with hundreds of wicks burns outside day and night, and the guild keeps building it up by sticking new candles to it.
- Inside, the guild displays and sells its best work. That includes candles of every color, lamps, chandeliers and waxwork constructions of notable people, dragons and abstract lattices, all made as fantastical candles.
- The hall has no campaign hook yet. [research]

**Court of the White Bull** — Plaza
- Long ago this plaza was grazing land, and an albino calf born here gave the White Bull Tavern its name. The tavern was destroyed in a spell battle between the archmage Thongalar the Mighty and the evil mage Shile Rauretilar, whose apprentices died with him. Azuth appeared to stitch the Weave back together, and a wrinkle remains.
- Magic brought to bear here sometimes goes awry, and the use of magic items and spells is forbidden in the area. The wiki describes the plaza as a livestock market (2e).
- Hook: a Waterdhavian who knows the ward will tell the story to a visiting wizard (see **Azuth**). No source gives a rule for how the magic goes wrong. [research]

**Weirdbottle's Concoctions** — Potion and elixir shop
- A cramped little shop on a side street, run by Skeemo Weirdbottle. Most of its potions and elixirs are nonmagical, and Dragon Heist names it as a source of discounted potions and poisons for Zhentarim characters.
- Hook: **The Poisoned Delivery** sends Doom Raiders members here to collect a coffer, and Skeemo knows each of them by name. In **Silencing Skeemo** he bolts from the shop with a satchel and a hired dray waiting.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Skeemo has sold the Doom Raiders to Manshoon's Zhentarim since before the party arrived, for rare components and protection from Watch inspections. The coffer in **The Poisoned Delivery** holds poison under a layer of mint.

**Fenerus Stormcastle's House** — Small house and message drop, Quill Alley
- A small house that the lamplighter Fenerus rents in an alley. Residents of the ward know him, and a map marks the alley with his name.
- Inside are the coded messages described in the Secret. A character who reads Drow decodes them with advantage on the DC 16 Intelligence check.
- Hook: **Gralhund Villa** sends a Bregan D'aerthe team here across the rooftops, and **Faction Outposts** sends the party here from three directions. A stakeout of the house leads to Krebbyg and then to the **Sea Maidens Faire**.

**Inn of the Dripping Dagger** — Inn
- A modest travelers' inn on Snail Street. The sign shows an icicle, which is what the name refers to. The page uses the campaign's street.
- Myl Dunpier runs it, keeps her ledgers in order and remembers Dalakhar clearly.
- Hook: in **The Death Mark** Myl lets the party into Dalakhar's room for an honest request, a Watch badge or 5 gp. The room was scrubbed before he left.

**Uza Solizeph's Bookshop** — Bookshop and Harper drop, Sorn Street
- A narrow three-story shop on Sorn Street between a glassblower and a cartographer. Uza, an old bookseller, waits at Felzoun's Folly, the tavern on the corner of Sorn Street and Salabar Street, and gives the party her keys.
- Hook: **The Dead Drop** sends Harpers in to rescue her cat from a gazer in the attic. Behind shelf seven the party finds a hand-copied cipher.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - The shop has hidden a Harper dead drop behind shelf seven for ten years, and Uza does not know it. A clerk named Tessalar Maeridge loads the drop, and he sold a copy of one packet to the Splinter. Skeemo Weirdbottle bought three volumes on noble scandals from Uza last month.

**The Trades Ward Plaza** — Public square with a well
- A plaza with a public well and benches, where the ward's water-carriers fill their barrels. A halfling woman called Halla sits on the west bench by the well every day from 16:00 to 16:30.
- Hook: three leads in **The Sleeping Asset** end at that bench. In **The Fouled Channel** a member who asks the Summerstrider network hears that a film lies on the water in the plaza well each morning and that horses refuse it.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Halla is Corene Wyldath, a Harper Spy carrying one of Nihiloor's devourers. The film comes from the Splinter's thrall-conditioning waste, which cultists pour into a Castle Ward cistern, and it has crept along a buried watercourse into the ward's wells.

**Virgin's Square** — Mercenary hiring square
- A round courtyard in the middle of a block bounded by the High Road, Scroll Street, Simple's Street, Snail Street and the Way of the Dragon. Most of it opens south onto Simple's Street, and Atkiss Alley enters it from the north (wiki, 2e).
- Bodyguards and hired swords gather here to find work, and the Bowels of the Earth, a tavern popular with adventurers and mercenaries, stands nearby.
- Hook: old Waterdhavians tell of a mimic that posed as a statue here for two winters and ate drunkards at night until two Watchmen prodded it with spears (wiki, 2e). The square has no campaign hook yet. [research]

**Trades Ward Magistracy Appeals Office** — District court office
- The appeals office of the ward's district magistracy employs four clerks. One of them, Orvyn Dall, a Waterdhavian human, was placed there seven tendays ago.
- Orvyn lives in a second-floor flat on Tallow Court. He leaves at 07:30 and comes home at 18:00, and a widow named Brenna Tull lives in the flat below.
- Hook: in **The Legate's Eyes** Vajra sends Force Grey here after three reversed rulings. She cannot act on the magistracy herself, because any move by the Blackstaff against the Watch opens a formal review.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - A devourer from Nihiloor's brood has ridden Orvyn for seven tendays. It steers which appeals reach which magistrate, and Orvyn is aware and cannot act or speak. If he dies, if Brenna calls the Watch or if a patrol sees the extraction, the Watch reviews the magistracy for 14 days.

**The Splinter's Safe Houses** — Houses, Shesstra Street and Nethpranter Street
- The Shesstra Street house stands three floors high between a chandler and a locksmith. It has no sign, a closed but unlocked door and a red lantern in the upper window, and no business is licensed there.
- The Nethpranter Street house, also three floors, stands across from a cargo yard. It has a guardroom on the ground floor, bunks above and a records room on top.
- Hook: **The Talking Mare** sends Harpers to watch the Shesstra Street house, and **The Doppelganger Auditions** leads them to the Nethpranter Street house.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Both are houses of the Splinter, Manshoon's cell. The Nethpranter Street house holds two resident Spies and the papers that name the cell's handler, Beldan Rusk. No file says the two houses are one house, so the page keeps them as two addresses.

## Characters of Interest

**Skeemo Weirdbottle (Lawful Evil, Rock Gnome, he/him)**
- The Doom Raiders' Master of Magic, a tidy alchemist who runs Weirdbottle's Concoctions. His politeness is exact and has no warmth in it.
- He keeps an index of the city's criminal and semi-criminal business and files every sale in it under the buyer's name. A character whose Debt of the City names him as Holder has already been entered in it.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Skeemo is a traitor who feeds intelligence to Manshoon's cell and House Gralhund and messages both sides by paper bird. If cornered he trades information for his life.

**Fenerus Stormcastle (Neutral Evil, Human, he/him)**
- A big, scar-faced lamplighter with gray hair, who lights lamps in the ward and minds his own business. He is a retired brigand and has spent considerable time in a Watch cell.
- He wants immunity and a quiet life, and he survives by saying little.

**Myl Dunpier (Neutral, Human, she/her)**
- The practical, middle-aged proprietor of the Inn of the Dripping Dagger, with ink-stained fingers. She takes no interest in her guests' business and a great deal in being paid on time.
- She keeps careful records because the Watch has asked about her guests twice this year, and she finds it easier to have the answers ready.

**Corene Wyldath (Neutral Good, Halfling, she/her)**
- A Harper Spy who has worked inside Xanathar's Guild's Dock Ward operation for four months under the name Halla Ironstave. She is composed, precise with her attention and sparing with words.
- Her reports are still accurate, but she has started asking questions about the Harpers.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - One of Nihiloor's custom Occupying Devourers has ridden Corene for three weeks. It keeps her mind alive, so she is aware and cannot act, and she can be saved if the devourer is forced out. She counts as one of three Harpers Nihiloor has taken.

**Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him)**
- A young drow gunslinger who runs the bookings of the Seven Masks Theater in the Dock Ward as a human called Kreb Sorrush. He is rash and cheerful, and he leaves the thinking to Fel'rekt Lafeen.
- His Trades Ward errand is the message pickup at Fenerus's house, every two days.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Krebbyg works for Bregan D'aerthe, and the messages he collects go to Jarlaxle. A party that follows him from the house reaches the **Sea Maidens Faire**.

## Life in the Ward

**Governing metaphor:** The Trades Ward is the city's shop floor, loud and lit at every hour, where the guilds keep the books from their halls and the Watch keeps the door from the street.

### Culture
- The ward flies green and purple, and its mascot is the mimic. The custom is said to date from the first choosing of mascots, when the ward took a chest of gold and was mocked by the other wards for not picking a creature.
- Every four years the ward unveils a new object and declares it the mimic. The object is the subject of rumor until the unveiling, and the practical jokes last for months afterward. Rock gnomes and wizards make illusory mouths lunge from real copies of it, and artisans make fakes of cake or paper that are crushed when someone takes them for the real thing. The current mimic is a tankard.
- On race and parade days, homes and businesses fly ward colors and bring out their mascots, as in every ward (Dragon Heist). The year of the last unveiling is not sourced. [research]
- The ward runs day and night. Balcony walkways carry foot traffic above the streets, and shop signs and advertisements compete for every eye.
- Temples of the ward: the wiki names the Plinth, an all-faiths temple that the Spellplague destroyed and that was still unrebuilt in 1479 DR, the Temple of Finder Wyvernspur, converted from a shop in 1372 DR, and a chapel of Deneir in the Zoarstar guildhall. The ward's current temples are [research]. See **Deneir** and **The Deities**.
- Tymora's Tower of Luck and Helm's Hall are not Trades Ward temples. The Dragon Heist Major Temples table names neither this ward, and the conflicts over where they stand are in Draft Notes.

### Defense
- The Watch has a heavy presence in the ward, with open patrols and officers out of uniform. The text gives no squad size, and the Castle Ward gets a pair of operatives and the Dock Ward eight to twelve. [research]
- Each ward is patrolled by a division of the Watch under a ward civilar (wiki, City Watch page). The Trades Ward's civilar is [research].
- A constable at the ward's Watch desk takes a death report and asks three questions about it, as in **Silencing Skeemo**.
- A Dungsweepers' Guild crew led by the foreman Berna and coordinated by Mareva Coile walks a circuit from the Market through the cooperage lanes toward Coin Alley (**The Long Watch**).
- The Lords' Alliance keeps a safe house at 14 Ironwick Lane, two rooms above a shut dyer's workshop. A lame former Watch quartermaster named Garrick Tull manages it, and a Redknife member opens the door with the phrase "Alliance weather". The house has a yard door, a loading hatch onto the lane and a roof hatch.

### Politics
- The district magistracy hears appeals, and its four clerks decide which appeals reach which magistrate. See **The Code Legal and the Magisters**.
- Guild membership rules, guild dues and the factions' operatives inside guild leadership are on **The Guilds**. The wiki lists a dozen guildhalls in the ward, among them the Citadel of the Arrow, the House of Song, the House of Textiles, Stationers' Hall and the Zoarstar.
- Which guilds hold the most weight on the ward's streets is [research].

### Economy
- The ward's trade is retail. The Dragon Heist text lists glove shops, shoe shops, jewelers, perfumeries, flower shops, cake shops, grocers, pottery stores and armor vendors, and says anything not illegal can be bought here, and most things that are.
- Older sources name a few shops: Riautar's Weaponry on the High Road, which sells second-hand weapons and fine bows, the Riven Shield Shop, with second-hand arms and armor, Saern's Fine Swords, Belmonder's Meats and Hendever's Coffins and Coffers on Coffinmarch (wiki, 2e). Whether they still stand in 1492 DR is [research].
- Lodging runs from the affordable Dagger's Rest to the very expensive Unicorn's Horn, where the High Road meets Waterdeep Way (wiki, 2e). Prices and rents in the ward are [research].
- A Trades Ward moneychanger can read the mint years of worn coin, and a draper can name the weave of a cloth (**Coin Pouches**).

### Lore
- Ahghairon founded the ward in 1035 DR with the Castle, Southern and Dock Wards. See **Waterdeep** for the four original wards.
- In the late 14th century DR, ghostly sightings appeared in streets across the ward over a tenday and more. Citizens, scribes and Watch members were confused, and the soothsayers Sambral and Sheireera each read them as a message from the dead (wiki). No source says what caused them. [research]
- The Honorable Knight, a walking statue nine stories tall, stands in a block between Snail Street and the Way of the Dragon, near the place where four wards meet. It bowed to the people who opposed it, sheathed its sword and set its shield down, and it faces southwest toward the harbor. A visitor who sees the statue to the south and west is standing in the Trades Ward. See **The Dragonward and Walking Statues**.
- The ward's north edge meets the **City of the Dead**, and the High Road runs through the narrowest part of the city beside it.

## Overview

The Trades Ward is Waterdeep's main shopping district, easily three times the size of the Market in the Castle Ward. Balcony walkways run the length of its blocks, signs jut from every wall, and the streets stay busy day and night. Many of the city's guilds keep their halls here, and the Watch patrols openly and in plain clothes. The ward flies green and purple, and every four years it unveils a new object and calls it the mimic.

## Read Aloud

Sensory anchors for the GM to build the passage from (not written out):
- Balcony walkways stacked five stories high, signs that leap out from the walls, and advertisements plastered over every flat surface.
- Crowds of carters, clerks and stall-keepers, and shopkeepers piling their sweepings by the curb while a Dungsweepers' crew pushes a cart through.
- The House of Light: a wagon-sized mound of wax with hundreds of wicks, lit by day and by night, with new candles stuck to its sides. The wiki adds a lemon scent (2e).
- The Court of the White Bull: an open plaza of livestock pens where a spell fizzles.
- The Honorable Knight, nine stories of stone with its shield point-down, standing over the rooftops between Snail Street and the Way of the Dragon.
- A tankard on a shop counter that snaps at a customer, and a cake tankard that collapses when a child grabs it.
- Tallow and wax smoke from chandlers' shops, and the flower shops and bakeries around them.
- The ward's overall look: bright, crowded and loud, and always for sale.

**Tone:** A loud, busy market town played light and a little greedy, with the Watch on every corner. Keep the Splinter, the devourers and the message drop in the Secret and GM Notes callouts.
