# North Ward

> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - The North Ward is the quiet residential ward in the northeast of Waterdeep. The party's tavern, Trollskull Manor, stands here, and so does the Gralhund family's villa on Saerdoun Street.
> - **Finding Floon** ends with Volo's deed to Trollskull Manor. **Trollskull Alley** runs the renovation, the neighbors, the Grand Opening on Ches 20 and the Twin Parades from this ward.
> - **Fireball!** sets off its blast in Trollskull Alley at first light on Ches 22 and sends the bodies to the ward's Watch station. Its nimblewright sweep clears Margaster House and points at Gralhund Villa.
> - **Gralhund Villa** is the quinpartite confrontation at the villa on Saerdoun Street, and its rooftop chase crosses the roofs of the ward.
> - **Faction Outposts** sends the party to the Zhentarim Interrogation House on Brindul Alley and the Cassalanters' Asmodean Shrine on Aveen Street.
> - Faction events set here: Harpers (the tailor and the rented lodging in the First Meeting, **The Talking Mare** at a stable on Brondar's Way, **The Cell Is Compromised** in a rented room at 6 Brondar's Way, **A Friend's House** at Ulbrinter Villa, **The Sleeping Asset** at the Watch post), Bregan D'aerthe (the Watch station in the First Meeting, **The Wazoo Affair** at a publisher's office, **Contact Severed**), Lords' Alliance (**The Archer Above**, across the rooftops and at the Watch house), Order of the Gauntlet (**The Black Viper Investigation** at the same publisher's office, **The Shrine on Aveen Street**), Doom Raiders (**Dread Lord**, at a saddler three streets from Trollskull Alley) and Emerald Enclave (**Summerstrider**, a report from the ward).
> - Location journals: **Trollskull Manor** and **Gralhund Villa** map the two sites in this ward. The **House of Inspired Hands** is in the Sea Ward, and no other mapped site sits here.

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the ward as a place to run: where the party lives, who its neighbors are, and which quiet houses hide the Harpers, the Zhentarim and the Cassalanters.

The North Ward is the part of Waterdeep that goes home in the evening, a ward of rowhouses and noble villas where the party's tavern stands and the city's day servants arrive each dawn.

## At a Glance

- **District Type:** Residential, with inns, taverns, gambling halls and shops. The wiki calls it the quietest ward in the city (*Volo's Guide to Waterdeep* 5e).
- **Boundaries:** The walls of the City of the Dead lie to the southeast, and the Trollwall to the east and north. The southern border follows Andamaar's Street and Trader's Way up to Shield Street, which forms the western border as far as Vondil Street. North of Vondil Street the High Road forms the western border all the way to the Trollwall (wiki, *City of Splendors* 2e). The cliffs of the Cliffwatch interrupt the wall between Endcliff Tower and the Watchway Tower in the City of the Dead (wiki, Dragon Heist).
- **Founded:** 1276 DR, when the ward was made an official ward of Waterdeep together with the **Sea Ward** (wiki, *The Grand History of the Realms* 3e).
- **Population:** No source gives a ward figure, and the wiki leaves the field blank. Its Inhabitants text names upper-middle-class Waterdhavians, successful merchants, lesser noble families and civic workers. For scale, the city figures are 1,347,680 across Waterdeep and the farmland it commands (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* 3e, 1372 DR), 132,661 counted as the metropolis, and about 200,000 in the city proper by the wiki's own estimate.
- **Lifestyle:** The wiki infobox leaves this blank. Its Inhabitants text and Dragon Heist describe comfortable to wealthy households, each able to afford a few servants who are hired by the day and go home to other wards at night.
- **Services:** Inns and taverns, gambling halls, shops, and two guildhalls, the House of Crystal (the glassblowers') and a House of Healing. The ward has a City Watch station with a cellar morgue, a Watch-manned torchtower, a Fellowship of Innkeepers representative and a free public walkway along the Cliffwatch.
- **Influences:** Green and orange are the ward's colors, and its mascot is the white dove, shown in flight. Many homes keep dovecotes on the roof, and great flocks circle the city at dawn and dusk (Dragon Heist).
- **Primary Peoples:** No ward figure exists. The city figures are humans 64%, dwarves 10%, elves 10% (mostly moon elves), halflings 5%, half-elves 5%, gnomes 3%, half-orcs 2% and others 1% (wiki, *City of Splendors* 2e).
- **Languages:** The wiki leaves this blank. [research]
- **Religion:** Holyhands House (all deities) and the Hospice of St. Laupsenn (**Ilmater**). The Major Temples table in Dragon Heist places both here, and the wiki's temple list for the ward agrees. No dominant faith is sourced for the ward. [research] See the temples bullet under Culture in Life in the Ward, and **The Deities** for the other wards' temples.
- **Governance:** No source names a ward government. The City Watch keeps a station that takes reports and holds the dead, and Endcliff Tower, a Watch-manned torchtower, overlooks the Cliffwatch. Who commands them is [research].
- **Architecture:** Rowhouses fill the gaps between the villas of the lesser noble houses (wiki, *Volo's Guide to Waterdeep* 5e). The inns and residences of the Cliffwatch have terraces and balconies that face east over the countryside. Trollskull Manor is four stories of stone with five chimneys and a turret.
- **Economy:** Taverns, shops and guildhalls serve residents who ride hire-coaches to work in other wards. Day servants arrive by dray each morning from the poorer parts of the city. Guild law applies as everywhere in the city.
- **Organizations:** House Gralhund and the Black Network resources in its villa, the Harpers (Ulbrinter Villa), Manshoon's Zhentarim (the Interrogation House), the Fellowship of Innkeepers (represented by Broxley Fairkettle), the City Watch (the station and Endcliff Tower), the Watchful Order (Ulkoria Stonemarrow and Rishaal the Page-Turner in Trollskull Alley) and the Guild of Glassblowers, Glaziers and Spectacle-makers (the House of Crystal).

> #### Secret
> - Manshoon's Zhentarim keep a safe house and interrogation center on Brindul Alley. Avareen Windrivver and Zorbog Jyarkoth run it, three black flying snakes nest near the ceiling as couriers, and Ott Steeltoes, a captured Xanathar's Guild agent, is strapped to the rack in the back room. Avareen carries a pass-amulet to Kolat Towers, and the Kolat Towers structure doc describes the house as a field office connected to the towers by a teleportation circle.
> - A party that searched Urstul Floxin's effects at Gralhund Villa holds documents that name the alley. Any other party can find the house by canvassing the ward for a day (DC 14 Investigation, no faction membership needed), and Zhentarim response teams carry orders to take their prisoners here.
> - Samara, a prisoner in **Xanathar's Lair**, says Manshoon's organization is based in a tower in the North Ward. That is a misdirection from overheard talk. Kolat Towers stands in the Southern Ward, and the Interrogation House is the Zhentarim site that a North Ward search turns up.
> - The Cassalanters keep an Asmodean shrine on Aveen Street, which runs along the line between the Field Ward and the North Ward. The main house on the North Ward side looks like an ordinary residence, and a chandler's cellar on the Field Ward side opens into it. Below it a Hell cyst, a pinprick of Hell on the Material Plane, powers a summoning circle that calls two imps every nine days.
> - Nobody outside the household and the cult knows the family worships Asmodeus, apart from Jarlaxle, whose spy Vessa moves in its social circle. The party reaches the shrine through **Faction Outposts** or the Order of the Gauntlet's **The Shrine on Aveen Street**. It is a separate site from the temple beneath **Cassalanter Villa**.

## Landmarks

**Cliffwatch** — Public walkway and inn row
- A public walkway follows the edge of a cliff about 100 feet high, with no rails or walls. The city wall breaks on either side of it because the cliff is steep enough to serve as the defense (Dragon Heist; wiki, *City of Splendors* 2e).
- The liveliest and loveliest part of the ward stands along it. Lavish residences and luxurious inns have terraces and balconies that look east over the countryside, and anyone can walk the path and take the same view for free.
- Hook: no quest or faction event uses the walkway yet. [research]

**Endcliff Tower** — Watch torchtower
- A three-story tower on Saerdoun Street, across from Endcliff Lane and just north of Zun Villa, where the Trollwall ends at the cliffs. The City Watch mans it and keeps watch over the cliff edge for any sign of invasion (wiki, *City of Splendors* 2e).
- Like every named torchtower it holds a debriefing room, a small armory, evidence storage and six holding cells for up to ten people. Three beacon fires are kept ready (wiki, *City System* 2e).
- Hook: no quest or faction event uses the tower yet. [research]

**Trollskull Alley** — Street of shops and the party's neighbors
- The party's tavern stands at its north end. The Bent Nail is a woodshop run by **Tally Fellbranch**, Steam and Steel is a forge run by **Embric** and **Avi**, and Corellon's Crown is a greenhouse kept by **Fala Lefaliir**.
- The Tiger's Eye is a detective agency run by **Vincent Trench**, Book Wyrm's Treasure is a bookshop run by **Rishaal the Page-Turner**, and a few doors down is Frewn's Brews, a rival pub owned by **Emmek Frewn**.
- Hook: the **Fireball!** blast hits the alley at first light on Ches 22. It kills twelve in a 20-foot radius, including three North Ward servants out on early errands, and it may kill Fala or Tally.

**Trollskull Manor** — Tavern
- A four-story stone building at 10 Trollskull Alley with five chimneys, a turret on the northeast corner, and balconies over the alley and a shared courtyard with an oak in it. Dragon Heist describes it as a historic building facing a wide cul-de-sac hemmed in by old residences and shops.
- The tavern has been closed for years, and the poltergeist **Lif** haunts it. He is the spirit of the last barkeeper, a dwarf, and has minded the building alone since 1479 DR.
- Hook: Volo gives the party the deed for finding Floon, and the building becomes their home base. The **Trollskull Manor** location journal maps it, and the Trollskull Manor guide runs the tavern.

**Gralhund Villa** — Noble villa
- A walled villa that fills a full block of Saerdoun Street in an upper-class residential neighborhood. The perimeter wall stands 12 feet high, the main gate faces the street under a two-story carriage arch, and only the Coach House roofline shows over the wall.
- Lord **Orond Gralhund** and Lady **Yalah Gralhund** keep a household here with guards, cooks, maids and their two children. A visitor without an appointment is refused at the gate.
- Hook: **Fireball!** ends with the detector's strongest signal outside the Faire pointing at this house, and the **Gralhund Villa** quest opens here. The **Gralhund Villa** location journal maps it.

> - Jarlaxle's ledger lists O. Gralhund of Saerdoun Street as the buyer of nimblewright NW-02, the runaway that the party's detector tracks to this house.

**Ulbrinter Villa** — Noble villa and Harper gathering place
- An estate on Delzorin Street between Vhezoar Street and Brondar's Way, just south of Trollskull Alley (Dragon Heist). **Remallia Haventree** keeps it as the lady of House Ulbrinter, and her salons are famous for their guest lists.
- A tailor at 8 Delzorin Street, Seldo Wynd, fits the Harper members for the salon, and Mirt has paid his bill. Seldo is a tailor and nothing else.
- Hook: Mirt sends the Harper members to Remallia's salon to find a spy on the guest list in **A Friend's House**. A separate rented house at 12 Delzorin Street, with five bunks, is the Harpers' lodging.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - The villa is one of the Harpers' secret gathering places, and Remallia's Harper identity stays hidden from the party until she chooses to reveal it. The lodging at 12 Delzorin Street is kept by an intermediary and has no link to the villa.

**Hospice of St. Laupsenn** — Temple of Ilmater
- Ilmater's one temple in Waterdeep, listed in the ward by the Major Temples table and by the wiki. No source describes the building, its size, who built it or who saint Laupsenn was. [research]
- Vhaspar Holmdreg (Lawful Good, Illuskan Human, he/him), a priest in his seventies who is half blind with cataracts, works here and rents a room in the west wing of Kalain's residence.
- Hook: Dragon Heist uses Vhaspar to reach **Kalain**, since he brings her food, firewood and painting supplies. No campaign file uses him or the Hospice yet. [research]

**Holyhands House** — Temple of all deities
- The Major Temples table lists it as the temple of all deities, and the wiki lists it among the ward's temples (*City of Splendors: Waterdeep* 3e). No source gives its keepers, its size or its appearance. [research]
- It is a separate row from the House of Inspired Hands, which the same table lists as Gond's temple in the Sea Ward. No source says the two are one building.
- Hook: the Deities pages cite it as the table's temple for any god without one of its own. No quest or faction event uses it.

**Thort's Findings** — Curio shop in the head of the Swordmaiden
- The head of the broken Swordmaiden statue sits in a stand of tall trees in the middle of the block bounded by Hassantyr's Street, Tarsar's Street, Whaelgond Way and Ussilbran Street. A door replaces the center of its jaw and mouth.
- Undevvur Thort, a wizened ex-adventurer who leans on a cane that some locals say is more than a cane, lives inside among many levels, staircases and landings. He buys oddments from adventurers and travelers and labels each one with a placard in beautiful, flowing handwriting that names it or guesses at its purpose.
- Hook: nobles and wealthy merchants rent his wares as props for themed revels, and sages, alchemists and wizards visit him for useful items. No campaign file uses the shop.

**North Ward Watch Station** — City Watch station
- The ward's Watch station takes reports from residents and keeps the dead in a cellar morgue, where clerics cast *gentle repose* on the bodies. Sergeant **Marek Dunmere** takes reports on the day shift.
- A lamp burns over the door, and the front room smells of lamp oil and wet wool. The faction events call the building the station, the house and the post, and the page treats them as one (see Draft Notes).
- Hook: the Watch takes the **Fireball!** bodies here by midmorning. A character with renown 1 or higher in Force Grey, the Harpers, the Lords' Alliance, the Order of the Gauntlet or the Zhentarim can ask that faction's representative to pay for *speak with dead*. **Barnibus Blastwind** and **Saeth Cromley** visit any party that reports what it has learned.

## Characters of Interest

**Remallia "Remi" Haventree (Chaotic Good, Sun Elf, she/her)**
- The lady of House Ulbrinter and the widow of Lord Arthagast Ulbrinter. She is a gracious hostess who remembers details about everyone's life, and her villa is full of loyal servants and protective wards.
- Hook: she hosts the salon in **A Friend's House**, where Mirt asks the party to find a spy among twelve guests.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Remi is the de facto Harper leader in Waterdeep and runs the faction's intelligence from the villa, sending messages to her spies by a silver raven figurine. Assassins killed her husband and destroyed his remains, and every precaution she takes comes from that loss.
> - She decides whether a party can be trusted with real responsibility, and she reveals herself as a Harper only when she chooses to.

**Yalah Gralhund (Neutral Evil, Tethyrian Human, she/her)**
- The lady of House Gralhund, whose family motto is "We see both sides." She presents herself as a gracious, politically astute noblewoman with a keen mind and the wisdom to tell friend from foe.
- Hook: she is the party's opponent at **Gralhund Villa**, and the day-path confrontation takes place in her bedroom.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Yalah is the true power in the house, and her husband Orond cowers behind her. She wants a seat as a Masked Lord and has been refused before, and the gold in the Vault is her last best chance.
> - She runs the Zhentarim forces at her villa as her employees, and she does not know that Manshoon is their master.

**Broxley Fairkettle (Lawful Good, Strongheart Halfling, he/him)**
- The Fellowship of Innkeepers representative for the North Ward, a laid-back, law-abiding halfling with mutton chops and bushy eyebrows. He is the father of nine and visits the party's tavern often to wish it well.
- He urges the party to join the guild "to avoid further harassment" and wants them registered before anything goes wrong with the building again. The membership cost is part of the tavern's regular expenses.

**Emmek Frewn (Neutral Evil, Illuskan Human, he/him)**
- A salty northerner who opened Frewn's Brews a few doors down and talks loudly about how adventurers ruin neighborhoods. He wants his pub to be the most successful tavern in the North Ward and the party's to fail.
- Hook: he calls on the party during the renovation of **Trollskull Alley** with a bottle of cheap wine, while his sabotage campaign is already running.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Emmek is a refugee from Neverwinter whose sisters bought him out of the family tanning business. He owes **Istrid Horn** a loan of 150 gp, enforced by Zhentarim thugs, and he pays the Shard Shunners, a halfling wererat gang, to sabotage the party.
> - He knows his pub is worse than the party's, and that is why he keeps up the sabotage.

**Ulkoria Stonemarrow (Neutral Good, Shield Dwarf, she/her)**
- Known as "the Gargoyle", an archmage of the Watchful Order whose face is frozen in a scowl that frightens adults and children alike. Nobody knows where she lives, and she is never seen without her shield guardian.
- She once owned the tavern in Trollskull Alley, and she stops in for a drink whenever she passes through the ward while her shield guardian waits outside. She keeps her criticism to herself if she dislikes what the party has done with the place.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Ulkoria sold the tavern, watched every later owner fail and visits to see whether this party will do better. She is a senior member of the Watchful Order and, in the campaign only, a Masked Lord of Waterdeep, though she never confirms it in the party's presence.

**Vincent Trench (Lawful Evil, Rakshasa disguised as a Human, he/him)**
- A sharp-dressed, pipe-smoking private detective who runs the Tiger's Eye across the alley. He speaks concisely, charges fairly and calls on new neighbors with a rate card.
- Hook: after the **Fireball!** blast he arrives at the scene and knows more than he says, and he is the party's best-connected neighbor for information.

> **[GM]**

> - Vincent is Valantajar, a rakshasa who has lived in Waterdeep for years under a string of identities and has grown fond of the city. His interest in the party serves himself and is not hostile at first.
> - Rishaal the Page-Turner has noticed that Vincent buys books on planar binding, and he shares his suspicion with a party that shows interest.

## Life in the Ward

**Governing metaphor:** The North Ward is a well-run household whose staff works by the day, where the family leaves for business each morning, the servants arrive to keep the house, and the street returns to the residents at dusk.

### Culture
- The ward wakes in a fixed order. Before dawn it is silent enough that a visitor hears a resident a street away open a sash and clear her throat, then the birdsong begins, and then the drays arrive with servants hired for the day (Dragon Heist).
- The servants wear the colors of their trades: launderers and cooks in white, chimney sweeps and housecleaners in black, valets and child-minders in gray, gardeners in green and tutors in blue. Residents leave in rattling hire-coaches, and within an hour the ward has become noisy and settled again until the evening reverses the whole process.
- Ward pride follows the colors green and orange and the white dove. Homes and businesses fly them on race and parade days, when the city's wards compete (Dragon Heist).
- The Gentle Mermaid is a festhall and gambling hall in the center of a courtyard surrounded by Sulmor Street, Hassantyr's Street, Copper Street and the High Road. The wiki says it held the largest gambling room in Waterdeep in the 1360s DR (*Volo's Guide to Waterdeep*), and tavern tales say the stone heads on its turrets speak to passersby (wiki, citing the novel *Thornhold*).
- Manycats Alley winds through the interior of a block between Julthoon Street and Trader's Way. Cats feed on scraps from the butchers' shops, and carved stone heads on the buildings whisper cryptic messages to anyone who walks the alley alone (Dragon Heist).
- Temples in the ward, as the Major Temples of Waterdeep table in Dragon Heist prints them. The other thirteen rows belong to other wards and are on **The Deities**.

### Defense
- Small squads walk daily and nightly rounds from the city's watch posts and blow tin whistles to bring more of the Watch (Dragon Heist). The North Ward's patrol size is [research].
- The Watch station holds the dead in a cellar morgue while its investigation runs, and the wiki says Endcliff Tower watches the cliff edge for invasion.
- Response Teams do not attack the party's tavern unprovoked while customers are present. A public massacre in a licensed North Ward establishment brings the City Guard, the Watchful Order and possibly Vajra Safahr in person within the hour (Trollskull Manor guide).
- Ulkoria Stonemarrow and Rishaal the Page-Turner are both Watchful Order members in good standing, and either can sign the written attestation that the Order's inspector asks for about the tavern's poltergeist.
- After the fireball, the Watch sweeps the ward for witnesses and a patrol may arrest a patron of the party's tavern (Trollskull Manor guide).

### Politics
- No source names a ward government or a ward leader. [research]
- Lesser noble houses keep villas among the rowhouses. The wiki lists Adarbrent, Agundar, Anteos, Gost, Gralhund, Hothemer, Hawkwinter, Kothont, Phull, Roaringhorn, Ulbrinter and Wands, and Dragon Heist adds Amcathra on the High Road, Margaster between Stabbed Sailor Alley and Shattercrock Alley, and Phylund on Copper Street.
- **Fireball!** clears Margaster House, where a household attendant nimblewright (NW-04) gives a moderate response and the family's logs are kept. The same sweep clears House Adarbrent, which the campaign places in the Sea Ward.
- The Fellowship of Innkeepers presses the party for membership through Broxley Fairkettle.

### Economy
- Residents work in other wards, and the ward's own trade serves their households: day servants, taverns, shops and guildhalls.
- The House of Crystal, the guildhall of the Glassblowers, Glaziers and Spectacle-makers, stands on Copper Street, where crystal balls and mirrors sell for 1 sp to 600 gp (wiki, *Volo's Guide to Waterdeep* 5e). The wiki also lists furriers, harp makers, a tobacconist, a pastry shop and Corondorr's Countinghouse, which trains guild members and shopkeepers in accounting.
- Gaxly Rudderbust publishes *The Waterdeep Wazoo* from a second-floor office at the corner of Immar Street and Stallion Street. Hessa Dorn keeps a stable on Brondar's Way between two bakeries.
- The Cassalanter Counting House, a Sea Ward banking operation, holds accounts throughout the ward, and the party's renovation loan may pass through it.
- Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) doubles foot traffic across the ward as civic ceremonies and street celebrations draw crowds, and the party's tavern earns one of its best days of the year.
- Rents and prices in the ward are [research].

### Lore
- The ward became an official ward of Waterdeep in 1276 DR, together with the Sea Ward.
- The Swordmaiden was a walking statue, nearly identical to the Honorable Knight apart from its female form and open-faced helm. It was felled during the Spellplague after it caused chaos and slaughter, and the ward's residents took it apart in their frustration. Parts of it now stand in buildings and as freestanding sculpture across the ward, and the statue is too broken to be animated. See **The Dragonward and Walking Statues**.
- Trollskull Manor has had a string of owners. Ulkoria once owned the tavern and sold it to a family of shield dwarves, who fell on hard times and sold it to a woman who ran it as an orphanage. She turned out to be a hag who cooked and ate the children. Volo bought the building recently, hoping its haunting would give him a chapter for his next book.
- The poltergeist Lif has minded the building alone since 1479 DR.

## Overview

The North Ward is the quietest ward in Waterdeep, a district of rowhouses and noble villas in the city's northeast corner. Merchants, lesser noble families and civic workers live here, and day servants arrive by dray each dawn to keep their houses. The Cliffwatch, a public walkway above a cliff, runs along the ward's edge, and white doves circle the rooftops at dawn and dusk. A long-closed tavern, Trollskull Manor, stands at the end of Trollskull Alley among old residences and shops.

## Read Aloud

Sensory anchors for the GM to build the passage from (not written out):
- The ward before dawn: a street so silent that a resident clears her throat a block away, then birdsong, then drays rolling in with servants in white, black, gray, green and blue.
- White doves circling over the rooftops and dovecotes at dawn and dusk.
- The Cliffwatch: wind off a drop of about 100 feet with no rail, terraces and balconies above it, and the countryside spread out to the east.
- The morning rush: footsteps on the sidewalks and hire-coaches rattling away, which lasts about an hour before the ward settles again.
- Trollskull Alley: sawdust at the door of the Bent Nail, hammer sounds and chimney smoke at Steam and Steel, a greenhouse with glass walls full of blossoms, and the orange-and-black sign of the Tiger's Eye.
- Trollskull Manor: four stories of stone, five chimneys, a turret, balconies over the alley and an empty sign above the door.
- Manycats Alley: cats among the butchers' scraps and carved stone heads on the walls that some walkers report whispering to them.
- The head of the Swordmaiden in a stand of tall trees, with a door where the mouth should be and placards in flowing handwriting on every oddment inside.
- The Watch station: a lamp over the door, and a front room that smells of lamp oil and wet wool, with a sergeant writing every name in a ledger.
- Gralhund Villa: a 12-foot wall that runs the length of a block, a carriage arch over the gate, and guards who refuse visitors without an appointment.

**Tone:** A tidy, comfortable ward where nothing seems to happen until the servants arrive, played straight and a little dry. Keep the Zhentarim and Cassalanter sites behind ordinary doors, and keep the secrets in the Secret and GM Notes callouts.
