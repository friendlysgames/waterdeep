# Dock Ward

> Structuring draft.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
> - The Dock Ward is Waterdeep's port ward, a district of piers, shipyards, warehouses and tenements that holds, with the Field Ward, most of the city's poor. Xanathar's Guild treats it as its main surface territory, and Manshoon's Zhentarim fight a low-level street war against it here.
> - **Finding Floon** is set here from start to finish. Volo sends the party to the Skewered Dragon, the Dock Ward investigation leads to the Candle Lane warehouse, and the sewers below the ward lead to the Guild hideout where Floon is held.
> - **Trollskull Alley** has the Doom Raiders assign **The Dockside Killer**, names Filthy Meg as a Mistshore source, hands out a Guild token for the Old Xoblob Shop after the Field of Triumph, and brings the Sea Maidens Faire fleet into the harbor on Ches 21.
> - **Fireball!** sends the nimblewright detector to a Dock Ward pier, where the *Heartbreaker* and *Hellraiser* are docked and the *Eyecatcher* lies at anchor. **Gralhund Villa** leaves Chirada's letter in the family library, which says the Second Eye was carried from a Dock Ward warehouse to Yellowspire.
> - **Faction Outposts** sends the party to Grinda Garloth's in Mistshore, the street-level entry to the Xanathar chain, and to the Seven Masks Theater, the entry to the Bregan D'aerthe chain. **Xanathar's Lair** lists Watch patrol windows in the Dock Ward drainage network among its intelligence, and **Sea Maidens Faire** is mapped from the fleet's pier here.
> - Faction events set here: Doom Raiders (**The Dockside Killer**, **The Missing Snobeedle**, the First Meeting at Istrid's warehouse, **Wolf**, **Ardragon**, **Dread Lord**, **Davil's Return**), Bregan D'aerthe (the Seven Masks Theater in the First Meeting, **The Handkerchief and the Girl**, **Three Nights**, **The Compromised Eye**, **The Theater's Back Room**, **Soldier**, **Officer**, **Houseless Noble**, **Coin Pouches**, **Kreb Drops the Cover**, **The Killer's Fate**), Emerald Enclave (**The Grells in the Dock Ward**, **Autumnreaver**, **Winterstalker**, **The Water Table Stirs**), Harpers (**The Talking Mare**, **The Doppelganger Auditions**, **A Friend's House**, **The Sleeping Asset**, **Harpshadow**, **Wise Owl**), Lords' Alliance (**The Long Watch**, **The Exile's Return**, **The Red Wizard's Price**, **The Archer Above**, **Stingblade**), Order of the Gauntlet (the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield, **Breaking Up the Brawl**, **The Shard Shunners**, **Marcheon**, **Vindicator**) and Force Grey (**The Trouble with Meloon**, **The Legate's Eyes**, **Senior Griffon**).
> - Location journals: **Zhentarim Warehouse**, **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** and **Sea Maidens Faire** apply. No other mapped site sits in this ward.

**Purpose:** Gives the GM the ward as a place to run: where the first investigation leads, whose territory each street is, and which waterfront doors open onto the campaign's secrets.

The Dock Ward is the part of Waterdeep that works for a living, a steep ward of piers, warehouses and tenements where the city's cargo comes ashore and its poorest residents carry it.

## At a Glance

- **District Type:** Port and working waterfront. Docks, shipbuilding yards, warehouses, poorhouses and tenements, with taverns, inns and festhalls for sailors (wiki, 2e-era lists; Dragon Heist).
- **Boundaries:** No source gives a full boundary, and the wiki's Dock Ward page has no boundary paragraph. The ward lies on the Great Harbor. The wiki's Southern Ward page puts the Dock Ward on the Southern Ward's west and south side, across the Way of the Dragon, and its South Gate page says the South Gate stood where the two wards met. Dragon Heist's orientation rule for the Honorable Knight puts a visitor in the Dock Ward when the statue stands to the north and east.
- **Founded:** 1035 DR by Ahghairon, as one of the four original wards with the Castle, Trades and Southern (Temple) Wards (wiki, 2e).
- **Population:** No source gives a ward figure, and the wiki leaves the field blank. Dragon Heist says that, aside from the Field Ward, this is where most of Waterdeep's poor live. For scale, the city figures are 1,347,680 across Waterdeep and the farmland it commands (*Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* 3e, 1372 DR), 132,661 counted as the metropolis, and about 200,000 in the city proper by the wiki's own estimate.
- **Lifestyle:** Poor. Dragon Heist says the residents often work the hardest while living under the harshest conditions, and that far too many inns charge by the hour.
- **Services:** Streetlamps that the Guild of Chandlers and Lamplighters repairs once a season and that are stolen or smashed within weeks. The Muleskull Tavern, the Dungsweepers' Guild's hall. Seaswealth Hall, the Fishmonger's Fellowship's hall above the fish markets. Ilmater's Safe Harbor, a soup kitchen (wiki). Shop signs appear only near the piers.
- **Influences:** Burgundy and orange, and a swordfish mascot that has always been painted green for reasons lost to time (Dragon Heist). The ward drafts its race champions from the sailors who come to the city.
- **Primary Peoples:** No ward figure exists. The city figures are humans 64%, dwarves 10%, elves 10% (mostly moon elves), halflings 5%, half-elves 5%, gnomes 3%, half-orcs 2% and others 1% (wiki, *City of Splendors* 2e).
- **Languages:** The wiki leaves this blank. Dragon Heist says only that the ward is home to some of the least literate people in the city. [research]
- **Religion:** The Chapel of the Steadfast Shield (Torm), which only the Order of the Gauntlet's files place here. The Dragon Heist Major Temples table has no Dock Ward row. The wiki lists Ilmater's Safe Harbor, a soup kitchen, which is not a temple. See the temples bullet under Culture in Life in the Ward. No dominant faith is sourced for the ward. [research]
- **Governance:** No ward officer is sourced. [research] The Watch walks squads of eight, rising to as many as a dozen at night, from a Dock Ward post and a watchhouse on Ship Street.
- **Architecture:** Warehouses, poorhouses and tall tenements pressed so close that midday light barely reaches the cobblestones. Steep streets with little room for pedestrians, and warehouses that often carry no sign at all.
- **Economy:** Docks, shipyards, warehouses and fish markets. Guild halls for the sea trades. Moneylending, fences, pawnbrokers and hired protection. Guild law applies as everywhere in the city.
- **Organizations:** Xanathar's Guild, Manshoon's Zhentarim, the Doom Raiders (Istrid Horn's warehouse, a Black Network safe house), Bregan D'aerthe (the Seven Masks Theater and the Faire's pier), the Harpers (Corene Wyldath and a chandler contact), the Emerald Enclave (Pier 17), the Order of the Gauntlet (the Chapel), the Dungsweepers' Guild, the Watch and the guilds of the sea trades.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Secret
> - The man killing elf and half-elf sailors off Ship Street is Soluun Xibrindas, a drow gunslinger of Bregan D'aerthe. Jarlaxle's public story is that he disowned Soluun for killing without orders, and the story is a cover. Jarlaxle knows about the murders and has not decided what to do, and Soluun carries a forged Bregan D'aerthe token so that the band can deny him. Nothing in **The Dockside Killer** lets the party learn this.
> - The ward is the front line of a street war between Xanathar's Guild and Manshoon's Zhentarim. It began when Manshoon's envoys came to Skullport three months before the fireball and Xanathar killed them. Brawls between the two are common, and the Watch is called to them often.

## Landmarks

**The Skewered Dragon** — Tavern
- A dilapidated dive that faces an alley between Net Street and Fillet Lane, with both front windows smashed and a ship's anchor lodged in the roof. Haggard dockworkers drink from huge tankards inside, and they talk to strangers only for a bribe or a good argument (Dragon Heist; wiki).
- Hook: in **Finding Floon**, Volo says Floon was last seen here. A regular remembers Floon leaving with Renaer Neverember around midnight, with five men following them out.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Manshoon's Splinter Zhentarim run the tavern as an informal ward headquarters, and Dragon Heist calls it Zhentarim-owned. The regulars are Splinter men on home ground, and they report to Kolat Towers, not to Davil Starsong.
> - They know who grabbed Floon and Renaer, and they either do not know the way to Candle Lane or will not say. If the party stalls, two Splinter thugs approach it near the tavern, warn it off the Renaer kidnapping and give away both answers.

**Old Xoblob Shop** — Curio shop
- A shop on the corner of Zastrow Street and Fillet Lane with a deep purple facade, a stuffed beholder in the window and a sign in elaborate letters. Inside, every wall and knickknack is purple, and lavender smoke drifts out of the door (Dragon Heist).
- Xoblob, a wizened deep gnome in plum robes, runs it and sells trinkets. He saw five men in black leather armor jump Floon and a well-dressed companion outside his door, and one of them had a winged snake tattoo on his neck.
- Hook: in **Finding Floon**, a new purple item or a DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check loosens his tongue. In **Trollskull Alley**, a Guild scout hands the party a token for the shop after the Field of Triumph.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Xoblob is an informant for Xanathar's Guild. The stuffed beholder is a magical sensor, and Xanathar occasionally watches the street through it. The party cannot discover this at Finding Floon level, so plant it if the scene fits and do not press it.

**Muleskull Tavern** — Tavern and Dungsweepers' guildhall
- A tavern on Ship Street that doubles as the hall of the Dungsweepers' Guild (wiki). It opens before dawn for dock workers, and the guild leases the cart yard behind it. No proprietor is sourced. [research]
- Hook: in **The Long Watch**, Lords' Alliance members meet a sweeping crew here at six bells every morning for a tenday and guard it on its rounds. In **The Dockside Killer**, the Doom Raiders watch the door from second bell to fourth for three nights, and a half-elf regular named Heldar is the next target.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - The campaign outline ties the Dungsweepers' Guild to the Dock Ward underworld, and no event says how. [research]

**Seven Masks Theater** — Theater
- A public theater for a lower-class audience, where ship captains and sailors get in free (Dragon Heist). Playbills name the owner as Rongquan Mystere, and a booking manager who calls himself Kreb Sorrush handles casting inquiries and schedules. The theater has a ghost that moves props during rehearsals.
- Hook: after **Fireball!**, two theater tickets on Faire stationery reach the party. In the Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting, orders are left at its box office. In **Faction Outposts**, the theater is the street-level entry to the Bregan D'aerthe chain, and members at renown 3 or higher walk in without questions.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - The theater is a Bregan D'aerthe front. Rongquan Mystere is a disguise of Jarlaxle's, and Kreb Sorrush is Krebbyg. One dressing room is kept empty as a safe house, with two drow guards inside whenever it is in use.
> - The ghost is Malcolm Brizzenbright, the theater's founder. He is not hostile, and he will tell a respectful visitor that "Rongquan" is a false identity and that drow come and go at irregular hours. Jarlaxle also uses the theater for neutral-ground meetings with other factions.
> - Soluun Xibrindas picks his victims from the audience here. See the Secret.

**Mistshore** — Burned-out harbor neighborhood
- A run-down neighborhood on the harbor's north shore that reeks of fish and scorched timber. A fire swept through it a little over a year ago, and most of the buildings are burned-out shells with sails for roofs. Nets and fishing poles line the dock, and the 15-foot water freezes over in winter (Dragon Heist).
- The wiki traces Mistshore to the Spellplague, when the neglected northern harbor filled with sunken hulls, and dates the fire to Mirtul 1491. It says the Watch refused to patrol the shore (wiki, past tense).
- Hook: in **Faction Outposts**, anyone who asks about Xanathar weapons or black-market arms in a Dock Ward tavern is pointed to Grinda Garloth within a day (DC 12 Charisma). In **Trollskull Alley**, a faction referral from Remallia Haventree or Yagra Stonefist lowers the DC to reach Filthy Meg from 16 to 12.

**Zhentarim Warehouse** — Warehouse, Candle Lane
- A warehouse at the back of a walled yard on Candle Lane, with painted-over windows and a black winged snake painted above the door handle. The gate is unlocked. The warehouse is mapped in **Zhentarim Warehouse**.
- Hook: in **Finding Floon**, the party arrives to find twelve corpses, four kenku searching the wreckage and Renaer Neverember hidden in a back room.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - The Zhentarim cell that ran the warehouse died in a Xanathar's Guild raid. The kenku are the Guild's, left to kill any Zhentarim who return. A cellar trapdoor leads down to the sewer route to the Guild hideout.
> - Chirada's letter in **Gralhund Villa** says the Second Eye was carried from a warehouse in the Dock Ward to Yellowspire. See Draft Notes.

**Istrid Horn's Warehouse** — Moneylending house
- A guarded warehouse on a Dock Ward street of salt-stained brick, with two broad-shouldered guards at the side door. Crates stack to the rafters, and a small lamplit office at the back holds Istrid's ledger.
- **Istrid Horn** lends from it for the Doom Raiders, up to 200 gp with 10 percent of the principal added at the end of each tenday.
- Hook: at 9 a.m. after the First Meeting, Yagra Stonefist leads new Doom Raiders members here. In **The Missing Snobeedle**, the party can bring Dasher's claim to Istrid, and in **Ardragon** a target waits in her cellar.

**Sea Maidens Faire Pier** — Private pier, Smugglers' Dock
- A private pier where the galleons *Heartbreaker* and *Hellraiser* dock across from each other, painted crimson and gold under Faire pennants, with carnival stalls and awnings between them. A ramp leads up to each deck, and the pier is busy enough that nobody notices a visitor until the visitor is aboard. The *Eyecatcher* lies at anchor a quarter-mile out in Deepwater Harbor, and a harbor skiff is the usual way to reach her.
- The fleet sails into harbor on Ches 21, the opening day of Fleetswake, and leaves at dawn on Tarsakh 20. Captain Zardoz Zord keeps regular hours on the *Eyecatcher* and welcomes interesting visitors. The pier is mapped in **Sea Maidens Faire**.
- Hook: in **Fireball!**, the nimblewright detector spins as the party comes within 500 feet of the pier, with too many overlapping signals to isolate one. In **The Dive**, Guild divers stage a charge against the fleet's hidden hull.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle. The crew are drow in human form, under an illusion projected from the gilded drow figurehead on each ship's forecastle. The submersible *Scarlet Marpenoth* keel-docks beneath the *Eyecatcher* once the fleet arrives.

**Xanathar Sewer Hideout** — Guild hideout beneath the ward
- Eleven rooms of a decommissioned vault below the Dock Ward, reached by following yellow chalk guildsign through the sewers from a grate half a block east of the Candle Lane warehouse. It is mapped in **Xanathar Sewer Hideout**.
- Q09 is a private cellar under the home of the Peabodys, a halfling family who brew beer on Fishgut Alley and have survived the ward by minding their own affairs. It gives the party a second way in and out.
- Hook: in **Finding Floon**, Floon is held in the boss chamber at 1 hit point, and Nihiloor leaves through a stone portal at the far end before the party can engage it.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Two Bregan D'aerthe agents are posing as Guild guards here, and they lifted the locket's Eye from the forced Q03 strongbox before dawn. The Guild does not know it is gone, and the party cannot recover it.
> - One guard was pulled off the watch that night and reassigned to a Dock Ward street crew. A captured goblin or Zemk grumbles about it.

**Pier 17 and the South Quay** — Pier and cargo quay
- Pier 17 stands on the south quay, the stretch of waterfront that the alley from the Muleskull runs toward. Sarna, a boat owner who watches the harbor for the Emerald Enclave, keeps her boat at the quay and is aboard at dawn.
- Hook: in **The Grells in the Dock Ward**, three people have vanished from Pier 17 on three nights, and the nest is a closed warehouse at the end of the quay. In **Winterstalker**, Sarna's notes report two flat barges that come in light to Pier 17 and leave riding low before each tide.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Xanathar's Guild has been cutting a cargo tunnel under the south quay for a season, and the barges carry the spoil out of it. A tenday ago the Guild broke into a passage from Undermountain, and two grells flew up through the breach from the Grell Hideout on Level 1.
> - The Guild left the grells alone because they keep the Watch and the quay hands away from Pier 17. A Guild agent paid two dockhands, Garrit Moll and Tobiah Ashby, to leave the pier on the night a seamstress named Mirsa was taken.

## Characters of Interest

**Istrid Horn (Lawful Evil, Shield Dwarf, she/her)**
- The Doom Raiders' Master of Trade and Coin, who lends from a guarded warehouse and makes every loan sound like a kindness. She calls borrowers "dear", taps her ledger when she names a figure and blesses each signed agreement with "Vergadain smile on it".
- Hook: a new Doom Raiders member can borrow from her the morning after the First Meeting. If a payment is missed, two of her collectors call that evening, speak plainly and leave without hurting anyone, and her manner cools until the debt is paid.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Istrid likes having people in her debt and treats every loan as leverage. Emmek Frewn owes her 150 gp, and Zhentarim thugs collect it.

**Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him)**
- A young drow gunslinger who runs the Seven Masks Theater's bookings as Kreb Sorrush. He is rash and cheerful, and he leaves the thinking to Fel'rekt Lafeen.
- Hook: he sends the theater invitation after **Fireball!**, and he is the contact for many Bregan D'aerthe missions, including **The Theater's Back Room** and **Kreb Drops the Cover**.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Krebbyg works for Bregan D'aerthe and reports to Jarlaxle. His errand outside the ward is the message pickup at Fenerus Stormcastle's house in the Trades Ward.

**Soluun Xibrindas (Neutral Evil, Drow, he/him)**
- A drow gunslinger who has hunted the waterfront for some weeks. He picks an elf or half-elf sailor out of a Seven Masks audience, follows him home for two nights and strikes on the third with a hand crossbow bolt and a curved shortsword. He takes the head and the bolt.
- Hook: the Doom Raiders hunt him in **The Dockside Killer**, and a bloodstained Seven Masks playbill is the clue that points to the theater.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Soluun hates surface elves and serves Jarlaxle with a zealot's devotion. He is the elder brother of Nar'l Xibrindas, whom he despises. See the Secret for the disownment.
> - If the party captures him, he sits in a cell at the Ship Street watchhouse for a little over a tenday. Then a solicitor with papers signed "Rongquan Mystere" posts surety and the magistrate releases him. Gorra is furious. **The Killer's Fate** settles his expulsion from the band.

**Corene Wyldath (Neutral Good, Halfling, she/her)**
- A composed halfling who lives in the ward as Halla Ironstave, precise in her attention and sparing with her words. She missed her check-ins three weeks ago.
- Hook: in **The Sleeping Asset**, Mirt wants her found before he decides what to ask of her.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Corene is a Harper deep-cover agent who has worked inside Xanathar's Guild's Dock Ward operation for four months. One of Nihiloor's custom Occupying Devourers rides her. She is aware and cannot act, and she can be freed if the devourer is forced out. The **Trades Ward** page has the bench where she sits.

**Filthy Meg (Neutral, Merfolk, she/her)**
- A heavily tattooed merfolk information broker who surfaces near Mistshore and knows things she has no business knowing. Most of Waterdeep treats her as a rumor the Dock Ward tells.
- Hook: in **Trollskull Alley**, Remallia Haventree or Yagra Stonefist names her as a reliable Dock Ward source, and she resurfaces in **Sea Maidens Faire** as the best independent source on the Faire's crew movements.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### GM Notes
> - Meg belongs to no faction, which is what makes her valuable. Jarlaxle gave her a ring of true sight as a retainer and gets harbor intelligence in return, and the Cassalanters have paid her for Field Ward surveillance twice. Her network of homeless residents in the Field Ward is her main source.

**Grinda Garloth (Neutral, Human, she/her)**
- A wizard who lives over a closed dock in Mistshore and rents out her apparatus of Kwalish to clients whose business is underwater. She also sells black-market arms and armor at rates she considers entirely reasonable.
- Hook: in **Faction Outposts**, her shop is the street-level entry to the Xanathar chain. She negotiates if the party offers her more than the Guild does, and she reports the visit afterward.

## Life in the Ward

**Governing metaphor:** The Dock Ward is the city's loading yard, where everything from the sea comes ashore before it is sorted, taxed or hidden, and the people who haul it live in the shadow of the warehouses.

### Culture
- Most residents are poor and work hard. Dragon Heist adds that the ward holds some of the least literate people in the city, that most of its taverns hold habitual drinkers, and that there is less reading material here than anywhere but the Field Ward.
- The wiki quotes Elminster calling the ward a perpetual brawl that spreads over acres and stinks of rotting fish. Dragon Heist says the Dock Ward was long the most dangerous district in the city and that the Field Ward has since taken the title. The campaign outline still calls the Dock Ward the most dangerous. This page does not rank the wards, and treats the Dock Ward as dangerous after dark.
- The streets are steep and hard to read. Shop signs appear only near the piers, and warehouses often have none, so a visitor either knows where to go or is lost and a likely mark for pickpockets. Streetlamps lose their candles and glass as fast as the lamplighters replace them, and locals carry their own light at night. Yellow chalk eye marks on alley walls show Xanathar's territory to anyone who knows them.
- Ward pride is burgundy and orange and the green swordfish. The ward drafts its champions from the rough sailors who come to the city, and residents complain that these champions are citizens of the sea more than of the ward. A sailor who registers with a magister and pays taxes may compete as freely as a long-term resident.
- Three Daggers Alley carries an old curse. Three daggers appear out of thin air and attack anyone who crosses it, and the attack comes at random (Dragon Heist).
- Temples of the ward: the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield, a Torm chapel, is the only one the campaign files place here. It is a briefing temple for the Order of the Gauntlet, and no file gives it a priest, a size or a street. The Dragon Heist Major Temples table has no Torm row and no Dock Ward row, and it lists Umberlee's Queenspire at "Beach". The Order's files name the Temple of Helm in the Castle Ward, while the table puts Helm's Hall in the Southern Ward. See **The Deities**.
- The Chapel has no campaign hook beyond the Order's briefings. [research]
- Fleetswake runs from Ches 21 to Ches 30. Flags snap along the harbor wall, ships pay no docking fees, and captains give at least 1 gp a day. Winners deliver their trophies to Umberlee's priests at the Queenspire, and the offerings go into the harbor at sunset on Ches 30.

### Defense
- The Watch walks the ward in squads of eight, rising to as many as a dozen at night, and a shrill tin whistle summons more of them. A pair of operatives walks the Castle Ward by comparison (Dragon Heist).
- The Dock Ward Watch post smells of wet wool and lamp oil, and a duty sergeant sits at a ledger behind a long desk. A watchhouse on Ship Street holds prisoners in cells, and the wiki lists a guard barracks and the East Torch Tower in the ward without describing either.
- Gorra (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) is a Harbor Watch officer who has walked the Ship Street rounds for years. Captain Ilsa Dalloway and Constable Harl Pimm (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him), a stolid constable who counts lamp oil in every theater on his route, work crowd duty at the Seven Masks Theater.
- Captain Staget is Dragon Heist's Dock Ward Watch captain. He is an uptight man who does not drink or believe rumors, and he lets the Guild and the Zhentarim wear each other down because he sees no reason to stop them. No campaign file uses him.
- Dragon Heist's Stone-trail text takes arrested characters to a Dock Ward courthouse. The campaign's magister courts sit in the Castle Ward, and no campaign file uses the Dock Ward courthouse.
- The Watch refused to patrol Mistshore in the wiki's account, and the Guild of Chandlers and Lamplighters skips the most dangerous parts of the ward.

### Politics
- No ward officer or council is sourced. [research]
- The Guild and the Zhentarim fight for the streets, and the Watch arrives late and often. In **Finding Floon**, Krentz's crew killed a Doom Raiders operative in the ward two nights before the party arrives, and the Splinter's thugs are under arrest at a crime scene on the way to the Skewered Dragon.
- The Lords' Alliance protects the Dungsweepers' Guild in **The Long Watch**, and its agents hunt Harko Swornhold, an exile who was last seen recruiting kenku in the ward, in **The Exile's Return**.
- The wiki lists the halls of nine guilds in the ward. They include the Shipwrights' House on Dock Street, Seaswealth Hall, Shippers' Hall on Oar Alley, Coopers' Rest, Full Sails, League Hall for the skinners and tanners, the Jesters' Clubhouse and the Metal House of Wonders. Guild rules are on **The Guilds**, and which hall carries the most weight is [research].
- Guildmaster Piers Hobb leads the Dock Ward haulers' guild from a guildhouse on the quay, as **Stingblade** shows.

### Economy
- The ward trades in cargo, ships and fish. The wiki lists a shipwright, a rope-maker, a tannery, a fishmonger and a warehouse that rents coffin-sized lockboxes, and Dragon Heist puts the shipyards and warehouses at the center of the sea trade. Whether the wiki's 1357 to 1372 DR shops survive in 1492 DR is [research].
- The Doom Raiders lend from Istrid's warehouse and keep a Black Network safe house in a loft above Tarrow's Tallow and Wick, a chandler's shop at 22 Sail Street run by Wenna Tarrow. A Wolf-rank member asks for "a pound of blue-wick tallow, on Istrid's account".
- Nella Fen, a chandler at 16 Fillet Lane, is a Harper contact in **Harpshadow** who watches carts, loads and the waterfront.
- Pawnbrokers and fences work out of the ward, among them Ostrin Brindle, a pawnbroker in his sixties, and Tarn Hobble, a fence who keeps a pawnshop on Net Street and Dock Street.
- **Esloon Bezant** sells escort and protection with a crew of five fighters, and **Maxeene** works a hire stand off Fillet Lane.
- Rents and prices in the ward are [research].

### Lore
- Ahghairon founded the ward in 1035 DR with the Castle, Trades and Southern Wards.
- After the Spellplague of 1385 DR the northern harbor was neglected and filled with sunken hulls, and Waterdeep's outcasts built a community on the wrecks. A fire destroyed much of Mistshore in Mirtul 1491, and most of its buildings still stand burned and abandoned.
- The Griffon, a walking statue near Peaktop Aerie on Mount Waterdeep, stands looking southeast over the Dock Ward. See **The Dragonward and Walking Statues**.
- Xanathar's Guild grew out of Skullport, the outlaw port below the city, and the Dock Ward is its main territory above ground.
- The festival calendar is in **Timekeeping and the Festival Calendar**.

## Overview

The Dock Ward is Waterdeep's waterfront, a steep ward of piers, shipyards, warehouses and tenements on the Great Harbor. Along with the Field Ward, it is where most of the city's poor live. Streetlamps are stolen or smashed as fast as they are replaced, so locals carry their own light after dark. The ward has an old reputation for brawls, and it flies burgundy and orange with a green swordfish for a mascot.

## Read Aloud

Sensory anchors for the GM to build the passage from (not written out):
- The streets after dark: salt air and the smell of rotting fish, tall tenements pressing together until midday light barely reaches the cobblestones, lamps with their glass smashed and candles missing, and yellow chalk eye marks on alley walls.
- The Skewered Dragon: both front windows smashed, a ship's anchor in the roof and haggard patrons drinking from huge tankards.
- The Old Xoblob Shop: a deep purple facade, a stuffed beholder in the window and lavender smoke drifting from the door.
- The Muleskull at six bells: strong tea and spilled beer, cold salt air at each opening of the door, and sweepers with brooms beside a wheeled cart.
- The Seven Masks Theater: playbills, a box office and a crowd of sailors admitted for free.
- Mistshore: burned-out shells with sails for roofs, nets and fishing poles along the dock, scorched timber and ice on the water in winter.
- The Faire's pier: crimson and gold galleons, pennants snapping in the harbor wind and carnival stalls under canvas awnings.
- The Watch post: wet wool, lamp oil and a duty sergeant writing in a ledger.
- The ward's overall look: steep, crowded, dim and busy.

**Tone:** A rough working harbor, played gritty and a little weary. Danger comes from particular gangs and particular alleys, and the people who live here are tired and decent. Keep the Guild, Zhentarim and Bregan D'aerthe business in the Secret and GM Notes callouts.
