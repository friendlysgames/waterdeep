# The Cell Is Compromised

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs after **A Friend's House**, once the party has met Davil Starsong. In this Event, the Harper members can:
>
> - Compare Mirt's evidence of a leak with the actual information they reported.
> - Ask what he knows without being given an unsupported accusation.
> - Adopt a private reporting protocol that bypasses the compromised register.
> - Distinguish awareness of the leak from identifying and removing its source.

### Hook

At 16:00 on the first day after both trigger conditions and the applicable evidence deadline below are met, Mirt's courier delivers a plain note to each participating Harper member. It asks them to meet at 19:00 in the third-floor front room of 6 Saerdoun Street, a North Ward tenement rented for the evening. Helping companions receive no summons to the private faction briefing.

### Background

Orren Vale is a Harper records-relay clerk who secretly supplies Manshoon's Splinter through Beldan Rusk. His legitimate work collects operational summaries entered by cell contacts, allowing him to copy names, addresses and timings without meeting every field agent. Mirt has traced the leak for a tenday but hasn't isolated Orren among fourteen people who handled the material.

Orren is polite and methodical, asking agents to repeat a date or spell a name while he finishes a register entry. He frames access as routine clerical work and avoids speculation, using complete, ordinary sentences rather than coded threats. This event keeps his identity DM-only; **The Sleeping Asset** supplies three ways to expose him.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Contacts Know**
>
> Manshoon's leadership and Rusk's part in the timelines below are DM-only facts. The Harpers know that the Black Network has split, without knowing who leads its hostile branch. Davil initially believes Floxin leads that branch; his observations don't establish the belief as true. Mirt names Rusk only if the members identified him through Tessalar's testimony or recovered correspondence and shared that evidence. Otherwise, use “the recipient” in place of Rusk in his accounts. None of this event's evidence identifies Manshoon. If the party has independently established his identity and leadership and told Mirt, he can use the name in later discussion.

>
> Use the first applicable incident in the priority below, keeping the party's recorded outcomes rather than inventing a different history.
>
> 1. **Salon inquiry:** if the members reported Jelenn Urmbrusk's strained response to Zhentarim discussion, Mirt entered a background query in the relay register the following morning. Exactly forty-eight hours later, Rusk received its wording. That evening, Davil's contacts saw a Splinter intermediary coach Jelenn on those same questions. Mirt's intended second operative had not received the query.

Forty-eight hours later, Rusk received the request and sent a courier to ask a customs clerk who had filed those exact questions. Davil's observers heard their wording before Mirt's intended operative had approached the clerk. The actual drawer or east-entrance brick remains in use on its established schedule. Tessalar retains his actual fate, and the ledger retains its recorded physical state.

Forty-eight hours later, Rusk warned the cell, which evacuated and burned its remaining papers that evening. Davil's observers recorded the departure before the assigned watcher arrived. If the party defeated or captured those residents, skip this incident and use the next applicable entry.

Rusk received it forty-eight hours after entry and posted a Splinter observer at 17:30. >
> Davil mentioned the observed movement during an ordinary conversation after the party met him, without knowing which Harper inquiry it matched. Mirt made the connection; the evidence doesn't imply that the Doom Raiders and Manshoon's Splinter are the same organization.

### The Rented Room

The room has a low ceiling and a single window overlooking the street, with a plain table and enough chairs for the summoned members. Mirt paid the rent in cash without registering an agent's name, and no operational record of this meeting enters Orren's register.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt waits at the table beneath the front window, with a folded account beside a bottle whose seal hasn't been broken. He gestures toward the chairs before opening the paper, pointing to a date written beside the first name on the sheet.
>
> > "Close the door and sit. Someone inside the cell has been passing our operational information to people working against us. I can show you what left us and when they acted on it. I don't have a name for the sender yet."

> [!social]**Mirt's Evidence**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a careful contact narrowing a leak without accusing every agent who handled a report.
>
> Mirt keeps the applicable incident's account within reach of the members, with the dates and recipients marked beside its entries. Use the matching account below, followed by their questions about timing and procedure. He leaves the wine sealed while reviewing the evidence and gives no unsupported accusation against any of the fourteen handlers.

If the salon inquiry is the established incident, Mirt gives this account:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt puts the salon inquiry beside the later observation, drawing a finger from one recorded date to the next.
>
> > "I entered a query about Jelenn's response the morning after your report. Two days later, that evening, Davil's contacts heard an intermediary coach her on those same questions, before my second operative had even received them."

If the customs-house inquiry is the established incident, use this account:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt unfolds the customs-house account and places its questions beside the courier's recorded inquiry, keeping both sheets facing you.
>
> > "Our contact entered my questions about the collection routine. Two days later, Davil's observers heard a courier ask a customs clerk who had filed those exact questions, before my intended operative approached the clerk. The collection point and schedule you established remain in use; this hasn't changed what happened to Tessalar or brought the ledger back."

If the occupied Shesstra Street safehouse supplies the incident, use this account:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt opens the watcher assignment and puts the observation of the departing residents beside it, leaving the address uncovered between the sheets.
>
> > "The watcher assignment entered our register the next morning. Two days later, that evening, the people still occupying Shesstra Street left and burned their remaining papers, before our watcher arrived. Davil's people saw them going. I haven't established who warned them, but their departure came before our planned visit."

If the routine meeting-room inspection supplies the incident, use this account:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt turns the inspection entry toward you and taps the observer's arrival time beneath the address, keeping the intended appointment visible beside it.
>
> > "I entered an inspection at fourteen Saerdoun Street for six in the evening, three days later. Davil's contacts saw an observer waiting at half past five on the inspection day, before any Harper arrived. The instructions appeared in our register, and their wording never left it by an authorized route."

> [!qna]**Who do you suspect?**
>
> > "Fourteen people touched the material that leaked. I won't give you fourteen names to accuse, and I won't pick one because I'd like the answer to be simple. I'm narrowing the route it took."

> [!qna]**What did Davil tell you?**
>
> > "He mentioned the movement his people saw. He didn't know which of our instructions it matched, but I did. The Doom Raiders watch the other Black Network branch for their own reasons, and this time their account helped us."

### Checking What Was Shared

The review covers sensitive information each member actually passed to another Harper contact, including recipient and time. No roll decides whether someone remembers their own reports; uncertain entries remain uncertain and don't become extra disclosures by assumption.

> [!exploration]**Pressing Mirt for Details**
>
> A successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check while asking whether the members themselves are suspected earns Mirt's assurance that this copy didn't come from them, using the response below. Failure leaves the evidence review open without suggesting their guilt.
>
> A successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check while comparing his dates notices that he has checked records extending beyond the last tenday. He began tracing the pattern earlier, even though the aligned incidents gave him his present confidence. Neither check identifies Orren or closes the leak.

If the Persuasion check succeeds, Mirt gives his assurance directly:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt leaves the account on the table and turns toward the person asking, with his hands resting beside the marked dates.
>
> > "I know this copy didn't come from you. That's the one answer I can give you now."

On failure, he keeps the review open:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt draws the first account back beside the empty review sheet, keeping both pages open while he waits for your answer.
>
> > "I'm following the route the material took. Give me the reports you actually sent, and we'll compare them with these dates before I name anyone."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt draws another sheet beside the account and leaves its first column blank while you explain who received each report. He writes the times you can give him, marking the uncertain ones before turning the sheet toward you for correction.
>
> > "Tell me what you sent and who received it. If you don't know what happened after that, say so. I need the route we can prove before I decide which part to watch."
>
> > "Check what I've written here. If I've put something against the wrong recipient or time, correct it before we carry that mistake into the next inquiry."

### The Private Protocol

Operational intelligence now goes face-to-face to Mirt, with paper birds carrying only meeting times and locations. The next appointment is at 09:00 two days later in the rear room of Seven Scales Counting House, 18 Sorn Street, rented separately from Tessalar's workroom. Later appointments are sent to the members individually, with their written locations settled before delivery.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt writes the next appointment on separate slips and passes one to each of you, then puts the information review inside his coat while the sealed bottle remains on the table.
>
> > "Keep the names and plans until we meet. The birds can carry an appointment, but they don't need to carry the work itself. If you've already sent something through another contact, bring me the account of it."
>
> > "Nine in the morning, two days from now. The rear room of Seven Scales Counting House, eighteen Sorn Street. I've rented it separately from Tessalar's workroom; bring the reports to me there."

> [!qna]**When does a registered report leak?**
>
> Mirt points to the register entry and the later observation times before folding the evidence sheet.
>
> > "The observations match instructions in our register, but I haven't proved the delivery routine yet. Bring operational reports directly to me while we trace it; another Harper being nearby is no evidence that your conversation was disclosed."

> [!qna]**Does this stop the leak?**
>
> > "It keeps your next report out of that route. We still need to find the sender and the route, then prevent further disclosures. Anything already shared stays with its recipients; we can't make them forget it."

> [!gamemaster]**The Fixed Leak Rule**
>
> Until **Harper Leak Closed**, every operational entry put into Orren's cell register is copied to Rusk exactly forty-eight hours after its entry. Safehouse, relay-contact and old dead-drop reports pass through that register; a private conversation with Mirt doesn't. No random roll is made, and no report leaks merely because another Harper exists nearby.
>
> Record the copied names, locations and timings as a delivered report when the deadline passes. Rusk passes those entries to Manshoon's cell at the same delivery, supporting specific foreknowledge in **Kolat Towers** without granting unseen abilities, complete plans or knowledge held only by the party. Closing the leak prevents queued copies still in Orren's control, while already-delivered information persists.
>
> Orren's Manshoon channel remains separate from Corene's parasite and Nihiloor's intelligence. This warning doesn't send copied information to Xanathar's Guild.

### Renown Opportunities

This standalone warning grants no Renown, gold or Milestone Points. Mirt's assurance and a safer reporting route are its immediate benefits; **The Sleeping Asset** carries the operational investigation and its mission awards.

### Aftermath

The members can share the new protocol with their helping companions after the private meeting, without treating those companions as Harper recruits. Awareness changes where the members send intelligence, while identifying the clerk and securing his access remain separate work.

### Concluding the Event

The warning concludes when the members have reviewed their disclosures and received the private reporting procedure.
