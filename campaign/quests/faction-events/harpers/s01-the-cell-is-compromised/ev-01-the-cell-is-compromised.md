# The Cell Is Compromised

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs after **A Friend's House** is complete and the party has met Davil Starsong. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive a plain note at Trollskull Manor summoning them to an unregistered room in the North Ward
> - Learn from Mirt that the Waterdeep Harper cell has been compromised: intelligence the party shared with Harper contacts has reached Manshoon's people
> - Press him on the details and hear what he knows without embellishment
> - Receive a new contact protocol: sensitive intelligence goes to Mirt directly, in person, at rotating addresses
>
> #### Background
>
> Mirt has been tracing this for a tenday. Three incidents aligned wrong — the speed of Splinter responses to Harper-held intelligence, a debrief fragment from Davil Starsong's network, and the pattern of what leaked: operational names and addresses, never analysis; sources, never conclusions. Someone inside the cell has been filtering for the most actionable material and passing it out. Mirt does not know who. He does not guess.
>
> Do not identify the double agent in this scene. The leak is a pattern Mirt has inferred from timing and circumstance. The name is the Mission 5 reveal. If the party asks directly who the double agent is, Mirt says he has fourteen people who touched the intelligence that leaked, which is too many to accuse and too many to trust. He has a trap running. The name closes in **The Sleeping Asset**.
>
> #### Which Incident to Use
>
> The specific incident that confirmed Mirt's suspicion depends on which missions the party has completed. Use the most recent applicable incident.
>
> **If Mission 1 (The Talking Mare) is complete:** The party reported the Shesstra Street address to Mirt. He dispatched a Harper watcher the next morning. Within two days the Splinter vacated and burned the building — a response too fast for independent discovery. Davil's people had been running their own coverage on Splinter movement in the Trades Ward; they passed the evacuation timing to the party as street noise after Gralhund Villa, with no idea what it meant. Mirt connected it.
>
> **If Mission 2 (The Dead Drop) is complete:** The party identified Tessalar Maeridge and reported his name and his handler's customs house drop to Mirt. Four days later, Tessalar was found dead in the Dock Ward — a Splinter cleanup, timed before any Watch alert could be filed. The only people who knew Tessalar's name in that context were the party and whoever Mirt briefed inside the cell. Davil's contacts confirmed the kill signature.
>
> **If Mission 4 (A Friend's House) is complete:** The party reported salon intelligence to Mirt, including Jelenn Urmbrusk's name in connection with Splinter pressure against Masked Lords. Mirt routed a quiet background query through a cell member to a second operative. The query never reached the operative. Jelenn was approached shortly after through a separate channel and had already been coached — coached precisely on the points in Mirt's query. Nobody outside the cell should have had that text.
>
> In all cases, Davil Starsong mentioned the corroborating detail as street intelligence — a casual observation during or after his own people's debrief with the party. He did not know it was a gap. Mirt made the connection himself.

### No Wine

A plain note arrives at Trollskull Manor, hand-delivered by a street urchin, with nothing magical about it. A North Ward address, a time, and four words in Mirt's cramped handwriting: *Come alone. Be careful.*

> [!readaloud]
> The note is plain parchment with nothing magical about it, and the street kid who brought it has turned the corner by the time you reach the door. Mirt's cramped hand gives a North Ward address and a time, and four words at the bottom.
>
> > *Come alone. Be careful.*

> [!readaloud]
> The room is on the third floor of a North Ward tenement, low-ceilinged and smelling of old plaster and someone else's supper seeping up through the floorboards. Mirt is seated at a plain table when you arrive, a sealed bottle of wine and a folded sheet of parchment covered in notes in front of him. He does not offer the wine, which is the first unusual thing about the meeting.
>
> > "Sit down. We have a problem."

> [!social]**The Old Wolf Without His Act**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan human, he/him) :: a moneylender and Harper operative; today, not performing anything.
>
> The usual architecture of Mirt's manner — the appetite, the rolling laugh, the way a joke does his heavier lifting — is somewhere beneath the surface tonight, and he is not reaching for it. His hands rest flat on the table. He waits for the door to close before he speaks.
>
> He tells the party plainly: someone inside the Waterdeep Harper cell is reporting operational intelligence to Manshoon's people. He knows because the Splinter acted on information that could only have moved through Harper channels, on a timeline that rules out independent discovery. He names the incident from the party's mission history and lets the arithmetic speak for itself.
>
> He does not have a name. He does not guess. He has fourteen people who touched what leaked, which is too many to accuse and too many to trust, and he has a trap running. When it closes, the party will know.
>
> A DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check draws one additional sentence: "I know it's not you. That's the only name I can give you." He means it as an operational conclusion, not as reassurance.
>
> A DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that Mirt's account of the three incidents is unusually precise for someone who speaks obliquely by habit. He has been working this for longer than a tenday.
>
> Conversation topics Mirt is willing to discuss include:
> - What leaked, how it could only have moved through Harper channels, and how he connected it
> - How Davil Starsong's information fit the picture
> - What he is running to narrow the list down
> - The new contact protocol and why it works
>
> Specific dialogue for Mirt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Who is the double agent?**
>
> > "I have fourteen people who touched what leaked. That's too many to accuse and too many to trust. I'm running something to narrow it down. When I have a name, you'll have a name."

> [!qna]**How does Davil Starsong connect to this?**
>
> > "Starsong mentioned something that fit a gap. He didn't know it was a gap."

> [!qna]**What exactly did Davil say?**
>
> > "The Doom Raiders watch the Splinter for their own reasons. Starsong shares when it benefits him. Right now it happens to benefit us."

> [!qna]**What are you running to find the leak?**
>
> He lifts the glass by the neck and sets it back down without opening it.
>
> > "Something that requires the leak to behave like a leak. When it closes, you'll hear about it."

### The New Protocol

Mirt slides the parchment across the table. Three items, written in the same cramped hand.

> [!readaloud]
> The folded parchment has three items written in Mirt's hand. He does not read them aloud.
>
> One: no operational intelligence (names, addresses, contact identifiers) through any Harper contact except Mirt himself, in person.
>
> Two: meeting times and locations by paper bird, as before. Everything else waits for the face-to-face.
>
> Three: if the party has briefed another Harper contact on sensitive material since their last meeting with Mirt, tell him what was said and to whom, and give him the complete picture.
>
> > "You're still working. You're just careful about who you hand things to."
>
> He folds the parchment, pockets it, and leaves the wine on the table.
>
> > "Find what you can. Keep it until we meet. I'll be in touch."

> [!gamemaster]**Operating Under Compromise**
>
> From this event until **The Sleeping Asset** exposes the double agent, treat all Harper cell contacts other than Mirt as potentially compromised. If the party passes sensitive intelligence through a Harper safe house, a cell contact, or a dead drop, that intelligence has a one-in-four chance of reaching Kolat Towers within forty-eight hours.
>
> Roll a d4 out of sight when the party shares information through any non-Mirt Harper channel; on a 1, Manshoon's people receive it within two days. Track what was shared — it becomes foreknowledge in **Kolat Towers**.
>
> If the party respected the new protocol throughout: note it when **The Sleeping Asset** opens. Mirt tells them the trap worked in part because certain channels went quiet.
>
> If the party passed sensitive material through a Harper contact anyway: that detail can surface in Corene Wyldath's post-mission debrief as intelligence Nihiloor already held — on record as a natural consequence, without an additional penalty.

### Concluding the Event

Mirt pockets the parchment. The wine bottle stays on the table, sealed. He leaves when the party does.

## Overview

Mirt calls the party to an unregistered room in the North Ward. The wine on the table goes untouched. The Waterdeep Harper cell has been compromised, and he still doesn't know who.

## Summary

Mirt summoned the party to a rented room in the North Ward and told them the Waterdeep Harper cell was compromised. Intelligence the party had reported to Harper contacts had reached Manshoon's people — confirmed through a detail Davil Starsong passed along without knowing its significance. Mirt did not have a name. He established a new protocol: sensitive intelligence goes to him directly, in person, until further notice. The wine stayed sealed.
