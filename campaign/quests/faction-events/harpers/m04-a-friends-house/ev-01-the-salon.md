# The Salon

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins with the invitation and ends when the party chooses whether to approach or follow Erystian. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Get suitable clothes through the paid fitting, or recover from a missed appointment.
> - Meet Remallia as a Harper contact for the first time.
> - Compare independent clues about Jarlaxle's cover while talking to the other guests.
> - Follow the impostor, speak with him at the salon or leave their assessment unresolved.

### The Brief

At noon three days before the salon, Remallia sends a named invitation to the Harper members and their assisting companions. Mirt's separate note goes to the members alone and asks them to identify someone wearing a false face without disturbing the hostess's dinner.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The invitation comes on heavy cream paper stamped with the Haventree crest, with each of your names written beneath the date and a line asking for formal dress. Folded inside is a second sheet in Mirt's blocky hand, giving the address of a tailor who has been told to expect you.
>
> > "Remallia wants you at her salon. Dress well, and listen to what people say. Somebody there is wearing a false face. Find out who, and don't make her sorry she invited us."

> [!exploration]**The Fitting and Recovery Appointment**
>
> Mirt has paid for hired formal clothes at Seldo's Fine Stitches, 8 Delzorin Street, with a fitting at 14:00 the following day. Seldo Wynd, the proprietor, finishes the alterations by 16:00 on the salon day and lends the clothes until noon the day after. No fee is due and no check is needed.
>
> A party that misses the fitting gets a recovery appointment at 17:00 on the salon day, which takes forty-five minutes and uses ready-made clothes with simple alterations. Guests who reach the villa without formal wear are still admitted, because the steward lends suitable coats and wraps and Remallia introduces everyone herself. A mistake with clothing never costs the party the mission.

At either fitting appointment, Seldo receives the invited guests:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Seldo Wynd takes Mirt's note at the counter and wheels a rack of formal clothes over beside the tall mirror, while an assistant lays pins and chalk out on the fitting table.
>
> > "Mirt's paid for the hire and the alterations, so we'll start with whatever fits best and adjust from there. You can keep everything until noon the day after the salon. Just bring it all back then, along with anything you've borrowed from the rack."

At the recovery appointment, he adds:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "We only have forty-five minutes for this, I'm afraid. I can do the simple alterations you need, if you're happy to choose from what's ready on the rack."

If the guests arrive without formal wear, the steward opens the cloakroom before admitting them:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The steward checks the invitation, then opens a cupboard of coats and wraps and pulls the nearest ones aside so you can see the sizes.
>
> > "Your names are on the invitation, so please come in. We can lend you a coat or a wrap before Lady Haventree makes the introductions, and you're welcome to leave it here when the evening ends."

### House Ulbrinter

The three-story Sea Ward villa opens its doors at 19:00. Twenty-four guests gather in the salon and the garden courtyard, dinner is served at 20:00 and the evening ends at 22:00. Remallia greets each invited character by name and gives them ordinary social access, without introducing them publicly as agents.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Driftglobes light the villa's facade above the low garden wall, and a steward checks your names against the invitation before leading you toward the reception room. Remallia leaves her place by the window to meet you, with a tray of drinks waiting beside the door.
>
> > "You must be the friends Mirt mentioned! Did Seldo have time to finish everything? Come in and let me take your coats. There's time for a drink before we sit down, and I'd love to hear how you've been settling into the city."

> [!social]**The Hostess Who Remembers**
>
> Remallia Haventree (Chaotic Good, Sun Elf, she/her) :: a gracious hostess who welcomes new operatives through ordinary conversation.
>
> Remallia pours the tea herself and gives each guest room to answer before she turns to the next. In public she talks about their arrival in the city and her long friendship with Mirt. At 19:20 she brings the Harper members to her study for the private briefing below, where she first tells them of her role in the Harpers. Companions stay in the salon as guests during that briefing.

> [!qna]**How do you know Mirt?**
>
> Remallia passes a cup across the table and sets the pot back on its tray.
>
> > "Mirt and I have worked together for years, though you'd have to ask him which of us has been more patient about it. Has he been looking after you properly since you arrived?"

> [!qna]**Do you know why Mirt invited us?**
>
> In the private study, Remallia closes the door before she answers.
>
> > "He asked whether I'd like to meet some of our newer members, and I was delighted to say yes. You're welcome to come to me directly after tonight, my dears, though I'd rather we kept our business out of the reception room."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt stands beside the wine tray in the study with a glass in his hand while Remallia shuts the door and takes a chair. The noise of the salon carries faintly through the wall as he pulls the guest list toward him.
>
> > "Sit. Remi runs the Harper network here. She knows what I've asked of you, and if you need a private room to talk, she'll give you one. Which guests have you met? Tell me what you saw before you name anyone."

> [!gamemaster]**Recovering a Social Mistake**
>
> A successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check smooths over an awkward introduction when a character tries to repair it. On a failure, Remallia reaches them within thirty seconds and steers the talk to another topic, so the evening stays open to them. An accusation against a named guest goes to the mistaken-identification branch in **The Confrontation**.

On a failed repair, Remallia joins the conversation:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia crosses the room with a fresh cup and turns toward Saeth, giving the conversation space to drift away from the awkward moment.
>
> > "Saeth, would you tell our guests what you've been looking into lately? I think they're finding their own way around the city, and you may have a few streets in common."

### Erystian Demarne

Jarlaxle approaches the new guests at 19:40 in the guise of a sandy-haired Illuskan man, dressed as the Luskan poet Erystian Demarne. His companion is introduced as a fellow traveler named Lethan. Lethan is a disguised Bregan D'aerthe guard, and he watches Jarlaxle's surroundings more closely than any traveling friend would.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia brings a sandy-haired young man across the room toward you and stops beside the garden door, where his companion moves a chair out of their way.
>
> > "May I introduce Erystian Demarne? He's been kind enough to bring us some of Luskan's poetry. Erystian, these are the guests Mirt hoped you would meet tonight."
>
> Erystian inclines his head to you while his companion takes a place beside the door.
>
> > "What brought you to Waterdeep? I hope it's left you a little time to enjoy the place."
>
> He waits for your answer with a pleasant smile, then goes on.
>
> > "Luskan has kept me busy, but Remallia's invitations are hard to refuse. Have you found time for the theaters here, or has the city given you too much other work already?"

> [!social]**The Poet's Performance**
>
> Erystian talks about real Luskan performances and asks about the party's work, answering personal questions with a detailed cover history. He turns one pointed question toward another guest, but the party can keep questioning him afterward. He stays friendly when someone notices a discrepancy, and he agrees to speak privately if asked.

> [!qna]**What brings you here?**
>
> Erystian turns his cup by the stem while he looks toward the musicians at the far end of the room.
>
> > "I'm a poet from Luskan, and I've been coming to Remallia's evenings for three months now. Somebody here has always seen a different corner of the city, and that gives me far more to think about than sitting alone over a page."

> [!qna]**Who is your companion?**
>
> > "Lethan? He's a fellow traveler, and we found it easier to make the journey together. He's wonderful at noticing when a chair has been left in someone's way."

If a pointed question first puts the cover under strain, Erystian redirects it once:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Erystian turns toward Serithka at the garden door and draws out the chair Lethan has moved for her.
>
> > "Serithka, may I borrow you for a moment? Our new friends were asking what I've found worth hearing here, and I'd much rather they heard about your music from you."

> [!exploration]**Comparing the Cover**
>
> Three observations are available without a check: his unusually costly clothes, Lethan's practiced guard behavior and a formal Luskan accent that sounds learned from a tutor. If the party combines any two and puts the discrepancy to Erystian, he admits in private that he has another identity, and he gives his name if asked directly. Identification needs neither a spell nor one high roll.
>
> Any character who watches him talk and makes a successful DC 20 Wisdom (Insight) check recognizes the controlled performance behind the accent and earns the same private acknowledgment. Any character who inspects his boots and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (History or Arcana) check identifies the drow-made sole pattern, which is another line of evidence. Costly clothes alone prove nothing about a name. The observations work by giving the party something to put to him in conversation.
>
> A character who has met Jarlaxle before recognizes his manner without a check once they speak with him. A BD member who hasn't met him has Advantage on the Insight check and needs only DC 10 for the boots, thanks to faction briefings.

### A BD Member's Private Choice

If a BD member attends, Jarlaxle asks them into the garden at 19:45. He keeps his disguise but drops the Luskan accent. He knows they arrived with the Harper guests and doesn't assume they have joined that faction.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Erystian waits beside the garden door until the nearby conversation has moved on toward the dining room, then holds the door open with a hand against the frame.
>
> > "Would you join me outside for a moment? There's a small matter I'd rather settle before we sit down to dinner."
>
> Out on the garden path he adjusts his cuff and speaks again, wearing the same face but without the Luskan accent.
>
> > "I'd like to know what Mirt is putting into the field, you see. You may tell him exactly who I am, or you may decline to confirm it tonight, whichever you prefer. If you choose discretion, I can give you something useful to do for one of his people."

> [!gamemaster]**Mara's Recovery**
>
> Jarlaxle offers the name and description of Mara Coppersail, a Human ticket clerk at the Sea Maidens Faire who wears a yellow scarf and is a Harper informant. He knows her cover is broken, and she doesn't. She finishes at the public ticket booth at 18:00 the day after the salon, and she takes the message "Mirt has a safer counting desk" as an instruction to leave with the messengers. Escorting her to Mirt's residence takes no check and starts no fight, and she brings the next day's crew shift schedule as intelligence.
>
> Mirt's own warning reaches Mara at noon two days after the salon. If the party acts before then, the BD member earns the BD bonus. After noon she has left on her own, and Mirt holds the same information. Neither discretion nor exposure costs BD standing automatically.
>
> A successful DC 16 Charisma (Deception) check brokers the three-way arrangement. The party tells Mirt that Erystian is a BD observer and passes on the warning as an anonymous tip, keeping Jarlaxle's name back. On a success the party earns the Harper base and profile awards, and the BD member who acts earns the recovery bonus. The correct-identification bonus stays unearned, since Mirt never hears the name. On a failure, both contacts ask who supplied the warning, and the party can answer honestly and still go after the recovery.

When Jarlaxle offers the recovery information, use the following words:

> [!readaloud]
>
> He glances through the garden doorway and turns back to you, still wearing the guest's face.
>
> > "Her name is Mara Coppersail. She works the public ticket booth at the Faire and wears a yellow scarf. Somebody has identified her Harper cover, and nobody has warned her yet. If you meet her when she finishes at six tomorrow and tell her 'Mirt has a safer counting desk,' she'll go with you, and you can take her straight to his residence. She has the next day's crew schedule, if you'd like something useful to bring him."

If the attempted three-way arrangement fails, each contact asks directly when receiving the warning:

> [!qna]**Mirt's question?**
>
> > "Who gave you that warning? If Mara's in danger, I'll act on it. I want to know whose word I'm taking before I write it down."

> [!qna]**Jarlaxle's question?**
>
> > "And what did you tell Mirt about the person who sent it? I'd like to know which part of our arrangement you mean to keep."

### The Other Guests

You can run these conversations before dinner and again between 20:45 and 22:00. A courteous, direct question about someone's stated concern gets the information without a check, unless noted otherwise.

> [!social]**Saeth Cromley, Retired Sergeant**
>
> Saeth (Lawful Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) loosens his collar and drifts toward anyone who mentions the warehouse cases. Dalen was Corene Wyldath's contact, but Saeth doesn't know that. The file appointment below needs no check.

> [!qna]**The warehouse break-ins?**
>
> Saeth loosens his collar and scratches his chin while he looks toward the garden's open doorway.
>
> > "Somebody's been doing professional work in those Dock Ward warehouses, friend. They walk off with exactly what they came for, and the boys are left sorting out the shit afterward. If you've found anything on the level, I'd like to hear it."

> [!qna]**The missing officer?**
>
> > "One more thing, sunshine. Dalen Voss vanished two months ago, and nobody's found a body. If his name's come up while you've been looking into those warehouses, I want to know about it."
>
> > "Come to the North Ward Watch post at nine tomorrow morning. I'll pull his file, and we'll see whether your account matches anything the boys wrote down."

> [!social]**Tessabrant Elamondra, Shipping Merchant**
>
> Tessabrant (Half-Elf, she/her) keeps a pocket account book close and hopes for discreet help with her lost shipments. She knows the goods and the buyers, but she has no idea what the dye purchase below is for.

> [!qna]**What has gone missing?**
>
> Tessabrant opens her pocket account book and holds the three marked entries out to you.
>
> > "The goods do arrive, but by the time the cart reaches the door the buyer has changed. I've paid to have the paperwork checked twice, and both copies looked perfectly sound. Somebody is making money on the difference."
>
> > "That's three shipments in four months. I'd be grateful for discreet help checking the buyers, if it's work you can take on without alarming the people who still pay me."

> [!qna]**Any unusual buyers?**
>
> She turns to a purchase total near the back of the book and keeps a finger under the buyer's name.
>
> > "The Cassalanters bought an enormous amount of imported dye. The total's here, if you'd like to compare it with their other purchases. I know what they bought, but I couldn't tell you what they mean to color with it."

> [!social]**Lord Aldric Talmost, Club Gossip**
>
> Aldric repeats fragments about the vault because they win him attention, including a Cassalanter cousin's claim of a significant windfall "not from trade." Any character who flatters him and makes a successful DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion) check gets him to name the family and repeat the wording. On a failure he promises to check his recollection and sends the same detail by note at noon the next day. He doesn't understand what the claim means.

> [!qna]**The windfall?**
>
> On a success, Aldric leans closer and sets his cup down beside his chair.
>
> > "It was a Cassalanter cousin who told me there'd be a substantial windfall, and he was quite definite that it was 'not from trade.'"
>
> > "I did hope someone here could tell me what he meant, though he seemed rather pleased that I couldn't."

On a failed check, Aldric postpones the answer and doesn't name the family tonight:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Aldric lifts his cup again and looks across the room, turning the handle between his fingers before he looks back at you.
>
> > "Let me be sure I have the name and the wording right before I repeat it. I'll send you a note tomorrow, once I've remembered which conversation it was."

> [!social]**Zalara Moonwhisper, Archivist**
>
> Zalara (Elf, she/her) works at the Font of Knowledge and carries a transcription of an unresolved administrative entry. It mentions a ceremonial lock but says nothing about who lives there now, what's deposited or which keys are needed today. The appointment she offers needs no check.

> [!qna]**What did the archive record say?**
>
> Zalara takes a folded transcription from her sleeve and opens it on the dry corner of the table.
>
> > "The entry is from 1244 DR, and it names the Vault of Dragons as 'extraordinary reserve, Lords' disposition.' There's a ceremonial lock in the description, but I haven't found the discharge entry where I'd expect it."
>
> > "So as far as I can tell, it's a reserve that was never formally discharged, unless the release was filed somewhere else. If the name means anything to you, I can pull the full document tomorrow and you can judge it for yourselves."
>
> > "Come to the Font of Knowledge archive at ten tomorrow morning. It may only be a filing error, and I'd rather you read the whole entry before making anything of it."

> [!social]**Farrak Iltimer, Gemcutter**
>
> Farrak (Dwarf, he/him) likes swapping stories about difficult commissions with anyone who knows a trade. His guess about a ceremonial display comes only from how heavy the rubies are. He knows nothing of any rite, pact or infernal patron.

> [!qna]**The unusual commission?**
>
> Farrak spreads his fingers on the table, measuring the setting he would need for a stone of that weight.
>
> > "The Cassalanters ordered blood-red rubies last month, and they specified an unusual cut for every one. They're heavier than anything I'd use in my usual jewelry, so I wondered whether somebody was fitting them into a ceremonial display."
>
> > "Twelve stones, all the same weight, and they checked each one before they paid. I don't know where they're putting them, but I'd love to see the fitting when somebody works it out."

> [!social]**Serithka Ondal, Composer**
>
> Serithka (Tiefling, she/her), whose Infernal Fiendish Legacy traces to Zariel, stays near the garden door where the room is quieter. Friendly conversation about her music opens the answer below, which supports the idea that Erystian has traveled widely and doesn't connect the Cassalanters to infernalism.

> [!qna]**What did Erystian say?**
>
> Serithka turns her cup toward the garden and glances across the room at Erystian.
>
> > "He knew about my legacy before I'd said a word about it, and then he changed the subject so neatly that I almost thought I'd imagined the question. I'm used to people asking about the horns, but that was different."

> [!social]**Jelenn Urmbrusk, Guarded Noblewoman**
>
> Jelenn (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) is privately a Masked Lord who owes a debt to Manshoon's people, and her sixteen guards wait outside instead of crowding the salon. Any character who studies her and makes a successful DC 18 Wisdom (Insight) check sees the strain behind her dismissive composure, though it doesn't reveal her office or her creditor. If anyone mentions the Zhentarim, she excuses herself within three minutes. She knows two other compromised Lords and names neither tonight. Any character who later sees her name on the Kolat Towers blackmail list and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence check remembers her from the salon.

> [!qna]**The Zhentarim?**
>
> Jelenn glances at the people within earshot, sets her cup on the nearest tray and turns toward the door before she answers.
>
> > "I think not. I came for Remallia's hospitality, and I won't discuss other people's affairs over her dinner table. Who sent you? No, never mind. Excuse me."

### Choosing How to Approach

Jarlaxle leaves at 21:30. If the party asks for a private conversation before then, he finishes it and departs afterward. Remallia keeps the salon open until 22:00, so anyone who stays can keep talking to the other guests.

### Renown Opportunities

Nothing is awarded here. **The Confrontation** records the mission's base Renown and bonuses, including for members who let Jarlaxle leave and report what they couldn't settle.

### Aftermath

The guest leads stay available whatever the party decides about Erystian. A conversation the party misses doesn't lock them out of the later quest it concerns, because that quest supplies its own clues.

### Concluding the Event

The party chooses whether to speak with Jarlaxle privately, follow him or let him go when he reaches the villa door.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Remallia Harper Contact Known** — mark after the private briefing in the study. From then on, later Harper missions allow direct contact with Remallia, while earlier rank support still goes through Mirt.
> - **Salon Guest Leads Recorded** — mark with the specific conversations the party actually heard. The later quests that read it use only those details, and none of them exposes the nobles' secret.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> If the party follows Jarlaxle or Lethan, continue with **The Tail**. If they speak with Jarlaxle privately at the villa, go straight to **The Confrontation**. If they let him leave or name another guest, run the matching report and calling-card branches in that event. This mission awards no Milestone Points.
