# Design Notes: A Friend's House

## A Salon with Practical Access

The invitation arrives three days before the event, which leaves time for a fitting and a fixed recovery appointment, so the party never has to find formal clothes at the last minute. The steward's borrowed coats mean a missed appointment can't close the mission. The 200 GP source reward is shared by everyone who attended, and it stays separate from each Harper's individual Renown.

The three events each ask for a different decision: whom to talk to, which route to follow and what to disclose once Jarlaxle is named. Guests whose information needs documents give the party a fixed follow-up appointment. That lets the evening open several investigations without asking the party to exhaust every conversation in one sitting.

## Remallia and the Guests

Remallia's place in the Harpers is disclosed privately here for the first time, while the public introductions protect everyone's covers and membership boundaries. Earlier rank support still goes through Mirt, and companions can attend the salon without hearing the private Harper briefing.

This draft keeps House Ulbrinter in the Sea Ward, as the restored mission has it. Appendix C, The Spy at Haventree's Party, Scene 1 puts the villa on Delzorin Street in the North Ward, between Vhezoar and Brondar's Way. That discrepancy is recorded here for later reconciliation, and no external campaign page has been edited.

The Cassalanter purchases are unusual commercial evidence and nothing more, because no guest knows of a pact, a ritual or an infernal patron. Farrak's guesses are about a ceremonial display, not a rite he has identified. Zalara's old administrative entry gives the party a reference to the vault, and it doesn't show that the present hoard has stayed there since 1244 DR.

## Identifying and Following Jarlaxle

The independent cover observations give the party something to put to Erystian, and Jarlaxle's own acknowledgment supplies the name that clothing alone couldn't prove. Recognizing him beneath Erystian doesn't expose his other disguises, including Captain Zardoz Zord. A party that reports uncertain evidence still gets a useful profile result, and it isn't credited with a discovery it never made.

The pursuit has a fixed courtyard, signal address and timing, and following either Jarlaxle or Lethan brings the party back to the conversation. The chalk code is a nonmagical signal, and the vendor takes a prepaid voucher in place of a circulating paper currency the setting doesn't have. If Jarlaxle leaves and the party declares that they follow, he stops and waits for them, so the scene needs no escape ruling.

## Discretion and Shared Rewards

The BD request belongs to a BD member, and its recovery bonus depends on getting help to Mara in time, so silence alone doesn't earn it. A negotiated anonymous warning can still give the party a Harper profile result without a correct-name bonus, since the name was withheld. No branch gives anyone a second faction membership.

Mara comes with a name, a description, a public booth, a recovery message and a deadline, so the offered intelligence leads straight to a rescue the party can carry out. Remallia's courier brings the 200 GP in separate sealed purses the following morning, and the paper bird carries only the note. The outcomes name every intended reader, and their integration into the wider campaign happens outside this folder.
