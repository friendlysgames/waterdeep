# The Confrontation

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins in the villa's private study or Shield Court, and ends with the members' report and subsequent contact. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Name Jarlaxle and choose how much to disclose to Mirt.
> - Ask about his interest in the Harper network and the compromised Faire contact.
> - Negotiate discretion without automatic faction penalties or new membership.
> - Resolve an incorrect accusation or an evening that ended without identification.

### Background

Jarlaxle has watched Mirt's contacts at three months of salons, using Erystian's identity to listen without showing his faction affiliation. He accepts the risk of recognition because he wants to assess these new agents and leave open another channel of negotiation. His acknowledgment establishes Jarlaxle beneath Erystian's guise, but it doesn't prove that Captain Zardoz Zord is the same person.

### The Private Conversation

At the villa, Remallia makes her study available when asked and keeps the other guests in the salon. In Shield Court, Jarlaxle waits beside the closed tailor until the party approaches, with Lethan arriving two minutes later if he checked the signal site.

If the party identifies him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Erystian listens while you set out the observations that brought you to his name, adjusting his cuff as you finish the account. He gives a small bow and speaks without the Luskan accent, keeping the same face as he turns back toward you.
>
> > "Jarlaxle Baenre, yes. I wondered how soon we would get to that part. Would you like to tell Mirt first, or hear why I've been accepting Remallia's invitations?"

> [!social]**The Observer Who Will Negotiate**
>
> Jarlaxle (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: a courteous adversary who asks what the party intends to do with its findings.
>
> Jarlaxle acknowledges BD membership and his attendance at Remallia's events, discussing the Harpers' position in the search for the vault. He doesn't disclose ship interiors, guarded routes or his full disguise network. He hears a specific proposal and either accepts its stated terms or declines plainly, without requiring a fight or a payment for silence.

> [!qna]**What were you watching?**
>
> > "Mirt's meetings, mostly, and the people he trusts to do things outside this house. Everyone is asking after the Stone, and I would rather understand the people asking than guess from their faction badges."

> [!qna]**What can you give us?**
>
> A successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check persuades Jarlaxle to provide Mara Coppersail's name, yellow-scarf description and recovery message from **The Salon**. Failure makes him ask for a specific mutual benefit, accepting the party's offer to pass Mirt a request for future talks as sufficient. A BD member who already received the information needs no second check.
>
> On success, or after the party offers the future-talks request, he gives the details:
>
> > "There's a ticket clerk at the Faire who thinks her Harper reports are still private. Her name is Mara Coppersail, and she wears a yellow scarf at the public booth. Meet her when she finishes at six tomorrow, tell her 'Mirt has a safer counting desk,' and escort her to his residence. She can bring the next day's crew schedule."

If the check fails and the party hasn't yet offered the future-talks request, Jarlaxle asks for that benefit:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle adjusts his cuff and waits beside the doorway, leaving room for you to propose the exchange before he continues.
>
> > "Would you take Mirt a request for another conversation? That would be useful to me, and enough to make this an exchange rather than something you expect me to supply without a reason."

> [!qna]**Will you pay for discretion?**
>
> > "I'd rather hear what you'd use it for. If you want another conversation, we can arrange one. If you want to report my name, you already have it, and I won't pretend I can buy back what you've noticed."

### Disclosure and Departure

The party can report the name, supply the anonymous BD-observer warning under the salon's negotiated arrangement or withhold confirmation. Each individual decides what they will say; a helping BD character doesn't acquire Harper membership by assisting this mission.

> [!gamemaster]**Leaving without a Forced Escape**
>
> Jarlaxle speaks for up to twenty minutes unless the party ends the conversation sooner, then thanks the hostess if still at the villa and walks away with Lethan. His departure doesn't automatically defeat a declared attempt to follow; he asks for privacy and waits for the party's answer. Following farther offers no extra mission award or forced combat, and the present conversation remains sufficient to establish the recorded identity.

If the party follows after this conversation, Jarlaxle stops beside Lethan:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle turns back toward you before rounding the corner, with Lethan waiting a few paces farther along the street.
>
> > "I'd like some privacy for the rest of my evening. Have you another question we should settle here, or can we leave the next conversation for a time we've agreed?"

If the party names another guest, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia comes beside you and places her hand against the open study door, while the accused guest remains with the dinner company.
>
> > "Would you join me in the study for a moment? I'd like to hear what led to that claim before we discuss it with anyone else."
>
> > "I'd like us to check the particulars before anyone is asked to defend themselves in front of the room. Tell me what you saw, and we can decide what question would clarify it."

> [!gamemaster]**Correcting the Accusation**
>
> The wrongly accused guest declines further conversation that evening, but an apology restores ordinary access on the following day. Jarlaxle stays until 21:30, leaving the remaining evidence paths available. If the party lets him leave without identification, the members can still report their uncertainty and the guest information they gathered.

### Mirt's Assessment

Mirt hears the Harper members privately before departure when possible, or at his residence at 09:00 the following morning. For a street confrontation, a paper bird sent immediately afterward meets the identification-bonus timing, with the detailed report delivered the next morning.

If Jarlaxle's name is reported, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt lays the guest list beside your observations and keeps his pen above the space beneath Erystian's name, with his glass set back from the papers.
>
> > "How long did he stay, and which guests did he speak with? Did he volunteer anything about another contact we should warn?"
>
> He writes your answers beside the names before setting the list down.
>
> > "He's been coming for three months. I'll check who spoke with him and what we put within his hearing. You've given us a name we can work with, but don't assume it's the only one he uses."

If no identity is confirmed, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt separates the guest leads from the observations about Erystian, keeping both sheets beside his glass and leaving the name entry blank at the top of the second.
>
> > "Which conversations did you pursue, and what did you learn from them? Send me anything further that would let us check the person using this alias."
>
> > "I'll keep the account as you gave it. If you haven't confirmed the name, there's no use writing one down as though you had. The other conversations may still give us work worth doing."

### Renown Opportunities

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Rewards**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains 3 base Renown for attending and reporting a considered assessment. Add +1 for correctly identifying Jarlaxle and reporting before he leaves the estate, or immediately after the street confrontation. Add +1 for reporting his alias and at least two concrete cover observations, regardless of whether the specific name was confirmed. These bonuses apply once to eligible members, with no Renown for unaffiliated helpers.
>
> An actual BD member gains the separate +1 recovery bonus only through the discretion or successful three-way arrangement and timely action at Mara's booth, as stated in **The Salon**. Reporting Jarlaxle honestly doesn't automatically lose BD standing, and no character gains membership in a second faction through this mission.
>
> At 09:00 the next morning, Remallia's courier delivers 200 GP to each character who attended, in separate sealed purses accompanied by a paper-bird note thanking them. This shared source reward survives an unresolved or mistaken identification; a note doesn't physically carry the coins.

When the reward arrives, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia's courier places the sealed purses beside the unfolded paper bird, checking the attendee names against a list before sliding each purse toward its recipient.
>
> > "Two hundred gold for each person who attended, with Lady Haventree's thanks for the evening. Her note belongs with it, and she asked me to make sure everyone received their own purse."
>
> The note reads:
>
> > "Thank you for joining us. I hope there will be time for another conversation when you have considered what you heard, and you are welcome to send word when you would like to call."

If the party acts on Mara's warning before the independent recovery, use this response when they give the agreed message at the ticket booth:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mara Coppersail closes the ticket book and pulls her yellow scarf away from the stool, folding the next day's crew schedule inside it before stepping around the booth.
>
> > "A safer counting desk, then. All right, I'll come with you to Mirt's residence. I've brought tomorrow's crew schedule, and I'd rather hear what has happened once we're off the pier."

### Aftermath

Forty-eight hours after the salon ends, a courier delivers Jarlaxle's embossed calling card to the party's lodging. A harp and crescent moon are crossed by a rapier; correct identification brings the existing mechanical-footman message, while other branches receive the invitation below.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The courier holds the envelope where you can read the names written on its outside, keeping the delivery sheet against the sealed flap.
>
> > "Are these the people I was sent to find? If the names are right, the envelope is yours and I can mark it delivered."
>
> Inside lies an embossed card with a rapier crossing a harp and crescent moon, and a short message written beneath it.
>
If Jarlaxle was identified, the message reads:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I hear you've been asking about a certain mechanical footman."

Otherwise, the message reads:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I'd be pleased to speak again when you've had time to consider the evening."

The mechanical-footman reference remains Jarlaxle's invitation to further inquiry, without granting the party a discovery they haven't made. Remallia can now receive the Harper members directly, while guest leads retain the exact information actually heard.

### Concluding the Event

The report concludes the mission, with Mara's recovery and the calling card following their stated schedules.

The party speaks with the observer beneath Erystian's identity, then chooses how to report the conversation and whether to help his compromised contact.

We resolved our assessment of Erystian and told Mirt the details we were willing to confirm. Remallia remained available afterward, and Jarlaxle's calling card left us another way to pursue what we had learned.
