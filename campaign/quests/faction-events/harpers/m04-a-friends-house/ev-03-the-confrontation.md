# The Confrontation

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when the party takes Erystian Demarne aside, at House Ulbrinter, on the canal bridge or under the lamp on Swords Street, and it ends with the calling card two days later. A party that never confronts him plays only the morning debrief. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Put its evidence to Erystian and hear him admit who he is.
> - Accuse the wrong guest and be steered away by Remallia.
> - Draw steel and watch Jarlaxle walk out.
> - Report to Mirt and Remallia at 09:00 and learn what Remallia is.
> - Collect 200 gp each and the calling card.
>
> Only Harper members attend the debrief. Companions can join the confrontation and collect their 200 gp at the door.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jarlaxle accepts being caught when the party earns it. He wants Mirt to know he is willing to talk, and he wants to see how good these Harpers are.
> - He never volunteers "Bregan D'aerthe", and he says it only to a character who was marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** before tonight. Anyone else hears "my people".
> - A character marked through a ledger never met Zord and is not recognized, while a character who met Zord face to face is greeted by him.
> - Lethan guards his exit, because Jarlaxle is never a fight.
> - Remallia has known from the start who the guest was, and Mirt asked her to wait until the morning to say what she is.

### Taking Erystian Aside

Remallia opens her study on request and keeps the other guests in the salon, while on the bridge and on Swords Street there is no one else about. In all three places Erystian agrees at once.

If the party has two lines of evidence, or a marked character has one, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Erystian listens while you lay out what you have seen, and when you finish he straightens his cuffs and looks at each of you in turn. He gives a small bow, and when he speaks again the Luskan accent has gone, though the face is still the young actor's.
>
> > "The hands, the answers or the hat? It hardly matters, because I should have worn gloves in any case."
>
> He puts his hand to his chest and smiles with obvious pleasure.
>
> > "Jarlaxle Baenre, since you've earned it, and I find that quite delightful. Most people spend the whole evening watching my eyes and never think to look at my hands."

If a character who met Zord face to face is present, add:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Erystian's gaze settles on one of you, and his smile widens as he tilts his head.
>
> > "And you were aboard the Eyecatcher, because I thought I knew the way you stand. Captain Zord thought well of you, and so do I."

If the party has fewer lines of evidence, Erystian parries and stays until 21:30:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Erystian turns his cup in his hand and laughs softly while he considers you.
>
> > "A poet's hands are a poet's business, and I do hope you've enjoyed the evening. If you find something that suits your suspicion better than my cuffs, bring it to me and we'll talk again."

> [!social]**The Man Under the Hat**
>
> Jarlaxle Baenre (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: a courteous adversary who asks what the party means to do with what it has learned.
>
> Jarlaxle is amused and relaxed, and he accepts the risk of being caught because he wants to size up these Harpers. He admits his name at once, answers questions with questions, and tests every offer for use. He never loses his temper, threatens anyone or demands payment for his silence, and he says nothing about ship interiors, guarded routes or the extent of his disguises.
>
> Jarlaxle is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Why he has watched Remallia's table for three months.
> - Why he let the party catch him.
> - The ticket clerk at the Faire.
> - His request for discretion.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone, the vault or the location of his people, and he compliments Mirt and every Harper name that comes up.

> [!qna]**What were you watching?**
>
> > "Mirt's table, mostly, and the people he trusts to carry things out of that house. Everyone in the city is asking after the same prize, and I'd rather understand the people asking than guess from their badges. It saves a great deal of time."

> [!qna]**Why let us find you?**
>
> Jarlaxle adjusts his hat before he answers.
>
> > "Because you earned it, and because Mirt will want to talk eventually, and I'd like him to know I'm willing. Three months of dinners, and not one guest has looked at my hands."

> [!qna]**Who do you work for?**
>
> > "For myself, and a number of people work for me. Ask again in a tenday and I may be a little more forthcoming."
>
> If the character was marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** before tonight, he adds:
>
> > "You know the name already, and Bregan D'aerthe is a company that prefers to be underestimated."

> [!qna]**The ticket clerk at the Faire?**
>
> > "Her name is Mara Coppersail. She works the public booth in a yellow scarf, and she keeps the ledger where anyone can read it. Somebody less courteous than I will notice her soon, and Mirt would want her out."

Before he leaves, Jarlaxle makes his request, and each character answers for themselves.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle straightens his hat and glances toward the nearest door, where Lethan waits with his hands loose at his sides.
>
> > "Lady Haventree will hear about this tomorrow, and she'll be very pleased with herself. I'd let her be, because it will make her more confident than she ought to be, and I'll find that useful."
>
> He looks at you with an attention that has nothing to do with the evening's manners.
>
> > "I have one small request. Tell Mirt and Remallia who I am, by all means, but keep my name out of the Watch's ledgers and the Lords' clerks. In return I'll stay clear of this house for a tenday."
>
> He tips the hat and waits for your answer.

> [!gamemaster]**The Discretion Request**
>
> A character who agrees promises to keep Jarlaxle's name from the City Watch and the clerks of the Lords, though Mirt and Remallia are not forbidden to know. Mark **Jarlaxle Discretion Agreement** for each character who agrees, with the name recorded. A character who declines suffers nothing, and Jarlaxle says only "Then we shall see each other again."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle nods once at each answer and turns toward the nearest door, with Lethan falling in behind him.
>
> > "You've been excellent company, and I expect I'll see you again."

Mark **Jarlaxle Unmasked** for each character present who was not already marked. Jarlaxle leaves by the nearest door, gate or alley, and nobody can follow him further tonight.

### The Wrong Guest

If a character names a guest other than Erystian, Remallia reaches the character within thirty seconds.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia rests her hand on the open study door, and the guest you named stays with the diners.
>
> > "Would you join me in the study for a moment? I'd like to hear what led you to that before we discuss it with anyone else. Tell me what you saw, and we can work out which question would clear it up."

> [!gamemaster]**Correcting the Accusation**
>
> The accused guest declines to speak with the party again that evening, and an apology the next day restores ordinary access. Erystian stays until 21:30, so every other evidence path stays open, and the party can still take him aside before then.

### If the Party Draws Steel

> [!hazard]**Jarlaxle Leaves**
>
> Jarlaxle is not a fight, so do not roll initiative for him if a character attacks him, Lethan or any guest.
>
> - Jarlaxle raises a hand, says one line in the voice he wants to be remembered for, and withdraws through the nearest door, balcony or crowd.
> - Remallia steps between the party and the guests before a second attack lands, because she does not fight a guest in her own house.
> - Her two **Guards** at the gate arrest the attacker and take no part in a fight, and Mirt vouches for the attacker at 09:00.
> - **Jarlaxle Discretion Agreement** cannot be made, the salon ends, and the card reads "Better luck next time."
>
> #### Lethan's Tactics
>
> Lethan uses the **Spy** stat block, and if the party has four or five characters, a second guard in the crowd uses the **Scout** stat block.
>
> To begin, Lethan steps between Jarlaxle and the nearest attacker and holds the door or the bridge for two rounds. During combat he:
>
> - Uses Cunning Action to Disengage and Hide.
> - Targets the character who is closest to Jarlaxle's exit.
> - Never pursues beyond the doorway.
>
> Lethan leaves over the roofs at 13 hit points and does not surrender. If he is held, he gives his name and nothing else. A character who lowers a weapon and apologizes to Remallia ends the scene at once.

### The Study at Nine

At 09:00 the next morning a steward shows the Harper members into Remallia's study. Companions receive their purses at the door. Mirt is at the window with his back to the room.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The study at House Ulbrinter is small and warm in the morning light, with a tea tray on the desk and a silver raven figurine beside the inkwell. Remallia pours for each of you herself, and Mirt turns from the window and takes the nearest chair.
>
> > "Sit. Tell me what you saw, one at a time, and start with the guests before you come to the actor."

The members tell Mirt what they spoke about, and he writes down each guest the party names. This is the moment to mark **Salon Guest Leads Recorded**, as **The Salon** sets out. Then read the case that fits.

If a member names Jarlaxle, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets his cup on the desk and looks at the guest list in front of him, and he does not touch the pitcher.
>
> > "Jarlaxle Baenre has sat in this house for three months, and I never saw him."
>
> He is silent a moment before he goes on.
>
> > "He could have left through the garden without a word, but he let you take him because he wants us to know he's looking. I'll find out what he saw."

If a marked member also tells him that Captain Zord of the Sea Maidens Faire is the same man, Mirt adds:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Then the carnival captain is his face on the water, and I'll want everything you know about that ship."

If the party named no one, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sorts the guest notes from the observations about Erystian and sets both beside his cup.
>
> > "Then we don't have a name yet. I'll go through the guest list tonight, so write down what you saw of the actor's hands and his friend, and I'll check it against the household."

If the party named the wrong guest, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt looks at the member who spoke, and his face does not change.
>
> > "Then the spy is still in the room, and the man you named is owed an apology. Take Remi's note to him this afternoon, and we'll look again."

Whatever the case, Remallia now says what she is.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia sets down the pot, touches the raven figurine once, and looks at you with the attention she gave your coats at the door.
>
> > "Mirt tells me you've earned the use of my name, so I should say plainly what it's worth. The Harper network in Waterdeep runs through this house. Mirt works in the open, and I do the quieter part."
>
> She slides a card across the desk.
>
> > "You've slept at twelve Delzorin Street, and the woman who gave you the key works for me. When you need something from the Harpers, you may come to me directly. Now tell me about yourselves again, because I like to know whom I'm feeding."

Mark **Remallia Harper Contact Known**, since each member who was present now has a direct line to her.

### Renown Opportunities

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains 3 base Renown for attending the salon and reporting to Mirt and Remallia. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the member correctly identified Erystian as Jarlaxle and reported it at the morning debrief, and **Jarlaxle Identity Exposed at Harper Salon** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** the member documented his cover with the alias and at least two concrete observations, and **Erystian Profile Reported** is marked. Observations can be the hat's aura, the face under the hat, the calluses, Lethan's guard, the mark on Swords Street or the remark about the clerk.
>
> Each bonus can be earned only once, and every character who attended, companions included, receives 200 gp from Remallia in a sealed purse.

Read the following when the purses are handed out:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The steward lays a sealed purse before each of you and checks your name against a list before he lets go of it.
>
> > "Two hundred gold, with Lady Haventree's thanks, and she asked me to be certain everyone got their own."

### Aftermath

If a member passes on Jarlaxle's remark about the clerk, Mirt sends two Harpers to the Faire's public booth that afternoon. Mara Coppersail leaves with her book and her yellow scarf, and no outcome is set. If nobody mentions it, she stays at the booth with her cover broken.

Jelenn Urmbrusk's reaction to the Zhentarim is the first thing Mirt writes down, and he says only that he will look into it.

Two days after the salon a courier delivers an embossed card to the party's lodging, showing a harp and crescent crossed by a rapier. If the party identified Jarlaxle, the message beneath reads:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I hear you've been asking about a certain mechanical footman."

Otherwise, it reads:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Better luck next time."

### Concluding the Event

The debrief ends the mission, and the card arrives two days later.

## Overview

The party faces the spy from Remallia's dinner and reports to Mirt, who sends a card back two days later.

## Summary

We put our evidence to Erystian and heard what he had to say, or we left him alone, and in the morning Mirt and Remallia heard the whole story. Remallia told us what she is, and two days later a card arrived from a man who had enjoyed the evening more than we had.
