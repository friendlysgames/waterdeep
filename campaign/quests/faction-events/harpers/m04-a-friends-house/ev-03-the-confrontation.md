# The Confrontation

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins in the villa's private study or in Shield Court, and ends with the members' report and the contact that follows it. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Name Jarlaxle and choose how much to tell Mirt.
> - Ask about his interest in the Harper network and about the compromised Faire contact.
> - Negotiate discretion, with no automatic faction penalty and no new membership.
> - Settle a wrong accusation, or an evening that ended without an identification.

### Background

Jarlaxle has watched Mirt's contacts through three months of salons, using Erystian's identity to listen without showing his faction. He accepts the risk of being recognized because he wants to size up these new agents and open another channel for negotiation. When he admits who he is, that establishes Jarlaxle beneath the Erystian guise, but it doesn't tell the party that Captain Zardoz Zord is the same person.

### The Private Conversation

At the villa, Remallia offers her study when asked and keeps the other guests in the salon. In Shield Court, Jarlaxle waits beside the closed tailor's shop until the party approaches, and Lethan arrives two minutes later if he went to check the signal site.

If the party identifies him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Erystian listens while you lay out the observations that led you to his name, and he adjusts his cuff when you finish. He gives you a small bow, and when he speaks the Luskan accent is gone, though the face is the same.
>
> > "Jarlaxle Baenre, yes. I did wonder how long it would take us to get to that part. Delightful. Would you like to tell Mirt first, or shall I explain why I've been accepting Remallia's invitations?"

> [!social]**The Observer Who Will Negotiate**
>
> Jarlaxle (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: a courteous adversary who asks what the party intends to do with its findings.
>
> Jarlaxle admits that he belongs to BD and that he attends Remallia's events, and he is happy to discuss the Harpers' position in the search for the vault. He doesn't reveal ship interiors, guarded routes or the extent of his disguise network. He listens to any specific proposal and either accepts its terms or turns it down plainly. He never demands a fight or a payment for his silence.

> [!qna]**What were you watching?**
>
> > "Mirt's meetings, mostly, and the people he trusts to do things outside that house. Everyone in the city is asking after the Stone, and I'd much rather understand the people asking than guess from their badges."

> [!qna]**What can you give us?**
>
> A successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check persuades Jarlaxle to give the party Mara Coppersail's name, her yellow-scarf description and the recovery message from **The Salon**. On a failure he asks for a specific mutual benefit, and he accepts the party's offer to carry Mirt a request for future talks as enough. A BD member who already received the information from him needs no second check.
>
> On a success, or once the party offers the future-talks request, he gives the details:
>
> > "There's a ticket clerk at the Faire who believes her Harper reports are still private. Her name is Mara Coppersail, and she wears a yellow scarf at the public booth. Meet her when she finishes at six tomorrow, tell her 'Mirt has a safer counting desk,' and take her to his residence. She can bring the next day's crew schedule, and I imagine Mirt will be glad of it."

If the check fails and the party hasn't yet offered the future-talks request, Jarlaxle asks for that benefit:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle adjusts his cuff and waits beside the doorway, leaving you room to propose the exchange before he goes on.
>
> > "Would you carry Mirt a request for another conversation? That would be useful to me, and it would make this a fair exchange instead of a favor you expect me to hand over for nothing."

> [!qna]**Will you pay for discretion?**
>
> > "I'd rather hear what you'd want it for. If you'd like another conversation, we can arrange one. If you mean to report my name, well, you have it already, and I won't pretend I can buy back what you've noticed."

### Disclosure and Departure

The party can report the name, pass on the anonymous BD-observer warning under the arrangement worked out at the salon, or decline to confirm anything. Each character decides what they will say. A BD character who helps on this mission doesn't become a Harper by doing so.

> [!gamemaster]**Leaving without a Forced Escape**
>
> Jarlaxle talks for up to twenty minutes unless the party ends the conversation sooner. If he is still at the villa, he thanks the hostess, and then he walks away with Lethan. If the party declares that they follow him, he stops, asks for privacy and waits for their answer instead of losing them. Following farther earns no extra award and starts no fight, and the conversation they have already had is enough to record his identity.

If the party follows after this conversation, Jarlaxle stops beside Lethan:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle turns back to you before he rounds the corner, with Lethan waiting a few paces farther along the street.
>
> > "I'd like some privacy for the rest of my evening. Is there another question we should settle here, or shall we leave the next conversation for a time we've agreed on?"

If the party names another guest, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia steps up beside you and rests her hand on the open study door, while the guest you named stays with the diners.
>
> > "Would you join me in the study for a moment? I'd like to hear what led you to that, before we discuss it with anybody else."
>
> > "I'd rather we checked the particulars before anyone is asked to defend themselves in front of the room. Tell me what you saw, and we can work out what question would clear it up."

> [!gamemaster]**Correcting the Accusation**
>
> The wrongly accused guest declines to talk any further that evening, but an apology the next day restores ordinary access. Jarlaxle stays until 21:30, so the party's other evidence paths stay open. If the party lets him leave without identifying him, the members can still report their uncertainty and the guest information they gathered.

### Mirt's Assessment

Mirt hears the Harper members privately before they leave when that is possible, or at his residence at 09:00 the following morning. After a street confrontation, a paper bird sent right afterward meets the timing for the identification bonus, and the detailed report follows the next morning.

If Jarlaxle's name is reported, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt spreads the guest list beside your notes and holds his pen over the space under Erystian's name, with his glass pushed back from the papers.
>
> > "How long did he stay? Which guests did he speak to? Did he offer anything about another contact we ought to warn?"
>
> He writes your answers beside the names and sets the list down.
>
> > "Three months he's been coming. I'll check who spoke to him and what we let him overhear. You've given us a name to work with. Don't assume it's the only one he uses."

If no identity is confirmed, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sorts the guest leads from the observations about Erystian and lays both sheets beside his glass, leaving the name blank at the top of the second.
>
> > "Which conversations did you follow up, and what came of them? Send me anything else that helps us check who's using that alias."
>
> > "I'll keep the account as you gave it. You haven't confirmed the name, so I won't write one down as if you had. The other conversations may still give us work."

### Renown Opportunities

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Rewards**
>
> Each Harper member who took part gains 3 base Renown for attending and reporting a considered assessment. Add +1 for correctly identifying Jarlaxle and reporting before he leaves the estate, or immediately after the street confrontation. Add +1 for reporting his alias and at least two concrete cover observations, whether or not the party confirmed his name. Each bonus is awarded once per eligible member, and companions who aren't Harpers earn no Renown from this mission.
>
> A BD member earns the separate +1 recovery bonus only through the discretion request or the successful three-way arrangement, and only by reaching Mara's booth in time, as **The Salon** describes. Reporting Jarlaxle honestly doesn't cost BD standing on its own, and nobody joins a second faction through this mission.
>
> At 09:00 the next morning, Remallia's courier delivers 200 GP to each character who attended, in separate sealed purses with a paper-bird note of thanks. This is a shared source reward, so it arrives even when the party never identified anyone or accused the wrong guest. The paper bird carries only the note, and the courier brings the coins.

When the reward arrives, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia's courier lays the sealed purses beside the unfolded paper bird and checks each attendee's name against a list before sliding the purse across to its owner.
>
> > "That's two hundred gold for each of you who came last night, with Lady Haventree's thanks. The note goes with it. She asked me to be sure everyone got their own purse."
>
> The note reads:
>
> > "Thank you for joining us. I hope we'll find time for another conversation once you've had a chance to consider what you heard. Do send word when you'd like to call."

If the party acts on Mara's warning before the independent recovery, use this response when they give the agreed message at the ticket booth:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mara Coppersail closes the ticket book, pulls her yellow scarf off the stool and folds the next day's crew schedule inside it before she steps out of the booth.
>
> > "A safer counting desk, is it? All right, I'll come with you to Mirt's. I've got tomorrow's crew schedule with me, and I'd like to hear what's going on once we're off the pier."

### Aftermath

Forty-eight hours after the salon ends, a courier delivers Jarlaxle's embossed calling card to the party's lodging. It shows a rapier crossed over a harp and crescent moon. A party that identified him gets the mechanical-footman message already written for this card, and every other branch gets the invitation below.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The courier holds the envelope out where you can read the names on the front, keeping the delivery sheet pressed against the sealed flap.
>
> > "Are you the people I was sent to find? If the names are right, it's yours, and I can mark it delivered."
>
> Inside is an embossed card showing a rapier crossed over a harp and crescent moon, with a short message written beneath.

If Jarlaxle was identified, the message reads:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I hear you've been asking about a certain mechanical footman."

Otherwise, the message reads:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I'd be pleased to speak again when you've had time to consider the evening."

The mechanical-footman line invites the party to keep asking questions, and it hands them no discovery they haven't made. Remallia now receives the Harper members directly, and each guest lead holds only what the party actually heard.

### Concluding the Event

The report ends the mission, and Mara's recovery and the calling card follow on the schedules given above.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Jarlaxle Identity Exposed at Harper Salon** — mark when Erystian is established as Jarlaxle, at the salon or during the pursuit. **Sea Maidens Faire** reads it as prior knowledge of Jarlaxle, and it doesn't identify Zord for the party.
> - **Erystian Profile Reported** — mark when Mirt receives the alias and at least two concrete observations. **Faction Outposts** and **Sea Maidens Faire** read those specific details, not a generic Advantage.
> - **Mara Coppersail Recovered** — mark when Mara reaches Mirt's residence after the warning. Later Harper inquiries keep her shift-schedule intelligence, and she is out of the compromised post.
> - **Jarlaxle Discretion Agreement** — mark with the individuals who accepted and the terms they actually promised. BD follow-up reads those commitments, and they enroll no Harper member.
> - **Remallia Harper Contact Known** — mark after the private disclosure. Later Harper missions allow direct briefings and services through her.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> **The Cell Is Compromised** can trigger after this salon once the party has met Davil. **The Sleeping Asset** becomes available at Renown 10 and 6th level. Pursue the recorded guest leads through their named quests. This mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

The party speaks with the observer beneath Erystian's identity, then chooses how to report the conversation and whether to help his compromised contact.

## Summary

We settled our assessment of Erystian and told Mirt the details we were willing to confirm. Remallia stayed available afterward, and Jarlaxle's calling card gave us another way to follow up what we had learned.
