# A Friend's House: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Harper member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level after **The Doppelganger Auditions**. Companions can attend the salon as Remallia's guests, but they do not hear Mirt's brief or the morning debrief.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 5th-level characters.*
>
> There is no planned fight. Jarlaxle is never a fight, and his bodyguard Lethan uses the Spy stat block only if the party draws steel. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** audits Lethan for three, four and five characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

At noon a paper bird tells each eligible member to come to the Yawning Portal at 17:00. Mirt is waiting at his corner table. He says that one of the guests at Remallia Haventree's dinner tomorrow evening is a spy for someone remarkable, and he wants to know who the spy is and whom he serves.

## Background

Remallia Haventree is the lady of House Ulbrinter on Delzorin Street in the North Ward, and for years she has been the quiet center of the Harpers in Waterdeep. Her salons are famous for their guest lists. For three months a charming young actor from Luskan has been on them, and he has been listening more than he has been talking.

Mirt cannot attend, because the actor knows his face. He sends the members instead and tells them only as much as they need to hold a conversation.

## The Salon

The members hire formal clothes from a tailor on Delzorin Street and arrive at House Ulbrinter at 19:00 with any companions they bring. Twelve guests are in the salon. The party talks to the ones who are worth talking to, and each of them carries a small worry that Mirt will want to hear about. Erystian Demarne is the guest who needs the most attention. He answers every question perfectly, and the party has to decide which of his answers to trust.

## The Tail

At 21:30 Erystian leaves with his valet. If the party follows, it crosses a canal bridge where the valet keeps watch and reaches a boarded building on Swords Street. A party that is spotted gets a friendly conversation on the bridge instead of the building.

## The Confrontation

The party can take Erystian aside with enough evidence and hear him admit who he is, name the wrong guest and be steered away by Remallia, or draw steel and watch him walk out. At nine the next morning the Harper members report to Mirt in Remallia's study, where she tells them what she is. The party is paid 200 gp each for the evening, and two days later a calling card arrives.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Harper member gains 3 base Renown for attending the salon and reporting to Mirt and Remallia. Members can earn two bonuses of +1 Renown each: one for correctly identifying the spy, and one for documenting his cover. Companions gain no Renown, but every character who attended receives the 200 gp.

## Aftermath

Remallia becomes a direct contact for the members, and the Harpers now have a name to go with three months of reports. Jarlaxle knows exactly who was clever enough to catch him, and he sends the party a card to say so. The guests' small worries are written down, and **The Cell Is Compromised** reads them. **The Sleeping Asset** becomes available at Renown 10 and 6th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): briefs the members and takes their report, and cannot go to the salon himself.
- **Remallia Haventree** (Harpers): hosts the salon as a noblewoman and tells the members her real role in the morning.
- **Jarlaxle Baenre** (Bregan D'aerthe): attends the salon as "Erystian Demarne, a young actor from Luskan" and lets himself be caught by anyone who earns it.
- **Lethan** (Bregan D'aerthe): Jarlaxle's valet and bodyguard, who stands where he can see every door.
- **Seldo Wynd**: the tailor at Seldo's Fine Stitches, 8 Delzorin Street, whom Mirt has paid.
- **Saeth Cromley** (City of Waterdeep): a retired Watch sergeant with a missing officer on his mind.
- **Tessabrant Elamondra**, **Aldric Talmost**, **Zalara Moonwhisper**, **Farrak Iltimer** and **Serithka Ondal**: guests with small leads about merchants, rumors and records.
- **Jelenn Urmbrusk** (City of Waterdeep): a proud noblewoman who travels with sixteen guards and leaves if the Zhentarim come up.
- **Mara Coppersail** (Harpers): a ticket clerk at the Sea Maidens Faire whose cover has been seen through.

## Dangers & Enemies

No combat is planned. If the party attacks a guest, Remallia steps between them and the guest, her two **Guards** at the gate arrest the attacker, and Jarlaxle walks away. Lethan, a **Spy**, holds a door or a bridge for two rounds to cover Jarlaxle's exit and leaves over the roofs at 13 hit points.

## Overview

Remallia Haventree is giving a dinner at her house on Delzorin Street, and Mirt asks the party to find the spy on her guest list. They talk to twelve guests, may follow a suspect into the night, and report to Mirt the next morning.
