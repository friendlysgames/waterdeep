# Design Notes: Harpers First Meeting

## An Individual Invitation

Renaer's recommendation and the Good-aligned invitation trigger are still the way into this meeting, but tickets and box seats now follow the actual recruitment candidates. This replaces the restored draft's two tickets and the source appendix's whole-party tickets with the approved individual-membership design. A party can hold several Harper candidates alongside characters who have joined other factions, and each candidate answers Mirt for themselves.

The invitation trigger adds no alignment test at the theater door, and ordinary companions can attend the public performance without entering the private recruitment conversation. Renown, rank and member benefits go to the characters who accept, and recording their names with **Harpers Joined** keeps the existing outcome while giving later missions an exact membership record.

## Time to Get Dressed

The noon invitation gives candidates the afternoon to sort out their clothes, and the recovery route spells out every leg of the journey. A 5:30 p.m. departure from the theater allows 30 minutes to the tailor, a 45-minute fitting and 30 minutes back, which lands at the 7:15 p.m. intermission. Candidates already at the shop can start at 6 p.m., and anyone later gets a concrete appointment for the following evening.

Intermission arrivals get the same conversation and offer. Mirt judges their questions and never pretends to have watched a first act they missed. Neither clothing trouble nor the recovery appointment shuts a candidate out of recruitment for good.

## Shelter Without a Reveal

The lodging at 12 Delzorin Street, with its five bunks and its key procedure, is an approved original detail that makes the Watcher benefit usable straight away. Remi maintains it through intermediaries, so new members need only their key and address card to walk in and rest. The rented lodging is separate from her villa, which saves her Harper reveal for **A Friend's House**, Mission 4.

## A Meeting Before Work

The evening introduces Mirt and gives candidates room to ask about the Harpers, with the play supplying a conversation about political judgment. The **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** invitation examination and the **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** assessment observation carry over the restored mechanics, including the hidden assessment criteria. Mirt's private offices stay GM information, and the meeting gives no Grand Game briefing and no assignment.

The event keeps **Harpers Joined**, individual Renown 1 and the Watcher benefits, with **The Talking Mare** available at level 2. It adds no reward outside the approved plan and no Milestone Point. Encounter balance doesn't apply because the meeting has no combat.

## The Performance and Early Conversation

The timed stage scenes are original to this Lightsinger production, and the council's orders, missing stores and broken promises make the approved political-will theme visible. They aren't source-book history and they don't point at the Grand Game. The opera stays in Giant as the source has it, and a Common libretto at every box seat puts the actor lines within reach of every candidate with no language gate or check.

Four beats fill the first half at 6:30, 6:42, 6:55 and 7:10 p.m., followed by the agreed 7:15 intermission. Two optional later scenes give candidates something to watch after Mirt leaves at 7:35, with the final bows ending at 8:30. Staying earns no extra reward, and arrivals during the interval don't get credit for scenes they missed.

Mirt's early hospitality has spoken answers for wine, food, the theater, the libretto, Renaer and the invitation. His public manner allows profanity and expansive talk, and pointed work questions get a safe brush-off. Recruitment begins at intermission, when his manner drops to business, and his private offices, assessment criteria and the other Harpers stay undisclosed.

## Structural References

The approved checkpoint plan governs the repairs, and the current Harpers guide, recruitment event and Notable Figures pages supply the campaign dependencies. WDH's recruitment passage supplies the theater meeting and silver pin, while its immediate mission assignment gives way to the established remix's separate dispatch. Ember's **Sheltered Campsite**, **The Winding Trail** overview and **Event Characteristics** supply the model for the scene, the conversation and the journal summary. The sibling design page keeps editorial rationale outside the playable scenes.
