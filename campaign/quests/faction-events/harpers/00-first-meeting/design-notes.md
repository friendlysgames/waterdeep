# Design Notes: Harpers First Meeting

## An Individual Invitation

The restored draft treated the invitation as a party-wide event and marked **Harpers Joined** as a single True or False. WDH and Appendix B send tickets for the whole party. The event now sends one ticket per unaffiliated character whom Renaer recommended, and each candidate answers for themselves. The outcome is marked per character with a name, so later missions have an exact membership record. Companions can watch the opera and gain nothing, and a character already in another faction gets no offer. This matches the Doom Raiders and Bregan D'aerthe First Meetings.

## One Evening, Two Gears

The event stays at the theater. Mirt plays the loud Old Wolf through the first act and drops to his business voice at intermission, which is the switch the voice profile uses to show he is serious. The opera is kept as the source gives it, sung in Giant, with a Common libretto printed for the table's sake. The earlier drafts scripted the opera scene by scene with clock times, and a tailor schedule with a recovery appointment. Those are cut, and the tailor takes one sentence. The qna answers cover the Harpers, the chain of command, the offer, other loyalties, refusal and the first job, and nothing else.

## What Mirt Withholds

Mirt names the Black Network's split only if a player raises Manshoon, and he never names the man. His Masked Lord seat and Laeral's friendship stay hidden until the High Harper rank event. The lodging at 12 Delzorin Street is kept by an intermediary, because the organization page hides Remallia's Harper role until **A Friend's House**.

## Source Departures

WDH and Appendix B give the first mission at the pin. The remix holds it back, and **The Talking Mare** arrives by its own brief at 2nd level. The invitation says Sea Ward in WDH and Appendix B. The Lightsinger is placed in the Castle Ward, as WDH's own area text, the Factions Guide and the earlier drafts do.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- No new NPC names. The lodging address (12 Delzorin Street, five bunks) is carried over from the previous version. Mirt's lines about the Dock Ward and about "asking Renaer to find me" are original.
- **The Factions Come Calling** (`campaign/quests/act-i/trollskull-alley/ev-04-the-factions-come-calling.md`) still writes **Harpers Joined** as a True or False heading and names Remallia Haventree as a Harper referral for Filthy Meg, which breaks the hidden-Remi rule.
