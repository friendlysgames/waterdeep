# The Tail

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration Event begins at 09:30 after the second interview evening if Edric remains unidentified. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Recognize the repeated observer and choose whether to confront or follow him.
> - Obtain the safehouse address by shadowing, bargaining or searching his carried note.
> - Recover files linking his surveillance to the exposed noble-house contacts.
> - Resolve Bonnie's crew by informing her of Edric's betrayal, even if he escapes capture.
>
> Use the safehouse sections alone if the party identified Edric during the interviews and follows that lead. In the ordinary opening of this event, his 09:00 report has already gone out.

### Background

Edric delivered his interview account at 09:00 and then took on the look of a Dock Ward day laborer so he could watch the characters' movements. His report gives faces and disclosed affiliations and keeps Harper members apart from their helpers. It adds to the earlier surveillance on Mattrim, and it tells the Splinter nothing about plans or capabilities that nobody has demonstrated.

> [!gamemaster]**Evidence and Its Limits**
>
> The files establish that Edric was reporting, who the exposed contacts are and that Rusk runs a customs-house channel. Edric's true allegiance stays with you unless the party has separately proved it. Mirt describes the reporting network the members documented, and neither the files nor an admission of betrayal names Manshoon.

### A Repeated Face

At 09:30, Edric starts following the first interviewed character to leave their lodging, or the whole group if several leave together. He stays forty feet behind when street traffic allows and watches from the opposite side of the street when they stop.

> [!exploration]**Recognizing the Observer**
>
> Any character with a Passive Perception of 15 or higher notices the same day laborer in three locations over the course of an hour. A character who deliberately doubles back, pauses without warning or takes a roundabout route can instead make a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check to notice him right away. After a failure, someone can try another maneuver ten minutes later, and nobody has to wait for a successful roll before asking about the repeated face.
>
> If nobody notices him, Edric follows until 12:30 and then returns to Nethpranter Street to file a supplement. Record the places and contacts he saw in those three hours.

Once someone recognizes him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The laborer you passed near your lodging is across the street again, hands tucked inside his coat, watching the shop doors. When you turn toward him, he glances at a cart and starts walking back the way you came.

### Confronting or Following

If the party follows Edric and keeps him in sight, he leads them to the safehouse when he returns at 12:30. A successful DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check keeps the pursuit unnoticed, and on a failure he notices the party and offers the bargain below. The party can also watch him go in from the next street corner without following him inside.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The laborer stops beside a shuttered shop as you approach and turns to face you while another group squeezes past between the carts. His face shifts as he draws breath and becomes that of a middle-aged merchant, though the coat and the mud on his boots stay the same.
>
> > "So you know who I am. Give me thirty seconds to leave and I'll tell you where the reports go, and what you do with the house after that is up to you."

> [!exploration]**Cutting Off His Exit**
>
> Any character who moves to block Edric's path through the crowd and makes a successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check stops him before he escapes the immediate confrontation. On a failure he slips toward the next corner, and the party can keep following or call the Watch, which only gives him another obstacle to get past before he can vanish.
>
> If he's detained or defeated, any character who searches his possessions and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the folded Nethpranter Street dispatch. A deliberate ten-minute search finds it without a check. Either way the address is the party's even if he refuses to bargain, and Shape-Shift doesn't change his clothing or papers.

If the party accepts the bargain, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Edric points along the street toward the Trades Ward, keeps his other hand well clear of his coat and waits beside the shuttered shop.
>
> > "It's the three-floor house on Nethpranter Street, across from the cargo yard, with shuttered windows and no sign on the door. Start counting once I reach the corner, and give me the thirty seconds we agreed."
>
> He walks off without changing his face again, hands clear of his coat until the street bends out of sight.

If Edric is detained, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Edric stops looking toward the street now that his exits are blocked, and he keeps the merchant's face while he rests his hands against the shutter behind him.
>
> > "Are you calling the Watch, or are we still talking about letting me go? You've stopped me leaving, and I'd like to know what happens next."
>
> The folded paper in his coat carries an address and a time, with no name for whoever is meant to receive it.

> [!hazard]**Edric if Cornered**
>
> Use the ordinary 2024 **Doppelganger** and the tactics from **The Doppelganger Auditions**. Edric bargains before he fights, uses Unsettling Visage and Disengage to get away and attacks a blocker only when no route is left. He surrenders at 10 HP or fewer while still cornered, and changing guise doesn't Hide him. The fight ends with his escape, release, surrender or defeat. If the chase reaches the house, he arrives with whatever resources he has left.

### The Nethpranter Street Safehouse

The safehouse stands across from a cargo yard on Nethpranter Street in the Trades Ward, and it isn't the Shesstra Street building. It has three floors with one main room each: a ground-floor guardroom, a second-floor sleeping room and a third-floor records workspace. The street door is closed but unlocked, and the windows are shuttered.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The address leads you to a narrow house opposite a yard where cargo crates wait under canvas, with the shutters closed across its upper windows. The street door carries no business sign, and a worn groove beneath it shows where people have dragged boxes across the threshold.

> [!hazard]**The Two Resident Spies**
>
> Two 2024 **Spies** are on the ground floor, one at the table ten feet inside the door and one beside the stairs. Edric hands over his supplement at the door and leaves on his next turn. He joins the fight only if the party intercepts him before he goes.
>
> #### Resident Tactics
> The residents challenge unfamiliar visitors at the threshold and draw weapons if intruders push past them toward the stairs.
>
> During combat, the residents:
> - Attack separate intruders when possible and use Cunning Action to retreat toward the staircase.
> - Protect the upstairs files and pursue no farther than thirty feet beyond the door.
> - Surrender when one is defeated and two conscious opponents remain within thirty feet of the survivor.
>
> The encounter ends when the residents surrender or are defeated, or when the party withdraws beyond pursuit range. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** covers the tougher combined Edric-and-two-Spies fight and the warnings for smaller parties. Watching the house and reporting its address is a complete noncombat resolution.

If unfamiliar visitors approach the threshold, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The one at the table rises as the door opens, and the other stays at the foot of the stairs with a hand on the rail.
>
> > "Who are you here to see, and what have you brought for them? Wait right there and give me a name, because this isn't a house people wander through."

> [!exploration]**The Operational Files**
>
> Any character who searches the third-floor desk and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds all three relevant files. Ten minutes spent reading the labeled folders finds them without a check.
>
> - Edric's two weeks of surveillance confirm that the Splinter knows Mattrim is a Harper agent.
> - The noble-house list names Lysa Fenwick at House Amcathra and Teren Moss at House Rosznar, along with their ordinary contact routines. They're the same two assets from **The Dead Drop**, not two new people.
> - Correspondence names Beldan Rusk as the customs-house reporting contact, which ties the operation to Tessalar's established channel.
>
> If **Harper Contacts Relocated** is already marked, the files only describe the contacts' former routines, and no second relocation is needed. Otherwise, warning Mirt starts their move to 12 Delzorin Street within forty-eight hours. None of the files shows any knowledge of noble infernalism.

If the party leaves with the files, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The folder holds a description of Mattrim beside a list of his usual evenings at the Portal, followed by two household names marked against a familiar cipher. A separate letter gives customs-house collection instructions, with Beldan Rusk's name written under the last entry.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt receives the participating Harper members at 20:00 after their final report, or at 09:00 the next morning if they get back later. This continuation doesn't add a second base award or another assessment fee.

> [!gamemaster]**The Safehouse Bonus**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains +1 Renown for finding the safehouse and reporting its address or files, provided they haven't already earned that mission bonus. Identifying Edric now can't win back the pre-delivery bonus, since his interview report left at 09:00. Bonnie's operative-role bonus is still available if the members make the required case after exposing him.

### Aftermath

Mirt tells Bonnie what the members found, and she excludes Edric at once from all crew work and private meetings, whether he was captured or escaped. Mark **Harper M3 Complete** after that exclusion and the assessment report. The mission never leaves Edric embedded for the later Enclave mission.

When Mirt brings the finding to Bonnie, read or paraphrase the following before her response:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets the members' account beside Bonnie's meeting list, taps Edric's name and leaves the custody notes within her reach.
>
> > "Edric's been feeding a hostile reporting network, and this account shows what the members found. Take him out of the crew now, caught or not. The others need to know before they work with him again."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie draws the next meeting list toward her and lays four sheets beside it, leaving Edric's name off the page while Mattrim takes a chair nearby.
>
> > "Well, shit, is he in custody, or did he get away? Tell me what happened when you caught up with him, so I can give the others a straight account of it."
>
> She writes a separate message for each of the remaining colleagues, folds each one with the names inside and passes them to Mattrim.
>
> > "Get these to the others before the evening shift, would you? They need to hear which work has ended before somebody turns up wearing a face they know."
>
> > "He won't work with us again. If he's still out there and you see him, tell me, and I'll make sure the others don't mistake him for someone they've agreed to meet."

> [!gamemaster]**What the Splinter Retains**
>
> Edric's delivered report stays with the Splinter even if the safehouse is cleared. Later Splinter opposition can use the faces, affiliations and routes he actually observed, and at Kolat Towers personnel recognize the reported characters and question their cover identities. This adds no unnamed defenders, gives the Splinter no knowledge of abilities nobody showed and takes no essential clue away. Note what the report actually contained beside the outcome, so the later event can read it.

### Concluding the Event

The members' report ends the pursuit, and Bonnie's exclusion settles the crew whether or not Edric is in custody.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Edric Identified** — mark when surveillance or the files establish his betrayal; Bonnie reads this before excluding him.
> - **Edric Report Delivered** — mark at 09:00 if not intercepted; **Kolat Towers** and later Splinter opposition read only the recorded observations.
> - **Edric Captured** — mark when the party or Watch keeps him in custody; later Harper reports preserve that status instead of assuming an escape.
> - **Nethpranter Safehouse Reported** — mark when the members give Mirt its address or files; **Faction Outposts** reads the secondary location, and the single mission bonus is recorded here.
> - **Harper Contacts Relocated** — mark when both known noble-house contacts reach 12 Delzorin Street within forty-eight hours of warning Mirt; **Cassalanter Villa** reads the preserved contact chain.
> - **Harper M3 Complete** — mark once the assessment is reported and Bonnie excludes Edric; **The Doppelganger Problem** reads his absence even if he escaped.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**

## Overview

An unidentified Edric follows the interviewed characters the next morning, allowing them to expose his surveillance and trace the report to a Trades Ward safehouse.

## Summary

We found Edric watching our movements and learned where his reports were delivered. We told Mirt what Edric's recipients had received, and Bonnie removed Edric from her crew, though his earlier disclosures couldn't be recalled.
