# The Tail

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration Event begins at 09:30 after the second interview evening if Edric remains unidentified. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Recognize the repeated observer and choose whether to confront or follow him.
> - Obtain the safehouse address by shadowing, bargaining or searching his carried note.
> - Recover files linking his surveillance to the exposed noble-house contacts.
> - Resolve Bonnie's crew by informing her of Edric's betrayal, even if he escapes capture.
>
> Use the safehouse sections alone if Edric was identified during the interviews and the party follows that lead. His 09:00 report is already delivered in this event's ordinary opening.

### Background

Edric delivered his interview account at 09:00, then adopted a Dock Ward day laborer's appearance to observe the characters' movements. His report identifies faces and disclosed affiliations, retaining the distinction between Harper members and their helpers. The report supplements earlier surveillance on Mattrim; it doesn't give the Splinter knowledge of plans or capabilities nobody demonstrated.

> [!gamemaster]**Evidence and Its Limits**
>
> The files establish Edric’s reporting, the exposed contacts and Rusk’s customs-house channel. His true allegiance remains DM-only unless the party has separately proved it. Mirt describes the reporting network the members documented; neither these files nor an admission of betrayal identifies Manshoon.

### A Repeated Face

At 09:30, Edric begins following the first interviewed character to leave their lodging. If several leave together, he follows the group; he remains forty feet behind when street traffic permits and watches from the opposite side when they stop.

> [!exploration]**Recognizing the Observer**
>
> A Passive Perception score of 15 or higher notices the same day laborer in three locations over an hour. A character who deliberately doubles back, pauses unexpectedly or takes a circuitous route can instead make a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check to notice him immediately. Failure leaves another deliberate maneuver available after ten minutes; no one needs to wait for a single successful roll to begin asking about the repeated face.
>
> If unobserved, Edric follows until 12:30, then returns to Nethpranter Street to file a supplement. Record only the actual places and contacts he saw during those three hours.

Once someone recognizes him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The laborer you passed near your lodging stands across the street again, with his hands tucked inside his coat while he watches the shop doors. When you turn toward him, he looks away toward a cart and begins walking in the direction you came from.

### Confronting or Following

Following Edric while keeping him in sight leads to the safehouse when he returns at 12:30. A successful DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check keeps the pursuit unnoticed; failure makes him notice the party and offer the bargain below. The party can also watch him enter from the next street corner without attempting to enter behind him.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The laborer stops beside a shuttered shop when you approach, turning to face you while another group passes between the carts. His face changes as he draws breath, becoming that of a middle-aged merchant, although the coat and mud on his boots remain the same.
>
> > "You know who I am, then. Give me thirty seconds to leave and I'll tell you where the reports go. You can decide what to do with the house afterward."

> [!exploration]**Cutting Off His Exit**
>
> A successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check blocks Edric's path through the crowd before he escapes the immediate confrontation. This corrects the former Dexterity (Athletics) notation without changing the DC. Failure lets him move toward the next corner; the party can continue following or call for the Watch, which requires him to deal with another obstacle rather than granting an automatic disappearance.
>
> If detained or defeated, a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the folded Nethpranter Street dispatch among his possessions. A deliberate ten-minute search also finds it without a check. It supplies the address even when he refuses to bargain, and his clothing and papers remain unchanged by Shape-Shift.

If the party accepts the bargain, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Edric points along the street toward the Trades Ward, keeping his other hand clear of his coat while he waits beside the shuttered shop.
>
> > "It's the three-floor house on Nethpranter Street opposite the cargo yard, with shuttered windows and no sign on the door. You'll find it across from that yard on Nethpranter Street, as I said. Start counting when I reach the corner, and give me the thirty seconds we agreed."
>
> He walks away without another change of face, keeping his hands clear of his coat until the street bends out of sight.

If Edric is detained, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Edric stops looking toward the street once his exits are blocked, keeping the merchant's face he adopted outside while his hands rest against the shutter behind him.
>
> > "Are you calling the Watch, or are we still discussing release? You've stopped me leaving, and I'd like to know what happens next."
>
> The folded paper in his coat bears an address and a time, with no name identifying the person expected to receive it.

> [!hazard]**Edric if Cornered**
>
> Use the ordinary 2024 **Doppelganger** and the tactics in **The Doppelganger Auditions**. He uses Unsettling Visage and Disengage to escape, bargains before fighting and attacks only a blocker when no route remains. He surrenders at 10 HP or fewer while still cornered; changing guise doesn't Hide him automatically. Combat ends with escape, release, surrender or defeat, with ordinary resources retained if pursuit reaches the house.

### The Nethpranter Street Safehouse

The safehouse stands opposite a cargo yard on Nethpranter Street in the Trades Ward, separate from the Shesstra Street building. Its three floors each contain one main room, with a closed unlocked street door, shuttered windows, a ground-floor guardroom, a second-floor sleeping room and a third-floor records workspace.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The address leads to a narrow house opposite a yard where cargo crates are waiting under canvas, with shutters closed across its upper windows. The street door has no business sign, and a worn groove beneath it shows where people have dragged a box across the threshold.

> [!hazard]**The Two Resident Spies**
>
> Two 2024 **Spies** occupy the ground floor, one at the table ten feet beyond the door and one beside the stairs. Edric delivers his supplement at the door and leaves on his next turn, joining their combat only if the party intercepts him before he departs.
>
> #### Resident Tactics
> The residents challenge unfamiliar visitors at the threshold and draw weapons if intruders push past them toward the stairs.
>
> During combat, the residents:
> - Attack separate intruders when possible and use Cunning Action to retreat toward the staircase.
> - Protect the upstairs files and pursue no farther than thirty feet beyond the door.
> - Surrender when one is defeated and two conscious opponents remain within thirty feet of the survivor.
>
> The encounter ends when the residents surrender or are defeated, or when the party withdraws beyond pursuit range. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** includes the stronger combined Edric-and-two-Spies branch and smaller-party warnings. Observing the house and reporting its address remains a complete noncombat resolution.

If unfamiliar visitors approach the threshold, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The person beside the table rises as the door opens, while the other remains at the foot of the stairs with a hand against the rail.
>
> > "Who are you here to see, and what have you brought for them today? Wait at the door while you give me the name, because this house isn't open for people to wander through."

> [!exploration]**The Operational Files**
>
> A successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds all three relevant files in the third-floor desk; ten minutes spent reading its labeled folders does the same without a check.
>
> - Edric's two weeks of surveillance confirm that the Splinter knows Mattrim is a Harper agent.
> - The noble-house list identifies Lysa Fenwick at House Amcathra and Teren Moss at House Rosznar, with their ordinary contact routines. These are the same two assets from **The Dead Drop**, rather than two additional unnamed people.
> - Correspondence names Beldan Rusk as the customs-house reporting contact, connecting the operation to Tessalar's established channel.
>
> If **Harper Contacts Relocated** is already marked, the files describe the contacts' former routines and no second relocation is needed. Otherwise, warning Mirt starts their move to 12 Delzorin Street within forty-eight hours. Neither file contains knowledge of noble infernalism.

If the party leaves with the files, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The folder contains Mattrim's description beside a list of his usual evenings at the Portal, followed by two household names marked against a familiar cipher. A separate letter gives the customs-house collection instructions, with Beldan Rusk's name written beneath the last entry.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt receives the participating Harper members at 20:00 after their final report, or at 09:00 the next morning if they return later. This continuation doesn't grant a second base award or another assessment fee.

> [!gamemaster]**The Safehouse Bonus**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains +1 Renown for finding the safehouse and reporting its address or files, only if they haven't earned that mission bonus already. Identifying Edric now can't recover the pre-delivery bonus, because his interview report left at 09:00. Bonnie's operative-role bonus remains available if the members make the required case after exposing him.

### Aftermath

Mirt tells Bonnie the finding, and she excludes Edric from all crew work and private meetings immediately, whether he was captured or escaped. Mark **Harper M3 Complete** after this exclusion and the assessment report; it never leaves him embedded for the later Enclave mission.

When Mirt brings the finding to Bonnie, read or paraphrase the following before her response:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt places the members' account beside Bonnie's meeting list and points to Edric's name, leaving the custody notes within her reach.
>
> > "Edric supplied a hostile reporting network with information, and this account gives you what the members found. Exclude him from the crew now, whether he's been caught or is still out there. The others need to know before they work with him again."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie draws the next meeting list toward her and lays four sheets beside it, leaving Edric's name off the page as Mattrim takes a chair nearby.
>
> > "Is he in custody, or did he get away? Tell me what happened when you caught up with him, so I can give the others an accurate account."
>
> She writes a separate message for each remaining colleague and folds them with the names inside before passing them to Mattrim.
>
> > "Get these to the others before the evening shift, please. They need to know which work has ended before someone turns up wearing a familiar face."
>
> > "He won't work with us again. If he's still out there, tell me when you see him, and I'll make sure the others don't mistake him for someone they've agreed to meet."

> [!gamemaster]**What the Splinter Retains**
>
> Edric's delivered report persists even if the safehouse is cleared. Later Splinter opposition can use the faces, affiliations and routes actually observed; at Kolat Towers, personnel recognize those reported characters and question their cover identities. This doesn't add unnamed defenders, grant knowledge of unshown abilities or remove essential clues. Record the actual report alongside the outcome for its intended later reader.

### Concluding the Event

The members' report ends the pursuit, and Bonnie's exclusion settles the crew regardless of Edric's custody status.

## Overview

An unidentified Edric follows the interviewed characters the next morning, allowing them to expose his surveillance and trace the report to a Trades Ward safehouse.

## Summary

We found Edric watching our movements and learned where his reports were delivered. We told Mirt what Edric’s recipients had received, and Bonnie removed Edric from her crew even though his earlier disclosures couldn't be recalled.
