# The Tail

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Combat Event occurs the morning after the second evening of **The Doppelganger Auditions**, when Edric Tanner has not been held at the Portal, and it ends when the members report to Mirt. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Follow Edric from the Portal door at 08:30, or notice him following them at 09:30.
> - Cut him off in the street, watch him change faces, or take his bargain.
> - Search him for the address of the safehouse on Nethpranter Street.
> - Read the files in the house, with or without a fight.
> - Report to Mirt and hear Bonnie bar Edric from her crew.
>
> Play this Event if **Edric Identified** is not marked, or if it is marked and the members chose to follow Edric. Skip it if Bonnie held him. Only Harper members attend the report, and companions can help with everything before it.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Edric reports to two resident Spies at a three-floor house on Nethpranter Street in the Trades Ward, across from a cargo yard. The house has a guardroom on the ground floor, bunks above and a records room on top.
> - His report gives the faces of the members and companions and the affiliations he saw them state. It does not separate helpers from Harpers if the members did not.
> - The records room holds three things: two weeks of notes on Mattrim, a list naming Lysa Fenwick of House Amcathra and Teren Moss of House Rosznar, and letters naming Beldan Rusk as the handler.
> - Rusk collects these papers from the house. The members learn his name here for the first time unless **Handler Ledger Read** is marked.
> - If the members followed him, Edric reaches the house at 09:00. If he is not stopped first, mark **Edric Report Delivered**.
> - If the members did not identify him, he delivered the report at 09:00 and now follows the first interviewer to leave their lodging.

### A Face in the Crowd

If **Edric Identified** is marked, the members wait near the Portal door. At 08:30 Edric walks out in his teamster guise and heads toward the Trades Ward. A member who follows him makes a DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check. On a failure he notices the tail, and the chase below begins at once.

If **Edric Identified** is not marked, Edric starts at 09:30 and follows the first member to leave their lodging, or the whole group if they leave together. He stays forty feet behind and crosses the street whenever they stop.

> [!exploration]**Spotting the Observer**
>
> A character with a Passive Perception of 15 or higher notices the same day laborer in three places within the hour. A character who doubles back or stops without warning can instead make a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check to see him at once. A character who fails can try another trick ten minutes later.
>
> If nobody spots him, Edric follows until noon and then goes back to the house. The members learn nothing this morning, and the address stays open to the other routes below.

Read the following aloud when someone spots him:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The laborer you passed near your lodging is across the street again, hands in his coat, watching the shop doors. When you turn to look at him, he glances at a passing cart and starts to walk the way you came.

### Edric Runs

If the members confront Edric or give chase, he runs. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The laborer stops beside a shuttered shop as you come toward him and turns to face you. His face softens and shifts as he draws breath, and it becomes a gray-haired merchant's, though the coat and the muddy boots are the same. A cart rattles past and the crowd bunches around it.
>
> > "Right, so you know me. Give me thirty seconds to walk away and I'll tell you where the reports go."

> [!hazard]**Edric in the Street**
>
> Edric uses the ordinary 2024 **Doppelganger**, with no added phases. He wants to reach the house, and a fight is the last thing he chooses.
>
> #### Edric's Tactics
>
> Edric opens by changing his face as a bonus action and Dashing into the crowd. His coat, hat, bag and boots do not change with his face.
>
> During the chase, Edric:
>
> - Breaks line of sight at a corner, changes face and Hides, but only if he reaches a corner and has an action to spend in the same turn.
> - Changes his coat with a Utilize action when he is out of sight, which is the only way to shake a tracker who is watching his clothes.
> - Uses Unsettling Visage when blockers close in on a crowded street. The 15-foot Emanation frightens bystanders as well as the members and turns the crowd into difficult terrain.
> - Attacks with his two Slams, using the first-round Advantage once, only when no route is left.
>
> Edric flees at 26 hit points or fewer. He surrenders if he is cornered at 26 hit points or fewer, or if he is Grappled below half. A member who spends an action to strip his hat or coat makes him surrender at once. He will not die for the Splinter, but he will fight on while the way out is open.
>
> Edric alone is the whole fight in the street, whatever the party's size. If he reaches the house, he joins the residents described below.

- **Keeping him in sight:** A member who tracks Edric makes a DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check each round, because his coat gives him away.
- **Cutting him off:** A member who tackles him or blocks an alley makes a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check. On a success he is Grappled, and he escapes the grapple on a DC 11 check. On a failure he gets past.
- **Taking the bargain:** A member who accepts his offer lets him walk away for thirty seconds and learns the address in exchange. Edric is not held, and **Edric Report Delivered** is marked if his report has not yet reached the house.

> [!exploration]**Finding the Address**
>
> The address of the safehouse can be reached three ways, and the members need only one.
>
> - **Search Edric:** Any character who searches him after he is held and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds a folded dispatch with the Nethpranter Street address. If he is Grappled, the search succeeds without a check.
> - **Follow him:** A member who follows him unseen to the door sees which house he enters.
> - **Ask Bonnie:** Bonnie traced his payments to Nethpranter Street and gives the street to anyone who asks. One house on it faces a cargo yard and shows no sign, with its upper shutters closed.

If Edric surrenders, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Edric stops looking toward the street once his exits are blocked, and he keeps the merchant's face while he rests his hands against the shutter behind him. He glances at the crowd, which has formed a ring around him.
>
> > "Right, so are we calling the Watch, or are we still talking about letting me go? You've stopped me leaving, and I'd like to know what happens next."

> [!social]**The Cornered Teamster**
>
> Edric Tanner (Neutral Evil, Doppelganger, he/him) :: a cornered informant who stops joking the moment his exits are blocked.
>
> Edric bargains and does not threaten. He trades the address for a head start and gives nothing else. He does not name any buyer. A member who threatens him gets the same answer, and a member who offers him a way out of the city gets his full attention.
>
> Edric is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The safehouse and who is inside.
> - Where he would go if he were let go.
> - What the report says, which he tells in outline.
>
> He will not discuss his buyer, or what the Splinter knows about Bonnie.

> [!qna]**Who are you working for?**
>
> > "Right, you'll want a name, and I haven't got one. A house takes my reports and a man collects them. That's all I know."

> [!qna]**What did you tell them?**
>
> > "Faces and what you said. Who was a Harper and who only poured. I never saw a plan, because you never showed me one."

If the members hold Edric, mark **Edric Captured**. Mirt takes him from the members, and the members do not learn what becomes of him.

### Nethpranter Street

Read the following aloud when the members reach the house:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A narrow house stands opposite a yard where cargo crates wait under canvas, and the shutters on its upper windows are closed. The street door carries no sign. A worn groove beneath it shows where boxes have been dragged across the threshold.

> [!hazard]**The Residents**
>
> Two resident guards are on the ground floor, one at a table ten feet inside the door and one beside the stairs. Use the roster for the party's size:
>
> - **Three characters:** a **Spy** and a **Bandit** minder.
> - **Four characters:** a **Spy** and two **Bandits**.
> - **Five characters:** two **Spies**.
>
> If Edric is in the house, he replaces one of these. At three characters he fights with a **Bandit**, at four with two **Bandits**, and at five with a **Spy**.
>
> #### The Residents' Tactics
>
> The residents challenge unfamiliar visitors at the threshold and draw weapons if visitors push toward the stairs.
>
> During combat, the residents:
>
> - Attack separate intruders and use Cunning Action to fall back toward the stairs.
> - Protect the files upstairs and pursue no farther than thirty feet past the door.
> - Yield when a Spy reaches 13 hit points or fewer. A Spy who is cornered at 9 hit points or fewer surrenders, and the second Spy surrenders with the first. A Bandit yields at 5 hit points or fewer.
>
> Watching the house and reporting its address is a complete resolution without a fight.

If the members approach the door, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The one at the table stands as the door opens, and the other stays at the foot of the stairs with a hand on the rail.
>
> > "Who are you here to see, and what have you brought for them? Give me a name before you come another step."

> [!exploration]**The Records Room**
>
> Any character who searches the desk on the third floor and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds all three files. A character who spends ten minutes reading the labeled folders finds them without a check.
>
> - **Notes on Mattrim:** Two weeks of notes record his evenings at the Portal and show that the Splinter knows he is a Harper agent.
> - **The noble-house list:** The list names Lysa Fenwick at House Amcathra and Teren Moss at House Rosznar, with their routines. They are the two contacts named in the cipher from **The Dead Drop**, and not new people.
> - **The letters:** The letters name Beldan Rusk as the contact who collects reports at the customs house, which ties this house to the channel Tessalar Maeridge served.
>
> If **Harper Contacts Relocated** is marked, the two contacts are already safe, and the files only describe the routines they have left behind. Otherwise Mirt moves them within two days of the report. None of the papers shows any knowledge of the Cassalanters.

Read the following aloud when the members leave with the files:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The folder in your hands holds a page on Mattrim with his usual evenings written in a neat column, and below it two household names with their daily routes. A letter at the bottom gives instructions for collecting reports at the customs house, signed with the name Beldan Rusk.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt waits in the upstairs room at the Portal in the evening, or the next morning if the members come back late. Bonnie and Mattrim are with him. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sits with a fresh jug at his elbow and the members' account laid out beside it. Bonnie stands behind the chair across from him, and Mattrim sits near the window with his lute across his knees.
>
> > "Edric is out of the crew as of now, caught or not. Say it to the others before they meet a face they know."

If **Edric Captured** is marked, Bonnie speaks first. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie listens to the end, folds her arms and lets out a breath.
>
> > "Well, shit. He's in a cell and I'm glad of it. I'll tell the others tonight. Thank you for bringing him in alive."

If Edric escaped, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie listens to the end and then draws a fresh sheet toward her, writing the names of her four colleagues in a neat column and leaving Edric's off it.
>
> > "He won't work with us again. If you see him, tell me, and I'll make sure the others don't mistake him for someone we've agreed to meet."

If **Bonnie Harper Operative** is marked, she adds that she will watch the Portal for him and send word through the members. Mark **Harper M3 Complete** once Bonnie has spoken.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> This Event awards no base Renown or gold, because both were paid in **The Doppelganger Auditions**. Each participating Harper member gains the following bonus when the party meets its condition. Companions gain no Renown.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the safehouse is found and its address or files are reported to Mirt. This bonus can be earned only once.

### Aftermath

Bonnie bars Edric from all crew work and tells her four colleagues what happened. If **Edric Report Delivered** is marked, his report stays with the Splinter, and **Kolat Towers** (unconverted) decides what its personnel do with the faces and affiliations in it. The report adds no defenders and no knowledge of abilities the members have not shown. If it was not delivered, the papers in the house are all the Splinter holds on the members.

If Edric is held, the members hear nothing more about him. If the files went to Mirt, he moves the two noble-house contacts and thanks the members in his own plain way.

### Concluding the Event

Bonnie's exclusion of Edric settles the crew, whether or not he is in custody.

## Overview

The morning after the interviews, Edric Tanner tails the party or leaves for his buyer. The party follows him to a Trades Ward safehouse and reads what it holds.

## Summary

We followed Edric through the Trades Ward until he led us to a house on Nethpranter Street. We took what was inside to Mirt, and Bonnie barred Edric from her crew, though what he had already told the Splinter could not be taken back.
