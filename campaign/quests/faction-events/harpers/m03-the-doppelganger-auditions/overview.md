# The Doppelganger Auditions: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Available to a Harper member at Renown 5 and 4th level after **The Dead Drop**. Companions can assist the interviews and investigation without joining the Harpers.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 4th-level characters.*
>
> Edric uses the ordinary 2024 **Doppelganger**, with two **Spies** at the optional safehouse. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** audits separate and combined encounters for three, four and five participating combatants.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

At 17:00 Mirt visits the eligible members at the Yawning Portal and asks what they make of five doppelgangers who work with Bonnie. Mattrim has recommended the crew, and Mirt wants the members to hear them out before he decides what relationship the Harpers should offer.

## Background

Bonnie leads a six-person crew: herself, Edric Tanner, Kael, Syla, the Scholar and the Merchant. Edric joined two months ago and supplies information to the Manshoon Splinter, including a report that identifies Mattrim as a Harper agent. Bonnie confirmed his betrayal three weeks ago after checking a tipped-off operation, and Mattrim learned a week ago that she knew of trouble, though not where it came from.

Bonnie has held off expelling Edric while she traces his contacts, and she also wants to see how the prospective Harper clients judge him. She protects her crew's identities and jobs whether or not she takes a role with the Harpers. Edric uses the interviews to watch the participating characters, and his report doesn't treat someone who merely helps a Harper as a member.

## Meeting the Crew

Bonnie reserves the upstairs room for two evenings, with Kael and Syla interviewed on the first and Edric, the Scholar and the Merchant on the second. Mattrim makes the introductions and stays nearby, and Bonnie asks the interviewers how they plan to protect the information she shares.

## Asking for Evidence

The party can test Edric's claimed work with concrete cargo questions, examine the false teamster's hands or pick up on his rehearsed answers in conversation. Bonnie confirms specific evidence when pressed, and Mattrim adds afterward, in private, that she has been investigating on her own.

## The Recommendation and Its Consequences

Mirt hears the members' recommendations in private and, once Edric is excluded, offers the four remaining colleagues a limited arrangement. Bonnie can negotiate a full operative role on her own terms. If Edric is still unidentified, **The Tail** begins the next morning and gives the party another chance to expose him and reach his safehouse.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Harper member gains 3 base Renown for completing the five interviews and delivering a considered report. Every contributing character, assisting companions included, receives the shared assessment fee of 50 GP.

- **+1 Renown:** identify Edric and report him to Mirt before his interview report is delivered at 09:00 the next morning.
- **+1 Renown:** recruit Bonnie as a full Harper operative after establishing Edric's betrayal and making the case for her independent investigation.
- **+1 Renown:** find the Trades Ward safehouse and report its operational files or address to Mirt. This bonus applies once across both events.

## Aftermath

Bonnie excludes Edric from her crew before **Harper M3 Complete** is marked, whether he's captured, released or still at large. The other four keep their jobs and their choice about future work. **The Doppelganger Problem** reads the completed outcome as a crew whose traitor is already gone.

Reports Edric has already delivered stay with the Splinter. A recovered safehouse file lets Mirt move the exposed contacts, though the Splinter still knows about Mattrim and the characters Edric observed during the interviews. **A Friend's House** becomes available at Renown 8 and 5th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): commissions an assessment and negotiates the resulting arrangement.
- **Mattrim Mereg** (Harpers): introduces his friends and shares what he learned about Bonnie's investigation.
- **Bonnie**: leads the crew and judges whether the Harpers can be trusted with their identities.
- **Edric Tanner** (Manshoon Splinter): impersonates a teamster and reports on Harper contacts.
- **Kael**, **Syla**, **the Scholar** and **the Merchant**: Edric's four colleagues, each with different motives and none of them a traitor.

## Dangers & Enemies

Edric bargains or runs before he fights. Two Spies guard the safehouse on Nethpranter Street in the Trades Ward, and the party can use the site as a lead by watching it and reporting, without going inside.

## Overview

The party interviews Bonnie's colleagues at the Yawning Portal, deciding whom they would trust while Bonnie asks what the Harpers would expect of them.
