# The Doppelganger Auditions

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins with Mirt's personal visit and ends with the members' recommendation, or passes into **The Tail** if Edric remains unidentified. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Interview five colleagues across two evenings without disclosing their identities to the public room.
> - Test Edric's claimed work through three independent approaches.
> - Learn why Bonnie held back her own findings and negotiate her relationship with the Harpers.
> - Intercept Edric before his new report leaves, or continue the investigation during his surveillance.

### The Brief

Mirt finds the eligible Harper members at the Yawning Portal at 17:00 and takes them to the upstairs private room. He briefs members only, and companions join afterward if the members want help interviewing the crew.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mattrim is playing beside the entrance when Mirt comes in, and the big man stops to listen to half a verse before he turns toward your table, one hand resting on the stair rail.
>
> > "That's the worst thing I've heard all week, Mattrim, and I fucking love it. Come on, you lot, upstairs. I can't talk business over a lute."
>
> In the private room he pulls a chair up to the table, waits for the door to close and sets his notes down. When he speaks again his voice is low and level.
>
> > "Mattrim wants us working with Bonnie's people. I've said no twice and he keeps asking. So you'll hear them, all five she brings, and tell me what arrangement you'd trust. Recommend nothing before you've heard them."

> [!social]**Mirt's Second Opinion**
>
> Mirt keeps the assessment in the private room and doesn't share Mattrim's recommendation with the members. He wants their own judgment and hasn't settled on an answer himself. He doesn't ask them to endorse the crew before they've met it, and his one worry is covered below.

> [!qna]**What worries you?**
>
> > "They can change faces. That's useful to us and it's dangerous to us. I want to know what they expect from the work and whether they keep to an agreement. Mattrim likes them. I need a second view."

> [!gamemaster]**The Existing Report**
>
> Edric has already told the Splinter that Mattrim is a Harper agent. The account of these interviews isn't filed yet, and he delivers it at 09:00 on the morning after the second evening unless someone intercepts him. It describes the characters present and names only the affiliations they disclosed or that Edric verified, so helping companions aren't recorded as Harpers.

### The First Evening

Mattrim introduces the party to Bonnie at 18:30. She has reserved the upstairs room and schedules Kael for 19:00 and Syla for 19:30, half an hour each, though Kael cuts his short.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mattrim carries his lute up the stairs behind you while Bonnie clears cups off the table and checks that the door shuts properly. She sets down a fresh jug and a clean sheet of paper within your reach, then turns to face you.
>
> > "Ask whatever you need to, love, but I'd like to know who ends up holding your notes afterward, especially if you're writing down where everyone works."
>
> Mattrim drags a chair over beside the window and settles with the lute across his knees.
>
> > "They're my friends, so I'm keeping my mouth shut while you talk to them, which will be a first, but please give them time to answer, because I've made enough of a mess explaining this myself."

> [!social]**Bonnie's Assessment**
>
> Bonnie (Neutral, Doppelganger, she/her) :: a working barmaid who protects the crew's employment while considering a new client.
>
> Bonnie keeps her familiar barmaid guise in the public room and talks about shapeshifting only upstairs. She asks who will hold the notes, how names will be protected and whether saying no would cost anyone their current job. Clear answers satisfy her, and she doesn't ask for recruitment in return for access. Any character who watches her during the interviews and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that she studies the interviewers' judgment as closely as she studies her colleagues. The check doesn't point to Edric.

> [!qna]**Are you applying too?**
>
> > "I'm deciding whether we'd work well together. The others can speak for themselves, and I'd rather you heard them without me jumping in on every question."

> [!qna]**How many are in your crew?**
>
> > "There are six of us, counting me, and you'll meet the other five over these two evenings before we sit down and talk about what you think would work."

> [!qna]**What protection do you need?**
>
> Bonnie turns the clean sheet over so the blank side faces the room.
>
> > "Keep the names up here and off the tables downstairs, please, and tell me what happens if someone says no. Do they keep the job they came in with? I need that answer before I ask any of them to trust you with more."

> [!social]**Kael, the Retired Sailor**
>
> Kael presents as a tall, angular sailor. He keeps his coat on and leaves at 19:20 whatever the interview has covered. He talks about old ship work easily enough but doesn't want Harper membership and has only come because Bonnie asked. Any character who watches him and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check confirms that he's simply uninterested and isn't hiding loyalty to an enemy. Asking him directly whether he wants the work gets the same answer without a check.

> [!qna]**Why did you come?**
>
> > "Bonnie asked me to hear you out. I've got work already, and I won't have another employer telling me where to be after dark. If that's what this is, count me out."

> [!qna]**What was your ship work?**
>
> Kael pushes his sleeve back from the cup, though he keeps his coat on.
>
> > "I sailed for years before I retired. I know how to hold my place in a crew. Taking another man's orders is what I'm done with."

At 19:20, Kael closes the interview:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Kael gets up, tugs his coat straight and leaves his cup untouched beside the jug as he turns toward the door.
>
> > "I've heard you, and I don't want the work. Tell Bonnie I came, like she asked. I've got somewhere to be tonight, so I'll leave you to the next one."
>
> His boots go down the stairs, and the door to the common room opens and swings shut behind him.

> [!social]**Syla, the Merchant's Widow**
>
> Syla presents as a middle-aged Tethyrian widow and asks about each responsibility before she answers anything. She doesn't know yet what life she wants, and she asks whether the Harpers would take limited work without trying to control her other identities. Any character who listens to her and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check confirms that her uncertainty is genuine. Concrete terms get her talking about them without a check.

> [!qna]**Would you accept the work?**
>
> > "What would I have to give up to do it? I don't just mean the hours, although those matter too, because I'd also want to know whether you'd expect me to use the same name every time I met one of you."

### The Second Evening

Bonnie brings Edric at 19:00 the following evening, the Scholar at 19:30 and the Merchant at 20:00. Each gets half an hour, and Bonnie stays available afterward to talk over the party's observations.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie holds the door for the next guest and nods toward the empty chair beside your notes.
>
> > "This is Edric Tanner. He's been with us two months. You've got until half past, and then the next one comes up."
>
> Edric takes the chair opposite you, sets his cup down beside the jug and looks around the table with a friendly grin.
>
> > "Mind if I pour while we talk? I gather you're curious about the Dock Ward routes, so have you been down that way lately, or is the work you've got in mind somewhere else?"
>
> He tops up his cup and leans back before he goes on.
>
> > "If you've ever been stuck behind a wagon down there, you've probably watched me trying to get the bloody thing moving. What would you want from someone who knows those streets? I'll say now, not everybody's glad to see me asking questions."

> [!social]**Edric's Teamster Cover**
>
> Edric Tanner (Doppelganger, he/him) :: a Splinter informant presenting as a cheerful Dock Ward teamster.
>
> Edric has been in the crew for two months. He talks routes and cargo with practiced warmth and asks the party about their assignments in return, and his interest is an observer's survey of them and not real work. He claims to know nothing about Harper operations until someone tells him a detail. An unsupported accusation won't make him confess. If the party presents specific evidence, he asks for a private arrangement instead of arguing on.

> [!exploration]**Three Ways to Test the Cover**
>
> - **Practical work:** Any character who asks what Edric personally does when a loaded cart wheel sticks gets a description of a route and not of unloading or repairing. Asking again for the steps he takes shows that he can't describe the work. These concrete follow-up questions need no check.
> - **Conversation:** Any character who listens to his answers about supposed experiences and makes a successful DC 16 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that they sound rehearsed, in the same wording every time he's asked. This exposes a false work history, though an odd mannerism alone isn't proof of treachery.
> - **Hands:** Any character who examines Edric's offered hands and makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds no callus pattern matching his claimed daily work. The mismatch comes from the guise he chose, since a Doppelganger could copy calluses if he thought of it.
>
> Any of these findings gives Bonnie something specific to compare with her own evidence. After a failed check, practical questioning and Bonnie's conversation are still open.

> [!qna]**What do you do when a loaded wheel sticks?**
>
> Edric draws a finger through a wet ring beside his cup, tracing the turn of a street.
>
> > "You want the wider turn by the wagon yard, because the carts bunch up when somebody cuts the corner and then the drivers behind start shouting. I try to keep clear of that stretch myself."

> [!qna]**What steps do you take to free it?**
>
> Edric takes his finger off the table and reaches for the jug again.
>
> > "Well, it depends which wagon you've got, and mostly I just tell the driver where he should've turned. I couldn't give you the repair steps from here without knowing more about the wagon."

> [!qna]**What do you know about Harper operations?**
>
> > "Honestly, I only know what you're telling me tonight, since nobody's given me your assignments. What would you want somebody working those streets to know?"

> [!social]**The Scholar**
>
> The Scholar presents as a young academic and answers warmly until someone asks what the work should pay. Any character who watches him and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check finds that he's weighing the commercial value of Harper affiliation and isn't hiding an enemy employer. If asked for his terms directly, he honestly asks for payment by the job, with no promise of permanent membership.

> [!qna]**What would make you accept?**
>
> > "I'd want to be paid for the work and told clearly what I'm agreeing to. I've heard plenty about doing things for the right reasons, but I've still got rent to pay."

> [!social]**The Merchant**
>
> The Merchant presents as a Dock Ward import dealer who likes predictable arrangements. He talks about deliveries, expenses and notice periods and has no hidden agenda. He agrees to occasional paid work if Bonnie confirms the client is reliable. He offers cooperation because the arrangement suits him, and he doesn't pretend to a devotion he hasn't developed.

> [!qna]**Can we rely on you?**
>
> > "You can count on me to do what we've agreed, so long as I know the terms before I start. Bonnie's kept our work steady, and I'm happy to hear what she recommends."

### Bonnie's Findings

At 20:30, Bonnie asks to hear the party's observations in the order they made them. If the party names Edric and gives a concrete cover mismatch from above, she discloses her investigation. If they offer only vague suspicion, she asks for a specific example, and every evidence route stays open.

> [!readaloud]
>
> After the last guest has gone, Bonnie sits down, moves the spare cups away from your notes and keeps the interview list in front of her.
>
> > "Which interview do you want to start with? Tell me what you noticed while each of them was talking, and we'll go through it in the order it happened."

If the party names Edric with a concrete cover mismatch, Bonnie continues:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie folds her hands on the table and waits until you've finished, then turns the interview list around so that Edric's name faces you.
>
> > "Three weeks ago one of our jobs got tipped off, and I found a payment afterward. It led to Edric. I've been trying to trace who took his reports, and I wanted to see whether you'd find the same trouble before I trusted you with it."

If the party offers only vague suspicion, use this response instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie leaves the interview list where it is, draws your notes toward her and runs a finger down the entries before sliding them back to you.
>
> > "What did he say or do that I can check against what I know? Feeling uneasy about someone doesn't tell me which part of his story failed."

> [!qna]**Why didn't you tell Mattrim?**
>
> > "He knew I was checking something, but I hadn't given him Edric's name, because I wasn't going to have the whole crew questioned in the middle of the fucking bar while I still had a chance of finding the buyer."

### Mattrim and the Recommendation

At 20:45, after Bonnie's conversation, Mattrim takes the participating interviewers aside, whether or not they identified Edric. He learned a week ago that Bonnie had found a betrayal, but he doesn't know which colleague she suspected unless the party has told him.

> [!readaloud]
>
> When the last footsteps have gone down the stairs, Mattrim puts the lute in its case and pulls his chair up beside yours. His voice stays as easy as ever, though he takes longer over each thing he says.
>
> > "I think one of them's told somebody about us, or about who I work for, anyway. Bonnie said a week ago she'd found some trouble and was checking it out, but she never gave me a name."
>
> > "Shit, I should've pushed harder. Whatever you recommend, I'll hear it, but I wanted you to know she was trying to protect them, even if she should've brought us in sooner."

Mirt receives the members at his residence at 21:30, or at 09:00 the next morning if they put the visit off. A report that arrives at 09:00 is too late for the pre-delivery bonus unless Edric has already been intercepted.

> [!gamemaster]**Mirt's Decisions**
>
> If the party has identified Edric, Mirt asks Bonnie to exclude him at once and offers Kael, Syla, the Scholar and the Merchant occasional work through her, with no automatic enrollment in the Harpers. Kael declines, Syla holds her answer, and the Scholar and the Merchant accept paid work. Bonnie gets a neutrality pact unless the party makes the case for a fuller role.
>
> Any character who argues for Bonnie's role and makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check, or who makes a direct case citing her three-week investigation and her specific protective conduct, persuades Mirt to offer her an operative role. Bonnie accepts only if her crew's identities stay protected, intelligence goes through the participating Harper members and she can decline jobs. Mirt agrees to those terms, and the party decides whether to recommend that relationship and keep it going.
>
> If Edric is still unidentified, Mirt offers the five colleagues a provisional arrangement through Bonnie without enrolling them. Edric delivers his report at 09:00 and begins **The Tail**. Don't mark **Harper M3 Complete** until that investigation, or Bonnie's later exclusion of him, settles his place in the crew.

If the members persuade Mirt to offer the operative role through the stated check or direct evidence, he makes this offer:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt lays Bonnie's account of the payment beside the proposed terms, leaves both sheets within her reach and turns to face her.
>
> > "You traced the betrayal and tried to protect your crew while you did it. I'd like you working with us as an operative. Their names stay protected, and reports come through these members. You can turn down any job that puts them at risk."

If Bonnie accepts the operative arrangement, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie reads Mirt's written terms, rests a finger under the line for reports and leaves the agreement unsigned beside her cup.
>
> > "Reports go through these people, my crew's names stay protected, and I can say no when a job would expose them. Is that what you're offering? I want to be sure before I put my name to it."
>
> Mirt adds the report route beneath the existing terms and turns the sheet back toward her.
>
> > "You can say no, and their names are protected under what's written here. Reports come through the members we're working with. That's in the agreement now."
>
> Bonnie signs, folds the second copy so the names are inside and hands it to you.
>
> > "I'll bring you what we learn, and I'll tell you when a job puts the others at risk. I'd like the same from you before you ask us to take one."
>
> > "When can we meet upstairs at the Portal to settle the first piece of work? I'll want something specific before I take it to the others."

If the crew keeps the neutrality pact, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets the proposed jobs to one side while Bonnie checks the clause that protects her crew's names, and he leaves the signed copy beside her cup.
>
> > "Their names stay protected. We'll bring individual jobs through these members, and you can decide whether the terms suit you."
>
> Bonnie folds her copy, stands beside the chair and checks that the names are inside before she slips it into her pocket.
>
> > "I'll hear those jobs when you have them. We haven't agreed to join you, but we can look at work that keeps the others safe."
>
> She takes the signed copy with her and heads for the stairs, leaving Mirt's copy beside the proposed jobs on the table.

When Bonnie passes an occasional-work offer to her four remaining colleagues, each answers according to the choice made in the interviews:

> [!qna]**Kael's answer?**
>
> > "No, thank you. I've got the work I want, and this hasn't changed my mind. Bonnie knows where to find me for what we already do together."

> [!qna]**Syla's answer?**
>
> > "I'd like to keep the terms and think them over. I haven't decided what life I want next, and I don't want to promise you anything before I have."

> [!qna]**The Scholar's answer?**
>
> > "Yes, I'll take it, as long as I'm paid by the job and the terms are settled before I start. Send it through Bonnie so we both know what I've agreed to."

> [!qna]**The Merchant's answer?**
>
> > "Bonnie's happy with the client, and the terms cover what I need. I'll take occasional work through her, provided I get the notice we agreed."

If Edric is identified, Mirt makes the exclusion part of either arrangement:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt draws a line under Edric's name and leaves the other four on the assessment sheet.
>
> > "Edric's out of the crew as of now. We can offer the others occasional work through you, no membership required. Give them the terms and let them choose."

If Edric remains unidentified, Mirt makes only a provisional offer:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt keeps all five interview names on the sheet and slides the proposed arrangement across to Bonnie.
>
> > "We'll try occasional work through Bonnie first. Nobody's enrolled by taking a job, and we'll review it once we've seen how it goes."

### Intercepting Edric

If the party identified Edric during the interviews, they can confront him before 09:00. If he's still free, he leaves the Portal at 08:30 carrying the folded report to the safehouse on Nethpranter Street. Following him uses the address and files from **The Tail**, though without that event's premise that his report has already been delivered.

> [!gamemaster]**Outcomes for the Direct Investigation**
>
> When the party uses those sections, record **Edric Captured**, **Nethpranter Safehouse Reported** and **Harper Contacts Relocated** from **The Tail** whenever their conditions are met, even though you skip that event's ordinary opening. At 09:00, mark **Edric Report Delivered** unless the party intercepted him first, and that includes the case where Edric was identified but stays free. **Edric Identified** records the finding by itself, while **Edric Report Prevented** and **Edric Report Delivered** record what the Splinter received.

> [!hazard]**An Optional Doppelganger Encounter**
>
> Edric uses the standard 2024 **Doppelganger**, with no extra phases or HP resets. If he's physically cornered, he bargains for release and a thirty-second head start in exchange for the address.
>
> #### Edric's Tactics
> Edric begins near the nearest available exit, and his clothes and the report he carries stay with him through every change of guise.
>
> During combat, Edric:
> - Uses Unsettling Visage against pursuers and changes guise with his Bonus Action when the crowd offers concealment.
> - Takes Disengage and moves through an unblocked exit rather than staying to attack.
> - Uses his two Slams against the nearest conscious blocker only when no escape route remains.
>
> The Doppelganger's printed first-round Advantage applies once in the encounter. He surrenders if his exit stays blocked and he falls to 10 HP or fewer. The encounter also ends with an agreed release, a successful escape or his defeat. Changing shape doesn't Hide him or change his clothing.

### Renown Opportunities

Each participating Harper member gains 3 base Renown for the interviews and the considered report. Mirt pays every contributing character 50 GP as an assessment fee, helping companions included.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets a row of purses beside the assessment sheet, counts one out for everybody who helped with the interviews and pushes them across the table.
>
> > "Fifty gold for each contributor, and that includes the people who helped without joining us. Make sure they get theirs. You did what I asked."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Bonuses**
>
> Add +1 Renown for identifying and reporting Edric before the 09:00 delivery, +1 for Bonnie's accepting the full operative arrangement and +1 for finding the safehouse and reporting its files or address. Each goes to participating Harper members only, and the safehouse bonus can't be earned a second time in **The Tail**.

### Aftermath

Bonnie excludes Edric as soon as the evidence identifies him. She tells her four remaining colleagues and bars him from their private meetings and crew work, and she doesn't need him captured to do it. If he's still at large after **The Tail**, she excludes him once she hears, and the reports he has already delivered stay with the Splinter.

### Concluding the Event

The recommendation settles the crew's arrangement. If Edric is still unidentified, the investigation into him continues in the following event.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Edric Identified** — mark when concrete interview evidence or later surveillance establishes his betrayal; Bonnie reads this finding, while later events check the separate report outcome for delivery.
> - **Edric Report Prevented** — mark when interception stops the 09:00 delivery; later Splinter encounters keep his older report on Mattrim but lack this new interview account.
> - **Bonnie Harper Operative** — mark when she accepts Mirt's full terms; later Harper briefings can request Portal intelligence through the participating members.
> - **Bonnie Neutrality Pact** — mark when she accepts protected identities and optional paid work without membership; later requests leave the choice to her.
> - **Harper M3 Complete** — mark after the assessment is reported and Bonnie has excluded Edric from her crew, whether he was captured or escaped; **The Doppelganger Problem** reads his absence.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> If Edric is still unidentified, continue with **The Tail** at 09:30 the next morning. Otherwise its safehouse material stays available if the party follows his lead. **A Friend's House** becomes available at Renown 8 and 5th level, and this mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Five members of Bonnie's crew meet the party over two evenings to discuss what they would expect from Harper work.

## Summary

We interviewed Bonnie's colleagues and compared their answers before recommending an arrangement to Mirt. Whether Edric's new report was stopped, and whether Bonnie agreed to become a Harper operative, depended on what we found.
