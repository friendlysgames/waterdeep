# The Doppelganger Auditions

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Harper member reaches Renown 5 and 4th level after **The Dead Drop**, and it ends when the members have reported to Mirt. Mirt briefs them in person at the Yawning Portal, and in this Event the party can:
>
> - Hear Mirt warn them that one of the five doppelgangers has already sold the party's Harper affiliation.
> - Interview Kael and Syla on the first evening, and Edric, the Scholar and Bonnie on the second, while Mattrim plays at the next table.
> - Catch Edric's cover story through four independent tells.
> - Hear what Mattrim and Bonnie each know, and tell Bonnie what they noticed.
> - Report to Mirt, collect 50 gp each, and offer Bonnie a place with the Harpers on her own terms.
>
> Only Harper members attend Mirt's brief and report. Companions can sit at the next table for every interview and help with everything else.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Bonnie leads five doppelgangers: herself, Edric Tanner, Kael, Syla and the Scholar. They came to Waterdeep over a year ago and live on honest wages.
> - Edric joined two months ago and sells what he learns to the Splinter. He has already told his buyer that Mattrim is a Harper agent. He will deliver a report on the interviewers at 09:00 tomorrow, at a house in the Trades Ward.
> - Bonnie traced a payment from a tipped-off job to Edric three weeks ago. She has kept quiet because she wants to see whether the Harpers can catch him, and because she wants to know who will hold her crew's names.
> - Mattrim learned a week ago that Bonnie had found trouble. She has not told him who.
> - Kael, Syla and the Scholar are not traitors. None of the three will be recruited, and Mirt does not ask about them.
> - In the common room Bonnie wears her Tethyrian barmaid form and never talks about what she is. The others wear human guises.

### The Brief

Mirt finds each eligible member in the common room of the Yawning Portal on the evening after they qualify. He briefs members only, and companions wait at the hearth. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The common room of the Yawning Portal is loud tonight, with adventurers shouting over dice and Durnan's barmaids pushing trays through the crowd. Mattrim Mereg sits on a stool by the hearth, picking at a lute with three strings. At a back table near the stairs, Mirt lifts a hand and pushes a chair out with his foot.
>
> > "There you are, lads and lasses, and about time, because this jug won't empty itself. Bring your cups upstairs. I can't talk business over that lute."

When the members reach the upstairs room, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The upstairs room has a table and six chairs, and the noise from the common room comes through the floor as a low hum. Mirt fills each of your cups before he sits and turns his own a quarter turn on the wood. He is not smiling.
>
> > "Mattrim Mereg is one of ours. He has made friends with five doppelgangers who live in this city, and he wants us to recruit them. Speak to each one and tell me which arrangement you would trust."
>
> He looks at each of you in turn before he goes on.
>
> > "You should hear this from me. In my considered opinion, one of the five has already sold the news that you are Harpers. I don't know which one. That is part of what you are there to find out."

> [!social]**The Moneylender Upstairs**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a senior Harper who plays the jovial moneylender downstairs and drops the act when the door closes.
>
> Mirt uses his business voice: short sentences, no swearing, and a glass refilled whenever one runs low. He calls the members "lad" and "lass". He does not name the doppelganger he suspects, because he does not want the members to follow his guess.
>
> Mirt is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What he wants from the interviews, which is a second opinion.
> - Mattrim, and why Mirt has said no to him twice.
> - What the Harpers could use a crew of shapeshifters for.
> - The fee, and what he expects in the report.
>
> He will not discuss which of the five he suspects, the Stone of Golorr, or any Harper office.

> [!qna]**What do you want from us?**
>
> > "A second opinion. Mattrim likes these people, and I need someone who doesn't yet. Talk to each of them and tell me what arrangement you would trust."

> [!qna]**Why would the Harpers want doppelgangers?**
>
> > "They can change faces, lad, and that is worth a great deal to us. It is also the reason I don't trust them yet."

> [!qna]**Who sold us out?**
>
> > "I don't know. I have an opinion and I'm keeping it. Watch how each of them answers, and notice who was ready for your questions."

> [!qna]**What do we get?**
>
> > "Fifty gold for everyone who helps, companions included. The Harpers remember who does good work."

### Threestrings at the Hearth

Mattrim arranges both evenings. Each interview takes place at the back table by the cellar stair, where the common-room noise covers a quiet conversation, and he plays at the hearth close enough to listen. Read the following aloud when the members first speak to him:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mattrim finishes a verse, tucks the lute under one arm and crosses to your table with a lopsided smile. He sits and lowers his voice, though the room is loud enough that nobody could overhear.
>
> > "Evening, friends. Mirt's told you the shape of it. They're my friends, so be kind, because they'll know if you aren't and they'll stop being honest. I'll be at the hearth playing something terrible, and I'll be listening."

> [!social]**The Bard by the Door**
>
> Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg (Lawful Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a Harper agent who plays three strings badly on purpose and has spent two years befriending Bonnie's crew.
>
> Mattrim is friendly and a little rueful, and he plucks a string while he thinks. He talks about the crew warmly and steers away from anything that sounds like an accusation. He swears mildly and at himself. He does not know which of them sold the Harpers out.
>
> Mattrim is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The crew's jobs and how long he has known them.
> - Bonnie, whom he vouches for.
> - Why he wants the Harpers to take them on.
>
> He will not discuss what any of them are while the common room is full.

> [!qna]**What do they do for a living?**
>
> > "Work, friend. Bonnie pours for Durnan, Kael does odd jobs on the docks, Syla keeps accounts for a widow's trade, the Scholar copies for a bookseller, and Edric drives a cart, he says. Five of them, rent paid."

> [!qna]**How long have you known them?**
>
> Mattrim plucks the lowest string and lets it ring.
>
> > "Two years of drinks and one bad winter. I know what they are and I like them anyway. Buy a bard a drink and he'll tell you anything. Well. Something."

> [!qna]**Is Bonnie in charge?**
>
> > "She is, and she'll interview you as hard as you interview her. Don't be rude to any of them. I mean it."

### The First Evening

Kael arrives first and Syla follows. Each interview runs about twenty minutes. Read the following aloud when Kael sits down:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A tall, thin man with grey stubble and a sailor's rolling walk crosses the common room to the back table. He sits across from you with his arms folded and says nothing until one of you speaks. Behind him, Mattrim starts a slow tune at the hearth.

> [!social]**The Retired Sailor**
>
> Kael (Neutral, Doppelganger, he/him) :: a tall, angular man in a sailor's coat who came because Bonnie asked.
>
> Kael answers in short sentences and keeps his coat on. He leaves when the twenty minutes are up, whatever the members have asked. He wants no Harper work and does not hide it. His habit is to end an argument with "Not my watch."
>
> Kael is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Why he came, and why he is leaving.
> - His odd jobs on the docks.
> - Whether he would take Harper work, which he would not.
>
> He will not discuss what he is, and he says so flatly.
>
> Any character who watches him and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that he is uninterested and is not hiding loyalty to anyone. Asking him directly whether he wants the work gives the same answer without a check.

> [!qna]**Why did you come?**
>
> > "Bonnie asked. I've got my work and no wish for another master. Not my watch."

> [!qna]**Would you take Harper work?**
>
> > "No. Tell her I came, like I said I would."

> [!qna]**What do you do on the docks?**
>
> > "Haul, mend, fetch. I sailed for years, so I know how to take a post. I'm done with taking orders from men I don't know."

Read the following aloud when the twenty minutes are up:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Kael stands, tugs his coat straight and leaves his cup untouched beside the jug. He nods once to the table and walks toward the door through the crowd, and the chair across from you stays empty for a moment before a woman sits down in it.

> [!social]**The Merchant's Widow**
>
> Syla (Neutral, Doppelganger, she/her) :: a middle-aged Tethyrian woman in good, plain clothes who keeps the books for a trade widow.
>
> Syla answers most questions with a question, and she means them. She is using the interview as much as the members are. Her habit is to say "And if I said yes?" when she wants terms. She has not decided what life she wants.
>
> Syla is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What Harper work would ask of her.
> - Whether she would keep her other names.
> - Why she came to Waterdeep.
>
> She will not discuss what she is, or whether she trusts the rest of the crew.
>
> Any character who listens to her and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that her uncertainty is real and she is not hiding anything. Offering concrete terms gets her talking about them without a check.

> [!qna]**Would you take the work?**
>
> > "What would I have to give up? And if I said yes, would I use the same name every time I met one of you?"

> [!qna]**Why did you come to Waterdeep?**
>
> > "Because it was large enough to disappear in, and then I found I didn't want to. Isn't that a strange thing to want?"

### The Second Evening

Edric comes first, then the Scholar. Bonnie speaks to the members last, after closing. Read the following aloud when Edric sits down:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A stocky man in a leather jerkin drops into the chair and pours himself a cup from your jug before anyone offers. He grins around the table like a man who has found three old friends. At the hearth, Mattrim's tune slows a little.
>
> > "Right, so you're the ones Mattrim's been talking about. Mind if I pour? Edric Tanner, Dock Ward carts, twelve years. What would you want from someone who knows those streets?"

> [!social]**The Cheerful Teamster**
>
> Edric Tanner (Neutral Evil, Doppelganger, he/him) :: a warm, talkative man who joined Bonnie's crew two months ago and sells what he learns to the Splinter.
>
> Edric begins nearly every answer with "Right," and tells a story with the same shape each time. He asks the members about their assignments while he claims to know nothing. He never loses his temper, and an unsupported accusation only makes him laugh. If the members confront him with evidence at the table, he stays pleasant, finishes his cup and leaves by the door.
>
> Edric is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - His routes and cargo.
> - The Dock Ward.
> - What he knows about the Harpers, which he says is nothing.
>
> He will not discuss Bonnie's investigation, which he does not know about, or any buyer.

> [!qna]**What do you carry?**
>
> > "Right, so mostly salt fish and rope out of the harbor yards. Twelve years of it. You'd know the route if you drove it."

> [!qna]**Tell us about the Dock Ward.**
>
> > "Right, it's warehouses and gulls, isn't it? Fillet Lane, Net Street, the Skewered Dragon on the corner. You can't miss any of it."

> [!qna]**What do you know about the Harpers?**
>
> > "Right, only what you tell me tonight, and that's nothing. What would you want a man on those streets to know? Who are you working for?"

> [!exploration]**Four Ways to Catch Edric**
>
> The cover has four independent weaknesses. Any one gives the members something to report, and two together are enough for Bonnie to act.
>
> - **Harper fishing:** Any character who asks what he knows about the Harpers notices that he denies everything and then asks what the members are working on. This needs no check.
> - **His Waterdeep:** Any character who asks him to describe the Dock Ward notices that he gives a list of streets with no people, no complaints and no favorite stall. It sounds like a survey and not like a man's home. This needs no check.
> - **Rehearsed answers:** Any character who watches him across several questions and makes a successful DC 16 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that his stories repeat in the same words and arrive a half-second early.
> - **His hands:** Any character who takes his hand, or watches him reach for the jug, and makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check sees no rope or rein calluses. A character who fails can try again when he pours.
>
> Edric reads surface thoughts at will, and he uses them only to choose his next question. It does not change any of his answers.

Read the following aloud when the Scholar sits down:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Edric leaves with a wave, and a young man in an ink-stained scholar's robe takes his chair with a leather satchel on his knee. He looks at your cups, then at the pin on whichever of you wears one, and smiles as if the evening has gone well.

> [!social]**The Copyist**
>
> The Scholar (Neutral, Doppelganger, he/him) :: a charming young copyist who sees a business in Harper favor.
>
> The Scholar is friendly and forthcoming about everything but his own interests. He names a price for everything, and his habit is to say "Call it forty a job." He is not a danger to anyone, and his loyalty goes to whoever pays.
>
> The Scholar is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the work would pay.
> - What a Harper pin could open in the city.
> - How much the crew has to lose.
>
> He will not discuss what he is, or whether he would sell the Harpers a favor for a better offer.
>
> Any character who watches him and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that he is weighing what Harper affiliation would be worth. Asking him his price directly gets it without a check.

> [!qna]**What would make you accept?**
>
> > "Pay by the job, terms settled before I start. Call it forty a job. I've heard a lot about doing the right thing, and I still have rent."

> [!qna]**What could a pin open?**
>
> > "In this city? Doors, I'd think, and a few of them worth walking through. I'd like to know which, before I say yes."

### After Last Call

When the common room has emptied, Bonnie locks the door of the stockroom behind the bar. She keeps her barmaid form but drops the tavern voice. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stockroom smells of barrels and lamp oil, and Bonnie sets a lantern on a cask before she sits on a crate across from you. She has been on her feet since noon, and she stretches her back before she speaks.
>
> > "Well. I do the evenings for Durnan and the nights for myself. Ask whatever you like, but I'd like to know who holds your notes afterward, because my people's names are on every page you write."

> [!social]**The Barmaid After Hours**
>
> Bonnie (Neutral, Doppelganger, she/her) :: the crew's leader, in the Tethyrian human form she wears to work, who is deciding whether the Harpers are worth her crew's names.
>
> In the stockroom Bonnie is flat, dry and practical, and she swears mildly. She asks as many questions as she answers. She never talks about what she is in the common room. She will not name the traitor, because she wants to see whether the members can.
>
> Bonnie is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Her crew's jobs, and what happens to them if they say no to the Harpers.
> - Who will keep the notes, and who will see the names.
> - What she needs from a client.
>
> She will not name the person who sold the Harpers out, or say how long she has known.
>
> Any character who watches how she asks her questions and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that she is judging the members as closely as they judged her crew.

> [!qna]**Are you applying too?**
>
> > "I'm deciding whether we'd work well together. The others can speak for themselves, and I'd rather you heard them without me jumping in."

> [!qna]**Do you suspect anyone?**
>
> Bonnie smiles without humor.
>
> > "That's what you're here for, isn't it? Tell me what you saw, and I'll tell you if it matches."

> [!qna]**What protection do you need?**
>
> > "My crew's names stay in your heads and off your paper. Anything you learn through us comes to the Harpers through you, not around you. And if any of us says no to a job, nobody holds it against us."

### What Mattrim Knows

After Bonnie lets the members go, Mattrim waits on the stairs with his lute in its case. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mattrim sits on the third stair with the lute case across his knees and waits for the stockroom door to close. When he speaks, his voice is as easy as ever, but he takes longer over each word.
>
> > "I think one of them told someone about you. About what you are. I don't know which one, and I've been over every conversation and keep coming up wrong."
>
> He plucks a string and silences it with his palm.
>
> > "Bonnie told me a week ago she'd found some trouble. She wouldn't say who. I think she's known for about three weeks, and she thinks she can fix it herself. She can't. Shit, I should have pushed her harder."

### What You Noticed

The members return to Bonnie, who waits for their account. She asks them to give it in order. Compare what they report with the four tells.

- **Two or more tells, or Edric named with Mattrim's help:** Bonnie confirms it. Mark **Edric Identified** and read the first branch.
- **One tell:** Bonnie says that is a start and asks what else they saw. She does not confirm anything. If the members add no more, read the second branch.
- **No tells, or a wrong name:** Bonnie listens without comment and changes the subject. Read the second branch.

If Edric is identified, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie listens to the end without moving and then turns the lantern a little so her face is in shadow. When she speaks, her voice has gone flat.
>
> > "Edric. I traced a payment from a tipped-off job to him three weeks ago. I wanted to see if you could do what I did. Well, shit. You did, and in two evenings."

Bonnie offers the members a choice. Edric eats breakfast at the Portal every morning before work, and she can sit him down at 08:00 and keep him there until Mirt takes him. She says that she can also let him walk, and the members can follow him to wherever he delivers his report.

- **Bonnie holds him:** Edric's report is never delivered, and **The Tail** does not occur. Mirt takes Edric the next morning and does not discuss what happens next. Mark **Harper M3 Complete** after the report to Mirt.
- **The members follow him:** continue with **The Tail** the next morning. Edric leaves the Portal at 08:30.

If the members have not named Edric, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie lets the lantern burn for a moment and then reaches for her keys. She does not look disappointed, and she does not look surprised either.
>
> > "Maybe I'll find out tomorrow how good you are. I won't make it easier for you. Faces are just work clothes, and the one you want hasn't taken his off yet."

Bonnie names nobody. **The Tail** begins at 09:30 the next morning, after Edric has delivered his report.

### The Report to Mirt

Mirt waits upstairs with a fresh jug and takes the members' account without interrupting. He asks who they would trust with a pin. Bonnie is the only member of the crew who passes. If a member recommends another doppelganger, Mirt asks them why and weighs their answer, and he does not recruit that person. Kael declines, Syla stays undecided and the Scholar wants pay by the job.

Read the following aloud when a member names Bonnie:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets down his cup and looks at the table for a moment, and the corner of his mouth lifts.
>
> > "Bonnie. Yes. I thought it might be Bonnie."
>
> He turns toward the door and calls down the stairs for her. When she arrives he rises, which he does for very few people, and pulls out a chair.
>
> > "Sit, Miss Bonnie. I have an offer, and you may refuse it."

Mirt offers Bonnie a place as a Harper operative with the Yawning Portal as her base. He does not name terms, and he asks her what she needs.

> [!qna]**What are your terms?**
>
> > "My crew's names stay with the three of you and nobody else. What I learn at the bar comes to you, and you bring me the work, not Mirt. And I can say no to any job that puts one of mine at risk."

> [!qna]**Can you refuse?**
>
> Bonnie glances at Mirt, who nods once.
>
> > "I can. I'm weighing it, and you'd all do better to let me."

> [!gamemaster]**Bonnie's Decision**
>
> Bonnie accepts if the members agree to all three of her terms: names protected, reports routed through the participating members, and the right to decline any job. Mirt agrees to them whatever the members say. Bonnie refuses if any member asks for the names or true faces of her crew, or presses her to enroll Kael, Syla or the Scholar. If she refuses, she stays a friendly contact, pours for the members at the Portal and passes them rumors, and **Bonnie Harper Operative** is not marked.
>
> If Edric is identified, Mirt tells the members that Bonnie will exclude him from the crew once he is held. If he is not, Bonnie says she will deal with him when the Harpers have shown they can.

Read the following aloud when Bonnie accepts:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie reads the terms Mirt has written, runs a finger beneath the line about her crew's names and nods. She picks up the pen and signs, then folds the second copy and hands it across the table.
>
> > "Reports go through you three. I'll tell you when a job puts the others at risk, and I'd like the same from you before you ask us to take one."

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt pays the fee before the members leave. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets a row of purses on the table, counts one out for each person who helped with the interviews and pushes them across with two fingers.
>
> > "Fifty gold each. That includes the people who helped without joining us. Make sure they get theirs."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains 3 base Renown for the interviews and the report. Every contributing character, companions included, receives 50 gp. Companions gain no Renown. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:
>
> - **+1 Renown:** **Edric Identified** is marked before his report is due at 09:00.
> - **+1 Renown:** Bonnie accepts the operative role and **Bonnie Harper Operative** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** the safehouse is found and reported. **The Tail** awards this bonus.
>
> Each bonus can be earned only once.

### Aftermath

Bonnie bars Edric from all crew work as soon as the members confirm him, wherever he is. The other three keep their jobs and their freedom to say no. Mattrim is relieved and a little competitive about having a Harper contact in his own tavern.

Edric's earlier report on Mattrim has already reached the Splinter and cannot be recalled.

### Concluding the Event

The report to Mirt ends the interviews. If Edric was not held, the pursuit continues in **The Tail** the next morning.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Edric Identified** — mark when Bonnie confirms the members' evidence against Edric; read by **The Tail**, which opens on the following-him branch when it is marked.
> - **Bonnie Harper Operative** — mark when Bonnie accepts Mirt's offer on her three terms; read by **The Tail**, where Bonnie answers the members as a Harper contact.
> - **Harper M3 Complete** — mark here only if Bonnie holds Edric at the Portal; otherwise **The Tail** marks it. Read by **The Doppelganger Problem** (Emerald Enclave Mission 3), which treats the traitor as already dealt with.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Continue with **The Tail** the next morning unless Bonnie is holding Edric. **A Friend's House** becomes available when an individual Harper member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level. This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Mirt asks the party to interview five doppelgangers who work with Mattrim's friend Bonnie and tell him which ones they would trust. One of them has already sold the Harpers out.

## Summary

We interviewed Bonnie's four colleagues over two evenings at the Yawning Portal while Mattrim played by the hearth. Mirt had warned us that one of the five had sold us, and we compared what we noticed with what Bonnie and Mattrim told us.
