# Design Notes: The Doppelganger Auditions

## The Interview as a Mutual Assessment

Bonnie asks who will hold the notes because taking on a client could expose everyone who works with her. The party judges the five colleagues, and its answers also shape the relationship she is willing to accept. She held back what she knew, and that has consequences, but it doesn't cancel the protective work she was doing.

Bonnie leads a crew of six before Edric is excluded, and she sits apart from the five interviews. Kael's disinterest, Syla's uncertainty and the Scholar's wish to be paid are all legitimate positions, and none of them is a sign of betrayal. The Merchant's readiness to keep a useful agreement gives the party another kind of trust to weigh.

## Concrete Evidence and Ordinary Monsters

The cargo questions, the false work history and the hands examination each support an accusation against Edric independently. Bonnie compares a concrete observation with her own payment evidence, so a failed check leaves questioning open and doesn't close the mystery. Mattrim adds his account after the interviews, which lets the party reach a judgment first.

Edric is the standard 2024 Doppelganger and not a boss with added phases. Shape-Shift changes his appearance but not his clothes, doesn't hide him and doesn't give him a fresh HP pool. The Strength (Athletics) check to block him keeps the original DC 13 and uses the skill's normal ability, and the fight is optional throughout.

## The Tail and Shared Intelligence

The Tail happens after the new report has left, so it gives a second chance to investigate without recalling intelligence that was already delivered. Shadowing him, the bargain and the folded dispatch each lead to the same Nethpranter Street address. The two Spies are a separate roster from Edric, and the branches where interception puts all three in one fight are audited in the mechanics reference.

The household files concern Lysa Fenwick and Teren Moss, the same contacts exposed in the bookshop cipher. Their relocation is recorded once, through Harper Contacts Relocated, and the safehouse bonus is earned once across the two events. The assessment fee is 50 GP per contributing character in both, while Harper Renown goes only to participating members.

## Completion and External Readers

Harper M3 Complete requires Bonnie to exclude Edric, whether he is captured or escapes. That gives The Doppelganger Problem the absence it expects, and the party doesn't have to kill or detain him. Both events name the outcome's intended later reader, and the companion documents outside this pass are unchanged.

Bonnie's three-week discovery and Mattrim's one-week awareness are different pieces of information and don't contradict each other. The notable-figure pages still carry older crew counts and ability wording. The approved mission plan and the verified 2024 mechanics govern these events, and those contradictions are recorded for later integration.
