# Design Notes: The Dead Drop

## Uza and Fillipa

***A precise account.*** Uza's detailed description keeps the monster identifiable without making her distress a joke, and her request gives the party a person and an animal to help before the intelligence investigation begins. The shared spellbook remains a gift to the assisting party regardless of Tessalar's fate, preserving a tangible reward without granting companions Harper membership.

***The rescue objective.*** Fillipa's rafters give one character something useful to do while others handle the creature or threatened stock, and her retrieval check costs an action on failure without adding arbitrary falling damage. Raising the attic's central ridge resolves the older conflict between an eight-foot ceiling and a cat ten feet above the floor, while disabling the gazer allows rescue without requiring its death.

## The Inventory Timer

***Saving throws and stock.*** The former inventory rule relied on an eye ray missing an attack roll, which its saving-throw rays never made. Three fixed end-of-round deadlines now threaten books, a staircase shelf and manuscripts, with concrete Utilize actions that protect each category. This makes the secondary objective visible before the loss occurs and gives the party ways to divide its effort beyond attacking a low-HP enemy.

***The converted creature.*** The Gazer has no 2024 *Monster Manual* entry, so its legacy source is converted explicitly in the **Harpers Mechanics Reference** rather than being mislabeled as a current published block. The shop uses the ordinary creature without a familiar bond, a fire ray or a remote observer, preserving its four rays while removing unsupported information transfer.

## What Tessalar Has Already Done

***The physical copy.*** Tessalar delivered the cipher before the gazer entered the shop, giving the breach a definite sequence and leaving evidence that doesn't depend on inventing a scrying effect. Turning him prevents future reports without retracting delivered intelligence, while warning Mirt starts the distinct forty-eight-hour relocation of Lysa Fenwick and Teren Moss.

***The three resolutions.*** Recruitment, arrest and warning-off remain different choices, with a cooperating double agent, a fixed destruction deadline or the end of his reporting respectively. None automatically kills Tessalar, and later missions must retain his recorded fate instead of assuming the party selected the recruitment route.

***The ledger's contents.*** Recovering, copying and destroying the ledger provide useful but different results, with additional identities available only from actually reading the marked pages. Public account-review access keeps the evidence reachable without forcing another combat encounter, and the deadline follows the party's resolution rather than a random loss decided after the fact.

## Later Harper Operations

***The contact chain.*** **Harper Contacts Relocated** gives **Cassalanter Villa** a precise mitigation result, preserving household contacts without giving them knowledge of private noble secrets. The existing external reader requires separate integration beyond this folder; the mission's outcome records the intended connection without editing that reader here.

***A surviving informant.*** Tessalar's actual fate carries into **The Cell Is Compromised** and **The Sleeping Asset**, allowing those investigations to distinguish his reporting from Orren Vale's separate leak. The dead drop's relocation and the ledger's destruction also determine which physical collection point remains available, preserving the consequence of the party's evidence choices.
