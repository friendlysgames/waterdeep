# Design Notes: The Dead Drop

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C and WDH give only a cat rescue: Uza Solizeph, a gazer in the attic, stray rays that threaten her stock, a spellbook and a side lead about Skeemo Weirdbottle's purchases. They award 1 Renown, and WDH awards it only if the gazer is defeated. The dead drop, the clerk, the cipher and the handler's ledger come from the earlier remix drafts, kept here to give the mission a second half that needs decisions. Base Renown is 2 as the Harpers guide sets it, with two bonuses for listed conditions.

## The Gazer

Earlier drafts made the gazer a Splinter tool sent to search for a document. WDH describes gazers as beholder-spawn, so this one is a stray that came after the cat and has no handler. That also removes the need for it to know anything about the drop. The 2014 rule that a missed ray rolls a d6 does not fit 2024 rays, which use saving throws, so the stock rule replaces it. One gazer is trivial on Hit Points at every party size, and the pressure comes from Fillipa on the beam and the shelves behind each target. The 12-foot ridge resolves the source's contradiction between a rafter 10 feet up and 8-foot ceilings.

## Tessalar and the Ledger

The earlier version set twelve outcomes, six of them ledger states, and ran a clock for each resolution. This version keeps six outcomes and one deadline: Rusk collects the book at a fixed hour, and the members either read it first or do not. Each of the three resolutions for Tessalar has a short concrete result that **The Cell Is Compromised** can read, and Warned Off means the room is cleared, as that event assumes. The ledger gives Rusk's name and "two more contacts" at a Brindul Alley address, never the names of the Interrogation House staff, so **Faction Outposts** keeps its own reveal. Rusk's name appears only on the slips, which is why Mirt in **The Cell Is Compromised** says it only if **Handler Ledger Read** is marked. **Fillipa Rescued** was cut because nothing reads it. The Cassalanter contact consequence and "What the Breach Proves" were cut for the same reason.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Invented: Tessalar Maeridge, Beldan Rusk, Lysa Fenwick, Teren Moss, the Seven Scales Counting House at 18 Sorn Street. Uza's "decade" of service, the 50 gp and the cipher's contents also come from the earlier drafts.
- New here: Tessalar as the loader of the drop and a hired clerk of Mirt's go-between, so that Mirt can name him. The customs-house ledger location and the 18:00 collection are the earlier drafts' and are kept.
- Fillipa's 2 Hit Points are an assumption, since no cat record was in the data. The Skeemo lead has no outcome.
- **Faction Outposts** is expected to read the Brindul Alley address through **Handler Ledger Read** (unconverted). **The Sleeping Asset** must read the three Tessalar outcomes and both ledger outcomes.
