# The Dead Drop: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Available to a Harper member at Renown 3 and 3rd level after **The Talking Mare**. Companions can help with the shop, the clerk and the ledger without belonging to the faction.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> The fight in the attic uses one **Gazer** at every party size. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** gives the 2024 block and audits it for three, four and five participating combatants.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

At noon Mirt's paper bird reaches each Harper member with a short note. A bookseller named Uza Solizeph has a monster in her shop on Sorn Street, her cat is still inside, and the shop is a Harper dead drop that she does not know about.

## Background

Uza's shop has hidden a Harper drop behind shelf seven for ten years. A young clerk named Tessalar Maeridge carries Mirt's sealed packets to it and believes he is running letters for a scholars' circle. A week ago a man in a grey coat paid him 50 gp to copy one of those packets. The sheet named two Harper assets in noble houses, and the Splinter now has the copy.

The gazer in the attic has nothing to do with any of this. It is a stray beholder-spawn that came through the skylight three days ago after Uza's cat.

## The Brief

The members meet Uza at Felzoun's Folly, at the corner of Sorn and Salabar. She describes the creature with great precision, hands over the keys to the shop and asks for her cat first and her books second.

## The Bookshop and the Attic

The shop has three floors between a glassblower and a cartographer. The gazer is in the attic, hunting Fillipa along a ridge beam twelve feet up. Every ray that misses can ruin stock, and the party can end the fight by killing the gazer or by carrying the cat out through the door.

## The Loose Brick

Behind shelf seven the members find a coded sheet that someone copied by hand. Mirt reads it, moves the two assets named on it, and gives them the name of the clerk who loads the drop.

## Tessalar Maeridge

The party questions Tessalar after the counting house closes. They can turn him into a double agent, hand him to the Watch or warn him off, and each choice has a different result for the handler's next move.

## The Customs-House Ledger

The handler keeps a ledger in the second drawer of the rejected-cargo desk at the customs house. The members can read it before the handler collects it, and it gives them his name and the address of two more contacts.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Harper member gains 2 base Renown for clearing the shop and reporting to Mirt. Bonuses of +1 each go to members who turn Tessalar and to members who read the ledger before it is collected.

## Aftermath

Uza gets her shop and, if the party earned it, her cat back. Mirt moves the two assets to the Delzorin Street lodging within two days. The handler learns about the party's choice at Tessalar's desk, and **The Cell Is Compromised** and **The Sleeping Asset** read what became of the clerk and the ledger. **The Doppelganger Auditions** becomes available at Renown 5 and 4th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): sends the brief, reads the cipher and takes the members' report.
- **Uza Solizeph**: a Mulan bookseller of about seventy who owns the shop and does not know what it hides.
- **Fillipa**: Uza's eleven-year-old black-and-white cat, stuck on the ridge beam.
- **Tessalar Maeridge**: a 23-year-old records clerk who loaded the drop and sold a copy of its contents.
- **Beldan Rusk** (the Splinter): the handler who paid Tessalar and collects the ledger.
- **Lysa Fenwick** (Harpers): a laundry clerk in House Amcathra named on the cipher.
- **Teren Moss** (Harpers): an inventory clerk in House Rosznar named on the cipher.
- **Skeemo Weirdbottle** (Doom Raiders): bought three volumes on noble scandals from Uza last month.

## Dangers & Enemies

One **Gazer** in the attic, a stray that fights until it dies or until Fillipa leaves the room. Tessalar and the customs clerks do not fight. The danger in the rest of the mission is a handler who collects his ledger on a fixed evening.

## Overview

A bookseller asks the Harpers to save her cat from a monster in her shop. Behind her shelves the members find that someone has been copying a Harper dead drop.
