# The Dead Drop: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Available to a Harper member at Renown 3 and 3rd level after **The Talking Mare**, with assisting companions welcome during the operation.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> The single-gazer encounter uses the converted creature and the combatant counts from the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**, and the cat and the stock give the party extra objectives besides the fight.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

A paper bird finds the eligible Harper members at noon, carrying Mirt's request to help Uza Solizeph, a bookseller whose shop has been taken over by a creature. Uza waits at Felzoun's Folly, two streets from her premises on Sorn Street, while her cat Fillipa is still inside.

## Background

For a decade Uza has hosted a Harper dead drop behind shelf seven of her philosophy section without knowing it. Three months ago Tessalar Maeridge, a young counting-house clerk among her customers, accepted 50 GP from a Splinter handler to watch that section. He found the loose brick, copied the cipher note and handed over the copy before a gazer entered the shop three days ago.

The gazer was sent to search for a second document, which Mattrim Mereg lost three months earlier and which no longer exists. Its search drove Uza out of the shop and trapped Fillipa in the attic, where she has stayed ahead of it by jumping between the shelves and the roof beams. Tessalar's delivery has already exposed partial identifiers for Lysa Fenwick, a laundry clerk at House Amcathra, and Teren Moss, an inventory clerk at House Rosznar, and stopping his future reports can't take that copy back.

## Uza's Shop

Uza gives the party her keys and asks them to bring Fillipa out alive. Upstairs, the party fights or disables the gazer, rescues the cat and secures the stock before three fixed inventory deadlines pass. The gazer stays hostile, but only some of the characters need to attack it while the others rescue Fillipa.

## Behind the Philosophy Shelf

The loose brick hides the original cipher and an empty compartment that once held the missing document. Marks on the paper show that someone copied it by hand, and Mirt recognizes Tessalar from his recent visits to the shop. A warning to Mirt also sets off the relocation of the two exposed contacts to protected lodging at 12 Delzorin Street, which is finished within forty-eight hours.

## The Clerk and the Ledger

Tessalar can be turned, reported to the Watch or warned off while Mirt relocates the drop. He tells the party where his handler's ledger sits in the Trades Ward customs house, and the party can recover, copy or destroy it before the Splinter takes it away. Reading the ledger shows two more agents, and burning it unread denies the handler the evidence without revealing what it said.

## Renown Opportunities

Each Harper member who helps clear the shop and reports the breach gains 2 base Renown. Bonuses come only from the two conditions below, and each applies to every eligible participating member:

- **+1 Renown:** turn Tessalar into a cooperating double agent who stops genuine reports and delivers the agreed false report.
- **+1 Renown:** recover or destroy the handler's ledger before its collection or burn deadline. Destroying it unread still denies the handler, while copying it reveals the additional identities but leaves the ledger with its handler and earns no bonus.

Uza gives the spellbook to the assisting party as a shared gift whatever happens to Tessalar. Companions gain no Harper membership or Renown by helping.

## Aftermath

Uza becomes a Trades Ward book contact, and Mirt relocates the dead drop and handles the exposed contacts according to the party's report. **Harper Contacts Relocated** records that the contacts were moved in time, and **The Tail** reads it. It doesn't take back any intelligence already delivered, and it tells nobody anything about a noble family's secrets. **The Doppelganger Auditions** becomes available at Renown 5 and 4th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): sends out the call for the members and hears their private report.
- **Mattrim Mereg** (Harpers): used the dead drop and lost the secondary document three months ago.
- **Uza Solizeph**: a bookseller who describes the damage in careful detail and asks for Fillipa's safe return.
- **Fillipa**: Uza's eleven-year-old black-and-white cat, sheltering in the attic rafters.
- **Tessalar Maeridge** (Splinter informant): a frightened clerk who can cooperate or be handed to the Watch.
- **Lysa Fenwick** and **Teren Moss** (Harpers): the noble-house contacts whose partial identifiers have already been copied.

## Dangers & Enemies

One converted **Gazer** holds the attic. It has no remote familiar link, and no other creatures arrive during the shop encounter. Tessalar's handler threatens the contact chain through information and the destruction of the ledger, and doesn't force a second combat.

## Overview

A bookseller has fled her shop after a floating creature chased her out, and her cat is still among the shelves upstairs. The party helps her win the shop back and finds out why someone took an interest in her philosophy collection.
