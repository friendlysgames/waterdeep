# The Dead Drop: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Available to a Harper member at Renown 3 and 3rd level after **The Talking Mare**, with assisting companions welcome during the operation.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> The one-gazer encounter uses the converted creature and actual combatant counts in the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**, with the cat and stock providing additional objectives.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

A paper bird reaches the eligible Harper members at noon, carrying Mirt's request to help Uza Solizeph, a bookseller whose shop has been occupied by a creature. Uza waits at Felzoun's Folly, two streets from her Sorn Street premises, while her cat Fillipa remains inside.

## Background

Uza has unknowingly hosted a Harper dead drop behind shelf seven of her philosophy section for a decade. Tessalar Maeridge, a young counting-house clerk among her customers, accepted 50 GP from a Splinter handler to watch the section three months ago. He found the loose brick, copied the cipher note and delivered that copy before a gazer entered the shop three days ago.

The gazer was sent to search for a second document, which Mattrim Mereg lost three months earlier and which no longer exists. Its search drove Uza out and trapped Fillipa in the attic, where the cat has used shelves and roof beams to stay ahead of it. Tessalar's delivery already exposed partial identifiers for Lysa Fenwick, a laundry clerk at House Amcathra, and Teren Moss, an inventory clerk at House Rosznar; stopping future reports cannot retract that copy.

## Uza's Shop

Uza gives the party her keys and asks them to bring Fillipa out alive. Upstairs, they can fight or disable the gazer while rescuing the cat and securing stock before three fixed inventory deadlines pass. The creature remains hostile, but rescuing the cat doesn't require every character to spend each turn attacking it.

## Behind the Philosophy Shelf

The loose brick contains the original cipher and an empty compartment for the missing document. Physical copying traces replace the former remote eye-link, and Mirt identifies Tessalar from his recent visits. A warning to Mirt also starts the relocation of the two exposed contacts to protected lodging at 12 Delzorin Street, completed within forty-eight hours.

## The Clerk and the Ledger

Tessalar can be turned, reported to the Watch or warned off while Mirt relocates the drop. He provides his handler's ledger location in the Trades Ward customs house, allowing the party to recover, copy or destroy the file before the Splinter removes it. Reading identifies two additional agents; burning an unread ledger denies evidence without revealing its contents.

## Renown Opportunities

Each Harper member who participates in clearing the shop and reports the breach gains 2 base Renown. Add only the following listed bonuses to each eligible participating member:

- **+1 Renown:** turn Tessalar into a cooperating double agent who stops genuine reports and delivers the agreed false report.
- **+1 Renown:** recover or destroy the handler's ledger before its collection or burn deadline. Its destruction satisfies the denial objective even when unread; copying reveals the additional identities but leaves the ledger available to its handler.

Uza gives the spellbook to the assisting party as a shared gift, irrespective of Tessalar's fate; companions gain no Harper membership or Renown through helping.

## Aftermath

Uza becomes a Trades Ward book contact, while Mirt relocates the dead drop and manages the exposed assets according to the party's report. **Harper Contacts Relocated** records timely mitigation for **Cassalanter Villa**; it neither removes delivered intelligence nor implies knowledge of any noble family's secrets. **The Doppelganger Auditions** becomes available at Renown 5 and 4th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): dispatches the members and receives their private report.
- **Mattrim Mereg** (Harpers): used the dead drop and lost the secondary document three months ago.
- **Uza Solizeph**: a bookseller who describes the damage precisely and asks for Fillipa's safe return.
- **Fillipa**: Uza's eleven-year-old black-and-white cat, sheltering in the attic rafters.
- **Tessalar Maeridge** (Splinter informant): a frightened clerk who can cooperate or be handed to the Watch.
- **Lysa Fenwick** and **Teren Moss** (Harpers): the noble-house contacts whose partial identifiers have already been copied.

## Dangers & Enemies

One converted **Gazer** occupies the attic, with no remote familiar link and no additional creatures arriving during the shop encounter. Tessalar's handler threatens the contact chain through information and the destruction of the ledger, rather than forcing another combat encounter.

## Overview

A bookseller has fled her shop after a floating creature chased her out, leaving her cat among the shelves upstairs. The party can help her recover the shop while learning why someone was interested in its philosophy collection.
