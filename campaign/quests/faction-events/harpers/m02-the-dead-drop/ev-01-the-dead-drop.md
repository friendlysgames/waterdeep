# The Dead Drop

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Investigation and Social Event begins when Mirt's paper bird reaches an individual Harper member and ends when the members report to him at the Delzorin Street lodging. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Meet Uza Solizeph at Felzoun's Folly and take the keys to her bookshop.
> - Clear a gazer out of the attic and bring her cat Fillipa down from the ridge beam.
> - Find the loose brick behind shelf seven and the copied cipher that lay behind it.
> - Turn the clerk Tessalar Maeridge, hand him to the Watch or warn him off.
> - Read the handler's ledger at the customs house before it is collected.
> - Receive Uza's spellbook, whatever happens to Tessalar.
>
> Only Harper members receive the bird and report to Mirt. Their companions can help with every other part of the mission.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Uza's shop has been a Harper dead drop for ten years, and she does not know it. She thinks Mirt is an old customer whose friends sometimes browse.
> - Tessalar Maeridge, 23, a records clerk at the Seven Scales Counting House, has carried Mirt's sealed packets to the brick behind shelf seven since last spring. He believes he runs letters for a scholars' circle.
> - A week ago a man in a grey coat paid Tessalar 50 gp to copy what he carried. Tessalar copied one sheet naming Lysa Fenwick of House Amcathra and Teren Moss of House Rosznar. The Splinter has the copy.
> - The man is Beldan Rusk, a Splinter handler. Tessalar never learned his name. It appears on the collection slips in Rusk's ledger.
> - The gazer is a stray beholder-spawn that came through the cracked attic skylight after Fillipa. It has no handler and no link to the Splinter, and it knows nothing about the drop.
> - Skeemo Weirdbottle bought three volumes on noble families' scandals from Uza last month. That is true, and nothing else in this Event depends on it.

### The Brief

At noon a paper bird finds each Harper member wherever they are lodging. Read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A paper bird flutters down onto your table and spreads its wings flat, and the note folded inside is written in Mirt's heavy, even hand.
>
> > "Uza Solizeph keeps a bookshop on Sorn Street in the Trades Ward. We have used it as a drop for ten years, and she does not know. Something has got into the shop and her cat is still inside. She waits at Felzoun's Folly, on the corner of Sorn and Salabar. Take her keys and bring out the cat first. Tell her nothing. If you find a sheet of ours in the shop, send a bird. M."

Felzoun's Folly stands two streets from the shop. Uza has slept in a rented room above it for three days. When the members arrive, read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Felzoun's Folly is a small tavern full of clerks and booksellers eating bread at long tables, and the smell of ink and woodsmoke hangs under its low beams. An elderly woman in a grey shawl sits alone by the front window with a cup of tea. A ring of keys and a handwritten list lie beside her saucer, and she raises one hand to catch your eye.
>
> > "You must be Mirt's friends. Thank you for coming so promptly. I have written everything down, because it is easier to keep the order right on paper."

> [!social]**The Bookseller with a List**
>
> Uza Solizeph (Lawful Good, Mulan Human, she/her) :: a bookseller of about seventy who describes a monster in her shop the way she would describe a damaged first edition.
>
> Uza is precise and unflappable. She keeps her notes squared on the table, corrects anyone who exaggerates, and does not raise her voice. The Watch has stopped listening to her because her stories run long, but this one is accurate. She gives the members her keys freely, asks for no payment and does not ask who they are.
>
> Conversation topics Uza is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The creature, its size and what it has done to the shop.
> - Fillipa, and where she hides.
> - The layout of the three floors and the keys.
> - Who visits the shop.
>
> She does not know about the drop and cannot be led to it. If a member says the word Harper, she nods politely and goes on with her list.

> [!qna]**What is in the shop?**
>
> Uza measures a circle in the air with both hands.
>
> > "It's about the size of a human head, round, with one large eye in the middle and four smaller eyes on stalks. It floats. I came back from market three days ago and found it chasing Fillipa up the stairs, and it chased me out as well."

> [!qna]**Where is Fillipa?**
>
> > "In the attic, I believe. She's black and white and eleven years old, and I hear her complaining from the street whenever the creature goes near her. She has been very clever about it, but she cannot keep it up forever."

> [!qna]**Why is it there?**
>
> > "I can only say what I saw. It ignored the books and followed Fillipa through every room. One roof tile above the attic skylight was cracked when I looked up from the street, so I suppose it came in that way."

> [!qna]**How do we get in?**
>
> Uza slides the ring of keys across the table and rests a finger on the longest.
>
> > "That is the street door and the brass one is the back. There's a lockbox behind the counter with the spares and fifteen gold in it, in case you have to break something to reach her."

> [!qna]**Who visits the shop?**
>
> > "My regulars, mostly. Mr. Maeridge from the counting house at number eighteen comes most tendays, and he stands at the philosophy shelf longer than a man should who never buys from it. I don't mind. I'd like the books kept, where possible, but Fillipa certainly can't be replaced."

### The Bookshop

The shop is three floors between a glassblower's and a cartographer's. Uza's keys open the street door and the back door, and every inner door is unlocked. When the members open the street door, read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The street door opens onto a narrow shop with shelves up both walls and a long counter at the back. Books lie open across the floor, and a glass display case has been knocked onto its side. A sharp smell like a lightning strike hangs in the air, and from somewhere above comes the long, aggrieved yowl of a cat.

> [!exploration]**The Lower Floors**
>
> The stairs at the back are intact and the members cross the ground floor without a check.
>
> - **Ground floor.** A lockbox behind the counter holds the spare keys and 15 gp. Shelf seven, the philosophy section on the east wall, has been raked clean of books.
> - **Second floor.** Uza's bedroom, sitting room and kitchen. A hand-mirror is broken on the floor and a herb pot is smashed. Nothing else is damaged.
> - **Attic stairs.** Narrow, with a hatch at the top. The cat's yowls come through it every few seconds.

### The Gazer in the Attic

The attic is the third floor, 30 feet by 20, with eaves 8 feet high and a ridge beam 12 feet above the floor. Shelves of rolled maps and paper bundles line both walls, and a binding table stands in the middle. The hatch is at one end and Fillipa's end of the beam is 20 feet away. When the first member looks through the hatch, read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The attic runs the length of the building under a steep roof, with rolled maps and paper-wrapped bundles stacked on shelves along both walls. A long binding table stands in the middle, covered in bone folders and tins of paste. High on the ridge beam a black-and-white cat lies flat with her ears back, and a floating ball of grey flesh turns its eyestalks toward you from just beneath her.

> [!hazard]**The Gazer under the Ridge**
>
> One **Gazer** fights at every party size, using the 2024 block in the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**. It hovers 5 feet below the beam, next to Fillipa. The gazer is no danger on Hit Points alone, so the pressure comes from the cat and the shelves.
>
> - **Stats:** AC 13, 13 Hit Points, Fly 30 feet (hover), Bite +5 for 1 piercing damage. It makes no other attacks.
> - **Eye Rays:** on its turn it rolls 1d4 twice, rerolls duplicates and aims each ray at a creature within 60 feet. Every save is DC 12.
>   - **1, Dazing:** Wisdom save or Charmed until the start of its next turn, with halved Speed and Disadvantage on attack rolls.
>   - **2, Fear:** Wisdom save or Frightened until the start of its next turn.
>   - **3, Frost:** Dexterity save or 10 (3d6) cold damage, and no damage on a save.
>   - **4, Telekinetic:** Strength save or a Medium or smaller creature moves 30 feet straight away from the gazer. A creature pushed down the hatch falls 10 feet for 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
>
> #### The Gazer's Tactics
>
> While any character is within 10 feet of it, the gazer fires Eye Rays at the nearest two characters. When nobody is within 10 feet, it uses Aggressive to fly up to Fillipa and Bites her.
>
> - Fillipa has 2 Hit Points. The first bite takes 1 and she bolts to the far end of the beam. A second bite kills her.
> - The gazer does not flee or surrender. It fights until it is dead or until Fillipa has left the room.
>
> #### Fillipa and the Beam
>
> - **Climb the rafters:** DC 10 Strength (Athletics). A character who reaches the beam carries Fillipa down with a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check. A rope or ladder from the floor needs no check.
> - **Carry her out:** a character who takes Fillipa through the door ends the gazer's reason to stay. It follows 1 round for every 30 feet, loses interest and drifts out through the skylight.
> - **After the gazer dies:** Fillipa comes down 30 seconds later and goes to the first character who crouches without staring at her.

> [!exploration]**The Stock**
>
> Track the damage to Uza's stock as the fight runs.
>
> - **Trigger:** a Frost Ray that a target saves against, or a Telekinetic Ray that moves a target, ruins stock if a bookshelf stands within 30 feet behind the target. A target in a clear aisle, or with a wall behind them, costs nothing.
> - **Cost:** each strike ruins 10 gp of stock. The total stops at 100 gp.
> - **Result:** up to 30 gp, Uza says nothing. From 40 to 100 gp, she sighs and asks Mirt to cover it. Renown does not change either way.

When the members bring Fillipa to Felzoun's Folly alive, read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fillipa presses her head into your palm as you carry her down the stairs, and she purrs all the way to the tavern. Uza is on her feet before you reach the table, and she gathers the cat against her chest and checks each paw in turn.
>
> > "There you are, Fillipa. Let me look at you. Thank you, all of you. I'll send for the glazier tomorrow."

If Fillipa died, Uza sits with her hands folded, listens to the whole account and says only that she would like to put the cat's things away herself. She stays reserved with the party from then on, but she still gives the spellbook.

> [!gamemaster]**Uza's Spellbook**
>
> Once the shop is safe Uza gives the members a used wizard's spellbook, whatever has happened to the cat or to Tessalar. The binding is loose and every spell is legible: *Comprehend Languages*, *Detect Magic*, *Identify* and *Shield*, and *Invisibility*, *Locate Object* and *Misty Step*. A wizard can copy them by the normal rules.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Uza takes a worn spellbook from a bundle behind her chair and presses the loose binding together as she opens it. She checks that the last written page is still tied to the cover before she puts it in your hands.
>
> > "A wizard left this with me when he couldn't settle his account. The stitching needs mending, but the writing is sound, and I think you'll get more use from it than I will."

> [!qna]**Has anyone bought anything unusual lately?**
>
> Uza consults her list before she answers.
>
> > "A gnome came in last month, Skeemo Weirdbottle, who keeps the concoctions shop in this ward. He bought three volumes on the histories and scandals of noble families, and he paid very well. He asked about no family in particular, which made me wonder which one he wanted."

### The Loose Brick

Shelf seven is the philosophy section. The drop sits in the wall behind it.

> [!exploration]**Behind Shelf Seven**
>
> Any character who examines the raked shelf and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds fresh scratches around one brick. A ten-minute search finds the brick without a check. Behind it lies a folded sheet of coded marks, and the stone around it shows grooves where someone pressed a second sheet against the first.

> [!exploration]**The Copied Cipher**
>
> The grooves show without a check that the sheet was copied. Any character who examines the paper and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) check finds no trace of scrying on it. The gazer's eyes did not read this sheet. Somebody sat down with a pen and copied it.

When a member sends a bird, Mirt comes to the back door of the shop within the hour. Read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The back door opens and Mirt steps in, stooping under the lintel in a plain brown coat. He does not look at the fallen books. He holds out one thick hand for the sheet and reads it at the window with his head tilted.
>
> > "Lysa Fenwick, laundry clerk at House Amcathra. Teren Moss, inventory clerk at House Rosznar. Both mine. I'll move them to the Delzorin Street lodging inside two days."
>
> He folds the sheet into his coat and looks at the scratched brick.
>
> > "The lad who loads this drop is Tessalar Maeridge, a clerk at the Seven Scales Counting House, eighteen Sorn Street. He thinks he carries letters for a scholars' circle. Find out who paid him, and don't frighten him more than you must."

> [!gamemaster]**Mirt in the Shop**
>
> Mark **Harper Contacts Relocated** when Mirt reads the sheet. A turned Tessalar cannot undo what the Splinter already holds, so the move happens whatever the members do next. If the members never send the bird, Mirt reads the sheet at the debrief and gives the same lines there. Mirt does not stay, and he does not meet Uza.

### Tessalar Maeridge

The Seven Scales Counting House is at 18 Sorn Street. It closes at 18:00, and Tessalar rents a room above it with a separate door. Read the following aloud when the members find him.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The counting house is a narrow room with a brass rail across it and a row of tall desks, where a thin young man is putting away his pens. He has ink on both cuffs and dark rings under his eyes. When he sees the folded sheet in your hand he stops, and his own hands go flat on the desk.
>
> > "I carry letters for the scholars' circle. I don't read them. I only copied the one, and I've regretted it every night since."

> [!social]**The Clerk Who Took the Money**
>
> Tessalar Maeridge (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: a 23-year-old records clerk, thin and ink-stained, who took 50 gp from a stranger and has not slept properly since.
>
> Tessalar is frightened and eager to talk. He asks for the stairwell if other clerks are near, and he answers every question in full once he is out of earshot. He has no loyalty to the man who paid him. He has never heard the word Harper and does not know who the circle really is.
>
> Conversation topics Tessalar is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The packets he carries to the shop, and how often.
> - The man in the grey coat and the 50 gp.
> - What he copied and where he left it.
> - What he will do now.
>
> He does not know the man's name, where he lives or who he works for.

> [!qna]**Who paid you?**
>
> > "A man in a grey coat. He found me outside the counting house a week ago and gave me fifty gold to copy what I carried. He never gave a name, and he always pays under the stair, in a folded purse."

> [!qna]**Where did the copy go?**
>
> > "I left it in a book in the second drawer of the rejected-cargo desk at the customs house. It's on the Trades Ward side. There's an ink mark inside the cover. The man took the sheet the next morning, but the book stays in the drawer."

> [!qna]**Did you read the sheet?**
>
> > "I looked at it. It was marks, and I couldn't read it. I thought the circle would never find out, and now I think somebody could be hurt."

> [!qna]**What happens to me?**
>
> > "I don't know, and I won't run if you tell me to stay. I only want nobody else hurt because of me."

> [!exploration]**Turning Tessalar**
>
> Any character who tells him what the copy has put at risk and makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check persuades Tessalar to act as a double agent. On a failure he is too afraid to write another report. He still tells the truth, and the members can warn him off or hand him to the Watch.

> [!gamemaster]**The Three Resolutions**
>
> - **Turn him.** Mark **Tessalar Turned**. That evening Tessalar writes a false report saying that Uza threw the sheet away and the drop is abandoned. He leaves it in the ledger drawer for Rusk, and from then on he brings Mirt every instruction he receives. He can point Mirt to the place where Rusk collects.
> - **Hand him to the Watch.** Mark **Tessalar Arrested**. Officers take him without a struggle and he tells them everything. Within two days a man paid by the other side has seen him in his cell, and Tessalar stops talking to anyone from the Harpers.
> - **Warn him off.** Mark **Tessalar Warned Off**. Tessalar packs that night, stays away from the shop and leaves Waterdeep. The next morning Rusk's people clear his room. Mirt gets no double agent and no prisoner.

If the members turn Tessalar, read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar pulls a clean sheet across the desk and writes slowly, holding the page flat with the ruler.
>
> > "I'll say Uza threw the old sheet away and nobody uses the shop now. Will you read it before I leave it? I'd like to get it right, and I'll tell Mirt whatever they send back."

### The Customs-House Ledger

The Trades Ward customs house keeps its rejected-cargo desk at the back of the hall. The members can learn about the ledger in three ways, and any one is enough.

> [!exploration]**Finding the Ledger**
>
> - **Ask Tessalar.** Any resolution in which he is talking tells them: he describes the desk, the second drawer and the ink mark inside the cover.
> - **Search his desk.** A character who searches Tessalar's account book and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds a receipt slip for a customs-house desk, with the drawer number written on it.
> - **Follow him.** At 17:00 Tessalar leaves the counting house on an errand to the customs house. A character who trails him and makes a successful DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check sees him open the second drawer of the rejected-cargo desk and close it again.

The customs house is open 09:00 to 18:00. The drawer is unlocked, and a clerk lets any visitor read the rejected-cargo accounts at the desk.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A customs clerk lifts a stack of forms off the rejected-cargo desk and sets it on the counter, leaving the second drawer clear for you.
>
> > "Rejected accounts are in the second drawer. You're welcome to read them here and copy any entries you need, but the book stays at the desk."

> [!exploration]**Reading the Ledger**
>
> The ink mark leads to two pages tucked in among the ordinary cargo figures. Ten minutes of reading or copying them shows:
>
> - A payment of 50 gp to Tessalar Maeridge, dated a week ago.
> - An entry for the cipher copy, with a collection slip signed *B. Rusk*.
> - Two more payment lines for informants whose collections go to a house on Brindul Alley.
>
> Mark **Handler Ledger Read** when the members have read or copied the marked pages. Taking the book away is not needed, and the clerk will not allow it.

> [!gamemaster]**Rusk's Collection**
>
> Rusk takes the whole book at 18:00 on the second day after the members first enter the shop. If the Watch arrests Tessalar, he takes it at 18:00 on the day after the arrest if that is sooner. Once he has the book, mark **Handler Ledger Lost** unless **Handler Ledger Read** is already marked. The two outcomes never both apply.
>
> A drawer found empty means Rusk has been. The clerk says only that a man in a grey coat signed for the account book and left.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt receives the members in the common room of the Delzorin Street lodging at 20:00 on the evening they finish, or at 09:00 the next morning. Companions wait outside. Read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sits at the long table in the lodging's common room with a jug at his elbow, and he fills each glass as you sit down. His voice is low and level.
>
> > "Sit. Tell me what you did with the lad, and then tell me about the ledger."

Read the branch that matches the outcome the members marked for Tessalar.

If the members marked **Tessalar Turned**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt nods slowly and turns his glass a quarter turn on the table.
>
> > "Good. He'll tell us each time the man comes, and I'd like you to leave him to me from here. He's young, and he's no use to us if he thinks he's being watched."

If the members marked **Tessalar Arrested**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt is silent for a moment before he speaks, and he does not look at the jug.
>
> > "The Watch has him, then. That was a fair choice and it cost us the use of him. I'll have someone ask him questions through the cell door, though I doubt he'll answer."

If the members marked **Tessalar Warned Off**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt rubs his beard with one broad hand.
>
> > "You let him run. He was scared and he's young, and I won't say you were wrong. But the man who paid him will find an empty room and know someone was there."

If the members marked **Handler Ledger Read**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt reads your copied pages twice, tracing the signed slip with a finger, and writes a short line beneath it.
>
> > "Rusk. That's the name on the slips, and there's an address on Brindul Alley for two more of his people. I'll have it watched. It tells us who got paid, but not who pays Rusk."

If the members marked **Handler Ledger Lost**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt hears your account to the end and sets his glass down.
>
> > "Then the handler has his book and we have nothing in writing. We'll find him another way. You did well to reach the shop before the Watch did."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains 2 base Renown for clearing the shop and reporting to Mirt. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** turn Tessalar and mark **Tessalar Turned**.
> - **+1 Renown:** read the ledger before Rusk collects it and mark **Handler Ledger Read**.

### Aftermath

Uza reopens the shop within the tenday and sends the members a note of thanks. If the stock loss reached 40 gp, she has already asked Mirt to cover it. She never learns that her philosophy shelf was a Harper drop, and Mirt moves the drop to a new place.

Lysa Fenwick and Teren Moss arrive at 12 Delzorin Street within two days of Mirt reading the sheet and sleep behind a curtain in the rear room. The Splinter keeps everything already delivered, and it knows both names. The members can ask Mirt to confirm the move, but he does not let them meet either contact.

Rusk reacts to what the members did with Tessalar and the ledger, and **The Cell Is Compromised** and **The Sleeping Asset** both read it. Skeemo Weirdbottle does not know that Uza mentioned him.

### Concluding the Event

The members' report to Mirt concludes the mission. Record what happened to Tessalar and to the ledger, because later events read both.

## Overview

The party rescues a bookseller's cat from a gazer and finds that the Harper dead drop behind her shelves has been copied. They deal with the clerk who sold it and try to read his handler's ledger.

## Summary

We cleared a gazer out of Uza Solizeph's bookshop and carried her cat down from the rafters. Behind shelf seven we found a cipher that someone had copied by hand, and Mirt traced it to a young clerk named Tessalar. We decided what to do with him and what to do about the handler's ledger.
