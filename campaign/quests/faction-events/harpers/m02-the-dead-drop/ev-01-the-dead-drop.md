# The Dead Drop

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Social Event begins when Mirt's paper bird arrives and ends when the party resolves Tessalar's part in the breached drop. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Bring Fillipa out of Uza's shop while protecting stock during the gazer encounter.
> - Find the copied cipher and warn Mirt about two exposed noble-house contacts.
> - Turn Tessalar, hand him to the Watch or warn him off the operation.
> - Recover or deny the handler's ledger before its fixed destruction deadline.
>
> Mirt briefs and debriefs only the eligible Harper members. Companions can help with the rescue and the investigation, but they earn no Harper Renown.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Breach Proves**
>
> Tessalar names Beldan Rusk as the man who paid him, and the marked ledger supplies two further names and an address. Who these people answer to stays a GM secret unless the party finds other evidence. Mirt decodes the names on the list without learning who commands them, and Tessalar has never heard of Manshoon.

### The Brief

At noon, the paper bird carries Mirt's request to the Harper members wherever they are lodging. The note sends them to Felzoun's Folly first, before they go into Uza's locked shop.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A folded paper bird flutters down beside you and spreads its wings across the table, and under the creases you can make out a note in Mirt's handwriting. The last sentence has been squeezed into the margin, with the ink running almost to the edge of the paper.
>
> > "An old friend of mine, Uza Solizeph, keeps a bookshop on Sorn Street in the Trades Ward. She's done us quiet favors for twenty years. Something has got into her shop and she's frightened. Go and see what needs doing. She doesn't know what she's been helping with. Keep it that way."

> [!gamemaster]**The Dispatch**
>
> The outside of the note gives Felzoun's Folly as Uza's temporary address, on the corner of Sorn and Salabar, two streets from the shop. Mirt sends the members to the rescue first, and he doesn't learn that Tessalar has copied the cipher until they report the evidence below. Uza never finds out about the Harper dead drop during this mission.

### Tea at Felzoun's Folly

Uza sits near the tavern's front window with a cup of tea, watching everyone who comes through the door for news of her shop. When the party offers help, she starts the conversation below and hands over her keys freely, with no payment and no check.

> [!readaloud]
>
> By the front window of the tavern, an older woman sits with both hands wrapped around a cup of tea, and she glances up each time the door opens onto the street. Beside her saucer lie a ring of keys and a list of book titles, several of them crossed out and written again underneath. When she sees you looking, she half rises and beckons you over.
>
> > "If you've come about the shop, please sit down and let me explain what's happened. I don't want anyone taking that creature for something harmless, and my cat is still up there."

> [!social]**The Bookseller with a Written Account**
>
> Uza Solizeph (Lawful Good, Mulan Human, she/her) :: an elderly bookseller who describes frightening events with the same care she gives her catalogues.
>
> Uza is frightened for Fillipa, but she keeps the keys beside her hand and points to her written notes whenever someone interrupts with a guess about the monster. The conversation below covers the creature's appearance, the three floors and the cat's hiding places. She asks for no payment and no declaration of faction affiliation, and she lends the keys to anyone who offers to bring Fillipa home.

> [!qna]**What is inside?**
>
> Uza measures a circle in the air with her hands before she picks up the keys.
>
> > "It's about the size of a human head, with one big eye in the middle and smaller eyes on stalks. It knocked over the whole philosophy section and hung there staring at the wall, and then it went upstairs after Fillipa."

> [!qna]**Where is Fillipa?**
>
> > "She's black and white, and she's eleven years old. I heard her in the attic this morning, complaining rather loudly whenever that thing went near her. She's been jumping between the shelves and the beams, but I can't leave her to manage it for another night."

> [!qna]**What do you need from us?**
>
> Uza slides the keys across the table, keeping a finger beside the largest one.
>
> > "This one opens the street door, and the others are for the rooms upstairs. Please bring Fillipa out alive. I can replace some of the books and I can mend a broken shelf, but I can't replace her."

> [!qna]**How is the shop laid out?**
>
> Uza turns over her list and sketches the staircase along the edge of the page.
>
> > "The books and manuscripts are on the ground floor, and my rooms are above them. The attic holds the new acquisitions and the binding table. If she's still complaining the way she was this morning, you'll hear Fillipa long before you reach her."

### Sorn Street Bookshop

The street door is locked and opens with Uza's key, and every interior door is unlocked. The ground floor holds the damaged philosophy shelves and manuscript cases, the second floor is Uza's living quarters, and the third floor holds acquisitions and bookbinding tools beneath eight-foot eaves and a twelve-foot ridge.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Uza's key turns stiffly in the lock, and the street door opens onto a shop floor buried in fallen books and loose papers beneath an overturned display case. The stairs at the back are clear, and from somewhere above you comes a long, aggrieved meow followed by the sound of a bundle sliding off a table.

> [!exploration]**The Floors below the Attic**
>
> The party crosses the ground floor to the intact stairs without a check, and examining shelf seven shows fresh scratches around one brick behind it. The second-floor bedroom and sitting room have minor damage, and a broken hand-mirror lies beside the kitchen doorway. While crossing the second-floor landing, the party hears Fillipa and the gazer above, which gives them time to agree on who will rescue the cat and who will protect the stock.

When the party reaches the attic landing, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bundles of maps and uncatalogued books fill one side of the attic, and a binding table stands near a tall shelf that leans toward the stair landing. High on a beam above it a black-and-white cat crouches, and the floating creature beside her swings its smaller eyes toward the doorway as you arrive.
>
> Loose books have been packed into an open crate next to the table, and a wrapped bundle of manuscripts sits beside a jar of binding paste. The gazer's big central eye settles on the first of you through the door.

> [!hazard]**The Attic Gazer**
>
> One **Gazer**, using the 2024 conversion in the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**, starts ten feet from the workshop table. Fillipa sits on a beam twenty feet from the landing and ten feet above the floor. The rescue and stock measures below stay available during combat. Eye rays use saving throws, so a successful save never counts as a missed attack that damages a shelf.
>
> #### Gazer Tactics
> The gazer turns from Fillipa to the intruders as soon as it sees them, and it uses Eye Rays against two different characters when it can.
>
> During combat, the gazer:
> - Uses its randomly selected Eye Rays on visible intruders, with the saving throws and durations printed in the conversion.
> - Hovers inside the attic and uses Aggressive to close on a visible opponent, and never retreats through the roof.
> - Stays in the shop because it was sent to find the missing secondary document, which no longer exists.
>
> The encounter ends when the gazer dies or is disabled and the party carries Fillipa out of the attic. The gazer never retreats, but disabling it makes the rescue possible without killing it.
>
> #### Rescuing Fillipa
> Any character who climbs the shelves and makes a successful DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check reaches Fillipa and carries her down. On a failure the attempt uses the character's action, and nobody falls. Once the gazer is disabled, Fillipa comes down on her own after thirty seconds and takes the hand of a character who crouches and offers it.
>
> #### Securing the Stock
> At the end of rounds 1, 2 and 3, an active gazer damages the unsecured stock listed below, one category per round. If the gazer is dead or Incapacitated, that round's loss doesn't happen, and a missed deadline never moves its damage to a later category.
> - **Round 1:** unbound books worth 15 GP fall under scattered binding tools and tear. One Utilize action moving their crate ten feet into the storage alcove prevents this loss.
> - **Round 2:** the loose shelf topples across the stair landing and turns the stairs into Difficult Terrain. One Utilize action bracing it with the workbench's wooden support keeps it standing, and two Utilize actions clear it afterward.
> - **Round 3:** the rare manuscripts fall into spilled binding paste and are ruined. One Utilize action moving their bundle ten feet into the storage alcove prevents this loss.
>
> The stock hazard deals no damage to creatures, and characters who reach the workshop unnoticed can secure a threatened item before initiative begins.

If Fillipa is carried out alive, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fillipa presses her head into your palm as you carry her down the stairs, purring in a low, steady rumble all the way out the door. At Felzoun's Folly, Uza is on her feet before you reach the table, and she gathers the cat against her chest and leaves the keys lying beside her untouched tea.
>
> > "There you are, Fillipa. Let me look at you, darling. Thank you for bringing her back to me. Now tell me what happened up there while I check whether she's hurt."

If Fillipa dies, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Uza pulls a chair up to the table and sits with her hands folded in her lap, looking from the empty doorway to the keys you have brought back.
>
> > "Please sit down and tell me what happened to her. I'd like to hear it before I go back into the shop."
>
> When you finish, she takes the keys and holds them between her palms.
>
> > "Is the creature still there, or may I go upstairs now? I'd like to put her things away myself."

> [!gamemaster]**Uza's Shared Gift**
>
> Once the shop is safe, Uza gives the assisting party a used spellbook, whatever became of Tessalar and even if Fillipa was hurt or lost. After harm to her cat she is reserved with the party, but she doesn't take the gift back. The binding is loose and all seven spells are legible: *Comprehend Languages*, *Detect Magic*, *Identify*, *Shield*, *Invisibility*, *Locate Object* and *Misty Step*. Normal Wizard copying rules apply.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Uza takes a worn spellbook from a bundle behind the counter and opens it carefully, pressing the loose binding together as she turns the pages. She checks that the last written sheet is still fixed to the cover before she puts the book in your hands.
>
> > "A wizard left this with me when he couldn't settle his account. The stitching needs mending, but the writing is sound, and I think you'll get more use out of it than I will."

### The Loose Brick

The philosophy shelf hides the original cipher, and the empty space beside it once held Mattrim's missing secondary document. Tessalar copied the note before the gazer arrived and left physical traces that the party can show Mirt.

> [!exploration]**Behind Shelf Seven**
>
> Any character who examines the damaged philosophy section and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the loose brick, with a folded cipher behind it and an empty compartment sized for another paper. A deliberate ten-minute search finds the brick without a check, so a failed attempt never closes off the breach.
>
> > Fine scratches run along one side of the brick, and the paper behind it shows shallow grooves where someone pressed through a second sheet to copy the writing.

> [!exploration]**The Copied Cipher**
>
> Any character who examines the paper and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) check determines that it carries no scrying effect, so the marks come from copying and not from an eyebeam. The physical copy is visible on inspection with no check, and the Arcana check only adds that distinction. *Comprehend Languages* doesn't decode the secret message.
>
> Mirt reads the cipher when he is warned and identifies Lysa Fenwick of House Amcathra and Teren Moss of House Rosznar as the exposed contacts. Their jobs give them access to their households, and neither knows anything about the private affairs of any noble family.

> [!gamemaster]**Warning Mirt**
>
> A paper bird or an in-person report to Mirt starts the relocation at once. Within forty-eight hours of the warning he moves both contacts into protected lodging at 12 Delzorin Street, adding two screened cots to the rear room so the five existing bunks stay free for their occupants. Mark **Harper Contacts Relocated** when both have arrived. Turning Tessalar stops future disclosures but doesn't replace the relocation, and the Splinter keeps everything already delivered.
>
> Tessalar works at the Seven Scales Counting House, 18 Sorn Street, and started visiting the shop three months ago. Mattrim lost the secondary document in the same period. It no longer exists, so no search can recover it.

If the members visit Mirt with the cipher, read or paraphrase the following. A paper-bird warning gets the same information in his written reply:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt lays the cipher flat under a lamp and follows one of the contact marks with a thick finger, then starts a separate note for each name. He folds the relocation orders and turns the copied paper back toward you, speaking in the low, level murmur he keeps for business.
>
> > "Lysa Fenwick, laundry clerk at House Amcathra. Teren Moss, inventory clerk at House Rosznar. I'll move them before anyone uses this copy to find their doors."
>
> Mirt sets the orders beside the lamp and writes an address on a separate sheet, which he slides across to you.
>
> > "Tessalar Maeridge is next. He works at the Seven Scales Counting House, eighteen Sorn Street, and he's been visiting Uza for three months. Mattrim lost the other document about then. It's gone for good, so leave that search alone."

### Tessalar Maeridge

Tessalar works at the Trades Ward counting house until 18:00 and then goes back to his rented room above it, which opens onto the same front door. The copied cipher shows him how much danger Uza and the contacts are in, so the party has leverage and doesn't need a fight.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar sits over a column of figures with a ruler laid across the page and a stack of delivery slips at his elbow, and he looks up as you come through the door. His eyes go from the copied marks to your faces, and then he sets down the ruler and pushes his chair back from the desk.
>
> > "I was paid to notice who went to that shelf, that's all. I never knew anyone would send something into her shop, or that there were people's names behind those marks."

> [!social]**The Clerk Who Took the Money**
>
> Tessalar is a frightened young clerk who took 50 GP to report unusual visits to the philosophy section, and he has no real loyalty to the Splinter. He pushes his account book within reach and keeps his hands flat on the desk while the party questions him. He would rather cooperate than be arrested, and he doesn't resist the Watch. His instructions, the delivered copy and the handler's ledger are covered below.

> [!qna]**What did you send?**
>
> Tessalar pulls a blank sheet toward himself and sketches the shape of the loose brick.
>
> > "I copied the whole note before the creature ever showed up, and I put the original back where I found it. The copy went into the ledger at the customs house that evening. By the next morning someone had collected it."

> [!qna]**Where is the ledger?**
>
> > "It's at the Trades Ward customs house, in the second drawer of the desk for rejected cargo accounts. There's a little ink mark inside the cover, so that's how you'll know you've got the right book. The drawer isn't locked."
>
> > "The man who paid me calls himself Beldan Rusk. He collects the pages after I've gone home, so I couldn't tell you where he goes with them."

> [!qna]**Will you help us?**
>
> > "If you tell me what to put in the next report, I'll write it. I don't want anyone else hurt because of me, and I can't keep pretending I don't know what these people are doing."

> [!exploration]**Turning Tessalar**
>
> Any character who explains the danger and makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check gets Tessalar to agree to stop genuine reports and act as a double agent. On a failure he is too afraid to file another report, and the party can still warn him off or ask the Watch to arrest him. If he is turned, his first false report says that Uza threw the missing document away months ago and the drop has been abandoned. That gives the Harpers a window and doesn't suggest that any other document survives.

> [!gamemaster]**The Three Resolutions**
>
> - **Turn him:** Tessalar files the agreed false account at the next 18:00 after the confrontation and gives Mirt copies of any future instructions. If the confrontation happens after 18:00, he files the following day. Mirt relocates the old drop immediately, and no genuine disclosure leaves Tessalar's hands again.
> - **Report him to the Watch:** The Watch arrests Tessalar and he goes without resistance. The Splinter burns its customs-house ledger twenty-four hours after the arrest, unless the party has already recovered or destroyed it.
> - **Warn him off:** Tessalar stops visiting the shop and filing reports. Mirt relocates the drop but gains no double agent. Rusk collects and removes the ledger at 18:00 two days after the party confronts Tessalar, unless it has already been recovered or destroyed. After collecting it, Rusk burns it at midnight and starts using the replacement east-entrance loose brick described below.

If Tessalar agrees to cooperate, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar moves the delivery slips aside and pulls a clean sheet across the desk, writing slowly while the ruler holds his account book open.
>
> > "I'll say Uza threw the missing document away months ago and the drop's been abandoned. Would you read it before I copy it out? I want to get it right, and I'll bring Mirt anything they send back."
>
> He signs his name at the bottom and waits while you read, with the money he was paid still folded inside his account book.

If the Watch takes him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar stands when the Watch officers come in, hands them his account book and puts on his coat, with one finger still resting on the marked payment entry.
>
> > "The fifty gold's written down there, next to the date. I took it for watching the shop, and I copied that paper before the creature came."
>
> One officer stays at his side while the other turns to you with a pencil held over the open book.
>
> > "Where were the copies delivered? Tell me the place and how you found out, and we'll put it down properly."

If the party warns him off, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar takes the reporting instructions from under his desk and puts them in your hands, then shuts the drawer where he kept them.
>
> > "I won't go near the shop again, and I won't file another report. Has Uza gone back yet? I'd like to know she can work there again, even if she never wants to see me."
>
> He waits for your answer before he pulls his unfinished accounts back toward him.

If the attempt to turn Tessalar fails, read or paraphrase the following before the party chooses another resolution:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar holds a blank report under his ruler without writing a word, and he glances at the front door each time footsteps cross the pavement outside.
>
> > "I can't send them another page. They'll know something's wrong, and I don't know what they'll do when they come looking. Take the instructions if you want them, but I just can't make myself write it."

### The Customs-House Ledger

The ledger sits in an unlocked second drawer during the customs house's public account-review hours, 09:00–18:00. If Tessalar has been turned, he can walk the party to the desk as an accounts inquiry. Anyone else can ask to inspect the rejected cargo entries and reach the drawer in the same hours without a check. Taking the book openly makes a clerk ask that it stay at the desk, while copying the marked pages in place takes ten minutes and counts as no theft.

When the party asks to inspect the cargo accounts, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A customs clerk lifts a stack of forms off the rejected-cargo desk and sets it on the counter, leaving the drawer clear for you.
>
> > "Rejected accounts are in the second drawer. You're welcome to read them here and copy whatever entries you need, as long as the book stays at the desk."

If the party takes the book openly, add:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The clerk puts down the forms and steps around the counter, holding out a hand toward the ledger as you lift it from the desk.
>
> > "That book belongs to the accounts office, I'm afraid. Please leave it here. Copying an entry is one thing, but it doesn't give you leave to carry off the ledger."

> [!exploration]**Reading before Removing**
>
> The marked ledger holds Tessalar's payment and two additional Splinter agent identifiers, which appear after ten minutes of reading or copying its marked pages. Mirt decodes them as Avareen Windrivver and Zorbog Jyarkoth, partners working from the windowless house on Brindul Alley. Bringing him the book or a copy gives him those names and the address, and a ledger destroyed unread reveals neither.
>
> If Tessalar was turned, he removes the ledger at the party's request before he files the false report. First he leaves a slip in the marked account drawer saying that the customs clerks are checking the book and that future collections go beneath the loose brick beside the east entrance. This is his established reporting channel, and Rusk retrieves the instruction at the next 18:00 after it is deposited. Tessalar puts the false report at the brick for that same collection, and Mirt's courier watches from the public cargo yard across from the entrance.
>
> If the party removes or destroys the ledger without Tessalar's help, Rusk finds the drawer empty and switches to the east-entrance brick at the next 18:00, leaving a collection instruction for his surviving informants. If a Watch arrest burns the book or a warn-off collection removes it, he uses the same brick from the following day. The physical ledger outcome therefore decides whether later missions watch the original drawer or the replacement brick.
>
> The party can copy the ledger before its deadline, take it over the clerk's objection or destroy it, in which case the accounts office reports the loss to the Watch. Copying leaves the book in place and earns no denial bonus. A recovered ledger stays with Mirt at his residence, and a destroyed or burned one survives only in the copies made beforehand.
>
> > The ink mark leads to two pages of payments tucked beneath ordinary cargo figures, and the same contact symbols repeat beside Tessalar's fifty-gold entry.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt receives the participating members privately at his residence at 20:00 once they have finished, or at 09:00 the next morning if the investigation runs later. He records the rescue, what became of Tessalar and whether the ledger was read, copied or denied before it could be removed.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains 2 base Renown for clearing the shop and reporting the breach. Add +1 Renown when Tessalar becomes a cooperating double agent and files the agreed false report, and +1 Renown when the party recovers or destroys the ledger before its deadline. Copying alone reveals the identities but doesn't deny the handler his book, so it earns no bonus. Destroying the ledger earns the bonus even though nothing unread can be learned from it. Companions who assist receive no Harper Renown.

### Aftermath

Uza becomes a recurring Trades Ward contact who helps the party find books and scholars, and she is never told that her philosophy section hosted a Harper drop. Mirt keeps the contacts' new lodging private, though members can ask him to confirm that the relocation is complete.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt checks the two relocation orders against a messenger's receipt and slides them under the cipher and the account of Tessalar's interview. He draws the ledger notes closer and leaves Tessalar's name uncovered beside them.
>
> > "Will Tessalar report again? What would he put in it? And the ledger. Is it still in the customs house, or did you bring it away or burn it? I need to know where it is before we watch another collection."

If the members brought readable marked pages or the ledger, Mirt identifies the additional contacts:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt places the marked pages beside the cipher, checks the repeated symbols against his notes and writes two names beneath the address they share.
>
> > "Avareen Windrivver and Zorbog Jyarkoth. They work together out of a windowless house on Brindul Alley. These pages give us the names and the address, and I'll keep them with the rest of your evidence."

When the report is complete, Mirt closes the account folder:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "You've given me enough to act on. Uza can go back to her books, and I'll make sure the people named in that copy aren't sitting there waiting for a knock."

### Concluding the Event

The members' report concludes the mission. Past exposure, future reporting and the rescued contacts are recorded as separate results, and no single clean-or-compromised state covers them.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Harper Cipher Copied** — mark when the party finds the copying evidence; Mirt's later briefings read that exposure, whatever happens to Tessalar.
> - **Harper Contacts Relocated** — mark when Lysa Fenwick and Teren Moss reach 12 Delzorin Street within forty-eight hours of the warning to Mirt; **The Tail** reads it.
> - **Tessalar Turned** — mark when he agrees to cooperate and files the false report; **The Cell Is Compromised** and **The Sleeping Asset** treat him as a living double agent.
> - **Tessalar Arrested** — mark when the Watch takes him into custody; later Harper missions read his arrest and the ledger's twenty-four-hour burn deadline.
> - **Tessalar Warned Off** — mark when he stops reporting and has been neither recruited nor arrested; later Harper missions know he is alive and don't treat him as a double agent.
> - **Handler Ledger Read** — mark when the party reads or copies the marked pages and passes them to Mirt; later Harper intelligence can use the two additional identifiers.
> - **Handler Ledger Denied** — mark when the party recovers or destroys the ledger before collection or burning; **The Sleeping Asset** reads the matching physical-state outcome and uses the replacement east-entrance brick.
> - **Handler Ledger Recovered** — mark when the party or a turned Tessalar removes the book and passes it to Mirt; **The Sleeping Asset** uses the replacement east-entrance brick and keeps the recovered evidence.
> - **Handler Ledger Destroyed** — mark when the party destroys the book before its deadline; **The Sleeping Asset** uses the replacement brick and has only the evidence copied before the destruction.
> - **Handler Ledger Collected** — mark when Rusk removes it at the warn-off collection deadline; **The Sleeping Asset** uses the replacement brick, and the ledger is burned at midnight.
> - **Handler Ledger Burned** — mark when the Splinter destroys it twenty-four hours after Tessalar's arrest or at midnight following collection; **The Sleeping Asset** uses the replacement brick and has only the evidence copied earlier.
> - **Fillipa Rescued** — mark when she is brought to Uza alive; later visits show Uza's gratitude or reserve according to the cat's injuries.

## Overview

The party enters a bookseller's shop to rescue her cat and finds signs that someone has been copying papers hidden behind a philosophy shelf.

## Summary

We helped Uza win back her shop and found that a clerk had copied a Harper cipher before the creature arrived. We followed the report through Tessalar to a customs-house ledger and told Mirt what had reached the handler.
