# The Dead Drop

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Social Event begins when the paper bird arrives and ends when the party resolves Tessalar Maeridge's fate. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from a paper bird carrying Mirt's handwritten message.
> - Find Uza Solizeph at Felzoun's Folly, two streets from the shop, working through a thorough account of recent events.
> - Clear the gazer from the attic, keeping Fillipa alive.
> - Discover the breached dead drop behind the philosophy section's loose brick and identify what the gazer read.
> - Identify and then turn, arrest, or warn off Tessalar Maeridge, whose choice determines how much residual intelligence the Splinter retains.
> - Receive a used spellbook from Uza's back collection regardless of how Tessalar's situation resolves.
>
> #### Background
>
> The Harper dead drop behind the philosophy section loose brick was used by Mattrim Mereg for his network contacts. Tessalar Maeridge began visiting the shop three months ago and identified the location for his Splinter handler. The gazer retrieved the cipher note's contents through its eyebeam — someone on the other end was watching — and has been searching for a secondary document that Mattrim misplaced three months ago. The document no longer exists.
>
> Tessalar's handler uses a drop location at the Trades Ward customs house: a ledger in the second drawer, a specific mark inside the cover. If the party finds and destroys or recovers this ledger before the Splinter can burn it, Mirt gains two agent identifiers he did not have before.
>
> If the Splinter has received the cipher contents and Tessalar is not turned: two Harper assets in noble houses are compromised. This affects Mirt's Cassalanter social contacts in **Cassalanter Villa** unless the party resolves Tessalar's situation cleanly. Mirt never says this directly, but his Cassalanter intelligence becomes thinner until the contact chain is restored.

### The Brief

The paper bird arrives at Trollskull Manor. Its handwriting is slightly less neat than usual.

> [!readaloud]
> A paper bird settles on the windowsill and waits. The note inside is longer than Mirt usually writes, and the hand is a little hurried.
>
> > *"An old friend named Uza Solizeph runs a bookshop on Sorn Street in the Trades Ward. She's done us favors for twenty years. Something's gotten into her shop and she's frightened. Go see what needs doing."*
>
> There is a postscript crowded into the margin: *"She doesn't know what she's been helping us with. Keep it that way."*

### The Shop

Uza is at Felzoun's Folly, two streets over from the shop. She is not difficult to find.

> [!readaloud]
> Felzoun's Folly is quiet at this hour, and Uza Solizeph is near the back wall, sitting with a cup of tea held in both hands and working her way through a thorough account of recent events for anyone who will listen. She is giving an accurate and complete description of what happened. The people around her appear to find this dramatic.

> [!social]**The Bookshop Owner**
>
> Uza Solizeph (Neutral, Illuskan human, she/her) :: a Trades Ward bookseller of twenty years' experience; precise, thorough, not easily alarmed; currently being accurate at a group of mildly uncomfortable strangers.
>
> Uza does not dramatize. She catalogs. She describes the creature that entered her shop with complete technical accuracy — spherical, roughly the size of a human head, one large central eye, smaller eyes on stalks, strong smell of ozone — in the same tone she would use to note that a shipment of manuscripts was delayed. She is affected by what happened, but her response to being affected is to become more thorough, not more distressed.
>
> She lends the party her keys. Her only stated requirement is that Fillipa come out alive.
>
> Uza does not know she has been hosting a Harper dead drop for ten years. She will not be told; Mirt's note is explicit on this point.
>
> Conversation topics Uza is willing to discuss include:
> - The creature's behavior in the shop, which she describes with technical precision.
> - Fillipa's rearguard action, which she describes with something close to pride.
> - The damage to her inventory, which she catalogs without placing blame.
> - Her keys and what each one opens.
>
> Specific dialogue for Uza on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**What did it look like?**
>
> She sets down her tea to gesture with both hands.
>
> > "Spherical. Roughly the size of a human head, perhaps slightly larger. One large central eye pointing forward, smaller eyes on flexible stalks arranged above the central one. Each stalk eye has what appears to be a small pupil. The smell when it uses them is very sharp — like lightning held in a room. I've never encountered anything like it, but I believe it matches descriptions of a gazer, which is a small beholder-kin creature, if that is useful."

> [!qna]**Your cat?**
>
> A brief pause before she answers, which is not her usual manner.
>
> > "Fillipa is eleven years old and she has been in the attic rafters for three days. She has been knocking books and rolled maps into the creature's path and retreating whenever it gets close. She has not been struck yet. I would like this to continue."

> [!qna]**The inventory?**
>
> > "The philosophy section has sustained significant damage — books knocked from the shelves, display cases of illuminated manuscripts overturned. The ozone smell has penetrated the bindings on the first two shelves. I have not been able to assess the second and third floor damage from outside." She considers. "There is a bundle of rare manuscripts on the third floor that I am concerned about."

The bookshop is three stories on Sorn Street. Each floor has been through the situation differently.

> [!gamemaster]**Shop Layout**
>
> **Ground Floor (Shop):** The philosophy section, shelf seven near the back wall, is the primary damage zone — books on the floor, display cases of illuminated manuscripts overturned. The smell is ozone-sharp, like lightning held in a room for days. The staircase to the second floor is intact.
>
> **Second Floor (Living Quarters):** Uza's apartments — bedroom, sitting room, kitchen, more books from her personal collection. Minor incidental damage. The gazer passed through here on the way up.
>
> **Third Floor (Attic Workshop):** Low ceiling, eight feet. Half storage — rolled maps, uncatalogued acquisitions in paper bundles — half workspace with a long table of bookbinding tools. Fillipa is ten feet up in the roof beams, uninjured, highly agitated, and has been providing sustained feline commentary on the situation for three days. The gazer is hunting her through the rafters.

### The Attic

The gazer is hostile and will not retreat. It has been instructed to stay until it finds the secondary document, and it is still looking.

> [!readaloud]
> The attic smells strongly of ozone, sharp and electric, and in the rafters above the long worktable a black-and-white cat is crouched on a crossbeam, watching something move below with the fixed concentration of an animal that has been doing this for days. The gazer is near the far wall, one eye stalk swiveling upward when it hears you come in.

> [!hazard]**The Gazer**
>
> The gazer (Gazer stat block, 2024 *Monster Manual*) is at the far end of the attic when the party enters, with Fillipa eight feet above it in the rafters.
>
> #### Gazer Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the gazer turns to face the largest cluster of party members and uses its eye rays immediately. It prioritizes characters who move toward it over those who stay at the door.
>
> Over the course of combat, the gazer will prioritize:
> - Maintaining distance from melee attackers, using the worktable and storage bundles as obstacles.
> - Targeting any character who climbs toward the rafters.
>
> The battle ends when the gazer is killed. It will not disengage or retreat.
>
> #### Stray Eye Rays
>
> Any stray eye ray that misses a character: roll a d6. On a 1–2, it strikes inventory.
> - **First hit:** Books worth 15 gp destroyed.
> - **Second hit:** A shelf collapses into the stairwell, creating difficult terrain on the stairs until cleared.
> - **Third hit:** A bundle of rare manuscripts catches arcane fire and is ruined.
>
> Uza notes each category of damage without placing blame.
>
> #### Fillipa
>
> Fillipa does not come down until the gazer is dead. She does not accept comfort from anyone before that point. A character who attempts to coax her down during combat finds she has moved further into the rafters. After the gazer is dead, she descends, assesses the situation, and accepts a reasonable amount of attention.

### What It Was Looking For

After the gazer is dealt with, the philosophy section on the ground floor is the next stop.

> [!readaloud]
> The philosophy section is a mess of books knocked from their shelves, and the smell of ozone has gotten into the bindings. Shelf seven, near the back wall, has a gap near the baseboard where the books have been pushed aside, and the mortar around one brick is slightly cleaner than the mortar around its neighbors.

> [!exploration]**The Dead Drop Brick**
>
> Any character who examines shelf seven at the philosophy section and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds a loose brick. Behind it: a folded cipher note and a small space sized for a second document that is not there.

> [!exploration]**The Scry-Residue**
>
> Any character who examines the cipher note and makes a successful DC 14 Arcana check detects fading scry-residue on the paper. Someone on the other end was watching when the gazer read it.
>
> > The cipher note contains partial contact identifiers for Harper assets in two noble houses. The knotwork cipher is Mattrim Mereg's system.

When reached, Mirt goes quiet for a moment. The secondary document he mentions carefully does not exist — Mattrim misplaced it three months ago. "That's going to be a problem." He gives the party one name: Tessalar, a clerk who started visiting the shop around the time the breach happened.

### The Informant

Tessalar Maeridge is a human clerk in his early twenties, working at a Trades Ward counting house two streets from Uza's shop. He started visiting the philosophy section three months ago, which is when his Splinter handler recruited him.

> [!readaloud]
> Tessalar Maeridge is not difficult to find — Mirt's description is precise. He is sitting at his counting house desk with the cautious stillness of someone who has been waiting for a consequence to arrive and is not entirely surprised that it has.

> [!social]**The Clerk**
>
> Tessalar Maeridge (Neutral, Tethyrian human, he/him) :: a young counting house clerk who made a mistake he did not understand the scale of, and knows it now.
>
> Tessalar took fifty gold pieces and instructions to report anything unusual about the philosophy section of a bookshop. He did not anticipate a gazer. He did not anticipate anyone getting hurt. He is scared, guilty, and not committed to the Splinter in any operational sense — his handler gave him a drop location and a cover story, and he has been using both without enthusiasm.
>
> He knows his handler's drop location: a ledger at the Trades Ward customs house, second drawer, a specific mark inside the cover. He did not expect anyone to ask him about this.
>
> A successful DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check, or simply confronting him with specific evidence of what the gazer read, is enough to flip him. He will give the party the customs house ledger location and cooperate with whatever they recommend.
>
> Conversation topics Tessalar is willing to discuss include:
> - How he was recruited, which he describes with obvious discomfort.
> - What he reported, which he can detail accurately.
> - His handler's customs house drop location, which he gives under any meaningful pressure.
>
> Specific dialogue for Tessalar on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**How were you recruited?**
>
> He looks at the desk before he answers.
>
> > "A man found me at Felzoun's. He knew my name and he knew I owed money. He said it was just watching — report anything strange about the philosophy section, nothing more than that. He gave me fifty gold." A pause. "I thought it was nothing. I didn't think it was nothing."

> [!qna]**What did you report?**
>
> > "A loose brick behind shelf seven. I saw someone check it once, just quickly, and then leave. I told him about the brick and about when people checked it. That's all I told him. I swear that's all."

> [!qna]**Your handler's location?**
>
> He gives it without a check if the party has confronted him with the gazer's activity.
>
> > "A ledger at the customs house on the Trades Ward side. Second drawer. There's a mark inside the cover — a small cross, bottom right corner. He checks it every few days."

> [!gamemaster]**Tessalar's Three Outcomes**
>
> **Turn him:** A successful DC 13 Charisma check (or simply confronting him with evidence) flips Tessalar. He becomes a double agent filing false reports. The Splinter's view of the Harper dead drop goes dark. His handler investigates eventually, but the Harpers have a window before that happens. This earns +1 Renown.
>
> **Report him to the Watch:** Tessalar is arrested. The Splinter investigates the breach; the customs house ledger is burned within a day. Clean for Tessalar's conscience; no secondary gain for the Harpers.
>
> **Warn Mirt and relocate the drop:** No secondary benefit, but clean. Mirt relocates the drop and thanks the party for the discretion. Tessalar is warned off.
>
> Regardless of Tessalar's fate, Uza gives the party a used spellbook from her back collection — a wizard who owed her more than he could pay. Four first-level spells and three second-level; the binding is coming loose but the text is sound.

### Concluding the Event

The Splinter's damage to the dead drop is done, but how much residual intelligence they hold depends entirely on what the party did with Tessalar.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Tessalar Turned** — mark when Tessalar is flipped as a double agent rather than arrested or warned off; award +1 Renown. The Splinter's view of the dead drop goes dark. Read by **Cassalanter Villa** (Mirt's Cassalanter social contacts remain intact; the asset compromise was intercepted).
> - **Handler's Ledger Recovered** — mark when the customs house ledger is recovered or destroyed before the Splinter burns it; award +1 Renown. Mirt gains two additional agent identifiers. Read by no downstream events directly, but noted in Mirt's intelligence file.
> - **Assets Compromised** — mark when the cipher note's contents reached the Splinter and Tessalar was not turned. Read by **Cassalanter Villa** (Mirt's Cassalanter intelligence is thinner; one social contact chain is broken until restored).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Uza becomes a recurring Trades Ward contact. She does not know she hosted a Harper dead drop and will not be told. She will help the party find books, references, and people who are very interested in very specific things.
>
> **Harper Mission 3 — The Doppelganger Auditions** becomes available when the party reaches Renown 5 and 4th level.

## Overview

A Harper dead drop in a Trades Ward bookshop has been breached. A gazer is inside, the shop owner's cat has been running a rearguard action against it for three days, and the clerk who sold out the location is still visiting the philosophy section on his handler's instructions.

## Summary

We cleared a gazer from Uza Solizeph's bookshop on Sorn Street and discovered a breached Harper dead drop behind the philosophy section's loose brick. Fillipa survived. We traced the breach to Tessalar Maeridge, a Trades Ward clerk who had been recruited by a Splinter handler for fifty gold pieces and instructions to watch one shelf. His handler's drop location at the customs house gave us leverage. Uza gave us a spellbook from her back collection in thanks, remarking that its binding was coming loose, which it was.
