# The Extraction

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Combat Event begins when the party decides to move Corene Wyldath, and it ends when Mirt has heard how her case and the records leak ended. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Get Corene off the plaza and into Mirt's cellar, past the Guild minders who watch her.
> - Force the devourer out with a ward, with magic or with a voice she trusts.
> - Kill the host, or leave her in place and feed Nihiloor a false report.
> - Name Tobin Harrask, the clerk who sells the Harper register, by any of three independent paths, and close the leak.
> - Report to Mirt and hear what he makes of the result.
>
> Only Harper members attend the debrief, and companions can help with everything before it.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The **Occupying Devourer** and its Extraction Procedure are in the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**. Hold starts at 3, each Break lowers it by 1, and the devourer is expelled at 0. This page gives the numbers the table needs.
> - The devourer wants to meet Mirt and follows the members to the cellar if they promise that meeting. It does not suspect a trap until the door is barred.
> - Tobin Harrask copies each register entry to Beldan Rusk exactly 48 hours later. The relay stamps every entry at 17:00, and Tobin carries the copies due that day out of the relay at 17:00.
> - He leaves them at the customs house at 17:30 in the second drawer of the rejected-cargo desk, and Rusk collects at 18:00.
> - Corene saw Tobin hand a packet to a Splinter courier outside the customs house two months ago. She thought it was Dock Ward business and told no one. The devourer knows it and will not say.
> - Until **Harper Leak Closed** is marked, every operational detail a member passes to a Harper contact other than Mirt reaches Rusk 48 hours later, as **The Cell Is Compromised** sets out.

### Moving Her

Corene stays on the bench until 16:30. To take her, a character has to give her a reason to leave, and the strongest reason is Mirt.

> [!exploration]**Getting Her Out**
>
> - A character who tells Corene that Mirt wants to see her tonight in a cellar off Sorn Street gets her agreement without a check. The devourer wants to meet Mirt and agrees a beat too fast, and a character who makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that.
> - A character who tries to force her off the bench in the plaza starts the fight below with the Hard roster.
> - The minders follow her wherever she goes. A character who leads her through the Cloth Market arcade and makes a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check loses them. On a failure, they reach the cellar door and attack there.
>
> If Corene is taken inside, Mirt bars the door from the stair above. He does not enter the room.

> [!hazard]**The Guild Minders**
>
> The minders are Nihiloor's plaza watchers in dock-carter coats. Use the roster for the party's size, and use the Hard roster if the devourer was alerted or the party forced her off the bench.
>
> - **Three characters:** two **Tough**. Hard roster: two **Tough** and a **Spy**.
> - **Four characters:** three **Tough**. Hard roster: three **Tough** and a **Warrior Veteran**.
> - **Five characters:** four **Tough**. Hard roster: four **Tough** and a **Warrior Veteran**.
>
> #### The Minders' Tactics
> The minders want Corene back and do not want to be seen killing in the Trades Ward.
>
> During combat, the minders:
> - Hold the street and block the way to the cellar, with Pack Tactics against any character within 5 feet of another minder.
> - Strike the character who is holding Corene before any other.
> - Retreat if Corene is inside the cellar, if the Warrior Veteran is down, or if a Watch patrol arrives in the second round.
>
> A minder reduced to half hit points or fewer surrenders if he is cornered, and he knows only that a woman paid him by the day.

### The Cellar

Mirt's cellar is a stone room under a closed cooper's shop off Sorn Street, with a barred door, two benches and a drain in the floor. Two vials of holy water stand on a shelf. If the party has no caster, Mirt sends for Remallia. She arrives within the hour and supplies one casting of *Dispel Magic* at 4th level, which is one Break and no more.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cellar smells of damp stone and old wine, with two benches against the wall and a lamp burning beside two small glass vials. Corene sits on the nearer bench with her book on her knees. Her eyes move from the door to your hands.
>
> > "Where's Mirt? He said the cellar, so I'll wait. Confirmed. Who else is on this?"

Once the door is barred, the devourer stops pretending and fights with Corene's body and weapons. The members need to hold her first, Restrained, Grappled or Incapacitated without being killed.

> [!hazard]**The Hosted Corene**
>
> Corene uses the **Spy** stat block, Small, with 27 hit points. The devourer inside her has Total Cover, so spells and attacks that target it fail and damage goes to the host. It keeps its own Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma and uses the host's attacks.
>
> #### The Devourer's Tactics
> The devourer wants to reach the door and the street, and it wants Corene alive and useful.
>
> During combat, the devourer:
> - Uses Cunning Action to Disengage or Dash toward the door.
> - Attacks the character nearest the door, with a Shortsword or Hand Crossbow for 12 damage on a hit.
> - Leaves any downed character alive.
> - Uses Slip Out when Hold is 1, unless the host is warded, and runs at speed 40.
>
> The encounter ends when Corene is held, extracted, killed or escapes into the street. A melee attack that reduces her to 0 hit points can leave her Unconscious at 1 HP under the nonlethal rule. Any other hit that reduces her to 0 puts her on death saving throws, and the devourer stays inside.

### Forcing It Out

The members can use any combination of the three routes below. Breaks from every source add together and can come from different characters in the same round.

> [!exploration]**The Extraction Procedure**
>
> Corene must be held before any route can start. Hold begins at 3 and returns to 3 one hour after the last Break.
>
> - **A ward.** A caster casts *Protection from Evil and Good* on Corene, using a vial of holy water worth 25 gp and keeping Concentration. At the start of each of Corene's turns she makes a DC 12 Intelligence saving throw with Advantage. A success is 1 Break and a failure is Strain. A warded host cannot be occupied again.
> - **Magic, Dispel Magic.** Cast with a 3rd-level slot, it needs a spellcasting ability check against DC 14 and gives 1 Break, or Strain on a failure. Cast with a 4th-level slot or higher, it gives 1 Break automatically.
> - **Magic, other spells.** *Remove Curse* gives 1 Break if the devourer fails an Intelligence saving throw (+2) against the caster's spell save DC, and Strain if it succeeds. *Greater Restoration* gives 2 Breaks and costs diamond dust worth 100 gp. *Telekinesis*, *Banishment* and *Dispel Evil and Good* do nothing.
> - **A voice she trusts.** A helper within 5 feet uses an action to make a Charisma (Persuasion) check. The DC is 14 with an anchor and 18 without, and a success is 1 Break and a failure is Strain.
> - **Anchors.** Corene's anchors are her Harper pin, the name "Halla Ironstave" spoken as a question, and Dalen Voss if **Salon Guest Leads Recorded** is marked and the party carries Saeth Cromley's account. A helper who is the anchor has Advantage, a stranger shouting has Disadvantage, and lying does not work.
> - **Strain.** Each failed attempt deals 2d6 psychic damage to Corene, never more than 14 and never below 1 hit point. At 1 hit point the next failure resets Hold to 3 for an hour, and only *Greater Restoration* earns Breaks until it passes.
>
> With one route running, expect four or five rounds. With all three running, expect two or three. When Hold falls to 1 by the third route, Corene surfaces.

When Hold falls to 1 through the voice of a helper, read the following aloud and then let her go under again:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Corene's eyes stop following the door and settle on the person who has been speaking. Her mouth works, and a voice comes out that is lower and rougher than the one you have heard all afternoon.
>
> > "Fuck. I'm here. I'm still here."

When Hold reaches 0, the devourer is expelled.

> [!hazard]**The Expelled Devourer**
>
> The **Occupying Devourer** appears within 5 feet of Corene at full hit points: Tiny, AC 12, 28 hit points, speed 40. Corene is Incapacitated until the end of her next turn.
>
> #### The Devourer's Tactics
> The devourer tries to take a new host before it runs.
>
> During combat, the devourer:
> - Uses Occupy Body on the nearest eligible creature, which is Incapacitated, a Small or Medium Humanoid or Beast, and has 10 hit points or fewer. Corene is eligible if Strain left her at 10 hit points or fewer and she is not warded.
> - Otherwise uses Devour Intellect on the nearest creature and runs for cover.
> - Does not leave the cellar, because the door is barred.
>
> The encounter ends when it dies or is trapped under a barrel or in a bottle. It has 28 hit points and no way out.

### What the Party Decides

After the diagnosis the members choose among three endings. They can change their minds until the devourer is out.

**Extract Corene.** Run the procedure above. When the devourer is dead or trapped, Corene sits on the bench with one level of Exhaustion and answers in fragments. A Long Rest returns her memory in full, and she remembers the occupation as a long dream in which she saw and heard everything and could do nothing. Mark **Corene Rescued**.

**Kill the host.** Mirt does not order it and does not argue with it. If Corene is reduced to 0 hit points and dies, the devourer is forced out within 5 feet at full hit points, and the members face the Expelled Devourer as written. Mark **Corene Lost**. The Guild's Dock Ward sites rise to Alert for 14 days or stay at any higher tier, and a Watch patrol that saw the killing in the plaza opens a file.

**Leave her in place.** The members keep Corene under watch and feed Nihiloor one false report through her. The devourer must not be alerted, so *Detect Thoughts* and accusations spoil it and the Hosted Corene fight begins. Mark **Corene Left in Place** while the occupation continues.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt draws the curtain over the small window of the private room and sets a sealed envelope on the table. A single lamp shines on it, and he looks around at the members before he taps it once.
>
> > "Wil will put this in her hand at the bench tomorrow at four. It says a Harper cart will collect an escaped Guild clerk at the Shrimp Street warehouse, rear door, at eight the next evening, and there is no clerk and no cart."

The minders carry the report. Any member who watches the Shrimp Street warehouse from the public street at 19:45 on the day after Wil delivers it sees two Guild guards leave their posts and watch the rear door until 20:30. Mark **Nihiloor False Report Confirmed** when they do. The absence lasts forty-five minutes and changes nothing else about the lair. Corene stays occupied and at risk, though a later extraction can still rescue her.

### Corene's Debrief

After one Long Rest, a rescued Corene sits with Mirt and the members and goes through her four months. Read the version that fits.

If **Xanathar's Lair** has not been played, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt has spread the account sheets across a table by the window. Corene finds the Shrimp Street warehouse on the top sheet and puts a finger on the cellar stair.
>
> > "Ahmaergo and Noska Ur'gray run the Dock Ward routes, and the restricted deliveries all went to Nihiloor's rooms in the lair. I kept the labels with my account, so give them to Mirt before you go below."

If **Xanathar's Lair** has been played, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt has spread the account sheets across a table by the window. Corene turns the top sheet toward you and counts three addresses on Shrimp Street with her finger.
>
> > "Those warehouses are still open under Ahmaergo's captains, and Nihiloor kept a clerk in the Watch office and two runners on the docks. They're the ones still carrying orders, and I can tell you who meets whom."

Corene's intelligence is four months of Dock Ward routes and the names of two Guild captains. Before the lair, it adds the labelled deliveries to Nihiloor's rooms, areas X23 to X27. After the lair, it names the Guild sites and the puppets that remain.

### The Register

The records relay is a back room above a stationer's at 19 Brondar's Way, where Tobin Harrask works from 09:00 to 18:00. Mirt's signed note admits the members. They can use any of the three paths below in any order, and one path is enough to name him.

> [!gamemaster]**Private Protocol**
>
> If **Harper Private Protocol Adopted** is marked for a member, Mirt can rule out every channel that member kept to. That gives the member Advantage on the Path One check below. If it is not marked, one of that member's reports went through the relay and Rusk has it, with no further mechanical cost.

> [!exploration]**Path One: The Dates**
>
> A character sets the incidents Mirt gave in **The Cell Is Compromised** beside the relay's duty roster and makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check. Each copy left the building 48 hours after its entry, and Tobin was on the desk every time. The other thirteen people who touched the material were each away at least once.
>
> On a failure, the comparison takes until the next morning and gives the same answer. This path is always open.

> [!exploration]**Path Two: The Pickup**
>
> This path is open if **Handler Ledger Read** or **Tessalar Turned** is marked. Mirt or Tessalar then names the second drawer of the rejected-cargo desk at the customs house, where a clerk leaves a packet at 17:30 for the handler to collect at 18:00. The register shows which days a copy falls due.
>
> A character who watches the cargo yard from cover on one of those days and makes a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check sees Tobin leave a packet. On a failure, Tobin sees the watcher, turns around and returns to the relay without leaving it. The character has seen his face either way.
>
> If **Handler Ledger Lost** is marked and **Tessalar Turned** is not, this path is closed.

> [!exploration]**Path Three: What Corene Remembers**
>
> This path is open once Corene has finished a Long Rest. She describes a thin man with ink on his cuffs and a blue scarf who handed a packet to a Splinter courier outside the customs house two months ago. Mirt names him as soon as she finishes the description.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The room above the stationer's smells of ink and old paper, with ledgers stacked along one wall and a single window over the desk. A thin man with ink on his cuffs and a blue scarf at his neck looks up from his copying and opens the register beside his tray.
>
> > "Good morning. Which date would you like to check? If you tell me the name as well, I can find the entry quickly."

> [!social]**The Clerk**
>
> Tobin Harrask (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a thin clerk in his forties who has copied the register for six years and has wanted something better for most of them.
>
> Tobin is polite and quick to help, and he fills silences with offers of spare copies. If the members show him the evidence, he lays his pen down and keeps his hands on the desk. He does not fight, though a bolting Tobin heads for the back stair.
>
> Tobin will discuss the following topics:
>
> - The entries he copied and the dates they left the building.
> - Beldan Rusk, whom he knows only as the man who collects from the drawer.
> - The post he was promised.
>
> Tobin does not know who leads the Splinter, and he will not discuss anything he does not know.

> [!qna]**Who is Rusk?**
>
> > "He collects the packets at the customs house at six, and I've never seen where he goes after. He said there were people who looked after clerks, and I thought that would be a post for me."

> [!qna]**What did you copy?**
>
> > "Every operational entry, two days after it went in, and I made the copies myself. The box under my desk holds the ones that haven't gone out yet, and the key is on my belt."

If Tobin runs, a character who stops him on the back stair makes a successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. On a failure he escapes, and the members use the spare key Mirt gave them to open the box.

> [!exploration]**Closing the Route**
>
> Mirt signs Tobin's suspension and removes his access to the register. The members take the register, the duplicate sheets and the dispatch box and read out which copies have already left.
>
> Mark **Harper Mole Identified** when the evidence names Tobin. Mark **Harper Leak Closed** when his access is gone and the box is emptied. Tobin's escape does not keep the leak open.
>
> Copies already delivered stay with Rusk, so list their contents for later Splinter opposition. Mirt asks the members to leave Rusk alone, because a handler they know is better than one they do not.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt takes the members' report at 20:00 on the day it ends, or at 09:00 the next morning, and he asks for Corene's state and for the register on separate sheets. Read the branch that fits.

If **Corene Rescued** is marked, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt reads the report with his hands folded on the table, and then he pours for each of you.
>
> > "She's alive, and she'll be useful again in a tenday if she rests first. Three devourers, you know, and three of ours. Nihiloor has been collecting Harpers all season, and I'd like to finish it."

If **Corene Lost** is marked, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt reads the report slowly and puts his pen down at the end of the page. He does not speak for a moment.
>
> > "Was the creature out of her before she died? I'll tell the people who need warning. Three devourers, and three of ours, so when you go in, finish it."

If **Corene Left in Place** is marked, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt turns the report over and reads the back with his mouth set in a thin line.
>
> > "She's still in there, and I don't like it. Three devourers, and three of ours. We'll do this again when the Guild stops looking at her."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains 4 base Renown for reporting to Mirt, whatever happened to Corene and whether or not the leak closed. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:
>
> - **+1 Renown:** Corene is extracted alive, and **Corene Rescued** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** **Harper Leak Closed** is marked before any entry made after Mirt's brief has reached Rusk.
>
> Companions who are not Harper members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

### Aftermath

A rescued Corene spends a tenday resting at the cellar and then returns to field work. A lost Corene leaves the Harper cell short one Spy in the Dock Ward. A Corene left in place keeps meeting contacts at the bench, and Mirt does not use her for anything else.

Nihiloor learns that a link has gone dead the moment it does, and the Dock Ward sites stay at Alert if the host was killed. Rusk does not know Tobin is gone until a copy fails to arrive. Mirt expects the Splinter to replace Tobin with someone harder to find.

### Concluding the Event

The members' report ends the mission. Record what happened to Corene and what happened to the register separately, because later events read each.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Corene Rescued** — mark when Corene is free of the devourer and alive; read by **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted), which reads her intelligence whether or not the heist has run.

> - **Corene Left in Place** — mark while the party deliberately keeps the occupation; read by **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted), which reads the actual planted report.
> - **Nihiloor False Report Confirmed** — mark when the party sees the guards leave their posts; read by **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted).

We took Corene to Mirt's cellar and dealt with what lived in her head. We also found Tobin Harrask, who had been selling every Harper report to a Splinter handler, and we shut the route he used.
