# The Sleeping Asset: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Harper member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level, after **A Friend's House**. Companions can help with everything except Mirt's brief and debrief.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 6th-level characters.*
>
> Corene uses the **Spy** stat block while the custom **Occupying Devourer** rides her. Guild minders use **Tough** and, on a Hard day, a **Warrior Veteran**. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** sets every roster for three, four and five characters and gives the full Extraction Procedure.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Harper members who report to Mirt.

## Hook

At 05:00 Mirt lets himself into the Trollskull Manor taproom through the back door, using a key he had copied. Corene Wyldath, a Harper Spy living in the Dock Ward as "Halla Ironstave", missed her check-ins three weeks ago, and Mirt wants her found before he decides what to ask of her.

## Background

Corene has worked inside the Xanathar Guild's Dock Ward operation for four months. Three weeks ago one of Nihiloor's devourers took her, and it has used her since to learn who the Harpers are. The second problem is older. Someone in the Harper records relay has been copying every operational entry to a Splinter handler, and **The Cell Is Compromised** has already told the members that a leak exists.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> The devourer is a custom variant that keeps Corene's brain alive. She is aware and cannot act, and she can be saved by forcing the devourer out. Nihiloor has put devourers into three Harpers, and Mirt says so at the end. Corene's cover is sound, and her reports are accurate, but her questions are about the Harpers instead of the Guild.
>
> The mole is Tobin Harrask, a records-relay clerk. He copies each entry to Beldan Rusk 48 hours after it is entered, hoping the Splinter will pay him for it. Mirt says "the handler" unless **Handler Ledger Read** or **Nethpranter Safehouse Reported** is marked. Tobin's confession also names Rusk. Nobody in this mission names the Splinter's leader.

## Finding Corene

Mirt gives the members three leads, and each ends at the same bench in the Trades Ward plaza where Corene meets her contacts. The members find her there, and the diagnosis comes from watching how she answers.

## The Extraction

Corene follows the members to a private cellar if they promise her a meeting with Mirt. Guild minders follow them. The members hold her, force the devourer out with a ward, with magic or with a voice she trusts, or they choose to kill her or to leave her in place and feed her a false report.

## The Register

The leak runs through the records relay, and there are three independent ways to name Tobin Harrask: the dates of the incidents Mirt gave them, the customs-house pickup, and what Corene remembers once she is free. The members confront him, remove his access and secure the copies still waiting in his box.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Harper member gains 4 base Renown for reporting to Mirt. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** Corene is extracted alive.
- **+1 Renown:** **Harper Leak Closed** is marked before any entry made after Mirt's brief reaches the handler.

Companions who are not Harper members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Xanathar's Lair** reads whether Corene was rescued, lost or left in place, and her intelligence works whether or not that heist has run. The rank events and **Kolat Towers** read **Harper Leak Closed**. **The Stone's Other Master** becomes available when an individual Harper member reaches Renown 13 and 7th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): briefs the members and takes their report, and stays out of the devourer's sight.
- **Corene Wyldath** (Harpers): the Spy under the cover name Halla Ironstave, carrying the devourer.
- **Remallia Haventree** (Harpers): can supply one casting of *Dispel Magic* if Mirt asks her.
- **Wil Keen**, **Pell Tormar** and **Sella Brant** (Harper allies): a courier, a harbormaster's assistant and a Field Ward landlady who each saw Corene.
- **Tobin Harrask** (Harper records relay, secretly the Splinter's): the clerk who copies the register.
- **Beldan Rusk** (the Splinter): the handler who collects the copies, who never appears in person.
- **Nihiloor** (Xanathar's Guild): the mind flayer behind the devourer, who never appears.

## Dangers & Enemies

The hosted Corene fights as a **Spy**, with the **Occupying Devourer** inside her, and Guild minders hold the street with **Tough** and a **Warrior Veteran**. The expelled devourer is Tiny, has 28 hit points and tries to take a new host. Killing Corene sends the Guild's Dock Ward sites to Alert for 14 days.

## Overview

A Harper agent in the Dock Ward has stopped reporting, and Mirt asks the party to find her. They also have to find out who in the Harper records is selling the Splinter every report.
