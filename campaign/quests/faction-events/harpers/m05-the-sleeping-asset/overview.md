# The Sleeping Asset: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> This mission is available to Harper members at Renown 10 and 6th level after **A Friend's House**. If the trigger for **The Cell Is Compromised** was met, that event has already given the party an early warning. If it wasn't, this brief explains the investigation from the start.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 6th-level characters.*
>
> The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** audits the standard Spy host, the custom intellect-devourer variant and the optional two-Spy interception. It counts the combatants who actually take part, so the number of party members doesn't set the combat baseline.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

Mirt arrives before dawn and asks the members to find Corene Wyldath and to look into a separate leak in the Harper register. He briefs the members in private. Their companions can help with the searches, and with the capture and rescue afterward.

## Background

Corene spent four months undercover in Xanathar's Dock Ward operation under the name Halla Ironstave. Her last proper check-in was three weeks ago, and nine days of legitimate isolation during a Guild investigation followed it. Twelve days ago Nihiloor implanted a brain-preserving parasite in her. It suppresses her control of her own body, keeps her alive, and uses her memories to maintain the cover.

Orren Vale, the Harper records-relay clerk, separately copies registered operational reports to Beldan Rusk for Manshoon's Splinter. Each copy reaches Rusk forty-eight hours after the entry is made. The two channels feed different villains and don't pass intelligence to each other, and rescuing Corene leaves Orren's access untouched.

## Finding Both Routes

Three witnesses each send the party to Corene's regular bench in the Trades Ward. Mirt also authorizes three ways to get at the register leak: inspecting Orren's annotated register, watching the customs-house collection point, and feeding the register a false extraction report with unique wording. Any one of these identifies the clerk, so the party never has to work through all three.

## The Agent at the Bench

Corene answers familiar questions, but she keeps asking for operational names she wouldn't normally need. The party can work out what has happened to her by watching her for a while, by examining her, or by probing her thoughts. Each method stays open if another one fails. How to handle a living victim whose body answers to an enemy is the party's decision.

## Extraction and Closure

The party can end the curse with its own magic, or bring Corene alive to Remallia's prepared extraction room. Killing the host and leaving her in place both have fixed consequences, and leaving her in place also lets Mirt plant a specific false report. Whatever happens to Corene, the party still has to secure Orren's access and his queued copies before the register leak counts as closed.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Harper member gains 4 base Renown for investigating Corene and reporting how her case ended, along with the register findings or the fact that they remain open.

- **+1 Renown:** release Corene alive by ending the occupation curse, then bring her safely through one Long Rest.
- **+1 Renown:** leave her in place and confirm, through the warehouse response described in the event, that Mirt's single false report reached Nihiloor.

Each bonus is earned once, when its condition is completed. Choosing an approach and not finishing it earns nothing. Helping companions gain no Harper Renown or membership.

## Aftermath

A rescued Corene gives the party four months of Dock Ward intelligence once she has rested. A planted report draws a limited surveillance response at the warehouse. If Corene dies before extraction, Guild Dock Ward sites rise to at least Alert for fourteen days. Closing Orren's route stops any copies still queued under his control, though anything delivered earlier stays with the Splinter.

**Xanathar's Lair** reads Corene's recorded outcome and the specific intelligence that goes with it. That is separate from the general Renown 30 benefit, which the party doesn't receive early. **The Stone's Other Master** becomes available at Renown 13 and 7th level once the Stone has at least one restored Eye.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** and **Remallia Haventree** (Harpers): Mirt runs the investigation, and Remallia supervises the extraction.
- **Corene Wyldath** (Harpers): a living Halfling informant occupied by Nihiloor's custom parasite.
- **Orren Vale** (Harpers, secret Manshoon informant): the records-relay clerk, who hopes betrayal will buy him security.
- **Beldan Rusk** (Manshoon Splinter): receives Orren's copies at the established collection point.
- **Nihiloor** (Xanathar's Guild): receives the parasite's daily report and doesn't appear in this mission.

## Dangers & Enemies

Corene's occupied body uses the ordinary 2024 Spy statistics, and the expelled parasite uses the verified variant in the mechanics reference. Two Spies may attend the false-destination trap. Orren avoids a fight and can be arrested or allowed to escape, so he never becomes a boss encounter.

## Overview

The party searches for an absent Harper agent and the clerk behind a separate reporting leak, choosing how to protect a living victim while securing the compromised records.
