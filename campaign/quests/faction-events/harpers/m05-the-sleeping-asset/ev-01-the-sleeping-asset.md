### Background

Corene's cover is Halla Ironstave, a warehouse accounts runner. She missed her check-in three weeks ago, then remained legitimately isolated for nine days before Nihiloor implanted his parasite twelve days ago. The custom creature preserves her brain and body under an occupation curse, using her memories while suppressing voluntary control. It reports to Nihiloor at 18:00 each day through the fixed campaign link in the mechanics reference.

Orren Vale is a Waterdhavian Human records-relay clerk, he/him, working at the Harper room at 22 Saerdoun Street from 09:00 to 18:00. He offers spare copies and receipts, asking for spellings and dates in ordinary clerical language. He sends registered reports to Beldan Rusk exactly forty-eight hours after entry, hoping Manshoon's people will give him a secure administrative post. Private Mirt briefings aren't registered and don't enter this channel.

### Corene at the Well

Corene arrives on time, looking rested and responding with the familiar precision of a field agent. Her answers below use Corene’s memories of Guild work and turn toward current Harper contacts and plans.

> [!exploration]**Establishing Occupation**
>
> A successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check during conversation notices the sustained eye contact and rehearsed half-second pauses, establishing a change in her normal responses. After five minutes, a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine) check recognizes signs of the brain-preserving occupation variant: involuntary reactions missing beside intact learned behavior and a magical interruption of voluntary control. Both findings together establish the diagnosis; neither makes her a willing traitor.
>
> A character who asks Corene to repeat a personal memory Mirt supplied can also compare it with her account. Mirt's fixed example concerns her first report, when rain ruined the warehouse tally and she recopied it while complaining about wet paper. The original report reads, “Rain spoiled the warehouse tally. I recopied the figures and delivered the account before the next collection. The totals were correct.” The parasite recites it word for word, then can't explain why that mattered to her, returning to present contact names. Taking that exact mismatch to Remallia establishes the diagnosis through her examination without another check. This remains available after failed rolls.
>
> A successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices her questions becoming intelligence collection. *Detect Thoughts* uses its printed rules: surface reading reveals controlled work questions without automatically alerting her; deeper probing uses the spell's Wisdom save and awareness rules. A successful deeper probe encounters the parasite's separate intention to report at 18:00, providing another diagnostic path. The spell doesn't expose a hidden creature as a visible target.

### Direct Extraction
