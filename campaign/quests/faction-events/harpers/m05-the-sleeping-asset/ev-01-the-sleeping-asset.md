# The Sleeping Asset

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Social Event begins with Mirt's private brief and ends once the party has reported how Corene's case was resolved and what the register investigation found. During the Event, the party can:
>
> - Find Corene through three witnesses and work out what is controlling her.
> - Expose Orren through his records, through surveillance of the collection point, or through a bait report with unique wording.
> - Extract the parasite directly, or use Remallia's prepared live-capture service.
> - Resolve Corene's case by killing her or by deceiving the Guild through her, without ever treating her as a willing traitor.
> - Remove Orren's access and secure his queued copies before marking the leak closed.

### The Brief

At 05:00, Mirt lets himself into the taproom through the back door with the copied key he keeps for emergencies. If the party is staying somewhere else, he waits outside the rear entrance and asks to be let in. He gives the operational brief in a private room to the participating Harper members, and helping companions can join the fieldwork afterward.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom is dim and smells of last night's ale when you come downstairs. Mirt is already waiting beside one of the tables, still in his coat, with a folded account in one broad hand. He keeps it closed while the serving staff drift past, then glances at the last date on it once the members have followed him into the private room.
>
> > "Gather the Harper members somewhere we can talk in private, because I have two matters for you and neither one is meant for the taproom."
>
> When the door is shut, Mirt unfolds the account and lays it flat.
>
> > "Corene Wyldath hasn't checked in properly for three weeks. She's spent four months inside the Guild's Dock Ward operation, and something there has changed, so I want you to find her before we decide what we can safely ask of her."
>
> > "The register leak is a separate matter, and we finally have a way to examine it. Find out who is sending the reports and tell me what has gone out and what we can still stop, because closing one channel won't close the other."

> [!qna]**Where do we begin?**
>
> > "Dena Voss will meet you outside the Shrimp Street warehouse at ten, and Pell Tormar is at the cargo office from nine until five. Halla rents a room at Three Lanterns House, seven Windborne Way, so any of the three may have seen her."
>
> > "For the register, my signed authority gets you into the record room at twenty-two Saerdoun Street, where you can compare the dispatch entries with what you've reported. You can also follow a clerk carrying the next due packet, or I can send different destinations through the fourteen handlers and see which one draws attention."

> [!qna]**What personal memory can we ask about?**
>
> > "Ask her about her first warehouse report, when rain ruined the tally and she recopied it, complaining the whole time about wet paper. She gave me the figures correctly, but she was furious about doing the work twice, so hear what she remembers before you show her my account."

### Background

Corene's cover is Halla Ironstave, a warehouse accounts runner. She missed her check-in three weeks ago and was then legitimately isolated for nine days. Nihiloor implanted his parasite twelve days ago. The custom creature preserves her brain and body under an occupation curse and uses her memories while it suppresses her voluntary control. It reports to Nihiloor at 18:00 each day through the fixed campaign link in the mechanics reference.

Orren Vale (Waterdhavian Human, he/him) is a records-relay clerk at the Harper room at 22 Saerdoun Street, where he works from 09:00 to 18:00. He offers spare copies and receipts and asks for spellings and dates in ordinary clerical language. He sends each registered report to Beldan Rusk forty-eight hours after it is entered, hoping Manshoon's people will reward him with a secure administrative post. Mirt's private briefings are never registered, so they never enter this channel.

### Corene at the Well

Corene arrives on time. She looks rested and answers with the precision you'd expect from a field agent. Her answers below draw on Corene's memories of Guild work, and every one of them turns toward current Harper contacts and plans.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The plaza around the public well is busy with people filling buckets and crossing between the market stalls. On the west bench, a Halfling woman sits with a wrapped account book on her lap, watching the crowd before she turns to look at you. She shifts along the bench to make room and turns the book so its opening faces her.
>
> > "You're the contact. Good. We've got about ten minutes before I have to go back. Who's reading the reports now, and who else is helping you with this?"

> [!social]**A Familiar Agent under Control**
>
> Corene Wyldath (Neutral Good, Halfling, she/her) is a living Harper agent whose controlled answers hide an occupation curse.
>
> The real Corene is loyal and can't direct her own body. She isn't an informant willingly serving both factions. The parasite gives the answers below and keeps her remembered work habits intact. If the party notices the steering, use her response under **The Occupied Host** before she tries to leave for the warehouse.

> [!qna]**Why didn't you check in?**
>
> > "The Guild was checking every route out of the warehouse, so I stayed where the papers were moving. I had no safe way to send a message. Who did Mirt ask to cover the gap?"

> [!qna]**Who sent us? Who needs these names?**
>

> [!qna]**What happened to your first warehouse tally?**
>

> [!exploration]**Establishing Occupation**
>
> Any character who talks with Corene and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check notices her fixed eye contact and rehearsed half-second pauses, which shows that her normal way of answering has changed. Any character who spends five minutes with her and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine) check recognizes the signs of the brain-preserving occupation variant: involuntary reactions are missing while learned behavior stays intact, and something magical is cutting off her voluntary control. Both findings together establish the diagnosis, and neither makes her a willing traitor.
>
> A character who asks Corene to repeat the personal memory Mirt supplied can compare it with her account. Mirt's example is her first report, when rain ruined the warehouse tally and she recopied it while complaining about wet paper. The original report reads, "Rain spoiled the warehouse tally. I recopied the figures and delivered the account before the next collection. The totals were correct." The parasite recites it word for word, then can't explain why any of it mattered to her and goes back to asking for current contact names. Taking that mismatch to Remallia establishes the diagnosis through her own examination, with no further check, and the route stays open after failed rolls.
>
> Any character who listens to her questions and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that they have turned into intelligence collection. *Detect Thoughts* works by its printed rules. A surface reading reveals controlled work questions and doesn't alert her, while deeper probing uses the spell's Wisdom save and awareness rules. A successful deeper probe finds the parasite's separate intention to report at 18:00, which gives the party another way to the diagnosis. The spell doesn't reveal the hidden creature as a visible target.

### Direct Extraction

> [!gamemaster]**Ending the Occupation Curse**
>
> *Remove Curse* cast on a visible Corene ends the custom occupation at once. No locating check is needed and the parasite gets no save. The parasite appears in the nearest unoccupied space within 5 feet of her, breaking ties in the order north, east, south, west. If that ring is full, it appears in the nearest free space beyond it, using the same order. *Greater Restoration* ends the curse the same way. Corene keeps her current HP, and her living brain needs no regrowth.
>
> The hidden parasite has Total Cover, so it can't be targeted directly with *Telekinesis* or *Banishment*, and moving or banishing Corene doesn't pull it out. *Protection from Evil and Good* stops a new occupation, but it doesn't end an existing curse or give a new saving throw.
>
> In an unprepared street extraction, roll the parasite's initiative. It acts on its next turn and gets no free immediate attack. Its rules for entering a new host still apply as the mechanics reference gives them: the target must be an Incapacitated Small or Medium Humanoid or Beast within 5 feet with 10 HP or fewer, and it must then fail a DC 12 Intelligence save. An expelled creature can't take another healthy host.

> [!hazard]**The Expelled Parasite**
>
> Use the complete 2024 **Intellect Devourer**, with the occupation replacement and fixed communication rule from the mechanics reference.
>
> #### Parasite Tactics
> The parasite starts in the space where it was ejected and heads for the nearest open exit.
>
> During combat, the parasite:
> - Moves toward an open escape route and doesn't stay beside Corene.
> - Uses Multiattack against different available targets if it is blocked, keeping the printed Claw and Devour Intellect saves.
> - Tries a new occupation only when the custom variant's requirements are met, meaning a damaged and Incapacitated host.
>
> The encounter ends with its escape, containment or defeat. Ejection cuts the 18:00 link at once, and Nihiloor notices the missed report at 18:00 but doesn't learn where the extraction happened.
