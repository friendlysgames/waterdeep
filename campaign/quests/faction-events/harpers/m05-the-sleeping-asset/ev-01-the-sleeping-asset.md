# The Sleeping Asset

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Social Event begins when Mirt arrives at Trollskull Manor before dawn and ends when Corene's situation is resolved. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Mirt in person, before dawn, through Trollskull Manor's back door
> - Locate Corene Wyldath through three independent investigation beats in the Dock Ward and Field Ward
> - Identify the signs of intellect devourer occupation through sustained interaction, not a single roll
> - Discover that the devourer knows when Detect Thoughts is cast on it
> - Resolve Corene's situation through one of three paths: extract the devourer, eliminate her, or use her as a double agent
>
> #### Background
>
> Nihiloor implanted the intellect devourer twelve days ago. The devourer is using Corene as a surveillance asset: she has been attending Harper contact meetings and reporting everything back through Nihiloor's psychic link. She is not in distress. She is not a prisoner. She genuinely appears functional. Nothing about her surface behavior is wrong except for things that require close observation or specific diagnostics to notice.
>
> Nihiloor's devourers have touched three Harper assets total — this fact surfaces in the **Xanathar's Lair** briefing hooks, where Mirt offers tactical support for the lair heist if the party commits to eliminating Nihiloor. Corene's situation is the evidence behind that request.
>
> The connection between Corene's devourer and Nihiloor is psychic. Detect Thoughts that penetrates below the surface layer reaches the devourer's monitoring function — and the devourer knows the spell is active the moment it is cast.

### The Brief

Mirt comes in through the back door of Trollskull Manor before dawn, which means he had a key copied at some point. He sits in the taproom until someone comes downstairs.

> [!readaloud]
> The back door opens sometime before the first light. Mirt is in the taproom when you come downstairs, a large man in a traveling coat, sitting at the bar with an untouched drink in front of him and the look of someone who has not slept. He does not bother with a greeting.
>
> > "I need to know if she's dead, captured, or something worse. And if it's something worse: I need you to handle it."
>
> He pushes the drink aside.

> [!social]**The Moneylender Before Dawn**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan human, he/him) :: a moneylender and Harper handler, here before dawn through a key he copied, which is how he behaves when something has gone genuinely wrong.
>
> Mirt is in business mode and stripped of the Old Wolf's warmth. His sentences are short and complete and he wastes nothing. He has been patient for three tendays and he is not anymore. He will answer the necessary questions clearly and does not swear in this register.
>
> Conversation topics Mirt is willing to discuss include:
> - Who Corene is and what she was doing
> - What he knows, which is less than he would like
> - How to report back when the party has an answer
>
> Specific dialogue for Mirt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Who is she?**
>
> > "A halfling named Corene Wyldath. I've had her embedded in Xanathar's Dock Ward operation for six months. She uses the assumed name 'Halla Ironstave' in the Dock Ward. She's good — she checks in on schedule, her reports are clean. Three tendays ago she stopped checking in."

> [!qna]**Is she dead?**
>
> > "I would know if she were dead. I have enough contacts in the Dock Ward to know if a body turned up. She is not dead." He pauses. "That's why I'm here."

> [!qna]**What do you know about her last movements?**
>
> > "She was seen at a Guild warehouse on Shrimp Street three tendays ago, during a Watch sweep. My contact said she was unusually calm. That's wrong. Nobody who's been doing that work for six months stays that calm during a sweep." He tilts his glass without drinking from it. "I don't know what's wrong. That's the job."

> [!qna]**How do we report back?**
>
> > "Paper bird. Or come find me. If it's bad, come find me."

### Phase 1 — The Search

Three independent investigation beats lead to Corene's regular meeting point in the Trades Ward.

> [!readaloud]
> The Dock Ward is well into its morning work by the time you arrive. Early deliveries crowd the lanes, dray horses work in harness, and the smell of the harbor sharpens the cold air. The stablehands and dock workers move around you without breaking pace.

> [!exploration]**Harper Dock Ward Contacts**
>
> Available to party members with Renown 3 or higher. Their contacts in the Dock Ward describe a halfling woman matching Corene's description at a Guild warehouse on Shrimp Street three tendays ago, during a Watch sweep. She was calm in a way that nobody with six months of embedded work is calm during a sweep.

> [!exploration]**The Harbormaster's Assistant**
>
> A minor Harper sympathizer who handles cargo routing. He has seen a halfling woman fitting Corene's description twice recently at Guild meetings she had no reason to attend as cover — cargo reviews that handle routing decisions, not street-level work. This is not what she was placed to observe.

> [!exploration]**The Boarding House**
>
> Asking in the Field Ward under the name "Halla Ironstave" finds her landlord: she paid three months in advance last tenday. That is not like her. He gives her current address and notes she has been at home more than usual lately.
>
> All three leads converge on her regular meeting point: a specific bench in the Trades Ward plaza she uses for dead drops.

### Phase 2 — The Encounter

Corene is at the bench at the expected time. She looks fine.

> [!readaloud]
> You find her on the bench in the Trades Ward plaza, the correct bench at the expected time. She is calm and unhurried, well-rested and neatly dressed, sitting with her hands in her lap and no particular urgency about her. She gives you her full attention the moment you approach.
>
> > "You're the contact. Good." She glances at the street behind you once, out of habit, or what passes for habit. "We have time. I'm sorry about the silence — there was a Guild operation I had to stay close to. No opportunity to check in safely."
>
> Her eye contact does not waver.

> [!social]**Halla Ironstave**
>
> Corene Wyldath (Neutral Good, halfling, she/her) :: Harper field agent, six months embedded in Xanathar's Dock Ward operation; currently hosting an intellect devourer; everything about her surface behavior is correct and slightly wrong.
>
> The devourer has been careful. Corene's cover is intact, her reports have been plausible, and nothing about her at a glance is visibly wrong. The tells are subtle and require engagement to surface.
>
> - Her eye contact is too steady. People doing tense fieldwork for three tendays have involuntary breaks; hers do not.
> - Her speech is too considered. Every sentence has a half-second of processing before it arrives, and it reads as preparation rather than doubt, as though she is reviewing what a response should look like.
> - **DC 15 Wisdom (Insight)** during conversation catches both patterns together and identifies them as wrong.
> - **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine)**, made after at least five minutes of sustained conversation: identifies signs of intellect devourer occupation — flat affect, absence of involuntary reactions, a faint smell of psychic residue that is difficult to place.
>
> If she realizes the party is getting close, she begins asking specific questions about the party's current plans, schedules, and contacts — gathering more intelligence before her handler is alerted. A successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** check recognizes this as intelligence-gathering, not conversation.
>
> **Detect Thoughts:** Surface thoughts are flat and controlled, stripped of Corene's fear and improvisation. Below that layer, she is monitoring the party and transmitting back. The devourer is aware of the spell the moment it is cast. It does not react visibly. It knows.
>
> Conversation topics she is willing to discuss include:
> - Her absence, for which she offers a plausible explanation
> - Her recent intelligence from the Dock Ward, which is real but incomplete — she has been at Guild meetings she was not placed to observe
> - The party's current assignments and who else they are working with

> [!qna]**Your absence?**
>
> She answers without a pause.
>
> > "A routing review that had three Guild captains in one room. That doesn't happen. I had to stay close. There was no safe opportunity to send anything." She pauses for what reads as deliberation. "The information was worth the silence."

> [!qna]**What have you seen recently?**
>
> > "Two Guard captains I hadn't seen before at the Shrimp Street warehouse last tenday. A cargo review on the fourteenth that included redirected manifests — not the kind that end up in the Watch logs. I can pull the details. I need to know who's reading this. Who's your handler now?"

### Phase 3 — Resolution

**Extract the Devourer**

Locating the devourer inside the skull requires a successful **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)** check, assisted by Detect Thoughts or a similar diagnostic. Once located, it can be expelled by magic.

- **Telekinesis** is the most direct approach. The devourer makes a **DC 16 Strength saving throw** to resist; on failure, it is expelled and immediately hostile (**Intellect Devourer** stat block).
- **Banishment** works if the caster makes a successful **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana)** check to target the devourer rather than the host.
- Any spell that forcibly moves or displaces a Tiny creature within a confined space achieves the same result.

When the devourer is expelled, it is hostile and attacks. It is Tiny and has limited combat options outside a host. After a Long Rest, the real Corene can be debriefed: six months of Xanathar Guild Dock Ward operations, two Guard captain names, and the layout of one Dock Ward distribution warehouse. Nihiloor's psychic link to the devourer goes dead the moment it is expelled — Nihiloor knows the extraction happened, but knows nothing of what the party discovered.

**Kill Her**

Mirt accepts it without comment when informed. The devourer transmits a breach report before dying — Nihiloor knows the Harpers have identified the deception. The Xanathar Guild goes to increased alert in the Dock Ward for two tendays.

**Leave Her in Place — Double Agent**

The Harpers feed false information through her dead drop for the next tenday. Mirt plants one specific piece of false intelligence into Nihiloor's awareness: guard patrol timing that creates a window, a dummy meeting location, or a name that draws attention away from the correct one. This planted intelligence is now inside Xanathar's lair before the party enters it in **Xanathar's Lair**.

The risk is real: Nihiloor is intelligent and may detect the manipulation if the planted information contradicts something he can verify independently. The DM should decide what was planted before **Xanathar's Lair** begins, because the party will encounter its effect in the lair without knowing whether their plant succeeded.

### Concluding the Event

Mirt, after the party reports:

> [!readaloud]
> Mirt listens to the full account without interrupting. When the party finishes, he is quiet for a moment, then he closes his notes.
>
> > "Three devourers. Three of ours. Nihiloor's been running an asset collection operation inside this city for months."
>
> He looks at the table for a moment, then back at the party.
>
> > "When you go in — finish it."

## Overview

A Harper informant embedded in Xanathar's Dock Ward operation stopped checking in three tendays ago. Mirt needs to know if she is dead, captured, or something worse. She is something worse.

## Summary

The party located Corene Wyldath at her regular dead drop bench in the Trades Ward, where she had been sitting with an intellect devourer in her skull for twelve days. The devourer was aware of the investigation. The party's resolution — extraction, elimination, or controlled deception — determined what tactical intelligence the Harpers could bring to the Xanathar lair heist.
