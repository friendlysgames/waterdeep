# The Talking Mare: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> This mission becomes available after a character joins the Harpers through **First Meeting**, with no prior Harper mission required.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd–3rd level characters.*
>
> The five-character encounter baseline counts everyone assisting the operation, including companions who belong to another faction or remain unaffiliated.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points, and its Renown belongs to the individual Harper members who complete the assignment.

## Hook

Mirt sends each participating Harper member a matinée ticket for the Lightsinger Theater, inviting them to his private box at 13:00 the following afternoon. This later appointment is separate from the recruitment evening in **First Meeting**, and companions can join the operation after the private brief.

## Background

A Harper-sympathetic druid gave Maxeene a permanent enchantment years ago, allowing her to understand and speak Common while she continued her dray work. She has since passed on conversations overheard in the Dock Ward, where passengers rarely pay attention to the horse pulling their wagon.

The Manshoon Splinter has inferred that a Harper-connected horse works somewhere among those routes, but its agents haven't identified her. Vell has watched Fillet Lane from a hire-coach for two days, waiting for a contact to lead her to the right animal. Four days ago, Maxeene carried a pale man with a carved wooden wand to an unmarked building on Shesstra Street in the Trades Ward, which houses two resident Splinter Spies.

## The Matinée and the Dray Routes

Mirt asks the members to learn what Maxeene has heard and help her move if the Dock Ward is becoming unsafe. The Wagonworks Stable, Orvel the urchin and the Dock Ward stablehands independently direct the party to Fillet Lane, so a failed inquiry never closes the search.

## The Coach across the Lane

Maxeene waits with a merchant's wagon while Vell watches from the opposite side of the street. The party can identify the watcher before making contact, obscure the conversation with the wagon, confront Vell or allow her to follow them afterward. An open conversation exposes Maxeene's identity; moving her later protects her from immediate surveillance but doesn't erase that knowledge.

## Maxeene's Account

Maxeene describes the Shesstra Street building and an argument between a sun elf and a half-orc who rode to the Yawning Portal two weeks ago. She asks to move to the Candle Lane stables, where Mirt can arrange her new work. Following Vell provides another path to the safehouse, but reporting the address doesn't require entering it or defeating its residents.

## Renown Opportunities

Each Harper member who takes part in the mission and reports its result to Mirt gains 2 base Renown. Each eligible member also gains the following listed bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** mark **Maxeene Cover Preserved** by relocating Maxeene to Candle Lane before Vell confirms her identity, with no surviving tail learning the new stable.
- **+1 Renown:** kill Vell or retain her in custody before she delivers her report, or follow her to Shesstra Street and report the safehouse address to Mirt. Brief detention followed by negotiated release doesn't earn the neutralization bonus. Merely hearing the address from Maxeene doesn't meet this additional surveillance condition.

Helping companions receive no Harper membership or Renown through this assignment, and the two bonuses can each be earned only once.

## Aftermath

Mirt receives the account privately from the Harper members, arranges Maxeene's move if needed and records the address as evidence of the hostile surveillance. **Faction Outposts** can use Shesstra Street as an early investigation lead, while **The Dead Drop** becomes available to eligible Harper members at Renown 3 and 3rd level.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): provides the private brief and receives the members' report.
- **Maxeene** (Harpers): a speaking draft mare who remembers passengers and wants safer stabling.
- **Vell** (Manshoon Splinter): watches the dray routes and reports to the Shesstra Street safehouse.
- **Orvel**: a Dock Ward urchin who knows where Maxeene works this afternoon.
- **Hessa Dorn**: keeper of the Candle Lane stables, who accepts Mirt's written relocation order.

## Dangers & Enemies

Vell uses the **Spy** from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, preferring surveillance and reporting to a fight. Two resident **Spies** occupy the Shesstra Street safehouse, with neither a forced intrusion nor an automatic third guard when Vell arrives.

## Overview

A speaking draft mare has been gathering information while carrying passengers through Waterdeep, and Mirt asks the party to find her. Learning what she remembers also gives them a chance to help her leave a route that has become unsafe.
