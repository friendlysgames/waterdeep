# The Talking Mare: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Available to an individual Harper member who has marked **Harpers Joined** and reached 2nd level. Companions can join the search and the street work without belonging to the faction.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> The watcher Vell is an ordinary 2024 **Spy**, with a hired **Bandit** or **Tough** added by party size. The optional house on Shesstra Street holds one or two more **Spies** and a **Bandit** or two. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** audits both fights for three, four and five participating combatants.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

A paper bird reaches Trollskull Manor at breakfast with a ticket for Private Box C at the Lightsinger Theater. Mirt briefs the Harper members there at the 13:00 matinee. A talking draft mare named Maxeene works the Dock Ward hire stands, and he wants her account before someone else finds her.

## Background

Maxeene is a grey roan draft mare with a white blaze. A Harper-friendly druid gave her a permanent enchantment years ago, so she understands and speaks Common, and she has listened to her passengers ever since. Almost nobody expects a horse to be paying attention.

A woman has been asking Dock Ward stablehands about a hire-horse that acts strangely. Mirt does not know who she is, and he suspects the Black Network has split and the other half wants his informants. Vell is a Splinter Spy who has watched Maxeene's stand from a hire-coach for two days. She has not yet heard the mare speak.

## The Brief

Mirt briefs the members in Box C and leaves at 13:30. He gives them a description, two apples and a signed order that releases Maxeene from her dray and admits her to a stable on Brondar's Way in the North Ward.

## Finding Maxeene

Three independent inquiries in the Dock Ward lead to her hire stand off Fillet Lane. An urchin named Orvel sells the answer for a silver piece and an apple, the stablehands give it for a Persuasion check, and the Wagonworks drivers know her without any check.

## The Hire Stand

Maxeene plays an ordinary horse in public. The party can see through the act, steer her somewhere quiet and win her trust with Mirt's pin, knowledge of the Delzorin Street lodging or a courteous manner. A hire-coach with its curtains half drawn stands across the lane.

## What Maxeene Heard

She carried a pale man with a carved wooden wand to an unmarked three-floor house on Shesstra Street in the Trades Ward, which has a red lantern in the upper window. Two days ago she also carried a sun elf and a half-orc woman who talked about hiring spies to find the Xanathar Guild's hideouts. She asks to leave the Dock Ward.

## The Watcher in the Coach

Vell follows the party once they take Maxeene off her stand. The party can confront her, bargain with her, shadow her to Shesstra Street or fail to notice her at all. A fight happens only if someone forces it.

## The House on Shesstra Street

The party can watch the house, check the ward's business directory, or go in. Two residents hold the ground floor and surrender at set limits. A list of Dock Ward hire stands lies in the upstairs office.

## A Stable on Brondar's Way

Hessa Dorn keeps a stable on Brondar's Way in the North Ward and takes Maxeene on Mirt's order. If Vell has followed the party all the way there, the Splinter learns the address and Maxeene's cover is gone.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Harper member gains 2 base Renown for finding Maxeene and reporting to Mirt. Bonuses of +1 each go to members who keep Maxeene's cover, and to members who neutralise Vell or follow her to Shesstra Street and report what they saw.

## Aftermath

Maxeene is either safe at Hessa Dorn's stable, safe but known to the Splinter, or still on her stand and gone within a tenday. Mirt puts a watcher on the house on Shesstra Street if he is given the address. **The Dead Drop** becomes available at Renown 3 and 3rd level.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): briefs the members and takes their report, and wants Maxeene's account and Maxeene safe.
- **Maxeene** (Harpers): a draft mare with a permanent enchantment, who has overheard a great deal and wants to be taken seriously.
- **Vell** (the Splinter): a Spy who watches the hire stand from a coach and reports to the house on Shesstra Street.
- **Orvel**: a Dock Ward urchin who knows where Maxeene stands for a silver piece and an apple.
- **Hessa Dorn**: the stablekeeper on Brondar's Way who takes Maxeene in.
- **The Shesstra Street residents** (the Splinter): two guards who keep the house.

## Dangers & Enemies

No combat unless the party attacks or holds Vell, or forces its way into Shesstra Street. Vell is a 2024 **Spy** who disengages at half Hit Points and surrenders only when cornered. The residents are **Spies** or a **Spy** with **Bandit** minders, scaled to party size, and they flee over the back roof before they die.

## Overview

A Harper contact asks the party to find a talking draft horse in the Dock Ward and hear what she has overheard. Someone else is watching her too.
