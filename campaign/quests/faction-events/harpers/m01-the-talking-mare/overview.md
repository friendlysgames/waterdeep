# The Talking Mare: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> This mission opens once a character has joined the Harpers through **First Meeting**, and no earlier Harper mission is needed.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd–3rd level characters.*
>
> The five-character encounter baseline counts everyone helping with the operation, including companions who belong to another faction or to none.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Harper members who complete the assignment.

## Hook

Mirt sends each participating Harper member a matinée ticket for the Lightsinger Theater, inviting them to his private box at 13:00 the next afternoon. This is a separate appointment from the recruitment evening in **First Meeting**, and companions can join the operation after the private brief.

## Background

Years ago a Harper-friendly druid gave Maxeene a permanent enchantment that lets her understand and speak Common while she goes on with her dray work. Since then she has passed along conversations overheard in the Dock Ward, where passengers rarely notice the horse pulling their wagon.

The Manshoon Splinter has worked out that a Harper-connected horse works somewhere along those routes, but its agents haven't found her yet. Vell has watched Fillet Lane from a hire-coach for two days, waiting for a contact to lead her to the right animal. Four days ago Maxeene carried a pale man with a carved wooden wand to an unmarked building on Shesstra Street in the Trades Ward, where two resident Splinter Spies live.

## The Matinée and the Dray Routes

Mirt asks the members to find out what Maxeene has heard and to help her move if the Dock Ward is getting dangerous for her. The Wagonworks Stable, Orvel the urchin and the Dock Ward stablehands each point the party to Fillet Lane on their own, so one failed inquiry never ends the search.

## The Coach across the Lane

Maxeene waits with a merchant's wagon while Vell watches from the opposite side of the street. The party can spot the watcher before making contact, use the wagon to hide the conversation, confront Vell, or let her follow them afterward. Talking openly exposes Maxeene's identity, and moving her later protects her from immediate surveillance without undoing what Vell has learned.

## Maxeene's Account

Maxeene describes the Shesstra Street building and an argument between a sun elf and a half-orc who rode to the Yawning Portal two weeks ago. She asks to move to the Candle Lane stables, where Mirt can arrange her new work. Following Vell is a second way to find the safehouse, and the party can report the address without going inside or fighting the residents.

## Renown Opportunities

Each Harper member who takes part in the mission and reports the result to Mirt gains 2 base Renown. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** mark **Maxeene Cover Preserved** by moving Maxeene to Candle Lane before Vell confirms her identity, with no surviving tail learning the new stable.
- **+1 Renown:** kill Vell or hold her in custody before she delivers her report, or follow her to Shesstra Street and report the safehouse address to Mirt. Detaining her briefly and then agreeing to release her doesn't earn this bonus, and neither does simply hearing the address from Maxeene.

Companions who aren't Harpers gain no membership or Renown from this assignment, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

Mirt hears the account privately from the Harper members, arranges Maxeene's move if she needs one and records the address as evidence of hostile surveillance. **Faction Outposts** can use Shesstra Street as an early investigation lead, and **The Dead Drop** becomes available to eligible Harper members at Renown 3 and 3rd level.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): gives the private brief and receives the members' report.
- **Maxeene** (Harpers): a speaking draft mare who remembers her passengers and wants safer stabling.
- **Vell** (Manshoon Splinter): watches the dray routes and reports to the Shesstra Street safehouse.
- **Orvel**: a Dock Ward urchin who knows where Maxeene works this afternoon.
- **Hessa Dorn**: keeper of the Candle Lane stables, who accepts Mirt's written relocation order.

## Dangers & Enemies

Vell uses the **Spy** from the 2024 *Monster Manual* and would rather watch and report than fight. Two resident **Spies** live in the Shesstra Street safehouse. The party never has to break in, and Vell doesn't automatically join them as a third guard when she arrives.

## Overview

Mirt asks the party to find a speaking draft mare who has been listening to her passengers across Waterdeep. Finding out what she remembers also lets them help her leave a route that has become unsafe.
