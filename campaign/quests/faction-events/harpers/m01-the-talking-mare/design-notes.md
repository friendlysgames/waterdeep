# Design Notes: The Talking Mare

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C and the Dragon Heist table give a short errand: find a talking draft horse, earn her trust and hear what she has overheard. They also give Maxeene's act in public, the trust rules, the dislike of "girl" and mane-patting, and the sun elf and half-orc passengers, who match Davil Starsong and Yagra Stonefist. Dragon Heist puts the reward at 1 Renown. Both sources leave the watcher a minor Xanathar Guild Spy named Vosk, who reports to Keln Trigos.

## What Changed

The watcher is Vell, a Splinter Spy, as the user settled earlier. The Harpers' enemy in the first mission is the other half of the Black Network, so the Xanathar angle is dropped and the Davil and Yagra recognition stays as an optional DC 12 check. The Shesstra Street house gives the Splinter a place on the map, and Faction Outposts can pick it up.

The search keeps three independent inquiries, so no single roll ends it. Vell now has four handlings (confront, bargain, shadow, be tailed) and a clear rule for when she learns Maxeene is the asset: when she sees or hears her speak, or follows the party to the stable. That rule makes the Wagonworks yard safe and the alley beside the stall dangerous, and it gives the choice of a quiet place a consequence. The bargain is a trap that a DC 14 Insight check reveals.

Renown follows the guide: 2 base, with +1 for keeping the cover and +1 for neutralising Vell or following her. The source's once-per-act intel service from a protected Maxeene is dropped, since it would need a fixed answer for every act. The four outcomes are read by Mirt's debrief and by the three pages named in the Event Outcomes block.

## Departures from the Source

Maxeene sits at a Dock Ward stand off Fillet Lane, not in the Trades Ward at Salabar and Immar. She has a white blaze, not a star. The relocation goes to Hessa Dorn's stable on Brondar's Way in the North Ward, not Candle Lane, because Candle Lane is the Zhentarim warehouse street in the Dock Ward. The Tally Fellbranch and Fala Lefaliir shortcut is dropped, since Orvel, the stablehands and the Wagonworks drivers already give three routes. The watcher's reporting address is a Splinter house, not the Terasse Estate. Manshoon appears only in a GM block, and only as the truth the party cannot name until **Manshoon Named** is marked.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Vell, Orvel, Hessa Dorn, the Wagonworks Stable, the pale man with the carved wand, Brondar's Way as the stable's street. Minor NPCs are voiced from the event text, since none has a Notable Figures page. Maxeene's Notable Figures page says "unremarkable coloring" and places her outside the Yawning Portal, which disagrees with the white blaze and the Dock Ward stand. The Harpers Mechanics Reference carries the audits. The Splinter's source for the talking horse is a stablehand's gossip, and the draft does not link it to the relay leak that **The Cell Is Compromised** describes.
