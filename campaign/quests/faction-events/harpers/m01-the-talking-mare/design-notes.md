# Design Notes: The Talking Mare

## On the Mission's Texture

The original source gives Maxeene as a pure information delivery mechanism — the party finds her, she tells them things, they leave. That structure reduces a remarkable NPC to a quest bulletin board. The revision gives Maxeene a voice, preferences, and a request of her own (the Candle Lane stables), and her social register actively rewards the party for treating her as a peer rather than a horse. The mission becomes about how the party approaches an unusual intelligence asset. The information is the same; the relationship has to be earned by how they conduct themselves.

Vell as active pressure. The original mission has no countdown and no opposition. The Splinter's presence is mentioned in backstory but never generates action. Vell has been placed on Fillet Lane specifically to force a decision: the party cannot take their time interviewing Maxeene without consequences. The hire-coach in the lane is the timer. Relocating Maxeene quickly is better than not relocating her. Following Vell back to Shesstra Street opens the **Faction Outposts** outpost thread earlier than the Splinter expects anyone to find it.

## On the Shesstra Street Thread

Maxeene's description of the pale man and the red lantern is the mission's secondary payoff — a Splinter safehouse the party can hand to Mirt immediately. He does not explain what he does with it. This is a deliberate example of how Harper operations work: the party contributes pieces, and the picture is larger than any one contribution. The address surfaces again in **Faction Outposts** as one of the Splinter outpost entries.

## On the Sun Elf and the Half-Orc

The overheard argument between the sun elf and the large half-orc is seeding material only. It is intentionally unresolved here and not connected to any named NPC at this stage. The GM should note it in session notes; it may become meaningful later, or may remain texture. The line "The war doesn't help anyone until we know who's running it" is ambiguous enough to apply to several ongoing Grand Game threads, and that ambiguity is what makes it worth keeping.

## Cross-Faction Connection

The Shesstra Street safehouse feeds directly into **Faction Outposts** as an early Splinter outpost thread. Parties who report the address to Mirt after this mission give the Harpers a head start on mapping the Splinter's Trades Ward presence — a payoff for a first-tier mission that would otherwise leave no structural trace on the rest of the campaign.
