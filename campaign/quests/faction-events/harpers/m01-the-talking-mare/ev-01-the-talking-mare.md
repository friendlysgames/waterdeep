# The Talking Mare

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social/Exploration Event begins when the party arrives at Lightsinger Theater and ends when they report back to Mirt. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Mirt in Private Box C before the first act begins.
> - Follow three independent paths through the Dock Ward to locate Maxeene on Fillet Lane.
> - Speak with Maxeene, who describes two intelligence threads and asks to be relocated.
> - Detect Vell watching from a hire-coach on Fillet Lane and choose how to handle her.
> - Relocate Maxeene to the Candle Lane stables before Vell confirms her identity.
> - Report the Shesstra Street address to Mirt, either from Maxeene's intelligence or from following Vell.
>
> #### Background
>
> Mirt had a Harper-sympathetic druid cast a permanent Speak with Animals on Maxeene years ago as a favour. She has been passing observations to the Harpers ever since, in the casual, gossipy manner of a horse who finds people interesting, and her intelligence is informal and often more useful than structured reports. The Splinter does not know which Dock Ward horse is the asset. Vell has been in the hire-coach on Fillet Lane for two days, waiting for a Harper contact to identify the right horse for her.
>
> Maxeene is pulling a merchant's wagon on Fillet Lane this afternoon. The Shesstra Street address she names is a Manshoon Splinter safehouse, and it feeds directly into the outpost seed for the Splinter's Trades Ward operation in **Faction Outposts**.

### The Brief

Mirt sent matinée tickets to Trollskull Manor without a note attached. That is, by itself, the message.

> [!readaloud]
> Mirt is in Private Box C when you arrive, seated well back from the rail with a folded note on the armrest beside him. The stage is set but the curtain is still down. He glances up as you file in, takes a moment to look at each face in turn, and then turns back to the empty stage.
>
> > "There's a draft mare named Maxeene working a dray route in the Dock Ward. She listens to everything and she remembers it. I need to know what she's heard before someone less friendly to us finds her first."
>
> He slides the note across without looking at you. It has a Dock Ward route marked on one side and the word *apple* underlined on the other.
>
> > "She responds well to courtesy."

He does not wait for the curtain. His glass is still on the armrest when you look back.

> [!social]**The Errand**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan human, he/him) :: a moneylender and Harper handler; in business mode, brief, declarative, and done with the subject.
>
> Mirt considers the brief complete. He will take one or two questions before the curtain bell gives him a reason to wave the party off, but he answers only what is necessary and does not elaborate. In business mode he does not swear, and his sentences are short and plain.
>
> The note covers Maxeene's afternoon route and the apple as the recognition signal. He will not explain why the apple specifically. ("She asked for one. The enchantment came with opinions.")
>
> Conversation topics Mirt is willing to discuss include:
> - Maxeene's route and timing, which the note covers.
> - Whether she is in danger, which he will confirm without detail.
> - How to deliver a report, which is a paper bird to the usual address.
>
> Specific dialogue for Mirt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**What do you know about Maxeene?**
>
> > "She has a permanent enchantment. Listens, talks — common tongue, horse concerns, observations about her routes. She's been useful for some time. The note has her afternoon circuit."

> [!qna]**Is she in danger?**
>
> > "Someone is looking for her. They don't know which horse yet, which is why I need you there before they do."

> [!qna]**What does the Splinter want with her?**
>
> > "They know there's a Harper horse somewhere in the Dock Ward. They don't know which one. That's the full extent of what I know." He picks up his glass. "Go find her."

> [!qna]**How do we report back?**
>
> > "Paper bird."

### Finding Maxeene

The Dock Ward runs on dray schedules, and the working horses are everywhere. Three independent paths lead to Maxeene's location on Fillet Lane this afternoon.

> [!readaloud]
> The Dock Ward's afternoon shift is well underway when you arrive. Dray horses and heavy wagons fill the lanes between the warehouses, and the smell of salt, tar and horse dung is thicker than the sea wind. The stablehands and drivers work around you without breaking pace.

> [!exploration]**The Wagonworks Stable**
>
> Any character who asks around the working dray stables near the Dock Ward gate finds a driver willing to talk.
>
> > "Grey roan with a star marking? Yeah, I know her. Never acts spooked, always waits at exactly the right corners without being told — I find it convenient, honestly. She's usually on the Fillet Lane route in the afternoons, pulling for Merchant Aldur."

> [!exploration]**Orvel the Urchin**
>
> Orvel is a Dock Ward boy who has traded on Maxeene's intelligence before and knows her daily schedule. A character who looks for a Dock Ward urchin working the warehouse lanes finds him without a check.
>
> He will give Maxeene's current location for a silver piece and one of the apples from the route note. He does not explain how he knows her schedule. Fillet Lane, a merchant's wagon, star marking.

> [!exploration]**Asking the Stablehands**
>
> Any character who asks stablehands across two or more yards about a horse that seems unusually attentive and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check gets the specific route: Fillet Lane, this afternoon, pulling a merchant's wagon.

### Fillet Lane

Fillet Lane runs through the middle of a working warehouse district, wide enough for two wagons to pass. The midafternoon foot traffic is steady, which means a figure holding still in a parked coach stands out, if anyone thinks to notice.

> [!readaloud]
> Fillet Lane is busy with afternoon deliveries, wagons moving in both directions, workers loading and unloading under the warehouse overhangs, and the noise of chain and timber and shod hooves on cobblestone. A grey roan mare is in harness three blocks down, stopped at a corner while a delivery is carried inside, and she has a clear white star marking between her eyes. She turns her head when she hears her name.

> [!exploration]**The Hire-Coach**
>
> Any character with a Passive Perception of 14 or higher, or who actively watches the lane, notices that the hire-coach parked across from the merchant's wagon has not moved in at least forty minutes. The driver is watching the lane rather than waiting for a fare.
>
> Vell (Spy stat block) is inside. She has been here for two days, waiting for someone to identify the right horse. Once the party approaches Maxeene, Vell can confirm which horse is the Harper asset. She will then follow the party when they leave, or report back to her handler at the Shesstra Street safehouse if she believes she has been spotted.

> [!gamemaster]**Limiting Vell's Sight Line**
>
> Approaching Maxeene openly in a group while Vell is watching gives Vell a clear confirmation of the asset's identity. The party can limit this by approaching through the crowd, by sending one character while the others maintain position and obscure the coach's line of sight, or by dealing with Vell before approaching the horse at all. If the party speaks with Maxeene before dealing with Vell, the **Maxeene Relocated** outcome no longer removes Vell's intelligence — the Splinter already knows which horse she is.

> [!social]**The Grey Roan**
>
> Maxeene (Neutral, Draft horse, she/her) :: a Harper intelligence asset with a permanent Speak with Animals enchantment and a Castle Ward social register she has been collecting for years.
>
> Maxeene has been doing this for a long time. She knows the difference between someone who treats her as a colleague and someone who talks to her like she is simple. She goes silent and stays a horse if the party addresses her in the slow, careful cadence some people use with children and foreigners, or if they try to hurry her. She answers freely and at length if the party gives her the apple from the note and speaks to her as they would any contact.
>
> She is chatty. Left to herself, she starts with the quality of the apple and works outward to the things she has seen. She is precise about what she observed and vague about what it meant, in the manner of a very good witness who has no interest in politics.
>
> Conversation topics Maxeene is willing to discuss include:
> - Recent passengers and routes, which she remembers in excellent detail.
> - The pale man who hired her dray four days ago and where she took him.
> - The sun elf and the half-orc who argued on her dray two tendays ago.
> - The Candle Lane stables, which she would like to move to.
>
> Specific dialogue for Maxeene on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Recent passengers?**
>
> She turns to check that the delivery is still ongoing before she answers.
>
> > "A pale man with a carved wooden wand hired my dray four days ago. Dock Ward to Shesstra Street, Trades Ward. Three floors, no signage. Red lantern in the upper window. He smelled of lamp oil and nerves. Served him right."

> [!qna]**The sun elf and the half-orc?**
>
> > "Two tendays ago. Dock Ward to the Yawning Portal. They argued most of the way — the elf wanted to keep looking for someone, the half-orc said it was too exposed. The elf said, 'The war doesn't help anyone until we know who's running it.' Then we arrived and they stopped. I don't know what war. Nobody thinks the horse is listening."

> [!qna]**Where would you like to go?**
>
> > "The stables on Candle Lane. The Dock Ward has felt strange lately, and I would like to be somewhere with better oats. Is that an apple?"

> [!exploration]**The Shesstra Street Address**
>
> Any character who examines the route note after Maxeene describes the pale man's destination and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check confirms that the Shesstra Street building matches no licensed business in the Trades Ward directory. The red lantern in the upper window is a Splinter contact marker.

### Resolution

**Relocating Maxeene:** The party arranges new stabling on Candle Lane, through Mirt or on their own initiative. Mirt pays for it. Maxeene is moved, the Splinter's search for the Harper horse goes cold, and Maxeene continues as an active asset. If Vell has already confirmed Maxeene's identity before the relocation, moving her still removes the surveillance window, but the Splinter will know they were identified.

**Vell follows the party:** If the party leaves Fillet Lane without handling Vell, she follows them. A Passive Perception of 15, or any active Perception check during deliberate movement such as taking a roundabout route or doubling back, notices the same hire-coach a few streets behind. Vell can be confronted directly (she is not immediately hostile but will attempt to disengage), shaken off, or followed back to the Shesstra Street safehouse — the same address Maxeene described.

**The Shesstra Street safehouse:** Three agents (Spy stat blocks) are inside. Reporting the address to Mirt earns his quiet, considered attention. He does not explain what he does with it. The address is a Manshoon Splinter outpost seed in **Faction Outposts**.

### Concluding the Event

Mirt's response when the party reports in is brief. If they give him the Shesstra Street address, he says: "She told you about Shesstra Street." Not a question.

## Overview

A draft mare named Maxeene has been passing useful observations to the Harpers from her Dock Ward dray routes for years. A Manshoon Splinter operative is watching Fillet Lane from a hire-coach, waiting for someone to identify the right horse. Mirt needs Maxeene relocated before the Splinter makes her.

## Summary

We found Maxeene on Fillet Lane, a grey roan draft mare with a permanent Speak with Animals enchantment and strong opinions about her current stable situation. She described a pale Splinter operative she had transported to an unmarked building on Shesstra Street, and recalled a conversation between a sun elf and a half-orc arguing about a war neither of them named. A Splinter watcher named Vell had been in a hire-coach on the lane for two days, waiting for us to do her identification work for her.
