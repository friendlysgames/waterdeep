# The Talking Mare

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Investigation Event begins when Mirt briefs the Harper members at the Lightsinger Theater and ends when they report to him. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Mirt's brief and find Maxeene through three independent inquiries in the Dock Ward.
> - Win her trust, steer her out of public view and hear what she has overheard.
> - Spot Vell's coach, then confront her, bargain with her, shadow her or be followed by her.
> - Move Maxeene to a stable on Brondar's Way in the North Ward.
> - Watch the Splinter house on Shesstra Street, or go inside.
>
> Only Harper members attend Mirt's brief and debrief. Their companions can help with every other part of the mission.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Maxeene is a grey roan draft mare with a white blaze and Intelligence 10. A druid's permanent enchantment lets her speak Common. In public she plays an ordinary horse.
> - Her hire stand is a cobbled yard off Fillet Lane in the Dock Ward. Her owner collects the day's fares at dusk and does not know she talks.
> - Vell is a Spy of the Splinter, the Black Network cell that broke away. The Splinter is Manshoon's. Nobody in this Event can say so unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for that member.
> - The Splinter heard from a stablehand's gossip that a Dock Ward hire-horse acts oddly. Vell has watched the stand for two days and has not heard the mare speak.
> - Vell confirms Maxeene the moment she sees or hears the mare speak, or when she follows the party and Maxeene all the way to Brondar's Way.
> - The house on Shesstra Street is a Splinter safehouse. The pale man Maxeene carried there left it two days ago and does not return in this Event.
> - Maxeene's two passengers from two days ago are Davil Starsong and Yagra Stonefist of the Doom Raiders. Her account of them is one of three clue paths that link the Doom Raiders to the Grand Game.

### The Brief

A paper bird reaches Trollskull Manor at breakfast. It carries a ticket for Private Box C and a note that says "13:00, bring an appetite." Mirt receives the Harper members there during the matinee and leaves at 13:30, before the second act. Companions can wait in the street outside. Mirt is in business gear throughout, so he is short, plain and does not swear.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The matinee crowd is still settling into the galleries when you reach Private Box C. Mirt sits at the back with a jug of wine and a folded note on his knee, and he waves you toward the empty chairs without getting up.
>
> > "Sit. There's a draft mare called Maxeene who works the hire stands in the Dock Ward. She listens to her passengers, and she forgets nothing."
>
> He pours a glass for each of you and holds out the note, with two apples wrapped in a cloth beneath it.
>
> > "Someone has been asking Dock Ward stablehands about a horse that acts strangely. I want her account, lad, and I want her moved before they find the right horse."

> [!social]**The Old Wolf in Business Gear**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan human, he/him) :: the senior Harper of the Waterdeep cell, who briefs in the back of a theater box so the orchestra covers his voice.
>
> Mirt refills every glass without being asked and keeps a hand over the note when an usher passes. He answers in short, complete sentences. He wants Maxeene's account of the Zhentarim and anything else she has heard, and he wants her out of the Dock Ward. He does not ask the members to promise a result.
>
> Conversation topics Mirt is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Maxeene's looks, her enchantment and what she is like to deal with.
> - The signed order, the stable on Brondar's Way and Hessa Dorn.
> - The woman who has been asking about a strange horse.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, his own offices, the Cassalanters, the vault or who runs the other half of the Black Network.

> [!qna]**Who is Maxeene?**
>
> > "A grey roan mare with a white blaze. A friendly druid gave her a permanent enchantment years ago, so she speaks Common. She's been listening to passengers ever since."

> [!qna]**How do we find her?**
>
> > "Ask in the Dock Ward. She works the hire stands, and the dray men know their horses better than their wives. She plays a horse in public, so don't shout at her."

> [!qna]**What do we ask her?**
>
> > "Anything she's heard about the Black Network. Names, places, who hired her. Let her finish. She wanders, but the useful part is in there somewhere."

> [!qna]**Why now?**
>
> > "A woman asked a stablehand if any dray horse acts oddly. The Black Network has split, and I don't know which half she works for. Neither half is a friend."

> [!qna]**Can we move her?**
>
> Mirt hands over a second paper, folded around a signature.
>
> > "Hessa Dorn keeps a stable on Brondar's Way, in the North Ward. This order releases Maxeene from her dray and admits her to Hessa's. I've paid both. Let her choose."

> [!gamemaster]**The Note, the Apples and the Order**
>
> The note gives Maxeene's description and the Dock Ward, but not her stand. The apples are the courtesy Mirt's note calls for. The signed order releases her from her owner and admits her to Hessa's stable, so the party needs no money or haggling to move her. Each member also carries the silver pin from **Harpers First Meeting**.

### Finding Maxeene

The Dock Ward has dozens of working dray horses, but every inquiry below ends at the same hire stand off Fillet Lane. They are independent, so one failed roll never stops the search.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Carts crawl along the Dock Ward streets while their drivers shout across the rattle of wheels. Outside the Wagonworks yard, two stablehands brush down a pair of bays, and a boy sits on a low wall and watches the traffic go by.

> [!exploration]**The Wagonworks Drivers**
>
> The drivers in the Wagonworks yard know the grey roan with the white blaze as soon as the party describes her. No check is needed. One of them says she waits at the right corners without being told and stands most afternoons at the hire stand off Fillet Lane.

> [!social]**The Boy on the Wall**
>
> Orvel (Unaligned, Human, he/him) :: a Dock Ward urchin who has talked with Maxeene before and sells what he knows.
>
> Orvel will tell the party where she stands for 1 sp and one of the apples. No check is needed. He knows nothing about Vell or the Splinter and does not care to learn.

> [!qna]**Where is she?**
>
> Orvel eyes the cloth around the apples before he points along the street.
>
> > "The stand off Fillet Lane, most afternoons. A silver and an apple, and I'll walk you to the corner. The other apple's for her. She'll want it."

> [!qna]**Will she talk to us?**
>
> > "She talks to me. I just ask where she's been and let her go on. Don't pat her mane or call her girl, though. She hates that shit."

> [!exploration]**The Stablehands**
>
> Any character who describes Maxeene to the stablehands and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check learns that she stands at the hire stand off Fillet Lane in the afternoons. On a failure, the stablehands shrug and send the party to the Wagonworks drivers, who give the answer without a check.

### The Hire Stand

The stand is a cobbled yard with a hitching rail, a water trough and room for three drays. A greengrocer's stall sells apples at the lane's corner for a copper each. Maxeene is hitched to an empty hire-dray with no driver in sight, and a hire-coach stands across the lane.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A grey roan mare stands alone at the hitching rail, hitched to an empty dray, with a white blaze down her face and a clean harness. She flicks her tail at the flies and watches you cross the yard without any sign that she has noticed you.
>
> Across the lane, a hire-coach waits with its curtains half drawn, and its driver dozes on the box.

> [!exploration]**A Horse Playing a Horse**
>
> Maxeene will not speak in a public street. If the party addresses her openly, she stays a horse, with her ears forward and her eyes on the middle distance.
>
> Any character who watches her and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that she is deciding not to answer, as a person might. On a failure, the character sees a very calm horse.
>
> Any character who leads her somewhere quiet makes a successful DC 11 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check. A character who simply hires the dray and drives it to a quiet place needs no check. The Wagonworks yard is out of sight of the lane, and the party can shut its gate. The alley beside the greengrocer's stall is not, because the coach can see the mouth of it.

> [!exploration]**The Coach without a Fare**
>
> The coach has stood across the lane for forty minutes, and its curtain edge moves whenever someone walks up to the mare. Any character with a Passive Perception of 14 or higher notices that the coach has no fare. Any character who watches it on purpose and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check also sees a woman behind the curtain.
>
> On a failure, the party has a second chance on the road. See **The Watcher in the Coach**.

> [!social]**The Mare with Opinions**
>
> Maxeene (Unaligned, Draft Horse, she/her) :: a Harper asset who enjoys conversation and expects it to be a courtesy.
>
> Maxeene is warm, placid and a little vain about her ears. She drifts from the useful to the trivial and back, and she interrupts important news to comment on hay. She remembers destinations and what her passengers said very exactly, but she knows no names she was not told. She dislikes being called "girl", having her mane patted by strangers, and being spoken to slowly and loudly.
>
> Conversation topics Maxeene is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Her passengers, where she took them and what they said.
> - Hay, hitching posts and the people who stand near them.
> - Moving to a stable where the Dock Ward is not so strange.
>
> She bolts if a fight starts, and she cannot be fetched back until it is over.

> [!exploration]**Winning Her Trust**
>
> Maxeene talks to anyone who shows Mirt's silver pin, or who names the Harper lodging on Delzorin Street. No check is needed. Anyone else must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check.
>
> - **Offers the apple first:** The character has Advantage on the check. The party can also buy apples at the greengrocer's stall.
> - **Treats her as a professional:** The character has Advantage on the check.
> - **Calls her "girl", pats her mane, or speaks slowly and loudly:** The character has Disadvantage on the check.
> - **A failure:** She turns her head away and says nothing more to that character. Another character, or the same one after an apology and an apple, can try again.
>
> A member who shows the pin should do it away from the coach, since Vell can see a pin from across the lane.

### What Maxeene Heard

Maxeene takes the apple before she answers anything. She talks in a quiet place, and she is chatty once she starts.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Maxeene takes the apple from your hand and chews it while she turns her head to check the yard behind you. Then she steps closer and lowers her voice below the creak of the dray.
>
> > "Is that an apple? Thank you, that's very decent of you. I've had a long afternoon, and I was starting to think nobody would ask me anything worth answering."

> [!qna]**Why the act?**
>
> > "Nobody thinks the horse is listening, and that's how I like it. If I answered everyone, I'd be a sideshow, and the drivers would take me to market for the novelty."

> [!qna]**Any unusual passengers?**
>
> > "Four days ago a pale man with a carved wooden wand hired me to Shesstra Street, in the Trades Ward. It's a house of three floors with no sign and a red lantern in the upper window. He never looked back at the dray, which I noticed, because most people check their bags."

> [!qna]**Anyone else?**
>
> She flicks an ear toward the lane.
>
> > "Two days ago a sun elf and a half-orc woman hired me from Salabar Street to the Yawning Portal. The elf did the talking. The half-orc had a tattoo of a winged snake on her neck, and she watched the street all the way."
> >
> > "They wanted to hire spies to find the Xanathar Guild's hideouts, and he wanted three names by the end of the tenday. He also said there was no use starting a war until they knew who was running it."

> [!qna]**Does anything seem strange?**
>
> > "That coach has stood across the lane for two afternoons. Nobody gets in or out, and it smells of lamp oil and nerves. The Dock Ward has felt wrong all tenday, and the hay isn't even good."

> [!qna]**Would you like to move?**
>
> > "Yes, I would. Somewhere with better hay and fewer coaches. If Mirt's paid for it, I'll go to whatever stable he says, as soon as someone takes this harness off."

> [!exploration]**What the Account Gives**
>
> Any character who has met Davil Starsong and Yagra Stonefist and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check recognises them from her description. On a failure, the character has only the description.
>
> Any character who looks up Shesstra Street in the Trades Ward business directory, which any guild office keeps, and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds that no business is licensed at the three-floor house. On a failure, the clerk cannot find the entry either way, and the character can try the next guild office.

### The Watcher in the Coach

Vell stays in her coach while the party works the stand. When the party takes Maxeene off the stand, she follows, in the coach until the streets narrow and then on foot. She confirms Maxeene the moment she sees or hears the mare speak, which is why the Wagonworks yard is safe and the alley beside the stall is not. If the party never notices her, she follows them all the way to Brondar's Way.

On the road, any character with a Passive Perception of 15 or higher notices the same coach behind them. Any character who watches for pursuit and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check notices it too. If nobody notices, Hessa Dorn does. See **A Stable on Brondar's Way**.

The party has four ways to handle her.

- **Confront her.** The party approaches the coach before moving Maxeene. Vell plays a woman waiting for a merchant and will not start a fight in a public lane.
- **Bargain with her.** If the party blocks her and presses, she offers to report that the horse is only a horse, if she is let go. Any character who watches her and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that she will report the truth anyway.
- **Shadow her.** The party lets her go and follows her to Shesstra Street, twenty minutes away. Each character who follows makes a DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) check. If at least half succeed, she does not notice. Otherwise she leads the party through a loop of back streets and slips away. They still hold the address from Maxeene.
- **Be tailed.** The party leaves the stand without dealing with her, and she follows to Brondar's Way.

> [!social]**The Woman in the Coach**
>
> Vell (Neutral Evil, Human, she/her) :: a Spy of the Splinter, patient and hard to rattle, who wants to return with a name and without being held.
>
> Vell keeps the curtain across her lap and her hands where they can be seen. She never speaks first. She answers questions with questions and gives nothing away until she is held or is bargaining for her release. She has worked for the Splinter for a year and does not know who leads it.
>
> Conversation topics Vell is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Whom she is waiting for, which is a merchant who has not come.
> - The terms of a release, if she is cornered.
> - The Shesstra Street house, only if she is held and below 13 Hit Points, or has surrendered.

> [!qna]**Why are you watching us?**
>
> Vell glances at the coach door before she turns back.
>
> > "I'm waiting on a merchant who said he'd be here after midday. If you're going to keep me standing in the lane, tell me what I've done."

> [!qna]**Where do you report?**
>
> If she has surrendered, she answers. Otherwise she shrugs.
>
> > "A house on Shesstra Street, with a red lantern upstairs. It has a back roof you can reach from the cooperage next door. Let me go, and you can look at it yourselves."

> [!hazard]**If Vell Is Forced to Fight**
>
> Combat begins only if the party attacks Vell or physically holds her and she refuses terms. Vell uses the 2024 **Spy** stat block with no phases. Add hirelings by party size, following the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**:
>
> - **Three combatants:** Vell alone.
> - **Four combatants:** Vell and one **Bandit**, a hired runner.
> - **Five combatants:** Vell and one **Tough**, the coach driver, who wakes on the first round.
>
> The hirelings know only a woman who paid by the day. They have never heard Vell's employer named.
>
> #### Vell's Tactics
> Vell begins beside the coach and uses it for half cover against anyone across the lane.
>
> During combat, Vell:
> - Uses Disengage as a bonus action whenever a character is adjacent, then runs for the nearest corner.
> - Uses Dash as a bonus action when nobody is adjacent and a route out of sight is open.
> - Attacks the conscious character who blocks her way out, with her Shortsword or Hand Crossbow.
>
> She disengages at 13 Hit Points and heads for the roofline or the coach. She surrenders only if she is cornered at 13 Hit Points or fewer, or is Grappled or Restrained and has been hit at least once. The **Bandit** yields at 5 Hit Points or when Vell yields. The **Tough** yields at 16 Hit Points or when Vell yields, and has Advantage against a character within 5 feet of Vell.
>
> A character who lowers their weapon and makes a successful DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check, or who offers terms while Vell is surrounded, ends the fight at once. If Vell escapes, she reports Maxeene to the Splinter that evening.

If Vell is held, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vell sits against the coach wheel with her hands in plain sight, watching the lane while a cart rolls slowly past. When it has gone, she turns to whoever stands nearest.
>
> > "Shesstra Street, the house with the red lantern. Two people keep the ground floor. You can reach the roof from the cooperage next door. I'm done talking, so decide what you're doing with me."

Mirt takes custody of a held Vell. He sends a carter to the Wagonworks yard at dusk, and the Splinter stops expecting her report.

### The House on Shesstra Street

The house on Shesstra Street is optional. The party can hand the address to Mirt without going near it. The house stands in the Trades Ward between a chandler and a locksmith, with a closed but unlocked door and a red lantern in the upper window.

> [!exploration]**Watching the House**
>
> Any character who watches the house for an hour sees two people in grey coats come and go, and sees the lantern lit at dusk. If Vell is free, she arrives, spends five minutes inside and leaves. A character who watches the roof from the cooperage next door sees that the back roof is easy to climb.
>
> A party that watches the house and reports what it saw has done what Mirt needs. No one is required to go in.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The house stands three floors high with shuttered windows, wedged between a chandler and a locksmith. A red lantern hangs unlit in the upper window, and no sign marks the door. A man in a grey coat leans on the doorframe and picks at his teeth.

Two residents hold the house and are scaled by party size. They have no connection to Maxeene.

> [!hazard]**If the Party Forces the Door**
>
> The residents are 2024 **Spies**, with **Bandits** as minders, and they hold the ground floor. If Vell reached the house first, she reports and leaves on her next turn, so the roster stays as listed. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** audits the fight for three, four and five combatants:
>
> - **Three combatants:** one **Spy** and one **Bandit**.
> - **Four combatants:** one **Spy** and two **Bandits**.
> - **Five combatants:** two **Spies**.
>
> #### The Residents' Tactics
> The residents draw weapons when intruders force the door, and keep the staircase behind them.
>
> During combat, the residents:
> - Attack separate intruders when they can, and use Cunning Action to fall back toward the staircase.
> - Guard the upstairs office and pursue no farther than 30 feet beyond the front door.
> - Flee over the back roof when the first resident yields.
>
> A **Spy** yields at 13 Hit Points and surrenders if cornered at 9 Hit Points or fewer. The second yields as soon as the first does. A **Bandit** yields at 5 Hit Points, or when a **Spy** yields. They do not know who leads the Splinter.
>
> A character who offers a ruse at the door, such as a lost delivery crew for a neighbouring shop, and makes a successful DC 14 Charisma (Deception) check reaches the ground floor and sees the stairs before the residents grow suspicious. On a failure, the residents bar the door and the party has not entered.

> [!exploration]**The Upstairs Office**
>
> The upper floors hold sleeping rooms and an office with a desk. Any character who searches the desk finds a list of Dock Ward hire stands, with six names crossed out and the stand off Fillet Lane circled. It shows who sent Vell. The Splinter leaves the house within two days of anyone reporting this address.

### A Stable on Brondar's Way

Hessa Dorn keeps a stable on Brondar's Way, between two bakeries in the North Ward. The walk from the Dock Ward takes about forty minutes with a mare in tow. Unharnessing Maxeene at the stand takes ten.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A heavyset woman in a leather apron stands in the gate of a long stable, with a bucket in each hand and a lead rope looped over one shoulder. She reads the signature on Mirt's order, then looks past you at the street.
>
> > "Bring her in. Mirt's paid for the stall and the hay, and there's water ready. Was anyone behind you on the way over?"

> [!social]**The Stablekeeper**
>
> Hessa Dorn (Lawful Neutral, Human, she/her) :: a brusque stablekeeper who keeps Harper horses and asks no questions about their riders.
>
> Hessa is gruff with people and gentle with animals. She notices anyone who has followed a customer to her gate. She wants Maxeene out of the street in under a minute.

> [!qna]**Is she safe here?**
>
> > "As safe as a stable gets, with a lock on the gate and a good dog. If someone's followed you, I'd sooner know now, before I open it."

> [!qna]**Can we speak to her again?**
>
> > "Mirt knows where to find her, and so do you. Come by in the morning, and she'll tell you more about the hay than you want to hear."

If Vell has followed the party and nobody has noticed her, Hessa does. She keeps the gate shut and says, "Woman across the street, on foot. She stopped when you stopped." The party can deal with Vell here by any of the handlings above, or lose her through the Wagonworks yard first and come back. If the party opens the gate in front of her, Vell learns the stable and Maxeene's cover is gone.

If Maxeene reaches the stable unseen, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Maxeene steps into a stall with fresh hay and a clean water bucket, and lowers her nose to the straw. She turns her head to look at you over the half door.
>
> > "Oh, that is much better. Do tell Mirt I'm here, and I'll see what the passengers on this side of the city have to say."

If the party leaves Maxeene on her stand, she is hitched to the dray at dusk when the owner returns. Mirt moves her himself after the debrief, and a tenday later the stand is empty.

### Aftermath

Maxeene ends the mission in one of three places. If her cover held, she keeps working for Mirt on her new circuit. If Vell saw her, she spends a tenday at Hessa's and then goes north of the gate for a month. If she was left on her stand, Mirt moves her himself and the Splinter finds an empty stand a tenday later.

Mirt has a watcher on Shesstra Street within a day of hearing the address. Maxeene's account of the sun elf and the half-orc woman stays with Mirt, who does not say what he makes of it.

### Concluding the Event

Mirt's debrief ends the assignment. Record Maxeene's location and what the Splinter knows of her, because later contacts read it.

## Overview

The party searches the Dock Ward for a talking draft horse and learns what she has overheard. Someone else is watching her.

## Summary

We found Maxeene at a hire stand off Fillet Lane and learned that she had carried a man to a house on Shesstra Street. She had also carried two people who talked about hiring spies against the Xanathar Guild. A woman in a coach had been watching her, and we told Mirt what we did about it.
