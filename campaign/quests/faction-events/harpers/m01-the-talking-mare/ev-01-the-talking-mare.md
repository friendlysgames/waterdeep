# The Talking Mare

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Exploration Event begins at Mirt's matinée appointment and ends when the Harper members report what happened to Maxeene. Over the course of it, the party can:
>
> - Find Maxeene through three independent inquiries in the Dock Ward.
> - Spot Vell's surveillance before exposing the horse, or deal with it after the conversation.
> - Learn the Shesstra Street address and hear an unresolved account of two passengers arguing about the faction war.
> - Relocate Maxeene, shadow Vell or investigate the optional Splinter safehouse.
>
> Only Harper members attend Mirt's brief and debrief, but their companions can help with every part of the street operation.

> [!gamemaster]**Surveillance and Allegiance**
>
> Only you know that Vell answers to Manshoon. Maxeene can describe her passengers and where she took them, and following Vell shows where she reports, but neither tells the party which branch of the Black Network holds the house or who leads it. Mirt writes down what the members saw and doesn't add an affiliation they haven't established and reported themselves.

### The Brief

Mirt meets the invited members in his box at the Lightsinger Theater at 13:00, after their recruitment evening is over. The performance begins at 13:15 and he leaves at 13:30, well before the first act ends.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Ushers are still steering the afternoon crowd to their seats when you reach Mirt's private box, where a jug of wine stands on the railing. Mirt pours a glass for each of you and settles back with his eyes on the stage, turning a folded note over in his fingers.
>
> > "Sit. There's a draft mare called Maxeene working a dray route in the Dock Ward. She hears everything and forgets none of it, and I want her account before someone less friendly to us finds her."
>
> He hands you the note, with two apples wrapped in a cloth beneath it, and taps a word underlined near the bottom.
>
> > "She responds well to courtesy."

> [!social]**The Old Wolf at the Theater**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a Harper contact who keeps his voice low enough that the orchestra covers it.
>
> Mirt refills the members' glasses without being asked and keeps a hand over the folded note whenever someone passes the box. He is in business mode, so he answers briefly and doesn't swear. He will talk about Maxeene's looks, her enchantment and her relocation, and he keeps the wider network to himself. He wants her account and what the members verify with their own eyes, and he doesn't ask them to promise a result.

> [!qna]**Where do we begin?**
>
> Mirt opens the folded paper and puts a finger beside Maxeene's name.
>
> > "She's a grey roan with a star on her forehead. Ask the dray drivers in the Dock Ward. They'll know today's route better than I do."

> [!qna]**How does she speak?**
>
> > "A druid did her a favour years ago. She understands Common and speaks it well enough. You don't need a spell to talk to her."

> [!qna]**What should we ask?**
>
> > "Ask about unusual passengers, where she took them and anything they said about the Zhents. Let her finish before you decide what matters."

> [!qna]**Can we move her?**
>
> Mirt hands over a second paper, folded around an order with his signature on it.
>
> > "If she wants out of that route, take her to Hessa Dorn on Candle Lane and show her this. I've paid for the stabling and settled with the dray owner for the lost work."

> [!qna]**What do we receive?**
>
> > "You'll have done useful work for us, and I'll see it's recorded. Bring me her account, and tell me what you did about her safety."

> [!gamemaster]**The Order and the Apples**
>
> The note gives Maxeene's name and description, the Dock Ward dray circuit and the underlined word *apple*, but not the street she is working today. The party finds that through the inquiries below. The second paper tells Hessa to take Maxeene in at once, and because Mirt has paid the wagon owner, releasing her needs no purchase or haggling.

### Asking after Maxeene

The afternoon dray routes bring horses through several busy Dock Ward streets, and each of the three inquiries below points to Fillet Lane. They stay open even if the party fails a check somewhere else.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Carts crawl through the Dock Ward traffic, their drivers shouting across the rattle of wheels as horses pick their way past crates stacked along the pavement. Outside the wagon yard, two stablehands brush down a pair of bays while a boy on a low wall watches the wagons go by.

> [!exploration]**The Wagonworks Stable**
>
> The dray drivers know the grey roan with the star as soon as the party describes her, and they give her afternoon route without a check. One of them adds that she waits at the right corners without being told, and points the party toward the merchant wagons on Fillet Lane.
>
> > "Fillet Lane, this afternoon. She's pulling the merchant's wagon, the grey with the star, and she knows that route better than he does."

> [!social]**The Boy beside the Wagon Yard**
>
> Orvel is a Dock Ward urchin who has talked with Maxeene before and knows where she is working today. He'll tell the party for 1 SP and one of the apples, with no check needed. He can talk about where she works and whether she likes visitors, but he knows nothing about Vell or the Splinter.

> [!qna]**Where is she?**
>
> Orvel eyes the cloth around the apples before pointing along the street.
>
> > "Fillet Lane, pulling a merchant's wagon. I'll show you which way for a silver and one of those apples. The other one's for her, she'll want it herself."

> [!qna]**Will she speak to us?**
>
> > "She spoke to me. I just asked where she'd been and let her go on. Don't grab her face or fuss with her mane, though, because she hates that shit."

> [!exploration]**The Dock Ward Stablehands**
>
> The stablehands have seen plenty of horses like her on the circuit, but a description of her star marking and her attentive way jogs their memory. Any character who describes Maxeene and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check learns that she is pulling a merchant's wagon on Fillet Lane this afternoon. On a failure, the stablehands send the party to the Wagonworks drivers, who give the route without a check.
>
> > "Oh, the one with the star, yes. The merchant took her down Fillet Lane after the midday deliveries, so you should still catch her there."

On a failed check, use this direction instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stablehand runs the brush down a bay's shoulder and glances toward the wagon yard, where a driver is coiling his reins beside the gate.
>
> > "Can't place her from that, I'm afraid. Ask the Wagonworks drivers, though. They keep track of the afternoon routes, and they'll know which merchant has her."

### The Hire-Coach

Maxeene waits with her wagon on Fillet Lane while Vell watches from a hire-coach across the street, hoping a Harper contact will identify the horse. The coach has been parked for forty minutes when the party arrives, and Maxeene stays until 17:00 unless the party moves her.

> [!readaloud]
>
> On Fillet Lane a grey roan mare stands beside a merchant's wagon while workers carry bundles through the shop door, her harness clean and the white star bright on her forehead. Across the lane a hire-coach waits with its curtains half drawn, and its driver shifts on the seat and watches the loading go on.
>
> The mare turns her head as you come near, following you until her nose drops toward the cloth you are carrying.

> [!exploration]**A Coach without a Fare**
>
> Characters with a Passive Perception score of 14 or higher notice that the coach hasn't moved, even though the driver keeps looking along the lane. Any character who watches it on purpose and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check also sees a passenger following the workers and the horse through a gap in the curtains.
>
> > A hand draws the curtain back an inch whenever someone walks up to the mare, then lets it fall as the workers pass between them.

> [!gamemaster]**When Vell Identifies Maxeene**
>
> Vell confirms the asset when she sees someone talking with Maxeene, or hears the mare speak while the speaker is in view. An apple offered without conversation doesn't identify a Harper asset, and calling the mare by name only gives Vell a horse worth watching.
>
> The wagon blocks Vell's view of its far side, so one character can talk with Maxeene there while companions keep the coach busy by asking for a fare. No check is needed while the wagon hides the mare's head and the talk stays below the noise of the lane. Vell can get a view by leaving her coach, and the companions see her do it. She needs one round to cross the lane before she can confirm the conversation.
>
> If the party approaches openly and talks while she is watching, mark **Maxeene Identified** at once. Relocating Maxeene afterward still protects her, but the early-relocation bonus is gone.

If Vell leaves the coach to see around the wagon, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The coach door swings open and a woman steps down into the lane, one hand on the frame as a cart rolls between you. She looks toward the far side of the wagon and starts across behind the cart while the driver twists round in his seat.
>
> > "You coming back, then? I've got the coach standing here until you tell me we're done."

> [!social]**The Watcher in the Lane**
>
> Vell is a Manshoon Splinter operative who wants to get back with an identification and avoid being held. During a confrontation she keeps the loading wagon in view and edges toward her coach whenever there's a gap. Blocking that route gives the party leverage for the address-for-release exchange below. She doesn't know why Maxeene can speak, and she knows nothing of the wider Harper network.

> [!qna]**Why are you watching us?**
>
> Vell glances toward her coach before turning back to the speaker.
>
> > "I'm waiting on a merchant who said he'd be here after midday. If you're going to keep me standing in the street, at least tell me what you think I've done."

> [!qna]**Where do you report?**
>
> If the party has blocked her route or detained her, she gives the address in exchange for release.
>
> > "There's a building on Shesstra Street with a red lantern upstairs and no sign outside. That's where I take my reports. Let me go, and you can look at it yourselves."

> [!hazard]**Intercepting Vell**
>
> Vell uses the **Spy** from the 2024 *Monster Manual* as printed, with a single HP pool, her Cunning Action and no phases. Combat starts only if the party attacks her or physically detains her and she refuses release terms. Simply confronting her doesn't start a fight.
>
> #### Vell's Tactics
> Vell begins beside the coach, looking for a way out of the street and using the vehicle for Half Cover against attacks from across the lane.
>
> During combat, Vell:
> - Uses Disengage as her Cunning Action when an opponent is adjacent, then moves toward the nearest open street corner.
> - Uses Dash as her Cunning Action when no opponent is adjacent and a route out of sight is open.
> - Attacks the conscious character blocking her escape when neither movement option gets her away.
>
> She surrenders once two conscious opponents stand within 5 feet of her and both street exits are blocked, and she offers her report address for release. The encounter ends when she escapes, surrenders, is defeated or is released on the terms she negotiated.

### What Maxeene Remembers

If Vell is captured, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vell sits beside the coach with her hands where you can see them, watching the workers finish loading across the lane. When the last bundle goes through the shop door, she turns to whoever stands nearest.
>
> > "It's the three-floor building on Shesstra Street, with the red lantern upstairs and no sign outside. Now, who's staying with me when the Watch shows up, and what are you telling them?"

If Vell negotiates her release, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vell backs toward the coach with one hand raised, keeping the open door behind her until you clear a path.
>
> > "Shesstra Street, red lantern upstairs, no sign outside. That's what we agreed, so let me get in the coach."
>
> She climbs in once the way is clear, and the driver pulls away as she shuts the door.

If Vell escapes, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vell slips around the last wagon and vanishes into the street traffic, and the coach driver stands on his footboard and peers toward the corner.
>
> > "She's gone and left me standing here? Then one of you will have to settle the fare before I take another passenger."
>
> Maxeene watches the corner for a moment, then turns her attention back to the people beside her harness.

Maxeene takes the apple and speaks when someone treats her with ordinary professional courtesy. She answers questions about her passengers first and asks to change her route afterward. If someone talks down to her or pats her mane, she stops speaking until that person apologizes and gives her room.

If someone talks down to Maxeene or pats her mane, read or paraphrase the following before she stops answering:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Maxeene draws her head away from the offered hand and swings round until the wagon shaft is between her shoulder and the speaker.
>
> > "Please keep your hands off my mane. I can hear you perfectly well from there, and I'd much rather you spoke to me the way you'd speak to anyone else you were asking for help."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Maxeene takes the apple from your hand and chews while the workers carry another bundle out to the wagon, turning her head to keep them in view. Once they've gone back inside, she shifts closer and drops her voice below the noise of the passing wheels.
>
> > "Is that an apple? Thank you, that's very decent of you. I've had a rather long afternoon, and I was starting to think nobody would ask me anything worth answering."
>
> > "The merchant's been in and out of that shop for an hour now, and every time he says this bundle is the last."

> [!social]**The Mare with a Passenger's Memory**
>
> Maxeene (Unaligned, Draft Horse, she/her) :: a Harper asset who enjoys conversation and expects the same courtesy as anyone else.
>
> Maxeene speaks Common through her permanent Harper enchantment, so nobody needs *Speak with Animals* to talk with her. She remembers destinations and what her passengers said very accurately, but she doesn't know their names unless someone said them in her hearing. She'll talk about the pale man's trip to Shesstra Street, the sun elf's argument with a half-orc and the Candle Lane stables she would like to move to.

> [!qna]**Any unusual passengers?**
>
> Maxeene shifts the harness away from her shoulder before answering.
>
> > "Well, there was a pale man with a carved wooden wand, four days ago. He hired my dray from the Dock Ward to Shesstra Street, in the Trades Ward. It's a building of three floors with no sign, and a red lantern in the upper window."
>
> She turns her nose toward the merchant's shop as another worker comes out.
>
> > "He went inside without a word to anyone at the door, and he never looked back at the wagon. I remember that because most passengers at least check they've got their bags."
>
> > "I waited while the driver counted the fare, so I had a good look at the window and the lantern hanging just inside it."

> [!qna]**What did the others say?**
>
> > "Two weeks ago a sun elf and a very large half-orc hired me to go from the Dock Ward to the Yawning Portal, and they argued most of the way. The elf wanted to keep looking for someone, and the half-orc said that was too exposed. Then the elf said, 'The war doesn't help anyone until we know who's running it.'"
>
> Maxeene flicks her tail as the cart beside her begins to move.
>
> > "I couldn't tell you who they meant. Neither of them used a name while I was listening, and nobody thought to explain it to the horse."
>
> > "The large one watched the street every time we slowed down, while the elf talked as though they had all afternoon to settle it."

> [!qna]**Would you like to move?**
>
> > "I would, yes. I'd like to go to the stables on Candle Lane, if that can be managed. The Dock Ward has felt strange lately."
>
> She looks toward the hire-coach and draws one hoof back from the gutter.
>
> > "The hay there is good, and I won't miss standing next to a gutter full of shit every afternoon. If you've brought permission from Mirt, I'd be very happy to go once someone takes this harness off."
>
> > "I've worked this route long enough to notice when someone keeps turning up at the same corners, and I'd rather have different passengers for a while."

> [!exploration]**The Unmarked Address**
>
> Maxeene's description gives the Shesstra Street address without a check, and it stays a lead even if the party never meets Vell. Any character who compares the account with the Trades Ward business directory and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check confirms that no business is licensed at the three-floor building.
>
> > The directory lists both neighboring premises, but no business name or proprietor appears in the space between their street numbers.

> [!exploration]**The Coach behind Them**
>
> If the party leaves Fillet Lane without dealing with Vell, she follows in the coach until they reach a street too narrow for it, then continues on foot. Characters with a Passive Perception score of 15 or higher notice the same coach several streets behind them. Any character who watches for pursuit and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check spots it too, with the same result.
>
> > The coach turns into the street after you've passed the corner, and slows again when you stop beside a shop window.
>
> The party can lose Vell by going in through the Wagonworks yard's street gate and out through the far passage, which bends behind the stable buildings and can't be seen from the street. Once the gatekeeper sees Mirt's order, he lets the party and Maxeene in and turns Vell away, whether she comes by coach or on foot. This route gets Maxeene to Candle Lane unseen. If the party follows Vell instead, she reaches Shesstra Street twenty minutes after leaving Fillet Lane.

If the party uses the Wagonworks passage, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gatekeeper checks Mirt's signature and swings the yard gate inward, pointing past the stable buildings toward a narrow passage behind them.
>
> > "Take her through the far passage behind the stables, and lead her slowly where it narrows. I'll keep the gate while you get her out to the other street."

If Vell reaches the gate afterward, add:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gatekeeper hauls the gate across Vell's path and holds the bar against its frame, leaving her on the street side.
>
> > "This is a private yard, and the folk who came through had an order for the mare. You can wait out here with the other fares while they take her through."

### Candle Lane or Shesstra Street

Hessa Dorn accepts Mirt's order at the Candle Lane stables, and the merchant releases Maxeene as soon as the party shows the signed order. Unharnessing takes ten minutes and the walk to Candle Lane takes twenty, so the party has to deal with any watcher still following them.

When the party presents the order to the merchant, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The merchant sets his bundle on the wagon and reads the order beside Maxeene's shoulder, checking the signature before he reaches for the harness buckle.
>
> > "Well, Mirt's settled the day's work, so that's that. Give me ten minutes to get this harness off her, and you can take her wherever the order says."

> [!gamemaster]**Relocation before or after Identification**
>
> If Vell hasn't identified Maxeene and the party leaves without a tail, Hessa changes the mare's route the next morning and her cover holds. Mark **Maxeene Relocated** and **Maxeene Cover Preserved**, which meets the early-relocation bonus condition.
>
> If Vell has identified her, relocating through the Wagonworks passage still takes her out of immediate surveillance. Mark **Maxeene Relocated** and keep **Maxeene Identified**. Mirt suspends her intelligence work for a tenday while Hessa changes the route, and she becomes available again afterward, though the Splinter keeps her description.
>
> If Vell follows the move all the way to Candle Lane, she learns the new stable as well. Hessa keeps Maxeene out of dray work for ten days, then sends her to a North Ward circuit with a standing point at the junction of Saerdoun Street and Trollskull Alley. Mirt pays for a covered wagon to carry her there before dawn on the eleventh day. Her reporting resumes on that circuit, but the Splinter keeps her description and the Candle Lane address, and this move doesn't preserve her cover.
>
> If she stays on Fillet Lane after being identified, the Splinter watches her route and she stops reporting within seven days. Mirt pulls her from dray work when the members tell him, but the chance to protect an unidentified asset is gone.

If Maxeene arrives safely at Candle Lane, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Hessa takes Mirt's paper, checks the signature and opens a stall where fresh hay has been spread beneath a clean water bucket.
>
> > "Bring her in here. Mirt's paid for the stall, and I'll have water ready in a moment. Was anyone behind you on the way over?"
>
> Maxeene steps inside and lowers her nose to the hay, then turns toward you as Hessa takes the lead rope from your hand.
>
> > "Oh, that's much better, thank you. Do tell Mirt where you've brought me, and I'll see what the passengers on the new route have to say."

> [!hazard]**The Shesstra Street Safehouse**
>
> The three-floor building has no sign, a red lantern in the upper window and a closed but unlocked front door. Two **Spies** hold the ground floor, one ten feet behind the door and one at the staircase, and the upper floors hold sleeping rooms and an office with a desk. Vell gives her report at the door and leaves on her next turn, so the roster stays at two unless the party intercepts her first.
>
> #### The Residents' Tactics
> The residents draw their weapons when intruders force their way past the entrance, keeping the staircase behind them.
>
> During combat, the residents:
> - Attack separate intruders when they can, and use Cunning Action to fall back toward the staircase if pressed.
> - Guard the upstairs office and pursue no farther than thirty feet beyond the front door.
> - Surrender when one resident is defeated and two conscious opponents stand within thirty feet of the survivor.
>
> The encounter ends when both residents are defeated or surrender, or when the party withdraws beyond their pursuit limit. If the party intercepts Vell before she leaves, she can join the same encounter, and the **Harpers Mechanics Reference** covers the combined and smaller-group cases. The party can watch the door and upper window, report the address or leave without going in, and the mission is complete without a fight.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt hears the participating Harper members' account at his residence at 20:00 that evening, or at 09:00 the next morning if they are still investigating. Companions wait outside during the private debrief, and the members can share their own report with them afterward.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets a plate of food beside the wine jug and pulls a chair back from the table, making room for you to spread out your notes. He waits while you settle in and arrange the papers in front of him.
>
> > "Sit. Tell me what she said first, then what you saw yourselves. I need to know which parts I can put someone to work on tonight."

After hearing the account, Mirt sets his glass beside the notes:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Where is she now? Did anyone follow you from Fillet Lane, and did they see where you left her?"

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each Harper member who took part and reports the result gains 2 base Renown, even if Maxeene's identity was exposed. Add +1 Renown for **Maxeene Cover Preserved**, which needs relocation before identification and no surviving tail learning the new stable. Add +1 Renown for killing Vell or holding her in custody before she delivers her report, or for following her to Shesstra Street and reporting that address. A brief detention ending in negotiated release doesn't earn the neutralization bonus. Each bonus applies once, and relocating after exposure limits the harm without earning the cover bonus.

### Aftermath

If the members report Shesstra Street, Mirt adds the address to his investigation of the hostile surveillance, which gives **Faction Outposts** a lead backed by the party's own observations. Maxeene's account never names the sun elf or the half-orc, and this mission supplies no further identity for them.

If they report the address, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt writes Shesstra Street on a fresh sheet and lays it beside your notes, running a finger under the description of the red lantern.
>
> > "Did you watch the door, or did you go inside? Tell me what you saw. I'll have someone look into it and find out who else has been using that building."
>
> He writes your answer beneath the address and folds the sheet into his pocket.

If Maxeene was left exposed, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets down his glass and takes out the stable order again, adding a short line beneath his signature before he rings for a servant. Once the servant has carried the paper out, he turns back to your account of the coach on Fillet Lane.
>
> > "I'll get her off that route. They've had a look at her now, so she'll need some time before she works for us again."

### Concluding the Event

The members' report ends the assignment. Record Maxeene's location and what the Splinter knows about her separately, so later visits can follow the result.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Maxeene Relocated** — mark when she reaches Candle Lane or Mirt completes the withdrawal requested at the debrief; later Harper contacts read her location and availability.
> - **Maxeene Cover Preserved** — mark only when she reaches Candle Lane before Vell identifies her and without a surviving tail learning the new stable; later Harper contacts can use her new route without suspending her work.
> - **Maxeene Identified** — mark when Vell sees or hears the speaking contact clearly; relocation doesn't remove it, and later Splinter surveillance retains her description.
> - **Vell Neutralized** — mark when Vell is dead or held in custody before she delivers her report; brief detention followed by negotiated release doesn't qualify, and Mirt's outpost preparation reads whether her report can still be delivered.
> - **Shesstra Street Reported** — mark when the members give Mirt the address, whether learned from Maxeene or Vell; **Faction Outposts** reads this as an available Splinter lead.

## Overview

The party looks for a speaking draft mare who has spent her working days listening to her passengers, and decides how to keep her safe.

## Summary

We found Maxeene on Fillet Lane and heard about an unmarked building on Shesstra Street and two passengers arguing on their way to the Yawning Portal. A woman in a hire-coach was watching for someone to identify the mare, and we told Mirt everything we had learned.
