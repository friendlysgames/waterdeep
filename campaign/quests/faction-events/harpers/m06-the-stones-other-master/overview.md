# The Stone's Other Master: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Harper member reaches Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers** and before the Vault of Dragons opens. Companions can fight beside the members and sit in on the study without belonging to the faction.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 7th-level characters.*
>
> The raid is a Hard fight against the Mage Ysolde Marne with Spies, Toughs and a Tough Boss by party size. The second fight, in the cellar, is Ysolde alone. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** audits both for three, four and five participating combatants.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

Mirt comes to Trollskull Manor at 00:15 and asks the Harper members for three days with the Stone of Golorr before the vault is opened. A Harper seer felt something old and patient answer the Stone from far below the city, and he wants to learn what the link is before anyone relies on it again.

## Background

The Stone holds Golorr, an aboleth. When it woke, another aboleth answered from Undermountain Level 4. Mirt's seer, Ivara Dunn, felt the answer, and she traced it to a living person: Jalester Silvermane, the Lords' Alliance agent who spends his evenings beside the Yawning Portal's well. Jalester doesn't know, and he is still loyal to the Open Lord. Members who heard the Emerald Enclave's reports in **The Water Table Stirs** recognise the description.

The Splinter has watched the manor since the Stone woke. Its survivors wait for Mirt's coach to leave and raid the manor at 01:30. If **Kolat Towers** left Manshoon operational, they carry his last orders. If it left him Destroyed, they act for themselves.

## The Brief

Mirt asks for three days with the Stone, tired and plain, and does not swear. The party can ask what Ivara felt, why three days and where the study would happen, and then Mirt leaves to fetch his coach and Ivara.

## The Raid

At 01:30 Ysolde Marne and her raiders break in for the Stone. She takes it first, using *Mage Hand* or a grab, and escapes by *Misty Step* and *Fly* if she can. The party can fight, talk her down or take her alive. Her sending stone carries one message and one reply.

## The Chase

If Ysolde escapes with the Stone, she waits in a cellar under a shuttered cooperage off Salabar Street for a courier due at 06:00. The party finds her by a prisoner, a watcher or Mirt's outriders, and then fights her or talks her down.

## Mirt's Return and the Three Days

Mirt returns at 02:00 and takes the party's answer. The party can agree, bargain to stay with the Stone or refuse, and each answer has a different cost. If the study runs, it finishes at 06:00 on the fourth morning with the guardian's oath and, for a member who asks, Jalester's name.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Harper member gains 4 base Renown for facing the raid and reporting to Mirt. Bonuses of +1 go to members who complete the study and to members who take Ysolde's sending stone.

## Aftermath

Mirt accepts a refusal without penalty, and the study and the sending stone feed **Vault of Dragons**, and Jalester's name feeds **An Audience with the Open Lord**. This is the last Harper mission, and **Wise Owl** and **High Harper** follow at Renown 25 and 50.

## Involved Characters

- **Mirt** (Harpers): asks for the Stone, returns at 02:00 and takes the party's answer.
- **Ivara Dunn** (Harpers): the seer who felt the answer and conducts the study.
- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance): the unwitting contact the study identifies, who does not appear in this mission.
- **Ysolde Marne** (the Splinter): the Mage who leads the raid.

## Dangers & Enemies

The raid is a Hard fight against Ysolde with Spies, Toughs and a Tough Boss, chosen by party size, and the cellar is Ysolde alone with no daily spells. Ysolde casts no *Contingency*, and nobody escapes to a fortress. The real danger is losing the Stone to the courier at 06:00.

## Overview

Mirt asks the Harper members for three days with the Stone of Golorr before the vault is opened, because a seer felt something answer it from below the city. Before the night is over, Splinter survivors raid Trollskull Manor to take it.
