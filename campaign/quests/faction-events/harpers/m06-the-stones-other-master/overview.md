# The Stone's Other Master: Overview

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available at Renown 13 and 7th level, and only after the Stone of Golorr has been partially awakened (at least one Eye installed). Triggers during Arc J — The Vault of Dragons preparation phase.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 7th level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Mirt** (Harpers) — arrives after midnight through the front door; exhausted; asking, not demanding
- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance) — the individual the Stone's study will identify as psychically compromised if the PCs comply; not present in this mission; he does not know
- **Splinter Squad** — two Thugs and a Mage under a squad leader carrying a sending stone keyed to Manshoon; arrives within two hours of Mirt's arrival; knows the Stone is at Trollskull Manor

## Dangers & Enemies
- Splinter Squad: two Thugs, one Mage, one squad leader (Spy stat block with a contingency spell)
- The Mage carries a contingency spell to get the Stone back to Manshoon if obtained

## Overview

Mirt arrived at Trollskull Manor after midnight, through the front door this time. He sat with an untouched drink until someone came downstairs. He was direct: something had woken up when the party used the Stone. A Harper seer had felt a resonance — something old and patient below the city. He wanted three days with the Stone before it opened the vault.

He was asking. He looked tired.

The negotiation was interrupted by a Manshoon Splinter squad that had been watching Trollskull Manor and moved on the building within two hours of Mirt's arrival. Whether the party had agreed or refused, the Splinter wanted the Stone.

The mission's resolution shaped two things: what tactical intelligence the Harpers brought to Arc J, and whether the party learned the name of the individual the Stone had identified as psychically compromised.
