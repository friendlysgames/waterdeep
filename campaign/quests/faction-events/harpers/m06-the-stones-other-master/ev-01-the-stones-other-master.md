# The Stone's Other Master

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Combat Event occurs after **Kolat Towers** and before the Vault of Dragons opens. It begins when Mirt comes to Trollskull Manor after midnight and ends when the participating members report to him, at dawn if the party refuses or three days later if the Stone goes to his manor. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Mirt's request for three days with the Stone, then agree, bargain for terms or refuse.
> - Defend the Stone against a Splinter raid at 01:30, or talk the raid's leader into walking away.
> - Take the leader's sending stone and hear what answers it.
> - Chase the Stone across the city if the leader escapes with it.
> - Learn what Mirt's seer found, including the vault guardian's oath and, if they ask, who the Stone has touched.
>
> Mirt briefs and debriefs only the Harper members. Companions can fight beside the members and sit in on the study, but they earn no Renown.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The Stone holds Golorr, an aboleth. Illuun, another aboleth, dreams on Undermountain Level 4 and has answered the Stone each time it grew stronger. The Harper seer Ivara Dunn felt the answer when the Stone woke. Neither she nor Mirt knows Illuun's name.
> - Illuun listens through Jalester Silvermane, the Lords' Alliance agent who spends his evenings beside the Yawning Portal's well. He doesn't know, and he is still loyal to Laeral. The contact is always Jalester and never Renaer.
> - A Splinter watcher on a roof across the alley has watched Trollskull Manor since the Stone woke. He is a street watcher, so **Harper Leak Closed** makes no difference to the raid.
> - The raiders are survivors of the Splinter's Waterdeep cell, led by the Mage Ysolde Marne. They wait for Mirt's coach to leave because they will not face him.
> - **Kolat Towers** decides who stands behind them. If its **Manshoon operational?** result is Alive or Simulacrum Only, Ysolde carries Manshoon's last orders: take the Stone, hold it in a cellar and hand it to a courier at 06:00. If the result is Destroyed, nobody gave an order. The survivors mean to take the Stone for themselves, because whoever holds it can sell it or buy a way out of the city.
> - Nobody in this Event says Manshoon's name or calls Kolat Towers his home unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for that member, and Mirt says "the Splinter" until then.

### The Brief

Mirt reaches Trollskull Manor at 00:15 and has the night staff wake the Harper members. He puts his request to everyone who holds a claim on the Stone, because Harper membership gives him no say over another person's property.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom is dark except for one lamp on the long table, and the fire has burned down to a few red coals. Mirt sits with his coat still on and an untouched cup at his elbow. He pours a drink for each of you as you sit down.
>
> > "Sit. I'm sorry about the hour, and I wouldn't have come if it could wait. One of our seers, Ivara Dunn, felt something answer when the Stone woke. It was old and patient, and it was a long way under the city."
>
> He turns the bottle on the table until the label faces him.
>
> > "I want three days with the Stone before anyone uses it to open the vault. Ivara needs to learn what that link is, and I'll bring the Stone back."

> [!social]**Mirt, the Old Wolf at Work**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a big man in plain brown wool, tired, and in his business manner.
>
> Mirt speaks in short, complete sentences tonight. He does not swear or laugh, and he tilts his head when he decides how much to say. He answers each question once and does not repeat himself.
>
> Mirt will discuss the following topics:
>
> - What Ivara felt and what he wants the three days for.
> - Where the study would happen and how long it takes.
> - Whether the Splinter has shown an interest in the Stone.
>
> Mirt will not guess what the thing below the city wants, because he doesn't know. He says "the Splinter" and does not name its master.

> [!qna]**What did she feel?**
>
> > "Ivara says it was old and patient, and a long way down. She felt it notice the Stone, and she was sick for a day afterward. She can't tell me more, and she's angry about that."

> [!qna]**Why three days?**
>
> > "She works in stages and has to rest between them. I won't hurry her, because a rushed reading is worse than none."

> [!qna]**Where would the study happen?**
>
> > "In my cellar, where the wards are good and Ivara trusts the walls. We'll talk about who goes with the Stone when I come back."

> [!qna]**What if we say no?**
>
> > "Then it's no, and I go home and sleep badly, because it's your Stone. I'd ask you to tell me to my face, though, and not by bird."

> [!qna]**Is the Splinter after it?**
>
> > "They've wanted it a long time. I don't know that they'll try tonight, but I wouldn't leave it on a shelf."

> [!exploration]**A Description You Know**
>
> A character who heard Melannor Fellbranch or Jeryth Phaulkon report on the dreaming presence in **The Water Table Stirs** recognises Ivara's words without a check. Jeryth called it old, patient and hungry. A character who says so to Mirt gets the reply below.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt stops with the bottle tilted over a cup, then sets it down and takes a small book from inside his coat.
>
> > "Say that again from the beginning, and leave nothing out. If the Enclave felt it too, then two seers agree, and I'd rather hear it from you than guess."

When the questions slow, Mirt leaves at 01:00. His coach waits in the alley, and the Splinter watcher sees it go.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt stands and buttons his coat to the throat, then leaves the untouched cup where it is and walks to the front door.
>
> > "I'll be back at two with my coach and Ivara. Think it over, and keep the Stone where you can reach it."
>
> The door closes behind him, and the coach wheels fade along the alley.

### The Raid

The raid begins at 01:30, half an hour after the coach leaves. The raiders know the manor, the staff and where the Stone was seen. They don't know the party's answer, because the party hasn't given one.

> [!exploration]**Seeing Them First**
>
> A character posted at a window or in the alley sees the raiders gather at 01:20 without a check. Any other character who looks into the alley makes a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check to see them. If the party strikes first or bars both doors, Ysolde can't spend her first turn on the Stone. She opens with Arcane Burst, and the roster below steps down one row. Three combatants face Ysolde alone, four face the three-combatant roster and five face the four-combatant roster.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom is dark except for the banked fire, and the shutters rattle in the wind off the harbor. Boots scrape on the cobbles of Trollskull Alley, and the front latch lifts with a soft click. A woman in a grey traveling cloak steps inside with one hand held out flat, and armed figures spread through the room behind her.
>
> > "Put the Stone on the table and walk upstairs. Nobody here has to bleed for it."

> [!gamemaster]**The Stone Plan**
>
> Ysolde's first action is to take the Stone, and how she does it depends on where it is. The Stone is small enough for *Mage Hand*.
>
> - **Unattended and within 30 feet of her:** she casts *Mage Hand* as her Magic action, and the hand brings the Stone to her at once.
> - **In a satchel or on a character:** a raider takes the Utilize action and makes a Strength (Athletics) check, contested by the holder's Strength (Athletics). Spies and Toughs have no Athletics proficiency.
> - **Held in a character's hand:** the raider must Grapple the holder first.
> - **Getting clear:** whoever holds the Stone needs a bonus action Dash to get away, and a Spy uses Cunning Action. The Stone is **taken** when a raider carrying it leaves the building.
> - **Escape:** Ysolde casts *Fly* and uses *Misty Step* on the turn after she takes the Stone. A Spy carries it out by Dashing.

> [!hazard]**The Splinter Raid**
>
> Use the **Mage**, **Tough Boss**, **Spy** and **Tough** stat blocks from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, unchanged. The 2024 Mage has no *Contingency*, and Ysolde casts none. Count every combatant on the party's side, companions included. Mirt is away until 02:00 and is not counted. The **Harpers Mechanics Reference** audits these rosters as Hard.
>
> - **Three combatants:** Ysolde Marne (Mage) and one Spy.
> - **Four combatants:** Ysolde, the Tough Boss and one Spy.
> - **Five or more combatants:** Ysolde, the Tough Boss, two Spies and one Tough.
>
> If the party starts the fight hurt, drop one Spy or the Tough from the roster.
>
> #### Ysolde Marne's Tactics
> Ysolde opens by taking the Stone and starts her Arcane Bursts in round 2.
>
> During combat, the raiders:
> - Have Ysolde follow **The Stone Plan** in round 1, then make her three Arcane Bursts at three different conscious characters each round.
> - Have Ysolde cast *Fireball* only on three or more clustered characters, and only when the Stone is outside the area.
> - Have Ysolde use Protective Magic for *Counterspell* on any spell that would pull the Stone out of her reach, and for *Shield* otherwise.
> - Have the Spies go for the Stone's holder, using Cunning Action to Hide and Disengage.
> - Have the Tough Boss hold the front door and push the nearest character 10 feet with his Warhammer. Toughs use Pack Tactics against anyone within 5 feet of a raider.
>
> Ysolde retreats at 40 Hit Points. With the Stone she leaves by *Misty Step* and *Fly*. Without it she sends "Failed" on her sending stone and leaves the same way. She surrenders if cornered at 20 Hit Points or fewer, and she gives up her sending stone and the cell's cellar in exchange for her life.
>
> The Spies disengage when two raiders are down or the Stone leaves the building. The Tough Boss and any Tough fight until Ysolde leaves, then surrender.
>
> The fight is over when the raiders are down, surrendered or gone.

> [!exploration]**Talking Her Down**
>
> Any character who studies Ysolde during the fight and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that she does not want to die for this. A character who then offers her safe passage out of the city, once a raider is down, makes a DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check, with Advantage if the Insight check succeeded.
>
> On a success, Ysolde orders the raiders to stand down and walks out with her sending stone, leaving the Stone behind. On a failure, she doesn't believe the offer and the fight goes on. The raiders are not hunted afterward, and no outcome is marked for it.

If Ysolde takes the Stone and escapes, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Ysolde closes her hand around the Stone and steps sideways through empty air to land at the window. The shutters bang open under her hand, and she rises into the dark over Trollskull Alley. By the time anyone reaches the sill, she is gone over the rooftops.

>
> Ysolde will discuss the following topics:

> - Where she was taking the Stone.
> - The sending stone in her pocket.
>
> Ysolde will not say how many survivors the cell has left or where they sleep.

> [!qna]**Who sent you?**

If the result is Destroyed, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!qna]**Where were you taking it?**

### The Sending Stone

Ysolde carries one stone of a matched pair. It is a smooth grey stone that fits the palm. A holder touches it, speaks up to 25 words, and a reply comes back within the minute. The pair gives one message each way per day, and breaking one makes the other mundane. Ysolde sends "Stone taken" or "Failed" as soon as the outcome is clear.

> [!gamemaster]**What Answers**
>
> The mate's holder replies to the message Ysolde sent. If she is captured before she hears it, the reply waits in the stone until a member first touches it.
>
> - **"Stone taken":** "Good, so stay hidden until someone comes for it at dawn."
> - **"Failed":** "Understood, so break the stone and go to ground."
> - **No message sent:** the stone is silent. At the next dawn a member can send up to 25 words, and the reply is: "Who is this? Say what you want, or put the stone down."
>
> If the result is Alive or Simulacrum Only, the voice is dry and level, and no member can place it unless **Manshoon Named** is marked. If the result is Destroyed, the voice belongs to a surviving Splinter officer whom nobody in the party knows.

If the voice belongs to an Alive or Simulacrum Only Manshoon and **Manshoon Named** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt listens with his head tilted and the stone held close to his ear. He sets it down on the table when the voice stops.
>
> > "I don't know that voice. Whoever it is, they're used to being obeyed."

If the voice belongs to an Alive or Simulacrum Only Manshoon and **Manshoon Named** is marked, read this instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt listens with his head tilted and the stone held close to his ear. He sets it down on the table when the voice stops.
>
> > "That's Manshoon, or something that talks for him. I'd know it anywhere, and I wish I didn't."

If the result is Destroyed, read or paraphrase the following whether or not **Manshoon Named** is marked:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt listens with his head tilted and the stone held close to his ear. He sets it down on the table when the voice stops.
>
> > "That isn't a voice I know, and someone is still giving orders, so the Splinter isn't finished."

### The Chase

This scene runs only if Ysolde leaves with the Stone. She lands in a cellar under a shuttered cooperage off Salabar Street, and she waits there for the courier who is due at 06:00. Her other raiders do not return to her. The Spies and Toughs who got away run for the city gates.

> [!exploration]**Three Ways to the Cellar**
>
> - **A prisoner:** a captured raider other than a Tough gives the address when asked. No check is needed once the raider has surrendered.
> - **A watcher:** a character who watched Ysolde leave and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check saw her come down on the roofs above Salabar Street.
> - **Mirt's outriders:** two Harper outriders saw a flying woman land, and Mirt has the address when he returns at 02:00. This works if the first two ways fail, but it costs the party 30 minutes.
>
> Mirt stays at the manor, and his outriders watch the cellar door without going in. They are watchers and not fighters, so the party goes in alone. The party must reach the cellar before 06:00, when the courier arrives and takes the Stone.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A stair behind the shuttered cooperage leads down into a low brick cellar that smells of damp oak and old tar, and one lamp burns on an upturned barrel. Ysolde Marne sits beside it with the Stone in her lap and a grey stone in her hand, and she looks up at the sound of boots on the steps.
>
> > "I wondered whether you or the courier would reach me first. Close the door and tell me what you want."

> [!gamemaster]**Ysolde in the Cellar**
>
> Ysolde has no daily spells left. She has Arcane Burst and her at-will spells, and she starts with the Hit Points she left the manor with. The party rolls initiative with Advantage, because she is waiting for a courier. The raiders from the manor do not return, and this is a second fight on the same night, so a party that arrives hurt should take the talking route first.
>
> - **Talk:** the **Talking Her Down** checks apply, with no raider needing to be down first. On a success she hands over the Stone and keeps her sending stone.
> - **Fight:** Ysolde uses the **Mage** stat block and fires her three Arcane Bursts at three different characters each round. She can't retreat, so she surrenders at 20 Hit Points or fewer and gives up the sending stone with the Stone.

If the party reaches the cellar after 06:00, the courier has taken the Stone and the cellar is empty. Mark **Stone Taken by Splinter** when the Event ends.

### Mirt's Return

Mirt's coach reaches the alley at 02:00 with two outriders and Ivara Dunn. He finds the aftermath and waits in the taproom until dawn. He does not leave before the party gives its answer, and he does not fight in this Event.

If the Stone is still in the party's hands, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Hooves and wheels sound in the alley, and the front door opens on Mirt with his coat thrown open. A slender woman with ink on her cuffs follows him in and stops at the sight of the room. He looks over the overturned chairs and the faces around the table before he speaks.
>
> > "Is anyone badly hurt? Then sit, and tell me what came through that door."

If the Stone was taken and not yet recovered, read this instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Hooves and wheels sound in the alley, and the front door opens on Mirt with his coat thrown open. A slender woman with ink on her cuffs follows him in and stops at the sight of the room. He looks at your empty hands before he speaks.
>
> > "They took it, so tell me which way they went, and my outriders will start now."

Mirt asks for the party's answer once the Stone is back in the party's hands.

> [!gamemaster]**Three Answers**
>
> - **Agree.** Mirt takes the Stone at 06:00 in his coach and returns it at 06:00 on the fourth morning. The party does without the Stone for three days, and Mirt leaves two outriders in the alley.
> - **Bargain.** The party can ask that the Harper members and up to two companions stay with the Stone at Mirt's manor for all three days. Mirt agrees at once. The cost is the time, because those who stay can't act elsewhere. The gain is that Mirt answers the question about who the Stone has touched on the second evening, not only on the fourth morning.
> - **Refuse.** Mirt accepts, stays until dawn and leaves, and the party gets no study and no guardian intelligence. He takes no offence and sets no penalty.

> [!qna]**May we watch the study?**
>
> > "Yes, if you bargain for it. Three days in my cellar is a long time, though, and Ivara won't talk while you're hovering, so sit where she tells you."

> [!qna]**What do you owe us?**
>
> > "A debt, if you give me the time. I keep a ledger, and I pay what's in it."

If the party refuses, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt nods slowly and pushes his chair back from the table. He waits until grey light shows at the shutters before he says another word.
>
> > "I understand, and you should keep it safe. Tell me if it shows you anything you didn't expect. Ivara thinks the thing below is waiting for the vault to open. She can't say why, and neither can I."

### The Three Days

The study runs from the handover at 06:00 to 06:00 on the fourth morning, in a cellar at Mirt's manor that Ivara has warded. No Eye comes out of the Stone, and nobody's attunement changes. If the Stone is lost or taken at any point, the study stops and no result is produced.

> [!exploration]**What the Study Finds**
>
> The study produces two findings, and Mirt reports both at the fourth morning's debrief.
>
> - **The guardian.** The vault's guardian is a gold dragon called Aurinax. He is bound by an oath to Neverember's authority, and anyone with real authority can release him from it. Threats won't move him, and this finding is always given.
> - **The link.** The Stone's answer from below runs through a living person, and Ivara traced it to Jalester Silvermane. Mirt tells the members that a person is involved and asks whether they want the name. He gives it only when a member asks, and never for free.
>
> A member who stays for the study can ask on the second evening. A member who doesn't can ask at the debrief or at any later meeting with Mirt.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt receives the participating members at his manor. If the study ran, he does so at 06:00 on the fourth morning. If the party refused, he does so at the taproom at dawn. If the Stone was taken and not recovered, he does so at dawn in the taproom.

If the study ran, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The dining room at Mirt's manor smells of hot bread and candle wax. The Stone sits in a velvet cloth beside his plate, and Ivara is asleep in a chair by the cold hearth with her head on her arm. Mirt pushes the Stone across the table to you.
>
> > "Sit. Nothing in it has moved. Ivara has two findings, and the first is about the vault. Its guardian is a gold dragon called Aurinax, bound by oath to Neverember's authority. A real release from that authority frees him, and a threat won't."

> [!qna]**What answered from below?**
>
> > "Another like Golorr, Ivara thinks, awake somewhere in the dark and as old as the city. She can't name it, but she says it listens, and it has been listening a long time."

> [!qna]**What is the second finding?**
>
> Mirt lays both hands flat on the table.
>
> > "The link runs through a living person. He's someone you know, and he doesn't know it himself. Once you have the name you have to decide what to do with it. Do you want it?"

If a member asks for the name, read or paraphrase the following, and mark **Jalester Compromise Identified**:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "It's Jalester Silvermane, and Ivara traced the link to the Yawning Portal and the hours he spends beside its well. He doesn't know, and I don't believe he serves it willingly. Keep your plans off his route until this is dealt with."

> [!qna]**Can it be cured?**
>
> > "Not by us, though Ivara thinks it ends when the thing below does, and that's work for Undermountain. Whether Laeral hears of this is for you to decide, and I won't decide it for you."

If no member asks for the name, Mirt writes it in his book and says it will keep until someone asks.

If the party refused, the Stone is still in its hands, and Mirt's farewell readaloud above stands as the debrief.

If the Stone was taken and not recovered, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt stands at the shutters while the light grows, and he does not turn when he speaks. Ivara waits by the door with her hands folded.
>
> > "They have it, and I'll find where it went. The vault can't be opened without it, and they know that as well as we do. Go home and rest, and I'll send a bird."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains 4 base Renown for facing the raid and reporting to Mirt. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** complete the three-day study and receive its results, and mark **Stone Study Completed**.

### Aftermath

If the party refuses, Mirt's door stays open and the Harper contact stays, and he does not ask again.

If Ysolde is captured, she goes to Mirt's people unless the party lets her go. A released Ysolde disappears from the city by morning. The raiders who surrender are held at Mirt's manor and learn nothing more than Ysolde did.

Mirt does not approach Jalester. He moves Harper business away from the Yawning Portal and tells no one else. Whether Laeral learns of the compromise depends on the party, as **An Audience with the Open Lord** describes.

Ivara sleeps for most of the day after the study and does not speak of what she felt.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the participating members have reported to Mirt. Record the study, the raid and the Stone separately, because the party can complete the study and still lose the sending stone, or the other way round.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Stone Study Completed** — mark when the Stone returns from the three-day study; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), which uses the guardian's oath and the link to Undermountain.
> - **Jalester Compromise Identified** — mark when a member asks Mirt for the name and he gives it; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) and by **An Audience with the Open Lord**, whose Jalester Question treats it as a free-text reference.
> - **Splinter Sending Stone Recovered** — mark when a member holds Ysolde's sending stone; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), which decides what the mate's holder knows.
> - **Stone Taken by Splinter** — mark if the Stone is not in the party's hands when the Event ends; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).

## Overview

Mirt asks the Harper members for three days with the Stone of Golorr before the vault is opened, because a seer felt something answer it from below the city. Before the night is over, survivors of the Splinter raid Trollskull Manor to take it.

## Summary

Mirt came to Trollskull Manor after midnight and asked for three days with the Stone, because a Harper seer had felt something old and patient answer it from below the city. Splinter survivors raided the manor before he returned, and we dealt with them and with the Stone as best we could. We still don't know what is down there, or why it has been listening.
