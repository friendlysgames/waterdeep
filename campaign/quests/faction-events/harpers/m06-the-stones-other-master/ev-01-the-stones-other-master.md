# The Stone's Other Master

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social/Combat Event occurs when Mirt arrives at Trollskull Manor after midnight during the preparation phase for **Vault of Dragons**. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Mirt's request for three days with the Stone of Golorr and decide whether to comply
> - Press him on what Harper seers detected below the city and what the Stone may reveal
> - Negotiate added intelligence about the vault's secondary safeguards in exchange for access
> - Defend Trollskull Manor against a Splinter squad that moves regardless of the negotiation's outcome
> - Run down the squad leader and recover his sending stone, which is keyed to Manshoon's voice directly
> - Learn — if they comply and ask directly — the name of a contact psychically compromised by an abolethic influence below the city
>
> #### Background
>
> The Stone of Golorr contains the transformed soul of the aboleth Golorr. Installing an Eye activates it, and that activation produces a psychic resonance that reached Illuun, an abolethic dreamer on Undermountain Level 4. Harper seers detected the resonance and passed it to Mirt. What Mirt does not share initially: the Harpers believe the Stone can identify individuals psychically compromised by Illuun's influence, and they have two suspected assets.
>
> The individual the study will name is **Jalester Silvermane** (Lawful Good, Cormyrean human), the Lords' Alliance operative at the Yawning Portal. Illuun's influence reached him through extended time near the portal's edge. He is unaware of the compromise and believes his reports and loyalties are entirely his own. If the party has had little meaningful contact with Jalester, use **Renaer Neverember** instead — the revelation lands harder with a familiar face.
>
> A Splinter watcher has been tracking the Stone's magical signature since activation and reported Mirt's location before the negotiation was settled. The squad moves regardless of what the conversation produces.

### The Brief

Mirt comes in through the front door tonight, which is unusual. He settles into the taproom with an untouched drink and waits for someone to come downstairs.

> [!readaloud]
> The front door opens not long after midnight, and Mirt lets himself in without knocking. He settles into the taproom with a drink in front of him that he doesn't touch, and he sits there in the dark until you appear.
>
> He looks up when you appear, and he looks tired in a way he is not trying to disguise.
>
> > "Sit down. There's something you need to hear."
>
> He folds his hands on the table and waits for you to settle.
>
> > "Something woke up when you used the Stone. One of our seers felt it — something below the city. I need three days with the Stone before you use it to open the vault. I'll give it back. You have my word."
>
> He is making a request, not an ultimatum, and he is waiting for an answer.

> [!social]**The Old Wolf at Midnight**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan human, he/him) :: a moneylender and Harper operative who normally performs ease; tonight he is not performing anything.
>
> This is Business Mirt: short sentences, plain words, no swearing, no jokes. He arrived through the front door rather than the roof or the back — he wanted the party to know this was not a covert visit. He is asking for something, not taking it, and that distinction matters to him.
>
> He explains what he knows without embellishment: the Stone holds a consciousness, not just a map, and that consciousness is connected to something old and patient below the city. If the party opens the vault while that connection is active, they may be offering it exactly what it has been waiting for. He does not mention the compromised contact unless pressed directly.
>
> If pressed on what the Stone can do beyond opening the vault, he tells the truth: the study may identify individuals whose thinking has been influenced from below. He cannot promise the results will not name someone the party trusts.
>
> Negotiating for additional intelligence: Mirt will share Harper findings on a vault safeguard not visible on the ceremonial lock, in exchange for access. He offers this even without full trust; he needs the three days more than he needs the upper hand.
>
> Refusing outright: Mirt accepts it. "I understand. Keep it safe." He means it without sentiment, and he stays for whatever comes next — he was in the building when the squad arrived.
>
> Conversation topics Mirt is willing to discuss include:
> - What the resonance was and where it leads
> - What three days of study will produce
> - What the Stone can do beyond opening the vault
> - What happens if they use the Stone before the study runs
> - What vault intelligence he will share in exchange for compliance
>
> Specific dialogue for Mirt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**What did the seer feel?**
>
> He turns the untouched glass in his hands.
>
> > "A dreamer. Something old, beneath the city — well below anything you've been in yet. Our seer felt the activation as a pulse and followed it down. What answered is not something I can name without more time."

> [!qna]**What will three days give you?**
>
> > "A clearer picture of what the Stone is. It holds a mind, not a map — imprisoned, waiting. Three days lets us work with it carefully and come back with what we learn, rather than simply using it and handing whatever is below exactly what it has been waiting for."

> [!qna]**What aren't you telling us?**
>
> His head tilts slightly before he answers.
>
> > "The Stone may be able to identify people in this city whose thinking has been influenced from below — not willing agents, not aware of it themselves. We have two suspected contacts. If the study finds what we think it will find, you will have a name. I cannot promise it will not be someone you know."

> [!qna]**What if we refuse?**
>
> > "Then keep it safe and use it when you're ready. I understand the position." He lifts the glass and sets it down without drinking. "I'm not going to leave you to whatever comes next. I was already here when it got here."

> [!qna]**What do we get for saying yes?**
>
> > "There is a safeguard on the vault that does not appear on the ceremonial lock — it would not appear in any documentation you've seen. I know about it through sources I will not name. You will find it eventually, but finding it in the room is considerably worse than knowing about it before you arrive."

Once the party has made their decision, proceed to **The Raid**.

### The Raid

A Splinter watcher was tracking the Stone's magical signature and reported Mirt's location before the party made any decision. The squad arrives within two hours of Mirt's arrival. Three agents: two **Thugs** and one **Mage**. A squad leader — use the **Spy** stat block — hangs back in the street to watch and report.

> [!gamemaster]**The Squad's Approach**
>
> The squad's behavior depends on what the party decided during the negotiation.
>
> **If the party agreed to hand over the Stone:** The Splinter intends to take it in the street before any transfer completes. The assault team breaks cover the moment someone steps outside with the Stone.
>
> **If the party refused:** The Thugs breach the taproom directly while the Mage covers the front from the street. They know the Stone is on the premises.
>
> **The Mage's contingency:** She carries a contingency spell that fires if she takes 30 or more damage in total. If she has the Stone when it triggers, it teleports to Kolat Towers. If she never holds the Stone, the contingency has nothing to attach to.

If the party agreed to transfer the Stone and someone steps outside, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> The street outside Trollskull Alley is quiet, and then it is not. Two figures step out of the shadows across the road and move fast, coming straight for whoever is carrying the Stone.

If the party refused and the Splinter attacks the building directly, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Something heavy hits the taproom door and the latch gives, and two figures push through into the room with blades out, moving with the directness of people who knew what the layout would look like before they came through it.

> [!hazard]**The Splinter Squad**
>
> The Mage holds back at range while the Thugs press into melee to occupy the party.
>
> #### Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the Mage takes a position outside or near a window and opens with ranged spells. The Thugs move into melee and target whoever they believe is carrying the Stone.
>
> Over the course of combat, the Mage will prioritize:
> - Getting physical access to the Stone if an opening appears
> - Staying below 30 total damage taken to avoid triggering her contingency
>
> Over the course of combat, the Thugs will prioritize:
> - Keeping the party engaged in melee and away from the Mage
> - Protecting her flanks if she moves toward the Stone
>
> The battle ends when the Mage and both Thugs are down, surrendered, or have withdrawn to cover the squad leader's escape.

> [!gamemaster]**The Squad Leader**
>
> The squad leader is watching from a position that covers the exits. Any character who scans outward during the fight and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check spots him. He does not enter the building; he reports. If the assault team is neutralized, he runs. A character who breaks away from the combat in time can pursue him on foot.

> [!exploration]**The Squad Leader's Sending Stone**
>
> Any character who runs down the squad leader and searches him finds a polished black stone carried in a small inner pocket, warm to the touch.
>
> > The stone has a faint buzz in the palm, like something waiting for contact. It has been paired with another stone somewhere in the city, or beyond it.
>
> Any character who activates the stone hears a voice on the other end. A character who has heard Manshoon speak before can make a DC 12 Intelligence check; on a success, they recognize the voice immediately. A character who has not heard Manshoon speak hears a composed, measured voice requesting a status report in precise terms.
>
> Mirt, shown the stone, does not explain what he will do with it unless asked. He can push false intelligence toward Manshoon before the Kolat Towers operation, or listen for traffic. He does not take the stone unless the party offers it.

### Resolution

What the three days produce, and how the mission ends, depends on the negotiation's outcome.

> [!gamemaster]**If the Party Complied**
>
> Mirt returns to Trollskull Manor on the third day and hands the Stone back without ceremony. The study produced three things: confirmation of the Stone's abolethic nature (a mind, imprisoned and waiting, not an artifact in the conventional sense); Harper intelligence on a vault safeguard the party had not accounted for; and, if they ask directly whether the study found anything else, a name.

If the party asks what else the study found, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Mirt sets the Stone down on the table between you.
>
> > "It knows it's going to be used. It has been patient. I don't know for what."
>
> He gives it a moment, and then goes on.
>
> > "The study found a name. Someone in your intelligence chain has been influenced from below — not a willing agent, not even aware of it. The influence reached him through extended time near the Yawning Portal's well. It's Jalester Silvermane."
>
> He lets it sit, watching your faces.
>
> > "He does not know. What you do with it is your decision. The Alliance contact chain should be treated as potentially monitored until he is out of the field or the influence is severed. Severing it means finding what is below. That is not a problem you solve during this campaign."

If the party complied but Jalester had minimal campaign contact, replace his name with Renaer Neverember and adjust Mirt's final line: "Everything he has told you about his father's vault plans — about who knew what and when — should be re-examined for what Illuun may have had access to through him."

> [!gamemaster]**If the Party Refused**
>
> Mirt departs after the raid, disappointed but not hostile. The vault tactical advantage is lost; the study never ran. The squad leader's sending stone is still available if he was caught. Before he goes, Mirt picks up his coat and says: "I understand. Keep it safe." He means it, and he goes.

### Concluding the Event

The Splinter raid ends this event. Mirt's visit to Trollskull Manor has been observed and reported regardless of how the negotiation went.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Mirt's Study Complete** — mark when the party gave Mirt the Stone and he returned it three days later. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (vault guardian intel available; vault briefing is fuller).
> - **Jalester Compromise Known** — mark when the party asked about the study's findings and Mirt named Jalester Silvermane. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (Lords' Alliance contact chain treated as potentially monitored during convergence).
> - **Renaer Compromise Known** — mark when Renaer Neverember was named instead of Jalester. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (Renaer's information about Neverember's vault plans treated as potentially observed by Illuun).
> - **Sending Stone Recovered** — mark when the squad leader's sending stone is in the party's possession. Read by **Kolat Towers** (Mirt can feed false intelligence toward Manshoon before the raid; reduces preparation difficulty by one step).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> The Splinter raid confirms that Manshoon knows the Stone is with the party and knows where they live. This shapes **Kolat Towers** — his people have had time to analyze what the squad observed, and preparations for that operation will reflect it.
>
> If **Jalester Compromise Known** was marked: the intelligence Jalester has passed to the Lords' Alliance about the party's plans is suspect. The **Vault of Dragons** overview notes how this affects the convergence scene.
>
> If **Renaer Compromise Known** was marked: everything Renaer told the party about his father's vault plans must be re-examined for what he may have passed to Illuun unknowingly.
>
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Overview

Mirt arrived at Trollskull Manor after midnight and asked for three days with the Stone before the vault opens, citing a psychic resonance Harper seers had detected below the city. Whether the party agrees or refuses, the Manshoon Splinter moves on the building that same night — a Splinter watcher had already reported Mirt's location before any decision was made.

## Summary

Mirt arrived after midnight and asked for three days with the Stone of Golorr, citing a resonance Harper seers had detected below the city. A Manshoon Splinter squad moved on Trollskull Manor within two hours regardless of what the negotiation produced. The squad leader's sending stone was keyed to Manshoon's voice directly. Whether the party complied shaped two things: what tactical intelligence the Harpers brought to the vault operation, and whether the party learned the name of a familiar contact who had been compromised by an abolethic influence below the city.
