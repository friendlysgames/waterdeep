# Design Notes: Brightcandle

## Source Benefits with Concrete Procedure

Appendix B's Harper table grants the potion or low-level scroll and Spy backup, and the campaign guide adds the persona infrastructure. The event keeps one requisition per mission and one operative per quest for each member, and it names the supplier, the phrases and the response times so nothing is left for the GM to prepare.

Agents count as allies when they are present in combat, using the standard 2024 Spy and tier-adjusted Power. Several callers each keep their own allowances, so the party's membership split never reduces or multiplies one character's entitlement.

## Persona and the Salon Gate

The fixed civilian employer, receipts and vouching contacts give the member a playable default straight away. The player chooses the background details and the cover name, and the packet is complete without any further GM decision. Documents support conversation, and they grant neither magical anonymity nor automatic success.

Before **Remallia Harper Contact Known**, Mirt gathers the answers and delivers the mentor's work. Afterward Remallia can ask directly, in her own unhurried way, which keeps the salon as the first disclosure of her faction identity. The finished persona follows the same three-day schedule on either branch.
