# Design Notes: Brightcandle

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B grants a potion or low-level scroll for each mission and a Spy as backup, and the Factions Guide adds a Harper mentor who teaches one persona. The sources name no supplier, no agent and no procedure, so this event supplies them. The potion or scroll comes from one named apothecary with one passphrase. The Spy comes through Mirt's table at the Yawning Portal.

## What Changed

The old draft sent Remi to every Brightcandle meeting, which exposed her before **A Friend's House**. She now appears only when **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked for the member. Otherwise Mirt asks the three questions and says "a friend" writes the papers, so the salon remains her first reveal. The previous version's quarterly phrase table is gone, because one phrase is easier to run. Its shared availability ledger of field names is gone as well. Perrin Valt is the Spy, and a second Spy, Ash, covers the case where he is busy.

Ally Power comes from the mechanics reference: a Spy is 22 at levels 1 to 4 and 17 at levels 5 to 10. The rank is normally reached at 5th level or later, so the Tier 1 figure is a fallback.

## Departures from the Source

Appendix B says "once per arc", and this event says once per quest because arcs are retired. The persona default (a Vale & Reed Imports clerk named Varn, with receipts confirmed by two shopkeepers from **Harpshadow**) is invented, as is the DC 14 flaw check. The event reads **Harpshadow Reached**, **Remallia Harper Contact Known**, **Harper Leak Known** and **Harper Leak Closed**, and writes **Brightcandle Reached** for **Wise Owl**.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Evin Talver, Blue Bottle Apothecary (24 Sorn Street), Perrin Valt ("Reed") and Ash, and the passphrase "The blue bottle wants a fresh label". The cover surname Varn and the firm Vale & Reed Imports are also invented. Nella Fen, Orin Dask and Wil Keen come from **Harpshadow** and **Wise Owl**. Open item: the apothecary's "blue ledger" phrase uses the word ledger, which also names the register in **The Cell Is Compromised**. It should be reviewed if the main session wants the two kept apart. The field name Reed and the firm Vale & Reed share a word by accident.
