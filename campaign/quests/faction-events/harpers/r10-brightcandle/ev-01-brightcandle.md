# Brightcandle

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Harper member first reaches Renown 10, which usually happens just after **A Friend's House**. Mirt names the rank at his manor and, if the member has learned of her, Remallia Haventree joins him. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn where and how to requisition one *Potion of Healing* or one low-level *Spell Scroll* for each mission.
> - Learn how to call Perrin Valt, a Harper Spy, as backup for one operation in each quest.
> - Answer three questions and receive a first persona, with papers and two shopkeepers who vouch for it.
>
> Only the member attends. Companions who are not Harper members are not invited.

### The Noon Bird

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 10, a paper bird finds them wherever they are lodging. Hold this Event until **Harpshadow Reached** is marked for the member.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The paper bird drops onto the table in front of you and unfolds itself. The note inside is in the same heavy, even hand as before.
>
> > "Dinner at my house, eight tonight. Come hungry. M."

Every qualifying member is invited on their own account. Members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the bird at their first surface lodging on return, and the dinner moves to 20:00 on the following evening.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Brightcandle is the rank where Mirt trusts a member with stores and agents that could hurt the Harpers if misused. He has decided to give it, and the Renown only prompted him.
> - Evin Talver and Perrin Valt are Harpers. Evin sells potions and scrolls from a real shop, and Perrin works as a Spy in the city.
> - Remallia Haventree maintains the persona papers. If **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked for the member, she attends and asks the questions. Otherwise Mirt asks them and says only that "a friend" writes the papers. Nobody names her as a Harper.
> - Nella Fen and Orin Dask, the shopkeepers from **Harpshadow**, are the persona's two vouching contacts. They are told only the cover surname.
> - Vale & Reed Imports is a Harper front. Nobody in the firm knows what the Harpers are.
> - If **Harper Leak Known** is marked and **Harper Leak Closed** is not, Mirt takes every operational request in person and keeps the plan out of the relay register. The Spy and the scroll never go through a paper bird.
> - Nobody at this dinner can say Manshoon's name, and Mirt does not swear.

### Naming the Rank

If **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is not marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The dining room of Mirt's manor is lit by a single branch of candles, and the table is set for each of you with a cold pie in the middle. Mirt sits at the head with a jug at his elbow and refills your glass without asking. A folded note lies beside his plate.
>
> > "Sit. Eat something first. You've done good work for us, and the rank for that is Brightcandle. It's yours now. There are three things that come with it."

If **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The dining room of Mirt's manor is lit by a single branch of candles, and a sun elf woman in a grey gown sits at his left hand with a white teapot. She pours a cup for each of you before Mirt has finished refilling the glasses. A folded note lies beside his plate.
>
> > "Sit. Eat something first. You know Remi, so I won't introduce her. The rank is Brightcandle, it's yours, and three things come with it."

> [!social]**The Old Wolf on Business**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a huge, gray-bearded moneylender and senior Harper who holds a second office he does not mention.
>
> Mirt speaks in short, complete sentences and tilts his head when he decides how much to say. He refills a glass whenever one runs low, and he does not joke tonight. The Brightcandle dinner is a loan with terms, and he gives it that way.
>
> Mirt is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The stores, the field agent and the persona, one at a time.
> - Why the rank belongs to the member and not to the party.
> - The Delzorin Street lodging and the contacts from **Harpshadow**, if the member raises them.
>
> Mirt will not discuss his other office, the Stone of Golorr, the Cassalanters or the leader of the other cell.

> [!qna]**Why Brightcandle?**
>
> > "Because your reports have been right every time I checked them. I've read them all, and I'd like you to have better tools for the next ones."

> [!qna]**Do my companions share it?**
>
> > "No. The rank is yours. They can stand beside you on any job, but they can't send for a scroll or a Spy in your name. I'll turn them away."

> [!qna]**What happens if my Renown drops?**
>
> > "You keep the rank and the benefits sleep. When you earn the Renown back, they wake."

> [!qna]**What does it cost?**
>
> > "Nothing in coin. I keep count of what you take, and I expect the work to be worth it. That's a debt, not a gift."

> [!exploration]**Reading Mirt**
>
> A character who watches Mirt and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that he sits where he can watch both doors, and that he counts the servants when they leave. If the check fails, nothing else happens. Mirt believes the Harpers in Waterdeep are compromised and says nothing about it before **The Cell Is Compromised**.

### The Stores

Mirt hands over the folded note. It has the apothecary's address on it and one sentence, the passphrase.

> [!exploration]**One Requisition per Mission**
>
> Each Brightcandle member can requisition one *Potion of Healing* or one *Spell Scroll* of a cantrip or 1st-level spell for each mission. A mission here means a Harper faction mission or a named campaign quest such as **Fireball!**.
>
> - **Contact:** Evin Talver, Blue Bottle Apothecary, 24 Sorn Street, open 09:00 to 18:00.
> - **Passphrase:** the member says "The blue bottle wants a fresh label" at the counter. Evin then takes the request. Said anywhere else, the phrase gets the member nothing.
> - **Notice:** Evin needs 24 hours. The member names the mission and, for a scroll, the spell. The item is ready at the same hour the next day and waits until the mission ends.
> - **Limit:** one item per member per mission. A request nobody collects before the mission ends is lost.
> - **Price:** none. Evin writes the member's name and the mission on a card and keeps the card.
> - **Forgotten phrase:** Evin sells the member ordinary goods at the counter and sends them back to Mirt. Nothing is spent, and Mirt repeats the phrase at his table the same evening.
> - **Companions:** a companion who is not a Harper member gets nothing, even carrying the member's note.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and the stores are suspended until the Renown is restored.

When the member first goes to the shop, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Blue Bottle Apothecary is crowded with stoppered jars, and bunches of dried herbs hang from the rafters over the counter. The air smells of camphor and mint, and an apprentice sweeps the floor by the door while a customer waits for change. The apothecary lifts a hand to you without hurrying.
>
> > "Good morning. What can I find you today? If it's a scroll, tell me the spell, and I'll want a full day to prepare it."

> [!social]**The Apothecary**
>
> Evin Talver (Lawful Good, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a thin, careful apothecary in his fifties, with stained fingers and a pair of spectacles he keeps pushing up his nose.
>
> Evin is friendly with customers and exact with Harpers. He asks the member's name once, writes it on a card and does not ask it again. He keeps the Harper stock in a locked drawer behind the counter.
>
> Evin is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The item, the spell and the collection day.
> - Remedies for ordinary ailments, at length, if the member lets him.
>
> Evin will not discuss other Harpers, Mirt's other business or what the member plans to do with the item.

> [!qna]**Can I change the spell after I order?**
>
> > "Not once I've started. A scroll is written for one spell, and I can't unwrite it. Come back with a new request and a day's notice, and the first one is wasted."

> [!qna]**What if someone follows me here?**
>
> > "Then buy a headache powder and go home. I sell a great many, and nobody finds that odd. I will not send you anything by bird, so come yourself."

### The Field Agent

Mirt explains the second benefit while the pie is cut.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt takes a stub of pencil from his coat and writes a second line under the apothecary's address. He turns the note toward you and taps the line twice.
>
> > "One agent, once in each quest, for one job. Tell me the place and the hour. Bring me the request here at the Portal, never by bird. He's a good Spy, but he has not been living inside your head."

> [!exploration]**Calling Perrin Valt**
>
> Each Brightcandle member can call one Harper Spy for one operation in each quest.
>
> - **Contact:** Mirt, at his corner table in the Yawning Portal any evening from 18:00 to 22:00.
> - **Request:** the member names the quest, the operation and a surface place to meet. If the operation is below the city, the place is the Yawning Portal.
> - **Arrival:** Perrin Valt, field name Reed, waits at the place exactly 24 hours after the request. He works the one operation and leaves when it is finished or the party withdraws.
> - **Limit:** one call per member per quest. A call withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent. A call nobody makes before the quest ends is lost.
> - **If Perrin is busy:** Mirt sends a second Spy, field name Ash, with the same statistics. Two members who ask for the same operation share one Spy, and only the first to ask spends a call.
> - **Companions:** the Spy takes orders from the member who called him, and from nobody else.

> [!gamemaster]**Perrin Valt as an Ally**
>
> Perrin uses the **Spy** stat block. He counts as an ally only when he joins a fight, and an ally who is still on the way does not lower the difficulty. In CR 2.0 terms, a Spy is Power 22 for a party of levels 1 to 4 and Power 17 for levels 5 to 10. Add that to the party's Power and recalculate the encounter.
>
> #### Perrin's Tactics
>
> To begin combat, Perrin finds cover and fires his hand crossbow at whoever threatens the member who called him. During combat, he:
>
> - Targets the enemy the member is attacking, then the nearest enemy that has not yet acted.
> - Uses Cunning Action to hide between shots, and to disengage if he drops below 10 Hit Points.
> - Refuses to attack anyone who has surrendered, anyone who is helpless or any bystander.
>
> He withdraws if the member calls a retreat or if he is the last of the group still standing. He leaves with the party and does not stay to fight alone.

When Perrin meets the member for the first operation, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Perrin Valt is waiting at the place you named, a plain travel bag over one shoulder, and he watches the street on both sides while you cross to him. He is a lean man in a brown coat that has seen many winters. He nods once when he recognises you.
>
> > "Reed. Mirt gave me the job and the place. Tell me how you're going in and where you want me. I'll tell you if I see a problem."

> [!social]**The Field Agent**
>
> Perrin Valt (Neutral Good, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a lean Harper Spy in his forties, known in the field as Reed, who works alone by preference and works for the member for as long as the job lasts.
>
> Perrin is polite and watchful. He wants the plan, the exits and the names of the people the member does not want hurt. He does not swear, and he does not give his real name to anyone he meets on a job.
>
> Perrin is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The job: the place, the hours, who is there and how to leave.
> - What he has seen in the ward over the past month.
>
> Perrin will not discuss other Harpers, Mirt, or what he does when he is not on a job.

> [!qna]**Will you stay with us after?**
>
> > "For this job, yes. When it's done, I report to Mirt. Ask him again for the next quest, because I can't be your standing guard."

> [!qna]**What if the plan puts an innocent in the way?**
>
> > "Then I stop and say so. I'll still help you change the plan, but I won't help you shoot at someone who is only standing there."

### The First Persona

The third benefit is a persona, a civilian identity with papers and people who can confirm it.

If **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia sets down the teapot and lays a blank employment letter on the cloth beside two small receipts. She waits until you have looked at the spaces left for a hometown and an earlier trade.
>
> > "Where did you grow up, and what work did you do before Waterdeep? And what trade could you discuss with a stranger tomorrow without preparing? Tell me what is yours to keep. I would rather give you a history you can use than one you must memorise."

If it is not marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt lays a blank employment letter on the cloth beside two small receipts and leaves room for a hometown and an earlier trade. He takes up his pencil to write down what you say.
>
> > "Where did you grow up? What did you do before Waterdeep? What trade could you talk about with a stranger tomorrow? I'll pass your answers to a friend who writes the papers. They come back through me in three days."

> [!exploration]**The Default Cover**
>
> The member answers three questions: where they grew up, what they did before Waterdeep and what trade they could pass for. They keep as much of the truth as they like.
>
> - **Cover:** a traveling order clerk for Vale & Reed Imports. The member keeps their given name or picks another, and the cover surname is Varn unless the player names a different one.
> - **Papers:** an employer's letter dated three months earlier, an order ledger and two receipts. One is for a candle order delivered to Nella Fen last month, and one is for a map order delivered to Orin Dask two weeks ago.
> - **Vouching:** Nella Fen at 16 Fillet Lane and Orin Dask at 9 Street of Silks confirm the delivery on their own receipt if the member or anyone else asks in person. They know nothing else about the persona.
> - **Another trade:** if the member chooses a different trade, the employer's role changes to match. The two deliveries stay.
> - **Arrival:** a shop runner brings the packet to the member's lodging at noon on the third day after the dinner.
> - **What it does:** an official or guard with a reason to doubt the papers needs a DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check to find a flaw. A clerk with no reason to doubt them accepts them.
> - **What it cannot do:** it fails a records search outside the two deliveries and any magical check. It gives no disguise, no immunity and no automatic success on a Charisma check. Outside Waterdeep, the papers pass casual scrutiny, but Nella and Orin cannot confirm anything.
> - **Limit:** one persona. A member who loses it gets no replacement from this rank.

> [!qna]**What name will be on the papers?**
>
> > "The first name you choose and Varn after it, unless you'd like another. Vale and Reed Imports will call you a traveling order clerk. The letter is three months old, and the two deliveries are real."

> [!qna]**Who vouches for me?**
>
> > "A chandler on Fillet Lane and a map-seller on the Street of Silks. Both know you only as Varn. Go and buy a candle from one of them before you rely on it."

If the member visits Nella Fen after the packet arrives, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nella Fen looks up from her counter, and the beeswax on her fingers shines in the lamplight. She does not need to check the ledger before she answers.
>
> > "Varn's candle order came through last month. I've the receipt here if you'd like to see the date. Where the clerk went afterwards, I couldn't tell you."

If the member visits Orin Dask, he gives the same answer about a map order two weeks ago, rolling his stock closed before he speaks.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Brightcandle member earns further Renown from missions and from the Earning Renown list in the Harpers page of the Factions Guide.

### Aftermath

The member can tell their companions what the stores, the Spy and the persona can do. The benefits stay individual, and Evin, Perrin and Mirt turn away a companion who asks in the member's name.

If **Harper Leak Known** is marked and **Harper Leak Closed** is not, Mirt reminds the member that requests go to him in person. He asks that they name the quest and the place and keep the plan to themselves.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member holds the apothecary's note and has answered the persona's three questions.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Brightcandle Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Mirt hands over the note and names the rank; read by **Wise Owl**, which checks it before naming the next rank. Track separately for each member the mission each item was collected for, the quest each Spy call was made in and the surname on the persona.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three benefits are active for this member from the next morning, and the persona arrives at noon on the third day. **Wise Owl** occurs when this member reaches Renown 25. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Mirt names a Harper member a Brightcandle and gives them a potion or scroll for each mission, a Spy for one operation in each quest and a first persona with papers and vouching shopkeepers.

## Summary

We were named Brightcandles over dinner at Mirt's manor, and he gave each of us the address of an apothecary on Sorn Street and a phrase to say at the counter. A Spy named Reed will come to one job in each quest if we ask Mirt at the Portal. We answered three questions about ourselves, and three days later a packet arrived with a traveling clerk's papers.
