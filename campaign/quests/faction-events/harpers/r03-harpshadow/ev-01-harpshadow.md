# Harpshadow

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — next visit to Mirt, or Mirt seeks the character out within a tenday — after one party member's Harper renown reaches 3. In this Event:
>
> - Mirt names the character's specific recent work and what it demonstrated to him
> - He names the Harpshadow rank: a readier ear and an extended network
> - He provides a folded paper with cover names and meeting protocols for three ward contacts: a chandler in the Dock Ward, a map-seller in the Trades Ward, and a barber in the Castle Ward
> - Each contact can pull current street-level intelligence on one faction per tenday; all three refresh after ten days
> - He answers one question about faction activity or Waterdeep politics as the first use of the new privilege

**Background (DM only)**

Mirt tracks renown by observation and by reports from Remi's network. He does not announce thresholds; he acts on them. The Harpshadow promotion is deliberate — he waited until he was confident the character would use the network carefully. The three contacts operate under civilian cover and know nothing about each other. They do not know who sent the party to them, and they will not confirm their Harper affiliation to anyone who approaches without the correct opening phrase. Mirt provides the phrase.

The folded paper uses a simple substitution cipher the contacts can decode on request. It is standard Harper field protocol, not itself secret — it limits casual interception and establishes that the bearer knows how the network communicates.

### The Meeting

Mirt is somewhere inconvenient when this scene fires. A corner table in a Castle Ward coffee house, a balcony above a goods yard in the Dock Ward, or — on rare occasions — his manor receiving room when he actually is home. Wherever it is, he already has something to eat.

He gets to it without preamble.

> *"You've been useful. The kind of useful that earns more trust."*

He places a folded paper on the table.

> *"Three contacts. Chandler on Fillet Lane, map-seller off the Street of Silks, barber behind the Castle Ward Watch station. Any of the three can pull current intelligence on a faction you're watching — known agents, movement patterns, what's changed in the last tenday. One request each. They refresh in ten days."*

He does not name them — just location and cover. He explains the deniability: the contacts know nothing about each other, they do not know who sent the party, and they will not confirm their Harper affiliation to anyone who approaches without the correct opening phrase. He writes the phrase on the folded paper below the contact locations.

> *"The network works because the pieces don't know about the other pieces. Don't give them reasons to change that."*

He answers one question about faction activity or Waterdeep politics as the first demonstration of the new channel — directly, without comment, and without editorializing what it implies. He waits to see what the character does with the answer.

> [!profile]**Running Mirt at Harpshadow**
>
> The promotion is acknowledged and immediately moved past. Mirt does not make ceremonies. If a character seems tempted to use the contacts for personal matters rather than faction intelligence, his response is a short silence and then a change of subject. The contacts are for actionable intelligence. He trusts the character to understand the distinction.

## Overview

Mirt finds a moment to acknowledge what you have been doing right. He names it plainly, hands over a folded paper with three contact locations and an opening phrase, and explains what the Harpshadow network gives you: one request per contact per tenday, each contact pulling current intelligence on whichever faction you name. He answers a question himself, as the first use of the channel, and moves on.

## Summary

Mirt named the character a Harpshadow and handed over cover protocols for three ward contacts — a chandler in the Dock Ward, a map-seller in the Trades Ward, and a barber in the Castle Ward. Each can pull current intelligence on one faction per tenday. He demonstrated the channel by answering one question directly and then changed the subject.
