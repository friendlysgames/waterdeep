# Harpshadow

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — the next visit to Mirt, or Mirt seeks the character out within a tenday — after one party member's Harper renown reaches 3. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Mirt name the character's recent work and what it demonstrated to him
> - Receive a folded paper with cover names and meeting protocols for three ward contacts: a chandler in the Dock Ward, a map-seller in the Trades Ward, and a barber in the Castle Ward
> - Request current intelligence on one faction per contact per tenday, with a ten-day refresh cycle
> - Ask Mirt one direct question about faction activity or Waterdeep politics as the first demonstration of the new channel
>
> #### Background
>
> Mirt tracks renown by observation and by reports from Remi's network. He does not announce thresholds; he acts on them. The Harpshadow promotion is deliberate — he waited until he was confident the character would use the network carefully. The three contacts operate under civilian cover and know nothing about one another. They will not confirm their Harper affiliation to anyone who approaches without the correct opening phrase, which Mirt writes on the folded paper.
>
> The paper uses a simple substitution cipher the contacts can decode on request. It is standard Harper field protocol, not itself secret — it limits casual interception and establishes that the bearer knows how the network communicates.

### The Meeting

Mirt is somewhere inconvenient when this scene fires: a corner table in a Castle Ward coffee house, a balcony above a goods yard in the Dock Ward, or his manor's receiving room on the rare occasion he actually is home. Wherever it is, he already has something to eat.

> [!readaloud]
> Mirt is at the table when you arrive, working through a plate of food with the settled ease of someone who has nowhere pressing to be. He nods toward the seat across from him and sets the food aside.
>
> > "You've been useful. The kind of useful that earns more trust."
>
> He places a folded paper on the table.

> [!social]**The Old Wolf**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan human, he/him) :: a moneylender and Harper operative; in business mode, which means short sentences and no performance.
>
> Mirt acknowledges the promotion and moves past it immediately. He does not make ceremonies of these meetings; the recognition and the briefing are the same thing. He explains the contact network's deniability plainly — the contacts know nothing about one another, do not know who sent the party to them, and will not confirm Harper affiliation without the correct opening phrase — because the party needs to understand how the network works in order to use it correctly.
>
> He answers one question about faction activity or Waterdeep politics at the end of the meeting, directly, without editorializing what the answer implies. He waits to see what the character does with it.
>
> If a character seems inclined to use the contacts for personal matters rather than faction intelligence, Mirt goes quiet for a moment and then changes the subject. The contacts are for actionable intelligence. He trusts the character to understand the distinction.
>
> Conversation topics Mirt is willing to discuss include:
> - Where to find the three contacts and what cover they operate under
> - How to approach each contact and what the opening phrase is
> - What kinds of requests the contacts can and cannot answer
> - How the deniability structure of the network works
> - One direct question about faction activity or Waterdeep politics
>
> Specific dialogue for Mirt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Who are the contacts?**
>
> > "Chandler on Fillet Lane, map-seller off the Street of Silks, barber behind the Castle Ward Watch station. Location and cover only — no names. The paper tells you how to approach them, and the opening phrase is at the bottom."

> [!qna]**What can they tell us?**
>
> > "Current intelligence on a faction you're watching. Known agents, movement patterns, what's changed in the last tenday. One request each, one faction at a time. They reset in ten days." He picks up the last of his food. "Ask for analysis and you'll get a polite explanation of why that's not their job."

> [!qna]**Why don't they know about each other?**
>
> > "Because if one of them is ever compromised, the others are not. The network works because the pieces don't know about the other pieces. Don't give them reasons to change that."

### Concluding the Event

Mirt finishes what he was eating, waits for the character to pocket the folded paper, and wraps up the meeting without ceremony. The question the character asked has been answered; that was always the plan.

## Overview

Mirt finds a moment to acknowledge what the character has been doing right. He names it plainly, hands over a folded paper with three contact locations and an opening phrase, and explains what the Harpshadow network provides: one request per contact per tenday, each contact pulling current intelligence on whichever faction the character names. He answers one question himself as the first use of the channel, and moves on.

## Summary

Mirt named the character a Harpshadow and handed over cover protocols for three ward contacts — a chandler in the Dock Ward, a map-seller in the Trades Ward, and a barber in the Castle Ward. Each can pull current intelligence on one faction per tenday, with a ten-day refresh. He demonstrated the channel by answering one question directly and then wrapped up the meeting.
