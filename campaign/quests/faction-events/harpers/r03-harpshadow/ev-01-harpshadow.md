# Harpshadow

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Harper member first reaches Renown 3, which usually happens right after **The Talking Mare**. Mirt names the rank over dinner at his manor and gives that member the three Harpshadow benefits. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Ask Mirt the first question of his direct channel and get an answer built only from confirmed facts.
> - Learn the names, addresses and opening phrase of three ward contacts.
> - Learn how and when to ask the contacts questions, and how long each one takes to recover.
> - Learn that the three contacts sell goods to Harpers at a tenth off.
>
> Only the member attends. Companions who are not Harper members are not invited.

### The Noon Bird

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 3, a paper bird finds them wherever they are lodging. The note is in Mirt's hand.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The paper bird lands on the sill and folds itself flat in your hand. The note inside is short and written in a heavy, even script.
>
> > "Dinner at my house, eight tonight, Sea Ward, the street behind the Naval Harbor. Come hungry. M."

Every qualifying member is invited on their own account. Several members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the bird at their first surface lodging on return, and the dinner moves to 20:00 on the following evening. The note changes nothing else.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Mirt is home tonight because he chose to be. He is almost never home otherwise.
> - He has read the member's reports and decided they will use a wider channel carefully.
> - The three contacts are real Harper assets with civilian shops. They do not know each other and never receive each other's names.
> - If **Shesstra Street Reported** or **Handler Ledger Read** is marked for the member, Mirt has read what they brought. He knows nothing more than the report says.
> - Mirt does not know who leads the other branch of the Black Network. He says "the Splinter" and "the other cell" and never a name. Nobody at this dinner can say Manshoon's name, even if **Manshoon Named** is marked for someone else.
> - Remallia Haventree plays no part. Mirt does not mention her, and nobody at the table names her as a Harper.
> - The dinner is business. Mirt does not swear, does not tell stories and does not laugh.

### Naming the Rank

> [!readaloud]
>
> The dining room of Mirt's manor smells of roast duck and black pepper, and a single servant lays one place for each of you before leaving by the kitchen door. Mirt sits at the head of the table with a heavy jug at his elbow and refills your glass without asking. A folded paper lies beside his plate.
>
> > "Sit. Eat something first. I've read your reports, and the rank we give people who write them well is Harpshadow. It's yours now."

He lets the member eat before he says anything else. When the plates are cleared, he slides the folded paper across the table.

> [!social]**The Old Wolf on Business**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a huge, gray-bearded moneylender who is a Harper and holds a second office he does not mention.
>
> Mirt is level and direct tonight. He tilts his head while he thinks and refills a glass whenever one runs low. He speaks in short, complete sentences, and the absence of his usual bawdy talk is deliberate. He wants the member to understand that the rank is a working tool.
>
> Mirt is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What Harpshadow gives the member, one benefit at a time.
> - The three ward contacts and how to approach each one.
> - How to use his own channel.
> - The pin and the lodging at Delzorin Street, if the member brings them up.
>
> Mirt will not discuss his other office, the Stone of Golorr, the Cassalanters or the vault. He has nothing to say about the leader of the other cell beyond what he already knows.

> [!qna]**What does Harpshadow give me?**
>
> Mirt counts three fingers on the tablecloth.
>
> > "You can put one question to me every ten days and questions to three people in three wards, and at those three shops you pay a tenth less. I'll go through each one."

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not the whole party's?**
>
> > "I write ranks by name, so your companions can help you, and they should, but the contacts answer to the paper in your hand and a companion can't spend it."

> [!qna]**Who are the contacts?**
>
> > "Read the paper, three shops in three wards. They don't know each other, and I'd like to keep it that way, so don't carry the paper where anyone can read it."

> [!qna]**What if I lose my Renown?**
>
> > "Then the rank stays and the benefits sleep until you earn it back. That's fair."

> [!exploration]**Reading Mirt**
>
> A character who watches Mirt during the dinner and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that he is checking the windows and the kitchen door more often than a man in his own house should. If the check fails, nothing else happens here. The reason is his private worry that the Harpers in Waterdeep have been compromised. He tells nobody until **The Cell Is Compromised**.

### Mirt's Question

The first benefit is a direct question to Mirt every ten days. The member asks the first one tonight, over the cleared table.

> [!exploration]**Using Mirt's Channel**
>
> - **How to ask:** the member writes the question on a single sheet and leaves it with the keeper of the Delzorin Street lodging, or hands it to Mirt in person if they find him.
> - **Notice:** Mirt answers by sealed note delivered to the member's lodging at noon the next day. If the member is away from Waterdeep, the note waits at their first surface lodging on return.
> - **Limit:** one question per member every ten days. The count starts on the day the member asks, and tonight's question counts as the first.
> - **Delay:** the note comes at noon the next day and cannot be hurried.
> - **Content:** Mirt answers from what is confirmed. If he has nothing confirmed, he says so, and that question is not spent.
> - **Later questions:** if the member asks how worried a faction is about the party, Mirt gives the faction's current escalation tier in his own words, from the **Escalation** section of its Factions Guide page. He covers Xanathar's Guild, the Splinter and the Cassalanters. If the member names any other faction, he says the Harpers have little on them and the question is not spent.
> - **Companions:** a companion who is not a Harper member cannot send a question, and Mirt does not answer one.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the channel and contacts are suspended until the Renown is restored.

Ask what the member wants to know, then use the matching answer below. Mirt answers in the dinner conversation, and the answer counts as the first use of his channel.

> [!qna]**What do you know about the house on Shesstra Street?**
>
> If **Shesstra Street Reported** is marked for the member, use this answer. Otherwise use the answer to the unestablished question.
>
> > "You told me about a three-floor house on Shesstra Street with a red lantern upstairs, and that gave me a place to watch. What I still can't prove is who gives the orders inside."

> [!qna]**What did the handler's ledger show?**
>
> If **Handler Ledger Read** is marked for the member, use this answer. Otherwise use the answer to the unestablished question.
>
> > "The marked pages record a payment to Tessalar and two more contacts at an address on Brindul Alley. Nothing in it says who gave the orders. A ledger like that only shows who got paid."

> [!qna]**Who leads the other branch of the Black Network?**
>
> > "The Black Network has split, that much I know, but I've no proof of who runs the other branch. If you find a name attached to orders, bring me the evidence, because I won't send you chasing a guess."

> [!qna]**Something you haven't established**
>
> Use this for any question Mirt cannot answer from confirmed facts.
>
> > "I haven't established that, so I'll keep the question with my reports and tell you if anyone brings me proof. This one isn't spent, and you can ask another."

If the member asks a second question at the table, Mirt answers once with the unestablished answer and says the channel opens again in ten days.

### The Ward Contacts

The folded paper lists three contacts and the opening phrase. Mirt reads the phrase aloud once and has the member say it back to him.

> [!exploration]**The Three Ward Contacts**
>
> - **Opening phrase:** the member says, "May I leave a receipt for the next delivery?" at the counter. The contact then leads them to the rear workroom. Said anywhere else, the phrase gets the member nothing.
> - **Hours:** 09:00 to 17:00. A shop that is shut has a shuttered window, and the contact does not open it.
> - **One question per contact per member every ten days.** The count runs from the day that member asks that contact. One contact's use does not touch another's, and a companion's question never spends the member's.
> - **Reply:** the contact writes the answer, and a shop runner delivers it sealed to the member's lodging at noon the next day. A member who is away finds it waiting.
> - **Content:** each contact answers with one fact they could see themselves, taken from the **Outposts** and **Response Teams** entries in the Factions Guide for their ward. Anything beyond their ward gets "I can't see that far," and the question is not spent.
> - **Contacts:** a member can approach any of them first and return to the others later.
>
> **Nella Fen**, chandler, 16 Fillet Lane, Dock Ward. She watches carts, loads and the waterfront.
>
> **Orin Dask**, map-seller, 9 Street of Silks, Trades Ward. He watches premises, cargo routes and changes in who rents what.
>
> **Bram Pell**, barber, 7 Swords Street, Castle Ward. He hears about appointments, visitors and Watch patrols.

> [!social]**Nella Fen, Chandler**
>
> Nella Fen (Neutral Good, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: a stout chandler in her fifties, with wax under her nails, who sells candles and keeps her ears open.
>
> Nella is friendly and brisk. She leads the member into the workroom behind the counter, where the air is thick with beeswax. She writes everything down and burns the draft. She does not ask the member's name.
>
> Nella is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Which carts came to the waterfront this week, and whose.
> - What the dockhands say about the Guild.
> - Candle prices, at length, if the member lets her.
>
> Nella will not discuss the other two contacts, Mirt or the Harpers by name.

> [!qna]**Who else do you work for?**
>
> > "I sell candles to half of Fillet Lane, and I answer questions for one customer. Leave a receipt, tell me the load, and I'll send word tomorrow."

> [!qna]**Can you find out what is inside the warehouse?**
>
> > "I can tell you who goes in and what rolls out. What happens behind a shut door isn't something I can see, and I won't guess."

> [!social]**Orin Dask, Map-Seller**
>
> Orin Dask (Lawful Neutral, Chultan Human, he/him) :: a thin, careful map-seller who rolls every map closed before he speaks.
>
> Orin is polite and a little dry. He makes room behind the counter, between racks of paper tubes, and keeps his voice low. He answers exactly what was asked and nothing more.
>
> Orin is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who rents which building on the Trades Ward streets he sells to.
> - Cargo routes between the warehouses and the harbor.
> - Maps, and which ones are wrong.
>
> Orin will not discuss the other two contacts, Mirt or anything he did not see himself.

> [!qna]**What do you want in return?**
>
> > "Nothing, since a receipt is a receipt and the paper you carry has already paid for it. Ask me about a building or a route and I'll tell you what I know."

> [!qna]**Can you tell me what the neighbors think?**
>
> > "I can tell you what changed on the street, such as who is hiring, who stopped and who has new locks. The neighbors are your business."

> [!social]**Bram Pell, Barber**
>
> Bram Pell (Neutral, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a talkative barber with a good razor who wipes the same chair twice while he listens.
>
> Bram is cheerful and chatty. He walks the member past the waiting customers to the back of the shop, talking about the weather the whole way. He writes down what he saw and marks the rest as talk.
>
> Bram is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who has been in for a shave, and who was in a hurry.
> - Which Watch patrols have changed their hours.
> - Weather, taxes and the price of oil.
>
> Bram will not discuss the other two contacts, Mirt or anything about his own accounts.

> [!qna]**How do I know the answer is true?**
>
> > "I'll write down what I saw and mark what I only heard. The marks are the useful part, so read them."

> [!qna]**Would you ever sell me out?**
>
> > "Customers ask me that over a razor, so I've had time to think. No. But I'd tell Mirt first if I thought you'd been followed."

If the member forgets the phrase, the contact keeps the talk ordinary and sends them back to Mirt:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The chandler takes your order for a pound of tallow and wraps it in paper. She does not look up, and she does not lead you toward the back.
>
> > "That's all I have for you today. If you've another matter, bring Mirt's written receipt when you come back."

Nothing is spent. The member leaves a note with the lodging keeper, and Mirt's written receipt reaches them at 09:00 the next morning.

### The Faction Rate

The third benefit is a discount at the same three shops.

> [!exploration]**The Faction Rate**
>
> - **Where:** at Nella Fen's, Orin Dask's and Bram Pell's shops, and nowhere else. No other shop in Waterdeep counts as a Harper supplier.
> - **How:** the member says the opening phrase, then buys. The contact charges nine-tenths of the published 2024 price, rounded up to the next copper piece.
> - **What it covers:** mundane goods the shop actually sells and that have a published price. Magic items, unpriced work and secret documents are excluded.
> - **Companions:** a member who buys for a companion pays the same discount. The companion gets no separate discount and cannot buy at it.

> [!qna]**Why is it cheaper for me?**
>
> > "The Harpers look after their own, and the shops know whom to look after. Pay at the counter and don't brag about it."

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Harper member earns further Renown from missions and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Harpers page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions what the contacts said and what Mirt answered. The channel, the contacts and the discount stay individual benefits, and Mirt, Nella, Orin and Bram will not serve a companion who asks in the member's name.

If **Harper Leak Known** is marked for the member and **Harper Leak Closed** is not, Mirt adds one instruction before the member leaves. Contact questions are to name a place or a load, never the party's plans.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has the paper, has asked their first question and knows how to reach all three contacts.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Harpshadow Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Mirt hands over the paper and names the rank; read by **Brightcandle**, which checks it before naming the next rank. Track separately for each member the date of their last question to Mirt and to each contact.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three benefits are active for this member from the next morning, and Mirt's next answer opens ten days after tonight. **Brightcandle** occurs when this member reaches Renown 10. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Mirt names a Harper member a Harpshadow at dinner and gives them three ward contacts, a direct question every tenday and a discount at the contacts' shops.

## Summary

We were named Harpshadows over dinner at Mirt's manor, and he gave each of us a paper with three ward contacts and an opening phrase. We asked him one question that night, and the contacts will answer one question each every ten days.
