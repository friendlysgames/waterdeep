# Harpshadow

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Harper member first reaches Renown 3. Mirt names the rank and hands that character the contact protocols, and each recipient's use is tracked separately. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Ask the first direct question through Mirt's new intelligence channel.
> - Learn the locations and cover names of three ward contacts.
> - Confirm request and refresh limits without exposing the contacts to one another.

### Hook

At noon on the day after the member reaches the threshold, Mirt sends a paper bird inviting them to his manor at 20:00. A member who is away from Waterdeep receives it at their first safe surface lodging after returning, and the appointment moves to 20:00 the following evening. The meeting is private and only qualifying Harper members attend, because promotions and benefits don't enroll the companions who help them.

### Background

Mirt has followed the member's reports and trusts them now with a wider request channel. He passes along recommendations from the network without naming Remallia before **A Friend's House**. The civilian contacts know only their own channel and never receive a list of the other contacts, which keeps them deniable without asking the member to invent a cover procedure.

### The Folded Paper

> [!readaloud]
>
> The dining room of Mirt's manor smells of roast duck and black pepper, and the old moneylender tops up your glass from a heavy jug before he draws a folded paper out of his sleeve. He opens it on the tablecloth to show three addresses in a careful hand, then turns it toward you so you can read the short phrase written beneath the last one.
>
> > "Fuck me, that was good work, and I'm not the only one who's noticed. You've earned more access. We call the rank Harpshadow, and it lets you put questions through people who know their wards. Ask them only what they can see from their own counters, and keep the other names off them."

> [!social]**Mirt's Practical Promotion**
>
> Mirt is warm about the promotion and turns level and exact once he reaches the contacts. He uses the questions below to find out what the member needs, and he offers more food when the instructions are clear.

> [!qna]**Who should I ask?**
>
> > "Three people. Nella Fen keeps a chandler's shop at sixteen Fillet Lane and watches the carts and the waterfront. Orin Dask sells maps at nine Street of Silks and knows premises and cargo routes. Bram Pell cuts hair at three Shield Street and hears about appointments and Watch routines. Each of them knows their own ward, and none of them needs the others' names."

> [!qna]**What are you watching now?**
>
> > "Tell me which faction is worrying you and what you need to learn. Then give a contact one question they can answer from where they work. If none of the three wards touches it, bring the question back to me."

> [!qna]**How often can I ask?**
>
> > "One question to each contact every ten days, counted from the day you use that contact. My own answers run on a separate ten-day count, and another member's question never spends yours."

### The Ward Contacts

> [!exploration]**The Three Civilian Covers**
>
> The paper lists three contacts, available 09:00–17:00. At the counter the member asks, "May I leave a receipt for the next delivery?" and the contact takes them into the rear workroom before discussing intelligence. A member who has forgotten the phrase follows the receipt exchange below: Mirt provides a written receipt at 09:00 the next morning, and the channel isn't treated as burned.
>
> - **Nella Fen**, chandler at 16 Fillet Lane, Dock Ward: known agents and movements around cart yards and the waterfront.
> - **Orin Dask**, map-seller at 9 Street of Silks, Trades Ward: changes in premises, cargo routes and commercial cover identities.
> - **Bram Pell**, barber at 3 Shield Street, Castle Ward: public political appointments, Watch routines and faction visitors seen locally.
>
> The member can approach any of them first and come back to the others later, since each contact runs on a separate ten-day schedule.

> [!social]**Nella Fen, Chandler**
>
> Nella (she/her) sets her order slate on the counter and leads the member into the rear workroom, where the air is thick with beeswax and tallow.
>
> > "Leave the receipt with me, and tell me what you're after. If it's the carts or the waterfront, name the person, the time or the load you want checked, and I'll send back whatever I can verify tomorrow at noon."

> [!social]**Orin Dask, Map-Seller**
>
> Orin (he/him) rolls a street map closed and makes room for the member behind the counter, between the racks of paper tubes.
>
> > "Show me the premises or the route you mean, and I'll compare it with what's changed on the street lately. What I can't do is tell you what goes on behind a door I've never been through, so please don't ask me to guess."

> [!social]**Bram Pell, Barber**
>
> Bram (he/him) finishes wiping down his chair and brings the member past the waiting customers to the back of the shop.
>
> > "Ask me about someone whose appointments or Watch visits I might really see from this chair. I'll write down what I witnessed and mark the rest as talk, so you can tell which is which."

Each contact answers one specific faction-intelligence question per recipient every ten days, drawing on the established campaign record of that ward. The sealed written reply reaches the member's lodging at noon the next day and separates what the contact saw from what is rumor. If the member is out, the reply stays sealed at the lodging until they return; it isn't lost or rerouted through another contact. A question beyond the contact's access gets an honest answer that they can't see that far. Each ten-day refresh counts from that recipient's own use, and one contact's use never touches another's.

If the member asks about the faction rate at any of the three counters, the contact gives the ordinary sales answer:

> [!qna]**What is the faction rate?**
>
> > "For ordinary goods we stock, you pay nine-tenths of the listed price, and I round the whole purchase up to the next copper piece. Magic, private records and work with no listed price aren't covered, I'm afraid."

At these three suppliers, the member pays 90% of the published 2024 price for ordinary mundane stock, and the final purchase total rounds up to the nearest CP. The discount covers only stock that has a published price and that the supplier really sells. Magic items, unpriced grooming services and commissioned secret documents are excluded, and no other shop in Waterdeep counts as a Harper supplier. This is the source's faction-rate benefit made concrete.

If the member has forgotten the opening phrase, the contact keeps the conversation ordinary and sends them back to Mirt:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Bring Mirt's written receipt when you come back. I'm happy to take an ordinary order today, but we'll talk about the other matter once I've seen it."

At 09:00 the next morning, Mirt gives the member the receipt:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Take this back to the same counter. You haven't lost the contact and you haven't spent a question, so keep it with the paper until you remember the phrase."

### The First Question

During the meeting Mirt answers one direct question about faction activity or Waterdeep politics. He uses confirmed current campaign facts and says so when the network hasn't established the answer. That question counts as this member's first use of his own one-question-per-ten-days channel, and the three ward contacts stay unused.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt repeats your question back to you, then leafs through the reports stacked at his elbow until he finds what has been confirmed. He writes the date beside the answer and slides the folded contact paper back across the table to you.
>
> > "I'll give you what's confirmed and tell you which part is still rumor. If another report changes the answer, I'll tell you what changed. Keep the two kinds of information apart in your head."

If **Shesstra Street Reported** is marked and the member asks what their earlier inquiry established, Mirt answers from the party's report:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "You told me about a three-floor house on Shesstra Street with a red lantern upstairs, and that gave me a place to watch. What we still need is proof of who directed the work inside."

If **Handler Ledger Read** is marked, the members brought the marked pages or a copy to Mirt, and this member asks what the book established, use his answer:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "The marked pages record Tessalar's payment and two more contacts, and the names and the address are on the copy you brought me. Nothing in it says who gave them their orders."

If the member asks who leads the hostile Black Network branch before that identity has been verified and shared, Mirt answers plainly and doesn't guess:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "The Black Network has split, that much I know. I've no proof of who leads the other branch. If you find a name attached to orders, bring me the evidence, because I won't send you chasing a guess."

If the requested fact is beyond all confirmed reports, use this answer for that question:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I haven't established that. I'll keep the question with the reports and tell you if someone brings me evidence. That's my answer for today, and I know it isn't the one you wanted."

### Renown Opportunities

The promotion grants no extra Renown, gold or Milestone Points. It recognizes this character's existing Renown and opens the listed member benefits.

### Aftermath

The member can share what they learn with companions, but the contacts' recognition, requests and discount stay individual benefits. If **Harper Leak Known** is marked without **Harper Leak Closed**, Mirt remains the route for sensitive reports, because the contact network can't resolve that compromise itself.

### Concluding the Event

The promotion concludes when the member has the paper and understands the request procedure.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Harpshadow Reached** — mark with this recipient's name when Mirt delivers the protocols; **Brightcandle** reads their prior rank, with contact and Mirt refresh dates tracked individually.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three contacts are available immediately, and Mirt's next direct question opens ten days after this meeting. **Brightcandle** occurs when this member reaches Renown 10. No Milestone Points are awarded.
