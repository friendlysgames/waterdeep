# Design Notes: Harpshadow

## Individual Access

The threshold is Renown 3 for each Harper member, with a fixed next-day appointment and a surface-return procedure for absent operatives. Companions may use shared information afterward without gaining the member's rank or a separate request allowance.

Named civilian covers, addresses and an opening phrase make the network usable immediately. Mirt's one question and each contact's own request are separate ten-day resources, tracked from the recipient's actual use rather than silently pooled across the party.

## Source and Secrecy

Appendix B's Harper rank table includes faction-rate supplier pricing. The scoped event gives a concrete ten-percent mundane discount at its named suppliers, without extending it to unrelated businesses or magical stock. The campaign guide's added three-ward network remains intact.

Earlier contact support doesn't reveal Remallia's faction role before the salon. A warning about the register likewise remains distinct from closing its leak, and the contacts answer within established access rather than acquiring secret knowledge merely because a rank was reached.
