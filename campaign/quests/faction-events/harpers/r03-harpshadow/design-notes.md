# Design Notes: Harpshadow

## Individual Access

The threshold is Renown 3 for each Harper member, with a fixed next-day appointment and a surface-return procedure for operatives who are away. Companions can use shared information afterward, but they don't gain the member's rank or a request allowance of their own.

Named civilian covers, addresses and an opening phrase make the network usable straight away. Mirt's one question and each contact's own request are separate ten-day resources, and each one is tracked from the recipient's actual use so that nothing pools across the party.

## Source and Secrecy

Appendix B's Harper rank table includes faction-rate supplier pricing. This event turns that into a concrete ten-percent discount on mundane stock at its three named suppliers and keeps it away from unrelated businesses and magical stock. The three-ward network comes from the campaign guide and stays as written.

Contact support at this rank doesn't reveal Remallia's faction role before the salon. A warning about the register is a separate matter from closing its leak, and the contacts answer from the access they already have, so reaching a rank never gives them secret knowledge.
