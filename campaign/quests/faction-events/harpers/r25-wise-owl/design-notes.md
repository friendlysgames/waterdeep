# Design Notes: Wise Owl

## Benefits as Procedures

Appendix B gives the Wise Owl a Laeral audience and operational cover "per arc". The Factions Guide adds informants, a 24-hour priority-target warning and a second persona. Neither says how any of them works, so each benefit is now a per-member procedure with a contact, a place, a notice, a limit and a loss rule. "Per arc" becomes per quest, because arcs are retired.

The informants follow the Guide's DC 13 Charisma check and its two-tenday penalty, which is 20 days. The check only words the request. Mirt refuses a vague request before the roll, a request inside the posting's access gets Advantage, and each posting has a stated limit, so no single roll decides what the member learns. One request per informant per quest keeps three small people from being used up.

## Where the Requests Are Spoken

Every request and every answer goes through Mirt in person, at his Yawning Portal table. The records-relay register only records entries passed to Harper contacts other than Mirt, so the informants never reach it. That keeps Darron Quill, who clerks for the Splinter, out of the leak that **The Cell Is Compromised** describes. Once **Harper Leak Closed** is marked, answers and warnings can travel by sealed bird.

The priority-target warning needed a trigger the table can see. It fires when a villain faction's tier rises to Alert or Lockdown, or when the Cassalanters switch to hostile mode. The warning names a team leader only if the member has already met a team from that faction, so it never gives away a roster.

## Laeral and the Second Persona

The reply note confirms a channel and spends nothing. An audience needs a danger with a deadline, and it has fixed results: a Watch commander informed and patrols doubled for danger to a street, or an escort for one named person. Anything wider is turned back by Mirt before it reaches the Palace, so a member cannot use Laeral to settle faction politics. Mirt's easy use of her first name is the only hint of his office. **High Harper** carries the reveal.

Remallia attends the persona only if **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked. Otherwise Mirt relays and the packet does not say who made it. The Guide wants the second persona; no source says how it works, so it is a three-year trading history with a cartwright to confirm it and a plain Advantage on one kind of check.
