# Design Notes: Wise Owl

## Bounded Access

The source rank gives the member urgent access to Laeral and cover for a sensitive operation, and the approved campaign guide adds informants, a warning and a second persona. Fixed service forms make the cover concrete. A broken cart can tie up a street, but it can't order a guarded site's occupants to do anything, and papers never guarantee a success.

Each named posting has limited access, and the DC 13 Charisma activation and the twenty-day failure consequence stay as they were. A recovered Mara stays recovered and doesn't return to her compromised cover, and a collapsed faction stays collapsed. The source allows one specific question, so each activation returns one answer and the member never receives a full intelligence dossier.

## Warning and Identity Infrastructure

The guide promises delivery within twenty-four hours, so the event counts detection and transmission together against that limit. The warning protects the individual member and shares only the threat the network actually observed. Companions can hear about it but don't gain the rank.

Laeral's note confirms how to make an urgent request and doesn't spend an audience before the character needs one. The second persona comes with its own default name, employer and vouching associate, and the two days of preparation carry over from the existing event. Remallia's role stays behind Mirt's relay until the salon outcome identifies her openly.
