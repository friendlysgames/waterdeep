# Design Notes: Wise Owl

## Bounded Access

The source rank opens urgent access to Laeral and cover for a sensitive operation, while the approved campaign guide adds informants, warning and a second persona. Fixed service forms make the cover concrete without commanding a guarded site's response or granting an automatic success.

Each named posting has limited access, with the existing DC 13 Charisma activation and twenty-day failure consequence retained. A recovered Mara stays recovered rather than resuming her compromised cover, and a collapsed faction remains collapsed. The source's one specific question is applied per activation, avoiding an invented complete intelligence dossier.

## Warning and Identity Infrastructure

The guide promises delivery within twenty-four hours, so the event includes both detection and transmission in that limit. It protects the individual qualifying member without giving companions their own rank, and shares only the threat actually observed.

Laeral's note confirms an urgent request procedure without spending an audience before the character needs it. The second persona has its own default name, employer and vouching associate, with the two-day preparation kept from the existing event. Remallia's role remains behind Mirt's relay until the salon outcome identifies her openly.
