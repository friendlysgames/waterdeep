# Wise Owl

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — the next visit to Mirt, or Mirt seeks the character out within a tenday — after one party member's Harper renown reaches 25. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Mirt name the Wise Owl rank and what reaching it required
> - Receive details on operational cover: forged documents, coordinated distractions, or arranged witnesses, once per quest, routed through Remi
> - Learn the names of three embedded informants (one in Xanathar's operation, one in the Sea Maidens Faire, one near Manshoon's people) and how activation works
> - Receive a villain-targeting warning: when any villain faction designates the character as a priority target, Mirt's network detects it within 24 hours
> - Watch Mirt send a silver raven to Open Lord Laeral Silverhand and receive her reply confirming a private audience before the meeting ends
> - Receive a second, better-documented *persona* from Remi within two days
>
> #### Background
>
> Renown 25 marks the point where Mirt stops being cautious with someone. A Wise Owl has demonstrated judgment under pressure: intelligence delivered cleanly, civilians not harmed, the organization's trust not broken. The benefits here are not ceremonial. Each of the three embedded informants is a real operative with real access and real risk.
>
> Laeral Silverhand is the Open Lord of Waterdeep and, less publicly, one of the Seven Sisters. Mirt's ability to arrange a private audience with her is a real favor that costs him political capital. He extends it because the rank has earned it, not as a courtesy. The audience is for urgent matters, not a standing resource.
>
> Remi's second persona is built around a gap in Waterdeep's merchant registry she has been cultivating for this purpose. The documentation is more elaborate than the first: a legitimate-looking trading history, receipts, and a guild associate who will confirm three years of dealings in person.
>
> Informant activation is a DC 13 Charisma check — the craft of framing a request the informant can act on without exposure. Failure means the informant is unavailable for two tendays but is not burned.

### The Meeting

Mirt's manor in the Sea Ward. He is actually home this time, which is unusual enough that the staff seem slightly off-balance. The receiving room has maps he has not stacked neatly and a plate of food he has already been working through.

> [!readaloud]
> The manor's receiving room smells of old maps and woodsmoke from a fire that has been going for some time. There is a plate of food on the side table that Mirt has been making his way through, and maps spread across most of the available surface. He offers none of it and gets directly to the rank.
>
> > "Wise Owl. The name is more flattering than what it describes — which is mostly waiting, watching people you can't question directly, and knowing when something you're about to spend is worth more than the answer it buys."

> [!social]**The Old Wolf**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan human, he/him) :: a moneylender and Harper operative; for the first time in their meetings, this is the session where his actual position begins to come into view.
>
> Mirt is unhurried here, and slightly more open than at Harpshadow or Brightcandle — the trust is established. He explains the resources in order, each one briefly and completely, and does not undersell any of them. The informants in particular he names without pretense: real operatives with real access and real risk, reachable through him alone.
>
> When he sends the silver raven partway through the meeting and a reply arrives before they leave, he handles it as a small administrative task. He reads the note, refolds it, and slides it across the table. Anyone watching carefully will have questions about his relationship with the Open Lord. Ask him directly and he answers with something warm and evasive.
>
> Conversation topics Mirt is willing to discuss include:
> - Operational cover and what Remi can arrange
> - The three informants and how to activate them
> - The villain-targeting warning and what it gives the party
> - What the Laeral audience is for and what it is not
>
> Specific dialogue for Mirt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**About operational cover?**
>
> > "Forged documents, coordinated distractions, witnesses who didn't see anything. One per quest, arranged through Remi. You tell her what you need; she tells you what's possible." He refills his glass. "Don't ask for things that require more than three days to arrange."

> [!qna]**About the informants?**
>
> He sets down the glass.
>
> > "One in Xanathar's operation. One in the Sea Maidens Faire. One close enough to Manshoon's people to hear the important conversations. You reach them through me — one specific question, not 'tell me everything.' If they can answer, they will. If they can't or won't that tenday, they're unavailable, and you try again."
>
> The activation mechanic is a DC 13 Charisma check — framing the request so the informant can act on it without exposure.
>
> > "Ask precise questions. Vague requests get vague answers at best and a compromised asset at worst."

> [!qna]**About the targeting warning?**
>
> > "When any of the four villain factions puts your name on their priority target list, I hear about it within a day. You hear about it within a day of that." A pause. "That is not a guarantee of safety. It is enough time to change your plans."

> [!qna]**Who is Laeral Silverhand to you?**
>
> He tilts his head slightly and considers the question.
>
> > "An old acquaintance with a very demanding job. She is good at it." He does not elaborate further, though his tone carries genuine warmth he does not explain.

### The Silver Raven

Partway through the meeting, Mirt crosses the room, opens the window latch, and sends a silver raven with a practiced motion. The raven departs upward and southeast without circling.

He comes back to the table and pours wine.

> [!readaloud]
> Within the hour — before the conversation is finished — the raven returns and lands on the open window latch. Mirt takes the folded note from its leg, breaks the plain wax seal, and reads it once. He refolds it and slides it across the table without comment.
>
> The note is in a clean, unhurried hand: a meeting time, a location in the Palace quarter, and three words at the close: *"Mirt speaks well."* It is signed with a simple L.

> [!gamemaster]**Laeral Silverhand**
>
> Laeral Silverhand is the Open Lord of Waterdeep and, less publicly, one of the Seven Sisters and a former Chosen of Mystra. She is one of the most politically powerful individuals in Faerûn and carries herself as someone who has been around long enough to find most things interesting rather than alarming. A private audience with her is not a common courtesy — it is a real exchange of trust, and she will remember how it is used. The note Mirt slides across the table confirms the audience but sets no duration; that depends on what the party brings her.

> [!qna]**What is this audience for?**
>
> > "She's the Open Lord. Don't bring her a problem you could solve on your own." He finishes the wine in his glass. "Save it for something that genuinely requires her."

### The Second Persona

Remi does not appear at this meeting. A folded document arrives at Trollskull Manor within two days: cover name, three-year trading history, receipts, and a guild associate who will confirm the story in a face-to-face conversation. The documentation is more elaborate than the first persona and is suited to contexts where the character needs credibility rather than simple anonymity.

### Concluding the Event

The meeting winds down when the topics are covered. Mirt refills his glass. The maps stay where they are.

## Overview

Mirt names the character a Wise Owl at his manor in the Sea Ward. He explains operational cover for sensitive missions, three embedded informants the character can activate with a precisely framed question, and a standing warning when any villain faction designates the character as a priority target. Partway through the meeting he sends a silver raven; a note from Open Lord Laeral Silverhand confirms a private audience before they are finished talking. Remi Haventree's second, better-documented persona arrives within two days.
