# Wise Owl

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — next visit to Mirt, or Mirt seeks the character out within a tenday — after one party member's Harper renown reaches 25. In this Event:
>
> - Mirt names the Wise Owl rank and what reaching it required
> - He explains operational cover: forged documents, coordinated distractions, or arranged witnesses — once per quest, routed through Remi
> - He names three embedded informants (Xanathar Guild, Sea Maidens Faire, Manshoon's Splinter) and how activation works: one specific question each, DC 13 Charisma check, failure means the informant is unavailable for two tendays
> - He describes the villain-targeting warning: when any villain faction designates the character as a priority target, Mirt's network detects it within 24 hours
> - He sends a silver raven to Laeral Silverhand during the meeting; a reply arrives before they finish talking, confirming a private audience when the character has an urgent matter
> - Remi delivers a second *persona* — better-documented than the first — within two days

**Background (DM only)**

Renown 25 marks the point where Mirt stops being cautious with someone. A Wise Owl has demonstrated judgment under pressure: intelligence delivered cleanly, civilians not harmed, the organization's trust not broken. The benefits here are not ceremonial. Each of the three embedded informants is a real operative with real access and real risk. Mirt knows approximately what they have access to, not exactly what they know on any given day.

Laeral Silverhand is the Open Lord of Waterdeep and, less publicly, one of the Seven Sisters. Mirt's ability to arrange a private audience with her is a real favor that costs him political capital. He extends it because the Wise Owl rank has earned it, not as a courtesy. The character should understand that the audience is for urgent matters — not a standing resource.

Remi's second persona is built around a gap in Waterdeep's merchant registry she has been cultivating for this purpose. The documentation is more elaborate than the first: a legitimate-looking trading history, receipts, a guild associate who will confirm three years of dealings.

### The Meeting

Mirt's manor in the Sea Ward. He is actually home this time, which is unusual enough that the staff seem slightly off-balance. The receiving room has maps he hasn't stacked neatly and a plate of food he has already been working through.

He offers none of it and gets directly to the rank.

> *"Wise Owl. The name is more flattering than what it describes — which is mostly waiting, watching people you can't question directly, and knowing when something you're about to spend is worth more than the answer it buys."*

He explains the operational cover first: forged documents, coordinated distractions, witnesses who saw nothing. One per quest, routed through Remi's side of the network.

> *"You tell her what you need. She tells you what's possible. Don't ask for things that require more than three days to arrange."*

### The Informants

Three embedded contacts. Mirt names their posting without naming them.

> *"One in Xanathar's operation. One in the Sea Maidens Faire. One close enough to Manshoon's people to hear the important conversations. You reach them through me — one specific question, not 'tell me everything.' If they can answer, they will. If they're unavailable that tenday, they're unavailable."*

The activation is a DC 13 Charisma check — the craft of framing a request the informant can act on without exposure. Failure means the informant is unavailable for two tendays but not burned.

> *"Ask precise questions. Vague requests get vague answers at best and a compromised asset at worst."*

### The Warning System

Brief.

> *"When any of the four villain factions puts your name on their priority target list, I hear about it within a day and you hear about it within a day of that. That's not a guarantee of safety. It's enough time to change your plans."*

### The Silver Raven

Mirt crosses the room, opens the window latch, and sends the silver raven with a practiced motion. The raven departs upward and southeast without circling.

He comes back to the table and pours wine.

Within the hour — before they have finished talking — a reply arrives. The raven returns with a folded note sealed in plain wax. Mirt reads it, refolds it, and slides it across the table.

The note is in Laeral Silverhand's hand: a meeting time, a location in the Palace quarter, and three words at the close: *"Mirt speaks well."*

> *"She's the Open Lord. Don't bring her a problem you could solve on your own."*

> [!profile]**Laeral Silverhand**
>
> Laeral Silverhand is the Open Lord of Waterdeep — the city's elected chief magistrate, the public face of its government, and, less publicly, one of the Seven Sisters and a former Chosen of Mystra. She is one of the most politically powerful individuals in Faerûn and maintains the air of someone who has simply been around long enough to find most things interesting rather than alarming. A private audience with her is not a common courtesy — it is a real exchange of trust, and she will remember how it is used.

> [!profile]**Running Mirt at Wise Owl**
>
> This is the first meeting where Mirt's actual position starts to come into focus, for players who are paying attention. He sends a silver raven to the Open Lord and a reply arrives within the hour. He reads it without visible surprise. He handles the whole sequence as a small administrative task. Anyone watching carefully should have questions about the nature of his relationship with Laeral Silverhand. Ask him directly and he answers with something warm and evasive.

### The Second Persona

Remi does not build this one in the room. A folded document arrives at Trollskull Manor within two days: cover name, three-year trading history, receipts, and a guild associate who will confirm the story in a face-to-face conversation. More substantial than the first persona, and suited to contexts where the character needs credibility rather than anonymity.

### Concluding the Event

#### Wise Owl Reached: True / False

True when Mirt hands the character Laeral's reply note. This flag is read by **High Harper** (r50) to confirm prior rank, and by mission events that reference informant access or the Laeral audience benefit.

He explains operational cover for sensitive missions, three embedded informants you can activate with a precise question, and a standing warning when villain factions flag your name. Partway through the meeting, he sends a silver raven; a note arrives before you leave. Laeral Silverhand confirms a private audience when you have an urgent matter worth her time. He says not to bring her problems you could solve yourself.
