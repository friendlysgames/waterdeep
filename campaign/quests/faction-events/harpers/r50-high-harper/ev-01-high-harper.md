# High Harper

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Harper member first reaches Renown 50, which usually happens during **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. Mirt receives the member in his library at 20:00, with Variel Duskwhisper as courier witness and Remallia Haventree at the table if her Harper role is known. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Order copies from the Harper archive of the North, one topic at a time.
> - Call a team of three Spies for one operation, and ask Mirt to come along on one mission.
> - Send a covered wagon to any place in the city to collect them.
> - Send a dossier on a villain faction to Laeral's office.
> - Receive a third persona, with the surname Talar unless they pick another.
> - Hear Mirt admit that he is a Masked Lord, and use or hold one request in his name.
>
> All of these benefits belong to the qualifying member. Companions gain no rank, no team and no request of their own.

### The Silver Raven

Hold this Event until **Wise Owl Reached** is marked for the member. At noon on the first day the member is in Waterdeep after reaching Renown 50, a silver raven finds them wherever they are lodging. Members who qualify together get one raven each and attend together, and each answers for themselves. Companions who are not Harper members are told to wait in the manor kitchen.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A silver raven no bigger than a man's hand lands on the sill and holds out one leg. A slip of paper is tied there, sealed with a harp pressed into red wax.
>
> > "My garden gate at eight tonight. Your friends are welcome to supper in the kitchen. M."

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Mirt is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep, and Laeral Silverhand knows it. He has told nobody in the field except Remallia, and tonight he tells the member because a High Harper plans around what he can do.
> - Remallia sits in only if **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked for the member. If it is not, Mirt hands over the persona himself and says only that "a friend" prepared it.
> - Variel Duskwhisper never repeats what he hears. He leaves the library before the reveal.
> - Mirt does not swear tonight. He names no other Masked Lord to anyone, and he says "the Splinter" unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member.
> - The Harpers suspect the Cassalanters and know nothing more. Nothing in this Event tells the member what the Cassalanters are.
> - If **Vault of Dragons** has resolved, which is usual at this rank, the Grand Game is over. Dossiers then concern the people and crimes that survived it.
> - If **Harper Leak Known** is marked and **Harper Leak Closed** is not, Mirt takes archive topics, dossiers and mission requests in person and never by bird. The wagon call may go by bird, because the courier acts within two hours and the relay copy leaves forty-eight hours later.

### Naming the Rank

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt's library is a long room lined with maps, and a fire has been burning in the hearth since the afternoon. Variel Duskwhisper sits on a stool by the far window with a fiddle across his knee, tuning one string. Mirt pushes a chair out with his foot and refills the glass in front of it before you sit.
>
> > "Sit. I am almost never home, and I'm home tonight. High Harper is the name of the rank, and it's yours. Six things come with it, and then I have one more thing to tell you."

If **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked for the member, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia Haventree sits at the end of the table with a white teapot and pours a cup for each of you. A silver raven figurine stands beside the sugar bowl, and she touches its wing once before she speaks.
>
> > "I'm glad it's you. Do you take sugar, and how is your family keeping? I do like to know whom I'm feeding."

> [!social]**The Old Wolf at His Own Table**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a huge, gray-bearded moneylender and senior Harper, who is home for once and speaking plainly.
>
> Mirt is Friendly and unhurried. He speaks in short, complete sentences and tilts his head while he decides how much to say. He refills a glass whenever one runs low, he calls the member "lad" or "lass", and he gives each benefit its terms the way he would give a loan.
>
> Mirt is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The six benefits, one at a time, and how to ask for each.
> - Laeral, in plain terms, once he has made the reveal.
> - What he expects from a High Harper in return.
>
> Mirt will not discuss the Stone of Golorr or the Cassalanters. He will not name any other Masked Lord.

> [!qna]**Do my companions share the rank?**
>
> > "No. The rank is yours, and so is everything that comes with it. Your friends can stand beside you on any job. They can't send for a wagon or a team in your name, and I'll turn them away."

> [!qna]**What does the rank cost?**
>
> > "Nothing in coin. You'll spend it on work that matters, and I'll keep count. That's a debt, not a gift."

> [!qna]**What if my Renown drops?**
>
> > "You keep the rank, and the benefits sleep until you earn the Renown back. A request already delivered stays delivered."

> [!social]**The Courier Witness**
>
> Variel Duskwhisper (Neutral Good, Wood Elf, he/him) :: a slow-talking wood elf bard who carries Harper papers across the city under the cover of a touring musician.
>
> Variel is Friendly and says as little as the evening allows. He sips his one drink between sentences and tunes the same string while he listens. He never names a person outside a song, and he never raises his voice.
>
> Variel is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The archive copies, and when he will bring them.
> - Laeral's receipt, and when he will bring it.
>
> Variel will not discuss what Mirt says in the library, or anyone's business but his own.

### The Archive

> [!readaloud]
>
> Variel sets a flat leather case on the table and rests two fingers on the lid. He looks at Mirt, waits for the nod and then looks at you.
>
> > "Hm. Name a thing, friend, and I'll bring what we have on it. Ten tomorrow, here in the library."

> [!qna]**What is in the archive?**
>
> > "Five years of our reports from the North. You'll find the informants' accounts, the movements of the factions and every operation we ran, with how each one ended. Names of anyone still in the field are cut out before you read."

> [!qna]**Will it tell me what nobody knows?**
>
> > "No. It tells you what we saw and when we saw it, gaps included. If we never solved a thing, the file doesn't solve it either."

> [!exploration]**Archive Records**
>
> Each High Harper can request copies on one named topic: a faction, a person, a place or an operation in the North.
>
> - **Contact:** Mirt, at his manor or at his Yawning Portal table any evening from 18:00 to 22:00. If **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked, the member can ask Remallia at House Ulbrinter on Delzorin Street instead.
> - **Delivery:** At 10:00 the next day, Variel brings the copies to Mirt's library. The member reads them there, and Variel takes them away again.
> - **Contents:** Informant reports, faction movements and operations with their recorded outcomes, covering the last five years. Answer from the campaign record, and include what the Harpers failed to learn.
> - **Removed:** The identity of every Harper embedded in a faction today. If the topic is such a person, the file names the post and leaves the name blank.
> - **Limit:** One topic per request and one request per day. The Event sets no limit on the number of topics.

### The Team

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt takes a stub of pencil from his coat and draws three small circles on the margin of the city map. He taps each one in turn.
>
> > "Three Spies for one job. Give me the objective, the place and the hour two days ahead. They'll meet you wherever you name, and they work to your plan."

> [!qna]**Where do they meet me?**
>
> > "At a surface place you choose, or at the Yawning Portal if the job is below the city. They're ordinary people in traveling cloaks, and they'll arrive together."

> [!qna]**What won't they do?**
>
> > "They won't hurt anyone who isn't part of the fight. If your plan puts a bystander in the way, they'll tell you, and they'll stop. When the job is done or dead, they leave."

> [!exploration]**Three Spies for One Operation**
>
> Each High Harper can call one team of three Spies, once.
>
> - **Contact:** Mirt, in person, at his manor or at his Portal table.
> - **Notice:** Two days. The member gives the objective, the meeting place and the hour. Mirt refuses a request with less notice, and nothing is spent.
> - **Arrival:** The three Spies wait at the named place at the named hour. The first Spy to speak takes the member's plan.
> - **Duration:** The team works the one operation. It leaves when the objective is finished, abandoned or lost.
> - **Limit:** The use is spent when the team begins the agreed operation. A request withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent.

> [!gamemaster]**The Spies as Allies**
>
> Use the **Spy** stat block three times. Ally Power counts only for Spies who take part in a combat. For a party of levels 5 to 10, three Spies add 51. From level 11, they add 45. Add that to the participating characters' Party Power before recalculating the difficulty and the day cost.
>
> Each Spy follows **Perrin's Tactics** in **Brightcandle**. The team withdraws if its first Spy falls, and a hard fight costs it one casualty.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Three people in plain traveling cloaks wait at the place you named. The tallest steps forward while the other two watch the street.
>
> > "We're here for the job. Tell us how you're going in and where we pull out. If the route puts anyone innocent in danger, we'll say so."

### Mirt Comes Along

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt stands with his hands on the back of a chair and does not sit down for this part. He looks at you for a moment and then at the fire.
>
> > "Once, on a mission you choose, I'll come with you. I'm not as young as I was. I have been in worse places than wherever you're going, and I can look after myself."

> [!qna]**Will you go below the city?**
>
> > "Yes. I'll go as far down as you take me, and I'll come back up with you. I won't stay for a second mission, so choose well."

> [!qna]**Will you fight for us?**
>
> > "I'll fight what needs fighting. I won't raise a hand against the Watch or a Lord in the street, and I won't strike anyone who has put down a weapon."

> [!exploration]**One Mission with Mirt**
>
> Each High Harper can ask Mirt to accompany one mission of their choice, once.
>
> - **Contact:** Mirt, in person. The member names the mission and the departure point on the surface.
> - **Arrival:** Mirt meets the member at the departure point at 09:00 the next day. He stays until the mission is resolved.
> - **If he is away:** The member waits in the order that requests were made. Mirt keeps the first 09:00 after he returns.
> - **Limit:** Mirt joins one mission at a time. The use is spent when he leaves the departure point with the party.
> - **Refusal:** Mirt will not join a mission whose plan harms people who are not part of the fight, and in that case the use is not spent.

> [!gamemaster]**Mirt as an Ally**
>
> Mirt uses the WDH record: CR 9, AC 16, 153 Hit Points, Initiative +4 (14), Passive Perception 15. He makes three attacks, two with his +1 longsword at +9 for 14 slashing damage (16 with two hands) and one with his +1 dagger for 10 piercing damage. He has Brute, Evasion, Sneak Attack (14 damage once per turn) and the Parry reaction. The mechanics reference holds the full block.
>
> Ally Power counts only in a combat where he is present. Mirt adds 85 for a party of levels 5 to 10 and 70 from level 11. Mirt with three Spies adds 136 and 115. Add the total to the participating characters' Party Power before recalculating the difficulty and the day cost.
>
> #### Mirt's Tactics
>
> Mirt stays next to the member who asked for him and takes the closest enemy. During combat, he:
>
> - Attacks the enemy an ally has already engaged, so that Sneak Attack applies.
> - Uses Parry against any melee attack aimed at the member.
> - Withdraws with the party at 50 Hit Points or fewer, and does not leave anyone behind.
> - Refuses to attack the Watch, a Lord, or anyone who has surrendered.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt comes up the street at nine in his traveling coat, with his sword on his hip and a coil of rope over one shoulder. He looks into each doorway before he stops beside you.
>
> > "Morning. Tell me what's changed since you asked, and then we go."

### The Wagon

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt draws the map toward him and lays his pencil on the ward where you were last seen. He keeps his hand flat beside it.
>
> > "If you need getting out, tell us where you are and who you are. We'll send a wagon. Two hours, and you're across the city in twenty minutes."

> [!qna]**How do I call the wagon?**
>
> > "Send a bird to me, or give word to any Harper you trust. Tell us where you are, how the courier will know you and where you want to be set down. It has to be a place inside the city."

> [!qna]**What if they can't reach us?**
>
> > "They wait half an hour at the nearest back door or service gate. If nobody gets out to them, they withdraw and come again two hours later, and a bird tells you the hour."

> [!exploration]**Covert Extraction**
>
> Each High Harper can call a covered wagon to any place in Waterdeep, as often as a courier is free. The call does not use up the team benefit.
>
> - **Request:** The member sends Mirt a paper bird, or gives the message to any Harper contact. It names the member's location, a way for the courier to recognise them and a safe destination in the city.
> - **Arrival:** Two hours later, a courier arrives with a covered wagon and three Spies, at the street or service entrance nearest to the location. The couriers go in the order Wil Keen, Della Morn, Corin Bell.
> - **Open location:** The Spies collect the member and every companion named in the request. The wagon drives them to the destination in twenty minutes, and nobody asks what happened.
> - **Guarded location:** The wagon waits at a service entrance for thirty minutes. The occupants treat it as a delivery. The Spies cannot break a lock or fight a way in, and they take anyone who reaches the wagon. If nobody does, they withdraw and return for another thirty minutes two hours later, and a paper bird names the hour.
> - **Below the city:** The wagon cannot go underground. The member must reach a surface exit, and the nearest one the wagon can reach is the Yawning Portal.
> - **Couriers:** A courier is out from the call until the recovery ends. If all three are out, a new call waits for the first one to return.

> [!gamemaster]**The Wagon and the Spies**
>
> The three Spies use the **Spy** stat block and add Power only if they must fight their way out. For a party of levels 5 to 10 they add 51. From level 11, they add 45. The courier drives the wagon and shelters during any fight, and does not count as a combatant.

If the wagon reaches an open location, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A covered wagon rattles to a stop at the curb, and the driver looks you over from the seat. Three people in cloaks climb down from the back and open the rear flap.
>
> > "Right, you're the ones I was told about. Tell me who's coming with you, then get in. It's twenty minutes across the city, and you can tell Mirt the rest when you're safe."

> [!gamemaster]**The Couriers**
>
> - **Wil Keen:** a Harper courier in his thirties who chews a stalk of straw. He speaks in the voice of the wagon readaloud above.
> - **Della Morn:** a broad woman in her fifties who wears a carter's apron. She says nothing until the member is seated and then says only the destination.
> - **Corin Bell:** a lanky man in his twenties who talks the whole drive and never asks what happened.

### The Dossier

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt takes a fresh sheet from the drawer and writes the day's date at the top. A second sheet lies underneath it with the Open Lord's seal drawn in the corner.
>
> > "If you want a faction exposed, bring me its name and the evidence you really have. I'll send it to Laeral's office tonight. Variel will bring the answer back."

> [!qna]**What will the Open Lord's office do?**
>
> > "It reads what you sent and writes down what it can verify and what it can't. A crime that's proven goes into the Watch's ordinary investigation. A claim with no proof stays a claim."

> [!qna]**Can I name the Cassalanters?**
>
> > "You can name anyone. I distrust them as much as you do, and so does half the city. The office files that as suspicion, and only what you can prove goes on the receipt."

If **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member, Mirt answers this question:

> [!qna]**Can I name Manshoon's cell?**
>
> > "Name Manshoon if you have the evidence for it. The office will list what you can prove about him and what you can't, the same as for any faction."

If **Manshoon Named** is not marked, he answers this instead:

> [!qna]**Can I name the Splinter?**
>
> > "Name the Splinter if you have evidence for it. The office will list what you can prove about it and what you can't, the same as for any faction. We don't yet know who leads it."

> [!exploration]**A Formal Exposure Request**
>
> Each High Harper can ask the High Harpers to lay one villain faction's activities before the Open Lord, once per faction.
>
> - **Contact:** Mirt, in person. The member gives the faction's name and the evidence they have.
> - **Dossier:** Mirt sends it to Laeral's office that evening.
> - **Receipt:** At 10:00 three days later, Variel brings a receipt from the office to Mirt's library. It lists the submitted claims, the facts that are corroborated and the gaps that remain.
> - **Result:** Verified crimes enter the city's Watch investigation. Unproven claims stay claims, and nobody is declared guilty.
> - **Fallen factions:** If the faction has already fallen, the dossier concerns its survivors and the crimes still open.
> - **Cassalanters:** Suspicion never becomes knowledge. The receipt mentions the Cassalanters' pact or their infernalism only if the party found the evidence and put it in the dossier.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Variel sets a sealed receipt on the library table beside Mirt's copy of your dossier. He slides both toward you and goes back to tuning his string.
>
> > "Hm. The office wrote down what it can swear to and what still wants witnesses. Read the second part twice, friend."

If it is not marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!qna]**Who vouches for Talar?**
>
> > "A chandler on Fillet Lane, a map-seller on the Street of Silks and a cartwright in the Sea Ward. Each knows you by this name and by no other. Go and buy something from them before you rely on it."

> [!exploration]**The Third Persona**
>
> Each High Harper has one third persona, separate from the first two.
>
> - **Name:** The member names a surname at the table. If they name none, the surname is Talar.
> - **Cover:** A senior purchasing agent for Vale & Reed Imports, with a registry extract, a guild letter and three years of quarterly receipts for coastal orders.
> - **Vouching:** Nella Fen, 16 Fillet Lane, and Orin Dask, 9 Street of Silks, confirm the dealings on their own receipts. Ilen Castor, a cartwright at 6 Shield Street in the Sea Ward, confirms the transport orders when shown a receipt number. None of them knows the other two personas.
> - **Effect:** The papers stand up to ordinary commercial scrutiny. The member has Advantage on a Charisma check to be believed as a purchasing agent when the other party can check the papers.
> - **Limits:** The papers give no disguise, no legal office and no automatic success. They fail a magical check, and outside Waterdeep nobody can confirm them.
> - **Delivery:** The packet is handed over tonight. The member keeps the first two personas.

### The Masked Lord

Variel finishes his drink, puts the fiddle under his arm and leaves. If Remallia is at the table, she stays.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The latch clicks behind Variel, and Mirt sets his glass down beside the map. He waits until the fire has settled before he looks back across the table.
>
> > "There is one more thing, and I've told nobody in the field. I'm a Masked Lord of Waterdeep."

If a member says they worked this out earlier, Mirt asks how they did it, nods at the answer and carries on. The guess costs nothing and needs no check.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt tilts his head and looks at each of you in turn. He waits until the fire pops before he goes on.
>
> > "Laeral knows. I'm telling you because we'll be planning together, and you ought to know who you're planning with. Keep my seat out of public accounts."

Remallia, if she is at the table, keeps her eyes on her cup. She has known for years.

> [!qna]**Who else knows?**
>
> > "Laeral knows. I won't say who else does, and I won't name any other Lord. If you ask me again, you'll get the same answer."

> [!qna]**May I tell my companions?**
>
> > "You may, but choose carefully. Don't put it in a letter, and don't put it in a song."

> [!qna]**Will you introduce me to the other Lords?**
>
> > "No. I can use my own office for you, once, and I'll explain how. The rest of the Lords have their own business."

### One Request

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt takes a clean sheet from the drawer and writes your name across the top. He leaves the rest blank and lays it between you.
>
> > "I can give you one request in my name. It can be Watch cooperation for an operation, a hearing before the Lords' Court, or a warrant of immunity for one person. Use it now, or keep it until you know what you need."

> [!qna]**What will you refuse?**
>
> > "I won't put my name to anything that harms the city or hides an attack on people who aren't part of the fight. I can't reach past Waterdeep's own authority either. I'll tell you before you spend it, and you can change the request."

> [!qna]**What does a request buy me?**
>
> > "An act within the city's own authority. It isn't a verdict, and it doesn't put every guard in Waterdeep at your command. Write down one operation, one faction or one person, and the facts that justify it."

> [!exploration]**One Masked Lord Request**
>
> Each High Harper owns one request, which can be made now or at any later time. The member submits it in writing to Mirt, naming one of these forms:
>
> - **Watch cooperation:** The member names one operation, its objective, its place and its date. Three days after Mirt accepts, he delivers a sealed instruction to the responsible Watch station. It gives the member access to the relevant Watch records and assigns a liaison who coordinates the Watch's existing resources for that operation. It does not raise a new squad or put the member in command.
> - **Lords' Court hearing:** The member names a faction and supplies the evidence they have. Three days after Mirt accepts, he delivers a docket receipt, and the hearing is at 10:00 the next day. The court weighs the evidence and decides guilt at the hearing and nowhere else.
> - **Warrant of immunity:** The member names one person and one existing offense or pursuit. Three days after Mirt accepts, he delivers a sealed warrant to that person and a notice to the Watch. It ends the city's pursuit for that matter. Other offenses and anything done afterward are not covered.
>
> Mirt refuses a request that knowingly harms the city, that hides an attack on people who are not part of the fight, or that needs authority beyond Waterdeep. He gives the reason when he receives the request, before the use is spent, and the member can change the request or keep the use. The request is spent only when Mirt accepts it. A discussion, a refused request or a withdrawn draft spends nothing.

If the member invokes now and Mirt accepts, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt reads the request back and signs it. He writes a date at the bottom, three days from tonight, and folds the sheet into his coat.
>
> > "That will be done on the day I've written. Nothing else will come of it unless you ask me for it separately."

If Mirt refuses the request as written, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt reads the request twice and sets the pencil down. He slides the sheet back across the table and does not sign it.
>
> > "I won't put my name to that request as written. You haven't spent your use, so find me another way to deal with the problem."

If the member holds the request, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt slides the blank sheet into a folder with your name on the cover and sets the folder on a shelf behind him, then refills your glass.
>
> > "Keep it. Bring me a signed request when the need is real, and I'll give you the date before I spend it."

Remallia stays for a last cup of tea if she is at the table. She asks the member one question she has been holding since the salon, and the member answers or deflects as they please. Mirt refills the glasses, and the companions are called in from the kitchen for supper.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown, gold or Milestone Points.

### Aftermath

The member can tell their companions what the rank gives them. A companion who asks for a wagon, a team or a request in the member's name is turned away. A member whose Renown later falls below 50 keeps the rank, and the benefits are suspended until their Renown returns to 50. A request already delivered stays delivered.

Record separately for each member the date of the team and Mirt's mission, the surname on the third persona, each dossier sent, and the form and delivery date of the Masked Lord request. An unspent request carries into **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when Mirt has made his reveal and the member has taken the persona and answered the question of the request.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **High Harper Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Mirt has made his reveal and handed over the persona; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) and by **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** content that checks the rank.
> - **Masked Lord Request Invoked** — mark with the member's name, the form and the delivery date only when Mirt accepts a valid request; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) and by **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** content that checks whether the single use remains.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> The archive answers at 10:00 the next day, the team needs two days' notice, Mirt joins at the next free 09:00 and an accepted request is delivered in three days. This is the final Harper rank, and its benefits stay available for later quests. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

A silver raven calls a Harper member to Mirt's library, where he names them a High Harper and explains what the rank gives. He then tells them he is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep and offers one request in his name.

## Summary

We became High Harpers at Mirt's table, with Variel playing in the corner. Mirt gave us an archive, a team of Spies, a wagon and his own company on one mission, along with a third persona. He told us he sits among the Masked Lords, and each of us either used the one request he offered or kept it for later.
