# High Harper

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs after an individual Harper member reaches Renown 50. Mirt names the rank, explains its resources and privately reveals his seat among Waterdeep's Masked Lords. The member can:
>
> - Arrange archive access, a coordinated team or Mirt's personal accompaniment.
> - Learn the city extraction and faction-exposure procedures.
> - Use their single Masked Lord request now or hold it for later.
>
> These benefits belong to the qualifying member. Companions can help plan an operation, but they gain no rank and no invocation of their own.

### Hook

Mirt sends a silver raven at noon on the first day the qualifying member is available in Waterdeep, which is within ten days of reaching the threshold if they stay in the city. It carries a sealed invitation for 20:00 that evening, by way of his manor's garden gate. A member who is away receives the same appointment on the first evening after they return to the surface. Companions are welcome for supper afterward, but the rank meeting itself is private to qualifying Harpers.

### Background

Mirt's public work as a moneylender, his Harper responsibilities and his Masked Lord seat all belong to one man. Laeral knows who he is and relies on him as an adviser, and the new High Harper can now plan work that needs more than his field contacts.

Variel Duskwhisper, a wood elf Harper courier, witnesses the promotion and never repeats its private business afterward. Remallia joins only if **Remallia Harper Contact Known** has already identified her to this member. If it hasn't, Mirt relays her arrangements and presents her prepared packet himself, and Variel never gives away who she is.

This rank usually arrives during Undermountain play. If **Vault of Dragons** has resolved, the benefits still apply, and exposure requests deal with the activities that survive it. They don't reopen the Grand Game or restore a defeated faction.

### Resources for the Work Ahead

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt meets you at the garden door with a glass already in his hand and leads you through the house into the library, where Variel has set a sealed register beside the evening's papers. He pours a glass for you, drops into the chair across the table and turns the city map so it faces the work you have brought him.
>
> > "High Harper. You've earned the place, and there are people we can put behind your work now. Tell me what you're facing, and we'll make these arrangements worth having before you go back out."

Variel keeps his hand on the sealed register until Mirt finishes the greeting:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Variel sits in the corner with his one drink, tuning a string on the fiddle across his knee, and looks up when Mirt stops talking.
>
> > "Hm. The register's here when you're ready, friend. I'll bring whatever copies you ask for tomorrow morning."

> [!qna]**What can I read in the archive?**
>
> > "Five years of our northern reports, with the movements, the informants and the outcomes we recorded. Name a topic, and Variel will bring the copies here at ten tomorrow for you to read. We cut out the names of anyone still embedded, of course, and an old file won't answer a question that nobody's managed to solve yet."

> [!qna]**What is this third identity?**
>
> > "Talar, by default, with your own given name in front. You're a senior purchasing agent for Vale and Reed with three years of coastal orders, and the papers are here tonight. Nella and Orin know the supply dealings, and Ilen has the cartwright receipts. Pick another surname if you want one, before you use it."

> [!exploration]**Archives and a Third Persona**
>
> The member can request the Harpers' intelligence records for the North from the past five years: historical informant reports, faction movements and operations with their recorded outcomes. Send a named topic to Remallia if she is known and to Mirt if she isn't. At 10:00 the following day, Variel brings the relevant copies to Mirt's library for supervised reading, with current embedded identities removed. The records contain established campaign history and observations, including the gaps in what the Harpers learned, so the archive can't answer a mystery that is still unresolved.
>
> The third persona is ready at this meeting. By default it uses the member's chosen given name with the surname Talar, as a senior purchasing agent for Vale & Reed Imports. The packet includes commercial registration, three years of quarterly receipts and orders for coastal deliveries. Nella Fen and Orin Dask confirm their existing supply dealings, and cartwright Ilen Castor at 6 Shield Street confirms the transport orders by receipt number. A player can choose another cover name before using the packet. The papers hold up under ordinary business scrutiny and give no magical disguise, no legal office and no automatic success on a check.

If **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked, Remallia joins the private meeting and sets the packet beside Mirt's register:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Remallia pours the tea herself and slides the packet across the table, between the register and Mirt's glass, with a smile for each of you.
>
> > "Would Talar suit you, or is there another name you'd answer to more comfortably? The coastal orders are ready under either one. Could you read me a receipt aloud before you take the papers? I'd much rather hear a question now than have you find a gap at a guarded door."

If Remallia isn't known, Mirt asks for the cover-name choice and keeps the packet's preparer private:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Talar's on the papers, unless you'd rather carry another surname. Say so before you leave, and I'll have the records changed to match. Whoever prepared them stays out of your account."

If the member chooses another cover surname, the three vouching contacts use it when they confirm the same recorded transactions. Ilen speaks only to a receipt number that has been presented to him:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Ilen looks up from his cartwright book in the yard behind his shop with the presented receipt in one hand.
>
> > "That's the transport order for this number, yes, and I've dealt with the purchasing agent on these receipts for three years. That's the business I can vouch for, and I'll not say more than that."

> [!qna]**Can I take a team below the city?**
>
> > "Three operatives for one coordinated job, with two days' notice. Give me the objective, the place and the hour. They'll meet you at the surface point you name, or at the Yawning Portal before a descent. When the job's done they leave, because they're not a standing guard."

> [!qna]**Can you come yourself?**
>
> > "I'll come once, on a mission of your choosing. If I'm free, I'll meet you at nine tomorrow morning at your departure point on the surface. If I've gone off with another member, you wait your turn in the order you asked, and I meet you at the first nine after I'm back, since there's only the one of me to go around."

> [!gamemaster]**A Team and Mirt's Accompaniment**
>
> Each High Harper can call on three standard 2024 Spies for one coordinated operation, once. The member gives Mirt two days' notice with the objective, the meeting place and the start time. The three operatives arrive together at that surface location, or at the Yawning Portal before an Undermountain descent. Use the shared availability ledger in **Brightcandle**, taking unused names in the order Reed, Ash, Elm, Briar, Willow and Alder, then recipient-labelled sets. Brightcandle assignments and extractions share that ledger, so a field name that is already deployed isn't assigned again until its operative returns.
>
> The team follows the agreed objective, and its first assigned operative coordinates movement. The Spies refuse to attack uninvolved civilians and leave when the objective is completed, abandoned or made impossible by the loss of its target. Record the use when the team begins the agreed operation. The team never becomes a permanent retinue.
>
> Separately, each High Harper can ask Mirt to accompany one mission of their choice, once. If he is available, he meets them at the named surface departure point at 09:00 the following day with his equipment and stays until that mission is resolved. A member who asks while he is away joins a queue in the order requests arrive, and Mirt departs at the first 09:00 after he returns from the previous mission. Each member keeps their own allowance, Mirt accompanies only one mission at a time, and he adds ally Power only in a combat where he is present. He can go on a descent and fights when the mission requires it, using his CR 9 statistics from the mechanics reference and not a generic Veteran. He refuses to harm uninvolved people on purpose, and if a proposal asks for that, the benefit stays unused.
>
> Count only the allies who take part in a combat. Three Spies add Power 66, 51, 45 or 24 at party tiers 1 through 4, and Mirt adds 110, 85, 70 or 55. Add these to the participating PCs' Party Power before recalculating difficulty and day cost. Granting the rank, or having a team somewhere else, adds no ally Power. Mirt's HP 153, AC 16, attacks, Sneak Attack, Evasion and Parry are the documented Waterdeep statistics, and his defensive role for a specific encounter applies only when that situation calls for it.

When a team arrives for its named operation, the first assigned operative coordinates its movement and addresses the member:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Three people in plain traveling cloaks wait at the surface point you named, and the tallest of them steps forward while the other two check the street.
>
> > "We're here for the job you named. Give me your approach and where we withdraw to. The three of us will move together, and if the route puts anybody uninvolved in danger, we'll tell you."

If Mirt accompanies this member, he arrives at the recorded departure point at the next available 09:00:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt comes up the street at nine in his usual weapons and traveling coat, glancing at each doorway before he stops beside you.
>
> > "Morning. I'm here for this mission. Tell me what's changed since you asked, and then we go. Nobody sends me anywhere else until we're finished."

### Extraction and Exposure

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt draws the city map closer across the library table and lays a pencil on the part of Waterdeep where you were last seen. A second sheet lies underneath it, ready for evidence that might go to Laeral's office.
>
> > "If you need getting out, tell us where you are and where you need to be. And if you want a faction exposed, bring me the evidence you have. We'll carry it to people who can act on it."

> [!qna]**How do I call the wagon?**
>
> > "Send me where you are, a description the courier can recognize and a safe address inside the city. Two hours after I get it, a covered wagon and three operatives come to your street or service entrance. A guarded door stays guarded, mind. They'll work with the place as they find it."

> [!qna]**What if they cannot reach us?**
>
> > "They wait thirty minutes at a guarded entrance and try to get you out. If they can't, they pull back and return for another thirty minutes two hours later, and you get a bird telling you when. Once you're aboard, the drive across the city takes twenty minutes."

> [!exploration]**Covert City Extraction**
>
> Send Mirt the member's location, a recognizable description and a chosen safe destination within Waterdeep, by paper bird or an existing courier. Two hours after he receives the request, an available courier brings a covered wagon to that location's street or service entrance, along with three available Spies from the shared operative ledger. Extraction doesn't use up the separate one-operation team benefit. Couriers have their own availability entries. Assign Harl Keen first, then Della Morn, then Oren Bell, and for further simultaneous requests use a courier named with the recipient's surname and the given name Tamsin, adding a numbered dispatch entry if needed. Mark the courier and wagon as assigned until the recovery ends, so Harl and the same wagon never go to two calls at once.
>
> At an open location, the detail collects the member and their named companions at once. At a guarded location, it waits at the service entrance for thirty minutes and uses the building's established entry routes and occupant responses to reach them. It brings the recovery effort, and the locks, guards and custody are all still there. The assigned courier then drives the recovered people to the chosen destination, and the drive takes twenty minutes within the city. Nobody asks the member to explain what happened. If the detail can't reach them during that attempt, it withdraws and returns to the same entrance for another thirty-minute attempt two hours later, and it sends a paper bird naming the return time. The procedure covers city locations, and an Undermountain party has to reach a surface exit before the wagon can recover them.
>
> Each recovery detail uses three standard Spies, with the ally Power above if it joins a combat. Its courier drives and shelters during a fight and doesn't fight as another combatant.

If the extraction detail reaches an open location, the assigned courier checks the member's description before opening the covered wagon:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A covered wagon rattles to a stop at the curb, and the driver looks you over from the seat before climbing down to open the back.
>
> > "Right, I've got your destination. Tell me who's coming with you and then get in, because it's a twenty-minute drive across the city. You can tell Mirt the rest once you're safe."

If guards or a closed route prevent an immediate pickup, the courier speaks from the service entrance before the first thirty-minute attempt:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The courier reins in the wagon at the service entrance you named and looks up at the guarded door while three operatives climb down from the back.
>
> > "We'll try your route for half an hour. The guards and the locks are still there, and my three will work with whatever this place gives them. If we can't reach you, watch for a bird with the second time."

> [!qna]**Can I bring a faction before Laeral?**
>
> > "Bring me the faction's name and whatever evidence you really have, and I'll send the dossier this evening. Variel brings Laeral's office receipt back at ten, three days from now. It sorts what's corroborated from what still needs proof. It won't declare anybody guilty."

> [!gamemaster]**A Formal Exposure Request**
>
> The member can ask the High Harpers to present a villain faction's activities to the Open Lord. The member gives Mirt the faction's name and the evidence they actually have, and he sends the dossier that evening. At 10:00 three days later, Variel returns a receipt from Laeral's office listing the submitted claims, the corroborated facts and the remaining gaps. Verified crimes enter the city's existing Watch investigation procedure, and unsupported allegations stay allegations.
>
> Suspicion of the Cassalanters never gives anyone knowledge of their pact or infernalism. That evidence enters the dossier only if the party has discovered it and reported it. If the named faction has fallen, the request concerns its surviving members and unresolved crimes. This Harper service is separate from the single Masked Lord invocation.

If the member submits an exposure dossier, Variel brings the receipt at the fixed 10:00 appointment three days later:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Variel sets the sealed receipt from the office beside Mirt's copy of the dossier in the library and slides both toward you, then goes back to tuning his fiddle.
>
> > "Hm. The office wrote down what it can verify and what still wants witnesses. Read both of them before you decide what to send next."

> [!gamemaster]**Laeral's Office Receipt**
>
> The receipt lists the submitted claims, the corroborated facts and the gaps that the party's own evidence establishes. Its written heading reads: "Received for review by the Open Lord's office. Verified matters proceed through the Watch; unresolved claims remain open for evidence." It names no secret villain leader, infernal pact or Masked Lord beyond what the dossier proved.

### Mirt's Confidence and the Request

> [!readaloud]
>
> Variel closes the library door behind him, and Mirt sets his glass down beside the register and waits for the latch to settle before he looks back across the table at you.
>
> > "There's something else you should know before you decide how to use my help. I'm a Masked Lord of Waterdeep. Laeral knows, and I work closely with her. I'm telling you because we'll be planning together, and you ought to know who you're planning with."

Mirt answers questions about his own responsibilities and doesn't name any other Masked Lord. He asks for discretion in the speech below. Companions who arrive for supper aren't told, and the member decides afterward what to share with them.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt takes a fresh sheet from the drawer, writes your name across the top and sets it beside the papers you're taking home. When he explains the request, he leaves the sheet unsigned, so you can come back with a particular need and not have to settle one over the table tonight.
>
> > "One formal request from you. That's Watch cooperation for an operation, proceedings against a faction, or immunity for a named person. Hold it until you know what you need. If the terms would do the city harm, I'll tell you."

> [!qna]**Who else knows?**
>
> > "Laeral knows who I am, but I'll not name the other Lords for you. Keep my seat out of public accounts and ordinary dispatches, and don't go proving it by putting anyone else in danger."

> [!qna]**What does a request buy me?**
>
> > "It gets you an act within the city's authority. It's not a verdict, and it doesn't put every guard in Waterdeep at your command. Put one operation, faction or person on the signed sheet, with the facts that justify it. If I can't use my office for it, I'll say so before you spend your one request."

> [!exploration]**One Masked Lord Invocation per High Harper**
>
> The qualifying member owns one use and can invoke it now or later. Helpers can advise on the resulting operation or take part in it, and that gives them no use of their own. The member submits a signed request naming one of these forms:
>
> - **Watch cooperation:** Identify one operation, its objective, place and date. Three days after submission, Mirt delivers a sealed instruction to the responsible Watch station. It permits access to relevant Watch records and assigns a liaison to coordinate the Watch's existing resources for that operation. It doesn't create a new combat squad or hand command of the Watch to the member.
> - **Lords' Court proceedings:** Name a faction and provide the available evidence. Three days after submission, Mirt supplies the docket receipt and a hearing at 10:00 the following day in the Lords' Court. The process forces formal consideration of the evidence, and the court decides guilt only at the hearing.
> - **A warrant of immunity:** Name one person and the specific existing offense or pursuit to be covered. Three days after submission, Mirt delivers the sealed warrant to that person and sends notice to the Watch. It ends Waterdeep's pursuit for the named matter. Proceedings for other offenses continue, and the person stays liable for anything done later.
>
> Mirt declines a request that knowingly enables harm to the city, conceals an intended attack on uninvolved people or needs authority beyond Waterdeep's jurisdiction. He gives that reason when he receives the request, before the use is spent, and the member can revise the request or keep the use. A valid request uses up the invocation when Mirt accepts it, and the fixed three-day delivery is recorded at that moment. An appointment or an unspent discussion doesn't use it up.

If Mirt accepts a valid Watch cooperation request, he records the operation, the objective, the place and the date:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I'll send the responsible station a sealed instruction in three days. A liaison will share the relevant records and coordinate whatever the Watch already has for this operation. It won't put every watchman under your command, and it won't raise another squad."

If Mirt accepts a valid request for Lords' Court proceedings, he checks the faction's name against the evidence submitted:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "You'll have the docket receipt in three days and a hearing at ten the following morning. The court will weigh what you brought. It won't call an accusation a conviction before anyone's heard the evidence."

If Mirt accepts a valid warrant of immunity request, he writes down the person's name and the existing matter covered:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "One person and one named offense or pursuit. In three days I'll send the warrant to that person and a notice to the Watch. It ends the city's pursuit for that matter, though not for any other offense or anything done afterward."

If a proposal would knowingly enable harm to the city, Mirt refuses it before the use is spent:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I won't put my name to that request as written. It would do the city harm, and I won't use this office to arrange it. You haven't spent your use on it, so go and find me another way to deal with the problem."

If the request would conceal an intended attack on uninvolved people, he gives the narrower reason:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "You're asking me to hide an attack on people who aren't part of this fight. I won't do it. Your use is still yours, but this request goes no further."

If it needs authority beyond Waterdeep's jurisdiction, he names that limit:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "That order would reach past Waterdeep, and my office has no authority there. I'll hear a city request from you, but this one isn't valid, and you haven't spent your use."

If the member keeps the request for later, Mirt slides the unsigned sheet into their rank folder:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Keep the use. Bring me a signed request when the need is real, and I'll give you the delivery date before I spend it."

If the member holds the request, Mirt keeps the unsigned sheet in their rank folder. If they invoke it, he records the delivery date before he invites the waiting companions in to supper. Variel leaves with the sealed promotion record at 21:00.

### Renown Opportunities

The promotion grants no additional Renown, gold or Milestone Points. Each recipient records their own team deployment, personal accompaniment and Masked Lord use, and extraction and archive requests follow the procedures above.

### Aftermath

The third persona is available immediately, and the member keeps the earlier ones. The member can carry these benefits into Undermountain play, including an unspent invocation, and a resolved city faction stays resolved. Sensitive field reports continue privately through Mirt until **Harper Leak Closed**, and neither this promotion nor Mirt's confidence is ever entered in Orren's dispatch register.

### Concluding the Event

The event concludes when Mirt has made his reveal and delivered the third persona and the support procedures. Record later uses against the named recipient, and don't mark the whole party as High Harpers.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **High Harper Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Mirt reveals his seat and hands over the packet; **Vault of Dragons** and future Undermountain events read that member's accompaniment and support access.
> - **Masked Lord Request Invoked** — mark with the member's name, accepted request and delivery date only when Mirt accepts a valid invocation; **Vault of Dragons** and future Undermountain events read whether that individual's single use remains available.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Accepted invocations resolve in three days, team deployments need two days' notice and Mirt departs at the next available 09:00 under his mission queue. This is the final Harper rank, and its benefits stay available for later quests. It awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

A silver raven invites the qualifying Harper to Mirt's library, where he explains the support available to a High Harper and trusts them with his seat as a Masked Lord. The member can make one formal request or keep it for a later need.

## Summary

We became High Harpers and received our third persona, and we learned how to call on the archive, a team, Mirt's company, a city extraction and an exposure request. Mirt told us privately that he sits among the Masked Lords, and we either used our one formal request or kept it for later.
