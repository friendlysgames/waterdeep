# High Harper

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs after an individual Harper member reaches Renown 50. Mirt names the rank, explains its resources and privately reveals his seat among Waterdeep's Masked Lords. The member can:
>
> - Arrange archive access, a coordinated team or Mirt's personal accompaniment.
> - Learn the city extraction and faction-exposure procedures.
> - Use their single Masked Lord request now or retain it for later.
>
> These benefits belong to the qualifying member. Companions can help plan an operation without receiving their own rank or invocation.

### Hook

Mirt sends a silver raven at noon on the first day the qualifying member is available in Waterdeep, within ten days of reaching the threshold if they remain in the city. It brings a sealed invitation for 20:00 that evening through his manor's garden gate. A member away receives the same appointment on the first evening after surface return. Companions are welcome for supper afterward, while the rank meeting remains private to qualifying Harpers.

### Background

Mirt's public work as a moneylender, his Harper responsibilities and his Masked Lord seat coexist. Laeral knows his identity and relies on him as an adviser, while the recipient can now plan work that calls for more than his field contacts.

Variel Duskwhisper, a wood elf Harper courier, witnesses the promotion without repeating its private business afterward. Remallia joins only if **Remallia Harper Contact Known** has already identified her to this member; otherwise Mirt relays her arrangements and presents her prepared packet himself. Variel never supplies her identity early.

This rank usually arrives during Undermountain play. If **Vault of Dragons** has resolved, the benefits persist, with exposure requests addressing surviving activities rather than reopening the Grand Game or restoring a defeated faction.

### Resources for the Work Ahead

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt meets you at the garden door and leads you into the library, where Variel has placed a sealed register beside the evening’s papers. Mirt pours a glass for you before taking the chair across the table, with the map turned toward the work you have brought him.
>
> > "High Harper. You've earned the place, and there are people we can put behind your work now. Tell me what you're facing, so we can make these arrangements useful before you go back out."

Variel leaves his hand on the sealed register until Mirt finishes the greeting:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Hm. The register is here when you're ready. I'll bring the copies you ask for tomorrow morning."

> [!qna]**What can I read in the archive?**
>
> > "Five years of our northern reports: movements, informants and the outcomes we recorded. Name the topic. Variel brings the relevant copies here at ten tomorrow for you to read. Current embedded names stay out of them, and an old file cannot answer a question nobody solved."

> [!qna]**What is this third identity?**
>
> > "Talar, by default, with your chosen given name. Senior purchasing agent for Vale and Reed, three years of coastal orders. The papers are here tonight. Nella and Orin know the supply dealings; Ilen has the cartwright receipts. Choose another surname if you want one before you use it."

> [!exploration]**Archives and a Third Persona**
>
> The member can request the Harpers' intelligence records for the North from the past five years: historical informant reports, faction movements and operations with their recorded outcomes. Send a named topic to Remallia if known, otherwise to Mirt. At 10:00 the following day, Variel brings the relevant copies to Mirt's library for supervised reading, with current embedded identities removed. The records contain established campaign history and observations, including gaps in what the Harpers learned; archive access doesn't manufacture an answer to an unresolved mystery.
>
> The third persona is ready at this meeting. Its default uses the member's chosen given name with the surname Talar, as a senior purchasing agent for Vale & Reed Imports. The packet includes commercial registration, three years of quarterly receipts and orders for coastal deliveries. Nella Fen and Orin Dask confirm their existing supply dealings, while cartwright Ilen Castor at 6 Shield Street confirms the transport orders by receipt number. A player can choose another cover name before using the packet. It supports ordinary business scrutiny without granting magical disguise, legal office or automatic success on a check.

If **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked, Remallia joins the private meeting and puts the packet beside Mirt's register:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Would Talar serve you, or is there another name you can answer to comfortably? The coastal orders are ready under either choice. Please read one receipt aloud before you take the papers; I would rather hear a question now than leave you to find a gap at a guarded door."

If Remallia is not known, Mirt asks for the cover-name choice while keeping the packet's preparer private:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Talar is ready on the papers, unless you'd rather carry another surname. Say so before you leave, and I'll have the records use your choice. The person who prepared them stays out of your account."

If the member chooses another cover surname, the three vouching contacts use that chosen name when confirming the same recorded transactions. Ilen speaks only to a presented receipt number:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "The transport order matches this number in my cartwright book. I've dealt with the purchasing agent on these receipts for three years; that is the business I can confirm."

> [!qna]**Can I take a team below the city?**
>
> > "Three operatives for one coordinated operation, with two days' notice. Give me the objective, place and hour. They meet at the surface point you name, or at the Yawning Portal before a descent. They leave when that operation ends; they aren't a standing guard."

> [!qna]**Can you come yourself?**
>
> > "Once for a mission you choose. If I'm free, I meet you at nine tomorrow morning at the surface departure point. If I've gone with another member, your request waits in the order it reached me; I meet you at the first nine after I return. There is only one of me."

> [!gamemaster]**A Team and Mirt's Accompaniment**
>
> Each High Harper can call on three standard 2024 Spies for one coordinated operation, once. Give Mirt two days' notice with the objective, meeting place and start time. Three available operatives arrive together at that appointed surface location, or at the Yawning Portal before an Undermountain descent. Use the shared availability ledger in **Brightcandle**, taking unused names in the order Reed, Ash, Elm, Briar, Willow and Alder, then recipient-labelled sets. Brightcandle assignments and extractions occupy the same ledger, so a field name already deployed isn't assigned again until its operative returns.
>
> The team follows the agreed objective, with its first assigned operative coordinating movement. It refuses attacks on uninvolved civilians and leaves when the objective is completed, abandoned or made impossible by losing its target. Record the use when the team begins the agreed operation; it doesn't become a permanent retinue.
>
> Separately, each High Harper can ask Mirt to accompany one mission of their choice, once. If available, he meets them at the named surface departure point at 09:00 the following day, with his equipment, and remains through that mission's resolution. Another member's request while he is away enters a queue in receipt order, departing at the first 09:00 after he returns from the preceding mission. Each member retains their own allowance, while Mirt accompanies only one mission at a time and adds ally Power only where he is actually present. He can accompany a descent and fights when the mission requires it, using his actual CR 9 statistics in the mechanics reference rather than a generic Veteran. He refuses deliberate harm to uninvolved people; a refused proposal leaves the benefit unused.
>
> Count allies actually participating in a combat. Three Spies add Power 66, 51, 45 or 24 at party tiers 1 through 4; Mirt adds 110, 85, 70 or 55. Add these to the participating PCs' Party Power before recalculating difficulty and day cost. Merely granting a rank or having a team elsewhere adds no ally Power. Mirt's HP 153, AC 16, attacks, Sneak Attack, Evasion and Parry remain the documented Waterdeep statistics, with the encounter-specific defensive role used only when that situation calls for it.

When a team arrives for its named operation, the first assigned operative coordinates its movement and addresses the member:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "We're here for the operation you named. Give me the approach and the withdrawal point. The three of us will move together, and we'll tell you if the route puts uninvolved people in danger."

If Mirt is accompanying this member, he arrives at the recorded departure point at the next available 09:00:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt carries his usual weapons and checks the street ahead before joining the member at the departure point.
>
> > "I'm here for this mission. Tell me what changed since your request, then we'll go. I won't be sent to another place until we're finished."

### Extraction and Exposure

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt moves the city map closer and leaves the pencil beside the member’s location. A separate sheet lies beneath it for evidence that could be sent to Laeral’s office.
>
> > "If you need getting out, tell us where you are and where you need to reach. If you want a faction exposed, bring the evidence you have. We'll carry it to people who can act on it."

> [!qna]**How do I call the wagon?**
>
> > "Send me where you are, a description the courier can recognize and a safe address inside the city. Two hours after I receive it, a covered wagon and three operatives come to your street or service entrance. A guarded door remains guarded; they'll work through the place as it stands."

> [!qna]**What if they cannot reach us?**
>
> > "They wait thirty minutes at a guarded entrance and make the attempt. If they cannot get you out, they withdraw and come back for another thirty minutes two hours later. A bird gives you that return time. Once you're aboard, the city drive takes twenty minutes."

> [!exploration]**Covert City Extraction**
>
> Send Mirt the member's location, a recognizable description and a chosen safe destination within Waterdeep by paper bird or an existing courier. Two hours after he receives the request, an available courier brings a covered wagon to that location's street or service entrance, with three available Spies drawn from the shared operative ledger. Extraction doesn't spend the separate one-operation team benefit. Couriers have their own availability entries: assign Harl Keen first, then Della Morn, then Oren Bell; further concurrent requests use a distinct courier named with the recipient's surname and the given name Tamsin, adding a numbered dispatch entry if needed. Mark courier and wagon assigned until that recovery ends, without sending Harl or the same wagon to two simultaneous calls.
>
> At an open location, the detail collects the member and their named companions immediately. At a guarded location, it waits at the service entrance for thirty minutes and uses the building's established entry routes and occupant responses to reach them; this benefit supplies the recovery effort without making locks, guards or custody disappear. The assigned courier then drives the actual recovered people to the chosen destination, taking twenty minutes within the city. No account of the member's predicament is demanded. If they can't be reached during that attempt, the detail withdraws to the same entrance for another thirty-minute attempt two hours later, and sends a paper bird naming that return time. The procedure applies to city locations, with an Undermountain party needing to reach a surface exit before wagon recovery.
>
> Each recovery detail uses three standard Spies, with the ally Power above if it actually joins a combat. Its courier drives and shelters during fighting rather than adding another combatant.

If the extraction detail reaches an open location, the assigned courier checks the member’s description before opening the covered wagon:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I have your destination. Tell me who is coming with you, then get aboard; we have a twenty-minute drive through the city. You can tell Mirt the rest after you're safe."

If guards or a closed route prevent immediate collection, the courier speaks from the established service entrance before the first thirty-minute attempt:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "We'll try the route you've named for half an hour. The guards and locks are still there; my three people will work with what this place gives us. If we cannot reach you, watch for a bird with the second time."

> [!qna]**Can I bring a faction before Laeral?**
>
> > "Bring the faction name and the evidence you actually have. I'll send the dossier this evening. Variel returns Laeral's office receipt at ten, three days from now. It will separate what is corroborated from what still needs proof; it won't declare anyone guilty."

> [!gamemaster]**A Formal Exposure Request**
>
> The member can ask the High Harpers to present a villain faction's activities to the Open Lord. Deliver the faction's name and the actual evidence to Mirt, who sends the dossier that evening. At 10:00 three days later, Variel returns a receipt from Laeral's office listing the submitted claims, corroborated facts and remaining gaps. Verified crimes enter the city's existing Watch investigation procedure; unsupported allegations remain allegations rather than becoming convictions.
>
> Suspicion of the Cassalanters never supplies knowledge of their pact or infernalism. Such evidence enters the dossier only if the party has actually discovered and reported it. If the named faction has fallen, the request concerns its surviving members and unresolved crimes. This Harper service is separate from the single Masked Lord invocation.

If the member submits an exposure dossier, Variel brings the receipt at the fixed 10:00 appointment three days later:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Variel lays the sealed office receipt beside Mirt’s copy of the dossier, leaving both in the member’s reach.
>
> > "Hm. The office listed what it can verify and what still needs witnesses. Read both before you decide what to send next."

> [!gamemaster]**Laeral's Office Receipt**
>
> The receipt lists the submitted claims, corroborated facts and gaps established by the party's actual evidence. Its written heading reads: "Received for review by the Open Lord's office. Verified matters proceed through the Watch; unresolved claims remain open for evidence." It doesn't identify a secret villain leader, infernal pact or Masked Lord beyond what the dossier proved.

### Mirt's Confidence and the Request

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets his glass beside the register as Variel closes the library door, then looks back across the table once the latch settles.
>
> > "There's something else you should know before you decide how to use my help. I'm a Masked Lord of Waterdeep. Laeral knows, and I work with her closely. I'm trusting you with it because we'll need to plan together."

Mirt answers questions about his own responsibilities without identifying other Masked Lords. His request for discretion is spoken below. Companions arriving for supper receive no automatic reveal; the member chooses what to tell them afterward.

> [!readaloud]
>
> He takes a fresh sheet from the drawer and writes your name at its top, then places it beside the papers you can take away. When he explains the request, he leaves it unsigned so you can return with a specific need rather than settling one over the table tonight.
>
> > "One formal request from you. Watch cooperation for an operation, proceedings against a faction, or immunity for a named person. You can hold it until you know what you need, and I'll tell you if the terms would harm the city."

> [!qna]**Who else knows?**
>
> > "Laeral knows. I won't name the other Lords for you. Keep my seat out of public accounts and ordinary dispatches; it isn't a story to prove by putting another person in danger."

> [!qna]**What does a request buy me?**
>
> > "It gets an act within the city's authority, not a verdict or command of every guard in Waterdeep. Put one operation, faction or person on the signed sheet, with the facts that justify what you need. I'll tell you if I cannot use my office for it before you spend your one request."

> [!exploration]**One Masked Lord Invocation per High Harper**
>
> The qualifying member owns one use and can invoke it now or later. Helpers may advise or participate in the resulting operation, without acquiring an additional use. The member submits a signed request naming one of these forms:
>
> - **Watch cooperation:** Identify one operation, its objective, place and date. Three days after submission, Mirt delivers a sealed instruction to the responsible Watch station, permitting access to relevant Watch records and assigning a liaison to coordinate existing Watch resources for that operation. It doesn't create a new combat squad or transfer command of the Watch to the member.
> - **Lords' Court proceedings:** Name a faction and provide the available evidence. Three days after submission, Mirt supplies the docket receipt and a hearing at 10:00 the following day in the Lords' Court. The process compels formal consideration of the evidence without deciding guilt before the hearing.
> - **A warrant of immunity:** Name one person and the specific existing offense or pursuit to be covered. Three days after submission, Mirt delivers the sealed warrant to that person and sends its notice to the Watch. It ends Waterdeep's pursuit for the named matter, preserving proceedings for other offenses and liability for later acts.
>
> Mirt declines a request that knowingly enables harm to the city, conceals an intended attack on uninvolved people or requires authority beyond Waterdeep's jurisdiction. He names that reason on receiving the request, before spending the use, and the member can revise or retain it. A valid request consumes the use when Mirt accepts it, with the fixed three-day delivery recorded then. An appointment or unspent discussion doesn't consume it.

If Mirt accepts a valid Watch cooperation request, he records the operation, objective, place and date:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I'll send the responsible station a sealed instruction in three days. A liaison can share relevant records and coordinate the Watch's existing resources for this operation. It won't put every watchman under your command or create another squad."

If Mirt accepts a valid request for Lords' Court proceedings, he checks the faction name against the evidence submitted:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "You'll have the docket receipt in three days, and a hearing at ten the following morning. The court will consider what you brought. It won't call an accusation a conviction before anyone has heard the evidence."

If Mirt accepts a valid warrant of immunity request, he writes the person's name and the existing matter covered:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "One person and one named offense or pursuit. In three days I'll send the warrant to that person and notice to the Watch. It ends the city's pursuit for that matter, not for other offenses or anything done afterward."

If a proposal would knowingly enable harm to the city, Mirt refuses it before spending the use:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I won't put my name to that request as written. It would cause the city harm, and I won't use this office to arrange it. You haven't spent your use; bring me another way to address the problem."

If it conceals an intended attack on uninvolved people, he gives the narrower reason:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "You are asking me to hide an attack on people who aren't part of this fight. I won't do it. Your use remains yours, but this request goes no farther."

If it requires authority beyond Waterdeep's jurisdiction, he names that limit:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "That order would reach beyond Waterdeep, where my office has no authority. I can hear a city request from you, but this one isn't valid and hasn't spent your use."

If the member keeps the request for later, Mirt slides the unsigned sheet into their rank folder:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Keep the use. Bring me a signed request when the need is real, and I'll give you the delivery date before I spend it."

If the member holds the request, Mirt keeps the unsigned sheet in their rank folder. If they invoke it, he records the delivery date before inviting the waiting companions to supper. Variel leaves with the sealed promotion record at 21:00.

### Renown Opportunities

The promotion grants no additional Renown, gold or Milestone Points. Each recipient records their own team deployment, personal accompaniment and Masked Lord use, while extraction and archive requests retain their stated procedures.

### Aftermath

The third persona is immediately available, with the earlier personas retained. The member can carry these benefits into Undermountain play, including an unspent invocation, without assuming that a resolved city faction has returned. Sensitive field reports continue privately through Mirt until **Harper Leak Closed**; this promotion and its confidential reveal are never entered into Orren's dispatch register.

### Concluding the Event

The event concludes when Mirt makes his reveal and delivers the third persona and support procedures. Record later uses against the named recipient rather than marking the whole party as High Harpers.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **High Harper Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Mirt reveals his seat and hands over the packet; **Vault of Dragons** and future Undermountain events read that member's accompaniment and support access.
> - **Masked Lord Request Invoked** — mark with the member's name, accepted request and delivery date only when Mirt accepts a valid invocation; **Vault of Dragons** and future Undermountain events read whether that individual's single use remains available.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Accepted invocations resolve in three days; team deployments require two days' notice and Mirt departs at the next available 09:00 under his mission queue. This is the final Harper rank, with benefits available for later quests and no Milestone Points awarded.

## Overview

A silver raven invites the qualifying Harper to Mirt's library, where he explains the support available to a High Harper and entrusts them with his Masked Lord identity. The member can make one formal request or retain it for a later need.

## Summary

The qualifying character became a High Harper, received their third persona and learned the archive, team, accompaniment, extraction and exposure procedures. Mirt revealed his Masked Lord seat privately, with the recipient's single formal invocation used only if they submitted a valid request.
