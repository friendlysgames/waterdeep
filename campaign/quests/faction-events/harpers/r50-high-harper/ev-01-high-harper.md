# High Harper

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — Mirt contacts the character within a tenday — after one party member's Harper renown reaches 50. In this Event:
>
> - A formal silver raven summons the character and their companions to Mirt's manor
> - Mirt, Remi Haventree, and Variel Duskwhisper as witness convene a small inner circle
> - Mirt names the High Harper rank and lays out the full benefits: Harper archive access for the North, a three-agent team for one operation, his personal accompaniment on one mission of their choosing, a covert city-wide extraction option, a formal request to the High Harpers to expose a villain faction, and a third *persona*
> - Mirt reveals that he is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep — information he has shared with no one in the field except Remi
> - He offers the party one formal Masked Lord invocation: Watch cooperation on a specific operation, Lords' Court proceedings against a faction, or a warrant of immunity for a named individual — to use now, or hold
> - The party must choose: invoke the request during this event, or hold it for when they need it most
>
> > [!design]**Expected in Dungeon of the Mad Mage**
> >
> > Parties are unlikely to reach Renown 50 during Dragon Heist. This event is designed to fire during Dungeon of the Mad Mage, where Mirt's Masked Lord authority carries more weight and his offer to accompany the party on a mission below the city has genuine personal risk attached to it. If **Vault of Dragons** has already resolved when this event fires, the Grand Game is over; Mirt names the extraction and immunity options as the most practical remaining uses of the Masked Lord invocation, rather than the faction-exposure option.

**Background (DM only)**

High Harper is not a rank Mirt announces. He waited until he was certain, then sent the raven. The three other Masked Lords he trusts most have never been told he operates in the field. The character has now been given a confidence he extends to no one in the Waterdeep cell except Remi.

Variel Duskwhisper (wood elf, neutral good, he/him) is present as Harper courier witness — ceremonial protocol that dates to when Harper field promotions required a neutral courier to attest. He does not speak during the ceremony and will not confirm what he heard to anyone who asks afterward.

The three-agent team is a coordinated deployment: simultaneous entry, a protective detail, or a layered operation requiring multiple operatives at once. They operate under their own internal chain of command and will not take orders that compromise the network. One operation; they are not a standing resource.

The archive access covers Harper intelligence records for the North: informant reports from the past five years, faction movement patterns, historical operations and their outcomes. It does not give access to the current identities of embedded operatives.

The Masked Lord reveal is real. Mirt's equanimity is real. He chose to tell the character because the rank has earned it, and because having a High Harper who does not know what Mirt can actually do limits what they can plan together. He is calm; the decision was significant.

### The Summons

The invitation arrives differently this time. Not a paper bird — a silver raven, carrying a letter sealed in wax with a harp device, stamped with a signet ring. The note is brief:

> *"Mirt's manor. The third hour of the evening watch. Come in by the garden gate and bring whoever you'd want at your back."*

No cipher. No cover direction. Direct.

### The Manor

The library is larger than it needs to be, full of maps still in use and books that have clearly been read. The fire has been going long enough to take the chill out properly.

Remi Haventree is already seated when they arrive. Variel Duskwhisper stands near the far window, holding his mandolin by the neck, watching the door. He nods when the party enters and says nothing.

> [!npc-narrative]**Variel Duskwhisper**
> A wood elf standing near the window with a mandolin held loosely by the neck, watching the door with the unhurried patience of someone who has been in this kind of room before. He nods once when you enter and does not speak. He looks like a musician. He is paying attention to everything.

Mirt pours wine for everyone. He does not sit down.

### The Rank

He gets to it without delay, because he has never liked drawing things out.

> *"High Harper. You've earned it. I'm not going to tell you what it required — you were there."*

He explains the benefits in order. Archive access: the Harper network's intelligence records for the North — five years of informant reports, faction movements, historical operations. Available through Remi. The three-agent team: coordinated, one operation, not a standing resource.

Then personal accompaniment. He takes a beat here.

> *"Once. A mission you choose. I am not, as you may have noticed, as young as I was. But I have been in worse places than wherever you're likely to take me, and I can look after myself."*

He explains the extraction option — covert Harper recovery from anywhere in the city, no questions asked. Then the formal exposure option: a request to the High Harpers to lay a villain faction's activities before the Open Lord.

Remi hands over the third persona documentation across the table. She built it before the meeting; she had been confident about the outcome.

> [!profile]**Running Mirt at High Harper**
>
> This is the only meeting where Mirt is slightly uncomfortable, and the discomfort is small and real. He has run field operations for decades from behind a persona of cheerful vulgarity; being seen clearly costs something, even by people he trusts. Play it as a brief pause before the reveal, a moment where he seems to consider whether to say the next sentence before saying it. The pause carries weight.

### The Revelation

Mirt sets down his glass. The shift in manner is slight but legible.

> *"There's one more thing. I don't say this to people in the field. I haven't said it to anyone in this cell except Remi."*

A brief pause.

> *"I'm a Masked Lord of Waterdeep."*

He waits. He watches the party's response. He is calm during the silence; the decision was significant, and it is behind him.

He explains what it means. The Lords of Waterdeep govern the city's highest affairs from behind masks, anonymous in principle even to each other. His public life as a moneylender is real. His Harper work is real. The Masked Lord seat is the third layer, the one that makes the other two possible, and the one he has never named to anyone carrying a sword for a living until now.

> *"Laeral knows. She put me on the Lords' Council, years ago. That's the full accounting. You're the first field operatives I've trusted with it."*

Remi does not react. She has known for years.

### The Offer

Mirt crosses his arms.

> *"The rank comes with something I can give you that I cannot give anyone else. A formal invocation of Masked Lord authority. Watch cooperation on a specific operation; Lords' Court proceedings opened against a faction; a warrant of immunity for someone the law would otherwise pursue. One request. One use."*

He is clear about the constraint.

> *"I can't use it twice — the other Lords would notice the pattern. And I won't invoke it for anything that compromises the city or embarrasses the Council. Within those limits: it's yours to spend whenever you need it."*

**The choice:** The party must decide whether to invoke the Masked Lord request now or hold it.

**If they invoke now:** Mirt asks for the specific request in writing and tells them three days. He is direct and efficient; he has already considered the likely requests and knows which ones are feasible. He is slightly relieved to have it decided.

**If they hold:** Mirt nods.

> *"Sensible. It's worth more when you know what you need."*

Either way, the formal part of the evening is over. Mirt refills his glass. Remi asks the character a question she has been holding since their first meeting. Variel plays something unhurried and does not explain what it is.

### Concluding the Event

#### High Harper Reached: True / False

True when Mirt makes the Masked Lord reveal and the character acknowledges it. This flag is read by future **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** content that references Mirt's involvement below the city, and by **Vault of Dragons** for the personal accompaniment offer.

#### Masked Lord Request Invoked: True / False

True if the party called in the Masked Lord request during this event. False if they held it. This flag is read by **Vault of Dragons** (Mirt's personal accompaniment offer carries different weight depending on whether the request is still available) and by Dungeon of the Mad Mage content where Masked Lord authority may resolve situations that cannot be solved by field work alone.

## Summary

At Mirt's manor, with Remi Haventree and Variel Duskwhisper as witness, the character was named High Harper. Mirt laid out the rank's resources: Harper archive access for the North, a coordinated three-agent deployment for one operation, his personal accompaniment on one mission, covert city-wide extraction, a formal exposure option through the High Harpers, and a third persona. Then he told the character he is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep — a confidence he has shared with no one in the field except Remi. He offered one formal invocation of that authority and asked whether they wanted to spend it now or hold it.
