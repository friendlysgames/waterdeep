# High Harper

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — Mirt contacts the character within a tenday — after one party member's Harper renown reaches 50. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive a formal silver raven summons to Mirt's manor at the third hour of the evening watch
> - Meet Mirt, Remi Haventree, and Variel Duskwhisper as courier witness in a small inner-circle convening
> - Hear Mirt name the High Harper rank and explain its full benefits: archive access, a three-agent team, his personal accompaniment on one mission, covert city-wide extraction, a formal exposure request to the High Harpers, and a third *persona*
> - Hear Mirt reveal that he is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep — a confidence he has shared with no one in the field except Remi
> - Decide whether to invoke the Masked Lord invocation now or hold it: Watch cooperation on a specific operation, Lords' Court proceedings against a faction, or a warrant of immunity for a named individual
>
> #### Background
>
> High Harper is not a rank Mirt announces. He waited until he was certain, then sent the raven. The three other Masked Lords he trusts most have never been told he operates in the field. The character has been given a confidence he extends to no one in the Waterdeep cell except Remi.
>
> Variel Duskwhisper (Neutral Good, wood elf, he/him) is present as Harper courier witness — ceremonial protocol that dates to when Harper field promotions required a neutral courier to attest. He does not speak during the ceremony and will not confirm what he heard to anyone who asks afterward.
>
> The three-agent team is a coordinated deployment: simultaneous entry, a protective detail, or a layered operation requiring multiple operatives at once. They operate under their own internal chain of command and will not take orders that compromise the network. One operation; they are not a standing resource.
>
> The archive access covers Harper intelligence records for the North: informant reports from the past five years, faction movement patterns, historical operations and their outcomes. It does not include the current identities of embedded operatives.
>
> The Masked Lord reveal is real. Mirt's equanimity is real. He chose to tell the character because the rank has earned it, and because having a High Harper who does not know what Mirt can actually do limits what they can plan together.
>
> #### Expected in Dungeon of the Mad Mage
>
> Parties are unlikely to reach Renown 50 during Dragon Heist. This event is designed to fire during Dungeon of the Mad Mage, where Mirt's Masked Lord authority carries more weight and his offer to accompany the party on a mission below the city has genuine personal risk attached to it. If **Vault of Dragons** has already resolved when this event fires, the Grand Game is over; name the extraction and immunity options as the most practical remaining uses of the Masked Lord invocation, rather than the faction-exposure option.

### The Summons

The invitation arrives differently this time. Not a paper bird — a silver raven carrying a letter sealed in wax with a harp device and stamped with a signet ring. The note is brief.

> [!readaloud]
> The silver raven arrives at Trollskull Manor with a letter sealed in wax, stamped with a signet ring alongside the harp device. The note inside asks you to come to Mirt's manor at the third hour of the evening watch, enter through the garden gate, and bring whoever you would want at your back. It carries no cipher, no cover direction, and no return address.

### The Manor

The library at Mirt's manor is larger than it needs to be, full of maps still in use and books that have clearly been read. The fire has been going long enough to take the chill out properly.

> [!readaloud]
> The library is larger than you expected. Maps cover most of the wall space alongside books that have clearly been read, and the fire has been burning long enough that the room has a proper warmth to it. Remi Haventree is seated near the hearth with a glass of wine. A wood elf stands near the far window holding his mandolin loosely by the neck and watching the door. When you come in, he nods once and says nothing.
>
> Mirt pours wine for everyone and does not sit down.
>
> > "Sit. This won't take long, and then we can talk about something else."

> [!gamemaster]**Variel Duskwhisper**
>
> Variel Duskwhisper (Neutral Good, wood elf, he/him) is present as ceremonial courier witness, a Harper protocol that predates most current cell structures. He stands near the window with his mandolin and watches the door with the unhurried patience of someone who has been in this kind of room before. He nods when the party enters and does not speak during the ceremony. He will not confirm to anyone afterward what he heard in this room.

### The Rank

Mirt gets to it without delay.

> [!readaloud]
> > "High Harper. You've earned it. I'm not going to tell you what it required — you were there."
>
> He starts with the archive: Harper intelligence records for the North, five years of informant reports, faction movements, and historical operations, all available through Remi at any time. He moves to the three-agent team, a coordinated deployment for one operation rather than a standing resource, and pauses before what comes next.
>
> > "Once. A mission you choose. I am not, as you may have noticed, as young as I was. But I have been in worse places than wherever you're likely to take me, and I can look after myself."
>
> He explains the extraction option (covert Harper recovery from anywhere in the city, no questions asked), and then the formal exposure option: a request to the High Harpers to lay a villain faction's activities before the Open Lord.
>
> Remi reaches across the table with the third persona documentation. She had prepared it before the meeting; she had been confident about the outcome.

> [!social]**The Old Wolf**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan human, he/him) :: a moneylender and Harper operative; at High Harper, slightly uncomfortable in a way that is small and real.
>
> This is the only meeting where Mirt is not fully at ease. He has run field operations for decades from behind a persona of cheerful vulgarity; being seen clearly costs something, even by people he trusts. The discomfort shows as a brief pause before the Masked Lord reveal — a moment where he seems to consider whether to say the next sentence before saying it.
>
> The offer of personal accompaniment is made plainly and without bravado. The pause before it is real, and it is not theatrical.
>
> Conversation topics Mirt is willing to discuss include:
> - The archive access and how to request records through Remi
> - The three-agent team and how to deploy them
> - What personal accompaniment means and what it does not
> - The extraction option and when it applies
> - The formal exposure request and what it can achieve
> - What the Masked Lord invocation covers and what it cannot do
>
> Specific dialogue for Mirt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**About the archive access?**
>
> > "Five years of field reports for the North — faction movements, informant intelligence, historical operations and what they produced. Remi has the keys. She'll pull what you need when you ask for it." He considers for a moment. "There are no current operative identities in there. That does not change."

> [!qna]**About the three agents?**
>
> > "Coordinated deployment. Three operatives moving at once — simultaneous entry, a layered operation, a protective detail. One operation, not a standing resource. They take their orders from their own chain first; they will not do anything that compromises the network. But they're good, and they will work." He refills the nearest glass. "Two days' notice."

> [!qna]**About coming with us?**
>
> He is quiet for a moment.
>
> > "Once. You choose the mission and I come along. I have been in worse places than wherever you're planning, and I have not survived this long by being careless. Choose carefully, because you only get the one."

### The Revelation

Mirt sets down his glass. The shift in manner is small but legible.

> [!readaloud]
> > "There's one more thing. I don't say this to people in the field. I haven't said it to anyone in this cell except Remi."
>
> He takes a breath before saying the next part.
>
> > "I'm a Masked Lord of Waterdeep."
>
> He waits, watching the room, and keeps his hands flat on the table while whatever silence follows runs its course.

He explains what it means: the Lords of Waterdeep govern the city's highest affairs from behind masks, anonymous in principle even to each other. His public life as a moneylender is real. His Harper work is real. The Masked Lord seat is the third layer, the one that makes the other two possible, and the one he has never named to anyone carrying a sword for a living until now.

> [!readaloud]
> > "Laeral knows. She put me on the Lords' Council, years ago. That's the full accounting. You're the first field operatives I've trusted with it."
>
> Remi's expression does not change; she has known this for years, and she is not surprised by it being said aloud.

### The Offer

> [!readaloud]
> Mirt crosses his arms and leans back against the bookcase behind him.
>
> > "The rank comes with something I can give you that I cannot give anyone else. A formal invocation of Masked Lord authority. Watch cooperation on a specific operation; Lords' Court proceedings opened against a faction; a warrant of immunity for someone the law would otherwise pursue. One request. One use."
>
> He names the constraint before anyone asks.
>
> > "I can't use it twice — the other Lords would notice the pattern. And I won't invoke it for anything that compromises the city or embarrasses the Council. Within those limits: it's yours to spend when you need it."

The party must decide whether to invoke the request now or hold it.

If the party invokes the request now, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Mirt asks for the specific request in writing and says three days. He is direct about the mechanics, and something in his manner settles slightly once the decision is spoken.

If the party holds the request, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> He gives a single nod and reaches for his glass.
>
> > "Sensible. It's worth more when you know what you need."

Either way, the formal part of the evening is over. Mirt refills his glass. Remi asks the character a question she has been holding since their first meeting. Variel plays something unhurried near the window and does not explain what it is.

### Concluding the Event

The evening winds down on its own terms. No ceremony for the close — Mirt never liked those.

## Overview

A silver raven — sealed with wax and a signet ring — arrives at Trollskull Manor requesting the character's presence at Mirt's manor at the third hour of the evening watch. Remi Haventree and Variel Duskwhisper are already there when the party arrives. Mirt names the High Harper rank, explains what it grants, and then tells the character something he has told no one in the field except Remi: he is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep. He offers one formal invocation of that authority, to use now or hold for when it is needed most.

## Summary

At Mirt's manor, with Remi Haventree and Variel Duskwhisper as witness, the character was named High Harper. Mirt laid out the rank's resources: Harper archive access for the North, a coordinated three-agent deployment for one operation, his personal accompaniment on one mission, covert city-wide extraction, a formal exposure option through the High Harpers, and a third persona. Then he told the character he is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep — a confidence he has shared with no one in the field except Remi. He offered one formal invocation of that authority and asked whether they wanted to spend it now or hold it.
