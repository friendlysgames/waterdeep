# Design Notes: High Harper

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B lists the High Harper rank as archives, three agents and Mirt accompanying a mission. The Factions Guide adds a covert city extraction, a formal exposure request, a third persona and the Masked Lord reveal "if the party has not already worked it out". Neither says how any of it works, so each benefit is now a per-member procedure with a contact, a notice, a delivery and a limit. Mirt appears as a Masked Lord and a close advisor of Laeral in Waterdeep Dragon Heist, and the remix keeps that and turns it into a secret that he tells at this rank.

## What Changed

The restored draft had Mirt say that Laeral "put me on the Lords' Council". No source says so, so he now says "Laeral knows" and nothing more, and he names no other Lord. A member who guessed earlier, through Mirt's habit of calling her by her first name in **Wise Owl**, can say so at the table and loses nothing. The meeting is at 20:00 on the first day the member is in Waterdeep, and Remallia attends only if **Remallia Harper Contact Known** is marked. Variel plays the fiddle in the corner and leaves before the reveal.

The exposure dossier takes only evidence the party has found, which keeps Cassalanter suspicion from becoming knowledge. Splinter wording follows **Manshoon Named**. The invocation has three forms, a three-day delivery and three refusals that Mirt gives before the use is spent, so a refused request costs nothing. Mirt uses the Waterdeep Dragon Heist CR 9 record from the mechanics reference. The team of three Spies and the Power totals come from the same file.

## Departures and Renown

The restored draft let a party hold the invocation without limit and gave Mirt a "third persona" with no detail. The persona now matches the second one from **Wise Owl**: Vale & Reed Imports, two vouching contacts, a plain Advantage and no legal weight. The six missions bring a member to Renown 19 on their base awards, so Renown 50 needs about thirty-one more from the Earning Renown list and from **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. The rank is meant for that book, and this Event awards no Renown.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: the couriers Della Morn and Corin Bell (Wil Keen comes from **Wise Owl**), the one-topic-a-day archive limit, the one-dossier-per-faction limit, the Yawning Portal as the wagon's pickup for a party underground, and the third persona's two vouchers, Maera Brandt (rope-maker, 14 Wickerwork Row, South Ward) and Tomas Ardel (glazier, 3 Mender's Court, Castle Ward). Nella Fen, Orin Dask and Ilen Castor already vouch for the first two personas, so the third uses neither them nor their wards. Corin Bell was renamed from Oswin Bell so he can't be confused with Chef Oswin Barr in the Trollskull Manor staff guide. The three-Spy team is unnamed.

Logged for the out-of-scope notes: the mechanics reference holds Mirt's block, and the Bestiary does not yet. **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) and **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** must read **High Harper Reached** and **Masked Lord Request Invoked**. Appendix B says the Harpers know the Cassalanters are Asmodean cultists, which this Event ignores. Waterdeep Dragon Heist says Mirt is "least concerned" with hiding his identity, and the remix overrides that on purpose.
