# Design Notes: High Harper

## Personal Confidence and Individual Benefits

The rank's archive, team, accompaniment, extraction, exposure and third-persona benefits all remain, with their named procedures and timings. Each qualifying member owns their own resources and invocation. Companions can take part in an operation, and that gives them neither faction membership nor a second use.

The reveal keeps Mirt's Masked Lord identity and his advisory relationship with Laeral. We removed the unsupported claim that she appointed him, along with any claim that he is anonymous to his fellow Lords, since they would obviously know him. Remallia's part in the meeting still follows the salon identity outcome, so an unusually early promotion doesn't reveal her Harper role ahead of that event.

## Support and City Authority

Teams use standard 2024 Spies, and Mirt keeps his actual Waterdeep CR 9 statistics. Tier-adjusted ally Power applies to allies who take part in a fight, and remote support adds none. The extraction procedure sends a real recovery team and repeats the attempt once, and the obstacles and occupants of the location being entered stay as they are.

The Masked Lord invocation starts a specified civic action with a three-day response. It doesn't guarantee a verdict or erase future offenses. Holding the invocation keeps the member's use, and a request Mirt declines as harmful doesn't spend it. Faction exposure draws on evidence the party has actually discovered, which protects Cassalanter secrecy and respects the aftermath of defeated factions.
