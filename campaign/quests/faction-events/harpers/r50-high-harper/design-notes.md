# Design Notes: High Harper

## Personal Confidence and Individual Benefits

The existing rank's archive, team, accompaniment, extraction, exposure and third-persona benefits remain available, with named procedures and timings. Each qualifying member owns their own resources and invocation; companions can participate without receiving faction membership or another use.

The reveal preserves Mirt's Masked Lord identity and his advisory relationship with Laeral. The unsupported claim that she appointed him is removed, along with categorical anonymity claims that conflict with his trusted fellow Lords. Remallia's participation still follows the salon identity outcome, so an unusually early promotion doesn't reveal her Harper role ahead of that event.

## Support and City Authority

Teams use standard 2024 Spies, while Mirt retains his actual Waterdeep CR 9 statistics. Tier-adjusted ally Power is supplied for participating allies, without counting remote support as combatants. The extraction procedure provides a real recovery team and repeat attempt while retaining the obstacles and responses in the location being entered.

The Masked Lord invocation initiates a specified civic action with a three-day response, rather than guaranteeing a verdict or erasing future offenses. Holding it preserves the individual's use; declined harmful requests don't spend it. Faction exposure uses evidence actually discovered, maintaining Cassalanter secrecy and the consequences of defeated factions.
