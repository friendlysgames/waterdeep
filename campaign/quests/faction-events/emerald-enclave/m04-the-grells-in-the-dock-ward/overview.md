# The Grells in the Dock Ward: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Emerald Enclave member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level, after **The Doppelganger Problem**. Companions can help with everything except Melannor's brief and debrief, which only members attend.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 5th-level characters.*
>
> The fight is a Standard encounter against two **Grells**, with two **Tough** lookouts added at five characters. The **Emerald Enclave Mechanics Reference** audits the rosters for three, four and five characters, the flight at half hit points and the fight under a 30-foot ceiling.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Emerald Enclave members who report to Melannor.

## Hook

At 08:00 Melannor Fellbranch comes to Trollskull Manor himself, with mud on his boots and a folded sketch in his hand. Three people have vanished from Pier 17 on three nights, and he believes the third is still alive.

## Background

A crew from Xanathar's Guild has been cutting a cargo tunnel under the south quay for a season. A tenday ago it broke into a passage from Undermountain, and two grells flew up through the breach. They nested in a closed warehouse at the end of the quay, and the Guild did not drive them out, because they keep the Watch and the quay hands away from Pier 17.

A well-dressed Guild agent paid two dockhands to leave the pier on the night Mirsa was taken, so that the boats under it could load without witnesses, and Mirsa overheard the payment before a grell found her.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The grells came up from the Grell Hideout on Level 1 of Undermountain. A grell that escapes goes back down the same way, and **Autumnreaver** reads that.
> - The first two victims, a night porter and a net-mender, are dead in the nest. Mirsa is alive only because the grells have not yet fed on her.
> - The Guild agent does not know that Mirsa heard him, and the Guild does not know that the Enclave has noticed Pier 17.
> - Melannor does not know any of this, and he never learns the agent's name. He knows the grells, the three victims and the smell of lightning.

## The Brief

Melannor tells the members about the three victims in the taproom, and he explains why he came himself. He gives them his sketch and says he will walk with them to the warehouse door and no farther.

## The Search

Three leads each name the warehouse, and any one of them is enough. The party can question the harbor master's aide and the pier hands, read the smell and the missing cats on the south quay, or match Melannor's sketch to a roofline.

## The Nest

Two grells hold a closed salt-store with a 30-foot ceiling. Mirsa lies bound on the cargo-hoist platform, and the second grell stays on her until the party disturbs it. A grell reduced to half its hit points flees through the open loading leaf, and a character can bar the leaf to stop it.

## Phaulkonmere

Jeryth Phaulkon gives a *charm of heroism* to each character who fought. Melannor takes the members' report in the garden, and companions wait in the outer garden.

## Pier 17

Mirsa describes the two dockhands, the well-dressed man and the boats under the pier. The party tells nobody, tells Melannor alone, or tells Melannor and the Watch.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Melannor, whether Mirsa lived or not. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** Mirsa is freed before either grell flees.
- **+1 Renown:** both grells are destroyed.
- **+1 Renown:** Mirsa's account of Pier 17 is reported to Melannor and to the Watch.

Companions who are not Emerald Enclave members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Mirsa Rescued** is read by **The Fouled Channel**, where Melannor mentions her in a line. If a grell escaped, Melannor reports two days later that it went under the south quay, and **Autumnreaver** reads **Grell Escaped** for its Dock Ward route. **Pier 17 Investigated** is read by **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) as a casing aid at X4. **The Fouled Channel** becomes available when an individual Emerald Enclave member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave): briefs the members in person, holds the warehouse door and hears the report.
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (Emerald Enclave): speaks from the oldest oak and gives the *charm of heroism*.
- **Mirsa** (Independent): an elderly Dock Ward seamstress held in the nest, who overheard the payment at Pier 17.
- **Hesta Droit** (City of Waterdeep): the harbor master's aide, who keeps a log of complaints about the smell.
- **Garrit Moll** and **Tobiah Ashby** (Independent): the two dockhands paid to leave Pier 17.
- **The well-dressed man** (Xanathar's Guild): the agent who paid them, never named.
- **The Guild lookouts** (Xanathar's Guild): two watchers from Pier 17 who join the fight at five characters.

## Dangers & Enemies

Both grells use the **Grell** block from the 2024 *Monster Manual*. They paralyze with their tentacles, hover out of reach under the ceiling and flee at 27 hit points or fewer. A prey rule keeps a grell from finishing a creature it has Paralyzed. The two lookouts at five characters use the **Tough** block and flee when the grells fall.

## Overview

Melannor Fellbranch asks the party to find the creatures taking people from a Dock Ward pier, and he walks with them to the door of their warehouse. A seamstress may still be alive inside.
