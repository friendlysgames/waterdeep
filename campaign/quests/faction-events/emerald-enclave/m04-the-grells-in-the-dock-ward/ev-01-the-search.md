# The Search

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Exploration Event begins when Melannor Fellbranch reaches Trollskull Manor in person and ends at the door of the south-quay warehouse. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Melannor's brief in the taproom and learn who has been taken and why he did not send an animal.
> - Question the harbor master's aide and the pier hands about the three disappearances.
> - Read the smell of ozone and the missing cats along the south quay.
> - Match Melannor's sketch to a roofline, or ask the Summerstrider network for the Dock Ward.
> - Walk to the warehouse with Melannor, who stops at the door.
>
> Only Emerald Enclave members attend Melannor's brief, and their companions wait in the kitchen. Companions help with every scene after it and earn no Renown.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - A crew from Xanathar's Guild has cut a cargo tunnel under the south quay for a season. A tenday ago it broke into a passage from Undermountain, and two grells came up and nested in a closed salt-store at the end of the quay.
> - Three people were taken on three nights, which were a night porter, a net-mender and Mirsa. Mirsa was taken two nights ago, and nobody was taken last night.
> - A well-dressed Guild agent paid two dockhands to leave Pier 17 on the night Mirsa was taken. Neither Melannor nor the harbor master's aide knows this.
> - Melannor comes in person because the cats and gulls of the Dock Ward have left, and twenty-five words cannot carry what he has to say. This is the deliberate break from the animals that have carried his earlier messages.
> - He went to the Watch the day before yesterday and was told the south waterfront was not their concern.

### The Brief

Melannor reaches Trollskull Manor at 08:00, while the taproom is still being swept. He asks the members to speak with him at a table and the companions to wait in the kitchen. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The front door opens before anyone has finished sweeping, and a half-elf in plain brown clothes steps inside with mud on his boots. He looks around the taproom and counts who is there. Then he sets a folded square of paper on the nearest table and speaks in a low, level voice.
>
> > "Flying creatures have taken three people from Pier 17 in the Dock Ward, one on each of three nights. I went to the Watch the day before yesterday, and they told me the south waterfront is not their concern. The third person may still be alive."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper at the Door**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the humorless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, who has come to the party himself. No animal could carry everything he needs to say to the party.
>
> Melannor speaks in full sentences with no contractions, and his voice does not change when the news is bad. He is afraid for the woman in the nest and answers each question the moment it is asked. He calls each member by name and answers a joke by explaining why it is untrue. He walks to the warehouse and no farther.
>
> Melannor is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What grells are and how they take their prey.
> - The three people who were taken, and when.
> - Why he came in person and why the Watch turned him away.
> - Why a Guild crew would dig under the harbor.
> - Why he will not go inside.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, and he cannot say what the Guild wants under the harbor.

> [!qna]**What are grells?**
>
> > "They are flying aberrations from the deep places. Their tentacles paralyze, and they carry their prey home alive. Lightning does nothing to them, and they cannot be knocked down."

> [!qna]**Who was taken?**
>
> > "A night porter, a net-mender and a seamstress named Mirsa. She was taken two nights ago, and nobody was taken last night. I believe the creatures are keeping her."

> [!qna]**Why did you come yourself?**
>
> > "The cats and gulls of the Dock Ward have left, and an animal can carry only twenty-five words. What I must tell you is longer than that."

> [!qna]**Who is digging under the harbor?**
>
> > "My animals say a Guild crew has worked beneath the south quay for a season. The soil is uneasy there. I do not know what the crew is digging for."

> [!qna]**Will you come inside?**
>
> > "I will walk with you to the door and wait there with a cart. I have never fought anything that paralyzes, and two of us carried out would not help her."

### The Search

The south quay is a half-hour walk from Trollskull Manor. Three leads each name the warehouse, and the party needs only one. A party that follows all three also learns how the building is laid out. The party can split up to follow them at the same time.

> [!exploration]**The Pier and the Aide**
>
> Any character who questions the hands on Pier 17 and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check learns that all three people vanished near the south quay. Hesta Droit, the harbor master's aide, has logged a smell of lightning from a closed salt-store at the quay's end for three days. She adds that the building has a cargo-hoist platform above its stacks.
>
> - **Character tells Hesta the Watch has ignored her log:** The character has Advantage on this check, because Hesta has wanted someone to say so.
> - **On a failure:** Hesta is busy with a ship's clearance, and the pier hands will not talk to strangers about it. The character can try again after an hour at the pier, or leave the building to the other two leads.

> [!exploration]**The Smell and the Cats**
>
> Any character who walks the quay and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) check traces the smell of ozone and the missing cats to the salt-store. The cats that normally prowl the warehouses are gone. Overturned crates lie at the harbor-side doors, where one loading leaf hangs open.
>
> - **Character casts *Speak with Animals* on a gull:** The character succeeds automatically. The gull says the birds will not land on that roof because the air there bites.
> - **On a failure:** The wind off the harbor carries the smell everywhere, and the character cannot say which building it comes from.

> [!exploration]**Melannor's Sketch**
>
> Melannor drew the roofline of the south quay from a hired skiff the night before last, and he stayed on the water. The sketch shows four closed buildings, and only one has a gantry with a chain-hoist hanging from it. A character who walks the quay with the sketch matches it to the salt-store in about an hour and needs no check.
>
> A member can instead ask for the Dock Ward report, either by asking Melannor at the gate or by leaving a note in the gate box. The answer comes back by noon the next day, with the fixed entry from **Summerstrider** and one added line, which says the salt-store at the end of the south quay has had no cart at its door in a tenday.

If Tarsakh 20 has passed, the fixed entry also speaks of digging beneath the harbor, and Melannor says that matches what his animals reported. Before then the entry concerns another matter, and he says so.

Once the party has the building, read the following aloud as it comes into view:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The salt-store stands at the end of the south quay, a long brick building with boarded windows and a gantry jutting from its harbor wall. Gulls crowd every roof along the quay except this one. The air near the walls has the dry, sharp smell of a storm about to break. Melannor stops beside the street door and looks up at the gantry, and a hired cart stands in the road behind him.
>
> > "I will wait here. Bring her out."

Melannor stays at the door through the whole of **The Nest**. A grell that flees over the harbor crosses the quay in plain sight, and he sees it go.

### Concluding the Event

The Event ends when the party opens the street door or enters by the open loading leaf. Continue with **The Nest**.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> This Event marks no outcomes, and nothing is awarded here. **The Nest** marks **Mirsa Rescued**, **Grell Escaped** and **Pier 17 Investigated**.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Continue with **The Nest**. Only Emerald Enclave members attend Melannor's debrief, and **The Grells in the Dock Ward** awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Melannor Fellbranch brings news of three disappearances at Pier 17, and clues along the south quay lead to the warehouse where he believes the creatures nest.

## Summary

Melannor Fellbranch came to Trollskull Manor himself to tell us that something was taking people from Pier 17. We followed the smell of lightning and the missing cats to a closed salt-store on the south quay. Melannor stopped at the door, and we went in.
