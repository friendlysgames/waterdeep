# The Nest

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat and Social Event begins when the party enters the salt-store and ends when the members report to Melannor at Phaulkonmere, and in it the party can:
>
> - Cross the hall under the eyes of two grells and fight them, or draw them off.
> - Climb to the nest and free Mirsa with an action and a Strength (Athletics) check.
> - Bar the open loading leaf to stop a grell from escaping.
> - Carry Mirsa to Phaulkonmere, where Jeryth Phaulkon gives a *charm of heroism* to each character who fought.
> - Hear Mirsa's account of Pier 17 and decide whom to tell.
>
> Only Emerald Enclave members attend Melannor's debrief, but the *charm of heroism* is a deliberate party-wide gift. It goes to each character who fought the grells, member or not, and companions receive it at the oak before they wait in the outer garden.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The grells came up from the Grell Hideout on Level 1 of Undermountain, through the breach the Guild's tunnel crew cut a tenday ago. They keep the nest on the cargo-hoist platform and hunt at night, but they are awake and hungry now.
> - Grell 1 hangs in the beams above the street end of the hall. Grell 2 clings to the platform beside Mirsa.
> - Two long bundles of webbing at the foot of the stack hold the night porter and the net-mender, who are dead.
> - The two Tough lookouts at five characters are Guild watchers from Pier 17. They are paid to keep the quay hands away and know nothing of the grells' origin.
> - A grell does not finish a creature it has Paralyzed. It carries its prey home alive, so the party always has a round to answer each Paralysis.

### The Salt-Store

The party can enter by the street door at the west end or by the open loading leaf in the east wall. Read the following aloud when the first character steps inside:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The hall smells of lightning about to strike, and the dry charge of it lifts the hair on your arms. Dim light falls through the boarded windows onto crate stacks along the north wall. One leaf of the harbor doors stands open at the far end, and two long grey bundles of webbing hang against the stack. An old woman lies bound on a platform ten feet up, and she lifts her head when the door opens. Something large uncurls from the beams above you and drops toward the nearest of you.

> [!gamemaster]**The Layout**
>
> The hall is about 90 feet long and 60 feet wide, with a 30-foot ceiling and beams at 25 feet. The street door is at the west end, and the loading doors are in the east wall facing the harbor.
>
> - **The stack:** Crates stand 10 feet high along the north wall. The cargo-hoist platform on top is the nest, and it is 40 feet from the open leaf.
> - **Climbing:** Climbing the stack costs double movement, and a DC 10 Strength (Athletics) check hoists a character up it. A chain-hoist rope hangs from the gantry beam to the floor and gives melee characters a second way up.
> - **The catwalk:** A narrow catwalk runs above the loading doors at 12 feet.
> - **Altitude:** A grell hovers 10 to 20 feet up. Its Tentacles reach 10 feet and its Beak reaches 5. A grell hovering 10 feet over a character can grapple it from beyond any melee weapon, but then it cannot use its Beak. Ranged weapons and spells reach it at any height.
> - **Senses and immunities:** Blindsight 60 feet means darkness and invisibility do not help. A grell is immune to lightning, Blinded and Prone.
> - **Falling:** A grappled character who escapes, or is dropped, falls at most 15 feet and takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage per 10 feet fallen.

> [!hazard]**Two Grells in the Salt-Store**
>
> Both grells use the **Grell** stat block from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, and you use the row for the party's size, counting companions.
>
> - **Three characters:** Grell 1 fights from round 1, and grell 2 leaves Mirsa at the start of round 3.
> - **Four characters:** Both grells fight. Grell 1 starts in round 1, and grell 2 leaves Mirsa at the start of round 2.
> - **Five characters:** Both grells fight as for four characters, and two **Tough** lookouts arrive in round 3.
>
> In every row, grell 2 leaves Mirsa at once if a character attacks it or frees her. If any character began the fight at half hit points or fewer, use the step-down. At three characters grell 2 stays on Mirsa until she is freed, at four it stays until she is freed or round 3, and at five the lookouts do not come.
>
> #### Grell 1's Tactics
> To begin, grell 1 drops from the beams toward the nearest character and uses Paralyzing Tentacles on that character.
>
> During combat, grell 1:
> - Uses Paralyzing Tentacles on the nearest character who is not Paralyzed, and its Beak on a different creature within 5 feet.
> - Never makes a Beak attack against a creature it Paralyzed earlier in the same round, or against a creature at 0 hit points.
> - Hovers 10 to 20 feet up, and comes within 5 feet only when it wants its Beak.
> - Carries a Paralyzed character toward the nest with Abduct, and never rises above 15 feet while it holds one.
> - Flees when it is reduced to 27 hit points or fewer, as described below.
>
> #### Grell 2's Tactics
> To begin, grell 2 stays on the platform within 5 feet of Mirsa and attacks only a creature that comes within its reach.
>
> During combat, grell 2:
> - Leaves Mirsa at the start of the round given in the row, or at once if a character attacks it or frees her.
> - Then fights as grell 1 does, and never targets a creature that is already Paralyzed in the same round. It picks the nearest other creature.
> - Flees when it is reduced to 27 hit points or fewer.
>
> #### The Lookouts' Tactics
> To begin, the two lookouts come in from the quay in round 3 and take the catwalk above the loading doors with their Heavy Crossbows.
>
> During combat, the lookouts:
> - Fire at the character nearest a grell. A grell within 5 feet of that character counts as their ally for Pack Tactics.
> - Spend an action to reopen a barred leaf from the catwalk.
> - Flee when both grells have fallen or fled, and surrender if they are Grappled.
>
> A captured lookout says only that he was paid to watch the doors and send word.
>
> #### Fleeing and Barring the Door
> A grell at 27 hit points or fewer drops anything it holds, flies and dashes for the open leaf, and goes out over the harbor. It does not return to this fight, and **Grell Escaped** is marked.
>
> A character who closes the open leaf (Utilize, DC 12 Strength (Athletics)) removes the exit. The leaf must be closed before the fight, or before any grell reaches 27 hit points. A cornered grell fights to 0 hit points, and this is the only way to destroy both, since grells do not surrender.
>
> The fight ends when both grells are destroyed or have fled. Paralysis ends when the target succeeds on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw at the end of its turn. A member's *charm of restoration* also ends it, with *Lesser Restoration* for 1 charge. A grappled character escapes with a successful DC 12 check.

> [!gamemaster]**Drawing Them Off**
>
> No route destroys a grell without a fight, but two routes spare the party part of it.
>
> - **The lure:** A character can make a loud noise at the street end of the hall, with a thrown crate, a shout or a cantrip such as *Thaumaturgy*. The noise draws grell 2's attention for one round, and Mirsa can be freed in that round at DC 12 even if a grell stands within 5 feet.
> - **The withdrawal:** A party that frees Mirsa and carries her out of the street door can end the fight there. The grells do not follow into the street, and both go under the south quay within the tenday, so mark **Grell Escaped**.

### Freeing Mirsa

> [!exploration]**The Bound Seamstress**
>
> Mirsa lies bound in grey webbing on the platform, 10 feet up. A character who reaches her can spend an action to free her with a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check. The DC is 14 if a grell is within 5 feet of her, and a failure can be retried with another action.
>
> - **While she is bound:** Grell 2 keeps its attention on her, and freeing her ends that.
> - **Once she is free:** Mirsa is a bystander who cannot walk or fight, and a character who carries her has Speed halved.
> - **The cost:** Each action spent on her is an action not spent on the grells, and grell 2 joins the fight sooner.

When a character frees her, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The webbing parts, and Mirsa's thin hands close on your sleeve with surprising strength. She smells of brine and sailcloth, and she keeps her eyes off the beams.
>
> > "Get me down from here, love, and don't let that fucking thing near me again. I've looked at those beams for two days, and I'll be happy never to see them again."

### Out of the Salt-Store

Melannor waits at the street door with the cart. If the party carries Mirsa out alive, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor is standing where you left him, with the cart behind him and a blanket folded over the seat. When you carry Mirsa out he goes still for a long moment, then nods once and spreads the blanket across the cart bed.
>
> > "Lay her here on the blanket, please. We will take her to Phaulkonmere, where she can rest and be seen to."

If a grell escaped, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor has been watching the harbor, and he turns his head toward you before he speaks.
>
> > "One of them crossed the water and went under the surface. I will ask the gulls where it surfaced, if any gull will speak to me."

If Mirsa died, read the following aloud instead of the first branch:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor looks at what you carry, and his voice does not change.
>
> > "Then we will see that she is buried well. Lay her gently on the cart, and tell me everything you saw inside."

### The Open Gate

The cart reaches Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward in about two hours, with Melannor walking at its head. Read the following aloud when the party arrives:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The iron gate of Phaulkonmere stands open before the cart reaches it. Inside, the hedges and mossy paths run back much farther than the street would allow, and everything in the garden looks older than the houses around it. The oldest oak on the lawn is wider than the cart. As Melannor lifts Mirsa down, a woman's voice speaks from no direction, and the leaves of the nearest hedge turn toward you.
>
> > "You brought her out. Come in."

> [!social]**The Voice in the Oak**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, disembodied presence, she/her) :: the patron of Phaulkonmere, who speaks in two short sentences and lets the garden answer.
>
> Jeryth is Friendly toward the party. A warm breeze moves through the branches, and the grass lies flat where a character who fought walks. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest" and never swears. She speaks only to those she has reason to address, and she does not repeat herself.
>
> Jeryth is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The charm she has given.
> - Whether Mirsa will recover.
>
> She will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault or the grells' origin.

> [!qna]**What is this warmth?**
>
> > "A charm. Use it when you need it."

> [!qna]**Will Mirsa recover?**
>
> > "She sleeps. The garden is watching her."

> [!exploration]**The Charm of Heroism**
>
> Jeryth gives a *charm of heroism* to each character who fought the grells, member or not. A character fought if they were inside the salt-store while a grell was in the fight and attacked, defended, freed Mirsa or carried her. The character feels a warmth gather in the palm and fade, and no object appears.
>
> - **Using it:** The holder takes a Magic action and gains the benefit of a *potion of heroism*. That is 10 Temporary Hit Points and the effect of *Bless* for 1 hour with no Concentration, and then the charm vanishes.
> - **Who:** Only the holder can use it. It cannot be lent, traded or given away, and a second charm is never given.
> - **Loss rule:** The charm has no Renown condition, and a companion who leaves the party keeps it.

Members take a Long Rest in the garden under the haven rules, and companions rest with them while Mirsa is put to bed in the villa. Melannor tells the members that he will hear their report at noon tomorrow, after Mirsa has spoken.

### Mirsa's Account

Mirsa wakes at dusk and asks for the people who brought her out. Companions may listen, because her account is not an Enclave secret. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirsa sits on a bench in the kitchen garden with a blanket around her shoulders and a mug of broth in both hands. The color has come back into her face, and she looks up as you cross the grass.
>
> > "There you are. I've been waiting all day to tell somebody, and the druid keeps telling me to rest. Sit down, then, and I'll tell it straight."

> [!social]**The Seamstress on the Bench**
>
> Mirsa (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: an elderly Dock Ward seamstress, shaken and angry, who wants someone to do something with what she overheard.
>
> Mirsa is brisk and ill-tempered, and she swears when she is frightened. She was at Pier 17 delivering mended sailcloth to a brig, because its captain paid on delivery. She speaks quickly, corrects herself on small details, and asks the party what it plans to do with her account.
>
> Mirsa is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What she saw at Pier 17 before the grell found her.
> - The two dockhands and the man who paid them.
> - The boats under the pier.
>
> She does not know the man's name or whom he works for, and she will not guess.

> [!qna]**What happened at Pier 17?**
>
> > "I was waiting for my coin when a man paid two dockhands to clear off the pier for the night. It was five gold each, which is a lot to be paid for doing nothing, and he told them it was all handled."

> [!qna]**Who were the dockhands?**
>
> > "I know them both by sight, Garrit Moll and Tobiah Fenn. They drink at the tavern on the quay most nights. They took the money and went, and neither one looked back at me."

> [!qna]**What did the other man look like?**
>
> > "He was dressed too well for the docks, in a long grey coat that had never been near a fish. There was a ring on his thumb, and he never looked at the water."

> [!qna]**Did you see anything else?**
>
> > "Flat boats tied low under the pier, with men passing crates down through a hatch on a rope. Then something came out of the dark, and I don't remember much after that, shit."

> [!exploration]**If Mirsa Died**
>
> Her account is lost, but Garrit Moll and Tobiah Fenn can give the same facts. Any character who finds them at the quay tavern and makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check hears their story. It covers the payment, the grey-coated man and the boats under the pier, and putting 10 gp on the table gives Advantage on the check.
>
> - **On a failure:** The dockhands say only that they were paid to take the night off. The character can try again the next evening.
> - **The Watch:** Neither man will repeat his story to the Watch, so the third Renown bonus is out of reach.

### Whom to Tell

Mirsa asks what the party will do with her account. The party chooses among three answers, and Melannor leaves the choice to it.

> [!exploration]**Who Hears It**
>
> - **Nobody:** Mirsa goes home, the Guild agent never learns what she overheard, and nothing is marked.
> - **Melannor alone:** He thanks the party, writes it down, and sends the Enclave's animals to watch Pier 17 for two nights. Mark **Pier 17 Investigated**.
> - **Melannor and the Watch:** Melannor says the Watch will listen if Mirsa reports for herself. A character who takes her to the Dock Ward post the next morning hears her tell it to the duty sergeant, with no check. Mark **Pier 17 Investigated**, and the Watch posts two constables on Pier 17 for a tenday.

Read the following aloud at the post:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Dock Ward Watch post smells of wet wool and lamp oil, and a duty sergeant sits at a ledger behind a long desk. He looks up as Mirsa comes through the door on a stranger's arm, and she puts both palms flat on the wood.
>
> > "I was on Pier 17 three nights ago, and I heard a man pay two dockhands to leave it. Write that down, love, every word, and then tell me the Watch isn't interested."
>
> The sergeant picks up his pen.

### Renown Opportunities

The members report to Melannor in the garden at noon the next day, and companions wait in the outer garden. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor kneels in the herb bed at the foot of the oak with a handful of cut mint. He sits back on his heels when you cross the lawn, and behind him the leaves move in a wind you cannot feel.
>
> > "Please report to me. I wish to hear what you found in the warehouse, and what Mirsa said."

If **Mirsa Rescued** is marked, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor listens to the end without moving, then nods once.
>
> > "Mirsa is awake this morning and has asked for her needle case. That is all I wished to hear, and I thank you for it."

If Mirsa died, read the following aloud instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor listens to the end and looks at the bed in front of him.
>
> > "Then tell me what you learned about the pier, so that someone answers for it."

If **Grell Escaped** is marked, he adds the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "One of them is loose in the harbor. I will ask the animals where it surfaced and tell you when I know."

If both grells were destroyed, he adds the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Then the quay is clear, and the cats may return to it when they are ready. I will tell the animals so."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Melannor, whether Mirsa lived or not. Companions gain none, and each bonus can be earned only once. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:
>
> - **+1 Renown:** Mirsa was freed before either grell fled.
> - **+1 Renown:** both grells were destroyed.
> - **+1 Renown:** Mirsa's account of Pier 17 was reported to Melannor and to the Watch.

### Aftermath

Two days after the nest, Melannor walks to Trollskull Manor at 08:00 and gives the members what the animals have learned.

- **Grell Escaped:** The grell went under the south quay and into the Guild's tunnel, and the crew has stopped work on that stretch. The grell, or both if the party withdrew, goes back down to the Grell Hideout, and **Autumnreaver** reads it.
- **Pier 17 Investigated:** The animals saw two flat-bottomed boats come under the pier after midnight and take crates down a hatch by rope. Two Guild soldiers waited on the lowest stair while the boats loaded, and then the boats left toward the harbor mouth. These are the boats of the cargo dock at X4 in **Xanathar's Lair**, and if the Watch was told, the boats stayed away on the second night.
- **Mirsa Rescued:** Mirsa goes home to her shop in the Dock Ward, and **The Fouled Channel** mentions her in a line.

### Concluding the Event

The members' report to Melannor ends the mission, and the three outcomes below are read by later events.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Mirsa Rescued** — mark when Mirsa leaves the salt-store alive; read by **The Fouled Channel**, where Melannor mentions her in one line.
> - **Grell Escaped** — mark when a grell flees through the open leaf, or when the party withdraws with a grell alive. It is read by the Aftermath of this Event and by **Autumnreaver**, where the Dock Ward route leads to a grell in the Grell Hideout.
> - **Pier 17 Investigated** — mark when the party gives Mirsa's account, or the dockhands', to Melannor. It is read by **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted), as a casing aid at X4 if the lair has not run.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> **The Fouled Channel** becomes available when an individual Emerald Enclave member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level. A member who first reaches Renown 10 here also qualifies for **Autumnreaver**, and this faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

The party fights two grells in a Dock Ward warehouse and frees a seamstress from their nest. At Phaulkonmere she describes what she overheard on Pier 17.

## Summary

We went into a closed salt-store on the south quay and fought the creatures that had been taking people from Pier 17. Jeryth gave each of us who fought a charm of heroism, and Mirsa told us that a well-dressed man had paid two dockhands to leave the pier. Who was he, and what were the boats under it carrying?
