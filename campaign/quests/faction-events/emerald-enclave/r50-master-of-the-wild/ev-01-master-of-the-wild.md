# Master of the Wild

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Ceremony Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Enclave renown reaches 50. Multiple animals arrive simultaneously at the promoted character's location — more than one, more than usual, carrying no words. In this Event:
>
> - Three animals arrive at once in silence, summoning the character to Phaulkonmere.
> - The estate has been prepared: six Enclave rangers and druids are gathered in the garden.
> - Jeryth addresses the character before the assembled Enclave, names them Master of the Wild, and bestows a *charm of vitality* (supernatural gift, 2024 DMG) on every party member present.
> - Melannor presents the full operational support: six rangers and druids, and the CR 2 beast compact.
> - The event closes with one genuine choice recorded as a flag.

This rank requires sustained faction engagement through missions and Grand Game actions well beyond what the campaign's main arc provides. **Background (DM only)**

The Master of the Wild title is the Enclave's formal acknowledgment that this character has done sustained work in the service of natural balance, at scale, over time. Jeryth does not give this rank to people she's known for a tenday. The six rangers and druids gathered in the garden are not decorative: they are available for one major operation, and Melannor has briefed them.

The *charm of vitality* is Jeryth's personal gift, bestowed through Mielikki's authority. It extends to every party member present at Phaulkonmere at the time of the ceremony, once per campaign — Jeryth's way of marking the party's sustained work alongside the promoted character. She will not explain this or comment on it after. The charm settles into each person present the way the *charm of restoration* did at the First Meeting: without announcement.

The choice at the end of the event is genuine. Melannor asks whether the character will formally accept the Enclave's watch on Illuun from Undermountain — to track Illuun's reach from below, report what they find, and serve as the Enclave's eyes in the deep. Accepting does not obligate the character to abandon the party or take on solo missions. It opens Enclave support in Undermountain and makes the character the formal point of contact for whatever the Enclave learns below. Declining is also a real choice, and Melannor will not press it.

### The Silent Summons

Three animals arrive at once. A grey pigeon lands on the promoted character's window ledge. A white cat sits in the street below, looking up. A brown owl is already on the rooftop opposite, watching. None of them speak.

They wait.

Characters who open the window or step outside find the three animals watching, patient and clearly coordinated. They were sent. A DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) check is enough to confirm that all three are focused on the same character.

The animals lead the way to Phaulkonmere, each taking a different route through the city, reconverging at the corner of the Southern Ward nearest the estate. They stop at the gate and remain there when the character enters.

### The Gathered Enclave

The estate has been prepared. Six individuals wait in the garden's broadest clearing: four humans, one half-elf, one wood elf. Some are armed. All are dressed for field work, not ceremony. They stand without arrangement, at ease with each other, the self-consciousness of ceremony long past.

**Melannor Fellbranch** (Neutral Good, Half-Elf Druid, he/him) stands nearest the path. He nods once when the character arrives. Nothing else.

The six are rangers and druids drawn from the Enclave's Waterdeep network. They have worked in the city's parks, sewers, harbor margins, and the Undermountain approach tunnels. They do not introduce themselves unless the character asks. They are here for the ceremony and, after it, for whatever operation the character needs.

### Jeryth Speaks

Jeryth's voice arrives over the clearing when the character has been still for a moment. She addresses the character directly, and the six Enclave members are quiet.

> > "You've worked in this city's living fabric for long enough that I've stopped watching to see whether you'd hold. I know the answer. So do you."

She takes a moment.

> > "Master of the Wild. It means what it sounds like. Not mastery over wild things — mastery *as* a wild thing. The balance you've protected here is part of what you are now."

The garden itself responds: a slow movement through the estate's leaves, stem to tip, as if something underneath shifted in its sleep. The six Enclave members notice it. Melannor closes his eyes once and opens them.

> > "Stay. I have something to give you and your companions."

### The Charm of Vitality

Jeryth speaks to the whole of the party present — not only the promoted character.

> > "This is for all of you. You've kept someone we depend on alive and capable. That earns a debt from the wild."

Every party member present at Phaulkonmere receives a *charm of vitality* (supernatural gift, 2024 DMG). The charm settles into each person the way the *charm of restoration* did at the First Meeting: without announcement, without visible effect, without Jeryth remarking on it afterward.

A character who asks what happened gets a brief answer from Melannor:

> > "Something from Jeryth. It's better to have it than not."

This gift is once per campaign and covers only those present in the garden at this moment.

> [!tip]**The Charm of Vitality**
> The *charm of vitality* (2024 *Dungeon Master's Guide*) lets its bearer give themselves the benefit of a *potion of vitality* as a Magic action, once; then the charm vanishes. The charm is deliberately selected as distinct from the *charm of restoration* given at Rank 1 (joining) and the *charm of heroism* given upon completing **The Grells in the Dock Ward** (Mission 4) — the gift at Master of the Wild is one of endurance and natural resilience, fitting Mielikki's domain.

### The Operational Support

Melannor speaks once Jeryth falls silent.

> > "The six behind me are yours for one operation. One only. They have skills and they have limits. Tell me what you need and I'll tell you whether they can provide it."

He names the second benefit plainly.

> > "And beasts. Any beast of moderate danger that can understand a clear instruction will do one thing for you if it doesn't endanger them. This isn't a command — it's a compact. They know what you've done. They're willing."

He does not elaborate on how the beasts know. He does not know precisely how Jeryth communicates it, and he has learned not to ask.

### The Choice

The ceremony is over. The six Enclave members remain in the garden, at ease. Melannor walks with the character toward the east gate.

He stops before they reach it.

> > "There's something else. Not a requirement of the rank. A request."

He faces the character directly — an unusual thing for Melannor, who usually finds a wall to address.

> > "Whatever is sleeping below this city, it hasn't stopped. Jeryth has been managing it from above. But the reach is going deeper. We need someone inside Undermountain who will track it, report to us, and tell us where the root is anchored."

He waits.

> > "I'm not asking for a commitment you can't keep. I'm asking whether you want this. The Enclave's support below is different from what we can offer up here. We know those tunnels. You'd have contact, supply, and intelligence from inside Undermountain that no other faction can provide."

He waits for the character's answer. He does not press it either direction.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### The Character's Decision
>
> This is a genuine choice with no correct answer. Accepting opens Enclave support in Undermountain and makes this character the formal contact for what the Enclave learns below during the **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** arc. Declining is complete. Melannor nods and does not return to it. Either path has no effect on Dragon Heist.

This flag is read in Dungeon of the Mad Mage events that track active Enclave presence in the deep.

## Overview

After reaching Renown 50 with the Emerald Enclave, three animals arrive in silence and lead the promoted character to Phaulkonmere, where six Enclave rangers and druids have gathered in the garden. Jeryth names the character Master of the Wild before the assembled Enclave and bestows a charm of vitality on every party member present — a gift from Mielikki for sustained work in the city's defense. The character then faces a choice: accept the Enclave's commission to track the dreaming presence into Undermountain, or decline.

## Summary

We reached Renown 50 with the Emerald Enclave. Three animals came for us in silence and led us to Phaulkonmere, where six Enclave rangers and druids were gathered. Jeryth named the character Master of the Wild, and a charm of vitality settled into everyone present in the garden — no announcement, no ceremony, the way Jeryth does things. Melannor told us the six are available for one operation, and that beasts would help if asked. At the gate, he asked whether we'd take on a commission into Undermountain to track what's sleeping below the city. We answered.
