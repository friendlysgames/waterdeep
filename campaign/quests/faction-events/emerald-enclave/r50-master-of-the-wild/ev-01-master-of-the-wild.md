# Master of the Wild

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Emerald Enclave member first reaches Renown 50, which usually happens during **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. Three animals find the member on the surface and lead them to Phaulkonmere, where Melannor Fellbranch waits with six rangers and druids. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Be named Master of the Wild by Jeryth Phaulkon, who gives a *charm of vitality* to every character present.
> - Lead the six, three rangers and three druids under Sorrel Hartwick, on one major operation.
> - Ask a Beast of CR 2 or lower to carry out one clear task, once in each quest or on each level of Undermountain.
> - Accept or decline the Illuun Watch, the Enclave's commission to follow the thing below into Undermountain.
>
> The rank, the six, the compact and the commission belong to the qualifying member, and companions gain none of them. The charm is the one deliberate exception, and it goes to every character present, member or not.

### The Three Animals

Hold this Event until **Winterstalker Reached** is marked for the member, and hold it again until the member is on the surface. A Tiny Beast crosses between levels of Undermountain only on foot, so no animal can reach a member below ground. The animals find the member on the first morning they are above ground in Waterdeep. They wait in the Yawning Portal courtyard if the member came up through the well, and at Trollskull Manor otherwise. Every qualifying member is met on their own account, so members who surface together are met together and recorded one by one.

Read the following aloud, changing the place to match.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A grey pigeon sits on the courtyard wall, and a white cat waits on the cobbles below it with its tail curled round its feet. On the roof across the way a brown owl watches, though it is full morning. None of them moves while you cross the yard, and none makes a sound. When you stop, the cat gets up, the pigeon lifts off the wall, and the owl turns its head toward the south.

Jeryth sent the animals herself, so no *Animal Messenger* is cast and no baritone speaks. The 25-word limit does not apply, because nothing is said. Use the Raven block for the pigeon, with the Cat and Owl blocks as printed.

> [!exploration]**Following the Animals**
>
> The animals lead the member to Phaulkonmere on foot and wing, each by a different street. They meet again at the corner of the Southern Ward nearest the gate and stay there when the member goes in.
>
> - **Speak with Animals:** each animal gives the same answer, "The garden asked." It says nothing more.
> - **If the member stays:** the three animals return to the same place at dawn each day until the member walks to Phaulkonmere.
> - **Companions:** any companion may follow, and the animals pay them no attention.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jeryth sent the animals herself, because the rank is hers to give. Melannor prepared the clearing when she told him the day, and the six have waited there since dawn.
> - The six are the Enclave's working strength in Waterdeep. They keep its parks, sewers and harbor margins, and none of them has been deeper than the first level of Undermountain.
> - The *charm of vitality* is Jeryth's third gift after the *charm of restoration* and the *charm of heroism*. She goes on to say nothing about it afterward.
> - The commission is a real request. Melannor accepts a refusal, and declining costs the member nothing.
> - The Enclave has no reach below the first level. Everything it knows of the thing below comes up through the water, and Melannor cannot say where it lies beyond "below the Castle Ward".
> - The thing below is Illuun, an aboleth in the Grotto of Madness on Level 4. Jeryth learned the name when the anchor broke in **The Dreamer's Reach**. She gives it only to a member for whom **Anchor Destroyed** or **Illuun Contact** is marked.
> - If **The Dreamer's Reach** has run and **Anchor Destroyed** is not marked, Jeryth is still in the roots. She wakes a tenday after the anchor falls, so hold this Event until she does.

### The Clearing

Phaulkonmere stands in the Southern Ward. Companions walk in with the member and stand in the clearing, because the charm is theirs too. They hear every explanation and gain nothing from it. Read the following aloud when the party reaches the gate:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open before you reach it, and the garden inside is older than the street around it. The gravel path runs toward the oldest oak, where Melannor Fellbranch waits among the roots with his hands folded. Six people in field clothes stand in the clearing beside him, with bows and staves on their shoulders. They stop talking when you come into view. A woman's voice speaks from the leaves, the gravel and the air together.
>
> > "Come to the oak."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor nods once as you reach the roots. The six stand at ease along the edge of the clearing, and the pigeon, the cat and the owl settle on the garden wall behind them.
>
> > "Good morning, Master of the Wild. These six have waited here since dawn, and Jeryth wishes to speak before I explain anything."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper at the Oak**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere and a druid, who has swept the clearing and gathered the six himself.
>
> Melannor is Friendly and plain. He stands with his hands folded and speaks in even, complete sentences. He uses no contractions, never swears and answers a joke with a literal reply. He explains each arrangement once and then takes questions.
>
> Melannor is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each arrangement gives the member and what it asks in return.
> - The six, the beasts and the commission.
> - The garden and its animals.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault, the Splinter's master or the house one block north. He answers each by saying that it is not his to say. To a member for whom neither **Anchor Destroyed** nor **Illuun Contact** is marked, he calls the thing below "whatever is sleeping below" and has no name for it.

> [!qna]**What does Master of the Wild give me?**
>
> Melannor holds up one finger for each arrangement as he names it.
>
> > "Jeryth will name you and give a charm to everyone here. The six will serve you once, and a beast of modest danger will do one task for you. After that I will ask something of you, and you may refuse."

> [!qna]**Why did the animals not speak?**
>
> > "Jeryth sent them herself, and she does not use words where a sign will serve. I believe she wanted you to come to the garden without being told why."

> [!qna]**What happens if my Renown falls?**
>
> > "The rank stays with you, because you earned it and nobody can take that away. The six, the compact and the marks wait until your Renown is restored. A charm already given stays with whoever holds it."

### Jeryth Names the Rank

Jeryth speaks to the member from the oak. The six and Melannor bow their heads, and the leaves overhead turn together. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The leaves overhead stir although the morning is still, and the voice speaks from every side at once. A warm breeze crosses the clearing and lifts the hem of Melannor's coat.
>
> > "Master of the Wild. The Lady of the Forest knows your name."

> [!social]**The Voice in the Garden**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, she/her) :: a disembodied presence in the gardens of Phaulkonmere, who has chosen to name the member before the whole Enclave.
>
> She speaks one or two short sentences at a time and never repeats herself. Jeryth is Friendly and distant, and the garden answers with her through turning leaves, opening flowers and a warm breeze. She never swears and takes no side in mortal politics. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest".
>
> Jeryth is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The charm she is about to give.
> - What dreams below, which she answers in one sentence.
>
> She will not discuss the gold, the Stone, the vault or any faction. She says "That question is for the Lords to settle." to any question about the gold.

> [!qna]**Why a charm for my companions?**
>
> > "They stand beside you."

> [!qna]**What about the gold?**
>
> > "That question is for the Lords to settle."

> [!qna]**Will you speak to me below?**
>
> The leaves go still for a moment.
>
> > "No. I do not follow you down."

What Jeryth says next depends on **The Dreamer's Reach**. Read the first branch to a member for whom neither **Anchor Destroyed** nor **Illuun Contact** is marked:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The leaves stir again, and a cool draught moves across the clearing from the north.
>
> > "Something dreams below. Find where it is rooted."

If **Anchor Destroyed** is marked, read the following to each member of that party, one at a time. Nobody else in the clearing hears it.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The voice drops to a murmur that only you can hear, and the rest of the clearing goes on waiting. The roses by the path lean toward you.
>
> > "The anchor is broken. The thing that dreams is called Illuun."

If **Illuun Contact** is marked for the member, read this after the previous branch:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The leaves above you turn together, and the murmur stays low.
>
> > "Illuun has seen you. It will know you when you come near."

A companion who holds **Illuun Contact** is not told the name here. Melannor and the six hear only the naming.

### The Charm of Vitality

Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jeryth's voice rises, and this time everyone in the clearing hears it. The oak lets fall a few green leaves, and the six step back to give you room. Warmth gathers in your chest and is followed by one slow pulse, and nothing appears in your hands.
>
> > "This is for all of you. Use it when you are hurt."

> [!exploration]**The Charm of Vitality**
>
> Jeryth gives one *charm of vitality* to each character in the clearing when she names the rank, member or not. A character who is not in the clearing receives none.
>
> - **Using it:** the character takes a Magic action, and the charm gives the benefit of a *potion of vitality*. The charm then vanishes.
> - **The benefit:** it removes all Exhaustion levels and ends the Poisoned condition. For 24 hours, each Hit Point Die the character spends restores its maximum number of hit points.
> - **Limit:** one charm per character, with no recharge. The holder cannot lend it or give it away.
> - **Later arrivals:** a character who joins the party after this Event receives none, and Jeryth does not give a second charm.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 50 keeps the charm.

A character who asks what happened gets a short answer from Melannor.

> [!qna]**What did she give us?**
>
> > "A gift from Jeryth for the day you are hurt badly. It is better to have it than not."

### The Six

Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The six come forward in a loose line and stop an arm's length from you. A woman with grey in her braid leans on a staff of pale ash, and a tall wood elf beside her carries a longbow across her back. The rest are a half-elf woman in a tar-stained coat and a stout man with seed packets at his belt. A lean man with a quiver and a broad man in an oilskin coat stand at the end of the line.
>
> > "Sorrel Hartwick leads them, and beside her are Naerith Evenmoor, Ealdra Hollin, Tavish Merrow, Rolan Dace and Hob Lathen. Each of them has worked for the Enclave in this city for many years."

> [!social]**The Warden of the Parks**
>
> Sorrel Hartwick (Neutral Good, Human, she/her) :: a druid in her fifties who leads the six. She has tended Waterdeep's parks and the margins of the City of the Dead for twenty years.
>
> Sorrel is Friendly and practical. She speaks in plain sentences with contractions, and she swears once in a while when something annoys her. She treats the member as the one in command and says so before anyone asks.
>
> Sorrel is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who the six are and what each of them can do.
> - How to call them and how long they stay.
> - The routes under the city, which she has walked as far as the first level.
>
> She will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault or the Splinter. She knows nothing of Undermountain below the first level and says so.

> [!qna]**What can the six do?**
>
> > "Ealdra, Tavish and I are druids, so we hold a corridor with roots and thunder. Rolan, Hob and Naerith are rangers, so they shoot, scout and find the road, though not all six go into every fight."

> [!qna]**Who goes into the fight?**
>
> > "I go first, then Rolan, Ealdra and Naerith, one fewer than your party has fighters. Tavish and Hob keep the camp and the rear. I'll tell you if that changes."

> [!qna]**How long will you stay?**
>
> > "Until the job's done or you've been ten days below. Then we walk back up the stairs, and that's the end of it. We don't come twice, so don't waste us on shit a rope would fix."

> [!qna]**Will you go to the fourth level?**
>
> > "We'll go as far down as you take us. None of us has been past the first level, though, and nothing but our feet works down there."

> [!exploration]**Calling the Six**
>
> Each Master of the Wild can send for the six once and lead them on one major operation.
>
> - **Contact:** Sorrel or Melannor at the gate by day, or at any hour for a member who holds the east gate key (**East Gate Key Held**). A written request left in the gate box is answered by a note in the box by noon the next day. A member below ground cannot send for the six and must surface first.
> - **The request:** the member names the objective, the place and the hour. A request with no named objective is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Notice:** three days. A request with less notice is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **The place:** the Yawning Portal courtyard or a surface point in Waterdeep. The whole company arrives together, and it goes as far down as the member takes it.
> - **Fighting allies:** the six fight as one fewer than the member's party. Three PCs get Sorrel and Rolan, four add Ealdra, and five add Naerith. The rest keep the camp and the rear and do not fight.
> - **Duration:** the operation ends when the objective is met or abandoned, or after ten days below ground. The six then walk back up the stairs.
> - **Limit:** one operation for the member, spent when the six begin it. A request withdrawn before then is not spent, and a fallen member of the six is not replaced.
> - **Orders:** the six take orders from the member who called them and from nobody else. A companion cannot call them.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who orders the six to harm the Watch, a Lord or a creature that has surrendered ends the operation. The six leave at once, and the use is spent.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 50 keeps the rank, and the six wait until the Renown is restored. A spent operation stays spent.

> [!gamemaster]**The Six as Allies**
>
> Sorrel Hartwick, Ealdra Hollin and Tavish Merrow use the 2024 **Druid** block, as printed for Melannor and without his traits. Rolan Dace, Hob Lathen and Naerith Evenmoor use the **Scout** block. Sorrel, Tavish, Rolan and Hob are human, Ealdra is a half-elf and Naerith is a wood elf.
>
> Ally Power counts in full for the allies who fight, and the first-turn knockout bonus does not apply to them. For a party at 8th level, add the totals below to Party Power before recalculating the difficulty and the day cost:
>
> - Three PCs: Sorrel and Rolan add 35, for a Party Power of 167. The Bruising budget is 100.2 and the Bloody budget is 125.25.
> - Four PCs: Sorrel, Rolan and Ealdra add 58, for a Party Power of 234. The Bruising budget is 140.4 and the Bloody budget is 175.5.
> - Five PCs: Naerith joins them, which adds 70 for a Party Power of 290. The Bruising budget is 174 and the Bloody budget is 217.5.
> - From level 11, a Druid is worth 19 and a Scout 7, so the three rows add 26, 45 and 52.
>
> No druid reaches twice the Power of an 8th-level character, so the High-Power Warning does not apply. Either rebuild the fight around the larger total or keep the base budget of 79.2, 105.6 or 132 and treat the six as a safety net.
>
> #### The Six's Tactics
>
> The six stay close to the member and work to the member's plan. During combat, the six:
>
> - Have the druids open with *Entangle* on the largest group, then use *Thunderwave* and hold *Moonbeam* on a group, and fire Verdant Wisp from range.
> - Have the rangers shoot from cover with the Longbow and call out the shortest safe route.
> - Have each druid withdraw at 22 Hit Points and each ranger at 8.
> - Never cast a spell that would reshape the dungeon's walls, floors or ceilings, because that magic fails in Undermountain.
>
> The six withdraw to the nearest safe exit if two of them are down, and they do not leave the member behind. They do not fight the Watch, and they do not kill a captive.

### The Beast Compact

Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor walks to the garden wall, where a fox and a heron have been watching the clearing from the flower beds. The fox gets up and comes to his boots, and the heron turns its head to follow.
>
> > "Any beast of modest danger that can follow a plain instruction will do one task for you, if it does not put the beast in danger. This is a compact, and it does not need a command. They know what you have done in this city."

> [!qna]**Why do the beasts help?**
>
> > "The Lady of the Forest asked them, and they remember what you did for the city. I do not know how Jeryth tells them, and I have learned not to ask."

> [!qna]**Will the beast fight for me?**
>
> > "It will fight beside you until it is hurt badly. It will not die for you, and I would not ask it to."

> [!exploration]**The Beast Compact**
>
> Each Master of the Wild can ask a wild or trained Beast to carry out one clear task. The compact is separate from the paddock beast of Autumnreaver, and a member can use both.
>
> - **Who:** any Beast of CR 2 or lower that the member can instruct. A Celestial, an Aberration or a Monstrosity is not a Beast, so the compact does not reach the Giant Eagle.
> - **The instruction:** the member speaks to the beast with *Speak with Animals*, or makes the task clear with a **DC 11 Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check. A failed check can be tried again after 1 minute.
> - **The task:** one task the beast can finish within 8 hours of beginning it. Examples are guiding the party along a route, scouting a corridor, guarding a door or carrying an item to a place on its own level.
> - **Danger:** the beast refuses a task that sends it against a creature of higher CR than its own. When the task is a fight, it fights until it falls to half its Hit Points and then flees.
> - **Limit:** one task per member in each quest, counted as the sprig is counted, and one more on each level of Undermountain the member enters. The task is spent when the beast begins it.
> - **Undermountain:** the beast never leaves its own level, so it cannot be asked to cross to another.
> - **Who else:** companions benefit from the task and cannot ask for it.
> - **Loss rule:** an order to attack the Watch, a Lord or a creature that has surrendered is refused, and the task is spent.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 50 keeps the rank, and the compact waits until the Renown is restored.
>
> A beast that fights adds its Power only while it fights. A CR 2 beast adds 23 at levels 5 to 10, and a CR 1 beast adds 17. A CR 1/2 beast adds 12, and a CR 0 beast adds 1. From level 11, the figures are 19, 15, 7 and 0. A task that avoids combat adds nothing.

### The Illuun Watch

Melannor asks to speak with the member alone. Companions wait by the oak, and the six go back to the clearing. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor walks with you along the gravel toward the east gate and stops before you reach it. The garden is quiet, and the animals on the wall have turned to watch him. He faces you directly, which he rarely does, and speaks in the same even voice as before.

Read the branch that matches the member. If neither **Anchor Destroyed** nor **Illuun Contact** is marked, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I have one request, and it is not a condition of the rank. Whatever is sleeping below this city has not stopped. Our reach ends at the first level, and yours does not. Will you follow it down and tell us where it is rooted?"

If **Anchor Destroyed** is marked, read the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I have one request, and it is not a condition of the rank. Illuun has not stopped, though the anchor is broken. Our reach ends at the first level, and yours does not. Will you follow it down and tell us where it is rooted?"

> [!qna]**What does the Enclave want?**
>
> > "You would watch for the thing's reach, mark what you find and report to me when you come up. I would not ask you to leave your companions or to go alone."

> [!qna]**Where is it?**
>
> > "Below the Castle Ward, and deeper than any of us has gone. Neither Jeryth nor I can say more."

> [!qna]**What do I get?**
>
> > "Three signs to mark on stone in the first three levels. A courier will answer each one. Below the third level, the Enclave cannot reach you."

> [!qna]**What if I refuse?**
>
> > "Then you refuse, and nothing else changes. I will not raise it again."

If the member accepts, mark **Illuun Watch Accepted** with the member's name and read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor takes a folded slip of oiled paper from inside his coat and places it in your hand. Three marks are drawn on it in charcoal, a circled leaf, a leaf over an arrow and a leaf over a drop of water.
>
> > "Thank you. Scratch these on stone and a courier will come to you. Please visit the gate when you are above ground, and tell me what you have seen."

If the member declines, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor nods once and puts his hands together again.
>
> > "I understand. The gate is open to you whenever you wish, and I will not raise this again."

> [!exploration]**The Three Marks**
>
> A member who accepts holds the slip of oiled paper. The member can scratch or chalk a mark on stone on Levels 1 to 3 of Undermountain, and an Enclave courier answers it.
>
> - **Contact:** the mark itself. The courier is a ranger of the network (Scout block) who comes alone and does not fight.
> - **The circled leaf (Supply):** the courier brings a tenday of rations and water for up to six people, and a healer's kit for each of them.
> - **The leaf over an arrow (Message):** the courier takes up to 25 words to Melannor at the gate and brings the reply back.
> - **The leaf over a drop (Water):** the member leaves a sample of water at the mark. The courier takes it to Melannor, who answers that a sample from the Level 4 river or lake is "tainted" and any other sample is "clean".
> - **Delay:** the courier arrives two days after the mark on Level 1, four days on Level 2 and six days on Level 3. It waits one day at the mark and then leaves. A Message or Water reply takes the same time again.
> - **Limit:** one courier per member in each tenday. Below Level 3 the marks do nothing, because couriers cross levels on foot only.
> - **Who else:** only the member can call a courier. A companion can scratch a mark, and nobody answers it.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who leads an enemy to a mark or a courier loses the marks for a tenday.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 50 keeps the slip, and the courier does not answer until the Renown is restored.

### Leaving Phaulkonmere

Melannor opens the east gate for the member and the companions. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The six walk back toward the clearing in twos, and the pigeon lifts from the wall and crosses the roofs. The owl follows it, and the cat goes up the street toward the north. Behind you the leaves of the oldest oak turn over together, and the voice speaks once more from the warm air at your back.
>
> > "Rest here."

### Mad Mage Seeds

> [!gamemaster]**Seeds for Dungeon of the Mad Mage**
>
> These are one-line facts. *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* has no Emerald Enclave content, so use these and no others.
>
> - Illuun holds the Grotto of Madness on Level 4 (area 16) with three chuul. An intangible projection of it meets intruders in area 15 and urges them to leave. A character who holds **Illuun Contact** is sensed on approach to Level 4.
> - Halaster left his mark on the grotto, where he turned an otyugh to stone on the island in area 16a. He lured the kuo-toa to worship it as Bulba-Slopp, though the Enclave knows nothing of this. Halaster causes none of the surges the Enclave has felt.
> - A svirfneblin druid left Zurkhwood Grove (Level 4, area 13) a few months ago, after casting *awaken* on seven zurkhwoods. He went down the River of the Depths, and the Enclave has never met him.
> - Wyllow, a moon elf archdruid, rules Wyllowwood on Level 5 (tower in area 6), has forgotten the surface and turns violent when her forest is threatened. The Enclave has no contact with her.
> - Spells cannot reshape Undermountain's walls, floors or ceilings, so *Stone Shape*, *Wall of Stone* and *Earthquake* leave the dungeon unchanged. *Control Weather* needs the outdoors, and Jeryth's sprig works on the surface only.
> - The six, the beasts and the couriers all cross between levels on foot. The transport and *Sending* limits are recorded in **Force Grey Commander** and are not repeated here.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown, gold or Milestone Points, and the Master of the Wild has no higher rank to reach. Further Renown comes from the ordinary rewards listed on the Emerald Enclave page of the Factions Guide.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions what they were offered. The six, the compact, the commission and the marks stay with the member, and Sorrel and Melannor turn away a companion who asks in the member's name. A member whose Renown later falls below 50 keeps the rank, and the six, the compact and the marks are suspended until the Renown returns.

Record separately for each member the date the six were called and the quest or level of each compact task. Also record the date of the last courier and whether the commission was accepted, and note by name which characters received a charm.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has been named, has heard how the six and the compact work and has answered Melannor's request.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Master of the Wild Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Jeryth names the rank in the clearing. Read by **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** (unconverted) content that checks the member's Emerald Enclave standing.
> - **Illuun Watch Accepted** — mark with the member's name when they accept Melannor's request. Read by **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** (unconverted), where the three marks and the Enclave's interest in Level 4 apply.
>
> This Event also reads **Winterstalker Reached** (the hold) and **East Gate Key Held** (the gate hours). It reads **Anchor Destroyed** and **Illuun Contact** for what Jeryth says and whether Melannor names the thing below.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> The six, the compact and the marks are active for this member from the next morning, and the marks work only if **Illuun Watch Accepted** is marked. This is the final Emerald Enclave rank, and its benefits stay available for later quests. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Three silent animals lead an Emerald Enclave member to Phaulkonmere, where Jeryth names them Master of the Wild and gives everyone present a charm of vitality. Melannor then offers the six, the beast compact and a commission into Undermountain.

## Summary

We were named Masters of the Wild after three silent animals led us to Phaulkonmere. Jeryth gave every one of us in the clearing a charm of vitality. Sorrel Hartwick and five other rangers and druids will serve us on one operation, and any beast of modest danger will do one task for us. Melannor asked each of us to watch the thing below the city, and we gave him our answers.
