# Master of the Wild

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Ceremony Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Enclave renown reaches 50. Three animals arrive simultaneously at the promoted character's location in silence, summoning them to Phaulkonmere. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Follow three silent animals to Phaulkonmere and find six Enclave rangers and druids gathered in the garden
> - Hear Jeryth name the character Master of the Wild before the assembled Enclave
> - Receive a *charm of vitality* (supernatural gift, 2024 DMG) for every party member present
> - Learn the operational support at this rank: six rangers and druids for one operation, and a CR 2 beast compact
> - Accept or decline Melannor's commission to serve as the Enclave's contact in Undermountain
>
> #### Background
>
> The Master of the Wild title is the Enclave's formal acknowledgment that this character has done sustained work in the service of natural balance, at scale, over time. Jeryth does not give this rank to people she's known for a tenday. The six rangers and druids gathered in the garden are not decorative: they are available for one major operation, and Melannor has briefed them.
>
> The *charm of vitality* is Jeryth's personal gift, bestowed through Mielikki's authority. It extends to every party member present at Phaulkonmere at the time of the ceremony, once per campaign. The charm settles into each person present the way the *charm of restoration* did at the First Meeting: without announcement.
>
> The choice at the end of the event is genuine. Melannor asks whether the character will formally accept the Enclave's watch on Illuun from Undermountain — to track its reach from below, report what they find, and serve as the Enclave's eyes in the deep. Accepting opens Enclave support in Undermountain and makes the character the formal point of contact for whatever the Enclave learns below. Declining is also a real choice, and Melannor will not press it.
>
> #### Expected in Dungeon of the Mad Mage
>
> Parties are not expected to reach Renown 50 during Dragon Heist. This rank requires sustained faction engagement through missions and Grand Game actions well beyond what the campaign's main arc provides. This event is designed for the **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** and may fire before or after the **Vault of Dragons** resolution.
>
> If **The Dreamer's Reach** (Mission 6) is complete: Jeryth's closing remarks may reference the dreamer's surface anchor having been destroyed, and the commission in The Choice shifts focus to Undermountain's deeper levels. If **Vault of Dragons** has not yet resolved: Jeryth's remarks are forward-looking — something old is still waiting to be addressed.

### The Silent Summons

Three animals arrive at the promoted character's location simultaneously: a grey pigeon on the window ledge, a white cat in the street below, a brown owl on the rooftop opposite. None of them speak.

> [!readaloud]
> A grey pigeon has landed on the window ledge, and when you look down to the street a white cat is sitting below, looking up at the same window. The rooftop across the way holds a brown owl that had settled in before you noticed either of the others, patient in the way of an animal that has been waiting for some time. All three are watching the same person, and none of them speak.

A DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) check is enough to confirm that all three are focused on the same character. Characters who open the window or step outside find the animals watching, patient and clearly coordinated.

> [!readaloud]
> When you step outside, all three animals orient toward you at once and begin to move. The pigeon takes the air while the cat and owl find their own routes — the cat through the alleys, the owl along the roofline — and all three come back together at the corner of the Southern Ward nearest Phaulkonmere, stopping at the gate while you pass through.

### The Gathered Enclave

The estate has been prepared. Six individuals wait in the garden's broadest clearing when the character arrives.

> [!readaloud]
> Six people are standing in the garden's broadest clearing when you arrive — four humans, one half-elf, one wood elf — most of them armed and all of them wearing field gear and worn boots, standing without arrangement and at ease with one another in the way people are when they have worked together long enough that they stopped thinking about it. Melannor Fellbranch is nearest the path, and he nods once when you reach him.

The six are rangers and druids drawn from the Enclave's Waterdeep network. They have worked in the city's parks, sewers, harbor margins, and the Undermountain approach tunnels. They do not introduce themselves unless the character asks. They are here for the ceremony and, after it, for whatever operation the character needs.

### Jeryth Speaks

When the character has been still for a moment, Jeryth's voice arrives over the clearing.

> [!readaloud]
> The six Enclave members go quiet when the voice arrives, coming from everywhere in the garden at once.
>
> > "You've worked in this city's living fabric for long enough that I've stopped watching to see whether you'd hold. I know the answer. So do you."
>
> For a moment the only sound in the clearing is wind moving through the upper branches of the oldest oaks.
>
> > "Master of the Wild. It means what it sounds like. The balance you've protected here is part of what you are now."
>
> A slow movement passes through the estate's leaves from stem to tip as though something deep underneath had shifted in its sleep, and the six Enclave members all notice it together while Melannor closes his eyes once and opens them.
>
> > "Stay. I have something to give you and your companions."

### The Charm of Vitality

Jeryth speaks to the whole of the party present, not only the promoted character.

> [!readaloud]
> > "This is for all of you. You've kept someone we depend on alive and capable. That earns a debt from the wild."
>
> Something settles into each person standing in the garden — no visible effect, no announcement — the way the *charm of restoration* did at the First Meeting.

Every party member present at Phaulkonmere receives a *charm of vitality* (supernatural gift, 2024 DMG). This gift is once per campaign and covers only those present in the garden at this moment.

If a character asks what happened, Melannor answers.

> [!readaloud]
> > "Something from Jeryth. It's better to have it than not."

> [!gamemaster]**The Charm of Vitality**
>
> The *charm of vitality* (2024 *Dungeon Master's Guide*) lets its bearer give themselves the benefit of a *potion of vitality* as a Magic action, once; then the charm vanishes. It is deliberately distinct from the *charm of restoration* given at Rank 1 and the *charm of heroism* given upon completing **The Grells in the Dock Ward** (Mission 4). Note which characters were present in the garden.

### The Operational Support

Melannor speaks once Jeryth falls silent.

> [!readaloud]
> When Jeryth falls silent, Melannor turns toward the character and crosses the clearing toward them, giving the six rangers and druids a brief glance before he speaks.
>
> > "The six behind me are yours for one operation. One only. They have skills and they have limits. Tell me what you need and I'll tell you whether they can provide it."
>
> He holds the character's gaze while he says the next part.
>
> > "And beasts. Any beast of CR 2 or lower that can understand a clear instruction will do one thing for you if it doesn't endanger them — they've been told what you've done, and they're willing."

He does not elaborate on how the beasts know. He has learned not to ask Jeryth about it.

### The Choice

The ceremony is over. The six Enclave members remain in the garden, at ease. Melannor walks with the character toward the east gate and stops before they reach it.

> [!readaloud]
> He stops a few paces from the gate and faces the character, with the garden quiet behind them and the path back to the house visible over their shoulder.
>
> > "There's something else I want to ask. It isn't part of the rank."
>
> He holds the character's gaze while he waits to see that they've heard him.
>
> > "Whatever is sleeping below this city, it hasn't stopped. Jeryth has been managing it from above, but the reach is going deeper, and we need someone inside Undermountain who will track it, report to us, and tell us where the root is anchored."
>
> He holds the silence for a moment before he finishes.
>
> > "I'm not asking for a commitment you can't keep. I'm asking whether you want this. The Enclave's support below is different from what we can offer up here. We know those tunnels. You'd have contact, supply, and intelligence from inside Undermountain that no other faction can provide."

> [!gamemaster]**The Character's Decision**
>
> This is a genuine choice with no correct answer. Accepting opens Enclave support in Undermountain and makes this character the formal contact for what the Enclave learns below during the **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** arc. Declining is complete. Melannor nods and does not return to it. Either path has no effect on Dragon Heist.

## Overview

After reaching Renown 50 with the Emerald Enclave, three animals arrive in silence and lead the promoted character to Phaulkonmere, where six Enclave rangers and druids have gathered in the garden. Jeryth names the character Master of the Wild before the assembled Enclave and bestows a charm of vitality on every party member present — a gift from Mielikki for sustained work in the city's defense. The character then faces a choice: accept the Enclave's commission to track the dreaming presence into Undermountain, or decline.

## Summary

We reached Renown 50 with the Emerald Enclave. Three animals came for us in silence and led us to Phaulkonmere, where six Enclave rangers and druids were gathered. Jeryth named the character Master of the Wild, and a charm of vitality settled into everyone present in the garden — no announcement, no ceremony, the way Jeryth does things. Melannor told us the six are available for one operation, and that beasts would help if asked. At the gate, he asked whether we'd take on a commission into Undermountain to track what's sleeping below the city. We answered.
