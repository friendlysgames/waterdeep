# Design Notes: Master of the Wild

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B and the Factions Guide give Master of the Wild a *charm of vitality* for every character present, up to six rangers and druids for one major operation, and Beasts of CR 2 or lower that perform one task. The restored draft added three silent animals, six unnamed Enclave members, Jeryth's long ceremony speeches and a choice about an Undermountain commission. It described the charm as granting Advantage on Constitution saving throws, which is wrong, and it left the six and the compact with no stat blocks or limits. Its outcome **Undermountain Commission Accepted** is also the name of a Lords' Alliance rank outcome.

## What Changed

The six are now Sorrel Hartwick, Ealdra Hollin, Tavish Merrow, Rolan Dace, Hob Lathen and Naerith Evenmoor: three Druids and three Scouts, with the four humans, half-elf and wood elf the restored draft listed. They fight as one fewer than the party, and the event gives the Power totals for 8th level and from level 11. The *charm of vitality* now follows the 2024 rules as a *potion of vitality* used with a Magic action. The compact has an instruction check, a refusal against higher-CR targets and a limit, and the commission has three marks answered by a courier on Levels 1 to 3.

Jeryth's speeches are cut to one or two short sentences. Her naming line is the same for everyone, and a second, private line gives the name Illuun only to a member for whom **Anchor Destroyed** or **Illuun Contact** is marked. Melannor calls the thing "whatever is sleeping below" for everyone else. The commission outcome is **Illuun Watch Accepted**. The Event reads **Winterstalker Reached** for its hold and writes **Master of the Wild Reached**.

## Departures from the Source

The restored draft branched on whether **Vault of Dragons** had resolved, and this one branches on **The Dreamer's Reach**, because the party's knowledge of the thing below depends on that mission. The animals arrive when the member is on the surface, since a Tiny Beast cannot cross levels except on foot. If the mission has run and the anchor still stands, Jeryth is in the roots and the Event waits until she wakes. The six are called once, as the source says "one major operation", while the compact is limited per quest and per level of Undermountain, because Dungeon of the Mad Mage has no named quests.

The Mad Mage seeds are one-line facts from Levels 4 and 5 of the adventure. The transport and *Sending* limits stay with **Force Grey Commander**, and Laeral's missing agents stay with **Lioncrown**.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: the six names and species, Sorrel's age and her twenty years in the parks, and the order in which the six fight. Also invented are the three-day notice and the ten-day cap, the withdrawal points of 22 and 8 (half the blocks' Hit Points), the DC 11 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check, the 8-hour task limit and the higher-CR refusal for the compact. The courier, the three marks, the two, four and six day delays and the water-sample answer are new. So are "The garden asked.", the fox and the heron, and the fact that Jeryth learned the name Illuun when the anchor broke.

A member whose Renown falls below 50 keeps the rank and the charm, and the six, the compact and the marks pause. The Level 11 Power figures (Druid 19, Scout 7) come from the Tier 3 column of the mechanics reference. The charm texts are unverified in the downloaded data and follow the reference.
