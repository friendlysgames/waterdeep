# Design Notes: Emerald Enclave First Meeting

## An Individual Offer

The restored draft treated the cat's invitation and the offer as party-wide and marked **Emerald Enclave Joined** as one True or False for the whole party. The event now follows the Harpers, Doom Raiders and Force Grey models. Every character who belongs to no other faction is a candidate and answers for themselves, and the outcome is marked with each name. The nature-aligned note in **The Factions Come Calling** only decides whom the cat finds first. The gate is open, so there is no Offer Closed outcome and no second message. A candidate who defers walks back in later, and Melannor repeats the offer in a few sentences.

## The Cat and the Message

WDH's question ("Interested in joining the Emerald Enclave?") is back, and the restored draft's 17-word message is gone. The new message is 20 words, inside the 25-word *Animal Messenger* limit, and the count sits in a GM line under the readaloud. The cat is described once, in full, so later Enclave events can point back to it. Phaulkonmere's four constants are written once in the arrival block: the open gate, the garden older than its street, the voice with no source, and the oak as reference point.

## Secrets and Departures

Phaulkonmere moves from the Sea Ward to the Southern Ward, as WDH and Appendix B have it. WDH also puts it one block south of Kolat Towers, so a GM line records the fact and nobody points that way. Melannor's will-not-discuss list covers the Stone, the vault, the master of the other cell and the house to the north, with a written answer if a player names Manshoon. The vault gold is Jeryth's line, relayed by Melannor, with no Persuasion check, because it is a public position. The restored Stone request from Jeryth is cut, since the Stone is off limits here. Melannor's alignment is Neutral Good where WDH says Chaotic Good, to match his Notable Figures page. WDH has him assign a first mission at once, and the remix holds it back. **The Undercliff Scarecrows** arrives by its own pigeon at 2nd level.

The *charm of restoration* now has the 2024 DMG text, with 3 charges and a charge cost for each spell. The haven gets a procedure: who enters, the hours, the limits, the response-team rule and a loss rule. The renovation help replaces the manor guide's *Fabricate*, which is a wizard spell, with druid craft plus donated timber. The 250 gp and the oak-tree ask stay.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- The folder adds no new named NPCs, and its invented details are the two unnamed Enclave druids, the two-hour walk from Trollskull Alley, the eight-friend limit on the haven, the two-tenday loss penalty, the three pulses that mark the charm, the cat's tie-break order and Melannor's earlier visit to Trollskull Alley that lets the spell name it. Any of these can change without affecting the rest of the event.
- Melannor's exit line is "I will be in touch." because the brief's "I'll be in touch." contradicts his no-contractions profile, and the coordinator confirmed the longer form.
- The brief counts its suggested message as 21 words, but the message as written is 20 words.
- The 2024 DMG text of the charm gives no material components, so the event states that the charm needs none and *Greater Restoration* needs no diamond dust.
- The manor guide's line that Enclave druids replace the Watchful Order ward with monthly visits (`02-operating-costs.md:28`) is not delivered here. The brief does not address it.
- **The Factions Come Calling** (`ev-04:57`, `:103-104`) still has *Fabricate* and the True or False heading. The Factions Guide (`04-emerald-enclave.md:37`) still names a retired "Jeryth's Manner" sidebar.
