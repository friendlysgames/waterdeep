# Emerald Enclave First Meeting

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when at least one party member follows the white cat's invitation to Phaulkonmere during **The Factions Come Calling** Event. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive a verbal invitation delivered by a white cat speaking in Melannor Fellbranch's baritone
> - Walk the Phaulkonmere estate gardens with Melannor, who names the Enclave's concerns directly and without warmth
> - Hear Jeryth Phaulkon's voice carry through the garden and accept or decline an offer of sanctuary
> - Those who accept receive a *charm of restoration* settling into them with no ceremony
>
> #### Background
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon has been an estate-bound presence for decades, a Chosen of Mielikki who can no longer leave Phaulkonmere in physical form. She is present throughout the estate in a way no visitor can fix their eyes on. The invitation by white cat is standard Enclave practice; Melannor uses a cat with a permanent *Speak with Animals* effect as his preferred long-range messenger across the city's rooftops.
>
> The offer of Phaulkonmere as neutral ground is genuine and established. No faction will openly violate it. When Jeryth states this, she is stating something she has already tested. The *charm of restoration* settles into a new member the instant they accept, with no visible sign. The character simply knows something is different. Jeryth does not remark on it.

### The Cat at the Window

A white cat appears at a Trollskull Manor window one morning and waits there until someone notices it.

> [!readaloud]
> A white cat is sitting on the windowsill when someone looks that way, with folded paws and the settled composure of an animal that has no doubt it will be attended to. When you are close enough, it speaks — not its own voice, but a man's low, even baritone that comes from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > "Melannor Fellbranch, Phaulkonmere. The gate will be open when you arrive. The gardens are worth seeing."
>
> It drops from the sill before anyone can answer. By the time anyone reaches the window, it is gone.

> [!exploration]**Animal Messenger**
>
> Any character who watches the cat depart and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) check recognizes the delivery as *animal messenger*, a druid's preferred long-range communication.

### A Walk Through Phaulkonmere

The gate stands open when the party arrives. Melannor Fellbranch is just inside.

> [!readaloud]
> The gate stands open, and a half-elf in plain outdoor clothes stands just inside it, watching your approach with the patient stillness of someone who has already formed his assessment of what he is about to see. He turns without greeting and leads you in, at a pace that expects to be followed.
>
> The estate smells of wet earth and something older than the neighborhood around it , deep and green the way an old-growth forest smells, as if the ground here has been working at something longer than the house above it has stood. The paths curve farther into the property than the property seems to have room for.
>
> > "The Enclave's concern is disruption to the natural order," Melannor says, without slowing. "Scenic preservation is not the work. Someone has been releasing monsters into the city's sewers. Construction scaffolding covers four wards and sits on ley lines that have held stable for centuries. These are concrete problems that require solving."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: a humorless half-elf druid who considers the work more important than the company and does not attempt to conceal this.
>
> Melannor answers questions precisely and waits. He calls party members by name, correctly, every time. His sentences are plain and complete. He does not recognize a joke when he hears one and answers it literally when it is aimed at him. His affection for the work comes through in specificity — particular plants, specific soils, named ley lines — rather than in warmth. When the conversation approaches the disturbance beneath the Castle Ward, his answers come more slowly and stop short of finishing.
>
> Conversation topics Melannor is willing to discuss include:
> - What the Enclave's actual concern is in Waterdeep
> - The beholder operating beneath the city
> - Why he approached this party specifically, after **Finding Floon**
> - The question of the vault gold (*requires a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check*)
>
> Any character who succeeds on a DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that Melannor's pace and sentence length both change when the conversation reaches whatever is happening beneath the Castle Ward. He is not being evasive. He genuinely does not know what it is, and the not-knowing troubles him.

> [!qna]**What does the Enclave actually care about?**
>
> He says it without slowing his pace.
>
> > "Disruption. The natural order of this city has been under sustained pressure for three years through monster releases into the sewers, ley line damage from construction, and faction violence in four wards. We are not a scenic preservation society. We respond to disruptions that have real consequences for the city."

> [!qna]**About the beholder in the sewers?**
>
> > "A beholder is an aberrant creature with no legitimate place in any ecosystem. It operates beneath this city with the Guild's support. It is a practical problem that has not yet been resolved."

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> He pauses at the edge of a garden bed to examine something in the soil, then straightens and walks on.
>
> > "I observed how you handled the Xanathar sewer hideout during your search for Floon Blagmaar. I found it instructive." He does not elaborate on what the instruction was.

> [!qna]**About the vault gold?**
>
> *Requires a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check.*
>
> He stops walking for a moment and looks at whoever asked.
>
> > "The gold belonged to Waterdeep before Lord Neverember took it. It will belong to Waterdeep again when the Lords decide it does. That question is for the Lords to settle. I have no further opinion on it."

### Jeryth's Voice

Midway through the gardens, a woman's voice arrives from no fixed direction.

> [!readaloud]
> Somewhere in the middle of the estate, the quality of the air changes — a warmth that has nothing to do with the weather, and a sense of attention directed at you from everywhere at once. Then a woman's voice, clear and calm, arrives from the direction of the oldest oak, or perhaps not from any direction at all.
>
> > "You came."
>
> A pause. The leaves of the oak turn very slightly in a wind that is not touching anything else.
>
> > "My name is Jeryth Phaulkon. I cannot appear to you the way you would expect. But I am here, and this ground is safe — whatever faction has reason to move against you, none of them will do it here."

> [!social]**The Patron of Phaulkonmere**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, disembodied presence, she/her) :: a Chosen of Mielikki whose physical form is inseparable from Phaulkonmere; she speaks rarely and each sentence is final.
>
> Jeryth speaks in one or two short sentences, spaced far apart, each complete and without qualification. She does not repeat herself. She does not chatter. The garden answers along with her — a warm breeze when she is moved, flowers tilting slightly when her attention focuses. She has no interest in the Grand Game and no side in mortal politics.
>
> Conversation topics Jeryth is willing to discuss include:
> - Whether Phaulkonmere is genuinely safe
> - The disturbance below the Castle Ward
> - What she wants from the party in exchange for sanctuary
>
> Her silence on a question is its answer.

> [!qna]**Is Phaulkonmere truly safe?**
>
> > "No one will move against you here. That is not a rule. It is a fact I have established."

> [!qna]**What is the disturbance below the Castle Ward?**
>
> The leaves of the oldest oak turn toward the party.
>
> > "Something old. Patient. I have been absorbing it for months, and it is getting stronger. I do not know its name yet."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us?**
>
> > "Reports on aberrant creatures anywhere in the city. Awareness of mind-touched citizens near the sewers. And if you acquire the Stone of Golorr — do not keep it in Waterdeep long."
>
> She says nothing more on the subject.

### Accepting Membership

Jeryth's offer is plain: Phaulkonmere is neutral ground from this moment forward, available to the party and to anyone they bring through the gate. Characters who accept find a *charm of restoration* settling into them with no ceremony — no visible sign, just the certainty that something has changed. Jeryth does not remark on it. Characters who decline are still welcome to visit.

Melannor walks the party to the gate when the visit is done. If the party declines, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> At the gate, Melannor holds it open and glances back once across the estate.
>
> > "The offer stands. You know where the gate is."

If the party accepts, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> At the gate, Melannor holds it open for the party and looks back across the gardens with the same expression he has worn throughout the visit.
>
> > "I'll be in touch."

### Concluding the Event

Characters who accepted the offer walk out of Phaulkonmere as Emerald Enclave members at Renown 1.

## Overview

A white cat delivers Melannor Fellbranch's verbal invitation to Phaulkonmere, where the estate's humorless groundskeeper walks the party through gardens that smell older than the Sea Ward and names what the Emerald Enclave actually cares about. Midway through, the disembodied voice of Jeryth Phaulkon introduces herself and offers Phaulkonmere as neutral ground with no conditions attached.

## Summary

We followed a white cat's invitation to Phaulkonmere and met Melannor Fellbranch, who walked us through the estate and named what the Emerald Enclave is actually concerned with: disruption, aberrant creatures in the sewers, ley lines under construction scaffolding. Midway through the gardens, the voice of Jeryth Phaulkon introduced herself and offered the estate as sanctuary. Those who accepted membership found a charm of restoration settle into them on the walk out.
