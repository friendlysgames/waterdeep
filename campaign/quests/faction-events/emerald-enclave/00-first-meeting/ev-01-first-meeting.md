# Emerald Enclave First Meeting

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs during **The Factions Come Calling**, after a white cat delivers Melannor Fellbranch's message to the characters the Emerald Enclave wants to recruit. It begins when the cat reaches Trollskull Manor and ends when the last candidate who visited leaves Phaulkonmere. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Hear the cat's invitation at Trollskull Manor and walk to Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward, where the gate stands open.
> - Meet Melannor Fellbranch inside the gate, where he asks after his brother Talisolvanar, and walk the garden with him.
> - Ask Melannor about the Enclave, the beholder, the unease below the city and the vault gold, and hear Jeryth Phaulkon speak from the oldest oak.
> - Decide individually whether to join, with no assignment handed out today, and walk away freely if they refuse.
> - Receive a *charm of restoration* and the use of Phaulkonmere as a haven if they accept, and share the Enclave's help with the renovation of Trollskull Manor.
>
> #### Candidates and Companions
>
> Every character who belongs to no other faction is a candidate, and each candidate answers for themselves. The cat delivers its message to the first candidate who is a druid, a ranger or a cleric with a nature domain. If no candidate is, it goes to the one with the highest Wisdom score. A tie goes to the name that comes first alphabetically. The nature-aligned note in **The Factions Come Calling** only decides whom the cat finds first. Nobody is tested for alignment or class at the gate.
>
> Every candidate in the room hears the message, and any candidate can walk to Phaulkonmere whether or not the cat chose them. Melannor makes no offer to a character who already belongs to another faction. Those characters and any companions can walk the garden and listen, and they gain nothing from the visit. If no character qualifies, the cat never comes and the Event does not occur. If every candidate declines, end the Event with no membership outcome.
>
> #### What Is Actually True
>
> - Jeryth decided to make the offer before the party arrived. Melannor does not know that, and she never says so.
> - The offer has no hidden price. The Enclave wants allies who will report what is wrong with the water, the earth and the animals, and it asks for nothing else.
> - The Enclave has no stake in the vault gold. Jeryth considers the question beneath her, and Melannor repeats her answer.
> - Jeryth has felt a dreaming presence below the Castle Ward for months and does not know its name. Melannor knows only that the soil is uneasy.
> - Nobody in this Event names the master of the Splinter or the Cassalanters. Kolat Towers stands one block north of the garden. Neither Melannor nor Jeryth points toward it or says what it is before **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener.

### The White Cat

> [!gamemaster]**A Cat at the Sill**
>
> The cat arrives at first light on the day the Emerald Enclave row of **The Factions Come Calling** fires. It reaches the window of whichever room the chosen candidate is working in at Trollskull Manor. Melannor cast *Animal Messenger* on it the evening before, naming Trollskull Alley, which he has visited while calling on his brother.
>
> The cat is a white cat (**Cat** stat block, Tiny Beast, CR 0). It finds its recipient by description, speaks the message once in Melannor's low baritone from somewhere behind its eyes, and leaves. Anyone nearby hears it. It carries no reply, because the message asks for none. Every Enclave messenger works the same way, and later events refer back to this description. Read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A white cat sits on the sill of the window with its paws folded under it, and it watches the room with perfect calm. When the nearest of you looks up, it opens its mouth, and a low, even man's voice comes from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > "Interested in joining the Emerald Enclave? Come meet us at Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward. The gate will be open."
>
> The cat waits a moment to see whether anyone will answer, then drops from the sill into the alley and trots away between the barrels.

> [!gamemaster]**The Message Count**
>
> The message is 20 words, inside the 25-word limit of *Animal Messenger*. If a character tries to catch the cat, it finishes the message first and then slips free. Nobody can follow it across the rooftops, and nobody needs to, because the message names the place.

> [!exploration]**A Druid's Messenger**
>
> Any character who watches the cat leave and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana)** check recognizes *Animal Messenger*, a druid's spell. That character also knows that the caster must have visited Trollskull Alley and named it. A character who fails still has everything needed to find the gate. The message gives the estate and the ward, and anyone at the Yawning Portal can point the way to Phaulkonmere.

### The Open Gate

> [!gamemaster]**Phaulkonmere**
>
> Phaulkonmere is a walled estate of gardens and an old villa in the Southern Ward, owned by the Tarm and Phaulkon families, though only Jeryth lives there now. The walk from Trollskull Alley takes about two hours. Four constants hold every time the party arrives, and later Enclave events repeat them.
>
> - The gate stands open before the party arrives, and Melannor waits just inside it from dawn to dusk.
> - The garden is older than the street around it, and its paths run farther than the walls seem to allow.
> - Jeryth's voice has no source. It comes from the leaves, the gravel and the air together.
> - The oldest oak is the point of reference. Every path in the garden bends back to it.
>
> The villa and the garden are the Enclave's haven, and the **Springwarden Benefits** below set its rules. Response teams may watch the street but never enter. Read the following aloud when the party reaches the gate.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The street outside Phaulkonmere is narrow and busy with carts, and an iron gate stands open between two weathered stone posts. Beyond it the garden is thick with old trees and clipped hedges, and it looks much older than the houses on either side. Gravel paths wind inward toward a great oak whose branches reach over the villa roof. A half-elf in plain brown clothes waits just inside the gate with his hands folded in front of him, and he watches you come up the street.
>
> > "Good morning, and welcome to Phaulkonmere. I am Melannor Fellbranch, and I would like to know whether Talisolvanar is well. He is my brother, and he works as a carpenter in your alley."

> [!gamemaster]**Talisolvanar**
>
> This line pays off the introduction of Tally Fellbranch in **The Neighbors**, where the party does not yet know he has a brother. Melannor calls him Talisolvanar every time. Any character can answer him, and if someone says Tally is mending the door frames at the manor, Melannor nods and says:
>
> > "Good, I am glad to hear it. Talisolvanar should visit when his work in the alley allows it."
>
> If a character jokes about the name or the visit, Melannor answers the joke literally and says, "That is not humorous." Then he gives a plain account of why Tally has not come, since Tally has been busy with other people's doors. Melannor then turns up the path and the party follows him in.

### The Walk with Melannor

> [!gamemaster]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor leads the party along the paths toward the oak at an even pace. He stops at a bed of roses or a wall of ivy whenever a question needs an answer. He is friendly and humorless, speaks in full sentences without contractions, and never laughs or swears. Companions and characters who belong to other factions can walk with the candidates and ask their own questions.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gravel path curves farther than the walls of the estate should allow, and the air smells of wet earth and something older than the city. Melannor walks without hurrying and without looking back, and the garden seems to open ahead of him. He stops beside a bed of roses and turns to face you.
>
> > "You may ask me whatever you wish about the Enclave, and I will answer everything that I am able to answer."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the friendly but humorless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere and the Enclave's contact in Waterdeep, who has already decided he likes the party.
>
> Melannor is Friendly toward the candidates and says exactly what he means. His baritone never changes, whether he speaks about the roses or about a death in the Castle Ward. He answers a joke by explaining why it is untrue, and he calls each person by name, correctly, every time. When he speaks of the earth below the Castle Ward his sentences begin to trail off, which is the only sign that he is troubled. Melannor has Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, and magic cannot put him to sleep.
>
> Melannor is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Enclave is for, which he describes as keeping the balance between the city and the wild.
> - The beholder under the city, which he calls a problem that has not yet been solved.
> - The unease in the soil, which he cannot explain.
> - Why the Enclave noticed the party, since his animals saw what it did in the Dock Ward sewers.
> - The vault gold, on which he repeats Jeryth's answer.
> - What members receive, and why a character who belongs to another faction receives nothing.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, what the vault holds, the master of the other cell or the house one block north of the garden. He turns each aside with "That is not mine to speak of."

> [!qna]**What is the Enclave for?**
>
> Melannor touches a rose leaf with one finger and turns it over.
>
> > "The Enclave keeps the balance between the city and the wild places around it. We watch the soil, the water and the animals. Most people do not notice when the ground changes, so we notice for them."

> [!qna]**The beholder?**
>
> > "A beholder lives beneath this city and feeds on its sewers. Creatures of that kind do not belong in any natural order. The Guild that serves it digs wherever it likes, and the tunnels grow stranger each month. This is a problem, and it has not been solved."

> [!qna]**What is wrong below the city?**
>
> Melannor looks at the ground, and his sentences slow down.
>
> > "The soil is uneasy. Animals have begun to avoid certain cisterns. Jeryth has felt something beneath the Castle Ward for months, and she does not know what it is. Neither do I. I would like to find..."
>
> He stops, and then continues in the same level voice.
>
> > "I would like to know what it is, and so would Jeryth, because it troubles her more than it troubles me."

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> > "My animals watched what you did beneath the Dock Ward. You went into the sewers to bring a missing man home, and you came out again. The Enclave has use for people who do that."

> [!qna]**The vault gold?**
>
> Melannor tilts his head toward the oak at the center of the garden.
>
> > "Jeryth has answered that question before, and I will repeat her words to you. She says, 'That question is for the Lords to settle.' The Enclave takes no side in it."

> [!qna]**What do members get?**
>
> > "Jeryth gives each member a charm of restoration, and Phaulkonmere is open to you and your friends as a haven. When the Enclave has work, I will send word by animal, and you may refuse any task I send."

> [!qna]**Can we keep our other loyalties?**
>
> > "The Enclave does not take members who answer to another cause. You are welcome to walk the garden and listen, and so are your companions. Each of you answers for yourself."

> [!gamemaster]**If a Player Raises the Stone, the Vault or Manshoon**
>
> Melannor does not discuss the Stone of Golorr or what the vault holds. If a player names Manshoon, Melannor neither confirms nor denies anything, because he has no knowledge to confirm. He answers:
>
> > "I do not know that name from anything the animals have told me in all this time. Shall we walk on together?"
>
> If a player asks about the house one block north, Melannor answers "I do not discuss my neighbours." and walks on without looking in that direction. Nobody in this Event says what the building is.

> [!exploration]**His Sentences Trail Off**
>
> Any character who watches Melannor while he speaks of the soil and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** check notices that he is troubled. His sentences begin to trail off whenever the Castle Ward comes up, and he does not know why. A character who fails hears only a calm man describing a garden, and nothing else depends on the result.

### Jeryth's Voice

> [!gamemaster]**A Voice with No Source**
>
> Jeryth speaks when the party reaches the oldest oak at the center of the garden. She is a Chosen of Mielikki who has lived as a disembodied presence in the gardens for several years, and she cannot be harmed. Her voice comes from the leaves, the gravel and the air together, and the branches move when she speaks, even on a windless day.
>
> She says one or two short sentences at a time, with long silences between them, and she never repeats or explains herself. She speaks only of the garden, the seasons and what dreams below, and she takes no interest in mortal politics. Read the following aloud.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The path ends beneath the oldest oak in the garden, where its roots lift the paving stones into low humps. The air is still until every leaf on the tree turns at once, and a woman's voice speaks from all around you.
>
> > "You came."
>
> Melannor steps back and folds his hands, and the leaves settle.

> [!social]**The Voice in the Oak**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, disembodied presence, she/her) :: the patron of Phaulkonmere, who speaks rarely and means every word.
>
> Jeryth is Friendly toward the candidates, and she shows it through the garden. A warm breeze moves through the branches, flowers open along the path, and the grass lies flat where a candidate walks. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest" and never swears. If a player asks about the vault gold, she answers once with "That question is for the Lords to settle." and says nothing more on the subject.
>
> Jeryth is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who she is and why she cannot appear.
> - What dreams below the Castle Ward.
> - Whether the garden is safe.
> - What she asks of the Enclave's members.
>
> She will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault, her family or how she came to be in the garden. She does not know the name of what dreams below, and she says so once.

> [!qna]**Who are you?**
>
> > "Jeryth Phaulkon. I serve the Lady of the Forest, and this garden is mine to keep."

> [!qna]**What is below?**
>
> Every leaf on the oak goes still for the length of a breath.
>
> > "Something dreams below. I have felt it for months."

> [!qna]**Are we safe here?**
>
> > "No one will raise a blade against you in this garden. Rest here."

> [!qna]**What do you ask of us?**
>
> > "Tell Melannor what is wrong. He will tell me."

> [!exploration]**The Leaves Go Still**
>
> Any character who listens while Jeryth speaks of what lies below and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check notices something. Every leaf on the oak stops moving for a breath on the word "below". The pause happens whether or not the character notices it. A character who fails learns nothing extra. The garden is calm again by the next sentence.

### Each Candidate's Answer

> [!gamemaster]**Recording the Answers**
>
> Ask each candidate for their answer and record each one separately. A candidate who accepts becomes an Emerald Enclave member at **Renown 1** with the **Springwarden** rank. Joining takes no check, and mixed answers cause no argument with Melannor. A candidate who declines or defers keeps their current status, and characters who already belong to another faction get no offer. Mark **Emerald Enclave Joined** with the name of each candidate who accepts.

If a candidate accepts, read or paraphrase the following, using their name:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A warmth gathers in your open palm, and three faint pulses follow it, one after another. The warmth fades without leaving a mark, and the leaves overhead rustle once.
>
> > "It is done. Rest here."
>
> Melannor inclines his head and offers his hand.
>
> > "Welcome to the Emerald Enclave, Name, and thank you for your answer. The gate will be open to you from now on."

If a candidate declines or asks for more time, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor listens to your answer and nods once, and the leaves above you stay quiet.
>
> > "I understand your answer. The gate will stay open, and you are welcome to walk in the garden whenever you wish. If you change your mind, come and tell me so."

A candidate who returns later is recorded the same way. The gate is open when they arrive, and Melannor repeats the offer in a few sentences. Jeryth speaks once, and the charm and the haven are given as above.

> [!gamemaster]**Springwarden Benefits**
>
> Each accepting candidate gains the following, and nothing goes to a decliner or a companion. The benefits belong to the member and are tracked for each member separately. Companions can be the target of the charm and can enter the haven with the member, and they gain nothing else.
>
> - **Renown 1:** the member starts as a Springwarden, and Melannor greets the member by name from then on.
> - **The charm:** see **The Charm of Restoration** below.
> - **The haven:** see **The Haven** below.

> [!exploration]**The Charm of Restoration**
>
> Jeryth gives each accepting member one *charm of restoration*. The member feels it as the warmth and three pulses described above, and no object appears. The charm has 3 charges.
>
> - **Using it:** The member takes a Magic action and spends charges to cast a spell on a creature the member touches. *Lesser Restoration* costs 1 charge, and *Greater Restoration* costs 2 charges. The charm needs no material components, so *Greater Restoration* needs no diamond dust.
> - **Limit:** The charges never refresh, and when the last charge is spent the charm vanishes and Jeryth does not give a second one.
> - **Who:** Only the member can use the charm, which cannot be lent or taken, and a companion can receive the spell but cannot cast it.
> - **Loss rule:** A member whose Renown later falls below 1 keeps the charm and any unspent charges.
> - **Not a party item:** Each member holds a separate charm with its own 3 charges, and a member who joins later receives a new one.

> [!exploration]**The Haven**
>
> Phaulkonmere is neutral ground for Enclave members and their friends. The following rules apply to each member separately.
>
> - **Who may enter:** The member and up to eight companions or friends who arrive with the member. A companion who arrives alone is turned away politely at the gate.
> - **Hours:** The main gate is open from dawn to dusk, and Melannor greets visitors there. After dusk the gate is shut and nobody answers it. A member who holds the east gate key from **A Seat at Phaulkonmere** can enter at any hour.
> - **What it gives:** A Long Rest in the garden or the villa's outbuildings, with no fee. Melannor also provides free food and care for any animals the party brings.
> - **What it does not give:** It is not a hiding place from the law. If a Watch officer with a warrant asks at the gate, Melannor answers truthfully, and he does not shelter a member from a lawful arrest.
> - **Response teams:** The response team of any faction never enters Phaulkonmere and never fights inside its walls. The team may watch the street outside and follow a member who leaves.
> - **Loss rule:** A member who leads an enemy into the garden on purpose, or draws steel inside the walls, loses the haven for two tendays. Melannor tells the member at the gate, and Jeryth tightens the vines until the weapon is put away. A member whose Renown falls below 1 keeps the rank, and the haven is suspended until the Renown is restored.

### Leaving Phaulkonmere

> [!gamemaster]**The Renovation Help**
>
> This benefit belongs to the party, not to the members, because Trollskull Manor is a shared holding. It is given once, when the first candidate accepts, and later acceptances do not repeat it. Melannor mentions it on the walk back to the gate, and it costs the party nothing.
>
> - **The druids:** Within a tenday of the meeting, two Enclave druids arrive at Trollskull Manor on a morning the party names. Using *Mold Earth*, *Shape Water*, *Plant Growth* and *Stone Shape*, they dig out the flooded cellar, drain the courtyard and reseat the loose foundation stones. They also clear the overgrowth, and the whole work takes a single day.
> - **The timber:** The Enclave donates seasoned timber for the frames and floors, and Tally Fellbranch lays it at no charge to the party.
> - **The saving:** The renovation total of Trollskull Manor drops by **250 gp**, and the saving is applied once.
> - **The oak:** The druids ask the party to leave the oak in the courtyard undisturbed and to allow silverbark to grow on it. The ask is a request and not a condition, and the help is given whatever the party answers.

> [!gamemaster]**Walking Out**
>
> Melannor hands out no assignment, and **The Undercliff Scarecrows** reaches each new member later, by its own grey pigeon, once the member is 2nd level. Read the following aloud when at least one candidate has accepted.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor walks you back along the gravel path, past the roses and the ivy wall, and stops just inside the open gate. Behind him the oak moves its branches once, and then the garden is still.
>
> > "The Enclave will send druids to Trollskull Manor, and they will ask one thing about the oak in your courtyard. Thank you for coming. I will be in touch."

If every candidate declined, read the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor walks you back along the gravel path and stops just inside the gate. The street beyond it is as busy as when you arrived.
>
> > "Thank you for coming this morning, and please remember that the gate will stay open for any of you."

### Concluding the Event

The meeting ends when the last candidate leaves Phaulkonmere, and no assignment is handed over today.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Emerald Enclave Joined** — mark for each character who accepts the Enclave's offer, and record the character's name. A later acceptance at the gate is marked the same way. The member is at Renown 1 with the Springwarden rank. Readers are:
>   - the **Emerald Enclave** Factions Guide page;
>   - every Emerald Enclave faction event beginning with **The Undercliff Scarecrows**, and **A Seat at Phaulkonmere**;
>   - **The Factions Come Calling** (unconverted, and its row still writes a True or False heading);

## Overview

A white cat carries Melannor Fellbranch's invitation to specific characters, who walk to Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward to hear the Emerald Enclave's offer.

## Summary

### After the Meeting

A white cat spoke to us in a man's low voice and asked whether we wanted to join the Emerald Enclave. We walked to Phaulkonmere, where Melannor Fellbranch took us through the garden to an oak and a voice named Jeryth Phaulkon. Each of us gave our own answer, and Melannor told us he would be in touch.

### Without a Private Meeting

We carried on with the renovation at Trollskull Manor without answering the white cat or changing any of our faction affiliations.
