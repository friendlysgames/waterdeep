# Autumnreaver

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Enclave renown reaches 10. Melannor arrives at Trollskull Manor in person — not by animal messenger this time. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Walk with Melannor from Trollskull Manor to Phaulkonmere
> - Hear Jeryth confirm her spell access directly, and ask about specific spells
> - Receive a charcoal map of three hidden sewer routes into Undermountain
> - Select a trained beast companion from the estate's working paddock
>
> #### Background
>
> The Autumnreaver rank is where the Enclave's druid-tier resources open fully. Jeryth is investing deliberate divine attention in this character, one use per quest. The three sewer routes on Melannor's map are genuine, maintained by the Enclave, and unmonitored by the Watch or any faction. They connect to Undermountain's upper levels through natural channels that predate the sewers by centuries.
>
> The beast companion is a trained working animal from Phaulkonmere's stock. It returns to the estate after each operation and assists only once per quest. Melannor states this plainly and will not elaborate.

### Melannor at the Door

Melannor arrives at Trollskull Manor in the morning, dressed for travel. He makes no announcement.

> [!readaloud]
> There is a knock at the door — firm, neither urgent nor tentative. Melannor Fellbranch is on the step when it opens, dressed for the road and looking at the promoted character with the steady attention of someone who has decided how the morning will go.
>
> > "Autumnreaver. Walk with me."
>
> He turns toward the street before an answer comes.

### The Walk

Melannor waits until they are clear of the Alley's foot traffic before speaking. He keeps a moderate pace and says nothing for the first block.

> [!readaloud]
> He says nothing for the first block and keeps a moderate pace, and when he does speak he doesn't turn his head.
>
> > "Most people at this rank ask about the spells before I finish the first sentence. I'd rather explain everything first. Questions after."

He covers the three benefits in the order they will be useful.

### Jeryth's Spell Access

At Phaulkonmere, Jeryth's voice arrives over the path before Melannor has finished his opening line.

> [!readaloud]
> Melannor has begun saying something about the rank's practical shape when the garden interrupts him — Jeryth's voice arriving from the direction of the oldest oaks, calm and unhurried.
>
> > "Once per quest. Any druid spell of fifth level or lower. No components from you — I provide what is needed. Tell me what you require and when. I'll have it ready."
>
> The garden is quiet for a moment while the character takes in what she's said.
>
> > "I'll also know when you need it before you ask. I prefer that you ask anyway."
>
> Melannor looks toward the oaks and says, to no one in particular:
>
> > "I'll come back to it."

> [!social]**The Voice in the Garden**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Disembodied Presence, she/her) :: the spirit of Phaulkonmere, present wherever the oldest roots run in the garden.
>
> Jeryth has said what she came to say. She will confirm specific spells if asked, and she confirms them plainly and completely. She has every druid spell of 5th level or lower available to her.
>
> Conversation topics Jeryth is willing to address include:
> - Which spells she has available
> - What the one-per-quest cadence means in practice
>
> Specific dialogue on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Which spells?**
>
> > "Mass cure wounds. Conjure elemental. Wall of stone. Contagion. I have them all — anything of fifth level or lower that a druid could cast."

> [!qna]**What does once per quest mean?**
>
> > "Each quest in your work counts as one. If you ask for two in the same quest, the answer is silence, and then: once is the agreement."

> [!gamemaster]**Running Jeryth's One-Per-Quest Cadence**
>
> Each named quest in the Quest Quick Reference counts as one. Track one use per quest per character. A character who asks for a second casting in the same quest hears Jeryth's silence followed by: "Once is the agreement."

### The Sewer Routes

Melannor produces a folded charcoal map from his coat. Three routes are marked.

> [!readaloud]
> Melannor unfolds a charcoal map on the garden's low stone wall. Three routes are marked in his hand — plain notations: clear distances and three horizontal lines over a leaf wherever an entry point sits.
>
> > "These connect to Undermountain's upper levels without passing through Watch or faction territory. We've maintained them for twelve years. Each entry is marked with three horizontal lines over a leaf. You'll recognize it."
>
> He produces a second copy, identical to the first, and holds it out.
>
> > "That one is yours."

The three routes are:
- A drainage channel behind the Sea Ward dyer's district
- A sealed-and-Enclave-maintained tunnel from the Southern Ward's deep cisterns
- A sealed entry in the Dock Ward's abandoned fishmeal warehouse

### The Beast Companion

Melannor brings the character to the estate's working paddock at the north end of Phaulkonmere.

> [!readaloud]
> The paddock at the north end of the estate contains the Enclave's three working animals. A brown bear has taken most of the available shade against the far fence. A dire wolf stands at the gate end, watching. The giant eagle is on a post near the center that looks like it has been replaced at least twice.
>
> > "One of these will assist in one operation per quest. Your choice. They return here after. They are working animals."
>
> He stays where he is while the character looks the animals over.

The character selects one. Melannor notes it without ceremony.

> [!readaloud]
> > "I'll let them know."

> [!gamemaster]**The Enclave's Working Animals**
>
> **Brown Bear** — dependable in a fight; useful for carrying gear in difficult terrain. Follows clear single-step commands. Poorly suited for underground work.
>
> **Giant Eagle** — aerial surveillance and fast extraction. Carries one person in flight. Sharp instincts; suspicious of strangers.
>
> **Dire Wolf** — tracking, intimidation, close combat. Bonds more readily with one handler. Effective in sewers.
>
> Use the corresponding stat blocks from the 2024 *Monster Manual*. The chosen animal appears at Trollskull Manor's stable yard the following morning without ceremony or explanation.

### Concluding the Event

Melannor returns to his groundskeeping without further comment. The character has the map, the beast companion selected, and Jeryth's spell access confirmed.

## Overview

After reaching Renown 10 with the Emerald Enclave, Melannor arrives at Trollskull Manor's door in person and walks the promoted character to Phaulkonmere. Jeryth confirms she will cast any druid spell of 5th level or lower on the character's behalf once per quest. Melannor hands over a charcoal map of three hidden sewer routes into Undermountain that avoid Watch and faction territory. The character picks a trained beast from the estate's working paddock.

## Summary

We reached Autumnreaver rank with the Emerald Enclave, and Melannor came to the door himself for the first time. At Phaulkonmere, Jeryth confirmed she would cast any fifth-level or lower druid spell on our behalf once per quest. Melannor handed over a charcoal map of three sewer routes into Undermountain's upper levels, clear of Watch and faction territory. We chose a beast companion from the estate paddock; it turned up at our stable yard the next morning without explanation.
