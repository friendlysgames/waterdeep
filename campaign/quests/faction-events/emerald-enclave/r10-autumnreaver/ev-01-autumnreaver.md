# Autumnreaver

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Enclave renown reaches 10. Melannor arrives at Trollskull Manor in person — not by animal messenger this time. In this Event:
>
> - Melannor appears at the door without explanation and walks with the character to Phaulkonmere.
> - Jeryth addresses the character directly and confirms her spell access in her own terms.
> - Melannor hands over a charcoal map of three hidden sewer routes into Undermountain.
> - The character selects a trained beast companion from the estate's working paddock.

**Background (DM only)**

The Autumnreaver rank is where the Enclave's druid-tier resources open fully. Jeryth is investing deliberate divine attention in this character, one use per quest. The three sewer routes on Melannor's map are genuine, maintained by the Enclave, and unmonitored by the Watch or any faction. They connect to Undermountain's upper levels through natural channels that predate the sewers by centuries.

The beast companion is a trained working animal from Phaulkonmere's stock. It returns to the estate after each operation and assists only once per quest. Melannor states this plainly and will not elaborate.

### Melannor at the Door

**Melannor Fellbranch** (Neutral Good, Half-Elf Druid, he/him) appears at Trollskull Manor's door in the morning, dressed for travel. He looks at the promoted character.

> > "Autumnreaver. Walk with me."

He turns toward the street before an answer comes.

### The Walk

Melannor waits until they are clear of the Alley's foot traffic before speaking.

> > "Most people at this rank ask about the spells before I finish the first sentence. I'd rather explain everything first. Questions after."

He covers the three benefits in the order they'll be useful.

### Jeryth's Spell Access

At Phaulkonmere, Jeryth's voice arrives before Melannor has finished his opening line.

> > "I'll come back to it," he says, to no one in particular.

**Jeryth Phaulkon** (Neutral Good, disembodied presence, she/her) speaks from the direction of the oldest oaks.

> > "Once per quest. Any druid spell of fifth level or lower. No components from you — I provide what is needed. Tell me what you require and when. I'll have it ready."

A pause. Then:

> > "I'll also know when you need it before you ask. I prefer that you ask anyway."

Characters who ask about specific spells get specific answers. Jeryth confirms she has *mass cure wounds*, *conjure elemental*, *wall of stone*, *contagion*, and every other druid spell of 5th level or lower.

> > [!tip]**Jeryth's One-Per-Quest Cadence**
> > Each named quest in the Quest Quick Reference counts as one. If a character asks for two castings in the same quest, Jeryth's answer is silence, then: "Once is the agreement." Track the use per quest.

### The Sewer Routes

Melannor produces a folded charcoal map from his coat. Three routes are marked.

- A drainage channel behind the Sea Ward dyer's district
- A sealed-and-Enclave-maintained tunnel from the Southern Ward's deep cisterns
- A sealed entry in the Dock Ward's abandoned fishmeal warehouse

> > "These connect to Undermountain's upper levels without passing through Watch or faction territory. We've maintained them for twelve years. Each entry is marked with three horizontal lines over a leaf. You'll recognize it."

He produces a second copy, identical to his, and holds it out.

> > "That one is yours."

### The Beast Companion

Melannor brings the character to the estate's working paddock at the north end of Phaulkonmere. Three animals are quartered there: a brown bear occupying most of the available shade, a dire wolf watching from the paddock gate, and a giant eagle perched on a post that looks like it has been replaced at least twice.

> > "One of these will assist in one operation per quest. Your choice. They return here after. They are working animals."

The character selects one. Melannor notes it without ceremony.

> > "I'll let them know."

> > [!profile]**The Enclave's Working Animals**
> > **Brown Bear** — dependable in a fight; useful for carrying gear in difficult terrain. Follows clear single-step commands. Poorly suited for underground work.
> > **Giant Eagle** — aerial surveillance and fast extraction. Carries one person in flight. Sharp instincts; suspicious of strangers.
> > **Dire Wolf** — tracking, intimidation, close combat. Bonds more readily with one handler. Effective in sewers.

### Concluding the Event

#### Autumnreaver Reached: True / False

The promoted character reached Renown 10 with the Emerald Enclave. This flag is read by the **Winterstalker** rank event (r25) and by mission events that check whether the character has access to beast companion support or Jeryth's 5th-level spell casting.
