# Autumnreaver

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Emerald Enclave member first reaches Renown 10. That usually happens at the debrief of **The Grells in the Dock Ward**, and bonus Renown from earlier missions can bring a member to 10 sooner. Melannor Fellbranch comes to the member's door in person, walks them to Phaulkonmere and names the rank. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn how to ask Jeryth Phaulkon for one casting in each quest, made through a living sprig and chosen from a fixed list of druid spells.
> - Choose a beast from the estate's paddock, a Brown Bear, a Dire Wolf or a Giant Eagle, to work beside them on one operation in each quest.
> - Take a charcoal map of three sewer routes that end on the first level of Undermountain.
>
> Only the member is addressed. Companions who are not Emerald Enclave members wait on the benches by the gate and gain nothing.

### Melannor at the Door

Hold this Event until **Summerstrider Reached** is marked for the member. At two in the afternoon on the day after the member reaches Renown 10, Melannor knocks at the door of their lodging, which is Trollskull Manor for most members. He breaks from the animals for this rank and comes himself. Every qualifying member is met on their own account, so members who cross the threshold together walk together and are recorded one by one.

If the member is out on a mission or an operation, or away from Waterdeep, Melannor sends no animal after them. He returns to their door at two each afternoon and waits until the member is back, and the meeting takes place on the afternoon after they return. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Two slow knocks sound on the door, and the man on the step waits with his hands folded in front of him. Melannor wears a green coat with pruning shears at his belt and carries a shallow basket of seedlings.
>
> > "Good afternoon, Autumnreaver. These seedlings are for your window boxes, and they need water twice a day. For a rank, I come myself."
>
> He waits while you take the basket, and then he turns his head toward the south.
>
> > "Please walk with me to Phaulkonmere. I will explain the three benefits at the gate, and I will take your questions after each one."

Melannor walks the member to the gate of Phaulkonmere, two hours on foot. A line of pigeons keeps up with him along the rooftops. He says nothing until the crowns of tall oaks show above the walls of the Southern Ward.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jeryth cannot leave the garden, so she casts through a sprig of the oldest oak. Each casting takes attention she would otherwise give to the water under the city, which is why she gives one casting in each quest.
> - The paddock animals are working stock raised at Phaulkonmere. The Giant Eagle is a Celestial and not a Beast, so the spells and skills that work on animals do not work on it.
> - The three routes follow channels older than Waterdeep's sewers. Enclave druids found them twelve years ago and have kept them clear since, and no Watch patrol or faction knows they exist.
> - The Enclave has walked each route only as far as the first level of Undermountain and has never gone deeper.
> - Nobody in this Event can say Manshoon's name, or call Kolat Towers his home, unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member. Melannor says "the Splinter" or "the other cell" if the subject comes up.
> - Phaulkonmere is one block south of Kolat Towers, and before the gate nobody points at the towers or says what they are.
> - Jeryth has felt a dreaming presence below the city for months. She does not know its name, and she says only "Something dreams below."

### Naming the Rank

Phaulkonmere stands in the Southern Ward, and the gate is open before the member arrives. Melannor leads the member in by the main gate. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open when you reach it, and the street outside is paved and busy with afternoon carts. Past the gate the paving gives way to grass, and the oaks overhead are older than the walls built around them. Melannor leads you along the gravel to the oldest oak, whose roots cross the path in front of its trunk.
>
> > "Welcome back, Autumnreaver. We will begin here at the oldest oak, because the sprig is Jeryth's to give. Please wait until she has finished before you ask me anything."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper in Person**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere and a druid. He walks the city himself for this rank, because it asks for more than an animal can carry.
>
> Melannor is Friendly and plain. He walks at an even pace, stops to straighten a plant while he talks and answers a joke with a literal reply. He uses no contractions, never swears and calls the member by name. He explains all three benefits before he takes a question.
>
> Melannor is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Autumnreaver benefit gives the member, and how to use it.
> - Why every benefit works once in each quest.
> - The three routes and what waits at their ends.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault, the dreaming thing's name, the Splinter's master or the house one block north. He answers each by saying that he does not know, or that it is not his to say.

> [!qna]**What does Autumnreaver give me?**
>
> Melannor holds up one finger for each benefit as he names it.
>
> > "Jeryth will cast one spell for you in each quest, through a sprig you will carry. One animal from our paddock will work beside you in each quest. I will also give you a map of three routes under the city."

> [!qna]**Why did you come yourself?**
>
> > "A message can be heard by anyone who stands nearby. These arrangements are for you alone, and I would rather say them at your own door."

> [!qna]**Why only once in each quest?**
>
> > "Jeryth watches the garden and the water under the city, and a casting takes her attention from both. The animals have work at the paddock between operations."

> [!qna]**What happens if my Renown falls?**
>
> > "The rank stays with you, because you earned it. I will ask you to leave the sprig and the map with me at the gate. The animal will wait in the paddock, and everything returns when your Renown does."

### Jeryth's Sprig

Melannor stops beside the oldest oak, and Jeryth speaks. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The leaves of the oldest oak stir although the afternoon air is still, and a clear woman's voice speaks from the garden around you. A hand's length of green wood lies across the root beside Melannor's boot, with three leaves still on it.
>
> > "Take the sprig. I will cast through it."
>
> The vines along the wall loosen, and Melannor lifts the sprig from the root and holds it out to you in both hands.

> [!social]**The Voice in the Garden**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, disembodied presence, she/her) :: a disembodied presence in the gardens of Phaulkonmere, who casts through a sprig because she cannot leave them.
>
> She speaks one or two short sentences at a time and never repeats them. Jeryth is Friendly and distant. The leaves move when she speaks, and the garden answers with a warm breeze. She never swears and takes no side in mortal politics. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest".
>
> Jeryth is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The sprig and the castings she makes through it.
> - What dreams below, which she answers in one sentence.
>
> She will not discuss the gold, the Stone, the vault or any faction. She says "That question is for the Lords to settle." to any question about the gold.

> [!qna]**What will you cast?**
>
> > "What Melannor reads to you. Nothing else."

> [!qna]**Why a sprig?**
>
> > "I do not leave the garden. The sprig carries my hand."

> [!qna]**Is there anything I should know?**
>
> > "Something dreams below. Do not stay down long."

> [!qna]**Can you cast twice in one quest?**
>
> The leaves go still for a moment.
>
> > "Once is the agreement."

> [!exploration]**Casting Through the Sprig**
>
> Each Autumnreaver carries one sprig, cut from the oldest oak and wrapped in oiled cloth. It stays green for as long as the member holds the rank. Melannor replaces a lost or burned sprig at the gate.
>
> - **Contact:** Melannor at the gate by day, or at any hour for a member who holds the east gate key (**East Gate Key Held**). A written request left in the gate box is answered by a note in the box by noon the next day.
> - **The request:** the member names the quest, one spell from the list below and the day. A request with no named quest or spell is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Notice:** three days. A request with less notice is refused and spends nothing, and a request withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent either.
> - **The casting:** on the day named, from dawn to dusk, the member holds the sprig and says the spell's name aloud. Jeryth casts it at that moment as though she stood where the sprig is. A touch spell reaches a creature the member touches with the sprig hand. A day that passes without the member asking is not spent.
> - **Place:** the surface of Waterdeep, including its cellars and sewers. The sprig does not work in Undermountain, because Jeryth casts from the garden.
> - **Numbers:** each spell is cast at its listed level. Jeryth has a spellcasting modifier of +4, a spell save DC of 15 and a spell attack bonus of +7. She holds Concentration from the garden for the spell's full duration, and nothing that happens to the member breaks it.
> - **Limit:** one casting per member per quest. A quest is one of the named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**. Events, faction missions and rank events are not quests. The casting counts against the quest the party is in when the member asks, and a casting not asked for before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Who else:** only the member can ask, though a companion benefits when the spell's targets include them.
> - **The list, level 1:** *Cure Wounds*, *Healing Word*, *Entangle*, *Thunderwave*, *Longstrider* and *Speak with Animals*.
> - **The list, level 2:** *Lesser Restoration*, *Protection from Poison*, *Moonbeam* and *Animal Messenger*. Jeryth can send a message only to a place she has visited, which is the gate of Phaulkonmere.
> - **The list, level 4:** *Stone Shape*.
> - **The list, level 5:** *Greater Restoration*, *Mass Cure Wounds*, *Wall of Stone*, *Conjure Elemental* and *Contagion*. Jeryth supplies the diamond dust for *Greater Restoration*, which is worth 100 gp, and no other casting costs gold.
> - **Not on the list:** *Revivify*, *Reincarnate* and every other spell that returns the dead. Jeryth answers a request for one with "No." and nothing is spent.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who asks Jeryth to harm the Watch, a Lord or a creature that has surrendered loses the sprig for a tenday. The loss begins when Melannor hears of it.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and the sprig is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!qna]**Can Jeryth bring someone back from the dead?**
>
> > "No. Jeryth casts nothing that returns the dead, and I did not ask her to when we agreed on this list."

### The Paddock

Melannor leads the member north across the estate to the working paddock. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The paddock lies at the north end of the estate, behind a low wall of stacked stone and a gate of silver-grey boards. A brown bear lies in the shade of the wall with its chin on its paws. A dire wolf watches from the gate with its ears forward, and a giant eagle stands on a post beside the water trough.
>
> > "Choose the one you want. It will work beside you once in each quest, and it will come back here when the work is done. These are working animals, and they are not pets."

> [!qna]**Which should I choose?**
>
> Melannor looks at each animal in turn.
>
> > "The bear holds a line and carries gear. The wolf tracks well, and it fights best beside you in a tunnel or a street. The eagle scouts from above and strikes from the air."

> [!qna]**Why can I not talk to the eagle?**
>
> > "The eagle is no animal of this world. It understands Common but cannot speak, and my spells for talking to animals do not reach it."

> [!qna]**Can the bear eat the Watch?**
>
> > "That is not humorous. The animals will not fight the Watch, a Lord or anyone who has surrendered. If you ask them to, I will take the animal back for a tenday."

> [!exploration]**Calling the Beast**
>
> The member's beast stays in the paddock until they call it. The choice is permanent, except when the beast is replaced.
>
> - **Contact:** Melannor at the gate by day, or at any hour for a member who holds the east gate key. A written request left in the gate box is answered by a note in the box by the next dawn.
> - **The request:** the member names the quest, the operation, a surface place in Waterdeep and the hour. A request with no named operation is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Notice:** one day. A request with less notice is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Arrival:** the beast is waiting at the place at the hour named. It comes on its own, and Melannor does not come with it.
> - **The operation:** it runs from the moment the member meets the beast until the member is back at a safe lodging or dismisses it. The beast then returns to Phaulkonmere on its own.
> - **Limit:** one operation per member per quest, counted as the sprig is counted. A call not made before the quest ends is lost, and a call withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent.
> - **Undermountain:** the beast crosses between levels only on foot, so the Giant Eagle walks down a stair or a ramp and never flies down a shaft.
> - **Who else:** the beast takes orders from the member who called it and from nobody else. A companion cannot call it, and two members in one party each call their own.
> - **If it falls:** Melannor suspends the benefit for a tenday and then replaces the beast once. The member chooses again from the paddock, and Melannor does not replace it a second time.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who orders the beast to attack the Watch, a Lord or a surrendered creature loses the benefit for a tenday. The beast refuses the order, and the loss begins when Melannor hears of it.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and the beast is withdrawn to the paddock until the Renown is restored.

> [!gamemaster]**The Beast as an Ally**
>
> The member's beast uses the **Brown Bear**, **Dire Wolf** or **Giant Eagle** stat block. The Giant Eagle is a Large Celestial and not a Beast, so *Speak with Animals*, *Animal Messenger* and Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks do not work on it. It understands Common and Primordial and cannot speak.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, each is a CR 1 ally. Its Power follows the party's level: 22 for levels 1 to 4, 17 for levels 5 to 10 and 15 for levels 11 and above. Add that to the party's Power when it fights and recalculate the encounter. The first-turn knockout bonus does not apply to allies, and an ally who is on the way does not lower the difficulty.
>
> Count the beast in full when you build the encounter. With a beast at Tier 2, the party's Power for three, four or five characters is:
>
> - **5th level:** 113, 145 or 177.
> - **6th level:** 122, 157 or 192.
> - **7th level:** 140, 181 or 222.
> - **8th level:** 149, 193 or 237.
>
> #### The Beast's Tactics
>
> To begin combat, the beast moves to the enemy nearest the member, and the eagle attacks from above. During combat, it:
>
> - Attacks whoever is attacking the member, then the nearest enemy.
> - Fights beside the member, so the Dire Wolf's Pack Tactics works whenever a character is within 5 feet of its target.
> - Knocks a target Prone with its Bite or Claw when a character stands within reach to follow up.
>
> It withdraws at half its Hit Points, which is 11 for the bear and the wolf and 13 for the eagle, or when the member calls it off. It goes home on its own and is ready for the next quest. It does not attack a surrendered creature, the Watch or a Lord, and it stands down when the party does.

### The Three Routes

Melannor leads the member back to the water trough at the edge of the paddock. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor takes a folded paper from inside his coat and spreads it over the lid of the water trough. The map is drawn in charcoal, with the streets in thin lines and three routes in heavy black. Each route ends at the same small mark, three short lines above a leaf.
>
> > "These routes lead under the city to the first level of the dungeon beneath the Yawning Portal. We have kept them clear for twelve years, and each entrance carries this mark."
>
> He draws a second copy from his coat, identical to the first, and holds it out.
>
> > "This copy is yours, and I would like you to keep it dry and out of sight."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor lays a finger on each route in turn and speaks in the same even voice for all three.
>
> > "The Sea Ward route comes up in a flooded room with a statue in it. Something sits in the water north of the statue. The Southern Ward route ends in an empty room, and I have met no one there."

The Dock Ward route depends on what the party has done. If the party has not played **The Grells in the Dock Ward**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor's finger stops on the third route, and he is silent for a moment before he speaks.
>
> > "The Dock Ward route ends in a large room with black pillars. Our cats will not go past the first pillar, and I do not know what waits there. Please go carefully, and bring light."

If **The Grells in the Dock Ward** has played and **Grell Escaped** is marked with one grell fled, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor's finger stops on the third route, and he turns his head to look at you.
>
> > "One grell left the warehouse by the loading doors and went under the south quay. This route runs beneath the quay, and I believe the creature went home. Please treat the end of this route as occupied."

If **Grell Escaped** is marked with both grells fled, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor's finger stops on the third route, and he looks at you for a long moment.
>
> > "Both grells left the warehouse by the loading doors and went under the south quay. This route runs beneath the quay, and I believe they went home together. Please treat the end of this route as occupied by two."

If **The Grells in the Dock Ward** has played and **Grell Escaped** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor's finger stops on the third route, and he nods once.
>
> > "The grells that came out of the warehouse are dead. I walked the Dock Ward route to its end afterward, and the room there is empty."

> [!exploration]**The Three Routes**
>
> Every route runs to **Level 1** of Undermountain and no deeper. Each entrance is a heavy grate or door, unlocked, with its hinges oiled and the three-lines-over-a-leaf mark cut into the lintel. The passages are wide enough for a Large beast to walk.
>
> - **The Sea Ward route:** the entrance is a drainage channel behind the dyers' district, where the water runs blue and red from the vats. The walk takes two hours. It ends at a low arch in the east wall of area 3, the Slanted Room, a few steps from the mouth of its sloping tunnel. The floor there is wet and the water is shallow, and a psychic gray ooze lies under it north of the statue.
> - **The Southern Ward route:** the entrance is a sealed iron door at the end of a service ledge in the deep cisterns. The walk takes three hours and ends in area 22, the Empty Room, which holds burned torch stubs and discarded potion bottles. Nothing lives there, so the room makes a safe place to rest.
> - **The Dock Ward route:** the entrance is a sealed hatch in the floor of an abandoned fishmeal warehouse, where a few sacks of old meal still stand by the wall. The walk takes ninety minutes. It ends at a narrow door in the south wall of area 5, the Grell Hideout, and what waits there follows **Grell Escaped**.
>
> The Dock Ward route reads **Grell Escaped**. If it is marked, area 5 holds the grell or grells that fled the warehouse in **The Grells in the Dock Ward**, hungry and at full Hit Points. The roster for all three states is in the scene below.

> [!exploration]**Using the Routes**
>
> - **Contact:** the member's copy of the map. Melannor at the gate answers questions about a route and takes reports of a breach.
> - **Place:** the three entrances only. The map shows no others.
> - **Notice:** none. The entrances open to anyone who reaches them.
> - **Limit:** none per quest. A party that reaches Level 1 by a route cannot be pulled out by spell, because teleportation fails in Undermountain, so the party walks back.
> - **Who else:** the member, any companions the member leads, and the member's beast. No faction's Response Team uses the routes or knows them.
> - **Breach:** a member who finds an entrance broken, a route flooded or a creature in the way reports it at the gate. Melannor seals that entrance from below within a day, and it opens again after he has walked it, a tenday later.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who leads the Watch, a Response Team or an enemy to an entrance, or lets one follow them down, loses that route for a tenday. Melannor seals that entrance, and the other two stay open.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank. Melannor takes back the map and seals all three entrances until the Renown is restored.

> [!qna]**Where do the routes end?**
>
> > "Each one ends on the first level of the dungeon, in a room that I have marked on the map. They go no deeper, and what lies below the first level is not ours."

### The Grell at the End of the Dock Ward Route

This scene happens when a party first reaches area 5, never during this Event. Refer to **Level 1** of Undermountain in the Dungeon of the Mad Mage for the rest of the level.

> [!hazard]**The Grell Hideout**
>
> The route's narrow door opens in the south wall of a 50-foot-square room with five pillars of black bone. The alcoves to the north and east hold the grells, which float among the pillars and chase anyone who runs. The roster follows the state of the route:
>
> - **Grell Escaped is marked with one grell fled:** one **Grell** at full Hit Points. At 5th level its Power is 30 against party Power of 96, 128 or 160, which costs 9.8%, 5.5% and 3.5% of the party's resources. That is Mild at every size, with a Day Cost of 2.
> - **Grell Escaped is marked with both grells fled, or the party has not played The Grells in the Dock Ward:** two **Grells**, Power 60. That costs 39.1% at three characters (Bruising, Day Cost 4), 22.0% at four (Bruising, 4) and 14.1% at five (Mild, 2). At three characters the second grell joins in round 3.
> - **The grells were destroyed and Grell Escaped is not marked:** the room is empty and nothing happens.
>
> Add the member's beast at Power 17 if it is present, and recalculate if the party is above 5th level.
>
> #### The Grell's Tactics
>
> To begin combat, the grell hovers 10 feet above the floor among the pillars and uses Paralyzing Tentacles on the nearest character. During combat, it:
>
> - Uses Paralyzing Tentacles on the nearest character who is not already Paralyzed. It never makes its Beak attack against a creature it Paralyzed earlier in the same round or against a creature at 0 Hit Points.
> - Carries a Grappled character toward its alcove, never higher than 15 feet.
> - Stays 10 feet up when it can, out of reach of melee weapons, and comes within 5 feet only to use its Beak.
> - Avoids a creature that another grell Paralyzed in the same round, and picks the nearest other character.
>
> A grell flees at 27 Hit Points or fewer, unless it is holding a Paralyzed creature. It Dashes down the long hall away from the room, drops any creature it is grappling and does not return.
>
> **Non-combat route.** A character who throws a sack of the old fishmeal into an alcove (a Utilize action) draws that grell to feed for 1 minute. During that minute the party can cross along the south wall to the long hall. This works only if nobody attacks the grell or comes within 10 feet of it, and each sack draws one grell.

### Leaving Phaulkonmere

Melannor walks the member back along the gravel to the gate. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gravel is loud under your boots, and the pigeons on the wall turn their heads as you pass. Melannor stops at the threshold and faces you with his hands clasped in front of him.
>
> > "The soil is uneasy. I would be glad if you kept to the routes I marked, and came back up the same way."
>
> He goes back along the path toward the oak, and the pigeons on the wall follow him with their heads.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Autumnreaver earns further Renown from the missions and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Emerald Enclave page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions what Melannor and Jeryth said and where the routes end. The sprig, the beast and the map stay individual benefits, and Melannor and Jeryth turn away a companion who asks in the member's name.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard all three arrangements, has chosen a beast and has left Phaulkonmere with the sprig and the map.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Autumnreaver Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Melannor names the rank at Phaulkonmere; read by **Winterstalker**, which checks it before naming the next rank. Track separately for each member the beast chosen and whether it has been replaced. Also track the quest of the last sprig casting, the quest of the last beast operation and any route that is sealed.
>
> This Event also reads **Summerstrider Reached** (the hold), **Grell Escaped** (the Dock Ward route), **East Gate Key Held** (the gate hours) and **Manshoon Named** (the speaker's wording).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three benefits are active for this member from the next morning. **Winterstalker** occurs when this member reaches Renown 25. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Melannor Fellbranch comes to an Emerald Enclave member's door, names them an Autumnreaver and explains the rank's druid casting, working animal and three routes under the city.

## Summary

We were named Autumnreavers when Melannor came to the door himself and walked us to Phaulkonmere. Jeryth will cast one spell for each of us in every quest through a sprig of the oldest oak. A bear, a wolf or an eagle will work beside us once in every quest. Melannor gave each of us a charcoal map of three routes under the city, and they end on the first level of the dungeon below the Yawning Portal.
