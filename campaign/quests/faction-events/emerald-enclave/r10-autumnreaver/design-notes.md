# Design Notes: Autumnreaver

## The Sprig

Appendix B and the Factions Guide give Jeryth one casting of any druid spell of 5th level or lower in each quest, with no components. The restored draft kept that wording and named *mass cure wounds*, *conjure elemental*, *wall of stone* and *contagion* as examples, with no place, notice or list. Read literally, it gave every member *Revivify* and *Reincarnate* once a quest. Jeryth now casts through a living sprig of the oldest oak, on three days' notice, from the fixed list in the mechanics reference. The list leaves out every spell that returns the dead and includes *Greater Restoration*, with Jeryth covering the 100 gp of diamond dust. Dragon Heist ties her casting to a renown equal to the spell's level, and the remix drops that rule.

## The Beast and Ally Power

The paddock keeps its three animals. The Giant Eagle is a Celestial in the 2024 Monster Manual, so the text says that *Speak with Animals* and Animal Handling do not reach it. Each beast is a CR 1 ally with Power 22, 17 or 15 by tier, and it counts in full. The restored draft had the beast appear at the stable yard the next morning, and the call is now a request at the gate with a day's notice. One operation per quest and one replacement follow the Force Grey rule for Ysmay Halvane and Dovrin Tesk. The beast withdraws at half its Hit Points, which is 11 for the bear and the wolf and 13 for the eagle, because the eagle has 26.

## The Three Routes and Grell Escaped

The restored draft sent the routes into Undermountain's upper levels with no destinations, and the Level 1 key has no sewer connection. Each route now ends at a fixed area: the Slanted Room (area 3), the Empty Room (area 22) and the Grell Hideout (area 5). Area 5 holds the grells that **The Grells in the Dock Ward** drives out, so the Dock Ward route reads **Grell Escaped**. It holds the escaped grell if the outcome is marked, the key's two grells if the mission has not played, and nothing if both grells died. The fight is written once here for the table that gets there first.

## Departures from the Source

Melannor still arrives at the door in person, and he now waits for a member who is away. "Once per arc" in Appendix B becomes once per quest, as the restored draft already had it. The twelve years of maintenance and the three-lines-over-a-leaf mark are kept. The restored draft said the routes avoid the Watch and every faction. That becomes a loss rule, and a member who leads a pursuer down loses the route for a tenday.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented in this draft:
- The seedlings, the nine o'clock visit, and the oiled cloth and three leaves of the sprig.
- Jeryth's spell save DC of 15 and spell attack bonus of +7.
- The one-day notice for the beast.
- Travel times of two, three and one and a half hours.
- The arch and door where the Sea Ward and Dock Ward routes meet areas 3 and 5.
- The dyers' vats, the fishmeal sacks used as bait and the tenday for a sealed entrance.

The city's cellars and sewers count as the surface for the sprig, because the mechanics reference says only "surface".
