# Winterstalker

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Enclave renown reaches 25. An owl delivers Melannor's summons at dusk — a different animal and a different hour than any previous communication. In this Event:
>
> - An owl arrives at dusk with Melannor's brief summons.
> - At Phaulkonmere, Melannor confirms he will accompany the party on one mission per quest, in person.
> - Jeryth expands her spell access to 8th level and names the commitment plainly.
> - Melannor demonstrates the advance intelligence benefit with a current example.
> - A letter arrives from Sarna Dath at the south quay, opening the automatic harbor network reports.

**Background (DM only)**

The Winterstalker rank is the second-highest in the Enclave. Jeryth's 8th-level access represents a meaningful increase in divine attention, and she acknowledges it as such. She will not say it costs her greatly, but she will note that eighth level requires care about when it is asked.

Melannor's personal accompaniment is offered without ceremony. He dislikes leaving Phaulkonmere for extended periods, though he will not say so. He shows up when called.

Sarna Dath is a harbor fisherfolk contact who has filed environmental reports for the Enclave for four years. Her blue-and-white boat is moored at the south quay. At this rank, her focus shifts from environmental anomalies to vessel movements: departure schedules, cargo manifests, anything on the water that doesn't match the harbor master's records.

### The Owl at Dusk

An owl lands on the promoted character's window ledge as the light fades. When it has the character's attention, it speaks in **Melannor Fellbranch**'s voice.

> > "Winterstalker. Come tomorrow morning."

It departs without waiting.

### At Phaulkonmere

**Melannor Fellbranch** (Neutral Good, Half-Elf Druid, he/him) meets the character at the gate and walks them to the pavilion in the garden's eastern section — a covered seat near the oldest oaks that the party may not have noticed before. He takes a seat on the bench and looks at the character for a moment before speaking.

> > "This is the rank where I stop sending things and come myself."

### Melannor's Accompaniment

Melannor states what personal accompaniment means: one mission per quest. He carries a full druid's capabilities into the field. He will take the party's direction, but he has his own judgment about what to do when the plan stops working, and he will use it.

> > "Call me when it matters. I'll be there."

> > [!tip]**Running Melannor in the Field**
> > Use the **Druid** stat block. Melannor favors terrain-control spells: *spike growth*, *entangle*, *plant growth*, *fog cloud*. He will not spend a spell slot on something the party could handle with a sword. He returns to Phaulkonmere after the mission, immediately, regardless of what happens next.

### Jeryth's 8th-Level Access

Jeryth's voice arrives over the pavilion without preamble.

> > "I'll cast spells up to eighth level for you now. Once per quest, still. Eighth level is significant attention. Choose what you ask for."

She gives the character a moment.

> > "Sunburst. Control weather. Earthquake. Animal shapes. I can cast them. Ask when it's worth asking."

> > [!tip]**Managing Jeryth's 8th-Level Spells**
> > At this rank, Jeryth can cast *control weather*, *sunburst*, *earthquake*, *tsunami*, *antipathy/sympathy*, *animal shapes*, and all other druid spells of 8th level or lower. She is aware of how much attention this requires. She won't refuse a reasonable request, but a character who asks for *earthquake* during a minor complication hears silence — then: "Find another way."

### The Advance Intelligence Benefit

Melannor produces a folded document from his coat — a current example from the network. A network member recently mapped the behavioral patterns of a **Giant Crocodile** found in the Southern Ward cisterns: approach and retreat patterns, feeding times, a sketch of its resting position in the coldest hours of morning. A note at the bottom in Melannor's hand: "Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks. Advantage on first Initiative roll within this location."

> > "If you know a beast, plant creature, or elemental is at your target before you arrive, we'll have a report ready. Tell me the location. The network usually turns it around in 24 hours."

He folds the document and leaves it on the bench without taking it back.

### The Harbor Contact

A letter has been left at Phaulkonmere's gate, addressed to the promoted character by name. It is brief.

*Sarna Dath — south quay, blue-and-white boat. I run fish. I also keep track of ships that depart at wrong hours. Melannor says you're trustworthy. Ship manifests, cargo movements, departure schedules: if it seems like something you should know, I'll write it down and send it along. Find me at the quay.*

Melannor has no further commentary about Sarna.

> > "She's reliable."

> > [!tip]**Sarna's Reports**
> > Sarna's handwritten notes arrive by gull once per tenday at minimum — more often when shipping is active. She tracks departures from the Sea Maidens Faire fleet, unusual night cargo in the deep harbor, and any manifests that don't match the harbor master's records. Her reports arrive automatically; the character doesn't have to ask.

### Concluding the Event

#### Winterstalker Reached: True / False

The promoted character reached Renown 25 with the Emerald Enclave. This flag is read by the **Master of the Wild** rank event (r50) and by any quest or mission event that checks whether Melannor can be called into the field or whether Jeryth's 8th-level spells are available.

## Overview

After reaching Renown 25 with the Emerald Enclave, an owl arrives at dusk with Melannor's summons. At Phaulkonmere, he confirms he will accompany the party on one mission per quest in person. Jeryth expands her spellcasting to 8th level and names the commitment plainly. A current advance intelligence report demonstrates how beast-tracking works, and a letter from a harbor contact named Sarna Dath opens a direct line to ship manifests and departure schedules.

## Summary

We reached Winterstalker rank with the Emerald Enclave. At Phaulkonmere, Melannor told us he would come in person now, not just send messages. Jeryth expanded her spell access to 8th level and made clear that eighth level requires careful judgment about when to ask. We saw a sample advance intelligence report on a creature in the Southern Ward cisterns, and a letter arrived from Sarna Dath at the south quay — she tracks vessel movements and will send us reports automatically.
