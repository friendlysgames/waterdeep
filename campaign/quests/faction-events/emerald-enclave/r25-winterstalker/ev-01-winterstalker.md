# Winterstalker

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Emerald Enclave member first reaches Renown 25. That usually happens during or after the lair heists. Melannor Fellbranch names the rank at Phaulkonmere and gives that member the four Winterstalker benefits. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn how to call Melannor into the field for one mission in each quest.
> - Learn how to ask Jeryth Phaulkon for one casting of an 8th-level druid spell in each quest, made through the sprig.
> - Ask for an advance report on the Beasts, Plants and Elementals at a place before the party goes there.
> - Receive a harbor note from Sarna Dath, a fisher on the south quay, every tenday.
>
> All of these benefits belong to the qualifying member. Companions who are not Emerald Enclave members wait on the benches by the gate and gain nothing.

### The Owl at Dusk

Hold this Event until **Autumnreaver Reached** is marked for the member. At dusk on the day after the member reaches Renown 25, an owl finds them wherever they are lodging. Use the Owl stat block, which is a Tiny Beast of CR 0. Every qualifying member is invited on their own account, so members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one.

A member who is away from Waterdeep or below the surface finds the owl at their first surface lodging on return, at the next dusk, and the meeting moves to the following morning. If someone kills the owl before it speaks, a second one arrives at the next dusk. The six missions bring a member to Renown 19 on their base awards, so a member needs about 6 more from mission bonuses and the Earning Renown list in the Factions Guide before this rank arrives. Every procedure below works whenever it comes.

> [!readaloud]
>
> As the light fades, a small brown owl settles on the sill without a sound and fixes its yellow eyes on you. After a moment a deep, even voice speaks from somewhere behind those eyes.
>
> > "Winterstalker. The rank is yours. Come to Phaulkonmere tomorrow morning. Four matters need explaining, and your companions may wait at the gate."
>
> The owl turns its head once toward the street, then lifts off the sill and glides away over the roofs.

> [!gamemaster]
>
> The message is 22 words, and anyone nearby hears it, so a companion at the table knows where the member is going. The owl carries no reply, and Melannor expects the member in the morning.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jeryth cannot leave the garden, so the 8th-level castings run through the same sprig as the lower ones. They take more of her attention than the lower castings, which is why she asks for a week's notice.
> - Melannor dislikes leaving Phaulkonmere for long. He comes when he is called, keeps his own judgment when the plan fails and returns to the garden when the mission ends.
> - An advance report covers every Beast, Plant and Elemental that the location's entry places there. It says nothing about creatures of any other type.
> - Sarna Dath is one of the two south quay fisherfolk in the Summerstrider network. She is the only one who knows the Enclave by name, and she has sent it reports for four years.
> - Sarna reports only what she sees from her boat and the quay. She does not know who owns any ship she writes about, and neither does Melannor.
> - The carnival fleet is Jarlaxle's, and Bregan D'aerthe sails it. Nobody in this Event says either name. Sarna calls the ships "the carnival fleet" and the people aboard "the *Eyecatcher*'s crew".
> - Nobody in this Event names the Splinter's master, and nobody points at the building one block north of the garden. Melannor knows neither, and he says so if he is asked.
> - Jeryth has felt a dreaming presence below the city for months. She does not know its name, she says only "Something dreams below.", and nobody in this Event names Illuun.

### Naming the Rank

Phaulkonmere stands in the Southern Ward, and the gate is open before the member arrives. A member who holds the east gate key (**East Gate Key Held** is marked for them) can come at any hour. Otherwise they come at nine by the main gate. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open when you reach it, and the street outside is busy with morning carts. Past the gate the paving gives way to grass, and the garden inside is older than the street around it. The oldest oak rises ahead with its roots across the path, and Melannor waits beside it with a pair of pruning shears at his belt.
>
> > "Good morning, Winterstalker. The rank is yours, and four matters come with it. Please walk with me to the pavilion, and I will explain each one."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper at His Post**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere and a druid, who agrees at this rank to leave the garden and walk into the field.
>
> Melannor is Friendly and plain. He walks at an even pace, stops to straighten a plant while he talks and answers a joke with a literal reply. He uses no contractions, never swears and calls the member by name. He explains all four benefits before he takes a question.
>
> Melannor is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Winterstalker benefit gives the member, and how to use it.
> - Why the rank belongs to one member and not to the party.
> - Sarna Dath and what she writes.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault, the dreaming thing's name, the Splinter's master, the house one block north or who owns the ships Sarna watches. He answers each by saying that he does not know, or that it is not his to say.

> [!qna]**What does Winterstalker give me?**
>
> Melannor holds up one finger for each benefit as he names it.
>
> > "I will walk beside you on one mission in each quest. Jeryth will cast a spell of the eighth level for you. I will give you a report on the animals, plants and elementals at a place before you go there. Sarna Dath will write to you about the harbor."

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not the party's?**
>
> > "I write each rank beside a name, and this rank has no line for your companions. They may rest in the garden, but I will not walk into the field at their request."

> [!qna]**What happens if my Renown falls?**
>
> > "The rank stays with you, because you earned it. All four benefits wait until your Renown is restored, and I will tell you at the gate when that happens."

### Melannor in the Field

Melannor leads the member east along the gravel to the pavilion, a roof of living willow over a plain bench, beside the oldest oak. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The willow roof of the pavilion throws a moving shade across the bench, and the oldest oak stands close enough that its roots lift the corner of the floor. Melannor sits at one end with his hands on his knees and waits until you are seated before he speaks.
>
> > "The garden needs me, and I do not leave it often. I will leave it for you, once in each quest, and I will stay until the work is done."

> [!qna]**What will you do on a mission?**
>
> > "I will follow your plan. If the plan fails, I will stand between the danger and anyone who cannot run, and I will use my own judgment from there."

> [!qna]**What will you not do?**
>
> > "I will not strike the Watch or a Lord, and I will not strike anyone who has surrendered. I will not kill a captive, whatever the plan says."

> [!qna]**What if a fight goes badly for you?**
>
> > "I will withdraw when I have lost half my strength, and I will take any of you who has fallen if I can reach you. Please let me go when I do."

> [!exploration]**Melannor's Service**
>
> A mission here is one task inside the quest the party is playing, such as a raid, an infiltration or a search. The six Emerald Enclave faction missions are not eligible, because their scenes fix where Melannor stands.
>
> - **Contact:** Melannor at the gate by day, or at any hour for a member who holds the east gate key. A written request left in the gate box is answered by a note in the box by noon the next day.
> - **The request:** the member names the quest, the mission, the surface place where it starts and the hour. A request with no named mission is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Notice:** three days. A request with less notice is refused and spends nothing, and a request withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent either.
> - **Service:** Melannor meets the member at the place at the hour named. He stays until the mission is finished or abandoned, or until three days have passed. Then he returns to the garden.
> - **Limit:** one mission per member per quest. A quest is one of the named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**, and events, faction missions and rank events are not quests. The use counts against the quest the party is in when Melannor joins, and a call not made before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Underground:** Melannor walks into Undermountain on foot with the party. The limits in **Below the Surface** apply to everything he casts there.
> - **Several members:** each Winterstalker has a use of their own. Two members who ask for the same mission share one Melannor, and only the first to ask spends a use.
> - **Who else:** Melannor follows the plan of the member who called him, and his companions come along with the party. He takes no order from a companion who is not an Enclave member.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who orders Melannor to attack the Watch, a Lord or a surrendered creature loses his service for a tenday. He refuses the order, and the loss begins when he returns to the garden.
> - **If he falls:** Melannor withdraws before that happens. If the party keeps him in a fight until he falls to 0 Hit Points, he is Unconscious and stable, Jeryth's roots carry him back to the garden by morning, and his service is closed to every member for the rest of the quest.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and Melannor's service is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!gamemaster]**Melannor as an Ally**
>
> Use the 2024 **Druid** stat block for Melannor (AC 13, 44 Hit Points). He also has Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, he cannot be magically put to sleep, he has Darkvision 60 feet, and he speaks Common and Elvish.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, a Druid is a CR 2 ally. His Power is 28 for levels 1 to 4, 23 for levels 5 to 10 and 19 for level 11 and above. Add it to the party's Power only for a combat in which he fights, and recalculate the difficulty and the day cost. Party Power with Melannor at levels 5 to 8:
>
> | Level | 3 PCs | 4 PCs | 5 PCs |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 5 | 119 | 151 | 183 |
> | 6 | 128 | 163 | 198 |
> | 7 | 146 | 187 | 228 |
> | 8 | 155 | 199 | 243 |
>
> Melannor is light for a Renown 25 ally. Build the fight from the allied total, or keep it at the base budget and treat him as a safety net, and pick one for the scene. At levels 9 and 10, add 23 to the party's Power from the CR 2.0 level table.
>
> #### Melannor's Tactics
> To begin combat, Melannor stands between the enemy and anyone who cannot run and casts *Entangle* on the ground in front of the enemy. During combat, he:
>
> - Casts *Thunderwave* when two or more enemies stand within reach of him.
> - Casts *Moonbeam* (Concentration) on a group once *Entangle* and *Thunderwave* are spent.
> - Uses Verdant Wisp from range on the enemy that is hurting the most injured character, and Vine Staff only against an enemy within 5 feet of someone who cannot run.
> - Saves his daily spells for a fight that is going badly, and uses none on something the party could handle with a sword.
>
> Melannor withdraws toward the exit the member named when he is reduced to 22 Hit Points, and he takes a fallen character with him if he can reach one. He stops fighting when the member calls it off or the mission ends. He does not fight the Watch or a Lord, and he never kills a captive.

### Jeryth's Eighth Level

Jeryth speaks while the member and Melannor are still seated in the pavilion. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The leaves of the willow roof stir although the air is still, and a clear woman's voice speaks from the garden around you. When you take out the sprig from the oldest oak, it has put out a fourth leaf, pale green and still curled.
>
> > "Eighth level takes much of me. Ask a week ahead, and ask for what matters."
>
> The leaf opens slowly in your hand, and a warm breeze crosses the pavilion.

> [!social]**The Voice in the Garden**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, she/her) :: a disembodied presence in the gardens of Phaulkonmere, who casts through a sprig because she cannot leave them.
>
> She speaks one or two short sentences at a time and never repeats them. Jeryth is Friendly and distant. The leaves move when she speaks, and the garden answers with a warm breeze. She never swears and takes no side in mortal politics. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest".
>
> Jeryth is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The 8th-level list and the castings she makes through the sprig.
> - What dreams below, which she answers in one sentence.
>
> She will not discuss the gold, the Stone, the vault or any faction. She says "That question is for the Lords to settle." to any question about the gold.

> [!qna]**Which spells can you cast?**
>
> > "The six Melannor will read to you. Nothing else."

> [!qna]**Why a week?**
>
> > "I must prepare the garden to do without me."

> [!qna]**Can you cast it below the city?**
>
> > "No. My hand ends at the surface."

> [!qna]**What do you ask of a Winterstalker?**
>
> > "The Lady of the Forest asks that you leave the land better than you found it."

> [!qna]**Is there anything I should know?**
>
> > "Something dreams below."

> [!exploration]**Casting an Eighth-Level Spell**
>
> The sprig from **Autumnreaver** now offers a second list. Its first list stays available, and the two share one use in each quest.
>
> - **Contact:** Melannor at the gate by day, or at any hour for a member who holds the east gate key. A written request left in the gate box is answered by a note in the box by noon the next day.
> - **The request:** the member names the quest, one spell from the list below, the place on the surface and the day. A request with no named quest, spell or place is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Notice:** seven days for a spell of the eighth level. A request with less notice is refused and spends nothing, and a request withdrawn two full days ahead is not spent either. The first list keeps its notice of three days.
> - **The casting:** on the day named, the member holds the sprig and says the spell's name aloud. Jeryth casts it as though she stood where the sprig is, with a spellcasting modifier of +4, a spell save DC of 15 and a spell attack bonus of +7. She holds Concentration from the garden for the spell's full duration, and nothing that happens to the member breaks it.
> - **Place:** the surface of Waterdeep, including its cellars and sewers. The sprig does not work in Undermountain, because Jeryth casts from the garden. *Control Weather* also needs open sky, so it fails in a cellar or a sewer.
> - **Limit:** one casting per member per quest, from either list. The use counts against the quest the party is in when the member asks, and a casting not asked for before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Who else:** only the member can ask, though a companion benefits when the spell's targets include them.
> - **Refusal:** Jeryth refuses *Earthquake*, *Sunburst* and *Tsunami* when the area covers an occupied building, a street with passers-by or a Watch post. She answers "Find another way." and nothing is spent.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who asks Jeryth to harm the Watch, a Lord or a creature that has surrendered loses the sprig for a tenday. The loss begins when Melannor hears of it.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and the 8th-level list is suspended until the Renown is restored. The first list stays open while the Renown is 10 or higher.
>
> The six spells on the list:
>
> - *Animal Shapes* (8th level): willing creatures within 30 feet each become a Large or smaller Beast of CR 4 or lower for 24 hours. They cannot cast spells in beast form.
> - *Antipathy/Sympathy* (8th level): an object or a place the member names repels or draws creatures of one kind the member chooses.
> - *Control Weather* (8th level): the weather around the sprig changes. It needs open sky.
> - *Earthquake* (8th level): the ground shakes in a wide circle at the place named, on the surface only.
> - *Sunburst* (8th level): a blast of sunlight burns and blinds the creatures in a sphere at the place named.
> - *Tsunami* (8th level): a wall of water rises at the place named.
>
> Spells of the 6th and 7th level are on neither list, so *Heal* and *Heroes' Feast* are not available. No spell that returns the dead is on either list, and neither is *Wish*. Jeryth answers a request for any of them with "No." and nothing is spent.

> [!gamemaster]**Below the Surface**
>
> The sprig does not work in Undermountain. These limits from Dungeon of the Mad Mage apply to everything else the Enclave offers there:
>
> - Teleportation, *plane shift* and *word of recall* fail, and so does any spell that moves a creature from one level to another.
> - Spells cannot reshape the walls, floors or ceilings of the dungeon. *Earthquake* opens no fissure and drops no ceiling, and *Stone Shape* changes nothing. *Wall of Stone* can still raise a new wall in a corridor.
> - *Control Weather* needs the outdoors and fails below ground.
> - *Sending* never reaches Halaster.
> - Beasts cross between levels only on foot, so Melannor's *Animal Messenger* reaches nobody on another level.

### The Advance Report

Melannor stays on the bench and takes a folded sheet from inside his coat. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor smooths a folded sheet on the bench with the flat of his hand. The paper holds a pencil sketch of a flooded cellar, with a long dark shape drawn at the deep end and rows of small neat figures down the margin.
>
> > "A watcher in the Field Ward drew this last month. It shows a giant crocodile in the cellar of a shuttered tannery, and I do not know how it came there. It rests at the deep end in the cold hours before dawn, and it feeds at dusk."

> [!qna]**What does the report do for me?**
>
> > "Tell me the place you mean to go. My watchers will tell me which animals, plants and elementals live there. The report will be ready the next morning, and it will help you with each of them."

> [!qna]**Does it tell me what else is hiding there?**
>
> > "It does not. My watchers know the animals, the plants and the elementals, and the rest is for you to find."

> [!exploration]**Asking for an Advance Report**
>
> - **Contact:** Melannor at the gate by day, or at any hour for a member who holds the east gate key. A written request left in the gate box is answered by a report in the box at the next dawn.
> - **The request:** the member names one place on the surface of Waterdeep, including its cellars and sewers, by street or landmark. A request for a place in Undermountain is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Notice:** one day. The report is ready at dawn the next morning.
> - **The report:** it lists every Beast, Plant and Elemental that the place's entry names, with the number, where each rests, when it is awake and what it eats or fears. A Celestial, a Fey, a Construct or any other type is not in it. If nothing of those three types lives at the place, Melannor says so and nothing is spent.
> - **Beasts:** the member has Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks to calm or direct a Beast the report names. The check does not work on a Plant or an Elemental.
> - **Plants and Elementals:** the member has Advantage on Intelligence (Nature) checks to read the creature's pattern, avoid its attention or find its weakness.
> - **The first Initiative roll:** the member has Advantage on the first Initiative roll they make at the place against a creature the report names.
> - **Duration:** the Advantage lasts for one visit, from the member's arrival at the place until they leave it.
> - **Limit:** one report per member each tenday, counted from the day of the member's last request. A report that lists nothing is not counted.
> - **Who else:** the Advantage belongs to the member alone. A companion can read the report and gains none.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who uses a report to hunt or capture a creature that has hurt nobody loses the service for a tenday when Melannor hears of it.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and the reports are suspended until the Renown is restored.

### The Harbor Notes

Melannor leads the member along the gravel to the gate box, which stands on a post beside the gate, and takes a folded square of paper from it. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The paper is a square of fish-wrapping that smells of brine and tar, and your name is written across it in pencil in a quick, square hand. Melannor holds it out with both hands and waits while you unfold it.
>
> > "This came to the box at dawn. Please read it here, and I will answer what I can."
>
> The letter inside reads:
>
> > Sarna Dath, south quay, blue-and-white boat. I run fish for a living, and I watch the harbor because I can't help it. Melannor says you can be trusted with what I see. Every tenday I'll write down which ships came or went at the wrong hours and what they carried. I'll write what I saw and not a word I didn't. Find me on the quay at dawn if you've got questions.
>
> A second slip is folded inside the letter, and Melannor waits for you to read that one as well.

The slip carries the first note from the lists under **Sarna's Harbor Notes** that applies on the day of the meeting. Read it to the member aloud, in Sarna's words.

> [!qna]**You said the watchers would stay unknown to me.**
>
> > "I did, and I kept that promise until now. Sarna is the only watcher who knows the Enclave by name. A report on a ship must reach you in time, so I made the exception."

> [!qna]**Whose ships does she watch?**
>
> > "I do not know. She writes what she sees at the quay and does not guess, and neither do I. What it means is for you to find out."

> [!qna]**Can I trust her?**
>
> > "She is reliable. She has sent us reports for four years, and I have found none of them false."

> [!exploration]**Sarna's Harbor Notes**
>
> - **Contact:** a note from Sarna in the Phaulkonmere gate box every tenday. The morning after a note arrives, Melannor sends a pigeon to the member's lodging with the line "Sarna has left a note for you in the gate box." That line is 11 words. It is his own *Animal Messenger*, and it does not count against the member's Summerstrider relay. Sarna's boat lies at the south quay, and she is aboard at dawn.
> - **Place:** the quays of the Dock Ward and the private piers of the Sea Ward that can be seen from the water.
> - **Notice:** none. The first note is the slip inside Sarna's letter, and the member's tenday count starts from the day of the meeting.
> - **The notes:** each is fixed, and the lists below give them in order. The member reads the next unread note from the list that applies on the day it arrives. A member who starts after the fleet has sailed begins with the Pier 17 note.
> - **A question:** the member can go to the boat at dawn and ask Sarna one question about the latest note. She answers only what she saw.
> - **Limit:** one note per member every tenday, counted from the day of the member's last note, and one question for each note.
> - **Who else:** the notes and the question belong to the member. A companion who asks is given a fish and told to ask the member.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who brings a Response Team, a ship's crew or anyone else who watches ships to Sarna's boat, or who names her to a faction, loses her notes for a tenday. Sarna moors elsewhere for that tenday, and Melannor tells the member where when it ends.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and Melannor asks Sarna to hold her notes until the Renown is restored.
>
> **Before Tarsakh 20**, while the carnival fleet is in port:
>
> - **The first note:** The carnival fleet keeps the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser* at a private pier in Smugglers' Dock, and the *Eyecatcher* lies at anchor a quarter-mile out. The *Eyecatcher*'s crew never comes ashore to drink, and a skiff takes their supplies out at dusk. On parade days the pier is nearly empty. The fleet sails at dawn on Tarsakh 20.
> - **Later notes:** Nothing new at the pier. The stores are still going aboard, and the sailing day stands.
>
> **From Tarsakh 20**, once the fleet has sailed, one note in each tenday, in this order. After the fourth, the notes begin again from the first:
>
> - **Pier 17:** Two flat barges come in light to Pier 17 and leave riding low before each tide. The dockhands have stopped talking about it, and two of them took coin and left the ward.
> - **The trader:** A single-masted trader with no name on her stern ties up at the west quay once a tenday after dark. Sealed crates go straight onto a covered wagon, and the same six men in grey coats walk beside it and never ahead of it.
> - **The Sea Ward barge:** A covered barge leaves a private pier in the Sea Ward at midnight and returns before dawn. The harbor master's clerk takes a coin each tenday to leave the trips out of the book, and the crew will not answer a hail.
> - **The black skiff:** A narrow black skiff with no lamp comes in from open water to Smugglers' Dock after moonset. The people who step off keep their hoods up, and none of them speaks.

> [!gamemaster]**What Sarna Has Seen**
>
> Sarna names no owner, and Melannor knows no more than the notes say. If the Faire's departure has been moved up to Tarsakh 15, use that date in place of Tarsakh 20 throughout this Event. The fleet sails on that date whatever the party did. The notes map to the villain factions as follows:
>
> - **The carnival fleet:** Jarlaxle's Bregan D'aerthe sails it, and its crew are drow in human form. Sarna takes them for odd sailors.
> - **Pier 17:** the barges belong to Xanathar's Guild and carry spoil out of its cargo tunnel under the south quay. The two hands who left are Garrit Moll and Tobiah Fenn, from **The Grells in the Dock Ward**.
> - **The trader:** it belongs to Manshoon's Zhentarim and carries reagents for the Splinter's thrall-conditioning. The wagon goes to Kolat Towers in the Trades Ward.
> - **The Sea Ward barge:** it carries the Cassalanter household's private supplies to the villa's jetty. Sarna suspects only that a rich house is hiding something, and nobody in this Event knows more.
> - **The black skiff:** its passengers are Bregan D'aerthe couriers, who land to meet the company's agents in the city after the fleet has gone.
>
> A note gives the party a lead to one lair. It opens no path that the lair's own quest does not give.

> [!social]**The Fisher on the South Quay**
>
> Sarna Dath (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: a south quay fisher in her fifties, who keeps a blue-and-white boat and a sharp eye on every hull that passes it.
>
> Sarna is Friendly once the member has said who sent them, and busy either way. She guts fish while she talks and does not stop for a question. She swears mildly and often. She answers what she saw and refuses to guess at the rest.
>
> Sarna is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The latest note, ship by ship.
> - The tides and the quay hands, in passing.
>
> She will not discuss who owns a ship, what a crew is doing in port or anything she has not seen with her own eyes.

> [!qna]**Whose ships are they?**
>
> > "Damned if I know. I see hulls, crates and crews, and I never see the lease. Find that out yourselves, and be careful doing it."

> [!qna]**Is there anything you left out of the note?**
>
> > "Not a thing. If I'd seen more, I'd have written it down. Come back when there's a new note, and I'll tell you what I can."

### Leaving Phaulkonmere

Melannor walks the member the last few steps to the threshold. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gravel is loud under your boots, and the pigeons on the wall turn their heads as you pass. Melannor stops at the threshold and faces you with his hands clasped in front of him.
>
> > "The soil is uneasy. I will be glad to walk beside you when you call me."
>
> He turns back toward the oak, and the pigeons on the wall settle.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Winterstalker earns further Renown from the missions and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Emerald Enclave page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions what Melannor and Jeryth said and what Sarna wrote. Melannor's service, the 8th-level casting, the advance report and the harbor notes stay individual benefits, and Melannor, Jeryth and Sarna turn away a companion who asks in the member's name.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard all four arrangements, has read Sarna's first note and has left Phaulkonmere. Melannor's service, the casting, the first report and the first question for Sarna can be asked for at any later visit.

## Overview

Melannor Fellbranch names an Emerald Enclave member a Winterstalker and explains the rank's four benefits: his own company in the field, a spell of the eighth level from Jeryth Phaulkon, advance reports on animals and plants, and harbor notes from a south quay fisher.

## Summary

We were named Winterstalkers when an owl told us to come to Phaulkonmere. Melannor will walk beside each of us on one mission in every quest, and Jeryth will cast a spell of the eighth level through the sprig. He also gave us a report on a crocodile in a flooded cellar, to show how his watchers work. A fisher named Sarna Dath will write to us about the ships in the harbor.
