# Winterstalker

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Enclave renown reaches 25. An owl delivers Melannor's summons at dusk — a different animal and a different hour than any previous communication. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Melannor's dusk summons by owl
> - Hear Melannor confirm he will accompany the party on one mission per quest, in person
> - Hear Jeryth expand her spell access to 8th level
> - See a current advance intelligence report demonstrating how beast-tracking works
> - Receive a letter from Sarna Dath at the south quay, opening automatic harbor network reports
>
> #### Background
>
> The Winterstalker rank is the second-highest in the Enclave. Jeryth's 8th-level access represents a meaningful increase in divine attention, and she acknowledges it as such. She will not say it costs her greatly, but she will note that eighth level requires care about when it is asked.
>
> Melannor's personal accompaniment is offered without ceremony. He dislikes leaving Phaulkonmere for extended periods, though he will not say so. He shows up when called.
>
> Sarna Dath is a harbor fisherfolk contact who has filed environmental reports for the Enclave for four years. Her blue-and-white boat is moored at the south quay. At this rank, her focus shifts from environmental anomalies to vessel movements: departure schedules, cargo manifests, anything on the water that doesn't match the harbor master's records.

### The Owl at Dusk

An owl lands on the promoted character's window ledge as the light fades.

> [!readaloud]
> As the daylight goes from the windows, an owl drops onto the ledge outside — large and patient, watching whoever is nearest with the particular intensity of a trained animal with a message to deliver. When the window is opened, it speaks in Melannor's voice.
>
> > "Winterstalker. Come tomorrow morning."
>
> It departs without waiting for a response, dropping off the ledge and disappearing into the evening before anyone has turned around.

### At Phaulkonmere

Melannor meets the promoted character at the gate the following morning and walks them to the pavilion in the garden's eastern section — a covered seat near the oldest oaks that the party may not have noticed before. He takes his seat on the bench and looks at the character for a moment before speaking.

> [!readaloud]
> The pavilion sits near the oldest oaks in the eastern section of the garden, sheltered and quiet, with the bench worn in a way that suggests it is used for exactly this kind of conversation. Melannor takes his seat and looks at the promoted character for a moment.
>
> > "This is the rank where I stop sending things and come myself."

### Melannor's Accompaniment

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: an Emerald Enclave druid who manages Phaulkonmere's grounds and, at this rank, comes into the field when called.
>
> Melannor states what personal accompaniment means without embellishment: one mission per quest, full druid capabilities, his own judgment when the plan stops working. He will take the party's direction. He will not pretend he has no opinion about how things are going.
>
> Conversation topics Melannor covers at the pavilion include:
> - What personal accompaniment means in practice
> - How the advance intelligence benefit works
> - Sarna Dath and what she tracks
>
> Specific dialogue is presented below.

> [!qna]**What does "come yourself" mean?**
>
> > "Call me when it matters. I'll be there." A pause. "One mission per quest. I carry what I carry. If the plan stops working, I use my own judgment. You can expect that."

> [!gamemaster]**Running Melannor in the Field**
>
> Use the **Druid** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*). Melannor favors terrain-control spells: *spike growth*, *entangle*, *plant growth*, *fog cloud*. He will not spend a spell slot on something the party could handle without him. He returns to Phaulkonmere after the mission, immediately, regardless of what happens next.

### Jeryth's 8th-Level Access

Jeryth's voice arrives over the pavilion without preamble.

> [!readaloud]
> Jeryth's voice arrives over the pavilion, calm and unhurried, without any sound of approach.
>
> > "I'll cast spells up to eighth level for you now. Once per quest, still. Eighth level is significant attention. Choose what you ask for."
>
> She is quiet for a moment, letting that settle, before she continues.
>
> > "Sunburst. Control weather. Earthquake. Animal shapes. I can cast them. Ask when it's worth asking."

> [!gamemaster]**Managing Jeryth's 8th-Level Spells**
>
> At this rank, Jeryth can cast *control weather*, *sunburst*, *earthquake*, *tsunami*, *antipathy/sympathy*, *animal shapes*, and all other druid spells of 8th level or lower. She is aware of how much attention this requires. She won't refuse a reasonable request, but a character who asks for *earthquake* during a minor complication hears silence and then: "Find another way."

### The Advance Intelligence Benefit

Melannor produces a folded document from his coat — a current example from the network. He hands it over.

> [!readaloud]
> The document is dense and specific: hand-lettered behavioral notes on a Giant Crocodile found in the Southern Ward cisterns — approach and retreat patterns, feeding times, a sketch of its resting position in the coldest hours of morning. At the bottom, in Melannor's hand: *Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks. Advantage on first Initiative roll within this location.*
>
> > "If you know a beast, plant creature, or elemental is at your target before you arrive, we'll have a report ready. Tell me the location. The network usually turns it around in 24 hours."
>
> He folds the document and leaves it on the bench without taking it back.

> [!qna]**How do I request a report?**
>
> > "Tell me the location and the creature type. I'll have the network confirm what's there and map what it does. Twenty-four hours, usually. The note at the bottom is what the report delivers — advantage on the relevant checks."

### The Harbor Contact

A letter has been left at Phaulkonmere's gate, addressed to the promoted character by name.

> [!readaloud]
> There is a letter on the gateposts, addressed to the promoted character by name, written in a hand that is compact and even across the whole page.
>
> > *Sarna Dath — south quay, blue-and-white boat. I run fish. I also keep track of ships that depart at wrong hours. Melannor says you're trustworthy. Ship manifests, cargo movements, departure schedules: if it seems like something you should know, I'll write it down and send it along. Find me at the quay.*

> [!qna]**Sarna Dath?**
>
> Melannor has no further commentary. He picks up the letter and sets it back down.
>
> > "She's reliable."

> [!gamemaster]**Sarna's Reports**
>
> Sarna's handwritten notes arrive by gull once per tenday at minimum — more often when shipping is active. She tracks departures from the Sea Maidens Faire fleet, unusual night cargo in the deep harbor, and any manifests that don't match the harbor master's records. Her reports arrive automatically; the character does not need to ask.

### Concluding the Event

Melannor walks the character to the gate and returns to his groundskeeping without ceremony.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**

>

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> The **Master of the Wild** rank event fires when this character's renown reaches 50. Parties are not expected to reach Renown 50 during Dragon Heist — this event is designed for the **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. This event does not award a Milestone Point.

## Overview

After reaching Renown 25 with the Emerald Enclave, an owl arrives at dusk with Melannor's summons. At Phaulkonmere, he confirms he will accompany the party on one mission per quest in person. Jeryth expands her spellcasting to 8th level and names the commitment plainly. A current advance intelligence report demonstrates how beast-tracking works, and a letter from a harbor contact named Sarna Dath opens a direct line to ship manifests and departure schedules.

## Summary

We reached Winterstalker rank with the Emerald Enclave. At Phaulkonmere, Melannor told us he would come in person now, not just send messages. Jeryth expanded her spell access to 8th level and made clear that eighth level requires careful judgment about when to ask. We saw a sample advance intelligence report on a creature in the Southern Ward cisterns, and a letter arrived from Sarna Dath at the south quay — she tracks vessel movements and will send us reports automatically.
