# Design Notes: Winterstalker

## Benefits as Procedures

The restored draft gave the four benefits as short statements, such as Melannor "accompanies the party" and Jeryth casts "up to eighth level". An advance report arrived "usually in 24 hours", and a gull brought harbor notes. Each is now a per-member procedure with a contact, a place, a notice, a limit and a rule for companions. It also has a loss rule and a suspension when Renown falls below 25. The owl's message is 22 words, counted in a GM line under the readaloud, where the restored version ran four. The animals carry the summons, and the benefits themselves run through the gate, the gate box and the sprig, as in the other Enclave rank events.

## Melannor, Jeryth and the Advance Report

Melannor uses the 2024 Druid block with the Dragon Heist traits, at Power 28, 23 or 19 by tier. The event treats him as a safety net, because that is light for a Renown 25 ally. His mission is any task inside a named quest. The six Enclave faction missions are excluded, since The Grells in the Dock Ward and The Dreamer's Reach say where he stands. Dragon Heist makes him Chaotic Good, and the campaign keeps Neutral Good. If he falls, he is carried home and closed to every member for the rest of the quest. In Force Grey a dead Rhendar ends the benefit, and this event spares the garden's keeper because he appears again in later quests.

Jeryth's list is the six 8th-level spells in the mechanics reference. The research report listed *Heal* and *Heroes' Feast*, which are 6th level, and the event says so. The Factions Guide gives one casting a quest "up to 8th level", so the 8th-level list shares its use with the 5th-level list from Autumnreaver. Seven days' notice is invented. The advance report fixes the Guide's wording: Animal Handling works on Beasts only, and Intelligence (Nature) covers Plants and Elementals. The crocodile site moved from the Southern Ward cisterns to a shuttered tannery in the Field Ward. It no longer overlaps the Castle Ward cisterns of The Fouled Channel and The Dreamer's Reach.

## Sarna Dath's Harbor Notes

The restored notes covered the carnival fleet for ever, though the fleet sails on Tarsakh 20. The notes now follow the date. Before it, they cover the fleet and say what Sarna sees. After it, one fixed note a tenday covers a ship from each villain faction in turn. Sarna names no owner and no speaker says Jarlaxle, Zardoz or Bregan D'aerthe, so only the GM block ties each note to its faction. Summerstrider says the south quay watchers do not know the Enclave by name, so Sarna becomes the one exception, and Melannor says so when he is asked.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented in this draft:
- Sarna's alignment, species, age and voice, since she has no profile.
- The Field Ward tannery and its crocodile.
- The pavilion's willow roof and the sprig's fourth leaf.
- The seven-day, three-day and one-day notices.
- The pigeon's eleven-word line.
- The four ship notes, with their west quay, six grey coats, Sea Ward barge and black skiff.
