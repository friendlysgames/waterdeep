# Summerstrider

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Emerald Enclave member first reaches Renown 3, which happens at the debrief of **The Undercliff Scarecrows** together with **A Seat at Phaulkonmere**. Melannor Fellbranch names the rank at the gate of Phaulkonmere and gives that member the three Summerstrider benefits. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn how to send one message through Melannor's *animal messenger* relay every tenday.
> - Hear which healing spells Jeryth Phaulkon casts in the garden, and when.
> - Ask Melannor for one report per ward every tenday from the Enclave's network of watchers.
>
> Only the member is addressed. Companions who are not Emerald Enclave members wait at the gate or in the outer garden and gain nothing.

### The Grey Pigeon at Noon

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 3, a grey pigeon finds them wherever they are lodging. Use the Raven stat block, which is a Tiny Beast of CR 0. Every qualifying member is invited on their own account, so several members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the pigeon at their first surface lodging on return, and the meeting moves to dusk on the following day. If someone kills the pigeon before it speaks, a second one arrives at the next noon.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A grey pigeon drops onto the sill with a clatter of wings and walks along it, turning its head to look at each of you in turn. It stops in front of you, and a deep, even voice speaks from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > Summerstrider. The rank is yours. Come to Phaulkonmere this evening, by the east gate if you hold the key. Three matters need explaining.
>
> The pigeon waits a moment, then opens its wings and flies off over the roofs.

> [!gamemaster]
>
> The message is 23 words. Anyone nearby hears it, so a companion at the table knows where the member is going. The pigeon carries no reply, and Melannor expects the member at dusk.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jeryth has watched the member's work through the roots and the groundwater. The rank records her decision to keep a closer eye on the member, and Melannor's book follows her.
> - The healing is a favor she grants to people who are hurt doing the Enclave's work. She does not offer it to anyone else, and she does not offer it for free to a stranger.
> - The network is made of ordinary people: gardeners in the City of the Dead, park wardens near the Field of Triumph and two fisherfolk on the south quay. They do not know they report to a druid, and they tell Melannor what they notice.
> - Nobody in this Event can say Manshoon's name, or call Kolat Towers his home, unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member. Melannor says "the Splinter" or "the other cell", and the Trades Ward report stays closed until the outcome is marked.
> - Phaulkonmere is one block south of Kolat Towers. Before the gate, nobody points at the towers or says what they are.
> - Nobody in the Enclave knows what the Sea Ward villa is hiding, and nobody names its owners. Melannor reports only what the butterflies do.
> - Jeryth has felt a dreaming presence below the city for months. She does not know its name, and she says only "Something dreams below."

### Naming the Rank

Phaulkonmere stands in the Southern Ward, and the gate is open before the member arrives. If the member holds the east gate key (**East Gate Key Held** is marked for them), they can arrive at any hour and Melannor meets them at the east gate. Otherwise they come at dusk by the main gate. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open when you reach it, and the street outside is paved and noisy with evening traffic. Past the gate the paving gives way to grass, and the oaks overhead are older than the walls built around them. The oldest oak stands in the middle of the garden with its roots across the path, and Melannor waits beside it with his hands clasped in front of him.
>
> > Summerstrider. Good evening. Please walk with me, and your companions may wait by the gate, where the benches are.

If companions come along, they wait on the benches by the gate, and Melannor speaks to them no further. Jeryth does not speak until the healing scene below.

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere and a druid, who sends the Enclave's messages by animal and delivers his reports in a calm baritone.
>
> Melannor is Friendly and plain. He walks slowly and stops to straighten a plant while he talks. He uses no contractions, never swears and does not recognise a joke, and he answers one with a literal reply. He calls the member by name every time.
>
> Melannor is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Summerstrider benefit gives the member, and how to use it.
> - Why the rank belongs to one member and not to the party.
> - The watchers behind the network, in broad terms and without names.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault, the dreaming thing's name, the Splinter's master, or the house one block north. He answers each by saying that he does not know, or that it is not his to say.

> [!qna]**What does Summerstrider give me?**
>
> Melannor holds up one finger for each benefit as he names it.
>
> > You may send one message each tenday, and Jeryth will heal injuries you take in our work. I will also give you one report each tenday from every ward. I will explain each in turn.

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not the party's?**
>
> > I write ranks by name. Your companions may help you, and they are welcome in the garden, but the rank has no line for them. Jeryth heals the person she has named.

> [!qna]**Who are the watchers?**
>
> > Gardeners, wardens and fisherfolk. They notice what the soil and the water do, and they do not know the Enclave by name. I would like them to stay unknown.

> [!qna]**What happens if my Renown falls?**
>
> > The rank stays with you, because it was earned. The three benefits wait until your Renown is restored. I will tell you when that happens.

### The Relay

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor leads you along a gravel path to a wooden box mounted on a post beside the gate, with a slot in the lid and a leaf carved on the front. A row of pigeons and crows sits along the wall above it and watches without moving.
>
> > This is the gate box. Write your message and leave it here, or give it to me when I am at the gate. It may be twenty-five words long, and no longer.

> [!exploration]**Sending a Message**
>
> - **Contact:** Melannor at Phaulkonmere, or a written note left in the gate box at any hour. He collects the box at dawn and at noon.
> - **The request:** the member names a recipient by description and a place Melannor has visited, and writes the message in 25 words or fewer. Melannor has visited every ward of the city, the Undercliff and the farms outside the walls. A request for a place he has not visited is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **The animal:** Melannor casts *Animal Messenger* on a Tiny Beast of CR 0. He uses a pigeon or a crow for most wards and a falcon (the Hawk block) for the Field Ward and Undercliff. The animal speaks the message once in Melannor's baritone and leaves. Anyone nearby hears it.
> - **The reply:** none is carried unless the message asks for one. Then the animal waits for up to 25 words and brings them back to the member's lodging.
> - **Delay:** within Waterdeep and its farmland, the message arrives in a few hours, and Melannor sends it within the hour when the member asks in person.
> - **Limit:** one message per member each tenday, counted from the day of the member's own last request. Sending through the gate box counts from the day Melannor collects it. A refused request is not spent.
> - **Range:** not Undermountain. A Tiny Beast crosses no level on its own, and the relay reaches nowhere below the surface.
> - **Companions:** a companion who is not an Enclave member cannot send, and Melannor refuses a request made in the member's name by anyone else.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who uses the relay to lure a response team to Phaulkonmere loses it for a tenday when Melannor hears of it.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the relay is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!qna]**Can I send it to someone who has not met you?**
>
> > Yes. The animal finds a recipient by description, so tell me what the person looks like and where they will be. I do not need their name.

> [!qna]**Can the animal answer questions on the way back?**
>
> > It cannot. It carries the words you give it and brings back what the recipient says within twenty-five words. I would keep the question short.

### Jeryth's Healing

Melannor leads the member to the oldest oak and stops beside its roots. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The leaves of the oak stir although the evening air is still, and a clear woman's voice speaks from the garden around you. You cannot tell where it comes from.
>
> > You carry old hurts from this work. Rest here.
>
> The vines along the wall loosen, and a warm breeze crosses the path.

> [!social]**The Voice in the Garden**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, she/her) :: a disembodied presence in the gardens of Phaulkonmere, who speaks one or two short sentences at a time and never repeats them.
>
> Jeryth is Friendly and distant. The leaves move when she speaks, and the garden answers her with flowers and breezes. She never swears and takes no side in mortal politics. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest".
>
> Jeryth is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Healing the member's injuries from Enclave business.
> - Rest in the garden.
> - What dreams below, in one sentence.
>
> She will not discuss the gold, the Stone, the vault or any faction. She says "That question is for the Lords to settle." to any question about the gold.

> [!qna]**What do you want in return?**
>
> > Nothing. Come back when you are hurt.

> [!qna]**What dreams below?**
>
> > Something old. It dreams below. Watch the water.

> [!qna]**Who gets the dragon's gold?**
>
> > That question is for the Lords to settle.

> [!exploration]**Healing at Phaulkonmere**
>
> - **Contact:** Jeryth, in the garden. The member walks in and names one spell from the list. No request is needed in writing.
> - **Place:** Phaulkonmere only, and the casting comes from the garden. The garden is open by the main gate by day, and at any hour for a member who holds the east gate key.
> - **Which injuries:** only those taken in Enclave business since the member's last visit, which means any Enclave mission, any report the member followed from the network or any fight the member took to protect an animal, a plant or an Enclave contact. Other injuries get "That was not our work." and nothing is spent.
> - **The list:** *Cure Wounds* (level 1), *Healing Word* (level 1), *Lesser Restoration* (level 2) and *Protection from Poison* (level 2). Jeryth casts each at its lowest level, with a spellcasting modifier of +4 and no costly components. She casts nothing above 2nd level here.
> - **Limit:** one casting per member for each Long Rest the member finishes. A member who rests in the garden can ask once on waking.
> - **Who else:** the member only. A hurt companion receives nothing, but may rest in the garden under the haven rules of the First Meeting.
> - **Notice:** none. Jeryth knows when a member is hurt before they reach the gate.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who brings a pursuer into the garden or fights inside the walls loses the healing for a tenday. The response teams never enter, but they may watch the street.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the healing is suspended until the Renown is restored.

### The Network

Melannor takes a folded paper from his coat and unfolds it on the lid of a rain barrel. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The paper is a ward map of Waterdeep with a different mark in each district, a leaf, a feather, a fish or a small cross. Melannor lays a finger on the Southern Ward, where the gardens stand, and turns his head to look at you.
>
> > Every tenday I will give you one report from each ward. Ask me for the ward you want, and I will tell you what my watchers noticed. I will not tell you what it means.

> [!gamemaster]
>
> Each report below is fixed, so read it as written. Melannor gives them in his own voice and does not explain them. If the member asks what a report means, he answers, "I do not know. I would like to."

> [!exploration]**Asking for a Report**
>
> - **Contact:** Melannor, at the gate or in the garden by day. He gives the report in person. A written request left in the gate box is answered by a note in the box by noon the next day. No animal carries a report, because the longest is longer than 25 words.
> - **The request:** the member names one ward. Melannor gives that ward's report from the table below.
> - **Limit:** one report per ward per member each tenday, counted from the day of that member's last request for that ward. The seven wards are counted separately, and one report does not spend another.
> - **Who else:** the member only. The member can tell companions what Melannor said, but Melannor gives a companion who asks nothing.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who gives the reports to a response team or sells them loses the network for a tenday when Melannor hears of it.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the network is suspended until the Renown is restored.
>
> The fixed reports:
>
> - **Sea Ward:** "A butterfly garden at the back of one villa keeps tropical species alive that should not survive Waterdeep's climate. Something in that garden is not natural. I do not mean the butterflies. I mean whatever is keeping them alive."
> - **Dock Ward, before Tarsakh 20:** "Fish keep away from the water beneath the hull of the *Eyecatcher*. The water there changes temperature in ways a ship should not cause."
> - **Dock Ward, from Tarsakh 20:** "Odd fungi follow the drains below the harbor. The water flow is cut against its gradient. Small animals will not return from certain junctions. Something large is being dug."
> - **Southern Ward:** "Jeryth has felt a wound in the ley lines near Coachlamp Lane for a tenday and a half. It is not healing. The Enclave has no lead on the cause."
> - **Trades Ward:** if **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member: "Dead birds lie at the foot of an invisible wall. The grass is dead in a band three feet wide around it. No insects live within twenty feet. Find the anchor, destroy it, and the field collapses." Otherwise: "Nothing the animals will carry."
> - **Field Ward and Undercliff:** if **Splinter Site Reported** is marked for the member: "The Undercliff animals will not cross the north fields at dusk. The chalk circle in the north fields is cold and empty. Nobody has come back to it." Otherwise: "The Undercliff animals will not cross the north fields at dusk."
> - **Castle Ward:** "Nothing the animals will carry. The cisterns run slow and quiet."
> - **North Ward:** "Nothing the animals will carry."

> [!qna]**Who are the watchers?**
>
> > Gardeners in the City of the Dead, park wardens near the Field of Triumph and two fisherfolk on the south quay. They send me what they notice, and I pass it to you.

> [!qna]**Why do some wards say nothing?**
>
> > The watchers see nothing there that they can carry to me. It does not mean nothing is happening. It means they have not seen it.

> [!qna]**What is the Splinter?**
>
> > It is the other cell of an old network, and it is working in this city. I do not know who leads it. I would tell you if I did.

### Leaving the Garden

Melannor walks the member back to the gate, and the gravel is quiet under their boots. He stops at the threshold and says, "The soil is uneasy. Please use these as they are meant to be used." Then he goes back to the oak, and the pigeons on the wall follow him with their heads.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Summerstrider earns further Renown from missions and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Emerald Enclave page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions what Melannor said and what the reports hold. The relay, the healing and the network stay individual benefits, and Melannor and Jeryth do not serve a companion who asks in the member's name.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard all three benefits and left Phaulkonmere, and the relay, the healing and the first report can be used at any later visit.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Summerstrider Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Melannor names the rank at the gate; read by **Autumnreaver**, which checks it before naming the next rank. Track separately for each member the day of the last message sent, the day each ward was last asked about and the Long Rest after which the last healing was cast.
>
> This Event also reads **East Gate Key Held** (the garden's hours for the healing), **Splinter Site Reported** (the Field Ward report) and **Manshoon Named** (the Trades Ward report).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three benefits are active for this member from the next morning. **Autumnreaver** occurs when this member reaches Renown 10. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Melannor Fellbranch names an Emerald Enclave member a Summerstrider and explains the rank's three benefits: a message relay, healing in the garden and a report from each ward.

## Summary

We were named Summerstriders at Phaulkonmere after a grey pigeon told us to come. Melannor will send one message for each of us every tenday, Jeryth heals injuries we take in Enclave work, and he gives each of us a report from each ward every tenday. Each of us has our own rank, and our companions can only wait at the gate.
