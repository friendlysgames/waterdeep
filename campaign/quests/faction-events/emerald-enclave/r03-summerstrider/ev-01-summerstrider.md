# Summerstrider

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Enclave renown reaches 3 — either their next visit to Phaulkonmere or, if they don't visit within a tenday, a pigeon finds them wherever they are. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive a brief summons in Melannor's voice from a grey pigeon
> - Be walked through three practical benefits of Summerstrider rank at Phaulkonmere
> - Hear Jeryth confirm her healing attention directly, from the garden
>
> #### Background
>
> The Summerstrider rank marks the first meaningful threshold the Enclave cares about. Springwardens are members; Summerstriders are trusted. The practical difference: Jeryth is now willing to keep partial awareness on this character when they are injured in Enclave work. She simply does it.
>
> The network Melannor names — gardeners in the City of the Dead, park wardens near the Field of Triumph, fisherfolk along the south quay — has been tracking environmental anomalies since before the campaign began. The characters aren't the reason it exists. They now receive its reports.

### The Pigeon

A grey pigeon finds the promoted character at the first available moment: on a window ledge if they're indoors, on their shoulder if they're outside.

> [!readaloud]
> A grey pigeon arrives on the window ledge and taps at the glass until someone notices it. When the window opens, it steps inside and ruffles itself once, then speaks in a low baritone that belongs unmistakably to Melannor Fellbranch.
>
> > "Phaulkonmere, when you have a spare hour. No urgency."
>
> It turns itself around on the ledge and goes back out the window before anyone has a chance to reply.

A DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) check identifies the effect as *animal messenger*.

### At Phaulkonmere

Melannor meets the promoted character at the gate — not waiting, exactly, but already there when they arrive. He falls into step beside them as they pass through.

> [!readaloud]
> Melannor Fellbranch is at the gate when you arrive, dressed for groundskeeping and holding a pair of pruning shears that he sets down on the gateposts when he sees you, and he falls into step beside you before the gate has fully swung open.
>
> > "Summerstrider. You've earned it. Come through."

He walks without pausing to gauge a reaction. He has three practical matters and covers them in order.

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: an Emerald Enclave druid who manages Phaulkonmere's grounds and delivers every kind of news in the same measured baritone.
>
> Melannor is precise and unhurried. He is giving the character a practical briefing on three benefits that are now theirs; he does not editorialize or congratulate beyond the single word of acknowledgment at the gate. He walks and talks at the same time, because the groundskeeping doesn't stop.
>
> Conversation topics Melannor covers during the walk include:
> - The animal messenger relay, and how it works in practice
> - Jeryth's healing attention for injuries sustained on Enclave business
> - The environmental observation network and who sends its reports
>
> Specific dialogue for Melannor on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**The animal messenger relay?**
>
> > "Once per tenday, I relay a message to any address in Waterdeep or the surrounding environs. Give me a name and a ward, and I'll have something there by morning, usually. Responses come back by evening. The system's been running for years."

> [!qna]**Jeryth's healing?**
>
> > "She already knows when you've taken harm on this work. She doesn't need you to ask." He keeps walking. "Any character injured on Enclave business finds her ready when they return here. No payment. No appointment."

> [!qna]**The network?**
>
> > "Gardeners at the City of the Dead, two fisherfolk on the south quay, park wardens near the Field of Triumph. They send me what they notice. I pass it to you." He pauses at the corner near the eastern beds. "Environmental observations across the city's wards — unusual animal behavior, contaminated soil or water, magical disturbances. One briefing per ward per tenday."

> [!gamemaster]**What the Network Reports**
>
> Reports are flavor that can carry early warning on environmental threads: a fisherfolk noticing unusual tides, a gardener whose composting soil died overnight, a warden whose bird feeders went empty. Use them to seed clues into between-session time.

### Jeryth Confirms

As Melannor finishes the walk, Jeryth's voice arrives from the deepest part of the garden — brief and direct, with the leaves on the nearest branches turning slightly toward the sound.

> [!readaloud]
> Near the oldest oaks, a voice settles into the air around you, coming from no particular direction.
>
> > "When you are hurt in this work, come back here. I know what I'll find before you arrive."

> [!social]**The Voice in the Garden**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Disembodied Presence, she/her) :: the spirit of Phaulkonmere, present in the garden wherever the oldest roots run.
>
> She has said what she came to say. She will not elaborate on the healing unless the character asks a specific question, and even then her answer is one or two sentences at most.

Melannor nods once.

> [!readaloud]
> > "That's settled, then."
>
> He walks the character back through the garden to the gate and unlatches it for them, then picks up the pruning shears he left on the gateposts when they arrived.

### Concluding the Event

Melannor returns to the groundskeeping. The character leaves Phaulkonmere as a Summerstrider with three new practical benefits confirmed by both parties.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Summerstrider Reached** — mark when the promoted character leaves Phaulkonmere after this event. Read by the **Autumnreaver** rank event (r10) and by any mission event that checks whether the animal messenger relay or Jeryth's healing access is available.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**

> The network's first environmental report can be delivered by pigeon within a day or two after this event — one observation from any ward — establishing the briefing cadence as a lived benefit rather than a table entry.
>
> The **Autumnreaver** rank event fires when this character's renown reaches 10. This event does not award a Milestone Point.

## Overview

After reaching Renown 3 with the Emerald Enclave, a grey pigeon finds the promoted character with a quiet summons to Phaulkonmere. Melannor walks them through the Summerstrider rank's practical benefits: an animal messenger relay reaching anywhere in Waterdeep, healing from Jeryth for injuries sustained on faction business, and access to environmental observation reports from a city-wide network of gardeners, park wardens, and harbor fisherfolk.

## Summary

We returned to Phaulkonmere after reaching Summerstrider rank. Melannor walked us through how the animal messenger relay works, confirmed that Jeryth heals injuries sustained on Enclave business, and introduced us to his network of contacts who track environmental disturbances across the city's wards. Jeryth's voice confirmed the healing, briefly, from somewhere deep in the garden.
