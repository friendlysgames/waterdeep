# Summerstrider

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Enclave renown reaches 3 — either the character's next visit to Phaulkonmere or, if they don't visit within a tenday, a pigeon finds them wherever they are. In this Event:
>
> - A grey pigeon delivers Melannor's invitation in his calm baritone.
> - Melannor meets them at Phaulkonmere and names the rank without ceremony.
> - He walks through how the *animal messenger* relay works in practice.
> - Jeryth speaks briefly from the garden to confirm her healing attention.
> - Melannor introduces the environmental observation network by name and method.

**Background (DM only)**

The Summerstrider rank marks the first meaningful threshold the Enclave cares about. Springwardens are members; Summerstriders are trusted. The practical difference: Jeryth is now willing to keep partial awareness on this character when they are injured in Enclave work. She does not make a gift of it. She simply does it.

The network Melannor names — gardeners in the City of the Dead, park wardens near the Field of Triumph, fisherfolk along the south quay — has been tracking environmental anomalies since before the campaign began. The characters aren't the reason it exists. They now receive its reports.

### The Pigeon

A grey pigeon lands on the promoted character's shoulder, or on the nearest window ledge if they're indoors, and taps until let in. When it has the character's attention, it speaks in **Melannor Fellbranch**'s low baritone.

> > "Phaulkonmere, when you have a spare hour. No urgency."

It departs without waiting for a response. A DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) check identifies the effect as *animal messenger*.

### At Phaulkonmere

**Melannor Fellbranch** (Neutral Good, Half-Elf Druid, he/him) meets the character at the gate — not waiting, exactly, but already there when they arrive. He falls into step beside them.

> > "Summerstrider. You've earned it. Come through."

He walks without pausing to gauge a reaction. He has three practical matters and covers them in order.

**The animal messenger relay.** Once per tenday, Melannor relays a message to any address in Waterdeep or the surrounding environs. The character provides a recipient's name or location. He sends a suitable creature — pigeon to the market wards, gull to the harbor district, a nighthawk after dark. Response time depends on distance and whether the recipient writes back; Melannor collects replies through the same network.

> > "Give me a name and a ward. I'll have something there by morning, usually. Responses come back by evening. The system's been running for years."

**Healing access.** Any character injured on Enclave business — any operation they were running for the faction — finds Jeryth ready to cast whatever restorative spell is needed when they return to Phaulkonmere. No payment required. No request beyond walking into the garden.

> > "She already knows when you've taken harm on this work. She doesn't need you to ask."

**The network.** Melannor's contacts file reports on environmental observations across the city: unusual animal behavior, contaminated soil or water, magical disturbances in the wards. Characters at Summerstrider rank receive one briefing per ward per tenday.

> > "Gardeners at the City of the Dead, two fisherfolk on the south quay, park wardens near the Field of Triumph. They send me what they notice. I pass it to you."

> > [!tip]**What the Network Reports**
> > Reports are flavor that can carry early warning on environmental threads: a fisherfolk noticing unusual tides, a gardener whose composting soil died overnight, a warden whose bird feeders went empty. Use it to seed clues into the between-session period.

### Jeryth Confirms

As Melannor finishes, Jeryth's voice carries from the deepest part of the garden — brief, not elaborated.

> > "When you are hurt in this work, come back here. I know what I'll find before you arrive."

She says nothing else. Melannor nods once.

> > "That's settled, then."

He walks them to the gate.

## Overview

After reaching Renown 3 with the Emerald Enclave, a grey pigeon finds the promoted character with a quiet summons to Phaulkonmere. Melannor walks them through the Summerstrider rank's practical benefits: an animal messenger relay reaching anywhere in Waterdeep, healing from Jeryth for injuries sustained on faction business, and access to environmental observation reports from a city-wide network of gardeners, park wardens, and harbor fisherfolk.

## Summary

We returned to Phaulkonmere after reaching Summerstrider rank. Melannor walked us through how the animal messenger relay works, confirmed that Jeryth heals injuries sustained on Enclave business, and introduced us to his network of contacts who track environmental disturbances across the city's wards. Jeryth's voice confirmed the healing, briefly, from somewhere deep in the garden.
