# Design Notes: Summerstrider

## Individual Procedures

Each Summerstrider benefit is a per-member procedure with a named contact, a place, a notice rule, a limit and a loss rule. The relay is one message of 25 words or fewer each tenday, counted from the member's own last request and sent by *Animal Messenger* from Melannor or the gate box. Healing happens at Phaulkonmere only, once per Long Rest, for the member alone and from the fixed list of four spells in the mechanics reference. The network gives one fixed report per ward each tenday, and nothing pools across the party.

The restored draft let the GM choose each network report and let Jeryth cast "whatever restorative spell is needed". Both are now fixed. *Greater Restoration* moved to r10 because its diamond dust costs 100 gp, and the four remaining spells have no costly components.

## Source Departures

Reports are given by Melannor in person or by a note in the gate box, because the longest runs past 25 words and an *Animal Messenger* cannot carry it. The Southern Ward line says "a tenday and a half" where the structure file says two weeks, to keep spans in tendays. The Trades Ward report is gated on **Manshoon Named**, since Phaulkonmere stands one block south of Kolat Towers and no row may point at the towers before the gate.

The Dock Ward has two rows, one for each side of Tarsakh 20, when the carnival fleet sails. The Castle Ward and North Ward rows are invented fixed lines. The Sea Ward report omits the imps of the structure file, because Melannor knows nothing of the villa's owners.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: the gate box, the benches by the gate, the dusk meeting, the falcon for the Field Ward relay, and the Castle Ward and North Ward lines. Jeryth's species is not given in her Notable Figures page, so her social block lists only her alignment, her title and her pronouns.
