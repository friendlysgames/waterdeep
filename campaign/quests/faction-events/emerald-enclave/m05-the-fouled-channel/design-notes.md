# Design Notes: The Fouled Channel

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C stops at the grells, so this mission is a repo invention. Its seeds are Gerrick's sealed drain, which Appendix C says "will be useful later", and the guide's one-line premise of contamination reaching Phaulkonmere. The restored draft gave one long descent, a roll of 1d6 for the cultists, a condition called Dazed and a healing gift from Jeryth. The cutting, the cache and the logbook are kept.

## What Changed

The mission now has three ways down: Gerrick's tunnel, the direction of the dead edge and the Summerstrider report. The cultists arrive at dusk on a fixed three-day schedule, so a party that learns it can ambush them at the stair, and bars the stair to earn the first bonus. The fight is a Cultist Fanatic with one Tough per character, whose Hold Person is held back to round 2 so it cannot start a Paralyzed-plus-Pack-Tactics kill.

The film poisons through a DC 12 Constitution save every ten minutes, and the cutting costs a DC 13 Wisdom save every two minutes. The party can lower the cost by staying out of the water and sharing the hold. The healing gift is gone, because **Summerstrider** already gives Jeryth's healing at Phaulkonmere, and Melannor's "debt repaid" line is story only. The waste is thrall-conditioning residue, not a second intellect-devourer programme.

## Departures from the Source

The sign is a dead patch and a film on the water. Dying herbs and leaving birds belong to the Cassalanter hook, so they are not used. The tunnel runs west, as the Undercliff lies east of the city. Raeve Solnath is now Imre Daskov, to avoid Vira Solkan in **Smoke in the Tower**, and the Manshoon name appears only behind **Manshoon Named**. The cutting's ten minutes plant the conduit that **The Dreamer's Reach** follows, in GM text only.
