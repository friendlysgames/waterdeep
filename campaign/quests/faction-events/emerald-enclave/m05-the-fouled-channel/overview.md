# The Fouled Channel

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Emerald Enclave Mission 5. Available at Renown 9 and character level 6.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 6th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave) — delivers the mission in person; provides the Phaulkonmere herb cutting for the purification; hands it over with the care of someone parting with something precious
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (spirit of Phaulkonmere) — her dying herb beds are the symptom; she purifies the contaminated channels through the cutting when the source is sealed; she offers the party her healing afterward, which Melannor explains as her way of marking a debt repaid
- **Raeve Solnath** (Manshoon Splinter arcanist) — the same arcanist behind the Mission 1 scarecrows; absent during this mission; his name is in the logbook; his arrest in **Faction Outposts** depends on the party's choices here
- Two unnamed Cultist assistants (Splinter operatives) — arrive on a delivery schedule during or after the cache discovery; will not fight; flee to report if not caught

## Dangers & Enemies
Two **Cultists** (2024 *Monster Manual*) on a delivery schedule. They flee rather than fight. Catching them before they reach the tunnel requires a DC 14 Dexterity check to interpose, or any holding spell. A caught cultist answers simple questions but knows only their delivery task — not Raeve's location, not the operation's purpose.

## Overview
Melannor's herb beds are dying overnight, one patch slightly larger than the last, working east toward Phaulkonmere's root systems. Jeryth is silent about it, which Melannor reads as concern. The source is underground: Raeve Solnath — the same Splinter arcanist responsible for Mission 1's scarecrows — has been using a sealed section of the Castle Ward cisterns to dispose of alchemical waste from intellect devourer grafting experiments. The waste is psychically resonant and has reached Phaulkonmere's groundwater.

The party descends through Gerrick Goodbarrel's drainage tunnel, traces the contamination upstream, and finds the Splinter cache in a sealed side-chamber with six alchemical vessels and a coded logbook. Raeve's two cultist assistants arrive on schedule during or after the discovery. If the cache is destroyed before they can report the breach, the delivery schedule ends. If they escape, the contamination spreads to the Trades Ward water supply and Watch operations in **Faction Outposts** are impaired.

Jeryth's purification through the herb cutting clears the channels that same evening. Her promise of healing to the party is never spoken directly — Melannor explains it the next morning as the way she marks a debt repaid.
