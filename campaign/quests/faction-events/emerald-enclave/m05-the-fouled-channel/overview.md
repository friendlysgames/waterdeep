# The Fouled Channel

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Emerald Enclave Mission 5. Available at Renown 9 and character level 6.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 6th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

For three mornings in a row, Melannor has found a patch of dead herbs in the beds closest to Phaulkonmere's eastern wall, each patch slightly larger than the last. Jeryth has said nothing about it, which Melannor has learned to read as concern. The contamination is moving east toward the estate's deeper root systems.

The source is underground. Raeve Solnath — the same Manshoon Splinter arcanist responsible for the animated scarecrows in Mission 1 — has been using a sealed section of the Castle Ward cisterns to dispose of alchemical waste from failed intellect devourer grafting experiments. The waste is psychically resonant and has been introduced to the eastern channel twice weekly for six tendays. It has now reached Phaulkonmere's groundwater through an underground stream. Raeve's intent was long-term: soften a Watch-heavy district through slow cognitive suppression in the district water supply. He is not present during this mission.

Melannor has one lead: the drainage tunnel in the southern Undercliff terraces that Gerrick Goodbarrel described after Mission 1. If the contamination is moving underground, that tunnel is the likely path.

## Involved Characters
- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave) — delivers the mission in person at Phaulkonmere; provides the Phaulkonmere herb cutting for the purification; hands it over with the care of someone parting with something precious
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (spirit of Phaulkonmere) — her dying herb beds are the symptom; she purifies the contaminated channels through the cutting once the source is sealed; offers the party her healing afterward, which Melannor explains the next morning as her way of marking a debt repaid
- **Raeve Solnath** (Manshoon Splinter arcanist) — absent during this mission; his name is in the logbook; his arrest in **Faction Outposts** depends on the party's choices here
- Two unnamed Cultist assistants (Splinter operatives) — arrive on a delivery schedule while the party is at the cache; will not fight; flee to report if not stopped

## Dangers & Enemies
Two **Cultists** (2024 *Monster Manual*) on a delivery schedule. They flee rather than fight. Catching them before they reach the east channel passage requires a successful DC 14 Dexterity check to interpose, or any spell that holds or restrains. A caught cultist answers simple questions but knows only their delivery task — not Raeve's location, not the operation's purpose.

## Overview
Phaulkonmere's herb beds are dying overnight, and the source is in the Castle Ward cisterns.
