# The Fouled Channel: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> **The Fouled Channel** becomes available when an individual Emerald Enclave member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level, after **The Grells in the Dock Ward**. Companions can go down to the cisterns and fight beside the members without belonging to the faction. They wait in the outer garden during the brief and the debrief.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 6th-level characters.*
>
> The dusk fight is a 2024 **Cultist Fanatic** with one **Tough** guard per character, and the **Emerald Enclave Mechanics Reference** audits it for three, four and five characters. The film in the water is an exposure hazard with no Day Cost, and Jeryth's cutting is a psychic hazard that costs hit points and nothing more.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

At dawn a crow lands on the oak in the courtyard of Trollskull Manor and speaks a message in Melannor Fellbranch's voice. A dead patch is growing in Phaulkonmere's beds, and the water in the garden carries a film.

## Background

For five tendays the Splinter has poured the waste of its thrall-conditioning into a Castle Ward cistern. The alchemy keeps the Splinter's mind-controlled servants docile, and its dregs are easier to dump in a sealed channel than to carry through the streets of the Trades Ward. A crew of cultists brings the jars down a stair every third day at dusk, and a Fanatic guards them since a rat-catcher found the stair.

Nobody meant the damage. The film dulls anyone who drinks it, and it has crept along a buried watercourse into the Trades Ward wells and the groundwater under Phaulkonmere. Melannor and Jeryth know only that the beds are dying and the water is wrong. Gerrick Thatchley's sealed drain in the Undercliff leads to the same cisterns.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The waste is residue from the Splinter's thrall-conditioning, and Imre Daskov, the arcanist who woke the scarecrows in **The Undercliff Scarecrows**, brews it. He never comes to the cistern.
> - Imre works at Kolat Towers, one block north of Phaulkonmere. Nobody in this mission points toward it, says what it is, or says Manshoon's name unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member being addressed.
> - If **Pool Destroyed** is marked, nothing changes. The conditioning is the Splinter's own work and has nothing to do with Nihiloor.
> - Something below the cistern notices the conduit that Jeryth's cutting makes. That is the cause of **The Dreamer's Reach**, and nobody in this mission can learn it.

## The Brief

A crow brings Melannor's message at dawn, and the members walk to Phaulkonmere to see the dead patch for themselves. Melannor gives them a cutting from the sage beside the oak and tells them to plant it only after the source is gone.

## Three Ways Down

The film lives in the Castle Ward cisterns, and the party can reach them three ways: Gerrick's tunnel, the direction of the dead edge, or the Summerstrider report from the Trades Ward. Gerrick helps the party open his drain, and he is short with them if a farm burned in the Undercliff.

## The Channel

A chamber of old stone holds the head of a western channel, where a skin of colour rides on the water. A party that spends ten minutes or more on the slick walkway risks a poisoning that lasts until a Short Rest.

## The Cache

Six wax-sealed jars of grey sludge sit in a side chamber with an oilskin logbook. The logbook is in cipher, and decoding it names Imre Daskov.

## The Cultists

At dusk Sevrin Garrow, a Splinter Fanatic, brings his guards and two carriers down the stair to pour the jars. The party can fight them, bar the stair, talk the guards down or take a carrier alive.

## The Cutting

The party plants Melannor's cutting in the sluice, and Jeryth works through it for ten minutes. The water answers with psychic echoes, and the party can share the burden.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 4 base Renown for ending the dumping and reporting to Melannor. Bonuses of +1 each go to members whose party destroys the cache with no cultist escaping, who name Imre Daskov by decoding the logbook, and who take a cultist alive and question him.

## Aftermath

The dead patch stops at the line it reached that morning, and the rill runs clear. **Imre Identified** matters to **Kolat Towers**, and **Cultists Escaped** matters to **Vault of Dragons**. **The Dreamer's Reach** becomes available when an individual Emerald Enclave member reaches Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**.

## Involved Characters

- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave): sends the crow, gives the party the cutting and takes the report.
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (Emerald Enclave): the voice in Phaulkonmere's garden, who works through the cutting.
- **Gerrick Thatchley** (independent): the farmer whose sealed drain is the party's first way down.
- **Sevrin Garrow** (the Splinter): the Fanatic who guards the dusk delivery.
- **Piet Lorn** and **Odda Fenn** (the Splinter): the carriers who pour the jars, and who know only that.
- **Imre Daskov** (the Splinter): the arcanist whose logbook the party finds, who never appears.

## Dangers & Enemies

One 2024 **Cultist Fanatic** and one **Tough** per character fight at dusk, and the Toughs run when the Fanatic falls. The two carriers use the **Cultist** block and do not fight. The film is a Constitution hazard, and the cutting is a Wisdom hazard that deals psychic damage.

## Overview

A dead patch is spreading through Phaulkonmere's beds, and the water in the garden has a film on it. The party follows the water into the cisterns beneath the Castle Ward to find the cause.
