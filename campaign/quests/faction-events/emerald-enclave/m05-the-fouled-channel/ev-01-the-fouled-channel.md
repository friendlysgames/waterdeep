# The Fouled Channel

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration Event begins when Melannor summons the party to Phaulkonmere. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Melannor at Phaulkonmere and see the dying herb beds firsthand
> - Descend through Gerrick's drainage tunnel into the Castle Ward cisterns
> - Identify and follow the contaminated eastern channel to its source
> - Find the Splinter cache and decode the logbook naming Raeve Solnath
> - Stop the two cultist assistants before they can report the breach
> - Complete Jeryth's purification by placing the herb cutting in the channel
>
> #### Background
>
> Raeve Solnath is the same Manshoon Splinter arcanist behind Mission 1's scarecrows — an operative who prefers field testing in locations he considers deniable. He has used a sealed section of the Castle Ward cisterns as an alchemical disposal site for six tendays, introducing psychically resonant waste from failed intellect devourer grafting experiments through a twice-weekly delivery schedule. His intention was slow cognitive suppression across a Watch-heavy district. The contamination has now reached Phaulkonmere's groundwater. Raeve is not present during this mission. Two cultist assistants operate the delivery schedule.

### The Brief

The party comes to Phaulkonmere to find Melannor in the garden, crouching beside a dead patch in the eastern herb beds.

> [!readaloud]
> Phaulkonmere's garden is alive and well-tended everywhere except along the eastern wall, where a patch of the herb beds has gone grey and dry and dead. Melannor is kneeling at its edge when you arrive, studying the boundary of it, and he straightens when he hears you.
>
> > "Three mornings running. Each time it is larger than the last, and it is working east." He looks at you. "Jeryth has said nothing about it. I have learned to read her silence as concern." He gestures at the dead herbs. "This is underground. Something is moving through the water table beneath the estate."
>
> He leads you to the edge of the dead patch and points east toward the wall.
>
> > "I have one lead. There is a drainage tunnel in the south Undercliff terraces — Gerrick Goodbarrel mentioned it after Mission 1, said something had come up through it six years ago and it had been sealed since. If the contamination is moving underground, that is the path." He looks at you steadily. "I need you to go down and find the source."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: an Emerald Enclave druid who manages Phaulkonmere's grounds and delivers concern in the same tone he would use for anything else.
>
> Melannor has been watching these beds for three mornings. He knows the pattern. He does not yet know the cause, but he knows it is underground and he knows it is moving toward the root systems, and the dead edge of the herb bed tells him he does not have much time. He is calm. He is always calm.
>
> He will provide the party with everything he knows, which is not much: the dying beds, the tunnel lead, and his read on Jeryth's silence. He does not yet have the herb cutting. He will provide it when the party returns and the source has been dealt with.
>
> Conversation topics Melannor is willing to discuss include:
> - What he has observed and when
> - What Jeryth's silence means, in his estimation
> - The drainage tunnel Gerrick described after Mission 1
> - What the party should be looking for underground

> [!qna]**What are we looking for?**
>
> > "Something wrong in the water. An unusual smell, a film on the surface, a quality that should not be there. If it is an alchemical source, there may be a visible sign. If it is magical, follow the direction of the contamination upstream."

> [!qna]**How long do we have?**
>
> He looks at the dead patch again.
>
> > "The beds closest to the wall will be gone in two days. After that, the contamination reaches the deeper root systems, and Jeryth will not be able to work freely in the estate until it clears." A pause. "So: not long."

> [!gamemaster]**If Farm Damaged by Fire Is Marked**
>
> If the **Farm Damaged by Fire** outcome is marked from Mission 1, Melannor adds one thing before the party departs:
>
> > "Gerrick Goodbarrel is still farming that land. He is aware of the tunnel location. He also remembers the fire from your last visit. I would suggest not referencing it if you see him."

Once the party has what they need, proceed to **Gerrick's Tunnel**.

### Gerrick's Tunnel

The drainage tunnel entry is in the south Undercliff terraces, accessible through Trollgate in the South Ward. The bricked archway and its iron grate are easy to find once the party has the location.

> [!readaloud]
> The archway is exactly where Gerrick described it, at the southern edge of the terraces below the city walls, half-buried in grass and vines that have been growing over it for years. The iron grate is fitted into the arch, and someone has been at the hinges recently — the rust around the seal is disturbed, and there is earth moved inward on the tunnel floor, from the other side.
>
> Inside, the tunnel is brick-lined and runs east in a long, wet passage, cold and narrow, smelling of still water and old stone. After two hundred feet it opens into the Castle Ward cistern access, a larger vaulted space with three channels running in different directions.

The mineral smell of still water is present through the whole descent. The cistern access has no light source of its own.

> [!gamemaster]**Cistern Access**
>
> The cistern access is a roughly circular chamber, ten feet across, with three narrow channels branching from it. The north and west channels smell of clean water. The eastern channel does not.

### The Contaminated Channel

The difference between the channels is detectable before any check is attempted.

> [!readaloud]
> The eastern channel has something wrong with the water. The surface carries a thin iridescent film, faintly opalescent in your light, catching and throwing colors that do not belong to ordinary water. The smell is different here too — not the mineral smell of the rest of the cisterns, but something chemical underneath it, faint and unpleasant in a way that is difficult to name.

> [!exploration]**Reading the Contamination**
>
> Any character who examines the eastern channel and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Investigation) check identifies the film as alchemical waste and determines that the source is further upstream. A character who succeeds and is also proficient in Arcana recognizes the quality of the smell as thought-residue — the signature of psychic waste from magic that involves the mind.
>
> Extended exposure in the contaminated section — more than 10 minutes of contact with the air or water — imposes the Dazed condition until a Short Rest.

Following the film upstream leads east through a narrowing passage and to the source.

### The Cache

A fitted stone in the eastern cistern wall, recently moved and replaced, marks the side-chamber. The stone is cool and slightly displaced at one edge.

> [!readaloud]
> The fitted stone moves easily once you find the seam, sliding out of the wall to reveal a small side-chamber barely large enough to stand in. Six alchemical vessels are arranged in a row against the far wall, each one sealed with wax and partly submerged to keep them cool. A logbook in oilskin sits on a dry ledge above them.

> [!exploration]**The Logbook**
>
> Any character who examines the logbook and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check decodes the Splinter cipher inside. The logbook contains six tendays of delivery dates, visit frequency, and the name of the operation's coordinator: **Raeve Solnath**. The dated entries are precise enough to calculate when the Trades Ward water first became affected.
>
> > The entries are in a careful hand, column by column: dates, quantities added to the channel, a brief note per delivery. The last entry is two days ago. The name on the inside cover, written once in the same hand, is *Raeve Solnath*.

While the party examines the cache, the two cultist assistants arrive.

> [!gamemaster]**The Cultist Arrival**
>
> Raeve's two Cultist assistants enter from the east channel passage while the party is still in the side-chamber. They came to make the next delivery. When they see the party, they turn and run immediately.
>
> Any character who moves to intercept before they reach the east channel passage must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity check, or use any spell that holds or restrains, to stop them. A caught cultist answers directly: they were paid to add vessels to the eastern channel every three days; they have done this for six tendays; they know nothing beyond a message drop address. They cannot identify Raeve.
>
> If both cultists escape before being stopped, Raeve relocates the cache within two days. The contamination continues into **Faction Outposts**, and the Trades Ward City Watch operates at Disadvantage on Wisdom checks for the duration of **Faction Outposts** as the cognitive suppression spreads through the district water supply.

### Purification

With the cache destroyed and the source sealed, Jeryth can clear the channels — but she needs a conduit into the cistern system to do it.

The party returns to Phaulkonmere to report. Melannor is waiting.

> [!readaloud]
> Melannor listens to the report without interrupting. When it is finished, he is quiet for a moment, and then he goes to the herb beds near the eastern wall and kneels beside the dead patch. He takes a cutting from the nearest living stem with care that is difficult to watch — the precision of someone removing something they cannot afford to lose — and holds it out to you in both hands.
>
> > "Jeryth can clear the channels if she has a path into them. This will give her one. Take it into the contaminated section and place it in the water."

The party descends through Gerrick's tunnel again with the cutting and carries it into the contaminated eastern channel.

> [!readaloud]
> The cutting changes when you place it in the eastern channel. The glow starts at the stem and spreads outward through the water slowly, the way heat spreads through stone, and the smell of rain and turned earth fills the cistern, replacing the chemical quality entirely. Jeryth concentrates through it for ten minutes. The iridescent film on the water surface dissipates. The water clears and the eastern channel smells of nothing but cold stone and clean water.

When the party returns to Phaulkonmere that evening, the herb beds along the eastern wall are restored. The dead patch is gone.

Jeryth speaks once.

> [!readaloud]
> The garden is quiet and warm when you enter. The herb beds along the eastern wall are whole again, the grey stems replaced as if the dead patch was never there. From somewhere in the garden, a voice settles over the space, calm and complete.
>
> > "The water remembers what was put into it. So will I."

## Overview

Phaulkonmere's herb beds are dying overnight, and the source is in the Castle Ward cisterns.

## Summary

The party descended through Gerrick's drainage tunnel into the Castle Ward cisterns and traced a psychically resonant contamination to a Manshoon Splinter cache in a sealed side-chamber. They destroyed the cache, recovered a coded logbook naming Raeve Solnath as the operation's coordinator, and completed a purification ritual with a Phaulkonmere herb cutting that cleared the channels and restored the estate's herb beds that evening.
