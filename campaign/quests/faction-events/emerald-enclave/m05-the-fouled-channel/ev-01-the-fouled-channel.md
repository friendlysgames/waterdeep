# The Fouled Channel

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Combat Event begins when Melannor's crow reaches an Emerald Enclave member at Trollskull Manor and ends when the members report to him at Phaulkonmere. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Melannor's 23-word message and see the dead patch in Phaulkonmere's beds.
> - Reach the Castle Ward cisterns by three ways: Gerrick's tunnel, the direction of the dead edge, or the Summerstrider report from the Trades Ward.
> - Follow the film up the western channel, and take the exposure or avoid it.
> - Find the Splinter's cache, decode the logbook and learn the name Imre Daskov.
> - Meet the cultists who arrive at dusk, fight them or bar their stair, and take one alive.
> - Plant Melannor's cutting in the sluice and endure the ten minutes Jeryth needs.
>
> Only Emerald Enclave members receive the crow and attend the brief and the debrief. Their companions can help with everything in between.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The waste is residue from the Splinter's thrall-conditioning, the alchemy that keeps the Splinter's mind-controlled servants docile. Imre Daskov brews it and has sent it down the cistern stair every third day at dusk for five tendays. He is the arcanist who woke the scarecrows in **The Undercliff Scarecrows**, and the logbook is in the same hand as that page.
> - Nobody meant the damage. The film dulls anyone who drinks it, and it has crept along a buried watercourse into the Trades Ward wells and the groundwater under Phaulkonmere.
> - Sevrin Garrow, a Splinter Fanatic, has guarded the deliveries since a rat-catcher found the stair two tendays ago. Piet Lorn and Odda Fenn carry the buckets and know only the schedule.
> - Imre works at Kolat Towers, one block north of Phaulkonmere, and never comes to the cistern. Nobody in this Event points toward the towers, says what they are, or says Manshoon's name unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member being addressed. Until then Melannor says "the Splinter".
> - If **Pool Destroyed** is marked, nothing changes. The conditioning is the Splinter's own work and has nothing to do with Nihiloor.
> - Something below the cistern notices the conduit that Jeryth's cutting makes. That is the cause of **The Dreamer's Reach**, and nobody in this Event can learn it. Jeryth feels it and says nothing.

### The Brief

The crow finds the member with the highest Wisdom score, or the higher Constitution score on a tie, at dawn on the first morning after a member qualifies. The Splinter's next delivery falls at dusk on that same day. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The oak in the courtyard of Trollskull Manor is grey with early light when a crow drops onto its lowest branch. It walks along the branch, turning its head toward each of you in turn, and stops in front of one of you. The crow speaks in a low, even baritone that seems to come from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > "A dead patch at Phaulkonmere grows each morning, and the garden water carries a film. Please come today and bring light and rope."
>
> The crow waits for an answer that does not come, then lifts off the branch and flies south over the roofs.

The message is 23 words, which fits the 25 words the 2024 *Animal Messenger* spell allows. The crow uses the Raven block, carries no reply and does not wait for one, and others nearby hear the message.

The walk to Phaulkonmere takes about two hours. Companions wait in the outer garden by the gate while the members meet Melannor. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open before you reach it, and nobody is near to have opened it. The garden inside is older than the street around it, with moss on the walls and roots lifting the paving. Along the west wall a stretch of beds lies grey and bare, and the soil at its edge is darker than the rest. The paths all bend back toward the oldest oak, where Melannor Fellbranch waits beside a bundle wrapped in a damp cloth. A calm woman's voice comes from the leaves and the gravel together.
>
> > "Come. The water is wrong."
>
> Melannor nods to each of you in turn and says your names before you can introduce yourselves.

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper at the Oak**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the humourless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, who has watched the dead patch grow for three mornings.
>
> Melannor speaks in plain, complete sentences at an even pace, and he does not use contractions, recognise a joke or swear. His sentences trail off when he speaks of the earth beneath the Castle Ward. He is worried about Jeryth and does not say so.
>
> Conversation topics Melannor is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The dead patch, which moves about a hand's breadth each morning from the west wall.
> - The film on the garden rill, which he first saw on the second morning.
> - The three ways down to the cisterns.
> - The cutting he carries, and when to plant it.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault, or the house one block north of the garden. He turns each of these aside with "That is not mine to speak of."

If **Fouled Channel Requested** is marked for a member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor folds his hands in front of him and looks toward the oak.
>
> > "Jeryth asked for this on the day the fish came ashore on the south quay. She asks for very little, so I sent for you before anyone else."

If **Mirsa Rescued** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor glances toward the gate with his hands folded.
>
> > "Mirsa the seamstress came to the gate recently to thank Jeryth for her help. She sat among the roses for an hour, and Jeryth warmed the air around her."

> [!qna]**What is killing the beds?**
>
> > "There is poison in the water, and it is carried through the ground from west to east. Jeryth feels it in the roots, and I can see it in the soil. Someone made it, because nothing in nature smells like that."

> [!qna]**Where do we begin?**
>
> > "There are three ways down to the cisterns under the Castle Ward. Gerrick Thatchley's old drain is one of them. The direction of the dead edge is another, and the Summerstrider network may know a third."

> [!qna]**What is in the bundle?**
>
> > "It is a cutting of the sage beside the oak. Jeryth can work through it, but only once the source of the poison is gone. Carry it dry, and do not set it in the water before then."

> [!qna]**Who did this?**
>
> > "I do not know who made it, and I would like to. If you find a mark or a name, bring it to me, and I will give it to Jeryth."

> [!qna]**What should we know?**
>
> > "Do not drink the water, and do not stay in it longer than you must. The smell is sweet and flat, and I am told that it clings to the skin."

### Three Ways Down

The film comes from the Castle Ward cisterns, and the party can reach them three ways. Each way is enough alone, and the party can try them in any order. Gerrick's tunnel and the cistern house stair both end in the chamber at the head of the western channel, described in **The Channel**. The Trades Ward hatch ends at the foot of the cultists' stair, 60 feet west of the cache.

#### The Dead Edge

> [!exploration]**The Dead Edge**
>
> Any character who studies the dead edge of the beds with Melannor and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Nature)** check works out that the poison runs along a buried watercourse from the northwest. The garden's rill draws its water from a public cistern house in the Castle Ward. The watercourse passes under the Trades Ward on the way, and Melannor has a key to the cistern house door, which he gives to that character.
>
> On a failure, Melannor reads the edge himself after an hour and gives the same answer, and the party loses the hour and nothing else. The cistern house has a stair of forty feet that opens on the north side of the chamber at the head of the western channel.

#### The Network's Report

> [!exploration]**The Network's Report**
>
> A member who asks the Summerstrider network about the wells of the Trades Ward hears the report of its water-carriers. The film lies on the water in the plaza well each morning, and horses refuse it. Customers who drink it grow slow and agreeable.
>
> A carter who keeps his cart in a shed behind the plaza says men with a key open a trapdoor under the shed at dusk every third day. They carry sealed jars down, and he has not asked why.
>
> This report is separate from the fixed Trades Ward report in **Summerstrider** and does not use up the member's request for that ward. Only members can ask the network, and a member passes the report to the party.
>
> The trapdoor is padlocked. The lock opens to a **DC 15 Dexterity** check with thieves' tools. A character without the tools forces it with a **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** check, which draws the carter, and he asks no questions for 5 gp. A failed attempt costs the party a minute and nothing more.
>
> Forty feet of steps lead down to a narrow landing, the foot of the cultists' stair. The cache chamber lies 60 feet east of it along the channel.

#### Gerrick's Tunnel

If **Drainage Tunnel Learned** is marked, the party goes straight to the outlet below the southern terraces, and Gerrick hears them at the grate. If it is unmarked, Melannor sends them to Gerrick's barn in the Undercliff, where Gerrick grumbles, names the outlet, and says he will follow with tools. The Undercliff lies about two hours east of Phaulkonmere. Read the following aloud when the party reaches the outlet:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The outlet is a low brick arch set into the hillside below the southern terraces, where the cliff path meets the city wall. Mortared stone fills it behind an iron grate rusted into place, and water seeps from the foot of the grate down to the stream. A stout halfling climbs the path toward you with a pry bar on one shoulder and a sledge in his other hand.
>
> > "I thought I heard someone at my drain. You're back, then, and I suppose you want it open."

If **Farm Damaged by Fire** is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The halfling stops two paces off with the sledge hanging from one hand. He looks at each of you for some time, and a burned outbuilding stands behind him with new timber against its walls.
>
> > "You want the drain open. Fine. I told the Enclave it was theirs, and I keep my word, whatever I think of you. I'm still rebuilding my barn or Tuckerman's shed, so don't expect me to be cheerful about it."

Gerrick says "my barn" if the Sackcloth One's field burned and "Tuckerman's shed" if the Blanket's pasture burned. If both burned, he says "my barn and Tuckerman's shed".

> [!social]**The Farmer at the Drain**
>
> Gerrick Thatchley (Neutral, Lightfoot Halfling, he/him) :: a halfling farmer of about fifty who has worked this land for thirty years and swears at the Guard by habit.
>
> Gerrick is gruff and practical, and he helps anyone the Enclave sends. He wants the drain closed again behind them, with a lock he can see from his barn. He answers each question in a sentence or two, and if **Farm Damaged by Fire** is marked, he answers in one sentence and offers no tools until asked.
>
> Conversation topics Gerrick is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What lies behind the seal, and how long it has been shut.
> - What came up through the drain six years ago.
> - The tools he lends and the lock he wants.
> - The Guard, if someone asks.
>
> He will not discuss his neighbours' names, because they drove the thing back and do not want it remembered.

> [!qna]**What is behind the seal?**
>
> > "A brick drain that runs west under the wall to the cisterns. It's been shut six years, and nobody has gone down it since, so I can't tell you what's in there."

> [!qna]**What came up out of it?**
>
> > "I never saw it. I was at market, and by the time I got home the neighbours had run it back down with torches and half the thatch was burning. They won't tell me what it was, and I've stopped asking."

> [!qna]**Can we use your tools?**
>
> > "Take the pry bar and the sledge. It's an hour's work between you, and I'll lend a hand. The Guard wouldn't lift a finger, the lazy fucks."

> [!qna]**Anything we owe you?**
>
> > "Hang the grate back on the new hinges when you're done and put the padlock on it. I keep one key, and you give the other to Melannor. An open drain beside my barn is no good to me."

> [!exploration]**Opening the Seal**
>
> Opening the seal takes the party and Gerrick one hour, with no check. Gerrick leaves the pry bar and the sledge with the party and goes back to his barn. When the party returns through the drain, it rehangs the grate and locks it with the padlock Gerrick left on a hook beside the arch. The party keeps the spare key and gives it to Melannor at the debrief.
>
> Behind the grate, a brick tunnel slopes down into the dark and runs west for about half a mile. It arrives at the east side of the chamber at the head of the western channel.

### The Channel

The chamber at the head of the western channel is the heart of the cistern complex. Gerrick's tunnel enters it from the east, and the cistern house stair from the north. A raised dais of dry stone runs around its edge. The western channel enters from the west and runs 400 feet from the chamber to the cache and 60 feet farther to the foot of the cultists' stair. A walkway runs beside the water the whole way. When the party first reaches the chamber, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Your light finds a round chamber of old stone about ten feet across, with a raised dais around its edge. A grated sluice sits in the middle of the floor. Water slides out of the western channel and gathers over the sluice in a slow swirl. A skin of oily colour rides on top of it in patches of green and violet. The smell that comes off it is sweet and flat, like a sickroom with the window shut.

> [!exploration]**Reading the Film**
>
> - Any character who examines the water and makes a successful **DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Investigation)** check identifies the film as alchemical waste.
>   That character also sees that it comes from upstream, to the west.
> - Any character who smells the water and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana)** check recognises a tincture that keeps a person calm and obedient. Someone has boiled it down and thrown it away.
>
> A party that makes neither check can still follow the film upstream, because the checks add information and do not gate the way.

The cache chamber is a side room off the western channel. The party can find it three ways, and each is enough.

> [!exploration]**Finding the Cache**
>
> - **The film.** The colour thickens as the channel runs west, and it pools in a side opening 400 feet from the chamber. Any character who follows it finds the opening.
> - **The walkway.** Boot scuffs and drips of red wax mark the stones leading to the opening. A character who studies the walkway and makes a successful **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check sees them.
> - **The rats.** The cistern rats circle at the opening and will not go in. A character who casts *Speak with Animals* on them, or studies them and makes a successful **DC 11 Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check, learns that they fear the smell.

> [!hazard]**The Film**
>
> Time on the walkway, in the water or in the cache chamber counts as exposure. Time on the dais in the first chamber does not. Walking the 400 feet takes about three minutes at the pace the slick stones allow.
>
> A character who spends 10 minutes or more in the exposure makes a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw**. The character repeats the save after each further 10 minutes of exposure, until a save fails.
>
> - **Failure:** the character is Poisoned until it finishes a Short Rest. A Poisoned character has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks, and cannot be Poisoned again by the film.
> - **Success:** nothing, and the count starts again.
> - **Remedies:** a Short Rest outside the exposure. A member can also spend 1 charge of a *charm of restoration* to cast *lesser restoration* on the character. Cloths over the nose and mouth do nothing, because the film acts through the skin.
>
> About half of a party is Poisoned after the first ten minutes, and the hazard carries no Day Cost.

### The Cache

The opening in the channel wall leads into a side chamber 12 feet across. A fitted stone has been slid aside, and the cultists' lantern hooks hang beside it. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A low side chamber opens off the channel, with a trough of film-covered water along its floor. Six jars of grey glass stand in the trough, each sealed with red wax and wedged upright with stones. A dry shelf runs along the back wall at the height of your chest. An oilskin book lies on it, tied shut with a cord, and the cover is stamped with a circle split by a jagged line.

Any character who saw the page in **The Undercliff Scarecrows** knows the mark. Melannor names it the Splinter's mark if the party shows him.

> [!exploration]**The Logbook**
>
> Any character who studies the logbook for 10 minutes and makes a successful **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** check decodes the cipher. The log lists a delivery every third day at dusk for five tendays, with the number of jars and a line of notes for each. The last entry reads, in clear text, that a rat-catcher found the stair and that S. Garrow now stands guard. The inside cover bears the owner's name in the same hand as the Undercliff page: *I. Daskov*.
>
> - **Character has seen the Undercliff page:** the character has Advantage on this check.
> - **Character studies the log in clean air on the dais:** the study takes the same 10 minutes but counts as no exposure.
>
> On a failure, another character can try after a Short Rest. If nobody succeeds before the party leaves the cistern, Melannor and Jeryth work the cipher out in two days. Mark **Imre Identified** then, and the party earns no bonus for it.

> [!exploration]**Burning the Cache**
>
> Each jar holds about a gallon of grey sludge under its seal. A character who breaks a seal with a Utilize action and sets a torch, a flask of oil or any fire spell to the sludge burns it to white ash in a minute.
>
> The six jars take six actions in all, from any characters, and the thin smoke drifts up the stair and harms no one. Melannor will not let Jeryth work while the jars stand, because the water would foul again within a tenday, so mark **Cache Destroyed** when the last jar is ash.

### The Cultists

The crew keeps the schedule whatever the party does. A delivery falls at dusk on the day the crow arrives and every third day after that. If the party is above ground at dusk, the crew pours its jars without it, and the next delivery falls three days later at dusk. The party learns the schedule from the Trades Ward report, from the logbook, or by waiting.

The cultists come down the stair in file. Sevrin Garrow leads, the guards follow, and Piet Lorn and Odda Fenn carry buckets and a lamp pole at the back. The party's position decides where the fight starts.

- **At the stair foot.** A party waiting there with its lights out surprises the crew, so every cultist has Disadvantage on initiative rolls. The stair is five feet wide, so a character in its foot blocks it for everyone above.
- **In the channel.** The Toughs form at the mouth of the cache chamber and the party meets them on the walkway.
- **In the cache chamber.** The party faces the chamber mouth, and the Toughs hold it.

Read the following aloud as the crew arrives:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A bolt scrapes back at the top of the stair, and a square of lantern light comes down the steps one at a time. A broad man in a dark coat leads the way with a short blade already drawn, and a file of armed men and women follows him. Behind them two carriers walk bent under buckets and a pole lamp, and nobody speaks until the leader sees you.
>
> > "That's the Enclave's work, and you're standing in my cistern. Piet, get back behind the lamp. Everyone else, with me, and quickly."

> [!hazard]**The Dusk Delivery**
>
> **Sevrin Garrow** (Neutral, Human, he/him) is a **Cultist Fanatic**, and each of his guards is a **Tough**. Piet Lorn and Odda Fenn use the **Cultist** block, carry the buckets and do not fight. Count every character who fights, companions included.
>
> - **Three characters:** the Fanatic and three Toughs.
> - **Four characters:** the Fanatic and four Toughs.
> - **Five characters:** the Fanatic and five Toughs.
>
> Drop one Tough if the party is hurt before dusk, or if two or more characters are Poisoned when the cultists arrive. The fight is Bruising at every party size (Day Cost 4).
>
> #### Sevrin Garrow's Tactics
> At the start of combat, Sevrin casts *Spiritual Weapon* as a bonus action and uses *Command* (Drop or Grovel) on the nearest character who reaches for the jars. The Toughs take the chamber mouth and use Pack Tactics.
>
> During combat, Sevrin and the Toughs:
> - Fight with Pact Blade (Sevrin) and Mace (the Toughs) against any character who stands next to a Tough.
> - Use *Hold Person* from round 2 onward, and only on a character with no ally within 5 feet.
> - Focus the character Sevrin has Commanded or held.
> - Never follow the party onto the dais of the first chamber.
>
> Piet and Odda drop their burdens and run for the stair as soon as the first blow lands. The fight ends when Sevrin reaches 0 hit points. The Toughs then drop their weapons or run, and they surrender if Grappled, and Sevrin never stands down.
>
> **Capture.** A character within reach of a runner makes a **DC 14 Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check to take it alive. Any holding spell, such as *Hold Person* or *Entangle*, does the same without a check. Piet is the easiest to catch, because he runs with a lamp pole.
>
> **Non-combat route.** Once the party holds the stair foot, a character who calls on the Toughs to put down their weapons and go home makes a **DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check.
> On a success the Toughs withdraw up the stair, and Sevrin fights on alone, outnumbered. On a failure the fight starts as written, with the party acting first.
>
> **Escape.** Mark **Cultists Escaped** when anyone of Sevrin's company, including a carrier, climbs the stair and gets away, and a runner who is dead or held does not count.

The captive talks freely if nobody threatens him again.

> [!social]**The Carrier**
>
> Piet Lorn (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: a thin, frightened carter's hand who carries buckets for four silver a delivery and wants to go home.
>
> Piet answers every question quickly and does not lie, because he has nothing worth protecting. He knows the schedule, the cart shed and the face of the man who pays him. He does not know who sends the jars, or what is in them.
>
> Conversation topics Piet is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Who hired him, and what he does on a delivery.
> - Where the jars come from.
> - Sevrin Garrow, whom he fears.
>
> He will not discuss anything else, because he knows nothing else.

> [!qna]**Who hired you?**
>
> > "A man at a tavern on the Trades Ward side. He said it was disposal work that paid four silver a night, and that I shouldn't ask questions. I carry and I pour, and then I go home."

> [!qna]**Where do the jars come from?**
>
> > "A cart leaves them in the shed at noon every third day, and the driver wears a hood, so I've never seen his face. Mister Garrow checks every seal before we go down."

> [!qna]**What is in them?**
>
> > "I don't know, and I don't want to know. It smells like a sickroom, and my hands go numb if I spill any. Shit, I should've quit when the rat-catcher came."

> [!qna]**Who is Sevrin Garrow?**
>
> > "He's the man who stands over us on every delivery, and he never says who he works for. He told us a second man would know if we talked, and I believed him."

### The Cutting

When the cache is destroyed and the cultists are dealt with, the party can plant Melannor's cutting. It goes in the sluice in the middle of the first chamber, where the water is deepest. The current there runs down to the water table beneath the Southern Ward. Read the following aloud when a character sets it in the water:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cutting sinks to the floor of the sluice, and its stem lights from the cut end upward in a soft green glow. The glow spreads out over the water, and the smell of rain and turned earth rises to cover the sweet flat smell. The film thins and draws back from the light. High above you, somewhere in the stone, a woman hums one low note.

Jeryth works through the cutting for 10 minutes. The cutting must be held upright in the sluice by one character standing in the water. Characters on the dais stay dry and make no saves.

> [!hazard]**The Water Answers**
>
> Each character in or touching the water makes a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** at the end of every 2-minute interval, five saves in all.
>
> - **Failure:** the character takes 7 (2d6) psychic damage and is Frightened until the end of its next turn. While Frightened, the character cannot willingly move closer to the cutting.
> - **Success:** nothing.
> - **Sharing the hold:** a character on the dais takes the cutting from the holder with a Utilize action at the end of an interval. The party can take turns this way.
> - **Cost:** a character who holds the cutting for all ten minutes takes about 17 psychic damage on average and 35 at worst, and there is no Day Cost. The cutting holds as long as someone grips it, and the saves are the only cost.

At the end of the tenth minute, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The water around your knees runs cold and flows against the current for the space of three breaths. The glow in the cutting dims, steadies and brightens again, and the water lies still. A thread of green light runs from the stem down through the grate of the sluice and out of sight.

> [!gamemaster]**What Notices**
>
> Something below the cistern noticed the conduit while it formed. The thread of light now runs from the sluice into the deep pipe, and the party follows it in **The Dreamer's Reach**. Nobody in this Event can learn this, because Jeryth feels it and says nothing and Melannor feels nothing at all. Do not name Illuun, and do not let a character who asks about the cold water learn more than that it was cold.

### Renown Opportunities

The members return to Phaulkonmere that evening, and companions wait in the outer garden. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Lamplight lies on the gravel paths of Phaulkonmere when you come back through the open gate. Along the west wall the dead patch has stopped at the line it reached that morning, and a pale shoot has pushed up at its edge. Melannor waits beneath the oldest oak with his hands folded, and a calm voice speaks from the leaves.
>
> > "The water is clean. Rest here."

Melannor gives each member the same acknowledgement in turn. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor looks at each of you and inclines his head.
>
> > "The rill runs clear, and the dead patch has stopped growing. Jeryth does not thank anyone, so I will say it for her. The garden counts its debt to you as repaid."

> Melannor pauses for a moment with his hands folded.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 4 base Renown for ending the dumping and reporting to Melannor. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the party destroyed the cache, **Cache Destroyed** is marked, and **Cultists Escaped** is unmarked.
> - **+1 Renown:** the party decoded the logbook itself, and the member named Imre Daskov to Melannor.
> - **+1 Renown:** the party took a cultist alive and questioned him before the debrief.
>
> Each bonus can be earned only once. No healing is given at the debrief, because Melannor's debt line is story only and **Summerstrider** already covers Jeryth's healing at Phaulkonmere.

### Aftermath

The dead patch stops at the line it reached that morning, and new shoots come up at its edge over the next tenday. The rill runs clear, and the Trades Ward wells stop clouding within three days, while Gerrick has a padlocked grate beside his barn and a key that the Enclave holds.

If **Cultists Escaped** is marked, the Splinter learns within a day that its channel is found. It moves its Trades Ward watchers and pays sergeants of the Watch to ignore reports about the cisterns. **Vault of Dragons** reads this as reduced Watch cooperation in the Trades Ward. If the party took a cultist alive, Piet Lorn goes free after the debrief and leaves Waterdeep.

Imre Daskov stays at Kolat Towers, and he is angry about the loss of the cache. **Kolat Towers** reads **Imre Identified**. **The Dreamer's Reach** reads **Cache Destroyed**.

Melannor's thanks end the assignment, and the three outcomes below should be recorded because later quests read them.
