# Design Notes: The Undercliff Scarecrows

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C gives three scarecrows in three territories, a halfling farmer with a pitchfork, a streambed funnel, a DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) prediction and a fire rule. It rewards +1 renown, and Gerrick owes a favour about a sealed drain only if the party saved him from pitchforking a scarecrow. The party could fight all three in one intensive effort. The Splinter arcanist, the chalk circle and the journal page came from the first rewrite and are kept.

## What Changed

The scarecrows are now fought one territory at a time. The 2024 Scarecrow cannot Paralyze and strike in one turn, but two can, so the territories hold them apart and the text says why. Each fight rewards one trick: the Sackcloth One a sheep in a fold, the Pumpkin Head a runner and the stream cut, and the Blanket a lantern on a post. The Burn track turns the source's fire warning into a clock. It sits only in the two dry fields, so the orchard is a safe place for fire and the others make it a choice.

Each of the three beats now has three routes. The territories have Gerrick, DC 14 Survival on the tracks and the animals. The site has Gerrick, DC 13 Investigation and the Sackcloth One's range. The tunnel has Gerrick, Melannor and the outlet itself. The debrief at Phaulkonmere is new, and renown is 2 base with +1 each for three days, the page and no burned farm. The 24-word pigeon message replaces a 44-word briefing.

## Departures from the Source

Gerrick's tunnel is unconditional. Appendix C ties it to saving him from his own pitchfork, which would make it unreachable for most parties, and **The Fouled Channel** needs it. The source's dry streambed becomes a running stream so that a bucket has water. The source says "fire suppresses their Damage Immunity", which is 2014 wording, and the 2024 Scarecrow is Vulnerable to fire. The Blanket's "supernatural senses" become tactics, since no stat block gives them.

"Trollgate" is dropped because Waterdeep Dragon Heist does not use it, and the drain now runs west, since the Undercliff lies east of the city. "Manshoon Splinter" becomes "the Splinter" in everything players read or hear. Gerrick Goodbarrel is renamed Gerrick Thatchley to avoid Marda Goodbarrel in **The Missing Snobeedle**, and his tone changes only in **The Fouled Channel**.
