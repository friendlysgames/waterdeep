# The Undercliff Scarecrows

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Emerald Enclave Mission 1. Available at Renown 0 and character level 2.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

A Manshoon Splinter arcanist used the Undercliff's farming terraces as a testing ground for an animation ritual last tenday, succeeded beyond his intentions, and departed before dealing with the results. All three scarecrows became fully animated and hostile. A farmer was struck unconscious. The City Guard considers animated agricultural fixtures beneath their threshold of response, and Melannor assigns the party as the only available response.

The arcanist's testing site remains in the field: a chalk animation circle on a flat stone in the eastern draw near the northern farms, with a discarded journal page noting that all three scarecrows activated simultaneously and that he departed before they could reorient. Finding this site and reporting it to Melannor gives the Enclave their first direct evidence of Splinter field testing in the Undercliff.

Gerrick Goodbarrel, the halfling farmer who has been watching from his north field for three days with a pitchfork and settled impatience, will give the party the location of an old sealed drainage tunnel in the southern terraces at the end of the mission. He mentions it the way he mentions everything — as one piece of local geography among others. The tunnel connects east to the Castle Ward cisterns and was sealed six years ago after something came up through it. It becomes the access point in **Emerald Enclave Mission 5 — The Fouled Channel** and **Mission 6 — The Dreamer's Reach**.

## Involved Characters
- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave) — the party's Enclave contact; delivers the mission via trained pigeon; does not accompany the party
- **Gerrick Goodbarrel** (farmer) — a halfling farmer of about fifty; has been watching the scarecrows' patterns for three days; provides tactical assessment and knowledge of the terrain, including the sealed drainage tunnel

## Dangers & Enemies

Three **Scarecrows** (2024 *Monster Manual*). Each occupies a distinct territory in the Undercliff terraces and operates by its own pattern. The Sackcloth One moves at dusk and targets livestock first. The Pumpkin Head pursues anything that runs at night and should not be fled from. The Blanket holds a road-adjacent position and cannot be approached unseen, but moves toward sustained light. All three are combustible; uncontrolled fire in dry fields spreads to at least one nearby farm during combat.

## Overview

Three animated scarecrows are loose in the Undercliff farming terraces east of Waterdeep's walls. They have attacked livestock and struck a farmer unconscious. The City Guard will not respond.
