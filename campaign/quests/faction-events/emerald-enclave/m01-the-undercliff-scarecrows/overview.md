# The Undercliff Scarecrows: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> **The Undercliff Scarecrows** becomes available when an individual Emerald Enclave member who has marked **Emerald Enclave Joined** reaches 2nd level. Companions can walk the Undercliff and fight the scarecrows without belonging to the faction.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> Each fight is a single 2024 **Scarecrow**, and the roster is the same at three, four and five characters. The scarecrows are fought one territory at a time on separate days, and the **Emerald Enclave Mechanics Reference** audits the fights and the fire rule.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

On the first morning after a member qualifies, a grey pigeon finds that member at Trollskull Manor and speaks a message in Melannor Fellbranch's voice. It says that three scarecrows have come alive on the farms of the Undercliff, east of the walls, and that the City Guard will not come.

## Background

About a tenday ago a Splinter arcanist drew a chalk circle in a sheltered draw east of Gerrick's north field and animated three scarecrows at once. He had not expected all three to wake, and he left before he could bring them under control. They now hold three territories of their own. The Sackcloth One walks the northern fields, the Pumpkin Head haunts the central orchards, and the Blanket stands on the southern terraces.

Gerrick Thatchley, a halfling farmer of about fifty, has watched them for three days from his barn in the north, and he is angry that nobody has come. He also knows a sealed brick drain that runs west from the southern terraces to the cisterns under the Castle Ward.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The Splinter is the Zhentarim cell that broke away from the rest, and nobody in this mission names its master.
> - The journal page in the draw is in the same hand as the logbook in **The Fouled Channel**, and its writer is Imre Daskov, whom nobody here can name.
> - The drain was sealed six years ago after a chuul scout from Illuun's brood came up through it. Gerrick's neighbours drove it back with fire, and Gerrick never learned what it was, so **The Dreamer's Reach** pays this off.
> - The scarecrows have no master, so nothing controls them now and they stay inside their territories.

## The Brief

The pigeon speaks a message of 24 words. A member who walks to Phaulkonmere instead finds Melannor at the gate, and he answers questions in his flat, even way while the companions wait outside.

## The Undercliff

The farm terraces lie a morning's walk east of the city. The party can find all three territories by three independent routes: Gerrick, the tracks in the fields, or the behaviour of the animals.

## Gerrick

Gerrick Thatchley waits in his north field with a pitchfork and swears at the Guard. He shares what he has seen, mentions the old drain in passing, and lends buckets, shovels and a ewe.

## The Three Scarecrows

Each scarecrow is fought alone, on its own ground, with one Long Rest between fights. Each has a habit the party can use, whether a decoy, a runner or a lantern. Fire works well against them, but a dry field can carry it to a barn.

## The Testing Site

The chalk circle and its journal page lie in a draw beside the northern fields, and the party can find it three ways. The page proves that the scarecrows were made on purpose.

## The Sealed Tunnel

The outlet of Gerrick's drain is plain to see in the Blanket's territory. The party learns where it is from Gerrick, from Melannor or from the outlet itself.

## Debrief at Phaulkonmere

The members report to Melannor at Phaulkonmere, where he reads the page, names the Splinter and says what the Enclave will do with it.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 2 base Renown for ending the scarecrows' attacks and reporting to Melannor. Bonuses of +1 each go to members whose party destroys all three within three days, who show Melannor the testing-site page, and whose party leaves no farm burned.

## Aftermath

Gerrick gets his farm back and tells his neighbours who came, and Melannor passes the page to Jeryth. A member whose Renown reaches 3 here is called to the garden by **A Seat at Phaulkonmere**, and **Summerstrider** follows. **Ten Nights in the City of the Dead** becomes available at Renown 3 and 3rd level.

## Involved Characters

- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave): sends the pigeon, answers questions at Phaulkonmere and takes the report.
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (Emerald Enclave): the voice in Phaulkonmere's garden, who speaks one line at the debrief.
- **Gerrick Thatchley** (independent): the farmer whose land the party fights across and who knows the drain.
- **The Splinter's arcanist** (the Splinter): drew the circle and left, and never appears.

## Dangers & Enemies

Three 2024 **Scarecrows**, one per territory. A Scarecrow that Paralyzes a character can then strike with critical hits, so the party never fights two together. The Scarecrow takes double damage from fire, and fire in the dry fields can burn a farm. Nothing else in the Undercliff is hostile.

## Overview

Three scarecrows have come alive on the farms east of the city. The party hunts them across the Undercliff and finds out who woke them.
