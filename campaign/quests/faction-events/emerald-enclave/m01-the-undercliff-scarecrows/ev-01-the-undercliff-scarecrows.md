# The Undercliff Scarecrows

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat/Exploration Event begins when the pigeon briefing arrives at Trollskull Manor. In this Event:
>
> - Three animated scarecrows occupy separate territories in the Undercliff terraces east of Waterdeep's walls.
> - Each has a distinct behavioral pattern: the Sackcloth One moves at dusk and targets animals; the Pumpkin Head pursues anything that runs; the Blanket holds a road-adjacent position and cannot be approached unseen.
> - Gerrick Goodbarrel has tracked the three scarecrows for three days and can predict their next appearance point with a DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) check or his direct guidance.
> - The Splinter arcanist's testing site is findable in the northern fields; reporting it to Melannor is one renown condition.
> - Fire works but risks spreading; uncontrolled fire damages at least one farm and Gerrick notices.
> - After the mission, Gerrick gives the party the location of a sealed drainage tunnel in the southern terraces.

**Background (DM only)**
A Manshoon Splinter arcanist used the Undercliff as a testing ground for an animation ritual last tenday. The ritual succeeded beyond his intentions — all three scarecrows became fully animated and hostile — and he departed before dealing with the results. The testing site is still in the field: a chalk animation circle in a sheltered draw near the northern farms, with a discarded journal page describing the ritual and an excited notation that all three scarecrows activated simultaneously. The arcanist saw no reason to conceal it. Finding this site and reporting it to Melannor confirms these were intentional experiments, not natural manifestations, and gives the Enclave their first direct evidence of Splinter field testing in the Undercliff.

### The Undercliff

The farming terraces east of the walls, accessible through Trollgate in the South Ward. Spring: green, actively worked, smelling of earth and cold harbor air. The visible signs of trouble are immediate — tools dropped mid-task at the edge of a field, a garden gate swinging open, the conspicuous absence of birds.

> [!narrative]
> The farm tracks here are quiet in a way that is not peaceful. A set of tools leans against a stone wall no one came back for. At the far end of a long field, a horse stands perfectly still, facing north.

A DC 12 Wisdom (Perception or Survival) check, or half an hour of walking the terraces, establishes the general territories of all three scarecrows.

### Gerrick Goodbarrel

**Gerrick Goodbarrel** is in his north field with a pitchfork and an expression of pure, settled impatience. He has been waiting three days for the city to send someone. The party qualifies.

> [!npc-narrative]**Gerrick Goodbarrel**
> A halfling farmer stands in his north field with a pitchfork and an expression of pure, settled impatience. He looks to be about fifty, practical and sun-weathered. He has been waiting three days for the city to send someone, and his manner makes clear he has already formed his tactical assessment and is simply waiting to share it.

He does not ask for help so much as share his tactical assessment. He has watched the scarecrows' movement patterns and identified the streambed running east-west through the middle terraces as a natural funnel — anything moving north to south must cross it. He also noticed chalk marks on a flat stone in the eastern draw near the northern farms: "Arcanist's work, probably. Didn't touch it."

A DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) check, or following Gerrick's guidance, allows the party to predict the next appearance point for each scarecrow.

#### The Drainage Tunnel

Gerrick has farmed this land for thirty years. He mentions the old brick-lined drainage tunnel beneath the southern terraces the way he mentions everything — as one piece of local geography among others. It was sealed six years ago after something came up through it. He never learned what, and he does not press the point. The party may or may not register this. The tunnel is not immediately useful. It becomes the access point for Missions 5 and 6.

### The Three Scarecrows

Each encounter takes place in terrain suited to its habits. Use **Scarecrow** stat blocks (2024 *Monster Manual*).

#### The Sackcloth One
Northern fields. Moves mostly at dusk. It does not pursue people directly — it goes for livestock first, then characters who stand between it and the animals. Characters with mounts or pack animals must account for this. Its preference can be turned against it: an animal decoy draws it into the open and into terrain the party controls.

#### The Pumpkin Head
Central orchards. Operates at night. It pursues anything that runs — horses, dogs, anyone moving quickly — with singular focus. Running is always the wrong choice. It can be led through controlled retreats into terrain of the party's choosing, drawn between orchard rows toward a prepared position.

#### The Blanket
Southern terraces near Trollgate road. The most recently animated; it has established territorial positions near the road and attacks anyone who enters its station. It has supernatural senses and cannot be surprised. It moves toward sustained light — a held torch or a *light* cantrip draws it out of position.

### Fire Tactics

All three scarecrows are combustible. A contained fire is a valid tactic. An uncontrolled fire in open dry fields spreads to at least one nearby farm during combat. Gerrick notices. He says nothing at the time. He accounts for it in his tally of what is owed.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### The Arcanist's Testing Site
> The site is in a sheltered draw near the northern farms, not far from the Sackcloth One's territory. A chalk animation circle, roughly eight feet across, is drawn on a flat stone outcrop. At the center: a discarded journal page. The page describes the ritual and carries an excited notation — "all three activated simultaneously, unexpected — departed before they could reorient." This constitutes evidence of intentional Splinter field testing. Reporting it to Melannor is one renown condition.

## Overview

Three animated scarecrows are terrorizing the Undercliff farms east of Waterdeep's walls, and the City Guard will not respond.

## Read Aloud

> [!narrative]
> A pigeon lands on a windowsill at Trollskull Manor one morning, clears its throat with the deliberate clarity of a trained bird, and speaks in a calm baritone: "Animated scarecrows are terrorizing farms east of the city walls. Three of them. Livestock have been attacked and a farmer was struck unconscious. The City Guard is occupied with the gang war and considers animated agricultural fixtures beneath their threshold. Something must be done." The pigeon departs.

## Summary

The party traveled to the Undercliff terraces and destroyed three animated scarecrows left loose by a Manshoon Splinter arcanist's field experiment. A halfling farmer named Gerrick Goodbarrel provided tactical support and, at the end, gave the party the location of a sealed drainage tunnel in the southern terraces.
