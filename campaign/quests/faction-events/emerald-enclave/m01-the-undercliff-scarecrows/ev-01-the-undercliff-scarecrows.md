# The Undercliff Scarecrows

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Combat Event begins when Melannor's pigeon reaches an Emerald Enclave member at Trollskull Manor and ends when the members report to him at Phaulkonmere. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Melannor's 24-word message, or walk to Phaulkonmere and question him in person.
> - Find the three scarecrows' territories by three routes: Gerrick, the tracks, or the animals.
> - Fight the Sackcloth One, the Pumpkin Head and the Blanket one at a time, using a decoy, a runner or a lantern.
> - Keep fire out of the dry fields, or accept the risk of burning a farm.
> - Find the Splinter's testing site and its journal page by three routes.
> - Learn where Gerrick's sealed drain comes out, by three routes.
>
> Only Emerald Enclave members receive the pigeon and attend the debrief. Their companions can help with everything in between.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - A Splinter arcanist animated all three scarecrows from a chalk circle about a tenday ago. He wrote that all three woke at once and that he left before he could reorient them. Nothing controls them now.
> - The journal page is in the hand of Imre Daskov, the arcanist in **The Fouled Channel**. Nobody in this Event can learn his name.
> - Melannor calls the arcanist's cell "the Splinter" and never names its master. Nobody in this Event says Manshoon.
> - Gerrick's drain was sealed six years ago after a chuul scout from Illuun's brood came up through it. His neighbours drove it back with fire. Gerrick never learned what it was, and nobody in this Event can tell him.
> - Each scarecrow stays in its own territory. A party cannot drag a second one into a fight.
> - Run one territory per day and give the party a Long Rest between fights, so the Day Costs do not add. Fighting all three in one day is not offered.
> - The farms have penned their stock and shut their doors since the third night. For three days after the party arrives, the scarecrows hurt nothing. On the fourth night each scarecrow still standing breaks into a pen and kills stock.

### The Brief

The pigeon reaches the member with the highest Wisdom score, or the higher Constitution score on a tie. It arrives at breakfast on the first morning that member has marked **Emerald Enclave Joined** and reached 2nd level. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> You are at breakfast in the common room of Trollskull Manor when a grey pigeon lands on the windowsill and walks along it, tilting its head at each of you in turn. It stops in front of one of you and speaks in a low, even baritone that seems to come from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > Three animated scarecrows are killing livestock on the Undercliff farms east of the walls. The Guard will not come. Find Gerrick Thatchley, a farmer.
>
> The bird waits a moment for an answer that does not come, then flaps out over the alley and is gone.

The message is 24 words, which fits the 25 words the 2024 *Animal Messenger* spell allows. The pigeon uses the Raven block, carries no reply and does not wait for one. Others nearby hear the message.

A member who wants more can walk to Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward, where Melannor answers questions at the gate. Companions wait outside the gate. If a member goes, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open, and Melannor Fellbranch waits just inside it with a watering can at his feet. Behind him a path of old flagstones leads between flower beds toward a garden you cannot see the end of. He nods to each of you in turn and says your names correctly before you can introduce yourselves.

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper at the Gate**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the humourless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, who sends the Enclave's missions by animal and reports grim news as if describing the weather.
>
> Melannor speaks in plain, complete sentences at an even pace. He does not use contractions, he does not recognise a joke, and he never swears. He answers each question once and waits. He is worried about the farmers and does not show it.
>
> Conversation topics Melannor is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The scarecrows, and what the Enclave knows of them.
> - The Guard's refusal to act.
> - The old drains of the Undercliff.
> - What the Enclave offers for the work.
>
> He will not discuss what troubles the soil under the Castle Ward, because he cannot name it, and he does not guess aloud.

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> > You have joined the Enclave, and the Undercliff is our ground. I have no one else free this tenday. The farmers cannot wait for the Guard.

> [!qna]**A joke?**
>
> > That is not humorous. Three scarecrows are loose in the Undercliff, and livestock are already dead. A farmer named Gerrick Thatchley has watched them for three days.

> [!qna]**Who did this?**
>
> > Scarecrows do not wake on their own. Someone made them, and I would like to know who. I suggest you look for the place where it was done.

> [!qna]**The Guard?**
>
> > The Guard is occupied with the gangs and considers animated scarecrows beneath its notice. I do not agree. If nothing is done, a farmer will be hurt.

> [!qna]**Drains?**
>
> > The Enclave's gardeners keep a map of the Undercliff drains. One brick tunnel runs west beneath the southern terraces toward the Castle Ward cisterns. It was sealed six years ago.

If a member asks this, mark the party as having learned the drain from Melannor. That is one of the three routes in **The Sealed Tunnel**.

> [!qna]**What do we get?**
>
> > The Enclave does not pay in coin. Your standing with us will grow, and the farmers will remember what you did.

### The Undercliff

The farm terraces of the Undercliff lie a morning's walk east of the city, past the walls. The party reaches them in the afternoon of the first day. Three territories lie inside them: the northern fields hold the Sackcloth One, the central orchards hold the Pumpkin Head, and the southern terraces hold the Blanket. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The farm tracks of the Undercliff wind between low stone walls and green terraces, and nobody is working in any of them. A set of tools leans against a wall where someone put them down and did not come back. At the far end of a long field a cart horse stands rigid with its head turned north, and a few sheep are huddled against a wall below it, not grazing. A farmer's garden gate swings open in the wind as you pass.

Finding the territories can be done three ways. Each gives the same result: where each scarecrow holds its ground, and where it will appear next.

> [!exploration]**Finding the Territories**
>
> - **Gerrick.** Gerrick has watched all three for three days and tells the party everything he knows. See **Gerrick**.
> - **The tracks.** Any character who walks the fields and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Survival)** check reads the straw trails and flattened furrows well enough to name each scarecrow's territory and predict its next appearance.
> - **The animals.** The cart horse faces north because the Sackcloth One walks the fields there, and the huddled sheep are watching the southern road. A character who casts *Speak with Animals* on either learns this at once. Any character who studies the animals and makes a successful **DC 11 Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check works it out.
>
> A party that tries none of these walks the terraces for half an hour. That finds all three territories but not the next appearance points, and the party cannot prepare the ground.

> [!gamemaster]**Prepared Ground**
>
> A party that knows each scarecrow's next appearance can set up before the fight. Gerrick lends the decoy and the lantern for it. A party without that knowledge still fights, and the scarecrow opens the fight at 30 feet from the nearest character instead.

### Gerrick

Gerrick Thatchley waits in the middle of his north field. He has been waiting three days for the Guard, and the party is the first help to arrive. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Gerrick Thatchley stands alone in the middle of a stubble field with a pitchfork in one hand and a lantern in the other, glaring at the hedge along its far side. He is a stout halfling of about fifty with a weathered face, and he does not turn around when your boots crunch on the stubble.
>
> > If you're the Guard, you can turn around and fuck off. They told me a fortnight, and that was five days ago.
>
> He looks over his shoulder at you and lowers the pitchfork a little.
>
> > You're not the Guard, are you? Good. The fucking Guard hasn't done a useful thing in forty years.

> [!social]**The Farmer in the Stubble**
>
> Gerrick Thatchley (Neutral, Lightfoot Halfling, he/him) :: a halfling farmer of about fifty who has worked this land for thirty years and has run out of patience.
>
> Gerrick swears freely at the Guard and at the scarecrows, and he is gruff but not unkind to anyone who has come to help. He has watched the three scarecrows from his barn for three days and noticed their habits. He would sooner do the work himself than ask for help, and he is relieved that he does not have to.
>
> Any character who watches him and makes a successful **DC 11 Wisdom (Insight)** check sees that he is more frightened for his cart horse than angry at anyone.
>
> Conversation topics Gerrick is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Where the scarecrows are and what they have done.
> - How to use the stream and the orchards against them.
> - Anything strange he has noticed in the north.
> - Where the party can rest.
>
> He lends the party two buckets, two shovels, a wool blanket, two lanterns and a ewe. He will not lend his laying goose.

> [!qna]**Where are they?**
>
> > One in my north fields, from dusk, going after anything with wool on it. One in the orchards at night, chasing whatever runs. The third stands by the milestone on the farm road. I call them Sackcloth, Pumpkin Head and the Blanket.

> [!qna]**What have they done?**
>
> > Three of my chickens and my best laying goose. The Pumpkin Head ran Marigold, my cart horse, halfway to the river, and she hasn't been right since. Old Tuckerman lost two ewes, and one of his lads got knocked flat.

> [!qna]**Any advice?**
>
> > There's a stream cut through the middle terraces with steep banks, and anything crossing it has to climb in and out. Fire works on straw, but the stubble's dry as tinder and my barn's sixty feet from this field. Do it down by the water if you do it at all.

> [!qna]**The Blanket?**
>
> Gerrick spits into the stubble before he answers.
>
> > It stands by the milestone right over my old drain. The drain comes out below the southern terraces, where the cliff path meets the city wall. We bricked it up six years ago after something came up out of it. The neighbours ran it off with torches before I got there, and nobody would tell me what it was.

If a character asks about the drain, mark the party as having learned it from Gerrick. That is one of the three routes in **The Sealed Tunnel**.

> [!qna]**Anything odd up here?**
>
> > Chalk marks on a flat stone in the eastern draw, past my north field. Arcanist's work, I'd say, though I didn't touch it. I'm not stupid.

> [!qna]**Where can we rest?**
>
> > The hayloft's dry and clean. Keep your lanterns out of it, or I'll have your hides.

### The Three Scarecrows

The party fights the scarecrows one at a time. Each stays in its own territory, so the party chooses the order and the day. A scarecrow keeps any damage it has taken and does not follow anyone out of its ground.

> [!hazard]**Why Never Two Together**
>
> A Scarecrow's Terrifying Glare can Paralyze a character, and every hit against a Paralyzed character within 5 feet is a critical hit. One scarecrow needs two turns to do both. Two scarecrows can do it in a single round, and a 2nd-level character does not survive the second critical hit. That is why the territories keep them apart. Do not let one leave its ground, and do not run two fights at once.
>
> The Day Costs are Bruising at three characters and Mild at four or five. Three fights on three separate days leave the party rested for each.

> [!hazard]**Fire in the Dry Fields**
>
> The Scarecrow takes double damage from fire. The north stubble field and the southern terrace pasture are dry, and the other ground is green. Track the **Burn** from 0 to 6 in a dry field only:
>
> - A dry-field space is first set alight by a fire spell, a thrown oil flask, alchemist's fire or a burning scarecrow stepping into stubble: Burn +2 at once.
> - At the end of each round in which any patch is still burning: Burn +1.
> - A character spends an action beating out flames with the blanket or a shovel (Utilize, **DC 10 Strength or Dexterity** check): Burn -1.
> - A character within 30 feet of the stream spends an action throwing a bucket (no check): Burn -2.
>
> A scarecrow that takes fire damage is alight. At the end of each of its turns it sets its own space alight, which counts as a new ignition in a dry field. At Burn 6 the nearest farm's outbuildings catch, and the fire is out at 0. Mark **Farm Damaged by Fire** when the scene ends. A party that uses no fire risks nothing. The quick route costs the clock.

#### The Sackcloth One

The Sackcloth One walks the northern stubble fields from dusk. It goes for livestock first and ignores people while an animal is near. A stone sheepfold stands in the middle of the field, and Gerrick's barn lies 60 feet beyond the field's edge. Read the following aloud as dusk falls:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stubble fields run flat and grey under an orange sky, and a stone sheepfold stands in the middle of them with one ewe shut inside. You wait behind the barn wall. Something tall walks out of the hedge along the eastern edge, a figure of sackcloth stuffed with straw and tied to a pole, and it turns its head toward the ewe and starts across the field.

> [!hazard]**The Sackcloth One**
>
> One **Scarecrow**. The field is dry, so the Burn rules apply, and the only remedy here is beating out flames. Gerrick's barn is the nearest outbuilding.
>
> If the party set the ewe in the fold before dusk, the Sackcloth One ignores the party completely until the ewe is dead or gone.
>
> #### The Sackcloth One's Tactics
> At the start of combat, with the ewe in the fold, the Sackcloth One walks to the fold. Without a decoy, it uses Terrifying Glare on the first character who attacks it or crosses the eastern hedge.
>
> During combat, the Sackcloth One:
> - Spends its first turn reaching the fold and its second tearing the gate open, ignoring the party even when attacked, so the party gets two rounds of free attacks.
> - Kills the ewe on its third turn if it is still standing. Mark that the party has lost the ewe, and the +1 for three days is lost with it.
> - Turns on the character who last attacked it once the ewe is dead, using Terrifying Glare on that character. On its next turn it moves to the Paralyzed character and Claws.
> - Does not attack a creature at 0 hit points and turns to the next nearest instead.
>
> The fight ends when the Sackcloth One is destroyed. It does not flee. The party can withdraw past the hedge, and it stays in the field.
>
> **Non-combat route.** A character who leads the ewe on a rope across the field crosses it unharmed, because the Sackcloth One ignores people near an animal. Crossing does not destroy it.

#### The Pumpkin Head

The Pumpkin Head walks the central orchards at night and chases anything that runs. The orchard grass is green and wet and does not carry fire. A stream cut runs along the south end of the rows, with steep banks and water at the bottom. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Pale blossom hangs over the apple rows in the dark, and the wet grass muffles your steps. At the far end of the lane stands a thin body of sticks and old clothes topped with a carved gourd, its hollow face turned toward you. When the first of you breaks into a run, it twists on its pole and starts down the row after that character.

> [!hazard]**The Pumpkin Head**
>
> One **Scarecrow**. The orchard is green, so the Burn rules do not apply and fire is safe here. A scorched trunk is the worst a burning scarecrow can do.
>
> The stream cut is the funnel. It is six feet deep and cannot be crossed without climbing down and up, and crossing takes the Pumpkin Head its whole turn.
>
> #### The Pumpkin Head's Tactics
> At the start of combat, the Pumpkin Head uses Terrifying Glare on the character who runs, meaning any character who takes the Dash action in its sight.
>
> During combat, the Pumpkin Head:
> - Chases the runner, moving toward the Paralyzed character on its next turn to Claw.
> - Spends its whole turn climbing into and out of the stream cut when it crosses, so a runner on the south bank is out of its reach.
> - Does not attack a creature at 0 hit points and turns to the next nearest instead.
>
> A party that sends a runner dashing across the cut and waits on the south bank 10 feet back gets a full round of ranged attacks while the Pumpkin Head climbs.
>
> The fight ends when the Pumpkin Head is destroyed. It does not flee. The party can withdraw out of the orchard, and it stays there.
>
> **Non-combat route.** Walking at a normal pace draws no pursuit, so the party can cross the orchard without running.

#### The Blanket

The Blanket stands by the milestone on the farm road at the foot of the southern terraces, facing the road with its back to the hill. It cannot be crept up on, and it walks toward any steady light. The pasture beside it is dry grass, with Tuckerman's shearing shed 60 feet from its edge, and the stream runs along the pasture's north side. If the party hung the lantern, read the following aloud as the light fails:

> [!readaloud]
>
> At the foot of the terrace a stone milestone stands beside the farm road, and a tall figure wrapped in a grey horse blanket stands next to it, facing down the road. The lantern you hung on a fence post near the stream steadies as the wind drops. The figure turns its head toward the flame and starts to walk across the pasture.

> [!hazard]**The Blanket**
>
> One **Scarecrow**. The pasture is dry, so the Burn rules apply. A bucket can be thrown from the stream at any patch within 30 feet of it. Tuckerman's shearing shed is the nearest outbuilding.
>
> If the party hangs a lit lantern on the fence post nearest the stream at dusk or after, the Blanket leaves its post and walks to the lantern. By day it holds its post, and no lantern draws it.
>
> #### The Blanket's Tactics
> At the start of combat, the Blanket uses Terrifying Glare on the character carrying a light. If the light hangs on a post, it walks to the post and stands under it, and does not use Glare until a character attacks it or the flame goes out.
>
> During combat, the Blanket:
> - Stares at the lantern for its first round, giving all attacks against it Advantage that round.
> - Moves to a Paralyzed character on its next turn and Claws.
> - Does not attack a creature at 0 hit points and turns to the next nearest instead.
> - By day, uses Terrifying Glare on the first character to come within 30 feet of the milestone.
>
> The fight ends when the Blanket is destroyed. It does not flee. The party can withdraw down the road, and it stays at its post.
>
> **Non-combat route.** A character who stays more than 30 feet from the milestone is not noticed. The outlet of Gerrick's drain can be seen from that distance.

### The Testing Site

The chalk circle lies in a sheltered draw east of Gerrick's north field, close to the Sackcloth One's ground. The party can find it three ways.

> [!exploration]**Finding the Draw**
>
> - **Gerrick.** If the party asked about anything odd, Gerrick points out the flat stone in the eastern draw. See **Gerrick**.
> - **The fields.** Any character who spends an hour searching the north fields and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check notices a path of broken chalk dust that leads east to the draw.
> - **The Sackcloth One.** The scarecrow's patrol never passes the eastern hedge, because the circle holds it close. Any character who has watched or fought it sees that it turns back at the same place every time, and the draw lies just beyond.
>
> A party that tries none of these finds the draw on the second day when Gerrick, passing it on his rounds, mentions the stone again.

When the party reaches the draw, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> In a hollow shielded by two hawthorn hedges, a flat slab of rock lies bare among the grass. A ring of white chalk about eight feet across is drawn on it, with marks around the rim that are smudged by rain but still plain. Three straw-and-rope bundles sit at the points of a triangle in the middle, and a sheet of paper is pinned under a stone at the center.

> [!exploration]**Reading the Circle**
>
> Any character who studies the marks and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana)** check recognises an animation ritual built to wake three objects at once. It has no binding line, so nothing holds or controls what it woke. If nobody makes the check, Melannor reads the circle at the debrief.

> [!exploration]**The Journal Page**
>
> The page is a single sheet torn from a notebook, covered in a cramped hand. Any character who reads it learns what it says:
>
> > Undercliff field test, third night. Chalk circle, eight feet. Three vessels, straw and cloth. All three activated simultaneously, unexpected — departed before they could reorient.
>
> At the foot of the page a small circle is drawn and split by a jagged line. Melannor knows this as the Splinter's mark. The party can take the page.

### The Sealed Tunnel

Gerrick's drain runs west under the wall from the southern terraces to a cistern access in the Castle Ward. Its outlet lies below the southern terraces, where the cliff path meets the city wall. The party learns where it is by three routes, and each marks **Drainage Tunnel Learned**.

> [!exploration]**Learning the Outlet**
>
> - **Gerrick.** He mentions it when asked about the Blanket. If the party has not learned it by the time the last scarecrow falls, he tells them as they leave, unprompted, and says the Enclave may want it.
> - **Melannor.** He knows the Undercliff drains and tells the party if a member asks. See **The Brief** and **Debrief at Phaulkonmere**.
> - **The outlet.** It is in plain sight from the farm road, just beyond the Blanket's milestone. Any character who stands at the terrace edge and looks down sees it without a check.

When a character looks at the outlet, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Below the southern terrace, where the cliff path runs into the city wall, a low brick arch sits in the hillside. It has been filled with mortared stone and an iron grate rusted into place. Water seeps from the foot of the grate and runs down into the stream.

> [!gamemaster]**The Seal**
>
> The seal is two feet of mortared stone behind an iron grate. Nothing behind it can be reached in this Event, and nothing behind it is awake. If the party digs at it, Gerrick asks them to stop and says the Enclave will ask for it when it is needed. **The Fouled Channel** gives the party a reason to open it later.

When the last scarecrow is down, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Gerrick leans on his pitchfork at the edge of the field and looks over the quiet stubble. He turns to you and nods once.
>
> > Thank you, all of you. If the Enclave ever needs that drain, you tell them where it is.

If **Farm Damaged by Fire** is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Gerrick stands at the edge of the burned ground with his pitchfork hanging from one hand, and he looks at the barn for a long moment before he turns to you.
>
> > Straw's gone, and so is the roof. Fine. You still got them, and I won't forget that. If the Enclave ever needs that drain, you tell them where it is.

### Debrief at Phaulkonmere

The members report to Melannor on the evening they finish, or after breakfast the next day if they are late. Companions wait at the gate. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open before you reach it, and no one is near to have opened it. The garden inside is older than the street around it, with moss on the walls and roots lifting the paving stones. A calm woman's voice says "Rest here," and it comes from no direction in particular while the leaves stir along the path. Melannor waits beside the oldest oak in the garden with a trowel in his hand, and he sets it down when he sees you.

Jeryth speaks nothing else in this Event. Melannor is as described in **The Brief**. Let the members report in their own words, then continue with the questions below.

> [!qna]**The page?**
>
> Melannor takes the sheet and reads it twice without changing his expression.
>
> > This is the Splinter's mark, the cell of the Zhentarim that broke away from the rest. They tested a ritual on your farmers' fields and left it running. I will give this to Jeryth.

Mark **Splinter Site Reported** when a member hands Melannor the page.

> [!qna]**What is the Splinter?**
>
> > The Splinter is the Zhentarim cell that does not answer to the others. The Enclave has watched it for some time. I do not know why it came to the Undercliff.

> [!qna]**The drain?**
>
> Melannor looks toward the oak for a moment before he answers.
>
> > I will ask nothing of you about it yet. The soil there is uneasy, and I would like to know why.

Use this answer only if **Drainage Tunnel Learned** is marked.

### Renown Opportunities

Melannor ends the debrief once he has what he needs. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor looks at each of you in turn and folds his hands in front of him.
>
> > You did what was asked, and the Undercliff is safe again. The Enclave thanks you. I will send word when there is more.

If the party destroyed all three scarecrows within three days, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor tilts his head, which is the closest he comes to surprise.
>
> > Gerrick tells me no animal and no person was hurt after you arrived. That is better than I hoped. The Enclave will remember it.

If **Splinter Site Reported** is marked, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor lays the page on a flat stone by the oak and sets his trowel on it to hold it.
>
> > You found the place where this began. That is more than the Guard would have done.

If **Farm Damaged by Fire** is marked, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor does not change his tone.
>
> > I am told a barn burned. Fire was a permitted tool, but I would have preferred the stream. I hope Gerrick forgives it.

If **Farm Damaged by Fire** is unmarked, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor nods slowly.
>
> > Gerrick tells me his barn still stands. I am glad of it.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 2 base Renown for ending the scarecrows' attacks and reporting to Melannor. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the party destroyed all three scarecrows within three days of arriving, before more livestock or people were hurt.
> - **+1 Renown:** the member showed Melannor the testing-site journal page, and **Splinter Site Reported** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** the party left no farm burned, meaning **Farm Damaged by Fire** is unmarked.

### Aftermath

Gerrick tells his neighbours who came, and the farms open their doors again. Melannor gives the page to Jeryth, who does not answer. A member whose Renown reaches 3 here is called to Phaulkonmere by **A Seat at Phaulkonmere**, and **Summerstrider** follows at noon the next day. The Splinter's arcanist is long gone and does not return in this Event.

### Concluding the Event

Melannor's thanks end the assignment. Record what the party found and whether a farm burned, because later events read each of these.

## Overview

The party hunts three animated scarecrows across the farms of the Undercliff and finds the place where they were made.

## Summary

We walked out to the Undercliff and met a halfling farmer named Gerrick Thatchley, who told us where the three scarecrows hold their ground. We destroyed them one at a time and found a chalk circle with a journal page signed by a split circle. Melannor says the mark belongs to the Splinter, and Gerrick says there is an old drain below the terraces.
