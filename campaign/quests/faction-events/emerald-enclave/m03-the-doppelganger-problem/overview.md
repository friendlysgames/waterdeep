# The Doppelganger Problem

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Emerald Enclave Mission 3. Available at Renown 3 and character level 4.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 4th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

Doppelgangers are operating freely out of the Yawning Portal while four factions compete for the same city, the same information, and the same room. The Enclave cannot permit unaligned shapeshifters reading everyone's loyalties where intelligence is the most dangerous thing on the table. Bonnie and her crew must leave Waterdeep.

The mission is a negotiation. Bonnie already knows why the party is there. She has already decided what she will accept. One of her crew has been selling party names to the Manshoon Splinter, and she has been sitting on that knowledge, waiting for the moment when it would be most useful to spend it.

If the party lets Bonnie handle the traitor in her own way, she honors the departure agreement and gives them a parting gift: the name of the Splinter operative who bought the intelligence from her crew. That name is **Kelso Fiddlewick**. Melannor passes it to both the Harpers and the Lords' Alliance independently, and it becomes an active investigation thread in **Faction Outposts**.

## Involved Characters
- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave) — delivers the mission via falcon; includes a postscript about Jeryth's emphasis
- **Bonnie** (doppelganger, crew leader) — present at her usual station at the Yawning Portal; has already anticipated this conversation; has been holding the traitor's identity in reserve, waiting for the right moment to use it
- **The Traitor** (doppelganger, Manshoon Splinter informant) — one of Bonnie's five crew members; sold party names to a Splinter operative; actively undermines the negotiation when the party arrives

## Dangers & Enemies

Five **Doppelgangers** (2024 *Monster Manual*). Violence is not expected and Jeryth's instruction — "peacefully if possible," repeated three times — is not decorative. The traitor may provoke a fight if the party handles the reveal clumsily, but Bonnie will intervene cleanly if she has the standing to do so.

## Overview

Five doppelgangers are operating freely out of the Yawning Portal. One has been selling intelligence to the Manshoon Splinter. Bonnie already knows, and she is waiting for the party to give her a reason to act.
