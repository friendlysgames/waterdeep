# The Doppelganger Problem

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when Melannor's falcon reaches an eligible Emerald Enclave member and ends when a member reports to Melannor at Phaulkonmere. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Melannor's demand at Phaulkonmere: Bonnie's doppelganger crew leaves Waterdeep, peacefully if possible.
> - Reach Bonnie at the Yawning Portal in the open, through Durnan or through her crew, and speak with her in the back room after closing.
> - Find out which of the five has been selling names, by his objections, by Bonnie's payment trail or by what Melannor's falcon saw.
> - Settle the crew's departure within two tendays, or argue to Melannor that Bonnie is no threat if she serves the Harpers.
> - Learn that Kelso Fiddlewick bought the names, and report to Melannor.
>
> Only Emerald Enclave members attend Melannor's brief and debrief. Their companions can come to every other scene. The dove in **The Receipt** arrives after the debrief and changes nothing.

> [!gamemaster]**Who Knows What**
>
> - Bonnie leads five doppelgangers: herself, Edric Tanner, Kael, Syla and the Scholar. They came to Waterdeep over a year ago and live on honest wages. Bonnie wears a Tethyrian barmaid form at the Portal and never talks about what she is in the taproom.
> - Edric joined two months ago. He reads names from patrons' thoughts and sells them to Kelso Fiddlewick, the names of Enclave members and their companions among them. Kelso buys stolen information and sells it to whoever pays, and he never names a customer. In truth the Splinter is his customer, and a Shard Shunners runner carries Edric's paper to Beldan Rusk's house on Nethpranter Street.
> - Bonnie traced a payment to Edric three weeks ago and has told nobody. She reads surface thoughts at will, so she knows why the party has come before anyone speaks.
> - Melannor learned what Bonnie is from his animals, cats in the Portal's cellar and gulls over the quay. Mattrim Mereg is not the only person outside the crew who knows.
> - Durnan knows nothing of what Bonnie is and will not hear it from the party.
> - **Harper M3 Complete** is world state. If any Harper member has marked it, Edric is already out of the crew, his red-wheeled cart is gone from the yard, and he does not enter the back room.
> - Nobody in this Event mentions Manshoon.

### The Brief

Melannor's falcon reaches each eligible member at breakfast on the day the mission opens. It is his *Animal Messenger* spell, and companions nearby hear the message too. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A falcon drops out of the morning sky and lands on the sill of the taproom window, and the sparrows there scatter. It fixes one yellow eye on you and speaks in a low, level baritone that seems to come from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > "Doppelgangers operate from the Yawning Portal. They must leave Waterdeep, peacefully if possible. Come to Phaulkonmere first, and I will explain."
>
> The falcon waits a moment, finds no question in the room and takes off over the rooftops toward the south.

> [!gamemaster]**Message Count**
>
> The message is 21 words, spoken once. The falcon carries no reply and uses the Hawk block if anyone attacks it.

Members reach Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward by midmorning. Companions wait at the gate or in the outer garden. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The iron gate of Phaulkonmere stands open before you reach it. Inside, hedges, climbing roses and mossy flagstones run back much further than the street would allow, and everything in the garden looks older than the houses around it. The oldest oak in the middle of the lawn is wider than a cart. A woman's voice answers from no direction, and the leaves on the nearest hedge turn toward you as it speaks.
>
> > "Rest here."
>
> Melannor Fellbranch kneels beside the oak with a trowel in one hand and a bed of seedlings at his knees. He sets the trowel down and wipes his hands on his apron before he walks over to you.

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the humourless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, who delivers grim news in a calm baritone.
>
> Melannor stands with his hands folded and states every fact in the same even voice. He does not recognise a joke and answers one literally. He wants the crew gone without a fight, and he repeats the demand as often as anyone asks. He allows the crew two tendays and no longer.
>
> Conversation topics Melannor is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Why the Enclave wants the crew out of Waterdeep.
> - What the animals have seen, including the teamster with the red wheel.
> - Where each of the five can be found.
> - How much time the Enclave will allow.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr or the Splinter's master.

> [!qna]**Why must they leave?**
>
> > "Five people who can read thoughts and wear any face sit in the one room where every faction in the city talks. The Enclave has no quarrel with them. We cannot leave that room open to whoever pays them."

> [!qna]**What have the animals seen?**
>
> > "Bonnie leads them and pours drinks at the Portal. A sailor works the docks, a bookkeeper works for a widow, and a copyist works for a bookseller. A teamster drives a cart with one red wheel. Two mornings ago my falcon watched him give folded paper to a halfling runner on the quay."

> [!qna]**How did you learn what Bonnie is?**
>
> > "Cats hear a great deal in cellars, and gulls watch the quay. Over a season they told me the same thing many times. I did not need to ask them twice."

> [!qna]**What if she refuses?**
>
> > "Then I will ask again. Jeryth said peacefully three times. She does not usually say anything three times."

> [!qna]**How long can we give her?**
>
> > "She may have two tendays to settle her affairs. I will not allow longer. Report to me here when you have spoken with her."

### The Portal

The Yawning Portal stays open until 01:00, when Durnan bars the door. Bonnie works the floor until then and will not talk about what she is in the taproom. The party has three ways to reach her, and each ends with the same arrangement: the back room, after closing. Companions come with the party. Read the following aloud when the party enters:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Yawning Portal is loud and crowded this evening, and patrons lean over the rail of the great well to watch a rope sink into the dark. Durnan works the bar with a rag over one shoulder, and a barmaid with a brown braid carries a tray of stews through the crowd without spilling any. She sets a mug in front of a regular, makes him laugh, and glances across the room at you.

**Asking Bonnie in the open.** A character who asks Bonnie at the bar for a private word gets the following answer without a check. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie sets a fresh mug in front of you before you finish asking and wipes the same patch of bar twice.
>
> > "Dark ale, love, and no, not here. Durnan bars the door at one. I use the back room after that, so bring your friends and I'll bring the key."

**Asking Durnan.** A character who asks Durnan for the back room is sent to Bonnie's end of the bar for the rest.

> [!social]**The Man Behind the Bar**
>
> Durnan (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: the Yawning Portal's owner, who polishes the same mug through every conversation and knows nothing of what Bonnie is.
>
> Durnan answers in as few words as he can and points instead of giving directions. He lends the back room after closing to anyone who asks and stays out of what is said there. He will not discuss Undermountain.

> [!qna]**About Bonnie?**
>
> > "Best barmaid I've had."

> [!qna]**Can we talk to her privately?**
>
> > "After close. Back room's free."

> [!qna]**Is something wrong with her?**
>
> > "Pours well. Doesn't steal."

> [!qna]**What if there's trouble?**
>
> > "Not in my taproom. Don't make it my fucking problem."

**Through the crew.** Melannor's animals have seen where each of the other four works. A character who finds one of them and says Bonnie's name and the word Enclave gets the same answer from each: after closing, in the back room. A character who threatens any of them gets the same answer in a colder voice, and Bonnie hears of it before the party arrives.

- **Kael** unloads cargo on the Dock Ward quay at dusk, and he says only "Not my watch. Ask Bonnie. Back room, after close."
- **Syla** keeps accounts for a widow in the Trades Ward, and she answers with a question: "And if I said no? Back room, after close, and you can ask her."
- **The Scholar** copies at a bookseller's stall, and he names a price: "Call it forty a job. Back room, after close. Tell her I sent you."

Any character who watches the stable yard between 22:00 and 23:00 sees the following without a check, unless **Harper M3 Complete** is marked, in which case the yard is empty. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A stocky man in a leather jerkin brings a cart into the stable yard and unhooks the horse, and one wheel of the cart is painted red. A halfling in a yellow scarf comes in from the lane while he works. The halfling takes a folded sheet from the man's coat, presses a coin into his palm, and is back through the gate before the horse has finished drinking.

> [!exploration]**The Red Wheel**
>
> The stocky man is Edric Tanner, and the halfling is a Shard Shunners runner. The runner will not talk and runs for the Field Ward if followed. A character who reports what Melannor's falcon saw and now sees it too needs no check to connect the two.

### The Back Room

Durnan leaves the back room unlocked at 01:00 and goes upstairs. Bonnie arrives a few minutes later. The talk runs about an hour, and companions may sit in. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The back room smells of lamp oil and old barrels, and a single lantern burns on a cask beside a table with five stools. Bonnie shuts the door behind her, hangs her apron on a peg and sits on the nearest stool. She wears the same face as in the taproom, but the warm tavern voice is gone and she speaks flat and dry.
>
> > "Well, shit. Enclave. I've been hearing it all evening over the stew pot, so you can skip the speech. Say what you came to say and I'll tell you what it costs."

> [!social]**The Barmaid After Hours**
>
> Bonnie (Neutral, Doppelganger, she/her) :: the crew's leader, who reads surface thoughts at will and has already decided the Enclave's request is reasonable.
>
> In the back room Bonnie is economical and practical, with a dry humour about her own nature. She swears mildly and asks as many questions as she answers. She wants someone to say what leaving costs her before she agrees, and she trusts a plain statement over a threat. She will not discuss her crew's real faces or where they came from.
>
> Conversation topics Bonnie is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What leaving would cost the crew.
> - Why the Enclave cares and how long she needs.
> - Durnan, and what he must never learn.
> - Who else knows what she is.

> [!qna]**What are you?**
>
> > "A doppelganger, as you've been told. Faces are just work clothes. That's all you need and more than most people get."

> [!qna]**What does leaving cost you?**
>
> > "Five wages, a room with a good stove, and a year of people not asking questions. I like it here, which surprised me. I didn't plan on that."

> [!qna]**Why does the Enclave care?**
>
> > "Because I read minds in a room where every faction talks. I'd worry about me too. I'd just rather you asked than sent a falcon."

> [!qna]**Does Durnan know?**
>
> > "He thinks I'm a girl from Tethyr with a bad knee. He pays me fairly and he's never asked. Keep it that way."

> [!qna]**Who else knows what you are?**
>
> > "Apparently every cat in the cellar. That's how you found out, isn't it?"

### The Traitor

Edric Tanner joins the talk, having read the party's thoughts at the bar and followed them to the back room. If **Harper M3 Complete** is marked, he is not there, and the rules for that case follow the buyer's name below. Read the following aloud when the party has spoken with Bonnie for a few minutes:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The kitchen door opens, and a stocky man in a leather jerkin comes in with a jug in one hand and three cups hooked on his fingers. He sets the cups on the table and grins around at you as though you were old friends.
>
> > "Right, so this is the Enclave. Edric Tanner, Dock Ward carts. Nobody asked the crew what we think, and that doesn't seem fair."

> [!social]**The Cheerful Teamster**
>
> Edric Tanner (Neutral Evil, Doppelganger, he/him) :: a warm, talkative doppelganger who joined Bonnie's crew two months ago and sells what he learns.
>
> Edric begins nearly every answer with "Right," and argues in the language of fairness. He says the Enclave has no say over neutral ground, that Bonnie has agreed to nothing and that the crew was left out. Every argument is for himself. He reads surface thoughts at will and uses them to choose his next question. He stays pleasant if accused, finishes his cup and leaves by the kitchen door.
>
> Conversation topics Edric is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The crew's right to a say.
> - His routes and cargo.
> - What the Enclave is, and who gave it authority.
>
> He will not discuss his buyer or his pay.

> [!qna]**What do you want?**
>
> > "Right, a say. Five of us live here, and one druid with a falcon doesn't get to decide it for the lot."

> [!qna]**Who gave the Enclave authority?**
>
> > "Right, nobody did. So why is the crew packing, and why is Bonnie the only one being asked?"

> [!exploration]**Three Ways to Edric**
>
> Three independent routes show that Edric sold the names. Any one is enough for a character to tell Bonnie.
>
> - **His objections:** Any character who listens to him and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that he argues for himself and not for the crew. His eyes stay on Bonnie and never on the others, and he never mentions what Kael, Syla or the Scholar want.
> - **Bonnie's trail:** If a character has said what leaving costs her, Bonnie slides a folded slip onto the table once Edric has spoken. It lists three payments of 5 gp over three weeks, each traced from a Field Ward fence's till to Edric's boot. This needs no check.
> - **The animals:** A character who heard Melannor's report of the teamster with the red wheel, or watched the yard, knows what Edric did two mornings ago. Saying so aloud needs no check.
>
> If Edric learns that the party suspects him, he stays pleasant and keeps pouring.

When a character tells Bonnie that Edric sold the names, by any route, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie sets her cup down and looks at Edric for some time before she looks back at you.
>
> > "He's the one who sold your names. I traced the coin three weeks ago and wanted to see whether anyone else would. Edric, you're done with the crew. Out."
>
> Edric drains his cup, shrugs and walks to the kitchen door.
>
> > "Right. I know when I'm not wanted."

Bonnie then gives the buyer. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie waits until the kitchen door has closed and then turns the lantern up a little.
>
> > "You'll want the buyer too. Kelso Fiddlewick, Field Ward, a halfling with a scar along his jaw. He buys what people steal and sells it to whoever pays, and he never says who."

Mark **Traitor Identified**. Edric leaves Waterdeep within the tenday, with one of Melannor's animals following him to the gate.

If **Harper M3 Complete** is marked, Edric is already gone. Bonnie says the same words about him in the past tense as soon as a character asks who sold the names or says what leaving costs her, and she gives Kelso's name in the same way. Mark **Traitor Identified**.

If nobody names Edric by any route, Bonnie lets him finish his cup and sends him out to the kitchen herself. She does not give Kelso's name, and **Traitor Identified** is not marked.

> [!gamemaster]**If the Party Sides with Edric**
>
> A character who repeats Edric's objections to Bonnie as though they were true, or who presses her to leave without hearing the crew, has sided with Edric. The agreement in **The Departure** then needs a DC 18 check, and Bonnie does not give Kelso's name. **Traitor Identified** is not marked.

> [!gamemaster]**If Steel Is Drawn on Bonnie**
>
> If a character draws a weapon on Bonnie, she shifts into a stranger's face, says "Well, shit," and is out through the kitchen before anyone can move. Roll no initiative for her. The crew leaves the city that night. The mission fails and awards no Renown. Mark **Doppelgangers Departed** and **Bonnie Departed** at once, and do not mark **Traitor Identified**. No dove arrives in **The Receipt**. The Emerald Enclave Mechanics Reference shows why the party cannot win this fight: the crew together is Impossible at every party size.

> [!hazard]**If the Party Lays Hands on Edric**
>
> Combat begins only if a character attacks Edric, grabs him or draws steel on him. Durnan opens the door at once and says "Not in my taproom," and the fight moves to the kitchen and the stable yard. Edric fights alone at every party size as an ordinary 2024 **Doppelganger**. Bonnie stays out of it.
>
> #### Edric's Tactics
> Edric changes face with Shape-Shift as a bonus action and makes for the kitchen door, using Slam only on whoever blocks it.
>
> During combat, Edric:
> - Uses Unsettling Visage whenever two or more characters stand within 15 feet, then Slams the character in his way.
> - Dashes and changes face when he drops to 26 hit points or fewer. His clothes, hat and bag do not change, so a tracker picks him out of a crowd with a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check each round.
> - Loses a tracker only by changing clothes or breaking line of sight, and he needs a corner and an action for either.
> - Surrenders at once if a character strips his hat or coat, and also if he is cornered at 26 hit points or fewer or Grappled below half.
>
> A character who cuts him off in a street and makes a successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check Grapples him, with an escape DC of 11. Searching him takes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check and is automatic if he is Grappled. It finds the runner's drop and Kelso's name.
>
> The fight ends when Edric surrenders, escapes out of sight or is dropped. A surrendered Edric tells Bonnie everything, and she bars him from the crew. A character who lays hands on Edric forfeits the third Renown bonus, and the non-combat route is to let Bonnie send him out.

### The Departure

Bonnie asks for two tendays. Melannor will not allow longer, and she knows it. If **Bonnie Harper Operative** is marked, read the following aloud before the check:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie takes a folded paper from her apron and lays it on the table without opening it.
>
> > "I signed with Mirt, so I'm not a stranger to the Harpers. The others will go. I'll see to that. Ask your druid whether a Harper who reports to Mirt still counts as a threat."

> [!exploration]**Asking Bonnie to Go**
>
> Any character who tells Bonnie plainly what the crew gives up and still asks her to go convinces her without a check. Any character who makes the demand and makes a successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check convinces her too. The DC is 12 if the character names the cost first, and 18 if the party sided with Edric.
>
> - **On a failure:** Bonnie says she will answer when someone has said what it costs. A character who then names the cost convinces her without another check.
> - **If the party sided with Edric and fails:** Bonnie goes to the Phaulkonmere gate the next morning and agrees to Melannor herself, and the party hears of it from him.

When Bonnie agrees, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie lets out a long breath and stretches her back, and for a moment she looks like a barmaid at the end of a double shift.
>
> > "Two tendays. Kael will say it isn't his watch, Syla will ask what happens if she stays, and the Scholar will want paying. They'll go. Keep your cats out of Durnan's cellar, and don't tell him what I am."

Mark **Doppelgangers Departed**. The grace period is two tendays from this night. Bonnie keeps her terms: she closes her affairs, the crew leaves one by one, and the Enclave's animals stay away from the Portal. Edric leaves ahead of the others unless he is already gone.

> [!gamemaster]**The Grace Period**
>
> At the end of the second tenday, mark **Bonnie Departed** unless the party has won her reprieve. If **The Doppelganger Auditions** (Harpers Mission 3) runs inside the grace period, it can still mark **Bonnie Harper Operative**, and the party can go back to Melannor and argue her case. Once **Bonnie Departed** is marked, **The Doppelganger Auditions** is unavailable because the crew is gone, and Force Grey's scenes at the Portal play without her.

> [!exploration]**Bonnie's Case**
>
> This applies only if **Bonnie Harper Operative** is marked. At Phaulkonmere, at the debrief or on any visit before the grace period ends, any character who argues to Melannor that Bonnie is no threat and makes a successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check convinces him to let her stay. The character has Advantage if an individual Harper member of the party vouches for Bonnie's Harper terms: her crew's names kept private, her reports routed through the members, and her right to refuse any job.
>
> - **On a success:** Edric, Kael, Syla and the Scholar still go. Bonnie stays at the Portal. Do not mark **Bonnie Departed**.
> - **On a failure:** Melannor's demand stands. Bonnie leaves with the others when the grace period ends, and the party gets one attempt.

### The Receipt

Three days after the grace period ends, a dove lands on the windowsill at Trollskull Manor with a folded note in its beak. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A grey dove lands on the sill of your window and waits until you take the folded note from its beak. Inside is a single sheet with three dots in a triangle in the middle and nothing else.

The next time a member shows the note to Melannor, he reads it. If **Bonnie Departed** is marked, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor turns the sheet over once and hands it back.
>
> > "That is a doppelganger notation mark. It is her name, essentially. She is confirming that she has gone. It is, in its way, a receipt."

If the party won her reprieve, read the following aloud instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor turns the sheet over once and hands it back.
>
> > "That is a doppelganger notation mark. It is her name, essentially. She is confirming that the others have gone and that she has stayed. It is, in its way, a receipt."

If the crew left after steel was drawn, no dove arrives.

### Renown Opportunities

A member reports to Melannor in the garden the morning after the back room, or after the grace period if the party chose to wait. Companions wait at the gate. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor is on his knees in the vegetable bed with a basket of weeds beside him, and he sits back on his heels as you cross the lawn. The oak above him moves in a wind you cannot feel.
>
> > "Report. Have they agreed to leave?"

If Bonnie agreed to the departure, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor listens to the end without moving, then nods once.
>
> > "Two tendays is within what I allowed. The soil in the Portal's cellar will be easier when they have gone. I thank you for doing it peacefully."

If **Traitor Identified** is marked, he adds the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Kelso Fiddlewick. I will remember the name. I do not know who buys from him, and I will not guess."

If the party won Bonnie's reprieve, read the following aloud instead of the first branch:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor is silent for the length of a long breath.
>
> > "Four will leave and one will stay, and she will report to the Harpers. That is more than I expected from the argument. I will tell the animals to leave the Portal alone."

If the crew fled after steel was drawn, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor listens to the end and looks at the bed in front of him.
>
> > "Then they are gone. That was the request. It was not the manner I asked for."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 3 base Renown for meeting Bonnie and reporting to Melannor. Companions gain none. If steel was drawn on Bonnie, nobody gains Renown or any bonus.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** **Traitor Identified** is marked. Edric is named and Bonnie gives Kelso's name.
> - **+1 Renown:** the departure is settled without violence, with Bonnie leaving or staying as the outcome falls. Nobody draws steel on Bonnie or her crew, and she agrees to the demand or the party wins her reprieve.
> - **+1 Renown:** Bonnie deals with Edric herself. No character grapples, searches, strikes or follows him. If **Harper M3 Complete** is marked, this is earned when the party leaves Edric alone.
>
> Each bonus can be earned only once.

### Aftermath

Bonnie closes her affairs in two tendays, and the crew leaves one by one. If the party won her reprieve, she keeps her place at the Portal and Durnan never learns what she is. Edric is out of the crew either way, and Melannor's animals follow him to the gate.

Kelso Fiddlewick keeps selling to whoever pays. He does not know who gave his name, and he will not volunteer it. The Splinter keeps buying from him.

Melannor tells the animals to leave the Portal alone once the crew is gone.
### Concluding the Event

The report to Melannor ends the assignment. Record who sold the names, who bought them and whether Bonnie stays, because later events read it.

## Overview

Melannor asks the party to persuade a crew of doppelgangers at the Yawning Portal to leave Waterdeep. Their leader, Bonnie, already knows why the party has come.

## Summary

We met Melannor at Phaulkonmere and spoke with Bonnie in the Portal's back room after closing. One of her crew had been selling names to Kelso Fiddlewick, and she agreed to take the crew out of Waterdeep. We do not know who buys from Kelso.
