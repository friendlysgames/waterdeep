# The Doppelganger Problem

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event begins when the falcon briefing arrives at Trollskull Manor. In this Event:
>
> - All five doppelgangers are present at the Yawning Portal; Bonnie is working the evening shift.
> - Bonnie signals the party to a quiet table before they can approach — she has been expecting this conversation.
> - A DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check convinces her to agree; the DC drops to 12 if the party engages honestly with the cost of what they are asking.
> - The traitor doppelganger actively undermines the negotiation; a DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check distinguishes self-interest from principle.
> - If the party names that distinction, Bonnie reveals the traitor herself.
> - If Bonnie agrees and the party lets her handle the traitor, she names Kelso Fiddlewick as the Splinter contact who bought the intelligence.
> - Three days after departure, a dove arrives at Trollskull Manor with three dots in a triangle.

**Background (DM only)**
If `Harper M3 Complete: True` — if the Harper mission The Doppelganger Auditions has already run — Bonnie has already dealt with the traitor internally. She is aware the party may know her through that channel and acknowledges it if they raise it. The traitor doppelganger is not present in the taproom. The Complication phase below does not occur; the negotiation is cleaner and faster. The name of the Splinter contact is still available, given as before.

If Harper M3 has not run, this mission is the initial discovery. The traitor is present and the reveal happens here for the first time.

### The Yawning Portal — Evening

The taproom at capacity. Eight factions in thirteen conversations, none of them with each other. **Bonnie** is behind the bar in a form the party may recognize if they frequent the Portal, or in a forgettable service-staff form if they do not. She sees them the moment they enter. In her expression: the calibrated acknowledgment of a complication she has already prepared for.

She signals them to a corner table before they can approach the bar.

> > "I know what you're here for. And I know why the Enclave wants it. It's reasonable." She looks at the table. "Waterdeep is the most interesting city I have lived in. I've lived in eleven cities. I've been here eight months and I have not had to be anyone I didn't choose to be." She looks up. "How long do I have?"

A DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check convinces her to agree to a departure timeline. The DC drops to 12 if the party acknowledges the genuine cost of the request — engages with what they are taking from her rather than presenting it as a straightforward compliance issue. She is not asking for sympathy. She is asking to be seen clearly. Characters who do that earn her respect; it changes the tone from obligation to accord.

### The Complication

One of the five doppelgangers is in the taproom. He has been monitoring the conversation from across the room and approaches the table once the negotiation finds its footing. He does not want to leave Waterdeep. His objections are smooth and layered in principled language — the Enclave has no jurisdiction over neutral ground; Bonnie has not formally agreed to anything; this is being decided without the crew's input.

A DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check cuts through the framing. His objections are self-interested. He is not arguing for anyone else's right to stay. He is arguing for his own, using the language of collective principle as cover.

If the party names this distinction directly to Bonnie — says it out loud rather than just noting it — she goes still. She looks at him for a long moment. Then she looks at the party.

> > "He's the one who sold your names."

She has been sitting on this for some time. The timing is right now. He leaves when she tells him to. He knows better than to push back.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### If the Party Handles It Wrong
> If the party aligns with the traitor's objections, or tries to compel a group departure without engaging Bonnie's position, the negotiation hardens. The Persuasion DC rises to 18. Bonnie eventually agrees — the Enclave's request is reasonable and she knows it — but the name of the Splinter contact is not offered. She honors a deal; she does not reward a poor negotiation with a gift.

### The Agreement

Bonnie asks for two tendays to arrange departure for her crew. She will honor this. There is no deception at this stage — she is settling an account and creating no new problems.

As a parting courtesy, she names the Manshoon Splinter operative who bought the intelligence from her traitor. The name is **Kelso Fiddlewick** — a Field Ward gang leader with connections the party may already know from Order of the Gauntlet work. Melannor passes the name to both the Harpers and the Lords' Alliance independently.

> > "Consider it a receipt," she says. "For being straightforward about it."

### Concluding the Event

> **[GM]**
>
> **+1 Renown if** Bonnie agrees to a departure timeline and honors it without violence.
> **+1 Renown if** the traitor is identified and the Splinter contact named — whether through the party's work, Bonnie's reveal, or Harper M3's resolution carried forward.
> **+1 Renown if** the party allows Bonnie to handle the traitor herself rather than imposing a resolution. She does it cleanly and it costs the party nothing.

## Overview

Five doppelgangers are operating freely out of the Yawning Portal. One is selling intelligence to the Manshoon Splinter. Bonnie already knows.

## Read Aloud

> A falcon arrives at Trollskull Manor — Jeryth's choice for urgent work. The briefing: "Doppelgangers are operating out of the Yawning Portal. Shapeshifters of no fixed loyalty, moving freely through the city while four factions compete for the same ground. The Enclave cannot permit it. They need to leave Waterdeep. Peacefully, if possible."
>
> A postscript, in Melannor's own voice rather than the bird's flat delivery: "Jeryth said 'peacefully if possible' three times. I'm including that in case it's useful."

## Summary

The party negotiated with Bonnie, leader of a doppelganger crew operating from the Yawning Portal. She agreed to a two-tenday departure timeline and, as a parting courtesy, named Kelso Fiddlewick as the Manshoon Splinter operative who had been buying intelligence from a traitor in her crew.
