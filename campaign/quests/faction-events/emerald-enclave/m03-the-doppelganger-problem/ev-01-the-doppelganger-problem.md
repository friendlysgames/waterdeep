# The Doppelganger Problem

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when the falcon briefing arrives at Trollskull Manor. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Melannor's falcon briefing and travel to the Yawning Portal
> - Be signaled to a corner table by Bonnie before they can approach the bar
> - Negotiate a departure timeline with Bonnie, whose terms are reasonable and whose position is already decided
> - Identify the traitor in the crew through a DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check and name the distinction to Bonnie
> - Allow Bonnie to handle the traitor herself, earning her parting gift: the name Kelso Fiddlewick
> - Receive the dove's confirmation of departure three days later
>
> #### Background
>
> If the party marked **Harper M3 Complete** — if the Harper mission **The Doppelganger Auditions** has already run — Bonnie has already dealt with the traitor internally. She acknowledges this if the party raises it. The traitor is not present in the taproom. The Complication phase below does not occur; the negotiation is cleaner and faster. The name of the Splinter contact is still offered, as before.
>
> If **Harper M3 Complete** has not been marked, this mission is the initial discovery. The traitor is present and the reveal happens here.

### The Brief

A falcon arrives at Trollskull Manor. Jeryth chooses the falcon for urgent work.

> [!readaloud]
> A falcon lands on the windowsill at Trollskull Manor and delivers the briefing in Melannor's flat baritone.
>
> > "Doppelgangers are operating out of the Yawning Portal. Shapeshifters of no fixed loyalty, moving freely through the city while four factions compete for the same ground. The Enclave cannot permit it. They need to leave Waterdeep. Peacefully, if possible."
>
> A pause, and then Melannor adds a postscript in his own voice rather than the falcon's flat delivery.
>
> > "Jeryth said 'peacefully if possible' three times. I am including that in case it is useful."
>
> The falcon departs.

### The Yawning Portal — Evening

The taproom at capacity. Eight factions in thirteen conversations, none of them with each other. **Bonnie** is behind the bar in a form the party may recognize if they frequent the Portal, or in a forgettable service-staff form if they do not. She sees them the moment they enter.

> [!readaloud]
> The Yawning Portal in the evening is busy in the way of a room where too many kinds of business are conducted in too little space, voices low and chairs angled away from each other. Whatever form Bonnie is wearing tonight, she is behind the bar, and she sees you the moment you come through the door.
>
> Before you can cross to the bar, she catches your eye and tips her head toward a corner table that has stayed empty all evening. She arrives there two minutes after you do, carrying a cloth and wiping the table down as if that were the reason.
>
> > "I know what you're here for. And I know why the Enclave wants it. It's reasonable." She looks at the table. "Waterdeep is the most interesting city I have lived in. I've lived in eleven. I've been here eight months and I have not had to be anyone I didn't choose to be." She looks up. "How long do I have?"

> [!social]**The Shape in the Room**
>
> Bonnie (Neutral, Doppelganger, she/her for this form) :: a doppelganger crew leader who has already decided what she will accept and is waiting for the party to get there.
>
> Bonnie in private is economical and practical, without warmth but with an easy, dry humour about her own situation. She has anticipated this conversation. She has already decided that the Enclave's request is reasonable and that she will agree to it. What she has not decided is how much she will give beyond agreement, and that depends on how the party handles the next few minutes.
>
> She is protective of her crew and asks for clarity about the cost before accepting any terms. She is not asking for sympathy. She is asking to be seen clearly.
>
> A DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check convinces her to agree to a departure timeline. The DC drops to 12 if the party engages honestly with what they are taking from her — acknowledges the genuine cost of the request rather than presenting it as a straightforward compliance issue.
>
> Any character who succeeds on a DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check detects that Bonnie has already made up her mind about the departure, and that her hesitation is about something else.
>
> Conversation topics Bonnie is willing to discuss include:
> - The terms of the departure and how long she needs
> - The cost of what the party is asking, if the party is willing to name it
> - What she can offer in exchange for a fair arrangement
>
> What she will not discuss: what she is, in the taproom's main room.

> [!qna]**Why do you need to leave?**
>
> She sets the cloth on the table and looks at the party.
>
> > "Because five shapeshifters reading every conversation in the city's most contested tavern while four factions are making decisions that will matter for a decade is exactly the kind of thing your organization cannot permit. I know that. I am not arguing the point."

> [!qna]**How long do you need?**
>
> > "Two tendays. I need to close some arrangements and make sure my people have somewhere to go. Two tendays and we are not your problem." She says it flat. "I do not skip agreements."

> [!qna]**Is there anything you can offer?**
>
> > "Depends on how this goes. I have information worth having, if the conversation goes well." She does not elaborate yet.

### The Complication

One of the five doppelgangers is in the taproom. He has been watching the conversation from across the room and approaches the table once the negotiation finds its footing.

> [!readaloud]
> A figure approaches the corner table — one of the Portal's staff, in a face the party may have seen before — and takes a position near Bonnie without being invited. He speaks without sitting down.
>
> > "The Enclave has no jurisdiction over neutral ground. Bonnie hasn't agreed to anything formally. And this is being decided without the crew's input."
>
> Bonnie does not look at him.

> [!gamemaster]**The Traitor's Position**
>
> The doppelganger's objections are self-interested. He does not want to leave Waterdeep. He is using the language of collective principle as cover for his own situation, and he has reasons to stay that he cannot name in front of Bonnie. A DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check cuts through the framing. His objections are not principled. He is arguing for his own right to stay, using the rest of the crew as justification.

> [!exploration]**Reading the Traitor**
>
> Any character who watches the exchange and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check distinguishes self-interest from principle.
>
> > His objections arrive as arguments for the crew's autonomy, but his eyes stay on Bonnie, not on the party. He is not asking for anyone else's right to stay. He is asking for his own, and using collective principle as the frame because it is harder to refuse.

If the party names this distinction directly to Bonnie — says it out loud rather than just noting it — she goes still. She looks at him for a long moment. Then she looks at the party.

> [!readaloud]
> Bonnie goes still. She looks at him across the table with the patient expression of someone who has been waiting for the right moment to spend something she has been holding. After a long beat, she looks back at the party.
>
> > "He's the one who sold your names."
>
> He leaves when she tells him to. He knows better than to push back.

> [!gamemaster]**If the Party Handles It Wrong**
>
> If the party aligns with the traitor's objections, or tries to compel a group departure without engaging Bonnie's position, the negotiation hardens. The Persuasion DC rises to 18. Bonnie eventually agrees — the Enclave's request is reasonable and she knows it — but the name of the Splinter contact is not offered. She honors a deal. She does not reward a poor negotiation with a gift.

### The Agreement

Bonnie asks for two tendays to arrange departure for her crew. She will honor this.

> [!readaloud]
> Bonnie folds the cloth and sets it on the seat beside her.
>
> > "Two tendays. I'll honor it." She pauses for a moment. "As a parting courtesy — the name of the Splinter operative who bought from him. Kelso Fiddlewick. Field Ward. He has connections you may already know." She stands and picks up the cloth. "Consider it a receipt. For being straightforward about it."

### Concluding the Event

The doppelganger crew departs within two tendays. Three days after Bonnie leaves, a dove arrives at Trollskull Manor carrying a folded note with three dots in a triangle — a doppelganger notation mark, her name essentially.

> [!readaloud]
> Three days after Bonnie's departure, a dove arrives at Trollskull Manor's window carrying a folded note. Inside: three dots arranged in a triangle, nothing else.
>
> When Melannor is shown it: "That is a doppelganger notation mark. Her name, essentially. She is confirming she left." A pause. "It is, in its way, a receipt."

## Overview

Five doppelgangers are operating out of the Yawning Portal. One has been selling intelligence to the Manshoon Splinter. Bonnie already knows, and she is deciding when to spend it.

## Summary

The party negotiated with Bonnie, the leader of a doppelganger crew operating from the Yawning Portal. She agreed to a two-tenday departure timeline. As a parting courtesy, she named Kelso Fiddlewick as the Manshoon Splinter operative who had been buying intelligence from the traitor in her crew.
