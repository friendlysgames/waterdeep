# A Seat at Phaulkonmere

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Emerald Enclave member first reaches Renown 3, which usually happens at the debrief of **The Undercliff Scarecrows**. It plays out only for Emerald Enclave members. It begins at dusk on the day of that debrief, when a white cat finds the member, and it ends when the member leaves the garden by the east gate. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Hear a white cat carry Melannor Fellbranch's call to the east gate of Phaulkonmere.
> - Take a key to the east gate from Melannor and learn how it works.
> - Walk to the oldest oak and hear Jeryth welcome the member to the garden.
> - Put a few questions to Melannor and Jeryth about the key and the garden.
>
> Only Emerald Enclave members are called. Companions who are not members can walk to the east gate and wait on the benches inside it, and they gain nothing.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jeryth has watched the member's work through the roots and the groundwater. She wanted to see whether her choice at the First Meeting was sound, and tonight is her answer.
> - Melannor made the key on the night after the First Meeting and has kept it on his workbench since. He was waiting until the member had done work for the Enclave.
> - The welcome has no price. Neither of them asks for a report, a mission or a favour tonight, and the key obliges the member to nothing later.
> - Nobody in this Event names the Stone of Golorr, the vault or the master of the Splinter. Kolat Towers stands one block north of the garden. Neither Melannor nor Jeryth points that way or says what the building is before **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener.
> - Jeryth has felt a dreaming presence below the city for months and does not know its name. If she is asked, she says "Something dreams below." and nothing more.

### The Cat Again

At the end of the debrief, Melannor casts *Animal Messenger* on the white cat that carried the First Meeting invitation, naming Trollskull Alley, which he has visited. The cat is the **Cat** stat block, a Tiny Beast of CR 0. It finds its recipient by description, speaks the message once in Melannor's low baritone from somewhere behind its eyes, and leaves. If several members reached Renown 3 at the debrief, one cat comes and the message is for all of them. Change the first sentence of the readaloud to match what the member is doing at the manor, then read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Lamps are being lit along Trollskull Alley. A white cat sits on the courtyard wall of the manor, beneath the oak, with its paws folded under it. It watches you cross the yard and does not move until you stop in front of it. Then its mouth opens, and a low, even man's voice speaks from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > "You have done the work. Come to the east gate when you are ready."
>
> The cat waits a moment for an answer, then drops from the wall and trots away between the barrels.

> [!gamemaster]**The Message Count**
>
> The message is 14 words, well inside the 25-word limit of *Animal Messenger*. It asks for no reply, and the cat carries none. Anyone in the courtyard hears it, so a companion who is present knows where the member is going. If a character catches or kills the cat, it finishes the message first, because the spell holds it to that.

The spell lasts 24 hours, and the cat waits at the manor until it ends. A member who does not hear the cat in that time gets the key from Melannor at the east gate on their next visit, and this Event is played then. **Summerstrider** fires at noon the next day whether or not the member has come. If the member has not yet taken the key, play this Event at the east gate first.

### The East Gate

The east gate lies in the garden wall, in a lane behind the estate, and the walk from Trollskull Alley takes about two hours. The member can go at once or at any time before the pigeon arrives at noon, and Melannor is at the gate whenever they come. Phaulkonmere's four constants hold as the First Meeting set them. The gate stands open before the member arrives, the garden is older than the street around it, Jeryth's voice has no source, and the oldest oak is the point of reference. Read the following aloud when the member reaches the gate:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The lane along the east wall of Phaulkonmere is narrow, and the only light comes from a lantern hanging on a hook beside the gate. A small iron gate stands open in the stone with ivy trailing over its frame. Beyond it the garden is thick with old trees, much older than the cobbles of the lane. The crown of the oldest oak rises above the villa roof. Melannor waits just inside the gate with his hands folded in front of him.
>
> > "Good evening, Name, and thank you for coming. I have something for you, and I would like to give it to you at this gate."

Use the member's name, and change "evening" to match the hour. Companions stop at the benches inside the gate, where Melannor does not speak to them and Jeryth does not address them.

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper at the Gate**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the friendly but humourless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, who made a key to solve a practical problem and wants no fuss made about it.
>
> Melannor is Friendly toward the member. He made the key because the main gate closes at dusk, and he says nothing more sentimental than that. A member who thanks him gets one nod, and he looks at the ivy along the wall until the moment passes. He speaks in full sentences without contractions, and he does not swear or laugh.
>
> Melannor is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Why he made the key, and why he is giving it now.
> - How the key works, who may use it and what happens if it is lost.
> - Whether the garden keeps off the Watch and the people who follow the member.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, what the vault holds, the dreaming thing's name, the master of the other cell or the house one block north of the garden. He turns each aside with "That is not mine to speak of." If a player names Manshoon, he answers as he did at the First Meeting.

Melannor gives the key when the member has greeted him. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor takes a plain iron key on a loop of green cord from inside his coat and holds it out on his open palm. The bow is worn smooth, and the teeth are cut in the shape of small leaves.
>
> > "This is the east gate. It opens from either side, so you do not need to knock or announce yourselves."
>
> He looks at the ivy along the wall while you take it.

> [!exploration]**The Leaf-Cut Teeth**
>
> Any character who examines the key and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Nature)** check sees that the leaves are oak, and that the iron has no rust although it has sat in a damp workshop for some time. A character who fails still holds a working key, and nothing depends on the result.

> [!qna]**Why a key?**
>
> > "The main gate closes at dusk, and I do not want you standing in the lane after dark. This key opens the east gate at any hour."

> [!qna]**Why now?**
>
> > "I made the key on the night after you first came. I was waiting until you had done work for the Enclave, and now you have."

> [!qna]**Can I lend it?**
>
> > "No. The lock was made for you, and it will not open for anyone else. Friends who walk through with you may enter, up to eight of them."

> [!qna]**What if I lose it?**
>
> > "Come to the main gate by day and ask me, and I will make another. Nobody else can use the first one, so you need not worry about it."

> [!qna]**Are we safe here?**
>
> > "In the garden, yes. People who follow you may watch the lane, but they do not come through the gate. A Watch officer with a warrant will get a truthful answer from me."

> [!exploration]**The East Gate Key**
>
> The key belongs to the member it is given to, and each member holds a separate one. The following rules apply to each member separately.
>
> - **Contact:** Melannor, at the east gate. He gives one key to each member who is called, and companions get none.
> - **Place:** the east gate of Phaulkonmere and nowhere else. The key opens no other lock.
> - **Hours:** any hour, by day or by night. The key turns in the lock from either side. Without it, the gate is shut after dusk and nobody answers it.
> - **Notice:** none. The member sends no word ahead, because Jeryth knows who is at the gate. Melannor comes to the oak by morning if the member asks for him.
> - **Delay:** none. The key works from the moment Melannor hands it over.
> - **What it gives:** entry, and the haven set out in the Springwarden Benefits of the **Emerald Enclave First Meeting**. That means a Long Rest in the garden, food, and care for the member's animals, all free.
> - **What it does not give:** it is no refuge from a lawful warrant. The response team of any faction never enters Phaulkonmere and never fights inside its walls, and it may watch the lane and follow a member who leaves.
> - **Who else:** up to eight companions or friends who pass through the gate with the member. A companion who arrives alone is turned away politely.
> - **Lending:** the key is personal. In any hand but the member's it opens nothing, and the member still has the use of it.
> - **Loss:** a lost or stolen key opens nothing for anyone else. Melannor makes a new one at the main gate by day.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who leads an enemy through the gate on purpose, or draws steel inside the walls, loses the key's use for two tendays. Melannor tells the member at the main gate.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 1 keeps the key, and it does not open the gate until the Renown is restored.

### Jeryth's Welcome

Melannor walks the member along the gravel path to the oldest oak. Jeryth does not speak on the way. She speaks when the member reaches the roots, and she says one or two short sentences with a long silence between them. She does not repeat herself. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor walks ahead without hurrying, and the paths of the garden bend toward the oldest oak in the middle of it. The air is still among its roots, which lift the paving stones into low humps. When you reach the trunk, every leaf above you turns at once, and a woman's voice settles into the air ahead of you.
>
> > "I know what you did."
>
> The garden stays silent for the length of a breath, and then the voice speaks again.
>
> > "This garden is yours now. Come when you need it. There are no conditions."
>
> The leaves settle, and a warm breeze crosses the path. Melannor steps back and folds his hands.

> [!social]**The Voice in the Oak**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, disembodied presence, she/her) :: the patron of Phaulkonmere, who has said what she meant to say and answers little more.
>
> Jeryth is Friendly toward the member, and she shows it through the garden. Flowers open along the path, the grass lies flat where the member walks, and the leaves move when she speaks. She does not say "well done" or "you are welcome", because either one would suggest the member owes her something. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest" and never swears.
>
> Jeryth is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - How she knew what the member did.
> - Whether the welcome has a price.
> - What dreams below the Castle Ward, which she answers in one sentence.
>
> She will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault, her family or how she came to be in the garden. She answers any question about the gold with "That question is for the Lords to settle."

> [!qna]**What did we do?**
>
> > "The roots carried it to me, and the water after them."

> [!qna]**Is there a price?**
>
> Every leaf on the oak goes still, and Jeryth does not answer the question twice.
>
> > "Rest here."

> [!qna]**What is below?**
>
> > "Something dreams below."

Jeryth says nothing more, and Melannor takes that as the end of the welcome. Read the following aloud when the member leaves:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor walks you back along the gravel to the east gate, where the lantern still burns on its hook. He stops just inside the threshold.
>
> > "The gate will open for you now, at any hour. I will send word by pigeon at noon tomorrow."
>
> He waits until you have stepped into the lane, and then he turns back toward the oak.

### Renown Opportunities

This Event awards no Renown. The member reached Renown 3 in **The Undercliff Scarecrows**, and the welcome pays nothing further.

### Aftermath

The key stays with the member, and the pigeon arrives at noon the next day for **Summerstrider**. That Event reads **East Gate Key Held** to decide the hours. A member with the key is met at the east gate at any hour, and a member without it is met at the main gate at dusk. A member who never took the key comes by the main gate, and the garden's night hours stay shut to them.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member holds the key, has heard Jeryth speak and has left through the east gate.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **East Gate Key Held** — mark with the member's name when Melannor hands over the key, and add the name of any member who takes it on a later visit; read by **Summerstrider**, which sets the hours of the rank meeting and the healing by it, and by the haven's hours in the **Emerald Enclave First Meeting**.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> **Summerstrider** fires at noon the next day for each member marked. **Ten Nights in the City of the Dead** becomes available when an individual Emerald Enclave member reaches Renown 3 and 3rd level. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

A white cat calls an Emerald Enclave member back to Phaulkonmere, where Melannor Fellbranch gives them a key to the east gate and Jeryth Phaulkon welcomes them to the garden.

## Summary

A white cat told us to come to the east gate of Phaulkonmere when we were ready. Melannor Fellbranch gave each of us a key and said that it would open the gate at any hour. Jeryth spoke from the oldest oak and told us the garden was ours, with no conditions. Melannor said that a pigeon would find us at noon.
