# A Seat at Phaulkonmere

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires after the party completes their first Emerald Enclave mission (any mission). In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the same white cat as the First Meeting, with a different message this time
> - Walk into Phaulkonmere and find the estate already at ease with them
> - Receive a key to the east gate from Melannor
> - Hear Jeryth speak from the garden, briefly, confirming the safe haven in her own terms
>
> #### Background
>
> Jeryth was aware of what the party did on the mission through the same network of roots, soil, and groundwater she uses to know everything that passes near Phaulkonmere. She watched with the interest of someone who needed to see whether the choice they made at the First Meeting was sound. It was. This visit is the answer to that question.
>
> Melannor has prepared one practical thing: a plain iron key to the east gate. It has sat on the groundskeeper's workbench since the night after the First Meeting. He was waiting for the party to earn it.

### The Invitation

The same white cat appears at Trollskull Manor's window.

> [!readaloud]
> The white cat from the First Meeting is sitting on the sill again, its tail curled around its feet. When someone opens the window it looks in at the room, and Melannor's voice comes out of it at the same unhurried pace as before.
>
> > "You've done the work. Come when you're ready."
>
> The cat drops down to the street before anyone can answer and walks off toward the North Ward. It named no hour and gave no task, and it did not seem to be in any hurry at all.

### Phaulkonmere in Welcome

The east gate stands open when the party arrives. Melannor is there.

> [!readaloud]
> The east gate is standing open when you arrive, and Melannor Fellbranch steps aside to hold it for you without a word of greeting. He falls in beside you as you walk into the garden.
>
> The estate carries on around you as if you had always been walking here. The birds in the old oaks go on with their business, and the hedges and the ivy stay still as you pass, with none of the watchfulness you felt the first time.

Melannor walks at the party's pace. Near the eastern wall, he stops and draws a plain iron key from his coat.

> [!readaloud]
> Near the ivy along the eastern wall he stops and takes a plain iron key out of his coat.
>
> > "East gate. Opens from either side. No need to knock or announce yourselves."
>
> He holds it out and looks at the ivy while the party takes it.

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: an Emerald Enclave druid who manages Phaulkonmere's grounds and does not say more than the situation requires.
>
> He has no other words for the key. To him, the key solves a practical problem. If a party member thanks him, he nods once and finds something nearby to look at. He will walk them to the oldest section of the garden as though that were where he was already heading.

> [!gamemaster]**Melannor's Manner**
>
> The key means: you belong here, come when you need to. He will not say that. His acknowledgment is the walk and the key itself.

### Jeryth's Welcome

Jeryth does not speak during the walk. She speaks when the party reaches the broadest oaks — the deepest part of the garden, where the light is thick even in mid-afternoon. Her voice comes from the path ahead of them.

> [!readaloud]
> As you reach the oldest oaks, Jeryth's voice comes from the path a few steps ahead of you, as though she has been standing there waiting for some time.
>
> > "I know what you did."
>
> The leaves overhead go still for a moment while she lets that settle, and then she goes on.
>
> > "This garden is yours now. Come when you need it. There are no conditions."

> [!social]**The Voice in the Garden**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Disembodied Presence, she/her) :: the spirit of Phaulkonmere, speaking directly to the party.
>
> She has chosen these words precisely rather than "well done" or "you're welcome" because those phrases carry implication she does not intend. She means what she said: the garden is theirs, at any hour, for any need, with no debt created. She will not repeat it. If a party member asks a follow-up question, she answers only if the question has a meaningful answer. Silence is not unkindness.

### Concluding the Event

This event sets no outcomes.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Phaulkonmere is now confirmed as a safe haven in practical terms. Any party member with the east gate key may enter at any hour without announcement. No faction will openly violate the estate: it is neutral ground under a Chosen of Mielikki's protection. If the campaign enters a period of intense faction pressure, the party has a named fallback with no strings.
>
> The **Water Table Stirs** Event begins from **Fireball!** onward, as the Stone of Golorr's activation strengthens the psychic resonance Jeryth has been managing below Waterdeep. This event does not award a Milestone Point.

## Overview

After completing their first Emerald Enclave mission, the party returns to Phaulkonmere and finds themselves welcomed as allies — with a key to the east gate and a few words from Jeryth herself.

## Summary

We completed our first Emerald Enclave mission and returned to Phaulkonmere as people who belong there. Melannor gave us the key to the east gate — we can come at any hour, no notice needed. Jeryth spoke from the garden. The safe haven the Enclave offered from the start is real now.
