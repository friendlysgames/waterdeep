# A Seat at Phaulkonmere

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires after the party completes their first Emerald Enclave mission (any mission). In this Event:
>
> - Melannor's invitation arrives by cat — the same method as the First Meeting, but different in tone.
> - The party arrives at Phaulkonmere and finds the estate already at ease with them.
> - Melannor confirms the safe haven practically, with a key to the east gate.
> - Jeryth speaks from the garden — brief, exact, nothing withheld and nothing elaborated.
> - No renown is awarded. No new mechanical benefit is introduced. The Springwarden safe haven is confirmed as lived reality rather than stated offer.

**Background (DM only)**
Jeryth was aware of what the party did on the mission through the same network of roots, soil, and groundwater she uses to know everything that passes near Phaulkonmere. She did not watch with investment. She watched with the interest of someone who needed to see whether the choice they made at the First Meeting was sound. It was. This visit is the answer to that question.

Melannor has prepared one practical thing: a plain iron key to the east gate. It has sat on the groundskeeper's workbench since the night after the First Meeting. He was waiting for the party to earn it.

### The Invitation

The same white cat appears at Trollskull Manor's window. It speaks in Melannor's voice, unhurried:

> > "You've done the work. Come when you're ready."

It drops to the street before the party can respond. No hour, no assignment, no urgency — and the absence of urgency is deliberate.

### Phaulkonmere in Welcome

The east gate stands open when the party arrives. Melannor is there, not waiting precisely, but present — the way a person ends up near the door when someone they expect is due. He steps aside to hold it, says nothing, and walks with them into the garden.

The estate moves around them without theater. Birds in the estate oaks go on with their business. The plants are simply present, untroubled. Phaulkonmere knows who is walking through it.

Melannor walks at the party's pace. No tour — he covered the grounds at the First Meeting. Near the eastern wall, he stops and draws a plain iron key from his coat.

> > "East gate. Opens from either side. No need to knock or announce yourselves."

He holds it out. He looks at the ivy along the eastern wall while the party takes it.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Melannor's Manner
> He has no other words for the key. He is not being sentimental; he is solving a logistics problem. The key means: you belong here, come when you need to. He will not say that. If a party member thanks him, he nods once and finds something to look at nearby. He will walk them to the oldest section of the garden and stop there, as though this were where he was already heading.

### Jeryth's Welcome

Jeryth does not speak during the walk. She speaks when the party reaches the broadest oaks — the deepest part of the garden, where the light is thick even in mid-afternoon. Her voice settles into the air ahead of them, not behind.

> > "I know what you did."

A pause. The garden is quiet.

> > "This garden is yours now. Come when you need it. There are no conditions."

She falls silent. The garden continues as it was.

> [!profile]**Jeryth's Manner Here**
>
> No ceremony, no warmth — only precision. She has chosen these specific words rather than "well done" or "you're welcome" because those phrases carry implication she does not intend. She means what she said: the garden is theirs, at any hour, for any need, with no debt created. She will not repeat it. If a party member asks a follow-up question, she answers only if the question has a meaningful answer. Silence is not unkindness.

## Overview

After completing their first Emerald Enclave mission, the party returns to Phaulkonmere and finds themselves welcomed as allies — with a key to the east gate and a few words from Jeryth that carry more weight than ceremony would.

We completed our first Emerald Enclave mission and returned to Phaulkonmere as people who belong there. Melannor gave us the key to the east gate — we can come at any hour, no notice needed. Jeryth spoke from the garden. The safe haven the Enclave offered from the start is real now.
