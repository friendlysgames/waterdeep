# Design Notes: A Seat at Phaulkonmere

## What the Source Gave

The restored draft gave three things. The first was a white cat that says "You've done the work. Come when you're ready.", and the second was a plain iron key to the east gate with Melannor's line about it. The third was Jeryth's two lines, "I know what you did." and "This garden is yours now. Come when you need it. There are no conditions." It awarded no Renown and set no flags, and all three beats stay in this version. Jeryth's lines are verbatim, while Melannor's line is rewritten as full sentences because his voice profile allows no contractions.

## What Changed

The restored draft fired for the party after any first Emerald Enclave mission. It now fires for each member who first reaches Renown 3, at dusk on the day of the debrief for **The Undercliff Scarecrows**. **Summerstrider** follows at noon the next day. The cat's message is 14 words, counted, and it names the east gate so that a member who has never used it knows where to go. The cat is the First Meeting's cat, so the event points back to that description instead of writing a new one.

The key was a prop in the restored draft. It is now a procedure with a contact, a place, hours, a notice rule, a limit on friends, a lending rule, a loss rule and a Renown-loss rule, in the shape of the Springwarden haven it extends. The key is personal and never lent, and it opens only for the member it was made for. A replacement is made at the main gate by day. **East Gate Key Held** is marked per member and read by **Summerstrider**, so the hours of that meeting no longer depend on a sentence in a Next Steps block.

## Departures from the Source

The restored Next Steps said no faction would openly violate the estate, and that is now the response-team line in the key procedure, which matches the First Meeting. The restored Next Steps also tied **The Water Table Stirs** to this event, and that link is cut because the surges have their own triggers. Phaulkonmere's four constants are written once, in the arrival readaloud, and nobody points at the house one block north.

  - the oak-leaf teeth and green cord of the key;
  - the benches inside the gate and the cat on the courtyard wall under the oak;
  - the 24-hour fallback, which follows the spell's duration.
- The eight-friend limit and the two-tenday loss rule are copied from the First Meeting.
