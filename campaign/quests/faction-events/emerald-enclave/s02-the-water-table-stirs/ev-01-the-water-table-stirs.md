# The Water Table Stirs

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Information Event fires five times, once each time the Stone of Golorr grows stronger, and it plays out only for Emerald Enclave members. Each surge brings one sign in the city, one request from Jeryth Phaulkon or Melannor Fellbranch, and sometimes a Renown award. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - **Surge One:** watch the starlings circle the harbor after **Fireball!** and give Melannor an account of aberrant creatures.
> - **Surge Two:** see the oak roots move once the Stone is attuned, and hear the request to keep it moving.
> - **Surge Three:** hear Jeryth describe the dreaming thing after the first Eye is restored, and report what the first heist met. A second path follows her awakened rat to a hidden staircase.
> - **Surge Four:** press a ward-seal into a cellar wall after the second Eye is restored, and hear Jeryth ask for **The Fouled Channel**.
> - **Surge Five:** hear Jeryth's voice begin to fail after the third Eye is restored, and learn what the oak in the Trollskull Manor courtyard will show.
>
> Only individual Emerald Enclave members receive the pigeon, meet Melannor and Jeryth, and earn Renown. Companions wait at the gate or in the outer garden, hear the members' account afterward and gain nothing.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Illuun, an aboleth dreaming on Undermountain Level 4, answers the Stone of Golorr, which holds an aboleth's memory. Each time the Stone grows stronger, Illuun presses harder toward the surface through the Castle Ward water table.
> - Jeryth has held it back for months. She does not know its name and says only that something dreams below. In Surge Three she calls it old, patient and hungry, and only this Event has her say "hungry".
> - Nobody in this Event names Illuun. A member learns the name through **Illuun Contact**, through **Anchor Destroyed**, or from the Stone if it named Illuun at Full Awakening.
> - The surges cause no harm. The dreams, the fish and the black water are the dreamer feeling for the Stone.
> - Jeryth's request in Surge Four is sound. The channel she uses is fouled by the Splinter's thrall-conditioning waste, which **The Fouled Channel** traces. The cutting that mission plants opens the conduit that **The Dreamer's Reach** follows, and Jeryth cannot foresee that.
> - Melannor and Jeryth say "the Splinter". Nobody names Manshoon, and nobody points at the house one block north of the garden.
> - Jeryth's full silence is the opening of **The Dreamer's Reach**, and it waits for that mission's gate.

### How the Surges Fire

Each surge answers one trigger, and the triggers arrive in whatever order the campaign allows. Count the Eyes restored and never which lair heist restored them.

> [!gamemaster]**The Five Surges**
>
> | Surge | Fires | The sign in the city | The request | Renown |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | One | Dawn on Ches 25, after three evenings of starlings | Starlings circle the harbor | An account of aberrant creatures | +1 |
> | Two | Dawn after one character attunes to the Stone | Oak roots move two inches | Keep the Stone moving | None |
> | Three | Dawn after the first Eye is restored | Seven cistern workers share one dream | Report the first heist, or follow the rat | +1 |
> | Four | Dawn after the second Eye is restored | Fish beach on the south quay | Close a crack, then take **The Fouled Channel** | +1 |
> | Five | Dawn after the third Eye is restored | Wellwater runs black in the Castle Ward | Watch the courtyard oak | None |

Melannor delivers every surge by grey pigeon with his *Animal Messenger* spell. The pigeon is a Tiny Beast of CR 0 and uses the Raven stat block. It finds a member by description, speaks once in his low baritone from somewhere behind its eyes, and flies off. It carries no reply, and anyone nearby hears the message. Every message in this Event is 25 words or fewer, and the count sits under each one. Change the first sentence of each pigeon readaloud to match where the member is.

- If two triggers fall on the same day, play the earlier surge at dawn and the later surge at the next dawn.
- If several members are together, one pigeon speaks for all of them.
- If a member is away or joins late, Melannor tells them each missed surge in order at their first visit, with one sentence for each sign, and their Renown lines stay open.

### The Garden Gate

Every meeting in this Event takes place at Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward, about two hours' walk from Trollskull Alley. Read the following aloud at a member's first arrival, with the member's name and the right hour in the greeting. Give later arrivals a single sentence in its place, and have companions wait at the gate in the outer garden.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open before you reach it, and nobody is near to have opened it. The garden inside is older than the street around it, with moss on the walls and roots lifting the paving stones. Every path bends toward the oldest oak, where Melannor Fellbranch waits with his hands folded. The leaves above him turn toward you as you come up the path.
>
> > "Good morning, Name, and thank you for coming so promptly. I will tell you why Jeryth and I sent for you."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper at the Oak**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the humourless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, who reports bad news in the same level baritone as good.
>
> Melannor speaks in plain, complete sentences at an even pace. He uses no contractions, does not recognise a joke and never swears. He calls each member by name, correctly, every time, and he is worried about Jeryth without ever saying so.
>
> Melannor is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The disturbance Jeryth feels in the groundwater beneath the Castle Ward.
> - The sign of the day, and what the roots and the animals are doing.
> - What Jeryth asks of the member, and what would count as done.
>
> He will not discuss what the Stone holds, the vault, the Splinter's master or the house one block north of the garden. He turns each aside with "That is not mine to speak of."

### Surge One: The First Stirring

This surge fires for every Emerald Enclave member at dawn on Ches 25, wherever the member is staying. Melannor passed on Jeryth's first report in **Fireball!**, when she felt a sharp disturbance in the Castle Ward on the night of the fireball. This surge continues that report, and no second visit is staged to repeat it. Harbor fisherfolk call the starlings an ill sign, and the flocks have circled for three evenings.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Dawn light reaches the rooftops over Trollskull Alley. Beyond them a flock of starlings circles the harbor in a tight ring that folds inward instead of scattering. A grey pigeon lands on the courtyard wall of the manor and turns its head to look at each of you. It stops in front of one of you, and a low, even man's voice speaks from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > "Jeryth's disturbance has not faded since the fireball. Bring me any account of aberrant creatures from the sewers or Gralhund Villa."
>
> The pigeon waits for an answer that does not come, then flaps over the wall and away toward the south.

The message is 21 words, which fits inside the 25 words that *Animal Messenger* allows.

The member can give the account on any visit to the oak, and the visit needs no other errand. Read **The Garden Gate** on the first arrival, then run the report.

> [!qna]**What is the disturbance?**
>
> > "It is a pressure in the groundwater below the Castle Ward, and Jeryth has held it back for months. After the fireball she felt it turn toward the city."

> [!qna]**What do you need from us?**
>
> > "I need any account of a creature that was neither a beast nor a person. Tell me where you met it, how it moved, and what it did to the animals near it."

> [!exploration]**What Counts as a Report**
>
> Melannor accepts any account that names a place, a creature and a time, and the member tells it in their own words without a check. Each route below is enough alone, and it can be told by a member who was there or one who heard it from someone who was.
>
> - **Finding Floon:** the gazer at the tunnel junction in the Xanathar sewer hideout. The mind flayer who set an intellect devourer down in the room where Floon was held also counts.
> - **Gralhund Villa:** the Guild's gazers near the villa, or anything the party saw of the mind flayer's movements. Melannor also asked about these in his debrief after the villa.
> - **The sewers:** any aberrant creature met below the Dock Ward during the investigations.
> - **+1 Renown:** the member gives Melannor an account by any route above, and the award is made once per member.
>
> A member with no such account has nothing to report and earns nothing. Melannor writes the place on the map in his shed and says Jeryth will want to hear it.

### Surge Two: The Stone Speaks

This surge fires at dawn on the day after one character attunes to the Stone, which **Faction Outposts** begins around Ches 25. The roots of the oldest oaks in the eastern garden have moved two inches toward the Castle Ward overnight. Melannor has stakes in the ground to show it, and Jeryth does not speak in this surge.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A grey pigeon is waiting on the sill of the manor window when you open the shutters. It looks you over from head to tail before it speaks. The voice that comes from somewhere behind its eyes is the low baritone you heard before.
>
> > "Come to the garden when you can. The oaks have moved, and Jeryth has a request."
>
> It flaps off the sill and over the roofs without waiting.

The message is 16 words, which fits inside the 25 words that *Animal Messenger* allows.

Melannor is in the eastern garden and not at the oak. Read **The Garden Gate** first if this is the member's first arrival, then read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor kneels among the oldest oaks of the eastern garden, where a row of thin stakes runs across the ground from the trunks. The roots have pushed past the last stake, and a band of fresh earth marks their new edge. He stands and wipes his hands when he sees you.
>
> > "I set these stakes at the edge of the roots a tenday ago. The roots have since moved two inches toward the Castle Ward, and they moved overnight."
>
> He presses the nearest stake back into the soil.
>
> > "Jeryth asks that you keep the Stone moving from this day forward. It should not lie in one place for more than a tenday at a time."

> [!qna]**Why must the Stone move?**
>
> > "Jeryth says that what dreams below finds the Stone more easily when it rests. A Stone that travels is much harder for it to find, and she did not say more than that."

> [!exploration]**Keeping the Stone Moving**
>
> The request is met when the Stone sleeps under a different roof at least once in every tenday. The count runs from the night it was attuned until the third Eye is restored. A night at the Yawning Portal, at Trollskull Manor or in a rented room each counts, and no check is needed.
>
> This surge awards no Renown. Record only whether any tenday passed with the Stone under one roof every night, because Surge Five reads it.

### Surge Three: Eyes Opening

This surge fires at dawn after the first Eye is restored, whichever lair heist restored it. In the tenday before, seven cistern workers across the Castle Ward told the Watch the same dream, of drowning upward through wet stone while a vast eye looked down. None of them knows the others, and the Watch filed the reports as fever and moved on, though Melannor's harbor network flagged them. A character who asks at a Castle Ward cistern house hears the dream from the workers, with no check.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A grey pigeon walks along the bar at Trollskull Manor between the empty mugs, and the few patrons in the taproom stop talking to watch it. It stops in front of one of you and opens its beak. The voice that speaks from somewhere behind its eyes is low and even.
>
> > "Seven cistern workers dreamed of drowning upward through stone. Jeryth will speak with you in the garden today."
>
> The pigeon hops to the windowsill and flies out over the alley.

The message is 18 words, which fits inside the 25 words that *Animal Messenger* allows.

Read **The Garden Gate** first if this is the member's first arrival, then read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor waits beside the oldest oak, and every leaf above him is turned toward you. A calm woman's voice comes from the leaves and the gravel together, with no one near to speak it.
>
> > "Something dreams below. It felt the first Eye open."
>
> The leaves stay turned for several breaths, and then the voice speaks again.
>
> > "It is old, patient and hungry."

> [!social]**The Voice in the Oak**
>
> Jeryth Phaulkon (Neutral Good, Chosen of Mielikki, disembodied presence, she/her) :: the patron of Phaulkonmere. She speaks only to those she has reason to address, and she answers in one or two sentences.
>
> Jeryth leaves long silences between her answers, and the garden answers with her. Leaves turn, and a cool wind crosses the path when she is troubled. She calls Mielikki "the Lady of the Forest", never swears and never repeats herself.
>
> Jeryth is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Whether the dreaming thing is dangerous.
> - Whether it knows the party.
> - What she can do against it.
>
> She will not discuss the vault, the Splinter or any faction's quarrel. She answers any question about the gold with "That question is for the Lords to settle."

> [!qna]**Is it dangerous?**
>
> > "It can harm. It has not tried yet."

> [!qna]**Does it know who we are?**
>
> > "It knows the Stone. It does not know you."

> [!qna]**Can you stop it?**
>
> > "I hold it back. That is all I can do now."

Jeryth then makes her request to the member in two separate turns.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The garden is silent for a long moment, and the cool wind crosses the path once. The voice comes from the oak a final time.
>
> > "Tell Melannor what you met below."
>
> A single leaf drops from the lowest bough and lands on the gravel at your feet.
>
> > "Then tell him whatever you meet next, before you follow it."

> [!exploration]**Reporting the First Heist**
>
> Melannor listens to a member's account of the first lair heist and writes it on a slate. The account counts when it names one creature or person from that heist who obeyed another mind's orders, whether implanted, charmed or dominated. If the heist met no such creature, the member can instead report strange behaviour of animals, water or sleepers seen during it.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the member gives Melannor the account, and the award is made once per member.

If **Xanathar's Lair** has not yet run, Jeryth offers a second path after the request. If it has run, she offers nothing more and the surge ends here.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The voice speaks once more, and a grey rat climbs out from between the roots of the oak. It sits up on the gravel in front of you, washes its whiskers and looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "My rats found the stair under the Castle Ward. One will show you."
>
> The rat speaks in a small, dry voice.
>
> > "Walk slowly, please. I am not fast on the stones."

> [!exploration]**The Rat's Way**
>
> The rat is a Tiny Beast that uses the Rat stat block with Intelligence 10, and it speaks Common, as in *Dragon Heist*. It leads the party from a drain in the kitchen yard through the Castle Ward sewers, and the walk takes about two hours. It knows every turning, so the party needs no check to follow it.
>
> - **Where the stair goes:** it descends from a secret staircase in the Castle Ward sewers to the entrance of the beholder's lair, area X1 in *Dragon Heist*. **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) keys the rooms below.
> - **What the rat gives:** the route and nothing else. It cannot fight, it stops at the top of the stair, and it returns to Phaulkonmere by itself.
> - **If the party is not ready:** the rat remembers the way, and a member can ask Jeryth for it again after the party has planned its approach.

### Surge Four: The Path Found

This surge fires at dawn after the second Eye is restored. Fish in three wards have been schooling in tight patterns and beaching themselves on the south quay in waves. The fish survive, turn and slide back into the water. The two constables who took the report are the two who handled the cistern workers' dreams, and the Watch is not investigating.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A grey pigeon sits on the corner of the table in front of you with its feathers ruffled, and it waits until the room is quiet. A low man's voice speaks from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > "Fish are beaching themselves on the south quay in waves. Jeryth asks that you come to the garden today."
>
> The pigeon takes off through the open door without a backward look.

The message is 19 words, which fits inside the 25 words that *Animal Messenger* allows.

The road to Phaulkonmere can run along the south quay. If the party passes it, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fisherfolk stand along the edge of the south quay with nets in their hands and no work to put in them. Silver fish flop across the stones in waves of a hundred at a time, and every wave slides back into the harbor by itself. A harbor worker with a tally slate calls odds on the next wave to a ring of dockhands. Two constables at the far end of the quay watch the water.

Read **The Garden Gate** first if this is the member's first arrival, then read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor waits at the oldest oak with a small disc of bark in his hand, and the voice reaches you on the path before you arrive. It settles into the gravel ahead of you from no direction at all.
>
> > "Two Eyes are open now. It has found the water under the Castle Ward."
>
> The leaves hang still, and the pause runs longer than before.
>
> > "There is a crack in a cellar on Selduth Street. Close it with the seal Melannor holds."
>
> The silence stretches longer again, and not a leaf moves in it.
>
> > "The channel under the Castle Ward is fouled by something made. Clear it before the third Eye opens. I am asking."

If **The Fouled Channel** is already complete, replace her last turn with the following, and do not mark **Fouled Channel Requested**:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "The channel runs clean. Rest here."

Otherwise mark **Fouled Channel Requested** for each member who hears the request. The crow for **The Fouled Channel** still arrives on the first dawn after a member qualifies, and nothing in this Event hurries it.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor holds out the bark disc on his open palm. It is resin-hardened and warm to the touch.
>
> > "Press this into the bare stone of the cellar on Selduth Street, in the Trades Ward. The merchant is Bertio Caskwall, and he does not need to know what it is."

#### The Cellar on Selduth Street

Bertio Caskwall hired a crew three tendays ago to dig out his cellar, and the crew cracked a stone in the east wall. His workers have complained of cold in that corner ever since. The disc is cut from the oldest oak, and Jeryth's voice reaches the cellar through it.

> [!social]**Bertio Caskwall**
>
> Bertio Caskwall (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a stout wine merchant in his fifties, allied to no faction, who wants his cellar finished before the summer vintage arrives.
>
> Bertio is cheerful and a little anxious, and he talks about his casks whenever he is nervous. He lets in anyone who seems to know old foundations, and he does not ask what the disc is. He answers in short, practical sentences.
>
> Bertio is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The crew he hired, the cold in the east corner and his stock.
>
> He will not discuss what the crew was paid, which he considers his own business.

> [!qna]**What happened to the cellar?**
>
> > "My crew dug out the east wall and found a cracked stone. They have refused to work in that corner for a tenday because of the cold, and I cannot blame them."

> [!qna]**Can we have a look?**
>
> > "If you know anything about old foundations, then please do have a look at it. I have lost a good deal of time already, and I would rather someone sensible looked."

> [!exploration]**Getting Down the Cellar Stairs**
>
> Bertio lets the party in by any one of three routes.
>
> - **Persuasion:** a character who speaks as a foundation specialist and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check.
> - **A trade:** a character proficient with mason's tools or carpenter's tools talks about the foundation like a mason, and needs no check.
> - **A fee:** the party pays Bertio 5 gp for a survey, and he leads them down himself.
>
> A failed check costs only the time it takes to try another route.

> [!exploration]**The Exposed Node**
>
> A hairline crack runs through the foundation stone of the east wall, and a thin cold draught breathes out of it. A character who searches the wall and makes a successful **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** check finds it at once. Without that, the draught leads a patient searcher to the crack after an hour.
>
> Pressing the bark disc into the crack closes it, and nothing in the cellar threatens the party or calls for a fight.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** a member presses the seal into the crack, and the award is made once per member who does so.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The bark disc sinks into the crack, and the draught stops. A smell of rain and turned earth fills the cellar, and a woman's voice speaks one word from the stone itself.
>
> > "Good."
>
> The voice does not return, and when you lay a hand against the corner the cold there is gone.

Bertio's workers return to that corner the next morning and finish digging out the cellar without complaint.

### Surge Five: Before the Silence

This surge fires at dawn after the third Eye is restored. Three households in the Castle Ward wake to wellwater that has gone black with sediment, and it clears within minutes. One belongs to a fisherwoman whom Melannor knows by name, and she says the water smelled very old.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A grey pigeon walks along the rail of the stair at Trollskull Manor as you come down in the morning, and it stops two steps above you. The low voice from behind its eyes is slower than before.
>
> > "Jeryth's voice is failing. Come to the garden now, while she can still speak."
>
> The pigeon beats its wings once and drops over the rail into the alley.

The message is 14 words, which fits inside the 25 words that *Animal Messenger* allows.

Read **The Garden Gate** first if this is the member's first arrival, then read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor stands at the oak with a stoppered glass jar in one hand and mud dried on both knees. The water in the jar is black with sediment, and as you watch, the dark settles to the bottom and leaves it grey. A woman's voice reaches you in pieces from the leaves and the gravel together, with a long silence after each piece.
>
> > "The third Eye is open. It heard."
>
> The leaves tremble for several breaths before she speaks again.
>
> > "My voice is failing. A silence is coming."
>
> The garden holds still, and then the voice speaks once more.
>
> > "Rest here."

Mark **Silence Warning Given** for each member who hears her, and then read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor sets the jar on a root of the oak and folds his hands.
>
> > "When her voice stops, no animal will leave this garden, so I cannot send for you. On the morning after, the oak in your courtyard will put out shoots in every direction, and you should come to me as soon as you see it."

> [!qna]**What can we do until then?**
>
> > "Do what Jeryth asked in the channel, if that work is not yet done. Keep the Stone moving from house to house, and watch the oak in your courtyard each morning."

> [!qna]**Will she be there at the vault?**
>
> > "If the silence comes before the vault is opened, she cannot ward the opening. She asked me to say so plainly, and I have done so."

> [!gamemaster]**Jeryth's Silence**
>
> - **When it begins:** Jeryth's full silence is the opening of **The Dreamer's Reach**. It begins three days before the oak puts out its shoots, and the shoots appear on the morning after an individual Emerald Enclave member meets that mission's gate. The gate is Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**. Until then she speaks in short bursts, and the haven and her healing at Phaulkonmere work as written.
> - **At the Vault:** if no member has met the gate when the party reaches **Vault of Dragons**, Jeryth is already silent there. She cannot ward the opening, and **The Dreamer's Reach** runs after the Vault.
> - **Promise:** nobody in this Event says the mission will come within a tenday, or on any named day. Melannor knows only that the oak will tell him.
> - **Fouled Channel unfinished:** if **Fouled Channel Requested** is marked and **The Fouled Channel** has not been played, Melannor adds one line. "The channel is still fouled, and Jeryth asked for that first."
>   **The Dreamer's Reach** presupposes the cutting planted in that mission. A member who reaches Renown 13 without it plays **The Fouled Channel** first.
> - **Stone left in one place:** if any tenday passed with the Stone under one roof every night, Melannor adds the following line, and it changes nothing else. "Jeryth says the Stone lay too long in one place, and the dreamer found its weight."

### Renown Opportunities

This Event awards no base Renown. Each participating Emerald Enclave member can gain 1 Renown, once, from each of Surge One, Surge Three and Surge Four, awarded in that scene, while Surge Two and Surge Five award none. A member who plays a missed surge later can still earn its line, and companions gain none.

### Aftermath

The surges leave no trace in the city beyond the signs themselves. The Watch does not connect the starlings, the dreams, the fish and the wellwater, and no faction learns what Jeryth said.

A Harper in the party who heard Jeryth in Surge Three recognises her words in **The Stone's Other Master**, where Mirt's seer feels an answer from below.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the fifth surge has fired and each member has heard Jeryth's warning, and it writes the two outcomes below.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Fouled Channel Requested** — mark with the member's name when Jeryth asks for the channel in Surge Four, unless **The Fouled Channel** is already complete. It is read by **The Fouled Channel**, where Melannor says Jeryth asked.
> - **Silence Warning Given** — mark with the member's name when they hear Jeryth's warning and Melannor's instruction about the oak in Surge Five. It is read by **The Dreamer's Reach**, where Melannor speaks of the failing voice.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**

## Overview

Something dreaming below the Castle Ward stirs each time the Stone of Golorr grows stronger, and Jeryth Phaulkon warns the Emerald Enclave's members five times.

## Summary

A grey pigeon brought us Melannor's word each time the water beneath the Castle Ward stirred. Starlings circled the harbor, the oak roots moved, and cistern workers dreamed of drowning. Jeryth told us something old, patient and hungry dreams below. She asked us to keep the Stone moving, close a crack on Selduth Street and clear the fouled channel. By the last warning her voice was failing, and Melannor said the oak in our courtyard would tell us when she fell silent.
