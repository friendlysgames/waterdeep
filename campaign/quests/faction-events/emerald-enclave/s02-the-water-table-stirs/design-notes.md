# Design Notes: The Water Table Stirs

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B has no standalone event for the Enclave, so the five surges are a repo invention. *Dragon Heist* gives only one related beat, in which Jeryth has awakened rats searching the sewers. She has one lead Enclave members to a secret staircase in the Castle Ward sewers that descends to the beholder's lair. The restored draft kept the five triggers and signs (starlings, oak roots, seven cistern dreamers, beaching fish, black wellwater). It gave each surge a vague renown line, said "Mission 5" in the fiction, used a `[GM]` zone and ended on `Milestone: None`. The previous version added cranium rats, intellect devourers and Bertio Caskwall's ward-seal.

## What Changed

Every surge now has a trigger, a sign, a request and a named `+1 Renown:` line or none. The triggers count Eyes and never name a lair heist. One grey pigeon carries Melannor's message in all five surges, and each message is counted at 25 words or fewer. Phaulkonmere's four constants are written once, in **The Garden Gate**. Surge One now refers to the **Fireball!** debrief, because Melannor already passed on Jeryth's first report there. Nobody visits a second time. Jeryth says "old, patient and hungry" only in Surge Three, and the name Illuun never appears. Surge Four asks for **The Fouled Channel** and marks **Fouled Channel Requested**. Surge Five marks **Silence Warning Given** and tells the member that the Trollskull oak will show the silence. That is the sign **The Dreamer's Reach** uses.

## Departures from the Source

The cranium rats and the devourer surge are cut. Nihiloor owns the intellect devourer programme, and the 2024 data has no cranium rat. The ward-seal stays as Surge Four's short task, with no fight, and its renown drops from +2 to +1 so the ladder stays on named +1 lines. Surge Five no longer promises **The Dreamer's Reach** within a tenday. Jeryth's full silence begins when a member meets that mission's gate, or at the Vault if none has. The rat path in Surge Three is new to the Enclave pages and follows the *Dragon Heist* beat for members.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Invented: Bertio Caskwall's cellar, the summer vintage and the Selduth Street address. The street appears only in the previous version and in no source. The grey pigeon as this Event's messenger, the two-hour sewer walk and the unnamed awakened rat are also invented. Bertio is voiced from the Event text, since no profile exists.
- Open: `campaign/structure/arc-f-xanathars-lair.md` line 59 puts area X1 at a false wall below Skullport, while *Dragon Heist* has the Castle Ward staircase lead to X1. **Xanathar's Lair** must reconcile them, and it should read the rat's route as a fourth way in. No outcome records that the party followed the rat.
- Open: this Event's Renown can lift a member from Renown 11 to 14 before **The Fouled Channel**. **The Dreamer's Reach** presupposes that mission, so Surge Five says a member who reaches Renown 13 without it plays **The Fouled Channel** first. The Factions Guide gate row should say the same.
- Open: `campaign/quests/act-ii/gralhund-villa/ev-09-aftermath.md` line 113 has a second Melannor debrief request about the villa. Surge One accepts an account of the villa as a report, so the two requests share one answer.
- Open: **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) should read **Silence Warning Given** and treat Jeryth as silent at the opening when no member has met **The Dreamer's Reach** gate. Its Jeryth line at `arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md` line 47 needs an "if not run" default.
- Open: the Stone's naming of Illuun at Full Awakening has no outcome yet. Surge Three's "old, patient and hungry" is therefore the only description until the lair quests are converted.
