# Ten Nights in the City of the Dead

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Emerald Enclave Mission 2. Available at Renown 1 and character level 3.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

Bones are going missing from City of the Dead mausoleums. A rogue necromancer animates them — that person's identity is never discovered and never matters. What matters is where the skeletons go: directly toward the Brandath family crypt, every time, with the same single-minded purpose. Sir Ambrose Everdawn holds the southern section and needs the northern half covered for ten nights.

The skeletons are not random. The Vault of Dragons lies beneath the Brandath crypts. The disturbance has been probing upward through the skeletons, testing what holds above it. On the tenth night, it withdraws, not resolved but patient: it has decided to wait. Sir Ambrose has felt this wrongness for months. Jeryth has been tracking the same disturbance through Waterdeep's underground water table and ley lines. Neither has yet named what lies beneath the Brandath crypts, and neither will say more than they know.

Jeryth's response to the party's pattern intelligence — "Tell me the moment they open it" — is her first direct reference to the vault. She does not explain. It threads forward to **Vault of Dragons**.

## Involved Characters
- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave) — delivers the mission via crow messenger; relays Jeryth's response to the pattern intelligence through a second bird
- **Sir Ambrose Everdawn** (champion of Kelemvor) — a Tethyrian veteran of approximately sixty; covers the southern section; collegial once the party demonstrates competence; names the Brandath crypts at the final dawn; a minor contact in the City of the Dead from this point forward
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (Phaulkonmere) — present only as a voice through Melannor; her single-line response to the skeleton intelligence plants a hook that runs directly into **Vault of Dragons**

## Dangers & Enemies

**Skeletons** (2024 *Monster Manual*). Six appear at a time, from night five onward, moving with consistent directional purpose toward the Brandath family crypt and back. Their combat capability is moderate. Their behavior is the mission.

## Overview

Bones are going missing from the City of the Dead, and the skeletons that result are moving with a purpose no one has yet named. Sir Ambrose Everdawn needs someone to cover the northern section for ten nights while he patrols the south.
