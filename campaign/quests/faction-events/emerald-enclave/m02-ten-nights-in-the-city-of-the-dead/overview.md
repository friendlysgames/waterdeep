# Ten Nights in the City of the Dead: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Emerald Enclave member reaches Renown 3 and 3rd level, after **The Undercliff Scarecrows**. Companions can help with everything except the visits to Melannor at Phaulkonmere.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> The only fights are the **Skeleton** patrols on nights 4, 6 and 8. Sir Ambrose Everdawn uses the **Knight** stat block and stays out of them unless the party calls him. The **Emerald Enclave Mechanics Reference** sets the skeleton count for three, four and five characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

A crow finds each eligible member at Trollskull Manor in the middle of the morning and speaks in Melannor Fellbranch's voice. Sir Ambrose Everdawn needs the northern half of the City of the Dead patrolled for ten nights, and the patrol starts at sunset.

## Background

Bones have gone missing from three mausoleums in the northern section over the past tenday. Ambrose has walked the cemetery for fifteen years, and he has felt the north go wrong for months. He asked the Enclave for help because he can only watch half the grounds.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> The Vault of Dragons lies under the Brandath grove, and something in it presses against its own wards. The dead nearest the grove walk toward that pressure, so the skeletons come to the grove every time and turn back at the treant. No necromancer exists for the party to find. Jeryth Phaulkon has felt the Brandath ground go wrong without knowing why, and Ambrose knows only that the wrongness centres there. Nobody in this mission learns what lies below, and nothing here opens the Brandath doors.

## The Brief

The crow's message is 24 words long. A member who visits Phaulkonmere before sunset finds Melannor at the oak and can ask him why Jeryth wants this done.

## The Ten Nights

Ambrose waits at the south gate at sunset and gives the party the north. Each night the party chooses posts at the northeast wall, along the mausoleum row or at the Brandath grove, where two Watch officers keep a station. Skeletons walk in on nights 4, 6 and 8 and follow the same path every time.

## The Pattern

The party can document where the skeletons go by three independent routes: watching their path, reading Ambrose's account of the robbed mausoleums, or reading the Watch officers' log. A member who reports the pattern to Melannor at Phaulkonmere hears Jeryth's reply three hours later.

## The Final Dawn

At the tenth dawn Ambrose names the Brandath crypts. He does it unasked if the party reported the pattern, and otherwise he waits to be asked and told why.

## The Brandath Grounds

The party can walk the main path to the edge of the Brandath grove. It sees the sealed doors, the containment warding on them and an elm that watches. Nothing is opened.

## The Debrief

Melannor pays 100 gp to every character who stood all ten nights, member or not. Only members hear his debrief, and only members gain Renown.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 2 base Renown for reporting to Melannor at Phaulkonmere. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** the party documented the pattern and reported it to Melannor and Ambrose before the tenth night, and **Pattern Documented** is marked.
- **+1 Renown:** all ten nights were held and **Bones Kept Safe** is marked.
- **+1 Renown:** a character asked Ambrose about the Brandath crypt and gave a reason before he spoke of it.

Companions who are not Emerald Enclave members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**The Doppelganger Problem** becomes available when an individual member reaches Renown 5 and 4th level. **Jeryth's Message Received** is read by **The Dreamer's Reach**, and **Brandath Crypts Visited** is read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), where Ambrose recognizes the party and waves it through.

## Involved Characters

- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave): sends the crow, receives the pattern report and pays the Enclave's 100 gp.
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (Emerald Enclave): answers the pattern report through Melannor.
- **Sir Ambrose Everdawn** (independent): the Kelemvorite knight who walks the south of the cemetery and names the Brandath crypts.
- **Officer Jonn Bannock and Officer Ysa Terrow** (City Watch): the two officers at the Brandath grove, who keep a log.

## Dangers & Enemies

The skeletons use the **Skeleton** stat block from the 2024 *Monster Manual*. They do not seek a fight, and they attack only characters who block them or strike them. The Brandath treant is never fought, and it drives off anyone who tries the doors without hurting them.

## Overview

Bones are going missing from the City of the Dead, and a Kelemvorite knight needs the north patrolled for ten nights. The party walks the grounds after dark and learns where the dead are going.
