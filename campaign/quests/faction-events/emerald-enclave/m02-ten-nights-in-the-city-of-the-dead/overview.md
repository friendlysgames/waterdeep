# Ten Nights in the City of the Dead

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Emerald Enclave Mission 2. Available at Renown 1 and character level 3.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave) — delivers the mission via crow messenger; passes the skeleton-pattern intelligence to Jeryth; conveys her response
- **Sir Ambrose Everdawn** (champion of Kelemvor) — a Tethyrian veteran of approximately sixty; covers the southern section; collegial once the party demonstrates competence; names the Brandath crypts at the final dawn; becomes a minor contact in the City of the Dead
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (spirit of Phaulkonmere) — present only as a voice through Melannor; her single-line response to the skeleton intelligence plants a hook that runs directly into Arc J

## Dangers & Enemies
**Skeletons** (2024 *Monster Manual*). Six at a time when they appear, from night five onward. They are mindless and move with consistent directional purpose — toward the Brandath family crypt, then returning. Their combat capability is moderate. Their behavior is the mission.

## Overview
Bones are going missing from City of the Dead mausoleums. A rogue necromancer animates them — never identified, never caught — and the skeletons move with a single-minded purpose the party can map across multiple nights: always toward the Brandath family crypt, then back. Sir Ambrose Everdawn holds the southern section and needs the north covered for ten nights.

The ten-night patrol phase fills with patrol rhythm, skeleton encounters, and the building pattern of movement. On the tenth night the activity stops without explanation, as if whatever was pressing against something has decided to wait instead. At the final dawn, Ambrose names what the patrols have been circling. Whether or not the party enters the Brandath crypt, that conversation changes what they understand about what lies beneath it.

Jeryth's response to the pattern intelligence — "Tell me the moment they open it" — is her first direct reference to the vault. She does not explain. It threads forward to Arc J.
