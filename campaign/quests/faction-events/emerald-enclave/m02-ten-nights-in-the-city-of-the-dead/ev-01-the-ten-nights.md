# The Ten Nights

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Exploration Event begins when Melannor's crow reaches the Emerald Enclave members at Trollskull Manor, and it ends when the tenth night closes at dawn. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take the crow's message and, if a member goes to Phaulkonmere before sunset, question Melannor.
> - Meet Sir Ambrose Everdawn at the south gate and take the northern half of the City of the Dead.
> - Choose a post each night: the northeast wall, the mausoleum row or the Brandath grove.
> - Face the skeletons on nights 4, 6 and 8, and watch where they walk.
> - Document the pattern by three independent routes and report it to Ambrose and Melannor.
> - Hear Jeryth's reply through Melannor.
>
> The patrol runs from sunset to sunrise and the party rests by day. Each skeleton night is a separate fight, and their Day Costs do not add. Only Emerald Enclave members receive the crow and see Melannor. Companions can patrol and fight with everything else.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Bones went missing from the Halleth, Corlan and Tessenden mausoleums in the northern section over the past tenday. All three stand on a line between the northeast wall and the Brandath grove.
> - The Vault of Dragons lies under the Brandath grove. The skeletons walk toward it, pause at the edge of the treant's ground and turn back. Nobody in this Event knows what is below.
> - Ambrose has felt the north go wrong for months. He does not offer anything about the Brandath crypt unless he is asked and given a reason.
> - Ambrose takes the south and the party takes the north. Dragon Heist has this the other way round, and the design notes record the change.
> - Jeryth does not know what the pressure is. Her reply is a request to hear if anything answers it.
> - Nobody in this Event names the Splinter, the Cassalanters or Illuun.

### The Brief

The crow reaches each member in the middle of the morning, wherever they are in Trollskull Manor. Others nearby hear it too.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A black crow drops onto the sill of the nearest window and looks straight at you with one bright eye. When it opens its beak, a low and level baritone comes out from somewhere behind its eyes.
>
> > "Bones are missing from the City of the Dead. Sir Ambrose needs the north patrolled for ten nights, starting at sunset. Jeryth says go."
>
> The crow waits a moment, then shakes out its wings and flies off over the rooftops.

The message is 24 words, and it asks for no reply. Any local at a cemetery gate sends the party to the south gate, where Ambrose waits.

A member who walks to Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward before sunset finds Melannor under the oak. Companions wait at the gate.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open before you reach it, and the garden beyond is older than the street that grew up around it. Moss covers the paving, and the oldest oak in the middle is thicker than any tree in the neighbourhood. A woman's voice seems to come from nowhere and everywhere when the leaves stir.
>
> > "Rest here."
>
> Melannor Fellbranch looks up from the roots of the oak, brushes the soil from his hands and walks over to meet you.

The arrival text is read once. Use it again as written if the members return to report the pattern.

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper at the Oak**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the Enclave's groundskeeper, who delivers bad news in the voice of a man describing the weather.
>
> Melannor answers each question in complete, even sentences and waits for the next one. He does not use contractions, does not joke and does not swear. If a member makes a joke, he answers it literally. He calls each member by name.
>
> Melannor is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What he knows of the missing bones, which is what Ambrose told the Enclave.
> - Why the Enclave helps with the dead, because the ground does not forget them.
> - Who Ambrose is and what he expects.
> - What the Enclave pays for ten nights.
>
> He will not discuss what lies under the Brandath ground, because he does not know.

> [!qna]**Why does Jeryth want this?**
>
> > "Jeryth said go three times. I mention it because she does not usually repeat herself, and I did not ask her why."

> [!qna]**Who is Sir Ambrose?**
>
> > "He is a knight of Kelemvor, about sixty years old, and he has walked the cemetery for fifteen. He is formal, and he asks the living to explain themselves."

> [!qna]**What do we do if something comes?**
>
> > "Deal with it, and note where it came from and where it went. Ambrose says the note matters more than the fight."

> [!qna]**What does the Enclave pay?**
>
> > "One hundred gold pieces to each of you who stands all ten nights. The Enclave also counts the work toward your standing."

> [!qna]**Why the City of the Dead?**
>
> > "The soil there is uneasy, and it has been for some months. That is all I know."

### Sir Ambrose Everdawn

Sir Ambrose Everdawn (Lawful Neutral, Tethyrian Human, he/him) is a broad, heavy man of about sixty who has walked the cemetery for fifteen years. The gauntlet pin on his collar is silver and marks him as a knight of Kelemvor. He waits at the south gate at sunset on the first night.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The south gate of the City of the Dead stands open in the last of the light, and driftglobes drift in from the city to hang among the headstones. A broad man in plain travelling clothes waits beside the gate with a worn sword at his hip. When you come near, he removes one gauntlet, touches the nearest headstone and puts the glove back on.
>
> > "Good evening. You are on the grounds of the dead. Walk softly here."

> [!social]**The Warden of the Dead**
>
> Sir Ambrose Everdawn (Lawful Neutral, Tethyrian Human, he/him) :: the Kelemvorite knight of the City of the Dead, who expects the living to explain themselves before he helps them.
>
> Ambrose speaks in formal, complete sentences and never uses a contraction. He calls Kelemvor "the Judge", and he names the family of every plot he passes. He does not swear on the grounds of the dead. His dry humour appears once or twice over ten nights, and only if the party has earned his respect. A successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check on the first night, or a clean first skeleton night, turns him from reserved to friendly. Without either, he gives instructions and expects results.
>
> Ambrose is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The missing bones, the three mausoleums and the dates.
> - The patrol, its posts and the two Watch officers.
> - The rest of the cemetery and its families.
> - The Brandath ground, only if a character asks and says why.
>
> Any character who watches him and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check senses that he knows more about the north than he has said. He is waiting to hear that the party is paying attention.

> [!qna]**What has been happening?**
>
> He looks north across the headstones before he answers.
>
> > "Bones have gone from three mausoleums in the northern section over the past tenday. The Halleth, the Corlan and the Tessenden were all opened and emptied. The north has felt wrong for several months, and I cannot say why."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us?**
>
> > "Cover the northern section from sunset to sunrise, and I will cover the south. Record where the risen dead appear and where they go. We meet at the central gate path at dawn."

> [!qna]**Why do you need help?**
>
> > "I am one man, and the grounds are wide. The Enclave was willing to send someone, and I accepted."

> [!qna]**The Brandath grove?**
>
> > "Why do you ask? The Brandath family is old, and their ground is sealed. A Watch station stands at the grove, and I do not open the doors."

Ambrose answers the Brandath question with these public facts, and he asks for a reason before he says more. If the character gives one, he tells what he knows in **The Final Dawn**. A character who asks and gives a reason at any dawn meeting counts as having asked, for the third Renown bonus.

### The Northern Section at Night

The northern half is older than the south, with weathered monuments and inscriptions worn to partial letters. Two Watch officers, Officer Jonn Bannock and Officer Ysa Terrow, keep a station on the Brandath path with a lantern and a ledger.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The northern section lies quiet under the driftglobes, which move in slow paths between the headstones. The older monuments here have lost most of their lettering, and a thin frost lies on the grass along the main path. At the centre of the section a grove of elm and birch stands around a mausoleum, and two Watch officers sit at a lantern beside the path with a ledger open between them.

Each night every character chooses a post and holds it from sunset to sunrise. The party can change posts between nights. A character who stands all ten nights earns the gold.

> [!exploration]**The Three Posts**
>
> - **The northeast wall.** The iron railing here is bent outward at one gap. A character at this post sees the skeletons come in on nights 4, 6 and 8, and can step into their way.
> - **The mausoleum row.** The Halleth, Corlan and Tessenden stand in a line between the wall and the grove. A character here sees the open doors and the emptied shelves, and meets the skeletons as they pass.
> - **The Brandath grove.** The Watch station is here. A character at this post sees the skeletons stop and turn on nights 4, 6 and 8, and can speak with the officers.
>
> On skeleton nights, characters at the wall and the grove join the characters on the row when the skeletons arrive, and the whole party fights together. A character who leaves the party's post empty on a skeleton night sees nothing of that night's path.

> [!social]**The Watch at the Grove**
>
> Officer Jonn Bannock (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) and Officer Ysa Terrow (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: two City Watch officers on the late shift, who are bored, cold and glad of company.
>
> Bannock does the talking and Terrow keeps the ledger. They keep the party off the Brandath ground and will show the ledger to a member who asks politely, with no check. They have no idea why the north is wrong and say so.
>
> The officers are willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The ledger, which records every disturbance near the grove for the past two months.
> - What they saw on earlier nights.
> - Sir Ambrose, whom they respect.

> [!qna]**What does the ledger say?**
>
> Terrow turns the ledger toward you on her knee.
>
> > "Seven entries since the thaw. Frost on the grove side of the path with no cause, footprints in it, and a cold patch by the doors. Every entry is the same stretch of ground."

> [!qna]**Have you seen anything walk?**
>
> > "Not before you came. Terrow and I have only seen the frost and the prints. They all lead toward the doors and stop at the elm."

### Skeleton Nights

Skeletons walk in on nights 4, 6 and 8. On the other nights nothing moves. On each skeleton night, read the opening below at about the third hour after midnight.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A scraping sound comes from the northeast wall, and a line of skeletons climbs through the gap in the railing. They walk single file along the main path in the light of the driftglobes, and none of them looks at you. They are heading for the grove.

> [!hazard]**Skeleton Patrol**
>
> The skeleton count equals the number of characters, and a party of five faces a sixth skeleton on night 8 only. Companions count as characters.
>
> - **Three characters:** 3 skeletons each night.
> - **Four characters:** 4 skeletons each night.
> - **Five characters:** 5 skeletons on nights 4 and 6, and 6 on night 8.
>
> #### Skeleton Tactics
> To begin, the skeletons walk the path from the wall toward the grove and ignore every character who is not in the way.
>
> During combat, the skeletons:
> - Attack a character who blocks the path or strikes them, using the Shortsword against an adjacent target and the Shortbow if blocked with nothing adjacent.
> - Do not focus fire and do not pursue a character who leaves the path.
> - Stop at the edge of the grove for one round, then turn and walk back the way they came.
> - Take double damage from bludgeoning, so a mace or a club kills one in a single good hit.
>
> The patrol ends when every skeleton is destroyed, or when the survivors reach the northeast wall and climb out, going inert on the road outside. A skeleton that leaves takes its bones with it.
>
> A party that stands clear takes no damage, since the skeletons do not leave the path. The only cost is that **Bones Kept Safe** is lost, because a skeleton that walks out takes its bones with it. Skeletons do not surrender and do not flee.

On night 4 the party sees the path for the first time. On night 6, a character who watched on night 4 can match the entry point, the route and the pause. On night 8 the sequence is the same, and the party can see that nothing varies.

### The Pattern

Three independent routes show that the skeletons are going to the Brandath crypt. Any one of them is enough to document the pattern.

> [!exploration]**Three Routes to the Pattern**
>
> - **The skeletons' path.** Any character who has seen two skeleton nights and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check writes down the entry point at the wall, the route along the row, the one-round pause at the grove's edge and the turn. A character who writes the notes after each skeleton night needs no check after the third.
> - **Ambrose's account.** From night 5 on, Ambrose reports at dawn that the south is quiet and that the wrongness lies north and a little east of the middle. A character who asks him to mark the three emptied mausoleums on the cemetery plan sees that all three stand on a line toward the Brandath grove. No check is needed.
> - **The Watch ledger.** The seven entries in the officers' ledger all describe frost, footprints and cold on the grove side of the path. A character who reads the ledger and makes a successful DC 11 Intelligence (Investigation) check sees that each entry points at the doors.
>
> **Result of 16 or higher on the first route:** The character also notes that the skeletons stop at a line on the ground, and not at the doors. The line lies beneath the roots of the largest elm.

The pattern is documented when the party has put any one of these routes before both Ambrose and Melannor before the tenth night begins. Ambrose hears it at any dawn meeting.

### Reporting to Melannor

A member walks to Phaulkonmere by day to report the pattern. Companions wait at the gate. Melannor takes the report in the garden, then goes to the oak and stands there. The leaves do not move for three hours.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor listens to the whole report with his hands folded in front of him, then walks to the oak and stands with one palm against its bark. The garden is silent. The members wait on the stone bench beside the path while the shadow of the oak crosses the paving.

After three hours, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor comes back along the path at the same unhurried pace, and the leaves of the oak stir once as he passes. He stops in front of you and waits until you are looking at him.
>
> > "Jeryth has heard the report. She says this. Tell me if anything below answers."
>
> His face does not change.
>
> > "I asked her once what below means. She did not reply."

Mark **Jeryth's Message Received** for each member who hears it. Melannor does not know what Jeryth means, and he says so if asked.

### Concluding the Event

The tenth night closes at dawn with the party at the central gate path. Nothing is awarded here. Continue with **The Final Dawn**.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Pattern Documented** — mark when the party has put the skeletons' path, Ambrose's account or the Watch ledger before both Ambrose and Melannor before the tenth night begins; read by **The Final Dawn**, where Ambrose names the Brandath crypt unasked.
> - **Bones Kept Safe** — mark when every skeleton that walked in on nights 4, 6 and 8 was destroyed inside the grounds, so that none left with its bones; read by **The Final Dawn**, which reads it for Melannor's debrief and the Renown bonus.
> - **Jeryth's Message Received** — mark for each member who hears Melannor relay her reply; read by **The Dreamer's Reach** and **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Continue with **The Final Dawn**. **Ten Nights in the City of the Dead** awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

For ten nights the party patrols the northern City of the Dead beside a Kelemvorite knight. Skeletons walk through it on three of them.

## Summary

Melannor's crow sent us to the City of the Dead to patrol the north beside Sir Ambrose Everdawn. Skeletons walked in on three nights, and each time they went to the same grove and turned back. We took our notes to Melannor and heard Jeryth's reply.
