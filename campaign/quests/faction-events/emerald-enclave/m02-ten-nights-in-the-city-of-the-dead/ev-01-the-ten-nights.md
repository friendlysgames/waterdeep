# Ten Nights — The Patrol

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event spans ten nights in the northern section of the City of the Dead. In this Event:
>
> - The party covers the northern section; Sir Ambrose Everdawn covers the south; they compare notes at dawn each morning at the central gate path.
> - Skeletons appear from night five onward on a 10% cumulative chance per night — always moving toward the Brandath family crypt, then returning.
> - The pattern of directional movement is the mission's central discovery; documenting and reporting it to Melannor and Ambrose before the tenth night is one renown condition.
> - The Brandath crypt can be observed from outside; entering without authorization is possible and will be reported.
> - If the party reports the pattern, Melannor passes it to Jeryth; she is silent for three hours, then sends a single message.
> - The event ends at the final dawn; proceed to the **Ten Nights — The Final Dawn** Event.

**Background (DM only)**
The skeletons are animated by a rogue necromancer. That identity is never discovered and never matters. What matters is their destination: the Brandath family crypt, again and again. The Vault of Dragons lies beneath the Brandath crypts. Sir Ambrose has felt a psychic wrongness in the northern section for months — something old, contained, and restless. Jeryth has been tracking the same disturbance through Waterdeep's underground water table and ley lines. Neither has named it. The skeletons are its first upward reach — the disturbance probing the wall above it, testing what holds.

### Sir Ambrose Everdawn

He waits at the southern gate of the City of the Dead at sunset on the first night. A Tethyrian man of approximately sixty, broad and heavy, in plain traveling clothes with a sword that carries the look of long use. His only sign of office is a small silver gauntlet pin — Kelemvor's, not Tyr's. He is patient: unhurried, used to long dark hours and nothing moving.

> [!npc-narrative]**Sir Ambrose Everdawn**
> A broad, heavy Tethyrian man of about sixty, in plain traveling clothes. The sword at his hip carries the look of long use. His only sign of office is a small silver gauntlet pin — Kelemvor's, not Tyr's. He is unhurried: the patient stillness of someone accustomed to long dark hours and nothing moving.

He explains the situation plainly. Bones have been going missing from three mausoleums in the northern section. He patrols the south half after dark. He needs the party to cover the north. At dawn he will meet them at the central gate path.

> > "I've patrolled this cemetery for fifteen years. The northern section has felt wrong for months. Not fresh-wrong — old-wrong. Patient, like it's waiting for something."

A DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check, or demonstrated competence through the early nights, converts his initial skepticism to collegial partnership. Without it, he assigns the patrol with specific instructions and expects results only. Over the ten nights, he becomes willing to say more: he has encountered this quality of wrongness twice before in his career, both times associated with something ancient and technically contained.

The daily dawn meetings are brief. He listens to what the party reports. He shares what he observed in the south. By the fifth night, if the party has been tracking the skeleton movement, he has begun to note the same directional pull from his side of the cemetery.

### The Northern Section at Night

The City of the Dead after dark fills with driftglobes drifting among the headstones in slow, purposeless paths — placed by the Mortuary to ease late mourning visits. The northern section is older, monuments more weathered, inscriptions harder to read. The Brandath family crypt stands at the section's center: carved marble, sealed iron doors, a Watch officer rotation visible from the main path. Two officers, assigned after the third skeleton incident.

#### Skeleton Encounters

Beginning on night five, roll 1d10 at the start of the patrol. Add +1 per previous night that passed without an encounter. On 10 or higher: six **Skeletons** appear in the northern section.

When skeletons appear, they are already mid-motion — moving in one direction. Toward the Brandath crypt. If attacked and destroyed, they stop. If not immediately engaged, they reach the crypt's exterior, linger near the sealed doors for one round, then reverse and leave the cemetery.

This directional movement is the discovery the mission is built around. A character who watches rather than immediately attacking notices it on the first occurrence. A character who actively tracks it across multiple nights — noting entry point, path, crypt-side pause, and reversal — can document the pattern with precision. That documentation is one renown condition.

#### The Brandath Crypt — Exterior

The crypt can be observed from the main path without approaching. From outside: the building has the quality of a place that is deliberately not letting something out. The sealed iron doors carry residue of old magic — not active, not recent, but layered over decades. A DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check identifies it as containment warding of the type used when something was sealed inside rather than kept out.

Entering without authorization requires picking the sealed iron doors (DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)) or forcing them (DC 18 Strength). The Watch officers on rotation are present and will report a break-in to their sergeant. What the party finds inside is covered in the **Ten Nights — The Final Dawn** Event.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### If the Party Enters Early
> Melannor learns of the breach within a day through the Enclave's city contacts. He addresses it directly: "Jeryth asked me to say — patience. It's been there a long time. It will be there when you are ready to go back with proper authority." He does not know what is inside. He is conveying the instruction faithfully. Jeryth knows.

### Reporting the Pattern

When the party brings Melannor evidence of the directional movement — notes, testimony, a map of the paths — he passes it to Jeryth immediately. She is silent for three hours. Then she sends a single message through a songbird that lands on the window at Trollskull Manor:

> > "Tell me the moment they open it."

Melannor, reading the party's expression when he shares this: "She didn't say what 'it' is. She didn't need to, apparently." He is not being evasive. He genuinely does not know. But he recognizes the weight of the instruction.

## Overview

For ten nights, the party patrols the northern City of the Dead and watches the skeletons do something methodical.

## Read Aloud

> [!narrative]
> A crow arrives at Trollskull Manor — Melannor later explains that Jeryth chooses the crow when she considers something urgent. The briefing: "Bones are going missing from the City of the Dead. Someone is raising skeletons. Sir Ambrose Everdawn, a champion of Kelemvor, has been asked to patrol the southern half for ten nights. He needs someone to cover the northern section. Jeryth says go." A pause in the crow's delivery. "She said it three times. I mention this because she doesn't usually repeat herself."

## Summary

The party began a ten-night patrol of the northern City of the Dead alongside Sir Ambrose Everdawn. Skeletons appeared on multiple nights, each time moving with clear directional purpose toward the Brandath family crypt before retreating.
