# Ten Nights — The Final Dawn

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Social Event occurs at dawn on the tenth night. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Meet Sir Ambrose at the central gate path for the final dawn debrief
> - Hear him name the Brandath crypts as the source of the disturbance
> - Enter the Brandath crypt with Ambrose's vouching, or persuade him to provide it
> - Find evidence inside the crypt of something sealed below the floor — large and magically significant
> - Understand what Jeryth's earlier message was warning them about
>
> #### Background
>
> The disturbance has pulled back. The thing anchored beneath the Brandath crypts — the Stone of Golorr's resonant connection to the Vault of Dragons below — has been probing upward through the skeletons, testing what holds. On the tenth night, it withdraws. Not resolved: waiting. Sir Ambrose has held this section for fifteen years. He knows the northern section is wrong and will say it plainly now. He will not stop the party from looking inside the crypt. He will not look himself.

### The Tenth Dawn

At the central gate path, the light is gray and thin. Ambrose is already there.

> [!readaloud]
> Sir Ambrose is at the central gate path when you arrive, standing with his back to the south section and his eyes on the northern monuments. The light is gray and thin and the driftglobes are going out one by one as the sky comes up.
>
> The northern section went quiet at the third hour. Nothing had left it; everything simply stopped moving, the way a held breath is still, as if whatever was pressing against the surface from below decided to pull back instead.
>
> Ambrose listens to your report and then looks north for a long moment without speaking.
>
> > "The Brandath crypts. Whatever is happening there — the bones were not the point. They were a symptom." He does not shift his gaze from the northern monuments. "Be careful near that mausoleum."

> [!social]**The Warden at Dawn**
>
> Ambrose is more forthcoming at the tenth dawn than he was on the first night. Ten nights of competent observation has earned the party his full cooperation. He knows the crypt is the source. He will say it. He has no authority over what is inside, and he will not pretend he does.
>
> Conversation topics Ambrose is willing to discuss at the final dawn include:
> - What the Brandath crypt is and what he knows about it
> - What he has felt in the northern section over the past several months
> - Whether he would vouch for the party with the Watch officers
>
> Any character who asks about the crypt directly and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check framed as a professional obligation — after ten nights watching skeletons move toward it, understanding what is inside matters — finds Ambrose willing to help. He does it without enthusiasm, but he believes in what the party just did.

> [!qna]**About the Brandath crypt?**
>
> He takes a breath before answering.
>
> > "The Brandath family vault. Four generations sealed inside. The crypt itself has felt wrong for as long as I have patrolled this section — old wrong, not fresh wrong. I had encountered this quality of wrongness twice before in my career, both times associated with something ancient and technically contained. I did not know what was below the Brandath vault. I still do not." He is quiet for a moment. "I think you should."

> [!qna]**Will you vouch for us with the Watch?**
>
> > "You patrolled ten nights. You kept the northern section. You documented what was moving and where it went." He looks toward the Watch post. "Yes. I will vouch for you. I would prefer this be done properly."

### Entering the Brandath Crypt

With Ambrose's vouching, the Watch officers open the crypt without further obstacle. Without his vouching, the Watch officers will not open it; forcing or picking the doors remains possible (DC 15 Dexterity with thieves' tools, or DC 18 Strength), and Ambrose covers it — he will not report a break-in after ten nights.

> [!readaloud]
> The Brandath crypt is a family vault: carved names on the walls, stone sarcophagi for four generations, old mourning offerings dried to powder. The air is cold stone and something mineral, something that is not decay — just presence, coming from below.
>
> The floor draws attention immediately. The central flagstone is different from the rest: cut from rock that does not match the local quarry, older by centuries, fitted with unusual precision. Anyone who concentrates on the stone can feel magical residue in it, a faint tingling warmth under the fingertips.

> [!exploration]**The Sealed Floor**
>
> Any character who examines the central flagstone and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) or Intelligence (History) check reads the residue clearly.
>
> > Below this floor, something with significant arcane charge has been sealed for decades. The warding was placed intentionally, from above. Whatever is below was not trying to escape when it was sealed — it was being preserved.
>
> The party learns that something large and magically significant is sealed below the Brandath family vault. They do not learn what it is, what it contains, or who sealed it. This is the correct amount of information at this stage. Jeryth said "tell me the moment they open it" three times, and the party now understands why she did.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Crypt Reveals**
>
> Jeryth knew. She has known for some time. The party now understands why she said it three times. Whatever is below the crypt floor is connected to the Stone of Golorr and the Vault of Dragons beneath it. The party is not meant to understand the full chain of connection at this point; they are meant to understand that the chain exists, and that Jeryth is watching it.
>
> This location is revisited in **Vault of Dragons**, where the Brandath Crypts serve as one approach path to the vault. Parties who explored here have prior knowledge of the crypt layout and the magical signature of the sealed floor.

Sir Ambrose is a reliable minor contact in the City of the Dead from this point forward.

## Overview

The tenth dawn brings Sir Ambrose naming the Brandath crypts as the source of everything the patrol has been circling.

## Summary

The skeleton activity stopped on the tenth night without explanation. At the final dawn, Sir Ambrose named the Brandath crypt as the source of the disturbance, and parties who entered found evidence of something sealed below the floor — something old, arcane, and deliberately preserved. Jeryth had said "tell me the moment they open it" three times. Standing inside the crypt, the party understood why.
