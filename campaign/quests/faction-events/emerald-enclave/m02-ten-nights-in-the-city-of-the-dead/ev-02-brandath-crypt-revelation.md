# Ten Nights — The Final Dawn

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Social Event occurs at dawn on the tenth night. In this Event:
>
> - The skeleton activity stops at the third hour after midnight without explanation.
> - At the final dawn meeting, Sir Ambrose names the Brandath crypts as the source of the disturbance.
> - The party can enter the Brandath crypt now, with Ambrose's tacit cooperation or by persuading him to vouch with the Watch (DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)).
> - Inside the crypt, the party finds evidence of something large and magically significant sealed below the floor — they do not learn what it is.
> - Whether or not the party enters, the attunement flag is set at the end of this Event.

**Background (DM only)**
The disturbance has pulled back. The thing anchored beneath the Brandath crypts — specifically the Stone of Golorr's resonant connection to the Vault of Dragons below — has been probing upward through the skeletons, testing what holds. On the tenth night, it withdraws. Not gone: waiting. It decided to be patient instead.

Sir Ambrose has held the City of the Dead for fifteen years. He knows the northern section is wrong. He does not know what lies beneath the crypt, but he knows what the skeletons were doing, and he will say it plainly now. He will not stop the party from looking inside. He will not look himself.

### The Tenth Dawn

At the central gate path, the light is gray and thin. Ambrose is already there. He waited.

The northern section went quiet at the third hour. No encounter, no approaching figures. The quiet had a quality to it — not empty, but deliberate, the withdrawal of something that had been pressing against a surface and decided to pull back instead.

Ambrose listens to the party's report. He looks north across the headstones for a long moment.

> > "The Brandath crypts. Whatever is happening there — the bones weren't the point. They were a symptom." He does not move his gaze. "Be careful near that mausoleum."

He has said what he can say. He has no authority over what is inside the crypt. The Watch officers are still at their post.

### Entering the Brandath Crypt

If the party wants to go in, Ambrose can help. A DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check, framed as a professional need to understand what they have been protecting for ten nights, persuades him to accompany the party to the Watch post and vouch for them. He does it without enthusiasm — he believes in legal process — but he believes in what the party just did for ten nights.

Without his vouching, the Watch officers will not open the crypt. Forcing or picking the doors is possible, as in the Patrol Event, but no longer produces a Watch report — Ambrose covers it.

#### Inside the Crypt

A family vault. Carved Brandath names on the walls, stone sarcophagi for four generations, old mourning offerings dried to powder. The smell of old stone and a mineral undertone — something below, not decaying, just present.

The floor draws attention immediately: the central flagstone is different from the others. Cut from rock that does not match the local quarry, older by centuries, fitted with unusual precision. The magical residue in the stone is palpable to anyone who concentrates on it.

A DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or History) check reads the residue clearly: below this floor, something with significant arcane charge has been sealed for decades. The warding was placed intentionally, from above. Whatever is below was not trying to escape when it was sealed — it was being preserved.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### What the Crypt Reveals
> The party learns that something large and magically significant is sealed below the Brandath family vault. They do not learn what it contains, what it is, or who sealed it. This is the correct amount of information at this stage. It makes Jeryth's earlier message — "tell me the moment they open it" — land with full weight. She knew. She has known for some time. The party now knows why she said it three times.
