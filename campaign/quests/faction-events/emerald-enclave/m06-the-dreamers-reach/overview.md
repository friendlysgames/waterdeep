# The Dreamer's Reach

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Emerald Enclave Mission 6. Available at Renown 12 and character level 7.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 7th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

Jeryth Phaulkon has fallen silent. For three days her voice has been absent from Phaulkonmere's garden; the plants grow without direction, tilting toward nothing; the birds in the estate oaks are roosting facing outward. Melannor believes she is fighting something — and he is right.

The Castle Ward cistern channel disrupted during Mission 5 gave **Illuun**, an abolethic dreamer anchored to Undermountain Level 4, a direct path into the underground channels Jeryth uses to maintain her connection to the city's water table. Illuun has been extending psychic reach upward through Waterdeep's underground water system for decades, barely perceptible — the Stone of Golorr's activation created a resonance it responded to, pushing more actively toward the surface. Jeryth has been absorbing and deflecting this influence for months. The Mission 5 disruption opened a gap, and Illuun pressed through it.

Jeryth is winning. She is resisting and holding, but the effort consumes her ability to communicate. She has 48 hours before she either breaks the connection or withdraws too deep into Phaulkonmere's earth to surface in time for **Vault of Dragons**. Destroying the anchor severs Illuun's reach through this specific surface path. It does not harm Illuun itself, which is too far below to be touched by this action.

Any party member who examines the anchor before destroying it experiences direct psychic contact with Illuun. Illuun does not speak to them or control them; it simply notices them, something immense, patient and very old registering their presence. Illuun is now personally aware of that character. If they descend to Undermountain Level 4, Illuun will sense their arrival before they reach it.

## Involved Characters
- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave) — delivers the mission in person from inside the garden; provides a *scroll of Water Breathing* if the party needs it, and a piece of Phaulkonmere bark that glows near the anchor; visibly afraid for Jeryth
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (spirit of Phaulkonmere) — has been silent for three days, fighting Illuun's psychic reach through the disrupted cistern channel; speaks again when the anchor is destroyed; gives the party the Phaulkonmere ward and her **Vault of Dragons** assurance

## Dangers & Enemies
Two **Chuul** (2024 *Monster Manual*). Crustacean aberrations that have occupied the deepest chamber for years, drawn by proximity to the anchor. The flooded floor creates Difficult Terrain in the deep sections of the chamber. The anchor is not a combat threat, but examining it before destroying it requires a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw; failure inflicts the Frightened condition and leaves the character personally registered to Illuun.

## Overview
Jeryth Phaulkon has fallen silent. Something below the Castle Ward has found a path through the cisterns and is pressing against her.
