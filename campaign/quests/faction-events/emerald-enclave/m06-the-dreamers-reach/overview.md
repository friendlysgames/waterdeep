# The Dreamer's Reach: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Emerald Enclave member reaches Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**. Seventh level requires all four lair heists, so a party that ran three heists reaches it only after **Vault of Dragons** and plays this mission then. The Vault does not treat it as a prerequisite, and companions can help with everything except Melannor's brief and debrief.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 7th-level characters.*
>
> The fight is a Day Cost 4 encounter against the 2024 **Chuul**, with one to three chuul by party size. The **Emerald Enclave Mechanics Reference** audits the rosters, the prey rule, the anchor and the Ward ring. A party that plays after the Vault is 8th level and uses the same rosters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Emerald Enclave members who complete the assignment.

## Hook

No animal brings this mission, and the sign is the oak in the Trollskull Manor courtyard, which has put out shoots in every direction on the morning after a member qualifies. Melannor's druids asked the party to leave that oak alone, so every Enclave member knows it is his business. The members walk to Phaulkonmere, where the gate stands open and Melannor waits in a garden that grows without direction.

## Background

Jeryth Phaulkon has held something back from the Castle Ward water table for months. It is Illuun, an aboleth dreaming on Undermountain Level 4, and the Stone of Golorr woke it. Each Eye that the party restored made it press harder toward the surface, and Jeryth reported each pressure through Melannor in **The Water Table Stirs**.

In **The Fouled Channel**, Jeryth rooted a cutting in the cistern to clear the waste, and the cutting gave her a way into the Castle Ward water. Illuun followed that way back to her, and over the last tenday it pushed a fragment of itself into a chamber below the cisterns and let it grow into an anchor. Three days ago the pressure became too great for her to speak and resist at once.

Six years ago a chuul scout from Illuun's brood came up Gerrick Thatchley's drain. His neighbours drove it back with fire and sealed the arch, and Gerrick never learned what it was. The chuul in the deepest chamber belong to the same brood, and this mission answers the question that **The Undercliff Scarecrows** left open.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jeryth wakes the moment the anchor breaks, and she speaks only at the oak. If 48 hours pass first, she withdraws into the roots and wakes a tenday after the anchor falls.
> - The anchor and Jalester Silvermane are two separate routes of Illuun's reach. Destroying the anchor does not end the link through Jalester at the Yawning Portal's well. **The Stone's Other Master** and **An Audience with the Open Lord** therefore still run as written.
> - Nobody in this mission names Illuun. Melannor and Jeryth say that something dreams below, and a character learns the name only if the Stone named it at Full Awakening.
> - Illuun cannot be fought here, because the anchor is a fragment and breaking it does the aboleth no harm.
> - If **Cache Destroyed** is marked, the cistern is clean when the party passes through it. If it is not marked, the Splinter's film still lies on the eastern channel.

## The Brief

Melannor waits beneath the oldest oak. He explains that Jeryth has been silent for three days and has 48 hours left. He then gives the party a *spell scroll* of *water breathing* and a strip of bark that glows near the anchor.

## Gerrick's Drain

Gerrick Thatchley opens the old seal below the southern terraces. The first ten feet of the tunnel are scorched from the fire that drove the chuul scout back. A piece of its shell is still caught in the seal.

## The Cisterns

The tunnel runs west into the Castle Ward cistern from **The Fouled Channel**, and a passage closed with fitted stones leads down from it. The party finds the anchor's chamber by three routes, which are the bark, the current in the lower cisterns and the roots of Jeryth's cutting.

## The Threshold

A flooded passage leads to the deepest chamber, and the party crosses it with the scroll or by swimming. As each character steps through the far arch, a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw decides whether the pressure of the thing below costs that character a turn.

## The Brood

One to three chuul guard the anchor, depending on party size. They sense magic, they Paralyze what they grapple, and they stop attacking when the anchor falls.

## The Anchor

A character who examines the anchor makes a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw and marks **Illuun Contact** on a failure. One hit of 15 or more fire or radiant damage destroys the anchor, and weapons can wear it down.

## Jeryth Wakes

When the anchor breaks, Jeryth wakes. At the oak she speaks twice, Melannor gives the party the Phaulkonmere Ward, and he takes the members' report.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 4 base Renown for descending to the anchor and reporting to Melannor. Companions gain none. Bonuses of +1 go to members whose party destroys the anchor and breaks the brood, who report the contact in full, and whose party finishes inside Jeryth's 48 hours.

## Aftermath

**Anchor Destroyed** and **Illuun Contact** feed **Master of the Wild** and **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), and **Illuun Contact** also feeds Dungeon of the Mad Mage. This is the last Emerald Enclave mission. **Winterstalker** follows at Renown 25 and **Master of the Wild** at Renown 50.

## Involved Characters

- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave): waits in the garden, briefs the members and takes their report.
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (Emerald Enclave): silent for three days, wakes when the anchor breaks and speaks twice.
- **Gerrick Thatchley** (independent): opens his drain for the party and never learns what came up it unless they tell him.
- **The chuul** (Illuun's brood): guard the anchor in the deepest chamber.
- **Illuun** (aboleth, never present): the dreamer on Undermountain Level 4 whose fragment is the anchor.

## Dangers & Enemies

The chuul use the 2024 **Chuul** stat block unchanged, with the prey rule that keeps their Paralyzing Tentacles from ending a character on the first turn. The anchor is a Tiny object with 60 hit points that a single hit of 15 or more fire or radiant damage destroys. The entry save and the examine save are the other dangers, and neither deals damage.

## Overview

Jeryth Phaulkon has fallen silent, and her garden has lost its sense of direction. Melannor sends the party down an old drain to find what is pressing on her.
