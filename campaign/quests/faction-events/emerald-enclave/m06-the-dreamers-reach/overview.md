# The Dreamer's Reach

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Emerald Enclave Mission 6. Available at Renown 12 and character level 7.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 7th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Melannor Fellbranch** (Emerald Enclave) — delivers the mission in person from inside the garden; provides a Water Breathing scroll and a piece of Phaulkonmere bark that glows near the anchor; visibly afraid for Jeryth
- **Jeryth Phaulkon** (spirit of Phaulkonmere) — has been silent for three days, fighting Illuun's psychic reach through the disrupted cistern channel; speaks again when the anchor is destroyed; gives the party the Phaulkonmere ward and her **Vault of Dragons** assurance
- Two **Chuul** servants — drawn to Illuun's anchor over years of proximity; attack on sight; the flooded chamber restricts movement around them

## Dangers & Enemies
Two **Chuul** (2024 *Monster Manual*). Crustacean aberrations that have occupied the deepest chamber for years. The flooded floor creates Difficult Terrain in deep sections. The anchor is not a combat threat, but examining it before destroying it requires a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw; failure inflicts the Frightened condition and leaves the character personally registered to Illuun.

## Overview
Jeryth Phaulkon has fallen silent. For three days her voice has been absent from Phaulkonmere's garden; the plants grow without direction; the birds in the estate oaks roost facing outward. Melannor believes she is fighting something — the cistern channel disrupted during Mission 5 gave Illuun, an abolethic dreamer anchored to Undermountain Level 4, a direct path into the underground channels Jeryth uses. She is resisting and winning, but the effort consumes her ability to communicate. She has 48 hours before she either breaks the connection or withdraws too deep to surface in time for **Vault of Dragons**.

The party descends deeper than Mission 5 — past the cache level, through flooded lower cisterns, to the chamber where Illuun's surface anchor point has grown into the floor over centuries. Two chuul guard the chamber. Destroying the anchor severs the connection. When the party returns to Phaulkonmere, Jeryth is speaking again.

She gives the party a woven root ward and confirms she is ready for **Vault of Dragons**. She does not need notice anymore. She is already listening.
