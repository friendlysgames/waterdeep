# The Anchor

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat and Exploration Event follows **The Descent**. It begins when the party crosses into the deepest chamber, and it ends when Melannor Fellbranch has taken the members' report at Phaulkonmere. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Fight or outwit the chuul that guard the anchor in a flooded chamber.
> - Examine the anchor first and risk contact with the thing below.
> - Destroy the anchor with one hit of 15 or more fire or radiant damage, or wear it down with weapons.
> - Return to Phaulkonmere and hear Jeryth Phaulkon wake.
> - Receive the Phaulkonmere Ward and report to Melannor.
>
> Melannor takes the report from the Emerald Enclave members only, and companions wait in the outer garden, where Jeryth's voice still carries. Companions can fight, swim and carry the ring, and they earn no Renown. **Illuun Contact** is marked for any character who fails the examine save, member or not.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The chuul are Illuun's brood. They defend the anchor until it falls, and then they break and retreat into the lower channel.
> - The anchor is a fist-sized fragment of the thing below on a dry plinth. Destroying it cuts this path and does Illuun no harm.
> - A character who examines the anchor feels how old and patient the thing is. That character learns its name only if the Stone named it at Full Awakening, and nobody else in this Event says it.
> - Jeryth wakes the moment the anchor breaks, wherever the party is, and she speaks only at the oak. She explains neither of her lines, and Melannor does not know what they mean.
> - If the 48 hours ended first, Jeryth is in the roots. She wakes at the oak a tenday after the anchor falls and speaks both lines then.

### The Deepest Chamber

The chamber is round and about forty feet across, with a fitted-stone ceiling ten feet above the water. The water is chest-deep and still, and it is Difficult Terrain for the party only. A dry plinth five feet wide stands thirty feet from the threshold with the anchor on it. A flooded side passage in the east wall leads to the lower channel. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The chamber is round and black, and the water in it lies as flat as a mirror. In the middle a stone plinth rises out of the surface, and a dark lump the size of a fist sits on top of it. The bark in your hand glows so brightly that you could read by it. Two long grey-green shapes lie below the surface on either side of the plinth, and their feelers turn slowly toward the magic you carry.

If five characters are present, add this:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A third shape lies along the far wall, pale against the stone.

> [!hazard]**Illuun's Brood**
>
> Use the **Chuul** stat block from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, unchanged. Count every combatant on the party's side, companions included. Melannor and Jeryth are not counted. The **Emerald Enclave Mechanics Reference** audits the rosters at a Day Cost of 4 at every party size. A party that plays after **Vault of Dragons** uses the same rosters at 8th level. The audit applies no +4 for first-turn paralysis, because the prey rule and the entry delay stop the chain.
>
> - **Three combatants:** one Chuul in round 1. A second arrives from the side passage at the start of round 3, or at once if a character enters the side passage.
> - **Four combatants:** two Chuul in round 1.
> - **Five or more combatants:** three Chuul in round 1.
>
> The chuul move through the flood freely, and a chuul that ends its turn fully submerged has total cover until its next turn and surfaces to attack.
>
> #### The Chuul's Tactics
> At the start of combat, the chuul lie submerged beside the plinth. They take no actions until the end of the round after the one in which the last character crosses the threshold. They sense magic within 120 feet, so the party cannot surprise them.
>
> During combat, the chuul:
> - Go for the nearest character, and for the one holding the bark when two are equally near.
> - Grapple with their Pincers and use Paralyzing Tentacles on a Grappled character.
> - Follow the prey rule below.
> - Do not flee when hurt and do not surrender.
>
> The prey rule keeps one turn from ending a character:
> - A chuul that has Paralyzed a creature uses only one Pincer against it on later turns, and the other Pincer goes to a different creature.
> - A chuul never attacks a creature at 0 Hit Points.
> - A second chuul never attacks a creature in the round it is first Paralyzed, and it picks the nearest other creature.
>
> The fight ends when the anchor is destroyed, when every chuul is destroyed, or when the party withdraws. When the anchor falls, the surviving chuul stop attacking and retreat into the lower channel, which counts as driven off. If the party withdraws through the flooded passage, the chuul do not follow, and they keep any damage they took.
>
> **Non-combat route.** Destroying the anchor ends the fight without killing a chuul.
>
> **Counterplay.** *Lesser Restoration* ends the Poisoned condition and so the Paralysis, though the Grapple remains. A Grappled character escapes with a successful **DC 14 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check.

### The Anchor

Reaching the plinth takes a full round of wading, and the chuul stay between the party and the stone until they are driven off or the anchor falls. Read the following aloud when a character stands beside the plinth:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Up close the lump on the plinth is dark as wet slate, and thin veins pulse slowly across its surface. The water around the plinth is perfectly still and very cold. The bark in your hand has gone bright green, and the glow reaches the ceiling.

> [!exploration]**Examining the Anchor**
>
> Any character within 10 feet of the plinth who takes the Study action to examine the anchor makes a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**. A character makes this save once. On a failure the character has the Frightened condition, with the anchor as its source, and marks **Illuun Contact** with the character's name.
>
> - **While Frightened:** the character has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while the anchor is in line of sight, and cannot willingly move closer to it.
> - **Ending it:** the character repeats the save at the end of each of its turns and ends the effect on a success. It ends on its own after 1 minute.
> - **On a success:** the character feels only cold, and nothing is marked.
>
> The Phaulkonmere Ward does not exist yet, so it cannot help with this save. Read the following aloud to a character who fails:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Something very large and very old turns its attention toward you. It does not speak or threaten you, and it notices you with a patience that has no end. Then the attention moves away, and the cold of the water comes back.

A character who holds **Illuun Contact** knows that the thing below is alive, ancient and patient, and that it has noticed that character in particular. If the Stone named Illuun to that character at Full Awakening (unconverted), the character recognises the name now. Otherwise the character knows no name.

> [!exploration]**What the Anchor Is**
>
> Any character who studies the anchor and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana)** check works out that it is a tether of living flesh and that fire or radiance would burn it away where a blade would not. On a failure, Melannor's answer at the oak and the anchor's own reaction still serve. Lesser fire or radiant damage makes the nodule hiss and the bark flare.

> [!hazard]**The Anchor**
>
> The anchor is a Tiny object with AC 12 and 60 hit points. It resists bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage, and it is immune to poison and psychic damage.
>
> - **The 15-damage rule:** a single instance of 15 or more fire or radiant damage destroys it outright, whatever its hit points. One instance is one attack, one spell or one effect, and all its dice are added together. A critical hit doubles the dice. The anchor does not resist fire or radiant damage.
> - **Area spells:** roll the damage once. The anchor automatically fails Dexterity saving throws, so it takes the full roll. A *fireball* (8d6, average 28) destroys it. *Burning hands* at 3d6 (average 10.5) does not, and its damage stays on the anchor's 60 hit points.
> - **Not cumulative:** two instances of 8 fire damage do not add together.
> - **Without a source:** a party with no fire or radiant hit of 15 or more wears the anchor down with weapons. It takes 120 points of weapon damage because of its resistance, which is about four rounds for a party of four while the chuul press.
>
> When the anchor is destroyed, the brood breaks. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The dark lump splits along its veins and shrivels like a leaf in a fire, and the glow of the bark flares and steadies. The chuul stop where they are, turn their feelers toward the east wall and sink into the side passage. The water around the plinth begins to move again, and the cold goes out of it.

A character who holds **Illuun Contact** feels the attention that settled on that character lift and turn away, and the GM marks **Anchor Destroyed**.

### Jeryth Wakes

Jeryth wakes when the anchor breaks, and the party finds the garden changed when it returns. The walk home takes five hours. If the 48 hours have ended and the anchor still stood at that moment, skip to the last readaloud of this scene. Otherwise read the following aloud when the party reaches the gate:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open before you reach it, and the garden inside is older than the street around it. The roses lean toward the sun again, the ivy climbs the wall, and the fox and the heron are gone from the beds. Melannor Fellbranch stands beneath the oldest oak with a new leaf on his palm, and a woman's voice speaks from the leaves, the gravel and the air together.
>
> > "Make sure no one is watching from the water."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor bends and lifts a ring of woven green stems from the roots at the foot of the oak. It is the width of a thumb and still damp with sap.
>
> > "This was not here this morning. I believe she made it for you."
>
> He holds it out to the nearest of you, and the leaves settle overhead as the voice speaks once more.
>
> > "I don't need notice anymore."

If a player asks what either line means, the oak is silent and Melannor says that he does not know. If **Vault of Dragons** has already resolved, Melannor adds that Jeryth speaks of Undermountain now. The next water the party descends to is the water she means.

If the 48 hours ended before the anchor fell, read the following aloud when the party reaches the gate:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The last leaf on the lowest bough of the oak has fallen, and the garden is still. Melannor stands with his palm flat against the trunk and does not turn around.
>
> > "She has gone into the roots. She will wake a tenday after the anchor falls, if it falls."

> [!exploration]**The Phaulkonmere Ward**
>
> The ring belongs to the whole party, not to a member. It is a woven ring of living stems, worn or held by one creature at a time, and passing it takes a Utilize action.
>
> - **Effect:** a creature that wears it on a finger or wrist, or holds it in a hand, has Advantage on saves against being Charmed or Frightened by Aberrations.
> - **Statistics:** the ring has AC 10 and 10 hit points. It can be targeted, and an area effect hits it only if it is worn and the wearer is inside the area.
> - **Fraying:** at 0 hit points it frays and gives no benefit, and a frayed ring that takes any damage is destroyed. A frayed or damaged ring heals fully overnight when laid on a plant growing on the Phaulkonmere estate.
> - **Limits:** it does not stop Paralyzing Tentacles, which Poison and Paralyze and do not Charm or Frighten.

### Debrief at the Oak

Melannor takes the members' report beneath the oak, and companions wait in the outer garden. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor listens with his hands folded and asks four questions, one after another. He asks where the anchor stood, what guarded it, what the bark did and, if any of you touched it, what you felt.

A report is in full when the members answer all four questions. If no character holds **Illuun Contact**, he drops the fourth, and a report that answers the other three is in full.

> [!qna]**What was it?**
>
> > "I do not know what it is called, and Jeryth has not said. It reached up from a long way below, and it stopped when the anchor broke."

> [!qna]**The shell?**
>
> Use this answer if the party brought the shell from the drain.
>
> > "That is a chuul. One came up Gerrick's drain six years ago, and I think it was looking at the city. Whatever sent it has been patient for a long time."

> [!qna]**Is it finished?**
>
> > "The anchor is destroyed, and the cisterns will clear within a tenday. Whatever is below is still there, and Jeryth has not told me that this was the only way it listened."

If the party destroyed the anchor before the forty-eighth hour, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor looks up at the lowest bough of the oak, where a handful of leaves still hang.
>
> > "You were in time. The Enclave thanks you, and the garden will be glad of you whenever you come."

### Renown Opportunities

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Emerald Enclave member gains 4 base Renown for descending to the anchor and reporting to Melannor. Companions gain none, and each bonus can be earned once.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the anchor is destroyed and the chuul are beaten or driven off before the party leaves the chamber.
> - **+1 Renown:** the contact is reported to Melannor in full, as the debrief defines it.
> - **+1 Renown:** the anchor is destroyed inside Jeryth's 48 hours.

### Aftermath

Gerrick learns what came up his drain only if the party tells him. If it does, he swears for a full minute at the Guard and asks the Enclave to send druids to reseal the arch. Jeryth's two lines are the Enclave's last word on the Vault. The Ward stays with the party, and a character who holds **Illuun Contact** is registered to the thing below until Undermountain says otherwise. A party that plays this mission after the Vault hears the same lines, and Jeryth does not explain them.

### Concluding the Event

The Event ends when Melannor has taken the report. Record the anchor and the contact separately, because the party can destroy the anchor and leave every character untouched.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Anchor Destroyed** — mark when the anchor is destroyed by any means; read by **Master of the Wild**, where the commission depends on it. It is also read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted, arc-j line 47), where Jeryth needs no notice and the ward stays active. If this mission has not run, the Vault keeps its own notice rule.
> - **Illuun Contact** — mark with the character's name for each character who fails the examine save, member or not; read by **Master of the Wild**. It is also read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), where Melannor notes that the character is registered. Dungeon of the Mad Mage reads it too, and the character is sensed on approach to Level 4.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>

## Overview

The party fights the brood that guards a dark anchor in a flooded chamber and decides what to do with the anchor. Back at Phaulkonmere, Jeryth speaks again.

## Summary

We fought the chuul around a dark anchor beneath the Castle Ward and cut it loose from whatever dreams below. Jeryth woke and told us to watch the water, and Melannor gave us a ring of living stems. We still do not know what the dreamer is called, or whether it listens another way.
