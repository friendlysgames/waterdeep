# The Dreamer's Reach

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat/Exploration Event begins when Melannor summons the party to Phaulkonmere. In this Event:
>
> - Jeryth has been silent for three days; Melannor believes she is actively resisting Illuun's psychic pressure through the disrupted cistern channel.
> - The party descends through Gerrick's tunnel again, deeper than Mission 5, into flooded lower cisterns.
> - Entering the deepest section requires a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw; failure imposes the Dazed condition for one round as Illuun registers their presence.
> - Two Chuul occupy the deepest chamber and attack on sight; flooded floor is Difficult Terrain in deep sections.
> - The abolethic anchor is embedded in the chamber floor; destroying it requires 15+ fire or radiant damage in a single hit.
> - Examining the anchor before destroying it requires a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw; failure inflicts Frightened and leaves the character personally registered to Illuun.
> - When the party returns to Phaulkonmere, Jeryth speaks and bestows the Phaulkonmere ward.

**Background (DM only)**
**Illuun** is an abolethic dreamer anchored to Undermountain Level 4. It has been extending psychic reach upward through Waterdeep's underground water table for decades, barely perceptible. The Stone of Golorr's activation created a resonance Illuun responded to, pushing more actively toward the surface. Jeryth, connected to Phaulkonmere's root systems and water table, has been quietly absorbing and deflecting this influence for months. Mission 5's disruption of the cistern system opened a path directly into the underground channel Jeryth uses, and Illuun has been pressing through that path for three days.

Jeryth is winning. But the effort consumes her ability to communicate, and she has 48 hours before she breaks the connection or withdraws too deep into Phaulkonmere's earth to surface for months — which means she would not be available to ward the vault opening in **Vault of Dragons**.

Destroying the anchor severs Illuun's reach through this specific path. It does not harm Illuun, which is too far below to be touched by this action. Illuun has now registered whoever disturbed its surface operations. Any party member who received direct psychic contact is personally known to it.

### The Garden at Dusk

Melannor is standing in Phaulkonmere's garden when the party arrives, facing the eastern wall. The plants are growing normally, but without direction — no tilt toward sunlight, no lean toward Jeryth's attention. The birds in the estate oaks are roosting facing outward.

> > "She's still there. I can feel her presence in the estate. She's fighting something." He turns. "The disturbance in the ground beneath the Castle Ward — it's stronger than it's been in months. Mission 5 cleared the Splinter's contamination. That should have quieted it. Instead it got louder." He meets the party's eyes. "I think whatever you disturbed down there noticed you. And I think you need to go back and finish it."

He provides two things: a scroll of *Water Breathing* if the party needs it, and a piece of Phaulkonmere bark. He holds the bark out carefully. "Jeryth managed that much. It glows in the anchor's presence. You'll know when you're close."

### The Descent

Through Gerrick's tunnel again, to the cistern access — then further down. A passage sealed with fitted stones, removable by hand, leads below the Mission 5 level. The air is colder here, and the water is moving, fed by underground sources the upper level doesn't reach.

The deeper cisterns are flooded: knee-depth in most sections, chest-depth in others. Movement through the deep sections is Difficult Terrain. The abolethic influence is present from the moment the party descends — not a combat effect, but a quality of wrongness, the way a space feels when something very old and very patient has occupied it for a long time.

**DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** when the party enters the deepest section. On a failure: the Dazed condition for one round as Illuun's passive awareness registers the intrusion.

### The Deepest Chamber

Two **Chuul** servants have occupied this chamber for years, drawn by proximity to the anchor. They attack on sight without hesitation or communication.

The flooded floor restricts movement — knee- to chest-depth water, uneven stone underfoot. Effective ranged attacks from the chamber entry are possible while the chuul must wade through the middle distance.

#### The Anchor

Embedded in the chamber floor, submerged, in a section that has not been dry for decades: a dark irregular nodule, fist-sized, slightly warm, faintly iridescent when the Phaulkonmere bark's glow touches it. Surrounded by a quality of stillness that comes from something that has been in one place without moving for a very long time.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Examining the Anchor
> If a character examines the anchor before destroying it, they make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure: a flash of psychic contact — not communication, not control, awareness. Something immense and patient and very old, noticing them. Not hostile. Not welcoming. Registering.
>
> Mechanically: the **Frightened** condition (**DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** to end at the start of each turn) for up to one minute. When it ends, the character knows with certainty that what they felt is alive, is below, and has registered them personally. They do not know its name. They know its age and its patience.
>
> This registration matters for Undermountain: if this character descends to Undermountain Level 4, Illuun is aware of their presence before they reach it.

**Destroying the anchor:** Any weapon or spell dealing fire or radiant damage, minimum 15 damage in a single hit, severs it. The nodule dissolves into the water. The water clears. The quality of wrongness leaves the chamber.

### Jeryth Wakes

When the party returns to Phaulkonmere, Jeryth is speaking. Her voice carries the quality of something restored — quieter than usual, spent but present, the way a person sounds after a sustained effort has finally ended.

> > "You went below. I felt it when it broke." A pause. "Thank you."

She does not ask what they saw. She says once, without elaboration:

> > "It is still there. Further below. It will not reach me again through that path." Another pause. "But the vault — when the Stone opens it — make sure no one is watching from the water."

She falls silent again, the natural silence of someone present and attentive.

She creates a ward — a small woven ring of Phaulkonmere root — and gives it to the party as a group artifact. Any character holding it has advantage on saving throws against the Charmed and Frightened conditions caused by aberrations while the ward is intact (10 HP, AC 10; repaired by contact with any Phaulkonmere plant overnight).

She says one more thing, once:

> > "I'll be ready when you call for the vault. I don't need notice anymore."

### Concluding the Event

Record whether any party member experienced direct psychic contact with Illuun by examining the anchor before destroying it. This flag is read in Undermountain Level 4: if True, any PC who received the contact is personally registered to Illuun and will be sensed on approach to Level 4.

## Overview

Jeryth Phaulkon has fallen silent. Something below the Castle Ward has found a path through the cisterns and is pressing against her.

## Read Aloud

> Melannor is standing in Phaulkonmere's garden when you arrive. The plants grow normally, but without direction. The birds in the estate oaks are roosting facing outward, which he later describes as wrong in a way he cannot fully explain.
>
> > "She's still there. I can feel her presence in the estate. She's fighting something." He turns. "I think whatever you disturbed down there noticed you. And I think you need to go back and finish it."

## Summary

The party descended into the deepest level of the Castle Ward cisterns, destroyed a centuries-old abolethic anchor point in Illuun's surface network, and freed Jeryth Phaulkon from the psychic hold on her connection to the estate. On their return, Jeryth gave the party a Phaulkonmere ward — a woven root ring providing advantage against aberration Charmed and Frightened conditions — and confirmed she was already prepared for **Vault of Dragons**.
