# The Dreamer's Reach

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat/Exploration Event begins when Melannor summons the party to Phaulkonmere. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Melannor in the garden and observe the estate's wrong silence firsthand
> - Descend through Gerrick's tunnel again, deeper than Mission 5, into flooded lower cisterns
> - Navigate the flooded deepest section and resist Illuun's passive psychic awareness
> - Defeat two chuul guarding the anchor chamber
> - Destroy the anchor and sever Illuun's surface reach
> - Return to the garden and receive Jeryth's ward and her confirmation for **Vault of Dragons**
>
> #### Background
>
> Illuun is an abolethic dreamer anchored to Undermountain Level 4. It has been extending psychic reach upward through Waterdeep's underground water table for decades, barely perceptible at the surface. The Stone of Golorr's activation created a resonance Illuun responded to, pushing more actively toward the surface. Jeryth, connected to Phaulkonmere's root systems and the city's water table, has been absorbing and deflecting this influence for months. Mission 5's disruption of the cistern system opened a path directly into the underground channel Jeryth uses, and Illuun has been pressing through that path for three days.
>
> Jeryth is winning. But the effort consumes her ability to communicate, and she has 48 hours before she breaks the connection or withdraws too deep into Phaulkonmere's earth to surface for months — which means she would not be present for **Vault of Dragons**.
>
> Destroying the anchor severs Illuun's reach through this specific path. It does not harm Illuun, which is too far below to be touched by this action. Illuun has now registered whoever disturbed its surface operations. Any party member who experienced direct psychic contact is personally known to it.

### The Brief

Melannor is standing in Phaulkonmere's garden when the party arrives. He is facing the eastern wall.

> [!readaloud]
> Phaulkonmere's garden looks correct from the gate: the plants are growing, the beds are whole, the estate is alive and tended. Something is still wrong. The plants grow without direction, without the lean toward sunlight and toward Jeryth's attention that you have learned to read as normal here. The birds in the estate oaks are roosting facing outward, all of them.
>
> Melannor turns when he hears you. He looks like a man who has been standing in the same place for a long time.
>
> > "She's still there. I can feel her presence in the estate. She's fighting something." He turns back toward the eastern wall for a moment, then faces you again. "The disturbance in the ground beneath the Castle Ward — it's stronger than it was. Mission 5 should have quieted it. Instead it got louder." He meets your eyes. "I think whatever you disturbed down there noticed you. And I think you need to go back and finish it."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: an Emerald Enclave druid who manages Phaulkonmere's grounds and is, for the first time, afraid of something below the earth in a way he cannot categorize.
>
> Melannor knows Jeryth is still present in the estate. He does not know what she is fighting, or how long she can hold. He has been standing in the garden since this morning, feeling the silence where her attention should be, and he is out of alternatives. He holds everything he has to give in his hands: the bark and the scroll.
>
> His voice does not change. It never changes. But he is shorter in his answers than usual, and he does not pause for effect.
>
> Conversation topics Melannor is willing to discuss include:
> - What he knows about Jeryth's situation and the time constraint
> - What the Phaulkonmere bark does
> - Whether the party needs the Water Breathing scroll
> - What he expects them to find down there

> [!qna]**What does the bark do?**
>
> He holds it out. It is a small piece, no larger than a thumb.
>
> > "Jeryth managed that much — she sent it through the estate this morning while I was standing here. It glows in the anchor's presence. You will know when you are close."

> [!qna]**How long does she have?**
>
> > "Forty-eight hours. If she breaks the connection herself in that time, she withdraws too deep to surface again before **Vault of Dragons**. If you destroy whatever is sustaining that connection from below, she can come back." A pause. "Forty-eight hours is an estimate. It may be less."

> [!qna]**What are we looking for?**
>
> > "Go past the Mission 5 level. There is a passage deeper. Follow the bark. Destroy what the bark points you toward." He looks at you steadily. "Come back."

Melannor also provides a *scroll of Water Breathing* if the party wants it. He does not say whether it will be needed.

Once the party is ready, proceed to **The Descent**.

### The Descent

The route is familiar through Trollgate and the south Undercliff terraces to Gerrick's tunnel, and then down through the brick-lined passage to the cistern access. This time the party goes further.

> [!readaloud]
> Gerrick's tunnel is unchanged: brick-lined, wet, cold, two hundred feet of it before the cistern access opens up. The three channels branch in their usual directions, and the eastern one is clean now, the water clear and smelling of nothing but stone. But there is a fourth passage at the back of the cistern access that you did not use before — fitted stones in the wall, removable by hand, sealing off a stairway leading down into cold air and the sound of moving water from below.
>
> The deeper cisterns are flooded. Knee-depth in most sections, chest-depth in others, the water clear but the stone floor uneven underfoot. Moving through the deep sections is Difficult Terrain. The place changes as you descend, and it is hard to say how: there is no new smell and no new sound, only a growing feeling of wrongness, the way a space feels when something very old and very patient has occupied it without moving for a very long time.

> [!gamemaster]**Illuun's Passive Awareness**
>
> When the party enters the deepest flooded section, each character makes a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the character is Dazed for one round as Illuun's passive awareness registers the intrusion — not a targeted attack, but the sensation of being noticed by something vast that was not previously paying attention.

The Phaulkonmere bark glows when the party enters this section. It brightens as they move east.

### The Deepest Chamber

The anchor chamber is at the far end of the deepest section. The bark is fully lit when the party reaches the entrance.

> [!readaloud]
> The chamber at the end of the passage opens wider than the tunnels above it, the ceiling higher, the floor submerged under a foot of water in the shallows and chest-deep toward the center. The bark in your hand is bright now, glowing evenly, and it points toward the chamber floor near the center of the space.
>
> Two large crustacean shapes occupy the shallow ledges to either side of the entrance, still and watchful in the way of things that have held their position in the same space for a long time. When they register the party, they move.

Two **Chuul** have occupied this chamber for years, drawn by proximity to the anchor. They attack on sight without hesitation or communication.

> [!hazard]**Two Chuul**
>
> Both chuul use the **Chuul** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*).
>
> #### Chuul Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, both chuul move from the shallow ledges toward the nearest characters. They attack whoever enters the water first.
>
> Over the course of combat, the chuul will prioritize:
> - Grappling characters and using their Tentacles to potentially Stun them
> - Positioning in the deep central section where the flooded floor creates Difficult Terrain for the party
> - Staying between the party and the anchor
>
> The battle ends when both chuul are destroyed or driven from the chamber.
>
> #### Terrain
>
> The shallow ledges along the chamber walls are normal terrain. The central floor is flooded knee- to chest-depth; movement through it is Difficult Terrain. Effective ranged attacks from the chamber entry are possible while the chuul must wade through the middle distance to reach the party.

> [!exploration]**The Anchor**
>
> The anchor is embedded in the chamber floor in the deep central section, submerged, in a spot that has not been dry for decades. Any character who enters the deep section can see it once the combat is resolved.
>
> > A dark, irregular nodule, fist-sized, sits lodged into the stone of the chamber floor, slightly warm against the cold water surrounding it, faintly iridescent when the Phaulkonmere bark's glow reaches it. The quality of stillness in the water around it is the stillness of something that has been in one place without moving for a very long time.

> [!gamemaster]**Examining the Anchor**
>
> If a character examines the anchor before destroying it, they make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure: the Frightened condition (DC 15 Wisdom saving throw to end at the start of each turn) for up to one minute. When the condition ends, the character knows with certainty that what they felt is alive, is below, and has registered them personally. They do not know its name. They know its age and its patience.
>
> This is load-bearing. Mark the **Illuun Contact** outcome for each character who failed the saving throw.

Destroying the anchor requires any weapon or spell dealing fire or radiant damage, minimum 15 damage in a single hit. The nodule dissolves into the water. The water clears. The quality of wrongness leaves the chamber.

### Jeryth Wakes

When the party returns to Phaulkonmere, the garden is different. The birds have turned from the wall. The plants are leaning.

> [!readaloud]
> Phaulkonmere's garden is itself again when you come through the gate: the plants leaning toward the last light, the birds in the oaks facing inward. Melannor is standing near the eastern wall and he looks at you once and lets out a breath. Then a voice comes from the garden, quieter than usual, spent but present, the way a person sounds after a long sustained effort has finally ended.
>
> > "You went below. I felt it when it broke."
>
> A pause.
>
> > "Thank you."

Jeryth speaks again without being asked.

> [!readaloud]
> > "It is still there. Further below. It will not reach me again through that path." Another pause, longer. "But the vault — when the Stone opens it — make sure no one is watching from the water."
>
> She falls silent, and the silence is the natural kind: someone present and attentive, not absent.
>
> A small ring of woven root appears at the edge of the nearest herb bed. She makes it without ceremony.

The woven root ring is a group artifact: the **Phaulkonmere Ward**. While any character carries it, that character has Advantage on saving throws against the Charmed and Frightened conditions caused by aberrations. The ward has 10 HP and AC 10. It repairs itself overnight if left in contact with any Phaulkonmere plant.

Jeryth adds one more thing.

> [!readaloud]
> > "I'll be ready when you call for the vault. I don't need notice anymore."

### Concluding the Event

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Anchor Destroyed** — mark when the anchor is destroyed and both chuul neutralized before the party leaves the cisterns. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (Jeryth is present and ready; her wards are already prepared when the vault opens).
> - **Illuun Contact** — mark for each party member who failed the DC 15 Wisdom saving throw when examining the anchor. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (Jeryth references the contact in the vault) and any Undermountain Level 4 content (characters marked here are personally registered to Illuun; it senses their approach before they reach it).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> **+1 Renown** if the **Anchor Destroyed** outcome is marked and both chuul are neutralized before the party leaves the cisterns.
> **+1 Renown** if any party member's **Illuun Contact** experience is reported to Melannor in full detail. He records it and sends the account to Jeryth. She references it in **Vault of Dragons**. Characters who received the contact are also now personally registered to Illuun.
>
> Jeryth is ready for **Vault of Dragons**. She has already prepared her wards. The party does not need to give her advance notice — she is listening and will be present when they call.
>
> If the party reports what they perceived during the anchor examination to Mirt (Harpers): the connection between the Stone of Golorr and Illuun's reach becomes concrete intelligence. Mirt goes quiet for a long moment. Three days later a paper bird arrives at Trollskull Manor with a single line: *"That name is Illuun. Don't say it below the cisterns."*

## Overview

Jeryth Phaulkon has fallen silent. Something below the Castle Ward has found a path through the cisterns and is pressing against her.

## Summary

The party descended into the deepest level of the Castle Ward cisterns, destroyed a centuries-old abolethic anchor point in Illuun's surface network, and freed Jeryth Phaulkon from the psychic hold on her connection to the estate. On their return, Jeryth gave the party a Phaulkonmere ward — a woven root ring providing Advantage against aberration Charmed and Frightened conditions — and confirmed she was already prepared for **Vault of Dragons**.
