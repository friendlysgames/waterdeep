# Design Notes: The Dreamer's Reach

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C stops at the fourth Emerald Enclave mission, so this mission is a repo invention. Its seeds are Illuun on Undermountain Level 4, who keeps pet chuul in Dungeon of the Mad Mage, and the Vault of Dragons line that has Jeryth ready without notice. The restored draft was one event with a "Dazed" entry save and a chuul that "Stuns". It also had a True or False flag for the contact and a Mirt epilogue that named Illuun.

## What Changed

The mission is two events. **The Descent** carries the 48-hour clock, the three routes to the chamber and the entry save, and it marks nothing. **The Anchor** carries the fight, the anchor and the wake. The entry save now leaves a character Incapacitated until the end of its next turn, and the chuul wait a round so the save never lands on an attack. The chuul Paralyze, and a prey rule replaces the +4 that would make the fight impossible. The anchor has statistics and a 15-damage rule, with a weapon route for parties that have no fire or radiance. Rosters follow party size and hold at 8th level after the Vault.

## Departures from the Source

The anchor and Jalester Silvermane are two separate routes of Illuun's reach. Destroying the anchor does not end the link through Jalester, so the Harper and Lords' Alliance sixth missions still run as written. Mirt's epilogue is cut, because the Harper mission has Mirt not know the name. The name now reaches a character only if the Stone named it, and Melannor and Jeryth say that something dreams below. Jeryth's two lines are unchanged.

The six-years-ago question from **The Undercliff Scarecrows** is answered by the chuul scout, the scorched seal and the shell. The mission follows the Force Grey precedent, so it runs at 7th level after Kolat Towers, and a three-heist party plays it after the Vault.
