# The Descent

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration Event occurs after **Kolat Towers** and after **The Fouled Channel**. It begins when an individual Emerald Enclave member reaches Renown 13 and 7th level and the party sees the sign in the Trollskull Manor courtyard. It ends when the last character has crossed into the deepest chamber and made the entry save. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Melannor Fellbranch explain that Jeryth Phaulkon has been silent for three days and has 48 hours left.
> - Take a *spell scroll* of *water breathing* and a strip of glowing bark.
> - Go down Gerrick Thatchley's drain and find the marks of what came up it six years ago.
> - Find the anchor's chamber by three routes, which are the bark, the current in the lower cisterns and the cutting left in **The Fouled Channel**.
> - Cross a flooded passage with the scroll or by swimming, and feel the pressure at the threshold of the deepest chamber.
>
> Melannor briefs only the Emerald Enclave members. Companions wait in the outer garden for the brief, hear it from the members and go down with them.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Illuun, an aboleth dreaming on Undermountain Level 4, has pushed a fragment of itself up the conduit that Jeryth's cutting opened in **The Fouled Channel**. The fragment is the anchor, and it sits on a stone plinth in a chamber below the cisterns.
> - Jeryth cannot speak and resist at once, so she has been silent for three days. In 48 hours she withdraws into the roots, and the clock starts when Melannor finishes the brief.
> - Melannor reads the clock from the oldest oak. Its lowest bough holds a fixed number of living leaves, and they fall at an even rate.
> - Nobody in this Event names Illuun. Melannor and Jeryth say only that something dreams below, and Melannor does not know what it is called.
> - The chuul in the deepest chamber are Illuun's brood. A chuul scout of the same brood came up Gerrick's drain six years ago, and his neighbours drove it back with fire before they sealed the arch, so Gerrick never learned what it was.
> - The anchor is one of two routes that Illuun uses. The other runs through Jalester Silvermane at the Yawning Portal's well (**The Stone's Other Master**, **An Audience with the Open Lord**). Nobody in this Event knows about it.
> - Nobody in this Event says Manshoon's name, or calls Kolat Towers his home, unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member being addressed. Melannor says "the Splinter" and does not mention the towers.

### The Brief

No animal brings this mission. The sign is the oak in the northeast corner of the Trollskull Manor courtyard, which Melannor's druids asked the party to leave alone. Every Enclave member knows the oak is Melannor's business, and a member who marked **Silence Warning Given** knows why. Read the following aloud on the morning after a member meets the gate:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Morning light reaches the courtyard of Trollskull Manor, and the oak in the northeast corner has changed overnight. Green shoots have burst from the bark in every direction, some pointing down into the cobbles and some back into the trunk. The silverbark has split in long strips along the lowest limb, and a sparrow sits on the limb with its head turned toward the south. It does not move when you step beneath the branches.

The walk from Trollskull Alley to Phaulkonmere takes about two hours. Read the following aloud when the party reaches the gate:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gate of Phaulkonmere stands open before you reach it, and no one is near to have opened it. The garden inside is older than the street around it, and every plant in it has lost its direction. Roses lean away from the sun, and ivy climbs down the wall instead of up it. A fox, a heron and a dozen sparrows sit among the beds with their faces turned toward the oldest oak, and no voice moves the leaves. Melannor Fellbranch waits beneath the oak with his hands folded and mud on his knees, and he says each of your names before you reach him.
>
> > "Good morning, and thank you for coming. I did not send for you, because no animal will leave the garden while Jeryth is silent."
>
> He touches the oak with two fingers, and a leaf from its lowest bough drops into his palm.
>
> > "Jeryth has not spoken for three days. The soil is uneasy, and I believe she is holding something back with all her strength. There are enough leaves left on this bough for forty-eight hours, and after that she will withdraw into the roots."

If **Silence Warning Given** is marked for a member, Melannor says this to that member before he goes on:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Jeryth warned you that her voice was failing. I am sorry that it has failed completely."

If **Jeryth's Message Received** is marked, he adds this:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "She asked to be told if anything below answered. It has answered her instead, and she cannot spare the breath to speak."

Then read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Melannor takes a rolled scroll and a strip of bark the length of a hand from his satchel. The bark glows a soft green in his palm, though the morning in the garden is bright.
>
> > "The scroll is *water breathing*, and the bark is oak that Jeryth cut on the first night. It will brighten as you come near what holds her."

> [!social]**The Groundskeeper Without Rest**
>
> Melannor Fellbranch (Neutral Good, Half-Elf, he/him) :: the humourless groundskeeper of Phaulkonmere, who has not slept in three days and has not changed his voice.
>
> Melannor speaks in plain, complete sentences at an even pace, and he does not use contractions, recognise a joke or swear. He keeps one hand on the oak while he talks. His sentences trail off when he speaks of what is below, which is the only sign of how afraid he is for Jeryth.
>
> Melannor is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What has happened to Jeryth, and what the forty-eight hours mean.
> - The scroll, the bark and the way down.
> - What the animals will and will not do.
> - Why he stays in the garden.
>
> He does not know what dreams below, and he says so once. He will not discuss the vault gold or the Splinter's master, and he turns each aside with "That is not mine to speak of."

> [!qna]**Where is Jeryth?**
>
> > "She is here, in the roots and the water, and nothing can harm her in this state. She can be lost, and that is what I fear."

> [!qna]**What is below?**
>
> Melannor's hand stays on the oak, and his voice slows.
>
> > "Jeryth says that something dreams below the Castle Ward. When she worked in the cistern, she opened the water to herself. I think that whatever dreams there has found the door, and I..."
>
> He stops, and then he continues in the same level voice.
>
> > "I do not know what it is called, and I would like to."

> [!qna]**How do we find it?**
>
> > "The bark will brighten as you near it. The lower channels are cold, and the water in them moves, though I cannot say in which direction."

> [!qna]**And when we find it?**
>
> > "Jeryth left no instruction before she went silent. Oak fears fire, and I believe that a great deal of fire or light at once would unmake it. I would try that before I tried a blade."

> [!qna]**Which way is down?**
>
> > "The cistern doors in the Castle Ward have flooded to the roof in three days. Gerrick Thatchley's drain runs higher, and it is still dry enough to walk. It leads west from the Undercliff to the chamber where the fouled channel begins, and behind the dais there is a passage closed with fitted stones."

> [!qna]**Why can't you come?**
>
> > "Someone must stay beside the oak, because Jeryth will wake toward the garden. The garden needs a person in it when she does, and I will be here when you return."

> [!qna]**What do we get?**
>
> > "The Enclave does not pay in coin, and I will not pretend that it does. Your standing with us will grow, and Jeryth will remember what you did."

> [!gamemaster]**The Forty-Eight Hours**
>
> The clock starts when Melannor finishes the brief and stops when the anchor is destroyed, and the times below are the only ones that count:
>
> - **Walking:** the walk between Phaulkonmere and the outlet in the Undercliff takes two hours each way.
> - **Underground:** the tunnel and the cistern take one hour, and the way from the cistern to the deepest chamber takes three.
> - **Mistakes:** each failed routing check below costs one hour.
> - **Rest:** a Long Rest costs eight hours.
> - **The end:** if the anchor still stands when the forty-eighth hour ends, Jeryth withdraws into the roots. The anchor can still be destroyed afterwards, and she wakes at the oak a tenday later.

### Gerrick's Drain

The farm road runs through the Undercliff terraces to the milestone. The outlet of the drain lies below the southern terrace, where the cliff path meets the city wall. If **Drainage Tunnel Learned** is marked, the party already knows the place. Otherwise Melannor's answer and the farm road lead the party to it, and the outlet is in plain sight without a check.

Gerrick is at the outlet because the stream below the drain has run cold for three days. His ewes will not drink from it. The seal has one of two states, and the soot and the shell are in the first ten feet of brick in both.

If the party opened the seal in **The Fouled Channel**, the grate hangs on new hinges under a padlock. Gerrick opens it with his key, and Melannor holds the spare. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The brick arch below the southern terrace is closed by an iron grate on bright new hinges, with a padlock through the hasp. Cold water seeps from the foot of the grate and trails down to the stream, where a line of ewes stands on the far bank and refuses to drink. Gerrick Thatchley sits on the milestone with a key in his fist. He gets up when he sees you coming down the road.
>
> > "The stream's been cold for three days, and my ewes won't touch it. Melannor's lot always turn up when something's wrong, so I take it you're going in."
>
> He turns the key in the padlock and swings the grate wide.

If the party never opened the seal, because it used the dead edge or the Trades Ward well, read the following aloud instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The brick arch below the southern terrace is filled with mortar and an iron grate gone brown with rust. Cold water seeps from the foot of the grate and trails down to the stream, where a line of ewes stands on the far bank and refuses to drink. Gerrick Thatchley sits on the milestone with a crowbar across his knees. He gets up when he sees you coming down the road.
>
> > "The stream's been cold for three days, and my ewes won't touch it. Melannor's lot always turn up when something's wrong, so I take it you're going in."
>
> He sets the crowbar into the seam of the grate and heaves, and the grate comes away with a long shriek of rust.

> [!social]**The Farmer at the Milestone**
>
> Gerrick Thatchley (Neutral, Lightfoot Halfling, he/him) :: a halfling farmer of about fifty who trusts the Enclave more than the Guard. He has watched his land go strange for three days.
>
> Gerrick swears freely at the Guard and at the cold stream, and he is gruff but kind to anyone who has come to help. He will not go into the tunnel himself, because his knees and his farm are staying where they are.
>
> Gerrick is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What came up the drain six years ago, which he never saw.
> - The cold stream and the ewes.
> - Whether the party wants the grate left open behind it.
>
> He will not discuss the Guard without swearing, and he does not guess at what the creature was.

> [!qna]**What came up the drain?**
>
> Gerrick spits into the stubble before he answers.
>
> > "I never saw it, because the neighbours came running with pitch and straw and burned it back down the tunnel before I got here. We bricked the arch the next morning. Nobody has ever told me what it was, and I stopped asking."

> [!qna]**Anything odd about the water?**
>
> > "It's cold, and it shouldn't be, because the stream's fed from the sun side of the hill. Something's wrong underneath it, and I don't need a druid to tell me that."

> [!qna]**Should we close the grate behind us?**
>
> > "Leave it open, and I'll stand here until you're back. The fucking Guard wouldn't notice if the whole hill fell in, so somebody has to watch."

The first ten feet of the tunnel carry the marks of that night.

> [!exploration]**What Came Up the Drain**
>
> Black soot streaks the brick arch, and the old seal bears a long burn that no cooking fire would leave. Any character who looks inside sees the soot without a check.
>
> - Any character who searches the old seal and makes a successful **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check finds a curved piece of grey-green shell wedged in a crack. It is ridged like a crab's and as long as a forearm.
> - Any character who examines the shell and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Nature)** check recognises the tip of a chuul's pincer. A chuul is an aberration that grapples its prey and paralyzes it.
> - **On a failure:** Melannor names the shell at the debrief, and nothing in this Event depends on the result.
>
> The shell is the tip of the scout's pincer, broken off six years ago, and nothing in the tunnel is hostile.

### The Cistern

The tunnel runs west for about half a mile and arrives at the chamber at the head of the western channel from **The Fouled Channel**. The chamber is about ten feet across, with a raised dais around its edge and a grated sluice in the floor. The water in the lower cisterns has risen since the party last came, which is why the city doors are flooded. The film comes from the western channel, and the cutting that Jeryth rooted in the sluice is the only thing in the water that is green.

If **Cache Destroyed** is marked, a cutting was planted in **The Fouled Channel**. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Your light finds a round chamber of old stone about ten feet across, with a raised dais around its edge. The water that slides out of the western channel is clear and black over the grated sluice, and a green cutting trails long white roots from the sluice bars. A faint smell of rain hangs in the air.

If **Cache Destroyed** is not marked, no cutting was planted. Read the following aloud instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Your light finds a round chamber of old stone about ten feet across, with a raised dais around its edge. The water that slides out of the western channel gathers over the grated sluice under a skin of oily green and violet. A sweet, flat smell hangs in the air.

Three stones in the wall behind the dais are fitted without mortar, and the party did not look for them in **The Fouled Channel**. They lift out by hand, one at a time, and no check is needed. Behind them a stair of about thirty steps drops into the cold, and the bark in the party's hands brightens a little with every step. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stair ends in a flooded hall with six arched channels, and the water around your knees is moving. It drifts slowly past your legs toward the arches, and the cold of it climbs through your boots. The bark glows brighter in your hand.

If a cutting was planted, white roots also creep along the wall beside the steps and vanish into the water.

### Three Ways to the Chamber

Only one of the six channels leads to the anchor. The party needs one of the routes open to it, and a failed check never ends the search.

> [!exploration]**Finding the Right Channel**
>
> - **The bark.** The bark brightens when a character carries it toward the right channel and dims in the others. This route needs no check. A party that loses the bark in the water still has the other two routes.
> - **The current.** Any character who studies the water and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Survival)** check sees that every channel drifts, very slowly, toward one arch. On a failure the party tries two wrong channels and loses 1 hour before the dead ends show the right one.
> - **The conduit.** The roots of Jeryth's cutting have crept down the stair and into one channel, because that was the path the thing below followed. If **Cache Destroyed** is marked, the cutting was planted and the roots lie plain on the stone. They lead the party to the right channel without a check. If it is not marked, no cutting was planted and this route is unavailable, so the bark and the current remain.

### The Flooded Passage

The right channel runs level for a hundred feet, and then the roof comes down to meet the water. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The roof of the channel slopes down until the black water touches it, and the passage runs on beneath the surface for thirty feet. At the far end an arch of fitted stone glows green, lit by the bark in your hand. The current pulls gently at your legs, and every drift of silt settles toward the arch.

> [!exploration]**Crossing the Flooded Passage**
>
> - **With the scroll:** a character who can cast *water breathing* (a druid, ranger, sorcerer or wizard) reads the scroll as a Magic action while the party stands within 30 feet. Up to ten willing creatures breathe water for 24 hours, and everyone crosses at normal speed with no check. The effect ends before a party that rests twice returns. That party swims home.
> - **Swimming:** a character without the effect makes a **DC 12 Strength (Athletics)** check to swim the thirty feet. On a failure the current carries the character back to the near end, the attempt costs 10 minutes, and the character can try again.
>
> If no character can read the scroll, the swim is the only way across, and Melannor's gift stays in the pack.

### The Threshold

The party crosses one character at a time, and the chuul stay out of sight below the surface. Read the following aloud as the first character climbs out of the water:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The passage rises out of the water into a wide round chamber, and the bark throws green light across a ceiling of fitted stone. The air is cold and utterly still. As each of you steps through the arch, a slow pressure gathers behind your eyes, and your thoughts begin to thin.

If a character fails the save below, read the following aloud to that character:

> [!readaloud]
>
> For a moment you cannot lift your arms or find a word, and then your thoughts come back slowly.

> [!hazard]**The Pressure at the Threshold**
>
> Each character makes a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** on crossing the threshold of the deepest chamber, once per crossing. On a failure the character has the Incapacitated condition until the end of that character's next turn. A character who steps back out of the chamber and crosses again makes the save again.
>
> Roll initiative when the first character crosses. The chuul lie submerged and take no actions until the end of the round after the one in which the last character crosses. The save therefore never lands on top of an attack, and the effect deals no damage.

### Renown Opportunities

Nothing is awarded here, because Mission Renown is paid at the end of **The Anchor**.

### Aftermath

The party can turn back at any point, and the anchor stays where it is. The clock does not stop while the party rests or argues, and Gerrick keeps the grate open for as long as the party is below.

### Concluding the Event

The Event ends when the last character has crossed the threshold and made the entry save, and the GM carries forward who is Incapacitated and who holds the bark.

## Overview

Jeryth Phaulkon has gone silent, and Melannor Fellbranch sends the party down Gerrick's drain to find what is pressing on her. The way leads through the cisterns to a flooded chamber.

## Summary

We found Melannor in a garden that had lost its sense of the sun, and he told us Jeryth had been silent for three days. We went down Gerrick's drain with a scroll and a glowing piece of bark, and we crossed a flooded passage into a chamber that pressed on our minds. We still do not know what waits beside the water.
