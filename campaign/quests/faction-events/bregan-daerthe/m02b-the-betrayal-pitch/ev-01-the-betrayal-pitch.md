# The Betrayal Pitch

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration Event is a rigged test: Jarlaxle is evaluating the party on shipboard infiltration, their response to unexpected complications, and whether they will take a job without sufficient information. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from J.B. Nevercott at the Yawning Portal and ask him what he knows
> - Choose among three approach routes to the *Eyecatcher*, each with different risk profiles
> - Navigate past the ship's crew, who are placed to make the approach feel earned without stopping anyone
> - Discover the *Ledger of Commissioned Automata* in the stateroom and decide whether to take it
> - See Zardoz Zord boarding early, and decide what to make of his smile
>
> #### Background
>
> There is no obsidian compass. Jarlaxle invented it. The *Eyecatcher* is his ship, Zardoz Zord is his persona, and the crew are his people, running at half complement and under orders not to physically intercept anyone who reaches the stateroom corridor. They will make themselves visible and move toward sounds, but they will not block the corridor or use force.
>
> The *Ledger of Commissioned Automata* is open on the chart table to the most recent entries — Jarlaxle's complete record of every Nimblewright sold, with purchaser names, delivery addresses, and programming notes. The voided Cassalanter entry is visible on the open page. Jarlaxle placed it there. He wants to see if the party is smart enough to take the thing they were not asked to take.
>
> Zardoz Zord boards early by design, so the party sees his face at the moment they are most committed to a clean exit. This is not a threat. It is information he wants them to have.

### The Brief

J.B. Nevercott finds the party at the Yawning Portal. Same booth. Same coat. He has already ordered.

> [!readaloud]
> The man in the expensive overcoat is at a booth when you arrive, and he has already ordered a round. He has one eye covered by a silk patch, and he greets the first of you through the door with the unhurried warmth of someone who planned to be here first and is pleased the arrangement worked out.
>
> > "Good. I was hoping it would be you."
>
> He gestures to the open seats.
>
> > "I have a retrieval job. There's a vessel in the harbor — the *Eyecatcher*, the Sea Maidens Faire flagship. Captain Zardoz Zord's personal ship. I need an item retrieved from his stateroom this evening."
>
> He holds his thumb and index finger two inches apart.
>
> > "A carved obsidian compass, about so. It'll be on the wardrobe shelf or the chart table. It shouldn't take long to find."
>
> He slides a folded harbor chart across the table. The *Eyecatcher* is marked in ink that looks recent.
>
> > "The captain is ashore this evening for a performance. His crew is reliable but not especially alert. I can provide a skiff and a suggested route to the hull."
>
> He names a fee: one hundred gold pieces, paid in person after the compass is in his hand.

> [!social]**The Publishing Contact**
>
> J.B. Nevercott (Neutral, Human (apparent), he/him) :: a Bregan D'aerthe contact with a talent for appearing before you in places you didn't mention you'd be.
>
> Nevercott is unhurried, precise, and genuinely curious about the party — he asks about what they have been doing, whether they have met interesting people, and how they have found the city, and he listens to the answers with more attention than his schedule would seem to require. He will not explain who he works for. He will not explain why he wants the compass, or why a man of his evident resources cannot obtain it himself. He is cheerful about this.
>
> He names 100 gp, payable after the compass is in his hand. He does not discuss Zardoz Zord beyond what the logistics require.
>
> Conversation topics Nevercott is willing to discuss:
> - The approach logistics: the harbor chart, the crew complement, this evening's performance window
> - Why the item needs to arrive in his hand rather than by any simpler means
> - The party's recent activities and impressions of Waterdeep, which he asks about with apparent warmth

> [!qna]**What is the compass for?**
>
> > "A personal matter. I find that knowing why a thing is wanted tends to produce opinions about whether it should be wanted, and opinions are less useful to me than a compass in my hand by morning. I appreciate your discretion."

> [!qna]**Who is Zardoz Zord?**
>
> He turns the harbor chart slightly on the table so the marked vessel faces you.
>
> > "A carnival captain. He runs the Sea Maidens Faire — you may have seen the ships in the harbor. He'll be at a performance this evening and won't be back until well after midnight. That's your window."

> [!qna]**Why can't you get it yourself?**
>
> > "I'm a recognizable man in certain circles, and those circles overlap somewhat with the sort of people who keep careful records about who boards their ships after dark. You don't have that complication. Not yet, at least."

> [!qna]**What if we're caught?**
>
> A small pause.
>
> > "A carnival captain's crew, late at night, finding someone who took a wrong turn on a ship full of curiosities? I wouldn't worry excessively. Zardoz has a sense of humor about that sort of thing. Stay sensible and it won't come to that."

> [!qna]**The pay?**
>
> > "One hundred gold pieces, paid in person after the compass is in my hand. I'll find you. I tend to."

Once the party accepts, Nevercott refills the nearest glass and stands.

> [!readaloud]
> He slides the harbor chart the rest of the way across the table and straightens his coat.
>
> > "The performance starts at the ninth bell. I'd suggest leaving by the eighth — no reason to give the crew more time than they need to notice."
>
> He tips well and leaves without looking back.

Proceed to **Planning the Approach**.

### Planning the Approach

The *Eyecatcher* is anchored in the deep harbor, and the party has the better part of the evening before Zardoz Zord's shore performance ends. J.B. Nevercott's skiff is tied at the Dock Ward's south quay, exactly where the harbor chart marks it.

> [!exploration]**Reaching the Eyecatcher**
>
> Three approach options are available.
>
> **Swimming:** Cold harbor water, 60 feet to the hull. A DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check for each swimmer; characters with a swim speed succeed automatically. Arrival on the hull's shadow side is unobserved.
>
> **Skiff:** Nevercott's skiff has a shuttered lantern at the stern. Docking at the service ladder is quiet at a walking pace; rushing takes a DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check to keep the dinghy from knocking against the hull.
>
> **From below:** Characters with water breathing or similar magic can approach the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, a submarine shape attached to the *Eyecatcher*'s keel. The hatch is not locked from below and opens into the lower deck, thirty feet from the stateroom stairs. This is the quietest of the three approaches.

### The Ship

Four crewmembers are visible on deck, three below. All are drow in disguise.

> [!readaloud]
> The *Eyecatcher* rides easy at anchor in the outer harbor, larger than it looked from the quay. Bright-painted carnival banners hang slack in the night air, and the music from the Faire's tents drifts faintly across the water. The ship itself is dark except for a single lamp at the stern rail, and the watch moves in slow arcs across the main deck, each member covering their section without crossing paths.

> [!exploration]**Observing the Crew**
>
> A character who watches the deck for at least a minute and succeeds on a DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that the crew moves like trained soldiers rather than sailors — precise headings, no idle conversation, each one watching a separate quadrant of the deck.
>
> A group DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check is required to move between decks. On a failure, the nearest crewmember pivots toward the sound and moves to investigate; they do not call out, and they will not block the corridor, but they will be standing in it when the party arrives. A second failure on the same deck brings two crewmembers together. They position themselves where the party can see them, and they hold. They are watching, not stopping.

### The Stateroom

The stateroom is at the aft end of the main cabin, the door unlocked.

> [!readaloud]
> The stateroom is larger than expected. A desk sits against one wall under a brass lamp, and a weapons rack holds more variety than any one person needs. An elaborate wardrobe takes up most of the far wall. In the center of the room, the chart table holds harbor charts, tide tables, and a large open ledger, its most recent entries face-up.
>
> There is no obsidian compass on the chart table. There is a carved jade compass on the wardrobe shelf.

> [!gamemaster]**The Ledger**
>
> The open ledger is the *Ledger of Commissioned Automata*, a complete customer registry. A DC 11 Intelligence (Investigation) check identifies it as a record of commissioned Nimblewrights: customer names, delivery addresses, model specifications, and pricing. A DC 13 check reads the most recent visible entry — a voided Cassalanter commission, cancelled with a forfeited deposit and no stated reason, dated nine months ago. A character who has seen Nimblewright construction work understands that a custom order of this size takes months of advance planning: the Cassalanters commissioned a spy device and then stopped. A DC 15 check allows one further extrapolation — they did not cancel because they lost interest, but because they had realized the device would be used to watch them as much as to watch others.
>
> The cover has no name. Nothing in the ledger connects it to Jarlaxle.

### The Exit

Zardoz Zord boards before the party clears the stateroom corridor. A skiff was already waiting below the starboard rail.

> [!readaloud]
> The top of the boarding ladder fills, and a man in a full carnival captain's costume pulls himself over the rail — the hat, the coat, all of it. He sees you at once. He does not reach for a weapon. He does not call out to the crew.
>
> He watches you move back toward your own skiff with the expression of someone who expected exactly this outcome and is not disappointed by it.
>
> > "I do hope J.B.'s commission was specific enough. He can be vague."
>
> He steps aside to let you pass.

### Concluding the Event

J.B. Nevercott meets the party in person two days later at the Yawning Portal, same booth. He is not surprised by whatever they brought back.

> [!readaloud]
> He is already seated when you arrive, and there is a flat-sided leather case open on the table beside his cup. Inside are one hundred gold pieces and a single flat token — carved obsidian, shaped like a compass. He counts the coin to the nearest hand and slides the token across without comment.
>
> > "The compass was perhaps a little vague as a description. I should have been more specific."
>
> He asks about the party's time in the city, whether they have met interesting people, and what they have been doing. He is warm and unhurried, and in no apparent hurry to leave.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**

>

> - **Ledger Retrieved** — mark when the party takes the *Ledger of Commissioned Automata* regardless of the nominal commission; read by **Fireball!** (Nimblewright thread), **Sea Maidens Faire** (Ledger as evidence), and the supplementary renown award. Krebbyg, when told: "Good. They make decisions under pressure."
> - **Zardoz Seen** — mark when Zardoz Zord boards early and the party sees his face; read by **Sea Maidens Faire** (the party may connect Zardoz and Nevercott before the mission forces the point).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Award +1 Renown when the party returns any compass to Nevercott, jade or obsidian. Award an additional +1 Renown (supplementary) if the party took the Ledger, whether or not they also returned the compass.
>
> The *Ledger of Commissioned Automata*, if kept, becomes an investigation prop for **Fireball!** and a revelation tool for **Sea Maidens Faire**. The voided Cassalanter entry tells a careful reader more about that family's operational wariness than any social interaction would.
>
> The party has now seen Zardoz Zord and met J.B. Nevercott twice, and both men had the same smile. Whether they connect the two before **Sea Maidens Faire** is a function of player attention; this mission does not prompt them toward it.
>
> This conditional mission is complete. Proceed to **Three Nights** Mission (M3).

## Overview

J.B. Nevercott wants a compass from a ship's stateroom. The ship belongs to a man named Zardoz Zord. The compass does not exist. The ship belongs to J.B. Nevercott.

## Summary

The party infiltrated the *Eyecatcher* and reached the stateroom, which contained no obsidian compass but did hold the *Ledger of Commissioned Automata* — a complete record of every Nimblewright Jarlaxle had sold, including a voided Cassalanter commission. Zardoz Zord boarded early and watched them leave without intervening. J.B. Nevercott paid one hundred gold pieces two days later and seemed unsurprised by whatever they brought back.
