# The Betrayal Pitch: Overview

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available between Missions 2 and 3. This mission requires at least one party member who is a Bregan D'aerthe member and has made direct contact with Jarlaxle in one of his personas — either by meeting Zardoz Zord at the Sea Maidens Faire, or by working with J.B. Nevercott during **The Wazoo Affair**. The two men are the same man. If the party has not met Jarlaxle in at least one of his personas, this mission does not exist in the campaign.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

There is no obsidian compass. Jarlaxle invented it. The *Eyecatcher* is his ship, Zardoz Zord is his persona, and the crew runs at half complement under private orders not to physically intercept anyone who reaches the stateroom corridor. What sits open on the chart table — placed deliberately — is the *Ledger of Commissioned Automata*, Jarlaxle's complete record of every Nimblewright he has sold, including a voided Cassalanter commission dated nine months ago. Jarlaxle wants to find out whether the party is clever enough to take what they were not sent for.

Zardoz Zord boards early by design. The party sees his face at the moment they are most committed to a clean exit, and he does not stop them. J.B. Nevercott pays the following day without showing any surprise about what the party brought back.

## Involved Characters
- **J.B. Nevercott** (Bregan D'aerthe) — gives the mission brief; is Jarlaxle; is robbing himself
- **Zardoz Zord** (Bregan D'aerthe) — the captain the party is nominally stealing from; is also Jarlaxle; boards the *Eyecatcher* from shore just as the party is leaving
- **Eyecatcher crew** (Bregan D'aerthe) — drow operatives in disguise; running at half complement; specifically instructed not to block the stateroom corridor, but they will make the approach feel earned

## Dangers & Enemies
No combat triggers unless the party creates one. The crew will not engage unless attacked. The risk is being seen before reaching the stateroom — a failed group Stealth check, not a hostile encounter.

## Overview

J.B. Nevercott found the party again, same coat, same easy manner. He needed a carved obsidian compass retrieved from the stateroom of the *Eyecatcher*, the Sea Maidens Faire flagship, while the captain was ashore for a performance. He could provide a skiff. There was no compass.
