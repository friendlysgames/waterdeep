# The Betrayal Pitch: Overview

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available between Missions 2 and 3.
>
> > [!warning]+ Conditional Mission — Two Requirements
> > This mission is only available when:
> > 1. The party includes at least one Bregan D'aerthe member, AND
> > 2. That member has made direct contact with Zardoz Zord (met him at the Sea Maidens Faire, or met J.B. Nevercott during The Wazoo Affair — they are the same man).
> >
> > If neither condition is met, this mission does not exist in the campaign. Do not offer it to a party that has not met Jarlaxle in at least one of his personas.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **J.B. Nevercott** (Bregan D'aerthe) — gives the mission brief; is Jarlaxle; is robbing himself
- **Zardoz Zord** (Bregan D'aerthe) — the captain the party is nominally stealing from; is also Jarlaxle; boards the *Eyecatcher* from shore just as the party is leaving
- **Eyecatcher crew** (Bregan D'aerthe) — drow operatives in disguise; running at half complement; the "alert" members are specifically instructed not to catch the party if they reach the stateroom corridor, but they will make the approach feel earned

## Dangers & Enemies
No combat triggers unless the party creates one. The crew will not engage unless attacked. The risk is being seen before reaching the stateroom — a failed group stealth check, not a hostile encounter.

## Overview

J.B. Nevercott found the party again, same coat, same easy manner. He had a job. A vessel in the harbor — the *Eyecatcher*, the Sea Maidens Faire flagship, Captain Zardoz Zord's personal ship — contained an item he wanted retrieved. A carved obsidian compass, about so big, from the captain's stateroom. The captain would be ashore all evening for a performance. He could provide a skiff.

There was no compass.

Jarlaxle wrote the job himself. The *Eyecatcher* is his ship, Zardoz Zord is his persona, and he knew exactly what the party would find inside the stateroom — the *Ledger of Commissioned Automata*, his complete record of every Nimblewright sold, open on the chart table to an entry with a voided Cassalanter commission. He wanted to see if the party would take the thing they were not asked to take.

The crew was at half complement and specifically instructed not to catch anyone who reached the stateroom corridor. Zardoz Zord returned early from the performance and watched the party come back to the skiff. He seemed pleased about something. J.B. Nevercott paid the next day without being surprised by anything that had happened.
