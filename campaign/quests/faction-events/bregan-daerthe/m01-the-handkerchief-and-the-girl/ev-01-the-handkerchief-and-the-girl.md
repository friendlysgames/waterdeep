# The Handkerchief and the Girl

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event runs across two locations: Lady Ashford's reception in the Castle Ward and Vessin's converted crate near the waterfront. In this Event:
>
> - The party has multiple routes into the reception and multiple ways to acquire the handkerchief; none require combat.
> - The coded embroidery is discoverable mid-mission. Vessin can confirm it is intact but will not decode it.
> - Krebbyg is not present. He receives the debrief at Seven Masks the following morning.
> - The mission closes when the handkerchief reaches Vessin.

**Background (DM only)**

The handkerchief contains a coded message hidden in the anchor embroidery — eleven specific knots that only Bregan D'aerthe field operatives can read. **Vessin** is a tiefling girl of sixteen, a D'aerthe informant who has been working the Dock Ward waterfront for eighteen months. Her contact method uses a specific embroidery house that also supplies Lady Ashford's household. Six weeks ago, a delivery went to Ashford instead of Vessin; Ashford kept it because the anchor motif matched her collection. Krebbyg does not fully understand the situation — he knows only that Jarlaxle wants the handkerchief delivered and that Vessin has been out of contact.

Vessin is Jarlaxle's best-positioned observation asset in the Dock Ward. He values her considerably more than he has indicated to Krebbyg.

### Lady Ashford's Reception

Sorn Street in the Castle Ward, late evening. The house is three stories, lanterns lit at the gate, twenty or so guests audible through the ground-floor windows. Ashford runs loose, pleasant receptions; unfamiliar faces are unwelcome only when they cannot produce a reasonable story.

Entry options:
- **DC 12 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion)** at the door covers any plausible connection — a mutual acquaintance, an invitation lost in transit, a business introduction through a family friend. A party member with established social standing in any ward has Advantage.
- Attending as admirers of the Sea Maidens Faire, which Ashford attended the previous tenday, requires no check. Guests will make conversation and expect it in return.

**Lady Mirilin Ashford** — *Host. Silk collector. Incurious about her own good fortune.*

She moves through the party with genuine ease. She has no reason to be suspicious. The handkerchief is in her left coat pocket — she touches it absently when she discusses textiles, which she often does.

Acquiring the handkerchief:
- **DC 12 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** during any close social contact — a toast, a handshake, a shared moment at the refreshment table. On a natural 1, Ashford looks puzzled rather than alarmed; she checks her pocket, finds nothing, and assumes it slipped.
- **DC 12 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion)** to convince Ashford to surrender it voluntarily — a wager, a story about the anchor motif's personal significance, a trade offer. On a success, she hands it over charmed rather than suspicious.
- A **DC 14 Charisma (Performance)** check or equivalent distraction by another party member grants Advantage on the Sleight of Hand check.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### If Ashford Realizes the Handkerchief Is Gone
> She mentions it to a servant near the end of the evening. She does not connect it to the party and does not contact the Watch. No mechanical consequence, but it is noted: this is a clean job or it is not, and Krebbyg will ask.

### The Knots

Characters who examine the handkerchief carefully and succeed on a **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check notice the anchor embroidery is inconsistent — knot spacing that does not follow any decorative logic. A **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana or History)** check, or any character with experience in coded communication, identifies it as information-bearing.

Fully decoding the message requires a **DC 17 Intelligence (Investigation)** check and time to work through the pattern. On success, the reader extracts partial content: a name, a date, and a phrase in a cipher they do not have the key for. Vessin holds the key. She will not share it, but she will confirm the message arrived intact.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### If the Party Decodes the Message
> Krebbyg, when told about it at debrief, pauses. Then: "How did you recognize the pattern?" He listens to the answer and files it. He says nothing further. Jarlaxle hears about it within the week.

### Vessin

Corner of Net Street and Dock Street in the Dock Ward: a converted harbor crate on a raised frame, door painted yellow. Vessin opens before the party knocks — she heard them on the approach stairs. Small, dark-haired, tiefling, her movements economical in the way of someone who has spent years not being noticed.

She takes the handkerchief and counts the knots with her thumb. Her posture changes when she finishes. Six weeks of silence ending in a single count.

> > "Tell Kreb: still watching. Nothing changed."

Then:

> > "Thank you. He wouldn't have sent people worth sending."

She offers nothing further. She does not invite the party in. The door closes quietly.

She is not being dismissive. She is simply finished.

### Concluding the Event

> **[GM]**

> **+1 Renown (supplementary)** if the party identifies the knot coding and asks Vessin about it. She confirms without explaining. Krebbyg, told at debrief: "Good eyes." That is the entire comment.

## Overview

A theater booking manager with a relaxed manner and a single job: find a silk handkerchief at a Castle Ward reception and deliver it to a tiefling girl near the waterfront.

## Read Aloud

> Two theater tickets arrive in an unsigned envelope — good seats for the Sea Maidens Faire Debut Parade in the Dock Ward. A short note is tucked beneath them: *Afterward, speak to the booking manager at Seven Masks.*
>
> The booking manager is compact, professional, and a half-shade too still for a human. He shakes your hand. He orders drinks without asking if there is a round going. He sits down.
>
> > > "A small favor. There's a noble hosting a reception tonight in the Castle Ward — Lady Mirilin Ashford, house on Sorn Street. She carries a silk handkerchief embroidered with a silver anchor. I'd like you to obtain it and deliver it to a young tiefling woman named Vessin, who lives near the waterfront — corner of Net Street and Dock Street, in a converted crate with a yellow door."
>
> He slides a folded street map across the table.
>
> > > "The reception is open to presentable guests. I'd prefer you not start anything."

## Summary

The party attended Lady Ashford's reception in the Castle Ward, acquired a silk handkerchief embroidered with a silver anchor, and delivered it to Vessin at her converted crate near the waterfront. Vessin confirmed the delivery and passed a message for Krebbyg. Two days later, an anonymous coin pouch arrived: 50 gp and one silver piece marked with a small anchor.
