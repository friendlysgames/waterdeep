# Design Notes: The Handkerchief and the Girl

## Vessin as a Long-Term Thread

***The informant the party does not know they met.*** Vessin is introduced here as a delivery target — a mysterious teenager who takes the handkerchief, says three sentences, and closes the door. Her significance is entirely invisible at Level 2. She reappears in Arc H as Jarlaxle's most valuable harbor observation asset, and a party that has met her has a face to put to the name when she comes up in play. This is the difference between an abstract NPC and a person the table remembers.

The restrained introduction is intentional. She does not explain herself, does not perform warmth, and does not give the party anything to follow up on. That restraint signals her competence before the party knows her competence matters. Jarlaxle, if asked about her in a later mission, says only: "She's very good." The party already knows what that looks like.

## Krebbyg's Deliberate Opacity

***Not explaining the handkerchief.*** Krebbyg never tells the party what the handkerchief contains, who Vessin is, or why the job matters. This is not a structural omission — it is his established character. He operates on a need-to-know basis, and the party does not need to know. The coded embroidery is discoverable for curious parties: they get partial information confirming the handkerchief matters, plus a Vessin interaction that can run longer if they ask the right question.

The opacity sets up the faction's long-term dynamic. Bregan D'aerthe will never fully explain what it is doing or why. The party's role is to be competent at jobs whose full context is withheld. The coin pouches — anonymous, unexplained — reinforce this. The relationship is built through consistent delivery, not disclosure.

## The Anchor Scratched on Silver

***Jarlaxle's long signature.*** The silver piece with the scratched anchor is placed here as a detail to surface later. A party that keeps it will be able to verify Jarlaxle's identity in Arc H when he produces a matching token — a small, almost private confirmation that the man they have been working for through intermediaries was watching from the beginning. This is not a mechanic to announce; it is a detail to plant and trust the table to notice.
