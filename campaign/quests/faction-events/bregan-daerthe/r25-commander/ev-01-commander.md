# Commander

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Bregan D'aerthe member reaches Renown 25. Zardoz Zord names the rank over a private dinner, and Pelsha and Vorn, the two gunslingers he assigns, stand in the room while he explains it. If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, Jarlaxle himself receives them aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* instead. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn how to ask the company for one of three favours each quest: a private audience with a Masked Lord, passage in or out of Waterdeep, or the disappearance of one named person for six days.
> - Learn how to call a crew of two gunslingers and four drow for one major operation each quest, and meet the first pair.
> - Learn how to ask for the captain's company on one operation each quest, or for the substitute he sends when he stays away.
> - Name the Masked Lord the member would trust to carry a letter to the Lords' Alliance, and hear Zardoz's reaction.
>
> All of these benefits belong to the qualifying member. Companions can join an operation, and they gain no rank, crew or favour of their own.

### Hook

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 25, a playbill with a price circled in red is pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor. It is the company's usual summons from **Kreb Drops the Cover**, and the circled number is the bell at which the member goes to the harbor steps. Hold this Event until **BD Officer** is marked for the member. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the playbill waiting at their first lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the next evening at the same hour. Every qualifying member is invited and recorded separately, and companions who are not Bregan D'aerthe members wait outside and gain nothing from the evening.

The six missions bring a member to Renown 19 on their base awards, so a member needs about 6 more Renown from other sources before this rank arrives. Those sources are the bonus lines in each mission, the +1 from **Dinner with Zardoz**, the Earning Renown list in the Bregan D'aerthe page of the Factions Guide, the +1 or +2 a member can earn in the cabin scene of **Fireball!**, and the +2 a member earns in **Gralhund Villa** when the party holds the Stone. This rank usually arrives during or after the lair heists, and every procedure below works in whatever order the quests were played.

Who receives the member, and where, depends on three things.

- **Zardoz Introduced is marked and the Faire is still in Deepwater Harbor:** Zardoz receives the member in the captain's dining cabin (area J10) aboard the *Eyecatcher*. The circled number is 8, and the skiff leaves the harbor steps at half past eight with Fel'rekt rowing.
- **Zardoz Introduced is marked and the Faire has sailed on Tarsakh 20:** Zardoz receives the member in the cabin of a hired lighter moored at the end of the Dock Ward piers. He stayed behind with a handful of the company and the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, which lies under the lighter. The circled number is 8, and the skiff leaves the harbor steps at half past eight.
- **Jarlaxle Unmasked is marked for the member:** Jarlaxle receives the member in the forward lounge of the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, wherever she is moored. Read the Jarlaxle versions below in place of the Zardoz versions. A member who has the outcome is not blindfolded, and Fel'rekt takes them in through the captain's cabin above, as he did in **The Killer's Fate**.
- **Zardoz Introduced is not marked:** the circled number is 9, and Krebbyg receives the member in the Seven Masks booking office. Read the Krebbyg version below.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle Baenre (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him). Aboard his ships he wears a magical guise projected by the gilded figurehead on each vessel, and every crew member looks like an attractive human while the ship's figurehead is in range. On the lighter there is no figurehead, so the crew look like what they are and Zardoz wears his *hat of disguise* as Zord. Nobody in this Event says the name Jarlaxle in speech or readaloud unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member being addressed, and until then every Bregan D'aerthe speaker says "the captain" or "J." This Event does not mark **Jarlaxle Unmasked**, and a member who guesses the name aloud does not mark it either. Zardoz answers a guess with a delighted roar and a free ticket. Zardoz never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan.
>
> This is not the member's first look at the man at the top of the company. Several earlier events put the member in front of one of his personas, and nothing here treats the rank as an introduction. What the rank changes is how much of the company's strength the member may borrow. Jarlaxle gives it because a Commander who can call a crew, a favour or the captain himself is a Commander who will come back with something worth having. The company's real interest is the political picture in Waterdeep, and he asks the member one question about it tonight.
>
> Jarlaxle knows that the Cassalanters are bound to Asmodeus and have pledged their children in a contract, because his doppelganger agent Vessa, who has moved in their social circle for months, reads thoughts. No speaker in this Event tells a member who has not worked it out, and a favour never buys the answer. If **Cassalanter Pact Shared with BD** is not marked and a member says in their own words that the Cassalanters are bound to a devil, or shows proof, the speaker stops deflecting and admits that the company has known for some time, in the words of the admission in **The Theater's Back Room**, without naming Vessa, and the outcome is marked. If it is already marked, the speaker discusses it plainly.
>
> **Kreb Unmasked** is marked for nearly every member who reaches Renown 25, because **Kreb Drops the Cover** runs after the second mission. Krebbyg and Fel'rekt drop their human cover in front of such a member. If it is not marked, Krebbyg stays in his Kreb Sorrush cover and keeps out of the scene.
>
> The favours, the crews and the captain's company are each limited by quest, and the **Commander Ledger** later in this Event records the limits per member and across the party. A **quest** means one of the campaign's ten named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**. Events, faction missions and rank events are not quests, and a use is counted against the quest the party is in when the member asks.

### Naming the Rank

If Zardoz is receiving the member aboard the *Eyecatcher*, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The captain's dining cabin glows with lamplight on gold filigree, and the mahogany table is laid for two with a bottle of wine and a plate of small fried fish between the glasses. Zardoz stands behind it in his scarlet shirt with the plumed hat on the sideboard, and he is pouring before you have reached a chair. A tall woman with white hair in a long braid stands against the door with her hands folded, and a lean man in a stevedore's coat leans beside the stern window with his arms crossed, and both look like handsome humans and neither of them smiles.
>
> > Commander! Salt and fucking thunder, my darling, sit down, because I've wanted to say that word aloud to you for a fortnight and Krebbyg wouldn't let me. It means the company has watched you long enough to decide it would like to go on watching, and that it now owes you three favours, two gunslingers, four of the quietest people on the coast and a great deal of my time. Eat a fish first, and then I'll explain every one of them badly.

If Zardoz is receiving the member aboard the lighter after Tarsakh 20, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cabin of the lighter is low and smells of tar and old bunting, and the Faire's crimson pennants have been nailed along the beams so thickly that the lamp throws red light across everything. Zardoz sits at a plank table with the plumed hat beside a bottle and two glasses, and the harbor slaps at the hull on the other side of the wall. A grey-skinned drow woman with white hair in a long braid stands against the door with her hands folded, and a lean drow man in a stevedore's coat leans beside the porthole with his arms crossed, and neither of them smiles.
>
> > Commander! Salt and fucking thunder, my darling, sit down, and forgive the furnishings, because the grand ships have sailed and I've been left with a bucket and a very loyal gull. It means the company has watched you long enough to decide it would like to go on watching, and it now owes you three favours, two gunslingers, four of the quietest people on the coast and a good deal of my time. Have a glass, and I'll explain all of it badly.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead of either Zardoz version:

Jarlaxle sits at a chart table with his coat off and his boots crossed on the opposite chair, and a wide-brimmed hat with a long white feather hangs from a hook beside his head. Jarlaxle looks up from a chart he does not bother to fold away.
>

If **Zardoz Introduced** is not marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The booking office of the Seven Masks smells of stage paint and cold tea, and Krebbyg sits on the edge of the desk with a gilt-edged card in one hand and a schedule in the other. A tall woman and a lean man in stagehands' aprons wait against the wall by the locked door and look past you at nothing. Krebbyg stands up, flaps the card at you, and talks before he has finished standing.
>
> > Darling, there you are, it's Commander, don't ask me how it works because I've only just been told, and the captain says he'd love to say it himself and he'll write when he's got a free evening. Those two are Pelsha and Vorn, they're yours, and I'll go through the rest slowly with Fel in a minute, because he explains things better than I do and he never skips the bit about the limits.

In that case Krebbyg gives each procedure below in his own words, and Zardoz does not appear. The member keeps Krebbyg as their only contact and does not mark **Zardoz Introduced**, so the substitute for the captain's company applies to them.

> [!social]**The Host Who Pours First**
>
> Zardoz Zord (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in the guise of an Illuskan human, he/him) :: the flamboyant captain of the Sea Maidens Faire, who is the man at the top of Bregan D'aerthe and is delighted to be handing out the company's best toys.
>
> Zardoz is loud, generous and warm, and he fills every silence within a breath. He answers a hard question with a story, an offer of free tickets or a shout for the next dish, and the question he actually cares about is always the one buried in the middle of the story. When the cover strains his accent thins and his words turn suddenly elegant, and he calls the member "my dear" in a voice that does not belong to a sailor. He swears happily and often.
>
> Zardoz is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The three favours, and what each one will and will not do.
> - Pelsha and Vorn, and the four drow who go with them.
> - The captain's company, which he calls a very good idea for other people.
> - Free tickets, at length.
>
> Any character who watches Zardoz while he talks and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that the delight is real, and that a second, steadier attention underneath it is waiting for the member to say something about the city. A member who has marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** needs no check and knows whose attention it is. A member who fails the check sees a host who would like another fish eaten.

> [!qna]**Why me?**
>
> Zardoz tops up both glasses before he answers.
>
> > Because you came back, my darling, and that's rarer than being clever, and I've had a great many clever people through this cabin who couldn't be found the week after. Krebbyg reads me the file aloud while I'm eating, and by the time he reached the Eye I'd stopped chewing, which is the highest compliment I know.

> [!qna]**What does the company want in return?**
>
> > Why, nothing, my friend, not tonight, because you've paid already, in advance, which is unheard of! You'll go on doing what you do, and when the company has a small thing to ask, which it will, I'll ask it to your face and not through a hat. There's one question I'd like an answer to before the evening ends, but that comes after the pudding.

### The Three Favours

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz lays three playing cards face up on the table between the glasses, a queen in a mask, a ship under full sail and a sleeping man in a nightcap, and he taps each one with the stem of his glass as he goes along.
>
> > One favour a quest, my darling, and I'll say it twice because everyone hears it wrong. One, a private audience with a Masked Lord, which gets you a seat in a room and no promises about what the Lord thinks of you. Two, passage into Waterdeep or out of it without the Watch knowing, one party and one direction for one window of twelve hours. Three, I make one named person vanish for six days, with no harm done and no questions and not a scrap of paper afterward. You pick one a quest, and you don't get one of each, and you'd be amazed how many people nod politely and then try for all three.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle slides three playing cards across the chart, a queen in a mask, a ship under full sail and a sleeping man in a nightcap, and he lays one finger on each in turn without looking down.
>
> > Once each quest, I can do one of three things for you. A private audience with a Masked Lord, with your identity kept out of it and the agenda yours, since I only arrange the introduction. Passage into Waterdeep or out of it without the City Watch hearing of it, for one party, in one direction, within one window of twelve hours. Or the disappearance of one named person for six days, with no harm done and no questions asked and no record kept. You will notice I said one of three and not one of each, and I mention it only because people in your position sometimes hear it the other way round.

> [!qna]**How do you do it?**
>
> If Zardoz is speaking:
>
> > With a great deal of free fried fish, my friend, and a better class of acquaintance than you'd credit! Don't ask me for the particulars, because the particulars are why it works.
>
> If Jarlaxle is speaking:
>
> > Competently.

> [!qna]**Can the favour fail?**
>
> > It can be refused, my darling, and if I refuse it, it isn't spent, and you ask again with something I can actually do. What it can't do is go wrong halfway and leave you holding the bill. Once I've said yes, the thing is done or I'll tell you why not.

> [!exploration]**One Favour per Quest**
>
> Each Commander can ask for one favour per quest. The member leaves a note in the left pocket of the grey coat on peg seven in the Seven Masks cloakroom, or gives it to Krebbyg at the stage door, naming the favour and the details below. The company answers by the next dusk with a playbill pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor. A circled 1 means yes and a circled 0 means the favour is refused and not spent.
>
> - **Use:** The use counts against the quest the party is in when the company says yes. A favour nobody called before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Refusals:** The company refuses any target who is the Open Lord or a Masked Lord (for the disappearance), any child, any sitting Watch officer or member of the Lords' Alliance council, anyone inside a warded stronghold, a vault or Castle Waterdeep, and anyone the company has reason to protect, which includes every Notable Figure that Bregan D'aerthe lists as its own. A member can send a new note after a refusal.
> - **Loss rule:** The company withdraws every favour from a member who names the company or its drow to a Watch officer or to anyone outside it, whose Renown falls below 25, or who marks **BD Contact Severed**. A member whose Renown returns to 25 has the favours again.
> - **Shared quests:** Two Commanders in the same party each have their own favour and use it separately.
>
> **A: The Audience.** The member names the Lord by the name the member believes the Lord wears, and the company gives its answer by the next dusk. The company can reach four Lords, and the member does not learn the Lord's name from the answer, since the Lord stays masked.
>
> | The member names | The masked Lord is | The audience is |
> |---|---|---|
> | Mirt, or a Harper or a moneylender | Mirt | A back room at his Sea Ward manor, ten minutes after dark |
> | Durnan, or the Yawning Portal | Durnan | The Yawning Portal's cellar, at the hour the taproom closes |
> | Ulkoria Stonemarrow, or the Watchful Order | Ulkoria Stonemarrow | A reading room in the Castle Ward, an hour before noon |
> | Jelenn Urmbrusk, or a noble house | Jelenn Urmbrusk | A closed carriage in the Sea Ward, on a drive of fifteen minutes |
>
> If the member names someone else, the company says it cannot arrange that Lord and the favour is not spent. The audience happens within three days of the yes. The member may bring one companion, and the Lord listens for ten minutes and answers in character from that Lord's Notable Figures page, in the voice of someone who has agreed to listen and promised nothing. The audience guarantees the meeting and nothing else.
>
> **B: The Passage.** The member names the direction, the hour the twelve-hour window opens, and up to six people, the member included. The company needs six hours' notice. Out of Waterdeep, Fel'rekt's skiff leaves from a Dock Ward quay, or a Faire provisioning wagon goes through the gate with the party under the canvas. Into Waterdeep, the skiff lands the party on the same quay, or the wagon carries them through. The passage covers the travellers and what they carry on their persons. It does not cover cargo, a prisoner or a creature larger than Medium, and it ends when the window closes.
>
> **C: The Disappearance.** The member names one person and the place where that person will be within the next three days. The company takes the person from that place within three days, keeps them for six days in a comfortable berth, and returns them unharmed to the place they were taken from, remembering nothing of the interval. Nothing in it points to the member or the company. A person who is not within the city's wards at the time is refused.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Favours Cannot Do**
>
> The audience guarantees a meeting and says nothing about the outcome, and a Lord treats a member who arrives by Bregan D'aerthe's arrangement with polite caution. The passage cannot be extended or stacked on a second window in the same quest. The disappearance cannot be used to remove someone for good, and the person returns on the seventh morning whatever the member does in the meantime. If the member asks the company to keep a person longer, the company declines and does not spend the use.

### The Crews

Zardoz turns to the two figures by the door, and the member meets the first pair.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets down his glass and waves a hand toward the tall woman by the door, who steps forward with her hands still folded, and then toward the lean man at the window, who unfolds his arms and does not move any closer.
>
> > This is Pelsha, who is older than everyone else in this room and has the patience to prove it, and that is Vorn, who asks more questions before an operation than you'll have thought of in your life. Between them they can shoot the buttons off a magistrate's coat from across a street, and the four people they bring with them are better at not being noticed than anybody I've ever paid. On one major operation a quest, you ask, and they come.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, Jarlaxle says the same in his own words:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle gestures with two fingers toward the woman at the door and then toward the man at the porthole, and both of them incline their heads by a few degrees.
>
> > Pelsha and Vorn, who have worked for me longer than you have been alive, and who follow orders within the scope of an operation. They do not take risks that serve your plan at the expense of the company, and you will find them reliable within that limit. For one major operation each quest, they come, with four of my people, and they do what you plan.

> [!social]**The Gunslingers at the Door**
>
> Pelsha (Neutral, Drow, she/her) and Vorn (Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: the two drow gunslingers the company assigns to a Commander, and the first pair of two.
>
> Pelsha is calm and unhurried, and she treats recklessness in colleagues as bad weather. She gives plain answers in short, complete sentences, and she says "Understood" when she has heard a plan and will do it. Vorn speaks seldom during an operation and often before one, asking questions the member had not thought of. He swears rarely and flatly, and Pelsha does not swear at all. Both keep their right hands near their pistols while they talk, and neither will say anything about the captain.
>
> Pelsha and Vorn are happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What an operation needs before they can join it, which is mostly Vorn's questions.
> - What they will refuse, which Pelsha answers.
> - The four drow who go with them, whom they call "the crew" and do not name.
>
> Any character who watches Vorn through his questions and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that each question is a gap in the plan he intends to cover himself, and that he has worked out where the plan will fail before the member has finished explaining it.

> [!qna]**What will you do?**
>
> Pelsha unfolds her hands before she answers.
>
> > We do whatever the plan needs within reason, from cover and from range, which mostly means shooting what you point at and holding a door while you work. We don't take the lead, because it's your operation, and we don't do anything that would bring a Watch officer to the captain's door.

> [!qna]**What won't you do?**
>
> Vorn looks at Pelsha before he answers.
>
> > We won't kill a child or a bystander, and we won't strike a Watch officer who hasn't struck first. We won't do a thing that shows anyone a drow face who shouldn't see one, and we leave when the objective is done, or the plan can't work, or either of us is down. That last one is Pelsha's rule, and I'd keep it if I were you.

> [!qna]**What if one of you dies?**
>
> > Then the captain finds someone else, and it takes a tenday. You'll have spent your use for the quest, so please don't spend us on a bad plan.

> [!exploration]**One Crew per Quest**
>
> Each Commander can call one crew for one major operation per quest. An **operation** is a Bregan D'aerthe mission, a lair heist, or any outing the member names that has an objective, a place and an hour. The member gives Krebbyg or the grey coat on peg seven three days' notice with the objective, the place and the hour the operation begins.
>
> - **Meeting:** The crew waits at a surface place the member names, at the hour named. Pelsha takes the member's plan, and Vorn asks his questions on the first evening.
> - **Limits:** The crew refuses to attack children or bystanders, refuses to attack a Watch officer who has not struck first, refuses any act that would show the company's drow to someone outside it, and refuses an operation against the company or the Faire. It withdraws when the objective is finished, abandoned or impossible, and when either gunslinger falls.
> - **Use:** The use counts against the quest the party is in when the crew begins the operation. A use that nobody called before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Losses:** A crew member who dies or is captured is replaced a tenday later, and the use stays spent.
> - **Shared jobs:** Two Commanders who ask for the same operation share one crew, and only the first to ask spends a use. Two different operations in a quest can each have a crew, one per member, up to the two crews that exist.
> - **Not with the captain:** The crew and the captain's company do not go on the same operation. Zardoz says "I won't put the whole ship in one doorway."
> - **Loss rule:** The crew is withdrawn by the same events that withdraw the favours.

> [!gamemaster]**The Commander Ledger**
>
> Record, for each Commander: the quest of the last favour, which crew is assigned, the quest of the last crew use, the quest of the last use of the captain's company, and the Lord the member named in **The Seat** below.
>
> The company has two crews, and no more. The first Commander named in a party is assigned Crew One, and the second is assigned Crew Two. A third Commander, or a fourth, waits for whichever crew is free, and a crew serves only one operation at a time. Requests are filled in the order they are made.
>
> | Crew | Gunslingers | Drow | Notes |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | One | Pelsha (Drow Gunslinger) and Vorn (Drow Gunslinger) | four drow (**Scout**) | The guide's Commander row: two Drow Gunslingers and four drow |
> | Two | Ilmra Kelnozz (she/her) and Brythe Mizzrym (he/him), both Drow Gunslingers | four drow (**Scout**) | Invented for this Event |
>
> Ilmra and Brythe use the same stat blocks and obey the same limits. Ilmra is blunt and Brythe is polite, and neither says anything about the captain.
>
> #### The Crew as Allies
>
> Use the **Drow Gunslinger** stat block from *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, page 201, for each gunslinger, because there is no 2024 equivalent. Use the 2024 **Scout** for each of the four drow, and add Darkvision 120 feet, Fey Ancestry and Sunlight Sensitivity from the gunslinger's traits, and swap the Longbow for a Hand Crossbow (range 30/120 feet).
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, a Drow Gunslinger is a CR 4 ally with Power 38 and a Scout is a CR 1/2 ally with Power 12, for a party of levels 5 to 10. A full crew adds 124. At levels 11 and above, a gunslinger is 32 and a Scout is 7, and a full crew adds 92. Count only the crew members who take part in a combat, add that to the participating characters' Party Power, and recalculate the difficulty and the day cost. A crew that is waiting elsewhere adds nothing.
>
> A full crew roughly doubles a party of four, so build the fight around the larger total or keep the crew out of it. At level 7, a party of four has Power 164, and with a crew it is 288.
>
> #### The Crew's Tactics
> Pelsha stays within 30 feet of the member and fires at whatever threatens the member or the drow. During combat, the crew:
>
> - Fires from cover at the nearest threat, and the Scouts flank the same enemy as the gunslingers.
> - Offers surrender before it fires on anyone who is not attacking.
> - Falls back toward the exit the member named if either gunslinger is reduced to half their Hit Points.
> - Stops when the member calls it off.

### The Captain's Company

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets down the glass and rubs his hands together, and his face goes through a very quick series of expressions before it settles on a grin.
>
> > Now, the bit I'm least keen on. If you want the captain himself on an operation, you say so by dusk the day before, and you'll get him for the whole of it, and I'll give you fair warning that he is dreadfully expensive. I'm told he fights like a man with a great deal to lose. The thing is, my darling, he doesn't come for people he hasn't told you about, so if he sends his regrets, don't take it personally. I'll send someone better suited.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle lifts his glass and speaks without looking at the member.
>
> > Once each quest, I come with you. I fight at full capacity, and I follow your plan, because it is your operation and I have learned the value of not crowding capable people. If the plan is wrong, I will tell you before we go in, and I will not tell you during. This is a resource, and I would ask you to use it when the situation justifies it and not when you want the company.

> [!exploration]**The Captain's Company**
>
> Each Commander can ask once per quest for Jarlaxle to join one operation. The member gives Krebbyg or the grey coat on peg seven notice by dusk on the day before the operation begins.
>
> - **If Jarlaxle Unmasked is marked for the member:** Jarlaxle comes. He meets the party at the hour named, wearing his *hat of disguise* as a lean human swordsman with a feathered hat, and the member introduces him to companions as "the captain." He arrives, fights and leaves, and he does not comment on his own performance afterward.
> - **If Jarlaxle Unmasked is not marked:** The company sends a substitute, and the use is spent. Krebbyg brings Zardoz's regrets on a gilt ticket, and Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, both Drow Gunslingers, join the operation. They obey the same limits as a crew.
> - **One captain only:** There is one Jarlaxle. If two Commanders ask for different operations that start on the same day, the first to ask gets him and the second gets the substitute. If two Commanders ask for the same operation, only the first spends a use.
> - **Limits:** He refuses an operation against the company or the Faire, and he will not stay past the operation's end. If he is reduced below 30 Hit Points he looks for a way out, and he does not sacrifice himself.
> - **Not with the crew:** The captain and a crew do not go on the same operation.
> - **Loss rule:** The same events that withdraw the favours withdraw the company.

> [!gamemaster]**Jarlaxle as an Ally**
>
> Use the 2024 **Pirate Captain** for Jarlaxle, because there is no 2024 Swashbuckler. He fights at full capacity, targets the most dangerous adversary first, and uses his items freely. If he is reduced below 30 of his 84 Hit Points, he uses an escape item and leaves, and he is not coming back that day.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, a Pirate Captain is a CR 6 ally with Power 65 for levels 5 to 10 and 50 for levels 11 and above. Add that to the party's Power and recalculate. If the encounter is heavy on his items, count him as CR 8 (Power 85). The substitute is two Drow Gunslingers, for Power 76 at levels 5 to 10 and 64 at 11 and above.

### The Seat

When the pudding is cleared, Zardoz sets down his fork and asks the question that the company has been collecting answers to since the first mission.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A steward in a white jacket clears the plates, and Zardoz leans on the table with his elbows among the crumbs and his chin on his fist, looking at you with the whole of his attention for the first time since you sat down.
>
> > Now, my darling, a thing I've wanted to ask you all evening and couldn't find a way to slip in sideways. If you had to hand a letter to a single Masked Lord, one you trust to carry it to the Lords' Alliance council and read it before they sealed it, which Lord would it be? You needn't know their name. Tell me who you'd go to, and I'll tell you whether I'd have chosen the same.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle sets down his glass and folds his hands on the chart, and his face is perfectly pleasant.
>
> > I would like to ask you one thing about the city, and I would like an honest answer. If you had to give a letter to a single Masked Lord, one you trusted to carry it to the Lords' Alliance council and to read it first, who would you choose? You need not know the name. Tell me whom you would go to.

> [!exploration]**Naming the Seat**
>
> The member answers in their own words, naming a Lord by name, by trade or by what they do. The company can arrange four Lords, and Zardoz knows which one the member means.
>
> - **A Lord the company can reach (Mirt, Durnan, Ulkoria Stonemarrow or Jelenn Urmbrusk):** record that Lord's name against the member as the **Seat**. Zardoz toasts the choice, and the first audience favour the member asks for in the campaign is granted with no refusal of that Lord, and the meeting is a half hour and not ten minutes.
> - **Someone who is not a Masked Lord, or a Lord the company cannot arrange:** Zardoz says "Marvelous guess, and quite wrong," and lets the member answer once more. If the second answer fails, nothing is recorded, and the question is not asked again.
> - **A refusal to answer:** Zardoz roars his approval, pours another glass and tells a story about a customs officer who answered every question with a question. Nothing is recorded, and the member loses nothing.
>
> A member who names a Seat has told the company which Lord they lean on, and the company watches that Lord's door for the rest of the campaign. A member who names Mirt, a Harper, tells Bregan D'aerthe something it did not know about where the member's loyalties sit. **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) reads the Seat, because Jarlaxle's proposal needs a Lord to carry the letter.

If the member names a Seat, Zardoz neither confirms nor denies that the person is a Masked Lord, because the company never says so aloud. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz raises his glass an inch off the table and studies you over the rim of it, and then he nods slowly.
>
> > Well, that's an answer I'll be thinking about on the stairs, my darling, and I won't say whether I agree. I'll remember it, though, and so will Krebbyg, who writes everything down in a book he thinks I haven't found.

In the Jarlaxle version he says the same thing in his own voice, with less noise and a smile. The **Vault of Dragons** proposal is not made tonight. Jarlaxle's offer to a Commander, to return the vault's gold publicly in the party's name in exchange for a letter of introduction to the Lords' Alliance council, is delivered by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) once the vault is within reach. If the party already knows of the Vault of Dragons, add the following to whichever speaker has been reading:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The speaker tips a hand toward you before going on.
>
> > There'll be a favour I'll ask of a Commander when that vault comes open, one the size of the thing itself, and I'd sooner say so now than have you hear it from a stranger. I'll put it to you properly when the time comes.

### Renown Opportunities

Zardoz walks the member to the rail, presses a free ticket into their hand, and waves the plumed hat until the skiff is a hundred feet from the ship.
