# Commander

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Bregan D'aerthe member reaches Renown 25. Zardoz Zord names the rank over a private dinner while Pelsha and Vorn, the two gunslingers he assigns, stand by. If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, Jarlaxle receives them aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* instead. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Ask the company for one favour each quest: a private audience with a Masked Lord, passage in or out of Waterdeep, or the disappearance of one named person for six days.
> - Call a crew of two gunslingers and four drow for one major operation each quest, and meet the first pair.
> - Ask for the captain's company on one operation each quest, or for the substitute he sends when he stays away.
> - Name the Masked Lord the member would trust to carry a letter to the Lords' Alliance, and hear Zardoz's reaction.
>
> All of these benefits belong to the qualifying member. Companions can join an operation but gain no rank, crew or favour of their own.

### Hook

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 25, a playbill with a price circled in red is pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor. This is the company's usual summons from **Kreb Drops the Cover**, and the circled number is the bell at which the member goes to the harbor steps. Hold this Event until **BD Officer** is marked for the member. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the playbill at their first lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the next evening at the same hour. Every qualifying member is invited and recorded separately. Companions who are not Bregan D'aerthe members wait outside and gain nothing.

The six missions bring a member to Renown 19 on their base awards, so about 6 more Renown must come from other sources:

- The bonus lines in each mission.
- The +1 from **Dinner with Zardoz**.
- The Earning Renown list in the Bregan D'aerthe page of the Factions Guide.
- The +1 or +2 in the cabin scene of **Fireball!**.
- The +2 in **Gralhund Villa** when the party holds the Stone.

This rank usually arrives during or after the lair heists. Every procedure below works in whatever order the quests were played.

Where the member is received depends on which outcomes are marked.

- **Zardoz Introduced is marked and the Faire is still in Deepwater Harbor:** Zardoz receives the member in the captain's dining cabin (area J10) aboard the *Eyecatcher*. The circled number is 8, and the skiff leaves the harbor steps at half past eight with Fel'rekt rowing.
- **Zardoz Introduced is marked and the Faire has sailed on Tarsakh 20:** Zardoz receives the member in the cabin of a hired lighter moored at the end of the Dock Ward piers. He stayed behind with a handful of the company and the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, which lies under the lighter. The circled number is 8, and the skiff leaves the harbor steps at half past eight.
- **Jarlaxle Unmasked is marked for the member:** Jarlaxle receives the member in the forward lounge of the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, wherever she is moored. Read the Jarlaxle versions below instead of the Zardoz versions. The member is not blindfolded, and Fel'rekt takes them in through the captain's cabin above, as in **The Killer's Fate**.
- **Zardoz Introduced is not marked:** the circled number is 9, and Krebbyg receives the member in the Seven Masks booking office. Read the Krebbyg version below.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle Baenre (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him). Aboard his ships, the gilded figurehead on each vessel projects a guise that makes every crew member look like an attractive human while the figurehead is in range. The lighter has no figurehead, so the crew look like what they are and Zardoz wears his *hat of disguise* as Zord.
>
> - Nobody in this Event says the name Jarlaxle in speech or readaloud unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member being addressed. Until then every Bregan D'aerthe speaker says "the captain" or "J."
> - This Event does not mark **Jarlaxle Unmasked**, and a member who guesses the name aloud does not mark it either. Zardoz answers a guess with a delighted roar and a free ticket.
> - Zardoz never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan.
>
> This is not the member's first look at the man at the top of the company, since several earlier events put the member in front of one of his personas. The rank changes how much of the company's strength the member may borrow. Jarlaxle grants it because a Commander who can call a crew, a favour or the captain is one who will come back with something worth having. Tonight he asks one question about the political picture in Waterdeep.
>
> Jarlaxle knows the Cassalanters are bound to Asmodeus and have pledged their children in a contract, because his doppelganger agent Vessa, who has moved in their social circle for months, reads thoughts. No speaker in this Event tells a member who has not worked it out, and a favour never buys the answer.
>
> - If **Cassalanter Pact Shared with BD** is not marked and a member says in their own words that the Cassalanters are bound to a devil, or shows proof, the speaker stops deflecting and admits the company has known for some time, in the words of the admission in **The Theater's Back Room**, without naming Vessa. Mark the outcome.
> - If it is already marked, the speaker discusses it plainly.
>
> **Kreb Unmasked** is marked for nearly every member who reaches Renown 25, because **Kreb Drops the Cover** runs after the second mission. Krebbyg and Fel'rekt drop their human cover in front of such a member. If it is not marked, Krebbyg stays in his Kreb Sorrush cover and keeps out of the scene.
>
> The favours, crews and captain's company are each limited by quest, and the **Commander Ledger** later in this Event records the limits per member and across the party. A **quest** means one of the campaign's ten named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**. Events, faction missions and rank events are not quests. A use counts against the quest the party is in when the member asks.

### Naming the Rank

If Zardoz is receiving the member aboard the *Eyecatcher*, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Lamplight glows on the gold filigree of the captain's dining cabin. The mahogany table is laid for two with a bottle of wine and a plate of small fried fish. Zardoz stands behind it in his scarlet shirt, with the plumed hat on the sideboard, and he is pouring before you reach a chair. A tall woman with white hair in a long braid stands against the door with her hands folded. A lean man in a stevedore's coat leans beside the stern window with his arms crossed. Both look like handsome humans, and neither smiles.
>
> > Commander! Salt and fucking thunder, my darling, sit down! I've wanted to say that word to you for a fortnight. Krebbyg wouldn't let me. It means the company has watched you long enough and wants to keep watching. It owes you three favours, two gunslingers and four of the quietest people on the coast. Eat a fish first, and I'll explain every bit of it badly.

If Zardoz is receiving the member aboard the lighter after Tarsakh 20, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cabin of the lighter is low and smells of tar and old bunting. The Faire's crimson pennants are nailed along the beams, and the lamp throws red light on everything. Zardoz sits at a plank table with the plumed hat beside a bottle and two glasses, and the harbor slaps at the hull. A grey-skinned drow woman with white hair in a long braid stands against the door with her hands folded. A lean drow man in a stevedore's coat leans beside the porthole with his arms crossed. Neither smiles.
>
> > Commander! Salt and fucking thunder, my darling, sit down, and forgive the furnishings! The grand ships have sailed, and I've been left with a bucket and a very loyal gull. It means the company has watched you long enough and wants to keep watching. It owes you three favours, two gunslingers and four of the quietest people on the coast. Have a glass, and I'll explain all of it badly.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead of either Zardoz version:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The forward lounge of the *Scarlet Marpenoth* is a low brass cabin with the hull curving overhead. Amber lanterns burn against the dark harbor outside the porthole. Jarlaxle sits at a chart table with his coat off and his boots crossed on the opposite chair. A wide-brimmed hat with a long white feather hangs on a hook beside him. A white-haired drow woman stands at the door with her hands folded, and a lean drow man leans beside the porthole. Jarlaxle looks up from a chart he doesn't bother to fold away.

If **Zardoz Introduced** is not marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The booking office of the Seven Masks smells of stage paint and cold tea. Krebbyg sits on the edge of the desk with a gilt-edged card in one hand and a schedule in the other. A tall woman and a lean man in stagehands' aprons wait against the wall by the locked door. Krebbyg stands up, flaps the card at you and starts talking before he has finished standing.
>
> > Darling, there you are, it's Commander, don't ask me how it works because I've only just been told! The captain says he'd love to tell you himself, and he'll write when he's got a free evening. Those two are Pelsha and Vorn, they're yours. Fel will go through the rest in a minute, because he explains it better than I do and never skips the limits.

In that case Krebbyg gives each procedure below in his own words, and Zardoz does not appear. Krebbyg stays the member's only contact, the member does not mark **Zardoz Introduced**, and the substitute replaces the captain's company.

> [!social]**The Host Who Pours First**
>
> Zardoz Zord (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in the guise of an Illuskan human, he/him) :: the flamboyant captain of the Sea Maidens Faire and head of Bregan D'aerthe, delighted to hand out the company's best toys.
>
> Zardoz is loud, generous and warm, and he fills every silence at once. He answers a hard question with a story, an offer of free tickets or a shout for the next dish, and the question he cares about is buried in the middle of the story. When the cover strains, his accent thins, his words turn elegant, and he calls the member "my dear" in a silky voice. He swears happily and often.
>
> Zardoz is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The three favours, and what each one will and will not do.
> - Pelsha and Vorn, and the four drow who go with them.
> - The captain's company, which he calls a very good idea for other people.
> - Free tickets, at length.
>
> Any character who watches Zardoz while he talks and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that the delight is real and that a steadier attention underneath it is waiting for the member to say something about the city. A member who has marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** needs no check and knows whose attention it is. A failed check shows a host who wants another fish eaten.

> [!qna]**Why me?**
>
> Zardoz tops up both glasses before he answers.
>
> > Because you came back, my darling, and that's rarer than being clever! I've had a great many clever people through this cabin who couldn't be found the week after. Krebbyg reads me the file aloud while I'm eating, and when he reached the Eye I stopped chewing, which is the highest compliment I know.

> [!qna]**What does the company want in return?**
>
> > Why, nothing, my friend, not tonight, because you've paid already, in advance, which is unheard of! You'll go on doing what you do, and when the company has a small thing to ask, I'll ask it to your face and not through a hat. There's one question I'd like answered before the evening ends, but that comes after the pudding.

### The Three Favours

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz lays three playing cards face up between the glasses, a queen in a mask, a ship under full sail and a sleeping man in a nightcap. He taps each one with the stem of his glass as he talks.
>
> > One favour a quest, my darling, and I'll say it twice, because everyone hears it wrong! One, a private audience with a Masked Lord, which gets you a seat in a room and no promises. Two, passage into Waterdeep or out of it without the Watch knowing, for one party, one direction and one window of twelve hours. Three, I make one named person vanish for six days, with no harm done and not a scrap of paper afterward. You pick one a quest, not one of each. You'd be amazed how many people nod politely and try for all three.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle slides three playing cards across the chart, a queen in a mask, a ship under full sail and a sleeping man in a nightcap. He lays one finger on each in turn without looking down.
>
> > Once each quest, I can do one of three things for you. A private audience with a Masked Lord, with your identity kept out of it and the agenda yours, since I only arrange the introduction. Passage into Waterdeep or out of it without the City Watch hearing of it, for one party, in one direction, within one window of twelve hours. Or the disappearance of one named person for six days, with no harm done, no questions asked and no record kept. You will notice I said one of three and not one of each. People in your position sometimes hear it the other way round.

> [!qna]**How do you do it?**
>
> If Zardoz is speaking:
>
> > With a great deal of free fried fish, my friend, and a better class of acquaintance than you'd credit! Don't ask me for the particulars, because the particulars are why it works.
>
> If Jarlaxle is speaking:
>
> > Competently.

> [!qna]**Can the favour fail?**
>
> > It can be refused, my darling. If I refuse it, it isn't spent, and you ask again with something I can actually do. What it can't do is go wrong halfway and leave you holding the bill. Once I've said yes, it's done, or I'll tell you why not.

> [!exploration]**One Favour per Quest**
>
> Each Commander can ask for one favour per quest. The member leaves a note in the left pocket of the grey coat on peg seven in the Seven Masks cloakroom, or gives it to Krebbyg at the stage door, naming the favour and its details. By the next dusk the company pushes a playbill under the door of Trollskull Manor. A circled 1 means yes, and a circled 0 means the favour is refused and not spent.
>
> - **Use:** The use counts against the quest the party is in when the company says yes. A favour nobody called before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Refusals:** The company refuses any target who is the Open Lord or a Masked Lord (for the disappearance), any child, any sitting Watch officer or member of the Lords' Alliance council, anyone inside a warded stronghold, a vault or Castle Waterdeep, and anyone the company has reason to protect, which includes every Notable Figure that Bregan D'aerthe lists as its own. A member can send a new note after a refusal.
> - **Loss rule:** The company withdraws every favour from a member who names the company or its drow to a Watch officer or to anyone outside it, whose Renown falls below 25, or who marks **BD Contact Severed**. A member whose Renown returns to 25 has the favours again.
> - **Shared quests:** Two Commanders in the same party each have their own favour and use it separately.
>
> **A: The Audience.** The member names the Lord by the name they believe the Lord wears. The company can reach four Lords, and it answers by the next dusk without confirming the Lord's name, since the Lord stays masked.
>
> | The member names | The masked Lord is | The audience is |
> |---|---|---|
> | Mirt, or a Harper or a moneylender | Mirt | A back room at his Sea Ward manor, ten minutes after dark |
> | Durnan, or the Yawning Portal | Durnan | The Yawning Portal's cellar, at the hour the taproom closes |
> | Ulkoria Stonemarrow, or the Watchful Order | Ulkoria Stonemarrow | A reading room in the Castle Ward, an hour before noon |
> | Jelenn Urmbrusk, or a noble house | Jelenn Urmbrusk | A closed carriage in the Sea Ward, on a drive of fifteen minutes |
>
> If the member names someone else, the company says it cannot arrange that Lord, and the favour is not spent. The audience happens within three days of the yes. The member may bring one companion. The Lord listens for ten minutes and answers in character from that Lord's Notable Figures page, having agreed to listen and promised nothing.
>
> **B: The Passage.** The member names the direction, the hour the twelve-hour window opens, and up to six people, the member included. The company needs six hours' notice. Out of Waterdeep, Fel'rekt's skiff leaves from a Dock Ward quay, or a Faire provisioning wagon takes the party through the gate under the canvas. Into Waterdeep, the skiff lands the party on the same quay, or the wagon carries them in. The passage covers the travellers and what they carry on their persons, not cargo, a prisoner or a creature larger than Medium. It ends when the window closes.
>
> **C: The Disappearance.** The member names one person and the place where that person will be within the next three days. The company takes the person from that place within those three days, keeps them for six days in a comfortable berth, and returns them unharmed to the same place with no memory of the interval. Nothing points to the member or the company. A person outside the city's wards at the time is refused.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Favours Cannot Do**
>
> - **Audience:** It guarantees a meeting and nothing about the outcome. A Lord treats a member who arrives by Bregan D'aerthe's arrangement with polite caution.
> - **Passage:** It cannot be extended or stacked on a second window in the same quest.
> - **Disappearance:** It cannot remove someone for good. The person returns on the seventh morning whatever the member does, and if the member asks the company to keep them longer, the company declines and does not spend the use.

### The Crews

Zardoz turns to the two figures by the door, and the member meets the first pair.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets down his glass and waves toward the tall woman by the door, who steps forward with her hands still folded. He then waves toward the lean man at the window, who unfolds his arms and stays where he is.
>
> > This is Pelsha, who is older than anyone in this room and has the patience to prove it! That is Vorn, who asks more questions before an operation than you've thought of in your life. Between them they can shoot the buttons off a magistrate's coat from across a street. The four people they bring are better at not being noticed than anybody I've ever paid. One major operation a quest, you ask, and they come.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, Jarlaxle says the same in his own words:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle gestures with two fingers toward the woman at the door and then the man at the porthole, and both incline their heads.
>
> > Pelsha and Vorn have worked for me longer than you have been alive, and they follow orders within the scope of an operation. They will not take risks that serve your plan at the expense of the company, and within that limit you will find them reliable. For one major operation each quest they come with four of my people, and they do what you plan.

> [!social]**The Gunslingers at the Door**
>
> Pelsha (Neutral, Drow, she/her) and Vorn (Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: the two drow gunslingers the company assigns to a Commander, the first of two pairs.
>
> Pelsha is calm and unhurried and treats recklessness in colleagues as bad weather. She gives plain answers in short, complete sentences and says "Understood" when she has heard a plan and will carry it out. Vorn speaks seldom during an operation and often before one, asking questions the member hadn't thought of. He swears rarely and flatly, and Pelsha never swears. Both keep their right hands near their pistols while they talk, and neither will say anything about the captain.
>
> Pelsha and Vorn are happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What an operation needs before they can join it, which is mostly Vorn's questions.
> - What they will refuse, which Pelsha answers.
> - The four drow who go with them, whom they call "the crew" and do not name.
>
> Any character who watches Vorn through his questions and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that each question is a gap in the plan that he intends to cover himself, and that he knows where the plan will fail before the member finishes explaining it.

> [!qna]**What will you do?**
>
> Pelsha unfolds her hands before she answers.
>
> > We do whatever the plan needs within reason, from cover and from range. That mostly means shooting what you point at and holding a door while you work. We don't take the lead, because it's your operation. We don't do anything that would bring a Watch officer to the captain's door.

> [!qna]**What won't you do?**
>
> Vorn looks at Pelsha before he answers.
>
> > We won't kill a child or a bystander. We won't strike a Watch officer who hasn't struck first. We won't show a drow face to anyone who shouldn't see one. We leave when the objective is done, when the plan can't work, or when either of us is down. That last one is Pelsha's rule, and I'd keep it.

> [!qna]**What if one of you dies?**
>
> > Then the captain finds someone else, and it takes a tenday. You'll have spent your use for the quest, so please don't spend us on a bad plan.

> [!exploration]**One Crew per Quest**
>
> Each Commander can call one crew for one major operation per quest. An **operation** is a Bregan D'aerthe mission, a lair heist, or any outing the member names that has an objective, a place and an hour. The member gives Krebbyg or the grey coat on peg seven three days' notice with the objective, the place and the hour the operation begins.
>
> - **Meeting:** The crew waits at a surface place the member names, at the hour named. Pelsha takes the member's plan, and Vorn asks his questions on the first evening.
> - **Limits:** The crew refuses to attack children, bystanders or a Watch officer who has not struck first, to show the company's drow to anyone outside it, or to act against the company or the Faire. It withdraws when the objective is finished, abandoned or impossible, or when either gunslinger falls.
> - **Use:** The use counts against the quest the party is in when the crew begins the operation. A use that nobody called before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Losses:** A crew member who dies or is captured is replaced a tenday later, and the use stays spent.
> - **Shared jobs:** Two Commanders who ask for the same operation share one crew, and only the first to ask spends a use. Two different operations in a quest can each have a crew, one per member, up to the two crews that exist.
> - **Not with the captain:** The crew and the captain's company do not go on the same operation. Zardoz says "I won't put the whole ship in one doorway."
> - **Loss rule:** The crew is withdrawn by the same events that withdraw the favours.

> [!gamemaster]**The Commander Ledger**
>
> Record for each Commander:
>
> - The quest of the last favour.
> - Which crew is assigned.
> - The quest of the last crew use.
> - The quest of the last use of the captain's company.
> - The Lord the member named in **The Seat** below.
>
> The company has two crews and no more. The first Commander named in a party is assigned Crew One and the second Crew Two. Any further Commander waits for whichever crew is free, because a crew serves one operation at a time. Requests are filled in the order made.
>
> | Crew | Gunslingers | Drow | Notes |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | One | Pelsha (Drow Gunslinger) and Vorn (Drow Gunslinger) | four drow (**Scout**) | The guide's Commander row: two Drow Gunslingers and four drow |
> | Two | Ilmra Kelnozz (she/her) and Brythe Mizzrym (he/him), both Drow Gunslingers | four drow (**Scout**) | Invented for this Event |
>
> Ilmra and Brythe use the same stat blocks and obey the same limits. Ilmra is blunt and Brythe is polite, and neither says anything about the captain.
>
> #### The Crew as Allies
>
> - Use the **Drow Gunslinger** stat block from *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, page 201, for each gunslinger, since there is no 2024 equivalent.
> - Use the 2024 **Scout** for each of the four drow. Add Darkvision 120 feet, Fey Ancestry and Sunlight Sensitivity from the gunslinger's traits, and swap the Longbow for a Hand Crossbow (range 30/120 feet).
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, for a party of levels 5 to 10, a Drow Gunslinger is a CR 4 ally with Power 38 and a Scout is a CR 1/2 ally with Power 12, so a full crew adds 124. At levels 11 and above a gunslinger is 32 and a Scout is 7, so a full crew adds 92. Add only the crew members who take part in a combat to the participating characters' Party Power, then recalculate the difficulty and the day cost.
>
> A full crew roughly doubles a party of four, so build the fight around the larger total or keep the crew out of it. At level 7, a party of four has Power 164, and with a crew it is 288.
>
> #### The Crew's Tactics
> Pelsha stays within 30 feet of the member and fires at whatever threatens the member or the drow. During combat, the crew:
>
> - Fires from cover at the nearest threat, and the Scouts flank the same enemy as the gunslingers.
> - Offers surrender before it fires on anyone who is not attacking.
> - Falls back toward the exit the member named if either gunslinger is reduced to half their Hit Points.
> - Stops when the member calls it off.

### The Captain's Company

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets down the glass and rubs his hands together. His face runs through several expressions before it settles on a grin.
>
> > Now, the bit I'm least keen on! If you want the captain himself on an operation, say so by dusk the day before, and you'll get him for the whole of it. Fair warning, he's dreadfully expensive. I'm told he fights like a man with a great deal to lose. But he doesn't come for everyone, my darling, so if he sends his regrets, don't take it personally. I'll send someone better suited.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle lifts his glass and speaks without looking at the member.
>
> > Once each quest, I come with you. I fight at full capacity and I follow your plan, because it is your operation. If the plan is wrong, I will tell you before we go in. Use me when the situation justifies it.

> [!exploration]**The Captain's Company**
>
> Each Commander can ask once per quest for Jarlaxle to join one operation. The member gives Krebbyg or the grey coat on peg seven notice by dusk on the day before the operation begins.
>
> - **If Jarlaxle Unmasked is marked for the member:** Jarlaxle comes. He meets the party at the hour named, wearing his *hat of disguise* as a lean human swordsman with a feathered hat. The member introduces him to companions as "the captain." He fights and leaves without comment.
> - **If Jarlaxle Unmasked is not marked:** The company sends a substitute, and the use is spent. Krebbyg brings Zardoz's regrets on a gilt ticket, and Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, both Drow Gunslingers, join the operation. They obey the same limits as a crew.
> - **One captain only:** There is one Jarlaxle. If two Commanders ask for different operations that start on the same day, the first to ask gets him and the second gets the substitute. If two Commanders ask for the same operation, only the first spends a use.
> - **Limits:** He refuses an operation against the company or the Faire, and he will not stay past the operation's end. If he is reduced below 30 Hit Points he looks for a way out, and he does not sacrifice himself.
> - **Not with the crew:** The captain and a crew do not go on the same operation.
> - **Loss rule:** The same events that withdraw the favours withdraw the company.

> [!gamemaster]**Jarlaxle as an Ally**
>
> Use the 2024 **Pirate Captain** for Jarlaxle, since there is no 2024 Swashbuckler. He targets the most dangerous adversary first and uses his items freely. If he is reduced below 30 of his 84 Hit Points, he uses an escape item and leaves for the day.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, a Pirate Captain is a CR 6 ally with Power 65 for levels 5 to 10 and 50 for levels 11 and above. Add that to the party's Power and recalculate. If the encounter is heavy on his items, count him as CR 8 (Power 85). The substitute is two Drow Gunslingers, for Power 76 at levels 5 to 10 and 64 at 11 and above.

### The Seat

When the pudding is cleared, Zardoz asks the question the company has been collecting answers to since the first mission.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A steward in a white jacket clears the plates. Zardoz leans his elbows on the table among the crumbs and rests his chin on his fist. He watches you closely for the first time since you sat down.
>
> > Now, my darling, a thing I've wanted to ask you all evening! Say you had to hand a letter to a single Masked Lord, one you trust to carry it to the Lords' Alliance council and read it first. Which Lord would it be? You needn't know the name. Just tell me who you'd go to.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle sets down his glass and folds his hands on the chart with a pleasant expression.
>
> > I would like to ask you one thing about the city, and I would like an honest answer. If you had to give a letter to a single Masked Lord, one you trusted to carry it to the Lords' Alliance council and to read it first, whom would you choose? You need not know the name.

> [!exploration]**Naming the Seat**
