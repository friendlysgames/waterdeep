# Design Notes: Commander

## Benefits as Procedures

The restored draft listed three favours, a pair of gunslingers and the captain's company as sentences, and left "once per quest" without an owner or a definition. Each benefit is now a procedure that belongs to one member.

- A **quest** means one of the ten named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**. The use counts against the quest the party is in when the company says yes, which matches the **Officer** page.
- The favours have a contact (the grey coat on peg seven or Krebbyg), a notice period, a refusal list and a loss rule.
- The audience has a fixed table of four Lords, each with a place and hour, so nothing waits on GM improvisation.
- The disappearance and the passage keep the draft's limits of six days and twelve hours, and add the places and persons they cannot touch.

## The Crew Ledger

The draft had one Pelsha and one Vorn for any number of Commanders. The ledger now has two crews and no more.

- The first Commander in a party holds Crew One, the second holds Crew Two, and later Commanders wait for whichever is free.
- Two Commanders who ask for the same operation share one crew, and the first to ask spends the use, as in the Doom Raiders rank page.
- Crew Two is Ilmra Kelnozz and Brythe Mizzrym, who are invented.
- The crew and the captain's company cannot go on the same operation, because the roster plus Jarlaxle roughly doubles a four-person party. A member can still use both in one quest on different operations.

Pelsha and Vorn are Drow Gunslingers from *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* (Power 38 each). The four drow are 2024 **Scouts** with drow senses added (Power 12 each). A full crew is 124 at levels 5 to 10 and 92 at 11 and above. Jarlaxle as an ally is a **Pirate Captain** (Power 65 at levels 5 to 10, 50 at 11 and above, 85 if his items are heavily counted). The substitute pair is Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, also Drow Gunslingers.

## The Captain and the Substitute

The draft had Jarlaxle accompany the party on one mission per quest, with no gate. It is now gated on **Jarlaxle Unmasked**, because his presence in the field is the clearest way a member would see who he is. A member without the outcome gets Zardoz's regrets on a gilt ticket, and the lieutenants take his place.

This is not the first rank event Jarlaxle appears in, since the earlier ones feature Nevercott and Zardoz, who are him. What is new is that a Commander who holds **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is received by him in the forward lounge of the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, with the draft's own opening lines.

If the Faire has sailed on Tarsakh 20, Zardoz stays behind with a handful of the company and the submarine. The event places him in the cabin of a hired lighter above the *Marpenoth*'s mooring. The lighter is invented.

## The Seat

The decision beat is the question Jarlaxle asks a Commander: which Masked Lord would the member trust to carry a letter to the Lords' Alliance council. The Factions Guide says the company has been collecting answers about which Masked Lords carry weight, and this is where the member gives one.

The answer costs something. The company knows which Lord the member leans on and watches that Lord's door. In return, the first audience favour the member asks for reaches that Lord for half an hour instead of ten minutes. A member who guesses wrong has one more attempt, and a member who declines loses nothing.

The four reachable Lords are Mirt, Durnan, Ulkoria Stonemarrow and Jelenn Urmbrusk, each confirmed as a Masked Lord on their Notable Figures pages (Ulkoria's is "never confirmed in her presence", and Durnan's is "secret"). Zardoz never confirms any of them aloud.

## The Vault Proposal

The draft said **Vault of Dragons** hands Commander members Jarlaxle's Lords' Alliance proposal. The Factions Guide (line 33) places it at "Dread Lord renown", which is the Doom Raiders' top rank. Bregan D'aerthe's ranks are Initiate, Soldier, Officer, Commander and Houseless Noble, so this event keeps the draft's reader, the Commander.

The proposal is not made here. The outcome tells **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) that a member holding it receives the proposal and that the member's Seat is the Lord Jarlaxle asks to carry the letter. A speaker names the Vault tonight only if the party already knows of it.

## Renown and Timing

The six missions award 2, 2, 3, 3, 4 and 4 base Renown, which brings a member from Renown 1 to 19. Rank 25 needs at least six more from outside the missions:

- The bonus lines in each mission.
- The +1 from **Dinner with Zardoz**.
- The Earning Renown list in the Factions Guide: +1 for new intelligence, +2 for a Golorr artifact, +1 for protecting Nar'l's cover, +1 for using the Faire as cover, +2 for taking the shipping records without the company's knowledge, and +1 once per quest for unexpected creativity.
- The +1 or +2 in the cabin scene of **Fireball!** (event 4).
- The +2 for a delivered Stone in **Gralhund Villa** (event 9).

This event awards none of its own. The rank usually lands during or after the lair heists. Renown 50 needs content from **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**, as in the Doom Raiders and Houseless Noble pages.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Ilmra Kelnozz and Brythe Mizzrym (Crew Two), the lighter, the ledger fields, the Masked Lord table's places and hours, the Seat and its benefit, and the substitute rule. Pelsha and Vorn are invented in the draft, with alignments (Neutral) and the voices written here. They have no voice profile. Pelsha is calm, plain and complete, and Vorn asks questions and speaks flatly. Neither swears much.
