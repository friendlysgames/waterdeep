# Design Notes: Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting

## An Individual Offer and an Individual Severance

The restored draft marked **Bregan D'aerthe Joined** and **BD Contact Severed** as party-wide flags, so one report to the Watch closed Bregan D'aerthe to everyone for the whole campaign. The event now follows the Harpers and Doom Raiders models. Every character who belongs to no other faction is a candidate and answers for themselves, and both outcomes are marked with the character's name.

The Watch branch is the hard case. A character who gives the sergeant their name is severed for good, which is the rule the Factions guide and **Trollskull Alley** both state. The watchers still vanish at dusk for the whole party. Nevercott keeps his word to "be in touch regardless" and calls on Ches 13 for any candidate who did not give a name. The source says contact ends "for the time being", and the guide says "permanently". The event follows the guide for the severed character and the source for everyone else, so one player's report cannot remove another player's story.

An attack on Nevercott also marks **BD Contact Severed**. The source gives him no combat or escape behavior. The restored draft invented a glass bead and an obscuring cloud that kept him away for three tendays, and both were cut. He now walks out through the crowd, and the attacker is closed out like a reporter.

## The Surveillance Cast

Appendix B and WDH name the watchers as the three lieutenants: Fel'rekt Lafeen and Krebbyg Masq'il'yr by night, Soluun Xibrindas by day. The draft ignored all three and made Ryvarra the watcher. She is now the Portal observer her Notable Figures page describes, with three months of tenure, a DC 14 Insight tell and a habit of leaving a table with a full glass and a straight chair. **Finding Floon** gives her two weeks, and **The Neighbors** places her in the alley. The event keeps her at the Portal and adds an occasional morning walk past the manor, which reconciles the two without editing either.

The lieutenants keep a fixed shift: the pair by night, Soluun by day. Soluun's day watch and his Dock Ward killings are the same man on two schedules, which fits WDH's note that he stays out of the sun. The killings appear only in GM text, so the party cannot connect him to **The Dockside Killer** until that mission shows them. The elf or half-elf glance in the daytime confrontation plants the habit without a consequence.

Fel'rekt's whisper in the night confrontation is the only speech any watcher gets. It avoids Krebbyg's name so that **Kreb Drops the Cover** keeps its reveal.

## The Haberdasher Scene

Appendix B has Nevercott keep the fiction until a drow character steps aside. That fails for a party without a drow, so the event has him wait a quarter of an hour for a drow candidate and then ask the first candidate to show him the courtyard. Every candidate gets the scene separately. The offer and its key phrases come from the source: the eye patch calling card, the name of Bregan D'aerthe, the black card with a silver ship, "declining costs you nothing" and "I'll be in touch shortly regardless". He does not brief the first assignment here. He delivers **The Handkerchief and the Girl** in person at Trollskull Manor at dusk on Ches 20, the eve of the Twin Parades, which replaces the draft's unsigned theater tickets.

Nevercott has no voice profile in `character-voices`, so he is voiced from Appendix B: a courteous, amused Sea Ward haberdasher with long, well-balanced sentences, no swearing and one precise question for every three he answers. He never names Jarlaxle and never confirms anything, and a player's guess meets polite incomprehension. A profile for him belongs in the skill if the user wants one.

## Initiate Benefits

The guide gave Initiates a safe house aboard the *Heartbreaker* or *Hellraiser* and equipment at cost, with no procedure for either. The event writes both. The safe house has a gangway, a hammock, a three-night limit and a loss rule, and Fel'rekt's stall has a price table. The prices, the three-night limit, the ten-bolt sets, the lenses and the pre-order through the Seven Masks box office are invented. The Ches 21 opening date follows the campaign calendar, which has the Faire fleet arrive that morning. The prices are modest but still matter to a party at 2nd level. Any of them can be cut or changed without affecting the rest of the event.

## Omissions and Out-of-Scope Notes

The renovation financing that **The Factions Come Calling** attaches to Bregan D'aerthe (up to 1,250 gp, repayable in "operational access") is not carried here, since no source text decides how Nevercott would offer it. Quilm, the drow bouncer candidate, has no role in the event.

Marek Dunmere, the Watch sergeant, is invented for this event, and no Notable Figures page exists for him. He is voiced from the event text. The Notable Figures pages for Soluun, Fel'rekt and Krebbyg list the **Drow Gunslinger** stat block. The events use it for Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, and use the 2024 **Scout** for Soluun, as **The Dockside Killer** does.

Two inconsistencies remain outside this event. The Factions guide and **The Factions Come Calling** still call both outcomes party-wide and the Watch report an unconditional, permanent closure. **Finding Floon** and **The Neighbors** disagree with each other and with the Ryvarra page about her tenure and where she is seen.
