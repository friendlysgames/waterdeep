# Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs during **The Factions Come Calling**. It begins on Ches 3, when Bregan D'aerthe's watchers start following the party, and ends when J.B. Nevercott leaves Trollskull Manor after his visit. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Spot the drow watching Trollskull Manor and work out whom they follow.
> - Notice Ryvarra at her reserved table in the Yawning Portal and catch the slip in her accent.
> - Wait, confront the watchers and find a black eye patch, or report them to Sergeant Marek Dunmere at the North Ward Watch station.
> - Receive J.B. Nevercott, a haberdasher, who names Bregan D'aerthe and offers a first assignment to a candidate who steps aside.
> - Decide individually whether to join, and walk away freely if they refuse.
> - Visit Fel'rekt Lafeen's stall and the Faire's ships to use the Initiate Benefits after joining.
>
> #### Candidates and Companions
>
> Every character who belongs to no other faction is a candidate, whatever their ancestry, and each candidate answers for themselves. The watchers stay closest to drow characters. Nevercott makes no offer to characters who belong to another faction. They and any companions can stay for the hat patter but gain nothing from the visit.
>
> If no character qualifies, Nevercott talks about hats for a quarter of an hour, sells nothing and leaves, and the Event ends with no membership outcome.
>
> #### What Is Actually True
>
> J.B. Nevercott is Jarlaxle Baenre in a *hat of disguise*, so nobody in the room can describe his face afterward. He never admits to being anyone else, even if a player guesses, and nobody in this Event says Jarlaxle's name aloud. **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked later, for each member separately. Until then, Bregan D'aerthe speakers say "the captain" or "J."
>
> Jarlaxle is testing the party to see who notices the watchers and what they do about it. A report to the Watch counts as an answer.
>
> The watchers are Jarlaxle's three lieutenants. Fel'rekt Lafeen and Krebbyg Masq'il'yr shadow the party from dusk until dawn, and Soluun Xibrindas takes the day from shade and doorways. After dark Soluun also hunts elf and half-elf sailors in the Dock Ward on his own account, the subject of the Doom Raiders mission **The Dockside Killer**. Jarlaxle knows and has not decided what to do. Nothing in this Event lets the party connect the two, and Soluun never speaks to anyone.
>
> Ryvarra has watched the well from the Yawning Portal's balcony for three months and sends Jarlaxle a report every tenday. Her latest report names the party, and she tells the lieutenants where the party goes.
>
> #### When Things Happen
>
> - **Ches 3:** the watchers pick up the party.
> - **If the party confronts a watcher:** Nevercott knocks at 10 a.m. the next morning.
> - **If the party does nothing:** Nevercott knocks at 10 a.m. on Ches 10.
> - **If a candidate reports the watchers to the Watch:** the watchers are gone by dusk the same day, and the Watch's letter arrives the next morning. Nevercott still knocks at 10 a.m. on Ches 13 for every candidate who did not report.
> - **If several branches occur:** the earliest date applies, and Nevercott speaks only to candidates who have not reported.

### Shadows on the Alley

> [!gamemaster]**Who Is Watching and When**
>
> Fel'rekt and Krebbyg work the night in turns, so one of them is always at the mouth of Trollskull Alley or on a roof across from the manor. Soluun takes the day from doorways and awnings on the shaded side of the street. If the party includes a drow character, the watchers follow that character. If not, they follow the characters who belong to no faction. All three move through crowds without being remembered.

> [!exploration]**A Glimpse of the Watchers**
>
> A character with a passive Wisdom (Perception) score of 18 or higher glimpses a watcher once each night and once each day while the watch lasts. A character with a lower score can spend an hour watching the street, once per day, and glimpses the same figure on a successful **DC 18 Wisdom (Perception)** check.

If a character glimpses the night watchers, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Late in the evening, while you close the shutters on the front windows of Trollskull Manor, you notice two slender figures standing in the mouth of the alley opposite, just beyond the lamplight. The nearer one turns his head, and his white hair and dark grey face catch the glow from your window. When you look again, the alley is empty.

If a character glimpses the day watcher, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A delivery cart blocks half of Trollskull Alley when you notice a hooded figure standing in a shuttered doorway across the street, keeping to the shade. A narrow face with dark grey skin turns toward the manor and then away. The figure stays still until the cart rolls on, and then the doorway is empty.

> [!exploration]**Whom They Follow**
>
> Any character who has glimpsed a watcher can spend a day or an evening tracking where the watchers stand and make a **DC 15 Wisdom (Insight)** check.
>
> - **Success:** The watchers pay particular attention to the drow in the party, or to the characters who belong to no faction if there are no drow.
> - **Failure:** The character is sure the manor is watched but cannot say whom the watchers care about.
>
> - **Result of 20 or higher:** The character also works out that the watchers keep two shifts, a pair at night and a single figure by day.

### The Reserved Table

> [!gamemaster]**The Observer at the Portal**
>
> Ryvarra (Neutral, Drow, she/her) sits at the reserved table nearest the rail of the Yawning Portal's observation balcony every evening from sundown until closing, and any character who visits the Portal during the renovation can see her. If the party marked **Ryvarra Identified** in **The Neighbors**, where she walks through Trollskull Alley one morning to look at the manor, the characters recognize her on sight. Otherwise she is a stranger.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A handful of tables on the balcony above the Yawning Portal's taproom look down on the well, and at the one nearest the rail sits a woman in layered Calishite silks the color of dark honey, with a patterned scarf over her hair. A full glass of red wine stands beside an untouched plate of cheese, and she watches the crowd around the well with mild interest. When she notices you looking, she inclines her head politely.
>
> > "Good evening, honoured friends. Forgive me for staring. I've been in Waterdeep three months now, and the well still hasn't stopped surprising me."

> [!social]**The Cloth Merchant**
>
> Ryvarra (Neutral, Drow, she/her) :: Jarlaxle's field observer at the Yawning Portal, who plays a Calimshan cloth merchant in the middle of a long negotiation.
>
> Ryvarra is Friendly in manner and Indifferent in fact. She speaks in polished merchant's small talk and calls the party "honoured friends". Her ordinary questions each add a line to her next report. She never drinks from her glass and never writes anything down in public. When a question turns toward her, she answers with a detail about cloth and asks something back.
>
> Ryvarra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Her trade, which is silk from Calimport, and the guild that is slow to agree on a price.
> - The Yawning Portal and the people who climb back out of the well alive.
> - The party's own recent adventures, which she asks about in more detail than a merchant would.
>
> She will not discuss where she lodges, whom she writes to, or anything about Bregan D'aerthe.

> [!qna]**Your trade?**
>
> Ryvarra turns the stem of her glass a quarter turn on the table without lifting it.
>
> > "Silk, mostly, and some fine cotton, all out of Calimport. I've spent three months waiting for a guild here to agree to a price, and negotiations are slow. In the meantime I've found this is the most interesting table in the city."

> [!qna]**Why the Portal?**
>
> > "A merchant needs to know who is passing through, and the well brings up a better class of traveler than the docks do. I like to see who goes down and who comes back."

> [!qna]**Aren't you drinking?**
>
> Her eyes go to the glass and come back with no change in her smile.
>
> > "It's a Tethyrian red, and it wants an hour to open, and I'm in no hurry. Please tell me about your evening instead. I did hear there was a troll."

> [!qna]**What have you seen of us?**
>
> > "I'm afraid I'm only a spectator, honoured friends. I saw the brawl and the troll, and I confess I wondered who fought first and who stood back. Merchants are curious about that sort of thing, and it helps us decide whom to trust with an order."

> [!exploration]**A Slip in the Accent**
>
> Any character who talks with Ryvarra for a few minutes and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** check notices two things. Her Calimshan accent slips on certain words into a flatter, more precise cadence, and the wine in her glass is exactly as full as when she sat down.
>
> - **Result of 19 or higher:** The character also notices that her questions about the brawl and the troll concern who struck first and how long each fight lasted, more than a merchant would care to know.

If a character accuses her of watching the party, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Ryvarra listens to the whole accusation with her head tilted a polite degree, and then she lays her napkin beside her plate and rises.
>
> > "I'm afraid you've mistaken me for someone more interesting, honoured friends. Please enjoy your evening, and do give my regards to the barman."
>
> She goes down the balcony stairs without hurrying. When you look at the table again, the glass is still full and the chair is pushed square to the table, and the people at the next table cannot tell you what she looked like.

Ryvarra does not return to that table. From the next evening she sits at the far end of the balcony, and the watchers keep their shifts at the manor whatever the party says to her.

### What the Party Does

#### The Party Watches Back

A party that notices the watchers and does nothing runs the same branch as one that never notices them. The glimpses repeat each day and night until Nevercott's knock on Ches 10. A character who tries to follow a watcher loses them within three streets, because the watchers split up at the first corner and know the crowds better than any visitor. Proceed to **The Haberdasher at the Door**.

#### The Party Confronts the Watchers

A character who crosses the street toward a watcher meets no resistance. The watchers do not fight and say no more than a word or two.

If the confrontation happens at night, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The two figures in the alley mouth turn away as soon as you start across the street, without hurry and without alarm. One of them whispers to the other in a bright, young voice.
>
> > "Stay behind me, and slowly, please. I really don't want anybody getting hurt."
>
> They back into the dark between two houses, and by the time you reach the corner they are gone. A black leather eye patch lies on the cobbles where the nearer one stood.

If the confrontation happens by day, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The hooded figure in the doorway turns from you as you cross the street and steps back into the dark of the shuttered shop without a word. When you reach the doorstep the back door of the shop is swinging on its hinges, and a black leather eye patch lies on the threshold where the figure stood.

The eye patch is plain, worn soft and unmarked, and examining it reveals nothing else. If the confrontation is by day and the party includes an elf or half-elf, the hood turns toward that character and stays a moment longer than it does for the others. This is Soluun's habit and has no effect here, but **The Dockside Killer** builds on it. J.B. Nevercott knocks at the front door of Trollskull Manor at 10 a.m. the next morning. Proceed to **The Haberdasher at the Door**.

#### The Party Reports to the Watch

Sergeant Marek Dunmere takes reports at the North Ward Watch station on the day shift. Whichever character visits the station, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The North Ward Watch station smells of lamp oil and wet wool, and a heavyset sergeant with grey in his moustache sits at the front desk with a ledger open and a pen in his hand. He listens to your report without interrupting, writes down every name you give him, and sets the pen down only when you have finished.
>
> > "Drow, you say, and watching a tavern that hasn't opened yet. I'll put it in the book and send a constable down the alley tonight. If they come back, you come straight to me, and not to anyone else."

> [!social]**The Desk Sergeant**
>
> Marek Dunmere (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: the North Ward's day-shift desk sergeant, who has heard every kind of complaint and writes down all of them.
>
> Dunmere is Indifferent at first and Friendly by the end of a clear report. He speaks in plain, unhurried sentences, asks for names and times, and never promises more than a constable and a line in the ledger. He takes a report about drow as seriously as any other report and is mildly curious why anyone would watch a building with no customers.
>
> Dunmere is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Watch can do about people who stand in doorways, which is send a constable to look at them.
> - The state of Trollskull Alley, which he remembers as a street with bad luck.
> - What the party saw, in as much detail as they care to give.
>
> He knows nothing about Bregan D'aerthe, and he will not ask.

> [!qna]**Will you arrest them?**
>
> > "For what? Standing in a doorway isn't a crime in this city, thank the gods, or I'd have half the Sea Ward in a cell. If a constable finds one of them with a blade out, I'll have something to work with. Until then it's a line in the ledger and a lantern in the alley."

> [!qna]**What if they come back?**
>
> > "Then you tell me, and I'll have a second constable posted there. I'd rather be told twice than not at all."

> [!gamemaster]**Marking the Report**
>
> Mark **BD Contact Severed** for each character who gives Dunmere their name. A character who stands beside the reporter and gives no name is not marked, and neither is any companion. The watchers are gone from Trollskull Alley by dusk, for the whole party. Jarlaxle has the report from his Watch contacts before the ink is dry.
>
> Jarlaxle closes the file on each reporting character. He treats them as a civilian variable in **Sea Maidens Faire**, and nobody from Bregan D'aerthe raises membership with them again. Ryvarra stays at the Portal and keeps filing her reports, because nothing she does is against the law.

The next morning, read or paraphrase the following to the reporting characters:

> [!exploration]**A Letter from the Watch**
>
> A young constable delivers a sealed letter to the front door of Trollskull Manor. The letter reads:
>
> > To the proprietors of Trollskull Manor. The North Ward Watch thanks you for your report of yesterday. A constable has inspected Trollskull Alley and found no criminal activity. Please bring any further concern to this station. M. Dunmere, Sergeant.

Nevercott still comes for the candidates who gave no name. He knocks at 10 a.m. on Ches 13, and he does not mention the Watch unless a candidate raises it. If a reporting character speaks to him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott tips his hat to you with perfect courtesy, and then his eyes move on to the others.
>
> > "The Watch is a fine institution, and I'm glad you found it helpful. I'm afraid my business today is with your friends, though if you ever need a hat I'll give you a fair price."

Proceed to **The Haberdasher at the Door** for the candidates who did not report.

### The Haberdasher at the Door

> [!gamemaster]**The Visit**
>
> Nevercott knocks at 10 a.m. on the day the branch above sets. He keeps his hat box closed, stays on the step until invited in, talks about hats with whoever is present, and takes candidates aside one at a time.
>
> The address on his card is in the Sea Ward. A character who checks it finds nobody there has heard of him.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The man on the doorstep wears a deep burgundy coat with brass buttons, a neatly tied cravat and a broad hat that sits a little too large on his head. A round hat box hangs from one hand by a ribbon. He has a pleasant, open face, and he holds out a small card.
>
> > "Good morning to you all. J.B. Nevercott, of the Sea Ward, hats and accessories of quality. I heard that the new proprietors of Trollskull Manor were people of taste, and I hoped to introduce myself before you went elsewhere."

> [!social]**The Haberdasher**
>
> J.B. Nevercott (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in a hat of disguise, he/him) :: a courteous and amused Sea Ward haberdasher who enjoys the visit more than he shows.
>
> Nevercott is Friendly and in no hurry. He speaks in long, well-turned sentences, compliments the party's taste and shares his opinions about hat brims for as long as anyone will listen. He asks one precise question for every three he answers. He never confirms anything about himself beyond what is printed on the card, and he never swears or raises his voice. If a character presses him about Bregan D'aerthe before a candidate has stepped aside, he answers with something about hats.
>
> Nevercott is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Hats, on which he has strong opinions and no samples to show.
> - Trollskull Manor, its neighbors and its odd history of owners.
> - The Sea Ward trade and the carnival season, which he says is good for brims.
> - What the party does for a living, on which he is better informed than he lets on.
>
> He will not discuss his employer, where his shop stands, or why a haberdasher carries no samples.

> [!qna]**Your hats?**
>
> Nevercott lifts his own hat by the crown and turns it in the light from the doorway.
>
> > "Straw for summer, felt for winter, and a very good beaver for the gentleman who has an opinion about beavers. A brim ought to balance the shoulders and flatter the jaw, and a wide one is no use at all on the harbor. The samples are at the shop, naturally, and today I came only to introduce myself."

> [!qna]**Who told you about us?**
>
> > "Nobody told me anything. A tavern that has stood shut for years is about to open its doors, and I make a point of knowing every new proprietor in the North Ward before my competitors do. It's the first rule of my trade."

> [!qna]**Where is your shop?**
>
> > "In the Sea Ward, as the card says, though I spend most of my days calling on customers. A hat sells best at the customer's own door, with a mirror and a little encouragement, so I seldom stay in one place long enough to be found."

> [!exploration]**The Haberdasher's Eyes**
>
> Any character who talks with Nevercott for a few minutes and makes a successful **DC 18 Wisdom (Insight)** check notices two things. He looks at the characters' hands and weapons more often than at their heads, and he has not once glanced at the hats he says he sells.
>
> Any character who casts *Detect Magic* on him sees a faint aura of illusion around the hat. He answers cheerfully that of course it is enchanted, since a man in his trade needs every advantage, and that no, it is not for sale.

> [!gamemaster]**If a Player Names Jarlaxle**
>
> Nevercott never admits to being anyone but J.B. Nevercott, even to a character who guesses correctly. The guess does not mark **Jarlaxle Unmasked**, which a later event sets. He enjoys the guess and answers:
>
> > "I'm afraid that name means nothing to me, though I'm always glad to hear of a gentleman who might be in need of a hat. Is he a friend of yours?"

> [!gamemaster]**Taking a Candidate Aside**
>
> Nevercott keeps up the hat patter until a candidate steps aside, and then he follows. If the party includes a drow candidate, he waits up to a quarter of an hour for that candidate to step aside. If none does, or no drow candidate is present, he asks the first candidate in the room for a favor:
>
> > "Would you be kind enough to show me your courtyard? A hat should be judged in good light."
>
> He sees every candidate separately, drow candidates first and the others in the order they volunteer. Companions and characters who belong to another faction wait inside, and he tells them politely that a fitting is a private matter.
>
> If a character who belongs to another faction asks to be included, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > "I'm afraid I know whose colors you wear, and I'd think less of you if you asked me to ignore them. My offer is for people with no one else's name on them, but you're welcome to a hat whenever you like."
>
> If a character attacks Nevercott, he neither fights nor runs. He raises one hand, says pleasantly that this is a good moment to end the fitting, and walks out the front door with the hat box. The crowd in the alley swallows him within three streets, and nobody can follow. Mark **BD Contact Severed** for the attacker.

### The Private Word

If a candidate was confronting the watchers, read or paraphrase the following in the courtyard:

> [!readaloud]
>
> In the courtyard Nevercott glances back at the kitchen door, lowers his voice and sets his hat on the rim of an old barrel. The brisk patter goes out of him.
>
> > "The eye patch is a calling card. We leave one for people we think are worth knowing, and you earned yours by turning around and walking straight toward the people who were following you. Most people never see us at all, and the ones who do usually run to someone in a uniform."

If the party did nothing, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> In the courtyard Nevercott glances back at the kitchen door, lowers his voice and sets his hat on the rim of an old barrel. The brisk patter goes out of him.
>
> > "You've had company at your door for a tenday, and I'm the one who sent it. I wanted to know what sort of people had bought Trollskull Manor, and a tenday of watching tells me more than any conversation across a counter."

If the party marked **Ryvarra Identified**, or a character recognized Ryvarra at the Portal, add the following before he goes on:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A small smile crosses his face as he looks you over.
>
> > "I gather you had already noticed the lady at the Portal. That saves us all a little time."

In every case, continue:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott meets your eyes directly for the first time and speaks more slowly than he did on the doorstep.
>
> > "My name isn't J.B. Nevercott, and I don't sell hats, though I do have strong opinions about them. I represent Bregan D'aerthe, a mercenary company with operations across Faerûn and, for some months now, here in Waterdeep. Most factions in this city would pay a great deal to borrow our resources, and we take a particular interest in capable people who are making themselves noticed."

If the candidate is a drow, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Most of my colleagues are drow, so you would be among people who understand where you come from. A good many of them left it behind on purpose and don't look back."

If the candidate is not a drow, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Most of my colleagues are drow. We aren't particular about the company we keep, provided it's capable, and I've found it matters far less to the people involved than to the people who hear about it afterward."

Then:

> [!readaloud]
>
> He reaches into his waistcoat and takes out a plain black card with a small ship embossed in silver on one face, and he holds it out between two fingers.
>
> > "I'd like to offer you a first assignment, a small one. Declining costs you nothing, and accepting costs very little. I won't brief it today, but I'll call at the manor again at dusk on the twentieth, the evening before the Twin Parades, and you can hear the details then if you decide to come. Keep the card whatever you decide. I'll be in touch shortly regardless."

> [!social]**The Man Without a Hat**
>
> J.B. Nevercott (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in a hat of disguise, he/him) :: the same courteous haberdasher with the patter put away, who is exactly as amused as before and a good deal more precise.
>
> Without the fiction, Nevercott is Friendly and unhurried. His sentences are longer and better balanced than on the doorstep, and he answers with a straight account of what he will say and a plain refusal for the rest. He never lies about the offer and never says who leads Bregan D'aerthe. If pressed, he says the captain will be glad to meet anyone who stays.
>
> Nevercott is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What Bregan D'aerthe is, in the terms of a mercenary company that sells information, hired hands and introductions.
> - Why he chose this candidate, which he describes as having been noticed by the right people.
> - What the first assignment involves, only that it is small and he will explain it when he calls again at dusk on Ches 20.
> - What refusal costs, which is nothing at all.
>
> He will not discuss the captain's name, the ships, or anything about the other members.

> [!qna]**What is Bregan D'aerthe?**
>
> > "We're a mercenary company, and a rather old one. We sell information, hired hands and introductions, and most of our members came from somewhere they were glad to leave. In Waterdeep we mostly watch and listen."

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> > "You noticed things, you didn't panic, and you've been seen doing both. We don't ask for more than that at the start, and most people can't manage even half of it."

> [!qna]**Who do you work for?**
>
> > "For the company, naturally, and a company has a captain. Mine prefers to remain a rumor for the time being. If you stay, you may meet him, and I expect he'll enjoy it."

> [!qna]**What's the assignment?**
>
> > "I'll tell you when I call again on the evening before the Twin Parades, once you've had time to think it over. It's small, it's legal enough to survive a bad day, and it asks for a steady hand and a little discretion. I wouldn't call it dangerous, but I would call it interesting."

> [!qna]**What does it pay?**
>
> > "A small assignment pays a small sum, and the gold isn't the point. What follows is better, and I'd rather you judged us by that. If you accept, you won't find us ungenerous."

> [!qna]**Do you serve Lolth?**
>
> Nevercott's smile thins very slightly, and he picks his hat up and turns it by the brim.
>
> > "Gods, no. My colleagues left the Spider Queen behind on purpose, and most of them left in a hurry and in a bad temper. You won't find anyone among us with a kind word for her, and I'd advise you not to ask them about her at dinner."

> [!qna]**Were you the ones watching us?**
>
> > "My people were, and I apologize for the intrusion. They're good at it, and I'd rather you'd found it flattering than rude. A tenday is a long time to stand in a doorway, and they've earned a drink."

> [!qna]**What if we say no?**
>
> > "Then you keep the card, you keep your evenings, and we part on friendly terms. I'll go on finding you interesting, and you needn't ever think about us again if you choose."

### Each Candidate's Answer

> [!gamemaster]**Recording the Answers**
>
> Ask each candidate for their answer and record each one separately. A candidate who accepts and belongs to no faction becomes a member of Bregan D'aerthe at **Renown 1** with the **Initiate** rank. Joining takes no check. A candidate who declines or defers keeps their current status, and a character who belongs to another faction has no offer to answer.
>
> Nevercott writes the name of each new member in a small ledger, and the lieutenants hear it within a day. A member proves their standing to other members with the black card, which is personal and cannot be passed on.

If a candidate accepts, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott puts his hat back on, takes a small ledger from inside his coat and writes your name in a tiny, neat hand before closing it with a snap.
>
> > "Splendid. You're one of us as of this moment, and I'll call on you again at dusk on the twentieth. A young man named Fel'rekt Lafeen will keep a stall on the pier once the carnival ships tie up on the morning of the twenty-first, and he'll see you properly equipped. Until then, leave any order at the box office of the Seven Masks Theater, in the Dock Ward. When the ships are in and you need a bed, show the card to the mate on the *Heartbreaker* or the *Hellraiser*."

If a candidate declines or asks for more time, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott inclines his head to you, lifts his hat from the barrel and settles it back on his head.
>
> > "Then you've lost nothing, as I told you, and I'm glad of the conversation. I'll call again on the evening before the parades all the same. If you change your mind afterward, take the card to the box office of the Seven Masks Theater, in the Dock Ward, and ask for the booking manager. Someone will see that I hear of it."

> [!gamemaster]**Deferring and Changing One's Mind**
>
> - A candidate who defers can accept when Nevercott calls at Trollskull Manor at dusk on Ches 20. He repeats the offer once and briefs **The Handkerchief and the Girl** in the same visit.
> - A candidate who declines at that visit is not asked again by him.
> - A candidate who changes their mind afterward can take the black card to the Seven Masks Theater box office. Nevercott repeats the offer at Trollskull Manor within three days, and the candidate is recorded under the same outcome.

> [!gamemaster]**Initiate Benefits**
>

## Overview

Drow figures follow the party around Trollskull Manor, and a courteous haberdasher in a burgundy coat knocks at the door with an offer.

## Summary

### After the Meeting

We noticed figures watching Trollskull Manor from doorways and rooftops, and a haberdasher called J.B. Nevercott arrived with a hat box and a great many opinions about brims. When he got us alone he told us he represented a mercenary company called Bregan D'aerthe and offered us a first assignment, which he said would cost us nothing to refuse. Each of us gave our own answer, and we were told to expect him again at dusk on the eve of the Twin Parades.

### After a Report to the Watch

We told Sergeant Dunmere about the watchers, and by dusk they were gone from Trollskull Alley. A letter came the next morning saying the Watch had found nothing, and we did not hear from Bregan D'aerthe again.

### Without a Private Meeting

We carried on with the renovation of Trollskull Manor and noticed nothing worth reporting.
