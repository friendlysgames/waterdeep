# Bregan D'aerthe — Houseless Noble

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Ceremony Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Bregan D'aerthe renown reaches 50. Jarlaxle Baenre, operating openly, summons them to the Scarlet Marpenoth for a ceremony conducted in D'aerthe tradition. In this Event:
>
> - Jarlaxle names the character a Houseless Noble of Bregan D'aerthe's inner circle, the first surface operative to hold the rank in the company's history
> - The ceremony takes place on the Scarlet Marpenoth, attended by Krebbyg, Fel'rekt, and whoever among the lieutenants is still alive and free
> - Jarlaxle explains the Underdark network, the standing contacts in Menzoberranzan and beyond, and what the Scarlet Marpenoth's crew will do at the character's request
> - The character faces one genuine choice: whether to accept a blank favor (a future obligation Jarlaxle will name at his choosing) in exchange for full inner-circle access, or to hold the rank without the obligation at partial access
> - **Vault of Dragons** context: if this event fires after **Vault of Dragons** resolves, the Underdark network references shift: the Luskan Lords' Alliance bid becomes a known outcome, and Jarlaxle's forward-looking remarks are about Undermountain rather than Waterdeep
>
> > > [!design]**Expected in Dungeon of the Mad Mage**
> >
> > Reaching Renown 50 in Bregan D'aerthe requires completing all six missions at full bonus renown plus significant campaign contribution. Most parties will not reach this threshold during **Dragon Heist**. This event is written to work whether it fires before or after **Vault of Dragons**; see the Vault Context note in Scene 3. If it fires during Undermountain, the Underdark network is immediately relevant: the contacts in Menzoberranzan are operational assets below the surface. Run this event whenever it fires; it does not expire.

### Scene 1: Below the Waterline

The Scarlet Marpenoth at anchor. The gnome engineers have been sent to the upper deck. The forward lounge has been rearranged: the chart table pushed back, three lanterns added, a low bench set against the curved hull. It is deliberate and spare.

Krebbyg and Fel'rekt are present. If any other lieutenants survive and are free: they are here too, standing without crowding the space. No one speaks when the character descends the ladder.

Jarlaxle stands at the center of the lounge in his full coat, hands still, watching the door. The hat is on, the coat is on, and nothing about him is angled toward an exit. He is here.

> "You are here because you have earned a rank that D'aerthe has never given to a surface operative. I want to be precise about what that means, because I am always precise when the facts are significant."

He gestures toward the bench. He does not sit.

> "Houseless Noble is the rank of the inner circle. It means D'aerthe's full weight behind your work — everywhere, not just in Waterdeep. It means names I have not shared with anyone outside this hull. It means the Scarlet Marpenoth and its crew at your call. And it comes with one condition."

He pauses. He lets the submarine's quiet fill the pause.

> "I will name it. Then you decide."

### Scene 2: The Ceremony

Jarlaxle reaches into his coat and produces a small black coin, older than currency. The surface is blank on one side, engraved on the other with a symbol the character may recognize from D'aerthe materials: a stylized spider caught in its own web, crossed by a blade.

> "D'aerthe began as a house with no house. Everyone who joins the inner circle is Houseless by definition — it is not an insult. It is the origin. We chose it."

He sets the coin on the character's palm.

> "In the Underdark, this marks you as belonging to no house and to one company. On the surface, it is a coin. I recommend treating it as the former. The people who recognize it will not be fooled by the latter."

Krebbyg grins. Fel'rekt nods. If anyone else is present, they acknowledge the moment in their own way: Soluun (if present and not yet gone by his own choices) looks away; the engineers' footsteps overhead stop, as if they can hear through the hull.

> "The Underdark network: contacts in Menzoberranzan, surface brokers from Luskan to Calimshan, and a standing invitation to operate under D'aerthe's protection anywhere in Faerûn. The names come in writing, tonight, before you leave the ship."

He produces a sealed leather packet and sets it on the bench beside the character. The seal is plain black wax.

> "The Scarlet Marpenoth and its crew are yours for any mission you undertake. The engineers will insist on being told the destination first. That is non-negotiable; they feel strongly about it."

From overhead: a muffled acknowledgment, possibly defensive.

> [!profile]**The Lieutenants at the Ceremony**
>
> ***Krebbyg.*** Louder than the occasion warrants, treating ceremony the way he treats everything else: as something to be met at full volume. He will say something like: "Surface Noble. I told him you'd make it." He told no one this.
>
> ***Fel'rekt.*** Quiet but present. He shakes the character's hand with both of his, which is the D'aerthe equivalent of a speech.
>
> ***Others.*** Adjust attendance based on who is alive and free. Empty seats are not discussed.

### Scene 3: The Condition

> "The blank favor. This is what I ask."

He does not sit down for this either.

> "At some point — I cannot tell you when, because I genuinely do not know yet — I will ask you to do one thing. Not a mission. Not a formal assignment. A thing I need done that I cannot assign through the regular channel, because the regular channel is you. You will have context when I ask. You will have the right to refuse. And if you refuse, you lose the inner-circle access — not the rank, not the coin, not the contacts. The access. The Scarlet Marpenoth returns to being a ship you can ask to borrow."

He folds his hands.

> "This is the trade. Full access now, for one obligation I hold. Partial access now, for no obligation. The rank is yours either way — I am not withdrawing the coin. I am asking whether the coin comes with a string attached."

**The character's options:**

**Accept the blank favor.** Full inner-circle access as listed in the Ranks & Benefits table. The blank favor is held by Jarlaxle, who will name it at a dramatically appropriate moment during Undermountain or afterward. The obligation is real — he will call it in.

**Decline the blank favor.** The rank is recognized. The coin is genuine. The network contacts are delivered tonight. The Scarlet Marpenoth's crew will accept a request from the character, but the response requires Jarlaxle's approval rather than being guaranteed. He does not resent the refusal. He respects it, fractionally.

**Ask what the favor might be.** Jarlaxle answers honestly.

> "I don't know. That is not evasion. If I knew what I would need from you, I would ask for it now and call it a mission. I am asking for the option, not the outcome. I have found that the people worth trusting are the people who can tolerate that."

**If the character asks whether he has ever called such a favor in before:**

> "Yes. Three times. Two of the people who accepted it are still working with D'aerthe. One is not, because she declined when I named it. She is fine. I did not hold it against her. I did take away the access."

> [!note]**The Blank Favor in Play**
>
> The blank favor should be called in during Dungeon of the Mad Mage at a moment that requires genuine sacrifice or difficult choice — not cruelty, but something the character would not have agreed to in advance if they had known. Suggested categories: protecting a D'aerthe asset at personal cost, keeping a D'aerthe secret that conflicts with a party ally's interests, or traveling to a location in the Underdark that the character has reason to avoid. When called, give the character time to respond before consequences arrive.

### Scene 4: After

Jarlaxle hands the sealed packet to Krebbyg, who delivers it to the character. He pours two glasses of something amber and sets one near the character without asking.

> "The Vault of Dragons resolved the way it resolved."

He marks the transition.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Vault Context
>
> If **Vault of Dragons** has already resolved: Jarlaxle's remarks shift. The Luskan bid is a known outcome. He speaks about Undermountain with the forward focus of someone whose Waterdeep project is complete. He says: "The harbor work is done. What's below is the next picture." He does not explain what he means. He doesn't need to.
>
> If **Vault of Dragons** has not yet resolved: He says nothing about the Luskan bid or the vault. The ceremony stands alone. The Underdark network is described as operational and current; its relevance to the vault is for the character to work out.

He drinks. He looks at the hull.

> "D'aerthe has always been a company of people who had nowhere else to go. You had somewhere else to go. You came anyway."

This is the closest thing to sentiment that Jarlaxle will offer in a professional context. It lasts about three seconds.

> "Finish the drink. The engineers need the ship back."

### Concluding the Event

#### BD Houseless Noble Reached: True / False

The character has been named a Houseless Noble of Bregan D'aerthe's inner circle. The coin, the network contacts (delivered in the sealed packet), and the standing invitation to operate under D'aerthe's protection are theirs. Read by any future campaign events involving D'aerthe in Undermountain, and by any GM event that checks for inner-circle access.

#### BD Blank Favor Accepted: True / False

The character accepted the blank favor condition. Jarlaxle holds an unspecified future obligation. Read when Jarlaxle names the favor — the GM should track this flag and prepare the moment. If False, the Scarlet Marpenoth's crew is available on request pending Jarlaxle's approval rather than guaranteed.

## Overview

Jarlaxle names the character a Houseless Noble of Bregan D'aerthe's inner circle, the first surface operative to hold the rank, in a ceremony aboard the Scarlet Marpenoth attended by the surviving lieutenants. The ceremony includes a coin marking inner-circle membership, the Underdark network contacts, and a genuine choice: accept a blank favor Jarlaxle will name in the future, or hold the rank without the obligation.

## Summary

The character was named a Houseless Noble of Bregan D'aerthe aboard the Scarlet Marpenoth, in a ceremony attended by the surviving lieutenants. Jarlaxle delivered the rank's benefits — the Underdark network contacts sealed in a leather packet, the Scarlet Marpenoth's availability, and the standing invitation to operate under D'aerthe's protection — and presented one condition: a blank favor, unspecified, to be named at his choosing. The character accepted or declined.
