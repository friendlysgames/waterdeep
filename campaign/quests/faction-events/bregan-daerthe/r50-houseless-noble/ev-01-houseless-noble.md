# Houseless Noble

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Bregan D'aerthe member reaches Renown 50, which happens during **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. The member is called to the *Scarlet Marpenoth* by a black card, and the captain of the company receives them there with Krebbyg and Fel'rekt. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Receive the black coin of the inner circle and hear what it stands for.
> - See who is missing from the table, which depends on what became of Soluun, Nar'l and the lieutenants.
> - Take the sealed packet of names and learn how to use the network it opens.
> - Learn how to call the inner circle's muster on one major operation and how to ask for a voyage aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*.
> - Accept or decline the blank favor, and live with what either answer costs.
>
> All of these benefits belong to the qualifying member. Companions gain none of them.

### Hook

The Event waits for the first evening on which the member is back in Waterdeep after reaching Renown 50. It does not interrupt a descent, and the card is found on the doormat of Trollskull Manor when the party next comes up from below. A member who has marked **Seven Masks Back Room** receives the card at the dressing room instead, as described in the next scene. If several members qualify together, they are called to the same evening, and each takes the coin and gives an answer alone.

The renown figure needs a plain statement. The six missions award 19 Renown at most (1 for joining and 18 in base awards, with no bonuses), so no member reaches 50 on the missions alone. The other 31 points come from the Earning Renown list in the Bregan D'aerthe page of the Factions Guide and from play in **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. This Event awards none of them, and it fires whenever the member crosses the line, whether that is before or after **Vault of Dragons** has resolved.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> The captain is Jarlaxle Baenre, who has been J.B. Nevercott and Zardoz Zord to the members until now. A member who has marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** sees him as himself and hears his own name from him. A member who has not marked it meets Zardoz Zord, who stayed behind in Waterdeep with a handful of the company when the Faire sailed on Tarsakh 20 and who has been Zardoz to that member ever since. If **Zardoz Introduced** is not marked for the member, Krebbyg introduces him at the foot of the ladder, he says it is a pleasure, and **Zardoz Introduced** is marked now. Zardoz never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan, and every line below that needs them is spoken by Jarlaxle or written in the packet.
>
> The rank is real. The coin, the packet, the muster and the ship are all the company's own to give, and none of it is a trick. The blank favor is a real hold on the member, and Jarlaxle knows exactly what he will use it for. He does not say so because the member would carry it differently if they knew, and the event states it in full in **The Favor** so that you do.
>
> Bregan D'aerthe rejects Lolth. The spider on the coin is the goddess the company walked away from, caught in her own web with a blade through her, and every speaker mentions her with contempt. Nobody in this Event knows anything about the Cassalanters except suspicion, and Manshoon is not named. If the Splinter comes up, the speakers say "the other cell."
>
> Pelsha and Vorn, the two Drow Gunslingers assigned at **Commander**, stand at the door and do not speak. Breena Bafflestone, the gnome engineer, is at her post in the engine room and is not at the table.

### The Coin

The host takes a small black coin from a waistcoat pocket. Read the matching block.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> > "Now here's the grand thing, my darling. That's the old eight-legged lady with a knife in her, and the company keeps her on the coin so we all remember who we told to go to hell. You'll be a Houseless Noble from tonight, and that means you belong to no house at all, only to us, and it's the best address on the coast!"

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle turns a small black coin over between two fingers, and in the lantern light you can see that one face is smooth and blank and the other carries a stamped design of a spider caught in its own web with a thin blade slanting through its middle. He sets it on your palm without ceremony and keeps his hand beneath yours for a moment.
>
> > "The spider is the goddess my people were taught to kneel to, with a knife through her, and it's the nearest thing the company has to a prayer. I'm told the humans find it ominous. We find it funny. You're a Houseless Noble from tonight, which means you belong to no house and to one company, and I'd like you to understand that it's the only kind of nobility I've ever respected."

> [!exploration]**The Coin**
>
> Any character who examines the coin and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or History) check recognizes the design as an old drow house sigil with the spider canceled by the blade, and understands it to be a mark of defiance and not of worship. Any character who makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check while the host speaks notices that his amusement is real and that the contempt for the spider is the only part of the speech he means entirely.
>
> - **Auto:** A member who has marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** succeeds on the Arcana or History check without rolling.
> - **Failure:** The character takes the coin for a company token and learns nothing about what the spider means.
>
> The coin is a token of the rank and nothing more. It has no magic. A member who shows it at a drop or a ship's ladder is recognized as inner circle, and a member who loses it can have a replacement struck within a tenday by Krebbyg or Fel'rekt, once.

### The Packet

The host hands over a sealed packet of black wax and flat leather. A lieutenant on the bench says the next line, because the host does not discuss the packet's contents.

If **Vault of Dragons** has resolved, which is usual at this rank, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fel'rekt slides the packet along the bench until it touches your hand, and he waits for you to take it before he lets go. He looks pleased with himself and a little embarrassed about it.
>
> > "That's the names. The captain says to tell you the harbor work is finished and the packet's for what comes next, and he'd like you to open it somewhere with a door that locks. I'd read the first page twice."

If **Vault of Dragons** has not yet resolved, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fel'rekt slides the packet along the bench until it touches your hand, and he waits for you to take it before he lets go. He looks a little troubled, as though he has been told to say something that he does not like.
>
> > "That's the names. The captain says it's yours whether the vault opens or not, and he'd like to hear the day it does, so he's not the last to know. He made me say it exactly like that. I'd read the first page twice."

If Fel'rekt is gone, Krebbyg says the same lines, with a "darling" in the second sentence. If both are gone, Pelsha puts the packet in the member's hand and says only: "The captain says it's yours."

The packet holds four leaves, and each leaf is one drop. A member who opens it finds the cover business of each, the phrase to say, and what the drop can do.

> [!exploration]**The Four Leaves**
>
> The leaves are written in a small neat hand that every member recognizes as Krebbyg's. The first page of each reads aloud as follows, and the second page of the Menzoberranzan leaf is the one Fel'rekt means.
>
> > **Skullport.** "A ropewalk on the lower quay. Ask for a hank of black cord and say you're buying for the company. Xanathar's Guild holds the whole place, so you are to say nothing of us in the street and use the drop only if no Guild agent has followed you here."
>
> > **Menzoberranzan.** "A shop for lamps in the lower ward, reached by a messenger who will find you. You are to say the coin's phrase twice. The house we know in Undermountain is House Auvryndar, which is low in rank, patient, and allied with the other cell. Its wizard Rizzeryl keeps rooms on the second level, among the arcane chambers, and has no quarrel with anyone who leaves him alone."
>
> > **Luskan.** "A cooper's yard on the harbor road. Say you need a barrel that will not leak and show the coin. The man you speak to has never heard of us and will help you anyway."
>
> > **Calimport.** "A spice stall in the market of coins. Ask the keeper whether the saffron is true. She will say it is never true, and then she will take you in."

A drop does four things for a member who shows the coin and says the phrase. These are the same in every city.

- **Shelter:** the drop's keeper takes the member and up to four guests vouched for at the door into a back room for up to three nights. Nobody asks a question and the room is not searched.
- **A written page:** once per tenday at each drop, the member asks for what the company knows about that city, its factions and one named person or place in it, and receives a written page the next dusk. It never names an infernal pact as fact, because the company knows only suspicion. It never names Manshoon and calls him "the other cell."
- **A surety:** once per tenday in each city, if the member is held by a watch or a guard, the drop pays up to 500 gp to have the member released the same day. It will not pay if the member was seized for harming the company.
- **A guide:** the drop lends a local guide for one day. The guide uses the **Scout** stat block from the 2024 *Monster Manual* and will leave the member if the day's work turns against the company.

> [!exploration]**Limits and Losses**
>
> The packet belongs to the member who received it and has no effect for anyone else. A companion who walks into a drop with the member's phrase and no coin is told the shop is closed.
>
> - **Renown below 50:** if the member's Renown falls below 50, the drops go dark, the coin stays with the member, and the benefits return when Renown reaches 50 again.
> - **Leaving the company:** a member who leaves Bregan D'aerthe or marks **BD Contact Severed** has the coin recalled by Fel'rekt or Krebbyg within the day, and the packet burns in the member's own hands as they say the phrase.
> - **Acting against the company:** a member who is ordered by a drop's keeper to stand down because they are working against the company and refuses loses all four drops and the coin. This is a loss of the rank, and Renown is set to 49.
> - **A lost packet:** a stolen packet is reported to the company by the keeper at the next drop the thief tries, and the thief is not helped. The member is not given another.

> [!gamemaster]**The Undermountain Seeds**
>
> The Menzoberranzan leaf and the Skullport leaf are the packet's two lines into **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. They carry facts from the adventure and nothing more. House Auvryndar is a low-ranking house of Menzoberranzan that is consolidating its power in Undermountain and is allied with the Zhentarim. Its drow mage Rizzeryl holds rooms called the Base de Résistance on the second level of Undermountain with a quasit and eight wererats, and he works for Auvryndar and for the Zhentarim against the Xanathar Guild. Xanathar's Guild controls Skullport and is trying to secure the uppermost levels of Undermountain.
>
> Bregan D'aerthe does not appear in the adventure, and the company has no scripted ties to any of these people. The leaves state what the company has heard, and the Menzoberranzan drop will not say how it hears it.
>
> Skull Island in Skullport has two harbors, each 60 feet deep, with steel augers at their mouths that rise on chains and pierce the hulls of passing ships. The *Scarlet Marpenoth* never enters either harbor, and her engineers refuse to try. They put passengers ashore at a landing outside the mouths.

### The Muster

Pelsha walks to the table and sets a folded sheet on it without sitting down, and the host nods once. The sheet lists the inner circle's muster, which is a pool of ten people on call for one major operation. Soluun is not on it, because he has been expelled or killed.

> [!qna]**Who is in the muster?**
>
> Pelsha nods toward the door, where Vorn stands.
>
> > "Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, and Vorn and I, and four of our people who've run work for the captain for years. Ilphrin Quiss is in it too, since she answers to the company before she answers to anyone else. And the captain himself, if the operation's big enough to need him."

> [!qna]**What can we ask them to do?**
>
> > "Anything the company can do in a day or two of work. They follow your plan within the limits the captain sets, and they come out at the first safe exit when the job's done. They won't turn on the company, and they won't kill a child or a bystander, and Fel'rekt won't kill anyone he can avoid killing. Ask him first."

> [!exploration]**One Major Operation**
>
> Each Houseless Noble can call the muster on one major operation at a time. The member gives the host or a lieutenant five days' notice, names the objective, the place and the hour, and picks one package:
>
> - **Lieutenants:** Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, for a quiet job that needs two people who know the city.
> - **Roster:** Pelsha, Vorn and four drow, for an assault or a guard.
> - **Strike:** the Lieutenants and the Roster together, for a major assault.
> - **Full muster:** the Strike package plus Ilphrin Quiss and Jarlaxle himself, approved only if the member accepted the blank favor and the target is a villain faction's holding, force or leader.
>
> The package meets the member where the member names, and the lead operative takes the member's plan. The operation runs until the objective is finished, abandoned or made impossible. A package that is out cannot go out again until it is back, and fallen operatives are replaced a tenday later. Only one muster exists, so two Houseless Nobles who ask for overlapping dates share it in the order they asked. The Roster of **Commander** is the same pool as the Roster here, so a member cannot call it once under each rank.
>
> A member who declined the blank favor may call only the Lieutenants package, once per tenday.

> [!gamemaster]**The Muster as Allies**
>
> Krebbyg, Fel'rekt, Pelsha and Vorn use the **Drow Gunslinger** stat block from *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, because the 2024 *Monster Manual* has no drow gunslinger. The four drow use the **Scout** stat block with Darkvision 120 feet and Sunlight Sensitivity added and a hand crossbow in place of the longbow. Ilphrin Quiss uses the **Spy**, and Jarlaxle uses the **Pirate Captain**. Soluun is not in the pool, so he adds nothing. If you ever bring him back, he adds 38 for a party of levels 8 to 10.
>
> Ally Power counts in full for operatives who take part in a combat. For a party of levels 8 to 10, the Lieutenants add 76, the Roster 124, the Strike package 200, and the full muster 282. From level 11, they add 64, 92, 156 and 221. Add the total to the participating characters' Party Power before recalculating the difficulty and the day cost. The full muster roughly triples a party of three and more than doubles a party of five, so build the fight around the larger total or send the Lieutenants or the Roster unless the operation is a set piece.
>
> If Krebbyg or Fel'rekt is gone, the Lieutenants package is the survivor alone and adds 38 (levels 8 to 10) or 32 (from level 11). The Strike package then adds 162 or 124, and the full muster 244 or 189. If both are gone, the Lieutenants package does not exist, the Strike package equals the Roster, and the full muster adds 206 or 157.
>
> #### The Operatives' Tactics
> The lead operative stays close to the member and directs the others to the member's objective. During combat, the muster:
>
> - Has Fel'rekt call for surrender twice, in plain words, before he shoots, and he does not kill anyone who can still be taken alive.
> - Has Krebbyg follow Fel'rekt's lead and fire at whatever target Fel'rekt names.
> - Has Pelsha and Vorn hold the flanks and use cover, with the four drow skirmishing from range.
> - Has Jarlaxle, if present, fight at full capacity and look for an exit once he is reduced to 30 Hit Points or fewer.
>
> The muster withdraws to the nearest safe exit if its lead operative falls or if half of it is down, and it does not leave the member behind.

### The Marpenoth

Breena Bafflestone's crew takes the member where the member asks to go, and the access is full for a member who accepts the favor. What it is worth depends on the state of the ship, as the entries below set out for **Marpenoth Saved**, **Marpenoth Crippled** and **Marpenoth Lost**.

> [!exploration]**A Voyage Aboard the Marpenoth**
>
> The member asks Fel'rekt, or Krebbyg, or the dressing room at the Seven Masks if **Seven Masks Back Room** is marked, for a voyage, and names the destination in the same breath. The engineers insist on knowing it first, and they do not change it halfway.
>
> - **Notice:** three days.
> - **Passengers:** the member and up to five others, for a total of six passengers, and up to 2 tons of cargo. The vessel is 80 feet long, 20 feet in the beam, with AC 20, 300 Hit Points and a damage threshold of 15. It travels at 2 miles per hour underwater, which is 80 feet per round.
> - **Where she goes:** any harbor or deep water the pilots have charted. The packet's chart covers the waters of Waterdeep and the water the company reaches in Skullport. The pilots will not enter Skull Island's harbors.
> - **What she is for:** carrying and hiding. She has no weapons. If the hull is attacked, the pilots surface or dive to break contact and put the passengers ashore at the next safe landing.
> - **One at a time:** only one voyage runs at a time, and two Houseless Nobles who ask for overlapping dates share her in the order they asked.
>
> **If Marpenoth Saved is marked:** she is available as described.
>
> **If Marpenoth Crippled is marked:** she cannot dive for a tenday after the ceremony, and the first voyage is available from the eleventh day.
>
> **If Marpenoth Lost is marked:** until she is raised, thirty days after the ceremony, the crew offers the salvage lighter instead, for harbor and coastal waters only, with the same notice and the same passengers. From the thirty-first day the full vessel is available.
>
> **If the member declined the favor:** the host must approve each voyage in person, which adds three days to the notice. He approves it only if the destination is Waterdeep's harbor or a drop on the packet's surface leaves. The engineers will not take a Houseless Noble who declined into the waters of Undermountain.
>
> **Loss rule:** a member who orders the vessel into harm, such as through the mouth of a Skull Island harbor, has the passengers put ashore at the next safe landing and cannot call a voyage for a tenday.

### The Favor

The host sets down the glass and turns to face the member. If the member has not yet been told that this is the condition, the host says so now. Read the matching block.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz hangs the plumed hat on the back of a chair, and the bluster goes out of his shoulders without going out of his voice. He leans his hands on the chart table and speaks more slowly than you have ever heard him.
>
> > "Now, my darling, here's where I stop being funny. The company would like to ask you for one favor someday, and I won't say what it is, because if I did you'd carry it differently, and I'd rather you carried it honest. You can say yes and have everything on this table. You can say no and keep the rank, and the coin, and the half of the packet that isn't below the street, and not one soul will think the worse of you for it."

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle takes off the hat, which he has not done in front of you before, and sets it on the chart table with the feather lying across the chart. He stands with his hands open at his sides and speaks in the same light voice, a little slower than before.
>
> > "I should like to ask you for one favor, at a time of my choosing, and I'd rather not tell you what it is. If I told you, you'd carry it differently, and I'd rather you carried it honestly. You may say yes and have everything on this table. You may say no, keep the rank, the coin and the half of the packet that lies above the street, and nobody here will think the worse of you."

> [!qna]**What is the favor?**
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked, Zardoz answers in his own voice.
>
> > "Ha! A fine question, and a fair one, and a better man than me would answer it. I'll tell you three things instead, friend. It will cost you something, it will never ask you to hurt a friend of yours, and I'll only ask it once."
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked, Jarlaxle answers instead.
>
> > "I'd rather tell you what it won't be. It will not ask you to harm anyone who travels with you, it will cost you something you would have kept, and I will ask it once. Beyond that, I would be spoiling it."

> [!qna]**Has anyone refused?**
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked, Zardoz answers.
>
> > "Once, my darling, and she's fine, and we still drink together! She said no when I named it, and the company took her access away, and not one word of blame. That's the shape of it. Three people had the offer before you, and two said yes."
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked, Jarlaxle answers.
>
> > "Once. She declined when I named it, and she is quite well, and I did not hold it against her. I did take away the access, because that was the bargain. Three people have been offered it before you, and two accepted."

> [!qna]**What do I lose if I say no?**
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked, Zardoz answers.
>
> > "Less than you'd think and more than you'd like! The half of the packet that goes below the street gets burned in front of you, which is a shame, because the writing's lovely. The ship takes you only if I say so, and I'll say so when the road's on the right side of the harbor. And the full muster stays shut, though the lads will still come when you call."
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked, Jarlaxle answers.
>
> > "The leaves that go below the street are burned in your presence, which is a pity, since the handwriting is good. The ship takes you only on my say, and I'll give it for the harbor and for the places where we keep drops. The full muster closes, though the lieutenants will come when you call them."

> [!gamemaster]**The Favor and Its Price**
>
> The member who accepts is bound to a single favor, which Jarlaxle calls once during **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. The favor is a sealed letter. On the first day the member's party is in the same area as Rizzeryl on the second level, or on the thirtieth day after the ceremony if that comes first, Ilphrin Quiss sends the member word through the company's own Sending, naming the day and the place, and a flying snake delivers the letter the same dusk. The letter is for Rizzeryl. It is a probe: the company offers House Auvryndar a safe road for one of its couriers out of Skullport in return for the Xanathar Guild's mooring list, and nothing else.
>
> The member must carry the letter to Rizzeryl unopened, hand it over in person, and say nothing of it to any Zhentarim, Doom Raider or other ally in the party who is not a Bregan D'aerthe member, until Rizzeryl has read it. Rizzeryl will talk to a party that leaves him alone, as the adventure says, and he reads the letter without a fight. The cost is the secret, which sits badly with any ally who works for the Zhentarim and may come to resent it. It is not a killing and it does not ask the member to harm a companion.
>
> The member may refuse when the favor is named. A member who refuses loses the full muster, the guaranteed ship and the Menzoberranzan and Skullport leaves, exactly as a member who declined at the ceremony, and the Renown stays at 50. A member who carries the letter keeps everything, and the favor is spent.

### The Answer

> [!exploration]**The Answer**
>
> The host holds the offer open for a tenday. The member may answer at once, may ask for the tenday and answer before it ends, or may say nothing, which the host treats as a refusal on the tenth day. During the tenday the packet's surface leaves, the coin, the Lieutenants package and a harbor voyage all work, and the two Underdark leaves stay sealed until an answer is given. A member who answers takes one of the two paths below, and the answer is recorded by name.

If the member accepts, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The host picks up the glass again and holds it out to you, and when you take it, the host touches the rim of the other glass to yours. Fel'rekt lets out a breath he has been holding, and Krebbyg, if he is aboard, says something under his breath about it being about time.
>
> > "Then drink with us, and finish the glass, because the engineers want the ship back. You'll hear from us when it's time, and you'll know the day, and I'd ask you to keep the coin where nobody else can see it."

> [!readaloud]
>
> The host nods, and Fel'rekt takes the packet gently out of your hands. He tears the lower leaves out of it one by one, and lays them on the chart table, and puts a match to them in a brass dish while you watch. The smoke goes up past the lantern, and the host looks at it with every sign of mild regret.
>
> > "That's what we agreed, and I'm not angry. The coin's yours, and so is the rank, and the lieutenants are yours to call. Come and see us when you change your mind. Finish the drink. The engineers need the ship."

### Renown Opportunities

This Event awards no Renown, gold or Milestone Points. The six missions award at most 19 Renown, counting the point for joining, so the 31 points that bring a member to 50 come from Earning Renown and from play in **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. The coin and the packet do not earn Renown of their own, and the company has no higher rank to reach.

If a member's Renown falls below 50, the packet, the muster and the ship are suspended and the coin stays with them. They return when Renown reaches 50 again.

### Aftermath

The member can tell their companions what they were offered, but the packet, the muster and the ship stay with the member, and a companion who asks in the member's name is turned away. Nobody aboard will say how the member's answer was received, and the host does not discuss it. A member who accepted is told the day the favor is called by Ilphrin's Sending. A member who declined finds the Underdark leaves gone and the ship's crew more careful about its destinations.

## Overview

The leader of Bregan D'aerthe calls a member aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth* to name them a Houseless Noble of the company's inner circle and to offer a favor in exchange for the full run of its ship and its network.

## Summary

We were named Houseless Nobles aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, and each of us was handed a black coin with a spider and a knife on it and a sealed packet of names. The captain showed us how to call the muster and how to ask for a voyage aboard the ship. He asked each of us for a favor he wouldn't describe, and we each said yes or no. What did he mean to ask?
