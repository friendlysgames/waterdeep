# Design Notes: Houseless Noble

## Placement and the Renown Arithmetic

The restored draft said a member reaches Renown 50 by completing "all six missions at full bonus renown" and that most parties would not get there during **Dragon Heist**. The arithmetic supports neither claim. The base awards total 19 with the point for joining, so Renown 50 depends on the bonus lines, the Earning Renown list in the Factions Guide and play in **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. The Hook now says so and places the Event as the Doom Raiders' Dread Lord event does. It holds the Event until **BD Commander** is marked and fires it on the first evening the member is back in Waterdeep after crossing the line, so a descent is never interrupted. The Event awards no Renown, as with every rank event.

## Who Speaks and Why Zardoz Cannot Explain the Packet

A member who has marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** gets Jarlaxle himself, and every other member gets Zardoz Zord, so each key beat has a paired readaloud. Zardoz never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan, and the network is mostly the Underdark and Luskan. The packet therefore carries its own explanation on written leaves, and the lieutenants hand it over, so Zardoz stays in voice without the scene losing content. The coin's spider is the rejected goddess in both versions. Zardoz mocks "the old eight-legged lady with a knife in her", and Jarlaxle calls the coin a joke at her expense.

## The Empty Seats

The draft had one line, "Empty seats are not discussed." The rewrite makes them the emotional centre of the Event and delivers them through Fel'rekt, who can say what the host will not.

- Soluun's chair branches on **Soluun Expelled** (with or without **Soluun Sold the Mooring**, which **The Dive** sets), **Soluun Killed** and **Soluun Killers Named**.
- Nar'l's place branches on the four outcomes of **The Compromised Eye**. The Eliminated line does not claim Soluun is dead, because he may have been expelled alive.
- No outcome records the lieutenants' fate anywhere in the Bregan D'aerthe folder, so the Event branches on a plain condition (killed or taken in play). It names **Sea Maidens Faire** and **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** as the places that could cause it. This is logged below.

## The Berth and the State of the Ship

The draft put the ceremony on a ship "at anchor" and ignored the Faire's departure and the limpet-charge mission. The Event now branches on **Marpenoth Saved**, **Marpenoth Crippled** and **Marpenoth Lost**, the three states **The Dive** sets.

- The saved ship lies under the outer end of the old Faire pier at Smugglers' Dock, as in **The Dive**. The Faire sailed on Tarsakh 20, and Jarlaxle stayed behind with a team and the submersible.
- The crippled ship is on a slip in a rented boathouse with her lounge intact.
- The lost ship is on the harbor floor, and the lounge furnishings are on a salvage lighter above her.

A lost ship is raised thirty days after the ceremony and a crippled one dives again in a tenday, so the benefit that names her is always concrete.

## Benefits as Procedures

The Factions Guide gives the rank one line. Each part of that line is now a written procedure with a contact, limits and a loss rule.

- **The packet** has four leaves (Skullport, Menzoberranzan, Luskan and Calimport), each with a cover, a phrase and four services: shelter, a written page, a surety of up to 500 gp and a one-day guide. The Menzoberranzan and Skullport leaves close if the member declines the favor.
- **The muster** is the ally pool from the mechanics reference, in four packages: Lieutenants, Roster, Strike and full muster. It gives Power for levels 8 to 10 and from level 11, corrections when a lieutenant is missing, and Soluun counted as zero. The full muster is gated on accepting the favor and on a villain faction target, matching the Doom Raiders' rule for the full pool. The Roster draws on the same two crews as **Commander** (Crew One is Pelsha and Vorn, Crew Two is Ilmra Kelnozz and Brythe Mizzrym), so a member cannot call a crew once under each rank. The full muster adds the member's own Spy from **Officer** in place of a single named one.
- **The ship** uses the stats from the *Scarlet Marpenoth* page (AC 20, 300 Hit Points, damage threshold 15, 80 feet long, 6 passengers) and a fixed notice, passenger limit, destination rule and loss rule.

## The Blank Favor and What Declining Costs

The draft let a member decline for "partial access," which was soft, because the captain would approve the crew anyway. A declined favor now costs three things.

- The Underdark leaves of the packet are burned in front of the member.
- The ship takes the member only with the host's approval, and only to the harbor and the surface drops.
- The full muster closes, leaving only the Lieutenants package.

A member who accepts and then refuses when the favor is named loses the same things. This keeps the choice real in both directions, as the Doom Raiders' seat does.

The draft's favor was "I genuinely do not know yet," which left the GM to improvise. The Event now settles it. The favor is a sealed letter for Rizzeryl, the drow mage of House Auvryndar in the Base de Résistance on the second level. It offers a safe road for an Auvryndar courier in exchange for the Xanathar Guild's mooring list. The cost is the secret, which the member keeps from any Zhentarim or Doom Raider ally in the party until Rizzeryl has read it. This is the old draft's "keeping a secret that conflicts with a party ally's interests" category, and it asks nothing of a companion. The host does not describe the favor because telling would change how the member carries it.

## Mad Mage Hooks and What Was Dropped

The adventure supports three seeds.

- House Auvryndar is a low-ranking Menzoberranzan house consolidating in Undermountain and allied with the Zhentarim. Rizzeryl works for it from the Base de Résistance and has no quarrel with anyone who leaves him alone.
- The Xanathar Guild controls Skullport and is trying to secure the upper levels.
- Skull Island's two harbors are 60 feet deep, with steel augers that pierce the hulls of passing ships, so the *Scarlet Marpenoth* never enters either.

The adventure contains nothing of Bregan D'aerthe, so every tie between the company and these people is stated as what the company has heard.

Sarev Oust is dropped. He had no basis in the adventure, and the leaves now do the work he did.

## Invented and Open Items

Invented in this Event:

- the boathouse slip and the salvage lighter;
- the four drop covers (a ropewalk, a lamp shop, a cooper's yard, a spice stall) and their phrases;
- the 500 gp surety;
- the thirty-day and ten-day repair times;
- the sealed letter and the courier offer;
- the water route that lets the submersible reach Skullport, which the adventure does not give and the engineers keep to themselves.

The lounge comes from the restored draft, and the *Scarlet Marpenoth* page does not key it.

Logged for the out-of-scope notes:

- **The Dive** sets **Marpenoth Saved**, **Marpenoth Crippled**, **Marpenoth Lost** and **Soluun Sold the Mooring**, and this Event reads all four.
- No Bregan D'aerthe event or outcome records the fates of Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, so the Event branches on a plain condition. **Sea Maidens Faire** should set an outcome if either can fall.
- **Commander** is the source of Pelsha and Vorn, who stand at the door and speak only where this Event gives them lines. Krebbyg and Fel'rekt remain the *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* Drow Gunslinger here, as in **Commander**.
- The Factions Guide's Houseless Noble row gives the ship and the network unconditionally. The Event adds the favor and its price, and the guide row should say so.
- `SOURCE_GUIDE.md` claims Bregan D'aerthe connections at Sargauth. The adventure has none.
