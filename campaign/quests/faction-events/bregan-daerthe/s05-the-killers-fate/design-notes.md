# Design Notes: The Killer's Fate

## Why This Event Exists

**The Dockside Killer** leaves Soluun Xibrindas alive in two of its three outcomes, and Soluun's own page calls his disownment a cover story. Nothing in the Doom Raiders' mission or in the restored Bregan D'aerthe drafts settled what the company does about a drow agent who has been seen killing in Waterdeep, and **The Compromised Eye** called the disownment real while its own background called it a cover. The user ruled that Jarlaxle expels him for real, because an agent seen killing openly in the city is a liability. This Event is where Bregan D'aerthe's members watch that ruling, so that **The Compromised Eye**, **The Dive** and **Houseless Noble** can read an outcome and not an assumption.

The Event is a standalone because no mission carries it. Its trigger is the calendar of **The Dockside Killer**, which a Bregan D'aerthe member may not have played, and it awards no Renown because the ruling isn't a job. It is the one Bregan D'aerthe Event in which every possible effect on a member is a loss or a choice.

## The Ruling Was Always Coming

Soluun's disownment and his forged disc were built so that the company could deny him, and the ruling makes the denial true. The Event doesn't treat that as a betrayal of a loyal man, because Soluun believes he is being tested, and the fiction depends on Jarlaxle believing that a zealot will stay a zealot. Jarlaxle is wrong, and that is the engine of **The Dive**, where the expelled Soluun sells the *Scarlet Marpenoth*'s mooring. Planting it here lets that mission read as the consequence of a scene the members attended.

The Event also lets the member learn that the disownment was a cover story, which is a fact the Doom Raiders and the rest of the city can't learn from **The Dockside Killer**. It is delivered by the company itself, in the ruling, so that no member has to guess.

## Whose Voice Delivers It

Nevercott is Jarlaxle in a *hat of disguise*, and the rule that nobody says "Jarlaxle" until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for a member forces a choice. Two versions of the ruling exist, one in Nevercott's voice beside an empty chair and one in Jarlaxle's own voice from the chair. The empty chair with a hat hung on its arm lets Nevercott speak for "the captain" without anyone aboard having to explain why the captain isn't there, and it makes the ruling feel like a verdict from an absent authority.

The Jarlaxle version runs only when every member present has the outcome, because a single member who lacks it would see Nevercott vanish and Jarlaxle appear. At a mixed table the Nevercott version runs and the member with the outcome sees through the hat in silence, which is how the organization page says the company treats the two identities.

Soluun never addresses Nevercott as "captain" in the readaloud. He speaks of the captain in the third person and looks at the empty chair first, which keeps the identity intact for members who don't know it and lets those who do notice that he knows.

## Krebbyg Aboard

Krebbyg appears as himself only if **Kreb Unmasked** is marked for every member present, matching the rule that he drops the cover only in private. The *Scarlet Marpenoth* is a private place, but a member who has never seen him without the wig can't be shown a drow bookkeeper at midnight. Otherwise he is at the Seven Masks Theater running the evening house, and Fel'rekt does the work with two guards. The rule is stated as a table-wide gate for the same reason as the Jarlaxle gate: one scene can't show two faces to one audience.

## Why Blindfolds

The *Scarlet Marpenoth* sits beneath the *Eyecatcher*, and a member who has not met Zardoz Zord would learn the ship's secret the moment they stepped out of the cabin that holds the trapdoor. A blindfolded passage keeps that secret for members who don't have **Jarlaxle Unmasked**, and a DC 16 Perception check lets an attentive member infer the carnival without anyone confirming it. Members who have the outcome are rowed openly and go in through the captain's cabin, matching their earlier visit. Fel'rekt's reluctance with the blindfolds is meant to show how little the company enjoys secrecy among its own.

## Three Choices and What Each Costs

The brief asked for real choices, so each one has a price paid in the same place it is made.

- **Pleading** cannot reverse the expulsion, because the Event is where the ruling becomes true and a plea that undid it would unwind the whole premise. It changes how Soluun leaves, with his weapons and 50 gp, and the pleader answers for him in **The Dive**. A member who pleads for a killer who then sells the mooring loses Renown, and that makes the plea a decision with weight.
- **Pressing** gives two facts, each behind its own checks, and both are plants. The Brother result tells a member that the "weak one" has been burying Soluun's trail with the other side's paper, which feeds **The Compromised Eye**. The Mooring result confirms what the speaker has already said aloud. The speaker says it unprompted so that no member can fail every check and still enter **The Dive** unwarned, and pressing adds the detail that Soluun knows the hours the hatch stands open.
- **Marking** needs no check and costs Soluun's attention. Fel'rekt watches him for a tenday, and the member who named him becomes the person Soluun looks for. It is the most cautious choice and carries the longest shadow.

A member may take only one of the three, so a table of members has to divide the room, and different members can make different choices without cancelling each other.

## The Witness Beat

A Bregan D'aerthe member who stood in the alley of **The Dockside Killer** is the one person who watched the company's own agent be caught or killed by outsiders. Decision 6 says that the company knows within a tenday and Nevercott raises it once, coolly, and that the penalty is 1 Renown. The beat is written as a short exchange in the corridor with a stated test, because a penalty applied by a die roll alone would be a coin toss.

The explanation has to give two of three things, so that a member who plays honestly and thinks about what the company would want gets through, and a member who shrugs doesn't. The Persuasion or Deception check then decides whether the delivery lands. A fellow member can vouch for a failed explanation, which gives the table a way to help a friend without the loss being automatic. The Event deliberately has nothing worse than the Renown loss, in line with the decision, and a member who loses Renown can earn it back with ordinary mission awards.

A member who struck the blow that killed Soluun faces a higher DC. That is the one place the Event distinguishes between watching and acting, and it follows from the company's view of the Killed variant.

## The Killed Variant

If **Soluun Killed** was marked, the company has the Luskan sailors' description and Jarlaxle's interest turns personal. There is no expulsion, because there is nothing to expel, and the scene shrinks to a conversation in which the speaker asks nothing of the members that would force them to betray a friend. The point is to let Bregan D'aerthe members carry the knowledge that the company knows who the Doom Raiders' companions are, and to let **Sea Maidens Faire** pick that up when Jarlaxle meets the party. The witness beat runs in this branch too, with the higher DC for the killing blow.

## Timing

A captured Soluun is released a little over a tenday after the ambush, so the Event runs on the night of the release and treats the company's own surety as the hand that freed him. An escaped Soluun returns before Tarsakh 20, so the Event runs on the twenty-fourth night or on Tarsakh 18, whichever comes first, because the Faire sails on Tarsakh 20 and the ruling has to land before that. If no Doom Raider ran **The Dockside Killer**, the company learns of the killings from Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, and the Event runs ten nights after the member's debrief for **The Handkerchief and the Girl**, with Soluun treated as Escaped.

## Out-of-Scope Notes

- **The Dockside Killer** describes the silver disc as a forged token whose knotwork a Bregan D'aerthe member can spot. This Event treats the forgery as the company's own work, struck at Jarlaxle's order so that the company could deny Soluun, and doesn't name who made it.
- The Fireball! cabin scene, which is where **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is expected to be marked, and **Sea Maidens Faire** (unconverted) must read the outcomes listed in the Event. **Sea Maidens Faire** in particular should read **Soluun Expelled**, **Soluun Killers Named** and the three per-member outcomes.
- The **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** doesn't yet carry an entry for a fight aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, so the hazard block points to it and gives no numbers.
- **The Compromised Eye**, **The Dive** and **Houseless Noble** are the named readers of the outcomes and should be drafted against this page. **The Dive** must apply the Renown loss for a successful plea and read **Soluun Pressed** for the Advantage on the lookout check.
- Breena Bafflestone is named as the engineer on watch, taken from the *Scarlet Marpenoth* page, and no other new NPC is invented.
