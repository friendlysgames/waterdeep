# Design Notes: The Killer's Fate

## Why This Event Exists

**The Dockside Killer** leaves Soluun Xibrindas alive in two of its three outcomes, and Soluun's own page calls his disownment a cover story. Nothing settled what the company does about a drow agent seen killing in Waterdeep, and **The Compromised Eye** called the disownment real while its own background called it a cover. The user ruled that Jarlaxle expels him for real, because an agent seen killing openly in the city is a liability. This Event is where Bregan D'aerthe's members watch that ruling, so **The Compromised Eye**, **The Dive** and **Houseless Noble** can read an outcome and not an assumption.

The Event is a standalone because no mission carries it. Its trigger is the calendar of **The Dockside Killer**, which a Bregan D'aerthe member may not have played. It awards no Renown because the ruling isn't a job, so every possible effect on a member is a loss or a choice.

## The Ruling Was Always Coming

Soluun's disownment and his forged disc were built so the company could deny him, and the ruling makes the denial true. Soluun believes he is being tested, and Jarlaxle believes a zealot will stay a zealot. Jarlaxle is wrong, and that drives **The Dive**, where the expelled Soluun sells the *Scarlet Marpenoth*'s mooring. Planting the ruling here lets that mission read as the consequence of a scene the members attended.

The ruling also tells members the disownment was a cover story. The Doom Raiders and the rest of the city can't learn that from **The Dockside Killer**, so the company delivers it itself and no member has to guess.

## Whose Voice Delivers It

Nevercott is Jarlaxle in a *hat of disguise*, and nobody says "Jarlaxle" until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for a member. So the ruling has two versions: Nevercott's beside an empty chair, and Jarlaxle's own from the chair. The empty chair with a hat on its arm lets Nevercott speak for "the captain" without anyone explaining why the captain is absent.

The Jarlaxle version runs only when every member present has the outcome, because a member who lacks it would see Nevercott vanish and Jarlaxle appear. At a mixed table the Nevercott version runs, and the member with the outcome sees through the hat in silence, as the organization page says the company treats the two identities.

Soluun never calls Nevercott "captain" in the readaloud. He speaks of the captain in the third person and looks at the empty chair first. Members who don't know the identity learn nothing, and members who do notice that he knows.

## Krebbyg Aboard

Krebbyg appears as himself only if **Kreb Unmasked** is marked for every member present, matching the rule that he drops the cover only in private. A member who has never seen him without the wig can't be shown a drow bookkeeper at midnight. Otherwise he is at the Seven Masks Theater running the evening house, and Fel'rekt does the work with two guards. The gate is table-wide for the same reason as the Jarlaxle gate: one scene can't show two faces to one audience.

## Why Blindfolds

The *Scarlet Marpenoth* sits beneath the *Eyecatcher*, and a member who hasn't met Zardoz Zord would learn the ship's secret on stepping out of the cabin that holds the trapdoor. Blindfolds keep that secret from members who lack **Jarlaxle Unmasked**, and a DC 16 Perception check lets an attentive member infer the carnival without anyone confirming it. Members who have the outcome are rowed openly and enter through the captain's cabin, as on their earlier visit. Fel'rekt's reluctance with the blindfolds shows how little the company enjoys secrecy among its own.

## Three Choices and What Each Costs

The brief asked for real choices, so each one has a price paid where it is made.

- **Pleading** can't reverse the expulsion, because a plea that undid it would unwind the premise. It changes how Soluun leaves, with his weapons and 50 gp, and the pleader answers for him in **The Dive**. A member who pleads for a killer who then sells the mooring loses Renown, which gives the plea weight.
- **Pressing** gives two facts, each behind its own checks, and both are plants. The Brother result tells a member that the "weak one" has been burying Soluun's trail with the other side's paper, which feeds **The Compromised Eye**. The Mooring result adds that Soluun knows the hours the hatch stands open. The speaker states the basic fact unprompted, so no member can fail every check and still enter **The Dive** unwarned.
- **Marking** needs no check and costs Soluun's attention. Fel'rekt watches him for a tenday, and the member who named him becomes the person Soluun looks for. It is the most cautious choice and carries the longest shadow.

A member may take only one of the three, so a table has to divide the room, and different members can choose differently without cancelling each other.

## The Witness Beat

A Bregan D'aerthe member who stood in the alley of **The Dockside Killer** watched outsiders catch or kill the company's own agent. Decision 6 says the company knows within a tenday, Nevercott raises it once and coolly, and the penalty is 1 Renown. The beat is a short exchange in the corridor with a stated test, because a penalty set by a die roll alone would be a coin toss.

The explanation has to give two of three things, so a member who plays honestly and thinks about what the company wants gets through, and a member who shrugs doesn't. The Persuasion or Deception check then decides whether the delivery lands. A fellow member can vouch for a failed explanation, so the table can help a friend and the loss isn't automatic. Nothing worse than the Renown loss follows, in line with the decision, and the loss can be earned back with ordinary mission awards.

A member who struck the blow that killed Soluun faces a higher DC. That is the one place the Event distinguishes watching from acting.

## The Killed Variant

If **Soluun Killed** was marked, the company has the Luskan sailors' description and Jarlaxle's interest turns personal. There is nothing to expel, so the scene shrinks to a conversation in which the speaker asks nothing that would force a member to betray a friend. Members come away knowing the company knows who the Doom Raiders' companions are, and **Sea Maidens Faire** picks that up when Jarlaxle meets the party. The witness beat runs here too, with the higher DC for the killing blow.

## Timing

A captured Soluun is released a little over a tenday after the ambush, so the Event runs on the night of the release, and the company's own surety is the hand that freed him. An escaped Soluun returns before Tarsakh 20, so the Event runs on the twenty-fourth night or on Tarsakh 18, whichever comes first. The Faire sails on Tarsakh 20, and the ruling has to land before that. If no Doom Raider ran **The Dockside Killer**, the company learns of the killings from Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, and the Event runs ten nights after the member's debrief for **The Handkerchief and the Girl**, with Soluun treated as Escaped.

## Out-of-Scope Notes

- **The Dockside Killer** describes the silver disc as a forged token whose knotwork a Bregan D'aerthe member can spot. This Event treats the forgery as the company's own work, struck at Jarlaxle's order so the company could deny Soluun, and doesn't name who made it.
- The Fireball! cabin scene, where **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is expected to be marked, and **Sea Maidens Faire** (unconverted) must read the outcomes listed in the Event. **Sea Maidens Faire** should read **Soluun Expelled**, **Soluun Killers Named** and the three per-member outcomes.
- The **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** has no entry for a fight aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, so the hazard block names its stat blocks and gives no new numbers. Soluun uses the 2024 **Scout** as in **The Dockside Killer**, and Fel'rekt and Krebbyg use the **WDH Drow Gunslinger** from their Notable Figures pages.
- **The Compromised Eye**, **The Dive** and **Houseless Noble** are the named readers of the outcomes and should be drafted against this page. **The Dive** must apply the Renown loss for a successful plea and read **Soluun Pressed** for the Advantage on the lookout check.
- Breena Bafflestone is the engineer on watch, taken from the *Scarlet Marpenoth* page. No other new NPC is invented.
