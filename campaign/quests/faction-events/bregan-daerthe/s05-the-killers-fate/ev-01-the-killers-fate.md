# The Killer's Fate

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when Bregan D'aerthe settles what to do with Soluun Xibrindas after the Dock Ward killings, and it plays out only for Bregan D'aerthe members. Soluun is the drow gunslinger whom the Doom Raiders meet in **The Dockside Killer**, and until tonight the company's public disownment of him has been a cover story. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Answer Nevercott's summons and be taken to the *Scarlet Marpenoth* beneath the *Eyecatcher*, blindfolded unless they already know whose ship it is.
> - Be taken aside by Nevercott if they stood in the alley during **The Dockside Killer**, and explain themselves to him.
> - Watch the ruling delivered and Soluun stripped of the silver disc he wears at his neck.
> - Plead for Soluun, press him for what he knows while he packs, or name him to the company as a future threat.
> - Ride with Soluun to the south quay and watch him put ashore.
> - If Soluun is dead, hear what the company has learned about who killed him.
>
> Only Bregan D'aerthe members attend. Companions who aren't members are not invited and never learn that the summons arrived. Nobody in this Event says the name Jarlaxle in speech or readaloud unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for that member, and the speakers say "the captain" instead. This Event awards no base Renown.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Soluun Xibrindas is the elder brother of Nar'l Xibrindas and a drow gunslinger who has served Jarlaxle with a zealot's devotion for years. He kills surface elves and half-elves because he was raised to, and for some weeks he hunted them along the Dock Ward waterfront, choosing his victims from the audiences of the Seven Masks Theater. Jarlaxle knew and hadn't decided what to do. The company's public story, that Soluun was disowned for killing without orders, was something Jarlaxle put about so that nobody could trace the killings to him, and the silver disc at Soluun's neck is a forgery that the company had struck on purpose so that it could deny him.
>
> What changed is that Soluun was seen. The Doom Raiders stood in the alley, the Harbor Watch has his description, and the hired Luskan sailors who guarded the street end have been talking in the taverns of the quay. An agent who kills openly in Waterdeep is a liability to every other agent, and Jarlaxle has decided to make the cover story true. Soluun is expelled tonight and leaves with nothing but his clothes, unless a member pleads for more.
>
> Soluun doesn't think of it as an ending. He believes it is a test, or a mistake the captain will mend, and that belief is the only thing keeping him from spitting in Nevercott's face. It will not last. Jarlaxle believes that Soluun is too devoted to sell the company's secrets, and he is wrong. Soluun knows where the *Scarlet Marpenoth* lies and how it moves, and **The Dive** shows what a zealot does with that after the captain refuses him. His brother Nar'l has spent two months burying Soluun's trail inside Xanathar's Guild, and **The Compromised Eye** shows what that cost.
>
> Nevercott is Jarlaxle in a *hat of disguise*, and Fel'rekt and Krebbyg know it. They will not say so to a member who hasn't earned the name.

> [!gamemaster]**The Night of the Ruling**
>
> The Event runs on one night, an hour before midnight, and which night depends on what **The Dockside Killer** recorded. If several members are in the party, they all receive the same card and attend together.
>
> - **Soluun Captured:** The night he is released. A solicitor posts surety at the Ship Street watchhouse a little over a tenday after the ambush, on the twelfth day, and the company arranged it themselves because Jarlaxle wanted Soluun free and aboard to hear the ruling. Fel'rekt meets him at the watchhouse gate with his coat and his weapons, and Soluun comes aboard in the grey shirt the cells gave him, with bruised wrists and three days of grime on his coat.
> - **Soluun Escaped:** The twenty-fourth night after the ambush, or the night of Tarsakh 18 if that comes first. Soluun walks back aboard after weeks of hiding in the harbor, sunburnt across the cheekbones, though he would never admit it.
> - **No outcome marked, because no Doom Raider ran The Dockside Killer:** Treat Soluun as Escaped. The company learns of the killings from Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, who traced them to Soluun after the fifth body, and the Watch has doubled its patrols along Ship Street. The Event runs ten nights after the member's debrief for **The Handkerchief and the Girl**.
> - **Soluun Killed:** The tenth night after the ambush. Run **If Soluun Was Killed** in place of the ruling.
>
> A member who joins Bregan D'aerthe after the Event has run doesn't attend. Mark **Soluun Expelled** anyway, and Fel'rekt tells the new member what happened the first time he is asked.

> [!gamemaster]**Who Is Aboard**
>
> - **J.B. Nevercott** delivers the ruling and keeps order. If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for every member present, Jarlaxle delivers it himself from the captain's chair and Nevercott is not aboard. If any member present lacks the outcome, run Nevercott's version. A member who has the outcome sees through the hat, may say so at the table, and gets no answer from anyone.
> - **Fel'rekt Lafeen** is always aboard. He fetches the members, takes the disc and rows Soluun ashore.
> - **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** is aboard as himself only if **Kreb Unmasked** is marked for every member present. Otherwise he is at the Seven Masks Theater running the evening house in his Kreb Sorrush cover, and Fel'rekt does the work with the two guards. Krebbyg never appears as Kreb Sorrush aboard the boat.
> - **Two drow guards** from the *Marpenoth*'s complement stand at the ladder to the upper deck. They don't speak.
> - **Breena Bafflestone**, the gnome engineer, is on watch in the engine room as the members pass, and the gnome pilots on the lower deck keep their backs to the ladder.

### The Card

A black card with a silver ship reaches each member on the afternoon of the night in question, slid under the door of Trollskull Manor, and no one sees who brings it. Companions who aren't members see only that a card arrived.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A black card with a ship embossed in silver lies on the mat inside the door of Trollskull Manor, and a line has been written along its bottom edge in a small neat hand. It is the same kind of card that Nevercott left with you at your first meeting, and the ink is still wet at one corner.
>
> > "The foot of Ship Street, on the south quay, an hour before midnight. Wear something dark and bring nothing you can't leave on a boat, and please don't mention it to your friends. J.B. N."

### The Passage

The south quay is where the alley from **The Dockside Killer** ends, and a member who stood in that alley knows the steps below the chandlery on sight. Fel'rekt waits at the bottom of them with a skiff.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked for every member present, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The south quay is empty at this hour, and the only light comes from a shuttered lantern on the stern of a skiff tied at the foot of the steps. A young drow in a velvet cloak is holding the painter, and he grins at you as you come down, counts the number of you off on his fingers, and holds up a handful of strips of black cloth.
>
> > "Hi, it's Fel'rekt, again. The captain's rule for tonight is that nobody rides in with open eyes, and I'm sorry about that, because it isn't you, it's the rule for everyone who hasn't been in before. I'll hold your elbow the whole way, and nobody's going to push you in the harbor, I promise. Please sit in the middle, because the skiff's a bit tippy."

If **Kreb Unmasked** is marked for every member present, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A second drow sits in the bow of the skiff with a ledger open on his knee and a pencil behind his ear, and he waves the pencil at you without looking up from the page.
>
> > "Evening, darling, don't mind me, I'm only here for the paperwork, and Fel does the talking. Mind the third thwart, it's got a splinter that has it in for everybody."

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for every member present, read or paraphrase the following instead, and don't use the blindfolds:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fel'rekt is waiting at the bottom of the steps with the skiff, and he nods to you and holds out a hand to help you aboard. He rows out from the quay without a word about blindfolds, and a big carnival ship comes up out of the dark off the bow a quarter of a mile from shore, with its stern lantern burning and its rigging hung with unlit paper lamps.
>
> > "Evening. We go in through the captain's cabin tonight, the way you've been before. He asked me to say that he's sorry to wake you for this."

The ride takes twenty minutes. The blindfolded members are taken up a rope ladder and through a door, along a passage and down a hatch, and Fel'rekt's hand never leaves an elbow.

> [!exploration]**What the Blindfold Hides**
>
> Any character who rides blindfolded and makes a successful **DC 16 Wisdom (Perception)** check smells burnt sugar and fish oil, hears the creak of a very large hull overhead, and notices that the ladder climbs the side of a ship and not down into a boat. The character concludes that the *Scarlet Marpenoth* lies beneath a big vessel in Deepwater Harbor, and that the vessel is a carnival.
>
> - **Auto:** A character who has already been aboard the *Eyecatcher* and who is not blindfolded knows the ship on sight.
> - **Failure:** The character is taken down a ladder and through a hatch and learns only that the boat is brass and cold, and that a gnome woman said "evening" as they passed an open door.
>
> Nobody confirms what a character concludes. If anyone says the word Faire aloud, Fel'rekt says that he didn't hear it, and Krebbyg, if he is there, says that he didn't either.

When the party reaches the stateroom corridor, Fel'rekt takes the blindfolds off. A brass plate on the bulkhead beside the ladder says *Scarlet Marpenoth* in Lantanese script, and members who can read it learn the boat's name.

### A Word in the Corridor

Nevercott takes aside every member who stood in the alley during **The Dockside Killer**, whether that member took part in the fight, watched from the street or arrived afterward and saw Soluun taken or dead. A member who wasn't there skips this scene. Nevercott takes the others one at a time, and the rest go up the ladder with Fel'rekt while he waits.

> [!gamemaster]**What Nevercott Knows**
>
> Nevercott has the Luskan sailors' description of the party, and Fel'rekt has recognized the member from it, because he has been watching the member since the First Meeting. Nevercott doesn't suspect the member of betraying the company. He wants to know two things, which are whether the member knew what Soluun was and what the member did with what they saw. He asks once, in the corridor and out of earshot of the rest, and he doesn't raise it again. If the member answers well, it is over.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott is standing at the foot of the ladder to the upper deck with his hat in his hand, and as the others file past, he lays two fingers on your arm and nods toward a bulkhead lamp a few steps along. He waits until the rest of the party has climbed out of earshot, and then he looks at you and smiles without any warmth at all.
>
> > "You were there."

> [!social]**The Haberdasher Without His Smile**
>
> J.B. Nevercott (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in a hat of disguise, he/him) :: Bregan D'aerthe's contact with the party, who is Jarlaxle in a hat and is giving a new member a polite chance to explain a very awkward evening.
>
> Nevercott is cool, unhurried and exact. He asks the question and waits, and he is not angry, because he rarely is, and the lack of anger is the part that is hard to bear. He wants to hear that the member didn't know what Soluun was before the alley and that the member kept the company out of the Watch's hands afterward. He doesn't want an apology. He speaks in long courteous sentences and never raises his voice.
>
> Nevercott is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who was in the alley, which he already knows.
> - What the member knew of Soluun before that night.
> - What the member told the Watch, the Doom Raiders or anyone else afterward.
> - What the company will do about Soluun, which he says the member will see for themselves in a few minutes.

> [!qna]**Who told you?**
>
> > "Two Luskan sailors who were paid to guard the end of a street, and they described you to a barkeep who drinks with some people I know. It was a very small world tonight, and it'll be a smaller one by morning. I'm not displeased that you know it."

> [!qna]**Am I in trouble?**
>
> > "That depends on what you say next, and I'd like you to take a moment before you say it. The company doesn't mind that you stood in a street, since people stand in streets all the time. It minds very much when a member stands in the one where one of its own is killing people and doesn't think to mention it. Tell me what you knew and what you did."

The member answers in their own words, and the check below decides whether the answer holds.

> [!exploration]**Explaining Yourself**
>
> A member's explanation counts as good if it gives Nevercott at least two of the following:
>
> - That the member was there as a friend's companion, took the job without knowing anything about Soluun, and learned only in the alley what he was.
> - Something the member did to keep the company out of the Watch's hands, such as saying nothing to Gorra about the Seven Masks Theater, leaving the playbill with someone who wouldn't read it, or refusing to say what the disc was.
> - Something the company doesn't yet have, such as what Gorra told the party about the bodies, what the Doom Raiders now know about the theater, or the playbill itself if the member holds it.
>
> A member who gives a good explanation and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion or Deception)** check convinces Nevercott. Mark **Soluun Witness Explained** for that member. A member who gives fewer than two of the three, or who gives a good explanation and fails the check, is penalized. Mark **Soluun Witness Penalized** and take 1 Renown from that member, to a minimum of Renown 1.
>
> - **Advantage:** The member hands Nevercott the playbill from the alley.
> - **Disadvantage:** The member struck the blow that killed Soluun, if Soluun is dead, and the DC is 15 instead of 13.
> - **Failure, with a fellow member:** Another Bregan D'aerthe member who wasn't penalized can speak for the member and make a successful **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion)** check, once. The penalty is cancelled and **Soluun Witness Explained** is marked instead.

If the member is convinced, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott listens to the whole account with his head tilted and his hat turning slowly in his hands, and he doesn't interrupt once. When you have finished he looks at the floor for a moment and then puts the hat back on his head.
>
> > "Thank you, that's very much what I'd hoped to hear. We'll say no more about it, and I'd like you to say no more about it either. Please go up with the others now, and mind the third rung, because it's loose."

If the member is penalized, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott listens to you without moving, and when you have finished he nods slowly, as though a very small account has been settled in a ledger he keeps in his head. He puts the hat back on and steps aside to let you pass.
>
> > "I see. I'm sorry it's that, because I'd rather it had been something else. You'll find the company remembers a thing like this for a little while, and that is all it will do about it. Please go up."

The Renown a penalized member loses can be earned back with ordinary mission awards, and nothing else follows from it.

### The Captain's Chair

The upper deck of the control room holds a bronze periscope, a teak stand with a dark crystal ball on it and a tall chair upholstered in dark red leather. The lower deck below it is the pilots' station, and the pilots have their backs turned.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked for every member present, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The upper deck of the control room is small and lit by a single hanging lamp, and the bronze periscope has been swung to one side to make room. A tall chair of dark red leather stands against the far bulkhead, empty, with a wide-brimmed hat hung on one arm of it, and J.B. Nevercott stands beside the chair with his hands folded in front of his coat. Fel'rekt takes his place against the wall, and two drow guards stand at the top of the ladder with their hands loose at their sides.
>
> > "Thank you all for coming. Please stand where you can see the ladder, because someone is going to come up it in a minute, and I'd like you to see his face when he does."

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for every member present, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The upper deck of the control room is small and lit by a single hanging lamp, and the tall chair of dark red leather against the far bulkhead is occupied. A drow with a wide-brimmed hat, a long white feather and a patch over one eye sits in it with one boot on the opposite knee and his fingers laced, and he nods to each of you as you come up the ladder. Fel'rekt takes his place against the wall, and two drow guards stand at the top of the ladder with their hands loose at their sides.
>
> > "My friends, thank you for coming so late. I'm afraid I've asked you here to watch something unpleasant, and I would rather you saw it done properly than heard it described badly afterward. Please stand where you can see the ladder."

Fel'rekt goes down to fetch Soluun, who has been told only that the captain wants him in the control room. The members hear him on the rungs a moment before he appears.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A drow comes up the ladder with his hand crossbow on his hip and his chin raised, and he is already speaking before his head clears the deck. His coat is stiff with dirt, and a silver disc on a cord hangs at the open collar of his shirt. He sees the tall chair, and whoever stands or sits beside it, and he stops talking.
>
> > "I came the moment I got word, straight from the quay, and I'll tell the captain all of it. The Watch had nothing on me that I didn't want them to have, and the humans never could hold me, and I did every bit of it for the company."

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for every member present, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Soluun drops to one knee as soon as he sees the chair, with his head bowed and the hand crossbow still at his belt. The drow in the chair looks at him for a long time before he speaks.

The speaker gives the ruling now.

If Nevercott delivers it, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott lets Soluun finish and waits a moment longer, so that the room is quite still. He takes one step away from the empty chair and speaks in a mild, level voice, the way a man reads out a tradesman's bill.
>
> > "Soluun Xibrindas, the captain has heard everything the Harbor Watch, the broadsheets and our own people have to say about the Dock Ward, and he has decided. You killed in the open in a city where we've spent a year learning not to be seen, and a Watch officer now knows your face and the face of every person who stood in that alley. The disc you wear was made at the captain's order so that the company could deny you, and it will not need to be a lie any longer."
>
> He nods to Fel'rekt, who steps forward and waits.
>
> > "You are no longer of the company. You'll go ashore tonight with the clothes you stand in, and you will not come aboard again. The captain has asked me to say that he's grateful for every year you gave him, and that this is the end of them. Please don't make it harder than it is."

If Jarlaxle delivers it, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle lets Soluun finish and waits a moment longer, so that the room is quite still. He lifts one hand from his knee and speaks in a light, pleasant voice that never rises, the voice of a man declining a second cup of wine.
>
> > "Soluun, my dear, I have heard everything the Harbor Watch, the broadsheets and my own people have to say about the Dock Ward, and I'm afraid I've decided. You killed in the open in a city where I've spent a year teaching the rest of you not to be seen, and now a Watch officer knows your face and the faces of everyone who stood in that alley. That disc was made so that the company could deny you, and I find I'd rather it were true."
>
> He turns the hand over, and Fel'rekt steps forward and waits.
>
> > "You are no longer of the company. You'll go ashore tonight with the clothes you stand in, and you will not come aboard again. I am grateful for every year you gave me, and I'm sorry to say that those years are over."

Whichever version is read, the speaker then turns to the members and adds the following in the same voice:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The speaker turns his head and looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "He has slept aboard this boat for a year, and he knows where she lies and how she moves. I'd like you to hear me say it aloud, so that nobody is surprised later. The captain judges that risk worth bearing, and he won't be moving her this month."

### Soluun Answers

Soluun doesn't fight. He is outnumbered by the speaker, Fel'rekt and two guards, and he is too much of a believer to put a hand on his weapons in front of the chair. What he does is argue, and the members have a few moments to watch him do it.

> [!social]**The Zealot Dismissed**
>
> Soluun Xibrindas (Neutral Evil, Drow, he/him) :: a fanatical drow killer who has been told within the minute that the captain he worships no longer wants him.
>
> Soluun is stunned, then furious, then obedient, in that order, and the three states don't last long enough for anyone to relax. He speaks to the speaker in a flat low voice that rises into fervor whenever he says "the captain", and he speaks to the members in orders and insults. He spits when a surface elf is named. If a member is an elf or half-elf, he calls them surface-blood to their face and doesn't mean it kindly. He never apologizes, he never says Lolth's name with anything but disgust, and he doesn't mention his brother except as "the weak one". He doesn't believe the captain means it until Fel'rekt lifts the disc from his neck.
>
> Soluun is happy to discuss the following topics, though only with someone who isn't the speaker:
>
> - The captain, whom he defends and thanks and defends again.
> - The Doom Raiders and the alley, which he talks about with disgust.
> - The disc, and what it stands for.
> - Why he did what he did, which he explains with relish.

> [!qna]**You can't mean this?**
>
> Soluun stares at the speaker, and his hand moves toward the crossbow and stops.
>
> > "I did everything for you. Every single one of them was for you, and I'd have taken a dozen more before the tide turned. You can't send me out of here with nothing, because I have nowhere to go and nobody to serve, and I know what I'm worth. Tell me this is a test, and I'll pass it."

> [!qna]**What about the disc?**
>
> Soluun touches the silver disc at his throat, a spider in its web crossed by a blade.
>
> > "I always knew the band never struck it, and I wore it because the captain gave it to me, and it was the only thing that was ever mine. That spider is the goddess I walked away from, and I'm glad of the knife through it. Take it, then, and see how it feels to hold."

> [!qna]**Which god do you pray to?**
>
> > "None. I walked away from that spider-bitch's rotten faith and I've never once missed her. The captain gave me better work than she ever did, and he never asked me to kneel for it. I'd kneel to him all night if he asked me, and he'd see that I meant it."

> [!qna]**Why the sailors?**
>
> Soluun looks at the members and then away from them, as if he has already decided what they are.
>
> > "Because they walk your streets as though the sun belonged to them, and someone has to count them. Filthy fucking half-breeds, the lot of them, and you all smell the same. If the captain has a quarrel with that, he can tell me to my face."

The members may now act. They can speak for Soluun to the speaker, press Soluun while he packs, or name him to the company as a danger. Each member may take at most one of the three, and different members may take different ones. All of it happens before Fel'rekt takes the disc.

### Speaking for Soluun

A member who pleads for Soluun asks the speaker to let him keep something. The expulsion itself is not open to change, and the speaker says so as soon as anyone asks for it.

> [!gamemaster]**What a Plea Costs**
>
> A member who pleads must first say aloud that they will answer for Soluun, because the speaker refuses to hear a plea without a guarantor. The cost is real. If anything belonging to the company is sold, lost or harmed through Soluun before Tarsakh 20, that member loses 1 Renown when it comes to light, to a minimum of Renown 1, and **The Dive** is where it comes to light. A member who is already down 1 Renown from the witness beat can still plead, but has Disadvantage on the check.

> [!exploration]**Pleading for Him**
>
> Any character who argues for Soluun and makes a successful **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion)** check persuades the speaker to let him leave with his hand crossbow, his curved shortsword and a purse of 50 gp. The member has accepted the cost above, and the speaker hands the purse to Fel'rekt with an air of mild regret.
>
> - **Advantage:** The character argues from what Soluun has done for the company, and names a specific year or job.
> - **Disadvantage:** The character is marked **Soluun Witness Penalized**, or says that Soluun should be forgiven for the killings.
> - **Failure:** The speaker declines, and Soluun leaves with nothing but his clothes. The member owes nothing, and no one else may plead once two members have failed.
>
> Mark **Soluun Pleaded For** for a member who succeeds.

If the plea succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The speaker considers you for a long moment, and then lets out a slow breath and lifts a hand toward Fel'rekt.
>
> > "Very well. He keeps his weapons and a little money, because you've asked for it and I'd rather you had a reason to remember me kindly. I'd like you to understand what you've just agreed to, however. If he turns them on the company, you answer for it, and I won't ask twice."

If the plea fails, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The speaker listens to the whole argument without interrupting and then shakes his head, not unkindly.
>
> > "That was well said, and I'm sorry to refuse it. The ruling is made, and he leaves as he is. I'd ask you to let the matter go."

### Ten Minutes in the Stateroom

Fel'rekt takes Soluun down to U3, his stateroom, to pack whatever he is allowed to take, and the speaker tells the members that they have the time it takes. Soluun keeps his weapons on his belt until the disc comes off, and Fel'rekt stands in the cabin door with a hand crossbow lowered and a look of deep unhappiness.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stateroom is a narrow brass cabin with a bunk, a footlocker and a row of cleaning tools laid out on a folded cloth, and Soluun is stuffing the last of his belongings into a canvas bag with short rough movements. A lantern swings from a hook in the ceiling and throws his shadow back and forth across the bulkhead. He doesn't look up when you come in, and he doesn't stop packing.

> [!exploration]**What He Knows**
>
> Each member may try one of the following approaches, and a failed attempt gives Soluun a reason to spit at the member's feet. Two failures in a row cut the time short, and Fel'rekt says "That's time" before anyone else can try.
>
> **His brother.** Any character who appeals to Soluun's loyalty and makes a successful **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** check, or who listens to his rants and makes a successful **DC 16 Wisdom (Insight)** check, hears him let slip that the weak one has been cleaning up after him with the other side's paper for two months, and that nobody asked him to. The character doesn't learn Nar'l's name or where he sits. Mark **Soluun Pressed** and note the Brother result for that member.
>
> **The boat.** Any character who watches Soluun while he packs and makes a successful **DC 15 Wisdom (Insight)** check sees that he is committing the cabin to memory, looking along the bulkheads and the ladder and the hatch at the end of the corridor. A character who then presses him and makes a successful **DC 16 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check hears him say that he knows where she lies, how long she takes to move and which hours the hatch stands open. Mark **Soluun Pressed** and note the Mooring result for that member.
>
> - **Advantage:** The character is an elf or half-elf, and Soluun is too angry to guard his tongue.
> - **Disadvantage:** The character pleaded for him, and Soluun despises being pitied.
> - **Failure:** The member learns nothing from Soluun beyond what the speaker said aloud.

If a member learns what he knows about his brother, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Soluun slams the footlocker lid and finally looks up, and his lip curls at something that isn't any of you.
>
> > "The weak one has been burying my work for two months with the other side's own paper, and nobody asked him to. He thinks I don't know. He thinks he's clever and careful, and he's going to drown in it, because the other side can read as well as he can."

If a member learns what he knows about the boat, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Soluun's hands stop on the strap of the bag, and he looks along the corridor toward the ladder and the hatch at the end of it. His voice is very low, and there is something almost like grief in it.
>
> > "I know where she lies, and how long she takes to get under, and what hours that hatch stands open. I could draw it for anyone with my eyes shut. You may tell the captain I said so, if you like, because he's already told you he knows."

### A Word About Later

A member who thinks Soluun is a danger to the company or to the city can say so aloud, in front of the speaker and Soluun, and the speaker listens with real attention. The member doesn't need a check, and the member pays in Soluun's hatred.

> [!gamemaster]**What Marking Him Does**
>
> The speaker thanks the member and tells Fel'rekt, and Krebbyg if he is present, that the two of them will watch Soluun for a tenday and report where he goes. The member who marked Soluun is the person Fel'rekt warns first if Soluun moves against the company. Mark **Soluun Marked a Threat** for that member. Soluun hears every word and learns who said it, and the member becomes the face he looks for later.

If a member marks Soluun, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The speaker turns his head to look at you, and the room is very still. Soluun has stopped packing and is looking at you as well, and Fel'rekt, in the cabin door, glances once at the speaker and then at the floor.
>
> > "Thank you. That's an honest thing to say and a difficult one, and I'll see it's acted on. Fel'rekt, you'll keep an eye on him for a tenday, and you'll tell this member first if he so much as buys a rope."
>
> Soluun hasn't looked away from you.
>
> > "I'll remember your face better than you'll ever remember mine."

### The Disc

When the ten minutes are over, Fel'rekt takes Soluun back up the ladder to the upper deck. He asks Soluun to lift his chin, and Soluun does.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Soluun stands in the center of the upper deck with his bag over one shoulder and his chin raised, and he doesn't speak. Fel'rekt lifts the hand crossbow and the shortsword from his belt and hangs them on his own, and then he slips two fingers under the cord at Soluun's neck and draws the silver disc over his head, the spider and the blade on its face catching the lamp as it swings. He closes his hand around it and holds it out to the speaker, who shakes his head.
>
> > "Keep it, Fel'rekt, for the time being. I'd like it broken in the harbor, since it's no use to anyone, and it would be pleasant if you did it yourself."
>
> Fel'rekt nods and puts the disc in his pocket, and for a moment he looks as though he'd like to say something to Soluun that he isn't allowed to say.

If a member pleaded for Soluun successfully, Fel'rekt gives Soluun back his hand crossbow, his shortsword and a purse of 50 gp at the quay, and not before.

### The South Quay

The speaker tells the members that they will ride with Soluun as far as the shore, so that they can say they saw it done. Fel'rekt rows, and Krebbyg, if he is aboard, sits in the stern with a second crossbow across his knees.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The skiff bumps against the foot of the quay steps below Ship Street, where the alley from the Muleskull ends in a patch of unlit cobbles, and the harbor slaps at the pilings on either side. Soluun climbs out first with his bag over his shoulder, and he stands on the bottom step and looks up the dark slot of the alley without moving.
>
> > "I'll be back. You'll see I'm right about the captain, and when he sees it too, he'll send for me."

If nobody pleaded successfully, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fel'rekt waits until Soluun has gone a dozen steps, and then he reaches into his cloak and takes out the silver disc. He looks at it in his palm for a moment and drops it into the harbor between the skiff and the pilings, and the water closes over it.
>
> > "Sorry. I really wanted it to go another way."

If **Soluun Pleaded For** is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fel'rekt waits until Soluun has gone a dozen steps and then calls after him, and tosses the hand crossbow, the shortsword and a small leather purse onto the cobbles at his feet. Soluun picks them up one at a time and doesn't look back. Fel'rekt turns to the member who spoke for him and drops his voice.
>
> > "Sorry. I really wanted it to go another way, and I hope he's worth it. You'd better remember that you said he was."

If a member attacked Soluun aboard, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Soluun turns on the steps and looks at the member who struck him, and he touches the bruise on his own jaw with two fingers.
>
> > "You'll want to watch the water, because I'm going to be in it."

### Provoking the Crew

Soluun doesn't fight aboard, and Nevercott doesn't want a fight in the captain's boat. A member who draws a weapon or strikes Soluun, Fel'rekt or a guard has begun one.

> [!hazard]**A Fight Aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth***
>
> A fight breaks out on the upper deck of the control room, or in the stateroom corridor if the member provoked Soluun while he packed. Nevercott doesn't fight, and he stays by the chair with his hands folded. Soluun uses the **Scout** from the 2024 *Monster Manual* with the changes described in **The Dockside Killer**. Fel'rekt also uses the **Scout**, with a hand crossbow in place of the longbow, and Krebbyg, if he is aboard, uses the same block. The two guards are **Warrior Veterans**. This fight is not built to be won. The crew outclasses a party of 2nd or 3rd level, so it lasts three rounds at most, and the crew strikes only characters who are attacking. Soluun's own numbers are the Scout figures in section 1 of the **Doom Raiders Mechanics Reference**.
>
> #### Soluun's Tactics
> Soluun fires at the member who provoked him, and otherwise at the nearest character.
>
> During combat, Soluun:
> - Draws his hand crossbow and fires at the provoker from the nearest cover.
> - Uses his shortsword on anyone who closes with him.
> - Makes for the ladder and the hatch if he is reduced to 20 Hit Points or fewer, and the guards stop him at the ladder.
>
> #### The Crew's Tactics
> The guards move to the ladder and block it. Fel'rekt calls for the fight to stop twice, in plain words, before he fires, and he fires only at a member who is attacking Soluun.
>
> During combat, the crew:
> - Hold the ladder and the corridor so that nobody can reach the hatch.
> - Target a character who has attacked Soluun, Fel'rekt or a guard, and otherwise do nothing.
> - Stop fighting the moment Nevercott says "Enough."
>
> The battle ends when Soluun has been reduced to 10 Hit Points or fewer, at which point he lowers his weapons, or when Nevercott says the word at the end of the third round. A blow that would reduce Soluun to 0 Hit Points leaves him at 1 Hit Point and Unconscious instead, because the guards have been told that he goes ashore breathing.
>
> Each member who struck first loses 1 Renown, to a minimum of Renown 1, and Nevercott ends the meeting. Mark **Soluun Provoked** for each member who attacked Soluun. Soluun goes ashore with a bruise and a grudge, and Fel'rekt takes the rest of the evening at a brisk pace.

### If Soluun Was Killed

If **Soluun Killed** was marked in **The Dockside Killer**, the company has heard the news from the two Luskan sailors who guarded the street end. The Event runs on the tenth night after the ambush, aboard the same boat, and it replaces the ruling with a short conversation. The card, the passage and the witness beat run as written, and a member who struck the blow that killed Soluun is held to the higher DC in the witness beat.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked for every member present, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The upper deck of the control room is warm and lit by a single lamp, and J.B. Nevercott stands beside the empty chair with his hat in his hands and an unhurried look on his face. Fel'rekt is against the wall with his arms folded across his chest and his eyes on the floor.
>
> > "Thank you for coming. I'm afraid I've some bad news that I'd prefer you heard from me. Soluun Xibrindas is dead, and the people who killed him are known to us. I'll tell you what I know, and then I'll ask each of you what you know, and I'd like you to be honest."

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for every member present, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jarlaxle sits in the tall chair with his hat on one knee and his long feather drooping, and he doesn't smile when you come up the ladder. Fel'rekt is against the wall with his arms folded and his eyes on the floor.
>
> > "My friends, thank you for coming. I'm afraid I've a piece of news that I wanted you to hear from me. Soluun is dead, and I know who killed him."

The speaker tells the members what the company has learned.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The speaker gives you a moment before he goes on, and he speaks in the same mild voice, though it has a little less ease in it than before.
>
> > "Two Luskan sailors saw a party of strangers in an alley off Ship Street, and one of them drinks with a man who works for me. They described the faces and the weapons. I don't need them to tell me who your friends are, because I think some of you already know. Soluun was useful and unwise, and I did not like him, and I like it even less that someone else decided he was finished. It's become a personal matter for the captain, and I'd like each of you to remember that when you next sit down with the people who did it."

> [!social]**The Company Wants Names**
>
> J.B. Nevercott (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in a hat of disguise, he/him) :: Jarlaxle in a hat, who is cooler than usual and not at all pleased.
>
> The speaker is Nevercott, or Jarlaxle Baenre himself if **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for every member present. The speaker doesn't blame the members for the company they keep, and he says so. He wants them to understand that the killing has changed the way the captain sees the Doom Raiders and the people around them, and that the captain would prefer a member of the company to be on the right side when the names are spoken. He asks nobody to betray a friend. If Jarlaxle is present, he says the same things in his own words and with more sting.
>
> The speaker is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who killed Soluun, which he says he already knows.
> - Whether the members' friends are in danger from the company, which he says depends on them.
> - The reason the captain is taking it personally, which he doesn't give.

> [!qna]**Are our friends in danger?**
>
> > "That depends on what they do next, and I won't insult you by saying more than I know. The captain would sooner talk to them than do anything else, and he usually gets the talk he wants. I'd advise your friends not to go looking for the Seven Masks Theater, and I'd advise you not to bring them to it."

> [!qna]**Why does the captain care?**
>
> > "Soluun was his, in a way I'd ask you not to inquire into, and he was killed by strangers who didn't know that. The captain has a long memory for a matter of that kind, and he likes to repay it in person. I wouldn't read more into that than I've said."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us?**
>
> > "Your good judgment, mostly, and your honesty if you're asked. A member who stood in an alley and says plainly what they saw is a member the company can depend on, and one who says nothing is a different matter. That's all I'll ask."

The members aren't asked to name anyone, and nobody is expelled. Mark **Soluun Killers Named**.

### Aftermath

Fel'rekt rows the members back to the south quay and leaves them on the steps. Soluun isn't seen in the Dock Ward again before **The Dive**, and the company doesn't move the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. If Soluun was killed, the company says nothing more to the members about it, and the only change is that Nevercott's manner with them is cooler for a tenday.

## Overview

A card from the company calls Bregan D'aerthe's members aboard a boat they have never seen. They are asked to watch while the company decides what to do with the drow gunslinger from the Dock Ward.

## Summary

A black card took us out to the south quay at night, and Fel'rekt rowed us to a boat hidden under a ship in the harbor. We watched Nevercott strip Soluun of his silver disc and put him ashore, and we were each asked what we'd seen in the alley off Ship Street. Soluun swore he'd be back, and we didn't know whether to believe him.
