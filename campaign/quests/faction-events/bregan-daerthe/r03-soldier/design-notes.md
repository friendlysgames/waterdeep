# Design Notes: Soldier

## Individual Procedures

Each Soldier benefit is a per-member procedure with a named contact, a place, a notice rule, limits, a loss rule and a counter.

- **Report channel:** Krebbyg, asked for by a slip or a counter request and answered by a playbill at dawn.
- **Faire cover support:** Odalys Vane at the *Heartbreaker*'s gate desk, with one hour's notice, three visits in each block of ten days and a twenty-day loss.
- **Pawnbroker discount:** Ostrin Brindle on Dock Street, with a 300 gp cap per block and a one-day delay.

The **BD Soldier** outcome records the three counters per member, and nothing pools across the party.

The three benefits stay apart from the Initiate set that **Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting** already grants. Fel'rekt's gear stays at cost and is excluded from the 20% discount, the berth stays a bed, and the costume room has no hammocks.

## The Report Ladder and the Lost Source

The report is a fixed ladder: the Guild's escalation tier toward the party, plus the next response team not yet beaten. Both come from the Response Teams and Escalation sections of the **Xanathar's Guild** Factions Guide page. Every report is prepared in advance, outposts and the lair stay unspoiled, and the guide's rule holds that reports give movement and contacts and never layout. Nobody names Nar'l before **The Compromised Eye**, so the notes say "our friend inside".

The channel degrades by **The Compromised Eye** outcome.

- **Nar'l Cleared** is treated as **Nar'l Active**.
- **Nar'l Extracted** and **Nar'l Eliminated** replace the source with Fel'rekt's waterfront watchers, who count crews and can't judge the Guild's temper.
- **Nar'l Eliminated** also halves the frequency, as Jarlaxle's displeasure.

The rank itself is never lost.

## Timing and Delivery

On base awards a member reaches Renown 3 right after **The Handkerchief and the Girl**, so the playbill arrives on Ches 23, after the Fireball explosion on Ches 22. The Faire cover support exists only from the fleet's arrival on Ches 21 to its sailing on Tarsakh 20.

**Kreb Unmasked** can be marked here only if the member ignored the playbill long enough to run **The Wazoo Affair** and **Kreb Drops the Cover** first, so the Krebbyg-present branch is rare. The old draft's "substitute Krebbyg if it fires late" line became three explicit cases: neither outcome marked, **Kreb Unmasked** and **Zardoz Introduced**.

## Source Departures

- The restored draft called **Three Nights** "Mission 4". Guide 08 numbers it Mission 3, and the line is gone.
- Guide 08 gives Soldier "Jarlaxle shares one piece of intelligence". Here Nevercott delivers it and Krebbyg writes it, and nobody says Jarlaxle's name unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked.
- The draft's dead-drop lamp post and ticket window are replaced by the **Kreb Drops the Cover** channel, the grey coat on peg seven.
- The draft let Soldiers into the Faire's "preparation room". That is now a costume room with a cover name, a test and limits. The *Eyecatcher* stays excluded.
- The draft folded Fel'rekt's gear into the Soldier discount. Guide 08 gives it to Initiates, so the discount now covers a separate goods list.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Odalys Vane, Ostrin Brindle and Brindle and Daughter on Dock Street, the three cover names (Pell Harrowgate, Tamsin Orr, Corwin Alder), the costume room and rehearsal booth on the pier, and the 300 gp, three-visit and twenty-day numbers. Odalys and Ostrin are voiced from the event text, because Nevercott has no profile. A Nevercott profile belongs in `character-voices` if the user wants one.

For the out-of-scope log:
- Guide 08 and `player-factions-overview.md` say "the party" where Soldier is individual.
- The costume room and booth are not in the Sea Maidens Faire location files. The Faire's sailing date of Tarsakh 20 agrees with **The Dive**.
- A member whose Renown drops below 3 keeps the Soldier rank and the three benefits are suspended, the same rule as **Officer**, **Commander** and **Houseless Noble**.
- **BD Soldier** is read by **Faction Outposts** (unconverted) for the Seven Masks door staff.
