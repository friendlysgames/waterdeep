# Bregan D'aerthe — Soldier

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Bregan D'aerthe renown reaches 3 — the next visit to Nevercott at Seven Masks Theater, or Nevercott sends word within a tenday. J.B. Nevercott runs the promotion. In this Event:
>
> - Nevercott names the character a Soldier, marks the promotion in D'aerthe fashion — clipped, professional, precise
> - He explains the tenday intelligence arrangement (Nar'l Xibrindas's reports) and establishes its rules
> - He confirms Sea Maidens Faire access for cover identities and discreet meetings
> - He demonstrates the 20% discount by producing a small inventory of drow-made equipment and inviting a purchase at that rate
> - No combat; one beat of faction color about how D'aerthe marks loyalty
>
> This event precedes BD Mission 4 (**Three Nights**). After Mission 4, Zardoz Zord is the active contact — but the r03 event should be run before that point. If it somehow fires later, substitute Krebbyg for Nevercott and note that "J" sends his regards.

### The Meeting at Seven Masks

Nevercott sends word through the usual channel: a Seven Masks playbill left at the manor's door, a particular scene marked with a small pencil dot. The note means the booking manager's office, two hours after the evening show. No urgency; this is routine.

The back office at Seven Masks is small, well-organized, and permanently smells of candle wax and old programs. Krebbyg has cleared the space and is not present for this particular meeting. Nevercott stands when the character enters — the only concession to ceremony he will make.

> "Soldier. That is the rank. I am told you have earned it."

He does not elaborate on who told him, or how the decision was made, or what the deliberations looked like. In D'aerthe, the rank appears when the work has justified it. The announcement is the process.

### The Intelligence Arrangement

Nevercott slides a folded card across the desk. It has no heading.

> "Beginning now: once per tenday, you may request an intelligence summary drawn from our embedded network. Reports on Xanathar's movement patterns, the beholder's current obsessions, which Guild members have been seen on the surface and when. Our source inside the organization is reliable."

He does not name Nar'l Xibrindas. He says "our source" with the deliberate patience of someone who has learned that names are a form of currency best spent carefully.

> "You request through Krebbyg. He will have the summary within 24 hours, delivered to a dead drop of your choosing. The summaries describe movement patterns and surface contacts. They do not describe lair layout. The distinction is deliberate."

The dead drop system is practical: a chalk mark on a specific lamp post in the Dock Ward, a folded note left inside a false back of a ticket window at Seven Masks. The character establishes their preferred method now.

> [!note]**The Intelligence Limit**
>
> One request per tenday, not one per scene. If the party burns the request on minor information and a genuine operational need arises later in the same tenday, there is nothing to be done. Nevercott noted the distinction was deliberate. He meant it.

### Sea Maidens Faire Access

> "The Sea Maidens Faire. When you have need of a cover identity or a meeting that cannot happen in a ward where you are known, speak to the stage manager at the Faire's main entrance. Give the phrase: *the autumn program*. You will be given access to a preparation room on the Heartbreaker and a name for the evening."

He adds one qualification.

> "The access does not extend to the ships themselves. The pier carnival is your stage. The Eyecatcher is not part of this arrangement."

He does not explain what the Eyecatcher is. That is forthcoming, or it is not.

### Equipment at Rate

Nevercott opens a satchel that has been sitting beneath the desk and sets three items on the surface: a hand crossbow, a vial of bolts marked in a cipher, and a thin leather case containing a pair of goggles.

> "Drow manufacture. Through Fel'rekt Lafeen, on commission. Soldiers purchase at cost — which, in practical terms, is twenty percent below what the market charges. The quality is better than the market's. Fel'rekt will know you're coming."

He closes the satchel and does not offer it. The sample is a demonstration; the purchase is a separate transaction with Fel'rekt.

> [!profile]**Profile: J.B. Nevercott at Soldier Rank**
>
> ***Persona.*** He has been watching the character's work since the first assignment. He does not compliment it. He gives them a rank. To Nevercott, the rank is the compliment.
>
> ***What he does not say.*** He does not say Jarlaxle chose them specifically. He does not indicate that Soldier rank is unusual. He speaks as though the whole thing is routine, because for Bregan D'aerthe, it is.
>
> ***Voice.*** "Soldier. That is the rank." — No theater. No build-up.

## Overview

J.B. Nevercott, the haberdasher who is not a haberdasher, summons the character to the Seven Masks Theater back office and names them a Soldier of Bregan D'aerthe. No ceremony. He explains the intelligence arrangement, the Faire access, and the equipment rate, then tells them Fel'rekt will be expecting them.

## Summary

Nevercott named the character a Soldier at a meeting in Seven Masks's back office. He described the tenday intelligence summaries available through Krebbyg, confirmed Sea Maidens Faire access for cover identities, and demonstrated the equipment discount with three items from a satchel he returned to the floor when the demonstration was done.
