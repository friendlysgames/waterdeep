# Soldier

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Bregan D'aerthe member first reaches Renown 3, usually right after **The Handkerchief and the Girl**. J.B. Nevercott names the rank in the booking office of the Seven Masks Theater and gives that member the three Soldier benefits. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Hear the rank named and receive the first report from the company's source inside Xanathar's Guild.
> - Learn how to ask for one report every tenday, and what replaces the source if he is lost.
> - Choose a standing cover name from the Sea Maidens Faire's hire list and prove they can wear it.
> - Learn how to use the Faire's costume room and meeting booth while the fleet is in port.
> - Learn how to order restricted goods at a fifth off from a pawnbroker on Dock Street.

### The Playbill

At dawn on the second day after the member reaches Renown 3, a playbill is pushed under the door of wherever the member sleeps. For a member who reached Renown 3 at the debrief for **The Handkerchief and the Girl** on Ches 21, that is Ches 23.

- Every qualifying member is invited on their own account. Members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one.
- Companions who aren't members are not invited. An usher at the stage door turns away anyone who follows and claims to know nothing of a meeting.
- A member who can't attend finds the same playbill under the door on each of the next three dawns. After that, Kreb Sorrush hands it across the booking-office counter on the member's next visit, and the meeting takes place that night.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A playbill for the Seven Masks Theater lies on the floorboards inside your door, folded once, with a pencil dot beside the last act of *The Duke's Last Supper*. A line runs along the bottom margin in a neat, slanting hand.
>
> > "The booking office at ten bells tonight, after the house has emptied. Please come alone, and please bring your card. J.B. N."

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> The reports come from Nar'l Xibrindas, a drow mage who serves as Xanathar's advisor and casts *Sending* to Jarlaxle from inside the Guild. Nevercott never names him and says only "our source". Jarlaxle allows one report in ten days because every *Sending* risks the caster's life if the Guild notices a pattern.
>
> J.B. Nevercott is Jarlaxle Baenre in a *hat of disguise*. Nobody in this Event says the name Jarlaxle in speech or readaloud. Bregan D'aerthe speakers say "the captain" or "J."
>
> - A member who has marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** sees through the hat and may say so at the table.
> - Nevercott answers with a remark about brims, and the Event runs exactly as written.
>
> Jarlaxle knows the Cassalanters are bound to Asmodeus and have pledged their children in a contract, because his doppelganger agent Vessa reads thoughts in their social circle. The report channel never carries this, and no speaker tells a member who hasn't worked it out. If a member says in their own words that the Cassalanters are bound to a devil, or shows proof, Nevercott (or Krebbyg) stops deflecting and admits the company has known for some time, in the words of the admission in **The Theater's Back Room**. Vessa is not named, and **Cassalanter Pact Shared with BD** is marked for that member.
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr writes the reports from the source's *Sendings*, and the pencil dot on the playbill is his. Once the source is lost, Fel'rekt Lafeen's watchers on the waterfront supply the second part of every report, as described in **When the Source Is Lost** below.

> [!gamemaster]**Initiate and Soldier Benefits, Kept Apart**
>
> Every member already holds the Initiate benefits from the **Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting** Event: a hammock berth aboard the *Heartbreaker* or the *Hellraiser*, Fel'rekt's drow-made gear at cost from his pier stall, and the company's professional courtesy. This Event adds three Soldier benefits and takes nothing from the Initiate set. The sets do not stack.
>
> - **Fel'rekt's gear stays at cost.** The Dock Street discount covers other goods and never lowers a price on Fel'rekt's stall.
> - **The berth stays a bed.** The costume room is a place to change and meet, and it has no hammocks.
> - **The report is new.** The berth and the stall give no intelligence.
>
> A member whose Renown later falls below 3 through a penalty keeps the Soldier rank. The three Soldier benefits are suspended until Renown returns to 3, and the Initiate benefits are unaffected.

### The Booking Office

The meeting is at ten bells in the booking office of the Seven Masks Theater in the Dock Ward. The evening house empties at nine bells, and the soprano is still rehearsing in the next room through thin walls. The office is a narrow room off the lobby with a schedule pinned to every wall. Who receives the member depends on the member's outcomes:

- **Neither Kreb Unmasked nor Zardoz Introduced is marked:** Nevercott receives the member alone. Kreb Sorrush waits in the lobby in his human cover and does not come in.
- **Kreb Unmasked is marked:** Krebbyg sits in the room as himself. This is rare, because **Kreb Drops the Cover** follows **The Wazoo Affair**, which needs Renown 3. It happens only when a member ignored the playbill long enough to run both first.
- **Zardoz Introduced is marked:** Nevercott no longer appears to this member. Krebbyg runs the meeting as himself and says the haberdasher has gone back to hats. He gives every line below that Nevercott speaks in his own words and his own run-ons.

If Nevercott receives the member alone, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The booking office has been cleared for the night, and the schedules on its walls rustle whenever the lobby door moves. A single lamp burns on the desk, and J.B. Nevercott stands behind it in his burgundy coat with a folded playbill in one gloved hand. He waits until you have closed the door before he speaks.
>
> > "Good evening, and thank you for coming so late. The rank of Soldier is yours, and I'm told it was earned fairly. Please sit, because there are three arrangements to explain, and I'd like you to hear them all before you ask anything."

If Kreb Unmasked is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The booking office has been cleared for the night, and the schedules on its walls rustle whenever the lobby door moves. J.B. Nevercott stands behind the desk in his burgundy coat with a folded playbill in one gloved hand. Krebbyg sits on the corner of the desk with his brown wig in his lap and his white hair loose on his shoulders, swinging one boot and flipping through a schedule.
>
> > "Evening, darling, don't mind me, the soprano's in the next room and I'm only here because Fel says the paperwork's mine."
>
> Nevercott waits for him to finish and then turns to you with a small bow.
>
> > "Thank you for coming so late. The rank of Soldier is yours, and I'm told it was earned fairly. Please sit, because there are three arrangements to explain, and I'd like you to hear them all before you ask anything."

If Zardoz Introduced is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The booking office has been cleared for the night, and the schedules on its walls rustle whenever the lobby door moves. Krebbyg sits on the corner of the desk as himself, with his wig in his lap and a folded playbill in one hand. He swings a boot against the desk leg while he waits for you to shut the door.
>
> > "Evening, darling. The hat man's gone back to his hats, so it's me, and I'm sorry, because I'm worse at this than he was. You're a Soldier now, you earned it, and there are three things I'm supposed to explain, so sit down and I'll try to get them in order."

> [!social]**The Haberdasher with the Folded Card**
>
> J.B. Nevercott (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in a hat of disguise, he/him) :: Bregan D'aerthe's contact with the member, who delivers a rank the way other men deliver a parcel.
>
> Nevercott is Friendly and unhurried. His sentences are long, courteous and balanced, and he never swears. He answers each question with exactly as much as he is willing to say and a pleasant refusal for the rest. He is satisfied with the member and doesn't say so, and he never says the captain's name. If pressed about the source, he repeats that the less anyone says about the source, the longer the source lives.
>
> Nevercott is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Soldier rank gives the member, one benefit at a time.
> - Why the reports come only once in ten days.
> - What the report says and what it never says.
> - Whether the member may share a report with companions.
>
> He will not name the source, the captain or anything about the ships.

> [!qna]**What does Soldier get me?**
>
> Nevercott counts the arrangements off on the fingers of one gloved hand.
>
> > "Three things, and none of them costs you anything. Once every ten days you may ask for a report from a source we keep inside Xanathar's Guild. On the pier, the Faire will lend you a name and a room while the ships are in. On Dock Street, a gentleman who sells what licensed shops won't will charge you a fifth less than a stranger."

> [!qna]**Who is the source?**
>
> > "I'd rather not say, and I'd rather you didn't wonder about it aloud. He casts a spell to reach us, and each cast costs him something. Please make sure no one who works for the Guild ever sees what he sends."

> [!qna]**Why only once in ten days?**
>
> > "Every report is a risk to the person who writes it, and I won't take that risk twice in ten days to settle anyone's curiosity. If a report arrives late or short, please don't press Krebbyg. He'll only tell you he doesn't know."

> [!qna]**Can I tell my companions?**
>
> > "You may tell them what a report says, since you know them best. If it reaches the Guild or the Watch through one of them, though, the company will count it against you."

> [!exploration]**The Thumb on the Card**
>
> Any character who watches Nevercott explain the reports and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check notices two things. He runs a gloved thumb along the edge of the playbill each time he says "our source", and he looks at no one while he says it. The character concludes that the source is a living person the company worries about, not a ledger or a drop. A character who fails learns nothing more, and the meeting goes on as written.

### The First Report

Nevercott hands the member the folded playbill, and the member keeps it. Reading the margin takes a minute.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott holds out the playbill. When you unfold it, a note runs up the margin in a quick, looping hand with a great deal of underlining, and a smudge of greasepaint marks one corner.
>
> > "That's the first of them, and it's yours to read now. Please read it here, then burn it in the stove at your lodging. Paper has a way of outliving the man who wrote it."

The note has two parts. Read the part that matches the Guild's current escalation tier toward the member's party, as set out in **Running the Villains**. Then read the part that names the next response team the party has not beaten.

> [!exploration]**What the Margin Says**
>
> The first part tells the member how alarmed Xanathar's Guild is about their party. Krebbyg writes it in these words:
>
> - **Unaware:** "Darling, they haven't the faintest idea who you are. Our friend inside says the Guild is counting heads in the Dock Ward and yours aren't on the list, so for fuck's sake keep it that way."
> - **Suspicious:** "They've started asking who you are, darling. Our friend inside says a gazer's been sent to look around the Dock Ward and their people are listening for your names, so stop leaving them on things."
> - **Alert:** "They know who you are, darling. Our friend inside says they want your faces on the wall of every guardroom they own, so sleep somewhere other than your own bed, and I mean that, Fel checked."
> - **Lockdown:** "They've dropped everything else to find you, darling. Our friend inside says the Xanathar asked for your names in person, so go to ground on the *Heartbreaker*, use the berth, and don't go out alone."
>
> The second part names the first Guild response team the party has not beaten. The teams come in this order, from the Response Teams section of the **Xanathar's Guild** page in the Factions Guide:
>
> - **Gorath's team:** "Watch for Gorath's crew next, four duergar who enjoy hurting people. They're who the Guild sends first when somebody needs a lesson."
> - **Korgstrod's team:** "Next up is Korgstrod Uxgulm with three duergar, a half-ogre and a gazer, and they hit a lot harder than Gorath's lot."
> - **Noska's team:** "That leaves Noska Ur'gray with six bugbears and a gazer. They're who the Guild sends when it's done sending messages."
> - **No team left:** "They've got no crews left to send onto the street, darling. The next move is the Xanathar's own, so make sure you've got somewhere to be."
>
> A report never describes the lair, names a Guild source or says where the Xanathar is. A member who asks Krebbyg for any of those is told "our friend doesn't draw maps, darling", and nothing is spent.

### Asking Again

Nevercott closes the first arrangement by telling the member how to ask for the next report. If Krebbyg is present, he repeats the method in his own words and adds a sentence about the soprano.

> [!exploration]**Asking for a Report**
>
> Each Soldier can ask for one report every tenday, counted from the day of that member's last request. The first report is the one in tonight's playbill, so the first tenday starts today.
>
> - **Contact:** Krebbyg Masq'il'yr, who is Kreb Sorrush in public.
> - **How to ask, before Kreb Unmasked:** the member asks Kreb Sorrush at the booking-office counter for "a box at the Tuesday matinee". He writes it in his schedule and says box four is sold out, darling, loud enough for the whole lobby to hear.
> - **How to ask, after Kreb Unmasked:** the member leaves a slip reading "Tuesday" in the left pocket of the grey coat on peg seven in the cloakroom, the dead drop from **Kreb Drops the Cover**.
> - **The answer:** at dawn the day after the request, a playbill with the report in its margin is pushed under the door of the member's lodging.
> - **Notice:** none. The request can be made at any hour the theater is open.
> - **Limits:** a request made before ten days have passed is not spent. Krebbyg returns the slip or the box request the next dawn with "sold out" written across it. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the playbill under the door of their first surface lodging on return, and the tenday still counts from the day they asked.
> - **Companions:** the member may read the margin to anyone they trust. A companion who repeats it where a Guild agent or the Watch can hear counts against the member.
> - **Loss rule, careless:** if a Guild agent or the Watch finds a playbill the member left lying around, Krebbyg stops the channel for twenty days.
> - **Loss rule, warning the Guild:** if a report is used to warn the Guild that it has a source, the channel is cut for the rest of the campaign, and Krebbyg tells the member so to their face.

> [!gamemaster]**When the Source Is Lost**
>
> **The Compromised Eye** decides what happens to Nar'l and changes what a Soldier's reports carry from the day it concludes. Check the party's outcome each time the member asks for a report.
>
> | Outcome | What the member receives |
> |---|---|
> | None marked, **Nar'l Cleared** or **Nar'l Active** | Both parts, at dawn the day after the request, one request every tenday. |
> | **Nar'l Extracted** | The second part only, from Fel'rekt's watchers on the waterfront, at dawn two days after the request, one request every tenday. |
> | **Nar'l Eliminated** | The second part only, from the same watchers, at dawn two days after the request, one request every twenty days. |
>
> The watchers count Guild crews on the street and nothing else, so they can't judge the Guild's temper. When Nar'l has been extracted, Krebbyg opens the note with this line:
>
> > "Our friend inside is out of the Guild now, darling, so this is only what Fel's watchers on the waterfront can count. It's two days late, sorry."
>
> When Nar'l has been eliminated, Krebbyg writes less and opens with this line:
>
> > "You know why there's nobody inside any more. This is what Fel's watchers can count from the quay, you'll only get one every twenty days now, and I'm not going to talk about it."
>
> The member keeps the Soldier rank in every case. Under **Nar'l Eliminated**, Jarlaxle has cut the channel in half in displeasure, and no later event restores it.

### A Name for the Evening

Nevercott ends the meeting by giving the member a card. On its back he has written *the autumn program* and *Odalys Vane, pier gate, Heartbreaker*. The second arrangement exists only while the Sea Maidens Faire fleet is in the Dock Ward, from the morning of Ches 21 until the fleet sails on Tarsakh 20. In that window the member can visit Odalys Vane (Neutral, Drow in a human guise, she/her), the Faire's stage manager. She keeps a folding desk at the foot of the *Heartbreaker*'s ramp from the 6th bell of the morning to the 10th bell of the evening. She looks like a brisk human woman in her forties whenever anyone is within sight of the ship's gilded figurehead.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Faire's pier is crowded with families and off-duty sailors queuing for the shooting gallery, and the two crimson galleons tower over the stalls with their pennants cracking in the wind. At the foot of the *Heartbreaker*'s ramp, a woman in a plain grey dress sits at a folding desk with a pencil in her hair. She checks names off a ledger with a wooden ruler while a juggler hovers at her elbow.
>
> > "Go and find your clubs, Tobin, I've told you three times. Yes? Can I help you, love, or are you another one of mine who's lost his rehearsal call?"

When the member says "the autumn program", read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The woman looks up from the ledger, and the ruler stops moving. She glances at the juggler, who takes the hint and goes, and then at the card in your hand.
>
> > "Autumn program, is it? Thank fuck, I thought you were the second fiddler. Odalys Vane, stage management. Nevercott's sent word you need a name for the evening, so sit on that crate and I'll show you the hire list."

> [!social]**The Stage Manager**
>
> Odalys Vane (Neutral, Drow in a human guise, she/her) :: the Sea Maidens Faire's stage manager, who runs the hire list and treats every Soldier as a new hand who might be useful.
>
> Odalys is brisk, friendly and a little harried. She gives short instructions to the hands and long, patient explanations to anyone she thinks will get something wrong. She swears casually at jugglers and lighting hands but never at a member. She checks her ledger before every answer, and she takes the pencil out of her hair when she is about to be firm. She calls the member "love" and won't discuss anything below the main deck, the *Eyecatcher*, or the captain.
>
> Odalys is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The hire list, and which name suits which kind of person.
> - How long the name lasts and who will confirm it.
> - What the member may and may not do on the pier.

> [!qna]**What's the autumn program?**
>
> > "It's a hire we don't put in the book, love. Nevercott calls it a favor and I call it Tuesdays, because that's when the paperwork comes in. I give you a name, and any of my hands will swear to it if a constable asks."

> [!qna]**Can I go aboard the *Eyecatcher*?**
>
> Odalys takes the pencil out of her hair and points it at the harbor, where a third ship rides at anchor a quarter of a mile from the pier.
>
> > "No, and I'd say the same if you were the Open Lord herself. That one's the captain's, and there's no ramp. You go out by invitation and by skiff. Everything I give you stops at the *Heartbreaker*, the *Hellraiser* and the pier."

> [!qna]**How long do I keep the name?**
>
> > "Until the twentieth of Tarsakh, while the ships are in the harbor. Then the Faire sails and the name goes with us, so find yourself another one."

The Faire's hire list has three names open to a Soldier. Each is entered on the Faire's books with a week's wages already drawn, so any hand who is asked will confirm it. The member chooses one now.

> [!exploration]**Three Names on the Hire List**
>
> - **Pell Harrowgate, a stagehand.** The member wears work clothes and a leather apron. The pier's Watch patrols and the harbor wardens never stop Faire hands carrying crates, so the member can carry one sealed crate or chest up to four feet long through the pier gate in either direction without being asked what is in it.
> - **Tamsin Orr, a ticket-seller.** The member wears a red waistcoat and carries a cashbox. A seller can stand at the gate or in the crowd at any hour the ramps are open and talk to anyone without being remembered. The member has Advantage on the first Charisma (Deception) check made on any evening to explain why they are loitering on the pier.
> - **Corwin Alder, a fiddler in the Faire's band.** The member wears a velvet jacket and carries a fiddle case with a false bottom. The case holds one weapon no longer than a shortsword or a hand crossbow, and harbor wardens wave a band musician through without opening it.

Before the member leaves the desk, Odalys tests the name. She plays a harbor warden on the pier, and the member answers her questions in the cover for a full minute.

> [!exploration]**Trying the Name On**
>
> Odalys drops her voice into a warden's gravel and asks the member who they are, whom they work for and what they are carrying. A member who answers in the cover and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Deception or Performance) check passes, and the name is theirs from that evening.
>
> - **Failure:** Odalys stops the scene, tells the member plainly what gave them away, and coaches them for the rest of the hour. The name is theirs from the next evening, and nothing else is lost.
> - **Result of 17 or higher:** Odalys writes "good" beside the name in her ledger, and her hands treat the member as an old colleague. This has no further effect.

> [!exploration]**Using the Name**
>
> The member uses the Faire's cover support through the following arrangements. Each Soldier keeps a separate count.
>
> - **Contact:** Odalys Vane at the gate desk.
> - **Place:** the gate desk, the pier carnival, the public decks of the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser*, a costume room belowdecks on the *Heartbreaker*, and a canvas rehearsal booth behind the shooting gallery.
> - **Notice:** one hour. The member gives Odalys the phrase "the autumn program" and the name they chose. She needs the hour to ready the room and send her hands elsewhere, and she won't open it to a member who arrives without notice.
> - **The costume room:** the member can change clothes, store one bag and rest on a bench for up to two hours. It has no hammocks, and the Initiate berth is a separate benefit.
> - **The meeting booth:** the member can bring up to three guests who are not Watch officers, Xanathar's Guild agents or Cassalanter staff. Odalys turns those away at the gate when she recognizes them. A hand stands outside the canvas for the hour.
> - **How long:** one evening per visit, ending at the 10th bell. The member can use the support at most three times in each block of ten days counted from this meeting.
> - **What it is not:** access to the *Eyecatcher*, to any space belowdecks other than the costume room, or a refuge from the Watch. The cover protects a stranger on the pier and doesn't make a wanted person unwanted.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who starts a fight on the pier, knowingly brings a Watch officer aboard, or lets a non-member use the name loses the Faire's support for twenty days when Krebbyg hears of it.
> - **End:** the support ends the day the fleet sails on Tarsakh 20.

### The Pawnbroker on Dock Street

The third arrangement is a shop, and the front of Nevercott's card gives its address. Brindle and Daughter, Pawnbrokers, stands on Dock Street between a net-maker and a closed fish stall. It is open from the 9th bell of the morning until dusk, and the discount applies from the member's first visit.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The window of Brindle and Daughter holds a crowd of candlesticks, a brass sextant, a stuffed gull and a tray of mismatched cutlery. A young woman sweeps the step and glances along the street more often than she looks at the broom. Inside, a wiry man in his sixties stands behind the counter with a jeweler's loupe pushed up onto his forehead, polishing a silver ladle with a grey cloth.
>
> > "Mind the tray on your left, dear, it's all spoons and they bite. Is that a black card? Lovely, I do like a customer who comes prepared. Ostrin Brindle, and you'll be wanting the captain's price."

> [!social]**The Fence with the Ledger**
>
> Ostrin Brindle (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a Dock Ward pawnbroker in his sixties who sells the company's people what a licensed shop won't and keeps his books as neatly as a priest.
>
> Ostrin is Friendly and chatty about everything on his shelves, and he is careful to know nothing about his customers. He wipes whatever he is holding while he talks and calls everyone "dear". He swears mildly, and rarely, when a customer tries to haggle him below the rate. The company has paid him for six months, and he doesn't know where the black card comes from.
>
> Ostrin is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The goods he stocks, and what each one costs.
> - How the captain's price works and how to order.
> - The state of Dock Street trade, which he finds disgraceful.

> [!qna]**What's the captain's price?**
>
> Ostrin sets down the ladle and taps the counter once.
>
> > "A fifth off the published price on the goods in my back room, dear, and nothing off the rest. I can't put those goods in the window and a licensed shop can't sell them. People with the right card get the better rate."

> [!qna]**Why not just buy from Fel'rekt?**
>
> > "Because Fel sells you crossbows and bolts at cost and I sell you everything else, dear, and the captain's particular that the two don't mix. For a hand crossbow, see Fel. For a set of tools to open a lock, see me."

> [!qna]**How soon can I have it?**
>
> > "By dusk tomorrow, if you order today. You collect at the back door after dark and pay on pickup. I don't hold stock for people I haven't met, so please don't ask me to hurry."

> [!exploration]**The Captain's Price**
>
> A Soldier pays four-fifths of the published 2024 price for the following goods when bought through Ostrin: Thieves' Tools (25 gp, so 20 gp), a Forgery Kit (15 gp, so 12 gp), a Disguise Kit (25 gp, so 20 gp), a Poisoner's Kit (50 gp, so 40 gp) and Basic Poison (100 gp, so 80 gp). Magic items, unpriced goods and anything on Fel'rekt's stall are excluded.
>
> - **Contact:** Ostrin Brindle.
> - **Place:** Brindle and Daughter on Dock Street. The member shows the black card on the first visit and says "the captain's price" on every later one.
> - **Notice:** none, during shop hours.
> - **How to order:** the member names the goods at the counter, and Ostrin writes the order in his ledger against the member's name. A member can place one order per visit.
> - **Limit:** the combined published prices in a member's orders may not exceed 300 gp in each block of ten days counted from this meeting.
> - **Delay:** the goods are ready at dusk on the day after the order. The member collects them at the shop's back door after dark and pays on collection.
> - **Who else:** the discount is for the member's own orders. A member who buys for a companion pays the same price, and the companion gets no separate discount.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who names the shop to a non-member or brings one inside loses the discount for ten days when Krebbyg hears of it.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Soldier earns further Renown from the missions and from the rewards listed in the Earning Renown section of the **Bregan D'aerthe** page in the Factions Guide.

### Aftermath

The member may tell companions what they have learned and where the shop and the pier desk are. The report channel, the Faire name and the pawnbroker's discount stay individual benefits, and Krebbyg, Odalys and Ostrin won't serve a companion who asks in the member's name. If **Zardoz Introduced** is marked later, Nevercott stops appearing to that member, and the three arrangements continue unchanged through Krebbyg.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has read the first report and left the booking office. The Faire scene and the pawnbroker scene can run at any later time inside the benefit's window. A member who never visits either still holds the rank.
