# The Compromised Eye

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Social Event begins in the Castle Ward sewers and resolves based on which approach the party chooses. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the brief from Krebbyg at an unusual hour and ask him what he knows
> - Locate Nar'l's sewer contact point and wait for him to arrive
> - Overcome Nar'l's hostile initial attitude and persuade him to engage
> - Choose one of three resolution options, each producing a different campaign state for **Xanathar's Lair**
>
> #### Background
>
> Nar'l Xibrindas serves as Xanathar's advisor and secretly reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending* spell every three days. He has been in place for three years. His intelligence on the panopticus surveillance routes and the lair's internal layout is Jarlaxle's single most valuable source.
>
> Nar'l's cover has been degrading for two months. He learned that his brother Soluun was operating in Waterdeep under a forged Bregan D'aerthe token — unauthorized, disowned, conducting operations that were attracting Guild attention — and he has been intercepting internal reports and suppressing information before it reached the beholder. The gaps in his summary reports were inevitable.
>
> Ahmaergo flagged the gaps two months ago and has been building a case. He has not yet formalized a request for Nihiloor interrogation. If he does, the situation becomes irreversible.
>
> Krebbyg's instruction to the party before they leave: "He stays alive and in place if possible. He is more valuable there than anywhere else." This is the mission's constraint, not a suggestion.

### The Brief

Krebbyg arrives in the early afternoon — not his usual hour. He is more concise than usual.

> [!readaloud]
> Krebbyg turns up just after the midday lull, which is unusual — he normally sends a note. He has a look on his face that the theater has never quite succeeded in hiding, which is the look of someone managing a problem he didn't make.
>
> > "Nar'l Xibrindas. He's our deepest asset in Xanathar's Guild — Xanathar's own advisor, three years in. Xanathar has started asking questions about him. We don't know the full picture yet."
>
> He looks at the table.
>
> > "We think Soluun may be the cause."
>
> He says the name carefully. He watches your faces for recognition.
>
> > "Soluun is Nar'l's brother. He was disowned from Bregan D'aerthe before this operation began. If Nar'l has been covering for his brother's activity in the city, Xanathar's people may have traced it back through the gaps in Nar'l's reports."
>
> He folds his hands on the table, which is unusual for Krebbyg.
>
> > "Jarlaxle needs to know what's happening before Nihiloor gets involved. Once the mind flayer is interested in Nar'l, the situation becomes significantly worse. He stays alive and in place if possible. That's the instruction."

> [!social]**The Theatre Manager**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Elf (Drow), he/him) :: Bregan D'aerthe operative running the Seven Masks Theater's bookings under the name Kreb Sorrush.
>
> Krebbyg is concise today, which itself communicates how serious this is. He does not have Jarlaxle's full picture of what Ahmaergo's investigation looks like from inside the Guild, and he says so. He knows the contact protocol for reaching Nar'l in the Castle Ward sewers, and he provides it. He confirms that Soluun appeared in Doom Raiders M1 as an unauthorized operative in the Dock Ward — the connection between that encounter and this one is here for parties who ran both chains.
>
> Conversation topics Krebbyg is willing to discuss:
> - The contact protocol: the chalk spider mark on the east wall of the Castle Ward main sewer junction, changed every tenday; wait up to four hours
> - Soluun's connection to the situation, which he describes factually and without judgment
> - The instruction to keep Nar'l alive and in place, which he repeats if pressed

> [!qna]**What exactly is Soluun doing?**
>
> > "Dock Ward. Operating under a forged token — Bregan D'aerthe, but not authorized. We don't know what job he's running. We know it's attracted attention. If Nar'l's been cleaning up after him, Ahmaergo will have the thread."

> [!qna]**What does Nar'l know about us?**
>
> > "He knows someone Jarlaxle trusts is coming to the contact point. He doesn't know who. He's going to be hostile. That's expected — it's a controlled location and he's under surveillance pressure. Get him talking. Use the code phrase." He slides a folded strip of paper across. "Don't read it here."

> [!qna]**What are our options?**
>
> > "Three, more or less. Cover the gaps with a false report, get him out entirely, or discredit whoever Ahmaergo is using as a source. Jarlaxle prefers he stays in place if there's a clean way to do it. If there isn't, alive and out is second choice." He pauses. "Dead is not on the list."

Proceed to **The Castle Ward Sewers**.

### The Castle Ward Sewers

Nar'l's contact protocol: a small stylized spider in chalk on the east wall of the Castle Ward main sewer junction. Characters who reach the junction and make the mark wait. Within four hours, Nar'l arrives.

> [!readaloud]
> The Castle Ward sewers smell of old stone and moving water, and the only light is what you brought. The main junction is a wide oval chamber where three channels meet, the current slow and steady. The east wall is marked with old chalk signs from other hands — work schedules, maintenance notes, initials — and you add the spider to the collection.
>
> The four-hour wait ends without warning. The air beside the east wall folds once, and a cloaked figure steps through from nowhere, moving immediately to press against the stone. He is compact and older than you would expect for a drow, with a stillness about him — his hands, his shoulders, even the set of his hood — that looks like something practised over years rather than natural calm. He takes in the chalk mark on the wall, and then he takes in you, and he does not move.

> [!social]**The Double Agent**
>
> Nar'l Xibrindas (Neutral Evil, Elf (Drow), he/him) :: Xanathar's personal advisor and Jarlaxle's most deeply embedded intelligence source, three years under cover.
>
> Nar'l is hostile on first contact, and he has good operational reasons for it. Strangers at his contact point could be an Ahmaergo probe, and his default assumption is that any new contact is a test. He is managing fear with every professional tool he has, and the result looks like controlled stillness. His hands do not shake.
>
> A DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check, or presenting the code phrase Krebbyg provides, shifts Nar'l to Indifferent within three exchanges. On a failure, he does not leave — but he does not engage either. Additional attempts after the first require a different approach: a different party member speaking, a different opening, or a disclosure of specific information that only Jarlaxle's people would hold.
>
> Once he is willing to talk, he says:
>
> > "I know why Xanathar is suspicious. I have been covering for Soluun."
>
> He does not apologize. He does not explain his reasoning.
>
> Conversation topics Nar'l is willing to discuss once cooperative:
> - What he has been covering for, stated in precise and minimal terms
> - Ahmaergo's investigation, which he describes with the professional care of someone who has been watching it develop
> - The three options for resolving the situation, which he will analyze with the detachment of someone whose life depends on the analysis

> [!qna]**Why did you cover for Soluun?**
>
> His voice is even.
>
> > "He is my brother."
>
> He does not elaborate. He understands that this is not a satisfying answer operationally. He offers it as the only answer he has.

> [!qna]**How much does Ahmaergo know?**
>
> He glances toward the junction's exits before answering.
>
> > "He knows there are gaps. He does not know why. He has a Watch informant feeding him a thread — low-level, Dock Ward, activity near the harbor. Soluun's activity." A pause. "He has not yet taken it to the Master. When he does, Nihiloor will be the next step. I would prefer not to find out what Nihiloor considers a polite interview."

> [!qna]**Do you want to get out?**
>
> > "What I want is irrelevant. What I can survive is the question. If extraction means losing three years of position, Jarlaxle loses his best window into the lair. If staying means Nihiloor, the loss is total. Tell me what you are proposing, and I will tell you whether it is viable."

Proceed to **The Options**.

### The Options

Three approaches are viable. The party chooses one. Nar'l will execute whichever option the party commits to, and he will not change direction once work begins.

> [!exploration]**Option A — The False Report**
>
> Nar'l drafts a misleading incident report for Ahmaergo explaining the intelligence gaps as deliberate misdirection of a Watch informant who was operating too close to Guild interests. The report requires one piece of corroborating external evidence to hold up: a Watch broadsheet entry, a guild record, or a statement from a named contact that supports the narrative.
>
> Assembling the corroborating material requires a DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) or Charisma (Deception) check. On a success, Nar'l submits the report and Ahmaergo is satisfied — the investigation closes. On a failure, the report is submitted but Ahmaergo remains suspicious; the party has bought two tendays at most, and Nar'l's standing is formally under review.
>
> Nar'l remains in place. His intelligence channel to Jarlaxle is intact.

> [!exploration]**Option B — Extraction**
>
> Get Nar'l out of Xanathar's lair before anyone notices he is gone. He returns to collect what he can carry — one hour — then transits the Castle Ward tunnels to a pre-agreed exit. Two **Spies** guard the main junction; they are aware of unusual activity in the sewer system.
>
> A group DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) check is required to move with Nar'l past their patrol route. On a failure, one sentry investigates toward the sound; on a second failure, both are alerted and attack.
>
> Extraction removes Nar'l from danger permanently. His value as an inside source is gone. He is alive, safe, and available in **Xanathar's Lair** and **Kolat Towers** as a guide who knows the lair layout from three years of personal observation, including Nihiloor's patrol patterns and the eastern access routes.

> [!exploration]**Option C — Discredit Ahmaergo's Source**
>
> The party inserts someone into Xanathar's Guild's outer organization — via **Faction Outposts** contacts or a DC 16 Charisma (Deception) insertion — and discredits the Watch informant thread before Ahmaergo can formalize the investigation.
>
> Discrediting the thread requires three independent steps: identifying which informant Ahmaergo is using (DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)), constructing a counter-narrative that exposes that informant as unreliable (DC 14 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion)), and delivering it through Guild channels without the delivery itself appearing suspicious (DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) group check).
>
> On full success: Xanathar's investigation closes, Nar'l returns to full standing, and Jarlaxle regains a clear intelligence channel. On partial success (one failed check): the investigation closes, but Ahmaergo records the anomaly — a second attempt on Nar'l is more likely in the future. On failure: the insertion is detected and the party must extract before the outer organization identifies them.

> [!gamemaster]**If the Party Kills Nar'l**
>
> Killing Nar'l is an action against the mission brief. Krebbyg is quiet when told. He says: "That was not the instruction." The **Nar'l Eliminated** outcome is marked. Jarlaxle does not arrange the private dinner. The mission technically completes — Xanathar's investigation of Nar'l ends because Nar'l is gone — but Jarlaxle's assessment of the party drops significantly, and this affects both his posture in **Sea Maidens Faire** and the intelligence available in **Xanathar's Lair**.

### Concluding the Event

The resolution option determines Nar'l's status for all downstream quests. Mark the appropriate outcome below and read only the matching consequence.

If the **Nar'l Active** outcome is marked, Jarlaxle acknowledges the mission with a private dinner at Seven Masks — in person, as Zardoz Zord, for the first time since the party became operationally trusted. See the [**Dinner with Zardoz**](../s03-dinner-with-zardoz/ev-01-dinner-with-zardoz.md) Standalone Event.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Nar'l Active** — mark when Nar'l is alive and his cover is intact by any method (Options A or C on success); read by **Xanathar's Lair** (Nar'l is still in place and can be leveraged as an intelligence source during the heist operation), **Sea Maidens Faire** (Jarlaxle's posture toward the party reflects a successful mission), and the renown award below.
> - **Nar'l Extracted** — mark when Nar'l is extracted alive and brought safely out of the sewer system (Option B); read by **Xanathar's Lair** and **Kolat Towers** (Nar'l is available as a direct information source and lair guide; he knows Nihiloor's patrol patterns and the eastern access routes from three years of personal observation) and by the supplementary renown award.
> - **Nar'l Eliminated** — mark when Nar'l is killed or permanently removed from play; read by **Xanathar's Lair** (entry paths that rely on insider knowledge are unavailable) and **Sea Maidens Faire** (Jarlaxle's posture toward the party is noticeably cooler, and the private dinner does not fire).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Award +2 Renown if Nar'l is alive and Xanathar's immediate investigation is closed by any method. Award an additional +1 Renown (supplementary) if Nar'l is extracted alive via Option B. Award no renown if Nar'l is killed.
>
> If **Nar'l Active** is marked, proceed to the [**Dinner with Zardoz**](../s03-dinner-with-zardoz/ev-01-dinner-with-zardoz.md) Standalone Event. If **Nar'l Eliminated** is marked, the dinner does not fire and Mission 5 proceeds directly.

## Overview

Jarlaxle's deepest asset inside Xanathar's Guild is compromised. The party finds him in the sewers and decides whether to cover for him, extract him, or eliminate the threat another way.

## Summary

The party reached Nar'l Xibrindas through his Castle Ward sewer contact protocol and convinced him to engage despite his initial hostility. He confirmed that he had been covering for his brother Soluun's unauthorized presence in Waterdeep. The party chose a resolution — false report, extraction, or source discrediting — and Jarlaxle acknowledged the mission with a private dinner at Seven Masks, in person, as Zardoz Zord.
