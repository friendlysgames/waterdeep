# Twelve Minutes

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Combat Event occurs when the party has heard Nar'l Xibrindas's trade in **The Compromised Eye** and chooses what to do with him. It ends when each member reports to Krebbyg and receives the captain's thanks. Every beat inside the lair costs minutes from the twelve on the clock, and in this Event the party can:
>
> - Discredit the case against Nar'l so that he stays in place, by doctoring his reports, handing him a gift from the company and buying off the fence who told Ahmaergo's clerks about a drow in a Guild coat.
> - Extract Nar'l by packing what must not be left behind and getting him to the X1 door before the grell and the Guild's response catch them.
> - Eliminate him, by arguing against his last offer and then facing a mage who fights only when there is nothing else to do.
> - Meet the grell and the Guild's response if the clock runs out, or meet the four drow at the head of the stair with an order they have not mentioned.
> - Report to Krebbyg in the booking office behind the Seven Masks stage door, and receive the gold statuette the next morning.
>
> Only Bregan D'aerthe members attend the debrief and receive Renown. Companions can help with everything else, and each companion who went down the stair receives a statuette.

> [!gamemaster]**The Minute Ledger**
>
> The party spends minutes from the twelve that **The Compromised Eye** started. Anyone still in the office or the corridor at the twelfth minute meets the grell. Minutes already spent stay spent if the party changes its mind, and the party can mix beats from different routes as long as it pays for each.
>
> - **Discredit route:** doctor the reports, 4 minutes. Hand over the packet, 2 minutes. The walk to X1, 3 minutes.
> - **Extraction route:** pack the spellbook and bag, 2 minutes. Take the false drawer, 1 minute. Burn the cipher log, 1 minute. The walk to X1 with Nar'l, 4 minutes, or 3 if someone steadies him.
> - **Elimination route:** the fight lasts at most 1 minute. Clearing the desk costs the minutes listed in **The Desk and the Shelf**. The walk to X1, 3 minutes.
>
> The opening exchange cost 2 minutes, and each question the party asked cost 1. A party with minutes to spare can spend them on questions or on the desk.
>
> If Nar'l is dead or missing at the twelfth minute, the grell raises the alarm, and the Guild's response reaches the X1 door at the sixteenth minute. A character who is not through the door and has not barred it by then fights the response at the door.

### Clear His Name

The party leaves Nar'l at his desk and goes after the case. The work runs in two places, with six minutes of forgery and a gift in the office and a conversation with a fence the next morning. **Nar'l Cleared** needs both halves to go well, and **Nar'l Active** is what remains when one of them does not.

> [!exploration]**Doctoring the Reports**
>
> The three unsent Dock Ward reports describe three nights on the quay, and each says that quay watchers saw a slight figure with a hand crossbow whom they took for a drow. The party rewrites them so that Nar'l can file them with a believable excuse for the delay, and the new account blames a human. Nar'l checks every line against the Guild's own style. The character who composes the story needs 4 minutes.
>
> Any character who writes the new account and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check or DC 14 Charisma (Deception) check makes the reports hold together. Other characters can give the Help action.
>
> - **Soluun Killed is marked:** The character has Advantage on the check, because the Guild's watchers know the killings stopped on a particular night.
> - **Neither Soluun outcome is marked:** The character has Disadvantage on the check, because the killer is still on the quay and the Guild may see him again.
> - **Result of 19 or higher:** Nar'l also adds a line on the Guild's own rivals that will make Ahmaergo's clerks glad to believe the story.
>
> If the check fails by 4 or less, two reports hold and one has a gap that Nar'l says he cannot explain. The case stays open, and the discredit route cannot reach **Nar'l Cleared**. If it fails by 5 or more, Nar'l refuses to put his name on the reports, and the discredit route ends in **Nar'l Active** at best.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nar'l reads the three revised sheets slowly with the stylus held in his fist and turns the second one over to check the back. At the end he squares the pages on the blotter and lets out a long breath.
>
> > These will do, and I'm obliged to you. The clerks will want to believe a human, because a human is easier to explain to the Master than a drow. I'd like to add the other thing before the grell returns, if you have it.

The packet is the other thing. Nar'l takes it from whichever member holds it, breaks the silver seal and reads the four sheets while the party watches. This costs 2 minutes.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nar'l reads each of the four sheets twice before he sets it down. He looks at the name at the top of the first and the street at the bottom of the last, and a muscle moves in his jaw.
>
> > Dorrim Ketch, of the Black Network, who works the quay at night and wears a long grey coat. Your principal has handed me a real man to hang this on, and I understand what it cost him to do it. I'll copy it into a fourth report in my own hand and call it the result of my own inquiries.

When the party has spent its minutes in the office, it leaves. The walk to X1 costs 3 minutes, and the party is out of the lair as soon as it is through the slab. Nar'l stays at his desk and goes on with his Guild work, and he does not say goodbye.

The second half of the work happens the next morning in the Dock Ward, where Tarn Hobble keeps a pawnshop on Net and Dock Street. He is the second leg of Ahmaergo's case, and the party has until the clerk comes back at noon to settle him.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The pawnshop on Net and Dock Street is a narrow shopfront with a window full of mismatched boots and a brass scale on the counter. A thin human with ink on his cuffs is weighing a silver spoon against a handful of small copper weights, and he clears his throat and looks up at you over the scale.
>
> > If you're buying, say what, and if you're selling, say how much. If you're asking about what I told Ahmaergo's clerk, you're two days late and about forty gold short.

> [!social]**The Fence Who Sells Both Sides**
>
> Tarn Hobble (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: a Dock Ward fence who sold the Guild's clerks a sighting and would sell them the opposite for a better price.
>
> Tarn is quick, dry and tired, and he says "fuck" casually and without malice. He sells what he sees to whoever asks and has no loyalty to the Guild or to anyone who threatens him. He talks fast when he is nervous and slowly when he is lying, and he coughs in between. A character who offers money and a story he can believe finds him easy. A character who threatens him finds the door open behind him.
>
> Conversation topics Tarn is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What he saw on the quay, and on which nights.
> - What the clerk paid and what the clerk wanted.
> - What he would say to a different buyer for a better price.

> [!qna]**What did you see?**
>
> > A figure in a long coat with a Guild badge on the shoulder, slight and quick, and I took him for a drow from how he moved. I saw him twice, on the quay after midnight, and both times a body turned up on the stones by morning. I told the clerk what I saw, and I got paid for it.

> [!qna]**Who's the clerk?**
>
> > One of Ahmaergo's, a small man with ink on his thumbs who never gives a name and pays on the nail. He comes back at noon every day to ask if I've remembered anything else. I'm very good at remembering things, depending on what comes with them.

> [!qna]**Would you say something different?**
>
> Tarn coughs, and glances at the scale and then at your purse.
>
> > Depends who's asking and what they're offering. I'd sooner say I was drunk and saw a big man in a grey coat than keep telling the story that gets me looked at by something with tentacles. Make it worth more than the clerk's price.

> [!exploration]**Settling Tarn Hobble**
>
> The party has three ways to settle the fence, and a character who tries one can try another if the first fails.
>
> - **Outbid the clerk.** A character who offers 150 gp and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check persuades Tarn to tell the clerk that he was drunk and that the figure was a big man in a long grey coat. A character who describes Ketch's coat from the packet has Advantage. On a failure, Tarn pockets the money and keeps his story, and the 150 gp is gone.
> - **Frighten him.** A character who makes a successful DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation) check sends Tarn out of the city before noon. The clerk comes back to an empty shop and a missing informant, which draws Ahmaergo's interest. The case thins and does not close.
> - **Leave him alone.** The clerk visits at noon and Tarn tells the same story as before, and nothing the party did in the office contradicts it.
>
> Mark the result for the table: **Fence Settled** if Tarn was outbid, **Fence Gone** if he was frightened, **Fence Untouched** otherwise.

Three days after the party's visit, the clerk's report reaches Ahmaergo's desk, and the file is either closed or it is not.

> [!gamemaster]**What the File Does**
>
> Read the first line that fits.
>
> - **The reports held and Tarn was outbid:** The file closes. Mark **Nar'l Cleared**. Nar'l gets word to the company through the usual channel on the fourth day, and the grell stays at his shoulder with an order that has nothing left to act on.
> - **The reports held and Tarn was frightened away:** The file thins and does not close, because a missing witness is a fact too. Mark **Nar'l Active**.
> - **The reports held and Tarn was left alone:** The clerk's account still carries a drow in a Guild coat, and the reports contradict it. Mark **Nar'l Active**.
> - **The reports had a gap or Nar'l refused them:** Mark **Nar'l Active**. Nar'l has bought himself two weeks.

### Take Him Out

The party takes Nar'l with it. He hands over whatever the party asks for, and the party decides what to carry and what to leave. The grell's return is the only deadline, and every minute spent packing is a minute not spent on the walk.

> [!exploration]**What Nar'l Takes**
>
> Nar'l walks to the office door, stops there and asks the party to wait while he collects three things. Each costs the minutes shown.
>
> - **The spellbook and the bag (2 minutes).** Nar'l opens both drawers with the black key and takes the spellbook himself. He hands the captain's *bag of holding* to whichever member holds the letter.
> - **The false drawer (1 minute).** The *Report on the Seven Masks Theater* comes out of the drawer, and Nar'l watches the member who takes it. If the party leaves it, Ahmaergo's clerks find it by evening, and the Guild learns that the company owns the theater.
> - **The cipher log (1 minute).** A character who burns the log in the lamp keeps the Guild from learning anything from it. If the party leaves it, the Guild reads the names of the company's contacts in Waterdeep.
>
> If either the report or the log is left behind, **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) treats the Guild as one tier higher toward Bregan D'aerthe. The grell finds an empty chair at the twelfth minute whatever the party took.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nar'l lifts the spellbook out of the drawer with both hands and wraps it in its webbing before he tucks it into his coat. He slides the black leather bag across the desk to you, then picks up the lamp, looks at it for a moment and sets it down again.
>
> > I'd like to be out of this room by the time the bell rings again, and I'd prefer not to meet the grell in the corridor. I can walk quickly, though I haven't walked this corridor with anyone beside me in a year, and I'm not at all certain how I'll manage it.

The walk to X1 takes 4 minutes with Nar'l, because he is no runner and does not care to be carried. The corridor stays empty until the twelfth minute.

> [!exploration]**The Walk**
>
> A character who walks beside Nar'l and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that his hands have begun to shake and that he is counting his steps under his breath. The character can steady him with a word. A group that does so takes 3 minutes for the walk and reaches X1 before the grell turns back.
>
> If nobody steadies him, the walk takes 4 minutes, and the ledger above applies.

If the party is still in the office or the corridor at the twelfth minute, or has not barred the door at X1 by the sixteenth, read the clock-out wave in **When the Grell Returns**. Otherwise the party goes through the slab and bars it.

> [!exploration]**Barring the Slab**
>
> The slab at X1 swings inward on a counterweight and has an iron bar that rests in brackets on the passage side. Any character who sets the bar and makes a successful DC 14 Strength (Athletics) check or DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check jams it so that it holds for an hour. The grell cannot lift the bar, and Xanathar's people do not know the passage, so they spend the hour finding tools.
>
> The pursuit ends when the party and Nar'l are through the slab and the bar is set, or when the Guild's response has fled or fallen.

At the head of the stair, the four drow wait with the lantern shuttered. Arannis watches the party climb with Nar'l between them and steps forward when it reaches the grate.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Arannis stands at the head of the stair with his hand crossbow lowered, and Beldar, Rylvar and Draknafein have spread along the ledge behind him. He looks at Nar'l for a long moment, then at the bag, and holds out his free hand for it.
>
> > I'll take the captain's bag, thank you. I was told to ask you for the drow as well, so that he can be seen to by the people who know how. I'd rather not follow that order with you standing there, but I wanted you to hear me say so first.

> [!social]**The Man at the Top of the Stair**
>
> Arannis Nur'zekk (Neutral Evil, Drow, he/him) :: the spokesman of the four drow at the stair, who has been told to kill Nar'l if he comes up alive and would sooner not.
>
> Arannis does not enjoy the order and does not argue with it unless he is given a reason. He keeps his crossbow lowered and his hands where the party can see them. The other three look to him and do nothing until he moves. A member who holds the captain's letter outranks the order, and Arannis says so himself when it is shown to him.
>
> Conversation topics Arannis is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What his order was, and who gave it.
> - Why the captain gave two orders that disagree.
> - The bag, which he takes with some relief.

> [!qna]**Who gave you the order?**
>
> > The captain gave it to me and to the other three the evening before the letter went out. He wasn't sure the person he was sending could be trusted with a third request, and he wanted a second pair of hands standing by. He was being careful, which he usually is.

> [!qna]**Which order wins?**
>
> > The letter does, because it's the later one and it's in his own hand. If you show it to me, I'll stand down and tell the others to. If you've lost it, say a few kind words to me instead, and I'll listen.

If the member shows Arannis the letter or the black card with the silver ship, he stands down at once. A member who has neither needs a successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check, and Arannis stands down on a success. On a failure he keeps the crossbow raised for a long moment, then lowers it and tells the member that the captain will hear that the member argued. That member does not earn the +1 Renown for returning the bag.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Arannis reads the letter by the light of the shuttered lantern with his lips moving slightly, and he reads the third request twice. When he folds it again, his shoulders come down and he hands it back to you with a short nod to the others.
>
> > That's his hand, and that's the later order. Stand down, all of you. I'll tell him you walked up that stair with the man still breathing, and he can make of it what he likes.

Fel'rekt waits in the street above with a covered cart. He helps Nar'l under a tarpaulin, shakes everyone's hand in turn and drives the cart to the Faire's pier in the Dock Ward. He puts Nar'l aboard the *Heartbreaker*, and Nar'l says only that he is grateful.

### Silence Him

The party refuses the trade and acts on the captain's third request. Nar'l keeps bargaining and does not fight until he has no other choice.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nar'l watches your hands, and his own do not move from the blotter. His soft voice has gone thin and quick, and for the first time since you came in he does not glance at the door.
>
> > Wait. Wait. I'm more useful alive, to you and to anyone else, and I'd say your principal knows it, which is why he put the first two requests before the third. I'm offering you a way into this lair that nobody else can offer, and I'd like to live long enough to see you use it. Let's be sensible, please.

The party can still change its mind here, and it can stop at any moment by lowering a weapon. If the party presses on, he fights, runs or surrenders as the sequence below describes.

> [!hazard]**The Advisor Cornered**
>
> Nar'l uses the **Mage** stat block and wants the room to stay quiet. The corridor outside is empty until the twelfth minute, and he has no allies in the lair that he can call without raising the alarm. The grell is out of the room, and the fight lasts at most 1 minute.
>
> #### Nar'l's Tactics
> At the start of combat, Nar'l casts *Shield* when the first blow lands and uses *Misty Step* to leave any character adjacent to him.
>
> During combat, Nar'l:
> - Keeps the desk between himself and the party and casts *Invisibility* to break line of sight when he can.
> - Uses *Misty Step* to cross the room when he is grappled or cornered, and *Fireball* only if three or more characters stand together.
> - Throws eyescratch from his vial at any character who has him pinned, which forces a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. A creature that fails is Poisoned for 1 hour and Blinded while Poisoned, and *lesser restoration* ends the effect. Tell the table about the vial before the fight starts.
> - Surrenders when he has 40 Hit Points or fewer, or when his *Misty Step* and *Invisibility* are spent.
>
> The battle ends when Nar'l falls or surrenders. If he reaches the door, he stops there, because the corridor beyond belongs to the grell, and he turns back with his hands raised. If he surrenders, the party decides on the spot whether to finish him, to take him alive and switch to **Take Him Out**, or to leave him in place and switch to **Clear His Name**. The minutes already spent do not come back.
>
> #### Rosters
> Use the row that matches the number of participating combatants. Use the Hard row if the Guild is at Alert or Lockdown or the party attacked first.
>
> - **Three combatants, Standard:** Nar'l at two-thirds of his hit points. **Hard:** Nar'l at full hit points.
> - **Four combatants, Standard:** Nar'l alone. **Hard:** Nar'l and one **Bugbear Warrior**, a Guild runner who knocks with a message and walks into the fight.
> - **Five combatants, Standard:** Nar'l and one **Tough**, a Guild runner who knocks with a message and walks into the fight. **Hard:** Nar'l, one **Bugbear Warrior** and two **Tough**.
>
> If Nar'l is cornered with no other choice, he is at his most dangerous, and three Arcane Bursts a turn can drop a character on the first round. In that case run the Standard row for three combatants whatever the party's size, with Nar'l at two-thirds of his hit points and no messengers.

If the party kills Nar'l, it has the rest of the clock to clear the desk and leave.

> [!exploration]**What He Leaves Behind**
>
> A body in the office raises the alarm at the twelfth minute, and the party has until then to take what it wants. **The Desk and the Shelf** lists each find and its cost. The party can also take Nar'l's spellbook, which holds his prepared spells and *Sending*, and sell or keep it.
>
> - **The bag of holding (1 minute).** The captain's bag, found as listed.
> - **The false drawer (1 minute).** The *Report on the Seven Masks Theater*, which **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) counts as a lost clue path to **Sea Maidens Faire** if the party takes it.
> - **The cipher log (1 minute).** Burn or take it.
> - **Anything left behind.** If the party leaves the report or the log, the Guild reads it by evening, and **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) treats the Guild as one tier higher toward Bregan D'aerthe.
>
> The party leaves by the X1 door after its minutes, and the grell finds the body at the twelfth minute.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nar'l lies where he fell, and the stylus has rolled across the blotter and stopped against the lamp. Somewhere beyond the wall a brass bell rings a single stroke, and the corridor outside fills with a slow, soft sound like wet cloth dragged over stone.

### When the Grell Returns

The grell returns at the twelfth minute. If anyone is in the office or the corridor, the Guild's response arrives with it. If the office and corridor are empty and Nar'l is dead or missing, the alarm sounds and the response reaches the X1 door at the sixteenth minute.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A purple shape glides into the corridor with its tentacles trailing along the floor and stops when it sees you, its beak opening and closing. Behind it a brass bell begins to clang along the walls, and boots thunder on stone from the direction of the arena. A woman in a studded coat shouts for the corridor to be sealed, and a man with a spiked club shouts that the drow is dead.

> [!hazard]**The Guild Responds**
>
> The **Grell** arrives first, with a **Bandit Captain** and a squad of Guild muscle a round or two behind it. Nar'l, if he is with the party, does not fight. If the Guild is at Alert or Lockdown, or the party made noise, use the Hard row. If **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) ran before this mission and the grell died there, remove it from the roster.
>
> #### Rosters
> Use the row that matches the number of participating combatants.
>
> - **Three combatants, Standard:** one **Grell**, one **Bugbear Warrior** and one **Tough**. **Hard:** one **Grell**, one **Bandit Captain** and one **Bugbear Warrior**.
> - **Four combatants, Standard:** one **Grell**, one **Bandit Captain** and one **Bugbear Warrior**. **Hard:** one **Grell**, one **Bandit Captain** and two **Bugbear Warriors**.
> - **Five combatants, Standard:** one **Grell**, one **Bandit Captain** and two **Bugbear Warriors**. **Hard:** one **Grell**, one **Bandit Captain** and three **Bugbear Warriors**.
>
> #### The Grell's Tactics
> At the start of combat, the grell goes straight for Nar'l if it can see him, and otherwise for the character nearest the door.
>
> During combat, the grell:
> - Uses its Paralyzing Tentacles on the nearest creature that is not Nar'l, and its Beak on anyone it has Grappled.
> - Does not retreat. It will not stop until Nar'l is dead or it is destroyed.
>
> #### The Response's Tactics
> The Bandit Captain calls on the party to hand over the drow and lets the Bugbear Warriors close first. The Tough fires from behind the bugbears.
>
> During combat, the Guild squad:
> - Targets whoever is closest to Nar'l, and then whoever is Paralyzed.
> - Fights until the Bandit Captain falls, then flees back toward the arena.
>
> The fight ends when the party and Nar'l are through the slab at X1 and the bar is set, when the grell is destroyed and the squad has fled, or when the party surrenders Nar'l. A party that surrenders Nar'l has marked **Nar'l Eliminated**, because the grell kills him in front of it.
>
> #### Dramatic Moments
> At the start of each round after the first, narrate the next unused Dramatic Moment in order.
>
> - The corridor lights gutter as a draught from the arena pulls through, and a third voice shouts that the alarm is general.
> - Nar'l, if he is with the party, casts *Shield* on himself and says in a high, flat voice, "I'd like it noted that I'm still being useful."
> - The Bandit Captain calls for the party to give up the drow and walk away, and a bugbear laughs at the far end of the corridor.

### Reporting to Krebbyg

The party reports to Krebbyg in the booking office behind the Seven Masks stage door on the day after it leaves the lair, or on the day after the file closes if the party took **Clear His Name**. Companions wait in the alley outside. Krebbyg meets each member as himself, a young drow, because **Kreb Unmasked** was marked in **Kreb Drops the Cover**.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The booking office behind the stage door is a narrow room papered from floor to ceiling with old playbills. A desk buried under schedules stands in the middle, and a stuffed raven in a paper crown sits on top of a cabinet. Krebbyg perches on the edge of the desk with his boots on a chair and a half-eaten pear in one hand, and he waves it at the door as you come in.
>
> > Darling, there you are, shut the door, no, lock it, the ghost listens at that one. I've had the captain's notes since dawn and I've read them twice, and I'd like to hear the rest from you, because nobody ever tells me anything.

Read the case that matches the outcome the party marked.

If **Nar'l Cleared** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Krebbyg points the pear at you and grins.
>
> > Nar'l's file is closed, the clerk filed it away this morning, and he got word out through the usual channel an hour ago. The captain's been humming all day, which is never good for the rest of us. Fuck me, you actually did it, and nobody had to die.

If **Nar'l Active** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Krebbyg lowers the pear and rubs the back of his neck.
>
> > He's still in the chair, which is what the captain wanted, and the grell's still at his shoulder, which isn't. The file's still open in somebody's drawer, so Nar'l's bought himself a fortnight, maybe two. You can tell the captain it's "good" or "fuck" depending on who asks.

If **Nar'l Extracted** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Krebbyg puts the pear down on the desk, which you haven't seen him do before.
>
> > Fel's got him in the hold of the Heartbreaker, darling, and he hasn't stopped apologizing, which Fel says is how you know he's frightened. The captain says the second thing was what he wanted if the first couldn't be done, and you did it better than anyone could have asked. The Guild's going to be looking for him, though, so don't go walking in that part of the sewer for a while.

If **Nar'l Eliminated** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Krebbyg looks at the pear in his hand for a while, then sets it down on a schedule he doesn't seem to be reading.
>
> > Right. Well. Fuck. The captain read your account twice and said "thank you," and then he went out on deck and stayed there until dark, and I've never seen him do that. It was the third thing on the list, so you did what he asked, but I'd rather you didn't tell me about it.

> [!social]**The Stage Manager at His Desk**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: Fel'rekt's closest friend and the company's runner of errands, rash and cheerful and always late for something, who has nobody else to talk to about this.
>
> Krebbyg talks in run-on sentences that jump from the party to the ghost in his theater to a soprano who has not arrived, and he swears constantly without malice. He checks a schedule while he talks and does not read it. He says "the captain" and nothing else, and he answers a hard question with "Ask Fel." He is relieved if the party did well and miserable if it did not, and he hides neither.
>
> Conversation topics Krebbyg is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What happened to Nar'l, which he asks to hear in full.
> - What the captain thinks, which he knows only in outline.
> - Soluun, whom he mentions only if asked.
> - The statuette, which he says is the captain's way of being polite.

> [!qna]**What does the captain think?**
>
> > He says "well done," and he means it, and he'll put it in writing tomorrow because he likes writing things down. That's all I get, darling, I'm the one he sends the letters through. You'll get a statuette and a note, and he'll want to see you soon, but ask Fel what that means.

If **Soluun Expelled** is marked, read or paraphrase the following when a character asks about Soluun:

> [!qna]**About Soluun?**
>
> > He's gone, darling, put off the boat by the captain himself, for real this time, and it was overdue. Nar'l's the one who paid for it, and I don't think anybody told him until the stair. It isn't a story any more, so don't go saying it is.

If **Soluun Killed** is marked, read or paraphrase the following when a character asks about Soluun:

> [!qna]**About Soluun?**
>
> > Dead, darling, and I don't want to talk about who did it, because I'm being good about it and I'd like to stay good. Nar'l's going to hear it from somebody, and I'd rather it were somebody who liked him. The captain's not saying much, so be careful.

If neither is marked, read or paraphrase the following when a character asks about Soluun:
