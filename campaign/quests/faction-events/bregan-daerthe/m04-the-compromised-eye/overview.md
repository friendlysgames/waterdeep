# The Compromised Eye: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Available when an individual Bregan D'aerthe member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level after **Three Nights**. Companions can come down the stair and into the lair, and they can help with everything except the letter and the debrief.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 5th-level characters.*
>
> Nar'l Xibrindas uses the **Mage** stat block and wants to talk, and his grell bodyguard uses the **Grell**. The **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** sets the Guild's response for three, four and five combatants, and every route is built so that the party can finish without a fight if it watches the clock.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Bregan D'aerthe members who report to Krebbyg.

## Hook

At 08:00 a flying snake finds each eligible member and drops a letter on black paper written in silver ink. J.B. Nevercott has lost contact with Nar'l Xibrindas, the company's man in Xanathar's court, and he asks the member to keep him in place if that can be done, to bring him out if it cannot, and to see that he can never talk if neither is possible.

## Background

Nar'l has served as Xanathar's drow advisor for a year and has reported to Bregan D'aerthe by *Sending* every third day. Xanathar has become suspicious and gave him a grell bodyguard with orders to kill him at the first sign of disloyalty, and the grell keeps Nar'l from sending or receiving anything unobserved. His elder brother Soluun is the reason the Guild has a case against him. The Dock Ward killings were Soluun's work, Nar'l buried the reports that would have pointed at a drow, and Ahmaergo's clerks noticed the gap.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> J.B. Nevercott is Jarlaxle Baenre in a *hat of disguise*, and "the captain" is the same man aboard his ships as Zardoz Zord. Until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for a member, every Bregan D'aerthe speaker in this mission says "the captain" or "J.", and nobody names him aloud. Nar'l never names him either, and says "your principal" or "the man who pays you". No speaker in this mission names Manshoon, and nobody here has a reason to.
>
> The grell is the whole problem, because it makes Nar'l useless to the company and keeps him alive only as long as he is careful. The case Ahmaergo has built is real but thin. It rests on three unsent Dock Ward reports and on a fence named Tarn Hobble, who told the clerks that a drow in a Guild coat was seen near the quay on two of the nights the elves died. If the case is discredited, Xanathar loses his only reason to act, and the grell's order goes on waiting for a sign that never comes.
>
> **Soluun Expelled** (set by **The Killer's Fate**) and **Soluun Killed** (set by **The Dockside Killer**, a Doom Raiders mission) change how everyone speaks about him. If Soluun was expelled, the disownment is true now and nobody calls it a cover story. If he was killed, Nar'l grieves and is angry, and he shows both when his brother's name comes up. If neither is marked, the disownment is still a cover story that only members of the company know, and Krebbyg says so at the debrief.

## The Brief

Nevercott does not meet the member for this mission. His letter gives the three-tier order, a map to the secret staircase in the Castle Ward sewer, a sealed packet for Nar'l alone and the hour at which the grell leaves its post.

## The Staircase and the Trail

Four drow wait at the top of the staircase with orders to help the party whatever it costs, and they carry one further order that they do not mention. Below them a trail of faded red-eye guildsigns leads through the sewers to the secret door at area X1, and a failed check on the trail costs thirty minutes.

## The Meeting at X35

Nar'l's office is a short walk from X1 along a corridor he has cleared. The grell leaves for twelve minutes when the watch changes, and Nar'l opens with a trade: the location of Xanathar's Eye in Sylgar's bowl at X19 and a way past the Panopticus through X18, in exchange for his life. The party reads him with Insight and decides which of three routes to take, spending minutes from the clock as it talks.

## The Three Routes

The party can discredit the case so that he stays in place, extract him under pursuit or eliminate him. Each route has several beats, a minute cost for every beat inside the office and a way to fail that leaves the others open.

## When the Grell Returns

If anyone is still in the office at the twelfth minute, the grell comes back and a Guild response follows. If the office is empty, the alarm sounds and the response reaches X1 four minutes later.

## Reporting to Krebbyg

The party reports to Krebbyg in the booking office behind the Seven Masks stage door. The next morning each character who went down the stair receives a gold statuette worth 250 gp and a note.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Bregan D'aerthe member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Krebbyg. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** the party protects Nar'l's cover, meaning **Nar'l Cleared** is marked.
- **+1 Renown:** the party returns the captain's bag of holding and keeps the *Report on the Seven Masks Theater* out of the Guild's hands.
- **+1 Renown:** every character is through the X1 door by the eleventh minute and no alarm was raised.

Companions who are not Bregan D'aerthe members gain no Renown from this mission, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Dinner with Zardoz** fires on the second evening after each member reports, and it reads whichever of **Nar'l Cleared**, **Nar'l Active**, **Nar'l Extracted** or **Nar'l Eliminated** the member marked. **The Theater's Back Room** becomes available to an individual Bregan D'aerthe member at Renown 10 and 6th level. **The Dive** and the **Soldier** rank event read the Nar'l outcomes, and **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) reads them along with **Nar'l Bypass Learned**.

## Involved Characters

- **J.B. Nevercott** (Bregan D'aerthe): sends the brief and the tiered order, and does not appear.
- **Nar'l Xibrindas** (Bregan D'aerthe, secretly, and Xanathar's Guild): the compromised advisor, who trades his information for his life.
- **The grell** (Xanathar's Guild): Nar'l's bodyguard, with a standing order to kill him at the first sign of disloyalty.
- **Arannis Nur'zekk**, **Beldar Tlabbath**, **Rylvar Tlabbath** and **Draknafein Uriss** (Bregan D'aerthe): the four drow at the head of the stair, with orders they do not share.
- **Tarn Hobble** (independent): a Dock Ward fence whose word is half of the case against Nar'l.
- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** (Bregan D'aerthe): takes the party's report and delivers the captain's thanks.
- **Fel'rekt Lafeen** (Bregan D'aerthe): waits with a cart at the head of the stair if Nar'l is brought out.
- **Ahmaergo** (Xanathar's Guild): holds the case against Nar'l, and does not appear.
- **Soluun Xibrindas** (Bregan D'aerthe): Nar'l's elder brother, who does not appear and is the reason the Guild noticed.

## Dangers & Enemies

Nar'l fights only when cornered or attacked, and then he uses *Shield*, *Misty Step*, *Invisibility* and the eyescratch in his vial. The grell returns twelve minutes after the watch changes and fights until it is destroyed, and the Guild response of a **Bandit Captain**, **Bugbear Warriors** and a **Tough** arrives with it or four minutes behind it. The trail holds two **Giant Rats** and nothing else, and the four drow at the stair do not enter the lair.

## Overview

Bregan D'aerthe's man inside Xanathar's court has been found out. The party goes below the Castle Ward to reach him before the beholder's bodyguard does and decides what becomes of him.
