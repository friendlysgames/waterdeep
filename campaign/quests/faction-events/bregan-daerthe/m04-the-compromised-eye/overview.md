# The Compromised Eye: Overview

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available at Renown 7. Level 5 recommended. Three Nights must be complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 5th level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

Nar'l Xibrindas has been Jarlaxle's deepest inside source for three years, reporting on Xanathar's Guild from his position as the beholder's personal advisor. His cover has been degrading for two months. Soluun Xibrindas — Nar'l's brother, disowned from Bregan D'aerthe before this operation began — has been operating in Waterdeep under a forged Bregan D'aerthe token, and his unauthorized activities have attracted Guild attention. Nar'l has been intercepting internal reports and suppressing information before it could reach the beholder directly, and the gaps in his summaries have been noticed by Ahmaergo.

Ahmaergo has been building a case for two months. He has not yet formalized a request for Nihiloor interrogation, but once the mind flayer becomes interested in Nar'l, the situation becomes irreversible.

The party reaches Nar'l through his Castle Ward sewer contact protocol. He is managing fear with everything he has. Three approaches are viable: forge a false report to satisfy Ahmaergo, extract Nar'l from the lair entirely, or discredit Ahmaergo's source before the investigation can formalize. The choice shapes Nar'l's status going into **Xanathar's Lair**, and the consequences cannot be reversed.

## Involved Characters
- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** (Bregan D'aerthe) — delivers the brief at an unusual hour; knows the situation is time-sensitive; mentions Soluun's name carefully and watches the party's faces for recognition
- **Nar'l Xibrindas** (Bregan D'aerthe / Xanathar's Guild) — drow mage; Xanathar's advisor; Jarlaxle's deepest inside asset; has been covering for his brother Soluun's unauthorized activity; hostile on first contact; compact, precise, and managing fear with his entire professional toolkit
- **Ahmaergo** (Xanathar's Guild) — Xanathar's loyal taskmaster; has flagged gaps in Nar'l's intelligence reports to the beholder; the investigation is his; does not appear directly, but his conclusions shape the mission's urgency
- **Soluun Xibrindas** (disowned Bregan D'aerthe operative) — does not appear; the thread connecting his unauthorized presence in Waterdeep to Nar'l's exposure is the mission's background cause

## Dangers & Enemies
Two Guild sentries are aware of unusual sewer activity near the Castle Ward tunnels (Option B only). No combat triggers in Options A or C. The mission's primary challenge is navigating Nar'l's hostile initial attitude and then executing a resolution option under a tight timeline.

## Overview

Jarlaxle's deepest asset inside Xanathar's Guild is compromised. The party finds him in the sewers and decides whether to cover for him, extract him, or eliminate the threat another way.
