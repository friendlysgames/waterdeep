# Three Nights

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat and Social Event begins when the party finds Ott Steeltoes bound in the cellar of Trollskull Manor and ends when the iron bands take him away on the third night. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Question Ott, read the note a flying snake brings an hour later, and decide where in the manor to keep him.
> - Ask Kreb at the Seven Masks Theater what the captain wants. His answer depends on whether the member has seen him without his wig.
> - Hold the manor against the Guild's bugbears on Night One, with Lif's help if the party has treated the building well.
> - Spot the four Dungsweepers' Guild workers on Night Two, then fight them, move Ott out of their reach or clear the room.
> - Keep a Beholder Zombie away from Ott on Night Three until the bands take him.
>
> Only Bregan D'aerthe members receive the note and can visit Kreb. Their companions can take part in everything else and gain no Renown. Each night is its own adventuring day, and the party can take a Long Rest between nights.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Ott Steeltoes is Xanathar's fishkeeper, a dwarf who worships Zuggtmoy. He wears a skullcap stitched with dried beholder eyestalks and tends the goldfish Sylgar. Xanathar flies into a rage whenever the fish is neglected.
>
> Someone bound Ott in the iron bands of Bilarro and left him in the cellar of Trollskull Manor. The bands cannot be cut, picked, dispelled or forced open. Nobody knows who placed him there, including Ott and Bregan D'aerthe. The bands hold for three nights and then carry him away.
>
> Jarlaxle Baenre learned of the dwarf at dawn from the watchers he keeps on the alley and wrote the note himself. He wants to see what Xanathar spends to get his fishkeeper back. Fel'rekt Lafeen lies on the roof of the Bent Nail every night and counts on his fingers what comes through the doors. Fel'rekt never comes down, and if the party loses Ott, nobody in Bregan D'aerthe tries to recover him.
>
> The attacks are the Guild's own. The bugbears are ordinary Guild muscle. Some of the four Dungsweepers carry intellect devourers from Nihiloor's stock. The Beholder Zombie is one of the lair's guard creatures. Ott knows the Guild's ways and tells the party what is coming when asked.
>
> No Bregan D'aerthe speaker in this Event says "Jarlaxle". Krebbyg refers to him as "the captain", and the note is unsigned.

> [!gamemaster]**The Three Nights at a Glance**
>
> - **Morning, day one, 07:00:** The party finds Ott. The flying snake arrives at 08:00.
> - **Night One, midnight:** The bugbears hit the front door and the courtyard gate.
> - **Night Two, 19:00:** Four Dungsweepers take the long table at the back. They make their move at 23:00, or they leave at 22:00 if Ott is out of their reach.
> - **Night Three, midnight:** The zombie eats through the cellar grate. The bands take Ott at the end of the sixth round after it surfaces, or at once if it is destroyed.

### The Dwarf in the Cellar

Ott is in the cellar when the party wakes. The heavy door at the foot of the kitchen corridor stands open a hand's width. The first character to reach the corridor at about 07:00 hears a voice calling from below. Ott has been sitting against a wine barrel at the west end of the cellar, a few paces from the stairs, since before dawn.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A thin, anxious voice is calling up the cellar stairs. The door stands open on a dark that smells of cold stone and old wine. The voice breaks off when your boots touch the first step, then starts again, quicker than before.
>
> > "Hello? Is somebody there? Please tell me you're not from the Master. I've been sitting down here since before the bell, and I don't think I'm supposed to be."

When the party brings a light down the stairs, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A stocky dwarf sits on the flagstones with his back against a wine barrel and his boots stuck out in front of him. Broad iron bands circle his wrists and ankles, with no hinges, seams or lock. On his head is a leather skullcap with a ring of dried grey stalks sewn around the crown, and every stalk ends in a small glass eye that catches your lantern. He lifts both bound hands to straighten the cap and gives you a wide, relieved smile.
>
> > "Oh, thank the Lady of Rot, you're not his people. I'm Ott Steeltoes, and I'd stand up to shake your hand, but you can see how it is."

> [!social]**The Fishkeeper in the Cellar**
>
> Ott Steeltoes (Neutral Evil, Dwarf, he/him) :: Xanathar's nervous keeper of fish, a Zuggtmoy devotee in a hat of eyestalks, who is more worried about a goldfish than about himself.
>
> Ott talks fast and wanders between subjects. He is cheerful for a prisoner, because every hour away from the Xanathar is an hour in which nothing can be his fault. He asks for food in the first minute, has opinions about every tavern in Waterdeep and keeps returning to Sylgar, whom he calls "the little lord". When nervous he straightens the eyestalk cap, and sometimes he squeezes his eyes shut and strains to send a thought to the Xanathar. It has never worked. In rules terms he is a 2024 **Cultist Fanatic**, and he takes no part in any fight.
>
> Ott does not know who bound him, how he reached the cellar or why anyone wants him kept. He knows nothing about the Stone of Golorr or the Grand Game. He will not describe the lair, because he is certain the Master hears anything said about it. No argument changes his mind.
>
> Conversation topics Ott is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Who he is and what he does for the Xanathar.
> - How he came to be here, which he cannot explain.
> - The bands, and how little he understands them.
> - What the Guild will send to fetch him, which he has watched it send before.
> - Food, and Sylgar.
>
> Any character who listens to him and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that his cheerfulness is real and that he is glad to be away from the lair, whatever he says about the Master.

> [!qna]**Who are you?**
>
> Ott sits up as straight as the bands allow.
>
> > "Ott Steeltoes, keeper of the fish for the Xanathar, and it's not an easy post. I look after Sylgar, who's a goldfish, and he's the most important creature in that whole lair, whatever the others pretend."

> [!qna]**How did you get here?**
>
> > "I wish I knew, honestly. I went to sleep on my pallet under the bowl, same as every night, and woke up here with these things on. Oh shit, who's feeding the little lord? Nobody else knows the right flakes."

> [!qna]**What are the bands?**
>
> > "They won't come off. I tried for an hour, and I've tied a lot of knots in fish line in my time. There's no lock and no seam, and they don't pinch, which is the only nice thing I can say about them. I think they'll let go when they're ready."

> [!qna]**Why does the Xanathar want you back?**
>
> > "Because nobody else can keep the little lord alive. If I'm gone more than a day, the Master will scream at the ceiling and blame everyone in the lair. Then he'll send people to fetch me so he has someone to blame properly."

> [!qna]**What will the Guild send?**
>
> > "Bugbears first, because they're big and they don't ask why. Then the mind-flayer's little brain things, the ones that climb inside people and walk them about like puppets. They can feel a mind through a wall for three hundred feet or so, so you'd have to carry me a few streets away. And if he's really frantic, there's the big dead one that floats, the one with all the eyes. Zuggtmoy keep me, I hope it doesn't come to that."

> [!qna]**Can we get you anything?**
>
> > "Would you? Something hot, if the kitchen has it, and mushrooms if you can spare any. I can tell you what's wrong with every tavern in this city by the second bowl of stew, but I promise I'll be kind about yours."

> [!exploration]**The Iron Bands**
>
> No tool, spell or strength opens, cuts or removes the bands, and no attempt to do so harms Ott.
>
> Any character who examines the bands and makes a successful DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check recognizes the iron bands of Bilarro, an old binding that holds its wearer for a fixed time and then falls away. The character cannot tell how long that time is or who fitted the bands.
>
> The bands let Ott shuffle at 10 feet, and a character can carry him. Moving him does not change what the Guild sends, and the party can keep him in the cellar, upstairs or out of the manor altogether.

### The Note

About an hour after the party finds Ott, at 08:00, a flying snake arrives with the note. It goes to the nearest Bregan D'aerthe member in the taproom, or to the first member it finds if none is there.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A small flying snake with bright blue and orange scales comes in through the open shutter, circles the bar twice with its wings humming, and drops a folded card into the nearest hand. It leaves the way it came. The card is heavy black stock edged in silver, with a single line of neat handwriting.
>
> > *He is a guest. Three nights. Do not let Xanathar's people retrieve him.*

The card matches the one Nevercott left at the First Meeting, and companions can read it over the member's shoulder. The snake does not wait for an answer, and the card carries no name.

### Asking Kreb

Any member can visit the Seven Masks Theater in the Dock Ward at any time during the three days. The visit sets no outcome and changes nothing mechanical. Kreb tells the member what the people behind the card are doing, and his answer depends on what the member learned about him in **The Wazoo Affair**.

If the party includes a member who marked **Kreb Unmasked** and one who did not, Kreb greets the whole party in the lobby as Kreb Sorrush, then takes the member who marked it into the booking office alone and locks the door.

If the member did not mark **Kreb Unmasked**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The lobby of the Seven Masks smells of glue, old velvet and fresh paint. Two stagehands carry a painted flat of a castle across the floor, shouting at each other. A slim young man in a velvet waistcoat, with a brown wig and a pencil behind his ear, stands in the booking office doorway and turns the pages of a schedule as he talks.
>
> > "Kreb Sorrush, bookings, you want a dwarf, no, we haven't any dwarves, we had one in the chorus last year and he sang flat, darling. Anything that isn't a ticket, ask Fel, and Fel's out, he's always out, and the ghost has moved the fucking prop table again."

Kreb says nothing else of use. If the member presses him, he turns away to shout at a stagehand. The member learns only that Kreb knows what they mean and will not say.

If the member marked **Kreb Unmasked**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The booking office is a narrow room with a schedule pinned to every wall. The brown wig sits on a stand beside the inkwell. The young drow at the desk has white hair, a velvet waistcoat and no gloves, and he jumps up to turn the key in the lock as soon as you are inside.
>
> > "Darling, you came, good, sit anywhere except the green chair, the ghost sits in it. About the dwarf, I've been told to say nothing, and I'm going to say a great deal anyway, because I'm hopeless at saying nothing and Fel's not here to stop me."

> [!social]**The Booking Manager Without His Wig**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: the young drow behind Kreb Sorrush, who talks in run-ons, defers to Fel'rekt on anything serious and has no real answer to most of what the party asks.
>
> Krebbyg checks his schedule as he speaks and swears constantly. He knows the captain wrote the note, that Fel'rekt is on the Bent Nail roof, and that nobody in the company put the dwarf in the cellar. He does not know why the captain wants Ott kept, and his answers on that are guesses he reports as facts. He answers any question about the plan with "Ask Fel", and Fel'rekt is on the roof.
>
> Conversation topics Krebbyg is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Who Ott is and who left him in the cellar.
> - What the captain wants from the three nights.
> - Whether Fel'rekt will help.
> - The ghost, at length.

> [!qna]**Who is Ott?**
>
> > "A fish keeper, apparently the most important one in Waterdeep if you believe the Xanathar's people. I'd love to tell you more, but the captain wrote that note himself and Fel read it to me twice so I'd keep my mouth shut. Fel was very proud of that."

> [!qna]**Who put him in our cellar?**
>
> > "Not us, I swear on the ghost's props. Fel and I were counting heads on that alley the night before, and nobody carried a dwarf past us. Fel would have noticed, he notices everything, honestly it's exhausting. The captain doesn't know either, and he hates not knowing, so he wrote 'guest' on the card and got on with things."

> [!qna]**What does the captain want?**
>
> > "He wants to see what the Xanathar sends for a fish man, darling. Fel's up on the Bent Nail roof counting it on his fingers, so if you spot a dark shape up there, that's him, and don't wave. If you lose the dwarf, nobody's furious, but the captain will ask you about it very politely, and you don't want that."

> [!qna]**Will Fel help us?**
>
> > "No, that's the point, he only watches. The captain wants to see what you do on your own. I told Fel that was a bit much and he told me to fill in the ticket book, so I did, and I'm still annoyed. Ask Fel if you can find him, but don't hold your breath."

### Night One: The Bugbears

The Guild moves first with its muscle. Six bugbears set out from the docks before midnight with a hired mule and cart. They reach the alley as the shrine bell strikes twelve. The courtyard gate opens onto the kitchen, and the cellar door is in the kitchen corridor beyond it, so the courtyard group is the one that threatens Ott. The front door group only has to hold the taproom.

> [!gamemaster]**Lif's Help**
>
> If the party marked **Lif Appeased** in **Trollskull Alley**, Lif helps on the ground floor and the upper floors. At the start of each round, before any character acts, he produces the next effect from the list below, and the party spends no action on it. After the fourth round the list starts again at the second effect. Lif cannot attack or touch a creature, and he does not manifest in the cellar, so none of these effects can take place there.
>
> - **The doors:** Every door and shutter on the ground floor slams, and Lif drops the bar across the front door. A creature forces it by spending an action and making a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check, once per round.
> - **The shelf:** A rack of crockery or a shelf of casks tips onto a 10-foot square chosen by the party. Each creature there makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage on a failed save or half as much on a success, and the square becomes difficult terrain.
> - **The table:** The heaviest table slides across a 10-foot opening, such as the mouth of the kitchen corridor. A creature that crosses it spends 10 extra feet of movement, and a creature behind it has half cover.
> - **The ale:** A cask bursts across a 10-foot square. Each creature that enters the square or starts its turn there makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or falls Prone.
>
> If the party did not mark **Lif Appeased**, Lif gives no help and the list does not apply. On Night Two and Night Three the list begins at the second effect.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The shrine bell down the alley has barely finished striking twelve when every candle in the taproom gutters out. Wood scrapes at the front door as something large leans on it from outside, and a mule stamps in a cart's traces at the mouth of the alley. At the back of the manor a latch rattles in the courtyard gate, and a low, gravelly voice says something and doesn't bother to whisper.

If the party marked **Lif Appeased**, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The old mirror behind the bar clouds over, and a line of soot-grey letters crawls across the glass: **THEM. OUT.** The words are underlined twice.

> [!hazard]**Guild Muscle at the Doors**
>
> The gang is six bugbears, and six 2024 **Bugbear Warriors** at once would overwhelm 4th-level characters at any party size. Only part of the gang comes through the doors. The rest wait at the alley mouth with the mule and cart, and they drive off the moment a member of the first group is carried out or falls. The **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** audits the fight for three, four and five participating combatants, and the roster follows the number of characters who fight, companions included.
>
> - **Three combatants:** one **Bugbear Warrior** at the courtyard gate and one **Tough** at the front door.
> - **Four combatants:** one **Bugbear Warrior** at the courtyard gate, one at the front door, and a **Bandit** who shoots from the alley and follows the front door group in.
> - **Five combatants:** two **Bugbear Warriors** at the courtyard gate and one at the front door.
>
> #### The Attackers' Tactics
> At the start of combat, the courtyard group breaks the gate and heads for the cellar door at the end of the kitchen corridor, and the front door group breaks the street door and spreads through the taproom.
>
> During combat, the attackers:
> - Grab the nearest character who stands between them and the cellar door, haul that character aside with Abduct and strike with the Light Hammer.
> - Open the cellar door, go down and lift Ott as an action. A bugbear carrying Ott moves at 15 feet and heads for the nearest outside door.
> - Use the Tough and the Bandit as the front door group. They fight characters in the taproom and do not go near the cellar.
>
> The cart waits at the alley mouth, 60 feet from either door. Mark **Ott Lost** if a bugbear gets Ott into it.
>
> Ott takes no part in the fight. He shouts advice from the floor, squeezes his eyes shut when a bugbear hits something and tries once more to tell the Xanathar where he is.
>
> The attackers retreat to the alley once half the roster, rounded up, is down, or when a Watch patrol arrives. A lone attacker who is surrounded drops his weapon and surrenders. The bugbears are here for Ott and have no quarrel with the tavern. They ignore anyone who stays out of their way.

> [!exploration]**Turning the Gang Away**
>
> The party has three ways to end the night without finishing the fight.
>
> - **Empty cellar:** If Ott is no longer in the manor when the gang arrives, the bugbears break in anyway, search every room for three rounds and leave when they find nobody. They fight anyone who blocks them.
> - **Pay them off:** A character who offers each attacker 50 gp and makes a successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check convinces them to take the coin and leave. The bugbears fear the Master, so they accept only if the party has already beaten one of them or has moved Ott out of the manor. Otherwise the check fails and the fight goes on.
> - **Parley after the first fall:** Once any attacker is down, a character who makes a successful DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation) check makes the rest withdraw to the alley.

> [!gamemaster]**The Watch**
>
> If the party includes a member of the Lords' Alliance at Renown 3 or higher (Redknife), a patrol of two **Guards** reaches the front door at the start of the fifth round and the attackers flee. The Guards do not fight and do not count as combatants. Without a Redknife in the party, no patrol comes before morning.

If the party takes a bugbear alive, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The bugbear sits against the wall with his hammer on the floor and both hands open on his knees, breathing hard through a broken tusk. He will not look at the cellar door.

> [!social]**The Bugbear on the Floor**
>
> A Guild Bugbear Warrior (Chaotic Evil, Bugbear, he/him) :: a hired Guild bruiser paid by the day to carry a dwarf out of a cellar, who now wants only to leave.
>
> He answers in short sentences and fears the Master more than he fears the party. He knows only what the Guild told him and nothing about the devourers or the zombie.

> [!qna]**Who sent you?**
>
> > "The Master sent us. I carry things for him, that's all. He wants the dwarf back, and there's more coming after us, I heard the others say so. I never heard what, and I don't want to."

When the fight ends, the party has the rest of the night to itself. Ott stayed against the barrel through all of it and tells anyone who comes down that the Master will send something worse. If Ott is lost, read the following and end the mission:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cart at the end of the alley lurches forward with the dwarf slung across its tailboard like a sack of meal. He lifts his bound hands as it rattles away. "Tell somebody to feed the little lord!" he shouts, and then the mule turns the corner.

### The Day Between

The second day is quiet if the party lets it be. Ott eats everything the kitchen sends down, tells the party that the stew needs salt, and asks whether any of them knows how long a goldfish can go without feeding. If the party marked **Lif Appeased**, Lif polishes the bar all afternoon, and the portrait behind it has a small smile.

> [!exploration]**The Roof of the Bent Nail**
>
> Any character who watches the roofs across the alley during the day and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check sees a slim figure in a dark cloak on the roof of the Bent Nail. The figure lifts a hand, spreads the fingers, counts down on them, and ducks out of sight. A member who marked **Kreb Unmasked** recognizes Fel'rekt from the booking office.
>
> Fel'rekt does not answer if hailed and does not come down. He stays on the roof each night until the third night is over.

### Night Two: The Quiet Table

The hosts arrive at 19:00 and sit at the long table at the back by the hearth. Within a minute they know there is a single mind below ground and that it has not moved. They wait for the taproom to thin before they act.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom fills early, and the fire is lit against a thin rain that has fallen since dusk. Four people come in out of the wet together, in tar-stained leather aprons and heavy boots, and take the long table at the back. The barmaid brings four mugs of dark ale. They pay in small coin, lift none of the mugs, and sit without a word to one another.

If the party marked **Lif Appeased**, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The candle in the middle of their table gutters and leans every time one of them looks toward the corridor to the kitchen.

> [!exploration]**Four Quiet Guests**
>
> Any character who watches the table for a few minutes and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that these four are not resting after a shift. None has lifted a mug or spoken to the others, and each watches a different part of the room. Any character who looks closely at their faces in the firelight and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check sees that their pupils are as wide as coins in a bright room.
>
> - **Advantage:** The party marked **Lif Appeased**.
> - **Result of 20 or higher:** The character also notices that none of the four has blinked.
>
> Any character who has seen the pupils and makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check recalls that intellect devourers ride the bodies of the dead, sense every mind within 300 feet through walls and cannot be bargained with.
>
> If nobody spots them, they stand together at 23:00, when the taproom has thinned, and walk toward the kitchen corridor in step, and everyone in the taproom sees it.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True of the Four**
>
> Some of the Dungsweepers' Guild workers at the back table were taken in the old cisterns about a tenday ago by devourers from Nihiloor's stock. A 2024 **Intellect Devourer** that takes a body consumes the host's brain, so each host died when it was taken, and nothing the party does can save it. The devourers control the dead bodies, which still know the workers' names, languages and routes.
>
> Any character who examines a fallen host and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check finds that the host's skull holds no brain and has held none for days. The Dungsweepers who carry no devourer are ordinary workers. They were told to come to a Guild night out and keep to their seats, and they know nothing.
>
> The devourers are here for Ott and do not use Steal Body on a character who falls to 0 Hit Points. Each devourer senses minds within 300 feet through any barrier, so each knows there is exactly one mind below ground.

The party has four ways to meet the table.

> [!exploration]**Meeting the Table**
>
> - **Move Ott out of reach:** If Ott is more than 300 feet from the back table before 22:00, the devourers sense no mind below ground and keep to their mugs until 22:00. They then walk out together. A party that follows them sees them climb down a drain in the Dock Ward and vanish into the sewer.
> - **Clear the taproom:** Any character who announces closing early and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check empties the room of everyone but the four. The four remain until 23:00 and make their move in an empty room, and no regular is hurt.
> - **Confront the table:** If the party tells the four that the dwarf is not for sale, the nearest host answers in a flat voice that Ott belongs to the Master and the room can leave unharmed if he is handed over. When the party refuses, the fight starts.
> - **Wait for 23:00:** The party can hold the kitchen corridor and let the hosts come to it.

> [!hazard]**The Table at the Back**
>
> Four workers sit at the table, and the roster of hosts follows the number of characters who fight, companions included. The **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** audits the fight for three, four and five participating combatants. A hosted **Commoner** has AC 10, 4 Hit Points and a club, and the **Intellect Devourer** inside has Total Cover until the body falls. When the body drops, the devourer is forced into the nearest open space with its full 28 Hit Points.
>
> - **Three combatants:** one host and three plain **Commoners**.
> - **Four combatants:** one host, one **Tough** posing as the crew's foreman, and two plain **Commoners**.
> - **Five combatants:** two hosts and two plain **Commoners**.
>
> #### The Hosts' Tactics
> At the start of combat, the hosts stand and head for the kitchen corridor. The foreman, if there is one, shouts for the crew to sit down, and the plain workers drop their mugs and back toward the front door.
>
> During combat, the hosts:
> - Go for the cellar door and ignore characters unless blocked. A blocked host strikes with Claw and Devour Intellect and moves on.
> - Take Ott, as a pair if two hosts survive or alone if one does, and carry him at 15 feet to the cart at the alley mouth. Mark **Ott Lost** if they get him into it.
> - Do not use Steal Body on a fallen character, whatever the devourer would prefer.
>
> A devourer flees into the alley gutter when it is out of its host and reduced to 13 Hit Points or fewer, and it never comes back. The foreman surrenders when the last host falls. Plain workers hide, shout and flee, and fight with clubs only when cornered.

> [!social]**The Oldest of the Plain Workers**
>
> A Dungsweepers' Guild Commoner (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: a sewer sweeper in her fifties who does not like her crew's quiet guests and is relieved to be asked.
>
> She answers in nervous, rambling sentences and knows nothing about devourers. She tells a character who stops her at the door, or finds her afterward, what she saw.

> [!qna]**Who are they?**
>
> > "They came to our crew from the old cisterns about a tenday ago. None of them has eaten with us since, or taken off their boots, or said a word. The foreman told us it was a Guild night out and to keep our mouths shut. I thought it was a bad idea, but you don't argue with the foreman."

If the hosts take Ott to the street, read the farewell from Night One, with the hosts' cart in place of the bugbears' and the hosts walking beside it in step.

### Night Three: The Grate

Night Three begins at the midnight after Night Two. Ott spends the day eating and talking, and as the night comes on he stops talking and sits with his hands in his lap.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Every glass and tankard in the manor rings at once on a single high note that carries through the floorboards and the beds, then stops. In the silence a sound rises from below, a long wet grinding with the patter of falling masonry under it, as though something is eating its way through the stone.

If the party did not mark **Lif Appeased**, the glasses do not ring, and the grinding wakes the manor instead.

Characters in the cellar see the grate go. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The iron grate at the east end of the cellar turns grey and crumbles to dust, and a ragged hole opens in the flagstones. Something large and pale rises through the dust, a swollen mass with a ring of stalks around its crown and a clouded eye at the end of every stalk. A long tear runs down one side, and its wide mouth, full of broken teeth, hangs open. The eyes turn together toward the dwarf.

> [!gamemaster]**The Zombie's Reasons and Its Rules**
>
> The Guild sent a 2024 **Beholder Zombie** up the old sewer line to the grate in the cellar floor, the nearest way to the cellar. Part of the old tunnel fell on it on the way. The **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** audits the fight and flags it as a High-Power encounter. Four changes bring it within reach of 4th-level characters.
>
> - **Damage from the tunnel.** The zombie surfaces with reduced hit points: 31 for three combatants, 62 for four and all 93 for five.
> - **One Eye Ray on its first turn.** It rises through the hole slowly and has only one ray ready for the round it surfaces.
> - **Fixed ray order.** Its Eye Rays go in a fixed order, Fear Ray, Paralyzing Ray, Enervation Ray and Disintegration Ray, and then the order repeats. The Disintegration Ray cannot fire before its third turn.
> - **A mindless goal.** It aims every ray and every Bite at a character, never at Ott, because its goal is to pick him up and carry him back down the hole.
>
> The Disintegration Ray deals 27 (5d10) force damage and turns a creature reduced to 0 Hit Points by it to dust. A character with 27 Hit Points or fewer should take cover before the zombie's third turn. Lif's effects do not work in the cellar. If the party carries Ott upstairs, the zombie follows him up the cellar stairs and into the kitchen corridor, where Lif's effects apply on the ground floor.

> [!hazard]**The Zombie from the Sewer**
>
> The party chooses where to stand. A character in the cellar when the grate dissolves is in the zombie's line of sight on its first turn. A character upstairs has the cellar door and a stairway between them and it.
>
> #### The Zombie's Tactics
> At the start of combat, the zombie surfaces at the east end of the cellar and makes one Eye Ray at the nearest character it can see.
>
> During combat, the zombie:
> - Makes its Multiattack of two Eye Rays in the fixed order at the nearest character it can see, or Bites a character within its reach instead.
> - Flies toward Ott at 20 feet. If Ott is still against the barrel at the west end of the cellar, he is about 30 feet from the grate, and the zombie reaches him on its third turn.
> - Lifts Ott with its next action if it ends a turn adjacent to him with no character between them, and carries him toward the hole the turn after. A character within reach of the zombie while it holds Ott can make a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check to pull him free, or deal 20 damage to the zombie in one round to make it drop him. Mark **Ott Lost** if the zombie takes Ott down the hole.
> - Stays up through its Undead Fortitude trait until radiant damage or a critical hit lands, so the last few hit points take an extra round.
>
> The zombie does not surrender or retreat. At the end of the sixth round after it surfaces, or the moment it is destroyed, the bands flare and take Ott away. A zombie still alive then loses its goal and drops back down the hole.

> [!exploration]**Keeping Ott Away**
>
> The party has two ways to avoid the fight.
>
> - **Hold for six rounds:** A character who spends an action tipping a stack of casks across the 10-foot lane between the hole and Ott's corner makes a successful DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check slows the zombie for the round, because it must go around the casks. A party that keeps the zombie from Ott for six rounds ends the fight.
> - **Hide him:** The zombie has a passive Perception of 9. A character who spends an action putting Ott behind the casks and makes a successful DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check hides him. A frightened Ott squeaks, and a character who makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check keeps him silent. If the character fails either check, the zombie sees him at once.

When the six rounds are up, or the zombie falls, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The iron bands flare a dull red all at once, and the smell of hot metal fills the cellar. Ott squeezes his eyes shut, then opens them wide and looks down at his wrists as the red light climbs his arms.
>
> > "Oh, shit, that's new. Somebody please feed the little lord, he likes the flakes with the red ones."
>
> The light closes over him like a hand. When it fades, the bands and the dwarf are gone, and only a patch of warm flagstone remains where he sat.

If the zombie is still alive, it hangs over the hole for a moment with its eyes rolling, and then it turns and sinks out of sight the way it came. The hole in the cellar floor stays, and the Cellarers' and Plumbers' Guild inspector who flagged the old grate will want to see it.

If the party marked **Lif Appeased**, Lif polishes the bar for the rest of the night, and the portrait behind it has a small smile in the morning.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the bands take Ott, or when the Guild gets him first. The second black pouch arrives that afternoon in **Coin Pouches**, and Krebbyg does not come for Ott.

If **Ott Lost** is marked before the third night, the remaining nights do not happen. Ott returns to his bowl in the lair and tells the Guild that Trollskull Manor held him. If the bands take Ott, he reappears in Xanathar's fish room by morning with no memory of how he got there. He tells the Guild that humans held him in a cellar and that he does not know where. The Zhentarim take him during the Guild war.

## Overview

A bound dwarf in a hat of dried eyestalks turns up in the cellar of Trollskull Manor, and the party has three nights to keep him there against whatever comes for him.

## Summary

We found a dwarf named Ott Steeltoes bound in iron bands in our cellar, and a flying snake brought a card that called him a guest and told us to keep him from Xanathar's people. Bugbears came on the first night, four silent Dungsweepers, some of them already dead, on the second, and a Beholder Zombie through the cellar grate on the third. The bands took Ott away that night. We don't know where he went or who put him there.
