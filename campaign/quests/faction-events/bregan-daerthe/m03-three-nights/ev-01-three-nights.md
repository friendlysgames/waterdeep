# Three Nights

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat and Social Event begins when the party finds Ott Steeltoes bound in the cellar of Trollskull Manor and ends when the iron bands carry him away at the close of the third night. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Question Ott, read the note a flying snake brings an hour later, and decide where in the manor to keep him.
> - Ask Kreb at the Seven Masks Theater what the captain wants, and get an answer that depends on whether the member has seen him without his wig.
> - Hold the manor against the Guild's bugbears on Night One, with Lif's help if the party has treated the building well.
> - Spot four Dungsweepers' Guild workers on Night Two before they make their move, and decide whether to fight them, move Ott out of their reach or clear the room.
> - Keep a Beholder Zombie away from Ott on Night Three until the bands take him.
>
> Only Bregan D'aerthe members receive the note and can visit Kreb. Their companions can take part in everything else and gain no Renown. Each night is its own adventuring day, and the party can take a Long Rest between nights.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Ott Steeltoes is Xanathar's fishkeeper, a dwarf who worships Zuggtmoy. He wears a skullcap stitched with dried beholder eyestalks and tends the goldfish Sylgar, and Xanathar flies into a rage whenever the fish is neglected. Someone bound him in the iron bands of Bilarro, which cannot be cut, picked, dispelled or forced open, and left him in the cellar of Trollskull Manor. Nobody knows who did it, and that includes Ott and Bregan D'aerthe. The bands are set to hold for three nights and then release him, and when they do they carry him away with them.
>
> Jarlaxle Baenre learned of the dwarf at dawn from the watchers he keeps on the alley, wrote the note himself and decided to make use of the three nights. He wants to see what Xanathar spends to get his fishkeeper back, so Fel'rekt Lafeen lies on the roof of the Bent Nail every night and counts on his fingers what comes through the doors. Fel'rekt does not come down for any reason, and if the party loses Ott, nobody in Bregan D'aerthe tries to recover him.
>
> The attacks are the Guild's own. The bugbears are ordinary Guild muscle, the four Dungsweepers carry intellect devourers from Nihiloor's stock, and the Beholder Zombie is one of the guard creatures kept in the lair. Ott knows the Guild's ways well enough to tell the party what is coming, and he does so when asked.
>
> No Bregan D'aerthe speaker in this Event says "Jarlaxle". Krebbyg refers to him as "the captain", and the note is unsigned.

> [!gamemaster]**The Three Nights at a Glance**
>
> - **Morning, day one, 07:00:** The party finds Ott. The flying snake arrives at 08:00.
> - **Night One, midnight:** The bugbears hit the front door and the courtyard gate.
> - **Night Two, 19:00:** Four Dungsweepers take the long table at the back. They make their move at 23:00, or they leave at 22:00 if Ott is out of their reach.
> - **Night Three, midnight:** The zombie eats through the cellar grate. The bands take Ott at the end of the sixth round after it surfaces, or at once if it is destroyed.

### The Dwarf in the Cellar

Ott is in the cellar when the party wakes. The heavy door at the foot of the kitchen corridor stands open a hand's width, and the first character to reach the corridor at about 07:00 hears a voice calling from below. Ott has been sitting against a wine barrel at the west end of the cellar, a few paces from the stairs, since before dawn.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A thin, anxious voice is calling up the cellar stairs, and the door at the top stands open on a dark that smells of cold stone and old wine. The voice breaks off when your boots touch the first step, and then it starts again, quicker than before.
>
> > "Hello? Hello, is somebody there? Please tell me you're not from the Master, because I've been sitting down here since before the bell and I really don't think I'm supposed to be, and I'd like to know who I'm supposed to be afraid of."

When the party brings a light down the stairs, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A stocky dwarf sits on the flagstones with his back against a wine barrel and his boots stuck out in front of him. Broad iron bands circle his wrists and ankles, and they have no hinges, no seams and no lock, so that the metal looks as though it grew out of him. On his head he wears a skullcap of stitched leather with a ring of dried grey stalks sewn around the crown, and every stalk ends in a small glass eye that catches your lantern. He lifts both bound hands to straighten the cap and gives you a wide, relieved smile.
>
> > "Oh, thank the Lady of Rot, you're not his people. I'm Ott, Ott Steeltoes, and I'd stand up to shake your hand, but you can see how it is."

> [!social]**The Fishkeeper in the Cellar**
>
> Ott Steeltoes (Neutral Evil, Dwarf, he/him) :: Xanathar's nervous keeper of fish, a Zuggtmoy devotee in a hat of eyestalks, who is more worried about a goldfish than about himself.
>
> Ott talks fast and wanders between subjects. He is oddly cheerful for a prisoner, because every hour away from the Xanathar is an hour in which nothing can be his fault. He asks for food in the first minute, has opinions about every tavern in Waterdeep and keeps returning to Sylgar, whom he calls "the little lord". When he is nervous he straightens the eyestalk cap, and now and then he squeezes his eyes shut and strains with his whole face to send a thought to the Xanathar, which has never once worked. In rules terms he is a 2024 **Cultist Fanatic**, and he takes no part in any fight.
>
> Ott does not know who bound him, how he reached the cellar or why anyone wants him kept. He knows nothing about the Stone of Golorr or the Grand Game. He will not describe the lair, because he is certain the Master hears anything said about it, and no argument shifts him.
>
> Conversation topics Ott is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Who he is and what he does for the Xanathar.
> - How he came to be here, which he cannot explain.
> - The bands, and how little he understands them.
> - What the Guild will send to fetch him, which he has watched it send before.
> - Food, and Sylgar.
>
> Any character who listens to him and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that his cheerfulness is real and that he is glad to be away from the lair, whatever he says about the Master.

> [!qna]**Who are you?**
>
> Ott sits up as straight as the bands allow.
>
> > "Ott Steeltoes, keeper of the fish for the Xanathar, and don't let anyone tell you it's an easy post. I look after Sylgar, who's a goldfish, and he's the most important creature in that whole lair, and every one of them knows it, even the ones who pretend they don't."

> [!qna]**How did you get here?**
>
> > "I wish I knew, honestly. I went to sleep on my pallet under the bowl, same as every night, and I woke up here with these things on and nobody has said a single word to me since. Oh shit, who's feeding the little lord? Somebody has to feed him, and nobody else knows the right flakes."

> [!qna]**What are the bands?**
>
> > "They won't come off, I tried for an hour, and I've tied a lot of knots in fish line in my time. There's no lock and no seam, and they don't pinch, which is the only nice thing I can say about them. If you ask me, they're old, and I think they'll let go when they're ready and not before."

> [!qna]**Why does the Xanathar want you back?**
>
> > "Because nobody else can keep the little lord alive, and the Master knows it. If I'm gone more than a day he'll be screaming at the ceiling, and he'll blame everyone in the lair, and then he'll send people to fetch me so he has someone to blame properly. It's always me, and I've never worked out why."

> [!qna]**What will the Guild send?**
>
> > "Bugbears first, because they always send bugbears, since they're big and they don't ask why. Then the mind-flayer's little brain things, the ones that climb inside people and walk them about like puppets, and they can feel a mind through a wall for three hundred feet or so. If you want to hide me from those, you'll need to carry me a few streets away. And if he's really frantic, there's the big dead one that floats, the one with all the eyes. Zuggtmoy keep me, I hope it doesn't come to that."

> [!qna]**Can we get you anything?**
>
> > "Would you? Something hot, if the kitchen has it, and mushrooms if you can spare any, though I won't be fussy. I can tell you what's wrong with every tavern in this city by the second bowl of stew, and I promise I'll be kinder about yours."

> [!exploration]**The Iron Bands**
>
> No tool, spell or strength available to the party opens, cuts or removes the bands, and no attempt to do so harms Ott. Any character who examines the bands and makes a successful DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check recognizes them as the iron bands of Bilarro, an old binding that holds its wearer for a fixed time chosen when the bands are fitted and then falls away. The character cannot tell how long the time is or who fitted the bands.
>
> The bands let Ott shuffle at 10 feet, and a character can carry him. Moving him does not change what the Guild sends, and the party can keep him in the cellar, upstairs or out of the manor altogether.

### The Note

About an hour after the party finds Ott, at 08:00, a flying snake arrives with the note. It goes to the nearest Bregan D'aerthe member in the taproom, or to the first member it finds if none is there.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A small flying snake in bright blue and orange scales comes in through the open shutter, circles the bar twice with its wings humming, and drops a folded slip of card into the nearest hand before it climbs out the way it came. The card is heavy black stock edged in silver, and it carries a single line of neat, unhurried handwriting.
>
> > *He is a guest. Three nights. Do not let Xanathar's people retrieve him.*

The card matches the one Nevercott left at the First Meeting, and companions can read it over the member's shoulder. The snake does not wait for an answer, and the card carries no name.

### Asking Kreb

Any member can visit the Seven Masks Theater in the Dock Ward at any time during the three days. The visit sets no outcome and changes nothing mechanical. It tells the member what the people behind the card are doing, and it shows what a member has learned about Kreb since **The Wazoo Affair**.

If the party includes a member who marked **Kreb Unmasked** and one who did not, Kreb greets the whole party in the lobby as Kreb Sorrush, then takes the member who marked it into the booking office alone and locks the door.

If the member did not mark **Kreb Unmasked**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The lobby of the Seven Masks smells of glue, old velvet and fresh paint, and two stagehands are carrying a painted flat of a castle across the floor with a great deal of shouting. A slim young man in a velvet waistcoat, with a brown wig and a pencil behind his ear, stands in the booking office doorway turning the pages of a schedule as he talks.
>
> > "Kreb Sorrush, bookings, you want a dwarf, no, we haven't any dwarves, we had one in the chorus last year and he sang flat, so that's a no, darling. If it's about anything that isn't a ticket, you'll have to ask Fel, and Fel's out, he's always out, and the ghost has moved the fucking prop table again."

Kreb says nothing else of use, and he turns away to shout at a stagehand if the member presses him. The member leaves with nothing more than the knowledge that Kreb knows what they mean and will not say.

If the member marked **Kreb Unmasked**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The booking office is a narrow room with a schedule pinned to every wall, and the lamp on the desk is turned so that its light falls behind the man sitting there. The brown wig sits on a stand beside the inkwell. The young drow beneath it has white hair, a velvet waistcoat and no gloves, and he jumps up to turn the key in the lock as soon as you are inside.
>
> > "Darling, you came, good, sit anywhere except the green chair, the ghost sits in it. Before you ask about the dwarf, I've been told to say nothing, and I'm going to say a great deal anyway, because I'm hopeless at saying nothing and Fel's not here to stop me."

> [!social]**The Booking Manager Without His Wig**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: the young drow behind Kreb Sorrush, who talks in run-ons, defers to Fel'rekt on anything serious and has no real answer to most of what the party asks.
>
> Krebbyg checks his schedule as he speaks and swears constantly without noticing. He knows that the captain wrote the note, that Fel'rekt is on the Bent Nail roof, and that nobody in the company put the dwarf in the cellar. He does not know why the captain wants Ott kept, and the answers he does give are guesses he reports as facts. He answers any question about the plan with "Ask Fel", and Fel'rekt is on the roof.
>
> Conversation topics Krebbyg is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Who Ott is and who left him in the cellar.
> - What the captain wants from the three nights.
> - Whether Fel'rekt will help.
> - The ghost, at length.

> [!qna]**Who is Ott?**
>
> > "A fish keeper, apparently the most important fish keeper in Waterdeep if you believe the Xanathar's people, and I'd love to tell you more, but the captain wrote that note himself and Fel read it to me twice so I'd know to keep my mouth shut. Fel was very proud of that."

> [!qna]**Who put him in our cellar?**
>
> > "Not us, I swear on the ghost's props. Fel and I were counting heads on that alley the night before, and nobody carried a dwarf past us, and Fel would have noticed because he notices everything, honestly it's exhausting. The captain doesn't know either, and he hates not knowing, so he wrote 'guest' on the card and got on with things."

> [!qna]**What does the captain want?**
>
> > "He wants to see what the Xanathar sends for a fish man, darling, so Fel's up on the Bent Nail roof counting it on his fingers, and if you spot a dark shape up there, that's him, and don't wave. If you lose the dwarf, nobody's furious, but the captain will ask you about it very politely, and I'd rather you didn't have to find out what that's like."

> [!qna]**Will Fel help us?**
>
> > "No, that's the point, he watches, and he says the captain wants to see what you do on your own. I told him that was a bit much and he told me to fill in the ticket book, so I did, and I'm still annoyed. Ask Fel if you can find him, but I wouldn't hold your breath."

### Night One: The Bugbears

The Guild moves first with its muscle. Six bugbears set out from the docks before midnight with a hired mule and cart to carry the dwarf, and they reach the alley as the shrine bell strikes twelve. The courtyard gate opens onto the kitchen, and the cellar door is in the kitchen corridor beyond it, so the group that comes through the gate is the one that threatens Ott. The group at the street door only has to hold the taproom.

> [!gamemaster]**Lif's Help**
>
> If the party marked **Lif Appeased** in **Trollskull Alley**, Lif fights beside the party on the ground floor and the upper floors. At the start of each round, before any character acts, he produces one free effect from the list below, in order, and the party spends no action on it. After the fourth round the list starts again at the second effect. Lif cannot attack and cannot touch a creature, and he does not manifest in the cellar, so none of these effects can take place there.
>
> - **The doors:** Every door and shutter on the ground floor slams, and Lif drops the bar across the front door. A creature forces it by spending an action and making a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check, once per round.
> - **The shelf:** A rack of crockery or a shelf of casks tips onto a 10-foot square chosen by the party. Each creature there makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage on a failed save or half as much on a success, and the square becomes difficult terrain.
> - **The table:** The heaviest table slides across a 10-foot opening, such as the mouth of the kitchen corridor. A creature that crosses it spends 10 extra feet of movement, and a creature behind it has half cover.
> - **The ale:** A cask bursts across a 10-foot square. Each creature that enters the square or starts its turn there makes a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or falls Prone.
>
> If the party did not mark **Lif Appeased**, Lif gives no help and the list does not apply. On Night Two and Night Three the list begins at the second effect.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The shrine bell down the alley has barely finished striking twelve when every candle in the taproom gutters and goes out together. A heavy scrape of wood comes from the front door as something large leans on it from outside, and a mule stamps and blows in a cart's traces at the mouth of the alley. At the back of the manor a latch rattles in the courtyard gate, and someone says something low and gravelly in a voice that does not mean to be quiet.

If the party marked **Lif Appeased**, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The old mirror behind the bar clouds over, and a line of soot-grey letters crawls across the glass: **THEM. OUT.** The words are underlined twice.

> [!hazard]**Guild Muscle at the Doors**
>
> The Guild's gang is six bugbears strong, and six 2024 **Bugbear Warriors** at once would overwhelm 4th-level characters at any party size. Only part of the gang comes through the doors. The rest wait at the alley mouth with the mule and cart, and they drive away the moment a member of the first group is carried out or falls. The **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** audits the fight for three, four and five participating combatants, and the roster follows the number of characters who fight, companions included.
>
> - **Three combatants:** one **Bugbear Warrior** at the courtyard gate and one **Tough** at the front door.
> - **Four combatants:** one **Bugbear Warrior** at the courtyard gate, one at the front door, and a **Bandit** who shoots from the alley and follows the front door group in.
> - **Five combatants:** two **Bugbear Warriors** at the courtyard gate and one at the front door.
>
> #### The Attackers' Tactics
> At the start of combat, the courtyard group breaks the gate and heads for the cellar door at the end of the kitchen corridor, and the front door group breaks the street door and spreads through the taproom.
>
> During combat, the attackers:
> - Grab the nearest character who stands between them and the cellar door, haul that character aside with Abduct and strike with the Light Hammer.
> - Open the cellar door, go down and lift Ott as an action. A bugbear carrying Ott moves at 15 feet and heads for the nearest outside door.
> - Use the Tough and the Bandit as the front door group. They fight characters in the taproom and do not go near the cellar.
>
> The cart waits at the alley mouth, 60 feet from either door. Mark **Ott Lost** if a bugbear gets Ott into it.
>
> Ott takes no part in the fight. He shouts advice from the floor, squeezes his eyes shut when a bugbear hits something and tries once more to tell the Xanathar where he is.
>
> The attackers retreat to the alley once half the roster, rounded up, is down, or when a Watch patrol arrives. A lone attacker who is surrounded drops his weapon and surrenders. The bugbears are here for Ott and have no quarrel with the tavern, and they ignore anyone who stays out of their way.

> [!exploration]**Turning the Gang Away**
>
> The party has three ways to end the night without finishing the fight.
>
> - **Empty cellar:** If Ott is no longer in the manor when the gang arrives, the bugbears break in anyway, search every room for three rounds and leave when they find nobody. They fight anyone who blocks them.
> - **Pay them off:** A character who offers each attacker 50 gp and makes a successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check convinces them to take the coin and leave. The bugbears fear the Master, and they accept only when the party has already beaten one of them or has moved Ott out of the manor. Without either, the check fails and the fight goes on.
> - **Parley after the first fall:** Once any attacker is down, a character who makes a successful DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation) check makes the rest withdraw to the alley.

> [!gamemaster]**The Watch**
>
> If the party includes a member of the Lords' Alliance at Renown 3 or higher (Redknife), a patrol of two **Guards** reaches the front door at the start of the fifth round and the attackers flee. The Guards do not fight and do not count as combatants. Without a Redknife in the party the alley stays empty, because nobody in Trollskull Alley expects to see the Watch before morning.

If the party takes a bugbear alive, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The bugbear sits against the wall with his hammer on the floor and both hands open on his knees, breathing hard through a broken tusk. He will not look at the cellar door.

> [!social]**The Bugbear on the Floor**
>
> A Guild Bugbear Warrior (Chaotic Evil, Bugbear, he/him) :: a hired Guild bruiser who was paid by the day to carry a dwarf out of a cellar and does not want to be paid in any other way.
>
> He answers short questions in short answers, fears the Master more than he fears the party and knows only what the Guild told him. He does not know about the devourers or the zombie.

> [!qna]**Who sent you?**
>
> > "The Master sent us, and I carry things for him, that's the whole of it. He wants the dwarf back, and there's more coming after us, I heard the others say so. I never heard what, and I don't want to."

When the fight ends, the party has the rest of the night to itself. Ott has stayed against the barrel through all of it, and he tells anyone who comes down that the Master will be sending something worse. If Ott is lost, read the following and end the mission:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cart at the end of the alley lurches into motion with the dwarf slung across its tailboard like a sack of meal, and he lifts his bound hands as it rattles away. "Tell somebody to feed the little lord!" he shouts, and then the mule turns the corner and the cart is gone.

### The Day Between

The second day is quiet if the party lets it be. Ott eats everything the kitchen sends down, tells the party that the stew needs salt, and asks whether any of them knows how long a goldfish can go without feeding. If the party marked **Lif Appeased**, Lif polishes the bar all afternoon, and the portrait behind it has a small smile.

> [!exploration]**The Roof of the Bent Nail**
>
> Any character who watches the roofs across the alley during the day and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check sees a slim figure in a dark cloak on the roof of the Bent Nail. The figure lifts a hand, spreads the fingers, counts down on them, and ducks out of sight. A member who marked **Kreb Unmasked** recognizes Fel'rekt from the booking office.
>
> Fel'rekt does not answer if hailed and does not come down. He stays on the roof each night until the third night is over.

### Night Two: The Quiet Table

The hosts arrive at 19:00 and sit at the long table at the back by the hearth. They know within a minute that there is a single mind below ground and that it has not moved, and they wait for the taproom to thin before they act.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom fills early this evening, and the fire is lit against a thin rain that has been falling since dusk. Four people come in out of the wet together in tar-stained leather aprons and heavy boots, and they take the long table at the back without asking anyone's leave. When the barmaid brings four mugs of dark ale, they pay in small coin, lift none of the mugs, and sit without saying a word to one another.

If the party marked **Lif Appeased**, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The candle in the middle of their table gutters and leans every time one of them looks toward the corridor to the kitchen.

> [!exploration]**Four Quiet Guests**
>
> Any character who watches the table for a few minutes and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that these four are not resting after a shift. None of them has lifted a mug, none has spoken to the others and each watches a different part of the room. Any character who looks closely at their faces in the firelight and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check sees that their pupils are as wide as coins in a bright room.
>
> - **Advantage:** The party marked **Lif Appeased**.
> - **Result of 20 or higher:** The character also notices that none of the four has blinked.
>
> Any character who has seen the pupils and makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check recalls that intellect devourers ride the bodies of the dead, sense every mind within 300 feet through walls and cannot be bargained with.
>
> If nobody spots them, they stand together at 23:00, when the taproom has thinned, and walk toward the kitchen corridor in step. Everyone sees that.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True of the Four**
>
> The Dungsweepers' Guild workers at the back table were taken in the old cisterns about a tenday ago by devourers from Nihiloor's stock. A 2024 **Intellect Devourer** that takes a body consumes the host's brain, so the workers who carry devourers died the moment they were taken, and nothing the party can do saves them. The devourers control the dead bodies, which still know the workers' names, languages and routes. Any character who examines a fallen host and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check determines that the host's skull holds no brain and has not for days. The Dungsweepers who carry no devourer are ordinary workers who were told to come to a Guild night out and keep to their seats, and they know nothing.
>
> The devourers are here for Ott and do not use Steal Body on a character who falls to 0 Hit Points. Each devourer senses minds within 300 feet through any barrier, and each one knows there is exactly one mind below ground, and that it has not moved.

The party has four ways to meet the table.

> [!exploration]**Meeting the Table**
>
> - **Move Ott out of reach:** If Ott is more than 300 feet from the back table before 22:00, the devourers sense no mind below ground and keep to their mugs until 22:00. They then walk out together, and a party that follows them sees them climb down a drain in the Dock Ward and vanish into the sewer.
> - **Clear the taproom:** Any character who announces closing early and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check empties the room of everyone but the four. The four remain until 23:00 and make their move in an empty room, and no regular is hurt.
> - **Confront the table:** If the party tells the four that the dwarf is not for sale, the nearest host answers in a dead man's flat voice that Ott belongs to the Master and that the room can leave unharmed if he is handed over. When the party refuses, the fight starts.
> - **Wait for 23:00:** The party can hold the kitchen corridor and let the hosts come to it.

> [!hazard]**The Table at the Back**
>
> Four workers sit at the table, and the roster of hosts follows the number of characters who fight, companions included. The **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** audits the fight for three, four and five participating combatants. A hosted **Commoner** has AC 10, 4 Hit Points and a club, and the **Intellect Devourer** inside has Total Cover until the body falls. When the body drops, the devourer is forced into the nearest open space with its full 28 Hit Points.
>
> - **Three combatants:** one host and three plain **Commoners**.
> - **Four combatants:** one host, one **Tough** posing as the crew's foreman, and two plain **Commoners**.
> - **Five combatants:** two hosts and two plain **Commoners**.
>
> #### The Hosts' Tactics
> At the start of combat, the hosts stand and head for the kitchen corridor, the foreman, if there is one, shouts for the crew to sit down, and the plain workers drop their mugs and back toward the front door.
>
> During combat, the hosts:
> - Go for the cellar door and ignore characters unless they are blocked, and then they strike with Claw and Devour Intellect and move on.
> - Take Ott as a pair, if two hosts survive, or alone, if one does, and carry him at 15 feet to the cart that waits at the alley mouth. Mark **Ott Lost** if they get him into it.
> - Do not use Steal Body on a fallen character, whatever the devourer would prefer.
>
> A devourer flees into the alley gutter when it is out of its host and reduced to 13 Hit Points or fewer, and it never comes back. The foreman surrenders when the last host falls. Plain workers hide, shout and flee, and fight with clubs only when cornered.

> [!social]**The Oldest of the Plain Workers**
>
> A Dungsweepers' Guild Commoner (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: a sewer sweeper in her fifties who does not like her crew's quiet guests and is relieved to be asked.
>
> She answers in long, nervous sentences, and she does not know anything about devourers. She tells a character who stops her at the door or finds her afterward what she saw.

> [!qna]**Who are they?**
>
> > "They came to our crew from the old cisterns about a tenday ago, and none of them has eaten with us since, or taken off their boots, or said a word to us about it. The foreman told us it was a Guild night out and to keep our mouths shut, and I thought it was a bad idea, but you don't argue with the foreman."

If the hosts take Ott to the street, read the farewell from Night One, with the hosts' cart in place of the bugbears' and the hosts walking beside it in step.

### Night Three: The Grate

Night Three begins at the midnight after Night Two. Ott spends the day eating and talking, and as the night comes on he stops talking and sits with his hands in his lap.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Every glass and tankard in the manor begins to ring at once on a single high note that carries through the floorboards and the beds and the back of your teeth, and it stops as suddenly as it began. In the silence a sound rises from below, a long wet grinding with the patter of falling masonry under it, as though something is eating its way through the stone.

If the party did not mark **Lif Appeased**, the glasses do not ring, and the grinding wakes the manor instead.

Characters in the cellar see the grate go. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The iron grate at the east end of the cellar turns grey, then crumbles to a fine dust, and a ragged hole opens in the flagstones with a sound like a wet cough. Something large and pale rises through the dust, a swollen mass with a ring of stalks around its crown and a clouded eye at the end of every stalk. A long tear runs down one side where something heavy has fallen on it, and its wide mouth, full of broken teeth, hangs open. The eyes turn together and stop on the dwarf.

> [!gamemaster]**The Zombie's Reasons and Its Rules**
>
> The Guild sent a 2024 **Beholder Zombie** up the old sewer line to the grate in the cellar floor, the nearest way to the cellar where the Guild expects him to be. It rose with part of the old tunnel on top of it. The **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** audits the fight and flags it as a High-Power encounter. Four changes bring it within reach of 4th-level characters.
>
> - **Damage from the tunnel.** The zombie surfaces with the hit points the roster gives it. Three combatants meet a zombie with 31 Hit Points, four meet one with 62, and five meet one with all 93.
> - **One Eye Ray on its first turn.** It rises through the hole slowly and has only one ray ready for the round it surfaces.
> - **Fixed ray order.** Its Eye Rays go in a fixed order, starting with the Fear Ray, then the Paralyzing Ray, the Enervation Ray and the Disintegration Ray, and then the order starts again. The Disintegration Ray cannot fire before its third turn.
> - **A mindless goal.** It aims every ray and every Bite at a character, never at Ott, because its goal is to pick him up and carry him back down the hole.
>
> The Disintegration Ray deals 27 (5d10) force damage and turns a creature reduced to 0 Hit Points by it to dust, so a character with 27 Hit Points or fewer should take cover before the zombie's third turn. Lif's effects do not work in the cellar. If the party carries Ott upstairs, the zombie follows him up the cellar stairs and into the kitchen corridor, where Lif's effects apply on the ground floor.

> [!hazard]**The Zombie from the Sewer**
>
> The party chooses where to stand. A character in the cellar when the grate dissolves is in the zombie's line of sight on its first turn. A character upstairs has the cellar door and a stairway between them and it.
>
> #### The Zombie's Tactics
> At the start of combat, the zombie surfaces at the east end of the cellar and makes one Eye Ray at the nearest character it can see.
>
> During combat, the zombie:
> - Makes its Multiattack, two Eye Rays, in the fixed order at the nearest character it can see, and Bites a character within its reach instead if one is adjacent.
> - Flies toward Ott at 20 feet. If Ott is where the party found him, against the barrel at the west end of the cellar, he is about 30 feet from the grate, and the zombie reaches him on its third turn.
> - Spends its action on its next turn to lift him if it ends a turn adjacent to Ott with no character between them, and carries him toward the hole on the turn after. A character within reach of the zombie while it holds Ott can make a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check to pull him free, or deal 20 damage to the zombie in one round to make it drop him. Mark **Ott Lost** if the zombie takes Ott down the hole.
> - Stays up through its Undead Fortitude trait until radiant damage or a critical hit lands, so the last few hit points take an extra round.
>
> The zombie does not surrender or retreat. At the end of the sixth round after it surfaces, or the moment it is destroyed, the bands flare and take Ott away. A zombie still alive then loses its goal and drops back down the hole.

> [!exploration]**Keeping Ott Away**
>
> The party has two ways to avoid the fight.
>
> - **Hold for six rounds:** A character who spends an action tipping a stack of casks across the 10-foot lane between the hole and Ott's corner makes a successful DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check and slows the zombie for the round, because it must go around the casks. The zombie is slow and mindless, and a party that keeps it from Ott for six rounds ends the fight.
> - **Hide him:** The zombie has a passive Perception of 9. A character who spends an action putting Ott behind the casks and makes a successful DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check hides him. A frightened Ott squeaks, and a character who makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check keeps him silent. If the character fails either check, the zombie sees him at once.

When the six rounds are up, or the zombie falls, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The iron bands flare a dull red all at once, and the smell of hot metal fills the cellar. Ott squeezes his eyes shut, then opens them wide and looks down at his wrists as the red light climbs his arms.
>
> > "Oh, shit, that's new. Is somebody going to feed the little lord? Please, somebody feed him, he likes the flakes with the red ones."
>
> The light closes over him like a hand, and when it fades the bands and the dwarf are gone, and a patch of warm flagstone is all that remains where he sat.

If the zombie is still alive, it hangs over the hole for a moment with its eyes rolling, and then it turns and sinks out of sight the way it came. The hole in the cellar floor stays, and the Cellarers' and Plumbers' Guild inspector who flagged the old grate will want to see it.

If the party marked **Lif Appeased**, Lif polishes the bar for the rest of the night, and the portrait behind it has a small smile in the morning.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the bands take Ott, or when the Guild gets him first. The second black pouch arrives that afternoon in **Coin Pouches**, and Krebbyg does not come for Ott.

If **Ott Lost** is marked before the third night, the remaining nights do not happen. Ott returns to his bowl in the lair and tells the Guild that Trollskull Manor held him. If Ott vanishes with the bands, he reappears in Xanathar's fish room by morning with no memory of how he got there, tells the Guild he was held in a cellar by humans and does not know where, and is taken by the Zhentarim during the Guild war.

## Overview

A bound dwarf in a hat of dried eyestalks turns up in the cellar of Trollskull Manor, and the party has three nights to keep him there against whatever comes for him.

## Summary

We found a dwarf named Ott Steeltoes bound in iron bands in our cellar, and a flying snake brought a card that called him a guest and told us to keep him from Xanathar's people. Bugbears came on the first night, four silent Dungsweepers who were already dead on the second, and a Beholder Zombie through the cellar grate on the third. On the last night the bands took Ott away, and we do not know where he went or who put him there.
