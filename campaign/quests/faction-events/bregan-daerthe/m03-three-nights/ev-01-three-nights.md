# Three Nights

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat Event runs across three consecutive nights at Trollskull Manor. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the brief from Krebbyg before dawn, with Ott already in the basement
> - Talk with Ott during the days between attacks, and learn what he knows about Xanathar's lair
> - Defend the manor against three escalating night attacks from Xanathar's Guild
> - Capture the Night Two intellect devourer hosts alive and hand them to Krebbyg
> - Keep Ott alive and present on the fourth morning
>
> #### Background
>
> Ott Steeltoes is a dwarf in his fifties, wearing a fish-stained apron and iron restraints he cannot remove. He is not violent and will not attempt escape. He is worried about Sylgar — Xanathar's pet goldfish, whose care requires specific knowledge only Ott possesses — and he is not embarrassed about this. He will ask, on the first day, whether anyone knows anything about goldfish. The question is genuine.
>
> Each evening of the three nights, Krebbyg visits Ott in the cellar and has a quiet conversation with him. Ott, having decided he prefers to continue existing, answers questions about the panopticus blind spots, the beholder's daily routines, and which corridors Xanathar avoids when Sylgar is having a difficult day. He does not know he is providing intelligence for a **Xanathar's Lair** operation. He is simply talking to someone who seems interested. The party does not witness these conversations unless they specifically ask Ott about them afterward.
>
> Xanathar sends escalating force each night. This is not a tactical decision — it is a beholder losing the ability to manage his fear about Sylgar.

### The Brief

Krebbyg does not knock. He waits outside Trollskull Manor's front door before dawn until someone comes out.

> [!readaloud]
> The light outside is still grey, the street empty. Krebbyg Masq'il'yr is leaning against the wall beside your door with his arms folded and a look on his face that says he has been here a while and is trying very hard not to think about it.
>
> > "Right. Good. There's a man in your basement — he's been there since last night. Jarlaxle needs him kept there for three nights and returned undamaged. After that, Jarlaxle will explain everything."
>
> He straightens up and starts walking.
>
> > "He won't, actually. But he'll acknowledge it happened, which is as close as he comes."
>
> He pauses at the end of the step.
>
> > "Krebbyg. If you need me for anything, leave a note at Seven Masks, bookings. Don't use my name. Just say it's about the Tuesday matinee."
>
> And then he is gone down the street.

> [!social]**The Theatre Manager**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Elf (Drow), he/him) :: Bregan D'aerthe operative running the Seven Masks Theater's bookings under the name Kreb Sorrush.
>
> Krebbyg delivers briefs when Jarlaxle needs someone to, and he is prompt and precise about it, which is the only thing he is prompt and precise about. He does not explain the context or what the intelligence operation is for, because he was told not to and also because he is already late for something at the theater. If the party stops him to ask questions, he answers as briefly as possible and adds "Ask Fel" to anything that requires an opinion.
>
> Conversation topics Krebbyg is willing to discuss:
> - The basics of the arrangement: three nights, undamaged, a note at Seven Masks if something goes wrong
> - Ott, whose presence he confirms without explaining how he got into the basement
> - The theater's Tuesday matinee, which is overbooked and is not going to resolve itself

> [!qna]**Who is the man in the basement?**
>
> > "Ott Steeltoes. Dwarf. He takes care of a fish. It's important. Don't ask him about the fish, actually — he'll be there for an hour. Ask Fel if you need more context; I don't have time."

> [!qna]**Why is he here?**
>
> He has already taken three steps down the street.
>
> > "Jarlaxle needed somewhere the Guild wouldn't look. You're not on their list yet. Three nights, then I come back and take him. Simple."

> [!qna]**What if they come looking?**
>
> He stops and turns back, briefly.
>
> > "They will. That's why you're being asked, not just told." A pause. "Leave the note at bookings if it gets worse than you can handle. Tuesday matinee. Don't use my name."

Proceed to **Ott in the Basement**.

### Ott in the Basement

Ott Steeltoes is exactly as described: a dwarf in his fifties, sitting on an upturned barrel in the cellar with iron bands around his wrists and ankles. He is wearing an apron that still smells of fish flakes and an eyestalk hat tilted at a nervous angle.

> [!readaloud]
> The cellar is dim, and the man sitting on the barrel at the far wall is a dwarf in his fifties, stooped and smaller than you expected, with iron bands around his wrists and ankles, wearing an apron that carries the distinct smell of fish flakes and an eyestalk hat that he adjusts when he hears you on the stairs. He looks up. He does not look dangerous.
>
> After a moment, he says:
>
> > "You're not from the Master? Oh, thank the Lady of Rot." He adjusts the hat again. "Is the little lord all right? Is he swimming? Someone needs to be feeding him — the schedule is very specific."

> [!social]**The Fishkeeper**
>
> Ott Steeltoes (Neutral Evil, Dwarf, he/him) :: keeper of Sylgar, Xanathar's pet goldfish, and the only person in the Guild who knows what the fish eats.
>
> Ott is frightened — not ashamed of it, just frightened — and his fear mostly comes out as worry about Sylgar rather than worry about himself. He calculates quickly: Krebbyg visits each evening and asks him things, and Ott has decided that answering honestly puts him in a better position than refusing. He does not know the information he is providing will be used in a heist operation. He does not try to escape, because the iron bands are real and because he is not certain where he would go.
>
> If the party treats him decently — food, water, basic conversation — he becomes cautiously grateful. He does not like the Xanathar. He is very fond of Sylgar.
>
> Conversation topics Ott is willing to discuss:
> - Sylgar's care schedule, feeding requirements, and the precise weight of the bowl (he brings it up himself)
> - The Xanathar's moods and routines, which he describes with the weary candor of someone who has spent years being blamed for things he didn't do
> - His own situation, which he describes matter-of-factly and without self-pity

> [!qna]**What do you know about Xanathar's lair?**
>
> He adjusts the eyestalk hat.
>
> > "I know where everything is, if that's what you're asking. Guard changes, the beholder's route, which corridors the eyestalks don't reach — I know all of it. Six thousand pounds, that bowl, and I'm the one who moves it." He pauses. "I'm not telling you for free. But I'm also not not telling you, if you follow me. What are you going to do with it?"
>
> Ott answers any specific question about the lair honestly, once he's decided the conversation is going the direction he prefers. He is more forthcoming each day, and by the third evening he is answering without prompting.

> [!qna]**About the fish?**
>
> > "The little lord eats on a three-bell schedule. Morning, noon, and an hour after sunset — no more, no less, or he gets lethargic and then the Master blames me. The bowl weighs six thousand pounds, exactly. I know because I helped move it once and I'll never forget." He adjusts the hat. "Is someone feeding him? He's going to be fine. He's fine. He's always fine."

Proceed to the first night.

### Night One — The Muscle

Six **Thugs** and two **Bugbears** attack from the street entrance at midnight. No finesse — they want Ott back and have been told to make noise about it.

> [!readaloud]
> The first warning is the sound of the front door going in. Not a knock, not a lock picked — the door going in, frame and all, in one hard push. Then boots on the common room floor, and a voice that doesn't bother keeping quiet.
>
> > "Bring out the dwarf. Last chance before we go looking ourselves."

> [!hazard]**Night One**
>
> The two bugbears head directly for the basement stairs the moment they are through the door. Four thugs hold the common room to keep the party occupied. Both bugbears prioritize reaching the stairs over fighting the party; a bugbear that gets past the common room requires one party member to abandon the main fight to block it at the cellar door. The thugs will fight to their last hit point but scatter if the bugbears are both down.
>
> The battle ends when all eight attackers are down or scattered, or when a Watch patrol arrives (see below). A captured operative knows only that Xanathar wants the dwarf back and that more is coming.
>
> **Watch Response:** If the party has Lords' Alliance Renown 3 or higher, or has established a cooperative relationship with a Watch patrol in prior play, a Watch patrol responds to the noise after 4 rounds. The Guild operatives scatter when the Watch arrives, though one bugbear will make a final attempt to reach Ott regardless.
>
> The bodies, if any remain, are a scene-setting problem for the morning. Krebbyg, told about the attack: "Expected."

### Night Two — The Watchers

Four **Commoners** enter Trollskull Manor during evening hours — before midnight — and take a table near the back. They order drinks. They are polite and unremarkable. They do not talk to each other.

> [!readaloud]
> Four patrons have been sitting in the same corner for two hours now. They ordered drinks when they came in and have barely touched them. They are not talking to each other — not even a word — and each one is facing a different part of the room.

> [!exploration]**Noticing What Is Wrong**
>
> Any character with a Passive Wisdom (Insight) of 14 or higher notices something off about the four patrons: they are too coordinated, moving in a pattern that is not socializing. Each one is watching a different quadrant of the room.
>
> Each commoner hosts an **Intellect Devourer** that has burrowed into the base of the skull. The devourer is directing the host's movements and using their senses to scout the room for Ott's location.
>
> If the devourers locate Ott — whether through the host's senses or by observing a party member check on the basement — they attack at once. If Ott has been moved to a room not visible from the common room, the commoners finish their drinks and leave. Two days later, two of them return to a different establishment where a party member was observed.
>
> **Identifying the parasites:** A character who grapples a host and succeeds on a DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine) check identifies the parasite at the base of the skull. Removing an intellect devourer requires a targeted magical effect or a surgical extraction that kills the host. Hosts rendered unconscious have their devourers evacuate through the ears within one round and seek new hosts.

> [!hazard]**Night Two**
>
> If the devourers attack, each intellect devourer attempts to abandon its host and reach a new one. The devourers prioritize Ott above any party member. Over the course of combat, any devourer that cannot immediately reach Ott will attempt to incapacitate a party member and use that body to get to him. The battle ends when all four devourers are destroyed or contained, or when Ott is successfully moved out of the building.
>
> **If all four devourers are taken alive:** Krebbyg, given the four living devourers in a sealed container: "Good." He takes them and does not explain why. Tracing the individual devourers backward reveals the internal Guild command chain that authorized the Night Two operation.

### Night Three — The Beholder Zombie

Xanathar's frustration has passed the point where he is managing the situation with any operational sense.

A **Beholder Zombie** arrives at Trollskull Manor's rear after midnight and widens the cellar window first with its disintegration ray before coming through. The sound — a sustained grinding and falling masonry — gives the party one round of warning before it enters.

> [!readaloud]
> The sound starts in the cellar: a low grinding, like stone being dragged, and then a crack, and then falling masonry. One round of warning, maybe less, before whatever made that sound comes through the opening it just made.
>
> In the cellar, Ott looks at the widened window and at the shape pressing through it. He is quiet for a moment, and then he says:
>
> > "He'll send worse if I'm not back in another three nights."
>
> He means it as a statement of fact.

> [!hazard]**Night Three**
>
> The Beholder Zombie searches for Ott by scent and can track him through two floors, with Advantage on Perception checks to locate the dwarf. It moves toward Ott rather than toward the party if given the choice, and it has no orders beyond retrieving or destroying him. If it cannot retrieve Ott intact, it will attempt to destroy him rather than leave him.
>
> At the start of combat, the zombie attempts to press into the cellar to give its central eye room to operate. The cellar ceiling is low enough that the antimagic cone cannot cover multiple targets without the zombie pressing itself against the far wall; it will not fire the cone from inside the window frame, because it needs open ground before it opens the eye. Over the course of combat, the zombie prioritizes clearing space over attacking the party, and will use its eye rays on anyone between it and Ott. The battle ends when the zombie is destroyed or when Ott is moved to a location the zombie cannot reach before its next turn.

### Concluding the Event

Krebbyg arrives at Trollskull's front door at dawn on the fourth morning. He does not knock this time either.

> [!readaloud]
> There is a knock at the front door just as the light turns grey, except it is not quite a knock — the door opens before anyone answers, and Krebbyg steps inside, looks around the common room with the expression of someone assessing damage, and nods.
>
> > "Three nights. Good."
>
> He goes down to the cellar. Twenty minutes later he comes back up with Ott, who has his hands still bound, and the two of them leave without further explanation.
>
> Later that morning, someone notices the note on the bar — a folded piece of paper in a neat, unhurried hand. *Three nights. Thank you. —Z.Z.*

>

Over three evenings of quiet conversation with Krebbyg, he decided he prefers to continue existing. This was a reasonable decision.

## Overview

There is a man in the basement. Jarlaxle needs him kept there for three nights. Xanathar needs him back immediately.

## Summary

A dwarf named Ott Steeltoes spent three nights in Trollskull Manor's basement while Xanathar's Guild sent escalating forces to retrieve him: six thugs and two bugbears on the first night, then four commoners each hosting an intellect devourer, then a beholder zombie through the cellar window. Ott was delivered alive to Krebbyg on the fourth morning. A note on the bar read *Three nights. Thank you. —Z.Z.*
