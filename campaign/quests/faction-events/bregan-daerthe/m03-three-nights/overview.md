# Three Nights: Overview

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available at Renown 4. Level 4 recommended. The Wazoo Affair must be complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 4th level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

Ott Steeltoes is the keeper of Sylgar, Xanathar's pet goldfish. He is irreplaceable to the beholder, who cannot function when separated from Sylgar and trusts no one else to maintain the fish's specific care requirements. Jarlaxle took Ott for three nights to extract intelligence: the panopticus blind spots, the beholder's daily routines, which corridors Xanathar avoids when Sylgar is having a difficult day. He needed Ott held somewhere the Guild would not look immediately. Trollskull Manor was not on the Guild's list of Bregan D'aerthe assets.

Xanathar's response is not tactical planning. It is a beholder losing the ability to manage his fear. He sends escalating force each night because he is frightened, not because he is thinking clearly, and by the third night rational allocation of resources has left the building entirely.

On the fourth morning, Krebbyg arrives at dawn and takes Ott. A note is left on the bar in neat, unhurried handwriting: *Three nights. Thank you. —Z.Z.*

## Involved Characters
- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** (Bregan D'aerthe) — delivers the brief before dawn; collects Ott on the fourth morning; provides no further context
- **Ott Steeltoes** (Xanathar's Guild) — dwarf in his fifties; keeper of Sylgar, Xanathar's pet goldfish; wearing a fish-stained apron and an eyestalk hat; bound by iron bands; terrified and not ashamed of it; will not attempt to escape
- **Xanathar** (Xanathar's Guild) — does not appear; escalates his response each night as his fear about Sylgar overtakes his capacity for measured decisions

## Dangers & Enemies

Three separate combat encounters over three consecutive nights, each at Trollskull Manor:

- **Night One:** 6 Thugs + 2 Bugbears (MM stat blocks), attacking from the street entrance
- **Night Two:** 4 Commoners each hosting an Intellect Devourer (MM stat blocks), entering as ordinary patrons during evening hours
- **Night Three:** 1 Beholder Zombie (MM stat block), entering through the cellar window

## Overview

There is a man in the basement. Jarlaxle needs him kept there for three nights. Xanathar needs him back immediately.
