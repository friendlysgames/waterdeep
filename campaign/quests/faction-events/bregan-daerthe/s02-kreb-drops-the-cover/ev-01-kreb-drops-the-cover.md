# Kreb Drops the Cover

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event occurs once **The Wazoo Affair** (Mission 2) is complete and the party visits or is summoned to the Seven Masks Theater. In this Event:
>
> - Krebbyg ends the fiction that "Kreb Sorrush, booking manager" and "the person handling your assignments" are separate people.
> - He confirms he answers to the same employer as Nevercott, without naming who that employer is.
> - He does not name Jarlaxle. He will not be moved on this.
> - The event sets the stage for **The Betrayal Pitch** (Mission 2b) without revealing Jarlaxle's hand.
> - No checks required. No combat.

The Seven Masks Theater runs a midweek matinée on Tenday afternoons. By the time the show ends, the back room smells of greasepaint and the particular sawdust-and-lamp-oil combination the stage machinery produces. **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** is the only one still there when the party arrives — deep blue coat, hair loose, one hand resting idle on the holstered hand crossbow's grip.

He does not wait for them to sit.

> > "Nevercott paid you for the Wazoo job. Good. Then we can skip the part where I pretend to only book acts."

He runs his thumb along the hand crossbow's grip — once, twice, a habit he does not seem to notice — and then straightens against the costume rack.

> > "You've done two jobs now. Run them out of the Yawning Portal, out of here — wherever Nevercott finds you. Same jobs, same employer, same chain. I'm part of that chain." He tilts his head. "Figured that was worth saying out loud."

This is the admission: Kreb Sorrush the booking manager and the person handling D'aerthe logistics in the Dock Ward are the same man. He has been the face of the operation since Mission 1. He says this without apology and without theater, as if it barely needed saying — which, from his perspective, it did not. Two jobs in, the party had to know something was off about the booking manager who handled casting inquiries and black-pouch payments in the same afternoon.

He does not say who Nevercott works for. He does not say what Bregan D'aerthe's leadership looks like. He has been told what he can say, and he is comfortable with where the line is.

Any character who presses Krebbyg on the employer's identity and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check gets a cheerful deflection:

> > "That's not mine to say. Nevercott will tell you what he wants you to know, same as he always has."

On a result of 17 or higher, he adds:

> > "You've already met the relevant parties. That's as specific as I get."

He deflects because the question belongs to someone else, and he is entirely comfortable with that limit.

### Concluding the Event

#### Kreb Unmasked: True / False

**True:** Krebbyg has acknowledged that the booking manager and the D'aerthe paymaster are the same person, and that he answers to Nevercott's employer. The party understands the theater is an operational cover, not coincidence. Read by **Three Nights** (Mission 3), **The Compromised Eye** (Mission 4), **The Theater's Back Room** (Mission 5), and **The Dive** (Mission 6) — Krebbyg no longer performs the booking-manager fiction in any of these.

**False:** This event did not fire — either **BD Contact Severed** is True, or the mission sequence has not yet reached Mission 2. Krebbyg continues using his theater cover in all interactions.

## Overview

Two missions in, Krebbyg meets the party at the Seven Masks Theater and ends the fiction: the booking manager and the person running their assignments are the same man, answering to the same employer as Nevercott. Another assignment is on its way.

The party met Krebbyg at the Seven Masks Theater. He paid out for the Wazoo job and dropped the fiction: the booking manager and the D'aerthe paymaster are the same person, answering to the same employer as Nevercott. He mentioned that another assignment was coming, and left it at that.
