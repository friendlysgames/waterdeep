# Kreb Drops the Cover

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs three days after Nevercott pays the members at the Yawning Portal for **The Wazoo Affair**, and it plays out for each member who delivered the case and marked **Wazoo Exposé Published**. Helping companions are not invited and do not attend. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Watch Kreb Sorrush take off a human face in the booking office of the Seven Masks Theater and meet Krebbyg Masq'il'yr as himself.
> - Meet Fel'rekt Lafeen, who comes out of the costume cupboard to see how each member takes the news.
> - Question the two lieutenants about the cover, the theater and the rules the faction lives by.
> - Keep Bregan D'aerthe's drow out of the Watch's sight when a Dock Ward constable knocks on the office door.
> - Agree on the dead drop, the signal and the hours that Krebbyg's channel uses from now on.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Nevercott named Bregan D'aerthe at the First Meeting, so the organization's name is not what drops here. What drops is Krebbyg's own cover. Kreb Sorrush is a drow lieutenant who passes as a human stage manager with a brown wig, pale greasepaint and gloves he never takes off. It is an ordinary theater trade secret, and it fools almost everybody in the Dock Ward.
>
> The cover costs him half an hour before every matinee and forces him to hold the act through every conversation with a member. Fel'rekt argued that a contact who must wear a mask while taking messages will eventually slip, so members who have delivered two jobs have earned the face.
>
> From this Event on, Krebbyg is the members' day-to-day contact at the theater for drops, messages and word of rank events.
>
> - He does not brief **The Wazoo Affair**, **Three Nights** or **The Compromised Eye**. Nevercott still briefs **The Compromised Eye**.
> - After **Dinner with Zardoz**, he briefs **The Theater's Back Room** and **The Dive**.
>
> Krebbyg and Fel'rekt both say "the captain" and never Jarlaxle's name. Nevercott is the captain's haberdasher disguise, and both of them know it, but neither will confirm it. If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for a member, Fel'rekt may say the name once, in a low voice, and Krebbyg tells him to wait until they are out of the lobby.

### The Card

The card is slid under the door of Trollskull Manor before anyone is awake on the third morning after Nevercott's payment. It goes to each member who marked **Wazoo Exposé Published**. If a member brings a companion into the booking office, Kreb stays Kreb Sorrush for the whole visit, sells the companion two tickets, and asks the member to come back alone at the next matinee. The Event runs when the member returns.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A playbill has been pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor, folded once, with your name written across the top in a quick sloping hand. It advertises *The Duke's Last Supper* at the Seven Masks Theater in the Dock Ward, with a line added along the bottom margin.
>
> > Matinee at two bells, darling, and the booking office after. Ask for me. K.S.

### The Booking Office

The matinee lets out at four bells. Kreb waits in the doorway of the booking office, a narrow room off the lobby with a schedule pinned to every wall. He keeps the human act up until the door is locked.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The matinee has ended, and the lobby of the Seven Masks Theater is full of people in their good coats arguing about the last act while two ushers sweep programs off the stairs. A slim young man in a velvet waistcoat leans out of the booking office doorway with a pencil behind his ear and a schedule in one hand, and he waves you over before you have crossed half the room.
>
> > Kreb Sorrush, bookings, you got my card, lovely, come in, come in, mind the flat, the paint's wet and the ghost has been at the prop table again.
>
> He shuts the office door behind you, turns the key in the lock, and drops the schedule on the desk. Then he takes hold of his own hair with both hands.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The brown hair comes away in his hands, and white hair falls to his shoulders from underneath it. He drags a rag down one cheek, and it comes away streaked pale with greasepaint over black skin. He pulls off his gloves one finger at a time, tosses them on the desk, and grins at you with obvious relief.
>
> > Oh, thank fuck, the wig itches like a dead badger. The paint takes half an hour before every matinee, and Fel says I could do it in ten if I stopped talking, which I can't. So it's Krebbyg, or Kreb, I answer to both, and I'm a drow, as you've probably worked out. Before anybody shouts, the walls are thin and the soprano's next door.

> [!exploration]**Thinking Back**
>
> Any character who makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check, recalling how Kreb Sorrush behaved in **Coin Pouches** or in the Seven Masks lobby, remembers two things:
>
> - He kept his gloves on in a warm room and sat with the desk lamp behind him.
> - Every time the lobby door opened, he turned his face away before he answered.
>
> On a failure, the character remembers nothing unusual, and Kreb tells them all of it within the minute, delighted to have somebody to tell.

Fel'rekt has been standing in the costume cupboard since before the members arrived. A hinged rack on the back wall of the office swings open onto it, and how each member takes the news decides how he comes in.

If the members stay calm or ask a question, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> One of the costume racks swings out from the back wall on a hinge, and a second drow steps through the gap, tall and lean, with a hand crossbow held low at his side and a velvet cloak over one shoulder. He looks at Kreb first and then at you, and counts something off on his fingers before he speaks.
>
> > Hi, I'm Fel'rekt, and that's Kreb. We're with the captain, as you know, mostly. Sorry about the wig. I told him it would be easier to show you than to explain, and he agreed, and then he talked about the soprano for five minutes. If you have any trouble working beside drow, say so now. We'll let you walk out the front door, and nobody will think less of you.

If a member draws a weapon or moves for the door, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> One of the costume racks swings out from the back wall, and a second drow steps through the gap with a hand crossbow pointed at the floorboards and both palms turned outward. He keeps himself between you and Kreb and speaks fast, in a friendly voice.
>
> > Please put that down. I really don't want to shoot you, and I'd have to explain it to the captain. Kreb, behind me. You came here knowing who sends the cards, and nobody in this room will hurt you, so can we please sit down and talk?

Fel'rekt keeps the crossbow pointed at the floor until the weapon is lowered, then sits on the edge of the desk and carries on. A member who leaves the office without telling anyone has exposed nobody. Fel'rekt lets that member go, Kreb pushes a fresh card under the door of Trollskull Manor the next morning, and the Event runs from the start of this scene when the member returns.

> [!social]**The Stage Manager Without His Face**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: a young drow gunslinger who has run the Seven Masks' bookings as the human Kreb Sorrush, and who is more relieved than anyone that the greasepaint is off.
>
> Krebbyg talks in rapid run-ons that jump from casting to gossip to the ghost, flips through the schedule mid-sentence, and calls everyone "darling", now with a drow's grin. He gets bored with routine and says so. He defers to Fel'rekt on anything serious and answers "What's the plan?" with "Ask Fel". He never lets anyone insult the theater, says "the captain" and stops when Jarlaxle comes up, and can't resist stories about the soprano.
>
> Krebbyg is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Why he wore the cover and why it is off now, which he explains at length.
> - The theater, the soprano and Malcolm Brizzenbright's ghost.
> - What he does for the members from now on, which he hands to Fel'rekt after two sentences.
> - Nevercott and the captain, which he answers with as little as he can.
>
> Specific dialogue for Krebbyg on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Why the disguise?**
>
> Krebbyg waves the wig at you by its fringe.
>
> > Because a drow booking manager in the Dock Ward would last about a day, darling, and the captain wants the theater to last longer. So I put on the paint and the gloves and the wig, and I'm a perfectly boring human called Kreb who can't remember anyone's name. It isn't hard, honestly. Half the lobby is wearing a face on any given night. The wig's a crime against my head, though.

> [!qna]**Why show us now?**
>
> > Because you did the job for Nevercott and nobody got caught, and Fel says that's two jobs, so you've earned a face. Also I was tired of holding the act through every conversation. Sooner or later I'd have slipped in front of the soprano. Ask Fel if there's a better reason.

> [!qna]**Who is the captain?**
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > The captain's the captain, darling. He owns the ships, he writes the cards, and he's the reason I have a job, and that's as far as I go before I say something Fel wouldn't like. Anybody who isn't me can tell you the rest when he decides you should know.
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead:
>
> Krebbyg opens his mouth and Fel'rekt coughs once beside him.
>
> > You know who the captain is, you've stood in his cabin and heard him say it, so there's no point being coy in here. But we don't say the name past the lobby door, or anywhere the soprano might hear, because she repeats everything and gets it wrong.

> [!qna]**What about Nevercott?**
>
> > The hat man? He pays you, he gives you the job, he leaves, and I've never once seen him use a door. I'm not allowed an opinion on Nevercott, so I haven't got one. If you want to ask about his hat, ask Fel.

> [!qna]**Is there money in this?**
>
> > No envelope today, darling, I know, I looked in the drawer twice. Nevercott paid you for the Wazoo business and that's the end of it. Fel keeps the book, so if you think somebody owes you, ask him.

> [!qna]**What about the ghost?**
>
> > That's Malcolm, the founder. He's dead and furious, and he's moved the prop table four times since breakfast. He knows exactly what I am and calls the lot of us the new management, like it's an insult. If you ever talk to him, be polite, because he tells people things when they're polite.

> [!social]**The Lieutenant Who Plans**
>
> Fel'rekt Lafeen (Neutral Good, Drow, he/him) :: a bright young drow gunslinger and the captain's logistics lieutenant, who would rather disarm anyone than hurt them and is never far from Krebbyg.
>
> Fel'rekt speaks in encouraging sentences and counts his options off on his fingers. He checks on Krebbyg mid-sentence, swears in short bursts when excited, and offers a way out aloud whenever something looks likely to go wrong. He is warm toward the members and firm about discretion, because he knows what happens to drow the Watch catches. He never threatens anyone and never mentions Lolth.
>
> Fel'rekt is glad to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who else knows what the company is, and what the rule about exposure means.
> - Whether a member may tell the companions who go into the field with them.
> - The arrangements for the drop, the signal and the hours, which he gives in full once the rules are settled.
>
> Specific dialogue for Fel'rekt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Who else knows?**
>
> > Everyone in the company knows, and the members who've got this far, and as far as I can tell nobody else. The theater staff think Kreb is a nervous human with a bad temper. If you ever hear that somebody outside the company has worked it out, tell me before it reaches the Watch.

> [!qna]**What happens if someone finds out?**
>
> Fel'rekt folds down a finger before he answers.
>
> > Drow in Waterdeep get hunted, and that's before anyone works out whose coin they're spending. The company has been in this city for months without the Watch learning what half of us are, and the captain means to keep it that way. If you tell anyone outside the company, whether a Harper in a tavern or a priest in a confessional, or you point the Watch at us, you're out, and there's no second conversation. I'd hate that, honestly, because I like you.

> [!qna]**Can we tell our friends in the party?**
>
> > Which of your companions you tell is your decision, and I'd keep it to the ones you'd trust with your life. If one of them repeats it where the wrong person hears, the captain counts it against you, because you told them.

### The Constable at the Door

Once Fel'rekt has explained the rule about exposure, Constable Harl Pimm of the Dock Ward Watch arrives for the lamp oil count. He comes on every matinee day, always within a quarter hour of the show ending, and Kreb has forgotten that today is one of those days.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Three knocks sound on the office door, evenly spaced, and a man's voice calls through the wood.
>
> > Sorrush? It's Pimm from the Dock Ward watch house, here for the lamp oil count. Open up, if you would, because I've three more theaters before supper.
>
> Kreb freezes with the rag in one hand and mouths a long curse at the ceiling, and Fel'rekt slides behind the costume rack with the crossbow lowered.
>
> > Fuck, it's Pimm, he's never this early, Fel, the paint, I need five minutes for the paint.

> [!social]**The Constable with the Lamp Oil**
>
> Harl Pimm (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a stolid Dock Ward constable who counts lamp oil in every theater on his route, drinks Kreb's free tea, and has never in his life seen a drow.
>
> Pimm likes to talk about his route, his sore feet and the cost of lamp oil, and he is slow to suspect anything unusual in a theater. He dislikes being kept waiting and tries the office door handle if the delay runs past two or three minutes. He believes the booking manager is a nervous human.

Kreb needs five minutes to put the face back on. The members can buy them in either of two ways, and both are open at once.

> [!exploration]**Buying Five Minutes**
>
> - **Stall in the lobby:** A character who steers Pimm away from the office door and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check keeps him talking about the lamp oil for as long as Kreb needs. A character who asks Pimm about his sore feet or his route has Advantage on the check.
> - **Help with the paint:** A character who stays in the office, helps Kreb with the paint and the wig, and makes a successful DC 10 Dexterity (Disguise Kit) check has him ready in two minutes. Kreb opens the door himself with a cup of tea already poured.
> - **Failed stall:** Pimm tries the handle, and a second member can make the same check once. If no member succeeds, or nobody tries, Kreb opens the door half-painted.

If any member succeeds at either check, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The office door opens, and Kreb Sorrush stands in the doorway in his velvet waistcoat with a brown wig on his head and the greasepaint smooth across his face. He holds out a cup of tea in one gloved hand, and Pimm takes it with a nod.
>
> > There you are, Sorrush. Thanks for the tea. I'll have the count done in ten minutes if the stage door's unlocked, so lead on.

Pimm counts the oil in ten minutes and leaves the theater without looking at the office again. Fel'rekt comes out from behind the rack and nods at the member who bought the time, and Kreb sags against the desk.

If no member succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The office door opens, and Kreb stands there with one cheek still black and the other grey with paint and the brown wig crooked on his head. Pimm looks at him for a long moment, and then past him at the rag lying streaked on the desk.
>
> > Dress rehearsal, darling, you know how it is. The poisoned duke is a fright and I'm doing the makeup myself because the wardrobe woman's quit. Tea?
>
> Pimm accepts the tea, says nothing about the face, and counts the oil in ten minutes. At the stage door he stops to write in his notebook and glances once more at the lobby before he leaves.

If no member bought the time, Fel'rekt and Kreb take it badly, though neither blames the members. For the next ten days the booking office is closed to those members by day. They reach Kreb only through the cloakroom drop and the signal described below, and he meets them at the stage door after dark.

If a member tells Pimm that the booking manager is a drow, or points him toward the office with the intention of showing him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Pimm looks at the member for a moment, then gives a short, tired laugh and shakes his head.
>
> > Sorrush? A drow? Sorrush is as human as my mother-in-law, and he makes me pay for my own tickets, which no drow would do. You want to go and lie down, friend, the matinee's got to you.
>
> He finishes the count and goes without opening the office door. When the hall is empty, Fel'rekt steps out from the rack, and his face has lost most of its brightness.
>
> > That's the one thing I asked you not to do, and I asked you three minutes ago. You'll want to go now. Kreb, give them the door key. Nobody will follow you or hurt you. I'm sorry, I'd really hoped it wouldn't go this way.

Pimm does not believe the member, and the Watch does nothing. Mark **BD Contact Severed** for that member only, and that member leaves the Event. Kreb stays Kreb Sorrush to that member from now on, and the Seven Masks Theater is closed to them as a Bregan D'aerthe contact. The other members continue.

### The Channel

When Pimm has gone, Kreb locks the door again and takes the wig off, and Fel'rekt sits on the corner of the desk to give the arrangements. Every member receives this part, and it is the point of the visit.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fel'rekt sits on the corner of the desk, holds up one hand, and folds down a finger as he starts on each point.
>
> > There are four things, and they're easy. First, hours. Kreb is in this office from nine bells in the morning until the matinee lets out at four, every day the house is open. Ask for bookings when nobody else is in the lobby, and he'll lock the door like he did today. Second, if you can't come in, there's a grey coat on peg seven in the cloakroom that nobody ever claims. Anything you leave in its left pocket reaches Kreb by closing.
>
> He folds down a third finger and glances at Kreb, who is leaning against the costume rack and counting the tickets in his waistcoat.
>
> > Third, if it's urgent, leave half a torn ticket in the same pocket, and Kreb will meet you at the stage door at eleven bells that night. Fourth, when we need you, a playbill goes under your door with a price circled. The number is the bell you come to the stage door. Word that a rank is waiting for you comes the same way, and I'll say where when it does.

> [!qna]**And outside this room?**
>
> Fel'rekt nods toward the lobby door.
>
> > Outside this room, or anywhere somebody who isn't one of us can hear, he's Kreb Sorrush, a human, a bit loud, and you've never seen him without paint. If you meet him in the lobby, buy a ticket and complain about the ghost like everybody else. If you need drow gear at cost, bolts or a crossbow, that's me, and you find me by asking Kreb.

> [!qna]**Will Fel be here?**
>
> Krebbyg answers before Fel'rekt can.
>
> > Fel's never here, darling, he's out doing the captain's business, and today was a special case because I'd have talked you into a coma without him. If you need Fel, ask me, and I'll find him. He always tells me where he is, it's honestly exhausting.

> [!gamemaster]**The Channel**
>
> From the end of this Event, a member who marked **Kreb Unmasked** uses the following arrangements for every Bregan D'aerthe event that names Krebbyg as a contact.
>
> - **Hours:** Krebbyg is in the booking office from nine bells in the morning until the matinee lets out at four bells, on every day the house is open.
> - **Dead drop:** A grey wool coat hangs on peg seven in the cloakroom. A member leaves messages in its left pocket, and Krebbyg clears it at closing.
> - **Urgent signal:** Half a torn ticket in the left pocket brings Krebbyg to the stage door at eleven bells that night.
> - **Call from the company:** A playbill under the door of Trollskull Manor with a price circled summons the member to the stage door at that bell. Word that a rank event is waiting arrives the same way, and the rank event says where it is held.
> - **Public behavior:** Krebbyg is Kreb Sorrush in his cover whenever a non-member is in earshot, and anywhere except the locked booking office or the stage door after dark. The member treats him as a human.
> - **Exposure:** A member who tells anyone outside the company about a Bregan D'aerthe drow marks **BD Contact Severed**. A companion who repeats it counts against the member who told them.

### Leaving the Seven Masks

Kreb puts the wig back on in front of a small mirror above the desk and slips back into the cover as he works.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Kreb pins the wig into place, draws the greasepaint across his jaw with two quick strokes of his thumb, and pulls his gloves on one finger at a time. Fel'rekt slips back behind the costume rack, and the panel swings shut after him.
>
> > Kreb Sorrush, bookings, you must be here about the card, lovely, let me see what's on tonight, and no, I don't know what the soprano's playing, nobody does.
>
> He unlocks the door. A stagehand carrying a painted flat of a castle shouts at him from across the lobby, and he shouts back about the ghost.

### After the Cover

Krebbyg is the same person the members met in **Coin Pouches** or in the Seven Masks lobby, with the greasepaint off whenever the door is locked. He talks as much and defers to Fel'rekt as often as before. Fel'rekt is out on the captain's business most days, so the members deal with Krebbyg alone, and his answer to anything serious remains "Ask Fel".

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the members have heard the arrangements and left the booking office.

## Overview

Two jobs into their work for Bregan D'aerthe, the members are invited to the Seven Masks Theater after the matinee. In a locked booking office they meet the drow behind the theater's human stage manager and learn how to reach him from now on.

## Summary

A playbill with a note on it brought us to the Seven Masks Theater after the matinee, and Kreb Sorrush locked the booking office door and took off his wig. He is a drow named Krebbyg, and Fel'rekt Lafeen stepped out of the costume cupboard to tell us what happens to anyone who gives the company away. A Watch constable named Pimm knocked about the lamp oil while we were still talking, and we had to keep him from the door. We learned the dead drop, the signal and the hours, and we wondered what the captain meant to ask of us next.
