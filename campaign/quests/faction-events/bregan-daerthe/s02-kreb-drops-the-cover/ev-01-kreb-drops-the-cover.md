# Kreb Drops the Cover

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event occurs once **The Wazoo Affair** (Mission 2) is complete and the party visits or is summoned to the Seven Masks Theater. In this Event:
>
> - Krebbyg names Bregan D'aerthe for the first time — the organization, not its leader.
> - He delivers the Mission 2 payment and relays a message: a more interesting assignment is coming.
> - He does not name J.B. Nevercott's true identity or Bregan D'aerthe's leadership.
> - The event closes the door on Kreb's cover fiction and opens the way for **The Betrayal Pitch** (Mission 2b).
> - No checks required. No combat.

The Seven Masks Theater runs a midweek matinée on Tenday afternoons. By the time the show ends, the back room smells of greasepaint and the particular sawdust-and-lamp-oil combination the stage machinery produces. **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** is the only one still there when the party arrives — deep blue coat, hair loose, one hand resting on the grip of a holstered hand crossbow the way another person might drum their fingers.

He does not wait for them to sit. He has the envelope ready.

> > "That's from the management. A hundred gold and a note. Figure out which matters more."

The envelope contains 100 gp and a short printed card: *A more interesting assignment follows shortly.*

Krebbyg watches them read it. He runs his thumb along the hand crossbow's grip — once, twice, a habit he does not seem to notice — and then straightens against the costume rack with the look of someone whose timing has come good.

> > "Nevercott's coming back. A tenday, maybe less. Runs on his own schedule."

He lets a beat pass. His next line lands with more intention than the ones before it.

> > "You've done two jobs now. For us. I figure you deserve to know the name. Bregan D'aerthe." He tilts his head, watching how it lands. "Good outfit to work for. Someone always notices when a job goes right."

He does not explain what Bregan D'aerthe is, how long it has been operating in Waterdeep, or who runs it. He is sixteen months removed from the Underdark and still delighted by the surface habit of expecting explanation as a courtesy. The party has worked two jobs for the outfit already. The name is what they have earned.

Any character who presses Krebbyg on the organization's leadership and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check gets a cheerful non-answer:

> > "That's not mine to say. Nevercott will tell you what he wants you to know, same as always."

On a result of 17 or higher, he adds:

> > "We've been here a few months. Planning to stay a few more. That's as specific as I get."

He is not deflecting out of suspicion. He is deflecting because the question is not his to answer, and he is entirely comfortable with that.

### Concluding the Event

#### Kreb Unmasked: True
The party knows the organization's name: Bregan D'aerthe. This flag is read in **Three Nights** (Mission 3), **The Compromised Eye** (Mission 4), **The Theater's Back Room** (Mission 5), and **The Dive** (Mission 6), where Krebbyg no longer speaks around the name.

## Overview

Two missions in, Krebbyg meets the party at the Seven Masks Theater and names, for the first time, who they have been working for: Bregan D'aerthe. Another assignment is on its way.

## Read Aloud

> The back of the Seven Masks Theater empties fast after the matinée. Krebbyg is the last one there — envelope in hand before you have said a word.
>
> > > "That's from the management. A hundred gold and a note. Figure out which matters more."
>
> He watches you read. Then, with the ease of a man dropping a fact he has been saving:
>
> > > "You've worked two jobs for us now. I think it's only polite to tell you the name. Bregan D'aerthe." He tilts his head. "Good outfit."

## Summary

The party met Krebbyg at the Seven Masks Theater. He paid out for the Wazoo job and named, for the first time, the organization behind the work: Bregan D'aerthe. He mentioned that J.B. Nevercott would be back soon with something more interesting, and left it at that.
