# Kreb Drops the Cover

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs three days after Nevercott pays the members at the Yawning Portal for **The Wazoo Affair**, and it plays out for each member who delivered the case and marked **Wazoo Exposé Published**. Helping companions are not invited and do not attend. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Watch Kreb Sorrush take off a human face in the booking office of the Seven Masks Theater and meet Krebbyg Masq'il'yr as himself.
> - Meet Fel'rekt Lafeen, who comes out of the costume cupboard to see how each member takes the news.
> - Question the two lieutenants about the cover, the theater and the rules the faction lives by.
> - Keep Bregan D'aerthe's drow out of the Watch's sight when a Dock Ward constable knocks on the office door.
> - Agree on the dead drop, the signal and the hours that Krebbyg's channel uses from now on.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Nevercott named Bregan D'aerthe at the First Meeting, so the organization's name is not what drops here. What drops is Krebbyg's own cover. The Kreb Sorrush the members met in the booking office is a drow lieutenant who passes as a human stage manager with a brown wig, pale greasepaint and a pair of gloves he never takes off, which is an ordinary trade secret in a theater and fools almost everybody in the Dock Ward.
>
> The cover costs him half an hour before every matinee, and it forces him to hold the act through every conversation with a member. Fel'rekt has argued that a contact who must keep a mask on while he takes messages is a contact who eventually slips, so the members who have now delivered two jobs have earned the face. From this Event on, Krebbyg is the members' day-to-day contact at the theater for drops, messages and the channel that carries word of rank events. He does not brief **The Wazoo Affair**, **Three Nights** or **The Compromised Eye**, and Nevercott still briefs the last of these. After **Dinner with Zardoz** he briefs **The Theater's Back Room** and **The Dive**.
>
> Krebbyg and Fel'rekt both say "the captain" and never Jarlaxle's name. Nevercott is the captain's haberdasher disguise, and both of them know it, but neither will confirm it. If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for a member, Fel'rekt may say the name once, in a low voice, and Krebbyg tells him to wait until they are out of the lobby.
### The Card

The card reaches each member who marked **Wazoo Exposé Published** on the third morning after Nevercott's payment, and it is slid under the door of Trollskull Manor before anyone is awake. If a member brings a companion into the booking office, Kreb stays Kreb Sorrush for the entire visit, sells the companion two tickets, and asks the member to come back alone at the next matinee. The Event runs when the member returns.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A playbill has been pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor, folded once, with your name written across the top in a quick sloping hand. It advertises *The Duke's Last Supper* at the Seven Masks Theater in the Dock Ward, and the writer has added a line along the bottom margin.
>
> > Matinee at two bells, darling, and the booking office after. Ask for me. K.S.

### The Booking Office

The matinee lets out at four bells. Kreb is waiting in the doorway of the booking office, which is a narrow room off the lobby with a schedule pinned to every wall, and he keeps the human act up until the door is locked.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The matinee has ended, and the lobby of the Seven Masks Theater is full of people in their good coats arguing about the last act while two ushers sweep programs off the stairs. A slim young man in a velvet waistcoat leans out of the booking office doorway with a pencil behind his ear and a schedule in one hand, and he waves you toward him before you have crossed half the room.
>
> > Kreb Sorrush, bookings, you got my card, lovely, come in, come in, mind the flat because the paint's still wet and the ghost has been at the prop table again.
>
> He shuts the office door behind you, turns the key in the lock, and drops the schedule on the desk. Then he takes hold of his own hair with both hands.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The brown hair comes away in his hands, and white hair falls to his shoulders from underneath it. He drags a rag down one cheek, and the rag comes away streaked pale with greasepaint over black skin. He pulls off his gloves one finger at a time and tosses them on the desk beside the schedule, and he grins at you with a good deal of relief.
>
> > Oh, thank fuck, the wig itches like a dead badger and the paint takes half an hour before every matinee, and Fel says I could do it in ten if I stopped talking while I did it, which I can't. So it's Krebbyg, or Kreb, I answer to both, and I'm a drow, which you've probably worked out by now, and before anybody shouts, the walls in here are thin and the soprano's in the next room.

> [!exploration]**Thinking Back**
>
> Any character who recalls how Kreb Sorrush behaved in **Coin Pouches** or in the Seven Masks lobby and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check remembers that he kept his gloves on in a warm room and sat with the desk lamp behind him. The character also realizes that every time the lobby door opened, he turned his face away from it before he answered.
>
> On a failure, the character remembers nothing unusual, and Kreb tells them all of it himself within the next minute, delighted to have somebody to tell.

How each member takes the news decides how Fel'rekt comes in. Whatever they do, the back wall of the office, which is lined with costume racks, has a hinged rack that swings open on the cupboard behind it. Fel'rekt has been standing in that cupboard since before the members arrived.

If the members stay calm or ask a question, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> One of the costume racks swings out from the back wall on a hinge, and a second drow steps through the gap, tall and lean, with a hand crossbow held low at his side and a velvet cloak over one shoulder. He looks at Kreb first and then at you, and he counts something off on his fingers before he speaks.
>
> > Hi, I'm Fel'rekt, and that's Kreb, and we're with the captain, as you know, mostly. I'm sorry about the wig, because I told him it would be easier to show you than to explain it, and he agreed, and then he talked for five minutes about the soprano instead. If you have any trouble working beside drow, say so now and we'll let you walk out the front door, and nobody will think less of you for it.

If a member draws a weapon or moves for the door, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> One of the costume racks swings out from the back wall, and a second drow steps through the gap with a hand crossbow pointed at the floorboards in front of his own boots and both palms turned outward. He keeps himself between you and Kreb, and he speaks fast and in a friendly voice.
>
> > Please put that down, I really don't want to shoot you, and I'd have to explain it to the captain afterward. Kreb, behind me. You came to the Seven Masks knowing who sends the cards, and nobody in this room is going to hurt you, so can we please sit down and talk about it?

Fel'rekt keeps the crossbow pointed at the floor until the weapon is lowered, and then he sits on the edge of the desk and goes on as though nothing had happened. A member who leaves the office without telling anyone has exposed nobody. Fel'rekt lets that member go and Kreb pushes a fresh card under the door of Trollskull Manor the next morning, and the Event runs from the beginning of this scene when the member returns.

> [!social]**The Stage Manager Without His Face**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: a young drow gunslinger who has run the Seven Masks' bookings as the human Kreb Sorrush, and who is more relieved than anyone that the greasepaint is off.
>
> Krebbyg talks in rapid run-ons that swing from casting to gossip to the ghost without a breath, flips through the schedule on his desk in the middle of a sentence, and calls everyone "darling" with a drow's grin now on his face. He gets bored with anything routine and says so, he defers to Fel'rekt on anything serious, and he answers "What's the plan?" with "Ask Fel". He never lets anyone insult the theater. When the captain comes up he says "the captain" and stops, and he cannot resist telling stories about the soprano.
>
> Krebbyg is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Why he wore the cover and why it is off now, which he explains at length.
> - The theater, the soprano and Malcolm Brizzenbright's ghost, which he cannot leave alone.
> - What he does for the members from now on, which he hands to Fel'rekt after two sentences.
> - Nevercott and the captain, which he answers with as little as he can manage.
>
> Specific dialogue for Krebbyg on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Why the disguise?**
>
> Krebbyg waves the wig at you by its fringe.
>
> > Because a drow booking manager in the Dock Ward would last about a day, darling, and the captain wants the theater to last a lot longer than that, so I put on the paint and the gloves and the wig and I'm a perfectly boring human called Kreb who can't remember anyone's name. It isn't hard, honestly, it's theater, half the lobby is wearing a face on any given night. The wig's the worst of it, though, the wig is a crime against my head.

> [!qna]**Why show us now?**
>
> > Because you did the thing for Nevercott and nobody got caught, and Fel says that's two jobs, and you've earned a face. Also I got tired of holding the act through every single conversation, because sooner or later I'd have slipped and said something to you in front of the soprano. Ask Fel if there's a better reason, but I'd say that's most of it.

> [!qna]**Who is the captain?**
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is not marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > The captain's the captain, darling, he owns the ships and he writes the cards and he's the reason I have a job, and that's as far as I go before I say something Fel wouldn't like. You've taken his money and you've done his errands, and anybody who isn't me can tell you the rest when he decides you should know.
>
> If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following instead:
>
> Krebbyg opens his mouth and Fel'rekt coughs once beside him.
>
> > You know who the captain is, you've stood in his cabin and heard him say it, so there's no point being coy in here. But we don't say it past the lobby door, and we never say it where the soprano might hear, because she repeats everything and she gets it wrong.

> [!qna]**What about Nevercott?**
>
> > The hat man? He pays you, he gives you the job, he leaves, and I've never once seen him use a door. I'm not allowed an opinion on Nevercott, and Fel says I shouldn't have one, so I haven't got one, and if you want to ask about his hat you'll have to ask Fel.

> [!qna]**Is there money in this?**
>
> > No envelope today, darling, I know, I looked in the drawer twice. Nevercott paid you for the Wazoo business and that's the end of it, and Fel keeps the book, so if you think somebody owes you something he's the one who'd know.

> [!qna]**What about the ghost?**
>
> > That's Malcolm, the founder, and he's dead and furious and he's moved the prop table four times since breakfast. He knows exactly what I am and he calls the lot of us the new management, like it's an insult. If you ever want to talk to him, be polite, because he likes polite and he tells people things when they're polite.

> [!social]**The Lieutenant Who Plans**
>
> Fel'rekt Lafeen (Neutral Good, Drow, he/him) :: a bright young drow gunslinger and the captain's logistics lieutenant, who would rather disarm anyone than hurt them and is never far from Krebbyg.
>
> Fel'rekt speaks in medium, encouraging sentences and counts his options off on his fingers as he goes. He checks on Krebbyg in the middle of a sentence, he swears in short bursts when he is excited, and when something looks likely to go wrong he offers a way out aloud. He is warm toward the members, and he is firm about discretion because he knows what happens to drow the Watch catches. He does not threaten anyone, and he never mentions Lolth.
>
> Fel'rekt is glad to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who else knows what the company is, and what the rule about exposure means.
> - Whether a member may tell the companions who go into the field with them.
> - The arrangements for the drop, the signal and the hours, which he gives in full once the rules are settled.
>
> Specific dialogue for Fel'rekt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Who else knows?**
>
> > Everyone in the company knows, and the members who've got this far, and as far as I can tell nobody else. The theater staff think Kreb is a nervous human with a bad temper, and that's all they think. If you ever hear that somebody outside the company has worked it out, I'd want to hear it from you first, and not after it's reached the Watch.

> [!qna]**What happens if someone finds out?**
>
> Fel'rekt folds down a finger before he answers.
>
> > Drow in Waterdeep get hunted, and that's before anyone works out whose coin they're spending. The captain's company has been in this city for months without the Watch learning what half of us are, and he means to keep it that way. If you tell anyone outside the company, or let it slip to a Harper in a tavern or a priest in a confessional, or you point the Watch at us, you're out, and there's no second conversation about it. I'd hate that, honestly, because I like you, but that's how it goes.

> [!qna]**Can we tell our friends in the party?**
>
> > The ones who go in with you are yours to decide, and I'd say keep it to the ones you'd trust with your life. But if one of them says it where the wrong person hears, that falls on you, because you're the one who told them. The captain counts it against whoever opened their mouth first.

### The Constable at the Door

Once Fel'rekt has explained the rule about exposure, Constable Harl Pimm of the Dock Ward Watch arrives for the lamp oil count. He comes to the theater on every day the matinee plays, he is always at the booking office door within a quarter hour of the show ending, and Kreb has forgotten that today is one of those days.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Three knocks sound on the office door, evenly spaced, and a man's voice calls through the wood.
>
> > Sorrush? It's Pimm, from the Dock Ward watch house, and I'm here for the lamp oil count, so open up, if you would, because I've three more theaters before supper.
>
> Kreb freezes with the rag in one hand and mouths a long curse at the ceiling, and Fel'rekt slides behind the costume rack with the crossbow lowered.
>
> > Fuck, it's Pimm, he's never this early, he's early, Fel, the paint, I need five minutes for the paint.

> [!social]**The Constable with the Lamp Oil**
>
> Harl Pimm (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a stolid Dock Ward constable who counts lamp oil in every theater on his route, drinks Kreb's free tea, and has never in his life seen a drow.
>
> Pimm likes to talk about his route, his sore feet and the cost of lamp oil, and he is slow to suspect anything out of the ordinary in a theater. He does not like to be kept waiting, and he tries the office door handle if the delay runs past two or three minutes. He believes the booking manager is a nervous human and has no reason to think otherwise.

Kreb needs five minutes to put the face back on. The members can buy those minutes in two ways, and both are open at the same time.

> [!exploration]**Buying Five Minutes**
>
> Any character who goes out to the lobby, steers Pimm away from the office door and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check keeps him talking about the lamp oil for as long as Kreb needs. A character who asks Pimm about his sore feet or his route has Advantage on the check.
>
> Any character who stays in the office and helps Kreb with the paint and the wig, and makes a successful DC 10 Dexterity (Disguise Kit) check, has him ready in two minutes, and Kreb opens the door himself with a cup of tea already poured.
>
> On a failed stall, Pimm tries the handle, and a second member can make the same check once. If no member succeeds, or if nobody tries, Kreb opens the door half-painted.

If any member succeeds at either check, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The office door opens, and Kreb Sorrush stands in the doorway in his velvet waistcoat with a brown wig on his head and the greasepaint smooth across his face. He holds out a cup of tea in one gloved hand, and Pimm takes it with a nod.
>
> > There you are, Sorrush, and here's the tea, and I'll have the count done in ten minutes if the stage door's unlocked, so lead on.

Pimm counts the oil in ten minutes and leaves the theater without looking at the office again. Fel'rekt comes out from behind the rack and nods at the member who bought the time, and Kreb sags against the desk with the tea he never gave away.

If no member succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The office door opens, and Kreb stands there with one cheek still black and the other grey with paint and the brown wig crooked on his head. Pimm looks at him for a long moment, and then past him at the rag lying streaked on the desk.
>
> > Dress rehearsal, darling, you know how it is, the poisoned duke is a fright and I'm doing the makeup myself because the wardrobe woman's quit. Tea? There's tea.
>
> Pimm accepts the tea and says nothing about the face, and he counts the oil in ten minutes and goes. At the stage door he stops to write something in his notebook, and he glances once more at the lobby before he leaves.

If no member bought the time, Fel'rekt takes it badly and Kreb takes it worse, though neither of them blames the members. The booking office is closed to those members by day for the next ten days, and until then they reach Kreb only through the cloakroom drop and the signal described below, and he meets them at the stage door after dark.

If a member tells Pimm that the booking manager is a drow, or points him toward the office with the intention of showing him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Pimm looks at the member for a moment, and then he laughs, a short and tired laugh, and shakes his head.
>
> > Sorrush? A drow? Sorrush is as human as my mother-in-law, and he makes me pay for my own tickets, which no drow would do. You want to go and lie down, friend, the matinee's got to you.
>
> He finishes the count and goes without opening the office door. When the hall is empty, Fel'rekt steps out from the rack, and his face has lost most of its brightness.
>
> > That's the one thing I asked you not to do, and I asked you three minutes ago. You'll want to go now. Kreb, give them the door key. Nobody's going to follow you, and nobody's going to hurt you, and I'm sorry, I'd really hoped it wouldn't go this way.

Pimm does not believe the member, and the Watch does nothing. Mark **BD Contact Severed** for that member only, and that member leaves the Event. Kreb remains Kreb Sorrush to that member from this day on, and the Seven Masks Theater is closed to them as a Bregan D'aerthe contact. The other members continue.

### The Channel

When Pimm has gone, Kreb locks the door again, takes the wig out of his waistcoat and looks at it without enthusiasm, and Fel'rekt sits on the corner of the desk to give the arrangements. This is the only part of the Event that all members always receive, and it is the point of the whole visit.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fel'rekt sits on the corner of the desk, holds up one hand, and folds down a finger as he starts on each point.
>
> > There are four things, and they're easy. First, hours. Kreb is in this office from nine bells in the morning until the matinee lets out at four, on every day the house is open, and if you walk in and ask for bookings when nobody else is in the lobby, he'll lock the door the way he did today. Second, if you have something to pass on and can't come in, there's a grey coat on peg seven in the cloakroom that nobody ever claims, and anything you leave in its left pocket reaches Kreb by closing.
>
> He folds down a third finger and glances at Kreb, who is leaning against the costume rack and counting the tickets in his waistcoat.
>
> > Third, if it's urgent, leave half a torn ticket in that same pocket, and Kreb will find you at the stage door at eleven bells that night. Fourth, when we need you, a playbill goes under your door with a price circled, and the number is the bell you come to the stage door. Anything fancy comes the same way, including word that a rank is waiting for you, and I'll say where when it does.

> [!qna]**And outside this room?**
>
> Fel'rekt nods toward the lobby door.
>
> > Outside this room, and anywhere somebody who isn't one of us can hear, he's Kreb Sorrush, a human, a bit loud, and you've never seen him without paint. If you meet him in the lobby you buy a ticket and complain about the ghost like everybody else. And if you need drow gear at cost, bolts or a crossbow, that's me, and you'll find me if you ask Kreb.

> [!qna]**Will Fel be here?**
>
> Krebbyg answers before Fel'rekt can.
>
> > Fel's never here, darling, he's always out doing the captain's business, and today was a special case because I'd have talked you into a coma without him. If you need Fel, you ask me, and if I don't know, Fel will tell you where he is, he always knows, it's honestly exhausting.

> [!gamemaster]**The Channel**
>
> From the end of this Event, a member who marked **Kreb Unmasked** uses the following arrangements for every Bregan D'aerthe event that names Krebbyg as a contact.
>
> - **Hours:** Krebbyg is in the booking office from nine bells in the morning until the matinee lets out at four bells, on every day the house is open.
> - **Dead drop:** A grey wool coat hangs on peg seven in the cloakroom. A member leaves messages in its left pocket, and Krebbyg clears it at closing.
> - **Urgent signal:** Half a torn ticket in the left pocket brings Krebbyg to the stage door at eleven bells that night.
> - **Call from the company:** A playbill under the door of Trollskull Manor with a price circled summons the member to the stage door at that bell. Word that a rank event is waiting arrives the same way, and the rank event says where it is held.
> - **Public behavior:** With any non-member in earshot, and anywhere outside the locked booking office, or the stage door after dark, Krebbyg is Kreb Sorrush in his cover. The member treats him as a human.
> - **Exposure:** A member who tells anyone outside the company about a Bregan D'aerthe drow, in any words, marks **BD Contact Severed**. A companion who repeats it counts against the member who told them.

### Leaving the Seven Masks

Kreb puts the wig back on in front of a small mirror above the desk, and he starts the cover again as he works. He is Kreb Sorrush again by the time he reaches the lock.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Kreb pins the wig into place, draws the greasepaint across his jaw with two quick strokes of his thumb, and pulls his gloves on one finger at a time. Fel'rekt slips back behind the costume rack as he works, and the panel swings shut after him.
>
> > Kreb Sorrush, bookings, you must be here about the card, lovely, so let me see what's on for tonight, and no, I don't know what the soprano's playing, nobody does, it's a mystery.
>
> He unlocks the door, and a stagehand carrying a painted flat of a castle shouts at him from across the lobby, and he shouts back about the ghost.

### After the Cover

Krebbyg is the same person the members met in **Coin Pouches** or in the Seven Masks lobby, and from this Event on he is the same person with the greasepaint off whenever the door is locked. He talks as much as he did and he defers to Fel'rekt as often as he did. Fel'rekt is out on the captain's business most days and is rarely at the theater, so the members deal with Krebbyg on their own, and his answer to anything serious remains "Ask Fel".

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the members have heard the arrangements and left the booking office.

## Overview

Two jobs into their work for Bregan D'aerthe, the members are invited to the Seven Masks Theater after the matinee. In a locked booking office they meet the drow behind the theater's human stage manager and learn how to reach him from now on.

## Summary

A playbill with a note on it brought us to the Seven Masks Theater after the matinee, and Kreb Sorrush locked the booking office door and took off his wig. He is a drow named Krebbyg, and Fel'rekt Lafeen stepped out of the costume cupboard to tell us what happens to anyone who gives the company away. A Watch constable named Pimm knocked about the lamp oil while we were still talking, and we had to keep him from the door. We learned the dead drop, the signal and the hours, and we wondered what the captain meant to ask of us next.
