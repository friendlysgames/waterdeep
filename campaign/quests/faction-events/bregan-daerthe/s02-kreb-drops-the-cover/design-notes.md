# Design Notes: Kreb Drops the Cover

## What Drops and What Doesn't

The restored draft had Krebbyg name Bregan D'aerthe "for the first time". Nevercott has named it at the First Meeting by then, so the draft revealed nothing new. The event now drops the one thing the members have not seen, Krebbyg's own face. They have dealt with a human booking manager named Kreb Sorrush since **Coin Pouches** or a visit to the Seven Masks lobby, and this is the scene where he takes the wig off.

The restored draft also had Krebbyg pay 100 gp at the theater. That contradicted Nevercott's 80 gp at the Yawning Portal in **The Wazoo Affair**. It also carried an invented "sixteen months" backstory that the Notable Figures page does not support. Both are gone. The event pays nothing, the pouches stay in **Coin Pouches**, and Krebbyg's history is limited to what his page says.

## Choices in the Scene

The draft was a handed envelope and one optional check. The rewrite gives the member four decisions: how to react to a drow in the booking office, what to ask, whether to keep the faction's secret when a constable knocks, and whether to accept the channel's rules. Fel'rekt arrives because the profiles say Krebbyg defers to him on anything serious. The guide's discretion rule also needs someone other than Krebbyg to say it aloud.

Constable Pimm tests discretion. He is an ordinary Dock Ward Watchman with no link to Bregan D'aerthe, and nobody in the company planned his visit, which suits Krebbyg, who never plans. The constable gives the member three outcomes that matter:

- Stalling or helping with the paint costs nothing and earns a standing welcome.
- A poor result costs ten days at the booking office door.
- Pointing at the cover ends the relationship, as the guide says it must.

Pimm laughs the accusation off, so the Watch never acts on it and the consequence stays inside the faction.

## The Cover and the Jarlaxle Gate

The Notable Figures page says only that Krebbyg operates as "Kreb Sorrush". The greasepaint, brown wig and gloves are an invented method. It is a theater trade, and it explains why the cover costs him half an hour before every matinee. If another page gives Krebbyg a magical disguise, this event should follow it.

Krebbyg and Fel'rekt say "the captain" and never Jarlaxle. Nevercott is the captain's disguise, both of them know it, and neither will confirm it before **Jarlaxle Unmasked**. The conditional answer to "Who is the captain?" lets Fel'rekt cough Krebbyg quiet if the member has already met Jarlaxle.

## Why the Channel Is Specific

The restored draft closed by saying a more interesting assignment was coming. A contact the members use for every later event needs a drop, a signal and hours, so the event fixes them. The cloakroom coat, the torn ticket and the circled playbill price are simple enough for a GM to run without prep. The same channel carries word of rank events, which is why the rank events read **Kreb Unmasked**.

## Outcomes

- **Kreb Unmasked** is set per member. It is read by **Three Nights**, **The Compromised Eye**, **The Theater's Back Room**, **The Dive** and the rank events **Soldier**, **Officer** and **Commander**. **Houseless Noble** shows Krebbyg as himself unconditionally and does not read it.
- **BD Contact Severed** is a second writer of an outcome that the First Meeting also sets. **Contact Severed** reads it.
- Cut: The Pitch, the 100 gp envelope and the "more interesting assignment" card. **Three Nights** has no briefing scene.
