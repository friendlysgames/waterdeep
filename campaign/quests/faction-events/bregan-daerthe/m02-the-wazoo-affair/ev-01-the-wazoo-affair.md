# The Wazoo Affair

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Social Event takes place at the Wazoo building in the North Ward during an ongoing dinner party. In this Event:
>
> - Three entry routes give the party meaningful choices about risk and cover.
> - The only real obstacle upstairs is a wandering guest who is absorbed in back issues — manageable with stealth or a light social cover story.
> - The document placement is a skill check that can succeed or partially succeed.
> - The follow-up (Cassalanter solicitors at Rudderbust's door) plays out three days later and is delivered as a scene the party can observe or learn about secondhand.
> - J.B. Nevercott pays in person at the Yawning Portal, two days after publication.

**Background (DM only)**

The document is an exposé: unnamed noble families in Waterdeep's Sea Ward host private religious services to an infernal entity. The language is carefully vague. The information is accurate — this is the Cassalanters. **Jarlaxle** wrote it himself, using intelligence from **Nar'l Xibrindas** inside Xanathar's organization. He cannot publish it under any name that traces to him. Once published, the story will pressure the Cassalanters and force them to redirect resources toward damage control during **Faction Outposts**.

**Gaxly Rudderbust** — freelance journalist, fifties, deliberately disheveled, grey braid, ink stains on every item of clothing. His dinner parties run long and loud. His guests are mostly former journalists, political gossips, and one or two debtors he is successfully avoiding mentioning. He will publish the document without knowing its source, because the story is good and he can verify the core facts independently.

**J.B. Nevercott** will not explain who he works for. He will not explain why the document cannot be sent by normal means. He is cheerful about this.

### The Brief

J.B. Nevercott finds the party at the Yawning Portal — he does not explain how he knew to look there. He orders a round before sitting down. He introduces himself with a warm handshake and a coat that costs more than most merchants earn in a month.

> > "I have a publishing matter. There's a broadsheet in the North Ward called *The Waterdeep Wazoo* — perhaps you've read it. The editor is a man named Gaxly Rudderbust. I need a document delivered to his editorial desk before tomorrow's print run. The document is sensitive. It should appear to arrive without a sender."

He produces a sealed folder from his coat. He names the address — corner of Immar Street and Stallion Street — and the situation: Rudderbust is hosting a small dinner party downstairs tonight.

> > "None of his guests are expecting you."

He names a price. He does not explain what the document says.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### What the Document Says
> The sealed folder contains three pages in an unfamiliar hand — careful, specific, and written to read as investigative journalism rather than a tip. It accuses unnamed Sea Ward noble families of hosting private infernal worship, cites a single anonymous witness account, and includes a physical description of a ritual space that matches the Cassalanter villa's lower temple. Jarlaxle lifted the witness account from Nar'l's intelligence summaries and wrote the rest himself. Everything in the document can be independently verified by someone with access to Cassalanter household staff — which Gaxly will be motivated to find, once he has read it.

### The Wazoo Building

Corner of Immar Street and Stallion Street in the North Ward. A four-story building with the broadsheet's name painted above the ground-floor windows; lights and noise on the lower two floors. Three entry options:

**Front door:** A **DC 13 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion)** check to arrive as late guests who know someone inside. On success, the door servant waves them through — Gaxly runs loose parties. On failure, the servant goes to consult Rudderbust, which puts Gaxly between the party and the stairs for the next ten minutes.

**Back stairs:** An exterior staircase on the building's north side goes directly to the second floor. A **DC 12 Dexterity (Athletics)** climb to the landing, then **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth)** to pass unnoticed by a couple in the back garden who are arguing about something and mostly looking at each other.

**Kitchen entrance:** The kitchen door is propped open for air. A server passes every few minutes. A **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check timed against the server's route clears the entrance and gives unobserved access to the inner stairs.

### The Editorial Office

The second floor is unlit. Gaxly's editorial office is at the far end: a desk the size of a boat, covered in correspondence and proof pages, tomorrow's draft content stacked at center. The document needs to go in that stack, near the top.

**The wandering guest:** A dinner guest has drifted upstairs and is reading back issues in the outer room. He is absorbed and facing away. A **DC 11 Dexterity (Stealth)** check to pass the open doorway without drawing his attention, or a **DC 12 Charisma (Deception)** to explain being up here ("Looking for the necessary" works perfectly).

**Placing the document:** A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check to read Gaxly's filing system and place the document where he will find it in the morning print run. On failure, the document lands somewhere plausible but secondary — Gaxly finds it two days later, slightly delaying publication.

### The Follow-Up

*The Waterdeep Wazoo*, three days after the delivery: a front-page story, anonymously sourced, about certain noble families and private infernal worship. It is specific enough to be alarming and vague enough to be legally unprovable.

Within a tenday, **Victoro Cassalanter** sends two associates to speak with Rudderbust about his source. They are dressed as solicitors and carry threatening paperwork. Gaxly tells the truth: he found the document on his desk and he has no idea who left it. He will say this truthfully under any pressure because it is true. The associates leave without satisfaction.

Jarlaxle is satisfied. The Cassalanters are looking at Rudderbust rather than at him.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Observing the Follow-Up
> A party that is watching the Wazoo building when the Cassalanter associates arrive — or that hears about it from Gaxly afterward — can extract a useful piece of intelligence: the Cassalanters moved fast and used solicitor cover, which means their response apparatus is organized and already in motion. Krebbyg, told about this: "Good. They're burning resources." He does not explain why that is good.

### Concluding the Event

> **[GM]**
>
> **+1 Renown** if the document is delivered successfully and published.
> **+1 Renown (supplementary)** if the party observes the Cassalanter follow-up visit and reports it to Krebbyg. Jarlaxle, when told: "Confirmation is always nice."

## Overview

A publishing matter: plant a sensitive document on a North Ward editor's desk during a dinner party, without leaving a sender.

## Summary

The party infiltrated the Wazoo building during Gaxly Rudderbust's dinner party and placed J.B. Nevercott's sealed document in the editorial stack. Three days later it ran as the front page: an anonymously sourced exposé on infernal worship among certain Sea Ward noble families. Within a tenday, two Cassalanter associates arrived at the Wazoo dressed as solicitors. J.B. Nevercott paid 80 gp at the Yawning Portal and asked nothing about any of it.
