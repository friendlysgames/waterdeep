# The Wazoo Affair: Overview

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available at Renown 2. Level 3 recommended. The Handkerchief and the Girl must be complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

Jarlaxle has been running intelligence operations against all four of Waterdeep's major villain factions since his operation arrived in the city. Through Nar'l Xibrindas, his agent inside Xanathar's Guild, he learned that the Cassalanters are buying gold through a dozen intermediaries and that several of their servants have left service and vanished. He does not know why, and neither does anyone in the Guild; he suspects the charity is a front for something and means to find out what by making the family react. He cannot publish this intelligence under any name that traces to him — doing so would jeopardize Nar'l and confirm Bregan D'aerthe's presence in the city. The document therefore needs to arrive at Gaxly Rudderbust's editorial desk without a sender.

Once published, the exposé will force the Cassalanters to redirect resources toward damage control during **Faction Outposts**, which is exactly what Jarlaxle wants.

J.B. Nevercott delivers the mission brief in person at the Yawning Portal. He describes it as a publishing matter and does not say who the document concerns.

## Involved Characters
- **J.B. Nevercott** (Bregan D'aerthe) — tall, one eye covered by a silk patch, an overcoat that costs more than most merchants earn in a month; the party's recruiter from Trollskull Alley, now bringing a job with a price attached; finds them at the Yawning Portal; pays on completion; is Jarlaxle in another disguise
- **Gaxly Rudderbust** (independent journalist) — editor and sole full-time staff of *The Waterdeep Wazoo*; fifties, deliberately disheveled, grey braid, ink stains on every item of clothing; will publish the document without knowing its source
- **Victoro Cassalanter** (Cassalanter faction) — does not appear directly; his follow-up response confirms the exposé landed and provides a useful piece of intelligence about how the Cassalanters handle public pressure

## Dangers & Enemies
No combat. The Wazoo building has a dinner party running on the ground floor. The risk is detection by a wandering guest or the couple in the back garden. Entry and exit are stealth challenges, not combat encounters.

## Overview

A publishing matter: plant a sensitive document on a North Ward editor's desk during a dinner party, without leaving a sender.
