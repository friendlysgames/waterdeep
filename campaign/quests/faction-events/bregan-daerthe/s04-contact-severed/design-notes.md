# Design Notes: Contact Severed

## One Event for an Outcome Set Elsewhere

The restored draft restated an outcome the First Meeting already set ("Already set in **Trollskull Alley**") and spent its length listing what Jarlaxle would not do. The event now reads **BD Contact Severed** and never marks it. Two other events write the outcome. The **Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting** marks it for a character who gives Sergeant Dunmere their name or attacks Nevercott, and **Kreb Drops the Cover** marks it for a member who exposes the company's drow. This event is the one place that says what the outcome means, so a GM doesn't have to assemble the rule from three files.

## Individual Scope and the Mixed Party

The draft closed Bregan D'aerthe to the whole party after one report. Membership is individual in this campaign, so the event closes the door for the named character alone. A short block covers how a mixed party plays: members brief in private, a severed character can stand beside a member as a helping companion in public scenes, and **Kreb Drops the Cover** governs what a member may tell a severed friend. The block has been intentionally limited to the cases that come up at a table, which are the briefing, the booking office and the rank events. Each mission overview states its own companion rules.

## Permanence

The source says contact ends "for the time being" (`sources/adventure-wdh.json` near line 3600) and "for now" (`sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md` near line 719). The remix guide and `player-factions-overview.md` say permanent. The event uses a rule a GM can apply without prep: the door is closed for the rest of Acts I through III. That keeps the source's softness, since nothing says the closure outlasts the Faire, and it matches the guide for every scene the Bregan D'aerthe events cover. **Vault of Dragons** is named as the first place anything could change, and the event doesn't write that scene.

## Timing

The draft had the watchers vanish "two days" after the Watch visit. The First Meeting has them gone by dusk the same day, with the Watch's letter the following morning, and the event follows that. Nevercott still knocks on Ches 13 for candidates who gave no name, so the event points to the First Meeting for his line to the reporter.

## Three Routes In

A report to the Watch, an attack on Nevercott and an exposed drow produce three different characters. The first two have never heard the name Bregan D'aerthe. The third has held the black card and met Krebbyg without his wig. Each route gets its own opening beat and its own lines from Fel'rekt, and the first two learn nothing the company wouldn't have told them. The exposed member also gets the card back. The loss rule in the First Meeting says the mate refuses it, and a returned card is easier to run than a refusal that depends on the player visiting the ships.

## The Beats and the Choice

The draft had no scene. The rewrite has three.

- **The empty doorway** is a DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check with a stated fallback. A miss costs nothing, and the absence is confirmed by the third evening.
- **The pier** is the moment the door visibly closes. Fel'rekt's profile has him offer reassurance, and the company's lieutenants shouldn't look cruel for following Jarlaxle's rule.
- **The sale to Jalester Silvermane** gives the character something to decide. Selling pays 50 gp and sets **BD Watchers Sold**, and the cost lands at **Sea Maidens Faire**, where Jarlaxle's greeting turns cold. Declining pays nothing and costs nothing, so both options are defensible.

Jalester is the buyer because his page and profile fit a field agent who pays for information and asks one more question than he needs. A Lords' Alliance member gives the account as a report and takes no coin.

## What Was Cut

- The draft said no theater tickets arrive. **Fireball!** sends them to any party that investigates, and a severed character is closed out of membership only, so the tickets stay.
- The draft closed paths in **Sea Maidens Faire** and said that scene would never run a particular offer. The Faire is unconverted and decides its own gates. The event says only that the Faire reads **BD Contact Severed** and that **BD Watchers Sold** makes Jarlaxle's welcome colder.
- The draft's description of how other factions approach the party. Each faction recruits through its own First Meeting, so the event names all six and leaves the rest to them.
- The "Don't Restate First Meeting Content" warning, which the new structure makes unnecessary.

## Outcomes

**BD Contact Severed** is read here and marked elsewhere. **BD Watchers Sold** is new and has one reader, **Sea Maidens Faire** (unconverted). If the Faire is rewritten without a reader for it, cut the outcome.
