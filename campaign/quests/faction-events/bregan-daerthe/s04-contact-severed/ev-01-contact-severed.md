# Contact Severed

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Consequences Event occurs for each character marked **BD Contact Severed**. A character is marked in one of three ways: by giving Sergeant Marek Dunmere their name in the **Bregan D'aerthe First Meeting**, by attacking J.B. Nevercott at the door of Trollskull Manor, or by exposing a Bregan D'aerthe drow in **Kreb Drops the Cover**. The Event reads the outcome and never marks it again. In this Event, the severed character can:
>
> - Confirm that the watchers are gone, or get their card back if they were already a member.
> - Visit Fel'rekt Lafeen's stall on the Faire pier and learn that nobody in the company will deal with them any more.
> - Sell what they know about the watchers, and anything else the company showed them, to Jalester Silvermane of the Lords' Alliance.
> - Ask whether the door can be reopened, and hear that it cannot.
>
> The Event has no encounter. It ends when the character has heard Fel'rekt's answer and decided what to do with Jalester.
>
> #### Who Is Severed and Who Is Not
>
> Severance is individual, like membership. **BD Contact Severed** closes Bregan D'aerthe for the named character only. The other characters keep the status they earned in the First Meeting: members stay members, candidates who deferred can still accept, and companions stay companions. A character who is not marked skips this Event.
>
> #### What Severed Means
>
> The door is closed for the rest of Acts I through III. Nothing in Bregan D'aerthe's events reopens it, and the character cannot buy it back with an apology, gold, a favor or a member's word. **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) is the first quest that may offer the character anything from Jarlaxle's company, and **Sea Maidens Faire** (unconverted) decides what he says to them at the Faire.
>
> - **Renown and rank.** A character who had joined loses their Bregan D'aerthe Renown and rank. The Initiate Benefits end too: the berth aboard the *Heartbreaker* or the *Hellraiser*, the stall at cost and the protection of the response teams.
> - **Other factions.** A severed character with no other faction can still be recruited through that faction's own First Meeting: **Harpers First Meeting**, **Lords' Alliance First Meeting**, **Emerald Enclave First Meeting**, **Order of the Gauntlet: First Meeting**, **A Message from the Blackstaff** (Force Grey) or **Doom Raiders First Meeting**. One-faction membership applies as usual.
> - **How the company sees them.** Jarlaxle has closed his file and treats the character as a civilian variable, someone who may matter to the city but is no longer an asset. If the party raids a Bregan D'aerthe outpost in **Faction Outposts**, the response teams follow the standard escalation with no professional courtesy for this character.
> - **Fel'rekt's dinner.** The invitation to Remalia's on page 09 of the Trollskull Manor guide never fires for a severed character. It fires normally for any member in the same party.
> - **Jarlaxle's briefs and debriefs.** The Jarlaxle exception stands: his cross-quest briefs and debriefs fire for any party that deals with him. **Gralhund Villa** runs his brief for every party. A severed character hears the version for outsiders, and a member in the same party hears the operative version.
> - **The Faire.** **Fireball!** still sends the theater tickets to the drow characters, or to the group, because the tickets invite people to a show and don't offer membership. A severed character who goes to the Seven Masks finds a human booking manager named Kreb Sorrush who sells them a seat. **Sea Maidens Faire** (unconverted) reads **BD Contact Severed** to decide how Jarlaxle treats the character.
>
> #### How a Mixed Party Plays
>
> A party can hold a member, a deferred candidate, a companion and a severed character at once, and each follows their own track. Nevercott briefs members in person at Trollskull Manor at dusk on Ches 20 and tells the others that a fitting is private, as the First Meeting has him do for companions. A severed character can stand beside a member in the public part of a mission as a helping companion and gains no Renown. Briefings, the booking office at the Seven Masks and every rank event are private, and the severed character is not admitted.
>
> A member may tell a severed character what the company's rules let them tell any field companion, as **Kreb Drops the Cover** sets out. If the severed character repeats it to anyone, the cost falls on the member who told them.

### The Empty Doorway

This scene belongs to characters severed in the First Meeting, either by reporting the watchers to the Watch or by attacking Nevercott. It runs at dusk on the day the character was marked. A character who was already a Bregan D'aerthe member when **Kreb Drops the Cover** severed them skips ahead to **The Card Comes Back**.

- **Gave Sergeant Dunmere their name:** The watchers leave Trollskull Alley for the whole party by dusk, as the First Meeting says. Dunmere's constable walks the alley that night and the next and finds no one.
- **Attacked Nevercott:** The watchers stop following that character at dusk on the day of his visit. They keep following any candidate who has not yet given an answer.

> [!exploration]**Where the Watchers Stood**
>
> Any character who checks the places where the watchers were seen (the mouth of the alley opposite the manor, the shuttered doorway and the roof across the street) and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check confirms that nobody is standing there and nobody has taken their place. There are no dropped coins and no scuffed cobbles.
>
> - **Passive Wisdom (Perception) of 18 or higher:** The character notices the absence that evening without looking. This is the same threshold that glimpsed the watchers in the First Meeting.
> - **Failure:** The character sees an empty street and can't tell whether the watchers have left or are hiding. By the third evening no glimpse has repeated, and the question answers itself.

If a character confirms the watchers are gone, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The lamps along Trollskull Alley are being lit as the light fades. The shuttered doorway across from the manor stands empty, with a flower pot somebody has left on the step, and the roofline beyond the alley holds only a pair of pigeons settling in for the night. A cart rattles away toward the Dock Ward, and a neighbor leans out of an upper window to call a child in for supper.

A character who reported the watchers to the Watch also receives the sealed letter from the North Ward station the following morning, as the First Meeting describes. Nothing else follows from Bregan D'aerthe. Nevercott calls on Ches 13 for the candidates who gave no name, and the First Meeting covers his courtesy to the severed character.

Proceed to **At the Pier** once the Faire fleet arrives on Ches 21.

### The Card Comes Back

This scene belongs to a member who was severed in **Kreb Drops the Cover**. It runs at Trollskull Manor on the morning after the scene in the Seven Masks booking office, and it replaces **The Empty Doorway** for that character.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A plain envelope has been pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor while you slept, with your name on the front in a round, careful hand. Inside is the black card with the silver ship, which you were told never to give to anyone, and a scrap of paper folded around it.
>
> > I argued for a second conversation and I lost, and I'm really sorry about it. The berth on the ships and the stall are closed to you now. Please be careful out there. F.

The card no longer works. The mates on the *Heartbreaker* and the *Hellraiser* return it unread if the character shows it, and Fel'rekt strikes the character's name from his notebook of member purchases. From this day on, Kreb Sorrush is a human booking manager to the character, as **Kreb Drops the Cover** states.

Proceed to **At the Pier** when the character next goes near the ships.

### At the Pier

The Faire fleet ties up at the Dock Ward pier on the morning of Ches 21. From then on Fel'rekt Lafeen (Neutral Good, Drow, he/him), the young lieutenant who works the stall for the company's members, is there whenever the ramps are open. The scene runs the first time the severed character comes within a few paces of the stall. If the character never goes to the pier, Fel'rekt never speaks to them and the scene does not run.

If the character reported the watchers or attacked Nevercott, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Among the stalls crowding the pier below the crimson-and-gold ships, one stands a little apart with a few hand crossbows and leather cases laid out on a cloth. A young drow with white hair is draping a second cloth over them as you come near. His bright, eager face goes through several expressions before it settles on an apologetic one, and he steps out from behind the stall with the cloth still in his hands.
>
> > Hi, I'm Fel'rekt. You've probably seen me around Trollskull Alley without knowing who I was. I'm not allowed to sell you anything, or really to talk to you, because the captain says so, and I don't argue with the captain. Nobody's going to hurt you, I promise. We just don't deal with you any more.

If the character was a member who exposed the company's drow, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Among the stalls crowding the pier below the crimson-and-gold ships, the young drow you know from the Seven Masks is laying a cloth over a row of hand crossbows. He sees you and stops with one hand on the cloth. He winces, counts something off on his fingers and gives up on it.
>
> > Oh, shit, it's you. I wrote you a note, so I hope you read it. I'm sorry, I really am, but I can't sell you the crossbows or the lenses or anything else. The card's dead, and I looked for a way around that. There isn't one.

> [!social]**The Lieutenant at the Stall**
>
> Fel'rekt Lafeen (Neutral Good, Drow, he/him) :: a bright, earnest drow gunslinger who sells the company's equipment at cost and has been told to turn this character away.
>
> Fel'rekt is Friendly in manner and firm in fact. He speaks in medium, encouraging sentences, counts his points off on his fingers, and glances at the *Heartbreaker*'s rail because a mate is watching him. He swears in short bursts when he is flustered, never threatens anyone and never says a word against the character. He doesn't name Bregan D'aerthe to a character who never heard the name, and he doesn't mention Jarlaxle to anyone who hasn't been told.
>
> Fel'rekt is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Why the character is turned away, which he explains by the route that severed them.
> - Whether they are in any danger, which he settles at once in the negative.
> - Whether the decision can be reversed, which he answers truthfully.
>
> He won't discuss the company's other members, its ships, or the captain beyond that word.
>
> Specific dialogue for Fel'rekt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Why not?**
>
> If the character reported the watchers to the Watch, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > You went to the Watch about us, and the captain closes the book on anyone who does that. He doesn't hold it against you, and honestly I don't either. Most people would have done the same if somebody stood in their doorway for a tenday.
>
> If the character attacked Nevercott, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > You swung at the haberdasher, and the captain doesn't use people who do that. He didn't even need to fight you. He just walked out through the crowd, which he finds much funnier than I do. It's finished, though, and nothing you say will change it.
>
> If the character exposed the company's drow, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > You know why. You pointed at Kreb in front of a constable, and I told you what that would cost before it happened. I like you, and it doesn't change anything.

> [!qna]**Are we in danger?**
>
> Fel'rekt shakes his head before the question is finished.
>
> > Gods, no. Nobody in the company is going to follow you or hurt you or turn up at your door, and the captain was clear about that. If you stay out of our way, we'll stay out of yours. If we pass in the city, we'll nod and keep walking.

> [!qna]**Can we make it right?**
>
> > No, and I did ask, because that's the sort of thing I do. You can't buy it back or apologize your way in, and a friend standing up for you won't change it either. The captain says a thing once, and anyone who speaks up for you after that gets into trouble, so please don't put anyone in that spot.

> [!qna]**Who is the captain?**
>
> If the character was never told who leads the company, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > He's the one who decides, and I've already said more about him than I should. Please don't ask me again, because I'm bad at not answering and I'd hate to get us both in trouble.
>
> If the character exposed the company's drow, read or paraphrase the following instead:
>
> > You know who he is as well as anybody who's stood in the Seven Masks, and I'm not going to say it out here with half the pier listening. Have a good day, honestly. I mean that.

> [!gamemaster]**Knocking Again**
>
> A character who goes to the booking office of the Seven Masks Theater with the card, a gift or a story finds Kreb Sorrush behind the desk in his human cover. He sells them a ticket and nothing else, and he answers every question with the soprano or the ghost. A member who asks Nevercott or Krebbyg to reconsider on the character's behalf gets a polite refusal and loses nothing. The door stays closed through Act III, and no scene in Bregan D'aerthe's events reopens it.

When the conversation ends, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fel'rekt gives you a small, awkward wave, tucks the cloth under his arm and goes back behind the stall. As you turn away, he starts laying the crossbows out in a neat row again, whistling something tuneless under his breath.

### What You Know Is Worth Something

The severed character knows things about Bregan D'aerthe's watchers that other people in Waterdeep would pay to hear. The scene is the character's own decision, and nobody else has to be present. It becomes available once the character has met Jalester Silvermane at the **Lords' Alliance First Meeting**, whether or not they joined. It closes on the later of two dates: ten days after the character was marked, or Ches 20. If the window passes without the character approaching him, the scene doesn't run and nothing is marked.

Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him), the Lords' Alliance field agent, sits at a table by the stairs of the Yawning Portal in the evenings, or at Trollskull Manor if the Alliance found the party there. He pays anyone for information. A character who already belongs to the Lords' Alliance gives it as a report and takes no coin.

> [!social]**The Agent Who Asks One More Thing**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: a lean, courteous Lords' Alliance field agent in a dark doublet, who writes in a small notebook while he listens.
>
> Jalester is Friendly and a little tired. He asks short, specific questions, gives concise answers, thanks people formally for small favors, and always asks one question more specific than the conversation needs. He swears rarely and only at himself. He pays 50 gp for the account of what the watchers looked like, how they worked and where they stood, and he doesn't ask the character to join anything in return.
>
> Jalester is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the character saw, which depends on how they were severed and is listed below.
> - Who might pay for such watching. He has no answer yet and is plainly interested.
> - The price, which is 50 gp and does not move.
>
> He won't discuss what the Alliance does with information, or any agent's name but his own.
>
> Specific dialogue for Jalester on these topics is presented below.

The account the character can give depends on what they know:

- **Reported the watchers:** The character describes the figures in the alley mouth and the shuttered doorway, the shifts they kept (a pair at night, a single figure by day) and the way the watch ended at dusk after the report.
- **Attacked Nevercott:** The character gives the same account and adds the haberdasher's burgundy coat, the hat box, the card with the Sea Ward address that nobody there recognizes and the faint illusion around his hat.
- **Exposed the company's drow:** The character adds Kreb Sorrush and the Seven Masks booking office, the name Bregan D'aerthe, the captain and the black card with the silver ship. This account costs the company the most.

> [!qna]**What do you want to know?**
>
> Jalester turns to a fresh page in the notebook and uncaps his pen.
>
> > Start with the watchers, if you would. How many were there, when did they change over, and which way did they walk when they left? Then tell me how they dressed and spoke. I'll pay fifty gold for the whole account, and I'll ask for nothing more.

> [!qna]**Why do you want it?**
>
> > Someone put drow on a corner in the North Ward for a tenday, and the Lords' Alliance would like to know who paid them and why. I don't have an answer yet. One more detail, if you don't mind: did anyone ever speak to you before they left?

> [!qna]**Is it dangerous to tell you?**
>
> > It might be, if the people you're describing hear that you talked, and I can't promise they won't. They hear most things in this city. I'd rather you knew that before you decided.

> [!exploration]**What Jalester Wants**
>
> Any character who watches Jalester while he questions them and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** check notices that he cares less about the drow than about whoever pays them, and that he asks twice about anyone who spoke or gave an order. A **result of 18 or higher** also shows that he doesn't know the company's name, and that he writes it down twice if the character says it aloud.

The character decides whether to sell. If the character sells, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester writes steadily while you talk, stopping now and then to ask what color a cloak was or which hand held a crossbow. When you finish, he reads the page back to you, corrects two details with your help, and slides a small purse across the table with both hands.
>
> > Fifty gold, as agreed, and thank you. I'll keep this to myself as far as I can. In your place, I'd tell no one you gave it.

The character gains 50 gp, unless they belong to the Lords' Alliance. Mark **BD Watchers Sold** for the character. Within a tenday Ryvarra's report tells Jarlaxle that a severed character spoke with the Lords' Alliance, and he files the name beside the character's own.

If the character declines or lets the window pass, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester closes the notebook without writing anything on the page and caps his pen. He nods once and tucks the pen into his doublet.
>
> > Of course. If you change your mind, I'm usually at this table in the evenings, though I won't be here forever.

Nothing is marked, and nothing follows. A character who declines pays no price from Bregan D'aerthe or from the Lords' Alliance.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes for each severed character when they have heard Fel'rekt's answer and either sold their account to Jalester or declined. A character who never reaches the pier or the Yawning Portal carries on with the campaign, closed out of the company without a scene to mark it.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**

## Overview

A character who has been shut out of Bregan D'aerthe finds the watchers gone and the company's lieutenants unwilling to deal with them. Someone else in the city is willing to pay for what they know.

## Summary

The watchers who had followed us around Trollskull Manor vanished, and the company's young drow lieutenant told us on the pier that he wasn't allowed to deal with us any more. He said nobody would hurt us and that the matter couldn't be undone, and we believed him. Jalester Silvermane of the Lords' Alliance was willing to pay for whatever we could tell him about the people who had been watching us, and we had to decide whether to sell it.
