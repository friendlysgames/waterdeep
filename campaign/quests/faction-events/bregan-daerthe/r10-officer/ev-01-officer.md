# Officer

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Bregan D'aerthe member first reaches Renown 10, which usually happens as **The Compromised Eye** resolves. The member's contact names the rank at the Seven Masks Theater or aboard the *Eyecatcher*, depending on whether the member has marked **Zardoz Introduced**. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Choose one Uncommon magic item from a fixed table of six and hear what the company's file says about the habit behind that choice.
> - Meet the drow Spy who is assigned to them alone, and decide which of her three tasks she starts on.
> - Receive the nimblewright shipping folio, or the buyer-trace annotations if the party already holds the ledger from **Fireball!**
> - Learn how to ask for one intelligence assessment each quest, with a 48-hour wait and a stated gap.

### The Summons

On the morning after the member reaches Renown 10, a playbill is pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor with a price circled in red. This is the company's usual summons from **Kreb Drops the Cover**, and the circled number is the bell at which the member goes to the stage door of the Seven Masks Theater. Who receives the member there depends on one outcome.

- **If Zardoz Introduced is not marked for the member:** J.B. Nevercott receives them. This covers the usual case, where the rank is reached as **The Compromised Eye** resolves and the dinner in **Dinner with Zardoz** has not yet been held, and it also covers a member for whom that dinner never fires, because they never resolved the mission or refused both of Krebbyg's invitations. The circled number is 9, and Krebbyg walks the member up to the stage manager's office (area P6) behind the stage. If the dinner is still to come, it goes ahead two evenings after the member reports on **The Compromised Eye**, and Zardoz's remark that the haberdasher "has gone back to hats" lands a little later than it would have.
- **If Zardoz Introduced is marked for the member:** Zardoz Zord receives them aboard the *Eyecatcher*, in the captain's dining cabin (area J10). The circled number is 8, the skiff leaves the harbor steps at half past eight with Fel'rekt rowing, and the crossing is the one described in **Dinner with Zardoz**. If the Faire has sailed on Tarsakh 20, the *Eyecatcher* is gone and Zardoz receives the member in the stage manager's office instead, because he has stayed behind in Waterdeep with a handful of the company.
- **If the member never marked Kreb Unmasked:** the playbill still arrives, but Kreb Sorrush hands it over across the box office counter, in his human cover, and the member is taken up to the office through the stage door without another word.

Every qualifying member is invited on their own account and meets their contact alone. A companion who is not a Bregan D'aerthe member may ride in the skiff or wait in the lobby, and gains nothing from the evening. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the playbill waiting at their first lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the next evening at the bell the card names.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> J.B. Nevercott and Zardoz Zord are both Jarlaxle Baenre (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him), in a *hat of disguise* ashore and behind the magical guise of the gilded figurehead afloat. Nobody in this Event says the name Jarlaxle aloud unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member being addressed. Until then every Bregan D'aerthe speaker says "the captain" or "J." Both personas say "the company" for Bregan D'aerthe, and Zardoz never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan.
>
> Nobody in this Event names the Vault of Dragons unless the party has learned of it. The assessment service below has a gap that concerns it, and the speakers describe that gap without a name until the party knows what it covers.
>
> The rank is real and every benefit is the company's own to give. Jarlaxle has read the member's file and means to look at the member himself before he trusts them further, so the six items on the table, the choice among them and the Spy's first assignment are all small tests of how the member thinks. Nothing here can be failed, and the file only gets longer.

### Naming the Rank

If Nevercott is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stage manager's office is a narrow room above the prop store, smelling of candle wax and old paint, with one lamp burning on a desk that is buried under playbills. J.B. Nevercott stands beside it in his burgundy coat with the round hat box hanging from one hand, and he inclines his head to you before you have finished closing the door. A plain card and a folded folio lie on the blotter, and a woman in a grey scrivener's smock waits by the window with ink on her cuffs.
>
> > Good evening, and please sit, because I've been looking forward to this for some time. The company keeps a tally of what its people do, and yours has reached the figure at which we stop auditioning people and begin to rely on them a little. The word for that is Officer, and I should say the reliance is provisional, which I hope you'll take as the compliment it is meant to be.

If Zardoz is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The captain's dining cabin glows with lamplight on gold filigree, and the mahogany table is laid with six small boxes of different sizes on a white cloth, each with a silk ribbon around it. Zardoz stands behind the table in his scarlet shirt with a glass in one hand and the plumed hat in the other, and a woman in a grey scrivener's smock waits beside the clockwork valet with her hands folded. A steward closes the door, and Zardoz sweeps the hat toward the chairs before you have taken a step.
>
> > Officer! Salt and fucking thunder, my darling, do sit down, because the company has made you an Officer and I've wanted to say it aloud to somebody all week! It means we trust you a little more than we did, which is a great deal for us, and there's wine, and there's a woman by the valet you're going to like.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member and Zardoz is receiving them, add the following, in a low voice, after the toast:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets the glass down and bends toward you, and the sailor's roar goes out of his voice and leaves a smooth, light one.
>
> > I read your file myself, my dear, every page, which Krebbyg tells me is not usual for an Officer. Jarlaxle gives the rank, but on this deck Zord says the words, and I'd like you to know that both of us meant them.
>
> He straightens, flourishes the hat, and shouts for the valet to pour.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member and Nevercott is receiving them, Nevercott says nothing different. At the door, as the member leaves, he lifts the brim of his hat an inch and smiles, and nothing else is said.

> [!social]**The Man Behind the Rank**
>
> J.B. Nevercott (Chaotic Neutral, Human in appearance, he/him) :: the courteous haberdasher who stands in for the captain until the captain takes the member over himself.
>
> Nevercott is Friendly and unhurried. He speaks in long, balanced sentences, answers questions with a clear account of what he is willing to say, and never swears. He explains the rank the way a tailor explains a fitting, point by point and in order.
>
> Nevercott is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What an Officer receives, which he describes in the order of this scene.
> - Why the company promotes by observation, and what the file holds.
> - Ilphrin Quiss, and the limit on her loyalty.

> [!qna]**What does Officer get me?**
>
> > Four things, and you'll have all of them before you leave. You may take one Uncommon magic item from the six I've set out, you are given a drow Spy of your own, you receive the folio of nimblewright sales or the annotations to it, and you may ask for one intelligence assessment every quest. Each has a procedure and a limit, and the Spy will explain them better than I will, since she does the work.

> [!qna]**How did you decide on me?**
>
> > Nobody applies to the company, and nobody is interviewed, so the decision is made by people who watch you without saying so. Krebbyg and Fel'rekt write what they see, I read it, and the captain reads what I have read. When the pages are heavy enough, I come and say the word.

> [!social]**The Captain Who Feeds People**
>
> Zardoz Zord (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in the guise of an Illuskan human, he/him) :: the flamboyant captain of the Sea Maidens Faire, who is enjoying this far more than he shows any sign of hiding.
>
> Zardoz fills every pause, answers a hard question with a story or an offer of free tickets, and swears happily in long salted strings. The precise question he cares about is always buried in the middle of a ramble. He never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan, and he calls the company "the company" and nothing else.
>
> Zardoz is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What an Officer receives, which he lists in the wrong order and then corrects.
> - Free tickets, at length.
> - The woman by the valet, whom he calls his favorite scribe.

> [!qna]**What does Officer get me?**
>
> > Four things, my darling, and I'll count them off if the valet stops refilling my glass! First, you pick one of those six little boxes on the cloth, and it's yours for keeps. Second, there's a lady beside the valet who is yours from tonight, and she'll do your watching and your forging, and she's much better at both than I am. Third, there's a folio of nimblewright sales, and fourth, you can ask the company what it knows about anything in Waterdeep, once every quest, and I'll tell you it takes two days and not a moment less, no matter how much you shout.

> [!qna]**How did you decide on me?**
>
> > Well, the file got thick, friend, and Krebbyg reads it aloud to me when I'm eating, which is the only way anything gets read! I heard what you did at the Eye, and I heard what you didn't do, and I asked myself whether I'd like you at my table in a bad week. Here you are at it, so you can see how that went.

### The Spy

Ilphrin Quiss, a drow Spy, waits at the window in the stage manager's office, or by the clockwork valet in the dining cabin. Aboard the *Eyecatcher* she looks like an ordinary human woman to everyone, like every other drow on the ship. In the office she has the grey skin and white hair she has when no one else is watching. Whichever the case, she steps forward when the member's contact introduces her and nods once. She does not shake hands.

> [!social]**The Spy Who Keeps Shop**
>
> Ilphrin Quiss (Lawful Neutral, Drow, she/her) :: the company's operative in the Trades Ward, who has kept a calligraphy shop there for eight months and is now yours between missions.
>
> Ilphrin is polite, patient and exact. She speaks in complete, formal sentences, never swears, and says what she can and cannot do in the same breath. She is loyal to the company before she is loyal to the member, and she tells every Officer so on the first evening so that nobody can say they were misled.
>
> Ilphrin is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What she can do for the member between missions, and what she refuses.
> - The shop in the Trades Ward, and the hours she keeps it.
> - The company's rule that she reports each dusk.

> [!qna]**What do you do?**
>
> > I watch people and places for a day and a night and bring you a written report by the next dawn, I forge or alter one document in a day, or I carry a sealed note anywhere in the city before evening. I take one task from you in every tenday, and I take it in the order you ask. Please tell me which you would like first.

> [!qna]**Who do you answer to?**
>
> Ilphrin folds her hands before she answers.
>
> > To the company, and through the company to the captain, and I report to him each dusk by a Sending the company supplies. I will follow your direction on anything that doesn't touch the company's own affairs, and I won't pretend that limit doesn't exist, since I'd much prefer you learn it from me than by finding out. Within it you'll find I'm good at the work.

> [!exploration]**Calling the Spy**
>
> Each Officer has a Spy of their own, and the member decides which task she starts on before the evening ends. The member names one of three tasks, and the GM answers it in the member's lodging at the next dawn, with no roll.
>
> - **How to reach her:** the member leaves a note in the left pocket of the grey coat on peg seven in the Seven Masks cloakroom, with her first name written on the outside, or calls at her shop in person during the day. Krebbyg carries the note to her before closing.
> - **Watch:** she watches a named person, door or stall for one day and one night, and a written report reaches the member at the next dawn. The GM writes it from the relevant Notable Figures or location page: who came and went, at what hours, and with whom. She will not enter a place that needs more than her cover to enter.
> - **Papers:** she forges or alters one written document in a day. The result passes any ordinary inspection at a gate, a counter or a guardpost, and an official who has a reason to doubt it must make a DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check to find a flaw. It fails a records search and magical verification.
> - **Message:** she carries a sealed note to any person in the city before evening and brings back a spoken answer.
> - **Limit:** one task per tenday, and never during an operation. From the moment the member sets out on a Bregan D'aerthe mission or a lair heist until it ends, she belongs to the captain and reports to him by Sending.
> - **Taking her along:** once each quest, the member may ask her to join one operation that is not a Bregan D'aerthe mission, as a scout. She goes if the operation is not against the company, and she leaves it the moment it turns against the company.
> - **What she refuses:** any order to watch, forge against or follow the company, its ships, its theater, the captain or Nar'l Xibrindas. She refuses at once, in front of the member, and reports the order to Krebbyg that evening. A second such order in the same quest costs the member 1 Renown and benches her for a tenday.
> - **If she dies:** if the member orders her into a fight she cannot flee, the company does not replace her. If she dies by any other cause, the next name on the roster below arrives after a tenday, once.

> [!gamemaster]**Ilphrin as an Ally**
>
> Ilphrin uses the **Spy** stat block, with Darkvision 120 feet and Sunlight Sensitivity added from a drow's traits. Her hand crossbow is drow-made, and she keeps her thieves' tools in a calligrapher's pen case. When she is taken along as a scout, she scouts ahead, signals with a shuttered lantern, and avoids a fight she cannot leave.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, a Spy is a CR 1 ally with Power 17 for a party of levels 5 to 10 and 15 for levels 11 and above. Add that to the party's Power when she is present and recalculate the encounter. She is not counted for any operation she does not join, and she never fights for the member against the company.
>
> #### The Spy Roster
>
> Each Officer gets the next name, in the order the members reach Renown 10, so record the order as each member is named.
>
> | Officer | Spy | Cover |
> |---|---|---|
> | First | Ilphrin Quiss (she/her) | Calligraphy shop in the Trades Ward |
> | Second | Nyrae Zauvir (she/her) | Alterations stall in the South Ward |
> | Third | Dhaer Oussen (he/him) | Counter clerk at a pawnbroker in the Dock Ward |
> | Fourth | Velkyn Hune (he/him) | Tallyman for a grain factor in the Field Ward |
> | Fifth | Ghaena Tormyl (she/her) | Laundress in the Castle Ward |

### The Six Items

A member who has settled the Spy's first task turns to the table. The contact has set out six small boxes, and each holds an Uncommon magic item. The member takes one and the rest are put away.

If Nevercott is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott takes the lid off the hat box, and the six small boxes sit in a row inside it on a bed of tissue, each tied with a different ribbon. He turns the box so that the ribbons face you.
>
> > I've chosen six, because the company reads you as all of them at one time or another, and I'd like to learn which one you believe. Please take whichever you like, and I'll tell you what the file says about it.

If Zardoz is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz waves the glass across the cloth, and the six boxes shift a little on the white linen as he tips it.
>
> > Six, my darling, and don't ask me why six, because Krebbyg wanted eight and Fel'rekt wanted four and we compromised on a number nobody likes! Each of them is an honest little treasure, and each of them says something about you, and whichever you pick says something else. Go on and choose, I won't look, I'll look the whole time.

> [!exploration]**The Fixed Table**
>
> The member picks one item and keeps it. The other five are put away and are not offered again, and the choice is written into the member's file. Attunement and use follow the item's own entry.
>
> | Item | What the file says |
> |---|---|
> | *Cloak of elvenkind* | You have a gift for getting in and out unnoticed, and the file says you're less good at noticing when somebody has noticed you. |
> | *Gloves of thievery* | You take what you need with a careful hand, and the file records two occasions on which you left a trace you didn't know about. |
> | *Eyes of minute seeing* | You look at small things for a long time, and the file says you have asked the right question and the wrong one on the same evening. |
> | *Brooch of shielding* | You stand where the enemy can reach you so that your friends don't have to, and the file counts how many times that was a plan and how many times it was an accident. |
> | *Goggles of night* | You go into dark places more often than the company's own people do, and the file says you have never once asked for a lantern. |
> | *Boots of elvenkind* | You move well and you leave in good time, and the file says you've left three rooms before anyone knew you were in them. |
>
> Any character who watches the contact while the member chooses and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that the six are not a shop's stock. Each was bought for a reason in the file, and the contact is listening for which one the member reaches for first.
>
> - **If two members are named Officer at the same evening:** each makes a choice, and two members may take the same item, since each gets a fresh one.
> - **If the member gives the item away or sells it:** the company does not replace it, and the Spy remarks on it once at the next dawn.

After the member chooses, the contact reads the line for that item. If Nevercott is receiving the member, he says the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott takes the matching note from his waistcoat, unfolds it, and reads from it without looking up.
>
> > About that choice, the file says this, and I've copied it out exactly as Krebbyg wrote it. I'd ask you to forgive the handwriting, which is very bad, and the spelling, which is worse.
>
> He reads the line for the item aloud and sets the note on the desk.

If Zardoz is receiving the member, he says the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz catches up the plumed hat, claps it against his thigh, and unfolds a sheet of foolscap that has been used for something else on the back.
>
> > The file, my darling, the file! Forty pages, mostly gossip and one very good drawing of a gnome, and here's what it says about that very pick, and I'll read it in my best voice.
>
> He reads the line for the item aloud with a flourish and tosses the sheet to the valet.

### The Folio

The contact lays a folio on the table. Which folio it is depends on what the party already holds from **Fireball!**

If neither **Nimblewright Ledger Stolen** nor **Ledger Complete** is marked, and the member was not handed the ledger in Jarlaxle's cabin during **Fireball!**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The contact slides a leather folio across the cloth, four pages thick and bound with a green cord. Inside, the pages are covered in a tight, neat hand, in nine rows, each with a number, a date and a name.
>
> > This is every nimblewright the company has sold, by number, by buyer, by date, by district and by price. Most of it isn't public and none of it is flattering, and I'm told it's the only reliable way of tracing a construct in Waterdeep back to whoever paid for it. It's yours, and please keep it somewhere better than your pocket.

If **Nimblewright Ledger Stolen** or **Ledger Complete** is marked, or the member was handed the ledger in the cabin in **Fireball!**, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The contact slides a single sheet across the cloth, covered in a small pencil hand, and sets a pair of reading spectacles on top of it.
>
> > I understand you have seen the ledger itself, and I won't insult you by handing you a copy. These are the notes in the margin, which no thief has ever seen, and they say where each unit is kept in its buyer's house and when it last left it. They're Krebbyg's, so I'd treat the third column with care.

> [!exploration]**The Records**
>
> The folio is the Officer's own copy of the company's sales ledger, and it is used the same way in either case.
>
> - **What it holds (folio):** nine rows, NW-01 to NW-09, as the complete ledger in **Fireball!** gives them: buyer, address, date, balance and crystal number. NW-03 is marked VOID, and NW-05 to NW-09 stay redacted, because Ilphrin will not discuss any row the company has redacted.
> - **What it holds (annotations):** a column for each buyer in the ledger of where the unit is kept in the household and when it last left the house. For one named unit, a member who uses the annotations has Advantage on the first Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception) check they make to find or follow it.
> - **Tracing:** once each tenday, the member may name a nimblewright by its crystal number, its buyer or its description. Ilphrin reads the answer from the folio before dusk. For any other construct sold in Waterdeep, she takes the trace as a three-day task and brings back the buyer on the third dawn, from the quest that gives that buyer.
> - **Holding it:** the folio is the member's own copy, and a second Officer in the party receives their own. A member who loses the folio is not given another, and Ilphrin will still trace from memory for one more tenday.
> - **If the party has not yet been through the Sea Maidens Faire in Fireball!:** the contact says the folio is "still being copied" and the member receives it from Krebbyg after the party has visited the Faire.

The handing over awards no Renown. The +2 for retrieving the shipping records from the Faire in the Factions Guide is earned only by a member who takes them without the company's knowledge, and this Event does not pay it.

### Intelligence on Demand

The contact turns to the last benefit. If Nevercott is receiving the member, he says the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott closes the folio and sets his hands on it.
>
> > Once each quest, you may ask for a full assessment of any faction, any person or any place in Waterdeep, and you'll have it in forty-eight hours, written out by Krebbyg's hand and mine. It will be thorough as far as the company's reach goes. Where the company has an interest of its own there will be a gap, and I'm telling you so now, so that you won't be surprised.

If Zardoz is receiving the member, he says the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets the glass down at last and holds up two fingers, and then a third as though he'd lost count.
>
> > You ask, and two days later you get a written page on whoever you like, and I won't go faster, friend, so don't bother shouting at the valet! It's thorough, mind, thorough enough to frighten you. Where the company has an interest of its own, there'll be a gap in it, and I mention the gap now so you won't think I've forgotten.

If the party knows of the Vault of Dragons, add the contact's line to whichever speech was read:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The contact tips a hand toward you before continuing.
>
> > And if you ask about the Vault of Dragons, you'll receive everything we have except the one thing I'll tell you now we won't give. I'd rather say so than have you search the page for it.

> [!exploration]**Requesting an Assessment**
>
> The member asks once each quest, at the dead drop on peg seven or through Ilphrin, naming the subject. A quest is one of the campaign's named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**. Events, faction missions and rank events are not quests, and the use is counted against the quest the party is in when the member asks. An assessment not requested before the quest ends is lost.
>
> - **Delivery:** a written page is pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor at dusk, 48 hours after the request, and never sooner. Urgency changes nothing.
> - **A faction:** the leader and lieutenants by name, the faction's outposts and lair by address, how alert it is toward the party, and what it wants from the party this tenday.
> - **A person:** where they live and sleep, whom they meet in an ordinary tenday, whom they answer to, and one fear or debt the company knows.
> - **A place:** its entrances, its hours, who stands guard by day and by night, and one hazard.
> - **What it never says:** the company knows nothing of the Cassalanters but suspicion, so it never states an infernal pact as fact. Manshoon is never named, and the assessment calls the Splinter "the other cell."
> - **The gap:** every assessment on a subject that touches the company's own operations, its ships, its crew or the captain returns with one entry marked "withheld." An assessment on the Vault of Dragons returns complete except for how the vault is to be reached, which Jarlaxle means to use himself.
> - **If the subject is the company:** the contact declines and no use is spent.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Officer earns further Renown from the missions and from the Earning Renown list in the Bregan D'aerthe page of the Factions Guide.

If a member's Renown falls below 10, their Officer benefits are suspended. The Spy goes back to the company, the item stays with the member, and the folio, the assessment and the trace return when the member's Renown reaches 10 again.

### Aftermath

The member may tell their companions what they chose and whom they met. The item stays with the member, and the Spy, the folio and the assessment are individual benefits, so a companion who asks in the member's name is turned away. If the member leaves Bregan D'aerthe or marks **BD Contact Severed**, the Spy is withdrawn the same day, the item is theirs to keep, and nothing else carries over.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has chosen an item, named the Spy's first task and heard how to request an assessment.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **BD Officer** — mark with the recipient's name when the contact names the rank. Record for that member the name of their Spy, the item they chose, which folio they received (full or annotations), the quest of their last assessment, the tenday of their last trace and the quest in which they last took the Spy along. Read by **Commander**, which requires it.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All four benefits are active for this member from the next dawn. **Commander** occurs when this member reaches Renown 25. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

A Bregan D'aerthe contact names a member an Officer and hands over a personal Spy, a magic item chosen from six, the company's records of nimblewright sales and a standing offer of intelligence on request.

## Summary

We were named Officers of Bregan D'aerthe and given a Spy of our own, and we each chose one of six small boxes and heard what the company's file said about us. We took the nimblewright records, and we learned that we may ask for one assessment of anything in Waterdeep every quest, as long as we wait two days. What else is in that file?
