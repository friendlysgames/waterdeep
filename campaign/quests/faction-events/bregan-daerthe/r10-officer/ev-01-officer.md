# Bregan D'aerthe — Officer

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Bregan D'aerthe renown reaches 10 — the next contact with the current active persona, or that persona seeks the character out within a tenday. In this Event:
>
> - Jarlaxle (as J.B. Nevercott, if **Three Nights** has not yet been completed; as Zardoz Zord, if it has) names the character an Officer
> - A drow **Spy** is assigned as a personal operative; the Spy's name is Ilphrin Quiss — professional, quiet, loyal ultimately to Jarlaxle
> - The nimblewright shipping records are handed over, explained, and their operational significance made clear
> - Jarlaxle presents one Uncommon magic item personally selected for the character, with a brief remark that unsettles whoever receives it
> - The intelligence assessment service (once per quest, 48-hour turnaround) is offered and its limits explained
> - No combat; one beat of faction color about how D'aerthe evaluates people
>
> **Contact determination.** If **Three Nights** is not yet complete: run Nevercott in the Seven Masks office, using the established channel. If **Three Nights** is complete: Zardoz Zord summons the character to the Eyecatcher by way of a harbor skiff invitation — an ivory card delivered by a pier runner, no name, just a ship and a time.

### Nevercott (Pre-Mission 4 Path)

The same back office. The same smell of candle wax. Nevercott stands when the character arrives. He has done this every time.

> "Officer. The organization recognizes sustained contribution. This is what that looks like."

He sets a card on the desk. The card has a name on it: *Ilphrin Quiss*.

### Zardoz Zord (Post-Mission 4 Path)

The Eyecatcher's upper salon, evening. Zardoz Zord in a coat the color of harbor water at night, raising a glass toward the porthole before the character has finished climbing the ladder.

> "There you are. I was beginning to wonder if the harbor was putting you off. Sit down — I've been looking forward to this particular conversation."

He sets a card on the salon table. The card has a name: *Ilphrin Quiss*.

> "Officer, in D'aerthe terms, is the rank at which we stop testing and start trusting. Provisionally. You understand."

---

### Ilphrin Quiss

A drow spy, trained in surveillance, forgery, and the kind of patience that makes a talent for both useful. She has operated in Waterdeep for eight months under the cover of a Trades Ward calligraphy shop. She is available between missions. During missions, she reports back to Jarlaxle by Sending regardless of the character's wishes.

> "Her loyalty is to D'aerthe. To me, specifically. She will follow your direction on any assignment that does not place her in a position to acquire intelligence on D'aerthe's own operations. You'll find she is very good at her work and very clear about that limit."

> [!npc-narrative]**Ilphrin Quiss**
> A drow spy, trained in surveillance, forgery, and the kind of patience that makes a talent for both useful. She nods once when introduced. She does not shake hands.

Ilphrin Quiss is present at the end of this meeting, at the door or near the window, depending on the location. She nods once when introduced. She does not shake hands.

> [!note]**Running Ilphrin Quiss**
>
> She is a **Spy** (2024 *Monster Manual*). She follows the character's instructions between missions, performs reconnaissance, forges documents at DC 14, and survives most social encounters that don't require combat. If the party attempts to use her against D'aerthe interests, she refuses and reports the request to Jarlaxle without comment. She does not pretend the limit doesn't exist.

### The Nimblewright Shipping Records

A leather folio, four pages, written in a tight hand.

> "Every nimblewright sold in Waterdeep for the past two years, traced to buyer by date, district, and purchase price. The records were not public. They are now yours."

The records are the only reliable means of tracing any construct sold in the city back to its buyer — a thread that connects to the nimblewright investigation from **Fireball!** and allows the party to map the full network of clairvoyance crystals Jarlaxle deployed. Jarlaxle does not explain this. He watches to see how quickly they understand it.

> "Cross-reference with whatever you found at the House of Inspired Hands, if you were paying attention."

### The Gift

The contact produces a box. The contact chose the box with the same deliberate precision he applies to everything.

> "I selected this one myself. I have found, in my experience, that the right item reveals something about the person who receives it — specifically, that whoever selected it has been paying more attention than they were given credit for."

The item is an Uncommon magic item chosen to reflect something specific about the character: their preferred tactics, a weakness they have been concealing, an ambition they have not named aloud. The GM should select from the options below or choose one that fits, then write the contact's remark to match.

**Suggested items by character type:**
- *Cloak of elvenkind* — for a character who favors stealth and has been noticed doing it once too often
- *Eyes of minute seeing* — for a character who has asked the wrong investigative questions, or the right ones
- *Gloves of thievery* — for a character who has been careful in acquisitions but not quite careful enough
- *Brooch of shielding* — for a character who leads from the front and has taken too many hits to call it strategy

The remark accompanying the gift should be two sentences: what the item is, and one observation about the character that lands harder than expected.

> > "This is a *cloak of elvenkind*. You have excellent instincts for going unnoticed, and poor ones for recognizing when you already have been."

The character does not have to respond. The contact does not require it.

### Intelligence on Demand

> "Once per quest, you may request a full intelligence assessment — any faction, any NPC, any location in Waterdeep. Forty-eight hours. The assessment is thorough."

He pauses.

> "Thorough within D'aerthe's reach. If you ask about the Vault of Dragons, you will receive a great deal of useful information and one significant gap. I mention this so you are not surprised by the gap."

The service is available beginning the next quest. Requests go through Krebbyg or Ilphrin Quiss. The turnaround is always 48 hours, regardless of urgency.

> [!profile]**Profile: The Officer Meeting**
>
> ***Faction color.*** D'aerthe promotes by observation. No one applies. No one interviews. The contact either knows enough to offer the rank or does not, and the character learns which when the card appears on the table. The gift is the tell: someone has been cataloguing them in detail. This should be the moment the party stops thinking of Bregan D'aerthe as an employer and starts thinking of it as a faction with a file on each of them.
