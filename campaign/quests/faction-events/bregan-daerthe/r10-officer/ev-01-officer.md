# Officer

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when a Bregan D'aerthe member first reaches Renown 10, which usually happens as **The Compromised Eye** resolves. The member's contact names the rank at the Seven Masks Theater or aboard the *Eyecatcher*, depending on whether the member has marked **Zardoz Introduced**. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Choose one Uncommon magic item from a fixed table of six and hear what the company's file says about the habit behind that choice.
> - Meet the drow Spy assigned to them alone, and pick which of her three tasks she starts on.
> - Receive the nimblewright shipping folio, or the buyer-trace annotations if the party already holds the ledger from **Fireball!**
> - Learn how to ask for one intelligence assessment each quest, with a 48-hour wait and a stated gap.

### The Summons

Hold this Event until **BD Soldier** is marked for the member. On the morning after the member reaches Renown 10, a playbill with a price circled in red is pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor. This is the company's usual summons from **Kreb Drops the Cover**. The circled number is the hour at which the member goes to the stage door of the Seven Masks Theater. Who receives the member depends on one outcome.

- **If Zardoz Introduced is not marked for the member:** J.B. Nevercott receives them. This is the usual case, since the dinner in **Dinner with Zardoz** has not been held yet. It also covers a member for whom the dinner never fires, because they never resolved the mission or refused both of Krebbyg's invitations. The circled number is 9, and Krebbyg walks the member up to the stage manager's office (area P6) behind the stage. If the dinner is still to come, it goes ahead two evenings after the member reports on **The Compromised Eye**, and Zardoz's remark that the haberdasher "has gone back to hats" lands later than usual.
- **If Zardoz Introduced is marked for the member:** Zardoz Zord receives them aboard the *Eyecatcher*, in the captain's dining cabin (area J10). The circled number is 8, and the skiff leaves the harbor steps at half past eight with Fel'rekt rowing, as in **Dinner with Zardoz**. If the Faire has sailed on Tarsakh 20, Zardoz receives the member in the hired lighter moored at the end of the Dock Ward piers, the one used in **Commander**. He has stayed behind in Waterdeep with a handful of the company. The circled number and the skiff are the same.
- **If the member never marked Kreb Unmasked:** Kreb Sorrush hands over the playbill across the box office counter in his human cover, then takes the member up to the office through the stage door without another word.

Every qualifying member is invited separately and meets their contact alone. A companion who is not a Bregan D'aerthe member may ride in the skiff or wait in the lobby, and gains nothing. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the playbill at their first lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the next evening at the hour the card names.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> J.B. Nevercott and Zardoz Zord are both Jarlaxle Baenre (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him), in a *hat of disguise* ashore and behind the magical guise of the gilded figurehead afloat. Nobody in this Event says the name Jarlaxle aloud unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member being addressed. Until then every Bregan D'aerthe speaker says "the captain" or "J." Both personas call Bregan D'aerthe "the company," and Zardoz never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan.
>
> Nobody in this Event names the Vault of Dragons unless the party has learned of it. The assessment service has a gap that concerns it, and the speakers describe the gap without a name until the party knows what it covers.
>
> The rank is real, and every benefit is the company's to give. Jarlaxle has read the member's file and wants to watch the member himself before he trusts them further. The six items, the choice among them and the Spy's first assignment are small tests of how the member thinks. Nothing here can be failed, and the file only gets longer.

### Naming the Rank

If Nevercott is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stage manager's office is a narrow room above the prop store, smelling of candle wax and old paint. One lamp burns on a desk buried under playbills, and a plain card and a folded folio lie on the blotter. J.B. Nevercott stands beside it in his burgundy coat with a round hat box in one hand, and he nods to you as you close the door. A woman in a grey scrivener's smock waits by the window with ink on her cuffs.
>
> > Good evening, and please sit. I've been looking forward to this. The company keeps a tally of what its people do, and yours has reached the figure where we stop auditioning and start relying on you. The word for that is Officer. The reliance is provisional, and I hope you'll take that as a compliment.

If Zardoz is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Lamplight glows on the gold filigree of the captain's dining cabin. Six small boxes of different sizes, each tied with a silk ribbon, sit on a white cloth on the mahogany table. Zardoz stands behind it in his scarlet shirt with a glass in one hand and the plumed hat in the other. A woman in a grey scrivener's smock waits beside the clockwork valet with her hands folded. A steward closes the door, and Zardoz sweeps the hat toward the chairs.
>
> > Officer! Salt and fucking thunder, my darling, sit down! The company has made you an Officer, and I've wanted to say it aloud to somebody all week! It means we trust you a little more than before, which is a great deal for us. There's wine, and there's a woman by the valet you're going to like!

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member and Zardoz is receiving them, add the following, in a low voice, after the toast:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets the glass down and bends toward you, and the sailor's roar leaves his voice. The one that replaces it is smooth and light.
>
> > I read your file myself, my dear, every page, which Krebbyg tells me is not usual for an Officer. Jarlaxle gives the rank, but on this deck Zord says the words, and we both meant them.
>
> He straightens, flourishes the hat, and shouts for the valet to pour.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member and Nevercott is receiving them, he says nothing different. As the member leaves, he lifts the brim of his hat an inch and smiles.

> [!social]**The Man Behind the Rank**
>
> J.B. Nevercott (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in a hat of disguise, he/him) :: the courteous haberdasher who stands in for the captain until the captain takes the member over himself.
>
> Nevercott is Friendly and unhurried. He speaks in balanced sentences, says plainly what he will and won't discuss, and never swears. He explains the rank point by point, like a tailor explaining a fitting.
>
> Nevercott is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What an Officer receives, in the order of this scene.
> - Why the company promotes by observation, and what the file holds.
> - Ilphrin Quiss, and the limit on her loyalty.

> [!qna]**What does Officer get me?**
>
> > Four things, and you'll have all of them before you leave. You may take one Uncommon magic item from the six I've set out. You're given a drow Spy of your own, and you receive the folio of nimblewright sales or its annotations. And once every quest you may ask for one intelligence assessment. The Spy will explain the limits better than I can, since she does the work.

> [!qna]**How did you decide on me?**
>
> > Nobody applies to the company, and nobody is interviewed. People watch you without saying so. Krebbyg and Fel'rekt write what they see, I read it, and the captain reads what I've read. When the pages are heavy enough, I come and say the word.

> [!social]**The Captain Who Feeds People**
>
> Zardoz Zord (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in the guise of an Illuskan human, he/him) :: the flamboyant captain of the Sea Maidens Faire, who is openly enjoying himself.
>
> Zardoz fills every pause, answers a hard question with a story or an offer of free tickets, and swears happily. The one question he cares about is buried in the middle of a ramble. He never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan, and he calls Bregan D'aerthe "the company."
>
> Zardoz is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What an Officer receives, which he lists in the wrong order and then corrects.
> - Free tickets, at length.
> - The woman by the valet, whom he calls his favorite scribe.

> [!qna]**What does Officer get me?**
>
> > Four things, my darling, and I'll count them off if the valet stops refilling my glass! First, you pick one of those six little boxes, and it's yours for keeps. Second, the lady by the valet is yours from tonight, and she does your watching and your forging better than I do. Third, there's a folio of nimblewright sales. Fourth, you can ask the company about anything in Waterdeep once every quest, and it takes two days, no matter how much you shout!

> [!qna]**How did you decide on me?**
>
> > Well, the file got thick, friend, and Krebbyg reads it to me while I eat, which is the only way anything gets read! I heard what you did at the Eye and what you didn't do. Then I asked myself whether I'd want you at my table in a bad week. Here you are, so that went well!

### The Spy

Ilphrin Quiss, a drow Spy, waits at the window in the stage manager's office, by the clockwork valet in the dining cabin, or by the porthole in the lighter's cabin. Aboard the *Eyecatcher* she looks like an ordinary human woman, as every drow on the ship does. In the office or on the lighter, where there is no figurehead, she shows her grey skin and white hair. She steps forward when the contact introduces her and nods once. She does not shake hands.

> [!social]**The Spy Who Keeps Shop**
>
> Ilphrin Quiss (Lawful Neutral, Drow, she/her) :: the company's operative in the Trades Ward, who has kept a calligraphy shop there for eight months and is now yours between missions.
>
> Ilphrin is polite, patient and exact. She speaks in complete, formal sentences, never swears, and states what she can and cannot do together. She is loyal to the company before the member, and she tells every Officer so on the first evening.
>
> Ilphrin is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What she can do for the member between missions, and what she refuses.
> - The shop in the Trades Ward, and the hours she keeps it.
> - The company's rule that she reports each dusk.

> [!qna]**What do you do?**
>
> > I can watch a person or place for a day and a night and bring you a written report at dawn. I can forge or alter one document in a day. Or I can carry a sealed note anywhere in the city before evening. I take one task from you each tenday. Please tell me which you would like first.

> [!qna]**Who do you answer to?**
>
> Ilphrin folds her hands before she answers.
>
> > To the company, and through it to the captain. I report to him each dusk by a Sending the company supplies. I'll follow your direction on anything that doesn't touch the company's own affairs. I'd rather you hear that limit from me than discover it. Within it, I'm good at the work.

> [!exploration]**Calling the Spy**
>
> Each Officer has a Spy of their own. Before the evening ends, the member names which of her three tasks she starts on. The GM delivers the result at the member's lodging at the next dawn, with no roll.
>
> - **How to reach her:** the member leaves a note in the left pocket of the grey coat on peg seven in the Seven Masks cloakroom, with her first name on the outside, or calls at her shop by day. Krebbyg carries the note to her before closing.
> - **Watch:** she watches a named person, door or stall for one day and one night. A written report reaches the member at the next dawn. The GM writes it from the relevant Notable Figures or location page: who came and went, at what hours, and with whom. She won't enter a place that needs more than her cover.
> - **Papers:** she forges or alters one written document in a day. It passes any ordinary inspection at a gate, counter or guardpost. An official with a reason to doubt it must make a DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check to find a flaw. It fails a records search and magical verification.
> - **Message:** she carries a sealed note to any person in the city before evening and brings back a spoken answer.
> - **Limit:** one task per tenday, and never during an operation. From the moment the member sets out on a Bregan D'aerthe mission or a lair heist until it ends, she belongs to the captain and reports to him by Sending.
> - **Taking her along:** once each quest, the member may ask her to join one operation that is not a Bregan D'aerthe mission, as a scout. She leaves the moment the operation turns against the company.
> - **What she refuses:** any order to watch, forge against or follow the company, its ships, its theater, the captain or Nar'l Xibrindas. She refuses at once, in front of the member, and reports the order to Krebbyg that evening. A second such order in the same quest costs the member 1 Renown and benches her for a tenday.
> - **If she dies:** if the member orders her into a fight she cannot flee, the company does not replace her. If she dies any other way, the next name on the roster below arrives after a tenday, once.

> [!gamemaster]**Ilphrin as an Ally**
>
> Ilphrin uses the **Spy** stat block, with Darkvision 120 feet and Sunlight Sensitivity added from a drow's traits. Her hand crossbow is drow-made, and her thieves' tools are in a calligrapher's pen case. As a scout she goes ahead, signals with a shuttered lantern, and avoids a fight she cannot leave.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, a Spy is a CR 1 ally with Power 17 for a party of levels 5 to 10 and 15 for levels 11 and above. Add that to the party's Power when she is present and recalculate the encounter. She is not counted for any operation she does not join, and she never fights the company.
>

>
> | Officer | Spy | Cover |
> |---|---|---|
> | First | Ilphrin Quiss (she/her) | Calligraphy shop in the Trades Ward |
> | Second | Nyrae Zauvir (she/her) | Alterations stall in the South Ward |
> | Third | Dhaer Oussen (he/him) | Counter clerk at a pawnbroker in the Dock Ward |

### The Six Items

Once the member has named the Spy's first task, they turn to the table. The contact has set out six small boxes, each holding an Uncommon magic item. The member takes one, and the rest are put away.

If Nevercott is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott lifts the lid off the hat box. Six small boxes sit in a row on a bed of tissue, each tied with a different ribbon. He turns the box so the ribbons face you.
>
> > I've chosen six, because the company reads you as all of them at one time or another. I'd like to learn which one you believe. Take whichever you like, and I'll tell you what the file says about it.

If Zardoz is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz waves the glass across the cloth, and the six boxes shift on the white linen.
>
> > Six, my darling, and don't ask why! Krebbyg wanted eight and Fel'rekt wanted four, so we compromised on a number nobody likes! Each one's an honest little treasure, and whichever you pick says something about you. Go on and choose, I won't look. I'll look the whole time!

> [!exploration]**The Fixed Table**
>
> The member picks one item and keeps it. The other five are put away and not offered again. Attunement and use follow the item's own entry.
>
> | Item | What the file says |
> |---|---|
> | *Cloak of elvenkind* | You have a gift for getting in and out unnoticed, and the file says you're less good at noticing when somebody has noticed you. |
> | *Gloves of thievery* | You take what you need with a careful hand, and the file records two occasions on which you left a trace you didn't know about. |
> | *Eyes of minute seeing* | You look at small things for a long time, and the file says you have asked the right question and the wrong one on the same evening. |
> | *Brooch of shielding* | You stand where the enemy can reach you so that your friends don't have to, and the file counts how many times that was a plan and how many times it was an accident. |
> | *Goggles of night* | You go into dark places more often than the company's own people do, and the file says you have never once asked for a lantern. |
> | *Boots of elvenkind* | You move well and you leave in good time, and the file says you've left three rooms before anyone knew you were in them. |
>
> A character who watches the contact during the choice and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that the six are not shop stock. Each was bought for a reason in the file, and the contact is watching which one the member reaches for first.
>
> - **If two members are named Officer on the same evening:** each makes a choice, and both may take the same item.
> - **If the member gives the item away or sells it:** the company does not replace it, and the Spy remarks on it once at the next dawn.

After the member chooses, the contact reads the line for that item. If Nevercott is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott takes the matching note from his waistcoat, unfolds it, and reads without looking up.
>
> > About that choice, the file says this. I've copied it exactly as Krebbyg wrote it, so please forgive the handwriting and the spelling.
>
> He reads the line for the item aloud and sets the note on the desk.

If Zardoz is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz claps the plumed hat against his thigh and unfolds a sheet of foolscap with something else written on the back.
>
> > The file, my darling, the file! Forty pages, mostly gossip and one very good drawing of a gnome. Here's what it says about your pick, and I'll read it in my best voice!
>
> He reads the line for the item aloud with a flourish and tosses the sheet to the valet.

### The Folio

The contact lays a folio on the table. Which folio it is depends on what the party already holds from **Fireball!**

If neither **Nimblewright Ledger Stolen** nor **Ledger Complete** is marked, and the member was not handed the ledger in Jarlaxle's cabin during **Fireball!**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The contact slides a leather folio across the cloth, four pages thick and bound with a green cord. The pages hold nine rows in a tight, neat hand, each with a number, a date and a name.
>
> > This is every nimblewright the company has sold, by number, buyer, date, district and price. Most of it isn't public, and none of it is flattering. It's the only reliable way to trace a construct in Waterdeep back to whoever paid for it. It's yours, so keep it somewhere better than your pocket.

If **Nimblewright Ledger Stolen** or **Ledger Complete** is marked, or the member was handed the ledger in the cabin in **Fireball!**, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The contact slides a single sheet across the cloth, covered in small pencil writing, and sets a pair of reading spectacles on top of it.
>
> > I understand you've seen the ledger itself, so I won't insult you with a copy. These are the margin notes, which no thief has ever seen. They say where each unit is kept in its buyer's house and when it last left. They're Krebbyg's, so treat the third column with care.

> [!exploration]**The Records**
>
> The folio is the Officer's own copy of the company's sales ledger. It works the same way in either version.
>
> - **What it holds (folio):** nine rows, NW-01 to NW-09, as the complete ledger in **Fireball!** gives them: buyer, address, date, balance and crystal number. NW-03 is marked VOID. NW-05 to NW-09 stay redacted, and Ilphrin won't discuss any redacted row.
> - **What it holds (annotations):** for each buyer, where the unit is kept in the household and when it last left the house. For one named unit, the member has Advantage on the first Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception) check to find or follow it.
> - **Tracing:** once each tenday, the member may name a nimblewright by crystal number, buyer or description. Ilphrin reads the answer from the folio before dusk. For any other construct sold in Waterdeep, the trace is a three-day task, and she brings back the buyer on the third dawn, from the quest that gives that buyer.
> - **Holding it:** a second Officer in the party receives their own copy. A member who loses the folio gets no replacement. Ilphrin traces from memory for one more tenday.
> - **If the party has not yet been through the Sea Maidens Faire in Fireball!:** the contact says the folio is "still being copied," and Krebbyg hands it over after the party visits the Faire.

The handover awards no Renown. The +2 in the Factions Guide for retrieving the shipping records from the Faire goes only to a member who takes them without the company's knowledge.

### Intelligence on Demand

The contact turns to the last benefit. If Nevercott is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nevercott closes the folio and rests his hands on it.
>
> > Once each quest, you may ask for a full assessment of any faction, person or place in Waterdeep. You'll have it in forty-eight hours, written out by Krebbyg and me. It will be thorough as far as the company's reach goes. Where the company has its own interest there will be a gap, and I'm telling you now so it doesn't surprise you.

If Zardoz is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets the glass down at last and holds up two fingers, then a third.
>
> > You ask, and two days later you get a written page on whoever you like. I won't go faster, friend, so don't bother shouting at the valet! It's thorough enough to frighten you. Where the company has its own interest, there'll be a gap, and I'm telling you now so you won't think I forgot!

If the party knows of the Vault of Dragons, add the following to whichever speech was read:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The contact tips a hand toward you.
>
> > If you ask about the Vault of Dragons, you'll receive everything we have except one thing. I'd rather say so now than have you search the page for it.

> [!exploration]**Requesting an Assessment**
>
> The member asks once each quest, at the dead drop on peg seven or through Ilphrin, naming the subject. A quest is one of the named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**. Events, faction missions and rank events are not quests. The use counts against the quest the party is in when the member asks, and an assessment not requested before the quest ends is lost.
>
> - **Delivery:** a written page is pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor at dusk, 48 hours after the request, never sooner.
> - **A faction:** the leader and lieutenants by name, the outposts and lair by address, how alert the faction is toward the party, and what it wants from the party this tenday.
> - **A person:** where they live and sleep, whom they meet in an ordinary tenday, whom they answer to, and one fear or debt the company knows.
> - **A place:** its entrances, its hours, who guards it by day and by night, and one hazard.
> - **What it never says:** the company knows the truth about the Cassalanters through Vessa. An assessment never states the Asmodean pact or the children's contract to a member who hasn't worked it out. For that member, a page on the Cassalanters covers the house, staff, holdings and social calendar, and leaves the pact out.
>   - If **Cassalanter Pact Shared with BD** is marked for the member, the page states the pact and the contract plainly, without naming Vessa.
>   - The same applies to a member who tells the contact, in their own words or with proof, that the Cassalanters are bound to a devil. The contact then answers as Zardoz or Nevercott does in **The Theater's Back Room**, and the GM marks the outcome.
>   - Manshoon is never named. The assessment calls the Splinter "the other cell."
> - **The gap:** every assessment on a subject that touches the company's operations, ships, crew or the captain returns with one entry marked "withheld." An assessment on the Vault of Dragons returns complete except for how to reach the vault, which Jarlaxle means to use himself.
> - **If the subject is the company:** the contact declines and no use is spent.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Officer earns further Renown from the missions and from the Earning Renown list in the Bregan D'aerthe page of the Factions Guide.

If a member's Renown falls below 10, the member keeps the rank but their Officer benefits are suspended. The Spy goes back to the company and the item stays with the member. The folio, the assessment and the trace return when the member's Renown reaches 10 again.

### Aftermath

The member may tell their companions what they chose and whom they met. The Spy, the folio and the assessment are individual benefits, so a companion who asks in the member's name is turned away. If the member leaves Bregan D'aerthe or marks **BD Contact Severed**, the Spy is withdrawn the same day. The member keeps the item, and nothing else carries over.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has chosen an item, named the Spy's first task and heard how to request an assessment.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Cassalanter Pact Shared with BD** — mark for a member who tells the contact in their own words that the Cassalanters are bound to a devil, or shows proof; read by **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted), where Vessa helps that member openly, and by the assessment in this Event.
>
> - **BD Officer** — mark with the recipient's name when the contact names the rank. Record the member's Spy, chosen item, folio type (full or annotations), quest of last assessment, tenday of last trace, and quest of the last scouting trip. Read by **Commander**, which requires it.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All four benefits are active for this member from the next dawn. **Commander** occurs when this member reaches Renown 25. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

A Bregan D'aerthe contact names a member an Officer and hands over a personal Spy, a magic item chosen from six, the company's records of nimblewright sales and a standing offer of intelligence on request.

## Summary

We were named Officers of Bregan D'aerthe and given a Spy of our own. We each chose one of six small boxes and heard what the company's file said about us. We took the nimblewright records and learned we may ask for one assessment of anything in Waterdeep every quest, if we wait two days. What else is in that file?
