# Design Notes: Officer

## The Contact

The restored draft chose the contact by whether **Three Nights** was complete and summoned the member with an ivory card. Neither matches the finished events. **Dinner with Zardoz** retires Nevercott after **The Compromised Eye** and sets **Zardoz Introduced**, so this event keys on that outcome.

- A member without the outcome is received by Nevercott. That is the usual case, because the dinner is still two evenings away when the rank is reached. It also covers a member for whom the dinner never fires.
- The summons uses the playbill channel from **Kreb Drops the Cover**. The ivory card is cut because the company's own card is black with a silver ship.
- The event holds until **BD Soldier** is marked.
- If the Faire has sailed on Tarsakh 20, Zardoz receives the member in the hired lighter that **Commander** uses, since he stays behind with a team and the submarine.

## The Records

The draft's folio duplicated the ledger that **Fireball!** already hands over, since Jarlaxle gives the complete ledger to Bregan D'aerthe members in the cabin and the party can steal it. The records stay as the Officer benefit because the Factions Guide names them. A party that already holds the ledger gets buyer-trace annotations instead. These are invented: where each unit is kept in its buyer's house, and when it last left. They give Advantage on one check and add no new facts about the buyers. The handover pays no Renown, so the +2 for taking the records from the Faire stays a separate reward for a member who steals them.

## The Item Table

The draft left the Uncommon item to the GM, with four suggestions. The table now has six fixed items and the member chooses. The choice is the beat: each item carries a line from the file, and the pick shows which habit the member believes they have. The four original items stay (*cloak of elvenkind*, *eyes of minute seeing*, *gloves of thievery*, *brooch of shielding*), with *goggles of night* and *boots of elvenkind* added.

## The Spy as a Procedure

The Spy is now a per-member procedure with a contact, three tasks, one task per tenday, a refusal list, a loss rule and a roster. Ilphrin Quiss is the first Officer's Spy. Four more are named so a party of five Officers never shares one. She uses the **Spy** stat block with Darkvision 120 feet and Sunlight Sensitivity. Her Power is 17 for levels 5 to 10 and 15 from level 11, the mechanics reference's Tier 2 figure and its r50 pool-table Tier 3 figure. She is unavailable during missions and heists, as the draft said, except once each quest as a scout, which is where the Power figure applies.

A quest is one of the ten named quests, so the assessment's "once per quest" has a count. The assessment has a stated gap, and its speech never names the Vault of Dragons unless the party knows of it.

## Renown

The six missions award 2, 2, 3, 3, 4 and 4 base Renown, which brings a member from Renown 1 to 19. Renown 10 is usually crossed as **The Compromised Eye** resolves (1, 3, 5, 8, 11). Reaching Renown 25 needs at least six more from outside the missions:

- the bonus +1 lines in each mission;
- **Dinner with Zardoz**;
- the Earning Renown list in the Factions Guide (the intelligence, Golorr artifact, Nar'l, Faire-cover and creativity items);
- **Fireball!** (+1 or +2 for a member);
- **Gralhund Villa** (+2).

This rank event awards none of its own. **Commander** occurs at Renown 25.
