# Design Notes: Officer

## The Contact

The restored draft chose the contact by whether **Three Nights** was complete and summoned the member with an ivory card. Neither matches the finished events. **Dinner with Zardoz** retires Nevercott after **The Compromised Eye**, and it sets **Zardoz Introduced**, so this event keys on that outcome. A member without it is received by Nevercott, which covers the usual case, where the rank is reached as **The Compromised Eye** resolves and the dinner is still two evenings away. It also covers a member for whom the dinner never fires. The summons uses the playbill channel from **Kreb Drops the Cover**, and the ivory card is cut because the company's own card is the black one with a silver ship. The event holds until **BD Soldier** is marked. If the Faire has sailed on Tarsakh 20, Zardoz receives the member in the hired lighter that **Commander** uses, on the reading that he stays behind with a team and the submarine.

## The Records

The draft's folio duplicated the ledger that **Fireball!** already hands over, since Jarlaxle gives the complete ledger to Bregan D'aerthe members in the cabin and the party can steal it. The records stay as the Officer benefit, because the Factions Guide names them, but a party that already holds the ledger gets buyer-trace annotations instead. Those annotations are invented: where each unit is kept in its buyer's house, and when it last left. They give Advantage on one check and add no new facts about the buyers. The handover pays no Renown, so the +2 for taking the records from the Faire stays a separate reward for a member who steals them.

## The Item Table

The draft left the Uncommon item to the GM, with four suggestions. The table is now six fixed items and the member chooses. The choice is the beat: each item carries a line from the file, and the member's pick says which habit they believe they have. The four original items stay (*cloak of elvenkind*, *eyes of minute seeing*, *gloves of thievery*, *brooch of shielding*), with *goggles of night* and *boots of elvenkind* added.

## The Spy as a Procedure

The Spy is now a per-member procedure with a contact, three tasks, one task per tenday, a refusal list, a loss rule and a roster. Ilphrin Quiss is the first Officer's Spy, and four more are named so a party of five Officers never shares one. She uses the **Spy** stat block with Darkvision 120 feet and Sunlight Sensitivity. Her Power is 17 for levels 5 to 10 and 15 from level 11, which are the mechanics reference's Tier 2 figure and its r50 pool-table Tier 3 figure. She is unavailable during missions and heists, as the draft said, with one stated exception, once each quest as a scout, which is where the Power figure applies.

A quest is defined as one of the ten named quests, so the assessment's "once per quest" has a count. The assessment has a stated gap. Its speech never names the Vault of Dragons unless the party knows of it.

## Renown

The six missions award 2, 2, 3, 3, 4 and 4 base Renown, which brings a member from Renown 1 to 19. Renown 10 is usually crossed as **The Compromised Eye** resolves (1, 3, 5, 8, 11). Reaching Renown 25 needs at least six more from outside the missions: the bonus +1 lines in each mission, **Dinner with Zardoz**, the Earning Renown list in the Factions Guide (the intelligence, Golorr artifact, Nar'l, Faire-cover and creativity items), **Fireball!** (+1 or +2 for a member) and **Gralhund Villa** (+2). This rank event awards none of its own. **Commander** occurs at Renown 25.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Ilphrin Quiss (she/her, Lawful Neutral), Nyrae Zauvir, Dhaer Oussen, Velkyn Hune and Ghaena Tormyl, with their covers, and the Spy's three tasks and Sending report. Also invented: the buyer-trace annotations, the Spy's refusal penalty (1 Renown on a second order in a quest), the scout call-in, and the assessment's report shape by subject. Ilphrin has no voice profile, and she is voiced from this event: formal, patient, no swearing.

Logged for the out-of-scope notes: guide 08:58 says Jarlaxle assigns the Spy, where here it is the contact. **Fireball!** ev-04 still uses the old True/False format and hands the complete ledger to BD members without setting an outcome, so this event reads the cabin handover directly. **Houseless Noble** now draws each member's own Spy from this event, and **The Dive** has the Faire sail on Tarsakh 20, which this event follows.
