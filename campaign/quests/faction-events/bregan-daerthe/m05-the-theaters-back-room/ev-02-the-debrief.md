# The Debrief

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs after **Dinner with Zardoz**, when an individual Bregan D'aerthe member has reached Renown 10 and 6th level and **Windmill Raided** is marked. The party already went inside the windmill on Coachlamp Lane during **Faction Outposts**, so Bregan D'aerthe ran the handoff with Brimel Crestfall itself and asks for a report instead of sending the member to the Faire showcase. If **Windmill Raided** is not marked, run **The Theater's Back Room** and skip this Event. Only Bregan D'aerthe members attend. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Meet Zardoz Zord and Krebbyg Masq'il'yr in the dressing room at the end of the stage-left corridor, which the member enters by right for the first time.
> - Describe what they found in the windmill and hand over the map from its back room, if they took it.
> - Set their account beside Brimel's floor plan and see where the two agree.
> - Answer three questions that Zardoz buries in his stories.
> - Collect 200 gp and the key to the dressing room.

> [!gamemaster]**Before You Run This**
>
> Two **Faction Outposts** outcomes decide what the member brings to the table. The **Faction Outposts** conversion must write them when the party goes through the windmill, together with **Windmill Raided**.
>
> - **Windmill Map Taken** — the party took or copied the map of the City of the Dead, with the Brandath Crypt ringed in red, from the desk in the back room.
> - **Windmill Clean Exit** — the party left without the sentry imp escaping, without an alarm, and without Seffia Naelryke and Arn Xalrondar learning that anyone had been inside.
>
> Bregan D'aerthe bought Brimel's plan before the raid and has already collected it. Fel'rekt Lafeen took the folded sheet from Brimel at the Faire showcase two nights ago and shook off the Cassalanter watcher at the interval, and Brimel has been aboard the *Heartbreaker* since. The company still wants the member's firsthand account, because a floor plan drawn from memory and a room somebody has stood in are different things.
>
> If **Zardoz Introduced** is marked for the member, Zardoz is in the room and speaks in his normal voice. He keeps silent in **The Theater's Back Room** because a Cassalanter watcher sits in the house, and nobody listens in the dressing room tonight. If **Zardoz Introduced** is not marked, Krebbyg alone receives the member and uses the same lines, with "the captain" for Zardoz. If **Kreb Unmasked** is not marked, Krebbyg stays in his Kreb Sorrush cover with the door shut.
>
> No Bregan D'aerthe speaker says "Jarlaxle" in this Event. If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, Krebbyg may say the name once, in a low voice, and Zardoz does not.

### The Summons

A playbill with a red 6 circled on it lies under the door of Trollskull Manor at dusk, and it means the stage door of the Seven Masks Theater at six bells. Krebbyg leads the member down the stage-left corridor and knocks twice and three times at the last dressing room. The guards, two drow in stagehands' aprons who use the 2024 **Scout** statistics, let the member in.

If **Zardoz Introduced** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The last dressing room on the stage-left corridor smells of candle wax and cheap wine, and a long mirror ringed with candle stubs hangs above a washstand. A man in scarlet sits on a costume trunk with his boots off and a plumed hat on his knee, and a glass of something amber balances on the arm of a folded cot. He rises when you come in, waves the glass at the cots, and starts talking before the door has closed.
>
> > "Welcome, welcome, my darlings, to the finest dressing room on the Sword Coast, and sit anywhere, because the cots are all the best cot! I hear you've been into a windmill without so much as a ticket, and I haven't slept a wink for wanting to know whether the stairs creak."

If **Zardoz Introduced** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The last dressing room on the stage-left corridor smells of candle wax and cheap wine, and a long mirror ringed with candle stubs hangs above a washstand. Krebbyg drops onto the costume trunk with a playbill in one hand and a pot of tea in the other, and he waves a cup at the cots before he finishes his first sentence.
>
> > "Sit, darling, sit, anywhere, they're all the same cot. The captain sends his regards and he's busy with the ships, so you've got me, and I've got a list from Fel, which I've lost. You've been in that windmill, haven't you? I want to hear all of it."

> [!social]**The Captain on the Trunk**
>
> Zardoz Zord (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in the guise of an Illuskan human, he/him) :: the flamboyant captain of the Sea Maidens Faire, who is the man at the top of Bregan D'aerthe and talks without stopping.
>
> Zardoz fills every pause, answers a hard question with a story or an offer of free tickets, and calls the members "my darlings" by his second sentence. He swears happily and often. The precise question he cares about is always buried in the middle of a ramble, and he waits for the answer without letting his smile change. He never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan. If the cover strains, his accent thins and he says "my dear" in a silky voice that does not belong to a sailor.
>
> Conversation topics Zardoz is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The windmill, the stairs and what the members saw.
> - Brimel Crestfall, who is safe aboard the *Heartbreaker*.
> - Free tickets, at length.
> - The plan on the trunk beside him, which he is delighted with.
>
> Specific dialogue for Zardoz on these topics is presented below.

> [!social]**The Lieutenant with the Teapot**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: a young drow gunslinger who runs the Seven Masks' bookings and has the wig off in the safe house.
>
> Krebbyg talks in rapid run-ons, calls the member "darling," swears constantly and defers to Fel'rekt on anything serious. He pours tea whenever Zardoz stops talking. He is proud that the handoff worked without the member, and slightly offended that the member was in a windmill.

> [!qna]**Did the handoff go well?**
>
> Krebbyg pours a cup of tea and slides it across the trunk.
>
> > "Fel did it in an usher's coat on the night of the showcase, in section C, with the shanty on, and the watcher was in row six and never saw a thing. He shook her off at the interval with half the lobby, and he said it was a dream, honestly, though he says that about everything. Brimel's on the Heartbreaker with a mop in his hand, and he hasn't stopped apologizing."

> [!qna]**What does the plan show?**
>
> Zardoz taps a folded sheet on the trunk beside him.
>
> > "A stair, my darlings, and an apartment at the top of it, and a little room off the apartment marked with a cross and the word locked, which is a thing I never trust. It's drawn in a very tidy hand, and I'd say the man who drew it counts everything twice, and I'd like to see how it sits beside your story."

> [!qna]**Who is the watcher?**
>
> > "A woman in a grey hat who works for the lady of that house, so Brimel says, and I'd like her to stay a mystery. She didn't see Fel, and she didn't see you, and a mystery with no faces in it is the best kind. If she turns up again, tell me first and tell the ghost second."

### Reporting Back

The member tells the dressing room what they found. Play the report as a conversation and let the players choose what to say. Zardoz and Krebbyg interrupt, and Zardoz waits until a story is nearly finished before he asks his three questions.

> [!exploration]**The Plan and the Account**
>
> Any character who sets the plan beside the party's account and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check sees where the two agree. The plan and the account agree on the stair, the apartment door at the top and the locked back room. The plan adds three things the party did not see. The family rents the top floor and the sail room under the name of a Marra Selby, a servant carries baskets of candles, salt and wine as far as the second landing, and a note on the back of the sheet says a map of the City of the Dead lies on the desk in the back room with one mausoleum ringed in red.
>
> - **Auto:** the pencilled note on the back of the plan agrees with what the party saw, and Zardoz points to it. Mark **Brandath Lead from Brimel**.
> - **Result of 17 or higher:** the character notes that the plan shows the sail room as the only way into the apartment, which agrees with the party's own entry.

If **Windmill Map Taken** is marked, read or paraphrase the following when the member lays the map on the trunk:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz takes the map in both hands and holds it to the lamp, and the plumes on his hat on the trunk nod in the draught. He looks from the ring of red ink to the pencilled note on Brimel's sheet and back again, and his smile gets a shade wider.
>
> > "Now that is a very particular tomb, my darlings, with a very particular family name beside it. Two people who have never met each other, a steward and a bunch of heroes, both say the same thing about the same desk, and I like it when the world agrees with itself."

If **Windmill Map Taken** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz taps the pencilled note on the back of Brimel's sheet with one ring-heavy finger and lets the lamp catch it.
>
> > "A map on a desk with a ring in red ink and a family name beside it. You saw the same desk, did you? Then I have two witnesses who've never met, a steward and a bunch of heroes, and I like it when the world agrees with itself. I should so like to know who the family is, and I'm sure I'll find out."

> [!social]**Three Questions in a Story**
>
> Zardoz asks his questions while telling a story about a cook, a harbor and a goat that went to sea. Each is buried in the middle of a sentence and dropped on the member without warning, and he does not accept a quick answer. Each answer tells him how the raid went, and he does not mind a lie as long as he can tell it is one.
>
> The three questions are who saw the members' faces, whether anyone raised an alarm, and whether anyone followed them home.

> [!qna]**Did anyone see your faces?**
>
> Zardoz goes on stirring his drink with a finger while he asks.
>
> > "And the goat, you understand, had a very fine beard, and I remember thinking, who saw you? Anyone? Only I ask because the beard was recognizable, and so are people, and I should so like to know whether anyone in that house could pick you out of a line."

> [!qna]**Did anyone raise the alarm?**
>
> > "And the goat bleated like a ship's bell, mind you, which brings me to my next question, because I don't suppose you rang anything of the sort in that house. Any bells, any shouting, anyone running off down the lane to tell tales about who had been up the stairs?"

> [!qna]**Did anyone follow you home?**
>
> > "And the goat came back, naturally, the way they do, and so did the man who was following him. Tell me, my darlings, did you go home with a tail, or did you go home alone? I'd like to know before the harbor does."

Zardoz reacts to the member's answers according to the **Faction Outposts** outcomes, not the member's words.

If **Windmill Clean Exit** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets down his glass, claps the nearest member on the shoulder with a hand full of rings, and laughs until the cots rattle.
>
> > "Then nobody knows you were ever there, which is the best news since the day I won back my second-best hat! Brimel stays where he is, aboard my darling Heartbreaker, with a mop and a ticket, and he sails with us on the twentieth of Tarsakh like a gentleman."

If **Windmill Clean Exit** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz's smile does not change, and his glass goes down on the trunk with a small, careful click. The roar goes out of his voice, and the sailor's accent thins to something learned at a much older table.
>
> > "Then somebody in that house knows the windmill has been visited, my dear, and a house that knows it has been visited asks whether somebody inside talked. I shall move Brimel to my own ship tonight, and you will be very kind about it when you see him."

Krebbyg moves Brimel to the *Eyecatcher* that night, and Brimel stays there until the Faire sails.

### Payment and the Key

Zardoz pays out of a velvet purse at the end of the evening, and Krebbyg gives each member who attended a brass key stamped with a seven.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz tips a velvet purse onto the trunk, and two hundred gold coins slide out in a heap that Krebbyg starts counting out loud and loses track of. When he has finished, Krebbyg pushes a brass key stamped with a single seven across the lid.
>
> > "That's two hundred for the party, darling, however you like, and this is the room. Two knocks, a gap, three knocks, at any hour. The guards don't ask, the cots are clean, and if the ghost comes through the wall, tell him it's an emergency and he'll go away. You're not a visitor anymore."

> [!gamemaster]**The Back Room**
>
> The dressing room at the end of the stage-left corridor is Bregan D'aerthe's emergency safe house in the Seven Masks, and it is now open to the member by right. The terms are the same as in **The Theater's Back Room**.
>
> - **Entry:** two knocks, a gap and three knocks at the door, at any hour. Two drow guards, who use the 2024 **Scout** statistics, are always inside while the room is in use.
> - **What it holds:** six cots, a washstand, a locked costume trunk for the member's gear and a stock of drow-made crossbows and bolts that Fel'rekt sells at cost to Bregan D'aerthe members.
> - **Rest:** the member and up to five companions the member names to the guards can take a Long Rest there, for up to three nights in a row.
> - **Messages:** a message left with the guards reaches Krebbyg by the next dusk, and the grey coat on peg seven still works.
> - **Meetings:** **The Dive** briefs here, and the rank event **Houseless Noble** meets here when **Seven Masks Back Room** is marked.
> - **Loss rule:** the member loses access by marking **BD Contact Severed**, by bringing a Watch officer into the corridor, or by telling anyone outside the company that the room exists.
>
> **What changes if the member already has access.** At Renown 3 and above, the door staff admit the member to the theater and the booking office, and this does not include the dressing room. If the member entered the dressing room during **Faction Outposts** (the **Seven Masks Raided** outcome, which the **Faction Outposts** conversion must write), the entry was unsanctioned. After this Event the guards stop treating the member's knock as an intrusion, and the Alert tier that the raid set toward Bregan D'aerthe drops to Suspicious for that member.

### Renown Opportunities

>

> - **+1 Renown:** **Windmill Clean Exit** is marked.
>

### Aftermath

Krebbyg keeps the plan in the dressing room and copies the map in his own hand if the member brought it. Brimel sails with the Faire on Tarsakh 20 under a cook's mate's pass, and Zardoz never mentions the windmill to the member again unless the member does. The party has two independent sources for the Brandath Crypt, the windmill map from **Faction Outposts** and Brimel's note.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has reported and received the key.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Brandath Lead from Brimel** — mark for each member who sees the pencilled note on Brimel's plan; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), Scene 2, where Brimel's plan is a second clue to the Brandath Crypts alongside the windmill map from **Faction Outposts**.
> - **Seven Masks Back Room** — mark for each member who receives the key and the knock; read by **The Dive** and by the rank event **Houseless Noble**, which meet in the dressing room when it is marked.

## Overview

Bregan D'aerthe asks what the party found when it went into the windmill during Faction Outposts, compares the account with the floor plan a Cassalanter steward sold the company, and gives the party the key to the theater's back room.

## Summary

We told Zardoz and Krebbyg what we found in the windmill on Coachlamp Lane, and they set our account beside a floor plan a frightened Cassalanter steward had sold them. The two agreed on the stair, the apartment and a map in the back room with a tomb ringed in red. They paid us two hundred gold and gave us the key to the dressing room at the end of the stage-left corridor. Whose tomb is it, and what does the family want with it?
