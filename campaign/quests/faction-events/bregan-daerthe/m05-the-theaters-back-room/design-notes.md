# Design Notes: The Theater's Back Room

## Paid, Not Brave

The restored draft said Brimel Crestfall "agreed" to the handoff for reasons he had not articulated, and the readaloud said he was "paid." The two cannot both stand, and a man who sells his employers' secrets from conscience would have to know what the secret was. Brimel does not, so he is paid. The household's wine merchant, who has been on Bregan D'aerthe's books for two years, offered him 300 gp for a floor plan, he took 150 gp of it a tenday ago, and the other 150 gp is the envelope the member carries to the doorway. He wants to leave Waterdeep, and the berth aboard the *Heartbreaker* is the thing he cannot ask for.

His fear comes from **The Wazoo Affair**. The Cassalanters sent solicitors to a gossip sheet and then began questioning their own staff, and a steward who has carried baskets to the family's windmill for years reasonably believes he will be blamed. That link is the only one M5 needs from M2, and it holds with **Wazoo Exposé Published** and **Cassalanter Reaction Observed** as backstory. The chain asks for no knowledge of infernalism anywhere. Brimel knows about private gatherings, a hired cab with the curtains drawn, and Lord Victoro's word "resolution," which he takes for a debt. The restored line about Madam Ammalia visiting the week after her youngest was born is cut, because it points at the children's pact and the twins are eight.

## Zardoz in the Box

Zardoz Zord never lets a silence go unfilled, and his voice profile says so. In **The Theater's Back Room** he sits in the owner's box for the whole showcase and says nothing. The break is deliberate, and it is the loudest thing he does in the mission.

There are three reasons. A Cassalanter watcher in the house could hear him greet the party and learn that the Faire's captain is involved. It is the first mission that runs under his name, and a captain who talks through his own plot would draw every eye in section C. And a silent Zardoz tells the players how seriously the company takes the evening. The break ends in **The Debrief**, where nobody is listening and he is back to his usual self, with the sharpest questions of the campaign hidden in a story about a goat. If **Zardoz Introduced** is not marked, the box is empty and the hat sits on the rail, and Krebbyg carries the mission alone.

## Four Approaches to Florette

The restored draft resolved Florette with one DC 14 check, a Stealth roll followed by "the DM's judgment," and a street intercept with no consequence beyond a vague report. It also gave the handoff a single beat that could not fail. Each of those is gone.

Florette is now the 2024 **Spy** with four approaches, and a failure in any of them has a stated result: she notices a member's face, she follows Brimel at the interval, and the party faces two more checks before she can report. Three approaches (the double-booked seat, the lifted stub and the fire aisle) can leave her with nothing to report. The fourth, the service corridor, always ends in **Florette Reported**, because it is the choice to be seen. A party that takes it has not failed the mission and has paid a price that **Cassalanter Villa** will read. Captain Ilsa Dalloway and Constable Harl Pimm turn a crowded theater into a place where force costs something, and Pimm is the constable who counts lamp oil in **Kreb Drops the Cover**, so the Watch the party meets here is one it has already met.

The handoff has a failure branch as well. The grey coat on peg seven is the dead drop from **Kreb Drops the Cover**, so a missed handoff costs the party the doorway meeting and the berth, and not the plan.

## The Windmill, Corrected

The restored draft put the windmill in the North Ward and gave it a "lower-floor locked room serviced monthly by a man at odd hours." Both are wrong. The windmill is on Coachlamp Lane in the Southern Ward, and Seffia Naelryke and Arn Xalrondar live on the upper floor as ordinary tenants, as **Faction Outposts** has them. The monthly servicing was invented, and the draft's "North Ward address" is cut. What survives is a delivery route, because a steward does carry baskets, and the locked back room off the top-floor apartment, which is the room the desk and the Brandath map sit in.

Guide 08 says the family keeps the windmill "under a false name for private gatherings." That is kept as a lease in the name of Marra Selby, a name the plan records and Brimel says does not exist. Brimel's pencilled note about a map of the City of the Dead with one mausoleum ringed in red is the same clue the windmill's desk holds, which is why **Brandath Lead from Brimel** is a second independent path in **Vault of Dragons** under the Three Clue Rule.

## The Debrief

If **Faction Outposts** has already raided the windmill, a handoff is redundant, so the mission becomes a debrief. Bregan D'aerthe has run the exchange itself, Fel'rekt took the plan off Brimel at the showcase, and the company asks the member for what a floor plan cannot give, a firsthand account. The plan confirms what the party saw. The member walks out with the key because the dressing room is where Zardoz and Krebbyg want to have the talk, and **Seven Masks Back Room** is set there.

The raid can be clean or not. **Windmill Clean Exit** marked sends Brimel on to the *Heartbreaker* as planned, and not marked moves him to the *Eyecatcher*, where Zardoz can watch him. Nothing else in the Debrief depends on the raid's outcome.

## The Back Room

**Seven Masks Back Room** is the dressing-room safe house from **Faction Outposts**, which guide 08 describes as Jarlaxle's emergency safe house for agents. The Initiate benefit lists a safe house aboard the *Heartbreaker* or *Hellraiser*, not in the theater, and the door staff's Renown 3 briefing admits a member to the lobby and booking office, so the dressing room was never open to members. This mission is the first time it is. A member who raided it during **Faction Outposts** gets something different, which is forgiveness, and the Alert tier the raid set drops to Suspicious. **The Dive** and the rank events read the outcome, because Krebbyg briefs there and the rank events need a place that is not the stage door.

## Out-of-Scope Notes

Invented: Marra Selby (the lease name), Captain Ilsa Dalloway, the household wine merchant, the Net Street sailmaker's doorway, the printed berth pass and the seat phrase. Brimel Crestfall and Florette Cressyn keep their draft names and have no Notable Figures pages, so they are voiced from the event text. Constable Harl Pimm and Malcolm Brizzenbright are used as they are written in **Kreb Drops the Cover** and Malcolm's page.

Contradictions with files outside this folder, left unedited and listed for the Bregan D'aerthe section of the out-of-scope notes. `sources/SOURCE_GUIDE.md` line 219 puts the windmill in the North Ward, and `campaign/structure/arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md` lines 75 and 322 call it a "Manshoon outpost," though the Cassalanters run it. Guide 08 line 70 says "false name" while `arc-e-faction-outposts.md` has ordinary tenants. The **Faction Outposts** conversion must write **Windmill Raided**, **Windmill Map Taken**, **Windmill Clean Exit** and **Seven Masks Raided**, and its Alert-tier rule for the dressing room is the one this mission relaxes. **Cassalanter Villa** and **Vault of Dragons** are unconverted readers of **Florette Reported** and **Brandath Lead from Brimel**. The Notable Figures page for Ammalia Cassalanter lists this mission under "Featured in" and does not describe Florette. The mission assumes the Faire is still in Deepwater Harbor, which the calendar does not guarantee at 6th level.
