# The Theater's Back Room: Overview

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available at Renown 10. Level 6 recommended. The Compromised Eye must be complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 6th level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

For three missions, Jarlaxle has been building toward a single piece of intelligence: a street address in the North Ward and a floor plan of the converted windmill the Cassalanters use for purposes they do not discuss with their staff. The Wazoo Affair (Mission 2) placed public pressure on the family and put their household at the kind of administrative strain that makes careful people start paying attention to conversations they were never meant to hear. Brimel Crestfall, the Cassalanters' household steward, has been paying that kind of attention for two months.

Brimel overheard Victoro Cassalanter use the word *resolution* at a family dinner in connection with a "northern property." He knows the windmill's street address and that the family uses it for something they do not discuss with staff. He has agreed to pass this information along tonight, during a Sea Maidens Faire performer showcase at Seven Masks Theater. He has a ticket. He knows the seat number. He will not initiate contact.

Ammalia Cassalanter suspects Brimel may be sharing household information, and she has put Florette Cressyn, a professional investigator on her retainer, in the same theater tonight. Florette has no evidence yet. She is watching because Ammalia told her to watch, and she is patient enough to wait until she has something definite.

## Involved Characters
- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** (Bregan D'aerthe) — delivers the brief at Seven Masks with an unusual bearing; something is time-sensitive; receives the completed intelligence package
- **Brimel Crestfall** (Cassalanter household staff) — household steward to the Cassalanters; forties, competent, quiet, frightened; has a ticket to tonight's Faire showcase and will not initiate contact; holds the intelligence
- **Florette Cressyn** (Cassalanter retainer) — professional investigator on retainer to Ammalia Cassalanter; watching Brimel tonight; has no evidence yet and is looking for a reason to act before any handoff occurs
- **Zardoz Zord** (Bregan D'aerthe) — performing onstage as host of the Sea Maidens Faire showcase; his stage presence is both cover and alibi; he does not acknowledge the party's presence in the theater

## Dangers & Enemies
Florette Cressyn is the mission's principal obstacle. She is trained and discreet, and any obvious physical approach in a crowded theater draws the Watch. The real risk in this mission is exposure, and a fight is the surest way to cause it. A Watch officer is at the venue for crowd management and means the party no harm, though he is observant.

## Overview

A Cassalanter household steward has spent two months sitting on information he was never meant to hear. Tonight he has a ticket to the Sea Maidens Faire showcase at Seven Masks Theater, a seat in section C, and instructions for a one-question handoff. His employer has sent a professional to watch him.
