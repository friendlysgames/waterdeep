# The Theater's Back Room: Overview

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available at Renown 10. Level 6 recommended. The Compromised Eye must be complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 6th level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** (Bregan D'aerthe) — delivers the brief at Seven Masks with an unusual bearing; something is time-sensitive; receives the completed intelligence package
- **Brimel Crestfall** (Cassalanter household staff) — household steward to the Cassalanters; forties, competent, quiet, frightened; has a ticket to tonight's Faire showcase and will not initiate contact; holds the intelligence
- **Florette Cressyn** (Cassalanter retainer) — professional investigator on retainer to Ammalia Cassalanter; watching Brimel tonight; has no evidence yet and is looking for a reason to pull him in before he can hand anything over
- **Zardoz Zord** (Bregan D'aerthe) — performing onstage as host of the Sea Maidens Faire showcase; his stage presence is both cover and alibi; he does not acknowledge the party's presence in the theater

## Dangers & Enemies
Florette Cressyn is the mission's principal obstacle. She is trained and discreet; any obvious physical approach in a crowded theater draws the Watch. The risk is exposure, not violence. A Watch officer may be present at the venue.

## Overview

Krebbyg arrived at Seven Masks with a different bearing than usual — something had become time-sensitive. Brimel Crestfall, a Cassalanter household steward, was ready to hand over intelligence about a North Ward property the family used. He had a ticket to tonight's Faire performer showcase. A Cassalanter watcher also had a ticket and was looking for the reason to pull Brimel in before any handoff occurred.

The document Brimel held contained a street address in the North Ward and a hand-drawn floor plan of a converted windmill. The locked room on the lower floor — serviced monthly by a man who arrived at odd hours — was what Jarlaxle had been waiting for. The intelligence pointed toward the vault approach path through the Brandath Crypts, which Jarlaxle had been assembling piece by piece since Mission 2.

The party identified Florette in the lobby, dealt with her by some means, and received the document from Brimel during the performance's first musical number — one quiet pass, seat C, row 4, the right question asked in a half-voice. Brimel confirmed what he knew in a doorway two blocks from the theater, then went home. Krebbyg, given the document: "This is what Jarlaxle was waiting for."
