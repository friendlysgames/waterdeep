# The Dive

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat/Exploration Event takes place at night in Deepwater Harbor, split between the surface and the wreck at forty feet. In this Event:
>
> - The surface lookout must be neutralized before the party enters the water — a warning bird changes the mission from a recovery to a fighting retreat.
> - The Guild dive team is either staging to enter the wreck or already inside, depending on timing. Both states are described below.
> - Underwater combat applies standard conditions: no verbal communication, half movement speed without a swim speed, Disadvantage on non-piercing weapon attacks.
> - Krebbyg coordinates from the surface via rope signals throughout. The rope is the party's lifeline and their only communication with the surface.
> - The Eye #3 is inside the captain's cabin on the wreck's lower deck. It is not scattered on the seafloor.
> - The Eye 3 Recovered by BD flag is set at the conclusion.

**Background (DM only)**

Eye #3 of the Stone of Golorr went into the harbor during a confrontation aboard a hired ketch three tendays ago. The ketch went down within minutes; most of the crew swam clear but the Eye sank with the cargo. Jarlaxle's harbor network identified the wreck's position four days after the sinking. Xanathar's people identified it independently a tenday later — their dive team has been staging for two nights, waiting for calm water and a dark tide.

Tonight both sides move.

The wreck: a forty-foot ketch settled at forty feet on a slight list to starboard. The hull is intact. Visibility inside the hull is less than five feet — murky harbor water and silt disturbed by the sinking. The Eye #3 is in the captain's cabin aft, in a sealed document case that went down with the cargo. The case is locked; opening it requires a **DC 12 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools or Sleight of Hand)** check, or breaking the hasp.

The Guild dive team: **Krenick Durr** (Veteran, team leader), one or two additional **Veterans** depending on escalation tier. All are using *potions of water breathing* they consumed before entering the water. They are equipped with short swords (piercing; no Disadvantage underwater) and know to use them. They do not know BD is coming. They know exactly what they are looking for.

**Krebbyg** is in a hired rowboat positioned thirty yards off Pier 17, showing no light, using a rope system: **one slow tug** = all clear; **two quick tugs** = stop, hold position; **three rapid tugs** = surface immediately, emergency. The party ties off to the rope before descending. He does not dive. He does not explain why.

> [!profile]+ **Profile: Krebbyg Masq'il'yr**
>
> **Roleplaying Information**
> ***Resonance.*** Krebbyg should inspire quiet confidence in his professional competence, mild unease at how little he reveals about his own nature, and — in this mission specifically — something close to trust, because he is putting himself in an exposed position to support an operation he cannot control once they go under.
>
> ***Emotions.*** Krebbyg most often feels focused during active operations, professionally satisfied by clean outcomes, privately concerned about the party in a way he will not express, and genuinely amused by the gap between what the party knows and what the organization knows.
>
> ***Motivations.*** Krebbyg wants Eye #3 recovered for Jarlaxle, the operation to close without a Guild report reaching Ahmaergo, and the party to come back up.
>
> ***Inspirations.*** When playing Krebbyg, channel Cassian Andor (*Rogue One*), Luther Stickell (*Mission: Impossible*), and M (*James Bond*) — the handler who is more invested than the briefing suggests, steady under pressure, and more dangerous than his manner implies.
>
> **Character Information**
> ***Persona.*** To the party, Krebbyg is the booking manager, the relay, the calm presence who handles the operational details. To himself, he is a Bregan D'aerthe gunslinger who has done this kind of work for twenty years and prefers it when no one gets killed. Deep down, Krebbyg has a careful regard for the party that he will not acknowledge directly.
>
> ***Morale.*** Krebbyg does not fight at the rowboat unless someone comes at him directly. If boarded, he uses the water as cover and is gone before the attacker has oriented. He has a plan for this.
>
> ***Relationships.*** Krebbyg is Jarlaxle's operational fixer for Waterdeep. He has worked with Vessin for three years.

### The Pier

Pier 17, near midnight. The Guild's staging area is a hired dinghy tied off at the pier's outer end — two figures visible at the waterline, preparing dive equipment. Forty yards to their left, a third figure on a moored fishing vessel watches the harbor with a spyglass: the **Spy** lookout.

**The lookout** has a paper bird ready. If anything unusual happens at the pier — fighting, a second rowboat approaching, figures in the water near the Guild's position — the bird is sent within one round. Three **Thugs** arrive from a Dock Ward staging point within ten minutes of a warning.

Three approaches to neutralize the lookout:

- **Rooftop approach:** A dockside warehouse overlooks the moored vessel. **DC 12 Dexterity (Athletics)** to reach the roof, then **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** to reach the boat's deck from above without being heard.

- **Boat-side approach:** Swimming or rowing close under the moored vessel's shadow. **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** to close to boarding distance; the lookout is watching the water, not the hull.

- **Dock worker cover:** A work crew offloading a nearby vessel provides cover for movement on the pier. **DC 13 Charisma (Deception)** to blend into the activity; Advantage if the party has any dock contacts established in prior play.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### If the Lookout Sends the Bird
> The bird goes and cannot be recalled. The party has ten minutes to complete the dive and extract before three Thugs arrive by rowboat. Krebbyg gives two quick tugs the moment he sees the bird launch — the party knows time is limited. The Guild dive team, warned, becomes alert and will fight aggressively rather than retreating.

### Underwater

Characters enter the water from Krebbyg's rowboat (potions grant one hour of water breathing) and follow his bearing line south toward the wreck. The descent takes two minutes at a normal swimming pace.

**The wreck at forty feet.** The ketch's hull lists to starboard. The main hatch is jammed half-open — enough to enter, enough to fill the interior with silt-disturbed water. Visibility: five feet at best. Sound carries strangely at depth; the party can hear the Guild divers working before they can see them.

**If the Guild team is still staging on the surface:** The party enters the wreck uncontested. The locked document case is in the captain's cabin, aft past the cargo hold. **DC 12 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools or Sleight of Hand)** to open the hasp, or a DC 18 Strength (Athletics) check to break it without losing the contents. The Eye #3 is inside: a smooth stone eyeball, black iris, cold to the touch. It does not glow. Exiting the wreck before the Guild team enters requires surfacing before the party's timing runs out.

**If the Guild team is already in the wreck:** Krenick Durr and one or two Veterans are working aft. In five-foot visibility, the party has one round before contact. Options:

- **Fight:** Standard underwater combat conditions. Short swords only (piercing damage has no Disadvantage). **Krenick Durr** (Veteran stat block) is experienced underwater and does not panic; the other Veterans follow his lead.
- **Ambush:** Characters who entered quietly and moved fast can attempt a **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth)** group check for a surprise round.
- **Wait and take:** Let the Guild divers find the case, then intercept them as they exit the wreck. Higher-risk extraction but avoids fighting in a confined hull.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Merfolk in the Area
> At your discretion, a small group of merfolk observe the operation from deeper water but do not intervene. They have been watching the wreck since it sank. If the party treats them with respect (no aggressive movement toward them), one may surface briefly near Krebbyg's rowboat afterward. Krebbyg, asked about this: "There are always others in the harbor. You learn to work around them." This is a texture detail only — no mechanical function.

### Extraction

The return to the surface with Eye #3 is the mission's most exposed moment. Two threats are potentially active:

**Surviving Guild divers:** Any Veteran who was not neutralized or who fled will attempt to surface and warn or pursue. They are not suicidal — if clearly outmatched, they break off and surface rather than dying for the mission. A Veteran who surfaces intact can shout to the pier (if the lookout is still active) or use the dinghy to row clear.

**The Scarlet Marpenoth crew:** The Eyecatcher is anchored two hundred yards to the north. As the party surfaces, a rope ladder extends from the Scarlet Marpenoth's diving hatch — Jarlaxle's crew saw something moving underwater and is offering a pickup point. Using the rope ladder removes the party from any Guild pursuit immediately but requires the party to swim two hundred yards with whatever Veteran pursuit is still active. Krebbyg's rowboat is the closer option.

Krebbyg, when the party surfaces with the Eye or without it: he reads their hands before he reads their faces. He reaches for the first person over the gunwale first.

If True, Jarlaxle enters the **Sea Maidens Faire** negotiation holding Eye #3 and the BD alliance path opens from a position of strength. If False, Eye #3 is in Guild hands or lost; **Sea Maidens Faire**'s confrontation path becomes the more likely resolution.

## Overview

A sunken wreck, forty feet down, with Eye #3 locked in the captain's cabin. Krebbyg is in a rowboat on the surface. The Guild dive team is already in the water.

## Read Aloud

>
> > "Eye #3. It went into the harbor three tendays ago off a vessel that sank. Xanathar's Guild located the wreck. They have a dive team staging at Pier 17 off Sail Street. We have less than an hour."

> He slides a harbor chart across. A bearing line is marked in pencil — south by southeast from the pier, forty feet down.
>
> > "I have a rowboat. I'll hold position on the surface and run signals on the rope. One slow tug: all clear. Two quick: hold. Three rapid: surface immediately."
>
> He picks up his coat.
>

## Summary

The party neutralized the Guild's surface lookout and descended forty feet to a wrecked ketch in Deepwater Harbor, where Eye #3 of the Stone of Golorr lay sealed in the captain's document case. The Guild dive team was already in the water. Krebbyg coordinated from a rowboat on the surface via rope signals throughout. The Eye came up with the party. Jarlaxle knew by dawn.
