# The Dive

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat and Exploration Event occurs after **Kolat Towers**, in the last hour of darkness, when Fel'rekt Lafeen's watchers see a Xanathar's Guild dive team slip into the water beneath the old Faire pier at Smugglers' Dock. It ends when the party has dealt with the dive team and stands at the charge clamped to the hull of the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, and **Before Dawn** then runs the removal. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take the brief from Krebbyg Masq'il'yr and Fel'rekt, and collect potions of water breathing, spears and lamps from the armory crate.
> - Find the Guild's lookout, Nell Corvane, and take her, bribe her or fool her before she signals.
> - Descend 20 feet to the hull and meet the dive team led by Orlo Stannick.
> - Talk the team down, fight it, or fight it until Orlo accepts terms.
> - Learn from the team's papers, or from Soluun, who sold the Guild the berth.
>
> Only Bregan D'aerthe members receive the brief. Companions can come along and dive, and they gain no Renown. Nobody in this Event says the name Jarlaxle in speech or readaloud unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member being addressed, and the speakers say "the captain" or "J." instead.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Ahmaergo has had the Guild's clerks watching for the captain's submarine for months. The *Scarlet Marpenoth* rode beneath the *Eyecatcher* while the Faire was in harbor, and when the Faire sailed on Tarsakh 20 she came off the collar and went to a reserve berth under the outer end of the old Faire pier at Smugglers' Dock, where the *Heartbreaker* used to tie up. She lies on a sandy bottom in 20 feet of water, moored to the piles by two cables, with her engines cold and her ballast flooded so that nothing on the surface shows her. The captain stayed ashore after the Faire left, with Krebbyg, Fel'rekt and a small team, and the Faire's first mate has the *Eyecatcher*.
>
> Orlo Stannick took the charge down at about 03:30 with a deputy, three divers and two hired merfolk, clamped it beside a ballast seam and set the fuse for 05:30, which is first light on every night this Event can run. Nell Corvane stayed in the launch at the end of the pier to flash a boat crew waiting at the south quay if anything went wrong. Fel'rekt's watcher on Net Street saw the launch tie up at 03:35 and reached him at 03:45.
>
> The Guild learned the berth in one of three ways, and the GM text of **Before Dawn** and the debrief follows whichever applies:
>
> - If **Soluun Expelled** is marked, Soluun sold it. He slept aboard for a year, he knew where she goes when she is off the keel, and he sold the berth and the hours the hatch stands open to the Guild before the Faire sailed. He rides in the Guild's launch tonight as a guide. If **Soluun Killed** is marked instead, he is dead and takes no part.
> - Otherwise, if **Nar'l Active** or **Nar'l Cleared** is marked and **Soluun Killed** is marked, Nar'l Xibrindas leaked it. He has not forgiven the captain for being slow to avenge his brother, and he put the berth on Ahmaergo's desk when the grell was out of the room. If **Nar'l Active** is marked he also hoped it would buy him the case closed.
> - Otherwise the Guild found her by its own harbor search. Two of Ahmaergo's clerks and a paid ferryman logged the gnome engineers' ballast runs along the pier for three nights.
>
> The event never says whether the submarine carries an Eye, and nothing in it depends on who holds any of them. The Guild is after Jarlaxle's means of retreat, and Xanathar himself has not been told.
>
> The captain is Jarlaxle Baenre. Until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for a member, every Bregan D'aerthe speaker in this Event says "the captain" or "J.", and the Guild's people call him "the drow captain". Zardoz Zord never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan.

> [!gamemaster]**The Dawn Clock**
>
> The party tracks time in 5-minute ticks, starting with **8 ticks (40 minutes)** when the last member enters the water at 04:50. If **Soluun Marked a Threat** is marked for any member, Fel'rekt's watch on Soluun gave the party a warning ten minutes earlier, and the clock starts at **9 ticks**, with the party entering the water at 04:45. At 0 ticks the charge detonates at 05:30.
>
> - Swimming from the pier to the hull costs 1 tick, and each attempt to locate, free or defuse the charge costs 1 tick. Jettisoning costs 1 tick before the carry begins.
> - Combat costs no tick until it passes ten rounds. After that, each further five minutes of fighting costs 1 tick.
> - The surface line is a rope tied to the pier's outermost pile and held by Krebbyg. **Two quick tugs** mean the lookout has signalled and a boat is coming, with five minutes to spare. **Three rapid tugs** mean the boat is at the pier. One slow tug means all is well. A character can feel the tugs through the line and never needs a check.

### The Knock

At 03:45 Fel'rekt Lafeen knocks on the door of Trollskull Manor. He breaks the company's usual routine of playbills and circled prices because there is no time for it, and he asks only for the member and anyone the member will vouch for.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Someone knocks on the door of Trollskull Manor hard enough to rattle the latch, and when you open it Fel'rekt Lafeen is standing on the step in a dark cloak with his hand crossbow tucked into his belt. He is out of breath, his white hair has come loose from its tie, and he glances up and down the alley before he looks at you.
>
> > Hi, sorry, I know what time it is. We've got a Guild dive team under the old Faire pier, and they've put something on the captain's boat. Get your boots on and come with me, please, because we have about an hour, and Kreb is already there and panicking.

If a member is not in the room, Fel'rekt goes to that member next and the party follows when it can. A member without **Kreb Unmasked** hears the same speech from Fel'rekt, and Krebbyg stays in his Kreb Sorrush cover at the brief and says "Ask Fel" to anything operational.

> [!social]**The Lieutenant Who Plans**
>
> Fel'rekt Lafeen (Neutral Good, Drow, he/him) :: a bright young drow gunslinger and the captain's logistics lieutenant, who counts his options off on his fingers and would rather disarm anyone than hurt them.
>
> Fel'rekt talks in medium, encouraging sentences and swears in short excited bursts. He checks on Krebbyg in the middle of a thought, and he offers a way out aloud whenever a fight looks likely. He doesn't fight in this Event, because his hands are full of the party's gear and the surface line, and he calls for surrender twice in plain words before he does anything else.
>
> Fel'rekt is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Guild put on the hull and how it works, which he explains in full.
> - What the party needs to carry and how long it has.
> - Why the crew isn't doing this themselves, which he answers honestly.
>
> Specific dialogue for Fel'rekt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**What did they put on her?**
>
> Fel'rekt counts three fingers down as he answers.
>
> > It's a ship-breaker, which is two kegs of smokepowder in a case with a clamp and a clockwork fuse set for first light. It's got a pressure trigger too, so if you rip it off wrong, the fuse speeds up. That's the whole problem. Breena says it's sitting right beside a ballast seam, and if it goes off on the hull the boat's gone.

> [!qna]**Why can't the crew move her?**
>
> > Because the charge is clamped to her, so moving her just takes the charge along, and she's cold. Breena flooded the ballast and shut the engines down so the Guild couldn't see her from the surface, and it takes an hour to bring her up. Breena put her people ashore on the pier, and she's sitting in the control room by herself, and she's furious.

> [!qna]**Who sent them?**
>
> > Ahmaergo's clerks, going by the boat, and Ahmaergo does everything by procedure, so there'll be a lookout. Nobody knows how they found her, and I'd really like to ask somebody. There's another way than killing the lot of them, I think, but let's see what the divers want to do.

> [!social]**The Harbor Chart**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: a young drow gunslinger and the captain's fixer, who runs the Seven Masks' bookings as Kreb Sorrush and is frightened for a boat he has never once been allowed to pilot.
>
> Krebbyg talks in run-on sentences that jump from the pier to the soprano and back without a breath, and he swears constantly and without malice. He calls everyone "darling", he answers a serious question with "Ask Fel", and he checks a schedule in the middle of a sentence even at this hour. He holds the surface line during the dive, and he does not go into the water and says so cheerfully. He wants the boat saved, the divers talked to and nobody dead who doesn't need to be.
>
> Krebbyg is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The pier, the line and the signals, which he gives in full.
> - The ghost, whom he complains about for one sentence.
> - Why he does not dive, which he answers with a joke and does not explain.
>
> Specific dialogue for Krebbyg on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**How does the line work?**
>
> Krebbyg tugs the end of a coil of rope to show you.
>
> > It's tied to the outermost pile on the pier, and I hold the dry end, darling, and you carry the wet end down with you. One slow tug from me means all's well, two quick tugs mean the lookout's signalled and there's a boat coming, and three fast ones mean the boat's on the pier and for fuck's sake get out of the water. >
> > Ask Fel. No, the captain says there's a purse if you save her, and I'm not allowed to say how much, because I'd say it wrong and then he'd have to pay it. But he's very grateful already, he said so twice, and he said it with his hat off.

Fel'rekt supplies the party from a crate at the foot of the stairs.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Crate Holds**
>
> The crate holds one *potion of water breathing* for each member and each companion who will dive, and each potion lasts 24 hours. It also holds three sealed brass glow-globes from Breena's stores, each good for 10 feet of bright light underwater, a rack of spears and shortswords, and a coil of line. The party has no swim speed unless a character already has one, so it covers 15 feet a round in the water and has Disadvantage on melee attacks with any weapon that doesn't deal piercing damage. The spears and shortswords are there for that reason, and Fel'rekt says so. Ranged weapons automatically miss beyond their normal range and have Disadvantage within it, and anything underwater has Resistance to fire damage. Firearms don't fire.

### The Pier

The old Faire pier at Smugglers' Dock is a long stone-and-timber quay with a customs shed and a chandlery at its foot, and the *Heartbreaker* tied up at its outer end until the twentieth of Tarsakh. The water off the outer end is murky and 20 feet deep, and the *Scarlet Marpenoth* lies beneath it with her hull brushing the silt. A Guild launch is tied up at the end of the pier with a shuttered lantern in the bow, and a hooded figure sits in the stern.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The pier is dark and nearly empty at this hour, with the chandlery shuttered behind you and a cold wind off the harbor smelling of tar and fish scales. Out at the far end a small launch is tied against the outermost pile with its oars shipped and a covered lantern sitting on the bow thwart. A shape is hunched in the stern, and further along the quay another shape sits very still beside a stack of crates, and neither of them moves as the wind picks at their cloaks.

> [!exploration]**Spotting the Lookout**
>
> Any character who studies the launch from the chandlery's shadow for a minute and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** check sees that the hooded figure in the stern keeps turning its head toward the south quay, and that the shuttered lantern is angled to throw light that way. The character concludes that the figure is a lookout with a signal, and that a second boat is waiting on the quay beyond the pier.
>
> - **Advantage:** The member is marked **Soluun Pressed** with the Mooring result, and remembers how Soluun described the hatch and the angles of the pier.
> - **Disadvantage:** The party approaches carrying an unshuttered lamp.
> - **Failure:** The party cannot place the lookout and has to approach along the pier or from the water, and the lookout has Advantage on the check to spot the party.

> [!social]**The Lookout in the Launch**
>
> Nell Corvane (Neutral, Human, she/her) :: a Guild lookout with a shuttered lantern and a long habit of being paid on time.
>
> Nell has been watching harbors for the Guild for six years. She is calm and professional, she doesn't want to die for the dive leader's bonus, and she will flash the lantern the moment she sees anything that isn't the dive team. She is happy to be bought, if the price is right and the threat is credible, because she knows how Ahmaergo's clerks treat anyone who brings back a failure. She keeps a hand crossbow under her cloak and uses it only to buy time for the signal.
>
> Nell is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Her price, if the party offers one.
> - What the Guild promised the divers, which she knows and will sell.
> - Who is in the launch with her, if anyone.

There are three ways to deal with Nell, and the party can try more than one if a failed attempt leaves her unaware.

> [!exploration]**Taking Nell**
>
> **From the water.** Any character who slips into the harbor beside the pier and makes a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check, as a Group Check, comes up under the launch's shadow and pulls Nell over the side before she reaches the lantern. She is held, gagged and bound, and Fel'rekt takes her to the chandlery. No signal is sent.
>
> **From the pier.** Any character who works along the crates and makes a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check, as a Group Check, reaches the launch from the quay before she turns around. The effect is the same.
>
> **As an inspector.** Any character who walks out along the pier with a ledger and makes a successful **DC 14 Charisma (Deception)** check as a harbor inspector holds Nell in conversation about her mooring fee until the party is in the water. She stays free and keeps her bird until the party has gone down, and then she sends it.
>
> **Buying her.** Any character who offers Nell 150 gp and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check persuades her to look at the water until dawn. She takes the money and she says nothing to the Guild afterward, because she is not going to tell Ahmaergo that she took coin from the other side. A bought lookout does not stop the charge and does not signal, and she keeps the gold.
>
> If a check fails, Nell flashes the lantern in three long pulses toward the south quay, and the signal is sent. She then drops over the side of the launch and runs along the pier toward the quay.
>
> - **Auto:** **Soluun Marked a Threat** is marked for a member who is present. Fel'rekt's watch on Soluun has told the party which launch to watch, and the next check to approach her unseen has Advantage for that member.
> - **Advantage:** The character is a member of Bregan D'aerthe who has marked **Soluun Pressed** with the Mooring result.
> - **Disadvantage:** The character's armor is metal, noisy or both.

If the signal is sent, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A hooded figure in the launch snaps a shutter back, and three long pulses of yellow light go out across the dark harbor toward the south quay. She is over the side of the launch and running along the pier before the light has finished fading, and the boards drum under her boots.

If the party cannot reach her, she gets clear along the quay and **Guild Survivor Escaped** is marked at the end of the Event. If a character catches her within two rounds, she fights as a **Spy** only to buy a second signal. The Spy fights to the end if she is cornered, because the Guild punishes a lookout who surrenders.

> [!hazard]**The Lookout**
>
> Nell Corvane uses the **Spy** stat block from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, with a hand crossbow in place of the shortbow. She starts in the launch's stern.
>
> #### Nell's Tactics
> Nell opens by flashing the lantern if she has not done so, and then she tries to get clear of the party.
>
> During combat, Nell:
> - Uses Cunning Action to Dash along the pier toward the south quay and the Guild's boat.
> - Shoots at a character who closes with her only if she cannot run.
> - Hides behind the crates and the launch's hull to break line of sight, and flashes the lantern again if it is within reach.
>
> If Nell is reduced to 10 Hit Points or fewer she keeps fighting, and she dies or flees. She does not surrender. The fight is a single Mild encounter at worst, and it ends when she is dead, captured or out of sight.

If **Soluun Expelled** is marked, Soluun is in the launch's bow, with his hand crossbow in his belt and his silver disc gone from his neck. He sold the berth to the Guild's divers before the Faire sailed, and he rides tonight as the Guild's guide. Any character who reaches the launch sees him, and the sale comes to light. Mark **Soluun Sold the Mooring**.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A drow sits in the bow of the launch with a hand crossbow in his lap and no silver at his throat, and he looks up at you without any surprise. His coat is stiff with grime, and he doesn't raise the crossbow.
>
> > Surface-blood, and the captain's pets. You're late, but I'd have expected that. I told the Guild which hours the hatch stands open, and I told them where she lies, and the captain taught me to sell what's worth selling.

If **Soluun Marked a Threat** is marked for a member who is present, Soluun picks that member out and says, "I said I'd remember your face." If **Soluun Provoked** is marked for a member who is present, he looks first at that member and touches his jaw. If **Soluun Pleaded For** is marked for a member who is present, he looks at that member last and says nothing at all.

Soluun does not fight in the launch. He wants the Guild to finish what it has started, and he wants the captain to see it, and he leaves the moment anyone draws a weapon on him. He steps onto the pier, goes along the quay and over the side, and the harbor carries him off. If a character blocks his way and corners him, he fights as the WDH **Drow Gunslinger** (CR 4) with his hand crossbow and shortsword until he is reduced to 20 Hit Points or fewer. He then dives into the harbor and is gone, and he is not killed in this Event. Mark **Guild Survivor Escaped** in either case.

### Into the Water

The party enters the water at 04:50 from the pier's outer end, or at 04:45 if the extra tick applies. The dive to the hull takes the first tick of the Dawn Clock. Krebbyg holds the line at the outermost pile, and Fel'rekt waits on the quay with a crossbow at the shadowed chandlery end.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The harbor is cold and black, and the glow-globe in your hand lights a sphere of green water no wider than ten feet. Silt drifts through the light in slow flakes as you kick down along the pier's outer pile, and the line trails behind you and tugs once, gently, and goes slack. Twenty feet down, a long curved shape rises out of the dark, brass-bright where the lamplight touches it, with two cables running from its flank up toward the pier and the bubbles of the divers rising from its far side.

> [!gamemaster]**Underwater Rules**
>
> Use the 2024 underwater rules as written. A creature without a Swim Speed has Disadvantage on melee attack rolls with any weapon that doesn't deal piercing damage, and every foot of movement costs 1 extra foot, so a Speed 30 character covers 15 feet a round. A ranged weapon attack automatically misses beyond its normal range and has Disadvantage within it. Anything underwater has Resistance to fire damage. Deepwater Harbor at night counts as murky water, and visibility is 10 feet, or more in the glow-globe's light. The potions of water breathing mean that nobody needs to hold a breath. The *Scarlet Marpenoth*'s hull is not electrified tonight, because Breena has pulled the switch so that the party can touch her.

### The Dive Team

The dive team is working at the clamp when the party arrives. The charge is a squat steel case the size of a man's chest, clamped to the hull beside a ballast seam with a brass magnetic grip and a hook, and the divers hold station around it. Orlo Stannick has a lanyard looped round his wrist, and the other end runs to the charge.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Team Is Doing**
>
> The team does not know that Bregan D'aerthe is coming, and the divers are not on high alert. Orlo is floating above the case with a hand on the lanyard, because the Guild's order was to stay until the fuse had run. Tamsin Rooke (if present) is at the stern seam, and the divers keep a line between the case and the pier. The merfolk hover at the edge of the light. Their usual task is to watch the water.
>
> The charge itself is described in **Before Dawn**. The lanyard is Orlo's insurance, and if he sees the party reach the clamp he pulls it at the start of his next turn, which sets the fuse to a 10-round countdown. The lanyard is AC 12 with 1 Hit Point, and a slashing weapon cuts it with an action, or a character can make a **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check to unhook it from his wrist without his noticing.

> [!exploration]**Reaching the Clamp Unseen**
>
> Any character who watches the divers' bubbles for a round and makes a successful **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth)** check, as a Group Check, finds the gap between the Guild line and the hull and reaches the clamp from the dark side. On a success the party acts first, and Orlo does not pull the lanyard before it can be cut. On a failure the team has noticed the party, and the party rolls initiative without Advantage.
>
> - **Advantage:** The party puts out its glow-globes and works by the light of the divers' own lamps.
> - **Disadvantage:** A character wears metal armor.

> [!social]**The Salvage Diver**
>
> Orlo Stannick (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: the Guild's dive leader, a weathered man with scarred hands who has pulled wrecks off Waterdeep's harbor floor for the Guild for years.
>
> Orlo is practical and unemotional, and he doesn't enjoy this job. He has no love for the Guild's clerks, who pay a day late and count the dead from the other column. He will take a fair offer if one comes before the fighting turns against him, and he will not go back to the Guild afterward, because he knows what Ahmaergo does with a diver who comes home empty. He talks in short flat sentences, and he swears plainly when he is frightened.
>
> Orlo is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The terms, if the party offers any.
> - The charge, which he explains if he is asked and has accepted terms.
> - How the Guild found her, which he knows in outline.

> [!qna]**Call it off.**
>
> Orlo holds the lanyard loosely and watches your hands before he answers.
>
> > Call it off, or I pull this and you've got ten rounds to swim. I'm not paid enough to drown with you, so tell me what you're offering, and be fast, because I can't hold this much longer.

> [!qna]**What are the terms?**
>
> > You let us surface and go ashore with our lives, and I tell you where the clamp is and what the fuse is set to, and I call off the lanyard. I keep my own kit, and the Guild keeps its charge. If you want more than that, I'll fight, and I'd rather not, because I'm not good at it and I'm very good at diving.

> [!qna]**Who sold her berth?**
>
> If **Soluun Expelled** is marked, Orlo answers:
>
> > A drow in a Guild launch sold it to Ahmaergo's clerks before the Faire sailed, and he said he'd come tonight to watch it go. He's up there now, if he hasn't run, and he wasn't happy about the price.
>
> If **Nar'l Active** or **Nar'l Cleared** and **Soluun Killed** are marked, Orlo answers:
>
> > Ahmaergo had it from the Guild's own court, a soft-spoken drow in a Guild coat who gave it up over tea. I wasn't in the room, and I don't know the man's name.
>
> Otherwise Orlo answers:
>
> > The clerks logged your gnomes hauling ballast along the pier for three nights, and a ferryman on the Net Street steps told them the rest. Nobody sold it to us. We watched, and your people were careless.

If the party hears Orlo out and makes the terms above, he accepts them if a character makes a successful **DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check. He accepts at once if the party offers all four terms and one diver has already fallen.

- **Advantage:** The party offers the terms before any blow is struck, or Fel'rekt's call for surrender reaches the water.
- **Disadvantage:** Soluun's sale has come to light and the party has attacked the launch.
- **Failure:** Orlo says "Then it's the hard way," and the fight begins, though he still accepts terms later.

When Orlo accepts terms he calls off the lanyard, drops his trident and signals the divers to surface. If **Nell Corvane** was taken without a signal, no one signals, and the divers surface at the chandlery steps into Fel'rekt's hands. Fel'rekt takes them to a cellar under the Seven Masks, feeds them and asks them gentle questions, and he lets them go at the south quay at noon, with their lives and nothing else.

> [!hazard]**The Guild Dive Team**
>
> The team uses the **Warrior Veteran**, **Bandit Captain**, **Tough** and **Merfolk Skirmisher** stat blocks from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, with these changes for the water. Orlo's Greatsword becomes a trident (+5 to hit, 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) with two hands), and he keeps his Heavy Crossbow, which he does not use because ranged attacks are at Disadvantage underwater. > #### The Dive Team's Tactics
> At the start of combat, Orlo reaches for the lanyard if he is within reach of it, and the rest of the team closes on the nearest character who is holding a tool or weapon near the clamp.
>
> During combat, the team:
> - Has Orlo pull the lanyard in his first turn if the party is within 5 feet of the clamp and has not parleyed.
> - Has the Toughs fight in pairs, using Pack Tactics against the character closest to the charge.
> - Has Tamsin hold the stern seam and cover the divers, retreating to the surface at half her Hit Points.
> - Has the merfolk strike at characters who are carrying the charge or cutting the lanyard, using their swim speed to switch targets.
>

The fight ends when the team is dead, captured or fled.

> The fight is a Bruising encounter in the **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference**. The party does not need to win it to remove the charge, because a successful parley or an unseen approach settles it without a blow.

Any character who hears the ticking and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check understands that the fuse has jumped to a 10-round countdown and that the case must be carried clear of the hull.

## Overview

A Guild launch sits at the end of the old Faire pier and divers are working beneath it. The party swims down to find them at the hull of Bregan D'aerthe's submarine.

## Summary

Fel'rekt woke us before dawn with news that Xanathar's Guild was under the old Faire pier, and Krebbyg gave us potions and spears and a rope to hold. We took the lookout in the launch and went down twenty feet to the hull, where a dive leader named Orlo Stannick was guarding a steel case clamped beside a seam. How long did we have before first light?
