# The Dive

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat/Exploration Event takes place at night in Deepwater Harbor, split between the surface and a wrecked ketch at forty feet. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the brief from Krebbyg at Trollskull Manor near midnight and ask what they need to know
> - Neutralize the Guild's surface lookout before entering the water — a warning bird changes the entire operation
> - Descend to the wreck and recover Eye #3 before the Guild dive team, or take it from them if they arrive first
> - Return to the surface with Eye #3 and reach Krebbyg's rowboat or the Scarlet Marpenoth's rope ladder
>
> #### Underwater Conditions
>
> Standard underwater combat conditions apply throughout: no verbal communication, half movement speed without a swim speed, Disadvantage on all weapon attack rolls that do not use a piercing or thrusting weapon. Short swords have no Disadvantage; axes, clubs and hammers do.
>
> #### Background
>
> Eye #3 of the Stone of Golorr went into the harbor during a confrontation aboard a hired ketch three tendays ago. The ketch went down within minutes; most of the crew swam clear, but the Eye sank with the cargo. Jarlaxle's harbor network identified the wreck's position four days after the sinking. Xanathar's people identified it independently a tenday later, and their dive team has been staging for two nights, waiting for calm water and a dark tide.
>
> The wreck: a forty-foot ketch settled at forty feet on a slight list to starboard, hull intact. Visibility inside the hull is less than five feet, murky harbor water and silt disturbed by the sinking. Eye #3 is in the captain's cabin aft, in a sealed document case. The case is locked; opening it requires a **DC 12 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools or Sleight of Hand)** check, or breaking the hasp with a **DC 18 Strength (Athletics)** check without losing the contents.
>
> The Guild dive team: **Krenick Durr** (Veteran, team leader) and one or two additional **Veterans** depending on escalation tier. All consumed *potions of water breathing* before entering the water. They carry short swords and know to use them underwater. They do not know Bregan D'aerthe is coming. They know exactly what they are looking for.
>
> **Krebbyg** is in a hired rowboat positioned thirty yards off Pier 17, showing no light. Rope signals: **one slow tug** = all clear; **two quick tugs** = stop, hold position; **three rapid tugs** = surface immediately, emergency. The party ties off to the rope before descending.

### The Brief

Krebbyg arrives at Trollskull Manor near midnight.

> [!readaloud]
> The knock at the door is quiet and does not repeat. Krebbyg comes in without waiting, sets four stoppered vials on the table before anyone speaks, and puts a harbor chart down beside them. A bearing line is marked in pencil, running south by southeast from Pier 17 to a point forty feet down. He is wearing dark clothing and his coat is damp.
>
> > "Eye #3. It went into the harbor three tendays ago off a vessel that sank. Xanathar's Guild located the wreck. They have a dive team staging at Pier 17 off Sail Street. We have less than an hour."
>
> He taps the bearing line on the chart.
>
> > "I have a rowboat. I'll hold position on the surface and run signals on the rope. One slow tug: all clear. Two quick: hold. Three rapid: surface immediately."
>
> He picks up his coat.
>
> > "I don't dive."

> [!social]**The Theater Manager**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Elf (Drow), he/him) :: Bregan D'aerthe operative who runs Seven Masks Theater's bookings; in a rowboat tonight, thirty yards from a Guild staging area, holding the party's lifeline.
>
> Krebbyg is maximally concise, which communicates the urgency better than anything else he could do. He has done twenty years of this kind of work. This is the most exposed he has been across the entire BD mission chain; he is personally in a rowboat that a Guild patrol could find, with no cover and no reinforcements nearby. He will not explain why he does not dive. He delivers it as a fact and does not return to it.
>
> His care for the party shows in ways he will not name: he checked the bearing twice, set out four potions even if there are only three people diving, and gives the rope signals verbally three times to make sure they are remembered. When the party surfaces, his eyes go to their hands before their faces.
>
> Topics Krebbyg is willing to discuss:
> - The rope signals, which he will repeat as many times as asked
> - The surface lookout on the moored fishing vessel and why he has to be handled before anyone enters the water
> - What the Eye looks like: a smooth stone eyeball, black iris, cold to the touch; it does not glow

> [!qna]**How many Guild divers?**
>
> > "Two or three, depending on what they could pull tonight. Krenick Durr is leading — Veteran, experienced, he has done harbor work before. They have short swords and they know to use them."

> [!qna]**What if the lookout sends the bird?**
>
> > "Ten minutes until three Thugs arrive by rowboat, and the dive team stops retreating. Don't let the bird go."

> [!qna]**Why don't you dive?**
>
> He looks at the question for a moment.
>
> > "I don't."

Proceed to **The Pier**.

### The Pier

Pier 17 extends into a stretch of harbor where the dock lights do not reach. The Guild staging area is visible from the pier entrance.

> [!readaloud]
> Pier 17 is dark except for the distant lights of vessels anchored further out. A hired dinghy is tied at the pier's outer end, two figures crouching at the waterline and sorting through equipment by feel. Forty yards to their left, a third figure sits aboard a moored fishing vessel with a spyglass to his eye, watching the harbor with the patient attention of someone who expects to be useful soon, and the water around the dinghy is flat and still.

The lookout uses the **Spy** stat block and has a paper bird ready. If anything unusual happens at the pier — fighting, a second rowboat approaching, or figures in the water near the Guild's staging area — the bird is sent within one round, and three **Thugs** arrive from a Dock Ward staging point within ten minutes.

> [!exploration]**Rooftop Approach**
>
> A dockside warehouse overlooks the moored vessel from the east. A **DC 12 Dexterity (Athletics)** check reaches the roof; then a **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check to reach the vessel's deck from above without being heard.

> [!exploration]**Boat-Side Approach**
>
> Swimming or rowing close under the moored vessel's shadow. A **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check to close to boarding distance; the lookout is watching the water ahead, not the hull beneath him.

> [!exploration]**Dock Worker Cover**
>
> A work crew offloading a nearby vessel provides cover for movement on the pier. A **DC 13 Charisma (Deception)** check to blend into the activity; Advantage if the party has dock contacts established in prior play.

> [!gamemaster]**If the Lookout Sends the Bird**
>
> The bird launches and cannot be recalled. The party has ten minutes to complete the dive and extract before three Thugs arrive by rowboat. Krebbyg sends two quick tugs the moment he sees the bird go, so the party knows the clock has started. The Guild dive team, warned, fights aggressively rather than retreating.

### Underwater

Characters enter the water from Krebbyg's rowboat, using the potions. Each potion grants one hour of water breathing. The descent to the wreck takes about two minutes at a normal swimming pace.

> [!readaloud]
> The harbor water is cold and dark past the first few feet, and the bottom comes up slowly from the murk, old anchor chain and silt and the scatter of broken cargo that preceded the ketch down. Then the hull resolves out of the dark: forty feet down, listing to starboard, the main hatch jammed half-open. The water that got in when she sank stirred everything off the bottom and it has not settled. Visibility inside is less than five feet.

If the **Guild team is still staging on the surface**, the party enters the wreck uncontested. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> The interior of the hull is still and dark. Past the cargo hold, the captain's cabin is beyond an open doorway, a desk overturned by the sinking, loose papers drifting against the bulkhead, and a document case wedged tight against the aft wall.

If the **Guild team is already in the wreck**, the party can hear them working before they can see them. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Before you can see anything past the jammed hatch, you can hear movement in the aft section, something scraping metal and the muffled knock of someone working a door in five-foot visibility. They have not heard you yet.

> [!exploration]**Retrieve the Document Case**
>
> The locked case is in the captain's cabin, wedged against the aft bulkhead. A **DC 12 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools or Sleight of Hand)** check opens the hasp. A **DC 18 Strength (Athletics)** check breaks it without losing the contents. Eye #3 is inside: a smooth stone eyeball, black iris, cold to the touch, that does not glow.

> [!hazard]**Krenick Durr and the Guild Team**
>
> **Krenick Durr** (Veteran) and one or two additional **Veterans** are equipped with short swords and know to use them in the confined space.
>
> At the start of combat, Krenick uses the five-foot visibility to his advantage: any character who moves into his reach on their first turn does so without seeing him until they are within five feet. Over the course of combat, he positions himself between the party and the document case and stays there. The battle ends when the Guild team is neutralized, retreats to the surface, or when the party takes the case and exits the wreck.
>
> **Options in the wreck:**
> - **Fight:** Short swords have no Disadvantage underwater. All other melee weapons suffer Disadvantage.
> - **Ambush:** Characters who entered quietly and moved fast can attempt a group **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth)** check to gain a surprise round.
> - **Wait and take:** Let the Guild divers find the case, then intercept them as they exit the wreck. Higher-risk extraction but avoids fighting in a confined hull.

> [!gamemaster]**Merfolk in the Area**
>
> A small group of merfolk are observing the operation from deeper water. They do not intervene. If the party treats them with respect — no aggressive movement toward them — one may surface briefly near Krebbyg's rowboat afterward. Krebbyg, asked about this: "There are always others in the harbor. You learn to work around them." This is texture only; there is no mechanical function.

### Extraction

The return to the surface with Eye #3 is the mission's most exposed moment.

> [!readaloud]
> The harbor surface is dark and quiet. Krebbyg's rowboat is thirty yards off the pier, showing no light. Two hundred yards to the north, the Scarlet Marpenoth lies at anchor, and as you surface a rope ladder extends from a hatch near the waterline where Jarlaxle's crew has been watching.

**Surviving Guild divers** who were not neutralized will attempt to surface and warn or pursue. They are not suicidal — if clearly outmatched, they break off and surface rather than dying for the job. A Veteran who surfaces intact can shout to the pier (if the lookout is still active) or use the dinghy to row clear.

**The Scarlet Marpenoth's rope ladder** offers an immediate pickup that removes the party from any Guild pursuit, but it requires swimming two hundred yards with whatever pursuit is still active. Krebbyg's rowboat is the closer option.

When the party surfaces, Krebbyg's eyes go to their hands before their faces. He reaches for the first person over the gunwale.

## Overview

Eye #3 of the Stone of Golorr is in a wrecked ketch at forty feet in Deepwater Harbor, sealed in the captain's document case. Krebbyg is in a rowboat on the surface with a rope signal system. The Xanathar Guild dive team is staging at Pier 17.

## Summary

The party neutralized the Guild's surface lookout and descended forty feet to a wrecked ketch in Deepwater Harbor, where Eye #3 of the Stone of Golorr lay sealed in the captain's document case. The Guild dive team was in the water. Krebbyg coordinated from a rowboat on the surface via rope signals throughout. The Eye came up with the party, and Jarlaxle knew by dawn.
