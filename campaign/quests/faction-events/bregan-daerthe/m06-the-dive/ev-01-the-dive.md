# The Dive

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat and Exploration Event occurs after **Kolat Towers**, in the last hour of darkness. Fel'rekt Lafeen's watchers see a Xanathar's Guild dive team slip into the water beneath the old Faire pier at Smugglers' Dock. It ends when the party has dealt with the dive team and stands at the charge clamped to the hull of the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. **Before Dawn** then runs the removal. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take the brief from Krebbyg Masq'il'yr and Fel'rekt, and collect potions of water breathing, spears and lamps from the armory crate.
> - Find the Guild's lookout, Nell Corvane, and take her, bribe her or fool her before she signals.
> - Descend 20 feet to the hull and meet the dive team led by Orlo Stannick.
> - Talk the team down, fight it, or fight it until Orlo accepts terms.
> - Learn from the team's papers, or from Soluun, who sold the Guild the berth.
>
> Only Bregan D'aerthe members receive the brief. Companions can come along and dive, and they gain no Renown. Nobody says the name Jarlaxle in speech or readaloud unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member being addressed. The speakers say "the captain" or "J." instead.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Ahmaergo has had the Guild's clerks watching for the captain's submarine for months. The *Scarlet Marpenoth* rode beneath the *Eyecatcher* while the Faire was in harbor. When the Faire sailed on Tarsakh 20 she came off the collar and went to a reserve berth under the outer end of the old Faire pier at Smugglers' Dock, where the *Heartbreaker* used to tie up.
>
> - She lies on a sandy bottom in 20 feet of water, moored to the piles by two cables.
> - Her engines are cold and her ballast is flooded, so nothing on the surface shows her.
> - The captain stayed ashore with Krebbyg, Fel'rekt and a small team. The Faire's first mate has the *Eyecatcher*.
>
> Orlo Stannick took the charge down at about 03:30 with a deputy, three divers and two hired merfolk. He clamped it beside a ballast seam and set the fuse for 05:30, which is first light on every night this Event can run. Nell Corvane stayed in the launch at the end of the pier to flash a boat crew waiting at the south quay. Fel'rekt's watcher on Net Street saw the launch tie up at 03:35 and reached him at 03:45.
>
> The Guild learned the berth in one of three ways. The GM text of **Before Dawn** and the debrief follows whichever applies:
>
> - If **Soluun Expelled** is marked, Soluun sold it. He slept aboard for a year and knew where she goes when she is off the keel. Before the Faire sailed he sold the Guild the berth and the hours the hatch stands open, and the Guild seized him once the deal was done. He rides in the Guild's launch tonight as a prisoner made to guide it. If Force Grey's **Destroy the Intellect Factory** marked **Captive Freed** with Soluun as the captive, he is free and stays out of it, and the Guild works from what he sold them. If **Soluun Killed** is marked instead, he is dead and takes no part.
> - Otherwise, if **Nar'l Active** or **Nar'l Cleared** is marked and **Soluun Killed** is marked, Nar'l Xibrindas leaked it. He has not forgiven the captain for being slow to avenge his brother, and he left the berth on Ahmaergo's desk when the grell was out of the room. If **Nar'l Active** is marked, he also hoped it would buy him the case closed.
> - Otherwise the Guild found her by its own harbor search. Two of Ahmaergo's clerks and a paid ferryman logged the gnome engineers' ballast runs along the pier for three nights.
>
> The event never says whether the submarine carries an Eye, and nothing in it depends on who holds any of them. The Guild is after Jarlaxle's means of retreat, and Xanathar has not been told.
>
> The captain is Jarlaxle Baenre. Until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for a member, every Bregan D'aerthe speaker says "the captain" or "J.", and the Guild's people call him "the drow captain". Zardoz Zord never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan.

> [!gamemaster]**The Dawn Clock**
>
> The party tracks time in 5-minute ticks. At 0 ticks the charge detonates at 05:30.
>
> - **Start:** 8 ticks (40 minutes) when the last member enters the water at 04:50.
> - **Early warning:** If **Soluun Marked a Threat** is marked for any member, Fel'rekt's watch on Soluun gave the party a warning ten minutes earlier. The party enters at 04:45 and the clock starts at **9 ticks**.
> - **Costs:** Swimming from the pier to the hull costs 1 tick. Each attempt to locate, free or defuse the charge costs 1 tick. Jettisoning costs 1 tick before the carry begins.
> - **Combat:** It costs no tick until it passes ten rounds. After that, each further five minutes of fighting costs 1 tick.
> - **Surface line:** A rope tied to the pier's outermost pile and held by Krebbyg. Characters feel the tugs through the line and need no check.
>   - **Two quick tugs:** The lookout has signalled and a boat is coming, with five minutes to spare.
>   - **Three rapid tugs:** The boat is at the pier.
>   - **One slow tug:** All is well.

### The Knock

At 03:45 Fel'rekt Lafeen knocks on the door of Trollskull Manor and asks only for the member. He skips the usual routine of playbills and circled prices, because there is no time. A companion the member will vouch for can come along and waits at the stage door during the brief.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Someone knocks on the door of Trollskull Manor hard enough to rattle the latch. When you open it, Fel'rekt Lafeen is standing on the step in a dark cloak with a hand crossbow tucked into his belt. He is out of breath and his white hair has come loose from its tie, and he glances up and down the alley before he looks at you.
>
> > Hi, sorry, I know what time it is. There's a Guild dive team under the old Faire pier, and they've put something on the captain's boat. Get your boots on, please. We've got about an hour, and Kreb is at the theater panicking.

- If a member is not in the room, Fel'rekt goes to that member next and the party follows when it can.
- A member without **Kreb Unmasked** hears the same speech. Krebbyg stays in his Kreb Sorrush cover at the brief and says "Ask Fel" to anything operational.

> [!social]**The Lieutenant Who Plans**
>
> Fel'rekt Lafeen (Neutral Good, Drow, he/him) :: a bright young drow gunslinger and the captain's logistics lieutenant, who counts his options off on his fingers and would rather disarm anyone than hurt them.
>
> Fel'rekt talks in medium, encouraging sentences and swears in short excited bursts. He checks on Krebbyg mid-thought and offers a way out whenever a fight looks likely. He doesn't fight in this Event, because his hands are full of the party's gear and the surface line. He calls for surrender twice in plain words before he does anything else.
>
> Fel'rekt is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Guild put on the hull and how it works.
> - What the party needs to carry and how long it has.
> - Why the crew isn't doing this themselves.
>
> Specific dialogue for Fel'rekt on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**What did they put on her?**
>
> Fel'rekt counts three fingers down as he answers.
>
> > It's a ship-breaker. Two kegs of smokepowder in a case, with a clamp and a clockwork fuse set for first light. There's a pressure trigger too, so if you rip it off wrong, the fuse speeds up. Breena says it's right beside a ballast seam, and if it goes off on the hull, the boat's gone.

> [!qna]**Why can't the crew move her?**
>
> > She's cold. Breena flooded the ballast and shut the engines down so the Guild couldn't see her, and it takes an hour to bring her up. Even then, the charge is clamped to her, so it would just come along. Breena put her people ashore, and she's sitting in the control room by herself, and she's furious.

> [!qna]**Who sent them?**
>
> > Ahmaergo's clerks, going by the boat. He does everything by procedure, so there'll be a lookout. I'd really like to know how they found her. There's another way than killing the lot of them, I think, so let's see what the divers want to do.

### The Pier

The old Faire pier at Smugglers' Dock is a long stone-and-timber quay with a customs shed and a chandlery at its foot. The *Heartbreaker* tied up at its outer end until the twentieth of Tarsakh. The water off the outer end is murky and 20 feet deep, and the *Scarlet Marpenoth* lies beneath it. A Guild launch is tied up at the end of the pier with a shuttered lantern in the bow and a hooded figure in the stern.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The pier is dark and nearly empty, with the chandlery shuttered behind you and a cold wind off the harbor smelling of tar and fish scales. Out at the far end a small launch is tied against the outermost pile with its oars shipped and a covered lantern on the bow thwart. A shape is hunched in the stern, and further along the quay another sits very still beside a stack of crates. Neither moves as the wind picks at their cloaks.

> [!exploration]**Spotting the Lookout**
>
> Any character who studies the launch from the chandlery's shadow for a minute and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** check sees that the hooded figure keeps turning toward the south quay, and that the shuttered lantern is angled that way. The character concludes that the figure is a lookout with a signal, and that a second boat waits on the quay beyond the pier.
>
> - **Advantage:** The member is marked **Soluun Pressed** with the Mooring result, and remembers how Soluun described the hatch and the angles of the pier.
> - **Disadvantage:** The party approaches carrying an unshuttered lamp.
> - **Failure:** The party cannot place the lookout and has to approach along the pier or from the water. The lookout has Advantage on the check to spot the party.

> [!social]**The Lookout in the Launch**
>
> Nell Corvane (Neutral, Human, she/her) :: a Guild lookout with a shuttered lantern and a long habit of being paid on time.
>
> Nell has watched harbors for the Guild for six years. She is calm and professional, she doesn't want to die for the dive leader's bonus, and she flashes the lantern the moment she sees anyone who isn't the dive team. She can be bought if the price is right and the threat is credible, because she knows how Ahmaergo's clerks treat anyone who brings back a failure. She keeps a hand crossbow under her cloak and uses it only to buy time for the signal.
>
> Nell is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Her price, if the party offers one.
> - What the Guild promised the divers, which she knows and will sell.
> - Who is in the launch with her, if anyone.

There are four ways to deal with Nell, and the party can try more than one if a failed attempt leaves her unaware.

> [!exploration]**Taking Nell**
>
> - **From the water.** A character who slips into the harbor beside the pier and makes a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check, as a Group Check, comes up under the launch's shadow and pulls Nell over the side before she reaches the lantern. She is held, gagged and bound, and Fel'rekt takes her to the chandlery. No signal is sent.
> - **From the pier.** A character who works along the crates and makes a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check, as a Group Check, reaches the launch from the quay before she turns around. The effect is the same.
> - **As an inspector.** A character who walks out along the pier with a ledger and makes a successful **DC 14 Charisma (Deception)** check as a harbor inspector holds Nell in conversation about her mooring fee until the party is in the water. She stays free and keeps her lantern until the party has gone down, and then she flashes it.
> - **Buying her.** A character who offers Nell 150 gp and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check persuades her to look at the water until dawn. She keeps the gold and says nothing to the Guild afterward, because she won't tell Ahmaergo she took coin from the other side. A bought lookout does not stop the charge and does not signal.
>
> If a check fails, Nell flashes the lantern in three long pulses toward the south quay, and the signal is sent. She then drops over the side of the launch and runs along the pier toward the quay.
>
> - **Auto:** **Soluun Marked a Threat** is marked for a member who is present. Fel'rekt's watch on Soluun has told the party which launch to watch, and the next check to approach her unseen has Advantage for that member.
> - **Advantage:** The character is a member of Bregan D'aerthe who has marked **Soluun Pressed** with the Mooring result.
> - **Disadvantage:** The character's armor is metal, noisy or both.

If the signal is sent, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A hooded figure in the launch snaps a shutter back, and three long pulses of yellow light go out across the dark harbor toward the south quay. She is over the side and running along the pier before the light has faded, and the boards drum under her boots.

- If the party cannot reach her, she gets clear along the quay and **Guild Survivor Escaped** is marked at the end of the Event.
- If a character catches her within two rounds, she fights as a **Spy** only to buy a second signal. She fights to the end if cornered, because the Guild punishes a lookout who surrenders.

- If **Soluun Pleaded For** is marked for a member who is present, he looks at that member last and says nothing.

Soluun does not fight in the launch. He wants the Guild to finish what it started, and he wants the captain to see it.

- **If a character corners him:** He fights as the 2024 **Scout** with the changes in **The Dockside Killer**, using his hand crossbow and shortsword. At 20 Hit Points or fewer he dives into the harbor and is gone. He is not killed in this Event.

### Into the Water

The party enters the water at 04:50 from the pier's outer end, or at 04:45 if the extra tick applies. The dive to the hull takes the first tick of the Dawn Clock. Krebbyg holds the line at the outermost pile, and Fel'rekt waits on the quay at the shadowed chandlery end with a crossbow.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The harbor is cold and black, and the glow-globe in your hand lights a sphere of green water no wider than ten feet. Silt drifts through the light as you kick down along the pier's outer pile, and the line trails behind you, tugs once and goes slack. Twenty feet down, a long curved shape rises out of the dark, brass-bright where the light touches it, with two cables running from its flank up toward the pier and the divers' bubbles rising from its far side.

> [!gamemaster]**Underwater Rules**
>
> Use the 2024 underwater rules as written.
>
> - **Melee:** A creature without a Swim Speed has Disadvantage on melee attack rolls with any weapon that doesn't deal piercing damage.
> - **Movement:** Every foot costs 1 extra foot, so a Speed 30 character covers 15 feet a round.
> - **Ranged:** A ranged weapon attack automatically misses beyond its normal range and has Disadvantage within it. Firearms don't fire.
> - **Fire:** Anything underwater has Resistance to fire damage.
> - **Visibility:** Deepwater Harbor at night counts as murky water. Visibility is 10 feet, or more in a glow-globe's light.
> - **Breathing:** The potions of water breathing mean nobody needs to hold a breath.
> - **The hull:** The *Scarlet Marpenoth* is not electrified tonight, because Breena has pulled the switch so the party can touch her.

### The Dive Team

The dive team is working at the clamp when the party arrives. The charge is a squat steel case the size of a man's chest, held to the hull beside a ballast seam by a brass magnetic grip and a hook. The divers hold station around it. Orlo Stannick has a lanyard looped round his wrist, and the other end runs to the charge.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Team Is Doing**
>
> The team does not know Bregan D'aerthe is coming, and the divers are not on high alert. Orlo floats above the case with a hand on the lanyard, because the Guild's order was to stay until the fuse had run. Hesk Rooke (if present) is at the stern seam, and the divers keep a line between the case and the pier. The merfolk hover at the edge of the light and watch the water.
>
> The charge itself is described in **Before Dawn**. The lanyard is Orlo's insurance.
>
> - If Orlo sees the party reach the clamp, he pulls the lanyard at the start of his next turn. This sets the fuse to a 10-round countdown.
> - The lanyard is AC 12 with 1 Hit Point. A slashing weapon cuts it with an action.
> - A character can instead make a **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check to unhook it from his wrist without his noticing.

> [!exploration]**Reaching the Clamp Unseen**
>
> Any character who watches the divers' bubbles for a round and makes a successful **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth)** check, as a Group Check, finds the gap between the Guild line and the hull and reaches the clamp from the dark side.
>
> - **Success:** The party acts first, and Orlo does not pull the lanyard before it can be cut.
> - **Failure:** The team has noticed the party, and the party rolls initiative without Advantage.
>
> - **Advantage:** The party puts out its glow-globes and works by the light of the divers' own lamps.
> - **Disadvantage:** A character wears metal armor.

> [!social]**The Salvage Diver**
>
> Orlo Stannick (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: the Guild's dive leader, a weathered man with scarred hands who has pulled wrecks off Waterdeep's harbor floor for the Guild for years.
>
> Orlo is practical and unemotional, and he doesn't enjoy this job. He has no love for the Guild's clerks, who pay a day late and count the dead from the other column. He takes a fair offer if it comes before the fighting turns against him, and he won't go back to the Guild afterward, because he knows what Ahmaergo does with a diver who comes home empty. He talks in short flat sentences and swears plainly when he is frightened.
>
> Orlo is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The terms, if the party offers any.
> - The charge, if he is asked and has accepted terms.
> - How the Guild found her, which he knows in outline.

> [!qna]**Call it off.**
>
> Orlo holds the lanyard loosely and watches your hands before he answers.
>
> > Call it off, or I pull this and you've got ten rounds to swim. I'm not paid enough to drown with you. Tell me what you're offering, and be fast.

> [!qna]**What are the terms?**
>
> > You let us surface and go ashore with our lives. I tell you where the clamp is and what the fuse is set to, and I call off the lanyard. I keep my kit, and the Guild keeps its charge. Want more than that, and I'll fight. I'd rather not. I'm not good at it, and I'm very good at diving.

> [!qna]**Who sold her berth?**
>
> If **Soluun Expelled** is marked, Orlo answers:
>
> > A drow in a Guild launch sold it to Ahmaergo's clerks before the Faire sailed. He said he'd come tonight to watch it go. He's up there now, if he hasn't run, and he wasn't happy about the price.
>
> If **Nar'l Active** or **Nar'l Cleared** and **Soluun Killed** are marked, Orlo answers:
>
> > Ahmaergo had it from the Guild's own court, a soft-spoken drow in a Guild coat who gave it up over tea. I wasn't in the room, and I don't know his name.
>
> Otherwise Orlo answers:
>
> > The clerks logged your gnomes hauling ballast along the pier for three nights, and a ferryman on the Net Street steps told them the rest. Nobody sold it to us. We watched, and your people were careless.

If the party hears Orlo out and offers the terms above, he accepts them when a character makes a successful **DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check. He accepts at once if the party offers all four terms and one diver has already fallen.

- **Advantage:** The party offers the terms before any blow is struck, or Fel'rekt's call for surrender reaches the water.
- **Disadvantage:** Soluun's sale has come to light and the party has attacked the launch.
- **Failure:** Orlo says "Then it's the hard way," and the fight begins. He still accepts terms later.

When Orlo accepts terms he calls off the lanyard, drops his trident and signals the divers to surface. If **Nell Corvane** was taken without a signal, no one signals, and the divers surface at the chandlery steps into Fel'rekt's hands. Fel'rekt takes them to a cellar under the Seven Masks, feeds them, asks them gentle questions and lets them go at the south quay at noon, with their lives and nothing else.

> [!hazard]**The Guild Dive Team**
>
> The team uses the **Warrior Veteran**, **Bandit Captain**, **Tough** and **Merfolk Skirmisher** stat blocks from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, with these changes for the water:
>
> - **Orlo:** His Greatsword becomes a trident (+5 to hit, 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) with two hands). He keeps his Heavy Crossbow but doesn't use it, because ranged attacks are at Disadvantage underwater.
> - **Hesk Rooke:** Her Scimitar becomes a spear. Her Pistol stays in the launch, because firearms don't fire underwater.
> - **Toughs:** They carry spears in place of maces.

>
> #### The Dive Team's Tactics
> At the start of combat, Orlo reaches for the lanyard if he is within reach of it, and the rest of the team closes on the nearest character holding a tool or weapon near the clamp.
>
> During combat, the team:
> - Has Orlo pull the lanyard in his first turn if the party is within 5 feet of the clamp and has not parleyed.
> - Has the Toughs fight in pairs, using Pack Tactics against the character closest to the charge.
> - Has Hesk hold the stern seam and cover the divers, retreating to the surface at half her Hit Points.
> - Has the merfolk strike at characters who are carrying the charge or cutting the lanyard, using their swim speed to switch targets.
>
> If Orlo falls, the Toughs break for the surface. If Orlo is reduced to 16 Hit Points or fewer while surrounded, he surrenders on the terms above and orders the others to stop. The merfolk flee the moment Orlo falls or surrenders. Fel'rekt calls twice from the surface line for the team to stop, in plain words, and he does not fire. The fight ends when the team is dead, captured or fled.
>
> The fight is a Bruising encounter in the **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference**. The party does not need to win it to remove the charge, because a successful parley or an unseen approach settles it without a blow.

If Orlo pulls the lanyard, the fuse is set to a 10-round countdown and the charge can only be jettisoned. Read or paraphrase the following:

## Overview

A Guild launch sits at the end of the old Faire pier and divers are working beneath it. The party swims down to find them at the hull of Bregan D'aerthe's submarine.

## Summary

Fel'rekt woke us before dawn with news that Xanathar's Guild was under the old Faire pier, and we collected potions, spears and a rope to hold. We dealt with the lookout in the launch and went down twenty feet to the hull, where a dive leader named Orlo Stannick was guarding a steel case clamped beside a seam. How long did we have before first light?
