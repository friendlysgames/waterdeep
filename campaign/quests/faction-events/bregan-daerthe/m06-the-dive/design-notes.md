# Design Notes: The Dive

## A Charge on the Hull

***The mission used to recover an Eye from a wreck, and now it defends a boat.*** The restored draft sank a ketch with Eye #3 aboard and sent the party down after it, with a Guild dive team racing for the same prize. That contradicted the campaign's own canon, because the Eyes' holders are decided by the lair heists and by **Sea Maidens Faire**, and **Xanathar's Lair** treats the beholder as not knowing what the Eyes are. It also put a mission at 7th level, after all four heists, that recovered an Eye the party had already collected or lost. The mission now follows the Factions Guide: Xanathar's divers clamp a limpet charge to the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, and the party removes it before dawn. The event never says whether the submarine carries an Eye, so it stays order-agnostic about who holds them.

***The Guild is after an asset, not a prize.*** Ahmaergo treats Bregan D'aerthe as the Guild's enemy and a submarine as the one thing the captain cannot replace. No faction in this mission wants anything the party can pick up and carry off, and that keeps the stakes on the boat and on the people who are standing around it.

## After the Faire Sails

***The Faire left on Tarsakh 20.*** The draft had the *Eyecatcher* anchored 200 yards from a pier and Krebbyg saying that the Faire was committed to Waterdeep for another season. Neither fits the calendar, which closes the Faire's paths on Tarsakh 20 and has the ships leave on schedule, so the event states that the first mate took the *Eyecatcher* and the captain stayed ashore with Krebbyg, Fel'rekt and a small team. The arc-h structure doc puts a drow mage, not the captain, at the *Eyecatcher*'s helm, so Zardoz Zord can stay in Waterdeep without contradicting it. The submarine came off the keel collar and went to a reserve berth under the outer end of the old Faire pier at Smugglers' Dock, 20 feet down, moored to the piles with her engines cold. Soluun knew that berth because he slept aboard, and a sale he made before the Faire sailed fits the mooring the Guild found.

## Where the Leak Comes From

***Three sources, one question.*** The Guild finds the berth by Soluun's sale if **Soluun Expelled** is marked, by Nar'l's grief if **Soluun Killed** is marked with **Nar'l Active** or **Nar'l Cleared**, and by its own harbor search otherwise. Each source has a way for the party to learn it: Soluun is in the launch and his hand is on Orlo's sketch, Orlo says a soft-spoken drow in a Guild coat gave the Guild the berth, and the clerks' logs are the third answer. Krebbyg and Fel'rekt react to each according to their profiles, and Fel'rekt's "There's another way" is the answer to Nar'l. **Nar'l Extracted** and **Nar'l Eliminated** fall under the harbor search, because the mission reads whether Nar'l is still in place and a dead or removed Nar'l has nothing to leak.

***Soluun's earlier choices land here.*** **Soluun Pressed** (Mooring) gives Advantage on the check to spot the lookout, **Soluun Marked a Threat** gives Fel'rekt's earlier warning and one extra tick, and **Soluun Provoked** and **Soluun Marked a Threat** both make Soluun pick that member out. **Soluun Pleaded For** costs 1 Renown when the sale comes to light, which is the cost the plea in **The Killer's Fate** promised. Soluun himself cannot be killed in this Event. He is a lieutenant-level threat who would be a Power 38 addition to a fight the party is meant to take in the water, and a death here would need an outcome that **Houseless Noble** is not written to read.

## The Dawn Clock

***A procedure with consequences at every step.*** The charge is invented, because no source gives a limpet charge's rules. The procedure has four layers: locate (when the party has been scattered), free the clamp, then defuse or jettison, and a clock that costs a tick for every attempt. The old draft's mission came down to a lookout check, a descent and a case-opening check, so every step here has a failure that costs time or changes the fuse. The Hair Trigger and the lanyard turn a slow clock into a 10-round carry, which rewards a party that cut the lanyard or parleyed with Orlo. Jettisoning is always possible, and defusing needs two good checks, so no single roll decides the mission.

***Detonation ranges decide the outcome.*** The clamped charge deals 300 damage to a 300-Hit-Point submarine that has a damage threshold of 15, which is exactly her structure. A charge within 30 feet deals 150, and one at 31 to 60 feet deals 60. The brief named Crippled and Lost as the failure states, so **Marpenoth Crippled** covers both of the middle ranges, because the seam splits either way and the repair is the same tenday and about 5,000 gp.

## Non-Combat Routes and Surrender

***Every fight has a way out.*** The lookout can be taken from the water or the pier, fooled as an inspector or bought for 150 gp. The dive team can be talked down on four stated terms with a **DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check, with Advantage if the party offers terms before the first blow. Orlo surrenders at 16 Hit Points or fewer, the merfolk leave when he falls, and the reinforcement crew breaks when its captain drops or two of its members fall. Fel'rekt calls for surrender twice in plain words, which is his profile, and Krebbyg and Fel'rekt never fight, because the Mechanics Reference rebuilds every roster when they do.

***The merfolk's function.*** The old draft's merfolk had no mechanical function. The two hired Merfolk Skirmishers are now native swimmers who switch targets in the water, and they join only if a character has a Swim Speed or *Freedom of Movement*. A party without a swim speed already fights at a disadvantage in the water, and the Mechanics Reference warns that the merfolk make the fight a row harder.

## Voices

***Krebbyg and Fel'rekt follow their profiles.*** The draft made Krebbyg a calm handler who "does not dive" for twenty years of reasons nobody explained. He is now rash, frightened and talkative, he says "darling" and "Ask Fel", and he does not dive because he would drown loudly. Fel'rekt arrives, counts options on his fingers and says there is another way. Zardoz is loud and generous, and he never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan. A member who has **Jarlaxle Unmasked** hears one private line in the captain's own voice, and no other speaker says the name.

***Orlo and Nell are plain people.*** Orlo is a salvage diver who has no love for the Guild's clerks, and Nell is a professional lookout who will sell her price. Neither is a villain, and both can be bought, talked down or fooled, which is how a faction mission about a bomb on a boat avoids a slaughter.

## Outcomes and Readers

***Marpenoth Saved, Crippled and Lost.*** The brief wanted **Marpenoth Saved** read by **Vault of Dragons** and **Houseless Noble**. Both are unconverted or unrevised, and the readers describe what each should read. **Vault of Dragons** reads the submarine as a transport for part of the vault gold, and 2 tons of cargo space holds about 200,000 gp in coin. **Houseless Noble** reads the ceremony's location and the submarine benefit.

***Soluun Sold the Mooring and Guild Survivor Escaped.*** The first gives **Houseless Noble** the empty seat's account and triggers the plea penalty. The second tells **Vault of Dragons** Scene 5 that the Guild knows which members dived.

## Source Departures

***Cut from the draft.*** Krenick Durr and the ketch wreck, Eye #3 as the prize, the Pier 17 off Sail Street setting, the paper-bird lookout, the rope-ladder pickup from the *Eyecatcher*, the "committed to Waterdeep for another season" line, the 15-Renown drow disguise resources and the merfolk-as-texture block are gone. The wreck and Krenick's name go with the Eye plot. The Renown 5+ extraction in the Factions Guide was never implemented in the Bregan D'aerthe folder and is not implemented here.

***Invented and unverified.*** Orlo Stannick, Tamsin Rooke and Nell Corvane are invented. The limpet charge, the Dawn Clock, the detonation figures and the repair time are invented in the Mechanics Reference. The reserve berth at the old Faire pier is invented, and the pier's location comes from the Sea Maidens Faire area overview, which puts the *Heartbreaker* and *Hellraiser* at a private pier at Smugglers' Dock. The 150 gp bribe, the 250, 100 and 0 gp purses, and the 05:30 first light are my own figures.

## Out-of-Scope Notes

These conflicts sit outside the Bregan D'aerthe folder and are logged for the out-of-scope notes. The Factions Guide (line 16) offers the *Scarlet Marpenoth* as an extraction vehicle at Renown 5 and after Mission 4, which nothing in the folder implements, and line 71 describes the submarine as "moored under the Eyecatcher" for this mission. The arc-h structure doc (lines 9, 71) puts the Faire's ships at a Mistshore pier while the Sea Maidens Faire area overview puts them at a Dock Ward private pier. The arc-j structure doc (line 55) reads "completed Bregan D'aerthe Mission 6" without an outcome, and it should read **Marpenoth Saved**, **Marpenoth Crippled**, **Marpenoth Lost** and **Guild Survivor Escaped**. **The Killer's Fate** says Soluun's plea cost "before Tarsakh 20", while this mission runs after the Faire sails, so the sale is dated before the Faire left and comes to light here. **The Theater's Back Room** has Zardoz tell the member that Brimel "sails with us on the twentieth", which reads as if Zardoz sails with the Faire, and this mission has him stay ashore.
