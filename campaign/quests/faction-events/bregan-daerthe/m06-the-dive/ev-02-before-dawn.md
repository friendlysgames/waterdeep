# Before Dawn

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Combat Event occurs when the party stands at the limpet charge clamped to the hull of the *Scarlet Marpenoth*, at the end of **The Dive**, with the Dawn Clock still running. It ends at first light, when the charge has been removed, has gone off, or has sunk the boat, and Krebbyg Masq'il'yr and Fel'rekt Lafeen meet the member on the pier. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Find the charge on the hull, if the fighting scattered the party, and free its clamp.
> - Defuse the charge, or carry it at least 60 feet from the hull and set it down.
> - Deal with the Guild's reinforcements, if the lookout sent a signal, when the party surfaces.
> - Learn who sold the berth, and tell Krebbyg.
> - Hear the captain's thanks, or his silence, depending on what is left of the *Scarlet Marpenoth*.
>
> Only Bregan D'aerthe members receive the debrief. Nobody in this Event says the name Jarlaxle in speech or readaloud unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member being addressed. Zardoz Zord never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan.

> [!gamemaster]**The Charge**
>
> The charge is a Guild ship-breaker: a steel case the size of a man's chest, clamped to the hull beside a ballast seam by a magnetic-and-hook grip. It holds two sealed kegs of smokepowder, about 150 pounds together, a clockwork fuse set for 05:30 and a pressure trigger that punishes anyone who pulls on it carelessly. Fel'rekt described its clamp, fuse and pressure trigger at the brief from what the Guild's divers were seen carrying.
>
> The party works through three steps, and every attempt at each costs 1 tick of the Dawn Clock, whether it succeeds or not.
>
> **Step 1: Locate.** If the party reached the clamp in **The Dive**, the charge is found and this step costs nothing. If a fight scattered the party, or the party has driven the team off before reaching the case, a character searches the hull for the charge. Any character who searches the hull and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** or **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** check finds the case, and the party makes one attempt per character per tick.
>
> - **Advantage:** Orlo Stannick or a diver has told the party which seam the case sits beside.
> - **Disadvantage:** The character has no light source. The glow-globes from Fel'rekt's crate are enough.
>
> **Step 2: Free the clamp.** The grip has to be released before the charge can be defused or carried. Any character who pries at the grip and makes a successful **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** check, or who works the latch and makes a successful **DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** check, drops the clamp, and a second character can use the Help action. If the check fails by 4 or less, the grip holds and the party spends the tick. If it fails by 5 or more, or the die shows a 1, the pressure trigger shifts, the striker clicks audibly within 5 feet, and the remaining ticks are halved, rounding down.
>
> **Step 3: Defuse or jettison.** The party chooses one, and it can change its mind after a failure.
>
> - **Defuse.** Any character who isolates the striker and makes a successful **DC 15 Dexterity (Tinker's Tools or Thieves' Tools)** check, or a **DC 17 Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation)** check, kills the charge, and the mission is won. If the check fails by 4 or less, the character spends the tick and can try again. If it fails by 5 or more, the fuse jumps to a **10-round countdown** and the charge can only be jettisoned.
> - **Jettison.** The case weighs about 150 pounds. A character with Strength 13 or higher can carry it alone, or two characters can carry it together, and a carrier cannot Dash unless the carrier has Strength 15 or higher. The case must come to rest at least 60 feet from the hull, and the party must then swim at least 60 feet from the case, which is 120 feet of travel and about 8 rounds without a Dash. A 10-round fuse makes that tight but possible. Jettisoning costs 1 tick before the carry begins.
>
> **The lanyard.** If Orlo Stannick pulled his lanyard in **The Dive**, the fuse is already a 10-round countdown, and Defuse is closed. The party jettisons, and the first round of every carrier's swim counts from the moment the case is free.
>
> If the clock runs to 0 or the 10-round countdown ends, the charge detonates as described in **Detonation**.

> [!gamemaster]**Detonation**
>
> When the charge detonates, each creature in the water within 30 feet of the case makes a **DC 16 Constitution saving throw**, taking 44 (8d10) force damage on a failed save and half as much on a successful one. Each creature from 31 to 60 feet away takes 22 (4d10) force damage on a failed save and half as much on a successful one. Beyond 60 feet, a creature takes nothing, though the blast is heard for a mile and a wall of bright bubbles breaks the surface.
>
> The blast's effect on the *Scarlet Marpenoth* depends on where the case is:
>
> - **Clamped to the hull:** 300 damage, which is far above her damage threshold of 15 and equal to her 300 Hit Points. Her structure fails and she floods and sinks. Mark **Marpenoth Lost**.
> - **Within 30 feet of the hull and not clamped:** 150 damage. Her ballast seam splits, she cannot dive or move until repaired, and the work takes a tenday and about 5,000 gp. Mark **Marpenoth Crippled**.
> - **31 to 60 feet from the hull:** 60 damage. The seam leaks, the repairs take the same tenday, and she is crippled in the same way. Mark **Marpenoth Crippled**.
> - **More than 60 feet from the hull:** no damage. Mark **Marpenoth Saved**.
>
> A defused charge also marks **Marpenoth Saved**. Breena Bafflestone is in the control room, 20 feet from the seam, and survives a sinking or a crippling blast. Her crew was put ashore at 03:50 and takes no harm.

### Beat by Beat

The party gets a few moments to choose how to spend its ticks, and the surface line tugs when a signal comes. If Nell Corvane sent a signal, two quick tugs arrive one tick after the signal, and three rapid tugs arrive one tick after that, when the boat is at the pier.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The steel case sits fat and cold against the hull beside a seam in the plating, with a brass grip biting into the metal on one side and a hook on the other. Rust-colored silt has settled around the clamp's edges, and a tiny dial on the case's flank turns with the slow persistence of a very patient clock. Above you, a rope gives a single gentle tug, and then another, quicker.

If the signal went out, read or paraphrase the following when the second tug arrives:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The rope against your wrist twitches twice, fast and hard, and then falls slack again. Overhead, the pier's pilings are black against a harbor that has begun to pale at the edges, and the sound of an oar knocks against a boat's side somewhere on the quay.

### The Reinforcements

If the lookout sent a signal, a rowboat of Guild muscle reaches the pier five minutes later, which is 1 tick. If the signal went out while the party was still on the pier, or before the party entered the water, the crew is already rowing and brings the escalated roster. The crew does not dive. It waits on the pier with its crossbows, and it cuts the surface line.

> [!hazard]**The Guild Boat**
>
> The crew uses the **Bandit Captain**, **Tough** and **Bugbear Warrior** stat blocks from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, and the roster counts the combatants on the party's side, including companions and not including Krebbyg or Fel'rekt. The crew carries crossbows and spears, and the Bandit Captain keeps her Pistol, because the fight is on the surface.
>
> - **Three combatants:** two Toughs and one Bugbear Warrior. If the signal went out early, add a Bandit Captain.
> - **Four combatants:** one Bandit Captain, two Toughs and one Bugbear Warrior. If the signal went out early, add a second Bugbear Warrior.
> - **Five combatants:** one Bandit Captain, two Toughs and two Bugbear Warriors. If the signal went out early, add two more Toughs and a third Bugbear Warrior.
>
> Fel'rekt and Krebbyg do not fight. When the boat docks they withdraw to the chandlery roof with the line, and Fel'rekt calls twice, in plain words, for the crew to put its weapons down.
>
> #### The Guild Boat's Tactics
> At the start of combat, the crew fires at the first character to surface and holds the end of the pier.
>
> During combat, the crew:
> - Has the Bugbear Warriors grapple characters who come within reach of the pier's edge and haul them onto the boards.
> - Has the Toughs fight in pairs, using Pack Tactics against a character on the pier.
> - Has the Bandit Captain shoot from the boat's stern and call the others back if she is reduced to half her Hit Points.
> - Targets the member Soluun picked out first, if **Soluun Marked a Threat** or **Soluun Provoked** is marked for a member and Soluun was in the launch.
>
> The crew does not follow into the water. It breaks and flees when the Bandit Captain falls or when two of the crew are down, and it surrenders if surrounded on the pier. The fight is a Mild encounter in the **Bregan D'aerthe Mechanics Reference** and a Bruising one if the signal went out early.

If the party stays below until the charge is gone, the crew waits on the pier for ten minutes after the last sound, and it is there when the party surfaces. If the party swims away along the bottom and surfaces 100 feet or more from the pier, the crew does not see the party until it reaches the steps.

If any member of the boat crew escapes, mark **Guild Survivor Escaped**.

### When the Charge Is Gone

When the charge is defused or has gone off, the clock is over, and the party swims back to the pier. The Harbor Watch rows out within ten minutes of any detonation, and Fel'rekt and Krebbyg take the party into the chandlery before then.

If **Marpenoth Saved** is marked, the charge is either dead on the seabed or a cloud of bubbles far from the hull. Read or paraphrase the following when the party surfaces:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The sky above the harbor has turned the color of pewter, and a long, low shape glows faintly under the pier's end where the sub's hull lamps are coming on. Krebbyg is sitting on the edge of the quay with his legs over the water and the end of the rope across his knees. He jumps up when he sees your heads, and Fel'rekt leans out from behind the chandlery's corner with a grin that splits his face.
>
> > Oh, shit, you did it. Kreb, they did it, she's still there! Come up, come up, I've got blankets, I've got a flask, I don't care if it's the captain's.

If **Marpenoth Crippled** is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The sky above the harbor has gone grey, and a wide stain of brown silt is spreading beneath the pier with a few brass fragments turning in it. Krebbyg is standing at the end of the quay with the rope in his hand, and he has stopped talking. Fel'rekt wades into the shallows to help you up the steps, and he doesn't look at the water.
>
> > She's floating, Breena says, but the seam's split and she can't dive, and it'll be a tenday and a lot of gold before she moves again. It could have been worse, and I'd say that's the best that can be said about it, and I'm very sorry.

If **Marpenoth Lost** is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The harbor is calm and the pier's boards are wet with spray, and a ring of oily silt and drifting bubbles is spreading across the water above the place where the boat lay. Krebbyg is crouched at the end of the quay with the severed end of the rope in his hands. Fel'rekt stands behind him with one hand on his shoulder, and neither of them looks up at you.
>
> > She's gone. Breena's out, and her people, and they're all alive, but the boat's on the bottom and she isn't coming up, and the captain is going to want to hear it from somebody. Ask Fel. No, don't ask Fel, I'll do it myself.

### Reporting to Krebbyg

Krebbyg and Fel'rekt take the party into the chandlery and sit them by the stove. Breena Bafflestone, a small gnome in a brass-buckled apron with oil on her cheek, comes ashore from the control room and sits in a corner without speaking. Zardoz Zord is there too if **Zardoz Introduced** is marked, and he arrives from the Seven Masks at dawn, in the same plumed hat, and he fills the room with noise.

The debrief is a conversation. What the party learned decides its content, and each answer below applies only to a party that has the facts.

> [!social]**The Fixer in the Chandlery**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: a young drow gunslinger who has spent an hour holding a rope and is only now allowed to be afraid.
>
> Krebbyg is subdued for the first sentence and then talks in run-ons again, because he can't help it. He asks what the party saw, he defers to Fel'rekt on anything serious, and he swears at whoever sold the berth in his own colorful way. If the party brings him a name, he doesn't say it at first, and he glances at Fel'rekt before he does.
>
> Krebbyg is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - How the Guild found her, which he wants to know in full.
> - What the captain will say, which he can only guess at.
> - Who is hurt, which is nobody.

> [!qna]**How did they find her?**
>
> If **Soluun Sold the Mooring** is marked, Krebbyg answers:
>
> > Soluun. That's his hand on the sketch, and his hours on the hatch, and the captain is going to be so quiet about this, darling, you can't imagine. He slept aboard for a year and he sold her like a sack of flour, and I'd say I told you so, but I don't think anyone ever told me anything.
>
> If **Nar'l Active** or **Nar'l Cleared** and **Soluun Killed** are marked and the party heard about the drow in the Guild coat, Krebbyg answers:
>
> > A soft-spoken drow in a Guild coat who gave it up over tea. That's Nar'l, it has to be, because who else is there? He was in pieces over his brother, and I thought he'd get over it, and I'm going to be sick. Fel, say something.
> >
> > We tell the captain, Kreb, and we don't do anything else before he decides. There's another way with Nar'l, I think, because he's grieving and he's frightened, but that isn't ours to pick.
>
> Otherwise, Krebbyg answers:
>
> > The clerks counted our gnomes carrying ballast for three nights, which is the sort of thing I'd have noticed if I ever looked at anything that wasn't a schedule. Nobody sold us. We were careless, darling, and I'd like that written down somewhere so nobody can say it was a traitor.

> [!qna]**Is the captain angry?**
>
> > He doesn't get angry, he gets polite, and I've never been so frightened of polite in my life. If you saved her he'll say something splendid, and if you didn't, he'll say something else and I'd like to be in another city. He said to tell you thank you either way, though, and he said it twice, and he meant it, I think.

> [!qna]**What happens to Nell?**
>
> If the party took Nell or any divers alive, Krebbyg answers:
>
> > Fel's got them in the cellar with soup, because Fel feeds people, and we'll let them go at noon with nothing, and they'll tell the Guild whatever they like. They won't be telling it anything that matters, because they were hired to dive and not to think.

If a member is marked **Soluun Pleaded For** and **Soluun Sold the Mooring** is marked, Krebbyg turns to that member, and the plea has come to light. Read or paraphrase the following to that member:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Krebbyg looks at you and then at the floor, and his usual run of words seems to catch on something. He pushes the sketch across the table with one finger and speaks more slowly than you have ever heard him.
>
> > You said you'd answer for him, and the captain's going to hold you to it, darling, and I'm sorry. It's a point off your name for this, and that's all he's going to do about it. Fel asked him to be gentle.

If **Zardoz Introduced** is marked, Zardoz sweeps into the chandlery and delivers the captain's thanks in his own noise. Read or paraphrase the following for the sub's state, in the matching case:

If **Marpenoth Saved** is marked:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A man in scarlet with a plumed hat shoulders the chandlery door open and fills the room with the smell of cold harbor air and rum. He claps Krebbyg on the back, kisses Breena Bafflestone on the top of her head, and flourishes the hat at you.
>
> > My darlings, my heroes, my wonderful wet friends! I have lost a great many things in my life, and my second-best hat is one of them, but I was not going to lose that boat. There's a purse on the table, and a ticket for each of you for the next time we sail into Waterdeep, and I won't hear a word about it. Free tickets for the heroes!

If **Marpenoth Crippled** is marked:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A man in scarlet with a plumed hat comes in slowly and takes the hat off at the door, and the roar has gone out of him. He looks at Breena, and at the stove, and at you, and the sailor's accent thins a little at the edges.
>
> > Well, she floats, my darlings, and that's more than a great many ladies can say after a night like that. It will cost me a tenday and a fortune, and I'd say it's cheap at the price. There's a purse on the table for hazard, and I'll have a great deal more to say when I've seen the hull.

If **Marpenoth Lost** is marked:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A man in scarlet with a plumed hat stands in the chandlery door for a long moment and doesn't come in. He looks at the harbor through the window and at Breena in her corner, and the plumed hat hangs from his fingers and swings.
>
> > Thank you for going down, all of you, and for trying. It was a lovely boat. I'd like a moment alone with her, so if you'd go with Fel'rekt and have some breakfast, I'd be grateful. There'll be no purse, my dears, and I'm not sorry, because you did what you could.

If **Zardoz Introduced** is not marked, Krebbyg delivers the same news, and the captain's thanks arrive that evening in an envelope with a line in a small neat hand, unsigned. The purse is in the envelope.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, the note is signed with a feathered "J." and Zardoz drops the sailor's voice for one sentence before he goes: "She was a lovely boat, my dear, and I thank you for the attempt."

### Renown Opportunities

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Bregan D'aerthe member gains 4 base Renown for taking part in the dive and reporting to Krebbyg, whatever the state of the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. Companions who aren't members gain none. Each member also gains the following, and each bonus can be earned once:
>
> - **+1 Renown:** **Marpenoth Saved** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** Orlo Stannick accepted terms and **Guild Survivor Escaped** is not marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** Nell Corvane was taken or bought before she signalled, and no reinforcement boat came.
>
> A member marked **Soluun Pleaded For** loses 1 Renown when **Soluun Sold the Mooring** is marked, to a minimum of Renown 1. The loss is taken once, in this Event, and Krebbyg tells the member so in the debrief.
>
> The purse in the captain's envelope is 250 gp for the party if **Marpenoth Saved** is marked, 100 gp if **Marpenoth Crippled** is marked, and nothing if **Marpenoth Lost** is marked.

### Aftermath

Breena Bafflestone and her crew move into the Seven Masks' dressing room if the sub is lost, and she goes back to the hull as soon as she is allowed if it is not. Fel'rekt lets the Guild divers go at noon, and Orlo Stannick leaves the city and doesn't come back.

If **Soluun Sold the Mooring** is marked, Soluun is not seen again before the party reaches **Vault of Dragons**, and Krebbyg and Fel'rekt both stop mentioning him. If Nar'l leaked the berth, Jarlaxle does not decide about Nar'l this month and no other event reads the matter. If the Guild found the berth by its own search, Ahmaergo files the failure under procedure and the Guild moves on to a new target.

If **Guild Survivor Escaped** is marked, Ahmaergo's clerks have a description of the members who dived by noon.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has reported to Krebbyg and Fel'rekt in the chandlery and the *Scarlet Marpenoth* has been put in one of three states.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark exactly one of the three **Marpenoth** outcomes, and keep any marks from **The Dive**.
>
> - **Marpenoth Saved** — mark when the charge is defused, or jettisoned and set down more than 60 feet from the hull. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), where Jarlaxle's team can carry part of the vault gold in the submarine's 2 tons of cargo space, and by **Houseless Noble**, where the ceremony is held aboard her and her crew is at the character's call.
> - **Marpenoth Crippled** — mark when the charge detonates within 60 feet of the hull and not clamped to it. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), where the submarine is under repair for a tenday and offers no transport, and by **Houseless Noble**, where the captain mentions the repair bill and the ceremony is held aboard once she is mended.
> - **Marpenoth Lost** — mark when the charge detonates clamped to the hull. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), where the captain's team has no submarine, and by **Houseless Noble**, where the ceremony is held on the *Eyecatcher* or elsewhere and the submarine benefit is replaced by access to another vessel.

## Overview

The party races the dawn to find a Guild charge on the hull of Bregan D'aerthe's submarine and get it off before it goes. Krebbyg and Fel'rekt wait on the pier and hold the line.

## Summary

We found Xanathar's charge clamped to the hull of the *Scarlet Marpenoth* and worked through the clock to free it and carry it clear. Krebbyg and Fel'rekt took us into the chandlery afterward and told us what they knew about how the Guild had found her. What will the captain do about the one who sold her berth?
