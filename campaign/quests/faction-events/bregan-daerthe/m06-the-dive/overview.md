# The Dive: Overview

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available at Renown 14. Level 7 recommended. The Theater's Back Room must be complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 7th level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

A small vessel carrying Eye #3 of the Stone of Golorr went down in Deepwater Harbor several tendays ago. The ketch sank forty feet to the bottom after a confrontation aboard, and the Eye went with the cargo. The wreck is recent enough to be undisturbed, shallow enough to reach without specialized equipment, and deep enough that it has been murky and unlit since it settled. Jarlaxle's harbor network identified the wreck's position four days after the sinking. Xanathar's people identified it independently a tenday later, and their dive team has been staging for two nights, waiting for calm water and a dark tide.

Tonight both sides move.

Krebbyg arrives at Trollskull Manor near midnight with four potions of water breathing on the table before the party has finished reading his face. He has a rowboat, a harbor chart with the bearing marked, and a rope signal system for coordinating through an environment where verbal communication is not possible. He does not dive. He does not explain why.

## Involved Characters
- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** (Bregan D'aerthe) — coordinates the operation from a rowboat on the surface; does not dive; communicates with the party via a rope signal system; the most exposed he has been in any mission
- **Xanathar Guild dive team** — two or three **Veterans** with *potions of water breathing* active; operating in the wreck when the party arrives or staging to enter; they know what they are looking for but do not know the BD team is coming

## Dangers & Enemies

- **Surface:** One Guild **Spy** watching from a hired dinghy near the anchorage; spyglass and a paper bird ready
- **Underwater:** Two to three Guild **Veterans** with water breathing active; standard underwater combat conditions apply (no verbal communication, half movement without a swim speed, Disadvantage on all non-piercing weapon attacks)
- **Additional risk (if dive team escapes):** Three additional Guild **Thugs** arrive by rowboat within ten minutes if a warning bird is sent

## Overview

Eye #3 of the Stone of Golorr is in a wrecked ketch at forty feet in Deepwater Harbor, sealed in the captain's document case. Krebbyg is in a rowboat on the surface with a rope signal system. The Xanathar Guild dive team is staging at Pier 17.
