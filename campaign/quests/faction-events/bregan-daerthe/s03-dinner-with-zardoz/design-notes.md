# Design Notes: Dinner with Zardoz

## A Handover, Not a Briefing

The restored draft had Zardoz meet the party "for the first time", give no mission and say almost nothing about himself. That was false on three counts, because the party has seen him at the Twin Parades, may have sat in his box at the Field of Triumph, and may have stood in his office during **Fireball!**. This version treats the dinner as the place where the member's contact changes hands. J.B. Nevercott retires, Zardoz Zord takes his place beside Krebbyg, and the member hears it from the new contact himself. Nevercott's retirement is a GM fact, and in the fiction the haberdasher "has gone back to hats", so a player who goes looking for him finds a closed door and a joke.

The restored draft also set Nevercott's retirement after **Three Nights** in some places and after this dinner in others. This page puts it after **The Compromised Eye**, which matches the order of the missions the member has actually run with him.

## The Loud Host and the Buried Questions

The old Zardoz was warm, precise and comfortable with silence. That is the real Jarlaxle's voice, and the voice profile gives the persona the opposite: loud, hearty, exclamatory and never silent. The three questions are now each hidden inside a ramble, because a host who never stops talking can ask the one question he cares about without anybody noticing it was a question. A DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check lets a sharp player catch it, and a failure costs nothing.

The sudden-elegance slip is the one place the cover strains. Members who have marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** get a private second line from him, so the dinner pays off a secret some players already hold without handing it to the rest of the table. This Event reads the outcome and never sets it. Fireball's cabin scene and **Sea Maidens Faire** are the natural writers, and both are unconverted.

## What the Answers Are Worth

The draft asked three questions and used none of the answers. Each course now has a check and a consequence. A true answer that names a person gets a booklet of free tickets sent to that person, which is a gift and a message together. A cover story that holds earns Regard and one that fails earns a laugh. A plain statement of strength earns Regard, and a deflection lets Zardoz finish the answer from Krebbyg's reports, which a DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check turns into the discovery that someone writes those reports down. Two Regard earns 1 Renown, once. The tally does not carry beyond the dinner, so the Event stays a standalone and does not become a hidden gate.

## Four Nar'l Outcomes, One Dinner

Zardoz never names Nar'l or the sewers, so the four outcomes show in the room and not in the talk. **Nar'l Cleared** gets the biggest welcome, a fourth course and the one remark about the Guild's inconsistencies that the draft gave to a successful Option C. **Nar'l Active** gets a host who watches the harbor and keeps the dinner short. **Nar'l Extracted** gets a toast to anyone who got a friend home. **Nar'l Eliminated** still gets the dinner, because Jarlaxle wants to see how the member behaves afterward, but the stewards are cooler, the free tickets are for the cheap seats and the member earns no Renown, which is consistent with **The Compromised Eye**.

## Cuts and Replacements

The obsidian token is cut. The restored draft gave it no reader, and the guide's identification token for the Faire's training mannequins is a different object. The dinner moves from a hidden room at the Seven Masks to the captain's dining cabin aboard the *Eyecatcher*, which is where the source puts it and where the **Officer** and **Commander** rank events already place Zardoz. The source has Zardoz call the Faire a carnival "based in Luskan", and this page drops the line, because Zardoz never mentions Luskan, drow or the Underdark. Krebbyg delivers the invitation through the company's own summons, a playbill with a circled price, and meets the member at the stage door after dark, since that is the only public place where he is himself. In every public place he is still Kreb Sorrush.

## Out-of-Scope Notes

The captain's dining cabin carries the source's area code J10, but the location journal's J10 is the *Heartbreaker*'s captain's cabin and the Sea Maidens Faire structure document describes an *Eyecatcher* J10 with a drow mage captain. The rank events now follow this page: a member without **Zardoz Introduced** is received by Nevercott at **Officer** and by Krebbyg at **Commander**, **The Theater's Back Room** and **The Dive**. **The Compromised Eye** hands the Nar'l outcomes to this dinner and no longer places it at the Seven Masks. The cover descriptor for Zardoz varies across files ("Illuskan", "Waterdavian", "Calishite"), and this page keeps "Illuskan" from the source and the Notable Figures page. **Zardoz Introduced** is read by **The Theater's Back Room**, **The Dive**, **Officer** and **Commander**. No new named NPC is invented, and "Regard" is a working term for this Event only.
