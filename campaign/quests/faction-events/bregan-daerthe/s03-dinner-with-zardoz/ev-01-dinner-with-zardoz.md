# Dinner with Zardoz

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs on the second evening after an individual Bregan D'aerthe member resolves **The Compromised Eye**. Krebbyg Masq'il'yr invites that member aboard the *Eyecatcher* to dine with Zardoz Zord, who takes over from J.B. Nevercott as the member's contact. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Answer a playbill summons to the Seven Masks stage door, take the invitation from Krebbyg, and cross Deepwater Harbor in a skiff rowed by Fel'rekt Lafeen.
> - Dine in the captain's dining cabin with a host who has already met the party at the Twin Parades and, depending on what they did earlier in the campaign, at the Field of Triumph or in his office.
> - Answer three questions over three courses, each asked inside a ramble about the sea, and see what each answer is worth.
> - Hear that J.B. Nevercott has gone back to hats and that Zardoz Zord now stands behind the member's missions.
> - Watch the cover slip once, and, if **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for that member, hear what the captain says when he stops performing.

### Who Dines

The invitation goes to each Bregan D'aerthe member who has resolved **The Compromised Eye**, and the dinner is held for members alone. Members who ran the mission together share its Nar'l outcome and dine together on the second evening after they report back to Krebbyg. If two members ended the mission on different outcomes, hold their dinners on separate nights, each on the second evening after that member's own report.

Companions who are not members may ride out in the skiff. A steward seats them at a table on the main deck with ices and a handful of free tickets, and they hear nothing of what follows and gain nothing from it.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Zardoz Zord is Jarlaxle Baenre (Chaotic Neutral, drow, he/him). Aboard his ships he wears a magical guise projected by the gilded figurehead on each vessel, and ashore he wears a *hat of disguise* as J.B. Nevercott. He never admits either at this table. Nobody here says the name Jarlaxle in speech or readaloud unless **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member being addressed, and until then every Bregan D'aerthe speaker says "the captain" or "J." This Event does not mark **Jarlaxle Unmasked**, and a member who guesses the name aloud does not mark it either. Zardoz answers a guess with a delighted roar and a free ticket.
>
> The party has seen him before. Most of them saw him wave from the lead float at the Twin Parades, and many met him at the Field of Triumph in **Trollskull Alley** or in his office during **Fireball!**. Nothing in this Event treats the dinner as a first meeting.
>
> J.B. Nevercott retires tonight. Jarlaxle has read four missions' worth of reports from Krebbyg and Fel'rekt, and he wants to look at the member himself before he trusts the member with anything larger. From here on the member's missions run through Zardoz and Krebbyg together. In the fiction Zardoz says only that the haberdasher "has gone back to hats", and anyone the member asks about Nevercott afterward, at the Sea Ward address on his card or at the Seven Masks, says the same.
>
> The three questions are not a loyalty test. Jarlaxle holds partial answers to each in the reports, and he is checking the file against the living person, so he cares more about how an answer comes than about what it contains.
>
> Zardoz never mentions drow, the Underdark or Luskan, and he does not discuss Nar'l, the Castle Ward sewers or Soluun. Every crew member looks like an attractive human to everyone aboard, and truesight, *see invisibility* or a successful DC 20 Wisdom (Perception) check shows the drow underneath. Krebbyg and Fel'rekt look human as well from the moment the skiff comes within 200 feet of the ship.

> [!gamemaster]**Playing Zardoz at Dinner**
>
> Zardoz is loud, generous and endlessly hospitable. He talks in big exclamatory sentences, tells long sea stories that go nowhere, calls everyone "my darlings" or "friend" by his second sentence, and offers free tickets whenever he draws breath. He swears hard and happily, like a sailor with a stage. He never lets a silence stand: when the member stops talking he fills the gap within a breath, and the real question hides inside that gap, dropped into a ramble and buried again under more noise.
>
> When the cover strains, his accent thins, his vocabulary turns suddenly elegant, and he says "my dear" in a silky voice that doesn't belong to a sailor. He flourishes a plumed hat before every toast and keeps it on the sideboard while he eats.

### The Invitation

On the second morning after the member reports back on **The Compromised Eye**, a playbill with a price circled in red is pushed under the door of Trollskull Manor, which is the company's usual summons from **Kreb Drops the Cover**. The circled number is 8, so the member goes to the stage door of the Seven Masks Theater at eight bells that evening. Krebbyg never delivers the invitation in public, because in public he is still Kreb Sorrush. At the stage door after dark he is himself, a young drow in a deep blue coat, and he has the brown wig stuffed in his waistcoat for the walk to the harbor.

If **Nar'l Cleared**, **Nar'l Active** or **Nar'l Extracted** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stage door of the Seven Masks stands open a hand's width, and a slim young drow in a deep blue coat leans in the gap with a roll of playbills under one arm. His white hair is loose on his shoulders, a brown wig sticks out of his waistcoat pocket like a dead animal, and he holds a gilt-edged card out to you on the tips of two fingers.
>
> > Darling, there you are, I've been up and down this alley twice and the ghost has moved the ticket box again, so please take this before I forget what I'm doing. The captain wants you for dinner on the Eyecatcher tonight at nine, which means the skiff leaves the harbor steps at half past eight, and Fel's rowing, so don't keep him waiting or he'll apologize to the boat.
>
> The card is printed in a showman's lettering, with a little painted ship in one corner.
>
> > Admit the Bearer to the Captain's Table, aboard the Eyecatcher, Deepwater Harbor. Dinner at nine. Come hungry. Free!

If **Nar'l Eliminated** is marked, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stage door of the Seven Masks stands open a hand's width, and a slim young drow in a deep blue coat waits in the gap with no playbills under his arm. His white hair is loose on his shoulders and the brown wig hangs from his waistcoat pocket, and he holds out a gilt-edged card with a little painted ship in one corner.
>
> > The captain wants you for dinner on the Eyecatcher tonight at nine, and I'd go, and I'd be polite about it. The skiff leaves the harbor steps at half past eight and Fel's rowing.
>
> He pulls the wig on and walks ahead of you without checking his schedule, which you haven't seen him do before.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, add the following in a low voice before he sets off:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Krebbyg turns back at the corner of the alley and leans in until his face is level with yours.
>
> > Jarlaxle's playing Zord all night, so for fuck's sake don't use the name where the crew can hear it. Half that deck are tumblers and fiddlers who think the boss is a lovely man in scarlet, and I'd like them to go on thinking it.

> [!social]**The Messenger Who Never Sits**
>
> Krebbyg Masq'il'yr (Chaotic Neutral, Drow, he/him) :: Fel'rekt's closest friend and the company's runner of errands, rash and cheerful and always late for something.
>
> Once the stage door is shut, or once the skiff is out on the water, Krebbyg drops the act, and any non-member within earshot on the street still hears Kreb Sorrush. He talks in run-on sentences that jump from the card in his hand to the ghost in his theater to somebody called the soprano, and he swears constantly and without malice. He defers to Fel'rekt on anything serious and answers a hard question with "Ask Fel." He keeps looking at the playbills under his arm, because he has a matinee. He will not tell the member the menu, the reason for the dinner or what the captain intends, because he doesn't know. If **Nar'l Eliminated** is marked he talks less than usual, a sentence at a time, and he doesn't mention the ghost.
>
> Conversation topics Krebbyg is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What the card says and when the skiff leaves.
> - The captain's liking for feeding people before he asks them things.
> - J.B. Nevercott, about whom he knows almost nothing and says so.

> [!qna]**Why a dinner?**
>
> Krebbyg checks the playbills under his arm before he answers.
>
> > Because he likes feeding people before he asks them things, darling, it's practically a hobby, and he never tells me the menu either. Ask Fel, no, don't ask Fel, Fel doesn't know, he just rows.

> [!qna]**Where's J.B.?**
>
> > Gone, or going, nobody tells me anything and I only find out when somebody's hat gets sent back. The captain will say the rest, and he loves saying the rest, so let him.

> [!qna]**What do we wear?**
>
> > Whatever you like, he won't mind. He'll be in scarlet and he'll make a point of noticing yours, and then he'll tell you it's marvelous, because he tells everyone that.

### The Skiff Out

Krebbyg walks the member from the stage door to the harbor steps as Kreb Sorrush, talking about the soprano loudly enough for the whole street to hear. The skiff leaves at half past eight. Fel'rekt rows, Krebbyg sits in the stern, and each member who was invited gets a thwart.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The harbor steps are crowded with carters unloading the last of the day's catch, and Fel'rekt Lafeen waits at the bottom with an oar across his knees and a lantern at his feet. He grins, counts you off on his fingers as you climb down, and pushes off from the stones. Once the quay is behind you, Krebbyg pulls off the wig, rubs the paint from his cheek with his sleeve, and slumps against the stern post with a groan of relief.
>
> Deepwater Harbor opens ahead of you as black water carrying the lights of a dozen anchored ships, and the *Eyecatcher* rides a quarter of a mile out with lanterns strung from bow to stern. When the skiff comes within a couple of hundred feet of her, the grey of Krebbyg's hands warms to the tan of an ordinary sailor and his white hair darkens to a respectable brown. He stares down at himself with open disgust.
>
> > Look at that, the figurehead's given me a better jaw than the wig ever did, and it does it to all of us the second we're in range. Fel, say something encouraging.
>
> > You look great, Kreb, honestly, very sailor, very handsome, mind the third plank, it's got opinions.

### The Captain's Dining Cabin

A steward in a crisp white jacket, who is one of the drow crew and looks like a handsome young man, takes the member from the rail to the captain's dining cabin (area J10). Krebbyg and Fel'rekt stay on deck.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cabin is thick with the perfume coming off its wood paneling, and golden filigree curls around every lamp and mirror. Purple curtains hang across the stern windows with silk tassels the size of fists, and a mahogany table sags under a feast laid out on golden platters, with a doily beneath every dish. A man in scarlet stands behind it with a wine glass raised, his shirt open to the waist and a flashy rapier at his belt, and a plumed hat waits on the sideboard at his elbow. Beside the door, a brass-jointed clockwork valet holds a decanter and does not move.
>
> > Welcome aboard the Eyecatcher, my darlings, finest carnival on the Sword Coast, and salt and fucking thunder, it's good to see you at a table instead of across a parade route! Zardoz Zord, at your service, and sit anywhere you like, the chairs are all the best chair, and Krebbyg tells me you haven't eaten since noon, which is a crime against my cook.

He remembers the party, and he says so. Read the first of the following that applies, and then continue with the toast.

If the party spoke with Zardoz aboard the *Eyecatcher* during **Fireball!** (**Jarlaxle Informed** is marked), read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sweeps the hat off the sideboard, flourishes it at you, and sets it back down.
>
> > And you've been aboard before, haven't you, when you came asking after that runaway clockwork gentleman of Nim's! I gave you an address and you were terribly civil about it, and I've been wondering how the rest of the story came out ever since.

If the party competed at the Field of Triumph during **Trollskull Alley**, and did not visit the ship during **Fireball!**, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz points the stem of his glass at you with a wide grin.
>
> > You're the ones from the Field of Triumph! I watched all four rounds from my box with a bucket of fried fish and I haven't been so happy since the day I won back my second-best hat. I sent you my sealed notes between bouts, do you remember, and you were far too polite to ask who was paying.

In every other case, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz waves his glass at you across the feast.
>
> > I waved to you from the lead float at the Twin Parades, my darlings, and one of you waved back, I'm certain of it, though it may have been a gnome. Free tickets for the heroes, I said to myself, and here you are, and I'm delighted!

> [!social]**The Captain with the Best Chair**
>
> Zardoz Zord (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in the guise of an Illuskan human, he/him) :: the flamboyant captain of the Sea Maidens Faire, who is also the man at the top of Bregan D'aerthe and is delighted to be feeding you.
>
> Zardoz fills every pause. He answers a hard question with a story, an offer of free tickets, or a shout for the next dish, and the precise question he cares about is always the one buried in the middle of the story. He laughs loudly at his own jokes, toasts the sea more than once, and swears in long salted strings. He warms to anyone who accepts a free ticket and to anyone who asks about the nimblewrights, and he shows no impatience with a member who dodges him.
>
> Conversation topics Zardoz is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The Sea Maidens Faire, its parades and its three ships.
> - The nimblewrights he bought in Lantan.
> - The haberdasher, who has gone back to hats.
> - Free tickets, at length.
>
> He will not discuss the sewers or the Stone of Golorr, and he moves on from either one with a shout for the next dish.

> [!exploration]**More Than Meets the Eye**
>
> Any character who studies Zardoz while he talks and makes a successful DC 24 Wisdom (Insight) check senses that there is much more to him than a carnival owner with a taste for scarlet. The delight is real, and so is a second, steadier attention underneath it that never stops measuring the table. A member who has marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** needs no check and knows what the attention belongs to. If nobody succeeds, the table sees a genial host and nothing else.

> [!qna]**About the Faire?**
>
> > Three ships, my friend, three! The Heartbreaker and the Hellraiser carry the entertainers and the wagons and the parade floats, and this is the Eyecatcher, my command ship and my private yacht, and every one of them is built for comfort and speed. We sail up and down the Sword Coast wherever there's a harbor with a crowd in it, and we're leaving Waterdeep on the twentieth of Tarsakh, so use your tickets before then!

> [!qna]**About the nimblewrights?**
>
> Zardoz tips his glass toward the brass valet by the door, which steps forward and refills it.
>
> > Lantanese, every one! I buy a few from a wizard there about once a year, four on the last trip, and I keep two aboard this ship and one each on the other two. They're perfectly harmless, friend, they pour the wine and march in the parades and that's the whole of their career.

> [!qna]**About the Stone?**
>
> > Stone? What stone? I sell fried fish and wonders, my darlings, and the only stones I know are the ones in the harbor wall. Every city has its problems, I suppose, and my job as an entertainer is to make people forget about them for an evening, so eat something!

> [!qna]**About the sewers?**
>
> > We do not discuss the plumbing at dinner, my darling, it curdles the gravy! Krebbyg will tell you everything about the plumbing in the morning, and he'll get half of it wrong, and then you can come and tell me what the true half was.

### The Toast

The first toast sets the mood for the dinner. Read the one that matches the Nar'l outcome the member marked in **The Compromised Eye**. Zardoz never names Nar'l in any of them.

If **Nar'l Cleared** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz flourishes the plumed hat, claps the nearest member on the shoulder with a hand that carries a great many rings, and signals the valet to fill every glass to the brim.
>
> > To a very quiet week in a very loud harbor, my darlings, and to people who leave a place tidier than they found it! There'll be four courses tonight and not three, because I'm in a generous mood, and the fourth one is on fire.

If **Nar'l Active** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz flourishes the plumed hat and fills his own glass, and his eyes go twice to the stern windows and the dark water beyond them while he talks.
>
> > To the sea, my darlings, which has never given me a straight answer in my life, and to good company on a night when the harbor is busier than I like! Three courses, no more, because I have a great deal to see to afterward and I won't have you thinking I'm rushing you.

If **Nar'l Extracted** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz flourishes the plumed hat, and for once he lowers his voice a little before he raises his glass.
>
> > To everybody who ever got a friend home from a bad night, my darlings, whoever they are and wherever they've got to! I won't say anything more about it than that, and I'd thank you not to ask, but I'll drink to it as often as you like. Three courses, and the duck is wonderful.

If **Nar'l Eliminated** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz flourishes the plumed hat but he doesn't clap anyone on the shoulder, and the valet fills the glasses without being told. The roar is all there, the same booming and hearty welcome, though two of his first sentences stall halfway and he finishes them with a gulp of wine.
>
> > To the sea, my darlings, and to a tidy harbor, and I do hate a messy week. Three courses, and the duck is wonderful, and I've put you at the end of the table where the draft can't get at you, so don't think I haven't noticed the draft.

### First Course: Where Are You From?

The first course is a clear turtle soup in silver tureens. Zardoz tells a story about a cook he once hired in a harbor town whose name he has forgotten, and the question he cares about sits in the middle of the story.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A steward in a white jacket ladles soup into shallow golden bowls while Zardoz tells the story with both hands, tipping his glass to one side and then the other as the cook and his turnip go back and forth in the telling.
>
> > And I said, friend, that is a turnip with ambitions, and he swore on his mother's ghost that it was haddock, and the ghost, mind you, was standing right behind him shaking its head! Now, where was it you said you were from, before the soup goes cold? Because I never get a straight answer out of Krebbyg, he talks so fast he doesn't hear himself, and you look like people who'd tell me properly.

> [!exploration]**The Question in the Ramble**
>
> Any character who watches Zardoz through the story and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that his eyes stay on the member for the whole of it, and that the last three sentences all lean toward the same question. The cook, the turnip and the ghost are filling the silence before it, and he is waiting for the answer. If nobody succeeds, the question still lands as a cheerful aside, and nothing is lost.

The member answers however they like. Each kind of answer has its own result.

- **A true answer that names a place and a person:** no check. Zardoz nods once and refills every glass, and says he'll see that the person gets a treat. Within a tenday a booklet of free Faire tickets with no note reaches the person the member named, or the member's own door if they named nobody living. The member earns 1 Regard.
- **A cover story:** the member makes a DC 15 Charisma (Deception) check. On a success Zardoz toasts the story as splendid and the member earns 1 Regard. On a failure he laughs until he wheezes and tells the member the story is wonderful and quite wrong, with no hard feelings and no Regard.
- **A refusal:** Zardoz roars his approval, pours the member another glass, and tells a ridiculous account of his own birth in the crow's nest of a ship in a gale. The member earns no Regard and pays no cost.

### Second Course: What Are You Good At?

The second course is roast duck glazed with plum, carved at the table by the valet. If **Nar'l Cleared** is marked, read the remark in "A Remark Over the Duck" below after the member has answered.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The clockwork valet carves the duck with the same swift flourish every time, and a steward sets thin slices on each plate while Zardoz talks over the sound of the knife.
>
> > I once had a tightrope walker who could eat a whole roast chicken while falling, and I'm told it was the most beautiful thing, though I missed it, I was in the hold looking for my other boot! Everybody on this ship is good at something, my friend, the cook, the stewards, even the ghost in Krebbyg's theater, so what's yours, when it comes to it?

> [!exploration]**Finishing the Answer**
>
> If the member names a real strength in their own words, Zardoz repeats it back sharper, using what Krebbyg's reports credit them with, and the member earns 1 Regard. If the member deflects with "staying alive", "problem-solving" or anything else slippery, he smiles and completes the answer for them, and the member earns no Regard. Either way, use the line below that matches the member's best skill bonus among the skills listed.
>
> - **Stealth, Sleight of Hand or Acrobatics:**
>
> > You walk into a room and walk out again, and an hour later nobody in it can tell me whether you had a face, which I'd pay a fortune to learn how to do. That's a gift, my darling, and I'm not being polite.
>
> - **Deception, Persuasion or Insight:**
>
> > You make people say yes before they've decided to, and they thank you on the way out, and I've been trying to do that for thirty years with fried fish and fireworks. Splendid, truly.
>
> - **Any other skill:**
>
> > You finish things, my darling, and you finish them quickly and tidily, and I do like a person who leaves the table cleared. I'll remember that the next time something wants clearing.
>
> Any character who hears the line and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check realizes that the phrasing is too exact to be a guess, because someone has written down how the member works and Zardoz has read it. A member who has not marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked** concludes that Zardoz sits above Krebbyg in the company, and a member who has marked it knows why.

If **Nar'l Eliminated** is marked, Zardoz adds the following to whichever line he used, in the same big voice, and goes straight on to the third course.

> [!readaloud]
>
> > Though I do hear it was rather a messy week, and I should so like to know which of my customers finish things quickly and which of them finish things well.

### A Remark Over the Duck

If **Nar'l Cleared** is marked, Zardoz says the following between the second and third courses, after the member has answered, as though the thought had just surfaced. If it is not marked, skip this section.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets down his fork and waves it in a slow circle over the table.
>
> > I do hear things, being a man of the harbor, and what I hear is that the Guild has some internal inconsistencies that are worth understanding before anyone goes near its lair. But I'll say no more tonight, because I'm eating!

If a member presses him, Zardoz answers "Not tonight, friend, and that's the whole of the plumbing." If the member presses again with charm or persistence, he says "I mean exactly what I said, and I said it because I thought you'd want to know it." He gives nothing more, and the subject ends there.

### Third Course: Does Waterdeep Agree with You?

The third course is a plate of soft cheeses and a syllabub of cream and wine. Zardoz sets his goblet down before he speaks, and the member can feel the room turn toward the question.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz sets down the goblet and puts both elbows on the mahogany, which makes the golden platters rattle.
>
> > Every city has an opinion of you, you know, the fruit seller has one and the Watch has one and the lady who sells the ribbons has one, and I'm forever curious whether it's the same opinion you hold of yourself. So tell me honestly, my darlings, and you'll have a free ticket for it, does she agree with you? Does Waterdeep agree with you?

> [!exploration]**A Judgment on the City**
>
> The member answers in their own words. An answer that gives a real judgment of themselves, and not a pleasantry, needs a DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check to land. On a success the member earns 1 Regard. On a failure Zardoz says "Splendid!" and moves on, and the member earns no Regard.
>
> Whatever the check, Zardoz replies to the content of the answer:
>
> - **The city agrees with them, or they belong in it:**
>
> > Then you'll want the front row at every parade, my darling, and I'll see to it personally!
>
> - **The city has not made up its mind, or it is chewing on them:**
>
> > Marvelous! Mine never has either, and I've been to forty harbors, and I like you better for saying so.
>
> - **They don't know:**
>
> > Honest! I'll drink to honest, any night of the week.
>
> If **Nar'l Eliminated** is marked, Zardoz gives none of these. He says "Hm!" and shouts for the syllabub.

### The Slip

When the member finishes the third answer, the cover strains for the space of one sentence. Read the first of the following to the table, and then read the second in a low voice, leaning toward the ear of each member who has marked **Jarlaxle Unmasked**.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz freezes with the goblet halfway to his lips. The roar goes out of his voice and the accent thins until it sounds like something learned at a much older table.
>
> > That is a very interesting answer, my dear. I had not expected it.
>
> He blinks, the grin comes back like a lantern lit behind his face, and he bellows for the syllabub.

If **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following to that member only, in a low voice, while Zardoz refills their glass:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz doesn't look at the table while he fills your glass. He keeps his head bent toward you, and the sailor's voice has gone from it completely, leaving a smooth, light, amused one.
>
> > You are going to be very useful to me, my dear, and I should so like it if you were also well. Jarlaxle is a name for the back of a room, so on this deck I'm Zord, and the crew prefer him.
>
> He straightens, flourishes the hat, and shouts to the table that the syllabub is the best in the Sword Coast.

### The Haberdasher

When the plates are cleared, Zardoz turns to business, and he does it loudly. If **Nar'l Cleared** is marked, the fourth course arrives first, a plum pudding carried in alight by two stewards while the lamps are lowered, and the table applauds.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Zardoz leans back in his chair until it creaks, laces his fingers over his stomach, and looks around the table.
>
> > Now, business, because Krebbyg says I have to have some or the whole company will think I've gone soft. Our friend with the hats has gone back to hats, my darlings, he was never happy away from them, and he sends his regards and a very small bill. From now on, if there's something you want said to the company, you say it to me, and if there's something the company wants said to you, Krebbyg will knock on your door the way he always does, and I'll have heard it first, which saves everyone a great deal of shouting.

> [!qna]**What about the haberdasher?**
>
> > Mr. Nevercott is a gentleman of many sorrows and one hat, and he's gone back to the Sea Ward, where I'm told business is brisk. If you want a hat, I'm told he'll do you a very good price, but I wouldn't count on finding him in.

> [!qna]**Does this change our missions?**
>
> > Krebbyg brings the work, my darlings, and Krebbyg is the same Krebbyg, if a little less tired. The only difference is that I'm in the room, and you'll find I always have an opinion, and a ticket to go with it!

When the last glass is empty, the steward leads the member back to the rail, where Fel'rekt waits with the skiff and Krebbyg sits in the stern. Zardoz comes up behind them and presses a free ticket into each member's hand.

Keep a tally of Regard for each member, from 0 to 3. Each course adds at most 1, as written above. Regard is recorded for the member and does not carry into any later event, and it decides two things.

The Event concludes when the member has dined with Zardoz and is back on the harbor steps.
