# Design Notes: Coin Pouches

## Why the Pouches Got a Trail and a Threat

The restored draft was a delivery with nothing to decide. The source gives only "small, unmarked black pouches of coins from an anonymous source", and the amounts, the note and the anchor were remix additions. The anchor is gone with the old handkerchief, because the Twin Parades version of **The Handkerchief and the Girl** has no knot code to decode. In its place the member gets a three-lead trace that fails on purpose. Each lead ends at someone who was paid ahead for one small job, and the pattern shows the member how Bregan D'aerthe keeps its drow out of sight: nobody ever meets two links of the chain.

The Dunstan Rook scene adds the source's third support, to buy off or remove people who threaten the party, which no other Bregan D'aerthe event used. Dunstan is a human bouncer on Emmek Frewn's payroll, so he threatens the member from within the current Act I state without touching **The Shard Shunners** or Kelso Fiddlewick. Fel'rekt buys him off with 40 gp and a coach seat, in keeping with his refusal to kill when another option exists. The contrast with the careful pouch chain is deliberate: the money trail is Nevercott's discipline, and the Dunstan trail is Kreb and Fel'rekt's habit of getting it done in an evening.

## Who Holds the Gold, and the Note

The pouch goes to one named member by a fixed rule, because membership is individual and the gold should have an owner. The note, *A more interesting assignment follows shortly*, lives only in this event. **Three Nights** should refer to the pouch without repeating the text. The 100 gp slip is cut from the same black stock as Nevercott's First Meeting card, which replaces the old "no watermark" detail and gives the member something to compare.

Krebbyg appears only as "Kreb Sorrush", and Fel'rekt only by name in Kreb's speech. The scene is written for the days before **Kreb Drops the Cover** and carries a one-line variant for a member who arrives after it.

## Outcomes

The old **Coin Pouches** outcome had no reader and is cut. Nothing in this event is read later, so it sets none.
