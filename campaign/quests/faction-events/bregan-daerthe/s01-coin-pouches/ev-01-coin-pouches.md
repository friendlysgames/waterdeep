# Coin Pouches

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This two-beat Standalone Event fires automatically — no player choice determines whether it occurs. Beat 1 fires two days after the **Handkerchief and the Girl** debrief. Beat 2 fires the afternoon of the fourth day in **Three Nights**, the same afternoon Ott departs with Krebbyg. In this Event:
>
> - Both pouches arrive without explanation. No messenger is visible when they are found.
> - The anchor scratched into the silver coin in Beat 1 connects to the anchor embroidery on the handkerchief from **The Handkerchief and the Girl** — available to any character who made the DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check in that mission.
> - Krebbyg, if asked about either pouch, does not engage the question.
> - Jarlaxle is establishing a pattern of generosity before he introduces himself in person.

### Beat 1 — Fifty Gold and an Anchor

Two days after the **Handkerchief and the Girl** debrief, a small black coin pouch appears at Trollskull Manor's front door. No one sees the delivery. No one hears a knock.

The pouch holds 50 gp in gold pieces and one silver piece. The silver piece has a tiny anchor scratched into the face, applied with care, as though by a blade tip held steady.

There is no note.

> [!info]**Tracing the Pouch**
> The party finds nothing useful. The cloth is plain black linen, no maker's mark. The coins are standard Waterdhavian mint. A canvass of the immediate neighborhood turns up one fact: someone dropped the pouch and walked away quickly. Unremarkably dressed, no face recalled, no faction symbol. The anchor on the silver piece is the only distinguishing detail. Any character who made the DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check during **The Handkerchief and the Girl** recognizes the motif immediately and can draw the connection to Vessin's handkerchief. Introduce no new NPCs — no messenger, no witness, no supplier.

### Beat 2 — One Hundred Gold and a Note

The afternoon Ott departs with Krebbyg, a second black coin pouch appears at Trollskull Manor's door. The delivery method is identical: no sound, no witness, no explanation.

This pouch holds 100 gp. A folded note is tucked inside:

> > *A more interesting assignment follows shortly.*

The handwriting is clear and unhurried. The paper carries no watermark.

### Concluding the Event

#### Coin Pouches: True / False

Set True when both pouches have arrived. Records that Jarlaxle's pattern of anonymous generosity — established across **The Handkerchief and the Girl** and **Three Nights** — is complete before direct contact. Read by the **Dinner with Zardoz** Standalone Event.
