# Coin Pouches

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event has two beats and plays out only for Bregan D'aerthe members. The first black pouch arrives two days after the debrief for **The Handkerchief and the Girl**. The second arrives on the afternoon that **Three Nights** ends. In this Event the party can:
>
> - Receive a pouch of 50 gp with no note, and try to work out who sent it.
> - Trace the pouch through three leads and learn why none of them goes anywhere.
> - Find out why Dunstan Rook, a bouncer who has been threatening the member, stopped standing outside Frewn's Brews.
> - Receive a second pouch of 100 gp with a note promising "a more interesting assignment".

### The First Pouch

The pouch is left at the front door of Trollskull Manor before first light, two days after Nevercott's debrief for **The Handkerchief and the Girl**. Nobody hears a knock and nobody sees who left it. Companions who are not Bregan D'aerthe members receive nothing from this Event. What the member tells them about it is up to the member.

> [!gamemaster]**Who Holds the Gold**
>
> The pouch belongs to the member who marked **Handkerchief Delivered** in **The Handkerchief and the Girl**.
>
> - If several members marked it, it belongs to the one with the higher Renown.
> - If their Renown is equal, it belongs to the one who spoke first.
> - That member owns the 50 gp outright and may share it or keep it. Nobody from Bregan D'aerthe asks about it afterward.
> - The second pouch follows the same rule for **Three Nights**. It goes to the Beat 1 recipient if that member also ran **Three Nights**.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The front step of Trollskull Manor is cold and damp when you open the door, and the alley is grey with early light. A small black linen pouch sits in the middle of the step, tied with a plain black cord, and nobody is in sight in either direction. When you pick it up it clinks, and it weighs about as much as a good dinner.

The pouch holds 50 gold pieces and nothing else. The linen is plain and the cord has no knot or mark. There is no note, no seal and no initial, and the coins carry no stain or smell that could show where they have been.

### Tracing the Pouch

A member who wants to know who sent the gold has three leads, and each can be tried once. Each lead takes a morning. Every lead ends at someone who was paid in advance to do one small thing and never saw the person behind it.

> [!exploration]**Lead One: The Cloth**
>
> A draper in the Trades Ward can identify the linen if a member shows it and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check. It is Calishite weave, dyed black in the Dock Ward and sold by the bolt. On a failure, the draper takes a day and asks 5 gp for the same answer. The member can pay without a check.
>
> The Dock Ward dyer who made it has sold black bolts of that weave to six buyers in the last month. Each paid cash and gave a different name. The dyer remembers them only as a cooper's boy, a woman with a laundry cart and four others in plain coats. Two buyers gave addresses, a cooper's yard and a laundry. At both, the staff say a stranger paid a few coppers to have a parcel held for an hour and then collected it, and neither remembers a face.

> [!exploration]**Lead Two: The Coins**
>
> A moneychanger in the Trades Ward can examine the coins if a member makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check. The fifty gold pieces come from at least a dozen different mint years, and none of them was bright when it was given up. On a failure, the moneychanger sorts the coins for 5 gp and says the same.
>
> A moneychanger on the next street adds that someone has been trading handfuls of silver for gold across the Trades Ward for about a tenday. It was never at the same counter twice and never more than three coins at a time. The changer who handled the most of it describes a different customer each time, one with a market basket, one with a stable boy's apron and one with sailor's tattoos. None of them seemed to be together.

> [!exploration]**Lead Three: The Doorstep**
>
> A member who asks around the alley and makes a successful DC 11 Wisdom (Perception) or Charisma (Persuasion) check learns that Squiddly watched the step from the kitchen window. On a failure, Squiddly asks for a copper before he says so, and the member pays it without a check. Squiddly saw a woman in a laundress's apron set a covered basket on the step, look at the manor for a moment, and walk back toward the Dock Ward without knocking.
>
> The laundress is Hanna Voss, who is easy to find at the pump on the next street. A man on the Dock Ward bridge handed her the basket and 2 sp the evening before. He told her only the name of the street and that nobody was to be disturbed. She says he wore a plain cap and an ordinary coat, and she can't say whether he was young or old.

A member who has finished two of the three leads can make a DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check to put what they heard side by side. A member who has finished all three needs no check. The pattern is that everyone who touched the pouch was paid ahead for a single small job, saw one step of the chain and never met anyone who saw a second step. No lead can be pushed further, and a member who keeps asking gets the same answers again.

The member learns something about Bregan D'aerthe from this. Whoever sent the gold wanted to reward the member without being thanked, and has a network of people who do small jobs without meeting each other. The same discipline that stops the trail keeps the faction's drow out of the Watch's sight.

### The Man Who Stopped Coming Around

Dunstan Rook is a human bouncer in his thirties who works the door at Frewn's Brews. Emmek Frewn pays him 2 gp a night to scare the party's staff. Since the Grand Opening he has stood across the alley from Trollskull Manor every evening. He has told the Beat 1 recipient twice that someone will lose fingers if the party's tavern keeps stealing Frewn's customers.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> On the day of the Twin Parades, Dunstan followed the Beat 1 recipient into the Dock Ward and came within a street of the crate at Net and Dock before he lost them. Fel'rekt, who was watching the member for Bregan D'aerthe, saw him.
>
> The night before the first pouch arrived, Fel'rekt and Krebbyg found Dunstan at his lodging above a chandler on Ship Street. Fel'rekt offered him 40 gp and a seat on the dawn coach to Baldur's Gate. Dunstan took both and left on it. Nobody was hurt, and nobody asked the member first.
>
> This is the third support the source gives Bregan D'aerthe, to buy off or remove people who threaten the party's people, and Jarlaxle does it as a matter of course. Dunstan's departure does not end Emmek's campaign against the manor. It only takes away his doorman.

On the morning after the first pouch arrives, the Beat 1 recipient finds Frewn's Brews with nobody on its doorstep.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Across the alley the door of Frewn's Brews stands open, and the stool where the doorman usually sits is pushed against the wall. Emmek Frewn is sweeping the step himself with short angry strokes, and he counts the coins in his apron pocket between each pass. He glares at you as soon as you step outside.

> [!social]**Emmek Frewn**
>
> Emmek Frewn (Neutral Evil, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: the bitter owner of Frewn's Brews, who blames adventurers for everything and cannot finish an apology.
>
> Emmek assumes the party had something to do with Dunstan's absence and says so loudly enough for the alley to hear. He denies hiring anyone and talks about "honest trade" while he counts coins. He never admits the sabotage. If the member points at him, he gets louder and no smarter.

> [!qna]**Where is your doorman?**
>
> Emmek stops sweeping and points the broom at the member.
>
> > "Gone, that's where. He walked out in the night without his wages, and I'd like to know what you lot said to him. Adventurers ruin a street, and now I've got no one on the door and a barrel of ale to move. He had rooms on Ship Street above the chandler's, the one with the green shutters, not that I care where he went."

> [!qna]**Did you hire him to threaten us?**
>
> > "I never hired anyone to do any such thing. Say he stood outside, say he looked at you sideways, a man can stand on a public street, can't he? And who pays for a good doorman now? I lost everything once already, did you know that?"

The rooms above the chandler's belong to Orla Pennick, a widow in her sixties who owns the building and lets four rooms. She is sweeping her own stairs when the member arrives.

> [!social]**Orla Pennick**
>
> Orla Pennick (Neutral Good, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: the landlady on Ship Street, who talks to anyone who stands still and keeps careful track of who owes her.
>
> Orla liked Dunstan well enough despite his job, and she is pleased to be asked about the two young men who visited him. A polite word at her door gets her talking. She tells the full story to a member who makes a successful DC 11 Charisma (Persuasion) check or offers 2 gp for her trouble.

> [!qna]**Did Dunstan leave on his own?**
>
> Orla leans on her broom and enjoys herself.
>
> > "Well, he left on his own two feet, if that's what you're asking, but he'd had visitors the night before. Two young men in dark cloaks came up the stairs about the second bell. One was chatty, in a velvet waistcoat, and called me darling twice before he'd said good evening. The other kept his hood up and counted things on his fingers while he talked. They were in there about an hour, and he came out looking like he'd been given a fright and a present at the same time."

> [!qna]**What did they say to him?**
>
> > "I wasn't listening, I'll have you know, but I heard the chatty one say something about a coach, and money, and that nobody was going to hurt him. Dunstan was gone before dawn with his bag, and he paid me up to the end of the month, which no tenant has done in years. The room's as he left it, if you want to look. I haven't had the heart to clear it."

> [!exploration]**Dunstan's Room**
>
> A member who searches the rooms and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check finds two things. One is a half-burned coach stub for a seat to Baldur's Gate, with the date of the dawn coach on it. The other is a playbill from the Seven Masks Theater pushed behind the washstand. On a failure, Orla finds both when she sweeps the room two days later and offers them without being asked.
>
> The playbill is for a performance of *The Duke's Last Supper*. Someone has written in the margin in a quick sloping hand, *Kreb S.*, with a time beside it that matches the night of the visit.

The Seven Masks Theater is in the Dock Ward. The booking office is a narrow room off the lobby, with a schedule pinned on every wall.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The lobby of the Seven Masks Theater smells of glue, old velvet and fresh paint, and two stagehands carry a painted flat of a castle across it with a good deal of shouting. A slim young man in a velvet waistcoat stands in the booking office doorway with a pencil behind his ear and a schedule in one hand. He flips pages as he talks to nobody in particular.
>
> > "Kreb Sorrush, bookings, how can I help, casting's on Tuesdays, sorry, darling, and the matinee's full, and the ghost moved the fucking prop table again this morning, so if you're here about the table you'll have to take it up with him."

> [!social]**Kreb Sorrush**
>
> Kreb Sorrush (Chaotic Neutral, Drow in a human guise, he/him) :: Krebbyg Masq'il'yr, who runs the Seven Masks Theater's bookings and hides his drow nature behind the cover of a human stage manager.
>
> Kreb talks in rapid run-ons that jump from casting to gossip to the ghost, and he calls everyone "darling". He defers to Fel'rekt on anything serious and answers "What's the plan?" with "Ask Fel". He treats the member as a stranger until they show him the black card with the silver ship. After that he treats them as one of his own.
>
> - A member who shows the black card gets the whole story.
> - A member without it can try a DC 15 Charisma (Deception) check to pass as someone who already knows.
> - On a failure, or if the member does nothing, Kreb deflects with theatre talk and gives away nothing.
> - A member who puts the playbill from Dunstan's room on the counter sees his patter stop for a breath before it starts again. That confirms the trail leads here.
>
> The scene is written for the days before **The Wazoo Affair** concludes. If the member reaches the theatre after **Kreb Drops the Cover** has fired, Krebbyg no longer hides what he is from the member. He gives the same answers in the same run-ons without needing the card or the check.

> [!qna]**Dunstan Rook?**
>
> If the member shows the black card or makes the Deception check, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > "Oh, darling, you should have said, come in the back, mind the flat, the paint's wet. Dunstan, yes, big lad, he followed you round the Dock Ward like a lost dog, so Fel had a word with him. Forty gold and a seat on the Baldur's Gate coach, and nobody laid a finger on him, Fel wouldn't. Don't tell Nevercott I was loud about it, he says I'm loud about everything."
>
> If the member has neither, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > "Dunstan who, darling? We've got a Dunstable in lighting, but he's seventy and he's not been anywhere since the Founders' Day before last. Casting's on Tuesdays, and if you're not here about a part I have a tragedy to cast by tonight."

> [!qna]**Who is Fel?**
>
> If the member shows the black card or makes the Deception check, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > "My friend, tall, never stops counting things on his fingers. He's got a plan for everything and I haven't got one for anything, so I just ask him. He's out, he's always out. Ask Fel yourself next time, he'll tell you why, he explains everything, honestly it's exhausting."

> [!qna]**Why didn't anyone ask me?**
>
> > "Because the captain doesn't ask, darling, he fixes it, and then you find a pouch on your step. Fel says people are happier if the problem just stops, and I think Fel's right, though I'd have asked, personally, I like a chat."

The member now knows that Bregan D'aerthe dealt with Dunstan without being asked, and without violence. The careful chain behind the pouch and the loud pair who bought Dunstan a coach seat belong to the same faction, and the discipline of the first is missing from the second.

### The Second Pouch

The second pouch arrives at Trollskull Manor on the afternoon that **Three Nights** ends. Ott vanished with the iron bands at the end of the third night, and the cellar has been empty since.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The common room of Trollskull Manor is empty, and afternoon light slants through the shutters onto the bar. A black linen pouch sits there that was not there an hour ago, with a folded slip of black card under the cord. When you open it, the handwriting is clear and unhurried.
>
> > *A more interesting assignment follows shortly.*

The pouch holds 100 gp. The slip is the same heavy black stock as the card Nevercott left at the First Meeting, edged in the same silver, and a member who holds the two side by side sees that they match. Nobody saw the pouch delivered. If the member asks Kreb about it, he flips a page of his schedule and says "Ask Fel". Fel'rekt is never at the theatre when the member calls.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when both pouches have arrived. The recipients keep the gold. A member who traced the pouch or found Dunstan's lodging has seen how the faction behaves when it believes nobody is watching.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> This Event sets no named outcomes, because no later event reads what it records. Each pouch's recipient holds the gold. The trail, the Dunstan scene and the note change nothing mechanical afterward.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> This Event is for Bregan D'aerthe members only. The next Bregan D'aerthe Event is **Kreb Drops the Cover**, after **The Wazoo Affair**. **The Compromised Eye** follows **Three Nights** when the member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Two anonymous black pouches arrive for a Bregan D'aerthe member, one after their first job and one after their third. The member can trace the first and learns what happened to the bouncer who had been threatening them.

## Summary

A black pouch with 50 gp was left on our step two days after the handkerchief job, and we could not find who sent it. Every lead ended at someone who was paid ahead and had seen only one step of the chain. Dunstan Rook stopped standing outside Frewn's Brews, and his landlady and Kreb Sorrush at the Seven Masks told us that Fel and Kreb had bought him a seat on the Baldur's Gate coach. On the afternoon after Ott vanished, a second pouch with 100 gp arrived with a note promising a more interesting assignment.
