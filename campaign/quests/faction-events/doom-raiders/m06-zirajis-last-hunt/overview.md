# Doom Raiders Mission 6 — Ziraj's Last Hunt

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> **Doom Raiders Mission 6.** Available at Renown 12 and character level 7.
>
> **Trigger:** Yagra Stonefist arrives at Trollskull Manor before dawn. This mission is not delivered by flying snake or in a scheduled meeting. It is Yagra at the door, armor on, not winded despite having run.
>
> #### Difficulty
> A mission for 7th-level characters.
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

Manshoon assembled a kill team from Skeemo's intelligence files. Six Splinter agents, specific orders: eliminate Ziraj the Hunter before he could interfere with Kolat Towers. Ziraj killed three of them on a warehouse roof on Sail Street. He took a hand crossbow bolt through the left shoulder and a second grazing his ribs. He was mobile but not fast, and he had thirty minutes of rooftop progress toward Corellon's Crown before the three remaining agents identified his general direction.

Yagra Stonefist had been tracking both the kill team and Ziraj's trail marks since the first exchange. The trail marks were a system Ziraj had taught her years before — small scratches on eaves at halfling height, a pattern invisible at street level. She followed them east toward Trollskull Alley, counted the remaining agents between Ziraj and Fala's shop, and ran to Trollskull Manor.

She appeared at the door before dawn, armor on, three sentences out before the party had finished waking. She had a route. She did not wait for the party to confirm they were coming.

Ziraj was between two chimneys on an inn roof at the east end of Trollskull Alley, with two bolts in him and his oversized bow across his knees. When the party found him he said three words: "Three left." Then nothing else. He had a rooftop diagram folded inside his coat that he had been planning to use himself.

## Involved Characters
- **Yagra Stonefist** (Doom Raiders) — delivers the mission in person; has a route; moves without waiting for the party to agree
- **Ziraj the Hunter** (Doom Raiders) — the Doom Raiders' most dangerous operational asset; two crossbow bolts in him; conscious; laconic even at reduced capacity; produces the rooftop diagram when he can speak clearly
- **Fala Lefaliir** (independent) — proprietor of Corellon's Crown in Trollskull Alley; keeps a room for Ziraj; awake when the party arrives; closes the shop without being asked; has done this before
- **Three Manshoon Splinter agents** (kill team) — two at street level, one on a lower rooftop; searching the general area; have not located Ziraj yet

## Dangers & Enemies

**Three Splinter agents** — use the **Spy** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*). Two are at street level in the Trollskull Alley approach corridor; one is on a lower rooftop with a crossbow. The kill team originally had six members; Ziraj killed three of them before taking his wounds. The three remaining are competent but do not know exactly where Ziraj is. The party has a positional advantage if they find him first.

## Overview

Ziraj the Hunter is on a rooftop with two crossbow bolts in him and a Manshoon Splinter kill team closing on his position. The party has twenty minutes.
