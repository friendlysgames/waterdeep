# Ziraj's Last Hunt: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Available to an individual Doom Raiders member at Renown 13 and 7th level, after **The Yellowspire Job**. Seventh level requires all four lair heists, so **Kolat Towers** has already run. Companions can join the hunt.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 7th-level characters.*
>
> The kill team is sized to the party: a Warrior Veteran and two Spies for three combatants, an Assassin and a Spy for four, and an Assassin and two Spies for five. The **Doom Raiders Mechanics Reference** covers the rosters, Ondra Kell's first-turn limit and the reason Ziraj isn't counted. The count includes companions and Yagra if she fights.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Doom Raiders members who complete the assignment.

## Hook

Yagra Stonefist reaches Trollskull Manor at 02:10, armor on and breathing evenly despite the run from Sail Street. A Splinter kill team is hunting Ziraj the Hunter across the rooftops, and he is wounded somewhere east of where she lost his trail marks.

## Background

After **Kolat Towers**, the Splinter's survivors swept the tower's outbuildings and found a rooftop hide overlooking the pass-amulet handoffs. Skeemo Weirdbottle's files, or Skeemo himself if **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** was marked, place Ziraj in Trollskull Alley. Ondra Kell, a field officer who survived the raid, took five agents to kill the man she thinks helped the party through the force field. Who sent her depends on the **Manshoon operational?** result from **Kolat Towers**: Destroyed (Vevette Blackwater, if free, or no one), Simulacrum Only, or Alive. Urstul Floxin plays no part, and nothing in the event assumes he lives.

Ziraj killed three or four of them on a warehouse roof off Sail Street before two crossbow bolts brought him down. He has been working toward Corellon's Crown ever since, marking the eaves with a code he taught Yagra years ago.

## Finding Ziraj

The party has 20 minutes before the kill team's sweep reaches the inn near the alley's mouth, and Corellon's Crown is only a minute or two from Trollskull Manor. It can follow the trail marks, watch the sweep and find the gap in its coverage, or go to Fala Lefaliir and ask. Every way works, a failed attempt costs five minutes, and getting Ziraj down off the roof and to the shop takes six.

## Carrying Him Out, Holding the Roof or Laying a False Trail

The party can carry Ziraj to Fala's shop across the line of the sweep, hold the roof with Ziraj's longbow covering the alley, or draw the team away with a false trail. Each choice exposes Ziraj to a different risk.

## The Kill Team

Ondra Kell commands a team of agents who don't know the party exists. They prefer Ziraj as a target, and if he drops to 0 Hit Points he has two rounds to be saved. Ondra can be taken alive, and she trades what she knows about the Splinter's plans for the vault.

## Corellon's Crown

Fala closes the shop without being asked and treats Ziraj at the worktable. If he lives, he speaks to the party in the longest sentences he uses with anyone but Fala.

## Davil's Debrief

Davil receives the participating members at the Yawning Portal. Ziraj's fate and what the party learned from Ondra decide what he says.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Doom Raiders member gains 4 base Renown for taking part in the hunt and reporting to Davil, whether or not Ziraj survives.

- **+1 Renown:** Ziraj is alive at the end of the Event.
- **+1 Renown:** the party takes Ondra Kell alive and learns what the Splinter intends at the vault.

Companions who aren't Doom Raiders gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

The outcomes feed **Ardragon**, where Ziraj's favor exists only if he survived, and **Vault of Dragons**, where the Splinter's survivors and Davil's contact briefing read the result. If Ziraj dies, Davil announces that the favor is gone and offers Yagra's Grab, a capture-only substitute, instead. This is the last Doom Raiders mission, and it doesn't set **Force Field Gap Intel**, which **The Yellowspire Job** already delivered.

## Involved Characters

- **Yagra Stonefist** (Doom Raiders): brings the news, guides the party to the trail marks and carries Ziraj.
- **Ziraj the Hunter** (Doom Raiders): the wounded target, who reports in counts and speaks at length only to Fala.
- **Fala Lefaliir** (independent): shelters Ziraj at Corellon's Crown and treats his wounds.
- **Davil Starsong** (Doom Raiders): debriefs the members at the Yawning Portal.
- **Ondra Kell** (Manshoon's Splinter): commands the kill team and can be taken alive.
- **The rooftop Spy** (Manshoon's Splinter): shoots from a lower roof 40 feet from Ziraj.

## Dangers & Enemies

Ondra Kell uses the **Warrior Veteran** block for three combatants and the **Assassin** block for four or five, with a limit of two attacks on her first turn. The other agents use the **Spy** block. Ziraj uses the **Assassin** block with an oversized longbow, starts at 38 Hit Points, and isn't counted as a combatant. The team is not a suicide squad and breaks off when two of its members are down.

## Overview

Yagra wakes the party in the small hours with news that Ziraj the Hunter is wounded on the rooftops of Trollskull Alley and a Splinter kill team is closing in. The party must reach him first.
