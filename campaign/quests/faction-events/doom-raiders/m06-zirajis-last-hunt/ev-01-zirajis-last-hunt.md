# Ziraj's Last Hunt

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event begins when Yagra leads the party from Trollskull Manor toward the eastern alley corridor. In this Event:
>
> - Two independent paths identify Ziraj's position on the inn roof before the kill team finds him.
> - The party chooses: extract Ziraj first, ambush the three agents first, or split and do both simultaneously.
> - Fala Lefaliir receives the party at Corellon's Crown and assesses Ziraj's wounds without waiting to be asked.
> - After Ziraj stabilizes, Fala has information about his wounds and the bolt mechanics — this is its own scene.
> - Ziraj produces the rooftop diagram of Kolat Towers' force field gaps when he can speak clearly.

**Background (DM only)**

Ziraj has been moving for thirty minutes since the last exchange. He is not at risk of dying from the wounds — he is at risk of being found before the party reaches him. The kill team's search pattern is closing from the south; if no one intercepts them in the next twenty minutes, they will reach Ziraj's position. Yagra knows this, which is why she ran.

The trail marks — three small scratches at eave height, spaced at Ziraj's intervals — form a line from the Sail Street warehouse east toward Trollskull Alley's far end. Yagra knows the system and can guide the party to the first mark; subsequent marks require either her guidance or a successful Perception check.

### Finding Ziraj

Rooftop sequence, Trollskull Alley and the adjacent streets east toward the Dock Ward. Yagra moves ahead without commentary, pointing with nods rather than gestures, staying below the roofline silhouette.

**Following the marks:** A **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** check scanning the eaves at roofline height, or a **DC 12 check** with Yagra's guidance, traces the scratch marks. Three marks. The third mark leads to the chimney space of the inn at the east end of Trollskull Alley.

**Tracking the kill team:** The two street-level agents are visible from above. Their search pattern — heading south, then circling — is legible from a rooftop vantage: they believe Ziraj is somewhere in the southern half of the alley corridor. Watching their pattern for two minutes identifies where they are *not* looking. A **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check triangulates the area they have not covered, which is the inn's north face.

**Finding Ziraj:**

He is between two chimneys on the inn's roof. Two crossbow bolts — one through the left shoulder, one across the ribs. He is conscious, sitting against the far chimney with his oversized bow across his knees and his good hand on it. He watches the party arrive with the expression of someone who already counted the possible outcomes and is neutral about which one he got.

He does not ask how they found him. He says:

> > "Three left."

Then nothing else. He has spotted the rooftop agent — visible from his position, on a lower roof forty feet south. The agent has not spotted any of them yet. Ziraj's thumb traces a slow arc in the agent's direction and then back. That is all the communication he is planning to contribute until something moves.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Ziraj's Wounds
> The bolt through his left shoulder limits his draw — his ranged attack is at Disadvantage until magically healed or until after a Long Rest. The rib graze does not limit his movement but costs him a reaction if he takes any damage before his wounds are treated. He knows this. He is not going to lie about his capabilities if asked, but he is also not going to announce them to the kill team.

### The Three

The party makes a decision before anyone has moved:

**Extract Ziraj first:** Moving a wounded half-orc across rooftops requires a **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** check from one character, or two characters acting together (automatic success with two). The route to Corellon's Crown takes twelve minutes at Ziraj's pace. The kill team completes their sweep pattern and picks up the trail within that window — they arrive at Fala's shop door within fifteen minutes of Ziraj reaching it. The fight happens at street level, outside the shop.

**Ambush the three:** Ziraj is willing. He can hold the rooftop position for two rounds of shooting before the shoulder goes, targeting the rooftop agent while the party handles the street. The party positions around the street agents' approach route. They are walking into terrain they have not cleared; the party has initiative advantage. The rooftop agent requires separate targeting — line of sight from Ziraj's position is clear, but any party character who moves to engage street-level threats does not have a shot at her.

**Split:** Two characters extract Ziraj while two engage the agents. Higher risk on both fronts; faster resolution on both. A single failed Strength check on the extraction side delays Ziraj's arrival at Fala's by two rounds. A single failed attack roll on the ambush side means the street agents call for each other before being neutralized.

The three Splinter agents each use the **Spy** stat block. They are not fighting to the last — when the odds shift clearly against them they attempt to disengage and report back. A kill team that cannot report back is worth more than one that can.

### Corellon's Crown

**Fala Lefaliir** is awake when the party arrives. They sleep lightly and they recognized Yagra's knock before the door opened. They close the shop without being asked — the sign flips, the door locks — and they begin examining Ziraj before anyone has finished explaining the situation.

The shop is dim at this hour, lit by one working lantern over the worktable. It smells of fresh herbs and dried flowers and something sharper underneath. Fala moves efficiently and without ceremony. They have done this before.

> > "Two bolts. Left shoulder, ribs. The shoulder one went deep."

They look at Ziraj. He looks at the ceiling. This is the ongoing nature of their communication.

### Fala's Report

When both bolts are out and Ziraj has accepted whatever healing Fala can provide, Fala turns to the party. They are precise and unhurried in the way of someone who has had time to think while working.

> > "The shoulder bolt has a grooved tip. Not standard crossbow work. Someone machined this for puncture depth."

The bolts are evidence of professional manufacture — not Guild equipment, not standard Splinter issue. Someone with metalworking access made them specifically for this operation. A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check confirms they do not match anything in Waterdeep's standard armorer stock. This detail is available for the party to follow up on later; Fala notes it as unusual and does not extrapolate further.

> > "He needs two days of rest before that shoulder carries any weight. He won't take two days."

She says this as a statement of fact, not a complaint. She has a supply of medicinal tinctures for the party if they were injured in the fight: each one functions as a *potion of healing* and is pressed into available hands without comment or charge.

### Ziraj's Gift

Twenty minutes after the bolts are out, when Ziraj has had something from Fala's back shelf and is as close to comfortable as he is going to get, he speaks. His voice is flat and direct in the way of someone who has decided that making something easy to understand is the right way to communicate.

> > "The force field."

He reaches into his coat with his good arm and produces a folded diagram. Kolat Towers' exterior from multiple angles, with three positions marked in small, careful script: the north approach, the tower's shadow at noon, the passage behind the miller's. Beside each mark, a note: *six hours, one minute.*

> > "Three gaps. They rotate every six hours; each gap is one minute."

He sets the diagram on the table.

> > "I watched the amulet deliveries. I was going to use it myself. I won't now."

A pause. He looks at the diagram, then at the party.

> > "Use it."

The diagram, combined with the pass-amulet ledger from Mission 5, gives the Doom Raiders two independent entry paths into Kolat Towers' outer defenses: the amulets bypass the force field entirely, and the rooftop diagram shows when the field briefly drops for those without an amulet. Davil, when shown both documents the following morning, is quiet for a moment before he speaks.

> > "Between these two things, we can get into Kolat Towers three different ways."

He slides both documents into an envelope and holds it out to the party.

> > "Hold onto them. You're going to need them."

## Overview

Ziraj the Hunter is on a rooftop with two bolts in him and three agents between him and safety. Get to him before they do.

## Read Aloud

> Yagra is already moving when she finishes the sentence. She does not look back to see if you are following.
>
> The rooftop trails east from the alley in the dark. Below, two figures move through a search pattern that is getting closer to where they are not yet looking.

## Summary

The party located Ziraj the Hunter on an inn roof in Trollskull Alley, extracted him to Corellon's Crown, and dealt with the three remaining Splinter kill team members. Fala Lefaliir treated his wounds. Ziraj produced a rooftop diagram of Kolat Towers' force field gaps — intelligence he had assembled over tendays of observation and intended to use himself, now handed to the party before he lost the capacity.
