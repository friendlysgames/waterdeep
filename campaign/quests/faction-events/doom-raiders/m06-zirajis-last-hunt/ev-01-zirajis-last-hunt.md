# Ziraj's Last Hunt

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event occurs when Yagra Stonefist arrives at Trollskull Manor before dawn with news that Ziraj the Hunter is on a rooftop with two crossbow bolts in him and a Manshoon Splinter kill team closing on his position. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Yagra Stonefist in person, before she leaves without waiting for them to finish waking up.
> - Follow two independent paths to identify Ziraj's position on an inn roof before the kill team finds him.
> - Choose their approach: extract Ziraj first, ambush the three agents first, or split and do both.
> - Bring Ziraj to Corellon's Crown and receive Fala Lefaliir's treatment of his wounds.
> - Recover Ziraj's rooftop diagram of Kolat Towers' force field gaps.
>
> #### Background
>
> Ziraj has been moving for thirty minutes since the last exchange on a Sail Street warehouse roof. He is not at risk of dying from his wounds, but he is at risk of being found before the party reaches him. The kill team's search pattern is closing from the south; if no one intercepts them within twenty minutes, they will reach his position. Yagra knows this, which is why she ran.
>
> The trail marks — three small scratches at eave height, spaced at Ziraj's intervals — form a line from the Sail Street warehouse east toward the far end of Trollskull Alley. Yagra knows the system and can guide the party to the first mark; subsequent marks require either her guidance or a successful Perception check.

### The Brief

Yagra arrives at Trollskull Manor without a flying snake and without a meeting time.

> [!readaloud]
> The knock at the door comes before dawn, and whoever answers finds Yagra Stonefist on the step in her armor, her red jade Zhentarim pendant out, not breathing hard despite the fact that she clearly ran.
>
> > "Ziraj's on a roof near the end of the alley. Two bolts in him — shoulder and ribs. Three of Manshoon's people are still up, searching south, and they're closing. He's got maybe twenty minutes before they find him."
>
> She steps back from the door and turns east without looking to see if you are following.
>
> > "I know where he is. Come now."

> [!social]**Moving Before You're Ready**
>
> Yagra Stonefist (Neutral, Half-Orc, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' muscle, giving the party their marching orders before they've had a chance to put their boots on.
>
> Yagra is in emergency mode: direct, short, no softening of the situation. She has already mapped the route, counted the remaining agents, and identified Ziraj's position. She is not asking if the party is coming. She leaves if anyone stops to deliberate past a sentence or two.
>
> Conversation topics Yagra is willing to discuss include:
> - Where Ziraj is and how she found him.
> - How many agents are left and where they are.
> - What happened on the warehouse roof.
>
> Specific dialogue for Yagra on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**How do you know where he is?**
>
> She is already moving toward the east end of the alley.
>
> > "Trail marks. Three scratches on the eaves at halfling height — he taught me the system years ago. I followed them from the warehouse."

> [!qna]**How many agents?**
>
> > "Three left. There were six. He got three of them on the warehouse roof before they put two bolts in him. The ones left are at street level and on a lower roof south of him, working a search pattern they haven't finished yet."

> [!qna]**How bad is he?**
>
> She glances at whoever asked.
>
> > "Conscious. His bow's across his knees. He's slow right now, and he's got eyes on all three of them. He's waiting."

Once the party is moving, proceed to **Finding Ziraj**.

### Finding Ziraj

Rooftop sequence, Trollskull Alley and the adjacent streets east toward the Dock Ward. Yagra moves ahead without commentary, pointing with nods rather than gestures, staying below the roofline silhouette.

> [!exploration]**The Trail Marks**
>
> Any character who scans the eaves at roofline height makes a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check to trace Ziraj's scratch marks, or a DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check with Yagra's guidance. There are three marks in all. The third mark leads to the chimney space of an inn at the east end of Trollskull Alley.

> [!exploration]**Tracking the Kill Team**
>
> Any character who watches the street-level agents' search pattern from a rooftop vantage and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check reads their movements: they are heading south, then circling back, working the southern half of the alley corridor. Watching for two minutes triangulates the section they have not covered — the inn's north face.

The marks and the search pattern point to the same roof.

> [!readaloud]
> Between two chimneys on the inn's roof, sitting against the far chimney with his oversized bow laid across his knees, is Ziraj the Hunter. He has two crossbow bolts in him — one through the left shoulder, one grazing his ribs — and he watches you arrive without speaking.
>
> > "Three left."
>
> His thumb traces a slow arc toward a lower roof forty feet south, where a figure is visible from his position, and then back. The agent has not spotted any of you yet.

> [!social]**Ziraj the Hunter**
>
> Ziraj the Hunter (Lawful Evil, Half-Orc, he/him) :: the Doom Raiders' most lethal operative; two bolts in him; waiting.
>
> Ziraj does not ask how the party found him. He does not thank them for coming. He provides the one piece of information they need — the count — and then waits for the party to make a decision. His thumb arc toward the rooftop agent is the total of what he is going to offer until something moves.
>
> He will not lie about his capabilities if asked directly, but he will not announce them unprompted.
>
> Conversation topics Ziraj is willing to address include:
> - Where the remaining agents are.
> - What he can and cannot do with his wounds.
>
> Specific dialogue for Ziraj on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Where are they?**
>
> He holds up two fingers, then one, pointing south, then up.
>
> > "Two street level. One roof. South."

> [!qna]**Can you fight?**
>
> He looks at his left shoulder.
>
> > "Ranged. Left arm's slow." A pause. "Ribs too, if I take a hit before Fala fixes it."

> [!gamemaster]**Ziraj's Wounds**
>
> The bolt through Ziraj's left shoulder limits his draw: his ranged attack is at Disadvantage until his wounds are magically healed or until after a Long Rest. The rib graze costs him a reaction if he takes any damage before treatment. He knows this and will not lie if asked, but he will not announce it to the kill team either.

### The Three

The party makes a decision before anyone moves.

**Extract Ziraj first.** Moving a wounded half-orc across rooftops requires a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check from one character, or two characters acting together (automatic success with two). The route to Corellon's Crown takes twelve minutes at Ziraj's pace. The kill team completes their sweep and picks up the trail within that window — they arrive at Fala's shop door within fifteen minutes of Ziraj reaching it. The fight happens at street level, outside the shop.

**Ambush the three.** Ziraj is willing to hold position for two rounds, targeting the rooftop agent while the party handles the street-level pair. The street agents are walking into terrain they have not cleared; the party has initiative advantage. The rooftop agent requires separate targeting — any party character who moves to engage street-level threats loses line of sight to her.

**Split.** Two characters extract Ziraj while two engage the agents. A single failed Strength check on the extraction side delays Ziraj's arrival at Fala's by two rounds. A single failed attack roll on the ambush side means the street agents call for each other before being neutralized.

> [!hazard]**Three Splinter Agents**
>
> The three remaining Manshoon Splinter operatives each use the **Spy** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*).
>
> #### Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the two street-level agents move together toward the noise and call to each other if either is attacked.
>
> Over the course of combat, the agents will prioritize:
> - Locating Ziraj's exact position over engaging the party.
> - Disengaging and withdrawing to report if outnumbered.
>
> The battle ends when all three agents are neutralized or driven off. A kill team that cannot report back is worth more than one that can.

### Corellon's Crown

Fala Lefaliir is awake when the party arrives, and they recognized Yagra's knock before the door opened.

> [!readaloud]
> Corellon's Crown is dim at this hour, lit by one lantern over the worktable, and it smells of fresh herbs and dried flowers and something sharper underneath. Fala closes the shop before the last of you are through the door — the sign flips, the lock turns — and then crosses to Ziraj without stopping to ask what happened.
>
> > "Two bolts. Left shoulder and the ribs. The shoulder one went deep."
>
> They look at Ziraj. He looks at the ceiling. They begin working.

> [!social]**Fala Lefaliir**
>
> Fala Lefaliir (Chaotic Good, Wood Elf, they/them) :: Corellon's Crown's proprietor; they have done this before, and they don't need an explanation to start.
>
> Fala works through the examination without conversation, extracting the bolts and applying what they have. They answer questions between tasks and do not stop working to answer them. Their care for Ziraj is visible in how precisely they work around the injuries rather than anything they say.
>
> Conversation topics Fala is willing to discuss include:
> - What the bolts tell them about who made them.
> - The tinctures they press into available hands when they're done.
>
> Specific dialogue for Fala on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**About the bolts?**
>
> Fala sets the shoulder bolt on the table beside the lantern.
>
> > "Grooved tip. Machined for penetration depth — that's not standard crossbow work. Someone built this to do exactly what it did. You won't find these at a Waterdeep armorer."

> [!qna]**Is he going to be all right?**
>
> They press a tincture into the nearest available hand without looking up from the wound.
>
> > "Two days before that shoulder carries weight. He won't take two days." They hand another tincture to the next person in reach. "These are for you. Drink them."
>
> Each tincture functions as a *potion of healing*.

### Fala's Report

When both bolts are out and the initial treatment is done, Fala turns to the party with the focus of someone who has been thinking while working.

> [!exploration]**The Custom Bolts**
>
> Any character who examines the shoulder bolt and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check confirms that it does not match anything in Waterdeep's standard armorer stock. Someone with metalworking access made these bolts specifically for this operation, machined for puncture depth rather than broadhead damage. The detail is worth following up on; Fala notes it as unusual and does not draw further conclusions.

### Ziraj's Gift

Twenty minutes after the bolts are out, when Ziraj has had something from Fala's back shelf, he speaks.

> [!readaloud]
> Ziraj reaches into his coat with his good arm and sets a folded diagram on the worktable — Kolat Towers' exterior from multiple angles, three positions marked in small, careful script beside each of them.
>
> > "The force field. Three gaps. They rotate every six hours; each gap is one minute."
>
> He looks at the diagram, then at the party.
>
> > "I watched the amulet deliveries. I was going to use it myself." He pauses. "I won't now. Use it."

When the party shows both the diagram and the pass-amulet ledger from Mission 5 to Davil, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Davil lays the ledger beside the diagram and is quiet for a long moment, longer than his usual considering silences.
>
> > "Between these two things, we can get into Kolat Towers three different ways."
>
> He slides both documents into an envelope and holds it out.
>
> > "Hold onto them. You're going to need them."

### Concluding the Event

Ziraj is at Corellon's Crown, recovering. Yagra stays for two days without explaining why. The diagram is in the party's hands.

When Yagra finally leaves, she says to no one in particular and to the party specifically:

> [!readaloud]
> > "We take care of our own."

Davil delivers a final note — a list of forty-three names with locations and last known roles: every Manshoon Splinter operative he has identified over three years. The note says "For **Kolat Towers**. Do what you think is right with what you find there." It is signed with his name and nothing more.

## Overview

Ziraj the Hunter is on a rooftop with two bolts in him and three agents between him and safety. Get to him before they do.

## Summary

We located Ziraj the Hunter on an inn roof in Trollskull Alley, extracted him to Corellon's Crown, and dealt with the three remaining Splinter kill team members. Fala Lefaliir treated his wounds and identified the bolts as custom-machined for penetration depth — not standard issue. Ziraj produced a rooftop diagram of Kolat Towers' force field gaps, intelligence he had assembled over tendays of observation and had intended to use himself.
