# Viper

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Doom Raiders member first reaches Renown 10, which usually happens around the end of **Silencing Skeemo**. Tashlyn Yafeera names the rank while Davil Starsong is in Watch custody, and Davil names it once he is back. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Decide whether to spend this quest's forged document now, and choose its type and cover identity.
> - Learn how to call Yagra Stonefist, or a substitute, as muscle for one operation per quest.
> - Meet the four specialists on the Network's bench and learn what each costs.

### The Summons

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 10, a flying snake finds them and carries an invitation for that evening. Who sends it depends on where Davil is:

- **While Davil Arrested is marked and Davil's Return has not played:** Tashlyn writes the note. The meeting is at 20:00 at the Dusty Ladle, a South Ward taproom, at the table in the back corner.
- **After Davil's Return has played, or if the rank is reached before Davil Arrested is marked:** Davil writes the note and receives the member in his curtained room at the Yawning Portal at 20:00. Tashlyn attends, standing by the curtain, because the specialist bench is hers.

Every qualifying member is invited on their own account and is recorded one by one. Companions who are not Doom Raiders members are not invited. A member away from Waterdeep finds the snake waiting at their first surface lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the next evening at the same hour.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Every benefit here is real, and every one is the Network's own to give. The forger, the locksmith, the alchemist and the costumer are independent tradespeople who owe the Doom Raiders favors. If **Silencing Skeemo** has played, the Network has no alchemist of its own, because Skeemo's shop is closed and Loria Finch has taken the bench seat. If **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** is marked, his files are in enemy hands as well. Nobody in this Event can name Manshoon unless **Manshoon Named** has been marked. Tashlyn says "Skeemo's masters" or "the other cell".

### Naming the Rank

If Tashlyn is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Dusty Ladle is a low, smoky taproom with sawdust on the floor and a fire of sea-coal in the hearth, and most of the patrons hunch over their bowls without looking up. Tashlyn sits in the back corner with her back to the wall and a full cup in front of her, and she nods at the empty chairs as you cross the room.
>
> > Viper. You've earned the name, and I'll tell you what it opens, so sit. Davil is still in a cell, so the arrangements run through me, and I don't intend to explain them twice.

If Davil is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil is already in his chair when you come through the green curtain, and the decanter is on the table beside five glasses. Tashlyn stands against the wall by the door with her arms folded, and a long shadow across the floor marks where Yagra has taken the landing outside.
>
> > Sit, please, my friends. You've been a great credit to us, and it gives me real pleasure to call you Viper. Tashlyn will tell you the particulars, since she keeps the ledgers and I only keep the book, yes?

> [!social]**The Master of Arms**
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Arms and Mercenaries, who runs the cell's practical business and thinks it is run better without speeches.
>
> Tashlyn does not sit unless she is receiving guests in a taproom, and she does not repeat herself. She swears at incompetence, never at members who have earned a rank, and she says "Adequate" only when she means high praise. She never says she misses Davil.
>
> Tashlyn is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The three Viper benefits and the limits on each.
> - Which tradespeople stand behind the Network's papers and its bench.
> - What happened with Skeemo, if **Silencing Skeemo** has played and the member asks.

> [!qna]**What does Viper get me?**
>
> > Once each quest you can have one forged document made, and once each quest you can have Yagra beside you for a single operation. You also have a specialist on the bench for one job a quest, and after that job the price is fifty gold a day.

> [!qna]**Who stands behind the papers?**
>
> Tashlyn turns her untouched cup a quarter turn on the table.
>
> > A scrivener in the Trades Ward called Halric Sennet, who has never been caught and doesn't intend to be. You'll meet him when you order, and you won't need to ask me how he does it. The bench is four people I trust, and I'll name them before you leave.

> [!qna]**What happened with Skeemo?**
>
> > He sold us to the other cell for years, and I should have seen it sooner than Davil did, which I'll remember. His shop is shut, so there's a halfling on the bench now, and I'll test her before I trust her with anything that matters.

If Davil is receiving the member, Tashlyn gives the same answers while he listens, and Davil adds this once she finishes:

> [!qna]**Is there anything you want from us?**
>
> Davil turns a small trophy on his bracelet.
>
> > Only what I've always asked for, my friends, which is that you come back in one piece and tell me what you found out. The rest is Tashlyn's department, and she will tell you I'm too generous with it.

### The Papers

The Network arranges one forged document per quest for each Viper member, counted against the quest the party is in when the member asks. A document is one of three kinds: identity papers, a professional license, or a letter of introduction.

> [!exploration]**One Document per Quest**
>
> The member names the kind of document and the cover identity, and the player chooses the name. The member asks Tashlyn or Davil, or tells Yagra at the bar of the Yawning Portal, and Halric Sennet brings the finished papers to the member's lodging at noon on the third day, with no charge and no roll for availability.
>
> - **What the papers do:** they pass any ordinary inspection at a gate, a counter, a guardpost or a noble's door. An official who has a reason to doubt them must make a DC 16 Intelligence (Investigation) check to find a flaw.
> - **What they cannot do:** they fail a records search. Identity papers name a person who appears in no register, a license is not in the guild's own book, and a letter of introduction fails if the person it introduces is asked about. They also fail magical verification, which is the limit the Factions Guide sets.
> - **How long they last:** for the rest of the quest they were made for and a tenday after it ends. Halric asks for them back or burns them.
> - **Decision:** the member decides at this meeting whether to order this quest's document now or to keep it for later in the quest. A quest's document not ordered before the quest ends is lost.

> [!social]**The Scrivener**
>
> Halric Sennet (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a soft-spoken Trades Ward scrivener in his sixties, with ink-stained fingers, who has never put his own name to anything.
>
> Halric is Indifferent to the member's reasons and Friendly to their patience. He asks about the cover identity in detail, because a story with holes is worse than no papers. He will not make a document for a kind of person who does not exist, such as a licensed Waterdhavian guild mage with no guild.
>
> Halric is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The cover identity, its hometown, its trade and its last employer.
> - How long the papers will last and what will make them fail.
> - Nothing else. He declines to say who his other clients are.

> [!qna]**What do you need from me?**
>
> > A name, a trade, a hometown and a reason for being wherever you're going, and I'd like them by tomorrow evening. I'll have the papers at your lodging by noon on the third day, and I'll ask you to bring them back when you're done.

> [!qna]**Will they hold up?**
>
> Halric lifts a blank sheet to the light and studies the grain.
>
> > Against a constable at a gate, or a clerk at a counter, they will hold beautifully. If someone writes to the guild to ask after you, I can't help you, because there is no guildsman to be found. Please don't forget that.

### The Muscle

Yagra Stonefist is the Network's muscle for each Viper member, for one operation per quest.

> [!exploration]**Calling Yagra**
>
> The member tells Tashlyn or Davil, or Yagra herself at the Portal bar, the quest and the operation. Yagra waits at the bar of the Yawning Portal the following evening at sundown and goes out with the member. An operation runs from the moment the member leaves the Portal with her until the member is back at a safe lodging, or until the member dismisses her.
>
> - **One Yagra:** Davil or Tashlyn keeps a single ledger. If two members ask for the same evening, the first gets Yagra and the second gets Brannoc Hale, below.
> - **Limit:** one operation per quest per member, and the ledger counts it against the quest the party is in.
> - **What she refuses:** she will not attack the helpless or a Watch officer who has not attacked first. She stays until the member orders a retreat, and she does not take orders from anyone but the member who called her.
> - **If she is not free:** Tashlyn sends Brannoc Hale in her place on the same terms.

> [!gamemaster]**Yagra as an Ally**
>
> Yagra uses the **Warrior Veteran** stat block with two changes. Her Greatsword attack is replaced by a pair of iron-studded gauntlets with the same attack bonus and damage, but bludgeoning. She also has Relentless Endurance once per day: the first time she is reduced to 0 Hit Points and not killed outright, she drops to 1 Hit Point instead. Brannoc Hale uses the unmodified **Warrior Veteran** block with his Greatsword.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, a Warrior Veteran is a CR 3 ally with Power 30 for a party of levels 5 to 10 and 25 for levels 11 and above. Add that to the party's Power when she or Brannoc is present and recalculate the encounter. An ally who is on the way does not lower the difficulty.
>
> #### Yagra's Tactics
>
> To begin combat, Yagra closes on the nearest enemy who is threatening the member and hits it with her gauntlets. During combat, she:
>
> - Targets whoever is attacking the member, then the largest enemy in reach.
> - Grapples and holds an enemy who tries to flee, instead of killing it.
> - Follows the member's agreed plan and stops if the member calls her off.
>
> She fights until she is dropped, or until the member orders a retreat and everyone else is out.

> [!social]**The Doorkeeper**
>
> Yagra Stonefist (Neutral, Half-Orc, she/her) :: Davil's bodyguard and the Network's muscle, who does exactly what she is asked and complains about it cheerfully.
>
> Yagra is Friendly, loud and blunt, and she swears in nearly every sentence. If the member has not yet arm-wrestled her, she offers before they leave the Portal, with no effect on the job.
>
> Yagra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The operation: where, who, and how many she might have to hit.
> - Ziraj, if the member asks, and she will tell them he is fine.
> - Nothing about Davil's business, which is Davil's.

> [!qna]**What will you do for us?**
>
> Yagra cracks her knuckles, one hand and then the other.
>
> > I hit whatever you point at, and I stay until you say we're done. I'm not your fucking bodyguard, though, so if you do something stupid I'll tell you it's stupid, and then I'll do it with you anyway.

> [!qna]**Will you stay after the operation?**
>
> > No, I've got a bar to sit at. You want me again, ask Tashlyn or ask me next quest. Shit, don't be a stranger, I like the work.

> [!social]**The Understudy**
>
> Brannoc Hale (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a scarred sellsword in his forties, trained by Tashlyn, who takes Yagra's place when she is out and never explains himself.
>
> Brannoc is Indifferent, dry and competent. He speaks in plain sentences, takes the money and the order, and does both without a word of complaint.
>
> He has one thing to say about the job:

> [!qna]**Why did she send you?**
>
> > Because Yagra's busy and I'm the man Tashlyn trusts to stand where he's put. Tell me where that is.

### The Bench

The Network keeps four specialists, and each Viper member may call one for a single job per quest at no charge.

> [!exploration]**The Specialist Bench**
>
> The member names the trade to Tashlyn, Davil or Yagra, and the specialist meets the member at a surface place in Waterdeep of the member's choosing at dawn the next morning. A job is a single task, finished in one day. After the free job each quest, the specialist charges 50 gp per day, paid at the end of each day. No specialist will go below the city.
>
> - **Halric Sennet, forgery:** copies a handwriting or seal from a sample within an hour and forges or alters one written document on the spot.
> - **Brenna Dolgar (Neutral, Shield Dwarf, she/her), lockpicking:** opens any non-magical lock of DC 20 or lower in one minute.
> - **Loria Finch (Neutral, Lightfoot Halfling, she/her), alchemy:** identifies any non-magical potion, poison or reagent on sight and prepares one dose of antitoxin, Basic Poison or acid overnight.
> - **Odalys Quenn (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her), disguise:** disguises up to four people in an hour, and an observer needs a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check to see through it.

> [!qna]**Who are they?**
>
> > Four people I've used for years, all on the surface and all for hire at fifty gold a day after the first job. I don't name them in writing, and they don't name me. Ask them what they can do, and they'll tell you truthfully.

> [!qna]**What if I want the forger as my specialist?**
>
> > Then he counts as your bench job and not as your paper, and you get both. The paper is one thing and a man in the room is another, so don't confuse them, and don't waste my forger on a door you could have opened.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Viper member earns further Renown from the missions and from the ordinary rewards in the Doom Raiders page of the Factions Guide.

### Aftermath

The member can tell their companions what they ordered and who they met. The papers, the muscle and the bench stay individual benefits, and a companion who asks in the member's name is turned away.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has decided about this quest's document and knows how to call Yagra and the bench.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Viper Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Tashlyn or Davil names the rank; read by **Ardragon** and by the Doom Raiders page of the Factions Guide. Track separately for each member the quest each benefit was last used in.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three benefits are active for this member from the next morning. **Ardragon** occurs when this member reaches Renown 25. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Tashlyn Yafeera, or Davil Starsong once he is back, names a Doom Raiders member a Viper and explains the rank's forged papers, hired muscle and specialist bench.

## Summary

We were made Vipers of the Doom Raiders and learned that the Network will forge us papers, lend us Yagra for an operation and put a specialist on our side once every quest. Each of us decided what to spend first.
