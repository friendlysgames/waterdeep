# Made Viper

### The Flying Snake

The snake arrives at whatever address the party uses for correspondence. The scroll is in Tashlyn's handwriting — close, controlled, no more words than necessary.

A taproom name. A time. A district.

### Tashlyn's Corner

The South Ward taproom she uses is not loud. She is already seated when the party arrives, back to the wall, a cup she has not touched in front of her. She watches them cross the room and nods at the chairs.

> > "Viper. You've earned the name."

She says it the way someone states a fact that required considerable evidence to establish. She lays out the Viper benefits the same way: one at a time, no ornamentation.

**Documents.** The Network can arrange one forged document per quest — identity papers, professional licenses, letters of introduction. The forgeries pass scrutiny without magical verification. Requests go through the usual channel; she needs three days' notice and a specific cover identity. She does not ask why.

**Muscle.** Yagra Stonefist is available to accompany the party on one operation per quest. Yagra is not a bodyguard and she will not pretend otherwise. She is there to handle what needs handling, and she handles it with a veteran's competence. If Yagra is unavailable for a specific operation, another Doom Raider of comparable capability can be arranged.

**Specialist access.** The Network maintains a bench of specialists — forgery, lockpicking, alchemy, disguise. One job per quest at no charge. After that, the rate is fifty gold per day. She does not name the specialists tonight; they will introduce themselves when they arrive.

> > "You don't reach Viper without knowing how to use what you're handed. So use it."

She stands. The cup stays untouched on the table.

> [!profile]**Profile: Tashlyn Yafeera**
>
> **Roleplaying Information**
> ***Resonance.*** Tashlyn should inspire respect for her martial competence, irritation with her unfettered superiority, and grudging trust when her efficiency as faction contact exceeds what anyone expected.
>
> ***Emotions.*** Most often: competitive pride, quick-burning anger, soldier's contempt for weakness, impatient competence, controlled fury channeled into precision rather than explosions.
>
> ***Motivations.*** Keep the Doom Raiders armed, trained, and operational. Prove she is the stronger leader. Best anyone who challenges her.
>
> ***Inspirations.*** Okoye (*Black Panther*), Lady Eboshi (*Princess Mononoke*), Amos Burton (*The Expanse*).
>
> **Character Information**
> ***Persona.*** To the world: Calishite bodyguard to a dwarf magister at the South Gate. To those she trusts: born to a well-off Calishite family, unfettered sense of superiority, quick to anger. She respects anyone who can beat her in melee and despises everyone else.
>
> ***Morale.*** She closes distance and stays there. Retreat offends her. Only a fight she is clearly losing makes her disengage, and she remembers the face of whoever forced it.
