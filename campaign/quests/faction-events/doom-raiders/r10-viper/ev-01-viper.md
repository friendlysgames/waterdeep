# Made Viper

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Doom Raiders renown reaches 10. Tashlyn Yafeera delivers the promotion, since Davil is in Watch custody during the window when Renown 10 is most likely reached — after Mission 3 and before Davil's return at the end of Mission 4. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive a meeting time and South Ward taproom address from Tashlyn's flying snake.
> - Receive the promotion to Viper rank from Tashlyn in person.
> - Hear the three Viper benefits named in order: forged documents, Yagra Stonefist as muscle, and specialist access.
>
> **If Davil Released is True** when this threshold is reached — an unusual sequence, meaning the character reached Renown 10 after Davil's return — Davil delivers the promotion at the Yawning Portal in his usual way. The benefits are identical; the warmth is his.

### The Flying Snake

The snake arrives at whatever address the party uses for correspondence. The scroll is in Tashlyn's handwriting: close, controlled, no more words than necessary. A taproom name. A time. A district.

### Tashlyn's Corner

The South Ward taproom Tashlyn uses is not loud. She is already there when the party arrives.

> [!readaloud]
> Tashlyn Yafeera is in the far corner when you arrive, her back to the wall, a cup she has not touched on the table in front of her. She watches you cross the room and nods at the chairs without standing.
>
> > "Viper. You've earned the name."
>
> She says it the way someone states a fact that required considerable evidence to establish. She lays out the benefits the same way: one at a time, no ornamentation.

> [!social]**Tashlyn Yafeera**
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: Master of Arms and Mercenaries; precise, superior, and impatient with questions she considers obvious.
>
> Tashlyn delivers the Viper benefits in the order she decides is logical and does not welcome interruption. She finishes her sentences before answering questions. She treats the promotion as a statement of evidence rather than a gift and does not say congratulations. When she is done, she stands, and the cup stays on the table.
>
> Conversation topics Tashlyn is willing to discuss include:
> - The forged document arrangement: what it covers and how to request it.
> - Yagra Stonefist's availability: conditions and limitations.
> - Specialist access: what the bench offers and the cost after the first job.
>
> Specific dialogue for Tashlyn on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**About the documents?**
>
> > "One forged document per quest — identity papers, professional licenses, letters of introduction. They pass scrutiny without magical verification. Three days' notice, a specific cover identity, through the flying snake channel." She does not look up from the table. "I don't ask why, and you won't explain unless I do."

> [!qna]**About Yagra?**
>
> > "Yagra Stonefist. One operation per quest." She lets that sit. "She is not a bodyguard, and she will not pretend otherwise. She handles what needs handling, with a veteran's competence. If she is unavailable for a specific operation, I'll arrange someone comparable."

> [!qna]**About the specialist bench?**
>
> > "Forgery, lockpicking, alchemy, disguise. One job per quest at no charge. After that, fifty gold a day — standard rate. They introduce themselves when they arrive."
>
> She stands. The cup stays on the table.
>
> > "You don't reach Viper without knowing how to use what you're handed. So use it."

## Overview

Tashlyn Yafeera acknowledged the promotion to Viper rank at a South Ward taproom: seated, untouched cup in front of her. She named the three things the rank opens and stood when she was done.

## Summary

After reaching Renown 10, the character was promoted to Viper rank by Tashlyn Yafeera. She explained the forged document arrangement, Yagra Stonefist's availability as muscle, and the Network's specialist bench, then ended the meeting by standing and leaving her drink untouched.
