# Design Notes: Viper

## Muscle and Yagra

Guide 07 says Yagra or another Doom Raider serves as muscle using the **veteran** stat block, and her Notable Figures page says **Thug (with modifications)**. Neither name exists in the 2024 Monster Manual. This event uses the **Warrior Veteran**, because a Viper is at least 5th level and a Tough at CR 1/2 would be no help. Yagra gets two changes drawn from her profile: a pair of gauntlets in place of the Greatsword, and Relentless Endurance as a half-orc. Brannoc Hale uses the plain block. Her Notable Figures page stays as written and is logged as a conflict.

Ally Power follows the mechanics reference: 30 for levels 5 to 10 and 25 from level 11. The reference gives no Tier 1 figure, which does not matter because Viper is never reached before 5th level in the expected run.

## Procedures and the Document Decision

Each benefit is per member and per quest with a named contact. The decision is whether to order this quest's forged document now or keep it, because an unused document is lost when the quest ends. The papers fail a records search and magical verification, and the event says so to keep them from becoming a master key. The specialist bench is four named tradespeople, and the forgery specialist and the forged document are separate benefits.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Davil's post-return venue follows **Davil Released** and its sealed-suite terms. Invented: the Dusty Ladle, Halric Sennet, Brenna Dolgar, Loria Finch, Odalys Quenn and Brannoc Hale. All are voiced from the event text. Loria replaces Skeemo on the bench when **Silencing Skeemo** has played, and that reading belongs in the out-of-scope notes. Whether Yagra's appearances in **The Yellowspire Job** and **Ziraj's Last Hunt** consume this rank's once-per-quest allotment is left to those missions and is not settled here. The Factions Guide "veteran" wording and the Notable Figures "Thug" wording need a 2024 fix.
