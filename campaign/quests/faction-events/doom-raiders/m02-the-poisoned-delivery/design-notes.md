# Design Notes: The Poisoned Delivery

## Why the Errand Has Four Answers

***A single delivery check was not a mission.*** The source mission asks the party to carry a coffer and then hands them a Wazoo notice they can do nothing about. The rewrite keeps that shape and gives the party a decision at every stage: whether to look inside, how to find out what is inside, what to do at the statue, and what to say to Davil. Handing over, warning, swapping and refusing each leave Rallygar, Esvele and Skeemo in a different state, and the outcomes record those states separately so a warned Viper who still got the poison is not confused with a party that walked away.

***Three clues, none of them mandatory.*** The DC 20 Arcana check and *Detect Poison and Disease* come straight from the source, and a 3rd-level party will often have neither. The numbed tongue, the herbalist, the missing batch number, Skeemo's watchfulness and the Wazoo notice give the same conclusion by independent routes, and nothing in the mission stops if the party misses all of them. The party learns the truth from the notice at worst, and that leaves them with the source's own dramatic question, which is what to do now that they know they were used.

## Esvele, Rallygar and the Poison

***Why Esvele wants the vials.*** In the source, the Black Viper simply collects a coffer and a merchant dies. Her Notable Figures page says she does not kill, and the source says her pattern includes "something more permanent." The rewrite settles the conflict by making Esvele the victim of the swap as well: she ordered what she was told was a mind-reading draught to loosen a mark's tongue, and Skeemo sold her poison. The Wazoo lead at DC 18 then tells the party that the Viper left two similar merchants alive this season, so the poison is the wrong hand's work.

***The signet ring and the Rosznar link.*** House Rosznar appears in the source only through the DC 14 lead about a "Rosznar-adjacent trading company." The ring under the glove gives the party a second, independent way to name her, and the Wazoo leads give a third. Esvele's signature line about locks appears once, in the greeting, so it stays hers and doesn't become a gag.

***Rallygar's death is the price of not looking.*** Rallygar lives in every path but one, so a party that warns Esvele, swaps the vials or refuses has a visible, checkable reason to feel good about it. The +1 Renown for keeping him alive exists so that the careful path is worth something. A party that hands the coffer over blind earns the base award and a hard lesson, and the mission never scolds them for it.

## Davil and Skeemo

***Davil does not know.*** Davil's line "Skeemo operates semi-independently" is verbatim from the source, and the rewrite keeps him evasive. What changes is why. His discomfort comes from how little of Skeemo's trade he sees, and Insight DC 15 tells the party so. He learns of Skeemo's treachery in **Silencing Skeemo**, and nothing in this mission tells him or hints that he suspected it earlier. This conflicts with **The Doom Raiders** Factions Guide page, which says Davil has suspected Skeemo "for months" and that Tashlyn "confirmed it during his arrest"; the conflict is logged.

***Skeemo never names his masters.*** No character in the event names them, and Skeemo says nothing that points toward the other cell. The GM block states the truth flatly so the GM can run the scene, and the players learn it from Tashlyn in **Silencing Skeemo**, where the gating is handled by **Manshoon Named**.

***The paper bird.*** Skeemo's Notable Figures page says he messages both sides by paper bird, so the bird that watches the exchange is his own tool. Davil's explanation that Skeemo "likes to know a delivery went well" is the first of several innocent explanations the party can compare against later.

## Source Departures and Loose Ends

***What was restored.*** The mint-scented vials, the DC 20 Arcana identification, the 15 pp pouch, Cevin Rallygar's death in the Wazoo, the DC 14 and DC 18 leads, and Davil's "semi-independently" line all come from the source. The God Catcher now follows the setting entry, a statue sunk to the hip with a floating stone sphere above its raised palm, and not the "frozen mid-stride" figure of the older draft.

***What was retired.*** The old arrest paragraph is gone, because Davil's arrest follows **Gralhund Villa** and belongs to **Davil's Arrest**. The old Gaxly Rudderbust cross-faction note is gone as well, because no other Doom Raiders event uses him and his Wazoo threads belong to other factions. "Coffer Returned" was a section heading in the old draft with no reader. It is now an outcome, and **Silencing Skeemo** reads it.

***What is logged.*** The following are recorded in the Doom Raiders out-of-scope notes: the gate of Renown 2 in **The Dockside Killer**'s Next Steps (this event now requires Renown 3); the M4 draft's prose readings of Mission 2, which should switch to these outcome names; the Doom Raiders guide's "Davil has suspected Skeemo for months" line; and the **Cassalanter Villa** reader for Esvele's first meeting. The unprofiled minor characters are the Trades Ward herbalist and Cevin Rallygar, who is only ever described in the Wazoo notice. The hire-coach driver never speaks.
