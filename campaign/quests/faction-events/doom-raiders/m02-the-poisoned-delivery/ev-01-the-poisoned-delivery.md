# The Poisoned Delivery

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when Davil Starsong's flying snake finds the Doom Raiders members who finished **The Dockside Killer**, and it ends when they report the delivery to Davil at the Yawning Portal. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Collect a silk-lined coffer of four vials from Skeemo Weirdbottle and learn, by any of several routes, that the "potions of mind reading" inside are poison.
> - Hand the coffer to the lady in the purple cloak at the God Catcher, warn her about the contents, swap the vials for a harmless copy, or refuse the errand.
> - Trace a Sea Ward merchant's death back to the coffer if the poison reaches its target.
> - Confront Davil, who deflects, and bring him evidence for extra Renown.
>
> Davil briefs and debriefs only the Doom Raiders members. Companions can carry the coffer and stand beside the members at the statue, but they earn no Doom Raiders Renown.

> [!gamemaster]**What Nobody Tells the Party**
>
> Skeemo Weirdbottle has been feeding Manshoon's cell and House Gralhund for longer than the party has known the Doom Raiders. He filled the four vials with a slow poison flavored with mint, labeled them as potions of mind reading, and used Davil's request for a courier as his cover. His masters want two things from the errand. They want to see how Doom Raiders couriers behave when they are handed a package and told nothing about it, and they want a Sea Ward merchant to die with the Black Viper's name hanging over the death.
>
> Esvele Rosznar ordered the vials through the Black Network so she could dose the brandy of Cevin Rallygar, a merchant who has been ruining small carters and who is suing a Sea Ward trading company that House Rosznar holds shares in. She believes the draught will loosen his tongue about where he keeps his ledgers. She means to rob him, as she has robbed two similar men this season, and she never means to kill anyone.
>
> If the poison reaches her, she pours a vial into Rallygar's decanter on the night of the third day after the delivery. Nothing loosens his tongue, so she cracks his strongbox by hand and leaves before the poison takes hold, and Rallygar is dead before dawn. If the party stops the poison, she still robs him that night, and he lives.
>
> Davil knows none of this. He does not suspect Skeemo of treachery, and the truth reaches him only in **Silencing Skeemo**. His evasions in this Event come from embarrassment at how little of Skeemo's trade he sees, and they are not the cover for anything he knows.

### The Brief

At breakfast, a flying snake finds each Doom Raiders member, wherever they are lodging, with the same note in Davil's hand. Only members who meet the mission's requirements receive it, and companions who are not Doom Raiders receive nothing.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A small flying snake drops from the rafters and coils on the table beside your plate, holding a rolled slip of paper in its jaws. It lets go of the paper as soon as you reach for it and waits with its head raised while you smooth the note flat. The handwriting is light and looping, and it reads:
>
> > My friends, I have a small errand for you that should be pleasant work. There is a shop in the Trades Ward called Weirdbottle's Concoctions, and the gnome who keeps it is a friend of ours named Skeemo. He has made some potions of mind reading for a client, and they are ready to collect. Please carry them to the God Catcher in the Castle Ward by sunset and give them to the lady in the purple cloak who will be waiting in a hire-coach. Keep whatever she gives you for your trouble, and come and see me at the Yawning Portal when it is done, yes?

The members can go straight to the shop, or they can visit Davil first. Davil is in his curtained room on the second floor of the Yawning Portal until noon.

> [!social]**The Master of Opportunities**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: a charming cell leader who makes every errand sound like a favor and who believes this one is simple.
>
> Davil pours for each visitor before he says a word, and he turns a small trophy on his charm bracelet while he listens. He is warm, unhurried and honestly fond of Skeemo. He does not know the client's name, he has not opened the coffer, and he sees no reason to. The topics below are all he can tell the members before the errand.
>
> Conversation topics Davil is willing to discuss include:
> - The client, whose name he never asked for.
> - What the coffer holds, according to Skeemo.
> - The tip, which he leaves entirely to the lady's generosity.
> - Why he chose the party for the errand.
>
> Any character who makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that Davil is telling the truth about the coffer and that he wants the members to be known to the Network's better customers. If nobody succeeds, Davil's answers simply sound sincere, which they are.

> [!qna]**The client?**
>
> Davil tilts the pitcher over your cup and takes his time before he answers.
>
> > I couldn't tell you her name, my friends. Skeemo handles his own customers, and he keeps that part of his ledger to himself, as a good alchemist should. She's expecting the coffer at sunset, and you'll know her by the purple cloak.

> [!qna]**What's in the coffer?**
>
> > Four potions of mind reading, if Skeemo says so, and I've never had a reason to doubt him. He has been a friend of mine for many years, and his work has always been sound. I haven't opened it, and I don't mean to.

> [!qna]**What do we get out of it?**
>
> > Whatever the lady gives you is yours, and the Network takes no share. I'm told she pays generously, and I'd like the people we work with to see that our couriers are worth paying.

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> Davil smiles and turns the trophy on his bracelet once before he answers.
>
> > Skeemo asked for reliable people, and I thought of you straight away. It's an easy errand, and easy errands are a good way for a client and a courier to get used to each other. You'll do it, yes?

### Weirdbottle's Concoctions

The shop stands on a side street in the Trades Ward, and Skeemo has the coffer waiting on the counter whenever the members arrive. He knows the party by name and treats them with the same careful courtesy he shows a paying customer.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A signboard with a bubbling bottle hangs above a narrow shopfront, and the smell of mint and warm glass drifts into the street when the door opens. Every shelf inside is labeled and sorted by function with the labels turned outward, and a wooden step stool stands at the end of the back shelf in a spot that looks measured. A gnome in a leather apron with a great many pockets waits behind the counter, and a small silk-lined coffer with a wax seal sits in front of him. A faint scent of mint rises from the seam of its lid.
>
> > Welcome to Weirdbottle's, madam, sir. Please don't touch the blue flasks. Strictly speaking, please don't touch any of them. You'll be Mr. Starsong's couriers, I take it, and I'm glad you're punctual, because the customer is not a patient woman.

> [!social]**The Precise Alchemist**
>
> Skeemo Weirdbottle (Lawful Evil, Rock Gnome, he/him) :: a tidy alchemist whose pleasantness is exact, complete and entirely without warmth.
>
> Skeemo adjusts his spectacles before every answer and names the price of anything you mention down to the copper. He says every friendly thing a friend would say, and the sympathy arrives a beat late. He is watching the party rather than the coffer, because his masters want to know whether these couriers look inside, ask questions or simply obey. He answers questions about the contents with the same handful of deflections and will not be pushed past them.
>
> Conversation topics Skeemo is willing to discuss include:
> - The coffer, which is paid for and should be carried in the hands.
> - The customer, whose privacy his ledger protects.
> - Davil, whom he calls Mr. Starsong and admires in a sentence or two.
> - The price of anything else in the shop.
>
> Any character who watches Skeemo while the party examines the coffer and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that he never once looks at the coffer and keeps his eyes on the party's faces and hands. This proves nothing by itself, and it is the first thing Davil will say has an innocent explanation.

> [!qna]**The coffer?**
>
> Skeemo sets his fingertips on the lid and slides it an inch toward you.
>
> > Four potions of mind reading, sealed and paid for. They're stable, though I wouldn't call them rugged, so I'd ask you to carry the coffer in your hands and not in a pack. The lady expects it at sunset, and she dislikes waiting.

> [!qna]**Can we open it?**
>
> > You may, of course, sir, but the wax is part of what the customer paid for, and a broken seal makes her ask questions I'd rather not answer. Strictly speaking, I'd prefer that you didn't. If you do, I'll reseal it for two silver, and I'll ask you why.

> [!qna]**Who is the customer?**
>
> > A client, madam, and clients are entitled to their privacy. I keep an exact ledger of everything I sell, down to the copper, and I have never once shown it to a courier. She'll find you at the statue, and I'm sure you'll manage very well.

> [!qna]**Why did he pick us?**
>
> > Mr. Starsong said he wanted reliable people, and I said I'd be glad to meet them. He is a hard man to refuse, isn't he? Always so pleasant about it.

> [!exploration]**Looking Over the Coffer**
>
> The coffer holds four stoppered vials of clear liquid in silk-lined slots, under a wax seal stamped with Skeemo's mark and a label reading *Potions of Mind Reading (4)*. Opening the lid cracks the seal. The vials look, smell and taste like mild mint, and any of the following leads a character to the truth, which is that the liquid is poison:
>
> - Any character who examines an open vial and makes a successful DC 20 Intelligence (Arcana) check identifies the liquid as a poison. A character who casts *Detect Poison and Disease* on the coffer learns the same thing automatically.
> - Any character who puts a drop on the tongue tastes only mint and makes a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the character's lips and tongue go numb within a minute and the character is Poisoned for 1 hour, and the numbness gives the poison away. On a success nothing happens, and nothing is learned.
> - Any character who takes a vial to a Trades Ward herbalist before 17:00 and pays 5 gp learns the truth in the time it takes her to sniff it. See **A Second Opinion** below.
>
> Two lesser clues point the same direction without proving anything. One is Skeemo's watchfulness, described in his profile above. The other belongs to any character who looks over the shelves and then the coffer and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check, and who notices that every bottle in the shop carries a batch number in Skeemo's hand while the four vials carry none.
>
> If nobody in the party learns the truth in the shop, the Wazoo notice described below gives them a second chance to work it out.

> [!qna]**These are poison.**
>
> If a character says so to his face, Skeemo looks at the open vial for a moment before he looks up.
>
> > Poison, sir? That would be a serious matter, and I'd want to be certain of it before anyone said the word to Mr. Starsong. I buy from three suppliers, and a mislabeled vial is not unheard of, though it has never happened in this shop. How dreadful. Leave the coffer with me and I'll trace the batch tonight, and I'll send a corrected set to the customer myself in the morning.

If the party accuses Skeemo and then keeps the coffer, he says only that it is theirs to carry and that he would not stand between a courier and her instructions. He does not change his story, he does not follow them, and he does not fight. Mark **Skeemo Alerted** whenever a member tells him to his face that the vials are poison.

> [!hazard]**Steel in the Shop**
>
> If the party draws weapons on Skeemo, he does not fight back. He uses the **Mage** stat block from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, which is far beyond a 3rd-level party, and he has no wish to be seen casting spells in the Trades Ward.
>
> #### Skeemo's Tactics
> At the first drawn weapon, Skeemo shatters a vial of thick grey smoke at his feet, which fills a 20-foot radius around the counter and leaves it Heavily Obscured for 1 minute.
>
> During the scuffle, Skeemo:
> - Keeps the counter between himself and the party.
> - Leaves through the rear door into the alley, and goes to the Yawning Portal to tell Davil that his couriers attacked him.
> - Never casts an attack spell in this scene.
>
> The scene ends when Skeemo leaves the shop, or when the party puts their weapons away. Mark **Skeemo Alerted**. Davil's greeting at the debrief is cold and courteous, and he asks for proof before he will hear a word against Skeemo. The party can avoid the fight entirely by taking the coffer and leaving, or by making their accusation in words.

### Between the Shop and the Statue

The walk from the Trades Ward to the Castle Ward takes about an hour, and the party has until 18:00. Three things can happen along the way, and none of them needs to happen for the mission to finish.

> [!exploration]**A Second Opinion**
>
> Any Trades Ward herbalist's stall will look at a vial for 5 gp until 17:00. The nearest one belongs to a stout woman with green-stained fingers who sniffs first and asks questions afterward. Opening a vial cracks the coffer's seal, and the party can reseal it with the Sleight of Hand check in **Making a Copy** below. If the party asks her nothing, she says nothing. If the party names Skeemo, she shrugs and tells them she wouldn't go back to that shop for a refund. She has nothing else to say and no wish to see the Watch.

When a member hands her a vial, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The herbalist pulls the stopper from the vial you hand her, sniffs once, and wrinkles her nose before she holds it out at arm's length.
>
> > That's mint laid over something bitter, and it isn't a cough remedy. Whoever made this wanted it to go down easily and to stop a heart by morning. Where did you get it?

> [!exploration]**Making a Copy**
>
> A party that wants to keep the poison and still deliver something needs four vials of harmless mint cordial, which costs 1 gp at any herb stall in the Trades Ward or the Castle Ward, and a way to open and close the seal without a crack. Any character who warms a knife over a flame and makes a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check lifts the wax, swaps the vials and sets the seal back down intact. A character who has proficiency with Thieves' Tools has Advantage on the check.
>
> On a failure, the seal cracks. The exchange still goes ahead at the God Catcher, but Esvele notices the crack, and the only explanation that works is the truth. See **The Warning** below. The party keeps the four real vials in either case.

> [!exploration]**The Bird on the Gutter**
>
> Any character who watches the rooftops as the party enters the Castle Ward and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check spots a small folded paper bird perched on a gutter, turning its head to follow the coffer. It follows the party to the statue and stays for the exchange. A character who follows it afterward sees it fly all the way back to the rear window of Weirdbottle's Concoctions.
>
> If the party shows Davil the bird, he says that Skeemo often sends paper messages and likes to know a delivery went well. That is plausible, and it is the first thing about Skeemo's habits the party can compare with what they learn later.

### The God Catcher

The hire-coach is in place by 17:45 and waits until 19:00. Esvele Rosznar rides in the back, and the party sees her only as a hooded figure in a purple cloak. The coach's driver says nothing to anyone.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The God Catcher looms over the street with its left leg sunk to the hip in the paving and one hand and one foot pressed against the stones, as if it were still trying to climb out. Its right arm reaches for the sky, and above the open palm a sphere of stone floats with a small carved door in its side. Washing lines, balconies and stairways crowd the statue's chest and shoulders where tenants have built their homes into the rock, and pale streaks of bird droppings run down from its eyes like tears. A hire-coach with the curtains half drawn waits in the shadow of the raised arm, and a black-gloved hand pushes the door open as you approach.

> [!social]**The Lady in the Purple Cloak**
>
> Esvele Rosznar, the Black Viper (Chaotic Neutral, Human, she/her) :: a noblewoman turned cat burglar who is in a hurry, amused, and careful to reveal nothing.
>
> Esvele keeps the hood of a purple cloak drawn low and does not offer her name. She talks as the Viper does, in low, quick, teasing sentences, and she compliments locks and insults their owners. She has a black velvet pouch of 15 platinum pieces (150 gp) ready in her lap before the party has stopped walking. She expects the exchange to take under a minute, and she leaves at once when it is done.
>
> Conversation topics Esvele is willing to discuss include:
> - The coffer, which she wants in her hands.
> - The pouch, which is the party's if the coffer changes hands.
> - Her business, which she will call none of the party's concern.
>
> Any character who watches her hand while she counts out the pouch and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (History) check sees the edge of a signet ring turned face-inward on her glove, carved with the crest of House Rosznar. If nobody sees it, the DC 14 lead in **The Wazoo Notice** points at the same house.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A gloved hand holds out a black velvet pouch through the coach door before you have fully stopped, and a low, amused voice comes from the shadow of the hood.
>
> > Right on time. I do like a courier who can read a clock. That's a lovely lock on the coffer, though I wouldn't trust a gnome's wax against anyone patient. Hand it over, and I'll hand you this, and then we'll both forget which statue we stood under.

> [!qna]**Who are you?**
>
> > A customer, and a busy one. If the gnome had wanted you to know my name, he'd have written it on the coffer. There's fifteen platinum in the pouch, which is a great deal more than a courier's fee, so I'd take it and be glad.

> [!qna]**What is it for?**
>
> > There's a merchant in the Sea Ward who has been ruining people I like, and I mean to have a conversation with him about his ledgers. Beyond that it's no concern of a courier's, and I'd rather it stayed that way.

The exchange resolves in one of three ways, depending on what the party has learned and decided. A fourth way, refusal, happens before the party reaches the statue.

> [!gamemaster]**The Ways the Exchange Can Go**
>
> - **Hand it over.** The party gives her the real vials, whether they know what they are or not. Mark **Poison Delivered**. Esvele takes the coffer, checks that the seal is whole, drops the pouch into the nearest hand and closes the door. The coach leaves for her estate in the Sea Ward. The party keeps the 15 pp, and a member who delivered knowing the truth earns no bonus and no penalty.
> - **Warn her.** A member tells Esvele the vials are poison. She accepts any proof at once: an identified vial, the numb tongue of a poisoned member, or the herbalist's word. Without proof, she listens to a member who makes a successful DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check, and on a failure she takes the coffer, pays, and leaves. Mark **Esvele Warned** in every case, and mark **Poison Delivered** as well if she took the vials.
> - **Swap them.** The party gives her the mint cordial in a whole seal, and she never learns anything. Mark **Poison Swapped**. She pays, and the party keeps the 15 pp and the four real vials.
> - **Refuse.** The party does not come to the statue, or comes and leaves with the coffer. Mark **Delivery Refused**. Esvele waits until 19:00, leaves, and sends a curt note to Weirdbottle's asking why her order failed. The party has no pouch and no penalty.

If the party warns her, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gloved hand closes on the pouch and draws it back into the coach. When the voice comes again it is flat and level, and the teasing has gone out of it.
>
> > Say that again, slowly, and show me what you're basing it on. If you're telling me the truth, I'll want to sit with it for a moment. If you're lying, I'd like to know why you'd bother.

If Esvele accepts the warning, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A gloved finger presses the stopper back into the vial, and Esvele takes a long breath through her nose before she speaks.
>
> > That fucking gnome sold me poison. Four vials, and every one of them meant for a man's brandy. You could have taken my money and gone, and you didn't, so I'll remember your faces. Keep the coffer and the evidence, and tell your people what that gnome has been selling.
>
> The coach door closes before you can answer, and the driver turns the horses toward the Sea Ward.

If the party delivers the coffer without warning her, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Esvele turns the coffer over once in her gloved hands and checks the seal, and then she drops the pouch into the nearest palm and closes your fingers around it.
>
> > Splendid. Tell the gnome I'm well pleased, and that I'll have news for him in a few days.
>
> The coach door shuts, the driver flicks the reins, and the wheels rattle away across the paving toward the Sea Ward.

If the members swapped the vials but the seal cracked, Esvele notices it before she pays:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Esvele runs a gloved thumb along the cracked wax and then tilts her hood toward you.
>
> > This seal has been opened. I'd like to hear why before I hand you anything.

The only explanation that works is the truth. A member who tells her turns the exchange into the warning above, and the party already holds the real vials as proof, so she accepts the warning at once.

> [!hazard]**If the Party Grabs the Coach**
>
> Esvele uses the stat block on her Notable Figures page, and she wants no fight with anyone. She knows the Castle Ward's alleys and doesn't linger.
>
> #### Esvele's Tactics
> At the first hand on the coach, Esvele calls out to her driver, opens the far door and drops into the crowd.
>
> During the scuffle, Esvele:
> - Uses the Disengage action to break free of anyone who grapples her.
> - Runs for the nearest alley and climbs out of reach.
> - Never attacks, and never returns to the coach.
>
> The scene ends when Esvele slips out of sight or the coach pulls away. The driver takes the pouch with him. The party keeps the coffer and forfeits the tip. Mark **Delivery Refused**. Esvele treats the party as hostile in **Cassalanter Villa**, where she is a cold obstacle and not a possible ally. The party can avoid all of this by handing over, warning or refusing.

### The Wazoo Notice

If the poison reached Rallygar, a crier at the corner of the Market waves a fresh sheet of the *Waterdeep Wazoo* on the morning of the fourth day. If **Esvele Warned**, **Poison Swapped** or **Delivery Refused** was marked, Rallygar lives, and the Wazoo carries only a short item about a strongbox forced in the Sea Ward. In that case, skip to **Davil's Answer**.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A crier at the corner of the Market shouts a headline over the noise of the stalls and waves a fresh sheet of the Waterdeep Wazoo in the air. The notice underneath is short, and you read it over a stranger's shoulder:
>
> > Cevin Rallygar, merchant of the Sea Ward, was found dead in his study this morning with his strongbox forced open and a brandy decanter at his elbow. His servants report a smell of mint on his lips. The Watch names no suspect and calls the death a poisoning. Mr. Rallygar had long been at law with a Sea Ward trading house, and leaves no heir.

> [!exploration]**Tracing Rallygar**
>
> The notice alone tells any character who smelled the coffer that the smell of mint on Rallygar's lips matches the vials. That is enough to raise the suspicion, and three more leads confirm it:
>
> - Any character who spends the day asking around the Sea Ward docks and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check learns that Rallygar was suing a trading house in which House Rosznar holds shares.
> - Any character who asks around the same docks and makes a successful DC 18 Intelligence (Investigation) check learns that Rallygar was the kind of merchant the Black Viper robs, and that she robbed two like him this season and left both alive. This death is the first to involve poison, which suggests the poisoner was someone other than the person who forced his strongbox.
> - Any character who shows the notice to the herbalist, or who has already identified the vials, recognizes without a check that the Wazoo describes the same poison they were carrying.
>
> If nobody can make either check, the party still has the notice and the mint, and Davil is happy to help them put it together.

### Davil's Answer

The members report to Davil at the Yawning Portal whenever they are ready, and he receives them at 20:00 on any evening after the exchange. A party that reports on the night of the delivery reaches him before the Wazoo carries any news, and it can return with the notice if Rallygar dies. Davil wants to hear whether the lady was satisfied.

If the party made a report to the Watch instead of Davil, the desk sergeant writes a statement and does nothing. The Doom Raiders hear about it by the following morning, and Davil greets the party with a cool, polite remark about how much he would have liked to hear it from them first. The debrief and the Renown otherwise proceed as normal.

If the party knows nothing beyond the delivery, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil looks up from a hand of cards he was dealing himself and gestures toward the empty chairs around his table before he pours a cup at each place.
>
> > Ah, there you are. Sit, please, and tell me how it went. Was the lady satisfied? I hope the tip was worth the walk.

If the party tells Davil about the poison, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil sets down his cards while you talk, and the small trophy on his bracelet stops turning. When you have finished he pours himself a cup he doesn't drink.
>
> > That's a serious thing you're telling me, my friends, and I'm sorry you were put in the middle of it. Skeemo operates semi-independently, you understand, and I don't see every transaction that passes through his shop. I regret that you were involved in something without full information. I'll speak with him myself this evening, and I'd ask you to leave the matter with me.

> [!qna]**Did you know?**
>
> Davil turns the trophy on his bracelet once, slowly, before he answers.
>
> > Would it change what you did if I said I had? I've been in this business a long time, my friends, and I've learned not to answer that sort of question in a room with the door open.

> [!qna]**Why would Skeemo do this?**
>
> > I honestly can't say. He's a careful man, and careful men make mistakes about suppliers. Let me ask him before we decide it was anything else.

> [!qna]**What do we do about the client?**
>
> > Nothing, for now. If she was warned, she'll keep her own counsel, and if she wasn't, the Watch will find its own way to whatever it finds. I'd like you to stay out of the Sea Ward for a tenday. Yes?

> [!exploration]**Reading Davil**
>
> Any character who makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check while Davil answers determines that he is not hiding any knowledge of the poison. What he is hiding is his discomfort that Skeemo's shop runs largely out of his sight and that a job under his name has cost someone's life. Davil does not suspect Skeemo of treachery at this point, and he does not learn the truth until **Silencing Skeemo**.
>
> If nobody makes the check, Davil simply sounds like a man who has been caught out and dislikes it.

That evening, Davil speaks to Skeemo. Skeemo blames an unnamed supplier, offers to replace the batch, and Davil accepts the explanation because he wants to. Nothing changes on the surface, and no member is present.

### Renown Opportunities

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Doom Raiders member who takes part in the delivery and reports to Davil gains 2 base Renown. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:
>
> - **+1 Renown:** Rallygar lives. The party warned Esvele, swapped the vials, or refused before the poison reached her.
> - **+1 Renown:** the party gives Davil real evidence of the poisoning. That means an intact vial, or, if the poison reached Rallygar, both Wazoo leads (the DC 14 and DC 18 findings) together with the notice.
>
> Companions who are not Doom Raiders gain no membership or Renown from this assignment, and each bonus can be earned only once. A member who delivered the poison knowingly and said nothing to Davil earns only the base award.

### Aftermath

The party keeps whatever Esvele paid. The 15 pp are theirs if they handed over or swapped, and the warning leaves them without the pouch. Davil records the mission as complete once the members have reported, and he says no more about the coffer.

What the members hold and say here shapes **Silencing Skeemo**, where Tashlyn Yafeera reads their choices from the outcomes below. Esvele Rosznar remembers the party's faces however the exchange went. A party that warned her carries a warmer first meeting into **Cassalanter Villa**, where she runs a parallel heist, and a party whose delivery killed Rallygar meets a cold and wary Viper who does not yet know who to blame.

### Concluding the Event

The report to Davil concludes the mission. Each outcome below is recorded separately, because a party can warn Esvele and still leave her with the poison.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Poison Delivered** — mark when Esvele leaves with the real vials, whether the party knew what they were or not; Rallygar dies on the third night. Read by **Silencing Skeemo**, where Tashlyn treats the death as Skeemo's work, and by **Cassalanter Villa**, where Esvele meets the party coldly.

## Overview

A flying snake sends the party to a Trades Ward alchemist for a small coffer, which they are to carry to a hooded lady near the God Catcher. The coffer is not what its label says.

## Summary

We collected a silk-lined coffer from Skeemo Weirdbottle and carried it to a hooded lady at the God Catcher, and afterward we reported to Davil at the Yawning Portal. What was really in the vials, and why Skeemo packed them, still troubles us.
