# The Poisoned Delivery

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event begins when the party arrives at Weirdbottle's Concoctions in the Trades Ward to pick up the package. In this Event:
>
> - Skeemo presents the coffer without explanation and answers no questions about the contents.
> - The coffer can be detected as mislabeled through investigation — the smell is wrong for mind-reading compounds.
> - Four resolution paths exist at the God Catcher: deliver as-is, warn Esvele, substitute the contents, or refuse.
> - Esvele's response shapes whether she becomes a future contact or simply departs.
> - Davil's arrest fires at the end of the event regardless of how the delivery went.

**Background (DM only)**

Skeemo has been a Manshoon Splinter double agent since before the party was recruited. He was bought with access to rare magical components and protection from the Watch's periodic shop inspections. He knows the party is coming. He prepared the mislabeled coffer deliberately, not from carelessness.

This mission's outcome goes into his intelligence report: the party's willingness to carry a package they were not permitted to inspect tells Manshoon exactly how much the Doom Raiders trust their own couriers, and whether this particular cell is cautious enough to be dangerous.

Davil does not know the contents of the coffer. He arranged a pickup, assumed it was legitimate, and moved on. He will not find out the truth until Mission 4.

### Weirdbottle's Concoctions

The shop in the Trades Ward is organized with a gnome's specific logic: labels facing forward, bottles grouped by function, the back shelves accessible only from a step stool that is always in exactly the same place. Everything is clean. The smell of alchemical compounds is layered and precise, nothing bleeding into anything else.

**Skeemo Weirdbottle** is behind the counter when the party arrives: small, meticulous, wearing a leather apron with more pockets than seems necessary. He knows the party by name. The coffer comes from under the counter and goes onto the surface with a quiet click.

> [!npc-narrative]**Skeemo Weirdbottle**
> A rock gnome alchemist, small and meticulous, wearing a leather apron with more pockets than seems necessary. His pleasantness is technically indistinguishable from warmth.

> > "It's paid for. Handle it carefully."

He does not answer questions about the contents.

> [!dialogue]**What's in the coffer?**
> "It's not your business, and if it were, I'd have told you."

He is pleasant in the way of someone who has decided that pleasantness is the most efficient surface to present. His attention, when the party turns to examine the coffer, never fully leaves them.

**Examining the coffer:**

The coffer is sealed with a wax stamp. A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check notices the lid is slightly warmer than it should be for a Trades Ward shop in this weather. The potions inside carry a faint alchemical smell that the label does not account for.

Uncorking one of the bottles — which breaks the seal — requires a **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check to detect the smell of oil of taggit, a contact poison, beneath the sweet aromatic cover used to mask it.

A broken seal changes the God Catcher exchange: Esvele notices immediately that the wax has been disturbed and waits for an explanation before the transaction goes further.

### The God Catcher

The God Catcher is one of the Walking Statues of Waterdeep — a vast stone figure with one hand outstretched over a Castle Ward intersection, frozen mid-stride since the Century of Skyfire. The hire-coach is waiting in its shadow at the arranged time.

**Esvele Rosznar** is a Tethyrian noblewoman in her late twenties, seated inside the coach with the hood of a purple cloak drawn up. She does not offer her name. She produces a black velvet pouch with fifteen platinum pieces before the party has fully stopped.

> [!npc-narrative]**The Hooded Noblewoman**
> A Tethyrian noblewoman in her late twenties, seated in the coach with the hood of a purple cloak drawn up. She produces a black velvet pouch with fifteen platinum pieces before the party has fully stopped. She does not offer her name.

She wants the coffer. The exchange is meant to take thirty seconds.

**Deliver as-is:** The exchange completes. The coach departs for the Sea Ward. A Watch broadsheet two days later carries an item about a Sea Ward noble family found incapacitated after a dinner party — no suspect, no apparent motive. A **DC 13 Intelligence** check or careful reading of the broadsheet connects the timing to the coffer's delivery.

**Warn Esvele:** She tests the coffer herself — her Arcana training is sufficient — and confirms the poison. She is not frightened. She is cold and precise. The velvet pouch goes back in her pocket. She instructs her driver to leave without explanation. She notes the party's faces with care. They will see her again.

**Substitute the contents:** If the party has access to genuine potions of mind reading or convincing fakes, the swap requires a **DC 14 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check to replace the contents without destroying the wax seal, and a **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana)** check to reseal it plausibly. Esvele inspects the coffer before paying; on success, the exchange completes cleanly; on failure, she notices the seal is wrong and requires an explanation before any money changes hands.

**Refuse to deliver:** The mission fails. The party returns the coffer to Davil, who was not expecting it back. He opens it. He goes quiet for a moment, then files it in the category of things he intends to examine more carefully. He does not accuse Skeemo today.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### The Coffer Comes Back
> If the party returns the coffer to Davil, his response is measured but tells a careful observer something important: he is not surprised that a delivery went wrong, only that the wrongness is this specific. He does not say Skeemo's name. He does not say anything more about it. He is adding this to a count he has been keeping for months.

### After the Delivery

Davil's arrest follows two days after **Gralhund Villa**, not after this mission. See the **Davil's Arrest** Event (s01) for that scene — it fires for Doom Raiders members at the start of **Faction Outposts**.

## Overview

Pick up a package from a Doom Raiders alchemist in the Trades Ward and deliver it to a contact near the God Catcher. The delivery is not what it appears to be.

## Read Aloud

> [!narrative]
> The coffer is small, silk-lined, sealed with Skeemo's wax. The label reads *Potions of Mind Reading (4)* in a hand so precise it looks printed. The seal is unbroken. The weight is right.
>
> Something is wrong with the smell.

## Summary

The party retrieved a package from Skeemo Weirdbottle and delivered it — or attempted to — to a contact near the God Catcher. The package contained poison, not the mind-reading potions on the label.
