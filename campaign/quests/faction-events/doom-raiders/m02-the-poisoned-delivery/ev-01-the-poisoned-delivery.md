# The Poisoned Delivery

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when a flying snake delivers Davil's note to Trollskull Manor. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Davil by flying snake and read his message
> - Collect the coffer from Skeemo Weirdbottle's shop in the Trades Ward
> - Detect that the coffer's contents are mislabeled through investigation
> - Meet Esvele Rosznar near the God Catcher and choose from four resolution paths
> - Return the coffer to Davil if they refuse the delivery

> [!gamemaster]**Skeemo's Situation**
>
> Skeemo has been a Manshoon Splinter double agent since before the party was recruited. He was bought with access to rare magical components and protection from the Watch's periodic shop inspections. He knows the party is coming. He prepared the mislabeled coffer deliberately, not from carelessness.
>
> This mission's outcome goes into his intelligence report: the party's willingness to carry a package they were not permitted to inspect tells Manshoon exactly how much the Doom Raiders trust their own couriers, and whether this particular cell is cautious enough to be dangerous.
>
> Davil does not know the contents of the coffer. He arranged a pickup, assumed it was legitimate, and moved on. He will not find out the truth until Mission 4.

### The Brief

A flying snake from Davil arrives at Trollskull Manor and waits on a windowsill or rafter until someone notices it.

> [!readaloud]
> A small flying snake is waiting on the windowsill when someone notices it, wings folded flat against its sides and a folded note tied neatly to its neck.
>
> The note, in Davil's unhurried hand, reads:
>
> > *A favour, if you have the time. Collect a package from Weirdbottle's Concoctions in the Trades Ward — ask for Skeemo — and deliver it to a contact waiting near the God Catcher in the Castle Ward at the second bell of afternoon. Keep what she pays you. — D.*

There is no one present to ask questions. Proceed to **Weirdbottle's Concoctions**.

### Weirdbottle's Concoctions

The shop in the Trades Ward is organized with a gnome's specific logic: labels facing forward, bottles grouped by function, the back shelves accessible only from a step stool that is always in exactly the same place.

> [!readaloud]
> Everything in Weirdbottle's Concoctions is where it belongs. Labels face outward, bottles are grouped by purpose, and the smell of a dozen different alchemical compounds fills the room without any of them bleeding into the others. A rock gnome in a leather apron with more pockets than seems strictly necessary is behind the counter, and he sees you before the door is fully open.
>
> > "You're here for the coffer. One moment."
>
> He steps away and comes back with a small silk-lined box, which he places on the counter with a quiet click. The label reads *Potions of Mind Reading (4)* in a hand so precise it looks printed.
>
> > "It's paid for. Handle it carefully."

> [!social]**The Precise Alchemist**
>
> Skeemo Weirdbottle (Lawful Evil, Rock Gnome, he/him) :: a tidy alchemist whose politeness has no warmth in it.
>
> Skeemo's pleasantness is technically indistinguishable from warmth. He greets the party by name, which he knew in advance, and answers questions with deflections that are technically reasonable. His attention, when the party turns to examine the coffer, never fully leaves them. Every response and hesitation will go into his report. He will not confirm or deny any specifics about the coffer's contents.
>
> Conversation topics Skeemo is willing to discuss include:
> - The pickup arrangements (already handled, nothing more to say)
> - His shop's inventory (happy to sell from open stock)
>
> Any character who watches Skeemo during the interaction and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check senses that he is observing them rather than serving them and that his answers are constructed to reveal nothing.
>
> Specific dialogue for Skeemo on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**What's in the coffer?**
>
> He adjusts his spectacles and looks up.
>
> > "It's not your business, and if it were, I'd have told you. Strictly speaking, your part is to deliver it."

> [!qna]**Who's the contact?**
>
> > "I wasn't told a name, and I didn't ask." He sets a hand lightly on the counter. "That's the arrangement, I believe."

> [!exploration]**Examining the Coffer**
>
> Any character who examines the sealed coffer and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check notices the lid is slightly warmer than it should be for a Trades Ward shop in this weather, and the coffer carries a faint alchemical smell that the label does not account for.
>
> > The wax seal is intact and the label is in order, but the coffer is warmer than the room, and something underneath the sweet aromatic scent used to mask the smell does not belong with any mind-reading compound.
>
> Uncorking one of the bottles — which breaks the wax seal — requires a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check to detect the smell of oil of taggit, a contact poison, beneath the sweet aromatic cover. A broken seal changes the God Catcher exchange: Esvele notices immediately that the wax has been disturbed and waits for an explanation before any money changes hands.

### The God Catcher

The God Catcher is one of the Walking Statues of Waterdeep — a vast stone figure with one hand outstretched over a Castle Ward intersection, frozen mid-stride since the Century of Skyfire. A hire-coach is waiting in its shadow at the second bell of afternoon.

> [!readaloud]
> The God Catcher's outstretched hand throws a long shadow across the intersection, and a hire-coach is waiting in it, the driver's eyes forward and the curtains drawn. Before you have fully stopped, the curtain opens a few inches and a black velvet pouch comes through the gap, followed by a woman's gloved hand held out for the coffer.

> [!social]**The Hooded Noblewoman**
>
> Esvele Rosznar (Chaotic Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: a Tethyrian noblewoman in her late twenties; she does not offer her name.
>
> Esvele has the controlled patience of someone for whom this transaction is one of several today. She produces payment before the party has fully stopped because she has done this before and time is money. She does not explain what she wants the potions for, and she does not expect to be asked. If the wax seal has been broken, she notices immediately — she has a trained eye for tampered goods — and her manner goes cold and exact while she decides how to proceed.
>
> Esvele is the Black Viper, a skilled thief who robs from Waterdeep's wealthy. The party does not learn this today unless they already know it from another source.
>
> Conversation topics Esvele is willing to discuss include:
> - The exchange itself (hand over the coffer, take the coin, done)
> - The broken seal, if it has been disturbed (she wants an explanation before she decides anything)
>
> Any character who studies Esvele during the exchange and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check senses that she is evaluating the party as carefully as she is evaluating the goods, and that she is deciding something about them.

The party has four options at the coach.

**Deliver as-is:** Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> The velvet pouch drops into your hand as the coffer passes through the curtain. The curtains close, and the coach pulls away into the afternoon traffic without any hurry.

A Watch broadsheet two days later carries an item about a Sea Ward noble family found incapacitated after a dinner party — no suspect, no apparent motive. Any character who makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence check or reads the broadsheet carefully can connect the timing to the coffer's delivery.

**Warn Esvele:** If the party tells Esvele the coffer contains poison rather than what the label claims, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> She holds the coffer in both hands for a moment, and then tests it herself — her fingers move once across the wax, and she goes still. She does not look frightened. She looks at each of you in turn, and the velvet pouch goes back inside the curtain.
>
> > "I'll remember your faces."
>
> The curtains close, and the coach moves on.

> [!gamemaster]**Esvele's Response**
>
> Esvele is not grateful. She is cold and precise, and she notes the party's faces with care. Her Arcana training confirms the poison. She will not explain where she was going to use the potions or who the target was. She does not say thank you. She says she will remember the party's faces, and she means it. They will see her again in **Cassalanter Villa**, where her parallel heist creates a different kind of intersection.

**Substitute the contents:** If the party has access to genuine potions of mind reading or convincing fakes and attempts a substitution before the exchange, read or paraphrase the following if successful:

> [!readaloud]
> The velvet pouch drops into your hand as the coffer passes through the curtain. Esvele holds it for a moment, inspects the seal, and then the curtains close and the coach departs without incident.

Replacing the contents requires a successful DC 14 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to swap the potions without destroying the wax seal, and a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check to reseal it convincingly. If either check fails, Esvele notices the seal is wrong and requires an explanation before any money changes hands.

**Refuse to deliver:** If the party returns the coffer to Davil rather than completing the exchange, read or paraphrase the following when they bring it back to the Yawning Portal:

> [!readaloud]
> Davil takes the coffer from your hands and sets it on the table, and the expression that crosses his face is not surprise. He opens it. He reads the label. He closes it again.
>
> He is quiet for a long moment.
>
> > "Thank you for bringing this back."

> [!gamemaster]**The Coffer Comes Back**
>
> Davil's response is measured but tells a careful observer something important: he is not surprised that a delivery went wrong, only that the wrongness is this specific. He does not say Skeemo's name. He does not say anything more about it. He is adding this to a count he has been keeping for months.

### Concluding the Event

Davil's arrest fires two days after **Gralhund Villa**, not after this mission. See the **Davil's Arrest** Event for that scene.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Delivery Completed** — mark when the party hands the coffer to Esvele by any means: as-is, after warning her, or with substituted contents; award +1 Renown. Read by **Silencing Skeemo** (Mission 4) — Skeemo's intelligence report records the result.
> - **Esvele Warned** — mark when the party warned Esvele or substituted the contents; award an additional +1 Renown. Read by **Cassalanter Villa** (Esvele's parallel heist starts warmer toward the party).
> - **Coffer Returned** — mark when the party refused the delivery and returned the coffer to Davil. Read by **Silencing Skeemo** (Mission 4) — Davil's count is one item older.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> If the party warned Esvele or refused the delivery and told Davil before his arrest, he thanks them quietly once, before the Watch comes, and says that whoever packed those potions did it with access to the Network's supplier chain. He does not say Skeemo's name. He has not confirmed it yet.
>
> Esvele Rosznar, warned or not deceived, remembers the party's faces. She is the Black Viper, and the party may encounter her again in **Cassalanter Villa**, where her parallel heist creates its own intersection point.
>
> **Doom Raiders Mission 3 — The Missing Snobeedle** becomes available when the party reaches Renown 3 and character level 4.

## Overview

Pick up a package from a Doom Raiders alchemist in the Trades Ward and deliver it to a contact near the God Catcher. The delivery is not what it appears to be.

## Summary

The party retrieved a package from Skeemo Weirdbottle and delivered it — or attempted to — to a contact near the God Catcher. The package contained poison, not the mind-reading potions on the label.
