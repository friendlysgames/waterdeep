# The Poisoned Delivery: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Available to a Doom Raiders member at Renown 3 and 3rd level after **The Dockside Killer**, with assisting companions welcome during the errand.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> There is no scripted fight. Both hazards in the event are fights the party starts, and each has a non-combat exit. Skeemo and Esvele are far too dangerous to fight at this level, so both run away.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Doom Raiders members who complete the assignment.

## Hook

A small flying snake finds each eligible Doom Raiders member at breakfast, carrying a note in Davil's hand. Skeemo Weirdbottle has made some potions of mind reading for a client, and the members are to collect them from his Trades Ward shop and deliver them to a lady in a purple cloak beside the God Catcher by sunset. Whatever she gives them is theirs.

## Background

Davil believes the errand is routine. He asked Skeemo for a customer's order, asked for reliable couriers, and never looked inside the coffer, because he has trusted Skeemo for years.

> [!gamemaster]**What Skeemo Did**
>
> Skeemo Weirdbottle is a traitor who has fed Manshoon's cell and House Gralhund since before the party was recruited. He replaced the customer's order with four vials of mint-flavored poison and used Davil's courier request as his cover. His masters wanted to see whether Doom Raiders couriers deliver a package they were told not to question, and they wanted a merchant's death pinned on the Black Viper.
>
> Esvele Rosznar, the Black Viper, ordered the vials to dose the brandy of Cevin Rallygar, a Sea Ward merchant she intended to rob. She believes the draught will loosen his tongue and has no idea it is poison. If it reaches her, Rallygar dies before dawn on the fourth day and the Waterdeep Wazoo runs a notice.

Davil does not know any of this, and the party has no reason to suspect Skeemo of betrayal at this point.

## The Brief

The flying snake delivers Davil's note to each member. The members can go straight to the shop, or they can visit Davil at the Yawning Portal first. He tells them what he knows, which is very little, and he does not suspect anything is wrong with the coffer.

## Weirdbottle's Concoctions

Skeemo hands over a silk-lined coffer sealed with wax and labeled as potions of mind reading. The vials smell, look and taste of mint. Any of four independent routes reveals the truth (an Arcana check or *Detect Poison and Disease*, a taste that numbs the tongue, a herbalist's opinion, or the Wazoo notice), and two lesser clues point the same way. Skeemo watches the party's reactions throughout. A party that accuses him meets a calm, rehearsed denial and a promise to trace the batch.

## The God Catcher

The exchange takes place at 18:00 beside the sunken statue in the Castle Ward. The party can hand the coffer over, warn Esvele, swap the vials for a harmless copy, or refuse to deliver. Each choice has a different consequence for Rallygar, for Esvele's opinion of the Doom Raiders, and for what Skeemo learns.

## The Wazoo Notice

If the poison reaches Esvele, Rallygar dies on the third night and the Waterdeep Wazoo reports it on the fourth morning. The notice mentions mint on his lips, and two investigation leads tie the death to a Rosznar-linked trading dispute and to the Black Viper's habits. In every other case Rallygar lives, and the Wazoo reports only a forced strongbox.

## Davil's Answer

The members report to Davil. If they tell him about the poison, he listens to the whole account and gives an answer that neither admits nor denies knowing. He says Skeemo operates semi-independently, promises to speak with him, and asks the party to leave the matter alone. Davil is telling the truth about his ignorance.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Doom Raiders member gains 2 base Renown for delivering or dealing with the coffer and reporting to Davil. Two +1 bonuses are available: one for keeping Rallygar alive, and one for bringing Davil real evidence of the poisoning. Companions gain no membership or Renown.

## Aftermath

The party keeps any pouch Esvele paid. Their choices are recorded as outcomes that **Silencing Skeemo** and **Cassalanter Villa** read. Davil's arrest follows **Gralhund Villa** and does not fall inside this mission. **The Missing Snobeedle** becomes available at Renown 5 and 4th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Davil Starsong** (Doom Raiders): sends the note, receives the report, and does not know what is in the coffer.
- **Skeemo Weirdbottle** (Doom Raiders; secretly a traitor): hands over the coffer and watches the party's reactions.
- **Esvele Rosznar, the Black Viper** (Independent): the lady in the purple cloak, who ordered the vials to dose a mark.
- **Cevin Rallygar**: a Sea Ward merchant whose fate depends on the party's choice.
- **A Trades Ward herbalist**: an optional expert who can identify the poison for 5 gp.

## Dangers & Enemies

There is no scripted combat. Skeemo uses the **Mage** stat block and Esvele uses the stat block on her Notable Figures page, and both flee from a fight. The danger is moral: the party may deliver a poison without knowing it, or deliver it knowingly.

## Overview

A flying snake sends the party to pick up a small coffer of potions from a Trades Ward alchemist and to carry it to a hooded lady at the God Catcher. Something about the errand is not what it seems.
