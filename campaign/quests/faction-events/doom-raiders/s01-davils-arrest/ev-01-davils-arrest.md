# Davil's Arrest

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires two days after the party exits **Gralhund Villa**, for Doom Raiders members only. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Learn that the Watch's city-wide Zhentarim sweep has caught Davil Starsong on association; he is held at Castle Waterdeep, not charged, pending Lords' questioning.
> - Receive Tashlyn Yafeera's first flying-snake message.
> - Meet Tashlyn in person within forty-eight hours: cold, operational, and clear about the difference between herself and Davil.
> - Work toward Davil's release and earn renown for it.
> - Accept that Tashlyn is the contact now, handling all mission briefings until the **Davil's Return** Event (s02).

### The Crackdown

By the morning of Ches 25th, the City Watch has sealed Gralhund Villa and begun processing twenty-three bodies. Within forty-eight hours the broadsheet runs the story, and the Watch begins a city-wide sweep of the Black Network, arresting known and suspected Zhentarim regardless of cell affiliation, legitimate business, or lack of evidence.

Two days after the villa, Davil Starsong is taken in for questioning at Castle Waterdeep. The case against him is association, not evidence. "Pending questioning by the Lords" is the official language; in practice it is indefinite. He suspects Floxin filed an anonymous Watch tip naming the Doom Raiders as persons of interest — a damage-control move from Manshoon's cell, designed to draw scrutiny away from the Splinter and onto the legitimate operation. He has no proof of this.

Doom Raiders party members are known associates of someone under arrest. Party members with visible Zhentarim connections should keep a low profile for a tenday.

### The First Message

A black flying snake arrives at wherever the party is staying, a few hours after the broadsheet hits the streets.

> [!readaloud]
> The broadsheet is still on the table when a black flying snake comes through the window and drops a scroll. The handwriting on it is not Davil's.
>
> > "Davil is unavailable. Continue normal operations. Await further contact."

### Tashlyn's Introduction

Within forty-eight hours, a second flying snake arrives with a time and a location: a corner of a South Ward taproom, away from the Guard's usual circuit.

> [!readaloud]
> When you arrive, Tashlyn Yafeera is in the corner, her back to the wall and a cup she has not touched on the table in front of her, and she does not stand.
>
> > "Davil talked too much and trusted too easily. I won't make that mistake."

> [!social]**Tashlyn Yafeera**
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Arms and Mercenaries; taking over because Davil is in a cell and the cell continues.
>
> Tashlyn does not soften the situation or offer reassurance. She introduces herself by name and title, states the situation with the efficiency of someone who has already processed her feelings about it, and explains what changes and what does not. She expects the same discretion Davil asked for, and she expects fewer questions about why.
>
> She serves as Captain of the City Guard at the South Gate under a dwarf magister named Vorondar Levelstone — a cover position she has cultivated over two years. Her intelligence on Manshoon's cell is sharper than Davil's because she spent a year building sources inside the Splinter rather than business contacts in the Trades Ward. She does not explain any of this unprompted.
>
> Conversation topics Tashlyn is willing to discuss include:
> - Davil's situation: what the Watch has and what they do not.
> - What changes and what does not: how briefings work now.
> - What the party can do to shorten Davil's detention.
>
> Specific dialogue for Tashlyn on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**How long will they hold him?**
>
> > "Indefinitely, under the Lords' questioning clause. The case is thin — association, no evidence — but thin cases still take time to collapse without someone pushing them." She picks up the cup and sets it back down without drinking. "The Watch has nothing that will hold him long-term."

> [!qna]**What can we do?**
>
> > "Document that his cell had no operational role in the villa incident. Find someone with jurisdiction over the case and the inclination to review it. Or get him a lawyer with the right contacts. Any one of those moves the case. Faster if you combine them." She does not say please. "It is not my job to tell you to try."

> [!qna]**What changes now?**
>
> > "Briefings come through me, by flying snake, same channel. Don't expect Davil's warmth. Do expect that I know more about Manshoon's cell than he does, because I built sources inside it while he was building business contacts in the Trades Ward." A pause. "The cell continues. Nothing else changes."

Her follow-up arrives that same evening, by flying snake: *"Keep a low profile. I'll be in touch."*

### Working for Davil's Release

The Watch has Davil on association, not evidence. His detention is indefinite under the Lords' questioning clause, but the case is thin, and substantive effort from the party can shorten it.

> [!exploration]**Gather Evidence of Innocence**
>
> Any character who documents that the Doom Raiders cell had no operational role in the Gralhund Villa incident — witness accounts, evidence of Floxin's Watch tip, something that attributes the bloodbath to Manshoon's cell — can bring it to a Watch captain with jurisdiction over Davil's case. This requires either a relevant contact (a Lords' Alliance Watch officer, a Harper liaison) or a DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion) check with the evidence in hand.

> [!exploration]**Pressure the Right Watch Captain**
>
> Any party member with Lords' Alliance Renown 3 or higher can request Jalester Silvermane's help; Jalester can put a word through official channels. Alternatively, a DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check aimed at the captain directly — with something concrete to offer, such as the Splinter's role, Floxin's name, or a piece of Watch-relevant intelligence — opens the case for review.

> [!exploration]**Arrange a Sympathetic Lawyer**
>
> Any character who makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check to find a suitable advocate in Waterdeep, followed by 50 gp, places one in front of Davil within three days. The Lords dismiss the case within a tenday; Davil is out before Mission 4 concludes.

Any one of these approaches counts. Multiple approaches in combination improve the odds and speed the release, but each requires real effort, not just coin.

> [!gamemaster]**Davil Is Grateful, in His Way**
>
> Davil's warmth does not disappear because he spent time in a cell. If the party worked for his release, he knows — Tashlyn will have told him — and he acknowledges it with the quiet, unhurried sincerity that is harder to deal with than a formal thank-you. He buys a round and does not make a speech. See the **Davil's Return** Event (s02) for the return scene.
