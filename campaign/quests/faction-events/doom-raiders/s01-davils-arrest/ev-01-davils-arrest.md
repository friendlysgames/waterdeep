### The Crackdown

By the morning of Ches 25th, the City Watch has sealed Gralhund Villa and begun processing twenty-three bodies. Within forty-eight hours the broadsheet runs (*Gralhund Villa Bloodbath*), and the Watch begins a city-wide sweep of the Black Network, arresting known and suspected Zhentarim regardless of cell affiliation, legitimate business or not.

Two days after the villa, Davil Starsong is taken in for questioning at Castle Waterdeep. The case against him is association, not evidence. "Pending questioning by the Lords" is the official language; in practice it's indefinite. He suspects Floxin filed an anonymous Watch tip naming the Doom Raiders as persons of interest — a damage-control move from Manshoon's cell, designed to draw scrutiny away from the Splinter and onto the legitimate operation. He has no proof of this yet.

Doom Raiders party members are known associates of someone under arrest — different, and only marginally better. Party members with visible Zhentarim connections should keep a low profile for a tenday.

### The First Message

A black flying snake arrives at wherever the party is staying. It carries a small scroll in neat, controlled handwriting. The message is unsigned.

> > "Davil is unavailable. Continue normal operations. Await further contact."

Four words of cover. Zero words of comfort.

> [!profile]**Who Is Tashlyn**
> **Tashlyn Yafeera** is the Doom Raiders' Master of Arms and Mercenaries. She serves as Captain of the City Guard at the South Gate under a dwarf magister named Vorondar Levelstone — a cover position she has cultivated over two years. Her intelligence on Manshoon's cell is sharper than Davil's because she has spent a year building sources inside the Splinter rather than business contacts in the Trades Ward. She communicates exclusively by flying snake and expects inference rather than explanation.
>
> She introduces herself by name at the meeting described below.

### Tashlyn's Introduction

Within forty-eight hours, a second flying snake arrives. This one carries a time and a location.

Tashlyn chooses the meeting place: a quiet corner of a taproom in the South Ward, away from the Guard's usual circuit. She's already seated when the party arrives, her back to the wall, a cup she hasn't touched in front of her. She doesn't stand.

Her first words to them:

> > "Davil talked too much and trusted too easily. I won't make that mistake."

She doesn't elaborate. She introduces herself by name — Tashlyn Yafeera, Master of Arms and Mercenaries — and states the situation with the efficiency of someone who has already processed her feelings about it: Davil is in Castle Waterdeep, the Watch has nothing that will hold him indefinitely, and the cell's operations continue. Briefings will come through the usual channel. She expects the same discretion Davil did, with fewer questions about why.

Her follow-up that same evening, delivered by flying snake: *"Keep a low profile. I'll be in touch."*

She doesn't say what that means. She'll contact the party when she's decided how much to trust people who walked into a noble's estate and started a war.

### Working for Davil's Release

The Watch has Davil on association, not evidence. His detention is indefinite under the Lords' questioning clause, but the case is thin. Substantive effort from the party can shorten it.

**Gather evidence of his innocence.** If the party can document that the Doom Raiders cell had no operational role in the Gralhund Villa incident — witness accounts, evidence of Floxin's Watch tip, something that attributes the bloodbath to Manshoon's cell — a Watch captain with jurisdiction over Davil's case can be persuaded. Requires either a relevant contact (a Lords' Alliance Watch officer, a Harper liaison) or a **DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion)** check with the evidence in hand.

**Pressure the right Watch captain.** The captain handling Davil's case responds to pressure from above. A party member with Lords' Alliance Renown 3+ can request Jalester Silvermane's help; Jalester can put a word through official channels. Alternatively, a **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation)** check aimed at the captain directly — with something concrete to offer (the Splinter's role, Floxin's name, a piece of Watch-relevant intelligence) — opens the case for review.

**Arrange a sympathetic lawyer.** A successful **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** check to find a suitable advocate in Waterdeep, followed by 50 gp, places one in front of Davil within three days. The Lords dismiss the case within a tenday; Davil is out before Mission 4 concludes.

Any one of these approaches counts. Multiple approaches in combination improve the odds and speed the release, but each requires real effort, not just coin.
