# Davil's Return

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the end of **DR Mission 4 — Silencing Skeemo**, for Doom Raiders members only. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Learn that Davil Starsong has been released from Watch custody; the Lords concluded his cell had no hand in the Gralhund violence.
> - Find Davil at the Yawning Portal bar, sitting with his drink before he says anything.
> - Receive the one piece of intelligence Davil delivers about Skeemo's files and Kolat Towers.
> - See Davil acknowledge, briefly and without ceremony, if the party worked for his release.

### Davil's Return

Davil is released at the end of the same tenday Mission 4 concludes. He was fond of Skeemo. Whatever he was processing in that cell is still visible in how slowly he settles onto the bar stool and how long he sits with his drink before he speaks.

> [!readaloud]
> He is at the bar when you arrive — the same stool, his lute slung across his back. He ordered without looking at the board, and he sits with his drink for a while before he says anything.
>
> > "You should know — whatever Skeemo knew about your operation is already in Kolat Towers. I'd keep that in mind for what comes next."
>
> He flags the barkeep over and orders a round without asking what you want.

> [!social]**Davil Starsong**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: back from Castle Waterdeep; quieter than before; still warm, but slower.
>
> This Davil is not the smooth elf of the curtained second-floor room. He buys the round without ceremony and without explaining why. He is glad the party is here, and that gladness comes out in what he does rather than what he says. He is not ready to talk about Skeemo. He will not bring it up.
>
> If the party worked for his release, he acknowledges it once, in the way of someone who means it and does not need to perform it.
>
> Conversation topics Davil is willing to discuss include:
> - What Skeemo's files in Kolat Towers means for what comes next.
> - How the cell runs from here: he and Tashlyn in parallel, each doing what they are good at.
>
> Specific dialogue for Davil on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**What does Skeemo's file mean for Kolat Towers?**
>
> He turns the charm bracelet, the way he does when he is weighing something.
>
> > "Skeemo had your methods, your identities, your timeline across this city. Manshoon has that file now. You'll need to account for what he knew when you're planning anything involving Kolat Towers." He doesn't explain further. "I'd plan on them knowing more than you'd like."

> [!qna]**Tashlyn's role from here?**
>
> > "She doesn't step down, and I'm not asking her to. She runs the intelligence work; I'm back for conversations, drinks, and the kind of informal debriefs that are easier at a bar than by flying snake." He glances at his glass. "You can work with both of us. Most things are better that way."

If the party worked for Davil's release (*Worked for Davil's Release* marked True), read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Between pouring the second round and something else he was about to say, he stops and turns the glass in his hand.
>
> > "I heard what you did. Thank you."
>
> He means it, and he does not make a thing of it.
