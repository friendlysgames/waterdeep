# The Approach

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Exploration Event occurs when an individual Doom Raiders member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level after **The Missing Snobeedle**, and it ends when Skeemo Weirdbottle runs, yields or is cornered. Tashlyn Yafeera briefs the members at first light, and in this Event the party can:
>
> - Hear Tashlyn name Skeemo as a traitor and order his death, with the condition that it looks like an accident.
> - Learn what Tashlyn already knows about the poison coffer from **The Poisoned Delivery**, and what the party's own choices there have cost or saved.
> - Study Weirdbottle's Concoctions, its hired dray and the paper bird that watches the street.
> - Enter through the back door, the front door or the roof, each of which leaves Skeemo in a different position.
>
> Only Doom Raiders members attend the brief and the debrief. Companions can wait at the gate of the wagon yard and help with every part of the mission after that.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Skeemo has sold the Doom Raiders to the Splinter for two months, and to House Gralhund before that. His masters in the Splinter want two things from him now: the party's habits, and a dead Sea Ward merchant to prove the Doom Raiders will carry anything they are handed. Skeemo's last errand failed to produce the second, or produced it at a cost, depending on what the party did in **The Poisoned Delivery**, and he has judged that his masters will stop finding him useful within days.
>
> He booked a hire-dray to leave from the alley behind his shop at 10:00 this morning. His escape route ends at Yellowspire, the Banite tower in the Castle Ward, where a teleportation circle connects to Kolat Towers. Nobody in the party knows about the circle, and the Doom Raiders believe Floxin leads the Splinter, so until **Manshoon Named** is marked every Doom Raider says "the other cell", "Floxin's cell" or "the Splinter". If **Manshoon Named** is marked, Tashlyn says "Manshoon's cell" in place of those phrases, and she may say the same about Kolat Towers if a player asks where the circle leads.

### The Wagon Yard at First Light

At 06:00 a flying snake finds each Doom Raiders member and tells them to come to the wagon yard beside the South Gate, the same yard where Tashlyn briefed **The Missing Snobeedle**. She has come off the night watch and is still wearing the grey coat of a City Guard captain. Companions wait outside the yard gate, where the carters can see them.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The wagon yard beside the South Gate is already busy in the grey light, with carters backing their teams between the loading docks and a farrier hammering somewhere behind the stables. Tashlyn Yafeera stands beside a stack of empty barrels with her hands clasped behind her back, and she watches you cross the cobbles without any change in her face. When you are close enough to hear her over the hammering, she speaks.
>
> > "Skeemo Weirdbottle has been selling us to the other cell for two months, and I've confirmed it three ways. Three of our safe houses are compromised and two of our informants were taken, so I'm not going to discuss whether it's true."

> [!social]**The Captain in the Yard**
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Arms and Mercenaries, running the cell while Davil is held, and colder than the party has seen her.
>
> Tashlyn stands through the whole briefing and answers what she is asked in the fewest sentences that are still complete. She is angry, and her anger makes her quieter and faster instead of louder. She has known Skeemo for years and does not say so. She never says she misses Davil, and if a member mentions him she says only that he will hear this from her when he is out and not before.
>
> Tashlyn is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What Skeemo has done, which she states as evidence and not as grievance.
> - How the killing must look, because she signs Guard reports for the South Gate and knows what the Trades Ward constables ask about a dead man.
> - The dray, the shop and the four hours the party has.
> - What Skeemo will offer when he is cornered, which she expects and dismisses in advance.
>
> She does not offer sympathy for the order she is giving. She swears only at incompetence, and a member who asks a question the brief already answered gets a flat look and the answer again.

> [!qna]**Why do you know all this?**
>
> Tashlyn takes a folded sheet from her coat and hands it to whichever member reaches for it first.
>
> > "I've had a man watching his shop since the night Davil was taken, and his paper birds fly off toward the Castle Ward every second evening. I had three of those exchanges written down before I said a word to anyone, and I won't say more about who watches."

> [!qna]**What do you want done?**
>
> > "He dies, and it looks like an accident. I'm a captain of the Guard, and I won't have a murder in the Trades Ward with a Doom Raiders name anywhere near the file. If the Watch opens one, it's your doing and mine, and I'll remember which of you was careless."

> [!qna]**What makes it an accident?**
>
> Tashlyn holds up three fingers and lowers them one at a time as she speaks.
>
> > "The constable who takes the report asks what killed him, who was there, and whether anything is missing. Give him a death the room explains, a street that saw nobody, and a shop nobody has been through, and the case closes in a day. Fail any one of those and it stays open."

> [!qna]**When and where do we find him?**
>
> > "He hired a dray to leave from the alley behind his shop at ten this morning, which gives you four hours. The shop is on a side street in the Trades Ward, and you've all been inside it. Spend the first hour looking at the building, because he has had months to prepare and you have had none."

> [!qna]**What if he offers us something?**
>
> > "He will, because Skeemo has never stood in a room without a price for everyone in it. My order stands, but you may bring him to me alive if you truly think he's worth more breathing, and I'll be in this yard from noon until dark. I won't tell you that's wrong. I'll only want you to be right."

> [!qna]**What about Davil?**
>
> > "He's in a cell at Castle Waterdeep on the strength of an unsigned note, and telling him this now would do nothing for him. The Lords release him when they get around to it. I'll tell him myself once he is out."

### What Tashlyn Already Knows

Tashlyn reads the party's record with Skeemo from the outcomes of **The Poisoned Delivery**. Read the paragraph for each outcome the members marked, in the order below. More than one usually applies, and each is a separate exchange that Tashlyn handles before she moves on to the next.

**Poison Delivered.** Cevin Rallygar died in his study in the Sea Ward, and Tashlyn has read the *Waterdeep Wazoo* notice. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn unfolds a copy of the *Waterdeep Wazoo* with a Sea Ward merchant's death on it and lays it on the barrel top between you. She does not look at any of you while she speaks.
>
> > "A man named Rallygar died in his study with mint on his lips. That was Skeemo's work, and you carried it, so I've decided it was his and not yours. I'll say that once and I don't want to hear about it again."

**Esvele Warned.** The Black Viper heard from a member that the vials were poison. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn taps two fingers against the folded sheet in her hand before she goes on.
>
> > "A woman in a purple cloak passed word through the Network's back channels that one of you told her what was in those vials. It reached me two days after it happened, and it matched what my watcher had already written down. It was good work, and it's the last time I'll say so this week."

**Delivery Refused.** The party never gave Esvele the coffer. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn looks from one of you to the next and settles on whoever spoke for the party at the God Catcher.
>
> > "You refused the delivery. I want to know what you did with the vials, and I want an honest answer, because I'll test whatever you tell me."

If the party still holds a real vial, Tashlyn takes one for her own apothecary and leaves the others with the party. If the party dumped or lost them, she nods once and says nothing more about it.

**Coffer Returned.** Davil has the coffer or a vial from it in his suite, and Tashlyn has been through his desk. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn takes a stoppered vial out of her coat and holds it up to the light so you can see the pale liquid inside.
>
> > "This came out of Davil's desk, and I had a Trades Ward apothecary test it yesterday. It's poison with mint laid over it, and the batch number matches Skeemo's ledger. Nobody had to persuade me, but it's why I believe the rest."

**Skeemo Alerted.** A member told Skeemo to his face what the vials held, or drew steel in his shop. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn's mouth tightens, and she looks past your shoulders at the yard gate before she speaks.
>
> > "One of you confronted him, so he knows what you think of him. He's had days to pack, and he'll be ready for a knock, so plan for that."

**Poison Swapped.** Tashlyn does not know about the swap, and the party does not have to tell her. Skeemo knows, and the shop scene below carries it.

If none of these outcomes applies, Tashlyn's own watcher is enough. Tashlyn says nothing about the coffer and moves to the dray.

### Weirdbottle's Concoctions

The shop stands on a side street in the Trades Ward, and every member has been inside it during **The Poisoned Delivery**. The party knows the ground floor: the front door onto the street, the counter, the shelves sorted by function, and the door behind the counter that leads to the back room. The rest of the building is described below.

> [!gamemaster]**The Building**
>
> The shop is a narrow two-story building of brick and timber, with a cooper's yard on one side and a rope-maker's shed on the other. The front door is bolted from inside and has a *Closed* card in the window. The back room has a worktable, a stove and a door onto the alley, and Skeemo's living quarters are upstairs. A stair rises from the back room to an upper workroom with a skylight set into the flat centre of the roof.
>
> - **Front door.** Bolted. Forcing it takes a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check and makes enough noise to be heard on both floors.
> - **Back door.** Bolted, and opening it quietly takes a DC 12 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check.
> - **The roof.** The cooper's yard has a ladder to a flat roof that stands one storey higher than the shop. Reaching the roof takes no check, and the skylight is a short jump from the cooper's roof. Anyone standing there can see into the upstairs workroom and can drop through the skylight.
> - **The alley.** A narrow lane behind the shop runs to a wider street thirty feet away, where the hire-dray waits from 09:30.
>
> Skeemo begins packing at 09:00. At 09:55 he leaves through the back door and boards the dray. If the party arrives after 10:00, the dray has gone, and **The Chase** begins with the party one failure behind.

Any character who spends an hour watching the shop from the cooper's yard and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check confirms that the back room is lit, that the skylight is propped open on a stick, and that nobody has opened the front door since dawn. A member who watches from the alley instead notices the hire-dray arrive at 09:30 with its driver and settle at the mouth of the lane.

> [!exploration]**The Paper Bird**
>
> A folded paper bird perches on the rain gutter above the alley door, turning its head to follow the street. It is the same kind of bird that followed the coffer in **The Poisoned Delivery**, and it carries a warning to Skeemo if it sees the party approach.
>
> Any character who watches the gutters and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the bird before it notices them. A character who spots it can destroy it with any hit from a thrown or ranged weapon, or snatch it from the gutter with a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check. Either way it never reaches Skeemo, and he does not know the party is outside.
>
> - **Bird destroyed or snatched before it sees the party:** Skeemo is unwarned, and the entry table below uses its first row.
> - **Nobody spots the bird:** it sees the party approach and flies to the upstairs window, and Skeemo is warned. Use the "warned" rows in the table below.
> - **Skeemo Alerted is marked:** treat Skeemo as warned regardless, because he is already dressed for the road with his satchel on his shoulder, and the back door is unlatched and needs no check.

> [!exploration]**The Dray**
>
> The hire-dray is a flat-bedded wagon with a canvas tilt, pulled by two horses, and its driver takes passengers to the Castle Ward gate for a copper a head. Five commuters climb aboard at the mouth of the lane between 09:40 and 09:50, bound for their work in the Castle Ward: a baker's apprentice with a tray of loaves, a laundress with a full basket, an elderly cobbler, a young clerk with a leather portfolio and a woman who sells ribbons. None of them knows Skeemo, and none of them will stay calm if a fight starts beside the wagon.
>
> Skeemo paid the driver 20 gp yesterday to wait until he boards. A member who offers the driver more can send him away, and any of the following works:
>
> - Paying the driver 25 gp or more, with a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check, sends the dray off with the commuters at 09:30, and Skeemo has no dray.
> - A successful DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation) check does the same without coin, though the driver tells the constable at the gate about the threat.
>
> If the dray is sent away, **The Chase** uses no Street Chase, and there are no commuters to protect.

### Getting In

The party can enter in three ways, alone or in combination. What matters is who is watching which exit when Skeemo learns that someone is there. Skeemo prefers to leave over fighting, and each approach decides which way he runs.

> [!exploration]**Three Ways In**
>
> **The back door.** A member who makes a successful DC 12 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check opens the alley door without a sound and finds Skeemo in the back room. A member who fails the check makes noise, and Skeemo hears it. If Skeemo is warned, he is already waiting at the foot of the stairs with the satchel on his shoulder.
>
> **The front door.** A member who forces the front door makes enough noise that Skeemo is on the stairs before it has finished swinging. He does not stop to argue.
>
> **The roof.** A member who climbs the cooper's ladder can reach the skylight in one round and see the upper workroom. Any two characters can then hold the roof and the alley: one covers the skylight and one watches the back door. The roof team only helps if the alley team is in place, and neither team can change places once Skeemo runs.
>
> Use the table below to place Skeemo when the party makes its move. Read each row as a full condition, and use the first row that fits.

| The party comes by | Skeemo | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Anyone who arrives after 09:55 | Gone in the dray | **The Chase** begins with a Street Chase, and the party starts with one failure already. |
| Roof and alley both held, before Skeemo runs | In the shop with both exits watched | **Cornered in the Shop.** Go to **The Reckoning**. |
| Back door, quiet, with Skeemo unwarned | In the back room, unaware | **Cornered in the Shop.** Go to **The Reckoning**. |
| Back door, quiet, with Skeemo warned | At the foot of the stairs, satchel on | He runs up the stairs. **The Chase** begins with a Rooftop Chase, and the party is one round behind. |
| Back door, and the check failed | Alerted by the noise | Same as the row above. |
| Front door, in any state | On the stairs | Same as the row above. |
| Alley only, roof unwatched | Upstairs, heading for the skylight | Same as the row above. |
| Roof only, alley unwatched | At the skylight, blocked | He takes the back stairs and the alley door instead. **The Chase** begins with a Street Chase, or with a Street Chase on foot if the dray is gone. |

The Rooftop Chase, the Street Chase and Cornered in the Shop are each described in the next two Events, and every row above leads to one of them.

### Skeemo in the Back Room

If the party enters through the back door and finds Skeemo unwarned, he is in the back room with the satchel open on the worktable and his spectacles pushed up onto his forehead, sorting the last of his reagents into a sealed case. If **Poison Swapped** is marked, read or paraphrase the following before he speaks:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The back room smells of mint and hot glass, and a stove ticks in the corner while a gnome in a leather apron packs vials into a padded case, one at a time, with a small pause over each. He looks up when the door opens, sets the vial down carefully, and adjusts his spectacles before he turns to face you.
>
> > "Strictly speaking, I expected a merchant in the Sea Ward to be dead by now, and I was rather disappointed to learn that he isn't. I do hope that's a coincidence. You'll forgive me, though, if I don't believe it."

Otherwise, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The back room smells of mint and hot glass, and a stove ticks in the corner while a gnome in a leather apron packs vials into a padded case, one at a time. He looks up when the door opens, sets the vial down carefully, and adjusts his spectacles before he turns to face you.
>
> > "Ah. I did wonder which of you Tashlyn would send, and I confess I hoped it would be later in the day. Please close the door, because the draught is bad for the reagents."

Skeemo does not attack, threaten or beg. He speaks only until he understands that the party means to kill him, and then he opens with the offer described in **The Reckoning**. If nobody speaks for a full round, he reaches for the satchel. The moment his hand touches it, he casts *Misty Step* to the upstairs workroom if the roof is unwatched, and the Rooftop Chase begins with the party one round behind.

### Concluding the Event

The Event ends when Skeemo runs or yields. If he is Cornered in the Shop, continue with **The Reckoning**. If he runs, continue immediately with **The Chase**.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Skeemo Warned** — mark when the paper bird reaches Skeemo, or when **Skeemo Alerted** applies; read by **The Chase**, which starts the party a round behind.
> - **Dray Diverted** — mark when the party sends the hire-dray away; read by **The Chase**, which then runs no Street Chase with commuters.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Continue immediately with **The Chase** if Skeemo ran, or with **The Reckoning** if the party cornered him inside. **Silencing Skeemo** awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Tashlyn Yafeera sends the Doom Raiders members after Skeemo Weirdbottle and gives them four hours and one condition. The party studies the shop and chooses how to go in.

## Summary

Tashlyn told us at first light that Skeemo Weirdbottle had betrayed the Doom Raiders, and she wanted him dead in a way that looked like an accident. We spent the morning studying his shop, his hired dray and the paper bird on his gutter before we made our move.
