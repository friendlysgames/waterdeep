# The Approach

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Social Event begins when Tashlyn Yafeera briefs the party at Dead End Alley and ends the moment Skeemo runs. In this Event:
>
> - Tashlyn delivers the confirmation of Skeemo's betrayal in person and sets the constraint: no Watch file tracing to the Network.
> - Three entry options exist for Weirdbottle's Concoctions; the chosen approach determines whether Skeemo is caught inside or escapes to the street.
> - This Event ends when Skeemo runs — either out a door, up through the skylight, or cornered on the roof.
> - Continue immediately with **The Chase** Event.

**Background (DM only)**

Skeemo knows the party is coming. He activated his warning network — a paper bird — when he saw them round the corner in Act 1. He does not know they are here today; he knows they are coming eventually. That distinction matters. He is not already out the door. He is in the back room with his satchel, and the paper bird is insurance, not an alarm.

His escape route: a dray parked around the corner on Copper Lane, hired yesterday, prearranged, with five random commuter passengers already aboard. From the Castle Ward, he can reach Yellowspire's teleportation circle by midday. If the party reaches the shop in the morning, before the dray hour, Skeemo is still there.

### The Briefing

Dead End Alley, Dock Ward, before dawn. Tashlyn is already there when the party arrives. She does not ask if they followed instructions to be unseen. She takes their presence as confirmation.

She does not waste words:

> > "Skeemo Weirdbottle has been selling us to Manshoon's cell. Three confirmed courier exchanges in two weeks with a known Splinter contact. He has also been feeding House Gralhund before that — which means Gralhund had our operational schedule, which means Manshoon had it when Gralhund fell."

A pause.

> > "This has been going on for longer than we knew. He needs to be eliminated. No trace back to the Network. The Watch cannot open a file on this."

She looks at the party.

> > "Everything he knows about your operation is already on Kolat Towers' wall. I am sorry about that."

### Weirdbottle's Concoctions

The shop on Copper Lane in the Trades Ward is closed at mid-morning — unusual for a working alchemist in the Trades Ward. The shutters are drawn. No sign hangs in the window.

A **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** check near the building: a paper bird exits through a crack in the upstairs shutter as the party rounds the corner. It folds itself into flight and heads northeast. Skeemo's warning network is active. He knows someone is outside. He does not know it is the party specifically — but he has begun preparing.

**Three entry options:**

*Quiet entry:* A **DC 12 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** check on the shop's back door. On success, the party enters the back room where Skeemo is sorting his satchel. He has not yet reached the skylight. He startles when the door opens — but does not run immediately if the party speaks first. The confrontation is social before it is physical.

*Forced entry:* The shop's front door breaks in under a Strength check or a kick. Skeemo hears the impact and goes immediately for the skylight at the back of the upper room. He does not stop to argue. He has been preparing for this.

*Rooftop approach:* Characters who circle to the building's rear and access the adjacent roof can attempt to cover the skylight exit before Skeemo reaches it. A **DC 14 Strength (Athletics)** check to be in position in time; success means the skylight is blocked before he clears the frame. He stops, considers the rooftop, and the confrontation happens on the tiles rather than inside.

### The Shop

If the party is inside before Skeemo runs:

**Skeemo Weirdbottle** is in the back room. His satchel is open on the worktable. He is meticulous even in flight preparation: each item in the satchel has a place. He looks up when the party enters with the expression of someone who has known this was coming and had hoped to be somewhere else when it arrived.

He does not threaten. He does not beg. He evaluates.

> > "So. It's today."

What happens next depends on the party's approach. He will not surrender voluntarily — his survival depends on reaching Kolat Towers — but a sufficiently overwhelming display of force in a closed room is a different calculation than a rooftop with flight available. A **DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation)** check in the back room, with the party between him and the skylight, forces the moment where he decides flight is not viable. On a failed check, he makes for the skylight.

### Concluding the Event

The Event ends when Skeemo runs — toward the front door, through the skylight, or up the drainpipe.
