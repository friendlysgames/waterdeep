# The Approach

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social/Exploration Event occurs when Tashlyn Yafeera delivers the mission brief at Dead End Alley and the party moves on Weirdbottle's Concoctions. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive confirmation of Skeemo's betrayal from Tashlyn in person and ask what she knows.
> - Approach the shop by one of three routes, each with a different chance of catching Skeemo before he runs.
> - Confront Skeemo inside the shop and decide whether to force the issue or let him negotiate.
>
> #### Skeemo's Warning Network
>
> Skeemo has a paper bird set to exit when someone unfamiliar rounds the corner. He does not know it is the party specifically; he knows it is someone, and he begins preparing. His escape route — the dray, the Castle Ward, Yellowspire's teleportation circle — is already arranged. This event ends when Skeemo runs for the door or the skylight.

> [!gamemaster]**Skeemo's Situation**
>
> Skeemo knows the party is coming eventually. He does not know they are here today. That distinction matters: he is not already out the door. He is in the back room with his satchel, and the paper bird is insurance, not an alarm. A dray is parked around the corner on Copper Lane, hired yesterday with five random commuter passengers already booked. If the party reaches the shop before the dray hour, Skeemo is still there.

### The Brief

Dead End Alley is narrow and shadowed even in daylight, and before dawn there is barely enough light to see the far end of it. Tashlyn has been there long enough to know which sounds belong to the street.

> [!readaloud]
> The alley is still dark when you arrive. Tashlyn Yafeera is flat against the far wall, watching the mouth of the alley, and she steps forward a single pace when she sees you come in. She does not lean against anything, she keeps her eyes on the entrance, and she does not waste time.
>
> > "Skeemo Weirdbottle has been selling us to Manshoon's cell. Three confirmed courier exchanges in two tendays with a known Splinter contact. He was feeding House Gralhund before that — which means Gralhund had our operational schedule, which means Manshoon had it when Gralhund fell."
>
> She glances at the alley entrance once, then back.
>
> > "This has been going on for longer than we knew. He needs to be eliminated. No trace back to the Network. The Watch cannot open a file on this."
>
> She looks at the party for the first time since they arrived.
>
> > "Everything he knows about your operation is already on Kolat Towers' wall. I am sorry about that."

> [!social]**The Master of Arms**
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Arms, standing in for Davil with fewer words and considerably more edge.
>
> Tashlyn is not warm and is not attempting to be. She delivers the intelligence as a briefing, not a conversation, and she has already decided what she wants the party to do. She stands throughout. She does not explain herself unless the explanation is operationally necessary. She is sorry about the intelligence exposure the way a soldier is sorry when a recon unit walks into an ambush: it is a failure, it happened, and the mission does not wait.
>
> She will not stay in the alley longer than needed. If the party asks questions she considers already answered by the briefing, she says so. She covered the Mission 2 question herself, if applicable. Any further questions about blame, Davil's awareness, or how long she knew are answered once and then dropped.
>
> Conversation topics Tashlyn is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The specific evidence against Skeemo: three courier exchanges, House Gralhund intelligence.
> - The constraint: no Watch file, no trace to the Network.
> - Where to find Skeemo: Weirdbottle's Concoctions on Copper Lane, Trades Ward.
> - His likely escape route: a dray, the Castle Ward, Yellowspire.
> - What happens if he reaches the teleportation circle at Yellowspire.

> [!qna]**The evidence?**
>
> > "Three confirmed exchanges with a Splinter contact in two tendays." She is not looking at the party when she says it. "Courier paths I traced myself. Before that, operational schedules in Gralhund's files that only came from inside the Network." A pause. "It is not a question."

> [!qna]**Why us instead of Doom Raiders?**
>
> > "My people are known to his contacts." She does glance at the party now. "Anyone I send signals what is coming before they arrive. You don't have that problem. Yet."

> [!qna]**What about Mission 2?**
>
> If the party warned Esvele or refused the delivery in Mission 2, read or paraphrase the following:
>
> > "I heard from Esvele's network. I noted it at the time." She does not say thank you. "I would have known sooner if more of us had been paying attention."
>
> If the party delivered the coffer without question, Tashlyn does not raise Mission 2 unless asked. If asked:
>
> > "Every delivery you ran for this faction has been logged in a report to Kolat Towers. Skeemo didn't need to intercept the coffer. He just wrote down that you brought it."

> [!qna]**What if he reaches Yellowspire?**
>
> > "Then he is at Kolat Towers. Out of reach until you can go there yourself, and he will know your faces when you arrive." She turns toward the mouth of the alley. "Don't let him reach it."

Once the party has what they need, proceed to **Weirdbottle's Concoctions**.

### Weirdbottle's Concoctions

The shop is on Copper Lane in the Trades Ward, closed at mid-morning — unusual for a working alchemist. The shutters are drawn, and no sign hangs in the window.

> [!readaloud]
> Weirdbottle's Concoctions occupies the ground floor of a building on Copper Lane with its shutters drawn across every window. A chalkboard that would normally list the day's stock hangs blank beside the door. The Trades Ward foot traffic passes it without a second glance, the way people pass any shop that is closed when it should be open.

> [!exploration]**The Paper Bird**
>
> Any character who watches the building for a moment and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check notices a paper bird exiting through a crack in the upstairs shutter as the party rounds the corner. It folds itself into flight and heads northeast.
>
> > A piece of paper the size of a sparrow slips through a gap in the upper shutter, folds once, and lifts into the air, flying northeast at rooftop height before the wind takes it out of sight.
>
> Skeemo's warning network has activated. He knows someone is outside and has begun preparing.

Three entry options are available, each with a different outcome if Skeemo spots the party coming.

> [!exploration]**Quiet Entry**
>
> The shop's back door can be opened with a successful DC 12 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check. On success, the party enters the back room where Skeemo is sorting his satchel. He has not yet reached the skylight. He startles when the door opens, but does not run immediately if the party speaks first — the confrontation is social before it is physical.

> [!exploration]**Rooftop Approach**
>
> Characters who circle to the building's rear and climb the adjacent structure can cover the skylight before Skeemo reaches it. A successful DC 14 Strength (Athletics) check puts a character on the roof in position before Skeemo clears the frame. He stops at the blocked exit, considers the rooftop, and the confrontation happens on the tiles rather than inside.

> [!gamemaster]**Forced Entry**
>
> If the party breaks in through the front door — by spell, strength, or a hard kick — Skeemo hears the impact and goes immediately for the skylight. He does not stop to argue, and the social confrontation does not occur. Continue to **The Chase**.

### The Shop

If the party enters quietly or blocks the skylight, they find Skeemo in the back room with his satchel open on the worktable, packed with the exacting order of someone who has been thinking about this departure for longer than this morning.

> [!readaloud]
> The back room is dense with shelves from floor to ceiling, every surface organized in Skeemo's careful manner — reagents sorted by family, costs in small precise handwriting on the labels. Skeemo is at the worktable with his satchel open in front of him, and he looks up when the door opens with the expression of someone who had hoped to be elsewhere when this conversation happened.
>
> > "So. It's today."
>
> He adjusts his spectacles. He does not reach for anything.

> [!social]**Weirdbottle, Cornered**
>
> Skeemo Weirdbottle (Lawful Evil, Rock Gnome, he/him) :: a traitor who has been ready for this conversation for months, and is evaluating whether now is the moment to run.
>
> Skeemo does not threaten. He does not beg. He evaluates: the party's position, the room's exits, the distance between himself and the skylight hatch in the upper corner. He is pleasant about it, because pleasant costs him nothing and he has been pleasant his entire life. He will not surrender voluntarily — his survival depends on reaching Kolat Towers — but a sufficiently overwhelming display of force in a closed room changes the calculation.
>
> He uses "strictly speaking" and precise figures even now. He offers information as a deflection tactic before he offers it as a real trade. A party that listens will notice that the most valuable names come only after he decides that flight is no longer viable.
>
> Conversation topics Skeemo is willing to discuss:
>
> - His arrangement with Manshoon: rare alchemical components, Watch-inspection protection for the shop.
> - What intelligence he passed: mission schedules, party composition, delivery routes.
> - Names inside Kolat Towers, offered carefully, with conditions attached.
>
> Any character who makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation) check with the party between Skeemo and the skylight convinces him that flight is not viable from this position. On a failed check, he makes for the skylight at the first opening.

> [!qna]**Why?**
>
> Skeemo sets a small glass container back in its place on the shelf.
>
> > "Access to rare components I could not otherwise source. An arrangement with Watch inspection that has protected this shop for three years. Strictly speaking, it was a sound business decision." He adjusts his spectacles. "I suspect that isn't the answer you came for."

> [!qna]**What did you tell them?**
>
> > "Mission schedules. Personnel attached to operations. The courier routes you used for deliveries. In point of fact, everything Davil knew about your work, I knew, and what I knew I reported." He tilts his head very slightly. "You understand that was not personal. It was an arrangement."

> [!qna]**Names. What names do you have?**
>
> He looks from the party to the skylight hatch and back again.
>
> > "That depends on whether we are negotiating. I have names Tashlyn would find useful. I also have a satchel packed and a route that does not require your agreement." He pauses. "Strictly speaking, I would prefer to discuss terms before anyone makes a decision they cannot reverse."

> [!exploration]**Cornering Skeemo**
>
> With the party positioned between Skeemo and the skylight, a character can make a DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation) check to convince him that flight is not viable from this position.
>
> - On success, Skeemo concedes the point and the negotiation continues. He does not fight a prepared party in a closed room.
> - On failure, he makes for the skylight at the first opportunity. Continue to **The Chase**.

### Concluding the Event

This event ends when Skeemo runs. If the party cornered and stopped him entirely in the shop, continue to **The Chase** for the **Resolution** section, which covers the elimination and the satchel's contents.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Quiet Entry Made** — mark when the party entered via the back door without prompting Skeemo to run immediately; read by **The Chase** (Skeemo was not at the skylight when the party arrived).
> - **Rooftop Position Taken** — mark when a character blocked the skylight before Skeemo reached it; read by **The Chase** (the dray route is not available; the ground chase does not occur).
> - **Forced Entry Made** — mark when the party broke the front door; read by **The Chase** (Skeemo went immediately for the skylight and is already in the air).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Continue immediately with **The Chase**. Skeemo is running, and the direction he went determines which chase path applies.

## Overview

Tashlyn Yafeera has confirmed Skeemo Weirdbottle is a traitor. The party moves on Weirdbottle's Concoctions, and Skeemo knows they are coming.

## Summary

Tashlyn Yafeera confirmed in person that Skeemo Weirdbottle had been feeding intelligence to Manshoon's cell for months. The party moved on Weirdbottle's Concoctions. Skeemo knew they were coming, and when the door opened, he ran.
