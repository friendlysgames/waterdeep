# The Reckoning

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when Skeemo Weirdbottle is cornered or yields, or when the party returns to the wagon yard after losing him. The scenes that follow the deal are for the Doom Raiders members only. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Skeemo's offer, test his claim to be a double agent, and decide what to do with him.
> - Kill him and stage the scene so the Watch closes the case as an accident.
> - Bind him and deliver him alive to Tashlyn, or let him go.
> - Go through his satchel and bring Tashlyn the paper-bird letters.
> - Report to Tashlyn at the wagon yard and receive her verdict.
>
> If Skeemo escaped, play only **Reporting to Tashlyn**.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Skeemo has sold the Doom Raiders to the Splinter for two months, and every safe house and informant Tashlyn lost went out through his paper birds. His claim to be a double agent is built on one true fact. Since the night of the fireball he has given the Splinter false addresses for two of the three safe houses they raided, because he wanted them to keep needing him. He did not arrange for the two informants to be taken, and he did not arrange for them to volunteer. They were simply lost, and he is lying about that part.
>
> He is afraid of his real masters. He believes they will kill him the moment he stops being useful, and the merchant who failed to die has told him that day has come. What he can truly offer is small. He knows where his paper birds go and who collects them, he knows a tower in the Castle Ward has a door that opens to his knock, and he knows that the people behind that door will not open it for anyone who comes after him.

### The Deal

When Skeemo is cornered, he does not fight. He raises both hands to shoulder height, keeping the satchel strap over his arm, and begins to talk the moment anyone draws breath to speak. If he fell from the air, he pushes himself up on one knee first and straightens his spectacles.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gnome is breathing hard, and there is a streak of soot across one cheek and a long scrape down his sleeve where the tiles caught him. He straightens his spectacles with two fingers, looks at each of you in turn, and lowers his hands a little to show that they're empty.
>
> > "Let's be sensible, please. Before you do whatever Tashlyn told you to do, I'd like to offer you something, and I'd like you to hear all of it. What I gave the other cell was partly real and partly invented, and I'm not the traitor she thinks I am. I have been working both sides, and I'm afraid I haven't been very well managed."

> [!social]**The Precise Alchemist**
>
> Skeemo Weirdbottle (Lawful Evil, Rock Gnome, he/him) :: a tidy alchemist whose pleasantness is exact, complete and entirely without warmth, and who has just found that he is out of moves.
>
> Skeemo goes calm and transactional once he is cornered. He never raises his voice, never swears and never pleads. His scorn shows as small corrections of what others say, and his insults are precise and clean. If Poison Swapped is marked, he opens with the line about the Sea Ward merchant from **The Approach** if the party has not already heard it.
>
> Skeemo is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - His claim to be a double agent, which he states as though it were a fact in evidence.
> - Tashlyn, whom he calls "your Captain Yafeera" and believes has never forgiven him for being clever.
> - What he would give for his life, which he names exactly.
> - The other cell, which he refuses to name beyond "my clients" until **Manshoon Named** is marked.
>
> He will not discuss Davil. A member who says the name gets a pause and a correction about grammar.

> [!qna]**Who are you really working for?**
>
> Skeemo adjusts his spectacles before he answers.
>
> > "Strictly speaking, I work for myself, madam. I have interests, and lately they've included staying alive. The people I've been writing to are not people one can leave, and I'd be grateful if you didn't make me say their names."

> [!qna]**Why should we believe you?**
>
> > "I don't expect you to, sir. I can give you three addresses I told them were safe houses, and you may check them yourself. Two of them were empty rooms I'd never seen a Doom Raider in, and I'd ask you to consider why I'd do such a thing for a master I was faithful to."

> [!qna]**What about the two informants they took?**
>
> > "Volunteers, as a matter of fact. They knew the risk, and they agreed to be seen so that my clients would trust me. It's a dreadful business, but it was done with their consent, and I'd be glad to demonstrate the whole arrangement if you'll give me an afternoon."

> [!qna]**What can you give us?**
>
> > "A name and a door. I can tell you who collects my letters, and I can tell you where I was going this morning, and what it would cost you to follow me there. I'd like to keep the satchel, but I'd sooner keep the rest of me."

> [!exploration]**Reading the Alchemist**
>
> Any character who listens to his account of the safe houses and the informants and makes a successful DC 18 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that he is telling a true story in part. Something in it is real, and something is left out, and he has chosen which is which with great care. The character cannot tell which half is true.
>
> Any character who watches his hands and his eyes while he talks and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that he is genuinely afraid, and that what he fears is not the party. He is afraid of whoever will read his letters next.
>
> - **Result of 23 or higher on either check:** The character also notices that he says "we" about the Doom Raiders once, and corrects himself.
> - **Character has identified the poison in a mint vial:** The character has Advantage on the DC 14 check, because the smell of mint on his hands is the same one.
>
> If nobody makes either check, his offer simply sounds sincere, which it is in every way except the ones that matter.

### What to Do with Him

The party can decide at any point after the deal. No check is needed to make the decision, and Skeemo does not press. If the members disagree, Skeemo waits for them to argue it out, and he does not use the delay to escape unless he has been struck after yielding.

> [!gamemaster]**Three Choices**
>
> - **Kill him.** Go to **Making It an Accident** below. Tashlyn's order stands, and the quality of the staging decides whether the Watch opens a file.
> - **Take him alive.** Go to **Alive to the Wagon Yard** below. Tashlyn said she would accept this, and she will want the party to be right.
> - **Let him go.** Skeemo thanks them precisely, walks out and heads for Yellowspire. Mark **Skeemo at Kolat Towers**, and play only **Reporting to Tashlyn**. Tashlyn does not shout when she hears it.

### Making It an Accident

The Trades Ward constable who takes the report asks three questions, and Tashlyn signs the paper that answers them. Each question is scored on its own, and each scores one point.

> [!exploration]**The Watch's Three Questions**
>
> **What killed him?** The death has to be something the scene explains. Skeemo arrives at this point with the wounds the party gave him, so Tashlyn's constable needs a death that covers them. Two methods work.
>
> - **The fumes.** The party seals the upstairs workroom and breaks the blue flasks over the stove. Any character who does this and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) check, or a DC 12 check if they have Alchemist's Supplies, makes a fume cloud that kills a helpless alchemist in about a minute without an explosion. The fire leaves him burned and explains every mark on him. On a failure, the flasks ignite and the fire spreads to the cooper's yard before the party can leave, so this question and the next both score zero.
> - **The bad batch.** If the party holds one of the four mint vials from **The Poisoned Delivery** (marked **Poison Swapped**, **Esvele Warned** or **Delivery Refused**, and not given to Davil in **Coffer Returned**), Skeemo can be made to swallow it. He is the only person in the shop with a reason to taste his own stock. This scores the point as long as the wounds the party gave him are covered, which takes a DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine) check to lay him out with the right bruising, or a fire. A party that delivered the poison in **Poison Delivered** holds no vials and cannot use this method.
>
> A death from spell or weapon wounds scores zero, because the constable reads the body first.
>
> **Who was there?** The constable asks the cooper next door and the woman who sells hot pies at the mouth of the alley. Both saw a crowd near the shop this morning.
>
> - Any character who spots them and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check learns that the cooper watched the roof and the pie seller watched the alley.
> - Either witness can be paid 10 gp or persuaded with a successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check to say they saw nobody. A witness who is threatened instead tells the constable about the threat.
> - If the dray was left standing or the commuters saw the party, the point is lost whether the witnesses were paid or not.
>
> This question scores one point if both witnesses are handled and no commuter saw the party. Otherwise it scores zero.
>
> **What is missing?** The constable walks through the shop and the workroom and asks what a thief would have taken.
>
> - Skeemo's coin purse (150 gp) and the *potion of mind reading* have to stay in the satchel, and the satchel has to stay with the body. A party that takes the gold scores zero.
> - The spellbook and the paper birds can go, because the Watch has no list of what an alchemist owns. The party can take the birds without losing the point.
> - Nothing in the shop can be ransacked, and any character who tidies the room and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check leaves it looking undisturbed.
>
> A score of **three** means the Watch rules it misadventure by the following evening. A score of **two** leaves an open file that goes cold in a tenday, and it never reaches the Network. A score of **one or zero** means a murder file. Mark **Watch File Opened**, and Tashlyn cannot close it.

### The Satchel

Whatever the party does with Skeemo, the satchel is the prize. It holds his spellbook, a *potion of mind reading*, a silk coin purse with 150 gp, and three folded paper birds tied together with thread.

> [!exploration]**The Paper Birds**
>
> The three paper birds are addressed in a cipher that Tashlyn can read in an hour. Any character who unfolds one and makes a successful DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check sees that the writing is a mercantile code used for lists of goods, and that every entry is a name, an address or a time. Tashlyn decodes them at the wagon yard. The writing is addressed to one person, and she reads the name aloud.
>
> The letters cost nothing to take, and they weigh nothing. A party that kills Skeemo and leaves the satchel otherwise untouched loses no point in the Watch's questions by taking them.

### Alive to the Wagon Yard

Tashlyn will be at the wagon yard from noon until dark. The party has an hour or two to get a cornered Mage there through the Trades Ward.

- A gag and bound wrists stop Skeemo from casting most of his spells. He needs voice and hands for *Invisibility* and *Fly*, but *Misty Step* needs only his voice, so a gag is needed as well. A gagged and bound gnome wrapped in a tarpaulin on a hand cart draws no attention in the Trades Ward.
- He comes without a fight, because the Doom Raiders' tack room is a better place to be than a Splinter knife. If a member strikes him after he yields, or threatens to kill him, he decides the deal is off and casts *Misty Step* into the crowd, then heads for Yellowspire. Mark **Skeemo at Kolat Towers**.
- Tashlyn takes him into the tack room. She sends the members out, and she keeps him there until Davil is released.

### Reporting to Tashlyn

Tashlyn waits in the wagon yard from noon. Only members attend, and companions wait at the gate. Read the case that fits.

If Skeemo died and the Watch closed the case as an accident (score of three), read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn is sitting on an upturned barrel when you come in, which she has never done in front of you, and she has a folded report across her knee. She looks at each of you for a moment and then nods once toward the gate.
>
> > "The constable at the Trades Ward desk asked me three questions this morning, and I answered them. They ruled it misadventure, and there's no file. That was adequate work, and I'll see that the cell hears it."

Then Tashlyn asks for the paper birds and reads them at the barrel:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn unfolds the first paper bird on her knee and reads it slowly, turning the paper to the light. It takes her some time, and when she reaches the end she folds it again and does not look up.
>
> > "Vevette Blackwater. Good. Now I have a name to go with the hand. I'll take these, and you can keep whatever else you found."

If Skeemo died and a Watch file opened (score of two or less), read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn is standing where she stood at dawn, and her coat is buttoned to the collar. She waits until you are close enough that she doesn't have to raise her voice.
>
> > "A constable came to my desk at ten, and I've spent the morning telling him it isn't my business. It's open, and it will stay open. Go home, keep your badges out of sight for a tenday, and don't come to the yard until I send a snake."

If the party delivered Skeemo alive, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn opens the tack room door and looks down at the gagged gnome on the hand cart for a long moment. Then she steps aside to let you carry him in, and she closes the door behind you.
>
> > "I gave you an order, and you've brought me a man. I'll listen to what he says, and then I'll decide if you were right. Go and get something to eat. I'll send a snake."

If Skeemo escaped or was let go, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn hears the account without interrupting, standing with her hands behind her back and her eyes on the gate. When you finish, she is silent for long enough that a carter starts shouting at his mule across the yard.
>
> > "Then he's with the other cell by now, and everything he knows goes to them tonight. Your names, your faces and the way you fight. Assume they've read all of it, and don't go anywhere alone."

If **Manshoon Named** is marked, Tashlyn says "Manshoon's cell" where she says "the other cell", and she names Kolat Towers as the place he will have gone if a member asks her where Manshoon keeps his home.

If the party delivered the paper birds to Tashlyn, mark **Skeemo Letters Recovered**. A party that killed Skeemo and took no birds, or lost them, gives her nothing to decode, and she says only that she will look elsewhere.

### Renown Opportunities

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Doom Raiders member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Tashlyn, whether Skeemo died, was taken or escaped. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:
>
> - **+1 Renown:** Skeemo is dead or in Tashlyn's tack room, and **Watch File Opened** is not marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** the paper birds reach Tashlyn, and **Skeemo Letters Recovered** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** no commuter in the Street Chase was harmed, or the dray was sent away before the chase.
>
> Companions who are not Doom Raiders gain no Renown from this mission, and each bonus can be earned only once. Skeemo's gold and potion stay in the satchel for a party that staged the scene, and go to the party otherwise.

### Aftermath

Skeemo's death, capture or escape changes the cell. A dead Skeemo leaves an empty chair at the table, and Davil is told by Tashlyn when he is released. A captured Skeemo stays in the tack room until Davil decides his fate in **Davil's Return**. An escaped Skeemo sits in the sanctum at Kolat Towers and recognizes the party on sight, and the tower's garrison is briefed on the party's methods.

If Skeemo is held alive, Tashlyn questions him for three days. What he gives her is small and real: the name of the hand that collects his letters, the coded knock that opens Yellowspire's door, and a warning about the northeast plate in the teleportation circle room. She gives the members the last two when **The Yellowspire Job** begins.

### Concluding the Event

The report to Tashlyn ends the mission. Davil is released on the first hearing day after the debrief, as **Davil's Arrest** sets out.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Skeemo Silenced** — mark when Skeemo is dead, however the Watch reads it. Read by **Davil's Return**, where Davil grieves a man he was fond of; by **Kolat Towers**, where no one briefs the garrison on the party; and by **Vault of Dragons**, where Skeemo is not present.
> - **Skeemo Captured** — mark when Tashlyn holds Skeemo alive in the tack room. Read by **Davil's Return**, where Davil decides what to do with him; by **The Yellowspire Job**, where Tashlyn gives the members the knock and the plate warning; and by **Kolat Towers**, where the garrison is not briefed.
> - **Skeemo Letters Recovered** — mark when the paper birds reach Tashlyn. Read by **The Yellowspire Job**, where Tashlyn's file names Vevette Blackwater, and by **Kolat Towers**, where she tells the party who receives the Splinter's reports.
> - **Watch File Opened** — mark it here when the Watch's questions score one or zero, in addition to the causes listed in **The Chase**. Read by **Davil's Return**, where Davil asks the party to stay out of the Trades Ward for a tenday, and by **The Yellowspire Job**, where Tashlyn warns that patrols in the Castle Ward have doubled.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> **Davil's Return** fires when the Lords' clerks release Davil on the first hearing day after the debrief, and it reads the outcomes above. **The Yellowspire Job** becomes available to an individual Doom Raiders member who reaches Renown 10 and 6th level. **Silencing Skeemo** awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Cornered, Skeemo Weirdbottle offers the party a bargain. They choose whether to kill him, to hand him to Tashlyn or to let him go, and they report the result in the wagon yard.

## Summary

We cornered Skeemo Weirdbottle and heard his claim to have been a double agent. We decided what to do with him, brought Tashlyn what he carried and learned what she thought of our choice.
