# Wolf

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Doom Raiders member first reaches Renown 3, which usually happens right after **The Dockside Killer**. Davil Starsong names the rank and hands that member the three Wolf benefits, and while Davil is in Watch custody Tashlyn Yafeera does it for him. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Choose which rival faction the informant web reports on first, and hear the result.
> - Learn the address, door phrase and house rules of the Dock Ward safe house.
> - Learn how to order restricted goods at a fifth off through Istrid Horn.

### The Snake at Noon

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 3, a flying snake finds them wherever they are lodging and carries an invitation for that evening. Who sends it depends on where Davil is:

- **Before Davil Arrested is marked, or after Davil Released is marked:** Davil writes the note and receives the member at 20:00 at the Yawning Portal. If **Davil Released** is marked and his suite is still sealed (release terms 0 or 1, which seal it for ten days), he has no lute or bracelet and meets the member in the back booth behind the kitchens, which Durnan lets him use. Otherwise he receives the member in his curtained room on the second floor.
- **While Davil Arrested is marked and Davil Released is not:** Tashlyn writes the note. The meeting is at the stone bench at the foot of the Poplar Walk in the City of the Dead, one hour before dusk on the same day. Yagra Stonefist walks the member to the gate and waits under the last poplar.

Every qualifying member is invited on their own account, so several members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. Companions who are not Doom Raiders members are not invited, and a companion who tags along is turned back at the curtain or the gate. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the snake waiting at their first surface lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the following evening at the same hour.

If **Tashlyn Contact** is not yet marked for the member, mark it at this meeting.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> The informant web is real. It is made up of dockhands, fences, two clerks at the Hall of Justice, and Tashlyn's reports from her post at the South Gate, and its information on Xanathar's Guild is good. Its information on the other cell is good about the cell's teams and poor about the cell's master. The Doom Raiders believe Floxin leads it, when Floxin is Manshoon's field agent and takes his orders from him. Nobody in this Event can say Manshoon's name unless **Manshoon Named** has been marked, and the web reports every cell fact as "the other cell" or "the Splinter", and adds "Floxin's cell" only if **Floxin Status** is Alive. If **Floxin Status** is Dead or Captured, Davil or Tashlyn tells the member that the man they took for the leader is gone and the cell still moves, so someone above him is giving the orders and they do not know who.

### Naming the Rank

If Davil is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil is pouring when you push through the green curtain, and the charm bracelet rattles against the decanter as he fills a glass for each of you. The two lamps are lit, the lute leans against the wall where it always does, and a folded square of paper lies beside his own glass.
>
> > Sit, please. I've had good reports of your work, and Istrid's clerks tell me your name comes up more often than most. The rank we give people in your position is Wolf, and it's yours now, yes?
>
> He slides the folded paper across the table and waits for you to open it.

If Davil is receiving the member in the back booth, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The back booth behind the Yawning Portal's kitchens smells of onions and lamp oil, and Davil sits in it with his sleeves turned back and no lute or bracelet in sight. He pours from a plain jug into cups that Durnan's cook has lent him, and a folded square of paper lies beside his own cup.
>
> > Sit, please, though I'm afraid the accommodations are poorer than they were. I've had good reports of your work, and the rank we give people in your position is Wolf, and it's yours now, yes?
>
> He slides the folded paper across the table and waits for you to open it.

In the booth, Davil has no bracelet to turn, so he rubs his bare wrist instead. The "A Glance at the Curtain" check below then notes that he glances at the kitchen door.

If Tashlyn is receiving the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The City of the Dead is green and still in the late light, and gardeners are raking the gravel between the mausoleums as you come down the Poplar Walk. Tashlyn stands beside the stone bench with her hands behind her back, and she holds out a small folded paper before you reach her.
>
> > Davil would have poured you a drink and made a speech, and I'm not going to. You've earned the rank of Wolf, the arrangements run through me while he's held, and I'll explain them once. Take this.

> [!social]**The Master of Opportunities**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: the leader of the Doom Raiders, who makes every benefit sound like a favor and is honestly pleased when a capable person earns one.
>
> Davil is Friendly and unhurried. He turns a small trophy on his bracelet while he explains and lets a silence sit when the member has a question to ask. He does not swear. He answers "why" with a smile and a question, and he never says why he wants to know something.
>
> Davil is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Wolf rank gives the member, one benefit at a time.
> - Where the informant web gets its news, in broad terms and without names.
> - Why the rank belongs to one member and not to the whole party.

> [!qna]**What does Wolf get me?**
>
> Davil counts on his fingers, unhurried, and stops at three.
>
> > Three things, my friends. Once every tenday I'll tell you what the street has heard about a rival of ours, there's a loft on the Dock Ward waterfront where you can sleep without anyone asking why, and there's a better price on the goods that a licensed shop won't sell you. I'll tell you how each one works, and you'll find they cost you nothing but a little patience.

> [!qna]**Where does the news come from?**
>
> > From people who watch for us, mostly dockhands, fences and a few clerks who owe Istrid a kindness. Tashlyn sees the Watch's reports from her post at the South Gate, and I'd be a fool not to listen to her. I won't give you names, since they'd rather I didn't, and you'll understand that soon enough.

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not the whole party's?**
>
> > Because I write ranks in my book by name, and each of you has earned a line of your own. Your companions are welcome at my table and the wine is good, but the book has no line for them, and Istrid's clerks read the book.

If Tashlyn is receiving the member, she answers the same questions in fewer words:

> [!social]**The Master of Arms**
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Arms and Mercenaries, who runs the cell in Davil's absence and talks like someone paying by the word.
>
> Tashlyn does not sit and does not repeat herself. She swears at incompetence and not at the members in front of her. She says "Adequate" only when she means high praise, and she does not say that she misses Davil.
>
> Tashlyn is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The three Wolf benefits, in the order the member will use them.
> - The informant web, which she describes as hers for the time being.
> - How long the arrangement lasts, which she says is until Davil is out.

> [!qna]**What does Wolf get me?**
>
> > Every tenday I give you one piece of news about a rival, and there's a loft in the Dock Ward for you to sleep in. You also get a fifth off the goods a licensed shop won't sell. Ask for each one the way I tell you, and don't invent your own way.

> [!qna]**Where does the news come from?**
>
> > From our informants, and from my post at the South Gate, where the Watch's reports cross my desk. That's all you need to know about it, and it's more than most people get.

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not the whole party's?**
>
> > Because the rank is yours, and it doesn't extend to anyone who hasn't earned it. Bring your friends to a mission if you like, but they get none of this, and they don't get told about it either.

### The First Piece of News

The paper Davil or Tashlyn hands over carries the safe-house address and the door phrase from **The Loft on Sail Street**. Before the meeting ends, the member chooses which rival the web reports on first, and that report counts as their first use of the tenday channel.

> [!exploration]**How the Informant Web Answers**
>
> Each Wolf member can ask for one piece of news per tenday, counted from the day of their last request, and the first piece is given at this meeting. To ask again, the member tells Yagra at the bar of the Yawning Portal (any hour from sundown to closing) which rival they want. The answer comes by flying snake at dawn the next day, in Davil's hand when he is free and in Tashlyn's when he is not. A member who is out of Waterdeep finds the snake waiting at their lodging on return, and the tenday still counts from the day they asked.
>
> The web watches two rivals closely, and it can answer only for those two. If a member names anyone else, Yagra says the web has nothing on them and the request is not spent.
>
> - **Xanathar's Guild.**
> - **The other cell**, which the Doom Raiders also call the Splinter.
>
> Every answer has two parts. The first is how alarmed the faction is about the party, taken from the faction's current escalation tier in **Running the Villains**. The second is the first response team the party has not yet beaten, named by its leader and its crew. The faction's teams run in this order:
>
> - **Xanathar's Guild:** Gorath with four duergar, then Korgstrod Uxgulm with a half-ogre and a gazer, then Noska Ur'gray with six bugbears and a gazer. When all three have been beaten, the answer says the guild has no crew left to send on the street and that the next move will be Xanathar's own.
> - **The other cell:** Vevette Blackwater with three or four hired thugs, then Agorn Fuoco with three or four hired thugs, then Urstul Floxin with three or four hired thugs. The third team exists only if Floxin survived **Gralhund Villa**, and if he did not, the answer after Agorn says the cell has no crew left on the street.

Davil gives the first part in these words, matching the faction's current tier:

- **Unaware:** "As far as I can tell they haven't noticed you at all, which is the best position to be in, yes?"
- **Suspicious:** "They've started asking who you are, and nothing worse than that, but I'd stop leaving your names on things."
- **Alert:** "They know who you are and they're looking for you now, so I'd sleep somewhere other than your own bed."
- **Lockdown:** "They've put everything else aside to deal with you, and I'd keep off the streets they walk for a while."

Tashlyn gives the first part in these words:

- **Unaware:** "They haven't noticed you, and I'd like that to stay true for the whole tenday."
- **Suspicious:** "They're asking who you are, so stop leaving them things to find, and keep it that way."
- **Alert:** "They know your faces and they're hunting for you, which is your own doing, so plan around it."
- **Lockdown:** "They've dropped everything else to come after you, and if you go out, go out armed and in company."

Both then give the second part in a single sentence built from the ladder above. Davil says, "If they turn on you, the crew to watch for is Gorath's, and he brings four duergar who enjoy their work." Tashlyn says, "The next crew is Gorath's with four duergar, and they will hit you hard." Each names the leader and crew of whichever team comes next.

> [!qna]**Which one should I ask about first?**
>
> Davil turns the trophy on his bracelet once before he answers.
>
> > Ask about whichever worries you, my friends, though I'll confess I'd be pleased if it were the other cell. The Xanathar's people are dangerous, but they do at least behave like a guild. The other cell's people behave like a rumor, and I would like a great deal more news of them.

If the member chooses the other cell, Davil or Tashlyn is visibly pleased, and adds one sentence in their own voice asking the member to bring back anything confirmed about that cell. Renown for such reports is earned as the Factions Guide describes and is not awarded here. If the member chooses Xanathar's Guild, Davil says that is a sensible worry and Tashlyn says nothing.

> [!exploration]**A Glance at the Curtain**
>
> If Davil is receiving the member, any character who watches him while he describes the web and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that his eyes go to the curtain when he says "informants" and that he lowers his voice for the rest of the sentence. Nothing more comes of it here, and a character who misses it loses nothing. The reason is his private suspicion that someone in his own cell is leaking, which he has told nobody.

### The Loft on Sail Street

The paper's second line gives the safe-house address, and it is the member's to use from the next morning.

> [!exploration]**The Safe House**
>
> The safe house is the third floor above Tarrow's Tallow and Wick, a chandler's shop at 22 Sail Street in the Dock Ward, a five-minute walk from the fish market. The shop is run by Wenna Tarrow, who has hosted the Doom Raiders for four years and takes a monthly fee from Istrid for it.
>
> The loft is a single long room with four cots, a barrel of rainwater, a week of dried rations, bandaging and two changes of plain clothes in common sizes. It has four exits: the shop stair, a back stair to the yard, a roof hatch onto the neighbouring warehouse, and a knotted rope from the rear window to the alley. No weapons are kept there.
>
> - **Door phrase:** the member asks at the counter for "a pound of blue-wick tallow, on Istrid's account". Wenna then takes them up the shop stair herself.
> - **Notice:** Wenna needs three hours to air the beds and send her lodger out, and she will not open the loft to a member who arrives without notice.
> - **Who may enter:** the member and up to three guests they vouch for at the counter. Guests get no benefit of their own and cannot come back without the member.
> - **How long:** up to three consecutive nights, and no more than six nights in any tenday. The loft counts as a safe place for a long rest.
> - **What it is not:** a refuge for the hunted. The Watch does not know the address, and Wenna will turn away anyone the Watch is chasing. A member who brings one inside, or who fights there, loses the loft for a tenday when Tashlyn hears of it.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tarrow's Tallow and Wick stands between a net-maker and a salt merchant, and the smell of hot fat and wick cotton drifts into Sail Street through the open door. Candles hang in bunches from the beams, and a broad woman in a leather apron is trimming a mould of wicks on the counter with a small pair of shears.
>
> > Oh, you'll be one of Davil's. Lovely. Mind the third stair from the top, it's been loose since my husband died and I've never had the heart to nail it down.

> [!social]**The Chandler**
>
> Wenna Tarrow (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: a widowed tallow chandler in her fifties who lets the Doom Raiders use her loft and prefers not to know why.
>
> Wenna is Friendly and talkative about everything except the loft's guests, and she tends to trail off into the price of wick cotton when a question gets too close. She asks for no names and forgets faces on purpose. She does not know the Doom Raiders are Zhentarim, or she says she does not.
>
> Wenna is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The state of the fish market and the price of tallow.
> - The rules of the loft, which she recites cheerfully and firmly.
> - Her late husband, if and only if the member asks about the stair.

> [!qna]**Who else uses the loft?**
>
> > Oh, I couldn't say, dear. Davil's people come and go, and I've learned not to count them. You can have a cot whenever you need one, as long as you give me my three hours and don't bring the Watch in on your heels.

> [!qna]**What if I need it tonight?**
>
> Wenna sets down her shears and shakes her head.
>
> > Not without notice, I'm afraid. I need the three hours to turn the beds and get my lodger out, and I won't have a guest walk in on a man in his nightshirt. Come back at dusk and I'll have it ready.

### The Ledger Desk

Any Wolf member can visit Istrid Horn's warehouse in the Dock Ward once the meeting is over. The description of the warehouse and its guards is in the **Doom Raiders First Meeting** Event, and Istrid recognizes the member by name from Davil's book.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Istrid's office glows with lamplight at the end of a corridor of stacked crates, and the shield dwarf behind the wide desk turns a page of her ledger with one finger and finds your name before you have finished sitting down.
>
> > Wolf, is it? Vergadain smile on it, dear. Now, I hear you'd like to buy a few things the shops on Sea Ward Way won't sell you, and I do enjoy being useful.

> [!exploration]**Ordering Restricted Goods**
>
> A Wolf member pays four-fifths of the published 2024 price, rounded up to the next silver piece, for the following goods when bought through Istrid: thieves' tools (25 gp, so 20 gp), forgery kits (15 gp, so 12 gp), disguise kits (25 gp, so 20 gp), poisons that have a published price (Basic Poison 100 gp, so 80 gp), and any weapon on the Player's Handbook equipment list bought without the Watch's license. Magic items, unpriced goods and anything Istrid does not stock are excluded.
>
> - **How to order:** the member names the goods at the ledger desk and Istrid writes the order against the member's name. Each Wolf member may have one open order at a time.
> - **Limit:** the listed price of one order may not exceed 500 gp.
> - **Delay:** delivery is ten days after the order, and it cannot be hurried.
> - **Payment and delivery:** payment is due on delivery. Istrid's carters bring the goods at dusk to the loft on Sail Street or to the member's lodging, as the member chooses at the desk. Goods left at the loft wait with Wenna for a tenday.
> - **Who else:** the discount applies only to the member's own order. A member who buys for a companion pays the same price, and the companion gets no separate discount.

> [!qna]**Why is it cheaper for me?**
>
> Istrid taps the ledger once with a finger.
>
> > Because the Network prefers its own people to be well equipped, dear, and it doesn't do the same for everyone. I'd be delighted to say it again if you'd like. A fifth off, ten days' notice, and payment when the goods arrive.

> [!qna]**Can I get it faster?**
>
> > Oh, I'm afraid not. The goods come from three different suppliers and I don't hurry any of them, and I'd rather you were patient than disappointed. Come and see me the moment you want to order and it will be with you in ten days.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Wolf member earns further Renown from the missions, and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Doom Raiders page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can share what the web tells them, and what the loft looks like, with their companions. The intelligence channel, the loft and the discount stay individual benefits, and Yagra, Wenna and Istrid will not serve a companion who asks in the member's name.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has the paper, has heard the first piece of news and knows how to reach the loft and the ledger desk.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Wolf Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Davil or Tashlyn names the rank at the meeting; it records the rank, and no later event reads it by name. Istrid's ledger desk in this Event uses it. Track separately for each member the date of the last news request, the nights used at the loft this tenday and any open goods order.
> - **Tashlyn Contact** — mark for the member if Tashlyn delivered the rank and the outcome was not already marked. It is defined in **Davil's Arrest**, and this Event only sets it.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three benefits are active for this member from the next morning, and the member's second piece of news opens ten days after this meeting. **Viper** occurs when this member reaches Renown 10, and Tashlyn delivers it if Davil is still in custody at that point. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Davil Starsong, or Tashlyn Yafeera while he is held, names a Doom Raiders member a Wolf and explains the rank's three benefits.

## Summary

We were made Wolves of the Doom Raiders, and the rank gave us news of our rivals every tenday, a loft in the Dock Ward and a better price on goods the shops won't sell. Each of us chose what to ask the informant web first.
