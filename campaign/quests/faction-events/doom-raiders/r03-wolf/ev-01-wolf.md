# Made Wolf

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Doom Raiders renown reaches 3 — the next visit to the Yawning Portal, or Davil's flying snake finding the character within a tenday. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the promotion to Wolf rank from Davil Starsong, named aloud.
> - Hear the three Wolf benefits named one at a time: the tenday intelligence channel, the Dock Ward safe house, and the restricted goods discount.
> - Understand that Tashlyn Yafeera manages the intelligence web, and that Davil does not apologize for this arrangement.
>
> **If Davil Arrested is True** when this threshold is reached, Tashlyn Yafeera delivers the promotion instead. A flying snake carries a meeting time and location; the scene plays as written below with Tashlyn in Davil's place and her characteristic precision replacing his warmth. The benefits are identical.

### The Curtained Room

The lute is on the wall where it always is. The charm bracelet rattles when Davil pours: one glass for everyone at the table, without asking, same as always.

> [!readaloud]
> The curtain parts and Davil is at the table, setting a glass in front of the empty chair, the charm bracelet at his wrist catching the candlelight as he sets the decanter down. He gestures toward the chair without looking up.
>
> > "Wolf. That's the name we use when someone has stopped being a potential problem and started being a reliable one."
>
> He sets the decanter down and names the three things the rank opens, without ceremony, the way someone names tools before handing them across a table.

> [!social]**The Master of Opportunities**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: a sun elf fixer who presents favors as gifts and debts as opportunities.
>
> Davil's manner in this meeting is warm and exact in equal measure. He names the Wolf benefits in plain terms without flourish, and he is clear that Tashlyn manages the intelligence web without apologizing for the arrangement. He frames everything as opportunity and resource rather than reward, and his gentle "yes?" at the end of most sentences draws the listener into the thought rather than waiting on their agreement.
>
> He will answer questions about any of the three benefits. He does not discuss the Network's sources.
>
> Conversation topics Davil is willing to discuss include:
> - The tenday intelligence feed: what it covers and how it arrives.
> - The Dock Ward safe house: what is inside and what it is for.
> - The restricted goods channel: what is available and on what terms.
>
> Specific dialogue for Davil on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**About the intelligence?**
>
> He turns the charm bracelet over once.
>
> > "The Network's informant web tracks rival faction movements across the city — Xanathar's Guild patrol rotations, Manshoon's Splinter communications, Watch surveillance priorities. One piece of actionable intelligence per tenday, filtered through Tashlyn Yafeera's judgment about what is worth passing along. It arrives by flying snake." He pauses. "Tashlyn manages the web. She decides what comes through and when, and she does not explain her methods. That is not a limitation — it is correct, yes?"

> [!qna]**About Tashlyn's role?**
>
> > "She runs the web because she built it. I don't have the same sources inside the Splinter that she does, and I know enough to admit that." He refills his glass. "She decides what is worth passing along. Her judgment has been good."

> [!qna]**The safe house?**
>
> Davil slides a small wax tablet across the table. The address is pressed into it.
>
> > "Third floor, four exits, Dock Ward. Someone will be there when you arrive — you need three hours' notice. I'd recommend memorizing the address before you leave." He watches to see if the party takes the tablet. "The safe house is not a refuge for someone the Watch is actively chasing. If you bring a wanted person inside, that becomes your problem, yes?"

> [!qna]**The supply chain?**
>
> > "Thieves' tools, forgery kits, disguise materials, unlicensed weapons, poisons — available through Network channels at twenty percent below the going street rate. That discount is real. The channel requires ten days' advance notice and a specific request; you are not going to walk in and browse." He finishes the thought with a small rueful smile. "The Network makes things easier to get for people who have earned the access. That is the whole arrangement."

Davil refills only his own glass. That is his way of saying the meeting is done.

> [!gamemaster]**The Dock Ward Safe House**
>
> The address Davil provides is a third-floor loft above a tallow chandler's shop near the fish market. The Doom Raiders keep it stocked with two changes of nondescript clothing, a tenday's worth of preserved rations, basic bandaging supplies, and a small lockbox containing 25 gp. Party members may spend time here between missions without Watch scrutiny. If the party brings a wanted person inside, they are responsible for what follows.

## Overview

Davil Starsong acknowledged the promotion to Wolf rank in his curtained room at the Yawning Portal, named the three things the rank opens, and refilled only his own glass — his way of saying the meeting was done.

## Summary

After reaching Renown 3, the character was promoted to Wolf rank by Davil Starsong at the Yawning Portal. He explained the tenday intelligence channel through Tashlyn Yafeera, the Dock Ward safe house, and the restricted goods discount, then indicated the meeting was over by not refilling anyone else's drink.
