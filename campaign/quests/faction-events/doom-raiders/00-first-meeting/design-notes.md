# Design Notes: Doom Raiders First Meeting

## An Individual Invitation

The restored draft treated the note and the offer as a party-wide event and marked **Doom Raiders Joined** as a single True or False for the whole party. The event now follows the Harpers model. Each eligible candidate gets a note, each answers for themselves, and the outcome is marked per recipient with the character's name. A character who already belongs to another faction gets no offer, and companions can sit in on the conversation but gain nothing. This keeps a mixed party from being forced into one affiliation and gives every later mission an exact membership record to read.

The trigger in **The Factions Come Calling** (evil-aligned or pragmatic characters) only decides when a note arrives. Nobody is checked at the door, and a candidate who is unsure can come back on any evening while **Trollskull Alley** runs.

## Who Knows What About the Other Cell

WDH has Davil describe the rival gang vaguely and never name a leader, and it hands the name Urstul Floxin to Tashlyn later as a rumor. The draft had Davil name Manshoon outright. That is wrong for this point in the campaign. No Doom Raider knows that Manshoon leads the Splinter, and the party learns it late, at the Faction Outposts Interrogation House, where **Manshoon Named** is set.

Davil therefore says Floxin leads "the other cell", and his ask becomes "anything on the other cell or Floxin". This gives the players a name to hunt before they learn the truth, and it keeps Davil's belief wrong in a way that pays off when the truth arrives. The truth sits in a GM-only block. If a player raises Manshoon's name anyway, Davil has a written answer that neither confirms nor denies it. His glance at the curtain plants his private suspicion of a leak without naming Skeemo.

## What Was Added

The restored draft had one scene and no way for a candidate to say no. The event now has a refusal path (Davil pours a second drink and Yagra walks the candidate out), a deferral path, and a late-acceptance rule. Yagra gets a real beat: the five greetings from Appendix B keyed to **Yagra Courteous**, an arm-wrestling exploration that changes how she addresses the character and nothing else, and a walk to the door. Davil answers eleven likely questions in his own voice, so a table that improvises off the script has something to read. Ziraj's roof watch and Tashlyn's two-line note introduce the faction's other faces without giving them a scene they don't yet need. Istrid appears the next morning because her 200 gp loan is the first tangible Fang benefit, and the draft left it as a sentence.

The loan cap follows the Factions Guide (200 gp at Renown 1). The interest rate follows WDH's 10 percent per tenday. The consequence for a missed payment (a visit from two collectors and one more tenday's interest) is an original decision, made so that a GM does not have to invent one.

## Source Departures and Out-of-Scope Notes

The five Yagra greetings are the draft's, tightened to the two outcomes **Finding Floon** actually writes. Appendix B's two extra variants (pulled her off Krentz, stayed out and she lost) are folded into the closest case. Her Durnan line uses **Durnan Acknowledged** and replaces Appendix B's troll-fight paragraph.

Several conflicts outside this folder are logged and were not fixed here. **Finding Floon** and the Factions Guide still write **Yagra Courteous** as a True or False flag. **The Factions Come Calling** lists a 400 gp Istrid loan for the renovation, where the guide says 200 gp. **Finding Floon** blames Krentz's crew for the Dock Ward killing while its own faction note credits the Splinter, so Yagra says only what she was told. **The Factions Come Calling** still says Yagra refers the party to Filthy Meg, and this event does not carry that referral. The event names the Interrogation House as the writer of **Manshoon Named**, which the **Faction Outposts** conversion must supply.

No NPC in this event lacks a voice profile. Durnan appears only as background and does not speak.
