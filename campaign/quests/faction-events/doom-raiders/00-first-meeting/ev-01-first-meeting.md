# Doom Raiders First Meeting

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs during **The Factions Come Calling**, after a flying snake delivers Davil Starsong's note to the characters the Doom Raiders want to recruit. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Follow the snake's invitation to the Yawning Portal, where Yagra Stonefist greets them in one of five ways depending on how they treated her in **The Yawning Portal** Event of **Finding Floon**.
> - Take up Yagra's arm-wrestling challenge, or decline it, before she leads them to the second floor.
> - Hear Davil Starsong describe the Doom Raiders, ask him about the rival Black Network cell in Waterdeep, and learn what the faction offers and asks in return.
> - Decide individually whether to join, with no assignment handed out tonight, and walk away freely if they refuse.
> - Meet Istrid Horn and receive a note from Tashlyn Yafeera the morning after they join.
>
> #### Candidates and Companions
>
> The snake visits every unaffiliated character whom **The Factions Come Calling** marks as eligible for the Doom Raiders: evil-aligned characters, or characters who took decisive and pragmatic action in **Finding Floon**. Each candidate receives a note of their own. The trigger only decides when the note arrives, and nobody is tested for alignment at the Portal.
>
> Davil makes no offer to a character who already belongs to another faction. Those characters, and any companions who did not receive a note, can sit in on the whole conversation, drink Davil's wine and ask questions, and they gain nothing from it. Each candidate answers for themselves. If no candidate is interested, end the Event with no membership outcome.
>
> #### What Nobody in This Event Knows
>
> The rival cell Davil describes is the Splinter, and it is led by Manshoon, who directs Urstul Floxin from behind him. Floxin is Manshoon's chief field agent and the face of the cell in the streets, but he is not its leader. Davil, Yagra and every other Doom Raider believe that Floxin leads the Splinter, and none of them has heard Manshoon's name connected to it.
>
> Nobody in this Event can say otherwise. **Manshoon Named** is marked later, at the Interrogation House in **Faction Outposts**, so until then every Doom Raider says "the other cell", "Floxin's cell" or "the Splinter". If a player raises Manshoon's name, use **If a Player Raises Manshoon** below.
>
> Davil also suspects that someone inside his own cell is leaking to the other side, and he tells nobody. He has never spoken that suspicion aloud, and he will not speak it tonight.

### The Flying Snake

> [!gamemaster]**A Note in the Night**
>
> The snake arrives after midnight on the first night a candidate qualifies, at the window of whichever room the candidate sleeps in at Trollskull Manor, and it waits on the sill until someone takes the scroll. It leaves as soon as the scroll is in hand. Doom Raiders couriers have silvery scales that shimmer through several colors, and their scrolls smell faintly of cedar. Floxin keeps black flying snakes, though nothing in the party's experience yet lets them tell the two apart. Read or paraphrase the following when a candidate goes to the window.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Something scratches at the shutter of your room, and when you open it you find a flying snake coiled on the sill, no longer than your hand, its silvery scales shifting through colors in the lamplight. A small scroll is tied about its body with a length of thread, and the smell of cedar drifts off the parchment as the snake lifts its wings and waits for you to take it.

> [!exploration]**The Scroll**
>
> The parchment carries a single line in a flowing hand, with no name and no seal:
>
> > "Want to be part of something big? Speak to Davil Starsong at the Yawning Portal."
>
> Any character who examines the snake and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana or Nature)** check recognizes it as a flying snake and knows that the Zhentarim train them as couriers across Faerûn. A character who fails, or who does not look, can get the same answer from anyone at the Yawning Portal's bar, since every regular there knows what the snakes mean.

### Back at the Portal

> [!gamemaster]**When the Party Arrives**
>
> Davil sits in his second-floor room from 8 p.m. until midnight every evening, and Yagra is at the bar from sundown until closing. Candidates can go the evening after the snake's visit or on any later evening while **Trollskull Alley** is running, and companions can come along. A character who arrives alone is taken upstairs on their own, and Davil repeats what he said to the others when they arrive.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom of the Yawning Portal is crowded and loud tonight, with adventurers and locals packed around long tables and hearth smoke hanging under the beams. In the middle of the room the great well breathes cold air up past its windlass, and the trophies of a hundred expeditions look down from the walls. Durnan works the bar without hurrying, and at the far end of it stands a half-orc woman more than seven feet tall, with purplish-grey skin, a mop of black hair and a red jade pendant on her chest. She turns from her tankard the moment you come through the door.

### Yagra at the Bar

Yagra Stonefist (Neutral, Half-Orc, she/her) is Davil's bodyguard and the Doom Raiders' door at the Yawning Portal. How she greets the party depends on **Yagra Courteous**, which **The Yawning Portal** Event marks when the party helped her in the brawl or talked the fight down with a successful DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion) check. If the party marked it, she has already told them Davil's name, and if they say that is why they came she raises an eyebrow and says nothing.

When **Yagra Courteous** is unmarked and a mixed party fits more than one case below, use the first that applies in this order: the party sided with Krentz's crew, the party tried to broker peace and failed, the party stayed out.

If **Yagra Courteous** is marked and the party fought beside her, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra sets her tankard on the bar and looks you over from boots to hair, cracking her knuckles one hand at a time. Then she grins and jerks her thumb toward the stairs at the back of the room.
>
> > "I remember that punch. Good fucking timing, too. Davil's upstairs, and he's been asking about you since that night."

If **Yagra Courteous** is marked and the party talked the fight down, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra turns from the bar with her arms folded, and her broad face gives nothing away until one corner of her mouth lifts and she nods toward the stairs.
>
> > "You talked Krentz's lot out of a fight I wanted. That was a shit thing to do to me, and it was clever as fuck, so I'll allow it. Davil's upstairs."

If the party sided with Krentz's crew, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra keeps her back against the bar as you come in and holds your eyes for a long moment, one broad hand flat on the wood beside her tankard.
>
> > "Davil sent for you, so I'm delivering the message, and that's all this is. Try anything stupid on my stairs and I'll put you through the fucking wall."

If the party tried to broker peace and failed, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra looks you over, works her jaw from one side to the other and picks up her tankard before she says anything.
>
> > "You tried to talk them out of it. I heard you. Didn't fucking work, did it? It was a decent try, though. Davil's upstairs."

If the party stayed out of the brawl, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra turns on her stool and studies you with the flat, unhurried look of someone checking an old bruise.
>
> > "Watchers. You stood there and watched. Fine, it wasn't your fight, and shit happens. Davil's upstairs."

If the party also marked **Durnan Acknowledged**, add the following before she turns toward the stairs:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra pauses and nods at you once.
>
> > "And you didn't run from the troll. Most people run like fucking rabbits."

> [!social]**The Doorkeeper**
>
> Yagra Stonefist (Neutral, Half-Orc, she/her) :: Davil's bodyguard and the Doom Raiders' door at the Yawning Portal, who says exactly what she means and greets strangers by challenging them to an arm-wrestle.
>
> Yagra is Friendly toward the party if **Yagra Courteous** is marked and Indifferent if it is not, but her manner is the same either way: loud, blunt and cheerful. She swears in nearly every sentence without meaning anything by it, cracks her knuckles before she speaks to strangers, takes words literally and misses sarcasm. She calls people by what they are ("you, the tall one") until they have earned a name. She never lies to a friend, never flatters, and never apologizes for winning.
>
> Yagra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Davil, whom she protects and speaks about the way someone speaks about the only person who ever asked what she wanted.
> - The brawl with Krentz's crew, which she is still angry about.
> - Her own past, which she tells flatly and without weight, and which makes most listeners uncomfortable.
> - Whether the party should join, on which she gives an honest answer and no sales pitch.
>
> She will not discuss Davil's business, the Doom Raiders' fronts or anything about the other cell. She says that is Davil's to tell.

> [!qna]**Who is Davil?**
>
> Yagra shrugs one massive shoulder and takes a swallow from her tankard.
>
> > "He's the boss, and the only one who ever asked me what I wanted to do with myself. He talks too much and drinks too much, but he pays on time. If somebody hurts him, I'll find them, and I'll be really fucking upset."

> [!qna]**The fight with Krentz's crew?**
>
> Her knuckles crack, one hand and then the other.
>
> > "They killed a friend of ours in the Dock Ward, or that's what I was told. Then one of them laughed about it right in front of me, so I hit him. Shit, I'd do it again tomorrow."

> [!qna]**Why are you with the Doom Raiders?**
>
> Yagra says it flatly and takes another swallow.
>
> > "I was on a slaver ship, chained to a bench. Davil's people took the ship, and he asked me what I wanted to do next. I said fight, so here I am. It's a good job."

> [!qna]**Should we join?**
>
> > "How the fuck should I know? It pays. It's not clean. They keep their word, and nobody's ever thrown me out for being loud."

> [!exploration]**Arm First, Talk After**
>
> Before she takes anyone upstairs, Yagra plants an elbow on the nearest table and offers her hand to the first candidate she looks at, then to each of the others in turn. Any character who takes her hand and makes a successful **DC 16 Strength (Athletics)** check holds her to a standstill until she calls it, and she buys that character a drink. On a result of 21 or higher the character pins her hand to the table, and the near end of the taproom goes quiet before Yagra bursts out laughing and demands another go.
>
> A character who fails loses without injury, and Yagra says "Fair fight" and claps them on the shoulder. A character who declines gets a shrug and a "Later, then." Yagra learns the name of anyone who sat down across from her and uses it from then on, while a character who declined stays "the short one" or "the one with the sword" until they have earned a name another way. The contest changes nothing about Davil's offer.
>
> Regardless of the outcome, Yagra leads the party through the taproom and up the stairs to the second floor.

### Davil's Room

> [!gamemaster]**The Curtained Room**
>
> Davil's business room is at the far end of the second-floor landing behind a heavy green curtain. It holds a round table, two hanging lamps, a decanter and enough chairs for everyone in the party, because Yagra brings more from the landing. His lute leans against the wall. The trophies on his charm bracelet come from monsters the Doom Raiders have killed, and it rattles softly when he pours. Davil pours for guests before he says anything else.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra draws the curtain aside on a small room lit by two hanging lamps, with a round table, a decanter and an elven lute leaning against the wall. A lean sun elf with amber skin and close-cropped golden hair shot with silver rises from his chair, and the charm bracelet on his wrist rattles softly as he lifts the decanter and begins to fill glasses before anyone has spoken.
>
> > "Sit, please. I'm Davil Starsong, and I'm delighted you came, because a flying snake in the middle of the night is a great deal to ask of anyone. Yagra, would you find a few more chairs for our guests?"

> [!social]**The Master of Opportunities**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: The leader of the Doom Raiders, a retired adventurer who makes every arrangement sound like a favor.
>
> Davil is Friendly toward the party and in no hurry. He speaks in graceful, medium-length sentences, often ends on a gentle question ("yes?"), and lets silences sit instead of filling them. When he weighs an answer he turns a small trophy on his charm bracelet. He answers "why" with a smile and a question of his own, and he never explains why he wants to know something. He does not swear, does not haggle crudely and does not threaten. Violence, in his mouth, is always about helping someone.
>
> Davil is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The Doom Raiders and what they do, which he frames as helping people who have run out of places to ask.
> - The rival cell of the Black Network in Waterdeep and its leader, Urstul Floxin.
> - What the faction offers and what it asks in return.
> - The other members of his cell, though he says little about Skeemo Weirdbottle beyond his trade.
> - Whether a character who belongs to another faction can join. He makes no offer and says so kindly.
>
> He will not discuss his suspicion of a leak, which nobody at the table has any way to raise. He offers no assignment tonight.

> [!exploration]**A Glance at the Curtain**
>
> Any character who watches Davil while he describes the other cell and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** check notices that he glances at the curtain and lowers his voice, the way a man does when he fears being overheard by someone he knows. Nothing more comes of it, and a character who misses it loses nothing. The reason is his suspicion of a leak inside his own cell.

Once everyone has a glass and a chair, Davil begins.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil sets the decanter down and turns a small silver trophy on his bracelet between two fingers as he looks around the table.
>
> > "I'll be plain with you, because I think you'd rather have that than a sales pitch. I'm Zhentarim, the Black Network. I know what that name means to a great many people in this city, and I won't waste your evening arguing with a reputation. My companions and I were adventurers once, and a few years back we took up with the Network because it let us go on helping people. Mostly we lend money, hire out sword arms and do other favors for those who have run out of places to ask."

> [!readaloud]
>
> His smile fades a little, and he glances toward the curtain before he goes on.
>
> > "There is a complication, and I'd like you to hear it from me. A second cell of the Black Network has come to Waterdeep, and it isn't ours. It tried to take over the Xanathar Guild not long ago and failed, and the war it started has been running through the streets ever since. As far as we can tell, it's led by a man named Urstul Floxin. We want that cell gone, and I promise you the Watch does too, yes?"

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil refills your glasses and leans back in his chair, his fingers loosely around his own.
>
> > "Here is what we can offer. Istrid Horn will lend any member up to two hundred gold at rates that won't make you feel robbed, and our fences pay sixty percent for goods a legitimate shop would take at fifty. As you prove yourselves, more follows from Tashlyn Yafeera and Ziraj and others you'll meet. In return we ask for capable, discreet work, and for anything you learn about that other cell or about Floxin. One clean piece of news a tenday is worth more to me than a grand report once a month."

> [!readaloud]
>
> He lifts his glass but doesn't drink from it.
>
> > "I won't ask for your answer tonight. Don't take too long over it, though. The city has run out of patience, and I'm afraid I have too. If you want a place at this table, tell me, and I'll write your name in my book."

> [!qna]**The Doom Raiders?**
>
> Davil tips his head toward the lute against the wall.
>
> > "Five of us started it, back when we were still walking into dungeons for coin. Yagra came along later, once she'd given us good reason to keep her. Between us we've built a few honest businesses and a few less honest ones, and the profit pays for people who need help. You'd be surprised how many do, yes?"

> [!qna]**Are you good people?**
>
> > "No, I wouldn't tell you that. I'd tell you we're useful and that we pay what we promise, which in my experience is rarer than being good. We don't hurt people for pleasure, and we don't leave our own to the Watch. Would you like to hear what we do instead?"

> [!qna]**Who are the others?**
>
> Davil counts them off on his bracelet, one small trophy at a time.
>
> > "Istrid Horn is a dwarf who lends money and does it very well, and she'll give you her terms herself. Tashlyn Yafeera handles arms and mercenaries, and she doesn't care for company. Ziraj is a hunter, and you may never hear him speak. Skeemo Weirdbottle is our alchemist, a gnome of very precise habits with a shop in the Trades Ward. You'll meet them as the work requires."

> [!qna]**The other cell?**
>
> The smile leaves his face while he talks.
>
> > "They come from the same Network we do, which is the embarrassing part. They arrived in Waterdeep with money and no manners, and they went after the Xanathar Guild before they'd learned who they were dealing with. When that failed, they took to the streets. Floxin is the name that keeps coming up, a large man in his forties who makes no effort to hide what he is."

> [!qna]**Does Floxin answer to someone?**
>
> > "Most people do, in the end. But I've paid a great many people to tell me who stands above Floxin, and none of them could give me a name. So I treat him as the one to watch, and I'd take it as a kindness if you did the same."

> [!gamemaster]**If a Player Raises Manshoon**
>
> Davil does not know that Manshoon leads the Splinter, and he does not confirm or deny it. His fingers stop on the bracelet for a moment, and he answers:
>
> > "Manshoon? I haven't heard that name from anyone who'd know since I joined the Network. If you've come across proof, I'd very much like to see it. Everything I have points at Floxin, and Floxin is who I'm asking you to watch for."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us?**
>
> > "Capable, discreet work. If you find a safe house, a name, or the hours when their messengers move, bring it to me or to Tashlyn, and it will be worth your while. We also ask you to keep the Watch out of our business, since they keep a file on us as it is. Tell me, have you ever seen a large man who behaves as if every room belongs to him?"

If a candidate describes such a man, Davil listens closely, asks where and when, and writes it in his book. He does not say why he wants to know. Renown for intelligence begins once the candidate is a member.

> [!qna]**What's the catch?**
>
> > "Discretion, mostly. If the Watch takes an interest in you because of something you did for us, we'll help where we can, but we won't march an army to Castle Waterdeep. And the Network remembers who repays what they owe. Istrid especially. You can refuse any job that frightens you, and nobody has been harmed for saying no."

> [!qna]**Can I keep my other loyalties?**
>
> Davil shakes his head, gently and without offense.
>
> > "Each of you answers for yourself, and I don't make offers to anyone who wears another's colors. Please don't take it personally. You're welcome at this table and the wine is good, and if your situation changes, you know where to find me."

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> A slow smile crosses his face.
>
> > "You did things over the last few days that people noticed, and I noticed too. I don't think I need to say more than that, do I? What I'd like to know is why you answered the note."

> [!qna]**What if we say no?**
>
> > "Then you finish your wine, and Yagra walks you out, and I hope you'll think well of us. If you change your mind, I'm in this room most evenings until midnight."

### Each Candidate's Answer

> [!gamemaster]**Recording the Answers**
>
> Ask each invited candidate for their answer and record each one separately. An accepting character who is still unaffiliated becomes a Doom Raider at **Renown 1** with the **Fang** rank. Joining takes no check, and mixed answers cause no argument with Davil. Characters who decline or defer keep their current status, and characters who already belong to another faction get no offer.
>
> Davil writes the name of every new member in his leather book, and the fences and Istrid's clerks receive the list within a day. Members are recognized by name and not by token, so there is nothing to carry.

If a candidate accepts, read or paraphrase the following, using their name:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil takes a leather-bound book from beneath the table, writes your name in it with a short quill and blows gently on the ink. He closes the book and offers you his hand.
>
> > "Welcome, then. I'm very glad. Istrid will see you about that loan whenever you like, and Yagra will show you the way to her in the morning. Tashlyn Yafeera will be in touch, and I may have a small job for you before long."

If a candidate declines or asks for more time, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil listens to your answer, turns the trophy on his bracelet once and reaches for the decanter to refill your glass.
>
> > "Then I've enjoyed the evening and lost nothing by it. Finish your wine, please, and think it over if you like. I'm in this room most evenings until midnight."

A candidate who returns later is recorded the same way. Yagra says "Back already?" when they come through the door and takes them upstairs without another word.

> [!gamemaster]**Fang Benefits**
>
> Each accepting candidate gains the following, and nothing goes to a decliner or a companion:
>
> - **Istrid's loan:** Istrid Horn lends the member up to **200 gp** at 10 percent interest per tenday, one loan at a time. The member collects it at her warehouse the next morning.
> - **The fences:** Stolen goods sell at 60 percent of value through the Doom Raiders' fences instead of the standard 50. Yagra shows new members the way on the same morning, and later sales need only the member's name.
> - **The book:** Davil's list is the only proof of membership, so a member who wants to prove it to another Doom Raider names Davil or Yagra.

### Leaving the Portal

> [!gamemaster]**Yagra Sees Them Out**
>
> Yagra walks every candidate down the stairs and to the door, whatever their answer, and speaks to each in turn. If the party includes both members and decliners, she speaks to the members first.

If the candidate accepted, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra waits at the foot of the stairs with a fresh tankard, which she pushes into your hand before you can decline it.
>
> > "Welcome to the shit end of the Black Network. Meet me here at nine in the morning and I'll take you to Istrid. Bring something to carry the gold in."

If the candidate declined, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra walks you to the door of the Portal with her arms folded, and her knuckles crack once before she speaks.
>
> > "No hard feelings. If you change your mind, Davil will still be upstairs and I'll still be here, drinking. Don't be a stranger, shit."

> [!exploration]**The Roof Across the Street**
>
> Ziraj watches everyone who leaves Davil's room tonight, member or not, from the roof across the street. Any character who steps out of the Portal, looks up at the roofline and makes a successful **DC 18 Wisdom (Perception)** check sees a huge figure crouched among the chimneys with a bow as long as a man's outstretched arms. If the character raises a hand, the figure nods once and is gone from the roof within a breath. A character who calls up to him gets a single rumbling answer from Ziraj (Lawful Evil, Half-Orc, he/him):
>
> > "Davil asked."
>
> A character who misses the check sees nothing. Ziraj leaves the roof a minute after the last candidate leaves the Portal, and no consequence follows.

### The Morning After

> [!gamemaster]**Two Deliveries**
>
> A flying snake reaches each new member at dawn with a note from Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her). At 9 a.m. Yagra leads the new members to Istrid's warehouse in the Dock Ward. Both scenes are for members only, and a decliner who comes along to watch is turned away at the warehouse door.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A flying snake with silver scales taps at your window before the sun is fully up, and the scroll tied around its body holds a few hurried lines in a sharp, upright hand.
>
> > "You're in. Don't embarrass Davil."

> [!readaloud]
>
> The warehouse stands on a Dock Ward street of salt-stained brick, and two broad-shouldered guards at the side door step aside for Yagra without a word. Inside, past crates stacked to the rafters, a small office glows with lamplight, and a shield dwarf sits behind a wide desk with a ledger open in front of her and a pen in her hand.
>
> > "Come in out of the damp, dears, and sit. Davil tells me you'd like to borrow a little money, and I do enjoy being useful."

> [!social]**The Lender**
>
> Istrid Horn (Lawful Evil, Shield Dwarf, she/her) :: The Doom Raiders' Master of Trade and Coin, who lends from a guarded Dock Ward warehouse and makes every loan sound like a kindness.
>
> Istrid is Friendly toward new members, and the warmth is real, though she also likes having people in her debt. She speaks in measured, complete sentences, states her terms clearly and repeats them once. She calls borrowers "dear", taps the ledger with one finger when she names a figure, and blesses every signed agreement with "Vergadain smile on it". She never raises her voice and never names a penalty as violence.
>
> Istrid is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The terms of the loan: up to 200 gp, no questions asked and no collateral, with 10 percent of the principal added at the end of each tenday, and the principal repayable whenever the member likes.
> - Her devotion to Vergadain, which she mentions once and does not press.
> - How the Doom Raiders' lending works in general, though never the details of any other borrower.
>
> She hands over the coin the moment the member signs. A member can hold one loan at a time.
>
> If a member misses a payment at the end of a tenday, two of Istrid's collectors (**Tough** stat block) call at the member's lodgings that evening, speak plainly and leave without hurting anyone. The debt gains the next tenday's interest, and Istrid's manner cools until it is paid.

> [!qna]**What are the terms?**
>
> Istrid taps the ledger once with a finger.
>
> > "Up to two hundred gold, dear, and nothing to put up for it. Ten percent of what you still owe is added at the end of each tenday, and you may repay whenever you please. That's the arrangement, and I'd be happy to say it again. Sign here, and Vergadain smile on it."

> [!qna]**What if we can't pay?**
>
> > "I'm sure it won't come to that. You're capable people, and I'd hate for us to have an awkward conversation. Let's not talk about awkward things on your first morning, dear."

> [!qna]**Who else borrows from you?**
>
> > "I never discuss another borrower's arrangements, dear, and I'd never discuss yours. It's how I keep everyone comfortable."

## Overview

A flying snake summons specific characters to the Yawning Portal, where Yagra Stonefist leads them upstairs to Davil Starsong and his offer.

## Summary

### After the Meeting

A flying snake brought us a note from Davil Starsong, and Yagra Stonefist led us upstairs at the Yawning Portal to hear him out. He told us about the Doom Raiders and the rival Black Network cell led by a man named Urstul Floxin, and he asked us to watch for anything about that cell. Each of us gave our own answer, and we were told to expect a word from Tashlyn Yafeera.

### Without a Private Meeting

We carried on with the renovation at Trollskull Manor without answering the flying snake or changing any of our faction affiliations.
