# Davil Starsong's Offer

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when at least one party member receives Davil Starsong's invitation during **The Factions Come Calling** Event. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Encounter Yagra Stonefist at the bar, whose greeting reflects the party's history from **The Yawning Portal** Event
> - Be led upstairs to Davil's curtained room on the second floor
> - Hear Davil name the Two Zhentarims split without being asked about it
> - Learn what the Doom Raiders offer and what the faction asks for in return
> - Accept or decline membership; accepting makes them Doom Raiders at Renown 1

### Yagra at the Bar

Yagra Stonefist is at the bar when the party arrives, her red jade Zhentarim pendant worn where anyone can see it, and she is watching the door.

> [!readaloud]
> The Yawning Portal is full and loud tonight, with most of the tables angled toward the great well at the center of the room, where two patrons are arguing quietly about something while a third pretends to eat. Yagra Stonefist is at the bar with one hand around a tankard, her red jade pendant out where nobody can miss it, and she has been watching the door.

How she greets the party depends on their history with her during **The Yawning Portal** Event in **Finding Floon**.

If the party helped her fight (**Yagra Courteous** is marked), read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> She sets the tankard down and looks the party over, revising something she thought she knew.
>
> > "Good punch. Better timing. He's upstairs."

If the party sided with the Guild (**Yagra Courteous** is not marked), read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> She holds the look for a beat before she says anything.
>
> > "Davil sent for you. I'm delivering the message. That's all this is."

If the party stayed out of the fight, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> She turns from the bar and takes them in the same way she did when she came around that first night.
>
> > "Watchers."
>
> A moment passes.
>
> > "He's upstairs."

If the party tried to broker peace and the DC 16 Persuasion check succeeded (**Yagra Courteous** is marked), read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Her shoulders drop a fraction, the closest she comes to ease with someone new.
>
> > "Nice words, that night. Davil's going to want to hear more. He's upstairs."

If the party tried to broker peace and the DC 16 check failed (**Yagra Courteous** is not marked), read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> She looks at them for a moment and nods toward the stairs.
>
> > "You tried to stop it. I heard you. Didn't work."

She leads them up regardless of history.

### Davil Starsong

The curtained room on the second floor holds a table, two chairs, and a decanter that is already open.

> [!readaloud]
> Davil Starsong is at the table when Yagra draws the curtain aside, and he pours without waiting to be asked, the small charm bracelet at his wrist rattling softly as he sets down the bottle. His lute leans against the wall behind him, unplayed.
>
> > "I'm Davil Starsong. I run the Doom Raiders — the Zhentarim cell that actually works for a living."
>
> He gives the decanter a half-turn and sets it down.
>
> > "You've probably heard there are two Zhentarim cells in Waterdeep right now. The other one is Manshoon's operation. Different philosophy. They're not ours and we're not theirs, and that war is already happening whether you're in it or not."
>
> He pours himself a second cup and waits for the party to settle.

> [!social]**The Master of Opportunities**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: a sun elf fixer who presents favors as gifts and debts as opportunities.
>
> Davil made up his mind to make this offer before the party arrived. He is warm, precise about the facts, and easy in the manner of someone who has sized people up for a living and likes what he sees. He turns the small trophies on his charm bracelet when he is weighing something and lets silences sit rather than filling them. He frames everything as opportunity and as helping people who cannot help themselves. He ends many of his sentences on a gentle "yes?" — not to seek confirmation but to include the listener.
>
> He will not name the other Doom Raiders in the room, explain who sold him the party's names, or discuss his personal history with Manshoon's faction. He is not forthcoming about what the trouble he mentions actually is.
>
> Conversation topics Davil is willing to discuss include:
> - What the Doom Raiders are and how they differ from Manshoon's cell
> - What the faction offers its members
> - What the faction asks for in return
> - His timeline for needing an answer
>
> Any character who makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check senses that Davil's fondness for capable people is genuine and that his patience for an answer has a limit he is not disclosing.
>
> Specific dialogue for Davil on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**The Doom Raiders?**
>
> > "Retired adventurers, most of us. We've done things with swords and ropes and a lot of bad decisions, and now we run a faction rather than a party." He turns the decanter a quarter-inch and sets it back. "We handle things the Watch won't touch, because they either don't care or can't. If that sounds like honest work to you, yes, then you've got the right idea about us."

> [!qna]**The Two Zhentarims?**
>
> > "Manshoon runs the other cell. He wants Waterdeep under his boot, and he'll burn whatever's in the way to get there." He picks up his cup without drinking from it. "We want the city stable and trading. We want people able to walk home at night. Those two philosophies are not compatible, and they are both happening in this city at this moment. I'd rather you understood that before you decided anything."

> [!qna]**What do you offer?**
>
> He leans back and sets the cup down.
>
> > "Istrid Horn handles lending — up to two hundred gold, no questions asked, at rates that won't ruin you. Tashlyn Yafeera has a post at the South Gate that gives her a useful view of the city's movements, and once you've earned her trust she'll share what she sees. And Ziraj" — a brief pause — "Ziraj handles certain things quietly. When the situation calls for it."

> [!qna]**What do you want in return?**
>
> > "Capable and discreet. That's the whole of it." He turns the bracelet once. "If you learn something about Manshoon's operation — a safe house, a name, anything confirmed — I'd like to know. One clean piece is worth more than a stack of guesses. We can sort out the details as they come, yes?"

> [!qna]**When do you need an answer?**
>
> > "Not tonight." He refills the nearest cup. "But the city doesn't have patience, and neither does the trouble that's coming. I won't wait forever, and I'd rather not have to ask twice."

He does not describe the trouble further. He pours himself another drink and waits.

### Concluding the Event

Davil does not press for an answer on the spot. If the party accepts, he acknowledges it simply and moves directly to practical matters, naming Istrid Horn's lending terms and noting that Tashlyn Yafeera will be in touch. If they decline, he refills the decanter and does not close the door aloud.

## Overview

A note bearing Davil Starsong's name arrived at Trollskull Manor: meet at the Yawning Portal. Yagra Stonefist was at the bar when the party arrived, and she led them upstairs to a curtained room where Davil explained the two Zhentarim cells, described what the Doom Raiders offer, and said what the faction asks for in return.

## Summary

We were invited to the Yawning Portal by a note from Davil Starsong, and Yagra Stonefist led us upstairs to his curtained room. He explained the split between his cell and Manshoon's Zhentarim without being asked, described the faction's resources, and told us what it asks for in return. He did not press for an answer that night.
