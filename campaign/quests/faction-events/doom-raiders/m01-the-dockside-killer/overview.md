# The Dockside Killer: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> This mission opens for each Doom Raiders member who has reached 2nd level, and no earlier mission is needed. Davil Starsong briefs it in person at the Yawning Portal.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> The encounter in the alley is built for three, four or five combatants, and it counts everyone who fights on the party's side, including companions who belong to another faction or to none. The **Doom Raiders Mechanics Reference** gives the roster for each size.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Doom Raiders members who complete the assignment.

## Hook

A Doom Raiders member who reaches 2nd level receives a flying snake at Trollskull Manor carrying a line in Davil's looping hand. He receives the members in his curtained room on the second floor of the Yawning Portal, pours for each of them, and asks them to find whoever is killing elf and half-elf sailors in the Dock Ward. Companions can join the operation after the private brief. If Davil has been arrested on Ches 26 and the mission is still unplayed, Tashlyn Yafeera gives the brief instead.

## Background

Soluun Xibrindas, a drow gunslinger of Bregan D'aerthe, has hunted the Dock Ward for some weeks. Bregan D'aerthe's public story is that Jarlaxle disowned him for killing without orders, but the story is a cover. Jarlaxle knows about the murders and hasn't decided what to do about them, and Soluun carries a forged Bregan D'aerthe token so the band can deny him if he is caught.

Soluun hates surface elves. He picks elf and half-elf sailors out of the audiences at the Seven Masks Theater, follows each one home and watches him for two nights. On the third he drops the man with a hand crossbow bolt in the back and takes the head with his curved shortsword. Three sailors are dead, and the Watch has found no bolt in any of them. The next target is Heldar, a half-elf deckhand who saw *Blood Wedding* at the Seven Masks a few nights ago and whose ship sails at dawn after the third night.

## The Brief

Davil gives the mission in person and pays a reward if the sailor lives. He asks the members to watch the Muleskull Tavern between second bell and fourth for three nights, and to give the killer to the Harbor Watch alive if they can. He says nothing about why the case matters to him.

## Three Nights on the Waterfront

On Night 1 the party learns the rhythms of Ship Street, and a Harbor Watch officer named Gorra questions them. A successful Persuasion check earns what the Watch knows, including the wound in each victim's back and the half-elf regular at the Muleskull. On Nights 2 and 3 Heldar leaves the tavern alone after third bell. The party has three independent ways to conclude that he is the target, and a failed check on any of them never ends the search.

## The Alley and the Roofs

On Night 3 Soluun waits in a recessed doorway in the alley toward the south quay, and two hired Luskan sailors hold the Ship Street corner. Spotting him takes a DC 18 Perception check, but the party can prepare by scouting the alley or by listening to Gorra. If the fight goes against him, Soluun flees over the roofs, and a chase decides whether he escapes or is cornered and taken. The bloodstained *Blood Wedding* playbill in his coat points to the Seven Masks Theater and its owner, "Rongquan Mystere".

## Renown Opportunities

Each Doom Raiders member who takes part in the stakeout and reports the result to Davil gains 2 base Renown. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** Heldar survives the ambush.
- **+1 Renown:** Soluun is taken alive and handed to the Harbor Watch, either directly or through Davil.

Companions who aren't Doom Raiders members gain no Renown from this assignment, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

Davil hears the report privately from the members, writes the theater's name in his book if they bring it and says only "That's interesting." **The Poisoned Delivery** becomes available to individual Doom Raiders members who reach Renown 3 and 3rd level. **Faction Outposts** and **Sea Maidens Faire** read what became of Soluun and the party's Seven Masks lead.

## Involved Characters

- **Davil Starsong** (Doom Raiders): gives the brief and receives the report.
- **Soluun Xibrindas** (Bregan D'aerthe): the killer, a drow gunslinger who hunts elf and half-elf sailors.
- **Gorra** (Harbor Watch): the officer on the Ship Street rounds who questions the party on Night 1 and takes the killer into custody.
- **Heldar**: a half-elf deckhand who drinks at the Muleskull and is Soluun's next target.
- **The hired hands**: two Luskan sailors Soluun paid to hold the Ship Street corner, who know nothing about him.

## Dangers & Enemies

Soluun Xibrindas uses the **Scout** from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, with a hand crossbow in place of the longbow, Darkvision 120 feet and Advantage on Stealth checks in dim light or darkness. He flees over the roofs at half hit points. His hired hands use the **Bandit** and **Tough** stat blocks according to the number of combatants on the party's side, and they flee when he does. Heldar is not a combatant.

## Overview

The Doom Raiders look for a killer who is taking elf and half-elf sailors off the Dock Ward's streets. Davil asks the members to watch the docks for three nights and protect the next likely victim.
