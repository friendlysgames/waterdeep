# The Dockside Killer

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Investigation/Combat Event begins when Davil Starsong briefs the party at the Yawning Portal and sends them to the Dock Ward. In this Event:
>
> - Three independent investigation paths surface the pattern and identify Heldar as the next target.
> - The party sets up on Night 3 (or later, at their pace) to intercept Soluun before he strikes.
> - Soluun fights at half hit points, then breaks for the rooftops; a three-check pursuit sequence determines whether he escapes.
> - A forged Bregan D'aerthe token on Soluun's person is the mission's most significant intelligence find.
> - If Soluun escapes, he resurfaces in **Sea Maidens Faire** watching the Sea Maidens Faire ships from the waterfront.

**Background (DM only)**

**Soluun Xibrindas** has been operating in Waterdeep for three weeks. He is renegade — disowned by Jarlaxle before the campaign began for conducting unsanctioned operations that created exposure risk for Bregan D'aerthe's presence in the city. He kills half-elves from personal hatred, with no faction authorization. The forged identification token is his own creation: a small silver disc with a stylized spider motif that does not quite match genuine D'aerthe tokens.

Heldar is a half-elf sailor who drinks alone at the Muleskull Tavern on Ship Street most nights and walks the same route back to his ship each time. He matched the previous three victims in every detail that mattered to Soluun, and Soluun has been watching him for two days. He does not know this.

### The Dock Ward at Night

The waterfront runs on a different clock than the rest of the city. Ships unload at midnight. Cargo handlers work between bells. The smell of salt, tar, and wet timber persists long after the evening tide. Ship Street is active enough that a small group moving with purpose draws no attention, but quiet enough that a figure staying still in a shadow eventually becomes visible.

Characters who spend the evening hours on Ship Street and the adjacent waterfront find three independent paths to the same conclusion:

**The shadow that does not move:** A **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check from the waterfront at midnight on Night 2 or later picks up something: one of the shadows near the Muleskull's side alley is staying in place while the others shift with the wind off the harbor. Soluun is already in position by the time most of the foot traffic has thinned.

**The name Heldar:** A **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** conversation with two or more dock workers surfaces a specific detail: one of them was drinking with the second victim the night before that one died — a half-elf named Heldar, who has been back every night since and still takes the same route home. He is at the Muleskull Tavern, late table, third bell.

**The pattern across the three sites:** A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check examining the three alley locations as a group identifies what they have in common: all victims were alone, all after midnight, all within three blocks of the Muleskull, all walking toward the eastern wharf. The killer is not choosing victims at random. The killer knows this stretch of the docks.

### The Ambush

Night 3, or later at the party's pace: Heldar leaves the Muleskull after last bell, moving east on Ship Street with the careful step of a man who has had three drinks too many and is compensating. Soluun is already positioned in the gap between two buildings off Net Street, blade drawn, watching the approach.

A **DC 18 Wisdom (Perception)** check spots Soluun before he strikes. On failure, Heldar has already taken a hit: 11 (3d6+1) piercing damage before anyone can react, and Soluun is out of the shadows and moving.

A character who positioned ahead of the route — stated this intent at the start of the evening — gains Advantage on the Perception check.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Heldar's Survival
> If Soluun strikes before the party can intercept him, Heldar is at 0 hit points but not dead — the wound was meant to stun, not kill, before the follow-through. One character's action stabilizes him without a roll. The party still has Soluun to deal with.

**Soluun Xibrindas** — use the **Scout** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*), with Advantage on Stealth checks in dim light or darkness and a hand crossbow substituted for the shortbow. He does not fight to the death. When reduced to half his hit points, he attempts to disengage and run. He goes up — a drainpipe, a crate stack, then the roofline above Ship Street.

**Pursuit across the rooftops:** Three **DC 14 Strength (Athletics)** checks to keep pace. On three successes before three failures, Soluun is cornered above a warehouse on Sail Street with nowhere useful to jump and surrenders rather than fall. On three failures before three successes, he clears the neighborhood and drops somewhere the party cannot follow.

The forged Bregan D'aerthe token is on Soluun's person: a small silver disc with a stylized spider motif. A **DC 14 Intelligence (History)** check, or a character with prior knowledge of genuine D'aerthe tokens, identifies the discrepancy — the knot work is wrong, the finish too polished. Someone made this for him or he made it himself.

When shown it, Davil goes quiet for a moment and does not explain why it interests him.

### Concluding the Event

> **[GM]**

## Overview

Three elf and half-elf sailors have been murdered in the Dock Ward. Find the killer before the next victim dies.

## Read Aloud

> Ship Street at midnight is not quiet, but it has a different kind of noise than the daytime — the creak of rigging, the slap of harbor water, a handful of workers moving cargo under lantern light. The Muleskull Tavern's windows are still lit at the near end of the block.
>
> You have the name Heldar, a route, and whatever position you chose before he left the bar.

## Summary

The party tracked and confronted a drow gunslinger responsible for three murders in the Dock Ward. Heldar survived. The renegade carried a forged Bregan D'aerthe identification token that Davil took an interest in. Whether the killer was stopped or fled over the rooftops, the faction now has a clearer picture of who is operating in their city.
