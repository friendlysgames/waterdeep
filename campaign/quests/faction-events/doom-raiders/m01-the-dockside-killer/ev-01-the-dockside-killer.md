# The Dockside Killer

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Investigation and Combat Event occurs when a Doom Raiders member who has reached 2nd level answers Davil Starsong's request to look into three murders in the Dock Ward. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Davil's brief in person at the Yawning Portal, which only Doom Raiders members attend.
> - Spend three consecutive nights on the waterfront around the Muleskull Tavern, where the Harbor Watch officer Gorra questions them on Night 1 and the half-elf sailor Heldar appears on Nights 2 and 3.
> - Learn from three independent sources that Heldar is the next target, and work out where the killer waits.
> - Fight or outwit Soluun Xibrindas and his hired hands in a dark alley off Ship Street, and chase him across the rooftops if he runs.
> - Recover a bloodstained playbill from the Seven Masks Theater that points toward Bregan D'aerthe and its owner, "Rongquan Mystere".
>
> Only Doom Raiders members attend Davil's brief and debrief. Their companions can help with every part of the waterfront work.

> [!gamemaster]**Who Is Killing the Sailors**
>
> The killer is Soluun Xibrindas, a drow gunslinger of Bregan D'aerthe who has hunted the Dock Ward for some weeks. Bregan D'aerthe's public story is that Jarlaxle disowned him for killing without orders. The story is a cover. Jarlaxle knows about the murders and hasn't decided what to do about them, and Soluun carries a forged Bregan D'aerthe token so that the band can deny him if he is caught.
>
> Soluun hates surface elves. He picks elf and half-elf sailors out of the audiences at the Seven Masks Theater, a Bregan D'aerthe front, and follows each one home after the show. He watches a victim for two nights before he acts. On the third he brings the man down with a hand crossbow bolt in the back and takes the head with his curved shortsword, then pulls the bolt out and carries it away. That is why the Watch has found three bodies with a small puncture in the back and no bolt.
>
> Heldar (Neutral, Half-Elf, he/him) is a deckhand on a coastal trader tied up at the south quay. His ship sails on the morning tide after Night 3, and he saw *Blood Wedding* at the Seven Masks a few nights ago, which is why Soluun chose him. Nothing in this Event lets the party learn that the disownment is a cover story. Davil knows Bregan D'aerthe only by reputation as a band of drow mercenaries, and when the playbill reaches him he understands that another power is working the Dock Ward. He tells nobody what he makes of it.

### The Brief

Once a Doom Raiders member reaches 2nd level, a flying snake with silvery scales finds them at Trollskull Manor with a line in Davil's looping hand asking them to come to the Portal that evening. Davil receives the members in his curtained room on the second floor, from 8 p.m. until midnight. Yagra lets members up and keeps companions in the taproom.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The curtained room at the end of the second-floor landing is warm and dim, and the round table already holds a decanter with enough glasses for everyone who followed Yagra up the stairs. Davil Starsong rises from his chair as you come in, the charm bracelet rattling on his wrist, and pours for each of you before he says a word. When the glasses are full he sits down and leaves his own untouched.
>
> > "Sit, please, and thank you for coming so promptly. Someone is killing elf and half-elf sailors in the Dock Ward. Three are dead so far, and every one of them was beheaded by a blade in the dead of night. Methinks the City Watch could use a little help, yes?"

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil turns a small silver trophy on his bracelet between two fingers while he waits for the questions to finish.
>
> > "The bodies were found in alleys off Ship Street, all within a short walk of the Muleskull Tavern. Each man was alone, and each was out long after midnight. The Watch has walked those alleys and found nothing, and I'd rather not wait for a fourth body. I'd like you to spend three nights around the Muleskull, between second bell and fourth, and see who walks home alone."

> [!readaloud]
>
> He lifts a hand toward Yagra's chair, which stands empty by the curtain, and then folds it around the stem of his glass.
>
> > "If you bring that sailor home alive, I'll pay fifty gold to each of you who stood in the street. And if you take the killer as well, please give him to the Harbor Watch. They keep a file on my friends and me, and I'd like one page of it to say we helped."

> [!social]**The Host with the Decanter**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: the leader of the Doom Raiders, who asks for a favor as though he were the one receiving it.
>
> Davil is Friendly toward the party and wants the work done quietly. He speaks in graceful, medium-length sentences, ends many of them on a gentle "yes?" and lets silences sit. He turns a trophy on his bracelet when he weighs an answer. He doesn't swear, threaten or haggle, and he never says why he wants to know something. He frames the job as help for people who can't help themselves, and he says nothing about the frightened dockworkers who load Network cargo or the extra Watch patrols that crowd the waterfront.
>
> Davil is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The three murders, of which he knows only what the Watch has let slip: two half-elves and an elf, a deckhand, a rigger and a ship's cook.
> - Where to stand and when, which he has already worked out from the places the bodies were found.
> - What happens to the killer if the party catches him. He wants him handed to the Harbor Watch alive if that is possible.
> - The reward, which is fifty gold to each character on the street if the sailor lives.
>
> He offers nothing about Bregan D'aerthe or the Seven Masks Theater, because he knows nothing about either yet. He doesn't ask the party to promise a result.

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> Davil tilts his head and smiles.
>
> > "Because you're new enough that nobody on the docks knows your faces, and capable enough that I trust you with a stranger's life. My own people are known on Ship Street. A killer who watches the docks would see them coming from a long way off, yes?"

> [!qna]**Where exactly do we start?**
>
> > "The Muleskull, on Ship Street. It's the last tavern on the block to close, and every body was found within a few minutes of its door. Watch who leaves alone after third bell, and see who is watching them leave. I'd love to tell you more, but I don't have it."

> [!qna]**Who were the dead men?**
>
> > "A deckhand, a rigger and a ship's cook, if the Watch has it right. Two half-elves and one elf, and none of them had done anything worse than drink late. If a family comes asking, please send them to me and not to the Watch."

> [!qna]**Is this the other cell's work?**
>
> > "I've asked, and I don't believe so. Floxin has no use for sailors, and the killings are too patient to be his people's style. It isn't ours either. That's about as far as my certainty goes, and I'm sorry it's not further."

> [!qna]**What if we catch him?**
>
> Davil sets his glass down and looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "Alive, if you can manage it, and to the Harbor Watch, not to me. I'd like the Watch to owe us something, and handing them a killer alive will do that better than dropping him in a canal. If he won't be taken, then I won't mourn him."

> [!qna]**Anything else?**
>
> > "One thing. If you find something that seems out of place, however small, bring it to me before you take it anywhere else. I'll be here every evening, and I do like to hear about the small things, yes?"

Regardless of what the members ask, Davil closes the meeting by wishing them luck and pouring the last of the decanter.

### Night One: The Waterfront

The party watches the Muleskull from second bell to fourth on three consecutive nights, and it may choose any vantage on Ship Street or the roofs above it. Night 1 shows the rhythm of the waterfront and brings the party to Gorra's attention. Heldar isn't on the street.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Ship Street is quieter after second bell than it was at dusk, though it never falls silent. Lantern light spills from the Muleskull's shutters across wet cobbles, and the last drinkers drift out in twos and threes, calling farewells and arguing over the way home. Beyond the buildings the harbor slaps at the quay pilings, rigging creaks, and a cat picks along a rain gutter above your heads. Down the street two Harbor Watch guards approach with a shuttered lantern, and the woman in front raises a hand in your direction.

> [!gamemaster]**The Rhythm of the Night**
>
> Anyone who keeps watch until fourth bell sees the same routine repeat. The Harbor Watch passes the Muleskull at half past second bell, the tavern stops serving at third bell, and the last drinkers leave by twenty past. Cargo handlers work at the quay until the tide turns. By Night 2 the party knows the timings well enough to say when a lone drinker will reach the alley that runs toward the south quay. No check is needed.

> [!social]**The Officer on Her Rounds**
>
> Gorra (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: a Harbor Watch officer who has walked the Ship Street rounds for years and has seen three sailors carried off it this season.
>
> Gorra is Indifferent toward strangers loitering near the Muleskull after second bell, and she hides her tiredness behind a brisk manner. She talks in plain, practical sentences, asks for names, and swears mildly when she is worn down. She is tired of the killings and of being told to do something about them without being given more guards. If a character convinces her that the party is here to help, she shares what the Watch knows. She also remembers their faces, and she is the person the party hands the killer to if they catch him.
>
> Gorra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What they are doing on Ship Street at this hour, which she asks first.
> - The three bodies: where they were found, how they died and what was in their pockets.
> - The half-elf sailor who drinks at the Muleskull most nights, whom she would watch if she had the guards.

> [!exploration]**Talking to Gorra**
>
> Any character who explains why the party is on Ship Street and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check convinces Gorra that they mean the Watch no harm. She notes their faces and tells them what the Watch knows, as set out in the answers below.
>
> - **Advantage:** The character offers to send her whatever the party sees.
> - **Disadvantage:** The party stands in the open with drawn weapons, or the character hides why they are here.
> - **Failure:** Gorra doesn't believe them. She tells them to clear off Ship Street, posts a guard at the Muleskull's door until third bell, and moves on. The party can watch from the quay end or the roofs instead, and the Muleskull's barkeep gives Heldar's name for a silver on Night 2 without a check.

If the check succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Gorra studies your faces one after another, lowers the hood of her lantern, and turns her head toward the Muleskull as its door bangs open on another pair of drinkers.
>
> > "Fine. Three men are dead, and I'd sooner have strangers on this street than a fourth funeral. If you see anything, shout, and I'll hear you from the corner. Stay out of the alleys unless you mean to be seen."

> [!qna]**Where were the bodies?**
>
> > "In doorways and side alleys off Ship Street, none of them more than a short walk from the Muleskull. Two were found by the dawn workers, and the third by a chandler opening his shop. Every one had been dragged into a recess where the lamps don't reach."

> [!qna]**How did they die?**
>
> Gorra drops her voice, though nobody nearer than the corner could hear her.
>
> > "The head was taken off with one clean stroke, and the purses were left alone. The Watch surgeon found a small round hole in each man's lower back, deep enough for a crossbow bolt. But there was no bolt in any of them. Somebody collected it."

> [!qna]**What did the dead carry?**
>
> > "The usual. Coin, a knife, a bit of rope. The second one had a stub in his boot from the Seven Masks Theater, but half the Dock Ward has one of those, so I didn't think much of it."

> [!qna]**Anyone we should watch?**
>
> > "There's a half-elf lad from one of the coasters who drinks at the Muleskull late most nights. I don't know his name. If I were hunting half-elves on this street, I'd choose him, but I can't put a guard on every drunk."

Once the check is resolved, the night passes without further incident. Read or paraphrase the following when the party ends its watch:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fourth bell rings from somewhere up the hill, and the last lantern in the Muleskull's window goes out. The street falls quiet except for the harbor, and a single cart rattles away toward the market with a lamp swinging from its tailboard.

### Night Two: The Half-Elf at Third Bell

On Night 2 Heldar leaves the Muleskull alone a little after third bell. He is drunk and humming, and he takes the alley toward the south quay without looking behind him. Soluun doesn't strike tonight. He watches each victim for two nights first, and tonight he is on the roofs.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Muleskull's door swings open at third bell and lets out a burst of laughter and a smell of spilled ale. A young man with a sailor's roll to his walk steps into the street, his coat over one shoulder. He has an elf's narrow ears and a human's stubble, and he is humming a tune he keeps losing halfway through. He waves to somebody inside, staggers once against the doorframe, and turns toward the alley that leads to the south quay.

The party has three independent ways to conclude that Heldar is the target, and each one stands without the others.

> [!exploration]**Who Walks Home Alone**
>
> Any character who watches the Muleskull's door on Night 2 sees that Heldar is the only elf or half-elf who leaves alone after third bell, and that he takes the same alley toward the south quay that he took the night before. No check is needed. A party that has spoken to Gorra also has her description of a late half-elf regular, and a party that didn't can get his name from the barkeep for a silver.
>
> > "Heldar. He's off one of the coasters, and he comes in every night his ship is in. Sails at dawn the day after tomorrow, if I remember right. He's a good lad, and a poor drinker."

> [!exploration]**The Still Figure**
>
> Any character who scans the rooftops across Ship Street while Heldar leaves and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** check sees one shadow above the chandlery that isn't moving with the wind off the harbor. It drops out of sight as soon as Heldar turns into the alley. Someone is watching him from the roof.
>
> - **Advantage:** The character watches from a roof or upper window of their own.
> - **Failure:** The character sees nothing, and the mission loses nothing by it. Gorra's tip, the barkeep's answer and Heldar's routine still put him in the alley on Night 3.

> [!social]**The Drunk with the Tune**
>
> Heldar (Neutral, Half-Elf, he/him) :: a good-natured deckhand in his twenties, three drinks past careful and unbothered by it.
>
> Heldar is Friendly toward anyone who buys him a drink and finds the idea that he is in danger funny. If a character warns him, he laughs it off, tells them he has been hit harder by the ship's cook, and insists on keeping to his usual road. His ship sails on the morning tide after Night 3, so tonight and tomorrow are his last nights ashore, and he means to enjoy them. He swears casually and cheerfully and doesn't take offense.
>
> Heldar is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - His ship and his shipmates, and how much he'll miss the Muleskull's stew.
> - The play he saw at the Seven Masks a few nights ago, *Blood Wedding*, with a girl who left him at the interval.
> - Whether he has noticed anyone following him, which he hasn't.
>
> If Heldar is somehow forced to fight, use the **Bandit** stat block.

> [!qna]**Are you being watched?**
>
> Heldar squints up the street and back at you.
>
> > "Watched? By what, a lass who wants her money back? Nobody's watching me, friend, and if they are, they've got a dull evening ahead of them. I drink, I walk home, and I go to bed."

> [!qna]**Where did you go this week?**
>
> > "The Seven Masks, three nights back, to see *Blood Wedding*. A cracking play, with a great deal of shouting. I'd have liked the second half, but the girl I brought decided she wasn't staying, and I drank her share of the wine."

> [!qna]**Do you walk alone every night?**
>
> > "Every night, and every night I get home. The alley down to the quay is my shortcut, and it's fifty paces of dark and one mean cat. If you're worried about me, come along, but I won't change my road for anyone."

If the party follows Heldar down the alley on Night 2, the alley is empty. The party can walk its length and find where it ends at the south quay.

> [!exploration]**The Alley in the Daylight**
>
> The alley runs fifty feet from Ship Street to the south quay through unbroken darkness. No driftglobes light it, and the buildings on either side stand close enough to blot out the sky. About thirty feet from Ship Street, a recessed doorway on the east wall gives a man room to stand out of sight. Any character who examines the alley by night or by day and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check finds a fresh gouge in the doorframe at the height of a crossbow and a brown stain on the step. This is the place a killer would choose. A character who was told by Gorra that the bodies were found in recesses has Advantage on the check.
>
> A character who succeeds knows where Soluun will be tomorrow night. The knowledge gives Advantage on the Perception check in the ambush below.
>
> - **Failure:** The character sees a dark alley and a doorway. The party can still guard Heldar, and the ambush check on Night 3 stands.

### Night Three: Heldar's Last Night Ashore

On Night 3 Soluun is in the doorway before Heldar arrives, and his two hired hands are at the Ship Street corner. They are Luskan sailors he paid to keep the street end quiet, and they don't know his name. The party may position itself before the evening starts, and a character who says so before Heldar leaves the Muleskull has Advantage on the Perception check below.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Heldar comes out of the Muleskull a little after third bell, alone again, with his coat over his shoulder and the same tune half-hummed under his breath. At the corner of Ship Street two men in sailors' jackets lean against the wall and pass a bottle back and forth without much of it going down. They glance up at Heldar as he passes, then at the door he came from, and then at the street behind him. Heldar turns into the alley that runs toward the south quay, and the dark takes him after a dozen steps.

> [!exploration]**The Two at the Corner**
>
> Characters with a Passive Perception score of 12 or higher notice that the pair at the corner aren't drinking. Any character who listens to them for a few breaths and makes a successful **DC 12 Wisdom (Insight)** check hears the flat vowels of Luskan sailors and sees that they are watching the street and not the tavern. A character who misses this learns the same when the pair close in behind the party.

> [!exploration]**The Shadow in the Doorway**
>
> Any character who watches the alley as Heldar walks into it and makes a successful **DC 18 Wisdom (Perception)** check spots Soluun crouched in the recessed doorway with a hand crossbow raised. Spotting him before he fires lets the party act first.
>
> - **Advantage:** The character stated a position ahead of Heldar's route before he left the tavern, or found the gouged doorframe on Night 2, or was told by Gorra that the bodies lay in recesses.
> - **Failure:** Soluun fires before the party reacts, and Heldar drops at 0 Hit Points under two bolts before anyone can speak.

If a character spots Soluun, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A shape stirs in the doorway ahead of Heldar, and light from the far end of the alley catches the pale wood of a hand crossbow lifting toward his back. Heldar walks on, humming, and doesn't look up. Behind you a boot scrapes at the corner of Ship Street as the two men there push off from the wall.

If nobody spots him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Something small and quick snaps in the dark ahead of Heldar, and he stops mid-note. He takes a half step, says "Eh?" in a puzzled voice, and folds to his knees on the cobbles as a second snap follows the first. A figure slides out of the doorway with a curved blade already in its hand, and behind you a boot scrapes at the mouth of the alley.

> [!hazard]**The Ambush in the Alley**
>
> Soluun Xibrindas uses the **Scout** from the 2024 *Monster Manual* with these changes. A hand crossbow replaces the longbow (+4 to hit, 5 (1d6+2) piercing damage, range 30/120), his shortsword is the curved blade that takes the heads, and he has Darkvision 120 feet and Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in dim light or darkness. He starts in the recessed doorway, thirty feet from Ship Street and fifteen feet beyond Heldar. His hired hands start at the Ship Street corner, and they close the alley mouth behind whoever followed Heldar in. Heldar isn't counted as a combatant. Count everyone who fights on the party's side, companions included.
>
> - **Three combatants:** Soluun and two **Bandits**.
> - **Four combatants:** Soluun and one **Tough**.
> - **Five combatants:** Soluun, one **Tough** and two **Bandits**.
>
> #### Soluun's Tactics
> Soluun fires at Heldar first if no conscious character stands within 5 feet of him. If one does, Soluun fires at the nearest elf or half-elf, and otherwise at the nearest character. Heldar falls to 0 Hit Points after two hits.
>
> During combat, Soluun:
> - Fires the hand crossbow from the doorway at the closest elf or half-elf, and otherwise at whoever bars the way to Heldar.
> - Leaves the doorway to behead Heldar if Heldar is at 0 Hit Points and no conscious character stands within 5 feet of him. Heldar dies if Soluun reaches him.
> - Uses his curved shortsword against any character who reaches the doorway, and shifts his fire to a spellcaster when one stands in the open.
> - Takes the Disengage action and runs for the roofs when he is reduced to 8 Hit Points or fewer.
>
> The playbill is folded inside his coat. The first time a hit brings Soluun to 8 Hit Points or fewer, it slips out and lands in the alley, and a character who spends an action can collect it. A character who reduces Soluun to 0 Hit Points kills him, unless they said beforehand that they are making a melee attack to knock him out, in which case he falls Unconscious at 1 Hit Point.
>
> #### The Hired Hands' Tactics
> The hired hands hold the mouth of the alley so nobody can bring the Watch in behind the party.
>
> During combat, the hired hands:
> - Advance down the alley behind the party and attack the character nearest the mouth, with a **Bandit** using a scimitar and a **Tough** using a mace.
> - Stay together, and try to keep the party away from the doorway while Soluun works.
> - Flee toward Ship Street when Soluun flees or when either of them is reduced to 0 Hit Points, and surrender if the party corners them.
>
> The encounter ends when Soluun has fled, surrendered or died and both hired hands have fled, surrendered or fallen. Soluun surrenders if the pursuit corners him on the roofs.
>
> The party doesn't have to fight. If a character spots Soluun and the party walks Heldar back out of the alley the way they came, the hired hands at the corner have no reason to stop them, and Soluun won't fire on a group of armed watchers. He slips away over the roofs without a fight and without losing the playbill, and the party learns nothing more about him tonight.

Regardless of how the fight opens, Heldar's life depends on the party.

> [!gamemaster]**Heldar at 0 Hit Points**
>
> A character within 5 feet of Heldar can spend an action to stabilize him, and no check is needed. Heldar lives as soon as one character does so. If everyone chases Soluun without stabilizing him, or if Soluun reaches him first, Heldar dies. The party chooses between the chase and the wounded man.

If the hired hands surrender, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taller of the two sailors drops his weapon on the cobbles and raises both hands, breathing hard.
>
> > "I don't know his name, I swear on my mother. A dark elf with a crossbow paid us twenty silver each to keep the street end quiet and make sure nobody followed the sailor. We were told nothing else, and I'd never have taken it if I'd known what it was for."

### The Chase over the Rooftops

When Soluun drops to 8 Hit Points or fewer, he takes the Disengage action and heads for the roofs. He goes up the crate stack beside the doorway, along a drainpipe and onto the roofline above Ship Street, and the party chooses whether to follow him.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Soluun snatches at a drainpipe and hauls himself up onto a stack of crates. His boots scrape on the slates above you as he vaults from one roof to the next, a dark shape against the low glow of the harbor. A loose tile slides past your shoulder and shatters in the alley, and the shape is already moving along the ridge toward the water.

> [!exploration]**Across the Rooftops**
>
> Each pursuing character makes a **DC 14 Strength (Athletics)** or **DC 14 Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check on each round of the chase. Count successes and failures across the whole group. Three successes before three failures corner Soluun on a warehouse roof above the south quay with nowhere useful to jump. Each failure lets Soluun turn and fire once, dealing 5 (1d6+2) piercing damage to the lead pursuer.
>
> - **Auto:** A character who casts *Misty Step*, *Fly* or *Spider Climb* to reach the roof succeeds without a check.
> - **Advantage:** The character has climbed or run these roofs before.
> - **Three failures before three successes:** Soluun drops onto a moored boat and vanishes into the dark. The party keeps the playbill from the alley and nothing else.

If the pursuit corners him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Soluun reaches the edge of the warehouse roof and stops, the drop to the quay black beneath him. He turns with the hand crossbow lowered and his shortsword loose in his other hand, and he spits on the slates between you.
>
> > "Go on, then. Every one of you is filthy surface-blood, and I won't thank any of you for a quick end. I would have taken a dozen more before the tide turned."

If the pursuit fails, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The dark shape crosses the last roof and drops out of sight behind a chimney. When you reach the edge, a rope is swinging against the wall and a lantern on a moored boat below is rocking on its hook. Somewhere across the water a splash follows a soft, dry laugh, and then the harbor is quiet again.

> [!social]**The Killer in Hand**
>
> Soluun Xibrindas (Neutral Evil, Drow, he/him) :: a drow gunslinger who serves Bregan D'aerthe with a zealot's devotion.
>
> Soluun shows no remorse and only disgust at being taken by people who aren't even elves. He speaks in hard, contemptuous sentences to anyone he counts as prey and spits when surface elves are named, and his voice rises into something close to fervor when he talks about the band he serves. He swears in rants at elves and half-elves and at any character who looks like one. He touches the forged token at his neck when he says the name of Bregan D'aerthe. He says nothing about being disowned, and he doesn't reveal that Jarlaxle knows what he does. He never apologizes.
>
> Soluun is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Whom he serves: Bregan D'aerthe and "the captain", and nothing more precise.
> - Why he kills surface elves, which he explains with visible relish.
> - The Seven Masks Theater, which he refuses to discuss and where his eyes flick briefly to the playbill.
>
> If a character has Soluun in hand and asks him about the goddess of his people, he answers with open contempt for Lolth, since Bregan D'aerthe rejects her.

> [!qna]**Who are you?**
>
> Soluun touches the silver disc at his neck before he answers.
>
> > "Soluun Xibrindas, of Bregan D'aerthe. I serve the captain, and whatever the surface has heard about me is a story told for the humans. Take me to your Watch. It won't hold me long."

> [!qna]**Why the sailors?**
>
> He looks past you at Heldar, and his lip curls.
>
> > "Because they walk your streets as though the sun belonged to them, and someone has to count them. Filthy fucking half-breed, that one, and you all smell the same. There will be more, whatever you do to me."

> [!qna]**Who sent you?**
>
> > "Nobody sends me. I go where the work is, and the captain knows my work well enough. That's all you'll have from me, so save your breath for the Watch."

> [!qna]**Which god do you pray to?**
>
> > "None. I walked away from that spider-bitch's rotten faith and I've never once missed her. The captain gives me better work than she ever did, and he doesn't ask me to kneel for it."

A captured Soluun gives up nothing else in this Event. A character who searches him, or who examines the playbill in the alley, finds the following.

> [!exploration]**What He Carries**
>
> A silver disc hangs on a cord at Soluun's neck, stamped with a spider caught in its web and crossed by a blade, which is the mark of Bregan D'aerthe. His belt pouch holds loose bolts wrapped in oiled cloth and three spent ones from the last kill. The playbill is stiff with dried blood and advertises *Blood Wedding* at the Seven Masks Theater, with the name "Rongquan Mystere" in small print as the proprietor. If Soluun escaped, the party has the playbill and nothing else, since the disc stays with him.
>
> Any character who handles the disc and is a member of Bregan D'aerthe sees at once that the knotwork is wrong and the finish too polished. The disc is forged, and someone made it for him or he made it himself. Other characters see a spider on a disc and learn nothing more.
>
> Any character who studies the playbill and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (History)** check recalls that the Seven Masks Theater has connections to Bregan D'aerthe. Waterdhavian gossip has long held that the drow mercenary band uses the theater as a meeting place.
>
> - **Auto:** A character who is a member of Bregan D'aerthe knows this without a check.
> - **Advantage:** The character has studied the disc and the playbill side by side.
> - **Failure:** The character can't place the theater's reputation. The party can ask around the Dock Ward's theater trade, or wait until **Faction Outposts** brings the lead back to them, and the check can be retried after a day of asking.
>
> Any character who studies the playbill and makes a successful **DC 14 Intelligence (History)** check also remembers that "Rongquan Mystere" has been in the broadsheets lately for a cultural investment backed by Luskan money.
>
> - **Failure:** The character remembers the name and nothing about the money. Any box-office clerk on the Dock Ward will say for a few coppers that Mystere is the owner and that Luskan gold has paid for the new gilding.

### Hand-off to the Watch

The commotion in the alley draws the Harbor Watch within a few minutes of the last blow. Gorra arrives with two guards, and she takes charge of any prisoner the party hands her.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Lantern light jerks down the alley and stops at the sight of the bodies and the bound man against the wall. Gorra pushes back her hood and looks from the sailor on the cobbles to the party, and then up at the rooftops above you.
>
> > "That's the killer? Gods, you did it. Take him back to the Ship Street cells, lads, and somebody find this one a blanket. And you, all of you, thank you. I won't forget this."

If the party has no prisoner, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Gorra arrives with two guards and lifts her lantern over the empty doorway and the scattered bolts on the cobbles.
>
> > "He got away, then. I'd have liked to see his face, but I'll take a live sailor for one night. Tell me what you can, and I'll take it to my captain in the morning."

If Heldar died, Gorra says nothing about a live sailor. She asks the party to describe everything they saw and thanks them for trying.

### Aftermath

Davil's response depends on what the party brings him.

If Soluun escaped, Davil notes in his book that the killer is still alive and turns to the next matter. Whatever the party learned about the Seven Masks link, Davil doesn't tell them why it interests him.

A captured Soluun sits in a cell at the Ship Street watchhouse for a little over a tenday. Then a solicitor with papers signed "Rongquan Mystere" posts surety, the magistrate signs the release, and Soluun walks out. Gorra is furious about it. Any member who asks her about Soluun after that tenday hears the following:

> [!qna]**What happened to the killer?**
>
> Gorra spits into the gutter before she answers.
>
> > "Somebody paid his surety. A solicitor came in with a signed order from a man called Rongquan Mystere, owner of the Seven Masks, and the magistrate let him walk. The whole watchhouse is furious about it, and there's nothing any of us can do."

If Soluun is dead, the murders stop, and Bregan D'aerthe learns within a tenday who killed him. If he escaped, he stays out of sight for some weeks and then returns to the harbor before Tarsakh 20.

Heldar sails on the morning tide. If he is alive, he leaves a message with the barkeep at the Muleskull thanking the party, and nothing more comes of him in this mission.

## Overview

Someone is killing elf and half-elf sailors in the Dock Ward. Davil Starsong asks the Doom Raiders to stake out the docks and find the killer before he strikes again.

## Summary

We staked out the Muleskull Tavern for three nights, met a tired Harbor Watch officer named Gorra, and followed a half-elf sailor named Heldar into a dark alley. A drow with a crossbow and a bloodstained Seven Masks playbill was waiting for him. Davil looked at the playbill for a long time, and we still don't know why it interested him.
