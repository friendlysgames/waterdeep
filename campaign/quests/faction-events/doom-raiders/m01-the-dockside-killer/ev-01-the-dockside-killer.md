# The Dockside Killer

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event occurs when Davil Starsong briefs the party at the Yawning Portal and sends them to the Dock Ward. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Follow three independent investigation paths along Ship Street to surface the pattern and identify Heldar as the next target.
> - Choose their position before Soluun strikes on Night 3 — or later, at their pace.
> - Intercept Soluun before or during his attack on Heldar.
> - Chase Soluun across the rooftops in a three-check pursuit sequence.
> - Find a forged Bregan D'aerthe token on Soluun's person and bring it to Davil.
>
> #### Combat Phases
>
> Soluun fights until reduced to half his hit points, then attempts to Disengage and go up — a drainpipe, a crate stack, then the roofline above Ship Street. The rooftop pursuit is a separate resolution sequence; see the **Rooftop Pursuit** section under **Soluun Xibrindas**.

> [!gamemaster]**Soluun's Situation**
>
> **Soluun Xibrindas** has been operating in Waterdeep for three tendays. He is renegade — disowned by Jarlaxle before the campaign began for conducting unsanctioned operations that created exposure risk for Bregan D'aerthe's presence in the city. He kills half-elves from personal hatred, with no faction authorization. The forged identification token is his own creation: a small silver disc with a stylized spider motif that does not quite match genuine D'aerthe tokens.
>
> Heldar is a half-elf sailor who drinks alone at the Muleskull Tavern on Ship Street most nights and walks the same route back to his ship each time. He matched the previous three victims in every detail that mattered to Soluun, and Soluun has been watching him for two days. Heldar does not know this.

### The Dock Ward at Night

The waterfront runs on a different clock than the rest of the city. Ships unload at midnight. Cargo handlers work between bells. Ship Street is active enough that a small group moving with purpose draws no attention, but quiet enough that a figure staying still in a shadow eventually becomes visible.

> [!readaloud]
> The Dock Ward runs on its own schedule. Past midnight, cargo crews still move under hanging lanterns, voices low, boots loud on the wet cobbles. The smell of salt, tar, and rope sits heavy in the air. The Muleskull Tavern's windows are lit at the end of the block — the one establishment on Ship Street that never seems to close.

Characters who spend the evening hours on Ship Street and the adjacent waterfront find three independent paths to the same conclusion.

> [!exploration]**The Shadow That Does Not Move**
>
> Any character who watches the waterfront at midnight on Night 2 or later and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check notices that one of the shadows near the Muleskull's side alley has not shifted with the wind off the harbor. Soluun is already in position by the time most of the foot traffic has thinned.

> [!exploration]**The Name Heldar**
>
> Any character who strikes up a conversation with two or more dock workers and makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check learns a specific detail: one of them was drinking with the second victim the night before that one died. That victim's drinking companion — a half-elf named Heldar — has been back at the Muleskull every night since and still walks the same route home. He is at the late table, third bell.

> [!exploration]**The Pattern Across Three Sites**
>
> Any character who examines all three alley locations as a group and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check identifies what they share: all victims were alone, all after midnight, all within three blocks of the Muleskull, all walking toward the eastern wharf. The killer knows this stretch of the docks. He is not choosing victims at random.

### The Ambush

Night 3, or later at the party's pace: Heldar leaves the Muleskull after last bell, moving east on Ship Street with the careful step of someone who has had three drinks too many and is compensating. Soluun is already positioned in the gap between two buildings off Net Street, watching the approach.

> [!readaloud]
> Ship Street at midnight is not quiet, but it has a different kind of noise than the daytime — the creak of rigging, the slap of harbor water, a handful of workers moving cargo under lantern light. The Muleskull Tavern's windows are still lit at the near end of the block.
>
> You have the name Heldar, a route, and whatever position you chose before he left the bar.

A character who stated their intent to position ahead of Heldar's route at the start of the evening gains Advantage on the following check.

> [!exploration]**Spotting Soluun**
>
> Any character who watches Heldar's approach and makes a successful DC 18 Wisdom (Perception) check spots Soluun before he strikes.
>
> - **Advantage:** A character who positioned ahead of the route gains Advantage on this check.
>
> If the check fails, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> A figure drops from above Ship Street — a drow, landing without a sound, his weapon already raised. He moves like someone who has watched this route for several nights running.

> [!gamemaster]**Heldar's Survival**
>
> If Soluun strikes before the party intercepts him, Heldar takes 11 (3d6+1) piercing damage and drops to 0 Hit Points — the wound was meant to stun, not kill, before the follow-through. One character's action stabilizes him without a check. The party still has Soluun to deal with.

> [!hazard]**Soluun Xibrindas**
>
> **Soluun Xibrindas** uses the **Drow Gunslinger** stat block, with Advantage on Stealth checks in dim light or darkness.
>
> #### Soluun's Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, Soluun engages from range, prioritizing whichever character moved to intercept him.
>
> Over the course of combat, Soluun will prioritize:
> - Repositioning into dim light or darkness on each turn, using cover along the building faces.
> - Targeting characters who have placed themselves between him and Heldar.
>
> The battle ends when Soluun is cornered, captured, killed, or driven from the area — or when he breaks for the rooftops at half his hit points.
>
> #### Rooftop Pursuit
>
> When reduced to half his hit points, Soluun attempts to Disengage and run. He goes up — a drainpipe, a crate stack, then the roofline above Ship Street. Any character who pursues must make three DC 14 Strength (Athletics) checks.
>
> - On three successes before three failures, Soluun is cornered above a warehouse on Sail Street with nowhere useful to jump. He surrenders rather than fall.
> - On three failures before three successes, he clears the neighborhood and drops somewhere the party cannot follow.

> [!exploration]**The Forged Token**
>
> After Soluun is captured or his body is searched, the party finds a small silver disc with a stylized spider motif on his person.
>
> Any character who examines the token and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (History) check, or who has prior knowledge of genuine D'aerthe tokens, identifies the discrepancy: the knot work is wrong, the finish too polished. Someone made this for him, or he made it himself.
>
> When shown the token, Davil goes quiet for a moment and does not explain why it interests him.

### Concluding the Event

The Dock Ward murders stop here — or Soluun runs. Either way, the party has a name, a forged token, and a question Davil declines to answer.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Heldar Survived** — mark when Heldar is alive at the end of the scene; award +1 Renown when Soluun is also stopped. Read by **Doom Raiders Mission 2 — The Poisoned Delivery** (Heldar becomes a waterfront contact).
> - **Soluun Captured** — mark when Soluun is taken alive and delivered to the Watch; award the supplementary +1 Renown. Read by **Sea Maidens Faire** (his Watch file is available as intelligence on an unauthorized drow operating under a forged token).
> - **Soluun Escaped** — mark when the rooftop pursuit ends in three failures before three successes. Read by **Sea Maidens Faire** (Soluun watches the ships from the waterfront and recognizes the party on sight; he has had several months to decide how he feels about that).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Heldar, sober the next morning and informed of what happened, pays the party 50 gp from thin savings and is embarrassed that this is all he has. He also knows the docks well: if the party ever needs passage information, ship manifests, or waterfront gossip in later quests, Heldar will help within his considerable limits.
>
> If Soluun escaped: Davil notes it in his file. Soluun resurfaces in **Sea Maidens Faire**, watching the ships from the waterfront.
>
> **Doom Raiders Mission 2 — The Poisoned Delivery** becomes available when the party reaches Renown 2 and character level 3.

## Overview

Three elf and half-elf sailors have been murdered in the Dock Ward. Find the killer before the next victim dies.

## Summary

We tracked down the killer behind three Dock Ward murders — a disowned drow named Soluun, operating without his faction's knowledge. Heldar survived the night. On Soluun's person: a forged Bregan D'aerthe token that Davil pocketed without comment.
