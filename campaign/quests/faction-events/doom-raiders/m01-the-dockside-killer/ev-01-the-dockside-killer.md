# The Dockside Killer

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event occurs when Davil Starsong briefs the party at the Yawning Portal and sends them to the Dock Ward. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Davil in person and ask him what he knows.
> - Follow three independent investigation paths along Ship Street to surface the pattern and identify Heldar as the next target.
> - Choose their position before Soluun strikes on Night 3 — or later, at their pace.
> - Intercept Soluun before or during his attack on Heldar.
> - Chase Soluun across the rooftops in a three-check pursuit sequence.
> - Find a forged Bregan D'aerthe token on Soluun's person and bring it to Davil.
>
> #### Combat Phases
>
> Soluun fights until reduced to half his hit points, then attempts to Disengage and go up — a drainpipe, a crate stack, then the roofline above Ship Street. The rooftop pursuit is a separate resolution sequence; see the **Rooftop Pursuit** section under **Soluun Xibrindas**.

> [!gamemaster]**Soluun's Situation**
>
> **Soluun Xibrindas** has been operating in Waterdeep for three tendays. He is renegade, disowned by Jarlaxle before the campaign began for conducting unsanctioned operations that created exposure risk for Bregan D'aerthe's presence in the city. He kills half-elves from personal hatred, with no faction authorization. The forged identification token is his own creation: a small silver disc with a stylized spider motif that does not quite match genuine D'aerthe tokens.
>
> Heldar is a half-elf sailor who drinks alone at the Muleskull Tavern on Ship Street most nights and walks the same route back to his ship each time. He matched the previous three victims in every detail that mattered to Soluun, and Soluun has been watching him for two days. Heldar does not know this.
>
> Davil does not know who the killer is when he briefs the party. He has a working theory he declines to share. When the party shows Davil the token, he immediately recognizes its significance, both that it is forged and what that implies about Bregan D'aerthe's presence in the city. He will not explain why.

### The Brief

Davil Starsong receives the party in his curtained room on the second floor of the Yawning Portal, and he is at the table with a second cup poured when they step through the curtain.

> [!readaloud]
> The curtain to the back room parts before you knock, and Davil Starsong is at the table filling a second cup, glancing up as you step through. His lute rests against the wall beside him, and the small charm bracelet at his wrist catches the candlelight as he sets down the bottle.
>
> > "Sit. Please."
>
> He waits for you to settle, then sets his cup down and folds his hands on the table.
>
> > "Three sailors found dead in the Dock Ward, all of them elves or half-elves, one each night for three nights, in different alleys off Ship Street, after midnight and alone each time. The Watch opened a file and set it aside almost immediately, because there was nothing there they were prepared to pursue."
>
> He turns his cup in his hands.
>
> > "I kept my people out of it until I could see a pattern, and now I can. The care in it is not accidental: the same stretch of the docks, the same hour, always alone, always well after midnight. Whoever this is has been deliberate about it, and there will be another one. You understand that as well as I do, yes?"
>
> He looks at you across the table with easy warmth.
>
> > "I need someone who can move through the docks at night without appearing to work them, and my own people can't manage that in this part of the city without drawing exactly the kind of notice I'd rather avoid. You don't have that difficulty. Find whoever this is and stop the next one, and I will consider that a very real service, yes?"

> [!social]**The Master of Opportunities**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: a sun elf fixer who presents favors as gifts and debts as opportunities.
>
> Davil made up his mind to offer this job before the party sat down. He is precise about the facts and warm toward the party, with the easy manner of someone who has sized people up for a living and likes what he sees. He turns the charm bracelet at his wrist when he is weighing something, and he lets silences sit rather than filling them. His lute stays on the wall during business.
>
> When he speaks, he frames everything as opportunity and as helping people who can't help themselves. He ends many of his sentences on a gentle question — "yes?" — not to seek confirmation but to include the listener. He does not yet call the party "my friends"; that comes after they've completed a job for him.
>
> He will not discuss his sources, whether he has a suspect, or what the forged token means when the party finds it. He is not forthcoming about why he kept his network out of the investigation until he had a pattern.
>
> Conversation topics Davil is willing to discuss include:
> - The three murders: who was killed, where, and when.
> - Why he needs outsiders rather than his own people.
> - Where to start the investigation.
> - The Doom Raiders' interest in stability in the Dock Ward.
>
> Any character who makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that Davil has a working theory about the killer's identity and is more concerned than his manner suggests, though he clearly declines to share what he knows.
>
> Specific dialogue for Davil on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Who were the victims?**
>
> > "Three sailors, all of them elves or half-elves," he says, "found in alleys off Ship Street, one each night for three nights, after midnight and alone each time." He pauses. "The Watch found nothing because there was nothing left to find. Whoever did this was patient about it, and patience worries me more than anger ever has, yes?"

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> > "My people are known in that part of the city," he says with a small rueful smile, "and not warmly. Someone keeping watch on those docks with any kind of Zhentarim association draws exactly the questions I'd rather not be fielding right now. You don't have that complication. Yet."

> [!qna]**What do you know about the killer?**
>
> > "Someone careful and patient," he says, "who has worked the same stretch of the docks at the same hour with targets who were always alone. That kind of consistency takes planning, and it takes familiarity with the ground, and you don't find the right place three nights running without having been there before." He turns his cup again. "I think that covers what I know, yes?"
>
> He turns the charm bracelet over once and leaves it at that.

> [!qna]**Where do we start?**
>
> > "Ship Street is where I'd start," he says. "Walk it at night, beginning at the Muleskull Tavern on the near end, since all three died within a few blocks of it, and the sites will tell you more together than they do separately. I'd look at them as a group rather than one at a time, yes?"

> [!qna]**The pay?**
>
> Davil's fingers find the charm bracelet at his wrist and turn one of the small trophies between his fingers.
>
> > "You will be compensated," he says. "There is coin in it, and beyond that my good opinion opens doors in this city that don't otherwise open. We can sort out the details when the work is done."

Once the party accepts, proceed to **The Dock Ward at Night**.

### The Dock Ward at Night

The waterfront runs on a different clock than the rest of the city, with ships unloading at midnight and cargo handlers working between bells. Ship Street is active enough that a small group moving with purpose draws no attention, but quiet enough that a figure staying still in a shadow eventually becomes visible.

> [!readaloud]
> The Dock Ward does not sleep when the rest of the city does, and past midnight the cargo crews are still working under hanging lanterns, voices low and boots loud on the wet cobbles, the smell of salt and tar and rope heavy in the air. The Muleskull Tavern is the only place on Ship Street with its windows still lit.

Characters who spend the evening hours on Ship Street and the adjacent waterfront find three independent paths to the same conclusion.

> [!exploration]**The Shadow That Does Not Move**
>
> Any character who watches the waterfront at midnight on Night 2 or later and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check notices that one of the shadows near the Muleskull's side alley has not shifted with the wind off the harbor. Soluun is in position before the foot traffic thins, and he does not move.
>
> > The figure in the gap between those buildings has not moved in twenty minutes. The dock workers have passed it twice without a second glance, and the shadow it casts does not shift with the wind off the harbor.

> [!exploration]**The Name Heldar**
>
> Any character who strikes up a conversation with two or more dock workers and makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check learns that one of them was drinking with the second victim the night before that one died.
>
> > "Heldar? Yeah, I know him. Half-elf, works the eastern wharf — he was drinking with the second one they found, the night before it happened, sitting right there at that table. He's been back every night since, same table, leaves around third bell, takes the same route back to his ship every time. I don't know why he keeps walking that way. I'd have taken a different fucking street by now."
>
> Heldar is at the Muleskull Tavern, late table, third bell.

> [!exploration]**The Pattern Across Three Sites**
>
> Any character who examines all three alley locations as a group and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check identifies the pattern among them: all three victims were alone, all after midnight, all within three blocks of the Muleskull, all walking toward the eastern wharf.
>
> > All three of the alleys are off the same stretch of Ship Street, within a few blocks of the Muleskull Tavern, and each victim was alone and well after midnight. Someone chose each of these locations deliberately and knew them before arriving.
>
> The killer knows this stretch of the docks and has not been choosing targets at random.

### The Ambush

Night 3, or later at the party's pace: Heldar leaves the Muleskull after last bell and moves east on Ship Street with the careful step of someone who has had three drinks too many and is compensating for it. Soluun has taken his position in the gap between two buildings off Net Street and is watching the approach.

> [!readaloud]
> Ship Street at midnight is quieter than the daytime but never truly still, and the rigging of the ships at anchor creaks in the wind off the harbor while a handful of workers move the last of the evening's cargo under lantern light. The Muleskull Tavern's windows are still lit at the near end of the block. You know Heldar's name and the route he takes home from there, and you're settled into the position you chose before last call.

A character who stated their intent to position ahead of Heldar's route at the start of the evening gains Advantage on the following check.

> [!exploration]**Spotting Soluun**
>
> Any character who watches Heldar's approach and makes a successful DC 18 Wisdom (Perception) check spots Soluun before he strikes.
>
> - **Advantage:** A character who positioned ahead of the route gains Advantage on this check.

If the check succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> The shadow in the gap between the buildings off Net Street has been there too long. The others shift with the wind off the harbor, but this one stays fixed. A figure is pressed flat against the wall, watching Heldar's approach, and he has not moved in several minutes.

If the check fails, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> A figure drops from above Ship Street, a drow, landing without a sound on the cobbles and bringing up a hand crossbow in the same motion. He has his eyes on Heldar and his back to the nearest shadow.

> [!gamemaster]**Heldar's Survival**
>
> If Soluun strikes before the party intercepts him, Heldar takes 11 (3d6+1) piercing damage and drops to 0 Hit Points, since the wound was meant to stun before the follow-through rather than kill outright. One character's action stabilizes him without a check, and the party still has Soluun to deal with.

> [!hazard]**Soluun Xibrindas**
>
> **Soluun Xibrindas** uses the **Scout** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*), with Advantage on Stealth checks in dim light or darkness and a hand crossbow substituted for the shortbow.
>
> #### Soluun's Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, Soluun engages from range, prioritizing whichever character moved to intercept him.
>
> Over the course of combat, Soluun will prioritize:
> - Repositioning into dim light or darkness on each turn, using cover along the building faces.
> - Targeting characters who have placed themselves between him and Heldar.
>
> The battle ends when Soluun is cornered, captured, killed, or driven from the area, or when he breaks for the rooftops at half his hit points.
>
> #### Rooftop Pursuit
>
> When reduced to half his hit points, Soluun attempts to Disengage and run, going up a drainpipe, then a crate stack, then the roofline above Ship Street. Any character who pursues must make three DC 14 Strength (Athletics) checks.
>
> - On three successes before three failures, Soluun is cornered above a warehouse on Sail Street with nowhere useful to jump. He surrenders rather than fall.
> - On three failures before three successes, he clears the neighborhood and drops somewhere the party cannot follow.

If the pursuit succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> The drow reaches the edge above Sail Street and stops there, looking down at the drop and then back at you, with nowhere useful left to go from where he is standing. After a moment, he raises his hands.

If the pursuit fails, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> He clears the gap between the buildings and lands on the far roof without slowing down. By the time you reach the edge, he has dropped below the roofline on the far side and is gone, and the harbor spreads out dark and wide below.

> [!exploration]**The Forged Token**
>
> After Soluun is captured or his body is searched, the party finds a small silver disc on his person.
>
> > It is a silver disc, about the size of a large coin, with one face etched in a stylized spider motif and the knotwork very precise, the finish smooth and even. The overall effect is of something crafted carefully to look official.
>
> Any character who examines the token and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (History) check, or who has prior knowledge of genuine D'aerthe tokens, can see that the knotwork is wrong and the finish is too polished for something meant to be carried and used in the field. Someone made this for him, or he made it himself.
>
> When shown the token, Davil goes quiet for a moment and does not explain why it interests him.

### Concluding the Event

The Dock Ward murders stop here, or Soluun runs with his work unfinished. Either way, the party has a name, a forged token, and a question Davil declines to answer.

If the **Heldar Survived** outcome is marked, read or paraphrase the following the next morning:

> [!readaloud]
> Heldar is at the waterfront the next morning, sitting on a dock post with the sober, careful stillness of someone who slept badly, and when he sees you he gets to his feet too quickly and has to catch himself.
>
> > "I — shit, I don't — they told me what you did." He holds out a small purse. "Fifty gold. It's all I've got on me right now. I know it's not near enough, just — take it, will you?"

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Heldar Survived** — mark when Heldar is alive at the end of the scene; award +1 Renown when Soluun is also stopped. Read by **Doom Raiders Mission 2 — The Poisoned Delivery** (Heldar becomes a waterfront contact).
> - **Soluun Captured** — mark when Soluun is taken alive and delivered to the Watch; award the supplementary +1 Renown. Read by **Sea Maidens Faire** (his Watch file is available as intelligence on an unauthorized drow operating under a forged token).
> - **Soluun Escaped** — mark when the rooftop pursuit ends in three failures before three successes. Read by **Sea Maidens Faire** (Soluun watches the ships from the waterfront and recognizes the party on sight; he has had several months to decide how he feels about that).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Heldar also knows the docks well. If the party ever needs passage information, ship manifests, or waterfront gossip in later quests, he will help within his considerable limits.
>
> If Soluun escaped: Davil notes it in his file, and Soluun resurfaces in **Sea Maidens Faire**, watching the ships from the waterfront.
>
> **Doom Raiders Mission 2 — The Poisoned Delivery** becomes available when the party reaches Renown 2 and character level 3.

## Overview

Three elf and half-elf sailors have been murdered in the Dock Ward. Find the killer before the next victim dies.

## Summary

We took a job from Davil Starsong: find whoever had been killing elf and half-elf sailors in the Dock Ward and stop the next murder. We staked out Ship Street, found the pattern, and waited on the night Soluun came for Heldar, the sailor who fit it. On Soluun's person: a forged Bregan D'aerthe token that Davil pocketed without comment.
