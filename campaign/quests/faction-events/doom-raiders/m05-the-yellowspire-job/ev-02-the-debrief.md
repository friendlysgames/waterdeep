# The Debrief

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs after **Davil's Return**, when an individual Doom Raiders member has reached Renown 10 and 6th level and **Yellowspire Raided** is marked. The party already went inside Yellowspire during **Faction Outposts**, so everything **The Yellowspire Job** would send them to fetch is already theirs, and the Doom Raiders ask for a report instead of a second raid. If **Yellowspire Raided** is not marked, run **The Yellowspire Job** and skip this Event. Only Doom Raiders members attend. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Sit down with Davil Starsong, Tashlyn Yafeera, Istrid Horn and Yagra Stonefist, and report what they found at Yellowspire.
> - Hand over the relay ledger and the coded letters, if they took them.
> - Answer Tashlyn's questions about the tower, and hear how the inner circle reacts.
> - Collect a payment from Istrid and an offer from Yagra.
> - Receive Ziraj's notes on the Kolat Towers force field at Corellon's Crown.

### The Summons

At dusk on the day the member qualifies, a flying snake with silvery scales brings a note to the member's lodgings that says only "The Portal. Tonight." The note is in Davil's looping hand. This Event reads **Davil Released** from **Davil's Return**. If the release terms left his suite sealed (release count 0 or 1, for the first ten days), the meeting is in the back booth behind the Yawning Portal's kitchens, and the booth is crowded. Otherwise it is in his curtained room on the second floor.

> [!gamemaster]**Before You Run This**
>
> Three Faction Outposts outcomes decide what the members bring to the table. The **Faction Outposts** conversion must write them when the party goes through Yellowspire.
>
> - **Yellowspire Ledger Taken** — the party removed the relay ledger from the circle chamber.
> - **Yellowspire Letters Taken** — the party removed the three coded letters from Amath Seccent's desk.
> - **Yellowspire Clean Exit** — the party left without the circle pulsing, a coin dropping in the call bowl, or Agorn Fuoco escaping.
>
> Until **Manshoon Named** is marked, every Doom Raider says "Floxin's cell", "the other cell" or "the Splinter". If **Floxin Status** from **Gralhund Villa** is Dead or Captured, they say "the other cell" and never "Floxin's cell". The truth is that Manshoon directs the Splinter from Kolat Towers, and Davil and Tashlyn may say so only after **Manshoon Named** is marked. The Skeemo outcomes from **Silencing Skeemo** change a few lines, and each change is noted where it occurs.

If the suite is unsealed, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil's room smells of cedar and warm wine, and four chairs have been pulled close around the round table. Tashlyn stands beside the curtain with her hands behind her back, Istrid sits with a ledger closed on her knee, and Yagra leans in the corner with a tankard. Davil pours for each of you before he says a word.

If the suite is still sealed, read the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The back booth behind the Yawning Portal's kitchens smells of frying onions and spilled ale, and it holds far more people than it was built for. Tashlyn stands at the mouth of the booth with her hands behind her back, Istrid has squeezed onto the bench with a ledger closed on her knee, and Yagra fills the corner with a tankard. Davil pours for each of you before he says a word.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil turns a small silver trophy on his bracelet and looks from face to face.
>
> > "I've heard that you went into the tower in the Castle Ward, and I'd like to hear it from you in your own words. Tashlyn has watched that place for two months and never once got past the door, so please forgive her if she questions you closely, yes?"

### Reporting Back

The members tell the table what they found. Play the report as a conversation and let the players choose what to say. The table reacts to what was taken.

> [!social]**The Inner Circle**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: the leader of the Doom Raiders, who makes every arrangement sound like a favor and listens longer than he speaks.
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: the Master of Arms and Mercenaries, who tests every report against her own watching.
>
> Istrid Horn (Lawful Evil, Shield Dwarf, she/her) :: the Master of Trade and Coin, who prices the information before she thanks anyone for it.
>
> Yagra Stonefist (Neutral, Half-Orc, she/her) :: Davil's bodyguard, who swears cheerfully and wants to know which door to kick.
>
> Davil speaks for the table and asks the first question. Tashlyn cuts in with short orders. Istrid waits until the end and then names a figure. Yagra asks only whether there is anyone left to hit.

> [!qna]**What did you take?**
>
> Davil leans forward and sets his glass down.
>
> > "Start with whatever you carried out of the tower, and then tell me what you left behind you. I don't mind a long answer, because I have been waiting a good while for this one."

If **Yellowspire Ledger Taken** is marked, read or paraphrase the following when the members hand over the ledger:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil takes the thin leather book in both hands and turns the pages one at a time while his bracelet trophies rattle softly against the table. Tashlyn reads over his shoulder, and Istrid watches the pages as though she could see the price on each one.
>
> > "Three carriers, and every handoff in a public place. Somebody thought that made them harder to take, and I suppose they were right. We know now where the amulets are and how they move, and we'll decide together how we want to take them."

If **Yellowspire Letters Taken** is marked, read or paraphrase the following when the members hand over the letters:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn takes the letters without a word and reads the first three lines. She reads them again more slowly, folds them along their creases and slides them into her coat.
>
> > "Blackwater is the officer who sends out their strike teams, and I can prove it now. I heard the name once before, and I wanted to see it written down by their own hand."

If **Skeemo Letters Recovered** is marked, Davil adds that the three letters corroborate the three paper-bird letters Tashlyn already decoded, and Tashlyn says that Vevette Blackwater has now been named twice by two different hands.

If the members took neither, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil lets the silence sit for a moment and turns the trophy on his bracelet once.
>
> > "Then you saw the place and you didn't empty it, and I don't count that as a disgrace in any way. A tower like that has more ways to hurt you than a ledger has pages, and I'd rather have you alive. Tell me what you did see, and we'll work with it, yes?"

> [!exploration]**Tashlyn's Questions**
>
> Tashlyn checks the report against two months of her own watching, and she asks each member one question about the tower. The questions cover the layout (how many floors and what is below), the people (how many acolytes, and who visits), and the circle (where the plate is and what happens if it is unpressed). Each member answers from what their character saw.
>
> - A member who answers honestly and fully satisfies her.
> - A member who lacks the detail can recover it with a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check, recalling the room from their character's own notes. On a failure, Tashlyn makes a short note and moves on, and the report still counts.
> - A member who lies to her makes a DC 15 Charisma (Deception) check against her passive Insight of 15. On a failure, she says "Again" and waits until the member tells the truth.
>
> She does not withhold the base Renown for a muddled account.

> [!qna]**What did you learn about the place?**
>
> Tashlyn answers for the table, and Istrid makes a small note.
>
> > "You found a Banite priestess with four followers, a circle in the cellar and a pressure plate that calls for help. I knew the first two things and only suspected the third. The plate is what I wanted to learn, and you have told me exactly how it works."

### What the Inner Circle Does

Istrid pays for the information and Yagra makes an offer.

> [!social]**Istrid Horn**
>
> Istrid is pleased and does not hide it. She speaks in measured, complete sentences, states her figure and repeats it once. She calls the members "dear", taps her ledger when she names an amount, and blesses every payment with "Vergadain smile on it".
>
> She pays 100 gp to each participating member for the report. If **Yellowspire Ledger Taken** is marked, she pays an extra 50 gp to each of them, because the ledger is worth more to her than to anyone else at the table. She pays in coin on the spot.

> [!qna]**What do you think it's worth?**
>
> Istrid taps the ledger once with a finger.
>
> > "Information that saves us a month of watching is worth a hundred gold to each of you, dear, and I'm happy to say it again. If you brought the book out with you, that is worth fifty more gold to each of you. Vergadain smile on it."

> [!social]**Yagra Stonefist**
>
> Yagra wants to be useful and is bored by reports. She says what she thinks in short, blunt sentences and swears in almost every one. She offers to go back to the tower with the members if Davil wants it cleared, and she offers again when Davil declines. She never lies to a friend.

> [!qna]**Should we go back?**
>
> Yagra cracks her knuckles one hand at a time.
>
> > "Fuck yes, I want to go back there. You said five of them and one fucking priest, so I'll kick the door in and we'll have the cellar cleared by supper."

Davil declines her offer, and the table agrees that the tower is a door the Doom Raiders would rather keep than burn.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil waits until Yagra sits down again and shakes his head with a small smile.
>
> > "Not yet, Yagra, please. That circle is the only door we have that doesn't need an amulet, and I'd like it to stay open until we need it. Let them go on thinking they're safe for another few weeks, yes?"

If **Yellowspire Clean Exit** is marked, Tashlyn adds that the circle has not been sealed and the relay has not been rebuilt, which is exactly what the Doom Raiders need. If it is not marked, Tashlyn says the other cell rebuilt the tower after the raid, and the party should assume Kolat Towers knows the circle was used.

If **Skeemo Captured** is marked, Davil mentions that Skeemo gave up the tower's knock and the plate, and Tashlyn says the report matches what he told her.

### Ziraj's Notes

Davil asked Ziraj to hand his surveillance notes on the Kolat Towers force field to whoever went into Yellowspire, whether or not they carried anything out. Ziraj shows them to Doom Raiders members the next morning at the back table of Corellon's Crown in Trollskull Alley. Companions may sit at the next table and get nothing.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Corellon's Crown smells of dried mint and horehound. A wood elf in an apron sets a pot of tea and four cups on the back table, and a huge half-orc sits against the wall with a stack of bark-paper sheets in front of him. Every sheet is covered in rows of tally marks and small drawings of tower walls.
>
> > "Drink this first. It's mint and horehound, and you look as though you need it."

> [!social]**Ziraj and Fala**
>
> Ziraj the Hunter (Lawful Evil, Half-Orc, he/him) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Assassination, who speaks in two to five words and reports in counts.
>
> Fala Lefaliir (Chaotic Good, Wood Elf, they/them) :: the herbalist who shelters him and pours tea before anyone speaks.
>
> Ziraj nods instead of saying yes and answers only what he is asked. Fala fills the silences and stares without blinking while the party talks. Neither will say more about Ziraj than the notes show.

> [!qna]**Why did you watch the towers?**
>
> Ziraj slides the top sheet across the table.
>
> > "Davil asked."

> [!qna]**How long did you watch?**
>
> > "Twenty nights."

> [!qna]**Can you read this for us?**
>
> Fala turns the sheet and traces a line with one finger.
>
> > "He writes in his own marks, and I've learned them because he taught me. Each cluster is a night, each tally is a gap, and each drawing shows where on the wall it opened. I'd rather not explain it twice, so please listen. Drink this first."

> [!exploration]**The Force Field Notes**
>
> A character who studies the notes for ten minutes and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check reads them without help. Fala reads them out loud if no one succeeds.
>
> The notes show three gaps in the force field on the upper walls of the towers. Each gap opens for 60 seconds and closes again, and a different gap opens two hours later. The cycle repeats every six hours: the first gap opens at dusk, the second two hours later, the third two hours after that, and the first again at the next six-hour mark. Each gap is at upper-floor height, and one is level with the bridge between the towers. The notes mark each gap's position on a rough sketch. The party keeps the sketch, which the **Kolat Towers** quest calls the force-field gap diagram.
>
> The notes also record that the gaps slid by a quarter of an hour on two nights when the garrison walked its posts twice, and Ziraj underlined both entries.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Ziraj taps the underlined entries with one thick finger and looks at Fala, who nods.
>
> > "They move."

### Renown Opportunities

Each participating Doom Raiders member gains 4 base Renown for delivering the Yellowspire information to Davil, whatever the party took out of the tower.

- **+1 Renown:** **Yellowspire Ledger Taken** is marked and the ledger is handed to Davil.
- **+1 Renown:** **Yellowspire Letters Taken** is marked and the letters are handed to Tashlyn.
- **+1 Renown:** **Yellowspire Clean Exit** is marked.

Each bonus is earned once, when its condition is complete. Helping companions gain no Doom Raiders Renown.

### Aftermath

Davil keeps the ledger in a locked drawer in his room at the Yawning Portal, and Tashlyn files the letters with the rest of her records. Istrid's coin is in the members' pockets, and Ziraj's sketch is in the party's hands. The Doom Raiders have nothing more to ask of the party until Kolat Towers falls.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the members have reported to the inner circle and received Ziraj's notes.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Relay Ledger Recovered** — mark when **Yellowspire Ledger Taken** is marked and a member hands the ledger to Davil; read by **Kolat Towers** Scene 1 (the Doom Raiders briefing) and Scene 3 (entry by captured amulets, where the ledger's refresh marks grant Advantage on the recalibration check).
> - **Vevette Letters Recovered** — mark when **Yellowspire Letters Taken** is marked and a member hands the letters to Tashlyn; read by **Kolat Towers** Scene 1, where Tashlyn names Vevette Blackwater's strike-team schedule.
> - **Force Field Gap Intel** — mark when the notes are in the party's hands; read by **Kolat Towers** Scene 1 and Scene 2 (the Rooftop Force-Field Gaps entry method).

The Doom Raiders ask what the party found when it went into Yellowspire during Faction Outposts, pay for the information and hand over Ziraj's notes on the Kolat Towers force field.

## Summary

We told Davil, Tashlyn, Istrid and Yagra what we found in Yellowspire, and they paid us for it. Ziraj gave us his notes on the gaps in the force field around Kolat Towers, and the circle in the cellar stays open for when we need it.
