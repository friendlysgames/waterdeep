# The Yellowspire Job

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration Event occurs after **Davil's Return**, when Davil Starsong asks an individual Doom Raiders member who has reached Renown 10 and 6th level to rob a Zhentarim relay tower in the Castle Ward. Only Doom Raiders members receive the brief and the debrief, and helping companions can join the fieldwork. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Davil and Tashlyn Yafeera explain the job, and learn whether Yellowspire has been rebuilt since the party last visited.
> - Watch the tower for a day and choose between the coded knock, the outer wall and a false referral to get inside.
> - Recover the relay ledger from the circle chamber in the cellar, past a pressure plate that alerts Kolat Towers.
> - Take three coded letters from Amath Seccent's desk, which name Vevette Blackwater as the coordinator of the Splinter's strike teams.
> - Leave before the reinforcements arrive through the teleportation circle, fight them, or wreck the circle.
> - Collect Ziraj's notes on the Kolat Towers force field, which Davil has asked him to hand over once the job is done.

### The Brief

At dusk on the day the member qualifies, a flying snake with silvery scales brings a note to the member's lodgings that says only "The Portal. Tonight." The note is in Davil's looping hand, which the member has not seen since before the arrest. Davil receives the members in his room on the second floor, and Tashlyn Yafeera stands by the curtain because she never sits during a briefing.

> [!gamemaster]**Which Yellowspire Is This?**
>
> Yellowspire is the tower from **Faction Outposts** (5B), and the Doom Raiders have been watching it for two months. This mission is a return visit for any party that has already been inside, so choose the state before the brief.
>
> - **First visit.** If **Yellowspire Raided** is not marked, use the layout, scaffolding and single floor plate described below, and use the first-visit column of the roster table.
> - **Return visit.** If **Yellowspire Raided** is marked, Amath's cell rebuilt the relay after the party's raid. The scaffolding has been taken down, the circle chamber has a second lock and a second floor plate, a pull-cord bell hangs inside the hoist door, more guards stay on site, and a larger group waits at Kolat Towers. Use the return-visit column of the roster table.
> - **Skeemo escaped.** If **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** is marked, Skeemo reached Kolat Towers through this very circle and told the Splinter what he knew about the Doom Raiders. Amath has been warned. The knock and the week's word have changed (they also change on a return visit), every DC in **Getting In** is 2 higher, the acolytes sleep in their robes, and the referral cover in **Approach Three** is a trap.
> - **Skeemo Captured.** If **Skeemo Captured** is marked, Tashlyn holds him and he gave her the current coded knock and a warning about the northeast plate. On a first visit, the knock beat in **Approach One** succeeds automatically and the northeast plate is known without a check. On a return visit, Skeemo's knowledge predates the rebuild, so the lead covers only the old northeast plate, and the new knock and second plate must be found. Tashlyn passes the leads on in the brief.
> - **Skeemo Silenced.** If **Skeemo Silenced** is marked, he is dead and nothing changes.
> - **Skeemo Letters Recovered.** If **Skeemo Letters Recovered** is marked, Tashlyn has already decoded three paper-bird letters that name Vevette Blackwater. Davil mentions this in the brief, and Tashlyn wants the letters in the desk to show what Vevette has ordered since. The recovered letters corroborate the paper-bird letters, and the **Vevette Letters Recovered** bonus is unchanged.
> - **Watch File Opened.** If **Watch File Opened** is marked, the Watch is tracking Doom Raiders movements. Castle Ward patrols are heavier, and a Watch pair now walks the lane from midnight to dawn, so deep night is no longer quiet. The pair passes the tower's door every half hour, and a character on the outer wall or at the door must make a DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check in each such pass or be seen. A seen party must be gone or explain itself to the pair, who arrest no one without a complaint from Amath, and she will not complain because the Watch would find the handprints. The pair records the names of anyone it sees.
> - **Manshoon Named.** Until **Manshoon Named** is marked, every Doom Raider says "Floxin's cell", "the other cell" or "the Splinter", and calls Kolat Towers the other cell's stronghold. The truth is that Manshoon lives there and directs Urstul Floxin from behind him. If **Manshoon Named** is marked, Davil and Tashlyn may use the name.

He is thinner than he was before Castle Waterdeep, and he still pours for each of you before he says a word. Tashlyn stands beside the curtain with her hands behind her back.

There is an old tower in the Castle Ward with pale yellow brick, and a priestess of Bane lives in it who keeps a teleportation circle in her cellar, yes?

### Watching the Tower

The party can spend a day watching Yellowspire from the lane, from a rented upper window or from the chandler's shop across the street. Daylight watching shows the door and the hoist door, and dusk shows the prayer hour. The party does not need to spend more than one day, and a second day changes nothing except the risk that a neighbor mentions strangers to Amath.

> [!exploration]**What a Day Shows**
>
> The party learns each of the following through two routes, so missing one check never leaves a gap.
>
> - **The door.** A stone-faced acolyte opens a peephole shutter on the outer door and speaks to each visitor before letting anyone in. Any character who watches the door for four hours and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check hears the knock and the word exchanged. The party already has the old knock from Davil, so this check matters only when **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** or **Yellowspire Raided** is marked, because both changed the knock and the word, and the DC is then 14.
> - **The prayer hour.** At dusk, candlelight fills the arrow slits of the upper level and low chanting carries into the lane for about an hour. Any character who watches from the lane and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check learns that every acolyte on duty is upstairs during that hour. The chandler across the street tells the same story to anyone who buys a candle for 5 sp.
> - **The outer wall.** On a first visit, wooden scaffolding covered in frost and pigeon droppings runs up the outside, and a plank hoist door on the upper level is visible from the roof of the building next door. Any character who studies the tower and makes a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the door. On a return visit, the scaffolding is gone, and the same check reveals the hoist door and a bell cord hanging beside it.
> - **The night.** Every night the door is locked at midnight, and the acolytes on duty sleep on cots on the upper level except one, who stays awake at the ground level. Any character who watches until dawn and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check confirms this. The chandler adds that the priestess has never once left the tower after dark.

> [!gamemaster]**Who Is in the Tower**
>
> Amath Seccent and her four acolytes live and work here, and the Splinter posts guards according to the size of the party. Use the table for the party's state.
>
> | Party size | First visit | Return visit | Spies through the circle (first / return) |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 3 | Amath, 4 acolytes | Amath, 4 acolytes, 1 Tough | 2 / 3 |
> | 4 | Amath, 4 acolytes, 1 Tough | Amath, 4 acolytes, 1 Tough, 1 Warrior Veteran | 3 / 3 |
> | 5 | Amath, 4 acolytes, 1 Tough, 1 Warrior Veteran | Amath, 4 acolytes, 2 Tough, 1 Warrior Veteran | 3 / 4 |
>
> Amath uses the 2024 **Priest** statistics, her acolytes use the 2024 **Priest Acolyte** statistics, and the guards use the 2024 **Tough** and **Warrior Veteran** statistics. The reinforcements that come through the circle are 2024 **Spies**. The **Doom Raiders Mechanics Reference** audits both rosters.
>
> Where they stand depends on the hour.
>
> - **Working hours** (dawn to dusk, and from the end of the prayer hour to midnight): Amath is at her desk on the upper level. One acolyte watches the door from the ground level, one plays cards in the cellar, and the other two stay with Amath. The guards, if any, stand in the vestibule and the ground level.
> - **Prayer hour** (dusk to one hour after): Amath and all four acolytes are on the upper level, and the guards stay at the door. The ground level and the cellar are empty. The stairs carry sound upward.
> - **Deep night** (midnight to dawn): Amath and all but one acolyte sleep on cots on the upper level. The awake acolyte sits by the ground-level fireplace. The guards sleep in the vestibule.
>
> The three black flying snakes that nest in the rafters hiss at strangers and fly to the arrow slits when anyone fights on the upper level, but Amath uses them for short errands and not as an alarm.

> [!gamemaster]**Agorn Fuoco**
>
> On a first visit, Agorn Fuoco (Neutral Evil, Human, he/him) is in the tower only if the party enters during working hours after dusk, when he sits by the ground-level fire and tunes a lute. He visits on those evenings to see Amath. He is not in the tower on a return visit, because the party's earlier raid frightened him into hiding. He never fights and has no stat block, and he flees the moment a fight starts. If the party catches him, or he is present when they enter peacefully, he offers his pass-amulet and Amath's schedule for his life before anyone asks. If he escapes, he does not use the circle, but the Splinter learns of the raid within a day.

### The Tower

The tower is narrow and old, and its bricks have a yellowish tinge that gives Yellowspire its name. Its rooms are stacked inside it in the following order.

- **The vestibule.** The outer door has a peephole with an iron shutter on the inside, and the walls are hung with rotting tapestries. Black handprints, right hands with the fingers held together, cover every inside surface of the tower and mark it as a temple of Bane.
- **The ground level.** A stone spiral stair curls up into the tower and down into the cellar, and a soot-blackened fireplace stands in the west wall.
- **The upper level.** This was a wizard's laboratory before Amath converted it. It has arrow slits, a rocking chair, a candle-lit shrine, Amath's writing desk and a row of cots. The three coded letters are in the desk's locked drawer.
- **The cellar.** A card table with four barrel stools stands in the middle of the room, and three crates of rations are stacked against the south wall. A sturdy oak door with a built-in lock stands in the north wall.
- **The circle chamber.** Behind the oak door, permanent arcane runes are inscribed in a circle on the floor. A brass call bowl is set into the ring's edge. An iron-bound relay box is bolted to the east wall. In the northeast corner is a flagstone that is slightly paler than the others.

The tower has two floors above ground and a cellar with the circle chamber beyond it. The scaffolding on a first visit runs up the outside as far as the upper level.

### Getting In

The party can use any approach or combine them. Each approach has several steps, and a failed step never ends the mission. It costs time and attention, and the party can fall back to another approach.

> [!gamemaster]**When an Approach Fails**
>
> If the party fails the door exchange in **Approach One** or is heard on the boards in **Approach Two**, the acolytes rise, close the shutters and bar the door. Every DC in **Getting In** rises by 2 for the rest of the visit, and the acolytes sleep armed if the party waits until deep night. This penalty stacks with the Skeemo penalty. If the party abandons the tower and comes back a day later, the penalty lasts until the next dusk.

#### Approach One: The Coded Knock

> [!exploration]**The Door Exchange**
>
> The door opens if the party succeeds at the knock and the word. If one of them fails, the acolyte fetches Amath, and the look decides. If both fail, the shutter closes and the party has to try another approach.
>
> - **The knock.** Two slow taps and three quick ones. If the knock has not changed, Davil's version works. If it has, a character who watched the door in the previous scene and made the DC 14 check knows the new one. Otherwise the shutter slides open, an eye glances over the party, and the acolyte says "Wrong."
> - **The word.** The acolyte asks for the week's word. A character who makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Deception) check says that Vevette changed the code and nobody thought to tell them, and the acolyte hesitates. A character who makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check catches the acolyte's glance at a slate behind the door, where the week's word is chalked. The party can use either.
> - **The look.** Amath studies the party from the ground level for a moment. A character who makes a successful DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check convinces her that they are the couriers she expects, and she waves the party in.
>
> During the prayer hour, no one answers the door. A character can pick the outer lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check, and the chanting upstairs covers the noise, so a character on the spiral stair must make only a DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check to go unheard, where at any other hour the same check is DC 14. The door is locked and barred in deep night, and the awake acolyte hears any attempt on it unless a character makes a DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth) check while working the lock.

#### Approach Two: The Outer Wall

> [!exploration]**Up the Boards**
>
> The way in is a plank hoist door on the upper level, barred on the inside, and reaching it takes three steps.
>
> - **Getting to the boards.** On a first visit, the party reaches the scaffolding from the roof next door or from the lane, unseen if each character makes a DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check. On a return visit, the scaffolding is gone, and each character must make a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check to climb the pale brick, or the party can rig a rope from the neighboring roof and climb with Advantage.
> - **The noise.** The boards rattle and creak. Each character on the scaffolding must make a DC 16 Dexterity (Stealth) check or be heard by the occupants of the upper level. On a return visit, the rope is quieter, and the check is DC 14.
> - **The bar.** The hoist door is barred from the inside, and a character can lift the bar with a wire through the gap by making a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check. Breaking the door takes a successful DC 18 Strength (Athletics) check, and the noise rouses everyone on the upper level. On a return visit, a bell cord runs from the door to the ground level, and a character who makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check sees it before it is disturbed. Cutting it takes a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check. If it is not cut, the bell rings, and the acolytes on the ground level rush up the stairs.
>
> The hoist door opens onto the upper level, so during the prayer hour it opens into the middle of the ritual. The party should use it during working hours, when only Amath and the acolytes with her are upstairs, or in deep night.

#### Approach Three: The Referral Cover

> [!exploration]**Rorik's Referral**
>
> Tashlyn cleared the name Rorik Halvars, a mercenary who runs legitimate contracts through the Splinter, and the party can claim to be his referrals for a pickup job. The story works in three steps.
>
> - **Selling it at the door.** A character who makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Deception) check convinces the acolyte at the peephole. On a failure, the acolyte asks for a contact and sends for Amath, who sets a second check at DC 15.
> - **The interview.** Amath receives the party at the ground level, and the scene below plays out as a social encounter. She asks who sent them and what they want.
> - **The window.** Amath sends one acolyte upstairs to fetch a parcel that does not exist, which leaves one acolyte with the party and Amath at the fire. A character who keeps Amath talking with a successful DC 13 Charisma (Performance or Deception) check gives a second character the chance to slip down the stair. That character makes a successful DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) check to reach the cellar unseen.
>
> If **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** is marked, Amath recognizes the name Rorik Halvars and plays along. She sends an acolyte to the cellar to drop a Zhentarim coin into the call bowl, which starts the ten-minute clock. A character who makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check notices that she is stalling, and a character who makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check hears a coin ring in the cellar and sees a gray glow beneath the cellar door.

> [!social]**Amath Seccent**
>
> Amath Seccent (Lawful Evil, Human, she/her) :: a Banite priestess in common clothes who keeps Yellowspire as a temple, a post office and a fallback position, and who is unfailingly polite about all three.
>
> Amath is composed and correct. She speaks in courteous, complete sentences and never raises her voice. She is committed to Bane in a way that does not feel like devotion to her, only alignment, and she treats weakness with controlled contempt. She receives guests as though she were hosting a tea, and any hostility reads as a small breach of manners. The handprints on the walls are the only thing in the tower she mentions with warmth.
>
> Amath is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Rorik Halvars, whom she claims to know slightly.
> - The tower, which she calls the House of Tyranny.
> - Her acolytes, whom she is proud of.
>
> She will not discuss the circle, the relay box or the ledger. She dislikes questions about Kolat Towers and answers them with a question of her own.

> [!qna]**Rorik sent us.**
>
> Amath folds her hands in front of her.
>
> > Did he? Rorik is a dear man, and it's kind of him to send such well-dressed people. If you'll wait by the fire, I'll ask one of my acolytes to fetch the parcel he mentioned, and you can tell me how he's keeping.

> [!qna]**What is this place?**
>
> > It is the House of Tyranny, though the neighbors think it's a private home, and I prefer that they do. We keep it as a house of worship, and I hope you will forgive the handprints. They are a devotion, and my acolytes are very particular about them.

> [!qna]**Who else comes here?**
>
> > Friends of the Lord of Tyranny, and friends of Rorik's, I'm sure. I find that people who ask who else comes here generally intend to visit them next, so I make a habit of not answering.

### The Relay Box

The ledger is in the circle chamber, and the chamber is where the trap is. Reaching the chamber takes a successful DC 20 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check on the oak door, or a successful DC 25 Strength (Athletics) check to force it, which is heard on the ground level. On a return visit, the door has a second lock, and the party needs both checks or Amath's key ring. Amath keeps her key ring on her belt, and a character who lifts it with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check while she is speaking can open the door without a roll.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The door swings inward onto a stone chamber with no furniture except an iron-bound box bolted to the east wall. Runes are cut into the floor in a wide circle, and a brass bowl sits in the ring's edge with three Zhentarim coins lying in the bottom. In the northeast corner, one flagstone looks a little paler than the rest.

> [!gamemaster]**The Circle Trap**
>
> The circle is permanent, and it is always active. Its runes are keyed to a pressure plate in the northeast corner, a flagstone stamped with a Zhentarim coin. While a creature weighing at least 50 pounds presses the plate, the runes stay dormant, and the circle can be crossed or the relay box opened. If anyone opens the box or crosses the ring while the plate is unpressed, the runes flash a dim gray for a heartbeat and send a silent signal to Kolat Towers. That starts the ten-minute clock. A coin dropped in the call bowl sends the same signal whether or not the plates are pressed, because the bowl is how the garrison calls for help on purpose.
>
> On a return visit, the chamber has a second plate in the southwest corner, and both plates must be pressed at once. The pulse also fires if only one is pressed.
>
> A character can find the first plate in one of three ways: Tashlyn's tip about the northeast corner, a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check in good light, or *detect magic*, which shows the rune lines running to the flagstone. The second plate is not in Tashlyn's tip. A character who traces the rune lines and makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check notices that they split toward a second corner, and a character who searches the southwest corner and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check finds the plate. *Detect magic* shows both.
>
> A pack or a stone weighing 50 pounds keeps a plate down as well as a person does, but a plate lifted for even a moment lets the runes flash.

> [!exploration]**The Relay Box**
>
> The box is bolted to the wall and locked. A character who makes a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check opens it cleanly. A character who forces the lid with a successful DC 18 Strength (Athletics) check opens it too, but the noise carries to the ground level. Inside are a thin leather-bound ledger and a tin of clay tokens.
>
> The ledger lists every carrier of a pass-amulet by name, the day each amulet was last refreshed and where each carrier hands it on. It names three carriers: Avareen Windrivver, who works from a house in Brindul Alley; Agorn Fuoco, who visits Yellowspire on some evenings; and the field crews that Vevette Blackwater sends out from Kolat Towers. On a return visit, each of those entries has been crossed out and replaced, which means the amulets in circulation are freshly issued.
>
> A character who reads the ledger for ten minutes and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check understands the refresh marks. That reveals which carrier holds the freshest amulet and the mark that Kolat Towers uses to recalibrate a stale one.

### The Desk Upstairs

The three coded letters are in a locked drawer in Amath's writing desk on the upper level.

> [!exploration]**Amath's Desk**
>
> A character who makes a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check opens the drawer cleanly. A character who breaks it with a successful DC 18 Strength (Athletics) check wakes or alerts everyone on the level. Amath's key ring opens it without a roll.
>
> During working hours, Amath sits at the desk, so the party needs a distraction to reach it. Bringing her downstairs through the referral cover works, as does a loud noise on the ground level. During the prayer hour, the desk is empty of people, but the upper level is full of worshipers, and no one can reach it unseen. In deep night, everyone sleeps on cots within a few paces of the desk, and each character crossing the room makes a DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check. A failure wakes one acolyte, who rises and calls for Amath.
>
> The letters are written in a simple substitution cipher. A character who reads them for one hour and makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check decodes them, and Tashlyn decodes them without a roll. They name Vevette Blackwater as the officer who coordinates the strike teams from Kolat Towers and list the operations she has planned for the coming month.

### Amath and Her Acolytes

If the party fights, Amath keeps her acolytes and guards in front of her and holds the tower's ground level, because the circle is beneath it and she will not leave it.

> [!hazard]**The House of Tyranny**
>
> Amath Seccent uses the 2024 **Priest** statistics, her acolytes use the 2024 **Priest Acolyte** statistics, and the guards use the 2024 **Tough** and **Warrior Veteran** statistics, in the numbers shown in **Who Is in the Tower**.
>
> #### Amath's Tactics
> Amath calls her acolytes to position and casts *Spirit Guardians* once two or more party members are within 15 feet of her. She uses Divine Aid to cast *Dispel Magic* on the first lingering spell the party leaves on her people or the room, to cast *Healing Word* on a fallen acolyte, and to cast *Lesser Restoration* on an ally who is poisoned or frightened. Otherwise she attacks with Radiant Flame at range and the mace in melee. She sends the acolyte from the cellar, or the nearest one, to drop a coin in the call bowl as soon as the fight begins, which starts the ten-minute clock. She yields at about 10 hit points.
>
> During combat, the acolytes:
> - Use Divine Aid to cast *Bless* on Amath before the fight starts.
> - Attack the nearest enemy with the mace and use Radiant Flame on spellcasters.
> - Flee or yield when the first ally falls or when they are at half hit points.
>
> The guards fight until Amath yields, and then they surrender. If the party fights on the upper level, the three flying snakes fly to the arrow slits and watch. A sleeping acolyte spends the first round rising and reaching for a weapon.
>
> A yielding acolyte trades the location of the coded letters for their life. A character who makes a successful DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation) check gets Amath to surrender, and she tells them nothing. A character who bluffs convincingly as a fellow Banite, with a successful DC 15 Charisma (Deception) check, can end the fight before it starts. On a failure, she fights on until she reaches about 10 hit points. A party that wants no fight at all can use the referral cover in **Approach Three** or the deep-night route past the sleepers.

### The Ten-Minute Clock

The clock starts when the circle pulses or when Amath or another acolyte drops a coin in the call bowl. A party that cannot see the runes flash can hear the coin ring in the bowl, and the runes glow a dim gray at the moment of the pulse. Announce the start of the clock openly and track it at the table.

Ten minutes after the clock starts, the circle glows and the reinforcements arrive.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The runes on the floor flare white, and the air above the circle bends like a heat haze. A moment later, figures in dark cloaks step out of the light with drawn blades, and one of them glances at the box on the wall before she looks up at you.

> [!hazard]**The Arrival**
>
> The reinforcements use the 2024 **Spy** statistics, in the numbers shown in **Who Is in the Tower**. They arrive in the circle chamber with orders to seize the relay box and capture anyone in the tower.
>
> #### The Reinforcements' Tactics
> The Spies climb the stair in pairs and use Cunning Action to Disengage and reposition. They target whichever character carries the ledger. They disengage and return to the circle once two of them are down or once the ledger has left the building.
>
> A party that is gone before the clock runs out meets no one. A party that waits in the chamber meets the Spies at the ring. A party can avoid the fight by leaving through the hoist door or the front door and losing the pursuers in the streets.

> [!exploration]**Wrecking the Circle**
>
> The party can prevent the reinforcements by destroying the circle before the clock runs out. A successful casting of *Dispel Magic* on the circle, made with a DC 15 spellcasting ability check, ruins it. A party without the spell can pour two flasks of acid along the inner ring and scrape the runes for one minute. Reinforcements then have to walk from Kolat Towers and reach Yellowspire one hour after the clock started.
>
> A wrecked circle is a permanent loss. It closes the Yellowspire entry method in **Kolat Towers** for the rest of the campaign, and Davil is unhappy about it. Mark **Yellowspire Circle Destroyed**.

### Getting Out

Leaving is a scene in its own right. The exit follows the way the party came in, and the checks depend on whether the tower is quiet or alerted.

- **Front door.** A character who makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Deception) check explains the departure to any acolyte who sees it. A failure delays the party at the door for two rounds.
- **Hoist door and outer wall.** Each character must climb down with the same checks as the climb up, and a rope makes it faster.
- **Stealth.** If the tower is quiet, the party crosses the ground level unseen with a successful DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) check, made once by the group using the lowest result. On a failure, an awake acolyte reaches the door as the party steps through it.

A clean exit means that neither the circle pulse nor Amath's coin fired, no acolyte saw the intruders, and Agorn Fuoco did not escape.

### Ziraj's Notes

Davil asked Ziraj to hand over his surveillance notes on the Kolat Towers force field to whoever went into Yellowspire, whether they came out with the ledger or not. Ziraj shows them to Doom Raiders members the next morning at the back table of Corellon's Crown in Trollskull Alley. Companions may sit at the next table and get nothing.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Corellon's Crown smells of dried mint and horehound. A wood elf in an apron sets a pot of tea and four cups on the back table, and a huge half-orc sits against the wall with a stack of bark-paper sheets in front of him. Every sheet is covered in rows of tally marks and small drawings of tower walls.
>
> > Drink this first. It's mint and horehound, and you look as though you need it.

> [!social]**Ziraj and Fala**
>
> Ziraj the Hunter (Lawful Evil, Half-Orc, he/him) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Assassination, who speaks in two to five words and reports in counts.
>
> Fala Lefaliir (Chaotic Good, Wood Elf, they/them) :: the herbalist who shelters him and pours tea before anyone speaks.
>
> Ziraj nods instead of saying yes and answers only what he is asked. Fala fills the silences and stares without blinking while the party talks. Neither will say more about Ziraj than the notes show.

> [!qna]**Why did you watch the towers?**
>
> Ziraj slides the top sheet across the table.
>
> > Davil asked.

> [!qna]**How long did you watch?**
>
> > Twenty nights.

> [!qna]**Can you read this for us?**
>
> Fala turns the sheet and traces a line with one finger.
>
> > He writes in his own marks, and I've learned them because he taught me. Each cluster is a night, each tally is a gap, and each drawing shows where on the wall it opened. I'd rather not explain it twice, so please listen. Drink this first.

> [!exploration]**The Force Field Notes**
>
> A character who studies the notes for ten minutes and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check reads them without help. Fala reads them out loud if no one succeeds.
>
> The notes show three gaps in the force field on the upper walls of the towers. Each gap opens for 60 seconds and closes again, and a different gap opens two hours later. The cycle repeats every six hours: the first gap opens at dusk, the second two hours later, the third two hours after that, and the first again at the next six-hour mark. Each gap is at upper-floor height, and one is level with the bridge between the towers. The notes mark each gap's position on a rough sketch. The party keeps the sketch, which the **Kolat Towers** quest calls the force-field gap diagram.
>
> The notes also record that the gaps slid by a quarter of an hour on two nights when the garrison walked its posts twice, and Ziraj underlined both entries.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Ziraj taps the underlined entries with one thick finger and looks at Fala, who nods.
>
> > They move.

If the party returns with nothing from Yellowspire, Tashlyn sends the notes anyway. She hands them over without ceremony and says: "You came back with nothing. Take these, and don't fail there."

### Renown Opportunities

Each participating Doom Raiders member gains 4 base Renown for entering Yellowspire and reporting to Davil, whether or not the party brought anything out.

- **+1 Renown:** recover the relay ledger and hand it to Davil.
- **+1 Renown:** recover the three coded letters and hand them to Tashlyn.
- **+1 Renown:** leave with no alarm sounded, meaning neither the circle pulse nor Amath's coin fired, no acolyte saw the intruders, and Agorn Fuoco did not escape.

Each bonus is earned once, when its condition is complete. Helping companions gain no Doom Raiders Renown.

### Aftermath

Davil receives the ledger at the Yawning Portal that evening or the next, and he reads it for a long time before he speaks.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil turns the ledger's pages one by one with his bracelet trophies rattling softly against the wood. Tashlyn stands behind his chair and reads over his shoulder. When he reaches the last page, he closes the book and looks up.
>

## Overview

Davil sends the party into Yellowspire, a Banite tower in the Castle Ward, to steal a ledger of pass-amulet carriers from the Splinter's relay. Afterward Ziraj hands over his notes on the Kolat Towers force field.

## Summary

We went into Yellowspire for Davil and took the relay ledger from the circle chamber in the cellar. Amath Seccent's acolytes, the pressure plate and the teleportation circle all cost us something on the way out. Ziraj gave us his notes on the gaps in the force field around Kolat Towers, and Davil now has a list of who carries an amulet.
