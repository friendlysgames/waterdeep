# The Yellowspire Job

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Stealth Event occurs when Davil Starsong briefs the party at the Yawning Portal and they move on Yellowspire. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Davil in person and ask what he knows about the tower.
> - Spend one day of surveillance to surface three independent entry approaches with different risk profiles.
> - Navigate the tower's two floors, find the pass-amulet ledger and the correspondence letters, and identify the concealed floor plate before stepping into the teleportation circle.
> - Exit before the ten-minute arrival clock expires — or fight four fresh agents if they don't.
>
> #### The Floor Plate
>
> The teleportation circle on the ground floor has a concealed trigger rune. Missing the floor plate starts a ten-minute clock and brings four agents from Kolat Towers. The plate is detectable at DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) in good light. The design assumes the party may miss it and provides the clock as a consequence rather than an immediate failure state.

> [!gamemaster]**Yellowspire**
>
> The tower is narrow, four stories, operating as a private residence with shuttered windows and foot traffic patterns that do not match the cover. The concealed floor plate is a flagstone in the northeast corner of the ground floor, slightly lighter than the others — deliberate, so Splinter agents can identify it in the dark. The teleportation circle is always active. Pressing the floor plate before stepping in deactivates the trigger rune for thirty seconds and allows safe transit. Stepping in without pressing it sends a silent signal to Kolat Towers; four agents arrive in ten minutes.
>
> The pass-amulet ledger is what Davil needs for **Kolat Towers**. The three coded correspondence letters in the second-floor desk name Vevette Blackwater as Kolat Towers' coordinating officer and outline the next month's operational priorities — that is what Tashlyn needs.

### The Brief

Davil is back at the Yawning Portal, in his usual chair, when the party arrives. He has a second cup waiting.

> [!readaloud]
> Davil Starsong is at his table by the wall when you come in, with a second cup set out across from him, and he stands when he sees you. The warmth is there when he settles back into his chair, though it is sitting lower than it used to, more in the eyes than in the voice. He slides a folded diagram across the table without preamble.
>
> > "Yellowspire. Old wizard tower on Copper Pot Lane, in the Trades Ward. Manshoon's cell uses it as a relay — there's a teleportation circle inside linked directly to Kolat Towers. They're storing something there I need."
>
> He opens the diagram: a careful exterior sketch of a four-story tower with notations in his own hand.
>
> > "The pass-amulet distribution ledger. Every Splinter lieutenant who carries a force-field amulet, their rotation schedule, when each amulet is refreshed. With it, we know exactly who to take the amulets from and when. Without an amulet, Kolat Towers' force field stops everything."
>
> He turns his cup in his hands.
>
> > "I know the coded knock. I have a cover name — Rorik Halvars, a mercenary Tashlyn cleared for a referral story. I've had the exterior under watch for months. I have not been inside."
>
> He smooths the edge of the diagram with one finger.
>
> > "I want the ledger, and I want them not knowing it's gone. There are three correspondence letters in the second-floor desk that Tashlyn needs — she'll know what to look for in them." He pauses. "This is not an officially authorized request. I say that so you know the scope of what I'm asking, yes?"

> [!social]**The Master of Opportunities**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: a sun elf fixer who presents favors as gifts and debts as opportunities — quieter now than he was before the arrest.
>
> Davil after Watch custody is the same man with something pared away. He is still precise about the facts and warm toward the party, still turns the charm bracelet at his wrist when he is weighing something, but the stories about the old days have dried up and he fills silences less than he used to. He calls the party "my friends" now.
>
> He delivered the Yellowspire brief with the same care he used to give the first briefings — the diagram, the referral name, the facts in order. He will not say whether Tashlyn sanctioned this. He will not say what he plans to do from outside. He will not explain why a man who has been watching Yellowspire for months has not gone inside himself.
>
> Conversation topics Davil is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The pass-amulet ledger: what it is, why it matters for Kolat Towers.
> - The correspondence letters: what Tashlyn is looking for in them.
> - The coded knock and the Rorik Halvars cover.
> - The diagram: what he observed from the exterior.
> - Whether Skeemo flagged Yellowspire to Kolat Towers before being stopped.
>
> Any character who makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that "not officially authorized" is Davil's way of saying he authorized it himself and chose not to tell Tashlyn.

> [!qna]**Why not go in yourself?**
>
> He turns the charm bracelet at his wrist, working one of the small trophies between his fingers.
>
> > "My face is known to two of the agents on that floor. Not well — we overlapped during a courier operation two years ago and I doubt they made much of me — but I don't want to find out I was wrong about that inside a Splinter safe house." He sets the cup down. "You have the advantage of not existing, as far as Manshoon's people are concerned, yes?"

> [!qna]**Is Skeemo there?**
>
> This question is only relevant if **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** is marked. If it is not marked, Davil says only that Skeemo is handled.
>
> If **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** is marked:
>
> > "He may have flagged us to them when he arrived. We don't know what he told them or whether they believed it." He is quiet for a moment. "Assume they know something is coming. The surveillance phase should tell you whether they've elevated security."

> [!qna]**What's the coded knock?**
>
> > "Two slow, three rapid. Anyone who works that door knows it. The harder part is the passphrase they'll ask for after — I don't have it, which is why Rorik Halvars exists. Tashlyn cleared him through a contact who does legitimate Splinter contracts. The story is that he sent you for a pickup job." He pauses. "I'd have someone ready with a smooth explanation for why Rorik didn't come himself."

> [!qna]**The amulets — what exactly do we need?**
>
> > "The ledger lists every lieutenant who carries a pass-amulet and the schedule for when each one is refreshed. Once we have that, we know exactly where each amulet is and when it changes hands." He slides the diagram a little further across the table. "Those handoffs happen in public places. That was deliberate on someone's part. It makes them harder to take. You'll need the ledger before you can figure out how." He folds his hands. "That's Kolat Towers' front door, my friends, yes?"

> [!qna]**The pay?**
>
> He finds the charm bracelet again.
>
> > "You'll be compensated. There is coin in it, and beyond that a name — Vevette Blackwater, if the letters confirm what Tashlyn suspects. That name is worth more than coin to the right people at the right moment." He sets the cup down. "We can sort the details when the work is done."

Once the party has what they need, proceed to **Surveillance**.

### Surveillance

One day of external observation, watching the Yellowspire entrance from the adjacent streets. Three independent entry approaches emerge from the watch.

> [!readaloud]
> Yellowspire is narrow and old, a wizard's tower wearing the cover of a private residence, with shuttered windows, no signage, and the foot traffic of a building where people arrive and leave without wanting to be seen doing either.

> [!exploration]**The Coded Knock**
>
> Any character who watches the front door over four hours and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check captures the knock pattern accurately: two slow taps, three rapid.
>
> > Two slow, three rapid — the same pattern every time, from every contact who comes and goes.
>
> This approach gets the party through the front door as apparent Splinter contacts. The agents inside will ask for a passphrase the party does not have. A DC 13 Charisma (Deception) check to deflect — "Vevette's code change, nobody told you?" — buys thirty seconds before they escalate or stand down.

> [!exploration]**The Rooftop Approach**
>
> Any character who circles the building and studies the adjacent roofline identifies a connection point at the third-floor level: a four-foot gap between Yellowspire and the building next door.
>
> A DC 14 Strength or Dexterity (Athletics) check to make the jump, made with Advantage if a rope is rigged across the gap first. Success brings the party onto the third floor with no agents between them and the stairs down to the second floor. They do not know about the teleportation circle's floor plate from this angle — a character who descends to the ground floor and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check in good light spots the lighter flagstone in the northeast corner before stepping into the circle.

> [!exploration]**The Referral Cover**
>
> Tashlyn's cleared name is Rorik Halvars, a former mercenary who runs legitimate contracts through the Splinter. The party claims to be Rorik's referral for a pickup job.
>
> A DC 13 Charisma (Deception) check to sell it. On success, the ground-floor agents let them in and return to their work. On failure, they ask for a contact number and wait while one goes upstairs to verify with the Mage.

### Entry

> [!readaloud]
> The inside of Yellowspire is a working relay office dressed in the quiet of a private home, with correspondence in coded stacks on the central table and two chairs facing each other, and at the room's center a permanent chalk diagram on the flagstones, a ringed circle with an activation rune visible at the step-in point. The flagstone in the northeast corner is very slightly lighter than the others.

> [!gamemaster]**Ground Floor**
>
> Two **Spies** are at the central table, working through correspondence. The teleportation circle occupies the room's center with the activation rune visible at the step-in point. The concealed floor plate is in the northeast corner; a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check in good light identifies it as slightly different from the surrounding flagstones.

> [!gamemaster]**Second Floor**
>
> One **Mage** is at a desk with notes, with *Counterspell* prepared. One Splinter agent is asleep in a cot against the far wall. The locked desk contains both the pass-amulet ledger and the three correspondence letters.
>
> - DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) opens the desk cleanly.
> - DC 18 Strength forces it — loud enough to wake the sleeping agent immediately.
>
> The sleeping agent, if woken, spends the first round of combat reaching for a weapon. The Mage uses *Counterspell* against the first visible spell of 1st level or higher cast in her line of sight. She does not Counterspell area effects she cannot see the origin of. She calls for the ground-floor agents when she hears fighting, not when she hears the desk open.

### The Ten-Minute Exit Clock

If the teleportation circle was triggered without the floor plate being pressed, a ten-minute window is active from that moment. The party must be out of the building before the clock runs.

> [!gamemaster]**The Circle's Clock**
>
> If the party triggered the teleportation circle without pressing the floor plate, four Splinter agents arrive at the ten-minute mark. They appear in the circle ready for a fight — they were warned. The party should have the ledger by this point if they moved quickly. The question is whether they are still in the tower. A party that identified the exit approach during surveillance and is already moving should clear in time. A party still on the second floor when the clock expires faces four fresh agents at the only ground-floor exit.

The exit path and time required depend on the entry approach.

> [!exploration]**Rooftop Exit**
>
> The four-foot jump back to the adjacent roof requires the same DC 14 Athletics check as entry. Three characters exit in one round each, and the party is clear before the clock matters.

> [!exploration]**Front Door Exit**
>
> A DC 13 Charisma (Deception) check to explain the departure convincingly to anyone watching. On failure, the remaining agents delay the party at the door — not combat, a conversation that costs two rounds.

> [!exploration]**Stealth Exit**
>
> As a group, one DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) check using the lowest result. On success, the party is through the building and out before anyone can report. On failure, a ground-floor agent tracks the sound and arrives at the door as the party is stepping through it.

> [!hazard]**The Four Backup Agents**
>
> If the four Splinter agents arrive before the party exits, they emerge combat-ready from the circle. Use the **Spy** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*) for all four.
>
> #### Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, two agents hold the circle and two advance toward the sound of the party. Over the course of combat, they prioritize blocking the front door and the stairwell. The battle ends when the party exits the building or all four agents are incapacitated.

### Concluding the Event

If the pass-amulet ledger is recovered and delivered to Davil, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Davil looks at the ledger for a long time, turning pages slowly. When he looks up, he has the manner of someone who has just confirmed something he needed to be certain about before he was willing to say it.
>
> > "We know where the amulets are. All of them." He closes the ledger and sets his hand flat on it. "When you're ready for Kolat Towers — tell me a day before."
>
> He does not explain what he plans to do from outside.

If the correspondence letters are recovered and delivered to Tashlyn, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Tashlyn reads through the three letters without comment, and when she finishes she sets them down in a neat stack and slides them back to the party.
>
> > "Vevette Blackwater coordinates the strike team deployments. She's who you'll want to find in **Kolat Towers** if you want to cut the response capability before it fires."
>
> She adds a page to her file. "Priority target."

## Overview

A Manshoon Splinter relay tower in the Trades Ward holds the pass-amulet ledger Davil needs for Kolat Towers. The job is to take it and leave before anyone there realises it is gone.

## Summary

The party infiltrated Yellowspire, Manshoon's Trades Ward relay tower, and retrieved the pass-amulet distribution ledger from the second-floor desk. Whether the exit was clean or the backup agents arrived, the Doom Raiders now know every lieutenant who carries a force-field amulet and when each one changes hands.
