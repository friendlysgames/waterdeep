# The Yellowspire Job

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Stealth Event begins when the party surveils Yellowspire and ends when they exit the tower. In this Event:
>
> - One day of surveillance surfaces three independent entry approaches, each with different risk profiles.
> - The teleportation circle on the ground floor has a trigger rune: missing the concealed floor plate starts a ten-minute arrival clock.
> - The pass-amulet ledger and three coded correspondence letters are in the locked desk on the second floor.
> - A clean exit requires the party to be gone before the clock expires or before anyone in the tower counts heads.
> - This mission is a mini-heist. Three entry approaches are presented as distinct options; the party chooses one.

**Background (DM only)**

Yellowspire is narrow, four stories, operating as a private residence with shuttered windows and foot traffic patterns that do not match the cover. The concealed floor plate is a flagstone in the northeast corner of the ground floor, slightly lighter than the others — deliberate, so Splinter agents can identify it in the dark. The teleportation circle is always active. Pressing the floor plate before stepping into the circle deactivates the trigger rune for thirty seconds and allows safe transit. Stepping in without pressing it sends a silent signal to Kolat Towers; four agents arrive in ten minutes.

The pass-amulet ledger is what Davil needs for **Kolat Towers**. The three coded correspondence letters are what Tashlyn needs: they name Vevette Blackwater as Kolat Towers' coordinating officer and outline the next month's operational priorities.

### Surveillance

One day of external observation, watching the Yellowspire entrance from the adjacent streets. Three independent entry approaches emerge:

**The Coded Knock**

Two slow taps, three rapid. A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check tracking the front door over four hours captures the pattern accurately. Any character who watches the door and makes the check has it memorized.

This approach gets the party through the front door as apparent Splinter contacts. The agents inside will ask for a passphrase the party does not have. A **DC 13 Charisma (Deception)** check to deflect — "Vevette's code change, nobody told you?" — buys thirty seconds before they escalate or stand down.

**The Rooftop Approach**

The adjacent building's roof connects via a four-foot gap to Yellowspire's third-floor level. A **DC 14 Strength or Dexterity (Athletics)** jump, made with Advantage if a rope is rigged across the gap first.

This bypasses the ground floor entirely. The party lands on the third floor with no agents between them and the stairs to the second floor. They do not know about the teleportation circle's floor plate from this angle — a character who descends to the ground floor and makes a **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check in good light spots the lighter flagstone in the northeast corner before stepping into the circle.

**The Referral Cover**

Tashlyn provided the name Rorik Halvars — a former mercenary who runs legitimate contracts through the Splinter. The party claims to be Rorik's referral for a pickup job.

A **DC 13 Charisma (Deception)** check to sell it. On success, the ground-floor agents let them in and return to their work. On failure, they ask for a contact number and wait while one of them goes upstairs to verify with the Mage.

### Entry

**Ground floor:** Two **Spies** at the central table, working through correspondence. The teleportation circle occupies the room's center — a ringed diagram in permanent chalk with the activation rune visible at the step-in point. The concealed floor plate is in the northeast corner; a **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check in good light identifies it as slightly different from the surrounding flagstones.

**Second floor:** One **Mage** at a desk with notes and a *Counterspell* prepared. One Splinter agent asleep in a cot against the far wall. The **locked desk** contains both the pass-amulet ledger and the three correspondence letters.

- **DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** opens the desk cleanly.
- **DC 18 Strength** forces it — loud enough to wake the sleeping agent immediately.

The sleeping agent, if woken, spends the first round of combat reaching for a weapon. He is not combat-ineffective; he just has a round of warning.

The Mage uses *Counterspell* against the first visible spell of 1st level or higher cast in her line of sight. She does not Counterspell area effects she cannot see the origin of. She calls for the ground-floor agents when she hears fighting, not when she hears the desk open.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### The Circle's Clock
> If the party triggered the teleportation circle without pressing the floor plate, four Splinter agents arrive at the ten-minute mark. They appear in the circle ready for a fight — they were warned. The party has the ledger by this point if they moved quickly. The question is whether they are still in the tower. A party that identified the exit approach during surveillance (rooftop or front door) and is already moving should be clear in time. A party still in the second floor when the clock expires walks into four fresh combat-ready agents at the only ground-floor exit.

### The Ten-Minute Exit Clock

If the teleportation circle was triggered, a ten-minute window is active from that moment. The party must be out of the building before the clock runs.

The exit path and time required depend on the entry approach:

**Rooftop exit:** The four-foot jump back to the adjacent roof requires the same check as entry. Three characters exit in one round each. Clear.

**Front door exit:** A **DC 13 Charisma (Deception)** check to explain the departure convincingly to anyone watching. On failure, the remaining agents delay the party at the door — not combat, a conversation that costs two rounds.

**Stealth exit:** As a group, one **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth)** check using the lowest result. On success, the party is through the building and out before anyone can report. On failure, a ground-floor agent tracks the sound and arrives at the door as the party is stepping through it.

If the four Splinter agents arrive before the party exits: they emerge combat-ready from the circle. The party has the ledger. Four **Spies** between them and the door is the last obstacle.

### Concluding the Event

> **[GM]**
>
> **+2 Renown if** the pass-amulet ledger is recovered and delivered to Davil. He looks at it for a long time. Then: "We know where the amulets are. All of them. When you're ready for Kolat Towers — tell me a day before." He does not explain what he plans to do from outside.
> **+1 Renown if** the three correspondence letters are recovered. They confirm Vevette Blackwater's operational role. Tashlyn adds this to her file and shares a summary with the party: Vevette coordinates the strike team deployments, which makes her a priority target in **Kolat Towers**.
> **+1 Renown (bonus) if** the exit is clean — no Splinter agent can confirm who broke in, and Yellowspire's security posture does not change in response. Davil receives the ledger and Manshoon's cell never learns what was taken.

## Overview

A Manshoon Splinter relay tower in the Trades Ward holds the pass-amulet ledger Davil needs for Kolat Towers. Get in, get it, get out without them knowing it's gone.

## Read Aloud

> [!narrative]
> Yellowspire is narrow and old, a wizard's tower wearing the disguise of a private residence — shuttered windows, no signage, the foot traffic of a building where people arrive and leave without wanting to be seen doing either. The roof connects to the adjacent building over a four-foot gap. The front door uses a coded knock. The side entrance is through a kitchen window that no one has bothered to lock.
>
> You have one day to watch it before you go in.

## Summary

The party infiltrated Yellowspire, Manshoon's Trades Ward relay tower, and retrieved the pass-amulet distribution ledger from the second-floor desk. Whether the exit was clean or the backup agents arrived, the Doom Raiders now know every lieutenant who carries a force-field amulet and when each one changes hands.
