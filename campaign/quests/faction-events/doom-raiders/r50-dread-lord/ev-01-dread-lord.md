# Dread Lord

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Doom Raiders member reaches Renown 50, which usually happens during **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. Davil Starsong waits for the member at the Yawning Portal and takes them up to his curtained room on the second floor, where the inner circle has gathered. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn how to send the Network's pool of eight operatives on one major operation.
> - Claim a safe house in any ward of Waterdeep.
> - Accept or decline a nomination for a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council, and live with what either answer costs.
> - Agree to the Doom Raiders' partnership for the vault, if **Vault of Dragons** has not yet resolved.
>
> All of these benefits belong to the qualifying member. Companions gain no rank, no seat and no operatives of their own.

### Hook

Davil does not send a flying snake. On every evening after the member reaches Renown 50, he sits at the table nearest the well in the Yawning Portal with the lute across his knees and waits, and Durnan points the member to him the first time they come up from below. Companions can wait in the taproom, and the rank meeting itself is for qualifying members only. If several members qualify, Davil sees them together, and each answers for themselves. A member whose name lacks **Ardragon Reached** has it marked now and gains the Ardragon benefits from **Ardragon** as well.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> Five people founded the Doom Raiders: Davil, Istrid Horn, Skeemo Weirdbottle, Tashlyn Yafeera and Ziraj the Hunter. Yagra Stonefist joined later. Nobody at this table says "we started as six".
>
> The Zhentarim Council is reforming, and the effort to reclaim Zhentil Keep needs members who can show they hold a city and run a cell that survives the real world. Davil wants the Doom Raiders to have a voice at that table. The seat costs dues and obligations, and the event states them in full. In speech, the Doom Raiders say "Manshoon's people" only if **Manshoon Named** is marked. Until then they say "the other cell" or "the Splinter", and the lines below give both where it matters. They say "Floxin's people" only if **Floxin Status** is Alive. If it is Dead or Captured, the Doom Raiders know the man they took for the leader is gone while the cell still moves, so someone above him gives the orders and they do not know who.

### The Well

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom of the Yawning Portal is loud tonight, and the well in the middle of it breathes its cold air up past the windlass. Davil sits at the table nearest the rim with his lute across his knees, and Durnan nods toward him as you come up the last of the stairs. Davil gets to his feet with his glass in his hand, which he has never done for you before.
>
> > "Ah, there you are. I have something to say to you, and I'd like to say it upstairs, where the others are waiting. Your friends are welcome to a drink down here on me, yes?"

### The Second Floor

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil's curtained room on the second floor is a long room, with a table down the middle that would seat six and lamps hanging from the rafters. Istrid Horn sits at the near end with a small ledger shut in front of her and both hands flat on top of it. Tashlyn stands beside the window with her arms crossed, and Yagra Stonefist leans against the door frame with her red jade pendant hanging outside her armour. One chair along the side of the table has been pushed in square to the wood, and Davil goes to the head of the table and waits until you have all found a place.
>
> > "Five of us started this, back when we were walking into dungeons for coin. Istrid, Tashlyn, Skeemo, Ziraj and me. Yagra came along later, and she made us better. I want to say something about the five before I say the rest."

Davil speaks next about the empty chair, according to what became of Skeemo.

If **Skeemo Silenced** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil rests a hand on the back of the pushed-in chair.
>
> > "Skeemo's chair stays empty. He sold us for months, and he is dead because of it, and I find I still set a place for him in my head. I would ask you to forgive me that."

If **Skeemo Captured** is marked, use the reading that matches his fate. If **Skeemo Exiled** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil rests a hand on the back of the pushed-in chair.
>
> > "Skeemo's chair stays empty. He sailed for Baldur's Gate, and I let him go, and I don't expect him back at this table."

If **Skeemo Handed to the Watch** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil rests a hand on the back of the pushed-in chair.
>
> > "Skeemo's chair stays empty. The Watch has him, and I am the one who put him there."

If **Skeemo Executed** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil rests a hand on the back of the pushed-in chair.
>
> > "Skeemo's chair stays empty. Tashlyn saw to him at dawn, and I am the one who decided it."

If **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** is marked and **Manshoon Named** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil rests a hand on the back of the pushed-in chair.
>
> > "Skeemo's chair stays empty, because he took his seat across the city with Manshoon's people. As far as any of us can tell, he is sitting in it still."

If **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** is marked and **Manshoon Named** is not marked, Davil says "with the other cell's people" in place of "with Manshoon's people", whatever **Floxin Status** is.

Tashlyn speaks about Ziraj next, and what she says depends on whether he lived.

If **Ziraj Survived** is marked, or **Ziraj's Last Hunt** has not played, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn does not turn from the window.
>
> > "Ziraj is on a job. He knows what this is."

If **Ziraj Fell** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Beside the window a second chair has been pushed in square to the wood, and no glass has been set in front of it. Tashlyn looks at it instead of at Davil.
>
> > "That one's Ziraj's. Nobody sits in it, and if anybody so much as puts a coat on it I'll break their fucking arm."

If the member's name is on **Ziraj's Favour Used**, Tashlyn adds one line after her remark about Ziraj, naming the target and who acted. If Ziraj acted, she says "Ziraj took [target] for you, cleanly. Adequate." If Yagra acted, she says "Yagra fetched [target] for you, breathing. Adequate."

> [!social]**The Host at the Well**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: the leader of the Doom Raiders, who has come down to the taproom to fetch the member himself.
>
> Davil is Friendly toward the member and in no hurry, and he has a great deal less to hide than he used to. He speaks in the same graceful sentences as always, and he lets silences sit. He wants the member at the Council table and says so, but he will not press. He does not swear and does not threaten, and he still explains nothing he does not choose to.
>
> Davil is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The Council, what it is for, and why he is proposing the member.
> - The old days of the five, in a sentence or two at a time.
> - The pool and the safe houses, which he leaves to Tashlyn and Yagra.
> - The vault, if it has not yet been opened.

### The Pool

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil looks across the table at Tashlyn, and she comes away from the window and sets a folded sheet on the wood in front of you without sitting down.
>
> > "Dread Lord. That's eight people on call for one major operation, all of them picked by me. You name the job, I want five days' notice, and Davil sets the limits. They do the work, and you do the planning."

> [!qna]**Who are the eight?**
>
> > "You get eight people, a mage and a priest with six fighters behind them. Brannoc Hale leads whichever team goes, unless he's out with a Viper, and then the other veteran does. They're good people, and I've been hard on every one of them."

> [!qna]**What can we ask them to do?**
>
> > "Anything a person can do in a day or two of work. They'll follow you through the Yawning Portal and as far down as you need to take them, and they'll come out at the first safe exit when the job's done or abandoned. They won't touch children, bystanders, or a Watchman who hasn't hit them first, and they won't turn on another Doom Raider."

> [!qna]**Can we send all eight?**
>
> > "Davil approves the full pool only against the four big factions, which means the Xanathar's people, the Splinter, Jarlaxle or the Cassalanters. For anything else you choose between a strike team and a cover team, and both are plenty."

> [!exploration]**One Major Operation**
>
> Each Dread Lord member can send one package of operatives on one major operation, once. The member gives Tashlyn or Davil five days' notice with the objective, the place and the hour, and picks one package:
>
> - **Strike Team:** the Mage, one Warrior Veteran, one Spy and one Tough, for an assault.
> - **Cover Team:** one Warrior Veteran, one Spy, one Tough and the Priest Acolyte, for an extraction, a distraction or a guard.
> - **Full pool:** all eight, approved only if the target is one of the four villain factions' holdings, forces or leaders.
>
> The team meets the member at the Yawning Portal or at a surface place the member names, and the leader takes the member's plan. The operation runs until the objective is finished, abandoned or made impossible. The use is spent when the team begins the agreed operation. Fallen operatives are replaced a tenday later. Only one pool exists, so two members who ask for overlapping dates share the pool in the order they asked, and a package that is out cannot go out again until it is back.

> [!gamemaster]**The Pool as Allies**
>
> Use the **Mage**, **Warrior Veteran**, **Spy**, **Tough** and **Priest Acolyte** stat blocks from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, unmodified. The pool is one Mage, two Warrior Veterans, two Spies, two Toughs and one Priest Acolyte.
>
> Ally Power counts only for operatives who take part in a combat. For a party of levels 5 to 10, the Strike Team adds 124, the Cover Team 65 and the full pool 189. From level 11, the Strike Team adds 97, the Cover Team 52 and the full pool 149. Add the total to the participating characters' Party Power before recalculating the difficulty and the day cost. The full pool roughly doubles a party of four, so either build the fight around the larger total or send the Cover Team unless the operation is a set piece.
>
> #### The Operatives' Tactics
> The leader stays close to the member and directs the team to the member's objective. During combat, the team:
>
> - Has the Mage open with its strongest area spell and then keep behind the Warrior Veteran.
> - Has the Spy and the Tough flank the enemy the Veteran is fighting.
> - Has the Priest Acolyte spend its one Divine Aid on *Bless* before the fight starts, or on *Healing Word* for whoever drops first.
>
> The team withdraws to the nearest safe exit if its leader falls or if two operatives are down, and it does not leave the member behind.

### The Safe Houses

> [!readaloud]
>
> Yagra pushes herself off the door frame and cracks her knuckles one hand at a time. She walks around the table until she is standing behind your chair, and she talks in the same flat voice she uses to tell people about her own past.
>
> > "Seven wards, seven houses. I've slept in most of them, and they're decent. You knock, say the goods and say whose account it's on, and they let you in. You don't need three hours' notice at any of them now, Wenna's included, and nobody asks you a fucking thing."

> [!qna]**Who do they belong to?**
>
> > "Istrid pays for them. The people who run them think they're doing her a favour, and mostly they are. Don't piss them off, because they know my face."

> [!exploration]**A House in Every Ward**
>
> Each Dread Lord member can use the safe houses below. The member asks for the goods named, on Istrid's account, and the keeper takes them in at any hour.
>
> - **Dock Ward:** the loft above Tarrow's Tallow and Wick (a pound of blue-wick tallow).
> - **Castle Ward:** a bookbinder's workshop in the lane behind the Yawning Portal (a sheet of marbled endpaper).
> - **Sea Ward:** a dressmaker's on Sea Ward Way (a yard of grey silk).
> - **North Ward:** a saddler's three streets from Trollskull Alley (a pair of stirrup leathers).
> - **Trades Ward:** a cooper's yard behind the guild halls (a hoop for a half-barrel).
> - **Southern Ward:** a boarding house near the ward's market (a room for the night, paid in advance).
> - **Field Ward:** a hay and feed yard at the edge of the ward (a bale of clean straw).
>
> The member can bring up to five guests who are vouched for at the door. A stay lasts up to five consecutive nights with no limit per tenday, and the house counts as a safe place for a long rest. A keeper will take in someone the Watch is chasing for one night, once per tenday per house, and the Watch does not know the addresses. The City of the Dead has no house, because nobody lives there to run one.

### The Seat

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil takes a slow breath and lets it go, and the room goes quiet around him. Istrid opens her ledger at last and does not look at it, and Tashlyn watches the member instead of the window.
>
> > "The Zhentarim Council is reforming, and it is choosing who sits at it. The effort to win back Zhentil Keep needs people who have held a city and run a cell that survived the real world. You have done both, inside a cell that I am very fond of. I would like to put your name forward, for a full seat, with a vote and a hand in what the Black Network becomes. That isn't a small thing, and I want you to understand what I'm offering before you answer."

If **Manshoon Named** is marked, which it nearly always is by now, Davil answers as follows:

> [!qna]**What is the Council?**
>
> > "It's the Black Network trying to build a spine out of the pieces Manshoon left behind. It's not his Council. It's the legitimate Network, and it needs members who can be held to account. A seat carries a vote in continental decisions and access to resources no regional cell controls."

If **Manshoon Named** is not marked, he answers this instead:

> [!qna]**What is the Council?**
>
> > "It's the Black Network trying to build a spine out of the pieces the other cell left behind. It's not their Council. It's the legitimate Network, and it needs members who can be held to account. A seat carries a vote in continental decisions and access to resources no regional cell controls."

> [!social]**The Keeper of the Ledger**
>
> Istrid Horn (Lawful Evil, Shield Dwarf, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Trade and Coin, who has already done the arithmetic on the seat.
>
> Istrid is Friendly and entirely practical. She states the terms clearly and repeats them once, taps the ledger when she names a figure, and blesses a signed agreement with "Vergadain smile on it". She never raises her voice, and she does not call the price a penalty.
>
> Istrid is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The dues and the obligations of the seat.
> - Where the Doom Raiders' money for the nomination comes from.
> - The vault, if the member raises it.

> [!qna]**What does the seat cost?**
>
> Istrid taps the ledger once.
>
> > "A tenth of the gold from any job Davil, Tashlyn or I send you on, dear, paid back into my books. And when the Council summons you, you answer within a tenday or the seat goes to someone who can. That's the arrangement, and I'd be glad to say it again. The rest is for Davil to tell you."

> [!qna]**What do I get for it?**
>
> > "A vote, which I'm told is very grand, and the Seal. Any cell of the Black Network on the Sword Coast will shelter you and four friends for five nights and tell you what it knows of its city. It's a good seal, dear, and I'd keep it clean."

> [!qna]**Who pays for the nomination?**
>
> > "I do, out of the cell's ledgers. It's not cheap, and it's worth it to me if you sit there. If you don't, my money still goes to the Council's door, and it carries someone else through it."

> [!qna]**Tashlyn, what do you think?**
>
> Tashlyn answers without turning her head.
>
> > "The Council is full of people who smile at you across a table, and half of them would sell this cell for a better room. Watch their hands. Don't give any of them a name you can't afford to lose."

> [!gamemaster]**The Seat and Its Price**
>
> A seat on the Council is per member. A member who accepts receives a **Network Seal**, a red jade disc on a cord. A cell of the Black Network anywhere on the Sword Coast will shelter the member and four guests for up to five nights and tell the member what it knows about its city and its factions. The seat also carries a vote, which has no rules effect until **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** gives the Council something to decide.
>
> The price has two parts. Istrid takes a tenth of the member's share of any gold from a job that Davil, Tashlyn or Istrid sent the member on. When the Council summons the member, the member must answer within a tenday or lose the seat, and the Council's summons is **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** content.
>
> A member who declines loses the seat, the Seal and the vote. Davil sends Tashlyn's name forward instead, and Tashlyn carries it with the cell's own money, which leaves the Doom Raiders answerable to her on the Council. A second decliner's seat goes to Istrid, and a third decliner's seat goes empty. Tashlyn becomes cooler toward a member who declined: she sends that member's crew and pool requests back in writing, and she volunteers nothing.

### The Vault

If **Vault of Dragons** has already resolved, which is usual at this rank, Davil does not raise the vault. Istrid asks once, and the matter ends with her answer. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Istrid slides her ledger a little closer, and her finger stops over a blank line near the bottom of the page.
>
> > "And the gold, dear? I only want to know where it landed, for the ledger. It's the sort of figure I like to have written down."

She writes down whatever the member says and does not comment on it. No partnership is offered, and **Vault Partnership Agreed** is not marked.

If **Vault of Dragons** has not yet resolved, Davil makes the partnership offer before the choice. Only one partnership exists for the party, so the first member to accept speaks for it, and other Dread Lords cannot add a second set of crews. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil turns a charm on his bracelet and looks from Istrid to the member.
>
> > "There is half a million in gold dragons somewhere in this city, and we would like to help you reach it. A tenth of what you take out of the vault is ours, and that is all we ask. In return you will have three crews. One draws a faction's people away from the gate when you need it, one opens a door the old way, and one takes the gold away in wagons. You keep the rest."

> [!gamemaster]**The Vault Partnership**
>
> The three crews line up with the scenes of **Vault of Dragons**:
>
> - **Distraction (Scene 5):** A crew of Doom Raiders draws one faction away from the City of the Dead gate. The member names the faction, and its forces arrive ten minutes later than the Faction-State Summary Table has them. The crew does not draw away Dagult Neverember's arrival.
> - **Locksmith (Scene 2):** Toben Ash, a retired burglar in his seventies, opens the steel vault door in the Old Crypts (DC 22 lock) in ten minutes, with no roll. He cannot open the adamantine antechamber doors, which open only to the dragon-scale ceremony.
> - **Wagons (Scene 6):** Four covered wagons with Network drivers wait at the City of the Dead gate, and each carries about 2,500 pounds, which matches the 10,000-pound hoard. They take the gold the party keeps to Istrid's Dock Ward warehouse. This covers the transport of the **Keep it, quietly** path, and any remaining response teams still complicate the route.
>
> The payment is a tenth of the gold the party carries out of the vault and keeps, paid from the stored hoard. If the party returns the hoard to Laeral for her finder's fee, the Doom Raiders take a tenth of the 50,000 gp fee (5,000 gp). If the party leaves the gold in place, nothing is owed. The sapphire in the replica of Mount Waterdeep is not part of the take.

### The Answer

> [!exploration]**The Nomination**
>
> Davil holds the nomination open for a tenday. The member may answer at once, may ask for the tenday and answer before it ends, or may say nothing, which Davil treats as a refusal on the tenth day. A member who answers takes one of the two paths below, and the answer is recorded by name.

If the member accepts, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil closes his eyes for a moment and then opens them with a smile.
>
> > "Good. I'll send your name forward tomorrow, and I'll ask you to go on being the person I put on the paper, yes?"
>
> Istrid makes a note in the ledger and taps it once. Yagra comes around the table and holds out one broad hand, and when you take it she shakes once and lets go.
>
> > "I'll stand outside the Council hall with my arms folded if you want me there. I'm not making a fucking speech."

If the member declines, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil nods slowly, and his smile does not leave his face.
>
> > "That is a reasonable answer, and I'm not disappointed, or not very. Tashlyn's name will go forward instead, and she will carry it well. Everything else I offered still stands."
>
> Yagra claps you on the shoulder hard enough to rock you forward.
>
> > "Fair fight. Shit, I'd have said no too."
>
> Tashlyn looks at you for the first time since you came in, and whatever she is thinking, she keeps it.

### Renown Opportunities

This rank awards no Renown, gold or Milestone Points. The Seal and the seat do not earn Renown of their own, and the Doom Raiders have no higher rank to reach.

### Aftermath

The member can tell their companions what they were offered, but the pool, the safe houses, the seat and the partnership stay with the member, and a companion who asks in the member's name is turned away. A member who accepted owes dues from the next job. A member who declined finds that Tashlyn's name is on the paper that goes to the Council.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard about the pool and the safe houses and has answered the nomination or asked for the tenday. Record each member's answer, package use and partnership separately.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Dread Lord Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Davil names the rank; read by **Vault of Dragons** (Davil's post-resolution conversation, and the vault partnership if it is still open) and by any **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** content that checks the Dread Lord rank.
> - **Council Nomination Accepted** — mark with the member's name when they accept; read by **Vault of Dragons** (Davil's post-resolution ambitions) and by **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** content that checks standing in the Black Network.
> - **Council Nomination Declined** — mark with the member's name and the name that went forward in their place, when they decline or the tenday lapses; read by **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** content that checks the Council's makeup.
> - **Vault Partnership Agreed** — mark with the accepting member's name only if **Vault of Dragons** has not resolved; read by **Vault of Dragons** Scenes 2, 5 and 6.
> - **Dread Lord Operation Used** — mark with the member's name, the package and the date when a team begins its operation; read by later **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** events that call on the pool.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Packages need five days' notice, and the seat's dues apply from the next job. This is the final Doom Raiders rank, and its benefits stay available for later quests. If the member accepted, the Council's summons is **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** content. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Davil Starsong waits at the Yawning Portal to bring a Doom Raiders member up to his suite, where the inner circle names them Dread Lord and offers a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council.

## Summary

We were named Dread Lords of the Doom Raiders in Davil's suite above the Yawning Portal. Tashlyn gave us eight operatives for one operation, and Yagra gave us a safe house in every ward. Davil asked us to take a seat on the Zhentarim Council, and each of us either accepted or turned it down.
