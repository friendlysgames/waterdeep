# Dread Lord

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Doom Raiders renown reaches 50. Davil Starsong delivers the promotion in person. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Be summoned by Davil himself — no flying snake; he comes to wherever the character is.
> - Gather in the third-floor room at the Yawning Portal with the surviving inner circle: Davil, Tashlyn, Istrid, and Yagra; Ziraj is absent.
> - Receive the Dread Lord rank, the eight-operative pool, and the ward-wide safe house network.
> - Hear Davil's proposal for a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council — a genuine position of continental influence.
> - Hear the vault partnership offer, if **Vault of Dragons** has not yet resolved.
> - Make one real choice: accept or decline the Council nomination.
>
> #### When This Event Fires
>
> Parties are not expected to reach Renown 50 during Dragon Heist. This event is designed to fire during the Dungeon of the Mad Mage arc, after a character has accumulated renown across the full Dragon Heist campaign and into the Undermountain descent. The Zhentarim Council framing looks ahead to the larger Black Network world and works whether **Vault of Dragons** has or has not resolved. If this event fires before **Vault of Dragons** resolves, run the conditional vault section. If it fires after, skip that section and note that the offer has lapsed.

### The Summons

Davil does not send a flying snake.

> [!readaloud]
> Davil Starsong comes himself — to wherever the character is, whether that is Trollskull Manor, a taproom they frequent, or somewhere in the warrens of Undermountain where the Network has found a way to get a message through. He arrives in his traveling clothes, his lute slung across his back, and does not announce himself with any particular ceremony.
>
> > "I have something to say to you in person. Bring the others if they're close."
>
> He does not explain further. He expects the party to follow.

### The Circle

Davil leads the party to the third floor of the Yawning Portal — not the curtained second-floor room where he does his ordinary business, but the larger room he keeps for occasions that require a table.

> [!readaloud]
> The others are in the room when you arrive, gathered in their positions: Tashlyn near the window with her arms crossed, Istrid at the table with both hands flat on the wood and a small ledger she is not consulting, Yagra near the door with her red jade Zhentarim pendant out.
>
> Davil goes to the head of the table and does not sit.

> [!gamemaster]**Ziraj's Absence**
>
> Ziraj is not here. Davil does not mention it. Tashlyn mentions it once, if it comes up: "He's on a job. He knows what this is."
>
> This was discussed before the party arrived. None of them are surprised by the nomination Davil is about to make.

> [!social]**The Inner Circle**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: here to say something he came in person to say.
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: says very little; assessing the character; storing whatever she observes.
>
> Istrid Horn (Lawful Evil, Shield Dwarf, she/her) :: watches the door when the party enters, then returns to the ledger she is not reading.
>
> Yagra Stonefist (Neutral, Half-Orc, she/her) :: stays near the door; does not make a speech.
>
> Davil is deliberate and specific. He names the emotional weight of what the cell has survived before he names the rank, and his warmth is present and real, but it is paired with the directness of someone who means every word and chose each one. His characteristic "yes?" is mostly absent tonight.

After a moment, Davil speaks.

> [!readaloud]
> > "We started as six. We're down to five people who matter and one empty chair where Skeemo sat. I want to be honest about that before I say the next thing."
>
> He lets the silence settle.
>
> > "Dread Lord. Eight Black Network operatives on call for one major operation — yours to deploy within operational limits I set. Safe houses in every ward of Waterdeep. And the thing I actually came here to say."

### The Proposal

> [!readaloud]
> > "I want to put your name forward. Not for a courtesy seat. For a full position — continental vote, continental access, the ability to shape what the Black Network becomes after Manshoon's operation is finished. I have watched you work for a long time. You belong at that table."
>
> Istrid's expression does not change, and Tashlyn keeps her eyes on the window across the room.
>
> > "A seat on that Council is not a Waterdeep problem. It is a considerably larger one. I want you to understand what I am offering before you answer."

> [!gamemaster]**The Reforming Zhentarim Council**
>
> The Council's reconstitution is tied to the broader effort to reclaim Zhentil Keep, Manshoon's original seat of power before the Spellplague fractured it. The reforming Council is not Manshoon's Council — it is the legitimate Black Network's attempt to build institutional structure from the pieces he left behind. A seat carries a vote in continental Black Network decisions, access to Network resources beyond what any regional cell controls, and the responsibility of maintaining a presence that can be held accountable to the larger organization. The seat is non-transferable. It is not withdrawn if the character declines tonight — Davil can hold the nomination open for a tenday.

> [!qna]**What does a Council seat mean?**
>
> > "A vote in continental Black Network decisions. Access to resources beyond what any cell controls on its own. The responsibility of maintaining something that can be held accountable to the larger organization." He watches the character across the table. "It is not a Waterdeep problem. Think about it that way."

> [!qna]**What about the empty chair?**
>
> Davil is quiet for a moment.
>
> > "It's empty. I haven't decided what to do about it. Not tonight."

### The Vault (Conditional)

> [!gamemaster]**Run This Section Only If Vault of Dragons Has Not Yet Resolved**
>
> If **Vault of Dragons** has already resolved, skip this section entirely. The vault offer has lapsed and Davil does not raise it. Move directly to **The Choice**.

While the larger matter sits on the table, Davil raises one more thing — quieter, more practical.

> [!readaloud]
> > "There's half a million dragons somewhere in this city, and we'd like to help you reach them. Ten percent of whatever the party walks away with. In exchange: a distraction crew that will draw one faction's attention away from the vault entrance at the moment you need it, a locksmith backup for whatever the approach requires, and a wagon team standing by for extraction. You keep the rest. You keep the credit."
>
> Istrid nods once from her end of the table, as though she ran the numbers before the meeting started.
>
> > "Think about it alongside the other thing. They're not unrelated."

### The Choice

The room waits.

> [!gamemaster]**Running the Choice**
>
> Davil has made two offers. The vault partnership can be arranged on its own timeline; the Council nomination cannot. He needs an answer on the nomination before the tenday is out, and he will say so if asked. If the character asks for time: "You have a tenday. The seat does not hold open indefinitely."

If the character accepts the Council nomination, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> > "Good. I'll send the name forward. Don't embarrass us."
>
> Tashlyn exhales, and it sounds almost like a laugh, and Istrid makes a note in the ledger.
>
> Yagra extends a hand across the table, shakes once, and releases it without a speech.
>
> Davil picks up the lute and holds it across his lap without playing.

If the character declines the Council nomination, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> > "That's a reasonable answer. The seat will find someone else. You still have everything I offered — eight operatives, the ward safe houses, the vault partnership if you want it. Nothing changes."
>
> He means it, and the warmth does not leave his voice.
>
> Tashlyn looks at you for the first time since you arrived, measuring something and setting it aside without comment.

### Concluding the Event

Read by **Vault of Dragons** (faction confrontation roster, Davil's vault partnership contribution, and Doom Raiders post-resolution dialogue) and by any Dungeon of the Mad Mage content that checks Dread Lord rank.

Read by **Vault of Dragons** (tone of Davil's post-resolution ambitions) and by any future Dungeon of the Mad Mage content that checks the character's standing within the continental Black Network.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> The eight Black Network operatives are available for one major operation — route deployment requests through Davil. Safe houses in every ward are active immediately.
>
> If **Vault of Dragons** has not yet resolved and the party accepts the vault partnership, coordinate the timing of the distraction crew, locksmith backup, and wagon team with **Vault of Dragons** Scene 1. The 10% split applies to whatever the party declares as their take at the vault's resolution.
>

## Overview

Davil Starsong came in person. He brought the inner circle to the third-floor room at the Yawning Portal, named the character Dread Lord, and proposed them for a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council. The choice was genuine. The room waited for an answer.

## Summary

After reaching Renown 50, the character was named Dread Lord by Davil Starsong in a private room at the Yawning Portal, with Tashlyn Yafeera, Istrid Horn, and Yagra Stonefist present. Davil proposed the character for a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council and, if the vault had not yet resolved, offered formal partnership for the vault operation in exchange for a 10% share of the take. The character chose whether to accept or decline the nomination.
