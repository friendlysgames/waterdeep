# Dread Lord

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Doom Raiders renown reaches 50. Davil Starsong delivers the promotion in person. In this Event:
>
> - Davil comes to find the character himself — no flying snake; he appears wherever the character is
> - The surviving inner circle of the Doom Raiders gathers in the third-floor room at the Yawning Portal: Davil, Tashlyn, Istrid Horn, and Yagra Stonefist; Ziraj is absent and his absence is noted without apology
> - Davil names the character Dread Lord, presents the eight-operative pool and the ward-wide safe house network
> - He proposes the character for a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council — a genuine position of continental influence within the Black Network
> - If **Vault of Dragons** has not yet resolved, Davil makes the formal vault partnership offer (distraction crew, locksmith backup, wagon extraction team in exchange for 10% of the take); this offer lapses permanently if the vault has already resolved
> - The character makes one real choice: accept or decline the Council nomination
>
> > [!design]**Expected in Dungeon of the Mad Mage**
> >
> > Parties are not expected to reach Renown 50 during Dragon Heist. This event is designed to fire during the Dungeon of the Mad Mage arc, after a character has accumulated renown across the full Dragon Heist campaign and into the Undermountain descent. The Zhentarim Council framing looks ahead to the larger Black Network world and works whether **Vault of Dragons** has or has not resolved before this event fires. If this event fires before **Vault of Dragons** resolves, run the conditional vault section; if after, skip it and note that the offer has lapsed.

### The Summons

Davil does not send a flying snake.

He comes himself — to wherever the character is when the threshold is reached, whether that is Trollskull Manor, a taproom they frequent, or somewhere in the warrens of Undermountain where the Network has found a way to get a message through. He arrives in his traveling clothes, lute slung across his back. He does not announce himself with any particular ceremony.

> > "I have something to say to you in person. Bring the others if they're close."

He does not explain further. He expects the party to follow.

### The Circle

Davil leads them to the third floor of the Yawning Portal — not the curtained second-floor room where he does his ordinary business, but the larger room he keeps for occasions that require a table. When they arrive, the others are already there.

**Tashlyn Yafeera** stands near the window with her arms crossed. She does not look at Davil when he enters.

**Istrid Horn** is seated at the table, both hands flat on the wood, a small ledger in front of her that she is not consulting. She watches the character as they come through the door.

**Yagra Stonefist** is near the door. Her red jade Zhentarim pendant is out.

Ziraj is not here. Davil doesn't mention it. Tashlyn does, once:

> > "He's on a job. He knows what this is."

Davil stands at the head of the table. He does not sit.

> > "We started as six. We're down to five people who matter and one empty chair where Skeemo sat. I want to be honest about that before I say the next thing."

He lets the silence settle.

> > "Dread Lord. Eight Black Network operatives on call for one major operation — yours to deploy within operational limits I set. Safe houses in every ward of Waterdeep. And the thing I actually came here to say."

### The Proposal

The Zhentarim Council is reforming. The Black Network has been fractured for years; Manshoon's disruption is one symptom of a larger structural failure, and the effort to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep has created a need for the Council to reconstitute itself with members who can demonstrate they hold a city, run a cell that survives contact with the real world, and make decisions the Network can defend. That is what this cell has done. That is what this character has done inside it.

> > "I want to put your name forward. Not for a courtesy seat. For a full position — continental vote, continental access, the ability to shape what the Black Network becomes after Manshoon's operation is finished. I have watched you work for a long time. You belong at that table."

Istrid's expression does not change. Tashlyn is still looking out the window. Neither of them is surprised. This has been discussed before the party arrived.

> > "A seat on that Council is not a Waterdeep problem. It is a considerably larger one. I want you to understand what I am offering before you answer."

> [!info]**The Reforming Zhentarim Council**
>
> The Council's reconstitution is tied to the broader effort to reclaim Zhentil Keep, Manshoon's original seat of power before the Spellplague fractured it. The reforming Council is not Manshoon's Council — it is the legitimate Black Network's attempt to build institutional structure from the pieces he left behind. A seat carries a vote in continental Black Network decisions, access to Network resources beyond what any regional cell controls, and the responsibility of maintaining a presence that can be held accountable to the larger organization. The Council seat is non-transferable. It is also not withdrawn if the character declines tonight; Davil can hold the nomination open for a tenday.

### The Vault (Conditional)

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Run This Section Only If Vault of Dragons Has Not Yet Resolved
>
> If **Vault of Dragons** has already resolved, skip this section entirely. The vault offer has lapsed and Davil does not raise it. Move directly to **The Choice**.

While the larger matter sits on the table, Davil raises one more thing — quieter, more practical.

> > "There's half a million dragons somewhere in this city, and we'd like to help you reach them. Ten percent of whatever the party walks away with. In exchange: a distraction crew that will draw one faction's attention away from the vault entrance at the moment you need it, a locksmith backup for whatever the approach requires, and a wagon team standing by for extraction. You keep the rest. You keep the credit."

Istrid nods once, which is her form of endorsement. She has already done the arithmetic.

> > "Think about it alongside the other thing. They're not unrelated."

### The Choice

The room waits.

Davil has made two offers. The vault partnership can be arranged on its own timeline. The Council nomination cannot: the nomination window is not indefinite, and Davil needs to know before the tenday is out.

**If the character accepts the Council nomination:**

> > "Good. I'll send the name forward. Don't embarrass us."

Tashlyn exhales — not quite a laugh, but close. Istrid makes a note in the ledger.

Yagra extends a hand across the table. She shakes once, releases, and does not make a speech about it.

Davil picks up the lute. He doesn't play anything tonight. He just holds it.

**If the character declines the Council nomination:**

> > "That's a reasonable answer. The seat will find someone else. You still have everything I offered — eight operatives, the ward safe houses, the vault partnership if you want it. Nothing changes."

He means it. The warmth does not leave his voice.

Tashlyn looks at the character for the first time since they arrived. She is assessing something, and she is storing it away.

## Overview

Davil Starsong came in person. He brought the inner circle to the third-floor room at the Yawning Portal, named the character Dread Lord, and proposed them for a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council. The choice was genuine. The room waited for an answer.

## Summary

After reaching Renown 50, the character was named Dread Lord by Davil Starsong in a private room at the Yawning Portal, with Tashlyn Yafeera, Istrid Horn, and Yagra Stonefist present. Davil proposed the character for a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council and, if the vault had not yet resolved, offered formal partnership for the vault operation in exchange for a 10% share of the take. The character chose whether to accept or decline the nomination.
