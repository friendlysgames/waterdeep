# Design Notes: Dread Lord

## Five Founders and the Empty Chairs

The restored draft had Davil say "we started as six" and gathered "the surviving inner circle" as four people with Yagra among them. Appendix B gives five founders (Davil, Istrid, Skeemo, Tashlyn, Ziraj) and has Yagra join later, so Davil now says five, and Yagra stands at the door as the one who came after. The pushed-in chair is Skeemo's and branches on **Silencing Skeemo**: **Skeemo Silenced** gives Davil's grief, **Skeemo Captured** gives a refusal to say more, and **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** gives a man who sits across the city, with the name gated on **Manshoon Named**. Ziraj's absence branches on **Ziraj's Last Hunt**: **Ziraj Survived** keeps the original "he's on a job", and **Ziraj Fell** puts a second empty chair at the window and changes Tashlyn's remark, as that mission's outcome text promises. Istrid, Tashlyn and Yagra each have real lines and a job in the scene: Tashlyn gives the pool, Yagra the safe houses, Istrid the price and the vault question.

## Benefits, the Choice and Its Cost

The eight operatives are one Mage, two Warrior Veterans, two Spies, two Toughs and a Priest Acolyte, as in the mechanics reference, and the event offers the reference's three packages with its Power totals for levels 5 to 10 and from level 11. The Tier 3 package figures are derived from the reference's Tier 3 per-creature values. The safe houses are one per ward, all on Istrid's account, with a better deal than Wenna's loft from **Wolf** (no notice, five nights, one chased guest a night). The City of the Dead has none.

Both answers to the nomination cost something. Accepting buys a red jade Seal and a vote, and it costs a tenth of the gold from Network jobs and a duty to answer the Council's summons within a tenday. Declining loses the seat, and Tashlyn's name goes forward instead, with a second decliner's seat going to Istrid and a third going empty, so the Doom Raiders answer to someone else at the Council table. Tashlyn also stops volunteering help. The seat is per member, and the tenday window is the same as the draft's; silence counts as a refusal on the tenth day.

The vault partnership keeps the draft's three crews and ties each to a scene in **Vault of Dragons**: the distraction crew to Scene 5's faction arrival, the locksmith to the DC 22 steel door in Scene 2, and the wagons to the hoard in Scene 6. The tenth applies to gold the party actually keeps. This Event normally fires after the vault has resolved, so the offer usually lapses, and Istrid asks one question for the ledger instead of Davil making an offer.

## Renown, Timing and Open Items

The six missions give a member Renown 19 at most, so Renown 50 depends on thirty-one more points from sources the Factions Guide does not list beyond its small Earning Renown awards. The rank is meant for **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**, and that content will have to supply the points. This rank event awards none.

Invented: Toben Ash, and the six safe-house cover businesses and their goods, which are voiced by no one. The existing references are Wenna Tarrow's loft and Brannoc Hale from the earlier rank events, and Brannoc is named in Tashlyn's line and never speaks. Logged for the out-of-scope notes: the Factions Guide's Dread Lord row and Appendix B still describe the Council seat without dues, the Vault of Dragons structure doc reads "whatever was promised at Dread Lord renown" without the partnership terms, and **Skeemo Captured** depends on what **Davil's Return** decides, so the chair line is deliberately vague. The Notable Figures page for Yagra calls her a "Thug", a name that does not exist in the 2024 Monster Manual; this event gives her no combat role.
