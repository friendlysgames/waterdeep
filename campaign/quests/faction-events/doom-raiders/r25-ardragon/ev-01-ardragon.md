### The Yawning Portal, Upstairs

The lute is in his hands this time, not leaning against the wall. He is working through something — nothing recognizable, just intervals — when the party comes in. He sets it in his lap and stays in his chair. He does not stand.

> > "Ardragon. We don't give that one to people we aren't ready to rely on for the hard things."

He is quieter than he was before his arrest. The warmth is still there. It takes a moment longer to reach the surface.

He names the three benefits. They are larger than Wolf or Viper, and he treats them accordingly — not with ceremony, but with the specificity of someone handing over tools that could do real damage in the wrong hands.

**The crew.** Three Network operatives — two trained as spies, one as an assassin. Available to assist in one heist or operation per quest. Davil does not introduce them tonight; they will introduce themselves when deployed. They have been briefed on the party's operational style. He expects the party to treat them as professionals, because they are. If any of the three are killed or compromised during an operation, replacements require a tenday to arrange.

**The informants.** The Network has sources inside Manshoon's cell. These are real people in dangerous positions, and Davil is careful about how often he activates them. Once per quest, the party can request specific intelligence — a safe house location, the identity of a Splinter lieutenant, the schedule of a particular operative. He makes a DC 14 Intelligence check when the request goes in; on a failure, the informant is unavailable for two tendays. He does not explain the reason. The party should not ask.

**Ziraj's favor.** Ziraj the Hunter owes Davil something. Davil is transferring that debt to the party: once per campaign, they can request that Ziraj neutralize one named target — captured alive or quietly eliminated, their choice. Ziraj makes his own assessment of the assignment. He will not take something he considers suicidal, and he will not explain his refusal. If he says no, the conversation is over.

> > "Ziraj is not a weapon you aim. He's a professional who makes his own assessments. Keep that in mind before you name a target."

He picks up the lute again. He plays three notes — something that resolves — and sets it back on the wall.

> [!info]**Ziraj's Favor**
>
> Ziraj the Hunter communicates through dead drops rather than flying snakes. Requesting his favor requires routing the message through Davil; Ziraj will make contact on his own timeline, typically within three days. He operates from Waterdeep's rooftops and from the back table at Corellon's Crown. He considers suicidal assignments an affront, not a challenge. If the party's named target is someone Ziraj assesses as beyond his margin of acceptable risk, he will refuse without appeal. This is a feature of the arrangement, not a flaw.
