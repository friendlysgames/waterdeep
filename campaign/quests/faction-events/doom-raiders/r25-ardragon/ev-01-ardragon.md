# Ardragon

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Doom Raiders member reaches Renown 25. Davil Starsong names the rank in his curtained room at the Yawning Portal, and Tashlyn Yafeera explains the crews she keeps for it. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn how to call a crew of one assassin and two spies for one operation per quest.
> - Frame one question per quest for the Network's informants inside the rival cell.
> - Decide whether to name a target for Ziraj the Hunter's favour tonight or to hold it, or to take Yagra Stonefist's substitute if Ziraj is dead.
>
> All of these benefits belong to the qualifying member. Companions can join an operation, and they gain no rank and no favour of their own.

### Hook

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 25, a flying snake finds them with a note in Davil's looping hand asking them to come to the Yawning Portal at 20:00. Hold this Event until **Davil's Return** has played. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the snake waiting at their first surface lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the next evening at the same hour. Every qualifying member is invited and recorded separately, and companions who are not Doom Raiders members wait in the taproom.

The six missions bring a member to Renown 19 on their base awards, so this rank usually arrives shortly after **Ziraj's Last Hunt** and often after **Kolat Towers**. It can arrive earlier, and every procedure below works in either order.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> The Network's informants are three small people inside the rival cell, which is Manshoon's Splinter. Manshoon leads it, and Urstul Floxin is his field agent. Tashlyn recruited all three after Davil's arrest, and Skeemo Weirdbottle never learned their names, which is why they are still alive.
>
> In speech, the Doom Raiders say "Manshoon's cell" only if **Manshoon Named** is marked, and by this rank it nearly always is. Until then they say "Floxin's cell", "the other cell" or "the Splinter", and the lines below give both versions where the name matters.

### Naming the Rank

Davil is quieter than he was before his arrest, and the warmth reaches the surface a little later than it used to. Tashlyn stands through the whole meeting.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The curtained room on the second floor of the Yawning Portal is lit by its two hanging lamps, and this evening the lute is in Davil's hands instead of leaning against the wall. He is working through a slow run of notes that never settles into a tune, and when you come through the curtain he lays the instrument across his knees and stays in his chair. Tashlyn stands against the wall beside the door with her arms folded, and a decanter and a row of glasses wait on the round table.
>
> > "Ardragon. It's the name we give to people we would trust with the hard things, and I'm very glad to say it to you. Sit, please, and pour yourself a glass. There are three matters to go through, and Tashlyn will tell me afterwards that I explained them badly, yes?"

> [!social]**The Quiet Host**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: the leader of the Doom Raiders, back from Castle Waterdeep and slower to smile than he was.
>
> Davil is Friendly toward the member. He speaks in the same graceful sentences as before his arrest, but he leaves longer silences between them, and he turns a trophy on his bracelet or touches a string of the lute when he is deciding how much to say. He is exact about the three benefits and treats Ziraj's favour with the care of a man handing over a weapon. He does not swear, does not threaten and does not explain why he wants to know something.
>
> Davil is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The crews, which Tashlyn keeps.
> - The informants, whose names he will not give.
> - Ziraj's favour, and what Ziraj will and will not do.
> - Skeemo, briefly, and only if the member raises him.
>
> Any character who watches Davil while he talks and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** check sees that he has not slept well and that his eyes go to the lute whenever Skeemo's name is mentioned. He does not comment on either, and a character who misses the check loses nothing.

> [!qna]**About Skeemo?**
>
> Davil's thumb stops on the bracelet, and he answers according to what became of Skeemo.

If **Skeemo Silenced** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "He was clever and careful, and I was fond of him, which I would rather I hadn't been. He sold us for years, and you were the ones who ended it. I don't know what to feel about that, so I've decided to feel nothing for the moment."

If **Skeemo Captured** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "He isn't at liberty, and what became of him is a matter between Tashlyn and me. I would rather not talk about it, if you don't mind, and I'm grateful that you asked gently."

If **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "He went to the other cell with everything he knew about us, and as far as anyone can tell he is still with what's left of them. Be careful about planning in any room he has stood in, yes?"

> [!social]**The Keeper of the Crews**
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Arms and Mercenaries, who runs the cell's practical business and never sits during a briefing.
>
> Tashlyn is Indifferent toward the member's feelings and Friendly toward their competence. She gives orders in as few words as she can and expects the member to infer the rest. She swears at incompetence and never at a member who has earned a rank, and she says "Adequate" only when she means high praise.
>
> Tashlyn is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The two crews, their limits, and what happens when one is lost.
> - What she will and will not send her people to do.

### The Crews

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tashlyn unfolds a sheet of paper on the round table and puts one finger on the first of two short lists of names, without sitting down, and each list has three names on it in the small square hand you know from her flying snakes.
>
> > "Two crews. Each one is an assassin and two spies, and I trained every one of them myself. You give me three days and a plan, and they'll go wherever the plan goes."

> [!qna]**What will the crew do?**
>
> > "Whatever you ask inside reason. The assassin leads and the spies scout ahead of her. They won't touch children or bystanders, they won't hit a Watchman who hasn't hit them first, and they won't say the Network's name to anyone. Anything else is yours to plan."

> [!qna]**What if one of them dies?**
>
> > "Then I find a replacement, and that takes a tenday. You still spent the quest's use. So don't get my people killed for some fucking amateur plan."

> [!qna]**Can all of us have a crew?**
>
> > "Each of you has your own use, one per quest. If two of you ask for the same job, one crew goes, and only the first to ask spends a use. I have two crews and I don't have a third, so a third request waits."

> [!exploration]**One Crew per Quest**
>
> Each Ardragon member can call one crew for one heist or operation per quest. The member gives Tashlyn or Davil three days' notice, at the Yawning Portal or by flying snake, with the objective, the place and the hour the operation starts.
>
> - **Meeting:** The crew waits at a surface place in Waterdeep that the member names, at the hour the operation starts. The assassin leads the crew and takes the member's plan.
> - **Limits:** The crew refuses to attack children or bystanders, refuses to attack a Watch officer who has not attacked first, and refuses any act that would name the Doom Raiders. It withdraws when the objective is finished, abandoned or impossible, and when the assassin falls.
> - **Use:** The use counts against the quest the party is in when the crew begins the operation. A use that nobody called before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Losses:** A crew member who dies or is captured is replaced a tenday later. The use stays spent.
> - **Shared jobs:** Two members who ask for the same operation at the same hour share one crew, and only the first to ask spends a use. Two different operations in a quest can each have a crew, one per member, up to the two crews that exist.

> [!gamemaster]**The Crews as Allies**
>
> Crew One is the assassin Drell Hask (she/her) and the spies Tovin Marsh and Ilsa Carrow. Crew Two is the assassin Nessa Thorne (she/her) and the spies Garrick Pell and Bree Ostler. Use the **Assassin** stat block for each leader and the **Spy** stat block for each spy, all from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, unmodified.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, an Assassin is a CR 8 ally with Power 85 and a Spy is a CR 1 ally with Power 17, for a party of levels 5 to 10. A full crew adds 119. Count only the crew members who take part in a combat, add that to the participating characters' Party Power, and recalculate the difficulty and the day cost. A crew that is waiting somewhere else adds nothing, and above level 10 the figures need recalculating with the CR 2.0 encounter tables.
>
> A full crew roughly doubles a party of three or four, so build the fight around the larger total or keep the crew out of it. The Assassin uses its full Multiattack, because the two-attack cap in the Doom Raiders mechanics reference applies to enemy Assassins only.
>
> #### The Crew's Tactics
> The leader stays within 30 feet of the member and attacks whatever threatens the member or a spy. During combat, the crew:
>
> - Sends the spies to flank the same enemy as the leader, using Cunning Action to Hide and Disengage.
> - Falls back toward the exit the member named if the leader is reduced to half her Hit Points.
> - Stops when the member calls it off.

When a crew arrives for its operation, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Three people in plain dark coats are waiting at the place you named, and the tallest of them, a lean woman with a braid coiled under her hood, steps forward while the other two check the street in opposite directions.
>
> > "We're here for your job. Tell me where we go in and where we come out. The two with me will scout ahead, and we'll follow your lead until you say we're done."

If Crew Two comes, the assassin is Nessa Thorne and says the same things in her own words. Neither assassin gives a last name unless the member asks for one.

### The Informants

Davil introduces the informants after Tashlyn has finished with the crews. If **Manshoon Named** is marked, which it nearly always is by this rank, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil takes three small folded slips from the lute case and lays them in a row on the table, and each slip has a single word written on it in pencil, which he turns so the words face you as he speaks.
>
> > "Tashlyn has three people inside Manshoon's cell. They're small people in dangerous places, and I won't spend them carelessly. Each of you may send one question down the line in every quest, and I'll carry the answer back myself."

If **Manshoon Named** is not marked, he says "Floxin's cell" in place of "Manshoon's cell", so his first sentence reads:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Tashlyn has three people inside Floxin's cell."

> [!qna]**Who are they?**
>
> Davil touches each slip in turn.
>
> > "I'll give you the trades and the names they go by, and nothing more. Linen washes bedding for the cell's safe houses. Tally copies accounts in a counting-house the cell uses. Buckle carries a sword for one of their street crews. Tashlyn chose all three, and I'd rather not know how."

> [!qna]**Why do you need them?**
>
> > "Because Skeemo told the other cell everything for years, and I would like to hear what they say about us now. It's a small thing to want. It has cost me a great deal of sleep."

> [!gamemaster]**Three Protected Postings**
>
> Requests go through Davil or Tashlyn, who know the informants' real names and will not give them to the member:
>
> - **Linen**, Wren Tolliver: launders bedding and clothes for the cell's safe houses in the Dock and Trades Wards. She can report who sleeps where, who arrives hurt, and which houses are emptied or restocked. She hears nothing said in a room.
> - **Tally**, Bastian Quill: a junior clerk in a Trades Ward counting-house that keeps the cell's accounts. He can report payments, wages and shipments that cross his desk. He never sees the strongroom or the private ledgers.
> - **Buckle**, Hesper Lund: a hired sword on one of the cell's street crews. She can report the day's muster, patrol routes, the crew house's posted assignments and what the crews are saying. She knows no lieutenant's plans.
>
> If **Skeemo at Kolat Towers** is marked, Linen can report where Skeemo has slept and Tally can report what he has been paid. If the cell's known premises have fallen, the informants report on its surviving people, and they cannot supply a new headquarters.

> [!exploration]**One Question per Quest**
>
> Each Ardragon member can send one question per quest to one informant. The member names the informant and frames one specific question, such as a shift, a delivery, a patrol route or a person expected somewhere, and makes a **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** check to word it so the informant can answer without being noticed. The check is the member's own roll, and Davil does not make it for them.
>
> - **Success:** Davil brings the answer to the member at noon the next day, by flying snake or at the Yawning Portal. It covers what that informant could have seen, within the access listed above. If the question goes beyond that access, the answer states the informant's limits and reveals no secret.
> - **Failure:** The informant is unavailable to this member for two tendays (20 days). The informant is neither dead nor exposed, and the quest's use is spent.
> - **After an answer:** The quest's use is spent. The member can ask the next informant in the next quest, and a different member can send their own question in the same quest.

When a successful question is answered within an informant's access, Davil reads out what the informant saw, and you write the observed facts from the campaign record beneath the matching introduction. Keep them to what that person could have seen:

> [!readaloud]
>
> **Linen:** "I've been through the houses on my round, and I've written down who was sleeping there and who came and went. I only saw it, and I heard none of it."
>
> **Tally:** "I went through the entries that crossed my desk in the days you named. The payments and shipments are copied out below, and I never saw the strongroom."
>
> **Buckle:** "I stood muster with the crew and read the board at the crew house. What I saw is written down, and anything I only heard is marked."

If a successful question goes beyond the informant's access, Davil reads the limits:

> [!readaloud]
>
> **Linen:** "I can tell you who sleeps in the houses I wash for. I can't tell you what they say in them."
>
> **Tally:** "I see what reaches a clerk's desk. The strongroom and the private books are locked to me."
>
> **Buckle:** "I know my crew's orders and the muster board. The lieutenants keep their plans to themselves."

If the Intelligence check fails, Davil gives the member the consequence:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil sets one of the slips aside on the table and puts his glass down on top of it, and then he turns to face you before he speaks.
>
> > "That line has gone silent, and I'd like to believe it's only caution. Give it twenty days before you try that one again. The other two are still open to you, in the next quest."

### Ziraj's Favour

> [!gamemaster]**Ziraj and the Debt**
>
> Ziraj the Hunter kills only when someone he cares about asks, and Davil asked him long ago. Davil now passes that debt to the member: once in the whole campaign, Ziraj will take one named person, alive or dead, without being told why. This is the member's own favour, tracked by name, and a second Ardragon member has a favour of their own.
>
> If **Ziraj Survived** is marked, use the first reading. If **Ziraj's Last Hunt** has not played, use the second, and tell the table that Ziraj is alive and on the roofs. If **Ziraj Fell** is marked, use the third.

If **Ziraj Survived** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil turns a charm on his bracelet and looks at the lute on his knees for a while before he lifts his eyes to you and begins to speak.
>
> > "Ziraj came to me himself after the night on the roofs. He said he owed you, and I pointed out that he already owed me, and we agreed that a single piece of work could settle both debts. So the work is yours to name. He will deal with one person for you, once, taken alive or killed where no one will find the reason. He decides for himself whether the job is sensible, and if he refuses, he won't tell you why."

If **Ziraj's Last Hunt** has not played, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil turns a charm on his bracelet and looks at the lute on his knees for a while before he lifts his eyes to you and begins to speak.
>
> > "Ziraj has owed me for longer than I like to remember, and I'm passing that debt to you. He will deal with one person for you, once, taken alive or killed where no one will find the reason. He decides for himself whether the job is sensible, and if he refuses, he won't tell you why."

If **Ziraj Fell** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Davil sets the lute down on the round table and keeps one hand on its neck while he looks across at you and speaks.
>
> > "Ziraj's favour went into the ground with him. I won't pretend otherwise, and I won't offer you something I can't give."
>
> The curtain moves behind Tashlyn, and Yagra Stonefist comes in without a greeting and stands behind your chair with her arms folded, looking at nothing in particular while she waits for Davil to finish.
>
> > "He told me what to do if he went first. You point at someone, and I'll go and get them, and I'll bring them back to you breathing. I won't fucking kill them for you, because that was his job and I'm not doing it."
>
> Davil looks down at the lute and the table between you for a moment, and then he lifts his head and goes on.
>
> > "It is a poorer thing than what he would have given you, and I am sorry for it."

> [!exploration]**Ziraj's Favour**
>
> The member gives Davil, or Yagra at the Yawning Portal bar, a sealed note naming one person, the place that person will be within the next ten days, and whether they are to be taken alive or killed. Ziraj hunts places, so a note without a place is returned unread. The note goes through Davil, who reads it, and he puts the name in his book.
>
> - **Answer:** Within three days, Ziraj scratches his mark on the windowsill of the member's lodging. Three short strokes mean he accepts, and one stroke means he refuses. A refusal does not spend the favour.
> - **What he refuses:** Ziraj refuses the four villain factions' leaders (the Xanathar, Manshoon, Jarlaxle, and Victoro or Ammalia Cassalanter), anyone inside a warded stronghold, force field or vault, any child, any Masked Lord or the Open Lord, any sitting Watch officer, and anyone who will not be inside Waterdeep's wards or harbour. A member who names a refused target can send a new note.
> - **If he accepts:** Within ten days of the note, at the place named, Ziraj does the work. Yagra brings word to the member at the Yawning Portal bar the same evening.
> - **Killed:** The target is dead, and nothing in the death points to the member or to the Doom Raiders.
> - **Alive:** The target waits in the cellar of Istrid Horn's Dock Ward warehouse, unharmed beyond what the taking needed. The member can question them there for up to a tenday, and afterwards Istrid's people carry them to any place in the city the member names. Use the target's Notable Figures page for what they know.
> - **Used:** The favour is spent when Ziraj accepts. It is the member's for the rest of the campaign until then.

> [!exploration]**Yagra's Grab**
>
> This replaces Ziraj's favour if **Ziraj Fell** is marked. It follows the same note, the same three-day answer (Yagra comes in person instead of scratching a mark) and the same refusals, and it takes a living target only. Yagra and two **Tough** hired hands do the work within ten days, and she refuses any target guarded by more than six armed defenders. The target is delivered to Istrid's cellar as above. It is once per member for the whole campaign, and it is spent when Yagra accepts.
>
> If a member holds an unspent favour from Ziraj when **Ziraj Fell** is marked, it becomes Yagra's Grab at that moment. A favour that was already spent stays spent.

> [!qna]**Do I have to decide tonight?**
>
> > "No, and I'd rather you didn't decide in a hurry. You may keep it as long as you like. But Ziraj is one man on a great many roofs, and if he falls while you hold it, the favour goes with him, and you'd have Yagra's hands and nobody else's. Once you name someone, it's done, and I'll want to know the name."

> [!qna]**What if Ziraj refuses?**
>
> > "Then you still have the favour, and you may name someone else. He won't explain, and please don't press him. He takes a suicidal job as an insult, and I've never known him to be wrong about which ones they are."

If the member names a target tonight, Davil writes the name, the place and the choice in his leather book. The note procedure above then runs from that evening. If the member holds the favour, Davil closes the book and says it will keep.

### Renown Opportunities

This rank awards no Renown, gold or Milestone Points. The informants' answers are Davil's own intelligence and do not count as a member's report of the rival cell. Each member earns further Renown from the missions and from the ordinary rewards in the Doom Raiders page of the Factions Guide.

### Aftermath

The member can tell their companions what the crew and the informants can do. The crews, the questions and the favour stay individual benefits, and a companion who asks in the member's name is turned away. Tashlyn sends the next briefing in her own hand.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member knows how to call a crew and send an informant question and has decided about the favour. Record each member's uses separately: the quest each benefit was last used in, the date an informant became unavailable, and whether the favour is held, spent or converted.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Ardragon Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Davil names the rank; read by **Dread Lord** and by **Kolat Towers** Scene 1 if the rank arrives before the raid, where the crew and Ziraj's favour are available resources.
> - **Ziraj's Favour Used** — mark with the member's name, the target, and whether Ziraj or Yagra acted, when the favour is accepted; read by **Dread Lord**, where Tashlyn mentions it.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Crews need three days' notice, informant questions are once per quest, and the favour is once in the campaign. **Dread Lord** occurs when this member reaches Renown 50, usually during **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Davil Starsong names a Doom Raiders member Ardragon and explains the Network's crews, its informants inside the rival cell and the favour of Ziraj the Hunter.

## Summary

We were made Ardragons of the Doom Raiders, and Tashlyn told us how to call a crew of spies and an assassin. Davil showed us the three informants inside the other cell, and he gave each of us a favour from Ziraj, or from Yagra if Ziraj is gone. Each of us decided whether to name a target or keep it.
