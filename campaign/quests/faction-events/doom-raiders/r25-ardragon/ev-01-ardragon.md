# Made Ardragon

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one character's Doom Raiders renown reaches 25. Davil Starsong delivers the promotion — he is back from Watch custody by the time this threshold is typically reached, after completing Missions 5 and 6. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the promotion to Ardragon rank from Davil, with more deliberateness than either of the earlier promotions.
> - Hear the three Ardragon benefits named: the three-person operational crew, the activated informants inside Manshoon's cell, and Ziraj the Hunter's favor.
> - Watch Davil play three notes on the lute and set it back on the wall.

### The Yawning Portal, Upstairs

The lute is in Davil's hands this time, not leaning against the wall. He is working through something — nothing recognizable, just intervals — when the party comes in. He sets the lute across his lap without rising.

> [!readaloud]
> The lute is in his hands when you come in, and he does not set it down right away. He works through a few intervals, watching you settle, and then lays it across his lap.
>
> > "Ardragon. We don't give that one to people we aren't ready to rely on for the hard things."
>
> He is quieter than he was before the arrest, the warmth slower to arrive now. He names each of the three benefits with the deliberateness of someone handing over tools that could do real damage in the wrong hands.

> [!social]**Davil Starsong**
>
> Davil Starsong (Neutral, Sun Elf, he/him) :: a sun elf fixer who presents favors as gifts and debts as opportunities; more deliberate since the arrest, the warmth slower to arrive.
>
> Davil treats the Ardragon promotion differently from Wolf or Viper. He flags each benefit's potential for collateral damage before handing it over, and his fondness for the party is specific: he knows what they did across six missions and speaks from that knowledge rather than from general goodwill.
>
> His characteristic "yes?" appears only once or twice here. The lute in his hands is the ceremony.
>
> Conversation topics Davil is willing to discuss include:
> - The three-person crew: availability, deployment, and replacement conditions.
> - The informants: activation conditions, the check, and the cooldown.
> - Ziraj's favor: how to request it and what Ziraj will refuse.
>
> Specific dialogue for Davil on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**About the crew?**
>
> > "Three Network operatives — two trained as spies, one as an assassin. Available to assist in one heist or operation per quest. They've been briefed on your operational style." He pauses. "They are professionals. I expect you to treat them as such. If any of them are killed or compromised, replacements require a tenday to arrange."

> [!qna]**About the informants?**
>
> His fingers find the charm bracelet and turn one of the small trophies.
>
> > "The Network has sources inside Manshoon's cell. Real people in dangerous positions, and I am careful about how often I activate them. Once per quest, you can request specific intelligence — a safe house location, a lieutenant's identity, a particular operative's schedule." He holds the trophy without releasing it. "I'll make a check when the request goes in; on a failure, the informant is unavailable for two tendays. I won't explain the reason, and I'd appreciate you not asking, yes?"

> [!gamemaster]**Activating the Informants**
>
> When the party submits an activation request, make a DC 14 Intelligence check on Davil's behalf. On a success, the requested intelligence arrives within 24 hours by flying snake. On a failure, the informant is unavailable for two tendays; Davil says only that the timing is wrong.

> [!qna]**About Ziraj's favor?**
>
> > "Ziraj owes me something. I'm transferring that debt to you — once per campaign, you can request that he neutralize one named target, captured alive or killed, your choice." He looks at the lute rather than at the party. "He makes his own assessment of the assignment. If he says the risk is beyond what he'll accept, he'll decline without appeal, and the conversation is over. Ziraj is not a weapon you aim. He's a professional who makes his own assessments. Keep that in mind before you name a target."

He picks up the lute again, plays three notes — something that resolves — and sets it back on the wall.

> [!gamemaster]**Ziraj's Favor: Logistics**
>
> Ziraj communicates through dead drops rather than flying snakes. Requesting his favor requires routing the message through Davil; Ziraj makes contact on his own timeline, typically within three days. He operates from Waterdeep's rooftops and from the back table at Corellon's Crown. He considers suicidal assignments an affront, not a challenge. If the party's named target is someone he assesses as beyond his margin of acceptable risk, he refuses without appeal.

## Overview

Davil Starsong acknowledged the promotion to Ardragon rank at the Yawning Portal with the lute in his hands. He named the three-person crew, the Network's informants inside Manshoon's cell, and Ziraj the Hunter's favor — and played three notes when he was done.

## Summary

After reaching Renown 25, the character was promoted to Ardragon rank by Davil Starsong. He described the operational crew available for heists, the conditions for activating the Network's informants inside Manshoon's cell, and the once-per-campaign favor Ziraj the Hunter owes to the party. He ended by setting the lute back on the wall.
