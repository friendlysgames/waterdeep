# Design Notes: Ardragon

## Benefits as Procedures

The restored draft named three benefits and left each as a sentence, with the informant check rolled by Davil and the favour "once per campaign" for the whole party. Each benefit is now a procedure that belongs to one member. The crew is a named pair of three-person crews with a stated notice, meeting, limits and loss rule, and the ledger stops at two crews so a party cannot stack them. The informants are three code-named people with stated access, and the DC 14 check is the member's own roll, written as Intelligence (Investigation) because the member is wording a question, not recalling a fact. A failed check spends the quest's use as well as benching the informant for two tendays, which is how the Factions Guide reads.

Tashlyn recruited the three informants after Davil's arrest, and Skeemo never learned their names. That is an invented fact, and it answers an obvious table question: how did informants survive a traitor at the top of the cell. The crew's Power (119 for levels 5 to 10) follows the mechanics reference, which gives no Tier 3 figure for the Assassin, so the event tells the GM to recalculate above level 10.

## The Favour and the Decision

The decision beat is the favour. Davil lets the member name a target tonight or hold it, and holding has a real cost: if Ziraj falls while the favour is unspent, it converts to Yagra's Grab, which takes a living target only and refuses guarded ones. Naming a target costs the member too, because the note goes through Davil and he writes the name in his book. Ziraj's refusal list is written out (faction leaders, warded strongholds, children, Masked Lords, Watch officers, anyone outside the wards) so "suicidal" is a stated rule and not a judgment call at the table.

The event reads the outcomes of **Ziraj's Last Hunt**. **Ziraj Survived** gives the rescue line. **Ziraj Fell** replaces the favour with Yagra's Grab, voiced by Yagra at her profile's swearing level. If the mission has not played, Ziraj is treated as alive. The mission's outcome text says that after **Ziraj Fell** Davil "says nothing of Ziraj's favor"; this event instead has him say so plainly and offer Yagra's substitute, which is logged below.

## Renown and Timing

The six missions award 2, 2, 3, 3, 4 and 4 base Renown, which brings a member from Renown 1 to 19. Rank 25 therefore needs at least six Renown from outside the missions: the bonus Renown listed in each mission and the items under Earning Renown in the Factions Guide. This rank event awards none of its own. Informant answers are Davil's intelligence, so they do not count as a member's report of the rival cell. The rank usually lands after **Kolat Towers** and **Ziraj's Last Hunt**, but every procedure works if it arrives first, and the event holds until **Davil's Return** has played.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: the crews (Drell Hask, Tovin Marsh, Ilsa Carrow; Nessa Thorne, Garrick Pell, Bree Ostler), the informants (Wren Tolliver as Linen, Bastian Quill as Tally, Hesper Lund as Buckle), the scratched-mark answer, and Istrid's cellar as the place captives are held. The crew leaders speak one line each and the informants speak only through Davil, all voiced from the event text. The Quiet Host and Keeper of the Crews use the Davil and Tashlyn profiles; Yagra uses hers.

Logged for the out-of-scope notes: the Factions Guide says Ziraj's favour is once per campaign and gives the crew and informants no stated limits beyond the check. **Ziraj's Last Hunt** says Davil omits the favour if Ziraj dies. The Doom Raiders guide and the **Kolat Towers** and **Vault of Dragons** structure docs still describe the mission outputs from the old draft. The lore page puts Istrid's lending in the South Ward while the First Meeting puts her warehouse in the Dock Ward, and this event follows the warehouse. **Davil's Return** is being rewritten, so the **Skeemo Captured** line deliberately says nothing about what Davil decided.
