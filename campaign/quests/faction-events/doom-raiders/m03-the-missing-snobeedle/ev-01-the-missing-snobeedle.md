# The Missing Snobeedle

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Investigation Event begins when Tashlyn briefs the Doom Raiders members at the South Gate and ends when they report back to her. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Tashlyn's brief and visit the Snobeedle Orchard to hear Blossom's account of her son.
> - Spend three days in the Southern Ward following three independent leads to the Shard Shunners.
> - Arrange a meeting with Dasher Snobeedle by earning the ward's trust or by pressing it.
> - Meet Dasher and Kelso Fiddlewick at the Waymoot at highsun, and hear Dasher's offer of 200 gp and his news about Emmek Frewn's money.
> - Claim the reward, buy Dasher's silence or walk away, and broker a meeting between Dasher and his mother.
> - Take Dasher's claim to Istrid Horn.
>
> Only Doom Raiders members attend Tashlyn's brief and debrief. Their companions can help with every other part of the mission.

> [!gamemaster]**Who Knows What**
>
> Dasher left home about six months ago, made friends among the halflings of the Southern Ward and met Kelso Fiddlewick. After two weeks of thinking it over, he asked Kelso to bite him. Every halfling in the ward who matters knows where he is and will not say so to a stranger. Blossom suspects most of the truth already. Tashlyn knows only what is written on the notice, and nobody in the Doom Raiders connects the Shard Shunners to Emmek's debts until Dasher says so.

### The Brief

Tashlyn meets the invited members in the wagon yard beside the South Gate at 17:00, after a flying snake brought the summons that morning. She briefs members only and does not sit. Companions wait by the yard gate until the members come out.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A line of freight wagons waits along the wall of the wagon yard while the drivers eat from tin plates and a mule stamps in its traces. A tall Calishite woman in a City Guard cloak stands beside the last wagon with her arms folded, and she watches you cross the yard without stepping forward to meet you.
>
> > You're on time, which is adequate. Davil is at Castle Waterdeep, so you answer to me now, and I don't repeat myself.
>
> She holds out a torn notice, still creased where it was nailed to a post, and waits until one of you has taken it.
>
> > The Snobeedles are the richest halflings in Waterdeep, and their youngest son, Dasher, has been gone six months. They're offering five hundred gold for word of him, and we could use five hundred gold. Find out where he is, and do it without giving the Watch a reason to look at you, since they already have a file on every one of us.

> [!social]**The Captain in the Yard**
>
> Tashlyn Yafeera (Lawful Evil, Calishite Human, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Arms and Mercenaries, running the cell while Davil is held.
>
> Tashlyn is in business mode. She stands through the whole briefing, finishes any answer the members are too slow to give, and never says please. She swears only at incompetence, and she has no patience for questions the notice already answers. She wants the reward for the Network and expects the members to bring it to her, and she will not say why the cell needs it so badly.
>
> Conversation topics Tashlyn is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The Snobeedle family, the notice and where it was posted.
> - The reward and the members' share.
> - The Watch, and what she expects the members to avoid.
> - Davil, if someone asks, and only to close the subject.

> [!qna]**Who are the Snobeedles?**
>
> > Halflings with an orchard and a meadery in Undercliff, east of the walls. The mother is Blossom, and she's the one who posted the notice. Look at the market by the Waymoot and the Yondalla shrine on Larkhaven Street, because that's where she nailed the rest.

> [!qna]**Where do we start?**
>
> Tashlyn taps the torn edge of the notice.
>
> > Go to the orchard if you want the mother's account, or to the Southern Ward if you want the son. He worked and lodged down there before he vanished, and halflings talk to halflings, so I'm giving you three days.

> [!qna]**What do we get?**
>
> > The Network takes the five hundred when the family pays, and you get a tenth of it, fifty gold, for the party to divide as you like. The rest of your pay is Renown, and I decide how much.

> [!qna]**What about the Watch?**
>
> > If I hear the Watch picked you up over some fucking halfling brawl, you'll answer to me before you answer to them, and I want this finished without anybody dead or arrested.

> [!qna]**What about Davil?**
>
> Her jaw tightens for a moment and then she looks toward the gate.
>
> > That's not your business. He's held at the castle until the Lords stop being stupid, and until then you report to me, so is there anything else?

### The Orchard

The Snobeedle Orchard and Meadery lies in Undercliff, a morning's walk east of the city. The visit is optional. It gives the party Dasher's description, a lead in the Southern Ward and, if the members listen carefully, a way to earn the halflings' trust later.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Rows of apple trees run down a gentle slope east of the city, with pickers on ladders in the lower rows and a long, low drinking hall at the bottom of the hill. The shop beside it is built for larger customers, and its door stands open on the smell of cider and warm honey. An old halfling woman with a walking stick waits on the porch with a tray of cups, and she begins pouring as you reach the steps.
>
> > You'll be here about Dasher. Come in and sit down, because the tea has been warm since I saw you on the road, and I'd sooner talk about him inside than in front of the pickers.

> [!social]**The Woman on the Porch**
>
> Blossom Snobeedle (Unaligned, Halfling, she/her) :: an old druid who runs the family orchard and has spent six months searching for her youngest son.
>
> Blossom is capable and kind, and she talks to the party as she would to a neighbour who might turn out to be a friend. She brings out a painted portrait of Dasher, a round-faced halfling with a wide grin, and she sets it on the table beside the cups. She wants her son alive and safe, and she would sooner have a letter in his own hand than a search that ends with him dragged home.
>
> Conversation topics Blossom is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What Dasher was like, and why she thinks he left.
> - The silver, if someone asks about his health.
> - The notices, the reward, and how she will pay it.
> - What she will do if he does not want to come home.
>
> Any character who listens to her and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that she already suspects a curse of some kind and is bracing for the answer. She will say so only if a character asks her directly.

> [!qna]**About Dasher?**
>
> > He was always the restless one. He hated the meadery, though he never said so until the last year, and he took work in the city to get away from the orchard. He lodged with friends in the Southern Ward and came home for the harvest suppers. Then one day the suppers stopped, and so did the letters.

> [!qna]**About his health?**
>
> Blossom looks at her cup for a moment before she answers.
>
> > Last year he stopped touching the silver at table. He said it gave him a rash, and I've never seen a halfling boy so careful about a spoon. He'd also go down to the cellar for three nights around the full moon and say he had a headache. I'm old, dear, but I'm not blind.

> [!qna]**About the reward?**
>
> > Five hundred gold, paid when I've seen him or held a letter in his own hand saying he's alive. I don't need him carried home, but I do need to know he's eating and that he knows the door is open.

> [!qna]**And if he won't come home?**
>
> > Then I'd sooner hear it from him than from a stranger. If you find him, tell him I'd take a letter over a lecture, and that his mother isn't the Watch.

> [!exploration]**Dasher's Portrait**
>
> Blossom lets the party keep the portrait. Any character who examines it and makes a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) check notices a thin white scar on Dasher's chin that the painter softened. Halflings in the Southern Ward recognise him from the portrait without a check.

### Three Days in the Southern Ward

The halfling community of the Southern Ward gathers in three places: the market by the Waymoot, the Yondalla shrine on Larkhaven Street, and a run of rented rooms above a cooperage on the same street. Each lead takes a day to work properly. A party that splits up can finish all three sooner, but nobody in the ward answers before the evening of the third day, because the message has to reach Kelso Fiddlewick and come back. Whichever lead the party follows, it arrives at the same fact: the Shard Shunners have Dasher, and the ward will carry a request to their leader.

Each lead can be worked in two ways. A party that spends the day being ordinary and decent earns a **Trust mark** and gets the fact freely. A party that bribes or threatens gets the same fact at once but earns no mark, and the halflings talk about it afterward.

> [!exploration]**Trust in the Southern Ward**
>
> The party earns one Trust mark for each lead where it does the ordinary, decent thing described there. A single threat from anyone in the party, at any lead, ends all future Trust marks, and a bribe of 10 gp or more gets the fact at that lead but no mark. The marks set the difficulty of the check that arranges the meeting, described under **Asking for the Meeting**.
>
> - **Three marks:** the check is DC 14 and the character has Advantage.
> - **Two marks and no threats:** the check is DC 14.
> - **Fewer than two marks, or any threat:** the check is DC 18.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The market beside the Waymoot fills a wide stretch of paved road south of the signpost, with halfling stalls selling pies, pins and pots of jam under bright awnings. Hawkers in scarves shout offers to guide travelers for a copper. A round-cheeked woman behind a pie stall counts change into a jar, and when you set Dasher's portrait beside her cash box she goes completely still.
>
> > I don't know where he is.

> [!social]**The Woman at the Pie Stall**
>
> Pippa Underbough (Unaligned, Halfling, she/her) :: a pie seller who once gave Dasher work on market days and now carries messages for the ward.
>
> Pippa is not hostile, only precise. She has said "I don't know where he is" to three other people this tenday, and she says it the same way every time. She likes anyone who buys a pie, stays to help fold boxes or carry a crate, and asks about her business before asking about him. She will not answer questions from someone who threatens her stall.
>
> Any character who watches her and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that she is lying to protect Dasher, not to hurt anyone.
>
> To earn a Trust mark, the party spends the day at her stall, buying, carrying and listening, and asks nothing about Dasher until she brings him up herself. Pippa then says the following:

> [!qna]**About Dasher?**
>
> > He helped me on market days for a whole summer, and he never once short-changed a customer. He's well and he's with people who look after him, and that's all I'll say to strangers, but if someone asks properly, I know the boy who'd carry a message to the man who'd know.

> [!qna]**Can you pass on a message?**
>
> > I can pass it on, but I won't promise what comes back. You've been decent to me all day, and I'll say so when I speak for you, so come to my stall on the evening of the third day and I'll tell you what he says.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The shrine of Yondalla on Larkhaven Street is a whitewashed cottage with a round green door and a hearth burning inside, and a few dozen halflings crowd the pews for the evening blessing. A gray-haired halfling in a plain robe sets fresh candles in the holders along the wall, and he glances up as you come in and then goes back to the candles.

> [!social]**The Keeper of the Hearth**
>
> Tolliver Greenbottle (Unaligned, Halfling, he/him) :: the shrine's caretaker, who watched Dasher grow from a lodger into someone who no longer belongs there.
>
> Tolliver answers questions at his own pace, and he answers them best when the party has first paid respect. A visitor who leaves an offering of at least 5 gp in the hearth box and stays for the whole blessing earns a Trust mark. He does not care for coin pushed across the offering plate to buy an answer.

> [!qna]**Has Dasher been here?**
>
> > He came every rest-day evening to light a candle for his mother, up to about three moons ago, and he doesn't come now. But on the first night of every tenday there's a coin in the box that wasn't there the night before, and I know whose hand puts it there.

> [!qna]**Can you get word to him?**
>
> > I can leave a note in the box, and the person who empties it will read it, though I can't promise he'll answer. If he does, I won't be able to make him do anything.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A narrow stair beside the cooperage on Larkhaven Street leads to a row of rented rooms, and the smell of oak shavings and damp barrel staves hangs in the passage. A stout halfling woman is scrubbing the landing with a brush, and she straightens up with her hands on her hips as you reach the top step.

> [!social]**The Landlady**
>
> Marda Goodbarrel (Unaligned, Halfling, she/her) :: the landlady of the rooms where Dasher once lodged, who was paid up in full by a stranger the week he left.
>
> Marda is short-tempered with anyone who arrives asking for something and warmer with anyone who offers to help. The party earns a Trust mark by carrying her water up the stairs or mending the landing rail, or by telling her honestly what Blossom said about wanting a letter more than a capture, if the party visited the orchard. She will not talk to anyone who dismisses the family's worry.

> [!qna]**Who paid the rent?**
>
> > A compact little fellow with a scar along his jaw, and the manners of a cheerful undertaker. He paid six months in advance, in silver, and took the room's contents away in a handcart. He said Dasher had gone into business with friends, and that he came down from the Field Ward every tenday to look in on things.

> [!qna]**What did he call himself?**
>
> > Nothing at first, but one day he left a note under the door about the rats in the cellar and signed it "the lads." I've never seen a man so pleased about a rat.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Ward Adds**
>
> Whichever lead the party works, the party can gather the following before the meeting. Dasher Snobeedle disappeared about six months ago. The family is deeply distressed and has posted notices in the ward. Several of his old friends in the Southern Ward have grown careful about who they talk to. The scarred halfling from the Field Ward is Kelso Fiddlewick, and anyone who has met him in **The Shard Shunners** recognises the description at once.

> [!exploration]**Asking for the Meeting**
>
> On the evening of the third day, Pippa waits for the party at her stall after it closes. Any character who speaks for the party and makes a successful Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check persuades her to arrange the meeting. The DC is 18, or 14 with two or more Trust marks and no threats, and a party with all three marks has Advantage.
>
> On a success, Pippa tells them that Dasher will meet them at the Waymoot at highsun the next day, and that he will bring friends.
>
> On a failure, Pippa shakes her head and says the ward will talk about it. The next morning a halfling boy finds the party and gives them the same message anyway. Kelso decided he would rather choose the ground himself than have the party keep asking. The meeting still happens at the Waymoot at highsun, but five more Shunners watch from the rooftops, Kelso does the talking, and Dasher's offer stays the same.

### The Waymoot at Highsun

The Waymoot is the crossroads at the south of the city where the High Road meets the Way of the Dragon. A small paved square just off it has a dry fountain, and the meeting takes place there at noon on the day after the third evening.

> [!readaloud]
>
> At the crossroads in the south of the city the tall signpost of the Waymoot points its painted arrows toward every gate, and hawkers in bright scarves call out offers to guide travelers for a copper. A little way off, a small square holds a dry fountain with a cracked basin, and the sun is nearly overhead as the bell of a nearby shrine rings noon.
>
> A halfling walks into the square without looking around him, a compact young man in a good wool coat with the easy stride of someone who has nowhere he would rather be. Two more halflings follow at a distance, one with a scar along his jaw and one a woman with a hand crossbow on her belt, and they stop beside a fruit cart close enough to help if it comes to that. The first halfling sits on the rim of the fountain and pats the stone beside him.
>
> > You're the people who've been asking about me. Sit down if you like, and let's have the conversation.

> [!social]**The Halfling Who Isn't Lost**
>
> Dasher Snobeedle (Neutral, Halfling, he/him) :: a wererat by choice, cheerful in the way of someone whose life is going well.
>
> Dasher is around thirty, compact and easy in his own skin. He was unhappy at home, in a family whose wealth and expectations were both larger than his interest, and he has found a community that he chose. He has harmed nobody. He knows the party works for the Doom Raiders, because word travels in the Southern Ward, and he is perfectly polite about it. He is not going home, and he says so without hostility.
>
> Conversation topics Dasher is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Where he has been, and why he left.
> - Why he joined the Shard Shunners, and why he chose the curse.
> - His mother, which is the only subject that makes him hesitate.
> - His offer to the party.

> [!qna]**Where have you been?**
>
> > I've been here and there, mostly in the Field Ward these days, with people who look after each other. I haven't been hiding exactly, but I didn't know how to write to the orchard without sounding like I was asking to come back.

> [!qna]**Why the Shard Shunners?**
>
> > Because they wanted me for what I could do, and not for what my family could pay. Kelso doesn't care about the meadery or the money. He cares whether I turn up when I said I would, and I've never had anyone ask that little of me and expect so much.

> [!qna]**Why choose the curse?**
>
> Dasher glances at the halfling with the scarred jaw, who shrugs.
>
> > I took two weeks to decide, and it isn't a punishment if you go in wanting it. I sleep well, I eat well, and I've never felt so much like myself. I know what my mother's going to think it is, but it isn't that.

> [!qna]**What about your mother?**
>
> > She's looking, and she should be. If she asks, I'd tell her I'm alive and not sick, and that I love her, and that I can't be what she wanted, but I haven't worked out how to say it so it doesn't sound like goodbye.

Kelso Fiddlewick stands by the fruit cart with Brynn Hilltopple. How he greets the party depends on what it has already done with him.

If the party marked **Mediation Path Taken** in **The Shard Shunners**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The halfling with the scarred jaw lifts two fingers from the fruit cart in greeting.
>
> > Well, if it isn't the mediators. The good news is that I'm in a generous mood, partner, but the shit news is that you're here about Dasher, and I can't be generous about him.

If the party ran **The Shard Shunners** but did not mark **Mediation Path Taken**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The halfling with the scarred jaw folds his arms and tilts his head as you come nearer.
>
> > You lot again. The lads remember the Endshift, partner, and so do I. I'll be civil, because it's daylight and there are Watchmen walking the crossroads, but the lads won't like this one bit.

If the party has not run that mission, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The halfling with the scarred jaw steps away from the cart and offers a small, neat bow.
>
> > I'm Kelso, of the Shard Shunners. Dasher says you've been asking about him all over the Southern Ward, partner, and I'd sooner hear the rest of it from you than from the lads on the rooftops.

> [!social]**The Smallest Gang Leader**
>
> Kelso Fiddlewick (Neutral, Lightfoot Halfling, he/him) :: the cheerful, scarred, businesslike leader of the Shard Shunners, who keeps every bargain and mentions the teeth only once.
>
> Kelso stands beside Brynn Hilltopple with his hands where everyone can see them, and he watches the party's hands more than its faces. He does not want a fight in broad daylight. He is more interested in what the party will do than what it says, and he will remember either. If the party marked **Traitor Identified** in **The Doppelganger Problem**, it knows Bonnie named him as a buyer of stolen information. He answers questions about that the same way every time: he sells what people will pay for, and he does not name his customers.
>
> Conversation topics Kelso is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Dasher, and how the Shunners treat their own.
> - The job on Trollskull Alley, if someone raises it.
> - The contract terms he expects, and what happens if they are broken.

> [!qna]**About Emmek Frewn?**
>
> > Frewn? Yes, the lads did the Trollskull job, partner, and the cask on the parade day was the wrong building, and I did send a note. No hard feelings, I hope. Contract's a contract, and we were paid to make a nuisance of ourselves, not to hurt anyone.

> [!qna]**What if we tell his family?**
>
> > Then you tell them, and I remember it. Dasher's choice is his own, partner, and I'd sooner you didn't take it from him. But the lads will be watching to see what you do, and so will I.

At this point Dasher makes his offer.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Dasher leans back on the fountain's rim and looks at each of you in turn, still friendly.
>
> > I know what the notice says. It says "safe return," and I am safe, because I've returned to myself, if you like. I'm not going home, and that's settled, so the only question is whether you tell them where I am.
>
> He takes a leather purse from inside his coat and sets it on the stone beside him.
>
> > There's two hundred gold here from the Shard Shunners' fund. I'm not calling it a bribe, because I'd rather call it what it is, which is the price of silence, so say you couldn't find me and it's yours.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Dasher waits a moment for an answer, and when none comes he lowers his voice and adds another thing, this one with no price on it.
>
> > Separate from all that, and you can have it whichever way you decide. The pub man on Trollskull Alley, Frewn. The money he paid us with came in a purse sealed with Istrid Horn's mark, and two of her collectors sat in a corner while he hired us. I thought you might like to know how your own people keep an eye on you.

> [!gamemaster]**What Dasher Knows**
>
> Emmek Frewn borrowed 150 gp from Istrid Horn and spent 50 gp of it hiring Kelso's gang against the party's tavern. Two of Istrid's collectors were in the room when he did. Istrid heard about it from them and did nothing to stop it, because a borrower who spends is a borrower who keeps borrowing. She did not order the sabotage. Dasher does not know how Istrid reacted, and neither do the Doom Raiders unless the party asks her.

### What the Party Decides

The party has three answers, and all three end peacefully unless someone draws a weapon. The party can also change its mind later, but Dasher and Kelso judge the answer it gives at the fountain.

**Claim the reward.** The party tells Blossom where Dasher is, or persuades Dasher to meet her first.

**Buy his silence.** The party takes the 200 gp and tells Tashlyn that Dasher could not be found.

**Walk away.** The party refuses every offer and leaves the Waymoot with nothing settled.

> [!exploration]**Brokering a Meeting**
>
> Any character who tells Dasher that Blossom would take a letter, or a single afternoon of his time, over any kind of hunt, and makes a successful DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check, persuades him to meet his mother once, at the Waymoot on the next market day, with Kelso watching. The character has Advantage if the party visited the orchard and can quote Blossom's own words to him.
>
> On a failure, Dasher refuses to meet anyone but agrees to write Blossom two lines, and nothing more. He asks the party not to give her the Field Ward or Kelso's name. Do not mark **Snobeedle Meeting Brokered**, but Blossom still pays the reward on receiving the letter.

If the party brokered the meeting and brings Blossom to the Waymoot on the next market day, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Blossom crosses the little square with her walking stick and a cloth-wrapped bundle under one arm. Dasher stands from the fountain's rim and does not move toward her, and she stops an arm's length from him and looks him up and down.
>
> > You're thinner. Have you been eating properly? I've brought apple cake, and you're going to have some, and then you're going to tell me where you're sleeping and whether it's warm.
>
> Dasher takes the cake and breaks off a piece. They sit on the fountain's rim together, and Kelso and Brynn turn away toward the cart to give them room.

The conversation lasts two hours and does not go the way either of them planned. Dasher agrees to send Blossom word every tenday and does not promise to come home. Blossom hands a purse with 500 gp to the nearest member and does not ask about the wererats. She will say only that she is sorry it took so long to hear it from him.

If the party gives Blossom Dasher's location against his wishes, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Blossom listens to the name of the gang and the ward it works in, and she thanks you in a level voice. She sets her cup down and folds her hands on her lap for some time before she speaks.
>
> > I'll go at once. I won't scold him and I won't bring the Watch, but I'll tell you plainly that he's going to know who sent me.

Blossom goes to the Field Ward the next morning. The Shunners have moved out overnight, and Kelso meets her at the door of an empty cellar with a note in Dasher's hand that says only that he is alive and does not want to be found. She sends the reward to Trollskull Manor by courier two days later, with a short card of thanks. Kelso closes the Shunners' doors to the party from that day forward.

If the party buys Dasher's silence, Dasher counts out the coins and shakes each member's hand. Kelso nods once. Blossom hears that the party could not find him, and she keeps the notices up.

If the party walks away, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Dasher watches you stand, nods once and picks up his purse from the stone.
>
> > That's fair, and I'd have liked a better answer from myself, honestly. If you see my mother, tell her I'll write when I know what to say, because I mean that.

> [!hazard]**If the Meeting Turns Violent**
>
> Combat begins only if a party member attacks Dasher or tries to grab him and carry him away. Dasher, Kelso and Brynn Hilltopple each use the ordinary 2024 **Wererat**, in halfling form, with no added phases or extra Hit Points. The **Doom Raiders Mechanics Reference** audits the fight for three, four and five participating combatants. Do not add Wererats beyond these three.
>
> #### The Shard Shunners' Tactics
> Kelso raises a hand at the first drawn weapon and lets the party act first. Brynn takes cover behind the fruit cart, and Dasher steps back to the fountain's far side.
>
> During combat, the Shunners:
> - Stay in halfling form and fight with the Scimitar and the Hand Crossbow. They shift to rat or hybrid form, the only forms with the Bite, only after a Shunner is reduced to 0 Hit Points. The Bite carries the Curse of Lycanthropy (DC 11 Constitution saving throw), and the Wererat has no damage resistances.
> - Focus on whichever character is trying to seize Dasher, and use the fountain and the fruit cart for cover.
> - Withdraw to the rooftop ledges from the third round, where Brynn and Kelso shoot with the Hand Crossbow and Dasher keeps out of reach.
>
> The fight ends when a Shunner is reduced to 0 Hit Points, when a party member lowers their weapon and offers terms, or at the end of the fourth round, when a Watch patrol turns into the square. On any of those, Kelso whistles and the Shunners break for the rooftops with Dasher between them. They do not pursue.
>
> A character who lowers their weapon and makes a successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check ends the fight at once. On a failure, Kelso stands the Shunners down anyway on any offer of terms, but **Shard Shunners Goodwill** is not marked. If a Watch patrol arrives, the party forfeits the bonus for keeping the Watch out of it.

### Istrid's Ledger

Members can take Dasher's claim to Istrid Horn at any time before the debrief. She receives them in her guarded Dock Ward warehouse as she would any Fang.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The warehouse is dry and warm, stacked with locked chests, and a broad-shouldered dwarf woman sits behind a long table with a ledger open in front of her and a tray of tea at her elbow. She taps one line with a finger, closes the book on it, and waves you to the chairs.
>
> > Come in out of the damp, dear. Sit. I hear you have a question about one of my accounts, and I'd like to answer it before it turns into a rumor.

> [!social]**The Lender in the Warehouse**
>
> Istrid Horn (Lawful Evil, Shield Dwarf, she/her) :: the Doom Raiders' Master of Trade and Coin, who lends warmly and remembers everything.

> [!qna]**About Emmek's loan?**

> [!qna]**Did you know about the Shunners?**

> Istrid taps the ledger once with one finger.

> [!qna]**What will you do now?**
>
> > If you tell me it troubles you, I'll have my people stay away from any hiring aimed at a member's property. Vergadain smile on it. I won't call in his loan on your account, though, because that isn't how I run this book.

### Aftermath

Blossom Snobeedle ends the mission either with news of her son or without it. If the meeting was brokered, she sees him once and will hear from him every tenday afterward. If the party gave up his location, she goes to the Field Ward and finds nothing but a note. If the party bought his silence or walked away, she keeps the notices up and waits.

Kelso Fiddlewick remembers all of it. If the party respected Dasher's wishes and kept the Watch out of it, Kelso is not hostile, and he lets the Shunners' people in the Southern Ward take the party's word. If the party gave up Dasher's location, or fought at the Waymoot, he closes his doors to the party and tells the halflings of the ward to do the same. His debt from **The Shard Shunners**, if the party holds it, is cancelled.

Istrid Horn keeps lending to Emmek Frewn and to everyone else. Tashlyn does not raise the subject again unless the party does.

## Overview

The party searches the Southern Ward for the missing son of a wealthy halfling family and decides what to tell his mother.

## Summary

We spent three days among the halflings of the Southern Ward and met Dasher Snobeedle at the Waymoot. He told us he was safe and would not go home, and he mentioned that Istrid Horn's money had paid for the trouble on Trollskull Alley. We decided what to tell his mother and what to tell Tashlyn, and Kelso Fiddlewick will remember which.
