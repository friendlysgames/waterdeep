# Junior Griffon

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — a visit to Blackstaff Tower, or a Sending within a tenday — after one party member's Force Grey renown reaches 3. In this Event:
>
> - Vajra sends a brief *Sending* to the promoted character
> - They meet at the standing desk in Blackstaff Tower
> - Vajra marks the promotion and explains the new arrangements: preparatory spells once a tenday, a signed quartermaster requisition, and the reference library on the second floor
> - She adds one line about what the rank means to her operationally
> - The promoted character leaves with a signed requisition and access to the library

**Background (DM only)**

Junior Griffon is the first real threshold. Gray Hand was probationary — a credential extended before the party had done enough to earn it. Renown 3 means they have reported a confirmed threat, handled a complication without creating a political problem, or otherwise demonstrated they are using the relationship correctly. Vajra's evaluation of them has not changed. The rank change confirms that her initial evaluation was accurate.

The preparatory spell is operational logic. A well-prepared team is less likely to produce an outcome she has to explain to Laeral Silverhand.

The reference library is a practical resource. Blackstaff Tower's collection holds material unavailable at Candle Lane or any guild archive — Vajra has spent three years acquiring anything relevant to arcane threat assessment. Characters who use it produce better intelligence. That is the only reason access exists.

### The Sending

The spell arrives during a quiet moment, brief and without preamble:

> *"Junior Griffon. Come to the Tower when you're free. New arrangements."*

No response required. The absence of urgency is itself a signal — this is administrative, not a crisis.

### Blackstaff Tower

The door opens before they reach the top step. Vajra is at the standing desk. She doesn't look up immediately. When she does:

> > "Junior Griffon. You've been using the access correctly. That was the thing I was waiting to confirm."

She sets a folded letter on the desk's edge — a signed requisition for the Tower's quartermaster. The quartermaster is Merris, a heavyset man on the third floor. His primary quality is that he does not ask where things are going.

> > "Mundane equipment. Common potions. If you need something outside that range, come to me directly. The quartermaster handles the routine."

The reference library is on the second floor, behind the door with the iron latch. She uses it for arcane threat research; they are welcome to anything not in her active stacks. Books removed from the building require her written authorization. She signs them; she does not commit verbally.

> > "The preparatory spells: I'll cast one before a mission, once a tenday, if you tell me in advance what you're walking into. Third level or below. *Nondetection*, *water breathing*, *see invisibility*, *speak with dead* — whatever the situation requires."

She pauses.

> > "Don't request it unless you know why you need it. I don't cast blind."

She is already back at her map when they reach the stair.

> [!dialogue]**What determines which spell you cast?**
> "What the mission requires. Come with a specific need and I'll tell you whether I can meet it. Come without one and I'll send you back to find out what you're missing."

> [!dialogue]**How much notice do you need?**
> "A tenday is ideal. I can manage less if the situation has moved faster than anticipated. Don't make a habit of the latter."

## Overview

Vajra Safahr contacted one party member by *Sending* and confirmed their promotion to Junior Griffon — the first real rank within Force Grey, beyond the probationary Gray Hand tier. At Blackstaff Tower, she provided a signed quartermaster requisition for mundane supplies and Common potions, explained the reference library on the second floor, and confirmed she will cast one preparatory spell of up to 3rd level before missions, once a tenday, with advance notice.

## Summary

Vajra confirmed the promotion to Junior Griffon at the standing desk, same as it always is. She handed over a signed requisition — Merris, third floor, no questions — and explained the library on the second floor. One preparatory spell per tenday, third level or below, only if we come with a specific need. She turned back to her map before we finished asking. The door closed on its own behind us.
