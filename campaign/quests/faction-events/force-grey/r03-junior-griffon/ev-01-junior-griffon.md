# Junior Griffon

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Force Grey member first reaches Renown 3, which usually happens right after **Consulting Hlam**. Vajra Safahr names the rank at her standing desk in Blackstaff Tower and gives that member the three Junior Griffon benefits. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn how to ask Vajra for one preparatory spell before a mission, once every tenday.
> - Use the reference library on the second floor and put one question to its stacks.
> - Draw mundane gear and Common potions from Merris, the quartermaster on the third floor.
>
> Only the member is addressed. Companions who are not Force Grey members can climb the stair and wait, and they gain nothing.

### The Sending at Noon

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 3, Vajra's *Sending* finds them wherever they are. Every qualifying member hears it on their own account, so several members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. A member who is away from Waterdeep hears it at the next noon after they return to the surface, and the meeting moves to the following morning.

Read the following aloud, changing the first sentence to match what the member is doing.

> [!readaloud]
>
> You are halfway through a meal when a dry, flat voice speaks inside your head, fast and clipped and without any greeting. Nobody else at the table reacts to it, and the talk around you carries on.
>
> > "Junior Griffon. You have earned the new rank. Come to the Tower tomorrow morning. There are three arrangements to explain. Do not keep me waiting."
>
> The voice stops as soon as it finishes, and you have room to answer in twenty-five words or fewer.

The *Sending* is exactly 25 words. Vajra files a bare acknowledgment without replying, and a member who does not answer is still received at the Tower.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Vajra's opinion of the party has not changed. Renown 3 tells her they have used the Gray Hand access as intended, and the rank is her book catching up with that.
> - The preparatory spell is not a favor. A team that arrives prepared gives her less to explain to the Open Lord.
> - The library holds what the Tower has gathered on magical threats. It has nothing on the Blackstaff itself, the Stone of Golorr or the vault.
> - The stacks name no leader for the other Black Network cell, and Vajra has no name either. Nobody in this Event says Manshoon's name unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member, and the stacks say "the Black Network has split" until then.
> - Merris does not know what his stock is used for, and Vajra wants it that way.
> - The Cassalanters are not in the stacks. A question about them gets "Not in these stacks."

### Naming the Rank

The member climbs on their own. The door opens as they reach the steps, the same as it did for the first meeting, and it opens for any companion who climbs with them.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The door of Blackstaff Tower swings open as you reach the steps, before you can knock, and the narrow stair beyond smells of old parchment. You climb past shelves of books and maps pinned to boards until you reach the study at the top.
>
> Vajra stands behind the desk with a pen in her hand and the Blackstaff leaning against the wall at her elbow. She finishes a line on the map before she looks up, and there is still no chair in the room.
>
> > "Junior Griffon. You have used the Tower's access properly, and I wanted to confirm that before anything else. Three arrangements come with the rank, and I will explain each one once."

> [!social]**The Blackstaff at Her Desk**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep and commander of Force Grey, who regards a rank as a matter of bookkeeping.
>
> Vajra is Indifferent toward the member and brisk without being cold. She stands throughout, states her conclusion first and then explains it in one or two sentences. She swears rarely in front of a member and mildly when she does. She changes the subject when anyone remarks on her youth.
>
> Vajra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Junior Griffon benefit gives the member, and how to use it.
> - Why the rank belongs to one member and not to the party.
> - What the member must tell her before she casts.
>
> She will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault, the master of the other Black Network cell, the Cassalanters, what is in the Blackstaff, Undermountain or her youth. She turns each aside with "Irrelevant." or "Next."

> [!qna]**What does Junior Griffon give me?**
>
> She taps three fingers on the desk, one after another.
>
> > "Three things. I will cast one preparatory spell on you before a mission, the library on the second floor answers a question each visit, and Merris on the third floor issues gear and potions."

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not the party's?**
>
> > "I write ranks by name. Your companions can come along for the spell and carry the gear, but they cannot ask for either, and they will not be told how."

> [!qna]**Why two days' notice?**
>
> > "Because I have a city to watch and a damned short list of free hours. I need two days to find one."

> [!qna]**What if my Renown falls?**
>
> > "The rank stays. The arrangements wait until you have earned your way back to it."

> [!exploration]**Reading Vajra**
>
> Any character who watches Vajra while she talks and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check sees ink on both her cuffs and a cup of tea gone cold at her elbow. She has been awake a long time. A character who fails sees nothing out of the ordinary. If anyone asks if she is all right, she answers "Irrelevant." and goes on with the next arrangement.

If **Offerings Kept** is marked, Vajra adds one line before she moves on, and it changes nothing else in this Event. Meritide's grudge reaches the Tower, and she does not ask what happened. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra turns a letter over on the desk so that you can see the Queenspire's seal pressed into the wax.
>
> > "The Queenspire has written to me twice about the harbor. Meritide Blackfin has your names and has not forgotten them."

**Offerings Kept** is read here when the party played **The Dragon in the Harbor** before this member reached Renown 3, as happens when a member joined the Gray Hands later than the others. It is party-level, so it applies to every member.

### The Preparatory Spell

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra sets down her pen and slides a narrow paper across the desk. It lists six spells in her cramped hand, each with a number beside it.
>
> > "Once every tenday I will cast one of these on you and your companions before a mission. Tell me the mission, the place and the hour two days ahead, because I don't cast blind."

> [!exploration]**Vajra's Preparatory Spell**
>
> - **Contact:** Vajra at the standing desk, or a written request left on it. If she is out, she answers by *Sending* within a day.
> - **The request:** the member names the mission, the place, the hour the mission starts and one spell from the list. A request with no named mission is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Notice:** two days. A request with less notice is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **The casting:** Vajra stands on the Tower steps within the hour before the stated start and casts on the member and each companion who goes on the mission, up to four. She casts as many times as the spell needs. She does not leave the Tower and does not go on the mission.
> - **The spell:** one spell per member per tenday, counted from the day of casting. A request withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent.
> - **Companions:** a companion who is not a Force Grey member cannot make the request, and Vajra does not answer one made in the member's name.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the spell is suspended until the Renown is restored.
>
> The six spells and their durations:
>
> - *Nondetection* (3rd level): 8 hours.
> - *Water Breathing* (3rd level): 24 hours.
> - *See Invisibility* (2nd level): 1 hour.
> - *Tongues* (3rd level): 1 hour.
> - *Darkvision* (2nd level): 8 hours.
> - *Protection from Energy* (3rd level): Concentration, up to 1 hour. The member names acid, cold, fire, lightning or thunder when asking, and each recipient concentrates on their own casting.

### The Reference Library

> [!readaloud]
>
> The second floor opens behind a door with an iron latch, and low shelves run back into the dark between standing lamps. A young apprentice in ink-stained grey sits at a lectern near the door, writing in a log, and she looks up when the latch clicks open for your letter.
>
> > "One question a visit. Tell me what you want to know, and I'll point you to the shelf."

> [!exploration]**The Latch**
>
> Any character who studies the latch and makes a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check sees that it is warded to read the Tower letter and to log whoever enters. Vajra looks at the log each morning. A character who casts *Detect Magic* learns the same thing without a check. A character who fails sees only an iron latch that opened.

> [!exploration]**Using the Library**
>
> - **Contact:** whichever Tower apprentice is on duty at the lectern. The door opens for the Tower letter at any hour.
> - **The question:** one question per visit, on a magical subject. That means a spell, a creature, an item, a practice, or a person known to use magic. A question on anything else gets "Not in these stacks," and the visit is not spent.
> - **The reading:** the apprentice brings the shelves, and the member reads for four hours. At the end, answer from the campaign record. Give what the Tower's stacks hold, state what they lack, and cover nothing the Tower has not gathered.
> - **Not held:** the Blackstaff itself, the Stone of Golorr, the vault, Undermountain and the Cassalanters. For the master of the other Black Network cell, the stacks say the network has split and name no leader. If **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member, the stacks also hold the old Black Network's public record.
> - **Limit:** one visit per member per day.
> - **Companions:** a companion can sit and read beside the member and cannot ask a question of their own. The member can share what the answer says.
> - **Removal:** a book leaves the building only on Vajra's signed slip. She signs one slip per member per tenday, for one book and three days. A book returned late suspends the library for that member for a tenday.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the library is closed to them until the Renown is restored.

### The Quartermaster

> [!readaloud]
>
> The third floor is one long room of locked cupboards, and a heavyset man in a leather apron sits behind a counter at the far end with a ledger open in front of him. He looks at your letter and then at your boots, and he turns the ledger around so you can reach the pen.
>
> > "Name goes here. Whatever you take, I write it down. Where it goes, I don't want to hear."

> [!social]**The Man Who Does Not Ask**
>
> Merris (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: the Tower's quartermaster, a heavyset man who records every item he issues and never asks what it is for.
>
> Merris is Indifferent and slow to speak. He licks his thumb to turn each page of the ledger and answers only what was asked. He does not swear, and he does not gossip about the other floors.
>
> Merris is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What he stocks, and how much a member can take.
> - How to sign for an item.
> - The two vials of holy water, if someone asks what to bring on a mission.
>
> He will not discuss where past items went, who else has drawn from the Tower, or anything above the third floor.

> [!qna]**What can I take?**
>
> > "Ordinary gear up to fifty gold in value, and two potions, either healing or climbing. That's per ten days. Beyond that, ask the Blackstaff."

> [!qna]**Don't you want to know what it's for?**
>
> > "No. If I knew, I'd have to forget it, and I'm bad at forgetting. Sign the book and tell me nothing."

> [!qna]**Is there holy water?**
>
> > "Two vials, in a cupboard I keep locked. They're not on the list. You get them when the Blackstaff's orders say so, and I write that in a separate line."

> [!exploration]**Merris's Stock**
>
> - **Contact:** Merris, on the third floor, at any hour.
> - **The stock:** in each block of ten days counted from this meeting, a member can draw mundane gear from the 2024 *Player's Handbook* up to 50 gp in published price, and up to two Common potions. The gear is adventuring gear and tools, with no weapons, armor or magic items. The potions are *potion of healing* and *potion of climbing*.
> - **Cost:** nothing. Merris writes each item against the member's name.
> - **Notice and delay:** none. He hands the items over at the counter.
> - **Holy water:** two vials of holy water (25 gp each) sit in a locked cupboard, outside the cap. Merris issues them only to a member whose brief from Vajra names them, as in **The Trouble with Meloon**. Anyone else is told "Not on the list."
> - **Companions:** a companion can carry and use anything the member signs for. A companion cannot sign, and Merris does not take a request made in the member's name.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who sells or gives away signed gear loses the requisition for a tenday when Merris hears of it.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and Merris stops issuing until the Renown is restored.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Junior Griffon earns further Renown from the missions and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Force Grey page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions what Vajra said and where the library and the quartermaster are. The spell, the library and the gear stay individual benefits, and Vajra, the apprentices and Merris will not serve a companion who asks in the member's name.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard all three arrangements and left the Tower. The library and Merris scenes can run at any later visit.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Junior Griffon Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Vajra names the rank at the desk; read by **Senior Griffon**, which checks it before naming the next rank. Track separately for each member the day of their last casting, the day of their last library visit and the gold and potions drawn from Merris in the current block of ten days.
>
> This Event also reads **Offerings Kept** (Meritide's grudge, one line) and **Manshoon Named** (the library's answer).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three benefits are active for this member from the next morning. **The Dragon in the Harbor** becomes available when an individual Force Grey member reaches Renown 3 and 3rd level, and this Event does not change that. **Senior Griffon** occurs when this member reaches Renown 10. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Vajra Safahr names a Force Grey member a Junior Griffon and explains three arrangements: a preparatory spell, the Tower's library and the quartermaster.

## Summary

We were named Junior Griffons at Vajra's standing desk after a *Sending* told us to come. She will cast one spell on us before a mission once every tenday, the library on the second floor answers one question a visit, and Merris on the third floor hands out gear and potions. Each of us has our own rank, and our companions can only carry what we sign for.
