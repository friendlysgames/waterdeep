# Design Notes: Junior Griffon

## Individual Procedures

Each Junior Griffon benefit is a per-member procedure with a named contact, a place, a notice rule, a limit and a loss rule. Vajra casts one spell from a fixed list of six on the member and up to four companions, on the Tower steps, with two days' notice and one casting every tenday. The library answers one question a visit from the campaign record, using the Harper archive method: what the stacks hold, with the gaps stated. Merris issues gear up to 50 gp and two Common potions in each block of ten days. Nothing pools across the party, and companions can carry what a member signs for but cannot ask for anything.

The six spells and their durations come from section 8 of the Force Grey mechanics reference, which was checked against the 2024 *Player's Handbook*. *Speak with Dead* from the restored draft is dropped, because it needs a corpse and Vajra does not cast blind. Because several of the spells touch a single creature, the event lets Vajra cast as many times as the spell needs and counts that as one use.

## Source Departures

The restored draft let Vajra sign book removals freely and gave Merris unlimited "mundane equipment and Common potions". Both now have limits: one signed slip per tenday, and the 50 gp and two-potion cap. The Common potion list is *potion of healing* and *potion of climbing*, as the mechanics reference sets it. The 2024 *Dungeon Master's Guide* also lists *potion of comprehension* as Common, and it is left out.

The restored *Sending* ran 10 words. The new one runs exactly 25 and opens with the rank's name, as the brief requires. The two vials of holy water now sit with Merris, matching **The Trouble with Meloon**, where Vajra sends the party to him. The mechanics reference calls Merris the "junior quartermaster" in one place and the brief repeats it, so the event keeps a single Merris.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: the unnamed library apprentice, the four-hour reading, the three-day book slip, the up-to-four companion limit on the spell, and Merris's alignment, species and thumb-licking. Merris is voiced from the event text. The 50 gp and two-potion numbers come from the mechanics reference.
