# Design Notes: Junior Griffon

## Individual Procedures

Each Junior Griffon benefit is a per-member procedure with a named contact, a place, a notice rule, a limit and a loss rule. Vajra casts one spell from a fixed list of six on the member and up to four companions, on the Tower steps, with two days' notice and one casting every tenday. The library answers one question a visit from the campaign record, using the Harper archive method, which states what the stacks hold and where they have gaps. Merris issues gear up to 50 gp and two Common potions in each block of ten days, and nothing pools across the party. Companions can carry what a member signs for but cannot ask for anything.

The six spells, their ranges and their durations come from section 8 of the Force Grey mechanics reference, checked against the 2024 *Player's Handbook*. *Speak with Dead* from the restored draft is dropped because it needs a corpse and Vajra does not cast blind. *See Invisibility* is replaced by *Longstrider* because the 2024 spell has Self range. Most of the list is Touch, so the event lets Vajra cast once per creature and counts that as one use, and she holds *Protection from Energy* from the Tower for its hour.

## Source Departures

The restored draft let Vajra sign book removals freely and gave Merris unlimited "mundane equipment and Common potions". Both now have limits: one signed slip per tenday, and the 50 gp and two-potion cap. The Common potion list is *potion of healing* and *potion of climbing*, as the mechanics reference sets it, and the 2024 *Dungeon Master's Guide* also lists *potion of comprehension* as Common, which is left out.

The restored *Sending* ran 10 words, and the new one runs exactly 25 and opens with the rank's name, as the brief requires. The two vials of holy water sit with Merris, matching **The Trouble with Meloon**, where Vajra sends the party to him. The mechanics reference once calls Merris the "junior quartermaster", so the event keeps a single Merris.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: the unnamed library apprentice, the four-hour reading, the three-day book slip, the up-to-four-companion limit on the spell, and Merris's alignment, species and thumb-licking. Merris is voiced from the event text, and the 50 gp and two-potion numbers come from the mechanics reference.
