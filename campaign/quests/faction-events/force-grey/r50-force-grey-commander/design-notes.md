# Design Notes: Force Grey Commander

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B and the Factions Guide give Commander as four veterans and one mage for any mission. They add a formal briefing of Laeral and her personal recognition, a scroll from the Tower's vault and one spell of any level from Vajra. The restored draft added a back-window scene, a Public or Private letter and two Laeral lines, one for each state of **Vault of Dragons**. It left the four veterans unnamed and let the table choose a "Rare-rarity spell from the Player's Handbook". It also let the final spell include *wish*.

## What Changed

Each benefit is now a per-member procedure with a contact, a notice, a limit and a loss rule. The team is Ysmay Halvane, Rhendar Orsk, Garrick Stoll and Sera Vantry, sized by the mechanics reference. The allies who fight number one fewer than the PCs, and the rest hold the Portal camp. The Event gives the Power totals for 8th level and for level 11 onward.

The scroll comes from a fixed list of twelve 4th- and 5th-level spells with no transport magic, and Vajra says a scroll is useless to a class without the spell. The final reserve is cast on the surface or at the Yawning Portal. It excludes *wish* and every transport spell, and it is called by *Sending* from below.

Laeral's primary text is post-Vault, with three readings that quote the party's gold path (returned, kept, left in place) in place of "the treasury records". A pre-Vault branch reads **Vajra Briefed**. Her letter adds a line for **Tower Attack Stopped**, **Splinter Testimony Recorded** and **Vajra Briefed**. She speaks in her profile voice, with no swearing and no mention of her own decline. Public or Sealed is per member, with a tenday to decide and silence counting as Sealed.

## Departures from the Source

The Sending is exactly 25 words and opens with the rank's name. It reaches a party underground and tells them to come up, and the Tower scenes wait until the member is on the surface. Vajra stands at her desk as she does in every other meeting, so the back-window scene and "Sit." are gone.

She says she has given the rank twice before in her three years as Blackstaff. The Mad Mage seeds are one-line GM facts. The Event does not duplicate Laeral's private request in the Lords' Alliance rank **Lioncrown**.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Garrick Stoll (ex-Watch sergeant) and Sera Vantry (half-elf tracker). Also invented are the tenday mission cap with a two-day rest and the one-time replacement of a fallen team member. The same goes for the withdrawal points of 32 for Garrick and 8 for Sera, the dusk-arrival rule for the final reserve, and the Sealed letter's one-officer reading.

Rhendar's eleven years and two-word speech come from the mechanics reference. Ysmay and Dovrin Tesk come from **Senior Griffon**. Garrick, Sera and Ysmay have no profile in the voices folder, so they are voiced from the Event text.

Logged for the out-of-scope notes: the Factions Guide and Appendix B describe the team as "four veterans and one mage" and the scroll as "any Rare spell scroll". Both need the new team and list. The Vault of Dragons structure doc (lines 41 and 51) reads Commander standing without these terms.

The Vault of Dragons outcomes for the gold path do not exist yet. Laeral's lines describe the path in plain words and need the outcome names when that quest is converted. The ruling that Ysmay avoids *Misty Step* below ground is the mechanics reference's, and it is unverified against the adventure.
