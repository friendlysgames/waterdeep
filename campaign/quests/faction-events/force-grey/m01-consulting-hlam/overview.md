# Consulting Hlam: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> **Consulting Hlam** becomes available when an individual Force Grey member who has marked **Force Grey Joined** reaches 2nd level. Companions can climb with the members and speak to Hlam without belonging to the faction.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> No combat is expected and no stat block is needed. Hlam is a Monk far beyond the party, and he answers any attack with a bow and a refusal to speak.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

At mid-morning a Force Grey member receives a *Sending* from Vajra Safahr. It names a monk on Mount Waterdeep's western slope and asks what threats he has perceived. It tells the member not to mention her name unless the monk asks.

## Background

Hlam is a Calishite monk of indeterminate age who has lived in a cave on Mount Waterdeep for longer than anyone has thought to check. He dislikes visitors who come for validation, and he judges every one of them by whether they answer him honestly. Two years ago he told Vajra she was not listening, and she has neither climbed the slope nor sent anyone up it since.

Hlam has felt the Splinter, the Black Network cell that broke away under Manshoon, drawing its net over the city again. He reads it as an old wrongness in a familiar shape and cannot give it a name. For tendays he has also felt a patient resonance beneath the city, which comes from the Stone of Golorr and the Vault of Dragons, though he does not know what it is.

Vajra knows the Black Network has split. She does not know about the buried thing, and she does not link it to the Splinter. Nobody in this mission says "Manshoon", "Stone" or "vault".

## The Brief

The *Sending* reaches the Force Grey member with the highest Wisdom score. It is exactly 25 words, and the member can send up to three replies, which Vajra answers in kind.

## The Climb

The path up the western slope starts in a city park and becomes unmarked above the treeline. One scramble across bare rock tests every character with a Constitution saving throw, and a failure costs a level of Exhaustion.

## The Cave

Hlam sits at the mouth of a narrow cave, facing the harbor. He asks who sent the party, and the answer sets the terms of the visit. His first answer is a warning that evil hides its face for now. His second is a riddle about a black net being drawn again over the city, and he will not explain it.

## What He Offers Unasked

When the party turns to go, Hlam speaks without being asked. He says the Blackstaff carries more than she knows, and that something buried beneath the city is waking.

## The Report

Vajra receives the Force Grey members at Blackstaff Tower and asks for everything Hlam said, in order. The members decide whether to give her the buried-thing message word for word, including the sentences about her.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Force Grey member gains 2 base Renown for climbing to Hlam and reporting to Vajra. Bonuses of +1 each go to members who give Vajra the last message word for word, and to members whose party won Hlam's second answer without invoking her authority.

## Aftermath

Vajra files the riddle and the buried-thing message separately and does not link them. Hlam goes back to watching the harbor. **The Dragon in the Harbor** becomes available at Renown 3 and 3rd level.

## Involved Characters

- **Vajra Safahr** (Force Grey): sends the brief by *Sending*, takes the report at Blackstaff Tower, and does not know what the buried thing is.
- **Hlam** (Order of the Even-Handed): a hermit monk on Mount Waterdeep who answers honest visitors and gives away one message unasked.

## Dangers & Enemies

There is no combat, and a failed Constitution saving throw on the climb costs one level of Exhaustion, which ends after a Long Rest. A character who raises a weapon against Hlam is warned with a single bow, and a second move against him ends the visit with no answers and no Renown.

## Overview

Vajra sends the party up Mount Waterdeep to ask a hermit monk what threats he has perceived. The climb is long, and the monk is hard to impress.
