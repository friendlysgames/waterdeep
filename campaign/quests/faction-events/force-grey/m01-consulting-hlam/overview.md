# Force Grey Mission 1 — Consulting Hlam

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available from the start of Force Grey membership. No prior mission completion required. Best placed before or during **Trollskull Alley**, while the party is still establishing its Waterdeep contacts.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission awards no Milestone Points.

Vajra has not sent anyone to Hlam in two years. He made it clear she was not listening to what he said. She is hoping the party is different.

Hlam is a monk of indeterminate age who has lived in a cave on Mount Waterdeep's upper slopes for longer than anyone has thought to check. He perceives things through sustained attention that most people never achieve, and he dislikes visitors because most of them want validation rather than information. What earns his cooperation is directness, honesty, and the willingness to acknowledge that the party does not fully know what it is asking for and is here anyway. Appeals to authority, urgency, or flattery lose it.

What he knows: Manshoon has returned, is rebuilding, and the shape of what he is building is recognizable to anyone who remembers Waterdeep's history before it had to be rebuilt. And there is something buried beneath the city that is becoming less buried. He does not know what it is. He knows what its resonance feels like: old, patient, hungry, and recently more awake.

## Involved Characters
- **Vajra Safahr** (Force Grey) — the Blackstaff; sends the mission by *Sending* and receives the party's report at Blackstaff Tower
- **Hlam** (independent) — a monk of indeterminate age living on Mount Waterdeep's upper slope; the party's target and the sole subject of this mission

## Dangers & Enemies

No combat encounters. The primary challenge is social — earning Hlam's cooperation through patience and honesty rather than authority or urgency. A failed Constitution saving throw on the ascent results in one level of Exhaustion, not injury.

## Overview

The party receives a *Sending* from Vajra Safahr instructing them to seek out a monk named Hlam on the slope of Mount Waterdeep and ask what threats he has perceived — without mentioning her name, and without overstaying.

The climb is real: the path narrows above the treeline and disappears once, requiring a short scramble. Hlam heard them long before they reached his cave. He responds to directness and does not respond to appeals to authority. With patience and honesty, he gives the party two pieces of intelligence: a warning about Manshoon's return and a rebuilding presence in the city, and an unprompted observation about something buried beneath the city that is becoming less buried.

The second piece, delivered without any question being asked, is the party's first thread toward the Stone of Golorr and the Vault of Dragons. Vajra, when she hears it, asks them to repeat it and writes it down.
