# Consulting Hlam

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social/Exploration Event begins when the party receives Vajra's Sending and decides to climb Mount Waterdeep. In this Event:
>
> - The path up the western slope begins in a maintained city park and becomes unmarked above the treeline.
> - A DC 12 Constitution saving throw determines whether the party arrives without Exhaustion; pacing the ascent or having an able climber assist grants Advantage or automatic success.
> - Hlam is seated in meditation facing the harbor. He heard the party much earlier than his stillness suggests.
> - His cooperation is earned through directness and honesty. Appeals to authority, urgency framing, or flattery impose Disadvantage on any check with him.
> - A DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check earns his first answer about evil's present concealment; a DC 15 check — or simply not pressing and waiting in silence — earns his second, naming Manshoon's return explicitly.
> - As the party prepares to leave, Hlam delivers an unprompted third message about something buried beneath the city.
> - This unprompted message is the party's first thread toward the Stone of Golorr.

**Background (DM only)**

Hlam is a monk of indeterminate age who has lived in a cave on Mount Waterdeep's upper slopes for longer than anyone has thought to check. He perceives things through sustained attention that most people never achieve. He dislikes visitors because most of them want validation rather than information. Vajra has not sent anyone to him in two years — he made it clear she was not listening to what he said. She is hoping the party is different.

What Hlam knows: **Manshoon** has returned, is rebuilding, and the shape of what he is building is recognizable to anyone who remembers Waterdeep's history. And there is something buried beneath the city that is becoming less buried. He does not know what it is. He knows what the resonance feels like — old, patient, hungry, and recently more awake.

### The Climb

The path up Mount Waterdeep's western slope begins in a maintained city park and becomes unmarked above the treeline. The harbor falls away below, then the Sea Ward roofline, then the horizon. The path narrows twice and disappears entirely once, requiring a short scramble across bare stone.

A **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** to arrive without Exhaustion. Taking extra time to pace the ascent grants Advantage; a character with a climbing speed or Expertise in Athletics who assists others grants them automatic success. The Exhaustion is not punitive — it marks the effort as real. The cave is visible only from above it: a narrow fissure in the western face with the view that makes everything else small.

### Hlam

He is seated in meditation facing the harbor. When footsteps reach the path, he opens his eyes. He heard them much earlier than that. Old in a way that resists estimation. His clothes match the rock. He looks at them once — brief, complete, nothing wasted.

His cooperation is not automatic. What he responds to: directness, honesty, the acknowledgment that the party does not actually know what they are asking for and is here anyway. What earns Disadvantage on any check: appeals to authority, urgency framing, or flattery. A character who simply states the truth — "We work for the Blackstaff. She needs to know what threats are moving against the city." — rolls straight or earns Advantage.

A **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check** earns his first answer:

> > "Evil's twin hides its face for now. Expect that to change before winter's end."

He will not elaborate if asked directly. But if the party sits with the answer rather than pressing — genuinely sits, in silence — he will look at them once and continue. A **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check**, or simply not pushing and waiting, earns the second:

> > "An archmage thought dead has returned. He is rebuilding something. The shape of it is familiar to anyone who remembers what this city was before it had to be rebuilt."

He means Manshoon. He is choosing what to say with characteristic precision.

### The Unprompted Message

As the party prepares to go — Hlam indicates the meeting's end by returning to face the harbor — he says one more thing. Not in response to a question.

> > "The Blackstaff carries more than she knows. Not the staff — she knows what the staff is. The city. She does not know what is buried beneath it, and the buried thing is waking." A pause. "Tell her that from me. She will understand the half of it that matters."

The Stone of Golorr's abolethic resonance has been faint but consistent for weeks; Hlam's sustained attention has caught it. Vajra, when the party delivers this message, goes still. She asks them to repeat it exactly. Then: "Thank you." A pause. "This doesn't leave the Tower."

### Concluding the Event

> **+1 Renown if** the unprompted message about the buried thing is delivered verbatim. Vajra asks them to repeat it once and writes it down.

## Overview

Vajra sends the party up Mount Waterdeep to consult a reclusive monk named Hlam about threats he has perceived.

## Read Aloud

> The Sending arrives in one character's head without preamble. Twenty-five words, each one precise:
>
> > "Hlam is a monk on Mount Waterdeep's slope. Seek him out. Ask what threats he has perceived. Don't mention my name. Don't overstay."
>
> The cave is a narrow fissure in the western face of the mountain, visible only from above. The man inside is seated in meditation, facing the harbor far below. He does not turn when you arrive. He opens his eyes.
>
> The view behind him is everything the city looks like from a distance that removes its noise.

## Summary

Vajra sent the party to consult Hlam, a monk living on Mount Waterdeep's western slope. He confirmed that a dead archmage has returned and is rebuilding in Waterdeep. Before they left, he offered an unprompted warning: something is buried beneath the city, and it is waking. Vajra received both messages and wrote down the second.
