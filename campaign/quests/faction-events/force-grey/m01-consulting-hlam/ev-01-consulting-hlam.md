# Consulting Hlam

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Exploration Event begins when Vajra's *Sending* reaches a Force Grey member and ends when the members report to her at Blackstaff Tower. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Vajra's 25-word brief and send up to three replies, each answered by a fresh *Sending*.
> - Climb Mount Waterdeep's western slope, where a failed saving throw on the scramble costs a level of Exhaustion.
> - Answer Hlam's question about who sent them, honestly, evasively or with a lie.
> - Win his first answer, and then his second answer, which is a riddle he will not explain.
> - Hear an unprompted warning about something buried beneath the city.
> - Report to Vajra, and decide whether to give her the last message word for word.
>
> Only Force Grey members receive the *Sending* and attend the report. Their companions can climb with them and speak to Hlam.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Hlam is a Calishite human Monk and the grand master of the Order of the Even-Handed. He heard the party on the path long before it arrived.
> - What he feels in the city is the Splinter, the Black Network cell that broke away under Manshoon, drawing its net again. He never says the name, and he does not say it when **Manshoon Named** is marked either.
> - What he feels beneath the city is the abolethic resonance of the Stone of Golorr and the Vault of Dragons. It has been faint and steady for tendays, and it is growing. He does not know what it is.
> - Vajra knows the Black Network has split. She does not know about the buried thing and does not link it to the Splinter.
> - Hlam has decided to give the last message whatever the party does. The checks decide only how smoothly the rest of the visit goes.
> - Hlam told Vajra two years ago that she was not listening, and she has neither climbed the slope nor sent anyone to him since.

### The Brief

The *Sending* reaches the Force Grey member with the highest Wisdom score, or the higher Constitution score on a tie. It arrives at mid-morning on the first day that member has marked **Force Grey Joined** and reached 2nd level. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> While you are busy with something else, a dry, flat voice arrives behind your eyes. It is fast and clipped, and nobody standing near you hears a word of it.
>
> > Hlam is a monk on Mount Waterdeep's western slope. Ask him what threats he has perceived. Don't mention my name unless he asks. Don't overstay.
>
> You know the voice is Vajra's and that you can answer it, in no more than twenty-five words.

The member can reply once or several times. Vajra answers the first three replies, each with a new *Sending* of exactly 25 words, and ignores a fourth. A reply that asks about her staff or about Undermountain gets no answer.

> [!social]**The Voice Behind the Eyes**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep and commander of Force Grey, who sends her orders by spell because the spell makes her cut every word she would otherwise waste.
>
> Vajra counts the words on her fingers as she sends, and she does not wait for thanks. She is dry and brisk, and she gives the member what the climb requires and nothing else. She has not climbed Hlam's slope in two years, and she gives the reason only if a reply asks.
>
> Conversation topics Vajra is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Who Hlam is and what he is likely to know.
> - Why she is not going herself.
> - What to say if Hlam asks who sent the party.
> - What to bring, which is nothing.
>
> She will not discuss the Blackstaff's contents, the Splinter's master, the Cassalanters or Undermountain.

> [!qna]**Who is Hlam?**
>
> > Hlam serves Tyr, the Even-Handed, and sees farther than my agents do. He dislikes visitors, so go up, listen, and bring back what he says.

> [!qna]**Why not go yourself?**
>
> > He told me I was not listening, and I have not climbed that slope since. Let him finish. I will hear it at the Tower.

> [!qna]**What if he asks who sent us?**
>
> > Tell him the truth. Hlam hears a lie before it is finished and does not forgive it. Give my name only when he asks you.

> [!qna]**What do we bring him?**
>
> > Nothing. Hlam has no use for gifts or urgency, and he marks both against you. Ask plainly, and wait if he does not answer you.

When the replies are done, the members can climb. Companions are free to join them.

### The Climb

The path up Mount Waterdeep's western slope begins in a maintained city park and becomes unmarked above the treeline. The walk takes most of the morning, and the party reaches the cave around highsun. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The path begins in a tidy city park and climbs past the last benches into scrub and loose stone. Behind you the roofs of the Sea Ward fall away, then the harbor, then a wide gray stretch of open water.
>
> Above the treeline the wind rises and the path narrows to a ledge twice, before it disappears beneath a slab of bare rock. You will have to scramble across the slab on your hands and knees, with the drop on your left.

> [!exploration]**The Ascent**
>
> Each character who climbs makes a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** at the slab. On a failure, the character arrives at the cave with one level of Exhaustion, which ends after a Long Rest.
>
> - **Advantage:** The party spends an extra hour pacing the climb, and every character has Advantage on the saving throw.
> - **Auto:** A character with a climbing speed, or with Expertise in Athletics, who helps the others across the slab succeeds automatically. The characters helped have Advantage.
>
> The way down needs no saving throw. Hlam makes no comment on anyone who arrives Exhausted.

### The Cave

Hlam sits at the mouth of the cave and has watched the party climb for most of an hour. He dislikes visitors who come for validation, and he answers anyone who answers him honestly. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cave is a narrow fissure in the western face of the mountain, and from below it cannot be seen. An old man sits cross-legged at its mouth, facing the harbor far beneath him, in grey clothes the color of the rock. A small kettle steams on a flat stone beside his knee.
>
> He opens his eyes as you reach the ledge, though he must have heard you long before that. He turns his head and looks at each of you once, then back at the harbor.
>
> > You want to know what I know. Everyone does.
>
> He fills a clay cup from the kettle and holds it out. When nobody takes it, he drinks it himself.

> [!social]**The Hermit on the Ledge**
>
> Hlam (Lawful Good, Calishite Human, he/him) :: a hermit monk of the Order of the Even-Handed, so old that nobody on the mountain can say how old, who gives only what he chooses to give.
>
> Hlam speaks softly and slowly, with long pauses between his sentences. He calls every visitor "student" and never swears. He answers a question with a question when he thinks it was the wrong one, and he does not explain a riddle or repeat an answer. If a character raises a weapon, he bows once and waits.
>
> Conversation topics Hlam is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What threats he has perceived, one piece at a time.
> - How he knows, and why he sits where he does.
> - Vajra, if the party has named her.
>
> He will not discuss his past or the Order's business. He knows no names, and he gives none.

Once everyone has found a place on the ledge, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Hlam sets the empty cup on the stone and waits until each of you is settled. He looks at the party for a long moment before he speaks.
>
> > Sit, student. The tea is gone, but the view is free, and nobody here is in a hurry. Who sent you?

> [!exploration]**Who Sent You?**
>
> Hlam asks once, and he watches whoever answers. Any character who watches him and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** check determines that he already suspects the answer and is waiting to see whether the party will say it.
>
> - **Honest answer:** A character who names Vajra, or says the Blackstaff sent the party, answers honestly. Hlam says "Good," and the visit goes on with no penalty.
> - **Evasion:** A character who dodges the question ("friends of the city") hears "Again, student," and Hlam asks once more. An honest answer then carries no penalty. A second evasion gives every character Disadvantage on checks with Hlam for the rest of the visit.
> - **A lie:** A character who names anyone else gets no reply while Hlam finishes his tea. Every character has Disadvantage on checks with Hlam for the rest of the visit, and an honest correction does not lift it.
> - **Authority, urgency or flattery:** A character who uses Vajra's title as a reason for Hlam to answer, calls the matter urgent, or flatters him has Disadvantage on the next check with him. Naming Vajra in answer to his question is not an appeal to authority.

> [!exploration]**Winning Hlam's Answers**
>
> Hlam gives his first answer to the first character who asks what threats he has perceived, unless Disadvantage applies.
>
> - **Disadvantage applies:** The asker makes a **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check with Disadvantage. On a failure, Hlam asks, "Why did you come up here?" An honest reply lets any character try again without Disadvantage.
> - **The second answer:** A character who presses for more and makes a successful **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion)** check gets it, with Disadvantage if Disadvantage applies. On a failure, Hlam says, "The mountain does not hurry, student," and nothing else.
> - **The silent way:** If the whole party sits without speaking for ten minutes, Hlam gives whichever answer is next, with no check. A failed check is never the end of the conversation.
>
> The second answer is won without invoking Vajra's authority unless a character used her title or the weight of the Tower as a reason for Hlam to speak.

> [!qna]**What threats have you perceived?**
>
> Hlam looks at whoever asked for a long moment before he answers.
>
> > Evil's twin hides its face for now. Expect that to change before winter's end.

If the party wins the second answer, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Hlam looks out over the harbor before he speaks again, and the wind moves the sleeve of his robe.
>
> > A black net lay over this city once, and people slept beneath it without knowing.
>
> He pours a second cup from the kettle and holds it a moment before he goes on.
>
> > Someone is drawing the net again, student. The weave is different, but I know the knots.
>
> > I do not explain riddles. Carry it down the mountain, and see what it fits.

> [!qna]**Whose hands are they?**
>
> > Why do you want me to say it for you, student? Look at the knots in your own city, and you will find the hands.

> [!qna]**How do you know?**
>
> > I sit here every day and look at the city. Some parts of it have gone quiet, and I notice quiet.

> [!qna]**What is Vajra to you?**
>
> Hlam answers only if the party has named her.
>
> > She climbed up here once, asked me a question and argued with the answer. Perhaps she has learned to send people who listen.

### What He Offers Unasked

Hlam ends the meeting by turning back to face the harbor. He does this when the party stops asking, when the second answer has been given, or when the party starts to leave. He then speaks without being asked, and he speaks whether or not the party won any answer. He does not repeat a word of it. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Hlam has turned back to the harbor when he speaks, and his voice carries over his shoulder without any effort.
>
> > The Blackstaff carries more than she knows.
>
> He waits before the next words, and the wind stirs the hem of his sleeve.
>
> > Not the staff. She knows what the staff is. The city.
>
> Another pause follows, longer than the first.
>
> > She does not know what is buried beneath it, and the buried thing is waking.
>
> He turns his head a little, though not far enough to look at you.
>
> > Tell her that from me. She will understand the half of it that matters.

The message is the seven sentences above, in that order. Hlam says nothing more, and the party can leave.

> [!exploration]**Keeping His Words**
>
> If the players write the message down as it is read, they hold it exactly. Otherwise any character who tries to recall it and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence** check remembers all seven sentences in order. On a failure, the character keeps the gist: the Blackstaff, the city, and something buried that is waking. The party can use its best result.

### The Report

Vajra receives the members at Blackstaff Tower on the evening they come down, or after breakfast the next day if they are late. Companions wait in the entry hall. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The door of Blackstaff Tower swings open before anyone reaches for it. Upstairs, Vajra stands at a tall desk in her study with a pen in her hand, and there is no chair for visitors.
>
> > Report. Everything he said, in order, and nothing you think he meant.

Let the members report in their own words. Vajra interrupts only to ask for Hlam's exact words. She writes down the first answer and says, "Go on."

If the members give her the second answer, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra's pen stops over the page, and then she writes the riddle down as it was spoken.
>
> > Of course it's a fucking riddle. He means the Black Network, the half that broke away. I knew that much already, but Hlam has never once given me a name.

When the members reach the last message, the report turns on one decision. They can give it to her word for word, or give her a summary. If a member drops the opening sentences about her, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra watches the member who spoke and does not pick up her pen.
>
> > You left out the beginning. He said something about me. Say it as he said it.

If the member gives it in full at any point before she dismisses them, the +1 Renown below is earned. If every member refuses, Vajra says, "Then I have the rest. Leave it," writes what she has, and does not press. Mark **Buried Thing Reported** all the same.

When a member gives the message word for word, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra stops writing at "buried beneath it" and goes still. The pen hangs over the page for several seconds before she looks up.
>
> > Repeat it.
>
> When the member does, she writes every word down in order. She reads the page over to herself, and her lips move.
>
> > Thank you. This doesn't leave the Tower.

If the members give only a summary, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra writes three lines, lays the pen across the page and goes still.
>
> > I have the gist. This doesn't leave the Tower.

> [!exploration]**Vajra's Stillness**
>
> Any character who watches Vajra and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** check determines that the message troubles her less than the fact that Hlam knew it before she did. She will not say so.

> [!qna]**Are the two warnings connected?**
>
> > I can't tell. They could be the same problem or two different ones. I'll keep them in separate files until something joins them.

> [!qna]**What is buried?**
>
> > I don't know. He said I'd understand half of it, and so far I understand none. Nothing the Watch has sent me fits.

> [!qna]**Are you alright?**
>
> > Irrelevant. You're the ones who climbed a mountain today, so go and sleep.

### Renown Opportunities

Vajra ends the report as soon as she has what she needs. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra caps her pen, lays the page on a short stack and squares the stack against the edge of the desk.
>
> > That was the job. You went, you listened, and you brought it back. Next.

If a member gave the last message word for word, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> She stops a member on the way to the door with a nod at the page.
>
> > You brought me his words and not your own. I'll remember that the next time I need something carried exactly.

If the party won Hlam's second answer without invoking her authority, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra looks down at the riddle on the page and then at the door.
>
> > He has never given me a second answer. I'll be asking you before I send anyone else up there.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Force Grey member gains 2 base Renown for climbing to Hlam and reporting to Vajra. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the member gave Vajra Hlam's last message word for word, including the sentences about her.
> - **+1 Renown:** the party won Hlam's second answer without invoking Vajra's authority.

### Aftermath

Vajra files the riddle and the buried-thing message in separate folders. She does not link them, and she tells nobody outside Force Grey what Hlam said. Hlam goes back to watching the harbor, and a member who arrived Exhausted loses the level at the next Long Rest.

The buried thing returns in **The Full Picture**, where Vajra uses it as a confirmation if the Cassalanter hook has not run. It is also one of the clue paths that lead to **Vault of Dragons**.

### Concluding the Event

Vajra's report ends the assignment. Record who heard Hlam's first answer and who gave her the buried-thing message, because later events read both.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Hlam Consulted** — mark with the name of each member who heard Hlam's first answer; read by **The Factions Come Calling** and **The Grand Opening** in **Trollskull Alley**, which test whether the party completed **Consulting Hlam**, and by **The Full Picture**.
> - **Buried Thing Reported** — mark with the member's name when Vajra has received the buried-thing message from that member, whether word for word or in outline; read by **The Full Picture** and by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>

>

## Overview

The party climbs Mount Waterdeep to ask a hermit monk what threats he has perceived, and carries his answers back to Vajra.

## Summary

We climbed Mount Waterdeep's western slope to ask the monk Hlam what threats he had perceived. He told us that evil hides its face for now, and he spoke of a black net being drawn over the city again. As we left, he warned that something buried beneath Waterdeep is waking. Vajra wrote down what we told her, and we do not yet know what is buried.
