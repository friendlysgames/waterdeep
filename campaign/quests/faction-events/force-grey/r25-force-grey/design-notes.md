# Design Notes: Force Grey

## Benefits as Procedures

The restored draft listed four benefits as sentences and gave Vajra's list as "that's the full list". Each benefit is now a per-member procedure with a contact, a notice rule, a limit and a loss rule, and "once per quest" means a named campaign quest, as in the Doom Raiders and Harper rank events. Companions can come along but cannot ask for anything.

The rank *Sending* is exactly 25 words and opens with the rank's name, where the restored version ran 15 words. Counted: Force(1) Grey(2) You(3) have(4) earned(5) the(6) rank(7) Come(8) to(9) the(10) Tower(11) tomorrow(12) morning(13) Four(14) arrangements(15) come(16) with(17) it(18) and(19) one(20) is(21) a(22) veteran(23) Be(24) punctual(25).

## The Badge, the Case and the Spell

The restored badge worked "while the badge is visible" and never said what that meant. It is now pinned to the outermost garment or held up in the open hand, in the sight of the person being influenced, and it covers city officials and military officers by named post. The charge suspension was "once per operative" with no scope, and it is now one Watch case per member for the campaign. Vajra has a stated list of cases she accepts, refuses and cannot touch, and the suspension takes effect after one day.

The 7th-level spell is the fixed list of five from the mechanics reference, and *Mordenkainen's Magnificent Mansion* keeps its full name. The ranges and durations follow the 2024 *Player's Handbook*. *Teleport* has a 10-foot range, so Vajra travels with the group and walks back. *Forcecage* and *Project Image* need Concentration, so she holds each one herself for its whole length. She casts only on the surface.

## Rhendar and Renown

Rhendar Orsk replaces the old name Rhendar Solne, which was too close to Vira Solkan. He is a 2024 **Warrior Veteran** (AC 17, 65 Hit Points), with Power 37, 30 and 25 by tier from the mechanics reference. His line about his own death keeps its source wording and gains a non-combat alternative, a staged death that Vajra grants when a life is at stake. If he does die, the benefit ends for everyone.

Renown 25 does not come from the missions alone. Joining and the six missions give 19 on base awards, **The Full Picture** adds 2, and the rest comes from mission bonuses and the Earning Renown list in the Factions Guide. This rank event awards none. It reads **Orvyn Restored** or **Orvyn Lost** for one line, and **Orvyn Left in Place** has no line.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Rhendar's alignment, his dwarf species and scarred splint armor, his refusals and his voice (he has no profile, so it comes from the event text), the Staged Death procedure, the case list, the badge replacement and misuse rules, and the Tower steps as the meeting point.
