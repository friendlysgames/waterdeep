# Force Grey

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — a visit to Blackstaff Tower, or a Sending within a tenday — after one party member's Force Grey renown reaches 25. In this Event:
>
> - Vajra sends a *Sending* to the promoted character
> - They meet at Blackstaff Tower; Vajra names the change plainly and without ceremony
> - She produces the Underclock badge and explains how it functions
> - She commits the 7th-level spell access once per quest, with conditions
> - She introduces the Veteran Force Grey member available for mission support
> - She explains the suspended charge or Watch investigation authority, available once per operative
> - One line on what this threshold means in terms of the trust relationship

**Background (DM only)**

Renown 25 is full Force Grey. It is not a routine promotion. Gray Hand, Junior Griffon, and Senior Griffon were tiered access — each real, each smaller than this. Force Grey status means Vajra is putting her personal authority behind the character's judgment. She has done it for fewer people than the Tower's staff roster would suggest.

The Underclock badge is issued to active Force Grey members only. It marks the wearer as operating under the Blackstaff's direct authority. City officials and military officers recognize it — not all of them, but enough that the first thirty seconds of most conversations are different. The Advantage on Charisma checks is the mechanical version of a messier human reality.

The suspended charge or Watch investigation is among the most significant things Vajra can do for an individual operative. She uses it carefully. It does not erase what the character did — it removes the institutional consequence while the investigation is active. If the party completed a mission requiring an extralegal action and the Watch opened a file, this is when that file becomes dormant.

The Veteran assigned for mission support has field history with Force Grey. They follow the party's operational lead, but they debrief to Vajra. She does not present this as optional.

### The Sending

The *Sending* is brief, but the weight of it is different from the others:

> *"Full Force Grey. Blackstaff Tower. We have arrangements to formalize and a veteran to brief."*

### Blackstaff Tower

The door opens before they reach the step. Vajra is at the standing desk, but she sets down her pen when they enter. This is the sort of conversation she has standing without a pen in her hand.

> > "Full Force Grey. This is the threshold that means I've stopped assessing your judgment and started relying on it."

She takes something from the desk drawer — a small disc of dark metal, the size of a coin but thicker, marked on one face with the Tower's seal. The Underclock badge. She sets it in front of the promoted character.

> > "This identifies you as operating under my direct authority. City officials and military officers recognize it. Wear it when you want the conversation to start differently. You have Advantage on Charisma checks with those parties while the badge is visible."

She explains the charge suspension without buildup:

> > "Once. Per operative at full Force Grey rank. If you've acquired a Watch file doing something the city needed but couldn't officially sanction, bring the case number. I'll use the Blackstaff's authority to suspend it. The file doesn't disappear. The weight behind it does."

A pause. The Blackstaff leans against the corner of the desk where it always is.

> > "Don't come to me with a file you opened carelessly. This authority is finite and I won't waste it."

The veteran assigned to mission support is a grey-haired dwarf standing near the window with the patient quiet of someone who has long since stopped needing to prove anything. Rhendar Solne. Eleven years with Force Grey, field-scarred, two words at a time.

> [!npc-narrative]**Rhendar Solne**
> A grey-haired dwarf stands near the window with the patient quiet of someone who has long since stopped needing to prove anything. Field-scarred. Eleven years with Force Grey. He acknowledges the party with a single nod and says nothing else.

> > "Rhendar. One mission per quest, up to seven days. You run the operation; he follows your lead. He reports to me on return."

Rhendar nods once. He does not elaborate.

> > "The spells. One of up to 7th level before a mission, once per quest. Tell me what you're facing and I'll tell you what I can provide. Same rules as before: come with a specific need."

She is already half-turned when she adds:

> > "This rank is about knowing what to do with being trusted. I expect you to demonstrate that you do."

> [!dialogue]**What can you do for us at this rank?**
> "Suspend a Watch investigation. Cast a 7th-level spell before a mission, once per quest. Lend you Rhendar for up to seven days per mission. Keep the political space around your operations intact, once per quest. That's the full list."

> [!dialogue]**What if Rhendar is killed during an operation?**
> "He's been hurt before." A pause. "Bring him back. If the operation genuinely requires his death, tell me before it happens. Not after."

## Overview

Vajra Safahr contacted one party member by *Sending* and confirmed their promotion to full Force Grey status — the threshold where the Blackstaff's personal authority stands behind a character's judgment. At Blackstaff Tower, she produced the Underclock badge (Advantage on Charisma checks with city officials and military officers while visible), committed a 7th-level preparatory spell once per quest, explained the single charge suspension available per operative at this rank, and introduced Rhendar Solne — a veteran Force Grey member available for one mission per quest for up to seven days.
