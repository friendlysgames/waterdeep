# Design Notes: Force Grey First Meeting

## An Individual Offer

The restored draft treated the *Sending*, the decline and **Force Grey Offer Closed** as party-wide flags, so one refusal could close Force Grey to everyone. The event now follows the Harpers, Doom Raiders and Bregan D'aerthe models. Every character who belongs to no other faction is a candidate and answers for themselves, and **Force Grey Joined** is marked with each name. WDH sends one *Sending* to one character, so that stays. The first goes to the candidate who spoke to Renaer last in the warehouse, a second goes to a different candidate the next morning, and **Force Grey Offer Closed** lists the names of the candidates who never answered. The Tower door opens for any candidate who climbs the steps, so the single *Sending* does not decide who is offered a place.

## The Sending

The restored draft claimed 25 words and delivered 19, which is WDH's own line. The event keeps that line and adds "Renaer has told me about you.", which brings it to exactly 25. The description of the voice appears once, in the arrival readaloud, and the second *Sending* reuses it. Re-firing after a level-up uses the same words, as WDH and Appendix B require.

## What Vajra Withholds

Vajra says nothing about the Stone, the vault, Undermountain, the staff or her youth, and she never repeats a name a player gives her. Manshoon is gated: the restored draft quoted his attempt on her life, which belongs to the Tower mission, so that quote is cut. Her reason for trusting Renaer (he, Laraelra Harsard and Meloon freed her from Khondar Naomal) sits in a GM block, because her voice profile says she never asks for sympathy. The earlier lines about Renaer pulling someone out of Xanathar's sewer and the Watch missing a Zhentarim operation for three months are cut. The sewer holds Floon and the warehouse holds Renaer, and nothing sources the three months.

## Source Departures

WDH and Appendix B give an assignment within days of joining. The remix holds it back, and **Consulting Hlam** arrives by its own *Sending* at 2nd level. The Tower is Appendix B's building near Swords Street. The restored "base of Mount Waterdeep" and "no signage" are dropped, as is the Calishite Lawful Neutral stat line, which the Notable Figures page contradicts. Her header now reads Neutral, Tethyrian Human. Her tenure is never stated, because the Notable Figures page and the Vajra guide disagree on it.

## Gray Hand Benefits and the Renovation

The Factions Guide lists Gray Hand benefits without procedures. The event writes each one with a place, a limit and a loss rule. The letter carries the member's name and cannot be lent. The Tiny Hut and the vault item are party-level because the manor is shared, and they are given once. They come from the manor guide's operating-cost page, since no other event delivers them.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- No new named NPCs. The Tower mage who casts *Tiny Hut* is unnamed, and the letter's wording, the potion list (climbing, water breathing, healing), the tie-break order for the first *Sending* Vajra's lone-arrival line and her unscored test question (a friend acting against their interests, which plants checking for enchantment before accusing) are invented. Any can change without affecting the rest.
- **The Factions Come Calling** (`campaign/quests/act-i/trollskull-alley/ev-04-the-factions-come-calling.md`) still writes **Force Grey Joined** as a True or False heading and names no recipient rule for the *Sending*. The row at line 61 expects the Tiny Hut and vault scrolls, which only this event delivers. The manor guide (`02-operating-costs.md:86`) matches.
- `campaign/guides/trollskull-manor/08-notable-patrons.md:55` gives Vajra as a Calishite and :57 has her mention Kolat Towers, both against the Notable Figures page and the Manshoon gate. Her Notable Figures page (:26) carries the Manshoon quote.
- `campaign/guides/factions/06-force-grey.md` and the organization page do not list the benefit procedures written here, and G06:30 says "binding circles" for the Cassalanter residue.
