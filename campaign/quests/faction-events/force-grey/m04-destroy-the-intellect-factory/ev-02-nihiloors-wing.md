# Nihiloor's Wing

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat and Exploration Event begins when the party reaches the wing door at X23 during **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted), and it ends when Vajra has heard the party's report at the Tower. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Cross X23 and X24 past the enthralled guards and take the placement records.
> - Free the captive who is chained in a chair in X25.
> - End the Spawning Pool with holy water, by breaking the basin, or with acid or fire.
> - Face Nihiloor and his pet, and drive him out of the wing.
> - Beat the enforcers to the wing door, slip past them or fight them there.
> - Report to Vajra and receive her commendation.
>
> Only Force Grey members attend Vajra's debrief, and companions can help with everything before it.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Four things hold Nihiloor in the wing, which are the Pool, the records, the captive and his pet. The second one the party takes from him sets him running, and he is never killed here, so **Nihiloor Fled** is always marked.
> - The wing is four rooms, where X23 holds Nihiloor's files, X24 the placement records, X25 the Pool, the captive and twelve specimens, and X26 the Puppet device. The party never has to enter X26.
> - One enthralled **Tough** stands in each room and wakes only when Nihiloor, the pet or an alarm does, and the pet rests on the rim of the Pool.
> - The wing wakes when a node falls, when a thrall is harmed, or when anything louder than speech sounds. Nihiloor then reaches the party's room at the end of the second round, and the thralls wake as he passes.
> - If **Nihiloor Identified Party** is marked, he is already in X25 beside the Pool with the thralls awake, and he speaks first. He may use *Dominate Monster* on round 1 in that case.
> - If **Devourer Escaped** is marked, the pet is the devourer that rode Meloon, and its first Claw goes to a character who held Meloon in **The Trouble with Meloon**.
> - Use the Hard roster unless a member is at half hit points or fewer, or a character has gone down in the lair before X23, and then use the Standard roster.
> - The files in X23 and the Puppet device in X26 belong to **Xanathar's Lair**, and this Event does not resolve either of them.

### The Wing Door

Nihiloor's wing is the only part of the lair the Guild avoids. Soldiers who draw a shift here ask to be moved within weeks, and none of them will say why.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The door at the end of the corridor is plain oak and unlocked, with a handle worn smooth by one hand. Beyond it a long room holds shelves of loose papers and a table laid out with folders, and a Guild soldier stands against the far wall with his arms hanging at his sides. A pressure sits behind your eyes like the start of a headache, and the soldier does not turn his head as you step inside.

Each character who goes below drinks a *potion of resistance* (psychic) here. If Meloon's account gave Advantage, it applies to the first Wisdom (Perception) or Dexterity (Stealth) check made at this door.

> [!exploration]**Crossing X23**
>
> The thrall in X23 is an enthralled Guild soldier who wakes only for Nihiloor, the pet or an alarm, so a character who walks past it needs no check.
>
> - **A character carries the courier's disk:** The thrall stays asleep as long as the bearer walks in front alone, and it wakes if anyone else crosses with the bearer.
> - **A character searches the files:** The folders hold Nihiloor's research on the four villain factions, so run the entry for X23 in **Xanathar's Lair**. Do not present the Manshoon section as Force Grey knowledge until **Manshoon Named** is marked.
> - **A character attacks the thrall:** The wing wakes, using the rule in **What Is Actually True**.

### The Placement Records

X24 is a narrow room with a locked cabinet, and the thrall in X24 stands beside it with the key on a cord at his belt.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The room smells of ink and old brine, and a cabinet with glass doors stands against one wall. Behind the glass lie six thick folders tied in grey ribbon, and a Guild soldier stands beside them with a key hanging from a cord at his belt. His eyes stay fixed on the opposite wall while you look the cabinet over.

> [!exploration]**Taking the Records**
>
> The cabinet is locked, and taking the folders is the first of the four nodes.
>
> - A character who picks the lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check opens it without a sound.
> - A character who breaks the glass makes a loud noise that wakes the wing, and a character who takes the key from the thrall's belt wakes the thrall.
> - The folders weigh about 20 pounds, and gathering them takes one action.
> - Four folders each hold a name, a post, a date and notes, and two hold a post with a blank where the name goes.

A character who opens the top folder and reads it finds the following.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The page is written in a small, neat hand, and a character who reads it aloud finds that it says:
>
> > Dall, Orvyn, appeals clerk at the Trades Ward district magistracy, placed and fully integrated. The host reports to the courier every fifth day, and it is cooperative and unremarkable, and nobody will miss him.

> [!gamemaster]**The Other Folders**
>
> The other three placed hosts are a Dock Ward Watch quartermaster's runner, a customs-house tally clerk and a harbormaster's messenger. The two pending folders name a Castle Ward captain's secretary and a magistrate's reader as targets, and the names themselves stay in the folders for later events to use. If **Placement Records Taken** is marked, Vajra keeps them.

### The Pool and the Captive

X25 is the center of the wing, and it holds the Spawning Pool, twelve specimens along the wall, the captive in a chair and the pet.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The room is wide and warm, lit from below by a dim blue-white glow. A round stone basin of brine sits in the middle, eight feet across, and three pale shapes the size of a fist drift slowly beneath its surface. Racks of glass-fronted tanks line the walls, each holding a small still body wired to the next, and a drow in a stone chair lifts his head while a grey thing on the basin's rim turns toward you.

> [!gamemaster]**The Pool**
>
> The Spawning Pool is a stone basin eight feet across and four feet deep, and the three spawn inside it are Tiny, harmless and unable to leave the brine. They die within 1 round of the Pool's end, and *Detect Evil and Good* shows aberrant presences in the brine. The twelve specimens are not creatures to fight, and a character who reads the notes beside them in full makes the DC 13 Wisdom saving throw from **Xanathar's Lair** or gains 1 level of Exhaustion.
>
> The Pool is destroyed when one of the methods below finishes, and a method interrupted partway leaves it damaged with the spawn alive. Hosts the Pool has already produced are not affected.

> [!exploration]**Ending the Pool**
>
> - **Holy water:** Four vials emptied into the brine kill the spawn in a violent reaction, and a sound like a distant scream carries through the lair. The enforcers arrive at X23 3 minutes after the scream.
> - **The basin:** A successful DC 18 Strength (Athletics) check breaks the stone, and a character who takes the Help action gives the roller Advantage. It is loud and fills the room with dust, and the enforcers arrive 3 minutes after the first blow.
> - **Acid or fire:** At least 7 acid or fire damage into the brine each round for three rounds, from flasks, a spell or any other source, ends the Pool with no attack roll. It is quiet, and the enforcers arrive 5 minutes after the first round.
>
> The enforcers arrive at the earlier of two times, which are the one above counted from the first sound, and 3 minutes after Nihiloor passes the wing door and wakes the Guild by telepathy.

> [!social]**The Captive in the Chair**
>
> Zaibon Kyszalt (Neutral Evil, Drow, he/him) :: a House Auvryndar drow taken in Skullport, drugged and Incapacitated, who does not know what is planned for him.
>
> Zaibon speaks slowly and formally through the drug, and he is haughty by habit and afraid underneath. He believes the party works for the Guild until it unlocks the shackles, and nobody has told him what Nihiloor intends.
>
> Zaibon is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who took him, which he blames on the Guild and not on House Freth.
> - What he heard in the chair, which is that the thing asked him questions inside his head.
> - His house's plans for Skullport, which he mentions to bargain and then regrets.
>
> Zaibon will not discuss his superior's whereabouts.

> [!qna]**Who are you?**
>
> > "I am Zaibon Kyszalt, of House Auvryndar, and you are not Guild, because the Guild does not unlock chairs. Tell me who sent you, and then tell me where this is."

> [!qna]**What did it do to you?**
>
> > "It asked me questions without a voice, about my house and about Skullport. Then it stopped asking, and that frightened me more than the questions did."

> [!qna]**Where will you go?**
>
> > "I will go down, if the tunnels allow it, and if you will not take me there then put me on a road. House Auvryndar does not forget a debt, even one owed to surface folk."

> [!gamemaster]**If Soluun Expelled Is Marked**
>
> Soluun Xibrindas sits in the chair instead of Zaibon. He sold the Guild's divers the *Scarlet Marpenoth*'s berth, and once the deal was done the Guild seized him, because Nihiloor wants a host who knows how the company works. Soluun wears a silver disc on a cord and carries no weapons, and he speaks in short, hard sentences without naming Jarlaxle or Bregan D'aerthe. If any character is an elf or half-elf, he spits and says "Surface-blood" before anything else.
>
> A party that knows him from **The Dockside Killer** may leave him in the chair. If the party frees him, he wants nothing from Force Grey and stays out of sight at the Tower until he can walk, and **Captive Freed** is marked only if he leaves the lair alive.

> [!exploration]**Freeing the Captive**
>
> The chair has four shackles, and Nihiloor carries the key and keeps it when he runs.
>
> - A character who makes a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check opens one shackle, so four shackles take four checks, and two characters can work at once.
> - A character without tools who breaks one shackle with a successful DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check makes a loud noise that wakes the wing.
> - The captive is Incapacitated for an hour and must be carried, which halves the carrier's Speed.
> - The captive's freedom is the second node if the records or the pet have already fallen, and the pet does not occupy him.

### Nihiloor

By default Nihiloor is in X26 at the Puppet device, with the pet on the Pool's rim. When the wing wakes, he comes into X25 with the thralls behind him.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A tall pale shape glides in from the dark doorway on the far side of the room, its head a mass of slow tentacles. It steps between you and the basin without hurrying, the grey thing on the rim hops onto its shoulder, and a cold, clear voice speaks inside your head.
>
> > "You are frightened, and that is an appropriate response to what you have found. Your minds are adequate, and I will examine them one at a time."

If **Nihiloor Identified Party** is marked, he is already beside the Pool when the party enters, and he says the following instead.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A mind flayer stands beside the basin with a small grey devourer on its shoulder, and it does not turn as you step through the door. The cold voice arrives behind your eyes before you have taken a second step.
>
> > "I have your faces from the one who sat at the large man's table, and I find it fascinating that you came here. You will be of use to me."

> [!hazard]**Nihiloor and His Children**
>
> Nihiloor uses the boss block in section 4.1 of the **Force Grey Mechanics Reference**. The pet is the **Occupying Devourer** from section 5.1 of the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**, and the thralls are **Tough**. Use the row for the party's size.
>
> - **Three characters:** Hard, Nihiloor and the pet. Standard, Nihiloor alone, with the pet joining on round 3.
> - **Four characters:** Hard, Nihiloor, the pet and two thralls. Standard, Nihiloor and the pet.
> - **Five characters:** Hard, Nihiloor, the pet and four thralls. Standard, Nihiloor, the pet and two thralls.
>
> Thralls join in this order: X25, X26, X24, X23. Thralls not in the roster stay where they stand.
>
> #### Nihiloor's Tactics
> To begin, Nihiloor steps between the party and whichever of the four things they reach for first, and he never uses Withdraw while two or more of them remain. The players can see this, and it is the Tell.
>
> During the first phase, Nihiloor:
> - Uses Tentacles on whoever touches a node, and *Mind Blast* when two or more characters stand in the cone.
> - Uses *Dominate Monster* only on the character standing between him and the pet or a node, and never on round 1 unless **Nihiloor Identified Party** is marked.
> - Uses *Interpose Thrall* when a thrall stands within 10 feet, and *Pull the Leash* when a creature damages him.
>
> The first phase ends when it reaches 0 hit points, when the second node falls, or when three thralls are down, and then the second phase begins. Nihiloor moves toward the wing door each turn with Withdraw, and on his way he:
> - Orders the pet to hold the room, and the pet fights to the death and does not follow him.
> - Leaves the nearest thrall in each doorway to fight until he is 120 feet away.
> - Uses *Mind Blast* and Tentacles only on a creature that blocks his way.
> - Uses *Dominate Monster* on one character who chases him, so that the character blocks the others for a round.
> - Does not stop to burn the records, the Pool or the notes.
> - Wakes the Guild by telepathy at the wing door, which starts the enforcers' clock, and enters the tunnel toward X4 about five rounds later.
>
> If the party blocks every route out, or reduces him to 0 hit points in the second phase, he casts *Plane Shift* and is gone, because Last Exit is a plot rule and he cannot die here. The thralls surrender or flee once he is far enough away.
>
> #### The Pet's Tactics
> To begin, the pet hops to Nihiloor's shoulder or stays on the rim, and it uses Claw and Devour Intellect on the nearest character. It never uses Occupy Body, because Nihiloor's devourers do not take hosts at random, and it fights to the death.
>
> The fight ends when Nihiloor leaves the wing or the party stops fighting. A party can end it without beating him by taking a second node quickly, because he runs when it falls.

After the second node falls, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The mind flayer's head tilts toward the far door, and the voice arrives in your skull as calm as before.
>
> > "The pet will hold this room while I leave it, and the programme can be rebuilt somewhere else. You will not be of use to me today."
>
> It starts across the room, and the guard in the doorway steps into its path.

> [!gamemaster]**The Puppet Device**
>
> X26 holds the psionic relay that keeps Xanathar under *Dominate Monster*, with Nihiloor's notes beside it, and this Event does not resolve it. If the party reaches X26, run the entry for it in **Xanathar's Lair**. If the party mentions the device to Vajra, she writes it down and says that it belongs to the lair operation.

### The Enforcers

The Guild stays out of the wing by habit, and the habit lasts until a scream or Nihiloor's call. After that, a squad comes from the arena side to the wing door.

> [!hazard]**The Guild Squad**
>
> The enforcers are a squad of Guild soldiers who do not know Nihiloor's secrets. Use the row for the party's size.
>
> - **Three characters:** two **Tough**.
> - **Four characters:** one **Tough Boss** and two **Tough**.
> - **Five characters:** one **Tough Boss** and four **Tough**.
>
> #### The Squad's Tactics
> To begin, the squad forces the wing door at X23 and calls for the thrall inside to stand aside.
>
> During combat, the squad:
> - Holds the doorway and fights whoever is nearest to it.
> - Fires crossbows from behind the Tough Boss, if one is present.
>
> The squad flees if the Tough Boss falls or if half of it is down. A party that cannot beat the squad can hide behind the cabinet and the thrall in X24 with a successful DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) group check, which lets the squad pass into X25 while the party slips out of the door behind it. A failure starts the fight at the door.
>
> The party's way back out of the lair belongs to **Xanathar's Lair**.

### Vajra's Debrief

Vajra takes the report at the standing desk, in the same sitting as her lair debrief if that quest has finished, and companions wait in the street. Mark the outcomes now, which are **Pool Destroyed** if a method finished, **Placement Records Taken** if the folders are on her desk, **Captive Freed** if the captive left the lair alive, and **Nihiloor Fled**.

If **Pool Destroyed** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra reads the account without looking up, and her pen does not move until she reaches the last line. She sets it down on the ledger and looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "The pool is gone, so nothing more will hatch from it, and that is the work I sent you to do. Well done, all of you."

If **Pool Destroyed** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra's pen stops on the line about the basin, and she does not raise her voice.
>
> > "Then the pool is still hatching, and he will place more hosts from it. I will plan for that, so tell me exactly what stopped you."

If **Placement Records Taken** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra unties the grey ribbon on the top folder and reads the first page, then the next, and her mouth tightens at the third.
>
> > "Shit, there are four placed and two more waiting for a name. I will read every page tonight and send for you when I know whom to go after first."

If **Captive Freed** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra looks at the drow the Tower's healer has helped into the room, and then she looks at you.
>
> > "He will be fed and kept in a locked room until he can talk. I won't hold a man the Guild already held, so when he can walk, he walks."

Nihiloor always fled, so read the following in every debrief.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra sets the report aside and takes a clean sheet of Tower letterhead from the desk. She writes a line, signs it, and slides a copy across to each of you.
>
> > "He ran, so he will build again somewhere else, which is why the records and the pool mattered. This commendation says what you did in that wing, but it is not a promotion, because the Tower keeps rank in a ledger and not in a letter."

The commendation is a sheet of Tower letterhead signed in Vajra's hand, naming the member and the wing. It is story only and changes no rank.

### Renown Opportunities

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Force Grey member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Vajra, whether the Pool fell or not. Companions gain none. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:
>
> - **+1 Renown:** **Pool Destroyed** is marked, and all three spawn died with it.
> - **+1 Renown:** **Captive Freed** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** **Placement Records Taken** is marked.
>
> Each bonus can be earned only once. In the variant where the lair already ran, award this Renown only to members whose party entered the wing.

### Aftermath

The Guild's enforcers search the wing and find the Pool ruined, the records gone and the thralls standing in the doorways, and the ones taken alive remember only standing in a room. Nihiloor is gone from the lair, and **Xanathar's Lair** decides where he turns up. If **Pool Destroyed** is not marked, new hosts keep arriving, and a member who reaches Renown 10 here also qualifies for **Senior Griffon**.

### Concluding the Event

Vajra's debrief ends the mission. Record the four outcomes separately, because later events read each of them.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Pool Destroyed** — mark when a method finished and the Pool is inert; read by **The Legate's Eyes**, where no new hosts arrive, and by **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted).
> - **Placement Records Taken** — mark when the folders are delivered to Vajra; read by **The Legate's Eyes**, where they are one of the leads to Orvyn Dall.
> - **Captive Freed** — mark when the captive leaves the lair alive; read by **Vajra's Debrief** in this Event, and by **The Dive** (Bregan D'aerthe) if the captive was Soluun.
> - **Nihiloor Fled** — mark whenever Nihiloor leaves the wing, which is always; read by **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) and **The Full Picture**.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> **The Legate's Eyes** becomes available when an individual Force Grey member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level. This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

The party fights its way into Nihiloor's wing, ends the pool that breeds his devourers and decides what else to carry out. Nihiloor flees, and Vajra writes the party a commendation.

## Summary

We reached Nihiloor's wing in Xanathar's Lair and destroyed what we could. Nihiloor ran when we took the second thing from him, and Vajra gave us a commendation. Where did he go, and who are the people named in the folders?
