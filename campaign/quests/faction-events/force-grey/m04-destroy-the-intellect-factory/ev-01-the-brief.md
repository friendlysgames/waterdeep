# The Brief

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when a Force Grey member who marked **M3 Complete** prepares for **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted), and it ends when the party leaves Blackstaff Tower for the lair. Vajra's brief comes in addition to her lair consultation in Scene 1 of that quest. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Vajra's *Sending* and take her brief at the standing desk.
> - Learn what she knows about the pool in Nihiloor's wing, and what she will not say.
> - Collect potions and holy water, and hear that she will pay for any *raise dead*.
> - Hear what Meloon remembers of the wing, if **Meloon Restored** is marked.
> - Learn how much Nihiloor already knows about them.
>
> Only Force Grey members attend the brief, and companions wait in the street and help with everything after it. Vajra's panopticus advice in the lair quest is separate from this brief, and this brief gives nothing for the sensors.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Nihiloor's wing is X23 to X26 of the lair, and there is no separate route, because the party reaches the wing while it runs **Xanathar's Lair**.
> - The wing holds a pool of brine in X25, placement records in X24 and a captive in X25, and **Nihiloor's Wing** describes all three.
> - Vajra learned of the pool from the one report of a Force Grey agent inside the Guild, who has not answered since, and she does not say so.
> - Destroying the pool stops new hosts but does nothing for the hosts already walking the city, and the records name four of them.
> - Mirt's "three of ours" in **The Sleeping Asset** counts Harpers, and Meloon and the hosts in the records are Force Grey's problem and separate from that count.
> - Until **Manshoon Named** is marked, nobody in this Event names Manshoon, and no Force Grey speaker names Jarlaxle or Bregan D'aerthe.

### The Brief

The *Sending* reaches every eligible member while the party is preparing for the lair. Vajra receives them that evening, and companions wait in the street.

> [!readaloud]
>
> You are halfway through a meal when a dry, flat voice arrives behind your eyes. It gives no greeting, it moves fast, and nobody near you hears a word of it.
>
> > "Nihiloor breeds devourers inside Xanathar's Lair, and you are going there. Come to Blackstaff Tower first, this evening. Members only. Companions wait in the street."
>
> The voice stops as quickly as it began, and you have room to answer in a few words if you want to.

The *Sending* is exactly 25 words, and a member can answer in up to 25 words, though Vajra does not reply.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The street door of Blackstaff Tower opens before anyone knocks, and the stair beyond climbs past more floors than the outside of the building shows. Vajra Safahr stands at the tall desk at the top with her sleeves pushed back, and small bottles and a rack of vials stand in rows beside her ledger. There is no chair for visitors, so she waits until you are all in the room and begins to speak.
>
> > "Nihiloor keeps a pool of brine in a wing of that lair, and even the Guild's own guards stay out of it. The basin is stone, eight feet across and four deep, and three devourers are growing in the glowing brine. I want the whole thing destroyed."

> [!social]**The Blackstaff at Her Desk**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep, who has laid her supplies out in rows and gives her orders in full sentences.
>
> Vajra has not slept much and does not mention it, and she waits for each answer before she goes on. She is blunt about the danger and does not soften it, and when someone asks if she is all right, she answers with one word.
>
> Vajra is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The pool, its size and the three ways to end it.
> - Nihiloor, who runs when the cost rises.
> - What the potions and the holy water are for.
> - The records and the people Nihiloor keeps, which she suspects and cannot confirm.
>
> Vajra will not discuss how she knows about the pool, the contents of the Blackstaff, or her own part in Meloon's past.

> [!qna]**How do you know about the pool?**
>
> > "I won't tell you that, and you don't need it to do the job. I know where the pool is and what it does, and I know it will be loud."

> [!qna]**How do we end it?**
>
> > "Pour four vials of holy water into the brine, or break the stone basin, which takes a strong back. You can also burn it with acid or fire for three rounds. Every one of those makes noise, so don't stay to admire the work."

> [!qna]**What about Nihiloor?**
>
> > "He's a mind flayer who has been using this city as a fucking hatchery, but he doesn't stand and die for his work. The pool is the mission, so if he runs, let him run."

> [!qna]**What about the people it already placed?**
>
> > "Destroying the pool stops the next ones, but it does nothing for the ones already walking around. Nihiloor keeps notes and he keeps people, and if you find either of them, bring them out."

> [!qna]**What are the potions for?**
>
> > "Everything he does to you is psychic, and the potion halves the damage. It won't stop him from stunning you, so drink it at the wing door and not before."

> [!qna]**What do we get?**
>
> > "I'll put what you did in writing, and I will cover the cost of any *raise dead* spells that become necessary."

Vajra says the last part without inflection. If a member asks whether she is all right, she looks at the ink on her fingers and says, "Irrelevant."

### What Is on the Desk

Vajra hands out the supplies herself, and Merris signs out the holy water because her brief names it, as in **The Trouble with Meloon**.

> [!exploration]**The Supplies**
>
> - **Potions of resistance:** one *potion of resistance* (psychic) for each character who goes below, member or companion, lasting 1 hour, so each character drinks it at the wing door.
> - **Holy water:** four vials worth 25 gp each, signed out to the member who holds the brief. Merris supplies his standing two, and Vajra adds two from her own study for the Pool.
> - **Water breathing:** one spare *potion of water breathing*, which lasts 24 hours, for a stretch of the sewer approach that has flooded.
> - ***Raise dead*:** Vajra covers the cost of any *raise dead* the lair makes necessary for a character who dies below.
>
> The potions are part of why **Nihiloor's Wing** uses its Hard roster by default.

If **Courier Identified** is marked, Vajra adds one more thing and reads or paraphrases the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra sets a small leaden disk on the desk with a beholder's eye stamped on its face, and she slides it across with one finger.
>
> > "Merris cast that from a wax impression of Dobb Ketterly's courier disk while he slept off a pint in the Dock Ward. It's only a copy, but whoever carries it walks in ahead of the others."

The disk is a plain lead copy and carries no magic. At X2 in the lair quest, at the Suspicious tier, it passes the Guild pass check. In the wing, the thrall in X23 treats the bearer as the courier and stays asleep while the bearer walks ahead alone, and anyone else who crosses X23 with the bearer wakes it.

### What Vajra Has Heard

Vajra learned what happened in **The Trouble with Meloon** from the party's own report. Read each case that applies.

If **Nihiloor Identified Party** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra turns a page in her ledger without looking at it, and her pen stops above the line.
>
> > "You sat at his table on the fifth day, and the thing was still in him on the tenth. The courier carried your faces to Nihiloor, so expect him to be waiting for you."

If **Devourer Escaped** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra caps her ink and looks at whichever of you held Meloon on the stool.
>
> > "The one that rode him got away and went home, and I'd wager it is on Nihiloor's shoulder now. It knows what you look like, so be ready for it to go for you first."

If neither is marked, Vajra says only that Nihiloor "may know your faces and may not, and I wouldn't bet on the second."

If **Meloon Lost** is marked, Azuredge leans against the wall behind her desk with a cloth draped over the blade, and nobody is invited to look at it. Vajra does not mention him unless a member does, and then she says that Meloon "was taken into that lair twice, and I'd like it burned for that."

### Meloon at the Tower

If **Meloon Restored** is marked, or neither Meloon outcome is marked and Vajra has since taken him at the Tower, Meloon waits in the guest room on the stair. Vajra lets the members see him before they go. He is back on his feet and wants to come, and she says no.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Meloon sits on the edge of a narrow bed in the Tower's guest room with Azuredge across his knees and both boots on. He looks up when you come in, and his big voice is still rough from disuse.
>
> > "Fuck me, you're going down there, so sit and let me tell you what I saw before you go. I walked that route twice and I couldn't stop my own feet."

> [!social]**The Man Who Walked It Twice**
>
> Meloon Wardragon (Neutral Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a barrel-chested Force Grey veteran who is loud and furious and has nowhere to put it.
>
> Meloon swears constantly, and he is sharper than he was a tenday ago. He remembers the lair as a series of doors he was walked through without choosing to, and he saw the wing only from its door.
>
> Meloon is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The route to the wing door, which he describes in order.
> - The guards, who stood in every room like furniture.
> - The thing on Nihiloor's shoulder.
> - Whether he can come, which Vajra has already answered.

> [!qna]**The route?**
>
> > "You go past the arena, and then there's a plain door with nobody guarding it. The Guild stays out of that wing, and the men who get posted there ask to be moved and won't say why."

> [!qna]**The guards?**
>
> > "There's one in every room, standing still with a face like a wet plate. They don't move until Nihiloor does, or the thing on his shoulder, or an alarm, so walk past them carefully."

> [!qna]**What's on his shoulder?**
>
> > "It's a devourer like the one that wore me, and he pets it like a cat. Hit it first if you can, because I don't think he'd leave the room without it."

> [!qna]**Can you come?**
>
> > "Vajra says no, and she's fucking right, because I still can't hold a sword for long. If you see that thing, hit it once for me."

Meloon's account gives the party Advantage on the first Wisdom (Perception) or Dexterity (Stealth) check made at the wing door. If **Devourer Escaped** is also marked, he adds that the one on Nihiloor's shoulder is probably the one that wore him, and that it will want the party.

### Going Below

The party now carries on with the lair quest. When it reaches the door of the wing at X23, play **Nihiloor's Wing**, which changes nothing about where the party enters, how it crosses the lair or what the panopticus does.

### If Xanathar's Lair Has Already Run

If **M3 Complete** is marked after the party has finished the lair, this Event is a debrief, and nobody gets the brief, the potions or Meloon's account. The *Sending* arrives after the party's lair debrief, and Vajra takes the report in the same sitting as the one the lair quest already sets out.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra's dry voice arrives as before.
>
> > "Xanathar's Lair is done. Report to Blackstaff Tower. Members only. Tell me what Nihiloor kept in his wing, and what you carried out of it."
>
> The voice stops as quickly as it began, and you have room to answer in a few words if you want to.

The *Sending* is exactly 25 words. At the Tower, ask what the party did in X23 to X26 and use the row that fits.

> [!gamemaster]**Reading the Wing**
>
> - **The party never entered the wing:** Mark nothing, and Vajra says "Force Grey will handle it." No Renown is awarded, and the pool stands as far as the campaign is concerned, so **The Legate's Eyes** finds new hosts arriving.
> - **The party brought the lair down with the smokepowder:** Mark nothing here, because the wing is rubble and Nihiloor escaped with his projects intact. The outcome block of **Nihiloor's Wing** is the only place that marks **Nihiloor Fled**. Vajra says the factory has moved below the city, and no Renown is awarded.
> - **The party entered the wing:** Mark the outcomes from what happened there, using the conditions in **Nihiloor's Wing**. Its outcome block is the only place that marks **Nihiloor Fled**. Award the Mission Renown from that Event, and play **Vajra's Debrief** from it.

## Overview

Vajra briefs the Force Grey members at the Tower before they enter Xanathar's Lair. She tells them about a pool of brine in Nihiloor's wing and sends them to destroy it.

## Summary

We prepared for Xanathar's Lair and Vajra called us to the Tower first. She told us about a pool in Nihiloor's wing where devourers grow, handed out potions and holy water, and said she would pay for any *raise dead*.
