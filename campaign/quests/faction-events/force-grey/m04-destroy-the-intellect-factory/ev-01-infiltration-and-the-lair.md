# Infiltration and the Lair

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event begins when the party receives Vajra's *Sending* and then reports to Blackstaff Tower before entering Xanathar's Lair. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Vajra's twenty-five-word *Sending* and meet her at Blackstaff Tower for the operation brief and potions.
> - Learn the lair's layout from Meloon Wardragon, if he was restored in Mission 3, using the devourer's residual memories.
> - Choose one of three entry routes into the lair, depending on prior mission work.
> - Navigate five named areas to reach the Spawning Pool, with a random encounter chance at each.
> - Encounter or avoid Nihiloor in X24, and decide whether to stabilize the unconscious **Soluun Xibrindas** there.
> - Reach X25, adjacent to the Spawning Pool, in preparation for the **Nihiloor and the Pool** Event.
>
> #### Entry Route Availability
>
> Check the party's current intelligence before the session and determine which routes are available. The Sewer Route requires intelligence from **Finding Floon** or Lords' Alliance Mission 1. The Gathering Point Route requires any Xanathar Guild passphrase or street contact from prior mission work. The Bregan D'aerthe Route requires an established relationship with Jarlaxle's organization — BD missions completed, the Mission 2 calling card received, or Sea Maidens Faire reconnaissance. A party that has none of these falls back to the Sewer Route using Herath's note from Lords' Alliance Mission 1.
>
> #### Nihiloor's Location
>
> Decide before the session whether Nihiloor is in X24 or X26. Nihiloor is present in X24 fifty percent of the time. If the party marked **Nihiloor Identified Party** in Mission 3, Nihiloor has been notified and chooses X24 — it is waiting on the route rather than at the pool. If that outcome was not marked, use the fifty percent split: roll or choose based on what works for the session. Nihiloor's behavior is the same in either location: escape first, *Mind Blast* only if blocked.

### The Brief

Vajra does not visit. The *Sending* arrives in one party member's head, without announcement.

> [!readaloud]
> The voice arrives without preamble, quick and clipped and already counting.
>
> > *"Nihiloor's Spawning Pool is in Xanathar's Lair. The source of the devourers. Infiltrate. Destroy the pool and all three inside. Come to the Tower first."*
>
> There is no follow-up and no opportunity to ask questions. The *Sending* is the whole brief except for what Vajra will tell them in person.

The party goes to Blackstaff Tower. Vajra has been waiting.

> [!readaloud]
> The potions are on the desk when you arrive — one for each of you, labeled in Vajra's hand: *psychic resistance*. She adds a spare *potion of water breathing* to the row without comment, and straightens.
>
> > "The pool is a circular basin of brine, eight feet across, four feet deep, slightly luminescent. Three devourers developing in the fluid. The pool and all three must be destroyed." A pause. "I will cover the cost of any *raise dead* spells that become necessary."
>
> She says it without inflection. There is a short silence.

> [!social]**The Blackstaff**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep; precise, impatient, and done performing warmth she does not feel.
>
> Vajra has located the pool. She has the potions ready and has already decided she is covering any *raise dead* costs, which means she has already thought through the scenario in which she has to. She states all of this without sentiment. The party should understand what covering *raise dead* costs means, and she trusts them to.
>
> Conversation topics Vajra will engage on:
> - The pool's dimensions and what is in it (already stated, but she will repeat on request).
> - Whether Nihiloor will be present (fifty percent; she does not know where it is right now).
> - Entry routes, if the party asks — she knows which are available to them.
> - The three destruction methods (she names them if asked; she does not volunteer them).
> - Escape timing — she gives the enforcer clock without sentiment.
>
> She will not offer encouragement, tell them to be careful, or explain how she located the pool.

> [!qna]**Will Nihiloor be there?**
>
> She looks at the row of potions and back at them.
>
> > "Fifty percent of the time. Its priority is escape, not engagement. The pool is the mission, not Nihiloor."

> [!qna]**How do we destroy the pool?**
>
> > "Four vials of holy water in the brine. Immediate reaction, three minutes before enforcers arrive. Physical destruction of the basin — DC 18 Strength, loud, three minutes. Sustained acid or fire, two dice per round for three rounds, quieter, five minutes if you move before the first enforcer completes the route." She adds: "Loud methods mean more urgency on the escape. Plan accordingly."

> [!qna]**The pay?**
>
> She gives them a flat look.
>
> > "I am covering raise dead. Take the potions."

> [!gamemaster]**Meloon's Intelligence (if Meloon Restored is marked)**
>
> If the party marked **Meloon Restored** in Mission 3, Meloon is available to brief the party before they leave. The devourer that occupied him had access to two prior visits to Xanathar's Lair in Meloon's memory. His recall is fragmentary — the devourer suppressed his own conscious memory of what it used him for — but the layout of X1 through X24 is familiar enough that he can sketch a rough map and identify the beholder zombie in X2. He does this at a table in the common room with a drink he actually touches and profanity at its natural frequency.
>
> > "Fuck, it's weird. I know the layout but I don't remember going. There's a beholder zombie in the second room. It reacts to movement. And Ahmaergo — duergar, thick as a wall — does random inspections in the connecting corridor. Don't let him run into you without a good story."
>
> The party has Advantage on the first Wisdom (Perception) check they make inside the lair if Meloon briefed them.

### Getting In

Three routes are available depending on prior mission work. Check entry route availability before the session (see Gamemaster's Summary).

**The Sewer Route** — Available if the party has sewer access from Lords' Alliance Mission 1 (Herath's note) or any intelligence on the Xanathar sewer hideout from **Finding Floon**. Enter through the Orb Confectioners' cellar using the passphrase "eight small eyes / around an ocular cake," through the sewer hideout, and into the lair's lower areas.

**The Gathering Point Route** — Available if the party has any Xanathar Guild passphrase or contact from **Finding Floon**, **Trollskull Alley**, OG Mission 1, or prior faction mission work. Access through one of the Terasse Estate gathering points; the party is escorted blind to the entry hall.

**The Bregan D'aerthe Route** — Available if the party has established a relationship with Jarlaxle's organization through BD missions or Sea Maidens Faire reconnaissance. Bregan D'aerthe operates independently of any devotion to drow religion; what they have in this district is a practical working passage into X1, maintained for their own reasons. Three **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) checks** are required to follow the guildsign through the underground passage without losing the thread.

> [!gamemaster]**No Route Available**
>
> A party with none of the above still has access to the Sewer Route. Herath's note from Lords' Alliance Mission 1 covers it, and a party at this mission level has had enough city time to have acquired it. If they genuinely lack it, use the Gathering Point Route with a brief investigation scene to establish the passphrase — one contact in the Dock Ward is sufficient.

### The Navigation Route

The route to X25 runs through five named areas. At each named area, roll 1d10: on a 10, a random encounter occurs. Roll 1d4 for the encounter type.

| d4 | Encounter |
|----|-----------|
| 1 | **Flutterfoot Zipswiggle** (goblin; skittish; can be bribed with food to look the other way) |
| 2 | **Ahmaergo** (duergar enforcer; conducting inspection; **DC 15 Deception** to pass as authorized) |
| 3 | **Nar'l Xibrindas** with his grell bodyguard (hostile unless convinced otherwise) |
| 4 | **Ott Steeltoes** (confused and suspicious; not immediately violent) |

**X1 — Entry.** The lair's first area. Use the location journal for Xanathar's Sewer Hideout as the area reference. This is the same space the party entered during **Finding Floon**. It is no less occupied.

**X2 — Watched Hall.** A beholder zombie and gas spores. The zombie responds to movement; the gas spores respond to magic. The party can trigger one or the other before the other reacts — they cannot trigger both simultaneously without giving both a response opportunity. Choose which threat to engage first.

> [!hazard]**The Watched Hall**
>
> At the start of any encounter here, the beholder zombie occupies the center of the hall and the gas spores cluster near the far walls.
>
> Over the course of the encounter, the beholder zombie will prioritize any character who moves in its sightline. The gas spores will drift toward any magical effect and react to being touched or struck. The battle ends when one threat is eliminated or bypassed — the party can choose to deal with the zombie and slip past the spores, or use a diversion to get the zombie's attention while others move through.
>
> A party that Meloon briefed knows about the zombie. They do not know about the gas spores unless they scout.

**X22 Corridor.** A connecting passage with no base inhabitants. The random encounter check applies.

**X24 — Extraction Chamber.** The location journal for Xanathar's Lair has the full room description. Read what follows before running this area.

> [!readaloud]
> The Extraction Chamber smells of damp stone and something older and wetter underneath it, and the room is wide enough that a portion of it stays in shadow regardless of where your light is. In the far corner, a figure is slumped against the wall with his back to the stone and his legs at angles that suggest he did not choose that position, and his chest moves with the shallow rhythm of someone alive and not much else.

> [!gamemaster]**Nihiloor in X24**
>
> If you determined Nihiloor is here before the session, it detects the party on approach (passive Perception 18) and does not immediately attack. From round one it moves toward the alternate exit. It uses *Mind Blast* (60-foot Cone, **DC 15 Intelligence**; failure: **4d8 + 4 psychic damage** and Stunned until the end of the target's next turn) only if the party blocks all exits. It uses *Dominate Monster* if cornered. Its goal is to leave, not to win.
>
> If Nihiloor escapes here, it resurfaces in **Xanathar's Lair**. Parties who ran it off here will recognize it there.
>
> If Nihiloor is destroyed or cornered here, it is gone entirely. This affects **Xanathar's Lair** — Xanathar's retinue has a vacancy and his mind-control network has lost its operational intelligence in Waterdeep.

> [!hazard]**Nihiloor**
>
> At the start of combat, Nihiloor is moving. It does not give the party a stationary target.
>
> Over the course of combat, Nihiloor will prioritize any action that moves it toward the exit over any action that deals damage. It uses *Mind Blast* as a last resort when the exit is blocked, then *Dominate Monster* when *Mind Blast* has already stunned the blocking characters. The battle ends when Nihiloor escapes or when it is reduced to 0 hit points.
>
> Use the **Mind Flayer** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*).

The drow in the corner is **Soluun Xibrindas**, alive, unconscious, in poor condition. Stabilizing him takes one action. Moving him at speed during the escape requires someone to carry or drag him — a concrete mechanical burden the party will feel in ev-02.

> [!gamemaster]**Soluun Xibrindas**
>
> Soluun has been claiming Bregan D'aerthe affiliation to stay alive. The Guild believes it is holding a BD spy. The affiliation is forged — he was disowned by Jarlaxle before the campaign began for unsanctioned operations, and the identification token he carries is his own creation. If the party encountered him during Doom Raiders Mission 1, they recognize him. A party that rescues him earns a personal debt from Soluun; whether Jarlaxle's organization extends that debt officially depends on the party's standing with Bregan D'aerthe and what Jarlaxle considers useful.

**X25 — Food for Thought.** The room adjacent to the Spawning Pool. Secure this room before entering X26. Proceed to the **Nihiloor and the Pool** Event.

### Concluding the Event

The party has reached X25. Proceed to the **Nihiloor and the Pool** Event.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Nihiloor Encountered in X24** — mark when Nihiloor is present in X24 and the party interacts with it (fleeing, fighting, or passing); read by **Nihiloor and the Pool** (Nihiloor is not in X26).
> - **Nihiloor Destroyed in X24** — mark when Nihiloor is brought to 0 hit points here; read by **Nihiloor and the Pool** and **Xanathar's Lair** (Nihiloor is gone from the campaign).
> - **Nihiloor Fled X24** — mark when Nihiloor escapes the room; read by **Nihiloor and the Pool** (Nihiloor is not in X26) and **Xanathar's Lair** (Nihiloor resurfaces there).
> - **Soluun Stabilized** — mark when the party uses an action to stabilize Soluun; read by **Nihiloor and the Pool** (escape imposes mechanical cost if the party carries him).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> The party is at X25. The Spawning Pool is through the next door. This event awards no Milestone Point.

## Overview

The party infiltrates Xanathar's Lair through one of three entry routes, navigates five areas to the Spawning Pool, and decides whether to rescue an unconscious drow prisoner along the way.

## Summary

We infiltrated Xanathar's Lair through our available entry route and moved through five areas to the Spawning Pool. The Watched Hall had a beholder zombie and gas spores that we had to deal with without triggering both at once. Nihiloor was in X24, or it wasn't — and either way, there was a drow in the corner of the Extraction Chamber, unconscious and alive, claiming he was Bregan D'aerthe. We stabilized him or we didn't. The choice would cost us something in the next room.
