# Infiltration and the Lair

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event begins when the party leaves Blackstaff Tower for Xanathar's Lair. In this Event:
>
> - Three entry routes exist; which are available depends on prior arc and mission work.
> - The navigation route to the Spawning Pool runs through five named areas: X1, X2, the X22 corridor, X24, and X25.
> - Each named area has a 1-in-10 random encounter chance; roll d4 to determine the encounter.
> - Nihiloor is present in X24 50% of the time; if present, it does not want to fight and moves toward the exit from round one.
> - X24 also contains a drow prisoner — Soluun Xibrindas — unconscious in the corner.
> - This Event ends when the party reaches X25 (Food for Thought), adjacent to the Spawning Pool. Proceed to the **Nihiloor and the Pool** Event.

**Background (DM only)**

This is the same lair the party passed through in **Finding Floon** — the Xanathar sewer hideout has a connection deeper into the lair structure. **Nihiloor** is a mind flayer managing intellect devourer production. It is not suicidal. Its priority in any confrontation is escape, and it has been doing this long enough to have an escape route.

**Meloon Wardragon**, if restored in Mission 3, can provide tactical intelligence before the party departs. The devourer that occupied him had access to his Force Grey memories, which included two visits to Xanathar's Lair. Meloon's recall is fragmentary — the devourer suppressed his own conscious memory — but the layout of X1 through X24 is familiar enough that Meloon can sketch a rough map and name the beholder zombie in X2. If Meloon is available, the party has Advantage on the first Perception check they make inside the lair.

**Soluun Xibrindas** in X24 is a drow who has been claiming Bregan D'aerthe affiliation to stay alive. The Guild believes it is holding a BD spy. The affiliation is forged. If the party has encountered Soluun during Doom Raiders Mission 1 (The Dockside Killer), they recognize him. He owes the party a personal debt if they rescue him — whether Jarlaxle's organization extends that debt officially depends on the party's standing with Bregan D'aerthe and what Jarlaxle considers useful.

### The Briefing

Vajra has the potions ready. She places them on the desk, one per character — *potion of psychic resistance* — and adds a spare *potion of water breathing* for the entry route that might need it. Then:

> > "I will cover the cost of any *raise dead* spells that become necessary."

She says it without inflection. There is a short silence.

> > "The pool is a circular basin of brine, eight feet across, four feet deep, slightly luminescent. Three devourers are developing in the fluid. The pool and all three devourers must be destroyed. Nihiloor, if you find it, is secondary. The pool is the mission."

### Getting In

Three routes exist. Which are available depends on prior work:

**The Sewer Route** — Available if the party has the sewer access point from Lords' Alliance Mission 1's aftermath (Herath's note) or has any intelligence on the sewer hideout from **Finding Floon**. Enter through the Orb Confectioners' cellar using the passphrase "eight small eyes / around an ocular cake," through the sewer hideout, and into the lair's lower areas.

**The Gathering Point Route** — Available if the party has any Xanathar Guild passphrase or contact gathered during the **Finding Floon** and **Trollskull Alley** investigations, OG Mission 1 (Pell's Field Ward intelligence), or Guild street knowledge from prior missions. Access is through one of the Terasse Estate gathering points; the party is escorted blind to the lair's entry hall.

**The Bregan D'aerthe Route** — Available if the party has established a relationship with Jarlaxle's organization (BD missions, the Mission 2 calling card, or Sea Maidens Faire reconnaissance). Bregan D'aerthe has an underground passage to X1. Three **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) checks** to follow the guildsign without losing the thread.

A party that has none of these must find entry through scouting and improvisation. Give them the sewer approach as a fallback — Herath's note from Lords' Alliance Mission 1 covers this, and if the party is at this mission level, they have had enough city time to have found it.

### The Navigation Route

The route to X26 runs through five named areas. At each named area, roll 1d10: on a 10, a random encounter occurs. Roll d4 for the encounter:

| d4 | Encounter |
|----|-----------|
| 1 | **Flutterfoot Zipswiggle** (goblin; skittish; can be bribed with food to look the other way) |
| 2 | **Ahmaergo** (duergar enforcer; conducting inspection; **DC 15 Deception** to pass as authorized) |
| 3 | **Nar'l Xibrindas** with his grell bodyguard (hostile if not convinced otherwise) |
| 4 | **Ott Steeltoes** (confused and suspicious, not immediately violent) |

**X1 — Entry:** The lair's first area. Use the location journal for Xanathar's Sewer Hideout as the area reference.

**X2 — Watched Hall:** A beholder zombie + gas spores. The zombie responds to movement; gas spores respond to magic. The party can trigger one or the other, but not both, before the other reacts — choose which threat to engage first.

**X22 Corridor:** A connecting passage. No base inhabitants. The random encounter check applies.

**X24 — Extraction Chamber:** Nihiloor is present here 50% of the time. If present, it detects the party on approach (passive Perception 18) and does not immediately attack — it moves toward the alternate exit in round one.

A drow is unconscious in the corner: **Soluun Xibrindas**, in poor condition, alive. Stabilizing him takes one action. He cannot be moved quickly without assistance; extracting him on the way back is more practical than on the way in.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### If Nihiloor Is Present in X24
> Nihiloor does not want to fight a prepared party. From round one it moves toward the exit — *Mind Blast* (60-foot Cone, **DC 15 Intelligence**; failure: 4d8+4 psychic + Stunned until end of next turn) only if the party blocks all exits. *Dominate Monster* if cornered. Its goal is to leave, not to win. The party's goal is the Spawning Pool, not Nihiloor; blocking the exit to fight it costs time and resources they need for the pool.
>
> If Nihiloor escapes here, it is gone from the lair for this operation. It resurfaces in **Xanathar's Lair**. Parties who ran it off here will recognize it there.
>
> If Nihiloor is destroyed or cornered here, it is gone entirely. This affects **Xanathar's Lair**: Xanathar's personal retinue has a vacancy, and his mind-control network has lost its operational intelligence in the city.

**X25 — Food for Thought:** The room adjacent to the Spawning Pool. Whatever is here when the party arrives must be dealt with before entering X26. Proceed to the **Nihiloor and the Pool** Event.

## Overview

The party infiltrates Xanathar's Lair through one of three routes and navigates to the Spawning Pool, passing through active areas and a potential encounter with Nihiloor along the way.

## Read Aloud

> The potions are on Vajra's desk when you arrive — one each, labeled in her hand. She places the last one down and straightens.
>
> > "The pool is eight feet across, four feet deep, slightly luminescent. Three devourers developing in the fluid. Destroy the pool and all three. Come back." She pauses. "I will cover the cost of any *raise dead* spells that become necessary."
>
> The lair is active. The corridor through X22 is cold and quiet, and the silence has the quality of something that knows you are there.

## Summary

The party infiltrated Xanathar's Lair using one of three available entry routes and navigated toward the Spawning Pool. They passed through the Watched Hall, encountered at least one complication in the navigation, and reached the Extraction Chamber — where a drow prisoner lay unconscious and Nihiloor may or may not have been waiting.
