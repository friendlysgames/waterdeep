# The Brief

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when a Force Grey member who marked **M3 Complete** prepares for **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted), and it ends when the party leaves Blackstaff Tower for the lair. Play it in place of Vajra's lair consultation in Scene 1 of that quest. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Vajra's *Sending* and take her brief at the standing desk.
> - Learn what she knows about the pool in Nihiloor's wing, and what she does not.
> - Collect potions and holy water, and hear that she will pay for any *raise dead*.
> - Hear what Meloon remembers of the wing, if **Meloon Restored** is marked.
> - Learn how much Nihiloor already knows about them.
>
> Only Force Grey members attend the brief. Companions wait in the street and can help with everything after it. Vajra's panopticus advice in the lair quest is separate from this brief, and this brief gives nothing for the sensors.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Nihiloor's wing is X23 to X26 of the lair. There is no separate route, because the party goes to the wing while it runs **Xanathar's Lair**.
> - The wing holds a pool of brine in X25, placement records in X24 and a captive in X25, all described in **Nihiloor's Wing**.
> - Vajra learned of the pool from the one report of a Force Grey agent inside the Guild, who has not answered since. She does not say so.
> - Destroying the pool stops new hosts. It does nothing for the hosts already walking the city, and the records name four of them.
> - Mirt's "three of ours" in **The Sleeping Asset** counts Harpers. Meloon and the hosts in the records are Force Grey's problem and are separate from that count.
> - Until **Manshoon Named** is marked, nobody in this Event names Manshoon. No Force Grey speaker names Jarlaxle or Bregan D'aerthe.

### The Brief

The *Sending* reaches every eligible member while the party is preparing for the lair. Vajra receives them that evening, and companions wait in the street.

> [!readaloud]
>
> You are halfway through a meal when a dry, flat voice arrives behind your eyes. It gives no greeting, it moves fast, and nobody near you hears a word of it.
>
> > "Nihiloor breeds devourers inside Xanathar's Lair, and you are going there. Come to Blackstaff Tower first, this evening. Members only. Companions wait in the street."
>
> The voice stops as quickly as it began, and you have room to answer in a few words if you want to.

The *Sending* is exactly 25 words, and a member can answer in up to 25 words, though Vajra does not reply.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The street door of Blackstaff Tower opens before anyone knocks, and the stair beyond climbs past more floors than the outside shows. Vajra Safahr stands at the tall desk at the top with her sleeves pushed back. Small bottles and a rack of vials stand in rows beside her ledger, and there is no chair for visitors.
>
> > "Nihiloor keeps a pool of brine in a wing of that lair, and the Guild's own guards stay out of it. The basin is stone, eight feet across and four deep, and the brine glows a little. Three devourers are growing in it. I want it destroyed."

> [!social]**The Blackstaff at Her Desk**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep, who has laid her supplies out in rows and gives orders in full sentences.
>
> Vajra has not slept much, and she does not mention it. She waits for each answer before she goes on. She is blunt about the danger and does not soften it. When someone asks if she is all right, she says one word.
>
> Vajra is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The pool, its size and the three ways to end it.
> - Nihiloor, who runs when the cost rises.
> - What the potions and the holy water are for.
> - The records and the people Nihiloor keeps, which she suspects and cannot confirm.
>
> Vajra will not discuss how she knows about the pool, the contents of the Blackstaff, or her own part in Meloon's past.

> [!qna]**How do you know about the pool?**
>
> > "I won't tell you that, and you don't need it to do the job. I know where it is, I know what it does, and I know it is loud."

> [!qna]**How do we end it?**
>
> > "Pour four vials of holy water into the brine. Or break the stone basin, which takes a strong back. Or burn it with acid or fire for three rounds. Every one of those makes noise, so don't stay to admire it."

> [!qna]**What about Nihiloor?**
>
> > "He's a mind flayer who has been using this city as a fucking hatchery. He doesn't stand and die for his work, so the pool is the mission. If he runs, let him run."

> [!qna]**What about the people it already placed?**
>
> > "Destroying the pool stops the next ones, and it does nothing for the ones already walking around. Nihiloor keeps notes and he keeps people. If you find either, bring them out."

> [!qna]**What are the potions for?**
>
> > "Everything he does to you is psychic, and the potion halves the damage. It won't stop him stunning you. Drink it at the wing door and not before."

> [!qna]**What do we get?**
>
> > "I'll put what you did in writing, and I'll pay for anything that kills you."

Vajra says the last line about *raise dead* without inflection. If a member asks whether she is all right, she looks at the ink on her fingers and says, "Irrelevant."

### What Is on the Desk

Vajra hands out the supplies herself. Merris signs out the holy water, and he issues it only because her brief names it, as in **The Trouble with Meloon**.

> [!exploration]**The Supplies**
>
> - **Potions of resistance:** one *potion of resistance* (psychic) for each character who goes below, member or companion. It lasts 1 hour, so drink it at the wing door.
> - **Holy water:** four vials of holy water, signed out to the member who holds the brief. Each is worth 25 gp.
> - **Water breathing:** one spare *potion of water breathing*, for a stretch of the sewer approach that has flooded. It lasts 24 hours.
> - ***Raise dead*:** Vajra covers the cost of any *raise dead* the lair makes necessary, for a character who dies below.
>
> The potions are part of why **Nihiloor's Wing** uses its Hard roster by default.

If **Courier Identified** is marked, Vajra adds one more thing and reads or paraphrases the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra sets a small leaden disk on the desk with a beholder's eye stamped on its face. She slides it across with one finger.
>
> > "Merris cast that from a wax impression of Dobb Ketterly's courier disk while he slept off a pint in the Dock Ward. It's a copy, and it shows nothing but a pass. Whoever carries it walks in ahead of the others."

The disk is a plain lead copy and carries no magic. At X2 in the lair quest, at the Suspicious tier, it passes the Guild pass check. In the wing, the thrall in X23 treats the bearer as the courier and stays asleep while the bearer walks ahead alone. Anyone else who crosses X23 with the bearer wakes the thrall.

### What Vajra Has Heard

Vajra learned what happened in **The Trouble with Meloon** from the party's own report. Read each case that applies.

If **Nihiloor Identified Party** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra turns a page in her ledger without looking at it, and her pen stops above the line.
>
> > "You sat at his table on the fifth day, and the thing was still in him on the tenth. The courier carried your faces to Nihiloor, so he knows who is coming. Expect him to be waiting."

If **Devourer Escaped** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra caps her ink and looks at whichever of you held Meloon on the stool.
>
> > "The one that rode him got away, and it went home. I'd wager it's on Nihiloor's shoulder now. It knows what you look like."

If neither is marked, Vajra says only that Nihiloor "may know your faces and may not, and I wouldn't bet on the second."

If **Meloon Lost** is marked, Azuredge leans against the wall behind her desk with a cloth draped over the blade. No one is invited to look at it. Vajra does not mention him unless a member does, and then she says Meloon "was taken into that lair twice, and I'd like it burned for that."

### Meloon at the Tower

If **Meloon Restored** is marked, or neither Meloon outcome is marked and Vajra has since taken him at the Tower, Meloon waits in the guest room on the stair. Vajra lets the members see him before they go. He is back on his feet and wants to come, and she says no.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Meloon sits on the edge of a narrow bed in the Tower's guest room with Azuredge across his knees and both boots on. He looks up when you come in, and his big voice is rough from disuse.
>
> > "Fuck me, you're going down there. Sit, and let me tell you what I saw before you go, because I walked it twice and I couldn't stop my own feet."

> [!social]**The Man Who Walked It Twice**
>
> Meloon Wardragon (Neutral Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a barrel-chested Force Grey veteran who is loud and furious and has nowhere to put it.
>
> Meloon swears constantly, and he is sharper than he was a tenday ago. He remembers the lair as a series of doors he was walked through without choosing to. He saw the wing only from the door, and he saw Nihiloor only twice.
>
> Meloon is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The route to the wing door, which he describes in order.
> - The guards, who stood in every room like furniture.
> - The thing on Nihiloor's shoulder.
> - Whether he can come, which Vajra has already answered.

> [!qna]**The route?**
>
> > "Past the arena, then a plain door with nobody guarding it. The Guild stays out of that wing. Men ask to be moved out of it, and they don't say why."

> [!qna]**The guards?**
>
> > "One in every room, standing still with a face like a wet plate. They don't move until Nihiloor does, or the thing on his shoulder, or an alarm. Walk past them quietly."

> [!qna]**What's on his shoulder?**
>
> > "A devourer, like the one that wore me. He pets it like a cat. Hit it first if you can, because I don't think he'd leave it."

> [!qna]**Can you come?**
>
> > "Vajra says no, and she's fucking right, because I still can't hold a sword for long. If you see that thing, hit it once for me."

Meloon's account gives the party Advantage on the first Wisdom (Perception) or Dexterity (Stealth) check made at the wing door. If **Devourer Escaped** is also marked, he adds that the one on Nihiloor's shoulder is probably the one that wore him, and that it will want the party.

### Going Below

The party now carries out the lair quest. When it reaches the door of the wing at X23, play **Nihiloor's Wing**. Nothing in this Event changes where the party enters, how it crosses the lair or what the panopticus does.

### If Xanathar's Lair Has Already Run

If **M3 Complete** is marked after the party has finished the lair, this Event is a debrief. Nobody gets the brief, the potions or Meloon's account. The *Sending* arrives after the party's lair debrief, and Vajra takes the report in the same sitting as the one the lair quest already sets out.

> [!readaloud]
>
> You are in the middle of something ordinary when a dry, flat voice arrives behind your eyes. It gives no greeting, it moves fast, and nobody near you hears a word of it.
>
> > "Xanathar's Lair is done. Report to Blackstaff Tower. Members only. Tell me what Nihiloor kept in his wing, and what you carried out of it."
>
> The voice stops as quickly as it began, and you have room to answer in a few words if you want to.

The *Sending* is exactly 25 words. At the Tower, ask what the party did in X23 to X26 and read the row that fits.

> [!gamemaster]**Reading the Wing**
>
> - **The party never entered the wing:** Mark nothing. Vajra says "Force Grey will handle it." No Renown is awarded. The pool stands as far as the campaign is concerned, so **The Legate's Eyes** finds new hosts arriving.
> - **The party brought the lair down with the smokepowder:** Mark **Nihiloor Fled** only. The wing is rubble, Nihiloor escaped with his projects intact, and Vajra says the factory has moved below the city. No Renown is awarded.
> - **The party entered the wing:** Mark the outcomes from what happened there, using the conditions in **Nihiloor's Wing**. **Nihiloor Fled** is marked whenever Nihiloor left the wing. Award the Mission Renown from that Event, and play **Vajra's Debrief** from it.

A party that played the wing in the lair and returns to the Tower sits down with Vajra now, and **Vajra's Debrief** reads the outcomes the GM marked.

## Overview

Vajra briefs the Force Grey members at the Tower before they enter Xanathar's Lair. She tells them about a pool of brine in Nihiloor's wing and sends them to destroy it.

## Summary

We prepared for Xanathar's Lair and Vajra called us to the Tower first. She told us about a pool in Nihiloor's wing where devourers grow, handed out potions and holy water, and said she would pay for any *raise dead*.
