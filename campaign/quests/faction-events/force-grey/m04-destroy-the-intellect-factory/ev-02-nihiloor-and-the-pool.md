# Nihiloor and the Pool

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat Event begins when the party enters X26 — the Spawning Pool. In this Event:
>
> - The pool is a circular basin of brine, eight feet across and four feet deep, slightly luminescent.
> - Three developing intellect devourers are in the fluid; they are not yet combat-capable but must be destroyed.
> - Three destruction methods exist, each with different noise profiles and timing consequences.
> - If Nihiloor did not encounter the party in X24, it is not present here; the pool can be destroyed without confronting it.
> - Escape after destruction is the final challenge — the method used determines how quickly enforcers arrive.

**Background (DM only)**

Area X26 is a dedicated production space. The pool is what Nihiloor built this operation around: a sustainable source of intellect devourers for placement across Waterdeep. Destroying it removes Nihiloor's production capacity entirely. The three developing devourers die with the pool; they are not yet capable of implantation.

The room itself has no occupants beyond the pool unless Nihiloor is present (if it did not appear in X24 and the DM determined it was here rather than there — decide Nihiloor's location before the session, not mid-encounter). If Nihiloor is here, it behaves as described in the Infiltration and the Lair Event: escape priority, *Mind Blast* only if blocked.

### The Spawning Pool

The pool sits in the center of X26. The brine is viscous, slightly warm, and luminescent with a dim blue-white light. The three devourers in it are visible as shapes below the surface — brain-sized, still, developing. They register as aberrant presences under *detect evil and good*.

Three methods destroy the pool:

**Holy water:** Four vials emptied into the brine cause an immediate violent reaction. The devourers die, the pool destabilizes, and the basin goes inert. The reaction produces a sound like a distant scream audible throughout the lair. **Three minutes before enforcers arrive.**

**Physical destruction:** **DC 18 Strength** to break the stone basin. Loud. Dust in the air. **Three minutes before enforcers arrive.**

**Sustained elemental damage:** 2d6 acid or fire damage per round for three rounds destroys the contents and ruins the pool's function. Quieter, but requires sustained exposure in a room that may not be empty. If Nihiloor is still present, each round of damage is a round it is not escaping. **Five minutes before enforcers arrive**, if the party starts moving before the first enforcer completes the route.

### Escape

Whatever method was used, the party needs to be out of the lair before the enforcers arrive or before the alert reaches the upper corridors.

**Route out:** The way they came, or through Area X21 if they identified an emergency exit during navigation. Moving through an alerted lair at speed is the final challenge of this mission.

The party is moving through a space that now knows something happened. Each area they cross requires either speed (Dash action available) or concealment (Stealth check against the responding enforcers' passive Perception of 12). Splitting the party for distraction is possible and viable — two characters drawing enforcer attention in one direction while two escape with the mission objective complete.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### The Drow Prisoner
> If the party stabilized Soluun Xibrindas in X24 and wants to bring him out, this is the moment that decision costs something. He is unconscious, weighs approximately 140 pounds, and requires someone to carry or drag him. This imposes either Disadvantage on Athletics checks during the escape sequence or the loss of one character's Dash option. The party chose; they carry the consequence.
>
> **Soluun Xibrindas**, when he eventually recovers, is a drow operating independently in Waterdeep under a forged Bregan D'aerthe identification token. The Guild believes it was holding a BD spy; Soluun has been claiming that affiliation to stay alive. If the party encountered him during Doom Raiders Mission 1, they recognize him. He owes the party a personal debt.

## Overview

The party reaches the Spawning Pool and must destroy it before the lair can respond.

## Read Aloud

> The basin sits in the center of the room, slightly warm, lit from within by a dim blue-white luminescence. Three shapes move slowly beneath the surface — brain-sized, not yet awake, not yet capable of doing what they will do if given more time. The smell is brine and something older.
>
> The pool is eight feet across. Four feet deep. This is what Nihiloor built. This is what has been producing the things that ended up behind the eyes of Meloon Wardragon and Orvyn Dall and a dozen others across this city.
>
> You have three minutes after the first sound.

## Summary

The party reached the Spawning Pool in X26 and destroyed it along with the three developing intellect devourers inside. They escaped the alerted lair. Nihiloor either fled during the mission or was destroyed. The Gray Hands graduated to full Force Grey status.
