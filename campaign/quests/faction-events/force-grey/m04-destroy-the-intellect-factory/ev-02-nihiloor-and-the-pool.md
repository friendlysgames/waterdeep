# Nihiloor's Wing

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat and Exploration Event begins when the party reaches the wing door at X23 during **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted), and it ends when Vajra has heard the party's report at the Tower. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Cross X23 and X24 past the enthralled guards and take the placement records.
> - Free the captive chained in X25.
> - End the Spawning Pool by holy water, by breaking the basin, or by acid or fire.
> - Face Nihiloor and his pet, and drive him out of the wing.
> - Beat the enforcers to the wing door, slip past them or fight them there.
> - Report to Vajra and receive her commendation.
>
> Only Force Grey members attend Vajra's debrief. Companions can help with everything before it.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Four things hold Nihiloor in the wing: the Pool, the records, the captive and his pet. The second one the party takes from him sets him running. He is never killed here, and **Nihiloor Fled** is always marked.
> - The wing is four rooms. X23 holds Nihiloor's files, X24 the placement records, X25 the Pool, the captive and twelve specimens, and X26 the Puppet device. The party never has to enter X26.
> - One enthralled **Tough** stands in each room. They wake only when Nihiloor, the pet or an alarm does. The pet rests on the rim of the Pool.
> - The wing wakes when a node falls, when a thrall is harmed, or when anything louder than speech sounds. Nihiloor then reaches the party's room at the end of the second round, and the thralls wake as he passes.
> - If **Nihiloor Identified Party** is marked, he is already in X25 beside the Pool and the thralls are awake. He speaks first, and he may use *Dominate Monster* on round 1.
> - If **Devourer Escaped** is marked, the pet is the devourer that rode Meloon. Its first Claw goes to a character who held Meloon in **The Trouble with Meloon**.
> - Use the Hard roster. Use the Standard roster if any member is at half hit points or fewer, or any character has gone down in the lair before X23.
> - The files in X23 and the Puppet device in X26 belong to **Xanathar's Lair**. This Event does not resolve them.

### The Wing Door

Nihiloor's wing is the only part of the lair the Guild avoids. Soldiers who draw a shift here ask to be moved within weeks, and nobody says why.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The door at the end of the corridor is plain oak, unlocked, with a handle worn smooth from one hand. Beyond it a long room holds shelves of loose papers and a table laid out with folders, and a Guild soldier stands against the far wall with his arms at his sides. A pressure sits behind your eyes, like the start of a headache, and he does not turn his head as you come in.

Each character who goes below drinks a *potion of resistance* (psychic) here. If Meloon's account gave Advantage, it applies to the first Wisdom (Perception) or Dexterity (Stealth) check made at this door.

> [!exploration]**Crossing X23**
>
> The thrall in X23 is an enthralled Guild soldier, and it wakes only for Nihiloor, the pet or an alarm. A character who walks past it quietly needs no check.
>
> - **A character carries the courier's disk:** The thrall stays asleep as long as the bearer walks in front, alone. If anyone else crosses with the bearer, the thrall wakes.
> - **A character searches the files:** The folders hold Nihiloor's research on the four villain factions. Run the entry for X23 in **Xanathar's Lair**, and do not read the Manshoon section aloud as Force Grey knowledge until **Manshoon Named** is marked.
> - **A character attacks the thrall:** The wing wakes. Use the rule in **What Is Actually True**.

### The Placement Records

X24 is a narrow room with a locked cabinet. The thrall in X24 stands beside it with a key on a cord at his belt.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The room smells of ink and old brine. A cabinet with glass doors stands against one wall, and behind the glass lie six thick folders tied in grey ribbon. A Guild soldier stands beside it with a key hanging from a cord at his belt, and his eyes stay fixed on the opposite wall.

> [!exploration]**Taking the Records**
>
> The cabinet is locked, and taking the folders is the first of the four nodes.
>
> - A character who picks the lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check opens it without a sound.
> - A character who breaks the glass makes a loud noise, which wakes the wing. A character who takes the key from the thrall's belt wakes him.
> - The folders weigh about 20 pounds. Gathering them takes one action.
> - Four folders have a name, a post, a date and notes. Two have a post and a blank where the name goes.

A character who opens the folders and reads them finds the following.

> [!readaloud]
>
> > Dall, Orvyn. Appeals clerk, Trades Ward district magistracy. Placed. Integration: complete. Reports by courier at the end of each tenday. Cooperative, unremarkable, will not be missed.

> [!gamemaster]**The Other Folders**
>
> The other three placed hosts are a Dock Ward Watch quartermaster's runner, a customs-house tally clerk and a harbormaster's messenger. The two pending folders name a Castle Ward captain's secretary and a magistrate's reader. Their names stay in the folders, and later events use them only if they choose. If **Placement Records Taken** is marked, Vajra keeps them.

### The Pool and the Captive

X25 is the center of the wing. It holds the Spawning Pool, twelve specimens along the wall, the captive in a chair and the pet.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The room is wide and warm, lit from below by a dim blue-white glow. A round stone basin sits in the middle with brine in it, eight feet across, and three pale shapes the size of a fist move slowly beneath the surface. Along the walls stand racks of glass-fronted tanks, each holding a still, small body wired to the next. A drow in a stone chair with iron shackles on his wrists and ankles lifts his head, and a small grey thing on the basin's rim turns toward you.

> [!gamemaster]**The Pool**
>
> The Spawning Pool is a stone basin eight feet across and four feet deep. The three spawn inside are Tiny, harmless and never leave the brine. They die within 1 round of the Pool's end. *Detect Evil and Good* shows aberrant presences in the brine. The twelve specimens are not creatures to fight, and the notes beside them carry the rule from **Xanathar's Lair**: a character who reads them fully makes a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or gains 1 level of Exhaustion.
>
> The Pool is destroyed when one of the methods below finishes. A method interrupted partway leaves the Pool damaged and the spawn alive. Hosts the pool has already produced are not affected.

> [!exploration]**Ending the Pool**
>
> - **Holy water:** Four vials emptied into the brine. The reaction is violent and kills the spawn, and a sound like a distant scream carries through the lair. The enforcers arrive at X23 3 minutes after the scream.
> - **The basin:** A successful DC 18 Strength (Athletics) check breaks the stone. A character can take the Help action to give the roller Advantage. It is loud, with dust in the air, and the enforcers arrive 3 minutes after the first blow.
> - **Acid or fire:** At least 7 acid or fire damage into the brine each round for three rounds, from flasks, a spell or any other source. No attack roll is needed. It is quiet, and the enforcers arrive 5 minutes after the first round.
>
> The enforcers arrive at the earlier of two times: the one above, counted from the first sound, or 3 minutes after Nihiloor passes the wing door and wakes the Guild by telepathy.

> [!social]**The Captive in the Chair**
>
> Zaibon Kyszalt (Neutral Evil, Drow, he/him) :: a House Auvryndar drow taken in Skullport, drugged and Incapacitated, who does not know what is planned for him.
>
> Zaibon speaks in slow, formal fragments through the drug. He is haughty by habit and afraid underneath. He believes the party works for the Guild until it unlocks the shackles. He has not been told what Nihiloor intends.
>
> Zaibon is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who took him, which he blames on the Guild and not on House Freth.
> - What he heard in the chair, which is that the thing asked him questions in his head.
> - His house's plans for Skullport, which he mentions to bargain and then regrets.
>
> Zaibon will not discuss his superior's whereabouts.

> [!qna]**Who are you?**
>
> > "Zaibon Kyszalt, of House Auvryndar. You are not Guild. They do not unlock chairs."

> [!qna]**What did it do to you?**
>
> > "It asked me questions without a voice. It asked about my house and about Skullport, and then it stopped asking, which frightened me more."

> [!qna]**Where will you go?**
>
> > "Down, if the tunnels let me. If you will not take me there, put me on a road. House Auvryndar does not forget a debt."

> [!gamemaster]**If Soluun Expelled Is Marked**
>
> Soluun Xibrindas sits in the chair instead of Zaibon. The Guild's watchers took him off the Dock Ward quay after the captain put him ashore, and Nihiloor wants a host who knows how the company works. Soluun wears a silver disc on a cord and has no weapons. He speaks to the party in short, hard sentences and says nothing of Jarlaxle or Bregan D'aerthe. If any character is an elf or half-elf, he spits and says "Surface-blood" before anything else.
>
> A party that knows him from **The Dockside Killer** may leave him in the chair. If the party frees him, he wants nothing from Force Grey and stays out of sight at the Tower until he can walk. Mark **Captive Freed** only if he leaves the lair alive.

> [!exploration]**Freeing the Captive**
>
> The chair has four shackles. Nihiloor carries the key and keeps it when he runs.
>
> - A character with Thieves' Tools who makes a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check opens one shackle. Four shackles take four checks, and two characters can work at once.
> - A character without tools who breaks one shackle with a successful DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check makes a loud noise, which wakes the wing.
> - The captive is Incapacitated for an hour and must be carried. The carrier's Speed is halved.
> - The captive's freedom is the second node if the records or the pet have already fallen. The pet does not occupy him.

### Nihiloor

By default Nihiloor is in X26 at the Puppet device, with the pet on the Pool's rim. When the wing wakes, he comes into X25 with the thralls behind him.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A tall, pale shape glides in from the dark doorway on the far side of the room, its head a mass of slow tentacles, and a cold, clear voice speaks inside your head. It steps between you and the basin without hurrying, and the small thing on the rim hops onto its shoulder.
>
> > "You are frightened. That is appropriate. Your minds are adequate, and I will take them one at a time."

If **Nihiloor Identified Party** is marked, he is already beside the Pool when the party enters, and he says the following instead.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A mind flayer stands beside the basin with a small grey devourer on its shoulder, and it does not turn as you step through the door. The cold voice arrives behind your eyes before you have taken a second step.
>
> > "I have your faces from the one who sat at the large man's table. Fascinating that you came. You will be of use."

> [!hazard]**Nihiloor and His Children**
>
> Nihiloor uses the boss block in the **Force Grey Mechanics Reference**, section 4.1. The pet is the **Occupying Devourer** from section 5.1 of the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**, and the thralls are **Tough**. Use the row for the party's size.
>
> - **Three characters:** Hard, Nihiloor and the pet. Standard, Nihiloor alone, with the pet joining on round 3.
> - **Four characters:** Hard, Nihiloor, the pet and two thralls. Standard, Nihiloor and the pet.
> - **Five characters:** Hard, Nihiloor, the pet and four thralls. Standard, Nihiloor, the pet and two thralls.
>
> Thralls join in this order: X25, X26, X24, X23. Thralls not in the roster stay still.
>
> #### Nihiloor's Tactics
> To begin, Nihiloor steps between the party and whichever of the four things they reach for first. He never uses Withdraw while two or more things remain. The players can see this, and it is the Tell.
>
> During the first phase, Nihiloor:
> - Uses Tentacles on whoever touches a node, and *Mind Blast* when two or more characters stand in the cone.
> - Uses *Dominate Monster* only on the character standing between him and the pet or a node, never on round 1 unless **Nihiloor Identified Party** is marked.
> - Uses *Interpose Thrall* when a thrall stands within 10 feet, and *Pull the Leash* when a creature damages him.
>
> The first phase ends when it reaches 0 hit points, when the second node falls, or when three thralls are down. The second phase begins. Nihiloor moves toward the wing door each turn, using Withdraw, and does the following on his way:
> - Orders the pet to hold the room. The pet fights to the death and does not follow.
> - Leaves the nearest thrall in each doorway to fight until he is 120 feet away.
> - Uses *Mind Blast* and Tentacles only on a creature that blocks the way.
> - Uses *Dominate Monster* on one character who chases him, so that character blocks the others for a round.
> - Does not stop to burn the records, the Pool or the notes.
> - Wakes the Guild by telepathy at the wing door, which starts the enforcers' clock, and enters the tunnel toward X4 about five rounds later.
>
> If the party blocks every route or reduces him to 0 hit points in the second phase, he casts *Plane Shift* and is gone. Last Exit is a plot rule: he cannot die here. The thralls surrender or flee once he is far enough away.
>
> #### The Pet's Tactics
> To begin, the pet hops to Nihiloor's shoulder or stays on the rim. It uses Claw and Devour Intellect on the nearest character, and it never uses Occupy Body, because Nihiloor's devourers do not take hosts at random. It fights to the death.
>
> The fight ends when Nihiloor leaves the wing or the party stops fighting. A party can end it without beating him by taking a second node quickly, because he runs when it falls.

After the second node falls, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The mind flayer's head tilts toward the door, and the voice arrives in your skull as calm as before.
>
> > "The pet will hold this room. The programme can be rebuilt elsewhere. You will not be of use today."
>
> It starts toward the far door, and the guard in the doorway steps into its path.

> [!gamemaster]**The Puppet Device**
>
> X26 holds the psionic relay that keeps Xanathar under *Dominate Monster*, with Nihiloor's notes beside it. This Event does not resolve it. If the party reaches X26, run the entry for it in **Xanathar's Lair**. If the party mentions the device to Vajra, she writes it down and says that it belongs to the lair operation.

### The Enforcers

The Guild stays out of the wing by habit, and habit lasts until a scream or Nihiloor's call. After that, a squad comes from the arena side to the wing door.

> [!hazard]**The Guild Squad**
>
> The enforcers are a squad of Guild soldiers who do not know Nihiloor's secrets. Use the row for the party's size.
>
> - **Three characters:** two **Tough**.
> - **Four characters:** one **Tough Boss** and two **Tough**.
> - **Five characters:** one **Tough Boss** and four **Tough**.
>
> #### The Squad's Tactics
> To begin, the squad forces the wing door at X23 and calls for the thrall inside to stand aside.
>
> During combat, the squad:
> - Holds the doorway and fights whoever is nearest the door.
> - Fires crossbows from behind the Tough Boss, if one is present.
>
> The squad flees if the Tough Boss falls or if half of it is down. A party that cannot beat them can hide: a successful DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) group check in X24, behind the cabinet and the thrall, lets the squad pass into X25 and the party slip out of the door behind it. A failure starts the fight at the door.
>
> The party's way back out of the lair belongs to **Xanathar's Lair**.

### Vajra's Debrief

Vajra takes the report at the standing desk, in the same sitting as her lair debrief if the quest has finished. Companions wait in the street. Mark the outcomes now: **Pool Destroyed** if a method finished, **Placement Records Taken** if the folders are on her desk, **Captive Freed** if the captive left the lair alive, and **Nihiloor Fled**.

If **Pool Destroyed** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra reads the account without looking up, and her pen does not move. When she reaches the last line, she sets it down on the ledger.
>
> > "The pool is gone, so there will be no more hatching from it. That's the work I sent you for. Well done."

If **Pool Destroyed** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra's pen stops on the line about the basin. She does not raise her voice.
>
> > "Then it's still hatching, and he'll place more of them. I'll plan for that. Tell me exactly what stopped you."

If **Placement Records Taken** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra unties the grey ribbon on the top folder and reads the first page, then the next. Her mouth tightens at the third.
>
> > "Shit. Four placed, and two more waiting for a name. I'll read every page tonight and send for you when I know who to go after first."

If **Captive Freed** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra looks at the drow the healer has helped into the room and then at you.
>
> > "He'll be fed and kept in a locked room until he can talk. I won't hold a man the Guild already did. When he can walk, he walks."

Nihiloor always fled, so read the following in every debrief.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra sets the report aside and takes out a clean sheet of Tower letterhead. She writes one line, signs it, and slides a copy to each of you across the desk.
>
> > "He ran, so he'll build again somewhere else. That is why the records and the pool mattered. This is a commendation for what you did in that wing. It is not a promotion, because the Tower keeps rank in a ledger and not in a letter."

The commendation is a sheet of Tower letterhead signed in Vajra's hand, naming the member and the wing. It is story only and changes no rank.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Portal is loud and warm, and Meloon stands on a bench near the fire with a mug raised, his voice carrying over the lute. Durnan works the bar with the usual rag over his shoulder, and Bonnie carries stews through the crowd. When you reach the bar, Durnan pushes four mugs across without a word.
>
> > "Put your coin away. House pays."
>
> Meloon laughs, and starts a story about a Force Grey operation that went wrong.

If **Meloon Lost** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Portal is quiet when you come in. Durnan sets a full cup at the end of the bar, in front of an empty stool by the fire, and wipes the counter beside it. He looks at you once and goes back to polishing the mug.
>
> > "On the house."

### Renown Opportunities

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Force Grey member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Vajra, whether the pool fell or not. Companions gain none. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:
>
> - **+1 Renown:** **Pool Destroyed** is marked, and all three spawn died with it.
> - **+1 Renown:** **Captive Freed** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** **Placement Records Taken** is marked.
>
> Each bonus can be earned only once. In the variant where the lair already ran, award this Renown only to members whose party entered the wing.

### Aftermath

The Guild's enforcers search the wing and find the Pool ruined, the records gone and the thralls standing in the doorways, and the ones taken alive remember only standing in a room. Nihiloor is gone from the lair, and **Xanathar's Lair** decides where he turns up. If **Pool Destroyed** is not marked, new hosts keep arriving. A member who reaches Renown 10 here also qualifies for **Senior Griffon**.

### Concluding the Event

Vajra's debrief ends the mission. Record the four outcomes separately, because later events read each.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Pool Destroyed** — mark when a method finished and the Pool is inert; read by **The Legate's Eyes**, where no new hosts arrive, and **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted).
> - **Placement Records Taken** — mark when the folders are delivered to Vajra; read by **The Legate's Eyes**, where they are one of the leads to Orvyn Dall.
> - **Captive Freed** — mark when the captive leaves the lair alive; read by **Vajra's Debrief** in this Event, and by the Bregan D'aerthe missions if the captive was Soluun (unwired).
> - **Nihiloor Fled** — mark whenever Nihiloor leaves the wing, which is always; read by **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) and **The Full Picture**.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> **The Legate's Eyes** becomes available when an individual Force Grey member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level. This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

The party fights its way into Nihiloor's wing, ends the pool that breeds his devourers and decides what else to carry out. Nihiloor flees, and Vajra writes the party a commendation.

## Summary

We reached Nihiloor's wing in Xanathar's Lair and destroyed what we could. Nihiloor ran when we took the second thing from him, and Vajra gave us a commendation. Where did he go, and who are the people named in the folders?
