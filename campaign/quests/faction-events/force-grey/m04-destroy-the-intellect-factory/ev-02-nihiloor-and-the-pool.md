# Nihiloor and the Pool

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat Event begins when the party enters X26 — the Spawning Pool. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Observe the pool and the three developing devourers inside it.
> - Choose a destruction method, each with a different noise profile and enforcer clock.
> - Escape the alerted lair before the enforcers arrive, using speed, concealment, or a distraction.
> - Bring out Soluun Xibrindas if they stabilized him in X24, at the cost of one character's movement options during the escape.
> - Encounter Nihiloor here if it was not present in X24 and was determined to be in X26.
>
> #### Nihiloor's Presence
>
> If the party marked **Nihiloor Encountered in X24**, **Nihiloor Destroyed in X24**, or **Nihiloor Fled X24**, then Nihiloor is not in X26. The pool can be destroyed without confronting it. If none of those outcomes are marked, Nihiloor is here. It behaves as described in ev-01: escape priority, *Mind Blast* only if blocked. Decide its starting position in the room before the party enters.
>
> #### The Enforcer Clock
>
> The method the party uses to destroy the pool determines how quickly the lair responds:
> - **Holy water** (four vials): three minutes before enforcers arrive.
> - **Physical destruction** (DC 18 Strength): three minutes before enforcers arrive.
> - **Sustained elemental damage** (2d6 acid or fire per round for three rounds): five minutes if the party begins moving before the first enforcer completes the route.
>
> The escape begins the moment destruction is confirmed.

### The Spawning Pool

The door to X26 opens onto a room that smells of brine and something older than brine. The pool is at the center.

> [!readaloud]
> The basin sits in the center of the room, slightly warm to the air around it, lit from within by a dim blue-white luminescence that makes everything else in the room look dark by comparison. Three shapes move slowly beneath the surface — brain-sized, wet, not yet awake, not yet capable of doing what they will do if given more time. The smell is brine and something beneath the brine that has no clean name. The pool is eight feet across and four feet deep, and it is where Nihiloor built whatever ended up behind the eyes of Meloon Wardragon and a dozen others across this city.

> [!gamemaster]**The Developing Devourers**
>
> The three devourers in the brine are not yet combat-capable. They register as aberrant presences under *detect evil and good*. They die with the pool when it is destroyed. They cannot be targeted individually — they are developing organisms, not creatures with initiative or actions. Do not give them stat blocks or let them act independently. They are part of the pool objective, not a separate threat.
>
> Area X26 has no other occupants unless Nihiloor is present. If Nihiloor is here, determine its starting position — near the pool, near the exit, or at the far wall — before the party enters.

### Destroying the Pool

The party has three methods available. The choice is theirs, based on what they brought and what they are willing to risk.

**Holy water.** Four vials emptied into the brine cause an immediate violent reaction. The devourers die, the pool destabilizes, and the basin goes inert within thirty seconds. The reaction produces a sound like a distant scream that carries throughout the lair. **Three minutes before enforcers arrive.**

**Physical destruction.** Breaking the stone basin requires a **DC 18 Strength check**. The sound of stone breaking carries. **Three minutes before enforcers arrive.**

**Sustained elemental damage.** Two dice (2d6) of acid or fire damage per round for three consecutive rounds destroys the contents and ruins the pool's function. This is the quietest method, but it requires sustained exposure in a room that may not be empty. If Nihiloor is still present, each round of damage is a round it is not escaping. **Five minutes before enforcers arrive**, if the party starts moving before the first enforcer completes the route.

> [!gamemaster]**If Nihiloor Is Present**
>
> Run Nihiloor the same way as in X24: it moves toward the exit on round one. The only difference is that in X26, there is only one exit — the way the party came in. Nihiloor does not want to fight. It wants past the party. If the party holds the exit and begins the elemental damage method, every round Nihiloor is not leaving is a round the pool is being destroyed. Running this combat as a difficult geometry problem — Nihiloor needs past, the pool needs tending, the enforcers are coming — gives it more weight than a straightforward fight.
>
> Use the **Mind Flayer** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*).

### Escape

Once the pool is destroyed, the party needs to be out of the lair before the enforcers arrive or the alert reaches the upper corridors.

**Route out:** The way they came in, or through Area X21 if they identified an emergency exit during navigation. Moving through an alerted lair at speed is the final challenge.

> [!hazard]**Escaping the Lair**
>
> At the start of the escape, the lair knows something happened. The enforcer clock is running.
>
> Each named area on the return route — X25, X24, X22, X2, X1 — requires either speed or concealment. For each area, the party can choose: move at full speed (Dash action available, no check required, but this does not allow stopping or interaction), or move cautiously and make a **Stealth check** against the responding enforcers' passive Perception of 12.
>
> The battle ends when the party exits the lair. If the enforcers catch up before the party exits, they must fight or surrender one round before reaching the exit — their choice.
>
> Splitting the party for a distraction is viable: two characters drawing enforcer attention in one direction while two others escape with the mission complete. Each group resolves their path independently.

> [!gamemaster]**Soluun During the Escape**
>
> If the party marked **Soluun Stabilized** in ev-01 and wants to bring him out, the cost arrives now. Soluun is unconscious, approximately 140 pounds, and requires someone to carry or drag him. This imposes either Disadvantage on Athletics checks during the escape or the loss of one character's Dash option on each area of the route. The party chose to stabilize him. They carry the consequence.
>
> When Soluun eventually recovers, he is a drow operating independently in Waterdeep under a forged Bregan D'aerthe identification token. He owes the party a personal debt. For parties who encountered him during Doom Raiders Mission 1, this is the same man they fought, arrested, or let escape — now owed to them.

### Concluding the Event

The party exits the lair. Vajra receives the report.

> [!readaloud]
> Vajra is at the standing desk when you return, and she looks at each of you in turn before her gaze settles on the potions — the ones that were not used and the ones that were — and she waits until you have said everything you have to say. When you finish the report, she closes the notebook.
>
> > "Well done."
>
> Without elaborating, she takes out a written commission for each member of the party — the Blackstaff's mark at the top, a name on each one, and a line of standing: Force Grey, full status.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Pool Destroyed** — mark when the Spawning Pool and all three developing devourers are eliminated; read by **Xanathar's Lair** (Nihiloor's production capacity is gone) and Mission 5.
> - **Nihiloor Destroyed in X26** — mark when Nihiloor is brought to 0 hit points here; read by **Xanathar's Lair** (Nihiloor is gone from the campaign entirely).
> - **Nihiloor Fled X26** — mark when Nihiloor escapes; read by **Xanathar's Lair** (Nihiloor resurfaces there, weakened).
> - **Soluun Rescued** — mark when Soluun exits the lair alive, stabilized or conscious; read by any mission or scene where Soluun's debt is relevant.
> - **Gray Hands Promoted** — mark when Vajra issues the written commissions; read by Mission 5.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> **+2 Renown** if the Spawning Pool is destroyed and all three developing devourers are eliminated.
> **+1 Renown** if the drow prisoner exits the lair alive, stabilized or conscious.
>
> The Gray Hands have graduated to full Force Grey status. Each character receives a written commission.
>
> Nihiloor is either gone from the city or somewhere in it, depending on what happened here. If it escaped X24 and X26, it resurfaces in **Xanathar's Lair** in a weakened operational state. If it was destroyed at any point, it is gone — Xanathar's mind-control network has no remaining operational intelligence in Waterdeep.
>
> **Force Grey Mission 5 — The Legate's Eyes** becomes available when the party reaches Renown 10 and 6th level. This mission awards no Milestone Point.

## Overview

The party reaches the Spawning Pool in X26, destroys it and the three developing devourers inside, and escapes the alerted lair before the enforcers arrive.

## Summary

We entered X26 and found the pool — eight feet across, four feet deep, lit from underneath, with three devourers developing in the brine. We destroyed it. The escape was the last test: an alerted lair, an enforcer clock, and either everything we needed to carry out of there or just ourselves. Vajra gave us our commissions when we came back. Full Force Grey status. She said "well done" and closed the notebook.
