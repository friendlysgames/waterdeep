# Destroy the Intellect Factory: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Force Grey member reaches Renown 8, or when a member who marked **M3 Complete** prepares for **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted). It has no level gate. This mission is an objective inside the lair quest and not a separate raid. Companions can help with everything except Vajra's brief and debrief.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 5th-level characters.*
>
> Nihiloor uses the boss block in the **Force Grey Mechanics Reference**, section 4.1, with an **Occupying Devourer** as his pet and enthralled **Tough** guards. The same section sets the rosters for three, four and five characters. The party can finish the wing without ever beating him, because he leaves once two of the four things he guards are lost.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. The Milestone Points for **Xanathar's Lair** belong to that quest.

## Hook

Vajra's *Sending* finds each eligible member while the party is preparing for the lair and tells them to come to Blackstaff Tower first. At the desk she tells them what she knows about a pool of brine in Nihiloor's wing, hands out potions and holy water, and says she will pay for any *raise dead* the work makes necessary.

## Background

Nihiloor breeds Occupying Devourers in a stone basin in his wing of the lair, and he has used them to put hosts into the Watch and the city's offices. Meloon Wardragon was one of them, and the records in the wing list the others. A drow is strapped in a chair beside the basin, waiting for the next devourer.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> The wing is area X23 to X26 of **Xanathar's Lair**, and this mission adds three things to the rooms that quest already describes. The pool and the captive sit in X25 beside the twelve specimens, and the placement records lie in X24. Four things hold Nihiloor in the wing: the pool, the records, the captive and his pet. When the second one is lost he runs, and he always escapes. This mission never kills him.
>
> The default captive is Zaibon Kyszalt, a drow of House Auvryndar. If **Soluun Expelled** is marked, Soluun Xibrindas sits in the chair instead. Soluun's disownment is real in that case. He sold the Guild's divers the *Scarlet Marpenoth*'s berth, and once the deal was done the Guild seized him. Vajra does not say how she knows about the pool, and the true answer is a Force Grey agent inside the Guild who sent one report and has not answered since. Until **Manshoon Named** is marked, no Force Grey speaker names Manshoon, and nobody names Jarlaxle or Bregan D'aerthe to the players.

## The Brief

Vajra's *Sending* calls the members to the Tower, where she gives them the pool's size and what it does, three ways to end it, and the warning that hosts already placed in the city stay hosts. Meloon, if he was restored, tells the party what he remembers of the wing, and the party's record from **The Trouble with Meloon** decides what Nihiloor already knows about them.

## The Wing

At X23 the party meets the first of four enthralled guards, who wake only when Nihiloor, his pet or an alarm does. X24 holds the placement records, with Orvyn Dall's folio among four, and X25 holds the pool, the twelve specimens, the pet and the captive. A party that takes any one of these without a sound has about two rounds before Nihiloor arrives, because he feels the loss through his programme.

## The Pool

Holy water, a broken basin or three rounds of acid or fire will end it, and each makes enough noise to start a clock. The enforcers reach the wing door at X23 in three or five minutes, and the party must be out, hidden or ready to fight them there.

## Nihiloor

He stands between the party and whichever thing they reach for first, and he leaves when the second one falls. On his way out he sacrifices the pet, wakes the Guild at the wing door and enters the tunnel toward X4. The party can chase him, block him until he vanishes, or let him go.

## The Debrief

Vajra reads the report at her standing desk, with the Lair's debrief if the quest has finished. She writes a commendation for each member that records what they did and gives no rank. A restored Meloon buys the party a round at the Yawning Portal, and Durnan charges nothing.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Force Grey member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Vajra. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** the pool is destroyed and all three spawn die with it.
- **+1 Renown:** the captive leaves the lair alive.
- **+1 Renown:** the placement records are delivered to Vajra.

Companions who are not Force Grey members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**The Legate's Eyes** becomes available when an individual Force Grey member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level, and it reads **Pool Destroyed** and **Placement Records Taken**. A member who reaches Renown 10 here also qualifies for **Senior Griffon**, which reads **Meloon Restored** or **Meloon Lost**. **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted) reads **Pool Destroyed** and **Nihiloor Fled**, and **The Full Picture** reads **Nihiloor Fled**.

## Involved Characters

- **Vajra Safahr** (Force Grey): briefs and debriefs the members and pays for any *raise dead*.
- **Nihiloor** (Xanathar's Guild): the mind flayer who breeds the devourers, who always escapes.
- **Meloon Wardragon** (Force Grey): gives the party his memory of the wing if **Meloon Restored** is marked.
- **Zaibon Kyszalt** (House Auvryndar): the default captive, a drow taken in Skullport.
- **Soluun Xibrindas** (independent, formerly Bregan D'aerthe): the captive instead, only if **Soluun Expelled** is marked.
- **Merris** (Force Grey): the Tower's quartermaster, who signs out the holy water.
- **Durnan** (independent): pours at the Yawning Portal for the party's round.
- **Nihiloor's thralls and the enforcers** (Xanathar's Guild): four enthralled soldiers in the wing, and a squad that answers the alarm.

## Dangers & Enemies

Nihiloor fights in two phases and cannot be killed here. His pet is an **Occupying Devourer** that fights to the death and never takes a host. Up to four **Tough** guards fight for him until he is far enough away, and then they surrender or flee. The enforcers are **Tough** and a **Tough Boss**. The three spawn in the pool are harmless, and the twelve specimens are not creatures to fight.

## Overview

Vajra sends the Force Grey members into Nihiloor's wing of Xanathar's Lair to destroy the pool that breeds intellect devourers. They also have to decide what to take out of it besides the pool.
