# The Tenday Watch

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Social Event begins when Vajra's Sending assigns the surveillance. In this Event:
>
> - The party observes Meloon Wardragon at the Yawning Portal over a tenday.
> - Each named day surfaces one behavioral beat; the party can observe others between them.
> - The Day 3 beat — the morning ritual — is accessible from the gallery above his room before dawn.
> - The Day 5 conversation beat requires the party to approach Meloon directly; this is permitted.
> - The Day 7 beat produces multiple confirmation options for the possession.
> - On any of the ten days, addressing Azuredge directly by name during the morning ritual opens the Azuredge Speaks beat.
> - This Event ends when the tenday ends. Proceed to the **Azuredge Confrontation** Event.

**Background (DM only)**

**Meloon Wardragon** is a Force Grey veteran — large, cheerful, known for telling stories that have no business being this funny at this distance. He is none of these things now. An intellect devourer from Nihiloor's network occupied his skull approximately three tendays ago. It is using him as an observation post for Force Grey's operations: attending Yawning Portal meetings, noting who the party speaks to, filing reports through a dead drop in the Dock Ward.

**Azuredge**, his sentient greataxe, has been fighting the devourer's control since it arrived. It is losing the argument about whether Meloon should expose himself, but it has prevented several worse things from happening. It needs someone to notice.

Vajra cannot observe Meloon herself — he would recognize official Force Grey surveillance and change his behavior immediately. The party is her best option for unmarked observation.

### Day 1 — Arrival

**Durnan** acknowledges the party's presence without comment. He knows what they are there for; Vajra told him. **Meloon's** table has two empty chairs that people used to fill automatically. They don't now.

Meloon sits with his drink and faces the portal. His attention is on the well with a quality that is not idleness — the specific vigilance of something cataloguing everything that passes.

### Day 3 — The Morning Ritual

A character positioned in the gallery above his room before dawn watches the ritual: Meloon sits on the edge of the bed, Azuredge across his knees, and stares at it. His lips move. No sound. This continues for several minutes. Then he straps the axe on and leaves.

A **DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check** reveals: the axe and the man are in genuine conflict. The axe is pressing for something. Meloon's refusal has the quality not of willpower but of something suppressing the articulation of that willpower.

#### Azuredge Speaks

Azuredge reaches toward a lawful or divine-adjacent character. This can occur on any morning of the tenday when a character addresses the axe directly by name — not Meloon, the axe — during this ritual (accessible from the gallery, or if the party has earned a conversation with Meloon and directly asked to see it).

A **DC 18 Wisdom (Insight) check**, or a **DC 14 check** combined with directly asking the axe a question ("Are you trying to tell us something?"), causes the character to feel three things in sequence, with complete certainty, delivered as pure emotional impression:

*There is something wrong with him.* (Urgency.) *I cannot reach him.* (Frustration.) *You can.* (Hope.)

This transforms the surveillance mission into something the party was not assigned: can they actually do something about what they found?

### Day 5 — Conversation

If the party approaches Meloon in conversation, he warms slightly to adventurers and asks questions about their work with apparent genuine interest. A **DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check** detects the tell: his responses lag — a beat too long between the party's words and his, as if something else is deciding how to respond.

He asks two questions for every one he answers. His questions are cataloguing questions, not conversational ones: who else is working this area of the city, what are their names, have they had any trouble with the Watch. The interest is real but its direction is wrong.

### Day 7 — Confirmation

Any of the following confirm the possession:

- *Detect evil and good* reveals an aberrant presence.
- *Detect magic* reveals Meloon himself as a magical source (the devourer's presence registers).
- A character who has been tracking the behavioral tells since Day 1 and Day 3 gains Advantage on any subsequent Arcana or Medicine check to identify the specific signature of intellect devourer occupation (**DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or Medicine)**).

> **[GM]**
>
> #### What the Party Now Knows
> By Day 7, the party has: Meloon's behavioral changes documented across multiple days; Azuredge's direct communication (if pursued); and one or more magical confirmations available. They have everything Vajra needs to act. They proceed to the **Azuredge Confrontation** Event to decide how this ends.

## Overview

Vajra assigns the party to watch a Force Grey veteran named Meloon Wardragon at the Yawning Portal for a tenday. Something is wrong with him.

## Read Aloud

> [!narrative]
> Vajra's Sending is twenty-five words, almost exactly:
>
> > "Force Grey operative Meloon Wardragon is behaving strangely. Observe him for a tenday. Do not approach him unless necessary. Report what you see."
>
> She does not explain why she cannot do this herself.
>
> Meloon's table at the Yawning Portal has two empty chairs that people used to fill automatically. They don't now. He sits with his drink, watching the portal's mouth, and the axe across his back catches the light with each small motion. The axe, you notice, is watching you.

## Summary

Vajra assigned the party a tenday of surveillance on Meloon Wardragon at the Yawning Portal. Over the watch, they observed behavioral tells consistent with intellect devourer possession — lagged responses, wrong-directional curiosity, a morning ritual with his axe that the axe appeared to be losing. On Day 7, the possession was confirmed through magic. Azuredge communicated directly if the party addressed it: something is wrong with Meloon, and the axe cannot reach him alone.
