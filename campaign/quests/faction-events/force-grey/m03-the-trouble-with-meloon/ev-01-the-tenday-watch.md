# The Tenday Watch

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Exploration Event begins when Vajra's *Sending* calls the Force Grey members to Blackstaff Tower, and it ends when Vajra accepts the party's report on Meloon Wardragon. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Vajra's brief at her standing desk and sign for two vials of holy water.
> - Watch Meloon at the Yawning Portal for a tenday, starting with Durnan and the empty chairs at Meloon's table.
> - Watch the morning ritual in his room, and feel Azuredge's three impressions by speaking to the axe by name.
> - Sit with the hosted Meloon on Day 5 and catch the wrong questions he asks.
> - Listen to Bonnie's warning, and follow the Guild courier who comes for Meloon's report.
> - Confirm the possession by several independent routes and report to Vajra.
>
> Only Force Grey members attend the brief and the report. Their companions can help with everything in between.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - An Occupying Devourer took Meloon's skull after the Field of Triumph, about three tendays before Day 1. It keeps his brain alive. Meloon is aware and cannot act, speak or cast.
> - The devourer walked Meloon into Xanathar's lair twice, both times before the watch. It has not left the Portal district since.
> - Its telepathy reaches only 60 feet, so it reports through a Guild-paid dockhand named Dobb Ketterly. He meets Meloon at the Portal at the end of each tenday, and the next meeting is at dusk on Day 10.
> - Azuredge is a sentient battleaxe and is awake. Each dawn the devourer must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw to touch it, and it has never drawn it. In public the axe stays in Meloon's room.
> - Durnan has noticed the change and does not know the cause. Bonnie has sensed it too, and she tells one character.
> - Vajra suspects a devourer and has no proof. Renaer, Laraelra Harsard and Meloon once pulled her out of Khondar Naomal's cells, and she never tells the party so.
> - Mirt's "three of ours" in **The Sleeping Asset** counts Harpers. Meloon and Orvyn Dall are Force Grey's hosts and are separate from that count.
> - Nobody in this Event mentions the Splinter, the Cassalanters or Manshoon.

### The Brief

The *Sending* reaches every eligible member at the start of the day. Vajra receives them that afternoon at Blackstaff Tower, near Swords Street in the Castle Ward, and companions wait in the street.

> [!readaloud]
>
> You are in the middle of something ordinary when a dry, flat voice arrives behind your eyes. It gives no greeting, it moves fast, and nobody near you hears a word of it.
>
> > "Meloon Wardragon is behaving strangely. Come to Blackstaff Tower before dusk. Members only. Stay away from the Yawning Portal until I have spoken to you."
>
> The voice stops as quickly as it began, and you have room to answer in a few words if you want to.

The *Sending* is exactly 25 words, and a member can answer in up to 25 words, though Vajra does not reply.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The street door of Blackstaff Tower swings open before anyone knocks, and the stair beyond climbs past more floors than the outside of the building has. Vajra Safahr stands at a tall desk at the top with a notebook open, and the room has no chair for visitors. She finishes a line, caps her ink and looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "Meloon Wardragon is one of mine, and he hasn't filed a report in three tendays. Half the Portal says he's drunk and the other half says he's cursed."
>
> She sets the pen down on the notebook.
>
> > "I think something is wrong with his mind, and I can't prove it. I can't go there myself, because he would see the Blackstaff coming, so I need you to watch him for a tenday and tell me what you saw."

> [!social]**The Blackstaff at Her Desk**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep, who is careful with her words tonight and writes nothing down while the members speak.
>
> Vajra gives her orders in full sentences and waits for each answer before she goes on. She cares about Meloon and does not say so. When someone presses her to be certain of what she cannot prove, she swears at the situation and never at the member. A member who asks if she is all right gets one word.
>
> Conversation topics Vajra is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What Meloon has done since he stopped reporting, as far as she knows.
> - The rules of the watch: ten days, no approach unless necessary, and no mention of her name.
> - Holy water from the quartermaster, if someone asks what to bring.
> - The reward, which she keeps vague.
>
> Vajra will not discuss how she knows Meloon or the contents of the Blackstaff.

> [!qna]**Why not watch him yourself?**
>
> > "If I walk into the Portal, he sees me within a minute, and so does anything that is riding him. I won't move against one of my own on a hunch, but I won't sit on my hands if I'm right either."

> [!qna]**What do you suspect?**
>
> > "Nihiloor breeds creatures that ride a person's skull, and I've seen what they leave behind. I would give a lot to be wrong, but if I'm right then some Guild fuck has been sitting inside my friend for weeks."

> [!qna]**What if he turns on us?**
>
> > "Then don't be in Durnan's taproom when it happens, because he will end the fight and he won't care who started it. Do not approach Meloon unless you have to."

> [!qna]**What do we carry?**
>
> > "Merris keeps two vials of holy water on the third floor, so sign for them on your way out. Don't ask him where they came from, because he won't know."

> [!qna]**What do we get?**
>
> > "You get my gratitude, and Renown if it goes well. If you do this properly, you'll leave with a good deal more than my thanks."

> [!qna]**Are you all right?**
>
> Vajra looks at the ink on her fingers before she answers.
>
> > "Irrelevant."

### Day 1 at the Portal

Day 1 is the first evening after the brief. The taproom is crowded, Durnan works the bar and Meloon sits near the fire. The party can watch from any table without approaching him.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Yawning Portal is loud and warm tonight, and patrons crowd around the well in the middle of the taproom. A bard tests a lute by the stairs while Durnan works the bar with a rag over his shoulder, and Bonnie carries four stews through the crowd without spilling any. Near the fire a huge man sits alone at a table meant for six, and the cup in front of him is full. He watches the door each time it opens, and then he watches whoever comes through it.

Meloon's table has two empty chairs that regulars used to fill without being asked. He answers anyone who speaks to him politely and briefly, and he leaves the table only to climb the stairs at closing, with no axe hanging at his back.

> [!exploration]**The Missing Axe**
>
> An hour after the party sits down, a drunk patron calls across the room and asks where the axe is. Meloon answers in a loud, easy voice that Azuredge is with the armourers.
>
> Any character who hears the answer and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that he is lying, because he answered before the patron finished asking and with no warmth at all.
>
> Any character who watches the table through the evening notices without a check that he never lifts the cup, never laughs and counts each new arrival at the door.

> [!social]**The Man Behind the Bar**
>
> Durnan (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: the Yawning Portal's owner, who has noticed Meloon's change and talks about it only to a quiet question.
>
> Durnan polishes the same mug through the whole exchange and answers in as few words as he can. He doesn't know what is wrong with Meloon, and he won't say a word of it where anyone can overhear. A character who asks at the far end of the bar gets the answers below. A character who asks aloud gets "Drink or the well."
>
> Conversation topics Durnan is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What has changed about Meloon in the last month.
> - Where Meloon sleeps and what overlooks his door.
> - His back room, if someone asks for it.
>
> Durnan will not discuss what lies below the well.

> [!qna]**About Meloon?**
>
> Durnan turns the mug a quarter turn in the rag.
>
> > "Hasn't sworn in a month."

> [!qna]**What's wrong with him?**
>
> > "Fuck if I know."

> [!qna]**Where does he sleep?**
>
> > "Upstairs, end of the hall."

> [!qna]**Can we use your back room?**
>
> > "Not here. Back room's free."

### The Morning Ritual

From Day 2 on, Meloon sits on the edge of his bed at dawn with Azuredge across his knees. His door stands ajar, and a gallery rail on the upper level looks across to it from about 20 feet away. Durnan has told the party where the rail is, or the party can find it by following Meloon up the stairs.

> [!readaloud]
>
> In the grey light before dawn, Meloon sits on the edge of his bed with a silver-and-electrum battleaxe laid across his knees. His lips move without sound, and his hands hover over the haft and never close on it while the axe lies still. After several minutes he lays his hands flat on his thighs, gets up and goes down the stairs without it.

> [!exploration]**Reading the Ritual**
>
> Any character who watches the ritual and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that the axe and the man are in a contest. The axe is pressing him, and his refusal does not look like his own will.
>
> Every dawn the devourer must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw to touch Azuredge. It succeeds on every dawn the party watches, which lets it rest its hands on the haft but never draw the axe, so do not roll and leave the axe in the room each day. *Detect Magic* shows no spell on Meloon, only the axe, which glows as a magic item.

Azuredge answers a character who speaks to it by name during the ritual, in a low voice from the gallery rail.

> [!exploration]**Azuredge Speaks**
>
> Any character who speaks to Azuredge by name and makes a successful DC 18 Wisdom (Insight) check feels the axe's attention turn toward them. The DC is 14 if the character asks the axe a question, such as "Are you trying to tell us something?"
>
> On a failure, the character feels a held breath and nothing more. The character has Advantage on the same check at the next dawn, and the Advantage stacks no further.

If the check succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> No voice speaks, but three thoughts arrive in your head one after the other, and you know each of them is true. The first is *something is wrong with him*, and it feels like a warning from someone who has been shouting it for weeks. The second is *I cannot reach him*, and it comes tired and angry before the third turns toward you and says *you can*.

> [!gamemaster]**Playing the Beat**
>
> The axe has no voice, so leave a pause after each impression so the player can take it in before the next one arrives. A character who tells Vajra what the axe said, and that it resists the devourer, earns a Renown bonus at the end of the mission.

### The Conversation

The Day 5 conversation is optional. Vajra's order allows it where necessary, and a party that never sits with Meloon never gives the devourer a close look at its faces. If the party sits with him, he is glad to have company. If the party tells him where it lives, he asks to see it and accepts an invitation to Trollskull Manor on any evening before Day 10 that the party names.

If the party marked **Meloon Met**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Meloon looks up before you reach the table and rises with his hand out, and the grip is as hard as you remember from the sand. He says each of your names correctly, one after another, and then praises a throw that none of you made in the final bout. His grin arrives a moment after his words.
>
> > "Meloon remembers the sand very well. Sit down, please, and Meloon will order for the whole table."

If the party did not mark **Meloon Met**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A huge man looks up as you reach the table and stands with his hand out. His grip is hard and brief, and his smile arrives a moment after his words.
>
> > "Meloon Wardragon, of Force Grey. Sit down, please, and Meloon will order for the whole table."

> [!social]**The Observation Post**
>
> Meloon Wardragon (Neutral Evil, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a Force Grey veteran with an Occupying Devourer behind his eyes, warm on the surface and curious in the wrong direction.
>
> The devourer has Meloon's manners and not his reasons. It asks two or three questions for every one it answers, and they are the questions of someone building a list: who else works this part of the city, what their names are, which wards the party walks. It calls itself Meloon when it isn't paying attention. It orders ale for the table and does not touch its own, and its answers arrive a beat late. It never swears.
>
> The devourer senses every thinking mind within 300 feet. In the crowded Portal that is the whole taproom, so it cannot pick out the watchers, but it knows the face of anyone who sits at its table.
>
> Conversation topics Meloon is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The party's recent work, with more interest than warmth explains.
> - Which wards the party walks and who else works there.
> - Force Grey's current business, which he steers toward.
> - Directions into Undermountain.
> - Azuredge, which he says is lent to a friend.

> [!qna]**Your recent work?**
>
> > "What have you been doing lately? Meloon has heard that the Guild is busy in the Dock Ward, so tell me which streets you worked and who was with you."

> [!qna]**About Azuredge?**
>
> He pats his shoulder where the strap should be.
>
> > "Azuredge is lent to a friend who needed her, and Meloon will have her back soon. Where do your people sleep at night, and does anyone keep watch?"

> [!qna]**About Undermountain?**
>
> > "Going down? Meloon knows a way, so take the sewer junction three tunnels in off the Dock Ward, because the Watch never walks it. Which entrance will you use?"

> [!exploration]**The Tell**
>
> Any character who talks with Meloon for a minute and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that his answers arrive late and his eyes do not follow the room. Something is wrong with the mind and not with the story.
>
> Any character who listens to what he asks and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that he is gathering names and places.
>
> Any character who heard his answer about the axe in the taproom and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check determines that "lent to a friend" is a second lie after "with the armourers".
>
> - **Character watched the ritual or spoke to Durnan:** The character has Advantage on these checks.
> - **Character has spoken to Durnan:** The character notices without a check that he does not swear.

### Bonnie

On the evening of Day 6, Bonnie takes one character aside. She chooses the first character who spoke to her this tenday, or the character with the highest Wisdom if nobody has.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Late in the evening Bonnie sets a fresh plate in front of you and wipes the same patch of bar twice. She tilts her head toward the empty end of the counter and waits until you follow.
>
> > "Something ain't right with him, love, and I've been wanting to tell somebody for a week."

If **Bonnie Harper Operative** is marked, or the party otherwise knows what she is, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie keeps her eyes on the stairs while she talks, and her voice is flat and dry.
>
> > "I read him once, from across the room, and I won't do it twice. There are two minds in his head, and the loud one reads lines while the quiet one is trying to get out. It felt the probe but couldn't tell who it came from."

Otherwise, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bonnie smooths her apron and gives you her usual warm look.
>
> > "Don't ask me how I know, because I couldn't tell you. He stopped laughing a month ago and he counts everyone who walks in, so if you're watching him, do it from somewhere he can't see."

> [!social]**The Barmaid Who Reads the Room**
>
> Bonnie (Neutral, Doppelganger, she/her) :: the Portal's barmaid, who read Meloon's surface thoughts once and has not tried again.
>
> Bonnie never tells a party that doesn't know what she is. A party that knows hears the truth in her plain private voice, and a party that doesn't hears a barmaid with sharp eyes. She is protective of the gang and wants the Portal left out of whatever happens.
>
> Conversation topics Bonnie is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What has changed about Meloon.
> - The dockhand who visits him at the end of each tenday.
> - What she will not do: read him again, testify to anyone, or fight in the taproom.

> [!qna]**Can you read him again?**
>
> If the party knows what she is, she answers in her flat private voice. Otherwise she laughs and points at the crowd.
>
> > "Not twice. The first time he went still all the way down, and I'm not stupid enough to try it again."
>
> > "Read him? I'm a barmaid, love, and I watch people for a living, which is all the reading I do."

> [!qna]**Is anyone else watching him?**
>
> > "A dockhand drops in near the end of every tenday for a quiet word with the big man, and I'll nod when he walks in so you'll know him."

### The Courier

On Day 8 or Day 9, whichever evening the party is watching the door, Dobb Ketterly comes in to leave word for Meloon. Bonnie nods when he enters.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A stocky man in a dock-carter's coat takes the corner stool by the stairs and orders a single pint. He pays in copper, says a few words to Bonnie across the bar, and drinks slowly with his eyes on the upper hall. When the cup is empty he leaves without looking at Meloon's table.

Dobb asked Bonnie to pass word to the big man that the tenth night is the usual night. He goes straight home to a loft above a net-maker's in the Dock Ward.

> [!exploration]**Following Dobb**
>
> Any character who follows Dobb and makes a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check reaches the net-maker's door unseen. The net-maker's wife calls him by name as he climbs the stair, and the character learns that Dobb Ketterly lodges there.
>
> On a failure, Dobb notices the tail and leads the character around the Dock Ward until he reaches his loft anyway, and the character learns only that he lives somewhere in the ward. The party can still learn his name from the meeting on Day 10, or from Meloon once Meloon is free.
>
> Mark **Courier Identified** in **The Extraction** when the party learns his name and lodging by any of these routes.

> [!gamemaster]**The Meeting on Day 10**
>
> If the devourer is still in place at dusk on Day 10, Dobb sits at Meloon's table for about ten minutes. Meloon speaks in a low voice and Dobb nods, and then Dobb leaves. A party that watched the meeting sees nothing more.
>
> The devourer tells him what it learned. If the party held the Day 5 conversation, that includes descriptions of everyone who sat at the table. Dobb carries the report to Nihiloor.
>
> If Vajra took Meloon at dawn on Day 10, Dobb waits at the corner table until the bell and leaves without a report.

### What You Can Confirm

Several routes show that something rides in Meloon's skull. They are independent, and none of them settles the mission alone.

> [!exploration]**What You Can Confirm**
>
> - **Watching.** The full cup, the empty chairs, the late answers, the questions, the missing swearing, the axe excuses and the ritual are all tells the party sees without a check.
> - **Eye contact and speech.** A DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check over a minute of conversation, as described in **The Tell**.
> - **His questions.** A DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check, as described in **The Tell**.
> - **Close study.** Any character who spends five minutes beside him and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine) check finds no spell and no disease. The pupils lag, and something is riding in the skull. A character who has watched the ritual has Advantage.
> - ***Detect Evil and Good*** senses an Aberration within 30 feet and does not alert the devourer.
> - ***Detect Thoughts*** shows two minds in one head, one of them muffled. It alerts the devourer.
> - **Azuredge.** The three impressions from **Azuredge Speaks**.
> - **The sewer directions.** Any character who asks around the Dock Ward about the junction and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check learns that it ends at a Guild post. The real Meloon knew better.
> - **Bonnie.** Her warning tells the character where to look and gives no proof Vajra can use.

> [!gamemaster]**If the Devourer Is Alerted**
>
> *Detect Thoughts* on Meloon, or an open accusation in the taproom, alerts the devourer. It does not fight in public, because a fight brings Durnan down on it. It says Meloon is tired, climbs the stair, bars his door and waits for the courier.
>
> From that moment it refuses every invitation, will not go to the Tower and answers no questions, so Vajra's route closes. The dawn ritual is the one time it can be found alone, and the party must act on that morning.

### The Report

The party can report whenever it has what Vajra needs. She acts at dawn on the day after an accepted report, so a report by the evening of Day 9 reaches Meloon before the courier does.

> [!readaloud]
>
> At the top of the Tower Vajra stands at her desk with a fresh page open and a wet pen in her hand, and she does not ask how the watch went before she speaks.
>
> > "Tell me what you saw, in order, and give me specific things instead of saying he seemed off. Then I want the days, and then I want whatever confirms it."

> [!exploration]**What Vajra Needs**
>
> Vajra asks three questions and writes the answers down. She accepts the report when the party has answered all three. No check is involved.
>
> - **What did he do?** Any two tells from the list under **What You Can Confirm**.
> - **Which days?** The day each tell was seen.
> - **What confirms it?** One of *Detect Evil and Good*, close study, *Detect Thoughts*, or Azuredge's impressions.
>
> If an answer is missing, Vajra says which one and the party returns to the watch. She is not angry, and she asks the same question again when they come back.

> [!qna]**Can we do it ourselves?**
>
> > "You can try it yourselves, but if it goes wrong there is no one standing there who can dispel it. I would sooner take him at the Tower."

> [!qna]**What will you do?**
>
> > "I'll order him to the Tower at dawn on Force Grey business, and he'll come, because a refusal would tell me everything. Stay away from the Portal tonight and let him think nothing has changed."

If Vajra accepts the report, continue with **The Extraction** at **Vajra Acts**. If the party alerted the devourer, or chooses to act without Vajra, continue at **Getting Him Alone**.

## Overview

Vajra asks the Force Grey members to watch a veteran at the Yawning Portal for a tenday, because something is wrong with him.

## Summary

Vajra called us to Blackstaff Tower and asked us to watch Meloon Wardragon at the Yawning Portal for a tenday. Durnan told us Meloon hadn't sworn in a month, and Bonnie told one of us that something ain't right with him. We found enough to take to Vajra.
