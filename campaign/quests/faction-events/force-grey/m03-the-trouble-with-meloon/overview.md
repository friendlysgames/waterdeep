# The Trouble with Meloon: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Force Grey member reaches Renown 5 and 4th level, after **The Dragon in the Harbor**. Companions can help with everything except Vajra's brief and debrief.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 4th-level characters.*
>
> The only fights are the hosted Meloon, who uses the **Warrior Veteran** stat block with the custom **Occupying Devourer** riding him, and the expelled devourer alone. Section 5 of the **Harpers Mechanics Reference** holds the stat block and the Extraction Procedure, and the **Force Grey Mechanics Reference** sets the rosters for three, four and five characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

Vajra's *Sending* finds each eligible member in the middle of an ordinary morning and calls them to Blackstaff Tower before dusk. She wants a Force Grey veteran watched at the Yawning Portal for a tenday, and she cannot go herself.

## Background

Meloon Wardragon is a Force Grey veteran and a favourite regular at the Yawning Portal. After the Field of Triumph, an Occupying Devourer from Nihiloor's brood took his skull, about three tendays before the watch begins. It keeps his brain alive and walked him into the Xanathar's lair twice before the watch. Its telepathy reaches only 60 feet, so it reports through a Guild-paid dockhand who meets Meloon at the end of each tenday.

His sentient battleaxe Azuredge is awake and fights the devourer. Each dawn the devourer must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw just to touch the axe, and it has never drawn it. Vajra suspects a devourer and cannot prove it.

## The Brief

Vajra receives the members at her standing desk, where there is no chair for visitors. She tells them to watch Meloon for ten days, to stay away from him unless they must, and to leave her name out of it. She sends them to Merris for two vials of holy water.

## The Watch

For ten days the party works from the Portal's taproom and its upper gallery. Durnan answers a quiet question in a few words, and Meloon's table has two empty chairs. His axe stays in his room all day and lies across his knees at dawn. A character who speaks to the axe by name feels three impressions, which are concern, frustration and hope.

## The Conversation

On Day 5 the party can sit with the hosted Meloon. He asks twice as many questions as he answers, never touches his ale, never swears, and gives sewer directions that end at a Guild post. A character who met him at the Field of Triumph hears the difference at once.

## Bonnie

On Day 6 Bonnie takes one character aside and says that something ain't right with him. A party that knows what she is hears how she knows, and a party that doesn't hears nothing more.

## The Courier

On Day 8 or 9 a Guild dockhand named Dobb Ketterly leaves word for Meloon, and the party can follow him home. On Day 10 at dusk he sits at Meloon's table and carries the report to Nihiloor.

## The Report

The party can confirm the possession by several independent routes and take it to Vajra. She acts at dawn the day after an accepted report, so a report by the evening of Day 9 reaches Meloon before the courier does.

## The Extraction

Vajra's way is three castings of *Dispel Magic* at the Tower. The party's way means getting Meloon alone in his room at dawn, in Durnan's back room or in Trollskull Manor, then holding him and forcing the devourer out with a ward, with Azuredge as an anchor, or with both. The hosted Meloon fights as a Warrior Veteran.

## What the Party Decides

Meloon can come out alive and free, die, or live while the devourer escapes. After a Long Rest he tells the party about the long dream and names the courier. Vajra's debrief reads each ending.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Force Grey member gains 3 base Renown for settling the matter, whether Meloon was restored or lost. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** Meloon is restored alive.
- **+1 Renown:** the party reports Azuredge's resistance to Vajra.
- **+1 Renown:** the courier is identified, or the extraction is finished before dusk on Day 10.

Companions who are not Force Grey members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

A restored Meloon returns to the Portal after a tenday, and Vajra hands over a *wand of secrets*. A lost Meloon leaves Azuredge in the Tower. If the party held the Day 5 conversation and the devourer was still in Meloon at dusk on Day 10, **Nihiloor Identified Party** is marked, and Nihiloor knows the party's faces. An escaped devourer carries the faces to him only in that case, and **Destroy the Intellect Factory** reads the outcome. Mirt's "three of ours" in **The Sleeping Asset** counts Harpers, and Meloon is a separate case.

## Involved Characters

- **Vajra Safahr** (Force Grey): briefs and debriefs the members, and casts the three *Dispel Magic* spells if the report is accepted.
- **Meloon Wardragon** (Force Grey): the veteran the devourer rides, aware and unable to act.
- **Azuredge** (Meloon's sentient battleaxe): fights the devourer each dawn and speaks to a character in three impressions.
- **Durnan** (independent): the Portal's owner, who has noticed and says almost nothing.
- **Bonnie** (independent): the Portal's barmaid, who warns one character.
- **Dobb Ketterly** (Xanathar's Guild): the dockhand who carries the devourer's reports.
- **Merris** (Force Grey): the Tower's quartermaster, who keeps the holy water.
- **Renaer Neverember** (independent): Meloon's closest friend, an anchor if the party sends for him.
- **Nihiloor** (Xanathar's Guild): receives the reports and never appears.

## Dangers & Enemies

The hosted Meloon uses the **Warrior Veteran** stat block with the **Occupying Devourer** riding him, and he never draws Azuredge. The expelled devourer is Tiny, has 28 hit points and tries to take a new host. Killing Meloon forces the devourer out at full hit points.

## Overview

Vajra asks the Force Grey members to watch a veteran at the Yawning Portal for a tenday. Something is wrong with him, and the party has to decide how far to go to find out.
