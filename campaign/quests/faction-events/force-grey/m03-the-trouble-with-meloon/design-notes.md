# Design Notes: The Trouble with Meloon

## What the Source Gave

The source mission is a tenday of observation, a morning ritual with a sentient axe, three impressions, and a cure by *Wish*. Appendix C also gives the Insight tells, the lagging answers and the *Detect Evil and Good* test. The earlier draft kept the cure by *Wish*, pointed a 4th-level party at a 4d6 Arcana roll, and gave the devourer the stat block that eats the brain.

## What Changed

The mission is two events. **The Tenday Watch** collects evidence, and **The Extraction** decides what to do with it. The devourer is the custom **Occupying Devourer**, so Meloon keeps his brain, and the cure is Hold, Breaks and Strain from the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**. Vajra's way is three 4th-level *Dispel Magic* casts with no ward and no roll. The party's way is a ward and Azuredge as an anchor.

The clock is the courier's meeting on Day 10. A party that reports by Day 9 gets Vajra to Meloon first. A party that sat with the devourer on Day 5 and left it in place gives Nihiloor its faces.

## Departures from the Source

Bonnie, the courier and the missing-axe excuses come from the Meloon guide, not from Dragon Heist. The guide's CR 10 stat block, its 120-foot telepathy, its Wish cure and its Field of Triumph possession were all rejected. Durnan notices on his own, because the guide's "he hasn't noticed" fits worse than Durnan's profile. Vajra never tells the party that Meloon helped pull her out of Khondar Naomal's cells.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Dobb Ketterly, the Guild courier, and his lodging above a net-maker's in the Dock Ward. Merris is kept from the rank events, and the "junior quartermaster" of the mechanics reference is read as Merris. **Meloon Met** is written in **Trollskull Alley** as an attunement and a True/False flag, so it should be reformatted there. The Notable Figures pages for Meloon and Nihiloor still say the devourer "ate his brain".
