# Smoke in the Tower

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Investigation/Combat Event begins when the party arrives at Blackstaff Tower. In this Event:
>
> - Three investigation paths identify Vira Solkan as the Splinter asset: the resonance source in the east workroom, a behavioral audit of Tower staff, and a credential forgery in the application files.
> - Vira attempts to trigger the disruptor or signal the strike team when cornered — whichever option remains available to her.
> - If the signal fires, six Splinter agents arrive at the Tower's exterior in fifteen minutes and breach through the ground-floor access point.
> - Vajra handles the Tower's active defenses; the party handles the breach.
> - A captured agent confirms Manshoon's personal direction of the operation.
> - The party is commissioned as Force Grey Commanders at the mission's end.

**Background (DM only)**

**Vira Solkan**, 26, is a junior arcanist assigned to Blackstaff Tower's second-floor research section six weeks ago. Her application is backed by forged credentials from Neverwinter Academy. Her assignment: identify the Tower's containment ward protocols and introduce a resonance disruptor into the ward architecture that will disable the Tower's defensive protections for a four-hour window. During that window, a six-person Kolat Towers strike team will move on Vajra directly.

Vira has been preparing the disruptor for ten days. She is two days from triggering it. The disruptor is a hollowed research text on her assigned shelf in the second-floor workroom — a physical object containing a precisely tuned resonance crystal that will discharge into the ward architecture when placed against the ward anchor.

The Kolat strike team is staging in three locations in the Castle Ward. They wait for a specific Sending pattern from Vira. They do not know the disruptor has been found; they are acting on the signal alone.

### Inside the Tower

Vajra provides a current roster of all forty-three people with Tower access and clears the party to move freely through communal spaces — workrooms, library, the reference floor — but not private quarters without cause. She does not accompany them. She returns to her desk and begins the paperwork she has been putting off, which is her way of managing the next two hours.

#### Three Identification Paths

Any two of the three paths are sufficient to confront Vira with confidence. All three together make the case airtight.

**The resonance source:** Vajra has detected the wrongness in the second floor's east workroom. A **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check** in that room narrows the source to a shelf section. Each document on the section can be examined; the hollowed text is **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** to identify as structurally modified — the spine is slightly thicker than the page count justifies, and the cover has been re-glued. The disruptor crystal inside is **DC 15 Arcana** to understand what it does. If the party removes it before confronting Vira, she has only the signal option remaining.

**The behavioral audit:** Vira has been at the Tower for six weeks and has made no close contacts among the staff. This is unusual. A **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check** with any three senior staff members — asking who has kept most to themselves — produces her name consistently. She has been polite, unremarkable, and deliberately invisible. Three separate staff members naming the same junior arcanist without prompting is evidence.

**The credential check:** Vira's Neverwinter Academy credentials are forged — good forgeries, but not perfect. A **DC 16 Intelligence (History) check** identifying the specific signatory format used on Neverwinter Academy certificates from the relevant year — compared against Vira's application documents — reveals a sealing mark not used until three years after her claimed graduation date. This requires access to the Tower's application files, which Vajra authorizes on request.

### Vira

When cornered or confronted with evidence, **Vira** drops any pretense efficiently. This possibility was in her planning. Her priorities in order:

1. Trigger the resonance disruptor — if it is still on the shelf.
2. Send the strike team signal — a prepared *Message* cantrip to a specific contact in the Castle Ward.
3. Surrender — if both options are gone.

She is not panicked. She is not suicidal. If cornered with the disruptor secured and the signal blocked, she puts her hands up. She will not answer questions without legal counsel, which she knows she is entitled to under the Code Legal, and she names the article.

If she triggers the disruptor before the party can stop her: the Tower's defensive ward protections go down for four hours. Vajra knows immediately — the ward failure registers at her desk like a physical blow — and the situation escalates dramatically. The strike team still arrives in fifteen minutes, but now the Tower has no active defenses. Vajra and the party handle this together; it is survivable, but costly.

If she sends the signal and the disruptor is still intact: the strike team arrives in fifteen minutes. The Tower's defenses are active, which the agents were not expecting. Their entry point — identified through six weeks of Vira's observation — is a ground-floor service access on the Tower's north face.

### The Strike Team

Six Manshoon Splinter agents — 2 **Spies**, 2 **Veterans**, 2 **Mages** (2024 *Monster Manual*) — breach through the north service access at the fifteen-minute mark. They do not know the disruptor failed. They expected to walk into an undefended Tower. The defenses are active; they are improvising.

Vajra handles the Tower's active defensive responses — wards that slow, redirect, and contain. The party handles the agents inside.

The Mages lead with *Fireball* in enclosed spaces if the party clusters; the Veterans provide the melee screen; the Spies attempt to flank and find Vajra's location. Their operational objective is Vajra, not the party. A party that stays between the agents and the upper floors forces a direct confrontation.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Capturing an Agent
> A Splinter agent who is persuaded to cooperate — **DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)**, or *Charm Person* — confirms: Kolat Towers. Manshoon personally directed the operation. The strike team was on standby for two weeks. Vira's embed took six weeks to prepare.
>
> This is direct evidence connecting Manshoon to an attempted assassination of the Blackstaff. Vajra can take it to Laeral Silverhand. A formal deposition, recorded in the party's presence, constitutes the clearest legal instrument Vajra has had on Manshoon's operation to date.

### Concluding the Event

> **[GM]**
>
> **+2 Renown if** the disruptor is found and removed before Vira can trigger it. The Tower's defensive protections are never compromised.
> **+1 Renown if** the strike team is neutralized. Kolat Towers has lost six operational assets and the element of surprise.
> **+1 Renown (supplementary) if** a captured strike team member's testimony connects the attack to Manshoon directly and is recorded in a formal deposition Vajra can use at Laeral's level. This is the Grand Game fully to Vajra's attention — if the party has not yet delivered a complete Grand Game briefing, the +2 Earning Renown action from the faction table triggers.

> - **Blackstaff Authority Extension:** Once per quest, the party can invoke the Blackstaff's authority to legitimize an otherwise-illegal action they needed to take to resolve a genuine arcane threat. Vajra covers it. She does not like being asked to use this and will say so clearly if it becomes habitual.

Laeral asks the party one question before the meeting ends: "Is there anything else I should know about Manshoon's operation?" The answer, at this point in the campaign, is yes. What the party chooses to share shapes the alliance structure available in **Kolat Towers** and **Vault of Dragons**.

## Overview

Something is wrong with Blackstaff Tower's containment architecture. Vajra has had a Manshoon Splinter asset in her organization for two months. She needs it found before it finishes what it started.

## Read Aloud

> The Tower's door-attendant arrives at Trollskull Manor in person. This has not happened before. She is already moving when you answer the door.
>
> Vajra is at the window when you arrive — not at the standing desk, which has been pushed to the wall. She turns.
>
> > "Something is wrong with the Tower's containment architecture — the resonance itself, not the ward structure. The kind of wrongness that comes from a point source, not a systemic failure." A pause. "I have forty-three people with Tower access. I have had a Manshoon Splinter asset in my organization for two months and I have not found them." She looks at you directly. "You are not the Blackstaff's staff. You can go places I can't. Find whoever it is before they finish what they started."
>
> The second-floor east workroom smells of parchment and the faint mineral undertone of sustained arcane work. One shelf is wrong. You are not sure how you know that, but you know it.

## Summary

Vajra assigned the party to find a Manshoon Splinter asset embedded in Blackstaff Tower. They identified **Vira Solkan**, a junior arcanist with forged credentials, through one or more investigation paths and secured her resonance disruptor before or after she triggered the strike team signal. Six Splinter agents breached the Tower's north service access; the party neutralized them. Vajra formally commissioned the party as Force Grey Commanders and delivered the captured testimony to Laeral Silverhand.
