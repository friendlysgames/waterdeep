# The Wrong Shelf

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Investigation Event occurs after **Kolat Towers**. It begins when Orla Venn, a Tower attendant, reaches Trollskull Manor at 07:30 with a summons from Vajra Safahr, and it ends when Vira Solkan has been taken, has run, or has sent her signal. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Vajra's brief in her study, where the standing desk has been pushed against the wall.
> - Narrow forty-three names to one by three independent routes: the resonance in the east workroom, the staff's memory of who keeps to herself, and a seal on a forged certificate.
> - Find the disruptor on its shelf and carry it to Vajra's coffer before it fires.
> - Confront Vira Solkan, who will trigger the disruptor, send her signal or surrender, in that order.
> - Take her sending stone before she can use it.
>
> Vajra briefs only the Force Grey members. Companions can walk the Tower beside a member and help with every search, but they earn no Renown and do not hear the private parts of the brief.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Vira Solkan is a junior arcanist on the second floor. She has worked at the Tower for six weeks on forged Neverwinter Academy credentials. The Splinter placed her before the party reached **Kolat Towers**.
> - Her disruptor is a hollowed research text on the east workroom shelf, with a tuned resonance crystal in the spine. It fires on her word from anywhere inside the Tower. If it fires, the Tower's upper wards drop for four hours.
> - She carries one of a matched pair of sending stones in her left sleeve. A Splinter Mage named Tavor Aldeth carries the other and waits in the Castle Ward with his raiders.
> - Vira signals at 22:00 on the second night, the second bell, whatever the party has done. A raiding party reaches the north service door fifteen minutes after her message.
> - Vajra has suspected a leak for two months. The resonance fault began six weeks ago, when Vira arrived. She has not connected the two, and she will not use a *Sending* while the fault lasts.
> - Nobody in this Event says Manshoon's name, or calls Kolat Towers his home, unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member being addressed. Until then Vajra says "the Splinter" and "the other cell".
> - **Kolat Towers** decides who stands behind Tavor. **The Breach** reads that result, and nothing in this Event depends on it.

### The Brief

Orla Venn comes to the manor on foot. Vajra has always reached the members by *Sending*, so the visit is the first sign that something is wrong. Orla waits in the taproom until every member in the building has heard her message, and Vajra briefs late arrivals in two sentences.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A young woman in a grey Tower tabard stands on the step of Trollskull Manor, out of breath, with Swords Street mud on her boots. She asks for each of you by name before she gives her own.
>
> > "I'm Orla Venn, from the Tower. The Blackstaff wants you there now, and she says she isn't using a Sending, and you'll understand why when you arrive."

The door of Blackstaff Tower opens as the party reaches the steps, as it always does. This time the stair hums faintly and the air smells of hot metal.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The study at the top of the stair is cold and bright, and the standing desk has been shoved against the wall. Letters lie in drifts on the floor around it. Vajra Safahr stands at the window with her back to the room, and she turns when Orla closes the door behind you.
>
> > "I'm not using a Sending today, because I don't trust this building to carry one. The containment is wrong, and the fault comes from a single point."
>
> She waits until Orla's footsteps fade on the stair before she goes on.
>
> > "Forty-three people have access to this Tower, and one of them works for the Splinter. I've looked for two months and I've found fuck all. You can go where I can't."

> [!social]**The Blackstaff Without an Audience**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep, speaking in private to people she trusts and swearing freely for the first time in any of their meetings.
>
> Vajra stands at the window for the whole brief and gives her orders in plain, full sentences. She is tired and angry at the Tower and not at the members. She swears the way she does at the end of a bad week, in rants and without apology. A member who asks if she is alright gets one word.
>
> Vajra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Why she is not using a *Sending*.
> - What the fault is and where it comes from.
> - The forty-three names, and where the members may and may not go.
> - Whether the Guild could be behind this.
> - What the members get for doing it.
>
> Vajra will not discuss what the Blackstaff holds, her youth, or the Open Lord's opinion of her. She turns each aside with "Irrelevant." or "Next."

> [!qna]**Why no Sending?**
>
> > "A Sending leaves my head through the same architecture that's failing. I don't know who else can hear it, so I won't use one inside this fucking Tower until I know."

> [!qna]**What is the fault?**
>
> > "It's a resonance in the containment, and a point source, not a general failure. Someone made it, and I think it's meant to take the wards down at a chosen moment."

> [!qna]**Who is it?**
>
> > "If I knew, I'd have had her in a cell already. It's someone for the Splinter, the other cell, and they've been patient."

If **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member asking, Vajra answers this way instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Someone for Manshoon, or for whatever answers to him now. They've been patient, and I'd like to know exactly how long."

> [!qna]**Could it be the Guild?**
>
> > "Nihiloor's people ride skulls, they don't plant crystals. Still, I checked the forty-three against what you brought me from the Hall of Records, and I'd check it again."

Add the line that matches the party's result in **The Legate's Eyes**, to every member who took part in it:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I checked every name on the roster against Dall's ledger, and nobody matched. Whoever this is doesn't answer to Nihiloor."

- If **Orvyn Left in Place** is marked and the ledger was not delivered, read or paraphrase the following:

- If **Orvyn Restored** or **Orvyn Lost** is marked and the ledger was not delivered, Vajra says only "The clerk's matter is closed. This is not that."

> [!qna]**What do we get?**
>
> > "Renown, if you find her, and more if you do it before she acts. I'll say how much afterward."

> [!qna]**Are you alright?**
>
> > "Irrelevant."

### Forty-Three Names

Vajra gives each member a copy of the roster. It lists each person's name, floor and working hours, and a clerk has marked nine names in red. She clears every communal space for the party: the workrooms, the library, the reference floor and the stairs. Private quarters stay closed unless two of the three findings below point at the same person.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra pulls a folded list from her coat and holds it out, and her thumb is stained with ink. The paper holds forty-three names in a clerk's neat hand.
>
> > "Those are the people with a key or a ward-mark. I'm staying in the study, and I'll hold the wards from there. Tell Orla if you need a door opened."

> [!exploration]**Nine Red Names**
>
> Any character who studies the roster and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check sees that only nine people hold a key to the east workroom, and only one of the nine joined the Tower in the last year. That name is Vira Solkan.
>
> - **On a failure:** Merris, the quartermaster on the third floor, tells the party the same when asked, with no check.
> - **What it proves:** it raises suspicion and nothing more. Vajra will not search a private room on one finding.

### The Resonance

The east workroom on the second floor is a long room of shelves, writing slopes and ward-instruments. Vira works at the third desk from the door. The fault is strongest at the shelf behind her.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The east workroom smells of parchment and warm brass. Rows of research texts stand on tall shelves above the writing slopes, and two junior arcanists copy at the far desks without looking up. One shelf near the third desk hums, very faintly, in a way you feel in your teeth.

> [!exploration]**Reading the Shelf**
>
> The party finds the disruptor in three steps. Each step has a check and a fallback, so a failed roll costs time and never ends the search.
>
> - **Narrow the shelf.** Any character who studies the room with ward-instruments and makes a successful **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)** check narrows the source to twelve books on one shelf. On a failure, Ysmay Halvane, a Tower mage, brings the party a better reader, and the result comes at 18:00 with no check.
> - **Find the book.** Any character who handles the twelve books and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check finds one with a spine too thick for its page count and a cover glued twice. On a failure, the party opens every book in an hour and finds it.
> - **Understand it.** Any character who examines the spine and makes a successful **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)** check understands that a tuned crystal inside will discharge into the ward anchor. On a failure, Vajra names it the moment she sees it.

> [!gamemaster]**The Disruptor**
>
> The disruptor is dangerous only when Vira speaks its word, and her word works anywhere inside the Tower.
>
> - **Taking it:** any character can lift the book and carry it. Nothing happens while Vira is silent.
> - **Removed:** the disruptor is removed when it sits in the warded coffer in Vajra's study, which takes five minutes, or when it leaves the Tower. Vira's word then does nothing.
> - **If it fires:** the wards on the second and third floors drop for four hours, and **The Breach** applies its disruptor rules.
> - **Vira's check:** she looks at the shelf at 18:00 each day. If the book is gone she says nothing and uses her stone at 22:00.

### Who Keeps to Herself

Vajra's staff will talk, but they will not volunteer a name they are not sure of. Three senior people know the second floor well enough to be useful.

> [!social]**Three Who Notice**
>
> Orla Venn (Neutral, Human, she/her) :: the Tower attendant, who runs errands to every floor and sees everyone twice a day.
>
> Orla chats and does not guess. Ysmay Halvane, a Tower mage who keeps the second-floor library, speaks in careful sentences. Merris, the quartermaster, licks his thumb and says as little as he can. None of the three swears, and each answers plainly.
>
> The three are willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who on the second floor keeps to herself.
> - Who works late and who stays out of the dining hall.
>
> None of them will name a suspect they are not sure of.

> [!qna]**Who keeps to herself?**
>
> > "Vira, I think. She's quiet and polite, and I don't believe she's said ten words to me since she came. She always asks if she's in the way." (Orla)
>
> > "Vira Solkan, on the second floor. She borrows nothing, stays late and tells me she likes the quiet." (Ysmay)
>
> > "Solkan. Never signs for a thing." (Merris)

> [!exploration]**Asking Without Leading**
>
> Any character who asks about the second floor without naming a suspect and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check gets all three names unprompted. A party that asks about Vira by name gets the same answers, but Orla mentions the question to a friend on the second floor, and Vira hears of it at 18:00.
>
> - **On a failure:** the staff give the same answer, with less confidence, and the party needs another finding before Vajra opens Vira's room.
> - **The door log:** Merris keeps the log of the Tower's door-wards. A two-hour reading, with no check, shows Vira leaving last on thirty-one of the last forty-two nights.

### The Seal

Vira's application folder sits in a cabinet in the clerks' office on the first floor. Vajra authorises its use on request, with a signed slip.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The clerks' office is a narrow room of cabinets and ledgers, and a window over the yard lets in grey light. A bored clerk slides Vira's folder across the counter. It holds a letter of introduction, a short history and a certificate from the Neverwinter Academy with a red wax seal.

> [!exploration]**The Wrong Seal**
>
> Any character who compares the certificate with the seal pattern the Academy used in the year Vira claims and makes a successful **DC 16 Intelligence (History)** check finds that her seal was not cut until three years later.
>
> - **On a failure:** Ysmay Halvane holds her own Academy certificate from the same year, and the two seals do not match, with no check.
> - **What it proves:** the credentials are forged. It does not show that Vira is a Splinter agent, and she can still claim she bought a bad certificate.

### Vira

Two findings out of three are enough to confront Vira with confidence, and all three leave her nothing to say. If the party has found nothing by 22:00 on the second night, Vira sends her signal and fires the disruptor, and the party meets her in **The Breach** as part of the raid.

> [!social]**The Quiet Junior**
>
> Vira Solkan (Neutral Evil, Human, she/her) :: a junior arcanist of twenty-six who has spent six weeks being forgotten.
>
> In cover, Vira is soft-spoken and deferential and apologises for being in the way. Exposed, her voice turns cold and businesslike. She is planning her exit in every sentence. She swears once, coldly, when she is exposed, and never again.
>
> Vira is willing to discuss the following in cover:
>
> - Her work on the second floor.
> - The wards, which she says she knows nothing about.
> - Why she stays late.
>
> Vira will not discuss the sleeve, the shelf, Neverwinter or who she works for.

> [!qna]**Do you know anything?**
>
> Vira looks up from her desk with an apologetic smile.
>
> > "Oh, sorry, am I in your way? I'm only on the second-floor rotation. I just log the readings."

> [!qna]**Why do you stay late?**
>
> > "I like the quiet. It's nothing, really."

Read the following when the party confronts her with two or more findings:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vira sets down her pen and folds her hands on the desk. Her shoulders drop, and the apology leaves her face. She speaks in a flat, clear voice that no one in the Tower has heard.
>
> > "Fuck. Well, you're too late, it's already in place."

> [!gamemaster]**Vira's Three Options**
>
> She acts in this order, using whichever option remains. She does not start a fight.
>
> 1. **Fire the disruptor,** if it is still inside the Tower and not in the coffer. This is an action and needs no roll.
> 2. **Send her signal.** She puts her hand on her sleeve and touches the stone. The message is exactly 25 words, and the Mage answers at once. The raiders reach the north service door fifteen minutes later.
> 3. **Surrender,** if the disruptor is gone and the stone has been taken. She puts her hands flat on the desk and asks for the article of the Code Legal that entitles her to counsel.
>
> If she sends the signal and the disruptor is still on the shelf, she fires it as well.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Stone Says**
>
> Vira sends the first message below if the disruptor is on its shelf, and the second if it is gone. Tavor Aldeth answers her at once with the third message. Only Vira hears the answer, and she repeats it if pressed.
>
> - **Disruptor on the shelf (25 words):** "Disruptor is set. Wards drop at the second bell, and the Blackstaff sits alone upstairs. Come now, by the north service door, and bring everyone."
> - **Disruptor gone (25 words):** "Disruptor is lost and the wards still hold. Come now by the north service door and bring everyone. The Blackstaff sits alone upstairs, I think."
> - **Tavor's answer (25 words):** "Understood. We move in fifteen minutes exactly. Leave the north service door unbarred, stay clear of the stair, and run if the Blackstaff comes down."
>
> After the message, neither stone works until the next dawn. If a member takes an unused stone and touches it, the member can send up to 25 words, and Tavor answers: "Who is this? Vira does not answer this way. Say the word we agreed now, or put the stone down and leave this city tonight." (25 words.) The voice that answers is dry and level, and no member can place it. If the **Kolat Towers** result was Destroyed, it belongs to a Splinter officer nobody has met. If **Manshoon Named** is marked and the result was Alive or Simulacrum Only, Vajra says it sounds like "Manshoon, or something that talks for him", and she says it only then.

> [!exploration]**Where the Stone Is**
>
> Any character who watches Vira while the evidence is laid out and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** check sees her thumb touch her left sleeve each time the word "signal" or "shelf" is said. A character who searches a held Vira for a minute finds the stone with no check.
>
> - **Taking it before she acts:** a character takes the stone with the Utilize action and wins a contested Strength (Athletics) check against Vira. A character who has Grappled her first has Advantage.
> - **She can send while Grappled:** sending is an action, and it needs only her hand on the stone.

> [!hazard]**Vira Runs**
>
> Vira uses the **Mage Apprentice** stat block from the 2024 *Monster Manual*. She is not counted in any roster. The room is the east workroom, with the door on the stair and a window onto the yard.
>
> #### Vira Solkan's Tactics
> Vira opens by acting on her three options, not by attacking.
>
> During combat, Vira:
> - Uses Disengage and *Misty Step* to reach the window or the stair, whichever is closer.
> - Uses Arcane Burst only on a character who blocks her way out.
> - Does not use her stone to call help if it is already spent.
>
> She flees at half her Hit Points. She surrenders if she is Grappled with the stone taken, or if she is cornered with the disruptor in the coffer. If she gets through the window, she runs for the Castle Ward and joins Tavor's raiders in **The Breach**.
>
> **Non-combat route:** a character who offers Vira the clerks' hearing, not the Watch's, and makes a successful **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion)** check persuades her to put her hands on the desk. If the check fails, she tries the window.

If Vira surrenders or is held, Orla takes her to the bare room at the top of the Tower, with the oak chair bolted to the floor. She speaks to no one. If she escapes, Vajra says nothing, writes a line in her notebook and looks at the clock.

## Overview

A Tower attendant arrives in person, and that alone tells the party that Vajra cannot trust her own channels. Somewhere among forty-three names is the one who has been tampering with the Tower's containment.

## Summary

Orla Venn brought us to Blackstaff Tower in person, because Vajra no longer trusted a Sending. We searched forty-three names for whoever was tampering with the containment, and we found a hollowed book on a shelf in the east workroom. We still need to find out whether Vira Solkan got her word out in time.
