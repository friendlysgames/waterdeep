# Design Notes: The Wrong Shelf

## What the Source Gave

The source gave a door attendant who arrives in person, forty-three names, three ways to identify a junior arcanist, a disruptor on a shelf and a six-agent strike team. Appendix C stops at the fifth mission, so the whole mission is a repo invention. It ended with a captured agent naming Kolat Towers and with the party named Force Grey Commanders.

## What Changed

The mission is two events. **The Wrong Shelf** is the investigation and marks nothing. **The Breach** marks everything. It now runs at 7th level after **Kolat Towers**, so the strike team is the Splinter's survivors and the captured agent no longer names Kolat Towers as news. Who sent them follows the **Manshoon operational?** result, as the Doom Raiders and Harper sixth missions do.

The six-agent roster had Power 224, which is impossible at every party size. The raid is now the Harper roster with a new Mage. Vira is a Mage Apprentice who runs, and she signals by sending stone and never by *Message*. The Tower's wards are a hard rule with a ten-round and a fifteen-round clock, and a staff-loss track in place of "Vajra handles the defences".

## Departures from the Source

The Commander rank and its "once per arc" benefits are gone. Ranks come only from the rank events, and the Open Lord's thanks is story only. Her meeting is a letter read aloud, and she no longer asks what else the party knows about Manshoon, because **The Full Picture** has already settled that. The door attendant is the deliberate break in the contact pattern: the party notices a *Sending* is missing before Vajra explains why.

Vajra's two months of suspicion and Vira's six weeks no longer conflict. The fault began when Vira arrived, and the suspicion is older.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Invented: Orla Venn, Corin Aldeth, and the staff track of Dessa Quillon, Harl Merrow, Ilsabet Crane, Jory Pellin and Sabel Orrin. None has a page, and each is voiced from the event text.
- The mechanics reference has the Mage send a stone message to his team in round 1. Vira's signal spends the pair until dawn, so Corin signals his raiders by hand and the stone message was cut.
- The bait route, the door-ward log, the coffer in Vajra's study and Ysmay's certificate are new. The oak-chair room is reused from **The Extraction**.
- **Vira Caught** counts a dead Vira, because readers only care that she is out of play.
- Outside this folder, `arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md` line 51 still reads "Mission 6 succeeded and named the party Force Grey Commanders", and the Notable Figures page for Vira still says Mage and quotes a Manshoon line for Vajra.
