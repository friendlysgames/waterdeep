# Force Grey Mission 6 — Smoke in the Tower

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Requires Force Grey Renown 14 and 7th level. Available after Mission 5 is complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 7th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Vajra Safahr** (Force Grey) — detects the resonance wrongness in the Tower's containment architecture; assigns the investigation; handles the Tower's active defenses during the strike team assault
- **Vira Solkan** (Manshoon Splinter) — a 26-year-old junior arcanist embedded at Blackstaff Tower for six weeks through forged Neverwinter Academy credentials; her assignment is to introduce a resonance disruptor into the ward architecture and signal a six-person strike team when the defenses are down

## Dangers & Enemies
**Vira Solkan** — not a powerful combatant; competent, prepared, and not suicidal. She attempts to trigger her disruptor or signal the strike team when cornered. If both options are removed, she surrenders.

**Six Manshoon Splinter agents** (2 **Spies**, 2 **Veterans**, 2 **Mages** — all use 2024 *Monster Manual* stat blocks) — the strike team staging in the Castle Ward. They move when Vira's signal fires. They arrive at the Tower's exterior in fifteen minutes and breach through a ground-floor access point they have had weeks to identify. They do not know the disruptor failed; they are acting on the signal.

## Overview

No Sending this time. A Tower door-attendant arrives at Trollskull Manor in person and is already moving when the party answers the door. Vajra is at the window when they arrive — not at the standing desk, which has been pushed to the wall.

The Tower's containment architecture has a resonance wrongness sourced to a specific point rather than a systemic failure. Vajra has forty-three people with Tower access. She has had a Manshoon Splinter asset in her organization for two months and has not found her. The party is not the Blackstaff's staff — they can go places she cannot.

Three investigation paths identify **Vira Solkan**: the resonance source in the second-floor east workroom; a behavioral audit showing Vira has made no close staff contacts in six weeks; and a credential check revealing a sealing mark on her Neverwinter Academy certificates that postdates her claimed graduation by three years.

When cornered, Vira moves immediately to trigger the disruptor or send the signal. If the disruptor is already secured, she signals. If the signal fires, the strike team arrives in fifteen minutes. Vajra handles the Tower's defenses; the party handles the agents who breach inside.

A captured agent who cooperates names Kolat Towers and confirms Manshoon personally directed this operation. Vajra can take that testimony to Laeral Silverhand.

The party leaves as Force Grey Commanders.
