# Force Grey Mission 2 — The Dragon in the Harbor

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Requires Force Grey Renown 2 and 3rd level. Available after Mission 1 is complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission awards no Milestone Points.

Zelifarn is a young bronze dragon who took up residence near an old elvish wreck forty feet below Deepwater Harbor's surface eleven days ago. He has surfaced twice without incident — once to examine a fishing boat, once to speak briefly with a sailor who fainted — and has not harmed anyone. His presence has reached the docks as rumor. Vajra has a more accurate account and wants the party's assessment of his intentions.

What Zelifarn has also noticed: something is attached to the underside of the *Eyecatcher*, one of the Sea Maidens Faire's vessels anchored in the deep harbor. The structure below the waterline does not match any conventional hull architecture. He would have examined it himself, but the ship's crew discouraged his approach. He is correct that they would have. The structure is a Bregan D'aerthe operational submarine.

Vajra does not know about the submarine before this mission. When the party tells her, she goes quiet and starts asking very specific questions about dimensions and attachment method. The intelligence they bring back from this mission changes how the city watches the *Eyecatcher*.

## Involved Characters
- **Vajra Safahr** (Force Grey) — the Blackstaff; sends the mission by *Sending* and provides potions of water breathing; receives the party's report
- **Zelifarn** (independent) — a young bronze dragon living near an elvish wreck forty feet below Deepwater Harbor; the party's primary contact and the source of the mission's most consequential intelligence
- **Meritide Blackfin** (Fleetswake variant only) — Dread High Priest of the Queenspire, a Darfellan priest of Umberlee; provides the hook during the Fleetswake festival as an alternative to Vajra's *Sending*

## Dangers & Enemies

No combat encounters under standard conditions. Zelifarn uses the **Young Bronze Dragon** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*) but is not hostile — his cooperation depends on the party's willingness to engage with his curiosity as a genuine transaction rather than an obstacle to clear. Drowning is possible if potions are mismanaged, though this requires active misfortune.

## Overview

Vajra's *Sending* sends the party underwater: a young bronze dragon has taken up residence near an old elvish wreck in Deepwater Harbor. Four potions of water breathing are on her desk when the party arrives, thirty minutes each. She wants his intentions assessed.

The harbor bottom is cold and dim, lit in the center by diffracted light from above. Zelifarn notices the party before they are within speaking range and covers the distance before anyone has formulated an approach. He is intensely curious about everything the party is carrying. His cooperation is best purchased through genuine exchange — a character who gives him something they actually use rather than a spare coin earns his regard for the rest of the conversation and, implicitly, beyond.

After enthusiastic negotiation, Zelifarn mentions conversationally that something is attached to the bottom of the *Eyecatcher* that does not match any hull architecture he recognizes. It comes out sometimes at night and goes back. Vajra's reaction to this intelligence is the best evidence of what it means.

Zelifarn resurfaces in **Sea Maidens Faire**, where parties who established a genuine relationship with him have prior standing as an approach vector and a moral thread.
