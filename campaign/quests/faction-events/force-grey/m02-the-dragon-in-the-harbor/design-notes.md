# Design Notes: The Dragon in the Harbor

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C and WDH give a short dive in which Vajra supplies potions, a young bronze dragon trades for gifts, and he mentions a second hull under the *Eyecatcher*. The reward is 1 Renown. The Vajra and Zelifarn guide supplies the Fleetswake hook, Meritide Blackfin and the idea that Zelifarn has been taking Umberlee's offerings. The restored draft kept the hook but never said who the thief was, so the Queenspire thread had no payoff.

## The Offerings Choice

The mission now has two decisions that sit on top of each other: whether Zelifarn returns the offerings, and whether the party tells Meritide the truth. Three routes get the offerings back and a fourth always works, so the choice is moral and never a roll. Umberlee does nothing on screen, and the cost of **Offerings Kept** is Meritide's grudge, which the Junior Griffon rank event mentions in one line.

The two openings follow the brief, with the Queenspire first during Fleetswake and the *Sending* on other dates, when Meritide meets the party at the quay. The guide's conch and megalodon fight were left out because they need a separate fight audit.

## The Clock and the Dragon

The restored draft used four 30-minute vials as a timer, but the 2024 *potion of water breathing* lasts 24 hours, so the potion cannot be the clock. Meritide's 48 hours is the only deadline, and it matters for one Renown bonus. Zelifarn never lies, so confirming his intentions cannot fail outright: an Insight check saves a trade, and asking him costs one. The Watch observation on the *Eyecatcher* was cut because **Sea Maidens Faire** is unconverted and should set its own response. Vajra says "a carnival fleet" and never names Jarlaxle or Bregan D'aerthe, and nothing about Zelifarn's past is stated, so **Sea Maidens Faire** keeps its own reveal.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Invented: the offerings (fourteen cups, three casks, six candle jars and a bell, about 400 gp), the cave under the wreck's bow, Zelifarn's three tendays in the harbor, and his rowboat measures for the vessel. The 80-foot, 20-foot size comes from the Sea Maidens Faire location notes.
- Meritide Blackfin has no Notable Figures page or voice profile, so his voice, alignment (Chaotic Evil) and pronouns (he/him) are assumed from the event text.
- The Notable Figures page for Zelifarn says Sea Dragon Wyrmling and says he reports to Jarlaxle, and the Bregan D'aerthe organization page puts him aboard the *Scarlet Marpenoth*. Both contradict this Event.
- The Twin Parades text says the fleet docks on Ches 21, but the Field of Triumph text says it is already docked, so this Event runs from whenever the fleet docks until Tarsakh 20.
- **Sea Maidens Faire** (arc-h:392-398) states a backstory for Zelifarn that this Event does not touch, and it is left unedited.
