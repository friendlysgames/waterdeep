# The Extraction

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Combat Event begins when the party decides to move Orvyn Dall, and it ends when Vajra has heard how his case and his ledger ended. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Search Orvyn's apartment on Tallow Court for the coded ledger in his desk.
> - Get Orvyn to a place where the devourer cannot call for help: the Tower through Vajra, his apartment after hours, or the cellar of Trollskull Manor.
> - Force the devourer out with a ward, with magic or with a voice he trusts, without killing a man with 4 hit points.
> - Extract Orvyn, kill the host, or leave him in place and feed Nihiloor a false docket.
> - Keep the neighbour quiet so that no Watch review opens.
> - Pass the Guild names to Jalester Silvermane, and report to Vajra.
>
> Only Force Grey members attend Vajra's debrief, and their companions can help with everything before it.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The **Occupying Devourer** and its Extraction Procedure are in section 5 of the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**. Hold starts at 3, each Break lowers it by 1, and the devourer is expelled at 0. This page gives the numbers the table needs.
> - Orvyn uses the **Commoner** stat block: AC 10, 4 hit points, Intelligence +0. The devourer inside him has Total Cover and does not fight. It controls the body, and the body shouts, flees or stands still.
> - An unwarded Orvyn is always eligible for Occupy Body when the devourer is expelled, because he is Incapacitated and has 10 hit points or fewer, so the ward must come first.
> - The devourer reports to Nihiloor only through Dobb Ketterly. If the link dies, Nihiloor learns it within a day. If **Pool Destroyed** is marked, no replacement host arrives.
> - Orvyn's ledger is a small coded book in the locked drawer of his writing desk. The devourer maintains it without Orvyn's help, and it lists the cases being steered and the Guild representatives' initials, **K.R.** and **A.Y.**
> - If **Meloon Restored** is marked, Meloon asks after Orvyn at the end of the Event and nothing else changes.

### The Desk on Tallow Court

Orvyn lives in a second-floor flat on Tallow Court in the Trades Ward. He leaves at 07:30 and comes home at 18:00. Brenna Tull (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her), a widow who lives in the flat below, shops until noon and sits at her window from the afternoon on.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The flat is two small rooms above a chandler's, with a cold hearth and a writing desk under the window. A brass clerk's seal sits on the blotter beside a stack of letters tied in blue thread. The air smells of ink and old tallow, and the bed in the next room is made as tightly as a barracks cot.

> [!exploration]**The Locked Drawer**
>
> Any character who enters the flat before noon needs no Stealth check. After noon, a character who crosses the court under Brenna's window and makes a successful DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check is not noticed.
>
> - **The lock.** A character who works the drawer and makes a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check opens it. On a failure, the lock holds and the character can try again, and the noise is not enough to be heard below.
> - **The ledger.** A character who reads it and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check breaks the cipher. It is a simple notation, and the entries list each case being steered, the magistrate and a reason. Two sets of initials, **K.R.** and **A.Y.**, appear beside the cases that matter most.
> - **The letters.** The blue-threaded letters are from Orvyn's sister, Imra, who writes from Daggerford. A character who reads them learns that Orvyn answers every letter on the day it arrives. The latest has had no reply for seven tendays.
>
> Mark **Orvyn Ledger Delivered** only when the ledger reaches Vajra. A party that leaves it in the drawer can return for it, but a drawer forced a second time wakes the devourer's suspicion, and it is alerted.

### Moving Orvyn

Orvyn cannot be taken in a crowd, and he cannot be taken while the devourer can call out to Dobb or a passing patrol. The party has three ways to find a quiet room. A party that alerted the devourer in **The Reversed Rulings** can still use the apartment or the cellar, but the Tower is closed, because the devourer refuses every invitation and the Guild minders watch the street.

> [!exploration]**Three Quiet Places**
>
> - **Through Vajra.** This route needs the ledger. Vajra reads it and says a Blackstaff holding a clerk's own book can act without a review. She sends a handwritten request for Orvyn to attend the Tower at 09:00 on a private matter of an old docket. The devourer comes, because refusing the Blackstaff would mark it, and the party may attend.
> - **The apartment.** The party waits for Orvyn at 18:30. A character who gets through the door under a pretext and makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check is let in. A character who works the lock instead and makes a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check finds him at the desk. On a failure, the devourer shouts through the door and **The Neighbour** begins.
> - **Trollskull Manor.** A member who invites Orvyn to the Manor as a petitioner's sponsor gets him there on any evening before Day 12. A companion bars the cellar door after he descends, and the cellar has no window for the devourer to shout from.
>
> If Vajra declines because the party has no ledger, she tells the members to bring her the paper first and sends them back to the desk on Tallow Court.

> [!hazard]**The Guild Minders**
>
> The minders arrive from the street when the devourer is alerted by *Detect Thoughts*, a failed Insight check said aloud, or Dobb seeing the party. Use the Standard roster, and use the Hard roster if the party forced Orvyn out of a public room. If **Pool Destroyed** is marked, remove two **Tough** from the roster, to a minimum of none. The Warrior Veteran stays.
>
> - **Three characters:** two **Tough**. Hard roster: two **Tough** and a **Warrior Veteran**.
> - **Four characters:** four **Tough**. Hard roster: three **Tough** and a **Warrior Veteran**.
> - **Five characters:** five **Tough**. Hard roster: five **Tough** and a **Warrior Veteran**.
>
> #### The Minders' Tactics
> The minders want Orvyn back and do not want to kill in the Trades Ward.
>
> During combat, the minders:
> - Hold the street door and the stair, with Pack Tactics against any character within 5 feet of another minder.
> - Strike the character who is holding Orvyn before any other.
> - Retreat when Dobb flees, when the Warrior Veteran is down, or when a Watch patrol arrives.
>
> Dobb Ketterly flees the moment the devourer is expelled. A minder reduced to half hit points or fewer surrenders if cornered, and he knows only that a dockhand paid him by the day.

### The Hosted Clerk

Once the party moves on him, the devourer drops its cover. Orvyn is a mild man in a thin coat, and the body obeys the devourer's will.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Orvyn's polite smile goes slack, and his hands flatten on the desk. His eyes sweep the room once, from the door to the window to the hands of whoever stands nearest, and then his chair scrapes back. A flat voice that does not sound like his own fills the little room.
>
> > "You are Watch spies, and I will answer nothing more that you ask. Stand away from the door at once."

> [!hazard]**The Hosted Clerk**
>
> Orvyn uses the **Commoner** stat block, with 4 hit points and a Club for 2 damage. The devourer inside has Total Cover, so spells and attacks that target it fail and damage goes to the host. Any damage to Orvyn risks his life, so the party should hold him and not hit him.
>
> A character who Grapples or Restrains him succeeds against saving throws of +0. A melee attack that reduces him to 0 hit points can leave him Unconscious at 1 hit point under the nonlethal rule. Any other hit that reduces him to 0 puts him on death saving throws, and the devourer stays inside.
>
> #### The Devourer's Tactics
> The devourer wants to reach the door or the window and call for the Watch or Dobb.
>
> During combat, the devourer:
> - Shouts "Watch!" from the second round. In the apartment, the shout begins **The Neighbour** at the end of round 3.
> - Dashes for the door, then the window, and uses the Club only on a character who blocks the way.
> - Leaves any downed character alive and turns to whoever blocks the way.
> - Uses Slip Out when Hold is 1, unless Orvyn is warded, and runs at speed 40.
>
> The encounter ends when Orvyn is held, extracted, killed or escapes into the street. An Orvyn who escapes brings the alerted roster in two rounds.

### Forcing It Out

The members can use any combination of the three routes below. Breaks from every source add together and can come from different characters in the same round.

> [!exploration]**The Extraction Procedure**
>
> Orvyn must be held before any route can start. Hold begins at 3 and returns to 3 one hour after the last Break.
>
> - **A ward.** A caster casts *Protection from Evil and Good* on Orvyn, using a vial of holy water worth 25 gp and keeping Concentration. Merris signs out two vials against Vajra's brief. At the start of each of Orvyn's turns he makes a DC 12 Intelligence saving throw with Advantage. A success is 1 Break and a failure is Strain. A warded host cannot be occupied again.
> - **Magic, Dispel Magic.** Cast with a 3rd-level slot, it needs a spellcasting ability check against DC 14 and gives 1 Break, or Strain on a failure. Cast with a 4th-level slot or higher, it gives 1 Break automatically.
> - **Magic, other spells.** *Remove Curse* gives 1 Break if the devourer fails an Intelligence saving throw (+2) against the caster's spell save DC, and Strain if it succeeds. *Greater Restoration* gives 2 Breaks and costs diamond dust worth 100 gp. *Telekinesis*, *Banishment* and *Dispel Evil and Good* do nothing.
> - **A voice he trusts.** A helper within 5 feet uses an action to make a Charisma (Persuasion) check. The DC is 14 with an anchor and 18 without, and a success is 1 Break and a failure is Strain.
> - **Anchors.** Orvyn's anchors are his brass clerk's seal, held in view, and his sister's name, Imra, spoken as a question. A helper who is the anchor has Advantage, a stranger shouting has Disadvantage, and lying does not work.
> - **Strain.** Each failed attempt deals 2d6 psychic damage to Orvyn, never more than 2 and never below 1 hit point. He survives two failures and stays at 1 hit point. A third failure resets Hold to 3 for an hour, and only *Greater Restoration* earns Breaks until it passes.
>
> With one route running, expect four or five rounds. With all three running, expect two or three. When Hold falls to 1 by the third route, Orvyn surfaces.

When Hold falls to 1 through the voice of a helper, read the following aloud and then let him go under again:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Orvyn's eyes stop following the door and settle on the person who has been speaking. His mouth works, and a thin, shaking voice comes out that is nothing like the one you heard at lunch.
>
> > "I didn't write those rulings. Please, get it out of me before it files another one."

Vajra's route is the non-combat way to do the same work.

> [!exploration]**Vajra's Route**
>
> - Vajra casts *Protection from Evil and Good* on Orvyn as he sits in the bolted chair at the top of the Tower, using holy water, and the room's door shuts behind him.
> - She then casts *Dispel Magic* with a 4th-level slot at the start of each of the next three rounds. Each casting is an automatic Break.
> - Orvyn still makes his own contest saves under the ward. A failure deals Strain, capped at 2 and never below 1 hit point. If three failures in a row reset Hold, Vajra waits one hour and begins again.
> - When the devourer is out, a warded Orvyn cannot be occupied, and Vajra kills the creature with her next action.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A pale thing the size of a cat's head slides out from behind Orvyn's ear onto the stone, with long thin legs folded under a lump of grey flesh. It scrabbles toward the shut door until Vajra says one word without raising her voice, and then it stops moving.
>
> Orvyn sags against the iron bands and gulps at the air, and his eyes find the page in front of him.
>
> > "Is it out of me? I can see my own handwriting on that page, and it looks like mine again."

### The Expelled Devourer

> [!hazard]**The Expelled Devourer**
>
> The **Occupying Devourer** appears within 5 feet of Orvyn at full hit points: Tiny, AC 12, 28 hit points, speed 40. Orvyn is Incapacitated until the end of his next turn.
>
> #### The Devourer's Tactics
> The devourer tries to take a new host before it runs.
>
> During combat, the devourer:
> - Uses Occupy Body on the nearest eligible creature, which is Incapacitated, a Small or Medium Humanoid or Beast, and has 10 hit points or fewer. An unwarded Orvyn is always eligible, and a warded Orvyn is immune.
> - Otherwise uses Devour Intellect on the nearest creature and runs for cover.
> - Flees when reduced to 14 hit points or fewer. It does not target a downed character unless the scene says so.
>
> In a barred room it cannot leave. In the apartment it runs for the window onto Tallow Court, and in the Manor cellar it runs for the stair. If it flees outdoors, it needs three consecutive rounds of Dash. Each round, a character who can see it makes a DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check and, on a success, keeps it in sight. After three rounds with nobody in sight, it is gone and reaches the Guild with the party's descriptions within a day.
>
> A caught devourer fights to the death, and the encounter ends when it dies, is trapped under a barrel or bottle, or escapes.

### What the Party Decides

After the diagnosis the members choose among three endings. They can change their minds until the devourer is out.

**Extract Orvyn.** Run the procedure. When the devourer is dead, trapped or gone, Orvyn sits with one level of Exhaustion and answers in fragments. A Long Rest returns his memory in full, and he remembers the occupation as a long dream in which he saw and heard everything and could do nothing. Mark **Orvyn Restored**.

**Kill the host.** Vajra does not order it and does not argue with it. If Orvyn is reduced to 0 hit points and dies, the devourer is forced out within 5 feet at full hit points, and the party faces the Expelled Devourer. Mark **Orvyn Lost**. A clerk's death in the Trades Ward opens a Watch review.

**Leave him in place.** The party keeps Orvyn under watch and feeds Nihiloor one false docket through him. The devourer must not be alerted, so *Detect Thoughts* and open accusations spoil it. Mark **Orvyn Left in Place** while the occupation continues.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra writes a short docket on Tower paper and seals it with a dull wax that carries no mark. She slides it across the desk and does not look up.
>
> > "File this through his office, and let it say that a Force Grey clerk sits in the Hall of Records and will report in three days. The Guild will send someone to look, and I want to know who."

The devourer passes the docket to Dobb at the next noon meeting. At 10:00 on the third day after, a member watching the Hall of Records steps from the public hall sees Ketha Rudd, a Guild accountant in a clerk's grey, and Alder Yost, a solicitor with a leather case, walk the shelves for an hour. Orvyn remains occupied and at risk, though a later extraction can still rescue him.

### The Neighbour

If the fight happens in the apartment and lasts past round 2, or the devourer shouts "Watch!" for two rounds, Brenna Tull comes up the stair before the third round ends and knocks on the door.

> [!exploration]**The Noise Below**
>
> A character who speaks to Brenna through the door and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check sends her back down. A bribe of 5 gp gives Advantage. On a failure, she goes to the street and calls the Watch, and a patrol of two **Guards** arrives in ten minutes.
>
> - **Watch review:** If Brenna calls the Watch, a patrol sees the extraction, or Orvyn dies, the Watch opens a review of the Trades Ward magistracy under the Code Legal, and for 14 days it logs every Tower request to the Watch, so Vajra cannot act there off the books, and the party loses the +1 Renown for a clean extraction.
>
> A party that extracts at the Tower or in the Manor cellar has no neighbour, and no review can open there unless Orvyn dies.

### What Orvyn Remembers

After one Long Rest in the Tower's guest room, a restored Orvyn sits up with his coat on and his hands folded in his lap and gives the party what he knows, while an Orvyn who was killed or left in place gives nothing here.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Orvyn sits on the edge of the narrow bed with a blanket across his knees and a cup of tea he has not touched. He looks up when you come in and tries to stand, and Vajra's apprentice waves him back down. His voice is thin and careful.
>
> > "Thank you, all of you. I'm sorry for the trouble I've caused, and I'd like to tell you everything before I forget it."

> [!social]**The Clerk Who Came Back**
>
> Orvyn Dall (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a thin, anxious appeals clerk in his forties who is ashamed of what his hands did.
>
> Orvyn is polite and apologises too often. He remembers the occupation as a long dream and knows what the devourer said aloud and did. He does not know what it passed to Nihiloor by telepathy.
>
> Orvyn will discuss the following topics:
>
> - The seven tendays, which he describes in pieces.
> - The two Guild representatives who reached the precinct.
> - The amended report he wants to file.
>
> Orvyn will not discuss anything that happened after the devourer took his hand.

> [!qna]**How long was it?**
>
> > "Seven tendays, and I watched myself stamp every ruling. I could read each page and I couldn't stop my hand."

> [!qna]**Who reached the precinct?**
>
> > "Ketha Rudd, a Guild accountant with a magistrate's seal she shouldn't have had, and Alder Yost, a solicitor who collected the dockets. I'd know them anywhere."

> [!qna]**The amended report?**
>
> > "I'll file it myself, and I'd like your names left out of it. The Watch would ask how I got well, and I can't answer that."

If the party marked **Meloon Restored**, Meloon arrives and sits with Orvyn for an hour. He tells him it gets better.

### The Names for Jalester

Vajra tells the members that Jalester Silvermane will want the two Guild names, and she does not object if they take them to him. Jalester comes to Trollskull Manor that evening or the next, in a plain doublet, and sits at a corner table.

> [!social]**The Alliance Agent**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: a lean, thinning-haired field agent in a dark doublet who asks one question more specific than the conversation needs.
>
> Jalester thanks the members for the names and writes both down. He will have two Alliance watchers follow the pair. He will not discuss the Open Lord's business.

> [!qna]**Why do you want them?**
>
> > "Understood. The Open Lord wants the Watch's appeals kept clean, and I can follow people the Tower can't. One more detail, if you don't mind: which of them signs the dockets?"

Nothing is marked and no Renown is awarded for sharing the names. If the party shares them with the Lords' Alliance, Vajra notes it in the positive column and says nothing more.

### Vajra's Debrief

Vajra takes the members' report the evening the case ends, or at 09:00 the next morning, at the standing desk. Companions wait at the street door. Read the case that fits.

If **Orvyn Restored** is marked and no Watch review opened, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra reads the report standing, with her pen still. When she turns the last page, she sets it down beside the ledger.
>
> > "He's awake, he's himself, and nobody in the Watch has written a word about it. The ledger names every case the thing touched, so the magistrates can undo them one at a time. I'll audit the other district clerkships myself."

If **Orvyn Restored** is marked and a Watch review opened, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra reads the report and her mouth tightens at the second page. She puts the pen down and breathes out through her nose.
>
> > "Shit. The clerk is whole and the review is open, so for fourteen days every request I send the Watch is logged. It could have been worse, but it'll be tedious. Leave me the ledger, and I'll start the audit anyway."

If **Orvyn Lost** is marked, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra says nothing for a moment, and her hand rests flat on the notebook. She does not look up when she speaks.
>
> > "A clerk died in the Trades Ward, and the Watch will ask why. I'll be asked too, and I'll answer. Leave me the report. We'll speak again when I'm ready."

If **Orvyn Left in Place** is marked, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra turns the report over and reads the back of the last page with her jaw set.
>
> > "He's still in there, and I don't like it. I'll have a Tower clerk watch him every day. When the Guild stops looking at him, we do this properly."

If **Orvyn Ledger Delivered** is marked, Vajra adds that the ledger is in a locked drawer of her own desk and that she has read it twice.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Force Grey member gains 4 base Renown for settling the matter with Vajra, however Orvyn ended. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** Orvyn is extracted alive, **Orvyn Restored** is marked and no Watch review opened.
> - **+1 Renown:** the ledger reaches Vajra, and **Orvyn Ledger Delivered** is marked.
>
> Each bonus can be earned only once.

### Aftermath

A restored Orvyn rests for a tenday and returns to his desk, and the three magistrates reopen the reversed rulings on Vajra's advice. A lost Orvyn leaves the appeals office short a clerk, and the review runs its 14 days. An Orvyn left in place keeps filing, under a Tower clerk's eye.

Nihiloor learns a link has gone dead within a day. If **Pool Destroyed** is marked, no replacement host arrives. Otherwise one of the two pending hosts on the placement list reaches the Trades Ward within a tenday. Vajra audits the other clerkships and keeps the names off the Watch's books.

### Concluding the Event

Vajra's debrief ends the mission. Record what happened to Orvyn and what happened to the ledger separately, because later events read each.
