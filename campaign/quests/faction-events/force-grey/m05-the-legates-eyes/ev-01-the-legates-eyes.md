# The Reversed Rulings

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Investigation and Social Event begins when Vajra's *Sending* calls the Force Grey members to Blackstaff Tower, and it ends when the members have met Orvyn Dall and know what rides in him. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Vajra's dossier on three reversed rulings and hear why she cannot use her authority.
> - Follow three independent leads to Orvyn Dall: the rulings in the Hall of Records, the appeals clerks at lunch, and a third lead that depends on **Placement Records Taken**.
> - Add a fourth lead by following Dobb Ketterly if **Courier Identified** is marked.
> - Hear from Meloon what the tells look like from inside, if **Meloon Restored** is marked.
> - Find out, by several separate methods, that something rides in Orvyn's skull.
>
> Only Force Grey members attend the brief, and their companions can help with everything after it.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Orvyn Dall is a Waterdhavian Human appeals clerk in the Trades Ward district magistracy. An Occupying Devourer has ridden him for seven tendays. It keeps his brain alive, so Orvyn is aware and cannot act or speak.
> - The devourer routes appeals to the magistrates it wants. Three have reversed rulings this month for the same Guild interests, and a fourth reversal was corrected two days later in Orvyn's hand, which is the devourer's mistake.
> - Its telepathy reaches 60 feet, so it reports through Dobb Ketterly, a Guild dockhand. He sits at the next table at the Bricklayer's Cup at noon on Days 2, 7 and 12, counting Day 1 as the morning after the brief.
> - Orvyn eats lunch at the Cup every working day. He works at the district magistracy from 08:00 to 18:00 and goes home to Tallow Court.
> - Vajra suspects a devourer and has no proof, and she cannot send the Watch or use her authority without opening a review of herself.
> - Mirt's "three of ours" in **The Sleeping Asset** counts Harpers. Orvyn and Meloon are Force Grey's hosts and are separate from that count.
> - If **Pool Destroyed** is marked, no new hosts arrive to replace Orvyn. Nobody in this Event mentions the Splinter, the Cassalanters or Manshoon.

### The Brief

The *Sending* reaches every eligible member in the middle of the day. Vajra receives them that evening at Blackstaff Tower, near Swords Street in the Castle Ward, and companions wait in the street.

> [!readaloud]
>
> You are in the middle of something ordinary when a dry, flat voice arrives behind your eyes. It gives no greeting, it moves fast, and nobody near you hears a word of it.
>
> > "Three Watch rulings reversed this month, all favouring the Guild. Come to Blackstaff Tower tonight. Members only. Say nothing of this to the City Watch."
>
> The voice stops as quickly as it began, and you have room to answer in a few words if you want to.

The *Sending* is exactly 25 words, and a member can answer in up to 25 words, though Vajra does not reply.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The street door of Blackstaff Tower opens before anyone knocks, and the stair beyond climbs past more floors than the building shows from outside. Vajra Safahr stands at a tall desk at the top with three sheets of paper laid in a row, and the room has no chair for visitors. She caps her ink and looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "Three magistrates in the Watch's appeals system reversed rulings this month, and every reversal went to the same Guild interests. I can see the pattern on paper, but I can't see what causes it."
>
> She turns the sheets so the names face you and taps the top one with the end of her pen.
>
> > "All three cases passed through the Trades Ward appeals office, where four clerks work. I want to know which of them it is, and I want it settled before the next ruling is heard."

> [!social]**The Blackstaff at Her Desk**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep, who has not slept well in a tenday and does not mention it.
>
> Vajra gives her orders in full sentences and waits for each answer. She swears at the situation in private and never at a member, and she answers any question about her health with one word. She is worried that the Watch's own appeals have become a Guild tool and cannot say so to the Lords.
>
> Vajra will discuss the following topics:
>
> - The three rulings and the appeals office that handled them.
> - Why she cannot act through the Watch, and what a review would cost her.
> - Holy water from the quartermaster, which she signs against this mission.
> - What she wants from the members, which is the clerk's name and a clean extraction.
>
> Vajra will not discuss the contents of the Blackstaff, and she does not guess who is behind the placements.

> [!qna]**Why not use your authority?**
>
> > "If the Blackstaff moves against the Watch, the Lords open a formal review of me and the magistracy. A review can't be buried, and I'm not ready to answer one. You are not the Blackstaff, so nobody will ask you to explain yourselves."

> [!qna]**What do you suspect?**
>
> > "Nihiloor has put devourers behind people's eyes before. If one is sitting in a clerk, then some shit-brained Guild breeder is rewriting the Watch's appeals one desk at a time."

> [!qna]**What if the Watch notices us?**
>
> > "Then a review opens, runs fourteen days and logs every request I send the Watch. Don't let a neighbour, a patrol or a corpse start one."

> [!qna]**What do we carry?**
>
> > "Merris has two vials of holy water signed against this mission. You'll need a ward if I'm right, and I won't have you improvising it."

> [!qna]**Are you all right?**
>
> Vajra looks at the ink on her fingers before she answers.
>
> > "Irrelevant."

If **Placement Records Taken** is marked, Vajra has already read the list the party brought out of the lair, and she hands over the clerk's name at the end of the brief, as described in **The Third Lead**.

### Meloon's Hour

If **Meloon Restored** is marked, Vajra tells the members that Meloon has asked to help and is waiting at the Yawning Portal. He sits at his old table with a full tankard and drinks from it.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Meloon waves you over before you reach the table, and he clears a bench with one sweep of his arm. His tankard is half empty, and Azuredge leans against the wall beside him. He lowers his voice to something close to a whisper, which is still louder than most men's talk.
>
> > "Fuck me, another one. Vajra told me what you're hunting, so listen. Every answer comes a beat late, like a man reading off a card, and the eyes never follow the room. Watch for that, and don't let it see you watching."

> [!exploration]**What Meloon Knows**
>
> Meloon knows the tells from inside, and he knows nothing about Orvyn Dall or the Trades Ward appeals office. He cannot say what the devourer passed on.
>
> - **Coached:** each member who listens to Meloon has Advantage on the first Wisdom (Insight) check they make against the hosted Orvyn in **What Is Wrong with Orvyn**.

### Three Rulings and a Fourth

The Hall of Records keeps the public appeals registry, open from 09:00 to 17:00. The clerk at the front desk hands over any ruling that has been entered.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Hall of Records is a long room of shelves and writing desks, with petitioners queued at a counter and clerks passing between them with armfuls of bound dockets. The air smells of ink and damp wool. A clerk at the front desk slides a ledger across without looking up and points to the shelf for the Trades Ward appeals.

The three rulings are Magistrate Hesper Aldon's voiding of a Shrimp Street warehouse lease held by a Guild rival, Magistrate Dorrin Estrel's dismissal of a debt claim against Tidewrack Cargo, and Magistrate Maud Tavish's striking of a fine on a Guild barge. A fourth entry is Estrel's reversal of a harbour chandler's lien, corrected two days later.

> [!exploration]**The Fourth Reversal**
>
> Any character who sets the three rulings side by side with the registry's entries and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the fourth reversal. The correction is in a clerk's hand and signed *O. Dall*, while the original reversal is in the magistrate's hand. Someone corrected a ruling they should not have been able to touch.
>
> On a failure, the comparison takes until the next morning and gives the same answer. This lead is always open.
>

The signature leads the party to the appeals office, where a porter says Orvyn takes his lunch at the Bricklayer's Cup on Copper Pot Lane at noon.

### The Office at Noon

The four clerks of the appeals office take their lunch at the Bricklayer's Cup, a cookshop on Copper Pot Lane with a window onto the street. Orvyn sits at the corner table by the window each day with a folded docket and a meat pie.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Bricklayer's Cup is crowded at noon, with clerks and masons sharing long tables and a cook shouting orders through a hatch. Four men and women in grey clerk's coats sit near the window, talking over their plates. One of them eats alone at the corner table with his eyes on the door, chewing at the same slow pace with every mouthful.

> [!exploration]**The Clerk Who Does Not Taste**
>
> Any character who watches the clerks through a meal and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight or Perception) check picks out Orvyn Dall. He eats at a flat, even pace and does not look at his food. His eyes follow the street door instead of the room, and when a colleague greets him, his smile arrives half a second late.
>
> On a failure, the character sees nothing worth noting. A second attempt at the next lunch costs nothing.
>
> - **Character was coached by Meloon:** the character has Advantage on the check.
> - **Day 2, 7 or 12:** Dobb Ketterly takes the next table at noon and stays about ten minutes. A character within 30 feet of him who does not make a successful DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check is seen, and the devourer is alerted.

If **Courier Identified** is marked, the party knows Dobb Ketterly's face and the net-maker's loft in the Dock Ward where he lodges. A character who waits at the loft on Day 2 or Day 7 and follows him at 11:30 makes a successful DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check to reach the Cup unseen, and then sees him sit down beside Orvyn. On a failure, Dobb notices the tail, walks on to the Cup and the devourer is alerted. This is a fourth lead.

### The Third Lead

The third lead depends on what the party holds. Read the first case that fits.

**Placement Records Taken.** Vajra has the list from the lair. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra lays a stained sheet beside the dossier and puts a finger on the third line. It lists four offices by name in the same flat hand, and two have been left unsigned.
>
> > "The Trades Ward appeals office, Orvyn Dall, placed seven tendays ago. I'll take the other three names myself. Orvyn Dall is yours."

**Corene's debrief.** If **Placement Records Taken** is unmarked, **Corene Rescued** is marked and **Xanathar's Lair** has been played, a Harper in the party has heard her debrief in **The Sleeping Asset**. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Harper in your group says it while the three of you study the dossier, and no one at the table interrupts.
>
> > "Corene said Nihiloor kept a clerk in the Watch office and two runners on the docks. It might not be your man, but the Watch office is where your rulings were made."

Corene's words give the party no name, so this lead still sends them to the Hall of Records or the Cup to confirm which clerk it is.

**The Test Appeal.** If neither case applies, Vajra gives the members a dull petition about a fence line between two cooperages. She asks them to file it at the appeals counter at 10:00. The clerk who takes it is Orvyn Dall, and he asks who else sponsors it and in which wards the petitioners sleep. A petitioner's clerk has no reason to ask either question.

### The Corner Table

When a member sits down across from Orvyn, he is polite and keeps his hands flat beside the plate. The devourer wears him well and answers a beat late.

> [!social]**The Clerk at the Window**
>
> Orvyn Dall (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a mild, thin appeals clerk in his forties with ink on his cuffs, whose mouth moves a moment after his eyes.
>
> The real Orvyn is conscientious and shy. The devourer keeps his manners and asks more than it answers, always about people: who else is working the case, where they sleep, which ward they walk. It never swears and never touches its drink. If pressed about the rulings, it says clerks file what magistrates sign.
>
> Orvyn will discuss the following topics:
>
> - The appeals office and how a docket reaches a magistrate.
> - The party's business, which he steers toward who is helping them.
> - His lunch, his sister and the weather, if the conversation stalls.
>
> Orvyn will not discuss the three rulings in detail, and he will not leave the Cup before the bell at one.

> [!qna]**About the rulings?**
>
> > "I file what the magistrates sign. Which of the three did you want to ask about, and who else is looking at them?"

> [!qna]**Are you well?**
>
> > "Quite well, thank you. Do you work for the Watch, or for someone above it?"

> [!qna]**Where do you go after work?**
>
> > "Home to Tallow Court, by six. Where do your people sleep, in case I need to send word?"

### What Is Wrong with Orvyn

Several methods show that something rides in Orvyn. Each is enough alone, and a failed attempt teaches the devourer nothing.

> [!exploration]**What Is Wrong with Orvyn**
>
> - **Eye contact and speech.** A character who talks with him for a minute and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that his answers arrive late and his eyes do not follow the room. Something is wrong with the mind, not the story.
> - **His questions.** A character who listens to what he asks and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that he is gathering names, wards and sleeping places.
> - **Close study.** A character who spends five minutes beside him and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine) check finds no spell and no disease. The pupils lag, and something is riding in the skull.
> - **His desk.** A character who inspects the appeals office after hours finds the real Orvyn's older petitions signed with a looping hand. The recent entries, including the fourth correction, are flat and exact.
> - ***Detect Evil and Good*** senses an Aberration within 30 feet and does not alert it. ***Detect Thoughts*** shows two minds in one head, one of them muffled, and alerts the devourer.
>
> *Detect Magic* shows no spell on Orvyn. An alerted devourer stops pretending, and the Guild minders close in. Use the alerted roster in **The Extraction**.

## Overview

Vajra asks the Force Grey members to find the clerk behind three reversed Watch rulings, and three leads take them to a quiet man at a window table.

## Summary

Vajra showed us three Watch rulings that all went the Guild's way and asked us to find which appeals clerk was behind them. We found Orvyn Dall at his lunch table, and every answer he gave us came a beat too late.
