# Design Notes: The Legate's Eyes

## What the Source Gave

Neither Waterdeep: Dragon Heist nor the Appendices contain this mission. The guide gives one line for it, and the Nihiloor page names Orvyn Dall as a current puppet. Everything else came from the restored draft: three reversed rulings, a fourth corrected in the clerk's hand, a coded ledger and a ban on triggering a Watch review. The title is unexplained in every source, and no NPC is a Legate, so the mission reads the "eyes" as the devourer behind Orvyn's.

## What Changed

The restored draft cured Orvyn with *Telekinesis* and a made-up save, and it called the devourer self-sustaining. The mission now uses the custom **Occupying Devourer** from the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**, with Hold, Breaks and Strain capped at 2 for a 4-hit-point Commoner. The devourer reports through Dobb Ketterly, the courier from **The Trouble with Meloon**, because its telepathy reaches only 60 feet.

The mission is two events. **The Reversed Rulings** finds Orvyn through three leads, and **The Extraction** moves him, forces the devourer out and settles his fate. Extract, kill and leave in place are all written, and the Watch review is defined in one bullet. Vajra's route needs the ledger, which makes the apartment search matter as more than a reward.

## Departures from the Source

The "twelve months of planned manipulation" is gone, since a devourer placed seven tendays ago cannot have started it. The Jalester bonus is dropped because no Lords' Alliance event reads it, and sharing the names is now an optional scene with no Renown. The "four months of clean Watch proceedings" line is dropped because the Vault has no beat that reads it.

The third lead reads **Placement Records Taken**. If it is unmarked, Corene's debrief clerk line from **The Sleeping Asset** stands in, and a Tower-filed test appeal covers a party that has neither, so three leads always exist. A restored Meloon coaches the party and gives Advantage on the first Insight check.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Magistrates Hesper Aldon, Dorrin Estrel and Maud Tavish, Ketha Rudd, Alder Yost, Brenna Tull, Orvyn's sister Imra in Daggerford, Tidewrack Cargo, and the Bricklayer's Cup on Copper Pot Lane, Tallow Court and the Hall of Records, none of which any setting page confirms. Orvyn has no Notable Figures page, so his alignment and the seven-tenday placement are mine. The courier's fifth-day schedule is mine.

Open: the Nihiloor and Meloon Notable Figures pages still say the devourer ate a brain. **Smoke in the Tower**, the **Force Grey** rank event and **Vault of Dragons** are unconverted readers. The Lords' Alliance does not yet read the Guild names.
