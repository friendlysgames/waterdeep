# The Full Picture

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when a Force Grey member brings Vajra Safahr the whole Grand Game, or when her *Sending* calls one in, and it ends when she seals a message to the Open Lord. It plays out only for Force Grey members, and it becomes available once the party has completed any one lair heist. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Lay out the four factions, the Stone of Golorr, the vault and a live deadline at Vajra's standing desk.
> - Hear Vajra confirm three findings of her own, each read from what the party has already done.
> - Take a correction about where the vault lies.
> - Watch the Blackstaff stir when the vault's sum is named, and watch Vajra go still before she writes.
> - Leave with a sealed message on its way to the Open Lord, and earn 2 Renown once in the campaign.
>
> Only Force Grey members are briefed. Companions can climb the stairs and speak, and they gain nothing.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Vajra knows that Nihiloor runs the Guild's intellect devourer programme. She does not know how far it reaches.
> - She knows the Black Network has split. She has traced one hand behind incidents in three wards, and she cannot put a name to it.
> - The other cell belongs to Manshoon, who commands it from Kolat Towers. Vajra learns this only from a member for whom **Manshoon Named** is marked.
> - The fourth faction is Bregan D'aerthe, led by Jarlaxle. Vajra has heard only of the carnival fleet and the *Eyecatcher*'s crew, and she never repeats a name the party gives her for them.
> - She suspects from arcane residue in three Watch reports that the Stone has resurfaced. She has no proof until the party supplies some.
> - Her scrying has found conjuration and abjuration residue beneath the Cassalanter villa. She has nothing else on the family, and nobody in Force Grey has more.
> - The Open Lord can act only on a complete picture. That is why Vajra counts the parts and will not write on three of four.
> - The Blackstaff moves on its own when the talk touches what it holds. Vajra never looks at it and never explains it.

### Asking for the Tower

Any Force Grey member can climb to Blackstaff Tower at any hour. The Event begins on the first visit after the party has completed a lair heist, when a member comes to give Vajra the full picture. The lair heists are **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire** and **Kolat Towers**.

If no member has come by the morning after the second lair heist ends, Vajra calls one in. The *Sending* goes to the Force Grey member with the highest Renown, and a tie goes to the name that comes first alphabetically. Read the following aloud, changing the first sentence to match what the recipient is doing:

> [!readaloud]
>
> You are counting the night's takings at the bar of Trollskull Manor when a dry, flat voice speaks inside your head. It offers no greeting, it speaks fast and clipped, and nobody else in the room hears it.
>
> > "Blackstaff here. The Tower holds Watch reports of unexplained trouble in three wards. Come at dusk. Bring everything you know, and anyone who helped you."
>
> The voice stops as soon as it finishes, and you have room to answer in twenty-five words or fewer.

Vajra ignores any answer, because she expects the member at dusk whatever it says. A member who arrives at another hour finds the door opening as it always does, and Vajra at her desk with the lamp lit.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Blackstaff Tower stands beside the street with no lamp at its door, and the door swings open before anyone reaches the steps. The narrow stair climbs past shelves of books and maps pinned to boards. At the top, Vajra stands at her tall desk with a pen in one hand, and there is still no chair for visitors. She looks up as you reach the landing.
>
> > "Come in. Start with the factions, then the Stone, then the vault, and I'll stop you if I need to."

### What Counts as the Full Picture

Vajra will not write to the Open Lord on a partial picture. The account is complete when each of the four elements is met by any one of its routes. An element is met when the member, or a companion standing beside them, gives the facts in the second column and says how they know.

> [!gamemaster]**The Full-Picture Test**
>
> | Element | The account must give | Route 1 | Route 2 | Route 3 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | The four factions | For each of Xanathar's Guild, the Splinter, House Cassalanter and the *Eyecatcher*'s crew, what it wants from the vault and one thing it has done to get it. | The party ran an outpost chain or a lair heist against that faction and can describe its work. | The party holds a ledger, letter, map or prisoner taken from that faction. | A named contact or a captured agent described the faction's plan to the party. |
> | The Stone of Golorr | That it holds the vault's location, that it needs three Eyes to open it, and where it is now. | The Stone is in the party's hands, and the member can say what it did when attuned. | The member saw an Eye restored during a lair heist and heard the Stone speak. | A faction contact or a captured document told the party what the Stone holds and needs. |
> | The vault | That it holds about 500,000 dragons of Neverember's embezzled gold, and that it lies beneath Waterdeep. | The Stone named the sum or the place. | Papers from a lair, an outpost or the Neverember household named the sum. | An enemy named the gold in a threat or a bargain, and the member can say who and when. |
> | A live deadline | One real date, and what happens when it arrives. | Founders' Day, Flamerule 1, the Cassalanters' charity feast. | Tarsakh 20, when the Sea Maidens Faire sails at dawn. | A faction has moved to Alert or Lockdown against the party, and the member can say what it is racing toward. |
>
> - Judge each element by the second column only. Ask for no proof beyond who, where and when.
> - The test needs what each faction does and never why. The Cassalanters' reasons are not part of it, and Vajra never asks for them.
> - If a player names Manshoon or Kolat Towers and **Manshoon Named** is not marked for that member, Vajra writes the word down and does not react.
> - If a player names Bregan D'aerthe or Jarlaxle, Vajra writes the name down and calls the faction "the carnival fleet" from then on.

> [!gamemaster]**When an Element Is Missing**
>
> Vajra stops at the first missing element in the table, asks the matching question below, and writes nothing. She asks only one question, even when two elements are missing, and she awards no Renown.
>
> - **The four factions:** "Which of the four have you watched work, and which are you guessing about?"
> - **The Stone:** "Where is the Stone now, and what does it need before it opens anything?"
> - **The vault:** "How much is in the vault, and who put it there?"
> - **A live deadline:** "What date are you working to, and what happens when it passes?"
>
> The member can return at any hour once they have the missing piece, and the door opens as before. Vajra picks up at **The Briefing** and does not repeat her greeting.

If an element is missing, read the following aloud, then speak the matching question as Vajra:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra lets you finish, then caps her pen and sets it across her notes. She looks at the member who spoke first and asks one question.

### The Briefing

The member and any companions give their account standing, because there is nowhere to sit. Vajra writes a few words each time a new name or date comes up and never interrupts.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra listens with her pen moving, and her eyes stay on the member who is speaking. She turns a page when the factions give way to the Stone, and she turns another when the account reaches the vault. When you stop, she sets the pen down on her notes.

> [!social]**The Blackstaff at Work**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep and commander of Force Grey, giving the party her full attention at the standing desk.
>
> Vajra is Friendly toward the party, which shows as patience and nothing warmer. She stands throughout, writes while others talk, and gives her conclusion before her reasons. Her swearing is casual and colourful in her own study, and she saves it for what angers her. She says "the Open Lord" for Laeral Silverhand and never discusses the Blackstaff.
>
> Vajra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The three things she has confirmed for herself.
> - What the party's account changes for the Open Lord.
> - The vault opening and the Lords' Alliance inquiry.
>
> She will not discuss what the Blackstaff holds, her youth, or what the Open Lord will do and when. She turns each aside with "Irrelevant." or "Next."

> [!exploration]**The Staff Stirs**
>
> The Blackstaff moves when a member first says aloud that the vault holds 500,000 dragons. Any character who is watching the staff at that moment and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check sees it shift against the desk with nobody touching it. A character who fails sees only that Vajra's pen stops for a moment. Nothing depends on the check, because every outcome of the Event stands whether or not anyone sees the staff move.

If a character succeeds, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> When the sum is named, the Blackstaff slides a finger's width along the edge of the desk and settles again. Nobody has touched it. Vajra keeps her eyes on her page.

Vajra does not look at the staff and does not answer questions about it. A member who asks gets "Next."

### What Vajra Already Had

When the account ends, Vajra turns back through her own notes and confirms three findings. She volunteers them without being asked, and each shows the party that their account matches what she holds. Read the matching case for each confirmation.

**First, Nihiloor's programme.** Use the first case that applies. If **Pool Destroyed** is marked, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra turns to the first page of her notes and taps a line near the top with her pen.
>
> > "Nihiloor's pool is gone, and you're the reason. He got out on his own feet, though, so he'll start another somewhere. I want to know where."

If only **Nihiloor Fled** is marked, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra turns to the first page of her notes and taps a line near the top with her pen.
>
> > "You went into his wing and he walked out of it. The pool still stands, so the programme goes on, and I need it ended."

If neither is marked, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra turns to the first page of her notes and taps a line near the top with her pen.
>
> > "Nihiloor runs the Guild's programme of implants. I've known that for a long time, and I've never been able to reach him or his pool."

**Second, a larger cell behind the Splinter.** If **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member who gave the account of the Splinter, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra draws out a second sheet covered in incident dates and lays it flat on the desk.
>
> > "The same casting signature is in Watch reports from three wards, with a different crew each time. That fits what you've told me about Manshoon. His cell is far bigger than the one you've met."

Otherwise, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra draws out a second sheet covered in incident dates and lays it flat on the desk.
>
> > "The same casting signature is in Watch reports from three wards, with a different crew each time. The Black Network has split, and the other cell is far bigger than the one you've met."

**Third, the residue or the buried thing.** Use the first case that applies. If Vajra has already given the party the **Cassalanter Villa** hook, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra touches a third line and lifts her head.
>
> > "The Tower's scrying still finds conjuration and abjuration residue under the Cassalanter villa, and it hasn't faded. I can see the outline of something. I still can't see what's inside it."

If the hook has not run and **Buried Thing Reported** is marked for a member present, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra touches a third line and lifts her head.
>
> > "Hlam told me the buried thing is waking, and I filed it without understanding it. Your account puts a vault under this city. I think that is what he felt."

If the hook has not run and **Hlam Consulted** is marked for a member present but **Buried Thing Reported** is not, read the following aloud, then let the member give Hlam's last message in their own words and finish with the reading above:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra touches a third line and looks at the member who climbed Mount Waterdeep.
>
> > "Hlam spoke to you. Tell me what he said at the end, in order, and then I'll tell you why I'm asking."

If none of these applies, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra touches a third line and lifts her head.
>
> > "My third item is a question, and I can't confirm it yet. If you learn anything about what lies beneath this city, bring it to me."

> [!gamemaster]**The Cassalanter Limit**
>
> Vajra holds external scrying evidence of conjuration and abjuration residue beneath the villa, and nothing else. She has no interior access, no document and no testimony about the family. If a member states more than they could have seen or recovered, she closes the thread with "I'll file what you saw. Not what you concluded." She files any physical evidence the party hands her and states no conclusion about its cause. Nobody in the room, Vajra included, says or implies that the family has made a bargain with anything.

Vajra then corrects one assumption. If a member named a ward, street or building as the vault's location, she answers that place. If nobody did, she gives the correction anyway. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra picks up her pen again and points it at the member who spoke about the vault.
>
> > "Leave whatever place you've guessed at alone. The Stone shows you where to go when you are ready, and you follow it."

### She Writes

When the correction is made, Vajra stops talking, and the study goes still.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra sets the pen down and goes still, with both hands flat on the desk. The study is silent except for the faint hum of the Blackstaff, and the pause runs on for longer than is comfortable. She does not look at the staff.

> [!exploration]**The Pause**
>
> Any character who watches Vajra during the pause and makes a successful **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** check determines that she settled what to do before the party finished. The pause is about what doing it will cost her. A character who fails sees a tired woman weighing a decision.

Vajra then takes a clean sheet and writes. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra pulls a clean sheet toward her and mutters at it before she begins to write.
>
> > "Half a million dragons under my city, and not one of those shitheads told me."
>
> She writes for a long minute without looking up, folds the sheet twice and melts a stick of grey wax over the lamp. Her mark goes into the wax.
>
> > "This goes to the Open Lord tonight. She needs the whole picture before she moves, and now she has it. Keep your own work quiet until she answers, because you don't want to be standing between her and four factions."
>
> She turns the sealed letter over once in her hand.
>
> > "This is the most important thing anyone has brought me, and I'll say so in the letter."

The door at the head of the stair opens by itself. Vajra goes on holding the letter.

### What Vajra Will Say

Vajra answers questions until the members stop asking. She keeps standing, and she keeps the letter in her hand.

> [!qna]**Will you be at the vault?**
>
> > "If you want me there, I'll come. I'll bring wards for whatever the vault holds, and you will give me the date as soon as you have it."

> [!qna]**What about the Lords' Alliance?**
>
> > "I've sent a discreet inquiry about the treasury records for Neverember's years. They'll answer when it suits them, and I'm not waiting for them."

> [!qna]**When will the Open Lord move?**
>
> > "I don't know, and I won't guess. Keep your work quiet, so nothing you do surprises her."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us now?**
>
> > "Carry on as you were. Bring me your honest account whenever you learn something new, including what you only suspect."

> [!qna]**Are you alright?**
>
> Vajra looks up from the letter for the first time since she sealed it.
>
> > "Irrelevant."

### Renown Opportunities

Each participating Force Grey member gains 2 base Renown for delivering the full picture, once in the campaign. Award it when Vajra seals the letter. Companions gain none.

### Aftermath

Vajra sends the letter by hand that night and does not report the Open Lord's reply. **Vault of Dragons** reads **Vajra Briefed** to decide whether the Open Lord arrives prepared or late.

A Force Grey member who missed the sitting can climb to the Tower later and give Vajra the account they heard at the table. She does not write again. She hears them out, adds the member to **Vajra Briefed**, and the member gains the 2 Renown.

The Lords' Alliance inquiry has no result in this Event. Nothing Vajra learns here changes the other factions, and none of them learns that she has written.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when Vajra has sealed the letter and answered the members' questions. It reads **Pool Destroyed**, **Nihiloor Fled**, **Buried Thing Reported**, **Hlam Consulted** and **Manshoon Named**, and it writes one outcome.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Vajra Briefed** — mark with the name of each Force Grey member present when Vajra seals the letter, and add the name of any member who gives a later sitting; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), which sets whether the Open Lord arrives prepared, and by **Force Grey Commander**, which branches on it.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> **The Full Picture** becomes available once the party has completed a lair heist and an individual Force Grey member brings Vajra the full account. Nothing else waits on it. Each member's next work is whichever mission or rank event their Renown has opened. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

A Force Grey member lays the whole Grand Game before Vajra Safahr at Blackstaff Tower, and she writes to the Open Lord.

## Summary

We laid out the four factions, the Stone, the vault and our deadline at Vajra's standing desk. She confirmed three things she had found for herself and told us to follow the Stone and leave our guesses alone. Then she went still for a long moment, and she wrote a sealed letter to the Open Lord. She asked us to keep our own work quiet until the Open Lord answers.
