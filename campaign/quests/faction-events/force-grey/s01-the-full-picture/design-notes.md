# Design Notes: The Full Picture

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B and the Force Grey guide give Vajra one big reward: when the party brings her the whole Grand Game, she goes still and begins to write. The restored draft kept that beat and added a four-element test, three findings she confirms, a correction about the vault, a staff that stirs at the sum, and a sealed message. This version keeps all of them, along with the check DCs (13 Perception, 14 Insight) and the Renown award of 2, given once in the campaign.

## What Changed

The test was a paragraph that Vajra judged by feel, and it let a party fail on a technicality. It is now a table with three routes per element, so every element has independent ways to be met, in line with the Three Clue Rule. The GM judges each element by the facts it must give, with no check. A missing element earns one question and a return visit, and nothing is lost.

The three confirmations now read earlier outcomes. Nihiloor's programme changes with **Pool Destroyed** and **Nihiloor Fled**. The larger cell is paired on **Manshoon Named**. The third finding is the Cassalanter residue only if the **Cassalanter Villa** hook has run, and otherwise Hlam's buried thing from **Consulting Hlam**, with a fallback for a member who heard Hlam but never reported. The staff stirring is narration only. Vajra never looks at it, and nobody explains it.

## Departures from the Source

The vouched non-member route is cut, so only Force Grey members are briefed and paid. The restored draft had Vajra name "an archmage at Kolat Towers" since Hlam's first report, which contradicted **Consulting Hlam**, where Hlam gives only a riddle. She now says "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named**, and she never names Kolat Towers. "Binding circles of significant scale" became "conjuration and abjuration residue" with "the outline", which keeps the Cassalanters at suspicion only. The Harper cross-reference is cut, the Lords' Alliance inquiry stays as a single answer, and Vajra says "the Open Lord" throughout. An optional *Sending* now calls a member in after the second lair heist, so the Event always fires.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- No new names. The *Sending* text, the "three wards" and the grey wax seal are invented details.
- Open: the Force Grey factions guide, `06-force-grey.md` line 30, still says "consistent with binding circles". It should read "conjuration and abjuration residue".
- Open: **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) reads **Vajra Briefed** in its old True/False form, and `arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md` line 51 still describes the Open Lord's arrival that way.
- Open: **Manshoon Named** is written by **Faction Outposts**, which is unconverted. Until it exists, the Splinter line is the default.
- Open: a member with neither **Hlam Consulted** nor a **Cassalanter Villa** hook gets a question in place of a third confirmation.
