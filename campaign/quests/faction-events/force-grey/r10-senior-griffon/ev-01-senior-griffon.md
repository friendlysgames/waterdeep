# Senior Griffon

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Force Grey member first reaches Renown 10, which usually happens as **Destroy the Intellect Factory** resolves. Bonus Renown from earlier missions can bring a member to 10 sooner, and the Event runs the same way when it does. Vajra Safahr names the rank at her standing desk in Blackstaff Tower and gives that member the Senior Griffon benefits. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Receive the Tower's *wand of secrets* for the party, if the party does not already hold one.
> - Learn how to call Ysmay Halvane, a Tower mage, to one operation in each quest.
> - Find that city officials, and any Masked Lord whose identity the member has learned, are Friendly by default.
> - Learn how to ask Vajra for written authorization to enter three kinds of restricted place.
>
> Only the member is addressed. Companions who are not Force Grey members can climb the stair and wait, and they gain nothing.

### The Sending at Noon

Hold this Event until **Junior Griffon Reached** is marked for the member. At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 10, Vajra's *Sending* finds them wherever they are. Every qualifying member hears it on their own account, so members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. A member who is away from Waterdeep hears it at the next noon after they return to the surface, and the meeting moves to the following morning.

Read the following aloud, changing the first sentence to match what the member is doing.

> [!readaloud]
>
> You are haggling over a coil of rope at a Dock Ward stall when a dry, flat voice speaks inside your head, fast and clipped and without any greeting. Nobody else at the stall reacts to it, and the seller keeps counting out your change.
>
> > "Senior Griffon. You have earned your place with me. Come to the Tower tomorrow morning. There is staff to assign and a requisition to sign."
>
> The voice stops as soon as it finishes, and you have room to answer in twenty-five words or fewer.

The *Sending* is exactly 25 words and opens with the rank's name. Vajra files a bare acknowledgment without replying, and a member who does not answer is still received at the Tower.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Vajra now plans with the party. Gray Hand and Junior Griffon tested whether the relationship was worth keeping, and Senior Griffon puts Tower staff in the party's hands, which she answers for to the Open Lord.
> - Ysmay Halvane is a mage of the Tower's staff and no spy. She reports to Vajra after every operation, and Vajra has no other eyes on the party.
> - The *wand of secrets* belongs to the party, not to the member. **The Trouble with Meloon** already gave the party one if Meloon was restored, so this Event hands one over only when the party has none.
> - Vajra will not name a Masked Lord to anyone. The courtesy applies only to a Masked Lord whose identity the member has already learned.
> - The authorization is Vajra's own judgment and not a rule of the city. She signs only what she can explain to the Watch afterward.
> - Nobody in this Event names the Splinter's master unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member. Vajra otherwise says "the Splinter" or "the other cell", and nobody names the Cassalanters at all.

### Naming the Rank

The member climbs on their own. The door opens as they reach the steps, the same as it did for the first meeting, and it opens for any companion who climbs with them.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The door of Blackstaff Tower swings open as you reach the steps, before anyone has knocked, and the stair beyond is cooler than the street. You climb past shelves and pinned maps to the study at the top. Vajra stands behind the desk with a folder under her left hand and a pen in her right.
>
> A woman in a grey academy coat waits by the window with a leather satchel on her shoulder. Vajra finishes the line she is writing before she looks up, and there is still no chair in the room.
>
> > "Senior Griffon, and you have earned it. I have read every report you filed, and they were accurate. Four arrangements come with the rank, and I will explain each one once."

> [!social]**The Blackstaff Planning**
>
> Vajra Safahr (Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her) :: the Blackstaff of Waterdeep and commander of Force Grey, who is lending out people she has to account for.
>
> Vajra is Indifferent toward the member, though she is more open than at the last meeting. She stands at the desk and gives her conclusion first, then one or two sentences of reasons. She swears rarely in front of a member and mildly when she does. She changes the subject when anyone remarks on her youth.
>
> Vajra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Senior Griffon arrangement gives the member, and what it costs.
> - Who Ysmay is and how to call her.
> - What Vajra needs to hear before she signs an authorization.
>
> She will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault, the master of the other Black Network cell, the Cassalanters, what is in the Blackstaff, Undermountain or her youth. She turns each aside with "Irrelevant." or "Next."

> [!qna]**What does Senior Griffon give me?**
>
> She counts on her fingers as she answers.
>
> > "I can give you a wand if you lack one, a mage once each quest, and the goodwill of the city's officials. I will also sign a written authorization when you ask, and Ysmay is the one that matters most."

> [!qna]**Why only once a quest?**
>
> > "She is the only mage I can spare, and I have a city to watch. You get one operation each quest with three days' notice, and I won't make it faster."

> [!qna]**Can we ask for a different mage?**
>
> > "You work with the one I assign, and Ysmay suits the way you operate. If it goes wrong in practice, come back to me with specifics and I will adjust."

> [!qna]**What if my Renown falls?**
>
> > "The rank stays with you, and the arrangements wait until you have earned your way back to ten. When you do, they start again."

> [!exploration]**The Folder**
>
> Any character who watches Vajra while she talks and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that the folder under her hand carries the member's name and is thick with dated pages. She has read all of it since the Gray Hand letter. A character who fails sees only a folder on the desk, and if anyone asks whether she is all right, she answers "Irrelevant." and moves to the next arrangement.

### The Wand of Secrets

Vajra opens the cabinet behind the desk, and what she says depends on the party's holdings. The wand is a party item, and a second Senior Griffon in the same party receives no second wand.

If **Meloon Restored** is marked and the party holds the *wand of secrets* from **The Trouble with Meloon**, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra glances at the satchel on your hip where the wand sits, then closes the cabinet again without taking anything out of it.
>
> > "You have the wand, so I will skip that part. Meloon asks after you at the Portal, and he says you have a drink coming. He phrased it more rudely than I will."

If **Meloon Lost** is marked and the party holds no wand, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra takes a wand from the cabinet and holds it out. Tower marks are carved into the grip, and her hand rests on the cabinet door a moment longer than it needs to.
>
> > "I kept this back after the Portal, and I should not have. I still have Azuredge downstairs, and I will not discuss it today."

If neither outcome applies (the party never played **The Trouble with Meloon**, or the wand was lost or sold) and the party holds no wand, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra takes a wand from the cabinet and sets it on the edge of the desk, with Tower marks carved into the grip, and then she turns the cabinet key in its lock.
>
> > "This is standard issue for people who work behind sealed doors. Sign for it with Merris on your way out, and keep it with the party."

If the party holds a wand and neither Meloon outcome is marked, Vajra says "Then you know what it does" and moves on.

> [!exploration]**The Wand**
>
> - **Who gets it:** the party, once. The member carries it and shares it with companions. Use the item's entry in the 2024 *Dungeon Master's Guide* for charges and what it detects.
> - **When it is not given:** if the party already holds a *wand of secrets*, whatever its source, Vajra gives none. A wand that was destroyed, sold or lost is not replaced.
> - **Paperwork:** Merris writes it against the member's name.

### Ysmay Halvane

Vajra turns her head toward the window, and the woman in the grey coat steps forward.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The woman crosses the study in a few steps and nods to you without offering her hand. She is slight and sharp-eyed, with ink on her thumb, and the satchel on her shoulder clinks softly with glass.
>
> > "Ysmay Halvane. I'm on the Tower's staff, and Vajra has lent me to you. Tell me where we're going and what we're facing, and I'll tell you what I can do about it."

> [!social]**The Tower's Mage**
>
> Ysmay Halvane (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: a mage of the Tower's staff in her thirties, who teaches evenings at the Academy and prefers a plan to an improvisation.
>
> Ysmay is Friendly toward anyone Vajra has vouched for, and brisk about it. She asks about exits, civilians and who else will be armed, and she writes the answers on a card. She swears rarely and mildly. She is loyal to the Tower before the member, and she says so on the first day.
>
> Ysmay is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What she casts, and the places she will not cast it.
> - The operation: where, who is there, how the party leaves.
> - How the party reaches her, and how long she needs.
>
> She will not discuss Vajra's private affairs, the Tower's other staff or the Blackstaff.

> [!qna]**What can you do?**
>
> > "I cast what a Tower mage casts, and I will cover you with a shield or a counterspell before I attack. I won't throw a fireball into a crowded street or a room you're standing in."

> [!qna]**Will you fight the Watch?**
>
> > "No, I won't raise a hand against the Watch or a Lord. If you order it, I will tell Vajra the same evening."

> [!qna]**Who do you report to?**
>
> > "I report to Vajra when it's over, and I tell her what I saw and what I did. I don't repeat what you say to each other when you think I'm not listening."

> [!qna]**Can you go below the city?**
>
> > "Yes, as long as we start from the Yawning Portal and you tell me before we go. I will bring rope and a spare lantern."

> [!exploration]**Calling Ysmay**
>
> Each Senior Griffon member can call Ysmay to one operation in each quest.
>
> - **Contact:** Vajra at the standing desk, or a written request left on it. If Vajra is out, she answers by *Sending* within a day.
> - **The request:** the member names the quest, the operation, the place to meet and the hour. A request with no named operation is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Notice:** three days. A request with less notice is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **The place:** a surface point in Waterdeep that the member chooses. If the operation begins by descending the shaft into Undermountain, the place is the Yawning Portal.
> - **Arrival:** Ysmay waits at the place at the hour named and goes out with the member. The operation runs until the member is back at a safe lodging or dismisses her.
> - **Limit:** one call per member per quest. A quest is one of the named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**. Events, faction missions and rank events are not quests. The call counts against the quest the party is in when the member asks, and a call not made before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Withdrawing:** a call withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent.
> - **Two members:** Ysmay serves one operation at a time. If two members ask for the same operation, she serves both, and only the first to ask spends a call. A second request for overlapping dates is refused, and nothing is spent.
> - **Debrief:** Ysmay reports to Vajra the same evening. Vajra does not question the member about it.
> - **Companions:** Ysmay takes orders from the member who called her and from nobody else. A companion who is not a member cannot call her, and Vajra does not answer a request made in the member's name.
> - **If she dies:** Vajra suspends the benefit for a tenday and then assigns a second mage, Dovrin Tesk (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him), who uses the same block and the same limits. Vajra does not assign a third.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and Ysmay is withdrawn until the Renown is restored.

> [!gamemaster]**Ysmay as an Ally**
>
> Ysmay uses the 2024 **Mage** stat block as printed (CR 6, AC 15, 81 Hit Points). She has three Arcane Burst attacks, *Fireball* twice a day, *Invisibility* twice a day, *Cone of Cold*, *Fly* and *Misty Step* three times a day, and Protective Magic three times a day for *Counterspell* or *Shield*, but she has no *Contingency*.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, her Power is 80 for a party of levels 1 to 4, 65 for levels 5 to 10 and 50 for levels 11 and above, following the party's level. Add that to the party's Power when she fights and recalculate the encounter. The first-turn knockout bonus does not apply to allies. An ally who is on the way does not lower the difficulty.
>
> With Ysmay at Tier 2, a party of three, four or five has Power 161, 193 or 225 at 5th level, 170, 205 or 240 at 6th, 188, 229 or 270 at 7th, and 197, 241 or 285 at 8th. Count her in full when you build the encounter.
>
> #### Ysmay's Tactics
>
> To begin combat, Ysmay finds cover and casts *Invisibility* on herself or on the member. During combat, she:
>
> - Uses *Shield* or *Counterspell* before she attacks, whichever protects the member who called her.
> - Targets the enemy the member is attacking, then the nearest caster.
> - Casts *Fireball* only on a target at least 40 feet from every ally and never in a crowded street or a closed room with the party in it.
> - Uses *Misty Step* to leave a melee, and *Cone of Cold* on three or more enemies in a line.
>
> She withdraws at 40 Hit Points or when the member calls a retreat. She does not fight the City Watch, a Lord or a Masked Lord, and she lowers her hands if the party does.

If Ysmay meets the member for the first operation, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Ysmay is waiting at the place you named with her satchel on her shoulder, and she is writing in a small book as you approach. She closes it when she sees you and tucks it into her coat.
>
> > "Halvane. Vajra gave me the job and the place, so tell me how you're going in and where you want me. I will tell you whether I see a problem."

### The City's Courtesy

Vajra moves on to the third arrangement while the member is still looking at the wand or the mage.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra taps the folder once with her pen and then sets the pen down on the desk beside the slip she has been writing.
>
> > "City officials will be Friendly to you while your standing with me is current, the way they would be to a Watch captain with clean hands. Don't treat it as a license."

> [!exploration]**Friendly by Default**
>
> - **Who:** any city official acting in an office, such as a Watch officer, a ward clerk, a Palace clerk or a magistrate, and any Masked Lord whose identity the member has already learned. Vajra does not name any Masked Lord, and the benefit does not reveal one.
> - **What Friendly means:** ordinary help and a fair hearing. It does not excuse a crime, and an official who has watched the member commit one is no longer Friendly toward that member.
> - **Stacking:** it does not stack with the Friendly toward the Watch that a Gray Hand already has, or with a Lords' Alliance or Harper courtesy. An official is Friendly, and no higher.
> - **Misuse:** a member who uses the courtesy to cover a crime loses it for a tenday when Vajra hears of it.
> - **Companions:** the courtesy belongs to the member. A companion standing next to the member is treated as a stranger.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and the courtesy is suspended until the Renown is restored.

### Written Authorization

Vajra pulls a blank slip from the folder and holds it up so the member can see it.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vajra lays a narrow slip on the desk and writes a single line along the top, then stops with the pen above the paper.
>
> > "For three kinds of place, I'll write permission: the City of the Dead at night, a sealed Watch evidence vault, or a private armory. Come when it isn't a crisis, and tell me what you want and why you want it."

> [!exploration]**What Vajra Needs to Hear**
>
> The member comes to the standing desk in person and answers four questions: which place, on what night, for what purpose and what they will take away. Vajra will not sign a request that leaves one of the four blank.
>
> - **The check:** a member who explains clearly makes a DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check. On a success, Vajra signs. On a failure, she says "Come back with the rest", and the member can ask again after one day with a fuller answer. A member who has answered all four questions plainly and in order needs no check.
> - **Crisis:** if any villain faction is at Lockdown toward the party, Vajra refuses every request with "Not this week" and spends nothing.
> - **The slip:** it names the member and up to four companions, one place and one night from dusk to dawn, and carries her mark. The officer on duty reads it and opens the gate, the vault or the armory.
> - **What it does not do:** nothing leaves a vault or an armory unless the slip lists it. A slip for the City of the Dead does not cover a crime committed there.
> - **Limit:** one slip per member per tenday. A slip not used is returned to her unspent.
> - **Misuse:** a slip used for another place, night or purpose is torn up, and the member loses authorization for a tenday.
> - **Companions:** a companion who is not a Force Grey member cannot ask for a slip, but can be named on the member's slip.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and authorization is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!qna]**What are the conditions for the authorization?**
>
> > "There are none. There is what I will sign and what I will not, so come with a clear need and I will tell you where it lands."

> [!qna]**Can you authorize a private house?**
>
> > "No, a private house is a magistrate's affair. I give you the three places I can stand behind in front of the Watch."

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Senior Griffon earns further Renown from the missions and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Force Grey page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions what Vajra said and how to reach Ysmay. The mage, the courtesy and the authorization stay individual benefits, and Vajra and Ysmay turn away a companion who asks in the member's name, though the wand is the one item the whole party shares.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard all four arrangements, has met Ysmay and has left the Tower.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Senior Griffon Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Vajra names the rank at the desk; read by **Force Grey**, which checks it before naming the next rank. Track separately for each member the quest of the last Ysmay call, the day of the last authorization slip and whether the courtesy is suspended.
>
> This Event also reads **Junior Griffon Reached** (the hold), **Meloon Restored** and **Meloon Lost** (one line at the wand) and **Manshoon Named** (the speaker's wording).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All four arrangements are active for this member from the next morning. **Force Grey** occurs when this member reaches Renown 25. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Vajra Safahr names a Force Grey member a Senior Griffon and introduces Ysmay Halvane, a Tower mage, along with a wand, official courtesy and written authorization.

## Summary

We were named Senior Griffons at Vajra's standing desk after a *Sending* told us to come. She gave the party a *wand of secrets* if it lacked one, and she introduced Ysmay Halvane, a Tower mage who will join one operation each quest if we ask three days ahead. City officials are Friendly to each of us now, and Vajra will sign a slip for the City of the Dead, a sealed evidence vault or a private armory if we explain ourselves clearly.
