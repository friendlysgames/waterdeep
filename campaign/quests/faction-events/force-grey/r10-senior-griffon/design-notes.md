# Design Notes: Senior Griffon

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B and the Factions Guide give Senior Griffon four things: a Tower mage for one operation per quest, a *wand of secrets* if the party lacks one, Friendly city officials and Masked Lords, and written authorization at DC 12 Charisma during a non-crisis period for the City of the Dead at night, sealed evidence vaults and private Watch armories. The restored draft named the mage Aldris Maeven, gave her no limits, and said "skip" when the party held a wand. It had no procedures and no Ally Power.

## What Changed

Each benefit is now a per-member procedure with a contact, a notice rule, a limit and a loss rule. Ysmay Halvane uses the 2024 **Mage** block with Power 80, 65 and 50 by tier, taken from the mechanics reference. She is called with three days' notice, once per quest, at a surface point or the Yawning Portal. The authorization is a short interview with four questions, a DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check with a plain-answer fallback, and a stated crisis rule: any villain faction at Lockdown. A failed check sends the member away for a day and settles nothing.

The wand is a party item, credited to **The Trouble with Meloon**. This event gives it only if the party has none, and the two Meloon outcomes add one line each. **Meloon Lost** is the case where M3 withheld the wand, so Vajra hands it over here.

## Departures from the Source

The Masked Lord clause now reads "a Masked Lord whose identity the member has already learned", because the Harper rank events keep those identities secret until Renown 50. The *Sending* runs exactly 25 words and opens with the rank's name; the restored one ran 14. The restored draft's "Aldris Maeven" is retired because it collides with Aldric Talmost and Aldric in the staff guide.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Dovrin Tesk (Ysmay's replacement), the Dock Ward rope stall, the four authorization questions, the crisis rule, the one-slip-per-tenday limit, the courtesy's misuse rule and Ysmay's evening teaching at the Academy. Ysmay is voiced from the event text, with no profile. Her Notable Figures page does not exist. Whether Ysmay's call in a quest that has already ended counts is settled by the quest-count line in the procedure. The Factions Guide, the Players' Guide and the GM Guide all say "the party receives a wand" and give no Masked Lord condition, and they need the same wording.
