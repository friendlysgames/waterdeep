# Design Notes: Lioncrown

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B and the Factions Guide give Lioncrown the Open Lord's backing, one charge dropped or a wanted status cleared, and a dedicated attaché. The Factions Guide adds three honest answers from Laeral. The restored draft added Sevel Dastar, a private request about two handlers and a cartography team, and a list of freeform answers. It branched on whether **Vault of Dragons** had resolved, and it had Laeral say that her power was "less than my enemies assume, more than my staff knows". Its outcome **Undermountain Commission Accepted** was a True or False heading.

## What Changed

The summons is now a vellum note carried by a Watch runner on the first day the member is above ground, because nothing reaches a member in Undermountain. The ceremony branches on **Laeral Told of Jalester** and **Laeral Doubts the Party** and never on the Vault. Sevel delivers the summons and is reintroduced as an attaché if Jalester was moved. The three answers are a table of fixed rows with a default line, a refusal line and a gate for each of **Manshoon Named** and **Jarlaxle Unmasked**. The charge has an order of precedence, a list of exclusions and a held-benefit rule. Sevel's attaché role has a seal, a contact, a Power total and tactics. The "political backing" is defined as the charge, the answers and the seal, with no condition attached.

The missing agents are Joroth Brighthelm's team from *Dungeon of the Mad Mage*. The Runestone is Jalester's separate errand and stays outside the card. The "twelve" Masked Lords are cut, and Laeral names none.

## Departures from the Source

Laeral admits her decline here, under user decision 3, and nowhere else. She does it privately, after Jalester and Sevel have left the room, because neither knows. The audience room is compared with the annex from **An Audience with the Open Lord** (40 by 24 feet against 20 by 16). The attaché belongs to the member who accepts him, where Appendix B assigns him to the party. In the adventure Joroth waits at the Yawning Portal and asks adventurers to find Falkir's Fist. Here he has already gone below, so the Eye of the Spider hook reaches the party through the card.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Telsa Corlis, the three unnamed mapmakers, the code phrases "Is the lamp lit?" and "Is the ledger closed?", and the chalked hand-in-a-circle. Also invented are Sevel's alignment (Lawful Neutral), his brass ring, the once-a-tenday seal and the 3-day writ delay. So are the charge exclusions, the 20 by 16 foot annex with a six-seat table, the 19:00 summons and the Level 3 last dispatch. The handler's acceptance of the Eye of the Spider is invented, and the 3 Renown award is the adventure's own.

Laeral's Notable Figures page says moon elf, while Waterdeep: Dragon Heist has her human. This Event follows the page. The luck blade offered with the Runestone errand is not carried over.
