# Six Bells at the Muleskull

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Investigation Event begins when Jalester Silvermane briefs a Lords' Alliance member at the well table in the Yawning Portal. It ends on the evening of the eighth day, when the party knows who is selling the crew's schedule. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Jalester's brief and meet Mareva Coile and Berna at the Muleskull Tavern at six bells.
> - Walk the Trades Ward circuit with the crew, and see how it works through short scenes on Days 2, 4, 6 and 7.
> - Follow the watcher on Tallowmere Street, read the schedule scrap that Herath finds, and open Mareva's duty book. Each of these three leads names Delvon Harsk.
> - Confront Delvon, warn him off, or leave him for Jalester to deal with.
> - Earn Berna's regard by how the party behaves on Days 2, 6 and 7.
>
> Only Lords' Alliance members attend Jalester's brief and debrief, and their companions can help with everything in between.

> [!gamemaster]**Who Knows What**
>
> - Delvon Harsk, a route supervisor, sells the crew's daily timings to Pirn because he owes a moneylender 300 gp. He did not know anyone would be hurt until three workers were beaten, and he has found no way to stop.
> - Pirn is a street agent of Xanathar's Guild who works at the edge of what his handler permits. The handler does not know Delvon's name, and Pirn never says whom he works for.
> - Neither Mareva nor Berna suspects Delvon. Berna keeps her own counsel about the beatings and does not guess at causes.
> - Jalester knows that the three beatings fell on nights when the crew's hours had changed. He does not know who sold the hours, and he sends no Guard escort and uses no overlook on this errand.
> - Berna's crew is clean. Other Dungsweepers' Guild crews are a separate matter, and **Three Nights** in the Bregan D'aerthe set covers one of them.

### The Brief

> [!gamemaster]**Beside the Well**
>
> The corner table stands beside the Yawning Portal's well, so anything said at it may reach something below the Portal, and Jalester does not know.

Jalester sends no note for this task, because **Lords' Alliance First Meeting** promised the first task at the well table in the evening. He waits there at dusk on the first evening after a member reaches 2nd level, and the Event begins when that member reaches the table. He briefs members only, and companions take stools at the bar where Durnan can see them.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom is loud with adventurers arguing over the long tables, and cold air rises from the well past the windlass. Durnan looks up as you come in, nods toward the corner and goes back to polishing a mug. Jalester Silvermane sits beside the well with his notebook open, and he rises to shake hands with each of you in turn.
>
> > "Thank you for coming, and please sit. I have a first task for you, and it is plain work, so I won't dress it up. A gang war has made the Dock Ward dangerous, and three workers of the Dungsweepers' Guild were beaten this week. The Alliance has offered the Guild protection, and I would like you to be that protection."

> [!readaloud]
>
> He turns a page of the notebook and reads from it without lifting the pencil.
>
> > "Meet the crew at the Muleskull Tavern, on Ship Street in the Dock Ward, at six bells tomorrow. There are four sweepers and a foreman named Berna, and they work the Trades Ward circuit. Walk it with them every morning for a tenday and keep them safe while they work. I want your report at this table on the evening of the tenth day, and I will not move that date. Do any of you know the cart yard behind the Muleskull?"

> [!social]**The Agent at the Well**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: a lean Lords' Alliance field agent in a dark doublet, who writes down every answer and briefs members alone.
>
> Jalester is Friendly and a little tired, and he keeps the notebook open while he talks. He asks short questions, says "Understood." when he accepts an answer and thanks each member formally for coming. He swears rarely and only at himself, so a curse from him is a sign that something has gone badly wrong.
>
> Jalester is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The crew, the route and the Guild's coordinator, Mareva Coile.
> - The three beatings, and what the Alliance does and does not know about them.
> - The deadline, and what the Alliance gives for a tenday of plain work.
> - What a member should do if the job goes wrong.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr or the vault, and he has no name for whoever beat the workers.

> [!qna]**Who are we guarding?**
>
> > "Five people, a foreman named Berna and four sweepers, who work the Trades Ward circuit. Their coordinator is Mareva Coile, and she will give you the schedule. She is sharp with strangers, and I would not take it personally."

> [!qna]**Why would anyone hurt sweepers?**
>
> > "I don't know yet. Each of the three was beaten on a night when the crew's hours had changed. That may be chance, or it may be that someone knows the timetable, and I would like to know which."

> [!qna]**Why does the Alliance care?**
>
> > "If the sweepers stop working, the Trades Ward is knee-deep in filth within a tenday, and the city's temper goes with it. The Watch is stretched thin, and the Alliance does what the Watch can't be seen doing."

> [!qna]**What do we get?**
>
> > "Nothing in coin, I am afraid. The Alliance counts your work in standing, and I will decide how much after I have your report. I write all of it down, and the Open Lord reads what I write."

> [!qna]**What if something goes wrong?**
>
> > "Send for the Watch first and for me second. If a message can't wait, leave it with Durnan for Silvermane's friends, and he will pass it on without a word."

> [!qna]**What if we miss a morning?**
>
> Jalester turns a page before he answers.
>
> > "Tell me by a note at the bar, and tell me why, and I will understand. If I haven't heard from you by the tenth evening, I will take the job as abandoned. Is that clear?"

A member who refuses the task triggers **Suspension and Dismissal**, which reads the deadline above, and Jalester says "Understood." and closes the notebook without argument.

### Six Bells at the Muleskull

The Muleskull Tavern stands on Ship Street in the Dock Ward and opens before dawn for dock workers. The Dungsweepers' Guild leases the cart yard behind it, so the crew gathers there at six bells each morning and walks to the Trades Ward. Mareva Coile waits at the corner table on the first morning with the supervisors' rotation, and Berna stands with the sweepers by the door. Delvon Harsk, a heavyset supervisor in a grey coat, counts the crew out of the yard each morning and back in each evening.

> [!readaloud]
>
> At six bells the Muleskull smells of strong tea and last night's spilled beer, and cold salt air comes in each time the door opens. Four sweepers stand by the door with brooms and rakes beside a wheeled cart that carries a large covered bin. A stout dwarf woman with a grey braid stands in front of them and looks you over without any friendliness.
>
> > "You're the escort."

> [!readaloud]
>
> At the corner table a woman of about forty-five sits over a cup of tea with a rolled sheet under her hand. She does not stand when you come over, and she slides the sheet toward you before anyone has sat down.
>
> > "That's the supervisors' rotation. Read it in the cart, because we start in ten minutes."

> [!social]**The Coordinator at the Corner Table**
>
> Mareva Coile (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, she/her) :: a sharp-tempered route coordinator of about forty-five who holds the crew's contracts and resents needing outside help.
>
> Mareva is Indifferent to the party and does not hide it. She keeps the Guild's duty book in the yard office, speaks to Jalester when she must and does not apologize for doubting people. She softens only toward those who work, and she is blunt about what a failure would cost her crew.
>
> Mareva is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The route, and when the crew finishes each day.
> - The three workers who were beaten.
> - The four supervisors who set the day's timings.
> - What happens to the city if her sweepers stop working.
>
> She will not open the duty book on the first morning, and she will not discuss the Guild's money.

> [!qna]**What happened to your workers?**
>
> > "Three of mine were beaten on their way home from the yard, on three nights this tenday. One has a broken arm. Nobody was robbed, and that is what bothers me."

> [!qna]**Who sets the hours?**
>
> > "Four supervisors set the day's timings and hand them to me. They are Delvon Harsk, Hadrin Wenlow, Bremma Dunnet and Cass Fildura. I trust all four, and I'll thank you not to upset them."

> [!qna]**Why do you need us?**
>
> > "I didn't ask for you. If my workers stop showing up because they're scared, the city fills with filth in a week, and that is worse than a black eye. So I'll take the help, and you'll take my instructions on the route."

> [!social]**The Foreman by the Door**
>
> Berna (Lawful Neutral, Shield Dwarf, she/her) :: the crew's foreman, a stout dwarf of about sixty with a grey braid. She has worked twenty years for the Guild and seventeen on this circuit.
>
> Berna is economical and does not make small talk. She expects the escort to stay close, look capable and keep out from under the cart. She softens over the tenday as she decides the party is useful, and it shows as acknowledgment and never as warmth.
>
> Berna is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What she expects from the escort on the route.
> - The circuit, which she has walked for seventeen years.
> - The crew, if someone asks about a sweeper by name.
>
> She will not talk about the beatings beyond what she saw herself.

> [!qna]**What do you want from us?**
>
> > "Stay close and look like you can handle a drunk. Keep out from under the cart, and don't talk to my sweepers while they're working."

> [!qna]**How long have you worked this route?**
>
> > "Seventeen years on this circuit and twenty with the Guild. I know every drain between the Market and Coin Alley, and I'd know if one smelled wrong."

### Eight Days in the Trades Ward

The crew walks the Trades Ward circuit from the Market through the cooperage lanes and out toward Coin Alley. It finishes at the yard behind the Muleskull before dark. Days 1 and 3 pass without a scene of their own, and the scenes below happen on the days given, so the party can attend all of them.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The crew works the Trades Ward at an even pace, with a sweeper on each side of the cart and Berna out front. Carters shout at the cart to move over, and shopkeepers pile their sweepings by the kerb without looking at anyone. By midmorning the bin is full and the lane behind you is clean, and Berna turns the cart toward the next street.

> [!gamemaster]**Berna's Regard**
>
> Berna's regard is counted in Trust marks, and the party can earn one on each of Days 2, 6 and 7 by the conditions given in each scene. **Day Nine** reads the total when it decides whether to mark **Berna Trusts the Party**.

### Day 2: The Watch Check

Near the Market, a Watch officer stops the crew for a routine check. He treats the sweepers with open contempt and takes little notice of the escort.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Near the Market a Watch officer steps in front of the cart with one hand raised and asks Berna for the crew's tally and the Guild's seal. He speaks to her in a bored voice, never looks at the sweepers and gives you one glance before he looks away. Berna hands over the papers with her jaw tight and her eyes level, and he waves the cart on.

If a member objects to the officer or steps between him and the crew, read the following two blocks on:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Two blocks on, Berna keeps her eyes on the cobbles and speaks without turning her head.
>
> > "Don't make it worse."

She means it as advice and not as a rebuke. The party earns a **Trust mark** if nobody argues with the officer, or if whoever did stops after her remark. A member who keeps arguing earns none for Day 2.

### Day 4: Herath's Purse

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Market alley is narrow and wet from last night's rain. Herath, the youngest of the sweepers, stops at a doorstep and picks up a small leather purse that someone dropped in a hurry. He counts twelve gold pieces and walks the purse to the Watch post at the end of the lane without a word to anyone. When he comes back to his broom he sees you watching, and he shrugs.
>
> > "It's not mine."

### Day 4: The Watcher

> [!exploration]**The Watcher on Tallowmere Street**
>
> Any character who watches the windows along Tallowmere Street and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check notices a lean young man in a brown jerkin. He keeps appearing at different second-floor windows ahead of the crew and withdraws each time the route turns a corner.
>
> - **On a failure:** Berna points him out on Day 5. She says he has stood at a window ahead of the crew since the first day, but she offers no guess about him.
> - **If a character approaches him openly:** He leaves by the back stair, and the window stays empty for the rest of the day. Pirn does not fight, and he uses the Spy's Cunning Action to run.

### Day 6: Berna Briefs

At the midday break Berna eats at a corner stall with the crew and talks about the dangers of the work. Herath found a crumpled sheet in the gutter under the Tallowmere Street window on Day 5. Berna hands it to the party at the end of her briefing.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The crew stops for midday at a corner stall that sells hot pies and weak ale, and the sweepers sit on upturned buckets with their brooms against the wall. Berna eats standing and watches the street, and she does not look at you or ask for reassurance when she begins to speak.
>
> > "We go into alleys nobody else goes into, and we move slow. We carry brooms and rakes, not weapons. If something happens to us, the Watch isn't quick about coming."

> [!readaloud]
>
> She wipes her hands on her apron and holds out a creased square of paper.
>
> > "The lad found this in the gutter under that window on Tallowmere Street. It isn't ours, and I don't know whose it is."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us on the route?**
>
> > "Two of you at the back of the cart and nobody in front of me. I lead, and you follow my pace. Anybody who wants to run ahead can do it on their own time."

A member who sits with the crew, asks Berna what she wants and then keeps to her order for the rest of the tenday earns a **Trust mark**. A party that ignores the order and goes ahead of her earns none for Day 6.

### Day 7: The Guild Dispute

> [!readaloud]
>
> At the corner of a lane near the cooperages a man with the night's drink still on him plants himself in front of the cart. He tells Herath that the alley is his guild's to sweep, and two carters slow down to watch. Berna steps in front of the cart and speaks in an even voice.
>
> > "That alley has been in dispute for six years, and no magistrate has ruled on it. Until one does, whoever cleans it first has cleaned it. Go home and sleep, friend. We'll leave you the cobbles tomorrow."

> [!exploration]**The Lane Ordinance**
>
> Any character who steps beside Berna and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (History) check recalls the city ordinance on shared lanes. The character can state it in one sentence, Berna nods, the man leaves, and the party earns a **Trust mark**.
>
> - **On a failure:** Berna finishes before the character can speak. The party earns no mark for Day 7 and suffers no penalty.

### Three Leads to Delvon

Delvon Harsk is the leak, and three independent leads name him. Any one is enough to reach him, and a failed check on one lead never closes the others. The party can work them on any day from Day 4 to Day 8.

> [!exploration]**Lead One: Following Pirn**
>
> Pirn leaves his window at the end of each shift and walks to the lane behind the cart yard. Any character who follows him from the window and makes a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check sees him wait in a doorway. Delvon Harsk comes out of the yard office, looks up the lane and walks away. Pirn then crosses to the back wall, works a loose brick free and takes out a folded sheet.
>
> - **On a failure:** Pirn notices the tail and loses it in the Market crowd. He is back at the same window the next morning, and the character can try again on any later evening.
> - **If a character closes with him in the lane:** A successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check holds him. Pirn does not fight, gives up Delvon's name at once and asks to be handed to the Watch and not to the Guild. He knows nothing of his handler, and on a failure he reaches the roofs and is gone.

> [!exploration]**Lead Two: The Schedule Scrap**
>
> The scrap Berna hands over on Day 6 is half a page of rough timings, marked by ward and hour for the next two mornings. Any character who compares it with the rota on the yard office door and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the same hand. The crossed sevens and looped d's match the entries Delvon Harsk makes.
>
> - **On a failure:** Berna looks over the character's shoulder, taps the sevens and says whose hand they are.

> [!exploration]**Lead Three: The Duty Book**
>
> Mareva keeps a book in the yard office that records which supervisor held the timing sheet each night. Any character who asks to see it and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check gets her to open it after a shift. Delvon held the sheet on the night of each beating, and no other supervisor held it on more than one of them.
>
> - **On a failure:** Mareva asks why the character wants it. Once the character tells her that the beatings were timed, she opens the book.

### The Supervisor in the Yard

Delvon checks the sweepers in at the yard at the end of every shift, so the party can speak to him on any evening from Day 4. The scene runs on the evening of Day 8 by default. If the party has no lead by then, skip to **Concluding the Event**.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cart rolls into the yard behind the Muleskull as the light goes, and the sweepers hang their brooms on the wall. Delvon Harsk stands at the gate with a tally board, ticking off each name. When you stop in front of him he lowers the board and looks at the cobbles.

> [!social]**The Supervisor in the Yard**
>
> Delvon Harsk (Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: a heavyset route supervisor in a grey coat. He sells the crew's hours to pay a debt and has waited for someone to catch him.
>
> Delvon is Friendly out of habit and ashamed. He knows he is caught the moment the party presses him, and he explains himself where most guilty men would lie. He will go wherever the party tells him.
>
> Delvon is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The debt, and the young man who pays him.
> - What he handed over and how often.
> - What he thinks will happen to him.
>
> He knows Pirn only by that name, and he does not know whom Pirn works for.
>
> Any character who asks him about the timings and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that he cannot meet their eyes. His answer runs too long for an honest denial, so the character knows he is hiding a payment. On a failure the character hears only a supervisor who is nervous about the Alliance.

> [!qna]**Have you passed the timings to someone?**
>
> > "Yes. A young man called Pirn pays me every week for the day's hours, and I owe a moneylender three hundred gold. I told myself the hours were harmless. I didn't know they'd hurt anyone, because I thought they only wanted to know when the route ran quiet."

> [!qna]**How does he pay you?**
>
> > "I leave a folded sheet behind a loose brick in the yard wall at dusk, and the coin is under it in the morning. I've never seen him take it. I only know the name he gave me."

> [!qna]**What will you do now?**
>
> > "Whatever you tell me. Mareva will want me in front of the Watch, and she isn't wrong."

> [!exploration]**What Becomes of Delvon**
>
> The party chooses one of three answers on Day 8, and **Day Nine** opens on the result.
>
> - **Take him to Mareva and the Watch:** Mareva sends the yard boy for a constable, and Delvon is held for conspiracy. He is later sentenced to three months' labour, and the beatings stop.
> - **Warn him off:** Delvon quits the Guild the next morning and leaves his keys on Mareva's desk. The beatings stop, and Mareva hears the reason from Berna and says nothing about it to the party.
> - **Leave him for Jalester:** Delvon works the next day as usual, and Pirn collects the next sheet. The beatings do not stop, and a sweeper from another crew is hurt on the night of Day 8.

### Concluding the Event

The Event ends on the evening of the eighth day, whatever the party has learned. Continue with **Day Nine**.

## Overview

Jalester Silvermane sends the party to guard a crew of sweepers on their Trades Ward circuit. Eight days of slow work turn up a watcher at a window and a leak in the crew's schedule.

## Summary

We met Mareva Coile and Berna at the Muleskull and walked the Trades Ward with their crew for eight days. A young man kept appearing in windows ahead of us, and we worked out who was selling the crew's schedule. Berna expects us at six bells again tomorrow.
