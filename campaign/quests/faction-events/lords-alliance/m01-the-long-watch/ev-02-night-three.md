# Night Three

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat/Investigation Event begins on the third night of the tenday detail. In this Event:
>
> - Three Xanathar Guild Thugs and Pirn ambush Mareva on Shank Street during her return from a late supervision check.
> - Pirn observes from twenty feet back and flees when the Thugs break. He can be tracked or captured.
> - If captured, Pirn names Delvon Harsk under light pressure.

### The Ambush

The attack comes from a side alley on **Shank Street, Dock Ward**, as **Mareva** returns from a late supervision check at the Dockside pumping station. Three Thugs emerge from the alley mouth. **Pirn** holds back twenty feet behind the group, in shadow, observing.

> [!npc-narrative]**Pirn**
> A young man — the same one who has been appearing at second-story windows and ahead of the route's circuit for three days. Tonight he holds back twenty feet behind the Thugs, in shadow, observing. He does not enter the fight. He is here to watch, not to act.

The Thugs fight until two of their three fall, then break south toward the harbor. Pirn, if not intercepted, slips into a service lane and disappears into the Dock Ward.

> [!combat]+ **Night Three Encounter**
>
> **Enemies:** 3 Thugs + Pirn (Spy stat block; Pirn stays out of melee)
>
> **Pirn's behavior:** Pirn does not engage in melee. He observes from twenty feet back. When the first Thug falls, he edges toward the service lane. When the second Thug falls, he runs.
>
> **Catching Pirn:** **DC 15 Survival** to track him through the Dock Ward alleys, or **DC 14 Athletics** to run him down. He is fast and knows the district. A failed Athletics check means he reaches a pre-set rope drop and is gone for the night — he resurfaces in the Dock Ward and can be found again with a day of investigation.

### If Pirn Is Captured

**Pirn** is a Xanathar Guild street agent, not an ideologue. He is motivated by coin and has no particular loyalty to his employer when cornered.

> [!dialogue]**Who's been giving you the route schedule?**
> "Route supervisor. Been giving me the schedule every tenday. Coin. I don't know what he owes or to who."

**DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation)**, or **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** with a specific promise of Watch leniency on the table (same result, volunteered more easily).

Pirn will not give the name of his handler above him in the Guild. He doesn't know it — that is by design.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Pirn's Downstream Value
> A captured and thoroughly questioned Pirn can name the Xanathar Guild street agent who authorized his operation: **Krentz**, who appears in **Trollskull Alley**'s sewers sequence. This is a bonus connection — the mission does not require identifying Krentz. If the party brings this name to the Watch or to Jalester before **Trollskull Alley**, it flags the Xanathar Guild's Dock Ward cell for increased Watch scrutiny during the first two quests.

### Act 3 — Finding the Source

After the attack, the question is how the ambush was timed so precisely. Three paths to the same answer:

#### The Attack Site

A **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** check at the attack site finds a folded paper in one of the Thugs' pockets — a rough schedule notated by ward and time. The handwriting does not match any of the surviving Guild workers, but it does match one of the supervisor duty rosters.

#### Questioning Mareva

> [!dialogue]**Who has access to the real-time route schedule?**
> Only four route supervisors: Delvon Harsk, Orryn Saltwick, Bremma Dunnet, and Cass Fildura.

**DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** required.

#### Questioning the Supervisors

When the party interviews the four supervisors, a **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** applied to **Delvon Harsk** reveals he cannot make eye contact when asked whether he has shared routing information with anyone outside the Guild. His answer is too long.

Delvon knows he is caught the moment the party focuses on him. He has been waiting for this and does not deny the payments when pressed directly — he explains them. He owed 300 gp to a moneylender. He didn't know what the Guild did with the information. He knows now.

> > "I didn't know they'd hurt anyone. I thought they just wanted to know when the route ran quiet."

He is not lying about not knowing. He is also not innocent.

### Resolution

**Delvon arrested and turned over to Watch:** Mareva is informed. She is quiet for a long moment, then says:

> > "Make sure they know he's not a killer. Just an idiot."

Delvon is charged with conspiracy and sentenced to three months' labor. His debt to the moneylender is restructured under Watch oversight. If Pirn was also captured, the Xanathar Guild contact chain is severed; if Pirn escaped, the attacks stop regardless once Delvon is removed from the route.

**Delvon warned off but not arrested:** He quits the guild the next day and disappears into the Trades Ward. The attacks stop. Jalester accepts this outcome but notes it in his assessment of the party — he gives them harder assignments going forward.

**PCs never identify the insider:** The attacks continue through the tenday. Mareva's workers organize their own protection rotation by Day 7, and the detail becomes redundant. Jalester pays the renown but his internal assessment drops a notch. Harder assignments follow.

### The Final Morning — Herath's Note

On the final day of the tenday, **Herath** presses a folded note into the party's hands as they part ways. The note gives the location of a sewer access point in the Trades Ward that his crew uses — a back-route into the sewer system that bypasses the Watch checkpoint on Coin Alley. He offers it with a shrug.

> > "In case you ever need to move fast."

He is not a Harper asset or an Alliance contact. He is a person who was helped and is returning the favor in the only currency he has.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Herath's Note — Downstream Use
> Herath's sewer access point becomes a viable entry path for **Xanathar's Lair** (the Sewer Route). If the party retained the note, they have a head start on that approach before they begin **Xanathar's Lair** reconnaissance. The note is not required — the sewer route remains available through other means — but it is the most organic path, arriving before the party knows they will need it.

## Overview

Night Three brings the ambush the party has been bracing for. When it ends, the harder question remains: how did they know exactly when and where to hit?

## Read Aloud

> [!narrative]
> Mareva is returning from the pumping station alone. She told you she would be thirty minutes. It has been forty-five. The Dock Ward at this hour is quiet in a specific way — not empty, just watching.
>
> The alley mouth on Shank Street opens without warning. Three figures move fast and certain, already between you and her.

## Summary

On Night Three of the detail, three Xanathar Guild thugs and a scout named Pirn ambushed Mareva on Shank Street. The party broke the ambush and had the opportunity to pursue and capture Pirn. An investigation uncovered **Delvon Harsk**, a route supervisor selling scheduling information to cover a debt. The tenday ended with the insider addressed, and Herath pressed a sewer access note into the party's hands on the final morning.
