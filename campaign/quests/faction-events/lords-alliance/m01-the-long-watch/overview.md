# Lords' Alliance Mission 1 — The Long Watch

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available from the campaign's first session. No prior Lords' Alliance missions required.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Involved Characters
- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance) — field agent; delivers the mission in person at the Yawning Portal; assesses the party's professionalism quietly across the tenday
- **Mareva Coile** (Dungsweepers' Guild) — route coordinator; sharp-tempered and skeptical of outside help; the party's direct client
- **Berna** (Dungsweepers' Guild) — field crew leader; dwarf woman of sixty with a grey braid; the crew's moral center; softens over the tenday through demonstration rather than charm
- **Herath** (Dungsweepers' Guild) — young Calishite sweeper; self-possessed and honest; gives the party a sewer access note on the final morning
- **Delvon Harsk** (Dungsweepers' Guild) — route supervisor; selling timing information to the Xanathar Guild to service a debt; the inside source of the attacks
- **Pirn** (Xanathar Guild) — street agent; coordinates the Night Three ambush; knows Delvon by name and function; can name Krentz if pressed

## Dangers & Enemies
The primary threat arrives on Night Three: three Xanathar Guild **Thugs** and **Pirn** (Spy stat block). The Thugs fight until two fall; Pirn observes from twenty feet back and flees when the Thugs break. Pirn can be tracked or captured. No combat occurs in the first Event — the tenday is surveillance and social work.

## Overview

Jalester assigned the party to protect **Mareva Coile**, a Dungsweepers' Guild route coordinator in the Dock Ward. The gang war had already cost her three injured subordinates, and she was the next logical target. The job was simple and unglamorous: walk her crew's route every day for a tenday, be seen, and keep things quiet.

The tenday passed in cold early mornings and long circuits through the ward. **Berna**, the field crew leader, ran her workers with minimum words and maximum precision and initially had no interest in the party beyond whether they could stay close and not get underfoot. Over the days they found a rhythm. On Day 4, a young sweeper named **Herath** found a coin purse and turned it in without discussion. On Day 4 also, a figure appeared at a second-story window on Margaster Street — ahead of the crew, then somewhere else, then ahead again.

Night Three ended the routine. **Pirn**, a Xanathar Guild street agent, coordinated an ambush on Shank Street with three thugs who hit Mareva on her return from a late supervision check at the Dockside pumping station. After the attack was broken, an investigation into how the timing could have been so precise uncovered **Delvon Harsk** — a route supervisor who had been selling scheduling information to Pirn in exchange for help with a debt he couldn't manage any other way.

The tenday closed with Mareva's crew intact, Delvon's role exposed, and Herath pressing a folded note into the party's hands on the final morning: the location of a sewer access point in the Trades Ward his crew used. He offered it as the only currency he had.
