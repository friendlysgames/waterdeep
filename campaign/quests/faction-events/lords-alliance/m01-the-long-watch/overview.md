# The Long Watch: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member reaches Renown 1 and 2nd level, after **Lords' Alliance First Meeting**. Companions can help with everything except Jalester's brief and debrief, which only members attend.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> The only fight is one **Carrion Crawler** at every party size, and it arrives with 38 hit points when the party has three characters. The **Lords' Alliance Mechanics Reference** audits the fight for three, four and five characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

On the first evening after a member reaches 2nd level, Jalester Silvermane waits at the corner table beside the Yawning Portal's well. A gang war has put the Dungsweepers' Guild in danger. He wants the party to walk one crew through the Trades Ward every morning for a tenday.

## Background

Three workers of the Dungsweepers' Guild were beaten in the Dock Ward this tenday, each on a night when the crew's hours had changed. Delvon Harsk, a route supervisor, has been selling the day's timings to a young street agent named Pirn because he owes a moneylender 300 gp. Delvon did not know anyone would be hurt, and he has found no way to stop.

Pirn works for Xanathar's Guild at the edge of what his handler permits, and the handler has never heard Delvon's name. Mareva Coile, who coordinates the crew, suspects nobody and wants her workers kept safe. Berna, the crew's foreman, has walked the Trades Ward circuit for seventeen years and expects very little from an escort.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Nobody in the mission learns that Pirn works for Xanathar's Guild until Jalester recognizes his description at the debrief.
> - The carrion crawler on the ninth day has no link to Delvon or Pirn. Guild crews moving through the drains below the Trades Ward drove it up a grate, and nobody in the mission learns this.
> - Jalester sends no Guard escort and uses no overlook for the crew. He knows only that the beatings fell on nights when the hours had changed.
> - Berna's crew is clean. Other Dungsweepers' Guild crews are a separate matter, and **Three Nights** in the Bregan D'aerthe set covers one of them.

## The Brief

Jalester briefs the members at the well table in the evening, and the companions sit at the bar. He asks the party to meet the crew at the Muleskull Tavern at six bells the next morning and to report to him on the evening of the tenth day. He has no name for whoever beat the workers, and he says so.

## Six Bells at the Muleskull

The party meets Mareva Coile and Berna at the Muleskull Tavern on Ship Street and walks the Trades Ward circuit with the crew for eight days. Short scenes on Days 2, 4, 6 and 7 show how the crew works and let the party earn Berna's regard. Three independent leads each name Delvon Harsk. They are the watcher on Margaster Street, a schedule scrap and Mareva's duty book, and the party decides what to do about him on Day 8.

## Day Nine

At highsun on the ninth day a carrion crawler climbs out of a sewer grate in a Trades Ward alley, while two wounded Watch Guards arrive behind the party. The party keeps the crew and the Guards alive and drives the crawler back down the drain. That evening Herath gives the party a note about a sewer hatch, and on the tenth day Berna says what she has seen on the circuit if she trusts them.

## The Debrief

On the evening of the tenth day the members report to Jalester at the well table. He asks one specific question about the evidence and decides what to do with the name the party gives him.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Lords' Alliance member gains 2 base Renown for walking the route for the full tenday and reporting to Jalester. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** No worker or Guard drops to 0 hit points in the fight on Day Nine.
- **+1 Renown:** The party names Delvon Harsk to Jalester with at least one of the three leads, which marks **Leak Closed**.
- **+1 Renown:** The party earns Berna's regard, which marks **Berna Trusts the Party**.

Companions who are not Lords' Alliance members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Leak Closed** is read by **The Exile's Return**, where Jalester's tone in the Brief depends on it, and by **Faction Outposts** (unconverted). **Berna Trusts the Party** is read by **Faction Outposts** (unconverted), where Berna's tips on Trades Ward drops depend on it. **Herath's Note Received** is read by **Xanathar's Lair** (unconverted), which can treat the hatch as a sewer approach. **The Exile's Return** becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member reaches Renown 3 and 3rd level, and a member who reaches Renown 3 here also qualifies for **Redknife**.

## Involved Characters

- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance): briefs the members at the well table and takes their report.
- **Mareva Coile** (Dungsweepers' Guild): the crew's route coordinator and the party's client, who keeps the duty book.
- **Berna** (Dungsweepers' Guild): the crew's foreman, a dwarf of about sixty whose regard the party can earn.
- **Herath** (Dungsweepers' Guild): a young Calishite sweeper who gives the party a note about a sewer hatch.
- **Delvon Harsk** (Dungsweepers' Guild): a route supervisor who sells the crew's timings to pay a debt.
- **Pirn** (Xanathar's Guild): a street agent who watches the route from a window on Margaster Street.
- **Durnan** (Independent): the Yawning Portal's owner, who keeps notes for Silvermane's friends behind the bar.
- **Two Watch Guards** (City of Waterdeep): wounded Market-post guards who chase the crawler up from the drains.

## Dangers & Enemies

The only fight is the carrion crawler on Day Nine, which uses the **Carrion Crawler** block from the 2024 *Monster Manual*. It cannot attack in the first round, a prey rule keeps it from biting a creature it has just Paralyzed, and it retreats down the grate at half its starting hit points. Pirn never fights and runs if a character approaches him openly.

## Overview

Jalester Silvermane sends the party to guard a crew of Dungsweepers' Guild workers on their Trades Ward circuit for a tenday. The work is slow, and someone seems to know where the crew will be.
