# Design Notes: The Long Watch

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C and the Dragon Heist table gave a tenday of escort in the Trades Ward for four dungsweepers and their dwarf foreman Berna, four day-beats on Days 2, 4, 6 and 7, and a carrion crawler on Day 9 at highsun with two wounded Watch Guards. The rewards were Berna's trust, which she will share if asked as one professional to another, and Herath's note about a sewer hatch past the Coin Alley checkpoint. The Lords' Alliance Factions Guide and **Trollskull Alley** still describe this version, so the rewrite keeps it.

## What Changed

The first draft dropped the crawler and invented a Night Three ambush on the Dock Ward, with a leak, a street agent and a supervisor behind it. Its first event ran only to Day 7 and then jumped to Night Three, and Herath's note arrived on a final morning that no event reached. The rewrite restores the source's tenday and crawler and keeps the leak as the middle of the mission. Delvon Harsk is found through three independent leads, which are the watcher at the window, the schedule scrap and the duty book, so a failed check never ends the investigation. There is no ambush, and the crawler is the only fight.

Jalester briefs at the well table and states the deadline, which **Suspension and Dismissal** reads. Berna's regard is a count of Trust marks on Days 2, 6 and 7, not a vague mood, and each of the three outcomes has a reader.

## Departures from the Source

Appendix C gives the crawler a "Stagger Saving Throw", which the 2024 stat block does not have, so the crawler uses Paralyzing Tentacles and a prey rule from the **Lords' Alliance Mechanics Reference**. The grate stands 60 feet from the alley mouth so the crawler cannot attack in round 1. At three characters the Guards have already wounded it. Herath hands over the note on Day 9 instead of the last morning, so that **Herath's Note Received** is written before the debrief. The mission awards 2 base Renown with three bonuses where the source gave a flat +1, to fit the shared ladder.

## Invented Names and Open Items

The following are new and have no Notable Figures pages:

- Mareva Coile, the route coordinator, and her four supervisors Delvon Harsk, Orryn Saltwick, Bremma Dunnet and Cass Fildura. Mareva's species is invented as Waterdhavian Human.
- Pirn, the street agent, who has no profile and no alignment on any page.
- Margaster Street, which the first draft placed in the Dock Ward and this version places in the Trades Ward.
- The cart yard behind the Muleskull, the loose brick in its back wall, the duty book and the cooper's yard with the sewer hatch.
- The two Market-post Guards and the Watch officer on Day 2, who are unnamed.

Items for the out-of-scope log:

- The Jalester Notable Figures page says he "gives underperformers harder assignments", and nothing in this mission does that. The page also lists this mission under its old title.
- The name Orryn echoes the staff candidate Orryn Tetch in the Trollskull Manor guide, and Margaster is a North Ward family name in **Fireball!**.
- **Faction Outposts** and **Xanathar's Lair** are unconverted, and they must read **Leak Closed**, **Berna Trusts the Party** and **Herath's Note Received**. The Lair's structure document has no sewer entry besides Ott's route to X15.
- The old files `ev-01-the-tenday-watch.md` and `ev-02-night-three.md` in this folder are obsolete and still present.
