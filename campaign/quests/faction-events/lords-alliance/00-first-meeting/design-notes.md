# Design Notes: Lords' Alliance First Meeting

## Somebody Answers

The earlier draft ended on the Condition, with Jalester leaving after three seconds, and then asked whether "the party" accepted. Nobody answered, nothing enrolled a character, the Cloak rank was never given, and the 200 gp grant that **The Factions Come Calling** promises never arrived. The event now follows the Harpers, Force Grey, Emerald Enclave and Doom Raiders models. Every character in no other faction is a candidate and answers for themselves, and **Lords' Alliance Joined** is marked with each name. The Condition stays word for word, with its three seconds. The earlier draft's facts about the lost operatives and the two assignments Jalester took back to Laeral are restored.

## A Closed Offer

The other First Meetings leave the door open to a deferred candidate. This one does not, because Jalester never chases. A candidate who declines outright is marked **Lords' Alliance Offer Closed**, and the offer is not repeated. A candidate who asks for time has ten days to answer at the table. The outcome is marked only on an explicit decline, or when the tenth day ends with no answer. A character who was in another faction when the meeting ran gets a late scene within a tenday of leaving it, which covers **BD Contact Severed**. The corner table beside the well replaces the earlier "if they are regular presences" rule. Jalester waits at dusk on the day the row fires. If nobody has come, he walks to the manor on the third morning, so the placement is fixed.

## The Cloak and the Grant

The Cloak carries no token, because Jalester sends the new names to the Watch houses, and the overlook covers one minor infraction each tenday before any charge is written. That wording keeps it apart from Redknife string-pulling, which undoes a charge or report after it is written. The patrol note comes from the Factions Guide, which gives it at Renown 3. Here it arrives with the Cloak at Renown 1, so a new member has it for the first missions. The grant is party-level, once, because the manor is shared. The Fellowship licence cut is passive and gets one line.

## Departures

The earlier draft cited the Candle Lane warehouse as a Xanathar Guild scene. The Finding Floon warehouse is the Splinter's dead cell with Guild kenku searching it, and Jalester's question now says which door the party used. Jalester, Laeral and every speaker say "the Splinter" until **Manshoon Named**. Jalester separates the cell upstairs from the Splinter without naming Jarlaxle, the carnival fleet or Kolat Towers. The corner table beside the well carries one GM line about something listening below, and that line does not name Illuun.
