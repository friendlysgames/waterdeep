# Design Notes: An Audience with the Open Lord

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B and C stop at the fourth Lords' Alliance mission. This mission therefore has no source text beyond the Dragon Heist table row and the Vault's use of the Masked Lord's Writ. The earlier draft supplied the shape, and it was kept. That means Laeral's three questions, the Splinter's offer of a Masked Lord accounting for a fifth of the vault, and the strategic argument that makes her decline. The Writ and the choice about Jalester were kept as well. The Vault page reads all of it, so the Writ stays the mission's reason to exist.

## What Changed

The gate is Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**, as in **The Dreamer's Reach** and **Smoke in the Tower**. A three-heist party plays the mission after the Vault. Laeral's lie rule is now a flat **DC 16 Charisma (Deception)** check, because the Archmage block has no Insight proficiency. Each question has a fixed answer table in the text, and the offer has a table by **Kolat Towers** state.

The Writ is never void, because the Vault reads it at its gate. The Jalester Question fires only if a character present learned the name from Mirt. The leak is what Jalester hears when the members report afterwards.

Laeral hears "something old and patient listening through the water", and nobody says Illuun. **Manshoon Named** gates her offer line even here, and the default says "the Splinter". **Laeral Told of Jalester** puts him on desk duty, and Sevel Dastar takes over every later contact.

The audience chamber is 40 feet by 24 feet with an 18-foot ceiling and a table for twelve. **Lioncrown** compares its annex to that room.

## Departures from the Source

The earlier draft let Jalester leak an audience he did not attend. It also called the Writ void if the gold left the city and fused two of the Renown conditions into one. All three are gone. It also named Manshoon in Laeral's speech and used "Illuun-compromised" in text a player could see.

## If Not Run

If the party never takes the audience, **Writ Issued** is not marked. The Vault should use a lesser Watch letter from Laeral's office, which admits the party to the gate after up to an hour of questioning and grants no Watch action. The letter cannot serve as Oath-Release.

The Splinter has no leverage over the Watch, and Jalester delivers no terms. He keeps his table at the well, so the feed stays open as if **Jalester Concealed** were marked.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Sergeant Maelis Venning and Watchman Jorin Larkin, the officers at the door.
- Sevel Dastar has no Notable Figures page or voice profile, so he has no social block and is a middle-aged human in a plain brown coat, as in **Redknife**, and carries the "(Neutral, Human, he/him) … Spy" label used everywhere else.
- Laeral's Notable Figures page says moon elf, while Waterdeep: Dragon Heist has her human. This mission follows the page and does not resolve the clash.
- Laeral's detection rule leans on the Archmage block, which the mechanics reference did not re-verify.
- The Vault page calls the cordon "the Vault district gate", which is not a Dragon Heist term.
- The Blackmail Ledger in **Kolat Towers** names Mirt as a Masked Lord, and Laeral never says so aloud here.
- **Kolat Towers**, **Vault of Dragons** and **Faction Outposts** are unconverted, and the Harper reader line for **Laeral Told of Jalester** lies outside this folder.
