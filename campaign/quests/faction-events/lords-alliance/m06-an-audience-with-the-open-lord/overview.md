# An Audience with the Open Lord: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member reaches Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**. Seventh level requires all four lair heists, so a party that ran three heists reaches it only after **Vault of Dragons** and plays this mission then. The Vault does not require it, and companions attend the audience with the members. Only the members take the report afterwards. A member marked **LA Suspended** is not summoned and receives no Brief until **LA Reinstated** is marked. A suspended member may attend as a companion and is named on the Writ like any character present. A dismissed member is still left off.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 7th-level characters.*
>
> The mission has no combat and no stat block. Laeral Silverhand detects lies with a flat **DC 16 Charisma (Deception)** check, which the **Lords' Alliance Mechanics Reference** records.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Lords' Alliance members who attend the audience and report to their handler.

## Hook

At 09:00 two Watch officers in dress uniform reach Trollskull Manor with a vellum summons signed by Laeral Silverhand. It invites the qualifying members and anyone they trust to Piergeiron's Palace at 17:00. If **Ledger Delivered** is marked, the summons arrives on the morning after the first member meets the gate. If the ledger was sold, it arrives on that same morning, whether or not **LA Grand Game Escalated** is marked.

## Background

Laeral has held the seat of Open Lord since Neverember's ouster, and she has had to learn what the Grand Game costs the city. Jalester's reports and the party's work have given her most of the picture, and she wants the rest from the people who were there. She also wants to see how they reason before she signs anything. If **Esloon Question Delivered** is marked, Esloon Bezant's question is already on her desk, and she answers it in one line during the third question. A member she met at **Warduke** is greeted by name, and she skips her introduction.

The Splinter has sent her an offer through a go-between, a complete accounting of the Masked Lords it has confirmed. The price is a fifth of the vault's contents before the treasury sees it. She has not decided, and she discloses the offer to the party because she wants their judgment.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Laeral issues the Writ whatever the answers, and the Writ is never void. It names every character present, member or not, except a dismissed member.
> - The audience chamber is 40 feet long and 24 feet wide, with an 18-foot ceiling, and **Lioncrown** compares its working annex to this room.
> - Jalester is not in the audience, so he learns what happened only when the members report to him afterwards.
> - Nobody names Illuun, and Laeral says "the Splinter" unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member addressed. If a character tells her about Jalester, she hears of "something old and patient listening through the water".

## The Summons

Sergeant Maelis Venning and Watchman Jorin Larkin bring the vellum at 09:00. Venning knows nothing of the reason, answers a few questions and waits while the members read. The summons has no deadline, and a member who declines twice is not pressed.

## The Palace

The party is searched at the gate and led through a waiting room to a long audience chamber. Laeral sits alone at the head of a table for twelve, and her opening line shows what she already holds from **Ledger Delivered**, **LA Grand Game Escalated** or **Vajra Briefed**.

## Question One: The Stone

Laeral asks who holds the Stone and where it is kept, and a table of the party's real states sets the true answer. A knowing falsehood needs a **DC 16 Charisma (Deception)** check, and a detected lie marks **Laeral Doubts the Party**.

## Question Two: The Four Factions

She asks what each rival wants. Four true lines cover Xanathar's Guild, the Splinter, the Cassalanters and the carnival fleet, and a character who states any two has answered. **Emira Identified**, **Vajra Briefed** and **Jarlaxle Unmasked** change how the lines are logged and counted.

## Question Three: The Offer

Laeral repeats the Splinter's offer and asks for the party's judgment. A strategic reason, backed by a check, makes her decline, but a moral appeal alone does not, and she accepts if no one argues. The state of **Kolat Towers** sets what the Splinter can do at the Vault if she accepts.

## The Writ

Laeral signs the Masked Lord's Writ and passes it to the party, naming everyone who came. It opens the Vault district gate within four rounds of presentation and authorises one Watch action.

## The Jalester Question

This scene runs only if a character present learned from Mirt that Jalester is compromised. Laeral asks what the party is not telling her, and the knowing character decides whether to answer. Telling her puts Jalester on desk duty and sends Sevel Dastar to the Portal.

## The Report

The members report to Jalester at the well table at 20:00, or to Sevel Dastar away from the well if Laeral was told. Whatever the members say to Jalester at the well may reach what listens below.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Lords' Alliance member gains 4 base Renown for attending the audience and reporting to their handler, and companions gain none. Bonuses of +1 go to members whose party answers Question One truthfully, answers Question Two with two true lines and argues the offer down.

## Aftermath

**Writ Issued**, **Splinter Offer Declined** or **Splinter Offer Taken**, **Laeral Doubts the Party**, and the Jalester outcomes feed **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted). **Laeral Told of Jalester** and **Jalester Concealed** also decide who handles **Warduke** and **Lioncrown**. This is the last Lords' Alliance mission, and if the party never takes the audience, the Vault uses the default in the design notes.

## Involved Characters

- **Laeral Silverhand** (City of Waterdeep): the Open Lord, who asks the three questions and signs the Writ.
- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance): takes the members' report at the well table, and is not present at the audience.
- **Sevel Dastar** (Lords' Alliance): a middle-aged human spy in a plain brown coat, who takes the report and every later contact if Laeral learns about Jalester.
- **Maelis Venning** (City of Waterdeep): the Castle Ward Watch sergeant who delivers the summons.
- **Jorin Larkin** (City of Waterdeep): the Watchman who stands beside her.
- **The Splinter** (Splinter cell): sends the offer through a go-between and is never present.

## Dangers & Enemies

The mission has no combat, and its risks are political. A detected lie marks **Laeral Doubts the Party**, and an accepted offer gives the Splinter leverage at the Vault. If anyone attacks, the Watch officers at the door end the audience at once.

## Overview

Laeral Silverhand summons the Lords' Alliance members to Piergeiron's Palace and asks three questions about the Grand Game. She also wants the party's judgment on an offer from the Splinter.
