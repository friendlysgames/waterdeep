# An Audience with the Open Lord

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when two Watch officers bring Laeral Silverhand's summons to Trollskull Manor. It ends when the members report to their handler on the evening of the audience. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Read the summons and attend the audience at Piergeiron's Palace at 17:00.
> - Answer Laeral's three questions about the Stone, the rival factions and an offer from the Splinter.
> - Argue the Splinter's offer down, or leave Laeral free to accept it.
> - Receive the Masked Lord's Writ, which names every character in the room.
> - Tell Laeral about Jalester, if anyone present learned the name from Mirt.
> - Report to Jalester at the well, or to Sevel Dastar if Laeral takes Jalester off duty.
>
> Only Lords' Alliance members take that report and earn Renown. Companions attend the audience and appear on the Writ, and a dismissed member (**LA Dismissed**) receives no summons.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The Writ is issued whatever the answers, and it is never void. Laeral's wish that the gold reach the treasury is a request in her own voice and not a condition.
> - The audience chamber is 40 feet long and 24 feet wide, with an 18-foot ceiling and a table that seats twelve. Only Laeral and the party are inside, and two Watch officers stand outside the double doors.
> - Jalester is not in the room, so he hears about the audience only from the members that evening. What they tell him is the only way their words reach him.
> - A lie told to Laeral passes only on a **DC 16 Charisma (Deception)** check, and she never challenges a lie at the table.
> - Nobody in this Event says Manshoon's name, or calls Kolat Towers his, unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member addressed. Laeral says "the Splinter" until then.
> - Nobody in this Event names Illuun. If a character tells Laeral about Jalester, she hears of "something old and patient listening through the water" and has no idea what it is.
> - The Splinter's offer is real, and Laeral raises it because she wants to hear how the party reasons. She says so only if asked.

### The Summons

Two Watch officers reach Trollskull Manor at 09:00. Sergeant Maelis Venning carries the summons and Watchman Jorin Larkin stands behind her. The summons names each Lords' Alliance member who qualifies and invites those they choose to bring. It carries no deadline, since this is a request and not an assignment, and Jalester sends no word with it.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Two Watch officers in polished breastplates and blue dress cloaks wait in the taproom, where the stools are still upturned on the tables and the floor is damp from the morning scrub. The sergeant holds a roll of vellum tied with white ribbon, and she looks over the room before she looks at you.
>
> > "Sergeant Maelis Venning, Castle Ward. I'm to give this to the Alliance members in this house and wait while it's read."
>
> She hands the roll to the nearest member, and the seal is a plain silver hand pressed into white wax. The vellum inside reads as follows.
>
> > The Open Lord of Waterdeep asks the favor of your company at Piergeiron's Palace at five o'clock this afternoon. Attendance is at your own discretion. You may bring those you trust. Laeral Silverhand.

> [!social]**The Officers at the Door**
>
> Maelis Venning (Lawful Neutral, Human, she/her) :: a Castle Ward Watch sergeant in dress uniform, with a Watchman beside her.
>
> Venning is courteous and exact, and she does not know why the summons was sent. She answers in as few words as the question allows and does not discuss the Open Lord's affairs. Jorin Larkin says nothing unless spoken to.
>
> Venning will discuss the following topics:
>
> - The hour, the palace entrance and who may come.
> - What the Watch has been told, which is nothing.
> - Whether the summons is an order.
>
> She will not guess at what the Open Lord wants.

> [!qna]**Is this an order?**
>
> > "It says discretion, and I've carried enough of these to know that means what it says. Nobody will come for you if you stay home."

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> > "I was told to deliver it and not to ask. If I had to guess, the Open Lord has heard your names more than once lately."

> [!qna]**Who can we bring?**
>
> > "Anyone you trust, up to the size of the chamber's table. They'll be searched at the gate like everyone else, and they'll sit where you sit."

> [!qna]**Is Jalester Silvermane coming?**
>
> > "He wasn't named to me, and I don't carry his schedule. I was told only to bring you to the palace by five."

> [!qna]**What do we wear?**
>
> > "What you'd wear to meet a Lord. Nobody will turn you away for a clean coat, though they will for a drawn blade."

A member who declines the summons gets a second note from the palace two days later, in the same hand, repeating the invitation once. A member who declines twice is not pressed, nothing is marked, and **Vault of Dragons** uses the default in the design notes.

### The Palace

The party is searched at the Castle Ward gate and led through three corridors to a waiting room. A clerk takes their names and writes each one in a book, and Laeral reads that book herself before she signs the Writ.

The doors open at 17:00. Companions are allowed in, and they sit with the members at the near end of the table.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The audience chamber is long and high, with tall windows down the left wall and a stone fireplace at the far end. A table laid for twelve runs down the middle of the floor, and late sun lies across it in broad stripes. One woman sits alone at the head of the table, a silver-haired elf in a plain grey gown, with a cup of tea at her elbow and a small stack of unopened letters beside it. She sets the cup down as you reach the door and gestures to the chairs nearest her.
>
> > "Please sit. I have a good deal to ask you, and I would like to begin while the light holds."

Before Laeral asks her first question, read or paraphrase each of the following that applies to the party's state.

If **Ledger Delivered** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral touches one of the letters in the stack and leaves it where it lies, and then she looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "I have read the ledger you brought me twice. It tells me where the money went, and it does not tell me who is still spending it."

If **LA Grand Game Escalated** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral folds her hands on the table in front of her and waits a moment before she goes on.
>
> > "Jalester gave me your account of the Stone and the vault, and I have kept it close. I want to hear the rest from you directly."

If **Vajra Briefed** is marked for any character in the room, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral touches the top letter in the stack, which carries a grey seal, and then she lays her hand flat on the table.
>
> > "The Blackstaff wrote to me on this. She has told me what she has seen, and I would rather not hear it twice."

> [!gamemaster]**What Laeral Already Holds**
>
> - **Ledger Delivered:** she knows the gold sits in a vault in Waterdeep and that Dunberton Holdings paid a custodian from it, though she has not learned who holds the Stone.
> - **LA Grand Game Escalated:** she knows the Stone has resurfaced in the city and that two villain factions are confirmed.
> - **Vajra Briefed** for a character in the room: she holds the Guild line and the Splinter line of Question Two, and that question adjusts for it.
> - None of these marked: this is her first full briefing, and she asks each question exactly as written.
> - Her opening line changes with the state, and nothing else about the audience does.

> [!social]**The Open Lord at Her Table**
>
> Laeral Silverhand (Chaotic Good, Moon Elf, she/her) :: the Open Lord of Waterdeep, a tall silver-haired elf in a plain grey gown, with a stack of letters she is not reading.
>
> Laeral speaks in complete, measured sentences and lets a silence run until someone fills it. She never swears in office and never raises her voice. She wants answers that are accurate more than answers that are flattering, and she writes in a small book while the party speaks. She uses a character's full name when she is displeased.
>
> Laeral will discuss the following topics:
>
> - The Stone, the vault and who is pursuing each.
> - The four rival factions and what each wants.
> - The Splinter's offer, and the party's judgment of it.
> - The Writ, once the questions are done.
>
> She will not discuss her own strength, the Blackstaff, Khelben Arunsun, or the names of any Masked Lord.

> [!qna]**Why did you call us?**
>
> > "Because you have been closer to this than my own agents, and I would sooner hear it from the people who were there. I have three questions, and I would ask you to answer them honestly."

> [!qna]**Are we in any trouble?**
>
> > "No. If you were, there would be more men at the door and fewer chairs at this table."

> [!qna]**Is this a test?**
>
> Laeral takes a moment before she answers.
>
> > "Every conversation I have is a test of something. I would rather you answered the question in front of you than wondered about the one behind it."

> [!exploration]**Laeral's Ear**
>
> Laeral weighs every answer, and she keeps one flat rule. A character who knowingly says something false must make a **DC 16 Charisma (Deception)** check, and that character alone makes it. A character who is merely mistaken has not lied and makes no check, though the answer earns no Renown bonus.
>
> - **On a failure:** Laeral says nothing at the table, and one detected lie is enough to mark **Laeral Doubts the Party** when the audience ends.
> - **Truthful answers from other characters:** these do not cancel a detected lie.
> - **Fallback:** a character who corrects a false statement before Laeral moves on to the next question is treated as having told the truth.

### Question One: The Stone

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral opens the small book, writes a line at the top of a fresh page and looks up at the party with her pen still in her hand.
>
> > "Who holds the Stone of Golorr tonight, and where is it kept? I will not ask where you found it."

The first answer given for the party stands, and another character can correct it before Laeral moves on. The answer earns the bonus only if it matches the party's real state in the table below.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is True About the Stone**
>
> - **The party has it:** the true answer names the character who carries it, or the place the party keeps it.
> - **Mirt has it for the three-day study:** the true answer is that the Stone is at Mirt's manor and returns to the party on the fourth morning.
> - **Stone Taken by Splinter** is marked and the Stone is not recovered: the true answer is that the Splinter holds it. The party does not know where.
> - **The Vault of Dragons has already run:** the true answer is wherever the party's own record says the Stone lies now, and Laeral accepts the party's word.
> - **"We do not know" when the party does:** this is a lie and calls for the check. The same words from a party that truly does not know are honest, though they earn no bonus.

> [!qna]**Is it safe?**
>
> > "It has not been stolen from this table, if that is what you mean. I have no illusions about how long any place stays safe once it is known."

> [!qna]**Why do you need to know where it is?**
>
> > "Because I cannot protect a thing I cannot find. I will not take it from you."

### Question Two: The Four Factions

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral turns to the next page of her book and waits, with the pen resting against her thumb, until she is sure you are ready.
>
> > "Four houses are circling that vault. Tell me what each of them wants, and tell me how you know. I would like your own words and not your guesses."

Each of the following lines is true, and a character who states the substance of a line in their own words has given it. Laeral logs behaviour without proof as "unconfirmed, credible", and such a line still counts toward the total.

> [!gamemaster]**The Four Lines**
>
> - **Xanathar's Guild:** wants the vault's gold as proof that it rules the city's underworld, and its crews hunt the Splinter.
> - **The Splinter:** wants the Stone and the gold, and will take both by force wherever it can reach them.
> - **The Cassalanters:** need the gold badly and pay hired agents to learn how to reach it. With **Emira Identified**, a character may name Emira Volstren as the household's agent, and Laeral logs the line as confirmed.
> - **The carnival fleet:** wants a seat for Luskan in the Lords' Alliance and holds an Eye of the Stone. With **Jarlaxle Unmasked**, a character may name Jarlaxle Baenre and Zardoz Zord, and Laeral says only that she knows the first name.
>
> Question Two is answered when a character states any two lines. If **Vajra Briefed** is marked for any character in the room, Laeral already holds the first two lines and counts them, so one line from the third or fourth is enough.

> [!qna]**What if we only suspect?**
>
> > "Then say that you suspect, and I will write that down. I have learned to value an honest guess over a confident error."

> [!qna]**Which of them frightens you most?**
>
> Laeral considers the question for some time before she answers.
>
> > "The one I cannot see clearly. I know what the Guild wants and what the Splinter wants, and I would like to know the rest as well."

> [!qna]**Who is the carnival fleet?**
>
> > "A fleet of ships that put in for the season and has not left. Luskan has asked openly for a seat at the Alliance table, and I have been watching who benefits from that."

### Question Three: The Offer

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral sets down her pen, closes the book over her thumb and looks at each of you in turn before she speaks.
>
> > "I have been offered something, and I would like your judgment before I decide."

Choose the variant that matches **Manshoon Named** for the member Laeral is looking at when she makes the offer. Use the default if it is marked for none of the members present.

By default, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "The Splinter has offered me a complete accounting of every Masked Lord whose identity they have confirmed. They ask permission to remove a fifth of the vault's contents before it reaches the treasury. Should I accept?"

If **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member she addresses, read this instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Manshoon's cell has offered me a complete accounting of every Masked Lord whose identity they have confirmed. They ask permission to remove a fifth of the vault's contents before it reaches the treasury. Should I accept?"

After a pause, she adds:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Do not tell me what you think I want to hear. I have enough of that."

> [!gamemaster]**What the Offer Is Worth**
>
> The offer is the same in every case, and its value changes with the state of **Kolat Towers**. Laeral says what it is worth if the party asks.
>
> - **Kolat Towers has not run:** the offer is live, and the Splinter counts as Alive for every purpose below.
> - **Manshoon operational? Alive or Simulacrum Only:** the offer is live, and the leverage is real.
> - **Manshoon operational? Destroyed:** the offer comes from the Splinter's survivors, who can still delay the Watch but hold no list worth exposing.
> - **Blackmail Ledger delivered to Laeral:** she already holds five names, and the argument below is easier by 2.
> - **The Vault of Dragons has already run:** the offer is a fifth of the gold the treasury has yet to enter in its books, and everything else stands.

The party argues in its own words. Laeral accepts a strategic reason and does not accept a moral appeal alone, since the offer already offends her and she has said so by asking.

> [!exploration]**The Strategic Argument**
>
> Any of the three reasons below is enough, and a character names one before rolling.
>
> - Accepting makes the Splinter a recognised power in Waterdeep.
> - Accepting sets a precedent that city officials can be extorted.
> - The list tells the Splinter which Lords are weakest, and it will use that after the gold is spent.
>
> A character who names one reason and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence** or **Charisma (Persuasion)** check persuades her. A character who names two reasons makes the check at **DC 11**, and one who names all three needs no check. If the Blackmail Ledger was delivered, lower each DC by 2.
>
> - **On a failure:** another character may offer a different reason once, at **DC 15**.
> - **After a second failure, or if no one argues:** Laeral accepts the offer.

If the party argues the offer down, read or paraphrase the following, and mark **Splinter Offer Declined**:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral is quiet for some time, and then she closes the book and sets it aside with her pen on top.
>
> > "You are right, and I am a little annoyed that I needed to be told. The offer is declined, and the Splinter will have no hold on this city through me."

If Laeral accepts the offer, read or paraphrase the following, and mark **Splinter Offer Taken**:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral nods slowly and writes a line in her book, and then she lays the pen down across the page.
>
> > "I understand your reasoning, and I will accept the offer on those terms. I will not pretend the arrangement is clean, and I will not ask you to manage what follows."

> [!gamemaster]**What the Offer Changes**
>
> - **Splinter Offer Declined:** the Splinter has no leverage at **Vault of Dragons**, and it cannot delay the Watch or threaten an exposure through Laeral.
> - **Splinter Offer Taken:** at the Vault the Splinter can delay Watch cooperation by up to two hours. It can also threaten to expose one Masked Lord, but only if the Blackmail Ledger was not delivered and **Manshoon operational?** is Alive or Simulacrum Only, or **Kolat Towers** has not run.
> - **After the Vault:** a declined offer sends the whole gold to the treasury, and an accepted offer sends a fifth of what remains to the Splinter's agents instead.

### The Writ

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral opens a drawer in the table and takes out a sheet of vellum already sealed at the foot. She turns the clerk's book toward herself, reads down the page, and writes each name on the sheet with a steady hand.
>
> > "This is the Masked Lord's Writ. It names each of you and everyone you brought. I hope that the gold reaches the treasury, all of it, and I have said so in the Writ as a wish. The Writ stands whatever you decide."

She signs it, blows once on the ink and passes it down the table.

> [!gamemaster]**The Masked Lord's Writ**
>
> The Writ names every character in the room, member or not. This is a deliberate gift to the whole party, as the statuette in **The Compromised Eye** is for Bregan D'aerthe, and a dismissed member is not in the room and is not named.
>
> - **The gate:** Watch officers at the Vault district gate defer to the Writ, and the gate opens within four rounds of presentation.
> - **One legal action:** Laeral authorises one Watch action on the party's behalf during **Vault of Dragons**. The party can use it against a rival faction's complication, a legal challenge or an arrest.
> - **Terms:** Jalester delivers these terms again at the Vault, or Sevel Dastar does if **Laeral Told of Jalester** is marked.
> - **If the Vault has already run:** the Writ is a record that the party acted for the city, and it has no other effect.
>
> A character who joins the party later is not named on it.

If a lie was detected, mark **Laeral Doubts the Party** now. She says nothing about it, and the Writ does not change.

### The Jalester Question

This scene runs only if **Jalester Compromise Identified** is marked and at least one character in the room learned the name from Mirt, or from a character who did. Otherwise Laeral rises and says "That will be all", and the audience ends.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral has risen to her feet by the head of the table, and she lets the silence run until one of you shifts in your chair.
>
> > "Before you go, tell me what you are not telling me. I would rather hear it here than learn it later."

The knowing character decides, in their own words, whether to tell her. If Harper Mission 6 ran, Mirt said that the choice was theirs and he would not make it for them.

If a character tells Laeral about Jalester, read or paraphrase the following, and mark **Laeral Told of Jalester**:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral listens to the whole account without moving, and when it is finished she sits down again and puts both hands flat on the table.
>
> > "Something old and patient, listening through the water of a tavern well. I have not heard the like in this city, and I do not know what it is. Jalester has done nothing wrong, and I will not have him treated as though he had."

She goes on in the same level voice.

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "From tonight he works at the palace, at a desk, and he does not go near the Yawning Portal. Sevel Dastar will take his duties. He will find you at the Portal, at a table away from the well."

If no character tells her, read or paraphrase the following, and mark **Jalester Concealed**:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral waits a moment longer than the silence requires, and then she nods and gestures toward the doors.
>
> > "Very well. That will be all."

> [!gamemaster]**What Telling Her Changes**
>
> - **Laeral Told of Jalester:** the party reports to Sevel Dastar that evening, away from the well. Whatever Jalester has already heard stays heard, he cannot escort the party at the Vault, and Sevel takes his place at every later Alliance contact.
> - **Jalester Concealed:** the party reports to Jalester at the well table, and the feed stays open. What the members tell him that evening, including the Stone's holder and the Writ's terms, can reach what listens below.
> - A character who lies to Laeral about Jalester makes the **DC 16 Charisma (Deception)** check, and a failure marks **Laeral Doubts the Party** and **Jalester Concealed**.
> - If the scene does not run because nobody present knows the name, mark **Jalester Concealed** too, so that the Vault always finds one of the two outcomes.

### Aftermath

Laeral has what she needs for the Vault whatever the answers were. If the offer was declined she sleeps easier, and if it was taken she has accepted a risk and will not say so aloud. If a lie was detected she remembers it and says nothing, and the Writ does not change.

If the party told her about Jalester, he sits at a palace desk with a pen and a ledger and learns why that same night. He takes it as an order and not a punishment, and Sevel Dastar is at the Portal from the next evening. If the party stayed silent, Jalester keeps his table by the well and the leak continues.

The Writ itself is a single sheet of vellum with a silver-hand seal, and Laeral keeps a copy in the palace chancery.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the members have reported to their handler. Record the Writ, the offer, the lie and the Jalester decision separately, because a party can earn any of them without the others.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Writ Issued** — Mark with the name of every character the Writ lists, when Laeral signs it. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), which sets the gate, the Watch action and Jalester's terms.
> - **Splinter Offer Declined** — Mark when Laeral refuses the offer after the party's argument. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), where the Splinter has no leverage.
> - **Splinter Offer Taken** — Mark when Laeral accepts the offer. Read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), where the Splinter can delay the Watch.
> - **Laeral Doubts the Party** — Mark when a character fails the Deception check against her. Read by **Lioncrown** and by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).
> - **Laeral Told of Jalester** — Mark when a character tells her what Mirt learned. Read by every later Alliance contact, which Sevel Dastar takes over, and by **Warduke**, **Lioncrown** and **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).
> - **Jalester Concealed** — Mark when the Jalester Question does not run, when no one tells her, or when the party lies about it. Read by every later Alliance contact, which stays with Jalester, and by **Warduke**, **Lioncrown** and **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).

## Summary

We went to Piergeiron's Palace at the Open Lord's request, and she asked us who held the Stone, what the factions wanted and whether she should take an offer from the Splinter. She gave us a Writ naming everyone we brought, and she wished that the gold reach the treasury. We still don't know what she wrote ahead of us to Jalester, or what the Splinter will do next.
