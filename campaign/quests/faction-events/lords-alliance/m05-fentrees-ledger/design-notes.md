# Design Notes: Fentree's Ledger

## What the Source Gave

No source text exists for this mission. Appendix B and Appendix C stop at the fourth Lords' Alliance mission, and the Factions Guide gives only a one-line summary of the ledger and its payoff. The first draft supplied the shape that survives. It had a dead investigator, a stolen ledger and a broker running two buyers at an inn. It also had three approaches and a debrief in which Jalester reads the pages twice.

## What Changed

The first draft opened with three checks in a row. The rewrite splits the mission at the change of place and clock. **The Missing Volume** is the investigation, with three independent leads to the Green Dragon and a free fourth for a member with **Stingblade Reached** marked. **The Green Dragon** is the evening at the inn, with a bell clock, three approaches and one fight.

Jalester writes the first task as a letter, which is the mission's deliberate break from his in-person contact. Laeral's chancery wanted the instruction filed. The courier carries written questions and answers, so the brief still has a social block and replies. His deadline is the last bell of the second evening, and **Suspension and Dismissal** reads it.

Noska Ur'gray no longer bids. His voice profile says he never negotiates, so Ossery Klenn, a Guild negotiator, takes the chair and Hallan Roke leads the bodyguards. The old Thug and Bandit escort becomes a **Tough Boss** with **Tough** fighters at each party size. Rezka's flight is a track and not a pursuit scene.

## Departures from the Source

The first draft dated the ledger to "1244 DR" and placed Fentree's house on Copper Street in the Trades Ward. It also had him die on "Greengrass morning". The ledger now covers the last two years of Neverember's term. The house is on Brasswick Lane, and Fentree died ten days before the letter. The ledger names "a vault custodian in Waterdeep" and no district, so it does not pre-empt the Stone's reveal.

Laeral is said to place the hoard in Neverwinter, which follows the guide. If **LA Grand Game Escalated** is already marked, the debrief reads the ledger as proof of who paid for the vault. It is no longer news of where the vault is. The three readings of pages 47 to 52 are independent, so no single check settles the mission.

## Invented Names and Open Items

The following are new and have no Notable Figures pages:

- Brasswick Lane in the Trades Ward, which replaces Copper Street.
- Tamsin Rook, the chancery courier, and Dessa Voll, the Caravan Court money-changer.
- Hallan Roke, the Tough Boss, and the Reader, a Guild clerk who is named only by Rezka.
- Ossery Klenn, Emira Volstren, Pravin Cloth, Orlass Hemmet and Rezka keep their first-draft names, and their alignments here are invented.
- The Green Dragon's house bell, which rings the hour from six, and Cellar Walk, the lane behind its yard.

Items for the out-of-scope log:

- Noska Ur'gray's Notable Figures page lists this mission, and he no longer appears in it. Dagult Neverember's page lists it too, though he never appears.
- The guide hook and the Vault of Dragons structure document use "Vault district" and "Mission 5" and must read the new outcome names.
- Laeral is a human in Dragon Heist and a moon elf on her Notable Figures page, and this mission does not resolve it.
