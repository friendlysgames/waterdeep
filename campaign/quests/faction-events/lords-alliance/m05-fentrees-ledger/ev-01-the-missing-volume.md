# The Missing Volume

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Investigation and Social Event begins when a palace courier brings Jalester Silvermane's letter to Trollskull Manor at 08:00. It ends at the Green Dragon Inn, when the party knows which rooms Rezka has let and when each meeting begins. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Read Jalester's formal letter and send questions back to him by the courier, who returns with his written answers within the hour.
> - Search Orvane Fentree's study on Brasswick Lane and find the empty fourth slot and a copper button.
> - Follow three independent leads to the Green Dragon Inn, which are the button, the neighbour Orlass Hemmet and the money-changer Dessa Voll.
> - Read the Watch's death report for nothing, if a member holds the Stingblade rank.
> - Learn the rooms and the hours of the meetings from Pravin Cloth, the innkeeper.
>
> Only Lords' Alliance members receive Jalester's letter, and their companions can help with everything else.

> [!gamemaster]**Who Knows What**
>
> - Orvane Fentree died ten days ago of a genuine heart attack. Nobody harmed him, and the Watch physician's note says so.
> - Rezka searched the study while the Watch clerk was in the front room, wearing a porter's coat she took from the pegs at the Green Dragon Inn. She has the fourth volume, and she has been looking for the best price ever since.
> - Jalester knows that the ledger is missing and that Laeral wants it found. He has not read it, and he does not know who took it.
> - Laeral has placed Neverember's hoard in Neverwinter for three years. Nobody in this Event knows that the ledger proves her wrong, except Rezka, who has read it and understood part of it.
> - Nobody in this Event names the Stone of Golorr, and Jalester says nothing about the vault until the debrief.

### The Brief

Jalester writes this task and does not speak it, because Laeral's chancery asked that the instruction be filed with her office. Tamsin Rook is a courier in the plain grey coat of the palace chancery. She knocks at Trollskull Manor at 08:00 on the morning after a member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level. She gives a sealed copy to each Lords' Alliance member and then waits in the taproom to carry questions to Jalester. Companions may listen, though the letter names the members only.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A young woman in a plain grey coat stands in the taproom doorway with a leather satchel on her hip. She hands each of you a folded letter sealed with grey wax and then waits beside the door with her hands clasped. The letter is written in a stiff, upright hand on plain paper, and it reads:
>
> > To the members of the Lords' Alliance at Trollskull Manor. Orvane Fentree, a retired trade investigator of seventy-one, died in his house on Brasswick Lane in the Trades Ward ten days ago. The Watch recorded natural causes, and I have no reason to doubt it. His private ledger is missing from his study, and the Open Lord has asked that it be found before anyone else reads it. I ask you to learn who took it and to bring it to me. I will be at the corner table beside the Yawning Portal's well tomorrow evening, from dusk until the last bell. If I have heard nothing from you by then, I will regard the task as abandoned. The bearer will carry any question to me and bring my answer back within the hour. Does any of you know the counting houses of the Caravan Court? J.S.

> [!social]**The Agent on Paper**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: a lean Lords' Alliance field agent who answers by letter this morning from the palace's lower offices.
>
> Jalester writes in a stiffer hand than he speaks, and his replies come back on half-sheets signed J.S. Each reply is short, formal and specific, and he thanks the members for the question. He swears at nobody on paper, and any curse from him would be a sign that something had gone badly wrong.
>
> Jalester is happy to answer questions about the following topics:
>
> - Who Orvane Fentree was, and what the Watch recorded about his death.
> - Why the Open Lord wants the ledger found.
> - What the entry slip enclosed with the letter allows at the house.
> - The deadline, and what happens if the party fails.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr or the vault, and he has not read the ledger himself.

Each reply below arrives on a half-sheet. Hand it to the table or read it aloud.

> [!qna]**Who was Orvane Fentree?**
>
> > He investigated trade disputes for the Caravan Court and kept private notes after he retired. The Watch found him in his chair, and a physician says his heart failed. Nobody harmed him.

> [!qna]**Why does the Open Lord want it?**
>
> > The Open Lord has not said, and I have not asked her. I know that she sent for the Watch's report on his death and read it twice.

> [!qna]**Who would take it?**
>
> > The Watch clerk noted that someone had searched the study. I think the thief knew what to look for. I cannot say more, and I would rather say so than guess.

> [!qna]**May we enter the house?**
>
> > A Hall of Justice entry slip is folded inside the letter. The constable at the door will honor it for today, and he will ask you to touch nothing that is sealed.

> [!qna]**What if we cannot find it?**
>
> > Then tell me at the table by the last bell tomorrow, and tell me what you tried. An honest failure costs you nothing with me. If I have heard nothing by then, I will take the task as abandoned.

> [!gamemaster]**The Deadline**
>
> The deadline is the last bell on the evening after the letter arrives. A member who refuses the task, or who sends no word and makes no report by the last bell, is a case for **Suspension and Dismissal**. A party that works honestly and does not find the ledger costs nothing, and Jalester says so at the table. A character who answers the letter's question with a yes has Advantage on the check in **Lead Three**. A Guild Artisan or a Merchant counts as a yes.

### Three Ways to the Green Dragon

Three leads each name the Green Dragon Inn, and any one of them is enough. A failed check costs the party an hour and never closes another lead. The party has until six o'clock in the evening to reach the inn, and the leads can be worked in any order or split between companions.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Brasswick Lane is a narrow street of tall joined houses, and a cooper's sign swings over the corner. A Watch constable beside the black door of the fourth house holds out a hand for the entry slip. A steep stair leads up to the study at the back of the second floor, where a high-backed chair faces a cold hearth.

> [!exploration]**Lead One: The Empty Slot**
>
> Any character who searches the study and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check finds three things. The dust on the shelf is disturbed in the pattern of someone who knew where to look. Three ledger volumes stand on the upper shelf and the fourth slot is empty and clean, so the missing volume left within the last ten days. A copper button lies under the window seat, stamped with a coiled dragon, and it matches no coat in Fentree's wardrobe.
>
> - **Reading the button:** A character who grew up in Waterdeep, or who asks the constable, knows the coiled dragon. It is the mark on a porter's coat at the Green Dragon Inn near the Caravan Court. Anyone else learns it from a stable-hand within the hour.
> - **On a failure:** The constable has watched the search from the doorway, and he points out the button. The search costs the party an hour.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A stout woman of about seventy leans from a window across the lane with a basket of washing on the sill. She watches you come out of the black door and calls down before you have crossed the cobbles.
>
> > "You've come about poor Mister Fentree, haven't you? I told the constable everything I saw, and I don't think anybody wrote it down."

> [!exploration]**Lead Two: Orlass Hemmet**
>
> Orlass Hemmet is a widow who has lived opposite Fentree for thirty years and notices everything in the lane. Any character who talks with her and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check hears her account.
>
> - **On a failure:** Orlass talks about the Watch and the state of the lane. A character who returns within the hour with a honey cake from the baker on the corner hears the account with no check.

When a character wins her trust, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Orlass leans out further and lowers her voice, though nobody else is in the lane.
>
> > "A red-haired woman, thirties, in a porter's coat that didn't fit her. She went in by the front door with a basket while the physician was upstairs. She came out the back with a satchel that hung heavier, and she hailed a barrow-boy and told him to mind it and take her to the Green Dragon."

> [!exploration]**Lead Three: The Message Drop**
>
> Dessa Voll keeps a changer's counter in the Caravan Court and passes sealed messages for 2 gp to people who want no street address. A red-haired woman who calls herself Torval has paid her to send any buyer who asks after "paper from the Fentree house" to the Green Dragon. Any character who asks among the Court's counting houses and makes a successful **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** check is sent to Dessa's counter. Dessa gives the message to anyone who pays her fee: Room 3, the Green Dragon, Mistress Torval, tonight.
>
> - **On a failure:** The search costs an hour, and a second attempt after the hour needs no check.

> [!exploration]**Lead Four: The Watch Report**
>
> A member who holds the Stingblade rank can read the Watch's report on Fentree's death in one hour with no check. It says "natural causes" and carries a clerk's note that the study had been searched. It also lists a copper button stamped with a coiled dragon as unclaimed property, which points the reader to the Green Dragon.

### The Booking

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Green Dragon stands just off the Caravan Court, a long timbered inn with a coiled dragon painted green above the door. Carters and factors fill the taproom, and a stooped man behind the bar wipes a mug as you come in. He looks up with polite interest and nods toward an empty table.
>
> > "Good day to you. Rooms by the night, a hot supper and a stall for your horse, and I'm Pravin Cloth if you need anything."

> [!social]**The Proprietor of the Green Dragon**
>
> Pravin Cloth (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: a stooped innkeeper of about sixty who has kept the Green Dragon for thirty years and asks his guests no questions.
>
> Pravin is Friendly and incurious. He confirms rooms to anyone who asks politely, shows his register for 5 gp, and forgets the conversation once it ends. He rings a brass house bell on the hour from six in the evening, because his carters like to know the time.
>
> Pravin is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Which rooms are let tonight, and for what hour.
> - The house bell and the hours it rings.
> - His porters, their coats and the stable yard.
>
> He will not discuss his guests' business, and he will not let anyone upstairs without a reason.

> [!qna]**Who has Room 3?**
>
> > "A Mistress Torval has had Room 3 for ten days, paid in advance. Her supper goes up at half past five, and she hasn't made trouble yet. Is she a friend of yours?"

> [!qna]**Who else is meeting here tonight?**
>
> > "Room 4 is held for a factor at second bell, and Room 6 for a lady at third bell. I ring the house bell from six, so first bell is six o'clock, second is seven and third is eight."

> [!qna]**May we see the register?**
>
> > "Five gold, and you may read it where you stand. The factor's name is Klenn, and the lady's is Volstren. I couldn't tell you more than that."

> [!gamemaster]**What the Register Gives**
>
> The register shows Room 4 let to "O. Klenn" and Room 6 let to "Mistress E. Volstren". Pravin shows it to any character who pays, and the name Volstren is the first half of **Emira Identified** in **The Green Dragon**.

### Concluding the Event

The Event ends when the party has the rooms and the hours, and play continues with **The Green Dragon**.

## Overview

A courier brings Jalester's letter about a dead investigator's missing ledger. The party searches his study and follows three leads to the Green Dragon Inn.

## Summary

We read Jalester's stiff letter and searched Orvane Fentree's study on Brasswick Lane. A button, a nosy neighbour and a money-changer all sent us to the Green Dragon Inn. Pravin Cloth told us that two buyers were coming to Rezka's rooms tonight, and who would reach her first?
