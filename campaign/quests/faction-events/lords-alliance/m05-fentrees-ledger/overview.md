# Fentree's Ledger: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level, after **The Archer Above**. Companions can help with everything except Jalester's debrief, which only members attend.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 6th-level characters.*
>
> The fight is a Standard encounter against a **Tough Boss** and his **Tough** fighters, with a Hard version when Rezka cannot flee. Rezka uses the **Spy** block, and Ossery Klenn and Emira Volstren use the **Noble** block. The **Lords' Alliance Mechanics Reference** audits the rosters for three, four and five characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Lords' Alliance members who report to Jalester.

## Hook

At 08:00 on the morning after a member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level, a palace courier brings each eligible member a formal letter on plain paper. Orvane Fentree, a retired trade investigator, died ten days ago, and his private ledger has vanished from his study. Jalester asks the party to find it before anyone else reads it.

## Background

Orvane Fentree was seventy-one when his heart failed in his house on Brasswick Lane in the Trades Ward. The Watch recorded natural causes, and the Watch was right. For twenty years after he retired he kept a private ledger in four volumes. The fourth follows a shell company named Dunberton Holdings as it paid a vault custodian in Waterdeep.

A Xanathar's Guild clerk paid Rezka, a freelance information broker, to watch Fentree after he asked the Caravan Court counting houses about Dunberton. She searched the study while the Watch clerk was still in the front room, read the fourth volume and kept it. For ten days she has looked for the best price, and she has let three rooms at the Green Dragon Inn for tonight.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The ledger shows that Dagult Neverember's shell company paid a custodian in Waterdeep during his last two years in office. Laeral Silverhand has placed his hoard in Neverwinter for three years, and the ledger proves her wrong.
> - Ossery Klenn bids for Xanathar's Guild, and Emira Volstren bids for a family that has an interest in Neverember's money. Emira is a hired agent and not household staff, and nobody in the mission knows more about the family than a suspicion.
> - Neither buyer knows the other is coming. Rezka arranged it herself, and she wants to leave Waterdeep before the Guild learns she held the book back.
> - Nobody in the mission names the Stone of Golorr, and Jalester has not read the ledger.

## The Letter

Jalester does not brief this task at the well table, because Laeral's chancery asked him to put it in writing so that the office can file it. Tamsin Rook, a chancery courier, waits in the taproom of Trollskull Manor and carries any questions to the palace. Jalester answers each one on a half-sheet within the hour, and he sets his deadline at the last bell of the next evening.

## The Study and the Leads

The party searches Fentree's study on Brasswick Lane and finds the empty fourth slot and a copper button. Three leads each name the Green Dragon Inn, and they are the button, the neighbour Orlass Hemmet and a money-changer named Dessa Voll. A failed check costs an hour and never closes the other two. A member with the Stingblade rank can also read the Watch's death report for nothing. Pravin Cloth, the innkeeper, confirms the rooms and the hour of each meeting.

## The Green Dragon

Rezka sits in Room 3 with the ledger. Ossery Klenn meets her in Room 4 at second bell, which is seven o'clock, and Emira Volstren meets her in Room 6 at third bell. The party can reach Rezka before the bidding or collapse the bidding with a lie, a threat or a better price. It can also let Ossery buy and stop him on the stair.

## The Interception

If Ossery buys the ledger, he leaves at half past seven with a Tough Boss named Hallan Roke. The number of hired fighters with him depends on the party's size. The hall and the stair are five feet wide, so only three of them can reach one target at a time. Rezka runs for the yard wall as soon as the fight starts, unless the party has already taken the back stair.

## The Ledger and the Debrief

Pages 47 to 52 can be read three ways, and each shows that the payments were made in Waterdeep. The members report to Jalester at the well table at dusk on the next evening. What he says depends on where the ledger ended up, on whether the party identified Emira and on whether **LA Grand Game Escalated** is marked.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Lords' Alliance member gains 4 base Renown for reporting to Jalester, whatever became of the ledger. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** the ledger reaches Jalester within 24 hours of recovery.
- **+1 Renown:** Emira Volstren is identified and reported, which marks **Emira Identified**.
- **+1 Renown:** Rezka is taken alive and gives a statement.

Companions who are not Lords' Alliance members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Ledger Delivered** is read by **An Audience with the Open Lord**, **Escalation to the Open Lord** and **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted). **Ledger Sold to the Guild** and **Ledger Sold to the Cassalanters** are read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted). The first is also read by **Xanathar's Lair** and the second by **Cassalanter Villa** (both unconverted), whichever has not yet run. **Emira Identified** is read by **An Audience with the Open Lord** and by **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted).

**An Audience with the Open Lord** becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member reaches Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**.

## Involved Characters

- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance): writes the letter, answers questions by half-sheet and takes the report at the well table.
- **Tamsin Rook** (City of Waterdeep): the chancery courier who carries the letter and the replies.
- **Orvane Fentree** (deceased, independent): a retired trade investigator whose ledger is the object of the mission.
- **Rezka** (Xanathar's Guild): a freelance broker who stole the ledger and is selling it to two buyers.
- **Ossery Klenn** (Xanathar's Guild): the Guild's negotiator, who bids 600 gp and avoids a fight.
- **Hallan Roke** (Xanathar's Guild): the Tough Boss who leads Ossery's bodyguards.
- **Emira Volstren** (independent): a hired agent who bids 400 gp and a favor for a family she will not name.
- **Pravin Cloth** (independent): the Green Dragon's proprietor, who confirms rooms and asks no questions.
- **Orlass Hemmet** and **Dessa Voll** (independent): a neighbour on Brasswick Lane and a Caravan Court money-changer.

## Dangers & Enemies

Hallan Roke uses the **Tough Boss** block and his hired fighters use the **Tough** block. Hallan breaks at 27 hit points or fewer, and the fighters break when he does. Rezka uses the **Spy** block and surrenders when Grappled or at 13 hit points or fewer. Ossery and Emira use the **Noble** block and do not fight, and Emira's two escorts use the **Guard** block.

## Overview

Jalester sends the party to recover a dead investigator's ledger from a broker who is selling it to two buyers at the Green Dragon Inn. The ledger shows where a former Open Lord's money went.
