# Lords' Alliance Mission 5 — Fentree's Ledger

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Requires Lords' Alliance Renown 8 and 6th level. Available after Mission 4 is complete.
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Involved Characters
- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance) — field agent; sends this mission by plain-paper courier in a more formal style than usual; Laeral Silverhand has a personal interest in the outcome
- **Orvane Fentree** (deceased) — retired trade investigator, 71; kept a private ledger for twenty years documenting guild irregularities; his death was genuine, his ledger stolen within an hour
- **Rezka** (Xanathar Guild) — freelance intelligence operative; stole the ledger and is running two buyers simultaneously at the Green Dragon Inn; will trade the ledger for safe exit from Waterdeep
- **Noska Ur'gray** (Xanathar Guild) — enforcer representing the guild's senior intelligence cell; has 600 gp and guild authority; Rezka's highest bidder
- **Emira Volstren** (Cassalanter household) — household representative; offers 400 gp and a favor; Rezka's second buyer; her presence confirms Cassalanter interest in vault intelligence
- **Pravin Cloth** (independent) — Green Dragon Inn proprietor; incurious about guests and their business

## Dangers & Enemies
**Rezka** (Spy stat block) surrenders without a fight if caught unprepared with the ledger in her hands. **Noska Ur'gray** (Thug stat block, veteran training) has two Bandit bodyguards. **Emira Volstren** (Noble stat block) has a Cassalanter house guard waiting outside the inn. The only combat risk is a direct interception of Noska after the bidding concludes.

## Overview

Jalester sent word by plain-paper courier — more formal than his usual style, which told the party this was not a routine assignment. An old trade investigator named **Orvane Fentree** had been found dead in his home in the Trades Ward on Greengrass morning. The Watch ruled it natural causes. His personal records — twenty years of guild and contract documentation — were missing from his study. Laeral Silverhand had a particular interest in those records.

Fentree had died of a genuine heart attack at 71. His ledger had been stolen by **Rezka**, a Xanathar Guild operative who had been assigned to shadow him and searched the home while the Watch was still on scene. The ledger contained entries from 1244 DR showing a Neverember-linked shell company still paying maintenance fees to a vault custodian in the Waterdeep Vault district. Correctly interpreted, the entries proved the Vault of Dragons was active and located in Waterdeep — not Neverwinter, where Laeral's intelligence had been pointing for years.

Rezka had created a bidding situation of her own making. Two buyers were meeting her tonight at the Green Dragon Inn: **Noska Ur'gray**, representing the Xanathar Guild's senior intelligence cell with 600 gp, and **Emira Volstren**, a Cassalanter household representative with 400 gp and a favor. The party had three approaches: take Rezka before the first meeting, disrupt both meetings, or wait for the bidding to conclude and intercept Noska in the street.

If the ledger reached Jalester, Laeral's intelligence corrected. She now knew the vault was in Waterdeep, active, and that Dunberton Holdings was the shell structure maintaining it. Mission 6 became available.
