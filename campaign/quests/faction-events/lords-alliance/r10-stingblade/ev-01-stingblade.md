# Stingblade

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Lords' Alliance member first reaches Renown 10, which usually happens at the debrief of **The Archer Above**. Bonus Renown from earlier missions can bring a member to 10 sooner, and the Event runs the same way when it does. Jalester Silvermane names the rank at his table beside the Yawning Portal's well and gives that member the three Stingblade benefits. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn how to ask Jalester for one introduction in each quest, chosen from a fixed list of eight Waterdhavians.
> - Learn how the Alliance pays for one genuine legal incident in each quest, up to a ceiling of 500 gp.
> - Learn how to read the City Watch's files, and which kinds of file a Stingblade may ask the file officer to open.
>
> Only the member is addressed, and companions who are not Lords' Alliance members sit at the bar and gain nothing.

### The Evening After

Hold this Event until **Redknife Reached** is marked for the member. At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 10, a Watch runner finds them wherever they are lodging and hands over a sealed note. The runner is an ordinary **Commoner** in a plain tabard and waits for no reply. Who sends the note depends on where Jalester is:

- **Before Laeral Told of Jalester is marked:** Jalester writes the note and seals it with a thumbprint of grey wax. He receives the member at dusk at the corner table beside the well.
- **After Laeral Told of Jalester is marked:** Sevel Dastar writes the note, signs it "S.D." and receives the member at a table in the taproom, well away from the well.

Every qualifying member is invited on their own account, so several members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. Companions who are not Lords' Alliance members are not invited, and a companion who tags along sits at the bar. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the runner at their first surface lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the following evening.

A member marked **LA Suspended** gets no note. When **LA Reinstated** is marked for that member, the note comes at the next noon. A member marked **LA Dismissed** never receives one.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A boy in a plain Watch tabard knocks at your door a little after noon and holds out a square of paper. It is folded in thirds and sealed with grey wax, and he runs off down the stairs before you break the seal. Inside are two lines in a small, upright hand.
>
> > Corner table, this evening, after sundown. Please come alone, and please bring this note. J.S.

> [!gamemaster]**Beside the Well**
>
> The corner table stands beside the Yawning Portal's well, so anything said at it may reach something below the Portal, and Jalester does not know. This applies to every scene in this Event that takes place at the table.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Jalester keeps the list of introductions short on purpose. Each of the eight has agreed to see Alliance people once on his word, and each of them may refuse a second request.
> - The solicitor and the file officer are paid from Laeral's discretionary fund through a Castle Ward clerk. Neither knows more of the Alliance than Jalester's name and the names on his list.
> - The Watch keeps every file request in a ledger, and Jalester reads that ledger each tenday. He learns what a Stingblade asked for, though he never learns what the file said unless the member tells him.
> - The household inquiries file concerns the Cassalanter villa. The Watch has never suspected the family of anything, and nobody who reads the file learns more than the three dismissed complaints it holds.
> - Nobody in this Event can name the master of the Splinter. If a player raises him, Jalester gives the answer in **If a Player Raises Manshoon** in the **Lords' Alliance First Meeting**.

### Naming the Rank

If Jalester is receiving the member, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom of the Yawning Portal is loud tonight, with hearth smoke hanging under the beams and the great well breathing cold air past its windlass. Durnan polishes a mug at the bar and tips his head toward the corner. Jalester sits there with his back to the wall, a short list ruled in his small hand lying open beside the notebook, and he rises to shake your hand before he offers a chair.
>
> > "Thank you for coming this evening, and please sit. You're a Stingblade. The rank used to mean something more martial than it does now, and I'd like you to hear all three arrangements before you ask me anything."

If Sevel Dastar is receiving the member, read the following aloud instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The table Sevel Dastar has taken stands under the stairs, far from the well, with a tankard of small beer and a short list lying open beside it. He is a human in a plain coat, and he nods to the chair across from him without getting up.
>
> > "My name is Dastar. Silvermane is elsewhere for now, so I'm handling his people, and I'll handle you the way he would. You're a Stingblade, which opens three things, and I'll take them in order."

> [!gamemaster]**If Sevel Is the Handler**
>
> Sevel gives every rule below as written, in his own plain words, signs notes at the drop "S.D." and asks no question at the end. The solicitor, the file officer and the eight on the list accept his word exactly as they accept Jalester's.

> [!social]**The Agent**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: a lean, courteous Lords' Alliance field agent in a dark doublet, who writes in a small notebook while he listens and goes through the three Stingblade benefits in the order the member is likely to need them.
>
> Jalester is Friendly and a little tired. He asks short, specific questions, gives concise answers and thanks people formally for small favors. He says "Understood." when he accepts an answer, and before the member leaves he asks one question more specific than the talk needs. He swears rarely, and only at himself.
>
> Jalester is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Stingblade benefit gives the member, and the limit on it.
> - The eight names on the list, and what each of them can and cannot do.
> - How the drop at the Portal works, and what a request to him should say.
> - What the Watch's files hold, in general terms, and how the file officer behaves.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault or any intelligence the Alliance is acting on now. He answers each by saying that it belongs to people with a higher rank than his.

> [!qna]**What does Stingblade get me?**
>
> Jalester touches the list, then the notebook, then lays a finger on the table as he names each one.
>
> > "Three things. Once a quest I can arrange an introduction to one of eight people. Once a quest the Alliance will pay for one real legal trouble, up to five hundred gold. And you may read the Watch's files, which most informants never see."

> [!qna]**How do I reach you?**
>
> > "Leave a note with Durnan at the bar and say it's for Silvermane's friends. My reply will be waiting there, signed J.S., so please write only what the benefit needs. You can also ask me here, in the evening, if I'm at the table."

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not my companions'?**
>
> > "The Alliance writes its ranks against names, and I have written yours. Your companions are welcome at the bar, and I'm grateful for their patience. The introductions, the solicitor and the files answer to you alone."

> [!qna]**What happens if my Renown falls?**
>
> > "The rank stays with you, because you earned it. The benefits wait until your Renown is back at ten, and I'll tell you when that happens."

### Introductions

Jalester starts with the introductions, and the table is still the well table, so **Beside the Well** applies.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester turns the list around on the table so that you can read the eight lines ruled in his upright hand. He keeps his eyes on you while the Portal's noise rises and falls around the table.
>
> > "Waterdeep's magistrates and merchants are not out of reach. They simply favor people they already know. Tell me whom you'd like to meet and why, and I'll arrange it before your next operation. I can manage one a quest, from this list of eight."

> [!exploration]**Asking for an Introduction**
>
> - **Contact:** the member asks Jalester at the table in the evening, or leaves a written request with Durnan at the Portal's bar, addressed to "Silvermane's friends". The request names one person from the list and gives a reason.
> - **Delay:** Jalester's reply is waiting at the bar by the next evening, signed "J.S.", and it names the hour and the place. The meeting takes place within 3 days of the request, at the person's office or home.
> - **The meeting:** the person is Friendly toward the member for that one meeting only and gives what the list says they give. The member may bring companions, and the person talks to the member alone.
> - **Limit:** one introduction per member in each quest. A quest is one of the named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**. The request counts against the quest the party is in when the member asks, and an unused introduction is lost when the quest ends.
> - **What it never buys:** a secret, a favor against the Alliance, or an act against the Watch. Jalester gives his reason for a refusal before the use is spent, and a refusal spends nothing.
> - **Who else:** the member only. A request made in the member's name by a companion is ignored.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who lies to the person met, or who uses the meeting to threaten them, loses the benefit for a tenday. Jalester hears of it within a day.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and the introductions are suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!exploration]**The List of Eight**
>
> Jalester reads the list aloud once, and the member may copy it. Each person gives the member exactly what is written here and nothing more.
>
> - **Magistrate Barnibus Blastwind**, a magistrate of the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors, receives the member at the Hall of Justice. He gives a half-hour hearing, reads one document or one piece of physical evidence, and says in writing whether it would stand before a magistrate.
> - **Lord Torlyn Wands**, a wizard of House Wands, receives the member at his rooms. He writes a letter of standing in the Wands name, and it opens one noble door: a dinner, a servants' entrance or a steward's ear.
> - **Saeth Cromley**, a retired Watch sergeant with a network in the Dock Ward, receives the member at a table at the Yawning Portal. He names a former watchman in any one ward whom the member chooses, and that man talks about the ward's recent trouble for an hour.
> - **Valetta**, a priest of Gond at the House of Inspired Hands, receives the member at the House. She examines one device or mechanism, or repairs one, within 3 days, and she works on no magic items.
> - **Factor Tessaly Brightwater**, a factor of the Caravan Court, receives the member at her counting-house. She opens one shipment manifest, which shows the cargo, the consignee and the route for the last tenday.
> - **Guildmaster Piers Hobb**, who leads the Dock Ward haulers' guild, receives the member at the guildhouse on the quay. He gives one favor: he holds a cart, a wagon or a quay hand for one day, or he tells what moved at night.
> - **Clerk Maelith Dray**, an under-clerk in the palace chancery, receives the member in the chancery. She gives one appointment slot with a named official within 3 days, or one stamped copy of a public document.
> - **Lady Perrine Ostler**, a Sea Ward patron of the Seawatch Charitable Fund, receives the member at her townhouse. She gives an invitation to one Sea Ward gathering and makes one introduction at it.
>
> The former watchman Saeth names is Edric Moss in the Dock Ward and Tilda Frayne in every other ward. Each repeats what the ward's officers have grumbled about for a tenday, which is three items from that ward's incident log.

> [!qna]**Can I ask for someone who is not on the list?**
>
> > "No, I'm afraid not. These eight have agreed to see us, and I can't ask anyone else on the Alliance's name. Which of the eight would help you most, and what is the reason?"

> [!qna]**I already know one of them.**
>
> > "Then you don't need me for the door, but the introduction still carries what the list says. Lord Torlyn writes a letter for you only if I ask him, and it costs the same use. Is that the one you want?"

> [!qna]**Will they tell me secrets?**
>
> > "They will tell you what the list says and what any honest person would say across a table. If you ask for a secret, they'll refuse, and they'll mention it to me. Please don't waste the use on that."

### Legal Coverage

Jalester gives the second benefit without being asked, and the table is still the well table, so **Beside the Well** applies.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester closes the list and lays his pencil on top of it while a man at the next table slaps the boards and laughs. He waits for the noise to pass before he speaks, and he does not look away from you.
>
> > "If you land in front of a Watch desk or a magistrate, the Alliance has a solicitor. She is faster than you will be, and she doesn't ask whose fault it was. The trouble must be real, and it must be yours."

> [!exploration]**Calling the Solicitor**
>
> - **Contact:** the member, or a companion carrying the member's name while the member is held, tells Durnan at the bar that "Silvermane's friends need a solicitor". Durnan sends for her at once, at any hour.
> - **Delay:** the solicitor reaches the Watch house or the court within 4 hours of the word reaching the Portal. She pays what is owed directly and takes no money from the member.
> - **The ceiling:** 500 gp for one incident in each quest, covering bail, fines and damages together. The Alliance pays up to that sum, and the member pays any amount above it.
> - **What counts:** an arrest, a charge or a damages claim that is genuine. The typical costs and what the Alliance covers are in the list below.
> - **What it never covers:** a crime against the Alliance, a Lord or the Watch, and a debt to a guild or a lender. Bail for murder or treason always runs above the ceiling.
> - **What it does not do:** it pays the cost and does not remove the charge. A magistrate still hears the matter. **String-Pulling** from the previous rank can remove a minor charge before it reaches one.
> - **Limit:** one incident per member in each quest, counted against the quest the party is in when the solicitor is called. An incident the member does not use before the quest ends is lost.
> - **Who else:** the member only. A companion's own trouble is not covered, and the solicitor says so at the Watch house.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who gives the solicitor false facts loses the benefit for a tenday. So does a member who commits a crime so that the Alliance will pay for it.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and the coverage is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!exploration]**What the Alliance Pays**
>
> The solicitor works from this list, and the amounts are the usual ones in Waterdeep.
>
> - **Covered:** bail for a minor assault or a brawl costs about 50 gp. A fine for an unlicensed weapon or a breach of the peace costs about 25 gp.
> - **Covered to the ceiling:** damages for a brawl in a tavern or a shop run from 100 to 300 gp. Damages for the arson of a shop run up to 500 gp.
> - **Not covered:** bail for murder or treason, which is above the ceiling, and a debt to a guild or a lender of any size.

> [!qna]**What if the damages are more than five hundred?**
>
> > "Then she pays the first five hundred and you pay the rest. I wouldn't count on more, because Laeral's fund has a bottom. Is there trouble now that I should know about?"

> [!qna]**Can the Alliance pay a debt I owe a lender?**
>
> > "No. The solicitor is for trouble with the Watch and the courts, and a debt is a private matter. Please don't try to route one through her. She will notice, and so will I."

### Watch Files

Jalester takes a moment with the third benefit, and the table is still the well table, so **Beside the Well** applies.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester opens the notebook to a page headed with a ruled line and turns it so that you can read the two columns below. The left column is short, and the right column is longer and crossed through in two places.
>
> > "The Watch writes everything down, and most of it sits in the central records office. Standard files take an hour. Classified files take four hours and a conversation with the file officer, and she will want a reason from you."

> [!social]**The File Officer**
>
> Clerk-Sergeant Ottel Raske (Lawful Neutral, Waterdhavian Human, he/him) :: the Watch's file officer at the central records office in the Castle Ward, a stooped, careful man in his fifties with ink on his cuffs who has kept the classified shelf for nineteen years.
>
> Raske is Indifferent to strangers and Friendly toward anyone who writes down what they want before they ask for it. He asks what the member needs the file for and answers a vague reason with a long silence. He remembers every request and every refusal.
>
> Raske is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Which files exist, by their shelf names, and which class each belongs to.
> - The hours of the counter, which run from the first bell to dusk.
> - What the member intends to do with what the file shows, as far as the member will say.
>
> He will not discuss the contents of a file the member has not asked for, or any request made by another member.

> [!exploration]**Reading a File**
>
> - **Contact:** the member gives their name at the central records office counter during its hours. Jalester sends the list of Stingblade names to the office when each member reaches the rank, so no token is needed.
> - **Standard files:** the member reads the file in the back room and copies what they need by hand. This takes one hour and needs no check.
> - **Classified files:** the member sits with Raske for four hours, and a classified request fills the day. The member makes a **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check to give a reason that satisfies him.
> - **Failure:** the request is flagged and a superior reviews it, and that file stays closed to that member for a tenday. Every standard file stays open, and the member may ask again for the classified file after the tenday.
> - **Limit:** no limit applies to standard files. A member can read one classified file in a day.
> - **What leaves the room:** nothing. The member copies by hand, and companions may read what the member copies. Only the member can ask for a file.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who uses a file to commit a crime, or who sells what it shows, loses file access for a tenday. Raske reports it to Jalester within a day.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and file access is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!exploration]**The Files**
>
> The member names the file, and Raske brings it. The member chooses the ward for any file that lists one.
>
> - **Standard, ward incident logs:** one ward's reports for the last tenday, with times and Watch patrol sightings.
> - **Standard, warrants outstanding:** names, charges and the magistrate who requested each warrant.
> - **Standard, missing persons ledger:** names, the last known place and the date reported.
> - **Standard, patrol rotations:** one ward's three daily patrol windows, listed by street.
> - **Standard, the archer case file:** three killings by heavy crossbow, with the shot angles and a note on a north-facing window.
> - **Standard, the Fentree death report:** a finding of natural causes, and a clerk's note that the study had been searched.
> - **Classified, the Splinter inquiry file:** an unsolved report of unusual cart and carriage traffic at a Trades Ward safe house. It names nobody, and it calls the people involved "the Splinter".
> - **Classified, Xanathar's Guild informant file:** a Castle Ward rowhouse with stretchers carried in at night, two sworn complaints and no arrests.
> - **Classified, the household inquiries file:** three closed inquiries against one Sea Ward house, which are a missing servant, a debt dispute and a noise complaint. All were dismissed.
> - **Classified, the harbor master's carnival file:** berth inspections of the carnival fleet, every one of which passed.
> - **Classified, informant identifications:** partial names of informants the Watch has paid, which is the detail Jalester volunteers below.

> [!gamemaster]**Two Files That Matter Later**
>
> The archer case file and the Fentree death report are free leads and never the answer. They sit beside the three leads that **The Archer Above** and **Fentree's Ledger** already give, and a member who skips them loses nothing.
>
> - **The archer case file:** it shows where the shots came from and that one window faced north. It gives a fourth lead toward the Pell Street room, and it names no street number, no landlord and no suspect. Once **The Archer Above** is complete, the file is marked closed and gives nothing new.
> - **The Fentree death report:** it records a natural death, then the clerk's margin note that a money-changer in the Caravan Court reported fresh coin from a woman who gave the Green Dragon Inn as her lodging. It gives the inn and nothing else: not the room, the hour, the buyers or what she carries.

> [!qna]**Which file should I start with?**
>
> > "Whichever one answers a question you already have. A file read out of curiosity costs you an hour and Raske's patience. What do you want to know first?"

> [!qna]**What if Raske refuses me?**
>
> > "Then try again after a tenday, with a better reason than the first. The standard files are open to you the whole time. Did he give you a reason for the refusal?"

Jalester volunteers one more thing about the files, without being asked.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester taps the last line in the right column and keeps his finger there while the Portal's noise carries on around the table. He does not look at the page, and he speaks in the same level voice.
>
> > "The informant identifications are partial, because the Watch usually knows only a code name and a general side. If you want to know who else feeds the city's officers about the Grand Game, they are a place to start. I've used them that way."

Jalester does not say what he found, and he explains only what the resource is good for.

### Leaving the Table

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester writes a line in the notebook and holds his pencil over the page as the crowd around the well shifts and shouts. The well breathes cold air across the corner of the table while he waits for you to look up.
>
> > "Is there a part of that you'd like me to go over again? One more detail, if you don't mind: which of the eight would you ask for first?"

Jalester writes down whatever the member answers and says "Understood.", and nothing in the Event depends on the answer. He gives no assignment tonight, and **Fentree's Ledger** reaches the member by its own contact once the member is 6th level. Then he tucks the notebook inside his doublet, stands and walks out through the Portal's front door.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Stingblade earns further Renown from missions and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Lords' Alliance page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions everything Jalester said, and may let them read what they copy from a file. The introductions, the solicitor and the file access stay individual benefits. Durnan, the eight on the list and the file officer will not act on a request that a companion makes in the member's name.

A member whose Renown later falls, or who is marked **LA Suspended**, keeps the rank. All three benefits are suspended until the Renown is restored or **LA Reinstated** is marked. A member marked **LA Dismissed** loses all three for good.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard all three benefits, has seen the list of eight and has answered Jalester's last question.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Stingblade Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Jalester, or Sevel Dastar, names the rank at the table; read by **Warduke**, which checks it before naming the next rank. Track separately for each member the quest of the last introduction, the quest of the last legal use and the date of the last classified file request, with any file flagged.
>
> This Event also reads **Redknife Reached** (the hold), **Laeral Told of Jalester**, which decides whether Sevel delivers it, and **LA Suspended**, **LA Reinstated** and **LA Dismissed**, which **Suspension and Dismissal** marks. It also reads **Manshoon Named** and **Jarlaxle Unmasked** for the wording of the Splinter and carnival files.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three benefits are active for this member from the next morning. **Warduke** occurs when this member reaches Renown 25, and Sevel Dastar delivers it if **Laeral Told of Jalester** is marked. **Fentree's Ledger** becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level, and it arrives by its own contact. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Jalester Silvermane names a Lords' Alliance member a Stingblade and explains the rank's three benefits: introductions to eight Waterdhavians, legal coverage and access to the Watch's files.

## Summary

We were named Stingblades of the Lords' Alliance, and Jalester Silvermane showed each of us a list of eight people he can introduce us to. Once every quest the Alliance will also pay a solicitor to cover one real legal trouble, up to five hundred gold. We may read the Watch's files, and the classified ones cost four hours and a good reason. Each of us has our own rank, and our companions can only wait at the bar.
