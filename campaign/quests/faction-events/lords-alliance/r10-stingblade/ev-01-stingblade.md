# Stingblade

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — Jalester's next visit, or within a tenday — after one Lords' Alliance member's Renown reaches 10. In this Event:
>
> - Jalester marks the Stingblade rank as a meaningful threshold: the character is no longer street-level, and the Watch file system is now open to them.
> - He makes the introduction benefit concrete: one named NPC per quest, requested in advance, arranged through Alliance contacts.
> - He confirms legal cost coverage without drama — the Alliance's legal team is faster and asks fewer questions than the character will.
> - He explains the Watch file access and its DC 12 Charisma requirement for classified cases, stated plainly.
> - He volunteers one operational detail about the file system's uses — not because he was asked, but because it explains the resource.

### The Promotion

The arrangement between Jalester and the character is established by now. He finds them at the Yawning Portal or Trollskull Manor, takes them aside, and says what he came to say.

> > "Stingblade. The rank used to mean something more martial than it does now. You should know that."

A beat.

> > "Currently it means you have access to things the Watch would not show an ordinary informant."

### The Benefits

**Introductions.** Jalester frames this as a brokered service, not a favor.

> > "Waterdeep's political and merchant class is not inaccessible. It is structured. If you need to meet someone specific — a guild factor, a Lords' ally, a trading company's principal agent — tell me who and tell me why, and I will arrange an introduction before your next operation. One per quest. I have limits on what I can justify through Alliance channels."

He adds, without elaborating further:

> > "Don't waste this on someone you could have approached yourself across a tavern table."

**Legal Coverage.** The Alliance covers bail, fines, and damages — one incident per quest. He describes this the way a man describes something he has had to use.

> > "If you end up in front of a Watch desk with coin owed to someone, the Alliance has a legal team. They are faster than you, and they do not ask whether it was your fault. Once per quest. The incident must be genuine — don't try to route a legitimate business debt through it."

**Watch Files.** He takes a moment with this one.

> > "Crime reports, witness statements, active investigation files. Any case currently open to Watch review, you can request access. Standard cases take an hour and a contact I have in the central records office. Classified cases — anything involving the Masked Lords, Watch command decisions, or ongoing covert operations — those take four hours and a conversation with the file officer."

He looks at the character.

> > "The file officer will not hand over classified material without reason. You'll need to make the case for it. DC 12. I'm telling you that plainly."

He means a Charisma (Persuasion) check with the file officer. A failed attempt is noted — a superior reviews the request, and the file officer will remember it. The character may try again in a tenday.

> > [!info]**Watch File Access**
> >
> > **Standard cases:** one hour, no check required.
> >
> > **Classified cases** (Masked Lords, Watch command matters, active covert operations): four hours + DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) with the file officer. On a failed check, the request is flagged and a superior reviews it. The character may try again after a tenday; the file officer remembers previous attempts.

### A Detail of How It Works

Jalester volunteers this without being asked — a piece of operational context.

> > "The Watch files include informant identifications. Partial ones — the Watch usually knows no more than a code name and a general affiliation. But if you are trying to determine who else is feeding intelligence to city authorities on the Grand Game, the files are a starting point. I have used them that way."

He does not say what he found. He is explaining what the resource is good for.

## Overview

Jalester Silvermane promoted the character to Stingblade — the middle rank of the Lords' Alliance. The rank opens Watch crime files, Alliance legal support, and brokered introductions to Waterdeep's political and merchant class.

## Summary

Jalester delivered the Stingblade promotion and walked the character through the rank's three practical tools: Watch file access (DC 12 required for classified cases), Alliance legal coverage once per quest, and one brokered introduction per quest. He noted that Watch files include partial informant identifications, and offered this as an explanation of the access's operational value.
