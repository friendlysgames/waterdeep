# Design Notes: Stingblade

## Fixed Tables

The source gave Stingblade as four speeches with no names, no ceiling and no file list. The three benefits are now per-member procedures with a contact, a delay, a limit and a loss rule, and each reads from a fixed table in the mechanics reference. Introductions come from a list of eight, legal coverage stops at 500 gp an incident, and the Watch files are a list of eleven. A player who asks for a name off the list is told the list is closed, so nothing is left to the GM.

## Files as Free Leads

The archer case file and the Fentree death report are standard files that give one lead each. They add a fourth lead to **The Archer Above** and a free lead to the Green Dragon booking in **Fentree's Ledger**. Neither gives a name, a room or an hour, so a Stingblade still has to find the rest through the missions' own three leads. The brief already treats the archer file as a free fourth lead. Stingblade is expected after the debrief of **The Archer Above**, so the file is marked closed once that mission is complete and mostly matters to a member who reaches Renown 10 early.

## Secrecy and Wording

The Splinter file says "the Splinter" and names nobody, and the carnival file says "the carnival fleet". The household inquiries file holds three dismissed complaints and no suspicion, which keeps the Cassalanters a matter of suspicion only. Every scene at the well carries the usual GM line about the listener below. Sevel Dastar delivers the Event if **Laeral Told of Jalester** is marked.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Factor Tessaly Brightwater, Guildmaster Piers Hobb, Clerk Maelith Dray and Lady Perrine Ostler (from the mechanics reference), Clerk-Sergeant Ottel Raske, the former watchmen Edric Moss and Tilda Frayne, and the unnamed solicitor. The 4-hour solicitor delay, the counter hours and the central records office in the Castle Ward are also mine. The collision check for the four surnames was not run.

For the out-of-scope log:
- The reference says the archer killings used a hand crossbow. Vhaspar's stat block uses a Heavy Crossbow, and the older **The Archer Above** text says arrows. The M4 drafter should settle one weapon.
- The Fentree report's margin note names the Green Dragon Inn as a lodging. **Fentree's Ledger** should accept that as a free lead before its own three.
- Barnibus, Torlyn, Saeth and Valetta are not voiced here, since the Event ends at the table. Their meetings are left to the table.
