# Design Notes: Escalation to the Open Lord

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B gives the Alliance one large reward: 2 Renown for briefing Jalester with enough evidence for him to escalate to the Open Lord, once in the campaign. The restored draft added a three-part threshold of the Stone's holder, the vault in Waterdeep and two confirmed factions. It also had Jalester write the report in the room, ask three questions and send an answer the next morning. This version keeps the threshold, the report, the questions, the award and the single payment.

## What Changed

The threshold was a paragraph, and its vault element rested on "the Stone's own revelation", which made the evidence the Stone's own claim. It is now a table of three routes per element, in the shape of Force Grey's **The Full Picture**. The Stone is never accepted as vault evidence, and a missing element earns one question and a return visit. The Cassalanter line stays at suspicion, as the secrecy rule requires.

Payment went from one character to each member at the table. The order with **Fentree's Ledger** is handled by two openings and a second note. If **Ledger Delivered** is marked, Jalester says the Open Lord already knows where the gold lies, and the briefing adds the Stone and the factions.

Laeral's answer moved from a runner's two lines to a half sheet read at the well the evening after. That lets route three of **Suspension and Dismissal** work. A suspended member can sit as a witness, is not paid and is named in Laeral's note. **Laeral Told of Jalester** puts Sevel Dastar at a table away from the well.

## Departures from the Source

The restored draft had Jalester say "He has read Fentree's notes", but **Fentree's Ledger** says he has not read the ledger, so that line is gone. Its second question asked how many Eyes were active, which needs a term Jalester has not been given, so it now asks what the Stone has said or done. Its third asked which factions were seen at the vault district, which no party can name yet, so it now asks which were met face to face.

The gate of Renown 10 for the audience is removed, because the audience needs Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**. The line "available from early Act III" is removed, since the three facts are the only trigger. The legal instrument ready for the Vault is cut, because the Writ belongs to the audience.

- Open: `r25-warduke/ev-01-warduke.md` line 10 calls this Event Laeral's first direct contact. She is absent from it, and that file is being rewritten.
- Open: `m05-fentrees-ledger/ev-02-the-green-dragon.md` line 346 says the summons follows **LA Grand Game Escalated** or Renown 13. `m06-an-audience-with-the-open-lord/overview.md` line 17 says it arrives whether or not the outcome is marked.
- Open: **Vault of Dragons**, **Faction Outposts** and **Manshoon Named** are unconverted. The old drafts also expected the outpost and lair quests to read **LA Grand Game Escalated**, and they no longer do.
- Open: Laeral's Notable Figures page says moon elf, while Waterdeep: Dragon Heist has her human, and she has no social block here.
