# Escalation to the Open Lord

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires when a Lords' Alliance member presents Jalester Silvermane with intelligence meeting the escalation threshold — the party's own initiative, not a mission Jalester assigned. In this Event:
>
> - The party briefs Jalester on what they know: the Stone's current location or current holder, the vault's location in Waterdeep (not Neverwinter, which is Laeral's current working assumption), and confirmed operations from at least two villain factions.
> - Jalester goes still for a moment, then writes up a formal report while the party is in the room.
> - The character who delivered the briefing earns +2 Renown. This award fires once in the campaign.
> - Laeral Silverhand is not present. Her response comes back through Jalester within a day.
> - Laeral's direct involvement — private audience, questions, Masked Lord's Writ — is reserved for **An Audience with the Open Lord** (Mission 6, requiring Renown 10 and 7th level). This event sets that in motion.
>
> This event is available from early Act III onward. It can fire earlier if the party has the necessary confirmed intelligence during Act II.

**Background (GM only)**

At campaign start, Laeral believes the vault is in Neverwinter. Jalester's field reports and Watch contacts have placed it there. She does not know the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced in Waterdeep, that it is the key to the vault's location, or that all four villain factions are actively competing for it. She is three moves behind.

The party changes that when they bring this intelligence to Jalester. He cannot sit on information of this scope — he escalates to Laeral immediately, in writing, and she begins moving. What she does with it is political: she begins applying pressure on the Lords to attend to the vault situation, and she starts treating the party as more than street-level operatives. That shift shapes everything that follows through **Vault of Dragons**, where a party that informed her early finds her a prepared ally rather than an uncertain variable.

**The Cassalanter problem:** The Cassalanters are widely suspected of things that make people uncomfortable. Nobody in the room knows what those things are. If the party tries to cite Cassalanter involvement as one of their two confirmed villain factions, Jalester flags it: suspicion without documented evidence does not count. Confirmed factions are those the party has directly observed operating, encountered at a faction outpost or lair, or has documented material connecting to the Stone or the vault. The two most likely confirmed factions by Act III are Xanathar's Guild and Manshoon's Zhentarim.

### The Evidence Threshold

Three things are required for the briefing to meet the escalation standard. All three must be present. A partial brief earns Jalester's thanks and a note to keep going, but not the escalation.

**The Stone's current location or current holder.** The party must be able to name where the Stone is or who has it right now — not who had it previously. A holder the party knows to be current as of the last tenday qualifies.

**The vault is in Waterdeep.** Laeral's working intelligence places the vault in Neverwinter. Correcting that is the single most valuable piece of information the party can bring her. The party must have evidence beyond rumor: the Stone's own revelation, Fentree's Ledger (Mission 5), or other documented confirmation.

**At least two confirmed villain factions.** Confirmed means directly observed or documented. Suspicion does not count. The Cassalanters are a special case: evidence about them gathered before the party enters their villa is circumstantial at best. If the party raises Cassalanter involvement, Jalester asks what they have specifically. If the answer is behavioral suspicion and nothing on paper, he notes it as "unconfirmed, credible" and does not count it toward the threshold.

> [!info]**One Award**
> The +2 Renown from this briefing fires once in the campaign, for the character who delivers the qualifying intelligence. If the party returns later with additional intelligence, Jalester thanks them. The renown award does not repeat.

### The Briefing

The party finds Jalester at the Yawning Portal — his usual position at a table near the far wall, a drink he is not enjoying and notes he is not finished with. He listens to a lot of people at that table. He is used to most of it not mattering.

He listens without interrupting. Read or paraphrase the following when the party finishes:

> Jalester sets down his pen. He looks at the notes he has been taking while they spoke, then at nothing for a moment. It is the longest pause you have seen from him.
>
> Then he picks the pen back up.
>
> > "Sit there a minute."
>
> He writes. He does not narrate what he is writing. When he finishes, he folds the paper, addresses it, and sets it aside for the runner he will find on his way out.
>
> > "You're telling me Laeral thinks it's in Neverwinter."
>
> Not a question.
>
> > "She'll want to know the Stone's situation directly. That's a different conversation — I can't schedule it for you, and I won't promise when it happens. But what you've given me goes up today."

The character who delivered the briefing earns +2 Renown immediately.

Any character who asks what Laeral will do with the intelligence and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check recognizes that Jalester already knows the answer but will not speculate aloud. He is disciplined about what he says on behalf of the Open Lord.

### Jalester's Clarifying Questions

Before closing, Jalester asks three questions. Answer them in the text regardless of whether the party volunteers the information.

**"Who has the Stone right now?"** He wants a name or a location, not a faction. If the party has the Stone, he notes that without comment and does not ask them to hand it over — that is not his call to make.

**"How many Eyes are active?"** If the party does not know what this means, he explains that the Stone has three dormant functions it reveals as it awakens, and he needs to know how far along that process is. He has read Fentree's notes. If the party does not yet have the Stone or has not awakened it, he marks this unknown.

**"Which factions have you personally seen at the vault district or at a location connected to it?"** He distinguishes between factions the party has encountered around the Stone and factions the party has seen at or near the vault itself. Both are useful, but the second is rarer.

He does not share what he learns from these answers with anyone other than Laeral and, through the standard briefing chain, the Watch units she will redirect.

### Laeral's Response

Within a day — by the next morning — a runner delivers a folded note to Trollskull Manor addressed to the character who gave the briefing. The note is in Jalester's handwriting, two lines:

> *The Open Lord has received your intelligence. Continue your current line of work.*
>
> *— J.S.*

That is all. Laeral does not make direct contact at this stage. She has what she needs to begin moving. When she is ready to speak with the party directly, she will make that known through proper channels — which is Mission 6.

> [!lore]**What Changes After This**
> Laeral begins treating the Grand Game as a Waterdeep problem, not a Neverwinter accounting matter. She redirects Watch intelligence resources toward the Stone's location and starts applying political pressure on the Lords to take the vault situation seriously. The party's standing with her is now that of people who brought her an actionable brief, not names on a Watch report.
>
> In **Vault of Dragons**: a party that escalated to Laeral early will find her politically prepared, with Watch cooperation already arranged and a legal instrument ready to issue. A party that never ran this event will find her present but improvising — she will still arrive, but the resources and the preparation will not be there.

### Concluding the Event

#### LA Grand Game Escalated: True / False

Set **True** when Jalester confirms the briefing meets the escalation threshold and sends the report up. Records that Laeral Silverhand has been formally informed of the Grand Game's true scope: Stone in Waterdeep, vault in Waterdeep, multiple active villain factions. Read in **Faction Outposts** (Laeral begins coordinating Watch intel, which the party may notice), **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire**, **Kolat Towers** (Laeral's awareness shapes what Alliance resources are available), and **Vault of Dragons** (determines whether Laeral is a prepared ally or an uncertain variable).

## Overview

The party brought Jalester Silvermane a complete intelligence brief: the Stone of Golorr's current location, confirmation that the vault is in Waterdeep rather than Neverwinter, and documented operations from at least two villain factions. Jalester went still for a moment, then wrote up the report himself with the party in the room. Laeral Silverhand now knows the shape of the Grand Game.

## Summary

The party briefed Jalester Silvermane at the Yawning Portal with confirmed intelligence on the Stone's current location, the vault's Waterdeep address, and at least two active villain factions. He wrote up the report immediately and sent it to Laeral Silverhand the same day. A note arrived at Trollskull Manor the following morning acknowledging receipt and asking the party to continue their current work.
