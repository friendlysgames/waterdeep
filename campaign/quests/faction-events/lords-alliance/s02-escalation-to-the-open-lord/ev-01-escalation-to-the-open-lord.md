# Escalation to the Open Lord

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when a Lords' Alliance member brings Jalester Silvermane the Stone's holder, the vault's place and two villain factions. It ends when Laeral Silverhand's answer is read at the well table the next evening. It is the party's own initiative, and Jalester assigns nothing. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Lay out the Stone, the vault and two villain factions at the corner table beside the Yawning Portal's well.
> - Hear that the Open Lord has placed Neverember's gold in Neverwinter for three years, and put her right.
> - Answer three specific questions from Jalester, which he writes into the report he sends to the palace.
> - Watch Jalester seal the report and send it to the palace, and earn 2 Renown once in the campaign.
> - Return the next evening to hear the answer that Laeral Silverhand sends back.
>
> Only Lords' Alliance members are briefed and paid, while companions sit at the bar and gain nothing.
>
> #### Who Sits at the Table
>
> - **Members in good standing** sit at the table, give the account and are paid for it.
> - **A suspended member** (**LA Suspended**) sits at the end of the table as a witness and may state facts. Jalester neither questions nor answers a suspended witness, and the witness is not paid.
> - **Dismissed members** (**LA Dismissed**), characters marked **Lords' Alliance Offer Closed** and companions sit at the bar. A member can call any of them to the table to state a fact, and they gain nothing.
>
> If every member at the table is suspended, Jalester listens and writes, skips his three questions and pays no one. Laeral's answer still names each suspended member in its closing line.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Laeral Silverhand believes Neverember's gold lies in Neverwinter, because Jalester's field reports and the Watch's contacts placed it there three years ago.
> - She does not know that the Stone has resurfaced in Waterdeep or that the factions are competing for it. If **Ledger Delivered** is marked, the ledger has already shown her that the vault is in Waterdeep.
> - Jalester cannot hold information of this size, so he writes to her the same night and sends the report by runner.
> - Laeral never comes to the Portal, and her answer reaches Jalester within a day.
> - The Splinter is Manshoon's cell, and he commands it from Kolat Towers. Until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener, Jalester says "the Splinter" or "the other cell".
> - The fourth faction is Bregan D'aerthe, led by Jarlaxle. Jalester knows only the carnival fleet and the *Eyecatcher*'s crew, and he never repeats a name the party gives him for them.
> - Waterdhavians distrust House Cassalanter for being too rich and too well bred. Nobody at the table knows more than that, and Jalester counts it for nothing.
> - Jalester has no way to test what the Stone says, so he treats it as the party's claim and never as proof.

### Asking for Jalester

Any member can bring the account to Jalester on any evening. He sits at the corner table from dusk until the last bell, and no summons is needed. The Event fires whenever the account passes the test below, and no date or quest gates it.

> [!gamemaster]**Beside the Well**
>
> The corner table stands beside the Yawning Portal's well, so anything said at it may reach something below the Portal, and Jalester does not know. This applies to every scene at the table, and here that includes where the Stone is kept.

If **Laeral Told of Jalester** is marked, Sevel Dastar takes Jalester's place at a table under the stairs, away from the well. Use **If Sevel Is the Handler** for every reply in this Event, and leave out Jalester's closing questions.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Portal is loud with the evening crowd and hearth smoke, and Durnan sends your companions to the bar with a nod. He points the members toward the corner table, where Jalester sits beside the well with his notebook open and a pencil in his hand. He stands when you reach the table and waits until each of you has a chair.
>
> > "Durnan tells me you asked for me, so please begin with the Stone, then the vault, then the factions. I will stop you only if I need a detail."

> [!gamemaster]**If Sevel Is the Handler**
>
> Sevel Dastar (Neutral, Human, he/him, **Spy**) is plain and dry, and he may swear lightly. He takes the account at a table under the stairs, and he asks the three questions in the same order. He signs everything "S.D." and reads Laeral's answer the next evening in the same place.
>
> Because the table is not beside the well, the line in **Beside the Well** does not apply to him.
>
> > "Silvermane's elsewhere, so you've got me. Give me the Stone, the vault and the factions, and I'll write it down for the Open Lord."

### What Counts as the Account

Jalester will not send a report to the Open Lord on a partial account. The account is complete when each of the three elements below is met by any one of its routes. An element is met when a member or a companion gives the facts in the second column and says how they know.

> [!gamemaster]**The Escalation Test**
>
> | Element | The account must give | Route 1 | Route 2 | Route 3 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | The Stone's holder | Where the Stone is now and who holds it, current to the last tenday. | The party holds the Stone, and the member can say where it sleeps at night. | A faction holder is named, and the member can say who told them or what they saw. | The member watched the Stone change hands or be moved within the last tenday, and can say who and where. |
> | The vault | That Neverember's gold lies in a vault beneath Waterdeep, and how the member knows. | **Ledger Delivered** is marked, or the member read pages 47 to 52 of Fentree's fourth volume before it was sold. | Papers from an outpost, a lair or the Neverember household name the gold or its place. | An enemy named the gold or its place in a threat or a bargain, and the member can say who and when. |
> | Two villain factions | For each of two factions, what it wants from the vault and one thing it has done to get it. | The party ran an outpost chain or a lair heist against that faction and can describe its work. | The party holds a letter, ledger, map or prisoner taken from that faction. | A named contact or a captured agent described the faction's plan to the party. |
>
> - Judge each element by the second column only, and ask for no proof beyond who, where and when.
> - The Stone is never accepted as evidence for the vault, because Jalester cannot test what it says. A member who cites it is asked for a paper or a name.
> - The two factions are most likely Xanathar's Guild and the Splinter, and any other faction counts by the same routes. Jalester says "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member addressed. After that, he says "Manshoon's cell" to that member only.
> - The carnival fleet counts as a faction on the same routes. Until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked, Jalester writes down any name a player gives for the fleet and says "the carnival fleet" from then on.
> - The Cassalanters count only on those routes, and suspicion never counts. If a member offers the family on suspicion, Jalester asks what is on paper and notes it as "unconfirmed, credible" without counting it.
> - The test asks what each faction does and never why, so the family's reasons are not part of it.

> [!gamemaster]**When an Element Is Missing**
>
> Jalester stops at the first missing element in the table, asks the matching question below and writes nothing. He asks only one question even when two elements are missing, and he pays no Renown.
>
> - **The Stone:** "Where does the Stone sleep tonight, and who has held it in the last tenday?"
> - **The vault:** "What tells you the gold lies under Waterdeep, and who put it in front of you?"
> - **The villain factions:** "Which two factions have you watched work, and what did each one do?"
>
> The member can return on any evening with the missing piece, and Jalester picks up at **The Briefing** without repeating his greeting.

If an element is missing, read the following aloud, then ask the matching question as Jalester:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester lets the member finish, then caps his pencil and lays it across the notebook while the taproom noise rises around the table. He looks at the member who spoke first, asks one question and waits for the answer.

### The Briefing

The members and anyone they call to the table give their account seated, in whatever order they like. Jalester writes while they speak and asks only for a name, a place or a date.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester listens with the pencil moving, and he turns a page whenever the account moves on to a new subject. When you finish, he sets the pencil down and says nothing for a long moment while the well breathes cold air across the table. Then he takes the pencil up again.

When the account meets every row of the test, Jalester replies. If **Ledger Delivered** is marked, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester turns back through the notebook to a page near the front and rests two fingers on it before he looks up.
>
> > "Thank you. The Open Lord has had Fentree's ledger since the night you brought it, so she already knows where the gold lies. She does not know who has the Stone, or who is racing for it. That is what she lacks, and I will write it exactly as you gave it."

Otherwise, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester sets the pencil on the page and looks at each of you in turn, with his other hand flat on the notebook.
>
> > "Thank you. The Open Lord has placed Lord Neverember's gold in Neverwinter for three years, and my reports and the Watch's put it there. You are telling me it lies under Waterdeep, with the Stone and two factions in the city. I will tell her tonight that those reports were wrong."

### The Three Questions

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester turns to a fresh page, writes the date at the top and looks up again with the pencil held ready.
>
> > "Understood. I have three more questions for the report, if you don't mind, and I would like each answered as exactly as you can."

> [!gamemaster]**The Three Questions**
>
> Jalester asks the questions in this order and writes each answer into the report. None of them changes what the Event pays or what Laeral's answer says.
>
> - **"Where does the Stone sleep at night, and who else is in the room?"** The member answers as exactly as they can. If the party holds the Stone, Jalester notes it without comment and does not ask for it, because that is not his to demand.
> - **"What has the Stone said or done while it was in your keeping?"** The member describes whatever the party has seen. Jalester writes it down and does not comment, because he has no way to test it.
> - **"Which of the factions have you met face to face, and which only on paper?"** He writes the answers in two lists, and a faction known only on paper stays in the report without counting toward the test.

### The Report

Read the following aloud when the questions are done:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester draws a clean sheet toward him across the table and writes for a long time without looking up. Now and then he stops to ask one of you for a spelling before he writes on. When he is done he folds the sheet in thirds and melts a stick of grey wax over the candle. He presses his thumb into it, and a Watch runner in a plain tabard steps in from the street to wait at the bar.
>
> > "This goes to the Open Lord tonight, and I will put my name to every word. Is there anything you told me that you would like left out?"

> [!gamemaster]**What the Report Holds**
>
> Jalester lists every person who sat at the table and marks any suspended member as suspended. He writes the account as the members gave it, with his three questions and the answers. He sends it by runner to the palace the same night, and it reaches Laeral before midnight.
>
> Mark **LA Grand Game Escalated** with the name of each member in good standing who sat at the table when Jalester sealed the report. Pay their Renown as **Renown Opportunities** sets out. A suspended witness is listed in the report but is not marked and not paid.

### What Jalester Will Say

Jalester answers questions until the members stop asking. He keeps one hand on the sealed report until the runner takes it.

> [!social]**The Agent at the Table**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: a lean Lords' Alliance field agent in a dark doublet, who keeps one hand on the sealed report while he answers.
>
> Jalester is Friendly and visibly tired. He gives short, exact answers and says "Understood." when he accepts one. He thanks each member formally before they leave, and he swears rarely and only at himself.
>
> Jalester is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What he has written to the Open Lord.
> - When the party can expect the Open Lord's answer.
> - Whether the party should change any of its other work.
> - Which of the party's facts he could not confirm for himself.
>
> He will not discuss what the Open Lord will do, or anything about the Stone beyond what the party has told him.

> [!qna]**Will the Open Lord see us?**
>
> > "I can't schedule that, and I won't promise a day. What you've given me goes up tonight, and her answer comes tomorrow. Please come back to this table at dusk tomorrow to hear what she says."

> [!qna]**What will she do with it?**
>
> > "I can't speak for her, and I won't guess. She will weigh the Lords and the Watch before she moves, and she doesn't explain herself to me."

> [!qna]**Why not give this to the Watch?**
>
> > "Because the Open Lord should hear it first, and from someone she trusts. A captain would send it up a chain of clerks, and a tenday would pass."

> [!qna]**What about House Cassalanter?**
>
> > "I have heard the same talk you have, and I can't write talk down as fact. Without paper, I note it as 'unconfirmed, credible' and no more. If you find something I can read, please bring it to me."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us now?**
>
> > "Carry on with whatever you were doing, and bring me anything new. Please say plainly which parts you saw yourselves and which you only suspect."

> [!qna]**Who else will hear this?**
>
> > "No one except the Open Lord, and the Watch units she chooses to redirect, so please be discreet. Does anyone outside this table know what you told me tonight?"

> [!exploration]**What He Will Not Say**
>
> A character who asks what the Open Lord will do can make a **DC 12 Wisdom (Insight)** check. On a success, they see that Jalester knows how she tends to respond and will not say. On a failure, they see only a careful man declining to guess, and nothing depends on the result.

### Laeral's Answer

Laeral's reply reaches Jalester by midday, and he reads it at the corner table at dusk on the evening after the briefing. Every member and witness who sat can come, and a member who cannot finds the same words copied at the drop at the Portal, signed "J.S."

If **Ledger Delivered** is marked, use the second note below. Read the following aloud first:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester is at the corner table with the notebook shut and a half sheet of paper under his hand. The Portal is loud with the evening crowd and hearth smoke, and Durnan sends your companions to the bar again. He waits until each of you has a chair, then unfolds the sheet and reads it aloud.

If the ledger has not been delivered, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > Your account reached me before midnight, and I have read it twice. For three years I have believed that Lord Neverember's gold lay in Neverwinter, and I was mistaken. The Stone, the vault and the factions competing for both are now a matter for the whole city. Continue your work, and bring Jalester Silvermane whatever you learn. I will speak with you myself when the time comes. Laeral Silverhand.

If **Ledger Delivered** is marked, read the following aloud instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > Your account reached me before midnight, and I have read it twice. Fentree's ledger told me where the gold lies, and you have now told me who holds the Stone and who is racing for it. Together they are a matter for the whole city. Continue your work, and bring Jalester Silvermane whatever you learn. I will speak with you myself when the time comes. Laeral Silverhand.

If **Laeral Told of Jalester** is marked, change "Jalester Silvermane" in either note to "Sevel Dastar".

> [!gamemaster]**Naming a Suspended Witness**
>
> If a suspended member sat at the briefing, Laeral's note carries one more line before her signature for each such member, using the member's name. Jalester reads it aloud:
>
> > I have also read the name of Name, who sat at that table under suspension. I do not wish it struck from the Alliance's book.
>
> That is the line that Route Three of **Suspension and Dismissal** reads. Run that Route now, and let **Suspension and Dismissal** mark **LA Reinstated**.

Read the following aloud when the note is finished:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester folds the sheet along its creases, slips it inside the notebook and looks around the table.
>
> > "That is the whole of her answer, and I thank you for coming back to hear it. If the Stone changes hands again, which of you will bring me the word?"

### Renown Opportunities

Each participating Lords' Alliance member gains 2 base Renown for bringing Jalester the account, once in the campaign. Award it to each member at the table at the moment Jalester seals the report. Companions, suspended witnesses, dismissed members and characters marked **Lords' Alliance Offer Closed** gain none.

The Event offers no bonus Renown. The sitting pays a suspended witness nothing, and a later reinstatement does not repay it.

A member who missed the sitting can give the account at the corner table on any later evening, from what they learned at the table. Jalester hears them out and writes no second report. He adds the member's name to **LA Grand Game Escalated**, and the member gains the same 2 Renown.

### Aftermath

Laeral now treats the Grand Game as a Waterdeep problem and turns Watch intelligence toward the Stone's holder. She presses the Lords to take the vault seriously, and she thinks of the party as people who brought her a usable account.

**The Green Dragon** reads **LA Grand Game Escalated**, so a ledger delivered afterward proves who paid for the vault and no longer shows where it lies. **An Audience with the Open Lord** opens with what Laeral already holds, and **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) finds her prepared, with Watch cooperation already arranged. A party that never runs this Event still meets her at the Vault, but without that preparation.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when Jalester has read Laeral's answer and the members have no more questions. It reads **Ledger Delivered**, **Manshoon Named**, **Jarlaxle Unmasked**, **LA Suspended**, **LA Dismissed**, **Lords' Alliance Offer Closed** and **Laeral Told of Jalester**, and it writes one outcome.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **LA Grand Game Escalated** — mark with the name of each member in good standing who sat when Jalester sealed the report. Add the name of any member who gives a later sitting. Read by **The Green Dragon**, which changes Jalester's debrief, and by **An Audience with the Open Lord**, which changes Laeral's opening. Also read by **Warduke**, which sets Laeral's first words to the member. Also read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted), which sets whether Laeral arrives prepared.

No Lords' Alliance event waits on it, and **An Audience with the Open Lord** keeps its gate of Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**. ## Overview

A Lords' Alliance member brings Jalester Silvermane the Stone's holder, the vault's place and two villain factions, and he sends the account to Laeral Silverhand.

## Summary

We sat at the corner table beside the well and told Jalester who held the Stone, where the vault lay and which two factions we had watched at work. He wrote a report to the Open Lord while we sat there, and he asked us three questions of his own. The next evening he read us her answer, which told us to carry on and to bring him whatever we learned next. Will she ever send for us?
