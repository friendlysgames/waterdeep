# Warduke

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Lords' Alliance member first reaches Renown 25. Jalester Silvermane names the rank at his table beside the Yawning Portal's well, and Laeral Silverhand receives the member at Piergeiron's Palace the next morning. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Learn how to call four Warrior Veterans from the Watch for one operation in each quest.
> - Receive a sheet on the villain factions' response teams every third day, left at the Portal's drop.
> - Learn how to send one written request in each quest to a Masked Lord whom Jalester will not name.
> - Meet Laeral in person and learn how to ask her for one private audience in each quest.
>
> Only the member is addressed, and companions who are not Lords' Alliance members sit at the bar and gain nothing.

### The Evening After

Hold this Event until **Stingblade Reached** is marked for the member. At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 25, a Watch runner finds them wherever they are lodging and hands over a sealed note. The runner is an ordinary **Commoner** in a plain tabard and waits for no reply. Who sends the note depends on where Jalester is:

- **Before Laeral Told of Jalester is marked:** Jalester writes the note and seals it with a thumbprint of grey wax. He receives the member at dusk at the corner table beside the well.
- **After Laeral Told of Jalester is marked:** Sevel Dastar writes the note, signs it "S.D." and receives the member at a table in the taproom, well away from the well.

Every qualifying member is invited on their own account, so several members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. Companions who are not Lords' Alliance members are not invited, and a companion who tags along sits at the bar. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the runner at their first surface lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the following evening.

A member marked **LA Suspended** gets no note. When **LA Reinstated** is marked for that member, the note comes at the next noon. A member marked **LA Dismissed** never receives one.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A boy in a plain Watch tabard knocks at your door a little after noon and holds out a square of paper folded in thirds. It is sealed with grey wax, and he runs off down the stairs before you break the seal. Inside are three lines in a small, upright hand.
>
> > Corner table at dusk. Please come alone, and please bring this note. You will be wanted at the Palace tomorrow morning, so keep it free. J.S.

> [!gamemaster]**Beside the Well**
>
> The corner table stands beside the Yawning Portal's well, so anything said at it may reach something below the Portal, and Jalester does not know. That includes every operation the member describes aloud and every request they speak. This applies to every scene in this Event that takes place at the table, and it does not apply to the Palace.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The four Veterans are seasoned Watch soldiers whom Laeral's office lends for one operation at a time. They follow the member's plan and report to their own captain afterwards.
> - Jalester reads the Watch's sightings of the response teams before he writes each sheet. A sheet is only as fresh as the last time someone saw the team.
> - The Masked Lord is a Lord of Waterdeep who owes Laeral a favor and acts through her office. The Lord is never named, and it is neither Mirt nor Jelenn Urmbrusk.
> - Jalester does not know which Lord it is, and he says so truthfully whenever he is asked.
> - Laeral reads a member's file herself from this rank on. If **An Audience with the Open Lord** has not run for the member, she has met them only on paper.
> - Nobody in this Event names the master of the Splinter. Until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener, Jalester, Sevel and Laeral say "the Splinter" or "the other cell".
> - Nobody in this Event names the owner of the carnival fleet. Until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the listener, the speakers say "the carnival fleet".
> - The Alliance has no proof against the Cassalanter household. The sheets call its crews unconfirmed and credible, and nobody here says why the household wants the gold.

Durnan polishes a mug at the bar and tips his head toward the corner. Jalester sits there with his back to the wall, and a small bundle of folded sheets lies beside his notebook and an untouched tankard. He rises to shake your hand before he offers a chair.
>
> > "Thank you for coming this evening, and please sit down. You're a Warduke, which is the senior field rank we give. Three benefits are mine to explain tonight, and the fourth is Laeral's to explain tomorrow. Please hear all of them before you ask me anything."

If Sevel Dastar is receiving the member, read the following aloud instead:

> [!readaloud]
>

>
> > "My name is Dastar. Silvermane is elsewhere for now, so I'm handling his people, and I'll handle you the way he would. You're a Warduke, and three things are mine to give tonight. She gives the fourth herself in the morning."

>
> Sevel gives every rule below as written, in his own plain words, signs notes at the drop "S.D." and asks no question at the end. The Veterans' captain and the Palace clerks accept his word exactly as they accept Jalester's.

> [!social]**The Agent**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: a lean, courteous Lords' Alliance field agent in a dark doublet, who writes in a small notebook while he listens and goes through the Warduke benefits in the order the member will need them.
>
> Jalester is Friendly and a little tired. He asks short, specific questions, gives concise answers and thanks people formally for small favors. He says "Understood." when he accepts an answer, and before the member leaves he asks one question more specific than the talk needs. He swears rarely, and only at himself.
>
> Jalester is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Warduke benefit gives the member, and the limit on it.
> - How the drop at the Portal works, and what a request to him should say.
> - What the Veterans will and will not do in the field.
> - What the Palace appointment is, and who will be there.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault or any intelligence the Alliance is acting on now. He answers each by saying that it belongs to people with a higher rank than his.

> [!qna]**What does Warduke get me?**
>

>
> > "Three things tonight, and Laeral explains the fourth herself. Four veterans from the Watch will fight beside you for one operation in each quest. A sheet on the villain crews arrives every third day. Once a quest, I carry a written request to a Lord I will not name."

>

### The Veterans

Jalester starts with the Veterans, and the table is still the well table, so **Beside the Well** applies.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester turns the notebook so that you can read four short lines ruled on the open page. A man at the next table slaps the boards and laughs, and Jalester waits for the noise to pass before he speaks.
>
> > "Four veterans from the Watch will fight beside you for one operation in each quest. I need three days' notice, and I need the place, the hour and the aim. They are soldiers, so please give them a task that a soldier can do."

> [!exploration]**Calling the Veterans**
>
> - **Contact:** the member leaves a written request with Durnan at the Portal's bar, addressed to "Silvermane's friends", or hands it to Jalester at the table. It names the quest, the operation, the surface place where it starts, the hour and the aim.
> - **Notice:** three days. Jalester's reply is waiting at the bar by the next evening, signed "J.S.", and it either confirms the meeting place or gives a reason for refusing. A request with less notice is refused and spends nothing.
> - **The operation:** any task inside a named quest on the surface of Waterdeep, including its cellars and sewers. The Veterans do not go down the Portal's well or into Undermountain. The six Lords' Alliance missions are not eligible, because their scenes fix who stands where.
> - **Service:** four Veterans meet the member at the place and hour named. They stay until the operation ends or is abandoned, or until three days have passed, and then they return to their captain.
> - **Limit:** one operation per member in each quest. A quest is one of the named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**, and events and rank events are not quests. The use counts against the quest the party is in when the Veterans arrive, and a call withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent. An unused call is lost when the quest ends.
> - **What they refuse:** the Veterans will not attack the Watch, a Lord, a captive or a creature that has surrendered, and they will not leave the city. Jalester turns back a request that needs any of these, and nothing is spent.
> - **Several members:** each Warduke has a use of their own. Two members who ask for the same operation at the same hour share one team, and only the first to ask spends a use.
> - **Who else:** the Veterans follow the plan of the member who called them, and they take no order from a companion who is not a Lords' Alliance member.
> - **Losses:** a Veteran who falls is replaced before the member's next call, and the use stays spent.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who uses the Veterans for a crime, or orders them against the list above, loses the service for a tenday. The Veterans leave when the order is given, and their captain reports to Jalester.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and the service is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!gamemaster]**The Veterans as Allies**
>
> Use the 2024 **Warrior Veteran** stat block for each of the four, unmodified. The senior of them is a grey-haired woman who speaks for the team, and none of the four has a name.
>
> In CR 2.0 terms, a Warrior Veteran is a CR 3 ally with Power 30 for levels 5 to 10 and 25 for level 11 and above. The number who fight is the number of player characters minus one, to a cap of four, and the rest hold the rear and add nothing. Party Power with the fighting Veterans added, and the Bruising budget in brackets:
>
> | Level | 3 PCs | 4 PCs | 5 PCs |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 5 | 156 (93.6) | 218 (130.8) | 280 (168) |
> | 6 | 165 (99) | 230 (138) | 295 (177) |
> | 7 | 183 (109.8) | 254 (152.4) | 325 (195) |
> | 8 | 192 (115.2) | 266 (159.6) | 340 (204) |
>
> At levels 9 and 10, add 30 for each fighting Veteran, and from level 11 add 25 for each. The four are a major force, because they add 120 to a five-character party's 220 at level 8. Build the fight from the allied total, or keep it at the base budget and treat the Veterans as the reserve, and pick one for the scene.
>
> #### The Veterans' Tactics
> To begin combat, the Veterans form a line in front of the member and anyone who cannot take a hit. During combat, they:
>
> - Fight with the Greatsword in melee and the Heavy Crossbow at range, and use Parry against the first melee hit each round.
> - Focus the enemy that is hurting the member or a companion the most.
> - Keep the Veterans who do not fight at the rear and at the named exit.
>
> A Veteran withdraws toward the exit the member named when it is reduced to 32 Hit Points or fewer, and it takes a fallen character with it if it can reach one. The team stops when the member calls it off. It does not attack the Watch, a Lord, a captive or a surrendered creature.

When the team arrives for its operation, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Four people in Watch grey stand at the place you named, with breastplates under plain cloaks and no tabards. The oldest is a grey-haired woman with a greatsword across her back, and she steps forward while the other three check the street in opposite directions.
>
> > "We're the Open Lord's loan to you, and we'll follow your plan until it ends. We won't touch the Watch, a Lord or anyone who has laid down their arms. Tell us where we stand."

> [!qna]**Will they follow any plan?**
>
> Jalester takes a moment before he answers.
>
> > "Any plan that is lawful, and most that are merely unwise. They will say so once if they think it is unwise, and then they will do it. Is there an operation you have in mind?"

> [!qna]**Can they come below the city?**
>
> > "No. They are the city's soldiers, and they will not go down the well. Everything above it is open to them."

### The Sheets

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester rests two fingers on the small bundle of folded sheets and slides it a few inches toward you. The bundle is tied with grey thread, and the top sheet is covered with short lines in his upright hand.
>
> > "Every third day a sheet will wait for you at the bar. It tells you where the response teams of the villain factions were last seen, and what each one is made of. Please burn each sheet when the next one arrives."

> [!exploration]**The Response-Team Sheet**
>
> - **Contact:** a sheet, folded in thirds and signed "J.S.", waits at the Portal's bar every third day, and Durnan hands it to the member who says "Silvermane's friends". The first sheet comes on the third morning after the rank is named.
> - **Cadence:** a sheet arrives every third day while the member holds the rank. If none has arrived by the fourth day, a team has moved or the courier is compromised, and Jalester says so at the table.
> - **Position:** the sheet gives the ward where each team last met the party. If the party has not met a team, it gives the ward of that faction's most recent outpost the party hit, and if neither applies it says "not located".
> - **Names:** a sheet names a team only after the member has met it. Until then it says "the first crew", "the next crew" or "the third crew", and it never names a leader.
> - **Wording:** the sheet says "the Splinter" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member, and "the carnival fleet" until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked. After that it may say "Manshoon's cell" and "Bregan D'aerthe". It never mentions imps, devils or a pact.
> - **Limit:** no limit applies, and the sheets are free. The sheet never reproduces a team's orders or its loot.
> - **Who else:** the member only. Companions can read the sheet, and Durnan hands nothing to a companion who asks.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who sells a sheet, shows one to a faction's agent or leaves one where it can be read loses the sheets for a tenday. Jalester hears of it within a day.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and the sheets are suspended until the Renown is restored.

### The Masked Lord

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester sets his pencil down on the notebook and glances toward the door before he looks at you. He rests both hands flat on the table and waits until you look up.
>
> > "There is a Lord of Waterdeep who owes Laeral a debt. I will not name that Lord, because I do not know the name. Once a quest, you may give me a written request, and the Lord may act on it."
>
> He holds your eye while the noise of the taproom carries on around the table.
>
> > "I'm telling you this accurately. I do not know who it is either."

> [!exploration]**Writing the Request**
>
> - **Contact:** the member writes the request and hands it to Jalester at the table, or seals it and leaves it with Durnan. It names one of the three forms below and gives the details that form needs.
> - **Answer:** Jalester's reply is waiting at the bar by the next evening, signed "J.S.", and it accepts the request or gives his reason for refusing it. The use is spent only when he accepts.
> - **Delivery:** on the fourth morning after Jalester accepts, the Lord's answer is waiting at the drop, in a sealed packet with no signature.
> - **Limit:** one request per member in each quest, counted against the quest the party is in when Jalester accepts. A discussion, a refusal or a withdrawn draft spends nothing, and an unused request is lost when the quest ends.
> - **Refused:** any request that harms the city, hides an attack on non-combatants or needs authority beyond Waterdeep.
> - **Who else:** the member only. The Lord does not give the member's name to anyone, and a request made in the member's name by a companion is ignored.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who gives false facts in a request, or who takes anything but copies from a sealed room, loses the benefit for a tenday. Jalester hears of it within a day.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and the requests are suspended until the Renown is restored.
>
> The three forms are fixed:
>
> - **Door:** one locked or sealed civic, guild or records room is entered under a Lord's key-warrant. The member names the room, the reason and the hour. The warrant admits the member and up to four companions for one hour. It does not cover a temple, a noble's private house or an Alliance office, and nothing leaves the room but copies.
> - **Seal:** the Watch freezes one named inquiry, with no searches, no arrests and no records read. The member names the inquiry by subject and ward. The freeze starts on the fourth morning and lasts ten days. It may not cover a murder or treason inquiry, or one into the Alliance or a Lord, and it does not end the inquiry.
> - **Pressure:** a letter of political pressure goes to one guild master, magistrate or noble house. The member names the target and one narrow ask, which is a delay, a meeting or a signature. The target does what the letter asks unless it would cost more than a tenday's income. The target may refuse, and the Lord does not give the member's name.

> [!gamemaster]**The Lord Nobody Names**
>
> The Lord is a fixed fact that no scene ever reveals. The answer comes back unsigned, and Jalester or Sevel can say only "a Lord I will not name". If a player guesses Mirt, the handler answers that he does not know and asks the player not to guess aloud at this table. The Masked Lord's Writ in **An Audience with the Open Lord** is a separate sheet for **Vault of Dragons** and does not share a use with this benefit.

> [!qna]**Why will you not name the Lord?**
>
> > "Because I don't know, and because Laeral can't acknowledge the Lords' names in public. I have never asked, and I advise you not to."

> [!qna]**What if the Lord refuses?**
>
> > "Then I will tell you before the use is spent, and you may write another request. The Lord answers to Laeral and not to us, so I can't press."

### The Long Room

The Palace has no well, so **Beside the Well** does not apply here. A Watch sergeant at the south gate reads the card, searches the member and any companions, and leads them through three corridors to Laeral's audience chamber. A clerk in the corridor asks how the member takes their tea, and a cup is waiting at the member's place in the room.

Up to two companions may come, and they sit with the member at the near end of the table. The chamber is 40 feet long and 24 feet wide, with an 18-foot ceiling, tall windows down the left wall and a table that seats twelve. Laeral waits alone, and the sergeant stands outside the double doors.

>
> - **Second meeting:** read the second-meeting passage, and add the doubt line if **Laeral Doubts the Party** is marked.
> - **First meeting:** read the first-meeting passage, and add the account line if **LA Grand Game Escalated** is marked for the member.
> - **Laeral Told of Jalester:** this decides only her answer to the question about Jalester, below.

> [!social]**The Open Lord Receives You**
>
> Laeral Silverhand (Chaotic Good, Moon Elf, she/her) :: the Open Lord of Waterdeep, a tall silver-haired elf in a plain grey gown, who keeps a cup of tea at her elbow and a small book under her hand.
>
> Laeral is Friendly and brief. She speaks in complete, measured sentences, lets a silence run until someone fills it and never raises her voice or swears in office. She uses a character's full name when she is displeased, and she gives each member fifteen minutes and wastes none of them.
>
> Laeral will discuss the following topics:
>
> - What a private audience can give, and how to ask for one.
> - How closely each villain faction is watching the member.
> - What the city's law makes of one act the member is planning.
>
> She will not discuss her own strength, the Blackstaff, Khelben Arunsun or the name of any Masked Lord, and she hands out no quest.

> [!qna]**What can I ask you for?**
>
> > "A Read or a Ruling. A Read tells you how closely one faction is watching you, in my own words. A Ruling tells you what the city's law makes of one act you are planning, and I write it down."

> [!qna]**Will you give us a task?**
>
> > "No. Assignments come from the Alliance's desk, and I am here to hear you. If I need something done, the Alliance will ask you in the usual way."

> [!qna]**How is Jalester?**
>
> If **Laeral Told of Jalester** is marked, Laeral answers with a small nod toward the door.
>
> > "He works at a desk in this building now, and he is well. He has not complained."
>
> In every other case, she answers in the same level voice.
>
> > "He is where he has always been, and he does the work well."

The introduction ends with her parting words, and it spends no audience.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral writes a line in her small book and closes it over her thumb. She stands, and the sergeant at the double doors steps forward to lead you out.
>
> > "Good work. That will be all."

> [!exploration]**A Private Audience**
>
> - **Contact:** the member asks Jalester at the table, or leaves a written request with Durnan at the bar. It names the result, either a Read with the faction or a Ruling with the act and the place.
> - **Delay:** Jalester's reply is waiting at the bar by the next evening, signed "J.S.", and it gives the audience on the third morning after he accepts, at 09:00 in the long room. The audience lasts fifteen minutes.
> - **Who attends:** the member, and up to two companions who sit and listen. Laeral speaks to the member alone, and a companion cannot ask for a result of their own.
> - **Limit:** one audience per member in each quest, counted against the quest the party is in when the audience is held. The introduction above spends nothing. A request withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent.
> - **Refused:** a Read of a Lord or the Alliance, a Ruling on an act against the Watch, a Lord or the Alliance, and any request for a quest, the Stone or the vault. Jalester gives his reason before the use is spent.
> - **Laeral's Ear:** a character who knowingly says something false at an audience makes a **DC 16 Charisma (Deception)** check. On a failure Laeral says "That will be all", the audience ends and the use is spent. A character who corrects the statement before she moves on is treated as having told the truth.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who brings a companion that attacks, threatens or lies to her loses the benefit for a tenday.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and the audiences are suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!exploration]**The Read**
>
> The member names one faction: Xanathar's Guild, the Splinter, the Cassalanter household or the carnival fleet. Laeral says the line below for that faction's current escalation tier, taken on the day of the audience. She says "the Splinter" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member, and "Manshoon's cell" after that.
>
> - **Unaware:** the faction has not noticed the party.
> - **Suspicious:** the faction has begun to ask who the party is.
> - **Alert:** the faction has the party's names and has set people to watch them.
> - **Lockdown:** the faction has closed its doors and will meet the party with force.
>
> She adds one sentence, which is that the answer holds for about a tenday and she would not rely on it for longer. She says nothing about why a faction wants anything, and she names no crew.

> [!exploration]**The Ruling**
>
> The member names one act and one place in Waterdeep. The act must be one the member means to commit within ten days. Laeral names its class and writes it in the chancery book, and the member may name a second act at the same audience.
>
> - **Minor:** an act on the overlook list, which is brawling without grievous injury, carrying an unlicensed weapon openly, trespass without theft or a breach of the peace. Laeral writes a ruling number on a slip. A Watch officer shown the slip within ten days treats that act at that place as settled and writes no charge. The slip covers the member alone.
> - **Serious:** theft, arson, forced entry into a home or damage to property. Laeral names the charge and the fine a magistrate would set, and she gives no cover.
> - **Forbidden:** a killing, an attack on an officer or a Lord, or damage to Watch property. Laeral says "I will not rule on this, and I ask you not to do it", and nothing is written down.

> [!gamemaster]**Running the Audience**
>
> Laeral gives the result named in the request and then answers the member's questions until fifteen minutes have passed. She ends the audience herself with "That will be all." The Palace sergeant waits outside the doors and hears nothing. The two passages below show how she gives a result, and any other faction or tier uses the same shape.

If the member asked for a Read and the faction is Suspicious, read or paraphrase the following, using the faction's name:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral opens her small book to a marked page and reads one line without looking up.
>
> > "The Guild has begun to ask who you are. That holds for about a tenday, and I would not rely on it for longer."

If the member asked for a Ruling and the act is Minor, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Laeral writes a number on a slip, signs it and slides it across the table to you.
>
> > "That is a minor matter, and this number says I have weighed it. Show the slip to any officer within ten days."

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Warduke earns further Renown from missions and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Lords' Alliance page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions everything Jalester and Laeral said. The Veterans, the sheets, the Masked Lord requests and the audiences stay individual benefits. Durnan, the Veterans' captain and the Palace clerks will not act on a request that a companion makes in the member's name.

A member whose Renown later falls, or who is marked **LA Suspended**, keeps the rank. All four benefits are suspended until the Renown is restored or **LA Reinstated** is marked. A member marked **LA Dismissed** loses all four for good.

A member who does not keep the Palace appointment keeps the rank and the other three benefits. The clerk holds the appointment for ten days. After that, the first audience the member requests includes the introduction and spends the quest's use.

## Overview

Jalester Silvermane names a Lords' Alliance member a Warduke and explains three benefits: four Watch veterans for one operation, a sheet on the villain response teams and a request to a Masked Lord. Laeral Silverhand receives the member at the Palace and explains the fourth, a private audience.

We were named Wardukes of the Lords' Alliance, and Jalester Silvermane explained how to call four veterans from the Watch for one operation in each quest. A sheet on the villain response teams will wait for us at the Portal's bar every third day, and we can send one written request to a Lord he will not name.
