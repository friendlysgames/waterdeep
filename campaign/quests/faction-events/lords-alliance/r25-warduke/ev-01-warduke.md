# Warduke

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause — Jalester's next visit, or within a tenday — after one Lords' Alliance member's Renown reaches 25. In this Event:
>
> - Jalester arranges a different kind of meeting: a specific time and location, given a day in advance.
> - **Laeral Silverhand** is present. She says four sentences, confirms the private audience benefit in person, and leaves. This is her first direct contact with the character unless the **Escalation to the Open Lord** event has already fired; if it has, she acknowledges the prior meeting without dwelling on it.
> - Jalester delivers the response team and intelligence benefits operationally.
> - The Masked Lord benefit is delivered by Jalester, not Laeral — she cannot acknowledge the Lords' identities publicly.
>
> **Background (GM only)**
>
> Laeral's brief appearance at a Warduke promotion is not ceremonial. Warduke is the threshold at which she begins treating a character as a known quantity rather than a field operative in someone else's reports. Her four sentences are a first impression she is forming, and she is letting the character know she is forming it. She is not there to be impressive. She is there to see them.
>
> The Masked Lord who takes a discreet interest at Warduke is predecided by the campaign but their identity is never revealed to the character, to Jalester, or to the party. All written requests through Jalester receive a single Lord's consideration — discreet, effective, and anonymous.

### The Meeting

Jalester sends a note the day before — not requesting a meeting, telling the character to be at the Yawning Portal at the ninth bell. He is already there when they arrive. So is **Laeral Silverhand**, standing near the far wall in a traveling cloak with no visible escort. She does not look like the Open Lord unless you already know what to look for.

Jalester speaks first.

> > "You've reached Warduke. I'm going to cover what that means operationally, and then someone else will say something brief."

### The Benefits — Jalester

**Response Team.** He is precise about what four Veterans means in the field.

> > "One operation per quest. I authorize them personally — you do not go above my head for it. Give me the target location, the objective, and a realistic entry window. They know their work. Do not ask them to do anything that would end their careers."

**Intelligence on Faction Response Teams.** He slides a folded document across the table — a map grid with notations.

> > "Current estimated positions of all four villain faction response teams. Updated every three days through Watch contacts. The updates come to you through the standard Alliance drop, same channel as the safe house keys. If the positions haven't updated in four days, assume something changed and proceed accordingly."

**The Masked Lord.** He pauses here in a way he does not usually pause.

> > "There is a Masked Lord who has taken a discreet interest in your work. I cannot tell you their name. I won't know their name. What I can tell you is this: once per quest, if you submit a written request through me — specific ask, specific problem — that Lord will use their authority to open a door, seal an investigation, or apply political pressure. The response comes back through me. No attribution."

He looks at the character.

> > "I am telling you this accurately. I do not know who it is either."

### The Benefits — Laeral

**Laeral Silverhand** steps forward from the wall. Four sentences:

> > "Private audiences. One per quest. You arrange them through Jalester, and I will make time."

She looks at the character for a moment.

> > "I'm not doing this to make you feel important."

A beat.

> > "Good work."

She nods to Jalester and leaves. The exchange takes less than two minutes.

> > [!lore]**On the Private Audiences**
> >
> > The Warduke private audience benefit is distinct from **Mission 6** (An Audience with the Open Lord). Mission 6 is a specific diplomatic encounter with a defined political outcome — the Manshoon offer, the Masked Lord's Writ, the intelligence exchange. Warduke audiences are consultations: the character can ask Laeral for her read on a situation, request she apply her authority to a specific problem, or brief her directly. She is not a quest dispenser at these meetings. One per quest; arrange through Jalester.

### Concluding the Event

#### Warduke Reached: True / False

Set **True** when Jalester and Laeral complete the promotion and the character has the map grid in hand. Read in the **Lioncrown** rank event (`r50-lioncrown/ev-01-lioncrown.md`), which does not fire until this flag is True. Response team access, Masked Lord requests, faction response team intelligence, and Laeral private audiences are tracked from this point.

Jalester Silvermane promoted the character to Warduke — the senior field rank of the Lords' Alliance. Laeral Silverhand was briefly present and confirmed the private audience benefit in person.

## Summary

The Warduke promotion was delivered at the Yawning Portal with Laeral Silverhand present. Jalester covered the operational benefits: a response team of four Veterans (one operation per quest, his authorization required), updated intelligence on all four villain faction response team positions every three days through the Alliance drop, and access to an anonymous Masked Lord's political authority once per quest through Jalester. Laeral confirmed she would meet with the character directly — one audience per quest, arranged through Jalester — and then left.
