# Design Notes: Warduke

## Benefits as Procedures

The source gave Warduke as four speeches and one line of Laeral's. Each benefit is now a per-member procedure with a contact, a notice, a limit, a refusal list and a loss rule. The four Veterans use the 2024 Warrior Veteran block at Power 30, with the fighting count set at the player characters minus one. They refuse the Watch, a Lord, captives and anyone who has surrendered, and the mechanics reference also has them refuse to leave the city. This event reads that as no descent into Undermountain. The response-team sheet and the Masked Lord table are copied from the mechanics reference without change, so the sheet names a crew only after the member has met it.

## Laeral and the Two Branches

The source put Laeral at the Portal as the member's first direct contact. **An Audience with the Open Lord** already seats the party in her room at Renown 13, and most members reach it well before Renown 25, so that line was wrong for them. The listener below the Portal's well is also a reason to keep her away from it. This event moves her to the long room at the Palace the next morning. The long room keeps the size stated in the Open Lord's audience. The event branches on **Writ Issued**: if it names the member, this is a second meeting and she never calls it a first. If it does not, she is meeting the member for the first time, and **LA Grand Game Escalated** adds one line. Her four keeper sentences survive, and a measured silence stays between them. The private audience is a Read or a Ruling. The Harper audience is an urgent Watch response to a dated danger, and the two do not overlap.

## Departures from the Source

The source named a Masked Lord whom the campaign decided in advance. The Lord is now never named, and the GM block says it is neither Mirt nor Jelenn Urmbrusk. The three request forms are Door, Seal and Pressure, which differ from the Harper forms, and the sheet carries the Splinter and the carnival fleet under those labels, and mentions no imps or devils. Laeral never admits her decline here, and the Lioncrown event is the one place that does.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented:
- The Read, which gives a faction's escalation tier in Laeral's words, and the Ruling with its three classes.
- The 09:00 appointment, the fifteen-minute length, the two companions and the three-morning delay.
- The unnamed grey-haired Veteran who leads the team, the chancery slip and the tea question.
- The rule that the sheet arrives with Durnan's "Silvermane's friends".

For the out-of-scope log:
- **An Audience with the Open Lord** describes Laeral as meeting the party for the first time. If a member reaches Renown 25 before it, this event has already introduced them, and that mission's opening lines should allow for it.
- Laeral is a moon elf on her Notable Figures page and human in Dragon Heist. This event follows the page.
- The Factions Guide Warduke row says "all four villain faction response teams" and "Veterans", and its private-audience wording does not match the Read and Ruling.
- The Warrior Veteran's refusal to leave the city is read here as barring Undermountain, so Lioncrown should not expect the team below.
