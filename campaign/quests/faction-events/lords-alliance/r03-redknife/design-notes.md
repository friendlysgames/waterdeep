# Design Notes: Redknife

## Individual Procedures

Each Redknife benefit is a per-member procedure with a contact, a notice rule, a limit and a loss rule. Requests go through Durnan's drop behind the Portal's bar, which the Lords' Alliance First Meeting already defines, and Jalester answers by note signed "J.S." The escort is two Guards for one errand a tenday on 24 hours' notice. The whistle is a second, unannounced call for four Guards, once a tenday. The two houses share a six-night allowance, and the string is one minor charge or report a tenday. Nothing pools across the party, and companions get nothing.

## What Changed From the Source

The draft gave every benefit as a speech with no limits, no contact and no counters. The old text sent notes "through the usual drop" without saying where the drop was, so the escort now runs through Durnan. The Cloak overlook and the string no longer overlap. The overlook is an officer's choice on the street before anything is written. The string removes a charge or report after it is on paper. The Factions Guide lists the Watch patrol schedules at Renown 3. The First Meeting already delivers them as the Cloak's patrol note, so this Event does not repeat them.

## Reading by Other Events

Bregan D'aerthe **Three Nights** puts two Guards at the manor door at the start of round five when a Redknife is in the party. The whistle patrol arrives in 1d4 + 1 minutes, so it cannot be that rule. The Manor Beat is a separate, fixed arrival for the manor only, and it spends no whistle. Doom Raiders **Davil's Arrest** has a Redknife ask Jalester for a courtesy note that succeeds without a check. A Word to a Sergeant is that note, and it is limited to one per case so it cannot stand in for a release.
