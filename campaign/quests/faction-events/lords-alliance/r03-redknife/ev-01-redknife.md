# Redknife

>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary

>
> - Jalester finds the character and pulls them aside briefly; he treats this as professional business, not ceremony.
> - He states the rank name exactly once, then moves directly to the practical changes.
> - He hands over a folded slip of paper with two safe house addresses — Castle Ward and Trades Ward.

> - He closes with the string-pulling benefit and its limits, plainly stated.

### The Promotion

Jalester seeks out the character at the Yawning Portal or Trollskull Manor — whichever is easier. He does not announce the meeting in advance. He takes the character aside from the rest of the group; not dramatically, just a half-step away from the noise.

He does not offer his hand. He says:

> > "You're a Redknife. That means a few things change."

He pulls a folded paper from his doublet and sets it on the table or presses it into the character's hand.

### The Benefits

**Watch Escort.** Jalester explains the escort arrangement precisely. He has filed this paperwork before.

> > "Two guards, one errand per tenday. Give me a day's notice — written, through the usual drop — and they will be where you need them. They are Watch officers. Don't use them for anything the Watch itself couldn't do officially."

**Safe Houses.** The folded paper has two addresses in Jalester's small, precise script. One in the Castle Ward, one in the Trades Ward.

> > "Clean rooms, maintained. Keys are with the building manager at each address — give the phrase 'Alliance weather.' Don't bring anyone inside who hasn't been vetted. If you need to clean up a scene before the Watch arrives, you've already lost the point."

**String-Pulling.** He saves this for last, and says it carefully.

> > "Minor charges. A Watch report that could be quietly revisited. Once per tenday, I can make one disappear through channels. Once. Minor. If you've done something that requires more than that, come to me before you go to the desk sergeant."

He looks at the character.

> > "Questions?"

He means it. He will answer specific questions about how any of the three benefits work in practice. He will not elaborate on the Alliance more broadly — he has said what he came to say.
