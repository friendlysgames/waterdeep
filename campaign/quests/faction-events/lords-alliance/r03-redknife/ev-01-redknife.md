# Redknife

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Lords' Alliance member first reaches Renown 3, which usually happens at the debrief of **The Long Watch**. Jalester Silvermane names the rank at his table beside the Yawning Portal's well and gives that member the three Redknife benefits. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Hear the rank named and ask Jalester how each benefit works.
> - Learn how to request a Watch escort of two Guards for one dangerous errand every tenday. The member also learns how to call a patrol with a whistle once every tenday.
> - Receive the addresses of two Alliance safe houses, one in the Castle Ward and one in the Trades Ward.
> - Learn how to ask Jalester to make a minor charge or a Watch report disappear once every tenday. The member also learns how to ask him for a courtesy note to a Watch sergeant.
>
> Only the member is addressed, and companions who are not Lords' Alliance members sit at the bar and gain nothing.

### The Evening After

At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 3, a Watch runner finds them wherever they are lodging and hands over a note. The runner is an ordinary **Commoner** in a plain tabard and waits for no reply. Who sends the note depends on where Jalester is:

- **Before Laeral Told of Jalester is marked:** Jalester writes the note and receives the member at dusk at the corner table beside the well.
- **After Laeral Told of Jalester is marked:** Sevel Dastar writes the note and signs it "S.D." He receives the member at a table in the taproom, well away from the well.

Every qualifying member is invited on their own account, so several members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. Companions who are not Lords' Alliance members are not invited, and a companion who tags along sits at the bar. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the runner at their first surface lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the following evening.

A member marked **LA Suspended** gets no note. When **LA Reinstated** is marked for that member, the note comes at the next noon. A member marked **LA Dismissed** never receives one.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A boy in a plain Watch tabard knocks at your door a little after noon and holds out a folded square of paper. He runs off down the stairs before you finish reading, and the paper holds three lines in a small, upright hand.
>
> > Corner table, this evening, after sundown. Please bring only yourself. J.S.

> [!gamemaster]**Beside the Well**
>
> The corner table stands beside the Yawning Portal's well, so anything said at it may reach something below the Portal, and Jalester does not know. This applies to every scene in this Event that takes place at the table.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The Guards are ordinary Watch officers lent by their sergeants on Jalester's word. They owe the member courtesy and nothing more, and they obey their sergeant before anyone else.
> - The Alliance rents both safe houses through a Castle Ward clerk, and retired Watch staff keep them. The managers tell Jalester each tenday who stayed and for how long.
> - String-pulling works because Jalester's seal makes a sergeant pause. It removes the paper and leaves the deed, so a victim who complains again is heard in the ordinary way.
> - Nobody in this Event can name the master of the Splinter. If a player raises him, Jalester gives the answer in **If a Player Raises Manshoon** in the **Lords' Alliance First Meeting**.

### Naming the Rank

If Jalester is receiving the member, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom of the Yawning Portal is loud tonight, with hearth smoke under the beams and the great well breathing cold air past its windlass. Durnan polishes a mug at the bar and nods toward the corner table. Jalester sits there with his back to the wall, and his notebook lies open beside a folded paper and a tin whistle on a cord. He rises, shakes your hand once and waits until you have a chair.
>
> > "Thank you for coming this evening, and please sit down, because I'd like you to hear all three arrangements before you ask anything. You're a Redknife. That means a few things change, and each of them comes with a limit that I'll state plainly."

If Sevel Dastar is receiving the member, read the following aloud instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The table Sevel Dastar has taken stands under the stairs, far from the well. A tankard of small beer sits beside a folded paper and a tin whistle on a cord. He is a human in a plain coat, and he nods to the chair across from him without getting up.
>
> > "My name is Dastar. Silvermane is elsewhere for now, so I'm handling his people, and I'll handle you the way he would. You're a Redknife, which means three things change, and I'll go through them in order."

> [!gamemaster]**If Sevel Is the Handler**
>
> Sevel gives every rule below as written, in his own plain words and signs notes at the drop "S.D.", and he asks no question at the end. The managers of the safe houses and the Guards accept his word exactly as they accept Jalester's.

> [!social]**The Agent**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: a lean, courteous Lords' Alliance field agent in a dark doublet, who writes in a small notebook while he listens and goes through the three Redknife benefits in the order the member will use them.
>
> Jalester is Friendly and a little tired. He asks short, specific questions, gives concise answers and thanks people formally for small favors. He says "Understood." when he accepts an answer, and before the member leaves he asks one question more specific than the talk needs. He swears rarely, and only at himself.
>
> Jalester is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Redknife benefit gives the member, and the limit on it.
> - How the drop at the Portal works, and what a note to him should say.
> - Why the rank belongs to one member and not to the whole party.
> - What the Watch will and will not do for a Redknife.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault or any intelligence the Alliance is acting on now. He answers each by saying that it belongs to people with a higher rank than his.

> [!qna]**What does Redknife get me?**
>
> Jalester touches the whistle, then the paper, then the notebook as he names each one.
>
> > "Three things. The Watch will lend you two Guards for one dangerous errand every tenday, and a patrol will answer your whistle once every tenday. You have two safe houses, one in the Castle Ward and one in the Trades Ward. And once every tenday I can make a minor charge or a Watch report disappear."

> [!qna]**How do I reach you?**
>
> > "Leave a note with Durnan at the bar and say it's for Silvermane's friends. He takes it without a word, and he has never lost one. My reply will be waiting there, signed J.S., so please write only what the benefit needs."

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not my companions'?**
>
> > "Because the Alliance writes its ranks against names, and I have written yours. Your companions are welcome at the bar, and I'm grateful for their patience. The Guards, the houses and the rest answer to you alone."

> [!qna]**What happens if my Renown falls?**
>
> > "The rank stays with you, because you earned it. The benefits wait until your Renown is back where it was, and I'll tell you when that happens."

### The Watch Escort

Jalester starts with the escort, and the table is still the well table, so **Beside the Well** applies.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester turns the notebook so that you can see a short list ruled in his small, upright hand. He keeps his eyes on you while he speaks, and the Portal's noise rises and falls around the table.
>
> > "The Watch will lend you two Guards for one dangerous errand every tenday. I need a day's notice in writing, through Durnan. They are Watch officers, so please don't use them for anything the Watch couldn't do officially."

> [!exploration]**Requesting an Escort**
>
> - **Contact:** the member leaves a written request with Durnan at the Portal's bar, addressed to "Silvermane's friends". It names the errand, the place and the hour.
> - **Notice:** the request must be left at least 24 hours before the hour named. Jalester's reply is waiting at the bar within twelve hours, signed "J.S.", and it either confirms the meeting place or gives a reason for refusing.
> - **The errand:** one dangerous errand inside the walls of Waterdeep. Examples are carrying coin or evidence through a rough ward, guarding a witness or standing watch at a meeting. It must be work the Watch could do officially. It must also finish within twelve hours of the hour named.
> - **The escort:** two **Guards** meet the member at the named place and stay until the errand ends or twelve hours pass. They defend themselves and the member, start no fights, chase no one and take any prisoner to the nearest Watch house. They are never counted as combatants in an encounter.
> - **Refused errands:** Undermountain, anywhere outside the city walls, and anything that needs a crime. Jalester refuses these in his reply, and nothing is spent.
> - **Limit:** one errand per member each tenday, counted from the day of that member's last escort.
> - **Who else:** companions walk with the member and the Guards, but a companion cannot ask for an escort. Jalester ignores a request made in the member's name by anyone else.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who orders the Guards to break the law or to leave the errand loses the escort for a tenday. The Guards leave at once when the order is given. Their sergeant files a report.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the escort is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!qna]**What counts as dangerous?**
>
> > "Anything the Watch might do with an escort of its own. That means carrying coin or evidence through a rough ward, walking a witness to a magistrate or standing watch at a meeting. It has to stay inside the walls and finish within twelve hours."

> [!qna]**Will the Guards fight for us?**
>
> > "They'll defend themselves and you, and they'll stand where you put them. They won't start a fight or chase anyone, and they won't break the law for you. Please don't plan a battle around them."

Jalester hands over the whistle once he has finished with the escort.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester lifts the tin whistle by its cord and lays it in your palm while the Portal's noise rises around the table. It is the plain kind every Watch officer carries, with a small notch cut into the mouthpiece.
>
> > "Blow this on a lit street when you're in trouble, and a patrol will come. You can call them once every tenday. They'll take a few minutes, because the nearest post is some streets away."

> [!exploration]**The Whistle**
>
> - **Contact and notice:** none. The member blows the tin whistle Jalester handed over tonight, and every post in the city knows the notch in the mouthpiece.
> - **Place:** any paved street inside the city walls that is lit, whether by daylight or by lamps. Alleys, courtyards, cellars, sewers, rooftops, the inside of a building and anywhere outside the walls are not covered.
> - **The patrol:** four **Guards** from the nearest post reach the member 1d4 + 1 minutes after the whistle. They order everyone to stand down, arrest anyone who attacks an officer or refuses, and take the member's account. They are never counted as combatants, and most fights are over before they arrive.
> - **Limit:** one whistle per member each tenday, counted from the day of that member's last whistle. A whistle blown with no danger spends the use, and Jalester sends that member no escort for a tenday afterward.
> - **Who else:** only the member's own whistle calls a patrol, and the Guards treat everyone standing with the member as the member's party.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who blows the whistle to draw the Watch onto an innocent person loses it for a tenday. A lost whistle is replaced at the bar the next evening.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the whistle is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!qna]**How fast will they come?**
>
> > "Between two and five minutes, depending on the street and the hour. A fight will usually be over before they arrive. Then they separate whoever is left, take names and write a report."

> [!gamemaster]**The Manor Beat**
>
> Trollskull Manor stands on a Watch beat of its own, and Jalester has told the sergeant that a Redknife lives there. At the start of the fifth round of any fight at the manor that includes a Redknife, two **Guards** from that beat reach the front door. They come whether or not anyone has blown a whistle, and this uses none of the member's tenday whistle. The Guards do not fight and do not count as combatants, and the attackers flee at the sight of them. **Three Nights** in the Bregan D'aerthe set reads this rule.

### The Safe Houses

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester unfolds the paper on the table and turns it around so that the two lines face you. Each holds an address in the same upright hand, one under the word Castle and one under the word Trades.
>
> > "These are clean rooms with a manager at the door. Give the phrase 'Alliance weather' at the counter, and you'll be given a key. Please bring nobody inside who hasn't been named. If you need to clean up a scene before the Watch arrives, you've already lost the point."

> [!exploration]**The Two Houses**
>
> - **The Castle Ward house:** 9 Quillmakers' Court, two rooms above a stationer's shop. The manager is Odette Brandle, a retired Watch clerk in her sixties who sorts quills at the counter. The exits are the front stair, a kitchen door onto the courtyard and a window onto the neighbouring roof.
> - **The Trades Ward house:** 14 Ironwick Lane, two rooms above a shut dyer's workshop. The manager is Garrick Tull, a lame former Watch quartermaster who sweeps the yard. The exits are the yard door, a loading hatch onto the lane and a roof hatch.
> - **The rooms:** each house has four cots, a barrel of rainwater, three days of dry rations, bandaging and two changes of plain clothes. No weapons are kept there. Neither house stands near Kolat Towers or Phaulkonmere.
> - **Phrase:** the member says "Alliance weather" at the counter or the yard gate, and the manager answers "Rain by morning" and hands over the key.
> - **Notice:** none. The manager holds the key from noon until midnight, and no one else arranges the rooms.
> - **Who may enter:** the member and up to three guests, each named to the manager. The manager writes the names in a ledger, and nobody unnamed gets a key or a bed.
> - **How long:** up to three consecutive nights in one house, and no more than six nights in any tenday across both. Either house counts as a safe place for a long rest.
> - **What it is not:** a hiding place from the law. The Watch knows both addresses, and the managers open the door to an officer carrying a magistrate's warrant.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who brings a pursuer, a captive, a corpse or stolen goods inside, or who fights there, loses both houses for a tenday. The manager reports to Jalester within a day.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the houses are suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stationer's shop at 9 Quillmakers' Court smells of lamp oil and sealing wax. A grey-haired woman in an ink-stained apron is sorting quills into bundles at the counter, and she keeps her hands on them when you come in. She nods once at the phrase and sets a brass key in front of you.
>
> > "Rain by morning, I'd think. It's the second floor, and the stair is steep, so mind your head."

> [!qna]**Who else uses the houses?**
>
> > "Other members, and sometimes a Watch witness who needs a quiet night. You won't meet them, because the managers keep the nights apart."

> [!qna]**What if the Watch comes to the door?**
>
> > "The manager opens the door if the paper is signed by a magistrate. Then you answer whatever you're asked. The houses keep your enemies off you, and a warrant still gets in."

### String-Pulling

Jalester saves this for last, and the table is still the well table, so **Beside the Well** applies.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester closes the notebook over his pencil and rests both hands on the cover while the taproom noise swells around the table. At the next table a man laughs and slaps the boards, and Jalester waits for the noise to pass before he speaks.
>
> > "Once every tenday I can make one minor charge or Watch report disappear. It has to be small, and you have to tell me what happened. If it is more than that, come to me before you see the desk sergeant."

> [!gamemaster]**Overlook and String-Pulling**
>
> The Cloak overlook from the **Lords' Alliance First Meeting** is an officer's choice on the street, made before anything is written down. String-pulling begins after a charge or a report is on paper. It removes the paper through a clerk, and only Jalester can ask for it.

> [!exploration]**Pulling a String**
>
> - **Contact:** the member asks Jalester at the table in the evening, or leaves a note at the Portal's drop. The request names the ward, the date, the officer and what the charge or report says.
> - **Delay:** Jalester's note is waiting at the bar by the next evening, and it reads "Done" or gives a reason for refusing. A charge he removes counts as never written by the next noon.
> - **What it covers:** one charge or one report that names the member, of a kind the Cloak overlook lists. Those are brawling without grievous injury, an unlicensed weapon carried openly, trespass without theft and a breach of the peace. It also covers a fine of 25 gp or less.
> - **What it never covers:** theft, a killing, damage to Watch property, anything done to an officer, or a matter already before a magistrate. Jalester gives his reason before the use is spent.
> - **Limit:** one string per member each tenday, counted from the day of that member's last string, and a refusal spends nothing.
> - **Who else:** the member only. A charge against a companion is not covered, and Jalester says so.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who lies to Jalester about the facts loses the benefit for a tenday. A member who uses a string to cover an offence off the list loses it for a tenday as well.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 3 keeps the rank, and the string is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!exploration]**A Word to a Sergeant**
>
> This is a separate use that spends none of the tenday string. **Davil's Arrest** in the Doom Raiders set reads it.
>
> - **Contact:** the member asks Jalester at the table or by note, and names the Watch officer and the case. The officer must be a sergeant or lower.
> - **What he writes:** a courtesy note asking the officer to review the case on its merits. Jalester sends it the same day, and no check is needed to obtain it.
> - **What it does:** the officer receives the member and sends the case up for review. It releases no prisoner, clears no charge and settles no verdict.
> - **Limit:** one note per case, with no tenday limit. Jalester refuses a note for a murder, a treason, or a case against the Alliance, a Lord or the Watch, and he gives his reason.
> - **Loss rule:** a suspended or dismissed member gets no note.

> [!qna]**How is this different from what the Watch overlooks already?**
>
> > "An officer can ignore a small thing on the street, before anything is written. Once a charge or a report is on paper, only I can take it off. I do that through a clerk."

> [!qna]**Can you do it for my friends?**
>
> > "No, it covers you and nobody else. If a friend is in trouble, tell me what it is, and I'll say what I can do."

### Leaving the Table

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester writes a line in the notebook and holds his pencil over the page as the crowd around the well shifts and shouts. The well breathes cold air across the corner of the table while he waits for you to look up.
>
> > "Is there a part of that you'd like me to go over again? One more detail, if you don't mind: which ward do you sleep in most nights?"

Jalester writes down whatever the member answers and says "Understood.", and nothing in the Event depends on the answer. He gives no assignment tonight, and **The Exile's Return** reaches the member by its own contact once the member is 3rd level. Then he tucks the notebook inside his doublet, stands and walks out through the Portal's front door.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Redknife earns further Renown from missions and from the ordinary rewards listed in the Lords' Alliance page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions everything Jalester said. The escort, the whistle, the houses, the string and the sergeant's note stay individual benefits. Durnan, the managers and the Watch will not act on a request a companion makes in the member's name.

A member whose Renown later falls, or who is marked **LA Suspended**, keeps the rank. All three benefits are suspended until the Renown is restored or **LA Reinstated** is marked. A member marked **LA Dismissed** loses all three for good.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard all three benefits, holds the paper and the whistle, and has answered Jalester's last question.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Redknife Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Jalester, or Sevel Dastar, names the rank at the table; read by **Stingblade**, which checks it first. Also read by **Davil's Arrest** in the Doom Raiders set, where a Redknife can ask Jalester for a courtesy note. **Three Nights** in the Bregan D'aerthe set reads it too, because the manor's beat sends Guards. Track separately for each member the date of the last escort, whistle and string, and the nights used in the houses this tenday.
>
> This Event also reads **Laeral Told of Jalester**, which decides whether Sevel delivers it, and **LA Suspended**, **LA Reinstated** and **LA Dismissed**, which **Suspension and Dismissal** marks.

## Overview

Jalester Silvermane names a Lords' Alliance member a Redknife and explains the rank's three benefits: a Watch escort, two safe houses and string-pulling.

## Summary

We were named Redknives of the Lords' Alliance, and Jalester Silvermane gave each of us a tin whistle and the addresses of two safe houses. Every tenday he will lend us two Guards for a dangerous errand, send a patrol to our whistle and make one small charge or Watch report disappear. Each of us has our own rank, and our companions can only wait at the bar.
