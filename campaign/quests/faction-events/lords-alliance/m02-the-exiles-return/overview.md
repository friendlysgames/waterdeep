# The Exile's Return: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member reaches Renown 3 and 3rd level, after **The Long Watch**. Companions can help with everything except Jalester's brief and debrief, which only members attend. A member marked **LA Suspended** receives no Brief until **LA Reinstated** is marked.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> The fight is a Standard encounter against a **Bandit Captain** with **Bandits** and, at five characters, a **Tough**. Two **Kenku** act as lookouts and start a four-round Watch clock. The **Lords' Alliance Mechanics Reference** audits the rosters for three, four and five characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Lords' Alliance members who report to Jalester.

## Hook

At 09:00 a Watch runner brings each Lords' Alliance member a card sealed in grey wax. That evening Jalester gives the order in a shuttered room above a glover's shop in the Castle Ward. An exile named Harko Swornhold is back in the Dock Ward, and the Alliance wants him found within three nights and "quietly put to the sword".

## Background

Harko Swornhold is fifty and was exiled three years ago after he tried to bribe Magister Kylynne Silmerhelve. He came back about two months ago and took work recruiting kenku for the street crews of Xanathar's Guild. He set up in a rope warehouse in Mudwagon Court, behind the Muleskull on Ship Street. Two weeks after he returned, a Splinter fixer named Veralax began paying him to carry messages between the Splinter and those crews. Harko took the coin and has carried nothing.

Jalester knows about the Guild work from a Watch informant. The law gives him no better remedy than another exile, which would last only three months, so the Alliance has chosen the sword.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Harko will surrender when he is hurt. He has been offered exile twice, has nowhere left to go and would sooner be a prisoner than a corpse.
> - The cipher note in his coat is a Guild courier's note that names "K", who is Keln Trigos of the Terasse Estate. Nobody in this mission can know that.
> - A thin Splinter broker named Sarvos Miln delivered Veralax's last purse at the back door of the Skewered Dragon. Harko does not know his name, only his face.
> - Lira, the Muleskull server who sees the end of the fight, is no agent of any faction.
> - Nobody in this mission names Manshoon. Jalester says "the Splinter", and Harko says "the Zhentarim, the bad kind".

## The Brief

Jalester gives the order in person in a closed room on Rannick Lane, away from the well table, because he will not say it in a crowded taproom. He states the three-night deadline in his own words and closes with a question about the Muleskull. Companions stay at the Manor.

## Three Ways to the Court

A fencewoman on Net Street, a retired pilot at the Muleskull and a Dungsweepers' Guild night man each name the rope warehouse in Mudwagon Court. Any one of them is enough, and a failed check delays a lead by a day and no more. A party that follows two or more also learns where both kenku sit.

## The Rope Warehouse

The party can reach the kenku unseen, talk Harko into a hearing or fight him. If either kenku whistles, two Guards reach the door at the end of round 4. Harko surrenders at half his Hit Points.

## The Turn

Harko offers what he knows in place of his life. The party reads the cipher note in his coat and learns where Veralax meets him. Then it chooses whether to kill him, hand him to the Watch or let him leave the city.

## The Witness

Lira walks into the court as the noise dies. What the party says to her in the next minute decides whether she reports it to the Watch, and whether Jalester has to bury the report.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Lords' Alliance member gains 2 base Renown for finding Harko and reporting to Jalester at the Yawning Portal, whether Harko lived or died. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** the matter ends with no Watch patrol at the warehouse and no report from Lira.
- **+1 Renown:** Harko's cipher note is recovered and given to Jalester.
- **+1 Renown:** Harko is delivered alive to the Watch or to Jalester.

Companions who are not Lords' Alliance members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Harko Taken Alive** is read by **The Red Wizard's Price** and by **Faction Outposts** (unconverted). **Terasse Cipher Taken** is read by **Faction Outposts** (unconverted) as a fourth path into the Terasse Estate. **Thayan File Obtained** is read by **The Red Wizard's Price**, where the sealed Hall of Justice file lowers the DC of one approach by 2 and makes **Hand Esloon to the Watch** automatic. It is marked only when Harko is alive in Watch or Alliance custody and tells his account.

A job that went loud costs each participating member the Overlook and String-Pulling for a tenday or two. **The Red Wizard's Price** becomes available at Renown 5 and 4th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance): delivers the order in person and hears the report at the well table.
- **Harko Swornhold** (Xanathar's Guild): an exiled Bandit Captain who recruits kenku for the Guild, takes coin from the Splinter and surrenders when hurt.
- **Veralax** (the Splinter): a fixer dressed as a gem merchant, who meets Harko at the Tarred Rope and is never seen in play.
- **Sarvos Miln** (the Splinter): the thin broker with ink-stained fingers who carries Veralax's coin, known here only by Harko's description.
- **Keln Trigos** (Xanathar's Guild): the "K" of the cipher note, named only in GM text.
- **Lira** (Independent): a Muleskull server in her early twenties who sees the end of the fight.
- **Marit Oake**, **Halvard Teague** and **Ibb Marsh** (Independent): a Net Street fencewoman, a retired pilot and a Dungsweepers' Guild night man, each of whom names the warehouse.
- **Two kenku** (Xanathar's Guild): the paid lookouts who whistle for the Watch.

## Dangers & Enemies

Harko uses the **Bandit Captain** block, with **Bandits** and, at five characters, a **Tough**. He fires his Pistol from behind crates and surrenders at 26 Hit Points or fewer. The two **Kenku** carry nothing, never count in the roster and surrender if their way out is blocked. A whistle brings a patrol of two **Guards** at the end of round 4, and a Watch report turns the job loud.

## Overview

Jalester orders the party to find an exiled adventurer in the Dock Ward and put him to the sword within three nights. The adventurer turns out to be worth more alive.
