# Design Notes: The Exile's Return

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C's LA-2 and the WDH row supply the order, "Find him and quietly put him to the sword", and Jalester's "Quietly." Harko is a fifty-year-old Bandit Captain who was exiled for bribing a magistrate. He has two kenku and a cipher note naming K at the Terasse Estate, and a Muleskull server sees what the party does. The source names three ways to find him, which are a fencewoman on Net Street, a retired pilot at the Muleskull and a night worker. Its dramatic question is what "quietly" means to a party that meets a witness.

## What Changed

The three source leads replace the draft's chain of eight-hour Investigation checks with a three-failure counter. Each lead alone names the warehouse, and a failed check delays a lead by a day for a small payment. That satisfies the Three Clue Rule without stranding the party.

Jalester briefs in person in a closed room, which is one of the four deliberate breaks from the well table. The debrief is at the well.

The draft's "harder assignments" are replaced by a stated cost. A job that goes loud, from a patrol or from Lira's report, spends the Overlook and String-Pulling that **Cloak** and **Redknife** would otherwise give, for a tenday, or two if Lira was threatened. The Watch Escort is never available on this job.

The surrender at 26 Hit Points is the turn of the mission. Killing, handing over and releasing Harko each leave a different outcome.

## Departures from the Source

Appendix C says Harko will not surrender, but the roster design and the brief both depend on his surrender. The fight is in a rope warehouse in Mudwagon Court, behind the Muleskull. That keeps Lira's back door close. The draft's backup location on Wharf Mast Lane is cut.

The draft's "either" order is restored to the sword. The Veralax payoff, a missing Splinter liaison at **Faction Outposts**, is dropped because nothing reads it. The Thayan file is a sealed Hall of Justice file compiled from Harko's questioning, so it is marked only when Harko is alive in Watch or Alliance custody and tells his account. A released Harko gives no file.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented here:

- Marit Oake, Halvard Teague and Ibb Marsh, the three contacts.
- Rannick Lane and the glover's shop.
- Mudwagon Court. The draft had Mudwagon Row, which is kept as the street that leads to it.
- The Tarred Rope on Dock Street, and the "every third day" meeting.
- The Coin Alley crossing and the sail loft, as the cipher note's two targets.

Kylynne Silmerhelve, Veralax and Lira are draft names with no Notable Figures pages, so they are voiced from the event text. Sarvos Miln appears here only through Harko's description.

Items for the out-of-scope log:

- The Esloon Bezant page lists him as featured in this mission, but this Event never names him. The file that Harko's questioning yields reaches him only in **The Red Wizard's Price**, and the listing belongs there.
- **Faction Outposts** (unconverted) must read **Terasse Cipher Taken** as a fourth path to the eastern staircase on Elsambul's Lane. It must also read **Harko Taken Alive** for the Guild's Dock Ward crews.
- Silmerhelve is also a Waterdhavian noble name, and the draft's use of it for a magistrate is kept.
- The mechanics reference lists no Overlook or String-Pulling cost, so the "job went loud" rule is this event's own. The Redknife event and the First Meeting should read it as a use of the once-per-tenday limits.
