# The Archer Above

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Investigation Event begins when Jalester Silvermane briefs a Lords' Alliance member at the well table in the Yawning Portal. It ends at the same table ten days after the handover, when he brings the dossier. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Jalester's brief and learn that a heavy-crossbow killer has shot three people from high windows.
> - Find the feed store on Pell Street through three independent leads, and look for the shooter's room.
> - Catch Vhaspar Coldwater at his table, or race him across the rooftops of the North Ward.
> - Hear him name Halek Farran, and identify the partial address among his belongings.
> - Warn the fourth target, and hand Vhaspar to Jalester at the Watch house.
> - Receive the dossier from Jalester at the well table ten days later.
>
> Only Lords' Alliance members attend Jalester's brief and debrief. Their companions can help with everything in between.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Vhaspar Coldwater is a retired Watch sharpshooter of fifty-seven who served twenty years in the Dock Ward. He now takes private contracts, and he owes no loyalty to the client who hired him.
> - Halek Farran is a hired agent working a household errand for the Cassalanters. He is a neat man in his fifties who smells of cedar and turns his signet ring inward, and he is not villa staff. He pays Vhaspar through an agency, a written note and a drop box.
> - The three dead people saw a fence the household had hired buy goods from a broker. The household wanted no witnesses, and Halek gave Vhaspar 400 gp for the three killings.
> - Vhaspar has killed every fifth night, and the last killing was last night. The fourth target is a Dock Ward longshoreman named Hollen Brask, and the next killing falls on the fifth night after the brief.
> - If Vhaspar dies, or if the cleaners reach him before the party does, Halek leaves Waterdeep within 48 hours and nobody names him.
> - Nobody in this Event knows why the household wanted the witnesses silenced. Jalester, Saeth and Vhaspar speak only of a family matter and a rich house that is too well liked to be clean.
> - Jalester sends no Guards and asks for no string-pulling. He wants the Alliance's name kept out of every Watch report.

### The Brief

Jalester waits at the corner table beside the well at dusk on the first evening after a member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level. The Event begins when that member arrives, and the companions sit at the bar where Durnan can see them.

> [!gamemaster]**Beside the Well**
>
> The corner table stands beside the Yawning Portal's well, so anything said at it may reach something below the Portal, and Jalester does not know.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom is crowded tonight, and the cold air from the well stirs the smoke under the beams. Jalester Silvermane sits at the corner table with his notebook open and a folded sheet of paper beside it. He rises to shake each of your hands in turn, and he waits until everyone has found a chair.
>
> > "Thank you for coming. Three people have been shot dead in the past twelve days, on the streets where the North Ward meets the Sea Ward. Each was struck by a heavy crossbow bolt from a high window, and nobody saw the shooter. The Watch has no suspect, and I want him alive. Whoever paid him matters more to me than he does."

> [!readaloud]
>
> He unfolds the sheet to show a map of the two wards, with three crosses and thin pencil lines running up from each of them.
>
> > "He has killed every fifth night, and the last was last night. The next falls on the fifth night from now. I need him in the North Ward Watch house by the evening of the fourth day. Do any of you know the rooftops north of Trollskull Alley?"

> [!social]**The Agent at the Well**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: the Alliance's field agent, here at his table by the well with a map and a deadline.
>
> Jalester is Friendly and a little tired, and he writes down every answer in his notebook. He asks one question more specific than the talk needs, such as which roofs a member has climbed. He says "Understood." when he accepts an answer, and he thanks each member formally for coming.
>
> Jalester is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The three dead, and what the Alliance thinks they saw.
> - What he wants from the shooter, and why alive.
> - The map, the Watch house and the card that opens its cells.
> - The deadline, and what happens if a member misses it.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault or any intelligence outside this case.

> [!qna]**Who are the dead?**
>
> Jalester turns a page in the notebook and reads the three names to himself before he answers.
>
> > "A candle-seller, a clerk from a Sea Ward counting-house and a woman who sold ribbons. Nothing joins them that I can find, and none of them had an enemy. I think they saw something they should not have, and someone paid to make sure they never spoke."

> [!qna]**Why alive?**
>
> > "A dead archer tells us nothing, and the man who paid him will simply hire another. I want the name on the contract. If the shooter hesitates, tell him the Watch can keep him breathing until he stands before a magistrate."

> [!qna]**Where is he?**
>
> > "A Watch informant saw a grey-haired man with a long canvas roll go to ground north of Trollskull Alley five days ago. That is all I have. I have drawn the angles of the three shots on this map, in case you can use them."

> [!qna]**Can the Watch help?**
>
> Jalester slides a plain white card across the table.
>
> > "Not openly, because the patrols are stretched thin and I want the Alliance's name out of every report. At the Watch house, show this card to the desk sergeant. He will hold a prisoner on my word."

> [!qna]**What if he fights?**
>
> > "He was Watch-trained, so he will not fight like a brawler. I would still rather you took him without bloodshed, but I will not blame you if he leaves you no choice. Please keep the people on the streets out of it."

> [!qna]**What if we miss the evening?**
>
> > "Leave word with Durnan, and tell me why, and I will understand. If I have heard nothing by the evening of the fourth day, I will take the job as abandoned. Is that clear?"

When the members agree, Jalester writes the date in his notebook, says "Understood." and goes back to his map. A member who refuses the brief, or who lets the fourth evening pass without trying anything, is what **Suspension and Dismissal** reads. A member who tries and fails has done what the brief asked.

### Three Ways to Pell Street

Jalester's informant gives a district and no door. The party needs the feed store on Pell Street, one block north of Trollskull Alley, before the fourth evening. Three leads each name the building, and any one of them is enough. A party that follows all three also learns the roofline. A failed check costs time and never ends the search.

> [!exploration]**The Linen Merchant**
>
> Any character who canvasses the shops along Tarnath Street and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check finds a linen merchant. The merchant saw a grey-haired man walk into Yander's Feed Store on Pell Street a week ago. He says the man walks like a soldier and carries a long roll of canvas, and he has come and gone at odd hours since then.
>
> - **On a failure:** The shopkeepers shrug and send the character to the chandlers. The same character can try again after an hour and a 1 gp purchase, or the party can follow another lead.

> [!exploration]**Saeth Cromley**
>
> Saeth Cromley, a retired Watch sergeant who still works cases with Magistrate Blastwind, spends his afternoons in the sergeants' room at the Hall of Justice. Any character who buys him a drink for 2 gp and describes the shooter gets the answers below, and no check is needed.

> [!social]**The Retired Sergeant**
>
> Saeth Cromley (Lawful Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a gravelly, easygoing old sergeant who gets witnesses talking by being unimpressive.
>
> Saeth is Friendly and in no hurry. He scratches his chin before the question that matters, and he says "One more thing" on his way out. He swears cheerfully, and he misses the Watch more than he admits. He knows where retired Watch men rent when they want a high room and no questions.
>
> Saeth is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Where a retired sharpshooter would go to ground.
> - How the Watch trains its archers.
> - The Cassalanters, if a member brings them up.
>
> He will not discuss Jalester or the Alliance.

> [!qna]**A grey-haired man with a long canvas roll?**
>
> Saeth scratches his chin and looks at the ceiling for a moment before he answers.
>
> > "Well, shit, that's a Watch walk. Twenty years of marching teaches a man to carry his shoulders like that, and the canvas is a bow case. He'll want a high room with a north view and a roof door. Try the feed stores on Pell Street, because Yander's lets rooms over the loft and never asks a name."

> [!qna]**Where would he run?**
>
> > "One more thing, friend. A sharpshooter goes over the roofs before he goes down any stair. From the ridge on Pell Street he runs east along the tannery roofs to the lane, then drops to the Dock Ward. I would walk that line before I bought a lantern."

> [!exploration]**The Angles**
>
> Jalester's map shows the three shot lines. A character who lays a ruler along them needs no check and about two hours. The lines cross over the rooftops one block north of Trollskull Alley, and every bolt came from a north-facing window on a top floor. Only two buildings on Pell Street fit, a chandler's with its shutters nailed and Yander's Feed Store.
>
> - **Across the street:** The feed store's top shutter stands open at a fixed angle that lets a man see the street without being seen. A window left that way on purpose confirms the building.

A member for whom **Stingblade Reached** is marked can instead read the Watch's Archer case file in one hour. The file gives the same three killings by heavy crossbow, the shot angles and the north window, and it serves as a free fourth lead.

### Yander's Feed Store

> [!readaloud]
>
> Pell Street is narrow and smells of oats and horse feed, and wagons wait nose to tail outside the grain doors. Yander's Feed Store has sacks stacked to the lintel, and an outside stair climbs the wall beside the loft hatch. A thin man in a flour-dusted apron watches you from behind the counter and wipes his hands twice before he speaks.
>
> > "If you're buying, I'm selling. If you're asking, I'd like to know who you are first."

> [!social]**The Man Behind the Counter**
>
> Yander Coirn (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: the nervy proprietor of Yander's Feed Store, who rents his top room to a man called Belvor.
>
> Yander is Indifferent and sweats when pressed. He rented the room a week ago for a tenday in silver, and he has not asked what is in the canvas roll. He is not afraid of Belvor, but he is afraid of the Watch closing his shop. He answers quickly once someone tells him no one will be hurt in his building.
>
> Yander is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Belvor, the room and the rent.
> - The hours Belvor keeps, and what he carries.
> - Whether the party can go up.
>
> He will not discuss his other lodgers or his accounts.

> [!qna]**Who is Belvor?**
>
> > "That's the name he gave, a grey-haired man with good manners. He paid a tenday in advance and takes the top room with the north window. He comes and goes at odd hours with that long roll of canvas, and I never asked him what's in it."

> [!qna]**Can we go up?**
>
> Yander looks at the stair, then at the door, and lowers his voice before he answers.
>
> > "If you're the Watch, I'll let you up and pretend I never saw you. If you're not, I'd sooner you waited until he's out, because he keeps a rope on that window and I don't want anyone hurt in my building."

> [!exploration]**Three Ways Up**
>
> The party can combine these, and each leaves the others open.
>
> - **The stairs:** A character who leads up the inner stair and makes a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check reaches the door unheard. On a failure a board cracks, and Vhaspar goes out of the window with the party on the roof behind him.
> - **The supper tray:** A character who makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check persuades Yander to carry up Belvor's evening tray. The party waits out of sight on the landing, and Vhaspar opens the door with his hands full, so catching him needs no check. On a failure Yander refuses, and the party uses another way.
> - **The door:** A character who kicks it in or opens it without warning finds Vhaspar already at the window, and the chase begins at once.

### The Room

Read the following aloud when the party reaches Vhaspar at his table, by the stairs or the tray:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The top room is narrow and bare, with a cot, a plank table under the north window and a coil of rope tied to the latch. A grey-haired man sits at the table with a pencil and a sketch of a street, and a heavy crossbow lies across the table beside his hand. He looks at each of you in turn and then lifts both hands slowly, palms open.
>
> > "All right. You got here first. I would like to keep my hands where you can see them, if that suits you."

> [!social]**The Man at the Table**
>
> Vhaspar Coldwater (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: a retired Watch sharpshooter of fifty-seven, grey-haired and tired, who sells his skill and does not pretend to be sorry.
>
> Vhaspar is Indifferent and practical, and he talks in short plain sentences. He is not confessing but offering a trade, and he wants a guarantee that the Watch will keep him alive. He swears once at most, and only about the client.
>
> Vhaspar is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who hired him, and how.
> - The three he killed and the fourth he did not.
> - What protection he wants.
> - Why he surrendered.
>
> He will not name Halek Farran until someone promises him Watch protection.

> [!qna]**What do you want?**
>
> > "A guarantee the Watch keeps me alive until I stand before a magistrate. The man who hired me will have me killed the moment he hears I've been taken. You have a name that can promise it, or you don't."

Jalester's card is enough: a character who shows it and says the Watch will hold him on Jalester's word persuades Vhaspar without a check. A character who has no card, or who will not promise, can make a **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation)** check instead. On a failure Vhaspar says nothing about Halek until a character fetches Jalester's name.

### The Chase

Read the following aloud when Vhaspar goes out of the window:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The window bangs open and the rope slides over the sill as the grey-haired man swings out and drops to the slates. He is on the ridge before you reach the frame, and he runs east along it with his bow case banging against his back. Below you the street is crowded with carts and shoppers who have not yet looked up.

> [!hazard]**The Chase Over the Roofs**
>
> The chase is a clock, because Vhaspar knows these roofs. The **Escape Track** runs from 0 to 6 and begins at 2.
>
> - **Each round:** The track rises by 1 as Vhaspar Dashes over roofs he has used for weeks. Then every pursuing character makes one chase check, **DC 13 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics)**.
> - **Falling:** For every two successes the track falls by 1, to a maximum fall of 2 a round.
> - **Ranged attack:** A character can make one ranged attack a round instead of a check. A hit against AC 17 lowers the track by 1.
> - **The route:** A pursuer who has Saeth's roofline or the Watch's Archer case file makes the check with Advantage.
> - **Return fire:** When the track is 2 or lower, Vhaspar turns once a round and fires his Heavy Crossbow at a pursuer (+3, 12 piercing). He does not otherwise attack.
>
> The track decides how the chase ends:
>
> - **Track 0:** He is cornered on a roof edge or in a dead-end loft. He surrenders if a character offers Watch protection, and otherwise he fights as described in **When the Party Attacks Him**.
> - **Track 6:** He drops to the lane and reaches his bolt hole, a salt-fish smokehouse on the Dock Ward quay that belongs to no faction. The party loses him for a night, as described in **The Smokehouse**.
> - **Failure:** A failed chase costs time and never the mission, because two other leads still reach the smokehouse.

### The Smokehouse

If the track reaches 6, Vhaspar spends the night in a smokehouse on the quay, behind a stack of salt barrels. The Skewered Dragon is not his hiding place, because the Splinter would sell him to whoever paid. A lead the party has not used finds the smokehouse at dawn.

- **The Linen Merchant's canvass:** The shopkeepers on the quay say a grey-haired man bought two loaves and a jug before closing. A character who asks along the quay and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check is sent to the smokehouse.
- **Saeth Cromley:** He names the smokehouse from the Watch's old list of places where a man can hide a bow case, and a character who asks him needs no check.

Halek's cleaners reach Vhaspar within 48 hours of the chase. A party that arrives after that finds him dead and the room empty, so **Halek Farran Named** is never marked and Halek leaves Waterdeep.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The smokehouse stands at the end of the quay with its louvres open, and grey smoke curls over the roof into a pale morning. Stacked salt barrels line the wall inside, and rows of split fish hang in the haze above them. Behind the barrels the grey-haired man sits against a post with his crossbow across his knees and one boot braced against the floor.
>
> > "I thought it would take you longer. Put your hands where I can see them, and I will put mine where you can see mine."

### When the Party Attacks Him

This fight happens if the party attacks Vhaspar in the room, or if the chase ends at Track 0 and he refuses to surrender. Halek left hired hands to watch the door, and they climb to the roof or the loft in round 2.

> [!hazard]**Vhaspar Coldwater**
>
> Vhaspar uses the **Warrior Veteran** stat block with his Heavy Crossbow, and the hired hands are **Tough** fighters. The **Lords' Alliance Mechanics Reference** audits the roster for three, four and five characters:
>
> - **Three characters:** Vhaspar and 2 **Tough** fighters.
> - **Four characters:** Vhaspar and 3 **Tough** fighters.
> - **Five characters:** Vhaspar and 4 **Tough** fighters.
>
> #### Vhaspar's Tactics
> To begin, Vhaspar fires from the window or the roof ridge, and the hired hands reach him in round 2. He aims for characters who carry a light, a symbol or a bow.
>
> During combat, Vhaspar:
> - Fires Heavy Crossbow volleys at the nearest armed character, and uses Parry only against melee hits.
> - Surrenders at 32 hit points or fewer if Watch protection is on the table. He calls out "Hold it, I'm done" and drops the crossbow.
> - Dashes out through a skylight or the window if he has a clear way out and no one has offered protection. The Escape Track then restarts at 3.
> - Leaves the hired hands to cover him, and they break when he surrenders or drops.
>
> The fight ends when Vhaspar surrenders, falls or reaches a roof that no pursuer can follow. He does not pursue the party into the street.
>
> The non-combat route is an offer made on the party's first turn. A character who shows Jalester's card and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check persuades Vhaspar to lower the crossbow, and the hired hands scatter. On a failure the fight goes ahead, and Vhaspar surrenders at the usual threshold.

If Vhaspar dies, **Halek Farran Named** is not marked and Halek leaves Waterdeep within 48 hours. No dossier is compiled, and Hollen Brask is warned or not as described in **The Fourth Name**.

### Vhaspar's Bargain

With Watch protection promised, Vhaspar talks freely. He answers each question once, and he does not ask what happens to him afterward.

> [!qna]**Who hired you?**
>
> > "A man named Halek Farran. I met him once, in a coffeehouse on Chandler Street in the Sea Ward. He was in his fifties and neat, and he smelled of cedar. He wore a signet ring and kept turning the crest in against his palm."

> [!qna]**How did he reach you?**
>
> > "Through a hiring agency in the Dock Ward, then a written note, then a drop box behind a chandler's. He never gave his reasons. He said the three had seen something that was not theirs to see, and I asked for nothing else."

> [!qna]**What did he pay?**
>
> > "Four hundred gold for the three. I spent eighty on the room and the rope, and the rest is in my coat. The fourth name was never paid for, because I hadn't done it yet."

> [!qna]**Who is the fourth?**
>
> > "A longshoreman named Hollen Brask. I haven't touched him. His sketch is in my coat with the name on the back. Warn him, if you like, because I don't want that one."

> [!qna]**Will you say this to a magistrate?**
>
> > "If your man keeps me alive until then. I know what a magistrate does with a man like me. I would sooner hear it in a courtroom than from Halek."

### His Belongings

Vhaspar's coat and bow case hold a sketch of a Dock Ward longshoreman with his name on the back and 320 gp in a leather purse. They also hold a scrap of paper that reads "...ilanter Villa, Sea Ward, side entrance gate". Vhaspar did not read the scrap closely, because it came with a second contract that Halek offered and Vhaspar never took.

> [!exploration]**The Scrap**
>
> The party needs to connect the scrap to the Cassalanter family's villa in the Sea Ward. Three independent ways do it, and any one is enough.
>
> - **The check:** A character who studies the hand and the wording and makes a successful **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation)** check places "...ilanter" as the Cassalanter name. The phrase "side entrance gate" is operational wording that someone wrote for use.
> - **Vhaspar:** A character who asks about the second contract and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check hears that Halek called it "the family's side gate". Vhaspar adds that he took the ink for the Cassalanter name.
> - **Saeth Cromley:** A character who shows him the scrap hears that the Watch posts a guard at the Cassalanter villa's service gate. Every sergeant in the Sea Ward knows it, and Saeth adds that the household is generous and well liked.
> - **On a failure:** The scrap stays unplaced, and the other ways stay open. A member for whom **Stingblade Reached** is marked can also read the Watch's household inquiries file, which holds three closed inquiries against one Sea Ward house and no suspicion.

> [!exploration]**The Fourth Name**
>
> The sketch shows a broad man in a tally clerk's hat on the Dock Ward quays, with "Hollen Brask, Dock Ward hauler" on the back. Any character who asks at the haulers' shed on the quay finds him by mid-afternoon without a check. Brask believes the warning once he sees his own sketch.
>
> - **Warned:** He spends a tenday with his sister in the Trades Ward and works under another name. A character who goes to the Watch house with him and tells the desk sergeant also gets him a patrol on his street.
> - **Not warned by the fifth night:** A hired crossbow kills him, and the third **+1 Renown** bonus is lost.

### The Handover

The party takes Vhaspar to the North Ward Watch house and shows the desk sergeant Jalester's card. Jalester arrives within the hour whatever the time, and he wants Vhaspar by the evening of the fourth day. A party that arrives later has not abandoned the job if a chase or the smokehouse cost the time, and Jalester says so.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The North Ward Watch house is a squat stone building with a lamp over the door, and the desk sergeant waits behind a counter with a ledger open. Jalester Silvermane stands at the cell door with his notebook in his hand, and he looks at Vhaspar for a moment without speaking. Then he turns to you and puts the pencil to the page.
>
> > "Thank you. The Watch will hold him on my word and keep him alive until he testifies. He will stand trial, and I will not tell him otherwise. Was there anything among his belongings that did not belong to a man of his trade?"

If a member names the scrap as the Cassalanter villa's side gate and gives Jalester the paper, mark **Villa Address Identified** and read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester takes the scrap and reads the line twice. He writes the address in the notebook, and for a moment he says nothing.
>
> > "A side gate on a Sea Ward house, written for someone who meant to use it. I will ask the Watch's Criminal Intelligence office to look at it discreetly, and I would like you to say nothing about this to anyone else. Come to my table in ten days, and I will have what they found."

If the party has not placed the scrap by the time Jalester asks, he takes the inventory from the sergeant and reads it aloud without comment on the address. He repeats "Come to my table in ten days," and a member can still name the scrap at the debrief.

### Renown Opportunities

Ten days after the handover Jalester expects the members at the corner table beside the Yawning Portal's well at dusk, and the companions sit at the bar.

> [!gamemaster]**Beside the Well**
>
> The corner table stands beside the Yawning Portal's well, so anything said at it may reach something below the Portal, and Jalester does not know.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Yawning Portal is busy tonight, and the cold air from the well stirs the smoke under the beams. Jalester sits at the corner table with a thin folder beside his notebook, and he rises as you come over. He waits until everyone has a chair before he opens the folder.
>
> > "Thank you for coming. The Watch moved Vhaspar to a cell with a guard of its own, and he has told the magistrate what he told you. I would like to hear it from you once more, in order, and then I have something to show you."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester slides a single page from the folder across the table. It is a sketch of a high wall with a side gate in it, and a column of times is written down the margin in a neat hand.
>
> > "This is the dossier. The Watch's office compiled Halek Farran's name, the guard rotation at the villa's service gate and a drawing of the outer wall. None of it proves anything, and I have shown it to no one. The Cassalanters have a spotless name, which is exactly why I would like to know what a hired agent of theirs was doing in the North Ward. Is there anything else you remember about the man in the coffeehouse?"

If a member named the scrap here instead of at the handover, mark **Villa Address Identified** and read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester takes the scrap from the evidence envelope and turns it over. He puts it down and writes the address in the notebook.
>
> > "I should have caught this myself, and I did not. I will ask the Watch's Criminal Intelligence office to look at it discreetly. Come back to my table in ten days, and I will have what they found."

If **Halek Farran Named** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester lays his pencil across the page and looks at the member who spoke last.
>
> > "Halek Farran, then. A hired agent for the household, and not one of the family's staff. I have a name and a coffeehouse, and I am grateful for both. I would like to hear exactly how he held the ring, if you remember."

If Vhaspar died before the handover, read or paraphrase the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester closes the empty folder and sets it down square to the edge of the table.
>
> > "Then the man who paid him is gone, and I have a dead shooter and a closed case. I understand how it came about, and I thank you for telling me plainly. Was the room searched before the Watch arrived?"

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Lords' Alliance member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Jalester at the debrief. Companions gain none, and each bonus can be earned only once.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** Vhaspar is alive in Watch custody and has named Halek Farran, and **Halek Farran Named** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** the address is identified as the Cassalanter villa and reported to Jalester, and **Villa Address Identified** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** the fourth target, Hollen Brask, is warned and alive at the debrief.

### Aftermath

What happens next depends on whether Vhaspar lived and what the party told Jalester.

- **Vhaspar alive:** He testifies before Magistrate Blastwind and goes to the cells for life, or to the rope. Halek Farran slips out of the Sea Ward within a day of the arrest, and the Watch never finds him.
- **Vhaspar dead:** Halek leaves Waterdeep within 48 hours, and no one in the Alliance learns his name.
- **The villa dossier:** If **Cassalanter Villa** has not run, the dossier gives the party a guard rotation at the service gate and rough dimensions of the outer wall. If it has run, the dossier adds only the Renown bonus.
- **Hollen Brask:** If warned, he returns to the quay after a tenday and does not speak of it. If not, he is dead, and the Watch records another killing from above.
- **The Cassalanters:** Nobody in the household learns that the party has the address. The family keeps its name and its charity, and the party has only a name and a gate.

### Concluding the Event

Jalester's debrief ends the mission, and the two outcomes below are read by later events.

## Overview

Jalester asks the party to find a retired Watch sharpshooter who has shot three people from high windows, and to take him alive.

## Summary

Jalester Silvermane told us a heavy-crossbow killer had shot three people in twelve days. We followed three leads to a feed store on Pell Street, and Vhaspar Coldwater named the man who hired him. We also found a scrap of paper pointing to a Sea Ward villa, and who is Halek Farran working for?
