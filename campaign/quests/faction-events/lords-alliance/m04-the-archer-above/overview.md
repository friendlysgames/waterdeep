# Lords' Alliance Mission 4 — The Archer Above

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Requires Lords' Alliance Renown 6 and 5th level. Available after Mission 3 is complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 5th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Involved Characters
- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance) — field agent; delivers the mission with the short version; needs Vhaspar alive
- **Vhaspar Holmbridge** (independent contractor) — retired City Watch sharpshooter, 57; hired through a Cassalanter intermediary; has one name still on his list he has not acted on; will cooperate if Watch protection is guaranteed
- **Halek Farran** (Cassalanter household) — steward handling sensitive errands for the Cassalanters; hired Vhaspar through three layers of intermediary; named by Vhaspar under custody
- **Yander Coirn** (independent) — proprietor of Yander's Feed Store on Pell Street; rented the room to "Belvor" a tenday ago and will confirm it on request

## Dangers & Enemies
**Vhaspar Holmbridge** (use Veteran stat block) is not a combat encounter if approached correctly. A quiet approach up the stairs catches him at his table without a weapon drawn. A loud approach or forced door sends him out the window for a rooftop chase (three-check Athletics sequence). He does not fight to the death. He surrenders when caught and cooperates once Watch protection is credibly offered.

## Overview

Three dead in the North Ward and Sea Ward transition — all killed with arrows from elevated positions, none of the bodies connected to any active faction operation. The Watch was overwhelmed. Jalester's contact reported the shooter had gone to ground near Trollskull Alley. Jalester needed him found and arrested alive; killing him made this worse.

The shooter was **Vhaspar Holmbridge**, a retired City Watch sharpshooter who had served in the Dock Ward for twenty years before taking private contracts. He had been hired through three layers of intermediary — a hire agency, a note, a drop box — originating from **Halek Farran**, a Cassalanter household steward tasked with closing loose ends around a money transfer six tendays ago. The three victims had witnessed that transfer by accident: a Cassalanter-hired fence paying a Xanathar Guild broker for ritual components that connected to the Cassalanters' infernal timeline. Halek had been tasked with ensuring no witnesses remained.

Vhaspar had one name still on his list that he had not acted on. Locating him required a three-stage investigation through the North Ward that ended at Yander's Feed Store on Pell Street, one block from Trollskull Alley. Getting to him before he used his exit protocol determined whether the confrontation was a conversation or a rooftop chase.

In Vhaspar's belongings: a sketch of his remaining target, a partial address reading "...ilanter Villa, Sea Ward, side entrance gate," and 320 gp remaining from his payment. Identifying that partial address as Cassalanter Villa and reporting it to Jalester was the mission's most important secondary beat — it became the foundation of the Alliance's **Cassalanter Villa** intelligence package.
