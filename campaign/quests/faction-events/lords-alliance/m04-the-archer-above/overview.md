# The Archer Above: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level, after **The Red Wizard's Price**. Companions can help with everything except Jalester's brief and debrief, which only members attend.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 5th-level characters.*
>
> The mission is a chase and an investigation. Vhaspar Coldwater, a **Warrior Veteran**, surrenders if offered Watch protection. If the party attacks him, he fights with **Tough** hired hands. The **Lords' Alliance Mechanics Reference** audits the rosters for three, four and five characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Lords' Alliance members who report to Jalester.

## Hook

At dusk Jalester Silvermane waits at the corner table beside the Yawning Portal's well with a map of three killings. A hired shooter has killed from high windows every fifth night, and the next killing is due on the fifth night from now. Jalester wants the shooter found alive by the evening of the fourth day.

## Background

Vhaspar Coldwater is a retired City Watch sharpshooter of fifty-seven who served twenty years in the Dock Ward. He now takes private contracts, and he has killed three people with a heavy crossbow from high windows in twelve days. A hired agent named Halek Farran paid him 400 gp through a hiring agency, a written note and a drop box, and met him once in a coffeehouse on Chandler Street.

The three dead people saw a fence hired by the Cassalanter household buy goods from a broker. Halek is not villa staff, and Vhaspar does not know why the household wanted them silenced. Jalester has no suspect and no motive. He knows only that the dead had nothing in common except what they saw.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Vhaspar has a fourth name on his list, a Dock Ward longshoreman named Dorrin Teague, and he has not acted on it.
> - Halek is a hired agent working a household errand for the Cassalanters. Nobody in this mission knows why the household wanted silence, and the party can only suspect.
> - If Vhaspar dies, Halek leaves Waterdeep within 48 hours and the party learns no name.
> - A scrap of paper in Vhaspar's coat reads "...ilanter Villa, Sea Ward, side entrance gate". It came with a second contract that Vhaspar never took.

## The Brief

Jalester briefs the members at the well table in the evening. He gives the deadline, hands over a card that opens the Watch house cells and says that the shooter must be taken alive. Companions sit at the bar.

## Three Ways to Pell Street

The party has a district and no door. Three independent leads each name Yander's Feed Store on Pell Street: a linen merchant on Tarnath Street, the retired sergeant Saeth Cromley, and the angles of the three shots on Jalester's map. A member who has reached **Stingblade** has a fourth lead in the Watch's case file.

## Yander's Feed Store and the Room

Yander Coirn rents the top room to a man he knows as Belvor. The party can climb the stairs quietly, persuade Yander to carry up the supper tray, or burst in. A quiet approach catches Vhaspar at his table with his hands raised.

## The Chase

A loud approach sends Vhaspar over the rooftops. The chase is a clock called the Escape Track, and each round he gains ground while the pursuers' checks pull him back. At Track 0 he is cornered and surrenders. At Track 6 he reaches a salt-fish smokehouse on the quay, and the party has until Halek's cleaners arrive to find him.

## When the Party Attacks Him

If the party attacks, Vhaspar fights with Tough hired hands and surrenders at 32 hit points or fewer if Watch protection is offered. A character with Jalester's card can persuade him to lower the crossbow.

## Vhaspar's Bargain and His Belongings

Vhaspar names Halek Farran once Watch protection is promised. His coat holds a sketch of the fourth target, 320 gp and the scrap, and three independent ways place the scrap at the Cassalanter villa in the Sea Ward.

## The Fourth Name

Dorrin Teague is working the Dock Ward quays. The party can warn him, and a warned Teague hides for a tenday.

## The Handover and the Debrief

The party hands Vhaspar to Jalester at the North Ward Watch house. Ten days later Jalester returns to the well table with a dossier compiled by the Watch's Criminal Intelligence office, if the party identified the address.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Lords' Alliance member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Jalester at the debrief. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** Vhaspar is alive in Watch custody and has named Halek Farran.
- **+1 Renown:** the address is identified as the Cassalanter villa and reported to Jalester.
- **+1 Renown:** the fourth target is warned and alive at the debrief.

Companions who are not Lords' Alliance members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

Vhaspar testifies before Magistrate Blastwind, and Halek Farran vanishes from the Sea Ward. **Halek Farran Named** is read by **Fentree's Ledger** and by **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted). **Villa Address Identified** is read by **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted), if it has not run. **Fentree's Ledger** becomes available when an individual Lords' Alliance member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance): briefs the members, takes Vhaspar at the Watch house and brings the dossier to the well table.
- **Vhaspar Coldwater** (Independent): a retired Watch sharpshooter and a **Warrior Veteran** who will name his client for protection.
- **Halek Farran** (Cassalanter household): a hired agent who paid Vhaspar on a household errand and vanishes if Vhaspar dies.
- **Yander Coirn** (Independent): the feed store's proprietor, who rents his top room to Belvor and wants no trouble.
- **Saeth Cromley** (City of Waterdeep): a retired Watch sergeant who recognises the shooter's walk and knows the roofs.
- **Dorrin Teague** (Independent): a Dock Ward hauler on Vhaspar's list who must be warned.

## Dangers & Enemies

Vhaspar uses the **Warrior Veteran** stat block with a Heavy Crossbow, and he fights only if the party attacks. His hired hands are **Tough** fighters, two at three characters, three at four and four at five. The chase is a clock rather than a fight, and Halek's cleaners are the real danger if the party loses Vhaspar.

## Overview

A hired shooter has killed three people from high windows in the North Ward and the Sea Ward. The party tracks him through three leads, takes him alive and learns who paid him.
