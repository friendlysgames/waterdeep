# Design Notes: Suspension and Dismissal

## What the Source Gave

The restored draft had a good scene and no trigger. Jalester arrived at Trollskull Manor and suspended a member for refusing or failing a mission "without compelling reason". No Brief in the Lords' Alliance set contained a refusal branch or a deadline.

The Appendix B line that the Alliance will "formally suspend or dismiss" a member is the only rule behind it. *Dragon Heist* says the same of "refusing to accept or complete" an assignment. The keeper lines survive: "I had you down as reliable," "This is done. Your Renown stays on the books. It doesn't do anything," and "I was wrong about you."

## The Trigger

Each of the first five Briefs now states its deadline in Jalester's speech, and the trigger table reads those words. **Six Bells at the Muleskull** is the tenth evening, **The Exile's Return** is dawn after the third night, and **The Red Wizard's Price** is the handshake at half past seven.

The Event fires on refusal, or on a deadline that passes with no step taken, and it never fires on an honest failure. That follows the Factions Guide line that a clean failure costs nothing, and it removes the old word "fails". **The Exile's Return** also defines a job that goes loud, and the Event leaves that cost where the mission put it.

## The Visit and the Compelling Reasons

The visit moves from Trollskull Manor to the well table, because every other Lords' Alliance contact now happens there. A runner's note at noon sets the evening, as in **Redknife**.

The old essay on compelling reasons becomes a table with a verdict on each row. Only a position the table remembers from play counts. The illness, captive and companion-faction rows give a GM the answer without judgment.

## Ways Back and Dismissal

The restored draft allowed one route, verified intelligence. The brief adds a named errand and Laeral's word after **Escalation to the Open Lord**. The intelligence route borrows the three-route shape from Force Grey's **The Full Picture**.

Dismissal needed a second refusal "while suspended", but a suspended member gets no Briefs, so the errand carries that test instead. A second trigger after reinstatement is also dismissal, which keeps the loop finite.

Renown is never reduced by this Event, and a dismissed member counts as unaffiliated at Renown 0, which lets the other First Meetings treat them like a severed Bregan D'aerthe member.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- **Invented here:** the two-packet errand with its receipts and three-day window, and the second-trigger rule.
- **Also invented:** the second-dawn rule for hearing of an offense, the two-day grace for a member who could not write, and the line that Jalester has dismissed an agent before.
- **Borrowed:** Odette Brandle and Garrick Tull come from **Redknife**.
- **M4 and M5 deadlines:** **The Archer Above** and **Fentree's Ledger** were unconverted when this was written. Their rows say "the deadline Jalester states", so fill in the exact words when they are done.
- **Escalation to the Open Lord:** route three needs that Event to let a suspended member sit as a witness, withhold the +2 and name them in Laeral's note.
- **Renown wording:** `players-guide/faction-affiliations.md` line 25 and `gm-guide/player-factions-overview.md` line 25 say serious offenses cost 2 Renown, which this Event does not do.
- **Writ:** whether a suspended member is summoned to **An Audience with the Open Lord** is left to that Event.
