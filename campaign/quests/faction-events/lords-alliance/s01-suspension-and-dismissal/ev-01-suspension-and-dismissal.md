# Suspension and Dismissal

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Administrative Event fires when a Lords' Alliance member refuses or fails a mission explicitly assigned by Jalester Silvermane without compelling reason. In this Event:
>
> - Jalester arrives in person to deliver the suspension — he does not delegate this.
> - The suspended character loses mission access and active Alliance support until reinstated.
> - One specific act of service unlocks reinstatement.
> - A second refusal while suspended, or a serious offense against the faction, triggers dismissal instead.
> - Renown is never reduced by suspension or dismissal. On dismissal, the score becomes inert.
>
> This event fires only for the character who refused or failed the assignment. Other party members with Alliance membership are unaffected.

**Background (GM only)**

The Lords' Alliance put its conditions on the table at recruitment. Jalester said them plainly at First Meeting: assignments are not optional. Suspension is the formal consequence of a choice the character made with full information.

**What counts as a compelling reason:**

A character has a compelling reason when the refusal reflects a consistent moral or practical position they have maintained throughout the campaign. Examples include: the mission requires an action the character has refused on principle before (assassination, betrayal of a named ally, participation in an illegal act the character has visibly objected to); or the mission directly contradicts a sworn obligation the character has demonstrated at the table.

What does not qualify: the mission is dangerous. The character disagrees with Alliance politics. Other quests took priority. The character accepted the assignment and stopped showing up — abandonment after acceptance is treated identically to a direct refusal without compelling reason.

**What the suspension covers:**

All benefits requiring Jalester's active cooperation are suspended: Watch escorts, safe house access through Alliance channels, introductions to political or merchant contacts, Alliance legal cost coverage, Watch file access, intelligence briefings, and dedicated attaché access. Mission access is removed entirely — no new assignments are offered, and the character cannot complete an outstanding mission for Renown purposes until reinstated.

The character's recognized operative status is technically suspended, but the Watch network moves slowly. Officers who already know the character will treat them as they have been treating them for a tenday or two before the formal withdrawal propagates. Jalester will not vouch for the character during this period.

The Renown score is unaffected. It stays where it is. It cannot drop below 0.

### The Suspension

Jalester comes to Trollskull Manor himself. He does not send a note requesting a meeting — this conversation is delivered in person, quietly, where the wrong people will not overhear it. He arrives at a time he calculates the rest of the party is unlikely to be present; he has been watching the manor's patterns long enough to know their schedule.

He knocks twice. He enters when let in and stays near the door. He does not take a seat.

> > "The mission you were assigned is past its window."

He does not ask for an explanation. He does not raise his voice.

> > "You're suspended from active operations. No assignments, no Alliance support, no introductions, no Watch escorts. Your Renown is where it is — I'm not touching that."

A pause. He is already turning something over in his mind: the next report he needs to write, the next piece he has to track down.

> > "There's a path back. Bring me verified intelligence — a named operative, a confirmed location, a specific plan the Watch can act on within a tenday. Bring me something I can forward. I'll reinstate you when you do."

He looks at the character directly.

> > "I had you down as reliable."

He leaves. He is writing in his small notebook before he reaches the door, the hand moving without him slowing down.

Any character who presses him for more gets one additional sentence with a **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check:

> [!dialogue]**Why are you doing this?**
> "I don't enjoy this. That's not the point."

He does not stop walking.

### Suspended Status

The suspension takes effect the moment the scene ends. The following are unavailable until reinstatement:

- Mission assignments. No new missions are offered, and outstanding missions cannot be completed for Renown.
- Watch escorts arranged through Alliance channels.
- Alliance safe house access.
- Introductions to political or merchant contacts.
- Alliance coverage of legal costs.
- Watch crime report and investigation file access (Renown 10 benefit).
- Alliance intelligence on faction response team locations (Renown 25 benefit).
- The dedicated attaché (Renown 50 benefit).

The following continue unchanged:

- The character's current Renown score. It cannot be reduced by suspension.
- Existing personal relationships with Watch officers the character has cultivated independently of Alliance channels. Those take time to cool.

> [!info]**Renown During Suspension**
> Renown cannot drop below 0 under any circumstances. A suspended character keeps their current score and retains their rank title on paper, but cannot earn new Renown and cannot access any rank benefits until Jalester reinstates them. On dismissal, the score is permanently inert: the number remains as a record, but unlocks nothing.

### The Reinstatement Path

The character must deliver verified intelligence on a villain faction operation: a specific address, a confirmed operative identity, or a planned action the Watch can act on within a tenday. Street talk and rumors do not qualify. The intelligence must be traceable to firsthand observation or a documented source the character can name.

When the character brings this to Jalester — at the Yawning Portal, at Trollskull Manor, or through a note that gets him to arrange a meeting — he reads it without comment. He adds it to his notes. He says:

> > "You're reinstated. Don't do this again."

That is the full reinstatement. Mission access and Alliance support are restored immediately.

### Dismissal

Two conditions trigger dismissal rather than, or in addition to, suspension:

**A second refusal while suspended:** The character, currently suspended, refuses or fails another assigned mission from Jalester without compelling reason.

**A serious offense:** The character betrays a mission, harms Alliance members, or actively works against the Alliance's interests. This triggers dismissal directly, bypassing suspension.

When dismissal is warranted, Jalester comes to Trollskull Manor again. The scene is shorter.

He knocks once, enters without waiting for an answer, and sets a folded note on the nearest surface. It is the character's formal termination from the Lords' Alliance, dated and signed by him. He does not explain its contents.

> > "This is done. Your Renown stays on the books. It doesn't do anything."

He looks at the character once.

> > "I was wrong about you."

He leaves. He does not look back.

The character loses all rank benefits permanently. Their Renown score remains as a record but unlocks nothing going forward. Laeral Silverhand will know this character as someone Jalester dismissed — the political weight of that knowledge surfaces in **Vault of Dragons**, where Alliance resources and Laeral's willingness to treat the party as allies are shaped in part by every member's standing.

> [!warning]**Dismissal Is Permanent**
> Dismissal cannot be reversed. A dismissed character may continue to pursue the Grand Game and interact with Alliance NPCs, but formal standing, rank benefits, and mission access are gone for the campaign. Jalester will engage the character as a Waterdhavian citizen, not as an operative.

### Concluding the Event

#### LA Suspended: True / False

Set **True** when the suspension scene concludes. Records that the character is suspended from active Lords' Alliance operations. Read in the **Reinstatement Path** above to determine eligibility, and in every subsequent **Lords' Alliance Faction Mission** brief — suspended characters are skipped until reinstated.

#### LA Reinstated: True / False

Set **True** when the character delivers qualifying intelligence and Jalester confirms reinstatement. Supersedes **LA Suspended**. Read in every subsequent **Lords' Alliance Faction Mission** brief: a reinstated character is eligible for assignments again.

#### LA Dismissed: True / False

Set **True** when the dismissal scene concludes. Permanent. Read in **Vault of Dragons** to determine Laeral Silverhand's attitude toward the character and whether Alliance resources are available during the vault operation.
