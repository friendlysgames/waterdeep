# Suspension and Dismissal

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Consequences Event occurs for an individual Lords' Alliance member who refuses a Brief or lets its stated deadline pass without trying. Jalester Silvermane delivers the news at the well table in the Yawning Portal, and in this Event the member can:
>
> - Hear the suspension, or give a reason from the table below that Jalester accepts, and keep their standing.
> - Lose the Alliance's briefs and rank benefits until Jalester marks the member reinstated.
> - Win reinstatement with verified intelligence, with a short errand Jalester names, or with Laeral's word after **Escalation to the Open Lord**.
> - Be dismissed for refusing the errand or for a serious offense, and keep a Renown score that no longer does anything.
>
> #### Who Is Subject
>
> The Event runs for one member at a time, like membership itself. Companions, characters in other factions and characters who never joined are not subject to it. A character who belongs to another faction can turn down a mission without consequence. A party can hold a member in good standing, a suspended member and a dismissed member at once, and each follows their own track.
>
> #### What Is Actually True
>
> - Jalester hates this part of the work. He has dismissed one agent before, and he reads the Brief's deadline back to himself before he writes anything down.
> - The Alliance suspends only people who chose it. A member who tried and failed, or who could not act, has done what the Brief asked, and Jalester says so.
> - The suspension is real and the way back is real. Jalester wants every suspended member to return, and he gives the three routes every time.
> - Laeral Silverhand knows none of this by name. Jalester writes the suspension in his report, and she reads what he writes.

### When This Event Fires

> [!gamemaster]**The Trigger**
>
> Check the Brief's deadline when a member's mission window ends, and fire the Event for any member who matches one of the two columns below. Each Brief from **Six Bells at the Muleskull** to **Fentree's Ledger** states its deadline in Jalester's speech, and this Event reads those words exactly.

| Brief | The member refuses | The deadline passes with no step taken |
|---|---|---|
| **Six Bells at the Muleskull** | At the Brief, at the well table. | No report at the well table by dusk on the tenth evening, and no note at the drop. |
| **The Exile's Return** | At the Brief, in the glover's room on Rannick Lane. | No step toward finding Harko by dawn after the third night. The night of the Brief is not counted. |
| **The Red Wizard's Price** | At the Brief, in the Trollskull taproom. | No attempt on the meeting before the handshake at half past seven tonight. |
| **The Archer Above** and **Fentree's Ledger** | At the Brief. | The deadline Jalester states in that Brief's speech. |
| **An Audience with the Open Lord** | Never. | Never. The summons is not an assignment and has no deadline. |

> [!gamemaster]**What Counts as a Step**
>
> A member has taken a step if they were present at any scene of the mission or sent word of an inquiry. A note at the drop that explains a delay also counts as a step. A member who sat at the bar while the others worked took no step. A member who worked beside the party took one even if every check failed.
>
> - **Six Bells at the Muleskull:** a missed morning with a note at the drop costs nothing. Jalester told the member so at the Brief.
> - **The Exile's Return:** a job that goes loud costs what that Event says and nothing here. A member who spares Harko has not refused, because Jalester asked to hear that decision.
> - **The Red Wizard's Price:** a member who tries and fails has done what the Brief asked, and Jalester says so in the debrief.
> - **Honest failure in any Brief:** an escaped target, a failed roll or a beaten party does not fire this Event.

### Reasons That Cost Nothing

> [!gamemaster]**Compelling Reasons**
>
> A reason excuses a member only if it fits a row below and reaches Jalester in time. The member leaves a note at the Portal's drop before the deadline. A member who could not write, because they were held, unconscious or detained, has two days after the deadline to explain.
>
> A reason that fits a row costs nothing, so Jalester marks nothing, says "Understood." and lets the mission lapse with no Renown for that member. A reason that fits no row, or that arrives late, leads to the visit below.

| The member's reason | Verdict |
|---|---|
| An illness or injury kept them from acting, and a healer or the Watch can say so. | Excused. |
| A named character the table has met was held captive, and the member left the mission to free that person. | Excused. |
| A companion's faction had an emergency that could not wait, the member joined it, and the table played it out inside the window. | Excused. |
| The Watch detained the member for part of the window. | Excused, because Jalester reads the Watch's books. |
| The mission asked for an act the member has refused on screen before, such as an assassination, a betrayal of a named ally or an illegal act. The member said so at the Brief. | Excused. Jalester gives the work to someone else. |
| The member raises that objection for the first time after the Brief. | Not excused. |
| The job was dangerous, the member disagrees with Alliance politics, another quest took priority, or the member simply stopped coming. | Not excused. |

Only a position the table remembers from play counts. A member who invents a principle after the refusal is judged on the last two rows.

### The Visit

At noon on the day after the deadline passes, or the day after a refusal, a Watch runner finds the member and hands over a folded note. The runner is an ordinary **Commoner** in a plain tabard and waits for no reply. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the runner at their first surface lodging, and the visit moves to the following evening.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The runner is gone down the stairs before you finish unfolding the paper, and the note holds two lines in a small, upright hand.
>
> > Corner table, this evening, after sundown. Please come alone. J.S.

> [!gamemaster]**Beside the Well**
>
> The corner table stands beside the Yawning Portal's well, so anything said at it may reach something below the Portal, and Jalester does not know. This applies to every scene in this Event that takes place at the table.

If **Laeral Told of Jalester** is marked, Sevel Dastar sends the note, signs it "S.D." and receives the member at a table under the stairs, away from the well. Use his lines in **If Sevel Is the Handler** instead of Jalester's, and leave out the question at the end.

If the member refused the Brief, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Portal is loud tonight, with hearth smoke under the beams and cold air rising from the well past the windlass. Durnan sends your companions to the bar and points you to the corner table. Jalester sits beside the well with his notebook closed, and he does not offer his hand.
>
> > "You told me at this table that you would not do the work, and I wrote that down. Is there a reason you want me to weigh?"

If the member let the deadline pass, read the following aloud instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Portal is loud tonight, with hearth smoke under the beams and cold air rising from the well past the windlass. Durnan sends your companions to the bar and points you to the corner table. Jalester sits beside the well with his notebook closed, and he does not offer his hand.
>
> > "The task you were given is past its day, and I have had no word from you. I would like to hear that I'm wrong, so tell me, am I?"

> [!social]**The Agent at the Well**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: a lean Lords' Alliance field agent in a dark doublet, who has taken no pleasure in sending the note and does not pretend otherwise.
>
> Jalester is Unfriendly tonight, and he stays courteous. He keeps the notebook closed until the member has spoken, and he writes down only the reason. He asks short questions, says "Understood." when he accepts an answer, and does not raise his voice. He swears at no one, and a curse from him tonight would only be aimed at himself.
>
> Jalester is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Whether the member's reason fits the table, which he answers at once.
> - What the suspension takes away and what it leaves.
> - The three ways back, which he names in full.
> - What he will write to Laeral, which is the facts and nothing more.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault or any intelligence the Alliance is acting on now. He will not argue the Condition, because he gave it at the First Meeting.

If the member gives a reason, check it against the table in **Reasons That Cost Nothing**. A reason that fits is excused on the spot. Jalester says "Understood. I'm glad to have been wrong," marks nothing, and ends the scene without a suspension.

> [!qna]**Can I explain?**
>
> Jalester opens the notebook at last and uncaps his pencil.
>
> > "Yes, once, and I will write it down as you say it. If your reason is one the Alliance accepts, this ends tonight and nothing is marked. If it isn't, I'll tell you plainly, and I won't make you argue for it."

> [!qna]**What does it cost?**
>
> > "Every brief, every rank benefit and every use of my name at the Watch houses. Your Renown stays where it is, and I won't touch it. The next section of the notebook says what you lose, and I'll read it to you if you like."

> [!qna]**Will you tell Laeral?**
>
> > "I tell her what I do and why, as I always do, and she reads it. I will write that you were reliable until now, because it's true, and I won't write more than the facts."

> [!qna]**Is there a way back?**
>
> > "There are three, and all of them are open to you tonight. I will go through them when we have settled what happens now. Do you want them in the order of how fast they are?"

If no reason fits, Jalester gives the verdict. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester sets the pencil across the notebook and looks at you for a moment before he speaks. The noise of the taproom rises around the table, and a mug is slapped down on the bar behind him.
>
> > "Then you are suspended from active operations, and the Alliance will send you no work, no help and no rank benefits until you are reinstated. Your Renown stays where it is, and I will not touch it."
>
> He turns a page in the notebook and names each of the three ways back, the way he would read a list of exits.
>
> > "I had you down as reliable, and I'm sorry to be wrong about it. One more detail, if you don't mind: are you still lodging at Trollskull Manor?"

Mark **LA Suspended** with the member's name. Jalester writes down the answer to his question and says "Understood." before he stands to go. A character who pressed him for more and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check gets one more sentence, and a failed check gets nothing further.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester stops with the notebook half closed in his hand and speaks without looking up from it.
>
> > "I don't enjoy this, but that is not the same as thinking it was wrong."

> [!gamemaster]**If Sevel Is the Handler**
>
> Sevel Dastar (Neutral, Human, he/him, **Spy**) is plain and dry, and he may swear lightly. He gives the verdict in the same order and signs everything "S.D."
>
> > "Silvermane's elsewhere, so this lands on me, and you're suspended from Alliance work until you're cleared. Your Renown stays as it is, and I'll tell you the three ways back."

### What Suspension Takes

> [!exploration]**Suspended Status**
>
> The suspension takes effect when the scene ends. The member keeps the rank title and the Renown score. Every other Alliance benefit is paused until **LA Reinstated** is marked.
>
> - **Briefs and debriefs:** no new Brief is offered. The member cannot earn Renown from any Lords' Alliance event, including the +2 in **Escalation to the Open Lord**.
> - **The Cloak:** the overlook and the patrol note. The Watch houses still know the member's face but no longer take Jalester's word for them.
> - **Redknife:** the Watch escort, the whistle, both safe houses, string-pulling, the sergeant's courtesy note and the manor beat's Guards.
> - **Stingblade:** introductions, legal coverage and Watch files, all three of which Jalester arranges.
> - **Warduke:** Laeral's private audience, the four Warrior Veterans, the response-team intelligence and the Masked Lord request.
> - **Lioncrown:** the removal of the outstanding charge, the three honest answers and the attaché.
> - **Rank events:** a rank event the member would have met while suspended waits, and its note arrives at noon on the day after **LA Reinstated** is marked.
>
> The member keeps the Renown score and the rank title, and the Civic Grant stays because it belongs to the party. The member also keeps the goodwill of any Watch officer cultivated without Alliance help. Companions can still stand beside a suspended member and gain nothing from the Alliance.

### The Ways Back

> [!gamemaster]**Three Routes**
>
> Jalester names all three routes at the visit, and the member can try them in any order. Reinstatement awards no Renown, and a route that fails costs nothing. The member can try the same route only once for the same facts.

#### Route One: Verified Intelligence

The member brings Jalester a firm account of a villain faction's operation. He reads it, asks one specific question and has the Watch confirm it by the next evening. Gossip does not count, and neither does suspicion about the Cassalanter household that has no paper behind it.

| The account must give | Firsthand | Document or prisoner | Named informant |
|---|---|---|---|
| A place, a name or a plan | The member watched it and describes it. | The member hands over the paper or the captive. | The member names a person who will speak to the Watch. |
| A day in the coming tenday that the Watch can act on | The member saw the day fixed. | The document carries the day. | The informant gave the day. |

> [!gamemaster]**An Incomplete Account**
>
> An account that gives only one row earns one question and a return visit. Jalester asks for the missing row, thanks the member and writes nothing down. The member can come back any evening, and the same intelligence is never accepted twice, either here or in a debrief.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester reads the page twice and holds it to the lamp before he copies two lines into the notebook. The well breathes cold air across the table while he writes, and a drunk at the next table begins to sing.
>
> > "Understood, and the Watch will confirm this tomorrow, so if it holds you are reinstated. Which hand did he keep on the table, if you remember?"

The next evening Jalester says "It held" and marks the reinstatement.

#### Route Two: The Errand

Jalester names a short errand that tests nothing but reliability. It runs for three days from the night the member accepts.

> [!exploration]**The Two Packets**
>
> - **The task:** carry a sealed packet to Odette Brandle at 9 Quillmakers' Court in the Castle Ward. Carry her reply to Garrick Tull at 14 Ironwick Lane in the Trades Ward, and bring his receipt back by dusk on the third day.
> - **The seals:** Jalester presses each in grey wax. A broken seal ends the errand, and Jalester treats it as a refusal.
> - **The managers:** Odette and Garrick give the member a receipt in the same hand. Neither will speak of what the packet holds, because they never open one.
> - **Delay:** a robbery, a missing manager or an injury the member can show is an honest failure. Jalester sets one new day, once, and the errand runs again.
> - **Failure to start:** a member who refuses the errand is dismissed. So is a member who accepts it and takes no step by dusk on the third day.

When the member returns with the receipts, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester checks each receipt against the wax in his notebook, then closes the book on both of them. The tankard at his elbow is still untouched, and the crowd around the well has thinned.
>
> > "Understood, and thank you. You are reinstated, and I would rather not do this twice. Are you still lodging at Trollskull Manor?"

#### Route Three: Laeral's Word

A suspended member who sat at the briefing in **Escalation to the Open Lord** has the chance to be named in Laeral's one-day note. The member earns no Renown at the briefing, and Jalester does not speak to them. When the note names a suspended member, Jalester reads the line at the next evening's table and marks the reinstatement.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester unfolds a half sheet in a firm, unfamiliar hand and reads it through before he lays it flat beside the notebook.
>
> > "The Open Lord has read your name, and she does not want it struck, so I won't contradict her. You are reinstated, and I thank you for sitting through that briefing."

### Reinstatement

Mark **LA Reinstated** with the member's name on the evening Jalester says the words, and remove **LA Suspended** for that member. Every benefit returns the next morning, and the member's Renown is unchanged. A rank event that waited for the member sends its note at noon the following day.

A member who is reinstated and then triggers this Event again is dismissed at the visit, with no further suspension. Jalester says only "I gave you a way back once, and I have no second one to give," and the dismissal follows.

### Dismissal

> [!gamemaster]**What Dismisses a Member**
>
> A member is dismissed in one of three cases, and no earlier suspension is needed for the second or third.
>
> - **The errand fails by the member's choice:** a suspended member refuses the errand in **Route Two**. The same applies if the third dusk passes with no step and no word.
> - **A second trigger after reinstatement:** the member refuses a Brief or lets its deadline pass after **LA Reinstated** was marked.
> - **A serious offense:** the member betrays a mission, harms an Alliance person, or works against the Alliance for another faction.
>
> An Alliance person is Jalester, Sevel Dastar, a Watch runner or officer carrying Alliance orders, or Odette Brandle or Garrick Tull. Betraying a mission means warning its target or selling its Brief to a villain faction. Working against the Alliance means taking another faction's pay to act against Jalester's work. Selling the ledger in **Fentree's Ledger** is a choice that mission already prices, and it is not an offense on its own.

Jalester learns of an offense by the second dawn after it happens, from the Watch or from the target. If he hears of nothing, he does not act, and the offense stays unmarked until he does.

The note comes at noon, as before, and the visit is shorter. If **Laeral Told of Jalester** is marked, Sevel Dastar sends it and receives the member under the stairs. If the offense was done to Jalester or to a runner carrying his orders, Durnan hands the member a sealed note at the bar. Jalester does not appear in that case.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester is already seated when you reach the corner table, with the notebook shut and a single folded sheet lying on the boards in front of him. The noise of the Portal washes over the table, and he does not look up until you have stopped beside the chair.
>
> > "This is done. Your Renown stays on the books. It doesn't do anything."

He slides the sheet across, and it holds the member's name, a date and his signature. He does not wait for the member to read it.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester stands and tucks the notebook inside his doublet, and the patrons nearest the well make room as he turns.
>
> > "I was wrong about you."
>
> He walks out through the front door of the Portal, and Durnan watches him go before he goes back to polishing a mug.

Mark **LA Dismissed** with the member's name, and treat it as permanent, because nothing in the campaign reverses it.

> [!gamemaster]**What a Dismissed Member Loses and Keeps**
>
> The member loses every Lords' Alliance benefit, brief and rank event for good. The Renown score stays on the record and does nothing.
>
> - **Eligibility elsewhere:** the member counts as unaffiliated for the other factions' First Meetings, at Renown 0. The score does not carry over, and the Alliance makes no second offer.
> - **The party:** the Civic Grant stays with the manor. A dismissed member can stand beside members as a helping companion and gains no Renown.
> - **The Writ:** the Writ in **An Audience with the Open Lord** names every character present except a dismissed member.
> - **Jalester:** he greets the member as a citizen of Waterdeep, politely and with no business to discuss.

### Staging the Scene

> [!gamemaster]**Running the Visit**
>
> Run the visit as a private conversation, because it belongs to the member and the other players are companions at the bar. Ask them to listen without speaking, or to step away if the table allows. If every player is a member who earned the visit, run it once with all of them and record each name.
>
> - **Several members at once:** Jalester addresses the member who reached the deadline first, then asks the others for their reasons in turn.
> - **A mixed result:** a member whose reason fits the table keeps their standing, and the member beside them who has none is suspended.
> - **No private moment:** if the others cannot leave, play the visit in the taproom with the companions at the bar. Give the rest of the table a summary at the end.

### Renown Opportunities

This Event awards no Renown. Suspension and dismissal never reduce it, and reinstatement never adds to it. A dismissed member's score stays as a record and unlocks nothing.

### Aftermath

A suspended member can still play every scene that does not need Alliance help. Jalester stays civil and offers no work, and he nods to the member in the Portal.

A reinstated member returns to the Briefs at the next one their Renown allows. The Vault of Dragons (unconverted) reads **LA Dismissed** to decide whether Laeral treats the member as one of the Alliance's people or as a citizen. A suspended member who reaches that quest without reinstatement is treated as a citizen as well.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes for a member when the visit ends, and again when the member is reinstated or dismissed.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **LA Suspended** — mark with the member's name when the visit ends in a suspension. Read by every Lords' Alliance Brief and rank event, which skip the member, and by **Escalation to the Open Lord** and the Vault of Dragons (unconverted).
> - **LA Reinstated** — mark with the member's name when Jalester says the member is reinstated, and remove **LA Suspended**. Read by every Lords' Alliance Brief and rank event, which resume for the member.
> - **LA Dismissed** — mark with the member's name when the dismissal scene ends. Read by the **Lords' Alliance First Meeting**, which makes no second offer and treats the member as unaffiliated, and by every other faction's First Meeting. Also read by **An Audience with the Open Lord** and the Vault of Dragons (unconverted).
>
> This Event also reads **Laeral Told of Jalester**, which decides whether Sevel Dastar delivers it.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> A suspended member can play on until reinstated, and a dismissed member continues as an unaffiliated character. Every other Lords' Alliance member continues with whichever events they have earned. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

A Lords' Alliance member who refused a Brief or let its deadline pass is called to the well table by Jalester Silvermane. He suspends them, names three ways back and warns what comes next.

## Summary

Jalester Silvermane sent a runner to tell us we were wanted at the well table. We had to explain why we had not done the work he gave us. He suspended us from active operations and told us that our Renown would stay as it was. He also described three ways back, and we set about choosing one.
