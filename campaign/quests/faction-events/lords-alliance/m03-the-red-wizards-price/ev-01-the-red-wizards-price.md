# The Red Wizard's Price

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Combat Event begins when Jalester Silvermane arrives at Trollskull Manor at noon. It ends when the members report to him at the Yawning Portal on the evening after the meeting, and in it the party can:
>
> - Take Jalester's brief in the taproom and learn that the Splinter is buying a Thayan wizard's crew tonight at the Skewered Dragon.
> - Find the room and the hour through three independent leads, in a tavern that belongs to the Splinter.
> - End the deal before the handshake by breaking up the meeting, handing Esloon to the Watch, turning him, or taking Sarvos Miln.
> - Fight Esloon Bezant, a full Mage who leaves once he is hurt, and hear the question he asks on his way out.
> - Report to Jalester and carry Esloon's question back to him.
>
> Only Lords' Alliance members attend Jalester's brief and debrief. Their companions can help with everything in between.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Esloon Bezant has lived in the Dock Ward for five years, selling escorts and protection with a crew of five **Tough** fighters. He is weighing the Splinter's offer because it is the only serious one he has received, and he never says why he left Thay.
> - Sarvos Miln is the Splinter's broker. He offers Esloon 1,000 gp and a standing arrangement, and the Splinter means to spend the crew on one job and then drop it.
> - The crew is wanted for a strike on the Watch checkpoint on Fillet Lane three nights from now. The strike opens a window for a Splinter package bound for Baldur's Gate.
> - Jalester heard of the meeting from a Dock Ward informant. He knows the tavern, the night and the name Holvar, and he does not know the room, the hour or the checkpoint.
> - The Skewered Dragon belongs to the Splinter, as the party may remember from **Finding Floon**. Its landlord, Rovan Skell, helps nobody, and any question about the Holvar booking in the taproom reaches Sarvos before he arrives.
> - Jalester sends no Guards, asks for no string-pulling and wants the Alliance's name kept out of every Watch report. Nothing in this Event depends on a rank benefit.
> - Nobody in this Event names the Splinter's master. Jalester, Sarvos and Esloon say "the Splinter", "the other cell" or "my principal". Jalester holds that line even for a member who has **Manshoon Named** marked.

### The Brief

Jalester reaches Trollskull Manor at noon and speaks to the members in the taproom, standing, with the companions waiting in the kitchen. He comes in person because the meeting is tonight and a note could not carry what he needs to ask, and the deadline is the handshake tonight.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom of Trollskull Manor is empty at noon, with stools upturned on the tables while the floor dries. Jalester Silvermane steps in from the alley with his notebook open in one hand, and he waves away the chair you offer.
>
> > "Thank you, but I can't stay. The Splinter, the Black Network's other cell and not Davil's people, is courting a Red Wizard named Esloon Bezant. He meets their broker tonight at the Skewered Dragon, in a room booked under the name Holvar. I think they want his crew for something I can't yet see. I need that deal dead before the two of them shake hands tonight, and I need it done so that nothing traces back to us. Can you manage both?"

> [!social]**The Man Who Will Not Sit**
>
> Jalester Silvermane (Lawful Good, Cormyrean Human, he/him) :: the Alliance's field agent, here in person at midday and short of time.
>
> Jalester stands inside the door with his notebook open and does not take the chair he is offered. He is courteous and brisk, and he writes down every answer the members give him. Each time the talk eases he asks one question more specific than it needs, such as which door the party means to use. He says "Understood." when he accepts an answer.
>
> Jalester is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Who Esloon Bezant is, and why the Alliance wants him turned or detained instead of dead.
> - What the Splinter wants from him, as far as Jalester can tell.
> - Why the Skewered Dragon is dangerous ground, and why the Watch cannot be asked for help under his name.
> - What the party may offer Esloon, and what the Alliance will honor.
>
> He will not discuss the Stone of Golorr, the vault or any intelligence outside this deal.

> [!qna]**Who is Esloon Bezant?**
>
> Jalester turns a page in the notebook.
>
> > "A Thayan exile who has lived in the Dock Ward for about five years and sells escorts and protection. He keeps five men and does not discuss his past. The Watch holds no active warrant on him here. I want him turned or detained, and I don't want him dead."

> [!qna]**What does the Splinter want?**
>
> > "His crew, I believe, for an operation I can't see yet. A broker named Sarvos Miln is making the offer. I don't have the room, the hour or the target, and I would rather say so than guess."

> [!qna]**Why the Skewered Dragon?**
>
> > "It's a Splinter house, so the broker will feel safe there. You won't be welcome, and the landlord will tell you nothing. Please ask your questions somewhere other than the bar, because anyone who asks there is remembered."

> [!qna]**Can the Watch help?**
>
> > "Not under my name. I won't send a patrol or ask a favor tonight, and I'd like the Alliance kept out of every report. If a sergeant acts on something a citizen shows him, that is the city doing its work."

> [!qna]**What can we offer him?**
>
> > "Whatever you can honestly give. The Alliance doesn't pay in coin, so any money is yours to spend. I can promise six months in which we leave his business alone, and I will keep that promise."

> [!qna]**When do you want the report?**
>
> > "Tomorrow evening at my table by the well, whatever the result. If it fails, I would rather hear it plainly than hear it late."

If **Thayan File Obtained** is marked, Jalester hands the members a sealed folder before he leaves. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Jalester takes a sealed folder from inside his doublet and sets it on the nearest table.
>
> > "This came out of the Watch's questioning of Harko. It names Esloon and some of the people around him, and it carries the Hall of Justice seal. Use it if it helps you, and please bring the folder back afterward."

> [!gamemaster]**The Thayan File**
>
> The folder holds the sealed Watch file on Esloon from **The Exile's Return**: his arrival in Waterdeep, his associates and the Thayan embassy's old interest in him. It makes **Hand Esloon to the Watch** possible, and it lowers the DC of one check in this Event by 2. A character names that check before rolling, and the reduction can be used once.

When the members agree, Jalester writes the hour in his notebook, says "Understood." and leaves for the alley, and the afternoon belongs to the party. A member who refuses the brief, or abandons it past the handshake without trying anything, is what **Suspension and Dismissal** reads. A member who tries and fails has done what the brief asked.

### Finding the Meet

Jalester has the tavern and the name Holvar and nothing else. The meeting starts at seven bells, and the party needs the room, the hour and the way out before then. Three leads each give the room and the hour, and any one of them is enough. A party that follows all three also learns the building's exits and Esloon's habits.

> [!gamemaster]**The Skewered Dragon**
>
> The tavern stands on Dock Street in the Dock Ward, a short walk from Fillet Lane. The Splinter runs it as an informal ward headquarters.
>
> - **The taproom:** A long low room with the bar on the left, a dozen tables and the only stair to the rooms above, and the street door opens onto Dock Street.
> - **The Holvar room:** A private dining room at the back of the ground floor, reached by a narrow passage past the kitchen. It holds a table for four and a shuttered window, and a second door onto Dock Lane stays bolted from the inside.
> - **The kitchen yard:** The kitchen's back door opens onto Dock Lane, where the pot-boy empties slops, and a cooper's yard faces the shuttered window.
> - **Who is there at seven bells:** Esloon's five **Tough** fighters sit openly at separate tables. Their leader, Dunmar Crale, takes the stool at the mouth of the passage. A **Bandit** called Cutter nurses a drink beside the street door and watches anyone who asks about the Holvar booking, while Rovan Skell tends the bar. A dozen Splinter regulars fill the rest and stay out of any fight.

> [!exploration]**The Pot-Boy on Dock Lane**
>
> Tam Ferrow, a boy of about eleven, empties slops at the kitchen door through the afternoon, and he sells what he has seen for 5 sp. A character who pays him, or who carries buckets with him and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check, hears three things. The back room is laid for two at seven bells, and Rovan told the cook to keep the passage clear. A lean man with a signet ring also walked the passage at midday and tried the Dock Lane bolt.
>
> - **On a failure:** Tam shrugs and goes inside, and the same character can pay the 5 sp instead and gets the same answer.

> [!exploration]**The Side Door**
>
> Any character who walks Dock Lane, studies the tavern's side wall and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check sees a bolted door with fresh grease on the bolt. Above it a shuttered window shows lamplight at the edges. Through the gap a card is pinned inside the shutter with HOLVAR and a seven on it. The window belongs to the back room, and the door is its way out.
>
> - **On a failure:** The cooper in the neighboring yard watches the character sweep the same stretch of wall twice. Nothing else happens, and the other two leads stay open.

> [!exploration]**Esloon's Supper**
>
> Yarra Moss runs a cookshop on Net Street where Esloon eats every evening at six. Any character who buys a meal, asks about the Thayan and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check learns that he cancelled tonight's supper. Instead he ordered two covered plates sent to the Skewered Dragon's back room at ten past seven, to be carried down the kitchen passage by her girl. She adds that he sits with his back to the wall and tips well. She also says he touches the marks on his scalp when anyone says "Thay".
>
> - **The tray:** A character who asks for it can carry the plates in her girl's place for 1 gp.
> - **On a failure:** Yarra says she does not discuss customers, and the character can try again after buying a second meal.

If a character asks Rovan Skell or any regular about Holvar, read or paraphrase the following when the party enters the taproom:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The taproom of the Skewered Dragon is long and low, and the air under the beams smells of fried fish and spilled ale. Flying snakes are inked on the forearms of half the men along the walls, and the talk thins as you cross the floor. A man with a scarred lip turns his stool to face the door while the landlord wipes a mug and watches you come to the bar.

> [!social]**The Landlord Who Knows Nothing**
>
> Rovan Skell (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: the Splinter's tenant at the Skewered Dragon, a heavy man who wipes the same mug while he refuses to help.
>
> Rovan gives strangers nothing and gives questions about Holvar less. He answers with the same flat refusal however he is asked, and a coin on the bar changes nothing. Cutter leaves his stool within a minute of the first question.
>
> Rovan is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Drinks, rooms and the price of both.
>
> He will not discuss the Holvar booking, Esloon Bezant or anything about the back room.

> [!qna]**About the Holvar booking?**
>
> Rovan wipes the same mug a second time.
>
> > "Nobody by that name drinks here. Order something or get the fuck out of my light."

> [!gamemaster]**A Warned Broker**
>
> A question about Holvar in the taproom warns Sarvos. He arrives ten minutes early by the Dock Lane door instead of the street, so the street intercept in **Take the Broker** is gone. Every other part of the Event runs as written.

### Seven Bells

The clock starts at ten to seven. Esloon and Sarvos shake hands at half past seven, and the deal is scuttled if any approach below ends it before then.

> [!gamemaster]**The Evening**
>
> - **Ten to seven:** Esloon arrives by the street door with his crew, nods to Dunmar Crale and goes to the back room alone, while his other four fighters take separate tables.
> - **Seven:** Sarvos arrives alone on foot from the Fillet Lane end of Dock Street and goes to the back room, and Cutter nods to him as he passes.
> - **Ten past seven:** Yarra's girl carries two covered plates down the passage.
> - **Half past seven:** Esloon and Sarvos shake hands. Sarvos leaves by the Dock Lane door, and Esloon leaves by the street.
>
> Steel or spells in the building bring Sergeant Deneth Pike's patrol to the street door at the end of the fourth round. Two **Guard** fighters come with him, and they arrest anyone still fighting. Each character the sergeant finds in the taproom pays a 25 gp fine for breach of the peace. Pike writes down names and never asks who sent anyone.

The four approaches can be combined, and **Break Up the Meeting** is the only one that needs a fight. **Hand Esloon to the Watch**, **Turn Esloon** and **Take the Broker** can each end the deal without a blow.

### The Back Room

Read the following aloud when the party first enters the back room:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The back room holds a single table laid for two, with one lamp on a bracket above it. A compact man with a shaved head sits with his back to the wall, and faded tattoos run from his scalp down behind his ears. Across from him a lean man with a signet ring stops talking and lays one hand on a folded paper. The man with the shaved head looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "You have come to the wrong room, and you are interrupting a negotiation. State your business, and I will tell you what it will cost you."

If the party enters before seven bells, Sarvos's chair is empty and Esloon sits alone with a cup in front of him. Change the third sentence to match.

> [!social]**The Red Wizard at the Table**
>
> Esloon Bezant (Neutral, Thayan Human, he/him) :: a tattooed Red Wizard exile who sells escorts and protection in the Dock Ward and names his price before his service.
>
> Esloon is Indifferent and exact. He speaks in short complete sentences, keeps his back to the wall and touches the marks on his scalp whenever Thay is mentioned. He is weighing the Splinter's offer because it is the only serious one he has had, and he will take a better one if it is real. He does not say why he left Thay, and a careless mention of it turns him cold.
>
> Esloon is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Splinter pays and what it asks in return.
> - What the party offers, and what refusing the Splinter would cost him.
> - His crew, and what he will not do with it.
> - Whether there is another offer for a man in his position.
>
> He will not discuss why he left Thay or who there wants him.

> [!qna]**What is the Splinter paying?**
>
> > "One thousand gold for the crew and a standing arrangement afterward, with half of the gold paid tonight. Sarvos calls the arrangement protection, and I would like to know what it protects me from. I have asked him twice."

> [!qna]**Why take it?**
>
> > "It is the only serious offer I have received in five years. A man in my position needs someone who will stand behind him. So far, nobody has offered, and I do not count a favor as an offer."

> [!qna]**What about Thay?**
>
> Esloon touches the marks on his scalp.
>
> > "That word is not for taverns. Say it loudly again, you stupid cunt, and we will both be dead by morning. It is home, formerly, and I will not discuss it."

> [!qna]**What can the Alliance offer?**
>
> > "Say what you can actually give me. I have heard a great deal about the Alliance and nothing that I could spend."

### Break Up the Meeting

Entering the back room after Sarvos has sat down forces a confrontation. If the party offers talk first, Esloon listens, and the fight begins when talk fails or someone strikes. On the first turn of any fight Sarvos leaves through the Dock Lane door. His flight ends the deal at once, so the fight that follows is about Esloon and not about the contract.

> [!hazard]**Esloon Bezant**
>
> Esloon uses the **Mage** stat block from the 2024 *Monster Manual* unmodified, and he fights alone at every party size. The crew in the taproom does not join him in the back room. The **Lords' Alliance Mechanics Reference** audits the fight for three, four and five characters.
>
> - **Three characters:** Esloon fights alone and withdraws once round 2 has ended, whatever his hit points. The fight ends with the deal broken and Esloon not beaten.
> - **Four and five characters:** Esloon fights alone and withdraws as described below.
>
> #### Esloon's Tactics
> To begin, Esloon rises, moves behind the table and reads the room. He stays in the back room and does not follow the party into the passage.
>
> During combat, Esloon:
> - Splits his three Arcane Bursts across at least two targets in round 1, and casts no area spell in that round.
> - Casts at most one area spell in the whole fight, *Fireball* or *Cone of Cold*. In round 2 he uses *Fireball* on the far side of the table. He does so only if neither Sarvos nor any of his own fighters stands within 20 feet of the center.
> - Never attacks a creature that is at 0 hit points.
> - Keeps *Protective Magic* for his reaction, using *Shield* against a hit and *Counterspell* against a spell.
> - Withdraws on his turn once he has taken 31 or more damage (50 hit points or fewer), or once round 3 has ended (round 2 at three characters), whichever comes first. To do so he uses *Misty Step* toward the Dock Lane door or the window, casts *Invisibility* on himself and leaves the building. He stays invisible until he attacks or casts a spell.
>
> Before he goes, he stops at the window and asks his question, as described in **What Esloon Wants**. If he cannot leave because his *Misty Step* uses are spent or no space is open to him, he surrenders at 20 hit points or fewer. He says "Take the book." The fight ends when Esloon has withdrawn or surrendered.
>
> #### Sarvos's Flight
> On his first turn Sarvos uses Cunning Action to Dash, which gives him 60 feet of movement for the turn, and he runs through the Dock Lane door. If a character holds that door he surrenders instead. If he is cornered in the room and forced to fight, he uses the **Spy** stat block and surrenders at 13 hit points or fewer or when Grappled.
>
> #### The Crew in the Taproom
> Dunmar Crale and four others are drinking in the taproom, and they scatter the round after Esloon leaves. A party that goes out and fights them anyway has a separate encounter against **Tough** fighters. There are two at three characters, three at four and four at five. They fight with Pack Tactics, break when Crale falls and surrender if Grappled.

When Esloon surrenders, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Esloon lays a thick leather book on the table and takes his hand away from it, and the lamp shows Thayan script along its spine. His other hand has stopped moving toward his coat.
>
> > "Take the book. It holds everything I have prepared, and I would like to be paid for the rest of what I know."

The spellbook holds the spells in the **Mage** stat block, and a wizard in the party can copy them under the usual rules. Esloon walks out by the street door once the party lets him, and the deal is broken.

### Hand Esloon to the Watch

Sergeant Deneth Pike walks the Dock Street patrol with two **Guard** fighters. He passes the Skewered Dragon's corner at seven bells and again at half past. This approach needs **Thayan File Obtained**, because the sealed Watch file is the only paper that gives Pike a reason to hold Esloon. Without the file, Pike says the Watch cannot detain a man on a stranger's word, and the party uses another approach.

> [!exploration]**The Sergeant and the File**
>
> A character who meets Pike at seven bells and gives him the file needs no check. Pike reads the Hall of Justice seal and the list of associates, enters the tavern and reaches the back room ten minutes later. A character who reaches him a little after seven bells, while he is still on the corner, must make him hurry. That takes a **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** check.
>
> - **On a failure:** The patrol reaches the back room at half past seven, as the handshake happens. The deal concludes, and Esloon is detained anyway.
> - **Esloon's choice:** He does not run from two Guards and a sergeant holding a file that names his associates. A man the Watch has to hunt cannot stay in Waterdeep, so he asks his question as he is led out.

### Turn Esloon

The party can reach Esloon alone in the ten minutes before Sarvos arrives, or reach him at his table. Esloon will not discuss terms in front of Sarvos. If Sarvos is in the room, Esloon steps into the kitchen passage with the character, and Dunmar Crale stays at his shoulder.

> [!exploration]**Reaching the Back Room**
>
> - **The kitchen passage:** A character who goes through the kitchen between ten to seven and seven and makes a successful **DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth)** check reaches the back room unseen and finds Esloon alone there. On a failure Crale stops the character at the passage mouth, and Esloon hears the party out only after Sarvos has arrived.
> - **The supper tray:** A character who bought the tray from Yarra Moss carries the plates in at ten past seven, and Crale lets them pass without a check.
> - **The front:** A character who asks Crale for Esloon is refused, and walking past him begins **Break Up the Meeting** at once.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Dunmar Crale, a thick man with a broken nose, puts one boot across the mouth of the passage and does not look up from his cup.
>
> > "Mr. Bezant is in a meeting, and he doesn't see visitors. Turn round and go back to the bar, or I'll show you out through the fucking window."

> [!exploration]**Three Ways to Make Him Refuse**
>
> Each way needs about five minutes of Esloon's attention and ends with him declining the Splinter's offer. A failed attempt closes that way and leaves the other two open.
>
> - **The forged letter:** The party spends 25 gp to make a letter look as though it traveled with a Splinter courier. It says the Splinter wants Esloon's spellbook and will settle his account once the strike is done. A character who presents it and makes a successful **DC 16 Charisma (Deception)** check convinces him. He reads it twice and puts it in his coat. On a failure he recognizes the forgery, and the letter is useless.
> - **The warning:** A character who tells Esloon that the Alliance knows of the recruitment and will make his life in Waterdeep difficult can make a **DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation)** check. On a success he decides the Splinter's money is not worth the attention. On a failure he invites the party to leave.
> - **The bribe:** The party offers 500 gp from its own purse together with Jalester's promise of six months in which the Alliance leaves his business alone. No check is needed. Esloon counts the coin, says "That is less than the Splinter offered," and accepts, because the Splinter's price is one he cannot read.

When any way succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Esloon studies you for a long moment, then stands, buttons his coat and says a few words to Dunmar Crale, who gathers the crew from the taproom.
>
> > "That is inconvenient for me, and I will remember who caused it. I will tell Sarvos that the arrangement is declined, and I will tell him nothing else."

Esloon asks his question before he goes, as described in **What Esloon Wants**. Sarvos finds an empty chair, waits until half past seven and leaves by the Dock Lane door, and the deal is scuttled.

### Take the Broker

Sarvos is a broker who avoids a fight, and he surrenders when two armed people stand over him. Taking him before the handshake scuttles the deal. Taking him after it does not, because the crew is already hired, but the party still has Sarvos.

> [!exploration]**Taking Sarvos Miln**
>
> - **On the street:** Sarvos walks west along Dock Street from the Fillet Lane end alone at seven bells. A character who waits at the mouth of Dock Lane and makes a successful **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth)** check reaches him before he calls out. On a failure he sees them and runs for the street door, Cutter and Crale come out, and the Watch clock starts from that moment.
> - **At the Dock Lane door:** A character in the lane when the bolt is drawn catches him as he comes out. This works before the handshake if the party has broken up the meeting, and at half past seven if the party has waited. Before the handshake the deal is scuttled, and after it the deal stands and Sarvos is still caught.
> - **Holding him:** The party keeps Sarvos alive and locked in a room at Trollskull Manor until the debrief. If he dies, the signed arrangement is still in his coat, but **Sarvos Captured** is not marked.

> [!social]**The Broker**
>
> Sarvos Miln (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: the Splinter's broker, a lean man with a signet ring who prefers to be agreeable and never names his principal.
>
> Sarvos is polite while he is useful and brisk when he is not. He bargains with whoever holds him, offers a small thing to avoid giving a large one, and swears once or twice when he is caught, but he will not name his principal.
>
> Sarvos is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What he was offering Esloon, and why.
> - What the Splinter wants for it.
> - What he expects the Alliance to do with him.
>
> He will not discuss his principal, the Splinter's other operations or anyone above him.

> [!qna]**Where is the strike?**
>
> Sarvos looks at the ring on his hand and then at the door.
>
> > "I'm a broker, not a soldier, and I don't die for contracts. The strike is on the Watch checkpoint on Fillet Lane, three nights from now. It clears a window for one cart, and the cart goes to Baldur's Gate."

> [!qna]**Who do you work for?**
>
> > "A principal who prefers not to be named, and I won't change that for you. Take me to the Watch, if you like. I've done nothing they can charge me with, and we both know it, damn you."

> [!exploration]**What Sarvos Gives Up**
>
> The checkpoint can be named in two ways. The signed arrangement in Sarvos's coat names the checkpoint on Fillet Lane, the night and the cart for Baldur's Gate, and anyone who searches him finds it. A character who questions him and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check gets the same answer from his own mouth. Sarvos adds the window the strike is meant to open. Either way, **Sarvos Captured** is marked once the party holds him alive with the checkpoint in hand.

### What Esloon Wants

Any approach that puts a character in front of Esloon opens his question. He asks it at the window when he withdraws, when he has declined the Splinter's offer, or as the Watch leads him out. A party that only takes Sarvos before the meeting can still walk into the back room. There it meets Esloon, who is waiting for a broker who does not come. Read or paraphrase the following to the character who last spoke to him:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Esloon stops where he stands and turns toward you, one hand lifted to the marks on his scalp.
>
> > "One question, and it is not about the deal. I have been in this city five years. The Splinter is the only party in that time that has offered me anything I could count. Is there another offer? Not from you personally, but from whoever decides these things."

Esloon takes any answer without comment and says "I will hear it when it comes." He does not wait for any promise from the party. A character who says they will carry the question to the right people has made no promise that binds the Alliance, and Esloon knows it.

### Aftermath

What happens to Esloon depends on how the evening ended.

- **Esloon turned:** He stays in the Dock Ward with his crew intact and no longer in the Splinter's pocket. If the question reached Jalester, he regards the Alliance as cautiously observant and not hostile.
- **Esloon detained:** He is held for ten days and released without charge, because the Thayan material does not survive the embassy review. Afterwards he leaves Waterdeep, and his crew disperses to other employers.
- **Esloon fought and withdrew:** He leaves the Splinter's company and keeps his crew, and he does not trust the party. If he surrendered his spellbook, he leaves Waterdeep within a tenday.
- **The deal concluded:** The Splinter's Dock Ward crews carry five extra hired fighters in **Faction Outposts** (unconverted), and the Fillet Lane strike goes ahead three nights later.
- **Sarvos Captured:** The two plain-clothes officers take him from Trollskull Manor in the morning. The strike on Fillet Lane fails in **Faction Outposts** (unconverted), and the Splinter loses its cargo window.

## Overview

Jalester asks the party to end a Splinter deal with a Thayan wizard at a Dock Ward tavern before it is sealed tonight.

Jalester Silvermane came to Trollskull Manor himself and told us the Splinter was buying a Thayan wizard's crew that night. Esloon Bezant asked us whether anyone had a better offer for a man like him, and what will Jalester say when we tell him?
