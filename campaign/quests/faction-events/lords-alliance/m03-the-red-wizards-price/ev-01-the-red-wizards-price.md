# The Red Wizard's Price

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social/Combat Event begins when Jalester arrives at Trollskull Manor. In this Event:
>
> - The party has roughly thirty minutes to scuttle the Esloon/Sarvos deal before it concludes.
> - Four distinct approaches are available: direct disruption, Watch exposure, turning Esloon, or neutralizing Sarvos.
> - Any approach that brings the party into direct engagement with Esloon opens his question about whether there is a real offer from the Alliance.
> - If the deal concludes uninterrupted, the Splinter gains eight additional pieces of muscle for the **Faction Outposts** checkpoint strike.
> - Sarvos captured provides actionable intelligence on the Watch checkpoint operation.

**Background (DM only)**

**Esloon Bezant** (modified Mage stat block; Chaotic Neutral; once per day he can cast *Counterspell* as a Reaction without spending a spell slot, replacing Arcane Recovery) left Thay four years ago after a failed coup against his chapter lord. He has an outstanding Thayan warrant with the Baldur's Gate embassy — not active in Waterdeep. He runs a crew of six Thugs and two Veterans in the Dock Ward who know better than to ask about his past.

The Splinter fixer meeting him is **Sarvos Miln** (Spy stat block), who handles contractor recruitment for Manshoon's Dock Ward operations. The operation Sarvos is recruiting for: a coordinated strike on a Watch checkpoint in the Dock Ward to create a window for a Splinter intelligence package bound for a Manshoon associate in Baldur's Gate. The meeting started an hour before Jalester briefed the party; it is still ongoing. The private dining room at the Skewered Dragon is booked under the name "Holvar."

The Skewered Dragon layout: taproom on the ground floor, single staircase, private dining room at the back with a second exit onto Dock Lane. Sarvos has one watcher in the taproom. The private room door has two of Esloon's Veterans standing outside it.

### Act 1 — Finding the Meet

Jalester has partial intelligence: the Skewered Dragon Tavern, tonight, under the name "Holvar." He does not know which room or when the meeting ends. The party has three quick reads available as they approach:

- **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) with the taproom host:** The "Holvar" booking is in the back corner room. Two people arrived separately — "a foreign-looking gentleman in a merchant's coat, and a broad man with a signet ring." The meeting is still ongoing.
- **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) at the taproom entrance:** A Splinter watcher (Bandit stat block) is nursing a drink near the door, positioned to observe anyone who comes in asking for the Holvar table.
- **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) of the building's exterior:** A second exit from the private dining room opens onto Dock Lane — a street-facing door, currently bolted from inside. This is Sarvos's exit when things go wrong.

### Act 2 — Options for Scuttling the Deal

The party has approximately thirty minutes from when they arrive. The deal is scuttled if any of the following resolves before it concludes. Use whichever combination the party attempts.

#### Option A — Disrupt the Meeting Directly

Entering the private room creates an immediate confrontation. Esloon's two Veterans are seated outside the dining room door. If the confrontation turns violent, Sarvos goes through the Dock Lane exit; if physically blocked from that exit, he surrenders. Esloon fights if cornered.

The cost of this approach: noise, Watch attention within four rounds if weapons are drawn in the taproom, and Sarvos escaping with the operation intact unless the Dock Lane exit is covered.

#### Option B — Expose Esloon to a Watch Patrol

Presenting Jalester's Thayan intelligence file (from Mission 2's Aftermath, if the party obtained it) to a Watch patrol gives them grounds to detain Esloon for a holding inquiry. A Watch patrol responds in three rounds. If Esloon is detained before the meeting concludes, Sarvos leaves without a deal and the Splinter loses this avenue.

Without the intelligence file: the Thayan warrant is not active in Waterdeep. The Watch will not detain Esloon on the party's say-so without supporting documentation. This approach requires the file.

#### Option C — Turn Esloon Against the Deal

Reaching Esloon before the meeting concludes requires a **DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth)** approach through the taproom kitchen to get into position before Sarvos finishes his pitch.

Once in position: a **DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion)** check, offering Esloon something the Splinter cannot — specifically, the Alliance's ability to quash future Thayan embassy inquiries in Waterdeep and any city where the Lords' Alliance has standing. On a success, Esloon declines the Splinter deal and departs. He does not become an Alliance asset. He simply removes himself from the equation.

The DC drops to 13 if the party acknowledged Esloon's actual position earlier in the negotiation — that he is being asked to accept a worse deal than the Splinter's for loyalty to a faction that has given him nothing. Jalester, when told about this conversation later, pauses and says: "He's not wrong that we're asking him for a favor without offering much. I'll look into whether there's something we can formalize." This is not a commitment. It is Jalester acknowledging the point.

#### Option D — Neutralize Sarvos

Capturing or following Sarvos rather than engaging Esloon collapses the deal from the other direction. Sarvos can be intercepted on the way in (he arrives separately, on foot, alone) or intercepted at the Dock Lane exit when the meeting concludes.

If captured before the meeting: the deal never closes and Esloon leaves without knowing why his contact failed to appear. If captured at exit after the meeting: the deal may have concluded; Sarvos is carrying a signed arrangement that is now unenforceable without him.

A captured Sarvos will not give up Manshoon's identity. He confirms the Watch checkpoint operation — the specific checkpoint, the window the strike is designed to create, and the Baldur's Gate destination for the cargo. This is the intelligence Jalester can use to pre-position Watch reinforcements.

### What Esloon Actually Wants

Any approach that puts the party in direct conversation with Esloon — Options A, C, or a confrontation that doesn't immediately escalate — may open this beat. After the initial exchange, whatever form it takes, Esloon leans back.

> > "I want to ask you something. Not about the deal."

He looks at the table for a moment.

> > "I fled Thay because they were going to kill me. Not because I had done something wrong by their standards — I had done something right, which amounted to the same thing. I have been in this city for five years. I have built something here. Not legitimate. But real."

He meets the party's eyes directly.

> > "The Zhentarim offered me protection. Real protection, not the Alliance's kind, where you exist at their convenience. I am considering it because it is the only serious offer I have received. Is there another offer? Not from you personally. From whoever decides these things."

He is asking whether there is a path in Waterdeep that does not end with him being used and discarded by whoever is currently convenient. The party can choose to bring this question back to Jalester.

Jalester's response, if asked: a long pause, then: "He's not wrong that we're asking him for a favor without offering much. I'll look into whether there's something we can formalize." This is not a commitment. It is an acknowledgment. If the party followed through on Esloon's behalf, Esloon's attitude toward the Alliance shifts from neutral to cautiously observant. He does not become an asset. He stops being a liability.

### Resolution

The deal is scuttled if any of the following is true: Esloon is detained or withdraws from negotiations; Sarvos is captured or neutralized; the meeting is directly disrupted before it concludes; or the Watch checkpoint operation is exposed and reported before the meeting ends.

**If the deal concludes:** Esloon and Sarvos shake hands in the private room and part through different exits. The Splinter gains eight additional pieces of muscle for the Dock Ward checkpoint strike. In **Faction Outposts**, the Splinter response team in the Dock Ward has two additional members when the party encounters it.

**If Esloon turned away and not detained:** He remains in the Dock Ward with his crew, nominally independent. He resurfaces in **Faction Outposts** as a possible hire for any faction with coin — the party may encounter him working for the Xanathar Guild or as a freelance complication, depending on whether the party brought his question back to Jalester.

**If Esloon detained:** He is held for ten days and released without charge — the Thayan warrant does not survive the embassy review process. He leaves Waterdeep. His crew disperses.

**If the checkpoint strike is exposed:** The strike still occurs in **Faction Outposts** but fails — the Splinter loses the cargo window and delays their Baldur's Gate communication by three tendays. One Splinter operation in **Faction Outposts** is noticeably disrupted.

## Overview

The Manshoon Splinter is negotiating a deal with a Thayan exile. Jalester needs it killed tonight. The party has thirty minutes and four ways to do it.

## Read Aloud

> Jalester arrives at Trollskull Manor at noon and doesn't sit down. He's moving faster than his usual style.
>
> > "The Zhentarim are courting a Red Wizard named Esloon Bezant. Manshoon's people, not Davil's. Former Thayan, probably exiled, has a crew of eight he calls contractors. They're negotiating right now. The deal is muscle for an operation we don't fully understand yet, but the Zhentarim faction running this isn't the kind you negotiate with. I need the deal killed tonight. I don't care how."
>
> He gives you the Skewered Dragon Tavern's location and the booking name. He is already turning for the door.

## Summary

Jalester sent the party to scuttle a deal between the Manshoon Splinter and a Thayan exile named **Esloon Bezant** before it concluded at the Skewered Dragon Tavern. The party had four approaches available and roughly thirty minutes. Any engagement with Esloon directly opened his question about whether the Lords' Alliance had anything real to offer someone in his position — a question Jalester, when told about it, acknowledged without answering.
