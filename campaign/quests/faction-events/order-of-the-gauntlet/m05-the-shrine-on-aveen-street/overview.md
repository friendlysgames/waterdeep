# The Shrine on Aveen Street

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Order of the Gauntlet Mission 5. Available at Renown 10 and character level 6. Follows **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**.*
>
> #### Sequencing
> This mission should be offered before **Cassalanter Villa** begins. It establishes a Cassalanter ritual foothold that **Cassalanter Villa** references, and it generates the four-day compression of the Founders' Day deadline that gives **Cassalanter Villa** its central urgency. If **Cassalanter Villa** has already resolved, this mission loses its downstream impact — Mission 6 follows normally, but the compressed deadline becomes historical context rather than active pressure.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 6th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

The shrine on Aveen Street is a converted basement that was leased two years ago through an intermediary to a civic association — that is what Orros the chandler was told, and he has not been downstairs in fourteen months. The basement holds a consecration circle in powdered iron filings and a stone table with an operational ledger on it: Lord Victoro Cassalanter's own handwriting, twelve ritual entries, names, dates, locations, and a Founders' Day deadline for a final completion rite called the Reckoning. A locked strongbox holds corroborating notes.

The Order has the shrine address from the Mission 4 investigation but cannot enter. Religious property provisions in the Code Legal protect private shrines against Order action without specific legal grounds. Citizens have no such restriction.

A bearded devil is bound to the ritual perimeter. It cannot leave the basement, but within those bounds it fights without restraint.

When the ledger leaves the shrine, Lord Victoro triggers an infernal rider in his contract — a clause that names and marks everyone who physically handled his ritual documentation. The consequences arrive in Mission 6.

## Involved Characters
- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet) — briefs the party at the Halls of Justice, not at Trollskull Manor; takes the ledger directly to the Lords' review petition after the mission
- **Orros** (chandler, Aveen Street) — the innocent shop owner who leased the basement two years ago to "a civic association"; nervous; has not been downstairs in fourteen months
- **Bearded Devil** — the guardian bound to the shrine's ritual perimeter; cannot leave, but fights without restraint within those bounds

## Dangers & Enemies
One **Bearded Devil** (2024 *Monster Manual*), bound to the shrine's ritual perimeter. It cannot leave the basement, but within those bounds it fights without restraint. Its *Beard* attack grapples and causes ongoing bleeding; a character reduced to 0 Hit Points by it is Cursed (unable to regain Hit Points) until a Short Rest with appropriate healing, or a *Remove Curse*.

## Overview
A consecrated basement under a chandler's shop holds the evidence Savra needs — and a bound devil guarding it.
