# The Shrine on Aveen Street: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level, after **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**. Companions can help with everything except Savra's Brief and debrief, which only members attend. A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** receives the summons as a note from Brother Hallin Ostrel, carried by a Priest Acolyte.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 6th-level characters.*
>
> The mission is an investigation and a raid on a cellar. The caretakers use the **Cultist Fanatic** block, the guardian uses the **Bearded Devil** block and the sentry uses the **Imp** block. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits the rosters for three, four and five characters, and Savra takes no part in the fight.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Order of the Gauntlet members who report to Savra.

## Hook

A Priest Acolyte brings a note from the Halls of Justice, and Savra meets the members at the east annex at the third bell past noon the next day. She has found a cellar under a chandler's shop on Aveen Street, and she wants the book she thinks it holds. The Garrowby Cartage dray calls at the yard on the seventh day after the Brief, so the members have six days to get inside.

## Background

Gysheer Omfreys's Garrowby Cartage receipt for nine crates and her day-book pointed at a cellar door in a yard on Aveen Street, and Savra found the lease in the Halls' rolls. A charity called the Aveen Street Almsgivers' Society took the cellar of Orros's chandlery six years ago, and nobody has met a member of it. Savra cannot enter without a magistrate's warrant, because the Code Legal protects any place of worship, and a warrant needs a document the Order does not have.

The cellar is a back way into the Asmodean shrine on Aveen Street, whose main house stands across the street in the North Ward. The party enters the stair room and the circle room, and the other areas of the shrine stay with the Cassalanters Factions Guide.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The ledger on the table in the circle room is in Victoro Cassalanter's hand. It holds twelve dated entries and gives no terms, no reasons and no other party.
> - The twelfth entry is dated Flamerule 1, Founders' Day, and it does not move. The household's plans keep to the same date whatever the party does.
> - A Watch clerk, Ennick Vause, sells the day's petitions to a man who pays for such news on the family's behalf. A filing alerts the Cassalanters within a day unless the party catches the clerk.
> - The Bearded Devil was posted after Gysheer was taken. It is bound to a ring of iron filings and wakes only when someone lifts the ledger, steps on the filings or breaks the circle.

## The Brief

Savra briefs the members at the east annex of the Halls of Justice, while the companions wait on the bench by the door-warden. She gives the deadline, three vials of holy water and three lawful ways to reach the cellar.

## Aveen Street

Orros, the nervous chandler, owns the building and has not been below for fourteen months. The party can ask him for the key, bring Father Corwen Thale for the Halls' fire inspection or go to the cellar door in the yard, where callers knock three times. A **Kelso's Debt** favour can empty the stair room or draw the Imp away.

## The Stair Room

A Cultist Fanatic in a carter's coat keeps the stair room. A quiet descent or the three knocks catches him by surprise, an inspection makes him stand aside, and a forced yard door finds him waiting with a knife.

## The Circle Room

The Bearded Devil stands in the ring with an invisible Imp above the table, among the candles, salt, iron filings and lamp oil that the crates carried. The ledger can be swapped for a stock book from Orros's shop without waking the devil, taken after a fight or copied from the strongbox, and the circle can be broken afterward.

## The Ledger

The book holds twelve entries of dates, initials and places, and three independent ways identify what kind of book it is. A recognized bargain in writing is the most any check can say.

## The Filing and the Chaplain

Savra takes the ledger to a Watch clerk's window and has it entered as an exhibit. Her chaplain then gives the party a Siamorphe holy symbol and says plainly that she does not know what it will do.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 4 base Renown for reporting to Savra at the debrief. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** the Bearded Devil is destroyed with no character reduced to 0 hit points, or the ledger leaves the cellar without waking it.
- **+1 Renown:** the ledger reaches Savra within 24 hours of leaving the cellar.
- **+1 Renown:** Orros is neither alarmed nor harmed, and he is not told what lay in his cellar.

Companions who are not Order of the Gauntlet members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once. The standing Earning Renown actions for evidence and for a devil of Challenge Rating 3 or higher pay once each and are not added here.

## Aftermath

**Ledger Recovered** is read by **The Writ and the Weight**, by **Whitehawk**, by **Vindicator** and by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted). **Shrine Destroyed** is read by **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted), if it has not run. **Cassalanter Register Filed** is read by **Vindicator**, by **The Writ and the Weight** and by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted). **Cassalanters Alerted** moves the Cassalanters one escalation tier toward the party and is read by **The Writ and the Weight** and by **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted). **The Writ and the Weight** becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**.

## Involved Characters

- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet): briefs the members at the Halls, waits there during the raid and enters the ledger.
- **Orros** (Independent): the chandler who leased his cellar to the Almsgivers' Society and has not been below for fourteen months.
- **Father Corwen Thale** (Temple of Tyr): leads the Halls' fire inspection and reads the ledger for the party.
- **Brother Haldric Wence** (Order of the Gauntlet): the door-warden of the Halls of Justice.
- **Ysolt Brannock** (Order of the Gauntlet): the Order's chaplain, who gives the Siamorphe holy symbol.
- **Ennick Vause** (City of Waterdeep): a Watch clerk who enters the ledger and sells the day's petitions.
- **Victoro Cassalanter** (the Cassalanters): the hand behind the ledger, who never appears.

## Dangers & Enemies

The caretakers are **Cultist Fanatic** fighters in plain coats, one at three and four characters and two at five on Standard. The guardian is a **Bearded Devil** bound to the circle, and an **Imp** watches from the rafters at four and five characters. The Hard roster adds a Cultist Fanatic at four and five characters and the Imp at three.

## Overview

Savra sends the party into a chandler's cellar on Aveen Street to find a book she cannot take herself. The party comes out with a ledger in Victoro Cassalanter's hand.
