# Design Notes: The Shrine on Aveen Street

## Sequencing Dependency

***Cassalanter Villa Integration.*** This mission functions as structural setup for **Cassalanter Villa**. Specifically, it generates two interconnected consequences that need to land before **Cassalanter Villa** plays out:

1. Savra's Lords' petition places the Cassalanters under formal legal scrutiny, adding ambient pressure to the villa heist and giving the party a legal foothold they can reference in **Cassalanter Villa**.
2. Lord Victoro's response — accelerating the Founders' Day timeline by four days — is the direct source of **Cassalanter Villa**'s compressed deadline urgency.

If the party completes **Cassalanter Villa** before running this mission, neither consequence lands with any force. Savra's calendar intelligence arrives as history, not a live clock, and the petition's legal pressure has nowhere to go. The mission still plays, and Mission 6 follows normally, but the timeline compression becomes backstory the party can only look back at rather than pressure they can feel.

The sequencing constraint is not about narrative order for its own sake. It is about whether this mission's payoff is forward-looking or retrospective. Offer it in the level 6 window, before the party has committed to the **Cassalanter Villa** heist timeline.

## The Shrine Destroyed Outcome

***Downstream Cassalanter Pressure.*** The **Shrine Destroyed** outcome is read by **Cassalanter Villa** as one element of the Cassalanter pressure state — a measure of how many footholds the family has lost before the Founders' Day confrontation. Destroying the shrine does not change the villa's encounter structure, but the GM should acknowledge it in **Cassalanter Villa** as a Cassalanter loss the family is reacting to behind closed doors: one more thing they were not prepared for, one fewer resource available on the day.
