# Design Notes: The Shrine on Aveen Street

## What the Source Gave

Dragon Heist and Appendix C stop at the fifth Order mission, so this mission and the next are repo inventions. The restored draft gave a single locked door, one Bearded Devil and a ledger that explained a soul-bargain, twelve acts, a Reckoning and a Founders' Day deadline, and it moved Founders' Day four days earlier through a Masked Lord contact. Those pieces fail the Cassalanter secrecy rule and the calendar. Founders' Day is a fixed festival, and the pact belongs to **Cassalanter Villa**, not to a cellar in the Field Ward.

## The Hybrid Shrine

The Asmodean shrine in the Cassalanters Factions Guide stands in the North Ward on Aveen Street. The chandler's shop stands in the Field Ward, so the draft puts the street on the ward line and runs a service passage from the cellar to the shrine house. Together with **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**, the cellar is a fifth independent path to the shrine, beside the Guide's three. Only the stair room and the circle room are keyed. The other six areas keep the Guide's text, and the Hell cyst and the Mirror of the Fiend are left for **Faction Outposts**.

## What the Ledger Says

The ledger holds twelve dated entries with names, initials and places, and a last line that is a bare date. A recognized bargain in writing is the most a check can find, and no one in the Event names a pact, its terms or a deadline. The twelfth line is Founders' Day because everyone in the city knows the date, and a party that connects the two has done so without help. Three entries tie to things the party has met, namely Gysheer, the Crispin Street room and Twelvedog Court, and the rest are initials and places the party can follow later.

## Departures from the Source

The Alert comes from the filing and from the Imp, not from a Masked Lord contact. A Watch clerk who sells petitions is the leak, and a party that catches him keeps the Cassalanters Unaware of the filing. The mission awards a Siamorphe holy symbol at its end so that **Cassalanter Villa** can read it from the party's possessions, and the chaplain says she does not know what it does. The quiet route is a swap with a stock ledger from Orros's shop, which gives the Bearded Devil a non-combat answer without letting it leave the ring.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Chaplain Ysolt Brannock, Ennick Vause and the Aveen Street Almsgivers' Society are new. A search of `campaign/` for "Ysolt", "Brannock", "Ennick", "Vause" and "Almsgiver" found no match.
- Orros appears in the restored draft and has no profile, so his voice comes from the Event text. "Orros" is not used on any other page.
- Savra's Notable Figures page says "neutral good" and "Tethyrian"; this Event follows the brief's Lawful Good and the First Meeting. The page needs the correction.
- The Notable Figures page for Sister Valdra links the villa to Asmodeus worship; she does not appear here.
- The Cassalanters Factions Guide says the shrine is "From outside, an unremarkable residence" and lists no chandlery. The cellar entrance is a small addition that the guide needs.
- The Code Legal rule that bars entry to a place of worship without a magistrate's warrant, the 3 gp rent, the Daggerford sister and the seventh-day carter are invented. The Halls of Justice stays in the Castle Ward, as the First Meeting fixes it.
- The cartage receipt and the sealed note belong to **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**, which the restored draft of that mission did not yet carry; the Brief reads them only through **Intelligence Shared**.
