# The Shrine on Aveen Street

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat/Exploration Event begins when Savra briefs the party at the Halls of Justice.
> In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Savra's brief at the Halls of Justice and ask what she knows about the shrine.
> - Approach Orros the chandler for access to his alley, or bypass him entirely.
> - Force or pick the locked cellar door in the alley behind the shop.
> - Navigate the shrine's infernal interior and deal with the bearded devil guardian.
> - Recover the operational ledger in Lord Victoro Cassalanter's own handwriting and the corroborating notes in the locked strongbox.
> - Deliver the ledger to Savra and watch her take it directly to the Halls of Justice.
>
> #### The Shrine's Contents
>
> The shrine is a converted basement used intermittently as a ritual preparation site. The current configuration is a consecration circle for one of the twelve infernal rites Lord Victoro is conducting under his devil contract. The ledger loose on the stone table is the most important document: twelve entries, each naming a specific ritual step, a specific location, and a date, with three entries referencing a Founders' Day deadline for a final completion rite called the Reckoning. The strongbox holds corroborating notes.
>
> Savra has the address from the Mission 4 investigation but cannot enter. Religious property provisions in the Code Legal protect private shrines against Order action without specific legal grounds. The party are citizens and have no such restriction.
>
> When the ledger leaves the shrine, Lord Victoro triggers an infernal rider in his contract — a clause that names and marks everyone who physically handled his ritual documentation. The consequences arrive in Mission 6.

### The Brief

Savra meets the party at the Halls of Justice, not at Trollskull Manor. She has been here most of the morning.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra meets you at the Halls of Justice, not at the manor. The Order's Cassalanter file now takes up three shelves, and she is working at a table near the window when you arrive, calm in the way that people are calm when they have finally reached the point where something can be done.
>
> > "The shrine on Aveen Street in the Field Ward — Gysheer named it. I cannot send Order knights into a private structure without legal grounds; this is still a shrine, protected under the Code Legal's religious property provisions. You, however, are citizens."
>
> She looks at you across the table.
>
> > "If you find physical evidence — a ritual record, a signed instruction from a named Cassalanter, anything that implicates a specific person in a specific act — I can take it to the Lords. I need it in hand, not described to me."

> [!social]**The Prepared Knight**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Neutral Good, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: a paladin of Tyr who has been building this case for months and has everything she knows laid out before her.
>
> Savra is composed and specific. She has been here since morning, and she knows exactly what she wants and what she needs. She will not overstate the danger inside the shrine because she does not know what is inside it. She will not understate the importance of the ledger if they find one.
>
> She can tell the party about Orros — the chandler is innocent and nervous, and she does not want him further alarmed. She cannot tell them what is inside the basement because she has never been inside it.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss include:
> - The shrine address and the alley layout, which she has from Gysheer's information.
> - Orros the chandler: innocent, leased the basement two years ago, has not been inside in fourteen months.
> - What she needs: a document naming a specific person in a specific act, not a confession.
> - The legal position: why she cannot enter, and why the party can.

> [!qna]**What's inside?**
>
> She pauses before she answers.
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly: I don't know. I have an address and a description from someone who summoned devils in a Field Ward courtyard. Whatever is in that basement, go in prepared for it."

> [!qna]**How dangerous?**
>
> > "Consecrated spaces sometimes have guardians. Plan for that."

> [!qna]**What about the chandler?**
>
> > "Orros. He's innocent, and I'd like him to stay that way. If you need his alley, tell him as little as you have to. He does not ask questions he does not want the answers to."

> [!qna]**The legal protection?**
>
> > "Religious property provisions under the Code Legal. They protect private shrines from Order action without specific grounds. You are not the Order. You are citizens, and citizens have no such restriction."

> [!qna]**What document specifically?**
>
> > "A ritual record. A signed instruction from a named Cassalanter. Anything that connects a specific person to a specific act, in that person's hand or with their mark on it. That is what I need."

### Aveen Street

The cellar entrance is a locked door set in the alley behind Orros's chandler's shop on Aveen Street.

> [!readaloud]
> The chandler's shop on Aveen Street is a narrow building between a cooper's and a shuttered spice merchant, with an alley running behind it wide enough for a single delivery cart. A cellar door is set flush into the alley cobbles near the shop's rear wall. The door is locked with a good-quality padlock, the iron clean and relatively new compared to the weathered timber around it.

> [!gamemaster]**Approaching Orros**
>
> If the party approaches the chandler's shop from the front, Orros is behind the counter, visibly uncertain. He will not obstruct access to his own alley. A successful **DC 10 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check** gets him to confirm he leased the basement two years ago to a civic association and has not been down since. He does not ask questions he does not want the answers to.

> [!exploration]**The Cellar Door**
>
> The padlock on the cellar door requires a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check** to pick, or **DC 15 Strength** to force.
>
> - **Advantage:** A character who mentions that Orros gave them permission to access the alley gains Advantage on the Persuasion check with him but not on the door itself.

### Inside the Shrine

The basement is cold for a building that has been occupied. The smell reaches the party before the light does.

> [!readaloud]
> The stairs go down about twelve feet into a low-ceilinged basement. Candles are burning on iron stands around the perimeter — not tallow candles, something else, casting a red-tinged light that turns the walls the color of old rust. A seven-pointed circle dominates most of the floor, drawn in what looks like iron filings, each of the seven points stained dark at the center. The smell is sulfur and something underneath that — older, harder to name.
>
> Against the far wall: a locked strongbox, a set of ritual implements laid out with the care of someone who expects to return to them, and a leather-bound ledger lying open on a stone table.

> [!gamemaster]**Forced Strongbox**
>
> If the strongbox is forced rather than picked, the lock is destroyed but the contents are intact. Savra takes it regardless of condition. The loose ledger on the stone table is the more important document; the strongbox contains supporting notes that corroborate its entries.

> [!hazard]**Bearded Devil Guardian**
>
> One **Bearded Devil** (2024 *Monster Manual*) is bound to the shrine's ritual perimeter. It cannot leave the basement, but within those bounds it fights without restraint.
>
> #### Bearded Devil Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the bearded devil attacks the first character who crosses the threshold of the consecration circle. It cannot be lured outside the perimeter or driven back through the cellar door.
>
> Over the course of combat, the devil will prioritize grappling with its *Beard* attack and then dealing ongoing bleeding damage. A character reduced to 0 Hit Points by its *Beard* attack is Cursed (unable to regain Hit Points) until a Short Rest with appropriate healing or a *Remove Curse*.
>
> The battle ends when the bearded devil is destroyed.

> [!exploration]**The Strongbox**
>
> The strongbox requires a successful **DC 16 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) check** to pick, or **DC 18 Strength** to force. Inside: supporting notes that corroborate the ledger's entries, in several different hands.

### The Ledger

The ledger is the document Savra has been waiting for. Any character who picks it up can read it without a check.

> [!readaloud]
> The ledger is open to a page of handwritten entries, each one dated, each one naming a specific step and a specific location. The handwriting is careful and consistent. Twelve entries in total, numbered. The first three cross-reference the Aveen Street address. Three of the later entries, spaced across the remaining pages, reference the same deadline — a date on Founders' Day — and the same phrase for whatever that date is meant to complete: the Reckoning.

> [!exploration]**Reading the Ledger**
>
> Any character who examines the ledger and makes a successful **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) check** understands the structure of what they are reading.
>
> > The entries follow the fulfillment schedule of a classical Asmodean soul-bargain — the type documented in Tyr's theological libraries. Each entry corresponds to one of the required ritual acts under an infernal compact. Twelve acts, all dated. Three remain. The Founders' Day deadline is real, and the Reckoning is the final act of completion.
>
> Any character who looks for a signature or a mark of ownership finds it on the inside front cover: a name written in the same careful hand as the entries, and a date of first entry two years ago.
>
> > *Victoro Cassalanter. Ches, Year of the Ageless One.*

### The Aftermath

The party brings the ledger to Savra. She recognizes what it is before they finish the sentence.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra takes the ledger from whoever is holding it and reads the first three entries before she says anything, then the front cover, then she sets it face-down on the table and is quiet for a moment.
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly: this is what I needed. This is what I've been building toward."
>
> She turns it back over and looks at the Founders' Day entries again.
>
> > "Three acts remaining. One deadline. And now it's four days sooner than it was — the petition will reach the Cassalanters through their Masked Lord contact within forty-eight hours. Victoro will accelerate the timeline when it does. I'll have the exact date for you as soon as I can calculate it from the entries."
>
> She tucks the ledger into a document case and rises.
>
> > "I'm going to the Lords' review office. Don't go anywhere useful in the next two days."

> [!gamemaster]**The Petition**
>
> Savra takes the ledger directly to the Halls of Justice and files a formal petition for Lords' review of the Cassalanter family's activities under the Code Legal's provisions on infernal compact. The petition does not result in immediate arrest — the Code Legal's religious protections are extensive and the Cassalanters have excellent lawyers. But it places the family under formal legal scrutiny.
>
> The Cassalanters are informed of the petition within forty-eight hours through their Masked Lord contact. Lord Victoro accelerates the Founders' Day timeline by four days. Savra calculates this from the ledger's dated entries and passes the intelligence to the party as soon as she has it: the Reckoning is now four days earlier than the party would otherwise expect.

## Overview

A consecrated basement under a chandler's shop holds the evidence Savra needs — and a bound devil guarding it.

## Summary

We raided the Cassalanter shrine on Aveen Street, destroyed the bearded devil bound to the perimeter, and recovered Lord Victoro's operational ledger from the stone table where he left it. Savra filed the petition the same afternoon. The Founders' Day deadline in **Cassalanter Villa** moved four days closer.
