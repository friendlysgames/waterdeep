# The Shrine on Aveen Street

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social, Exploration and Combat Event begins when a Priest Acolyte brings Savra Belabranta's note to a member at Renown 10 and 6th level. It ends in the Halls of Justice, when Savra's chaplain gives the party a holy symbol. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Hear Savra's Brief at the Halls of Justice and learn why the Order cannot go through the cellar door itself.
> - Question Orros, the chandler on Aveen Street, who leased his cellar to a charity and has not been below for fourteen months.
> - Enter the cellar with Orros's key, with a Halls fire inspection or through the alley door.
> - Fight or avoid the cellar watch and the Bearded Devil that guards the circle room.
> - Take Victoro Cassalanter's ledger and learn three independent ways what kind of book it is.
> - Break the circle, hand the ledger to Savra and watch a Watch clerk enter it in the register.
> - Receive a Siamorphe holy symbol from the Order's chaplain.
>
> Only Order of the Gauntlet members attend Savra's Brief and debrief. Their companions can come to the Halls, wait on the bench by the door-warden and help with everything else.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Aveen Street runs along the line between the Field Ward and the North Ward. Orros's chandlery stands on the Field Ward side, and its cellar is a back way into the Asmodean shrine described in the Cassalanters Factions Guide.
> - A service passage runs under the street from the cellar to the shrine house on the North Ward side. This Event keys the two rooms nearest the cellar and leaves the other six areas to the Guide.
> - The Aveen Street Almsgivers' Society is a charity with a name and no members. It leased the cellar and the passage from Orros two years ago, and a courier brings the rent every quarter.
> - The household posted the Bearded Devil after Gysheer Omfreys was taken in Twelvedog Court. The caretakers in the stair room are Cultist Fanatics in plain coats, and the circle summons two Imps every ninth day.
> - The ledger on the stone table is in Victoro Cassalanter's hand. It holds twelve dated entries, each with a date, a name or initials and a place, and it gives no terms and no reasons.
> - Nobody in this Event knows what the twelve entries add up to. Savra suspects, the chaplain doubts, and the best a check can say is that the book records a bargain in writing.
> - The twelfth entry is dated Flamerule 1, which is Founders' Day. Founders' Day does not move in this Event or in any later one.
> - Ennick Vause, a Watch clerk at the Halls, sells the day's petitions to a man who pays for such news on the family's behalf. When Savra enters the ledger, the Cassalanters hear of it within a day.
> - The carter calls at the cellar on the seventh day after the Brief and takes the ledger away in his empty crates. A party that arrives later finds the table bare and the strongbox holding a fair copy of the first eight entries.
> - Savra stays at the Halls. The Code Legal bars the Order from entering a place of worship without a magistrate's warrant, and a warrant needs a document that the Order does not have.

### The Brief

A Priest Acolyte in a plain grey surcoat brings the note to each qualifying member at Trollskull Manor on the morning after the member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level. Read the following aloud when the member unfolds it:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The note is folded once and sealed with grey wax pressed with a gauntlet, and the lines of writing inside slant to the left. It reads:
>
> > I have an address in the Field Ward and a reason I cannot walk through the door myself. Come to the east annex of the Halls of Justice at the third bell past noon tomorrow, and ask Brother Haldric for me. S.B.

A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** receives the same note from Brother Hallin Ostrel, signed "B.H.O." If **Shrine Address Recovered** is not marked, Savra says that she found the cellar herself in the Halls' lease rolls, and the Brief is otherwise the same. Read the following aloud when the members reach the annex:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The east annex of the Halls of Justice smells of lamp oil and damp wool, and petitioners wait along the wall with their papers. Brother Haldric Wence lifts a hand from the door-warden's bench and points down a short passage. Savra waits at the end of it beside a table, with a map of two wards pinned flat and a stool for each of you.
>
> > "Thank you for coming. I'll tell you plainly what I have, because it's less than I want. There's a cellar under a chandler's shop on Aveen Street, and I think the book I need is in it."

> [!social]**The Knight at the Map Table**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: the Order's knight in Waterdeep, a woman in her late twenties in a dark riding coat with a silver gauntlet pin at her collar.
>
> Savra is Friendly and exact. She pauses before every serious answer, says "I'll tell you plainly" and gives a single nod, but only to an honest answer. She swears rarely and at wrongdoing. She will not ask the party to break the law, and she will not pretend that the law leaves them only one road.
>
> Savra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The cellar, the lease and the chandler.
> - Why the Order cannot enter, and what the law allows a citizen.
> - What she wants from the cellar, and by when.
> - Gysheer Omfreys, and the name she gave under the spell.
> - Father Corwen Thale and the Halls' fire inspection.
>
> She will not discuss what the family worships, any bargain or pact, or anything she cannot prove.

> [!qna]**Why can't you go in?**
>
> Savra rests a finger on the Field Ward end of the map before she answers.
>
> > "The Code Legal protects any place of worship from entry without a magistrate's warrant. It doesn't ask whose god is worshipped. A warrant needs a document, and what I hold is one cultist's answers under a spell."

> [!qna]**Is it the Cassalanters?**
>
> > "Gysheer gave Victoro Cassalanter's name under a spell, and a spell isn't evidence. I'll tell you plainly that I think she told the truth. I can't prove it, so I won't say more than that."

> [!qna]**What do you want from the cellar?**
>
> > "I want a book or a letter in a hand I can name. It has to be found where a priest of Tyr can swear to it. Bring me the paper itself and not a description of it."

> [!qna]**When must it be done?**
>
> > "You have six days to get inside, because the carter calls on the seventh. I think the book leaves with his empty crates. Bring it to me within a day, so a lawyer can't say it sat in your pocket."

> [!qna]**How do we get in?**
>
> Savra taps three places on the map, one after another.
>
> > "Orros owns the cellar, and a landlord may open his own door. Father Corwen can lead the Halls' fire inspection of the tallow stores, and a priest's word counts. The alley door is housebreaking, and I'd rather you left it shut."

When the members agree, Savra gives them three vials of holy water in a cloth bag and tells them to ask Brother Haldric for Father Corwen on the morning they want the inspection. Companions on the bench hear nothing of this, and the members can tell them afterward.

### Aveen Street

Aveen Street is a short walk from the Halls and a long way from anywhere pleasant. Read the following aloud when the party enters the chandlery:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Aveen Street is a narrow cobbled lane between tanneries and tenements, and the Field Ward side smells of tallow and wet leather. Orros's chandlery has bunches of candles hanging in the window, and a bell rings over the door as you go in. A stooped man in a tallow-stained apron looks up from the counter and wipes his hands on a rag.
>
> > "Good day to you, and welcome. I've tapers and wax by the pound, if that's what you're after, or is it something else?"

> [!social]**The Nervous Chandler**
>
> Orros (Neutral, Human, he/him) :: a stooped, tallow-stained chandler of about sixty who owns the building and has never asked a tenant a question.
>
> Orros is Indifferent and anxious, and he apologizes when he has done nothing wrong. He charges the Almsgivers' Society 3 gp a month for the cellar and the passage, and he keeps a key to the shop stair in his till. He hears the alley door at dusk and tells himself it is the Society's people. He swears mildly if at all, and he will not look at an armed stranger for long.
>
> Orros is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The lease, the rent and the courier.
> - The stair behind his counter and the door in the alley.
> - The Halls' fire inspection, which is due.
>
> He will not discuss his debts, and he knows nothing about the Society's people.

> [!qna]**The cellar?**
>
> > "I let it two years ago to a charity, the Aveen Street Almsgivers' Society. They paid a year in advance, and a courier brings the rent every quarter. I haven't been down in fourteen months, and I never asked what they do there. I do hope that's all right."

> [!qna]**The Society?**
>
> Orros glances at the door before he answers.
>
> > "I've never met a soul from it. The courier wears a good coat, and the lease is signed with a name I can't read. They come and go by the alley door at dusk, I think. Is something wrong?"

> [!exploration]**Three Ways to the Cellar**
>
> Any one of these brings the party to the foot of the cellar stair, and the party can combine them. A failed check costs time and never ends the search.
>
> - **Orros's key:** A character who asks Orros for the key and makes a successful **DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion)** check gets it, with his promise that nobody will be hurt in his building. On a failure he asks them to come back after closing, and then he hands it over. The party goes down the shop stair after dark, and Orros stays upstairs, unalarmed.
> - **The fire inspection:** Father Corwen Thale leads the Halls' inspection of the tallow stores at the third bell past noon on the day the party asks, with a clerk and the party as his lay assistants. No check is needed, and the party goes down the shop stair with a priest of Tyr at its head. **The Stair Room** below explains how the caretaker takes it.
> - **The alley door:** A character who picks the lock makes a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** check, or forces the door with a successful **DC 15 Strength** check. The noise carries up to the shop, Orros is alarmed and the watchers below are ready, so use the Hard roster.

> [!exploration]**Kelso's Debt**
>
> If **Kelso's Debt** is marked and **Kelso's Debt Spent** is not, any Shard Shunner in the Field Ward will spend the favour here with a day's notice. The party chooses one of the three uses.
>
> - **Location:** Kelso gives the hours the Society's people keep. They change shifts at dusk and at midnight, and the stair room stands empty for a quarter of an hour between them. A party that goes down in that gap meets no Cultist Fanatic in the stair room.
> - **Distraction:** Two Shunners start a brawl in front of the alley door at dusk, and the caretaker climbs the alley stair to look. The stair room is empty for ten minutes, and the Imp in the circle room leaves its perch to watch the fight.
> - **Silence:** No Shunner and no listener the Shunners pay says a word to anyone about the party or about Orros for a tenday.

### The Stair Room

The cellar stair is a steep run of stone steps behind Orros's storeroom, and the alley door opens onto a short passage that meets it at the bottom. Read the following aloud when the party reaches the foot of the stair:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The cellar stair drops into cold air that smells of tallow and incense, and a lantern hangs at the bottom. The stair room is low and square, with sacks of iron filings along one wall. A shut door stands in the far wall, and a man in a carter's coat rises from the bench beside it.

The caretaker is a Cultist Fanatic, and he reacts to how the party arrived.

- **By the key, after dark:** A character who leads the descent and makes a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check brings the party down unheard. The caretaker is surprised and rolls Initiative with Disadvantage. On a failure he is ready, and the fight begins at once.
- **By the inspection:** The caretaker sees a priest of Tyr, a clerk and a lawful reason, and he does not fight. He says the cellar holds the Society's stores and that the far door is the vestry, which he has no key for.
- **By the alley door:** He is waiting with a knife, and the fight begins at once.

> [!exploration]**The Inspection**
>
> The far door is locked, and the inspection needs one more step to pass it. The party can combine these, and each works alone.
>
> - **The lock:** A character who picks it while Father Corwen keeps the caretaker talking makes a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** check. Father Corwen asks to count the tallow sacks, which takes ten minutes.
> - **The caretaker:** A character who persuades him that the Halls will close the cellar unless the vestry is opened for the priest makes a successful **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Deception)** check. He opens the door himself and stays in the doorway.
> - **On a failure:** The caretaker plants himself in front of the far door and tells the inspection to leave. A second failure, or any drawn weapon, starts the fight.

> [!hazard]**The Cellar Watch**
>
> The caretakers use the **Cultist Fanatic** block, the guardian uses the **Bearded Devil** block and the sentry uses the **Imp** block. Use the roster for the party's size, companions included. Savra stays at the Halls and Father Corwen stays at the top of the stair, so neither is counted.
>
> - **Three characters, Standard:** one Cultist Fanatic in the stair room and the Bearded Devil in the circle room. This is a Bruising fight with a Day Cost of 4.
> - **Four characters, Standard:** one Cultist Fanatic in the stair room, and the Bearded Devil and an Imp in the circle room. This is a Bruising fight with a Day Cost of 4.
> - **Five characters, Standard:** two Cultist Fanatics in the stair room, and the Bearded Devil and an Imp in the circle room. This is a Bruising fight with a Day Cost of 4.
> - **Three characters, Hard:** one Cultist Fanatic in the stair room, and the Bearded Devil and an Imp in the circle room. This is a Bloody fight with a Day Cost of 6.
> - **Four characters, Hard:** two Cultist Fanatics in the stair room, and the Bearded Devil and an Imp in the circle room. This is a Bloody fight with a Day Cost of 6.
> - **Five characters, Hard:** three Cultist Fanatics in the stair room, and the Bearded Devil and an Imp in the circle room. This is a Bloody fight with a Day Cost of 6.
>
> Use the Hard roster when the party forced the alley door, when Orros was alarmed or when the Cassalanters are at Alert or Lockdown. If the party shuts the door between the rooms and the noise does not carry, the rooms are separate fights. The Day Cost is then 4 at any size on Standard and at four and five characters on Hard, and 6 at three characters on Hard. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits the rosters.
>
> #### The Caretakers' Tactics
> To begin, a caretaker meets the nearest armed character with the Pact Blade and calls "Hold the stair!" Any others split their attacks across different targets.
>
> During combat, each caretaker:
> - Casts Spiritual Weapon as a bonus action on the first round, and uses Command to order "Halt" or "Drop" at a character who runs for the far door.
> - Saves Hold Person for a character the others cannot reach in a turn. Casting it is the caretaker's action, so no Pact Blade attack follows that turn, and a held character is not attacked before the caretaker's next turn.
> - Never attacks a creature at 0 hit points, and swears loudly at anyone who does.
> - Yields at 22 hit points or fewer, or when a character grapples him, and drops the blade.
>
> The fight in the stair room ends when every caretaker has yielded or fallen. The caretakers know nothing about the ledger beyond "the master's book", and a yielded caretaker will say that much and nothing more.

### The Circle Room

Read the following aloud when the far door opens:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A round chamber opens beyond the door, and red candles burn in iron stands around its walls. A ring of iron filings circles the middle of the floor, and a lean red devil with a ropy beard stands motionless inside it. A stone table stands against the far wall with a thick book open on it, and a strongbox sits beside it.

The Bearded Devil is bound to the ring. It wakes when a creature lifts the ledger from the table, steps on the filings or breaks the circle, and nothing else disturbs it. A character who walks to the table wakes nothing. The table stands five feet outside the ring, so the devil reaches anyone standing at it once it wakes. The Imp perches on a rafter above the table, invisible. A character who makes a successful **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)** check notices the candle flames lean toward the rafter, and a *Detect Evil and Good* spell finds it without a check.

> [!hazard]**The Bound Devil**
>
> The roster is the one in **The Cellar Watch**.
>
> #### The Devil's Tactics
> To begin, the Bearded Devil stands in the middle of the ring and waits. Once it wakes, it makes its Beard attack and its Infernal Glaive attack against two different characters whenever two are in reach.
>
> During combat, the Bearded Devil:
> - Never crosses the line of filings, and fights to 0 hit points. A character who stays more than 10 feet from the ring cannot be attacked, and the devil does not follow anyone who leaves the room.
> - Leaves a creature at 0 hit points alone.
> - Poisons with the Beard, so a poisoned character cannot regain hit points until the start of the devil's next turn. Its Glaive can leave an infernal wound, which a character stops with a **DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine)** check as an action.
>
> #### The Imp's Tactics
> To begin, the Imp casts Invisibility on itself and stings a character standing at the table. During combat it:
> - Flies above the reach of the party's melee fighters and stings the character with the fewest hit points nearby.
> - Flees up the passage to report when it is reduced to 10 hit points or fewer, or when the devil falls. If it escapes, mark **Cassalanters Alerted** at once.
>
> The Imp does not fight a party it cannot see coming, and a character who casts *Detect Evil and Good* or kills it before the devil wakes stops its report.

> [!exploration]**Taking the Ledger**
>
> Three independent ways remove the ledger. Any one of them works, and a failed check never costs the mission.
>
> - **The swap:** Orros keeps a spare stock ledger the same size in his shop. A character who lays it on the table as the real one comes off and makes a successful **DC 14 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check lifts the book without waking the devil. On a failure the devil wakes, and the fight begins with the character in its reach.
> - **The fight:** The party wakes the devil on purpose, defeats it as described in **The Bound Devil**, and takes the ledger from the table.
> - **The strongbox:** A character who opens it makes a successful **DC 16 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** check, or forces it with a successful **DC 18 Strength** check. Inside is the fair copy of entries one to eight, in the same hand. It serves as the ledger if the carter has already taken the book.
>
> A party that wants to pursue the rest of the shrine can follow the passage under the street. The remaining areas are in the Cassalanters Factions Guide, and the Event does not require them.

> [!exploration]**Breaking the Circle**
>
> A character who sprinkles a vial of holy water along the line of filings and scrubs for ten minutes breaks the circle without a check. Without holy water, a character makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Religion)** check. On a failure the circle holds, and it summons two Imps on the ninth day.
>
> - **Breaking it with the devil inside:** The Bearded Devil is freed, leaves the ring and hunts the party up the stair. Break the circle only after the devil has fallen.
> - **After the devil has fallen:** The party burns the candles and the rite goods in the iron stands, and mark **Shrine Destroyed** when the circle is broken and the goods are burned.

### The Ledger

The ledger is a heavy book in good leather, ruled in three columns for a date, a name and a place. Read the following aloud when a character opens the cover:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The inside of the cover carries a household seal pressed in red wax, and a line in a flowing hand beneath it reads *V. Cassalanter*. The pages are ruled in three columns, and only the first twelve lines are filled. The last line holds a date and nothing else.

> [!exploration]**The Twelve Entries**
>
> The entries run from the book's first page, and years are written at the head of each page.
>
> - **One:** 4 Alturiak, 1483 DR. H.S., a salt warehouse on the Dock Ward quays.
> - **Two:** 19 Ches, 1484 DR. "The Tethyrian", a grain loft in the Trades Ward.
> - **Three:** 2 Mirtul, 1485 DR. W.C., a house on Aveen Street.
> - **Four:** 28 Kythorn, 1486 DR. M.A., a chapel yard in the Castle Ward.
> - **Five:** 11 Eleint, 1487 DR. H.S., the Dock Ward warehouse again.
> - **Six:** 6 Hammer, 1488 DR. Gysheer Omfreys, the cellar under a chandlery on Aveen Street.
> - **Seven:** 23 Tarsakh, 1489 DR. A woman in grey, a tannery in the Southern Ward.
> - **Eight:** 14 Flamerule, 1489 DR. H.S., the salt warehouse a third time.
> - **Nine:** 3 Uktar, 1489 DR. M.A., the chapel yard again.
> - **Ten:** Last winter. Gysheer Omfreys, a room above a dyer's shop on Crispin Street.
> - **Eleven:** A few tendays ago. Gysheer Omfreys, Twelvedog Court.
> - **Twelve:** 1 Flamerule of this year, with no name and no place.

> [!exploration]**Three Ways to Read the Book**
>
> The party needs to learn what kind of book this is, and three independent ways each tell part of it. Any one is enough for Savra to file, and a failed check costs an hour and never closes the others.
>
> - **The layout:** A character who studies the columns and makes a successful **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion)** check recognizes the format of a written bargain, with each line a duty done on a date. The book names no terms, no other party and no consequence, and the character learns nothing more.
> - **Father Corwen:** He reads the book at the Halls in one evening and needs no check. He says what the layout check says, and adds that the hand is steady and the entries were written one at a time over years.
> - **The Halls' records:** A character who spends an afternoon in the record rooms and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check finds that three of the places and two of the names appear in Watch complaints that were filed and closed without findings.
>
> Savra compares the hand with Victoro Cassalanter's signature on a letter in the Halls' files and is certain of it. A character who holds the sealed note from **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court** can make the same comparison and finds the same hand.

### Renown Opportunities

Savra expects the members in the east annex within a day of the ledger leaving the cellar. Read or paraphrase the following when a member hands it over in time, and mark **Ledger Recovered**:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra takes the ledger in both hands and turns to the last page before she says anything. She reads the final line twice and then closes the book and sets it flat on the table.
>
> > "Thank you. I compared the hand with his signature, and it's his. I don't know what these twelve lines add up to, and I won't guess in front of a magistrate. The last line is a bare date, and the date is Founders' Day. I noticed that, and I won't build on it yet."

If the members hand it over more than a day after taking it, read or paraphrase the following instead, and mark **Ledger Recovered**:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra takes the ledger and checks the spine and the seal before she opens it. She does not smile.
>
> > "You took your time, and a lawyer will make something of that. I'll file it anyway. The hand is his, and the last line is Founders' Day. I'll tell you plainly that I'd rather have had it yesterday."

If the party recovered only the fair copy from the strongbox, read or paraphrase the following instead, and mark **Ledger Recovered**:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra counts the filled lines with a finger and frowns at the eighth.
>
> > "Eight entries, and the book is gone. It's enough to file on, but it's thinner than I wanted. Thank you for bringing it."

If the party keeps the ledger, mark **Ledger Recovered** and read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra looks at the member who spoke and does not look at the book. She is quiet for a moment before she nods once.
>
> > "That's your right, and I'll tell you plainly I wish you had made another choice. I can't petition on a book I haven't seen. Tell me when you change your mind."

If the party took nothing from the cellar, Savra thanks the members and says she will try the lease rolls again. Mark nothing.

The filing is the next scene whenever the ledger is in Savra's hands. Read the following aloud as Savra walks the members to the clerk's window in the main hall:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The clerk's window is at the end of a long counter in the main hall, where petitioners wait beneath tall arched windows. Ennick Vause, a heavyset clerk with ink on both cuffs, takes the ledger and turns it to face his register. He copies the first line, stamps the page with a wet seal and writes a number in the margin.
>
> > "Entered as an exhibit to the petition of the Order of the Gauntlet, on the matter of the Aveen Street cellar. You'll have the number on a slip by tomorrow, Knight Belabranta."

Mark **Cassalanter Register Filed** when the clerk stamps the page.

> [!gamemaster]**The Clerk's Slip**
>
> After the entry, Ennick Vause copies the petition's title onto a slip and puts it under his blotter, to be sold that evening. A character who watches him and makes a successful **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)** check sees the slip go under the blotter.
>
> - **If the party tells Savra before the entry is stamped:** She orders the register sealed for a tenday and takes the clerk's slip herself. The filing does not alert the Cassalanters.
> - **If nobody notices, or the party says nothing:** The Cassalanters hear of the filing within a day. Mark **Cassalanters Alerted**, which moves the Cassalanters one tier along Unaware, Suspicious, Alert and Lockdown, and leaves them at Lockdown if they are already there.

Savra takes the members through a side door into the chapel, where the last scene of the Event takes place:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The chapel is small and whitewashed, with a lamp burning before a plain stone altar. A heavy woman in a grey tabard stands beside it with a leather cord looped over her fingers. A silver gauntlet is stitched on her breast, and a brass sun with eight spokes hangs from the cord.
>
> > "Savra asked me for this a tenday ago, before she had the book. It's the sun of Siamorphe, the Lady of Nobility, and I've consecrated it. I can't tell you what it will do. I'd carry it into any noble house you mean to enter."

> [!social]**The Order's Chaplain**
>
> Chaplain Ysolt Brannock (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: the Order's chaplain at the Halls of Justice, a heavy, calm woman in her fifties who consecrates the Order's holy symbols.
>
> Ysolt is Friendly and unhurried, and she holds the symbol out in both hands until someone takes it. She blesses people she has just met, and she does not claim to know more than she does.
>
> Ysolt is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Siamorphe, and why the Order keeps a symbol of her.
> - What the symbol does, which she cannot say.
> - Savra, and the cult she left.
>
> She will not discuss what the family worships or any pact.

> [!qna]**Why Siamorphe?**
>
> > "The oldest noble houses in this city once kept her altar, and Savra asked me for her sun. I don't know what it will mean to the Cassalanters, or whether it will mean anything. If it does nothing, you have lost nothing."

The chaplain gives one holy symbol to the party, and the members choose who carries it. A Siamorphe holy symbol received here counts for **Cassalanter Villa**, which reads it from the party's possessions.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 4 base Renown for reporting to Savra at the debrief. Companions gain none, and each bonus can be earned only once.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the Bearded Devil is destroyed with no character reduced to 0 hit points, or the ledger leaves the cellar without ever waking it.
> - **+1 Renown:** the ledger reaches Savra within 24 hours of leaving the cellar, and **Ledger Recovered** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** Orros is neither alarmed nor harmed, and he is not told what lay in his cellar.
>
> The standing Earning Renown actions for evidence delivered to Savra and for a devil of Challenge Rating 3 or higher pay once each as the Factions Guide lists them. They are not added to the bonuses above.

### Aftermath

Savra's petition goes before a magistrate within a tenday, and the magistrate asks the Order for more. The filing makes the Cassalanters a name in the Halls' register and nothing more, because no magistrate will move against a house on twelve lines of a bargain's tally.

- **The ledger:** It stays in the Halls' vault under Savra's seal. If the party kept it, Savra's file has the cellar's address and the lease and no exhibit.
- **The shrine:** If the circle is broken, the Cassalanters lose the Imps it summoned, and the household notices the loss within a tenday. If it stands, the Imps come on the ninth day.
- **The Cassalanters:** If **Cassalanters Alerted** is marked, the household behaves more carefully, and the party feels the difference at the villa. Founders' Day does not move, and the household's plans keep to the same date.
- **Orros:** He sells the cellar lease back to a stranger and says nothing, unless the party told him the truth. In that case he leaves Waterdeep for his sister's house in Daggerford, and the chandlery stays shut for a tenday.
