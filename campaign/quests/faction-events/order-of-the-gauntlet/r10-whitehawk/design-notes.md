# Design Notes: Whitehawk

## What the Source Gave

The earlier draft gave Whitehawk as speeches with no counters. Surveillance had no limits, the potions had no definition of a main operation, and the item was left to the GM from a list that included two Rare items. The temple spells had no list, and the dossier was both a suspicion file and a promise of "Advantage on orientation". Each benefit is now a per-member procedure with a contact, a delay, a limit, a refusal list and a loss rule.

## What Changed

The item is a fixed table of four Uncommon items, read from the data. Ring of Protection and Bracers of Defense are Rare and are gone, and no generic +1 entry exists to offer. The temple list is the twelve spells of 2nd level or lower from the mechanics reference, with *Speak with Dead* left out because it is 3rd level. Surveillance reports have three fixed parts by target type, so no result is left to the GM. Savra's help at the lairs is now a table in this event, which gives the four structure documents a source. The dossier follows the 2024 ladder: Whitehawk lands at the debrief of **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**, before **The Shrine on Aveen Street**, so Savra says "the family" and the addendum holds only what the party has already found.

## Departures

The donation records and the Watch complaints both start in 1480 DR, which makes the twelve years agree with the span. The earlier draft began the complaints in 1479 and had Savra build the file alone. It is now a Knight's file, carried on by Savra for months. The Siamorphe symbol is given at the end of **The Shrine on Aveen Street** and appears here only for parties that reach the villa without it. The scouts refuse the Aveen Street shop until the Order's petition exists, so the benefit cannot replace that mission.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Neris Tavell and Perrin Shaldrake (the Scouts) and Sir Ardin Vellacott (the Kolat Towers Knight). Each was grep-checked against `campaign/` with no collision, though "Corin Bell" exists in the Harper r50 and was avoided.
- The main-operation definition for each quest, the whistle signal at Xanathar's Lair and the ten-minute holds are invented. The five structure documents should cite the Marcheon ally and these rows, and add no further Savra perks.
- The Siamorphe symbol row, the Kolat Towers lay-on-hands line and the City of the Dead gate rely on `arc-g`, `arc-i` and `arc-j`, which are unconverted. The Knight block has no lay-on-hands feature, so the 25 Hit Points are stated as Ardin's own act.
- The villa has no defined orientation check yet. The fallback gives the party the six room positions.
- The Torm chapel, the door-warden and the clerk come from the First Meeting and the tithe event. The Dragon Heist Major Temples table puts the Halls of Justice, Tyr's temple, in the Castle Ward. The retired `waterdeep-lore.md:36` once put it in the Sea Ward, and that tension is resolved.
