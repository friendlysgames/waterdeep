# Whitehawk

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member first reaches Renown 10, which usually happens at the debrief of **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**. Bonus Renown from earlier missions can bring a member to 10 sooner, and the Event runs the same way when it does. Savra Belabranta names the rank at the Halls of Justice and gives that member the six Whitehawk benefits. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Name one target for two Order Scouts to watch for three days, once in each quest.
> - Collect a *Potion of Healing* and a *Potion of Greater Healing* before each quest's main operation.
> - Choose one Uncommon item from a table of four, which the member keeps.
> - Ask the temples of the Triad to cast a spell of 2nd level or lower, with components under 100 gp covered.
> - Take the Order's Cassalanter dossier, a suspicion file that proves nothing and shows what is hidden.
> - Learn what Savra does at the four lairs once the party reaches them.
>
> Only the member is addressed. Companions who are not Order of the Gauntlet members wait outside and gain nothing, though the dossier is a party document that they may read.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The dossier is a suspicion file. A senior Knight of the Waterdeep chapter opened it years ago, and Savra has carried it for months. Nothing in it names a crime, a pact or a faith.
> - Savra does not know what the family worships, and the dossier cannot tell her. She says "the family" and "the house" throughout. If a player names a Cassalanter at the table, she uses that name from then on and adds that the file holds no proof.
> - The servant's account is real. The servant left the household and the Order never found her, so the account stays unsworn and ends mid-sentence.
> - The Scouts watch and report. They do not enter private buildings, follow a target below the ground or fight unless attacked.
> - Brother Haldric Wence, the door-warden at the Halls of Justice, keeps the Whitehawk list. Brother Hallin Ostrel, the Order's clerk, writes the receipts for the potions and the item.
> - Nobody in this Event names the master of the Splinter or the carnival fleet's owner. Savra says "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener, and she says "the carnival fleet" until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked.

### The Note at Noon

Hold this Event until **Marcheon Reached** is marked for the member. At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 10, a Priest Acolyte in a plain grey surcoat finds the member at their lodging and hands over Savra's note. The acolyte waits for no reply. The meeting is in the east annex of the Halls of Justice at the third bell past noon on the same day.

Every qualifying member is invited on their own account, so several members who cross the threshold together attend together and are recorded one by one. A member who is away from Waterdeep finds the acolyte at their first surface lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the following day. A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** gets the same note in Brother Hallin's hand, signed "B.H.O.", and the meeting runs unchanged.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A young man in a plain grey surcoat knocks at the door a little after noon and holds out a paper folded once. The seal is the Halls of Justice, and he walks off down the lane before you have opened it. The slanting handwriting inside is short.
>
> > Third bell past noon, east annex, the Halls of Justice. You have reached Whitehawk, and there are six things to give you. Come alone. S.B.

### Naming the Rank

> [!readaloud]
>
> The east annex of the Halls of Justice smells of lamp oil and wet wool, and petitioners wait along the wall with their papers. Brother Haldric lifts a hand from the door-warden's bench and points down a short passage. Savra stands at the end of it beside a long table, with two leather cases and a ledger laid out in a row.
>
> > "Whitehawk. You've earned more than I can give you in one afternoon, so I'll go through it in order. Stop me if you want a thing said again."

> [!social]**The Knight of the Gauntlet**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: a knight in her late twenties with her dark hair worn back, a strong jaw, a dark riding coat and a silver gauntlet pin at her collar.
>
> Savra stands for the whole meeting and states the hard fact before the reason. She pauses before every serious answer and gives a single measured nod only to answers she believes are honest. She calls Tyr "the Maimed God" and swears rarely, in short bursts aimed at wrongdoing.
>
> Savra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Whitehawk benefit gives the member and what limits it.
> - What the dossier holds and what it does not.
> - What she will do at the lairs, and what she will not.
>
> She will not discuss what the family worships, the Stone of Golorr or the vault. She answers each by saying she does not know enough and will not guess.

> [!qna]**What does Whitehawk give me?**
>
> Savra touches the ledger, then each case, as she names the benefits.
>
> > "Watchers for three days, potions before the big night, an item to keep, the temples' spells, a file on the family, and my help at the lairs. I'll tell you plainly what each one costs you, which is nothing but honesty."

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not the party's?**
>
> > "The Order writes its ranks against names, and I've written yours. Your companions can read the file and drink the potions you give them. They can't ask the Scouts for anything or send a note to the temples."

> [!qna]**What happens if my Renown falls?**
>
> > "The rank stays with you, and so does the item and the file. The Scouts, the potions, the temples and my help at the lairs wait until your Renown is back at ten."

> [!qna]**How do I reach you?**
>
> > "Leave a message with Brother Haldric at the door and say it is for me. He holds it on a shelf, and I read the shelf each evening. Do not send it by anyone else."

### Surveillance

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra opens the ledger to a page ruled in columns and turns it so you can read the headings. Two names are written across the top in a neat hand, and she rests one finger beneath them.
>
> > "These are Neris Tavell and Perrin Shaldrake, who watch for the Order. They are good at it, and they will not go anywhere a magistrate would not let me follow."

> [!exploration]**Posting the Scouts**
>
> - **Contact:** the member gives Brother Haldric a target and a reason, or tells Savra at the east annex. Savra approves or refuses within a day, and she gives her reason for a refusal.
> - **Delay:** Neris and Perrin, two Order **Scouts**, begin at dawn on the day after approval. They watch for three days, and an acolyte brings the written report to the member's lodging at noon on the fourth day.
> - **Limit:** one target per member in each quest. A quest is one of the named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**, and the request counts against the quest the party is in when the member asks. An unused watch is lost when the quest ends.
> - **A target is** one named person, one building or one street. The target must be on the surface of Waterdeep and must not be an Order member.
> - **Savra's offer:** a member who names no target is offered the gate and public ground floor of the family's house, which she has watched herself. A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** is not offered it and may still ask for any target in writing.
> - **What the Scouts refuse:** to enter a private building, to follow a target below the ground or out of the city, or to fight unless they are attacked. They also refuse a named Cassalanter family member until **Cassalanter Villa** has been played, and the Aveen Street shop until the Order's petition exists. Savra gives the second reason as "a team on that street could spoil what I mean to file".
> - **Who else:** the member only. A request made in the member's name by a companion is ignored.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who names a target to settle a private quarrel loses the benefit for a tenday. Savra hears of it within a day.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and the Scouts wait until the Renown is restored.

> [!exploration]**What the Report Holds**
>
> Every report has three parts, and the third is the oddity that fits the target. The Scouts write in plain lines and give descriptions, never names they have not seen written down.
>
> - **A person:** where the person slept on each of the three nights, the three places visited most, and the people met, each described by dress and manner. The oddity is that the person changed route on the second day, as if they suspected a follower.
> - **A building:** the hours of arrival and departure on each of the three days, a count of the visitors with one description of each kind, and the deliveries. The oddity is one visitor or delivery that does not match the building's trade.
> - **A street:** the carts, carriages and walkers at dawn, noon and dusk, with the commonest faces. The oddity is one cart that uses the street twice at the same hour on separate days.
> - **The family's house:** visitors, deliveries and a carriage after dark, and nothing from inside. The oddity is that the carriage leaves by the garden gate and returns before dawn.

> [!qna]**Can they follow someone inside?**
>
> > "No. They stand where a passer-by could stand, and they leave when they would be noticed. If you want a door opened, that is a different kind of help, and it belongs to someone else."

### Potions

> [!exploration]**Potions from the Infirmary**
>
> - **Contact:** the member sends word to Brother Haldric at least one day before the quest's main operation. Brother Hallin hands over the potions at the door-warden's bench, writes the receipt and asks for nothing in return.
> - **What the member gets:** one *Potion of Healing* and one *Potion of Greater Healing* in each quest. The member may drink them or give them to a companion.
> - **The main operation** is the night the party goes in. It is the Operation scene of **Xanathar's Lair**, **Cassalanter Villa**, **Sea Maidens Faire** and **Kolat Towers**, the first outpost entered in **Faction Outposts**, and the descent into the Brandath Crypts in **Vault of Dragons**.
> - **Limit:** one pair per member in each quest, counted against the quest the party is in when the member asks. A potion that is lost or stolen is not replaced.
> - **Who else:** the member only. Brother Hallin refuses a request that a companion makes in the member's name.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and the infirmary withholds the potions until the Renown is restored.

### The Item

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra opens the second case and sets four things on the table in a row, a small shield, a folded cloak, a brooch and a thin silver rod. None of them is polished, and the cloak has a mended hem. She waits until you have looked at each one.
>
> > "The Order took these from the dead and from the damned, and it keeps them for the living. Take one. The armory won't miss it, and I won't ask you to give it back."

> [!exploration]**The Four Items**
>
> The member chooses one item at the table and keeps it permanently. Brother Hallin writes the choice in the ledger, and no second item is given. A lost item is not replaced.
>
> - **Sentinel Shield (Uncommon):** while the member holds it, the member has Advantage on Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks, and it gives +2 AC as a Shield. It suits a member who is trained with shields.
> - **Cloak of Protection (Uncommon, requires attunement):** the member gains a +1 bonus to Armor Class and saving throws while wearing it.
> - **Brooch of Shielding (Uncommon, requires attunement):** the member has Resistance to Force damage and is immune to damage from the *Magic Missile* spell.
> - **Periapt of Wound Closure (Uncommon, requires attunement):** whenever the member makes a Death Saving Throw, a roll of 9 or lower becomes a 10. Hit Dice rolled to regain Hit Points restore double.

> [!qna]**Which one would you take?**
>
> > "I don't choose for you. A shield for a knight who already carries one, the cloak for anyone who doesn't, the periapt for a healer who goes down too often, and the brooch for a caster."

> [!qna]**Why not rings or bracers?**
>
> > "Because those are Rare, and the armory has none to spare. These four are what we have, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise."

### Temple Spellcasting

> [!exploration]**The Triad's Spells**
>
> - **Contact:** the member gives their name at the front desk of the Halls of Justice, the Temple of Helm in the Castle Ward or the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield in the Dock Ward, and says that Savra Belabranta vouches for them. The door-warden, Brother Haldric, is the contact at the Halls.
> - **What is cast:** one spell from the list below, cast by the priest on duty, at the temple, between the second bell of morning and dusk. The temple covers every component worth less than 100 gp, and the priest lends a holy symbol for *Bless*.
> - **Level 1:** *Bless*, *Cure Wounds*, *Detect Evil and Good* and *Healing Word*.
> - **Level 2:** *Aid*, *Augury*, *Calm Emotions*, *Gentle Repose*, *Lesser Restoration*, *Locate Object*, *Prayer of Healing* and *Zone of Truth*.
> - **Limit:** one spell for each temple each day, so a member who visits all three may receive three. The spell can reach the member and as many companions as it allows, and it is never cast away from the temple.
> - **What the priests refuse:** a spell cast against a living person the member names. *Zone of Truth* is cast only in a temple chamber on creatures who agree to enter it, and the priest asks no questions. *Detect Evil and Good* is cast only at a place the member names, and never at the family's house or the Aveen Street shop.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who brings a fight into a temple, or shows contempt for the faith, is turned away there for a tenday.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and the temples decline the spells until the Renown is restored.

### The Dossier

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra takes a leather-bound sheaf from the third case and lays it on the table between you. It is tied with a grey cord, and the pages inside have been handled by several people over several years. She sets her hand flat on top and looks at you before she speaks.
>
> > "This is the family the Order has been watching. You've heard of them, and I'll say no more than the file does. It doesn't prove what I think it does. It proves that somebody works hard to keep a house closed."

> [!exploration]**The Cassalanter Dossier**
>
> The sheaf is a party document. The member keeps it, and companions may read it. It holds four items and, if the party gave the Order more, an addendum.
>
> - **The layout:** a partial plan of the family's house, copied from the public building registry. It shows the ground floor only, which is the entrance hall, the garden mudroom, the reading room, the dining room, the kitchen and the ballroom. The upper floors and the cellars appear on no record.
> - **The donations:** twelve years of charitable gifts, back to 1480 DR. Every recipient is a registered body, and none of the people named as recipients can be found.
> - **The complaints:** four Watch complaints filed between 1480 and 1492 DR. All four were closed without findings, and three show no record that anyone investigated.
> - **The servant's account:** a handwritten page from a former servant, who writes of screaming above the top floor on certain nights. It has no date, names no circumstances and ends mid-sentence.

> [!gamemaster]**The Addendum**
>
> Savra adds a page in her own hand for each outcome the party has marked. She writes nothing about a pact, its terms, a deadline or what the family worships, because she knows none of it.
>
> - **Imp Captured:** the Halls hold a raven imp taken on Trollskull Alley in a warded cell. It has named no sender, and the page records only that someone sent it.
> - **Shrine Address Recovered:** the Aveen Street address, as found. The page records how the party found it and nothing more.
> - **Intelligence Shared:** copies of Gysheer Omfreys's sealed note and the cartage receipt for crates delivered to Aveen Street.
> - **Ledger Recovered:** a copy of the ledger. Savra then says the file is no longer all she has, and the dossier no longer reads as nothing admissible.
>
> If the party has played **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**, Savra also asks the question below. If none of these outcomes is marked, the addendum is blank, and she says so.

> [!qna]**What do you make of Twelvedog Court?**
>
> > "Somebody paid for those devils, and somebody wanted them in that court. I can't tell you who from this file. I'll tell you plainly that it moves my suspicion from a rumour to a hunt."

> [!qna]**Where is the servant now?**
>
> > "I don't know. I looked, and the Order looked before me. Nobody saw her leave, and nobody has seen her since."

> [!qna]**Can we use it against them?**
>
> > "No. It wouldn't last a minute in front of a magistrate. Whatever you find that is more than this, get it out whole. I need something I can file."

> [!exploration]**Studying the Layout**
>
> A member who studies the plan for an hour at the Halls learns where the six ground-floor rooms sit and how their doors connect. The upper floors and cellars are not on the plan, and the plan cannot help with them.
>
> - **At the villa:** when the party first enters the ground floor in **Cassalanter Villa**, the first Intelligence (Investigation) check anyone makes to place the rooms is made with Advantage if a member has studied the plan.
> - **Fallback:** if that quest has no such check at that point, the party simply knows where the six rooms are.
> - **If Cassalanter Villa has already been played,** the plan is a keepsake and gives nothing.

### Savra at the Lairs

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra closes the ledger and rests her hand on its cover. She speaks quietly enough that Brother Haldric, at the far end of the passage, goes on writing without looking up.
>
> > "When you reach the lairs, I'll be nearby. Not inside, because a knight in a Guild cellar is a war, and I can't start one. I'll tell you plainly what I do at each."

> [!exploration]**Savra at the Four Lairs**
>
> - **Contact:** the member tells Savra, or leaves word with Brother Haldric, the day before the party goes in, and names the place and the hour. A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** must ask in writing, and Savra answers within a tenday.
> - **Limit:** once for each lair quest, to the party, on the word of one Whitehawk member. If she fights, that counts as the member's Marcheon operation for the quest.
> - **Xanathar's Lair:** Savra gives the member three *Potions of Healing* the day before, in addition to the member's own potions. She waits across from the guildsign entrance and draws off a pursuing patrol for ten minutes if the party signals retreat with two long whistle blasts. She fights if called, withdrawing at 20 Hit Points.
> - **Cassalanter Villa:** Savra gives the member a Siamorphe holy symbol, consecrated by the Order's chaplain, if the party has not yet received one and holds evidence from **Faction Outposts** or has **Cassalanter Register Filed** marked. If the party enters by the charity ball, Savra attends in Gauntlet formal dress over armor, and her presence excuses one incident without immediate social consequence.
> - **Kolat Towers:** Savra and Sir Ardin Vellacott, a Knight of the chapter, wait at the party's extraction point with two *Potions of Healing*. Ardin restores up to 25 Hit Points once to a creature the member chooses, by touch, as an action.
> - **Vault of Dragons:** Savra stands at the main gate of the City of the Dead. If the Watch arrives before the party is ready, she meets it at the gate and holds it for ten minutes by talking, and she does not go in.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 10 keeps the rank, and Savra's help waits until the Renown is restored.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Whitehawk member earns further Renown from missions and from the ordinary rewards listed on the Order of the Gauntlet page of the Factions Guide, at their own pace.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions everything Savra said and may let them read the dossier. The Scouts, the potions, the item, the temples and Savra's help at the lairs stay individual benefits. Brother Haldric, Brother Hallin and the priests refuse a request that a companion makes in the member's name.

A member who is later marked **Quietly Reassigned** keeps the rank, the item, the potions, the dossier and the temples. Savra stops offering the family's house as a target and stops volunteering her help at the lairs. The member must ask in writing for both.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard all six benefits, has chosen an item and holds the dossier.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Whitehawk Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Savra names the rank at the table; read by **Vindicator**, which checks it before naming the next rank. Track separately for each member the quest of the last watch, the quest of the last potions, the item chosen and the date of the last temple spell.
>
> This Event also reads **Marcheon Reached** (the hold) and **Quietly Reassigned**, which changes how the note arrives and what Savra offers. It reads **Imp Captured**, **Shrine Address Recovered**, **Intelligence Shared**, **Ledger Recovered** and **Cassalanter Register Filed** for the addendum and the Siamorphe symbol. It reads **Manshoon Named** and **Jarlaxle Unmasked** for the wording of the Splinter and the carnival fleet.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All six benefits are active for this member from the next morning. **The Shrine on Aveen Street** becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level, and Savra summons the member to the Halls by note. **Vindicator** occurs when this member reaches Renown 25. This Event awards no Renown and no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Savra Belabranta names an Order of the Gauntlet member a Whitehawk and explains the rank's watchers, potions, magic item, temple spells and help at the lairs. She also hands over the Order's file on the family she suspects.

## Summary

We were named Whitehawks of the Order of the Gauntlet, and Savra showed each of us what the rank gives. Two Scouts will watch a target for us once every quest, and the infirmary will give us potions before the big night. I took an item from the Order's table, and the temples will cast their lesser spells for us. Savra also gave us her file on the family in the Sea Ward, which proves nothing and shows that someone works hard to keep the house closed.
