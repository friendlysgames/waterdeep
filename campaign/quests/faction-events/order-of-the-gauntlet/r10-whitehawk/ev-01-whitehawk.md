# Whitehawk

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one party member's OotG Renown reaches 10. Savra initiates this meeting — she sends word within a tenday and selects the location herself: the Halls of Justice, the same antechamber where the party took the oath. In this Event:
>
> - Savra delivers the Whitehawk promotion and explains the expanded benefits without embellishment.
> - She presents an Uncommon magic item from the Order's armory: one piece from the Armor, Ring, or Weapon category, chosen for the character's role against fiends and undead.
> - She explains the surveillance mandate and the potion supply.
> - She shares the Order's Cassalanter dossier — years of compiled observation, nothing admissible. The dossier shows something is hidden, not what it is.
> - Temple spellcasters of the Triad will now cast spells of 2nd level or lower for the character at no cost.

**Background (DM only)**

The Cassalanter dossier is a suspicion file. Nothing in it confirms infernalism, devil-worship, or any criminal act. It shows: charitable donations over twelve years that cannot be traced to any identifiable cause; four Watch complaints against the family closed without findings; a partial ground-floor layout of the villa assembled from public records; and one informal servant's account, never sworn, about screaming from above the top floor on certain nights. The servant no longer works for the family and hasn't been locatable since. Savra built this file herself. She knows exactly how thin it looks, and she knows that whatever it points to has been hidden by people who are good at hiding things.

> > [!info]**The Cassalanter Dossier**
> >
> > A leather-bound sheaf of assembled documents. Contents:
> > - A partial villa ground-floor layout, drawn from public architectural records. The upper floors are not represented.
> > - Twelve years of charitable donation records. Every recipient organization is registered. None of the actual recipients can be found.
> > - Four Watch complaints filed against the family between 1479 and 1492 DR. All four closed before findings were produced; three with no record of any investigation having begun.
> > - A handwritten account from a former servant: persistent screaming from above the top floor on certain nights. No date. No circumstances named. The account ends mid-sentence.

### The Whitehawk Meeting

The antechamber where the party took the oath. The bench is empty; no priest is waiting. Savra stands when the character arrives, which is her way of acknowledging that the visit means something.

> > "Whitehawk. You've earned the resources that come with it."

She runs through what changes.

The Order will put surveillance on one named suspect or location per quest — a two-person team for three days, written report at the end. The character names the target; Savra approves it. Findings are delivered within three days.

Before each major operation, the Order provides a *potion of healing* and a *potion of greater healing* from the Halls of Justice infirmary. One day's notice required.

### The Item

Savra sets a case on the bench.

> > "The armory has more than borrowed swords."

Inside is an Uncommon magic item from the Armor, Ring, or Weapon category, selected for this character's role against fiends and undead. It is not borrowed. It is a grant.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Selecting the Item
>
> Choose one Uncommon magic item suited to how this character fights: *ring of protection*, *+1 armor* of an appropriate type, *sentinel shield*, *bracers of defense* (for an unarmored character), or a weapon appropriate to their fighting style that grants an edge against fiends or undead. The item should be something they'll carry into the field. Note it on the character's sheet; it's permanent.

### The Dossier

Savra opens a second case and sets a leather-bound sheaf of documents on the bench.

> > "The family the Order has been watching. You've heard the name by now."

She doesn't say it, even here. She doesn't need to.

> > "This is what twelve years of observation produces when whatever you're looking at has been carefully hidden. It isn't evidence of what I think it is. It's evidence that something is being concealed by people who know how to conceal things."

She sets a finger on the partial villa layout: ground floor only, assembled from public records.

> > "The upper floors aren't on any public record. The basement isn't either."

She moves to the financial documentation.

> > "Every one of these donations goes to a registered organization. None of the recipients can be found. I've checked all of them."

She sets the servant's account last.

> > "This was taken informally. I've tried to find the person who wrote it. I haven't been able to."

The account sits on the bench between them.

> > "Whatever you find inside that villa that's more than what's in this file — get it out intact. I need something admissible."

She slides the case to the character.

### What the Triad Offers Now

Temple spellcasters of Tyr, Helm, and Torm will cast spells of 2nd level or lower for the character at no cost. This covers preparatory magic, healing tied to Order operations, and divination relevant to active investigations. The character arranges it directly with the temple.

> > "You've made enough of an impression with the clergy that they want you to have the resource. Use it for the work."

### Concluding the Event

#### Whitehawk Reached: True / False

**True:** The character holds OotG Whitehawk rank and has received the Cassalanter dossier. The partial villa ground-floor layout in the dossier provides Advantage on the character's initial orientation check in **Cassalanter Villa**. The surveillance mandate, potion supply, magic item, and temple spellcasting are all immediately available. Read by the **Vindicator** rank event (r25-vindicator), which checks this flag before its opening scene fires.

## Overview

Savra promoted the character to Whitehawk and delivered the rank's resources: Order surveillance on one named target per quest, potions before major operations, an Uncommon magic item from the armory, and access to temple spellcasting at no cost. She also handed over the Order's Cassalanter dossier — twelve years of watching a Sea Ward family, nothing in it admissible, everything in it suggesting that something is being carefully hidden.
