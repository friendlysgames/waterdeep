# What Gysheer Carried

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Investigation and Social Event begins when Gysheer Omfreys is held or has escaped and Savra Belabranta leads the party out of Twelvedog Court. It ends at dusk on the next day, when the members report to Savra at the east annex of the Halls of Justice. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Put three questions to Gysheer at a hearing under *Zone of Truth*, if she was held.
> - Examine her coat in the Halls' property room and find a sealed note and a cartage receipt, if she was held.
> - Search her rented room above the dyer's shop on Crispin Street, which works whether she was held or escaped.
> - Hear why Savra cannot go to Aveen Street herself and what she asks of the party instead.
> - Report to Savra and receive her answer to the question she asked in **The Black Viper Investigation**.
>
> Only Order of the Gauntlet members attend Savra's debrief. Companions help with everything else in this Event and earn no Renown.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Gysheer's coat holds two papers. One is a note sealed with black wax and the initials "V.C.". The other is the customer copy of a carter's receipt for nine crates carried to a cellar door on Aveen Street nine days ago.
> - The crates held black tallow candles, rock salt, iron filings and lamp oil. Savra asked "What are they moving?" in **The Black Viper Investigation**, and this is the answer.
> - Gysheer's room holds the second copies of three receipts, a day-book with the Aveen Street address in full. It also holds a list of Field Ward courts in her hand. Twelvedog Court is third, and the next two are crossed out.
> - "V.C." fits Victoro Cassalanter, and Gysheer says his name if she is asked at the hearing. Nobody in this Event states what the family worships, and Savra does not guess.
> - Savra cannot act on a name spoken at a hearing, because the Halls bind a statement made under *Zone of Truth* to the one who made it. She needs a document in a hand a magistrate can read.
> - Brother Hallin Ostrel keeps the property room and writes down every item twice. If the members find nothing, he hands them the inventory at dusk, and the note and receipt are on it.

### Leaving the Court

Savra takes Gysheer to the Halls of Justice in Castle Ward with her wrists bound and says nothing on the walk. The members follow, and if Gysheer escaped, Savra walks them to the Halls and tells them what she wants done.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Halls of Justice rise above the Castle Ward streets west of the Market, a long pale building with the Maimed God's scales carved over the doors. A line of petitioners waits along the wall, and a heavyset man in a plain grey surcoat sits on a bench at the entrance. Savra speaks to him in a low voice, and he rises and holds the inner door open.
>
> > "I am Brother Haldric Wence, door-warden of the Halls, and Savra has the east annex ready for you. Brother Hallin is waiting at the property room with everything she carried."

### Three Ways to the Address

Three leads each give the address on Aveen Street, and any one is enough. A failed check costs the party an hour and never closes another lead, and the leads can be split between members and companions. The hearing and the property room need Gysheer in custody, and the Crispin Street room does not.

> [!gamemaster]**Which Leads Are Open**
>
> - **Gysheer held:** The hearing, the property room and the Crispin Street room are all open.
> - **Gysheer escaped:** Only the Crispin Street room is open. Savra says so, and Hallin has nothing to inventory.
> - **If the party already knows Aveen Street:** The address from **Faction Outposts** is not news. Savra asks what the party took from it, and the note and receipt still matter because they tie Gysheer's name to the place.

### The Hearing

Savra holds the hearing in a bare room off the east annex at the third bell past noon, and she asks the bench for a priest. Sister Ilvane Haste, a priest of Tyr with grey in her hair, reads from the Halls' scroll of *Zone of Truth*. A party cleric can cast the spell instead.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The hearing room has a plain table, three stools and a window barred against the street. Gysheer sits very straight with her bound hands in her lap, and she looks at Savra and at nobody else. Sister Ilvane unrolls a scroll and reads three lines in a flat voice. She sets the scroll down, and the air in the room feels still and close.
>
> > "The spell is on the room for ten minutes, and you may be silent but you may not lie. The bench will enter your silence in the record as contempt, and the magistrates read that record."
>
> Gysheer's mouth twists, and she answers without turning her head.
>
> > "I lie to no one, and I will answer what I choose to answer."

> [!social]**The Summoner Under the Spell**
>
> Gysheer Omfreys (Lawful Evil, Human, she/her) :: a Tethyrian woman of about forty in good dark clothes. She is proud and precise, and she has taught herself never to show fear.
>
> Gysheer is Hostile to Savra and merely cold to everyone else. She speaks in complete, formal sentences and does not swear in front of strangers. She waived her saving throw because she believes she has nothing to be ashamed of, and she means to prove it by refusing whatever she dislikes.
>
> Gysheer is willing to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Her own acts today, which she describes without apology.
> - Why she called the devils into the court.
> - Whom she works for, if a character frames the question in a way that flatters her loyalty or threatens her sentence.
>
> She will not discuss anyone else who does the work, and no spell can make her.

The bench allows the party three questions in the ten minutes. A character asks one question at a time and makes a **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation)** check, with Persuasion appealing to her pride and Intimidation to the contempt entry. On a success Gysheer answers. On a failure she says "I do not answer that" and the question is spent.

> [!qna]**Who set you to this work?**
>
> > "I work for Lord Victoro Cassalanter of the Sea Ward, who pays well and asks for results. I have always given them to him."

> [!qna]**What was the work for?**
>
> > "Preparation of ground, which meant keeping the Field Ward frightened and the Watch looking at it. What the ground is for was never mine to know."

> [!qna]**Where does it end?**
>
> > "It ends at the shrine on Aveen Street, in a cellar, where I carry what it needs and others make use of it."

> [!qna]**Why call devils into a court?**
>
> > "I called them to empty it and for nothing more, and they slipped my hold. I regret what happened in the court, and I do not regret the work."

> [!qna]**Who else does this work?**
>
> > "That is not mine to give, and the spell can keep me from lying but it cannot make me speak."

The fourth question has no check, and the fifth is a refusal that spends a question. The first three answers name the employer, the purpose and the place, and the third alone gives the address. A party that fails all three checks loses nothing else, because the coat and the room are still open. When the ten minutes end, Sister Ilvane enters the answers in the record, and Gysheer is led back to the cells.

### The Property Room

The property room is a low whitewashed room off the east annex with shelves of labeled boxes. Brother Hallin Ostrel lays Gysheer's belongings on a table and writes each item twice in a ledger, as he does for every prisoner. The party may examine them with his permission.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Brother Hallin sets a dark coat, a plain steel knife, a purse and a small iron pendant on the table in a neat row. He dips his pen and writes without looking up, and the back of the pendant is scratched with a tally of fourteen marks.
>
> > "Everything she carried, and I have written it down. If you want to look closer, please touch nothing until I have finished the line."

> [!exploration]**Gysheer's Coat**
>
> A character who examines the coat for an hour can make a **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check. On a success the character finds a seam in the lining that was sewn shut by hand, with two folded papers inside it.
>
> - **The note:** A scrap of paper sealed with a blob of black wax pressed with the letters "V.C.". It reads in a firm hand: *Third night. The ground is ready when the lamps burn through. Keep away from the house. V.C.* Savra insists it be opened in front of Hallin so the record shows who broke the seal.
> - **The receipt:** The customer copy of a carter's receipt from Garrowby Cartage in the Dock Ward. It records nine crates of black tallow candles, rock salt, iron filings and lamp oil, carried for the order of "V.C." to Aveen Street, cellar door, and received by the house's mark. It is dated nine days before the court.
> - **On a failure:** The search costs an hour. Hallin finishes his inventory at dusk, lists both papers by their wording, and hands them to the party if Savra allows it.
>
> The knife, the purse of 14 gp and the pendant tell the party nothing more. The pendant is a plain iron disc with no god's sign on it.

### Savra's Ruling

Savra speaks to the members in the east annex once Hallin has closed the ledger. She says the same thing whether the address came from the hearing, the coat or neither. Companions may listen, though she addresses only the members.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra stands by the window with her hands behind her back and the light across her jaw. She waits until the door is shut before she speaks.
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly where I stand. Devil worship is not a crime in Waterdeep, and I can charge Gysheer only for the court. A name she gave under the spell binds her and nobody else, so I can't go into a man's cellar on it."
>
> She turns from the window and looks at each of you in turn before she goes on.
>
> > "You aren't knights of the Order, so what you do with what you know is your own business, and I can't send you. Bring me paper, in a hand a magistrate can read, and I will know what to do with it."

> [!qna]**Why can't you go in yourself?**
>
> > "A knight of the Order who forces a door on a spoken name is a knight in front of the Lords. A private cellar needs a warrant or a witness who isn't me, and today I have neither."

> [!qna]**Who is V.C.?**
>
> > "Initials. I can think of a house those letters fit, and I won't say it on a guess. If a hearing gave you a name, I've written it down beside the words 'under the spell'."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us?**
>
> > "Everything she carried, and anything from her room. Don't go to Aveen Street before I've told you how, and if the Watch asks, you saw devils in a court and nothing else."

> [!qna]**What happens to Gysheer?**
>
> > "She goes before the bench for the court, and I expect a fine and a tenday in the cells. Then she's free, because I can't hold her longer, however much I'd like to."

### The Room on Crispin Street

If the party wants the room, it stands on Crispin Street, two blocks from Twelvedog Court. Savra cannot search a private room without a warrant, but a landlady can let anyone in, and she says so in a voice that carries no instruction.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Crispin Street smells of woad and wet wool, and the dyer's shop at the corner has blue-stained cloth hanging from poles across the front. A stair at the side climbs to a single door on the upper floor. A broad woman with blue hands looks up from a vat in the yard and wipes them on her apron.
>
> > "That's my lodger's room, yes, and she pays a quarter ahead and keeps to herself. If she's in trouble, I'd like to know before the rent falls due."

> [!social]**The Dyer**
>
> Jessa Marrick (Neutral, Human, she/her) :: a broad, blue-handed dyer in her fifties who lets the room above her shop. She wants her rent more than she wants any story.
>
> Jessa is Indifferent and practical. She talks while she works and does not ask what the party wants with the room until she has been paid. She has noticed that her lodger goes out at odd hours and that carters came twice with covered boxes.
>
> Jessa is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Her lodger, who pays a quarter ahead and gives her no trouble.
> - The carters who came twice with covered boxes and carried them up the stair.
> - Whether the rent is paid, which it is for another eleven days.
>
> She will not let anyone into the room without being paid, and she will not discuss the Watch.

> [!exploration]**Getting In**
>
> Three ways open the door, and the party needs only one.
>
> - **Persuasion:** A character who tells Jessa that her lodger is in the Halls' cells can make a **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check. On a success Jessa hands over the key at once, because she wants to protect her rent.
> - **Coin:** Any character who offers 5 gp, or pays eleven days' rent in advance, gets the key with no check.
> - **Kelso's Debt:** If **Kelso's Debt** is marked and **Kelso's Debt Spent** is not, the party can spend it on the Location use. Kelso names Gysheer as the lodger and gives the hours Jessa spends at her vats. The party walks into the room in those hours with no key and no check, and marks **Kelso's Debt Spent** as **The Shard Shunners** describes.
> - **On a failure:** Jessa says the rent is the only thing she cares about. A second offer of coin works without a check.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The room is small and neat, with a narrow bed, a washstand, a locked chest and a table under the window. A day-book lies open on the table beside a candle stub, and the shelf above it holds a row of black tallow candles still in their paper. The air smells of wax and old salt.

> [!exploration]**Searching the Room**
>
> The search takes four hours, and it finds everything below with no check. A character who searches with care for the first hour can make a **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check. On a success the character finds the day-book's address page in that hour and saves the party three.
>
> - **The day-book:** It records the Aveen Street address in full, "cellar door in the yard, three knocks", with dates of eight visits.
> - **The receipts:** Second copies of three carter's receipts from Garrowby Cartage, each for crates carried to Aveen Street in the past tenday.
> - **The list:** A list of Field Ward courts in Gysheer's hand. Twelvedog Court is third, with "noon" beside it, and the two below are crossed out. Savra reads it as work still to come.
> - **The pouch:** An empty coin pouch tagged "V.C." in ink. It names no one and proves nothing alone.

### The Report

The members report to Savra in the east annex at dusk on the day after the court. Brother Haldric Wence sends them in, and Hallin has the ledger open on his desk by the door, while the companions wait in the passage.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The east annex is quiet at dusk, with the last petitioners gone and lamps lit along the wall. Savra sits behind a small table with a notebook open and her coat over the chair. She looks up when you come in and waits until each of you has sat.
>
> > "Tell me what you found, in the order you found it. Leave nothing out because you think it makes the work look untidy."

If the members give Savra the sealed note and the receipt, along with any papers from the room, mark **Intelligence Shared** and read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra reads the note twice and then lays the receipt flat beside it and runs one finger down the list of goods.
>
> > "Nine crates of candles, salt, iron and oil, carried to a cellar door on Aveen Street. I asked you what they were moving, and now we know. That is what a place looks like when someone is keeping a rite going. Thank you for telling me everything unasked."

If the members report the address but keep the papers, mark **Shrine Address Recovered** only and read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra writes the address in her notebook in her left-slanting hand and looks at you without lowering her pen.
>
> > "That's Aveen Street, and you didn't bring the paper. The address alone is worth less to me than what you're holding. Bring it when you're ready, and I won't ask again."

If the members used the hearing, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> She writes a second line under the first and tilts the page toward the nearest member so that you can all read it.
>
> > "Victoro Cassalanter, named under the spell by a hired summoner, and it's in my book as that and nothing more. I'll leave it there until I have something a magistrate would read."

If the members did not use the hearing and the papers carry only initials, add the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> She picks up the note again and taps the seal with one finger while she looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "V.C. is a pair of letters that fit two people in a house I've watched for a long time. I won't write either name on initials, and I need the whole of it in someone's hand."

If **Imp Captured** is marked, add the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> She closes the notebook on her thumb and leans back in her chair. She looks at the lamp for a moment before she speaks.
>
> > "The raven you caught is still in the Halls' cells, and it still says nothing. Somebody who can pay for a devil's time watched your door, and somebody paid for a flight of them. I don't know if it's one hand or two."

If Gysheer escaped, say the following instead of the line about papers:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra puts down her pen and rubs her eyes with the heel of one hand, and the lamp throws her shadow long across the wall.
>
> > "She's gone, then, and the room is all we have, which is more than I expected. Thank you for going."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra closes the notebook, stands and gives you a single short nod. When she speaks again her voice is lower.
>
> > "A note will come from the Halls when the file is ready, and until then you will stay away from Aveen Street. I mean that."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Savra. Companions gain none, and each bonus can be earned only once.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** all three residents of Twelvedog Court come through unharmed.
> - **+1 Renown:** the members report the Aveen Street address from the coat or the room, and the hearing plays no part in finding it.
> - **+1 Renown:** the members give Savra Gysheer's papers in full, and **Intelligence Shared** is marked.

### Aftermath

The bench fines Gysheer for endangering a public court and holds her for a tenday. Nobody visits her, and the Halls' clerk records no surety. The Cassalanters' watchers in the Field Ward report that she was taken, and they do not know what she said.

Orna Fesk brings a loaf to the Halls for Savra the next morning, and Hallin accepts it for her. If the party took the hearing, Sister Ilvane's record sits in a drawer at the Halls and is not read again. Savra keeps the Aveen Street address in her notebook and tells no one else at the Halls.

### Concluding the Event

Savra's report ends the mission, and later events read what the party found, so record it now.

## Overview

Savra holds the summoner and the party follows her papers to a cellar on Aveen Street. Savra explains why she cannot act on a name and asks for paper.

## Summary

We took Gysheer to the Halls of Justice and put three questions to her under a spell of truth. Her coat held a sealed note and a carter's receipt for nine crates, and her room on Crispin Street held the address in her own hand. Savra could not go in on a name, so what would we find in that cellar on Aveen Street?
