# Design Notes: Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court

## One Fight and One Question

The source gives a fight in a Field Ward court, a cultist who nearly confesses, and Savra's refusal to act on a confession drawn out by magic. The mission keeps all three. The restored draft ended in a single event, and the fight, the hearing and the search all sat in one file. It now splits where the place and the clock change: the court at noon, then the Halls of Justice and Crispin Street over the next day.

The new piece is the answer to Savra's question from **The Black Viper Investigation**. "What are they moving?" is answered by a cartage receipt for nine crates of candles, salt, iron filings and lamp oil, carried to a cellar door on Aveen Street. It appears in Gysheer's coat and again in her room, so a party that loses her still reads it.

## Compulsion Without Command

The source uses *Charm Person* or *Command* to get three answers out of Gysheer. The 2024 *Command* is a one-word order and cannot extract a statement, and *Charm Person* has no fixed DC. The mission replaces both with a hearing under *Zone of Truth*, read from the Halls' scroll or cast by a party cleric. Gysheer waives her save out of pride, can stay silent, and answers a question only if a character wins the check. That gives the party a lawful route and a real choice, because the extra Renown goes to the party that finds the address on paper and leaves the hearing alone.

Savra's constraint is the legal point of the source, now stated as a rule of the Halls: a statement made under the spell binds the speaker and nobody else. She names no pact and no family beyond "a house those letters fit" and a name written in her book "under the spell". Nobody in either event says what the family worships.

## Rosters and the Sigil

Spined Devils number one per character on Standard and one more on Hard, with Savra counted as a Knight, and Gysheer arrives as a separate Mild wave. The source gave five devils at every size, which is Oppressive at three characters. **Planted Story Exposed** decides Standard or Hard, because the handlers' lookouts are fewer when it is marked.

The chalk sigil at the alley mouth is new. It gives the fight the non-combat route the page model asks for, and it lets the working lapse at the end of round 8 as a retreat condition. The source's fallen-spine terrain is kept as a table rule from round 3.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- **Orna Fesk**, **Tamm Fesk** and **Harrow Dene** (the three residents), **Jessa Marrick** (the dyer), **Pim** (her boy) and **Sister Ilvane Haste** (the bench priest) are invented, and a search of `campaign/` found no collision. **Garrowby Cartage** is invented and has no match either. Gysheer has no voice profile and is voiced from the event text, formal and without swearing in front of strangers.
- **Brother Hallin Ostrel**, **Brother Haldric Wence** and the east annex come from **The Tithe** and the First Meeting. The Halls of Justice stands in the Castle Ward.
- **The cellar door.** The receipt and day-book say "Aveen Street, cellar door", and the yard door is "three knocks". **The Shrine on Aveen Street** must match, including its chandler's cellar.
- **Whitehawk** reads the note and the receipt as **Intelligence Shared**, and the rank event expects the debrief to be where a member reaches Renown 10.
- The Tithe's reassigned-member rule is honoured in the Brief and in the court, where Savra says only "This isn't about you".
