# Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level, after **The Shard Shunners**. Companions can help with everything except Savra's brief and debrief, which only members attend. A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** receives the Brief as a note from Brother Hallin Ostrel.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 5th-level characters.*
>
> The court holds one **Spined Devil** for each character on Standard and one more on Hard, and Savra Belabranta fights beside the party as a **Knight**. Gysheer Omfreys uses the **Cultist Fanatic** block and arrives as a separate wave. The three residents are **Commoners** and are never counted. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits both rosters for three, four and five characters. A party that finds the chalk sigil at the alley mouth can send the devils back without finishing the fight.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

An hour before noon Savra Belabranta knocks at Trollskull Manor and tells each member that spined devils are loose in a Field Ward court. Three residents are pinned behind a market cart. Savra is already on her way, she will fight beside the party, and she wants the summoner alive. She asks every member to bring whatever the summoner carried to the east annex of the Halls of Justice by dusk tomorrow.

## Background

Gysheer Omfreys is a Tethyrian woman in her forties who works for Victoro Cassalanter of the Sea Ward. She is a hired agent and not household staff. She knows her employer's name and nothing of his reasons. For a tenday she has been readying a cellar on Aveen Street for a larger working, and Victoro has told her to keep the Field Ward frightened and the Watch busy elsewhere.

This morning she chalked a sigil at the mouth of an alley off Twelvedog Court and called a flight of spined devils to clear the court of watchers. They slipped her hold at once and trapped three residents behind a cart. A Watch runner took word to the Halls of Justice, and Savra took the message herself. In **The Black Viper Investigation** she asked what the people watching Trollskull Alley were moving, and this mission answers her.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Gysheer carries a note sealed with the initials "V.C." and a carter's receipt for nine crates delivered to a cellar door on Aveen Street. Her room on Crispin Street holds the day-book and the receipt copies, so the address survives even if she escapes. The sealed note is not duplicated.
> - The crates hold black candles, rock salt, iron filings and lamp oil. The Order does not know what they are for, and Gysheer knows only that they feed a rite.
> - Savra suspects the Cassalanters and cannot prove it. She names no family in this mission until a document or a sworn answer does.
> - A chalk sigil at the alley mouth holds the devils to the court. Wiping it sends the survivors back, and the working lapses at the end of round 8 whatever the party does.
> - **Planted Story Exposed** matters because the handlers' lookouts are fewer when it is marked, so Gysheer has less warning and calls one devil fewer.

## Twelvedog Court

The party takes Savra's brief on the step and follows her into the Field Ward. The devils hold the rooflines, three residents hide behind a cart in the middle of the court, and Savra stands beside the party throughout. The party can fight the devils, draw their fire off the cart, or wipe the sigil that binds them. When the last devil falls or goes, Gysheer comes out of the alley with a knife.

## What Gysheer Carried

Savra takes Gysheer to the Halls of Justice if she is held. Three independent leads give the Aveen Street address: a hearing under *Zone of Truth*, Gysheer's coat in the property room, and her rented room on Crispin Street. Savra explains why she cannot act on the address alone and asks the party to bring her paper. The members report at the east annex at dusk tomorrow.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Savra. Bonuses of +1 each go to members in three cases. All three residents come through unharmed, the address is found on paper and not from Gysheer's hearing, and Gysheer's papers reach Savra in full. Companions gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Shrine Address Recovered** and **Intelligence Shared** are read by **The Shrine on Aveen Street** and by the Whitehawk rank event. **Imp Captured** and **Planted Story Exposed** are read here. **The Shrine on Aveen Street** becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 10 and 6th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet): briefs the members, fights beside them as a Knight, holds Gysheer at the Halls and takes the report.
- **Gysheer Omfreys** (hired agent of Victoro Cassalanter): the summoner, who attacks with a knife and can be questioned at a hearing.
- **Victoro Cassalanter** (Cassalanters): named by Gysheer under *Zone of Truth* and never present.
- **Brother Hallin Ostrel** (Order of the Gauntlet): the Order's clerk, who inventories Gysheer's belongings.
- **Brother Haldric Wence** (Order of the Gauntlet): the door-warden of the Halls of Justice, who sends members to the east annex.
- **Sister Ilvane Haste** (Temple of Tyr): the priest of the Halls' bench who reads the *Zone of Truth* scroll.
- **Orna Fesk**, **Tamm Fesk** and **Harrow Dene**: the three residents behind the cart.
- **Jessa Marrick**: the dyer on Crispin Street who lets Gysheer's room.

## Dangers & Enemies

The devils use the **Spined Devil** block, and Gysheer uses the **Cultist Fanatic** block. Savra uses the **Knight** block and withdraws at 20 hit points. The devils lapse at the end of round 8, and Gysheer yields at 22 hit points or fewer.

## Overview

Spined devils run loose in a Field Ward court while three residents hide behind a cart. The party fights beside Savra, then follows the summoner's papers to an address on Aveen Street.
