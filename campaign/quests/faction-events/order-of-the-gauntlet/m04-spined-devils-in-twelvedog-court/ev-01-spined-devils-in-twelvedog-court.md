# Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat/Social Event begins when Savra Belabranta arrives at Trollskull Manor before the usual hour.
> In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Savra's brief at the door and ask what she knows.
> - Fight five spined devils alongside Savra in Twelvedog Court while three residents shelter behind an overturned market cart.
> - Subdue Gysheer Omfreys when she emerges from the alley after the last devil falls.
> - Follow any of three independent paths to the Aveen Street shrine address: magical compulsion, Gysheer's belongings, or her rented room on Crispin Street.
> - Hear Savra's legal constraint and watch her release Gysheer.
>
> #### Who Summoned the Devils
>
> Gysheer Omfreys is a mid-level Cassalanter operative who conducted the opening act of a consecration ritual in the alley off Twelvedog Court. The five spined devils she summoned broke her direction at once. She stayed to finish when the fighting outside convinced her the Order's arrival was a coordinated suppression of the ritual rather than a Watch response to a disturbance.
>
> Savra does not know who summoned the devils. The Order's involvement began with a field report: infernal creatures, Twelvedog Court, Field Ward, three residents sheltering behind an overturned cart.
>
> The Cassalanters, monitoring through their imp network, know Gysheer was taken. They do not yet know what she said under compulsion.

### The Brief

Savra arrives at Trollskull Manor before the usual hour and is at the door when someone opens it.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra is at the door before the usual hour, coat on, sword at her side, and she is already turning as she speaks.
>
> > "Spined devils in Twelvedog Court, Field Ward. Someone summoned them and lost control. An informant's count is five, with three residents sheltered behind a market cart and not going anywhere until the things are cleared. I am going there now."
>
> She waits just long enough to see who is following her.

> [!social]**The Knight Moving**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Neutral Good, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: a paladin of Tyr who moves fast and says little when she already knows what needs to be done.
>
> Savra is in operational mode from the moment the door opens. She will answer questions while she moves and expects the party to keep pace with her. She states facts without inflection and does not soften any of them. The only additional detail she volunteers is what the party will need to act usefully when they arrive.
>
> She does not know who summoned the devils. Her informant gave her numbers and a location, not a name.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss include:
> - Spined devils: what they are and what to expect from them.
> - The court itself: the layout as her informant described it.
> - The residents: an informant's count; she says so plainly.
> - Whether to expect a summoner: she doesn't know and she says so.

> [!qna]**Spined devils?**
>
> > "Infernal creatures. They throw spines — barbed, poisoned, and they prefer range, which means they will be on rooflines and ledges rather than ground level. Get underneath them and the advantage reverses. Don't let them keep elevation."

> [!qna]**How many?**
>
> > "Five. That's the informant's count. Treat it as a minimum."

> [!qna]**The residents?**
>
> > "Three, behind a cart. Same source. Could be more, depending on who took cover before the report went out."

> [!qna]**Who summoned them?**
>
> She pauses for a moment before she answers.
>
> > "I don't know. That is what we find out when we get there, if at all."

> [!qna]**The pay?**
>
> > "The Order compensates its people. We can settle that when the work is done."

### Twelvedog Court

The party arrives in the Field Ward to find the square already in chaos, the devils active on the rooflines and window ledges ringing the court.

> [!readaloud]
> Twelvedog Court is a small square named for a kennel operation that closed thirty years ago, six buildings facing inward around a central stretch of cobblestones. Every shutter in the square is barred. Three residents are pressed behind an overturned market cart at the far end of the court, and the things on the rooflines and window ledges above them have been keeping them there, throwing spines that clatter off the cobbles every time someone moves.
>
> There are five of them. Two occupy the east roofline, one holds a window ledge above the alley mouth, and two have taken positions close to the cart on the building faces above. Savra reads the court in a moment and moves to put herself between the two near the cart and the residents.

> [!gamemaster]**Protecting the Residents**
>
> The three residents behind the cart are not combatants and will not emerge until the court is clear. The cart provides half cover. If a spined devil reaches the ground and moves toward the cart, one round of deliberate action from the party is enough to redirect it before the residents are at risk. If a resident takes damage during the fight, the base renown condition is still achievable as long as no one dies.

> [!hazard]**Five Spined Devils**
>
> Five **Spined Devils** (2024 *Monster Manual*) are distributed across the elevated positions ringing the court: two on the east roofline, one at the center window above the alley mouth, and two near the cart on the building faces above.
>
> #### Spined Devil Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the five devils hold their elevated positions and fire from range. Their *Hurl Spine* attack deals 2d10 piercing damage; DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or Poisoned for 1 minute.
>
> Over the course of combat, each devil will prioritize the highest available elevated position and reposition along rooflines rather than descend. If a devil cannot reach an elevated position and is forced to the ground, it closes to melee.
>
> From round three onward, fallen spines in the central 20 feet of the court count as Difficult Terrain.
>
> The battle ends when all five spined devils are destroyed.
>
> **Savra** fights at the party's side with disciplined efficiency, prioritizing the residents and killing what threatens them first.

### Gysheer Omfreys

The last spined devil falls, and a moment later a door opens in the alley wall.

> [!readaloud]
> The last spined devil hits the cobbles and stops moving, and the court goes quiet except for the sound of the residents shifting behind the cart. Then a door opens in the alley wall, and a woman in good clothes steps through it into the court. She is middle-aged and Tethyrian, and her clothes stand out here the way a wool coat stands out in the Field Ward — too good for this part of the city and too deliberate to be an accident. She holds a knife with the grip of someone who was taught how rather than learned by practice. She looks past the party and fixes her attention directly on Savra.
>
> > "You should not have come here. This was a consecrated working. You have disrupted the work of —"
>
> She stops. Her jaw closes, and the knife stays where it is.

> [!social]**The Operative**
>
> Gysheer Omfreys (Lawful Evil, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: a Cassalanter operative who will give nothing freely and cannot understand how the Order already knew about the ritual.
>
> Use the **Cult Fanatic** stat block for Gysheer. She is middle-aged, Tethyrian, in good clothes, and she holds her knife with the grip of someone who was taught rather than someone with experience.
>
> Gysheer is in shock and working to control it. Her contempt is directed specifically at Savra — the paladin's presence here means someone flagged the ritual, and that failure belongs to the network's security, not to her. She will not volunteer information, will not beg, and if freed will leave the Field Ward as quickly as she can. She is not suicidal about her odds and will stop fighting if the physical situation is impossible.
>
> She says nothing further without compulsion.

> [!qna]**The working?**
>
> Her jaw stays shut. She looks past whoever is speaking, back toward the alley door.

> [!qna]**Who sent you?**
>
> > "I am not saying anything to you."

> [!qna]**Aveen Street?**
>
> She does not react to the name at all, and if she recognizes it in her expression, she controls that too.

Any one of three independent paths leads to the shrine address on Aveen Street.

> [!exploration]**Magical Compulsion**
>
> *Charm Person* (DC 13 Wisdom saving throw), *Command*, or equivalent applied to Gysheer while she is subdued draws out three pieces of information in sequence: "operations for Lord Victoro Cassalanter of the Sea Ward," "preparation of ground," and "the shrine on Aveen Street."
>
> Any character who makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (History) check**, or who is familiar with the Code Legal, recognizes that Savra cannot take a legally actionable step against a named family based on a magically compelled confession alone.

> [!exploration]**Her Belongings**
>
> Any character who searches Gysheer's person while she is subdued and makes a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check** finds a folded ritual preparation note in her coat pocket.
>
> > The note lists a shrine address on Aveen Street, a delivery schedule for ritual components, and a Cassalanter household seal impression in the wax closure. The initialing at the bottom reads "Lord V.C."

> [!exploration]**Her Residence**
>
> Gysheer rents a room above a dyer's shop on Crispin Street in the Field Ward, two blocks from Twelvedog Court. A **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check** with the landlord gains access; confirming that her belongings are still inside is enough to justify entry. This search takes four hours and can be conducted after Gysheer has fled.
>
> > The room holds ritual preparation notes, the Aveen Street address written in full, and a letter on Cassalanter household stationery — unsigned, but authorizing the working and naming Lord Victoro as principal.

### Savra's Constraint

Savra waits until the party has done what they can with Gysheer, and then she speaks.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra looks at Gysheer for a moment after the questioning is done, and then she looks at whoever is holding her.
>
> > "Devil worship is not illegal in Waterdeep. Summoning spined devils in a residential courtyard may be — I'll need to consult the Code Legal. But acting against the Cassalanters on the confession of one cultist extracted under magical compulsion is not something I can do."
>
> She says it with the flat evenness of someone stating a fact they find distasteful.
>
> > "Not officially. What you choose to do with what you know — that is your decision. You are not the Order, and I cannot instruct you. What I can tell you is this: if you brought me real evidence — a document, not a confession — the Order would know exactly what to do with it."
>
> She releases Gysheer and watches her disappear into the Field Ward.

### Concluding the Event

The court is quiet. Savra stays where she is for a moment before she turns back to the party.

## Overview

Five spined devils are loose in a Field Ward courtyard. Three residents are sheltering behind an overturned cart. Savra is already at the door.

## Summary

We cleared Twelvedog Court with Savra, put down five spined devils, and caught the woman who came out of the alley after the last one fell. Three paths led to the same address — a shrine on Aveen Street that Savra has not been inside and cannot enter without grounds. We have the grounds now.
