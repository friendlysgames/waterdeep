# Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat/Social Event begins when Savra Belabranta arrives at Trollskull Manor already moving.
> In this Event:
>
> - Five spined devils are loose in Twelvedog Court, a Field Ward square; three residents are sheltering behind a market cart.
> - Savra enters from the south and fights at the party's side throughout.
> - Fallen spines create Difficult Terrain in the central 20 feet from round three onward.

**Background (DM only)**
**Gysheer Omfreys** — Tethyrian, middle-aged, in good clothes conspicuous in the Field Ward — summoned five spined devils as the opening act of a consecration ritual in an alley off Twelvedog Court. The devils escaped her direction immediately. Gysheer is still in the alley, deciding whether to flee or finish. She decides to finish when she hears the fighting, mistaking the Order's arrival for a coordinated suppression of the ritual rather than a response to a public disturbance.

The Cassalanters, monitoring through their imp network, know Gysheer was taken. They do not yet know what she said under compulsion.

### Twelvedog Court

A small square in the Field Ward named for a kennel operation gone thirty years. Six buildings face inward, every shutter barred. Three residents are pinned behind an overturned market cart, sheltering from the things on the roof edges and window ledges — five creatures that look designed to shoot thorns at people, which is exactly what they are doing.

Five **Spined Devils** (2024 *Monster Manual*). They prefer range, occupying rooflines and window ledges above the court. Their *Hurl Spine* attack deals 2d10 piercing; DC 13 Dexterity or Poisoned for 1 minute. From round three onward, fallen spines in the central 20 feet count as Difficult Terrain.

**Savra** enters from the south at a controlled walk, sword drawn. She reads the tactical situation in three seconds — two on the east roofline, one at the center window, two near the cart — and fights at the party's side with disciplined efficiency. She protects the residents. She kills what needs to be killed. Nothing more.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Protecting the Residents
> The three residents behind the cart are not combatants and will not emerge until the court is clear. The cart provides half cover. If a spined devil reaches the ground and moves toward the cart, the residents are in danger — the party should have enough warning from the devils' ranged preference to prevent this. If a resident takes damage during the fight, the base renown condition is still achievable as long as no one dies.

### Gysheer Omfreys

The last spined devil is still twitching when an alley door opens. **Gysheer Omfreys** (use **Cult Fanatic** stat block) — middle-aged, Tethyrian, in good clothes. She holds a knife with the grip of someone taught rather than experienced. Desperation and contempt directed at Savra specifically.

> [!npc-narrative]**Gysheer Omfreys**
> Middle-aged, Tethyrian, in good clothes that stand out in the Field Ward. She holds a knife with the grip of someone taught rather than experienced. Her contempt is directed at Savra specifically. "You should not have come here. This was a consecrated working. You have disrupted the work of —" She stops. She has said too much. Her jaw closes. The knife stays up.

She says nothing further and will flee or fight if not restrained.

### Three Paths to the Shrine

Three independent paths lead to the shrine address on Aveen Street. Any one is sufficient.

**Magical compulsion:** *Charm Person* (DC 13 Wisdom save), *Command*, or equivalent extracts three pieces in sequence: "operations for Lord Victoro Cassalanter of the Sea Ward," "preparation of ground," and "the shrine on Aveen Street."

**Her belongings:** A **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check** searching Gysheer's person — possible only if she is subdued — finds a folded ritual preparation note in her coat pocket. The note lists the shrine's address on Aveen Street, a delivery schedule for ritual components, and a Cassalanter household seal impression in the wax closure. The "Lord V.C." initialing is enough for Savra.

**Her residence:** Gysheer rents a room above a dyer's shop on Crispin Street in the Field Ward, two blocks from Twelvedog Court. A **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check** with the landlord, or simply observing that her belongings are still there, allows entry. Inside: ritual preparation notes, the Aveen Street address in full, and a letter — unsigned, on Cassalanter household stationery — authorizing the working and naming Lord Victoro as principal. The residence search takes four hours and can be done after Gysheer flees.

### Savra's Constraint

When Gysheer is subdued, Savra is quiet for a moment.

> > "Devil worship is not illegal in Waterdeep. Summoning spined devils in a residential courtyard may be — I'll need to consult the Code Legal. But acting against the Cassalanters on the confession of one cultist extracted under magical compulsion is not something I can do." She says it with the flat evenness of a fact she finds distasteful. "Not officially." A pause. "What you choose to do with what you know — you are not the Order. I cannot instruct you. What I can tell you is that if you brought me real evidence — a document, not a confession — the Order would know exactly what to do with it."

She releases Gysheer and watches her disappear into the Field Ward.

## Summary

Five spined devils summoned by a Cassalanter cultist escaped her control in Twelvedog Court. The party cleared the court with Savra, subdued or drove off the cultist, and extracted the first solid lead on the Cassalanter network — the shrine on Aveen Street.
