# Twelvedog Court

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat and Social Event begins when Savra Belabranta knocks at Trollskull Manor an hour before noon and tells the members that spined devils are loose in a Field Ward court. It ends when Gysheer Omfreys is held, yields or escapes. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Savra's brief on the step and follow her into the Field Ward.
> - Draw the devils away from three residents who are pinned behind an overturned cart.
> - Fight the devils with Savra beside them, or wipe the chalk sigil at the alley mouth and send the survivors back.
> - Fight, hold or lose Gysheer when she comes out of the alley with a knife.
>
> Only Order of the Gauntlet members attend Savra's brief. Companions help with every other part of the Event.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Gysheer Omfreys chalked a sigil at the mouth of the alley behind the east building this morning and called the devils to clear the court of watchers. They broke her hold within a minute and drove three residents behind a cart.
> - The sigil holds the devils to the court. Wiping it sends every surviving devil back in a puff of sulfur, and the working lapses at the end of round 8 whatever the party does.
> - Gysheer is a hired agent who answers to Victoro Cassalanter. She did not mean the devils to trap the residents, and she will not say so while she is fighting.
> - The handlers keep lookouts in the Field Ward to warn her. If **Planted Story Exposed** is marked, the lookouts are fewer, Gysheer is warned late and she calls one devil fewer.
> - Savra does not know who summoned the devils. A Watch runner brought her the news, and she has asked nothing of anyone yet.

### The Brief

Savra knocks once an hour before noon and does not come in, because she is already moving. She briefs the members on the step and answers five questions. If a member is marked **Quietly Reassigned**, run **When the Brief Comes by Note** instead.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra Belabranta stands on the step in her dark riding coat with her sword belted on and the silver gauntlet pin at her collar. She has already half turned toward the lane, and she speaks before the door is fully open.
>
> > "Spined devils are loose in Twelvedog Court, in the Field Ward. A Watch boy says three people are pinned behind a cart. I'm going now, and I'll fight beside you. Be in the court within the hour. Then bring me whatever the summoner carried, at the east annex of the Halls of Justice, by dusk tomorrow."

> [!social]**The Knight Already Moving**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: the Order's knight in Waterdeep, a woman in her late twenties with dark hair worn back, a strong jaw and a left-slanting hand.
>
> Savra is in a hurry and does not hide it, though she still pauses before each answer and says exactly what she knows. She swears rarely and only at wrongdoing, and she gives a single measured nod to an answer she believes. Today she has no time to explain the Order's reasons.
>
> Savra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Watch boy reported and where the court lies.
> - Who the summoner might be, which she does not know.
> - How she wants the fight run and what she wants kept alive.
> - What she wants brought back, and when.
>
> She will not discuss the Cassalanters or any family, and she will not wait for the party if they stop to talk.

> [!qna]**What is in the court?**
>
> > "Spined devils, on the roofs, and the boy couldn't count them. Three residents are behind a cart in the middle. I'll tell you plainly that I don't know more, and I won't guess."

> [!qna]**Who called them?**
>
> > "Somebody who knows how, and who wanted that court empty at noon. Devils don't wander into the Field Ward by themselves. If the summoner is still there, I want her alive."

> [!qna]**Are you fighting with us?**
>
> > "Yes, and this one is mine as much as yours. Get the residents clear first and the devils second. I'll take whatever is nearest the cart, and you tell me where you need me."

> [!qna]**Where is it?**
>
> > "Twelvedog Court, two streets east of the soup house, in the old kennel yard. Six buildings face inward, and every shutter will be barred. The south lane is the only way in."

> [!qna]**What do you want back, and when?**
>
> > "Whatever the summoner carries, and anything she had on her. Bring it to the east annex by dusk tomorrow, and don't read it out in the street. If she gets away, bring me that instead."

### When the Brief Comes by Note

A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** receives the Brief from a Priest Acolyte in a plain grey surcoat, who arrives an hour before noon and does not wait for a reply. Savra has gone ahead and meets the party in the court.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The acolyte hands you a folded paper sealed with the Halls of Justice wax and runs back down the lane. The note holds one short paragraph in a neat, upright hand.
>
> > Spined devils in Twelvedog Court, Field Ward, two streets east of the soup house. Three residents are pinned behind a cart. Savra has gone ahead and will be in the court. Bring whatever the summoner carries to the east annex by dusk tomorrow. B.H.O.

In the court Savra fights as the Order's rules require, and she is cold toward a reassigned member. She says only "This isn't about you" if the member speaks to her, and she protects the party exactly as she would anyone else.

### The Field Ward at Noon

The way to the court is two streets of crooked buildings, and the party reaches it without a check because the noise leads them there. Savra arrives at the south entrance a few steps ahead of them and does not slow down.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The south lane opens into a small cobbled court with six buildings facing inward, and every shutter on them is barred. An overturned market cart lies near the middle with three people crouched behind it, and spines stick out of its boards like nails. Small winged shapes crouch along the east roofline and on the window ledges, and a thorn whips down from the nearest one and cracks against the stones. Savra draws her sword and walks into the court without a word.

> [!gamemaster]**The Court**
>
> - **The south entrance:** The lane is 10 feet wide, and Savra holds it in round 1 so that nothing flies out into the ward.
> - **The cart:** The cart lies on its side near the west wall and gives half cover to the three residents behind it. They are Orna Fesk, a washerwoman, her son Tamm, who is ten, and Harrow Dene, an old cooper.
> - **The buildings:** A baker's shop stands on the west side, and its back door gives onto a yard and a lane behind. The cooper's yard, two empty houses and the old kennel-master's house fill the rest. All six shutters are barred from within.
> - **The rooflines:** The east roofline and the window ledges are 15 to 25 feet up. The devils perch there and fly down when they choose.
> - **The alley:** A narrow alley runs beside the kennel-master's house in the northeast corner. An alley door at its far end leads to a yard where Gysheer waits.

> [!exploration]**Reading the Court**
>
> The party can study the court for a round before the first spine falls, and the first look decides what the fight costs.
>
> - **Perception:** Any character who scans the court and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check sees a white chalk circle on the stones at the alley mouth, about 5 feet across, with eight marks spaced around its rim. A devil is perched on the lintel above it.
> - **Arcana or Religion:** A character who sees the circle and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion)** check knows that a mark like this binds summoned creatures to a place, and that wiping it would let them go.
> - **Savra:** If nobody has found the circle by the end of round 3, she calls out "The mark at the alley, wipe it" and points with her sword.
> - **On a failure:** The character sees only devils and a wrecked cart. Savra's call at the end of round 3 still gives the party the circle.

### The Devils

The fight begins when the party steps into the court. The cart devil, the one nearest the west wall, drops to the ground in round 2 and goes for the residents unless a character or Savra stops it. A character who attacks it, or who stands within 5 feet of it at the start of its turn, draws it off. Savra does the same in round 3 if no character has.

> [!hazard]**Spined Devils in the Court**
>
> The devils use the **Spined Devil** block and Savra fights as a **Knight**. The three residents are **Commoners** with 4 hit points each and are never counted. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits each roster, with Savra counted in full and companions counted as characters.
>
> - **Three characters, Standard:** 3 Spined Devils. This is a Bruising fight with a Day Cost of 4.
> - **Four characters, Standard:** 4 Spined Devils. This is a Bruising fight with a Day Cost of 4.
> - **Five characters, Standard:** 5 Spined Devils. This is a Bruising fight with a Day Cost of 4.
> - **Hard, at any size:** Add 1 Spined Devil. This is a Bloody fight with a Day Cost of 6.
>
> Use the Hard roster when **Planted Story Exposed** is not marked, or when the Cassalanters are at Alert or Lockdown. Gysheer is a separate wave with a Day Cost of 2, so the whole Event costs 6 on Standard and 8 on Hard. From round 3, fallen spines make the central 20 feet of the court Difficult Terrain.
>
> #### The Devils' Tactics
> To begin, the devils hold the east roofline and the window ledges and fire their Tail Spines at the nearest armed character. They use Flyby to leave reach without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
>
> During combat, the devils:
> - Use Infernal Fork on a character within reach and Tail Spine, which has a range of 20 feet, on anyone beyond it.
> - Send no more than two attacks at one character in a round, and never attack a creature at 0 hit points.
> - Send the cart devil to the ground in round 2, where it attacks Harrow Dene with its Infernal Fork unless it has been drawn off. Harrow has half cover, which gives him AC 12.
> - Spend one attack a round on Savra when she stands in the open, because she looks like the greatest threat.
> - Fly out over the roofs and vanish at the end of round 8, when the working lapses.
>
> A resident who is hit drops to 0 hit points and can be stabilized with a **DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine)** check or any healing. The residents leave the court on foot through the baker's back door if a character spends an action to lead them, and they follow without a check.
>
> #### Savra's Tactics
> To begin, Savra holds the south entrance for round 1 and then moves to whichever devil is nearest the cart. She fights with the Greatsword and uses Parry against melee hits.
>
> During combat, Savra:
> - Takes the cart devil in round 3 if no character has, and stays between it and the residents.
> - Calls to a wounded character by name and moves to stand beside them.
> - Withdraws to the south entrance at 20 hit points and keeps shouting directions from there.
> - Wants Gysheer alive, and says so before the last devil falls.

> [!exploration]**Wiping the Sigil**
>
> A character within 5 feet of the chalk circle can wipe it with one action and no check, though the devil on the lintel above it attacks anyone who stands there. When the circle is broken, every surviving devil vanishes in a puff of sulfur and the fight is over.
>
> - **What follows:** The alley door at the far end bangs open in the same round, and Gysheer comes out as described in **The Summoner**. She arrives angry and with the full fight still ahead of her.
> - **The cost:** The fight then costs only Gysheer's Day Cost of 2, and the party keeps its resources.
> - **The risk:** The character at the alley mouth faces the lintel devil first and Gysheer in the same round.

A party that leads the residents out through the baker's back door and holds the south entrance with Savra can wait for the working to lapse at the end of round 8. The devils then fly off over the roofs, and Gysheer comes out as described in **The Summoner**.

### The Summoner

Gysheer waits in the yard behind the alley door, listening. She comes out when the last devil falls or the sigil is wiped, and not before. If the party leaves the court with devils still in it, she stays where she is.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The last devil is still twitching on the stones when the alley door bangs open. A woman in good dark clothes steps out with a plain steel knife held low in one hand, the grip of someone who was taught and never tested. She is Tethyrian and about forty, and her eyes go to Savra and stay there.
>
> > "You should not have come here. This was a consecrated working. You have disrupted the work of..."
>
> She stops and shuts her mouth, and the knife stays up.

> [!hazard]**Gysheer Omfreys**
>
> Gysheer uses the **Cultist Fanatic** block. She is one Cultist Fanatic against the party and Savra, a Mild fight at every party size with a Day Cost of 2.
>
> #### Gysheer's Tactics
> To begin, Gysheer goes for Savra with her Pact Blade, because Savra is the one she hates. She casts *Spiritual Weapon* on the second round and holds *Hold Person* for a character the others cannot reach in one turn.
>
> During combat, Gysheer:
> - Makes no Pact Blade attack on a turn when she casts *Hold Person*, and never attacks a creature at 0 hit points.
> - Spreads her attacks across the party, with no more than two on one target.
> - Yields at 22 hit points or fewer if she is Grappled or held, and drops the knife with both hands open.
> - Dashes into the alley and out through the yard behind it if she is at 22 hit points or fewer and the alley door is not blocked.
>
> The fight ends when Gysheer yields, falls or escapes. A character who tells her "Put it down and you will be heard, not hurt" and makes a successful **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** check ends the fight without another blow, and she gives up the knife with a look of loathing. Savra will not kill a yielding creature.

If Gysheer escapes, she takes her coat with her and never goes back to her room on Crispin Street, because she expects the Order to watch it. Savra does not give chase, because the residents come first. The members continue with **What Gysheer Carried** and rely on the Crispin Street lead.

### Renown Opportunities

### Aftermath

The residents thank the party in their own way. Orna Fesk will not stop apologizing for the cart, Tamm picks up a spine and is told to put it down, and Harrow Dene sits on the kerb and says he has lived here forty years and never seen a thing like it. A resident who was hurt is carried to the Temple of Helm, whose healers treat the Watch's poor for free.

Savra gives the baker a few coins from her own purse for the shutters and the mess. The Watch's Field Ward post sends two Guards an hour later to rope off the court, and the sergeant asks no questions of anyone.

### Concluding the Event

The Event ends when Gysheer is held or gone, and the members follow Savra to the Halls of Justice.

We followed Savra into Twelvedog Court and found spined devils on the rooflines and three frightened residents behind a cart. We held the court with her, and a woman with a plain steel knife came out of the alley and stopped herself in the middle of a sentence. Whose work had we disrupted?
