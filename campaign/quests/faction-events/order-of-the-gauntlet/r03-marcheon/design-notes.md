# Design Notes: Marcheon

## What the Source Gave

The restored draft gave Marcheon as a single speech by Savra. It listed three benefits with no contact, no notice, no limit and no loss rule. Savra could accompany the character into one operation in each quest, the armory lent one mundane item each tenday, and the temples shared a weekly briefing. The Factions Guide and the Players' Guide carry the same three lines.

## Individual Procedures

Each benefit is now a per-member procedure. Savra's accompaniment is asked for through Brother Haldric Wence at the Halls of Justice, needs 24 hours, and is answered at the manor door. The armory is a single item each tenday, lent by Brother Osric Wickersham on the notice table. The temple briefings come from three fixed lists of four patterns each, so nothing is left for the GM to invent. Savra is a Knight ally who is a reserve at levels 2 and 3 and counts in full from level 4, using the allied Party Power figures from the mechanics reference.

## Departures From the Source

The draft said "per week" for briefings, which is now a tenday. The draft's armory cleric is replaced by a lay brother, because the old name collided with a Xanathar bartender. The draft's line that the Black Viper Investigation unlocks at third level is dropped, since the gate ladder already handles it.

Three rulings are mine. Savra's operation is one for the whole party each quest, because she is one knight. A scripted appearance in Twelvedog Court or The Writ and the Weight does not spend it. She refuses when the named opposition outnumbers the party and her by more than two to one. The fourth Torm pattern, the general fiendish signs, is given only after the member has been through Twelvedog Court, so the briefings cannot reveal devils before the party has met one.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Brother Osric Wickersham (the armory's keeper), the three lists of patterns, Savra's follow-up after a briefing, the 24-hour notice and the dusk answer at the manor door. The mechanics reference named the keeper Brother Dunstan Wickersham, which collides with Dunstan Rook in `bregan-daerthe/s01-coin-pouches`, so the first name was changed. "Osric" and "Wickersham" returned no hits in `campaign/`. The Torm chapel is the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield in the Dock Ward from the First Meeting, not the reference's Chapel of the Faithful Hand in the Trades Ward.
