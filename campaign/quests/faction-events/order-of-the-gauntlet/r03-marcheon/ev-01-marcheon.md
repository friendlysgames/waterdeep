# Marcheon

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one party member's OotG Renown reaches 3 — Savra's next visit to Trollskull Manor, or when she seeks the character out at the Halls of Justice within a tenday. The event centres the promoted character; the rest of the party is present. In this Event:
>
> - Savra delivers the promotion without ceremony and without waiting to be prompted.
> - She confirms what changes: she can now accompany the character into one dangerous operation per quest as a combat ally.
> - She opens the Order's armory: one mundane weapon or suit of armor per tenday, borrowed at no cost.
> - She explains the temple priest network — what the priests share, and why they share it.
> - A brief beat of faction color: what Marcheon means inside the Order and what it asks of those who hold it.

### The Promotion

Savra doesn't build to it.

> > "You've reached Marcheon. That changes what I can offer and what I can ask."

She gives the character a moment to respond, then moves directly to what it means in practice.

The armory is at the Halls of Justice, east annex — a cleric named Tobrin holds the key. One weapon or suit of armor per tenday, returned in serviceable condition. The equipment belongs to the Order and to the knights who will need it after them; the condition requirement is not negotiable.

### What She Can Do Now

At Marcheon, Savra can accompany the character into an operation — something with active opposition, in a place where the Order's presence would otherwise be too visible or too inflexible to fit.

Once per quest. She makes the offer when she judges it warranted; the character can also request it. She won't go in if she believes her presence will get the party killed.

> > "I don't volunteer for missions where the main value I bring is dying harder. Tell me what the operation looks like before you invite me, and I'll tell you if it makes sense."

She means this as a practical filter, not a disclaimer. She's been in enough operations to know which shape of fight needs a knight and which doesn't.

### The Temple Network

Priests of Tyr, Helm, and Torm hear things their parishioners don't intend to share. Confessions. Arguments in the vestibule. People who come to pray when they're frightened of something they can't name. The priests have been talking to Savra since she arrived in Waterdeep.

At Marcheon, that network opens to the character: unusual spiritual activity, known fiendish signs, parishioners who've said something that stuck. One briefing per week from the relevant temple, if there's anything worth briefing.

> > "They won't share names without the person's knowledge — Tyr's law. But they'll share patterns, and right now there are patterns worth knowing."

She doesn't elaborate. She'll share specifics when the character has a specific thread to run down.

### What Marcheon Asks

Savra holds the character's eye for a moment.

> > "Marcheon means I trust your judgment enough to follow it into a dangerous situation. Don't make me regret that. Not because of what happens to me — because of what it means about your judgment."

She doesn't add to this.

### Concluding the Event

#### Marcheon Reached: True / False

**True:** The character holds OotG Marcheon rank. Savra's combat accompaniment, the armory, and the temple priest network are all immediately available. Read by the **Whitehawk** rank event (r10-whitehawk), which checks this flag before its opening scene fires. Also read by any OotG mission that unlocks at Renown 3 or higher.

Savra promoted the character to Marcheon.
