# Breaking Up the Brawl

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social/Combat Event begins when Savra Belabranta briefs the party at Trollskull Manor and sends them to the Field Ward. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Savra in person and ask her what she knows.
> - Arrive at a Field Ward market square to find four thugs cornering a Xanathar Guild dealer named Pell.
> - Use the watching crowd's presence to their advantage: a dramatic, confident approach softens the thugs' resolve.
> - Disperse the thugs through three successful DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation) checks before three failures, or fall back on combat.
> - Signal Pell privately with a DC 12 Dexterity check, giving him a graceful way out before things collapse.
> - Question the subdued thugs and learn what little they know about who hired them.
> - Report back to Savra and deliver any intelligence on the Splinter intermediary.
>
> #### Notes on the Crowd
>
> The fifteen locals watching are entertainment-seeking. A dramatic, confident approach earns immediate deference and softens the thugs' position. A hesitant or cautious entrance invites the thugs to play to the gallery and hardens it. The party sets this tone the moment they round the corner.

> [!gamemaster]**Background**
>
> A Manshoon Splinter intermediary — already gone from the scene before the party arrives, and not findable today — paid four thugs to corner Pell, a Xanathar Guild dealer, in a Field Ward market square. The goal is visible Guild vulnerability in the ward, undermining their grip on the informal economy. Pell is not a hardened fighter. His two companions are, and they have been working to keep him from doing something expensive.
>
> The intermediary's departure is deliberate. No evidence trail leads anywhere today. What the party can learn comes only from the thugs themselves, who know they were paid but not by whom — a stranger approached them two days ago, paid in clean coin, and was gone before they could ask questions. This is thin intelligence, but Savra's Manshoon file takes everything.

### The Brief

Savra arrives at Trollskull Manor. There is a situation developing in the Field Ward, and she means to deal with it today.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra knocks once at the door and, when you open it, steps in without waiting and without taking off her coat, which tells you this is not a long conversation.
>
> > "There's a standoff developing in the Field Ward. Four thugs, paid by someone who already left the scene, have backed a Guild dealer and his companions against a grain stall. Fifteen locals are watching. If it collapses before someone gets there, people will get hurt and the neighborhood will remember who let it happen."
>
> She scans the room once to take in who is there, then brings her eyes back to the group.
>
> > "I need you there before it collapses. Stop it — without anyone dying, if you can manage it. The Order is paying attention to the Field Ward. This is part of how we make that clear."

> [!social]**On Assignment**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Neutral Good, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: a knight of the Order of the Gauntlet, atoning for a cult past through the work she has found that makes it meaningful.
>
> Savra is precise about what she knows and clear about what she doesn't. She has been watching the Field Ward long enough to recognize the Splinter's pattern — gangs paid to hit Guild interests, the payer always gone before the job starts — but she has no name for the intermediary yet. She notes that how the situation ends matters as much as that it ends.
>
> She will not accompany the party on this one. She has other work at the Halls of Justice today.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss before the party leaves include:
> - The situation in the market square and what she knows about the players in it.
> - Why the Order is watching the Field Ward.
> - What she wants the party to bring back, if anything.
>
> Specific dialogue for Savra on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Who are the thugs working for?**
>
> > "Someone who paid them two days ago and left before the job started. They may know a description. They almost certainly don't know a name." She pauses. "Bring me whatever they give you. The Manshoon file takes everything."

> [!qna]**Why does the Order care about a Guild dealer?**
>
> > "I don't care about the dealer. I care about the pattern. Someone has been paying Field Ward gangs to go after Guild interests, and they've been doing it carefully — clean coin, no names, gone before anything can be traced. That pattern has a name behind it, and I'd like to find it."

> [!qna]**Will you be there?**
>
> > "No. I'm at the Halls of Justice this afternoon. This one is yours."

> [!qna]**What if we can't stop it without fighting?**
>
> > "Then fight. I'd rather you didn't — the neighborhood is watching, and how it ends matters more than that it ends. But if it goes to steel, put them down without killing them if you can." She holds a beat. "The distinction matters to me."

> [!qna]**The pay?**
>
> She meets the question plainly.
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly: the Order doesn't settle invoices for field work. What I can give you is my good opinion and the renown that comes with it. That opens doors in this city that coin alone doesn't."

Once the party accepts, proceed to **The Field Ward at Noon**.

### The Field Ward at Noon

The Field Ward smells of cookfire smoke and damp earth — unpaved streets, buildings leaning against each other, the improvised density of a place built fast and never properly finished. The trouble is audible before it is visible: raised voices from a market square two streets over, the pitch a crowd uses when it has decided to watch rather than leave.

> [!readaloud]
> You can hear the trouble from two streets away — a particular kind of crowd noise, the pitch people settle into when they have decided to watch rather than leave — and when you round the corner into the square you see the reason. Four heavyset men have backed a slight figure and his two companions against a grain stall at the far end, and a loose ring of locals is standing at what each of them has independently calculated to be a safe distance. The barking is coming from a large brown dog pressed flat against the far wall, looking uncertain about where it should position itself in all of this.

The square holds the four thugs, **Pell** and his two companions pressed against the grain stall, fifteen locals at varying distances, and the dog. Pell is not a fighter; his companions are, and they are watching the exits.

### The Standoff

**Signaling Pell:** A **DC 12 Dexterity check** catches his eye and gestures without the thugs noticing. He will follow any lead for exiting without either side losing face.

**Dispersing without combat:** Three successful **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation) checks** before three failures disperses the thugs. Each failure emboldens them; by the third, they have decided the party is not a real threat and Pell is in immediate danger.

**Combat:** Four **Thugs** (2024 *Monster Manual*). When the odds shift clearly against them, they break. Knocking them unconscious rather than killing them is achievable — they are not committed fighters.

> [!hazard]**Four Thugs**
>
> The four thugs use the **Thug** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*).
>
> #### Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the thugs focus on Pell and his companions first, using the crowd's presence to buy time. Over the course of combat, they will prioritize any character who steps between them and Pell.
>
> The battle ends when the thugs are subdued, killed, or driven from the square — or when they decide the crowd's mood has turned against them and retreat on their own.

> [!gamemaster]**After the Fight**
>
> Any of the four thugs, if questioned after being subdued, can confirm they were paid by a stranger two days ago. The stranger was human, unremarkable in appearance, paid in clean coin, and gave no name. This is thin intelligence, but Savra's Manshoon file takes everything.

### The Debrief

The party reports back to Savra. She listens without interrupting. If they have any traceable detail about the intermediary — a physical description, a name overheard, anything — she thanks them plainly and files it.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra is at Trollskull Manor when the party returns, and she listens to the full account without interrupting, her expression steady and her hands still while you run through it. When you finish, she asks two things: whether anyone was killed, and whether the thugs said anything about who hired them. After she has both answers, she settles back in her chair.
>
> > "That goes to the Manshoon file," she says of the intermediary's description, and moves on.

### Concluding the Event

The Field Ward saw how this ended. That matters here, where official presence is rare and memory is long.

## Overview

A standoff in a Field Ward market square — four paid thugs, a cornered Guild dealer, fifteen locals watching. Stop it before it turns lethal.

## Summary

We interrupted an orchestrated confrontation in the Field Ward: four paid thugs cornering a Xanathar Guild dealer in a market square while the neighborhood watched. The standoff ended one way or another. The neighborhood saw how.
