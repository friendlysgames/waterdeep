# Breaking Up the Brawl

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social/Combat Event begins when Savra Belabranta briefs the party at Trollskull Manor and sends them to the Field Ward.
> In this Event:
>
> - The party arrives at a market square to find four thugs cornering a Xanathar Guild dealer named Pell.
> - A crowd of fifteen locals is watching; their presence hardens or softens the thugs' position depending on the party's approach.
> - Three DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation) checks, before three failures, disperses the thugs without combat.
> - Signaling Pell privately (DC 12 Dexterity) gives him a way to exit without escalation.
> - Combat — four Thugs — is the fallback if intimidation fails.
> - Savra notes whether the thugs were killed or left alive when the party reports back.
> - Any identifying detail about the Splinter intermediary goes to the Order's Manshoon file.

**Background (DM only)**
A Manshoon Splinter intermediary — already gone, not findable today — paid four thugs to corner **Pell**, a Xanathar Guild dealer, in a Field Ward market square. The goal is visible Guild vulnerability in the ward, undermining their control of the informal economy. Pell is not a hardened fighter. His two companions are, and they are working to keep him from doing something expensive.

The intermediary's departure is deliberate. No evidence trail leads anywhere today. What the party can learn comes only from the thugs, who know they were paid but not by whom — a stranger approached them two days ago, paid in clean coin, and left before they could ask questions.

### The Field Ward at Noon

The Field Ward smells of cookfire smoke and damp earth — unpaved streets, buildings leaning against each other, the improvised density of a place built fast and never properly finished. The trouble is audible before it is visible: raised voices from a market square two streets over, the pitch a crowd uses when it has decided to watch rather than leave.

The square holds the four thugs, **Pell** (use **Bandit** stat block) and his two companions pressed against a grain stall, fifteen locals at varying distances, and a large dog that belongs to none of the parties and is barking.

### The Standoff

The crowd is entertainment-seeking. A dramatic, confident approach earns immediate deference and softens the thugs' position. A hesitant or cautious approach invites the thugs to play to the gallery and hardens it.

**Signaling Pell:** A **DC 12 Dexterity check** catches his eye and gestures without the thugs noticing. He will follow any lead for exiting without either side losing face — he accepts a graceful out.

**Dispersing without combat:** Three successful **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation) checks** before three failures disperses the thugs. Each failure emboldens them; by the third, they have decided the party is not a real threat and Pell is in immediate danger.

**Combat:** Four **Thugs** (2024 *Monster Manual*). When the odds shift clearly against them, they break. Knocking them unconscious rather than killing them is achievable — they are not committed fighters.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### After the Fight
> Any of the four thugs, if questioned after being subdued, can confirm they were paid by a stranger two days ago. The stranger was human, nondescript, paid in clean coin, and gave no name. This is thin intelligence, but Savra's Manshoon file takes everything.

### The Debrief

The party reports back to Savra. She listens without interrupting. If they have any traceable detail about the intermediary — a physical description, a name overheard, anything — she thanks them plainly and files it. "That goes to the Manshoon file," she says, and moves on.

She notes whether anyone was killed. She does not labor the point.

## Overview

A standoff in a Field Ward market square — four paid thugs, a cornered Guild dealer, fifteen locals watching. Stop it before it turns lethal.

## Read Aloud

> [!narrative]
> The square is loud before it is visible. When you round the corner, you see the reason: four heavyset men have backed a slight figure and his two companions against a grain stall. A loose ring of locals surrounds them, keeping a careful distance. The barking is coming from a brown dog pressed flat against the far wall, looking uncertain about all of this.

## Summary

The party interrupted an orchestrated confrontation in the Field Ward — four paid thugs cornering a Xanathar Guild dealer in a market square. The neighborhood saw how it ended.
