# Breaking Up the Brawl

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Combat Event begins when Savra Belabranta briefs an Order of the Gauntlet member at Trollskull Manor. It ends when she hears the report at the same door that dusk. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Savra's brief and learn that four hired men mean to corner a Xanathar's Guild dealer in Mudlark Square at noon.
> - Read the crowd of fifteen locals and use its mood to settle the standoff.
> - Send the hired men away with three Intimidation successes, give Pell a graceful exit, or shame the hands before the crowd.
> - Fight the four hired men if the standoff fails, and knock them out instead of killing them.
> - Learn what the man who paid them looks like by three independent routes.
> - Find Pell at the Rusted Anchor in the Dock Ward and hold his debt.
>
> Only Order of the Gauntlet members attend Savra's brief and debrief. Their companions can help with every other part of the mission.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Two days ago a lean human man in his forties paid four idlers 5 gp each to corner Pell at noon on market day. He has grey at the temples, a limp in his left leg, ink on his right fingers and a brown coat mended at the elbow. He works for the Splinter, the Zhentarim's second cell, and he has left the ward.
> - The Splinter wants the Field Ward to watch a Guild dealer humiliated. It has also paid three gang leaders to look away when Guild men are hurt, and Pell has noticed.
> - The four hands are Harlan Crake, Dov Marl, Sef Bannon and Kirrel Stoat. They know they were paid and do not know by whom.
> - Pell sells spirits and stolen goods for the Guild. He is no fighter, and his companions Marn Ottery and Ketta Rowe are there to stop him doing something expensive.
> - Nobody in this Event names Manshoon, so Savra says "the Splinter" and "the other cell" while Pell says "the Zhents".
> - The intermediary cannot be found today. The trail ends at a Trade Ward stationer, and nothing in this mission leads past it.

### The Brief

Savra comes to Trollskull Manor at the third bell on the morning after a member reaches Renown 1 and 2nd level. She knocks once and waits to be asked in, as **Savra at the Door** describes in the **Order of the Gauntlet First Meeting**. She briefs members only, and companions wait in the common room.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra Belabranta stands on the step in her dark riding coat with the silver gauntlet pin at her collar, and she waits until the door is fully open before she lifts her head. She keeps both hands where you can see them as she speaks.
>
> > "Four men were hired to corner a Guild dealer named Pell in Mudlark Square, in the Field Ward, at noon today. The Watch won't go in there for that, and I won't watch it happen. Stop it before anyone dies, and I'll be back at this door at dusk to hear what you did."

> [!social]**The Knight on the Step**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: the Order's knight in Waterdeep, a woman in her late twenties with dark hair worn back and a strong jaw.
>
> Savra pauses before every serious answer and says exactly what she knows. She swears rarely, and only at wrongdoing. She nods once at an honest answer and does not nod at anything else. She has no wish to praise the party before it has done the work.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Pell, the four hired men and the square.
> - The Splinter, and what the Order knows about it.
> - How much force she wants used, and why.
> - What she wants brought back, and when.
>
> She will not discuss the Stone of Golorr or any case beyond this square.

> [!qna]**Who is paying them?**
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly: I don't have a name. The Zhentarim have a second cell in this city that isn't Davil's people, and I call it the Splinter. It has been paying Field Ward gangs to go after the Guild, which is a cheap fucking way to start a war in the streets."

> [!qna]**Why does the Order care about a Guild dealer?**
>
> Savra takes a moment before she answers.
>
> > "Pell sells bad spirits to people who can't afford them, and I'd gladly see him in front of a magistrate. I won't see him dead in a square with a crowd cheering. If the Splinter can make the Guild bleed in the open, the next square will be worse, and the people watching will pay for it."

> [!qna]**Do we have to fight them?**
>
> > "No. I want it stopped, and I don't care who wins. Talk them off if you can, and if you can't, put them down without killing them. Hurt, I can explain. Dead complicates things."

> [!qna]**Will you come with us?**
>
> > "No. The Field Ward knows my coat, and a knight in that square turns a quarrel into a message. You aren't known there, so be firm, be seen, and be gone by one."

> [!qna]**What do you want back, and when?**
>
> > "The square settled by one o'clock, and every member back at this door by dusk. If you learn anything about whoever hired those men, a face or a coat or a name overheard, bring it to me and it goes into the Splinter file."

When the members agree, Savra goes down the step. A member who refuses the brief is not penalized, and Savra does not raise the matter again unless asked. Her deadline is dusk, and she knocks at that hour whether or not the party has done anything.

### The Field Ward at Noon

Mudlark Square lies behind a grain merchant's yard, a cobbled space with a water trough on its east side and a covered grain stall on its west. The party reaches it by noon without a check, because the noise leads them there.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Field Ward smells of cookfire smoke and wet earth, and its unpaved lanes wind between crooked buildings that lean on each other for support. Raised voices carry from the square ahead, in the pitch a crowd uses when it has decided to watch rather than leave. When you reach the corner, four heavyset men have backed a slight man and two companions against a grain stall. About fifteen locals stand in a loose ring around them, and a large brown dog barks at all of it from beside the trough.

> [!gamemaster]**The Square**
>
> - **The hands:** Harlan Crake, Dov Marl, Sef Bannon and Kirrel Stoat stand in a half circle in front of the stall. Crake does the talking and keeps looking at the crowd.
> - **Pell and his companions:** Pell has his back to the grain sacks, and Marn Ottery and Ketta Rowe stand at his shoulders with their hands on their belts and start nothing.
> - **The crowd:** Fifteen locals watch from the edges, among them Brisa Hollin, a fruit seller who has pushed her cart to the ring's north side, and the dog belongs to nobody.

> [!exploration]**Reading the Square**
>
> The party can study the square before it speaks, and the first minute decides what each approach costs.
>
> - **Insight:** Any character who watches the hands and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check sees that Crake is playing to the crowd and is not looking at Pell. The hands are performing, and they will back down if a firm show of force lets them keep their pride. That character's first Intimidation check on the track below has Advantage.
> - **Perception:** Any character who scans the square and makes a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the narrow alley behind the grain stall, which is Pell's way out. The same character sees a grey paper twist tied to Crake's belt with green thread.
> - **On a failure:** The character sees only a loud square. Pell's exit can be learned by asking him, and the twist turns up when a hand is searched.

> [!exploration]**The Crowd**
>
> The crowd stays out of the fight and cheers or jeers from the edges, and the hands take their nerve from it. How the party enters sets the starting point for the track in **The Standoff**.
>
> - **Together and first:** If the party walks into the ring together and a member speaks to the hands before anyone else does, the track starts with 1 success.
> - **Hanging back:** If the party confers at the edge of the crowd or comes in one at a time, the hands play to the gallery and the track starts with 1 failure.
> - **Otherwise:** The track starts with no successes and no failures.

### The Standoff

Three approaches end the standoff, and they can be combined. The party can make a show of force, give Pell a way out, or shame the hands in front of the crowd. Each has a price in what Pell keeps.

> [!exploration]**A Show of Force**
>
> Any character can speak to the hands once per round and make a DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation) check. The party needs three successes before it scores three failures.
>
> - **First failure:** Crake steps closer to Pell and the crowd murmurs.
> - **Second failure:** Marl shoves Ketta Rowe against the grain sacks, and the crowd cheers.
> - **Third failure:** Crake lifts his mace and the fight begins as described in **The Hands Fight Back**.
> - **Three successes:** The hands look at one another and then at the crowd. They go off in four directions, and Crake sits down on the trough's rim at the far side of the square. The crowd drifts back to its business within a few minutes.

> [!exploration]**A Way Out for Pell**
>
> Any character can try to catch Pell's eye without the hands noticing. The character makes a successful DC 12 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check, gesturing to the alley behind the stall and then to the party.
>
> - **On a success:** Pell nods, whispers to his companions and walks into the alley on his own feet. The signal counts as one success on the track, and Pell is out of reach for the rest of the scene.
> - **On a failure:** Crake sees the gesture and takes it as a challenge. The signal counts as one failure on the track.

> [!exploration]**Shaming the Hands**
>
> A character who turns to the crowd and makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check shames the four men for coming four to one against three. The crowd turns on them, and the hands leave at once.
>
> - **The price:** The crowd then stops watching the hands and starts watching Pell, who has been seen needing strangers to save him. He loses face.
> - **On a failure:** The hands jeer back, and the shaming counts as one failure on the track.

> [!social]**The Dealer at the Grain Stall**
>
> Pell (Neutral Evil, Human, he/him) :: a Xanathar's Guild dealer in his mid-thirties, thin and well dressed, who looks as though he has not eaten today.
>
> Pell is frightened and hides it behind sarcasm. He swears freely, he does not thank anyone, and he does not say that he owes a debt. He keeps his hands low and away from his belt, because Marn and Ketta have told him to. He wants to leave the square on his own feet, with the ward still believing he can.
>
> Conversation topics Pell is willing to discuss include:
>
> - Who the four men are and why they came for him.
> - The money behind them, which he says smells like Zhent coin.
> - Where he drinks, if someone asks him.
>
> He will not discuss the Guild's business, Xanathar or the names of his customers.

> [!qna]**Why did they come for you?**
>
> > "Shit, I don't know. I sell what I sell and I've never touched these four. But somebody paid them, because Harlan Crake couldn't afford the mace he's holding. That's Zhent money, and I've seen it in three other squares this tenday."

> [!qna]**Where can we find you?**
>
> Pell straightens his coat and does not look at his companions.
>
> > "The Rusted Anchor, down in the Dock Ward, and I'm there most nights from dusk. Not that I'm inviting anyone, you understand, because I'm only telling you where the beer is."

**Keeping his face.** Pell leaves the square unhurt and with his face if all of the following hold:

- He walks out on his own feet, by the signal or because the hands broke without anyone speaking for him.
- Nobody shames the hands aloud, speaks for him in the crowd's hearing or takes hold of him.
- He loses no hit points.

If all three hold, mark **Pell's Debt Held** when the scene ends. A character who takes hold of Pell loses it, however kind the grip.

### The Hands Fight Back

The fight begins if the party scores three failures, attacks first or refuses the track. The four hands are Harlan Crake, Dov Marl, Sef Bannon and Kirrel Stoat.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Crake lifts his mace and the other three spread out across the cobbles, and the crowd falls back with a roar while the dog bolts for the alley. Behind the hands, Marn Ottery grabs Pell by the collar and drags him toward the grain stall.

> [!hazard]**The Hired Hands**
>
> Crake and Marl use the 2024 **Tough** stat block, and Bannon and Stoat use the **Bandit** stat block, as the party's size requires. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits the roster for three, four and five characters. Pell and his companions use the **Bandit** block and take no part. The crowd and the dog are never counted:
>
> - **Three characters:** Crake as a **Tough**, and Marl, Bannon and Stoat as **Bandits**.
> - **Four characters:** Crake and Marl as **Toughs**, and Bannon and Stoat as **Bandits**.
> - **Five characters:** Crake, Marl and Bannon as **Toughs**, and Stoat as a **Bandit**.
>
> #### The Hired Hands' Tactics
> To begin, the Toughs close on the nearest armed characters with their maces, and the Bandits fire light crossbows from behind the water trough. Each attack goes to a different character where it can.
>
> During combat, the hands:
> - Use Pack Tactics whenever another hand stands within 5 feet of the target, and spread their attacks so that no character is hit by every hand.
> - Turn on Pell only when no character is within reach of them at the start of their turn. A Bandit then shoots at him with a light crossbow (+3 against AC 12, 5 damage), and Pell loses his face and his full hit points.
> - Break and run when two of the four have fallen, or when the crowd turns on them. The crowd turns at the end of round 3, and each Intimidation success scored earlier brings that one round sooner.
> - Surrender if reduced to half their hit points while a character offers them a way out.
>
> A character who reduces a hand to 0 hit points can choose to knock that hand unconscious instead of killing it. The fight ends when the hands break, surrender or are all down. The non-combat route is the track in **The Standoff**, which can still end the fight in any round that a member makes the speech.

If a hand is knocked out, the survivors drag it into the Leaning Cask on the north side of the square, and the crowd turns its attention to the party.

### Who Paid

The hands know they were paid and not by whom. The intermediary's description has three independent routes, and any one of them is enough. A party that follows all three finds that they agree.

> [!gamemaster]**The Description**
>
> Each route gives the same man: lean, in his forties, grey at the temples, with a limp in his left leg, ink on his right fingers and a brown coat mended at the elbow. He spoke to the hands in the Trade Ward accent of a clerk, and he gave no name and took none.

> [!exploration]**Harlan Crake**
>
> Crake sits on the trough's rim if he has not been knocked out. If the party followed the hands, he sits at a table in the Leaning Cask. He talks to anyone who buys him a drink for 1 gp, and a character who presses him instead and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check gets the full description without paying.
>
> - **On a failure:** Crake gives only the limp and the coat. The party can try again after buying him a drink, or follow another route.

> [!qna]**Who hired you?**
>
> Crake turns the cup in his hands before he answers.
>
> > "Some clerk type, two days back. Walked with a limp, left leg, and he had ink all over his fingers. He paid five gold each in grey paper, said Pell would be in the square at noon, and told us what to say. I don't know his name, because he never gave one."

> [!exploration]**Brisa Hollin**
>
> Brisa Hollin sells pears from a cart on the square's north side, and she watched a stranger buy one two days ago. She answers any character who buys a pear for 1 sp and asks politely, with no check. She does not talk to anyone who is rude to her or who is holding a weapon.
>
> - **What she saw:** A limping man in a brown coat paid for a pear with a silver piece and asked her where the four men drank. He wiped the pear on a sleeve that had ink on the cuff. He went north up the lane toward the Trade Ward.

> [!exploration]**The Paper Twist**
>
> Every hand carries the pay he was given: five gold in a twist of grey paper tied with green thread. A character who searches a hand and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds a small stamp on the inside fold that reads "Halbrook and Daughters, Stationers, Trade Ward".
>
> - **At the stationer:** A clerk at Halbrook and Daughters in the Trade Ward remembers a limping customer with inky fingers who buys grey paper by the ream. She gives the same description as Crake and Brisa. The visit takes an hour and needs no check.
> - **On a failure:** The stamp is smudged. The party can search another hand's twist or follow another route.

### The Rusted Anchor

Pell does not seek the party out, but he is at the Rusted Anchor in the Dock Ward most nights from dusk, in a corner booth with his back to the wall. The visit is optional and can happen on any evening after the brawl.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Rusted Anchor is a low drinking house on the Dock Ward quay, with a ship's anchor bolted above the door and the smell of tar and spilled ale in the air. Pell sits in the corner booth with a cup in front of him, and Marn Ottery watches the door from the next table. He looks up as you come in and does not smile.
>
> > "You again. Sit, then, and don't make a show of it, because I owe nobody anything and I don't want this taken for payment."

If **Pell's Debt Held** is marked, Pell gives the list below to the first member who sits down and asks about the gangs. He hands it over as a favor he is not admitting to owing. If it is not marked, he sells the same list for 100 gp.

> [!gamemaster]**Pell's List**
>
> Pell knows which Field Ward gang leaders took the Splinter's money and which did not.
>
> - **Paid:** Gresh Tarn of the Ropewalk Crew, Old Veddic of the Wet Nets and Maudry Quist of the Cinder Lane lads.
> - **Not paid:** Kelso Fiddlewick of the Shard Shunners, whom the Splinter has tried and failed to buy.
> - **What Pell does not know:** Who the Splinter's paymaster is. He recognizes the grey paper twist as Zhent pay and says so, but he has never seen the clerk.

### Renown Opportunities

Savra knocks at dusk and waits to be asked in. Companions sit in the common room, and she asks the members to speak one at a time.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra stands on the step as the lamps are lit along Trollskull Alley, and her coat is damp from the dusk air. She waits until the door is fully open and then looks at each member in turn.
>
> > "Dusk, as I said. Tell me what you did, and tell me plainly, because I'll be writing it down."

Read the branch that matches what the party did. If anyone died in the square, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra listens to the end without moving, and when you finish she is silent for the space of a breath before she answers.
>
> > "Dead complicates things, as I said. I'll write it down as it happened, and I'll find the family if there is one. I'm not blaming you for what the hands did, and I won't pretend I'm pleased."

If nobody died, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra listens to the end with her hands folded behind her back, and when you finish she gives a single nod.
>
> > "Nobody dead, and the crowd saw the Order's people stop it before it started. That is what I asked for."

If a member gives her the intermediary's description or the stationer's mark, mark **Splinter Intel Reported** and read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra takes a small notebook from her coat and writes in a hand that slants to the left. She reads the line back to herself before she looks up.
>
> > "A limp, ink on the right hand, a stationer in the Trade Ward. That goes into the Splinter file, and it gives me more to work with than I had this morning."

If the members have nothing about the intermediary, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra puts the small notebook back in her coat without opening it, and she looks past you into the common room for a moment.
>
> > "Then the file has nothing new, and I'll say so to the people who read it. The Splinter will pay another set of hands before the next tenday is out."

If Pell left the square unhurt and with his face, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra tilts her head when you describe how Pell left the square by the alley behind the grain stall.
>
> > "He walked out on his own feet, and the Guild will notice that a dealer got away from the Order's people with his pride. I'd rather the ward remembered that than a pile of men on the cobbles."

Savra closes the debrief with one sentence about the next job.

> [!readaloud]
>
> She buttons her coat against the evening damp and turns toward the lane that leads out of Trollskull Alley.
>
> > "I'll knock again when I have something that needs you. Get some sleep before then."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 2 base Renown for reporting to Savra at the debrief. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the brawl ends with no death among the hands, Pell and his companions, or the onlookers.
> - **+1 Renown:** a member gives Savra the intermediary's description or the stationer's mark, and **Splinter Intel Reported** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** Pell leaves the square unhurt and with his face, and **Pell's Debt Held** is marked.
>
> Each bonus can be earned only once.

### Aftermath

Crake and the hands avoid Mudlark Square for a tenday. The Splinter's clerk learns that the Order interfered, and he changes his coat and the stationer he uses. Savra adds the party's notes to the Splinter file.

If Pell kept his face, he tells his Guild contacts that the Order's people stopped the hands and walked away. If he lost it, the Guild hears that strangers had to save him, and Pell avoids the party. The Field Ward talks about the standoff for a tenday, and what it says depends on whether anyone bled.

### Concluding the Event

Savra's debrief ends the mission, and the two outcomes below are read by later events.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Splinter Intel Reported** — mark when a member gives Savra the intermediary's description or the stationer's mark at the debrief; read by **Faction Outposts** (unconverted), where Savra's file on the Splinter's intermediaries opens.
> - **Pell's Debt Held** — mark when Pell leaves the square unhurt and with his face; read by **The Shard Shunners**, where Pell's list shows the Shunners unpaid, and by **Faction Outposts** (unconverted), where Pell names the paid gangs.

## Overview

Savra sends the party to a Field Ward market square, where four hired men have cornered a Xanathar's Guild dealer. The party stops them and learns who paid.

## Summary

We stopped four hired men from cornering a Xanathar's Guild dealer named Pell in Mudlark Square. The crowd watched how it ended, and we learned what the man who paid them looks like. Savra put it in the Splinter file, and Pell told us where he drinks. Who was the clerk with the limp, and why does the Splinter want the Guild bleeding in the Field Ward?
