# Breaking Up the Brawl: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 1 and 2nd level, after **Order of the Gauntlet First Meeting**. Companions can help with everything except Savra's brief and debrief, which only members attend.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> The fight in Mudlark Square uses the 2024 **Tough** and **Bandit** stat blocks, with four hired hands at every party size and more Toughs as the party grows. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits the roster for three, four and five characters. A party that sends the hands away with words fights nobody.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

At the third bell Savra Belabranta knocks on the door of Trollskull Manor and tells each member that four men have been hired to corner a Xanathar's Guild dealer in a Field Ward market square at noon. She wants it stopped before anyone dies, and she will be back at the door at dusk to hear what happened.

## Background

Two days ago a lean, limping man in a brown coat walked into the Field Ward and paid four idlers 5 gp each to corner a dealer named Pell. The man works for the Splinter, the Zhentarim's second cell, which is paying Field Ward gangs to look away when Guild men are hurt. The four hands know they were paid and do not know by whom.

Savra learned of it from a cook at a Field Ward soup house, who overheard the plan. The Watch rarely enters the ward, and the Order wants the Field Ward to see that someone is paying attention. Nobody in this mission names Manshoon, and Savra speaks of "the Splinter" and "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The intermediary has grey at the temples, a limp in his left leg, ink on his right fingers and a mended brown coat. He has left the ward, and his trail ends at a Trade Ward stationer.
> - Pell is no fighter, and his two companions, Marn Ottery and Ketta Rowe, are there to stop him doing something expensive.
> - Pell owes the party a favor only if he leaves the square unhurt and with his face, and he will not say so.
> - The Shard Shunners have refused the Splinter's money, and Pell knows it.

## The Brief

Savra briefs the members at the manor door and answers five questions about the square. She asks for it to be settled by one o'clock and for a report at dusk, and she says she will not come herself.

## The Field Ward at Noon

The party arrives at Mudlark Square to find four hired men, Pell, his companions and fifteen locals in a ring. The crowd sets the starting point for the standoff, and a check or two reads the hands and finds Pell's way out.

## The Standoff

The party has three ways to end it. A show of force needs three Intimidation successes before three failures. A signal to Pell lets him walk out through the alley behind the grain stall. Shaming the hands before the crowd works at once, but it costs Pell his face.

## The Hands Fight Back

If the standoff fails, the four hired men fight with maces and light crossbows. They break when two of them fall or when the crowd turns, and any of them can be knocked out instead of killed.

## Who Paid

The intermediary's description comes by three independent routes: the hands' leader, a fruit seller who saw the man two days ago, and the grey paper twist that held the pay. Any one is enough to give Savra something for the Splinter file.

## The Rusted Anchor

Pell is found in the Dock Ward most nights. A party that held his debt receives a list of the Field Ward gang leaders the Splinter has paid and those it has not.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 2 base Renown for reporting to Savra at dusk. Bonuses of +1 each go to members when nobody dies, when the intermediary's description reaches Savra, and when Pell leaves the square unhurt and with his face. Companions gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Splinter Intel Reported** and **Pell's Debt Held** are read by **Faction Outposts** (unconverted), and **Pell's Debt Held** is also read by **The Shard Shunners**. **The Black Viper Investigation** becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 3 and 3rd level.

## Involved Characters

- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet): briefs the members and takes their report, and does not come to the square.
- **Pell** (Xanathar's Guild): a dealer cornered in the square who is no fighter and will take a graceful exit.
- **Marn Ottery** and **Ketta Rowe** (Xanathar's Guild): Pell's companions, who guard him and take no part in a fight.
- **Harlan Crake**, **Dov Marl**, **Sef Bannon** and **Kirrel Stoat**: the four hired hands, of whom Crake leads.
- **Brisa Hollin**: a fruit seller in the square who saw the intermediary two days ago.
- **The intermediary** (the Splinter): an unnamed clerk with a limp, who has left the ward.

## Dangers & Enemies

The four hired hands use the **Tough** and **Bandit** stat blocks. Crake leads them, and they fight only if the standoff collapses, while Pell and his companions use the **Bandit** block and take no part. The crowd of fifteen is the real danger, because it hardens or softens the hands' nerve and decides what Pell keeps.

## Overview

A paid mob has cornered a Xanathar's Guild dealer in a Field Ward market square. The party stops it before anyone dies and learns what the man who paid them looks like.
