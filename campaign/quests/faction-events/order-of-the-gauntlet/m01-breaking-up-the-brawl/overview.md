# Breaking Up the Brawl

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Order of the Gauntlet Mission 1. Available at Renown 0 and character level 2.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet) — the party's Order contact; delivers the mission in person at Trollskull Manor; does not accompany on this one
- **Pell** (Xanathar Guild) — a dealer cornered in the market square; no fighter; willing to follow a lead for a graceful exit
- **Pell's companions (2)** — Guild-affiliated; trying to keep Pell calm; will fight if the standoff collapses

## Dangers & Enemies
Four **Thugs** (2024 *Monster Manual*). Paid by a Manshoon Splinter intermediary already gone from the scene. The crowd of fifteen locals amplifies or deflates their bravado — they respond to audience more than to threat. A dramatic show of force disperses them without combat; hesitation invites escalation.

## Overview
The Manshoon Splinter has been paying Field Ward gang leaders to attack Xanathar Guild members. The City Guard rarely enters the Field Ward, and the violence is building visible momentum. One confrontation — four thugs, a cornered Guild dealer, fifteen onlookers — is underway in a market square when the party arrives.

Savra's brief is simple: stop it before anyone dies, and make clear the Order is paying attention. The party can disperse the thugs through intimidation or combat. Either way, the Field Ward is watching, and how the standoff ends will be remembered.
