# Design Notes: Breaking Up the Brawl

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C's first Order of the Gauntlet mission sends the party to a Field Ward market square where four hired Thugs have cornered a Xanathar's Guild dealer named Pell. Three DC 12 Intimidation successes before three failures disperse the thugs, a DC 12 Dexterity check lets the party signal Pell, and Pell later owes a favor and knows which Field Ward gang leaders the Splinter has paid. The source is a single use-of-force problem with a reward and no debrief. The restored draft kept all of it, added one report line and left Pell's favor without a reader.

## What Changed

The mission is now three beats: reading the square, ending the standoff and finding out who paid. The crowd sets where the Intimidation track starts, and the Insight and Perception checks give the party an edge without settling anything. The signal to Pell is a Sleight of Hand check, and a failed signal counts against the track. Shaming the hands is a third way out that costs Pell his face, which gives the party a real choice about who pays.

The intermediary's description has three independent routes: Harlan Crake, Brisa Hollin and the stationer's stamp on the pay. Pell's favor is now a defined list with named paid and unpaid gangs, read by **The Shard Shunners** because the Shunners are on the unpaid side. The fight uses 2024 **Tough** and **Bandit** blocks. Four Toughs would be Devastating at three characters, so the roster moves weight from Bandits to Toughs as the party grows. Savra states her deadline in the brief and takes the report at the manor door at dusk.

## Departures from the Source

Thugs became Toughs and Bandits. The source's "Manshoon's Zhentarim" is the Splinter, and Savra and Pell never name Manshoon. The "no death" bonus is no longer an outcome, because only the renown total read it. The `+1 Renown` reward in the source is now a base of 2 and three `+1` lines, to fit the shared ladder. The source has Pell found "during Arc E"; the list is now available on any evening after the brawl, and it is sold for 100 gp if the debt was not held.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented here and collision-checked against `campaign/`: Mudlark Square, Harlan Crake, Dov Marl, Sef Bannon, Kirrel Stoat, Marn Ottery, Ketta Rowe, Brisa Hollin, the Leaning Cask, Halbrook and Daughters (Stationers), the Ropewalk Crew (Gresh Tarn), the Wet Nets (Old Veddic) and the Cinder Lane lads (Maudry Quist). Pell and the Rusted Anchor are from the source. None has a voice profile, so all are voiced from the event text.

- Pell echoes Pell Street in **The Archer Above**, Pell Ashford in the Trollskull staff guide, Bram Pell in Harpers **Harpshadow** and Pell Tormar in Harpers **The Sleeping Asset**. The source name is kept.
- **Faction Outposts** is unconverted and is the named reader of both outcomes.
- The **Marcheon** rank event title and the **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** name are assumed. The reference lives in `docs/plans/` until the main session moves it.
