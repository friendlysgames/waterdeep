# The Shard Shunners

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Investigation Event begins when Savra Belabranta knocks at the door of Trollskull Manor and briefs the Order of the Gauntlet members. It ends at the same door at dusk on the day after the party settles the matter. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Savra's brief and hear that a gang is robbing the night guards of the Endshift Tavern.
> - Take the brief by note from a Priest Acolyte instead, if a member is marked **Quietly Reassigned**.
> - Learn the cause from Trevar, the innkeeper, and from Wynne Cobb, the cook who saw it.
> - Find Kelso Fiddlewick through three independent leads.
> - Bring Trevar and Kelso together to settle three terms before the sixth night.
> - Fight the Shard Shunners in the dye-worker's yard if the party forces the matter.
> - Report to Savra and hear what she makes of the way it ended.
>
> Only Order of the Gauntlet members attend Savra's brief and debrief. Their companions can help with everything in between.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Fifty days ago Darrick Holstan, a night guard at the Endshift, found Danika Fiddlewick cutting through the back lane after dark. He took her for a thief, put a knife to her throat and told her he would cut her if he saw her near the tavern again. The blade did not cut her, and she has not left the dye-worker's yard since.
> - Wynne Cobb saw it from the scullery door and told Trevar that Darrick had chased a halfling girl off with a knife. Trevar fired him the next morning, never learned whose child she was and never learned that nobody had told her family.
> - Kelso waited thirty days for the Endshift to say anything. Twenty days ago the Shunners began lifting a few coins from the night guards, and the robberies have been nightly for the last tenday. Giant Rats cover the lane, and nobody has been hurt.
> - Kelso wants Trevar's apology to Danika, a payment of 50 gp toward her care and Darrick named to the Watch. He accepts any two.
> - Two off-duty Watchmen drink at the Endshift, and the Field Ward watch house will put a dozen Guards in the taproom on the sixth night from the brief, which Kelso will not walk into.
> - Kelso is not a Splinter agent but sells what people pay for, and the Splinter is one of his customers. Nobody in this Event learns that, and nobody names Manshoon.
> - Savra does not accompany the party, because the Marcheon benefit covers quests only and not faction missions.

### The Brief

Savra knocks once at dusk on the first evening after a member reaches Renown 5 and 4th level. She waits on the step until someone asks her in and then seats the members at the nearest table, with the companions at the bar. If a member is marked **Quietly Reassigned**, run **When the Brief Comes by Note** instead.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The knock is a single firm rap on the door of Trollskull Manor. Savra Belabranta stands on the step in her dark riding coat, with the silver gauntlet pin at her collar and rain on her shoulders. She comes in when asked, looks over the common room and waits until the Order's members have found chairs.
>
> > "Thank you for seeing me, and I'll tell you plainly what this is. Someone is robbing the night guards at the Endshift Tavern on Endshift Street, and the innkeeper says he has seen giant rats in the lane."

> [!readaloud]
>
> She unfolds a sheet of paper with a street drawn on it in a left-slanting hand, and she lays it flat between the cups.
>
> > "The Field Ward watch house means to put a dozen Guards in that taproom on the sixth night from now. If a wererat gang meets that many spears in a tavern, somebody dies. I want it settled before then, and I want nobody dead."

> [!social]**The Knight at the Table**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: the Order's contact in Waterdeep, who has put a drawn route and a deadline on the table.
>
> Savra is Friendly and formal. She pauses before each serious answer and says "I'll tell you plainly" before she gives it. She does not know why the gang chose the Endshift, and she says so. She swears rarely and only at wrongdoing.
>
> Savra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The Shard Shunners, and what the Field Ward says about them.
> - Why the Order cares about a few stolen purses.
> - The deadline, and what the Watch intends.
> - How she wants the matter ended.
>
> She will not discuss the Splinter, the Cassalanters or anything outside this case.

> [!qna]**Who are the Shard Shunners?**
>
> > "Halflings, and the ward says they're wererats, led by a man called Kelso Fiddlewick. He's careful, and the Watch has never held him. I've never spoken to him myself, so I can't tell you more than that."

> [!qna]**Why does the Order care?**
>
> > "Nobody in the Field Ward owes us anything, and the Watch's way will leave bodies in a tavern. I'd rather the Order be known there for settling a trouble than for breaking one."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us?**
>
> > "Find out why a gang that steals so little has stayed on one tavern for twenty days. Thieves don't stay that long without a reason, so find it and then end it, in whatever way is right, and tell me how you did."

> [!qna]**When?**
>
> > "Before the sixth night, counting tonight as the first. Settle it, and I'll knock at this door at dusk the next day to hear it. If you haven't settled it by the sixth night, come to me anyway."

> [!qna]**What if it comes to a fight?**
>
> Savra takes a moment before she answers.
>
> > "Then fight to stop them and not to kill them. I'd sooner not bury anyone over a few purses. If you can end it without drawing a blade, that is better, and I'll tell you plainly that I think it can be done."

> [!qna]**Will you come with us?**
>
> > "No, this one is yours to carry, and I'll hear how you did it when it's done."

When the members agree, Savra folds the route into the nearest hand and goes out the way she came in.

### When the Brief Comes by Note

A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** receives the Brief from a Priest Acolyte in a plain grey surcoat. The acolyte arrives at dusk and does not wait for a reply, and Savra does not come to the door.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The acolyte hands you a folded paper sealed with the Halls of Justice wax and goes back down Trollskull Alley. The note holds one short paragraph in a neat, upright hand, and a street is drawn on the back of the sheet.
>
> > Someone is robbing the night guards of the Endshift Tavern on Endshift Street in the Field Ward. The Watch means to put a dozen Guards in the taproom on the sixth night, counting tonight as the first. Settle it before then with nobody dead, and report to the east annex of the Halls of Justice at dusk on the day after. B.H.O.

The note answers none of the questions above, and the rest of the Event runs as written. The member reports at the east annex, as **Renown Opportunities** describes.

### The Endshift Tavern

The Endshift Tavern stands on Endshift Street in the Field Ward and never closes, because the people who drink there work in shifts. It lies beside the dye works, a short walk from the cart yard at the end of the street. Trevar is behind the bar at any hour.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Endshift is loud and warm and smells of wet wool and fried onions. Tanners in stained aprons share a long table with dockhands, two men in shirtsleeves with Watch cudgels hanging from their belts, and a pair of hired guards on stools by the door. A heavyset man behind the bar wipes the same mug twice while he watches you cross the room.
>
> > "You're the folk the Order sent, aren't you? I'm Trevar, so come and sit before the night crowd takes the good seats."

> [!social]**The Innkeeper**
>
> Trevar (Neutral, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: the heavyset keeper of the Endshift, who has run out of guards and has started reconsidering things.
>
> Trevar is Friendly and tired, having lost two guards in a tenday with a third leaving at the end of it. He talks quickly when he is nervous and slowly when he is ashamed, and he is slowest of all about Darrick. He wants the robberies to stop, and he will do almost anything that does not cost him the tavern.
>
> Trevar is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The pattern of the robberies, the route and the hour.
> - Darrick Holstan, and why he was dismissed.
> - The name Shard Shunners, which he has heard in the ward.
> - The Watchmen who drink at the Endshift.
>
> He will not talk about Darrick's family until a character has earned it, as described under **Darrick Named**.

> [!qna]**What has been taken?**
>
> > "A few silver from each man, never more than a purse, and always on the street between my door and the cart yard. Same route and same hour every night, a little after midnight. Whoever it is knows my rota better than I do."

> [!qna]**Who is Darrick?**
>
> Trevar looks at the counter before he answers.
>
> > "He worked my nights for two years. I let him go fifty days ago, because Wynne told me he'd chased some halfling girl off with a knife. I thought he'd had a drink too many and took a fright. I never asked who she was."

> [!qna]**Who are the Shard Shunners?**
>
> > "I've heard the name in the ward, that's all, halflings who are wererats if the folk are right. I've seen rats in my lane every night this tenday, big ones, and they don't run from my broom."

> [!qna]**Have you called the Watch?**
>
> > "The two lads in shirtsleeves are Watch, and they've told their sergeant, who's sending a dozen Guards on the sixth night. I didn't ask for that, but I can't stop him."

> [!exploration]**The Cook's Account**
>
> Trevar sends the party to the scullery if anyone asks who else knows about Darrick, and a kitchen boy will also point the way. Wynne Cobb talks freely to anyone who sits on a flour sack and does not take her away from the stove.
>
> Wynne Cobb (Neutral Good, Illuskan Human, she/her) is a broad woman in her fifties with a ladle in her fist. She saw the lane from the scullery door that night and has been unhappy about it since.

> [!qna]**What did you see?**
>
> > "Darrick had a knife at a little halfling girl's neck, and he was telling her to stay out of the lane. He was drunk, and she was frozen. When he let go she ran, and there was a thin red line under her chin. I told Trevar the next morning."

> [!qna]**Did you know her?**
>
> > "No, but she had a dyer's apron on, and she ran toward the yards. I've felt sick about it every night since. If Trevar had known she was cut, he'd have gone to her family himself."

### Finding Kelso

The Shunners are not hiding, and every lead below reaches the same place, the dye-worker's yard, where halfling day laborers gather in the late afternoon. Kelso receives visitors there. Any one lead is enough, and a failed check costs half a day without ending the search. The party has the days between the brief and the sixth night.

> [!exploration]**The Night Guards**
>
> The two hired guards by the door are the ones still on the rota. A character who buys them a round for 2 gp gets the answers below, and no check is needed.
>
> - The robber is a small man with a scar along his jaw, who stands under the lamp at the cart yard and counts the coins before he hands them back out.
> - The rats run east toward the dye works, and the guards have followed them as far as the vats.
> - A halfling lad trails each robbed man down the street and has never once been caught.

> [!exploration]**The Route at Night**
>
> The robberies happen a little after midnight on the street between the tavern door and the cart yard. Any character who keeps watch from the cart yard roof and makes a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check notices a halfling boy trailing the guard on his way home. The boy slips into the dye works afterward. A character who follows him sees him go through the gate of the dye-worker's yard.
>
> - **On a failure:** The boy sees the watcher, nothing is taken that night, and the party tries again the next night.

> [!exploration]**The Dye-Worker's Yard**
>
> Halfling laborers gather at the yard behind the vats in the late afternoon, and they talk only to people who do not press them. Any character who spends three hours there buying nothing and asking nothing reaches Kelso without a check. Any character who asks respectfully for the man with the scarred jaw and makes a successful DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check has word carried to Kelso within the hour.
>
> - **On a failure:** The laborers say nothing. The character can try again the next day, or the party can spend the three hours.

> [!exploration]**Pell's Tip**
>
> Pell, the Xanathar's Guild dealer from **Breaking Up the Brawl**, can be found at the Rusted Anchor in the Dock Ward. If **Pell's Debt Held** is marked, the lead is free, because the Shunners are on his list of gangs the stranger never paid. If it is not marked, Pell sells the lead for 100 gp, the price of his list in **Breaking Up the Brawl**. It needs no check.

If **Pell's Debt Held** is marked and the party visits him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Pell sits in the corner of the Rusted Anchor with his back to the wall and his two companions at the next table. He looks at you, thinks of the market square and sighs.
>
> > "Kelso's lot, is it? They never took the stranger's coin, so they're not on my list. The halflings gather behind the dyers on Endshift Street in the late afternoon, so bring some patience."

If **Pell's Debt Held** is not marked and the party pays the 100 gp, Pell pockets the coin and gives the same directions in a few words, without the sigh.

The party can also learn why Kelso is angry from Trevar and Wynne, and Kelso will confirm it himself. Any two of the three give the party the full story.

### Kelso's Yard

The party reaches the dye-worker's yard in the late afternoon, whichever lead brought it there.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The yard behind the dye works is hung with sheets of wool in red, blue and mustard, and the vats steam in the cold air. Halfling laborers sit on upturned buckets along the wall, and they stop talking when you come through the gate. A compact man with a scar along his jaw rises from a bench by the largest vat.
>
> > "Evening, partner. You'd be the Order lot who've been asking at the Endshift, and you'd better sit down, because you're making the lads nervous."
>
> A halfling girl in a dyer's apron watches from the doorway of the drying shed with a mug in both hands and does not come closer.

> [!social]**The Smallest Gang Leader**
>
> Kelso Fiddlewick (Neutral, Lightfoot Halfling, he/him) :: the cheerful, scarred and businesslike leader of the Shard Shunners, who keeps every bargain and mentions the teeth only once.
>
> Kelso is Indifferent at first and Friendly to anyone who negotiates clearly. He watches hands more than faces, repeats terms back word for word and mentions the lads at the moment someone raises a voice. He swears cheerfully and constantly, and he explains what happened to Danika without performance, dropping the chirpiness when he does.
>
> Kelso is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Why the Shunners have been working the Endshift's guards.
> - What would balance it, and the three things he wants.
> - Trevar, and whether Kelso believes he knew.
> - What the Watch intends, and what the lads will do about it.
> - The job on Trollskull Alley, if a character raises it.
>
> He will not discuss his customers or the Shunners' other business.

> [!qna]**Why the Endshift?**
>
> Kelso waits until the girl in the doorway has gone inside.
>
> > "A big man called Darrick Holstan, who worked nights there, put a knife to my sister's throat and said he'd cut her if she came near the place again, and she's fourteen. Nobody from that tavern ever came to say sorry, so the shit news is that the lads have been collecting."

> [!qna]**What would balance it?**
>
> > "Three things, partner, and I'll take any two. Trevar says sorry to Danika's face, where she can hear it. He pays fifty gold toward her care, because she hasn't slept right in fifty days. And Darrick's name goes to the Watch, with a reason. Contract's a contract, so let's agree it properly."

> [!qna]**Did Trevar know?**
>
> > "I don't think he did. He fired the man, and I believe he thought that was the end of it. But not knowing doesn't pay for anything, and nobody knocked on my door."

> [!qna]**What about the Watch?**
>
> > "The lads know about the dozen Guards. If I walk into that taproom, someone dies, and it won't only be the lads. So I'd like this finished before the sixth night, and I'll be honest, partner, I'd like it finished without fuss."

> [!qna]**The job on Trollskull Alley?**
>
> > "That was Emmek Frewn, and it was a separate contract. I did send a note about the cask on parade day, and no hard feelings, I hope. This one isn't business, it's my sister."

If a character threatens Kelso, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Kelso looks at the speaker for a moment, then at the rooftops. Small shapes crouch along the ridge of every shed and a dozen pairs of eyes catch the lamplight.
>
> > "You can shout at me, or you can remember there's eight of the lads in these walls. Your call, partner."

A character who threatens Kelso and makes a successful DC 17 Charisma (Intimidation) check makes the Shunners put away their crossbows. Kelso then says nothing more until the party returns the next afternoon. A failed check changes nothing, and Kelso remains civil. A threat of any kind leaves **Mediation Path Taken** and **Kelso's Debt** unmarked, even if the terms below are met.

### Three Terms

Kelso asked for three things, and each is a separate conversation with Trevar or with the Watch. The party decides in what order to take them, and Trevar goes along with all three if the party tells him what Wynne saw and what Kelso said.

> [!exploration]**The Apology**
>
> Kelso wants the apology said to Danika, at the yard, where she can hear it. Trevar will not apologise for what he did not know until someone who was there tells him. A character who repeats Wynne's account to him has him agree at once, and no check is needed.
>
> - **Without Wynne's account:** A character who makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check persuades Trevar to come to the yard. On a failure he asks for a night, speaks to Wynne in the morning and then agrees, which costs the party one day.

> [!exploration]**The Payment**
>
> Kelso wants 50 gp toward Danika's care, which covers a healer and a month's lodging away from the ward. The strongbox holds 30 gp after twenty days of robberies, and Trevar offers all of it.
>
> - **The party pays the rest:** A character who adds 20 gp from the party's purse completes the payment.
> - **Trevar pays it all:** A character who makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check persuades him to find the other 20 gp from his own pocket. The character must point out that the raids will cost him far more than 50 gp if they continue.
> - **On a failure:** Trevar pays the 30 gp, and the payment is complete only if the party covers the difference.

> [!exploration]**Darrick Named**
>
> Darrick Holstan lodges above a farrier's shop three streets from the Endshift and works the docks by day, though he does not appear in this Event. The desk sergeant at the Field Ward watch house will hold him for threatening a child with a blade once someone gives a statement.
>
> Trevar admits that Darrick is the nephew of his late wife when an honest character asks about Darrick's family. The statement can come in either of the two ways below.
>
> - **Trevar's statement:** A character who makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check has Trevar go to the watch house in person and name Darrick.
> - **Wynne's statement:** If Trevar refuses, Wynne goes to the desk sergeant with the party, and her account is enough to hold Darrick. She asks for no reward.
>
> The Watch holds Darrick within the day, and you mark this term met once a statement has been given.

### The Settlement

Kelso receives Trevar at the yard in the late afternoon, with the party at his shoulder. Count the terms the party has completed, and read the version below that matches the count.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Trevar comes through the gate of the dye-worker's yard with his hat in his hands. The laborers on the buckets watch him, and the girl in the apron steps out of the shed and stands very straight. Trevar stops in front of her and does not offer a hand.
>
> > "I'm Trevar, from the Endshift. One of my men put a knife to you, and when I threw him out I didn't ask who you were. I should have asked, and I'm sorry, lass. I'll make it right where I can."
>
> Danika looks at him for a long moment and nods once.
>
> > "Okay."

If at least two terms are met and no member threatened Kelso or the Shunners, read or paraphrase the following, mark **Mediation Path Taken** and **Kelso's Debt**, and give Kelso's Debt as described below:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Kelso looks at his sister, then at Trevar, and then at each of you. He takes his time, and the yard waits with him.
>
> > "Balance. The lads will stop tonight, partner, and you did that right, and I don't forget it. Tell your knight the Endshift is quiet."

If at least two terms are met but a member threatened Kelso or the Shunners, the harassment ends and **Mediation Path Taken** is not marked. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Kelso waits until Trevar has gone out of the gate and then keeps his voice level as he speaks to the party.
>
> > "The lads stop tonight because Danika asked me to, and we're square, but don't think I forgot how you came into my yard."

If fewer than two terms are met, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Kelso shakes his head, and his voice goes flat.
>
> > "That's not balance, partner. Come back with another one before the sixth night, or the lads do what the lads do."

The party can make up the missing terms and return to the yard before the sixth night. If the sixth night arrives with fewer than two terms met, the Watch fills the Endshift with Guards at midnight. The Shunners stay away, the robberies stop and two Shunners are arrested on the street, though nobody dies and nothing is balanced.

> [!exploration]**Kelso's Debt**
>
> When **Kelso's Debt** is marked, the party holds one favour from the Shard Shunners, and the debt belongs to everyone who stood in the yard.
>
> - **Asking:** Any character can ask any Shunner in the Field Ward, and the lads need a day's notice. The favour can be used once, in the Field Ward.
> - **Location:** If the party asks about a named person or building in the Field Ward, Kelso gives the street, the door and the hours the person keeps. No check is needed.
> - **Distraction:** If the party names a street where it needs the lads to draw eyes, they make a disturbance for ten minutes from a signal the party chooses. They stop at once if the Watch arrives.
> - **Silence:** In any other case, every Shunner and every listener the Shunners pay in the ward says nothing about the party to anyone for a tenday.
> - **Loss:** A member of the Doom Raiders who gives up Dasher Snobeedle's location in **The Missing Snobeedle** cancels the debt. A party that threatens a Shunner after the settlement also loses it.
>
> When the party asks, mark **Kelso's Debt Spent**.

### If the Party Forces It

This fight happens if a character attacks Kelso or the Shunners in the yard or on the route at night, though Kelso does not want it. He raises a hand at the first drawn weapon and lets the party act first.

> [!hazard]**The Shard Shunners**
>
> Kelso uses the **Wererat** stat block in halfling form, and Brynn Hilltopple, his second, uses it too. The Shunners have no added phases or extra Hit Points. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits the roster for three, four and five characters. Danika is taken indoors at the first blow, and the roster is as follows:
>
> - **Three characters:** Kelso and 2 **Giant Rats** from the dye vats.
> - **Four characters:** Kelso and Brynn Hilltopple.
> - **Five characters:** Kelso, Brynn and 1 **Tough**, a Shunner lookout from the rooftops.
>
> Do not add Wererats beyond these, whatever the size of the party.
>
> #### The Shard Shunners' Tactics
> To begin, Kelso stays in halfling form, steps behind a vat and fires the Hand Crossbow. Brynn takes the shed roof and does the same.
>
> During combat, the Shunners:
> - Spread their attacks across different characters, and no more than two attacks go to the same target in a round.
> - Shift to rat or hybrid form only after a Shunner is reduced to 0 Hit Points, because the Bite works only in those forms. The Bite carries lycanthropy (DC 11 Constitution saving throw), and neither Wererat bites a cursed creature or one at 0 Hit Points.
> - Use the vats, the drying lines and the shed for cover, and the Giant Rats attack together with Pack Tactics.
> - Leave through the gap in the back wall that Kelso scouted when the party arrived.
>
> The fight ends when a Shunner has fallen and a character lowers their weapon and offers terms. It also ends when Kelso is at 30 Hit Points or fewer, and at the end of the fourth round when a Watch patrol turns in. On any of these, Kelso whistles, the Shunners break for the rooftops and nobody pursues.
>
> A character who lowers their weapon and makes a successful DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check ends the fight at once. On a failure, Kelso stands the Shunners down anyway on any offer of terms, and the non-combat route is the settlement above.
After the fight, Kelso says "That's enough, partner" and sends word the next day that the lads will stop. The harassment ends and neither **Mediation Path Taken** nor **Kelso's Debt** is marked. The party can still bring Trevar and the Watch into it for the payment and for Darrick, which earns the third bonus. If anyone dies in the fight, the first bonus is lost.

### Renown Opportunities

Savra knocks at dusk on the day after the matter is settled, or on the seventh day if it never is. The members take the table in the common room and the companions go to the bar.

A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** reports to Savra at the east annex of the Halls of Justice at the same dusk hour instead. She does not come to the manor door for that member, and she reads the same branches below.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra comes in out of the cold with her coat damp at the shoulders and waits for the members to sit. She takes the chair across from you and looks at each of you in turn before she speaks.
>
> > "Report. Is the Endshift quiet, and how did you get it that way?"

If **Mediation Path Taken** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra listens to the whole account without moving, and when you reach Darrick she lays her hand flat on the table.
>
> > "A man put a knife to a child and the Watch heard nothing for fifty days. That's a fucking disgrace, and I'll tell you plainly I'm glad you found it. You made two sides sit down, and you did it without a blade."

If the harassment ended but **Mediation Path Taken** is not marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra listens to the account and does not nod.
>
> > "The Endshift is quiet, and that's what I asked for. You did it the hard way, and it'll be remembered in the Field Ward as the Order's way. Next time I'd prefer you asked what the thing was about first."

If the sixth night passed with the matter unsettled, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra hears the account out and her jaw tightens.
>
> > "The Watch filled that taproom, and two halflings are in a cell. Nobody died, and that's the best I can say. I wanted it settled before the Guards got there, and it wasn't."

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Savra at the debrief, and companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the harassment ends and nobody dies in the process.
> - **+1 Renown:** mediation succeeds and **Mediation Path Taken** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** Danika's payment is made and Darrick is named to the Watch.

### Aftermath

Trevar keeps his tavern and, if the settlement held, hires two new guards within the tenday. Wynne Cobb stays on in the scullery. Danika leaves the dye-worker's yard for her aunt's house with the payment in her pocket, and she sleeps through the night for the first time in fifty days.

Darrick Holstan, if named, spends a month in the cells and loses his post on the docks. Kelso remembers how the party came to his yard. If the party mediated, the Shard Shunners speak well of the Order in the Field Ward. A halfling there does not turn away from a gauntlet pin. If the party fought, the Shunners are civil and say nothing more.

Trevar never learns about Kelso's other business, and if a Doom Raiders member in the party later meets Kelso in **The Missing Snobeedle**, he greets them according to the outcome marked here.

### Concluding the Event

Savra's debrief ends the mission, and the outcomes below are read by later events.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
