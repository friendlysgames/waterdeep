# The Shard Shunners

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social/Exploration Event begins when Savra Belabranta briefs the party at Trollskull Manor. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive the mission brief from Savra in person and ask her what she knows.
> - Arrive at the Endshift Tavern and interview Trevar, who knows more than he realizes.
> - Navigate the Field Ward's informal social networks to find Kelso Fiddlewick.
> - Hear Kelso's side of the story and understand what he means by balance.
> - Propose any version of a resolution — mediation, compensation, or direct confrontation.
> - Facilitate a genuine apology from Trevar if the party chooses the mediation path.
>
> #### Notes on the Combat Option
>
> Combat is a failure state in this mission, not a primary path. Three **Wererats** in halfling form require silver or magic weapons for sustained damage and can be dispersed with a **DC 17 Charisma (Intimidation) check** — a high bar that reflects how much it takes to push Kelso off a position he is not wrong about. Savra's report notes which outcome the party chose.

> [!gamemaster]**Background**
>
> A former Endshift Tavern guard named **Darrick Holstan** put a blade to the throat of **Danika Fiddlewick**, fourteen years old, and told her if he saw her near the Endshift again he would cut her. **Trevar** dismissed Darrick for excessive behavior — he knew something had happened, not what. **Kelso Fiddlewick**, Danika's brother and the Shard Shunners' leader, has been taking money from Trevar's guards because no other price has been paid. He will not stop until something balances.
>
> Kelso is not wrong. He has not defined what balance means. The party can propose their own version.

### The Brief

Savra arrives at Trollskull Manor. Her voice when she is being mild is distinctive: careful and flat, the tactical picture making something distasteful.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra takes the offered seat and sets her hands on the table.
>
> > "Guards at the Endshift Tavern in the Field Ward are being robbed nightly. The innkeeper reports giant rats in the alleys. The Shard Shunners — a halfling wererat gang. They detest silver; that's how they got the name."
>
> She pauses before the next part.
>
> > "The Field Ward is not full of people who owe the Order goodwill. If you can solve this without killing anyone, the outcome will be better for everyone, including us. That's as far as I've gotten."

> [!social]**On Assignment**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Neutral Good, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: a knight of the Order of the Gauntlet, noting the method as carefully as the result.
>
> Savra is honest about the limits of what she knows. She has the surface facts — nightly robberies, a halfling wererat gang, an innkeeper with a problem — and a working instinct that this is not simple opportunism, because the Shard Shunners are working the same route at the same time every night, which takes patience. She has not had time to find out why.
>
> She will not accompany the party on this one. She notes what the party does and how they do it in her Order report.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss before the party leaves include:
> - What she knows about the robberies and the Shard Shunners.
> - Why the Order is watching the Field Ward.
> - What she hopes the party brings back.
>
> Specific dialogue for Savra on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**What do we know about the gang?**
>
> > "They're called the Shard Shunners because they hate silver. Wererat gang, halfling, working out of the Field Ward. They've been robbing the same guard on the same route for three nights running, which means they know the layout and they've been watching. I don't know why they started."

> [!qna]**Why does the Order care about a tavern robbery?**
>
> > "I care because the Field Ward is watching how the Order handles things. If we come in and put down a halfling gang without finding out why they started, that's what the ward remembers. I'd rather they remember something else."

> [!qna]**Will you come with us?**
>
> > "No. I'll hear how it goes when you're done."

> [!qna]**The pay?**
>
> She meets it without hesitation.
>
> > "The Order doesn't settle invoices. What I give you is my account of what you did, and that account matters in this city."

Once the party accepts, proceed to **The Endshift Tavern**.

### The Endshift Tavern

A working-class inn that never closes because its clientele works shifts. The Field Ward smells of cookfire smoke and damp earth, and the Endshift sits at the end of an unpaved block that turns to mud in the rain.

> [!readaloud]
> The Endshift Tavern sits at the end of an unpaved block in the Field Ward, wedged between a cooperage and a dye-works, with lantern light coming through frosted windows and the faint sound of people moving around inside at an hour when most of the city has gone quiet. A heavyset Illuskan man is standing in the doorway when you arrive, arms folded, watching the alley beside the building with the resigned focus of someone who has been watching it for six tendays and learned nothing. He spots you coming and straightens up.
>
> > "You're here about the rats. Good." He steps back to let you in. "I've had two guards quit this tenday. One more goes at the tenday's end. Something's watching, and it's patient."

> [!social]**The Innkeeper**
>
> Trevar (unaligned, Illuskan human, he/him) :: an Endshift Tavern innkeeper who dismissed the guard responsible for the incident without understanding the full story; capable of genuine amends when he does.
>
> Trevar is cooperative and increasingly willing to revisit assumptions he made six tendays ago. He fired Darrick Holstan for threatening a halfling and thought that was the end of it. He is beginning to connect things he did not connect at the time.
>
> What Trevar knows and will share freely:
> - The pattern is deliberate — same route, same time — which means someone is watching.
> - He has heard the name Shard Shunners in the ward.
> - Darrick was fired six tendays ago for threatening "a halfling kid."
>
> A character who asks directly about Darrick and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check** draws out the full story: Trevar found Darrick in the alley with a blade at a young halfling's throat, told her to stay away. He fired Darrick on the spot and has been wondering since whether he missed something.

> [!qna]**About Darrick?**
>
> Trevar is quiet for a moment.
>
> > "I fired him six tendays ago. Found him in the alley with a blade at a halfling kid's throat, telling her not to come back. I thought he was being paranoid — someone's cousin, someone who wouldn't leave the alley. I should've asked more questions." He looks at the empty alley. "I'm asking them now."

### Finding Kelso

The Field Ward's informal social networks: the right corner stalls, an unnamed bar three streets east, the dye-worker's yard where halfling day laborers gather late afternoon. A **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check**, or patient engagement across several hours without pressing, reaches Kelso.

> [!readaloud]
> You find him in the dye-worker's yard, a compact halfling with a scar along the jawline, sitting with his back to a wall and watching you come. He does not stand up. He waits until you are close enough that he does not have to raise his voice.
>
> > "I was wondering when someone would come. It's been six tendays. Figured the Order would be here sooner."
>
> He explains what happened to Danika without performance. His position is clear enough: Trevar didn't know. He believes that. It doesn't change what happened. He wants balance, and he hasn't decided what that means, because nobody has offered him anything to decide between.

> [!social]**The Shard Shunners' Leader**
>
> Kelso Fiddlewick (use **Wererat** stat block in halfling form; neutral, halfling, he/him) :: compact, scarred along the jawline, alert and quick to read a room before committing to it.
>
> Kelso is not looking for blood. He has been taking money from Trevar's guards because it is the only price anyone has paid, and he will keep doing it until something better is offered. He lays out the facts plainly: his sister, a blade, a threat. Trevar fired the guard and thought that was enough. It was not enough.
>
> He does not name what balance means. The party can propose their own version.
>
> Conversation topics Kelso is willing to discuss include:
> - What Darrick did and what it cost.
> - What he wants from Trevar.
> - Whether he would accept a genuine apology and a payment toward Danika's care.

> [!qna]**What happened?**
>
> > "Six tendays ago, your guard put a blade to my sister's throat. Danika. Fourteen. Told her if he saw her near the place again he'd cut her." He holds your eyes. "Trevar fired him. Good. Doesn't make Danika's throat whole."

> [!qna]**What would balance look like?**
>
> He is quiet for a moment.
>
> > "Trevar didn't know. I believe that. But what happened, happened under his roof. I want him to say that out loud to someone who matters. And something toward Danika's care — she doesn't sleep well." He pauses. "If he'll do that in front of you, I'll take it."

### Resolution

**Mediation:** Bring the situation to Trevar, who did not know the full story. His reaction when he hears it is a long silence, and then: "I'd pay something. And say what needs saying." If the party facilitates a meeting where Trevar makes a genuine apology and a modest payment toward Danika's care, Kelso accepts.

If the mediation succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Trevar and Kelso face each other in the Endshift's back room. Trevar speaks first.
>
> > "I didn't know what he did. I should've asked. I'm sorry — not for firing him, for letting it sit at that." He sets a purse on the table between them. "For Danika's care. It won't be enough. I know that."
>
> Kelso is quiet for a moment. Then he picks up the purse.
>
> > "Balance," he says. That is all.

**Direct confrontation:** Three **Wererats** (2024 *Monster Manual*) in halfling form. **DC 17 Charisma (Intimidation)** to disperse without fighting; silver or magic weapons required for sustained damage. Ends the harassment. Kelso notes the party's affiliation and files it in the Field Ward's informal memory. He does not forget.

> [!gamemaster]**Savra's Report**
>
> Savra names the mediation path in her Order report as an example of the right approach. A direct confrontation outcome earns a one-line acknowledgment: the harassment stopped. Both outcomes earn the base renown if no one dies in the resolution.

### Concluding the Event

The Field Ward watched this one too. The outcome — who paid what price, how the Order's agents handled it — is in the ward's informal record whether or not it is in the Order's.

## Overview

A halfling wererat gang is robbing guards at a Field Ward inn every night. The reason matters more than the crime.

## Summary

We investigated nightly guard robberies at the Endshift Tavern, traced them to the Shard Shunners — a halfling wererat gang led by Kelso Fiddlewick — and learned what was driving the retaliation. The resolution, whatever it was, left a mark on the Field Ward's informal reputation.
