# The Shard Shunners

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Order of the Gauntlet Mission 3. Available at Renown 4 and character level 4.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 4th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet) — briefs the party in person at Trollskull Manor; does not accompany; notes the method in her Order report
- **Kelso Fiddlewick** (Shard Shunners) — the gang's leader; halfling wererat; guarded; has cause; will accept a genuine resolution
- **Danika Fiddlewick** — Kelso's sister, fourteen; was threatened by the fired guard; the reason the harassment started
- **Trevar** (Endshift Tavern) — the innkeeper; dismissed the guard responsible without knowing the full story; capable of genuine amends
- **Darrick Holstan** (former Endshift guard, dismissed) — the cause of the original incident; not present in the mission

## Dangers & Enemies
Three **Wererats** (2024 *Monster Manual*) in halfling form if combat becomes necessary. Silver or magic weapons required for sustained damage. The Shard Shunners are not looking for a fight — Kelso wants balance, not blood. Combat is a failure state.

## Overview
The Shard Shunners — a halfling wererat gang — have been robbing guards at the Endshift Tavern in the Field Ward nightly. What looks like opportunistic crime is retaliation: a former guard named Darrick Holstan put a blade to the throat of Kelso Fiddlewick's fourteen-year-old sister and told her not to come back. Trevar fired Darrick for excessive behavior without knowing the full story.

Kelso has been taking money from Trevar's guards because no other price has been paid. He will not stop until something balances. The party can facilitate a genuine resolution — Trevar makes amends for what happened under his roof — or end the harassment by force. Savra notes the difference in her Order report.
