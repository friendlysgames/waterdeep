# The Shard Shunners: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 5 and 4th level, after **The Black Viper Investigation**. Companions can help with everything except Savra's brief and debrief, which only members attend.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 4th-level characters.*
>
> The mission is an investigation and a negotiation. Kelso Fiddlewick, a **Wererat** in halfling form, fights only if the party forces the matter, with **Giant Rats**, his second Brynn Hilltopple (also a **Wererat**) or a **Tough** added by party size. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits the roster for three, four and five characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Order of the Gauntlet members who report to Savra.

## Hook

At dusk Savra Belabranta knocks once at the door of Trollskull Manor and waits to be asked in. Someone is robbing the night guards of the Endshift Tavern in the Field Ward, and the Watch will put a dozen Guards in the taproom on the sixth night, so Savra wants the reason found and the robberies ended before then, with nobody dead.

## Background

Fifty days ago a night guard named Darrick Holstan found a halfling girl cutting through the back lane of the Endshift Tavern. He took her for a thief, put a knife to her throat and told her he would cut her if he saw her near the tavern again. The girl was Danika Fiddlewick, fourteen years old, and her brother Kelso leads the Shard Shunners, a gang of halfling wererats in the Field Ward.

Trevar, the Endshift's innkeeper, fired Darrick the next morning for frightening a halfling child. He never learned whose child she was, and nobody carried word to her family. Kelso waited thirty days for someone at the Endshift to say anything. Nobody did, so twenty days ago the Shunners began lifting purses from the tavern's night guards, and the robberies have been nightly for the last tenday.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Kelso hurts nobody and takes a few coins at a time. He will not stop until something balances, and he accepts any two of three terms: Trevar's apology to Danika, a payment toward her care and Darrick named to the Watch.
> - Trevar is not a villain, and he fired Darrick, believed it was enough and is now ashamed of how little he asked.
> - The Field Ward watch house plans to fill the Endshift's taproom with Guards on the sixth night. Kelso will not walk into that, and a fight with the Watch would end with dead halflings.
> - Kelso is not a Splinter agent. He sells what people pay for, and the Splinter is one of his customers. Nobody in this mission learns that or names Manshoon.

## The Brief

Savra briefs the members at the common room table while the companions wait at the bar. She gives the deadline, hands over a drawn route and says she does not know why a gang would pick one tavern for twenty days, and she will not come with the party. A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** gets the Brief by note from a Priest Acolyte instead, and the debrief moves to the east annex of the Halls of Justice.

## The Endshift Tavern

Trevar explains the pattern, which is the same route at the same hour every night, and the cook Wynne Cobb tells the party what she saw Darrick do and Trevar never heard.

## Finding Kelso

Three independent leads reach the dye-worker's yard where Kelso receives visitors: the night guards' account, a night watch on the route and the yard itself. Pell at the Rusted Anchor gives a fourth lead, free if **Pell's Debt Held** is marked and for 100 gp otherwise.

## Kelso's Yard

Kelso explains the cause without performance and names three terms, of which he accepts any two. A threat makes him mildly clear about how many of the lads are in the walls.

## Three Terms

Each term is a separate conversation. Trevar must apologise to Danika in person, pay toward her care and agree to name Darrick, and the party can make up what he lacks.

## The Settlement

Trevar comes to the yard and speaks to Danika. Kelso counts the terms met and either agrees that the matter is balanced or sets the party another try before the sixth night.

## If the Party Forces It

A fight is possible if a character attacks Kelso or the Shunners. Kelso yields when he is hurt, a Shunner falls or a Watch patrol turns in, and the harassment ends without the respect mediation earns.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 3 base Renown for reporting to Savra at the debrief. Each eligible member also gains the following bonuses when the party meets their conditions:

- **+1 Renown:** the harassment ends and nobody dies.
- **+1 Renown:** mediation succeeds and **Mediation Path Taken** is marked.
- **+1 Renown:** Danika's payment is made and Darrick is named to the Watch.

Companions who are not Order of the Gauntlet members gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Mediation Path Taken** is read by **The Missing Snobeedle** (Doom Raiders), where Kelso greets the party as mediators, and **Kelso's Debt** is a one-use favour that **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court** or **The Shrine on Aveen Street** may call. **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court** becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 8 and 5th level. This mission also reads **Quietly Reassigned**, which **The Tithe** marks.

## Involved Characters

- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet): briefs and debriefs the members and does not accompany the party.
- **Kelso Fiddlewick** (Independent): leader of the Shard Shunners, a **Wererat** in halfling form who wants balance.
- **Brynn Hilltopple** (Shard Shunners): Kelso's second, a **Wererat** who fights beside him if the party forces the matter.
- **Danika Fiddlewick** (Independent): Kelso's sister, fourteen, who was threatened with a knife and is owed an apology.
- **Trevar** (Independent): the Endshift's innkeeper, who fired the guard and can make amends.
- **Wynne Cobb** (Independent): the Endshift's cook, who saw what Darrick did.
- **Darrick Holstan** (Independent): the dismissed guard, who is not present and is named to the Watch.
- **Pell** (Xanathar's Guild): a dealer who points the way to the yard, free if his debt is held and for 100 gp otherwise.

## Dangers & Enemies

No combat unless a character attacks Kelso or the Shunners. Kelso is a **Wererat** in halfling form, with two **Giant Rats** at three characters, Brynn at four, and Brynn and a **Tough** at five. The deadline is the real danger, because the Watch's trap ends with dead halflings.

## Overview

A halfling gang is robbing the night guards of a Field Ward tavern. The party finds out why and decides how to settle it.
