# Design Notes: The Shard Shunners

## Cross-Faction NPC Overlap

***Kelso Fiddlewick.*** Kelso also appears in Doom Raiders Mission 3. DR-M3's Aftermath explicitly flags the overlap: the Doom Raiders encounter with Kelso approaches the Shard Shunners from a different angle and establishes a separate relationship thread with the gang. If the party has run DR-M3 before this mission, Kelso already knows them. His posture on first contact should reflect whatever happened in that encounter — he may be more willing to hear the party out, or more guarded, depending on how DR-M3 resolved.

The two missions approach Kelso for different reasons and do not conflict mechanically. The GM should acknowledge prior contact without forcing a combined resolution.
