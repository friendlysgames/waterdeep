# Design Notes: The Shard Shunners

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C gives a robbery at the Endshift Tavern, a wererat gang with a grievance, and three ways to end it: a fight, a threat or a mediated apology. Trevar, Kelso, the dye-worker's yard, Darrick Holstan and fourteen-year-old Danika all come from it. The mediation line "I'd pay something. And say what needs saying" is kept, as is Kelso's "Balance." The restored draft made mediation one apology and one modest payment, and it let force pay the same base Renown as talking.

## What Changed

Mediation is now a three-term balance: Trevar's apology to Danika, 50 gp toward her care and Darrick named to the Watch. Kelso accepts any two, so the party makes real choices, and each term is its own conversation with a fallback. Wynne Cobb is new, because Trevar cannot apologise for what he never heard and the party needs a second witness. The Watch is given a stake, since off-duty Watchmen drink at the Endshift, and that produces a deadline in Savra's mouth: a dozen Guards in the taproom on the sixth night.

The three leads to Kelso are the guards' account, a night watch on the route and the yard itself, and Pell's tip from **Breaking Up the Brawl** is a fourth when **Pell's Debt Held** is marked. The cause has three witnesses (Trevar, Wynne, Kelso), and any two give the full story. **Kelso's Debt** now has a defined spend: one use in the Field Ward, as a location, a distraction or silence, chosen by what the party asks. The fight uses the Wererat roster from the mechanics reference at Bruising, with no silver rule and no Regeneration. Savra briefs and debriefs at Trollskull Manor and does not accompany the party.

## Departures from the Source

The source pays force and mediation the same Renown. Here mediation earns two of the three bonuses and the favour, and force earns the Renown for ending the robberies and nothing more. Kelso is not a Splinter agent. The Splinter is only one of his customers, and the mission never says so to the players. Danika is a victim and stays out of the gang's operations, which departs from Kelso's Notable Figures page.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Wynne Cobb (the cook) is invented, and a grep of `campaign/` found no collision.
- Trevar's alignment (Neutral), Wynne's (Neutral Good) and the late wife's nephew link to Darrick are invented. Darrick's surname Holstan comes from the first draft and the source gives only "Darrick Holstan".
- Brynn Hilltopple comes from **The Missing Snobeedle**, where she is Kelso's second and a **Wererat** (a halfling woman with a hand crossbow). Her alignment is not given there.
- Kelso's Notable Figures page says Danika "works alongside him in the gang's operations", and it lists **The Black Viper Investigation** and **The Doppelganger Auditions** as Featured in. Both need a pass. The Notable Figures page for Savra says Neutral Good and Tethyrian, while this mission uses Lawful Good per the brief.
- The Marcheon benefit, "the Halls of Justice priests treat a cursed member at no cost" and the cure within a day are table rulings. Lycanthropy removal in the 2024 rules was not verified.
- The stale Kelso Splinter-intelligence line in the Emerald Enclave design notes and the out-of-scope log should be deleted.
