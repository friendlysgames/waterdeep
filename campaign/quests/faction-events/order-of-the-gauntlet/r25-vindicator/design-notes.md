# Design Notes: Vindicator

## What the Source Gave

The restored draft gave Vindicator as a run of Savra's speeches. It had an inquiry that needed no real exhibit, a Strike Team described as "paladins and clerics", a +1 weapon with no list and a history line about dead Vindicators. It named the Whitehawk dossier as enough to name Victoro Cassalanter. Each benefit is now a per-member procedure with a contact, a notice, a limit, a refusal list and a loss rule.

## What Changed

The inquiry needs a written exhibit that a magistrate would read, such as the Aveen Street ledger, so the Whitehawk dossier can no longer carry it. The six targets follow the mechanics reference, and each row says what the report finds and what it cannot say. Savra writes "devil worship" only after the party has met a devil. The Strike Team is three Knights with the ally Power figures from the mechanics reference, and the fighting count is the player characters minus one, to a cap of three. The weapon comes from a fixed table of nine. The history line is now a Book of the Fallen with four fixed entries, and two of the Knights stand as witnesses.

## Departures from the Source

The rank no longer mentions the Lords unless **Order Recognized** is marked, because **The Writ and the Weight** is the mission that puts the Order before them. The Strike Team's refusal list says "a magistrate" where the mechanics reference says "a Lord", for the same reason. The summons arrives at noon on the day after the threshold, matching the other Order rank events, and replaces the formal note a day ahead. The weapon's provenance is one cache from a diabolist's cellar, and the draft's second origin, a devil's effects, is dropped.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Dame Rhosyn Cullane, Sir Anselm Greave and Sir Merrick Tollwood (the Strike Team), and Sir Aldous Fenmore, Dame Joslyn Wardle and Sir Hadrian Strake (the dead Vindicators). Each was grep-checked against `campaign/` with no collision. "Anselm" appears only in `docs/plans/order-of-the-gauntlet-research/05-consistency-audit.md` as a suggested armory name that no event used. Rhosyn is voiced from the event text, since no profile exists. The years in the Book fall inside the fifteen-year span, and the cause of each death is invented.

For the out-of-scope log:
- `items.json` has no generic +1 weapon, so the +1 bonus, the Uncommon rarity and the nine weapon names are unverified. The bonus text is the 2024 rule as recalled, not read from the data.
- The mechanics reference calls the safe house's ward "Trade Ward". This event writes "Trades Ward", the form used in the Marcheon briefings.
- The Brisenne row uses the alias from the **The Black Viper Investigation** draft, and the Gysheer row reads **Intelligence Shared**, which that mission's final text must still write.
- The Factions Guide Vindicator row says "findings admissible before the Lords" and "paladins and clerics", and the Players' Guide and GM Guide rows match it.
- **Righteous Hand** names the same cache weapon in its draft, and its provenance line should follow this event's.
