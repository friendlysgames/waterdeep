# Vindicator

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event fires at the first natural pause after one party member's OotG Renown reaches 25. Savra sends a written summons one day in advance — formal, in her hand, bearing the Halls of Justice seal. The meeting takes place in the main chamber, not the antechamber. Two Order knights serve as witnesses. In this Event:
>
> - Savra delivers the Vindicator promotion with two knights present as formal witnesses.
> - She explains the investigation mandate: the Order can now open an official inquiry into a named individual or organization, with findings admissible before the Lords of Waterdeep.
> - She introduces the Strike Team by name: three paladins and clerics (use the **Knight** stat block) available for one operation per quest.
> - She presents a *+1 weapon* from the Order's seized reserves as a permanent gift.
> - She tells the character three of the four previous Waterdhavian Vindicators are dead. She tells them before they find out another way.

### The Summons

The note arrives at Trollskull Manor the morning before the meeting. Savra's handwriting — economical, slightly left-slanting — on paper with the Halls of Justice seal.

*Halls of Justice. Main chamber. Tomorrow, third bell past noon. Formal business.*

No explanation. No softening. The character who has been paying attention will know that formal business means a record is being kept.

### The Chamber

The main chamber is used for hearings before the Lords, sworn witness depositions, and formal oaths. Three of the four lanterns are lit. Two Order knights stand to either side of the dais — they don't introduce themselves before the ceremony begins.

Savra stands at the center.

> > "Vindicator is the rank at which the Order formally commits resources to your investigation. I'm asking the two witnesses here to acknowledge that."

The knights nod, in turn.

### The Investigation Mandate

At Vindicator, the character can request that the Order open a formal inquiry into a named individual or organization: diabolism, serious criminal conspiracy, or comparable offenses. The inquiry produces findings admissible before the Lords of Waterdeep.

> > "One inquiry at a time. I won't file against someone on suspicion alone — the evidence threshold is real. But at Vindicator, you've demonstrated judgment. When you bring me a name, I'll take it seriously."

She means the investigation will not be blocked by political pressure if she can prevent it. She doesn't promise it can't be blocked. There are Masked Lords on this matter whose interests she cannot fully map.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Filing the Investigation
>
> The character names a target — an individual or organization — and brings Savra enough predicate to justify the filing. The Cassalanter dossier received at Whitehawk is sufficient predicate to name Lord Victoro Cassalanter. The inquiry runs independently of the party's operations and produces a written report of findings within two tendays. Whether the Lords act on those findings depends on the political climate at the time of filing.

### The Strike Team

Savra gestures to the two witnesses.

> > "Vindicator rank includes access to a Strike Team. Three paladins and clerics, assembled for specific operations. These two are on the current rotation."

The knights give their names. They are professional; they are not trying to make an impression. They've been briefed on the character's operations. For a designated mission — one per quest — they operate under the character's tactical direction.

> > "They'll follow your judgment inside an operation. They won't follow an order they consider clearly unrighteous. That's not a limitation I'll apologize for."

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Strike Team
>
> Three Order members (use the **Knight** stat block, 2024 *Monster Manual*). Available for one operation per quest, designated in advance. They follow the character's tactical lead in good faith and will not execute orders they judge clearly unrighteous. Treat this constraint as characterization, not a mechanical limit — in practice, it almost never comes up against a party operating in good faith.

### The Weapon

Savra steps down from the dais and presents the character with a *+1 weapon* from the Order's seized reserves.

> > "This came out of a cache from a diabolist estate. We've held it for three years. I've been waiting for someone to put it in the field who'd actually use it."

She holds it until the character takes it.

> > "Yours. Not borrowed."

The weapon is a permanent grant. The character decides what type, within reason — a weapon suited to how they fight; Savra selected for the character's fighting style when she pulled it from the armory.

### What Vindicator Means

> > "Vindicator is the rank where the Order stops asking whether you're serious. Up to now, I've been asking you to help. From here, we're working together."

She closes the file she was holding and hands it to one of the knights to take away. The chamber is formal by design; she makes it briefly personal.

> > "Three of the four people who've held Vindicator in Waterdeep in the last fifteen years are dead. Two of them in operations against diabolism. I'm telling you that because I'd rather tell you than have you find out another way."

She lets that land.

> > "The work doesn't get safer at this rank. It gets more consequential."

### Concluding the Event

#### Vindicator Reached: True / False

**True:** The character holds OotG Vindicator rank. The formal investigation mandate is active, the Strike Team is available, and the *+1 weapon* is granted. Read by the **Righteous Hand** rank event (r50-righteous-hand), which checks this flag before its opening scene fires.

## Overview

Savra summoned the character to the main chamber of the Halls of Justice for the Vindicator promotion: a formal record, two knight-witnesses, a mandate to investigate named individuals with findings admissible before the Lords, a Strike Team of three paladins and clerics available per quest, and a *+1 weapon* from the Order's seized reserves. Before closing, she noted that three of the four previous Waterdhavian Vindicators died on operations.

## Summary

The character was promoted to Vindicator at the Halls of Justice with two Order knights as witnesses. The promotion carries a mandate to open formal investigations into named individuals, a Strike Team of three knights for one operation per quest, and a *+1 weapon* as a permanent gift. Savra noted the rank's history before the ceremony ended.
