# Vindicator

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member first reaches Renown 25. That usually happens after **The Shrine on Aveen Street** or **The Writ and the Weight**, and bonus Renown can bring it sooner. Savra Belabranta names the rank in the east annex of the Halls of Justice, with two Knights of the chapter standing as witnesses. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Name one target at a time for a written inquiry, and receive the Order's report on it in two tendays.
> - Call three Knights of the chapter to fight beside the party in one operation during each quest.
> - Choose a +1 weapon from the Order's cache and keep it for good.
> - Hear the Book of the Fallen read aloud, and decide whether to sign it.
>
> Only the member is addressed. Companions who are not Order of the Gauntlet members wait in the nave of the Halls and gain nothing.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The three Knights of the Strike Team are Dame Rhosyn Cullane, Sir Anselm Greave and Sir Merrick Tollwood. They serve the Waterdeep chapter under Savra and report to her after every operation.
> - Dame Rhosyn is the fourth Vindicator named in the Book of the Fallen, and the only one still alive. She and Sir Anselm stand as the witnesses, and Sir Merrick is on duty at the Field Ward gate.
> - The inquiry is a clerk's job done with care. Savra reads the exhibit as a magistrate would and writes down what it proves, and she adds no suspicion of her own.
> - Savra does not know what the family worships. Where a report says devil worship, it says so because the exhibit and the member's sworn account show it.
> - The +1 weapons came from a cache that the Order took from a diabolist's cellar in the Dock Ward three years ago. Brother Osric Wickersham keeps them in a locked rack in the armory.
> - Nobody in this Event names the master of the Splinter or the carnival fleet's owner. Savra says "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener, and she says "the carnival fleet" until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked.

### The Note at Noon

Hold this Event until **Whitehawk Reached** is marked for the member. At noon on the day after the member reaches Renown 25, a Priest Acolyte in a plain grey surcoat finds the member at their lodging and hands over Savra's note. The acolyte waits for no reply. The meeting is in the east annex of the Halls of Justice at the third bell past noon on the same day.

- **Several members:** members who cross the threshold together attend together, and each receives a note and is recorded by name.
- **Companions:** companions who are not Order members get no note, and one who walks to the Halls with a member waits in the nave.
- **Away from Waterdeep:** a member who is away finds the acolyte at their first surface lodging on return, and the meeting moves to the third bell past noon on the following day.
- **Quietly Reassigned:** a member marked **Quietly Reassigned** gets the same note in Brother Hallin Ostrel's hand, signed "B.H.O.", and the meeting runs unchanged.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A young man in a plain grey surcoat knocks a little after noon and holds out a paper sealed with grey wax. He is gone down the stairs before you break the seal, and the slanting handwriting inside is short.
>
> > Vindicator. East annex of the Halls of Justice, third bell past noon today. Formal business, with witnesses. Bring this note and come alone. S.B.

### Naming the Rank

> [!readaloud]
>
> The east annex of the Halls of Justice smells of lamp oil and wet wool, with petitioners waiting along the wall. Brother Haldric lifts a hand from the door-warden's bench and points you down a passage to a small room. Two knights in plain tabards flank a table, and Savra stands between them with a grey-bound book under her hand.
>
> > "You've reached Vindicator, which the Order writes into its roll before two witnesses. I'll tell you plainly what it gives you, and then you can ask me anything."

> [!social]**The Knight of the Gauntlet**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: a knight in her late twenties with her dark hair worn back, a strong jaw, a dark riding coat and a silver gauntlet pin at her collar.
>
> Savra speaks more slowly than usual, because Brother Hallin writes down what she says in front of the witnesses. She states each limit before the benefit it belongs to, and she pauses before every serious answer. She gives a single measured nod only to answers she believes are honest, calls Tyr "the Maimed God" and swears rarely, in short bursts aimed at wrongdoing.
>
> Savra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What each Vindicator benefit gives the member, and what limits it.
> - What an exhibit must show before she accepts a filing.
> - When the Strike Team will refuse an order.
> - The four people in the Book of the Fallen.
>
> She will not discuss what the family worships, the Stone of Golorr or the vault. She answers each by saying she does not know enough and will not guess.

> [!qna]**What does Vindicator give me?**
>
> Savra touches the book, then the rack at the end of the table, then the nearer knight's shoulder as she names each one.
>
> > "I'll open an inquiry for you, and three knights will fight beside you when you ask. You also keep a weapon from the cache, and the Book of the Fallen comes last."

> [!qna]**Why is it mine and not my companions'?**
>
> > "The Order writes its ranks against names, and I've written yours. Your companions can read the report and fight beside the knights. They can't file an inquiry, and they can't call the knights."

> [!qna]**How do I reach you?**
>
> > "Leave your paper with Brother Haldric at the door and say it is for me. He puts it on a shelf, and I read the shelf each evening. Nobody else carries a filing for you."

> [!qna]**What happens if my Renown falls?**
>
> > "You keep the rank, the weapon and any report already filed. New inquiries and the knights wait until your Renown is back at twenty-five."

### The Investigation

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra unrolls a sheet ruled in two columns, headed "Exhibit" and "Finding", and sets a pen beside it. Brother Hallin Ostrel waits at the end of the table with the Order's seal and a lump of grey wax.
>
> > "I can open one inquiry at a time, into a person or a body. Bring me a paper that a magistrate would read, and say who it names. I'll write what it proves and nothing more."

> [!exploration]**Filing an Inquiry**
>
> - **Contact:** the member names the target and hands an exhibit to Brother Haldric Wence at the door. Brother Hallin writes the receipt, and the member may instead hand the exhibit to Savra in the east annex.
> - **The exhibit:** a written paper that shows what the target did, such as a ledger or a signed statement. The table below names the paper that each target needs. A spoken account is not an exhibit, and a paper a companion found counts once the member hands it over.
> - **Savra's offer:** a member holding **Ledger Recovered** is offered Victoro Cassalanter as the first target. A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** is not offered him and may still file any target in writing.
> - **The answer:** Savra reads the exhibit within a day and leaves her answer on Brother Haldric's shelf, signed "S.B.". She accepts the filing or names the paper that is missing, and a refusal spends nothing.
> - **The report:** a Priest Acolyte brings the sealed report to the member's lodging at noon on the twentieth day after Savra accepts. It is signed "S.B." and countersigned by a clerk of the magistrates.
> - **Limit:** one inquiry may be open for each member at a time. The member may file the next once the report has been delivered, and a target already reported on is filed again only with a new exhibit.
> - **Targets:** the six in the table below. For any other target Savra says "I can't reach that one from a Waterdeep court", and nothing is spent.
> - **Who else:** the member only. A companion may read the report, and a filing made in the member's name by a companion is ignored.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who alters an exhibit, or files one they know to be false, loses the benefit for a tenday. Savra hears of it within a day.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and new inquiries wait until the Renown is restored. A report already accepted is still delivered.

> [!exploration]**The Six Targets**
>
> Each row gives the paper the member must bring, what the report finds and what it cannot say. A report is a finding of fact and not a charge.
>
> | Target | The exhibit | The report finds | The report cannot say |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Victoro Cassalanter | The ledger, once **Ledger Recovered** is marked, with the member's sworn account of where it was found | The ledger is in his hand and matches three signed charity receipts. The Aveen Street cellar was leased through a civic association. Devil worship is credibly shown, and the petition already filed stands. | What the entries are for, what the family expects to gain, or anything about the family beyond him |
> | Xanathar's Guild | A copy of a Watch complaint, or any paper the party took from the Guild | A rowhouse in the Castle Ward with stretcher traffic after dark, two sworn complaints, and an owner of record who is a front | The name of any Guild officer, or who leads the Guild |
> | The Splinter's safe house in the Trades Ward | **Splinter Intel Reported**, which makes Savra's brawl file and the member's sworn account the exhibit, or any paper the party took from the Splinter | Cart and carriage traffic twice a tenday, three tenants named only by initial, and rent paid a quarter ahead through a notary | Any name, and "Manshoon" until **Manshoon Named** is marked |
> | Gysheer Omfreys | **Intelligence Shared**, which puts her sealed note and the cartage receipt on file | A room above the dyer's shop on Crispin Street, courier payments, and the fact that summoning devils in a residential court is a chargeable act | Who employs her, beyond the initials on the note |
> | The household secretary Brisenne | **Forged Record Secured** | The alias is in no register, the forged entry matches a hand in three other filings, and the hire-coach is registered to a Sea Ward livery | Whose household she serves. If the member supplies it, the report writes "reported by the member". |
> | A named independent, for example Emmek Frewn | A paper naming one act, such as a receipt or signed statement showing who hired Kelso's gang against Trollskull Alley | He hired the gang, the coin was paid through a go-between, and Savra can refer the matter to the Watch | Anything about the Grand Game, or who lent him the coin |

> [!gamemaster]**What Savra Writes**
>
> - **Devil worship:** Savra writes "devil worship" only if **Cassalanter Register Filed** is marked or the party fought the devils of **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**. Otherwise the Victoro report says "unlawful rites", and she tells the member she wants a devil she can point at.
> - **The family:** Savra says "the family" until a player names a Cassalanter at the table. She then uses the name and adds that the report proves only what the exhibit proves.
> - **The Splinter:** speech and reports say "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member.
> - **No pact:** no report states a pact, its terms, a deadline or what the family worships, because Savra knows none of it.
> - **The magistrates:** the report goes to a magistrate of the Halls, who passes it on in the ordinary way. Savra can promise no date.
> - **The Lords:** if **Order Recognized** is marked, the Lords have already heard the Order's writ in **The Writ and the Weight**. Savra then adds that the report is admissible before them and that their clerk reads the Order's reports within a tenday. If it is not marked, she says nothing of the Lords.

> [!qna]**Can you arrest the people it names?**
>
> > "No, the Order has no power to arrest anyone in this city. I write what they did, and a magistrate decides what follows once I send it on."

> [!qna]**What if the exhibit isn't enough?**
>
> > "Then I'll tell you what's missing, and you can go and find it. Nothing is spent until I accept the filing, so bring it back whenever you have it."

### The Strike Team

> [!readaloud]
>
> The two knights step away from the table and stand side by side in front of the book. One is a woman in her forties with grey in her braid, and the other is a broad man a few years younger. Savra nods toward them without turning her head, and the woman speaks first.
>
> > "I'm Dame Rhosyn Cullane, and this is Sir Anselm Greave. A third knight, Sir Merrick Tollwood, is at the Field Ward gate. We are the chapter's strike team, and inside an operation we follow your plan."

> [!social]**The Living Vindicator**
>
> Dame Rhosyn Cullane (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: a Knight of the chapter in her forties with grey in her braid, who has held Vindicator for almost two years and leads the Strike Team.
>
> Rhosyn is dry and unhurried. She answers practical questions in full and personal ones in a few words, and she asks what an operation is for before she asks where it is. She does not swear.
>
> Rhosyn is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the team will and will not do in the field.
> - What the member should tell the team before an operation.
> - How she has held Vindicator, and what the work has cost her.
>
> She will not discuss Savra's past or what the Order knows about the family. She says those belong to Savra.

> [!qna]**Will you follow any plan?**
>
> > "Any plan that's lawful, and plenty that aren't wise. We'll say so once if we think a plan is foolish, and then we'll carry it out. We won't strike the Watch or anyone who has laid down a weapon."

> [!qna]**Can the team come below the city?**
>
> > "No, we serve the courts of this city, and the Order sends us nowhere those courts don't reach. Everything above the Portal's well is open to us, and so are the cellars and sewers."

> [!exploration]**Calling the Strike Team**
>
> - **Contact:** the member leaves a written request for "Savra Belabranta" with Brother Haldric Wence at the Halls, or hands it to Dame Rhosyn in the east annex. It names the quest, the operation, the surface place where it starts, the hour and the aim.
> - **Notice:** three days are needed. Savra's answer waits on the shelf by the next evening, signed "S.B.", and gives the meeting place or her reason for refusing. A request with less notice is refused and spends nothing.
> - **The operation:** any task inside a named quest on the surface of Waterdeep, including its cellars and sewers. The knights do not go down the Portal's well or into Undermountain. The six Order of the Gauntlet missions are not eligible, because their scenes fix who stands where.
> - **Service:** three Knights meet the member at the place and hour named. They stay until the operation ends or is abandoned, or until three days have passed, and then they return to the Halls.
> - **Limit:** one operation per member in each quest. A quest is one of the named quests, from **Finding Floon** through **Vault of Dragons**, and events and rank events are not quests. The use counts against the quest the party is in when the knights arrive, and a call withdrawn a full day ahead is not spent. An unused call is lost when the quest ends.
> - **What they refuse:** the knights will not attack the Watch, a magistrate, a captive or a creature that has surrendered, and they will not leave the city. They also refuse an order they judge clearly unrighteous, saying so once before they stand down. Savra turns back a request that needs any of these, and nothing is spent.
> - **Several members:** each Vindicator has a use of their own. Two members who ask for the same operation at the same hour share one team, and only the first to ask spends a use.
> - **Who else:** the knights follow the plan of the member who called them, and they take no order from a companion who is not an Order member.
> - **Losses:** a Knight who falls is replaced before the member's next call, and the use stays spent.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who uses the team for a crime, or orders it against the list above, loses the service for a tenday. The knights leave when the order is given, and Rhosyn reports it to Savra.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 25 keeps the rank, and the service is suspended until the Renown is restored.

> [!gamemaster]**The Strike Team as Allies**
>
> Use the 2024 **Knight** stat block for each of the three, unmodified. Each is a CR 3 ally with Power 30 for levels 5 to 10 and 25 for level 11 and above. The number who fight is the number of player characters minus one, to a cap of three, and the rest hold the rear and add nothing. Party Power with the fighting Knights added, and the Bruising budget in brackets:
>
> | Level | 3 PCs | 4 PCs | 5 PCs |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 6 | 165 (99) | 230 (138) | 265 (159) |
> | 7 | 183 (109.8) | 254 (152.4) | 295 (177) |
> | 8 | 192 (115.2) | 266 (159.6) | 310 (186) |
>
> At levels 9 and 10, add 30 for each fighting Knight, and from level 11 add 25 for each. At three PCs the two Knights add 60 to a base of 105 to 132, a rise of 45 to 57 percent. Build the fight from the allied total, or keep it at the base budget and treat the knights as the reserve. Pick one approach for the whole scene and keep to it. Hard rosters belong to allied scenes only.
>
> #### The Strike Team's Tactics
> To begin combat, the knights form a line in front of the member and anyone who cannot take a hit. During combat, they:
>
> - Fight with the Greatsword in melee and the Heavy Crossbow at range, and use Parry against the first melee hit each round.
> - Focus the enemy that is hurting the member or a companion the most.
> - Keep the knights who do not fight at the rear and at the named exit.
>
> A Knight withdraws toward the exit the member named when it is reduced to 26 Hit Points or fewer, and it takes a fallen character with it if it can reach one. The team lowers its blades when a creature yields and hands the prisoner to the Watch. If the Watch arrives, Rhosyn speaks for the team and names the Order as her authority.

When the team arrives for its operation, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Three people in plain cloaks stand at the place you named, with mail showing at their collars and no tabards. Rhosyn Cullane steps forward while Sir Anselm and Sir Merrick check the street in opposite directions.
>
> > "We're the chapter's, and we'll follow your plan until it ends. Tell us where to stand, and tell us now if any of it is unrighteous."

### The Weapon

> [!readaloud]
>
> Brother Osric Wickersham comes in from the armory passage with a long rack and sets it across the end of the table. A sword, an axe, a hammer and a bow lie on it, each tagged with a number.
>
> > "These came out of a cache we took from a diabolist's cellar three years ago. The Order kept them for someone who would use them. Choose one, and it is yours to keep, not borrowed."

> [!exploration]**Choosing a Weapon**
>
> - **The choice:** the member chooses one weapon from the table at the meeting, and Brother Osric writes it in the book. Savra chooses for a member who cannot decide, according to how that member fights.
> - **The bonus:** each is a +1 weapon (Uncommon). It gives a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with it, and it counts as magical. Proficiency works as normal, so a member should choose a weapon they know how to use.
> - **Permanent:** the member keeps the weapon for good. A lost weapon is not replaced, and no second weapon is given.
> - **Who else:** the member only. The weapon is the member's to carry, and a request for one made by a companion is ignored.
>
> | Category | Choices |
> |---|---|
> | Sword | longsword, rapier, shortsword |
> | Axe | battleaxe, handaxe |
> | Hammer | warhammer, maul |
> | Bow | longbow, shortbow |

### The Book of the Fallen

> [!readaloud]
>
> Dame Rhosyn lifts the grey-bound book and holds it open on her forearm so that you can read the last leaf. Four lines are written there in four different hands.
>
> > Sir Aldous Fenmore, Vindicator. Died in Eleint of 1479 DR at the burning of a Dock Ward warehouse. Operation against diabolism.
> >
> > Dame Joslyn Wardle, Vindicator. Died in Uktar of 1484 DR in a Trades Ward cellar, of wounds taken breaking a rite. Operation against diabolism.
> >
> > Sir Hadrian Strake, Vindicator. Died in Tarsakh of 1489 DR on the High Road, escorting a witness to a hearing. Killed by hired swords.
> >
> > Dame Rhosyn Cullane, Vindicator. Named in Flamerule of 1490 DR. Living.
>
> Savra waits until you have read to the end of the page before she speaks.
>
> > "Three of the four who held Vindicator in Waterdeep are dead. I'd rather you heard that from me than from a clerk. Two died hunting devil-worshippers, and the third was killed keeping a witness alive."
>
> She sets a pen on the open page and steps back from the table.
>
> > "The work does not get safer at this rank, and I won't tell you it does. Sign under Rhosyn if you want to, and the rank stands whether you do or not."

Whether the member signs is the member's choice, and nothing in the campaign reads it. Savra gives her single nod to a member who signs and says nothing to one who does not.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown. Each Vindicator member earns further Renown from missions and from the ordinary rewards listed on the Order of the Gauntlet page of the Factions Guide.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions everything Savra said and may let them read any report. The inquiry, the Strike Team and the weapon stay individual benefits. Brother Haldric, Brother Hallin and Dame Rhosyn refuse a request that a companion makes in the member's name.

A member whose Renown later falls keeps the rank and the weapon, and the inquiry and the Strike Team pause until the Renown is restored. A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** keeps all three benefits. Savra stops offering Victoro Cassalanter as a target, and the member names any target in writing.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has heard all three benefits, has chosen a weapon and has heard the Book of the Fallen read.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Vindicator Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Savra names the rank at the table; read by **Righteous Hand**, which checks it first. Track separately for each member the open inquiry with its target and the date its report is due. Also track the quest of the last Strike Team call and the weapon chosen.
>
> This Event also reads **Whitehawk Reached** (the hold) and **Quietly Reassigned**, which changes how the note arrives and what Savra offers. It reads **Ledger Recovered**, **Cassalanter Register Filed**, **Intelligence Shared**, **Forged Record Secured** and **Splinter Intel Reported** for the exhibits. It reads **Order Recognized** to decide whether Savra mentions the Lords. It reads **Manshoon Named** and **Jarlaxle Unmasked** for the wording of the Splinter and the carnival fleet.

## Overview

Savra Belabranta names an Order of the Gauntlet member a Vindicator before two Knights of the chapter and explains the rank's written inquiry, its Strike Team of three Knights and its +1 weapon.

## Summary

We were named Vindicators of the Order of the Gauntlet before two Knights of the chapter, and Savra Belabranta told each of us what the rank holds. She will open a written inquiry into any target we can show her a paper for, and three Knights will fight beside us once in every quest. Each of us chose a +1 weapon from a cache the Order took from a diabolist's cellar. Savra then read us the Book of the Fallen, where three of the last four Vindicators in Waterdeep are dead.
