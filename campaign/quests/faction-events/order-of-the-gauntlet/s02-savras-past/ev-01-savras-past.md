# Savra's Past

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event begins when Savra Belabranta walks an Order of the Gauntlet member to the east annex of the Halls of Justice. It can also begin when a member tells her who may have paid for the Howling Hatred, and it ends when the member answers the question she asks at the close. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Walk with Savra at dusk to a small chapel of Tyr and hear what she did in the Howling Hatred.
> - Bring her the suspicion that old Waterdhavian money paid for the cult, from Mirt or from Saeth Cromley.
> - Hear how she takes that news and what she refuses to say about it.
> - Answer her closing question and earn the single nod she gives only to honest answers.
>
> Only individual Order members are told, and companions are not invited, hear nothing and gain nothing.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Savra was in the Howling Hatred for three years and left it about three years ago, on the night of the storm. She joined the Order of the Gauntlet after she left.
> - She held the stairwell door while four initiates were bound to the roof parapet. She stopped two of the four from going over and could not stop the other two.
> - She saw one of the two who fell catch a drainpipe. She has never learned whether he lived, and she has never asked.
> - Two days later she gave the Halls of Justice every name she knew. Within a tenday the Watch made arrests, and the high priest was gone before they reached the tower.
> - Two of those arrested were released within the month. The Watch told Savra there was not enough evidence, and she believed it.
> - Savra never traced the cult's money. It came through a courier and notes in a formal hand, and she suspects old Waterdhavian money without naming a house.
> - Nothing in the campaign settles who paid. The Event leaves the Cassalanters a suspicion, and Savra neither confirms nor denies them.
> - Savra does not know what the family worships, and she says nothing about it in this Event.

### Savra Chooses the Hour

> [!gamemaster]**Who Hears This, and When**
>
> Each member hears this once, and two triggers fire it, with whichever comes first setting the scene.
>
> - **Trigger A, the walk.** A member who has reached Renown 3 and 3rd level, and who has seen Savra fight beside them at least once, is eligible. The fight can be **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**, a Marcheon operation or any other. Savra knocks at dusk on the third day after the fight.
> - **Trigger B, the suspicion.** The first time a member tells Savra that Cassalanter money lay behind the Howling Hatred, the Event fires wherever they stand. The member says it as a suspicion, and Savra never confirms it.
> - If the member is eligible for Trigger A, Trigger B runs the full scene at dusk the same day. If the telling comes after dusk, it runs at the next dusk. If the member is not eligible, Savra gives the short reply in **The Short Version**, and the full scene waits for Trigger A.
> - If the member is away from Waterdeep, Savra comes at dusk on the day they return. If **Quietly Reassigned** is marked for the member, she waits until the next dusk.
> - If the tithe visit in **The Tithe** falls on the same dusk, she holds this one back a day.

When Trigger A fires, read the following aloud. Savra declines to come in if she is invited, and says "Not here. Walk with me."

> [!readaloud]
>
> The lamplighter has reached the far end of Trollskull Alley when someone knocks once at the door. Savra Belabranta stands on the step in her dark riding coat, with the silver gauntlet pin at her collar and her hands empty at her sides. She waits for you to fetch your cloak.
>
> > "I'd like you to walk with me to the Halls of Justice. There's something I owe you, and I'd rather say it there than here."

If Trigger B fires and the member is eligible, Savra stops what she is doing. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra stops in the middle of a sentence and turns to face you, with her hands gone still at her sides, and she lets you finish without a word.
>
> > "Tell me exactly what you were told, and who told you."
>
> She listens to the end and does not ask a second question.
>
> > "Dusk, at the east annex of the Halls of Justice. Come to the side door, and I'll tell you the rest there."

### Where the Suspicion Comes From

The Cassalanter suspicion reaches an Order member by two routes, and either one fires Trigger B without proving anything.

> [!gamemaster]**Two Routes to Savra**
>
> - **Mirt, through a Harper.** In **Fireball!** Mirt tells a Harper member of Renown 1 or higher that Cassalanter money may have been behind the Howling Hatred three years ago. The Watch released two of its members after a quiet word from someone at Cassalanter Villa. He has no proof, and he shares it because he is worried. The Harper can pass the words on, and any Order member who hears them can carry them to Savra.
> - **Saeth Cromley, in the sergeants' room.** Saeth sits in the Watch sergeants' room at the Halls of Justice most afternoons. Any member who buys him a drink and asks about the Howling Hatred gets the account below, with no check needed. A Harper who already holds Mirt's account gets the same story from him as a second source.

> [!social]**The Retired Sergeant**
>
> Saeth Cromley (Lawful Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a gravelly, easygoing retired sergeant who gets people talking by being unimpressive.
>
> Saeth is Friendly and in no hurry, and he swears cheerfully like an old soldier. He scratches his chin before the question that matters, and he says "One more thing" on the way out. He calls strangers "friend" or "sunshine". He keeps to what he saw himself, because he has no patience with rumour.
>
> Saeth is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The Howling Hatred's collapse and the arrests that followed.
> - The two prisoners who walked out of the custody wing.
> - The steward who came to speak to the captain.
>
> He will not discuss why the steward came or who sent him, because he never learned.

> [!qna]**The Howling Hatred?**
>
> Saeth laughs shortly and waves the barmaid over for a second round.
>
> > "That was three years back, friend. A tower full of fools in the Dock Ward, preaching at the wind. We took the lot of them inside a tenday, and I'd have bet my pension it was done."

> [!qna]**Two of them walked?**
>
> > "They did, sunshine. They were out of the custody wing before supper, with nothing signed that I could read. The captain said the evidence had gone thin, and I said that was a hell of a coincidence."

> [!qna]**Who spoke for them?**
>
> Saeth scratches his chin before he answers.
>
> > "A steward came down to the wing that afternoon, in the Cassalanter household coat, and he talked low with the captain for a while. I can't tell you what was said, and I can't tell you he was sent. It's a charitable house, and they do like to send a kind word. One more thing: the captain looked relieved when the man left."

> [!gamemaster]**The Limit on Saeth**
>
> Saeth saw a household steward and no more. He does not know why the steward came or whether the family had any link to the cult. If a player pushes him toward a conclusion, he says "I saw a coat, friend. I didn't see a reason." Nobody in the account says anything about what the family worships.

### The Walk to the Annex

The east annex stands behind the main hall of the Halls of Justice, and the side door is unlocked at dusk for Savra alone. Other Order members who qualify at the same time may walk with her, and anyone else stays outside.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The east annex stands behind the main hall of the Halls of Justice, and its side door opens onto a short passage with one lamp burning. Savra leads you past a locked door marked for the armory to a narrow chapel at the end. A wooden statue of Tyr stands against the far wall with its right hand carved away at the wrist. Savra lights a second lamp from the first, sets it on the ledge and turns to face you.

> [!gamemaster]**The Howling Hatred**
>
> This is background for the GM, and Savra gives it only in pieces.
>
> - The Howling Hatred was an elemental air cult that worked in Waterdeep until about three years ago. It served Yan-C-Bin, the Prince of Evil Air, and preached that walls, contracts and gold kept the world from his wind.
> - Its ranks ran from Initiate through Windwalker to Windcaller, and a few ordained leaders sat above them. The rank names are invented for this campaign.
> - It met in a derelict Dock Ward tower for its last two years. Member offerings could not have paid for the tower, so money came from outside.
> - It broke in a single tenday of arrests. The high priest was never found, and no Watch record names the source of the money.
> - It is not active during the campaign, and its surviving members scattered or recanted.

### What She Did

Savra tells this standing, and she gives no nod while she tells it. Read the following aloud, pausing where the breaks fall:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra keeps her eyes on yours while she speaks, and her voice stays level while she keeps looking at the member nearest her.
>
> > "I was in the Howling Hatred for three years, and I've told you the name without telling you what I did. I was close to taking the Windcaller's oath."
>
> She pauses while the lamp on the ledge flickers in the draught from the passage, and then she goes on.
>
> > "The cult kept a tower in the Dock Ward. The high priest held a rite there on a night of storm, and he called it a test of faith. Four initiates were brought up to the roof. They were all newer than me, and he bound each of them to a ring-bolt in the parapet and told them to trust the wind."
>
> Her hand closes once at her side, and she looks at the floor between you before she speaks again.
>
> > "My task was the stairwell door. I was to stand there and see that nobody went down. I knew what the bindings were and I knew what the storm would do. I held that door like a fucking post, and I let it start."
>
> She looks past you at the statue for a moment, and then she looks back at the member nearest her.
>
> > "Two of them went over before I moved. Then I broke the circle and dragged the other two in off the ledge. One of the two who fell caught a drainpipe on the way down, and I saw his hands on it. I didn't see what came after."
>
> > "I walked out of that tower into the storm. Two days later I went to the Halls of Justice and asked for a cleric of Tyr. I gave them every name I had, and I expected a cell, but they took me in as a penitent. I'm still not sure they were right to."

> [!social]**The Woman at the Lamp**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: a knight of the Order in her late twenties, with dark hair worn back and a strong jaw, standing in the chapel.
>
> Savra is Friendly toward the member she has brought, and she tells the account without ornament. She pauses before each serious answer and says "I'll tell you plainly" before the hard parts. She calls Tyr "the Maimed God" and the party by name. She swears rarely, and only at wrongdoing, her own included.
>
> Savra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What happened on the tower roof, in the order she told it.
> - How the Halls of Justice took her in.
> - Who else knows, and what became of the high priest.
> - The cult's money, if the member has raised it.
>
> She will not discuss a family she cannot prove, what a noble house might want with a cult, or what the Order's other knights think of her. She ends each of these with "That's all I have."

> [!qna]**Why tell us now?**
>
> > "You've stood beside me with a weapon in your hand, and you should know who else stood there. I'll tell you plainly: I'd rather you heard it from me than found it out."

> [!qna]**Did you ever find out if he lived?**
>
> Savra answers at once, and her voice does not change.
>
> > "No."
>
> She adds nothing, and she does not reach for the lamp.

> [!qna]**Who else knows?**
>
> > "The cleric of Tyr who took my confession knows all of it. The Order knows the cult's name and not the night. Now you know, and that's three people."

> [!qna]**What happened to the high priest?**
>
> > "He was gone before the Watch reached the tower, and nobody has found him since. I have looked in every place I thought of, and I'll keep looking."

### The Cult's Money

Run this scene only if **Cult Funding Linked** is not yet marked and the member raised the suspicion at the Trigger B telling. If the member told Savra the suspicion earlier through **The Short Version**, skip to **The Ask**.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra's gaze comes back to you from the statue, and she takes a moment to answer what you told her earlier.
>
> > "The cult always had money, more than its members could give. It came through a courier from an office I never traced, with notes in a formal hand. It was old Waterdhavian money, the kind a family keeps for generations without drawing notice."
>
> > "Two of them walked free within the month. The Watch told me there wasn't enough evidence, and I believed it. If someone paid for that tower and those bindings, and has sat in temple every tenday since, then I was their tool. I'd like to know the name, and I don't have it."

> [!gamemaster]**What Savra Will Not Do**
>
> Savra names no family and gives no reason a noble house would fund an elemental cult. If a player names the Cassalanters and asks her to confirm, she answers "I have no proof of that name, and I won't say one I can't prove." She asks the member to bring her anything they find in writing.
>
> She does not say what the family worships, because she does not know.

> [!qna]**Who do you think paid?**
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly: I don't know, and I won't guess a name I can't prove. A guess from me would sound like a charge, and that would be wrong of me."

> [!qna]**Do you want us to look into it?**
>
> > "I want what you find brought to me, and nothing that gets someone hurt. If you learn who paid, tell me before you act on it."

### The Short Version

This applies when a member raises the suspicion through Trigger B before they are eligible for Trigger A. Savra gives the reply below on the spot and the member marks **Cult Funding Linked**. **Savra's Past Known** stays unmarked until the full scene runs.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra stands for a moment with her jaw set, and then she looks at the member who spoke.
>
> > "Thank you, it fits what I saw, and I can't prove it. I was in that cult, and the money was old Waterdhavian money. You'll hear the rest from me later, once I've fought beside you. Not before."

### The Ask

When she has finished, Savra looks at each member in turn. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra looks at each of you in turn, and the second lamp throws her shadow across the statue, and she waits until she has the attention of the member she brought.
>
> > "Now you know. I'll ask you plainly what you will do with it."

An answer is honest when it says what the member will do, and the table fixes Savra's reply to each kind of answer. Each member present answers for themselves.

> [!gamemaster]**Savra's Replies**
>
> | The member says | Savra's reply | Result |
> |---|---|---|
> | They will keep it between them and her | She nods once. "Good. That's all I ask." | Accepted. |
> | It changes nothing | She nods once. "Then we understand each other." | Accepted. |
> | They don't know yet | She nods once. "That's honest. Take the time you need." | Accepted. |
> | Any other plain statement of what they will do | She nods once. "Good enough." If they will tell someone, she adds "Tell them I gave it freely." | Accepted. |
> | A joke, a change of subject, or reassurance with no statement of what they will do | "That's not an answer." She asks the question once more. | Rejected the first time. |
> | A second evasion | "Think on it. Find me here at any dusk when you have one." She gives no nod. | The talk ends. |
>
> - A member who is sent away can return to the annex at any dusk and answer, with no cost.
> - The nod is given only for an honest answer, and Savra never nods during **What She Did** or **The Cult's Money**.
> - **Savra's Past Known** is marked when she finishes **What She Did**, whatever the answer to **The Ask**.

When every member present has answered, read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra pinches out the second lamp, takes the first from its hook and leads you back down the passage. At the side door she holds it open, and the lamplighters are working their way along the street outside.

### Renown Opportunities

This Event awards no Renown, and the Earning Renown actions do not apply to it. A member who answers well gains nothing for it, and companions are not present and gain none.

### Aftermath

Savra does not refer to the evening again unless a member asks, and she does not change how she briefs the next mission.

**Righteous Hand** reads **Savra's Past Known** to decide whether her investiture words to that member name the night on the tower. **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted) reads both outcomes. A member with **Savra's Past Known** marked sees her go quiet on the approach, and with **Cult Funding Linked** as well she says only "I want this done right." **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) reads both outcomes when Savra speaks for the party before the Lords, and if neither quest runs, nothing changes.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when every member present has answered, or been sent away to think on it. It reads **Quietly Reassigned** and writes two outcomes.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Savra's Past Known** — mark with the name of each member who hears **What She Did**. Read by **Righteous Hand**, **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted) and **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).
> - **Cult Funding Linked** — mark with the name of each member who tells Savra the funding suspicion, in the full scene or in **The Short Version**. Read by **Righteous Hand**, **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted) and **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).

## Overview

Savra Belabranta tells an Order member what she did in the Howling Hatred, and asks what they will do with it.

## Summary

Savra walked us to a small chapel of Tyr in the Halls of Justice and told us what she did on the tower roof in the Dock Ward. She held the door while four initiates were tied to the parapet, and two of them went over the edge. She asked what we would do with it, and we gave her an answer. We still don't know who paid for the cult.
