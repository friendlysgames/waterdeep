# Design Notes: Savra's Past

## What the Source Gave

The restored draft gave a two-trigger confession with a lore sidebar, a four-year cult tenure, an unwritten answer rule in The Ask and True/False flags read by quests that never read them. The deed on the tower roof, the four initiates, the drainpipe and the line "Now you know" are kept. Mirt's intel about Cassalanter money and the two released cultists comes from **Fireball!**, where only a Harper hears it.

## Triggers and Gates

Trigger A is now per member and fixed in time and place: Renown 3, 3rd level, one fight seen beside Savra, and a knock at dusk on the third day after. Trigger B had depended on a Harper, so Saeth Cromley is added as a second route a non-Harper party can reach, using his afternoon seat in the sergeants' room. His account is a steward in the household coat, which stays a suspicion. A member who raises the funding before Trigger A is eligible gets a short reply and **Cult Funding Linked**, and the full scene skips The Cult's Money later.

## Timeline and Voice

The cult tenure is three years and Savra left about three years ago, matching the First Meeting. Her statement to the Halls of Justice leads to the arrests, and two arrested members are released within the month, which joins her story to Mirt's intel without her knowing the cause. She swears once, at her own failure, in line with her voice profile. She gives no nod until The Ask, where the table fixes her reply to each kind of answer and the nod goes only to answers that say what the member will do.

## Departures from the Source

No Renown is awarded, as before. The two outcomes keep their names but are marked per member. The draft's readers (Cassalanter Villa and Vault of Dragons) are unconverted, so they are named as unconverted and tied to one concrete line each. **Righteous Hand** is added as a reader so the investiture can echo the night.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Invented: the chapel in the east annex, the storm-night timing of the arrests, Saeth's steward in the household coat, and the claim that Savra's statement caused the arrests.
- Unverified: the Howling Hatred rank names (Initiate, Windwalker, Windcaller); no *Princes of the Apocalypse* file exists in `sources/`.
- Open: no page decides who paid for the cult, so the Event leaves it a suspicion.
- Resolved: Saeth's Notable Figures page now says "Halls of Justice" as the Order pages do, and lists this event.
- Open: Savra's Notable Figures page says neutral good and Tethyrian; the brief fixes Lawful Good, Human.
- Open: the Quietly Reassigned delay of one dusk and the holdover rule against the tithe visit are mine, not the brief's.
