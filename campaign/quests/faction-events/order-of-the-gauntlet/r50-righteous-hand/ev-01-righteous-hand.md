# The Righteous Hand

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Ceremony Event fires at the first natural pause after one party member's OotG Renown reaches 50. Savra sends a formal written summons three days in advance. The ceremony takes place in the main hall of the Halls of Justice — not the chamber, the hall. In this Event:
>
> - The Order's senior paladins and clerics assemble as witnesses. **Sister Valdra** is among them.
> - Savra performs a formal investiture: each participating party member is knighted at the Halls of Justice, the title recognized by allied temples and civic authorities across the Sword Coast.
> - She presents the Order's mobilization pledge: six **Knights** and two **Priests** for one major operation, in writing.
> - The high priest of Tyr formally records the Resurrection pledge at the altar.
> - After the other witnesses leave, Savra presents the Undermountain Mandate — the senior council's decision to extend the Order's formal authority underground. The character chooses whether to accept it.
> - One binary flag set: **Undermountain Mandate Accepted**.
>
> > [!design]**Expected in Dungeon of the Mad Mage**
> >
> > Parties are not expected to reach Renown 50 with the Order of the Gauntlet during Dragon Heist. This event is expected to fire during Dungeon of the Mad Mage, after sustained service in Undermountain. It can run before or after **Vault of Dragons**; if **Vault of Dragons** has already resolved, see the note in the Concluding section.

**Background (DM only)**

Righteous Hand is the rank the Order gives for sustained moral courage under sustained pressure — not just tactical success, but holding a line at cost. Savra has given it once before in Waterdeep. The senior council agreed unanimously to grant it now, which she describes to no one. Sister Valdra mentions it afterward if asked.

The Undermountain Mandate came from two tendays of debate. The question was whether the Order's formal authority should follow its members underground. The council resolved to leave the choice to the character: whoever accepts the mandate will carry it, and they should carry it by choice. Savra agrees with this reasoning.

### The Summons

The note arrives at Trollskull Manor three days before the ceremony. Savra's economical, slightly left-slanting hand on paper bearing the full Halls of Justice seal.

*Halls of Justice. Main hall. [Date]. First bell past midday. Dress accordingly.*

*Dress accordingly* means presentable, armored, and ready. The character who has been paying attention will know this.

### The Gathering

The main hall holds sixty people at capacity. Today it holds the party, Savra, six senior Order members including Sister Valdra, and two priests of Tyr in full vestments standing to either side of the high altar.

The Order members stand without speaking. They have operated alongside people who did what this character has done, and they know what the work costs. Something older than ceremony holds them still.

Savra stands before the altar.

> > "The Order of the Gauntlet has one rank it awards less than any other. I've given it once before in Waterdeep, and that was four years ago. Today I'm giving it again."

### The Investiture

Savra draws her sword — the one she has carried since before the party met her — and holds it flat across both hands.

> > "Kneel."

The hall waits.

> > "By the authority of the Order of the Gauntlet and in the sight of Tyr, Helm, and Torm — I invest you as Righteous Hand. Sworn to pursue evil. Sworn to hold the innocent whole. Sworn to keep one another accountable. Rise."

The sword touches each shoulder in turn. The witnesses say together, in the form the Order uses for investitures: *"By the same witness."*

Savra sheaths the sword.

> > "The title carries weight outside Waterdeep. Allied temples and civic authorities across the Sword Coast will recognize it as a legitimate credential of the Order. You can use it in correspondence, before hearings, and at the door of any institution that respects what this organization stands for. Which isn't all of them. Enough."

Sister Valdra steps forward and presses a gauntlet pin into the character's hand — silver, finer than the one given at Chevall.

> > "Worth comparing, if you still have the first one," Sister Valdra says. It's the closest she gets to sentiment.

### The Righteous Hand's Charge

Savra presents three things.

**The mobilization pledge.** Six **Knights** and two **Priests** from the Order's Waterdeep chapter, for one major operation of the character's designating. The character names the operation and the date; the knights and priests arrive prepared. They operate under the character's tactical direction and will not carry out orders they judge clearly unrighteous. Savra produces the written pledge: signed, sealed.

**The Resurrection pledge.** The high priest of Tyr steps forward and sets a document before the altar.

> > "If you die in service against a fiend or undead, the Order of the Gauntlet will fund one Resurrection. Once. No other conditions. This is formally recorded with the Halls of Justice and with the temple."

He signs the record. Savra witnesses it.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### The Resurrection Record
>
> The pledge covers one Resurrection (5th-level spell, 2024 rules). It applies to the character who received the Righteous Hand investiture, or to one named ally of that character's designation; the designation can be made at any time after this event, before the need arises. The Order covers the 1,000 gp material component cost. The pledge fires once.

**The weapon.** Nothing new — the *+1 weapon* from Vindicator is already in the character's hands. Savra names, now, where it came from.

> > "It was recovered from a devil's effects. Not its original owner — the devil took it from someone. We took it from the devil." A pause. "You've carried it longer than anyone else who's had it. I thought you should know."

She says this without inflection — a fact she judged worth sharing.

### The Mandate

After the other Order members leave, the hall quiets. Savra sits on the edge of the altar steps. She's holding a folded document with the Order's full seal — not the chapter seal, the central authority.

> > "The council prepared something else. I want to explain it before I show it to you."

She sets the document on the step beside her.

> > "There's an argument that the Order's formal authority should follow its members wherever evil operates. That if you hold Righteous Hand rank and you're in the sewers, or beneath the city — wherever the work has taken you — that authority should apply there. The Halls of Justice, the allied temples, the civic cooperation we can deliver. All of it applicable underground."

> > "The council wrote the Undermountain Mandate. It would extend the Order's formal jurisdiction and resource commitment to operations in Undermountain. If something kills you there, we're coming for it. If something needs to be cleared from the lower levels and there's no one else, this mandate means the chapter has an obligation. Not just a willingness. An obligation."

She meets the character's eye.

> > "But it also means that if you accept the mandate and then don't pursue what's down there — the mandate doesn't expire. Neither does what it commits us to."

The document stays on the step. She doesn't push it toward the character. She doesn't pull it back.

> > "The council left it to you. I'll leave it to you."

#### Accepting the Mandate

The character takes the document and signs it before Savra as witness.

> > "Good," she says. "I'll file a copy with the central authority."

She tucks the signed document into her coat. She looks, briefly, like someone who has been carrying something for a long time and is now sharing the weight of it.

> > "Whatever is below us — I don't fully know yet what it is. You'll find out before I do. Send word when you have something to tell."

#### Declining the Mandate

The character leaves the document on the step.

Savra folds it and tucks it into her coat.

> > "All right."

No argument. No disappointment she allows to show.

> > "If you change your mind, come find me. The mandate stays active."

The Righteous Hand rank is unchanged. The decline carries no consequence to the character's standing.

### Concluding the Event

#### Undermountain Mandate Accepted: True / False

**True:** The character has formally accepted the Order's Undermountain Mandate, extending the Order's jurisdiction and resource obligation to Undermountain operations. Read in future Dungeon of the Mad Mage events where the Order's formal backing underground changes an outcome — specifically, any event where allied religious authority in Undermountain territory is relevant, or where the chapter's obligation to send aid is triggered.

**False:** The character declined. The mandate remains available; Savra keeps the document. It can be accepted at any later point with a visit to Savra.

## Overview

The Order of the Gauntlet assembled its senior members at the Halls of Justice to invest the character as Righteous Hand — the Order's highest rank. Savra performed the investiture herself, with Sister Valdra and six senior members as witnesses. The ceremony produced a formal knighthood recognized across the Sword Coast, a pledge of six knights and two priests for one major operation, and a Resurrection pledge recorded before the altar. After the witnesses left, Savra presented the Undermountain Mandate — the senior council's decision to extend the Order's formal authority underground — and left the choice of accepting it entirely to the character.
