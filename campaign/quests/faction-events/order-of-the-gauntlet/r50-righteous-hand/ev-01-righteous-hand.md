# Righteous Hand

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member first reaches Renown 50, which usually happens during **Dungeon of the Mad Mage**. A Priest Acolyte carries Savra's note to the member at their first surface lodging. Three days later Isaldric Veyne, the high priest of Tyr, invests the member in the main hall of the Halls of Justice. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Be invested as Righteous Hand and receive the Writ of Knighthood, a credential that opens temple doors across the Sword Coast.
> - Call on the Mobilization, six Knights and two Priests of the Waterdeep chapter, for one major operation in each quest.
> - Hold the Resurrection pledge, which pays for one casting of *Resurrection* by the high priest of Tyr.
> - Accept or decline the Undermountain Mandate, which lets the Order's help follow the member below the city.
>
> All of these benefits belong to the qualifying member, and companions gain none of them.

### The Note at Noon

Hold this Event until **Vindicator Reached** is marked for the member, and hold the note until the member is on the surface. The Order has no way to send word below the city, so the note waits until the member climbs back to the surface.

At noon on the first day the member is above ground, a **Priest Acolyte** in a plain grey surcoat finds them at their lodging. He hands over a note in Savra's left-slanting hand, on paper with the Halls of Justice seal, and waits for no reply. The note is signed "S.B." and names the investiture, which is on the third day after it arrives, at the third bell past noon.

- **Several members:** members who surface together receive their own notes and attend together, and each is recorded by name.
- **Companions:** companions who are not Order members get no note, and a companion who walks to the Halls waits in the nave.
- **Quietly Reassigned:** if the member is marked **Quietly Reassigned**, Brother Hallin Ostrel writes the note and signs it "B.H.O.", and the investiture runs unchanged.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A young man in a plain grey surcoat knocks at the door of your lodging a little after noon. He holds out a paper folded once and sealed with the Halls of Justice, and he is gone down the stairs before you have opened it. The handwriting inside slants to the left, and the note is only three lines long.
>
> > Righteous Hand. The main hall of the Halls of Justice, the third bell past noon, three days from today. Bring only those who have earned the rank. S.B.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - Savra has never held Righteous Hand, and the last holder was Sir Aldwin Dunhallow, a senior Knight of the Waterdeep chapter who died six years ago of a fever caught nursing fire victims.
> - Savra joined the Order after he died, so she knows him only from the roll and from what Father Corwen Thale says.
> - The Waterdeep chapter is Savra, the clerk's office and the rotation of about a dozen Knights and Priests who answer to her.
> - Six Knights and two Priests of that rotation stand in the hall as witnesses, and they are the Mobilization.
> - The Order's council sits outside Waterdeep and sealed the Writ of Knighthood before Savra signed it, because the rank is the council's to give.
> - Isaldric Veyne is a cleric of Tyr at the 13th level or higher who can cast *Resurrection*, and he needs no stat block because he never fights.
> - If **Savra's Past Known** is marked for the member, Savra adds one sentence at the end of The Investiture.
> - If **Order Recognized** or **Devil Sent Back** is marked, one speaker adds a line in The Investiture, and neither outcome changes a benefit.
> - Nobody in this Event names Manshoon or Jarlaxle unless **Manshoon Named** or **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked for the member, and nobody names Illuun.
> - The Cassalanters stay a suspicion in this Event, and no speaker states what the family worships.

### The Hall

The main hall of the Halls of Justice holds sixty people, and today it holds the member, Savra, Isaldric Veyne and the Mobilization. Brother Haldric Wence keeps the door, and Brother Hallin Ostrel waits at a side table with the Order's roll open. Father Corwen Thale stands among the witnesses, and members who are invested together stand side by side before the altar. Dame Rhosyn Cullane leads the six Knights, and Mother Odessa Leck and Father Nolan Bray wear the vestments of the two Priests.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The main hall of the Halls of Justice is cool and bright, with a long altar at the far end. Eight people stand in two lines beside the aisle, six with swords at their hips and two in priests' vestments. An old man waits at the altar with a sword laid across his palms, and Savra stands a pace behind him. The old man looks at you as you come down the aisle and lifts his head a little.
>
> > "Come forward and stand before the altar. I am Isaldric Veyne, the Even-Handed's servant in this house, and I will invest you today."

> [!social]**The High Priest of Tyr**
>
> Isaldric Veyne (Lawful Good, Human, he/him) :: the high priest of Tyr in Waterdeep, a tall man in his seventies with white hair and a silver set of scales on his chest.
>
> Veyne is Friendly and grave, and he speaks slowly in whole sentences that never need repeating. He calls Tyr "the Even-Handed", swears rarely and mildly, and answers every question the member asks without hurrying.
>
> Veyne is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the rank means and what the Order asks of the member in return.
> - Sir Aldwin Dunhallow, who held the rank before, and the sword on the altar.
> - The Resurrection pledge and how the casting is done.
> - The Writ of Knighthood and the places that honor it.
>
> He will not discuss Savra's past or any case the Order is working, and he answers each with "That is Savra's to tell, or the Order's."

> [!qna]**Why am I being invested?**
>
> > "You have done the Order's work for a long time, and you have done it well. The council read your record and sealed the writ without argument. I was glad to put my name beside theirs."

> [!qna]**Who held the rank before me?**
>
> > "Sir Aldwin Dunhallow, a senior Knight of this chapter, held it twelve years. He died six years ago, of a fever caught nursing fire victims. His sword has lain on this altar since, and today it touches your shoulders."

> [!qna]**What does the Order ask of me now?**
>
> > "Nothing you did not swear to already. You will be asked more often, and by more people, to stand when standing is required."

### The Investiture

Isaldric Veyne holds the investiture with Aldwin Dunhallow's sword, which has lain on the altar since his death. Savra stands at his side as a witness, and with several members he lays the sword on each in turn.

If **Order Recognized** is marked, Savra speaks first. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra steps up beside the altar and speaks to you before the old man lifts the sword.
>
> > "On the night of the hearing, the Order named you in writing. Today it names you before the altar, where the Order's roll is kept."

If **Devil Sent Back** is marked, Veyne speaks next. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Veyne glances down the hall toward the doors, and then he turns back to you.
>
> > "A devil came into this hall with a writ, and you sent it back down. I have not forgotten it, and this house will not forget it either."

Then read the investiture aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Veyne lifts the sword in both hands, and light from the high windows runs along a blade worn thin by sharpening. The witnesses on either side stand still, and someone shifts a boot on the stone behind you. He nods to the worn flagstones before the altar, and you kneel on the spot where he points.
>
> > "By the authority of the Order of the Gauntlet, I invest you as Righteous Hand. Tyr, Helm and Torm are witness to it, and so are the people in this hall. Rise, and carry it as Aldwin Dunhallow did."
>
> The flat of the blade touches your right shoulder and then your left, and the witnesses answer together.
>
> > "By the same witness."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra steps forward as Veyne lowers the sword, and she takes a silver gauntlet pin from inside her coat. It is finer work than the plain pin from your oath, and she fastens it to your collar herself.
>
> > "This pin is yours for good, whatever happens to your Renown. I'll tell you plainly that I have never held this rank myself. So I won't pretend to know what it will cost you."

If **Savra's Past Known** is marked for the member, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra finishes the pin and then looks up at you, with her hand still resting on your shoulder.
>
> > "You heard what I did at that stairwell door, and you stayed to hear the rest. I haven't forgotten that, and I won't, whatever rank you hold."

### The Writ of Knighthood

> [!readaloud]
>
> Veyne takes a rolled sheet of vellum from the altar and unrolls it to show two signatures at the foot. A heavy grey seal sits beside them, with a gauntlet pressed into the wax inside a circle. He holds it out in both hands, and Savra reads the first line aloud from beside him.
>
> > "Allied temples and civic authorities across the Sword Coast will honor this writ. Some will not, and you should expect that."

> [!exploration]**The Writ of Knighthood**
>
> Each Righteous Hand receives a writ of knighthood in their own name.
>
> - **What it is:** a vellum writ under the Order's central seal, signed by Savra and by Isaldric Veyne, that names the member and the rank. It proves who the member is and what they have done, and it commands nobody.
> - **Where it works:** the writ is honored in Waterdeep, Neverwinter, Baldur's Gate and Daggerford, and refused in Luskan. Elsewhere on the Sword Coast, a town honors it when it has a temple of Tyr, Helm or Torm.
> - **Contact:** the member shows the writ to a temple's door-warden or an official's clerk, who reads the seal and answers the same day.
> - **Standing:** the member may swear as a witness and file a written complaint before a magistrate or temple court in any city that recognizes the Order.
> - **The open door:** the member has Advantage on one Charisma (Persuasion) check to win a hearing with a Watch captain, a temple head or a magistrate.
> - **Lodging:** any temple of Tyr, Helm or Torm lodges the member and up to four companions for three nights a visit, free of charge.
> - **Correspondence:** Brother Hallin seals a letter for the member at the clerk's desk, and any temple or civic authority that respects the Order answers within a tenday.
> - **Limit:** the open door works once per official each tenday, and the writ grants no power to arrest and no immunity.
> - **Below ground:** the writ does nothing in Undermountain, where no official can read it, and a city that distrusts the Order ignores it.
> - **Loss rule:** a member whose Renown falls below 50 keeps the writ, and its effects wait until the Renown is restored.

### The Mobilization

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra turns to the witnesses on the left and nods once to the woman at the head of the line. The six Knights step forward together and halt, and the two priests in vestments stay where they stand. Savra waits until the last boot has stopped scuffing on the stone, and then she speaks to you.
>
> > "This is the Mobilization, six Knights and two Priests of the Waterdeep chapter. You name the operation and the day, and they come ready with three days' notice. Dame Rhosyn Cullane leads them, and Mother Odessa Leck leads the priests."

> [!qna]**Who are they?**
>
> > "They are the rotation I answer for at the Halls. I'd trust any of them with my back. Most of them you have met at one door or another."

> [!qna]**What will they do for us?**
>
> > "They fight where you point them, and they follow your plan until the work is done. They won't strike the Watch, a Lord or anyone who has surrendered."

> [!qna]**What if they are hurt?**
>
> > "A Knight steps back from the fight once she's lost half her strength. The priests stay behind the line and tend whoever falls."

> [!qna]**Can we call them below the city?**
>
> > "They'll go below the city only under the Mandate, which I'll show you when the others leave. Until then they march on the surface and nowhere else."

> [!exploration]**Calling the Mobilization**
>
> Each Righteous Hand can call the Mobilization, and the force serves one operation in each quest.
>
> - **Contact:** the member gives Savra the place, the enemy and the day, either in person at the Halls or in a note left with Brother Haldric Wence.
> - **Notice:** the force is ready three days after the member gives notice, and it meets them at the Halls or at a named place on the surface.
> - **The operation:** one major operation in each quest, against a named enemy, and the force follows the member's plan until the objective is done.
> - **One force:** the use is counted once for the whole party, because there is one force. If two members ask for the same day, the first to ask spends it.
> - **Below ground:** the force marches on the surface unless the member holds **Undermountain Mandate Accepted**, which is described in The Mandate.
> - **Refusals:** the force refuses an order to attack the Watch, a Lord or a creature that has surrendered, and it leaves at once if the member lied about the enemy.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 50 keeps the rank, and the Mobilization waits until the Renown is restored.

> [!gamemaster]**The Mobilization as Allies**
>
> The six Knights are Dame Rhosyn Cullane, Sir Anselm Greave, Sir Merrick Tollwood, Dame Corra Whitlock, Sir Gavric Ferrel and Sir Alaric Pryor. The two Priests are Mother Odessa Leck and Father Nolan Bray. Dame Rhosyn, Sir Anselm and Sir Merrick are also the Strike Team from **Vindicator**, so the two forces never take the field together.
>
> Use the **Knight** and **Priest** stat blocks from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, unmodified. The number of Knights who fight is the number of player characters minus one, up to six, and each adds Power only while it fights. A Knight is worth 30 from levels 5 to 10 and 25 from level 11. The Priests hold the rear and add nothing. Allied Party Power for three, four and five PCs, with the budgets in brackets:
>
> | Level | Knights fighting | Allied Party Power | Bruising budget | Bloody budget |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | 8 | 2 / 3 / 4 | 192 / 266 / 340 | 115.2 / 159.6 / 204 | 144 / 199.5 / 255 |
> | 9 | 2 / 3 / 4 | 207 / 286 / 365 | 124.2 / 171.6 / 219 | 155.25 / 214.5 / 273.75 |
> | 10 | 2 / 3 / 4 | 219 / 302 / 385 | 131.4 / 181.2 / 231 | 164.25 / 226.5 / 288.75 |
> | 11 | 2 / 3 / 4 | 236 / 323 / 410 | 141.6 / 193.8 / 246 | 177 / 242.25 / 307.5 |
>
> A Knight is below twice the Power of an 8th-level character (44), so the High-Power Warning does not apply. At five PCs the force raises Party Power by 55 percent at level 8, which is a major addition. When the member calls the Mobilization for a fight you build in advance, build it on the allied total. When the force answers an aid call in Undermountain, where the level's encounters are already sized for the base party, treat the Knights as a reserve that joins at the start of round 3.
>
> #### The Mobilization's Tactics
>
> - Dame Rhosyn holds the line beside the member's front-line character and uses Parry whenever a melee attack hits her.
> - The other Knights pair off against one enemy at a time and use the Heavy Crossbow at range and the Greatsword within reach.
> - The Priests stay behind the front line, use Divine Aid for *Bless* before the fight and for *Healing Word* on any ally at 0 Hit Points.
> - A Knight steps back at 26 Hit Points or fewer and joins the Priests.
> - The force falls back to the nearest exit if Dame Rhosyn falls or three Knights have withdrawn.
> - The force lowers its blades when a creature yields and keeps the prisoner for the member to question.

### The Resurrection Pledge

> [!readaloud]
>
> Veyne sets a sheet of parchment on the altar and dips a pen in the ink that Brother Hallin has brought. Savra stands beside him with her hands behind her back, and she watches the pen move across the page.
>
> > "If you die in the Order's work, the Order will pay for one casting of *Resurrection*. I will cast it myself, here in this house. The Order buys the diamond, and the casting is recorded today."

> [!exploration]**The Resurrection Pledge**
>
> Each Righteous Hand holds one pledge, recorded in their name on the Order's roll and on the altar.
>
> - **What it is:** one casting of *Resurrection*, a 7th-level spell that takes 1 hour, cast by Isaldric Veyne.
> - **The diamond:** the Order buys the diamond, which must be worth 1,000 gp or more, and the spell consumes it.
> - **Who:** the member, or one companion or ally whose name the member gives Brother Hallin in writing before the need arises.
> - **Limit:** one casting for each member, spent when the casting finishes.
> - **Delivery:** the body must be carried to the Halls of Justice on foot, up the stairs, because teleportation and *word of recall* fail below the city.
> - **Contact:** whoever carries the body asks Brother Haldric Wence at the door, and he sends for Veyne at once.
> - **Casting:** Veyne casts it within a day of the body's arrival, in the nave, and the Order asks nothing of the person it returns.
> - **Loss rule:** if the member's Renown falls below 50, the pledge stays on the roll and the Order will not fund the casting until the Renown returns.

### The Mandate

When the other witnesses have gone, Savra sits on the altar steps with a folded document that carries the Order's central seal. The witnesses file out with Isaldric Veyne, and Savra stays with the members who were invested, who hear the Mandate together and answer one by one.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The footsteps fade out of the nave, and the hall is quiet apart from the draught under the doors. Savra sits on the altar steps and sets a folded paper beside her, sealed with the Order's central seal.
>
> > "The council wrote the Undermountain Mandate, and I'll explain it before you decide. If you sign, the Order will answer one call from you on every level you enter. A courier carries your call up the stairs, and the answer walks down."

> [!social]**The Knight on the Steps**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: the Order's knight in Waterdeep, a woman in her late twenties with dark hair worn back, a strong jaw and a silver gauntlet pin at her collar.
>
> Savra is grave and exact, and she states each limit before she names the benefit it belongs to. She pauses before every serious answer, gives her single nod only to an honest one and swears rarely, in short bursts aimed at wrongdoing. She does not push the document toward the member, and she does not pull it back.
>
> Savra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Mandate gives the member and what it asks.
> - The aid call, the courier and the days an answer takes.
> - Why the council left the choice to the member.
>
> She will not discuss what lies in Undermountain, because she does not know, and she answers each question about it with "I don't know yet, and I won't guess."

> [!qna]**What does the Mandate give me?**
>
> > "It lets the Order follow you below the city. You can call for aid once on every level you enter, and the Mobilization may march down to meet you."

> [!qna]**What does it cost me?**
>
> > "Nothing in coin, and nothing in Renown. You carry the Order's name below, and what you do with it goes on the roll."

> [!qna]**Why is the choice mine?**
>
> > "Because I won't order anyone down that fucking hole, and the council wouldn't either. The council argued over it for two tendays before it left the choice to you."

> [!qna]**What happens if I say no?**
>
> > "Then I keep the paper, and nothing changes between us. If you come back and want it, I will fetch it the same day."

> [!qna]**What is down there?**
>
> > "I don't know yet, and I won't guess. You will see it before I do, so send word up when you have something."

> [!exploration]**Calling for Aid**
>
> Each Righteous Hand who signs the Mandate can send one aid call for every Undermountain level they enter.
>
> - **Contact:** the member writes the level, the place and the request, signs it and presses their pin into the wax. A carrier takes it up the stairs to Brother Teodric Naal, a Priest Acolyte who sits at a back table of the Yawning Portal from dawn to dusk.
> - **Delay:** Brother Teodric takes the note to Brother Haldric the same day. The answer reaches the named level's arrival stair after three days plus one day for every three levels of depth, rounded up.
> - **The Mobilization:** the member may ask for the force, which spends the quest's one operation, so a member who used it on the surface cannot call it below in the same quest.
> - **The Priests:** the member may ask instead for Mother Odessa and Father Nolan, who hold the arrival stair for one day and one night and carry any dead or wounded up to the Halls on request.
> - **Limit:** one aid call for each level the member enters, spent when the answer arrives. A level the member leaves and enters again gives no second call.
> - **No other word:** the Order casts no *Sending*, so every note and every answer travels on foot.
> - **Renown loss:** a member whose Renown falls below 50 keeps the Mandate, and the aid call waits until the Renown is restored.

If the member signs, mark **Undermountain Mandate Accepted** and read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> You take the pen from Savra and sign your name on the line below the council's seal. She reads the signature, gives her single nod and slides the paper into her coat as she gets to her feet.
>
> > "Good, then I'll file a copy with the council today. Send word up the stairs when you have something to tell me."

If the member leaves the paper, read the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> You leave the paper where it lies, and Savra folds it and puts it inside her coat without looking at it. She gets to her feet and waits at the foot of the steps until you have stood as well.
>
> > "All right, and I won't ask why you decided it. If you change your mind, come to the Halls and the paper will be here."

### Mad Mage Seeds

> [!gamemaster]**Seeds for Dungeon of the Mad Mage**
>
> These are one-line facts. *Dungeon of the Mad Mage* has no Order of the Gauntlet, so use these and no others.
>
> - **Level 15:** the ghost of Fidelio, a paladin of Tyr, haunts the Obstacle Course. A follower of Tyr can persuade him to rest with a **DC 20 Charisma (Persuasion)** check after someone else has killed Netherskull.
> - **Level 21:** Fazrian, once a planetar of Torm, is corrupted. A character it judges innocent can ask it to judge itself with a **DC 26 Charisma (Persuasion)** check.
> - **Level 22:** Dezmyr and Zalthar Shadowdusk, former paladins of Torm, are now death knights, and a shrine of Torm with a white gauntlet fresco stands in the hold.
> - **Level 23:** Aryx, a deva sent by Tyr to reach Fazrian, is trapped in a shield in the vaults.
> - The adventure has no Order of the Gauntlet. The white gauntlet is Torm's symbol, and Torm is one of the Triad the Order serves.
> - The transport and *Sending* limits are recorded in **Force Grey Commander** and are not repeated here. The Order has no *Sending*, so every courier and every Priest walks the stairs.

### Renown Opportunities

The rank event awards no Renown, gold or Milestone Points, and Righteous Hand has no higher rank to reach. Further Renown comes from the ordinary rewards listed on the Order of the Gauntlet page of the Factions Guide.

### Aftermath

The member can tell companions what they were given, but the writ, the Mobilization, the pledge and the Mandate stay with the member. Brother Haldric and Brother Hallin turn away a companion who asks in the member's name. A member whose Renown later falls below 50 keeps the rank and the pin, and the benefits pause until the Renown returns.

Record separately for each member:

- The companion or ally named in the Resurrection pledge, and whether the casting has been spent.
- Whether the Mobilization has been used in the current quest.
- The Undermountain levels where an aid call was spent.

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes when the member has been invested, has received the writ, the Mobilization and the pledge, and has answered the Mandate.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Righteous Hand Reached** — mark with the recipient's name when Veyne invests the member; read by **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** (unconverted) content that checks standing with the Order.
> - **Undermountain Mandate Accepted** — mark with the member's name when they sign the document; read by **Dungeon of the Mad Mage** (unconverted) for the aid call and for the Mobilization below ground.
>
> This Event also reads **Vindicator Reached** (the hold), **Savra's Past Known** (one line from Savra), **Order Recognized** (one line from Savra) and **Devil Sent Back** (one line from Veyne). It reads **Quietly Reassigned** to decide who writes the note. It reads **Manshoon Named** and **Jarlaxle Unmasked** only for the speakers' wording.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> The writ, the Mobilization and the pledge are active for this member from the next morning. If the member signed the Mandate, the first aid call is available on the first Undermountain level they enter. This is the final Order of the Gauntlet rank, and its benefits stay available for later quests. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

Isaldric Veyne, the high priest of Tyr, invests an Order of the Gauntlet member as Righteous Hand in the Halls of Justice, and Savra offers the Undermountain Mandate once the witnesses have left.

## Summary

We were invested as Righteous Hand in the main hall of the Halls of Justice by Isaldric Veyne, the high priest of Tyr. We received a writ of knighthood, the Mobilization of six Knights and two Priests, and a pledge to pay for one Resurrection. Afterward Savra showed us the Undermountain Mandate, and each of us signed it or left it on the steps.
