# Design Notes: Righteous Hand

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B and the Factions Guide give Righteous Hand a mobilization of six Knights and two Priests, a knighthood recognized by allied temples, and one free Resurrection. The restored draft added a summons to Trollskull Manor, a ceremony with Sister Valdra as witness, a Mandate that extends the Order underground and a branch on **Vault of Dragons**. It had Savra say she gave the rank "four years ago", named *Resurrection* a 5th-level spell and ended on a True or False heading with no outcome for the rank itself.

## What Changed

The note now waits until the member is on the surface, because nothing from the Order reaches Undermountain, and the investiture is three days after it arrives. Isaldric Veyne, the high priest of Tyr, invests the member with the sword of the last holder, Sir Aldwin Dunhallow, and casts the Resurrection. Savra has never held the rank. The Mobilization is six named Knights and two Priests with the ally Power figures from the mechanics reference, and the Strike Team from **Vindicator** is part of the six. The Knighthood is a written credential with four effects and a list of limits. The Resurrection is a 7th-level spell with a 1,000 gp diamond, cast once, with the body carried up by stairs. The Mandate is a signed paper that lets the Mobilization go below the city and gives one aid call for each level, answered on foot.

## Departures from the Source

The Vault branch is cut. Sister Valdra appears nowhere, as the Session 45 ruling requires, and the pin passes from Savra. The draft's weapon reveal is dropped, because **Vindicator** fixes one provenance. The Mobilization is one force for the whole party, not one per member, so two members who ask for the same day share it. The mandate's "one mobilization per significant threat" becomes one aid call per level, and the force counts against the quest's single operation. The pledge covers any death in the Order's work, not only a fiend or undead, which matches the draft's "no other conditions". **Order Recognized** and **Devil Sent Back** each add one line, which the **Writ and the Weight** outcomes promised.

## Invented Names and Open Items

Invented: Isaldric Veyne, Sir Aldwin Dunhallow, Dame Corra Whitlock, Sir Gavric Ferrel, Sir Alaric Pryor, Mother Odessa Leck, Father Nolan Bray and Brother Teodric Naal. Each was grep-checked against `campaign/` with no collision. Dame Rhosyn Cullane, Sir Anselm Greave and Sir Merrick Tollwood come from **Vindicator**, Brother Haldric Wence and Father Corwen Thale from the First Meeting, and Brother Hallin Ostrel from **The Tithe**. Dunhallow's twelve-year tenure, the fever and the six years are invented, and the death does not overlap the Book of the Fallen in **Vindicator**. The Veyne, Mobilization and Mandate lines have no voice profiles and are voiced from the event text.

Also invented are the list of cities that honor the writ, the six-day-plus rule for an aid call, the round-3 arrival of the reserve Knights and the courier's table at the Yawning Portal. The four Mad Mage seeds come from the adventure at levels 15, 21, 22 and 23.
