# The Imp at Dawn

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event follows the Black Viper Investigation Event by forty-eight hours — it triggers automatically. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Notice surveillance of Trollskull Manor beginning: a hire-coach passes twice on the first day; a small winged shape appears near the upper windows before dawn on the second night.
> - Detect the shape with Passive Perception 14, or with a DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) check.
> - Capture the imp before it vanishes, with a DC 16 Dexterity check or a restraint or banishment spell.
> - Deliver a captured imp to Savra, who files it at the Halls of Justice as the first hard evidence that whoever is watching the party employs an infernal creature.
> - Receive Savra's full debrief and hear her question: "What are they moving?"
>
> #### The Imp
>
> The imp is small, quick, and disciplined about its invisibility. Observation is its mandate — it is not aggressive and will flee rather than fight. Use standard **Imp** statistics (2024 *Monster Manual*).

> [!gamemaster]**Background**
>
> The Cassalanter household assigned the imp to monitor Trollskull Manor after the party's inquiry into Gaxly. It stays invisible during approach and retreat, and adjusts its position as lights move inside the building. Its capture would give the Order something it has been missing: physical evidence, on record, that an infernal creature was watching the party — and given the timing and context, a compelling circumstantial argument for who sent it.

### The Watch on Trollskull Alley

Surveillance begins as a background thread running across two days.

*Day one:* A hire-coach passes Trollskull Alley twice at a slow roll — not the behavior of a lost driver or a fare searching for an address.

*Night two, before dawn:* A character with **Passive Perception 14** notices a small winged shape near the upper windows, hovering and adjusting its position as the light inside moves from room to room.

> [!readaloud]
> It is the small hours of the second night when whoever is keeping watch notices a shape near the upper windows. It has its wings held close against its body and hovers at the level of the second-floor sill, adjusting its position by small degrees whenever the light inside changes, as though it is following something moving through the rooms. The movement is too deliberate for a bat, too controlled and patient for a bird startled from its roost, and it has been in roughly the same position long enough that any ordinary creature would have moved on.

### Catching the Imp

The imp lingers near the upper windows until it believes it has been noticed, then breaks for the rooftop.

> [!exploration]**The Imp by Sound and Air**
>
> Any character who actively searches for the shape and makes a successful **DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) check** locates it by the faint displacement of air near the eaves and the near-silence of its wings against cold stone.
>
> - **Auto:** A character with Passive Perception 14 or higher notices the shape without a check.

When the party commits to a direction, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> As soon as the first of you moves toward it, the shape lifts sharply from the sill and pulls back along the roofline in a single clean motion, rising until it loses definition against the darkness above the alley. You can track it for a moment by sound — the near-silence of something small and quick moving against cold stone — and then there is nothing, or nearly nothing, depending on how fast you move.

> [!hazard]**Catching the Imp**
>
> A **DC 16 Dexterity (Athletics or Acrobatics) check**, or any spell that restrains or banishes without first requiring a visible target, catches the imp before it clears the roofline. The imp does not fight; if it is not caught this round, it is gone.

If the check succeeds, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> When the restraint takes hold, the imp's invisibility drops away all at once, and what you have in hand is a small devil, roughly the size of a house cat, leathery-winged, with a barbed tail and the look of something that fully expected to be gone before you could reach it. It neither struggles nor speaks. It keeps watching you, looking at each face in turn with a slow, careful attention, as if it means to remember every one of them.

> [!exploration]**The Binding Marks**
>
> Any character who examines the captured imp and makes a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion)** check, or any character with a religious background, identifies the marks on its skin as those of a devil in true service rather than a summoned spirit. The marks say what it is. They do not say who bound it.
>
> > Thin lines of scar tissue run in a neat spiral from the base of each wing to the root of the tail, too regular to be wounds, like a brand that someone took great care over.

> [!gamemaster]**The Filing**
>
> Savra takes the imp directly to the Halls of Justice and files it formally. The record states that an infernal creature was deployed to watch Trollskull Manor, and notes that it appeared forty-eight hours after the party's first questions at the Wazoo. This is the most significant entry in the Order's file to date, and Savra treats it that way.

If the check fails, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> The imp clears the roofline above the alley and drops out of sight on the far side before you can close the distance. By the time you reach the edge there is nothing in the air and nothing moving in the lane below, only the dark rooftops running down toward the harbor.

> [!gamemaster]**If the Imp Escapes**
>
> Surveillance continues at a distance. Whatever sent the imp now knows the party is working for the Gauntlet. By Mission 4, Gysheer Omfreys' handlers have had warning enough to prepare.

### Savra's Debrief

Savra meets the party at day's end — at Trollskull Manor if it is safe, otherwise at a Watch post in the North Ward she has access to.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra meets you at the end of the day and listens to the full account from beginning to end without interrupting: the Gaxly interview, the Hall of Records, the Seffia meeting, and whatever happened with the shape at the upper windows. When it is over, she is quiet for a moment, looking at what she has written down, and then she looks up.
>
> > "What are they moving?"
>
> She turns back to her notes after she has asked it and does not try to answer.

> [!social]**The Debrief**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Neutral Good, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: hearing the full shape of the investigation for the first time.
>
> Savra listens without interrupting and does not summarize what she has heard back to the party. Her question — "What are they moving?" — is genuine and open: something is being transported or prepared, Seffia's surveillance of the party confirms that, and she does not know what it is. She will not speculate out loud on who is responsible for the imp until the evidence is stronger.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss include:
> - What the Hall of Records track found, which she finds significant.
> - What happened at the Seffia meeting, which she processes without visible reaction.
> - The imp, which she takes directly to the Halls of Justice the same evening.

> [!qna]**What does the imp mean?**
>
> She pauses before she answers.
>
> > "It means whoever sent it has access to something I'd like to know more about. I'm going to file it and let the record speak for itself. I'll tell you plainly: it's the most useful thing I've had in months."

> [!qna]**Who do you think sent it?**
>
> > "I think you know who I think sent it. I'm not going to say it until I can stand behind it."

### Concluding the Event

The mission closes with Savra's debrief. Whether the imp was caught or not, the investigation has moved forward, and both sides know the other is paying attention.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> Mark each outcome that occurs. Later events read them.
>
> - **Planted Story Exposed** — mark when the planted story is confirmed fabricated and reported to Savra, through the Hall of Records track, the Seffia confrontation, or both; award +1 Renown. Read by Mission 4 (Gysheer's handlers are less alert if this is marked).
> - **Forged Record Secured** — mark when the forged Hall of Records entry is recovered, or Seffia Naelryke is identified by name and her contact method delivered to Savra; award +1 Renown. Read by Mission 4.
> - **Imp Captured** — mark when the imp is taken alive and delivered to the Halls of Justice; award +1 Renown (bonus). Read by **Cassalanter Villa** (the Halls of Justice filing is in the record when the party moves against the family).

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> What the party did with Seffia determines the Cassalanter response rate through Mission 4: a deal accepted means quieter surveillance and deliberate patience; an open confrontation or refusal means faster escalation and a more alert response from Gysheer's handlers.
>
> When the party reaches Renown 4 and character level 4, **Order of the Gauntlet Mission 3 — The Shard Shunners** becomes available.

## Overview

Surveillance of Trollskull Manor begins. Before dawn, an imp watches the upper windows. The party has one night to catch it.

## Summary

We noticed something watching Trollskull Manor in the small hours of the second night after we talked to Gaxly — a small winged shape at the upper windows, hovering and adjusting as the light inside moved from room to room. We had one chance to catch it before it vanished. Savra heard the whole report and left with one question she had no answer to: what are they moving?
