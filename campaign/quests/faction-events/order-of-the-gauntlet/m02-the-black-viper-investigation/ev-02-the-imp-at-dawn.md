# The Imp at Dawn

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary

**Background (DM only)**
The imp was assigned by the Cassalanter household to monitor activity at Trollskull Manor after the party's inquiry into Gaxly. It is small, quick, and disciplined about its invisibility. Observation is its mandate — it is not aggressive. Its material form, once captured, carries infernal binding marks that constitute hard evidence of Cassalanter access to devil-bound servitors. Savra takes it seriously.

The imp uses standard **Imp** statistics (2024 *Monster Manual*). It stays invisible during approach and retreat. It will flee rather than fight.

### The Watch on Trollskull Alley

Surveillance begins as a background thread. Two visible signs, one per day:

*Day one:* A hire-coach passes Trollskull Alley twice at a slow roll — not the behavior of a lost driver or a fare searching for an address.

*Night two:* A character with **Passive Perception 14** notices a small winged shape near the upper windows — too deliberate for a bat, too controlled for a bird, hovering and adjusting its position as lights move inside the building.

### Catching the Imp

The imp is invisible during its approach and when it retreats. A **DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) check** locates it by sound and displaced air. A **DC 16 Dexterity (Athletics or Acrobatics) check**, or any spell that restrains or banishes without first requiring a visible target, catches it before it vanishes.

On capture: stripped of invisibility, the imp is unmistakably a Cassalanter servant. Its infernal binding marks — visible to any character with a religious background or a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) check** — identify its contractual origin. Savra takes it directly to the Halls of Justice and files it formally. Physical evidence of Cassalanter access to devil-bound servitors, on record.

**If not caught:** The imp departs and surveillance continues at a distance. The Cassalanters know the party is working for the Gauntlet. They plan accordingly. By Mission 4, Gysheer Omfreys' handlers have had warning.

### Savra's Debrief

Savra meets the party at day's end — at Trollskull Manor if it is safe, otherwise at a Watch post in the North Ward she has access to. She hears everything: the Gaxly interview, what happened at the Hall of Records, what happened with Seffia, the imp.

She listens without interrupting. Then she asks one question: "What are they moving?"

She has no answer. Neither does anyone else. But the question is open now, and she keeps it.

## Overview

Surveillance of Trollskull Manor begins. Before dawn, an imp watches the upper windows. The party has one night to catch it.

## Read Aloud

> [!narrative]
> It is the third watch when you see it — a shape near the upper window, too deliberate for a bat, hovering and adjusting as the light inside shifts. It is not moving toward you. It is watching.

## Summary

The Cassalanter imp network activated surveillance of Trollskull Manor within 48 hours of the Gaxly interview. The party had one opportunity to catch the imp before it vanished. Savra heard the full report and left with one question unanswered: what are they moving?
