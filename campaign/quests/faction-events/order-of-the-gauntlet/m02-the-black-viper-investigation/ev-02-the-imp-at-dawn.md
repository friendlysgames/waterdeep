# The Imp at Dawn

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Social Event begins on the afternoon of the second day, when a hired coach rolls past Trollskull Manor. It ends at dusk on the third day, when Savra Belabranta takes the members' report. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Notice the coach and a raven that watch Trollskull Manor.
> - Work out that the raven is a devil by three independent leads.
> - Watch the chimney through a night clock and choose the moment to take the raven.
> - Catch the imp and question it, or let it fly.
> - Finish any open lead from **The Black Viper Investigation** before dusk on the third day.
> - Report to Savra and hear the question she keeps.
> - Report at the east annex of the Halls of Justice instead of the manor, if a member is marked **Quietly Reassigned**.
>
> Only Order of the Gauntlet members attend Savra's debrief, and their companions help with everything else in the Event.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The raven is an imp in raven form. It is sent to count who comes and goes at Trollskull Manor, and a bond carries what it sees to whoever sent it.
> - The sender is the same person who sent the secretary. The villa keeps six imps in raven form, and the GM can take this one for the seventh sent out. Nothing the party can learn here proves it, and the imp's body carries no mark.
> - Nobody in this Event knows whose imp it is. The imp will not say, and Savra's file reads "a devil watched the party's house; the sender is unproven."
> - The imp does not fight unless it is cornered, and it flees whenever it is seen.
> - Nobody in this Event mentions Manshoon.

### Day Table

> [!gamemaster]**The Three Days**
>
> - **Day 1, dawn:** Savra knocks. The party questions Gaxly, visits the Hall of Records and leaves a red candle at Barrow's Wick. Night 1: the raven roosts on the chimney in every case, because the watcher has not yet been set.
> - **Day 2, morning:** The card from Barrow's Wick arrives. At highsun the party meets the secretary at the Steaming Anchor.
> - **Day 2, afternoon:** A hired coach passes Trollskull Alley. Night 2: the raven roosts in every case, and the watcher state sets how ready it is.
> - **Day 3, dawn:** The raven leaves the chimney. This is the party's last chance to take it.
> - **Day 3, dusk:** Savra knocks and takes the debrief, whatever the party has done. Anything not finished by then is not reported.

### The Alley

> [!readaloud]
>
> Trollskull Alley is quiet this afternoon, with a baker's boy whistling at the far end and washing hung between the upper windows. A hired coach with its blinds drawn rolls up the lane at a walking pace and turns at the end. A few minutes later it comes back the other way, and the driver keeps his eyes on the horses the whole time.

A character who watches the coach and makes a successful **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check sees that the driver never looks at the manor and that the blinds do not move. In a **Calm** state the coach passes once, and in a **Wary** or **Hunting** state it passes twice. A gloved hand paid the driver in advance, and he knows nothing else.

> [!gamemaster]**How Watchful the Watcher Is**
>
> What the party did with the secretary at the Steaming Anchor sets the state. A party that never met her counts as **Wary**. The raven roosts on Night 1 in every state, and a character who tries then uses **DC 14**. The state sets Night 2 and the dawn of Day 3 only.
>
> - **Calm** (played along): On Night 2 and at Day 3 dawn the raven sits loose on the chimney, and the check is **DC 14**.
> - **Wary** (showed the leaf, walked away, or never met her): On Night 2 and at Day 3 dawn the raven sits ready to fly, and the check is **DC 16**.
> - **Hunting** (held her): On Night 2 and at Day 3 dawn the raven flies at the first sound on the roof, and the check is **DC 18**.

### The Raven

A raven sits on the northernmost chimney, beside the turret, from dusk until dawn. Ravens do not roost on Trollskull Alley, and the pigeons keep away from the stack.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A black bird sits on the northernmost chimney with its head turned toward the manor, and it follows every lamp lit in a window with a slow roll of its neck. It does not preen or feed. The pigeons on the neighbouring roof have moved to the far end of the ridge and watch it from there.

Three leads each show that the raven is not a raven, and any one is enough. A failed check costs the party an hour and never closes another lead.

> [!exploration]**Lead One: The Watcher That Never Rests**
>
> A character who watches the chimney for two hours and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception or Survival)** check sees that the bird never feeds, sleeps or shifts its feet. On a failure, a second attempt after an hour needs no check.

> [!exploration]**Lead Two: The Wrong Animal**
>
> A character who studies it and makes a successful **DC 12 Wisdom (Animal Handling or Nature)** check notes that the pigeons avoid it and that its eye does not blink when the lamplight changes. On a failure, a stray cat in the lane arches its back and hisses until the party takes the hint.

> [!exploration]**Lead Three: Detect Evil and Good**
>
> A party cleric's own casting of *Detect Evil and Good* within 30 feet of the chimney senses a Fiend with no check. A priest of Tyr at the Halls of Justice will cast it for a 25 gp donation, if a character asks at the door-warden's desk by noon of the same day.

### The Night Clock

> [!gamemaster]**The Chimney Through the Night**
>
> - **19:00, dusk:** The raven lands on the northernmost chimney.
> - **22:00:** The taproom closes and the lamps go out floor by floor, and the raven's head follows each one.
> - **Midnight:** Every lamp is dark. The raven turns to face the front door and stays there.
> - **05:00:** In the first grey light it turns its head to the east.
> - **05:30, dawn:** It opens its wings and drops from the chimney. This is the last moment to take it.
>
> A character on the turret roof, or on the fourth-floor balcony, can reach the chimney's edge. A net bought at any Dock Ward chandler for 1 gp gives Advantage on the check below.

> [!exploration]**Taking the Raven**
>
> A character within arm's reach makes a **Dexterity (Athletics or Acrobatics)** check against the DC in the state table above. On a success, the character Grapples the bird. On a failure, the raven flies east over the roofs, too fast to follow, and does not return that night.
>
> - **Held:** The imp tries to slip the Grapple using its action. The escape DC is 8 plus the grappler's Strength modifier plus Proficiency Bonus.
> - **Held in a barrel:** An imp in a nailed barrel or an iron-bound chest cannot lift the lid. Trollskull Manor has a barrel in the cellar.
> - **Killed:** The body reverts to the red, horned shape of an imp and carries no mark. It is not captured, and it earns no bonus.

> [!hazard]**The Imp Cornered**
>
> The raven is an **Imp** in raven form. It flees when it is seen and fights only when held. The roster is one **Imp** at every party size, which makes a Mild fight with a Day Cost of 2. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits it.
>
> #### The Imp's Tactics
> To begin, the imp tries to slip the Grapple and Shape-Shifts into a spider or a rat if that helps.
>
> During combat, the imp:
> - Stings the character who holds it, once a round, and never a creature at 0 hit points.
> - Casts *Invisibility* on itself only if it gets free. It does so as an action and loses it the moment it attacks.
> - Flies at 60 feet in raven form if it breaks the Grapple, and does not return that night.
>
> The imp yields at 10 hit points or fewer, or when it is sealed in a barrel, because it gives up its post rather than die. A party that does not wish to fight can let it go, and it flies east where nobody can follow it across the rooftops.

> [!social]**The Raven in the Barrel**
>
> The Imp (Lawful Evil, Devil, it/its) :: a spiteful, quick-tongued devil the size of a cat, which swears freely and answers nothing it has not been ordered to.
>
> The imp speaks Common and Infernal. It mocks its captors and refuses every question about its master. A character who makes a successful **DC 18 Charisma (Intimidation)** check gets one true sentence from it. It carries no letter, no brand and no token.
>
> Conversation topics the imp is willing to discuss include:
>
> - What it thinks of Trollskull Manor and its guests.
> - How long it has been watching.
>
> It will not name its master, and it will not say where it flew from.

> [!qna]**Who sent you?**
>
> > "Fuck off, meat. You've got a barrel and a lot of free time, and I've got neither. Ask me again and see what you get."

> [!qna]**What were you counting?**
>
> The imp's eye rolls toward the nearest member.
>
> > "I was told to count who comes and goes, and I can count to a hundred. Your cook leaves at six, and you all come home drunk."

> [!qna]**What are you?**
>
> > "I'm the best thing you'll find in this shit-hole, and I'm very sorry to see you've spotted me. A devil, obviously, and one with better things to do than answer you."

### Renown Opportunities

Savra knocks at dusk on the third day and meets the members in the taproom corner while the companions go to the bar. She asks for the account in the order it happened.

A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** reports to Savra at the east annex of the Halls of Justice at dusk instead. She does not come to the manor for that member, and she reads the same branches below.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra stands in the doorway with the evening light behind her and waits until each member has sat. Then she takes the chair across from you, lays her gloves on the table and looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "I'll hear it from the beginning, and in order. Start with the Wazoo, and leave nothing out because you think it makes you look careless."

If the members report that the story is false and give any one of the three leads, mark **Planted Story Exposed** and read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra listens without interrupting and writes two lines in a small book as the last member finishes speaking.
>
> > "Then the story was made to be believed for about two days, and someone took trouble over it. A forged page, a coin strap and a woman with grey gloves who knew my name before she should have. I'll tell you plainly, that is better work than I expected."

If a member puts the forged leaf in her hands, mark **Forged Record Secured** and read or paraphrase the following. The same applies if a member gives her the secretary's alias and the candle at Barrow's Wick.

> [!readaloud]
>
> She turns the leaf to the window and reads the signature twice, and her mouth tightens as she does.
>
> > "That's a fucking good forgery, and a dead man's name on it. Brisenne is an alias, then, and a candle in Lamp Street is how she is reached. Good work."

If the imp is in a barrel and a member hands it over, mark **Imp Captured** and read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A member rolls the barrel across the floor, and Savra lays one hand on the lid and listens to the muttering inside.
>
> > "A devil that watches a house is a devil someone sent. I can't say who, and I won't guess in front of a witness. I'll have the priests ask it questions it won't answer. Tyr knows I've done worse."

If the imp got away, she says the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra's face does not change, and she sits with her hands flat on the table for a moment before she answers.
>
> > "Then it went home with a description of every one of you. That's a cost, and it can't be undone, so next time bring a net."

If the party played along with the secretary and reports it, Savra gives her single nod. If it held her, she reads the Watch's copy of the statement and says the clerk's order was in good form.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra shuts her notebook and looks at the window for a moment before she speaks.
>
> > "Someone spent a forger, a secretary, a coach and a devil to learn who was curious about a thief. People pay that much to keep one thing from being seen, so something is moving that cannot be seen. What are they moving?"

The members cannot answer, and Savra does not expect them to. **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court** answers the question.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 2 base Renown for reporting to Savra. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the planted story is confirmed false and reported, and **Planted Story Exposed** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** the forged record is recovered, or the secretary is named with her contact method, and **Forged Record Secured** is marked.
> - **+1 Renown:** the imp is taken alive and handed to Savra, and **Imp Captured** is marked.

### Aftermath

Gaxly Rudderbust finishes his follow-up and, if the party left him a note, prints a correction. The Hall of Records takes the forged leaf from the register. Ilmeth Waevlur tells his colleagues about it for a tenday.

Savra files her notes with the Halls of Justice as an observation of a devil, with the sender unproven. The people who sent the secretary learn, within a day or two, whether the raven came home. If it did, they learn the party's faces.

### Concluding the Event

Savra's report ends the mission, and the Event records what the party found because later events read it.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Planted Story Exposed** — mark when the members report the story as false with at least one lead; read by **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**, where the handlers' lookouts are fewer.
> - **Forged Record Secured** — mark when a member gives Savra the forged leaf, or the secretary's alias with the candle drop; read by **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted, if it has not run), and by **Vindicator**, where it serves as the exhibit for an inquiry into Brisenne.
> - **Imp Captured** — mark when a member hands the live imp to Savra; read by **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court** (Savra's debrief), the Whitehawk rank event and **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted, if it has not run).
>
> This Event also reads **Quietly Reassigned**, which **The Tithe** marks, to decide where the debrief is held.

## Overview

A hired coach and a raven watch Trollskull Manor after the party questions the Wazoo's publisher. The party works out what the raven is, decides whether to catch it and reports to Savra.

## Summary

We watched a hired coach pass our door and found a raven on the chimney that never ate or slept. Some of us took it in a barrel, and some of us let it fly. Savra heard everything and asked us one question we could not answer. What are they moving?
