# The Black Viper Investigation

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social/Exploration Event begins when the party receives a paper bird from Savra Belabranta. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Savra's brief by paper bird and proceed to interview Gaxly Rudderbust, the Wazoo publisher.
> - Break through Gaxly's two layers of resistance — source confidentiality first, then the candle signal — using coin, persuasion, or an honest appeal to his safety.
> - Pursue two parallel tracks simultaneously: the Hall of Records (forged birth entry) and the Seffia meeting.
> - Confirm the planted story is fabricated through Ilmeth Waevlur's analysis of the forged record.
> - Meet Seffia Naelryke at the Steaming Anchor and choose how to play the encounter.
> - Alert the Cassalanter household to the party's investigation — this happens the moment the party first speaks to Gaxly, regardless of what they do afterward.
>
> #### The Two Tracks
>
> Both tracks are available simultaneously. The party may pursue one, the other, or both. Track A can be completed in a single afternoon; Track B requires arranging a meeting and waiting until the following midday. Running both is possible within the same tenday.

> [!gamemaster]**Background**
>
> The Wazoo story is a planted trap. A Cassalanter household operative, **Seffia Naelryke**, invented the Black Viper's supposed Cassalanter twin identity and fed it to Gaxly before he could verify it. The goal is to identify who in Waterdeep is investigating the Cassalanters seriously enough to follow a lead about the family. Anyone who walks into Gaxly's office asking about the story is flagged immediately.
>
> Seffia visited Gaxly twice — once to plant the story, once two days after publication to check reception. She left a message drop: a red candle in the window of Barrow's Wick on Lamp Street in the Trades Ward signals willingness to meet. Gaxly will not volunteer this.
>
> The Hall of Records contains a forged entry — "Petra Cassalanter, born 1470 DR, deceased 1471 DR" — installed by a Cassalanter lawyer using stolen seals from a registrar who died in 1468 DR, two years before the alleged birth. This is infrastructure, not improvisation: the Cassalanters built a paper trail to support the planted story if anyone checked.
>
> The moment the party first speaks to Gaxly, word reaches the Cassalanter household — Seffia receives a description of the inquirers within the hour and already knows who she is talking to before any meeting is arranged. How the Cassalanters receive this intelligence so quickly is something the party can discover in **The Imp at Dawn**.

### The Brief

The paper bird arrives early, before Savra's usual hour.

> [!readaloud]
> A paper bird is at the window when someone notices it, wings folded over a small rolled note. Savra's handwriting is spare and deliberate.
>
> > *"Wazoo is reporting the Black Viper has returned. Interview the publisher. Find out what he actually knows. I will meet you when I'm done."*

### The Waterdeep Wazoo

A narrow second-floor room at the corner of Immar and Stallion Streets in the North Ward: fresh ink, cold coffee, broadsheet pages drying on strings overhead.

> [!readaloud]
> The door at the top of the stairs opens before you knock. A man of about forty looks out at you — professionally untidy, with the kind of dishevelment that has been carefully maintained. The office behind him matches: pages drying on strings, a cold cup of something on every flat surface.
>
> > "Whatever this is, I'm on deadline. Five minutes."

> [!social]**The Publisher**
>
> Gaxly Rudderbust (unaligned, Illuskan human, he/him) :: the Waterdeep Wazoo publisher; published a planted story about the Black Viper without verifying it; using source protection as cover for avoiding a larger conversation.
>
> Gaxly published the story and stands by it publicly. He promised not to name his source, and he is using that promise as cover for the fact that he is not entirely sure he didn't make a mistake. He responds to coin, to a direct appeal to his safety, or to the honest suggestion that the story may have been designed to trap the people following it — including him.
>
> A **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check** or 50 gp gets through layer one: his source told him the Black Viper is the Cassalanters' secret twin sister, hidden since birth. He published before he could verify it. He is not sure he regrets it.
>
> Conversation topics Gaxly is willing to discuss include:
> - The story and the source, once his first layer of resistance is broken.
> - Whether his source came back after the story ran, which is a warier conversation.
>
> Specific dialogue for Gaxly on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Who is your source?**
>
> He drums his fingers on the desk.
>
> > "Source protection is the only thing that separates this office from a broadsheet stall. I made a promise and I'm keeping it."
>
> A **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check** or 50 gp breaks through: "The source told me the Black Viper is a Cassalanter twin. Hidden since birth — a disfigurement, they said. The mask isn't vanity, it's concealment. I published it before I could confirm it, which I know." He stops there.

> [!qna]**Did your source come back after the story ran?**
>
> He becomes noticeably warier.
>
> > "They didn't come in. They left an arrangement."
>
> A **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check** — phrased as genuine concern for his safety, not information extraction — or an honest disclosure that the story may be a planted trap draws this out: a red candle in the window of Barrow's Wick on Lamp Street in the Trades Ward signals willingness to meet. He assumed it was meant for him to signal continued interest.

> [!gamemaster]**Imp Alert**
>
> The moment the party first speaks to Gaxly, word reaches the Cassalanter household. Seffia Naelryke has a description of the inquirers within the hour and already knows who she is talking to before any meeting is arranged.

### Two Tracks

Both tracks are available simultaneously once the party has spoken to Gaxly.

#### Track A — The Hall of Records

A day's work among clerks and registry shelves. **Ilmeth Waevlur**, a retired clerk who worked the Sea Ward records for thirty years and now volunteers three mornings a tenday out of habit, is the key contact.

> [!exploration]**The Forged Birth Entry**
>
> Any character who locates the "Petra Cassalanter" entry in the birth registry and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check** prompts Ilmeth to pull it and look at it critically. A mention of the Order of the Gauntlet gives the same result without a check.
>
> > After a long pause and a second look, Ilmeth sets the document flat on the shelf and taps one line of the signature. "This clerk died in 1468. The birth this record documents is dated 1470. Someone used his seals after he was in the ground." He sets it down carefully. "That's not a filing error. Someone with access to stolen official seals produced this. The girl this record describes was never born."
>
> A real crime produced this document — infrastructure built to support the planted story if anyone checked. The entry can be secured and brought to Savra.

#### Track B — The Seffia Meeting

Placing a red candle at Barrow's Wick triggers a reply the following morning: a meeting time, the Steaming Anchor tea house in the Trades Ward, midday.

> [!readaloud]
> The woman who arrives at the Steaming Anchor is Tethyrian, mid-thirties, dressed as a minor noblewoman. She takes the seat across from you with the ease of someone who chose it before she walked in, and she studies each of you before she says anything at all.
>
> > "I understand you've been asking about the Wazoo piece. That's interesting." She sets her hands on the table. "Tell me about yourselves."

> [!social]**The Operative**
>
> Seffia Naelryke (Lawful Evil, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: a Cassalanter household operative conducting an identification audit; her goal is to determine what the party knows and who they work for.
>
> Seffia already knows who the party is. Her goal at this meeting is to confirm what they know, assess whether they are a threat or an opportunity, and determine the extent of the Order of the Gauntlet's involvement. She will not confirm the planted story. Her questions are specific in ways that reveal she already knows quite a bit.
>
> A **DC 16 Wisdom (Insight) check** catches her tells: her questions about the party's other investigations are too specific, and her interest in Savra Belabranta by name arrives unprompted.
>
> Four options are available to the party at this meeting.
>
> **Play along:** Let Seffia believe the party has no Order connection and are independent operators. She offers 100 gp for everything they learn about the Gauntlet's investigation into the Cassalanters. Accepting and reporting to Savra: Savra is cold but not surprised. "Now we know what they want to track." The Cassalanters receive limited misinformation.
>
> **Expose the trap:** Confront Seffia with the forged birth record (requires Track A first). She goes still. Counter-offer: withdraw the investigation and the Cassalanters take no action against Trollskull Manor. She has two guards outside (use **Thug** stat block). Refusing and attempting to detain her brings the guards.
>
> **Detain Seffia:** Forces the guards into combat. Seffia will not break under ordinary pressure; **Charm Person** (DC 14 Wisdom saving throw) or equivalent extracts one significant piece of intelligence: "The Cassalanters know who the real Black Viper is. They are not trying to stop her. They need the Watch looking at Trollskull and the North Ward, not the Sea Ward. Something is being moved."
>
> **Walk away:** The party leaves without revealing themselves. Seffia files her report. The Cassalanter surveillance of Trollskull escalates to active tracking within twenty-four hours.

> [!gamemaster]**Seffia's Report and Cassalanter Response**
>
> Whatever outcome the meeting produces, Seffia files her report. The Cassalanter response to this encounter shapes Mission 4:
>
> - *Deal accepted:* Quieter surveillance and deliberate patience. By Mission 4, Gysheer Omfreys' handlers are less alert.
> - *Trap exposed or confrontation:* Faster escalation. By Mission 4, Gysheer's handlers are more prepared.
> - *Party walked away:* Active tracking begins within twenty-four hours.

### Concluding Act One

The investigation phase closes when the party has done what they can with Gaxly and pursued at least one track. Savra is not yet available for debrief — she has been at the Halls of Justice all day. She will meet them tonight, or in the morning.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Proceed to **The Imp at Dawn**. Regardless of how the investigation resolved, imp surveillance of Trollskull Manor begins within forty-eight hours of the party's first contact with Gaxly.

## Overview

The Waterdeep Wazoo has published a story about the Black Viper that Savra believes is a plant. The party investigates the publisher, the Hall of Records, and the operative who built the trap.

## Summary

We followed Savra's paper bird to the Waterdeep Wazoo, where the publisher had printed a story about the Black Viper that turned out to be planted. The Hall of Records showed the birth entry backstopping it was a forgery. We found the woman who built the trap and chose what to do with her. Whoever sent her already knew we were asking.
