# The Black Viper Investigation

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social/Exploration Event begins when the party receives a paper bird from Savra Belabranta.
> In this Event:
>
> - The party interviews Gaxly Rudderbust, the Wazoo publisher, who published the planted story.
> - Gaxly has two layers: source confidentiality first, then the contact method for the planted story's source.
> - Two parallel tracks are available simultaneously: the Hall of Records (forged birth entry) and the Seffia meeting.
> - The Hall of Records track confirms the story is fabricated — the clerk's signature predates the registrar's death by two years.
> - Seffia Naelryke conducts a social audit from the moment she enters; four options are available to the party.
> - Whatever the party does with Seffia, the Cassalanter imp network is alerted within hours of first contact with Gaxly.

**Background (DM only)**
The Wazoo story is a planted trap. A Cassalanter household operative, **Seffia Naelryke**, invented the Black Viper's supposed Cassalanter twin identity and fed it to Gaxly before he could verify it. The goal: identify who in Waterdeep is investigating the Cassalanters seriously enough to follow a lead about their family. The party's inquiry flags through the family's imp network within hours of the first conversation with Gaxly.

Seffia visited Gaxly twice — once to plant the story, once two days after publication to "check reception." She left a message drop: a red candle in the window of Barrow's Wick on Lamp Street in the Trades Ward signals willingness to meet. Gaxly will not volunteer this.

The Hall of Records contains a forged entry — "Petra Cassalanter, born 1470 DR, deceased 1471 DR" — installed by a Cassalanter lawyer using stolen seals from a registrar who died in 1468 DR, two years before the alleged birth. This is infrastructure, not improvisation: the Cassalanters built a paper trail to support the planted story if anyone checked.

### The Waterdeep Wazoo

A narrow second-floor room at the corner of Immar and Stallion Streets in the North Ward. Fresh ink, cold coffee, broadsheet pages drying on strings overhead.

**Gaxly Rudderbust** — professionally untidy, forty, with a dishevelment that is deliberate and studied. He has two layers.

> [!npc-narrative]**Gaxly Rudderbust**
> About forty, professionally untidy, the dishevelment deliberate and studied. The office matches him: broadsheet pages drying on strings overhead, coffee long cold.

> [!dialogue]**Who is your source for the Black Viper story?**
> He published the story. He stands by it. He promised not to name his source and is using that promise as cover for avoiding the larger conversation.

A **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check** or 50 gp breaks through: his source told him the Black Viper is the Cassalanters' secret twin sister, hidden since birth due to a disfigurement. The mask is concealment, not vanity. He published before he could verify it. He is not sure he regrets it.

> [!dialogue]**Did your source come back after the story ran?**
> Gaxly is warier here. The contact arrangement: a red candle in the window of Barrow's Wick on Lamp Street in the Trades Ward means someone is willing to meet. He has not used it. He assumed it was for him to signal continued interest.

A **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check** — phrased as genuine concern for his safety, not information extraction — or an honest disclosure that the story may be a planted trap draws this out.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Imp Flag
> The moment the party first speaks to Gaxly, a Cassalanter imp courier is notified through the family's network. Seffia Naelryke receives a description of the inquirers within the hour. She already knows who they are before any meeting is arranged.

### Two Tracks

Both tracks are available simultaneously. The party may pursue one, the other, or both.

#### Track A — The Hall of Records

A day's work among clerks and registry shelves. **Ilmeth Waevlur**, a retired clerk who worked the Sea Ward records for thirty years and now volunteers three mornings a tenday out of habit, is the key contact.

A **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check** or a mention of the Order of the Gauntlet prompts him to pull the "Petra Cassalanter" entry and look at it critically. His verdict, after a long pause: the document has correct formatting and genuine official seals, but the clerk's signature belongs to a registrar who died in 1468 DR — two years before the alleged birth. Someone with access to stolen seals produced a convincing forgery. A real person did not exist. A real crime did.

#### Track B — The Seffia Meeting

Placing a red candle at Barrow's Wick triggers a reply the following morning: a meeting time, the Steaming Anchor tea house in the Trades Ward, midday.

**Seffia Naelryke** — Tethyrian, mid-thirties, dressed to pass as a minor noblewoman. Gracious, composed, conducting a social audit from the moment the party enters. Her goal is to find out what they know and who they work for. She will not confirm the planted story. She will try to determine whether the party is a threat or an opportunity.

> [!npc-narrative]**Seffia Naelryke**
> The woman who arrives at the meeting is Tethyrian, mid-thirties, dressed to pass as a minor noblewoman. She is gracious, composed, and conducting a social audit from the moment she enters.

A **DC 16 Wisdom (Insight) check** catches her tells: her questions about the party's other investigations are too specific; her interest in Savra Belabranta by name arrives unprompted. She already knows who she is talking to.

**Four options:**

*Play along:* Let Seffia believe the party has no Order connection and are independent operators. She offers 100 gp for everything they learn about the Gauntlet's investigation into the Cassalanters. Accepting and reporting to Savra: Savra is cold but not surprised. "Now we know what they want to track." The Cassalanters receive limited misinformation.

*Expose the trap:* Confront Seffia with the forged birth record (requires Track A first). She goes still. Counter-offer: withdraw the investigation and the Cassalanters take no action against Trollskull Manor. She has two guards outside (use **Thug** stat block). Refusing and attempting to detain her brings the guards.

*Detain Seffia:* Forces the guards into combat. Seffia will not break under ordinary pressure; **Charm Person** (DC 14 Wisdom) or equivalent extracts one significant piece of intelligence: "The Cassalanters know who the real Black Viper is. They are not trying to stop her. They need the Watch looking at Trollskull and the North Ward, not the Sea Ward. Something is being moved."

*Walk away:* The party leaves. Seffia files her report. The imp network escalates from observation to active tracking.

> **[GM]**

> - *Party walked away:* The imp network escalates to active tracking within 24 hours.

### Concluding Act One

The investigation phase closes when the party has done what they can with Gaxly and pursued at least one track. Savra is not yet available for debrief — she has been at the Halls of Justice all day. She will meet them tonight, or in the morning.

## Overview

The Waterdeep Wazoo has published a story about the Black Viper that Savra believes is a plant. The party investigates the publisher, the Hall of Records, and the operative who built the trap.

## Read Aloud

> [!narrative]
> The paper bird arrives early, before Savra's usual hour. The message is in her hand, spare and direct: *"Wazoo is reporting the Black Viper has returned. Interview the publisher. Find out what he actually knows. I will meet you when I'm done."*

## Summary

The party investigated the Black Viper story in the Waterdeep Wazoo — a planted trap built by a Cassalanter operative named Seffia Naelryke. The planted birth entry at the Hall of Records confirmed fabrication. Seffia was located and confronted, dealt with, or avoided. The Cassalanter imp network was alerted.
