# The Black Viper Investigation

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Investigation and Social Event begins when Savra Belabranta knocks at Trollskull Manor at dawn. It ends at the Steaming Anchor tea house at highsun on the next day. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Savra's brief and hear why she thinks the Wazoo's story about the Black Viper is a plant.
> - Question Gaxly Rudderbust, the publisher, through two layers of reluctance.
> - Search the Hall of Records with Ilmeth Waevlur and learn that a birth entry is forged.
> - Follow three independent leads to the red candle at Barrow's Wick, the contact the planter left behind.
> - Meet the planter at the Steaming Anchor under her alias and answer her in one of four ways.
> - Hold her at the tea house with the Watch's help, if a character covers the kitchen passage first.
>
> Only Order of the Gauntlet members attend Savra's brief, and their companions help with everything else in the Event.

> [!gamemaster]**Who Knows What**
>
> - Four days ago *The Waterdeep Wazoo* printed that the Black Viper has returned and is the secret twin sister of Lady Ammalia Cassalanter, and the story is false.
> - A Tethyrian woman who calls herself Corisande Brisenne gave Gaxly the story. She came back two days ago to ask how it had been received. She is a household secretary hired for the work, and Brisenne is an alias.
> - The same people paid a forger to enter a birth for "Petra Cassalanter" in the Hall of Records, so that the story would survive a quick look.
> - Nobody in this Event knows who the Black Viper is. A member who read the Rosznar signet in **The Poisoned Delivery** may guess, and nothing here confirms it.
> - Nobody in this Event can prove who sent the secretary. Savra suspects, and she says so only if a member raises the family first.
> - Savra does not go with the party on this mission, whatever a member's rank. The Marcheon benefit covers quests, not faction missions.
> - Nobody in this Event mentions Manshoon.

### The Brief

Savra knocks once at dawn and waits on the step until a member asks her in. She asks the companions to give the members the taproom corner, and she stands for the whole brief with the broadsheet folded in one hand.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The knock comes once, early, while the taproom is still cold and the lane outside is grey with morning. Savra Belabranta waits on the step in her dark riding coat, with a silver gauntlet pin on her collar and a folded broadsheet in one hand. She comes in when one of you asks her, lays the broadsheet on the nearest table and turns to the members.
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly what this is, because you should know before you start. The Wazoo says the Black Viper is back, and that she is the secret twin of Lady Ammalia Cassalanter. I don't believe a word of it, and I want to know who does. I'm at the Halls of Justice all day, so go to the Wazoo and find out where Gaxly Rudderbust got his story. I'll come here at dusk, three days from now, and you'll tell me what you have."

> [!social]**The Knight at the Door**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: a Knight of the Order in her late twenties with dark hair worn back. She briefs standing and does not soften what she says.
>
> Savra pauses before every serious answer and says what she knows in plain order. She swears rarely and only at wrongdoing, and she gives a single nod to an honest answer and nothing to a managed one. She calls Tyr the Maimed God and voices every suspicion as a suspicion.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The Black Viper, and what the Watch knows of her.
> - Gaxly Rudderbust and the story he printed.
> - What the Order pays, and what it expects.
> - Why she cannot come with the party.
>
> She will not discuss what she suspects about any family unless a member names it first, and then only as a suspicion.

> [!qna]**Who is the Black Viper?**
>
> > "A masked thief who robs the great houses and has never been caught. The Watch keeps a file on her as thick as my forearm and has no name and no face to put in it. Nobody I trust claims to know who she is."

> [!qna]**Why does the Order care about a thief?**
>
> > "I don't care about the thief. A paper that prints a false story about a family is doing somebody's work, and I'd like to know whose. A lie that cheap usually has a purpose behind it."

> [!qna]**Who would plant a story like that?**
>
> > "Somebody who wanted to see who comes asking about it, and who would pay to find out. I can't tell you more yet, and I'd rather say so than guess."

If **Wazoo Exposé Published** is marked in **The Wazoo Affair**, Savra adds the following to her answer to this question:

> [!qna]**What about the other story in the paper?**
>
> > "The Wazoo printed devils among the noble houses, and days later it printed a twin. Somebody is using that paper to say things they cannot say themselves. I want to know who."

> [!qna]**Is it the Cassalanters?**
>
> Savra waits a moment before she answers, and her eyes stay on the member who asked.
>
> > "I've thought about that family for months, and I have nothing I could show a magistrate. If you can show me something, I'll thank you for it. Until then it's a suspicion, and I don't repeat those."

> [!qna]**What does the Order pay?**
>
> > "Standing, and the Order's trust. It pays no coin for this work, so if you need coin, say so now and I'll find someone who does."

> [!qna]**Will you come with us?**
>
> > "No. A knight in a coat is a reason for a man to stop talking, and you'll get more from Rudderbust without me. I'll hear all of it at dusk on the third day."

> [!gamemaster]**The Deadline**
>
> Savra returns at dusk on the third day after she knocks, which is the evening after the Steaming Anchor. A member who has done nothing by then reports nothing, and she says so flatly and sends no one after them. The day table in **The Imp at Dawn** fixes the rest of the clock.

### The Waterdeep Wazoo

Gaxly is at his desk in the morning and again from two bells until seven bells. The building is the one in **The Wazoo Affair**, at the corner of Immar Street and Stallion Street in the North Ward. If a member marked **Wazoo Delivery Witnessed**, Gaxly recognizes the party and greets it with cheerful suspicion.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A narrow stair brings you to a long room on the second floor, where damp broadsheet pages hang from strings across the ceiling. The air smells of fresh ink and cold coffee, and a rumpled man in a stained waistcoat looks up from a desk buried in proofs. Behind him a half-finished page pinned to a board reads BLACK VIPER RETURNS? and he waves you in with his pen.
>
> > "Customers, or creditors? Either way, mind the wet pages, and come in and tell me what you think of the front page."

> [!gamemaster]**The Board**
>
> The page on the board is the follow-up Gaxly is drafting, which keeps the board in **The Wazoo Affair** true. He pins it in front of every visitor and asks what they think of the headline.

> [!social]**The Publisher in the Proofs**
>
> Gaxly Rudderbust (Neutral, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: the publisher of *The Waterdeep Wazoo*, forty and ink-stained, who loves an audience and can ill afford to lose a source.
>
> Gaxly talks with relish about anything he has printed. He swears mildly and often, and he is proud of the Black Viper story and a little afraid of it. He has two layers of reluctance, which are his promise not to name his source and the arrangement she left him. He has not used the arrangement and does not want to mention it.
>
> Conversation topics Gaxly is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The front page, how it sold and how he checked it.
> - The follow-up on the board.
> - His source, once the first layer breaks.
> - Her return visit, once the second layer breaks.

> [!qna]**Who gave you the story?**
>
> > "I gave my word, and honestly it's the only thing I own that isn't pawned. Ask me anything else about the paper and I'll happily talk all day."

> [!qna]**Did you check it?**
>
> > "I meant to. She was so sure of herself, and the paper needed a front page by morning. So I printed it as hers, and I told myself the Hall of Records would back her up if anyone asked."

> [!exploration]**Layer One: The Promise**
>
> Any character who presses Gaxly and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check, or who pays him 50 gp, breaks the first layer. A character who holds **Black Viper Source Noted** has Advantage on the check, because the member can quote his own note back to him.
>
> - **On a failure:** Gaxly repeats that he gave his word. A second attempt after an hour needs no check if the party pays the 50 gp.
> - **On a success:** Gaxly says a Tethyrian woman in her thirties, well dressed and well spoken, brought him the story but gave no name. She said the Black Viper is Lady Ammalia's twin, hidden from birth by a disfigurement that the mask covers. He has been proud of the story for four days and uneasy for two.

> [!qna]**What did she look like?**
>
> > "Tall, straight-backed, grey gloves she never took off, and a voice that sounded like a magistrate's clerk. Tethyrian, I'd say, from the vowels. She paid for my coffee and left a very good impression."

> [!exploration]**Layer Two: The Second Visit**
>
> Gaxly is wary here, and any character who tells him the story may be a trap and shows real concern for his safety makes a **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion)** check. A character who tells him honestly that the party suspects a plant needs no check.
>
> - **On a success:** Gaxly admits the woman came back two days ago to ask how the story sold. She told him to put a red candle in the window of Barrow's Wick on Lamp Street in the Trades Ward if he ever had more to tell, and she would come.
> - **On a failure:** Gaxly says only that she came back once. The party still has two other ways to the candle, below.

### The Hall of Records

The Hall of Records is open from 09:00 to 17:00, and Ilmeth Waevlur sits the Sea Ward shelves on the mornings of the first and second days. He volunteers three mornings in every tenday. Gaxly's story is false, and a quick look at the register shows it.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Hall of Records is a long room of shelves and writing desks, with petitioners queued at a counter and clerks passing between them with armfuls of dockets. The air smells of ink and damp wool, and a white-haired man in grey clerk's sleeves squints at a ledger through spectacles beside the Sea Ward shelves. He sets down his pen as you approach and looks up with polite interest.
>
> > "You look like people with a question. I do hope it's a good one, because I've had nothing but wills this week."

> [!social]**The Retired Clerk**
>
> Ilmeth Waevlur (Lawful Good, Human, he/him) :: a retired clerk of seventy who worked the Sea Ward shelves for thirty years and still sits them by habit.
>
> Ilmeth is precise and fond of a good error. He speaks slowly, checks every date twice and is delighted by a forgery. He swears only at bad handwriting, and he warms at once to anyone who mentions the Order, because the Halls once found his sister's stolen deed.
>
> Conversation topics Ilmeth is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The Cassalanter births of 1452, which is where a twin would be recorded.
> - How registrars sign and seal their entries.
> - Registrar Orlan Telk, whom he served under.

> [!qna]**Is there a record of a twin?**
>
> > "There is a leaf for a Petra Cassalanter, entered on the same day as her sister. I'd like to look at it again before I tell you what I think of it, so give me a moment and a lamp."

> [!qna]**Who signed it?**
>
> Ilmeth turns the leaf to the light and says nothing for a long moment.
>
> > "Registrar Orlan Telk, and it's his hand, I'll grant you. He also died in the winter of 1450. This leaf is dated two years after his funeral, and I carried a candle at it myself."

> [!qna]**Can it be a mistake?**
>
> > "A clerk can miscount a column, but he can't sign after he's buried. The seal is genuine, and the ink is newer than the pages either side. Someone had the registrar's seal and a good hand, and they used both."

> [!exploration]**The Forged Leaf**
>
> Any character who asks Ilmeth for the Cassalanter births of 1452 and makes a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check persuades him to pull the leaf. A character who gives the Order's name needs no check. Ilmeth lifts the leaf out of the register for any member who signs for it, since it should never have been there.
>
> - **On a failure:** Ilmeth is polite and asks the party to return with a request on proper paper. A character who spends an afternoon at the registrars' roll and makes a successful **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)** check finds the same fact without him, which is that Telk's tenure ended in 1450.
> - **Without Ilmeth:** The party can prove the entry false from the registrars' roll, but it cannot take the leaf out of the register.
> - **With the leaf:** The party holds the forged record. It shows the planter went to some trouble, and it is the item that **Forged Record Secured** reads in **The Imp at Dawn**.

### Three Ways to Barrow's Wick

Three leads each name the red candle at Barrow's Wick, and any one is enough. They can be worked in any order, and a failed check on one never closes the others.

> [!exploration]**Lead One: Gaxly**
>
> If the party broke Layer Two above, Gaxly names Barrow's Wick and Lamp Street. He asks that it be left out of the follow-up.

> [!exploration]**Lead Two: The Scrivener**
>
> Hovan Dree rents the third floor and remembers every face that climbs the stair. He saw the woman twice and heard her leave her arrangement through Gaxly's door. A character who buys him a drink or makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check gets him to name the shop. He also kept the paper strap from the coins she paid him for directions, which carries the stamp of a Sea Ward counting house.

> [!exploration]**Lead Three: The Cab Stand**
>
> Pennet, the candlemaker's apprentice on the ground floor, saw the woman step out of a hired coach on the day she planted the story. A character who gives her a copper and asks about the coach learns that its driver waits at the Immar Street cab stand. Any character who finds him and makes a successful **DC 12 Wisdom (Insight)** check sees that he is honest when he says he took a grey-gloved woman to Lamp Street.

At Barrow's Wick the chandler lights any red candle that is left with him and puts it in the shop window for a silver. He passes on a reply card the next morning, and he knows no name.

> [!gamemaster]**The Candle**
>
> A member who leaves a red candle at Barrow's Wick on the first evening finds a card under the sill the next morning. The card says *The Steaming Anchor, Trades Ward, highsun.* If the party leaves the candle later, the meeting moves to highsun on the day after the card arrives. Any of the three leads above is enough to make the candle work.

### The Steaming Anchor

The meeting is at highsun on the second day, at a tea house with a kitchen passage at the back that leads to a lane. The secretary's hired guards sit near the street door. A character who studies the room on arrival and makes a successful **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check spots the passage, and a second character who posts at it before she moves can stop her leaving that way.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The Steaming Anchor is a narrow tea house with fogged windows and a long iron range, where kettles tick and a cook turns flatbreads on a griddle. A dozen patrons crowd the benches, and two broad men in plain coats near the street door look into their cups. At the back a tall woman in a grey wool gown sits alone with her gloves folded beside a pot of tea. She lifts one hand to you as you come in.
>
> > "Please sit, since I've ordered for the whole table. Mistress Brisenne, if you like. I think we may be able to help each other."

> [!social]**The Woman with the Gloves**
>
> Corisande Brisenne (Lawful Neutral, Tethyrian Human, she/her, an alias) :: a household secretary in her mid-thirties, dressed to pass as a minor noblewoman, who plants stories for pay.
>
> Mistress Brisenne is courteous, brief and exact, and she smiles at everything and tells the party nothing. She does not swear, and she pours tea for the whole table before she speaks. She wants to learn who is asking about the Black Viper and for whom, and she offers 100 gp for a fair account of what the party has learned.
>
> Any character who watches her and makes a successful **DC 16 Wisdom (Insight)** check notices her tells. Her questions about the party's other inquiries are too specific, and she says Savra Belabranta's name before anyone has mentioned it.
>
> Conversation topics Mistress Brisenne is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The Wazoo story and the pleasure she takes in it.
> - What the party has learned, and what it is worth.
> - Her employers, in the vaguest terms.
>
> She will not confirm that she gave Gaxly the story, and she will not name anyone she works for.

> [!qna]**Who are you?**
>
> > "I manage correspondence for a household that prefers to stay out of the newspapers. My name is Brisenne, and that is as much as you need to know."

> [!qna]**Did you give Gaxly the story?**
>
> > "I read the same paper as the rest of the city does. If you have reason to think otherwise, I would like to hear it."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us?**
>
> > "A fair account of what you've learned about the Black Viper, and who you're learning it for. The Wazoo has made some people nervous, and I'd like to ease them. I'll pay a hundred gold for your time."

> [!qna]**Who do you work for?**
>
> She sets down her cup before she answers.
>
> > "People who value discretion, and I do the same. Is Mistress Belabranta well? I understand she has taken an interest in the matter."

If a character takes the hundred gold and reports it honestly at the debrief, Savra gives her single nod. If a character takes it and says nothing, Savra says she would have preferred to hear it from them.

The party answers her in one of four ways. Each one sets how wary the watcher on Trollskull Manor is on the nights that follow, which **The Imp at Dawn** reads.

> [!exploration]**Option One: Play Along**
>
> The party takes her hundred gold and gives her a thin, harmless account of its work. A character who speaks for the party and makes a successful **DC 14 Charisma (Deception)** check persuades her that the party is independent and unimportant, and she pays. The watcher is **Calm**.
>
> - **On a failure:** She smiles, pays nothing and watches the party leave. The watcher is **Wary**.
> - **Reporting it:** Savra approves of the honest report either way. She does not approve of a party that gives the secretary real information about the Order.

> [!exploration]**Option Two: Show the Forged Leaf**
>
> A member who holds the forged leaf lays it on the table, and the secretary goes still. She answers with a counter-offer. If the party drops the inquiry, the people she serves will have no quarrel with Trollskull Manor. She leaves unhindered, and the watcher is **Wary**.
>
> - **What the party learns:** The leaf shows her the party is no fool. The offer is the closest she comes to an admission, and it names no one.
> - **If the party refuses the offer:** She finishes her tea, pays the bill and leaves by the street door with her guards.

> [!exploration]**Option Three: Hold Her**
>
> A party that wants her held needs the forged leaf in hand and a Watch patrol, which passes the tea house at the end of the fourth round. Without the leaf, the patrol's sergeant will not detain her, and she walks out with an apology. With it, he holds her for questioning on suspicion of forgery, and the watcher is **Hunting**.
>
> - **A character at the kitchen passage:** She cannot leave that way. She sits down again and waits for the patrol, which is the quiet route.
> - **No one at the passage:** She leaves through the kitchen on her first turn, and her guards hold the door for three rounds. See **If the Party Holds the Door**.
> - **A bluff:** A character who tells the guards the Watch is on the way and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation or Deception)** check persuades them to stand down.
> - **Held:** At the Watch post she gives her alias and says one sentence for the record. "The story was never meant to catch the Black Viper. It was meant to catch whoever came asking." A solicitor's clerk with an order in good form collects her before dawn.
> - **Magic:** A charm or compulsion spell makes her answer one question truthfully. Savra accepts nothing learned that way, so the secretary's bonus cannot be earned by it and the party must rely on the forged leaf.

> [!hazard]**If the Party Holds the Door**
>
> The guards are hired hands, so they use the **Tough** and **Bandit** blocks. Use the roster for the party's size, companions included.
>
> - **Three characters:** two **Tough** fighters. This is a Bruising fight with a Day Cost of 4.
> - **Four characters:** two **Tough** fighters and two **Bandit** fighters. This is a Bruising fight with a Day Cost of 4.
> - **Five characters:** three **Tough** fighters and one **Bandit**. This is a Bruising fight with a Day Cost of 4.
>
> The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits the fights. The secretary is not counted and does not fight. She leaves by the kitchen on her first turn unless a character covers the passage.
>
> #### The Guards' Tactics
> To begin, the guards stand and block the door, and they use the benches for cover.
>
> During combat, the guards:
> - Spread their attacks, with no more than two attacks on any one character.
> - Use Pack Tactics, since a Tough or Bandit beside the target counts as an ally.
> - Never attack a creature at 0 hit points.
> - Hold the door for three rounds so that the secretary can leave.
>
> The fight ends when the secretary is gone, when a Watch patrol of two **Guards** turns in at the end of round 4, or when a guard is Grappled. The guards surrender when the patrol arrives. If the party knocks out the guards and runs the secretary down, she surrenders at once, because she is a clerk and not a fighter. If the party shows the forged leaf to the sergeant, she is held.

> [!exploration]**Option Four: Walk Away**
>
> The party thanks her, takes nothing and leaves. She files her report, and the watcher is **Wary**. A party that never lights the candle is in the same state.

### Concluding the Event

The Event ends when the party leaves the tea house, whatever it did there, and time passes to the nights that follow.

## Overview

Savra sends the party to find who gave a Wazoo publisher a false story about the Black Viper. The trail runs through a forged birth record and a tea-house meeting with a woman who will not give her name.

## Summary

We took the Wazoo story to Gaxly Rudderbust and found that a Tethyrian woman with grey gloves had given it to him. Ilmeth Waevlur showed us that the twin's birth entry is signed by a registrar who died two years earlier. We met the woman at the Steaming Anchor under a name that was not hers. Who sent her, and who is watching Trollskull Manor now?
