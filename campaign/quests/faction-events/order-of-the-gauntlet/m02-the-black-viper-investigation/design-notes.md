# Design Notes: The Black Viper Investigation

## What the Source Gave

Appendix C gave the mission its spine. A Wazoo story says the Black Viper is Lady Ammalia's twin, a records check disproves it, the source was a well-dressed Tethyrian, and a small flying creature watches the party within two days. Savra's question asks who benefits from a lie that points investigators at a family. Everything else in the first draft was an addition, including the forged leaf, the red candle and the tea house.

## What Changed

The first draft sent a paper bird. Savra now knocks at dawn and says she is at the Halls all day, which matches her contact rule. The meeting NPC is no longer Seffia Naelryke. Her Notable Figures page makes her a windmill recluse who receives no visitors, so a household secretary under an invented alias takes the tea-house chair. The secretary is also a hired agent and not household staff.

The imp is a raven, which is how the villa keeps its imps. It is not invisible, so a character can notice it, and its sender is unproven. The first draft's binding marks, its formal Cassalanter filing and its line "the Cassalanters know who the real Black Viper is" are all cut. The household does not know that Esvele is the Viper.

The Charm Person line is replaced by a lawful route. A held secretary needs the forged leaf and a Watch patrol, and anything learned by magic is inadmissible. A day table fixes the timeline the first draft contradicted. The three leads to the candle, and the three leads to the raven, give each beat three independent paths.

## Departures from the Source

The forged record is dated 1452 DR, with a registrar who died in 1450 DR. It states no child count. The question "What are they moving?" is now answered in **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**, where Gysheer carries a cartage receipt for crates. The mission awards a base of 2 and three +1 bonuses, and it reads **Black Viper Source Noted** from **The Wazoo Affair** as Advantage on the first layer.

## Invented Names and Open Items

New names, each checked against `campaign/` with no collision: Corisande Brisenne (the alias), Registrar Orlan Telk, the Immar Street cab stand and the chandler's red-candle arrangement. Ilmeth Waevlur, the Steaming Anchor, Barrow's Wick and Lamp Street come from the first draft and appear nowhere else outside this folder.

Items for the out-of-scope log:

- **The Wazoo Affair** (`ev-01:27`) says Seffia Naelryke planted the story. This mission uses an alias, so the line needs a pass.
- Savra's Notable Figures page calls her "neutral good" and "Tethyrian"; the brief makes her Lawful Good and the page's Featured-in list needs her new mission titles.
- Seffia's page still lists this mission and gives her "Cult Fanatic," the 2014 name.
- The Factions Guide row says the trail touches the Grand Game "in ways Savra doesn't yet realize," which this rewrite does not support.
