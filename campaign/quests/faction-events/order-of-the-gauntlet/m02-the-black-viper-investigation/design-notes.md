# Design Notes: The Black Viper Investigation

## Cross-Faction NPC Overlap

***Gaxly Rudderbust.*** Gaxly also appears in Doom Raiders Mission 2 and Bregan D'aerthe Mission 2. In those missions he is entangled in overlapping Cassalanter-related press activity — the infernal worship exposé (DR-M2) and the BD coverage thread (BD-M2). If the party has run either of those missions before this one, Gaxly already knows them by face. His wariness or warmth in the first conversation should reflect what happened before. There is no mechanical consequence, but the GM should acknowledge prior contact rather than playing the scene as a cold introduction.

The two Wazoo stories — the Black Viper plant (OG-M2) and the infernal worship exposé (DR-M2) — may run within the same tenday if all three missions are in play simultaneously. The GM should decide which story breaks first, or treat them as concurrent Cassalanter crises: both are live in the paper at once, and Gaxly is visibly overwhelmed. If concurrent, Seffia's identification operation in this mission intersects with her employers already scrambling to contain the exposé from DR-M2.
