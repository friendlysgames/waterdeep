# The Black Viper Investigation: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 3 and 3rd level, after **Breaking Up the Brawl**. Companions can help with everything except Savra's brief and debrief, which only members attend.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> The only fights are optional. A hold at the tea house uses **Tough** and **Bandit** fighters, and a raven that is cornered uses the **Imp** block. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits both rosters for three, four and five characters.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points. Renown goes to the individual Order members who report to Savra.

## Hook

On the first dawn after a member reaches Renown 3 and 3rd level, Savra knocks at Trollskull Manor with a copy of *The Waterdeep Wazoo*. The paper says the Black Viper has returned and is the secret twin of Lady Ammalia Cassalanter. Savra does not believe it, and she wants to know who gave the story to the publisher. She is at the Halls of Justice all day and returns at dusk on the third day.

## Background

Four days ago the Wazoo printed a story that a Tethyrian woman brought to Gaxly Rudderbust. She came back two days ago to ask how it had been received, and she left him a signal. A forger had entered a birth for a twin sister in the Hall of Records some tendays before, using the seal of a registrar who died in 1450 DR.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The woman is a household secretary hired for this work, and Brisenne is an alias. She is not household staff, and her goal is to learn who follows the story.
> - The story is false. The Black Viper is not Lady Ammalia's twin, and nobody in this mission knows who she is.
> - The raven on the chimney is an imp. The GM can take the secretary and the imp for the work of the family the story names. Nothing in the mission proves it, and nobody in it knows more than Savra's suspicion.
> - Savra does not accompany the party. The Marcheon benefit covers quests, not faction missions.
> - Nobody in this mission mentions Manshoon.

## The Brief

Savra knocks once at dawn and waits to be asked in. She briefs the members alone, states her deadline and says why she cannot come. The companions give the members the taproom corner.

## The Wazoo and the Hall of Records

Gaxly Rudderbust gives up his source through two layers, and the second layer names the red candle at Barrow's Wick. Ilmeth Waevlur shows that the twin's birth entry is signed by a dead registrar. Three leads reach the candle, and any one is enough.

## The Steaming Anchor

The party meets the secretary at the tea house at highsun on the second day. She offers 100 gp for the party's account. The party can play along, show the forged leaf, hold her with the Watch's help or walk away.

## The Imp at Dawn

A hired coach and a raven watch Trollskull Manor. The party works out what the raven is by three independent leads and takes it at the chimney, or lets it fly. Savra's debrief falls at dusk on the third day.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 2 base Renown for reporting to Savra. Three bonuses of +1 each go to members who confirm and report that the story is false, who secure the forged record or name the secretary with her contact method, and who bring the imp to Savra alive. Companions gain none, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Planted Story Exposed** is read by **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**. **Imp Captured** is read by the same event, by the Whitehawk rank event and by **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted). **Forged Record Secured** is read by **Cassalanter Villa** (unconverted). **The Shard Shunners** becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 5 and 4th level.

## Involved Characters

- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet): briefs the members at the door and takes the report at dusk on the third day.
- **Gaxly Rudderbust** (independent): the Wazoo's publisher, who printed the story and knows the signal.
- **Ilmeth Waevlur** (independent): a retired clerk who identifies the forged entry.
- **Corisande Brisenne** (independent): an alias for the household secretary who plants the story.
- **Hovan Dree** and **Pennet** (independent): a scrivener and a candlemaker's apprentice who saw the secretary.
- **The Raven** (independent): an imp in raven form that watches Trollskull Manor.

## Dangers & Enemies

The secretary's two or four guards use the **Tough** and **Bandit** blocks and hold the door for three rounds. The raven uses the **Imp** block, flees when seen and fights only when held. Neither fight is needed.

## Overview

Savra sends the party to find who planted a false story about the Black Viper. The trail ends with a forged record, a secretary under a false name and a raven that is a devil.
