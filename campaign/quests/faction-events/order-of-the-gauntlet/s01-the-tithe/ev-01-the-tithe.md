# The Tithe

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Standalone Event occurs for an individual Order of the Gauntlet member after the party first carries a haul of 1,000 gp or more out of one operation. The count starts from the member's oath. Savra Belabranta knocks at Trollskull Manor at dusk on the second day after the haul reaches Waterdeep. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Pay 25% of their own equal share to a worthy cause before Savra knocks, and gain 1 Renown.
> - Pay after Savra has asked, and hear "Good." with no Renown.
> - Refuse, or pay less and not make up the rest, and be **Quietly Reassigned**, which ends only the extras Savra offers unasked.
> - Pay the declined amount later, and end the reassignment the next morning.
>
> Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member follows their own track. Companions and characters in other factions owe nothing and are never pressed.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The Order has no treasury rule beyond this one. Savra asks for a quarter of a member's own share because the Order expects its knights to live on less than they earn.
> - Savra never touches the money or the receipt. Brother Hallin Ostrel, the Order's clerk at the Halls of Justice, takes both, so she judges members by what they do and not by what they hand her.
> - A tithe paid before Savra asks is a gift. A tithe paid after she asks is a debt settled, and she says so by saying nothing more than "Good."
> - Savra does not rank the four causes. Her family raises griffons for the Griffon Cavalry, and she names no cause so that nobody thinks she is steering them toward her own.
> - Quietly Reassigned is a list and not a punishment. Savra needs every member she has for the work ahead, and she says as much when asked.

### When This Event Fires

> [!gamemaster]**The Trigger**
>
> Fire the Event for each Order of the Gauntlet member the first time the party carries a haul of 1,000 gp or more out of a single operation after that member took the oath. A member who joins later is asked about the first haul after their own oath, and an earlier haul never counts.
>
> - **What counts:** coin, trade goods and gems at face value, taken or earned in one operation, with magic items left out of the count.
> - **What does not count:** pay from a faction mission and the takings of the Trollskull tavern.
> - **The first likely case:** the two saddlebags of trade bars in the Gralhund coach house, 2,000 gp in all. Members who took the oath before the night of Ches 24 are asked on Ches 26.
> - **Once only:** the Event fires once for each member in the campaign. Later hauls are never asked of that member again.

> [!gamemaster]**The Sum**
>
> The party divides the haul evenly among its player characters, and the member owes 25% of that equal share. If the party splits the haul unevenly, the member still owes a quarter of the equal share, and every figure rounds up.

| Party size | Equal share of 2,000 gp | Tithe owed |
|---|---|---|
| 3 characters | 667 gp | 167 gp |
| 4 characters | 500 gp | 125 gp |
| 5 characters | 400 gp | 100 gp |

> [!gamemaster]**The Clock**
>
> Day one is the first dawn after the haul is in Waterdeep, and Savra asks at dusk on day two. Brother Hallin's desk closes at dusk, so a tithe must be paid and receipted before then to count as paid before she asks.

| When the member pays | What Savra says | Outcome | Renown |
|---|---|---|---|
| In full before dusk on day two | "Hallin has you down." | **Tithe Paid** | +1 once |
| In full by dusk on day three, after she asks | "Good." | **Tithe Paid After Asking** | None |
| Less than a quarter before she asks, and the rest by dusk on day three | "Good." | **Tithe Paid After Asking** | None |
| Refuses, or has paid nothing by dusk on day three | "All right." | **Quietly Reassigned** | None |

### Paying Before She Asks

A member who knows of the Order's expectation can pay at any time on days one and two. Savra explains it at the First Meeting, and other members may pass it on. The desk is in the east annex of the Halls of Justice, open from dawn to dusk.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The east annex of the Halls of Justice smells of lamp oil and damp wool, and a line of petitioners waits along the wall with their papers. A clerk's desk stands beside the door-warden's bench, where a thin man in a plain grey surcoat is writing in a ledger. He looks up when you reach the front of the line and sets down his pen.
>
> > "Brother Hallin Ostrel, for the Order. Give me your name, the cause and the sum you want to give, and I'll write the receipt while you watch."

> [!social]**Brother Hallin Ostrel**
>
> Brother Hallin Ostrel (Lawful Good, Human, he/him) :: the Order of the Gauntlet's clerk at the Halls of Justice, a thin, ink-stained man in his fifties who keeps the Order's accounts and the door-warden's message shelf.
>
> Hallin is patient with people who have never given money away in an organized fashion. He writes everything down twice, speaks in short practical sentences, and does not moralize. He gives each member a receipt naming the cause, the sum and the date. The Order's runner takes the coin across the city the same day.
>
> Hallin is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - The four causes, what each one does and where its money goes.
> - Whether a payment in trade goods or gems counts, which it does at face value.
> - What a member still owes if they pay in part.
> - Whether a payment can be split, which it can, with a receipt for each part.
>
> He will not discuss other members' payments, and he will not say what Savra thinks of any member.

> [!qna]**What counts as a worthy cause?**
>
> Hallin turns the ledger so you can read the short list on its first page.
>
> > "There are four, and any one of them is enough, or you can split the sum between them if you like. The Trollskull Alley fund doesn't count, and neither does your own party's purse."

> [!qna]**Can I pay in goods?**
>
> > "Trade goods and gems count, yes, at the face value I write down. A magic item doesn't count, and neither does a favour, but I'll note what you give and what it was worth."

> [!qna]**Does anyone else see this?**
>
> > "Savra reads my list each evening, and nobody else does. I don't tell the Order's other members who has paid, because it isn't their concern."

> [!exploration]**Worthy Causes**
>
> Hallin takes the coin for any of the four causes below, and Savra ranks none of them. A member can instead pay a cause directly and bring the receipt to Hallin to countersign before dusk.
>
> - **Temple of Helm, Castle Ward.** Helm's priests run a hospice wing for Watch veterans and their families, and the donation goes to the healing work.
> - **Halls of Justice, Legal Aid Fund.** Tyr's clergy argue for Field Ward residents who cannot afford a hearing before the Lords, and the fund is always short.
> - **Field Ward Soup Houses.** Three independent kitchens feed about four hundred people a day, and all three are underfunded.
> - **Griffon Cavalry Dependents Fund.** The families of Cavalry riders who were killed or maimed in service receive the money. Savra does not name this cause, and if a member chooses it she says nothing further.
>
> A payment counts when the member names the cause, hands over the sum, and Hallin writes the receipt with the date. Hallin refuses the Trollskull Alley fund, because the party would be tithing to itself.

### The Knock

At dusk on day two, Savra goes to the manor and knocks once. She speaks to each Order of the Gauntlet member in alphabetical order by character name, and she holds Hallin's evening list in one hand. Companions may stay in the room, but she addresses only members.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Dusk settles on Trollskull Alley, and the lamplighter has reached the far end of the lane when a single knock sounds at the manor door. Savra Belabranta waits on the step in her dark riding coat with a folded slip in one hand, and she comes in when you ask her and stays on her feet.

> [!social]**Savra Belabranta**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: a late-twenties knight of the Order with her dark hair worn back, a strong jaw, a dark riding coat and a silver gauntlet pin at her collar.
>
> Savra does not sit and does not accept tea, because she has one piece of Order business and wants to be done with it. She speaks plainly and states the hard part first. She gives a single measured nod only to answers she believes are honest.
>
> Savra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the Order expects, what it will do about a member who falls short, and how to put it right.
> - Where the money goes, which she describes in a sentence and then sends the member to Hallin.
> - Her own view of the haul, which she gives only when asked and only once.
>
> She will not discuss what she herself gave, which cause a member should choose, or the Cassalanters.

If the member has already paid, run **When the Tithe Is Paid Before the Knock**, and otherwise run **The Ask**.

> [!qna]**Why a quarter?**
>
> Savra considers the question for a moment before she answers.
>
> > "A quarter is enough that you feel it, and not so much that you can't live on the rest. I paid the same when I joined, and I won't ask you for more."

> [!qna]**What if I already spent my share?**
>
> > "Then you owe it from whatever you have left, and coin, trade goods and gems all count at Hallin's valuation. I don't care how you find it, only that you do."

> [!qna]**What if I refuse?**
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly. I stop offering you the extras, and the work still comes to you by Hallin's note. Your Renown stays where it is, and it ends the day you pay the quarter."

> [!qna]**Do my companions owe anything?**
>
> > "No, they're not in the Order and it isn't asked of them. If they choose to give, Hallin will write it down, but I won't press anyone."

> [!qna]**Which cause would you pick?**
>
> > "I don't pick, because if I named one you'd choose it to please me. That isn't what the gift is for."

### When the Tithe Is Paid Before the Knock

Savra reads the member's name off Hallin's slip, checks that the sum is a full quarter of the equal share, and says nothing about the cause. Mark **Tithe Paid** for the member and award 1 Renown, once only.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra unfolds the slip and runs her thumb down a short column of names until she reaches yours, and then she looks up.
>
> > "Hallin has you down for the full quarter, with a receipt. I didn't have to ask you for it, and I'll remember that."
>
> She gives a single short nod, folds the slip again and puts it inside her coat.

A member who paid less than a quarter before she knocked gets a different reading. Savra tells the member the sum still owed and the day it is due, on the same terms as **The Ask**. A member who completes the payment by dusk on day three is marked **Tithe Paid After Asking** and receives no Renown.

### The Ask

Read this aloud to a member whose name is not on Hallin's slip.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra stays by the door and folds her hands in front of her, and she looks at you and not at the table or the room.
>
> > "The Order asks one thing of its members after a haul like this one. A quarter of your own share goes to a cause that looks after people who can't look after themselves, and Hallin's list has no name of yours on it. Will you pay?"

Tell the member the exact sum from **The Sum** before they answer. A member can agree, refuse, or ask a question first.

#### The Member Agrees

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra gives one short nod and takes a step back toward the door.
>
> > "Good. Take it to Hallin by dusk tomorrow, and he'll write the receipt. I don't need to see it myself."

Savra says nothing more about the money and never raises it again. If the member pays by dusk on day three, mark **Tithe Paid After Asking** and award no Renown. If dusk on day three passes with the quarter unpaid, mark **Quietly Reassigned** at the next dawn instead. Hallin's note then arrives at noon with the terms of the remedy.

#### The Member Refuses

Savra asks once more so that nobody can say they misunderstood, and a member who haggles to a sum under a quarter is refusing.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra waits a moment with her hands folded, then asks again in the same level voice.
>
> > "Did I hear you correctly? You won't pay a quarter of your share."
>
> You tell her that she did, and she looks at you for a few seconds while her face does not change.
>
> > "All right. The tithe stays open until it's paid, and Brother Hallin will tell you the rest."
>
> She turns, steps down into the lane and walks toward the corner of Trollskull Alley.

Mark **Quietly Reassigned** for the member as soon as Savra has gone. At noon the next day, a Priest Acolyte in a plain grey surcoat brings Hallin's note to the member, and the note names the remedy. Read the following aloud:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The acolyte hands you a folded paper sealed with the Halls of Justice wax and leaves without waiting for a reply. The note holds one short paragraph in a neat, upright hand, and you read it standing in the doorway.
>
> > Haul recorded. A quarter of your equal share stands open. Pay it to any of the four causes through this desk and the matter is closed the next morning. B.H.O.

### What Quietly Reassigned Takes

> [!exploration]**Quietly Reassigned**
>
> Savra stops offering the member the optional extras, but she does not stop the work or touch the member's Renown or rank. The list below is complete.
>
> - **Briefs:** the member still receives each Brief that their Renown allows. The Brief arrives as a note from Brother Hallin, carried by a Priest Acolyte, and it restates the deadline. Savra does not come to the manor door, and the member gives the debrief to her at the Halls of Justice as usual.
> - **Marcheon:** Savra does not offer to fight beside the member. If the member asks for her in writing, through the door-warden, she answers within a tenday.
> - **Temple briefings:** the one briefing the member's rank gives each tenday stays, but Savra adds no follow-up briefings.
> - **Whitehawk:** the surveillance team that the rank gives stays. Savra does not offer a team for a place or person she has been watching herself.
> - **Rank events:** a rank event still occurs, and Hallin's note summons the member to it in place of Savra's.
> - **Fighting beside the party:** when Savra fights beside the party in **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court** or **The Writ and the Weight**, she does it coldly. She says only "This isn't about you," and she protects the party as she would anyone.
>
> The reassignment lasts until the member pays the declined quarter, and it costs no Renown.

### The Remedy

A reassigned member pays the declined quarter of their equal share, receipted by Hallin, to any of the four causes. They can pay at any time and need not go to Savra. Mark the payment as **Tithe Paid After Asking**, remove **Quietly Reassigned**, and award no Renown. At first light on the morning after the payment, Savra comes to the manor door.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A single knock sounds at the manor door a little after dawn, and the lane outside is still wet from the night. Savra Belabranta stands on the step with Hallin's slip in one hand and her breath showing in the cold.
>
> > "Hallin says the quarter is paid, and the matter is closed. Thank you."
>
> She hands you the slip and goes back down the lane.

### Renown Opportunities

This Event awards no base Renown, and companions gain none. Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member can gain the following bonus once in the campaign.

- **+1 Renown:** the member pays at least 25% of their equal share, and Hallin's receipt is dated before Savra knocks.

A member who pays after Savra has asked, or after being reassigned, gains no Renown.

### Aftermath

Savra says nothing more of the money, whatever the member chose. A member who paid before she knocked finds that she nods to them in the street, and a member who paid late finds that she treats them as she did before.

A reassigned member finds the manor door unvisited and a clerk's note in place of Savra's knock, with the work otherwise unchanged. Savra speaks to the member in the same way at the Halls of Justice, and she will not mention the reassignment unless asked. If the member asks why, she says "Because you chose it, and I'm not angry about that."

### Concluding the Event

The Event concludes for a member when Savra has left the manor, and it concludes again for a reassigned member when the remedy is paid.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Tithe Paid** — mark with the member's name when they pay a full quarter of their equal share and Hallin dates the receipt before Savra knocks; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).
> - **Tithe Paid After Asking** — mark with the member's name when they pay a full quarter by dusk on day three, or pay the declined quarter later as the remedy; read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).
> - **Quietly Reassigned** — mark with the member's name when they refuse, pay under a quarter and do not make it up, or let dusk on day three pass unpaid; remove it when the remedy is paid. Read by every Order of the Gauntlet Brief, which then arrives by Hallin's note. Also read by **Marcheon**, **Whitehawk**, **Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court**, **The Writ and the Weight** and **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted).
>
> This Event also reads **Order of the Gauntlet Joined**, which names the members it is asked of.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> Order of the Gauntlet Briefs continue to become available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches the Renown and level each requires. A reassigned member receives them by Hallin's note until the remedy is paid. This Event awards no Milestone Points.

## Overview

An Order of the Gauntlet member who helps carry out a haul of 1,000 gp or more is asked for a quarter of their own share. What the member does before and after Savra's knock decides how the Order treats them.

## Summary

Savra Belabranta came to the manor at dusk with a slip from Brother Hallin and asked what each of us would give from the haul. Those of us who had paid already were named on the list, and the rest of us were asked once and given a day. She told us plainly that anyone who refused would lose the extras she offered and nothing else.
