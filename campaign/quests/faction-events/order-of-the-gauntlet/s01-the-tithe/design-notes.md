# Design Notes: The Tithe

## What the Source Gave

The restored draft had a good scene and the wrong frame. Appendix B and the Factions Guide say a member who tithes at least 25% of a major hoard without prompting earns the Renown, and the original Alexandrian text expects the Order to ask for a quarter of the vault gold. The draft paid Renown whether the member tithed unprompted or when asked, treated the whole party as one payer, and sent the fallout to Sister Valdra.

The keeper lines survive: "Good." after payment, "All right." after refusal, and "Thank you" for the remedy. The five-cause list and Savra's refusal to name the Griffon Cavalry fund also survive.

## What Changed

The tithe is now owed by each member from their own equal share, so a non-member, a companion or a member of another faction is never pressed. A clock fixes the word "unprompted": Savra knocks at dusk on the second day, and only a payment receipted before that counts for the +1. Payment after she asks earns no Renown and a single "Good."

Brother Hallin Ostrel takes the coin and the receipt, so Savra judges the member and not the money. Quietly Reassigned is written as a list of what stops (the extras Savra offers unasked) and what stays (Briefs by clerk's note, the rank's benefits, Renown). The remedy ends it the next morning.

## Departures from the Source

The Trollskull Alley fund is removed, because the party would be tithing to itself. Sister Valdra is removed, because her Notable Figures page says Savra does not know her. The Vault of Dragons Final Approach does not currently read any tithe outcome (`arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md` line 49), so each outcome carries a note that the unconverted quest must adopt it. The earlier claim that a dossier stays "accessible at Renown 10" is dropped here, because Whitehawk owns the dossier.

The 1,000 gp threshold is an invention of the restored draft and is kept. Excluding faction mission pay and tavern takings is new. The Gralhund coach house (2,000 gp, `g03-coach-house.md` line 13) is named as the first likely trigger.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- **Brother Hallin Ostrel**, the Order's clerk at the Halls of Justice, is invented. A search of `campaign/` for "Hallin" and "Ostrel" found no match. His voice comes from the Event text, since no profile exists. The door-warden is unnamed here.
- **Notes:** Hallin's note is signed "B.H.O." and uses the grey-surcoat Priest Acolyte, matching the contact object in the First Meeting. Other Order events must treat Hallin's notes as the Briefs of a Quietly Reassigned member.
- **Readers to wire:** the Order Briefs, **Marcheon** and **Whitehawk** must each read **Quietly Reassigned**. The Event names M4 and M6 for the cold fight. **Savra's Past** does not read these outcomes, and a reassigned member there should be checked.
- **Halls of Justice ward** is not stated here. The First Meeting fixes it.
