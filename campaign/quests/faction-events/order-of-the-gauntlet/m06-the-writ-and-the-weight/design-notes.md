# Design Notes: The Writ and the Weight

## What the Source Gave

Appendix B and C stop at the fifth Order of the Gauntlet mission, so this mission has no source text beyond one row of the Factions Guide. The restored draft supplied the shape: a Horned Devil at midnight in the Halls of Justice, Savra fighting beside the party, two Priests of Tyr and a formal Recognition. Those were kept, along with the line that the visit proves the petition is working.

## What Changed

The gate is Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**, as in the other sixth missions. The villa always precedes it, so the writ now reads the Cassalanter Villa result in a four-row table. Nobody says "contractual counterpart" or "infernal rider", and the family does not send the devil in three of the four rows. The nave is the Halls of Justice, in the Castle Ward, and the hearing gives the night a reason.

The fight follows the mechanics reference. The Horned Devil is Power 130, the High-Power Warning sits in the hazard block, and the Priests fight at three characters (Setup B) and stay at the exhibit at five (Setup A). *Banishment* is a scroll or a character's cast at 6 to 16 percent, and the devil is sent back by *Diabolical Restoration*, not destroyed. The wards are *Spirit Guardians* and the hallowed vestry, with no change to Power.

## Departures from the Source

The restored draft gave the Priests 5th-level spell access and *Banishment* at DC 16, *Battle Frenzy* to the devil and a Founders' Day line that cannot stand after the villa. All are gone. The Recognition names every character present, member or not, and the Watch effect is now a defined minute of hearing, once a tenday per character.

**If Not Run.** If the party never plays the mission, **Order Recognized** is not marked. The Vault should use a plain letter from the Halls of Justice, which Savra writes and which grants the Watch's courtesy but no testimony. Savra still asks at the gate whether the Cassalanter matter is closed.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Magistrate Odrane Belcast and Sister Merrin Oakhollow are invented. A search of `campaign/` for "Odrane", "Belcast", "Merrin" and "Oakhollow" found no match. Father Corwen Thale, Brother Hallin Ostrel and Brother Haldric Wence come from the finished Order pages.
- The Halls of Justice is the Castle Ward temple of Tyr, as the First Meeting fixes it. The nave stands for r25's "main chamber".
- The bailiff is the Horned Devil's title only. No devil has a name in this mission.
- The scroll is Level 4 (DC 15, about 9 percent). The scroll reading rule for a class list is unverified, and so is the 2024 *Spirit Guardians* wording.
- The villa results come from `arc-g`, which is unconverted, as are **Vault of Dragons** and the readers of both outcomes.
- `arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md` line 49 still says "If Mission 6 succeeded" and should read **Order Recognized**.
