# The Writ and the Weight

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat Event begins when a Priest Acolyte brings Savra Belabranta's note to an Order of the Gauntlet member at noon. It ends when the members take her debrief in the east annex at the third bell the next morning. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Take Savra's brief in the east annex and read the black-edged notice that names every character in the party.
> - Choose where the Lords' exhibit sleeps tonight: the oak cabinet, a carrier's satchel or the hallowed vestry.
> - Help the magistrate admit the exhibit to the record by one of three routes.
> - Fight a Horned Devil that serves a writ at midnight, with Savra and two Priests of Tyr beside them.
> - Send the devil back to the Nine Hells, or try a scroll of *Banishment* at long odds.
> - Receive the Recognition, which names every character who stood in the hall that night.
>
> Only Order of the Gauntlet members attend Savra's brief and debrief. Companions attend the hearing, fight in the hall and are named on the Recognition, but they earn no Renown.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The Lords' court hears Savra's petition tonight in the nave of the Halls of Justice, in the Castle Ward. Magistrate Odrane Belcast presides from 20:00 and closes the record at 23:00.
> - The exhibit is the ledger if **Ledger Recovered** is marked. If only **Cassalanter Register Filed** is marked, it is the Watch clerk's copy of the register, and if neither is marked, it is Savra's own case file.
> - When the record closes, the exhibit goes into an oak cabinet in the north aisle under the Lords' seal. It travels to the Lords' court at dawn.
> - A black-edged notice was nailed to the Halls' west door at dawn. It names Savra and every character in the party, and it promises a writ served inside the Halls at midnight.
> - The server is a Horned Devil that calls itself the bailiff. Who sent it, and whom the writ names, depends on the Cassalanter Villa result in **Who Is Serving the Writ**.
> - In three of the four results the family has no hand in the visit. When **Cassalanters Exposed** is the result, the family did not ask for it and would not mourn it.
> - Savra speaks of the notice, the bailiff and the writ. She has only the party's account of the villa, so she does not say what the family worshipped or bargained, and she answers a character who does with "I have your word on that."
> - Nobody in this Event says Manshoon's name unless **Manshoon Named** is marked for the member addressed. Until then Savra says "the Splinter", and she says "the carnival fleet" until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked.

### The Brief

At noon a Priest Acolyte in a plain grey surcoat finds each qualifying member and hands over Savra's note. The meeting is in the east annex of the Halls of Justice at the third bell past noon, and Savra briefs members only. Companions come to the nave at 19:30, and the members decide what to tell them before then.

If **Quietly Reassigned** is marked for a member, Brother Hallin Ostrel writes the note in Savra's place and signs it "B.H.O.", and the brief itself is unchanged.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A young man in a plain grey surcoat knocks at the door a little after noon and holds out a paper folded once. The seal is the Halls of Justice, and he walks off down the lane before you have broken it. The handwriting inside slants to the left, and it is signed with two letters at the bottom.
>
> A notice was nailed to the Halls door at dawn, and it carries your names. The Lords hear my petition tonight, so come to the east annex at the third bell past noon. Bring the rest of your company to the nave by half past seven. S.B.

The east annex is quiet at this hour, and Brother Haldric Wence, the door-warden, sends the members down the passage without a word.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The east annex smells of lamp oil and wet wool, and the line of petitioners has been sent home early. Savra waits at the long table at the end of the passage, with a sheet of black-edged vellum laid flat under her hand, and she turns it toward you when you reach her.
>
> > "This was nailed to the west door at dawn. It names you and it names me, and it says a writ will be served inside the Halls at midnight. Read it, then tell me what you make of it."

The notice is a document that the players can read, and you write each character's name in its list.

> NOTICE OF SERVICE. A writ will be served at midnight tonight, within the walls of the Halls of Justice, on the persons named below, who may choose the place of service. Savra Belabranta; then the name of every character in the party, member or not.

> [!social]**The Knight Before the Hearing**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: the Order's knight in Waterdeep, in her late twenties, with dark hair worn back and a strong jaw.
>
> Savra has not slept, and she keeps her voice low and level. She pauses before every serious answer and gives her single nod only to an honest one. She swears rarely, in short bursts, and only at wrongdoing.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss include:
>
> - The notice, the hearing and what the Lords' court will do with the exhibit.
> - The two Priests of Tyr and what they will and will not do.
> - Where the exhibit should sleep, and who should hold it.
> - The scroll of *Banishment* in the strongroom, and how little she trusts it.
>
> She will not say what the family worshipped, and she will not guess who holds the claim.

> [!qna]**Who sent it?**
>
> Savra rests both hands on the table and takes a moment before she answers.
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly: I don't know, and I won't guess. A devil nailed that to my door, and devils serve paper for somebody. I only know the paper has your names on it."

> [!qna]**Why tonight?**
>
> > "The Lords close the record at eleven, and the exhibit sleeps in the north aisle until dawn. Whoever sent this wants it burned before the Lords can carry it out of here."

> [!qna]**Can we refuse to be named?**
>
> > "No, a notice like that finds its names wherever they stand. But it lets you choose the place of service, and I'd choose this hall. The roof of the Maimed God's house has held a long time."

> [!qna]**Will you fight beside us?**
>
> > "Yes. I've faced worse than a devil with a scroll, and I was on the wrong side of it then. Father Corwen and Sister Merrin will stand with us, and I won't ask more of them than they've offered."

> [!qna]**What do you want from us, and by when?**
>
> > "Be in the nave by half past seven, with your whole company. Tell me where the exhibit should sleep, because I'll put it where you say. The Lords rise at eleven and the bailiff comes at midnight."

When the members agree, Savra walks them to the nave and sets them to work. A member who refuses the brief is not penalized, and the notice still names that character.

### Who Is Serving the Writ

The writ reads differently for each result of **Cassalanter Villa**. Use the row that matches the result the party marked. If the villa has not run, use the last row.

> [!gamemaster]**The Four Writs**
>
> | Villa result | The writ names | The bailiff serves | The family |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **Contract Destroyed** | The characters who struck the statue or held the mace. | The Nine Hells' claims office, which wants the broken bond answered for. | Free of it, and no hand in the visit. |
> | **Cooperative / Gold Payment** | The characters who stood surety when the debt was paid. | Asmodeus's account, which wants the record of the payment burned. | Paid, silent and no hand in the visit. |
> | **Sacrifice Happened** | Every character who was in the villa on the last night. | Asmodeus's account, which wants the witnesses and the register of the dead gone. | Content, and no hand in the visit. |
> | **Cassalanters Exposed** (the default if the villa has not run) | Every character who carried evidence to the Lords. | The claim on the family's debts, which wants the evidence burned. | Held or in flight. They did not ask for it. |
>
> In every row the writ also names Savra, and the bailiff wants the exhibit. The bailiff never says "Asmodeus". It says "my lord".

### The Hall Before the Hearing

The members and their companions have from 19:30 until the hearing opens to prepare the nave. Brother Haldric Wence lets everyone in through the west door, and Father Corwen Thale and Sister Merrin Oakhollow wait at the altar steps.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The nave of the Halls of Justice is a long stone room with a vaulted ceiling, and forty lamps burn along the walls. A long table has been set before the altar steps, and an oak cabinet bound in iron stands in the north aisle. Two priests in grey robes wait near the steps, and a small oak door in the northeast corner is shut.
>
> > "I'm Father Corwen," says the older of the two, lifting the stump of his left wrist in greeting. "That door is the vestry, and it has been hallowed since before I came. Tell us where you want us."

> [!gamemaster]**The Nave**
>
> - **Size:** The nave is 60 feet long and 36 feet wide, with a ceiling 30 feet high. The west double doors open on a vestibule, and a window above them is the only opening large enough for the devil.
> - **The cabinet:** Oak and iron, in the north aisle 30 feet from the altar steps. It holds the exhibit once the record closes.
> - **The vestry:** A hallowed room 20 feet square in the northeast corner, behind an oak door with an iron lock. Father Corwen holds the key. A Fiend cannot willingly enter it, but *Hurl Flame* reaches through an open door.
> - **The lamps:** Forty lamps hold the nave bright enough to see the whole room from any aisle.

> [!social]**The Two Priests of Tyr**
>
> Father Corwen Thale (Lawful Good, Human, he/him) :: a gray-haired priest of Tyr with a calm, gravelly voice and a missing left hand, who calls the god "the Just God".
>
> Father Corwen is patient and curses mildly, if at all. Sister Merrin Oakhollow, a broad-shouldered priest in her forties, stands beside him and answers in as few words as she can. Both stand where the party puts them.
>
> Conversation topics the priests are willing to discuss include:
>
> - The vestry, its hallowing and the key.
> - What they can cast and when.
> - What they will do if Savra falls.
>
> They will not discuss the Order's cases, and neither will leave the nave.

> [!qna]**What can you do?**
>
> > "We can fight, and we can heal, and Merrin has a prayer that makes the air around her hard to cross. Savra's life comes first if she falls. We stabilize her before we do anything else."

> [!qna]**Will the vestry hold?**
>
> > "A devil can't walk into it of its own will, and I'd stake the Halls on that. It can throw fire through the door, though, so don't stand where it can see you."

> [!exploration]**Where the Exhibit Sleeps**
>
> The exhibit is a bound book or a stack of papers. The party chooses where it rests at 23:00, and the choice sets what the devil does first.
>
> - **The cabinet:** The oak is a flammable object that is neither worn nor carried, so one *Hurl Flame* from the devil ignites it. The cabinet burns for 3 rounds, and a character within 5 feet can pull the exhibit out as an action, with no check. A carried exhibit cannot be ignited.
> - **A carrier:** One character carries the exhibit in a satchel. The devil cannot burn it, so it goes for the carrier first, but its three attacks still go to at least two creatures each turn.
> - **The vestry:** Father Corwen locks the exhibit in an iron strongbox inside, and the devil cannot enter. It turns on the named characters at once, and it uses *Hurl Flame* at anyone visible through the door.

> [!exploration]**Bringing the Wards to Bear**
>
> The Halls give the party two wards, and neither changes the roster.
>
> - **Spirit Guardians:** Either Priest can cast *Spirit Guardians* (spell save DC 13) as her action on her first turn. The 15-foot emanation halves the speed of the devil inside it and deals 3d8 radiant damage, or half on a successful Wisdom save, when it enters or starts its turn there. A Priest who stands beside the exhibit holds the devil off it for as long as she keeps her concentration.
> - **The vestry door:** A character who stands 10 feet or more inside the vestry is out of the devil's melee reach. *Hurl Flame* still reaches through the open door, and the devil keeps firing at any character it can see.
> - **Effect:** With the wards in play the devil spends its early rounds crossing the aura and the door, which shortens the fight. The Day Cost and the band do not change.

> [!exploration]**The Strongroom Scroll**
>
> At 18:00 Brother Hallin Ostrel brings a *Spell Scroll* of *Banishment* from the Order's strongroom. It is a Level 4 scroll with spell save DC 15, and only a character whose class lists *Banishment* can read it.
>
> - **The devil's save:** Its Charisma saving throw is +8, and *Magic Resistance* gives it Advantage. The scroll lands about 9 times in 100.
> - **A character's own cast:** A spell save DC of 14 lands about 6 times in 100, a DC of 15 about 9 and a DC of 16 about 12.
> - **If it lands:** The devil is Incapacitated in a harmless demiplane, and the caster must hold concentration for the full 10 rounds. A Fiend still banished at the end of the minute does not return, and it counts as sent back.
> - **Reading:** The scroll is a chance and not a plan. Savra says so, and she asks the party not to build the night around it.

### The Hearing

The hearing opens at 20:00 and closes at 23:00. The Lords' court is a formality for the Order but not for the exhibit, which must enter the record before the court will carry it.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The nave has been set as a court, with a long table before the altar steps and the lamps turned up along the walls. Magistrate Odrane Belcast sits in the middle in grey robes, with a clerk on either side, and Savra stands at a lectern with her hands flat on the wood. The magistrate lifts a small bronze bell and rings it once.
>
> > "The Lords' court is open. We hear the petition of Savra Belabranta, and we hear it with the exhibit before us."

> [!social]**The Presiding Magistrate**
>
> Odrane Belcast (Lawful Neutral, Human, she/her) :: the Lords' magistrate, a heavy woman in grey robes who has heard petitions at the Halls for thirty years.
>
> Belcast is courteous and exact. She asks a witness each question once and writes the answer herself. She does not hint at her opinion of the family or the Order.
>
> Conversation topics Belcast is willing to discuss include:
>
> - How the exhibit enters the record, and who may speak to it.
> - Why the court sits at night.
> - What becomes of the exhibit when the record closes.
>
> She will not discuss what the Lords will decide.

> [!qna]**Why does the court sit at night?**
>
> > "The Lords are busy by day, and this petition has waited a long time. They will not wait for another tenday, so the court sits when they can."

> [!qna]**Who may speak to the exhibit?**
>
> > "Anyone who handled it or who saw it taken. I will hear one witness for each route, and the first to satisfy me admits it."

> [!qna]**What happens when the record closes?**
>
> > "It is sealed in the cabinet in the north aisle under the Lords' seal, and it goes to the Lords' court at dawn. Nobody opens it before then."

> [!exploration]**Admitting the Exhibit**
>
> The exhibit enters the record by any one of three independent routes. A party that follows all three finds that they agree.
>
> - **Savra's account:** She swears to how the exhibit was recovered or filed. This needs no check, and it works whenever **Ledger Recovered** or **Cassalanter Register Filed** is marked.
> - **A witness:** Any character who took part in the recovery or the filing gives an account and makes a successful **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check. On a failure, the magistrate asks for the account in writing, which costs an hour and no more.
> - **The clerk's stamp:** If **Cassalanter Register Filed** is marked, the Watch clerk's stamp on the filing admits the exhibit with no check.
>
> If no route is available, the magistrate admits the exhibit as an Order file and gives it less weight. Nothing in the hall changes.

When the record closes at 23:00, the magistrate seals the exhibit and the clerks carry it to its place. The Lords' court rises, and Magistrate Belcast leaves with her clerks by the west door.

### The Notice at Midnight

The hall empties, and only the party, Savra and the two Priests stay. Brother Haldric Wence bars the west door from outside and takes Brother Hallin Ostrel to the annex. The lamps stay lit.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The bell of the Halls tolls twelve, and the last stroke is still in the air when the window above the west doors bursts inward in a spray of glass. Something heavy lands on the flagstones of the vestibule with a sound like a dropped anvil, and the lamps gutter. A horned devil twice the height of a man unrolls a black-edged scroll in one clawed hand and steps into the nave.

Read the branch that matches the Cassalanter Villa result. If the villa has not run, read the last one.

If **Contract Destroyed** is the result, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "A writ is served on those who broke what was sealed in the Sea Ward. The bond is void, and my lord's claim now rests on the hands that voided it. Surrender the book that records it, and stand answerable."

If **Cooperative / Gold Payment** is the result, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "A writ is served on those who stood surety when the debt was paid. The account is closed, and the book in this hall is my lord's receipt. Surrender it, and stand answerable for what you saw."

If **Sacrifice Happened** is the result, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "A writ is served on those who stood in the house on the last night. My lord's account is settled, and its witnesses are not. Surrender the book that names the dead, and stand answerable."

If **Cassalanters Exposed** is the result, or the villa has not run, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "A writ is served on those who carried the family's name to the Lords. The family's debts stand, and my lord's claim answers all who dragged them into daylight. Surrender the book, and stand answerable."

The bailiff names Savra and each character in turn, and then it lowers the scroll and says no more. The fight begins when it takes its first action.

### The Midnight Hall

The party has had hours to arrange the nave, and the exhibit lies wherever the party left it. The bailiff acts first unless a character attacks before the scroll is lowered.

> [!hazard]**The Bailiff**
>
> **High-Power Warning.** The Horned Devil is Power 130 against 41 for each 7th-level character. That is 3.2 times a character and past the 2 times line in the CR 2.0 guide. It can remove a character in one exchange if its attacks pile up. The spread rules below are part of its stat block, and the bands only hold when the table follows them.
>
> Use the 2024 **Horned Devil** stat block: AC 18, 199 hit points, Fly 60 feet, Cha +8 and *Magic Resistance*. It has no *Battle Frenzy*. Savra uses the **Knight** block and withdraws at 20 hit points. The two Priests use the **Priest** block and withdraw at 19. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits the roster.
>
> - **Three characters:** The devil alone. Savra and both Priests fight. This is Bloody (Day Cost 6), and no easier band is possible at this size.
> - **Four characters, Standard:** The devil alone. Savra and both Priests fight. This is Bruising (Day Cost 4).
> - **Four characters, Hard:** The devil alone. Savra fights, and the Priests stay at the exhibit and do not fight. This is Bloody (Day Cost 6).
> - **Five characters, Standard:** The devil alone. Savra fights, and the Priests stay at the exhibit. This is Bruising (Day Cost 4).
> - **Five characters, Hard:** The devil and two **Imps**, the writ's clerks, who arrive with it. Savra fights, and the Priests stay at the exhibit. This is Bloody (Day Cost 6).
>
> Use Hard when the villa result is **Cassalanters Exposed** or the villa has not run, when the family stood at Lockdown, or when the party opens the fight. Hard has no separate roster at three characters.
>
> #### The Bailiff's Tactics
> To begin, the devil uses *Hurl Flame* on the exhibit if it rests in the cabinet. If the exhibit is carried or locked in the vestry, it fires at the nearest named character instead.
>
> During combat, the devil:
> - Divides its three attacks among at least two creatures each turn and never puts all three on one.
> - Uses *Infernal Tail* at most once a round, in place of one attack, on a creature within 10 feet.
> - Never attacks a creature at 0 hit points, because the writ names the living.
> - Flies 20 feet above the floor to use *Hurl Flame* on the cabinet or the carrier, and drops to *Searing Fork* when a character comes within 10 feet.
> - Turns on the named characters when the exhibit is out of reach, taking whichever is nearest and still dividing its attacks.
>
> The Imps, if present, turn invisible and sting the exhibit's carrier or the nearest Priest. They flee when the devil is sent back.
>
> The devil never surrenders. It leaves at first light, with the writ unserved, if the party shuts itself inside the vestry. The Priests stabilize Savra with no roll if she falls, and a Priest who does this does not attack that turn.

> [!exploration]**Sent Back, Not Destroyed**
>
> The bailiff is a Horned Devil, and a Horned Devil that dies outside the Nine Hells reforms there with all its hit points. When it reaches 0 hit points, its body goes up in sulfurous smoke, and you mark **Devil Sent Back**.
>
> - **A banished devil:** If a successful *Banishment* holds for the full minute, the devil is also sent back, and you mark **Devil Sent Back**.
> - **The exhibit burned:** If the cabinet ignites and no one pulls the exhibit out, the exhibit is lost. The Lords' clerks still hold the record of the hearing, and the petition stands, but the first Renown bonus is lost.
> - **If the party retreats:** A party that shuts itself inside the vestry is safe until first light, and the writ lapses unserved. **Devil Sent Back** is not marked, and Savra's debrief changes.

After the devil is gone, the lamps steady and the smell of hot iron lingers in the nave. The Priests tend whoever is hurt, and Savra sheathes her sword.

### Renown Opportunities

The debrief is at the third bell the next morning in the east annex. Savra holds it with members only, and Brother Hallin Ostrel waits at the clerk's desk with the Recognition. Companions wait in the nave.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Morning light falls through the annex windows onto the long table, where a single sheet of white vellum lies with the Halls of Justice seal pressed into the corner. Savra stands beside it in her dark riding coat, and the silver gauntlet pin catches the light. She waits until all the members are in the room before she speaks.
>
> > "Sit if you want to. I'll tell you what I think, and then I'll tell you what the Lords did."

If **Devil Sent Back** is marked, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra gives a single nod, and she lets it stand for a moment.
>
> > "It went back down with its writ unserved, and the exhibit went to the Lords at dawn. That is what I asked of you, and I'll write it down as it happened."

If the party shut itself in the vestry and the devil left at first light, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra does not nod. She looks at the long table and then at you.
>
> > "You lived, and the exhibit lived, and the bailiff left with its paper. It will come again for the names on that notice. I'd have liked it gone, but I won't pretend you were wrong to hide."

Read the line that matches the Cassalanter Villa result. If the villa has not run, read the last one.

If **Contract Destroyed** is the result, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Nobody in that family sent this. They are free of it because of you, and the bill has come to you instead. The Order will stand for you, and I'd like that written in the Recognition."

If **Cooperative / Gold Payment** is the result, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "The debt was paid, and Hell wants the receipt kept private. We keep the Lords' copy where Hell can't reach it. I don't like bargaining with devils, even to save children, but I like it less when they send a bailiff."

If **Sacrifice Happened** is the result, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "It came for the witnesses, and ninety-nine are dead. The Order failed them, and so did I. I won't let the book that names them burn as well."

If **Cassalanters Exposed** is the result, or the villa has not run, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "It came for the evidence the Lords hold, and that tells me the evidence matters. The family's debts stand, and so does the Lords' record. I'll tell you plainly that I'm glad you were in the hall."

If **Cassalanters Alerted** is marked, Savra adds one sentence:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "They knew about the petition from the day it was filed. A clerk at the Watch house talks too much, and I should have seen it."

Then Brother Hallin Ostrel hands over the Recognition, and Savra reads out the names.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Brother Hallin lays the white vellum on the table and dips his pen. Savra reads the names in the order they were nailed to the door, and he writes each one in a clerk's neat hand. When he has finished she picks up the sheet and holds it out.
>
> > "This is the Order's Recognition, and it names every one of you who stood in that hall, member or not. You're not knights and you've sworn nothing. But the Halls of Justice knows your faces now."

> [!gamemaster]**The Recognition**
>
> The Recognition is a single sheet under the seal of the Halls of Justice. It names every character present at the hearing and in the hall at midnight, member or not, and you mark **Order Recognized** when Savra hands it over.
>
> - **The Watch:** A Watch officer who reads a character's Recognition hears that character's account for one minute before laying a hand on them. Each character can use this once a tenday.
> - **The testimony:** Savra will testify to the character's conduct if a later hearing in Waterdeep needs her.
> - **The Vault:** **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) reads **Order Recognized** for the Watch's cooperation at the gate.
> - **The loss rule:** A character who strikes a Watch officer or the Order forfeits the Recognition's Watch effect for a tenday.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 4 base Renown for reporting to Savra at the debrief. Companions gain none.
>
> - **+1 Renown:** the bailiff is sent back before it burns the exhibit or comes within 5 feet of it.
> - **+1 Renown:** every character named on the notice survives the night.
> - **+1 Renown:** a character other than Savra or a Priest lands the final blow, or a *Banishment* cast by a party character holds for the full minute.
>
> Each bonus can be earned only once.

### Aftermath

The Lords' court carries the exhibit away at dawn and enters it in the record. The devil reforms in the Nine Hells with all its hit points and remembers every name on the notice. The Halls of Justice repairs its window within the tenday, and Brother Haldric Wence nails a plain board over the hole until then.

If **Devil Sent Back** is marked, Savra files the night in the Order's roll, and the Order's priests tell the other temples of the Triad. If the party hid in the vestry, she tells them the bailiff may come again.

In the **Cassalanters Exposed** result, the family stays in the hands of the Lords' court or in flight. In the other three results the Order holds no further case against the family, and Savra closes her file. **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted) reads **Order Recognized**, and **Righteous Hand** reads both outcomes when Savra names the investiture.

### Concluding the Event

Savra's debrief ends the last Order of the Gauntlet mission, and the two outcomes below are read by later events.

## Overview

Savra Belabranta's petition comes before the Lords' court at the Halls of Justice, and a devil serves a writ in the nave at midnight. The party fights it beside Savra and two Priests of Tyr, and the Order recognizes everyone who stood in the hall.

## Summary

We sat through the Lords' hearing on Savra's petition and waited in the nave for the writ that the black-edged notice promised. A Horned Devil came through the window at midnight and read our names. We fought it beside Savra and two Priests of Tyr until it went back to the Nine Hells, and in the morning she gave us the Order's Recognition. Who nailed that notice to the Halls door, and will the devil come back for our names?
