# The Writ and the Weight

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat Event begins when Savra summons the party to the Halls of Justice ahead of a midnight arrival.
> In this Event:
>
> - Savra arrives at Trollskull Manor earlier than expected, controlled with visible effort.
> - The Halls of Justice have been prepared: a consecrated hall imposing disadvantage on devil Wisdom saves and advantage on character saves against infernal fear.
> - Two priests with 5th-level spell access are present and can cast *Banishment* (DC 16 Charisma) if the party asks; they will not act first.

**Background (DM only)**
Lord Victoro's contract-bound infernal patron dispatched a **Horned Devil** with a specific mandate: recover or destroy the ledger and kill everyone who physically handled it. The devil has been in Waterdeep for six hours before the party is summoned, locating its targets methodically. Savra recognized the infernal notification protocol when it arrived at the Halls this morning.

The consecrated hall has been active since the petition was filed. The high priest opened this specific ritual space on Savra's request. The ledger is in a locked cabinet in an adjacent room; that room has its own door. The Horned Devil knows both facts and has its own approach plan.

### The Halls of Justice at Midnight

The consecrated hall holds the party, Savra, and two priests of significant ability. The theological protections are active:

- All devils have **Disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws** while inside the hall.
- All characters have **Advantage on saving throws against infernal fear effects** while inside the hall.

The hall has two exits — a main corridor door and a vestry door into the prayer space. The Horned Devil will attempt to use both to split the party's attention if given any opportunity to maneuver freely.

The two priests (use **Priest** stat block with 5th-level spell access) are positioned near the corridor door. They can cast *Banishment* (DC 16 Charisma save). They will not act first — this is the party's fight. They fire *Banishment* if a party member explicitly calls for it, or if the Horned Devil reaches the vestry door and the adjacent room becomes accessible.

Savra fights at full capacity. She is angry. It is controlled anger, the kind that comes from experience with exactly this type of enforcement action, and it makes her more effective.

### The Horned Devil

The **Horned Devil** (2024 *Monster Manual*) arrives at midnight through a window in the vestibule. It does not announce itself. It does not negotiate. Its combat priorities in order:

1. Kill the named targets — the party members listed in the infernal notice.
2. Reach the adjacent room and destroy the ledger.

It uses *Hurl Flame* at range while moving toward the ledger cabinet. If the party fully blocks its movement, it commits to melee with *Battle Frenzy*, targeting the nearest named character. It will disengage toward the cabinet whenever the party's formation breaks — even one round of uncontested movement toward the adjacent room door is enough to put the ledger at risk.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Priest Intervention
> The priests do not initiate. If the party explicitly asks for *Banishment*, or if the Horned Devil moves through the vestry door, one priest acts: *Banishment* (DC 16 Charisma save). On a success, the Horned Devil returns at the start of the following round and is aware of the attempt. On a failure, it is banished for 1 minute; if concentration holds for that duration, the banishment is permanent and the mission is complete.

### The Morning After

When the Horned Devil is destroyed or banished, the halls go quiet except for the smell of sulfur and scorch marks on the stone. The ledger is intact. Savra sits down on a pew and stays there for a moment.

> > "That's the first time they've sent something that heavy," she says. "Which means the petition is working." She looks at the party. "They're scared."

Two days later, the Lords' formal review returns a preliminary finding: sufficient grounds to proceed. The Cassalanters are officially under investigation. Lord Victoro Cassalanter attends a charity function the following tenday and smiles at everyone who approaches him.

### The Recognition

Before the party leaves, Savra gives each member a copy of the formal recognition — the Halls of Justice's mark, their names, their standing as Order associates. Not knights. Not sworn members. Recognized affiliates with legal standing in Waterdeep.

Two practical effects follow the recognition:
- Watch officers who see the party in conflict with a Cassalanter operative will not automatically intervene against them.
- In **Vault of Dragons**, if the Halls of Justice formally recognized the party as Order associates, Watch cooperation at the Vault district is marginally better. Savra will testify on the party's behalf if any **Vault of Dragons** complication goes legal.

> > "The Founders' Day timeline is tighter than we thought," she says. "Whatever you're doing with the vault — do it fast."

She shakes each person's hand, and she does not let go right away.

## Summary

A Horned Devil dispatched by Lord Victoro Cassalanter's infernal patron arrived at the Halls of Justice at midnight with orders to destroy the ledger and kill everyone who handled it. The party met it in a consecrated hall. When the fight was over, Savra gave each of them a formal Order recognition. The Lords' review proceeded.
