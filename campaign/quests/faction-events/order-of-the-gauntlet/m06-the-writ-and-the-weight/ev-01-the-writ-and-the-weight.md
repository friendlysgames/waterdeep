# The Writ and the Weight

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Combat Event begins when Savra arrives at Trollskull Manor earlier than expected and summons the party to the Halls of Justice.
> In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Savra's brief at the manor and ask what she knows about the threat.
> - Take up positions in the consecrated hall before midnight.
> - Face the Horned Devil when it arrives through the vestibule window.
> - Prevent it from reaching the adjacent room and the locked cabinet holding the ledger.
> - Ask the priests to cast *Banishment* if the moment calls for it.
> - Receive the Halls of Justice's formal recognition as Order associates after the fight.
>
> #### The Infernal Rider
>
> When the ledger left the Aveen Street shrine, Lord Victoro triggered an infernal clause in his contract requiring his contractual counterpart to recover or destroy any removed ritual documentation and to kill everyone who physically handled it. The clause named each person who touched the ledger. The Horned Devil has been in Waterdeep for six hours before Savra summons the party, locating its named targets methodically through infernal means.
>
> Savra recognized the notification protocol when the formal infernal filing arrived at the Halls of Justice. She chose the Halls as the confrontation ground because it is the most defensible position in Waterdeep for this type of engagement, with the relevant theological protections already active.

### The Brief

Savra arrives at Trollskull Manor before the usual hour, and she is not moving at her usual pace when she knocks.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra is at the door before the usual hour. She closes it behind her and stays there for a moment, which is not something she normally does.
>
> > "The Cassalanters have filed three procedural objections to the Lords' review. This morning the Halls of Justice received a formal notification through their infernal protocols — a petition filed by something identifying itself as Lord Victoro's contractual counterpart."
>
> She meets each of you in turn.
>
> > "Legally, that notification means nothing in Waterdeep. But it is a flag: something is coming to enforce it. Something in the Horned Devil range, based on the filing's classification tier. It named everyone who handled the ledger. I want you at the Halls of Justice tonight."

> [!social]**The Controlled Knight**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Neutral Good, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: a paladin of Tyr who has faced infernal enforcement before and is angry about it in the specific way that experience produces.
>
> Savra is controlled, and it is taking effort. She has been preparing for this confrontation since the petition was filed and she is not afraid of what is coming — she is angry, in the cold and specific way that comes from knowing exactly what infernal enforcement actions look like and what they do to the people they target. She will not pretend it is simple.
>
> She will not tell the party they should not be afraid. She will tell them what they are walking into and what advantages they will have.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss include:
> - The infernal notification: what the filing means and why she is treating it seriously.
> - The Halls of Justice: why she chose it and what the theological protections do.
> - The Horned Devil: what to expect from it, what it will prioritize.
> - The priests: they are capable, they will cast *Banishment* on request, they will not act first.
> - The ledger: it is in a locked cabinet in an adjacent room, and the devil knows this.

> [!qna]**A Horned Devil?**
>
> > "Big. Armored. It Hurls Flame at range while it moves and goes to melee with Battle Frenzy when it commits. It does not negotiate, and it will not stop until its mandate is fulfilled or it is banished. Plan for something that wants to be in two places at once."

> [!qna]**Why the Halls of Justice?**
>
> > "The consecrated hall gives us Disadvantage on devil Wisdom saves and Advantage on our saves against infernal fear effects. That is not nothing. There is no better ground in this city for this specific fight, and I have had since this morning to prepare it."

> [!qna]**What about the priests?**
>
> > "Two priests. Fifth-level spell access, which means Banishment. They will not act unless you ask them to or the devil reaches the vestry door. If it reaches that door, they act. Ask before then if you want it sooner."

> [!qna]**The ledger?**
>
> > "In a locked cabinet in the room adjacent to the hall. The devil's mandate is to destroy it, which means it will try to reach that room. We cannot let it disengage toward the door for any sustained period."

> [!qna]**Any chance of avoiding this?**
>
> She pauses, and it is a long pause.
>
> > "The ledger is in the Halls of Justice and the filing named you. The only place I know of where this ends in our favor is a consecrated hall with a Banishment option. I wouldn't have come to you if I thought we had a better hand."

### The Halls of Justice at Midnight

The party arrives at the Halls of Justice before the bell, and Savra is already there.

> [!readaloud]
> The consecrated hall is lit by the Halls of Justice's permanent fixtures, and it is quiet in the way that places of formal worship are quiet at night, with the smell of incense and old stone and the faint hum of active wards in the walls. Two priests of Tyr are positioned near the corridor door, and they acknowledge the party with a nod and nothing more.
>
> Savra is standing near the center of the hall, and she looks up when you come in.
>
> > "Take positions near the vestry door. If it reaches that door, it reaches the ledger. That door stays closed."

> [!gamemaster]**The Theological Protections**
>
> The active protections in the consecrated hall:
>
> - All devils have **Disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws** while inside the hall.
> - All characters have **Advantage on saving throws against infernal fear effects** while inside the hall.
>
> The hall has two exits: a main corridor door (where the priests are positioned) and a vestry door into the prayer space and the adjacent room with the ledger. The Horned Devil will attempt to use both exits to split the party's attention if given any opportunity to maneuver freely.

The Horned Devil arrives at midnight, through a window in the vestibule.

> [!readaloud]
> The midnight bell is still ringing when the vestibule window comes apart from the outside. There is no announcement and no pause — the thing that comes through the opening is enormous, scaled, and already moving toward the hall, and it has the look of something that knew exactly where it was going before it arrived.

> [!hazard]**The Horned Devil**
>
> One **Horned Devil** (2024 *Monster Manual*) enters through the vestibule window at midnight. It does not negotiate and does not announce itself.
>
> #### Horned Devil Tactics
>
> At the start of combat, the Horned Devil uses *Hurl Flame* at range while moving toward the vestry door. Its combat priorities, in order: kill the named targets (the party members listed in the infernal notice), then reach the adjacent room and destroy the ledger.
>
> Over the course of combat, the devil will prioritize whichever named target is nearest on each turn. If the party fully blocks its movement toward the vestry door, it commits to melee with *Battle Frenzy*, targeting the nearest named character. It will disengage toward the cabinet whenever the party's formation breaks — even one round of uncontested movement toward the vestry door is enough to put the ledger at risk.
>
> The battle ends when the Horned Devil is destroyed or banished.
>
> **Savra** fights at full capacity. Her anger is controlled and it makes her effective.
>
> #### Priest Intervention
>
> Both priests use the **Priest** stat block with 5th-level spell access. The priests do not initiate. If the party explicitly asks for *Banishment*, or if the Horned Devil moves through the vestry door, one priest acts: *Banishment* (DC 16 Charisma saving throw). On a success, the devil returns at the start of the following round and is aware of the attempt. On a failure, it is banished for 1 minute; if concentration holds for that duration, the banishment is permanent and the mandate is unfulfilled.

### The Morning After

When the Horned Devil is destroyed or banished, the halls go quiet except for the smell of sulfur and the scorch marks on the stone. Savra sits down on a pew.

> [!readaloud]
> The hall goes quiet when the devil is gone, and Savra sits down on the nearest pew and stays there for a moment. The priests check the vestry door and come back without saying anything. The smell of sulfur is going to be in the stone for a while.
>
> Savra looks up at the party from where she is sitting.
>
> > "That's the first time they've sent something that heavy. Which means the petition is working."
>
> She looks at each of you in turn.
>
> > "They're scared."

> [!gamemaster]**The Review**
>
> Two days later, the Lords' formal review returns a preliminary finding: sufficient grounds to proceed. The Cassalanters are officially under investigation. Lord Victoro Cassalanter attends a charity function the following tenday and smiles at everyone who approaches him.

### The Recognition

Before the party leaves, Savra gives each member a copy of the formal recognition.

> [!readaloud]
> Before you go, Savra produces a folded document for each of you — the Halls of Justice mark, your name, your standing as a recognized Order associate. She hands them out one at a time.
>
> > "Not knights. Not sworn members. Recognized affiliates with legal standing in Waterdeep."
>
> She explains the two practical effects without being asked.
>
> > "Watch officers who see you in conflict with a Cassalanter operative will not automatically move against you. And if anything in the vault situation goes legal, I will testify on your behalf."
>
> She adds one thing as the party is moving toward the door.
>
> > "The Founders' Day timeline is tighter than we thought. Whatever you're doing with the vault — do it fast."
>
> She shakes each person's hand and holds on for a moment before she lets go.

> [!gamemaster]**Formal Recognition**
>
> Two practical effects follow the recognition:
> - Watch officers who see the party in conflict with a Cassalanter operative will not automatically intervene against them.
> - In **Vault of Dragons**, if the Halls of Justice formally recognized the party as Order associates, Watch cooperation at the Vault district is marginally better. Savra will testify on the party's behalf if any **Vault of Dragons** complication goes legal.

## Overview

The Halls of Justice, midnight. A Horned Devil dispatched by Lord Victoro Cassalanter's infernal patron has arrived with orders to destroy the ledger and kill everyone who handled it.

## Summary

A Horned Devil arrived at the Halls of Justice at midnight with orders to destroy the ledger and kill everyone who touched it. We met it in a consecrated hall alongside Savra and the two priests. When it was over, Savra gave each of us a formal recognition from the Halls of Justice and told us the Founders' Day timeline was tighter than we thought.
