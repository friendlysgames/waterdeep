# The Writ and the Weight: Overview

> [!gamemaster]**Quest Requirements**
>
> Becomes available when an individual Order of the Gauntlet member reaches Renown 13 and 7th level, after **Kolat Towers**. Seventh level requires all four lair heists, so a party that ran three heists reaches it only after **Vault of Dragons** and plays this mission then. The Vault does not require it. Companions can help with everything except Savra's brief and debrief, which only members attend. A member marked **Quietly Reassigned** gets the note from Brother Hallin Ostrel, and the mission is otherwise unchanged.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 7th-level characters.*
>
> The fight uses the 2024 **Horned Devil** (CR 11, Power 130), with Savra as a **Knight** ally and two Priests of Tyr using the **Priest** block. The devil trips the High-Power Warning, so the event carries it in the hazard block. The **Order of the Gauntlet Mechanics Reference** audits the roster for three, four and five characters, with a Standard and a Hard setting.
>
> #### Milestone Progression
> This faction mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Hook

At noon a Priest Acolyte in a plain grey surcoat hands each qualifying member a note in Savra Belabranta's left-slanting hand. A black-edged notice was nailed to the door of the Halls of Justice at dawn, and it names Savra and everyone in the party. The Lords hear her petition that night, and the notice promises a writ at midnight.

## Background

Savra filed her petition with the Lords after the Aveen Street shrine, and tonight is the hearing. The exhibit that carries it is the ledger, the Watch clerk's copy of the register or Savra's own file, depending on what the party delivered. Whoever holds the claim over the Cassalanter matter wants the exhibit burned before the Lords carry it out. It has sent a Horned Devil to serve the writ.

The Cassalanter Villa result decides who sent the devil and whom the writ names, and this is the last faction mission to read it.

> [!gamemaster]**What Is Actually True**
>
> - The devil is a Horned Devil that calls itself the bailiff. It serves a writ, wants the exhibit and never says "Asmodeus".
> - In the first three villa results, the family has no hand in the visit. In the fourth, the family did not ask for it.
> - Savra says nothing of the family's worship or bargain unless the party told her.
> - Nobody names Manshoon before **Manshoon Named** or Jarlaxle before **Jarlaxle Unmasked**.

## The Brief

Savra briefs the members in the east annex at the third bell past noon. She shows them the notice, answers five questions and tells them to bring their company to the nave by half past seven.

## Who Is Serving the Writ

A table of four rows keyed to the Cassalanter Villa result sets what the writ names and whom the bailiff serves. The last row is the default if the villa has not run.

## The Hall Before the Hearing

The party chooses where the exhibit sleeps tonight, brings the hall's wards to bear and can take a scroll of *Banishment* from the strongroom. Father Corwen Thale and Sister Merrin Oakhollow stand where the party puts them.

## The Hearing

Magistrate Odrane Belcast hears the petition from 20:00 to 23:00. The exhibit enters the record by Savra's account, a witness or the Watch clerk's stamp, and any one is enough.

## The Notice at Midnight

The bailiff arrives through the window above the west doors, reads the writ for the villa result and names Savra and every character in turn.

## The Midnight Hall

The party fights the devil in the nave with Savra and, at the smaller party sizes, both Priests. The devil returns to the Nine Hells at 0 hit points, and a party that shuts itself in the vestry survives until first light.

## Renown Opportunities

Each participating Order of the Gauntlet member gains 4 base Renown for reporting to Savra at the debrief. Bonuses of +1 each go to members when the bailiff is sent back before it reaches the exhibit, when every named character survives and when a party character lands the final blow.

Companions gain no Renown, and each bonus can be earned only once.

## Aftermath

**Order Recognized** is a gift to every character present, member or not. **Order Recognized** and **Devil Sent Back** are read by **Righteous Hand**, and **Order Recognized** is also read by **Vault of Dragons** (unconverted). This is the last Order of the Gauntlet mission.

## Involved Characters

- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet): briefs the members, fights beside the party as a Knight and gives the Recognition.
- **Father Corwen Thale** (Temple of Tyr): a gray-haired priest of Tyr who holds the vestry key and fights as a Priest.
- **Sister Merrin Oakhollow** (Temple of Tyr): a priest of Tyr who stands beside him and fights as a Priest.
- **Brother Hallin Ostrel** (Order of the Gauntlet): the Order's clerk, who brings the scroll and writes the Recognition.
- **Brother Haldric Wence** (Order of the Gauntlet): the door-warden of the Halls of Justice.
- **Odrane Belcast** (City of Waterdeep): the magistrate who presides at the hearing.
- **The bailiff** (the Nine Hells): a Horned Devil that serves the writ.

## Dangers & Enemies

One **Horned Devil** (Power 130 against 41 for each character) is the fight. It divides its attacks among at least two creatures each turn, uses *Infernal Tail* once a round at most and never attacks a creature at 0 hit points. Two **Imps** join it at five characters on Hard. The Halls' wards, the vestry and a scroll of *Banishment* at long odds are the party's answers.

## Overview

A black-edged notice promises a writ at midnight, on the night the Lords hear Savra's petition at the Halls of Justice. The party defends the exhibit in the nave beside Savra and two Priests of Tyr.
