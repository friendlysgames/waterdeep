# The Writ and the Weight

> [!gamemaster]
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Order of the Gauntlet Mission 6. Available at Renown 14 and character level 7. Follows **Cassalanter Villa** resolution.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 7th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

When the ledger left the Aveen Street shrine, Lord Victoro triggered an infernal rider built into his contract — a clause requiring his contractual counterpart to recover or destroy any removed ritual documentation and to kill everyone who physically handled it. The rider named each person who touched the ledger. The Horned Devil dispatched under that clause has been in Waterdeep for six hours before Savra summons the party, locating its targets methodically.

Savra recognized the infernal notification protocol when the filing arrived at the Halls of Justice that morning. She chose the Halls as the confrontation site because it is the most defensible ground in Waterdeep for this type of engagement. The consecrated hall has been active since the petition was filed, with theological protections in place. The ledger is in a locked cabinet in an adjacent room, and the devil knows this.

## Involved Characters
- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet) — arrives at Trollskull Manor earlier than expected, controlled with visible effort; fights at full capacity throughout; has faced infernal enforcement before and is angry in the specific way that comes from that experience
- **Horned Devil** — dispatched by Lord Victoro's infernal patron with a specific mandate: destroy the ledger and kill everyone who touched it; has been in Waterdeep for six hours before the party is summoned
- **Two Priests of Tyr** (Halls of Justice) — present with 5th-level spell access; can cast *Banishment* (DC 16 Charisma saving throw) if the party asks or the Horned Devil reaches the vestry door; will not act first

## Dangers & Enemies
One **Horned Devil** (2024 *Monster Manual*). It does not negotiate. Its mandate is specific: destroy the ledger in the adjacent locked cabinet and kill the people who touched it. The consecrated hall imposes Disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws for devils and Advantage on saving throws against infernal fear effects for characters inside. It will maneuver to reach the cabinet — the party must prevent it from disengaging.

Savra fights at full capacity alongside the party.

## Overview
The Halls of Justice, midnight. A Horned Devil dispatched by Lord Victoro Cassalanter's infernal patron has arrived with orders to destroy the ledger and kill everyone who handled it.
