# The Writ and the Weight

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> *Order of the Gauntlet Mission 6. Available at Renown 14 and character level 7. Follows **Cassalanter Villa** resolution.*
>
> #### Difficulty
> *A mission for 7th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Savra Belabranta** (Order of the Gauntlet) — arrives earlier than expected, controlled with effort; fights at full capacity throughout; has faced infernal enforcement before and is angry in the specific way that comes from that experience
- **Horned Devil** — dispatched by Lord Victoro's infernal patron with a specific mandate: destroy the ledger and kill everyone who touched it; has been in Waterdeep for six hours before the party is summoned
- **Two Priests of Tyr** (Halls of Justice) — present with 5th-level spell access; can cast *Banishment* (DC 16 Charisma) if the party asks or the Horned Devil reaches the vestry door; will not act first

## Dangers & Enemies
One **Horned Devil** (2024 *Monster Manual*). It does not negotiate. Its mandate is specific: destroy the ledger in the adjacent locked cabinet and kill the people who touched it. The consecrated hall imposes disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws for devils and advantage on saving throws against infernal fear effects for characters inside. It will maneuver to reach the cabinet — the party must prevent it from disengaging.

Savra fights at full capacity alongside the party.

## Overview
Lord Victoro Cassalanter's infernal patron dispatched a Horned Devil to Waterdeep with a specific mandate: recover or destroy the ledger, and kill everyone who handled it. Savra summoned the party to the Halls of Justice — the most defensible position in Waterdeep for this confrontation, with the relevant theological protections already in place.

The fight happens at midnight. The consecrated hall gives the party an edge. The ledger is in an adjacent room and the Horned Devil will try to reach it throughout the fight. When the devil is destroyed or banished, the Halls of Justice formally recognize the party as Order associates — recognized affiliates with legal standing in Waterdeep, with two practical effects on the campaign's final act.
