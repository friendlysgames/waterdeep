# Order of the Gauntlet: First Meeting

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event fires when the Order of the Gauntlet row activates in **The Factions Come Calling** Event — Savra Belabranta arrives at Trollskull Manor without advance notice. It can happen at any point during the renovation period while that Event is active. Eligible characters: paladins, clerics, characters who demonstrated strong moral conviction during **Finding Floon**. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Receive Savra at the door and hear her name two targets: a beholder empire beneath the city and a Sea Ward noble family the Order has been watching for months without enough evidence to move on.
> - Ask about the family and receive only as much as Savra can stand behind.
> - Ask sincerely about Savra's past and learn she was in the Howling Hatred cult.
> - Accept or decline the offer; those who accept are taken to the Halls of Justice for a brief oath ceremony.
>
> #### Preparation
>
> Savra has been watching the party since they arrived in Waterdeep. She does not recruit strangers. What she observed was enough to bring her to the door; she will not say what it was.

> [!gamemaster]**Background**
>
> Savra was a Howling Hatred cultist. She left under circumstances she does not disclose. The Order took her in afterward, and she regards that as the clearest moral fact of her life. Her familiarity with how a cult plants itself inside ordinary institutions is exactly why the Order assigned her to Waterdeep.
>
> The Sea Ward family she will not yet name is the Cassalanters. She has been watching them for months: their charity is too generous to be explained by principle, their bloodline too carefully maintained, their piety too consistent to slip even once. Watch inquiries opened against them were closed before findings were produced. Savra's years in the Howling Hatred taught her what that pattern can hide, but she does not know what, if anything, is behind it here. She has no actionable evidence. Until she has it, she will not name the family, and she names them only if pressed — and even then, she gives nothing she cannot stand behind.
>
> The two targets she names give the party a preview of where the campaign is heading. Both become heist objectives in Act III. She is not asking for commitment to a plan. She is asking whether the party is capable of caring about active evil.

### The Knock

Savra arrives without a letter sent ahead. She knocks once.

> [!readaloud]
> The knock at the door is firm and unhurried, and when you open it a woman in her late twenties is standing in the frame with the particular stillness of someone who has been waiting long enough to settle in but not long enough to lose patience. Dark hair worn back, a long coat over plain traveling clothes, one hand resting at her side with the fingers loose. She takes a moment to look at each of you in turn before she speaks.
>
> > "Savra Belabranta. Order of the Gauntlet. I'd like five minutes."
>
> She doesn't step through the doorway. She waits for an invitation to come in.

> [!social]**The Knight Who Knocks**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Neutral Good, Tethyrian human, she/her) :: a knight of the Order of the Gauntlet, atoning for a cult past through the work she has found that makes it meaningful.
>
> Savra waits to be invited in, sits when offered, and does not make small talk. She carries the measured manner of someone who has learned to think before speaking. She is in her late twenties and looks older in a way that suggests she has been through something and came out the other side. Her warmth shows through directness: she has no interest in managing around uncomfortable truths.
>
> She states the hard thing first. She pauses before every serious answer. She listens with the same attention she brings to speaking.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss before the pitch include:
> - Why she chose to come in person rather than send a note.
>
> Specific dialogue is presented below.

> [!qna]**Why come in person?**
>
> She holds the door frame a moment before she answers.
>
> > "I don't send letters to people I haven't met. Seemed worth the walk."

### The Pitch

Inside, she sits and gets to it without warmup.

> [!readaloud]
> Savra takes a seat and sets her hands flat on the table.
>
> > "I'll tell you plainly what I want from you. The Order hunts active evil — not its history, not its echoes. Two things are active in this city right now, and I don't have the right people to go after them."
>
> She describes the first without pause.
>
> > "A beholder is running an empire beneath the streets. The Watch knows it exists. The Guard knows it exists. None of them want to be the ones to name the size of the problem, because it would be embarrassing. That's our problem more than theirs."
>
> She pauses before the second.
>
> > "The other one I'm not going to name yet. A Sea Ward family. Impeccable reputation. Charity that doesn't trace to anything I can verify. Watch inquiries opened against them and closed before findings were produced. I've been watching them for months and I don't have enough to move on."
>
> She lets that sit for a moment.
>
> > "The work will take you places the city would rather pretend don't exist. Some of it won't end cleanly. I need people who can move where a knight in good standing cannot."

> [!social]**The Pitch**
>
> Savra presents both targets plainly and without embellishment. She states the cost of the work without framing it as heroism. She does not try to close the deal by telling the party how good they are. She answers questions directly and does not soften answers to make them more comfortable.
>
> She will not name the Sea Ward family at this stage. A successful **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check** draws out: "Their charity doesn't lead anywhere I can verify. Their Watch inquiries close before findings are produced. That pattern doesn't happen by accident." On a failure, she holds: "When I'm ready to name them, I will." She will not say more than that in either case.
>
> She will not discuss her sources, what she believes is behind the family's charity, or how long she has been watching them.
>
> Conversation topics Savra is willing to discuss during the pitch include:
> - What the targets are and what the work involves.
> - Why she needs the party rather than her own people.
> - The cost of the work and what it will require.
> - The beholder guild, on which she has specific and current tactical knowledge.
>
> Specific dialogue for Savra on these topics is presented below.

> [!qna]**Why us specifically?**
>
> > "I look for people who hold a line when it costs them something. You've been in Waterdeep long enough to have shown me what I needed to see."

> [!qna]**Who is this Sea Ward family?**
>
> > "A Sea Ward family. Leave it there for now."
>
> A successful **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check** draws out more: "Their charity doesn't lead anywhere I can verify. Their Watch inquiries close before they produce findings. That pattern doesn't happen by accident." On a failure, she holds: "When I'm ready to name them, I will."

> [!qna]**What do you know about the beholder operation?**
>
> She leans forward slightly.
>
> > "More than most. I can tell you the shape of their street presence, which districts they work, how their enforcers operate, and what resources they've assembled below ground. That knowledge stays current. I keep it current."

> [!qna]**What if we push back on the targets?**
>
> > "On approach and timing, I'll hear you out. On what the targets are — no."

### The Darker Exchange

Any character who asks why Savra joined the Order, and asks as though they actually want to know, gets a direct answer.

> [!qna]**Why did you join the Order?**
>
> She pauses before she answers.
>
> > "I was in a cult. The Howling Hatred." She doesn't qualify it and doesn't explain what led her there. "I left. The Order took me in after. That's the shape of it."
>
> She says nothing more about what she did inside the cult. She is watching what the party does with what she has just told them. Characters who treat it as ordinary context about a person and move on earn a single measured nod — her tell for when someone has said or done something true. Characters who treat it as leverage lose the nod and have her attention in a different way, though she will not name what she noticed.
>
> Pressing her on what she did in the cult ends the conversation. She thanks them for their time, collects herself without visible emotion, and leaves. The offer remains open; she will work with the party if they seek her out at the Halls of Justice.

### The Decision

Savra does not apply pressure. Once the pitch is done, she waits.

If the party declines, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Savra stands and straightens her coat with one smooth tug at the lapels, then holds out a hand across the table.
>
> > "Understood. The offer stays open."
>
> She shows herself out the same way she came in, and the door closes behind her with very little sound.

If members of the party accept, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
> Savra holds still for a moment after the last person speaks, as if she had expected to wait longer than this. Then she nods once.
>
> > "Good. Let's finish it at the Halls of Justice."

### The Oath

The Halls of Justice are a short walk into the Temple District.

> [!readaloud]
> A priest of Tyr is waiting in an antechamber off the main nave, a narrow stone room with a single lantern hung from a chain and a wooden bench worn smooth from years of people sitting on it to wait. The ceremony is brief and without performance: a silver gauntlet passes from hand to hand among those who are joining, and each one holds it for the length of the oath — to act against active evil, to protect the innocent where possible, to hold one another accountable. The priest adds a formal acknowledgment at the end and says nothing else.
>
> Savra has been standing in the doorway with her arms at her sides through all of it, watching. When the gauntlet completes its round, she speaks.
>
> > *"The work does not wait."*

She means it as information — there is a Zhentarim-funded confrontation developing in the Field Ward, and she has been the only Order member in Waterdeep for longer than she would like.

### Concluding the Event

Any character who took the oath walks out of the Halls of Justice as a member of the Order of the Gauntlet at Renown 1.

## Overview

Savra Belabranta arrived at Trollskull Manor without warning and laid out the Order of the Gauntlet's priorities: a beholder empire beneath the city and a Sea Ward noble family the Order has been watching for months without enough evidence to move on. She named her own past when sincerely asked and said nothing more about what was in it. Those who accepted were taken to the Halls of Justice to take the oath.

## Summary

Savra Belabranta knocked at Trollskull Manor and asked for five minutes. She named two targets — a beholder empire beneath the city and a Sea Ward noble family whose charity the Order has been watching for months without result — and said plainly what the work would cost. When we asked about her past, she said she had been in the Howling Hatred cult, gave the name, and nothing more. Those of us who accepted were taken to the Halls of Justice, where a silver gauntlet passed among us and a priest of Tyr witnessed the oath. Savra said the work does not wait and walked out ahead of us.
