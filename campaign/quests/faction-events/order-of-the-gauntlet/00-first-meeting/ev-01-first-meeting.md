# Order of the Gauntlet First Meeting

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs during **The Factions Come Calling**, when the Order of the Gauntlet row fires. Savra Belabranta knocks once at Trollskull Manor and waits to be asked in. It begins when she knocks and ends when the last candidate has given an answer and she leaves the manor. A candidate who asked for time can still swear at the Halls of Justice later. In this Event, the characters can:
>
> - Let Savra in, or find her note if nobody is at the manor, and learn that the Order meets candidates at the Halls of Justice.
> - Ask why the Order wants them, what it does, what it costs and whether they can keep other loyalties.
> - Hear about the beholder under the city and the family in the Sea Ward whom she will not name.
> - Ask her sincerely about the Howling Hatred and learn that she was in it for three years.
> - Decide individually whether to join, and swear the oath at the Halls of Justice on any day they choose.
> - Become a Chevall at Renown 1 if they swear, and share the Order's help with the renovation of Trollskull Manor.
>
> #### Candidates and Companions
>
> Every character who belongs to no other faction is a candidate, and each candidate answers for themselves. Savra speaks first to a candidate with a Cleric or Paladin class or an Acolyte, Guard or Soldier background. If none qualifies, she speaks first to a worshipper of Helm, Torm or Tyr. After that she picks the candidate with the highest Wisdom score, and a tie goes to the name that comes first alphabetically. The note in **The Factions Come Calling** about paladins and clerics only decides whom she addresses first. Nobody is tested for class, alignment or faith at the door.
>
> Savra makes no offer to a character who already belongs to another faction, and she tells that character so. Those characters and any companions can sit and listen, and they gain nothing from it. If no character qualifies, the Event does not occur. If every candidate declines, end the Event with no membership outcome.
>
> #### What Is Actually True
>
> - Savra chose these candidates herself, from what Renaer Neverember and the rescue of Floon told her. The Order has no one else to send.
> - The family she will not name is the Cassalanters. She suspects them, she cannot prove anything, and she does not know what they worship, and she never names them in this Event.
> - The Howling Hatred was real, and Savra spent three years in it. She left around the time the Watch released two of its members, and the Order took her in afterward. What she did inside is hers, and she says so.
> - The Waterdeep chapter is Savra and a clerk's office in the east annex of the Halls of Justice. A rotation of about a dozen Knights and Priests answers to her. The priests of the Triad help without being members. Savra is not the only Order member in the city, and she does not say she is.
> - The Halls of Justice, the temple of Tyr, stands in the Castle Ward west of the Market. The Order's clerk's office keeps its seal there.
> - The oath asks three questions. A candidate who says plainly that they do not mean to oppose evil is not sworn, and the Event decides nothing else about an honest answer.
> - Nobody in this Event names the master of the Splinter, Jarlaxle's fleet or the Cassalanters' faith. Until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener, Savra says "the Splinter" or "the other cell".

### The Knock

> [!gamemaster]**Savra at the Door**
>
> On the morning the Order of the Gauntlet row fires, Savra knocks once at the front door of Trollskull Manor at the third bell and waits to be asked in. She does not push past the threshold and she does not knock twice. Every later Order event that brings her to the manor works the same way. Each of them refers back to this description.
>
> When nobody is at the manor, she leaves a note with Tally Fellbranch, who works on the door frames. Tally hands it to the first resident he sees. The note is one short paragraph, written in a left-slanting hand on paper with the Halls of Justice seal, folded once and signed "S.B." Read the following aloud when someone answers the door.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A woman in her late twenties stands on the step in a dark riding coat, with her hair pulled back from a strong jaw. A small silver gauntlet pin is fixed at her collar, and she keeps both hands visible at her sides. She waits until the door is fully open before she speaks.
>
> > "Savra Belabranta, Order of the Gauntlet. I'd like five minutes of your time, and I'll wait on this step until you ask me in."

If nobody is at the manor, read the note aloud to the first candidate who finds it:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The note is folded once and sealed with a plain disc of grey wax pressed with a gauntlet, and the short lines of writing inside slant to the left. It reads:
>
> > Savra Belabranta of the Order of the Gauntlet called and found no one at home. I would like to speak with the residents of Trollskull Manor about work that needs doing. Come to the Halls of Justice in the Castle Ward and ask the door-warden for me. I will see you at the third bell past noon on any day for the next tenday. S.B.

> [!qna]**Why come in person?**
>
> Savra keeps her hands where they are and does not move her feet.
>
> > "Because I'd want to see the face of anyone who asked me to risk my life. I'll tell you plainly what I want, and you can say no."

> [!qna]**Who sent you?**
>
> > "Nobody sent me, because Renaer Neverember speaks well of you, and so does what happened to Floon. That is enough for me to knock on a door."

If the candidates leave her on the step, she repeats the offer once and then waits without comment. Once she is invited in, she sits on the first chair offered and keeps her coat on.

### The Pitch

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra sits straight in the chair with her hands folded on her knee and looks at each of you in turn before she begins.
>
> > "The Order hunts active evil, the kind that is happening now. I'm the one it sent to Waterdeep, and I can't do the work alone. I'll tell you plainly what the work is."

> [!social]**The Knight of the Gauntlet**
>
> Savra Belabranta (Lawful Good, Human, she/her) :: a knight in her late twenties who looks older. She wears her dark hair back, has a strong jaw and speaks in a measured way.
>
> Savra is Friendly but guarded, and she pauses before every serious answer. She gives the hard fact first and the reasoning after it, and she softens nothing. Her warmth shows in what she does, and she gives a single measured nod to an honest answer. She calls Tyr "the Maimed God" and swears rarely, in short bursts aimed at wrongdoing.
>
> Savra is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - Why she picked this party, and what the Order is.
> - What the work involves and what it costs a member.
> - The beholder who is said to command the Guild beneath the city.
> - The Howling Hatred, if a character asks sincerely.
> - Whether a character can keep another loyalty.
>
> She will not discuss the family in the Sea Ward by name, what she did inside the Howling Hatred, the Stone of Golorr or the vault.

> [!qna]**Why us?**
>
> > "You went into a warehouse after a missing man and came out with him alive. You didn't ask what it would pay first. I need people like that, and I haven't got many."

> [!qna]**What does the Order do?**
>
> > "We find evil, we pursue it and we destroy it. In this city that means cults and the powers they serve. We also protect people who can't protect themselves, and that is most of the job."

> [!qna]**What does it cost?**
>
> Savra takes a moment to look at each of you before she answers.
>
> > "Some of the work happens where the city would rather not look, and some of it won't end cleanly. You'll be asked to give a quarter of what you earn on big jobs, and I won't dress that up."

> [!qna]**Who is the family?**
>
> > "A family in the Sea Ward, and I'll leave it there for now. Their charity can't be traced to any cause I can verify, and the Watch closes every complaint against them. I can't prove it, and it pisses me off."

A character who makes a successful **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** check also hears Savra add, "I've been building a case for months. When I have enough to take to the Lords, you'll hear the name." On a failure she holds to the answer above, and nothing else depends on the result.

> [!qna]**What about the beholder?**
>
> > "A beholder is said to run the Guild that works the sewers and the docks. Its agents have grown so numerous that no authority wants to count them. If you ask about its people, I'll tell you what I've seen."

> [!qna]**Can we keep our other loyalties?**
>
> > "No, because the Order doesn't share members, and I make no offer to anyone who wears another faction's colors. You are welcome to sit and listen anyway."

> [!qna]**I'm already in a faction. Can I swear?**
>
> Savra turns to the character who asked and answers without hurry.
>
> > "Not while you wear their colors, and I'm sorry for it. If that changes, come to the Halls and ask for me, and I'll hear you then."

> [!exploration]**What She Leaves Unsaid**
>
> Any character who watches Savra while she speaks of the family and makes a successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** check sees her jaw tighten at the word "charity". She is holding back a name. A character who fails sees a disciplined knight giving a plain answer, and nothing else depends on the result.

> [!gamemaster]**The Stone, the Vault and the Splinter**
>
> Savra does not discuss the Stone of Golorr or the vault. If a player asks about either, she gives this answer:
>
> > "I don't know enough to tell you, and I won't guess. Ask me about the Order, or about what I've seen."
>
> If a player raises Manshoon, she does not confirm it. She says "the Splinter" or "the other cell" and asks who told them. Davil's Doom Raiders and the Splinter are different, and she keeps them apart if a player mixes them up. Nobody in the scene names Kolat Towers.

### The Darker Exchange

> [!gamemaster]**Ask Sincerely**
>
> Any character who asks why Savra joined the Order, and who takes the time to listen to the answer, gets this reply. A character who asks as a challenge or a probe gets "That is my business." and nothing more. She is watching what the party does with the answer, and she does not say so.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra looks at the floor between her boots for a moment and then raises her eyes again, and her voice stays level.
>
> > "I was in a cult called the Howling Hatred for three years. I left about three years ago, and the Order took me in afterward, and that is the shape of it."

> [!qna]**What did you do in it?**
>
> > "I'll answer anything else you ask me, but I won't answer that one, because it's mine."

The question ends the talk if it is pressed a second time. Savra thanks the candidates for their time, stands and leaves. She returns the following morning at the third bell and gives the party one more chance. If a character presses the same question again, she does not return, and the party can still reach her at the Halls of Justice.

> [!exploration]**The Nod**
>
> Any character who treats her answer as context about a person and moves on earns a single measured nod. A character who treats it as leverage gets her full attention and no nod. No check is needed, and she does not say what she noticed. The nod tells a player that the answer was honest, and a character who earns it early will notice when it stops coming.

### Each Candidate's Answer

> [!gamemaster]**Recording the Answers**
>
> When the questions run out, Savra asks each candidate in order. The three questions are the ones the oath puts. "Will you find evil? Will you pursue it? Will you stand with those who can't stand for themselves?" Then she says "Will you swear it?" Record each answer separately, and mixed answers cause no argument with Savra.
>
> A candidate who accepts takes the oath at the Halls of Justice, as **The Oath** describes. The oath can be sworn on the same day or on any later day. The candidate becomes an Order of the Gauntlet member at **Renown 1** with the **Chevall** rank when it is sworn. A candidate who declines gets no argument, and the offer stays open. A candidate who asks for time can swear on any later day at the Halls.
>
> Mark **Order of the Gauntlet Joined** with the character's name when the oath is sworn. There is no closing outcome for a refusal, because the Order wants recruits.

If a candidate accepts, read or paraphrase the following, using their name:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra nods once and stands, and she crosses the room to the door with her hands empty. There she turns to face the candidate who accepted before she speaks.
>
> > "Good. Come to the Halls of Justice in the Castle Ward and ask the door-warden for me. You can swear today, or on any day you choose."

If a candidate declines, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra listens to the answer without any change in her face and then folds her hands again.
>
> > "I understand, and the offer stands, and I'll answer if you come to the Halls. Thank you for hearing me out."

If a candidate asks for time, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra nods and looks at the candidate for a moment before she answers.
>
> > "Take it, and nobody will hurry you. The Halls are open every day, so ask for me at the door when you are ready."

> [!gamemaster]**Chevall Benefits**
>
> Each member who swears gains the following, and nothing goes to a decliner or a companion. The benefits belong to the member and are tracked for each member separately. The three temples are the Halls of Justice, which is the temple of Tyr, the Temple of Helm in the Southern Ward, and the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield, a Torm chapel in the Dock Ward. The door-warden at the Halls of Justice is Brother Haldric Wence, a lay brother who holds messages "for Savra Belabranta" and keeps the member's name on a list.
>
> - **Renown 1:** the member starts as a Chevall, and Savra greets the member by name from then on.
> - **Healing and a bed:** see **The Temple Healing** below.
> - **The good word:** see **A Good Word** below.
> - **The dead:** see **The Channel to the Dead** below.
> - **Loss rule:** a member whose Renown later falls below 1 keeps the rank, and the benefits are suspended until the Renown is restored.
> - **Companions:** a companion can walk into a temple beside a member and gains nothing else.

> [!exploration]**The Temple Healing**
>
> Any temple of Tyr, Helm or Torm in Waterdeep treats a member as a friend of the Order. A priest casts *Cure Wounds* on the member and gives the member a bed for the night.
>
> - **Contact:** the member gives their name at the front desk of the temple and says that Savra Belabranta vouches for them. The door-warden is the contact at the Halls of Justice.
> - **Limit:** one casting of *Cure Wounds* each day at each of the three temples. A member can have up to three castings in one day by walking between them. The bed is for one night at a time, in a shared dormitory, for up to three nights in a row.
> - **Cost:** the healing and the bed are free. A priest casts nothing stronger without payment, and other services cost the usual temple rate.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who brings a fight into a temple, or shows contempt for the faith, is turned away there for a tenday.

> [!exploration]**A Good Word**
>
> A member who is arrested by the Watch can have Savra put in a good word and tend the member's affairs while the member is held.
>
> - **Contact:** the member, or a friend, leaves the member's name with Brother Haldric at the Halls of Justice. He carries the message to Savra within the hour.
> - **What she does:** Savra speaks to the Watch officer who holds the member. The Order's clerk pays the member's rent, feeds any animals and keeps the member's belongings safe until the member is released.
> - **Limit:** it applies to charges up to brawling, trespass, carrying an unlicensed weapon and breach of the peace. It does not help with a charge of theft, killing or harm to an officer. A member can use it once each tenday.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who lies to Savra about the charge loses the good word for a tenday.

> [!exploration]**The Channel to the Dead**
>
> The Order arranges a cleric through its temple network when a member wants *Speak with Dead* cast on a body the Watch holds, and **Fireball!** points here at Renown 1.
>
> - **Contact:** the member asks Brother Haldric at the Halls of Justice. He names a priest of Tyr who will cast it at the Watch station or the mortuary.
> - **Delay:** the casting is arranged within a day, and the body must still be available.
> - **Limit:** one casting for each body, and the member brings the questions in advance. A priest of Tyr refuses to question a body for gossip or for the member's own gain.
> - **Loss rule:** a member who lies to the priest about the questions loses the channel for a tenday.

### The Oath

> [!gamemaster]**The Halls of Justice**
>
> The oath is sworn at the Halls of Justice, the temple of Tyr in the Castle Ward west of the Market. It is open every day from the second bell of morning to dusk. Father Corwen Thale, a priest of Tyr, receives each member in an antechamber off the main nave with a stone floor, a lantern and a bench worn smooth. Savra is present when a member swears, whether it is the same day or a later one. Several members who swear together stand in the antechamber together. Read the following aloud when the first member arrives.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A priest in a plain grey surcoat leads you through the nave and into a small antechamber with a stone floor and one lantern. A bench worn smooth stands against the wall. Savra waits beside it, and on the bench lies a silver gauntlet.
>
> > "This is Father Corwen Thale," Savra says. "He will seal the oath for you, so take your time with it."

> [!social]**The Priest of Tyr**
>
> Father Corwen Thale (Lawful Good, Human, he/him) :: a gray-haired priest of Tyr with a calm, gravelly voice and a missing left hand. He has presided over the Order's oaths for twenty years.
>
> Father Corwen is Friendly and patient. He answers in short, plain sentences and says "the Just God" whenever he speaks of Tyr. He swears rarely, and only mildly.
>
> Father Corwen is happy to discuss the following topics:
>
> - What the oath asks of a member.
> - What the silver gauntlet means.
> - What the Order does in Waterdeep, as far as he knows it.
>
> He will not discuss Savra's past or any case the Order is working.

> [!qna]**What does the gauntlet mean?**
>
> > "It is the hand that strikes and the hand that holds, and the Just God asks both of you. Wear it when you swear, and then give it back."

> [!qna]**What if I break the oath?**
>
> > "Then you answer to your conscience and to Savra, in that order. The Order does not hunt its own, but it does expect you to say so when you fail."

> [!readaloud]
>
> The priest sets the silver gauntlet in your open hands and lays his own over them. Savra stands with her back to the door, and the lantern light shifts on the metal.
>
> > "Say it after me," Father Corwen says. "I will find evil, I will pursue it, and I will destroy it."
>
> You repeat the words and he nods, then goes on in the same level voice.
>
> > "I will protect those who cannot protect themselves. I will stand when standing is required."

> [!gamemaster]**Sealing the Oath**
>
> Father Corwen asks the three questions again, and a member who answers honestly is sworn. A candidate who says plainly that they do not mean to oppose evil is not sworn and may try again after a tenday. Nothing else depends on the answer, and the check ends once the member has replied. Read the following aloud when a member is sworn.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Father Corwen raises two fingers and speaks a short invocation to Tyr, and the sound carries to the far end of the nave. Then he takes the gauntlet and hands you a plain silver pin shaped like one.
>
> > "It is sealed," Savra says. "Pin it where you like, and welcome to the Order, Name."

The pin is a token of the Chevall rank and has no mechanical effect. No coin changes hands and nothing is signed. Mark **Order of the Gauntlet Joined** with the member's name.

### The Guild Hall

> [!gamemaster]**The Renovation Help**
>
> This help belongs to the party, because Trollskull Manor is a shared holding, and it is given once, when the first member swears, so later oaths do not repeat it. Savra offers it as she walks out of the Halls, and it costs the party nothing.
>
> - **The walk:** within a tenday of the first oath, Savra walks the party to one Guild hall, wearing the Order's insignia. A Guild clerk looks at the insignia, stamps the permit and closes the queue within a day.
> - **The saving:** the renovation total of Trollskull Manor drops by **100 gp**, and the saving is applied once. The figure matches the one in **The Factions Come Calling**.
> - **The watch-list:** a Watch patrol answers any disturbance at the manor within the hour. This is passive and needs no scene, and any Watch citation against the manor ends it for a tenday.
> - **Later changes:** the help stays with the party if a member later leaves the Order or loses Renown.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra pushes open the door of the Guild hall and walks to the head of a queue of tradesfolk without slowing. The clerk behind the desk glances at her pin, then at the permit in her hand, and reaches for his stamp.
>
> > "I'll take that one right now," he says. "Anything for the Order goes to the front of the line."

### Leaving

> [!gamemaster]**Walking Out**
>
> Savra hands out no assignment at the oath. **Breaking Up the Brawl** reaches each new member at Trollskull Manor the next morning at the third bell, once the member is 2nd level. Read the following aloud when at least one candidate has sworn.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra walks with you to the door of the Halls and stops on the top step, and in the street below a cart rolls past while a pair of Watchmen turn the corner.
>
> > "Come to the Halls whenever you need healing, or when you want to ask me something. There's trouble in the Field Ward that I'll tell you about tomorrow, because the work does not wait."

If no candidate swore, read the following instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Savra stands and nods to each of you, then goes out through the door into the alley, where a cart is rolling past the corner.
>
> > "The offer stands, and you know where the Halls are."

### Late Candidates

> [!gamemaster]**A Character Who Is Free Later**
>
> A character who was in another faction when this Event ran, and has since left it, is a candidate. A character who declined, or who asked for time, can also swear at any time. Each meets Savra at the Halls of Justice within a tenday of asking.
>
> - **Where:** Savra meets the candidate at the Halls of Justice at the third bell past noon. The scene runs in a shorter form with the same Pitch and the same oath, and the renovation help is not repeated.
> - **Who is excluded:** nobody is excluded, because the Order has no closed offer.

### Concluding the Event

The meeting ends when the last candidate has answered and Savra has left, and no assignment is handed over until the next morning.

## Overview

Savra Belabranta knocks once at Trollskull Manor and offers eligible characters a place in the Order of the Gauntlet. Those who accept swear the oath at the Halls of Justice.

## Summary

### After the Meeting

Savra Belabranta of the Order of the Gauntlet knocked at Trollskull Manor and waited to be asked in. She told us the Order hunts active evil and named a beholder under the city. She also mentioned a family in the Sea Ward she cannot yet prove anything against, and she said what the work costs. We asked about her past and she told us she spent three years in the Howling Hatred, though not what she did there. Those of us who accepted swore the oath at the Halls of Justice with a silver gauntlet in our hands and Father Corwen Thale to seal it.

### Without a Private Meeting

We carried on with the renovation at Trollskull Manor without opening the door to Savra or changing any of our faction affiliations.
