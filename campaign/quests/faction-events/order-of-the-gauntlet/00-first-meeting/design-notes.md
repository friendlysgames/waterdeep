# Design Notes: Order of the Gauntlet First Meeting

## Somebody Answers

The earlier draft ended on the oath, with Savra taking "members who accept" to the Halls of Justice, and then wrote one party-level outcome. Nobody was asked a question, the Chevall rank was never given, and the renovation help in **The Factions Come Calling** never arrived. The event now follows the Harpers, Force Grey, Emerald Enclave, Doom Raiders and Lords' Alliance models. Every character in no other faction is a candidate and answers for themselves, and **Order of the Gauntlet Joined** is marked with each name when the oath is sworn. The oath is a separate scene that can be sworn on any day, so a candidate who asks for time is never lost. Appendix B and Dragon Heist both give the oath as find evil, pursue it, destroy it, protect those who cannot protect themselves and stand when standing is required, and the page uses that wording.

## An Open Door

The earlier draft had Savra return after **Fireball!** or **Gralhund Villa** for a second approach. Those quests give her hooks to members only, so a non-member would never meet her again. The offer now stays open at the Halls, and there is no closing outcome, because the Order wants recruits. A character who leaves another faction meets Savra at the Halls within a tenday. The Savra contact object is written once here: she knocks once, waits to be asked in, and leaves a note signed "S.B." with Tally Fellbranch if nobody is home. Later events refer back to it.

## The Chapter and the Cult

The earlier draft called Savra "the only Order member in Waterdeep", while the rank events field a chapter and a council. The page now defines the chapter once: Savra, a clerk's office in the east annex, a rotation of about a dozen Knights and Priests, and the priests of the Triad who help without being members. Her cult tenure is three years, and she left about three years ago. The Chevall benefits are written as procedures with a temple, a contact, a limit and a loss rule. The renovation help follows the manor guide's non-monetary version and keeps the 100 gp saving from **The Factions Come Calling**.

## Departures

The earlier draft named Nihiloor and intellect devourers to a party that may never have seen them, and called the brawl Zhentarim-funded. The beholder is now "said to run the Guild", Nihiloor is not named and the brawl is left to **Breaking Up the Brawl**. "Non-evil" from Dragon Heist survives as a GM line, not a test. The Halls of Justice stands in the Castle Ward west of the Market, following Dragon Heist and Appendix B, and not the Temple District or the Sea Ward.

## Invented Names and Open Items

- Father Corwen Thale (priest of Tyr, seals the oath), Brother Haldric Wence (door-warden of the Halls of Justice) and the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield (a Torm chapel in the Dock Ward). Each was grep-checked against `campaign/` and has no collision.
- Savra's alignment follows the brief (Lawful Good). Her Notable Figures page says neutral good, and should be fixed by the companion pass.
- `ev-04-the-factions-come-calling.md:28` still gives paladins and clerics as the eligibility and `:106` still writes a True or False heading for Joined. The Trollskull manor guide still lists a different Order renovation offer at `02-operating-costs.md:83`.
- The Dragon Heist Major Temples table puts the Halls of Justice in the Castle Ward, which is the placement used here. The retired `waterdeep-lore.md:36` once listed Tyr among the Sea Ward temples, and that tension is resolved.
- The Howling Hatred's rank names are unverified and are not used on this page.
