### Approach — The Mechanical Sparrow Trap

The House of Inspired Hands occupies the corner of Seawatch Street and Shark Street in the Sea Ward. The toothed cog of Gond appears on every surface — doorknobs, gutter spouts, collar clasps, the wrought iron gate. The main hall is visible through the open double doors.

As the party approaches within 60 feet, a figure is visible on the rooftop parapet — slight, humanoid, absolutely still. It releases a mechanical sparrow.

**Mechanical Sparrow:** Initiative count 10. +0 to hit, 1d3 piercing damage. AC 14, 1 hit point. Can be intercepted with a readied action. It shatters on impact regardless of result.

The moment the sparrow is released, Nim disappears through a rooftop hatch and retreats to the attic. It is hoping to avoid the consequences of its miscalculation. A party that did not attack the sparrow, or that tries to signal peaceful intent before Valetta appears, may find Nim slightly less defensive.

### Valetta

The temple's senior cleric. A tall bronze dragonborn with amber-yellow eyes; machine oil smell of a woman who considers it a kind of perfume; deliberate, compact movements; the toothed cog worn as a collar clasp. Patient, methodical, protective of the temple's reputation. She was in the main hall when the sparrow was released and meets the party at the entrance.

She identifies Nim from any physical description — she built the sparrow trap years ago, she says; Nim has a gift for making things that do what she tells them. She escorts the party up the spiral staircase to the attic level without ceremony.

She finds the attic door locked with a mechanism she does not recognize — new since the last time she was up here.

**Attic Door — Three Bypass Methods:**
- **DC 20 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools):** The lock mechanism is Nim's own design. Higher than standard difficulty.
- **Knock spell:** Automatic success.
- **DC 17 Charisma (Persuasion) through the door** with Valetta's firm encouragement (Advantage): Nim opens it. Valetta's voice carries authority in this building.

### Nim's Attic

Packed floor to rafter with half-finished contraptions — copper tubing, gear-trains on wire racks, a dozen projects at various stages. Nim is in the corner: the size of a small child, crouching, watching the party with expressionless brass eyes.

Nim understands Common but cannot speak. It communicates in **Common Sign Language.**

**Standard communication:** Valetta translates. She misses nuance and has to ask Nim to repeat. The translation is accurate in content, compressed in emotion. Through Valetta, Nim conveys: it built a second nimblewright three months ago to ease its loneliness. The creation had an incomplete behavioral lattice — it panicked and fled the temple grounds approximately one month ago. Nim has a detector device it built to find it and never had the opportunity to use.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Common Sign Language — Player Moment
>
> Any character who selected Common Sign Language at character creation communicates with Nim directly, without Valetta's intermediary. Nim responds with visible relief: it signs faster, more precisely. It shares emotional texture that it would not bother conveying through partial translation — the loneliness that drove it to build a companion, the guilt about the behavioral lattice it knew was incomplete, the pride in the detector's design that it cannot quite suppress even now. Give the player a moment. Nim has been waiting a long time to have a real conversation.
>
> The information is the same either way. The experience of receiving it is not.

**What Nim Communicates:**
- Built the runaway three months ago. Motive: loneliness. The temple keeps Nim occupied but not accompanied.
- The runaway's behavioral lattice was incomplete when it fled — Nim had not finished its ethical framework. It does not know what the runaway might do.
- It built the detector to find it. It never used it.
- It wants the runaway found. It does not want it destroyed. It does not say this directly in Valetta's translation; a CSL speaker will know.

### The Nimblewright Detector

A 1-foot-long copper contraption with an umbrella-like metallic protrusion. *Wondrous Item, Uncommon (requires attunement). Magic Action to activate. While active, spins and clicks within 500 feet of any nimblewright other than Nim. Speed and volume increase with proximity; maximum intensity within 30 feet.*

Nim hands it over. It does not require persuasion; the party is already the best path to finding what it built and cannot find alone.

### Valetta's Reward

500 gp for proof of the escaped nimblewright's destruction, plus one invention of the party's choosing from the temple's collection. Valetta names the options:

| Invention | Mechanics |
|---|---|
| Adjustable stilts | Extend or retract as a bonus action; add 2 feet to reach; unstable terrain requires DC 10 Athletics or fall |
| Backpack parachute | Reaction to deploy while falling; prevents all falling damage; requires 10 minutes to repack |
| Barking box | Detects vibrations within 15 feet; activates for 8 hours per winding |
| Matchless pipe | Produces its own flame; lights itself with a command word; decorative |

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Nim's Preference
>
> If the party is willing to return the runaway intact rather than destroy it, Valetta cannot negotiate the reward on Nim's behalf — she does not have temple authority to offer an alternative. But Nim watches the party's response to the reward offer closely. If they ask whether destruction is necessary, or express reluctance to destroy something that didn't choose to run, Nim signs to Valetta: *the reward still stands, but tell them it would rather see it returned.*
>
> Valetta translates this accurately. A CSL speaker already knows.

### Three Independent Paths to the House of Inspired Hands (Recap)

From ev-02 — all three are equivalent:

1. **Rishaal's text.** Lantanese engineering book directly names the House of Inspired Hands.
2. **Faction intelligence.** Any faction contact can identify "nimblewright" from witness descriptions and direct the party to the Gond temple. Harpers fastest (Remallia knows the high priest personally).
3. **Watch cooperation.** Barnibus's Watchful Order contacts can identify the construct type within 48 hours of the blast (by Ches 24th). Slower than the other two paths.

## Overview

The party visited Gond's temple in the Sea Ward, where the resident nimblewright Nim built the runaway three months ago. Nim gave them a detector.

## Summary

The House of Inspired Hands identified the runaway nimblewright as Nim's creation — built to ease its loneliness, fled with an incomplete behavioral lattice one month ago. Nim provided a detector device and the temple's senior cleric offered 500 gp for proof of its destruction. The elimination sweep could now begin.
