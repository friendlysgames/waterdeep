# Kalain's Tower

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Optional Exploration/Social Event covers the visit to Kalain's tower near the harbor. In this Event:
>
> - Kalain is paranoid and fragmented — she believes every visitor is Neverember's assassin.
> - Two Persuasion checks gate the conversation's depth; a failed first check animates hostile paintings.
> - Kalain's small paintings animate as fetch-helpers; her monster paintings animate against intruders.
> - She reveals Victoro Cassalanter's recent visit — the first concrete Cassalanter-to-vault link.
> - Lord Dagult's lockbox contains four items that together constitute some of the most valuable intelligence in the campaign.
>
> **Trigger:** The party has Kalain's letter (Kalain's Letter: Received from ev-06) and chooses to follow the address, OR the party visits the harbor district and hears about a reclusive painter who knew Neverember from an NPC referral.

### Kalain's Tower

A dilapidated tower near the harbor — once notable, now overlooked. Kalain is a half-elf painter, once celebrated in Waterdavian artistic circles, now alone. Former companion of Lord Dagult Neverember. She dismissed her household staff years ago and has not hired replacements. The tower smells of linseed oil and something older.

### Arrival and Initial Contact

**Roleplaying Kalain:** She believes everyone who visits is secretly Dagult's assassin. She loses conversational threads mid-sentence and resurfaces on something different. She rubs her cheek with increasing agitation as she grows distressed. She is not dangerous by default — only if the party handles her badly.

Her paintings are alive in two senses. The smaller, older paintings — sleeping cats, bowls of fruit, quiet landscapes — animate figures as fetch-helpers for minor errands. She calls them into service without comment, as if this is simply what paintings are for. The newer paintings, stacked against the walls and turned mostly face-inward, are all monsters. They all wear the same face.

**DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) on arrival:** Kalain accepts that the party is not Dagult's agents. She settles slightly — still fragmented, but no longer at the edge. Conversation is possible.

**On a failure:** She calls one of the monster paintings to defend her. Medium animated figure; use Animated Armor stat block with 40 hit points and no Antimagic Susceptibility. She can be talked down from the combat with DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) at the start of each of her turns; success causes the painting to become still and Kalain to apologize in a disconnected way.

### Conversation with Kalain

Once settled, she speaks in fragments that require patience to assemble. She does not speak in order. Some things she says are about Neverember; some are about other things entirely; some are both.

What she knows — and will eventually share, in pieces, without prompting:

**Dalakhar worked for Neverember.** She says so openly. She adds that she is glad he is dead, meaning Neverember, not Dalakhar. It takes a moment to establish which dead man she is talking about.

**The Melairkyn ceremonial vault.** Neverember was obsessed in their final months together with something he called a "Melairkyn ceremonial vault." She cannot recall the specifics — the memory feels burned away, like a word she cannot retrieve. This is the *Stone of Golorr*'s influence, still active in anyone who has been near it. The suppression is pervasive and consistent. She cannot be prompted through it; the memory is genuinely inaccessible.

**Victoro Cassalanter's visit.** She will not volunteer this. If the party asks whether anyone else has come asking about Neverember's projects, she confirms: a man named Victoro Cassalanter visited a few weeks ago. He was asking many of the same questions Dalakhar had asked years before — the vault, Neverember's last projects, whether she kept his papers. He was polite. She found his politeness the most unsettling thing about the visit.

The lockbox is there. **DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** to open it; Kalain has the key and hands it over if asked and if the DC 14 check succeeded earlier.

### Lord Dagult's Lockbox

The lockbox contains correspondence Kalain kept without fully understanding why. Four documents:

**1. Neverember's crossed-out task list.** A page of notes in a cramped, efficient hand, most items struck through. One entry near the bottom is unfinished: *"move the dragon to the Melairkyn ceremonial vault."* The entry was never struck out. Pays off in Vault of Dragons: the dragon is Aurinax.

**2. Kraddoc correspondence.** Letters to Hammond Kraddoc of the Vintners', Distillers', and Brewers' Guild confirming large bribes paid to cover a contaminated-liquor scandal in the Dock Ward. Neverember's private leverage over the Guild. Not directly relevant to the Stone but potentially useful for a party building political assets.

**3. Melairkyn notes.** Research notes on the Melairkyn dwarves and their cult of Dumathoin. Most are fragments — partial citations, library call numbers, observations about the Vault of Dragons as a Dumathoin ceremonial site. One section is complete and explicit: *"A ceremonial vault can be opened by standing before Dumathoin's doors and striking the scale of a dragon with a mithral hammer in the place where the sun's light should fall."* The vault-opening method, written in Neverember's hand. Pays off in Vault of Dragons.

**4. The unsigned Eyes letter.** Dated four years ago, in a different hand: *"The last of the three Eyes has been secured."* No signature. No context. The implication: all three Eyes were in Neverember's hands four years ago, and he deliberately separated them from the Stone. A party that has been tracking the Eye count will recognize the significance immediately. Pays off in Vault of Dragons: confirms the separation was intentional, not a consequence of theft.

### Tone Note

The lockbox contains some of the most consequential intelligence in the entire campaign. The experience of finding it should not feel like a victory. Kalain's tower is a site of loss — a person who was broken by someone who did not notice breaking her, living among paintings of his face in monster form. The party gains the papers. They cannot fix anything here. That is the correct emotional register.

## Overview

Kalain kept Neverember's papers in a locked box without knowing what they contained. Her tower near the harbor held the vault-opening method, evidence of Neverember's intent to separate the Eyes from the Stone, and the first direct link between Victoro Cassalanter and the vault — weeks before the fireball.

## Summary

The reclusive painter Kalain received the party with suspicion and fragmented speech. Through careful conversation, she confirmed Victoro Cassalanter had visited weeks before asking the same questions. Lord Dagult's lockbox in her tower held four documents, including the vault-opening method and an unsigned letter confirming Neverember had deliberately separated all three Eyes from the Stone four years ago.
