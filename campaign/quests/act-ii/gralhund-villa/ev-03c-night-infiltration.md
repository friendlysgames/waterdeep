# Night Infiltration

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event covers entry into Gralhund Villa on the night of Ches 24th, with the raid already in progress. In this Event:
>
> - The party approaches a building where three factions are already in collision.
> - Four entry points are available; each puts the party into a different position within the chaos.
> - Hurv Taldred has transformed in the yard — the shadow threat is active from the moment the party crosses the perimeter.
> - Xanathar's gazers are still on the perimeter; the observation-team consequences from ev-02 carry forward.
> - Lord Orond is not in the library. He is barricaded in the guest suite (G15b) — covered in ev-05.
>
> **Trigger:** Party commits to the night path in ev-02 and approaches Gralhund Villa on the night of Ches 24th.

The fighting started roughly twenty minutes ago. The Zhent team entered through the Pantry (G6), cleared the ground floor, and pushed upstairs. The sounds are audible from the street before the party reaches the gate: something heavy falling, a shout cut short, a door being battered from one side.

The main gate is unlocked. The guard who was posted there was pulled inside when the alarm sounded.

### Entry Points

#### Main Gate (Unlocked)

The gate stands open. No resistance at the threshold. The cost is the yard.

Hurv Taldred has transformed — he is a shadow now, moving along the north wall. Two mastiff shadows range between the Coach House and the main entrance. They do not attack immediately; they stalk. Any party member reduced to 0 Strength by a shadow dies and rises as a shadow under Hurv's direction at the next dusk.

**DC 12 Stealth** (group check) to cross the yard without drawing immediate attention. On a failure, Hurv and the mastiff shadows engage.

The main entrance to the manor opens onto the great hall (G8): eight bodies on the floor, two live Zhent thugs still sweeping the ground floor.

#### Pantry Door (Already Open)

The Zhent team forced the east-side entry when they arrived. The door hangs open on a broken latch. Two bodies just inside — the head butler and a cook, both killed on entry.

This route bypasses the yard and the shadow threat entirely. The party enters the service area directly. From there, G5 (kitchen) and G7 (laundry) provide access to the main floor, and the back stairs (G19) reach the upper floor without passing through G8.

The quietest approach to the building, and the one the Zhents used. No check required to reach the door from the street if the party approached the east side.

#### Coach House Roofline to Balcony (G17)

The carriage arch route from ev-02 is still viable at night. **DC 10 Athletics** on the arch gains the Coach House (G3) roofline, bypassing the yard and the shadow threat entirely. From the roofline, the Balcony (G17) is reachable — **DC 14 Perception** to locate it at night against the dark stone face of the manor (no check if the party spotted it in ev-02).

This entry puts the party directly adjacent to G16. They skip most of the raid and arrive at the confrontation zone. Proceed to **The Gralhund Confrontation — Night** (ev-07) if they reach the G16 door without drawing attention.

#### Over the Wall

**DC 15 Athletics** from the street side, same as day. The drop into the yard puts the party in the same position as the main gate approach — Hurv and the mastiff shadows are active, and the group Stealth check applies.

No advantage over the main gate approach for equivalent effort.

### Observation Teams at Night

Xanathar's gazers are still on the perimeter. If the party was not spotted in ev-02, the same passive Perception applies now. Spotted parties: Xanathar now knows they entered with the raid in progress. The gazers will follow anyone who exits with something valuable. This activates their role in ev-08 if the Stone escapes.

If the party identified the Zhent watchers in ev-02, they can check: the Zhent observation post is dark. Floxin's team is already inside.

### What the Party Steps Into

**Via main gate or over the wall:** The yard is shadow territory from arrival. G8 is a battlefield — bodies and two Zhent thugs still working the ground floor.

**Via pantry door:** The ground floor service area is quiet. The fighting is above. The back stairs (G19) are the fastest route up without combat.

**Via balcony:** The party arrives at the upper floor directly. The foyer (G13) is a battlefield — the full night adversary roster is engaged there. G16 doors are ahead.

### Lord Orond

Lord Orond is not in G12 tonight. He barricaded himself in the guest suite (G15b) when the alarm sounded — wardrobe against the door, rapier in hand. Urstul Floxin is in G15a, trying to kick the door in.

For the full scene with Orond and Floxin, proceed to **The Guest Suite** (ev-05).

### Yalah's Contingency

If Yalah judges the confrontation has turned against her and the Stone is about to be taken, she may order the nimblewright in G11 to take the Stone and flee rather than surrender it. The construct activates on her command word spoken from G16. If the nimblewright escapes with the Stone, proceed to **The Rooftop Chase** (ev-08).

## Overview

The villa at night: a building mid-raid, three factions in collision, the yard transformed. Four ways in. Each one puts the party somewhere different inside the chaos.

## Read Aloud

> You hear it before you see it — something heavy going over on an upper floor, a door taking repeated impact, voices raised and then cut off. The gate at the end of Saerdoun Street is unlocked. The yard beyond it is dark. Something moves along the far wall that is not a guard.

## Summary

The party arrived at Gralhund Villa on the night of Ches 24th with the Zhentarim raid already in progress. They chose their entry point and stepped into the fighting.
