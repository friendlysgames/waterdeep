# The Front Door

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event covers the front-door approach to Gralhund Villa during the day. In this Event:
>
> - The party approaches the main gate openly and works through the household's admission process.
> - The head butler is the primary obstacle — not hostile, not cooperative, simply doing his job.
> - Three pretexts (official authority, personal connection, trade) each produce a different entry path.
> - A fourth outcome — confrontational or failed — exits to **Daytime Infiltration** (ev-03).
> - Lord Orond Gralhund is in G12; the party reaches him through guest channels or by slipping away from escort.
>
> **Trigger:** Party commits to the social approach on the day path. They walk up to the front gate of Gralhund Villa openly, in daylight, intending to be let in.

The household does not know what is coming tonight. The gate guards are on rotation. The head butler is three rooms away. Lady Yalah is upstairs, aware of the day's schedule and not expecting company.

### At the Gate

Two house guards at the main gate. They ask names and stated purpose. One stays; one goes inside for the butler. They take no independent action beyond this. They are not hostile.

The guards know: Lady Yalah does not receive unannounced visitors. Lord Gralhund takes callers. The butler handles all initial admissions. They do not know what is upstairs and will not speculate.

### The Head Butler

**Aldric Cray** has screened visitors for Lady Gralhund for eleven years. He arrives within five minutes — gray-haired, unhurried, carrying a small leather ledger. He notes the party's names before asking why they are here. He is difficult to embarrass and impossible to rush.

Three pretexts work. One does not.

#### Official Authority

A Watch credential (available if the party took Jalester's Cromley path in ev-01), a Lords' Alliance seal, or a Harper identification card carries weight. Cray recognizes the authority structures even if he has never met the issuing party.

**DC 10 Persuasion** — admitted as guests. He escorts them to the ground-floor receiving room and sends word to Lord Orond.

Without a credential: **DC 15 Persuasion** to convince Cray that interrupting Lord Gralhund is worth the household's attention.

#### Personal Connection

Naming Lord Orond as an acquaintance, or Renaer Neverember as a mutual contact, gets attention.

If the party mentions Renaer: Cray's expression does not change, but his note runs longer. Orond knows who Renaer is. Anyone connected to that name gets different consideration. **DC 12 Persuasion** — admitted; Orond will come down himself rather than wait to be summoned.

If they name Orond directly: **DC 12 Persuasion.** Orond will be asked, and will usually confirm a visitor rather than invite confrontation.

#### Trade or Service

A claimed delivery or a scheduled repair. Cray will not admit tradespeople through the front gate. He directs them to the servants' entry on the east side, near the Pantry (G6).

This gains access to the service areas — kitchen (G5), pantry (G6), laundry (G7). Reaching G12 from the servants' side means navigating the back stairs (G19) and slipping away from staff attention. **DC 12 Stealth** (group check).

#### Confrontational or Unpersuasive

Aggression, named demands, or a failed Persuasion met with pushback: Cray closes the gate without heat. The guards step closer. The front door is done.

The party must find another way in. Proceed to **Daytime Infiltration** (ev-03) for stealth and forced-entry options.

### Inside — The Escort

Admitted guests are brought to the ground-floor receiving room by a junior guard. The escort is polite, has been told to stay close, and has no interest in earning a complaint to the butler.

Options to slip away:

- **Distraction** (DC 12 Sleight of Hand or improvised urgency) — 2 minutes unescorted on a success.
- **Persuasion** (DC 14) — "We are guests, not suspects" with enough social confidence that the guard follows the convention rather than the instruction.
- **Stealth** (DC 11) — slip away during a brief lapse in attention.

The party does not need to lose the escort to reach Orond. If they wait in the receiving room, he arrives in about fifteen minutes. They simply have less freedom to explore.

### Lord Orond Gralhund

#### In the Receiving Room

Orond arrives careful and slightly formal. He is aware that callers who invoke Watch authority or Renaer Neverember's name are not social visits. He will talk. Cray may hover near the door.

**DC 12 Persuasion** (DC 10 if the party demonstrates knowledge of the Stone). The butler's proximity imposes Disadvantage on questions about Ches 22nd or the Zhentarim specifically — Orond may suggest moving to the library if those come up, where they will be less overheard.

#### In the Family Library (G12)

If the party slipped away from escort and found Orond here, he is caught mid-chapter: an illuminated historical volume open across his knees, a glass of something amber on the side table. His guard is down in the way that people are off-guard in their own libraries.

**DC 10 Persuasion.** No Disadvantage. No one nearby to overhear.

#### What Orond Confirms

Under either path:

1. The Stone of Golorr is an ancient creature transformed into an artifact; it holds the location of a hidden vault beneath Waterdeep containing enormous wealth.
2. Lady Yalah sent the nimblewright to Trollskull Alley on Ches 22nd. He calls the outcome "careless," and stops talking for a moment.
3. There is a religious affiliation — symbols in a trunk in the master bedroom — that he has never asked about directly.
4. The house funded Zhentarim agents who abducted Renaer Neverember two tendays ago. Yalah arranged it. The point was leverage to recover the Stone from Dalakhar. It went sideways: the gnome never came for the boy. He says this quietly, as if volume changes what it means.

If the party takes Orond into custody and brings him upstairs to G16, Yalah surrenders the Stone without a Charisma check. Hrabbaz stands down if Yalah looks at him first.

The Chirada letter on the G12 reading stand is visible to anyone who enters the library — no check required. See **G12 Searched** attunement below.

### Concluding the Event

#### Orond Confession Heard: True / False
Record whether the party questioned Orond and heard his four revelations. Shapes ev-09 aftermath — parties who heard the confession have documentation of Yalah's orders on Ches 22nd.

#### Chirada Letter Found: True / False
Record whether the party found and read *A Missive from Castle Ward* on the G12 reading stand. Pays off in **Faction Outposts**: Yellowspire, Chirada's name, and the Second Eye lead.

#### Front Door Visit Recorded: True / False
Cray noted the party's names in his ledger. Yalah is informed by evening: who came, what they claimed, and that they spoke with Orond. Read by ev-09 — shapes Yalah's debrief attitude and any Watch-related aftermath if the party used a Watch credential.

## Overview

The front door. Talk to the guards, talk to the butler, get invited in — or don't. Lord Orond is in the library.

## Read Aloud

> The guard at the gate does not reach for a weapon. He asks your names and your purpose. He writes nothing down. The gate stays closed while he listens, and stays closed while the other guard goes inside to get someone. The yard beyond is quiet. The manor does not look like somewhere you should be nervous about.

## Summary

The party approached Gralhund Villa through the front gate and worked through the household's admission process. They were let in — or weren't — on the strength of their pretext. They found Lord Orond in the library and heard what he was willing to say.
