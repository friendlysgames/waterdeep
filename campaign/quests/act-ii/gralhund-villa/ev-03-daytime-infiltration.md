# Daytime Infiltration

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social/Exploration Event covers the day-path entry into Gralhund Villa. In this Event:
>
> - The party enters a functioning guarded household with the Stone upstairs.
> - Three entry approaches are available — peaceful, alarmed, or forced — each with distinct consequences.
> - Alarm escalation unfolds in four beats if the estate's network is triggered.
> - Lord Orond Gralhund is in G12 during the day and will speak under gentle pressure.
> - The optional homebrew escalation elements are listed here for DMs who want a harder day encounter.
> - If the raid arrives while the party is inside, the event chains directly into ev-04.
>
> **Trigger:** Party arrives at Gralhund Villa during the day (before nightfall on Ches 24th). Follows from the day path commitment in ev-02.

The household is in ordinary operation: guards on rotation, servants moving between floors, Hurv Taldred and the mastiffs in the yard. The Stone is upstairs. The party must get there.

For the full adversary roster and room-by-room features, refer to the **Gralhund Villa Area Overview** and keyed room files.

### Entry Approaches

#### Peaceful Entry

The party approaches openly — the front gate, a visiting card, a polite explanation. The gate guard pages the head butler; someone will come. This approach gains access to the ground floor and, with success, to Lord Orond.

- **DC 12 Persuasion** to be received as guests.
- **DC 15** if the party mentions the fireball, the Zhentarim, or Floxin specifically.
- **Lady Yalah never appears for an unannounced visit.** She stays upstairs, aware of the visit but out of it. Orond may be fetched from G12.

Lord Orond under peaceful conditions is cooperative with effort — see the **Orond Gralhund** section below.

#### Alarmed Entry

If the party enters through a secondary point — scaling the wall, slipping through the Coach House — without triggering immediate detection but eventually drawing guard attention, the Alarm escalation timeline activates on the first loud noise, forced door, or guard encounter.

#### Forced Entry

Loud, fast, or aggressive approach. The Alarm network triggers immediately on the first door forced without a key or the first spell cast inside the perimeter (excluding Silence). Guards respond in sequence per the escalation timeline.

### Alarm Escalation Timeline (Day Context)

If the estate's Alarm network triggers at any point:

**Beat 1 — Immediate:** Two guards from G8 respond (1 round after trigger).

**Beat 2 — 1d4 rounds later:** Four armed-and-armored guards from G4 Barracks mobilize. Lady Yalah is alerted at this beat. She does not leave G16. She orders Hrabbaz to bar the door and activates any standing spell glyphs (if optional homebrew escalation is in play).

**Beat 3 — 1d4 rounds later:** Unarmored off-duty guards from G4 respond with improvised weapons.

**Beat 4 — 1d4 rounds later:** Remaining sleepers from G4 mobilize.

The Alarm network does not respond to whispered voices, Prestidigitation food-scents at the mastiff kennel, or the party moving through already-opened doors. It responds to forced locks, combat sounds, loud voices, and spellcasting flashes.

### Lord Orond Gralhund (G12 — Day Only)

Orond is in the Family Library (G12) during the day, reading at the padded chair, an illuminated historical volume open across his knees, a glass of something amber on the side table. He is the softest point of entry to the upper floor if the party reaches him through a peaceful approach or catches him before guards arrive.

Orond will not fight under any circumstances. He has paid others to take that risk for him.

Under DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion; Disadvantage if Yalah has already been alerted), he confirms:

- The Stone of Golorr is an ancient creature transformed into an artifact; it holds the location of a hidden vault beneath Waterdeep containing enormous wealth.
- Lady Yalah sent the nimblewright to Trollskull Alley on Ches 22nd. He calls the outcome "careless," and stops talking for a moment.
- There is a religious affiliation (symbols in a trunk in the master bedroom) that he has never asked about directly.
- The house funded Zhentarim agents who abducted Renaer Neverember two tendays ago. Yalah arranged it. The point was leverage to recover the Stone from Dalakhar. It went sideways: the gnome never came for the boy. He says this quietly, as if volume changes what it means.

If the party takes Orond into custody and brings him upstairs to G16, Yalah will surrender the Stone without a Charisma check. Hrabbaz will stand down if Yalah looks at him first.

> #### The Chirada Letter (G12 — Day and Night)
>
> Separately from Orond and the locked tome: a folded letter sits on the reading stand in G12 at all times. This is always-present — not a day-only feature.
>
> *A Missive from Castle Ward*, signed "Chirada," dated two weeks prior. The party can read it if they are in G12 and look at the stand — no check required.
>
> It names the Second Eye, Yellowspire in the Castle Ward, and a suspected teleportal. This is a direct feed into the Grand Game intelligence network the party will navigate in Arc E. Record in the **G12 Searched** attunement.

### Yalah's Contingency

If Yalah judges that the confrontation has turned against her and the Stone is about to be taken, she may order the nimblewright in G11 to take the Stone and flee rather than surrender it. The construct is dormant there, triggered by either the Alarm network or her command word spoken from G16. She does not do this reflexively; she reads the situation first. If the nimblewright escapes with the Stone, proceed to ev-08: The Rooftop Chase.

> #### Optional Daytime Escalation
>
> The daytime state provides no rival factions to absorb combat attrition — Zhents and guards fight each other at night, thinning the opposition before the party reaches G16. During the day, the party generates all of it. If a fully-equipped level 4 party appears likely to roll through the standard household, any of the following optional elements may be added. **Choose one; do not stack.**
>
> - **Animated Armor (2–4 suits):** Stationed in the upstairs hallway and G8. Triggered by the Alarm network.
> - **Spell Glyphs:** *Faithful Hound* on the upstairs landing; *Cloud of Daggers* on the G16 door; *Bless* and *Haste* on Yalah and Hrabbaz, triggering on G16 breach.
> - **Guard Mastiffs (2–4 additional):** High Perception, mobile; vulnerable to Prestidigitation treat-bribery (DC 10 Sleight of Hand).
> - **Proto-Beholder (1, G8):** 7–8 eyestalks, no antimagic cone. A mid-weight encounter without the CR 13 spike. Load the boss-design skill before finalizing the stat block. Do not use a standard beholder.
>
> Additionally, if Jarlaxle's team is present across the street and the nimblewright becomes a combat liability, his drow can remotely override the construct via its embedded crystal — the nimblewright drops to its knees (incapacitated, one round), then shakes free. Usable once. The party should not know this happened.

### The Raid Arrives

If the party is still inside when nightfall arrives on Ches 24th, or if the Zhent watchers spotted them entering and Floxin mobilizes early, the raid begins regardless. Hurv Taldred transforms into a shadow in the yard (G2). The Party can hear the pantry door give from inside the building.

Continue from current adversary positions into ev-04: The Zhentarim Raid. The day-to-night chain folds both events into a single session without a break.

## Overview

The villa during the day is a functioning household — guarded, armed, and operating without any indication of the crisis about to arrive. The Stone is upstairs.

## Read Aloud

> The gate opens. The yard beyond it is quiet — a groundskeeper moving along the far wall, mastiffs circling the Coach House, guards at the corners watching you come in. The manor itself is two stories of dressed stone, shuttered upper windows, smoke from the kitchen chimney. Nothing about it looks like a place that is about to decide the fate of everything you have been chasing for the past week.

## Summary

The party entered Gralhund Villa during the day. The estate was guarded and operational, Lady Yalah upstairs with the Stone. Lord Orond was in the library. Whatever the party did here set the shape of what came next: who they spoke to, what doors they opened, what they left standing.
