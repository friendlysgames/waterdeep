### Bad Time to Be a Zhent

By the morning of Ches 25th, the City Watch has sealed Gralhund Villa and begun processing twenty-three bodies. Lord Gralhund is in shock; Hrabbaz is dead. No Stone, no nimblewright, no Floxin.

The broadsheet (**Gralhund Villa Bloodbath**) runs within forty-eight hours. The Watch begins a city-wide crackdown on the Black Network, arresting known and suspected Zhentarim regardless of cell affiliation, legitimate business or not.

**Lady Yalah's status** depends on the confrontation outcome:
- **Missing:** Fled before the Watch arrived. A quiet want notice circulates through gate guards and ship registries.
- **In Watch custody:** She says nothing useful. She is skilled at this.
- **Dead:** Orond becomes the face of House Gralhund overnight, which is roughly as effective as it sounds.

Party members with visible Zhentarim associations should keep a low profile for a tenday. Characters seen at the villa and describable by surviving witnesses may receive a Watch summons for questioning within three days.

### Davil Arrested, Tashlyn Takes Over

Two days after the villa: Davil Starsong is taken in for questioning at Castle Waterdeep. The Watch's sweep has caught him on association, not evidence; "pending questioning by the Lords" is indefinite. He suspects Floxin filed an anonymous Watch tip naming the Doom Raiders as persons of interest, a damage-control move meant to draw heat away from Manshoon's cell.

A black flying snake arrives at wherever the party is staying. It carries a note from **Tashlyn Yafeera**, Doom Raiders Master of Stealth and Deception and now the party's de facto contact:

*"Davil is unavailable. Continue normal operations. Await further contact."*

Four words of cover. Zero words of comfort.

Tashlyn's first direct contact comes within forty-eight hours: *"Keep a low profile. I'll be in touch."* She does not explain what that means. She will be in touch when she has decided how much to trust a party that walked into a noble's estate and started a war.

### Istrid Horn's Approach

If the party participated in the confrontation at Gralhund Villa (directly, not secondhand), a flying snake finds them within forty-eight hours. On yellowed paper, in small, deliberate letters:

*"I have a business matter to discuss. Ahghairon's Statue, City of the Dead, after dark. Come alone or don't come."*

Signed with a symbol: an hourglass with a coin at the bottom.

**Istrid Horn**, Doom Raiders Master of Trade and Coin, is a shield dwarf fence and unlicensed moneylender. The Watch's crackdown has her terrified. She has outstanding loans to a half-dozen city officials, documented in a ledger she cannot afford to have found. She wants the party to hide her, or destroy the ledger, for a tenday while she makes arrangements to relocate to Baldur's Gate.

The meeting at Ahghairon's Statue proceeds **at highsun**. She chose the open hour deliberately. Too many witnesses for anyone to try anything. Istrid is alone, seated on a bench with a satchel at her feet and her eyes on the gate. She offers 10 platinum for information about what the Watch knows, and 40 platinum for a tenday of shelter.

**Branch Outcomes:**

**Help her.** She stays hidden for a tenday, then vanishes south. She leaves a handwritten note: the name and location of a Zhentarim courier cache in the South Ward that the party can raid without Manshoon's cell knowing for at least a month. Doom Raiders renown +2 if the party reports the arrangement to Tashlyn.

**Turn her in.** City Watch takes her. The moneylending ledger surfaces in the Watch's evidence log and quietly disappears within a week; someone on the force had a loan outstanding. The Watch contact improves for the party (reduced questioning on the Gralhund matter). Doom Raiders renown −2 if the party reports this to Tashlyn. Tashlyn does not explain why.

**Take her money and leave her.** She relocates independently. The cache note never comes. She holds no grudge. She extends no future goodwill.

**Mortal Enemy.** If any PC has designated Istrid Horn as their Doom Raiders Mortal Enemy contact: she cannot refuse to come, and the party holds the leverage. A PC with this history may extract the courier cache information and the full 40 platinum and shelter cooperation without a check, simply by naming the arrangement clearly. Alternatively: a PC who turns her in despite having named her mortal enemy forfeits the Mortal Enemy bond and takes Doom Raiders renown −3. Tashlyn's note arrives the next morning: *"That was our business. Not the Watch's."*

### Jarlaxle Returns the Stone (Conditional — *Stone Holder: BD*, No BD Operative)

If Bregan D'Aerthe holds the Stone and no BD operative is in the party, a package arrives at Trollskull Manor some time after Istrid's approach — the exact hour does not matter, but it is the same day. It is wrapped in dark cloth, unmarked, left at the door by someone no one saw.

Inside: the Stone of Golorr. And a card, blank on one side, written on the other in a small, precise hand:

*"Consider this an introduction. — J."*

Nothing else. No instructions. No conditions stated.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Why Jarlaxle Returns It
>
> He had the Stone. He could have opened it himself and sent an operative into the vault. He chose not to. The message is everything the card doesn't say: he knew who they were before they knew him, he is more useful to them with the Stone in their hands than in his, and he expects them to remember this when the time comes.
>
> This is not charity. It is the most effective possible investment in a relationship he has already decided will be valuable.
>

### Faction Debriefs

Within one to three days of the villa, the party's active faction contacts request accounts of what happened. Most use flying snakes or anonymous notes; the crackdown has made direct meetings risky.

**Harpers (Mirt).** A Harper agent in the crowd outside the villa recognized at least one PC. If the party reports in first, Mirt receives the account with measured satisfaction and asks no follow-up questions. If they don't, he sends a note in his own handwriting: *"I heard Saerdoun Street was lively last night. Whenever you have a moment."* He would appreciate being told things that concern him before he learns them from someone else.

**Lords' Alliance (Jalester).** His debrief request arrives within twenty-four hours. The Watch officers on the Saerdoun Street beat were transferred to a Far South Ward assignment the week before the raid. Jalester has traced the reassignment to a request from inside the Watch hierarchy; the source is unknown. He wants to know if the party saw any evidence of Watch coordination with the Xanathar Guild or the Gralhund household.

**Emerald Enclave (Melannor).** Gralhund Villa sits over a minor ley line the Enclave monitors as part of Jeryth Phaulkon's aberrant-activity survey. The Xanathar Guild's presence in the neighborhood destabilized it over the past week in a way that correlates with Nihiloor's movements below the city. Melannor wants to know what the party observed. For the map.

**Order of the Gauntlet (Savra).** Her debrief is a time and a location. She wants to know if any villa occupants showed signs of infernal corruption: unusual tattoos, branded symbols, liturgical items from proscribed cults. If the party found the G16 compartment, she confirms what they found and adds: *"The Cassalanters sent agents to Gralhund Villa during the confrontation. I have confirmed it from a separate source. This is not over."* She already knew.

**Force Grey (Vajra).** A full operational debrief: which factions were present, where each one entered and exited, whether any Watch checkpoint in the surrounding blocks was compromised or unmanned during the raid, and any direct evidence of Nihiloor's involvement. Xanathar Guild activity approaching a Watch checkpoint the Guild has never previously targeted is, in her judgment, a test. She wants to know if the test succeeded.

**Doom Raiders (Tashlyn).** Already covered above. "Keep a low profile. I'll be in touch." No debrief. No elaboration.

**Bregan D'Aerthe — BD Operative in Party (conditional — *Jarlaxle Informed*).** No back-room invitation. Fel'rekt's debrief arrives through the operative's established channel — a direct, brief message, not an open offer to the group.

The message assesses the mission against Jarlaxle's standing orders. Its tone depends on the arc outcome:

*Stone Holder: Party* — the operative delivered as ordered: *"Stone confirmed. Jarlaxle is satisfied. Your renown with the organization reflects the outcome."* Bregan D'Aerthe renown +2.

*Stone Holder: Zhentarim, Xanathar, or Nimblewright* (Stone lost entirely) — *"The Stone did not reach the party. Jarlaxle is reviewing the operation. Await contact."* No renown change. Jarlaxle will want an explanation before the next assignment.

In all cases, the operative is not blamed for outcomes beyond their control (the party's failure, another faction seizing the Stone mid-chase). The renown reflects what the operative personally did, not what the arc produced.

## Overview

The morning after the bloodbath. Twenty-three bodies, a city-wide Watch crackdown, and a party holding either the Stone of Golorr or the knowledge of where it went. Davil is in custody. Tashlyn is in charge. Istrid Horn wants a meeting.

## Read Aloud

> The broadsheet calls it the Gralhund Villa Bloodbath. It doesn't have your name in it, which is the best thing about it. Outside, the Watch is running men in black leather out of every ward in the city, and somewhere in a holding cell at Castle Waterdeep, Davil Starsong is trying to remember which stories are still useful.
>
> A flying snake finds you wherever you're staying and drops a note on the table.

## Summary

The party survived Gralhund Villa. The Watch crackdown reshaped the city overnight. Davil Starsong was arrested; Tashlyn Yafeera took over the Doom Raiders contact. Istrid Horn reached out for a private meeting. Faction contacts asked for accounts of what happened, each for their own reasons, each needing a different piece of the same night.
