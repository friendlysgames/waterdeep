# The Rooftop Chase

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Chase Event is the contingency for the quest's design mandate — it fires only if the Stone escaped the villa. In this Event:
>
> - The party gives chase to the Stone carrier across North Ward rooftops.
> - 2024 DMG chase mechanics govern the sequence; the North Ward d12 complication table provides environmental obstacles.
> - Xanathar's gazers may intervene — either as a tactical assist or as a response team intercept.
> - If the chase fails, the Stone reaches a faction safe house. The party enters ev-09 without it.
>
> **Trigger:** The Stone escaped ev-06 or ev-07 — taken by the nimblewright, by BD, or by a surviving Zhent — and the party chooses to pursue.

The party must see the Stone leave and decide to give chase. This event does not fire if they didn't see it go, or if they chose not to pursue.

### Trigger Conditions

**Nimblewright.** Yalah's contingency: the construct drops from the Balcony (G17) to the Coach House roofline and crosses from there. It moves at 60 feet per round and does not tire.

**Bregan D'Aerthe.** Fel'rekt's team exits via the rooftops. They move fast and know where they are going: a BD outpost in the Trades Ward (Fenerus Stormcastle).

**Zhentarim survivor.** A surviving Zhent thug grabbed the Stone in the confusion and is running for the nearest safe house rather than waiting for orders that aren't coming.

### Chase Mechanics (2024 DMG)

Use the 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide chase rules (DMG p. 254) as the mechanical chassis.

**Key 2024 rules in effect:**
- Participants may Dash a number of times equal to **3 + Constitution modifier** before requiring a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw** to continue Dashing.
- Opportunity attacks are suppressed during a chase, except from bystanders directly in the path.
- At the end of each round, the quarry may attempt a **Stealth check against the highest pursuer's passive Perception** to break line of sight and escape.

**Starting distance:** The quarry has a 60-foot lead when the chase begins (they exited the building before the party). Adjust if the party was already on the balcony or rooftop.

### North Ward Rooftop Complications

At the end of each participant's turn, roll a d12:

| d12 | Complication |
|-----|--------------|
| 1–2 | No complication. |
| 3 | Loose tile: DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone (does not end the chase). |
| 4 | Clothesline: DC 11 Dexterity saving throw or become entangled (Speed halved until a bonus action clears it). |
| 5 | Chimney gap: DC 13 Athletics to jump. Failure: drop 10 feet to a lower roofline (1d6 damage, still in the chase). |
| 6 | A sleeping dog wakes on a rooftop garden and barks — City Watch responds in 1d6 + 2 minutes. |
| 7 | Open skylight: DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or drop into the room below (end of the chase for that character; they must find another route). |
| 8 | Wet slate: Speed reduced by 10 feet this turn. |
| 9 | A Griffon Cavalry rider makes a pass overhead — DC 13 Stealth or spotted and reported. |
| 10 | A merchant's flagpole: DC 11 Athletics or become snagged, losing an action to pull free. |
| 11 | Loose fascia board gives way: DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or fall 15 feet to the street (1d6 damage + prone; ends the chase for that character on this rooftop). |
| 12 | The quarry drops something — a coin, a piece of cloth, a component pouch — onto the street below. Not the Stone, but a potential handhold. The pursuer may descend and follow the street instead; the quarry loses 30 feet of lead. |

### Xanathar's Gazer Intervention

Xanathar's gazers have been following anyone who left the villa with something valuable. They may intercede. The DM chooses one of two outcomes:

**Tactical assist:** A gazer swoops in and fires a paralytic ray at whoever is carrying the Stone, crippling them for one round and letting the party close distance. Xanathar would rather the party have the Stone than any other faction; he can always take it from the party later.

**Response team intercept:** If the DM wants a harder version of this scene, a Xanathar Guild response team cuts off the rooftop exit route, producing a three-way confrontation for the Stone. Refer to the Response Teams section of the **Xanathar's Guild** organization page for roster and tactics.

### If the Chase Fails

The Stone reaches its destination:

- **Nimblewright:** The construct delivers the Stone to a BD outpost (Fenerus Stormcastle, Trades Ward). BD now holds it.
- **BD team:** The Stone reaches Fenerus Stormcastle. Jarlaxle holds it.
- **Zhent survivor:** The Stone reaches a Manshoon safe house. Manshoon's cell holds it.

From any of these locations, the Stone moves to the faction's primary lair within 2d6 days. The party enters ev-09 without the Stone. Milestone 2 does not fire. The campaign continues, but the party arrives at every subsequent negotiation without the asset that makes them worth negotiating with.

### If the Chase Succeeds

The party recovers the Stone from the carrier. Set *Stone Recovered: True* and proceed to ev-09. Milestone 2 fires at the ev-09 opening.

### Concluding the Event

#### Stone Recovered: True / False
Record whether the party holds the Stone at chase end. True → Milestone 2 fires at ev-09 opening. False → party enters ev-09 without the Stone.

## Overview

The Stone is somewhere ahead on North Ward rooftops, moving fast. The party has a choice.

## Read Aloud

> The construct drops from the balcony without pausing, lands on the Coach House roof, crosses it in four strides, and is over the perimeter wall and gone. The North Ward rooftops spread out ahead in the dark, slate and tile and chimneys, a city laid flat.
>
> It is forty feet ahead. Sixty. Getting further.

## Summary

The Stone left the villa without the party holding it. A rooftop chase across the North Ward followed. Whether the party recovered it or it slipped away into a faction safe house, the quest ends here.
