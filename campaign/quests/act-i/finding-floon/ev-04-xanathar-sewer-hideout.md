# Xanathar Sewer Hideout

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event occurs when the party enters the sewers to follow Floon's trail. In this Event:
>
> - The party navigates to the hideout using guildsign (three DC 13 Wisdom [Survival] checks; advantage if following guildsign from the alley entrance)
> - A gazer guard blocks the junction midway — bypassed or defeated before reaching Q01
> - The party infiltrates an eleven-room Xanathar Guild cell (Q01–Q11)
> - Floon is alive in Q07; Nihiloor flees through the Q11 portal before the party can engage it
> - Q09 is a future Dock Ward resource (Trollskull Alley); Q11 is the Xanathar's Lair sewer-to-lair seed
>
> For keyed room details (Q01–Q11), see the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** journal.
>
> Completing this Event awards 1 Milestone Point and likely advances the party to Level 2.

### Sewer Navigation

The party enters the sewers through the grate half a block east of the warehouse yard. Inside: one foot of sewage, darkness, and Xanathar's yellow chalk guildsign marking the route at each junction.

**Three DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) checks** are required to navigate to the hideout:
- Advantage on all three checks if the party located the guildsign at the sewer entrance before descending
- On a success, the party follows the correct passage without alerting anyone
- **0 successes:** The party arrives at Q07 too late — interrogation over, Floon's body on the floor (arc failure state)
- **2 or more failures:** The party takes enough wrong turns to be heard at Q01; the Q02 goblins wake up

### The Gazer

Midway through the route, at a branching tunnel junction, a **gazer** (Xanathar Guild sentinel) watches the intersection. It is hostile and cannot be negotiated with.

**Bypass:** Group DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check. On success, the party neutralizes or slips past the gazer without alerting the hideout.

**Open combat:** Defeating the gazer in open combat should be treated as noise reaching Q01. Add the alert trigger even if the navigation checks were otherwise successful.

### The Hideout — Q01 to Q11

The complex is an eleven-room Xanathar Guild cell built into a decommissioned sewer vault. No natural light; goblins rely on darkvision. All interior doors are unlocked. Grum'shar assumes only those with guild business reach this place.

Alert state (goblins awake) is set by the navigation result and the gazer encounter. Refer to the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** journal for keyed room entries describing both states.

**Key rooms for this event:**

**Q02 — Watch Posts:** Two goblins, one per alcove. Asleep (unaware state) or awake (alert state). DC 9 Dexterity (Stealth) to sneak past sleeping goblins.

**Q05 — Sleeping Area:** Zemk (duergar, hostile) barricading the Q06 door against the gray ooze. Krentz is here if he survived ev-01, arguing that the blanket approach won't work. Neither notices intruders unless noise is made.

**Q07 — Boss Chamber:** See below.

**Q09 — Private Cellar:** Peabody halfling family brewing beer below Fishgut Alley. Trollskull Alley resource — provides a second sewer entry and exit point.

**Q11 — Escape Portal:** Stone pillar with Xanathar guildsign and circular key socket. See Nihiloor section below.

### Q07 — The Boss Chamber

When the party arrives (if the navigation result isn't the failure state):

**Grum'shar** (CE half-orc wizard) stands with his foot on Floon's chest, fire burning around his clenched fist, interrogating him. **Nihiloor** (mind flayer) is seated on a raised platform to the south, cradling an **intellect devourer**.

The moment it sees the party, it rises, sets the intellect devourer down (now loose and hostile), and glides toward the western double door, heading for Q11. It uses *dominate monster* only to clear its escape path if blocked. It doesn't fight the party. It doesn't speak. Grum'shar and the intellect devourer cover its escape.

**Floon** is alive but has 1 hit point. If healed, he hugs everyone. If the party asks what he knows: he doesn't know why he was taken. He's been unconscious for most of his captivity.

**Grum'shar's satchel** (recovered whether he fled or is defeated here): spellbook containing *burning hands*, *disguise self*, *false life*, *shield*, *unseen servant*, *witch bolt*.

**Chest** (unlocked, behind the stone chair): two potions of healing, 16 gp, 82 sp, 250 cp.

**Failure state** (0 navigation successes): Grum'shar and Nihiloor are gone. Floon's body is on the floor. The chest and satchel may still be present if the party arrives before Grum'shar looted them, but there is no rescue to perform.

### Nihiloor and Q11

Nihiloor glides from Q07 through the hideout's western corridor to Q11. If the party pursues, it is always ahead, moving with purpose through passages it knows by heart. Any creature that blocks its path encounters *dominate monster* (DC 15 Wisdom saving throw).

At Q11, it presses its 3-inch stone orb into the pillar's circular indentation. An opaque black doorway (8 feet tall, 4 feet wide) opens in the south wall. It steps through into area X22 of Xanathar's lair. The party cannot follow unless they somehow have the orb (impossible in normal play, since it carries the orb through). The portal closes 1 minute after the orb is removed from the indentation.

After Nihiloor escapes, Q11's pillar is inert. DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) identifies the indentation as a key socket. DC 16 Intelligence (History) identifies the guildsign as Xanathar's mark. See the **Q11 — Escape Portal** room entry in the **Xanathar Sewer Hideout** journal for full mechanic details.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Nihiloor — Restraint is the Point
>
> The party's encounter with Nihiloor is a sight-line. They see it rise, release the intellect devourer, and glide out of the room. This moment shows that Grum'shar was never the top of this structure. The goal is scale. Players should leave Q07 understanding that something they cannot yet face was running this cell. Its full scope surfaces across Trollskull Alley through Xanathar.s Lair.

### Volo's Payment

With Floon rescued, the party surfaces. Volo is waiting at the warehouse, at the Yawning Portal, or wherever the party set a rendezvous. He doesn't have the 100 gp per character. He produces the deed to a derelict tavern in Trollskull Alley, North Ward:

> "Worth considerably more than the gold owed. An investment in your futures."

DC 12 Wisdom (Insight): Volo is genuinely embarrassed. He offers to cover the gold difference "once the royalties arrive from Mordenkainen." He departs before anyone can press him.

The deed is real. **Trollskull Manor** is real, non-operational, and now the party's property.

### Concluding the Event

#### Floon Rescued: True / False

Floon is found alive in Q07 (1 hp) and survives the arc. He doesn't know why he was taken. He thanks everyone, invites the party to drinks he cannot afford, and reappears in Trollskull Alley as a Trollskull Alley regular.

#### Nihiloor Sighted: True / False

The party witnessed a mind flayer in the Xanathar Guild's sewer cell. They don't yet know what it is. This attunement pays off when the mind flayer's role in the Grand Game surfaces in Xanathar's Lair.

#### Trollskull Deed Accepted: True / False

The party holds legal title to a derelict tavern at 10 Trollskull Alley, North Ward. Trollskull Alley begins with this deed in hand.

## Overview

The sewers beneath the Dock Ward lead to an Xanathar Guild hideout carved into a decommissioned vault. Floon Blagmaar is alive inside. Barely. So is something else, something that leaves before the party can engage it.

## Read Aloud

> One foot of cold sewage. No light except what you brought. Yellow chalk eye-symbols at every fork, pointing the way someone else wanted you to go.

## Summary

The party navigated the Dock Ward sewers to an Xanathar Guild hideout, rescued Floon Blagmaar from the boss chamber, and witnessed a mind flayer escape through a stone portal. Volo paid with a deed to a derelict tavern called Trollskull Manor in the North Ward.
