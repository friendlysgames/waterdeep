# Saerdoun Street

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration Event covers the reconnaissance phase on Saerdoun Street and locks in the day/night commitment. In this Event:
>
> - The party observes the villa exterior and has the opportunity to identify all three faction observation teams.
> - Spotting each team earns tactical information; being spotted triggers consequences.
> - The party chooses when to enter — arriving before nightfall on Ches 24th (day state) or on the night of Ches 24th (night state).
> - The exterior DCs, Watch timing, and wall-scaling mechanics live here; room-specific DCs and interior features are in the **Gralhund Villa Area Overview**.
>
> **Trigger:** The party arrives on Saerdoun Street and begins scoping the estate.

### The Villa Exterior

The estate occupies a full block on Saerdoun Street, North Ward. The perimeter wall is old stone, reinforced, extending above head height. The main gate faces the street, a two-story carriage arch flanking it. The Coach House (G3) roofline is the only interior structure visible from street level. The upper floors of the manor rise behind it.

**If the PCs are sizing up the perimeter wall:**

Old stone, approximately 12 feet high (DC 12 Perception or 10 minutes of observation). DC 15 Athletics to scale from the street side. The interior yard slopes downward — the drop on the far side is shorter than the climb.

**If the PCs are trying the main gate:**

Close approach required. The latch does not yield to normal pressure; a faint resistance stops the mechanism before it catches. DC 15 Investigation to register the latch behavior. *Detect Magic* within 10 feet confirms *arcane lock*. Forcing it requires DC 25 Strength or DC 20 thieves' tools.

**If the PCs are looking for an approach that avoids the wall:**

DC 14 Perception spots the Balcony (G17) — recessed into the manor's north face, partially obscured by ornamental ironwork above the main entrance. To reach it: DC 10 Athletics on the carriage arch beside the gate gains the Coach House (G3) roofline without a wall check, and the balcony is accessible from there.

**If the PCs are watching for patrol patterns:**

A party observing from a fixed position for 30+ minutes notes the City Watch passing Saerdoun Street irregularly, roughly every 20 minutes. Any audible commotion outside the walls draws a Watch response in 1d6 + 4 minutes (1 mage, 6 veterans). Jalester, Cromley, or any city-wise contact can supply the same information without the wait.

### The Three Observation Teams

Three factions are already watching the villa. Spotting them is reconnaissance. Being spotted is a complication.

#### Zhentarim Watchers (DC 12 Perception)

Two Zhent thugs on nearby rooftops, one black flying snake courier between them. They are watching to ensure no one leaves before Floxin's team arrives. They noticed one of Xanathar's gazers several days ago but could not identify it. They are unaware of Jarlaxle's team in Artheyn Manor.

**If they spot the party approaching:** The flying snake carries word to Floxin. His strike team mobilizes and arrives in 10 + 2d6 minutes. The party has that window before the night adversary state becomes active regardless of actual time.

#### Xanathar's Gazers (DC 16 Perception)

Four gazers positioned at cardinal points around the estate. Each success on the Perception check reveals one; each additional 2 points of margin reveals another. The gazers followed Floxin here and are installed to understand what he wants from the Gralhunds. They do not intervene in anything that happens at the villa — they report and follow.

**If the party is spotted:** No immediate reaction. Xanathar now knows the party is involved. The gazers will follow anyone who exits the villa with something valuable — this activates their potential role in ev-08 if the Stone escapes.

#### Jarlaxle's Team at Artheyn Manor (DC 14 Perception — conditional on *Jarlaxle Informed*)

If the party set the *Jarlaxle Informed* attunement in Arc C, Fel'rekt Lafeen's team occupies the empty Artheyn Manor directly across Saerdoun Street. The owner has relocated to the Moonshae Isles and the building is dark.

Ground floor: Fel'rekt and two drow at a front-room watch post. Top floor: two drow gunslingers and six additional drow with a direct sightline to the villa's upper windows. *A Directive from Jarlaxle* sits on a side table in the ground-floor room — recoverable if the party searches Artheyn Manor before or after the raid. Jarlaxle's team has full situational awareness of both other factions' watchers. They are waiting for an opportunity, not a trigger.

If this condition is not set, Artheyn Manor is empty.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Reconnaissance Advantages
>
> A party that scouts carefully enters the confrontation with advantages their notes cannot replace:
>
> - **DC 14 Investigation or Survival:** Identify the Zhentarim watchers' sightline gaps. Reveals a blind approach route along the villa's west wall — the party can reach the perimeter without triggering the Zhent detection check.
> - **DC 15 Stealth (group check):** Approach the villa without being spotted by any team. On a failure, compare the lowest Stealth result to each team's passive Perception to determine who noticed first. Zhent watchers: passive 11. Gazers: passive 14. BD team (if present): passive 13.
> - **DC 15 Athletics:** Scale the 12-foot perimeter wall without assistance. The Coach House roofline provides a platform for the Balcony approach without this check.

### The Day/Night Commitment

The party must decide when to enter. This decision determines everything that follows.

**Day state** (arriving before nightfall on Ches 24th): The Gralhund household is in its normal configuration: guards at their posts, the nimblewright dormant in G11, Lady Yalah upstairs with the Stone. Floxin's team has not arrived. No bodies. Proceed to **ev-03: Daytime Infiltration**.

**Night state** (arriving on the night of Ches 24th, raid in progress): Floxin's team has already entered through the Pantry (G6). The raid is underway. At least a dozen bodies when the party arrives. Three factions in collision. Proceed to **ev-04: The Zhentarim Raid**.

The day/night choice should not feel like a coin flip. The DM should surface what the party knows — the Ches 24th deadline, the explicit faction warnings about the window — clearly enough that arriving early or late is a genuine choice with understood tradeoffs: a guarded household versus a building already mid-battle.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Day-to-Night Chain
>
> If the party enters during the day (ev-03) and is still inside when the night of Ches 24th arrives, the raid begins regardless. The DM should signal the approach of nightfall — growing dark outside the windows, Hurv Taldred beginning his yard rounds with a lamp — so the party can choose to withdraw, push for the confrontation, or brace for what comes through the Pantry. Once the raid begins, continue from ev-03 directly into ev-04 using the current adversary positions. Proceed to ev-05 (Floxin at G15) and then ev-07 (Night Confrontation).

## Overview

Saerdoun Street, North Ward. The villa is across the street. Three factions are already watching it. The party has until the night of Ches 24th.

## Read Aloud

> Saerdoun Street is quiet at this hour. The estate takes up a full block — old stone walls, reinforced, the main gate facing the street. You can see the Coach House roofline above the wall. The upper floors of the manor rise behind it. Somewhere on those rooftops, or in the building across the street, or behind that chimney, someone else is watching too.

## Summary

The party reached Saerdoun Street and observed the villa. Three factions are watching: Zhentarim watchers on the rooftops, Xanathar's gazers on the perimeter, and — if the party reached Jarlaxle in the Fireball investigation — a Bregan D'Aerthe team directly across the street. The party committed to a day or night entry.
