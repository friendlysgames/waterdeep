## Involved Characters

- **Lady Yalah Gralhund** (Gralhund household) — North Ward noble, de facto head of house; holds the Stone; has already revised her arrangement with the Zhentarim to keep it; breastplate, rapier, a brass key at her throat, and no intention of surrendering anything without a reason
- **Lord Orond Gralhund** (Gralhund household) — Yalah's husband; reads in the library during the day, barricades himself in G15b during the raid; will not fight; will talk under light pressure; knows more than he wants to
- **Hrabbaz** (Gralhund household) — Yalah's bodyguard; orog; nothing to say; has been paid to stand by a door, and he will stand by it
- **Urstul Floxin** (Manshoon's Zhentarim) — led the strike team; arrived on the night of Ches 24th with eight agents and lost most of them; trapped in the guest suite corridor by the time the party arrives; still armed; still operational; still will not name his master
- **Fel'rekt Lafeen** (Bregan D'Aerthe — conditional on *Jarlaxle Informed*) — the drow negotiator across the street; arrives through the balcony during the raid; his offer to Yalah is the cleanest sentence in the building: *"The Stone, Lady Gralhund. That is all I am here for."*
- **Hurv Taldred** (Gralhund household) — groundskeeper, cult fanatic; transforms to a shadow at nightfall
- **Istrid Horn** (Doom Raiders) — Doom Raiders Master of Trade and Coin; reached for Ahghairon's Statue within forty-eight hours of the bloodbath; terrified; has leverage and debts in both directions; the one quiet personal beat in a political aftermath
- **Davil Starsong** (Doom Raiders, player faction contact) — arrested in the Watch's post-Gralhund sweep; in custody for questioning; replaced in practice, if not in title, by Tashlyn
- **Tashlyn Yafeera** (Doom Raiders, player faction contact) — takes over party contact duties after Davil's arrest; first message: four words of cover, zero words of comfort
- **Mirt, Jalester, Vajra, Savra, Melannor** (player faction contacts) — each seeks a debrief within three days of the bloodbath, each for a different reason; listed individually in ev-09

## Dangers & Enemies

**Day state:** Gralhund house guards (veterans), Hurv Taldred (cult fanatic) + 2 mastiffs in the yard, Hrabbaz (orog) at the master bedroom door, the household nimblewright (if the Alarm triggers or Yalah activates it). Optional homebrew escalation (animated armor, spell glyphs, proto-beholder) available if the DM needs additional challenge — see ev-03.

**Night state:** Urstul Floxin (50 hp, no poison, CR 3) in the guest suite corridor; Hurv Taldred as shadow + 2 mastiff-shadows in the yard; 1–2 surviving Zhent thugs in the great hall and upstairs foyer; Hrabbaz; Yalah; the household nimblewright. Jarlaxle's gunslingers fire through the upstairs foyer windows if *Jarlaxle Informed* is set.

**Both states:** Xanathar's four gazers on perimeter — they observe, track, and report; they do not enter the estate.

## Overview

The investigation that began in Trollskull Alley ends at the gate of Gralhund Villa on Saerdoun Street. The Stone of Golorr is upstairs in Lady Yalah Gralhund's breastplate. Urstul Floxin's backup team arrives on the night of Ches 24th. Xanathar's gazers are already watching the perimeter. Jarlaxle's Bregan D'Aerthe has a team across the street, waiting for the right moment to move.

The party has a window of roughly forty-eight hours. They can enter the villa during the day — a guarded household, the Stone upstairs, the alarm one wrong move away from pulling everyone in the building into the stairwell. Or they can wait and enter that night, walking into a battle already in progress, with three factions contesting a building full of bodies.

The party should come out with the Stone. Every path through the building is designed to make that outcome achievable. The Rooftop Chase is the contingency — the catch if it slips past them inside. If the Stone escapes entirely, the campaign continues from a weaker position, but it continues. They have made the next conversation harder.
