# Zhentarim Warehouse

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Combat Event occurs when the party arrives at the Candle Lane warehouse. In this Event:
>
> - The party chooses an entry approach; their choice sets the kenku alert state
> - Four kenku occupy the building, searching the wreckage of the Xanathar Guild raid that killed Manshoon's cell
> - Renaer Neverember is hidden in Z02 — the party finds the wrong person, learns what happened, and gets new leads pointing down
> - Three independent paths confirm the Xanathar sewer hideout as Floon's location
>
> For keyed room details (Z01–Z05), see the **Zhentarim Warehouse** journal.
>
> Completing this Event awards 1 Milestone Point.

### Approaching the Warehouse

The warehouse sits at the back of a yard behind a high fence. The gate is unlocked. A black winged snake is painted above the front door handle: DC 10 Intelligence identifies it as the Zhentarim symbol.

**Three entry points**, all locked (DC 12 Dexterity with thieves' tools, or DC 10 Strength [Athletics] to force):
- Front door
- Loading bay on the east side
- A painted-over window on the west wall

### Kenku Alert State

The party's approach determines whether the kenku inside are aware of them.

**Unaware state:** Party attempts entry quietly with no prior noise in the yard. Group DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check against kenku passive Perception of 13. On success, the kenku are still searching the wreckage, unaware.

**Alert state:** Triggered by forced entry, combat in the yard, or a failed approach Stealth check. Alert kenku have hidden behind overturned furniture and are watching the entry points. Group DC rises to 16.

Refer to the **Zhentarim Warehouse** journal for keyed room entries describing both states in detail.

### The Kenku

Four kenku were left here by Xanathar's Guild to kill any Zhentarim who return. The Zhentarim cell that used this warehouse is already dead — killed in the raid. The kenku do not know the party is not Zhentarim; they are hostile on sight in alert state, suspicious in unaware state.

Kenku speak only in mimicked voices — fragments captured from conversations they have overheard. Under DC 10 Charisma (Intimidation), a captured or subdued kenku repeats, in three different voices:

- "Xanathar sends its regards." (deep, orcish-accented)
- "Tie up the pretty boy in the back room!" and "Follow the yellow signs in the sewers." (thin, nasally)
- "No time to loot the place. Just get him to the boss." (scratchy)

### Finding Renaer

**Renaer Neverember** (see Appendix A) is in Z02. He is unarmed, bruised, cooperative, and waiting for the kenku to be dealt with before he attempts to leave. When the kenku are handled and the building is confirmed clear, he emerges and speaks freely.

**What Renaer shares without prompting:**
- Five Zhentarim grabbed him and Floon outside Old Xoblob Shop two nights ago and brought them here
- They questioned him about his father's embezzled gold — money Renaer has refused to help his father hide
- They took his mourning locket, which he believed held a keepsake from his mother; he did not know there was a hidden compartment
- The Xanathar Guild attacked before the interrogation went further
- He slipped his bonds in the confusion and hid in Z02
- Through a gap in the door he watched Xanathar agents drag an unconscious Floon through the main room, out through the yard, and down the alley toward a sewer grate

**What Renaer does not know:** what the locket contained; where the Vault is; the Stone of Golorr's role in any of this. His ignorance is genuine.

**If asked about his father's enemies:** The Cassalanters have been quietly acquiring properties near the Brandath family tombs — his mother's family plots. He cannot explain it. He raises it as an oddity, not an accusation.

### Revelation #3 — Floon Is in the Xanathar Sewer Hideout

Three independent paths, all confirming the same route.

#### Path A — Renaer's Account

He watched Xanathar agents drag Floon through the main room, out through the yard, and down the alley to a sewer grate. He describes them: circle-eye tattoos, matching the dead Xanathar thugs in Z01. No check required.

#### Path B — Yellow Guildsign

Xanathar's agents left guildsign on the alley wall outside the warehouse yard, pointing toward the sewer grate half a block east. DC 11 Wisdom (Survival) to track their route through the yard to the grate; the guildsign is visible at the grate itself without a check once the party reaches it.

#### Path C — Kenku Interrogation

A captured kenku repeats under DC 10 Charisma (Intimidation): "Follow the yellow signs in the sewers" and "Just get him to the boss." A kenku that agrees to guide the party will attempt to betray them at the first opportunity — alerting the Q02 goblins before it can be stopped.

### Concluding the Event

#### Renaer Neverember Found: Renaer Alive at Arc Completion

Renaer is found in Z02 and survives the arc. He becomes a recurring contact in Arc B and beyond. His account of the locket seizure establishes Manshoon's cell's objective — the Eye of the Stone of Golorr.

#### Warehouse Cleared: All Kenku Dealt With

The kenku are neutralized (defeated, driven off, or subdued). The party controls the warehouse. Any loot in Z03 (four paintings, fifteen silver trade bars) is now accessible without contest.

## Overview

The Candle Lane warehouse held a surprise: the Zhentarim cell that kidnapped Floon is already dead, killed in an Xanathar Guild raid. Four kenku now search the wreckage. The party finds not Floon but Renaer Neverember — the Open Lord's estranged son, who was the kidnapping's actual target.

## Read Aloud

> A high fence around a warehouse yard. A black winged snake above the door. Inside: twelve corpses along the walls, overturned furniture, a room that was recently a crime scene and is now someone else's problem.

## Summary

The party cleared the Candle Lane warehouse, found Renaer Neverember alive in a back room, and learned that Floon was dragged through a sewer grate half a block east. The Zhentarim cell that ran this place is dead. Xanathar's people took Floon — mistaking him for Renaer.
