# The Yawning Portal

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social Event occurs when the party arrives at the Yawning Portal to meet their contact. In this Event:
>
> - Threestrings (Harper agent embedded at the Yawning Portal) introduces the party's situation and awaits Volo's arrival
> - A bar brawl erupts between Yagra Stonefist (Doom Raiders) and Krentz's Xanathar Guild crew
> - Brandoch descends into Undermountain; the crowd performs the Going Down Song
> - A troll hauls itself out of the well with six stirges attached; Durnan handles the troll while the party handles the stirges
> - Volo Geddarm arrives and hires the party to find Floon Blagmaar, 10 gp upfront, 100 gp or a deed on delivery
>
> Three villain factions are visible in this room before the party accepts any job. The event has no milestone.

### The Tavern in Full Swing

Merchants, adventurers, off-duty Watch guards, dock workers. **Threestrings** (Mattrim Mereg) performs near the back wall, a lute strung on three strings, which is all he claims to need. He catches the party's eye during a break between songs and steers them to a table. "Shouldn't be long. Have a drink. Something interesting happens here every night."

**NPCs present:**

**Durnan** — behind the bar. One question answered per exchange, never initiates conversation. A DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check reveals he is tracking every table simultaneously while appearing to focus on his work. He does not explain himself.

**Bonnie** — barmaid. Knows every face in the room; shares tavern gossip freely. Nothing she says is untrue; she controls what she reveals.

**Two veteran adventurers at the well table** — a dwarf woman and a half-elf man, currently taking bets on whether tonight's Dipper returns. They explain the custom cheerfully to anyone who asks.

**Ryvarra** (BD observer) — a drow woman in the observation balcony, posing as a Calimshan cloth merchant, untouched glass of wine in front of her. She has been at that table every evening for two weeks, cataloguing who descends into Undermountain and who comes back. She is never named in this arc. The party notices her only if they specifically ask who is watching the well; they get a description, nothing more.

### The Bar Brawl

Within fifteen minutes of the party's arrival, Yagra Stonefist crosses the room. She has been nursing a drink while watching a table of five Xanathar Guild thugs, led by **Krentz**, a man with eye-shaped tattoos on his shaved scalp. Two nights ago, Krentz's crew killed a Doom Raiders operative in the Dock Ward. When one of Krentz's companions says something audible about snake men getting what they deserved, Yagra moves.

Before she throws the first punch: "You had no quarrel with us. Those weren't your turf. That was my friend." Krentz: "Your friend." Pause. "He's dead." First punch.

Yagra's line is the first signal that the Dock Ward killing was personal, and that she is not the same faction as whoever runs the Candle Lane warehouse. The distinction surfaces here before anyone names it.

Durnan watches until Krentz's companions pile in: one tankard flung, the word "Out," three Xanatharians carrying Krentz's unconscious body through the door.

#### Party Branch — Helps Yagra

Fight ends faster. Durnan ejects Krentz's companions with less damage to furniture. Yagra: "I had it." She acknowledges the party — "Good punch. Better timing." — and buys them a drink. If they mention looking for work, she names Davil Starsong at the Doom Raiders. She is professional. Any warmth waits until the troll fight, if it comes at all. Award **Yagra's Courtesy** attunement.

#### Party Branch — Helps Krentz or the Xanathar Thugs

Yagra turns briefly on the party before Durnan ends it. She is not hostile after, but she will not work with this party without a significant reconciliation effort in Arc B or later. No attunement.

#### Party Branch — Stays Out

Yagra knocks Krentz unconscious; the four companions beat her unconscious in return; Durnan ejects all five. Yagra wakes at the bar, assesses the party once: "Watchers." No grudge, no warmth. No attunement.

#### Party Branch — Peaceful Resolution

DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion) to interrupt the fight before the first punch. The DC is high because Yagra wanted this fight. If successful, Krentz and his companions leave muttering. Yagra: "Nice words." Back to drinking. No grudge. Award **Yagra's Courtesy** attunement.

### The Dip and the Going Down Song

With the brawl settled, an announcement: **Brandoch**, a young man, is going into Undermountain tonight. He approaches the well theatrically, checks the rope, and rings the bell once. The crowd reacts immediately.

Threestrings picks up his lute and leads the Going Down Song from the back of the room: a Yawning Portal tradition older than Durnan's tenure, upbeat, bawdy, performed in the key of C major and poor decisions. The crowd joins the chorus; the two veterans at the well table sing loudest. Durnan tolerates it.

Brandoch descends. Three minutes pass. The bell rings three times. Cheers. He made it. Threestrings plays a brief victory riff.

Then the rope goes taut again — not a bell-ring rhythm. Something is climbing.

### The Troll Fight

Brandoch appears over the rim, moving fast. Six stirges cling to him. Behind him: a troll.

Durnan vaults the bar the moment the troll's head clears the rim. Greatsword in hand: "You focus on the stirges. The troll is mine." He does not look at the party when he says it.

Yagra (if conscious) joins the troll's flank without being asked and without taking orders.

**Six stirges (CR 1/8):** distributed among party members as initial targets. Brandoch collapses at the well's edge.

#### Party Branch — Handles Stirges Only

Durnan drives the troll back into the well. "You fought well," said to any character who killed a stirge. He returns to the bar. Yagra nods once. Award **Durnan's Acknowledgment** attunement.

#### Party Branch — Also Engages the Troll

Durnan keeps the troll's primary attention, preventing it from turning fully on party members who wade in. After: "Useful. But that was mine." Said without heat. Award **Durnan's Acknowledgment** attunement at higher register. Arc B tracks the difference.

#### Party Branch — Uses Fire or Acid on the Troll

Durnan: "Good." Highest register of praise. Yagra: "Experienced. Nice." Rewarding the correct use of fire signals that the room's regulars know exactly what they're doing. Award **Durnan's Acknowledgment** attunement at highest register.

#### Party Branch — Does Nothing

Durnan and Yagra handle everything. Durnan does not address the party after. He goes back behind the bar. No attunement.

Brandoch survives. He is at the bar in shock: he turned back immediately when he reached the bottom. Something large was in the dark. Durnan gives him a drink. Regulars at nearby tables note this as extremely unusual.

### Volo's Quest

Volo pushes through the settling crowd, steered to the party's table by Threestrings. He has already decided to hire the party; the troll fight confirmed his judgment. He describes Floon Blagmaar: wavy red-blond hair, early thirties, more charming than sensible, last seen at the Skewered Dragon two nights ago wearing princely garb.

**Terms:** 10 gp per character upfront; 100 gp per character when Floon is returned alive.

**DC 10 Wisdom (Insight):** Volo is honest but the 100 gp is a stretch; he is waiting on royalties from *Volo's Guide to Monsters* while his current project (*Volo's Guide to Spirits and Specters*) goes poorly. He does not know Renaer Neverember met Floon after Volo left the Skewered Dragon.

### Concluding the Event

#### Durnan's Acknowledgment: Party Engaged the Troll or Used Fire

The party demonstrated meaningful competence in a dangerous moment. Durnan's estimation of them adjusts upward in ways that manifest subtly in Arc B. He is slightly more communicative, and the Watch guards who know him give the party slightly more benefit of the doubt.

#### Yagra's Courtesy: Party Helped Yagra or Peacefully Resolved the Brawl

Yagra extends professional courtesy. She will not pretend this was warmth, but she names Davil Starsong to the party and will not work against them in Arc B without cause.

## Overview

The party's first night in Waterdeep drops them into a bar fight between two rival criminal factions, a troll emerging from a hole in the floor, and a missing-persons job from a famous writer who cannot quite afford to pay.

## Read Aloud

> The Yawning Portal is exactly what you were warned about: loud, crowded, and smelling of spilled ale and bad decisions. A man with a lute strung on three strings waves you toward a corner table. Behind the bar, a gray-haired man built like a siege engine wipes a tankard and says nothing at all.

## Summary

The party met their contact at the Yawning Portal, witnessed a brawl between Doom Raiders and Xanathar Guild operatives, survived a troll emerging from the Undermountain well, and accepted a job from the writer Volo Geddarm to find his missing companion Floon Blagmaar, last seen in the Dock Ward two nights ago.
