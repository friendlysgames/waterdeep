# Dock Ward Investigation

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration Event occurs when the party enters the Dock Ward following Volo's lead. In this Event:
>
> - The party pursues three independent paths to Revelation #1 (who took Floon and Renaer)
> - The party pursues three formal paths to Revelation #2 (Candle Lane warehouse address)
> - Proactive failsafes engage if all paths fail — the arc cannot stall here
>
> No milestone is awarded for this Event. The event ends when the party has the warehouse address.

### Dock Ward Environment

Streets are dark — most streetlamps smashed, candles taken from doorways. Salt air and something worse underneath. Tall tenements press together until midday light barely reaches the cobblestones below. Yellow chalk eye-symbols mark a few alley walls for those who know them: DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) to spot; recognizing them as Xanathar Guild territory requires a DC 14 Intelligence (History) check or criminal/guild background.

### Revelation #1 — Who Took Floon and Renaer

Three independent paths converge on the same answer: Zhentarim thugs grabbed two men outside Old Xoblob Shop two nights ago.

#### Path A — Blood in the Streets

Before the party reaches the Xoblob Shop or the Skewered Dragon, they cross a City Watch crime scene. A dozen guards have arrested three surviving thugs from a street brawl. The prisoners wear black leather armor; flying snake tattoos are visible on their necks and forearms. The Watch waves the party off, but the tattoos are in plain view.

These are **Manshoon's Splinter Zhentarim**. They report to Kolat Towers, not to Davil Starsong. DC 13 Intelligence (History) check with any criminal, faction, or guild background identifies the tattoo as Zhentarim; distinguishing which cell is not possible from a tattoo alone at this level.

#### Path B — Old Xoblob Shop

**Xoblob** (deep gnome, Xanathar Guild informant, paranoid) witnessed the attack outside his own shop. He is reluctant; a bribe of any purple item or a DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check loosens him.

He describes five men in black leather armor grabbing Floon and a well-dressed companion outside his door. One had a winged snake tattoo on his neck. One of the attackers grabbed the companion's silver necklace — pendant shaped like a horse, or possibly a unicorn.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Xoblob's Window
>
> The stuffed beholder mounted in Xoblob's window is a magical sensor through which Xanathar occasionally views the street. This is not discoverable at Arc A level. Plant it if it fits the scene; don't press it.

#### Path C — Skewered Dragon

A dim Dock Ward dive, operated by **Manshoon's Splinter Zhentarim** as an informal ward headquarters. Regulars here remember Volo and Floon from two nights ago. After Volo left, Floon stayed and met Renaer Neverember. They drank, played Three-Dragon Ante, and left together around midnight. Five men followed them out. "Those men haven't come back," says one regular. "Flying snake tattoos."

The current regulars are Splinter men on home ground; the party is in unfriendly territory if they push too hard without social skill. They know who grabbed Floon and Renaer. They don't know where Candle Lane is — or they won't say.

### Revelation #2 — Warehouse Location on Candle Lane

Three formal independent paths converge on the same answer: a warehouse at the end of Candle Lane.

#### Path A — Neighborhood Canvass

Asking around the blocks near the Xoblob Shop and crime scene, ten to fifteen minutes of active questioning:

- **A shellfish vendor on Sail Street** (dwarf woman, taciturn): DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) to engage; if persuaded, she has seen snake-tattoo men entering a warehouse on Candle Lane for months, bringing crates at odd hours.
- **A boy selling fish wrappers near the crime scene** (eleven years old, opportunistic): claims the men are sailors from the *Scarlet Gull* at the East Dock — a red herring. He overheard a Watch guard say something about sailors and filled in the rest. Will demand 2 cp for any further details, all invented, vague, or wrong.
- **Two dockworkers eating in a doorway on Dock Street** (both nervous): they have seen the snake-tattoo men; DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) (DC 11 if the party shows any faction credential or Watch badge) confirms: a warehouse at the end of Candle Lane. "Don't say we told you."
- **An old woman at an upstairs window on a cross alley**: noticed only if the party looks up. She has been watching the crime scene all morning. Calls down unprompted that she has seen those men going in and out of the Candle Lane place for a long time. No check required.

#### Path B — Prisoner Interrogation

The three arrested Splinter thugs are still at the crime scene while wagons are arranged. The Watch will not release prisoners or allow unsupervised access.

Options for party access:
- Name Floon Blagmaar or Renaer Neverember as kidnapping victims → the Watch sergeant (DC 14 Charisma [Persuasion]) is skeptical but allows a brief supervised conversation with one cooperative prisoner
- Bribe of 5 gp to a specific guard (DC 12 Charisma [Persuasion]) → five-minute supervised exchange
- City Watch background → invoke prior service without a check, limited to two questions per prisoner

Any cooperative prisoner names Candle Lane. A prisoner who refuses requires DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation) — possible but difficult with four guards present.

#### Path C — Floon's Bead Trail

Floon's necklace, a holy symbol of Lurue worked as a unicorn bas-relief on a blue pearl chain, shed beads during the struggle outside Xoblob's shop. The beads are pale blue against dark stone; DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) to spot the trail on the cobblestones.

Following the trail leads directly to the mouth of Candle Lane and ends at the warehouse gate. If the party already knows the address from another path, the trail still confirms which building on Candle Lane.

### Proactive Failsafes

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Failsafe Conditions
>
> If the party has reached the Dock Ward and exhausted every available path without learning the warehouse address, activate one of these. Use whichever fits the current scene geography.

**Failsafe A — Splinter Intimidation.** A pair of Manshoon's Splinter thugs approaches the party at or near the Skewered Dragon and warns them to stop asking questions about the Renaer kidnapping — naming the subject they should be investigating. This surfaces both Revelations #1 and #2 simultaneously.

**Failsafe B — Street Fight.** A Splinter squad arrives on Candle Lane while the party is nearby and fights a group of kenku in the street directly in front of the warehouse, effectively delivering the address by combat and establishing that the building is contested.

### Concluding the Event

No attunements are awarded for this Event.

## Overview

The Dock Ward investigation leads through street crime scenes, a suspicious shopkeeper, and rival faction territory to a single address: a warehouse on Candle Lane where Floon was last seen.

## Read Aloud

> The Dock Ward smells of salt and old blood. Most of the streetlamps are dark — not broken, just missing their candles. Down every alley, yellow chalk marks that mean something to someone.

## Summary

The party investigated the Dock Ward and traced Floon Blagmaar's kidnapping to a warehouse on Candle Lane. The kidnappers wore Zhentarim tattoos. Xanathar Guild markings cover the ward's alley walls.
