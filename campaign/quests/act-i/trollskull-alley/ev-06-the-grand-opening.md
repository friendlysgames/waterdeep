### Staff Hiring

In the days before opening, candidates arrive for each open position. The interview process is a low-stakes social encounter with real consequences: candidates have agendas, faction affiliations, and secrets that will matter later. Full candidate tables in the **Trollskull Manor** guide, Staff and Hiring.

Key framing:
- Candidates arrive through word of mouth and guild placement notices; the party does not advertise
- An appeased Lif handles the bar himself (**Lif Appeased** attunement), eliminating the bartender position; the party may still hire living staff
- An unappeased or destroyed Lif means the bartender position is open and competitive
- One Shard Shunner wererat may return for a second interview under a different name; **DC 15 Passive Perception** or **DC 14 active Perception** to recognize them from ev-02

### The Cassalanter Bank Hook

When the party's renovation financing required a formal loan or credit arrangement, their paperwork passed through the **Cassalanter Counting House** (a Sea Ward banking operation with accounts throughout the North Ward). Two days before opening, a messenger delivers a cask of Athkatlan vintage wine with a card: *"From the house of Cassalanter. We look forward to patronizing your establishment."*

No counting house clerk sends wine of this quality without instructions from above. **Thread: The Cassalanters Exist** (second path; first path was Renaer's aside in ev-03: "the Cassalanters are generous to causes they want to be seen supporting — keep that in mind").

### Opening Night

Starting Fame rolled or set per the **Trollskull Manor** guide, Fame. Key guests:

**Neighbors** — Tally, Embric and Avi, Fala, Rishaal (all four attend). Vincent Trench sends regrets via a note and a gift of expensive pipe tobacco. Ulkoria arrives late, drinks one ale, says "Acceptable," and leaves.

**Faction contacts** — Enrolled factions' primary contacts attend; their presence signals to the room that this tavern operates under faction awareness.

**Renaer Neverember** — Genuinely pleased. He loaned money for this; the result is better than the investment.

**Floon Blagmaar** — Loses a silver piece in the kitchen dice game with the prep cook and acts as though this is surprising.

**Volo Geddarm** — Attempts a review for his next publication. Spills ink on two pages.

**Lif** (if appeased) — Award **Lif Appeased** attunement here, if not already awarded. He is behind the bar before anyone asks. Bar tools laid out. Chalk slate updated. He has decided.

### Ammalia Cassalanter

Ammalia arrives without Lord Cassalanter. She is warm, generous, and interested in the party as people. She stays two hours, learns everyone's name, and leaves with the social grace that makes a room feel larger after she's gone. She is a diabolist counting the days to Founders' Day. Play none of it.

Her warmth is genuine — she finds capable people interesting, and she pays close attention to those who have survived a city doing its best to kill them.

Award **Ammalia Cassalanter Met** attunement.

**Thread: The Cassalanters Exist** (third path). The three-path redundancy is complete.

Cross-ref the **Ammalia Cassalanter** page in Notable Figures. Design Notes: her warmth must never be undercut with signaling; see the Ammalia Design Note in design-notes.md.

### Undermountain Seeds

Three threads land here. None resolves; all are designed to accumulate below the surface.

**The Skullport Trader** — A compact, sun-dark merchant arrives late in the evening with unusual inventory: river stones that glow under arcane examination, bottles of water that never warms, a vial described as "cave-aged, original vintage, not for the squeamish." He trades from a flat-bottomed boat at Mistshore and surfaces twice a year. He asks the party whether they've noticed more activity below recently. "The harbor bottom has been busy. More than usual. Something moving." Two ales, generous tip, no name. **Thread: Undermountain Is Active** (second path). Award **Undermountain Thread Activated** attunement on first seed received.

**Durnan's Warning** — Durnan sends a one-line message through Threestrings, delivered at the end of the evening: "More activity than usual below. Nothing you need to worry about. Yet." The word "yet" is Threestrings's addition. When asked, he says he's pretty sure Durnan meant it. **Thread: Undermountain Is Active** (third path).

**Force Grey Mission 1 payoff** — If the party completed Consulting Hlam before opening night, Hlam's warning about "evil's twin" and the recent deep-harbor movement correlate when placed next to the trader's observation. The party has now heard the same signal three times from three independent sources.

## Overview

After twelve days of renovation, six guilds, seven factions, and a gladiatorial tournament, the party opened Trollskull Manor. Full house on opening night. Ammalia Cassalanter attended, stayed two hours, learned everyone's name, and was exactly as warm as the wine she sent. Durnan sent a message through Threestrings. A merchant from the harbor bottom came and went without giving his name.

## Read Aloud

> The taproom fills before the hour mark. The bar is covered in tools you didn't lay out. The chalk slate has the night's specials in handwriting that isn't yours. Ulkoria Stonemarrow drinks one ale, says "Acceptable," and leaves. Ammalia Cassalanter stays two hours and knows everyone's name when she goes. By the end of the night you realize the building feels different. Like it has decided something.

## Summary

Trollskull Manor opened to the public after twelve days of renovation. Staff was hired, three Undermountain threads were seeded at the party, Ammalia Cassalanter attended as a warm and generous first patron, and Lif — if appeased — was behind the bar without being asked.
