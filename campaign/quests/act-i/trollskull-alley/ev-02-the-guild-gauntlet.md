# The Guild Gauntlet

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Event covers the permit acquisition sequence. It runs across the first week of the renovation period. In this Event:
>
> - The party visits six guilds to obtain required permits; each is a social encounter with its own texture
> - The Watchful Order's inspector notices Lif and introduces a deadline or a formality depending on ev-01's outcome
> - The Cellarers' inspector flags the sewer access grate (T7) — a seed for Fireball response team routing
> - Emmek Frewn begins his sabotage campaign: Stage 1 imposes −2 on permit inspection DCs for the first week
> - The Carpenters' Guild negotiation is the arc's first persuasion challenge with a concrete lever
>
> This Event has no milestone.

### The Permit Checklist

**Broxley Fairkettle** (Fellowship of Innkeepers) arrives within a day of the party's arrival, permit checklist in hand and apologetic expression already deployed. **Hammond Kraddoc** (Vintners', Distillers', and Brewers' Guild) appears uninvited alongside him, with his scribe Jinny and a second list. Between them, the party has the full picture:

Required guilds: Carpenters', Roofers', and Plaisterers' Guild (structural work); Cellarers' and Plumbers' Guild (basement and plumbing); Guild of Butchers (meat supply); Bakers' Guild (bread and pastries); Launderers' Guild (linens); Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors (fire suppression ward).

Each requires a site inspection and a fee. Cross-ref **Appendix F, Part 1** for the fee table.

### Emmek Frewn's First Move

Frewn spent the day after the party arrived speaking to the Carpenters' Guild foreman and the Cellarers' inspector about the party's "property destruction history" and "possible Watch complications" from the warehouse incident in Finding Floon. These conversations do not block any permit but impose a **−2 penalty on all guild inspection DCs for the first week**. The party will not know the source unless they ask and succeed on a **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check with the relevant inspector.

The Shard Shunner wererats in halfling form are visible in the alley behind Emmek during his **The Neighbors** Event visit, casing the manor as potential staff applicants and looking for entry points. **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** to notice them now.

Cross-ref **Appendix A** (Emmek Frewn profile) for the full escalation sequence.

### The Carpenters' Guild Negotiation

The foreman's quoted renovation timeline is **18 days**. A **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** check or 20 gp above standard fee reduces it to **12 days**. A character with the Guild Artisan background or proficiency in Artisan's Tools reduces the DC by 2.

Emmek's penalty applies. The effective DC is 16 unless bypassed with the gold or background modifier.

This is the party's first negotiation in a city where knowing what you're buying matters more than the price itself.

### The Cellarers' and Plumbers' Guild

The inspector finds the **sewer access grate at T7** in the manor's cellar and connects it to the city's sewer network through infrastructure predating the current building by at least two centuries. He flags it for "structural heritage documentation" — not a blocking condition, but it goes on the permit record.

Award **Sewer Grate Documented** attunement. This passage becomes relevant when faction response teams use the sewer approach, and when the party re-enters the sewer network in Fireball.

### The Watchful Order Inspection

The Order's inspector was dispatched because Rishaal filed a poltergeist notice during his own guild duties. He notices Lif within thirty seconds of entering the taproom. His report notation: "Haunting: active. Benign or hostile: TBD."

The Order requires written attestation that the haunting poses no public safety risk. Two qualified signatories in the alley: Rishaal the Page-Turner or Ulkoria Stonemarrow (both Watchful Order members in good standing).

| Lif State | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Appeased (ev-01 behavioral track active) | Follow-up inspection is a formality; 3 gp paperwork fee |
| Destroyed | Inspector adds a 25 gp disposal surcharge for "unauthorized poltergeist elimination" |
| Unresolved | This becomes a permit deadline — attestation required before operational license is issued |

### Other Guild Beats

**Justyn Rassk** (Guild of Butchers) delivers meat, demands a 3 gp gratuity above guild fees, and implies unpleasant things about future deliveries if he doesn't get it. This is a bluff — he has no capacity to follow through. **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** to read it.

**Hammond Kraddoc** appears at the Vintners' Guild inspection, chastises the party's current beverage selection, and suggests two specific guild products "the guild is emphasizing this tenday." Buying both reduces the first month's supply rate by 5 gp. He will never acknowledge this as a social visit.

Cross-ref **Appendix A** (Broxley Fairkettle profile, Hammond Kraddoc profile).

### Concluding the Event

#### Sewer Grate Documented: True / False

The Cellarers' inspector's flag is on the permit record. The T7 grate connects the manor's cellar to the city sewer network. Cross-ref **Fireball** (response team routing, sewer re-entry).

## Overview

Broxley Fairkettle and Hammond Kraddoc arrived on the same morning with two different checklists and the same expectation: that the party would visit six guilds, pass six inspections, and pay the associated fees before a single drink could be legally served. Emmek Frewn had already spoken to two of those inspectors.

## Read Aloud

> The permit checklist has seven items. The guild fees are due on inspection, not on completion. The Carpenters' foreman wants eighteen days and an extra twenty gold. The butcher wants a tip he is not owed. The Watchful Order's inspector wants to know if your poltergeist has a safety certification. Broxley Fairkettle keeps apologizing. Hammond Kraddoc keeps making notes.

## Summary

The party navigated six guild inspections and discovered that their competition had a head start — Emmek Frewn had already spoken to two inspectors about them. The sewer grate in the manor's cellar was flagged for heritage documentation. The Watchful Order needs a poltergeist attestation before they issue the fire suppression ward.
