# The Fireball

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Investigation Event opens Fireball and covers Ches 22nd — the blast itself and the full day of witness interviews that follow. In this Event:
>
> - The fireball kills twelve: Dalakhar, two Zhentarim, eight bystanders, and the Trollskull Alley victim.
> - The party has five minutes before the Watch seals the scene and controls access.
> - Seven witnesses spread across the alley hold distinct evidence fragments, available in any order throughout the day.
> - Renaer Neverember arrives within two hours carrying Dalakhar's paper bird — the arc's proactive failsafe for Revelation #2.
> - Three independent paths to Revelation #1 (a nimblewright caused the explosion) run through this event.
> - Three independent paths to Revelation #2 (the Stone of Golorr) begin here and extend into subsequent events.
> - All four villain faction response teams activate.
> - The Watch takes custody of all bodies by midmorning; *Speak with Dead* access methods are noted.
>
> **Trigger:** The party is at Trollskull Manor when the blast occurs, or returns to find the Watch already on scene. Either way, the five-minute window either begins or has already partially elapsed when they arrive.

### The Explosion

The fireball detonated at first light on Ches 22nd — a 20-foot-radius sphere of fire in Trollskull Alley, 8d6 fire damage, DC 15 Dexterity saving throw for any character in the blast area. The party is entitled to this save if they were in the alley; PCs inside Trollskull Manor are outside the radius.

Twelve bodies in the blast area when the smoke clears:

- **Dalakhar** — male rock gnome; burned cloak; boots and hem caked with dried gray sewer silt; clutching a dagger in a scorched fist; five 100 gp gems in a belt pouch. His pockets are turned out and emptied — someone searched the body after the blast, in a hurry.
- **Two cloaked male humans in leather armor** — one has a winged snake tattooed on his right forearm. The Black Network brand.
- **Two female humans and one male half-elf in plain clothes** — North Ward servants running early errands.
- **Four halflings** — two women who had been playing fiddle and flute; one man who had been dancing to the music; one boy, twelve years old, who had been dancing beside him. His name was Sarlo. He and Martem ran the same alley routes every morning.
- **One elderly female human in a gray traveling cloak** — her morning walk.
- **The Trollskull Alley victim** — the neighbor the party bonded with across Trollskull Alley. Dead. No ambiguity, no last-second survival. The selection was made before this arc began. Play the discovery straight.

The nimblewright threw the bead from the cooperage rooftop to the east, dropped to the street, rifled Dalakhar's body, took the Stone of Golorr, and fled east across the rooftops. Floxin was north of the blast radius — badly burned, conscious.

### The Investigation Window

Five minutes before the first six City Guard soldiers arrive and seal the alley. Twenty minutes before **Barnibus Blastwind** (meticulous dwarf mage, Watchful Order) and **Sergeant Saeth Cromley** appear with twenty constables and formally take charge.

After the Watch seals the scene, accessing the bodies requires DC 15 Stealth (avoid notice) or Watch cooperation (see below). Bodies are removed to the North Ward Watch Station by midmorning; *Gentle Repose* is cast on each.

#### Crime Scene Evidence

Four layers accessible during the five-minute window:

**Surface (no check):** Twelve bodies. Blast crater. Scorch ring, 20-foot radius. Every ground-floor window within thirty feet shattered.

**DC 15 Wisdom (Perception):** Three findings:
1. The Black Network tattoo on one of the dead humans — a Zhentarim operative.
2. Sewer silt dried on Dalakhar's boots and cloak hem — he came up through the sewers recently.
3. The rain barrel at the east end of the blast radius holds something metal. This is the *necklace of fireballs* (two beads remaining, broken clasp) — the chain caught on a chimney as the nimblewright fled east and the necklace fell into the barrel. **Martem Trec has already retrieved it.** A party that searches the barrel finds it empty; asking nearby witnesses about the barrel produces Martem.

**DC 14 Wisdom (Perception):** Dalakhar's pockets are turned out and emptied — someone searched the body after the blast, in haste.

**DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana):** The scorch pattern in the blast crater carries a construct-origin transmutation trace. The incendiary device was wielded by something nonmagical carrying a magic item — not a spellcaster, not any known humanoid or monster source. The signature is mechanically precise in a way that rules out every non-construct option. This is Revelation #1's direct evidence path.

#### Combination — Clue to Revelation #1

The Arcana trace (DC 16) combined with the broken necklace at the east end of the blast establishes:
- The bomb was thrown from the east, from above.
- The thrower was a nonmagical construct carrying a magic item.
- Result: a party with both findings can name "nimblewright" with a DC 13 Arcana or History check, or confirm the type when Floxin describes the "metal man."

### Urstul Floxin

Found north of the blast, badly burned. Gives his real name when asked — his cover is already burned worse than his coat. Merchant cover story collapses at DC 14 Insight. He watched what happened clearly: a metal man dropped from the cooperage roof, went directly to the gnome's body, took something clutched in one hand, and ran east across the rooftops.

His account is accurate because a false description would produce a false lead. He wants the Stone tracked so the Zhentarim can follow. Parties that pass DC 14 Insight can see this calculation behind the cooperation; they must decide what to do with a useful witness who is also a tail.

If left unattended, Floxin slips away within the hour. If detained, the Watch releases him within 24 hours — no grounds to hold a witness.

### The Watch Takes Over

**Barnibus Blastwind** reaches three private conclusions he will not share voluntarily:
1. The gnome was running from armed pursuers (at least three people).
2. All of them were moving toward the tavern at the end of the alley.
3. The blast originated from above and to the east.

DC 15 Persuasion extracts any one conclusion per successful check. He does not share all three at once. He is not withholding from malice — he is preserving the integrity of his investigation.

**Sergeant Cromley** directs the constable canvass. He has nothing useful to share and does not try.

### Three Independent Clue Paths to Revelation #1

The party needs to conclude that a nimblewright caused the explosion. Three independent paths reach this conclusion:

**Path A — Crime Scene Evidence.** DC 16 Arcana on the scorch pattern + the broken necklace from Martem establishes a nonmagical construct wielding a magic item. DC 13 Arcana or History names the type as a nimblewright.

**Path B — Floxin's Testimony.** Floxin's "metal man" description given freely. DC 13 Arcana or History identifies a nimblewright from his description.

**Path C — Witness Synthesis.** Cross-referencing Jezrynne (cooperage roof, "moved like a puppet on wires with no wires"), Martem ("a person made of metal, like a clock"), and Fala (burned man stooping over the gnome after the construct had already fled) produces a consistent construct description from three independent vantage points. A party that registered the nimblewright at the Twin Parades (Trollskull Alley ev-05 / ev-06) identifies it without a check.

Any one path is sufficient to trigger ev-02 (The House of Inspired Hands). All three can be reached during this event.

### The Witness Pool

Seven witnesses with distinct evidence fragments. The party can speak with them in any order. Most speak without a check; specific DCs apply where a witness is reluctant, traumatized, or concealing information. Interviews continue throughout the day alongside and after the five-minute investigation window.

#### Fala Lefaliir

Greenhouse proprietor, second floor above Trollskull Alley. Was watering plants when the blast blew out the windows. Saw a cloaked man stoop over one of the bodies — the gnome — after the blast, badly burned, limping north toward the Bent Nail, looking back over his shoulder. This is Urstul Floxin.

No check needed; Fala is shaken and willing.

**Important:** Fala saw Floxin, not the nimblewright. The nimblewright had already taken the Stone and fled before Floxin reached the body. This distinction matters when the party cross-references her account with Jezrynne's.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Fala Victim Fallback
>
> If Fala was the Trollskull Alley victim, she is dead in the alley. Substitute **Tally Fellbranch** as this witness — she watched the blast from the Bent Nail's doorway and provides the same account of the burned man limping north. The information is identical; the NPC differs.

#### Jezrynne Hornraven

Noblewoman. Had just left the Tiger's Eye detective office when the blast hit. Saw the nimblewright on the cooperage rooftop — it moved wrong, like a puppet on wires with no wires, had something in its hand, threw it down into the street, then ran east across the rooftops.

No check needed for the sighting itself. DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) to identify her description as consistent with a nimblewright — this is the synthesis check; simply identifying the type from her account.

**Note:** Her description of the construct running east narrows the origin to the cooperage roof, not the alley floor. Combined with the DC 16 Arcana crime scene trace, this eliminates "spellcaster standing in the alley" as an alternative.

#### Martem Trec

Twelve years old. Hiding behind the rain barrel at the east end of the blast radius — not in the street with the adult witnesses. Finding him requires active searching (DC 12 Investigation of the blast area or interviewing neighbors about where the children were) or a neighbor's direction.

Sarlo was there too — dancing to the music with the adults, the way he always did on warm mornings. Martem watched him die. He has the *necklace of fireballs*; it fell into his rain barrel when the nimblewright's chain caught on a chimney as it fled east.

He describes the construct without prompting if asked what he saw: "A person made of metal, like a clock, that ran across the roof and dropped the bead that killed them and didn't look back at all, not even once." He does not say Sarlo's name. He says "them."

**DC 8 Persuasion** (Advantage if the party shows genuine compassion rather than reaching for the item) to convince him to hand over the necklace. On a failure, he pulls it back — not because he wants it, but because he is not ready to trust a stranger with something this dangerous. He will try again later if the party returns.

**The Necklace:** 1 foot of chain, two beads remaining, clasp bent and broken. Wondrous item, uncommon. Each bead can be hurled up to 60 feet as an action; DC 12 Dexterity save, 4d6 fire damage on a failed save, half on a success. The broken clasp is a vulnerability — any failed Dexterity saving throw the carrier takes (from any source) requires them to also make DC 12 Dexterity or a random bead falls loose and detonates.

#### Vincent Trench / "The Rake"

The Tiger's Eye detective agency, across from the cooperage. Arrived after the explosion. DC 15 Insight: he knows more than he's saying — his affect is too calm, his eyes slightly too still.

DC 16 Persuasion: confirms he saw the construct from his window — "a construct of Lantanese manufacture, moving with purpose, heading for the harbor." He is a rakshasa. What he shares is accurate. What he withholds is that he recognized the nimblewright immediately and already knows who builds them and for whom.

He will not be persuaded beyond this threshold. Attempting magical compulsion on him fails (he is immune to such effects); he simply becomes unavailable for conversation.

#### Rishaal the Page-Turner

Book Wyrm's Treasure. No visual evidence of the blast — he was in the back of the shop with the morning delivery. Within an hour of being asked about mechanical constructs and the explosion, he produces a Lantanese engineering text from his stock: *Gond's Artifice: Mechanical Life in the Lantanese Tradition*, specifically the chapter on nimblewrights.

The text names the House of Inspired Hands (corner of Seawatch and Shark Streets, Sea Ward) as Waterdeep's only active temple of Gond and the foremost authority on Lantanese mechanical constructs in the city.

No check needed. Rishaal is a bookseller; this is what booksellers do.

#### Tally Fellbranch / Embric and Avi

Supporting witnesses. Tally saw the flash and the smoke from the Bent Nail's doorway. Embric noticed a glint of silver moving east across the rooftops a moment before the blast. Avi confirms the timing.

No single critical clue, but their accounts establish the timeline (the roof movement preceded the blast) and confirm the direction of flight from a third independent vantage point. Combined with Jezrynne and Martem, three witnesses independently place something moving east across the rooftops at the moment of the explosion.

### Renaer's Testimony

Renaer arrives within two hours of learning about the explosion — either from the party directly or from neighborhood conversation. He is pale and controlled. He carries Dalakhar's paper bird, sent that morning before the blast: *the Stone is in my hands; multiple factions are pursuing me; I am coming to Trollskull Manor because Renaer vouched for you.*

Renaer knows the *Stone of Golorr* is "the key to everything" but not about the Eyes or the vault's location. He carries guilt for not acting on the message immediately — he read it and set it aside for an hour.

When the party mentions Fala's (or Tally's) account of the burned man fleeing north, Renaer reaches out to Harper contacts at once. Within one day (the morning of Ches 23rd at the latest), he returns: the burned man is **Urstul Floxin**, Manshoon's chief field agent in Waterdeep. A North Ward resident saw Floxin — visibly burned and limping — enter Gralhund Villa on Saerdoun Street within hours of the blast. The City Watch sent two constables who were told everything was fine. No warrant was issued.

### Player Faction Contact Reactions

Each active faction contact responds to the explosion in their own register. Deliver as flying snake messages, chance encounters, or requested meetings.

**Harpers — Mirt (Renown 1+):** Takes a Harper member aside privately at some point before the party reaches the Sea Maidens Faire. The Cassalanters funded the Howling Hatred cult three years ago; he extracted their agents from Watch custody as a political courtesy. He is sharing it now because he is worried, which is different from being certain.

**Lords' Alliance — Jalester Silvermane (Renown 1+):** When the party's investigation reaches the Faire or the harbor district, Jalester mentions without pressure that the Sea Maidens Faire uses private docking arrangements that circumvent standard Watch inspection protocols. He is noting it as an irregularity someone should eventually care about.

**Emerald Enclave — Melannor Fellbranch (Renown 1+):** Passes along a report from Jeryth Phaulkon: she perceived a sharp psychic disturbance somewhere in the Castle Ward the night of the fireball. Cannot pinpoint the source. May not be connected to the nimblewright. Melannor is not certain enough to say it is.

**Order of the Gauntlet — Savra Belabranta (Renown 1+):** Contacts the party directly, without pleasantries. She was tracking a supply delivery route that crossed Trollskull Alley that morning. She wants to know if anyone was collecting from the bodies or watching the scene before the constables arrived. She has a list of names she suspects; she will not share it until the party's account confirms or complicates any of them.

**Force Grey — Vajra Safahr (Renown 1+):** A note the morning after. The explosion's magical residue carries a signature the Watchful Order describes as a nimblewright detonation — registered Lantanese manufacture, auditable through the House of Inspired Hands. Vajra wants the party's firsthand account of the blast site. She asks for what they saw, specifically, in sequence.

**Doom Raiders — Davil Starsong (Renown 1+):** Davil is still free during Fireball. He identifies Floxin immediately as Manshoon's blade. He suspects Floxin filed an anonymous Watch tip naming the Doom Raiders as persons of interest — damage control meant to draw Watch heat away from the Splinter cell. He wants proof of Floxin's direct involvement. He also wants to know if anyone arrived at the blast site early — before the Watch, before the other witnesses — and behaved in a way that suggested foreknowledge. He has a suspicion he is not ready to name.

**Bregan D'Aerthe (entry condition — party is visibly investigating):** Shortly after the party begins asking questions — in the Alley, at the House of Inspired Hands, in the harbor district — a folded note on Seven Masks Theater stationery arrives by unmarked runner: two tickets to tonight's performance, no explanation, no name. Any drow party member receives it directly. If no drow are present, it is addressed to "the group from the Alley."

### Villain Faction Response Teams

All four villain factions move on the crime scene within hours of the explosion.

- **Xanathar's Guild:** Gazers post to watch Trollskull Alley. DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) to notice a floating eyeball behind a chimney. They are collecting intelligence, not acting.
- **Manshoon's Zhentarim:** A parallel investigation runs through the Black Network's city contact network. The party may notice strangers asking the same questions in the same order (DC 14 Insight if they cross paths with a Zhent investigator at a witness).
- **Bregan D'Aerthe:** Observers activate conditionally — engagement scales with whether the party later reaches the Sea Maidens Faire.
- **Cassalanters:** Flowers and a warm handwritten condolence note delivered to Trollskull Manor — Ammalia's handwriting. No return address on the card.

## Overview

A fireball killed twelve people in Trollskull Alley at dawn. One of them was someone the party knew. Five minutes to read the scene before the Watch arrives — then a full day of witnesses, each with a piece of the picture.

## Read Aloud

> The sound arrives before the light — a concussive pressure that rattles every window on the alley and shoves you back a step even through the wall. When you get to the door, the smoke is already rising. Twelve bodies. The halfling women who played fiddle and flute on warm evenings lie in the street twenty feet apart, the instruments scattered and burned beyond use. The gnome at the center of the blast crater has his pockets turned out. Someone was in a hurry.
>
> You have maybe five minutes before you hear the whistles.

## Summary

A fireball detonated in Trollskull Alley at first light on Ches 22nd, killing twelve — including the Trollskull Alley victim. The crime scene, the witnesses, and Dalakhar's paper bird in Renaer's hands told the same story from three directions: a metal figure on the cooperage roof, a stolen Stone, every faction in Waterdeep already moving. The investigation had a category and a direction by nightfall. It needed one more day to get an address.
