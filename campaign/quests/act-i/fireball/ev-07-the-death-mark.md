# The Death Mark

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Optional Exploration Event covers the investigation of Dalakhar's last lodging at the Inn of the Dripping Dagger. In this Event:
>
> - Four access methods to Dalakhar's room, each playing differently.
> - Dalakhar's room was professionally scrubbed; DC 12 Investigation yields four findings.
> - Xanathar's death mark sits on the pillow — a warning left for a man who never came back.
> - The Xanathar response team approaches afterward if the party enters; intelligence shared scales with how many days have elapsed.
> - Four faction contacts react to the death mark with distinct intelligence fragments.
> - Kalain's Letter is the delayed trigger for ev-08 — fires if the party left contact information with Myl Dunpier.
>
> **Trigger:** The party investigates Dalakhar's movements and tracks him to the Inn of the Dripping Dagger (DC 15 Charisma (Investigation) over a day's inquiry through inns and lodging houses in the Trades Ward, or a faction tip from Mirt or Jalester).

### The Inn of the Dripping Dagger

A modest travelers' inn on Snail Street in the Trades Ward. The name refers to an icicle, not a blade — the sign outside confirms this with somewhat more art than the name suggests.

**Proprietor:** Myl Dunpier. Middle-aged woman, ink-stained fingers from the ledgers she keeps for the city accountant's office on her off days. Takes no interest in her guests' business; considerable interest in being paid on time. She remembers Dalakhar clearly — he was small, alone, paid in coin, asked for an upper-floor room with a window visible from the street, and left before breakfast though he had prepaid through the evening.

### Access to Dalakhar's Room

**Method A — Honest Request (DC 14 Persuasion):** The party explains truthfully that the gnome is dead and they are investigating the explosion. Myl allows access if the party promises not to remove anything, and she accompanies them. On a failure, she declines — she has no reason to let strangers into a paying guest's room, even a dead one. Try again with a different framing or try another method.

**Method B — Watch Authority:** Renown 1+ in any Watch-affiliated faction, or a Watch badge obtained at the crime scene (from Cromley or Barnibus), grants automatic entry. Myl knows her obligations.

**Method C — Bribery:** 5 gp buys access without conversation. She takes the coin, hands over the key, goes back to her ledger. 1 gp does not suffice; she is not insulted, just unavailable.

**Method D — Stealth:** DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) to locate the room by smell — sewer silt, distinctive, in the upper hallway corridor. DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) on the door lock. Standard inn lock, not unusual construction.

### Dalakhar's Room

Scrubbed clean before he left — professional habit. Sheets tucked tight, basin dried, wax drippings scraped away from the candleholder. The room is unremarkable. DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) finds four things:

1. **Fine gray ash** in the oil lamp's reservoir housing. Correspondence was burned here — cannot be reconstituted without *Identify* or *Legend Lore*. He destroyed his papers before leaving.
2. **Scratch pattern** under the writing table where something small was hidden. Whatever was there, he took it with him.
3. **Window latch tooling marks** on the exterior side, roughly two days old. Someone tested the latch from outside — a surveillance team establishing a route.
4. **The death mark.** On the pillow, placed deliberately: a **disk of black stone**, flat, polished, palm-sized. One face painted with a stylized eye — ringed iris, vertical slit pupil. Xanathar's personal sigil. *We know where you sleep. We could have killed you.* The agents were working from stale intelligence when they left it. Dalakhar never saw it; he was already gone. DC 13 Intelligence (History) or DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) to identify the symbol as Xanathar's personal sigil.

### The Xanathar Response Team

The room is under active surveillance. If the party enters and searches, the team approaches them in the common room downstairs or on the street afterward — not in the room itself.

The team consists of two Guild members in civilian clothes. They probe what the party knows, not what the party found. They share intelligence in exchange for intelligence, unconsciously — each piece the party provides yields a fragment in return.

**Intelligence level scales with time elapsed:**

| Days since Ches 22nd | Team's Intel State | What They Share |
|---|---|---|
| 1+ days | Briefed on Dalakhar's death; knows Floxin was following him | Inadvertently confirms Floxin as a pursuer; can mention Floxin staying at the Yellowspire safe house (North Ward) → leads to Gralhund Villa if the party hasn't found it yet |
| 3+ days | Team has been pulled | Room empty; no contact |

### Faction Contact Responses to the Death Mark

Show the mark to faction contacts. Four distinct reactions, each yielding different intelligence:

**Mirt (Harpers, Renown 1+):** Identifies the mark without hesitation — he has seen them before. Shares a rough map of observed Guild patrol patterns in the Trades Ward. Notes that Harper intelligence in this district may be tainted: Manshoon has placed double agents in the network. He says this quietly and does not elaborate.

**Vajra Safahr (Force Grey, Renown 1+):** Interested in what the mark implies about the Guild's operational sophistication — they were tracking Dalakhar in his lodgings. She has a partial dossier on Dalakhar: confirmed his role as Neverember's agent, notes he infiltrated Xanathar's organization before going dark. **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** to receive the dossier. It confirms Dalakhar was already a known quantity to Force Grey; they were not acting on the information.

**Jalester Silvermane (Lords' Alliance, Renown 1+):** Reports to Laeral Silverhand. Returns within two hours with a confirmation: Guild is operating above street level, possible Watch leak involved in the soft inquiry at Gralhund Villa. Offers 72-hour Watch ID papers (advantage on Persuasion checks with constables). Notes independently — as a third path to the Gralhund address if needed — that a North Ward household matching Gralhund Villa's description has been flagged as a possible Black Network safe house.

**Davil Starsong (Doom Raiders, Renown 1+):** Examines the mark with professional detachment. Confirms Guild death marks have been increasing over the last month in the Trades Ward. He is calculating whether the same intelligence leak that exposed Dalakhar's movements might expose his own cell's operations next — and whether Floxin filed the anonymous Watch tip naming the Doom Raiders as persons of interest, drawing Watch heat away from the Splinter cell. He does not share this calculation with the party unless pressed (DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)).

### Kalain's Letter — Delayed Trigger

Four days after Dalakhar's death (Ches 26th), a letter arrives at the Inn of the Dripping Dagger addressed to him. It is from Kalain.

**If the party left their names with Myl Dunpier** and asked her to contact them if anything arrived for the gnome: Myl sends a street child to Trollskull Manor with the sealed letter. The party receives it.

**If the party left no contact information:** The letter sits uncollected behind the bar. It is there if the party returns to the inn and asks.

The letter is personal — Kalain writes as if she and Dalakhar are resuming a conversation from years ago. She mentions Neverember's name twice. The address at the top of the letter is a tower near the harbor.

## Overview

Dalakhar's last room at the Inn of the Dripping Dagger held ash from burned correspondence, marks of a surveillance team's entry, and Xanathar's death mark on the pillow — left for a man who never came back. The Guild was watching him. So was everyone else.

## Summary

The investigation traced Dalakhar to his final lodging, where Xanathar's agents had left a death mark he never saw. His room was scrubbed clean; his papers burned; his correspondence gone. Faction contacts identified the mark and provided their own intelligence fragments. A letter from Kalain arrived four days after his death, addressed to him, and opened a path to the last piece of the vault puzzle.
