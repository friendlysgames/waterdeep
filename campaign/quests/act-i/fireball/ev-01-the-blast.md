# The Blast

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Investigation Event opens Arc C and occurs the morning of Ches 22nd, at first light. In this Event:
>
> - The fireball kills twelve: Dalakhar, two Zhentarim, eight bystanders, and the Arc B victim.
> - The party has five minutes before the Watch seals the scene and controls access.
> - Four evidence layers are available in that window; two lead to Revelation #1 (a nimblewright caused the explosion).
> - Urstul Floxin survives and gives a freely-offered account of the "metal man" that fled east.
> - The Watch takes custody of all bodies by midmorning; *Speak with Dead* access methods are noted.
>
> **Trigger:** The party is at Trollskull Manor when the blast occurs, or returns to find the Watch already on scene. Either way, the five-minute window either begins or has already partially elapsed when they arrive.

### The Explosion

The fireball detonated at first light on Ches 22nd — a 20-foot-radius sphere of fire in Trollskull Alley, 8d6 fire damage, DC 15 Dexterity saving throw for any character in the blast area. The party is entitled to this save if they were in the alley; PCs inside Trollskull Manor are outside the radius.

Twelve bodies in the blast area when the smoke clears:

- **Dalakhar** — male rock gnome; burned cloak; boots and hem caked with dried gray sewer silt; clutching a dagger in a scorched fist; five 100 gp gems in a belt pouch. His pockets are turned out and emptied — someone searched the body after the blast, in a hurry.
- **Two cloaked male humans in leather armor** — one has a winged snake tattooed on his right forearm. The Black Network brand.
- **Two female humans and one male half-elf in plain clothes** — North Ward servants running early errands.
- **Four halflings** — two women who had been playing fiddle and flute; two men who had been dancing to the music.
- **One elderly female human in a gray traveling cloak** — her morning walk.
- **The Arc B victim** — the neighbor the party bonded with across Arc B. Dead. No ambiguity, no last-second survival. The selection was made before this arc began. Play the discovery straight.

The nimblewright threw the bead from the cooperage rooftop to the east, dropped to the street, rifled Dalakhar's body, took the Stone of Golorr, and fled east across the rooftops. Floxin was north of the blast radius — badly burned, conscious.

### The Investigation Window

Five minutes before the first six City Guard soldiers arrive and seal the alley. Twenty minutes before **Barnibus Blastwind** (meticulous dwarf mage, Watchful Order) and **Sergeant Saeth Cromley** appear with twenty constables and formally take charge.

After the Watch seals the scene, accessing the bodies requires DC 15 Stealth (avoid notice) or Watch cooperation (see below). Bodies are removed to the North Ward Watch Station by midmorning; *Gentle Repose* is cast on each.

#### Crime Scene Evidence

Four layers accessible during the five-minute window:

**Surface (no check):** Twelve bodies. Blast crater. Scorch ring, 20-foot radius. Every ground-floor window within thirty feet shattered.

**DC 15 Wisdom (Perception):** Three findings:
1. The Black Network tattoo on one of the dead humans — a Zhentarim operative.
2. Sewer silt dried on Dalakhar's boots and cloak hem — he came up through the sewers recently.
3. The rain barrel at the east end of the blast radius holds something metal. This is the *necklace of fireballs* (two beads remaining, broken clasp) — the chain caught on a chimney as the nimblewright fled east and the necklace fell into the barrel. **Martem Trec has already retrieved it.** A party that searches the barrel finds it empty; asking nearby witnesses about the barrel produces Martem.

**DC 14 Wisdom (Perception):** Dalakhar's pockets are turned out and emptied — someone searched the body after the blast, in haste.

**DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana):** The scorch pattern in the blast crater carries a construct-origin transmutation trace. The incendiary device was wielded by something nonmagical carrying a magic item — not a spellcaster, not any known humanoid or monster source. The signature is mechanically precise in a way that rules out every non-construct option. This is Revelation #1's direct evidence path.

#### Combination — Clue to Revelation #1

The Arcana trace (DC 16) combined with the broken necklace at the east end of the blast establishes:
- The bomb was thrown from the east, from above.
- The thrower was a nonmagical construct carrying a magic item.
- Result: a party with both findings can name "nimblewright" with a DC 13 Arcana or History check, or confirm the type when Floxin describes the "metal man."

### Urstul Floxin

Found north of the blast, badly burned. Gives his real name when asked — his cover is already burned worse than his coat. Merchant cover story collapses at DC 14 Insight. He watched what happened clearly: a metal man dropped from the cooperage roof, went directly to the gnome's body, took something clutched in one hand, and ran east across the rooftops.

His account is accurate because a false description would produce a false lead. He wants the Stone tracked so the Zhentarim can follow. Parties that pass DC 14 Insight can see this calculation behind the cooperation; they must decide what to do with a useful witness who is also a tail.

If left unattended, Floxin slips away within the hour. If detained, the Watch releases him within 24 hours — no grounds to hold a witness.

### The Watch Takes Over

**Barnibus Blastwind** reaches three private conclusions he will not share voluntarily:
1. The gnome was running from armed pursuers (at least three people).
2. All of them were moving toward the tavern at the end of the alley.
3. The blast originated from above and to the east.

DC 15 Persuasion extracts any one conclusion per successful check. He does not share all three at once. He is not withholding from malice — he is preserving the integrity of his investigation.

**Sergeant Cromley** directs the constable canvass. He has nothing useful to share and does not try.

### Three Independent Clue Paths to Revelation #1

The party needs to conclude that a nimblewright caused the explosion. Three independent paths reach this conclusion:

**Path A — Crime Scene Evidence.** DC 16 Arcana on the scorch pattern + the broken necklace from Martem establishes a nonmagical construct wielding a magic item. DC 13 Arcana or History names the type as a nimblewright.

**Path B — Floxin's Testimony.** Floxin's "metal man" description given freely. DC 13 Arcana or History identifies a nimblewright from his description.

**Path C — Witness Synthesis.** Cross-referencing Jezrynne (cooperage roof, "moved like a puppet on wires with no wires"), Martem ("a person made of metal, like a clock"), and Fala (burned man stooping over the gnome after the construct had already fled) produces a consistent construct description from three independent vantage points. A party that registered the nimblewright at the Twin Parades (Arc B ev-05 / ev-06) identifies it without a check.

Any one path is sufficient to trigger ev-03 (The House of Inspired Hands). All three can be reached during this event and ev-02 (The Witnesses).

### Concluding the Event

#### Necklace of Fireballs: Recovered / Not Recovered

Record whether the party obtained the *necklace of fireballs* from Martem Trec. Two beads remaining. Broken clasp makes the object vulnerable — a failed Dexterity saving throw from any source while carrying it may detonate a bead (DC 12; handled in ev-02 if Martem is encountered then). Read by ev-02 (Martem introduction).

## Overview

A fireball killed twelve people in Trollskull Alley at dawn. One of them was someone the party knew. Five minutes to read the scene before the Watch arrives.

## Summary

A fireball detonated in Trollskull Alley at first light on Ches 22nd, killing twelve — including the Arc B victim. The party investigated the crime scene before the City Watch sealed it, finding evidence of a construct-origin attack. A surviving witness named Urstul Floxin provided the first description of the perpetrator: a metal man who fled east across the rooftops.
