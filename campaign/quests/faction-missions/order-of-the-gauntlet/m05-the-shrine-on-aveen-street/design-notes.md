# Design Notes: The Shrine on Aveen Street

## Sequencing Dependency

***Arc G Integration.*** This mission functions as structural setup for Arc G: Cassalanter Villa. Specifically, it generates two interconnected consequences that need to land before Arc G plays out:

1. Savra's Lords' petition places the Cassalanters under formal legal scrutiny, adding ambient pressure to the villa heist and giving the party a legal foothold they can reference in Arc G.
2. Lord Victoro's response — accelerating the Founders' Day timeline by four days — is the direct source of Arc G's compressed deadline urgency.

If the party completes Arc G before running this mission, neither consequence lands with force. The Reckoning is over. Savra's calendar intelligence arrives as history, not a live clock. The mission still plays; Mission 6 follows normally. But the timeline compression becomes backstory rather than pressure the party can feel.

The sequencing constraint is not about narrative order for its own sake. It is about whether this mission's payoff is forward-looking (it should be) or retrospective (it shouldn't). Offer it in the level 6 window, before the party has committed to the Arc G heist timeline.

## The Shrine Destroyed Flag

***Downstream Cassalanter Pressure.*** The `Shrine Destroyed: True / False` flag is read by Arc G as one element of the Cassalanter pressure state — how many footholds the family has lost before the Founders' Day confrontation. Destroying the shrine does not change the villa encounter structure, but the GM should acknowledge it in Arc G as a background Cassalanter loss the family is reacting to: one more thing they were not prepared for, one fewer resource on the day.
