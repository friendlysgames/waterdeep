# The Shrine on Aveen Street

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat/Exploration Event begins when Savra briefs the party at the Halls of Justice.
> In this Event:
>
> - The shrine entrance is a locked cellar door in an alley behind Orros's chandler's shop.
> - The basement is lit by infernal candles; a consecration circle in powdered iron filings dominates the floor.
> - A bearded devil guardian is bound to the ritual perimeter — it cannot leave, but fights without restraint inside.
> - A locked strongbox holds a secondary ledger; the primary ledger sits loose on a stone table.
> - The ledger is in Lord Victoro Cassalanter's own handwriting: twelve ritual entries, names, locations, and a Founders' Day deadline for the Reckoning.

**Background (DM only)**
The shrine is a converted basement leased two years ago through an intermediary and used intermittently as a ritual preparation site. The current configuration is a consecration circle for one of the twelve infernal rites Lord Victoro is conducting under his devil contract. The ledger constitutes the solid evidence Savra has been building toward — not a magically compelled confession, but Victoro's own handwriting naming himself.

**Orros**, the chandler upstairs, leased the basement to "a civic association." He has not been downstairs in fourteen months. He is nervous and innocent.

When the ledger leaves the shrine, Lord Victoro triggers an infernal rider in his contract — a clause that names and marks anyone who physically handled his ritual documentation. The consequences of that clause arrive in Mission 6.

### Aveen Street

The shrine entrance is a locked cellar door set in the alley behind Orros's chandler's shop. **DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** or **DC 15 Strength** to force it.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Approaching Orros
> If the party approaches the chandler's shop from the front, Orros is behind the counter, visibly uncertain. He will not obstruct access to his own alley. A **DC 10 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check** gets him to confirm he leased the basement two years ago and has not been down since. He does not ask questions he does not want the answers to.

### Inside the Shrine

The basement is lit by infernal candles producing red-tinged light. A seven-pointed consecration circle dominates the floor in powdered iron filings, blood-stained at each vertex. The smell is sulfur and something older.

Against the far wall: a locked strongbox (**DC 16 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)**; **DC 18 Strength** to force), ritual implements, and the operational ledger loose on a stone table.

The **Bearded Devil** guardian is bound to the shrine's perimeter — it cannot leave, but within those bounds it fights without restraint. Its *Beard* attack grapples and causes ongoing bleeding; a character reduced to 0 HP by it is Cursed (unable to regain HP) until a Short Rest with appropriate healing or a *Remove Curse*.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Forced Strongbox
> If the strongbox is forced rather than picked, the lock is destroyed but the ledger inside is intact. Savra takes it regardless of the strongbox's condition. The loose ledger on the stone table is the more important document; the strongbox contains supporting notes that corroborate its entries.

### The Ledger

The ledger is in Lord Victoro Cassalanter's own handwriting: twelve ritual entries, each naming a specific step, a specific location, and a date. **Gysheer Omfreys'** name appears twice. Three entries reference a Founders' Day deadline for a final "completion rite" called the Reckoning. The document does not explain what the Reckoning is.

A **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) check** confirms the ledger describes a classical Asmodean soul-bargain ritual fulfillment schedule — the type documented in Tyr's theological libraries. Savra will understand the infernal contractual structure immediately.

### The Aftermath

Savra takes the ledger directly to the Halls of Justice and files a formal petition for Lords' review of the Cassalanter family's activities under the Code Legal's provisions on infernal compact. The petition does not result in immediate arrest — the Code Legal's religious protections are extensive and the Cassalanters have excellent lawyers. But it places the family under formal legal scrutiny.

The Cassalanters are informed of the petition within 48 hours through their Masked Lord contact. Lord Victoro accelerates the Founders' Day timeline by four days. Savra calculates this from the ledger's dated entries and passes the intelligence to the party as soon as she has it: the Reckoning is now four days earlier than the party would otherwise expect.

### Concluding the Event

#### Shrine Destroyed: True / False
Record whether the party destroyed the consecration circle and the shrine's ritual contents after taking the ledger. This flag is read by **Cassalanter Villa** as a Cassalanter pressure mechanic — if the shrine was destroyed, the Cassalanters have lost one of their preparation footholds before the Founders' Day timeline compressed.

> **[GM]**
>
> **+1 Renown if** the bearded devil is destroyed or driven off and the shrine cleared.
> **+1 Renown if** the ledger is recovered intact and delivered to Savra within 24 hours.
> **+2 Renown (Earning Renown trigger) if** the ledger constitutes solid evidence of Cassalanter diabolism delivered to Savra — this triggers the +2 Earning Renown action from the faction table, regardless of how the other renown conditions resolved.

## Overview

A consecrated basement under a chandler's shop holds the evidence Savra needs — and a bound devil guarding it.

## Read Aloud

> Savra meets you at the Halls of Justice, not at Trollskull Manor. She has been here most of the morning. The Order's Cassalanter file now occupies three shelves. She is calm in the way that people are calm when they have finally reached the point where something can be done.
>
> > "The shrine on Aveen Street in the Field Ward — Gysheer named it. I cannot send Order knights into a private structure without legal grounds; this is still a shrine, protected under the Code Legal's religious property provisions. You, however, are citizens." She looks at you. "If you find physical evidence — a ritual record, a signed instruction from a named Cassalanter, anything that implicates a specific person in a specific act — I can take it to the Lords. I need it in hand, not described to me."

## Summary

The party cleared the Cassalanter shrine on Aveen Street, defeated a bound bearded devil, and recovered Lord Victoro's operational ledger. Savra filed a formal petition for Lords' review. The Founders' Day deadline in **Cassalanter Villa** compressed by four days.
