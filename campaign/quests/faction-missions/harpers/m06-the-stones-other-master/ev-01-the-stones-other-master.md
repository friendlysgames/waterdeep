# The Stone's Other Master

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social/Combat Event begins when Mirt arrives at Trollskull Manor after midnight and ends after the Splinter squad is dealt with and the Stone's fate is settled. In this Event:
>
> - Mirt asks for three days with the Stone. He is asking, not demanding. He looks tired.
> - He knows more than he says about what the Stone can do; a PC who presses directly gets the full truth.
> - A Manshoon Splinter squad of three agents moves on Trollskull Manor within two hours of Mirt's arrival — whether the party agreed or refused.
> - The squad leader's sending stone is keyed to Manshoon directly; it is intelligence as much as a combat trophy.
> - If the party complies, the three-day study yields tactical vault intelligence and, if they ask, the name of a psychically compromised contact.

**Background (DM only)**

The Stone of Golorr contains the transformed soul of the aboleth Golorr. Installing an Eye activates it, and the activation resonates with an abolethic dreamer below the city — Illuun, on Undermountain Level 4. Harper seers detected the psychic resonance and passed it to Mirt. What Mirt does not say initially: the Harpers believe the Stone can identify individuals psychically compromised by Illuun's influence, and they have two suspected assets.

The compromised individual the study will name is **Jalester Silvermane**, the Lords' Alliance operative at the Yawning Portal. Illuun's influence reached him through extended surveillance near the portal's edge. He is not aware of the compromise; he genuinely believes his reports and loyalties are his own. If the PCs have had little interaction with Jalester, the second suspected asset is **Renaer Neverember** — use whichever the party knows better. The revelation lands harder with a familiar face.

A Manshoon Splinter watcher has been tracking the Stone's magical signature since it was activated. They know Mirt is at Trollskull Manor tonight. The squad moves regardless of what the negotiation produces — they want the Stone, and Mirt's location has already been reported.

### Phase 1 — The Negotiation

Mirt sits with an untouched drink until a PC comes downstairs. When they do:

> > "Something woke up when you used the Stone. One of our seers felt it — something below the city. I need three days with the Stone before you use it to open the vault. I'll give it back. You have my word."

He is asking. He looks tired.

He explains what he knows: the Stone holds a consciousness, not just a map. That consciousness is connected to something old and patient below the city. "If you open the vault while that connection is active, you may be giving it what it has been waiting for."

**If pressed directly** — a PC who asks what else he knows, or who explicitly points out that he is holding something back — he tells them the rest. The Stone may identify psychically compromised individuals. He cannot promise the results will not touch someone the party trusts.

**Negotiating the terms:** The party can agree outright, negotiate for added intelligence, or refuse. Negotiating yields: Mirt shares Harper intelligence about the vault's secondary safeguards — things not visible on the ceremonial lock — in exchange for access. He will share this even if he is not fully trusted; he needs the three days more than he needs the upper hand.

**Refusing outright:** Mirt accepts it. "I understand. Keep it safe." He stays for the Splinter raid — he was already in the building, and he is not going to leave them to it — then departs afterward.

### Phase 2 — The Raid

Within two hours of Mirt's arrival, a Splinter squad moves on Trollskull Manor. Three agents: two **Thugs** and one **Mage**. The squad leader (Spy stat block) hangs back — in the street, watching — and carries a sending stone keyed to Manshoon directly.

**If the party agreed to hand over the Stone:** The attack begins while they are preparing the transfer. Mirt's location was reported, and the Splinter intends to take the Stone from whoever is carrying it in the street. The squad breaks cover as soon as someone steps outside with the Stone.

**If the party refused:** The Splinter attacks the tavern directly. They know the Stone is on the premises. The two Thugs breach the taproom while the Mage covers the front from the street.

The squad leader is not with the assault team. He is watching from a position that covers the building's exits. He can be spotted with a **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)** check during the fight. He flees if the assault team is neutralized — but if a PC breaks away from the combat to cut him off, he can be run down or cornered.

**The Mage's contingency:** She carries a contingency spell that triggers on taking 30+ damage: the Stone (if she has obtained it) teleports to Kolat Towers. If she never gets the Stone, the spell has nothing to attach to and does not fire.

**The squad leader's sending stone:** Keyed to a voice. If a PC has heard Manshoon speak before, a **DC 12 Intelligence** check identifies the voice on the other end of the stone. If they have not, the stone is still valuable — Mirt can use it to push false intelligence toward Manshoon, or to listen for traffic.

### Phase 3 — Resolution

**If the party complied (Stone handed to Mirt):**

Three days later, Mirt returns the Stone. The study produced:

- Confirmation of the Stone's abolethic nature. It is not a magical artifact in the conventional sense — it is a mind, imprisoned, waiting.
- Harper intelligence on one vault guardian the party had not accounted for. Mirt shares this without condition.
- If the party asks directly about the compromised individual: the Stone identified **Jalester Silvermane**. Illuun's influence reached him through extended exposure near the Yawning Portal's well. He is not a willing agent. He does not know.

What to do with the Jalester revelation is entirely the party's decision. The Alliance contact chain should be treated as potentially monitored until Jalester is removed from the field or the compromise is severed. Severing it requires destroying Illuun — an Undermountain objective.

**If the party refused:**

Mirt departs after the raid, disappointed but not hostile. The vault tactical advantage is lost. The squad leader's sending stone is still available if he was caught. Mirt: "I understand. Keep it safe." He means it.

### Concluding the Event

> **[GM]**
>
> **+1 Renown if** the party allows Mirt the three days and receives the study results — they learn the compromised contact's name and can act on it before **Vault of Dragons**.
> **+1 Renown if** the Splinter squad is defeated and the sending stone recovered. Mirt can use it to feed false information toward Manshoon or to listen for traffic.

## Summary

Mirt asked for three days with the Stone of Golorr, citing a psychic resonance that Harper seers had detected below the city. A Manshoon Splinter squad arrived within two hours and moved on Trollskull Manor regardless of the negotiation's outcome. The squad leader's sending stone was keyed to Manshoon directly.
