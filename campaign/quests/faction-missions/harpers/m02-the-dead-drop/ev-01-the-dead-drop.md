# The Dead Drop

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Exploration/Social Event begins when the paper bird arrives and ends when the party resolves Tessalar Maeridge's fate. In this Event:
>

> - The gazer is on the third floor, hunting Fillipa through the roof beams; it will not retreat.

**Background (DM only)**

The Harper dead drop behind the philosophy section loose brick was used by Mattrim Mereg for his network contacts. A Splinter informant in Uza's regular customer base identified the location and reported it. The gazer retrieved the cipher note's contents through its eyebeam — someone on the other end was watching — and has been searching for a secondary document that Mattrim misplaced three months ago. The document does not exist anymore.

Tessalar Maeridge began visiting the shop three months ago. He was recruited by a Splinter handler with 50 gold pieces and instructions to report anything unusual about the philosophy section. He did not anticipate a gazer attack and is not committed to the Splinter. His handler uses a drop location at the Trades Ward customs house.

If the Splinter has received the cipher note's contents, two Harper assets in noble houses are compromised. This affects Mirt's Cassalanter social contacts in **Cassalanter Villa** unless the party resolves the Tessalar situation cleanly.

### Act 1 — The Shop

Uza is at Felzoun's Folly, two streets over, holding a cup of tea with both hands. She is not given to dramatic exaggeration — she is given to detailed, precise, and comprehensive description of dramatic events, which other people experience as exaggeration. She describes the creature accurately: spherical, roughly the size of a human head, one large central eye, smaller eyes on stalks. It knocked over the philosophy section and then hovered there staring at the wall. She has a cat, Fillipa, black-and-white, eleven years old, who has been demonstrating tactical intelligence for three days — knocking books into the gazer's path and retreating to the attic rafters whenever it got close. Uza lends the party her keys. Her only stated requirement is that Fillipa come out alive.

The bookshop is three stories on Sorn Street. Each floor has been through the situation differently.

**Ground Floor (Shop):** The philosophy section, shelf seven, near the back wall, is the primary damage zone — books knocked to the floor, display cases of illuminated manuscripts overturned. The smell is faintly ozone-sharp, like lightning held in a room for days. The staircase to the second floor is intact.

**Second Floor (Living Quarters):** Uza's apartments — bedroom, sitting room, kitchen, more books from her personal collection. Minor incidental damage. The gazer passed through here on the way up.

**Third Floor (Attic Workshop):** Low ceiling, eight feet. Half storage — rolled maps, uncatalogued acquisitions in paper bundles — half workspace, with a long table of bookbinding tools. The gazer (Gazer stat block) is here, hunting Fillipa through the rafters. Fillipa is ten feet up in the roof beams, uninjured, highly agitated, and has been providing sustained feline commentary on the situation for three days. She will not accept comfort from anyone until the gazer is dead.

The gazer is hostile and will not retreat. It has been instructed to stay until it finds the secondary document. Any stray eye ray that misses a character: roll d6; on 1–2 it strikes inventory.

- **First hit:** Books worth 15 gp destroyed.
- **Second hit:** A shelf collapses into the stairwell (difficult terrain on the stairs until cleared).
- **Third hit:** A bundle of rare manuscripts catches arcane flame and is ruined.

Uza will note each category of damage without placing blame.

### Act 2 — What It Was Looking For

After the gazer is dealt with, examining the targeted shelf: a **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check finds a loose brick. Behind it: a folded cipher note and a small space sized for a second document that is not there.

A **DC 14 Arcana** check on the cipher note detects fading scry-residue. The gazer read this note through its eyebeam and someone on the other end was watching. The cipher note contains partial contact identifiers for Harper assets in two noble houses.

Mirt, when reached: he goes quiet for a moment. The secondary document he mentions carefully does not exist anymore — Mattrim misplaced it three months ago. "That's going to be a problem." He gives them one name: Tessalar, a clerk who started visiting the shop three months ago.

### Act 3 — The Informant

Tessalar Maeridge is a human clerk, early twenties, working at a Trades Ward counting house. He started visiting Uza's shop after a Splinter handler recruited him with 50 gold pieces and instructions to report anything unusual about the philosophy section.

He had no idea it would escalate to a gazer attack. He is scared, guilty, and not committed to the Splinter. A **DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion)** check flips him. He knows his handler's drop location — a ledger at the Trades Ward customs house, second drawer, a specific mark inside the cover.

**Turn him:** Tessalar becomes a double agent filing false reports. The Splinter's view of the Harper dead drop goes dark. His handler investigates eventually, but the Harpers have a window before that happens.

**Report him to the Watch:** He is arrested. The Splinter investigates the breach; the customs house ledger is burned within a day. Clean for Tessalar's conscience; no secondary gain for the Harpers.

**Warn Mirt and relocate the drop:** No secondary benefit, but clean. Mirt relocates the drop and thanks them for the discretion. Tessalar is warned off.

Regardless of Tessalar's fate: Uza gives the party a used spellbook from her back collection — a wizard who owed her more than he could pay. Four first-level spells and three second-level; the binding is coming loose but the text is sound.

## Overview

A Harper dead drop in a Trades Ward bookshop has been breached. A gazer is inside, a clerk sold out the location, and the shop owner's cat has been running a one-animal rearguard action for three days.

## Read Aloud

> The paper bird's message is longer than usual, the handwriting slightly less neat: *"An old friend named Uza Solizeph runs a bookshop on Sorn Street in the Trades Ward. She's done quiet favors for twenty years. Something's gotten into her shop and she's frightened. Go see what needs doing."* A postscript crowded into the margin: *"She doesn't know what she's been helping us with. Keep it that way."*

## Summary

The party cleared a gazer from Uza Solizeph's bookshop on Sorn Street, discovered a breached Harper dead drop behind the philosophy section, and traced the breach to Tessalar Maeridge, a Trades Ward clerk recruited by a Manshoon Splinter handler. The handler's drop location at the customs house became leverage. Fillipa survived.
