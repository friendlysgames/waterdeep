# The Talking Mare

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social/Exploration Event begins when the PCs arrive at the Lightsinger Theater and ends when they report back to Mirt. In this Event:
>
> - Mirt delivers the mission privately in a theater box, then leaves before the first act ends.
> - The Dock Ward has hundreds of dray horses; locating Maxeene requires asking around.
> - Maxeene speaks freely to anyone who treats her like a professional contact — condescension or horse-voice earns silence.
> - A Splinter operative named Vell is watching Fillet Lane from a hire-coach, waiting for someone to identify the right horse.
> - Maxeene can describe two intelligence threads: a Splinter operative she transported to Shesstra Street, and a suspicious conversation she overheard between a sun elf and a half-orc.
> - Relocating Maxeene before Vell can confirm her identity preserves her as an active Harper asset. Leaving her in place ends her usefulness.

**Background (DM only)**

Mirt had a Harper-sympathetic druid cast a permanent Speak with Animals on Maxeene years ago as a favor. She has been gossiping freely to people she finds interesting ever since, which means her intelligence is informal, observational, and often more useful than structured reports. The Splinter does not know which Dock Ward horse is the asset. Vell is waiting for a Harper contact to identify the target for her — she has been in the hire-coach on Fillet Lane for two days.

Maxeene is pulling a merchant's wagon on Fillet Lane this afternoon. The Shesstra Street address she names is a Manshoon Splinter safehouse. This feeds directly into the **Faction Outposts** outpost seed for the Splinter's Trades Ward operation.

### Act 1 — Finding Maxeene

The Dock Ward has dozens of working dray horses on daily routes. Three independent paths to Maxeene's location:

**The Wagonworks Stable:** A dray driver mentions a grey roan mare with a star marking who seems to understand things — never acts spooked, always waits at exactly the right corners without needing direction. He finds it convenient, not strange. She is usually on the Fillet Lane route in the afternoons.

**Orvel the Urchin:** A Dock Ward boy who has made use of Maxeene's intelligence before. He will trade her current location for a silver piece and one of the apples from Mirt's note.

**Dock Ward Stablehands:** A **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)** check while asking around earns the specific route — Fillet Lane, this afternoon, pulling a merchant's wagon.

### Act 2 — The Watcher

Maxeene has the star marking and turns her head when she hears her name. She is well-groomed and wearing good-quality dray harness. She is on Fillet Lane at the expected time.

A hire-coach has been parked across the lane for forty minutes. The driver appears restless. A **DC 14 Passive Perception** notices the coach has not moved despite the pretense of waiting for a fare. Vell (Spy stat block) is inside. She has been waiting for someone else to do the identification work for her. Once the PCs approach Maxeene, Vell moves in — either intercepting the conversation directly or settling in to follow the party when they leave.

Approaching Maxeene openly while Vell is watching allows her to confirm which horse is the Harper asset. Approaching through the crowd, or sending only one character while the others maintain position, limits Vell's sight line.

### Act 3 — What She Remembers

Maxeene speaks if given the apple and addressed with ordinary professional courtesy. Her voice is unhurried, with a slight Castle Ward upper-class cadence — she has been listening to those conversations for years.

She goes silent and stays a horse if treated as an animal. She goes silent and stays a horse if addressed in the slow, loud cadence some people use with children and foreigners. She answers directly if treated as a professional contact and given the apple as Mirt's note specified.

She is chatty. Left to her own inclinations, she starts with the apple and moves to the interesting things she has seen. Three threads worth drawing out:

> > "A pale man with a carved wooden wand hired my dray four days ago. Dock Ward to Shesstra Street, Trades Ward. Three floors, no signage. Red lantern in the upper window."

> > "Two weeks ago, a sun elf and a very large half-orc took my dray from the Dock Ward to the Yawning Portal. They argued most of the way. The elf wanted to keep looking for someone. The half-orc said it was too exposed. The elf said, 'The war doesn't help anyone until we know who's running it.'"

> > "I would like to be moved to the stables on Candle Lane. The Dock Ward has felt strange lately."

A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check while she is describing the pale man's destination confirms the Shesstra Street building matches no licensed business in the Trades Ward directory.

### Resolution

**Relocate Maxeene:** The party arranges new stabling on Candle Lane, through Mirt or on their own initiative. Mirt is pleased. Maxeene continues as an active Harper asset. Vell loses the target and reports back to her handler empty-handed.

**Leave her in place:** Vell confirms the identification and reports to her handler. The Splinter now has a surveillance window on a Harper asset's route. Maxeene is no longer viable as an intelligence resource within a week.

**Vell follows the party:** If the party leaves Fillet Lane without handling Vell, she tails them. A **DC 15 Passive Perception** (or an active Perception check if the party is watching for pursuit) notices the same hire-coach several streets back. Vell can be confronted, shaken off, or followed back to the Shesstra Street safehouse herself — the same address Maxeene described.

### Concluding the Event

> **+1 Renown if** Vell is neutralized or followed back to the Shesstra Street address and the location reported to Mirt. He says nothing immediately, but the address goes somewhere.

## Overview

A Harper-connected draft horse has been quietly gathering intelligence in the Dock Ward. A Manshoon Splinter operative is watching the dray routes, waiting for someone to identify her. Mirt needs the horse relocated before the Splinter makes her.

## Read Aloud

> Mirt is in the private box when you arrive, watching the stage with the expression of a man who has no real interest in the play. He pours wine he has already had delivered and gets to the point without looking away from the actors.
>
> > "There's a draft mare named Maxeene working a dray route in the Dock Ward. She listens to everything and she remembers it. I need to know what she's heard before someone less friendly to us finds her first."
>
> He hands over a folded paper with Maxeene's route and the word *apple* underlined.
>
> > "She responds well to courtesy."
>
> He's gone before the first act ends.

## Summary

The party located Maxeene, a Harper-connected draft mare with a permanent enchantment, on a Dock Ward dray route. She described a pale Splinter operative she had transported to an unmarked building on Shesstra Street, and recalled fragments of a suspicious conversation between a sun elf and a half-orc arguing about an unnamed war. A Splinter watcher named Vell had been monitoring Fillet Lane from a hire-coach, waiting for the party to do her identification work for her.
