# Fentree's Ledger

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Investigation/Social Event begins when the party receives Jalester's courier letter. In this Event:
>
> - Three investigation checks trace the ledger's theft to Rezka and her meeting at the Green Dragon Inn.
> - Three approaches are available: take Rezka before either meeting begins, disrupt the bidding, or intercept Noska after the sale concludes.
> - A captured or cooperative Rezka trades the ledger for safe exit from Waterdeep.
> - If Noska wins the bidding, the party must intercept him in the street with his two bodyguards.
> - Identifying Emira Volstren and reporting her name to Jalester confirms Cassalanter interest in vault intelligence.
> - The ledger reaching Jalester unlocks Mission 6.

**Background (DM only)**

**Orvane Fentree** died of a genuine heart attack at 71. His private ledger — maintained out of habit for twenty years after his retirement — contains entries from 1244 DR documenting a shell company named Dunberton Holdings paying maintenance access fees to a vault custodian in the Waterdeep Vault district. Correctly interpreted, these entries prove the Vault of Dragons is active and located in Waterdeep rather than Neverwinter. Laeral Silverhand's intelligence has been wrong for years. This ledger corrects it.

**Rezka** (Spy stat block) was assigned to shadow Fentree by a Xanathar Guild analyst who flagged him as a potential Grand Game threat. She searched the home while the Watch was still processing the scene and pocketed the ledger before anyone noted her. Now she is running two buyers simultaneously and planning to sell to the highest offer.

**Noska Ur'gray** (Thug stat block, veteran training) represents the guild's senior intelligence cell. **Emira Volstren** (Noble stat block) represents the Cassalanter household. Neither knows the other is in the building.

The Green Dragon Inn layout: ground floor taproom, single staircase, four rooms on the upper floor from one corridor. Rezka is in Room 3, reviewing the ledger. Noska meets her at second bell in Room 4. Emira meets her at third bell in Room 6.

### Act 1 — Finding Rezka

The investigation begins at Fentree's home on Copper Street, Trades Ward.

**Check 1 — DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) of the study:**
The dust on the shelves is disturbed in a consistent pattern — someone knew where to look. Three ledger bindings are present on the upper shelf, but the fourth spine slot is empty and clean, meaning the missing volume was pulled within the last two days. A single copper button on the floor near the window does not match any of Fentree's clothing.

**Check 2 — DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) with Fentree's neighbor, Orlass Hemmet:**
She saw "a red-haired woman, thirties, practical clothes" leaving Fentree's building the morning he died. The woman was carrying a satchel when she arrived. It looked heavier when she left.

**Check 3 — DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) among Trades Ward contacts:**
Asking about the red-haired woman by physical description finds a Caravan Court money-changer who confirms she is a freelance information broker seen regularly in the counting house quarter. She booked a private room at the Green Dragon Inn for tonight under the name "Torval."

### Act 2 — The Green Dragon Inn

Rezka's plan: meet Noska at second bell in Room 4, Emira at third bell in Room 6. She is in Room 3 herself, reviewing the ledger, until the first meeting begins.

**Arriving before second bell:** **Noska Ur'gray** is downstairs with two Bandit bodyguards at a corner table. **Emira Volstren** arrives five minutes after Noska, alone, with a Cassalanter house guard waiting outside the inn's entrance.

**Pravin Cloth**, the proprietor, is incurious about his guests' business. He confirms the room assignments to anyone who asks politely and does not ask follow-up questions.

Three approaches are available:

#### Take Rezka Before Either Meeting Begins

A **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth)** approach to Room 3, before second bell: Rezka is caught at the table with the ledger in her hands. She surrenders without a fight.

She offers a trade immediately — the ledger for safe exit from Waterdeep. The Xanathar Guild will come for her when the sale falls through, and she knows it.

> > "Take it. I'll give you everything that comes with it. Just get me out of the city. Your patron has Watch contacts — I know she does. I need a window and a head start, not money."

The party can take the ledger outright, honor the trade and provide the window she needs, or take her into Watch custody with the ledger. Jalester accepts all three outcomes. Honoring the trade costs nothing — Rezka leaves Waterdeep and the Guild loses an operative. Taking her into Watch custody provides a statement that confirms the Guild's active surveillance of Fentree, which is actionable intelligence for **Faction Outposts**.

#### Disrupt the Meetings

Starting a confrontation in the taproom before either meeting begins draws Noska and Emira's attention to each other. A **DC 14 Charisma (Deception)** check can convince each that the other has already made a deal with Rezka, collapsing the bidding competition and leaving Rezka with no active buyers. Rezka, upstairs, does not know this has happened. She will attempt to contact both buyers to reschedule — but the party has time to reach her room first while she waits.

This approach delays rather than prevents access to Rezka if poorly executed. If the Deception check fails: Noska or Emira becomes suspicious and the taproom dynamic deteriorates. Noska's bodyguards become alert; Emira's house guard enters the taproom.

#### Wait and Intercept After

If the party lets the bidding conclude: Noska wins at 600 gp. Rezka hands over the ledger in Room 4 at second bell and thirty minutes. Noska and his two bodyguards head for the door.

The party must intercept them in the street or on the staircase — three against three, plus Noska himself. Noska does not surrender easily; he fights until reduced to a third of his maximum HP, then breaks.

> [!combat]+ **Noska and Bodyguards**
>
> **Enemies:** Noska Ur'gray (Thug stat block, veteran training), 2 Bandits
>
> Noska carries the ledger on his person in an inner coat pocket. He attempts to pass it to one of his bodyguards if attacked; the bodyguard runs for the street if he receives it.
>
> If the bodyguard escapes with the ledger, Jalester receives it three days later through secondary intelligence channels — the Xanathar Guild does not keep it. This outcome is treated as a partial success: the ledger reaches Laeral eventually, but more slowly and without the Emira intelligence.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Emira Volstren — The Secondary Payload
> Regardless of which approach the party uses, identifying **Emira Volstren** by name and reporting her to Jalester confirms that the Cassalanter household has active intelligence interest in the vault's location. Jalester passes this to the Watch's Criminal Intelligence division. Combined with Mission 4's Halek Farran lead, it builds the picture of a household taking operational steps ahead of **Cassalanter Villa**.
>
> Identifying Emira requires either engaging her directly (she will not give her name voluntarily, but a **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** check notices the signet ring she wears inward-facing, matching the description of Cassalanter household staff) or following the Cassalanter house guard from the inn's entrance after Emira's meeting fails to occur.

### The Ledger

Fentree's ledger is a slim volume with a cracked spine and tight, precise handwriting. The 1244 DR entries on pages 47–52 document Dunberton Holdings paying quarterly maintenance fees to a "vault custodian, Vault district." A **DC 16 Intelligence (History or Investigation)** check to research Dunberton Holdings confirms it as a Neverember-linked shell company, placing an active Neverember vault in Waterdeep rather than Neverwinter.

Jalester, when he receives the ledger: reads these pages twice. Says nothing for a long moment. Then: "She's been wrong for three years. That's going to be a difficult conversation." He means Laeral. He is going to have it today.

## Overview

Laeral Silverhand needs a dead man's ledger before anyone else reads it. A Xanathar Guild operative has it and is running a bidding war at the Green Dragon Inn tonight.

## Read Aloud

> The letter arrives by regular courier — plain paper, Jalester's name at the bottom. More formal than his usual style.
>
> > *"An old trade investigator named Orvane Fentree was found dead in his home in the Trades Ward on Greengrass morning. The Watch has ruled it natural causes. His personal records — twenty years of guild and contract documentation — are missing from his study. Laeral Silverhand has a particular interest in those records. Find them before anyone else reads them."*

## Summary

Jalester sent the party to recover a private ledger stolen from a dead trade investigator whose records contained evidence of the Vault of Dragons' Waterdeep location. They traced the theft to a freelance Guild operative named **Rezka**, who was running a bidding war at the Green Dragon Inn between Xanathar Guild enforcer **Noska Ur'gray** and Cassalanter household representative **Emira Volstren**. The party had three approaches to recover the ledger before it reached the wrong hands. If the ledger reached Jalester, Laeral's three-year intelligence error corrected — and Mission 6 became available.
