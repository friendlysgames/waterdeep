# The Exile's Return

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Investigation/Combat Event begins when the party receives Jalester's Watch-sealed note. In this Event:
>
> - Three investigation phases locate Harko's rope warehouse in the Dock Ward. Three failures before three successes means the kenku spot the tail and Harko moves to a backup location.
> - Inside the warehouse, Harko fights until bloodied, then calls for surrender and offers Veralax's identity as a trade.
> - Lira — a Muleskull Tavern server who appears in the alley — gives the party sixty seconds to demonstrate whether they can operate quietly.
> - A captured Harko in Watch custody generates the sealed Thayan intelligence file used in Mission 3.
> - Veralax's identity reported to Jalester seeds a Splinter thread that pays off in Arc E.

**Background (DM only)**

**Harko Swornhold** (Bandit Captain stat block) is playing two sides. He is working as a low-level incitement organizer for the Xanathar Guild — recruiting kenku, spreading tension in the Dock Ward. A Manshoon Splinter fixer named **Veralax** found him two weeks after he returned to the city and offered him coin to serve as a communications cutout between the Splinter and Xanathar street crews. Harko accepted as insurance and has been taking Veralax's coin while deciding whether to actually set up the channel. He has not told the Xanathar Guild about Veralax.

His two **kenku** companions serve as sentries at the warehouse's two entry points and communicate warnings through memorized knock patterns. If combat breaks out, both immediately retreat to the street and begin mimicking a Watch whistle sequence. A real Watch patrol responds in four rounds.

**Lira** works evenings at the Muleskull Tavern, a block and a half from the rope warehouse. She came out the alley's back door at the wrong moment. She is not a faction asset and has no stake in the outcome beyond what she decides overnight. That decision depends entirely on how the party treats her in the sixty seconds after she appears.

### Act 1 — Three Days of Looking

Locating Harko requires structured investigation. Each phase represents eight hours of work. Accumulate three successes before three failures to find the warehouse. Three failures means the kenku spotted the surveillance — Harko moves.

#### Phase 1 — DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)

Working Dock Ward contacts, taverns, and whisper networks: a ship chandler on Sail Street mentions someone has been buying extra portions at the Dock Ward food stalls for the past tenday — enough for four or five, always picked up by the same beak-faced buyer. Kenku are not common in the Dock Ward. The stalls are near Mudwagon Row.

#### Phase 2 — DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)

Canvassing Mudwagon Row: the block has a derelict rope warehouse, shuttered for two years. A neighbor has heard scuffling and low conversation through the wall at night.

#### Phase 3 — DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) + DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth)

Watching the warehouse from the building across the lane confirms two kenku sentry positions: one at the street-facing alley mouth, one at the back service door. Harko moves between them at irregular intervals. The Stealth check avoids being spotted during surveillance.

**If three failures accumulate:** The kenku spotted the tail. A coded warning knock fires at both doors. Harko moves to the cellar of a closed cooperage on Wharf Mast Lane, three blocks east. The same three-phase structure applies there, with each DC reduced by 2 — Harko moved in haste and was less careful.

### Act 2 — The Warehouse

The rope warehouse has two entry points: the main street door (barred from inside; DC 15 Strength to force, or DC 13 Dexterity with thieves' tools to unlock the side hatch) and the back service door (unlocked but squeaky; DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) to open silently).

Inside: **Harko**, his two kenku, and two Bandits.

**Kenku behavior:** If combat starts, both kenku immediately retreat to the street and begin the Watch-whistle alarm. A Watch patrol arrives in four rounds. The party has that long to resolve the encounter before outside attention arrives.

**Harko:** Fights if cornered. Calls for surrender at or below half HP — raises his weapon hand and says clearly:

> > "I'm not dying for this."

He cooperates from that point forward.

> [!combat]+ **Warehouse Encounter**
>
> **Enemies:** Harko Swornhold (Bandit Captain), 2 Kenku, 2 Bandits
>
> **Four-round clock:** Watch patrol arrives at round 5. If the fight is still ongoing, the party must explain themselves to the patrol.
>
> **Kenku exit:** Both kenku flee at the start of combat and activate the alarm from the street. They do not return.
>
> **Harko's surrender:** He will not die for either faction. At or below half HP, he stops. He cooperates fully once he understands Watch custody is preferable to whatever the alternative is.

### The Witness — Lira

Whether Harko is dead or captured, the kenku flee the moment the fight breaks. The alley behind the warehouse goes quiet. Then rapid footsteps from the alley entrance. A young Calishite woman, early twenties, wearing a server's apron and carrying a tray she clearly set down in a hurry, rounds the corner and stops dead.

**Lira** works evenings at the Muleskull Tavern. She came out the back door. She looks at Harko, then at the party, then at Harko again.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### How Lira Decides
>
> Lira's decision the following morning depends entirely on what the party does in the sixty seconds after she appears.
>
> **If the party is immediately honest:** She is frightened but responds to directness. She knew Harko — he had been drinking at the Muleskull for two weeks, quiet, never trouble inside. She is not going to the Watch tonight. She says: "He wasn't a good person. But that's not the same as this being right." She leaves. In the morning she decides not to file a report.
>
> **If the party lies or hedges:** She says nothing and leaves. The Watch receives a report the following morning noting a body in the alley and a description of the party. Jalester receives it, sighs, and tells the party: "I said quietly." He does not formally discipline them — there is no official mission record — but he gives harder assignments going forward.
>
> **If the party threatens her:** She files the report with a full physical description. Jalester's note this time is shorter: two sentences. He is simply less available for a while.
>
> Jalester's instruction to do this "quietly" was a test of whether the party can operate at the level the Alliance needs. Lira is the reality of working in a city full of people. The party that handles her with honesty is the party the Alliance wants. The party that does not will still complete the mission — but Jalester notices. He always notices.

### Act 3 — The Deal

If Harko is alive, he offers information in exchange for Watch custody over execution. He delivers this clearly, not desperately.

> > "I've been running messages between two handlers who don't know about each other. One works for Xanathar. The other one — the one who found me two weeks after I got back into the city — goes by Veralax. Dresses like a gem merchant. Meets at the Bent Nail alehouse, Dock Ward, every Highsun. Middle booth. He's Zhentarim — the bad kind, not the ones who do business. He's trying to use me to pass information between his people and the Xanathar street crews. I've been taking his coin but I haven't set up anything yet."

This information is accurate, verifiable, and actionable. Veralax is a Manshoon Splinter fixer. The Bent Nail meeting is real.

**If Harko was killed:** The Splinter connection is lost unless the party searched the warehouse. A **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** of the warehouse finds a scrap in Harko's belongings reading "Veralax, Bent Nail, Highsun, middle booth" in his handwriting.

**Harko released for information:** Harko leaves Waterdeep within 24 hours. The Veralax information is good, but Jalester's mission objective — Harko's removal from the Dock Ward — is only partially fulfilled. Jalester accepts it and files the assessment. He does not comment on it. He notes it.

## Overview

Jalester sent the party to find an exiled adventurer working with the Xanathar Guild in the Dock Ward. The order was straightforward. The question was whether they could execute it quietly.

## Read Aloud

> The message arrives with a Watch seal, not a paper bird. Jalester's handwriting is precise.
>
> > *"An exiled adventurer named Harko Swornhold has returned to Waterdeep illegally. He was expelled three years ago for attempting to bribe Magister Kylynne Silmerhelve. He is now working with the Xanathar Guild to incite street violence. He was last seen recruiting kenku in the Dock Ward. Find him. Eliminate him or bring him to Watch custody; I'll accept either. Don't let him know you're coming."*

## Summary

Jalester sent the party to find **Harko Swornhold**, a banned adventurer working with the Xanathar Guild in the Dock Ward. They tracked him through three investigation phases to a derelict rope warehouse, confronted his crew, and had the opportunity to capture him alive — at which point Harko offered the name and meeting location of a Manshoon Splinter fixer named Veralax who had been paying him to set up a communication channel between the Splinter and Xanathar street crews. A Muleskull Tavern server named Lira witnessed the aftermath; how the party handled her determined whether the operation ended cleanly.
