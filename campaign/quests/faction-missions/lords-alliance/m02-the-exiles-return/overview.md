# Lords' Alliance Mission 2 — The Exile's Return

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Requires Lords' Alliance Renown 2 and 3rd level. Available after Mission 1 is complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Involved Characters
- **Jalester Silvermane** (Lords' Alliance) — field agent; delivers the mission by Watch-sealed note; his instruction to do this "quietly" is a test as much as an order
- **Harko Swornhold** (Xanathar Guild / Manshoon Splinter) — banned adventurer returned to Waterdeep illegally; working two sides simultaneously; will surrender when bloodied and offer Veralax's identity as a trade
- **Lira** (independent) — evening server at the Muleskull Tavern; witnesses the aftermath; her decision overnight shapes whether the mission ends quietly or with a Watch report
- **Veralax** (Manshoon Splinter) — Splinter fixer; attempted to use Harko as a communication cutout between the Splinter and Xanathar street crews; named if Harko is captured or the warehouse is searched

## Dangers & Enemies
**Harko Swornhold** (Bandit Captain stat block) with two **kenku** scouts and two **Bandits**. The kenku are not fighters — they retreat to the street at the first sign of combat and begin mimicking a Watch whistle pattern they memorized. A real Watch patrol responds in four rounds. Harko calls for surrender when bloodied and does not die for either faction paying him. If Harko is dead, the Splinter connection must be found through written evidence.

## Overview

Jalester sent the party a Watch-sealed note: an exiled adventurer named **Harko Swornhold** had returned to Waterdeep illegally and was working with the Xanathar Guild to incite street violence. He was last seen recruiting kenku in the Dock Ward. Find him. Bring him to Watch custody or eliminate him. Do not let him know you are coming.

Locating Harko required three investigation phases through the Dock Ward — kenku buying patterns near Mudwagon Row, a shuttered rope warehouse, and surveillance that confirmed the sentry positions. If the investigation went wrong and the kenku spotted the tail, Harko moved; but to a backup location where the same approach still worked at reduced DCs.

Inside the warehouse, Harko fought until bloodied, then called for surrender. He had been playing two sides and was willing to offer the second as a trade. A Manshoon Splinter fixer named **Veralax** had approached him two weeks after his return and offered coin to serve as a communication cutout between the Splinter and Xanathar street crews. Harko had been taking the money while deciding whether to follow through.

The moment that defined the mission's final shape was not the confrontation with Harko. It was **Lira**, a Muleskull Tavern server who came through the alley's back door at the wrong moment and had sixty seconds to decide what she would do about what she had just seen. The party that was honest with her finished the operation cleanly. The party that was not finished it anyway — and heard about it from Jalester the following morning.
