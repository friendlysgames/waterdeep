# Azuredge Confrontation

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Social/Combat Event follows the tenday observation. In this Event:
>
> - The party must decide: report to Vajra with specificity, attempt direct extraction, or report inconclusively.
> - Reporting to Vajra triggers her immediate action — she removes the devourer herself, at Blackstaff Tower, by *wish*.
> - Direct extraction: a 4th-level spellcaster can force the devourer to surface with a DC 18 Intelligence (Arcana) check; failure deals 4d6 psychic damage to Meloon; success forces the devourer out and makes it immediately hostile.
> - Inconclusive reporting: the devourer files its weekly report; Nihiloor's network has now identified the party by description; Mission 4 is expecting them.
> - Meloon, when restored, is disoriented and then grateful with the intensity of someone who has been not-themselves for weeks.
> - Vajra provides a wand of secrets on any successful resolution.

**Background (DM only)**

The party has everything they need. Now the question is what they do with it.

Vajra has been waiting for confirmation she could not officially seek. If they bring her a full, specific report — behavioral tells, Azuredge's communication, magical confirmation on Day 7 — she acts the following morning. She has been Blackstaff for three years and has been watching Meloon go wrong for two weeks without being able to name it officially. The party has given her the naming.

If the party attempts direct extraction themselves, they are improvising something that is not written in any manual. Success is possible. The cost of failure is paid by Meloon.

If the party reports inconclusively — without the behavioral specifics, the Azuredge communication, or any magical confirmation — the devourer does not know the surveillance happened and files its weekly report. Nihiloor now has descriptions of Force Grey's new Gray Hands. Mission 4's Spawning Pool will be expecting visitors.

### Path 1 — Report to Vajra

The report must be specific to trigger her action. Vajra asks three questions: what were the behavioral tells, when were they observed, and is there any magical confirmation. On a full, specific report, she acts immediately.

She casts *wish* herself — the only available method — and removes the devourer in a private session at Blackstaff Tower the following morning. She does not perform this casually. *Wish* costs something even for the Blackstaff. She does it without announcing this.

The party can observe the extraction if they ask. Meloon, restored, is disoriented and then grateful with the intensity of someone who has been not-themselves for weeks and only now understands what that means.

> > "How long?" he asks. A pause. Then: "Three weeks." He sits with that for a moment. "Three weeks."

He does not say anything else for a while. Later, he buys the party a drink. He does not say what it is for.

### Path 2 — Direct Extraction

A 4th-level spellcaster can attempt to force the devourer to manifest by disrupting Meloon's cognition through targeted magical stress. This is not written in any manual. The character explains their approach; the DM determines which roll applies.

A **DC 18 Intelligence (Arcana) check** determines whether the approach causes the devourer to surface and attempt to flee (on success), or deals 4d6 psychic damage to Meloon (on failure).

If the devourer surfaces: it is a small brain-shaped creature that immediately attempts to implant in the nearest living creature. The target makes a **DC 12 Dexterity or Intelligence saving throw** to avoid; on a failure, the character is at risk of possession for one round before the devourer can be targeted directly. Use the **Intellect Devourer** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*). It fights until destroyed or until it escapes.

Meloon, restored, is disoriented and then grateful. He does not know the details of what just happened, and the party can decide how much to tell him.

### Path 3 — Inconclusive Report

Without specificity — without the behavioral documentation, the Azuredge communication, or magical confirmation — Vajra cannot act. "I need something I can use," she says. "Observe him longer." She is not angry. She is exact.

The devourer files its weekly report. Nihiloor's network now has descriptions of Force Grey's new Gray Hands — their faces, names if the party introduced themselves, capabilities if they used magic near Meloon. Mission 4's Spawning Pool has been notified.

> **[GM]**

> #### Mission 4 Consequence
> If the party reported inconclusively here, increase Mission 4's difficulty: the lair's alert state is higher, and one additional encounter in the navigation route is aware that a party is en route. The Spawning Pool encounter's first round is not a surprise.

### Concluding the Event

> **+1 Renown if** Azuredge's communication is interpreted correctly and reported to Vajra as evidence of the axe's active resistance. She finds this quietly impressive and notes it in her Nihiloor dossier.

## Overview

The tenday ends. The party must decide what to do with what they found.

## Read Aloud

> Vajra receives you at the standing desk, which is where she has all conversations she has not yet decided the tone of. She has a notebook open.
>
> > "Tell me everything you observed. In order."
>
> She writes while you speak. When you finish, she sets down the pen.
>
> > "I'll handle it." A pause. "Tomorrow morning. At the Tower."
>
> The following morning, Blackstaff Tower smells faintly of magic spent and not entirely dissipated. Meloon is in a chair, disoriented. He looks at his hands, then at Azuredge propped against the wall. The axe is, by every available measure, relieved.

## Summary

The party reported their surveillance findings to Vajra, who removed the intellect devourer from Meloon Wardragon by *wish* at Blackstaff Tower. Alternatively, they attempted direct extraction. Either way: Meloon was restored. Vajra provided a *wand of secrets*. She noted in her Nihiloor dossier that Azuredge had been actively resisting the possession.
