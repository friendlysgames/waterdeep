# Force Grey Mission 4 — Destroy the Intellect Factory

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Requires Force Grey Renown 7 and 5th level. Available after Mission 3 is complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 5th-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Involved Characters
- **Vajra Safahr** (Force Grey) — provides potions and covers *raise dead* costs; receives the completed mission report
- **Nihiloor** (Xanathar's Guild) — the mind flayer managing the intellect devourer Spawning Pool; present in the lair 50% of the time; priority is escape over confrontation
- **Meloon Wardragon** (Force Grey, optional) — if restored in Mission 3, he can provide tactical intelligence about the lair from the devourer's residual memories
- **Soluun Xibrindas** (independent) — a drow prisoner in Area X24; a secondary rescue objective with cross-faction implications

## Dangers & Enemies
A working Xanathar Guild lair. The navigation route passes through active areas with multiple potential encounters. **Nihiloor** (use **Mind Flayer** stat block, 2024 *Monster Manual*) prioritizes escape but uses *Mind Blast* and *Dominate Monster* if cornered. Three developing intellect devourers in the Spawning Pool are not yet combat-capable but must be destroyed with the pool. A drow prisoner in X24 is a secondary objective.

## Overview

Vajra's Sending is spare and clear: the Spawning Pool — the source of Nihiloor's intellect devourer operation — is inside Xanathar's Lair. Infiltrate it. Destroy the pool. Come to the Tower first.

At the Tower, potions are on the desk: one *potion of psychic resistance* per character, a spare *potion of water breathing* for one entry route. She covers the cost of any *raise dead* spells that become necessary. She says it without inflection. The party should understand what it means.

The mission has two phases: navigating to the Spawning Pool, and destroying it. The navigation route crosses active areas where encounters are probable. Nihiloor may or may not be in the Extraction Chamber when the party passes through it. Three methods destroy the pool. The escape is the final challenge.

**Nihiloor appears again in Xanathar's Lair** if it escaped this mission; parties who drove it out here will recognize it. If it was destroyed or cornered here, it is gone — which affects whether Xanathar's mind-control network has remaining operational intelligence in the city.
