# Force Grey Mission 2 — The Dragon in the Harbor

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Requires Force Grey Renown 2 and 3rd level. Available after Mission 1 is complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 3rd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Involved Characters
- **Vajra Safahr** (Force Grey) — the Blackstaff; sends the mission by Sending spell and provides potions of water breathing
- **Zelifarn** (independent) — a young bronze dragon living near an elvish wreck forty feet below Deepwater Harbor; the party's primary contact and the source of the mission's most consequential intelligence
- **Meritide Blackfin** (Fleetswake variant only) — Dread High Priest of the Queenspire, a Darfellan priest of Umberlee; provides the hook during the Fleetswake festival as an alternative to Vajra's Sending

## Dangers & Enemies
No combat encounters under normal conditions. Zelifarn is a **Young Bronze Dragon** (2024 *Monster Manual*) but is not hostile — his cooperation depends on the party's willingness to engage with his curiosity as a genuine transaction rather than an obstacle. Drowning is possible if potions are mismanaged, though this requires active misfortune.

## Overview

Vajra's four-word Sending sends the party underwater: a young bronze dragon has taken up residence near an old elvish wreck in Deepwater Harbor. Four potions of water breathing are on her desk when the party arrives. She wants his intentions assessed.

The harbor bottom is cold and dim, lit in the center by diffracted light from above. **Zelifarn** notices the party before they are within speaking range and approaches at speed. He is intensely curious about everything the party is carrying. His cooperation is best purchased through genuine exchange — a character who gives him something they actually value rather than a spare coin earns his regard for the rest of the conversation and beyond.

After several minutes of enthusiastic negotiation, Zelifarn mentions conversationally that something is attached to the bottom of the *Eyecatcher* — one of the Sea Maidens Faire vessels — that does not match any hull architecture he recognizes. It comes out sometimes, at night, and goes back. He offers to help the ship with their security. He has decided they would say no.

The submarine is a Bregan D'aerthe operational vessel. Vajra's reaction to this intelligence is the best evidence of what it means: she goes quiet, picks up a pen, and starts asking very specific questions about dimensions and attachment method.

Zelifarn resurfaces in **Arc H — Sea Maidens Faire**, where parties who established a genuine relationship with him have prior standing as an approach vector and a moral thread.
