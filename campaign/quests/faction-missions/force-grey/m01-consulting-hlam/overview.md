# Force Grey Mission 1 — Consulting Hlam

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available from the start of Force Grey membership. No prior arc completion required. Best placed before or during Arc B, while the party is still establishing its Waterdeep contacts.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 2nd-level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission awards no Milestone Points.

## Involved Characters
- **Vajra Safahr** (Force Grey) — the Blackstaff; sends the mission by Sending spell and receives the party's report at Blackstaff Tower
- **Hlam** (independent) — a monk of indeterminate age living on Mount Waterdeep's upper slope; the party's target and sole subject of this mission

## Dangers & Enemies
No combat encounters. The primary challenge is social — earning Hlam's cooperation through patience and honesty rather than authority or urgency. A failed Constitution saving throw on the ascent results in Exhaustion, not injury.

## Overview

The party receives a Sending spell from Vajra Safahr instructing them to seek out a monk named Hlam on the slope of Mount Waterdeep and ask what threats he has perceived — without mentioning her name, and without overstaying.

The climb is real: the path narrows above the treeline and disappears entirely once, requiring a short scramble. Hlam received them long before they reached his cave. He responds to directness and does not respond to appeals to authority. With patience and honesty, he gives the party two pieces of intelligence: a warning about Manshoon's return and a rebuilding presence, and an unprompted observation about something buried beneath the city that is becoming less buried.

The second piece — delivered without any question being asked — is the party's first thread toward the Stone of Golorr and the Vault of Dragons. Vajra, when she hears it, asks them to repeat it and writes it down.
