# Design Notes: The Yellowspire Job

## Yellowspire as a Practice Heist

***Mission 5 is structural preparation for Arc I.*** The three-entry-approach format, the time clock, the concealed floor plate, the combat option if the clock expires — all of it is a scaled-down version of what Arc I asks for at a higher threat level. A party that ran Yellowspire cleanly arrives at Kolat Towers with one operational rehearsal behind them. A party that triggered the circle and fought their way out still succeeded, but they know the cost of a failed stealth approach in that format.

The design choice to include the ten-minute clock as its own section rather than a conditional note inside the surveillance section is intentional. The clock is the mission's primary dramatic engine. It should be treated as such — announced clearly when the trigger fires, tracked openly at the table, and resolved rather than quietly elided.

## Vevette Blackwater and the Dual Intelligence Thread

***The correspondence letters do two things.*** They confirm what Tashlyn already suspected from Skeemo's paper birds, adding corroboration. And they name Vevette Blackwater's operational function — coordinating strike team deployments — which means Tashlyn now knows who to target in Arc I to disable Kolat Towers' response capability. This is the intelligence pipeline the Doom Raiders have been building across four missions: Skeemo's paper birds (M4) point at a name; the correspondence letters (M5) confirm the name's function. By the time Arc I begins, the Doom Raiders have a clearer picture of Kolat Towers' command structure than any other faction.

## The Floor Plate

***The trap exists to be found, not to punish.*** The concealed floor plate is detectable at DC 13 — a reasonable check for 6th-level characters with Investigation proficiency. The design does not assume the party misses it. It assumes the party may miss it, and provides a ten-minute clock as the consequence rather than an instant failure state. A party that does miss the plate has ten minutes to complete their objective and exit; this is tight but not impossible. The agents who arrive are a fight, not a wipe.

The plate's design also serves a secondary purpose: it models the kind of security Kolat Towers uses. A party that encounters a concealed trigger mechanism at Yellowspire and recognizes the pattern arrives at Kolat Towers already thinking about what pressure plates might mean in that context.
