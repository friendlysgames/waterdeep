# Three Nights: Overview

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Quest Requirements
> Available at Renown 4. Level 4 recommended. The Wazoo Affair must be complete.
>
> #### Difficulty
> *An adventure for 4th level characters.*
>
> #### Milestone Overview
> This mission does not award a Milestone Point.

## Involved Characters
- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** (Bregan D'aerthe) — delivers the brief before dawn; collects Ott on the fourth morning; provides no further context
- **Ott Steeltoes** (Xanathar's Guild) — halfling in his fifties; keeper of Sylgar, Xanathar's pet goldfish; wearing an apron that still smells of fish flakes; bound by iron bands; terrified and not ashamed of it; will not attempt to escape
- **Xanathar** (Xanathar's Guild) — does not appear; escalates response each night as he becomes increasingly irrational about Ott's absence

## Dangers & Enemies

Three separate combat encounters over three consecutive nights, each at Trollskull Manor:

- **Night One:** 6 Thugs + 2 Bugbears (MM stat blocks), attacking from the street entrance
- **Night Two:** 4 Commoners each hosting an Intellect Devourer (MM stat blocks), entering as ordinary patrons during evening hours
- **Night Three:** 1 Beholder Zombie (MM stat block), entering through the cellar window

## Overview

Krebbyg arrived at Trollskull Manor's door before dawn and waited for someone to come out. There was a man in the basement — a halfling named Ott Steeltoes, who had been there since the previous night. Jarlaxle needed him held for three nights and returned undamaged. After that, Krebbyg said, Jarlaxle would explain everything. He paused, then corrected himself: Jarlaxle would acknowledge that it happened, which was as close as he came.

Ott Steeltoes is the keeper of Sylgar, Xanathar's pet goldfish. He is irreplaceable to the beholder, who cannot function when separated from Sylgar and who trusts no one else to maintain the fish's specific care requirements. Jarlaxle held Ott to spend three nights extracting intelligence: the lair layout, the panopticus surveillance network's blind spots, the beholder's daily routines. He needed Ott somewhere the Guild would not look immediately. Trollskull Manor was not on the Guild's list of Bregan D'aerthe assets.

Xanathar sent escalating forces each night: muscle first, then watchers, then something worse. Ott watched each attack from his restraints and said, on the third night, that Xanathar would send worse if he was not back within another three nights. He meant it as a statement of fact.

On the fourth morning, Krebbyg arrived at dawn and took Ott. A note was left on the bar in neat, unhurried handwriting: *Three nights. Thank you. —Z.Z.*
