# Three Nights

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Gamemaster's Summary
>
> This Combat Event runs across three consecutive nights at Trollskull Manor. In this Event:
>
> - Ott Steeltoes is already in the basement when the mission begins. He did not arrive through the front door.
> - Each night's attack comes after midnight. The manor's reputation and the party's Watch relationships may change how Night One resolves.
> - Night Two's threat is not visible until the party looks closely. The Passive Insight check is the moment it becomes a combat encounter.
> - Night Three is a single powerful creature. The cellar limits its most dangerous feature; use that constraint.
> - Ott's safety is the only success condition. The contents of his hidden pouch are the reward if he survives.

**Background (DM only)**

**Ott Steeltoes** is a halfling in his fifties, wearing a fish-stained apron and iron restraints he cannot remove. He is not violent and will not attempt escape. He is worried about Sylgar — Xanathar's pet goldfish, whose care requires specific knowledge only Ott possesses — and he is not embarrassed about this. He will ask, on the first night, whether the fish eats a consistent schedule when he is not there. The question is genuine.

Jarlaxle's three-night plan: each evening, Krebbyg visits Ott in the cellar and conducts a quiet conversation about Xanathar's lair. Ott, having decided he prefers to continue existing, answers questions about the panopticus blind spots, the beholder's daily routines, and which corridors Xanathar avoids when Sylgar is having a difficult day. He does not know he is providing intelligence for an Arc F operation. He is simply talking to someone who seems interested.

Xanathar sends escalating force each night. This is not a tactical decision — it is a beholder losing the ability to manage his fear about Sylgar. By the third night, rational allocation of resources has left the building.

### Night One — The Muscle

Six **Thugs** and two **Bugbears** attack from the street entrance, midnight. No finesse. They want Ott back and have been told to make noise about it. They are not trying to kill the party; they are trying to break in, grab Ott from the basement, and leave. Both bugbears head directly for the basement stairs. Four thugs hold the common room to keep the party occupied.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Watch Response
> If the party has Lords' Alliance Renown 3+ (Redknife rank) or has established a cooperative relationship with a Watch patrol in prior play, a Watch patrol responds to the noise after 4 rounds. The Guild operatives scatter when the Watch arrives, though one bugbear attempts to reach Ott regardless. A bugbear that reaches the basement stairs requires one party member to abandon the common room to block it.
>
> A captured operative knows only: "Xanathar wants the halfling. There's more coming."

The bodies, if any remain, are a scene-setting problem for the morning. Krebbyg, when told about the attack, nods. "Expected."

### Night Two — The Watchers

Four **Commoners** enter Trollskull Manor during evening hours — before midnight — and take a table near the back. They order drinks. They are polite and unremarkable. They do not talk to each other.

**Passive Wisdom (Insight) of 14 or higher** catches what is wrong with them: they are too coordinated. No idle conversation between them. Each one is watching a different quadrant of the room. They move in a pattern that is not socializing.

Each commoner hosts an **Intellect Devourer** that has burrowed into the base of the skull. The devourer is directing the host's movements and using their senses to scout the room for Ott's location.

If the devourers locate Ott — whether through the host's senses or by observing a party member check on the basement — they attack. If Ott has been moved to a back room, an upstairs bedroom, or anywhere not visible from the common room, the commoners finish their drinks and leave. Two days later, two of them return to a different establishment where a party member was observed — they are following the party now, not the location.

**Identifying and addressing the hosts:**
- A character who grapples one host and succeeds on a **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine)** check identifies the parasite.
- Removing an intellect devourer requires targeted magical effect or surgical extraction that kills the host.
- Hosts who are rendered unconscious have their devourers evacuate through the ears within one round and attempt to find new hosts.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### If All Four Devourers Are Taken Alive
> Krebbyg, given the four living devourers in a container: "Good." He takes them. He does not explain why. Jarlaxle needed to identify which specific Guild operatives Xanathar assigned to the search — tracing the individual devourers backward reveals the internal command chain that authorized the operation.

### Night Three — The Beholder Zombie

Xanathar's frustration has passed the point where he is managing the situation rationally.

A **Beholder Zombie** arrives at Trollskull Manor's rear after midnight, entering through the cellar window. It widens the opening first with its disintegration ray before coming through. The sound — a sustained grinding and falling masonry — gives the party one round of warning before it enters the cellar.

It searches for Ott by scent and can track him through two floors: Advantage on Perception checks to locate the halfling, and it will move toward him rather than toward the party if given the choice. It has no orders beyond "retrieve or destroy." If it cannot retrieve Ott intact, it will destroy him rather than leave him.

The cellar limits its central eye's antimagic cone: the ceiling is low enough that the cone cannot cover multiple targets without the zombie pressing itself against the far wall. It will not fire the cone from inside the window frame — it needs open ground before it opens the eye.

Ott, from his restraints, watches the zombie enter. He says quietly:

> > "He'll send worse if I'm not back in another three nights."

He means it as a statement of fact.

### Concluding the Event

> **[GM]**
>
> **+2 Renown** if Ott is alive and present on the morning of the fourth day. Krebbyg arrives at dawn and takes him. He does not acknowledge the attacks directly. He says: "Three nights. Good."
> **+1 Renown (supplementary)** if Night Two's intellect devourer hosts are taken alive and handed to Krebbyg. He receives them without explanation and files the information.

> **[GM]**
>
> #### Next Steps
>
> Ott disappears with Krebbyg before the party wakes on the fourth morning. A note on Trollskull's bar, in neat, unhurried handwriting: *Three nights. Thank you. —Z.Z.*
>
> That afternoon, the second anonymous coin pouch arrives: 100 gp, plus a note this time — *A more interesting assignment follows shortly.*
>
> Ott, returned to Xanathar's lair, tells the beholder only that he was held in a cellar by humans and does not know where. He does not mention anything connecting the experience to Bregan D'aerthe. Over three evenings of quiet conversation with Krebbyg, he decided he prefers to continue existing. This was a reasonable decision.
>

## Overview

There is a man in the basement. Jarlaxle needs him kept there for three nights. Xanathar needs him back immediately.

## Read Aloud

> Krebbyg is at Trollskull Manor's door before dawn. He does not knock — he waits until someone comes out. Then:
>
> > > "There's a man in your basement. He's been there since last night. Jarlaxle needs him kept there for three nights and returned undamaged. After that, Jarlaxle will explain everything."
>
> A pause.
>
> > > "He won't, actually. But he'll acknowledge it happened, which is as close as he comes."
>
> In the basement: a halfling in his fifties, wearing an apron that still smells of fish flakes, bound at the wrists and ankles by iron bands. He looks up. He does not look dangerous.
>
> He asks, after a moment, whether you know anything about goldfish.

## Summary

A halfling named Ott Steeltoes spent three nights in Trollskull Manor's basement while Xanathar's Guild sent escalating forces to retrieve him: six thugs and two bugbears on the first night, then four commoners each hosting an intellect devourer, then a beholder zombie through the cellar window. Ott was delivered alive to Krebbyg on the fourth morning. A note on the bar read *Three nights. Thank you. —Z.Z.*
