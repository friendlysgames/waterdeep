## Involved Characters
- **Krebbyg Masq'il'yr** (Bregan D'aerthe) — delivers the brief at an unusual hour; knows the situation is time-sensitive; mentions Soluun's name for the first time and watches the party's faces for recognition
- **Nar'l Xibrindas** (Bregan D'aerthe / Xanathar's Guild) — drow mage; Xanathar's advisor; Jarlaxle's deepest inside asset; has been covering for his brother Soluun's unauthorized activity; hostile on first contact; compact, precise, and managing fear with his entire professional toolkit
- **Ahmaergo** (Xanathar's Guild) — Xanathar's loyal taskmaster; has flagged gaps in Nar'l's intelligence reports to the beholder; the investigation is his; does not appear directly but his conclusions shape the mission's urgency
- **Soluun Xibrindas** (disowned Bregan D'aerthe operative) — does not appear; the thread connecting his unauthorized presence in Waterdeep to Nar'l's exposure is the mission's background cause

## Dangers & Enemies
Two Guild sentries are aware of unusual sewer activity near the Castle Ward tunnels (Option B only). No combat triggers in Options A or C. The mission's primary challenge is navigating Nar'l's hostile initial attitude and then executing a resolution option under a tight timeline.

## Overview

Krebbyg arrived at early afternoon — not his hour. He was more concise than usual. Nar'l Xibrindas, their deepest asset inside Xanathar's Guild, was under scrutiny. Xanathar had started asking questions. The likely cause was Soluun — Nar'l's brother, disowned from Bregan D'aerthe before this operation began — who had been operating in Waterdeep under a forged D'aerthe token. Nar'l had been quietly covering Soluun's tracks for months, and the gaps in his intelligence reports had been noticed by Ahmaergo. Once Nihiloor became interested, the situation would become significantly worse.

The party reached Nar'l through his Castle Ward sewer contact protocol — a chalk spider mark on the east wall of the main junction, followed by a four-hour wait until he arrived by Dimension Door, cloaked and visibly strained. He was managing fear with everything he had. He confirmed that he had been covering for Soluun. He did not apologize.

Three approaches were viable: forge a false report to satisfy Ahmaergo, extract Nar'l from the lair entirely, or discredit Ahmaergo's source before the investigation could formalize. The choice shaped Nar'l's status going into **Xanathar's Lair** — and the choice's consequences cannot be reversed.
