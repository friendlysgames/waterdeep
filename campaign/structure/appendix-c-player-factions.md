# Appendix C — Player Factions

Player characters in this campaign may join one or more of seven factions during Act I (Arc B). Six are the standard organizations of the Forgotten Realms — the Harpers, Lords' Alliance, Emerald Enclave, Order of the Gauntlet, Force Grey, and Zhentarim (Doom Raiders). The seventh is Bregan D'aerthe, Jarlaxle's drow mercenary company, available only to characters who follow the Arc C investigation to the Sea Maidens Faire and choose to work with Jarlaxle rather than against him.

Each faction has its own personality, resources, and agenda in the Grand Game. See **Appendix D** for per-arc faction missions and how each faction responds as events escalate.

---

## How Renown Works

Renown is tracked as a numerical score with each faction a character belongs to. It starts at 0 when a character joins and rises and falls based on their actions. The full renown rules appear in the *Dungeon Master's Guide* (2024). A summary for this campaign:

**Gaining Renown:** Completing a faction mission earns +1 to +3 depending on its importance. Upholding the faction's values in play earns +1. Achieving a major goal the faction cares about earns +2.

**Losing Renown:** Acting against faction interests costs −1 to −3. A public betrayal of the faction costs −5 or more at DM's discretion.

**Rewards:** Each faction unlocks perks at specific Renown Score thresholds. One-time rewards (marked with an exact score, not "score+") fire the first time that score is reached and never again.

If the campaign does not track renown, the DM decides when characters earn each perk based on demonstrated loyalty and service.

## Harpers

*"Weep for us, watch for us, and hope in us. We shall not fail thee."*

The Harpers are a scattered network of spellcasters, spies, and scholars bound by a code of noninterference in the natural order and opposition to tyranny. They maintain elaborate cover identities and a culture of plausible deniability; most Harper agents never know more than a handful of their colleagues. In Waterdeep, the organization operates loosely through Remallia "Remi" Haventree, with Mirt (a secret Masked Lord) as its most influential local contact and Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg in the field.

The Harpers want intelligence. They're watching what every faction does with the Grand Game and working to ensure no single power — including the city itself — gains unchecked control of the vault's gold.

**Faction Contact:** Mirt — the PCs' primary Harper contact throughout the campaign. Remallia Haventree is a senior Harper operative whose faction identity is kept hidden; she may become a direct resource at higher renown, but the PCs will not know her as a Harper until she chooses to reveal it.

**How to Earn Renown:** Complete Harper missions. Uphold the Harper Code: do not abuse magic, do not allow tyranny to flourish, and do not let any one faction accumulate disproportionate power. Share intelligence with the organization.

**Harper Personas:** A signature Harper perk is the *persona* — a false identity with documentation, cover story, and social backing. At Renown 10, a Harper mentor teaches one persona: a cover identity with a false name, appropriate clothes, forged documents, and one or two Harper contacts who will vouch for the story. Additional personas are learned at 25 and 40.

### Harper Renown

*Custom — designed for this campaign*

| Renown Score | Reward |
|:---:|---|
| 3+ | Harper safe house contacts in the Dock Ward, Trades Ward, and Castle Ward share street-level intelligence on any faction you ask about — known agents, movement patterns, recent activity. Each contact refreshes once per week. |
| 5+ | A Harper agent will shadow a named individual through the city for up to 3 days, reporting their movements and contacts. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again until your Renown Score increases. |
| 10 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 10, a Harper mentor teaches you one *persona* — a false identity with a cover name, documentation, appropriate clothes, and two Harper contacts who will vouch for the story in person. |
| 15+ | When any villain faction designates you as a priority target and begins preparing a response team, Mirt's network detects it and passes you warning within 24 hours. |
| 25 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 25, you receive a Harper Pin (silver) and learn a second persona. You can also call on a Harper Mage to accompany you on one mission. |
| 30+ | Harper informants embedded in the Xanathar Guild, the Sea Maidens Faire, and Manshoon's Splinter can be activated for a specific intelligence request — guard schedules, patrol routes, internal tensions. Requires a DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check; on a failure, the informant is unavailable for two weeks. |
| 40 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 40, you receive a Harper Pin (golden) and learn a third persona. Mirt reveals that he is a Masked Lord of Waterdeep — if the PCs haven't already worked it out. From this point forward, he can be asked to act in that capacity once: intervening in a legal matter, forcing a private audience with a city official, or obtaining classified information held by the Lords. One request, one use. |
| 50+ | You can request a covert Harper extraction from anywhere in the city — a distraction cell creates cover while agents guide you out. Alternatively, you may ask the High Harpers to formally expose one villain faction's activities to the Open Lord. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again. |

## Lords' Alliance

*"To preserve civilisation, threats must be eliminated."*

The Lords' Alliance is a coalition of rulers and noble houses that have decided, as a matter of survival, to cooperate. Waterdeep, Baldur's Gate, Neverwinter, Silverymoon, and a dozen smaller settlements pool intelligence and military muscle to hold the Sword Coast's civilized center against whatever comes next. In Waterdeep, Jalester Silvermane runs Alliance business — a Watch captain who files reports in both directions.

Five hundred thousand gold pieces is a political instrument. The Alliance knows this. They want the vault's gold returned to the city, with enough credit attached that the member settlements that helped recover it come away with something to show for their trouble.

**Faction Contact:** Jalester Silvermane

**How to Earn Renown:** Thwart threats to the North and to member settlements. Complete intelligence-gathering and enforcement missions. Advance the political interests of Lords' Alliance settlements, particularly Waterdeep.

### Lords' Alliance Renown

*Custom — designed for this campaign*

| Renown Score | Reward |
|:---:|---|
| 3+ | Lords' Alliance credentials mark you as an authorized agent. Watch officers defer to your judgment in ambiguous situations. Once per week, Jalester can pull a string to have minor charges dropped or a Watch report suppressed. |
| 5+ | You can access Watch crime reports, witness statements, and active investigation files for any case in the city. Classified cases require 4 hours and a DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check. |
| 10 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 10, your lord sends a letter of introduction. Nobles and senior officials in the Sea Ward and Castle Ward are Friendly to you by default. |
| 15+ | Alliance intelligence shares the current estimated locations of all four villain faction response teams, updated every 3 days through Jalester's Watch contacts. |
| 25+ | You can request an escort of four City Watch officers to accompany you on an officially sanctioned operation. They follow your lead for one mission and don't ask questions about the parameters. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again until your Renown Score increases. |
| 30+ | A Masked Lord takes a discreet interest in your work. Once per arc, you can submit a written request through Jalester — the Lord will use their authority to open a door, seal an investigation, or apply political pressure on your behalf. Their identity is never revealed. |
| 40 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 40, your lord presents you with one Rare magic item. |
| 50+ | Jalester arranges a private audience with the Open Lord, Laeral Silverhand. She will answer three direct questions about the Grand Game, the vault, or Waterdeep's political situation — honestly. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again. |

## Emerald Enclave

*"The natural order must be preserved, the balance must be kept."*

The Emerald Enclave is a loose network of druids, rangers, and survivalists who hold one conviction above all others: civilization is a guest in the natural world, not its landlord. They oppose anything that tips the balance too far — which, in Waterdeep, means watching the city's vast magical infrastructure for contamination and keeping a quiet eye on the Undermountain below. Melannor Fellbranch tends the grounds of Trollskull Alley and will recognize PCs who've spent time in the neighborhood. Jeryth Phaulkon works further afield.

The Enclave's stake in the Grand Game is indirect. Half a million gold pieces flooding Waterdeep's economy will reshape the city — and whatever reshapes the city reshapes the land around it. They're watching for signs it will spill outward: into the Neverwinter Wood, the High Moors, or down into Undermountain's ecosystems.

**Faction Contact:** Melannor Fellbranch; Jeryth Phaulkon

**How to Earn Renown:** Defend nature against despoilers. Oppose forces that damage natural balance (undead infestations, extraplanar contamination, unchecked magical pollution). Complete tasks for Enclave members.

### Emerald Enclave Renown

*Custom — designed for this campaign*

| Renown Score | Reward |
|:---:|---|
| 3+ | Melannor Fellbranch's network — gardeners, park wardens, harbor fisherfolk — passes you environmental observations: unusual animal behavior, contaminated soil, magical disturbances, any sign that faction activity is bleeding into the natural world. One report per ward, per week. |
| 5+ | Once per week, Melannor or Jeryth Phaulkon will cast Speak with Plants or Speak with Animals on your behalf. The information gathered must relate to events in the natural world — city gardens, the harbor, sewer passages, or underground tunnels. |
| 10 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 10, an Enclave elder teaches you Druidic. You also receive the locations of three Enclave safe sites in the city — hidden garden rooms and basement grow-spaces, warded against mundane and magical observation. |
| 15+ | The Enclave has mapped sewer passages connecting the city to Undermountain's upper levels without passing through Watch or faction territory. You receive three such routes, unknown to the City Watch and the villain factions. |
| 25+ | If you know a Beast, Plant creature, or Elemental inhabits a location you're planning to enter, the Enclave provides an advance intelligence report on its behavior, territory, and likely position. You have Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks and on your first Initiative roll against that creature in that location. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again until your Renown Score increases. |
| 30+ | The Enclave's harbor network shares ship information freely with you: manifests, passenger lists, unusual cargo. The Sea Maidens Faire's departure schedules and any significant cargo movements reach you automatically without requiring a check. |
| 40 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 40, Jeryth Phaulkon calls in a significant favor: a druid of sufficient power casts one spell of 5th level or lower from the druid spell list on your behalf. |
| 50+ | The Three acknowledge your service. Beasts of CR 2 or lower that can understand simple instructions will perform one clearly communicated task for you if it doesn't endanger them. You may use this once per arc. |

## Order of the Gauntlet

*"Faith is the greatest weapon against evil."*

The Order of the Gauntlet exists to fight evil, full stop. Undead, fiends, those who consort with them — the Order meets all of it with force. Its members are paladins, clerics, fighters, and monks who have made a simple calculation: hesitation costs lives. In Waterdeep, Savra Belabranta holds the local contact role. She's young, earnest, and still building the reputation that will make her dangerous.

The Cassalanters appear, on paper, to be exactly the kind of devout noble family the Order admires. They are, in fact, exactly the kind of Asmodeus-worshipping infernalists the Order exists to destroy. A PC who uncovers the truth and reports it will find the Order ready to act — quite possibly more aggressively than the party has planned for.

**Faction Contact:** Savra Belabranta

**How to Earn Renown:** Fight evil — particularly fiends, undead, and their servants. Complete Order missions. Uphold the values of justice and courage in the face of supernatural threats.

### Order of the Gauntlet Renown

*Custom — designed for this campaign*

| Renown Score | Reward |
|:---:|---|
| 3+ | Temples of Helm, Torm, and Tyr in Waterdeep provide free healing (Cure Wounds at 1st level, once per day per temple) and a place to sleep. Temple priests share information about suspicious parishioners, unusual spiritual activity, and known fiendish signs in their parishes. |
| 5+ | You earn the title **Knight of the Guard**. A temple contact begins monitoring the Cassalanter family's public religious activities and reports changes — unusual donations, new confessors, absences from worship. |
| 10 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 10, the Order gifts you one Uncommon magic item from the Armor, Ring, or Weapon category, selected for effectiveness against fiends and undead. |
| 15+ | Temple spellcasters of Helm, Torm, and Tyr cast spells of 2nd level or lower for you at no cost. In addition, Savra Belabranta shares the Order's existing dossier on the Cassalanters — years of observation compiled before the Grand Game began. It includes a partial layout of the villa's ground floor and documentation of the infernal contract. |
| 25+ | You earn the title **Knight of the Banner**. You can call on a Strike Team — three paladins or clerics (use the Knight stat block) prepared for fiendish opposition and carrying appropriate holy items. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again until your Renown Score increases. |
| 30+ | You earn the title **Knight of Justice**. Good-aligned temples across the city are Friendly to you by default. Devil worship is not illegal in Waterdeep. Savra knows this and will not act on suspicion alone. But if you bring her hard evidence of illegal activity — a physical contract, proof of the sacrifice plan, a summoned fiend — she commits the Order to direct action and does not wait for official channels. |
| 40 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 40, the Order gifts you one Rare magic item from the Armor, Ring, or Weapon category. |
| 50+ | You earn the title **Chapter Knight**. If you or an ally dies in service against a fiend or undead, the Order funds one Resurrection at no cost — once, no conditions. If Osvaldo Cassalanter was involved in their death, the Order sends a strike team to complete the mission. |

## Force Grey (Gray Hands)

*"Waterdeep stands because someone is willing to do what the Watch cannot."*

Force Grey — formally the Gray Hands — is Waterdeep's elite strike force, answering directly to Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff. The City Watch handles ordinary crime. Force Grey handles the rest: rogue archmages, fiendish infiltrators, Undermountain incursions, whatever the Open Lord decides can't be addressed through normal channels. The organization is deliberately small. Most members work alone or in pairs; only Vajra knows the full roster. Force Grey has no public presence. It leaves no paperwork.

Vajra recruits when she decides someone is capable and discreet. Characters who demonstrate both during Arc B may find her reaching out.

Force Grey doesn't appear in *Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn*; the renown rewards in this section are original to the remix.

**Faction Contact:** Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff

**How to Earn Renown:** Defend Waterdeep from supernatural threats. Complete missions assigned by the Blackstaff or authorized by the Masked Lords. Keep the Grand Game's collateral damage from destabilizing the city. Demonstrate absolute discretion.

### Force Grey Renown

*Custom — designed for this campaign*

| Renown Score | Reward |
|:---:|---|
| 3+ | The City Watch recognizes you as an authorized operative. Watch officers are Friendly to you by default and will share information, stand down from confrontations, and grant access to non-classified Watch records. |
| 5+ | You can requisition mundane equipment and Common potions from Blackstaff Tower's quartermaster at no cost. |
| 10 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 10, Vajra Safahr gifts you one random Uncommon magic item from the Wondrous Item category — chosen from items she believes will serve you. |
| 15+ | City officials and Masked Lords are Friendly to you by default. With Vajra's written authorization (obtained with a DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check during a non-crisis period), you can access restricted areas of Waterdeep: the City of the Dead at night, sealed evidence vaults, private Watch armories. |
| 25+ | You can call on one Veteran member of Force Grey to assist on a mission for up to 7 days. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again until your Renown Score increases. |
| 30+ | Vajra provides you an Underclock badge — a token recognized by Watch officers and city officials as carrying authority equivalent to a Masked Lord's writ. You have Advantage on Charisma checks made to influence city officials and military officers while the badge is visible. |
| 40 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 40, Vajra grants you access to Blackstaff Tower's scroll vault. You may choose any one Rare Spell Scroll from the collection. |
| 50+ | You receive a formal appointment as a Named Griffon — the highest operative rank in Force Grey. The Blackstaff will cast one spell of any level on your behalf. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again. |

## Zhentarim (Doom Raiders)

*"Everything — and everyone — has a price."*

The Zhentarim is a mercenary network — extralegal, pragmatic, and increasingly good at presenting a legitimate face when the situation calls for it. Waterdeep's cell is the **Doom Raiders**: Davil Starsong's crew of professional thieves, fixers, weapons dealers, and alchemists. Davil isn't interested in Manshoon's empire-building. He runs businesses. The Grand Game is a business opportunity, and he intends to come out of it with more floor space in Waterdeep than he went in with.

The Doom Raiders are a business, not an army. Davil Starsong rewards loyalty with access, not ceremony — useful contacts, professional resources, and the kind of quiet assistance that keeps the city's unofficial economy running.

**Faction Contact:** Davil Starsong — primary contact. Yagra Stonefist serves as the street-level go-between throughout (she greets new recruits and arranges direct meetings). **Arrest:** After the PCs complete two Doom Raiders missions, the City Watch arrests Davil and holds him at Castle Waterdeep pending questioning by the Lords of Waterdeep about the Zhentarim's role in the street violence. During his detention, contact passes to **Tashlyn Yafeera** — mission briefings continue to arrive by flying snake, but in a different hand. Davil is released weeks later once the Lords conclude his cell is not responsible for the recent killings. Tashlyn steps back to her normal role.

**How to Earn Renown:** Complete jobs assigned by Davil Starsong. Expand the Doom Raiders' business interests in Waterdeep. Acquire intelligence or leverage against rival factions. Do not embarrass the organization publicly.

### Zhentarim (Doom Raiders) Renown

*Custom — designed for this campaign*

| Renown Score | Reward |
|:---:|---|
| 3+ | Davil's network of contacts across the city passes you black-market intelligence: what the villain factions are buying, who's hiring extra muscle, unusual shipments moving through the Dock Ward. One piece of actionable intelligence per week. |
| 5+ | You can purchase restricted goods — thieves' tools, forgery kits, disguise kits, poisons, and unlicensed weapons — through the Doom Raiders' supply chain at a 20% discount, no questions asked. |
| 10 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 10, Davil (or Tashlyn Yafeera, if Davil is detained) introduces you to a Doom Raiders specialist — an expert in forgery, lockpicking, alchemy, or disguise — who assists on one job at no charge. After that, their standard fee is 50 gp per day. |
| 15+ | Davil maintains limited but real informants inside Manshoon's Splinter cell. Once per arc, you can request intelligence on Manshoon's current operations — safe house locations, agent names, or the current state of Kolat Towers' defenses. Requires a DC 14 Intelligence check; on a failure, the informant went quiet and this reward is unavailable for two weeks. |
| 25+ | Ziraj the Hunter owes Davil a favor, and Davil is cashing it for you. Once per campaign, you can request that Ziraj neutralize one named target — captured alive or eliminated quietly. Ziraj won't take assignments he considers suicidal; the DM adjudicates what he accepts. |
| 30+ | The Doom Raiders maintain a safe house in every ward — locked, warded, stocked with two weeks of rations and basic supplies. In addition, Davil can arrange for one villain faction's active response team to be misdirected or delayed by 48 hours. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again until your Renown Score increases. |
| 40 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 40, Davil pulls in a major favor: the Doom Raiders produce a full intelligence package on a lair of your choice — guard roster, patrol schedule, known magical wards, and at least one internal layout diagram covering the ground floor and first sublevel. |
| 50+ | Davil offers formal partnership for the vault operation. The Doom Raiders back the party's play at the Vault of Dragons: a distraction crew draws off one faction's forces, a locksmith backup stands by if the ceremonial lock resists, and a wagon team handles extraction logistics. In exchange, Davil takes 10% of whatever the party walks away with. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again. |

## Bregan D'aerthe

*"We are not your enemies. We are merely better informed than your friends."*

**Entry condition:** Bregan D'aerthe is only available if the Arc C nimblewright investigation leads the PCs to the Sea Maidens Faire and they choose to negotiate with or work alongside Jarlaxle rather than treat him as a target. If the PCs never reach the Faire through Arc C, Jarlaxle remains a background player and this faction is unavailable.

Bregan D'aerthe is a drow mercenary company that has spent decades building a presence above ground — through aliases, front businesses, and intermediaries too charming to question. In Waterdeep, the operation runs out of the Sea Maidens Faire: a traveling carnival that is exactly what it appears to be, and also something else entirely. Jarlaxle's real agenda in Waterdeep is political and long-game: he wants Luskan admitted to the Lords' Alliance, which would give Bregan D'aerthe legal standing across the Sword Coast. The Grand Game is a distraction he happens to find interesting.

Working for Jarlaxle means understanding one thing clearly. He is charming, he is generous, and there is always a layer to his plans he hasn't shared. PCs in the company are assets. He values them. He knows exactly what they are to him, and he expects them to know it too.

**Faction Contact:** Jarlaxle Baenre (as "Zardoz Zord" until unmasked)

**How to Earn Renown:** Complete missions assigned by Jarlaxle. Acquire intelligence, treasure, or leverage that advances Bregan D'aerthe's interests. Keep the company's operations secret from city authorities. Advance Jarlaxle's Luskan agenda where possible.

### Bregan D'aerthe Renown

*Custom — designed for this campaign*

| Renown Score | Reward |
|:---:|---|
| 3+ | You are extended professional courtesy throughout Bregan D'aerthe's network. Drow agents in the company recognize you as a contact and will not act against you without direct orders. You can use the Sea Maidens Faire as a safe house. |
| 5+ | Bregan D'aerthe's fences can reliably obtain and move exotic goods, contraband, and stolen property. You receive a 20% discount on black-market purchases brokered through the company. |
| 10 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 10, Jarlaxle gifts you one Uncommon magic item — personally selected, because he's already read your intentions more clearly than you'd like. |
| 15+ | You can request a Bregan D'aerthe intelligence assessment on any faction, NPC, or location in Waterdeep. Agents deliver the report within 48 hours. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again until your Renown Score increases. |
| 25+ | One of Jarlaxle's drow operatives (use the Spy stat block; skilled in disguise and waterborne operations) accompanies you on a mission. They follow your orders, but their loyalty is ultimately to Jarlaxle. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again until your Renown Score increases. |
| 30+ | Jarlaxle extends a personal favor. He can arrange any one of the following: a private audience with a Masked Lord, passage in or out of Waterdeep without City Watch knowledge, or the quiet disappearance of one named individual for 1d10 days. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again until your Renown Score increases. |
| 40+ | Jarlaxle names you a formal lieutenant of Bregan D'aerthe. All company operatives in Waterdeep defer to your instructions unless they conflict with Jarlaxle's direct orders. You receive one random Rare magic item from Jarlaxle's personal collection, presented with appropriate ceremony. |
| 50+ | Jarlaxle designates you the public face of Bregan D'aerthe in Waterdeep. You have access to the submarine *Scarlet Marpenoth* and its crew for any mission you undertake. Once you use this reward, you can't do so again. |
