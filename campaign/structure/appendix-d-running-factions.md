# Appendix D — Running the Player Factions

Each faction operates as an ongoing relationship, not a mission board. Contacts push for information, react to the PCs' choices in the Grand Game, and bring their own agendas to bear as events escalate. The missions below are structured as mini-arcs — each one has an investigation phase, a complication, and at least two resolution paths. No single skill check should determine success.

**A note on the Zhentarim:** Two Zhentarim cells operate in Waterdeep. Davil Starsong's **Doom Raiders** are the player-joinable faction covered in Appendix C. Manshoon's faction is the villain cell — called **the Splinter** throughout these notes. They are hostile to the Doom Raiders and to the PCs. When these missions say "the Splinter," they mean Manshoon's agents, not Davil's.

**Mission delivery:** Unless noted, missions arrive via the faction's preferred method — paper birds for Harpers, flying snakes for Zhentarim, animal messengers for the Enclave, Sending spells for Force Grey.

**Failure:** If a mission ends in failure, the situation changes — it doesn't simply reset. Targets escape, informants are compromised, and rival factions benefit. Each mission's Aftermath notes what specifically changes when things go wrong; use that, not a general reset.

**Renown:** Each mission lists a base renown award calibrated to its tier (Level 2–3 missions pay 2, Level 4–5 pay 3, Level 6–7 pay 4). Each mission also lists specific bonus conditions worth +1 each — award only those, not a general judgment call. A clean failure forfeits the award but costs nothing; a botched mission that causes collateral damage costs −1. Supplementary renown from upholding faction values (+1) and achieving major faction goals (+2) stacks on top — a dedicated faction member should reach Renown 30+ by the late heist arcs.

## Harpers

*Contact: Mirt (primary). Remallia Haventree: senior Harper; her faction identity is hidden from PCs until Mission 4.*

*Mirt rarely delivers missions in person — paper birds arrive at the tavern, or at wherever the PCs were last seen. He's always somewhere between hard to find and deliberately unavailable.*

### The Grand Game — Harper Stance

The Harpers know the Cassalanters are infernalists. They don't know the full scope of the vault situation until the PCs tell them. They want no single faction — including the Lords' Alliance, Force Grey, or the PCs — to walk away from the Grand Game with unchecked power. Their ideal outcome: the vault money is dispersed in ways that prevent political consolidation, the Stone of Golorr is handed to them for study, and whatever is dreaming below the city stays asleep.

**What the Harpers share proactively:**
- The Cassalanters' infernal history (after Mission 3, if PCs seem likely to encounter them)
- That the Waterdeep Harper network has been infiltrated by Zhentarim double-agents (after Mission 4, if the PCs have met Davil)
- Advance warning of faction response team deployments (at Renown 15+)

**What the Harpers ask for:**
- Regular intelligence updates on what the villain factions are doing
- The Stone of Golorr, or at minimum three days' access to study it (Mission 6)
- That the vault gold not be given to any one faction or individual

### Mission 1 — The Talking Mare
**Level 2 | 2 renown | No tangible reward**

**Hook**
Tickets to a matinée at the Lightsinger Theater arrive at Trollskull Manor — one per character, no note. When the PCs arrive, Mirt is waiting in a private box, watching the stage with the expression of a man who has no real interest in the play. He greets them without looking away from the stage, pours wine he's already had delivered, and gets to the point. "There's a draft mare named Maxeene working a dray route in the Dock Ward. She listens to everything and she remembers it. I need to know what she's heard before someone less friendly to us finds her first." He hands over a folded paper with Maxeene's route and the word *apple* underlined. "She responds well to courtesy."

He doesn't explain who he is or what the Harpers are. He assumes the PCs already know enough, or will figure it out. He's gone before the first act ends.

**Background** *(DM only)*
A Harper-sympathetic druid cast a permanent Speak with Animals on Maxeene years ago, a favor to Mirt. She gossips freely to anyone she finds interesting. She's been carrying a Manshoon Splinter operative on her route this week without knowing it, and the Splinter has sent a junior agent to locate her — waiting for someone else to identify which horse out of hundreds can actually speak.

**Act 1 — Finding Maxeene**
The Dock Ward has dozens of dray horses working daily routes. Asking around:
- A dray driver at the Wagonworks stable mentions a mare with a star marking who "seems to understand things" — never acts spooked, always waits patiently at the right corners. He finds it convenient, not strange.
- A Dock Ward urchin named Orvel will trade Maxeene's current location for a silver piece and one of the apples. He's found her useful in the past.
- A DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check while talking to stablehands earns more specific route information — she's pulling a merchant's wagon on Fillet Lane this afternoon.

**Act 2 — The Watcher**
Maxeene has the star marking and turns her head when she hears her name. A hire-coach has been parked across the lane for forty minutes. Inside is Vell (Spy stat block), a Splinter operative who has been waiting for someone else to identify the right horse. Once the PCs approach, she moves in — intercepting the conversation, or following them when they leave.

A DC 14 Passive Perception notices the coach hasn't moved despite its driver appearing restless. Approaching Maxeene openly lets Vell watch from a distance.

**Act 3 — What She Remembers**
Maxeene speaks if given the apple and addressed politely. She's chatty. Key information:
- A sun elf and a large half-orc took her dray two weeks ago, Dock Ward to the Yawning Portal. They argued quietly. The elf wanted to keep looking for someone; the half-orc thought it was too exposed. The elf said, "The war doesn't help anyone until we know who's running it."
- A pale man with a carved wooden wand hired her four days ago. No name, no conversation. Dropped at an unmarked building on Shesstra Street, Trades Ward — she describes it: three floors, no signage, a red lantern in the upper window.
- She'd like to be moved to the stables on Candle Lane. The Dock Ward has felt strange lately.

**Resolution**
*Relocate Maxeene:* Mirt is pleased. She continues as a Harper asset, now in safer stabling. The Splinter loses track of her.
*Leave her in place:* Vell confirms the identification and reports to her handler. Maxeene is no longer a viable intelligence asset.

If Vell is following the PCs when they leave: she can be spotted, confronted, or followed back to a Splinter safehouse in the Trades Ward. The Shesstra Street address Maxeene gave is the same building.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: Maxeene is relocated before the Splinter can confirm her identity. The Splinter's trail goes cold.
- +1: Vell is neutralized or followed back to the Shesstra Street address and the location reported to Mirt. He says nothing, but the address goes somewhere.

**Aftermath**
The Shesstra Street building is a Manshoon Splinter safehouse — one of the Arc E outpost seeds. Mirt thanks them and says nothing more about it immediately, but the address goes somewhere.

### Mission 2 — The Dead Drop
**Level 3 | 2 renown | Used spellbook (if Tessalar is caught)**

**Hook**
The message is longer than usual, the handwriting slightly less neat: *"An old friend named Uza Solizeph runs a bookshop on Sorn Street in the Trades Ward. She's done quiet favors for twenty years. Something's gotten into her shop and she's frightened. Go see what needs doing."* A postscript, crowded into the margin: *"She doesn't know what she's been helping us with. Keep it that way."*

**Background** *(DM only)*
Uza's bookshop has hosted a Harper dead drop for a decade — a loose brick behind a specific shelf section, used by Mattrim "Threestrings" Mereg for his network contacts. A Manshoon Splinter informant among Uza's regular customers identified the drop location and reported it. The Splinter sent a gazer to retrieve the cipher-message inside and read it through its eyebeam before the Harpers could recover it. The gazer is still searching for a secondary document it couldn't find.

**Act 1 — The Shop**
Uza is at Felzoun's Folly, two streets over, crying into a mug. She describes a "floating eye thing, all tentacles, that knocked over my philosophy section and then just hovered there staring." She has a cat, Fillipa, who is still inside. She lends the PCs her keys.

Inside: the shop is overturned in one specific area — the philosophy section, shelf seven, near the back wall. The gazer (use Gazer stat block from appendix B) is still searching, moving objects with its telekinetic eye. Fillipa is on the third floor, meowing.

The gazer is hostile. It retreats toward the damaged shelf section if cornered rather than fleeing — it's been instructed to stay until it finds the secondary document.

**Act 2 — What It Was Looking For**
After the gazer is dealt with (or driven off), examining the targeted shelf: a DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds a loose brick. Behind it: a folded cipher note and a small space sized for a second document that isn't there. A DC 14 Arcana check on the cipher note detects fading scry-residue — the gazer read this note through its eyebeam, and someone on the other end was watching. The cipher note contains partial contact identifiers for Harper assets in two noble houses.

Mirt, when reached for comment, goes quiet for a moment. The secondary document he mentions carefully doesn't exist anymore — Mattrim misplaced it three months ago. "That's going to be a problem." He gives them one name: Tessalar, a clerk who started visiting the shop three months ago.

**Act 3 — The Informant**
Tessalar Maeridge is a human clerk, early twenties, works for a Trades Ward counting house. He started visiting Uza's shop after a Splinter handler recruited him with 50 gold pieces and instructions to report anything unusual about the philosophy section.

He had no idea it would escalate to a gazer attack. He's scared, guilty, and not committed to the Splinter. A DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check flips him. He knows his handler's drop location — a ledger at the Trades Ward customs house, second drawer, a specific mark inside the cover.

*Turn him:* He becomes a double agent, filing false reports. The Splinter's view of the Harper dead drop goes dark. The handler investigates eventually.
*Report him to the Watch:* He's arrested. The Splinter investigates the breach; the customs house ledger is burned.
*Warn Mirt and relocate the drop:* No secondary benefit, but clean. Mirt relocates the drop and thanks them for the discretion.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: Tessalar is turned as a double agent rather than arrested or warned off. The Splinter's view of the dead drop goes dark, and the Harpers get a live feed from inside the cell.
- +1: The handler's ledger at the customs house is recovered or destroyed before the Splinter can burn it. Mirt gets two additional agent identifiers he didn't have.

**Aftermath**
If the Splinter received the cipher contents: two Harper assets in noble houses are compromised. The Harpers lose access to one of their Cassalanter social contacts (relevant to Arc G) unless the PCs resolve this cleanly. Mirt never says this directly but will be notably less forthcoming about Cassalanter intelligence until it's fixed.

### Mission 3 — The Doppelganger Auditions
**Level 4 | 3 renown | 50 gp per participating character (from Harpers)**

**Hook**
No paper bird this time. Mirt finds the PCs at the Yawning Portal personally — or sends Remallia Haventree in his place if he's unavailable, which is his way of saying this one matters. He's uncomfortable: "A member of our network — Mattrim Mereg, you may have met him at the bar — has gotten it into his head that we should recruit a gang of doppelgangers. I've been resisting the idea, but he's persistent and I need a second opinion I can trust. Talk to them. All five. Tell me what you actually think."

He specifically does not tell them which ones he suspects.

**Background** *(DM only)*
Bonnie runs a crew of five doppelgangers out of the Yawning Portal. Four are effectively neutral — they find their current arrangement convenient and would not actively harm the Harpers. One, currently going by "Edric Tanner," is a Manshoon Splinter asset. Edric has been embedded in Bonnie's crew for two months and has already filed one report about Mattrim Mereg's Harper affiliation. He'll use the interviews to gather more intelligence on the PCs.

Bonnie has known about Edric for three weeks. She ran quiet background checks on each crew member after a Guild operation went wrong — a job someone tipped off before it happened. She identified Edric's behavioral drift, confirmed her suspicion, and said nothing. She didn't expose him because she wanted to see whether the Harpers were competent enough to catch him themselves. If they are, she's interested in a real arrangement. If they aren't, a neutrality pact is the best she can offer them.

During the interviews, she's running her own assessment of the PCs. She's not a contractor hoping to be hired. She's an employer deciding whether to trust new clients.

**Act 1 — The Interviews**
Bonnie arranges meetings across two evenings at the Yawning Portal. The doppelgangers arrive in varied human guises: a Dock Ward merchant, a retired sailor, a young scholar, a watchman's widow, and "Edric" — who presents as a cheerful, open-hearted teamster, the most disarming of the five.

Each will answer questions freely. Skill checks aren't automatic gates — the PCs should ask smart questions. Useful approaches:
- Asking about specific Harper knowledge they shouldn't have (all five profess ignorance)
- Asking them to describe the Waterdeep they know (locals catch specific details; Edric's knowledge of the Dock Ward is too precise for a teamster, more like an observer's knowledge)
- DC 16 Wisdom (Insight) during conversation catches micro-tells: Edric blinks too infrequently and answers questions a half-second faster than natural
- DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) examining Edric's hands after the meeting notices no callus pattern consistent with teamster work

**Bonnie herself** is available before or after the formal interviews. She answers questions about the crew with apparent openness. A DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check catches what she's actually doing: she isn't nervous about the evaluation, she's conducting one. Her interest in the PCs' questions is too precise, her attention too directed. She isn't hoping to be hired. She's deciding whether to trust them.

If a PC asks her directly whether she suspects anyone in the crew, she deflects — "That's what you're here for, isn't it?" But if the same PC presses with specific observations, or names Edric with any supporting evidence, she goes quiet for a moment. Then: "Tell me what you noticed. In order. Starting from the first evening." She's not confirming anything. She wants to see if their work matches hers.

**Act 2 — The Tail**
If the PCs don't flag Edric after the interviews, he begins following one of them the next morning. He's skilled — DC 15 Passive Perception to notice, or a deliberate Perception check during any active movement. If he's caught following and confronted, he drops his current guise and adopts a new face immediately. He won't fight unless cornered — he'll try to disengage and report back. Following him leads to a safehouse in the Trades Ward, different from Shesstra Street but run by the same Splinter cell.

**Act 3 — The Report**
PCs report back to Mirt. He listens without interrupting.

*If Edric is correctly identified:* Mirt is visibly relieved. He recruits the other four under a provisional arrangement — they report to Bonnie, Bonnie reports nothing to the Harpers but agrees to a neutrality pact. Mattrim is quietly chastened. Edric is either neutralized (the Splinter loses their embedded asset) or left in place as a controlled asset feeding false information.

*If Bonnie confirmed she already knew:* The PCs have something Mirt doesn't — evidence that Bonnie's own operational security is better than his. If they bring this to him, he's quiet for longer than usual. "She ran her own check. Sat on it. Watched." He doesn't say anything else immediately. He's recalibrating. A PC can push him: with a DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check, or simply by making the case directly — Bonnie found Edric three weeks before the Harpers even knew to look — Mirt agrees to offer her a full operative role rather than a neutrality pact. She wants to name her own terms: her crew's identities stay protected, she routes intelligence through the PCs rather than directly to Mirt, and she retains discretion over which jobs she takes. Mirt accepts. Bonnie becomes a Harper operative with the Yawning Portal as her base of operations. Her crew's foot traffic access is a standing intelligence asset.

*If Edric is not identified:* All five are offered provisional recruitment. Within a week, Edric files a report. Names, descriptions, Harper affiliation. The Splinter now knows who the party is.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: Edric is correctly identified and reported to Mirt before any damage is done. The other four doppelgangers are retained under the neutrality pact.
- +1: Bonnie is recruited as a full Harper operative rather than left at a neutrality pact. Requires correctly identifying Edric AND making the case to Mirt with Bonnie's prior knowledge as evidence.
- +1: The Splinter safehouse Edric leads to is investigated and its contents reported. Mirt can clean house on two additional compromised assets.

**Aftermath**
If Edric is caught and the safehouse is investigated: it contains notes about two other Harper assets in the city — enough for Mirt to clean house before the Splinter acts. If Edric is missed: Manshoon's awareness of the PCs escalates, and the Kolat Towers raid (Arc I) has reinforced defenses tuned specifically to the PCs' known capabilities.

If Bonnie joins: she becomes a recurring contact — a source of Yawning Portal intelligence (who's meeting whom, what names are being asked about, what strangers came through) and a fallback safe house for the party. Her crew can provide short-term cover identities on a mission-by-mission basis. Mattrim is both delighted and slightly competitive about it.

### Mission 4 — A Friend's House
**Level 5 | 3 renown | 200 gp per attending character**

> **Design note — Pacing:** This mission is built to fill most or all of a 3-hour session. There is no time pressure and no combat trigger — the PCs move through the evening at their own pace. The "mission" (identify the impostor) is only one thread. A well-run session will have PCs stumble into two or three unrelated conversations that matter, gather information they didn't expect, and leave with more threads than they arrived with. Jarlaxle being identified early does not end the evening. Plan for at least 6–8 named NPC interactions. The guest list below gives enough material to sustain those. Not every NPC will connect with every party — let the PCs' instincts guide who they gravitate toward.

**Hook**
A formal invitation arrives at Trollskull Manor — heavy paper, Sea Ward seal, Haventree crest. Lady Remallia Haventree requests the pleasure of the party's company at a salon dinner at her estate. No further explanation. A shorter note arrives the same afternoon, in Mirt's cramped handwriting: *"Go. Dress well. Someone in that room is not who they appear to be."*

**Background** *(DM only)*
Remallia Haventree is a senior Harper operative whose faction identity has been hidden from the PCs until now. This dinner is the natural moment for that to surface — she's hosting in her own home, on her own terms. Mirt will be there. He'll let the implication land without announcing it.

Also in attendance: Jarlaxle Baenre, arriving as Erystian Demarne (a Luskan poet of some minor reputation, charming, improbably knowledgeable about everything). He has accepted Remallia's invitation through a mutual acquaintance — she doesn't know who he is. Jarlaxle has already marked Mirt across the room and is curious about the new faces he brought.

**The Guests** *(DM reference — create journal entries for any NPCs the party looks likely to interact with)*

Twenty to thirty guests attend. The following are named and fully playable. Others are generic color.

---

**Remallia Haventree** — *Host. Senior Harper operative (hidden).*
Moves through the room with the ease of someone who has run salons for thirty years. She greets everyone personally, remembers details about their lives, and asks better questions than she answers. She doesn't mention the Harpers. She doesn't mention Mirt, except to note they've worked together. She is watching the PCs more carefully than she appears to be.
*Topics:* City arts patronage. A new aqueduct proposal the Lords are quietly fighting over. How Waterdeep has changed since the Time of Troubles. If a PC mentions any of the villain factions casually: she listens without reacting, then changes the subject — and finds them again later in the evening.

**Mirt** — *Harper primary contact. Currently three glasses in and enjoying himself.*
He's positioned near the wine and is perfectly happy to spend the evening there. He's not working the room — he's watching it. If PCs check in with him, he nods toward whoever is interesting. He gives the mission briefing (find the impostor) privately when the moment allows, usually within the first thirty minutes.
*Topics:* He deflects most personal questions with a short story about someone else. He's unexpectedly knowledgeable about architecture and will genuinely engage on that. Ask about his past and he'll say something that sounds like a joke but isn't.

He's here because Remallia maintains careful relations with the Watch and always invites one or two commanders. Cromley is blunt, a little disheveled, and visibly relieved when anyone wants to talk about actual problems instead of art. He's been dealing with a series of warehouse break-ins in the Dock Ward — organized, professional, nothing taken that wasn't supposed to be moved. He thinks it's connected to a turf dispute but can't prove it.

A Watch officer who went missing two months ago; body never found. He'll share operational details freely with anyone who seems like they might actually help.

**Tessabrant Elamondra** — *Half-elf shipping merchant. Losing money and looking for quiet help.*
She runs a mid-tier trading company out of the Dock Ward. Three shipments in four months have been redirected — the goods arrive, but to the wrong buyers, who deny receiving them and whose paperwork is pristine. She suspects bribery somewhere in the chain but doesn't know where, and she can't go to the Watch without triggering an audit she can't afford. She's been quietly asking if anyone at the dinner knows anyone discreet.
*Topics:* Trade route disruptions (she'll describe the pattern without naming it as Guild activity, which it is). Dock Ward politics — which guild masters have gotten quietly wealthier. She'll mention she's heard the Cassalanters have been buying unusual quantities of a specific imported dye. (True. The dye is used in a ritual the PCs will encounter in Arc G.)
*What she wants:* Someone to track who is receiving her shipments. This is a potential side mission the DM can develop. It leads into the Xanathar Guild's Dock Ward operation.

---

**Lord Aldric Talmost** — *Minor noble, early thirties. Desperate to matter.*
He's from a family that was important two generations ago and isn't anymore. He knows it, and it makes him exhausting — he name-drops, implies connections he doesn't have, and hints at secrets he half-understands. He's heard fragments about "the vault situation" from someone at his club and has been working it into every conversation for a week.
*Topics:* He'll tell anyone who listens that "something big is happening" in the city's financial circles and that "certain families" are repositioning. He's vague because he doesn't actually know specifics. He does, however, mention that a Cassalanter cousin told him the family had a significant windfall coming — "not from trade." He thinks this makes him sound important. It's actually useful.
*What he knows:* The Cassalanters believe something is imminent. He doesn't know what. He can be pumped easily with flattery (DC 10 Charisma/Persuasion) or mild social pressure.

---

**Zalara Moonwhisper** — *Elven archivist. Attached to the Font of Knowledge.*
She's been researching historical Waterdhavian financial records for the past two years and is here because Remallia funds the archive. She has an archivist's mind — everything cross-referenced, nothing wasted. She's genuinely pleasant company if you can keep up with how fast she changes subjects.
*Topics:* The Font of Knowledge's collection. Historical Waterdhavian financial structures — how the Lords controlled liquidity in the city's early years. She will, if the conversation goes long enough, mention that she recently found records suggesting a significant sum was placed in trust under the Lords' authority over a century ago and never formally disbursed. She thinks it's a clerical error. It isn't.
*What she knows:* She has found a reference to the Vault of Dragons in an administrative record dated 1244 DR, filed under "extraordinary reserve, Lords' disposition." She doesn't know what it means. The PCs might. If they explain (or hint), she'll offer to pull the full document — which names the vault's ceremonial lock but not its location.

**Farrak Iltimer** — *Dwarf gemcutter, Trades Ward. No secrets. Genuinely enjoyable.*
He has the best stories of anyone in the room, none of them relevant to the campaign. He's at the dinner because Remallia bought a set of matching aquamarine pieces from him last year and they stayed friends. He sells to half the noble families in the city and treats them all the same — with cheerful disrespect that they find charming.
*Topics:* He'll tell a story about every family the PCs mention, usually unflattering and usually funny. He mentions in passing that the Cassalanters ordered a set of blood-red rubies last month — unusual cut, very specific weight. He doesn't know what they're for. "Twelve stones, all identical. Not for jewelry. Too heavy, wrong shape. Ritual pieces, if I had to guess. Not my business." He shrugs. It is the PCs' business.
*What he's useful for:* Comic relief, world texture, and that detail about the rubies (relevant to the Cassalanter infernal contract in Arc G). He'll be a friendly contact in the Trades Ward if treated well.

**Serithka Ondal** — *Tiefling composer. Zariel Fiendish Legacy. Nervous and perceptive.*
She composed a string quartet that premiered at Remallia's last salon and was invited back. She doesn't know anyone well and has spent most of the evening near the garden door, watching. She's observant in the way people are when they've spent a lot of time being watched themselves.
*Topics:* Music, obviously. The politics of cultural patronage in Waterdeep — who controls which venues, which composers are "approved." She'll mention that the Cassalanter family has been declining invitations to arts events they previously attended, which she finds strange; they were generous patrons until about six months ago.
*What she's noticed:* "Erystian Demarne" knows something is off about her Fiendish Legacy — she felt him clock it when they were introduced, the way someone does when they've spent time in the Underdark and know what they're looking at. He recovered instantly and said something charming about Luskan's cosmopolitan character. She found it unsettling and hasn't approached him again. If a PC befriends her and asks about him, she'll share this. It's not proof of anything, but it's a useful data point: whatever he is, he recognized a Tiefling's infernal origin on sight.

**Jelenn Urmbrusk** — *Masked Lord of Waterdeep (identity hidden). Proud, guarded, quietly desperate.*
A Tethyrian human woman in her fifties, precise in dress, deliberate in speech. She's at the dinner because she and Remallia move in the same civic circles — their families have shared legal and charitable obligations for two decades. She attends social functions to maintain the appearance of stability. She has a Sea Ward villa and sixteen guards she never travels without, though tonight she's come with only two.

Jelenn's money-lending business failed years ago — bad investments, a series of clients who defaulted, a market she misread. Rather than sell property or borrow publicly and let the gossip start, she borrowed from the Zhentarim. She has spent three years regretting it. Manshoon's people are not lenders; they are owners. She files favorable reports as a Masked Lord when they ask, covers up minor Watch infractions, dismisses two arrests she had no legal grounds to dismiss. She knows she is being used. She doesn't see a way out.

This is the weight a DC 18 Wisdom (Insight) check catches — not just power and caution, but the specific posture of someone maintaining a surface that is costing them something. She answers questions carefully because every answer is practice.

*Topics:* City infrastructure — she has genuine opinions about the aqueduct proposal and the guild licensing dispute in the Trades Ward. The cost of maintaining a household in the Sea Ward now versus twenty years ago (she keeps this almost abstract, but the personal dimension is legible). Remallia's salon (warm; they are actually friends). She will not discuss her financial situation or her Masked Lord status. If a PC mentions the Zhentarim, she excuses herself within three minutes.

*What she knows:* She knows the names of two other Masked Lords Manshoon is blackmailing — she's inferred it from which requests she was asked to coordinate with. She won't say this tonight. But if the PCs free her from Manshoon's operation (by destroying his power base in Arc I), she becomes a genuine contact: a Masked Lord who owes a debt she can't acknowledge publicly and will spend years quietly repaying.

*Campaign thread:* Jelenn's name appears on the blackmail list the PCs find at Kolat Towers in Arc I — along with Corylus Thann and three other Masked Lords. A PC who met her at the dinner and makes a DC 12 Intelligence check recognizes the name immediately. The connection is theirs to use as they choose.

**Act 1 — The Estate**
Haventree Villa is in the Sea Ward, three storeys, a garden courtyard open to the evening air. Remallia moves through the room with practiced ease. Mirt is near the wine.

When the PCs arrive, Remallia greets them personally and introduces herself. If a PC asks how she knows Mirt: "Oh, we've worked together for years." She doesn't elaborate. Mirt, if asked the same question later: "Remi and I go back." He also doesn't elaborate. Let the implication settle without a reveal scene.

Mirt gives the PCs the task privately, when the moment allows: someone in this room is wearing a face they didn't earn. He doesn't know who.

**Act 2 — The Approach**
Jarlaxle finds one of the PCs before they find him. He has already evaluated everyone in the room and decided the party looks interesting. He introduces himself as Erystian Demarne, asks about their work, listens with the particular attention of someone running an assessment.

Clues available without a roll: his clothes are of extraordinary quality for a working poet; his boots have a slight Underdark sole (designed for silence on stone); his companion — introduced as a fellow traveler from Luskan — defers to him with a precision that reads more like a bodyguard than a friend.

DC 20 Wisdom (Insight) catches the deliberate quality of his performance — not just confident, but controlled, every word a considered choice. A DC 14 Intelligence (History or Arcana) check after noticing the boots identifies them as drow-made.

His Luskan accent is plausible but slightly formal. Someone learned it from a tutor, not a street.

Jarlaxle will not be caught unless the PCs combine two lines of evidence or make the Insight check. If one of them is getting close, he redirects smoothly — asks Remallia something, draws a nearby nobleman into the conversation, suggests a walk in the garden.

**If a Party Member Is Bregan D'aerthe** *(DM only)*
A Bregan D'aerthe PC has seen Jarlaxle perform before — or has at minimum been briefed on what he looks like when he doesn't want to be recognized. They have Advantage on the DC 20 Insight check, and the DC 13 History/Arcana check to identify the boots drops to DC 10. A PC who has met Jarlaxle in person before this mission — at the Sea Maidens Faire or elsewhere — recognizes him without a roll.

More importantly: Jarlaxle clocks them first. Within thirty minutes of the party's arrival, he arranges a quiet moment — a refill at the wine table, a shared pause near the garden door — and says, without looking at them directly: "You and I are going to need a word before the evening gets complicated." He suggests the garden.

*In the garden:* Jarlaxle drops the Luskan affectation. Not the disguise — he doesn't need to — just the performance. He's direct: he knows they're here with the Harpers, he knows what they've been asked to do, and he isn't going to make it difficult for them. "You can report to your Harper friends that you couldn't confirm anything. Or you can tell them what you know, and I'll be curious to see what Mirt does with it. Either way, I'll know." He lets that land. Then: "Or we could talk about what I'm actually doing here, which is more interesting than whether you name me."

What he's actually doing: watching Mirt. He wants to understand how the Harpers are positioning for the Grand Game and what assets they're putting in the field. The PCs are part of that picture now.

*What he offers:* If the BD PC agrees to stay quiet about his identity tonight — not lie to Mirt, simply decline to confirm — he offers one piece of genuine intelligence: Remallia Haventree has a Harper contact embedded in the Sea Maidens Faire who doesn't know they've been compromised. He'll give a description. If the PCs act on it before the Harpers do, they'll find the asset already nervous and looking for an exit.

*The faction tension:* The BD PC is being asked to choose between Harper mission success and a direct request from Jarlaxle. Neither option costs faction standing automatically — Bregan D'aerthe does not have an explicit rule against working with Harpers, and the Harpers don't require their members to burn assets. But:
- Staying quiet and taking the intelligence: BD renown +1 if the party acts on the intelligence about the compromised Sea Maidens Faire contact before the Harpers discover it independently. Mirt gets no confirmation tonight.
- Naming Jarlaxle to Mirt anyway: The calling card still arrives. Jarlaxle is interested rather than offended — the BD PC chose the Harper faction when tested. He'll note it.
- Brokering a three-way arrangement — telling Mirt that "Erystian Demarne" is Bregan D'aerthe but not Jarlaxle specifically, and passing the intelligence to the Harpers as an anonymous tip — requires a DC 16 Charisma (Deception) check to keep both parties satisfied. Success earns the full Harper renown AND the BD renown. Failure: Jarlaxle sees what they did immediately, and so does Mirt.

**Act 3 — The Identification**
*Correct identification:* When pressed or named, Jarlaxle acknowledges it with a small bow — genuinely pleased. "You have good eyes. Mirt does choose well." He makes his excuses to Remallia (gracious, no alarm) and is gone within the hour. A calling card arrives at Trollskull Manor two days later: an embossed card with a harp and crescent moon crossed through with a rapier, and the words *I hear you've been asking about a certain mechanical footman.* No signature.

*Wrong identification:* Remallia is politely embarrassed on behalf of whoever was accused. Jarlaxle watches the scene from across the garden, quietly entertained. The calling card still arrives: *Better luck next time.* He's still interested — the attempt itself was the interesting part.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: Jarlaxle is correctly identified and the identification reported to Mirt before he leaves the estate.
- +1: The PCs document enough — the alias, the companion's bearing, the drow-made boots — that Mirt can build a working intelligence profile on Jarlaxle's cover identity, giving the Harpers a head start when the Sea Maidens Faire becomes a target again in Arc E.

**Aftermath**
Whether or not the PCs identify Jarlaxle, the calling card threads forward into Arc E — the Sea Maidens Faire becomes a Harper target again at that stage, and the name "Erystian Demarne" will resurface. Remallia is now a known Harper contact; the PCs can approach her directly from this point.

### Mission 5 — The Sleeping Asset
**Level 6 | 4 renown | Tactical intelligence on Xanathar's Lair (see Aftermath)**

**Hook**
Mirt meets them at Trollskull Manor in person. No message beforehand. He comes in through the back door, which means he had a key copied at some point, and he sits down with a drink before explaining: a Harper informant named Corene Wyldath, a halfling woman embedded in Xanathar's Dock Ward operation for six months, stopped checking in three weeks ago. He's been patient. He isn't anymore.

"I need to know if she's dead, captured, or something worse. And if it's something worse — I need you to handle it."

**Background** *(DM only)*
Corene was compromised by Nihiloor twelve days ago. An intellect devourer ate her brain. She's walking and talking and maintaining her cover, filing false reports that the Dock Ward operation is running normally. Nihiloor uses her as a surveillance asset; she's been attending Harper contact meetings and reporting everything back. She isn't a prisoner and won't appear distressed.

**Act 1 — The Search**
Three investigation beats to locate Corene:
- Harper contacts in the Dock Ward (accessible at Renown 3+) describe seeing a halfling woman matching Corene's description at a Guild warehouse on Shrimp Street, three weeks ago, acting unusually calm during a Watch sweep — no one who's been doing this work for six months stays that calm.
- The Dock Ward harbormaster's assistant (a minor Harper sympathizer) saw her twice recently at Guild meetings she had no reason to attend as cover.
- Asking around the boarding houses where she used the assumed name "Halla Ironstave" finds her landlord: she paid three months in advance last week — not like her.

**Act 2 — The Encounter**
Corene is found at her regular meeting point — a specific bench in the Trades Ward plaza she uses for dead drops. She looks fine. She's alert, well-rested, and says all the right things about her absence: a Guild operation she had to stay close to, no opportunity to check in safely.

DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) catches the wrongness: her eye contact is too steady, her speech too considered. A DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine) check, made after sustained interaction, identifies signs of intellect devourer occupation — flat affect, absence of involuntary reactions, the faint smell of psychic residue.

If she realizes they're getting close, she tries to extract more information before tipping off her handler. She asks specific questions about the party's current plans and location.

Detect Thoughts, cast on her: the surface thoughts are disciplined, controlled — not Corene's fear and careful improvisation but a flat operational calm. Deeper: she is monitoring the PCs and reporting, and the thing thinking through her is aware of the spell.

**Act 3 — Resolution**
*Remove the devourer:* Requires locating it inside the skull (DC 15 Arcana check, assisted by Detect Thoughts or similar), then magically extracting it. Telekinesis is the most direct option — the devourer makes a DC 16 Strength saving throw to resist being pulled free; on a failure, it is expelled and immediately hostile. Banishment works if the caster succeeds on a DC 14 Arcana check to target the devourer rather than the host. Any spell that forcibly moves or displaces a Tiny creature within a confined space achieves the same result. If successful, the real Corene needs a Long Rest before she can be debriefed. What she knows: six months of Xanathar Guild Dock Ward operations, two guard captain names, and the layout of one distribution warehouse. The devourer's intelligence connection to Nihiloor is severed.

*Kill her:* Grim. Mirt accepts it without comment. The devourer-Corene reports a breach before dying — Nihiloor knows the Harpers are onto the deception. The Xanathar Guild goes to increased alert in the Dock Ward for two weeks.

*Leave her in place as a double agent:* The Harpers feed false information through her dead drop for the next tenday. Mirt can plant one false piece of intelligence into Nihiloor's awareness — guard patrol timing, a dummy meeting location, a name that doesn't exist. High risk: Nihiloor is intelligent and may detect the manipulation.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: Corene is extracted alive with the devourer removed. Her six months of Dock Ward intelligence goes to the Harpers intact; she can continue field work after recovery.
- +1: The double-agent option is chosen and Mirt confirms the planted intelligence reached Nihiloor. One false piece of tactical information is now inside Xanathar's lair before the PCs ever enter it.

**Aftermath**
Resolving this mission activates the Renown 30+ Harper benefit (informants in Xanathar's Guild). If Corene is saved or used as a double agent: the Harpers can provide one tactical intelligence package for Arc F — patrol schedule for one section of Xanathar's lair, or the location of Nihiloor's lab. If she's killed: the information is lost but the Splinter operation is revealed to Mirt, who passes it to Vajra Safahr. Force Grey takes a greater interest in Xanathar's lair.

### Mission 6 — The Stone's Other Master
**Level 7 | 4 renown | Vault tactical intelligence (see Aftermath)**

**Hook**
Mirt arrives at Trollskull Manor after midnight, through the front door this time, and waits in the taproom until someone wakes up. When a PC comes downstairs, he's sitting with an untouched drink. He's direct: "Something woke up when you used the Stone. One of our seers felt it — something below the city. I need three days with the Stone before you use it to open the vault. I'll give it back. You have my word."

He's asking. He looks tired.

**Background** *(DM only)*
The Stone of Golorr contains the transformed soul of the aboleth Golorr. Installing an Eye activates it, and the activation resonates with the abolethic dreamer below the city (Illuun, Undermountain Level 4; this thread pays off later). Harper seers detected the psychic resonance and passed it to Mirt. What Mirt doesn't say: the Harpers believe the Stone can identify individuals psychically compromised by Illuun's influence, and they have two suspected assets. The compromised contact the study will name is **Jalester Silvermane** — the Lords' Alliance operative at the Yawning Portal. Illuun's influence reached him through the well during a period when he was working extended surveillance near the portal's edge. He is not aware of the compromise; he genuinely believes his reports and loyalties are his own. If the PCs have had little interaction with Jalester, the second suspected asset is **Renaer Neverember** — use whichever the party knows better, as the revelation lands harder with a familiar face.

A Manshoon Splinter watcher has been tracking the Stone's magical signature since it was activated. They know Mirt is meeting with the PCs tonight.

**Act 1 — The Negotiation**
Mirt explains what he knows: the Stone holds a consciousness, not just a map. That consciousness is connected to something old and patient below the city. "If you open the vault while that connection is active, you may be giving it what it has been waiting for. Three days. That's all I'm asking."

The PCs may comply, negotiate, or refuse. Negotiating yields: Mirt shares Harper intelligence about the vault's secondary safeguards (things not visible on the ceremonial lock) in exchange for access. He also admits, if pressed directly, that the Stone may identify compromised individuals. He can't promise the results won't touch someone the PCs trust.

**Act 2 — The Interception**
Whether the PCs agree or refuse, a Splinter squad of three agents has been watching Trollskull Manor and moves on the building within two hours of Mirt's arrival. They want the Stone. The squad leader carries a sending stone keyed to Manshoon directly.

If the PCs agreed to hand over the Stone: the attack begins while they're preparing the transfer — Mirt's location is known, and the Splinter intends to take the Stone from whoever is carrying it in the street.

If the PCs refused: the Splinter attacks the tavern outright. They know the Stone is on the premises.

The attack is not a full Kolat Towers assault — three agents, two Thugs and a Mage, with a contingency spell to get the Stone back to Manshoon if they obtain it.

**Act 3 — Resolution**
*If the PCs comply (Stone handed to Mirt):* The three-day study produces: confirmation of the Stone's abolethic nature, Harper intelligence on one of the vault's guardians the PCs haven't accounted for, and, if the PCs ask directly, the name of the individual the Stone identified as psychically compromised — Jalester Silvermane (or Renaer Neverember, per the Background note above).

*If the PCs refuse:* Mirt departs after the attack, disappointed but not hostile. "I understand. Keep it safe." The vault advantage is lost, but nothing is taken. The Splinter raid reveals the squad leader's sending stone — it's keyed to a voice the PCs may recognize if they've heard Manshoon before.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: The PCs allow Mirt the three days and receive the study results — they learn the compromised contact's name and act on it before Arc J.
- +1: The Splinter squad is defeated and their sending stone recovered. Mirt can use it to feed false information toward Manshoon, or to listen for traffic.

**Aftermath**
Mirt returns the Stone on the third day regardless of what was found. If the study identified Jalester Silvermane as compromised: any intelligence he has passed to the Lords' Alliance about the PCs' plans is now suspect — Illuun has had access to it. The Alliance contact chain should be treated as potentially monitored until Jalester is removed from the field or the compromise is severed (which requires destroying Illuun, an Undermountain objective). If Renaer was identified instead: everything he has told the PCs about his father's vault plans and the Enigma must be re-examined for what he may have revealed, unknowingly, to Illuun. The Splinter raid, whether or not it succeeded, confirms Manshoon knows the Stone is with the PCs and where they live.

### Mission 1 — The Long Watch
**Level 2 | 2 renown | No tangible reward**

**Hook**
Jalester meets the PCs at the Yawning Portal, picks a table far enough from the bar that the conversation won't carry, and gets to it: "A gang war is making the streets dangerous and the Guild workers nervous. We've offered protection to the Dungsweepers' Guild — specifically to their Dock Ward route coordinator, Mareva Coile. Three of her subordinates have been roughed up this week, and she's the next logical target. I need people willing to walk her route every day for a tenday. The Watch is stretched thin. This is real work." No recruitment speech. Six bells, Muleskull Tavern, Ship Street, Dock Ward.

**Background** *(DM only)*
The attacks on Dungsweepers' Guild workers aren't random gang violence. Xanathar Guild muscle is being fed Mareva's routing schedule by a guild insider: **Delvon Harsk**, a route supervisor who has been accepting small weekly payments from a Guild street agent named **Pirn** in exchange for timing information. Delvon isn't malicious — he's 300 gp in debt to a moneylender and desperate. He doesn't know what the Guild does with the information; he tells himself it's just scheduling data. The attacks have told him otherwise, but he's in too deep to stop without knowing who to confess to.

**Act 1 — The Muleskull Tavern**
Mareva Coile is 45, sharp-tempered, and skeptical of the protection detail. She briefs the PCs on the route: six hours of early-morning work, five daysweep teams, three wards. She gives them the supervisor rotation schedule and expects them to keep up without slowing her people down. Her concern is practical: "If my workers stop showing up because they're scared, the city fills with filth in a week. That's worse than a black eye."

The first three days pass without incident. On Day 4, a DC 13 Passive Perception during the morning route notices a figure watching from a second-story window on Margaster Street — a young man who keeps appearing at different points along the route, always slightly ahead of them.

**Act 2 — Night Three**
On the third night, a Xanathar strike: three thugs (use Thug stat block) and a scout (Spy stat block) named **Pirn** attempt to waylay Mareva on her return from a late supervision check at the Dockside pumping station. The attack comes from a side alley on Shank Street, Dock Ward. Pirn stays back — he's observing, not fighting, and will flee if the thugs are dropped.

The thugs fight until two of their three number fall, then break and run south toward the harbor. Pirn, if not intercepted, slips into a service lane and disappears into the Dock Ward. He can be tracked with a DC 15 Survival check or pursued with a DC 14 Athletics check — he's fast.

If Pirn is captured, he gives up Delvon Harsk under pressure (DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation)) or for a promise of Watch leniency (DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) with that specific offer on the table).

**Act 3 — Finding the Source**
After the attack, the question is how the ambush was timed so precisely. Investigation options:

- **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** at the attack site: one of the thugs has a folded paper in his pocket — a rough schedule notated by ward and time, in handwriting that doesn't match any of the surviving Guild workers but does match one of the supervisor duty rosters.
- **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) with Mareva**: she confirms only four route supervisors have access to the real-time schedule. She lists them: Delvon Harsk, Orryn Saltwick, Bremma Dunnet, and Cass Fildura.
- **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) when questioning supervisors**: Delvon can't make eye contact when asked whether he's shared routing information with anyone outside the guild, and his answer is too long.

**Resolution**
*Delvon arrested and turned over to Watch:* Mareva is informed. She's quiet for a long moment, then says: "Make sure they know he's not a killer. Just an idiot." Delvon is charged with conspiracy and sentenced to three months' labor. His debt to the moneylender is restructured under Watch oversight. Pirn's arrest or escape determines whether the Xanathar Guild contact chain is severed.

*Delvon warned off but not arrested:* He quits the guild the next day and disappears into the Trades Ward. The attacks stop. Jalester accepts this outcome but notes it in his report.

*PCs never identify the insider:* The attacks continue through the tenday. Mareva's workers organize their own protection rotation by Day 7, and the detail becomes redundant. Jalester pays the renown but his assessment of the PCs drops one notch internally — he gives them harder assignments going forward.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: Mareva's workers complete the full tenday without another successful attack.
- +1: Delvon Harsk is identified and arrested, or Pirn is captured and his connection to Delvon exposed.

**Aftermath**
If Pirn is captured and questioned thoroughly, he can name the Xanathar Guild street agent who hired him: **Krentz** (appears later in Arc B's sewers sequence). If the PCs haven't yet encountered Krentz, this is a name they can bring to the Watch — which flags the Xanathar Guild's Dock Ward cell for increased Watch scrutiny during the first two arcs.

### Mission 2 — The Exile's Return
**Level 3 | 2 renown | If Harko is taken alive: a sealed Thayan intelligence file (see Aftermath)**

**Hook**
The message arrives with a Watch seal, not a paper bird. Jalester's handwriting is precise: *"An exiled adventurer named Harko Swornhold has returned to Waterdeep illegally. He was expelled three years ago for attempting to bribe Magister Kylynne Silmerhelve. He is now working with the Xanathar Guild to incite street violence. He was last seen recruiting kenku in the Dock Ward. Find him. Eliminate him or bring him to Watch custody — I'll accept either. Don't let him know you're coming."*

**Background** *(DM only)*
Harko Swornhold (use Bandit Captain stat block) is not simply a Xanathar thug. He's playing two sides. He's working as a low-level incitement organizer for the Xanathar Guild, but a Manshoon Splinter agent named **Veralax** has also approached him — offering to use him as a communications cutout between the Splinter and Xanathar Guild street soldiers in the Dock Ward. Harko accepted the second offer as insurance. He hasn't told the Xanathar Guild about Veralax. If cornered, he'll offer both facts as a trade: Veralax's identity, meeting location, and a physical description.

Harko's two **kenku** companions (use the Kenku stat block) serve as his scouts. They're posted at the entrances to the warehouse he's using and communicate warnings through mimicked door-knock patterns.

**Act 1 — Three Days of Looking**
Locating Harko requires investigation. Each check phase represents eight hours of work. Three successes before three failures locates the warehouse; accumulating three failures means the kenku spot the PCs before they spot Harko and he moves.

- **Phase 1 — DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** (Dock Ward contacts, taverns, Guild whisper networks): a ship chandler on Sail Street mentions that someone has been buying "extra portions" at the Dock Ward food stalls for the past tenday — enough for a group of four or five, always picked up by the same beak-faced buyer. Kenku are not common in the Dock Ward. The stalls are near Mudwagon Row.
- **Phase 2 — DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** (Mudwagon Row): the block has a derelict rope warehouse, shuttered for two years. A neighbor has heard scuffling and low conversation through the wall at night.
- **Phase 3 — DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** (surveillance): Watching the warehouse from the building across the lane confirms two kenku sentry positions — one at the street-facing alley mouth, one at the back service door. Harko moves between them at irregular intervals. A DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check avoids being spotted during this observation.

*If three failures accumulate:* The kenku spot the tail and mimic a coded warning knock at both doors. Harko moves to a backup location: the cellar of a closed cooperage on Wharf Mast Lane, three blocks east. The same investigation structure applies to finding him there (DCs each drop by 2, since he moved in haste).

**Act 2 — The Warehouse**
The rope warehouse has two entry points: the main street door (barred from inside; DC 15 Strength to force or DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) to unlock the side hatch) and the back service door (unlocked but squeaky; DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) to open silently).

Inside: Harko, his two kenku, and two Bandit followers (use Bandit stat block). Harko fights if cornered but calls for surrender the moment he's bloodied (at or below half HP) — he doesn't want to die for either faction paying him.

If combat breaks out, the kenku immediately retreat to the street and begin mimicking a Watch whistle pattern they've memorized — an alarm that will bring a Watch patrol in four rounds. The PCs have that long to resolve the encounter before outside attention arrives.

**Act 3 — The Deal**
If Harko is captured or surrenders, he offers information in exchange for Watch custody over execution:

"I've been running messages between two handlers who don't know about each other. One works for Xanathar. The other one — the one who found me two weeks after I got back into the city — goes by Veralax. Dresses like a gem merchant. Meets at the Bent Nail alehouse, Dock Ward, every Highsun. Middle booth. He's Zhentarim — the bad kind, not the ones who do business. He's trying to use me to pass information between his people and the Xanathar street crews. I've been taking his coin but I haven't set up anything yet."

This information is: accurate, verifiable, and actionable. Veralax is a Manshoon Splinter fixer; the Bent Nail meeting is real. If the PCs act on it and report to Jalester, it seeds a Splinter thread the LA can pursue in Arc E.

**Resolution**
*Harko killed:* Mission fulfilled. The Splinter connection is lost unless the PCs found written evidence in his belongings (DC 14 Investigation of the warehouse: a scrap with "Veralax, Bent Nail, Highsun, middle booth" in Harko's handwriting).

*Harko taken to Watch:* He gives his full statement. Jalester receives the sealed intelligence file on the Splinter/Xanathar communication attempt. The Bent Nail address goes to Alliance Watch contacts for surveillance.

*Harko released for information:* Harko leaves Waterdeep within 24 hours. The Veralax information is good, but the LA's mission objective (Harko's removal) is partially fulfilled. Jalester accepts it, quietly notes it.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: Harko is eliminated or handed to Watch custody. He is no longer an active incitement vector in the Dock Ward.
- +1: Veralax is identified (from Harko's statement or the warehouse evidence) and the Bent Nail address is reported to Jalester before the end of the arc.

**Aftermath**
If Veralax's address reaches Jalester: Watch surveillance identifies Veralax as a Manshoon Splinter operative. He's picked up two weeks later. His arrest removes one Splinter communication channel in the Dock Ward — the Splinter response team in Arc E is missing one of its usual liaisons. If the PCs encounter the Arc E response team, one position in their ranks is noticeably understaffed.

If the Thayan intelligence file is recovered: it contains documentation of Esloon Bezant's history — his failed coup in Thay, his arrival in Waterdeep, and a list of associates. This file arrives in Jalester's hands and reduces the DC to locate Esloon in Mission 3 by 2 (the file has a last-known associate in the Dock Ward who can be approached directly).

### Mission 3 — The Red Wizard's Price
**Level 4 | 3 renown | No tangible reward**

**Hook**
Jalester finds the PCs at Trollskull Manor this time — arrives at noon, declines to sit. He's moving faster than usual. "The Zhentarim — not Davil's people, the other ones — are courting a Red Wizard named Esloon Bezant. Former Thayan, probably exiled, has a crew of eight he calls contractors. They're negotiating right now. The deal is muscle for an operation we don't fully understand yet, but the Zhentarim faction running this isn't the kind you negotiate with. I need the deal killed tonight. I don't care how."

**Background** *(DM only)*
Esloon Bezant (use Mage stat block, Chaotic Neutral, with the following modification: his Arcane Recovery is replaced by a once-per-day ability to cast *Counterspell* as a Reaction without using a spell slot) left Thay four years ago after a failed coup against his chapter lord. He has a warrant outstanding with the Thayan embassy in Baldur's Gate, but no active warrant in Waterdeep. He built a crew of six thugs and two veterans in the Dock Ward who know better than to ask about his past.

The Splinter fixer meeting him is **Sarvos Miln** (use Spy stat block), a Manshoon operative who handles contractor recruitment in the Dock Ward. The operation Sarvos is recruiting for: a coordinated strike on a Watch checkpoint in the Dock Ward that would create a window for a large cargo movement — the cargo being a Splinter intelligence package bound for a Manshoon associate in Baldur's Gate. The meeting is at the Skewered Dragon Tavern, a private dining room booked under the name "Holvar." The meeting started an hour before Jalester briefed the PCs.

**Act 1 — Finding the Meet**
Jalester has partial intelligence: the Skewered Dragon, tonight. He doesn't know which room or when it ends.

- **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) with the Skewered Dragon's taproom host:** The "Holvar" booking is in the back, the corner room. The host mentions two people arrived separately — "a foreign-looking gentleman in a merchant's coat, and a broad man with a signet ring." The meeting is still ongoing.
- **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) at the taproom entrance:** A Splinter watcher (Bandit stat block) is nursing a drink near the door — positioned to observe who comes in looking for the Holvar table.
- **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) of the building exterior:** A second exit from the private dining room opens onto Dock Lane — a separate street-facing door, currently bolted from inside.

**Act 2 — Options for Scuttling the Deal**
The PCs must choose an approach before the meeting ends (they have roughly 30 minutes from when they arrive):

*Disrupt the meeting directly:* Entering the private room creates an immediate confrontation. Esloon's two veterans are seated outside the dining room door as guards. Sarvos will flee through the Dock Lane exit if the confrontation turns violent; Esloon will fight.

*Expose Esloon to Watch:* Presenting Jalester's intelligence file (from Mission 2's Aftermath, if obtained) to a Watch patrol gives them cause to detain Esloon — the Thayan warrant isn't active in Waterdeep, but the file's contents justify a holding inquiry. A Watch patrol responds in three rounds. If Esloon is detained, Sarvos leaves without a deal and the Splinter loses this avenue.

*Turn Esloon against the deal:* Reaching Esloon before the meeting concludes (DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth) through the taproom kitchen to approach before Sarvos finishes his pitch) opens a negotiation. Esloon is a pragmatist who has survived by correctly reading which side will win. Offering him something the Splinter can't — specifically, the Alliance's ability to quash any future Thayan embassy inquiries — requires a DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion) check. On a success, Esloon declines the deal and departs. He doesn't become an Alliance asset, but he removes himself from the equation.

*Neutralize Sarvos:* Capturing or following Sarvos instead of Esloon collapses the deal from the other direction. Sarvos can be intercepted on the way in or out. If captured, he will not give up Manshoon's identity but will confirm the cargo operation — giving Jalester actionable intelligence about the Watch checkpoint strike. If Sarvos is taken to Watch custody, the operation is exposed and the Splinter loses its Dock Ward cargo window.

**Act 3 — Resolution**
The deal is scuttled if any of the following is true: Esloon is detained or withdraws from negotiations; Sarvos is captured or neutralized; the meeting is disrupted before it concludes; or the Watch checkpoint operation is exposed.

If none of these occur before the meeting ends: Esloon and Sarvos shake hands in the private room and part through different exits. The Splinter gains eight additional pieces of muscle for the checkpoint strike. In Arc E, the Dock Ward Splinter response team has +2 members when the PCs encounter it.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: The deal is scuttled by any method before it concludes.
- +1: Sarvos Miln is captured and identifies the Watch checkpoint as the Splinter's target — Jalester can pre-position Watch reinforcements, and the checkpoint strike fails when it occurs in Arc E.

**Aftermath**
If Esloon Bezant turned away: he remains in the Dock Ward with his crew, nominally independent. He resurfaces in Arc E as a possible hire for any faction with coin — the PCs may encounter him working for Xanathar Guild or as a freelance complication.

If Esloon detained: he's held for ten days and released without charge (the Thayan warrant doesn't survive the embassy review process). He leaves Waterdeep. His crew disperses.

If the checkpoint strike is exposed: it occurs anyway in Arc E, but fails — the Splinter loses the cargo window and delays their Baldur's Gate communication by three weeks. One Splinter operation in Arc E is noticeably disrupted.

### Mission 4 — The Archer Above
**Level 5 | 3 renown | Cassalanter intelligence file (see Aftermath)**

**Hook**
Jalester finds the PCs with the short version: "We have a sniper problem. Three dead in the North Ward and Sea Ward transition — all killed with arrows from above, none of the bodies connected to any active faction operation. The Watch is overwhelmed. My contact says he was spotted going to ground near Trollskull Alley. I need him found and arrested. Not killed — killing him makes this worse. Alive, he tells us who hired him."

**Background** *(DM only)*
The assassin is **Vhaspar Holmbridge**, 57, a retired City Watch sharpshooter who served in the Dock Ward for twenty years. He was hired through a Cassalanter household intermediary named **Halek Farran** — a steward who handles sensitive errands for the Cassalanter family. The three victims are not faction operatives. They are former witnesses to a money transfer six weeks ago in which a Cassalanter-hired fence paid a Xanathar Guild broker for a quantity of ritual components (the components connect to Arc G's infernal timeline). The victims saw the transfer by accident; Halek was tasked with closing that loose end.

Vhaspar doesn't know who hired Halek, and Halek doesn't know the full scope of the Cassalanter operation. Vhaspar took the contract for 400 gp. He's not ideologically motivated and will cooperate with Watch custody if it means he doesn't die. He has one more name on his list that he hasn't acted on yet.

Vhaspar is living in a rented room above **Yander's Feed Store**, Pell Street, North Ward — one block from Trollskull Alley.

**Act 1 — Investigation**
Finding Vhaspar:

- **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** (canvassing the North Ward, asking about a man watching rooftops or departing buildings at unusual hours): A linen merchant on Tarnath Street saw "a grey-haired man who walks like a soldier" checking in at Yander's Feed Store a week ago with a long rolled canvas under his arm. He came back at odd hours.
- **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) with the feed store's proprietor, Yander Coirn:** He confirms the man — gave the name "Belvor." Paid a tenday up front. Room is the top floor, north-facing window.
- **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)** while watching the building from across Pell Street: the north-facing window is slightly open, the shutter angled to provide a line of sight while leaving the interior in shadow.

**Act 2 — The Room**
Confronting Vhaspar requires getting into the feed store and up to the top floor without triggering his exit protocol. He has a length of rope tied to the window latch — if the door to his room is forced or opened without warning, he exits through the window and onto the roof.

*Approach quietly (DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) on the stairs):* Vhaspar is caught at his table, reviewing a sketch of his remaining target's walking route. He's alone, no weapon drawn. He raises his hands.

*Approach loudly or force the door:* He goes out the window. Catching him on the rooftops requires a DC 15 Athletics chase sequence (three checks, one success ends the chase; two failures means he reaches a pre-arranged rope drop to a back lane and escapes to a secondary location: the Skewered Dragon's cellar storeroom, Dock Ward).

Once caught, Vhaspar surrenders without further resistance. He says, quietly: "The name I was given for the last one — I haven't used it. You want to keep it that way, get me in front of someone who can guarantee my safety."

**Act 3 — The Interrogation**
In Watch custody or while the PCs hold him:

Vhaspar gives up Halek Farran — the Cassalanter steward who hired him — without needing a Persuasion check, provided he's promised Watch protection (Jalester can make this promise credibly). He confirms the hiring was through three layers of intermediary: a hire agency, a note, a drop box. He met Halek once, briefly, in a coffeehouse on Chandler Street, Sea Ward. He describes him: "Fifties. Neat. Smelled like cedar. Had a signet ring, kept turning it inward — the crest was facing his palm."

In Vhaspar's belongings: a sketch of his fourth target (a Dock Ward longshoreman), a partial address on a scrap of paper reading *"...ilanter Villa, Sea Ward, side entrance gate"*, and 320 gp remaining from his payment. A **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation)** check connecting the partial address to the known Cassalanter family location in the Sea Ward identifies it as Cassalanter Villa.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: Vhaspar is arrested alive and provides testimony identifying Halek Farran.
- +1: The partial address is identified as Cassalanter Villa and reported to Jalester before Arc G begins.

**Aftermath**
Jalester passes the Halek Farran lead and the Cassalanter Villa address to the Watch's Criminal Intelligence division. Within a week, they compile a basic dossier: Halek Farran's name, the Watch guard rotation at Cassalanter Villa's service entrance, and a sketch of the villa's walled exterior. This dossier becomes part of the LA's Arc G intelligence package (see Arc Hooks above). If the PCs are planning the Cassalanter heist, it gives them advance knowledge of the service gate timing before they conduct their own reconnaissance.

If Vhaspar was killed: the testimony is lost. Halek Farran cleans house — he disappears from Waterdeep within 48 hours. The dossier is never compiled, and the Cassalanter Villa service entrance information is not available through Alliance channels.

### Mission 5 — Fentree's Ledger
**Level 6 | 4 renown | Masked Lord's writ access (see Aftermath)**

**Hook**
Jalester sends a message to Trollskull Manor by regular courier — not a Watch seal, not a paper bird, just a folded letter with his name at the bottom. It's more formal than his usual style: *"An old trade investigator named Orvane Fentree was found dead in his home in the Trades Ward on Greengrass morning. The Watch has ruled it natural causes. His personal records — twenty years of guild and contract documentation — are missing from his study. Laeral Silverhand has a particular interest in those records. Find them before anyone else reads them."*

**Background** *(DM only)*
Orvane Fentree, 71, spent two decades tracking guild financial irregularities for the City of Waterdeep. He retired seven years ago but continued maintaining his private ledger out of habit. That ledger contains entries from 1244 DR showing a Neverember-linked shell company — Dunberton Holdings — still paying maintenance access fees to a vault custodian in the Waterdeep Vault district. If correctly interpreted, the entries prove the Vault of Dragons is active and located within Waterdeep — not Neverwinter. Laeral's intelligence has been wrong for years.

Fentree died of a genuine heart attack. His ledger was stolen by **Rezka** (use Spy stat block), a Xanathar Guild operative who had been assigned to shadow him after a guild analyst flagged Fentree as a potential threat to their interest in the Grand Game. Rezka searched the home while the Watch was still on scene, pocketed the ledger, and left before anyone noted her.

Rezka is now in a bidding situation of her own creation. She approached two buyers: **Noska Ur'gray** (a Xanathar Guild enforcer — use the Thug stat block, veteran training) representing the guild's senior intelligence cell, and a Cassalanter household representative named **Emira Volstren** (use the Noble stat block). Noska can pay 600 gp. The Cassalanters have offered 400 gp and a favor. Rezka is meeting with both tonight at the **Green Dragon Inn**, Bridge Street, Trades Ward — she's running them in adjacent rooms, planning to sell to the highest offer.

**Act 1 — Finding Rezka**
The investigation begins at Fentree's home, Copper Street, Trades Ward.

- **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** of the study: the dust on the shelves is disturbed in a consistent pattern — someone knew where to look. Three ledger bindings are present on the upper shelf but the fourth spine slot is empty and clean, meaning the missing volume was pulled within the last two days. A single copper button on the floor near the window doesn't match any of Fentree's clothing.
- **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) with Fentree's neighbor, Orlass Hemmet:** She saw "a red-haired woman, thirties, practical clothes" leaving Fentree's building the morning he died. She was carrying a satchel when she arrived; it looked heavier when she left.
- **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)** (Trades Ward contacts, asking about Rezka by physical description): a Caravan Court money-changer confirms she's a freelance information broker, seen regularly in the counting house quarter. She booked a private room at the Green Dragon Inn for tonight under the name "Torval."

**Act 2 — The Green Dragon Inn**
Rezka's plan: meet Noska at second bell in Room 4, Cassalanter representative Emira at third bell in Room 6. She's in Room 3 herself, reviewing the ledger, until the first meeting.

The Green Dragon's layout: ground floor taproom, single staircase, four rooms on the upper floor accessible from one corridor. The innkeeper, **Pravin Cloth**, is incurious about guests and their business.

Arriving before second bell: Noska is downstairs with two bodyguards (Bandit stat block each) at a corner table. Emira Volstren arrives five minutes later, alone but with a Cassalanter house guard waiting outside.

Options:
- *Take Rezka before either meeting begins (DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) approach to Room 3):* She surrenders without fight if caught unprepared with the ledger in her hands. She'll trade the ledger for safe exit from Waterdeep — the Xanathar Guild will come for her when the sale falls through, and she knows it. The PCs can choose to take the ledger outright or honor the trade.
- *Disrupt the meetings:* Starting a confrontation in the taproom draws Noska and Emira's attention to each other — a DC 14 Charisma (Deception) check can be used to convince each that the other has already made a deal, collapsing the bidding competition. This delays but does not prevent Rezka from rescheduling.
- *Wait and intercept after:* If the PCs let the bidding conclude, Noska wins at 600 gp. Rezka hands over the ledger in Room 4 at second bell and thirty minutes. The PCs must intercept Noska and his bodyguards in the street or on the staircase — three against three, plus Noska himself.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: The ledger reaches Jalester within 24 hours of recovery. Laeral's intelligence is updated.
- +1: Noska Ur'gray is captured or the Cassalanter representative Emira Volstren is identified by name and reported to Jalester — either confirms external interest in the vault's location.

**Aftermath**
If the ledger reaches Jalester: he passes it directly to Laeral. Her intelligence corrects — she now knows the Vault of Dragons is in Waterdeep, active, and that Dunberton Holdings was the shell structure used to maintain it. She accelerates her political preparation. Mission 6 becomes available.

If the ledger goes to Noska: the Xanathar Guild has confirmation of the vault's Waterdeep location before the PCs have the Stone of Golorr deciphered. In Arc F-G, the Xanathar Guild's intelligence about the vault's ward structure is one step ahead of the PCs. Xanathar's faction response team in Arc F has advance knowledge that a vault access attempt is coming.

If Emira Volstren gets it: the Cassalanters share it with their infernal advisors. In Arc G-H, the Cassalanters arrive at the vault approach with a partial map of the ceremonial lock's location.

### Mission 6 — An Audience with the Open Lord
**Level 7 | 4 renown | Masked Lord's Writ (see Aftermath)**

> **Design note — Trigger:** This mission activates only if Mission 5 succeeded and the ledger reached Laeral. If Mission 5 failed, replace the Hook with: *Jalester arrives at Trollskull Manor and tells the PCs that Laeral Silverhand wants to speak with them directly. She has heard about their work. She has questions only firsthand witnesses can answer.* The audience scene (Act 2 onward) runs the same from that point.

**Hook**
Two City Watch officers in full dress uniform arrive at Trollskull Manor in the morning with a sealed summons on Waterdeep's formal vellum. The Open Lord Laeral Silverhand requests the presence of the adventuring company at Piergeiron's Palace at the close of the afternoon bell. Attendance is characterized as voluntary. The summons is signed.

**Background** *(DM only)*
Laeral has been building toward this moment since the ledger arrived. She now knows the vault is in Waterdeep, that the Stone of Golorr has resurfaced, and that at least four factions are competing for access to it. She does not know the current location of the Stone. She does not know the PCs' exact position in the Grand Game, though she suspects they're closer to it than anyone officially sanctioned.

She intends to offer them a Masked Lord's Writ — a legal instrument that authorizes the vault operation and gives the PCs formal standing as city agents during Arc J. Without the writ, opening the vault is technically trespassing on a sealed Lords' estate. With it, the Watch stands down, the Vault district gate is opened, and any legal complication from Arc J is handled through official channels.

She will not give this writ freely. She wants three things: the current holder of the Stone (whatever they can tell her), confirmation that the gold will reach the city rather than a faction, and the PCs' judgment on a specific political question she is weighing.

**The Political Question:** One of the villain factions has approached Laeral with a deal. The specific offer: Manshoon's Splinter has offered Laeral a list of all five Masked Lord identities, none of which she currently holds, in exchange for permission to take 20% of the vault's contents before it reaches the treasury. She is genuinely considering it. Not because she wants to reward Manshoon, but because the list would give her political leverage over opponents within the Lords' structure who have been obstructing her recovery efforts.

She discloses this to the PCs openly. She does not present it as a test. It is, in fact, a test.

**Act 1 — Piergeiron's Palace**
The palace is everything it should be — clean marble, Watch officers at every door, a functionary who leads the PCs through three corridors and a waiting room to a private audience chamber with a long table and a fire that doesn't need tending. Laeral is already there.

Read aloud:
> The Open Lord of Waterdeep doesn't look like a statue come to life. She looks like a woman who has been working since before you woke up. She's sitting at the far end of the table with a cup of tea she isn't drinking, a small stack of letters she isn't reading, and the expression of someone who has already thought about this meeting from your side of it. When you enter, she sets the cup down. "Sit. I don't have a long speech for you. I have questions."

Laeral asks three questions:
1. "Who has the Stone of Golorr right now?"
2. "What do the other factions intend to do with the gold when the vault is opened?"
3. "I've been offered something. I'd like to know what you think before I decide."

She listens. She does not interrupt. She takes notes in a small book.

**Act 2 — The Manshoon Offer**
Laeral presents the Splinter offer plainly: "Manshoon's faction has offered me a complete list of all five Masked Lord identities in exchange for permission to remove twenty percent of the vault's contents before it reaches the city. I want to know if you think I should accept."

She adds: "Don't tell me what you think I want to hear. I have enough of that."

*If the PCs argue against the offer with a specific reason (DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) or a DC 13 Intelligence check to construct a convincing strategic argument):* Laeral considers for a long moment. "You're right that accepting it creates a precedent I'll regret." She declines the offer.

*If the PCs have no opinion, or argue for accepting it:* Laeral makes the deal with the Splinter. In Arc J, Manshoon holds a piece of political leverage — he can threaten to expose one Masked Lord identity publicly unless the PCs give him a concession during the vault endgame. Laeral will not be embarrassed about her choice, but she won't help them manage its consequences.

*If the PCs lie about the Stone's location or the factions' intentions (DC 16 Wisdom (Insight) on Laeral's part):* She doesn't call them on it at the table. After the meeting, the writ is still issued, but Jalester receives a private note from her that reads: "Keep an eye on them."

**Act 3 — The Writ**
Laeral rises and retrieves a sealed document from a locked drawer. The Masked Lord's Writ authorizes the party by name to enter the Vault of Dragons district and access the ceremonial lock under the authority of the Open Lord of Waterdeep. It includes a provision for Watch non-interference during the operation.

"The gold goes to the treasury. All of it. If you come out with it and take it somewhere else, the writ is void and what happens next is between you and the Lords." She looks at each of them once. "Good luck."

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: The PCs provide accurate information about the Stone's location and at least two factions' intentions during the audience.
- +1: The PCs successfully argue against the Manshoon offer, and Laeral declines it.

**Aftermath**
The Masked Lord's Writ gives the PCs a legal instrument for Arc J. City Watch officers who challenge them at the Vault district gate defer to it; the gate is opened within four rounds of presentation. One legal intervention is available during Arc J: Laeral will authorize Watch action on the PCs' behalf once — used to deal with a rival faction complication, a legal challenge, or an NPC arrest.

If the Manshoon offer was rejected: Manshoon's political leverage play in Arc J does not occur. The Splinter does not have a card to play at the vault.

If the Manshoon offer was accepted: In Arc J, Manshoon holds specific leverage — he can delay Watch cooperation with the PCs for up to two hours by activating a political contact, or threaten to expose one Masked Lord to the public press. The PCs may be forced to negotiate with him rather than fight him straight through Arc I.

If Jalester Silvermane was identified as Illuun-compromised (Harper Mission 6): any intelligence the PCs shared with him during this mission — including the Stone's location — has potentially reached Illuun. Laeral does not know about the compromise. The PCs face a choice: tell her now (she removes Jalester from active duty immediately, which disrupts LA operations in Arc J) or handle it themselves.
