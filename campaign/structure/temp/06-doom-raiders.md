## The Doom Raiders (Zhentarim)

*Contact: Davil Starsong, always at the Yawning Portal. He keeps a room upstairs and does all his business in the taproom, which he has decided is safer than a private office and more honest than a warehouse. He's a sun elf who looks like a man who has decided that charm is both a genuine quality and an excellent professional tool, and has spent enough decades cultivating the combination that he can no longer tell where one ends and the other begins. He uses an elven lute as a spellcasting focus and has it slung across his back during every meeting. He negotiates with grace and aplomb even when he's been drinking, which is most of the time. He is genuinely invested in ending the street violence Manshoon's cell has caused. His investment in ending the violence is the position of someone who cannot run a legitimate criminal enterprise in a city where an illegitimate one keeps shooting people.*

*His warmth is harder to deal with than coldness would be.*

*After Mission 2, the City Watch arrests Davil on suspicion of involvement in the Gralhund Villa incident. Contact passes to **Tashlyn Yafeera**, who communicates exclusively by flying snake. She is Master of Arms and Mercenaries and serves as Captain of the City Guard at the South Gate under Vorondar Levelstone, a dwarf magister whose trust she has carefully built over two years. She is precise and carries sharper intelligence than Davil, because she has been spending the past year building sources inside Manshoon's cell rather than business contacts in the Trades Ward. Her briefings use fewer words and expect more inference.*

*Davil is released several weeks after Mission 4. He comes back sharper, more deliberate. The warmth is still there, quieter now.*

### The Grand Game — Doom Raiders Stance

The Doom Raiders are the legitimate Black Network in Waterdeep, and it is at war with itself. Manshoon's Zhentarim splinter is a rival cell that infiltrated the same organization, actively working to destroy Davil's network, his people, and everything they built. This is the Doom Raiders' defining preoccupation. The Grand Game is secondary.

They also see the half-million dragons as the most significant opportunity of their careers. Efforts are underway to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep, and the reforming Zhentarim Council is choosing its membership. That money could buy Davil's cell a seat at the table — genuine continental influence rather than the precarious position of a regional branch operation. PCs who recover the gold and deliver it to the Doom Raiders are buying their patrons into something much larger than one campaign.

What the Doom Raiders do not tell the party, until Mission 4 forces the truth out: **Skeemo Weirdbottle has been a traitor from the start.** The Master of Magic has been feeding intelligence to Manshoon's cell — and to House Gralhund before that — since before the party was recruited. Everything the party has done for the faction has been visible to the Splinter. Davil has suspected something for months. Tashlyn confirmed it during his arrest.

**What the Doom Raiders share proactively:**
- Istrid Horn's lending operation is available to faction members at reasonable rates: up to 200 gp at Renown 1, up to 2,500 gp at Renown 10
- Tashlyn's City Guard position provides one piece of actionable Splinter intelligence per tenday at Renown 3+
- Ziraj the Hunter can be requested for a single operation per arc — captured alive or quietly eliminated. He will not take suicidal assignments. He does not argue about it.

**What the Doom Raiders ask for:**
- Anything the party learns about Manshoon's cell: safe house locations, lieutenant identities, communication methods, operational timing. One confirmed piece per tenday is worth more than a dramatic report filed once a month.
- Discretion. The Doom Raiders are criminals running legitimate-fronted businesses in a city where the City Watch already has their file. The party should conduct missions in ways that do not invite scrutiny.
- Loyalty in the specific sense that the Doom Raiders define it: you are one of theirs. When something goes wrong — and things will go wrong — the faction will do what it can for you. The same is expected in return.

**Grand Game goal:** Vault gold to the Zhentarim Council fund. Manshoon's cell dismantled before it can contest the vault. Skeemo's betrayal cleaned up internally without Watch involvement. Ziraj alive. Davil keeps the moral ledger as balanced as his business allows.

### Mission 1 — The Dockside Killer
**Level 2 | 2 renown | 50 gp if target survives**

**Hook**
Davil at the Yawning Portal, a second drink in front of an empty chair. He waits for the party to sit before he starts. "Someone is killing elf and half-elf sailors in the Dock Ward. Three dead so far, each one decapitated in an alley off Ship Street in the dead of night. The City Watch is investigating and getting nowhere, which is the Watch's characteristic rate of progress on anything that happens in the Dock Ward after dark." He slides a note across the table: the victims' names, the general stretch of wharf. "Find out what's happening and stop it." He pauses. "I have people who could do this, but you'll blend in better than my usual crew." He says it without inflection. It is accurate.

**Background** *(DM only)*
**Soluun Xibrindas** is a drow gunslinger operating independently — a renegade from Bregan D'aerthe, disowned by Jarlaxle for conducting unsanctioned operations that exposed the Sea Maidens Faire's presence in Waterdeep. He kills half-elves out of personal hatred with neither faction backing nor mission authorization. He is skilled, careful, and has been doing this for three weeks without leaving useful evidence. He carries a forged Bregan D'aerthe identification token as cover. The forgery is good enough to confuse a casual examination.

The Muleskull Tavern is on Ship Street in the Dock Ward, three blocks from the waterfront. **Heldar**, a drunk half-elf sailor, drinks there alone most nights and walks back to his ship from the same direction each time. He matches the pattern of the previous three victims. He does not know this.

**Act 1 — The Docks at Night**
Three nights of investigation surface the pattern. Characters who spend the evening hours on Ship Street and the adjacent waterfront, and who make one of the following, establish the thread:

- A **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check from the waterfront at midnight picks up something: one of the shadows near the Muleskull's side alley is staying in place while the others move with the wind off the harbor. This is Night 2 or later.
- A **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** conversation with two or more dock workers surfaces the name Heldar and the specific tavern: he was drinking with the second victim the night before that one died. He's been back every night since and doesn't know he's next.
- A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check examining the three alley sites as a group identifies that all three victims were alone, all after midnight, all within three blocks of the Muleskull, all walking the same route toward the eastern wharf. The killer is not random. The killer knows this stretch of the docks.

**Act 2 — The Ambush**
Night 3 (or later, at the party's pace): Heldar leaves the Muleskull after last bell, swaying, heading east on Ship Street. Soluun is already in position, in the gap between two buildings off Net Street, blade drawn, watching Heldar approach.

A **DC 18 Wisdom (Perception)** check spots him before he strikes. On failure, Heldar has already been hit: 11 (3d6+1) piercing before anyone can react, and Soluun is out of the shadows and moving. A character who positioned ahead of the route (described this intent at the start of the night) gains Advantage on the Perception check.

**Soluun Xibrindas** — use the **Scout** stat block (2024 *Monster Manual*), with Advantage on Stealth checks in dim light or darkness, and a hand crossbow instead of a shortbow. He fights until reduced to half his hit points, then attempts to disengage and flee over rooftops. He is fast and knows the Dock Ward's roofline.

*Pursuing Soluun:* Three **DC 14 Athletics (Strength or Dexterity)** checks to keep pace across the rooftops. On three successes, he is cornered above a warehouse on Sail Street and surrenders rather than jump. On failure, he escapes.

The forged Bregan D'aerthe token is on his person, a small silver disc with a stylized spider motif that does not quite match genuine D'aerthe tokens (a **DC 14 Intelligence (History)** check or prior knowledge of genuine D'aerthe tokens reveals the discrepancy). Someone made this for him or he made it himself. Davil, when shown it, is interested and does not explain why.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: Heldar survives and Soluun is stopped — killed, captured, or driven off and unable to return before the Watch investigation changes his hunting ground.
- +1 (supplementary): Soluun is taken alive and delivered to the Watch. His presence in the Dock Ward and his connection (or lack thereof) to Bregan D'aerthe becomes a Watch file that the party can reference in Arc H.

**Aftermath**
Heldar, sober the next morning and informed of what happened, pays the party 50 gp out of a sailor's thin savings and is embarrassed that this is all he has. He is not wrong to be embarrassed. He also knows the docks well and, if the party is ever looking for passage information, ship manifests, or waterfront gossip, will help within his considerable limits.

If Soluun escaped: he goes to ground, and Davil notes it in his file. The drow resurfaces briefly in Arc H if the party goes to the Sea Maidens Faire — he's watching the ships from the waterfront and recognizes the party on sight.

### Mission 2 — The Poisoned Delivery
**Level 3 | 2 renown | 15 pp from recipient (if delivered as-is)**

**Hook**
A flying snake, though Davil prefers face-to-face. The scroll tied to it reads: *"Weirdbottle's Concoctions, Trades Ward. Pick up a package from Skeemo — he'll know you're coming. Deliver it to a contact near the God Catcher in the Castle Ward. Look for a purple cloak. Keep what she gives you."* No signature.

**Background** *(DM only)*
Skeemo Weirdbottle has placed four **potions of poison** in a small silk-lined coffer, labeled — carefully, in a gnome's precise handwriting — as *potions of mind reading*. The recipient is **Esvele Rosznar**, the masked thief known as the Black Viper, who plans to use them to incapacitate marks before robbing them. She believes she is buying legitimate goods from a reliable supplier.

This is also a test. Skeemo is observing whether the party asks questions, inspects the package, or delivers without thinking. The result goes into his intelligence report to Manshoon. A party that delivers blindly is useful and controllable; a party that notices something is wrong and says nothing is interesting; a party that refuses is a problem he will flag.

Davil does not know the contents. He arranged a courier and didn't check the package. This matters later.

**Act 1 — Weirdbottle's Concoctions**
A cramped shop in the Trades Ward, the bottles organized with a gnome's compulsive precision. Skeemo is behind the counter: small, meticulous, pleasant in a way that has no warmth in it. He knows the party by name. He produces the coffer from under the counter and places it precisely on the surface. "It's paid for. Handle it carefully." He does not answer questions about the contents. "It's not your business, and if it were, I'd have told you."

The coffer is sealed with a wax stamp. A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check notices the lid is slightly warmer than it should be — the potions inside have a faint alchemical smell that is wrong for mind-reading compounds. Uncorking one of the bottles (breaking the seal) requires a **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** to detect the smell of oil of taggit, a contact poison, beneath the sweet aromatic cover.

**Act 2 — The God Catcher**
The God Catcher is one of the Walking Statues of Waterdeep, a huge stone figure in the Castle Ward with one hand outstretched over a street intersection. A hire-coach is waiting in its shadow at the arranged time. The woman inside wears a hooded purple cloak and does not offer her name.

**Esvele Rosznar** is a Tethyrian noblewoman in her twenties: composed, direct, evaluating everything. She wants the coffer. She produces a black velvet pouch with fifteen platinum pieces without being asked. The exchange is meant to take thirty seconds.

The party's choices shape what happens:

- *Deliver as-is:* The exchange completes. The coach departs for the Sea Ward (House Rosznar, Thunderstaff Way, if the party follows). A Watch broadsheet two days later: a Sea Ward noble family was found incapacitated after a dinner party. No suspect. The party can make the connection with a **DC 13 Intelligence** check or by reviewing the broadsheet carefully.
- *Warn Esvele:* She tests the coffer (her own Arcana training is sufficient) and confirms the poison. She is cold and precise about this. The pouch goes back in her pocket. She instructs her driver to leave. She does not explain who she is or what she intended, but she notes the party's faces with care. They will see her again.
- *Substitute the contents:* If the party has access to genuine potions of mind reading (or convincing fakes), this requires a **DC 14 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check to swap without breaking the seal and a **DC 13 Arcana** check to reseal plausibly. Esvele inspects the coffer before paying; on success, the exchange completes cleanly; on failure, she notices the seal is wrong and requires an explanation.
- *Refuse to deliver:* The mission fails. The party returns the coffer to Davil, who was not expecting it back. He looks at the coffer, opens it, and is quiet for a while. He does not immediately know what is in it. But he files it in the category of things he has decided to look at more carefully.

**Act 3 — The Arrest**
Davil is arrested two days after this mission. The Watch's post-Gralhund crackdown has swept up every Zhentarim figure they can find, legitimate or not. Davil is held at Castle Waterdeep pending questioning by the Lords. The flying snake arrives from Tashlyn: *"Davil is unavailable. Continue normal operations. Await further contact."* Four words of cover, zero words of comfort.

If the party questioned what was in the coffer and told Davil: he is grateful — quietly, before the Watch comes — and mentions that whoever put those potions together did it with access to the Network's supplier chain. He doesn't say Skeemo's name. He has not confirmed it yet.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +1: The delivery was completed by any means (deliver as-is, warn Esvele, substitute contents) — the contact interaction was handled.
- +1 (supplementary): The party warned Esvele or substituted the contents. Tashlyn hears about it (Esvele has her own network) and files it as a mark in the party's favor: they exercise judgment without being told to.

**Aftermath**
Davil is in custody. Tashlyn runs operations from the South Gate cover. The next mission brief arrives by flying snake in a different hand, without pleasantries.

### Mission 3 — The Missing Snobeedle
**Level 4 | 3 renown | 500 gp from the Snobeedle family (any outcome short of false report)**

**Hook**
Tashlyn's snake, a tight three lines: *"Snobeedle halfling family, Undercliff. Offering 500 gp for information on a missing son — Dasher. Investigate. Money goes to the Network if recovered. Don't cause trouble with the Watch."* The note uses "recovered" as if Dasher is an object. This is probably not accidental.

**Background** *(DM only)*
**Dasher Snobeedle**, nineteen years old, second son of the Snobeedle halfling family who run an orchard and meadery in Undercliff east of the city walls. He disappeared eight months ago. He was not taken. He walked into the Dock Ward, found the Shard Shunners, a halfling wererat gang, and chose to join them. He was infected with lycanthropy willingly, after two weeks of deliberation. He has not contacted his family. He does not plan to.

**Kelso Fiddlewick** leads the Shard Shunners. He knows exactly who Dasher is, what the family is offering, and what the party is going to want. He has spoken to Dasher about this possibility. Dasher's position: he is not going back. He means it.

The Snobeedle family does not know Dasher is a wererat. They believe he was taken. **Blossom Snobeedle**, Dasher's mother, is a druid of some years who has spent eight months believing her son is in danger. She is wrong about the nature of his situation. She is right that she has lost him.

**Act 1 — The Snobeedle Orchard**
The Snobeedle Orchard and Meadery is exactly what it presents as: tidy halfling buildings surrounded by fruit trees, a drinking hall with low ceilings and long tables, a shop with jars of varietal mead and fresh cider. **Blossom Snobeedle** receives the party in the drinking hall and gives them tea she has been preparing since they came up the road. Old druid, capable, quietly falling apart.

She has a handful of Dasher's belongings. Among them: a letter she found in his room, never sent, addressed to a friend she doesn't know. She's read it. She offers it reluctantly. The letter does not name a gang or a crime — it describes hating the meadery, feeling like the orchard is a sentence rather than a home, and an unnamed possibility he's been offered that "nobody here would understand." A **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** check reading the letter suggests whoever Dasher is with now, he went to them.

Blossom adds one thing: "He hated silver. Ever since he was little. He'd get a rash." She says it without understanding what it means.

**Act 2 — The Shard Shunners**
The Dock Ward, late afternoon. Three independent paths lead to the gang:

- Dock workers near Net Street who've seen a halfling crew moving goods at night; they know a face, not a name, but can describe Kelso's scarred jaw.
- A potion seller near Sail Street stocks silver-free pain tinctures, ordered by the same halfling woman every two weeks. She'll describe the customer if the party asks and offers coin or a plausible reason.
- A Xanathar Guild runner who was shaken down last tenday by a halfling crew on the east waterfront. He doesn't want to be shaken down again and will point generally at the Blushing Mermaid tavern on Sail Street.

**Kelso Fiddlewick** is at the Blushing Mermaid by late afternoon. He sees the party before they see him. He does not run. He is at his table when they arrive and looks at them with the specific patience of someone who has been expecting this.

"You're here about Dasher," he says. "He's not lost. He's where he wants to be." He is willing to arrange a meeting — on Dasher's terms, not the party's. "Talk to him first. Then decide what you're going to tell the family."

**Act 3 — Dasher**
Dasher is at a Shard Shunner den near the eastern waterfront — a converted boat storage building, low ceilings, eight halflings going about ordinary business. He is recognizable from a childhood portrait Blossom provided: same round face, new scar on his left hand, calmer than the photo would predict.

He is not going home. He explains this without apology or hostility. The meadery was suffocating. His parents are good people and he could not breathe. He chose this. The lycanthropy was part of the choosing. He is not in danger. He is home.

The party's options:

- *Report honestly to Blossom:* Dasher is alive, safe, and has chosen the Shard Shunners. She will come to the Dock Ward herself within a day. Dasher and Kelso will deal with her arrival. The party gets the 500 gp. Nothing is resolved.
- *Broker a family meeting:* A **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** check with Dasher (framing it as one conversation, his terms, no Watch involvement) gets him to agree. He comes to the Snobeedle Orchard on a market day, meets Blossom for two hours, and leaves. The conversation does not go the way either of them planned. Blossom pays. Dasher goes back to the Dock Ward.
- *Offer to find a lycanthropy cure:* Dasher did not ask for a cure. A **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion)** check to propose it as his choice, not the family's, with zero pressure attached. On success, he considers it. A *Remove Curse* cast by a willing character (or arranged through a temple) cures the lycanthropy if Dasher consents in the moment. He still does not go home — but he goes under different terms. He may return to the orchard on his own eventually. He does not say this.
- *Report Dasher as dead:* The Snobeedles grieve, the Watch is not involved, the Network gets 500 gp. Tashlyn considers this acceptable. Kelso, if he finds out later, will not forget it.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +2: The Snobeedle family receives accurate information about Dasher and he is not delivered to them against his will. Any path except a false report qualifies.
- +1 (supplementary): The family meeting is brokered — Blossom and Dasher have a real conversation. Tashlyn hears about this and says nothing. Her silence on this particular decision is itself a tell.

**Aftermath**
Kelso Fiddlewick remembers that the party dealt with his people with something approximating fairness. He is the same Kelso who features in OG-M3 — if the party has also worked with the Order of the Gauntlet, they have met him twice, in different capacities, and he has noticed. A Field Ward gang with reasons to not be actively hostile is a limited but real asset.

### Mission 4 — Silencing Skeemo
**Level 5 | 3 renown | Contents of Skeemo's satchel**

**Hook**
Tashlyn's flying snake arrives at an unusual hour — before dawn. The message is shorter than usual: *"Weirdbottle's Concoctions. Tonight. Do not be seen. Do not leave a trail. Meeting at midnight: Dead End Alley, Dock Ward."*

Tashlyn is there in person. This has not happened before. She does not waste time on explanation: "Skeemo Weirdbottle has been selling us to Manshoon's cell. Three confirmed courier exchanges in two weeks with a known Splinter contact. He has also been feeding House Gralhund before that, which means Gralhund had our operational schedule, which means Manshoon had it when Gralhund fell." A pause. "This has been going on for longer than we knew. He needs to be eliminated. No trace back to the Network. The Watch cannot open a file on this." She looks at the party. "Everything he knows about you is already on Kolat Towers' wall. I am sorry about that."

**Background** *(DM only)*
Skeemo Weirdbottle has been a traitor since before the party was recruited. Manshoon bought him with access to rare magical components and protection from the Watch's periodic shop inspections. House Gralhund used him for operational scheduling. His betrayal is the reason the Splinter has been one step ahead of the Doom Raiders throughout the internal war.

He has also been watching for exactly this moment. He has a prepared escape route: a dray hired for tomorrow, a route through the Castle Ward, and access to the teleportation circle at Yellowspire (Manshoon's Trades Ward outpost), which will deliver him directly to Kolat Towers. If he reaches it, he is out of reach until the party can go to Kolat Towers in Arc I.

He knows they are coming. He does not know when.

**Act 1 — The Approach**
Weirdbottle's Concoctions is closed at mid-morning — unusual for a Trades Ward shop. A **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** check near the building: a paper bird exits through a crack in the upstairs shutter as the party rounds the corner. He has a warning network. He's activating it.

Three entry options:
- *Quiet entry:* **DC 12 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** on the shop's back door. Skeemo is in the back room with his satchel, preparing to leave. The shop is empty of customers.
- *Forced entry:* Skeemo hears the door and goes immediately for the skylight. He casts *Fly* once he clears the building.
- *Rooftop approach:* Characters who circle to the building's rear and access the adjacent roof can attempt to block the skylight exit before he reaches it. **DC 14 Athletics** to be in position before he gets there; success means he is caught before he can cast.

**Act 2 — The Chase**
Skeemo on the street: he climbs into a dray parked around the corner — prearranged, five random commuter passengers already aboard — and the driver moves immediately into traffic. A **DC 14 Strength (Athletics)** check to catch the dray before it reaches a major thoroughfare; failure means he's in the Castle Ward flow, using crowds for cover.

Skeemo in the air: He casts *Fly* (if he reached the skylight) and goes north, staying low over rooftops to avoid Griffon Cavalry sightlines. Three rounds of ranged targeting before he casts *Greater Invisibility* on himself. With Greater Invisibility active: the approach requires a **DC 20 Wisdom (Perception)** or a spell that removes invisibility (*Faerie Fire*, *Glitterdust*, dust of tracking — any effect that reveals location). His AC is not high; the problem is finding him, not defeating him.

If the party loses him: Skeemo reaches the Yellowspire teleportation circle and arrives at Kolat Towers. He is now Manshoon's guest. The party will encounter him in Arc I, and he will recognize them and alert his new patron.

**Act 3 — The Resolution**
If Skeemo is caught — in the shop, in the alley, on the rooftop — how the party handles the elimination is their decision. Tashlyn's constraint is no Watch investigation tracing to the Network: quiet methods are preferable to obvious ones. The shop itself can be managed if the elimination is inside it; an alley death will be found quickly and investigated. A sufficiently creative staging (a shop accident, an apparent theft gone wrong) removes the Watch file entirely.

Skeemo's satchel contains: his spellbook (all prepared spells, useful reference even without Skeemo to cast them), a *potion of mind reading*, 150 gp in a silk coin purse, and three paper birds addressed to a Kolat Towers contact name — **Vevette Blackwater**. The paper birds are written in code, but even the coded content confirms: Skeemo was reporting to Manshoon directly. Tashlyn takes the paper birds and the coin purse. She leaves the rest.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +2: Skeemo is eliminated without opening a Watch investigation that touches the Network.
- +1 (supplementary): The satchel is recovered and the paper birds are delivered to Tashlyn. She reads them carefully and does not speak for a while. When she does: "Vevette Blackwater. Good. Now I have a name." She adds it to a file the party has not seen.

**Aftermath**
Davil is released from custody within the week — the Lords satisfied that his cell was not responsible for the Gralhund violence. He comes back to the Yawning Portal, orders a drink, and sits with it for a few minutes before saying anything. He was fond of Skeemo. He does not say this. He says: "You should know — whatever Skeemo knew about your operation is already in Kolat Towers. I'd keep that in mind for what comes next."

He also, quietly, buys the party a drink. He does not say what it's for.

### Mission 5 — The Yellowspire Job
**Level 6 | 4 renown | Pass-amulet distribution ledger; three coded correspondence letters**

**Hook**
Davil at the Yawning Portal, in person, for the first time since his release. He has pared something away. He wastes less time getting to the point.

"Yellowspire. The old wizard tower in the Trades Ward, on Copper Pot Lane. Manshoon's cell uses it as a relay: there's a teleportation circle inside linked directly to Kolat Towers. They're storing something there I need: the distribution ledger for the pass-amulets. Every lieutenant who carries one, their rotation schedule, when each amulet is refreshed." He looks at the party directly. "With that ledger, we know exactly who to take the amulets from and when. Kolat Towers has a force field — no amulet, no entry." A pause. "I want the ledger. I want them to not know it's gone." He has a diagram of the exterior, observed over months of his own surveillance. "This is not an officially authorized request. I mention this so you know the scope of what I am asking."

**Background** *(DM only)*
Yellowspire is a narrow four-story tower on Copper Pot Lane in the Trades Ward, operating as a "private residence" with shuttered windows and foot traffic patterns that don't match the cover. At any time, two to three Splinter agents occupy it: two **Spies** on the ground floor managing the communication traffic, one **Mage** on the second floor running the teleportation circle operations and research.

The teleportation circle is on the ground floor. It has a trigger rune — stepping into it without first pressing a concealed floor plate deactivates the rune for thirty seconds; missing this step summons four additional Splinter agents from Kolat Towers in ten minutes. The pass-amulet ledger is in a locked desk on the second floor. Three coded correspondence letters are in the same desk — they outline Kolat Towers' operational priorities for the coming month and name Vevette Blackwater as the coordinating officer (confirming what Tashlyn got from Skeemo's paper birds).

**Act 1 — Surveillance**
One day of external observation surfaces three independent entry approaches:

- *The coded knock:* Two slow taps, three rapid. Agents use this at the front door. A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check tracking the door over four hours captures the pattern accurately. Gets the party through the front door as apparent Splinter contacts; the agents inside will ask for a passphrase the party doesn't have — a **DC 13 Charisma (Deception)** check to deflect ("Vevette's code change — nobody told you?") buys thirty seconds.
- *The rooftop approach:* The adjacent building's roof connects via a four-foot gap to Yellowspire's third-floor level. A **DC 14 Athletics (Dexterity or Strength)** jump, made with Advantage if a rope is rigged first. Gets the party directly to the third floor, bypassing the ground-floor agents entirely.
- *The referral cover:* Tashlyn has a name — Rorik Halvars, a former mercenary who runs legitimate contracts through the Splinter. The party can claim to be Rorik's referral for a pickup job. A **DC 13 Charisma (Deception)** check to sell it; on success, the ground-floor agents let them in and go back to their work.

**Act 2 — Inside**
*Ground floor:* Two **Spies**. The teleportation circle occupies the center — a ringed diagram in permanent chalk with an activation rune visible at the entry step. The concealed floor plate is a flagstone in the northeast corner, slightly lighter in color than the others; a **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check in good light spots it. Failing to press it before entering the circle triggers the ten-minute arrival clock.

*Second floor:* One **Mage** at a desk with notes, one agent asleep in a cot. The Mage has *Counterspell* prepared and will use it against any first-level or higher spell she can see. The locked desk: **DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** to open cleanly. **DC 18 Strength** to force it — loud, wakes the sleeping agent immediately. The desk contains both the ledger and the three correspondence letters.

**Act 3 — The Exit**
Clean exit requires the party to leave before anyone counts heads or the teleportation circle backup clock has expired. As a group, one **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth)** check (use the lowest result) for the rooftop approach, or a **DC 13 Charisma (Deception)** to explain departure convincingly at the front door.

If the arrival clock is active: four Splinter agents appear in the teleportation circle at the ten-minute mark. They are combat-capable and alert. The party has a choice — fight through them, already having the ledger, or have been gone before they arrive.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +2: The pass-amulet ledger is recovered and delivered to Davil. He looks at it for a long time. Then: "We know where the amulets are. All of them. When you're ready for Kolat Towers — tell me a day before." He does not explain what he plans to do from outside.
- +1: The three correspondence letters are also recovered. They confirm Vevette Blackwater's operational role. Tashlyn adds this to her file and shares a summary with the party: Vevette coordinates the strike team deployments, which means she is a priority target in Arc I.
- +1 (bonus): The exit is clean — no Splinter agent can confirm who broke in. Davil receives the ledger, and Yellowspire never changes its security posture in response. The Splinter does not know what was taken.

**Aftermath**
Davil hands the party a copy of the ledger's most relevant page: the schedule showing which lieutenant carries a pass-amulet on which rotation, and where the handoffs happen. "Three lieutenants, three amulets," he says. "The handoffs are in public places. That was deliberate on someone's part. It makes them harder to take. You'll need to decide how you want to approach it." He folds the page and slides it across. "That's Arc I's front door."

### Mission 6 — Ziraj's Last Hunt
**Level 7 | 4 renown | Rooftop diagram of Kolat Towers' force field gaps**

**Hook**
No flying snake. **Yagra Stonefist** appears at Trollskull Manor's door at two in the morning. Armor on. Not winded despite clearly having run. She does not knock politely.

"Ziraj has been hit. He's moving but not fast. Manshoon sent six people. He got three." She is already stepping back from the door to move. "The other three are between him and Fala's shop. I need you to get to him before they do." She has a route planned. She does not wait to see if the party agrees.

**Background** *(DM only)*
Manshoon's kill team, assembled using Skeemo's intelligence files, identified **Ziraj the Hunter** as the Doom Raiders' most dangerous operational asset. Six Splinter agents with specific orders: eliminate him before he can interfere with Kolat Towers. Ziraj killed three of them on the rooftop of a warehouse on Sail Street. He took a hand crossbow bolt through his left shoulder and a second grazing his ribs. He is mobile. He is not fast.

He knows his destinations: **Fala Lefaliir's** herbalist shop at Corellon's Crown in Trollskull Alley is where he keeps a room. He has been moving toward it along the rooftops for thirty minutes. The three remaining agents are between him and the alley — at street level (two) and on a lower rooftop (one). They don't know exactly where he is. They know the general direction.

Ziraj left a trail, intentionally — three small marks scratched into eaves at intervals, a pattern he taught Yagra years ago. It is a halfling-height sign, meant to be invisible at ground level.

**Act 1 — Finding Ziraj**
Rooftop sequence, Trollskull Alley and the adjacent streets east toward the Dock Ward. Yagra climbs ahead without speaking and points where she means without gesturing dramatically.

Two independent paths to Ziraj:
- *Follow the marks:* A **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** check on the eaves at roofline height, or a **DC 12 check** with Yagra's guidance, traces the scratch marks east toward the alley's far end. Three marks lead to the third-floor chimney space of the inn at the east end of Trollskull Alley.
- *Track the agents:* The two street-level agents are visible from the roofline; their search pattern (heading south, then circling) triangulates the general area they think Ziraj is in. Watching them for two minutes narrows the search area enough that a **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check finds him.

Ziraj is between two chimneys on the inn's roof. Two crossbow bolts in him — one through the left shoulder, one across the ribs. He is conscious. He has his oversized bow across his knees and his hand on it. He looks at the party when they arrive. He does not ask how they found him. He says: "Three left." Then nothing else.

The rooftop agent is visible from Ziraj's position — the agent hasn't spotted any of them yet.

**Act 2 — The Three**
The party has a decision before anyone knows they've made it:

*Extract Ziraj first:* Supporting his weight to Fala's shop requires a **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** check from one character or two characters acting in tandem (automatic success with two). Moving a wounded half-orc across rooftops is slow — twelve minutes to reach Fala's. The street-level agents will finish their sweep pattern and find the trail within that window; they converge on Fala's shop door within fifteen minutes of Ziraj's arrival. Combat at street level, outside the shop.

*Ambush the three:* Ziraj is willing. He takes the roof position again — he can hold it for two rounds of shooting before the shoulder goes. The party positions around the street-level agents' approach route. Initiative advantage; the agents walked into terrain they haven't cleared. The rooftop agent requires separate handling (Ziraj can target her from range if the party manages the street threat first).

*Split:* Two characters extract Ziraj while two engage the agents. Higher risk on both fronts, faster on both.

**Fala Lefaliir** is awake when the party arrives — they sleep lightly and recognize Yagra's knock before the door opens. They close the shop without being asked and begin assessing Ziraj's wounds without ceremony. The shop is dim and smells of fresh herbs and dried flowers. Fala has done this before. They do not ask questions until they have the bolts out.

**Act 3 — Ziraj's Gift**
When he can speak clearly — after healing, twenty minutes of quiet, and a mug of something from Fala's back shelf — Ziraj is marginally more talkative than usual. Which is still almost nothing. He reaches into the inside of his coat with his good arm and produces a folded diagram.

"The force field," he says. "Three gaps. They rotate every six hours; each gap is one minute." The diagram shows Yellowspire's exterior from multiple angles, with three positions marked in small careful script. "I watched the amulet deliveries. The north approach, the tower's shadow at noon, the passage behind the miller's." He sets the diagram on the table. "I was going to use it myself. I won't now." A pause. "Use it."

The diagram is usable intelligence for Arc I. Combined with the pass-amulet ledger from Mission 5, the Doom Raiders have provided two independent entry paths through Kolat Towers' outer defenses: the amulets get through the field, and the rooftop diagram shows when the field is briefly penetrable for those who don't have an amulet. Davil, shown the diagram the next morning, understands immediately what it means.

**Renown Opportunities**
- +2: Ziraj is alive and extracted to safety. Davil hears this from Yagra, who was there and doesn't editorialize, and goes quiet before saying "Thank you" with more weight than the words usually carry.
- +1: All three remaining Splinter agents are neutralized. Manshoon's kill team is gone. The Doom Raiders have one fewer liability going into Arc I.
- +1 (supplementary): Ziraj's rooftop diagram is delivered to Davil. He studies it for two minutes, then sets it beside the pass-amulet ledger. "Between these two things," he says, "we can get into Kolat Towers three different ways." He slides both documents into an envelope. "Hold onto them. You're going to need them."

**Aftermath**
Yagra stays at Fala's shop for two days. She does not explain why. When she leaves she says, to no one in particular and to the party specifically: "We take care of our own." She goes back to the Yawning Portal.

Davil delivers a final note. A list of forty-three names, with locations and last known roles: every Manshoon Splinter operative he has identified over three years of watching them. "For Arc I," the note says. "Do what you think is right with what you find there." There is no closing. It is signed with his name.
