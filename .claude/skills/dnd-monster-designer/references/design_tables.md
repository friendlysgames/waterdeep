# Design Tables

## Hit Die by Size

Used in Step 5 (Hit Points). Average HP per die determines a creature's HP from its Hit Dice.

| Size | Hit Die | Avg HP per Die |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Tiny | d4 | 2.5 |
| Small | d6 | 3.5 |
| Medium | d8 | 4.5 |
| Large | d10 | 5.5 |
| Huge | d12 | 6.5 |
| Gargantuan | d20 | 10.5 |

HP formula: *(Avg HP per Die + CON modifier) × number of Hit Dice.*
