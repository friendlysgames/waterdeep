# Force Grey: Character Voices

---

### Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff
*The young Blackstaff: clipped, impatient, competent, and done performing warmth for anyone.*

- **Sound:** A sharp, quick, tired voice that sounds older than she is.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, efficient sentences, orders and conclusions. Her Sendings are exactly 25 words, telegraphic and complete.
- **Word choice:** Command vocabulary: deploy, contain, Force Grey, "my people". She calls Laeral "the Open Lord", precisely, and never mentions Khelben. She calls wizards who doubt her by their surnames.
- **Swearing:** Tired, casual and frequent in private ("I have not slept in two days, and some shit-brained cultist just opened a portal."). Clean in public, barely.
- **Personality in speech:** Burdened determination and defensive pride. Comments about her youth earn a cold stare and a subject change. Her dry humour surfaces unexpectedly, delivered deadpan. Exhaustion shows in shortened sentences, never in complaint.
- **Quirks:** She counts Sending words on her fingers. She answers "Are you alright?" with "Irrelevant."
- **Signature phrases:** "Irrelevant." / "Force Grey will handle it." / "Next."
- **Never:** She never discusses what's in the Blackstaff, never asks for sympathy, and never lets anyone see her doubt herself.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting (Sending):* "Blackstaff here. Rogue construct, Castle Ward, two hours ago. You are closest. Contain it, do not destroy it. Report to Blackstaff Tower by dusk tonight."
  - *Business:* "I need it done quietly and I need it done tonight. Can you do that, or do I send someone who can?"
  - *Under pressure:* "Down! Stay down. I've got it. I said I've got it."
