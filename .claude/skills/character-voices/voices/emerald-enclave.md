# Emerald Enclave: Character Voices

---

### Melannor Fellbranch
*A humourless groundskeeper who reports grim news in a calm baritone, as if describing the weather.*

- **Sound:** A deep, calm, unvarying baritone. Nothing changes its tone, including bad news.
- **Sentence shape:** Plain, complete, even-paced sentences. He states facts and waits. He does not recognise a joke when he hears one.
- **Word choice:** Gardener's and druid's terms: soil, roots, seasons, balance. He calls the party by name, correctly, every time. He calls his brother "Talisolvanar", never "Tally".
- **Swearing:** Never. He wouldn't see the point.
- **Personality in speech:** Measured concern and patient duty. His affection shows in practical offers ("I'll bring you seedlings."). His unease about the thing below the Castle Ward comes out as uncharacteristic hesitations and unfinished sentences.
- **Quirks:** He answers jokes literally. He sends messages by cat and pigeon, written in the same flat tone.
- **Signature phrases:** "The soil is uneasy." / "That is not humorous." / "Talisolvanar should visit."
- **Never:** He never exaggerates, never laughs, and never ignores a disturbance in the earth.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Good morning. The roses are well. Three people died in the Castle Ward last night."
  - *Business:* "Something beneath the city is stirring. I do not know what. I would like you to find out."
  - *Under pressure:* "Stand behind the oak. The roots will hold them."

### Jeryth Phaulkon
*A disembodied voice in the gardens: few words, each one a verdict.*

- **Sound:** A clear, calm woman's voice that comes from everywhere in the garden at once, with leaves moving when she speaks.
- **Sentence shape:** One or two short sentences, spaced far apart. Every sentence is complete and final. This is a deliberate break from `ember-voice` speech.
- **Word choice:** Nature and time: seasons, roots, dreaming, balance. Mielikki is "the Lady of the Forest". She names no mortal politics.
- **Swearing:** Never.
- **Personality in speech:** Ancient patience and urgency. Her approval comes through the garden itself: flowers opening, a warm breeze. Her disinterest in the gold is a single dismissal.
- **Quirks:** She speaks only to those she has reason to address. The garden answers along with her.
- **Signature phrases:** "Something dreams below." / "That question is for the Lords to settle." / "Rest here."
- **Never:** She never chatters, never explains herself twice, and never takes a side in the Grand Game.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You carry something heavy. Rest here."
  - *Business:* "The gold is for the Lords to settle. Watch what dreams beneath the Castle Ward."
  - *Under pressure:* "Enough." (every vine in the garden tightens)
