# City Officials: Character Voices

The people who run, police and quietly finance Waterdeep. Each speaks for an institution, and each has a private voice that slips out from under the official one.

---

### Laeral Silverhand
*The Open Lord: centuries of composure, weary precision, and a warmth she rations.*

- **Sound:** A cool, clear, low elven voice, never hurried. Weariness shows as long pauses, never as a raised tone.
- **Sentence shape:** Measured, complete, formal sentences in public; she weighs each clause. In private, shorter and drier, with the occasional wry aside.
- **Word choice:** Political and exact: "the city", "the Lords", "stability", "the Grand Game". She calls Vajra "the Blackstaff" in public and "Vajra" in private, pointedly. She speaks of Khelben only by name, and rarely.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Never in office. In disguise, out on the town, she enjoys a casual "shit" and a laugh no one would believe.
- **Personality in speech:** Weary resolve and calculation. She answers questions with the question behind them. Her concealed decline shows as deflection whenever magic is discussed directly. Her grief for Khelben comes out as silence.
- **Quirks:** She lets a silence run until the other person fills it. She uses a person's full name when she is displeased. In disguise she overdoes the common accent, badly.
- **Signature phrases:** "The city comes first." / "Tell me what you're not telling me." / "That will be all."
- **Never:** She never admits her power is diminished, never begs, and never discusses the Blackstaff's contents.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "You're the ones who found the Stone. Sit. I have perhaps ten minutes, and I'd like all of them to be useful."
  - *Business:* "Whoever holds that gold holds the city's future. I would prefer it be no one."
  - *Under pressure:* "Everyone behind me. Now. I'll explain later, if there is a later."

### Barnibus Blastwind
*A brilliant, awkward magistrate who can only speak in depositions.*

- **Sound:** A precise, slightly nasal voice, dry as paper, that rises in pitch when he is uncomfortable, which is often.
- **Sentence shape:** Long, formal, footnoted sentences with numbered points and qualifications. This is a deliberate break from `ember-voice` speech. He never contracts, and he answers casual questions as if under oath.
- **Word choice:** Forensic and legal: "the evidence indicates", "residual evocation", "the deceased", "in the first instance". He calls people by full name and title and calls Cromley "Sergeant".
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.* Never. He is appalled by violence, and profanity is its little cousin.
- **Personality in speech:** Methodical satisfaction and social discomfort. He guards his conclusions until he is sure, then delivers them all at once. Reluctant admiration for people who can talk to strangers comes out as awkward thanks.
- **Quirks:** He numbers his points aloud ("Firstly… secondly…"). He clears his throat before speaking to anyone he doesn't know. He turns to Cromley for translation.
- **Signature phrases:** "Firstly." / "The evidence indicates…" / "Sergeant, would you explain to them what I mean?"
- **Never:** He never guesses aloud, never threatens, and never uses his magic to harm anyone.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Barnibus Blastwind, Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors. I have some questions. Firstly, your whereabouts at the time of the detonation."
  - *Business:* "The residual evocation indicates a necklace of fireballs, detonated at close range, in the first instance by accident."
  - *Under pressure:* "I will not fight. I will, however, testify against every one of you. Sergeant?"

### Saeth Cromley
*A retired Watch sergeant with a sharp tongue, a loose collar, and a talent for making people talk.*

- **Sound:** A gravelly, easygoing voice with a laugh in it, a Dock Ward edge he never lost.
- **Sentence shape:** Casual, sardonic, medium sentences, and the "one more thing" question on the way out. He translates Barnibus into plain speech, in a single sentence.
- **Word choice:** Watch slang and street talk: "the boys", "a bad lot", "on the level". He calls Barnibus "the magistrate" with fond exasperation, and everyone else "friend" or "sunshine".
- **Swearing:** *Casual · Pearls · Rant.* Casual and cheerful, old-soldier style ("Well, shit, that's a body."). Toned down near Barnibus, out of fondness. An old sergeant's gems: "That witness couldn't find his own arse with a lantern and a map."
- **Personality in speech:** Wry amusement and patience. He gets witnesses talking by being unimpressive. He misses the Watch, and it comes out as nostalgia disguised as complaint.
- **Quirks:** He scratches his chin before the killer question. He adjusts a badge he isn't technically supposed to wear anymore.
- **Signature phrases:** "One more thing." / "What the magistrate means is…" / "On the level, friend."
- **Never:** He never takes a bribe, never talks down to common folk, and never admits he misses the job.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Saeth Cromley. Retired. Mostly. Don't mind the magistrate, he's friendlier than he sounds."
  - *Business:* "What the magistrate means is, somebody blew up a gnome in your street, and we'd like to know who."
  - *Under pressure:* "Get down, sunshine. That's not a firework."

### Jelenn Urmbrusk
*A proud, brittle noblewoman whose dismissiveness is the last wall around a terrible debt.*

- **Sound:** A clipped, aristocratic Tethyrian voice, cold and quick, that cracks under real pressure.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, dismissive commands and brush-offs in public. Longer and faster, with self-justification, when cornered.
- **Word choice:** Old-money formality: "I think not", "this is most irregular", "my people will see you out". She never names Manshoon, the Zhentarim or the debt.
- **Swearing:** *Stingy · Plain · Clipped.* Never in public. When her fear breaks through in private, a hissed "Damn them. Damn all of them."
- **Personality in speech:** Wounded pride and desperate anxiety. She dismisses help she needs. Suspicion of strangers shows as questions about who sent them. Terror of Manshoon leaks out as refusal to finish certain sentences.
- **Quirks:** She signals her guards with two fingers mid-sentence. She checks who is within earshot before any real answer.
- **Signature phrases:** "I think not." / "Who sent you?" / "My people will see you out."
- **Never:** She never admits the debt, never accepts help on someone else's terms, and never says Manshoon's name.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "I don't know you, and I have sixteen reasons not to care to. Who sent you?"
  - *Business:* "If, hypothetically, a lady owed certain people money, what would you want for making that go away quietly?"
  - *Under pressure:* "You don't understand what they can do. You don't — no. My people will see you out."
