campaign/structure/arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md:15: The gold needed a guardian, and Neverember had one in mind. Aurinax, an adult gold dragon, had lived openly in Waterdeep for decades — a privilege granted by the dragonstaff of Ahghairon, which let him ignore the dragonward that kept every other creature of his kind out of the city. The staff had come to him from Maaril, the last archmage to wield it, who went mad shortly before the Spellplague and fled the city, leaving the staff in Aurinax's care rather than see it fall to someone who might misuse it. Neverember offered a trade: Aurinax would keep the staff, and in exchange he would guard a vault beneath the city until Neverember himself, or someone Neverember named as an authorized vassal, came to remove what was stored there. Aurinax agreed. It seemed, at the time, like very little to ask of a being who measured his patience in centuries.
