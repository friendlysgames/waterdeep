campaign/guides/gm-guide/design-notes-running-the-campaign.md:13: The City of Splendors is, canonically, a place where every power that matters watches every other power, waiting for an opening. When you pick a season villain, you convert that political texture into a genre piece: a crime drama where the Dragon Cult is running things this year. The other three factions become background noise. A campaign where Jarlaxle is the season villain isn't a Dragon Heist campaign; it's a Jarlaxle story. The city feels smaller than it should.
