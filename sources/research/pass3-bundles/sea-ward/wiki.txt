The '''Sea Ward of Waterdeep''' was the wealthiest ward in [[Waterdeep]] and located in the northwest of the city.<ref name="VGtW-p51" /><ref name="CoS.W-p91" />

==Geography==
The ward was delimited by the [[Trollwall]] on the north and west, by [[Julthoon Street]] to the south, and by [[Shield Street]] to the east, extending to the [[High Road (Waterdeep)|High Road]] north of [[Vondil Street]].<ref name="CS-AG2e-p15">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Adventurer's Guide to the City|52}}</ref>

The ward contained the [[Field of Triumph]], [[Heroes' Garden]] and the [[Sea's Edge Beach]].<ref name="CoS.W-p91" /> It also contained some of the city's largest temples.<ref name="CS-CG2e-p9">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|9}}</ref>

==History==
The Sea Ward was officially included as one of the official wards of Waterdeep in the {{YearlinkName|1276}}, together with the [[Waterdeep/North Ward|North Ward]].<ref name="GHR3e-p128" /><ref name="CS-CG2e-p31" />

The '''Trollwall''' was a defensive barrier that encircled nearly the entirety of the city of [[Waterdeep]].<ref name="CoS.CG-p8" /><ref name="CoS.W-p18-19" /><ref name="WatN-p11">{{Cite book/Waterdeep and the North|11}}</ref>

==Location==
The Trollwall fully surrounded Waterdeep's eastern perimeter, from its southern tip at [[Deepwater Harbor]] to the northern end of the [[City of the Dead]],<ref name="CoS.CG-p8" /><ref name="WatN-p11" /> along the way acting as the eastern boundary of the [[Waterdeep/Southern Ward|Southern Ward]].<ref>{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Waterdeep|135}}</ref> It stopped at the northeastern corner of the City of the Dead, at a small stretch of land known as the [[Cliffwatch]],<ref name="CoS.CG-p8" /><ref name="CoS.W-p18-19" /> whose steep {{SI|100|ft}} cliff of the [[Cliffwatch (cliff)|same name]] acted as a natural barrier.<ref name="CoS.CG-p8" /><ref name="WatN-p11" /><ref name="CoS.CG-p43" /><ref name="CoS.W-p94">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|94}}</ref> The Trollwall then continued around Waterdeep's northern perimeter,<ref name="CoS.CG-p8" /><ref name="WatN-p11" /> beginning at the point in which the Cliffwatch's height was negligible enough for attackers to scale it,<ref name="WatN-p11" /> and formed the southern boundary of the [[Waterdeep/Field Ward|Field Ward]]<ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Player's Guide|128}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide|196}}</ref> before doubling back south to surrounded the western seacoast.<ref name="CoS.CG-p8" /><ref name="WatN-p11" />

==Structure==
In the [[Present Age]], the Trollwall was made of stone and measured {{SI|40|ft|long=yes}} tall.<ref name="CoS.W-p18-19">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|18-19}}</ref> The thickness of the barrier varied from {{SIrange|10|20|ft|long=yes}} in places,<ref name="CoS.CG-p8">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|8}}</ref> but was most commonly {{SI|15|ft|long=yes}} thick. The wall had crenellations on both of its sides.<ref name="CoS.W-p18-19" />

Throughout its length the Trollwall was studded with watch towers.<ref name="CS-p5">{{Cite book/City System|5}}</ref><ref name="FRCS2e-p105">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 2nd edition (revised)/A Grand Tour of the Realms|105}}</ref> These were known as Trollwall Towers<ref>{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Waterdeep|167}}</ref> or torchtowers,<ref name="edverse">{{Cite web/theedverse/2021-10-17 - Waterdeep's Torchtowers}}</ref> with those bearing names being considered "major towers".<ref name="CS-p6">{{Cite book/City System|6}}</ref>

'''Julthoon Street''' was a major east–west thoroughfare in [[Waterdeep]]. It was the dividing line between the [[Sea Ward of Waterdeep|Sea Ward]] to the north and the [[Castle Ward of Waterdeep|Castle Ward]] to the south as of the [[1276 DR|Year of the Crumbling Keep, 1276 DR]].<ref name="CoS.CG-31">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|31}}</ref><ref name="CoS.M">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Map}}</ref><ref name="VGtW-11">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Waterdeep|11}}</ref><ref name="WatN-cover">{{Cite book/Waterdeep and the North/Inside cover}}</ref>

==Geography==
Julthoon stretched from the cliffs of [[Gull Leap]] on the coast of the [[Sea of Swords]] to the [[High Road (Waterdeep)|High Road]] in the [[North Ward of Waterdeep|North Ward]]. At Gull Leap, Julthoon connected with [[Westwall Street]] and the [[Cliffride]].<ref name="VGtW-map">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Waterdeep/Map}}</ref> Along the way to North Ward it touched or connected with many other streets and alleys including [[Jelzar's Stride]], [[Seaeye's March]], [[Toalar's Lane]], [[Darselune Street]], [[Gothal Street]], [[Melshar's Street]], [[Haltovar Street]],<ref name="VGtW-map" /><ref name="CoSW-99">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|99}}</ref><ref name="WDH-map">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist/Map}}</ref> [[Tchozal's Race]],<ref name="WDH-map" /> the [[Sutherlane]],<ref name="CoSW-99" /><ref name="WDH-map" /> [[Mendever Street]], [[Sul Street]], [[Shield Street]],<ref name="VGtW-map" /><ref name="WDH-map" /><ref name="CoSW-95">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|95}}</ref> and [[Copper Street]].<ref name="VGtW-map" /><ref name="WDH-map" /><ref name="CoSW-97">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|97}}</ref>

==History==
In the [[1255 DR|Year of the Raging Flame, 1255 DR]], the walls protecting Waterdeep were expanded to keep up with the growth of the city. A portion of the new wall was built along the path of what would become Julthoon street when the walls were moved again in 1276 DR.<ref name="CoS.CG-p30,31">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|30, 31}}</ref> At the western end of the street, a 90 ft (27 m) [[Sahuagin Humbled|Walking Statue]] stood on Gull Leap<ref name="WatN-23">{{Cite book/Waterdeep and the North|23}}</ref><ref name="CoS.CG-p47">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|47}}</ref> until around the [[1370 DR|Year of the Tankard, 1370 DR]].<ref name="WDH-183">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|183}}</ref>

'''Shield Street''' was a road that was located in the city of [[Waterdeep]].<ref name="CS.M05">{{Cite book/City System/Map05}}</ref><ref name="WDH.M">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist/Map}}</ref><ref name="VGtW.M">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Waterdeep/Map}}</ref><ref name="IA.W">{{Cite interactive atlas|Waterdeep}}</ref>

==Location==
Shield Street was located in the northern section of Waterdeep's [[Waterdeep/Sea Ward|Sea Ward]] and formed part of the western border of the [[Waterdeep/North Ward|North Ward]]. The road branched south from [[Skulls Street]] and continued due south to eventually end on [[Trader's Way (Waterdeep)|Trader's Way]] at the border with the [[Waterdeep/Castle Ward|Castle Ward]]. Other streets intersecting Shield Street included [[Immar Street]], [[Chasso's Trot]], [[Vondil Street]], [[Delzorin Street]], [[Rough Road]], [[Sulmor Street]], [[Hassantyr's Street]], [[Wrightstone Street]], [[Julthoon Street]], and [[Manycats Alley]].<ref name="CS.M05"/><ref name="WDH.M"/><ref name="VGtW.M"/><ref name="IA.W"/>

The '''High Road''', largely known to citizens as just '''the High''',<ref name="drag410-p29">{{Cite dragon/410/Eye on the Realms: The Talking Door|29}}</ref> was one of the main streets of [[Waterdeep]], connecting its southern and northern regions. Together with the other boulevards that ran through the city in the north-south direction, it was heavily trafficked, and one of the main routes of the city's [[dray]]s and [[hire-coach]]es.<ref name="WDH5e-p170">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|170}}</ref>

==Geography==
The High Road started at the [[South Gate (Waterdeep)|South Gate]] of Waterdeep, where it continued south as the [[Trade Way]]. From there it led northward, crossing the city and exiting it from the [[North Gate (Waterdeep)|North Gate]], from where it continued north along the [[Sword Coast North]] as the [[High Road|road of the same name]].<ref name="CSW3e-p15" />

==Notable Locations==
In southern Waterdeep, the intersection between the High Road and the [[Way of the Dragon (Waterdeep)|Way of the Dragon]] was known as the [[Waymoot (Waterdeep)|Waymoot]]. A [[magic]] signpost at the center of the intersection indicated the direction and distance to locations outside the city.<ref name="WDH5e-p180">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|180}}</ref> [[Berendarr's World of Words]], a [[broadsheet]] store, was located here.<ref name="BoW">{{Cite web/Realmslore/Broadcryers of Waterdeep I: News for Sale}}</ref>

The '''Field of Triumph''' was a large outdoor stadium located in the [[Waterdeep/Sea Ward|Sea Ward]] of [[Waterdeep]].<ref name="CoS.CG-41">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|41}}</ref> 

==Location==
[[File:Field of Triumph's Gate.png||thumb|275px|''Crowds of people flock down Gulzindar Street towards the Lion Gate of the Fields of Triumph.'']]
The Field of Triumph was located at the southern end of the [[Street of the Singing Dolphin]], across from the [[House of Heroes]]. It was bordered by [[Julthoon Street]], [[Jelzar's Stride]], and [[Seaeye's March]].<ref name="CoS.map">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Map}}</ref> And up until the mid-14{{th}} century [[DR]], it was also adjacent to [[the Lions]] road.<ref name="CS">{{Cite book/City System/Map04}}</ref> The main entrance to the stadium was faced towards the end of [[Gulzindar Street]].<ref name="CoS.map" /><ref name="CoS.AGthC-16" /><ref name="VGtW-p51" />

==Structure==
This stadium stood over five stories tall.<ref name="CoS.W-p92">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|92}}</ref> Its main entrance was known as the Lion Gate, due to it being carved to give visitors the impression that they were walking into the maw of a gigantic [[lion]].<ref name="CoS.AGthC-16">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Adventurer's Guide to the City|16}}</ref><ref name="VGtW-p51">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Waterdeep|51}}</ref><ref name="WDH-p174">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|174}}</ref>

==Activities==
The Field of Triumph was the venue for many public spectacles from late spring until late autumn, weather permitting. The [[Open Lord]] of Waterdeep used the Field as a public forum to give proclamations to the city, but most of the time it was host to exciting events such as [[horse]] races, shows of [[magic]] and [[illusion]], martial displays of formation and skill, gladiatorial combats, and exhibitions of strange and wondrous creatures and monsters from all across Faerûn and beyond. Attendance often numbered in the thousands.<ref name="CoS.CG-41" /><ref name="CoS.AGthC-17">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Adventurer's Guide to the City|17}}</ref>
===Holidays===
During [[Waukeentide]], this coliseum was one of the buildings that the [[Guilds of Waterdeep]] traditionally held the gala festival and dance of [[Guildsmeet]] in.<ref name=CoS.AGttC-p29">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Adventurer's Guide to the City|29}}</ref><ref name="WDH-p185">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|185}}</ref> On [[Mirtul 9]], the final event of the holiday [[Plowing and Running]] would be held here.<ref name="WDH-p185" /> And on [[Founders' Day]], the stadium would be host to illusory shows of Waterdeep's history and martial exhibitions by the [[City Guard (Waterdeep)|City Guard]] and other warriors of note.<ref name=CoS.AGttC-p28">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Adventurer's Guide to the City|28}}</ref>

Once every four years, during [[Shieldmeet]], the [[Lords of Waterdeep]] would gather on the Field of Triumph to be reaffirmed as leaders of the city. They appeared in person, seated on sixteen thrones, wearing covered helms and [[robe]]s that disguised their identities.<ref>{{Cite book/Elfsong|207,297}}</ref>

After the Field of Triumph was constructed, its architectural designs were kept within the [[Map House (Waterdeep)|Map House]].<ref name="WDH-p174" />

In the {{YearlinkName|1360}}, the director of the Field of Triumph was the head of House [[Phylund]], Lord [[Urtos Phylund I]].<ref name="Du128-8" />

'''Heroes' Garden''' was a public garden in the far north of the [[Waterdeep/Sea Ward|Sea Ward]] of [[Waterdeep]],<ref name="WDH-p175">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|175}}</ref> one of the only public gardens in the city besides the [[Waterdeep/City of the Dead|City of the Dead]].<ref name="CoS.CG-p41">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|41}}</ref><ref name="CoSW-p92">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|92}}</ref>

==Description==
This was a large, lush park full of [[grass]] and trees, as well a number of ponds. Fine statuary was dotted around the park,<ref name="CoS.CG-p41" /><ref name="CoSW-p92" /> portraying many important figures in Waterdeep's history.<ref name="WDH-p175" /> Two of these were large, [[marble]] figures of the [[Open Lord]]s [[Baeron]] and [[Lhestyn]] (in her Masked Lady guise).<ref name="CoS.CG-p41" /><ref name="CoSW-p92" />

==Location==
This garden was bounded by [[Heroes' Walk]] and [[Trollkill Street]].<ref name="CoS.M">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Map}}</ref><ref name="CS-map05">{{Cite book/City System/Map05}}</ref><ref name="CoSW-95">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|95}}</ref><ref name="WDH5e-map">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist/Map}}</ref>

==Activities==
Heroes' Garden didn't receive a great deal of visitors due to its location in the Sea Ward's far north,<ref name="WDH-p175" /> but it was still actively tended to by local citizenry and members of the [[City Watch (Waterdeep)|Watch]], which included keeping the park free of debris.<ref name="CoS.CG-p41" /><ref name="CoSW-p92" />

[[Adventurer]]s often came to the park and regaled locals with tales of their exploits.<ref name="CoS.CG-p41" /><ref name="CoSW-p92" /> And every year during the summer, Heroes' Garden and Heroes' Walk would both be overtaken by the nobles of the [[Church of Siamorphe]] on their holy day, the [[Divine Pageantry]].<ref name="F&P-p107">{{Cite book/Faiths and Pantheons|107}}</ref>

==History==
In the wake of the [[Tower of Yintros]]'s destruction, the [[Lords of Waterdeep]] purchased the land on which it stood. They went on to clear the tower's debris and have its plot of land transformed into what would become known as Heroes' Garden.<ref name="CoSW-115">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|115}}</ref>

The '''North Ward''' was the northeastern sector of [[Waterdeep]].<ref name="WatN-p24" /><ref name="CoS-p92" /> It was famous for being the quietest ward of the city, consisting mostly of residential areas.<ref name="VGtW-p81" />

==Geography==
The North Ward was delimited by the walls of the [[Waterdeep/City of the Dead|City of the Dead]] to the southeast and the [[Trollwall]] to the east and north. Its southern border was at [[Andamaar's Street]] and [[Trader's Way]] up to [[Shield Street]], which marked the western border until [[Vondil Street]]. North of that, the western border was defined by the [[High Road]] the rest of the way to the Trollwall.<ref name="CoS.AG-12">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Adventurer's Guide to the City|12}}</ref>

The architectural style of the North Ward was dominated by rowhouses, which filled the gaps between the villas of lesser [[Nobility of Waterdeep|noble houses]]. The ward also counted with a variety of taverns and shops.<ref name="VGtW-p81" /><ref name="WDH5e-p176">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|176}}</ref>

===Geographical Features===
The most prominent feature of the North Ward was the [[Cliffwatch]], a section of the Waterdeep plateau that was so steep and high that it provided a natural defense, so the area was marked by an interruption of the Trollwall between the [[Endcliff Tower]] and the [[Watchway Tower]] in the City of the Dead. A walkway traversed the entire Cliffwatch, along which some of the best inns and taverns of Waterdeep were located, taking advantage of the beautiful landscape to the east.<ref name="WDH5e-p176" /><ref name="CS-CG2e-p43">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|43}}</ref>

==History==
The North Ward was officially included as one of the official wards of Waterdeep in the {{YearlinkName|1276}}, together with the [[Waterdeep/Sea Ward|Sea Ward]].<ref name="GHR3e-p128" /><ref name="CS-CG2e-p31" />

In [[1368 DR]], the noble [[Cassalanter]] family loaned two ten-million [[Golden lion (coin)|golden lions]] with a huge interest rate. The first loan was to [[Widow Silvermane]] who ran a [[lottery]] in the North Ward, and the second one went to the [[Field of Triumph]] Race Track in the [[Waterdeep/Sea Ward|Sea Ward]]. '''Dacer's Inn''' was a fine, old, and quiet luxury inn in the city of [[Waterdeep]] in the mid-to-late 14{{th}} century [[DR]]. It catered to the likes of rich sailors, who staggered from the [[Fiery Flagon]] up-street, and the constant traffic of [[pilgrim]]s and visitors to the neighboring [[House of Inspired Hands]].<ref name="VGtW-p61">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Waterdeep|61}}</ref>

==Location==
Dacer's was located along [[Seawatch Street]], just down from the House of Inspired Hands,<ref name="VGtW-p61" /> in the [[Waterdeep/Sea Ward|Sea Ward]] district of Waterdeep.<ref name="CoS-p42" /><ref name="VGtW-p61" /><ref>{{Cite book/Waterdeep and the North|23}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|92}}</ref>

==Structure==
Dacer's Inn was well-built<ref name="VGtW-p61" /> and stood three stories tall.<ref name="CoS-p42">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|42}}</ref>

==Interior==
Dacer's Inn incorporated all of the innovations and improvements that the local [[Gondar]] suggested, but abstained from most of "ostentatious frills" that some establishments north of [[Mount Waterdeep]] indulged in. Some of the accoutrements of the inn included recessed sliding bolts on bedroom doors, pumped water on tap in each room, [[dumbwaiter]]s that brought food up to the bedrooms, and alarm [[gong]]s on each floor.<ref name="VGtW-p61" />

==Services==
A stay at this establishment cost 8 [[gold piece]]s per head, per night, a fee that included the cost of stabling and simple meals. Beyond water, guests could pay for a [[bottle]] of [[zzar]] at a cost of 12 gp. And if guests desired food, Dacer's Inn would send runners out to order from cooks or street vendors<ref name="VGtW-p61" /> and have it delivered to the guest's room.<ref name="VGtW-p62" />

==Inhabitants==
The proprietress of the establishment in the 1360s century [[DR]] was one [[Amasanna Vumendir]].<ref name="VGtW-p62">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Waterdeep|62}}</ref>

The '''Fiery Flagon''' was a stately old tavern in [[Waterdeep]] in the mid-to-late 14{{th}} century [[DR]] that served excellent food.<ref name="CoS.CG-p42" /><ref name="VGtW-p56-57" /><ref name="CoS.AGttC-p17">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Adventurer's Guide to the City|17}}</ref>

==Location==
The tavern was located in the city's [[Sea Ward]],<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" /><ref name="WatN-p23" /><ref name="CoS.CG-p42" /><ref name="CoS.AGttC-p17" /> on the west side of [[Seawatch Street]], across from the [[House of Inspired Hands]] at the intersection with [[Shark Street]].<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" />

==Structure==
This building stood only a single story tall.<ref name="CoS.CG-p42" /><ref>{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|92}}</ref> Its entryway was a wide, [[iron]]-barred door.<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" />

==Interior==
As soon as one entered the Fiery Flagon, they were confronted with a descending staircase that lead down into the tavern proper. The cellar level larger was larger than the space taken up by the building above-ground, though it was still somewhat small and cramped,<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" /> its space being taken up by a large amount of seafaring decor and flotsam.<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" /><ref name="CoS.AGttC-p17"/> This included such things as [[anchor]]s, [[glassblowing|blown-glass]] floats, [[figurehead]]s worn by time out at sea, and a skeleton of an [[eye of the deep]] that had [[lamp]]s set in its eyesockets, projecting an eerie and flickering glow over the room. The floor was also taken up by numerous tanks of fish, that contributed to a damp feeling. The staff would extract the seafood to be served from these tanks.<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" />

Notably, hidden within the confines of the building was a secret access tunnel, that winded downward in long staircase, until eventually terminating at a [[ladder]] shaft down into caverns near the lawless city of [[Skullport]]. The entrance was guarded by a heavily barred door, warded to reflect back [[spell]]s like a ''[[ring of spell turning]]''. It also produced the sound of a [[gong]] whenever this defensive effect was triggered.<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" />

==Atmosphere==
This tavern was popular amongst sailors from across the [[Sword Coast]] and wider corners of the Realms,<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" /><ref name="CoS.AGttC-p17"/> often eager to swap tales,<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" /> due in part to its choice in decor.<ref name="CoS.AGttC-p17"/> Whilst it was not as refined an establishment as some taverns, like [[The Ship's Wheel]], it made up for it being a far safer dive than sailor frequented bars over in the [[Waterdeep/Dock Ward|Dock Ward]].<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" />

==Services==
The menu of the Fiery Flagon was primarily seafood, which the cooks would prepare and cook in front of the patrons. Additionally, they welcomed preparing any seafood that a patron brought in. Beyond seafood, the provender included [[bread]], [[cheese]], and [[grape]]s.<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" />

Despite its somewhat ramshackle appearance and having no drinking vessels fancier than [[tankard]]s, the Fiery Flagon charged just enough for its provender that most scabs and scoundrels stayed away. A [[plate]] of a simple meal cost 7 [[silver piece]]s. In terms of beverages, they charged 3 [[copper piece]]s for a rough [[ale]] and 5 for a good ale, 1 sp for a tankard of [[zzar]], and charged [[gold piece]]s for [[wine]]s and other spirits. One of their most expensive offerings was [[firebelly whiskey]], which cost 14 gp.<ref name="VGtW-p56-57" />

According to those in the know, the proprietor would allow access to the secret tunnel to Skullport in return for 5 silver pieces. Whenever this transaction was made, he would have a half-dozen armed men watch over the door as he used some manner of [[wand]] upon it.<ref name

''When the sale of dream spheres threatens the life of his newfound half sister, [[Danilo Thann]] joins forces with [[Arilyn Moonblade]] to uncover the source of this deadly trade.  Their search leads them into the dark heart of Waterdeep, and to personal secrets that could destroy them both.''

==Summary==
Lilly Thann, maid at the [[Pickled Fisherman]] and a thief, uses a magic item called a Dream Sphere to dream of wealth and importance, and forges a partnership with [[Isabeau Thione]]. 

[[Danilo Thann]] greets [[Arilyn Moonblade]], who has just returned to [[Waterdeep]] after being called by her [[Moonblade]] to aid forest elves. The increasingly frequent calls from the moonblade have begun to drive a wedge between the two, but Arilyn attends the Gemstone Ball with Danilo at Thann villa, where a miscast spell summons a monstrous rosebush. The bush fades, but Arilyn’s outfit is torn and she leaves Danilo to change. On her way back, she gets lost and overhears [[Oth Eltorchul]] talking to a group of powerful figures, including [[Khelben Arunsun]], [[Maskar Wands]], and [[Cassandra Thann]]. Oth attempts to convince them of the value of the Dream Spheres, but they decline to help him find ways to sell them. Arilyn leaves and descends a staircase, eventually encountering tracks of [[tren]], a race of lizardlike assassins.
Meanwhile, [[Elaith Craulnober]], also at the party, is accosted by Mizzen Doar, a gem merchant who drunkenly boasts of an “elf gem.” Elaith receives a map from a servant which leads him to Arilyn, who has discovered a tren ambush. Together, they slay all but one tren. Isabeau Thione, having left the Ball with Oth Eltorchul to spend the night at the Silken Sylph, is given a Dream Sphere and experiences a vision of herself in [[Zaranda Star]]’s courtroom. The vision convinces her that she deserves power, and even to be queen of Tethyr. She robs Oth. 

Danilo points out to Arilyn that the moonblade has not been reliable since entering the city, and thinking that he is the cause, attempts to break up with her. Danilo learns from Khelben Arunsun that the Thanns have elvish blood. 
When Oth berates Elaith for being robbed, Elaith learns of the Dream Spheres and surmises that they are being created by an elven artifact. He plans to take this artifact for himself.

'''Aurora's Realms Shop, Singing Dolphin Catalogue Counter''' was an outlet of the [[Aurora's Emporium]] chain in the [[Waterdeep/Sea Ward|Sea Ward]] of [[Waterdeep]] in the mid-to-late 14{{th}} century [[DR]]. It was one of six such outlets in the city.<ref name="VGtW-p58">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Waterdeep|58-59}}</ref><ref name="CoS-CG-p41">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|41}}</ref><ref name="CoS-AGttC-p17">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Adventurer's Guide to the City|17}}</ref><ref name="CoS-W-p92">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|92,95}}</ref>

==Location==
It lay on the [[Street of the Singing Dolphin]], on the western side and three shops north of the corner with [[Grimwald's Way]].<ref name="VGtW-p58"/><ref name="CoS-W-p92" />

==Description==
It was quite opulent and displayed all the finest and priciest items in the catalogue.<ref name="CoS-AGttC-p17" />

==Services==
This shop sold the various goods found in ''[[Aurora's Whole Realms Catalogue (in-universe)|Aurora's Whole Realms Catalogue]]''.<ref name="VGtW-p58"/><ref name="CoS-AGttC-p17" />

==Staff==
In the mid-1360s [[DR]], the handsome [[Orloth Theldarin]] worked here as a counter clerk with a reputation for taste and tact alike, and [[Saerghon Alir|Saerghon "the Magnificent" Alir]] was the service-mage, and quite arrogant for his mundane trade.<ref name="VGtW-p58" /><ref name="CoS-AGttC-p17" /> There were also six guards who worked shifts of three on and three off and were noted for their shiny weapons and fine uniforms.<ref name="VGtW-p58" />
