campaign/quests/act-i/finding-floon/design-notes.md:5: ***Investigation Restructuring.*** The source Chapter 1 presents the Dock Ward investigation as a sequential chain: Skewered Dragon → warehouse name → Renaer → sewer. A single failed Charisma check at the Skewered Dragon can stall the party indefinitely, because the adventure makes that tavern the sole source for the warehouse's location. The Three Clue Rule treatment distributes the warehouse address across four independent paths for Revelation #2: the Skewered Dragon itself (simultaneously serving Revelation #1 and #2), neighborhood canvass, prisoner questioning, and the dropped bead trail. No single failure blocks progress. Revelation #1 has three independent paths; Revelation #3 has three. The four-path Revelation #2 reflects the warehouse address being the bottleneck in the source, placing extra paths where the adventure was most fragile. Players who follow all paths simultaneously discover more about the world; players who follow one path successfully still reach the warehouse.
