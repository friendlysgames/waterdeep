campaign/guides/gm-guide/about-this-campaign.md:5: *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist Remix* rebuilds the original adventure as a genuine heist campaign for 2024 D&D 5th Edition, running from 1st through 7th or 8th level before handing off into *Dungeon of the Mad Mage*. Four villain factions compete for the same hidden fortune, all active from the first session, none of them waiting for your players to notice them. Over 29 to 51 sessions (roughly seven to thirteen months of weekly play), the party builds a home in Waterdeep's North Ward, investigates a murder, plans and executes four heist operations, faces a dragon in a vault, and decides what to do with 500,000 gold pieces. None of those decisions are scripted in advance.

The act has no single villain and no grand stakes, by design. Waterdeep opens district by district as the players earn their place in it: the guilds, the Watch, the neighbors, and the city's wider machinery. Act I works because the city feels like somewhere worth protecting before anything threatens it.
