'''Demogorgon''' ({{Pronounce|DEE|mo|gor|gun|refs=<ref name="Dr93-Apg-p26">{{Cite dragon/93/Ay pronunseeAYshun gyd|26}}</ref>|audio=Demogorgon.ogg}}) referred to as the '''Imprisoned One''' by some followers of [[Helm]],<ref name="BGII.ToB" /> was the [[demon lord]] and [[lesser deity]] of domination and the draining of energy,<ref name="MM-p88">{{Cite book/Monster Mythology|88}}</ref> an embodiment of madness and destruction that sought to drag all down into the infinite depths of the [[Abyss]].<ref name="MToF-pp144-145" /> He was known by many names, called '''Siosivash''' by the [[yuan-ti]],<ref name="FCI-p63" /> '''Leemooggoogoon the Deep Father''' by the [[kuo-toa]],<ref name="OotA5e-pp45,47-48" /> and '''Ahmon-Ibor the Sibilant Beast''' by the [[troglodyte]]s.<ref name="FCI-p63" /> The first [[tanar'ri]], formed from mortals' ancient fears,<ref name="Dr357-PoD-p21">{{Cite dragon/357/Demonomicon of Iggwilv: Demogorgon: Prince of Demons|21}}</ref> even his chosen race, the [[ixitxachitl]], dared not speak his name aloud;<ref name="FCI-p63"/> it alone could inspire a primal terror.<ref name="FCI-p62">{{Cite book/Fiendish Codex I: Hordes of the Abyss|62}}</ref> Inspiring fear in even other demon lords, his self-proclaimed title of '''Prince of Demons''' was won by virtue of power and influence; many demons challenged it, but none could defeat Demogorgon and claim it.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" />

==Description==
Demogorgon towered a full {{SI|18|ft|long=yes}} in height,<ref name="MM1e-pp16-17" /> his body at once sinuous like that of a snake and powerful like that of a great [[ape]]. Two baleful [[baboon]] heads, with blue and red faces similar to those of [[mandrill]]s,<ref name="MM1e-pp16-17" /><ref name="Archmage-pp350-351">{{Cite book/Archmage/Hardcover (2015)|350-351}}</ref> leered from atop his lumbering shoulders, from which two long tentacles writhed.  His lower torso was saurian, like a great reptile with blue-green, scaly skin. He had an immense forked tail.<ref name="MM1e-pp16-17" />

==Personality==
{{Fq

{{quote

Perhaps the only thing preventing Demogorgon from taking over the rest of the Abyss was his own dual nature. Each of Demogorgon's heads had its own name and personality, a duality that created an enduring internal conflict of his personality, while shaping all his actions and even his realm.<ref name="BoVD-pp125-127">{{Cite book/Book of Vile Darkness|125-127}}</ref><ref name="FCI-pp139-142">{{Cite book/Fiendish Codex I: Hordes of the Abyss|139-142}}</ref> His left head was named '''Aameul''' while the right was named '''Hethradiah''' (sometimes referred to as Hathradiah).<ref name="MToF-pp144-145" /><ref name="OotA-pp236-237" /> The two heads warred with each other, constantly seeking to obtain the upper hand on each other. Aameul, the more charismatic and calculating, relished deception and wished to break free from the other half. The more impulsive and feral Hethradiah relished destruction and did not wish to be separated.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" /><ref name="BoVD-pp125-127" /><ref name="Dr357-pp20-32">{{Cite dragon/357/Demonomicon of Iggwilv: Demogorgon: Prince of Demons|20-32}}</ref>

Despite this internal madness, Demogorgon was extremely creative and relished experimentation and innovation.<ref name="BoVD-pp125-127" />

==Abilities==
{{fq
| float  = 
| width  = 
| source = [[Mordenkainen]]<ref name="MToF-p27">{{Cite book/Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes|27}}</ref>
| text   = I studied Demogorgon's symbol. I thought that, by looking at it through a mirror, I might avoid its effects. If anything, viewing its reflection made its effect more potent and more subtle. It was [[Rary]] who saved me, and I thought he had been spared the symbol's effects. I don't have many regrets, but underestimating that symbol is one of my greatest.

Demogorgon preferred to avoid direct conflict, instead using magic to help his servants.<ref name="BoVD-pp125-127" /> Demogorgon could hypnotize with a gaze<ref name="MM1e-pp16-17" /> or drive creatures around him to terror and insanity just at the sight of him.<ref name="Archmage-pp350,357">{{Cite book/Archmage/Hardcover (2015)|350, 357}}</ref> His whip-like tail had the ability to drain the life energy right out of a living foe. His tentacles inflicted a withering disease that caused living creatures to rot away in an effect similar to that of ''[[cause disease]]''. Rotted limbs and bodies could only be cured if spells such as ''[[remove disease]]'' were cast very quickly.<ref name="MM1e-pp16-17" /><ref name="MM-p88" /> He was extremely resistant to magic.<ref name="EW1e-p37">{{Cite book/Eldritch Wizardry|37}}</ref>

An accomplished spellcaster, Demogorgon was capable of innately casting a number of spells, including, but not restricted to, ''[[detect magic]]'', ''[[dispel magic]]'', ''[[fear]]'', ''[[feeblemind]]'', ''[[major image]]'', ''[[project image]]'', and ''[[telekinesis]]''.<ref name="MToF-pp144-145" /> Unlike other demons, Demogorgon was capable of ''[[plane shift]]ing''.<ref name="MM-p88" />

Demogorgon was comfortable underwater.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" />

==Divine Realm==
Demogorgon lived on the [[Layers of the Abyss#Layer 88: Gaping Maw|88{{th}} layer]] of the [[Abyss]], known as the [[Gaping Maw]]. This was a layer consisting of a great sea of briny water broken by tall, sharp, ugly, rocky promontories. Demogorgon's palace, called [[Abysm]], consisted of two twin towers roughly shaped like tightly coiled serpents, crowned at the top with skull-shaped minarets. The two towers were linked by a bridge near the top. Most of the fortress lay underwater beneath the towers.<ref name="BoVD-pp125-127" /> The reefs and caverns near the fortress were home to [[aboleth]], [[kraken]], and [[ixitxachitl]]s, which constantly warred with each other and worshiped Demogorgon in his palace above.<ref name="Stormwrack-p9">{{Cite book/Stormwrack|9}}</ref>

Demogorgon also kept a fortress in the bog of the Gaping Maw called [[Ungorth Reddik]]. This was where he housed his armies of [[hezrou]]s, aboleths, [[scrag]]s, [[skum]], and [[ichythoid]] creatures. The fortress was guarded by unusual [[retriever]]s, [[golem]]s, and other evil [[construct]]s.<ref name="BoVD-pp125-127" /> Demogorgon was also served by [[abyssal marauder]]s.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" />

The only significant landmass of the layer was a vast continent covered in tropical jungles. Here, Demogorgon's capital city of [[Lemoriax]] was located.<ref name="FCI-pp139-142" />

==Activities==
Demogorgon conducted several experiments in his realm. He was credited with the creation of [[chimera]]s,<ref name="MM5e-p39">{{Cite book/Monster Manual 5th edition|39}}</ref> [[ettin]]s,<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50"/> [[khaasta]]s,<ref name="SK-p38">{{Cite book/Serpent Kingdoms|38}}</ref> [[merrow]]s,<ref name="MM5e-p219">{{Cite book/Monster Manual 5th edition|219}}</ref> [[retriever]]s,<ref name="FCI-p63" /> and with the turning of the first ever [[death knight]],<ref name="FCI-p141">{{Cite book/Fiendish Codex I: Hordes of the Abyss|141}}</ref> [[Kargoth|St. Kargoth]].<ref name="Dr79-SSS-p30">{{Cite dragon/79/Setting saintly standards}}</ref> He sometimes also blesses some ettins who devote to him by transforming them into [[Echo of Demogorgon|echos of Demogorgon]].<ref name="BPGotG-p132">{{Cite book/Bigby Presents: Glory of the Giants|132}}</ref> He also invented a plague known as [[scalepox cloud]], a series of clouds of mist that roamed both Gaping Maw and the [[Layers of the Abyss#Layer 90: The Guttering Grove|guttering Grove]].<ref name="DN4e-p69">{{Cite book/Demonomicon|69}}</ref>

==Worshipers==
[[File:Demogorgonwarlock.jpg|thumb|250px|''A warlock of Demogorgon.'']]
{{quote
|The dual-lived, dual-willed prince of the Abyss, master of the mysterious and king of the deviants, Demogorgon, you are my lord!
|[[Qill]], [[half-elf]] cultist of Demogorgon.<ref name="BoVD-p67"/>
}}
Demogorgon's cult was relatively small compared to "true" deities, but much larger than those of most fiends. He was worshiped by the intelligent manta ray race known as [[ixitxachitl]]s, who drew power from the Abyss itself in order to become more powerful spellcasters. In turn, vital energy drained by [[vampiric ixitxachitl]]s was transferred directly to Demogorgon via an unknown Abyssal mechanism.<ref name="MM-p88" />

Demogorgon's other worshipers included [[troglodyte]]s, [[kuo-toa]], and other [[humanoid]]s, particularly evil [[human]]s. His cult prospered in times of chaos and caused great destruction wherever it was found. Temples to Demogorgon were split in half, with one side representing Aameul and the other representing Hethradiah.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" /> Demogorgon's domains were [[Chaos domain|Chaos]], [[Corruption domain|Corruption]], [[Demonic domain|Demonic]], and [[Evil domain|Evil]].<ref name="FCI-p63" /> His symbol was that of a forked tail, often wrapped around a skull or sword.<ref name="BoVD-pp125-127" />

Demogorgon was sufficiently powerful to offer patronage pacts with [[warlock]]s,<ref name="PHB5e-p109">{{Cite book/Player's Handbook 5th edition|109}}</ref> who usually kept [[quasit]]s as familiars.<ref name="VGM5e-p219">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to Monsters|219}}</ref> His most devout followers were granted magic boons, such as an aspect of his dual minds that conferred resistance to mind-affecting effects. Their signature spells mimicked Demogorgon's own abilities, such as ''[[charm person]]'', ''[[enlarge or reduce person|enlarge/reduce]]'', and ''[[vampiric touch]]''.<ref name="MToF-p30">{{Cite book/Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes|30}}</ref>

===Aspects===
Mortals could not summon Demogorgon himself, but the most pious could use sacrifice to summon a temporary [[aspect]] of Demogorgon. The aspect was smaller and not as powerful as Demogorgon. An aspect of Demogorgon was always unbalanced, taking after one head more than the other.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" /><ref name="FCII.FA-pp1-4">{{Cite web enhancement/Fiendish Aspects II|1-4}}</ref>

==Relationships==
[[File:Demogorgon.png|thumb|right|300px|The Prince of Demons, on his way to beat up Orcus.]]
The hatred between [[Orcus]] and Demogorgon was legendary.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" /><ref name="MM1e-pp16-17" /> He was also a dedicated foe of [[Graz'zt]]<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" /> and had unsuccessfully attempted to conquer [[Fraz-Urb'luu]]'s realm.<ref name="Dr357-pp20-32" /> He hated [[Sekolah]] and encouraged his followers to kill [[sahuagin]].<ref name="MM-p88" />

Demogorgon was allied with the [[obyrith]] lord [[Dagon]],<ref name="FCI-p61" /> who often advised the heads of Aameul and Hethradiah separately. Dagon provided intellect, while Demogorgon provided brute strength.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" /> He was also frequently visited by [[Zuggtmoy]], who exchanged fungi at Gaping Maw and engaged with Demogorgon in several discussions. He was also allied with [[Ilsidahur]], lord of [[bar-lgura]]s.<ref name="Dr357-pp20-32" />

Demogorgon's romantic interests included the [[succubus]] [[Shami-Amourae]] and the Succubus Queen [[Malcanthet]], both of whom had manipulated him for their own benefits. Shami-Amourae, however, was imprisoned in the [[Layers of the Abyss#Layer 73: The Wells of Darkness|Wells of Darkness]] for her manipulations.<ref name="Du148">{{Cite dungeon/148/Wells of Darkness}}</ref><ref name="Dr357-pp20-32" /> He was the brother of another demon lord called [[Mandrillagon]].<ref name="DoD">{{Cite book/Dance of Demons}}</ref>

The [[balor]] [[Belaphoss]] considered himself to be the most powerful of Demogorgon's servants.<ref name="SCAG-p139">{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|139}}</ref>

==History==
===Origin and Ascension===
[[File:Demogorgon_in_darklake-5e.jpg|thumb|right|300px|''Demogorgon rampaging in the Darklake toward Sloobludop.'']]
There were conflicting legends about the origin of Demogorgon. One account claimed that he was the first of the [[tanar'ri]], who originated out of the consolidated fear of mortal souls, shortly after the [[obyrith]] [[Obox-ob]] was defeated by the [[Queen of Chaos]]. Cast aside as a deformity with his boneless arms and twin heads, Demogorgon lay hidden while the Queen of Chaos used [[sibriex]]es to better shape other primal mortal emotions into more refined tanar'ri.<ref name="Dr357-pp20-32" />

Another legend claimed that Demogorgon was originally a [[primordial]] and the first of his kind to set foot in the Abyss shortly after the formation of the plane, followed closely by Dagon, who challenged him for control of the plane. As he and Demogorgon fought, Obox-ob seized the opportunity to become the first Prince of Demons. A temporary alliance between Demogorgon, Orcus, and [[Baphomet]] was formed to defeat Obox-ob.<ref name="DN4e-pp7-8" />

According to this second legend, Demogorgon once had a single head until a blow from [[Amoth]] split it in two.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" /> Soon after that battle, Demogorgon defeated the stone [[primordial]] [[Storralk]]. He kept Storralk beneath his throne, torturing him for eternity through a ritual that created the [[ettin]]s from Storralk's blood and linked them to his body. Every time an ettin felt pain, that agony was transferred to Storralk.<ref name="MM24e-pp42-50" />

In both accounts, Obox-ob had since vowed to murder Demogorgon and reclaim his title.<ref name="FCI-p73">{{Cite book/Fiendish Codex I: Hordes of the Abyss|73}}</ref><ref name="Dr357-pp20-32" />

Following the defeat of the Queen of Chaos at the hands of the tanar'ri, the leading demon lords Graz'zt and Orcus then competed for the title of Prince of Demons. At that moment, Demogorgon, who had grown stronger during his exile, launched a massive assault throughout the plane to crush his rivals' forces. After he slew a dozen demon lords, draining their life forces with his tentacles, even Graz'zt and Orcus were forced to kneel. Thus, Demogorgon asserted his position as Prince of Demons.<ref name="DN4e-p9">{{Cite book/Demonomicon|9}}</ref><ref name="Dr357-pp20-32" />

At one point, Demogorgon and Dagon worked up an alliance of sorts, given the proximity of the [[Layers of the Abyss#Layer 89: The Shadowsea|Shadowsea]] to Gaping Maw.<ref name="FCI-p61" />

===Recorded History===
{{fq
| float  = 
| width  = 
| source = [[Davra Jassur]] of the [[Black Network]]<ref name="ddo">{{Cite game/Dungeons & Dragons Online||Anniversary Party}}</ref>
| text   = [[Bruenor Battlehammer|King Bruenor]],<br/>We have received strange news from Menzoberranzan, reports that parts of the drow city were destroyed by a being of unimaginable power. While you may be tempted to celebrate your enemy's misfortune and move on, I ask you to consider this: the creature that did this is still at large. We must take measures to protect ourselves. Over a millennium before the [[Era of Upheaval]], Demogorgon was shackled and imprisoned in [[Faerûn]]. within the fortress of [[Watcher's Keep]] in [[Tethyr]].<ref name="BGII.ToB">{{Cite game/Baldur's Gate II: Throne of Bhaal}}</ref>

During the [[Time of Troubles]], the magic maintaining Demogorgon's prison began to fail.<ref name="BGII.ToB" /> The Prince of Demons was fully aware of the presence of [[Gareth Dragonsbane]] and his allies when they descended into [[Abysm]] that same year.<ref name="ToB-p35">{{Cite book/The Throne of Bloodstone|35}}</ref> In response, [[Helm]] himself tasked an [[Knights of the Vigil|order of his knights]] to maintain the magic that kept "Imprisoned One" from escaping into the [[Prime Material plane]]. After some time the magic would expire, and members of the order would venture deep within the keep to complete the [[ritual]] that would sacrifice their life in order to restore the magical seal.<ref name="BGII.ToB" />

In the {{YearlinkName|1369}}, [[Odren]] and the knights sought to break the cycle of sacrifice their order undertook. The Knights of the Vigil petitioned the famous adventurer [[Abdel Adrian]] and his companions to perform the ritual in place of the knights and keep Demogorgon sealed away once again. While it was unknown if the ritual was performed again, Demogorgon was not sealed away forever and at some point returned to the Abyss.<ref name="BGII.ToB" /><ref name="Archmage-p357" />

On [[Nightal 15]], [[1486 DR]],<ref name="Archmage-p356">{{Cite book/Archmage/Hardcover (2015)|356}}</ref> [[Archmage of Menzoberranzan|Archmage]] [[Gromph Baenre]] weakened the barriers of ''[[faerzress]]'', inadvertently "summoning" Demogorgon and allowing him to pass from the Abyss to the [[Prime Material Plane]]. He arrived at the tower of [[Sorcere]] in [[Menzoberranzan]] and cut a swath of destruction as he exited the cavern.<ref name="Archmage-p357">{{Cite book/Archmage/Hardcover (2015)|357}}</ref> Gromph was able to weaken the ''faerzress'' because of his study of [[psionics]] and the psychic conveyance of a plan devised by the goddess [[Lolth]].<ref name="Archmage-p82">{{Cite book/Archmage/Hardcover (2015)|82}}</ref>

[[File:Drizzt vs Demogorgon.jpg|thumb|right|300px|''Drizzt and Demogorgon in battle.'']]

In [[1487 DR]], [[Drizzt Do'Urden]] defeated Demogorgon in Menzoberranzan by acting as a conduit for a barrage of magical energy, released upon him and held by the kinetic barrier of an [[illithid]] hive-mind.<ref name="Maestro-p264">{{Cite book/Maestro|264}}</ref>

In the late 15{{th}} century [[DR]], as a result of a vision of the demon lord in the [[Darklake]], a faction of the kuo-toa of [[Sloobludop]] started a cult of Demogorgon.<ref name="OotA5e-pp45,47-48" />

==Rumors & Legends==
Some myths concerning Demogorgon's past suggested that his hatred of Sekolah was earned during a time he spent as Sekolah's vassal, forced to do his bidding under the power of an [[artifact]].<ref name="MM-p88" />
