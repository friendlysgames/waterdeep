campaign/structure/arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md:83: *Sir Ambrose Everdawn.* An aging knight of Kelemvor who patrols the cemetery from sunset to sunrise, chasing off grave robbers and making sure the dead stay buried. His encounter with the party is predecided, not rolled: if the party enters after dark, they meet him — he intercepts anyone moving through the mausoleums at night as a matter of course, escorts trespassers out, and alerts the City Guard only if they refuse to leave. If the party has a plausible cover story (a Brandath-blood companion, a City Watch escort from Jalester's Mission 6, a writ from Laeral), he waves them through with a warning to be careful and be quick. If the party enters during the day, he does not appear at all — his patrol is nocturnal.
