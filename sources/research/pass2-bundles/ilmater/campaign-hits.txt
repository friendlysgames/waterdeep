campaign/setting/deities/tyr.md:31: - **Rank:** Tyr is a god of the Faerûnian pantheon and the head of the Triad, with Ilmater and Torm. The wiki calls him a greater god, and in its 2e and 3e sources the only lawful good one among the greater gods of Faerûn.
campaign/setting/deities/tyr.md:46: - His first major mark on Toril was the Procession of Justice in -247 DR. He came through a portal near the future Alaghôn in Turmish and led ten scores of archons against the chaotic and evil forces in the ruins of Jhaamdath. Ilmater allied himself with Tyr in -243 DR, and by the end of the campaign in -238 DR Tyr had destroyed Valigan Thirdborn, a god of anarchy. Torm joined them some years later to complete the Triad (*Faiths & Pantheons*; *The Grand History of the Realms*).
