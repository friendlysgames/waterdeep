campaign/structure/arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md:154: **The Broken Circle of Gods.** Scattered through the vault's frescoes — the Entrance Foyer's niches most explicitly — are images that should not exist: Dumathoin placing gems into a primeval range of mountains; Dumathoin visiting the illithid god Ilsensine, manifest as a disembodied emerald brain, and bathing in its psionic light; Dumathoin, Ilsensine, and Laduguer, god of the duergar, with hands clasped in a circle of friendship that official dwarven doctrine insists never existed. These images predate a falling-out between the three gods that the Morndinsamman has never fully explained. They are not necessary to **Vault of Dragons**'s plot — the party can walk past them without consequence — but a party that lingers and investigates is rewarded with one more thread connecting the vault's dwarven history to the deep, patient intelligences (illithid and aboleth alike) that the Undermountain campaign will eventually make explicit.
