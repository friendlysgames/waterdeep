campaign/setting/deities/siamorphe.md:31: - **Rank:** Siamorphe is a demigod (5e, *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* p.151; *Dragon Heist* calls her the same) and a demipower in 2e and 3e. In 4e she is an exarch who serves Amaunator, with the Eternal Sun as her dominion. In 3e she has no true allies or superiors among the gods.
campaign/setting/deities/siamorphe.md:35: - **Clergy:** Her priests are the Scions of Siamorphe, who most often serve as spiritual and practical advisers to the nobility (2e, 14th century DR). They revived elaborate rituals that had decayed under the previous Siamorphe and wrote new ceremonies for the noble families of Waterdeep and Tethyr, which were becoming fashionable. A baptism for a new heir can easily cost the family 10,000 gp (2e).
campaign/setting/deities/siamorphe.md:40: - **The quarrel with Tyr (1384 DR, 4e):** Shortly before the Spellplague, Tyr and Siamorphe, both then living in the House of the Triad, quarreled over a battle between Tethyrian and Calishite forces. She left to make her realm with Sune in Brightwater. Tyr sent Helm to Sune to explain his position, and Sune tried to make peace by proposing a marriage between Tyr and Tymora. Tyr agreed and began courting Tymora (*The Grand History of the Realms* p.159, 4e).
campaign/setting/deities/siamorphe.md:41: - **Origin, the line of mortal Siamorphes (2e):** The Divine Right is a semi-divine power that passes to a successor at the successor's dying moment, so that the current Siamorphe is always the latest in a lineage. The new holder is preferably a direct descendant of the last one, though anyone of noble blood will do. In the 13th and 14th centuries the holder was the Lady Siamorphe of Waterdeep, chosen when a riot killed her in 1256 DR. She took the role from a nobleman of Baldur's Gate.

campaign/setting/organizations/10-cassalanters.md:58: The Cassalanters are a noble family of Waterdeep and among the city's most prominent philanthropists. Victoro, a half-elf banker and worshipper of Lathander, and Ammalia, a wizard known for driving a hard bargain and for keeping the most beautiful butterfly garden in Waterdeep, fund temples, orphanages and civic improvements across the city. The family lives in a villa in the Sea Ward, is publicly known for its ancestral devotion to Siamorphe, and hosts public galas during Waukeentide, and the younger twins, Terenzio and Elzerina, are often seen at play in the gardens.
