campaign/setting/organizations/10-cassalanters.md:63: > - **Origins:** The Cassalanter name has stood in Waterdeep's noble registry for several generations. The Lords of Waterdeep ennobled the house in 1248 DR, about 244 years before the campaign, when they granted titles to several merchant houses (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *City of Splendors* 2e). The wiki lists Waukeen, Siamorphe and Asmodeus as the house's favored deities. Financial ruin was the trigger for the bargain, which was made three years ago, about 1489 DR. The deal also bought power, good health and long life.
