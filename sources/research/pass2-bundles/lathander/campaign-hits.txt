campaign/setting/notable-figures/cassalanters/01-victoro-cassalanter.md:23: **Persona.** To the world, Victoro is a devilishly handsome half-elf banker, philanthropist, and worshipper of Lathander whose profits fund charity, with an unreadable smile and a genuine warmth toward his younger children that makes the rest of what he is harder to see. To those he trusts, he is a secret priest of Asmodeus whose pact traded his three children's souls for power. Deep down, everything the Cassalanters tell the PCs about their situation is technically true; they do not mention that the ritual also requires poisoning 99 people at the Founders' Day feast. He has not entered the attic where Osvaldo is in months and cannot bring himself to go; that vigil belongs to Ammalia. He carries a ruby-tipped cane that functions as a rod of rulership.

campaign/setting/organizations/10-cassalanters.md:58: The Cassalanters are a noble family of Waterdeep and among the city's most prominent philanthropists. Victoro, a half-elf banker and worshipper of Lathander, and Ammalia, a wizard known for driving a hard bargain and for keeping the most beautiful butterfly garden in Waterdeep, fund temples, orphanages and civic improvements across the city. The family lives in a villa in the Sea Ward, is publicly known for its ancestral devotion to Siamorphe, and hosts public galas during Waukeentide, and the younger twins, Terenzio and Elzerina, are often seen at play in the gardens. Their eldest son is said to be at school in a faraway city, and more than one Waterdhavian has wondered aloud how a family can be so gracious, so prosperous and so fortunate all at once.
