'''Iggwilv''', also known as '''Tasha''' or '''Natasha''',<ref name="TCE5e-p4" /><ref group="note">Iggwilv and Tasha were originally separate characters until they were retconned to be the same person in [[Wikipedia:Expedition to the Ruins of Greyhawk|''Expedition to the Ruins of Greyhawk'']] in 2007.</ref> and the alias '''Zybilna''',<ref name="WBW5e-p161">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|161}}</ref><ref name="WBW5e-p204">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|204}}</ref> was an [[Oerth]]ian [[archmage]] and demonologist. She famously wrote the ''[[Demonomicon of Iggwilv]]'', a tome on demonic lore.<ref name="ERG3e-p8-9" /> 

Later in life she created her own [[Domain of Delight]] in the [[Feywild]],<ref name="WBW5e-p193" /> and ascended as one of the plane's powerful [[archfey]].<ref name="WBW5e-p193" /><ref name="WBW5e-p206">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|206}}</ref>

==Description==
[[File:Tasha2-5e.jpg|thumb|''Tasha in her study.'']]
The exact appearance of Iggwilv was unknown to many. Some described her as a ravishing beauty, while others claimed she was a bald and hideous crone.<ref name="RotE-p55">{{Cite book| author = [[Roger E. Moore]]| title = Return of the Eight| short_story = | date = May 1998| editor = [[Kij Johnson]]| publisher = [[TSR]]| ISBN = 0-7869-1247-2 | pages = {{{1|55}}}}}</ref> In fact Iggwilv was quite beautiful in her youth with raven hair that eventually went stark white.<ref name="WBW5e-p199" /> Even after becoming an archfey, she still retained a human-like appearance.<ref name="WBW5e-p205" /> 

==Abilities==
During her stay in the [[Abyss]], Iggwilv became extremely knowledgeable about the political hierarchy and goings on between the various powers within.<ref name="Dn149-p41" /> She was considered a master at [[summoning]] [[fiend]]s, planar exploration, [[necromancy|necromantic magic]],<ref name="RotE-p55" /> and later in life, gained command over [[fey]] beings.<ref name="WBW5e-p205" />

As an archfey, Iggwilv was immortal and could not die from aging.<ref name="WBW5e-p205" />

==Possessions==
[[File:Iggwilv_and_demonomicon-3e.jpg|thumb|left|200px|''Iggwilv and the ''Demonomicon''.'']]
Iggwilv had a vast collection of magic items, oddities and personal mementos that she kept within the [[Palace of Heart's Desire]] in her home plane of [[Prismeer]]. Notable among these were a [[Mirror of Heart's Desire|magical mirror]] that showed the individual gazing into it what they desired most in the reflection within; three [[jar of time|jars of time]], that displayed the visage of a being at three different points in their life,<ref name="WBW5e-p199">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|199}}</ref> a magical [[Chess (game)|chessboard]] gifted to her by the [[archmage]] [[Mordenkainen]],<ref name="WBW5e-p200" /> and the [[vorpal]] [[greatsword]] ''[[Snicker-Snack]]''.<ref name="WBW5e-p203" />

One of Iggwilv's most wondrous and powerful items was her ''[[Iggwilv's Cauldron|personal cauldron]]'', an [[artifact]] that she crafted with the assistance of her adoptive mother. It held two different forms and many magical properties, including the power to permanently 'freeze time' for everyone within a {{SIAdj|1000|ft}} radius.<ref name="WBW5e-p209-211">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|209-211}}</ref> 

Her personal library held [[tome]]s that originated from across the planes. Notable titles included ''[[The City That Waits]]'', ''[[I, Lyzandred]]'', ''[[Kingdom of the Ghouls]]'', ''[[The Many Masques of Mith]]'', ''[[Nadir of Nessus]]'', ''[[Prophecies of Explictica Defilus]]'', and ''[[Thingizzard's Night Balloon]]''.<ref name="WBW5e-p197" />

On her person Iggwilv wore a ''[[robe of the archmagi]]'' and an ''[[amulet of the planes]]''.<ref name="WBW5e-p205" /> During her time in the Abyss, she wielded a ''[[staff of power]]''.<ref name="Dn149-p44-52" />

==Activities==
While Iggwilv was most famous for creation of her namesake ''Demonomicon'', she also authored the unpublished tome, ''[[The Faceless Lord]]'', a more focused study on the [[Demon Prince]] [[Juiblex]],<ref name="WBW5e-p197">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|197}}</ref><ref name="WBW5e-p200">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|200}}</ref> and ''[[Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (in-universe)|Tasha's Cauldron of Everything]]''.<ref name="TCE5e-p5">{{Cite book/Tasha's Cauldron of Everything|5}}</ref>

===Spells===
Iggwilv was credited with a number of [[spell]]s, most of which were described in the ''Demonomicon''.<ref name="Dr336-p76-84">{{Cite dragon/336/Spellcraft: The Demonomicon of Iggwilv|76-84}}</ref> Her spells included: ''[[dolor (spell)|Dolor]]''; ''[[ensnarement]]''; ''[[exaction]]''; ''[[imbrue]]''; ''[[implore]]'';<ref name="Dr336-p76-84" /> ''[[lover's call]]'';<ref name="Dn149-p44-52" /> ''[[minimus containment]]'';<ref name="Dr336-p76-84" /> ''[[Tasha's caustic brew]]'';<ref name="TCE5e-p105,115">{{Cite book/Tasha's Cauldron of Everything|105,115}}</ref> ''[[Tasha's hideous laughter]],''<ref name="Dr359-p30" /> used ''circa'' the late 15{{th}} century [[DR]] in [[Waterdeep]] as a security measure against burglars;<ref name="WDH-p72">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|72}}</ref> ''[[Tasha's mind whip]]'';<ref name="TCE5e-p105,115" /> ''[[Tasha's otherworldly guise]]'';<ref name="TCE5e-p105,116">{{Cite book/Tasha's Cauldron of Everything|105,116}}</ref> and ''[[torment]]''.<ref name="Dr336-p76-84" />

Apart from spells developed by her, Iggwilv was credited the creation of ''[[Tasha's Atrocious Robe]]'' that was brought to [[Toril]] by planeswalker [[Mintassan]] the Magnificent. The [[enchanted]] [[robe]] was among the wizard's extensive collection of curios from across the multiverse, displayed in [[Mintassan's Mysteries]] in [[Westgate]] as of the {{YearlinkName|1374}}.<ref name="NWN2M">{{Cite game/Neverwinter Nights 2: Mysteries of Westgate}}</ref>

==Relationships==
[[File:Iggwilv and Graz'zt-4e.jpg|thumb|right|250px|''Iggwilv with her sometimes lover [[Graz'zt]].'']]
Iggwilv was considered one of the most powerful beings in the multiverse, on par with [[demon lord]]s.<ref name="Dn149-p41">{{Cite dungeon/149/Enemies of My Enemy|41}}</ref> 

Iggwilv had many confusing relationships with the demon prince [[Graz'zt]]. They were both lovers and enemies, their relationship constantly changing.<ref name="ERG3e-p8-9" /> Both have at various points been the consort, prisoner, and master to the other. While they had almost no trust between them, they also held no secrets from each other. Both Iggwilv and Graz'zt sought to use or betray the other, yet also supported or even rescued one another when in peril, and in their own twisted way, both genuinely shared a bond of love with the other. Iggwilv believed Graz'zt was the only demon worthy of her gaze while she was the only consort of Graz'zt that inspired feelings of jealousy (as evidenced when he attacked [[Fraz-Urb'luu]]).<ref name="Dr414-p1" />

While held prisoner by Graz'zt in his plane of the abyss, Iggwilv bore him many children. Graz'zt quickly and securely placed them in various locations and protected their identities from any beings that could bring them harm.<ref name="Dr414-p1">{{Cite dragon/414/History Check: The Iggwilv-Graz'zt Affair|1-5}}</ref>

The witch queen was an enemy of the [[Nine Hells]], owing partially to her interest in Graz'zt. She had a [[quasit]] [[familiar]]<ref name="DM150" /> named [[Iggrik]].<ref name="WBW5e-p203">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|203}}</ref> At one point she had four [[arcanaloth]] apprentices.<ref name="DM150">{{Cite dungeon/150/Prince of Demons|64}}</ref>

Iggwilv was the adopted daughter of [[Baba Yaga]],<ref name="WBW5e-p161">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|161}}</ref> the [[archfey]] sorceress known across the planes as the 'Mother of All Witches'.<ref name="WBW5e-p171" /> She had three adoptive sisters that together formed the [[Hourglass Coven]],<ref name="WBW5e-p4">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|4}}</ref> [[Bavlorna Blightstraw]], [[Skabatha Nightshade]], and [[Endelyn Moongrave]].<ref name="WBW5e-p201">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|201}}</ref>

For centuries, Iggwilv was aided by the [[Dwarf|dwarven]] clerk named [[Kalimanzaros]], who remained unfailingly loyal throughout their many years together.<ref name="WBW5e-p186">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|186}}</ref> During her time in the Feywild, Iggwilv garnered the aid of the [[high elf]] [[Raezil Uthemar]], who spied on other fey courts on her behalf.<ref name="WBW5e-p193">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|193}}</ref> As Zybilna, she was known to sponsor at least two warlocks, the amenable old [[warlock]] [[Madryck Roslof]] and the kenku Kettlesteam.<ref name="WBW5e-p18">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|18}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Iggwilv WBW.png|thumb|right|250px|''Iggwilv the Witch Queen, as an [[archfey]].'']]
{{Quote|I can't tell you how many times I've burned everything to the ground and started over.|Iggwilv, under her pen name ''Tasha''.<ref name="TCE5e-p39">{{Cite book/Tasha's Cauldron of Everything|39}}</ref>}}
Iggwilv was born in the world of Oerth and was adopted by the witch Baba Yaga, at which time she was known as Natasha "the Dark".<ref name="Dr83-p42" /> She became known later as Tasha, before adopting her alias of Iggwilv, although it was not clear whether any of those was her original name. It was at that early point that she was credited with inventing the ''Tasha's hideous laughter'' spell.<ref name="Dr359-p30" /> 

At one point she moved to [[Niflheim]], the second layer of [[Hades]].<ref name="Dn149-p44-52" />

Some time later Iggwilv settled in the [[Abyss]] and immersed herself in the study of [[demon]]-summoning and lore regarding demonic spirits. She sought to garner more power for herself, but made many enemies among the chaotic fiends and fled to worlds beyond for her safety.<ref name="WBW5e-p171">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|171}}</ref> Eventually, Iggwilv moved to her own Domain of Delight, known as Prismeer, in the Feywild, where she ruled from the Palace of Heart's Desire in the guise of Zybilna.<ref name="WBW5e-p205" /> Over time she transformed into a fey being and refocused her studies on that newfound state of existence rather than demonology.<ref name="WBW5e-p171" />

She was eventually betrayed by the three daughters of Baba Yaga—[[Skabatha Nightshade|Skabatha]], [[Bavlorna Blightstraw|Bavlorna]], and [[Endelyn Moongrave|Endelyn]]—the hags of the Hourglass Coven, and their cronies in the [[League of Malevolence]].<ref name="WBW5e-p190">{{Cite book/The Wild Beyond the Witchlight|190}}</ref> who used the magic of her ''[[Iggwilv's Cauldron|personal cauldron]]'' to freeze in time the Witch Queen and many of her palace's other inhabitants.<ref name="WBW5e-p4" />
