campaign/setting/deities/zariel.md:50: - In 1444 DR a vampire lord conquered Elturel, and a priest of Torm named Thavius Kreeg called on any power to save the city. Zariel appeared in a pillar of fire and gave him the Solar Insidiator, a sunlike sphere also called the Companion. It destroyed the vampire's army. The contract made the city, its lands and its oath-bound defenders forfeit to her, and after fifty years the Companion could take them (*Descent into Avernus* 5e).

campaign/setting/deities/zariel.md:65: > - **A warlock PC:** The Fiend patron in the 2024 *Player's Handbook* fits her (derived), and the wiki files her under Fiend patrons. No source gives Zariel-specific features. What her cults want is fighters who will go to war, and a patron who wants the souls of the greatest warriors in her ranks after death. What a pact costs is service for all time: her bargains are eternal and indissoluble, they read like ultimatums, and she demands loyalty. The wiki's worked example is Thavius Kreeg, who swore to submit to her in all matters for all time, and whose city was pulled into Avernus in 1492 DR.

campaign/setting/deities/zariel.md:71: Zariel is the archdevil who rules Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells, and she is the Hells' most committed general in the Blood War against the demons of the Abyss. Scholars say she began as an angel of Mount Celestia and became a devil after she led mortal soldiers into Avernus against her superiors' wishes. She wants the souls of the best fighters, and the soldiers and mercenary companies who bargain with her are said to find her bargains eternal.
campaign/setting/deities/zariel.md:73: **Tone:** Martial and unbending. Zariel is a general who recruits soldiers, so when a player asks about her, describe the war she is fighting and what she offers fighters before describing what she asks of them.
