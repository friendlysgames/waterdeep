'''Auril''' ({{Pronounce|AW|rill|audio=Auril.ogg|refs=<ref name="FaP-91"/>}}), called '''Saukuruk''' by her few worshipers among the [[Iulutiun]]s of the Great Glacier,<ref name="TGG-p39" /> was the [[neutral evil]] [[goddess]] of winter and cold in the [[Faerûnian pantheon]]. The embodiment of winter's cruelty<ref name="rofm-p274" /> and all its deadliest aspects,<ref name="Dragon378-32" /> '''Lady Frostkiss''' had a heart of ice to match her lethally cold beauty, eternally preserved beneath a sheet of rime.<ref name="FaP-91" /> As [[Talos]], leader of the [[Gods of Fury]], eroded her power over snow storms, '''The Cold Goddess'''<ref name="FR.FaA-p30" /> made her season even more frigid to remind the people in the north who controlled the cold.<ref name="FR.FaA-p31" />

The '''Frost Sprite Queen'''<ref name="D376-p10" /> was additionally seen as being synonymous with the [[Queen of Air and Darkness]] by some communities of [[fey]], such as those of the [[Shiverpine Forest]] in the [[Deep Wilds]].<ref name="Dragon367-p58">{{Cite dragon/367/Hall of the Frostmaiden|58-62}}</ref> However, after the Sundering the Queen of Air and Darkness was seen to be only impersonating Auril in order to retain followers on Toril.<ref>https://twitter.com/TheEdVerse/status/1228370295649599488?s=20</ref>

==Description==
Auril was known to take several distinct forms. The first was a furious figure of action known as '''The Frostmaiden''', the most frequently seen [[avatar]] in all but south and east Faerun. The Frostmaiden appeared as a lithe, attractive [[human]] woman with blue skin and a body made from ice and snow, her free-flowing hair long and white. She wore a fine, thickly furred gown and frost swirled around her.<ref name="FRCS.CotR-p10" /><ref name="FR.FaA-p31" /><ref name="FB-p42">{{Cite book/Frostburn|42}}</ref>

Auril's other form was the '''Icedawn''', an impassive apparition of icy hauteur that silently glided through the air. The Icedawn wore an ornate crown and hooked, spurred armor of opaque and light blue ice.<ref name="FR.FaA-p31" />

In the late 15{{th}} century, Auril was weakened by casting great magic night after night over Icewind Dale, and she manifested in her three mortal forms; the '''Cold Crone''' appeared as a {{SI|7|feet|long=yes}} tall, hunched bipedal creature with a snowy [[owl]]'s head topped by a pair of curled ram's horns. She had cloven hooves, arms ending in sharp, black talons, and grayish-white wolf fur covering her from the neck down.<ref name="rofm-p274-278">{{Cite book/Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden|274-278}}</ref>

Auril's second form was the '''Brittle Maiden''' or '''Lady Icekiss''', a {{SI|10|ft|long=yes}} tall figure of fearsome feminine aspect with a thin cloak of mist surrounding her and eyes that burned with a cold, blue light. Made entirely of ice and frost, her body crackled while she moved, with icy blades growing from her form at odd angles and breaking off after growing too long.<ref name="rofm-p274-278" />

Auril's third form was called '''Winter's Womb''' (or the '''Queen of Frozen Tears''' by her most devoted). It was a {{SI|3|ft|long=yes}} in diameter diamond of ice with facets and a sharp point at the bottom. Hovering in the air, the diamond contained her divine spark and radiated intense cold in every direction, her voice seeming to emanate from its heart.<ref name="rofm-p274-278" />

==Personality==
Arrogant and vain,<ref name="FB-p42" /><ref name="FaP-91" /> Auril was incapable of true feelings of love, honor, or other noble emotions,<ref name="FaP-91" /> yet adored her ice and all forms of beauty.<ref name="rofm-p274" /> From natural wonders, to art objects, to the artists themselves, the Frostmaiden froze them all in magical ice, preserving them from the ravages of time and hoarding them away for her viewing pleasure alone.<ref name="rofm-p274" /> Her ultimate goal was to cover the Realms and all other lands beneath her ice and snow.<ref name="Dragon378-32" /><ref name="FB-p42">{{Cite book/Frostburn|42}}</ref>

Despite being capricious, fickle and unpredictable,<ref name="FB-p42" /><ref name="FaP-91" /> Auril was also supremely cold, unfeeling and apathetic. She was incapable of mercy or compassion,<ref name="rofm-p274" /> a sadist that took great pleasure in torturing her enemies and harassing her foes.<ref name="FB-p42" /> She trapped offenders in blizzards and drove them insane with visions of warmth and the comforts of home, ultimately seeking to kill them with the sheer, bitter cold.<ref name="Dragon378-32" /><ref name="FaP-91"/>

Although all of this was true, many wise men and women questioned the source of her erratic behavior and have concluded that the goddess had something akin to [[multiple personality disorder]] (indicated by the dual nature of the ''[[Ring of Winter]]'' whose holder was the [[neutral good]] human Artus Climber),<ref name="VGtATM-p112">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to All Things Magical|112}}</ref> where each personality represented a different aspect of winter. The goddess was aware of her mental state, and at least one of her personalities was somewhat benign in nature and was worshiped by the [[Sossrim]] in the far north.<ref name="MentalState">{{cite web

==Divine Realm==
Auril had a [[divine realm]] called [[Winter's Hall]] in [[Pandesmos]], the topmost layer of [[Pandemonium]] in the [[Great Wheel cosmology]].<ref name="FR.FaA-p30" /><ref name="PoC-p90">{{Cite book/Planes of Chaos/The Travelogue|90}}</ref> [[Norse pantheon|Norse]] god [[Wikipedia:Loki|Loki]] also made Winter's Hall his abode, a place to hide away whenever he fell out of grace with the other [[Norse pantheon|Aesir]].<ref>{{Cite book/On Hallowed Ground|147}}</ref>

During the [[Spellplague]], Auril's realm was located within the [[Astral dominion|Astral Dominion]] of the [[Deep Wilds]] and was known as the [[Land Under Eternal Ice]].<ref name="Dragon367-p58" />

==Relationships==
In earlier centuries, Auril was a member of the Deities of Fury, along with [[Malar]], [[Umberlee]], and [[Talos]], and used their combined destructive powers to inspire much reverent fear and tribute.<ref name="rofm-p274" /> Her relationship with Talos was said to be close and cordial, but this was no obstacle from his attempt to usurp her following and she preferred not to rely on him due to his habit of responding but then directing all glory to himself.<ref name="FR.FaA-p155">{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|155}}</ref> Meanwhile she and Malar mutually hated each other. Umberlee was the only one she cooperated with to some degree of confidence,<ref name="FaP-91" /><ref name="FR.FaA-p31" /> but eventually the wrathful sea goddess came to despise her enduring cold and the motionless sheets of ice her roiling seas were turned into, forming an alliance with the other Gods of Fury against the Frostmaiden.<ref name="rofm-p274" />

Following the Spellplague, Auril gained significant power by siphoning the faiths of [[Ulutiu]], [[Aerdrie Faenya]], and [[Gruumsh]]/[[Talos]] into her own. In addition to her classic portfolio of cold and winter, the Frostmaiden claimed dominion over wind and storms, bringing her into direct conflict with the ancient primordial power [[Akadi]].<ref name="Dragon367-p61">{{Cite dragon/367/Hall of the Frostmaiden|61}}</ref>

Auril and [[Chauntea]] were mortal enemies, constantly battling each other.<ref name="FRCS.CotR-p11">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Set/Cyclopedia of the Realms|11}}</ref><ref name="PGtF-50">{{Cite book/Player's Guide to Faerun|50}}</ref> [[Sune]] opposed Auril, as she blamed her for the destruction of much that was beautiful.<ref>{{Cite book/Faiths and Pantheons|66}}</ref> [[Uthgar]] despised Auril because the [[Elk tribe]] abandoned him for her faith.<ref>{{Cite book/Faiths and Pantheons|85}}</ref> [[Rellavar Danuvien]], a minor [[elf|elven]] [[deity|god]] of cold, sought to interfere in the plans of Auril whenever he could.<ref name="DM236-p16">{{Cite dragon/236/The Seldarine Revisited|16}}</ref>

At one stage, Auril was in a relationship with [[Thrym]], god of the frost giants. During that time she became the mother to the [[empyrean]] daughter [[Nalkara]].<ref name="WDofMM5e-p301">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage|301}}</ref> At one point, long before the 14{{th}} century DR, Auril had a tryst with [[Garagos]]. The product of that relationship was a demi-god [[xixecal]], called [[Arctus]], the Walking Winter, who was imprisoned inside the [[Reghed Glacier]], growing powerful and filled with destructive rage over the centuries.<ref name="NWNdoom">{{Cite game/Neverwinter Nights: Doom of Icewind Dale}}</ref>

==Worshipers==
[[File:Aurilsymbol.png|thumb|right|250px||''The [[holy symbol]] of Auril.'']]
{{Main|Church of Auril}}
Sacrifices and veneration were primarily performed for the Frostmaiden out of fear.<ref name="FaP-91" /><ref name="FB-p42" />

In the [[North Faerûn|North]],<ref name="PGtF-14">{{Cite book/Player's Guide to Faerun|14}}</ref> Auril was a commonly worshiped, and feared, goddess and an integral part of northern cultures. Worship of the Frostmaiden was common in lands such as the [[Great Glacier]],<ref name="PGtF-12">{{Cite book/Player's Guide to Faerun|12}}</ref> the [[Icerim Mountains]],<ref name="PGtF-30">{{Cite book/Player's Guide to Faerun|30}}</ref> [[Sossal]]<ref name="RoF-p108">{{Cite book/Races of Faerûn|108}}</ref> and [[Vaasa]].<ref name="PGtF-15">{{Cite book/Player's Guide to Faerun|15}}</ref><ref name="RoF-p110">{{Cite book/Races of Faerûn|110}}</ref>

[[Northlander]]s considered Auril one of their primary deities and was a key part of their culture. She was feared and seen as the harbinger of winter upon the whole world. In the late autumn and over the winter, regular sacrifices were made to her. Sacrifices of food were placed upon rafts which were then set adrift in the ocean. Human sacrifices, usually prisoners of war or slaves, were placed on these rafts. Human sacrifice was a rare occurrence and was only seen during dire times.<ref name="tSF-p20">{{Cite book/The Savage Frontier|20}}</ref>

In the [[Moonsea]] region, followers of the Frostmaiden were shunned by society<ref name="MotM-p6">{{Cite book/Mysteries of the Moonsea|6}}</ref> and were most commonly encountered in [[Mulmaster]]<ref name="PotFS">{{Cite book/Pirates of the Fallen Stars|16}}</ref> and [[Zhentil Keep]]. During the winter in Zhentil Keep, followers of Auril openly recruited from the destitute, homeless and refugees. They were offered salvation, instead of the risk of freezing to death in an alley, if they joined the faith.<ref name="RoZK-p69">{{Cite book/Ruins of Zhentil Keep/Campaign Book|69}}</ref>

Worship and sacrifices to Auril were sparse in the [[Sea of Fallen Stars]] region, isolated to the northern shores.<ref name="PotFS" /> [[Illuskan]] invaders brought the worship of Auril to [[Tethyr]].<ref name="RoF-p104">{{Cite book/Races of Faerûn|104}}</ref>

Auril was a key part of the religion of [[taer]]s<ref name="UE-p14">{{Cite book/Unapproachable East|14}}</ref> and most taer clerics venerated the Frostmaiden.<ref name="UE-p73">{{Cite book/Unapproachable East|73}}</ref> 

Auril was one of the deities worshiped by [[Air genasi|air]] and [[water genasi]], especially those who lived in cold regions. Air genasi tended to be zealous in their worship, with a fanatical devotion to Auril. They enjoyed inflicting pain on other creatures using cold air.<ref name="RoF-p115-116">{{Cite book/Races of Faerûn|115-116}}</ref><ref name="RoF-p129">{{Cite book/Races of Faerûn|129}}</ref>

Auril was one of the deities worshiped by members of the [[Arcane Brotherhood]].<ref name="LoD-p110">{{Cite book/Lords of Darkness|110}}</ref>

Auril used [[frost giant]]s and [[winter wolves]] to deliver commands to her cults,<ref name="PoC-p90" /> while [[frostwind virago]]s<ref name="MM-V-p59" /> and [[winter hag]]s<ref name="DM367-p61">{{Cite dragon/367/Hall of the Frostmaiden|61}}</ref> were the handmaidens of the Frostmaiden.

Druids considered her to be among the [[First Circle]] of nature deities, and saw her as deserving of veneration in spite of her more violent tendencies relative to some other members of the Circle.<ref name="PHB5e-p69,294" />

===Holy Days===
* Midwinter (Night), [[Hammer 30]] to [[Alturiak 1]]. This was the most holy night of the year for the clergy, a festival of ice-dancing that lasted all night.<ref name="FR.FaA-p30" /> [[Druid]]s were also known to pay respect to the Frostmaiden on this date.<ref name="LotCSSB-52" />
* The [[Coming Storm]] and the [[Last Storm]], informal but enthusiastically celebrated rituals where the priests gathered and called howling ice storms down on a region to mark the onset and end of winter.<ref name="FR.FaA-p30" />
* [[Auril's Blesstide]], or Auril's Day, was a holiday (of sorts) held annually in the city of [[Waterdeep]] on the first day of new frost in the year. It was not so much a holiday as a way to seek to appease the Frostmaiden in hopes of a mild winter.<ref name="CoS-W-p15">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|15}}</ref>

In the {{YearlinkName|1450}}, Auril intervened in [[Calimshan]]'s [[Second Era of Skyfire]], freezing over the [[Skyfire Wastes]] and sending her followers to battle both [[Calim]]'s [[genie]]s and [[Memnon (efreeti)|Memnon]]'s [[efreeti]].<ref name="DM367-p61"/>

In the {{YearlinkName|1485}}, Chosen of Auril, [[Hedrun Arnsfirth]], began her war in Icewind Dale.<ref name="SCAG5e-p17">{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|17}}</ref>

In the late 15{{th}} century [[DR]], Auril encroached onto [[Umberlee]]'s domain by freezing her chaotic tides. Ending their alliance, Umberlee turned to the other Gods of Fury, Talos and Malar, and with their help forced Auril to retreat to the most frigid region of Toril: the [[Sea of Moving Ice]]. From an island known as [[Solstice]], the Goddess of Winter caused the [[Everlasting Rime]] beginning in the {{YearlinkName|1487}}. This seemingly eternal winter covered the area and extended over [[Icewind Dale]] for two years.<ref name="rofm-p274" /><ref name="rofm-p5">{{Cite book/Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden|5}}</ref> Sustaining such a powerful curse took a heavy toll on Auril,<ref name="rofm-p274"/> reducing her to a mortal form vulnerable to mighty adventurers.<ref name="FRAiF">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms: Adventures in Faerûn|119}}</ref> Yet, because she was ontologically a true goddess, she could not be permanently destroyed. So long as she retained her earthly worshippers, she could be reborn upon the next winter solstice, reclaiming her full divine power.<ref name="rofm-p276">{{Cite book/Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden|276}}</ref>

==Trivia==
Among the northern islands of the [[Trackless Sea]], such as [[Gundarlun]], the [[Purple Rocks]], [[Ruathym]] and [[Tuern]], the constant, bitterly-cold northwest winds were called "Auril's Breath".<ref name="tSF-p14">{{Cite book/The Savage Frontier|14}}</ref>
