campaign/setting/deities/zariel.md:38: - **Cults and cambions:** Her cults flourish in regions wracked by war. Their members are people who want to fight and lack the skill, people seeking fame through military service, refugees who cannot fight, and warriors who want a combat edge. Hobgoblins who left the goblinoid pantheon of Maglubiyet also join, and whole orders of knights, fighters' guilds and mercenary companies can fall under her sway. She has a taste for spawning cambions, and her cults are often headed by them (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* 5e).
