'''Garl Glittergold''' ({{Pronounce|garl|refs=<ref name="FP-p135"/>}}), also known as '''The Watchful Protector''', was a [[lawful good]] [[greater deity]] of [[gnome]]s. Garl led the [[Lords of the Golden Hills]] as the [[deity]] of luck, protection, and trickery.<ref name="FP-p135"/>
{{Quote|Most splendiferous, gregarious, and overall duplicitous of Gods (especially for the gnomes who embody those attributes quite cheerfully) is Garl Glittergold.|The introduction to ''[[Gnomefather]]'', a book featuring Garl Glittergold<ref name="BG3"/>}}

==Relationships==
Aside from the other non-[[evil]] members of the gnomish pantheon, Garl was allied with [[Yondalla]], [[Corellon Larethian]], and [[Moradin]]. His main companion was his intelligent battleaxe, [[Arumdina|Arumdina the Justifier]].<ref name="FP-p135"/>

Garl's tendency to jest with the various deities worshiped by the creatures and monsters of the [[Underdark]] resulted in him garnering many enemies, but chief among them was the [[kobold]] god, [[Kurtulmak]].<ref name="FP-p135"/>

==Worshipers==
{{Quote|Grant me but a fraction of thy wit, sire. It is all one could need.|A basic prayer.<ref name="BG3">{{Cite game/Baldur's Gate III}}</ref>}}
Garl was the most popular of the gnomish deities, and honored, in one way or another, by nearly all gnomes. His [[cleric]]s, known as Glitterbrights, wore war helms and gold belts. <ref name="FP-p135"/>

===Orders===
*[[Golden Watchful]]: A small knightly order of gnome paladins of Garl Glittergold<ref>{{Cite web/theedverse/2019-01-06 - Gnome paladins of Garl Glittergold}}</ref>

===Rituals===
On the 13{{th}} day of each month, the Forgotten Folk held the [[Communion of Laughter]] in his honor. It consisted of joke-telling contests, communal meals, dancing, prayers, and storytelling, and donations of [[gold]], which Garl's church used to the benefit of the entire community.<ref name="FP-p135"/>

==History==
According to legend, Garl Glittergold was exploring underground when he found a new cavern, and inside, a new people—the gnomes. He told them a joke and then led them out into the wider world.<ref name="DD-151"/>

According to legend, Garl let himself be captured by Kurtulmak, only to escape and cause the entire roof of Kurtulmak's cavern to fall on his head.<ref name="FP-p135"/>
