campaign/setting/notable-figures/emerald-enclave/02-jeryth-phaulkon.md:25: **Persona.** To the world, Jeryth is a disembodied female voice heard by anyone in the Phaulkonmere gardens. Her voice comes from the leaves, the gravel and the air together, and the branches move when she speaks. To those she trusts, she is a noblewoman who became a Chosen of Mielikki and grants sanctuary, spells, and charms. Her approval shows in the garden, where flowers open and a warm breeze moves along the path. Deep down, she is disinterested in the dragon hoard. When asked about the gold, she answers once, "That question is for the Lords to settle," and says nothing more. Her real concern is something old and patient that she senses dreaming beneath the city, and she does not know its name.
campaign/setting/notable-figures/emerald-enclave/02-jeryth-phaulkon.md:29: **Relationships.** Jeryth is the Chosen of Mielikki, the patron of Phaulkonmere, and the Emerald Enclave's highest authority in Waterdeep. Her relationship with the party runs through Melannor, who relays her words as she speaks them. Her direct interventions carry the weight of divine mandate.
campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:24: **Secondary Contact:** Jeryth Phaulkon, the lady of the Phaulkonmere estate. She was a noblewoman before she became a Chosen of Mielikki, and for several years she has lived as a disembodied voice in the gardens. She cannot be harmed in this state, and she can cast any druid spell. She sends no one on missions, and the animals carry only Melannor's words.
