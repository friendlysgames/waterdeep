'''Mielikki''' ({{Pronounce|my|LEE|kee|audio=Mielikki.ogg|refs=<ref name="FRCS1e-p12" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" />}}) was the [[neutral good]] [[goddess]] of forests and forest creatures (particularly [[dryad]]s) and the patron of [[druid]]s and [[rangers]] in the [[Faerûnian pantheon|Faerûnian]] and [[Finnish pantheon]]s.<ref name="FRCS1e-p12" /><ref name="Dragon54" /><ref name="FRA-p26" /><ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /><ref name="F&A-p112">{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|112}}</ref><ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref name="EGPEFR-p154">{{Cite book/Ed Greenwood Presents Elminster's Forgotten Realms|154-156}}</ref><ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" /><ref name="HoF-p115" /> The '''Lady of the Forest''' was also the goddess of autumn for a time, but [[Myrkul]] reclaimed that domain after his return during the [[Second Sundering]].<ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="SCAG p32">{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|35}}</ref> She was one of the nature gods of the [[First Circle]],<ref name="SCAG-p126">{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|126}}</ref> and scholars of other lands proposed she was known as '''Khelliara''', the '''Forest Maiden''', in [[Rashemen]],<ref name="FRA-p26" /><ref name="FRCG4e-p80" /><ref name="FRCS-203">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition|203}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|12}}</ref> and she had a [[half-elven]] [[aspect]] known as [[Khalreshaar]] on the isle of [[Evermeet]].<ref name="DD-142">{{Cite book/Demihuman Deities|142}}</ref>

==Personality==
Mielikki was cheerful with a ready smile and had confidence in herself and her path.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref name="HoF-p115" /> However, while she fully showed that confidence when in small groups, she was averse to formal speaking and being in charge of larger groups.<ref name="F&A-p112" /> She did not consider people her friends lightly, but she demonstrated fierce loyalty and protectiveness to those she did.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref name="HoF-p115" /> Her views tended to be those of the archetypal ranger.<ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /> Yet, in the late 15{{th}} century DR, mortals saw her as distant and spiritual, and not as human as some other gods. While not uncaring of mortals, her attention and blessings could be harder to attract.<ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" />

While she knew that death was a part of life and some must die so that others may live, she could not just accept injuries and deaths of both animals and friends. She regularly healed such stricken beings as she found them, rather than leave them to be part of the circle of life as Silvanus would.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref name="HoF-p115" />

==Description==
Mielikki's preferred [[avatar]] was the '''Supreme Ranger''', where she appeared as a tall woman of robust and shapely build and lithe grace. She moved with the spring of a leaping buck and the ease of a prowling [[panther]]. Her russet-colored hair was long and unbound so it hung around her shoulders and down her back, while her eyes were large and brown, and deep like pools. Her clothes were dull green and brown in color and she wore the [[leather armor]] commonly used by rangers.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref name="CoS-c18" />

[[File: Mielikki - Deities & Demigods.jpg|thumb|left|250px|''Mielikki as the Forest Queen, with wolf and songbirds.'']]
Her other avatar was the '''Forest Queen''', seeming to be a young maiden who had golden-hued [[moss]] and leaves for hair. She wore sheer yellow and green gossamer [[robes]], even in chill winter weather, and had summer songbirds fluttering around her person.<ref name="FRCS1e-p12" /><ref name="D&D1e-p60" /><ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FaP-p48" />

No matter her chosen form, Mielikki was {{SI|10|ft|long=yes}} tall, spoke in a rich low purr, and always walked on the air so her feet never made contact with ground, and thus didn't leave any tracks and didn't make any sound. Occasionally, she had a [[stag]]'s antlers emerging from her forehead, either already present when she appeared or growing slowly as mortals watched, though she displayed these less often after [[Beshaba]] adopted black antlers as her symbol, and had almost abandoned it by [[1372 DR]].<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FaP-p48" />

===Manifestations===
When not appearing as one of her avatars, Mielikki manifested most often in the form of a white unicorn that glowed and galloped in the air. This could [[teleport]] anywhere in Faerûn as she willed, communicate mentally with all living creatures, and cast spells with a touch of its [[alicorn]]. On other occasions, she appeared as a floating, glowing light of a blue-white or a green, gold, and rust hue. Through it, she could speak, envelop and carry items, and use her magic.<ref name="F&A-p113">{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|113}}</ref><ref name="DD&D" /> More subtly, she could be felt when gazing in wonderment at a unicorn and in the hearts of those who walked in peace in the woods.<ref name="CDM-p65" />

The faithful, however, believed her most common manifestation was her whispering voice, echoing forever through all the forests of the world via the rustling of leaves.<ref name="CDM-p65">{{Cite dragon/384/Channel Divinity: Mielikki|65-66}}</ref><ref name="F&A-p113-114" /> After a time of meditation and introspection, a good and faithful worshiper could listen to and comprehend these whispers. Some bards and rangers tried writing them down, and though they got different results each time, they generally found messages of understanding and preserving the forests and wildlife, and long-term listeners apparently became talented in tracking, survival, and [[zoology]].<ref name="F&A-p113-114">{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|113-114}}</ref> At other times, she might choose to speak more directly within a forest glade.<ref name="HoF-p115" />

To do her work, Mielikki might send [[dryad]]s, [[satyr]]s, [[Sprite (creature)|sprites]], [[swanmay]]s, [[treant]]s, or [[unicorn]]s, and forest animals like [[hawk]]s, [[wolves]], and [[songbird]]s.<ref name="F&A-p113" /><ref name="DD&D">{{Cite web enhancement/Deity Do's and Don'ts|13}}</ref><ref name="HoF-p115" /> [[Elk]] were sacred to her in her Finnish incarnation,<ref name="D&D1e-p60" /> while the white [[hart]] of the High Forest<ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Player's Guide|133}}</ref> and the mysterious [[Mielikkar]] were sacred to her in Faerûn.<ref>{{Cite book/The North: Guide to the Savage Frontier/The Wilderness|73}}</ref>

==Powers==
Mielikki was a supremely talented tracker, better than Malar and surpassed perhaps only by Gwaeron Windstrom.<ref>{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|105}}</ref><ref name="F&A-p112" /> As a master ranger and druid, she was skilled in all aspects of survival, hunting, wilderness lore, and more, and she was particularly skilled in bowmaking. Her greatest favored enemies were [[undead]], [[shapechanger]]s, and [[dragons]], followed by [[giant]]s and [[animal]]s. She was instantly alerted to any action that was despoiling a forest or threatened to do so.<ref name="FaP-p48" />

Mielikki could cast [[spells]] from any [[school of magic]] bar those of [[necromancy]] and [[illusion]] and from any [[sphere]] bar the reversed, harmful forms of spells in the [[healing sphere]] and [[necromantic sphere]].<ref name="FaP-p48" /> She was herself immune to necromancy spells and reversed, harmful forms of healing and necromantic sphere spells, and her very touch destroyed [[undead]].<ref name="D&D1e-p60" /><ref name="F&A-p112-113">{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|112-113}}</ref> Moreover, she cast all spells from the [[animal sphere]] and [[plant sphere]] that involved of course animals and plants at twice their normal power in all aspects and they were harder to resist.<ref name="F&A-p112" /> She cast spells as a druid and commanded all the spells and powers of the [[Animal domain|Animal]], [[Good domain|Good]], [[Plant domain|Plant]], and [[Travel domain]]s.<ref name="FaP-p48" />

In particular, simply by touching any [[tree]], Mielikki could [[create treant]]s at will and she could summon twelve forest creatures of one type to her side every two minutes, and they obeyed their goddess unto death.<ref name="D&D1e-p60" /><ref name="F&A-p112-113" /> She could summon great packs of wolves or flocks of hawks<ref name="D&D1e-p60" /> or up to a dozen or so unicorns; command plant creatures up to {{SI|13|mile|k|long=yes}} away; and speak to animals and cause them to grow in size or [[awaken|become intelligent]], or else [[deep slumber|fall asleep]].<ref name="FaP-p48" /> No woodland creature could harm her, even those controlled by others.<ref name="D&D1e-p60" />

Mielikki's very presence cancelled all mind-altering effects, such as [[charm]]s and [[psionics]], on all good and non-evil creatures up to {{SIrange|20|30|yard|long=yes}} from her person.<ref name="D&D1e-p60" /><ref name="F&A-p112-113" /> In addition, she had the [[psionic]] defense ''[[intellect fortress]]''.<ref name="D&D1e-p60" />

In combat, Mielikki was skilled in fighting with twin scimitars—her ''hornblades''—and in archery.<ref name="F&A-p112-113" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /> However, she did not engage in melee often, and favored her fists when she did. She would rather rely on her own skills and magic. She also sometimes used a bow, but its arrows struck not to inflict injury but to bring life—where they landed, forest plants appeared and grew in a matter of moments, even [[entangle|entangling]] whoever she wished.<ref name="F&A-p112-113" /> 

==Possessions==
When in battle, Mielikki sometimes fought with her two [[scimitar]]-sized ''[[hornblade]]s'', known as the ''[[Hornblades]]'',<ref name="F&A-p112-113" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /> which she sometimes lent to mortal rangers who embarked on great quests on her behalf.<ref name="F&A-p112-113" /> She also had her [[longbow]], named ''[[Hartsong]]''.<ref name="F&A-p112-113" /><ref name="FaP-p48" />

==Divine Realm==
Mielikki had long dwelled upon the [[Prime Material plane]] itself,<ref name="FRCS1e-p12" /><ref name="D&D1e-p60" /><ref name="FRA-p26" /><ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="OHG-p104" /> and though the precise location was unknown to others,<ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /> she made her home in a hidden grove near the headwaters of the Unicorn Run.<ref name="SD-p27" /> She only established a [[divine realm]] in the [[Outer Planes]] in the {{YearlinkName|1369}}. It's likely she helped Eldath move out there too.<ref name="F&A-p57" /><ref name="F&A-p112" />

After that, she resided in the [[Grove of the Unicorns]], which in the [[Great Wheel cosmology]] was found in [[Krigala]], topmost layer of the [[Beastlands (plane)|Beastlands]],<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="OHG-p104" /> and in the [[World Tree cosmology]] was located within the [[House of Nature]].<ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition|258}}</ref><ref name="PGtF3e-p158-159" />

Following the [[Spellplague]], by the late 15{{th}} century DR, Mielikki resided with Silvanus in his domain<ref name="FRPG-p154" /> in the [[Deep Wilds]] in the [[World Axis cosmology]].<ref name="FRCG4e-p63"/>

==Activities==
Mielikki most enjoyed exploring all the forests of Faerûn.<ref name="F&A-p112" /> In fact, she once claimed to Moongentle Lharinn that she'd never explored the deserts, ice-capped mountains, or cities of the world.<ref name="SD-p27" /> She cared for and defended these forests,<ref name="FRPG-p154">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Player's Guide|154}}</ref> and especially the [[High Forest]], according to the druids there.<ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition|165}}</ref> Though their reasons were unknown to mortals, she and Eldath protected the High Forest and prevented it from being logged by humans, elves, and others, and her followers claimed she would personally bring destruction down on those who would despoil it, regardless of their reasons.<ref name="TNTW-p58">{{Cite book/The North: Guide to the Savage Frontier/The Wilderness|58}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/Silver Marches|27}}</ref><ref name="D&D1e-p60" />

In particular, Mielikki was said to wander the [[Unicorn's Run]], a river within the High Forest, and to live nearby, and was often sighted in the area. Harpers held this to be obviously true and even made hazardous pilgrimages up the river.<ref>{{Cite book/Waterdeep and the North|10}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/The Savage Frontier|51}}</ref> South of it, the [[Delimbiyr Crescent]], too, was believed by the Harpers to be her favored hunting ground.<ref name="TLB">{{ Cite web/Legacy of the Green Regent/The Legacy Begins}}</ref> She watched over and protected the whole [[Delimbiyr Vale]], desiring to make the land a sanctuary for her faith and a showpiece of her ideals of harmony between nature and civilization.<ref name="WitGR">{{Cite web/Legacy of the Green Regent/What is the Green Regent}}</ref>

Mielikki also watched over rangers, druids, dryads and other forest beings,<ref name="D&D1e-p60" /><ref name="OHG-p104" /> as well as good people and lost children in any woodland, regardless of how evil the threats against them. In forest places that inspired wonder, Mielikki was said to prevail over all.<ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" /> She was the most responsive of deities, according to Elminster, always answering those who prayed to her and not only when convenient. She gave aid to the faithful within a year of their asking, and if she did not, she told them why they had not measured up, and only completing a great quest could change her mind.<ref name="D274-105" /> Sometimes, so that she might test the conduct of her rangers, Mielikki posed as a mortal woman lost and alone in the woods. But those who were observant would see she made no trail, and that her feet didn't even touch the ground.<ref name="F&A-p113" />

On [[Greengrass]], [[Midsummer|Midsummer Night]], and [[Shieldmeet]], Mielikki brought herds of unicorns together to ride through the forests all night long and [[teleport]] as many times as they liked and up to three times their normal range. At such times her faithful were allowed to ride them bareback, so they ended up travelling far and seeing the world.<ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref name="F&A-p114">{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|114}}</ref>

==Relationships==
[[File:Gods of Nature.jpg|thumb|right|350px|''A gathering of the gods of nature: Mielikki and her unicorn, [[Chauntea]], [[Eldath]], and [[Silvanus]].'']]
Mielikki was a servant of [[Silvanus]], the Oak-Father, together with [[Chauntea]], the Grain Goddess, and [[Eldath]], the Goddess of Singing Water.<ref name="FRCS1e-p12" /><ref name="FRA-p26" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref name="FRCS1e-p17">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Set/Cyclopedia of the Realms|17}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 2nd edition (revised)/Running the Realms|46,48,54}}</ref><ref name="FRCS2e-Cards" /><ref>{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|3,47,57,145}}</ref> Mielikki, Eldath, and Silvanus counted each other as allies, worked in harmony, and held true trust and affection for one another, and these relationships were mirrored in the alliances of their churches and priests.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" /><ref name="F&A-p57">{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|57,145}}</ref>

Mielikki's calmness tended to ease Silvanus's rage at those who harmed nature.<ref name="CDM-p65" /> Although her followers sometimes referred to her with the honorific '''Daughter to Silvanus''', Mielikki was not actually the daughter of Silvanus; rather, she considered him a father figure. This led to some confusion and a false myth told that Mielikki was the outcome of an affair between Silvanus and [[Hanali Celanil]], an elven goddess of love. Particular proponents of this were the half-elf followers of her Khalreshaar aspect.<ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="DD-142" /> Similarly, she thought of Eldath like a sister.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /> So close was their relationship that she gave her fellow goddess the nickname 'Datha' and they had been seen to hug one another like sisters.<ref name="CoS-c18" /><ref name="F&A-p57" /><ref group="note">3{{rd}}-edition's ''Faiths and Pantheons'' explicitly calls Silvanus her father and Eldath her sister, but the earlier 2{{nd}}-edition sourcebook ''Faiths & Avatars'' makes it clear that these were mortal misunderstandings.</ref> Despite these bonds, Mielikki followed her own path.<ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" /> 

While she served Silvanus, she in turn was served by [[Gwaeron Windstrom]], [[Lurue]], and [[Shiallia]].<ref name="FRCS1e-p17" /><ref name="FRA-p26" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref>{{Cite book/Powers & Pantheons|26,36,55}}</ref> Gwaeron served as Mielikki's messenger to the [[Seldarine|elven]], [[Lords of the Golden Hills|gnomish]], and [[halfling pantheon]]s when she needed a favor, owing to his good relations with them. He also served as her intermediary with mortals, usually rangers, in the North when direct speech was required, for which he was titled the Mouth of Mielikki.<ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 2nd edition (revised)/Running the Realms|57-58}}</ref><ref name="P&P-p27">{{Cite book/Powers & Pantheons|27}}</ref><ref name="SCAG-p29">{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|29}}</ref> When mortals prayed to her through him, he tracked her down to whichever forest she was concealed in and delivered the message.<ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" /><ref name="SCAG-p29" /> On some of her expeditions, Gwaeron accompanied Mielikki to lend her aid in tracking and in training her own rangers.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="P&P-p27" /> When pressed into battle, which was rare, or when hunting in the Beastlands, Mielikki rode upon the back of Lurue the Unicorn, who was a friend and ally.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref>{{Cite book/Powers & Pantheons|37}}</ref><ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" /><ref name="HoF-p115" /> Because of this close association and Mielikki's own unicorn symbol, some mortals confused the two.<ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" /> Lastly, Shiallia lent her services in planting seeds and nurturing seedlings in the High Forest and in midwifing for pregnant forest animals.<ref name="F&A-p112" /> After the Spellplague, Shiallia was counted as Mielikki's [[exarch]].<ref name="FRCG4e-p63" />

Mielikki was also allies with [[Shaundakul]], [[Lathander]], and [[Nobanion]], and was of course an enemy of [[Malar]], [[Moander]], [[Myrkul]], [[Talona]], and [[Talos]].<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FRCS3e-p246" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /><ref>{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|90,119,124,142-143,152,155}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/Powers & Pantheons|41}}</ref> Talos and Malar particularly hated Mielikki's dream for the Delimbiyr Crescent, and often had their cults bring destruction and bloodshed to her land and her people there.<ref name="WitGR" /> Among the non-human gods, she was an ally of [[Marthammor Duin]] of the [[dwarven pantheon]]; [[Angharradh]] (who took a motherly interest in her), [[Corellon Larethian]], [[Rillifane Rallathil]], and [[Solonor Thelandira]] (they were known to be romantically linked) of the [[elven pantheon]]; [[Baervan Wildwanderer]] of the [[gnome pantheon]]; and [[Sheela Peryroyl]] of the [[halfling pantheon]].<ref>{{Cite book/Demihuman Deities|74,97,100,120,132-133,137,172}}</ref> As Khalreshaar, she was believed to serve Rillifane Rallathil.<ref name="DD-142" /> She was counted among the so-called [[Gentle Sisters]]<ref name="SD-p27" /> and as part of the [[The Hexad|Sacred Hexad]].<ref name="EE.C-p2">{{Cite book/Elminster's Ecologies/Cormanthor|2,3}}</ref> Further afield, Mielikki was on good terms with the [[Oerth]] deity [https://greyhawkonline.com/greyhawkwiki/Ehlonna Ehlonna]. The two woodland goddesses got together often to catch up, relax, and drink [[oak]]root [[tea]]. Though Ehlonna had no presence on [[Toril]], Mielikki answered prayers on her behalf for any of Ehlonna's followers who happened to arrive there.<ref>{{cite web

To be her [[chosen]], the goddess granted her certain powers to make her a steadfast forest warrior.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="CoS-SotC-p9">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Secrets of the City|9-10}}</ref> She charged Jeryth with defending the High Forest on her behalf.<ref name="TNTW-p58" /> The Forest Queen bestowed on her Chosen an immortal unicorn companion, and after death these worthies would live on as disembodied spirits of certain natural sites.<ref name="HoF-p115" />

But a more long-standing champion was the [[Green Regent]], a chosen of Mielikki entrusted with defending the Delimbiyr Vale and her dream for it. Over centuries, every four years, this role was passed from person to person, each one chosen by Mielikki according to her current needs. In a special rite in Loudwater conducted by the [[Circle of the Stag]], she picked all kinds of people, and often not the most likely, but always proving to be right for the role and for her plans. The Green Regents were supported by their [[Scions of the Green Regent|Scions]], the remaining candidates picked by the goddess.<ref name="WitGR" /><ref name="AFR">{{Cite web/Legacy of the Green Regent/A Fellowship Rises}}</ref> She communicated with the Green Regents through dreams.<ref>{{Cite book/Humility|6}}</ref>

On the planes, her [[proxies]] were [[Tiera]] and [[Lemminkainen]].<ref name="OHG-p104" />

==Worshipers==
:{{Main|Church of Mielikki}}
{{Quote|Guide my path as only you know how - in whispering leaves and slowing grassland.|A basic prayer to Mielikki.<ref name="BG3">{{Cite game/Baldur's Gate III}}</ref>}}

Mielikki's worshipers were concentrated in the northern parts of Faerûn,<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="F&A-p113" /> namely in the [[Delimbiyr Crescent]] and [[Loudwater]],<ref name="TLB" /><ref name="WitGR" /> [[High Forest]], the [[North]], the [[Silver Marches]], and in the city of [[Silverymoon]]; in the [[Dalelands]], particularly [[Archendale]], [[Daggerdale]], [[Harrowdale]], and [[Shadowdale]]; and in [[Cormanthor]], as in [[Elventree]]. In the east, they could be found in [[Rashemen]], [[Aglarond]], and the [[Great Dale]], and in the south as far as the [[Chondalwood]].<ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition|23,116,123,126,130,137,160,165,171,173,201,203}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/Player's Guide to Faerûn|12,14,25,30}}</ref> She was among the major non-elven deities venerated in [[Myth Drannor]] in [[Cormanthyr]] before its fall.<ref>{{Cite book/Cormanthyr: Empire of the Elves|53}}</ref>

In these places, she was venerated by woodsmen among the [[human]]s, [[elves]] (especially [[wood elves]]), and [[half-elves]], as well as by [[dryad]]s, [[hamadryad]]s, and [[treant]]s,<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="F&A-p113" /><ref>{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|108,117}}</ref> and by forest creatures with no common gods of their own.<ref name="FRCS2e-Cards" /> Common folk appealed to her when children were lost in the forest, so she might protect and guide them. Woodcutters left simple shrines to her in thanks for their safety and for the wood they'd taken, and such shrines marked the limits of future hunting and logging. Otherwise, the Lady of the Forest was little talked about, except in peaceful woodland settings.<ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" />

The majority of [[ranger]]s revered Mielikki, as their leader, their watchful mother, and the supreme ranger.<ref name="F&A-p113" /> They were among her most fervent followers.<ref name="FRCS2e-Cards" /> Yet many of the rangers of the North prayed to Gwaeron Windstrom instead, worrying that Mielikki was too divine, wild, primal, and mysterious to be beseeched directly, while Gwaeron at least was once a mortal ranger like them.<ref name="SCAG-p29" /><ref name="SCAG5e-p21,34" /> Among [[adventurer]]s, Mielikki was followed by rangers of all temperaments,<ref name="Dragon54" /><ref name="FRCS1e-p18">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Set/Cyclopedia of the Realms|18}}</ref><ref name="FRA-p26" /> by [[druid]]s,<ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Adventures|15}}</ref> and by [[cleric]]s and [[thieves]] of [[good]] or [[chaotic]] bent.<ref name="FRCS1e-p18" /><ref name="Dragon54" /> Many [[Harpers]] also followed Mielikki,<ref name="F&A-p113" /> as did most members of the [[Emerald Enclave]].<ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition|273}}</ref>

Under her Khalreshaar aspect on Evermeet, she was mainly worshiped by a growing cult of half-elves. They believed her to be the daughter of Silvanus and [[Hanali Celanil]], and as such, the first truly half-elven deity.<ref name="DD-142" />

===Symbol===
[[File:Mielikki holy symbol HoF.png|thumb|right|''A holy symbol of Mielikki from around 1501 DR.'']]
Her traditional [[holy symbol]] was a white [[unicorn]]'s head, golden horned and blue eyed, facing sinister, that is, to the left.<ref name="FRA-p26" /><ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="FaP-p48" /> An alternative symbol showed a white unicorn over a green field.<ref name="FRCS1e-p17" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /> An older symbol was a white star on a green leaf,<ref name="FRCS1e-p17" /><ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /> specifically a tiny and dazzlingly bright white star balanced on a green leaf of [[oak]] or [[ash]] that was open and verdant.<ref name="F&A-p112" /> Within the Finnish pantheon, her symbol was an evergreen tree.<ref name="D&D1e-p60" /><ref name="OHG-p104" /> Her colors were forest green and red.<ref name="DD&D" /><ref name="D&D1e-p60" />

==History==
Mielikki hailed from the [[Finnish pantheon]].<ref name="FRCS1e-p12" /><ref>{{Cite book/Faiths & Avatars|14}}</ref> Though her fellow gods were tiring of life, Mielikki was still too young and vibrant to give in to their apathy, and so chose to establish a new following on Toril among its many nature-worshipers to stay strong. It's likely that [[Loviatar]] pursued her there to do the same, because of her old hatred for her.<ref name="OHG-p104" />

Followers of the Sacred Hexad believed their six nature deities made the forests of the world, particularly the great forest Cormanthor. They held that Mielikki and Rillifane had planted trees of all species and sizes.<ref name="EE.C-p2" />

An early legend held that while Mielikki drank from the shallows of [[River Rauvin]], the newly foaled goddess Lurue appeared before her in a shaft of moonlight. They became friends and Mielikki taught Lurue to chase the moon through the forests of the North. This site was later held to be sacred to both goddesses. Centuries later, the pair returned, in the guise of a ranger and her steed, and there found [[Moonsilver Inn]] and [[Silverymoon|Silverymoon Town]] (established [[574 DR]]<ref>{{Cite book/The Grand History of the Realms|92}}</ref>). They loved the place for the people's desire to build in harmony with the land, rather than cut the forests and despoil it. They blessed the inn, making it a sanctuary for the good, and the city of Silverymoon grew up around it.<ref>{{Cite book/Powers & Pantheons|39}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/The North: Guide to the Savage Frontier/Cities & Civilization|46}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/Silver Marches|56-57}}</ref> In the late 1400s DR, however, it was told that, after Mielikki was finished creating the forests, she sought a guardian and representative on the Material Plane to keep watch there. Agreeing, Selûne created Lurue and sent her to work for Mielikki.<ref>{{Cite book/Candlekeep Mysteries|114}}</ref>

An ancient and unsupported legend told that the human [[Khala]] of [[Imaskar]] had devoted themselves to the god [[Bane]] the Black Lord and perpetrated numerous atrocities. For their deeds, the gods of law inflicted curses upon them. Among them, Mielikki accused them of acting like beasts but committing human evils, and thus cursed them to cast human shadows to remind them on their crimes and lost souls. Out of all the gods' curses was born the [[peryton]], a vicious, stag-headed, eagle-bodied beast.<ref>{{Cite book/Elminster's Ecologies/The Great Gray Land of Thar|27–28}}</ref>

In the {{YearlinkName|627}}, the mage [[Ecamane Truesilver]] and his nine apprentices arrived in Silverymoon to found a magic school, saying that five goddesses—Mielikki, Eldath, Lurue, [[Mystra]], and [[Sehanine Moonbow]]—had called them there.<ref>{{Cite book/Cormanthyr: Empire of the Elves|42}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/The Grand History of the Realms|93}}</ref>

Among more than a dozen other deities, Mielikki manifested at the [[Dancing Place]] in [[High Dale]] in inspire the formation of the [[Harpers]] in the {{YearlinkName|720}}.<ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition|132}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book/The Code of the Harpers|21-22}}</ref> Mielikki continued to support the Harpers, by imbuing her power in their forest campsites, refuges, and meeting spots; blessing [[Master Harper]]s with tracking skills;<ref>{{Cite book/The Code of the Harpers|41,86,93}}</ref> and [[Harper priest]]s with a swift step.<ref>{{Cite book/Magic of Faerûn|29-30}}</ref>

The great [[hamadryad]] [[sorceress]] known as the [[Hamagess]] sacrificed herself to protect the High Forest from infestation by [[Abyss]]al vegetation during the rise of [[Hellgate Keep]] in the {{YearlinkName|882}}. Centuries afterward, "the Hamagess" would be adopted by the Mielikkians as an obscure name for the goddess herself.<ref>{{Cite web/Mintiper's Chapbook/Part 3}}</ref>

When the mighty mortal ranger Gwaeron Windstrom hunted and killed a number of manifestations and even an avatar of Malar the Beastlord, one after the other, Mielikki made him a demigod in her service and his church would grow out of hers.<ref name="P&P-p27" /><ref name="SCAG-p29" />

One dark night some time in the 10{{th}} century [[DR]], Mielikki sent a glowing [[spellbook]] to light the way of the legendary ranger [[Yornar|Yornar the Tracker]] while he was lost in the forest and harried by [[bugbear]]s. After he prayed for guidance on what to do with it, Mielikki visited him in his sleep, saying "Keep it with you and give it to one who will serve me. You will know when the time is right to bestow it." He even felt a kiss on his cheek, with a touch of fire yet as cool as spring water. The book came to be called ''[[Yornar's Trail Companion]]''.<ref name="PftF-p117">{{Cite book/Prayers from the Faithful|117}}</ref> Passed from ranger to priest and priest to ranger down through the centuries in a winding path around the Realms, it was thought Mielikki sent the book out to inspire and encourage her faithful and to strengthen the magic of her priests.<ref name="PftF-p118">{{Cite book/Prayers from the Faithful|118}}</ref>

In the 11{{th}} century DR, a priestess of Mielikki that lived in Amphail, [[Asaudrae Daerantree]], was killed by hunters while in the form of a [[stag]], giving rise to the [[Rite of the Stag Lass]]. Many of the faith and some in Amphail would come to believe Asaudrae to be Mielikki herself.<ref name="VGttN-19">{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to the North|19}}</ref><ref name="TN.CC-6">{{Cite book/The North: Guide to the Savage Frontier/Cities & Civilization|6}}</ref><ref name="D274-105">{{Cite dragon/274/The New Adventures of Volo: The Stag Lass|105}}</ref>

In the {{YearlinkName|1246}}, the ranger [[Rhighaermon O'Antlers]] sustained mortal injuries in an effort to recover ''Yornar's Trail Companion'' and bring it to the [[temple of Mielikki (Maerlar)|Mielikkian temple]] in [[Maerlar]] in [[Mulhorand]]. Then Mielikki, in the form of a lady clad in green, emerged from the trees and caused vines to ensnare, choke, and tear apart the pursuing [[doppelganger]]s with no more than her finger. She picked up the faithful Rhighaermon with a smile and disappeared, as the stunned priests fell to their knees and wept and praised her. A message carved on their altar would be found the next morning, as if it had always been there, encouraging the Mielikkians to continue passing the book on.<ref name="PftF-p119">{{Cite book/Prayers from the Faithful|119}}</ref>

After the line of the Green Regents had been broken by the [[Rensha]] lords of the Delimbiyr Vale for more than a century and a half, Mielikki intervened in the {{YearlinkName|1315}}. She took the form of a unicorn being hunted by a band of mercenary hunters hired by [[Pasuuk Rensha]] to seize its horn. After weeks, they tracked and finally tricked it into teleporting into a trap they'd set at a treacherous bend in the Unicorn Run. Or so they thought. The newest member, [[Nanathlor Greysword]], was ordered to inflict the killing strike—but hesitated, his heart breaking as he gazed into her eyes. Nanathlor vowed he would not kill such a magnificent beast and that he would defend it against all that would despoil it. Twelve hunters joined him, and together they mutinied against their captain. Afterward, Mielikki revealed herself as an avatar and made Nanathlor her new Green Regent and those who stood with him the Scions. Furthermore, she asked Nanathlor and his comrades to liberate the Delimbiyr Vale from the rule of the Renshas, which they did in the ensuing [[War of the Returned Regent]].<ref name="WitGR"/>

In the 1350s DR, Mielikki favored [[Florin Falconhand]], setting him quests, appearing to him no less than seven times, and even having her avatar begin a romantic relationship with him.<ref name="HoH-p107" /><ref name="EGPEFR-p154" /> One of these appearances was at the [[Green Door]] inn in [[Essembra]], [[Battledale]], where she also slew three swordsmen who'd killed the women there; Mielikki made them decay like leaves in autumn.<ref>{{Cite book/Volo's Guide to the Dalelands|60}}</ref> Finally, in the {{YearlinkName|1356}}, appearing before the [[Knights of Myth Drannor]] in the [[Myth Drannor|ruins of that city]], she bade him to marry [[Dove Falconhand|Dove Silverhand]], in her words, "because this union—and its issue—are needful". Their child was [[Azalar Falconhand]].<ref name="HoH-p107">{{Cite book/Hall of Heroes|107,111}}</ref><ref name="EGPEFR-p154" /><ref name="STSotL-p114">{{Cite book/Shadowdale: The Scouring of the Land|114-115}}</ref>

When the gods were cast down to the mortal world by [[Ao]] on [[Kythorn 15]] in the {{YearlinkName|1358}}, Mielikki was among the few Gentle Sisters who prayed to the [[overgod]] for guidance on how they should comport themselves. Yet they fell like the others, with Mielikki landing in, and destroying, her own grove by the headwaters of the Unicorn's Run. As she lay alone, stunned, and wracked with burning pain, Ao commanded Mielikki to "Know true mercy, kindness, and humility. Find the [[Tablets of Fate]] that divine hands stole away from [[Helm]]'s guardianship. Hope, and pray, and show lesser beings help and compassion." Not knowing how to find the tablets nor anything beyond her forests, Mielikki sought out the great mortal sage and Harper friend [[Elminster]] of [[Shadowdale]]. On the way, she heard [[Moongentle Lharinn]], a Harper ranger who'd kept vigil and prayed to Mielikki all through the night for guidance, and came to her in the cold misty dawn, appearing quite human. Mielikki told Moongentle all that had happened and told her to spread the word to all "good and gentle creatures" before going on her way.<ref name="SD-p27">{{Cite book/Shadowdale (adventure)|27}}</ref>

During the ensuing [[Time of Troubles]], Mielikki adapted well to avatar form and did not abandon her responsibilities,<ref>{{Cite book/Waterdeep (adventure)|7}}</ref> and since she already resided on the Prime Material plane, she suffered little. However, so many stray gods wandering the world gave her faithful no end of grief.<ref name="FRA-p26" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /> She was also seen in the company of Eldath.<ref name="F&A-p57" /> In one incident, on [[Kythorn 19]], in the [[Misty Forest]], Elminster apparently summoned both Mielikki and Eldath to his side to intercede for three Mielikkian rangers held captive and threatened with sacrifice by the crazed Eldathyn priest [[Ramtharage Druin]]. They declared they were sisters and friends and Ramthar was utterly shocked by meeting the two goddesses and released the rangers. The goddesses embraced one another, shook hands with Elminster, and departed, but not before Mielikki tousled his hair and told him he'd done well.<ref name="CoS-c18">{{Cite book/Cloak of Shadows|251-258|18}}</ref>

In the chaos of the aftermath, Mielikki claimed the portfolio of autumn from the deceased god Myrkul.<ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /> Despite this, Mielikki came to recognize that, with her service to Silvanus, as his power was growing, her own was dwindling, as were her faithful, which was threatening to reduce her to a [[demigod]]dess or less.<ref name="FRCS2e-RTR-p52" /><ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref>{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 2nd edition (revised)/Running the Realms|55}}</ref>

When [[Lander]] was slain by the assassin [[Bhadla]] in the {{YearlinkName|1360}}, he had a vision of the goddess Mielikki, who told the young ranger he had successfully completed his task to aid the Bedine.<ref name="PS-p280-281">{{Cite book/The Parched Sea|280-281|17}}</ref>

In the {{YearlinkName|1367}}, Mielikki appeared in a dream to Jeryth Phaulkon, a noble of Waterdeep, and offered to make her her chosen. Her reasons were more practical than mystical: initially, she wanted her new champion to stop the [[Hoar Fang]] [[gnoll]]s from laying waste to the [[Lurkwood]], and Jeryth happened to be their captive at the time. Imbued with the goddess's powers, Jeryth freed herself and her companions, met the Harper [[Bran Skorlsun]], and defeated the gnolls. Similarly, Mielikki appeared in a dream to [[Briosar Helmsing]], one of her priests, and gave him a quest to meet up with Jeryth.<ref name="CoS-SotC-p9" />

Finally, in the {{yearlinkName|1369}}, Mielikki ordered a complete restructuring of her clergy, including the reintroduction of druids. The action not only arrested the decline of her power, but bolstered it and gave her enough to establish her first divine realm in the Outer Planes.<ref name="F&A-p112" /><ref name="F&A-p113" />

In the early 1370s DR, Mielikki guided the young Azalar Falconhand to become a ranger who would slay the [[Susurrus]] and lead the good [[fey]] back into the [[Living Wood]] of [[Shadowdale]]. With the aid of adventurers, he achieved this in the {{YearlinkName|1375}}.<ref name="STSotL-p114" /> He was also proclaimed Lord of Shadowdale and brought fey back to the area.<ref>{{Cite book/The Grand History of the Realms|158}}</ref>

In the events of the [[Spellplague]] in the {{YearlinkName|1385}}, when a unicorn—possibly an avatar of Mielikki, or at least one of her servants—took [[Catti-brie]] and [[Regis]] away to a private paradise called [[Iruladoon]] she had prepared for them that would remain untouched by the turmoil to follow. This was presumably done as a favor to [[Drizzt Do'Urden]].<ref name="TheGhostKing367-375">{{Cite book/The Ghost King/MMP|367–375}}</ref>
