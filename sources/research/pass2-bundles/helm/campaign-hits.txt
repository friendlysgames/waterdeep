campaign/setting/deities/siamorphe.md:40: - **The quarrel with Tyr (1384 DR, 4e):** Shortly before the Spellplague, Tyr and Siamorphe, both then living in the House of the Triad, quarreled over a battle between Tethyrian and Calishite forces. She left to make her realm with Sune in Brightwater. Tyr sent Helm to Sune to explain his position, and Sune tried to make peace by proposing a marriage between Tyr and Tymora. Tyr agreed and began courting Tymora (*The Grand History of the Realms* p.159, 4e).
