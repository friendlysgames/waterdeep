campaign/structure/arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md:148: **Hall of Moradin.** Three hammer-shaped pillars run the length of this hall. A cracked mosaic depicting a dwarf smith crafting figures from black metal and diamond conceals a secret door — DC 17 Wisdom (Perception) to find, opening automatically for a dwarf or on a DC 17 Strength (Athletics) check for anyone else. A black pudding lurks in a break in the wall roughly halfway down the hall and attacks anyone who investigates the damage.
campaign/structure/arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md:150: **The Secrets Room of Harlsnod Darkshine.** A dust-filled chamber untouched since the Delzoun era. Five copper urns hold the vault architect's personal offerings: gemstones, coin, a *ring of warmth*, and a small silver statuette of a dwarf priest of Moradin. The urns are empty; the story of sealed secrets is a legend with nothing behind it. Harlsnod's actual tomb lies on Level 9 of Undermountain, a thread worth remembering for later.
