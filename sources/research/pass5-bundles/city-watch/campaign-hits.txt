campaign/guides/gm-guide/adventure-summary.md:21: Finding Floon opens on Ches 1 with a missing-persons job that pays in real estate: a deed to a dilapidated tavern in the North Ward. Trollskull Alley expands that into two tendays of renovation work, neighborhood politics, and faction recruiters appearing at the door with offers the party isn't yet equipped to fully refuse or trust. The act has no single villain and no grand stakes, by design. Waterdeep opens district by district as the players earn their place in it: the guilds, the Watch, the neighbors, and the city's wider machinery. The tavern opens to the North Ward on Ches 20; the Twin Parades fill Waterdeep's streets on Ches 21. By then the players know their neighborhood, their neighbors, and at least one faction whose name will matter later. Act I works because the city feels like somewhere worth protecting before anything threatens it.

The published adventure calls the Watch one of the most trusted police forces anywhere, after paladin-patrolled Elturgard, and tells a visitor who keeps the law to expect to be left alone.
campaign/setting/governance-and-guilds/city-watch.md:19: Newcomers confuse the Watch with the City Guard, and the published adventure offers Volo's mnemonic to keep them apart, which runs that the Guard guards the walls while the Watch watches all. The Guard is the city's army, and the Watch walks the streets that the Guard's walls enclose. The Commander of the Watch reports to the Open Lord. No source gives the Watch's total strength, so this page gives none.

campaign/setting/governance-and-guilds/city-watch.md:43: **The City Guard.** The Guard is Waterdeep's army, charged with the walls and gates, the government buildings, the harbor and the city's officials, and it patrols the roads to Amphail, Goldenfields and Daggerford.
