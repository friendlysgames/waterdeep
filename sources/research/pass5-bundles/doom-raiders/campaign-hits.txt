campaign/guides/factions/07-doom-raiders.md:7: The Doom Raiders are the legitimate Black Network in Waterdeep, and the faction is at war with itself. Davil's cell arrived in Waterdeep first. Manshoon's Zhentarim splinter came later as a rival cell, peeled off part of that network, and now works to dismantle Davil's people and everything they built. This internal conflict is the Doom Raiders' defining preoccupation. The Grand Game is secondary.
campaign/guides/factions/07-doom-raiders.md:9: The half-million dragons are another matter. Efforts are underway to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep, and the reforming Zhentarim Council is choosing its membership. That money could buy Davil's cell a seat at that table — genuine continental influence rather than the precarious position of a regional branch operation. Characters who recover the gold and deliver it to the Doom Raiders are buying their patrons into something much larger than one campaign.
campaign/guides/factions/07-doom-raiders.md:11: The Doom Raiders do not tell the party until Mission 4 forces the truth out. **Skeemo Weirdbottle has been a traitor from the start.** The Master of Magic has been feeding intelligence to Manshoon's cell and to House Gralhund since before the party was recruited. Everything the party has done for the faction has been visible to the Splinter. Davil has suspected something for months. Tashlyn confirmed it during his arrest.

The gaps are at upper-floor height (K9/K11 level), requiring a DC 14 Athletics check to climb to the entry point within the 60-second window.
