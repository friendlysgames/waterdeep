The '''Doom Raiders''' were an adventuring party that specialized in cleaning out the lairs of [[lich]]es (known as "[[dooms]]") during the late 15{{th}} century [[DR]]. They eventually joined the reformed [[Zhentarim]] in the late 1480s.<ref name="WDH-40">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|40}}</ref>

==History==
In [[1489 DR]], the Doom Raiders were sent to establish a Zhent foothold in Waterdeep.<ref name="WDH-198-199">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|198-199}}</ref>

In [[1492 DR]],{{WDH-time}} [[Davil Starsong]] and other Zhents were captured by the [[City Watch (Waterdeep)|City Watch]], following the [[Gralhund Villa Bloodbath]].<ref name="WDH-40-41">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist}}</ref>

'''Davil Starsong''' ({{Pronounce|DAH|vil|refs=<ref name="WDH5e-p4">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|4}}</ref>}}) was a [[sun elf]] agent of the [[Zhentarim]], operating within the city of [[Waterdeep]] during the late 15{{th}} century [[DR]]. He was known to keep a room at the [[Yawning Portal]], conducting all his business in the taproom.<ref name="WDH-198-199" /><ref name="WDH5e-p39-40">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|39-40}}</ref>

==Personality==
Davil Starsong was naturally capable of finding profitable business deals and making friends, which was ideal for his position within the Black Network. Being a sun elf, he had a natural aptitude for [[arcane]] [[magic]], making him a fine [[bard]], along with having the patience of someone who might live ten times longer than even his most fortunate friends. Unlike other sun elves, he was not aloof nor pretentious. He always traded with grace and aplomb even when drunk.<ref name="WDH-198-199" />

==Relationships==
Davil was a member of the [[Doom Raiders]] adventuring company. He and his former companions composed the leadership of the Zhentarim of Waterdeep.<ref name="WDH-198-199" />

==Possessions==
He used an elven [[lute]] as a spellcasting focus.<ref name="WDH-198-199" />

==History==
Davil was once an adventurer but together with his adventuring companions he decided to join the Zhentarim.<ref name="WDH-40">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|40}}</ref> In the year [[1489 DR]], they were directed to establish a Zhent foothold in Waterdeep.<ref name="WDH-198-199" />

In [[1492 DR]],{{WDH-time}} Davil was seen riding a carriage with a [[half-orc]] bodyguard, in the [[Dock Ward of Waterdeep|Dock Ward]] of Waterdeep.<ref name="WDH-37">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|37}}</ref> It was said he could put people he favored into contact with other acquaintances from the Zhentarim, who in turn provided profitable loans, sold magic, weapons, mercenaries, and even arranged assassinations.<ref name="WDH-198-199" /> He and other Zhents were captured by the [[City Watch (Waterdeep)|City Watch]] later that year, following the [[Gralhund Villa Bloodbath]].<ref name="WDH-56">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist}}</ref>

The '''Zhentarim''' ({{Pronounce|zz|HENT|ah|rim|refs=<ref name="FRCS1e-CR-p92">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Set/Cyclopedia of the Realms|92}}</ref>}}) also known as the '''Black Network''',<ref name="FRCS3e-p281">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition|281}}</ref> was a mercenary company and greater mercantile organization in [[Faerûn]],<ref name="SCAG-p78">{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|78}}</ref> who, over their 200 years of existence, had a storied history as a cadre of self-serving [[thieves]], spies, [[assassin]]s, and malevolent [[wizard]]s,<ref name="SCAG-p77">{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|77}}</ref> who for a time, were indentured by their leaders to serve dark gods [[Bane]]<ref name="FRCS3e-p282">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition|282}}</ref> and [[Cyric]].<ref name="C&D-p9">{{Cite book/Cloak & Dagger|9}}</ref> Over the decades, the organization experienced several leaps and bounds in terms of successes,<ref name="RoZK:CB-p34" /> but also major misfortune, particularly for their historical strongholds around the [[Moonsea]].<ref name="FRCG-p151">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Guide|151}}</ref> As of [[1489 DR]] the Zhentarim was headquartered in the [[Western Heartlands]], at [[Darkhold|Darkhold Castle]], nestled in the [[Darkhold Vale|valley of the same name]].<ref name="SCAG-p78" />

==Aims & Goals==
As an extension of the will of the dark wizard [[Manshoon]], the Zhentarim was initially formed as a mercenary company to support his rule over the [[Moonsea]] with the aim of spreading that control over all of Faerûn.<ref name="CaD-p32">{{Cite book/Cloak & Dagger|32}}</ref> After he recruited the faithful of Bane, unscrupulous merchants and lesser [[mage]]s to his cause,<ref name="RoZK:CB-p15">{{Cite book/Ruins of Zhentil Keep/Campaign Book|15}

Years (winters)<ref name="GTotR-p22" /> are referred to by names, each name consistent across the Realms. Each kingdom or city-state numbers years differently, usually to measure the reign of a dynasty or the current monarch, or since the founding of the country. The result is a hodgepodge of overlapping numbers that serve to confuse the ordinary person and frustrate the sage. The widespread differing year dates include the following:

==Methods of Reckoning==
===Dalereckoning (DR)===
Dalereckoning is taken from the {{YearlinkName|1}}, when the [[Standing Stone]] was raised by the [[elves]] of [[Cormanthyr]] and the [[human]]s of the [[Dalelands]]. Since this time, humans were permitted by the [[Elven Court]] to settle in the more open regions of the forests. In some texts, primarily those which do not have direct ties to Dalesfolk history, Dalereckoning is called Freeman's Reckoning (FR). The calendar is widely used in [[Faerûn]] but has not spread beyond its shores.<ref name="GTotR-p22" /><ref name="TGHotR-p4" />

===Cormyr Reckoning (CR)===
[[Cormyr]] Reckoning begins at the foundation of [[House Obarskyr]], the dynasty that still rules that land. The 25-year gap between Cormyr Reckoning and Dalereckoning has caused much of the confusion regarding elder days. Timelines and calendars of the period often use DR designators, but place the founding of Cormyr at 1 DR instead of [[26 DR]]. This is understandable, given that the two reckonings are from two nearby parties and spread by a third (the merchants of Sembia), but it causes learned sages to slam their heads violently against their desks trying to figure things out.<ref name="GTotR-p22">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 2nd edition (revised)/A Grand Tour of the Realms|22}}</ref><ref name="TGHotR-p4">{{Cite book/The Grand History of the Realms|4}}</ref>

===Tethyreckoning (TR)===
Tethyreckoning begins with Founding Year, Year 0 TR, in {{Yearlink|-212}}, when [[Darrom Ithal]] of [[Clan Ithal]] retook the city of [[Myratma]] from the [[Calimshan|Calishites]] in the [[Battle of the Purple Marches]] and was crowned king of [[Tethyr]] by the clans. This dating system was rarely used outside of official legal documents in Tethyr.<ref name="LoI1-T-p25">{{Cite book/Lands of Intrigue/Tethyr|25}}</ref>

===Northreckoning (NR)===
Used in the City of [[Waterdeep]], Northreckoning dates from the year [[Ahghairon]] became the first [[Lords of Waterdeep|Lord of Waterdeep]] ([[1032 DR]]). A more archaic system called Waterdeep Years (WY) dates from the supposed first use of Waterdeep as a trading post. This reckoning is now largely abandoned except in ancient texts.<ref name="GTotR-p22" /><ref name="TGHotR-p4" />

===Mulhorand Calendar (MC)===
One of the oldest calendars in use in the Realms, this ancient scheme of record-keeping dates from the founding of [[Skuld]], the City of Shadows, reputedly by a [[Mulhorand]]i god.<ref name="GTotR-p22" /><ref name="TGHotR-p4" />

===Netheril Year Calendar (NY)===
Used by the [[Netherese]], it places 0 NY on {{Yearlink|-3859}}, when several villages on the shore of the [[Narrow Sea]] combined for mutual protection, forming the [[Alliance of Seventon]], which later became [[Netheril]].<ref name="NEoM-p5">{{Cite book/Netheril: Empire of Magic/The Winds of Netheril|5}}</ref> The Netheril Year calendar was created by [[Nether]] to celebrate the union.<ref name="TGHotR-27">{{Cite book/The Grand History of the Realms|27}}</ref>

===Present Reckoning (PR)===
In an attempt to get a handle on the various number systems in use, a new type of reckoning has been implemented with the approval of such worthies as [[Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun]] of Waterdeep and [[Vangerdahast]] of Cormyr and carried abroad by the [[Harpers]]. All year-dates trace back to the [[Time of Troubles]] in the {{YearlinkName|1358}}, as year 0.<ref name="GTotR-p22-23">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 2nd edition (revised)/A Grand Tour of the Realms|22-23}}</ref>

===Amnian Calendar===
The local calendar of [[Amn]] was based on the founding of [[Athkatla]] and the opening of its market on [[Shieldmeet]] in {{YearlinkName|100}}, which served as year 0. However, the Amnian calendar was largely irrelevant and rarely used outside of some official documents, with the vast majority of the population using Dalereckoning instead.<ref name="LoI2-A-p19">{{Cite book/Lands of Intrigue/Amn|19}}</ref>

===Impilturan Calendar===
The old calendar of [[Impiltur]] was the primary reckoning tradition in the so-called [[Demonlands]], including [[Damara]], the [[Great Dale]], Impiltur, [[Narfell]], and [[Vaasa]]. It began with the founding of the kingdom of Impiltur, which in Dalereckoning occurred in the {{YearlinkName|-74}}.<ref name="DunMag.177-p77">{{Cite dungeon/177/Realmslore: Vaasa|77}}</ref><ref name="tGHotR-p58">{{Cite book/The Grand History of the Realms|58}}</ref

The '''Gralhund Villa Bloodbath''' was the name given by [[Waterdeep|Waterdhavian]] newspapers to an attack on [[Gralhund Villa]] by members of the [[Zhentarim]] in the {{YearlinkName|1492}}.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />{{Waterdeep-time}}

==Location==
The attack took place in Gralhund Villa, located between [[Saerdoun Street]] and [[Horn Street]] in the [[North Ward of Waterdeep|North Ward]].<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" /><ref name="WDH5e-map">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist/Map}}</ref>

==History==
===Causes===
House [[Gralhund]] and the Zhentarim maintained a tenuous alliance to achieve their common goal of accessing a treasure of [[Dragon (coin)|dragon]]s, which was accumulated by [[Dagult Neverember]] and left behind in the [[Vault of Dragons]] after he was ousted as the city's [[Open Lord]].<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" /><ref name="WDH5e-p5">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|5}}</ref> The Gralhunds provided a group of Zhents led by [[Urstul Floxin]] with shelter and money in exchange for a share of the treasure.<ref name="WDH5e-p44">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|44}}</ref>

The attack was a result of rising tensions between Urstul and [[Yalah Gralhund]] over which course of action to pursue regarding the ''[[Stone of Golorr]]'', the key to the vault's location. While Urstul, who was in possession of the stone at the time, wished to confer with [[Manshoon]] before handing it over to the Gralhunds, Yalah grew tired of having him as a middle man, and decided to take the stone by force and lock Urstul in his quarters. Her goal was to use the stone directly to pursue her objectives.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

===Battle===
Urstul, still injured by a ''[[fireball]]'' detonated by the rogue [[nimblewright]] under Yalah's control, rallied his allies in the estate to kill the guards and capture either Yalah or [[Orond Gralhund]] in exchange for the stone. During the confrontation, the nimblewright left the premises, carrying the stone with it.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

===Aftermath===
Within minutes of the beginning of hostilities, a [[City Watch (Waterdeep)|City Watch]] patrol arrived at the scene, led by [[Generic City Watch sergeant (Waterdeep)|sergeant]] [[Saeth Cromley]] and [[Barnibus Blastwind]] of the [[Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors]], and accompanied by a detachment of [[Griffon Cavalry]] riders for air support. The patrol thoroughly investigated the site and took witness accounts of all neighbors and bystanders, while also making arrests.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

The attack on Gralhund Villa had several unfavorable repercussions for the Zhentarim. Following the event, there was a crackdown on the organization by the City Watch, who arrested and interrogated any known members, regardless of whether or not they had been involved with the incident. This forced many members, such as [[Istrid Horn]], to hide and lay low for several days. In addition, the unflattering way in which local broadsheets portrayed the event damaged even more the group's already problematic reputation.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

==Combatants==
===Casualties===
Both the Zhentarim forces and the Gralhund household suffered heavy casualties, including three servants who had not been directly involved with the battle.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

==Chronology==
The adventure takes place after the events described in ''[[Hoard of the Dragon Queen]]'', ''[[The Rise of Tiamat]]'', ''[[Princes of the Apocalypse]]'',<ref name="WDH5e-p38-39">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|38-39}}</ref> and ''[[Tomb of Annihilation]]'',<ref name="WDH5e-p5">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|5}}</ref> but does not directly depend on them. It is not set in a particular year, but is assumed to be the [[1492 DR|Year of Three Ships Sailing, 1492 DR]] (see note).
