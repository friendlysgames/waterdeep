House '''Gralhund''' ({{Pronounce|GRAWL|hund|refs=<ref name="CS-WW2e-p15">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Who's Who in Waterdeep|15}}</ref>}}) was a [[Nobility of Waterdeep|noble house]] of [[Waterdeep]] since the [[1260 DR|Year of the Broken Blade, 1260 DR]]. Their primary business interests included contracting [[hiresword]]s and manufacturing [[weapon]]s.<ref name="NHoW-3" /><ref name="W&N-p47">{{Cite book/Waterdeep and the North|47}}</ref>
{{quote
|We see both sides.
|Gralhund family motto<ref name="WDH5e-p213">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|213}}</ref>
}}

==Organization==
The Gralhund family were of [[Tethyrian]] descent<ref name="NHoW-3" /> but included a few [[tieflings]] who claimed they were also of the blood of [[Gargauth]].<ref name="NHoW-8">{{Cite web enhancement/Noble Houses of Waterdeep|9}}</ref> The tieflings were usually sent to a Gralhund estate in [[Yartar]].<ref name="WDH5e-p53">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|53}}</ref>

==Activities==
In the [[1372 DR|Year of Wild Magic, 1372 DR]], the Gralhunds were competing fiercely with [[House Amcathra]] in [[blade]]-making, producing higher-quality swords and selling them at a lower price. Amcathra's reputation as the best in blades was in jeopardy because the Gralhunds were using a new alloy of [[steel]] with traces of [[mithral]] they bartered from [[Thaglar's Foundry]], run by [[Clan Xundorn]]<ref name="CoSW-61">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|61}}</ref> down in [[Skullport]].<ref name="D172-15">{{Cite dragon/172/Seeing the Sights in Skullport|15}}</ref>

In the [[1492 DR|Year of Three Ships Sailing, 1492 DR]],{{WDH-time}} the heads of the Gralhund family decided to ally with the [[Zhentarim]] in the search for the lost gold of [[Dagult Neverember]] under the promise of getting a fair share of the treasure. The Zhents, led by [[Urstul Floxin]], used Gralhund Villa as their headquarters while seeking out the [[Stone of Golorr]].<ref name="WDH5e-p44">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|44}}</ref>

However, not fully trusting Urstul, Yalah also sent one of her personal servants, a [[nimblewright]] with a ''[[necklace of fireballs]]'', to search for the [[gnome]] [[Dalakhar]]. The successive blast and the death of Dalakhar ruined the alliance between the [[Zhent]]s and the Gralhunds.<ref name="WDH5e-p44" />

When Urstul finally took possession of the Stone, he demanded explanations from Yalah about the nimblewright and her profiting from his injuries, and took the Stone.<ref name="WDH5e-p50">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|50}}</ref> This triggered a fight between Gralhund guards and Zhentarim agents that cost many lives and was remembered as the [[Gralhund Villa Bloodbath]].<ref name="WDH5e-p50-57">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|50-57}}</ref>

==Tactics==
[[Yalah Gralhund]] planned to use the intelligence resources of the Black Network in order to unveil some of the [[Masked Lord|Masked Lords of Waterdeep]] and blackmail or bribe her way into membership.<ref name="WDH5e-p220">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|220}}</ref>

==Base of Operations==
The Gralhund family kept a villa in Waterdeep's [[North Ward of Waterdeep|North Ward]] between [[Saerdoun Street]] and [[Horn Street]] off of [[Pony Way]].<ref name="WDH5e-p48">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|48}}</ref><ref name="WDH-map">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist/Map}}</ref> The villa grounds were enclosed by a {{SIAdj|12|foot|long=yes|adj=high}} stone wall with a main gate onto Horn Street. The entire compound was roughly wedge-shaped and was approximately {{SI|23000|sq ft|long=yes}} in total area. The main building was two stories tall and was approximately {{SI|13000|sq ft|long=yes}} total. A {{SIAdj|1580|sq ft|long=yes}}, single-story guard barracks adjoined the manor house, and there was a separate, single-story, {{SIAdj|2240|sq ft|long=yes}} stable that had an entrance onto Saerdoun Street.<ref name="WDH5e-p51-52">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|51-52}}</ref>

'''Orond Gralhund''' ({{Pronounce|oh|ROND|_|GRAWL|hoond|refs=<ref name="WDH5e-p4">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|4}}</ref>}}) was a noble patriarch of [[Gralhund|House Gralhund]] in [[Waterdeep]] in the early 1490s [[DR]].<ref name="WDH5e-p213-214">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|213-214}}</ref>

==Description==
Orond was short with a stocky build, but he was good-looking and well-dressed. He was born with a [[tiefling]]'s tail but it was removed when he was a boy.<ref name="WDH5e-p213-214" />

==Activities==
Orond seemed to spend most of his time watching mercenaries as they trained, paying bills,  and complaining about the cost of doing business in Waterdeep.<ref name="WDH5e-p213-214" />

==History==
When in [[1491 DR]] many members of the [[Lords of Waterdeep]] were killed in a [[Death Masks|nefarious plot]], Orond expected that his wife would fill one of the vacancies but that never happened, despite all the bribes and promises. Orond became deeply angry about this.<ref name="WDH5e-p213-214" />

Orond was approached by some [[Zhentarim]] loyal to [[Manshoon]] and they formed an alliance. The Zhent gained the coin and the resources of the Gralhunds for their operations in the city and in exchange granted to Orond all the information about the Masked Lords they could find.<ref name="WDH5e-p213-214" />

In [[1492 DR]],{{WDH-time}} the Gralhunds were involved in the search for the lost gold of [[Dagult Neverember]] under the promise of getting their fair share of the treasure. The Zhents led by [[Urstul Floxin]] used Gralhund Villa as headquarters in their search for the [[Stone of Golorr]].<ref name="WDH5e-p44">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|44}}</ref>

However, not fully trusting Urstul, Yalah also sent one of her personal servants, a [[nimblewright]] with a ''[[necklace of fireballs]]'', to search for the [[gnome]] [[Dalakhar]]. The successive blast and the death of Dalakhar ruined the alliance between the Zhents and the Gralhund.<ref name="WDH5e-p44" />

When Urstul was finally in possession of the Stone, he asked Yalah to explain about the nimblewright. She took advantage of his injures and took the Stone.<ref name="WDH5e-p50">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|50}}</ref>

When a fight started between the Gralhund guards and the Zhentarim, Orond hid behind a bed in one of the guest rooms, praying for his life.<ref name="WDH5e-p54-55">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|54-55}}</ref>

==Personality==
Orond was very angry with the Lords for their refusal of his wife. He was a little paranoid, always fearing attacks from strangers, but in the presence of Yalah he became quiet and timid. Orond was not smart or intelligent and deep down he knew this. He disliked common people and had a fragile ego.<ref name="WDH5e-p213-214" />

==Relationship==
Orond adored his wife. He trusted his bodyguard [[Hrabbaz]].<ref name="WDH5e-p213-214" />

'''Yalah Gralhund''' ({{Pronounce|YAW|law|_|GRAWL|hoond|refs=<ref name="WDH5e-p4">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|4}}</ref>}}) was a noble of [[Gralhund|House Gralhund]] in [[Waterdeep]] in the early 1490s [[DR]].<ref name="WDH5e-p220" />

==Activities==
Yalah used her time to monitor events in the city, kept a tight leash on her children, and uses her influence to collect secrets from the mouths of nobles, guildmasters, and commoners.<ref name="WDH5e-p220" />

==History==
When in [[1491 DR]] many members of the [[Lords of Waterdeep]] were killed in a [[Death Masks|nefarious plot]], Yalah and Orond expected that she would fill one of the vacancies but this never happened, despite all their bribes and promises.<ref name="WDH5e-p213-214">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|213-214}}</ref>

Orond was approached by some [[Zhentarim]] loyal to [[Manshoon]] and they formed an alliance. The Zhents gained the coin and the resources of the Gralhunds for their operations in the city and in exchange granted to Orond all the information about the Masked Lords they could find.<ref name="WDH5e-p213-214" /> Yalah planned to use the intelligence resources of the Black Network to unveil some of the Masked Lords and blackmail or bribe her way into membership.<ref name="WDH5e-p220" />

In [[1492 DR]],{{WDH-time}} the Gralhunds became involved in the search for the lost gold of [[Dagult Neverember]] under the promise of getting their fair share of the treasure. The Zhents led by [[Urstul Floxin]] used Gralhund Villa as headquarters in their search for the ''[[Stone of Golorr]]''.<ref name="WDH5e-p44">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|44}}</ref>

However, not fully trusting Urstul, Yalah also sent one of her personal servants, a [[nimblewright]] with a ''[[necklace of fireballs]]'', to search for the [[gnome]] [[Dalakhar]]. The successive blast and the death of Dalakhar ruined the alliance between the Zhents and the Gralhunds.<ref name="WDH5e-p44" />

When Urstul was finally in possession of the Stone, he asked Yalah to explain about the nimblewright. She took advantage of his injures and took the ''Stone''.<ref name="WDH5e-p50">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|50}}</ref>

When a fight started between the Gralhund guards and the Zhentarim, Yalah hid in her master bedroom with her bodyguard Hrabbaz. She gave the ''Stone'' to her nimblewright, with orders and a map as to where to take it.<ref name="WDH5e-p55">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|55}}</ref>

==Personality==
Yalah believed that it was only a matter of time before she could ascend among the Lords. From that position, she believed that she would guarantee the prosperity of the Gralhunds for future generations. Yalah had a keen mind and could easily identify friends and foes.<ref name="WDH5e-p220" />

==Relationship==
Yalah knew her husband [[Orond Gralhund|Orond]] adored her. She had the true loyalty of the bodyguard [[Hrabbaz]]. Yalah treated the Zhent master assassin [[Urstul Floxin]] and all the Zhentarim as her underlings.<ref name="WDH5e-p220" /> She had befriended the rogue nimblewright.<ref name="WDH5e-p44" />

==Appendix==
===Behind the Scenes===
The choice of villain in the module determines Yalah Gralhund's motivations: if the villain was [[Jarlaxle]], then she was a secret lover of the [[drow]]; if the villains were the [[Cassalanter]]s, then Yalah was a secret worshiper of [[Asmodeus]]; if the villain was the [[Xanathar]], then she had forged an alliance with the [[beholder]]; if the villain was [[Manshoon]], then she renegotiated her deal with the Zhentarim and reported directly to Manshoon.<ref name="WDH5e-p50">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|50}}</ref>

The '''Gralhund Villa Bloodbath''' was the name given by [[Waterdeep|Waterdhavian]] newspapers to an attack on [[Gralhund Villa]] by members of the [[Zhentarim]] in the {{YearlinkName|1492}}.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />{{Waterdeep-time}}

==Location==
The attack took place in Gralhund Villa, located between [[Saerdoun Street]] and [[Horn Street]] in the [[North Ward of Waterdeep|North Ward]].<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" /><ref name="WDH5e-map">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist/Map}}</ref>

==History==
===Causes===
House [[Gralhund]] and the Zhentarim maintained a tenuous alliance to achieve their common goal of accessing a treasure of [[Dragon (coin)|dragon]]s, which was accumulated by [[Dagult Neverember]] and left behind in the [[Vault of Dragons]] after he was ousted as the city's [[Open Lord]].<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" /><ref name="WDH5e-p5">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|5}}</ref> The Gralhunds provided a group of Zhents led by [[Urstul Floxin]] with shelter and money in exchange for a share of the treasure.<ref name="WDH5e-p44">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|44}}</ref>

The attack was a result of rising tensions between Urstul and [[Yalah Gralhund]] over which course of action to pursue regarding the ''[[Stone of Golorr]]'', the key to the vault's location. While Urstul, who was in possession of the stone at the time, wished to confer with [[Manshoon]] before handing it over to the Gralhunds, Yalah grew tired of having him as a middle man, and decided to take the stone by force and lock Urstul in his quarters. Her goal was to use the stone directly to pursue her objectives.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

===Battle===
Urstul, still injured by a ''[[fireball]]'' detonated by the rogue [[nimblewright]] under Yalah's control, rallied his allies in the estate to kill the guards and capture either Yalah or [[Orond Gralhund]] in exchange for the stone. During the confrontation, the nimblewright left the premises, carrying the stone with it.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

===Aftermath===
Within minutes of the beginning of hostilities, a [[City Watch (Waterdeep)|City Watch]] patrol arrived at the scene, led by [[Generic City Watch sergeant (Waterdeep)|sergeant]] [[Saeth Cromley]] and [[Barnibus Blastwind]] of the [[Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors]], and accompanied by a detachment of [[Griffon Cavalry]] riders for air support. The patrol thoroughly investigated the site and took witness accounts of all neighbors and bystanders, while also making arrests.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

The attack on Gralhund Villa had several unfavorable repercussions for the Zhentarim. Following the event, there was a crackdown on the organization by the City Watch, who arrested and interrogated any known members, regardless of whether or not they had been involved with the incident. This forced many members, such as [[Istrid Horn]], to hide and lay low for several days. In addition, the unflattering way in which local broadsheets portrayed the event damaged even more the group's already problematic reputation.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

==Combatants==
===Casualties===
Both the Zhentarim forces and the Gralhund household suffered heavy casualties, including three servants who had not been directly involved with the battle.<ref name="WDH5e-p52-56" />

==Organization==
During the 14{{th}} century, [[Challas Amcathra]] passed on his title as family patriarch to his eldest son, [[Arilos Amcathra|Arilos]]. His consort and wife, [[Miri Amcathra|Miri]], stepped down during this time and heir was passed to Challas' second son, [[Regnet Amcathra|Regnet]].<ref name="CS-WW2e-p9" />

As of [[1372 DR]], the house had 17 family members.<ref name="CoS.NHoW-p2" />

==Activities==
The Amcathra family were particularly well known for their trade in wine and their enterprises in [[sword]]-forging, [[horse]]-breeding and martial training.<ref name="WatN-p46">{{Cite book/Waterdeep and the North|46}}</ref> Their members were active patrons within the [[Vintners', Distillers', & Brewers' Guild]] as well as the [[Splendid Order of Armorers, Locksmiths, and Finesmiths]].<ref name="CS:W-p61" />

==Base of Operations==
The family owned a villa in the [[Waterdeep/North Ward|North Ward]], between the east side of the [[High Road (Waterdeep)|High Road]] and [[Ilzantil Street]] and between [[Hassantyr's Street]] and [[Tarnath Street]].<ref name="WDH5e-p13" /><ref name="CSW3e-p94,101">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|94,101}}</ref>

They also had holdings in [[Amphail (village)]] and [[Silverymoon]]. <ref name="CoS.NHoW-p2" />

==History==
The family's history dated back to [[Rorgar Amcathra]], who established a horse ranch near [[Rowan Hold]], shortly after the [[Trollwars|First Trollwar]]. They offered their services as horse-masters to the military of Waterdeep, whose cavalry were greatly depleted during the conflict, and rapidly garnered a fair amount of wealth. In [[1248 DR]], [[Hargrym Amcathra|Lord Hargrym]] accepted the city's offer of ennoblement, along with several other mercantile families, which ascended their status to that of Waterdhavian aristocracy.<ref name="CS:W-p61" />

Around [[1372 DR]], [[Gralhund|House Gralhund]] began to undercut the sale of Amcathran blades, offering [[weapon]]s that were forged from [[mithral]]-laced-[[steel]] was provided from the [[duergar]] [[Clan Xundorn]], which was highly superior in form and function. [[Arilos Amcathra|Lord Arilos]] suspected they were using an alloy that was unavailable to other [[smith]]s, and sought to hire adventurers to rout out the driving force behind their recent success or hopefully attain the source for his own family's use.<ref name="CS:W-p61" />

'''Skullport''', also known as the '''Port of Shadows''', was a subterranean city located far below [[Waterdeep]], within the 3{{rd}} level of [[Undermountain]] known as the [[Sargauth Level]].<ref name="FRCS3e-p179">{{Cite book/Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 3rd edition|179}}</ref><ref name="CoS.W-p108">{{Cite book/City of Splendors: Waterdeep|108}}</ref> While rumors of this subterranean domain of villainy persisted throughout the surface world, few of its inhabitants had any knowledge of any points of entry.<ref name="W.DoMM-p303">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage|303}}</ref>

{{Quote|''The Port of Shadow is unlike any port above or below the surface of Abeir-Toril. Not surprisingly, it is much like the Underhalls that surround it: difficult to quantify, enigmatic, dangerous and yet alluring, all at the same time.''|Alphraxis of Deneir, ''[[Skullport (sourcebook)]]'' by [[Joseph Wolf]]<ref name="Sk-p5">{{Cite book/Skullport|5}}</ref>}}

==Structure==
Built on an ancient [[Netherese]] [[Sargauth Enclave|ruin]], within an immense subterranean cavern, the shadowy port was a dark reflection of the ''City of Splendors'' above. Its structure was built in three tiers, interconnected by narrow catwalks and rickety scaffolding. Creaking houses built on stilts were haphazardly built in a dense network that formed a near shantytown.<ref name="W.DoMM-p303"/><ref name="Du200.BS">{{Cite dungeon/200/Backdrop: Skullport}}</ref>

Exact divisions between regions was a matter of debate, but Skullport was divided into four generally accepted wards: [[Skull Island]], [[Skullport/Port|the Port]], [[Skullport/Trade Lanes|the Trade Lanes]], and [[Skullport/Heart|the Heart]].<ref name="SP-p12">{{Cite book/Skullport|12}}</ref> Although no clear vertical boundaries existed, locals divided Skullport into three vertical tiers to aid navigation: the Crown, the Venter and the Dredge.<ref name="Du200.BS"/>

'''Urstul Floxin''' ({{Pronounce|UR|stool|_|FLOX|in|refs=<ref name="WDH5e-p4">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|4}}</ref>}}) was a high-ranking member of the [[Zhentarim]] in [[Waterdeep]] in the early 1490s [[DR]].<ref name="WDH5e-p216" />

==History==
In {{Yearlink|1492}},{{WDH-time}} Urstul Floxin was the leader of the Zhents headquartered in the villa of the [[Gralhund]] family. From there, he directed the search for the ''[[Stone of Golorr]]'', which was in possession of the [[gnome]] [[Dalakhar]].<ref name="WDH5e-p44">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|44}}</ref>

However, not fully trusting Urstul, [[Yalah Gralhund]] also sent one of her personal servants, a [[nimblewright]] with a ''[[necklace of fireballs]]'', to search for the [[rock gnome]]. Floxin and two of his minions managed to ambush Dalakhar but then a ''[[fireball]]'' launched by the nimblewright killed the rock gnome and ten more people, including the two Zhent minions. Despite some injuries, Urstul survived and finally took the ''Stone''.<ref name="WDH5e-p43-44">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|43-44}}</ref>

Then, finally in possession of the ''Stone'', Urstul went to question Yalah about the nimblewright. However, she took advantage of his injures and took the ''Stone'',<ref name="WDH5e-p50">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|50}}</ref> triggering the events of the confrontation later known as the [[Gralhund Villa Bloodbath]].<ref name="WDH5e-p56">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|56}}</ref>

Urstul was confined in the villa's parlor but managed to kill the guards and escaped.<ref name="WDH5e-p53">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|53}}</ref>

Next, he tried to find Lord [[Orond Gralhund]], who was hidden in one of the guest rooms, with a plan to capture and trade him to get back the ''Stone''. There Urstul was possibly confronted and defeated by some adventurers.<ref name="WDH5e-p54">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|54}}</ref>

If he survived, Urstul retreated to [[Kolat Tower]] with his master [[Manshoon]].<ref name="WDH5e-p159">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|159}}</ref>

==Personality==
Urstul was truly loyal to Manshoon, even after being punished.<ref name="WDH5e-p159" /> He was a charming and dangerous individual.<ref name="WDH5e-p216" /> 
 
==Relationship==
Urstul received his orders directly from a [[simulacrum]] of Manshoon. He had a collection of black [[flying snake]]s that he used as couriers.<ref name="WDH5e-p216" />

The '''''Stone of Golorr''''' was an [[artifact]] that contained the consciousness and memories of an [[aboleth]] named [[Golorr]].<ref name="WDH5e-p192" />

==Description==
The artifact was a small and glossy greenish-gray stone that could fit in the palm of a [[humanoid]]'s hand.<ref name="WDH5e-p192" />

==Powers==
The power of the ''Stone of Golorr'' could be called upon to access the aboleth's memory in a similar way as the ''[[legend lore]]'' spell. This power could be used up to three times, until the ''stone'' recharged at the next dawn.<ref name="WDH5e-p192" />

However, knowledge given by the aboleth was fleeting. Once a creature broke the attunement to the ''Stone of Golorr'', there was a chance that any knowledge acquired via the stone, and even possession of the stone itself, was forgotten by the user. This magically induced amnesia could sometimes be removed by a ''[[remove curse]]'' spell, but more powerful spells such as ''[[greater restoration]]'' had a better chance of success.<ref name="WDH5e-p192" />

As the trapped creature inside, the ''Stone of Golorr'' hungered for knowledge and did not like to stay attuned to the same individual for too long. It had an alien personality and saw itself as a [[god]]. If its user ignored the ''stone's'' demands, it could attempt to seize control of the user.<ref name="WDH5e-p192" />

==History==
The ''Stone of Golorr'' was in fact an aboleth that was transformed into an object. It could be freed if the stone was destroyed via an ''[[antipathy/sympathy]]'' spell cast with the appropriate parameters. If Golorr were freed in this way, it could go berserk and attack any nearby creatures.<ref name="WDH5e-p192" />

In the late 15{{th}} century [[DR]], the ''Stone of Golorr'' was used to store the knowledge of the location of the [[Vault of Dragons]], which contained a treasure that had been embezzled by [[Dagult Neverember]] shortly before he was ousted as the [[Open Lord]] of [[Waterdeep]].<ref name="WDH5e-p192" /> At some point the stone fell into the hands of [[Xanathar]]. Then in [[1492 DR]],{{WDH-time}} Lord Dagult ordered many of his agents to obtain the stone from Xanathar's lair, with one [[Dalakhar]] ultimately being the one to do so while the crime lord was in the middle of a meeting with the [[Zhentarim]].<ref name="WDH5e-p5">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|5}}</ref>

The '''North Ward''' was the northeastern sector of [[Waterdeep]].<ref name="WatN-p24" /><ref name="CoS-p92" /> It was famous for being the quietest ward of the city, consisting mostly of residential areas.<ref name="VGtW-p81" />

==Geography==
The North Ward was delimited by the walls of the [[Waterdeep/City of the Dead|City of the Dead]] to the southeast and the [[Trollwall]] to the east and north. Its southern border was at [[Andamaar's Street]] and [[Trader's Way]] up to [[Shield Street]], which marked the western border until [[Vondil Street]]. North of that, the western border was defined by the [[High Road]] the rest of the way to the Trollwall.<ref name="CoS.AG-12">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Adventurer's Guide to the City|12}}</ref>

The architectural style of the North Ward was dominated by rowhouses, which filled the gaps between the villas of lesser [[Nobility of Waterdeep|noble houses]]. The ward also counted with a variety of taverns and shops.<ref name="VGtW-p81" /><ref name="WDH5e-p176">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|176}}</ref>

===Geographical Features===
The most prominent feature of the North Ward was the [[Cliffwatch]], a section of the Waterdeep plateau that was so steep and high that it provided a natural defense, so the area was marked by an interruption of the Trollwall between the [[Endcliff Tower]] and the [[Watchway Tower]] in the City of the Dead. A walkway traversed the entire Cliffwatch, along which some of the best inns and taverns of Waterdeep were located, taking advantage of the beautiful landscape to the east.<ref name="WDH5e-p176" /><ref name="CS-CG2e-p43">{{Cite book/City of Splendors (boxed set)/Campaign Guide|43}}</ref>

==History==
The North Ward was officially included as one of the official wards of Waterdeep in the {{YearlinkName|1276}}, together with the [[Waterdeep/Sea Ward|Sea Ward]].<ref name="GHR3e-p128" /><ref name="CS-CG2e-p31" />

In [[1368 DR]], the noble [[Cassalanter]] family loaned two ten-million [[Golden lion (coin)|golden lions]] with a huge interest rate. The first loan was to [[Widow Silvermane]] who ran a [[lottery]] in the North Ward, and the second one went to the [[Field of Triumph]] Race Track in the [[Waterdeep/Sea Ward|Sea Ward]]. Horn Street was located in the northern section of Waterdeep's [[Waterdeep/North Ward|North Ward]]. The road branched southeast from a junction with [[Tower March]], eventually ending on the [[Trollmarch]] at the eastern outer wall of the city. Horn Street was also intersected by [[Whaelgond Way]], [[Pony Way]], [[Selrin Street]], and [[Lion Street]].<ref name="CS.M09"/><ref name="WDH.M"/><ref name="VGtW.M"/><ref name="IA.W"/>

'''Pony Way''' was a minor avenue of rock and gravel<ref name="CoS-map" /> in the [[Waterdeep/North Ward|North Ward]] of [[Waterdeep]].<ref name="CS-map" /><ref name="WDH-map" />

==Geography==
Pony Way ran from north to south, shooting off south from<ref name="CS-map" /><ref name="WDH-map" /> the corduroy-log paved road<ref name="CoS-map" /> [[Immar Street]], and terminating at<ref name="CS-map" /><ref name="WDH-map" /> a cobblestone paved avenue<ref name="CoS-map" /> by the name of [[Saerdoun Street]].<ref name="CS-map" /><ref name="WDH-map" />

Along the way, Pony Way was intersected from the east by [[Stallion Street]] and from the west by [[Horn Street]].<ref name="CS-map" /><ref name="WDH-map" />
