# Design Notes: The Cell Is Compromised

## A Warning before the Investigation

Mirt has enough timing evidence to justify a safer procedure without having enough to accuse an individual. The review asks the members what they actually shared, giving them a practical choice about future reports instead of making the warning itself a solved mystery.

Orren's legitimate relay work explains why an infiltrator can see material from contacts he has never met. His clerical voice stays ordinary and specific, while his identity remains DM-only until the later investigation supplies evidence. The private brief belongs to Harper members, who can explain the protocol to their companions afterward.

## Mission History Remains Intact

The priority list uses the most relevant recorded incident, with a routine register test available when the party didn't report any earlier site. Tessalar retains his actual fate, and the customs-house branch corroborates the inquiry without changing the established collection routine. An arrested clerk, a double agent and a warned-off survivor can all coexist with Orren's separate leak.

The register copies deliver exactly forty-eight hours after entry, making the consequence inspectable and recoverable before its deadline. The former random leak chance is removed, and private conversations with Mirt stay outside the register. Harper Leak Known records awareness, while Harper Leak Closed requires removing access and securing queued copies in The Sleeping Asset.

## Separate Villain Channels

Rusk receives Orren's reports for Manshoon's Splinter, with later opposition using only the information actually copied. That channel never becomes Nihiloor's information merely because Corene is compromised elsewhere, preserving the distinct methods and agendas of the two factions.

The scene awards no separate Renown or Milestone Points, and leaves the named investigation for the approved next mission. Existing external readers that confuse awareness with closure remain outside this writing scope; the event's outcomes state the intended distinction for later integration.
