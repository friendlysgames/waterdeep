# The Dead Drop

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Exploration and Social Event begins when Mirt's paper bird arrives and ends when the party resolves Tessalar's part in the breached drop. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Bring Fillipa out of Uza's shop while protecting stock during the gazer encounter.
> - Find the copied cipher and warn Mirt about two exposed noble-house contacts.
> - Turn Tessalar, hand him to the Watch or warn him off the operation.
> - Recover or deny the handler's ledger before its fixed destruction deadline.
>
> Briefing and debriefing belong to eligible Harper members, while companions can assist the rescue and investigation without receiving Harper Renown.

> [!gamemaster]**What the Breach Proves**
>
> Tessalar identifies Beldan Rusk as the man who paid him, while the marked ledger supplies two further names and an address. Their true allegiance remains DM-only unless other evidence establishes it. Mirt’s decoding identifies the listed people without revealing their commander or giving Tessalar knowledge of Manshoon.

### The Brief

At noon, the paper bird delivers Mirt's request to the Harper members wherever they are lodging. The note directs them to Felzoun's Folly before entering Uza's locked shop.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A folded paper bird lands beside you and spreads its wings across the table, revealing a note in Mirt's handwriting beneath the creases. The final sentence has been crowded into the margin, with the ink extending almost to the edge of the paper.
>
> > "An old friend named Uza Solizeph runs a bookshop on Sorn Street in the Trades Ward. She's done quiet favors for twenty years. Something's gotten into her shop and she's frightened. Go see what needs doing. She doesn't know what she's been helping us with. Keep it that way."

> [!gamemaster]**The Dispatch**
>
> The outside of the note gives Felzoun's Folly as Uza's temporary address, on the corner of Sorn and Salabar, two streets from the shop. The message makes the rescue the immediate task; Mirt doesn't know that Tessalar has copied the cipher until the members report the evidence below. Uza remains unaware of the Harper dead drop throughout the mission.

### Tea at Felzoun's Folly

Uza is seated near the tavern's front window with a cup of tea, watching everyone who comes through the door for news of her shop. Offering help opens the conversation below, with her keys available without payment or a check.

> [!readaloud]
>
> An older woman sits beside the window with both hands around a cup, looking up whenever the door opens onto the street. A ring of keys lies beside her saucer, along with a list of books whose titles have been crossed out and written again beneath them.
>
> > "If you've come about the shop, please sit down so I can explain what's happened. I don't want anyone mistaking the creature for something harmless, and my cat is still upstairs."

> [!social]**The Bookseller with a Written Account**
>
> Uza Solizeph (Lawful Good, Mulan Human, she/her) :: an elderly bookseller who describes frightening events with the same care she gives her catalogues.
>
> Uza is frightened for Fillipa, but she keeps the keys beside her hand and points to her written notes whenever someone interrupts with a guess about the monster. The conversation below covers the creature's appearance, the three floors and the cat's hiding places. Her rescue request and loan of the keys require no payment or declaration of faction affiliation.

> [!qna]**What is inside?**
>
> Uza measures a circle in the air with her hands before picking up the keys.
>
> > "It's about the size of a human head, with one eye in the middle and smaller eyes on stalks. It knocked over the philosophy section and stayed there staring at the wall before it went upstairs after Fillipa."

> [!qna]**Where is Fillipa?**
>
> > "She's black and white, and she's eleven years old. I heard her in the attic this morning, complaining rather loudly whenever that thing went near her. She's been jumping between the shelves and the beams, but I can't leave her to manage this for another night."

> [!qna]**What do you need from us?**
>
> Uza slides the keys across the table, keeping a finger beside the largest one.
>
> > "This opens the street door, and the others are for the rooms upstairs. Bring Fillipa out alive, please. I can replace some of the books, and I can mend a broken shelf, but I can't replace her."

> [!qna]**How is the shop laid out?**
>
> Uza turns over her list and draws the staircase along the edge of the page.
>
> > "The books and manuscripts are on the ground floor, with my rooms above them. The attic holds the new acquisitions and the binding table. You'll hear Fillipa before you reach her, if she's still complaining as she was this morning."

### Sorn Street Bookshop

The street door is locked, opening with Uza's key, and all interior doors are unlocked. The ground floor contains the damaged philosophy shelves and manuscript cases, the second floor contains Uza's living quarters, and the third floor holds acquisitions and bookbinding tools beneath eight-foot eaves and a twelve-foot ridge.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The key turns with a scrape, opening onto shelves of books and papers scattered across the shop floor beneath an overturned display case. The stairs remain clear at the back, and from somewhere above them comes a prolonged meow followed by the sound of a bundle sliding from a table.

> [!exploration]**The Floors below the Attic**
>
> Moving carefully through the ground floor reaches the intact stairs without a check, and examining shelf seven reveals fresh scratches around one brick behind it. The second-floor bedroom and sitting room have minor damage, with a broken hand-mirror beside the kitchen doorway. The party hears Fillipa and the gazer above while crossing the second-floor landing, giving them time to agree on who will rescue the cat and who will protect the stock.

When the party reaches the attic landing, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Bundles of maps and uncatalogued books fill one side of the attic, while a binding table stands near a shelf leaning toward the stair landing. A black-and-white cat crouches on a beam above it, watching the floating creature turn its smaller eyes toward the doorway as you arrive.
>
> Beside the table, loose books have been packed into an open crate, and a wrapped bundle of manuscripts rests near a jar of binding paste.

> [!hazard]**The Attic Gazer**
>
> One **Gazer**, using the explicit 2024 conversion in the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**, starts ten feet from the workshop table. Fillipa is twenty feet from the landing on a beam ten feet above the floor. The rescue and inventory measures below remain available during combat; the eye rays use saving throws, so a successful save never counts as a missed attack that damages a shelf.
>
> #### Gazer Tactics
> The gazer turns from Fillipa to the intruders as soon as it sees them, using Eye Rays against two different characters when available.
>
> During combat, the gazer:
> - Uses its ordinary randomly selected Eye Rays against visible intruders, keeping their printed saving throws and durations.
> - Hovers within the attic and uses Aggressive to approach a visible opponent without retreating through the roof.
> - Remains in the shop because it has been sent to search for the missing secondary document, which no longer exists.
>
> The encounter ends when the gazer dies or is disabled and the party removes Fillipa from the attic. It won't retreat, but disabling it can achieve the rescue without killing it.
>
> #### Rescuing Fillipa
> Any character who climbs the shelves and makes a successful DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check reaches Fillipa and carries her down; failure uses the action without a fall. Once the gazer is disabled, she comes down after thirty seconds and accepts a crouching character's offered hand.
>
> #### Securing the Stock
> At the end of rounds 1, 2 and 3, respectively, an active gazer damages the unsecured stock listed below. Death or the Incapacitated condition prevents that round's loss, and a missed deadline never shifts its damage to a later category.
> - **Round 1:** unbound books worth 15 GP fall under scattered binding tools and tear. One Utilize action moving their crate ten feet into the storage alcove prevents this loss.
> - **Round 2:** the loose shelf falls across the stair landing, making the stairs Difficult Terrain. One Utilize action bracing it with the workbench's wooden support prevents collapse; two Utilize actions clear it afterward.
> - **Round 3:** the rare manuscripts fall into spilled binding paste and are ruined. One Utilize action moving their bundle ten feet into the storage alcove prevents this loss.
>
> The stock hazard deals no damage to creatures, and characters who reach the workshop unnoticed can secure a threatened item before initiative begins.

If Fillipa is carried out alive, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Fillipa presses her head beneath your hand as you come down the stairs, making a low, sustained noise that follows you through the open door. At Felzoun's Folly, Uza rises before you reach her table and gathers the cat against her chest, leaving the keys beside the untouched tea.
>
> > "There you are, Fillipa. Let me look at you, darling. Thank you for bringing her back; please tell me what happened upstairs while I check whether she's hurt."

If Fillipa dies, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Uza draws a chair beside the table and keeps her hands folded in her lap, looking from the empty doorway to the keys you have brought back.
>
> > "Please sit down and tell me what happened to her. I need to hear it before I go back into the shop."
>
> When you finish, she takes the keys and holds them between her palms.
>
> > "Is the creature still there, or can I go upstairs now? I would like to put her things away myself."

> [!gamemaster]**Uza's Shared Gift**
>
> Uza gives the assisting party a used spellbook once the shop is safe, irrespective of Tessalar's fate and even if Fillipa was hurt or lost. She remains reserved with them after harm to her cat but doesn't retract this expressly shared gift. The binding is loose, and all seven spells remain legible: *Comprehend Languages*, *Detect Magic*, *Identify*, *Shield*, *Invisibility*, *Locate Object* and *Misty Step*. Normal Wizard copying rules apply.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Uza takes a worn spellbook from a bundle behind the counter and opens it carefully, holding the loose binding together while she turns the pages. She leaves it in your hands after checking that the last written sheet is still attached to the cover.
>
> > "A wizard left this with me when he couldn't settle his account. The stitching needs attention, but the writing is sound, and I think you'll have more use for it than I have."

### The Loose Brick

The philosophy shelf contains the original cipher, while the empty space beside it belonged to Mattrim's missing secondary document. Tessalar copied the note before the gazer arrived, leaving physical traces that the party can show Mirt.

> [!exploration]**Behind Shelf Seven**
>
> Any character who examines the damaged philosophy section and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the loose brick. Behind it lies a folded cipher and an empty compartment sized for another paper. A deliberate ten-minute search finds the brick without a check, so the breach remains discoverable after a failed attempt.
>
> > Fine scratches run beside the brick, and the paper behind it bears shallow lines where someone pressed through a second sheet while copying the writing.

> [!exploration]**The Copied Cipher**
>
> Any character who examines the paper and makes a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) check determines that it bears no scrying effect; the marks are copying impressions rather than an eyebeam's residue. The check adds this distinction without gating the evidence of a physical copy, which is visible on inspection. *Comprehend Languages* doesn't decode its secret message.
>
> Mirt reads the cipher when warned, identifying Lysa Fenwick of House Amcathra and Teren Moss of House Rosznar as the exposed contacts. Their work gives them household access without knowledge of infernal pacts or the Cassalanters' private affairs.

> [!gamemaster]**Warning Mirt**
>
> A paper bird or an in-person report to Mirt starts the relocation order immediately. He moves both contacts into protected lodging at 12 Delzorin Street within forty-eight hours of receiving the warning, adding two screened cots in its rear room so the five existing bunks remain available to their occupants. Mark **Harper Contacts Relocated** when both arrive. Turning Tessalar stops future disclosures but doesn't replace this mitigation, and the Splinter retains everything already delivered.
>
> Tessalar works at the Seven Scales Counting House, 18 Sorn Street, and began visiting the shop three months ago. Mattrim lost the secondary document during that same period; it no longer exists, so further searching cannot recover it.

If the members visit Mirt with the cipher, read or paraphrase the following. A paper-bird warning receives the same information in his written reply:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt lays the cipher flat beneath a lamp and traces one of the contact marks with his finger, then begins a separate note for each name. He folds the relocation orders before turning the copied paper back toward you.
>
> > "These marks identify Lysa Fenwick, a laundry clerk at House Amcathra, and Teren Moss, an inventory clerk at House Rosznar. I'll move them before someone uses that copy to find their doors."
>
> Mirt sets the orders beside the lamp and writes an address on a separate sheet for you.
>
> > "Tessalar Maeridge is the name you need next. He's at the Seven Scales Counting House, eighteen Sorn Street, and started visiting Uza three months ago. Mattrim lost the other document then; it no longer exists, so leave that search alone."

### Tessalar Maeridge

Tessalar works at the Trades Ward counting house until 18:00 and then returns to his rented room above it, remaining reachable through the same front door. The copied cipher makes the danger to Uza and the contacts clear to him, giving the party leverage without forcing a fight.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar is checking a column of figures when you enter, with a ruler laid across the page and a stack of delivery slips at his elbow. He looks from the copied marks to your faces, then puts the ruler down and moves his chair back from the desk.
>
> > "I was paid to notice who went to that shelf. I didn't know they'd send something into her shop, and I didn't know there were people's names behind the marks."

> [!social]**The Clerk Who Took the Money**
>
> Tessalar is a frightened young clerk who accepted 50 GP to report unusual visits to the philosophy section, with no committed loyalty to the Splinter. He pushes his account book within reach and keeps his hands on the desk while the party questions him. His instructions, delivered copy and handler's ledger are covered below; he prefers cooperation to arrest and offers no violent resistance to the Watch.

> [!qna]**What did you send?**
>
> Tessalar pulls a blank sheet toward himself and draws the shape of the loose brick.
>
> > "I copied the whole note before the creature arrived, then put the original back where I found it. The copy went into the ledger at the customs house that evening, and someone had collected it by the next morning."

> [!qna]**Where is the ledger?**
>
> > "In the Trades Ward customs house, in the second drawer of the desk marked for rejected cargo accounts. There's a small ink mark inside the cover, and that's how I know I've got the right book. The drawer isn't locked."
>
> > "The man who paid me calls himself Beldan Rusk. He collects the pages after I've left, so I couldn't tell you where he goes afterward."

> [!qna]**Will you help us?**
>
> > "If you'll tell me what to put in the next report, I can do it. I don't want this getting anyone else hurt, and I can't pretend I don't know what they're doing anymore."

> [!exploration]**Turning Tessalar**
>
> Any character who explains the danger and makes a successful DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check secures Tessalar's agreement to stop genuine reports and act as a double agent. Failure leaves him afraid to file another report; the party can still warn him off or ask the Watch to arrest him. If turned, his first false report states that Uza discarded the missing document months ago and the drop has been abandoned, giving the Harpers a window without inventing a new surviving document.

> [!gamemaster]**The Three Resolutions**
>
> - **Turn him:** Tessalar files the agreed false account at the next 18:00 after the confrontation and gives Mirt copies of future instructions. A confrontation after 18:00 schedules filing for the following day. Mirt relocates the old drop immediately, and no future genuine disclosure leaves Tessalar's hands.
> - **Report him to the Watch:** Tessalar is arrested without resistance. The Splinter burns its customs-house ledger twenty-four hours after the arrest, unless the party has already recovered or destroyed it.
> - **Warn him off:** Tessalar stops visiting the shop and filing reports. Mirt relocates the drop, without gaining a double agent; Rusk collects and removes the ledger at 18:00 two days after the party confronts Tessalar, unless it has already been recovered or destroyed. After collection, Rusk burns it at midnight and begins using the replacement east-entrance loose brick below.

If Tessalar agrees to cooperate, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar moves the delivery slips aside and draws a clean sheet across the desk, writing slowly while the ruler holds his account book open.
>
> > "I'll say Uza discarded the missing document months ago and the drop has been abandoned. Read it before I copy it, please. I want to get this right, and I'll bring Mirt anything they send back."
>
> He leaves his name at the bottom and waits while you read, with the money he received still folded in his account book.

If the Watch takes him, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar stands when the Watch officers enter and gives them his account book before putting on his coat, holding a finger against the marked payment entry.
>
> > "The fifty gold is recorded here, beside the date. I took it for watching the shop, and I copied the paper before the creature arrived."
>
> One officer stays beside him while the other turns toward you with a pencil held over the open book.
>
> > "Where were those copied papers delivered? Give me the place and how you learned it, so we can put the account down properly."

If the party warns him off, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar takes the reporting instructions from beneath his desk and puts them in your hands, then closes the drawer where he kept them.
>
> > "I won't go to the shop or file another report. Has Uza gone back yet? I'd like to know she can work there again, even if she doesn't want to see me."
>
> He waits for your answer before drawing the unfinished accounts back toward him.

If the attempt to turn Tessalar fails, read or paraphrase the following before the party chooses another resolution:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Tessalar holds the blank report beneath his ruler without writing, looking at the front door whenever footsteps cross the pavement outside.
>
> > "I can't send them another page. They'll know something's wrong, and I don't know what they'll do when they come looking. Take the instructions if you want, but I can't make myself write it."

### The Customs-House Ledger

The ledger is in an unlocked second drawer during the customs house's public account-review hours, 09:00–18:00. Tessalar can escort the party to the desk as an accounts inquiry if turned; otherwise, a character can request to inspect rejected cargo entries and reach it during the same opening hours without a check. Taking the book openly brings a clerk's request to leave it at the desk, while copying the marked pages in place requires ten minutes and no theft.

When the party asks to inspect the cargo accounts, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> A customs clerk lifts a stack of forms from the rejected-cargo desk and places it on the counter, leaving the drawer clear for your inspection.
>
> > "Rejected accounts are in the second drawer. You can read them here and copy the entries you need, provided the book stays at the desk."

If the party takes the book openly, add:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The clerk puts down the forms and steps around the counter, holding a hand toward the ledger as you lift it from the desk.
>
> > "That book belongs to the accounts office. Leave it here, please; copying an entry doesn't give you permission to carry away the ledger."

> [!exploration]**Reading before Removing**
>
> The marked ledger contains Tessalar's payment and two additional Splinter agent identifiers, visible after ten minutes spent reading or copying its marked pages. Mirt decodes them as Avareen Windrivver and Zorbog Jyarkoth, partners operating from the windowless house on Brindul Alley. Bringing him the book or a copy supplies those names and their address; destroying it unread reveals neither identity.
>
> If Tessalar was turned, he removes the ledger at the party's request before filing the false report. He first leaves a slip in its marked account drawer stating that the customs clerks are checking the book and future collections belong beneath the loose brick beside the east entrance. This is his established reporting channel, and Rusk retrieves the instruction at the next 18:00 after it is deposited. Tessalar places the false report at the brick for that same collection, and Mirt's courier watches from the public cargo yard across the entrance.
>
> If the ledger is removed or destroyed without Tessalar's help, Rusk switches to that east-entrance brick at the next 18:00 after finding the drawer empty, leaving a collection instruction for his surviving informants. After a Watch arrest burns the book, or warn-off collection removes it, he uses the same brick from the following day. The physical ledger outcome therefore determines whether later missions watch its original drawer or the replacement brick.
>
> The party can copy the ledger before its relevant deadline, take it despite the clerk's objection or destroy it and accept that the accounts office reports the loss to the Watch. Copying leaves it in place and earns no denial bonus. Once recovered, Mirt retains the book at his residence; once destroyed or burned, its contents survive only in copies actually made beforehand.
>
> > The ink mark points to two pages of payments beneath ordinary cargo figures, with the same contact symbols repeated beside Tessalar's fifty-gold entry.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt receives the participating members privately at his residence at 20:00 after their final report, or at 09:00 the next morning if their investigation runs later. He records the rescue, Tessalar's actual fate and whether the ledger was read, copied or denied before it could be removed.

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each participating Harper member gains 2 base Renown for clearing the shop and reporting the breach. Add +1 Renown when Tessalar becomes a cooperating double agent and files the agreed false report, and +1 Renown when the party recovers or destroys the ledger before its deadline. Copying alone reveals identities without denying the handler his book or earning this bonus. Destruction earns the denial bonus without inventing identities from unread evidence, and assisting companions receive no Harper Renown.

### Aftermath

Uza helps the party find books and scholars as a recurring Trades Ward contact, without being told that her philosophy section hosted a Harper drop. Mirt keeps the contacts' new lodging private, while allowing the members to confirm that the relocation has been completed.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt checks the two relocation orders against a messenger's receipt, then places them beneath the cipher and the account of Tessalar's interview. He draws the ledger notes closer while leaving Tessalar's name uncovered beside them.
>
> > "Will Tessalar report again, and what will he put in that report? Is the ledger still in the customs house, or have you brought it away or destroyed it? I need its actual whereabouts before we watch another collection."

If the members brought readable marked pages or the ledger, Mirt identifies the additional contacts:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt places the marked pages beside the cipher and checks the repeated symbols against his notes, writing two names beneath the address they share.
>
> > "Avareen Windrivver and Zorbog Jyarkoth. They work together from a windowless house on Brindul Alley. These pages give us their names and that address; I'll keep them with the evidence you've brought."

When the report is complete, Mirt closes the account folder:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "You've given me enough to act on. Uza can get back to her books, and I'll see that the people named in this copy aren't waiting for someone to knock on their doors."

### Concluding the Event

The members' report concludes the mission, with past exposure, future reporting and rescued contacts recorded as separate results rather than a single clean-or-compromised state.

## Overview

The party enters a bookseller's shop to recover her cat and learns that someone has been copying papers hidden behind a philosophy shelf.

## Summary

We helped Uza recover her shop and found that a clerk had copied a Harper cipher before the creature arrived. We traced the report through Tessalar to a customs-house ledger and told Mirt what had reached the handler.
