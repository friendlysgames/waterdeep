# Design Notes: The Doppelganger Auditions

## The Interview as a Mutual Assessment

Bonnie asks who will hold the notes because accepting a client could expose everyone who works with her. The party still judges the five colleagues, but its answers also influence the relationship she will accept. Her delayed disclosure has consequences without making her own protective work disappear.

The crew has six members before Edric is excluded, with Bonnie separate from the five interviews. Kael's lack of interest, Syla's uncertainty and the Scholar's desire for payment remain legitimate positions, rather than substitutes for evidence of betrayal. The Merchant's willingness to keep a useful agreement gives the party another form of trust to discuss.

## Concrete Evidence and Ordinary Monsters

Practical cargo questions, the false work history and the hands examination independently support an accusation against Edric. Bonnie compares a concrete observation with her own payment evidence, so failed checks leave questioning available instead of closing the mystery. Mattrim adds his account after the interviews, preserving the party's opportunity to reach a judgment first.

Edric uses the standard 2024 Doppelganger rather than a boss with added phases. Shape-Shift changes his appearance without changing clothes, hiding him automatically or producing a fresh HP pool. The corrected Strength (Athletics) check retains the original DC 13 while using the skill's normal ability, and the ordinary encounter remains optional throughout.

## The Tail and Shared Intelligence

The Tail occurs after the new report leaves, giving a second investigative opportunity without recalling intelligence already delivered. Shadowing, the bargain and the folded dispatch each reach the fixed Nethpranter Street address. The two-Spies roster is separate from Edric unless interception actually brings all three into one fight, with those branches audited in the mechanics reference.

The household files concern Lysa Fenwick and Teren Moss, the same contacts exposed in the bookshop cipher. Their relocation is recorded once through Harper Contacts Relocated, and the safehouse bonus is earned only once across the two events. The shared source assessment fee remains 50 GP per contributing character, while Harper Renown belongs only to participating members.

## Completion and External Readers

Harper M3 Complete requires Bonnie to exclude Edric, regardless of capture or escape. That condition supplies the absence expected by The Doppelganger Problem without requiring the party to kill or detain him. The outcome's intended external reader is named in the events, while existing companion documents remain outside this writing scope.

Bonnie's three-week discovery and Mattrim's one-week awareness describe different information, rather than contradictory dates for the same revelation. The existing notable-figure pages retain older crew counts and ability wording; the approved mission plan and verified 2024 mechanics govern these events, with those external contradictions recorded for later integration.
