# Wise Owl

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Harper member reaches Renown 25. Mirt explains operational cover, informant requests and an urgent audience channel, with a second persona delivered afterward. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Choose the cover service for a named sensitive operation.
> - Frame a specific request for one embedded informant.
> - Learn the priority-target warning procedure and the audience request route.
>
> All benefits belong to the qualifying member. Remallia's identity remains private until **Remallia Harper Contact Known**, with Mirt relaying earlier requests.

### Hook

At noon the day after this member reaches the threshold, Mirt sends a paper bird asking them to his manor at 20:00. A member away from Waterdeep receives the appointment for the evening after surface return. Several qualifying members can attend together, while companions wait outside the private faction meeting.

### Background

The Wise Owl's support combines local access with relationships the Harpers have maintained over years. Mirt can request specific observations from embedded operatives without promising everything a faction knows, and he can ask Laeral for an urgent appointment rather than granting the member authority to command the Open Lord's time.

Remallia prepares the second persona through her established registry infrastructure. Before the salon reveal, Mirt handles its questions and delivery, preserving the same paperwork without introducing her as a known Harper contact early.

### Cover for an Operation

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt lays an operational request sheet beside the map on his table and leaves space for the date and meeting point. He waits while you look over the three kinds of support listed there.
>
> > "Wise Owl. You can ask us to cover a sensitive operation now, and I'd like to hear what would help before we put people on the street. Tell me where the risk is and when the work begins."

> [!qna]**What kind of cover can you arrange?**
>
> > "One service for one sensitive operation in each quest. Give us three days' notice. We can make ordinary delivery papers, block a public entrance with a broken cart for ten minutes, or put two real witnesses on a cover appointment. Name the place and hour. None of that orders a guard to open a door."

> [!qna]**Do we all receive a service?**
>
> > "Each Wise Owl makes their own request. Helping you doesn't make another member or spend your allowance. We record the service when it is actually delivered for the operation you named."

> [!exploration]**One Cover Service per Quest**
>
> Each member can arrange one of the following for one sensitive operation per quest, with three days' notice. Deliver requests to Remallia if her identity is known, otherwise to Mirt in person. The requested site and time are player choices, with these fixed service forms:
>
> - **Documents:** Vale & Reed Imports supplies an employer's letter, delivery order and matching receipts for the member's persona, naming the chosen premises and delivery hour. These support a courier or buyer approach without forging a Watch warrant or compelling entry.
> - **Distraction:** Harper courier Harl Keen stages a broken-cart blockage outside the requested public entrance at the appointed time, with two hired porters moving spilled crates for ten minutes. It occupies the exterior traffic and anyone assigned to clear it; interior guards retain their own site's response rules rather than automatically abandoning their posts.
> - **Witnesses:** Nella Fen and Orin Dask accept a real counter appointment with the member before the operation and provide a consistent account of the cover business afterward. They confirm only the agreed civilian role and the appointment, declining speculation about what happened elsewhere.
>
> The service is used when delivered for the named operation. Multiple eligible members can request different services, each recording their own quest use; an unused request doesn't become a party-wide entitlement. Use the matching delivery exchange below when the chosen service begins.

If the member chooses documents, Mirt checks the requested premises and hour with them:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Vale and Reed will put the premises and delivery hour on an employer's letter, order and receipts. Carry them as business papers. They aren't a Watch warrant, and they won't make a doorkeeper believe you without conversation."

If the member chooses the distraction, Harl Keen checks the public entrance before he drives the cart into place:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Harl stops beside the loaded cart while the two hired porters settle the crates behind him.
>
> > "This is the entrance you named. I'll block it at the appointed time, and the porters will clear it after ten minutes. If someone comes out to help, that's their decision; I'm not promising anybody leaves a post inside."

If the member chooses witnesses, Nella and Orin meet them for the real counter appointment:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Nella puts the member's order beside her sales ledger and checks the appointment time before she signs.
>
> > "I can say you came here about this order at the hour written down. Ask me about the candle trade if you need to practice, but I won't tell anyone what happened at another address."
>
> Orin notes the same appointment on his map order, leaving the description of the other site blank.
>
> > "I'll confirm the visit and the work we discussed. I didn't follow you afterward, so I won't pretend I saw more."

### The Informants and Warnings

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt places three sealed request sleeves beside the cover sheet, leaving their field names turned inward. He pushes a clean sheet between them for the member’s question.
>
> > "One question they can act on. A shift, a delivery, a person expected at a place. They can't give you every conversation in the building, and I won't make them risk a cover for a request they can't answer."

> [!qna]**Which posting should I use?**
>
> > "Lantern can check Guild warehouse deliveries and shifts on a Dock Ward route. Canvas works near the Faire and can check public staff movements there. Slate sees surface cargo instructions from the other Black Network branch. Choose one whose work touches your question; I won't give you the person behind the name."

> [!qna]**When do I hear back?**
>
> > "Ask one specific question. If the posting can reach it, the answer comes at noon tomorrow. If the request fails, that channel closes to you for twenty days. Another posting remains available."

> [!gamemaster]**Three Protected Postings**
>
> Requests route through Mirt, who knows these operative identities but doesn't give them to the recipient:
> - **Lantern**, Dena Voss: Guild warehouse courier, able to check Dock Ward deliveries and guard changes on the route she works.
> - **Canvas**, Joss Bell: Faire ticket clerk, able to check public staff shifts, crew movements visible from the pier and scheduled appearances. If Mara was recovered earlier, Canvas is the separate posting; Mara isn't silently returned to a compromised job.
> - **Slate**, Darron Quill: Splinter cargo clerk, able to check shipments and instructions reaching the surface counting-house channel, without unrestricted access to Manshoon's sanctum.
>
> For each activation, the member frames one specific request and makes a DC 13 Charisma check. Success returns the answer at noon the following day if it falls within the posting's stated access; a request outside that access returns its limitation and doesn't invent a secret. Failure makes that informant unavailable to this caller for twenty days, preserving the two-tenday source consequence without declaring the asset dead or exposed. After an answered request, another specific request can be attempted through the same procedure; there is no automatic bulk dossier.
>
> If the faction has collapsed or the named premises ceased operating, Mirt returns that fact through the same channel and the operative's observations concern surviving surface members. This doesn't restore a defeated villain organization or supply a new hidden headquarters. Use the corresponding response below rather than inventing replacement premises.

For a successful request within the posting's access, Mirt passes the actual observed answer from the campaign record at noon the following day, written beneath the matching field-name introduction. Keep the observed facts on the page with that introduction; do not replace them with broader faction claims:

> [!readaloud]
>
> **Lantern:** "I checked the Dock Ward route you asked about. The deliveries and guard movements I actually saw are listed below; anything I heard from someone else is marked separately."
>
> **Canvas:** "I watched the public shifts and the pier at the time you named. What I could see is recorded below. I did not go below deck."
>
> **Slate:** "I checked the surface counting-house entries that reach my desk. The shipments and instructions I could read are copied below; I cannot speak for papers kept in private rooms."

On a successful request that exceeds the posting's access, Mirt delivers the informant's own limitation at noon the following day. The three field-name signatures remain separate; use the one addressed by the member:

> [!readaloud]
>
> **Lantern:** "I can check the Dock Ward deliveries and guards on my route. I cannot see a room I do not enter, and I won't name a person I have not watched."
>
> **Canvas:** "I can check the public shifts and crew movements visible from the pier. Private orders below deck are beyond this posting."
>
> **Slate:** "I can check shipments and instructions arriving at the surface counting house. I have no access to the commander's private rooms or papers."

If the Charisma check fails, Mirt gives the member the fixed consequence:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "That route won't answer you now. Count twenty days from today before you try that posting again. The others are still there if your question belongs to them."

If a faction's established premises have closed, Mirt says so when the answer returns:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "That site has closed. I can ask what its remaining surface people have been seen doing, but I won't hand you a new address because the old one is gone."

> [!exploration]**Priority-Target Warning**
>
> When any of the four villain factions designates this member a priority target, Mirt's network detects and delivers a warning within twenty-four hours of that designation. A paper bird gives the faction, the observed threat and the last confirmed location, with unknown plans left unknown. The twenty-four-hour limit includes both detection and delivery; it isn't doubled into two successive days.
>
> The warning belongs to the targeted Wise Owl, who can share it with companions. It doesn't cancel an attack already under way, disclose unobserved preparations or automatically reveal every faction's complete roster.

At the rank meeting, if the member asks what a future priority-target warning can do, Mirt answers:

> [!qna]**Does the warning stop an attack?**
>
> > "No. It reaches you within a day of their decision, with the last place we confirmed a threat. Move or prepare on that evidence; I can't promise every attacker is still where they were seen."

### Laeral's Reply and the Second Persona

At 20:15, Mirt sends a silver raven to the Palace requesting confirmation of the urgent-audience channel. The reply returns at 21:00 with the receipt code **Silver Raven Twenty-Five** and the following procedure; no audience is spent merely by receiving that note.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt opens the window for the silver raven, then returns to the table while it flies toward the Palace. When it comes back, he checks the seal and passes you Laeral’s reply, with the request procedure written beneath your name.
>
> > "She'll hear an urgent matter through this channel. Give me the danger and the deadline when you ask, so her office knows why it needs a place in the day."

Laeral’s sealed note confirms the channel without granting an audience on its own. The member can read its instructions:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Silver Raven Twenty-Five. Bring Mirt the particular danger or decision and its deadline when you need an urgent private audience. My office will receive a complete request through him; this confirmation alone reserves no hour and promises no agreement. — Laeral Silverhand"

> [!qna]**When would I meet her if the matter is urgent?**
>
> > "Once you bring me a specific urgent danger or decision and its deadline, I can arrange ten in the morning, two days later. East reception room at the Palace. Name a companion only if the request explains why they're needed. The appointment gets you heard; it doesn't make the Open Lord agree."

> [!gamemaster]**An Urgent Private Audience**
>
> The member sends Mirt a specific time-bound threat or decision requiring city authority, and he arranges a private appointment at 10:00 two days after receiving that request, in the Palace's east reception room. Laeral's staff names the member on the guest list, admitting companions only if the request names why they are needed. A matter without a particular danger or required decision can wait; the member retains the channel until an urgent request is made. It grants access rather than Laeral's agreement with the proposal.
>
> Remallia's second persona packet reaches the member's lodging exactly two days after the rank meeting. Its fixed default uses the member's chosen given name with the cover surname Dale and the role of purchasing agent for Vale & Reed Imports. A three-year trading history includes quarterly supply receipts, a registry extract and current orders matching the first persona's established contacts. Guild associate Ilen Castor, a licensed cartwright at 6 Shield Street, confirms three years of dealings in person when shown the packet's receipt number.
>
> The member can choose another cover name, but the fixed default completes the paperwork without a GM placeholder. The documents support ordinary commercial scrutiny and provide no automatic disguise, legal immunity or successful social check. If Remallia isn't yet known, the packet comes through Mirt without identifying its preparer.

At the rank meeting, Remallia asks for the member's chosen cover name if her Harper role is known:

> [!qna]**Do you want Dale on the papers?**
>
> > "Dale is ready as the default surname. Would another name sit more comfortably with the work you expect to do? Tell me now, and the same three years of records will support the name you choose."

If Remallia's role is not yet known, Mirt asks that question and sends the answer through his private relay:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "The next papers use Dale unless you choose another surname. Tell me before the packet is made. The trade history and Ilen's receipts will follow your choice; I won't name the person preparing them."

When the second persona is ready, Mirt delivers the packet or Remallia presents it if her Harper role is already known. Use the member's chosen cover surname in the following speech if it differs from Dale:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The new registry extract lies over three years of quarterly supply receipts, with a current order and Ilen Castor's cartwright address beside the employment letter.
>
> > "Your default papers use Dale as the surname and make you a purchasing agent for Vale and Reed Imports. The trading history runs back three years. Ilen Castor at six Shield Street can confirm those dealings when you show him this receipt number."

If Remallia presents the packet, use her answer for its limits:

> [!qna]**Will the papers convince anyone?**
>
> > "They give someone ordinary work to check. Would you show me which order you can explain without looking at the page? A real receipt helps, but I cannot promise what another person will believe."

If Ilen is approached in person with the receipt number, he checks his cartwright ledger. He uses the member's chosen cover surname in place of Dale if needed:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "That number matches the orders I've handled for Dale over three years. I can confirm the cartwright work in my books. I wasn't present for anything you did elsewhere."

### Renown Opportunities

This rank grants no extra Renown, gold or Milestone Points. The benefits recognize this individual member's existing threshold, with operational services and informant failures tracked for that recipient.

### Aftermath

The second identity joins the first rather than replacing its use, while audience access and priority warnings remain tied to the Wise Owl. Sensitive reports still go through Mirt privately until **Harper Leak Closed**, without routing a field request into Orren's register.

### Concluding the Event

The promotion concludes when Mirt hands over Laeral's reply and the member has the cover and informant procedures.
