# The Talking Mare

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social and Exploration Event begins at Mirt's later matinée appointment and ends when the Harper members report what happened to Maxeene. In this Event, the party can:
>
> - Find Maxeene through three independent inquiries in the Dock Ward.
> - Spot Vell's surveillance before exposing the horse, or deal with it after the conversation.
> - Learn the Shesstra Street address and hear an unresolved account of two passengers arguing about the faction war.
> - Relocate Maxeene, shadow Vell or investigate the optional Splinter safehouse.
>
> Mirt's brief and debrief include only Harper members, while their companions can assist every part of the street operation.

> [!gamemaster]**Surveillance and Allegiance**
>
> Vell’s allegiance to Manshoon is DM-only. Maxeene describes passengers and their destination; following Vell establishes her reporting route. Neither observation proves which Black Network branch occupies the house or identifies its leader. Mirt records what the members observed without supplying an affiliation they haven’t established and reported.

### The Brief

Mirt meets the invited members in his Lightsinger Theater box at 13:00, after their recruitment evening has already concluded. The performance begins at 13:15, and he leaves at 13:30, well before the first act ends.

> [!readaloud]
>
> The ushers are still finding seats for the afternoon audience as you enter Mirt's box, where a jug of wine stands beside the railing. Mirt pours a glass for each of you and settles back with his eyes on the stage, turning a folded note between his fingers.
>
> > "There's a draft mare named Maxeene working a dray route in the Dock Ward. She listens to everything and she remembers it. I need to know what she's heard before someone less friendly to us finds her first."
>
> He passes you the note, with two apples wrapped in a cloth beneath it, and points to a word underlined near the bottom.
>
> > "She responds well to courtesy."

> [!social]**The Old Wolf at the Theater**
>
> Mirt (Chaotic Good, Illuskan Human, he/him) :: a Harper contact who keeps his business conversation below the sound of the orchestra.
>
> Mirt refills the members' glasses and keeps the folded note beneath his hand whenever someone passes the box. His answers stay within this assignment, leaving the wider network private; the topics below cover Maxeene's appearance, enchantment and relocation. He wants her account and the members' verified observations, with no promised result required.

> [!qna]**Where do we begin?**
>
> Mirt opens the folded paper and places a finger beside Maxeene's name.
>
> > "She's a grey roan with a star on her forehead. Ask the dray drivers in the Dock Ward. They're more likely to know today's route than I am."

> [!qna]**How does she speak?**
>
> > "A druid did her a favour years ago. She understands Common and speaks it well enough. You don't need a spell to have this conversation."

> [!qna]**What should we ask?**
>
> > "Ask about unusual passengers, where she took them, and anything they said about the Zhents. Let her finish before you decide what's useful."

> [!qna]**Can we move her?**
>
> Mirt hands over a second paper, folded around an order bearing his signature.
>
> > "If she wants out of that route, take her to Hessa Dorn on Candle Lane. Show Hessa this. I've paid for the stabling and settled the dray owner's lost work."

> [!qna]**What do we receive?**
>
> > "You'll have done useful work for us, and I'll make sure that's recorded. Bring me her account and tell me what you did about her safety."

> [!gamemaster]**The Order and the Apples**
>
> The note gives Maxeene's name and description, the Dock Ward dray circuit and the underlined word *apple*. It doesn't reveal her current street, which the party learns through the inquiries below. The second paper authorizes Hessa to accept Maxeene immediately, and Mirt has paid the wagon owner so releasing her requires no purchase or negotiation.

### Asking after Maxeene

The afternoon dray routes bring horses through several busy Dock Ward streets, and each of the following inquiries points directly to Fillet Lane. These routes remain available even when the party has failed a check elsewhere.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Carts move slowly through the Dock Ward traffic, with drivers calling over the wheels as their horses edge past deliveries stacked along the pavement. Outside the wagon yard, two stablehands are brushing a pair of bays while a boy watches the passing wagons from a low wall.

> [!exploration]**The Wagonworks Stable**
>
> The dray drivers recognize the grey roan with a star marking as soon as the party describes her, and they give her afternoon route without a check. One driver recalls how she waits at the proper corners without needing directions, pointing the party toward the merchant wagons on Fillet Lane.
>
> > "You'll want Fillet Lane this afternoon. She's on the merchant's wagon, the grey with the star, and she knows that route better than he does."

> [!social]**The Boy beside the Wagon Yard**
>
> Orvel is a Dock Ward urchin who has spoken with Maxeene before and knows her current route. He asks for 1 SP and one of the apples in exchange for her location, accepting the payment without requiring a check. He will discuss where she is working and whether she enjoys visitors, but he doesn't know about Vell or the Splinter.

> [!qna]**Where is she?**
>
> Orvel looks at the cloth around the apples before pointing along the street.
>
> > "Fillet Lane, pulling a merchant's wagon. I can tell you which way, if you've got a silver and one of those apples. She'll want the other one herself."

> [!qna]**Will she speak to us?**
>
> > "She spoke to me. I asked where she'd been and let her tell me. Don't grab her face or fuss with her mane, because she hates that shit."

> [!exploration]**The Dock Ward Stablehands**
>
> The stablehands have seen many similar horses on the circuit, but asking about her star marking and unusual attentiveness narrows their recollection. Any character who describes Maxeene and makes a successful DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check learns that she is pulling a merchant's wagon on Fillet Lane this afternoon. On a failure, they direct the party to the Wagonworks drivers, who know the route without a check.
>
> > "I remember the star, yes. The merchant took her down Fillet Lane after the midday deliveries, so you should still catch her there."

On a failed check, use this direction instead:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The stablehand runs the brush down the bay's shoulder and looks toward the wagon yard, where a driver is coiling his reins beside the gate.
>
> > "I can't place her from that. Ask the Wagonworks drivers, though. They keep track of the afternoon routes, and they'll know which merchant's got her."

### The Hire-Coach

Maxeene waits with her wagon on Fillet Lane while Vell watches from a coach across the street, hoping a Harper contact will identify the horse. The coach has been parked for forty minutes when the party arrives, and Maxeene remains here until 17:00 unless the party moves her.

> [!readaloud]
>
> A grey roan mare waits beside a merchant's wagon as workers carry bundles through the shop door, her harness clean and the white star clear on her forehead. Across the lane, a hire-coach stands with its curtains partly drawn, while its driver shifts on the seat and watches the loading continue.
>
> The mare turns her head when you come near, following your approach before lowering her nose toward the cloth you are carrying.

> [!exploration]**A Coach without a Fare**
>
> Characters with a Passive Perception score of 14 or higher notice that the coach hasn't moved despite the driver's repeated looks along the lane. Any character who deliberately watches it and makes a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check also notices a passenger tracking the workers and horses through a gap in the curtains.
>
> > A hand draws the curtain back an inch whenever someone approaches the mare, then lets it fall as the workers pass between them.

> [!gamemaster]**When Vell Identifies Maxeene**
>
> Vell confirms the asset when she sees someone hold a conversation with Maxeene or hears the mare speak while the speaker is visible. An ordinary apple offered without conversation doesn't identify a Harper asset, and calling the mare by name alone gives Vell a horse to watch without confirming that she speaks.
>
> The wagon blocks Vell's view of its far side, allowing one character to speak with Maxeene while companions keep the coach occupied with an ordinary request for a fare. No check is needed while the wagon obscures the mare's head and the conversation stays below the lane's traffic. Vell can regain sight by leaving her coach, which the companions observe as it happens; she needs one round to cross the lane before she can confirm the conversation.
>
> If the party approaches openly and speaks while she is watching, mark **Maxeene Identified** immediately. Relocation afterward can still protect Maxeene, but the early-relocation bonus is no longer available.

If Vell leaves the coach to see around the wagon, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The coach door opens, and its passenger steps into the lane with one hand resting on the frame as a cart passes between you. She looks toward the wagon's far side and begins crossing behind the cart, while the driver turns in his seat.
>
> > "Will you be coming back? I've got the coach standing here until you tell me we're done."

> [!social]**The Watcher in the Lane**
>
> Vell is a Manshoon Splinter operative who wants to return with an identification and avoid detention. She keeps the loading wagon in view during a confrontation, moving toward her coach whenever an opening appears. Blocking that route gives the party leverage for the address-for-release exchange below; she doesn't know the reason Maxeene can speak or the wider Harper network.

> [!qna]**Why are you watching us?**
>
> Vell glances toward her coach before turning back to the speaker.
>
> > "I'm waiting for a merchant who said he'd be here after midday. If you're going to keep me standing in the street, tell me what you think I've done."

> [!qna]**Where do you report?**
>
> If the party has blocked her route or detained her, she gives the address in exchange for release.
>
> > "There's a building on Shesstra Street, with a red lantern upstairs and no sign outside. That's where I take my reports. Let me leave, and you can go look at it yourselves."

> [!hazard]**Intercepting Vell**
>
> Vell uses the **Spy** from the 2024 *Monster Manual*, with one ordinary HP pool and the printed Cunning Action. Combat begins only if the party attacks or physically detains her and she refuses release terms; confronting her does not force a fight.
>
> #### Vell's Tactics
> Vell begins beside the coach, looking for an open street exit while using the vehicle for Half Cover against attacks from across the lane.
>
> During combat, Vell:
> - Uses Disengage as her Cunning Action when an opponent is adjacent, then moves toward the nearest open street corner.
> - Uses Dash as her Cunning Action when no opponent is adjacent and a route out of sight remains open.
> - Attacks the conscious character blocking her escape when neither movement option gets her away.
>
> She surrenders once two conscious opponents stand within 5 feet and both street exits are blocked, offering her report address for release. The encounter ends when she escapes, surrenders, is defeated or receives the release she has negotiated; no additional phases or reaction resets apply.

### What Maxeene Remembers

If Vell is captured, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vell sits beside the coach with her hands held where you can see them, watching the workers finish loading across the lane. She turns toward whoever stands nearest when the last bundle passes through the shop door.
>
> > "It's the three-floor building on Shesstra Street with the red lantern upstairs, and there's no sign outside. Who's staying with me when the Watch arrives, and what are you telling them?"

If Vell negotiates her release, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vell backs toward the coach with one hand raised, keeping the open door behind her until you clear her path.
>
> > "You'll find it on Shesstra Street, with the red lantern upstairs and no sign outside. You've got the address we agreed on, so let me get into the coach."
>
> She climbs inside once the way is clear, and the driver pulls away when she closes the door.

If Vell escapes, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Vell turns beyond the last wagon and disappears into the street traffic, while the coach driver stands on his footboard and leans toward the corner.
>
> > "Are you leaving me here? You'll have to settle the fare before I take another passenger."
>
> Maxeene watches the corner for a moment before bringing her attention back to the people standing beside her harness.

Maxeene accepts the apple and speaks when addressed with ordinary professional courtesy, answering questions about her passengers before asking to change her route. Talking down to her or patting her mane makes her stop speaking until the offender apologizes and gives her room.

If someone talks down to Maxeene or pats her mane, read or paraphrase the following before she stops answering:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Maxeene draws her head away from the offered hand and turns until the wagon shaft stands between her shoulder and the speaker.
>
> > "Please keep your hands off my mane. I can hear you perfectly well from there, and I'd rather you spoke to me as you would anyone else you're asking for help."

> [!readaloud]
>
> Maxeene takes the apple from your hand and chews while the workers bring another bundle out to the wagon, turning her head to keep them in view. Once they have gone back inside, she shifts closer to you and lowers her voice beneath the passing wheels.
>
> > "Is that an apple? Thank you, that's very decent of you. I've had rather a long afternoon, and I was beginning to think nobody would ask me anything worth answering."
>
> > "The merchant has been in and out of that shop for an hour, and he keeps promising the next bundle will be the last."

> [!social]**The Mare with a Passenger's Memory**
>
> Maxeene (Unaligned, Draft Horse, she/her) :: a Harper asset who enjoys conversation and expects the same courtesy as anyone else.
>
> Maxeene speaks Common through her permanent Harper enchantment, with no *Speak with Animals* requirement for this conversation. She remembers destinations and passengers' words accurately, though she doesn't know their names unless someone used them in her hearing. She will discuss the pale man's Shesstra Street destination, the sun elf's argument with a half-orc and the Candle Lane stables she would prefer.

> [!qna]**Any unusual passengers?**
>
> Maxeene shifts the harness away from her shoulder before answering.
>
> > "A pale man with a carved wooden wand hired my dray four days ago. Dock Ward to Shesstra Street, Trades Ward. Three floors, no signage. Red lantern in the upper window."
>
> She turns her nose toward the merchant's shop as another worker comes out.
>
> > "He went inside without speaking to anyone at the door, and he never looked back at the wagon. I remember that because most passengers at least check they've brought their bags."
>
> > "I waited while the driver counted the fare, so I had a good look at the window and the lantern hanging just inside it."

> [!qna]**What did the others say?**
>
> > "Two weeks ago, a sun elf and a very large half-orc took my dray from the Dock Ward to the Yawning Portal. They argued most of the way. The elf wanted to keep looking for someone. The half-orc said it was too exposed. The elf said, 'The war doesn't help anyone until we know who's running it.'"
>
> Maxeene flicks her tail as the cart beside her begins to move.
>
> > "I couldn't tell you who they meant. They didn't use a name while I was listening, and nobody thought to explain the argument to the horse."
>
> > "The large one watched the street whenever we slowed down, while the elf kept talking as though they had the whole afternoon to settle it."

> [!qna]**Would you like to move?**
>
> > "I would like to be moved to the stables on Candle Lane. The Dock Ward has felt strange lately."
>
> She looks toward the hire-coach and draws one hoof back from the gutter.
>
> > "The hay there is good, and I won't miss standing beside this shit every afternoon. If you've brought permission from Mirt, I'd be pleased to go once they've taken the harness off."
>
> > "I've worked this route long enough to notice when someone keeps turning up at the same corners, and I'd rather have different passengers for a while."

> [!exploration]**The Unmarked Address**
>
> Maxeene's description gives the Shesstra Street address without a check, and it remains a lead even if the party never sees Vell. Any character who compares the account with the Trades Ward business directory and makes a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check confirms that the three-floor building has no licensed business at that address.
>
> > The directory lists both neighboring premises, but the space between their street numbers has no business name or proprietor beside it.

> [!exploration]**The Coach behind Them**
>
> If the party leaves Fillet Lane without dealing with Vell, she follows in the coach until they reach a street too narrow for it, then continues on foot. Characters with a Passive Perception score of 15 or higher notice the same coach several streets behind them. Any character who watches for pursuit and makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check also detects it, with the same result.
>
> > The coach turns into the street after you have passed the corner, slowing again when you stop beside a shop window.
>
> The party loses Vell by entering the Wagonworks yard through its street gate and leaving through the far passage, which bends behind the stable buildings and remains out of sight from the street. Shown Mirt's order, the gatekeeper admits the party and Maxeene while refusing Vell entry whether she arrives by coach or on foot. This route allows Maxeene to reach Candle Lane unseen. Following Vell instead leads to Shesstra Street, where she arrives twenty minutes after leaving Fillet Lane.

If the party uses the Wagonworks passage, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gatekeeper checks Mirt's signature and swings the yard gate inward, pointing past the stable buildings toward a narrow passage behind them.
>
> > "Take her through the far passage behind the stable buildings, and lead her slowly where it narrows. I'll keep the gate here while you get her through to the other street."

If Vell reaches the gate afterward, add:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The gatekeeper pulls the gate across Vell's path and holds the bar against its frame, leaving her on the street side.
>
> > "This is a private yard, and the people who came through had an order for the mare. You can wait outside with the other fares while they take her through."

### Candle Lane or Shesstra Street

Hessa Dorn accepts Mirt's order at the Candle Lane stables, with the merchant releasing Maxeene as soon as the party shows the signed authorization. Unharnessing takes ten minutes and walking her to Candle Lane takes twenty, during which the party must account for any watcher still following them.

When the party presents the order to the merchant, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> The merchant sets his bundle on the wagon and reads the order beside Maxeene's shoulder, checking the signature before reaching for the harness buckle.
>
> > "Mirt's settled the day's work, then. Give us ten minutes to get this harness off her, and you can lead her wherever the order says."

> [!gamemaster]**Relocation before or after Identification**
>
> If Vell hasn't identified Maxeene and the party leaves without a tail, Hessa changes the mare's route the following morning and her cover remains intact. Mark **Maxeene Relocated** and **Maxeene Cover Preserved**, which meets the early-relocation bonus condition.
>
> If Vell has already identified her, relocating through the Wagonworks passage still removes her from immediate surveillance. Mark **Maxeene Relocated**, retaining **Maxeene Identified**; Mirt suspends her intelligence work for a tenday while Hessa changes the route. She becomes available again afterward, but the Splinter keeps her description.
>
> If Vell follows the move all the way to Candle Lane, she learns the new stable as well. Hessa keeps Maxeene out of dray work for ten days, then sends her to a North Ward circuit with a standing point at the junction of Saerdoun Street and Trollskull Alley. Mirt pays a covered wagon to transport her before dawn on the eleventh day; her reporting resumes on that circuit, while the Splinter retains her description and the Candle Lane address. This move doesn't preserve her cover.
>
> If she remains on Fillet Lane after being identified, the Splinter monitors her route and she ceases reporting within seven days. Mirt withdraws her from dray work when the members tell him, but the opportunity to preserve an unidentified asset has passed.

If Maxeene arrives safely at Candle Lane, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Hessa takes Mirt's paper, checks the signature and opens a stall where fresh hay has been spread beneath a clean water bucket.
>
> > "Bring her in here. Mirt's paid for the stall, and I'll have water ready while you tell me whether anyone followed you."
>
> Maxeene steps inside and lowers her nose to the hay, then turns toward you as Hessa takes the lead rope from your hand.
>
> > "That's much better, thank you. Tell Mirt where you've brought me, and I'll see what the passengers on the new route have to say."

> [!hazard]**The Shesstra Street Safehouse**
>
> The three-floor building has no sign, a red lantern in the upper window and a closed but unlocked front door. Two **Spies** occupy the ground floor, one ten feet behind the door and one at the staircase; the upper floors contain sleeping rooms and an office with a desk. Vell gives her report at the door and departs on her following turn, so she isn't automatically added to the resident roster.
>
> #### The Residents' Tactics
> The residents draw their weapons when intruders force their way past the entrance, keeping the staircase behind them.
>
> During combat, the residents:
> - Attack separate intruders when possible and use Cunning Action to move toward the staircase if pressed.
> - Keep their attention on protecting the upstairs office, pursuing no farther than thirty feet beyond the front door.
> - Surrender when one resident is defeated and two conscious opponents stand within thirty feet of the survivor.
>
> The encounter ends when both residents are defeated or surrendered, or when the party withdraws beyond their pursuit limit. Intercepting Vell before she departs can add her to the same encounter, with the combined and smaller-group cases recorded in the **Harpers Mechanics Reference**. The party can observe the door and upper window, report the address or withdraw without entering; no resident fight is needed to complete the mission.

### Renown Opportunities

Mirt hears the participating Harper members' account at his residence at 20:00 that evening, or at 09:00 the following morning if they are still investigating. Companions wait outside the private debrief, and the members can share their own report with them afterward.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets a plate beside the wine jug and pulls a chair away from the table, leaving room for you to spread out your notes. He leaves the plate untouched while you settle into the chairs and arrange the papers before him.
>
> > "Tell me what she said first, then what you saw yourselves. I need to know which parts I can put someone to work on tonight."

After hearing the account, Mirt sets his glass beside the notes:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Where is she staying now? Did anyone follow you from Fillet Lane, and did they see where you left her?"

> [!gamemaster]**Mission Renown**
>
> Each Harper member who participated and reports the result gains 2 base Renown, including when Maxeene's identity was exposed. Add +1 Renown only for **Maxeene Cover Preserved**, requiring relocation before identification and no surviving tail learning the new stable. Add +1 Renown for killing Vell or retaining her in custody before she delivers her report, or following her to Shesstra Street and reporting that address. Brief detention followed by negotiated release doesn't earn the neutralization bonus. Each bonus applies once; relocating after exposure mitigates harm without earning the cover bonus.

### Aftermath

If the members report Shesstra Street, Mirt adds the address to his investigation of the hostile surveillance, giving **Faction Outposts** a lead supported by the party's own observations. The sun elf and half-orc remain unnamed in Maxeene's account, with no additional identity supplied by this mission.

If they report the address, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt writes Shesstra Street on a fresh sheet and places it beside your notes, running a finger beneath the description of the red lantern.
>
> > "Did you watch the entrance, or did you go inside? Tell me what you saw there. I'll have someone look at what you've brought me and see who else has been using that building."
>
> He records your answer beneath the address before folding the sheet into his pocket.

If Maxeene was left exposed, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets down his glass and takes out the stable order again, adding a short instruction beneath his signature before ringing for a servant. He waits until the paper has been carried from the room, then returns to the account of the coach across Fillet Lane.
>
> > "I'll get her off that route. They've had a look at her now, so we'll have to give her some time before she works for us again."

### Concluding the Event

The members' report ends the assignment, with Maxeene's location and the Splinter's actual knowledge recorded separately so later visits can follow the result.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Maxeene Relocated** — mark when she reaches Candle Lane or Mirt completes the withdrawal requested at the debrief; later Harper contacts read her location and availability.
> - **Maxeene Cover Preserved** — mark only when she reaches Candle Lane before Vell identifies her and without a surviving tail learning the new stable; later Harper contacts can use her new route without suspending her work.
> - **Maxeene Identified** — mark when Vell sees or hears the speaking contact clearly; relocation doesn't remove it, and later Splinter surveillance retains her description.
> - **Vell Neutralized** — mark when Vell is dead or retained in custody before delivering her report; brief detention followed by negotiated release doesn't qualify, and Mirt's outpost preparation reads whether her report can still be delivered.
> - **Shesstra Street Reported** — mark when the members give Mirt the address, whether learned from Maxeene or Vell; **Faction Outposts** reads this as an available Splinter lead.

## Overview

The party looks for a speaking draft mare whose passengers have given her useful stories to tell, while deciding how to keep her safe.

## Summary

We found Maxeene on Fillet Lane and heard about an unmarked Shesstra Street building and two passengers arguing on their way to the Yawning Portal. A woman in a hire-coach was watching for someone to identify her, and we reported what we learned to Mirt.
