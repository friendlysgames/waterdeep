# Harpshadow

> [!gamemaster]**Gamemaster's Summary**
>
> This Social Event occurs when an individual Harper member first reaches Renown 3. Mirt names the rank and gives that character the contact protocols, with each recipient's use tracked separately. In this Event, the member can:
>
> - Ask the first direct question through Mirt's new intelligence channel.
> - Learn the locations and cover names of three ward contacts.
> - Confirm request and refresh limits without exposing the contacts to one another.

### Hook

At noon the day after the member reaches the threshold, Mirt sends a paper bird inviting them to his manor at 20:00. If they are away from Waterdeep, it arrives at their first safe surface lodging after return, scheduling 20:00 the following evening. The private appointment includes qualifying Harper members only; promotions and benefits don't enroll their helping companions.

### Background

Mirt has followed the member's reports and now trusts them with a wider request channel. He receives network recommendations without naming Remallia before **A Friend's House**. The civilian contacts know their own channel and don't receive lists of the other contacts, preserving deniability without requiring the member to invent a cover procedure.

### The Folded Paper

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt sets a folded paper beside your glass and opens it to three addresses written in a careful hand. He turns the sheet toward you, with a short phrase written beneath the last entry.
>
> > "You've earned more access. We call the rank Harpshadow, and it means you can put questions through people who know their wards. Use what they can actually see, and keep the other names off their counters."

> [!social]**Mirt's Practical Promotion**
>
> Mirt uses the questions below to establish what the member needs, then offers food once the instructions are clear.

> [!qna]**Who should I ask?**
>
> > "Nella Fen at sixteen Fillet Lane watches carts and the waterfront. Orin Dask at nine Street of Silks knows premises and cargo routes. Bram Pell at three Shield Street hears about appointments and Watch routines. Each knows their own ward. They don't need each other's names."

> [!qna]**What are you watching now?**
>
> > "Tell me which faction is worrying you, and what you need to learn. Give a contact one question they can answer from where they work. If none of these wards touches it, bring the question back to me."

> [!qna]**How often can I ask?**
>
> > "One question to each contact every ten days, counted from when you use that contact. My answer has its own ten-day count. Another member's question doesn't spend yours."

### The Ward Contacts

> [!exploration]**The Three Civilian Covers**
>
> The paper lists the following contacts, available 09:00–17:00. At the counter, the member asks, "May I leave a receipt for the next delivery?" Each contact takes that member into the rear workroom before discussing intelligence. If the phrase is missing, use the fixed receipt exchange below; Mirt provides a written receipt at 09:00 the next morning without treating the channel as burned.
>
> - **Nella Fen**, chandler at 16 Fillet Lane, Dock Ward: known agents and movements around cart yards and the waterfront.
> - **Orin Dask**, map-seller at 9 Street of Silks, Trades Ward: changes in premises, cargo routes and commercial cover identities.
> - **Bram Pell**, barber at 3 Shield Street, Castle Ward: public political appointments, Watch routines and faction visitors seen locally.
>
> The member can use any one of these approaches, returning to the others on their own separate ten-day schedules:

> [!social]**Nella Fen, Chandler**
>
> Nella (she/her) sets her order slate beside the counter before leading the member into the rear workroom.
>
> > "Leave the receipt with me. If it concerns the carts or the waterfront, tell me which person, time or load you need checked. I’ll send what I can verify tomorrow at noon."

> [!social]**Orin Dask, Map-Seller**
>
> Orin (he/him) rolls a street map closed before he makes room for the member behind the counter.
>
> > "Show me the premises or route you mean. I can compare what has changed on the street, but I cannot tell you what happens behind a door I have never entered."

> [!social]**Bram Pell, Barber**
>
> Bram (he/him) finishes wiping his chair before bringing the member past the waiting customers.
>
> > "Ask about someone whose appointments or Watch visits I might actually see from here. I’ll write down what I witnessed, and mark the rest as talk."

Each contact can answer one specific faction-intelligence request per recipient per ten days, drawing on the established campaign record of that ward. They deliver a sealed written reply to the member's lodging at noon the next day, distinguishing observed facts from rumors. If the member is out, the reply remains sealed at that lodging until their return; it isn't lost or sent through another contact. Requests beyond their access receive that limitation rather than invented private secrets. Each ten-day refresh starts from that recipient's use, and one contact's use doesn't consume another's.

If the member asks about the faction rate at any of the three counters, use the contact’s ordinary sales answer:

> [!qna]**What is the faction rate?**
>
> > "For ordinary goods we stock, you pay nine-tenths of the listed price. I round the whole purchase total up to the nearest copper piece. That doesn’t cover magic, private records or work with no listed price."

At these three suppliers, the member pays 90% of the published 2024 price for ordinary mundane stock, rounding the final purchase total up to the nearest CP. It applies only to stock with a published price that the supplier actually sells, excluding magic items, unpriced grooming services and commissioned secret documents. It is the source's faction-rate benefit made concrete, without treating every shop in Waterdeep as a Harper supplier.

If the member has forgotten the opening phrase, the contact keeps the counter conversation ordinary:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Bring Mirt’s written receipt when you come back. I can take an ordinary order today, but we’ll speak about the other matter after I see it."

At 09:00 the next morning, Mirt gives the member the receipt:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "Take this back to the same counter. You haven't lost the contact or spent a question. Keep it with the paper until you remember the phrase."

### The First Question

Mirt answers one direct question about faction activity or Waterdeep politics during the meeting, using confirmed current campaign facts and stating when the network hasn't established the answer. That answer counts as this member's first use of his separate one-question-per-ten-days channel; the three ward contacts remain unused.

> [!readaloud]
>
> Mirt repeats your question, then checks the reports beside him for a confirmed answer. He writes the date beside the reply and returns the folded contact paper to you.
>
> > "I’ll give you what has been confirmed, and say which part is still rumor. If another report changes the answer, I’ll tell you what changed. Keep those two kinds of information separate."

If **Shesstra Street Reported** is marked and the member asks what their earlier inquiry established, Mirt answers from the party's report:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "You reported a three-floor house on Shesstra Street with a red lantern upstairs. Your account gave me a place to watch. We still need evidence of who directed the work inside."

If **Handler Ledger Read** is marked, the members brought the marked pages or a copy to Mirt, and this member asks what the book established, use his answer:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "The marked pages record Tessalar's payment and two additional contacts. The names and address are on the copy you brought me; nothing there establishes who directed them."

If the member asks who leads the hostile Black Network branch before that identity has been verified and shared, Mirt answers without making a guess into a fact:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "The Black Network has split. I don't have proof of who leads the other branch. If you find a name attached to orders, bring me the evidence; I won't send you after a guess."

If the requested fact is beyond all confirmed reports, use this answer for that question:

> [!readaloud]
>
> > "I haven't established that. I'll keep the question with the reports and tell you if someone brings me evidence. You have my answer for today, even if it isn't the one you hoped for."

### Renown Opportunities

The promotion grants no extra Renown, gold or Milestone Points. It recognizes this character's existing Renown and opens the listed member benefits.

### Aftermath

The member can share received intelligence with companions, while the contacts' recognition, requests and discount remain individual benefits. Mirt remains the route for sensitive reports if **Harper Leak Known** is marked without **Harper Leak Closed**; the contact network doesn't itself resolve that compromise.

### Concluding the Event

The promotion concludes when the member receives the paper and understands the request procedure.

> [!gamemaster]**Event Outcomes**
>
> - **Harpshadow Reached** — mark with this recipient's name when Mirt delivers the protocols; **Brightcandle** reads their prior rank, with contact and Mirt refresh dates tracked individually.

> [!gamemaster]**Next Steps**
>
> All three contacts are available immediately, and Mirt's next direct question becomes available ten days after this meeting. **Brightcandle** occurs when this member reaches Renown 10. No Milestone Points are awarded.

## Overview

Mirt gives a newly trusted member access to three ward contacts and answers a direct question through his own network.

## Summary

Mirt named the qualifying character a Harpshadow and provided the three contact protocols. We recorded that member's separate request dates and the answer received through Mirt.
