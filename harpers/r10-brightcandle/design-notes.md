# Design Notes: Brightcandle

## Source Benefits with Concrete Procedure

Appendix B's Harper table grants the potion or low-level scroll and Spy backup, while the campaign guide adds persona infrastructure. The event preserves one requisition per mission and one operative per quest for each member, giving the supplier, phrases and response times instead of leaving those details for preparation.

Agents count as allies when present in combat, using the standard 2024 Spy and tier-adjusted Power. Multiple callers retain their own allowances, so the party's membership split doesn't silently reduce or multiply one character's entitlement.

## Persona and the Salon Gate

The fixed civilian employer, receipts and vouching contacts produce an immediately playable default. The player can choose the background details and cover name, while no unanswered GM decision is needed to complete the packet. Documents support conversation rather than granting magical anonymity or automatic success.

Before Remallia Harper Contact Known, Mirt gathers the answers and delivers the mentor's work. Afterward Remallia can ask directly in her unhurried voice, preserving the salon as the first faction-identity disclosure. The finished persona retains the same three-day schedule on either branch.
