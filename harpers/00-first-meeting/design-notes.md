# Design Notes: Harpers First Meeting

## An Individual Invitation

Renaer's recommendation and the Good-aligned invitation trigger remain the route into this meeting, while tickets and box seats follow the actual recruitment candidates. This replaces the restored draft's two tickets and the source appendix's whole-party tickets with the approved individual-membership design. A party can include several Harper candidates alongside characters who have joined other factions, and each candidate answers Mirt for themselves.

The invitation trigger creates no further alignment test at the theater door, and ordinary companions can attend the public performance without entering the private recruitment conversation. Renown, rank and member benefits attach to the characters who accept; recording their names with **Harpers Joined** preserves the existing outcome while supplying later missions with an exact membership record.

## Time to Get Dressed

The noon invitation gives candidates time to arrange clothes before the evening, and the recovery route states every part of the journey. A 5:30 p.m. departure from the theater allows 30 minutes to the tailor, a 45-minute fitting and 30 minutes back, arriving at the 7:15 p.m. intermission. Candidates already at the shop can start at 6 p.m.; later arrivals receive a concrete appointment for the following evening.

Intermission arrivals receive the same conversation and offer, with Mirt assessing their questions rather than treating a missed first act as evidence he witnessed. Neither clothing trouble nor the recovery appointment introduces a permanent exclusion from recruitment.

## Shelter Without a Reveal

The lodging at 12 Delzorin Street, its five bunks and its key procedure are approved original details that make the Watcher benefit usable immediately. Remi maintains it through intermediaries, but new members need only their key and address card to enter and rest. The rented lodging is separate from her villa and preserves her Harper reveal for **A Friend's House**, Mission 4.

## A Meeting Before Work

The evening introduces Mirt and gives candidates room to ask about the Harpers, with the play supplying a conversation about political judgment. The **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** invitation examination and **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** assessment observation preserve the restored mechanics, including the concealed assessment criteria. Mirt's private offices remain GM information, and the meeting provides no Grand Game briefing or assignment.

The event retains **Harpers Joined**, individual Renown 1 and Watcher benefits, with **The Talking Mare** available at level 2. It adds neither a reward outside the approved plan nor a Milestone Point. Encounter balance is not applicable because the meeting contains no combat.

## The Performance and Early Conversation

The timed stage scenes are original material for this Lightsinger production, with council orders, missing stores and broken promises making the approved political-will theme visible. They aren't presented as source-book history or clues to the Grand Game. The source's Giant-language opera remains intact, while a Common libretto at every box seat makes the actor lines accessible without a language gate or check.

Four beats occupy the first half at 6:30, 6:42, 6:55 and 7:10 p.m., followed by the accepted 7:15 intermission. Two optional later scenes give candidates something to watch after Mirt's 7:35 departure, with final bows ending at 8:30. Staying grants no added reward, and arrivals during the interval aren't credited with seeing earlier scenes.

Mirt's early hospitality and questions have actual spoken answers for wine, food, the theater, the libretto, Renaer and the invitation. His public manner allows profanity and expansive conversation, while pointed work questions receive a safe deferral. Recruitment begins at intermission, with his private offices, assessment criteria and the other Harpers still undisclosed.
