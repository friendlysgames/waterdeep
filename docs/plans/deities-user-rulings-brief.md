Standing rule: canon lore is canon unless the campaign changed it for a stated design reason. Edit only what a ruling names. Ember voice. After each edit, update the Draft Note that raised the question so it reads "Settled" and states what the page now does.

1. **Avi's pronouns.** Dragon Heist writes Avi as "he" (adventure-wdh.json:3406, :3411). No design reason exists for "she". Change Avi to "he" everywhere ("wife" becomes "husband"; keep Embric and Avi as a married couple unless a file says otherwise). Fix eldath.md note.
2. **Amath.** Spelling is "Sercent" (published) everywhere in campaign/ and CLAUDE.md-independent docs, including the pages that say "Seccent". Keep her ethnicity explicit as the published adventure has it, a Mulan human, written the way other NPC pages write ethnicity (check how other pages write e.g. Illuskan or Tethyrian human).
3. **Corellon.** Keep "he" with the Form note. Mark the note Settled.
4. **Ghaunadaur.** "It". Mark the note Settled.
5. **Azuth.** Keep "lesser deity". Mark Settled.
6. **Istishia.** Plane: Elemental Plane of Water; the 4e Cresting Spires realm stays as tagged history.
7. **Auril's retreat.** Both locations are canon and they are the same event: she retreated to the Sea of Moving Ice, the frozen sea beside Icewind Dale, in the late 1480s DR, forced back by Umberlee, Talos and Malar. Make talos.md, umberlee.md, auril.md and malar.md say this and settle their notes.

12. **Moradin.** EGW is Exandria and has nothing to do with Faerûn. Keep it excluded and settle the notes for plane and holy day.
