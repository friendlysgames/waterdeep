### M1 The Talking Mare (`m01-the-talking-mare/`)

- **Gate and level:** "Available from the start of Act I… Harpers recruitment hook in Arc B" (overview:5–6). The level is 2nd–3rd (:9).
- **Hook:** Mirt delivers matinée tickets to Trollskull Manor. He briefs in a Lightsinger Theater box and leaves before the first act ends (ev-01:9, :89–97).
- **Act 1:** there are three independent routes to Maxeene.
  - Wagonworks Stable dray driver (a grey roan with a star).
  - Orvel the urchin: 1 sp plus an apple, with no check.
  - Stablehands: DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion).
  - All three point to Fillet Lane.
- **Act 2:** Vell (Spy) sits in a hire-coach and has been watching two days. She is spotted by Passive Perception 14, and she moves in once the PCs approach Maxeene.
- **Act 3:** Maxeene talks only if given the apple and treated as a professional.
  - Three threads:
    - A pale man with a carved wooden wand, taken to a three-floor building on Shesstra Street, Trades Ward, with a red lantern.
    - A sun elf and a half-orc quarrelling two weeks ago, with the line "The war doesn't help anyone until we know who's running it."
    - Her request to move to the Candle Lane stables.
  - DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) shows the building matches no licensed business.
- **Resolution branches:**
  - Relocate her.
  - Leave her in place: she is unusable within a week.
  - Vell tails the party: Passive Perception 15 spots her.
- **Renown:** only two conditional "+1" lines.
  - Maxeene relocated before Vell identifies her.
  - Vell neutralised, or followed to Shesstra Street with the address reported.
- **Hand-offs:** the Shesstra Street address is an Outposts seed, and M2 unlocks at Renown 3 and 3rd level.
- **Stat blocks:** Vell and three safehouse agents are "Spy". Nothing else.
- **Thin spots:** there is no encounter and no combat block. The Shesstra Street "three agents" appear only in the overview (:22). Maxeene's stat block is not given.

### M2 The Dead Drop (`m02-the-dead-drop/`)

- **Gate:** Renown 3 and 3rd level, after M1.
- **Hook:** a paper bird. Mirt's note says Uza "doesn't know what she's been helping us with".
- **Acts:**
  - **Act 1:** Uza Solizeph waits at Felzoun's Folly and hands over her keys. The shop has three floors. The gazer is in the attic hunting Fillipa.
    - Stray-ray rule: roll a d6 and on a 1–2 the ray hits stock, in three escalating categories: books worth 15 gp, a shelf collapsing into the stairwell, then rare manuscripts.
  - **Act 2:**
    - DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) finds the loose brick behind shelf seven.
    - DC 14 Arcana detects scry-residue. The cipher note names two Harper assets "in noble houses".
    - Mirt names Tessalar.
  - **Act 3:** Tessalar Maeridge is a 23-year-old clerk who took 50 gp from a Splinter handler.
    - DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) turns him.
    - The handler's ledger sits in the customs house, second drawer.
    - Three resolutions:
      - Turn him.
      - Report him to the Watch.
      - Warn Mirt and relocate the drop.
- **Reward:** a used spellbook with four 1st-level and three 2nd-level spells, whatever happens to Tessalar.
- **Renown:**
  - +1 if Tessalar is turned.
  - +1 if the ledger is recovered or destroyed before the Splinter burns it.
- **Downstream:** if the cipher reached the Splinter and Tessalar is not turned, "Mirt loses a Cassalanter social contact" (ev-01:77). M3 unlocks at Renown 5 and 4th level.
- **Stat blocks:** "Gazer stat block" only.
- **Gaps:**
  - Nothing says what "turn" mechanically gives.
  - Nothing gives the ledger's location rules or a deadline.
  - "Mirt's Cassalanter social contacts" contradicts the secrecy rule's spirit (see §7).

### M3 The Doppelganger Auditions (`m03-…/overview, ev-01, ev-02-the-tail, design-notes`)

- **Gate:** Renown 5 and 4th level, after M2.
- **Hook:** Mirt finds the party in person at the Yawning Portal, with no paper bird.
- **Interviews:**
  - Interviews run across two evenings.
  - Evening one: Kael (DC 12 Insight, uninterested, leaves after 20 minutes) and Syla (DC 13 Insight, undecided).
  - Evening two: Edric Tanner, the Scholar (DC 12 Insight, would monetise membership) and the Merchant (no agenda).
- **Spotting Edric:** four routes.
  - Asking for Harper knowledge.
  - Asking about his Waterdeep.
  - DC 16 Insight (preloaded answers).
  - DC 13 Investigation on his hands (no teamster calluses).
- **Bonnie:** DC 15 Insight shows she is evaluating the PCs. If pressed with evidence she says "Tell me what you noticed. In order."
- **Mattrim's reveal:** it comes after both evenings. One of the five reported the party's Harper affiliation, "Bonnie knows. She's known for about a week."
- **Report to Mirt (three branches):**
  - Edric identified: the other four are recruited provisionally and Bonnie gets a neutrality pact.
  - Bonnie's prior knowledge disclosed: a DC 13 Persuasion check, or the direct case, wins her a full operative role. She sets terms: her crew's identities stay protected, intelligence routes through the PCs, and she keeps discretion over which jobs she takes.
  - Edric not identified: all five are recruited provisionally and The Tail fires.
- **The Tail (ev-02):** it opens the morning after the second evening.
  - Spotting Edric: DC 15 Passive Perception or DC 14 active.
  - If confronted, he shifts to a new face. "DC 13 Dexterity (Athletics)" is a mis-pairing (Athletics is Strength).
  - Searching him: DC 14 Investigation finds the address.
  - The safehouse is a Trades Ward building with two Spies on the ground floor and files on the top floor. DC 12 Investigation finds all three contents:
    - Surveillance notes on Mattrim.
    - A partial list of two Harper assets in noble houses. This is the same list the M2 cipher chases.
    - Correspondence naming a handler.
  - If the safehouse is not found, "the Kolat Towers raid in Arc I has reinforced defenses tuned specifically to the PCs' known capabilities" (ev-02:58). This is both an R1 leak and an invented consequence.
- **Renown:** +1 Edric identified before damage. +1 Bonnie recruited as a full operative. +1 safehouse investigated and reported.
- **Gaps:**
  - No 50 gp assessment fee. This is a source reward both WDH and Appendix C give.
  - Edric has no stat block. "Doppelganger" is not named anywhere in the restored encounter text.
  - "Edric … adopts a new face" has no mechanics.
  - The crew count is wrong against the sources (§7).
- **Hand-offs:**
  - "Harper M3 Complete: True / False" is read by Emerald Enclave M3, which is wired.
  - M4 unlocks at Renown 8 and 5th level.
