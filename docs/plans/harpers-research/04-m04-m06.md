## 6. Mission depth

### M4
- **Strong:** the salon has many independent decision points. These include guests to talk to, which evidence lines to combine, the BD sidebar, three branches on Jarlaxle, and a wrong-accusation branch. Appendix C's DC 24 ladder is a single roll; both versions rework it.
- **Thin in the restored draft:**
  - The tail is three single checks with no failure branch.
  - No clock, so nothing in the evening forces choices.
  - No base renown or gold.
- **Could deepen** (draws on source beats): the probe-based DC reduction from Appendix C, an evening timeline (PREV used 19:00–22:00 with a 21:30 departure), and a consequence for failed social rolls.

### M5
- **Strong:** three independent leads, layered detection (behaviour, physical, Detect Thoughts), three resolutions.
- **Thin in the restored draft:**
  - It is one event.
  - Extraction is a single save; kill and double-agent are resolved by fiat.
  - "Devourer knows the spell is cast" has no consequence.
  - No time pressure or failure branch.
  - The double agent the guide and s01 require is missing.
- **Could deepen:** Thorvin Twinbeard (a Harper asset in the lair, arc-f :31) as an intel route, the Force Grey M5 template of three independent investigation paths, and a defined cost to the 2024 devourer rule.

### M6
- **Strong:** negotiation (ask or refuse), a forced raid, the study.
- **Thin in the restored draft:**
  - The negotiation has no rolls.
  - The raid has no tactics, no stat numbers and no Stone-loss branch.
  - "Contingency" is invalid.
  - The refusal branch gives nothing.
- **Could deepen:** the PREV Stone-loss and recovery tracking, sending-stone use, and tying the study to the EE Illuun surges.
