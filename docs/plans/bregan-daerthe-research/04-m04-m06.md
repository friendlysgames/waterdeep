**A. M6 contradicts the Eye #3 canon.**
- The restored M6 has Eye #3 sunk in a harbor wreck three weeks ago (m06 ev:18). Xanathar's divers hunt it (m06 ev:24).
- Repo canon is that Jarlaxle holds Eye #3 on the *Scarlet Marpenoth* and has done so since before the campaign.
  - `campaign/guides/gm-guide/running-the-villains.md:25` says "held by Jarlaxle Baenre, aboard the Scarlet Marpenoth. Jarlaxle acquired it before the campaign begins."
  - Guide 08:9 says the Eye is "mounted beneath the Eyecatcher's keel".
  - arc-h:11, :57, :99 and :249 put it in the U4 pedestal compartment.
  - s04 ev:38 says "the Eye is still aboard the Scarlet Marpenoth".
  - `running-the-villains.md:38` says Xanathar "does not know the Eyes' significance". M6 has his Guild locate and race for an Eye.
- Guide 08:71 describes a different M6: "Xanathar Guild divers have located the Scarlet Marpenoth submarine moored under the Eyecatcher and are staging to place a limpet charge on the hull… remove the charge before dawn detonation."
  - The limpet text appears only in guide 08:71. Nothing else in `campaign/` or `sources/` mentions it.
  - The wreck version was drafted from scratch in Session 26 (`session 26 handoff.md:43`: the source "had only a Hook and two lines of Background"). Session 18 had added the title "The Dive" to arc-j.
- Alexandrian PDF 9 (p.68) says the opposite of the repo canon: "Jarlaxle does not control an Eye." He may get one later, by stealing it from the PCs or beating them to Xanathar's Lair. PDF 8 (p.62) says the Eyes are held by the Cassalanters, Manshoon and Xanathar. SOURCE_GUIDE:285 assigns Eye #3 to Jarlaxle.

**Alexandrian Appendix C, Arc BD-4 "The Compromised Spy" (`sources/Appendix_C_-_Player_Faction_Missions.md:1593–1646`)**

- Route: a sewer passage under the Dock Ward with red-eye guildsigns, three DC 13 Perception checks, two giant rats (DC 12 Stealth), surfacing at area X1 (App C:1618–1620).
