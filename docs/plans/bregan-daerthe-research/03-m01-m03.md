## 0. Cross-cutting findings

These affect all four folders.

1. **Source shape.** WDH (3596–3635) and AppC have only four BD missions, with no M2b, M5 or M6. The drafts follow the guide and org-page mission delivery (Org page `/home/user/waterdeep/campaign/setting/organizations/07-bregan-daerthe.md:8`). AppC and WDH say J.B. Nevercott briefs M1, M2 and M4, and M3 has no briefing at all. The drafts have Krebbyg brief M1 and M3.
2. **Format.** All four folders still use retired formats:
   - `> **[GM]**` zones and `#### Milestone: None`.
   - `#### Milestone Overview`.
   - `## Read Aloud` with `> > >` nesting.
   - Third-person past-tense Summaries.
   - "Arc C/E/F/G/H" labels.
   - "**+1 Renown (supplementary)**" bonuses.
   - No `[!social]`, `[!hazard]` or `[!qna]` blocks, and no Event Outcomes at all. None of the four missions sets a named outcome. See section 6.
3. **Overview.md "## Overview" is not one line, and it spoils.**
   - M1 (`overview.md:22-28`) names Jarlaxle.
   - M2 (`overview.md:22-28`) says "Jarlaxle had written it himself".
   - M2b (`overview.md:29-37`) reveals the whole rig.
   - M3 (`overview.md:27-35`) reveals Jarlaxle's plan and the Z.Z. note.
4. **Renown and gates.**
   - Guide08 awards +2, +2 and +3 for M1, M2 and M3 (Guide08:66-68). That matches the CLAUDE.md calibration of 2 base for L2–3 and 3 base for L4–5. The drafts award +1 (+1), +1 (+1) and +2 (+1). Those numbers are AppC's (1/1/2/2) and are not guide-calibrated.
   - Draft gates: M1 "Renown 0" (`m01 overview.md:6`), M2 "Renown 2" (`m02 overview.md:6`), M3 "Renown 4" (`m03 overview.md:6`), M4 "Renown 7" (`m04 overview.md:6`).
   - Membership starts at Renown 1 (Initiate, Guide08:56), so "Renown 0" is impossible for a member.
   - With guide awards from a Renown 1 start, a member reaches 3 after M1, 5 after M2, 8 after M3 and 11 after M4 (base only).
   - So Soldier (Renown 3) is reached the moment M1 ends. The r03 draft says it "precedes BD Mission 4 (Three Nights)" (`r03-soldier/ev-01-soldier.md:15`). That is a mislabel: Three Nights is M3.
5. **Calendar and levels.**
   - Levels come from Milestone Points (`campaign/guides/gm-guide/adventure-summary.md:37-48`). L2 after Finding Floon, L3 after Trollskull Alley, L4 after Gralhund Villa.
   - The calendar is in `campaign/guides/gm-guide/structural-rules.md:42-48`. Twin Parades fall on Ches 21, the fireball on Ches 22, the villa on Ches 24, and Faction Outposts begin around Ches 25.
   - So M1 (L2) runs during or just after Trollskull Alley. M2 and M2b (L3) run in the Fireball! window. M3 (L4) cannot start before roughly Ches 24–25.
   - No draft in these four folders gives a date.
6. **Cassalanter secrecy (CLAUDE.md):** "No faction or NPC knows about the Asmodean pact… before the party discovers it". See M2 below. This conflicts with `campaign/setting/villains/jarlaxle.md:51`, which says "Knows about their infernal bargain through intelligence". It also conflicts with AppB/WDH, where the exposé is about devil worship.
7. **Jarlaxle identity and the reveal timeline conflict across docs.**
   - Org page (`07-bregan-daerthe.md:11`): the party meets "the real Jarlaxle Baenre" at Sea Maidens Faire. After Mission 4 he drops Nevercott but stays Zardoz.
   - Fireball ev-04 (`campaign/quests/act-ii/fireball/ev-04-the-sea-maidens-faire.md:30,110,142`) says "Zord is Jarlaxle Baenre". BD members get the persona dropped (hat off) in his cabin, and the Summary says the party "identified Jarlaxle Baenre".

   - `campaign/setting/villains/jarlaxle.md:3` says his identity stays hidden "until Sea Maidens Faire".
   - r10 and r25 switch contact at Three Nights (M3). The org page switches after M4.
   - Harper M4 (`campaign/quests/faction-events/harpers/m04-a-friends-house/overview.md:21,33`) has Jarlaxle admit who he is to the party at about L5.
   - No BD rank, Quest Requirements or outcome here pins down which scene is the first on-page "Jarlaxle" reveal.
