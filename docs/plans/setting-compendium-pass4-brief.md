## Rulings that bind every page

- The campaign's own decisions beat canon. **Standing rulings:** Xanathar is "it" and its Guild is always "Xanathar's Guild"; Manshoon is a clone of a clone, not an original; the Cassalanter pact is three years old, the contract needs 999,999 gp **and** ninety-nine souls, the family holds about 500,000 gp and the Vault's 500,000 covers the shortfall, **there are no soul coins** (the ninety-nine souls are the poisoned feast guests; the outposts gather gold and ritual components), Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) is the feast and Flamerule 11 is the contract due date; Dalakhar worked for **Dagult Neverember**; Kolat Towers is in the **Southern Ward**; the Field Ward is the most dangerous ward; the Guard and the Watch are separate; Barnibus is a Watchful Order investigator, not a Watch officer and not "compromised".
- **Cassalanter secrecy.** No faction or NPC outside the household and the cult knows about the Asmodean pact before the party finds it; use suspicion, never knowledge. The one exception is Jarlaxle, through Vessa, his doppelganger spy. He never tells Bregan D'aerthe members.
- **Bregan D'aerthe rejects Lolth.** No member worships her; they speak of her with contempt.
- **Zardoz Zord rule.** Jarlaxle's identity is hidden behind J.B. Nevercott and Zardoz Zord until **Sea Maidens Faire**. No Overview or player-safe line reveals it.
- **Event Outcomes, never flags.** **Jarlaxle Informed**, **BD Contact Severed** and **Ryvarra Identified** are Event Outcomes. Say "marked" or "set during the event", never "flag".
- Escalation uses four tiers only: Unaware, Suspicious, Alert, Lockdown. Quests, not arcs: never write "Arc X". Bold names for cross-references, never file paths or `.md` links. "Gamemaster" not "DM". "god of X" for a title, "goddess" only when it stands alone.
- Pronouns come from the existing Notable Figures page or the source. Never infer.
- Everything except `## Overview` is the GM layer. Write the Overview as a short public-safe entry (what an ordinary Waterdhavian knows), because the secrecy and Zardoz Zord rules apply to it.
- Open items go in a deletable `> **[GM]**` `#### Draft Notes` callout at the bottom. Inline `[research]` tags stay in place. A conflict you cannot settle from a ruling here stays a Draft Notes bullet; do not invent a resolution.
- Move existing text instead of rewriting it when it is correct. Change a table cell only for a stale fact, a ruling or a source-supported fix. No em dashes in prose you add, no punchlines, fragment stacks or "not X, more Y". Run the stale-word grep on your files: `grep -n "Xanathar Guild\|South Ward\|\bDM\b\|goddess of\|\.md)\|tapestry\|flag" <file>`.
