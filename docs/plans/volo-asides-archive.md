# Volo's Optional Asides (Archive)

These four asides were cut from the History era pages by user ruling. The setting and gazetteer pages will later be written in Volo's voice, with Elminster correcting his exaggerations, and these can be mined for that pass. Each aside is kept verbatim, with its source page and the section it followed.

## 1. Ahghairon's Gifts

Source: `campaign/setting/history/01-ahghairons-gifts.md`, after the last bullet of the closing section, before `## Overview`.

> *Dear reader, should you lay violets beneath Ahghairon's tower on his day, as I have, do it among the commoners and keep a long stride between yourself and the cage. Every one of them did, and so did I (a gentleman of letters ought to set an example, and mine was to stand at the back). The bones of the last fellow who tried it hang beyond the barrier still, and I looked at them rather longer than I intended before I made up my mind.*
> *~Volo*

## 2. Bregan D'aerthe's Long Road

Source: `campaign/setting/history/03-bregan-daerthes-long-road.md`, at the end of the Luskan section, before `### The Road to Waterdeep`.

> *I rate the long supper I took on the south bank three tankards, and the walk to the gate afterward four daggers, on account of the two very polite sailors who accompanied me. Luskan's captains dislike being called pirates in front of their own crews, and they dislike it a good deal more when the word comes from a guest at their table (I had taken it for a compliment, heaven help me). Say "captain," dear reader, and keep every other opinion for the road home.*
> *~Volo*

## 3. The Founding of Chains

Source: `campaign/setting/history/04-the-founding-of-chains.md`, at the end of the Doom Raiders section, before `### Manshoon's Waterdeep Operation`.

> *I confess I accepted a drink at the Yawning Portal from Davil Starsong, and that I stayed for the whole of the speech that came with it. His position, delivered with a patience I have rarely met in his trade, was that the Black Network is a trading company first and everything else a long way after (I wrote it down at once, having never heard a Zhentarim explain himself so politely).*
> *~Volo*

## 4. A Debt in Blood

Source: `campaign/setting/history/05-a-debt-in-blood.md`, at the end of the What the Cassalanters Built section, before `### The Recovery Plan`.

> *I cannot claim to know the Cassalanters well, having dined at their villa only twice, but on both evenings Lady Ammalia led me through her butterfly garden before the soup, naming every specimen and the country it came from. Arrive prepared to be educated (and to be hungry). She corrected my pronunciation of three of the names so gently that I wrote them down afterward, meaning to use them at my next dinner.*
> *~Volo*
