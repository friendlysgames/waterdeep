**Tissina Khyret** (Lawful evil, tiefling, she/her)

## Background

Tissina Khyret is an aging tiefling whom the household calls Madame Khyret. She is Ammalia Cassalanter's personal attendant and the family's chief housemaid, and she dresses in a long black gown and a headdress with black tassels. When she is not with her mistress, she spends most of her time in the master sitting room.

Tissina is fifty-eight and grew up in the Dock Ward as the daughter of a laundress who took in the linen of Sea Ward households. From the age of seven she carried baskets across the city with her mother, and she learned what each house wanted from its laundry before anyone said so. Strangers stared at her on those errands, and she learned early that a maid who kept the next thing ready was less likely to be turned away. She lost her mother the winter she turned thirty and laid her in a plot behind a Dock Ward almshouse.

Tissina went into service in the North Ward and spent most of her working life as a lady's maid. At forty-five she was dismissed from a cooper's household after a ledger went missing and the blame fell on the tiefling. A month later the ledger turned up in the son's coat, and nobody told her. That spring Ammalia married Victoro Cassalanter and went looking for an attendant of her own, and Tissina answered her questions about what she could do, worked a week's trial and was kept on without anyone asking for a reference. She found a house with a name for treating tieflings kindly, and in the thirteen years since she has never heard Ammalia remark on what she is.

Tissina has dressed Ammalia for every occasion since she arrived, and she has watched the rest of the staff replaced again and again while she stayed on. She was in the house when Osvaldo was born in 1480 DR and when the twins were born in 1483 DR. About 1489 DR, when the family's banking failed, she saw that Ammalia stopped eating at table and that the household accounts stayed on Ammalia's desk through the night, and she mentioned it to nobody.

Within a year of the bargain, Tissina joined the worship beneath the house, and she joined because Ammalia asked her to. Ammalia told her one evening in the master sitting room that the family kept an old practice below the house and wished her to share it, and Tissina agreed before the sentence was finished and has never doubted the choice. She knows the temple and attends the gatherings at midnight on the first day of every tenday, and she believes that the worship is a family spiritual practice. At the rites she stands on the perimeter beside Willifort Crowelle, Nana Rosse and Jandar Chergoba and sees that the other members behave. She keeps her regalia in a lockbox that she shares with Willifort, and each of them holds a golden key to it.

Tissina does not fully understand the contract's terms, because nobody has explained them and she has never asked. She was told that Osvaldo studies at a school in a faraway city, the same story the twins were given, and she knows only that the attic is forbidden.

**Personal goals.** Tissina wants a stone for her mother's grave. The plot is still marked with a wooden stake, and for years she has set aside coin from every quarter's wages toward a carved stone. She has never asked for a day to go to the almshouse, because a personal attendant is not given one and she does not leave Ammalia's side in public. She also wants to finish a bobbin-lace collar in her mother's pattern. She works on it in the sitting room whenever Ammalia is out, and she has unpicked it three times when a row came out crooked. She means to wear it on the day that she stands at the stone.

## Personality

To most people who meet her, Tissina Khyret is the composed tiefling attendant at Ammalia's shoulder. She wears a long black gown and a headdress with black tassels, and she speaks softly and courteously, offering a drink or a chair before anyone has asked. She calls Ammalia "my lady" and does not leave her side at a social function.

In the villa she is quick to notice anything out of place, and she tells Ammalia about it in a murmur. Asked about her mistress, she answers with simple loyalty and says little more.

## Connections

- **Ammalia Cassalanter:** The mistress whom Tissina has attended for thirteen years. Tissina joined the cult because Ammalia asked her to, and her devotion to Ammalia and her devotion to Asmodeus run together. She has never asked what the contract says, and nobody has offered to tell her.
- **Victoro Cassalanter:** The lord of the house, who leads the worship that Tissina attends.
- **Willifort Crowelle:** The chief butler and a fellow cult member. They share a lockbox for their regalia, each holds a golden key to it, and they keep order at the rites side by side.
- **Laiba "Nana" Rosse** and **Jandar Chergoba:** Fellow cult members, who keep order at the rites beside Tissina and Willifort.
- **Osvaldo Cassalanter:** The eldest son, whom Tissina believes to be at school in a faraway city.
- **Her mother:** A laundress, buried behind a Dock Ward almshouse. Tissina saves coin for a stone.

## Roleplaying Information

**Resonance.** Tissina should inspire surface courtesy; the specific unease of someone who anticipates needs too precisely; and cold dread in anyone who learns she is a cult fanatic.

**Emotions.** Tissina most often feels attentive devotion to Ammalia, theological certainty about Asmodeus, professional alertness to threats to the household, and the particular steadiness of someone who has never doubted the choice she made.

**Motivations.** Tissina wants to protect Ammalia and the household, serve Asmodeus through that service, and identify threats to the villa before they become problems.

**Inspirations.** When playing Tissina, channel Gríma Wormtongue (*The Lord of the Rings*), Theon Greyjoy (*Game of Thrones*, servile devotion), and Moneypenny (*James Bond*, loyal adjutant in a darker register).

### Voice

*Ammalia's composed tiefling attendant, who anticipates every need a little too precisely.*

- **Sound:** A soft, poised, deferential voice.
- **Sentence shape:** Short, courteous sentences and quiet offers. Among the cult: calm, devout certainty.
- **Word choice:** Public: service ("my lady", "may I bring you…"). Among the cult: devotion, spoken of Ammalia and her faith in the same breath.
- **Swearing:** *Never · Plain · n/a.*
- **Personality in speech:** Attentive devotion. She notices threats first and says so to Ammalia in a murmur. Publicly, her faith sounds like simple loyalty.
- **Quirks:** She offers something before it's asked for. She positions herself at Ammalia's shoulder.
- **Signature phrases:** "My lady." / "May I bring you something?" / "Something's amiss, my lady."
- **Never:** She never leaves Ammalia's side in public, and never speaks of her faith to outsiders.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *Greeting:* "Welcome. May I bring you something to drink? My lady will be with you shortly."
  - *Business:* "My lady is not taking appointments this tenday. I can pass on a message."
  - *Under pressure:* "My lady, behind me. Something's amiss."

## Character Information

**Persona.** To the world, Tissina Khyret is the tiefling attendant who stands near Ammalia at social functions and anticipates what is needed before it is asked. To those she considers threats, she is the first person in the villa to notice something is wrong. Deep down, her worship of Ammalia and her worship of Asmodeus flow from the same devotion, and she has never seen any contradiction between them.

**Morale.** In a fight, Tissina protects Ammalia first, using cult fanatic spells to hold threats at distance while Ammalia escapes.

**Relationships.** Tissina is Ammalia Cassalanter's personal tiefling attendant and a cult fanatic whose regalia is stored alongside Willifort Crowelle's in a shared lockbox.
