### 4.4 Encounters at 3, 4 and 5 characters

Rule questions for the main session to settle with `cr2-encounter-builder`:
- **First-turn KO check.** The grell can grapple and paralyse on its first turn (Con DC 11; a Paralyzed character is auto-critted at 5 feet). If the +4 applies, each grell is CR 7, Tier 2 Power 70, and two grells are 140 (212.7% / 119.6% / 76.6% HP lost at 3 / 4 / 5, impossible below five). The Force Grey reference applies +4 only to the Mage and Nihiloor and not to Tough, Veteran or the devourer (reference "Method", first-turn bullet). Suggested ruling: do not apply +4, but write a Special CR warning (as HMR 5.6 does for the devourer) and one safeguard: a grell uses Paralyzing Tentacles on the nearest character and will not target an already-Paralyzed one on the same round.
- Fleeing at half HP cuts a grell's real contribution to roughly 0.71 of listed Power (the heuristic in `force-grey-mechanics-reference.md` section 1 rule 5 and section 4), so real losses run lower.
- The warehouse is a ceiling fight: grells fly (Fly 30, hover), a 30-foot ceiling, ranged and spell players can answer, melee-only PCs cannot reach a grell that stays up. Say what the grell does (it dives to grapple, then climbs; Abduct carries a grappled creature at no extra cost).
