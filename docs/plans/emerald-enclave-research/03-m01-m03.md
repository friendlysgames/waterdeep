## 0. Headline findings

1. **Bonnie is the big problem (M3).** R and PREV have Bonnie leave Waterdeep in two tendays. FG M3, FG M4 and H3 all keep her at the Portal, and the Harper M3 gate is the same as the Enclave M3 gate once the Enclave is put on the shared ladder (R5/L4). The two missions can run in either order, so the exile ending breaks the converted pages. Recommended fix: **Option B, "terms, not exile"** (section 4).
2. **Gates and renown in R and PREV are off the ladder.** R/PREV gates are M1 "Renown 0, L2", M2 "R1, L3", M3 "R3, L4". The shared ladder is M1 joining/L2, M2 R3/L3, M3 R5/L4, with base renown 2/2/3. Base-only play reaches each gate (1 + 2 = 3, + 2 = 5, + 3 = 8).
3. **M2's Brandath crypt interior contradicts the converted-to-be Vault structure doc and WDH.** `arc-j` and WDH put a **treant** and a **glyph of warding** in front of the crypt. R/PREV let a 3rd-level party walk in with Ambrose's help and read a "sealed floor" that exists nowhere else. A decision is needed (section 3.3).
4. **M2's discovery can fail.** The R/PREV skeleton roll does not guarantee a single appearance by night 9 (about 15% chance of none), yet the pattern is the mission's central discovery (section 3.3).
5. **Messages are over the 2024 *Animal Messenger* limit.** The spell carries "a message of up to twenty-five words". The three briefings are 44, 43 and 39 words. FG made its *Sending* exactly 25 words.
6. **M1's tunnel has a single source** (Gerrick). That breaks the Three Clue Rule for the M5/M6 access point.
7. **Dead readers.** None of the M1 to M3 outcomes PREV lists is actually read where PREV says: no Faction Outposts reader exists, `arc-j` does not read **Brandath Crypts Visited**, and ev-02 never reads M2's own ev-01 outcomes. The one live outside reader is DR3 reading **Traitor Identified** (section 6).
8. **Kelso as a "Splinter operative" has no support** in the converted OotG M3 or DR3, and Faction Outposts (`arc-e`) never mentions him (section 5).

- **First-turn KO check.** One Scarecrow cannot Frighten (and so Paralyze) and then hit in one turn: Terrifying Glare and Fearsome Claw are separate actions with no Multiattack. The +4 does not apply. **Two** Scarecrows can combine them on one character: Glare makes a character Paralyzed, and a Claw hit on a Paralyzed target within 5 ft is automatically a critical hit (average 11 against a level-2 character with roughly 12 to 20 HP). That is the real danger and the reason to keep them apart.
- **Recommendation:** fight them one at a time, always. At 5 characters two may be combined only if the party deliberately forces it. The territories already separate them. Day Cost: three single fights are 12 (Debilitating) at 3 characters and 6 (Taxing) at 4 or 5. Say so in the hazard text and let the three fights be on separate nights or after a Long Rest. Add one Tough-sized wrinkle only if a table wants it: do not add monsters at 4 or 5 characters, add terrain pressure (the dry field fire clock) instead.
- **Fire:** the 2024 Scarecrow has *Vulnerability* to fire, so burning is genuinely effective (the source called it "suppresses their Damage Immunity", `AppC:744`, which is 2014 wording).
- **Blanket "cannot be surprised":** this is AppC colour (`AppC:752`), not a stat block trait. Present it as tactics (it faces the road and cannot be crept up on), not as a rule.
- A Terrifying Glare at level 2 is nasty: a Frightened (and Paralyzed) character is helpless until the end of the scarecrow's next turn. Give each scarecrow's hazard a plain tactic: the Pumpkin Head glares at the runner, the Sackcloth One ignores people while an animal is nearby, the Blanket glares at whoever carries the light.

### 4.3 Options

- **A. Exile stands (source, R, PREV).** Bonnie and the crew leave Waterdeep. Requires edits to H3, FG3 and FG M4, or a gate that forces EE M3 after both (not possible with equal gates). Keeps the source's moral weight. Breaks three converted pages.

Exile remains available only as the Enclave's opening position; the party decides whether to accept terms. A table with a Harper-member PC and an Enclave-member PC gets the intended conflict as a debate, not as a broken continuity.
- **C. Deferred exile.** Bonnie agrees to leave Waterdeep, but by the end of Flamerule (after the Vault) rather than in two tendays. Keeps the source ending and every Portal scene intact through Act IV. The Enclave's "cannot permit it" loses force, and the Bonnie Harper Operative path (H3) now carries an unpaid promise.
- **D. Order-gated.** EE M3 only becomes available after H3 or FG3 is skipped. Not workable, because the Enclave and Harper gates are equal and parties split across both.

### 4.4 Recommendation

**Option B.** It matches the converted pages, preserves the dilemma, and removes the departure-dependent outcomes. Concretely:
- Briefing: the Enclave wants the crew gone; Melannor includes "peacefully if possible".
- Stockroom scene after closing (not the taproom); Durnan says "Back room's free" (`FG3 ev-01:138-141`).

- The party's decisions: accept terms (default), insist on exile (DC 18; Bonnie then goes quiet and the Enclave gains nothing but a hostile informant; no part of the crew actually leaves), or let her handle Edric herself (the +1 she rewards).
- The Edric scene follows H3's version if **Harper M3 Complete** is marked (he is already out and the stockroom is cleaner), or plays in full if not.
- Parting gift: **Kelso Fiddlewick bought the names** (K2 above).
- Closing: Melannor's dove with three dots in a triangle (`R ev-01:71`) fires as "a receipt" confirming the terms, not a departure.
- Log the Enclave knowledge (the Enclave learned Bonnie's nature from its animals) in OOS, since `01-harpers.md:29` says only Mattrim knows.

Decision for the user: **A, B, C or D** (B recommended). If A is chosen, H3 `ev-01:17`, `:359-383`, FG3 `ev-01:235-283`, FG M4 `ev-02:277` and the Bonnie/Mattrim Notable Figures pages all need edits.
