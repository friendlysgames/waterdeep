# Force Grey Mechanics Reference (Session 42)

CR 2.0 audits for every Force Grey fight, Nihiloor as a boss, the Splinter raid on Blackstaff Tower, the rank-ally pools, and the fixed spell and item lists. Modelled on `harpers-mechanics-reference.md` (cited below as **HMR**). **HMR section 5 (the Occupying Devourer: stat block, Hold, Break, Strain, Extraction Procedure, recovery) is binding and is cited, never redefined.** Where a number below says "HMR", it is HMR's number.

## Method and conventions

- CR 2.0 only. % HP lost = (Enemy Power / Party Power) squared. Budget = Party Power x multiplier. Day Costs: Mild 2, Bruising 4, Bloody 6, Brutal 8, Oppressive 10, Overwhelming 13, Crushing 17, Devastating 25, Impossible 50. "Standard" = Bruising. "Hard" = Bloody.
- Monster Power, tier-adjusted (Tier 1 = party level 1-4, Tier 2 = 5-10, Tier 3 = 11-16):

| CR | 0 | 1/8 | 1/2 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 11 | 13 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 1 | 4 | 16 | 22 | 28 | 37 | 48 | 80 | 90 | 105 | 140 | 160 |
| Tier 2 | 1 | 3 | 12 | 17 | 23 | 30 | 38 | 65 | 70 | 85 | 130 | 150 |
| Tier 3 | 0 | 3 | 7 | 15 | 19 | 25 | 32 | 50 | 55 | 70 | 105 | 120 |

- Waves and boss phases are separate encounters. Day Costs add. Where I add wave percentages that is a rough heuristic of mine, not a skill rule (as HMR).
- Heuristic of mine (as HMR): Power is the square root of HP x DPR, so a creature with half the HP pool has about 0.71 of the Power. A creature that spends half its actions running contributes about half.
- Combatant counts include non-member companions. Bystanders, captives, victims, spawn and Vajra are never counted.
- **First-turn KO check.** The skill adds 4 to CR when a monster can kill or KO a PC on turn one. It applies to the 2024 Mage (HMR section 1 note) and to Nihiloor's full kit (section 4). It does not apply to the Warrior Veteran, the Occupying Devourer (HMR section 5), the Tough, or the Mage Apprentice.
- **Source status.** Blocks verified in HMR against 2024 *Monster Manual* records: Spy, Doppelganger, Mage, Tough, Tough Boss, Warrior Veteran, Scout, Guard, Commoner (CRs only), Intellect Devourer base, Mage Apprentice (CR 2, listed in HMR line 13). The main session then checked every block, spell, potion and item here against the 2024 5etools data (XMM, XPHB, XDMG) and corrected the values. The Verification table at the end records the result.

### Ally Power rules (all Force Grey ally boons)

1. **Counted in full.** Ally Power is added to Party Power at the party's tier (by the party's level, not the ally's), then the fight is built from the allied total. Alternative from HMR: keep the fight at the base budget and let the ally act as a safety net. Pick one per scene and say which.
2. **Only when it fights.** An ally who does not fight adds nothing. Vajra, Meloon after M3, Durnan, Merris, Renaer and the Tower staff are never counted (section 7).
3. **No +4 on allies.** The first-turn KO rule is applied to enemies. Ysmay counts as CR 6 (Power 65 at Tier 2).
4. **Roster columns stay at PC count.** An ally is not a fourth or fifth PC for the 3/4/5 rosters. Use the PC count, then add the ally to Party Power.
5. **Withdrawal.** Each ally has a stated withdrawal HP. Fighting to that point is about 0.71 of listed Power (HMR notes about 0.58 for Mirt, who leaves at a third). Verdicts use full Power, so real losses run lower.
6. **High-Power Warning.** An ally at 2x any PC's Power is treated as a safety net, not as a damage dealer.
7. **Notice and limits** (once per quest, days of notice) live in the rank events, not here.

### Tactics, thresholds, end conditions

- Hosted Meloon fights like a Warrior Veteran: Greatsword twice, Parry when hit. He never speaks, never swears, never draws Azuredge. He goes for whoever is working on him (a caster holding Concentration on the ward is the first target).
- Each failed attempt costs Strain, capped at half his max HP (32) per attempt and never below 1 HP (HMR 5.3). The party does not need to bring him down. If Meloon dies, the devourer is forced out at full HP and **Meloon Lost** applies (HMR 5.3(c)). Killing the host is a loss, not a method.
- At Hold 1 the devourer uses Slip Out and runs (Speed 40). A warded Meloon cannot be re-occupied.
- **Expelled devourer.** It appears at full HP, uses Occupy Body only on an eligible creature (Incapacitated, Small or Medium, 10 HP or fewer; HMR 5.3), else Devour Intellect on the nearest creature and runs. It flees when reduced to 14 HP or fewer.
- **Pursuit rule (not in HMR).** It needs 3 consecutive rounds of Dash. Each round a PC who can see it makes a DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check; on a success they keep it in sight and a PC who reaches it can attack. After 3 rounds with no PC in sight it is out of the Portal's reach: **Devourer Escaped**. A caught devourer fights to the death.
- **Portal variant.** If the fight happens in the common room, Durnan clears it on "Out." in the first round. Nobody in the room is a combatant.

## 4. M4 Destroy the Intellect Factory: Nihiloor's wing (level 5, Tier 2)

M4 is an objective layer inside Xanathar's Lair (brief, decision 1). It plays when the party reaches **X23 Competitive Intelligence Files, X24 The Infiltration, X25 The Experiments (Spawning Pool and twelve specimens), X26 The Puppet** (arc-f codes). Party Power 96 / 128 / 160. Bruising budgets 57.6 / 76.8 / 96. Bloody 72 / 96 / 120.

### 4.1 Nihiloor (boss-design)

**Concept.** The mind flayer who is the actual brain of the Xanathar Guild, caught in his wing while someone dismantles his programme. He does not defend the room. He measures what he can afford to lose and leaves when the answer changes.

**Boss Type and archetype.** Commander, **Field Marshal** (Leverage). Encounter Layer: Combat, with an Exploration edge (the wing is four rooms).

| Element | Puzzle | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Defining Trait | Sacrifice | The wing holds more objectives than the party can finish: the Pool (clocked), the placement records, the captive, and Nihiloor himself. Every node the party takes pushes him toward the door. They cannot have all four. |
| Strength | Division | The nodes sit in separate rooms (X23 to X26). Thralls and the pet pull the party apart. |
| Weakness | Crisis | Once he runs, what is left (the Pool's clock, the pursuit, the alarm) is a readable timeline. |

**The Tell.** Nihiloor never Dashes while at least two of the four nodes stand (Pool, records, captive, pet). He fights next to the node nearest the party. The second node lost sets him running. Players see it: he steps between the party and the pool, then the records, and the moment the second one falls he looks at the door.

**Shared statistics (Phase 1 and 2).** Medium Aberration, Lawful Evil. AC 15, Initiative +1 (11). Speed 30 ft. Str 11 (+0), Dex 12 (+1), Con 12 (+1), Int 19 (+4, save +7), Wis 17 (+3, save +6), Cha 17 (+3, save +6). Skills Arcana +7, Deception +6, Insight +6, Perception +6, Persuasion +6, Stealth +4. Senses Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16. Languages Deep Speech, Undercommon; telepathy 120 ft. **CR 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3).** Legendary Resistances: none in either phase. Reactions per round: 2 (max 1 per turn).
Shared traits: **Magic Resistance** (Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects). **Innate Spellcasting** (Intelligence, DC 15): at will *Detect Thoughts*, *Levitate*; 1/day each *Dominate Monster*, *Plane Shift* (self only).
**Source:** the 2024 Mind Flayer (XMM), verified: AC 15, 99 HP, CR 7, Speed 30 ft. and Fly 15 ft. (hover). Tentacles +7, 22 (4d8+4) Psychic, Grappled (escape 14) and Stunned while grappled. Extract Brain DC 15 Con, 55 (10d10). Mind Blast (Recharge 5-6) 60-ft Cone, DC 15 Int, 31 (6d8+4) Psychic and Stunned until the end of its next turn, half on a success. **Variant changes:** Extract Brain is removed (the campaign rule is occupation, not brain-eating; HMR 5). His hit points are split across two phases (50 + 49 = 99).

#### Phase 1: The Warden of the Wing (HP 50)

**Traits.** *Held Ground.* While at least two nodes stand, Nihiloor does not leave the room he is in. *Enthralled Servants.* Up to four creatures bound to him (section 4.3) obey without an action. They are not concentration effects.

**Actions.**
- *Tentacles.* Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) Psychic damage. A Medium or smaller target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14) and the Stunned condition until the grapple ends.
- *Mind Blast (Recharge 5-6).* 60-ft Cone. Intelligence Saving Throw: DC 15. *Failure:* 31 (6d8 + 4) Psychic damage and Stunned until the end of Nihiloor's next turn. *Success:* half damage.
- *Dominate Monster (1/day).* Per Innate Spellcasting. Used only on the PC who stands between him and the pet or a node, never on round 1 unless that applies.

**Reactions (2).**
- *Interpose Thrall.* Trigger: an attack roll or a spell targets Nihiloor and an enthralled creature is within 10 ft. Response: the thrall moves up to 10 ft and becomes the target instead.
- *Pull the Leash.* Trigger: a creature within 60 ft damages Nihiloor. Response: his pet devourer moves up to 40 ft without provoking and makes one Claw attack against that creature.

**Transition.** Phase 1 ends at 0 HP or when the second node falls, whichever comes first. All conditions on him end (framework rule).

#### Phase 2: The Retreat (HP 49)

**Traits.** *Flight Path.* On each of his turns Nihiloor moves toward the exit unless he is Grappled, Restrained or Stunned. *Last Exit.* Nihiloor cannot die in this phase. If he would be reduced to 0 HP, or if the party blocks every route out, he casts *Plane Shift* (self only, no action, cannot be countered) and is gone. This is a deliberate plot immunity set by brief decision 8 ("M4 never kills him"), not an HP-based mechanic.

**Actions.**
- *Withdraw (Bonus Action).* Dash.
- *Tentacles* and *Mind Blast* as Phase 1, used only on a creature that blocks the route or stands in the cone while he runs.
- *Dominate Monster* (if unused): on a blocker, so that the blocker becomes a wall for 1 round.

**Reactions (2).**
- *Interpose Thrall.* As Phase 1.
- *Mind Snare.* Trigger: a creature ends its move within 10 ft of Nihiloor. Response: Intelligence Saving Throw DC 15; *Failure:* Speed 0 until the end of its next turn.

#### The escape (threshold, route, what he does on the way out)

- **Threshold.** Any of these ends Phase 1 and starts the Retreat: (a) Phase 1 reaches 0 HP (half his full pool); (b) the second of the four nodes falls (Pool destroyed, records taken, captive freed, pet dead); (c) a thrall-collapse alarm: three thralls down.
- **Route.** X26 to X25 to X24 to X23, then the wing door and the corridor to **X4 Skullport Connection**, where the maintenance tunnel runs down toward Undermountain (arc-f, lines 111 and 143; his retreat toward X4 at half HP is already in arc-f's roster). Assumed length 300 ft total, so with Speed 30 and Withdraw (Dash) he covers 60 ft a round and reaches X4 in about 5 rounds. Adjust to the lair conversion's map.
- **On the way out (in order).**
  1. He gives the pet one telepathic order: hold the room. The pet fights to the death and does not follow (arc-f and AppC: he sacrifices it).
  2. Each room he leaves, the nearest thrall steps into the doorway and fights until he is 120 ft away.
  3. At the first blocker he uses Mind Blast (if recharged) back along the line, then Tentacles on the nearest creature that blocks his path.
  4. He Dominates one PC who is chasing him, so that PC blocks the others for a round. The effect ends when he leaves the wing or when Concentration drops.
  5. He does not stop to burn the records, the pool or the notes. He believes the programme is unreadable and the Pool unrecoverable.
  6. At the wing door he uses telepathy (120 ft) to wake the Guild: that starts the enforcer clock (section 4.4).
  7. At X4 he enters the tunnel and is gone. If cornered earlier, Last Exit.
- **Outcome.** **Nihiloor Fled** is always marked. A party that wins the pool, the records and the captive without catching him gets all three. He is never killed here.

#### CR math and tuning

- **Defensive.** 99 HP with AC 15 sits on the CR 5 row (99 HP, AC 15). **Offensive.** About 28-36 DPR averaged over three rounds (Mind Blast on two targets, Tentacles, Dominate), DC 15 against a CR 5 row of 14: CR 5-6. The XMM block carries CR 7 on the same numbers because of Magic Resistance, Dominate and the stun, so CR 7 stands. Tier 2 Power 70.
- **Per phase.** About 0.71 x 70 = 50 each (HP 50 and 49 of 99).
- **First-turn rule.** If he opens with Mind Blast and Dominate (the party blocks the exit or ambushes him), apply +4: CR 11, Tier 2 Power 130, about 92 per phase. Phase 1 alone: 91.8% Oppressive at 3 PCs, 51.6% Bloody at 4, 33.1% Bruising at 5.
- **Tuning up.** Allow Dominate on round 1. Add a thrall. **Down.** Drop the pet. Lower Mind Blast and Snare DCs to 14.
- **Divergences flagged.** HP is split across phases (framework: each phase has its own pool). Phase 2 is a retreat, not an escalation. Last Exit is plot immunity. No Legendary Actions. A balanced alternative, if the user later wants a killable Nihiloor, is to drop Last Exit and let Phase 2 end at 0 HP.

#### Reuse for Xanathar's Lair

Cite this block unchanged. If the lair meets him outside X26 (arc-f "drawn by a specific threat"), start in Phase 1 where he stands and run the same exit rules toward X4. The nodes are the lair's own (arc-f X24 records, X26 Puppet). If M4 ran, the pool is already gone; use three nodes and the same Tell.

### 4.2 The pet: Nihiloor's Occupying Devourer

### 4.3 Wing guards: enthralled thralls

- They fight until Nihiloor passes 120 ft from them. Then each is Stunned until the end of its next turn and, once it wakes, surrenders or flees. They do not know why.
- They do not pursue the party out of the wing.

### 6.2 The Mage first-turn rule

- The Mage spends round 1 on the objective, not on bursts: *Invisibility* on himself, then *Fly* or *Misty Step* to the stair foot, and a stone message to his team ("Door clear. Stair is next."). Bursts start round 2. Rosters above assume this.
- If the party ambushes him, holds the north service door from round 1, or the disruptor fired (section 6.5), he opens with Arcane Burst and the +4 column applies.
- He retreats at 40 HP (half) using *Misty Step* and *Fly*. He surrenders if cornered at 20 HP or fewer, trading the cell's location. Spies disengage when two raiders are down. Toughs fight until the leader leaves, then surrender.
- Non-combat route: DC 16 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) offers the leader safe passage once the raid has stalled. DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) shows he does not want to die for this.

### 6.3 Vira Solkan: Mage Apprentice who runs

- **Block.** Mage Apprentice (CR 2, Tier 2 Power 23; listed HMR line 13). AC 15, 49 HP, Arcane Burst (XMM, verified); she is a junior arcanist in cover, not a combatant. NF says Mage; this reference overrides it (brief).
- **Tactics.** She never starts a fight. When exposed she Disengages and uses *Misty Step*, runs for the nearest window or the north service door, and flees at half HP. She surrenders if Grappled or cornered, and says a single cold "fuck" (voice doc).
- **Counting.** She is not counted in the rosters above. If she is free and joins the breach, she replaces a Spy (3 PCs: Mage + Vira = 88, 51.2% Bloody; 4 PCs: Mage + Tough Boss + Vira = 126, 59.0%; 5 PCs: Mage + Tough Boss + Spy + Vira + Tough = 155, 57.2%). All Bloody (6).
- **The signal: sending stone, never *Message*.** She carries one stone of a matched pair; the Mage carries the other. The holder touches the stone and speaks up to 25 words; an answer returns within a minute; then neither stone works until the next dawn (2024 XDMG, verified). Sending the message is an action and needs Vira's hand on the stone, so she can send while Grappled.
- **Clock.** The strike team arrives 15 minutes after the stone message. Taking the stone first (Utilize with a contested Athletics check, or Grapple her first) prevents it. A stone taken intact carries the reply; the voice is not one a member can place unless **Manshoon Named** is marked.

### 6.4 The Tower wards (hard rule)

- **Floors.** Ground (door hall, north service door, the stair foot): never warded. Floor 2 (library behind its iron-latched door, the east workroom): warded. Floor 3 (quartermaster Merris, the upper stair): warded. The top floor and Vajra's study: warded and sealed.
- **Clock.** Starts the round the first raider crosses the north service door.
- **Rounds 1-10.** No raider can cross the stair landing to floor 2. A raider on the stair is stopped at the landing by an invisible wall of force (no save).
- **Round 11 on.** Floor 2 is open. From round 11 each round a raider spends above the ground floor, one of the Tower's staff is lost (named in the event).
- **Round 16 on.** Floor 3 is open. If a raider reaches Vajra's study, she ends the raid herself at the cost of sealing the Tower for a tenday. The party did not stop the attack.
- **What the party defends.** The north service door and the stair foot, not the floors above. Vajra holds the wards from her study and takes no part in the party fight. Forty-three people have Tower access (event text). The party holds the ground floor.
- **Won.** All raiders are down, driven out, captured, or the Mage has left, before round 11: **Tower Attack Stopped**.

### 6.5 The disruptor

- **What it is.** A hollowed research text with a tuned resonance crystal on the floor-2 east workroom shelf. It fires on Vira's word (within the Tower), or automatically when the first raider crosses the north service door if she is gone or caught.
- **If it fires.**
  1. The floor 2 and 3 wards drop for 4 hours. There is no ward clock; raiders can climb from round 1.
  2. One pulse: every creature in the Tower has its Concentration end and cannot cast a 3rd-level or higher spell until the end of its next turn.
  3. Vajra's study seal fails.
  4. The Tower staff and Vajra, free of the wards, take the second rank of raiders. The roster steps down one row (3 PCs: Mage alone, 95 with the +4 column, 59.6% Bloody; 4 PCs: Mage + Spy, 112, 46.6% Bloody; 5 PCs: Mage + Tough Boss + Spy, 150, 53.5% Bloody). The Mage opens with bursts.
- **Removed before it fires.** Tower Attack Stopped is unaffected; the party gets the clean row (section 6.1). The event handles the checks.
