### 2b. The pact: three years ago

> "In the late 15{{th}} century DR, the Cassalanters faced imminent bankruptcy. In order to prevent it, in {{Yearlink|1489}}{{Waterdeep-time}} Ammalia and Victoro struck a deal with the [[archdevil]] [[Asmodeus]], trading the souls of their children in exchange for instant financial comeback. Their eldest son, Osvaldo, was taken immediately and transformed into a [[chain devil]], while the younger twins Elzerina and Terenzio were scheduled to be taken on the occasion of their 9{{th}} birthday, one [[tenday]] after [[Founders' Day]] of {{Yearlink|1492}}.<ref name="WDH5e-p115-116">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|115-116}}</ref>"
— `sources/research/pass4-bundles/cassalanters/wiki.txt:45`

> "In [[1489 DR]],<ref name="WDH5e-p116" />{{WDH-time}} Victoro and his wife [[Ammalia Cassalanter]] signed a contract with Asmodeus, in which they agreed to give up the souls of their children in exchange for power, good health, and long life. Their eldest son, [[Osvaldo Cassalanter|Osvaldo]], was taken immediately and transformed into a [[chain devil]]. [[Terenzio Cassalanter|Terenzio]] and [[Elzerina Cassalnter|Elzerina]], their youngest twins, were also scheduled to be taken by the [[archdevil]] when they turned nine years old in [[1492 DR]],{{WDH-time}} to be transformed into [[lemure]]s. The only way to save the children was pay to the [[Nine Hells]] a tremendous amount of coin and the souls of numerous people in sacrifice.<ref name="WDH5e-p193-194">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|193-194}}</ref><ref name="WDH5e-p130">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|130}}</ref>"
— `sources/research/pass4-bundles/cassalanters/wiki.txt:176` (Victoro page, Dragon Heist 5e refs; the wiki takes 1489 DR from "three years ago")

> "**Span:** 1489 DR to 1492 DR, three years. The published adventure says only \"three years ago\" (lines 18294 and 18403). The year 1489 DR is that phrase counted back from 1492 DR. The Forgotten Realms Wiki states 1489 DR for the pact on its Cassalanter, Victoro, Ammalia and Osvaldo pages, and those figures derive from the same adventure line. The Alexandrian timeline dates Neverember's deposition to 1489 DR and gives no pact year. The campaign opens on Ches 1, 1492 DR."
— `campaign/setting/history/05-a-debt-in-blood.md:17`

The contract needs 999,999 gp and ninety-nine souls, both; the family holds about 500,000 gp and the Vault's 500,000 is the shortfall. Financial ruin was the trigger. Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) is the feast and Ammalia's operational window; Flamerule 11 is the contract due date. Victoro is publicly a Lathander worshipper and the house is publicly known for Siamorphe. \"Three centuries\" is unsourced; use \"several generations\"."
— `session 46 handoff.md:64` (**Osvaldo's age here is superseded by session 47 line 67**, quoted in 2e)

> "{@creature Ammalia Cassalanter|WDH} hosts a grand party on Founders' Day that begins in the early afternoon and ends at midnight. No one suspects that the food in the pavilion tents has been laced with midnight tears-a poison that will slay the impoverished guests at the stroke of midnight.
