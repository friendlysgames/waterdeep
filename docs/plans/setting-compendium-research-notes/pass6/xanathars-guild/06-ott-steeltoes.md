- Source rank used: campaign files, then the published adventure, then the Alexandrian PDFs and appendices, then the wiki.

### A8. How he came to put the Eye in Sylgar's bowl, and when

**Campaign, the placement (day-one fact, kept on the page)**
> "- **Eye #1, Xanathar:** Manshoon's Zhentarim stole it from the Protector's Enclave in Neverwinter. About three months before the fireball, Manshoon sent two lieutenants to Skullport with it as a gift. Xanathar ate them and kept the Eye, and Ott Steeltoes put it in the bowl of Sylgar, Xanathar's goldfish, because it was a pretty color. Xanathar does not know what the Eye is."
> — `campaign/setting/lore/stone-of-golorr-and-the-eyes.md:40`

**Campaign, Guild page (after the envoys)**
> "- **The Eye in the bowl:** Eye #1 of the Stone of Golorr sits in Sylgar's bowl in the lair's sanctum. Ott Steeltoes placed it there as a pretty stone after Xanathar killed the envoys who brought it, and Xanathar does not know what it is."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/08-xanathars-guild.md:125` (GM Notes)

**Campaign, Xanathar's Lair quest opener (backstory; the Structure doc, which is authoritative until the Lair journal exists)**
> "Three months before the Trollskull fireball, Manshoon dispatched two Zhentarim lieutenants to Skullport carrying a gift: Eye #1 of the Stone of Golorr, stolen from the Protector's Enclave in Neverwinter. The Eye was offered as a peace overture — proof that Manshoon's splinter faction could be a valuable partner in the Grand Game. Xanathar received the envoys in its throne room, listened to their proposal, and ate them. It kept the Eye and has never learned what it is. The Eye passed to Ott Steeltoes, who placed it in Sylgar's fishbowl because it had a pretty color and Xanathar liked Sylgar's bowl to look interesting."
> — `campaign/structure/arc-f-xanathars-lair.md:9`

**Campaign, the lair scene**
> "Eye #1 has been intentionally placed in Sylgar's fishbowl rather than a vault, safe, or guarded chamber. The reason is characterization."
> — `campaign/structure/arc-f-xanathars-lair.md:366`

> "Ott Steeltoes thought it was a pretty stone and placed it where Xanathar likes its fish's habitat to look interesting. It has been sitting there for months, protected less by paranoid security than by the fact that nobody knows it's there. This placement tells the party more about Xanathar's nature in one beat than any amount of descriptive prose: a being of tremendous power who keeps a world-altering artifact as an inadvertent pet decoration."
