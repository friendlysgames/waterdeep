## ERA 2
- CAMPAIGN villains/xanathar.md:21-22: "Xanathar" is a title; each holder inherited "the apparatus and the paranoia"; the Guild eliminates anyone who knows more than the holder, "after a century of this". Current holder Zushaxx ("it"); Kirukesai a previous holder (xanathar.md:23).
- ADVENTURE wdh:32756: "It isn't the first beholder to claim this mantle." Lair X33 "Crypt of Xanathars Past": four embalmed beholders in crystal cylinders, nine copper urns of disintegrated beholders; current Xanathar "gazing somberly" at the tombs (wdh:18014-18034). Lair is an "ancient dungeon complex originally built by Netherese wizards and expanded by beholders" (wdh:15126). Connected to Skullport by a 300-foot tunnel (wdh:17136) and to the Castle Ward sewers by a secret stair (wdh:15126).
- CAMPAIGN arc-f:167: X33 holds remains and effects of every previous holder "stretching back centuries", evidence the title is "claimed through murder, not inherited", the first holder's signed charter, journals of the last three Xanathars with "early dealings with Manshoon's predecessors"; only Nar'l knows the room exists.
- WIKI (Xanathar disambiguation, Xanathar_Guild.txt:121-159, Dungeon #206, Villains' Lorebook): the original Xanathar was a beholder hired by the Zhentarim to start a Skullport cell; it cut them off and built its own guild; the Guild was a freelance thieves' guild by 1357 DR. Kirukeskai (also spelled Kirueskai), a beholder slaver in Skullport, killed the original, took the name, ruled by 1372 DR, put eleven Masters over the Guild's domains, merged it with the Agents of the Eye; accounts of its death differ. Page Xanathar.txt:6-9 lists Kirukeskai (14th c.), Xandulzrithral (retired peacefully), Izulktur (briefly, 15th c.), Zushaxx (late 15th c.); Izulktur "replaced itself" with Zushaxx. Zushaxx is "at least the fifteenth Xanathar" by the WDH X33 count; appointed four lieutenants (Kal'dir, Quid, Sial Sapphire, Draak). By 1491 its only advisor was Nar'l; by 1492 it had taken over Skullport and killed a rival beholder, Nerozar. CAMPAIGN docx (Waterdavian's Guide, line 455, 480-482): "a string of beholders going back centuries"; "Zushaxx/The Eye arrived in Skullport in 1205 DR" as a slaver from the Graypeaks Hive; author's remix has Zushaxx plot with Nihiloor to kill Izulktur.
- WIKI Skullport (W Skullport.txt:233-290; Skullport sourcebook, City of Splendors, Dungeon #200, WDMM p.304): Netherese Sargauth Enclave built it about -750 DR; the flameskulls are its remnants (-339 DR collapse); Halaster arrived about 168 DR, had the Underhalls by 309 DR; his apprentice Shradin Mulophor settled Skullport in 1148 DR; the Keepers took the locks in 1166-1168; the Skulls destroyed Shradin in 1369; the Spellplague (1385) gutted the mantle; mithral revived it late in the 15th century; trade in slaves, drugs, cadavers, hireswords, smugglers (:153); Waterdeep sentenced criminals there (:256); Laeral spied there under an alias (:317).
- ADVENTURE wdmm:62188-62196: Skullport "held, claimed, or occupied over the years by duergar, drow, dwarves, crazed wizards, slavers, and pirates", now "under the sway of Xanathar", businesses pay for protection; Skull Island fortress garrisoned by Xanathar's minions; connected to Undermountain Level 3 by the River Sargauth and five passes (wdmm:62160); the Guild runs watch posts on Levels 1-2 (wdmm:795, 4026); Nerozar "challenged Xanathar for lordship of Skullport and lost" (wdmm:10570); the Zhentarim via Rizzeryl and House Auvryndar are trying to drive the Guild out (wdmm:6578); the thirteen flameskulls "consider themselves the true rulers" (wdmm:62196).
- Organization: ADVENTURE wdh:1423-1424 anyone can join after a serious crime, low ranks "have little inkling" the boss is a beholder; ranks Eyestalker, Agent of the Eye, Eye Ray, Guild Boss, Hand of the Eye (wdh:1203); bodyguards carry secret kill orders (wdh:1430); treachery common (wdh:32757). CAMPAIGN: Ahmaergo writes most orders; Nihiloor has run three projects including four months of dominating Xanathar; three response teams (08-xanathars-guild.md:21-25; guides/factions/09-xanathars-guild.md:5-13).
- Present: ADVENTURE wdh:472-474: during merger talks with Zhentarim envoys the Stone vanished from the lair; Xanathar killed the envoys, street war followed; Dalakhar was the thief; wdh:32760 its spies stole the Stone "a few years ago", opened the vault, a dragon drove it out. CAMPAIGN arc-f:9: Eye #1 reached Xanathar via Manshoon's envoys three months before the fireball.

## ERA 3
- Origin: WIKI (Road of the Patriarch, Menzoberranzan: City of Intrigue, FRCS 3e, WDH p.206): born to Matron Mother Yvonnel Baenre of House Baenre; birth order meant sacrifice to Lolth, the blade failed and Doquaio Baenre died instead; Lolth favored him and he rejected her (Jarlaxle_Baenre.txt:96); one of few male drow with real power. CAMPAIGN docx (Jarlaxle NPC Guide lines 51, 62): third son (docx gloss, not wiki). ADVENTURE wdh:32178: "flamboyant, swashbuckling drow iconoclast"; no origin story.
- Founding: ADVENTURE wdh:1297: "originally made up of the much-derided and dishonorable castaways of destroyed drow houses", almost all male. WIKI (Homeland, Reader's Guide to Drizzt; Bregan_D_aerthe.txt:39-77): founded by Jarlaxle, who left his house and name; recruited rogue males from destroyed houses of Menzoberranzan and Ched Nasad; House Baenre main client; began slowly in the 10th century DR; 1018 DR took part in the raid that acquired Zaknafein; membership doubled by 1118 DR; main base the Clawrift; close tie to House Baenre and brother Gromph. CAMPAIGN 07-bregan-daerthe.md:21 "outmaneuvering Underdark politics for generations"; BD rejects Lolth (CLAUDE.md); Soluun "despises Lolth" (02-soluun-xibrindas.md:22).
- Surface expansion: WIKI (Bregan_D_aerthe.txt:84-92): Calimport base 1366 DR "in part due to the influence of Crenshinibon"; then Luskan contacts; major presence through Ship Kurth by 1377 DR; 1376-77 DR helped bring down the Host Tower, Luskan became a free port; later opened trade with Mirabar. WIKI (Luskan.txt:55-103; Storm King's Thunder 5e): five High Captains (Taerl, Baram, Kurth, Suljack, Rethnor), each leading a "Ship"; after 1485 "under the sway of" Jarlaxle; infobox lists Jarlaxle as ruler in 1489; BD agent Beniago, in human guise, was High Captain Kurth by 1483. ADVENTURE wdh:32178: Jarlaxle "infiltrated the city of Luskan... declared himself its secret lord"; Lords' Alliance membership is "the latest" scheme (wdh:32179); Neverember's "fiercest political opponent" (wdh:22941); Laeral calls Luskan "a greedy pirate state" (wdh:26667). CAMPAIGN docx: "I made Luskan MY city".
- Faire and vault: ADVENTURE wdh:1299, 4744, 32180: Faire is "one of Jarlaxle's legitimate business enterprises", already based in Luskan, touring the Sword Coast; visited Lantan "a year ago" where he got the Scarlet Marpenoth and clockwork servants (wdh:23034, 25468); Zord has "operatives searching for" the Stone; Nar'l sent into the Guild "a year ago" (wdh:32323); WIKI Nar'l's mission 1491 DR. No passage says when or how Jarlaxle first learned of the vault. CAMPAIGN: ALEX 1 p.17 Jarlaxle "unaware of the Stone or the Grand Game" at start; Luskan technomancers built the nimblewrights; arc-h:15 the Eye "arrived through Bregan D'aerthe intelligence work", Nar'l placed "eleven years ago"; running-the-villains.md:25,41 Jarlaxle holds Eye #3 and knows the Stone, Eyes and vault; ALEX 8 p.65 Fleetswake is "the perfect cover"; Tarsakh 20 departure (structural-rules.md:50).

## UNSUPPORTED old-outline facts [research]

## Usable consequence-layered facts
1 X33 turns the "title" into a clue (dead-beholder crypt; title claimed through murder; only Nar'l knows; journals tie the Guild to the Zhentarim). 2 Zushaxx's rise rests on a quiet murder (replaced Izulktur, as Kirukeskai replaced the original). 3 Skullport has had many masters (Netherese, Shradin, Keepers, Skulls, the Guild); thirteen flameskulls consider themselves rulers. 4 The Guild reaches into Undermountain (watch posts Levels 1-2, Skull Island); House Auvryndar and the Zhentarim oppose it; Nerozar's challenge failed. 5 Jarlaxle's rise has a human cost (BD recruits castaways of destroyed houses; Nar'l lost his house; Soluun's zeal binds him, not Lolth). 6 Luskan's order is Bregan D'aerthe's (High Captains under Jarlaxle's sway after 1485; Laeral calls it a greedy pirate state). 7 Fleetswake is the Faire's cover; Lantan trip a year earlier supplies the ship. 8 Nar'l's placement date changes how long Jarlaxle has had a view of Skullport.
