**Origin (published)**
> "The {@item Stone of Golorr|WDH} is actually an aboleth transformed by magic. In this inanimate state, the aboleth can read the mind of any creature that attunes to the stone, as well as modify that creature's memory. A creature attuned to the stone can also extract information from the aboleth, including lore about Neverember's vault."

**Origin (Alexandrian)**
> "The Stone of Golorr is an artifact that once belonged to the Abolethic Sovereignty. For millennia it was held in a vault within the ancient floating city of Xxiphu on the world of Abeir, but it was lost during the Wailing Years."
> — `sources/18. Golorr Artifacts.pdf`, printed p.126 (PDF p.2)

> "The Stone is the transformed remnant of an aboleth named Golorr, a creature of vast memory that was polymorphed into artifact form centuries ago by a Netherese arcanist. It retains Golorr's memories, but cannot fully communicate them while its three Eyes are absent."
> — `sources/Act_III_Arc_E.md:849`

**Physical form**

**Eyes (campaign)**
> "He stripped out three gemstone Eyes the Stone needs to reveal anything useful. Getting into the vault requires recovering all three Eyes and restoring them to the Stone."
> — `campaign/guides/gm-guide/running-the-villains.md:9`

**Eyes (Alexandrian)**
> "After hiding the secret of the Vault's location in the Stone of Golorr, Lord Neverember chose to further secure it by blinding the Stone: He removed the Stone's three eyes, robbing it of the ability to both see and communicate its secrets, and hid those Eyes in different places."
> "In order to discover the location of the Vault, someone needs to restore the Eyes to the Stone of Golorr. (Which is simply a matter of having both in the same location and placing an Eye within an empty socket.)"
> — `sources/8. The Eyes of the Stone.pdf`, printed p.62 (PDF p.1)

**Eyes (published):** none. See Not found.

**Awakening (Alexandrian, what each count does)**
> "BLINDED: The Stone is inert."
> "1 EYE RESTORED: With one Eye restored, the Stone can communicate, but still not see the secrets it once contained."
> "2 EYES RESTORED: Although still crippled, the Stone (and a character attuned to it) can now \"see\" the location of the remaining Eye and the immediate area around it."
> "3 EYES RESTORED: The Stone of Golorr is fully restored and operates normally once again."
> — `sources/18. Golorr Artifacts.pdf`, printed p.127 (PDF p.3)
