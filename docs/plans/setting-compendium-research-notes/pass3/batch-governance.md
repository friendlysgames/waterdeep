### Q4. Conflicts between sources (both sides quoted; higher-ranked source named)

**C1. Are the Masked Lords known to each other?**
- Published adventure: `sources/adventure-wdh.json:30477` "But the identity of those other lords would be hidden even from each other" (quoted in Q1).
- Wiki: `wiki.txt:752` "The identities of the Lords, other than the Open Lord, were not supposed to be known to the public, although they were known to each other."
- Campaign: `campaign/setting/history/01-ahghairons-gifts.md:145`: "> - **Conflicts:** The Masked Lords' secrecy. `adventure-wdh.json` line 30477 hides them from each other. The wiki's Lords of Waterdeep page says they were known to each other (City of Splendors boxed set, 2e). The published adventure wins. Laeral says "I know them" in `r50-lioncrown/ev-01-lioncrown.md` lines 247-249, and the Warduke contact does not know the Lord in `r25-warduke/ev-01.md` line 227. The GM Notes reconcile the first by the Open Lord's reserved powers, which is a ruling for the user to confirm. The wiki also says most of the Lords were exposed and murdered after Ahghairon's death."
- Ranking: the brief places the published adventure above the wiki. The Masked Lords' secrecy is not one of the eight rulings, so the campaign may outrank the adventure outside the rulings. The campaign's own history note says "The published adventure wins", and the reserved-powers reading is marked `[research]` and awaits user confirmation. Flagged for the orchestrator.

**C2. Watch headquarters and Masked Lords' seat in the Castle Ward.**
- Ruling 1: the Castle Ward holds neither the Watch headquarters nor the Masked Lords' seat; petitioners meet the Masked Lords in the Palace audience chamber.
- Published adventure: `sources/adventure-wdh.json:31009` names the audience chamber (quoted in Q1). It names no Watch headquarters and no Masked Lords' seat.
- Campaign: `campaign/setting/waterdeep-lore.md:14`: "- **Castle Ward** (city center, government, Yawning Portal): seat of the Masked Lords; City Watch headquarters; Durnan's Yawning Portal is the campaign's literal and symbolic entry point"
- Wiki (City Watch page, `sources/research/pass3-bundles/city-watch/wiki.txt:76`): "The Watch's main base of operations was the [[Palace of Waterdeep]]."
- Campaign Castle Ward gazetteer: `campaign/gazetteer/waterdeep/castle-ward.md:173`: "The Watch's main base is the Palace of Waterdeep, which also holds the Guard's and the Watch's offices and the guard barracks beside its stables (wiki, 2e). Castle Waterdeep's garrison is in its landmark entry. The Dragon Heist text names no headquarters."
- Ranking: ruling 1 governs (the adventure names no headquarters; the wiki's Palace claim conflicts with ruling 1). The Palace itself is in the Castle Ward.

### Q4. Conflicts between sources

- Published adventure: `sources/adventure-wdh.json:30516` "This document is available in multiple languages at the Palace of Waterdeep, and (in the Common tongue) provided on request by the magisters at the gates and in the harbor."
- Ranking: the adventure outranks the campaign for this (not a campaign decision in the rulings). The adventure says "available" and "provided on request"; it does not say "posted". Flagged.

### Q4. Conflicts between sources

**C1. How many guilds?**
- Campaign: `campaign/setting/waterdeep-lore.md:31` "- 18 recognized guilds control every significant trade; operating without guild membership invites fines and Watch attention"
- Published adventure: `sources/adventure-wdh.json:30829` "No aspect of life in Waterdeep goes untouched by at least one of its more than forty guilds."
- Published adventure list: forty-four names at `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1115-1158` (Q5).
- Wiki: `wiki.txt:3` "As of {{Yearlink|1492}}, there was a total of over forty guilds in Waterdeep,"
- Ranking: the adventure outranks the campaign's "18" (no campaign decision on the count is in the rulings). The "18" has no source. Flagged.

**C2. Guild membership and the law (fines, Watch attention).**
- Campaign: `campaign/setting/waterdeep-lore.md:31` "operating without guild membership invites fines and Watch attention".
- Published adventure: `sources/adventure-wdh.json:30842` "Guild members have no lawful recourse to interfere in the business of someone who chooses to not join the organization."
- Wiki: `wiki.txt:13` "The guilds had no legal recourse to interfere with those who refused to join them."
- Ranking: the adventure and wiki both say there is no legal recourse, with no fines and no Watch involvement. The campaign's "fines" and "Watch attention" conflict with both.

**C3. Guild control of the trade.**
- Campaign: `campaign/setting/waterdeep-lore.md:31` "control every significant trade".
- Wiki: `wiki.txt:8` "and as a collective controlled 80% or more of the economic activity within their sphere of trade." (cited to Power of Faerûn p.74; the bundle line is a long line).
- Published adventure: `sources/adventure-wdh.json:30842` "Since that group includes virtually everyone who sells the necessities of life or offers shelter for a fee, the benefits of joining a guild swiftly become apparent to those who procrastinate in this regard."
- Ranking: the adventure outranks; the wiki gives an 80% figure; the campaign's "every significant trade" is stronger than either.
