# Pass 1 findings: Era 1 (Ahghairon's Gifts) and Era 7 (A City Watching Itself)
Tags: ADVENTURE (WDH = sources/adventure-wdh.json, line) / WIKI (page; book/edition from its <ref>) / ALEX (Alexandrian PDFs in sources/) / CAMPAIGN. Compiled from the researcher's report (Session 47). Not in the wiki folder: the dragonward page, Laws of Waterdeep (Code Legal), Ahghairon's Tower, Black Robes, Watchful Order, Seven Sisters.

## ERA 1
Sourced:
- ADVENTURE WDH 30475: Ahghairon, "in his 112th winter", defied Warlord Raurlor in 1032 DR; Raurlor wanted a northern empire; Raurlor's sword became a serpent and killed him. WIKI agrees: Ahghairon born 920 DR; 1032 DR starts Northreckoning (CoS 2e, GHotR 4e, SCAG 5e).
- WDH 30476-30477, 31009: Ahghairon set fire to the warlord's throne and sat on it unharmed; decreed he would sit openly while a council of near-equal lords ruled with him; those lords' identities hidden "even from each other" so none could be bribed or threatened; the throne is "the very one" the Open Lord uses now.
- WDH 30478-30479: Ahghairon "instrumental" in the black-robed magisters, the Griffon Cavalry and the guilds; ruled "over two centuries" until the magic sustaining him failed; entombed in his tower in the Palace courtyard behind an invisible force cage, a would-be thief's bones floating outside.
- WDH 31475-31479: Ahghairon's Day is Eleasis 1; Lords toast him and the Watchful Order, guildmasters toast the Lords in his name, commoners leave violets, the Open Lord tours taverns. WDH 6101: his marble statue in the City of the Dead. WDH 31447-31452: Founders' Day (Flamerule 1) "commemorates the birth of the city"; Castle Waterdeep veiled as Nimoar's log fort. Earlier layers: Illefarn's Aelinthaldaar, Clan Melairkyn, Halaster's tower, Warlord Nimoar (WDH 30472-30475).
- WIKI dated chain (Ahghairon; History of Waterdeep; CoS 2e, CS:Waterdeep 3e): tower built 1007 (he sealed Halaster's Hold); Azuredge made 1026; wards introduced 1035 (Castle, Trades, Dock, Southern/Temple); dragonward 1071 (cited to Dragons of Faerun, 3e: Ranressa Shiard returned riding the copper wyrm Galadaeros, Ahghairon placed a mythal); Griffon Cavalry from the 1235 siege; Kerrigan's betrayal 1246 (his only recorded betrayal); guilds ordered 1248; Ahghairon dies 1256. After: Guildwars 1256-1262, Lords Magister 1262-1273, Baeron and Shilarn restore the Lords 1273 (CoS 2e); Baeron to 1308, Lhestyn 1308-1314, Piergeiron 1314-1379. Gap: History of Waterdeep says Audreithra Teltorna succeeded Piergeiron in 1379 (an Ed Greenwood web post, not a book; the Lords of Waterdeep list omits her). Open Lord list to 1489 (CS:W 3e): Ahghairon, Baeron Silmaeril, Lhestyn, Piergeiron, Caladorn Cassalanter, Dagult Neverember, Laeral.
How the pieces work:
- Open Lord (WIKI CoS 2e / CS:W 3e): sole public Lord; titles Warden, Overmaster of the Guilds, Commander of the Watch, chief diplomat; reserved powers: vet and select new Lords, name the next Open Lord, fix the number of Lords; the Lords' Rule is reaffirmed at the Shieldmeet Council every four years. WDH: the Commander of the Watch reports to the Open Lord (31886); the Warden of Waterdeep is Elminster, who answers to Laeral (31836); the Open Lord sets Force Grey's mission goals (1341-1342); the only removal on record is Neverember, "voted out" (452).
- Masked Lords: WDH hidden even from each other; WIKI known to each other (CoS 2e); Lord's Ensemble: WDH 32302, Mirt wears it only for official meetings; WIKI identical black robes, speaking through the Open Lord.
- Dragonward (WDH 494-496): undispellable, originates under Ahghairon's Tower, dragons "physically unable" to enter the city or its sewers; does not cover the harbor or Undermountain; a dragon touched by the dragonstaff of Ahghairon can ignore it; Aurinax holds the staff, which Maaril gave him before the Spellplague (WDH 31745-31747). CAMPAIGN wording "without the Open Lord's permission" (waterdeep-lore.md:50); castle-ward.md:188,:260 rule the campaign wording stands and log the conflict.
- Code Legal (WDH 30514-30516): law and custom by precedent; copies at the Palace and with the magisters; magisters have broad discretion and "any Masked Lord can overturn" a ruling; origin not stated. CAMPAIGN additions: the Asmodean temple is a capital offence (waterdeep-lore.md:39, asmodeus.md:70).
- City Watch and Guard: WDH keeps both and ranks both (31822-31886); WIKI says Ahghairon restructured Raurlor's army and navy into the Watch and Guard; WDH credits Ahghairon only with magisters, Griffon Cavalry, guilds.
Conflicts: Masked Lords hidden from each other (WDH 30477) vs known to each other (WIKI CoS 2e); CAMPAIGN Laeral says "I know them" (r50-lioncrown/ev-01:247-249), the Warduke contact does not know the Lord (r25-warduke/ev-01:227); WDH wins. Magisters: WDH 30478 credits Ahghairon vs WIKI Black Robes instated 1273 (CoS 2e). Dragonward: WDH physical bar with staff exception vs waterdeep-lore.md:50 permission; Dragondown commemorates dragons fought in later centuries (WDH 31441-31442), after the 1071 ward. WIKI gap: Lords of Waterdeep says Lords governed "except for a six-year period" vs History of Waterdeep 1256-1273. Guard and Watch: WIKI (4e FRCG) Guard folded into the Watch by 1479 vs WDH 5e and castle-ward.md separate. history.md:16 "true founder" vs WDH's earlier settlements (30472-30475); WIKI the town became a true city in 1032. waterdeep-lore.md:28 "1,200 guards across three shifts" has no source (pilot-findings:68); waterdeep-lore.md:14 puts Watch HQ and the Masked Lords' seat in the Castle Ward, WDH names neither, WIKI gives the Palace as the Watch's base (castle-ward.md:170 marks it [research]); WDH 31008 says Castle Waterdeep is not the seat of rule.
Unsupported outline claims [research]: Open Lord "designed to be removed if they fail the city" (only the Neverember vote exists); dragonward, Code Legal, Watch "built as protections" by someone who "watched what tyranny looks like" (only Raurlor's empire plan and sealing Halaster's Hold touch it; dragonward trigger is Galadaeros, WIKI 1071); "built Waterdeep to survive bad rulers; the campaign is the test" is campaign framing; Era 1 time-before-campaign is arithmetic (1032 to 1492 = 460 years); Code Legal origin; Ahghairon's role in the Watch (WIKI only).
Hooks: the Open Lord still sits on Raurlor's burned throne (WDH 31009); anonymity cured bribery and Manshoon turned it into a blackmail target (a five-name ledger, two Lords squeezed; WDH 30308-30312, 30364-30376; Mirt's "I volunteered", arc-i:265); the dragonward is a tradeable object (dragonstaff passed Maaril to Aurinax; arc-j:218; Jarlaxle wants it); Ahghairon's tomb is the dragonward's origin and a walking statue (the Griffon) woke in 1479 to defend it (WDH 31193); the design failed once when its founder died (Guildwars); Ahghairon's Day Eleasis 1 falls 21 days after Flamerule 11; Ahghairon sealed Halaster's Hold (1007) and Laeral calls Halaster "the oldest danger under this city" (r50-lioncrown/ev-01:253).

## ERA 7
Sourced:
- WDH 32201-32203: Anamanue Laeral Silverhand, born 765 DR, fifth of Mystra's seven daughters; ex-Witch-Queen of the North, led the Nine, "met and married" Khelben, retired after his death; returned after the Spellplague and the Sundering, weakened; only Elminster knows the extent of her decline. WDH 32204: a few years ago Dagult was ousted; she "reluctantly" stepped in at the Masked Lords' request, was overwhelmed, then "settled nicely"; uses magic sparingly, relies on deputies, slips out of the Palace in disguise. WDH 32205-32206: she covets the Blackstaff (holds Khelben's soul); Vajra secretly sends Force Grey on unauthorized missions; Laeral "rationalizes her inaction" as a test of Vajra.
- WDH 1384 spies on adventurers; WDH 26632-26633 met Bregan D'aerthe spies invisible in a Dock Ward alley, warned by Harper spies in Luskan; WDH 528 Xanathar fears her; WDH 32160 Jalester and Faerrel helped her expose a plot to overthrow the government. WDH 32444: "several Masked Lords were assassinated in quick succession some years ago"; Orond expected Yalah to fill a vacancy and was passed over. Named Lords: Mirt, Corylus Thann, Jelenn Urmbrusk, Thardouk Starbuckler, Dorgar Adarbrent; Caladorn Cassalanter a former Lord (WDH 18404, 22713, 30308-30312, 32293).
- ALEX 13. Clues and Timelines p.98-99: Neverember proclaims himself ruler of Neverwinter in 1467 as Open Lord; 1489 DR Laeral returns and he is deposed; Laeral's auditors discover the embezzlement. ALEX 21. Faction Reports p.142-143: Gralhund journals date "the Lord Murders" to 1491 and call Neverember "former Open Lord" by Nightal 1491. ALEX 2. Other Factions p.20 ties Yalah's snub to Death Masks.
- WIKI: Laeral became Open Lord c. 1489 after Mystra asked her to stabilise the North (Rise of Tiamat 5e, Death Masks novel); Neverember ousted by a Masked Lords' vote c. 1489; the 1491 Cazondur conspiracy killed 13 Masked Lords; Laeral herself was killed and revived (Death Masks novel).
Campaign Laeral: moon elf, chaotic good, widow of Khelben; took the job because the Masked Lords asked; decline known only to Elminster except one Lioncrown (city-officials/01-laeral-silverhand.md:21; castle-ward.md:120-127). Lords' Alliance org page 02-lords-alliance.md:38: she believes the vault is in Neverwinter and does not know the Stone resurfaced; factions guide 03-lords-alliance.md:9 "three moves behind"; Lioncrown r50:257 "told it lay in Neverwinter, and I believed it"; Neverember "set it aside by decree" with a lock no Masked Lord could open. Wants the gold in the treasury (03:7); recovered "quietly" (waterdeep-lore.md:24); offers a 10% finder's fee, asking once (design-notes-running-the-campaign.md:93; arc-j:238); issues the Masked Lord's Writ whatever the answers (m06 overview:27); holds a Splinter offer of the Masked Lords' names for a fifth of the vault (m06 overview:23); Lioncrown: "Nobody is winning" (r50:261); her magic waned since the Spellplague and she hopes a fragment of Halaster's crystal will halt it (r50:33-36). Neverember Open Lord from at least 1467 (ALEX) or c.1468 (WIKI); WDH 13256 shows him Open Lord in 1475; about 21-22 years in office.
Conflicts: history.md:60-61 (suspects the vault exists, doesn't know where, lets factions exhaust themselves, "has contingencies") vs the newer pages above; newer wins (waterdeep-lore.md:24 "knows more than she lets on" partly overlaps). ALEX 2 p.21 Laeral is "considerably behind the curve", knows of the embezzlement and that a powerful artifact was stolen from the palace during the ouster vs campaign she does not know the Stone resurfaced (the "artifact stolen" fact is in no campaign file). Laeral on Khelben: WDH married; WIKI never married (CS:W 3e, Seven Sisters 2e); campaign widow; WDH wins. Species: WDH and WIKI human, CAMPAIGN moon elf (pilot-findings:71); Durnan as a Masked Lord is CAMPAIGN only. Vajra "three years in the post" (campaign) vs WIKI 7th Blackstaff, Nightal 11, 1479 (4e FRCG) vs WDH 31193 a Blackstaff, Tsarra Chaadren, at the Spellplague; WDH gives no tenure. WDH 30516 "Masked Lord can overturn a ruling" vs WIKI sole executive powers reserved to the Open Lord. WDH 26632-33 (Laeral meets BD spies), WDH 31836 (Elminster as Warden) and the Lord Murders (1491) appear in no campaign file. finding-floon/overview.md:53 and ev-03-zhentarim-warehouse.md:101 call Renaer "the Open Lord's estranged son"; Dagult is the former Open Lord. Neverember's ouster: old outline (Masked Lords investigated) vs ALEX (Laeral's auditors); arc-j:17 "Lord Regent" is used by no source. Caladorn Cassalanter: WIKI lists him as an Open Lord (CS:W 3e), WDH 18404 and CAMPAIGN a Masked Lord only. Span: ALEX 1489, WIKI c.1489, WDH "a few years ago", history.md "about three years": all fit; start year 1489 DR derived. Wiki internal: Storm King's Thunder says "only a few months" in office around 1490; fits 1489.
Unsupported outline claims [research]: "reformist"; "the kind of leader Ahghairon designed the system to produce"; the vault story "damages the Lords' institutional credibility"; "watching and choosing not to intervene yet"; "she has contingencies"; "quiet recovery over public confrontation" (only waterdeep-lore.md:24); moon-elf identity (CAMPAIGN only).
Hooks: Neverember hid a vault with a lock no Masked Lord could open, so the institution Ahghairon built was locked out of its own treasury (r50:257); Laeral's decline is the city's real defensive gap (she hides it, needs a Halaster crystal, Vajra and the Blackstaff unresolved; WDH 32205-32206); Manshoon's Masked-Lords ledger vs the Lord Murders of 1491 (a Lord system already bled and penetrated); Yalah passed over after the Lord Murders (WDH 32444; ALEX 21 p.143); the 10% fee and the Writ show a cautious institutionalist who also covets the Blackstaff and lets Vajra run unauthorized missions; 1492 is a Shieldmeet year (derived; WDH 616; WIKI Shieldmeet), the Lords' Rule reaffirmed at the Shieldmeet Council right after Midsummer, just after Flamerule 11; Jarlaxle's Lords' Alliance bid flows through Laeral (factions/08-bregan-daerthe.md:33) and she already met his spies (WDH 26632-33); Elminster is Warden of Waterdeep per WDH 31836, answering to Laeral: the one man who knows her decline also runs the Guard.
