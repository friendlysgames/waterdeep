## PART B: CALENDAR
Calendar of Harptos (WIKI, FRCS 3e pp.76-77; WDH 5e p.4; ADVENTURE 616-632): 12 months, 3 tendays of 10 days, five festival days between months, 365 days; tendays have no formal day names. Epithets (WIKI, 3e): Hammer Deepwinter; Alturiak The Claw of Winter; Ches The Claw of Sunsets; Tarsakh The Claw of Storms; Mirtul The Melting; Kythorn The Time of Flowers; Flamerule Summertide; Eleasis Highsun; Eleint The Fading; Marpenoth Leaffall; Uktar The Rotting; Nightal The Drawing Down. (SCAG 5e dropped the leading "the" in the Tarsakh epithet; WDH misspells Tarsakh as "Tarsahk".) Festival days: Midwinter (Hammer 30/Alturiak 1), Greengrass (Tarsakh 30/Mirtul 1), Midsummer (Flamerule 30/Eleasis 1), Highharvestide (Eleint 30/Marpenoth 1), Feast of the Moon (Uktar 30/Nightal 1); Shieldmeet follows Midsummer every fourth year (1492 is a Shieldmeet year by the wiki's rule, derived). Solar markers: equinox Ches 19, solstice Kythorn 20, equinox Eleint 21, solstice Nightal 20. WIKI: 1492 DR is "Year of Three Ships Sailing"; WDH names no year. (The file Year_of_the_Ageless_One.txt is the 1479 DR page.)

Festival table (waterdeep-lore.md:44-47) checked:
- Fleetswake Ches 21-30: dates correct (ADVENTURE 31390, WDH p.185; WIKI). Last tenday of Ches honoring the sea, maritime trade and sea gods (Umberlee, Deep Sashelas, Valkur). Boat races, Shipwrights' Ball, Copper Cup galas. Last two days = Fair Seas Festival (seafood, flower petals, Guard collections for Umberlee dumped into Umberlee's Cache at sunset on the final day). Ships pay no docking fees but captains give at least 1 gp a day (WIKI). Dock Ward is the centre.
- Waukeentide Tarsakh 1-10: correct (ADVENTURE 31401; WIKI). Caravance Tarsakh 1 (gifts), Goldenight Tarsakh 5 (all-night sales), Guildsmeet Tarsakh 7 (guild gala), Leiruin Tarsakh 10 (guild dues and charters renewed with the Lords). Cassalanter galas are a campaign function (arc-g-cassalanter-villa.md:75-79: Caravance, Goldenight, a Tarsakh 7 Charity Ball).
