## ERA 4
- Manshoon: born Zhentil Keep 1229 DR; founded the Zhentarim (Black Network) 1261 DR; joined by Fzoul Chembryl 1263; built trade routes Moonsea to Sword Coast (WIKI Manshoon.txt:79-89, Zhentarim.txt:196; FRCS 3e, Ruins of Zhentil Keep). WDH calls him "one of the founders" (wdh:560, 32216). Banedeath 1361; Cyric's fall and 1369-70 ruin of northern Zhentil Keep (Zhentarim.txt:211-221).
- Clones: stasis clone spell; Fzoul and Orgauth "slew" Manshoon 1370 DR; a dozen-plus clones fought (Manshoon Wars); three survived: one with the Zhentarim, one the vampire Orbakh in Westgate, one fled to Undermountain with Halaster (WIKI Manshoon.txt:91-103, FRCS 3e p283). WDH: a mishap woke all clones at once (wdh:32216), "at least three still alive" (32217); the Waterdeep clone hid in Undermountain, Halaster captured him and amputated his left arm, regrowth failed, prosthetic (wdh:32217); the hand is an Undermountain guardian (wdmm:26133). Simulacra are a separate tool ("creates copies of himself using the simulacrum spell", wdh:561, 32225).
- Zhentil Keep: WIKI (FRCG 4e) Shadovar razed it 1383 DR over the Zhentarim-phaerimm alliance; Spellplague 1385 (WDH wdh:30485 agrees). WIKI (SCAG 5e): headquarters at Darkhold as of 1489; interest in restoring the Keep 1489, "retaken" by 1491 (Zhentil_Keep.txt:117). CAMPAIGN 06-doom-raiders.md:45 "efforts underway to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep" (Appendix B:680).
- Doom Raiders / Davil: WDH: five adventurers who plundered lich lairs, joined the Black Network, "came to Waterdeep three years ago" (wdh:31916), so 1489 DR (WIKI Doom_Raiders.txt:32, Davil_Starsong.txt:76 state 1489, citing WDH). WDH: Waterdeep Zhentarim "fractured"; Manshoon's supporters want Xanathar destroyed; Doom Raiders "consider themselves the true Zhentarim of Waterdeep" (wdh:1445-1447). WDH has no "split" from Manshoon: the Doom Raiders founded the foothold, Manshoon is the later arrival.
- Manshoon in Waterdeep: WDH clone "infiltrated Waterdeep years ago", hidden in Kolat Towers (wdh:32215-32219, 26731); aims: bribe and blackmail the Masked Lords, oust Laeral, replace the Watch with Black Network mercenaries, then turn on Halaster. CAMPAIGN villains/manshoon.md:40-42 goals: the Stone for Weave knowledge to supplant Mystra, control of the underworld, the simulacrum as survival. CAMPAIGN organizations/09-manshoons-zhentarim.md:13 and Alexandrian 1. The Villains p.16: Manshoon "peeled off" the network the Doom Raiders established, learned through it that Neverember used Zhentarim local resources to embezzle.
- Kolat Towers: WDH built by wizard brothers Duhlark and Alcedor Kolat; Duhlark added the force field; Lady Gondafrey imprisoned 1379 DR (wdh:26863-26865, 28506); WIKI Kolat_Towers.txt:34 brothers bought it before 1368. CAMPAIGN structure/arc-i-kolat-towers.md:9-17: Alcedor died 1385, Duhlark nine years later; the Manshoon clone fled Undermountain, found the towers abandoned, spent two years building the Splinter; E7 simulacrum made seven years ago, ice-preserved; hub-and-spoke with the Interrogation House (North Ward) and Yellowspire (Castle Ward) via teleportation circles.
- Doom Raiders in campaign: "arrived several years ago", run fronts they believe in (06-doom-raiders.md:23); "active internal war" with the splinter (:25); structural-rules.md:7-9 Manshoon's cell does not acknowledge them, regards them as "deserters who stole his name"; Skeemo Weirdbottle a traitor since before recruitment (guides/factions/07-doom-raiders.md:11).

CONFLICTS (Era 4): 1 Direction of the fracture: history.md:39 says Davil's cell separates from Manshoon on pragmatic grounds; organizations/09:13, Alexandrian Villains p.16, guides/factions/07-doom-raiders.md:7, structural-rules.md:9, villains/manshoon.md:32 and wdh:31916 say Manshoon is the later arrival and peeled off / infiltrated the Doom Raiders' network. Newest pages win. 2 Simulacra vs clones: history.md:38 says Manshoon "died and was replaced by his own simulacra"; sources say clones, simulacra are a separate tool (notable-figures/manshoons-zhentarim/01-manshoon.md:23; organizations/09:15 "a clone of a clone"). 3 "Original or simulacrum" (history.md:40) is stale; the man in the towers is a clone claiming to be the true Manshoon; the open question is clone vs simulacrum (E7; E12/E13 by escalation tier). 4 Kolat Towers ward: waterdeep-lore.md:17 and arc-i:9 "Trades Ward"; wdh:26797, 32215 and WIKI "Southern Ward"; wdh:560 "Trades Ward". 5 Doom Raiders timing: arrived 1489 (WDH) vs Alexandrian Villains p.16 needing them present during Neverember's 1467-1489 tenure. 6 Splinter dating: arc-i:15 two years (about 1490) vs WDH "years ago"; the seven-year-old simulacrum predates that arrival. 7 Zhentil Keep: WIKI (SCAG 5e) retaken by 1491 vs campaign "efforts underway". 8 Eye custody in Kolat Towers: finding-floon/ev-03:54, z05-offices.md:11, arc-a:204 say the locket Eye went to Kolat Towers vs about-this-campaign.md:13, arc-i:341, villains/manshoon.md:40 (holds no Eye) vs Alexandrian 12. Lair Kolat Towers p.92,94 (an Eye in an astral vault in E9) vs Alexandrian 8. The Eyes p.62.

## ERA 6
- Tenure: Alexandrian 13. Clues and Timelines p.98: Open Lord from 1467 DR; WIKI Dagult_Neverember.txt:153 "circa 1467", Lords_of_Waterdeep.txt:144 and History_of_Waterdeep.txt:194 "circa 1468" (unexpanded template, book unverifiable); WDH confirms him as Open Lord in 1475 (wdh:13256, Kalain's portrait); WDH monthly 1-shard-per-person tax from "the first year of the reign" (wdh:30643). Ouster: Alexandrian 1489 DR, "Laeral Silverhand returns... Neverember is deposed" (p.98); WIKI circa 1489 citing The Rise of Tiamat and Death Masks (Dagult_Neverember.txt:211, Lords_of_Waterdeep.txt:144, Laeral_Silverhand.txt:153); WDH only "A few years ago" (wdh:32204). The ouster was decided while Neverember was in Neverwinter (wdh:452; WIKI Dagult:213).
- Stone and vault: WDH Neverember embezzled 500,000 gp, had a wizard erase the vault's location from his mind into the Stone, hid it in the Palace; after the ouster his spies took it from the palace but were killed leaving (wdh:451-452). Vault dwarf-built (Melairkyn per Alexandrian). WDH the Stone is an aboleth transformed by magic (wdh:453); Alexandrian a Netherese arcanist did it centuries ago (Act_III_Arc_E.md:849). Alexandrian: Neverember discovers the Stone in Neverwinter; his wife Lady Alethea Brandath dies and he learns of the Melairkyn vault beneath the Brandath Mausoleum (13. Clues p.98). CAMPAIGN arc-j-vault-of-dragons.md:13: he found the vault arranging his wife's interment and moved Lord Anri's remains. WDH: Neverember bargained with Aurinax, who keeps the dragonstaff in exchange for guarding the gold (wdh:454, 31746-31747; WIKI Dagult:206).
- The three Eyes (Alexandrian invention, not in WDH): Neverember keeps one in Neverwinter, gives one to Renaer in a mourning locket, hides one in the Brandath Mausoleum (Kalain's lockbox: "the last of the three Eyes has been secured", written four years ago, Act_III_Arc_D.md:598); Zhentarim steal the Neverwinter Eye from the Protector's Enclave and offer it to Xanathar, who kills the embassy and keeps it; the Cassalanters extract the Brandath Eye; Manshoon's cell takes the locket Eye from Renaer in 1492. CAMPAIGN holders: Eye #1 Xanathar, Eye #2 Cassalanters, Eye #3 Jarlaxle (Scarlet Marpenoth); Manshoon holds none (running-the-villains.md:19-30; setting/grand-game.md:32-36); no custody story for Jarlaxle's Eye.
- Laeral: WDH "reluctantly" took the post at the Masked Lords' request (wdh:32204); WIKI Mystra tasked her (Laeral_Silverhand.txt:153). CAMPAIGN city-officials/01-laeral-silverhand.md:15,21; gazetteer/waterdeep/castle-ward.md:121.
- Renaer: WDH a Harper, lives off his mother's inheritance, "Father and son detest one another" (wdh:32546-32547); "My father and I haven't spoken in years" (wdh:2121). CAMPAIGN independents-allies/02-renaer-neverember.md:22,26; independents-adversaries/01-dagult-neverember.md:21 ("Lord of Neverwinter"). Patreon Renaer guide: 1479 for the Blackstaff Tower rescue of Vajra (13 years before).
- Dalakhar: Neverember's gnome spy, sent in 1492 to retrieve the Stone (wdh:474, 4781; WIKI Dalakhar.txt:66); CAMPAIGN 06-dalakhar.md:26, killed in the fireball.
- Neverember extras: WIKI ties through advisor Rian Nightshade (Rise of Tiamat 5e p17), hostile rivalry with Jarlaxle (WDH p131) (Dagult:135,143); stayed Lord Protector of Neverwinter, there since 1468 (NCS 4e p86, Dagult:163); more despotic after the ouster, some Waterdhavians feared a war (Dagult:216,231).

## Usable consequence-layered facts
1 Neverember held the office about 1467 to about 1489; nearly every adult Waterdhavian lived under his taxes; the monthly shard-per-head (wdh:30643) left a poll-tax grievance. 2 The ouster happened while he was in Neverwinter (wdh:452): Laeral's seat began as a vote he could not contest in person. 3 Aurinax is still bound to a man who no longer holds the office that gave the promise weight (wdh:454; design-notes:105). 4 Manshoon's clone arrived in a city where Laeral, Khelben's widow, witnessed the Manshoon Wars (WIKI Manshoon.txt:96; Laeral on Lioncrown ev-01:273 "I have known that name longer than you have been alive"). 5 The Doom Raiders came in 1489, the year of the ouster; the Alexandrian has Neverember using Zhentarim resources for the embezzlement (flag the conflict). 6 The heir the factions circle holds the hidden Eye without knowing it (Renaer's locket from his mother; Alexandrian Villains p.16; finding-floon/ev-03:54-59). 7 The Zhentil Keep reclamation is why the Doom Raiders want the gold (06-doom-raiders.md:45). 8 Laeral believed the vault was in Neverwinter for years (lioncrown/ev-01:257; 02-lords-alliance.md:38), so the Lords' Alliance chased the wrong city.
