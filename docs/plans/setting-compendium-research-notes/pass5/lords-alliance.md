## 1. Tenets

**Civilization must be actively defended**

> "*"Civilization is the most fragile thing we have built. We will not let it fall."*"
— `campaign/setting/organizations/02-lords-alliance.md:12`

> "They are united by the conviction that civilization must be actively defended. In Waterdeep, the Alliance works as a pro-city intelligence and security apparatus and pays field agents to watch for threats to the city's stability, both outside the walls and within them."

**Professional conduct: agents, not adventurers; assignments are not optional**

> "The Alliance is the most demanding of the factions in professional conduct. Members are expected to behave like agents and not like adventurers, and a member who refuses a mission or breaks the Alliance's rules can be suspended or dismissed."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/02-lords-alliance.md:18`

> "One more thing. Assignments from us are not optional. If that changes your answer, now is the time to say so."

> "Each candidate gives their own answer after three seconds of silence, and a refusal is final."
— `campaign/guides/factions/03-lords-alliance.md:41`

> "A character who belongs to a faction other than the Lords' Alliance can turn down a mission without consequence."
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:3583` (published adventure, below the campaign in rank)

**The gold belongs to the city; the Alliance keeps institutional procedure**

> "In Laeral Silverhand's framing, the gold was taxed from Waterdhavian citizens and merchants, and Lord Neverember's ouster doesn't change who owns it. She will see it recovered and properly disbursed, and she will credit those who made the recovery possible, but she won't bend institutional procedure to do it."
— `campaign/guides/factions/03-lords-alliance.md:7`

> "The Alliance's position on the Grand Game is plain: the 500,000 stolen dragons belong to the city, and it wants them recovered and returned."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/02-lords-alliance.md:16`

**Work for the Open Lord; her field agents answer to her** (sources: Alexandrian PDF; published adventure; Appendix B)

> "{@creature Jalester Silvermane|WDH} (see {@adventure appendix B|WDH|12}) is an agent of the Lords' Alliance who reports directly to {@creature Laeral Silverhand|WDH}. ## 3. Grand Game Agenda and Overview

### Grand Game Agenda (existing text, verbatim, org page lines 32-34)

> "The Alliance wants the gold home. Members who bring the Grand Game to Jalester's attention draw Laeral's interest, and once she is fully informed she makes the recovery a political priority and gives the party real resources. The Alliance will not let the party keep the gold. In exchange for returning it, it offers renown and political standing that no other patron in the city can match. A character who pockets the gold and claims ignorance will find that Laeral is a thorough investigator."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/02-lords-alliance.md:34`

What the faction wants, from other sources:

> "The Lords' Alliance wants the 500,000 gold pieces returned to Waterdeep's city treasury."
— `campaign/guides/factions/03-lords-alliance.md:7`

> "- Intelligence on what the villain factions intend to do with the vault gold, and not only where it is."
— `campaign/guides/factions/03-lords-alliance.md:21`

> "- A commitment that the gold will reach Waterdeep's treasury or an approved dispersal, and not a single faction's coffers."
— `campaign/guides/factions/03-lords-alliance.md:22`

> "- The name of whoever holds the Stone of Golorr, whenever the party can safely disclose it. **Escalation to the Open Lord** is where a member does so."
— `campaign/guides/factions/03-lords-alliance.md:23`

> "The Alliance will not let the party keep the gold, but will offer extraordinary renown and political capital in exchange for returning it. Characters who pocket the gold and claim ignorance will find Laeral a very thorough investigator."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:264` (the DM Tip box, "Alliance Agenda in the Grand Game"; the full paragraph begins "If Lords' Alliance PCs bring the Grand Game to Jalester's attention, he escalates immediately to Laeral Silverhand.")

— `campaign/setting/organizations/02-lords-alliance.md:38`

> "The Open Lord of Waterdeep isn't exactly a "minor" faction, but Laeral Silverhand is considerably behind the curve in the current Grand Game. She knows that Lord Neverember embezzled 500,000 dragons from the city, but as *Dragon Heist* begins she is under the belief that he already has the money in Neverwinter."
