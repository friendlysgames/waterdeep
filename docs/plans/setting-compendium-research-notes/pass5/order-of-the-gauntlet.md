## 1. Tenets

**T1. Evil is hunted before it reaches the city.**

> "Evil does not rest. Neither do we."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/04-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:12` (epigraph). The same line is the epigraph in `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:402`.

> "The Order of the Gauntlet is a fellowship of paladins, clerics, and devout martial warriors who pursue and destroy evil actively — without waiting for it to come to them."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/04-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:14`

> "The order's mission is to seek out and destroy evil before it gains a foothold. The surge of violence in Waterdeep spurs members of the order to find adventurers who can help return peace to the city."
> — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1404`

> "The Order of the Gauntlet looks for members who seek to fight evil in all its forms. Adventurers who worship Helm, Torm, or Tyr are especially sought after."
> — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:3942`

> "As part of their duties the Order of the Gauntlet remained vigilant against any potential threats to the Realms, standing ready to stop any aggression. When necessary, they investigated potential threats or secretive organizations, and watched over those creatures they saw as inherently [[evil]]."
> — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/order-of-the-gauntlet/wiki.txt:43` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, citing FO.OotG, the 2014 Factions web article)

**T2. Faith is the weapon, and heroic demonstration is the method.**

> "The Order of the Gauntlet held three tenets:"
> "* Faith was a member's most potent weapon against evil"
> "* Great challenges required heroic demonstrations of both strength and valor"
> "* It was justified to punish an act of evil, not a thought or feeling"
> — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/order-of-the-gauntlet/wiki.txt:46-49` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, citing FO.OotG, 2014 Factions web article). These are the wiki's three tenets; use only with the edition tag.

> "Members of the order were strong in their faith, using it to stand against evil forces.<ref name="RoT-p12"/> While this faith ranged from believing in one's personal god, it extended also to those considered close friends, and especially to oneself."
> — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/order-of-the-gauntlet/wiki.txt:35` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, citing *The Rise of Tiamat* p.12, 5e adventure). The `<ref>` markup is part of the source line.

**T3. Punish deeds, not thoughts. Strike only when the evil is an act.**

> "The Order was clear on its methods of operation, and would never preemptively attack an opponent. They forewent the approval of local leadership when striking against evil.<ref name="RoT-p12"/> They struck their foes fast and hard, seeking decisive victories.<ref name="Drgn+.1.PF">{{Cite dragon+/1/Player Factions: Our Heroes?}}</ref> They would never punish someone who had not committed an evil act nor those who merely expressed immoral thoughts.<ref name="FO.OotG"/><ref name="SCAG=p85">{{Cite book/Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide|85}}</ref>"
> — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/order-of-the-gauntlet/wiki.txt:53` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Order of the Gauntlet*, citing RoT-p12, a Dragon+ article, FO.OotG and SCAG p.85). The first sentences conflict with the campaign's warrant-first method; see Section 6, C4.

> "Refuse to take part in or cover up an atrocity, even on faction orders (+1).** A character who holds a moral line and can explain why to Savra earns her respect in a way tactical victories cannot."
> — `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:53` (a renown mechanic on the Factions Guide page; the tenet is only the second sentence, "A character who holds a moral line and can explain why to Savra earns her respect in a way tactical victories cannot.")

**T4. Work through the law, with paper a magistrate would read.**

> "- A paper a magistrate would read, such as a ledger, a signed statement or a witness who will swear on the record. Savra cannot file on a compelled answer alone, and she asks the party to bring her paper."
> — `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:21`

> "Order of the Gauntlet agents may not enter a place of worship without a magistrate's warrant, which is what stops Savra in **The Shrine on Aveen Street**, and the design notes for that event call the rule invented."
> — `campaign/setting/governance-and-guilds/code-legal-and-magisters.md:57` (the same line opens "Campaign-only rules:"; the rule is campaign-only)

**T5. Honesty, and no managing around people.**

> "- Honesty. Savra cannot work with people she is managing around, and she knows when she is being managed."
> — `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:23`

> "**Persona.** To the world, Savra is a knight in her late twenties who looks older, dark hair worn back, strong jaw, the measured manner of someone who has learned to think before speaking. To anyone who asks her sincerely, she says that she spent three years in the Howling Hatred cult and left it in 1491 DR, about a year ago, and that the Order took her in afterward. She tells what she did in it only to a member she has come to trust. Deep down, her goodness is hard-won in a way that makes it more convincing, and she cannot work with people she is managing around."
> — `campaign/setting/notable-figures/order-of-the-gauntlet/01-savra-belabranta.md:21`

> "**Motivations.** Savra wants to atone for her time in the Howling Hatred cult, root out the infernal cults she hunts wherever they hide in Waterdeep, find out what the Cassalanters are concealing behind their charity, and build an Order chapter that values honesty above all."
> — `campaign/setting/notable-figures/order-of-the-gauntlet/01-savra-belabranta.md:15`

**T6. Atonement through service (stated of Savra; no source states it as a body-wide Order rule).**

> "Savra is trying to regain her honor by serving Tyr, thus atoning for the evil acts she committed as a member of an evil elemental cult called the Howling Hatred."
> — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:3950`

> "Some time later, Savra repented from her cultist days and became a member of the [[Order of the Gauntlet]]. Fully reformed, she began to serve [[Tyr]] in an attempt to atone for the evil acts she committed and to regain her honor."
> — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/order-of-the-gauntlet/wiki.txt:1152` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Savra Belabranta*, WDH5e-p39)

> "She has channeled that shame into an iron commitment to justice."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/04-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:20`

**T7. Diabolism first.**

> "The Order keeps a fixed order of threats: diabolism first, aberrant evil second, everything else after that."
> — `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:7`

> "The Order has a particular interest in the Cassalanters (active Asmodeus cultists conducting devil-bargain rituals) and in Xanathar's Guild (whose use of intellect devourers and enslaved creatures they consider an abomination)."
> — `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:406` (Alexandrian; the Asmodeus wording conflicts with campaign ruling 7; see Section 6, C1)

**T8. Stay outside the Grand Game and hunt the specific evil.**

> "The Order steps outside the Grand Game entirely. It does not position for the vault or compete for faction influence. It hunts specific evil, and if the party helps find it, the Order throws its full weight behind them when it matters."
> — `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:11`

**T9. Share the haul with a worthy cause.**

> "The Order also expects its knights to live on less than they earn, and it asks them to give a quarter of their share of a large haul to a cause that looks after people who cannot look after themselves."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/04-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:16` (the mechanic and its renown are in `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:52`; see Section 5)

**T10. Groups, not lone agents (sources conflict; see Section 6, C3).**

> "An individual can be a member of the faction and also a member of the clergy or a knight pledged to a specific god or temple. Members of the order act alone or in small groups. Some are Waterdavian natives; others hail from distant settlements and have come to the city on temple business."
> — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1405`

> "It was very uncommon to see a lone member of the Order as they almost always traveled in groups."
> — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/order-of-the-gauntlet/wiki.txt:55`

**T11. Temple friendship: the Order works with the temples and tests no one's faith.**

> "- **The Order's three temples:** The Order of the Gauntlet treats the Halls of Justice, the Temple of Helm and the Chapel of the Steadfast Shield as friendly to its members. Worship of Helm, Torm or Tyr only decides whom Savra addresses first, and nobody is tested for faith at the door."
> — `campaign/setting/deities/00-the-deities.md:401`

> "- **Campaign ties:** The Order of the Gauntlet and its temple benefits. The published *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* says that "Any non-evil character can join the Order of the Gauntlet in Waterdeep. The Trollskull Alley table says only that "worship of Helm, Torm or Tyr only decides whom Savra addresses first". The details are in the GM Notes below."

## 3. Grand Game Agenda and Overview

### Grand Game Agenda (existing page line 29)

> "The Order will move aggressively against the Cassalanters once they have solid evidence of whatever the family is hiding. Characters who uncover that evidence and share it with Savra gain a formidable ally in the endgame. Savra goes well out of her way for knights who give generously, and she stops offering favors to a knight who keeps the whole of a large haul. She does not raise it again."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/04-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:29`

Source of the first two sentences: `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:487`: "The Order will move aggressively against the Cassalanters once they have solid evidence of diabolism. PCs who uncover that evidence and share it gain both renown and a formidable ally in the endgame." The remix swapped "diabolism" for "whatever the family is hiding" (secrecy rule).

Third and fourth sentences: `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:487` ("...characters who give generously find Savra going well out of her way to support them; characters who pocket everything are quietly reassigned to less sensitive operations."). The "favors" sentence is a mechanic (Section 5).

**Duplication with the Factions Guide.** The Agenda repeats no Grand Game Stance sentence word for word. The closest Stance sentence is `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:9`: "She has been building toward a confrontation she knows will come. The party changes this." No duplicate to list.

**Tension within the campaign.** `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:11` says the Order "does not position for the vault or compete for faction influence" (T8). Agenda line 29 says a party that shares evidence gains "a formidable ally in the endgame". Flag only.

### What the faction wants from the Stone and the vault

> "The vault gold concerns Savra only as a function of who might seize it. Her fear is that 500,000 gold would fund the thing she suspects the Sea Ward family of doing, and she cannot yet say what that is. In the Splinter's or Xanathar's hands, it would be nearly as bad. In the city treasury, she has no objection."
> — `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:7`

> "Order speakers say \"the Splinter\" or \"the other cell\" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener, and Savra keeps Davil's Doom Raiders apart from the Splinter. They say \"the carnival fleet\" until **Jarlaxle Unmasked** is marked. Savra says \"the family\" until a player names the Cassalanters, and then she adds that she has no proof. Nobody in the Order knows what the family worships, and nobody names a pact."
> — `campaign/guides/factions/05-order-of-the-gauntlet.md:13`
