## 1. Tenets

**Keep the natural order; oppose what disrupts it**

> "The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers, and survivalists who work to keep civilization and the wild in balance."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:16`

> "The city is built on nature. We remember that. It would be good if everyone did."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:281` (the epigraph)

> "Balance is a better word. What we watch for are disruptions — things that shouldn't be where they are, pulling at what keeps the world from unraveling. Sometimes that is in a forest. Sometimes it is in a city. Sometimes it is in a sewer beneath a city, if the sewer happens to contain a beholder who has been expanding his territory for twenty years."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:331` (Melannor, at the garden bench)

> "The Enclave's Grand Game goal is containment. Whatever the Vault of Dragons wakes up, Jeryth wants it handled before it reaches the surface."
— `campaign/guides/factions/04-emerald-enclave.md:11`

> "- The order of nature must be preserved in all its iterations.
> - Any force that would disrupt that order had to be defeated."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:57-58` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, "Beliefs": the Elder Circle's "Greatest Tenets Number Three", citing *Baldur's Gate III*; Vilhon order; edition not stated)

**Aberrant and corrupting things draw the Enclave's attention**

> "It still watches anything that upsets the balance of the region, and a beholder crime lord building an empire in the sewers is exactly the aberrant corruption that draws its attention."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:16`

> "**Neutralize an aberrant creature that threatens the city or its environs (+1).** Grells in the harbor, chuul in the cisterns, undead walking in a cemetery, or any unnatural threat to Waterdeep's living fabric. Once per significant creature or infestation cleared."
— `campaign/guides/factions/04-emerald-enclave.md:49` (a mechanic line; quoted as a tenet source only)

**Warn first, then act; prefer the animal to the knife**

> "The Emerald Enclave had a well-established pattern of action when dealing with those violating nature. Firstly, the Enclave issued a simple warning to those deemed guilty. If the warning was ignored, the Emerald Enclave acted swiftly and decisively, stopping the wrongdoers by any means."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:104` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, "Tactics"; Vilhon order; cites *The Vilhon Reach* Player's Guide p.5; edition not stated)

> "**Return a dangerous or corrupted animal to safety rather than killing it (+1).** One award per incident in which the party makes a real effort to resolve a creature problem without killing. Melannor must be told for the renown to count."
— `campaign/guides/factions/04-emerald-enclave.md:51` (a mechanic line; quoted as a tenet source only)

**Take no side in the vault gold; help the loyal**

> "The Enclave has no stake in Neverember's gold and makes no demands about the Vault or its contents. Jeryth Phaulkon holds that the question belongs to the Lords, and the Enclave follows her. Its help is personal and magical rather than institutional, and it is given freely to those who prove loyal."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:18`

> "The Emerald Enclave actually has no interest in the Grand Game or Neverember's ill-gotten dragons. They'll assist PCs who have proven loyal to the faction but, perhaps refreshingly, will make no demands upon them for the Vault or its contents."
— `sources/3. Player Character Factions.pdf` PDF page 2 (printed p.24), transcribed by eye

> "Jeryth gives the same answer to anyone who asks: "That question is for the Lords to settle.""
— `campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:33`

**Aid to the injured, freely given**

> "The Enclave would always provide aid to anyone left fatigued or suffering injury."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:59` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, "Beliefs"; Vilhon order; cites *Baldur's Gate III*; edition not stated)

> "If you join us, this place is yours as a refuge. No questions asked, no favors owed for it — that is not how we work. What we ask instead is that you pay attention to what is being disrupted, and that you act on what you see. That is the whole of it."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:343` (Jeryth, at the gate)

**Sanctuary: no faction violates the gate**

> "No faction has ever openly violated the sanctuary."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:20`

**Silence about the wider game until a name is earned**

> "Enclave speakers say "the Splinter" or "the other cell" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for the listener."
— `campaign/guides/factions/04-emerald-enclave.md:13`

> "No Enclave speaker names Jarlaxle, Zardoz Zord, or Bregan D'aerthe to players."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:42`

**A united front in public** (Vilhon; `[research]` for the Waterdeep order)

> "Lastly, despite the Enclave's true feelings and conflicts, they always presented themselves as a united front to the outside world. Any disagreements were always only voiced internally and never outside the Oakenhall of Ilighôn."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:87` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, "Activities"; [ref tag removed]; cites *The Vilhon Reach* DMG Reference p.26)

**Post-Spellplague creed** (wiki)

> "The Emerald Enclave of the post-Spellplague era believed that the natural order had to be respected and preserved and that forces that would upset that natural order should be destroyed."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:89` (cites the *Factions: The Emerald Enclave* web page; [ref tag removed]; edition not stated; the sentence continues "They were also to keep elemental forces in check and prevent the wilderness and civilization from wiping out one another.")

**Doctrine and Rites** (the campaign gives only the join message and the charm) `[research]`

> "Initiates were required to serve neither good nor evil, nor to serve any other organization (apart from the churches of Eldath, Mielikki, or Silvanus), to be innocent of crimes against nature, and to have performed at least one significant deed that benefited nature in the Vilhon Reach. The rites of initiation were held each full moon at the [[Oakenhall]], which was the House of Silvanus at the heart of Ilighôn."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:101` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, "Initiation"; wikitext link markup kept; [ref tag removed]; cites *The Vilhon Reach* DMG Reference p.26; Vilhon order)

The campaign's join rite is the white cat's message and the charm: "Interested in joining the Emerald Enclave? Come meet us at Phaulkonmere in the Southern Ward. The gate will be open." (`campaign/guides/factions/04-emerald-enclave.md:37`) and the *charm of restoration* at Springwarden (`campaign/guides/factions/04-emerald-enclave.md:39`).

## 3. Grand Game Agenda and Overview

### Grand Game Agenda (existing text, verbatim, org page lines 31-33)

> "The Emerald Enclave has no interest in the gold, and its aid carries no hidden price. Jeryth gives the same answer to anyone who asks: "That question is for the Lords to settle." The Enclave will not hide the gold, seize it, or help anyone who does. Characters who need a refuge, a reliable spellcaster, or one relationship in the city that is not angling for something will find the Enclave invaluable in the campaign's later acts."
