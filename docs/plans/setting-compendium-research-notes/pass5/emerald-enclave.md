## 1. Tenets

**Keep the natural order; oppose what disrupts it** (campaign and Alexandrian sources; wiki)

> "The Emerald Enclave is an organization of druids, rangers, and survivalists who work to keep civilization and the wild in balance."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:16`

> "The city is built on nature. We remember that. It would be good if everyone did."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:281` (the epigraph)

> "Balance is a better word. What we watch for are disruptions — things that shouldn't be where they are, pulling at what keeps the world from unraveling. Sometimes that is in a forest. Sometimes it is in a city. Sometimes it is in a sewer beneath a city, if the sewer happens to contain a beholder who has been expanding his territory for twenty years."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:331` (Melannor, in the scene at the garden bench)

> "The Enclave's Grand Game goal is containment. Whatever the Vault of Dragons wakes up, Jeryth wants it handled before it reaches the surface."
— `campaign/guides/factions/04-emerald-enclave.md:11`

> "- The order of nature must be preserved in all its iterations.
> - Any force that would disrupt that order had to be defeated."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:57-58` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, "Beliefs"; "the Greatest Tenets Number Three" of the Elder Circle; cites *Baldur's Gate III*; edition not stated; Vilhon order)

**Aberrant and corrupting things draw the Enclave's attention** (campaign)

> "a beholder crime lord building an empire in the sewers is exactly the aberrant corruption that draws its attention."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:16` (the sentence continues "...". The full sentence: "It still watches anything that upsets the balance of the region, and a beholder crime lord building an empire in the sewers is exactly the aberrant corruption that draws its attention.")

> "Neutralize an aberrant creature that threatens the city or its environs (+1). Grells in the harbor, chuul in the cisterns, undead walking in a cemetery, or any unnatural threat to Waterdeep's living fabric. Once per significant creature or infestation cleared."
— `campaign/guides/factions/04-emerald-enclave.md:49` (a mechanic line; quoted as a tenet source only)

**Speak little and act on what is seen; no grand displays** (wiki, Vilhon)

> "The Emerald Enclave had a well-established pattern of action when dealing with those violating nature. Firstly, the Enclave issued a simple warning to those deemed guilty. If the warning was ignored, the Emerald Enclave acted swiftly and decisively, stopping the wrongdoers by any means."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:104` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, "Tactics"; cites *The Vilhon Reach* Player's Guide p.5; edition not stated)

> "Return a dangerous or corrupted animal to safety rather than killing it (+1). One award per incident in which the party makes a real effort to resolve a creature problem without killing. Melannor must be told for the renown to count."
— `campaign/guides/factions/04-emerald-enclave.md:51` (a mechanic line; quoted as a tenet source only)

> "The Emerald Enclave actually has no interest in the Grand Game or Neverember's ill-gotten dragons. They'll assist PCs who have proven loyal to the faction but, perhaps refreshingly, will make no demands upon them for the Vault or its contents."

> "That question is for the Lords to settle."

**Post-Spellplague creed** (wiki)

> "The Emerald Enclave of the post-Spellplague era believed that the natural order had to be respected and preserved and that forces that would upset that natural order should be destroyed. — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:89` (cites the *Factions: The Emerald Enclave* web page; [ref tag removed]; edition not stated; the sentence continues with "They also accepted that the wilderness could be harsh, and some could not survive it without aid, so they helped.")

The rites of initiation were held each full moon at the [[Oakenhall]], which was the House of Silvanus at the heart of Ilighôn."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/emerald-enclave/wiki.txt:101` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Emerald Enclave*, "Initiation"; [ref tag removed]; cites *The Vilhon Reach* DMG Reference p.26; Vilhon order)

## 5. Mechanics on the existing page

The org page names no renown value, no rank benefit and no mission title. Renown appears nowhere on the page. The mechanic-style lines follow.

**(a) Gamemaster's Summary, "Mission delivery" bullet (delete per brief step 2)**
> "- **Mission delivery:** *Animal Messenger*. A Tiny Beast finds the recipient, speaks Melannor's message once in his baritone, and leaves."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/03-emerald-enclave.md:7`
Factions Guide: the delivery method and the Summerstrider limit:
> "Once per tenday, Melannor sends a message of 25 words or fewer by *animal messenger* to a place he has visited. The reply comes back the same way."

Faction-wide rule:
> "**Mission delivery.** Unless noted, missions arrive via the faction's preferred method: paper birds for Harpers, flying snakes for Doom Raiders, animal messengers for the Enclave, *Sending* spells for Force Grey."
