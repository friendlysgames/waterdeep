## 1. Tenets

**Paranoia: anyone who knows more than the beholder is removed**

> Like all beholders, {@creature Xanathar|WDH} is a paranoid tyrant that charms and bullies its minions into servitude. The Xanathar Guild is made up of some of Waterdeep's most disreputable folk, as well as monsters forced into subservience or drawn to the beholder by the promise of treasure, food, or power. Treachery within the ranks of the guild is common as servants vie for the beholder's favor and affection. Such boons are fleeting, though, as the beholder is quick to distrust those who finagle their way into its good graces.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:32757`

> The beholder is caught up in the unbreakable grip of its own paranoia. It sees enemies everywhere, and lashes out at anyone it suspects of being a Zhentarim spy or assassin. Adventurers who attract its attention by dealing with known or suspected Black Network operatives are quickly branded as enemies that must be destroyed.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:32761`

> The Guild keeps continuity by eliminating anyone who knows more than the current Xanathar. What remains is a surveillance network with no institutional memory beyond what the current beholder chooses to believe.
— `campaign/setting/history/02-the-long-shadow.md:40`

**Fear and loyalty through power; trust is not understood**

> Xanathar is a beholder, paranoid, impulsive, and absolute in its conviction that everyone around it is either a tool or a threat. It holds territory through fear and loyalty through power, two things it understands. Trust, it doesn't understand at all.
— `campaign/setting/organizations/08-xanathars-guild.md:12`

**Membership is a serious crime**

> Anyone can join the Xanathar Guild, which, despite its name, doesn't have official guild status in Waterdeep. Before membership is granted, however, an applicant must pass a test that always involves the perpetration of a serious crime. Possibilities include murdering a guild member who has failed {@creature Xanathar|WDH} in some fashion, kidnapping a Waterdavian citizen, collecting a ransom, robbing a hire-coach, or looting a warehouse.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1423`

> To join, a member had to prove their ruthlessness, obedience, and loyalty by committing a serious crime—killing or selling a friend or family member into slavery—as well as paying 300 [[gp]].
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/xanathars-guild/wiki.txt:95` (ref tags omitted; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, cites *Expedition to Undermountain* p. 12; edition not tagged. The wiki's account of a past period; lower-ranked than the adventure line above.)

**Sylgar comes first**

> Its most visible eccentricity is Sylgar, its pet goldfish. Xanathar's attachment to the fish is total and terrifying. The Guild's agents who let anything bad happen to Sylgar can expect to be eaten. This is not a metaphor.
— `campaign/setting/organizations/08-xanathars-guild.md:14`

> The only creature {@creature Xanathar|WDH} truly cares about aside from itself is a fish, named {@creature Sylgar|wdh}, that it keeps in a large glass tank.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:32759`

> Anything involving Sylgar — Xanathar's goldfish — brings the faction directly to Lockdown without warning, regardless of the current tier. No prior incident is required. No warning shot follows.
— `campaign/guides/factions/09-xanathars-guild.md:77`

**Watchers are placed on every agent**

> Any member given an assignment would receive a bodyguard or assistant, such as a [[bugbear]], [[wererat]], [[kenku]], or [[gazer]], with secret instructions to spy on and assassinate the agent in case of failure.
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/xanathars-guild/wiki.txt:98` (ref tags omitted; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Xanathar's Thieves' Guild*, cites *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* p. 17, 5e)

> {@creature Xanathar|WDH} might send a {@creature gazer|vgm} (see {@adventure appendix B|WDH|12}) to help (and spy on) an up-and-coming guild member.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1432`

**Escalates on ambiguity; no intermediate tier**

> Xanathar's paranoia means its response to ambiguity is escalation. A single threat to an outpost pushes the faction directly from Suspicious to Alert, skipping the intermediate tier.
— `campaign/guides/factions/09-xanathars-guild.md:67`

**Hostile to outside overtures (the envoys)**

> Xanathar received the envoys in its throne room, listened to their proposal, and ate them. It kept the Eye and has never learned what it is.
— `campaign/structure/arc-f-xanathars-lair.md:9`

**Disposes of underlings it no longer trusts**

> {@creature Xanathar|WDH} has turned this room into a gladiatorial arena. It also uses this location to dispose of underlings it no longer trusts in a manner that it considers entertaining.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:17154`

**Treasure as proof of supremacy, not wealth (conflicts with the adventure; see C1)**

> It wants the gold as proof of supremacy and of its rule over the underworld, not primarily for wealth.
— `campaign/setting/villains/xanathar.md:61`

## 3. Overview

Existing text, `campaign/setting/organizations/08-xanathars-guild.md:40`:

> Xanathar's Guild is Waterdeep's largest criminal syndicate, run by a paranoid beholder from a fortified lair below the city. The Guild controls access to Skullport's smuggling networks, gladiatorial circuits, and a web of informants embedded throughout the wards. Its field operations are coordinated by Ahmaergo, and its three city outposts — a healer's clinic, a black market armorer, and a sweetshop front — form the chain that leads investigators toward the lair.
