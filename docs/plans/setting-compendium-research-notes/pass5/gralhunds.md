## 1. Day-one lore (as of Ches 1, 1492 DR)

**Who the house is**

> "The North Ward is the quiet residential ward in the northeast of Waterdeep. The party's tavern, Trollskull Manor, stands here, and so does the Gralhund family's villa on Saerdoun Street."
> — `campaign/gazetteer/waterdeep/north-ward.md:7`

> "The Gralhunds are a minor faction (at least compared to the four main villains) involved in the Grand Game. They simply hope to obtain the Vault for themselves, and use its riches to elevate their position in Waterdeep's high society."
> — `2. Other Factions.pdf`, p. 20

> "Many dismiss the Gralhunds as petty schemers, but the truth is that they have had to be clever and conniving in order to punch above their weight-class in Waterdeep's labyrinthine politics. Even now, as they play the Grand Game, they are struggling to compete with organizations that are far larger and have far more resources."
> — `2. Other Factions.pdf`, p. 20

> "House Gralhund (...) was a noble house of Waterdeep since the Year of the Broken Blade, 1260 DR. Their primary business interests included contracting hireswords and manufacturing weapons."
> — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/gralhunds/wiki.txt:29` (Gralhund, Forgotten Realms Wiki, edition not stated; "(...)" marks removal of the pronunciation template)

**Standing and the Masked Lord grievance**

> "Several Masked Lords were assassinated in quick succession some years ago. Yalah Gralhund expected a vacancy, was passed over, and wants a seat. House Gralhund joined Manshoon's Zhentarim partly to learn the Lords' identities and weaknesses. In the published adventure's Xanathar version, Yalah offers to return the Stone to it if it helps her create a vacancy. The Alexandrian files date the killings to 1491 DR and call them the Lord Murders."
> — `campaign/setting/governance-and-guilds/open-lord-and-masked-lords.md:65` (the 1491 date is checked in Section 6 C13)

> "When several of the Masked Lords were assassinated in quick succession some years ago, Lord Gralhund had expected his wife to fill one of the vacancies. That never happened, though, despite many promises and bribes. After she was passed over, Orond became insanely angry, and he has remained that way ever since."
> — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:32444`

> "When in 1491 DR many members of the Lords of Waterdeep were killed in a nefarious plot, Orond expected that his wife would fill one of the vacancies but that never happened, despite all the bribes and promises. Orond became deeply angry about this."

**Agents in Renaer Neverember's household**

> "Thinking outside of the box, they embedded agents in Renaer Neverember's household months ago. Because Renaer was estranged from his father, they knew it was a long-shot, but it paid off: The agents spotted Dalakhar's surveillance of Renaer and identified him as an agent of Lord Neverember. When Dalakhar was abruptly pulled off of that assignment, the agents followed a hunch, followed up, and discovered he had ended up in Xanathar's organization (presumably still on orders from Lord Neverember). The Gralhunds didn't know that Xanathar held the Stone of Golorr, but when it was stolen they were among the first to suspect (and then know) that Dalakhar was responsible."
> — `2. Other Factions.pdf`, p. 20

> "GM Tip: Remember that the Gralhunds also have agents in Renaer Neverember's home. If the PCs keep Renaer in the loop about their activities, those agents become a potential vector of information back to the Gralhunds."
> — `7. Other Response Teams.pdf`, p. 57

## 2. Tenets (candidate beliefs and habits)

Every tenet is sourced. Where a source is thin, the tenet says so.

- **"We see both sides."** The house keeps more than one arrangement open and uses each to read the others.
  > "We see both sides."
  > — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/gralhunds/wiki.txt:31`
  > "So while the Zhents use House Gralhund as a shield, the Gralhunds are using the Zhents to ascertain the identities and weaknesses of the Masked Lords."
  > — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:32445`

- **Information is currency.** The house buys intelligence from inside other organisations and from its own staff.
  > "Thinking outside of the box, they embedded agents in Renaer Neverember's household months ago."
  > — `2. Other Factions.pdf`, p. 20
  > "Skeemo is the Doom Raiders' Master of Magic, a traitor feeding intelligence to Manshoon's cell and House Gralhund, and a colleague Davil was fond of, which Davil does not say aloud."
  > — `campaign/setting/notable-figures/doom-raiders/04-skeemo-weirdbottle.md:25` (a Doom Raiders figure, cited here for the Gralhund purchase)
  > "Skeemo can add \"sellout\" to his credentials, his services having been bought by House Gralhund and the Black Network operatives loyal to {@creature Manshoon|WDH}."
  > — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:31945`

- **Take the seat, by any lever.** Yalah wants a Masked Lord's seat and plans to bribe, blackmail or extort her way in.
  > "Once she knows the identities and secrets of enough Masked Lords, Yalah is confident that she can bribe, blackmail, or extort her way into their ranks."
  > — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:32781`
  > "Yalah wants to become a Masked Lord through bribery, blackmail, or extortion, secure House Gralhund's prosperity for generations, and maintain control of the Zhentarim resources housed at her villa without Manshoon discovering she considers Urstul an underling."

- **Resentment of the Lords who passed her over.**
  > "**Emotions.** Yalah most often feels calculating ambition, controlled composure, sharp-eyed assessment of useful people, patient disdain for her husband's tantrums, possessive determination about House Gralhund's future, and banked fury at the Masked Lords who passed her over."

  > "Orond became deeply angry about this."
