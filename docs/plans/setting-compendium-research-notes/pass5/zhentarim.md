## 1. Tenets

**One true Manshoon; clones believe it**

> Deep down, he claims to be the one true Manshoon, as all surviving clones do.
— `campaign/setting/notable-figures/manshoons-zhentarim/01-manshoon.md:22` (the same line opens: "To the world, Manshoon does not exist; he is never seen entering or leaving Kolat Towers, using a teleportation circle for all movement.")

> Each of the clones was convinced that he was the real Manshoon and was inexplicably compelled to slay his rival clones.
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/zhentarim/wiki.txt:458` (ref tags omitted; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Manshoon*, cites *Cloak & Dagger* p. 13 for the Manshoon Wars; the 1370 DR event; edition not tagged on the wiki)

**Capture, do not kill; prisoners answer questions**

> He prefers to capture rather than kill (prisoners answer questions; corpses do not), and this habit creates tactical openings the party can exploit.
— `campaign/setting/notable-figures/manshoons-zhentarim/01-manshoon.md:24`

> Any Zhentarim team sent to attack the characters carries orders to take them as prisoners to the Interrogation House on Brindul Alley. ... Manshoon wants intelligence, not body counts, and underestimated enemies are more useful to him than dead ones.
— `campaign/guides/factions/10-manshoons-zhentarim.md:9` (the "..." is the sentence "Questioned team members can point the characters toward the house. The lighter roster is intentional." which the drafter should quote in full if used)

Counter-tenet in the same bundle: "This version of {@creature Manshoon|WDH} isn't spoiling for a fight. He commends the characters for making it this far and shows no concern for the Zhents they defeated to reach him, since he considers all his followers expendable." (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:30255`). The campaign's Factions Guide and NF say capture. The published adventure says he treats followers as expendable. Both are recorded; see C2.

**Stay hidden; no visible guards; the simulacrum takes the blow**

> {@creature Manshoon|WDH} creates copies of himself using the {@spell simulacrum} spell and takes great pains to conceal his identity, since his success hinges on not attracting the attention of others who would seek to thwart him before his plans come to fruition.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:561`

> {@creature Manshoon|WDH} posts no visible guards outside Kolat Towers, since he wants others to believe the site is abandoned.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:26798`

> The Zhentarim response teams are the weakest in raw muscle of any faction. This is deliberate. Drawing too much attention is the one mistake Manshoon won't make twice.
— `campaign/setting/organizations/09-manshoons-zhentarim.md:18`

**Blackmail for political leverage**

> Two Masked Lords on the Council are already compromised — Corylus Thann through scandal, Jelenn Urmbrusk through debt — and voting Manshoon's way without knowing why their interests have aligned.
— `campaign/setting/organizations/09-manshoons-zhentarim.md:16`

> The ledger doesn't reveal that Corylus Thann and Jelenn Urmbrusk are Masked Lords, or that {@creature Manshoon|WDH}'s blackmail of them also forces them to use their influence to cover up or dismiss crimes perpetrated by {@creature Manshoon|WDH}'s forces in Waterdeep.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:30298`

**Profit-first mercenary company; sabotage for competitors (campaign-era)**

> The Zhentarim is a shadow organization that trades mercenaries and goods (including weapons) for profit. It has long sought to gain political influence in Waterdeep, but the strength of the city's Masked Lords, nobility, and professional guilds makes that difficult.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1444`

> They were not above resorting to sabotage, blackmail, and arson to outright murder to undermine their mercantile or political opponents.
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/zhentarim/wiki.txt:67` (ref tags omitted; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Zhentarim*, cites *Lords of Darkness* p. 97; edition not tagged. Moonsea-era account; lower-ranked.)

**Loyalty to Manshoon alone; members do not name him**

> She does not willingly reveal who has the Stone unless magically compelled.

> Characters say "the Splinter" until **Manshoon Named** is marked for them, and only then connect her to Manshoon or to Kolat Towers (**Smoke in the Tower**).
— `campaign/setting/notable-figures/manshoons-zhentarim/09-vira-solkan.md:21` (this is the cell's naming rule, not a member's belief)
