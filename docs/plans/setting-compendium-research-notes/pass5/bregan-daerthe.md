## 1. Tenets

**Loyalty to the leader first, the company second**

> Jarlaxle's loyalties are to himself first and foremost, and to Bregan D'aerthe secondarily.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:32182`

> The group's leader, {@creature Jarlaxle Baenre|WDH}, is always looking for new members to fill the ranks, and loyalty is what matters to him most.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1297`

> To the world, Jarlaxle is Captain Zardoz Zord, a flamboyant human sea captain running the Sea Maidens Faire carnival. To those he trusts, he is the secret lord of Luskan, the commander of Bregan D'aerthe, and a drow iconoclast whose loyalties run to himself first and his operatives second.
— `campaign/setting/notable-figures/bregan-daerthe/01-jarlaxle-baenre.md:21`

**Non-lethal first; leverage before force**

> Bregan D'aerthe teams favor misdirection over lethal force. Jarlaxle's preference is non-lethal resolution — confused enemies are more useful to him than dead ones.
— `campaign/guides/factions/08-bregan-daerthe.md:77`

> He reaches for leverage before force.
— `campaign/setting/notable-figures/bregan-daerthe/01-jarlaxle-baenre.md:23` (the same line opens with a sentence that conflicts; see C6.)

**Information is the currency; sell to both sides**

> Bregan D'aerthe was also an information broker, willing to provide information for both sides of a conflict—for the right price. Its contacts reached even as far as the [[svirfneblin]] city of [[Blingdenstone]].
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/bregan-daerthe/wiki.txt:34` (ref tags omitted; Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Bregan D'aerthe*; the wiki's refs cite *Homeland* and *Menzoberranzan: The Houses*, edition not tagged)

> He acquires information, sells it selectively, positions operatives in every major faction, and acts decisively only when the odds are already arranged in his favor.
— `campaign/setting/organizations/07-bregan-daerthe.md:20`

**Infiltration: keep an asset inside each rival**

> Bregan D'aerthe is skilled at infiltrating criminal organizations. {@creature Xanathar|WDH}'s drow advisor, {@creature Nar'l Xibrindas|wdh} (see {@adventure appendix B|WDH|12}), is actually a Bregan D'aerthe spy.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1301`

> His asset inside Xanathar's organization — **Nar'l Xibrindas**, a drow mage who serves as the beholder's advisor and reports to Jarlaxle by *Sending* spell — keeps him informed of the Guild's movements.
— `campaign/guides/factions/08-bregan-daerthe.md:9`

**Cover: the Faire's parades hide illicit movement**

> The Sea Maidens Faire consists of three carnival ships (the {@i Eyecatcher}, the {@i Heartbreaker}, and the {@i Hellraiser}) crewed by disguised drow and a host of non-drow performers (musicians, acrobats, actors, and the like). The ships' cargo consists mostly of wagons and floats that can be hastily assembled and paraded through the city. The drow use these parades to draw attention away from their illicit activities.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1299`

**Recruit by watching first**

> Like the city's other factions, Bregan D'aerthe recruits quietly, watching candidates before approaching them.
— `campaign/setting/organizations/07-bregan-daerthe.md:18`

> D'aerthe spies shadow the group for several days before contact; a character who gives the Watch the surveillance report is closed out of Bregan D'aerthe for the rest of Acts I through III, and the other characters keep the offer.
— `campaign/guides/factions/08-bregan-daerthe.md:37`

**Membership: mostly male drow from destroyed houses; non-drow operatives are not members**

> Almost all Bregan D'aerthe members are male because female drow rarely condescend to take orders from a male. A female drow can earn a place in the faction by decrying the drow matriarchy and convincing Jarlaxle that she would be an asset to the brotherhood. Jarlaxle employs non-drow operatives as well, none of whom know they're working for him; such individuals aren't considered members of the faction.
— `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1298`

> Most of the band's members were males from destroyed houses, primarily fighters, thieves, and mages, with very few members having [[cleric]]al powers.
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/bregan-daerthe/wiki.txt:104` (ref tags omitted)

**Rejects Lolth (campaign rule)**

> This is where the campaign rule starts. Jarlaxle rejected Lolth, Bregan D'aerthe rejects her, and its members speak of her as the goddess they walked away from. Soluun gives Jarlaxle the devotion that a drow of the houses would give the Spider Queen.
— `campaign/setting/history/03-bregan-daerthes-long-road.md:34`

> Bregan D'aerthe has rejected Lolth as its goddess. No member worships her, keeps a shrine to her or invokes the Spider Queen with devotion, and members speak of her with contempt, as the goddess they walked away from. Do not give any company member a shrine, a prayer or a kind word for her.
— `campaign/setting/deities/lolth.md:73`

**Doctrine and Rites**

> Everything is a transaction. The only question is whether you know what you're paying.
— `campaign/setting/organizations/07-bregan-daerthe.md:16` (the org's epigraph; no other file contains it, see Section 2). The one practice the sources give is the operatives' sign language: "Members of Bregan D'aerthe use Drow Sign Language." (`sources/adventure-wdh.json:23069`). Any other creed or rite: `[research]`.

## 3. Overview

Existing text, `campaign/setting/organizations/07-bregan-daerthe.md:57`:

> Bregan D'aerthe is a mercenary company of drow exiles and surface operatives, led by Jarlaxle Baenre, running the Sea Maidens Faire carnival as cover for a city-wide intelligence network. He has positioned his people inside every major faction in Waterdeep. Joining him means working for the most capable operator in the city — one who will treat his agents well until their interests diverge from his.

Word counts (limit 21 words per sentence): sentence 1 is 29 words; sentence 2 is 11 words; sentence 3 is 25 words and reads as the closer. "every major faction" has no source (Section 2).

Public-face lines in other files:

> Captain Zardoz Zord is the flamboyant Illuskan master of the Sea Maidens Faire, a carnival that travels in three ships.
— `campaign/setting/villains/jarlaxle.md:130`

> A flamboyant Illuskan sea captain running the Sea Maidens Faire carnival out of Deepwater Harbor, theatrical, immediately charming, with a precision behind the showmanship that most people don't notice until the conversation is already over.
— `campaign/setting/notable-figures/bregan-daerthe/01-jarlaxle-baenre.md:29`

Player-safe check: the Overview names the "intelligence network" as the company's purpose. The Factions Guide states the same purpose as GM text: "Jarlaxle's purpose in Waterdeep is intelligence — what the city's power structure looks like from the inside, which Masked Lords carry real weight with the Lords' Alliance council, and what it would take to buy Luskan a seat at that table." (`campaign/guides/factions/08-bregan-daerthe.md:7`).
