## 1. Tenets

**T1. Deniable and discreet (sourced in the page and the Factions Guide).**

> "Its operations are deniable, its membership discreet, and its mandate broad: handle threats the City Watch and City Guard cannot or should not touch."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/05-force-grey.md:18`

> "Force Grey's value to the Open Lord lies partly in its deniability. Characters who resolve a problem without creating a political incident earn Vajra's appreciation in a way flashy violence doesn't. Once per quest."
> — `campaign/guides/factions/06-force-grey.md:50` (renown mechanic on the Factions Guide; the tenet is the first sentence)

> "Force Grey is allied to the Open Lord. If members of the Grey Hands or Force Grey bring the Grand Game to their attention, the Open Lord will quickly figure out what actually happened to the missing 500,000 gold dragons and she's going to want the money back."
> — `sources/3. Player Character Factions.pdf` p.24 (PDF page 2; transcribed from the page image)

**T2. Last resort, because the damage is theirs too (sourced in the wiki and the published adventure).**

> "They were called upon only as a last resort, however, since the Lords of Waterdeep tended to not be fond of their methods, namely the damage that might befall Waterdeep when they acted."
> — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/force-grey/wiki.txt:65` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Force Grey*, citing City of Splendors: Waterdeep p.37 and p.79; the wiki's "City of Splendors" is the 2e setting book)

> "They approached most problems in a brute force kind of way, causing collateral damage to property and infrastructure in their attempts to save as many lives as possible."
> — `sources/research/pass5-bundles/force-grey/wiki.txt:68` (same source)

> "Whenever Waterdeep has a problem that can't be handled by diplomats or the city's other armed forces, the Open Lord has the option to mobilize Force Grey. Such action is usually taken as a last resort, since some past members of the group have exhibited a tendency to indulge in wanton violence, causing as much damage as they ostensibly prevent."
> — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1341`

**T3. The Open Lord's cadre, with the Blackstaff choosing the team.**

**T4. Arcane threats first (the page's own framing).**

> — `campaign/guides/factions/06-force-grey.md:11`

> "Vajra's mandate is the city, and in particular the parts that arcane threats touch."

> "Her backing is not something that can be purchased or networked into. It is earned — and it comes with extraordinary expectations in return."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/05-force-grey.md:20`

Characters who complete these missions won't gain enough renown to join Force Grey yet, but they will gain something valuable: the Blackstaff's patronage."
> — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:3780` (conflicts with the page's "official cadre"; see Section 6, C2)

**T6. Share intelligence with people she trusts.**

> — `campaign/guides/factions/06-force-grey.md:15`

**T7. No performed warmth; she cares about the city.**

> "She is direct and professional. She cares about Waterdeep and the people in it, but she doesn't perform warmth she doesn't feel."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/05-force-grey.md:10`

**T8. Discretion in speech (the Splinter, Manshoon, Jarlaxle).**

> "Swear oaths to defend Waterdeep, its citizens, and its laws with their lives."

**Status and history.**

> "'''Force Grey''', otherwise known as the '''Gray Hands''', was a group of elite adventurers who worked directly for the government of the city of [[Waterdeep]]. Their job was to deal with threats to the city that [[City Guard (Waterdeep)|the Guard]] and [[City Watch (Waterdeep)|the Watch]] could not handle without risking significant loss of life."

> "The Gray Hands were answerable directly to the [[Lords of Waterdeep]].<ref name="CoS:W-p37"/> The [[Blackstaff]] gave orders to the members of Force Grey, but rarely worked with them directly unless the threat facing Waterdeep was particularly dire.<ref name="HL-p155"/> Around [[1492 DR]], the Gray Hands were under the control of Blackstaff [[Vajra Safahr]].<ref name="WDH5e-p37">{{Cite book/Waterdeep: Dragon Heist|37}}</ref>"
