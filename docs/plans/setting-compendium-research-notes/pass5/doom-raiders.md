## 3. Grand Game Agenda and Overview

### Grand Game Agenda (page line 44)

> "Davil's cell sees an enormous opportunity in the half-million dragons — efforts are underway to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep, and that money could buy the Doom Raiders a seat on the reforming Zhentarim Council. PCs who recover the gold and hand it to the Doom Raiders are effectively buying their patrons into continental leadership. This is a real choice with real consequences."
> — `campaign/setting/organizations/06-doom-raiders.md:44`

Published and Alexandrian sources for the same ground:

> "The Doom Raiders (described in appendix B) consider themselves the true Zhentarim of Waterdeep."
> — `sources/adventure-wdh.json:1447` (identity; quoted in T5)

> "The non-Manshoonian **Zhentarim** will see an enormous opportunity in the gold: Efforts are under way to reclaim and repair Zhentil Keep, and that money could be used to buy them a seat on the Zhentarim Council that's forming to do so. This could really raise the profile of the local Zhentarim network, and the PCs would basically be buying their way into the local leadership of the Black Network. (Or they could pursue this thread all the way to the ruins of Zhentil Keep if that's their predilection.)"
> — `sources/3. Player Character Factions.pdf` p.25 (PDF page 3; transcribed from the page image)

**Near-duplicate with the Factions Guide Grand Game Stance.** The Agenda's first clause repeats the Stance's wording:

> "The half-million dragons are another matter. Efforts are underway to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep, and the reforming Zhentarim Council is choosing its membership. That money could buy Davil's cell a seat at that table — genuine continental influence rather than the precarious position of a regional branch operation. Characters who recover the gold and deliver it to the Doom Raiders are buying their patrons into something much larger than one campaign."
> — `campaign/guides/factions/07-doom-raiders.md:9`

Not word-for-word. The clause "Efforts are underway to reclaim and rebuild Zhentil Keep" and the "buying their patrons" idea repeat across the two. List as a near-duplicate in Draft Notes.

**Settled ruling.** The CLAUDE.md "Sundering canon" rule says Zhentil Keep's rebuilding "hasn't happened *yet*" in 1492 DR, so the Zhentarim are still reforming a council. The page's "efforts are underway" matches it.
