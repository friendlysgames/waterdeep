### Operations (org page, lines 10-22), existing text, verbatim

> "*"We work from the shadows so that others may stand in the light."*"
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:12`

> "The Harpers are a loose confederation of bards, spies, sages, and do-gooders who have worked for centuries to preserve freedom and prevent any single entity from seizing dominion over Faerûn. They oppose tyranny, protect the vulnerable, and hoard information the way other organizations hoard gold. Their symbol — a silver harp cradled within a crescent moon — is known across the continent, though most Harper operatives are careful never to display it openly."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:14`

> "In Waterdeep, the Harpers maintain strong ties to the city's intellectual and artistic communities. Their primary concern in the current moment is the rivalry inside the Black Network and the ruthless newcomers they call the Splinter, which seems to act on Harper information. They are also quietly alert to the Stone of Golorr — they believe it contains intelligence of strategic importance and want it in their hands."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:16`

> "**The Waterdeep cell is compromised.** Agents of the Splinter have infiltrated the Harpers' local network. Information shared with Harper contacts has a meaningful chance of reaching them. The Harpers do not know who leads the Splinter or where it is based."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:18`

> "**Primary Contact:** Mirt the Moneylender — bluff, jovial sea merchant on the surface; in practice one of the most politically dangerous men in Waterdeep. Senior Harper, Masked Lord, and close confidant of Open Lord Laeral Silverhand. His manor in the Sea Ward is theoretically available for visits; in practice he is almost never home."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:20`

**Waterdeep Harper headquarters and size**

**The Moonstars splinter in Waterdeep**
> "For a few decades, there existed an offshoot branch of the Harpers, known as the [[Moonstars]]. This splinter sect acted separately from the two branches of the Harpers, operating in the sprawling metropolis of [[Waterdeep]], and at times undermining the efforts of true Waterdhavian Harpers."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/harpers/wiki.txt:72` (cites *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* 3rd edition p.275 and *City of Splendors: Waterdeep* p.73; [ref tags removed])

**The Harper Schism (1370 DR): Khelben and Laeral leave**
