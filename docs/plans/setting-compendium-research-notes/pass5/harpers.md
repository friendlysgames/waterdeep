## 1. Tenets

**Work from the shadows; information over force**

> "*"We work from the shadows so that others may stand in the light."*"
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:12`

> "Mirt will answer reasonable questions about the Harpers plainly: their Waterdeep operation is relatively small but well-connected; they work with information more than force; membership is discreet; rank and trust are earned not declared. He does not mention the Stone of Golorr, Manshoon, or the gang war's deeper origins. He is assessing, not briefing."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:115`

> "Harpers tended to work as discreetly as possible, preferring the weapons of knowledge and influence, rather than those of more violent means."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/harpers/wiki.txt:127` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, "Activities"; the sentence cites *Warriors and Priests of the Realms* pp.9-13; edition not stated)

> "Whenever possible, Harpers made most of their moves outside of public view so they could not be tracked and studied and they acted "behind the scenes" either in places of power or at opportune moments."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/harpers/wiki.txt:144` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, "Tactics"; [ref tags removed]; cites *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* 3rd edition p.274 and *Warriors and Priests of the Realms* pp.9-13)

**Hoard information; intelligence is the currency**

> "They oppose tyranny, protect the vulnerable, and hoard information the way other organizations hoard gold."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:14`

> "Information and knowledge gained is half the battle won against any foe."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/harpers/wiki.txt:154` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, "Communication"; a "Harper maxim"; cites *Dragon Magazine* 211; edition not stated)

**Oppose any single power, allies included**

> "Their overriding concern is the concentration of power in any single hand — including the Lords' Alliance, Force Grey, or the party itself."
— `campaign/guides/factions/02-harpers.md:7`

> "We resist anyone — anyone at all — who tries to seize enough power to stop being answerable to anyone else. That is the whole of the philosophy. The practical work is more varied."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:108`

> "No extreme is good. For freedom to flourish, all must be in balance: the powers of realms, the reaches of the cities and the wilderlands into each other, and the influence of one being over another."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/harpers/wiki.txt:115` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, "Harper Code", a quoted item of the Code; [ref tags removed]; cites *The Code of the Harpers* pp.8-11; edition not stated)

**Police their own; assume the cell is penetrated**

> "**The Waterdeep cell is compromised.** Agents of the Splinter have infiltrated the Harpers' local network. Information shared with Harper contacts has a meaningful chance of reaching them. The Harpers do not know who leads the Splinter or where it is based."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:18`

> "The Splinter's infiltration of the Waterdeep cell creates complications organically. The Harpers know only that the Black Network has split and that the Splinter acts on Harper information. Anything the party shares with Harper contacts can reach the Splinter, which rewards paranoid operational security."
— `campaign/guides/factions/02-harpers.md:19`

> "Harpers police their own. A Harper who hears the call of personal power can no longer hear the sweet song of the harp. A Harper who seizes power, and holds it above all else, is a traitor to the harp. Traitors must die, for freedom to live."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/harpers/wiki.txt:118` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, "Harper Code"; [ref tags removed]; cites *The Code of the Harpers* pp.8-11; edition not stated)

> "The Harpers of Waterdeep have been infiltrated by Zhentarim double-agents loyal to Manshoon."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:48` (inside an HTML advice box)

**Moral clarity: no harm to innocents on faction orders**

> "**Refuse to act against civilians on faction orders (+1).** A character who declines a mission that would require harming innocents — and explains why — earns quiet respect. The Harpers value moral clarity alongside operational effectiveness."
— `campaign/guides/factions/02-harpers.md:52` (a mechanic line, quoted as a tenet source only)

> "The Harpers value moral clarity alongside operational effectiveness. A character who declines a mission that would require harming innocents — and explains why — earns quiet respect, not punishment."
— `sources/Appendix_B_-_Player_Factions.md:138`

> "They were expected to help one another without hesitation or concern for cost and to ask nothing in return for the works they did."
— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/harpers/wiki.txt:120` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, "Harper Code", summary paragraph; [ref tags removed]; cites *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* 3rd edition p.274 and others)

— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/harpers/wiki.txt:100` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, "Attitude"; [ref tags removed]; cites *The Code of the Harpers* pp.8-11 and pp.12-13; edition not stated)

— `sources/research/pass5-bundles/harpers/wiki.txt:302` (Forgotten Realms Wiki, *Harpers*, "Long Years"; a block quote attributed to "Anonymous", citing *Dragon Magazine* 430; edition not stated; the wiki line ends with a stray apostrophe)

## 2. Operations and Key Members

### Operations (org page, lines 10-22), existing text

> "*"We work from the shadows so that others may stand in the light."*"
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:12`

> "The Harpers are a loose confederation of bards, spies, sages, and do-gooders who have worked for centuries to preserve freedom and prevent any single entity from seizing dominion over Faerûn. They oppose tyranny, protect the vulnerable, and hoard information the way other organizations hoard gold. Their symbol — a silver harp cradled within a crescent moon — is known across the continent, though most Harper operatives are careful never to display it openly."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:14`

> "In Waterdeep, the Harpers maintain strong ties to the city's intellectual and artistic communities. Their primary concern in the current moment is the rivalry inside the Black Network and the ruthless newcomers they call the Splinter, which seems to act on Harper information. They are also quietly alert to the Stone of Golorr — they believe it contains intelligence of strategic importance and want it in their hands."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:16`

> "**The Waterdeep cell is compromised.** Agents of the Splinter have infiltrated the Harpers' local network. Information shared with Harper contacts has a meaningful chance of reaching them. The Harpers do not know who leads the Splinter or where it is based."
— `campaign/setting/organizations/01-harpers.md:18`

> "**Primary Contact:** Mirt the Moneylender — bluff, jovial sea merchant on the surface; in practice one of the most politically dangerous men in Waterdeep. Senior Harper, Masked Lord, and close confidant of Open Lord Laeral Silverhand. His manor in the Sea Ward is theoretically available for visits; in practice he is almost never home."

> "The Harpers will happily allow the PCs to keep Neverember's gold. What they want is the Stone of Golorr.
