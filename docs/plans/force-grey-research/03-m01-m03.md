### 4.4 The Occupying Devourer: how M3 must use it (MR §5)

Binding references: stat block MR :249-294; Extraction Procedure MR :305-386; level-4 audit MR :411-418.

- **Hosted body.** Meloon uses the **Warrior Veteran** stat block (MR :418, :243). The devourer keeps Int/Wis/Cha, telepathy 60 ft and Detect Intelligence (300 ft), has Total Cover while inside, and Hold 3 (MR :276-280).
- **Detection (MR :309-319).** Insight DC 15 on speech; DC 14 on fishing questions; five minutes of study, DC 12 Arcana or Medicine; *Detect Thoughts* works but alerts the devourer (it acts at once: attack, Slip Out or flee); *Detect Evil and Good* senses the Aberration through the host and does not alert it. PREV's Day 7 text matches this table (PREV ev-01:146-156). **Gap:** PREV says *detect magic* "shows nothing, because the occupation is not a spell"; Azuredge is a magic item and would show. Say *detect magic* shows no spell on Meloon (only the axe).
- **Hold and Break.** A Break lowers Hold by 1; at 0 the devourer is expelled; Hold returns to 3 an hour after the last Break (MR :307). Breaks stack across sources and characters.
  - *Ward route* (MR :328-336): *protection from evil and good* (touch, 1 action, Concentration, holy water 25 gp consumed). At the start of each of the host's turns he makes a DC 12 Int save **with Advantage**: success = 1 Break, failure = Strain. A warded host cannot be re-occupied. Meloon has Int +0, so he succeeds about 70%.
  - *Magic route* (MR :337-347): *Dispel Magic* at a 3rd-level slot is a DC 14 check, 1 Break, Strain on failure; at a 4th-level slot or higher it is an automatic Break. *Remove Curse* and *Greater Restoration* also work. **The party at level 4 has no 3rd-level slot** (full casters get them at level 5), so this route is **Vajra only**.
  - *Anchor route* (MR :349-355): a helper within 5 ft uses an action for a Charisma (Persuasion) check, DC 14 with an anchor, DC 18 without; Advantage if the helper is the anchor and the host knows them; Disadvantage for a stranger shouting. **Azuredge is Meloon's anchor** (PREV ev-02:112). On Hold 1 by this route the host speaks one sentence in his own voice, then goes under (MR :354).
  - *Strain* (MR :357-361): 2d6 psychic per failed attempt, cannot exceed half max HP per attempt, cannot reduce below 1 HP; at 1 HP the next failure resets Hold to 3 for an hour.
  - *Holding.* The host must be Grappled, Restrained or Incapacitated without being killed (MR :323-326). The devourer fights with the host's attacks (Warrior Veteran: Greatsword twice, Parry) and uses Slip Out at Hold 1 (MR :365).
- **Expelled devourer** (MR :367-371): appears within 5 ft at full 28 HP, Tiny, AC 12, speed 40; the freed host has Incapacitated until the end of his next turn. On its turn it uses Occupy Body on the nearest eligible creature (Incapacitated, Small or Medium Humanoid or Beast, 10 HP or fewer, DC 12 Int save). **Meloon (65 HP) is not eligible** unless Strain left him at 10 HP or fewer. Otherwise it uses Devour Intellect on the nearest creature, Dashes and flees toward cover. Killing the host forces it out at full HP (MR :377).
- **Recovery** (MR :380-384): the freed host takes 1 level of Exhaustion and speaks in fragments until a Long Rest; a Long Rest returns his memory in full. **Meloon remembers** the occupation as a long dream in which he saw and heard everything and could do nothing, including the three tendays, the Day 5 conversation, Azuredge's contest each dawn, and any face-to-face meeting with a Guild minder or courier. **He does not know** what the devourer passed to Nihiloor by telepathy.
- **Reports reach Nihiloor in two ways.** The devourer's telepathy reaches only 60 ft (MR :370, :384), so a hosted devourer reports through a Guild minder or courier who meets the host (MR :384). R says "a dead drop in the Dock Ward" (ev-01:19). PREV says "a Xanathar's Guild courier, a dockhand in the Guild's pay who meets Meloon each tenday at the Yawning Portal" (ev-02:21). Recommend the PREV courier, since it can be seen and followed. Inside the lair (X-areas) the devourer can report directly to Nihiloor within 60 ft. This must be reconciled with PREV `m04/ev-01:75`, which says the devourer took Meloon into the lair "twice"; recommend those visits happened **before** the tenday, so no lair trip occurs during the watch.
- **Detect Intelligence** (MR :276): the devourer senses every creature with Int 3 or higher within 300 ft. In a packed Yawning Portal this is noise; it cannot pick out the observers. A character who stays at its table is a different matter. Say so in a GM line.

**Inconsistencies in PREV against MR to correct in the rewrite:**

1. PREV Path 1 says Vajra's ward and three *dispel magic* casts need "no roll and Meloon takes no Strain" (ev-02:79-80). MR Route 1 says a warded host rolls the DC 12 Int save each turn and a failure deals Strain. Either Vajra skips the ward (the dispels are automatic Breaks and Meloon, at 65 HP, is not eligible to be re-occupied), or she casts it and the saves apply. Recommend: three 4th-level *dispel magic* casts in three rounds, no ward, no roll, no Strain.
2. PREV Path 2 lists the ward route as the party's only magic route (ev-02:111) and says Vajra supplies nothing. A party with no ward-capable caster has only the anchor route (DC 14, Azuredge in view). The ward needs holy water the party may not have; the Junior Griffon quartermaster benefit (guide :55) covers mundane gear, so say the quartermaster has two vials of holy water.
3. PREV outcome **Mission 4 Alert** (ev-01:172) and **Nihiloor Identified Party** (ev-02:166) describe the same effect and fire from different events. Merge to one name (see 4.7).
4. PREV overview says Azuredge reaches a party member "on Day 7" (PREV overview:35; R overview:27); the event says "any morning" (ev-01:64-66 PREV; R :39). Use "any morning, during the ritual".
5. PREV expects Meloon to speak a full sentence directly after extraction ("What the fuck just happened?", ev-02:132), but MR says fragments until a Long Rest. Keep to fragments before the Long Rest.
6. R says the devourer "does not know the surveillance happened" (R ev-02:24) yet Nihiloor gets descriptions. Contradiction inside R. The devourer has **seen the party up close** on Day 5, so it will report them whatever the party tells Vajra (see 4.7 clock).
