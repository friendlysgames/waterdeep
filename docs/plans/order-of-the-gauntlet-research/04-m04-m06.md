3. **Gates and renown are wrong.** R gates: M4 R7/L5 (R m04/overview:6), M5 R10/L6 (R m05/overview:5-6 in the quote), M6 R14/L7 "Follows Arc G" (R m06/overview:6). 4. **The Cassalanter-secrecy exposure is severe and structural** (section 8 maps every line). The worst three: M5's ledger reveals the soul-bargain's structure, "twelve acts", a Founders' Day deadline and "the Reckoning" before **Cassalanter Villa** (PREV m05/ev:17, :125-133, :146); M6 tells the party and the Lords that Victoro has a "contractual counterpart" and an "infernal rider" (PREV m06/ev:17, :28, :32); and the guide's Missions table says outright "the party discovers it is a Cassalanter ritual preparation site" and "Lord Victoro's infernal contract triggered a contractual enforcement rider" (`guide:70-71`).
5. **M5 contradicts the canonical Aveen Street shrine.** `arc-e-faction-outposts.md:245-267` and `guides/factions/11-cassalanters.md:23-45, :79-82` define **the Asmodean Shrine, North Ward, Aveen Street** as a Faction Outposts location (eight areas, Hell cyst, Mirror of the Fiend, *Instructions from Lord Cassalanter*). OotG M5 invents a different building, in the **Field Ward**, under a chandler's shop (PREV m05/ev:74-77). The source for M4 (`Appendix_C:1148`, `adventure-wdh.json` Gysheer entry) points at the canonical shrine. This needs a user ruling (D1).
6. **M5/M6's calendar mechanics are impossible.** Founders' Day is a fixed festival, Flamerule 1 (`structural-rules.md:46`; `arc-e:69`), and the pact comes due Flamerule 11 (`structural-rules.md:47`). "Victoro accelerates the Founders' Day timeline by four days" (PREV m05/ev:156) cannot happen. The "Reckoning", "twelve rites" and the Masked Lord contact are OotG inventions that conflict with the canon of 99 cups of midnight tears and 99 soul coins (`structural-rules.md:46`, `villains/cassalanters.md:38`).
7. **M6's premise breaks after the villa.** A Horned Devil enforcing a contract rider (PREV m06/ev:15-19) assumes the contract stands. The villa resolves four ways (`arc-g:355-390`): Contract Destroyed, Cooperative/Gold Payment, Sacrifice Happened, Cassalanters Exposed. In three of them there is no live rider. M6 at R13/L7 after Kolat Towers (the LA/EE/FG precedent, `LA/m06/overview:5`) always follows the villa, so M6 must read the villa's result.
8. **2024 stat errors throughout** (section 7): "Cult Fanatic" is **Cultist Fanatic**; the Spined Devil has no *Hurl Spine*; the Bearded Devil has no grapple/bleed/"Cursed"; the Horned Devil has no *Battle Frenzy*, is CR 11, and "destroyed" must mean "sent back" (*Diabolical Restoration*); the 2024 Priest has no *Banishment* and no "5th-level spell access"; 2024 *Command* is a one-word order and cannot extract a confession; the Erinyes is CR 12, not 8.
9. **Encounter numbers are wrong at every size** (section 4.4, 5.4, 6.4). M4 as written is Oppressive at 3 characters with Savra. M5's lone Bearded Devil is Mild (8.2% / 4.6% / 2.9%) at level 6. M6's Horned Devil is Brutal (72%) at 3, Bloody at 4 and Bruising at 5 with Savra counted, and with the +4 first-turn KO adjustment Overwhelming (116%), Brutal (72%), Bloody (49%).
10. **Three Clue Rule holds in M4 (three paths to the address) but fails elsewhere.** M5 has one approach (find a door, kill a devil, take a ledger). M6 has no investigation at all, and M6's Banishment option is mathematically nearly useless (section 6.4).

## 4. M4: Spined Devils in Twelvedog Court (level 5)

### 4.1 What R and PREV do, and what is settled

Savra arrives at Trollskull Manor "before the usual hour" (PREV m04/ev:55-62). Five spined devils hold rooflines in Twelvedog Court, a Field Ward square named for a kennel gone thirty years; three residents are pinned behind a market cart (PREV m04/ev:100-127). When the last devil falls a woman in good clothes, **Gysheer Omfreys** (Cult Fanatic block), steps out with a knife, nearly confesses and shuts up (PREV m04/ev:129-160). Three independent paths lead to "the shrine on Aveen Street": compulsion (three phrases, PREV :166), her belongings (DC 13 Investigation, a note initialled "Lord V.C." with a Cassalanter wax seal, :170-174), her room above a dyer's shop on Crispin Street (DC 14 Persuasion or Intimidation with the landlord, four hours, :176-180). Savra's constraint: "Devil worship is not illegal in Waterdeep ... acting against the Cassalanters on the confession of one cultist extracted under magical compulsion is not something I can do ... if you brought me real evidence, a document, not a confession, the Order would know exactly what to do" (PREV :182-195). She releases Gysheer. The Cassalanters' imp network knows Gysheer was taken (PREV :51, :211).

**Settled (keep):** Gysheer Omfreys, Twelvedog Court and its six inward-facing buildings, three residents behind a cart, the Crispin Street room above a dyer's shop, the three paths, Savra's constraint and release, "Savra does not ask. She thanks them plainly" for full sharing, Cassalanters' imp-network knowledge. All except Gysheer and Twelvedog are R inventions with no collisions (checked: `Gysheer`, `Omfreys`, `Twelvedog` occur only in WDH, Appendix B/C, and the Victoro Notable Figures featured-in line; `Crispin Street` nowhere else).
**Drop:** `[GM]`, `Milestone`, "Hurl Spine", "Cult Fanatic", `Command` as an extraction tool, "+2/+2" renown, "Mission 5 opens once the shrine address is confirmed" as the only reader, "We have the grounds now" (PREV :193, contradicts Savra at :189-193), the "arrives already moving" read-aloud in R.

### 4.2 Source versus repo

Faithful to WDH and Appendix C in beats. Three departures worth recording: the repo renders Gysheer's compelled answers as "operations for Lord Victoro Cassalanter of the Sea Ward / preparation of ground / the shrine on Aveen Street" (same as `Appendix_C:1148`); the repo makes Gysheer *not* attack Savra (WDH: she does); and the 2024 stat names change (section 7). Keep Gysheer attacking Savra's party as WDH has her do: it gives the third act a reason for the Cultist Fanatic stat block.

### 4.3 Mission depth, Three Clue Rule and event split

Depth is fine for a mid-quest fight: fight, capture, three routes, a moral limit. What is missing: the table when Gysheer is not subdued (she flees: the Crispin Street room is the only route), a stated consequence for each Savra-release alternative, and what the party tells Savra.
**Recommended: one event** (single place, single clock, like DR m03 ev-01 minus its week of leads; the BD/EE splits were driven by a change of place and clock that M4 lacks).
Depth fixes: (1) **Three independent paths, each failing safe**: compulsion (spell list below), belongings (needs Gysheer subdued), room (works even if she flees; costs four hours; the landlord check can be replaced by simply being invited in by Savra's sword-and-badge, no check). (2) **What Savra says** is the only place the Cassalanters are named in Order speech, and it must stay suspicion (section 8). (3) **What the party does with the address**: tell Savra everything (Intelligence Shared), tell Savra nothing, or tell the Harpers/others; each has a stated consequence (Savra thanks plainly; Savra "knows they held back" and says so once; the outpost reader in 4.5).
**Spells that really work in 2024 (verified, `spells-xphb.json`):** *Charm Person* (Wis save, Advantage if the caster's allies are fighting the target; Friendly to the caster; she knows afterwards she was charmed); *Zone of Truth* (Cha save; the target "can't speak a deliberate lie" but may stay silent); *Detect Thoughts*/*Suggestion* (not verified here). *Command* is a one-word order (Approach, Drop, Flee, Grovel, Halt) and cannot extract a statement. The Cultist Fanatic's saves are Wis +4 and no Cha proficiency (Cha 13: +1). Replace "Charm Person (DC 13), Command" by "*Charm Person*, *Zone of Truth* or similar; the spell's own save DC applies" so no table has a DC fixed to the caster.
**Legal beat:** Savra's refusal to act on compelled testimony is the mission's point; keep it, but let it be about *Gysheer's testimony* (a single cultist under compulsion), never about "the Cassalanters being devil-worshippers".
