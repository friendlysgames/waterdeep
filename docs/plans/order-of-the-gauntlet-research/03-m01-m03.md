## 0. Headline findings

1. **The "Kelso Splinter-intel line" is a phantom.** `emerald-enclave/m03-the-doppelganger-problem/design-notes.md:28` and OOS `:762` say the OotG M3 design notes "claim Splinter intelligence from Kelso that its event does not contain." R and PREV M3 design-notes are seven lines and say nothing of the kind (`R m03/design-notes.md:1-7`; `diff` against PREV is empty). The claim lived in the *old Enclave* design notes (`docs/plans/emerald-enclave-research/05-consistency-audit.md:96`, which says R EE m03 notes:13 attributed to OotG M3 "intelligence about which gang leaders have been paid by the Splinter"). That intelligence is actually **M1's Pell's debt** (`R m01/ev-01:61`). Enclave already rewrote the line (K2: Kelso is a middleman, the Splinter is a customer in GM text only). Action: M3 adds nothing about the Splinter; delete both log lines.
2. **The wererat silver rule is 2014.** R says "Silver or magic weapons required for sustained damage" (`R m03/overview:22`, `ev-01:16`, `:40`); AppC says "Regeneration still applies" (`AppC:1086`). The 2024 **Wererat** (XMM p.325) is CR 2, AC 13, HP 60, no resistances, no Regeneration (section 3.4). Its Bite (rat or hybrid form only) carries lycanthropy, Con DC 11. DR3 already states "the Wererat has no damage resistances" (`DR3 ev-01:385`). R's three wererats are Crushing at 3 characters (148%).
3. **M2 breaks the Cassalanter secrecy rule at its end and contradicts three other files on Seffia.** R says a captured imp is "physical evidence of Cassalanter devil-binding" (`R m02/ev-02:14`), with "binding marks" that "identify its contractual origin" (`:35`) and the Halls of Justice filing it formally. OOS `:106` logs this as R2 (minor). Separately: the Seffia Notable Figures page is an Asmodean cult fanatic who "does not receive visitors" and sleeps in the Southern Ward windmill (`notable-figures/cassalanters/10-seffia-naelryke.md:83-89`), and her voice is clipped and operational (`voices/cassalanters.md:175-189`); R makes her a gracious tea-house socialite. And her extracted line "The Cassalanters know who the real Black Viper is" (`R m02/ev-01:63`) contradicts `arc-g:289-300` (the household does not know; Esvele robs them at their own party).
4. **M2 has a dead hook: "What are they moving?"** Savra asks it (`R m02/ev-02:15`, `:43`) and Seffia's coerced line is "Something is being moved" (`ev-01:63`). No file in the repo answers it (grep clean; M4 gives Gysheer and the Aveen Street address, not a "move").
5. **No outcomes exist in R; PREV's names are partly settled by readers.** Settled: **Mediation Path Taken** (read by DR3 `ev-01:257-271`, `design-notes:19`), **Black Viper Source Noted** (written by BD2 `ev-01:386`, read by M2), **Imp Captured** (read by Cassalanter Villa, OOS `:106`), the M1 **Splinter Intel Reported** name (read by Faction Outposts), and "Kelso's debt" (cancelled by DR3 `ev-01:511`).
6. **Gates and renown are off the shared ladder and have no base.** R: M1 "Renown 0, L2" (`R m01/overview:6`), M2 "Renown 2, L3" (`R m02/overview:6`), M3 "Renown 4, L4" (`R m03/overview:6`), M4 "Renown 7, L5" (`R m03/ev-01:60`).
