## 0. Headline findings

1. **M1 does not match its own source, the guide, or the Trollskull Alley journal.** WDH and AppC: a tenday of dungsweeper escort, nothing happens, then a **carrion crawler** comes up from a sewer grate on **Day 9** at highsun in the **Trades Ward** (AppC `:447-461`; WDH 2nd-level row). G03 `:65` and Trollskull Alley `ev-04:72` still say "carrion crawler on day nine". R/PREV dropped the crawler entirely and invented a Xanathar ambush ("Night Three") plus an insider plot (Mareva, Delvon, Pirn), set in the Dock Ward (R `m01/ev-01:7-14`, `ev-02:7-14`). Decision needed (section 3.2). Recommendation: keep both, as a hybrid.
2. **M1's timeline is broken.** ev-01 runs Days 1 to 7 and says "Night Three follows immediately" (R `m01/ev-01:14`, `:106-108`), but "Night Three" is the third night, before the Day 4, 6 and 7 beats. Herath's note comes on "the final morning" (R `ev-02:78`) while ev-01 ends on Day 7. Renown wants "the full tenday without another successful attack" (R `ev-02:96`) after an attack on Night Three.
3. **Esloon as a 2024 Mage cannot be fought by a 4th-level party.** The 2024 **Mage** is CR 6, 81 HP, three Arcane Burst attacks of 16 force each (48 a round at range 120). R puts him with "six Thugs and two Veterans" (R `m03/overview:21`, `ev-01:17`). Neither "Thug" nor "Veteran" exists in the 2024 data (use **Tough** and **Warrior Veteran**). Option A (disrupt the meeting) is Devastating or worse at every party size (section 3.4). Recommended fix: Esloon uses **Mage Apprentice** (CR 2).
4. **R's "modified Mage" text is 2014.** "Arcane Recovery replaced by a once-per-day *Counterspell*" (R `m03/ev-01:17`) has no 2024 referent. The 2024 Mage already has Protective Magic (3/Day, *Counterspell* or *Shield*), and the Apprentice has no reaction at all.
5. **Gates and renown are off the shared ladder, and nothing is a base.** R/PREV gates: M1 "first session" (`m01/overview:6`), M2 "Renown 2, 3rd" (`m02/overview:6`), M3 "Renown 4, 4th" (`m03/overview:6`), M4 "Renown 6, 5th" (`m03/ev-01:103`). The ladder is M1 joining/L2, M2 R3/L3, M3 R5/L4, M4 R8/L5. R awards two `+1` lines per mission and no base; M1's own brief promises "two renown" (`m01/ev-01:28`). No mission has the three `+1` lines, a `Mission Renown` block or a debrief scene.
6. **There are no Event Outcomes anywhere in M1 to M3, in R or PREV.** PREV kept `> **[GM]**` wrappers, `#### Milestone: None`, `Milestone Overview` and the retrofit sidebars (`[!npc-narrative]`, `[!dialogue]`, `[!narrative]`) (PREV `m01/ev-01:3`, `:116-118`; `m01/overview:3,11`). Every downstream promise is prose ("harder assignments", "Splinter team understaffed", "Esloon resurfaces in Faction Outposts") with no writer, no name and no reader.
7. **Half the downstream payoffs have no reader.** `arc-e` (Faction Outposts) never mentions Esloon, Sarvos, Veralax, Harko, a Dock Ward checkpoint strike or "understaffed" teams. `arc-f` (Xanathar's Lair) has three entry methods (Guildsign Trail, Gladiatorial Transport, Ott as guide) and no "Sewer Route" for Herath's note (`arc-f:55-63`). Krentz is in **Finding Floon** (ev-01 brawl, ev-04 Q05), not "Arc B's sewers" (R `m01/ev-02:42`).
8. **The brief's Kelso line is not an LA hit.** The Kelso line logged at OOS `:44` sits under `## Emerald Enclave` (OOS `:38`) and is about Enclave M3. LA M1 to M3 contain no Kelso. Section 6 lists the real LA hits from OOS `:23-31`.
9. **Standing-rule hits are few but real.** Manshoon gate in player-facing text (M3 Jalester speech, Overview and Summary; M2 Overview and Summary). 2014 monster names. A Jalester delivery by "Watch-sealed note" against the organization page, which says he delivers every mission in person. A **Bent Nail** collision with Tally Fellbranch's woodshop. Esloon's voice doc says he "never discusses why he left" and R has him explain it.

## 1. Source material

### 1.1 WDH and Appendix C, mission by mission

**M1, Protecting the Dungsweepers** (`AppC:398-469`; WDH 2nd row).
- Facts: Jalester briefs "Meet them at the Muleskull Tavern, on Ship Street in the Dock Ward, at six bells" (`AppC:413`, WDH 2nd row). Four dungsweepers (**commoners**) and a stout dwarf foreman of about sixty, Berna, grey braid, twenty years with the Guild, seventeen on the **Trades Ward** circuit (`AppC:424-431`). Incidents on Days 2, 4, 6, 7 (`AppC:435-445`), exactly as R's four beats, including Herath's 12 gp purse, Berna's Day 6 briefing and the six-year jurisdiction dispute. **Day 9 at highsun** a carrion crawler comes out of a sewer grate in a 20-foot Trades Ward alley, pursued by **two City Watch guards at about half HP** (`AppC:449-461`; WDH). Reward +1 renown; Berna's trust is "a minor but genuine asset" and she will share what she has seen "if asked as one professional to another" (`AppC:465`); Herath's folded note on the last day gives a Trades Ward sewer access point "that bypasses the Watch checkpoint on Coin Alley" (`AppC:467`).
- The AppC stat note cites a "Stagger Saving Throw DC 13" for the crawler (`AppC:455`). **No such trait exists** in the 2024 **Carrion Crawler** (XMM p.66): CR 2, AC 13, HP 51 (6d10+18), Multiattack of Paralyzing Tentacles (Con DC 12, range 10 ft, Poisoned and therefore Paralyzed until a save succeeds, after 1 minute it succeeds automatically) plus Bite +4, 7 (2d4+2) piercing plus 3 poison.
- Not in either source: Mareva Coile, Delvon Harsk, Orryn Saltwick, Bremma Dunnet, Cass Fildura, Pirn, Krentz, the Night Three ambush and the whole insider plot. All are R inventions (R `m01/ev-01:32-36`, `ev-02:16-64`).

**M2, Finding Harko** (`AppC:471-548`; WDH 3rd row).
- WDH: "Find him and quietly put him to the sword." Three DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) checks of 8 hours each before three failures. **Bandit Captain** with two **kenku** that "fight by his side".
- AppC: Harko is 50, broad-shouldered, "does not negotiate", has "been offered exile twice" and "will not surrender" (`AppC:515`). The kenku "flee if he is reduced to 0 hit points" and "carry nothing of intelligence value" (`AppC:517`). Scene is "the alley behind the Muleskull" (`AppC:507-513`). A **cipher note** in his coat names two disruption targets and "K", Keln Trigos at the **Terasse Estate** (`AppC:519`; renown reward `:546`). Lira is a Calishite server, early twenties, who saw the killing; honest or direct = she waits; lie or threaten = Watch report and Jalester's "I said quietly." (`AppC:523-540`). Dramatic question: "quietly" means "without report, without warrant, and without a body the Watch can trace" (`AppC:479`).
- AppC gives three sources for the search, which satisfy the Three Clue Rule by themselves: a **fencewoman on Net Street** (kenku at the Muleskull after dark), a **retired harbor pilot at the Muleskull** (raised voices in the alley, "big human, northern accent") and a **Night Soil Guild worker** (a man matching Harko after midnight near the Dock Street warehouses) (`AppC:499-503`).
- Not in either source: the rope warehouse on Mudwagon Row, Wharf Mast Lane, Bandit guards, Harko surrendering, Veralax, the Bent Nail meeting, the Thayan file on Esloon. R inverted the mission: the source's cipher note and a hard-man death became a surrender and a Splinter handoff.

**M3, Scuttling the Thayan Deal** (`AppC:550-614`; WDH 4th row).
- WDH: Jalester says "The Zhents are courting a Red Wizard of Thay named Esloon Bezant... Scuttle the deal, and do it fast!" Two routes: spend **25 gp** in bribes and succeed on **DC 16 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion)** to sow rumors of betrayal, or confront Esloon (**LE male Thayan human mage**) with his gang of **five thugs**, defeat or bribe them with **at least 500 gp**. AppC has Esloon "in his forties", "five years" in the city, shaved head with the faded remnants of unfinished Red Wizard tattoos, and "LE" (`AppC:575`).
- Not in either source: Sarvos Miln, the Skewered Dragon, the "Holvar" booking, the 30-minute clock, the Watch checkpoint strike, the Baldur's Gate cargo, the Thayan intelligence file from M2, Option B/C/D, the 2014 "modified Mage".
