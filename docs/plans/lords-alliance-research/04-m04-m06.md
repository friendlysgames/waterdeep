- M6 has no combat, so the level gate adds nothing mechanical,

Options:

- **C. Hybrid:** R13 and (L7 or the third Eye restored). Same practical effect as B and keeps the ladder wording visible.

**Recommend C** (B in effect), and document it against `harpers-out-of-scope-notes.md:238` and `:30`. The justification for departing from the EE ruling is that the EE ruling was driven by a level-7 roster fight and by the clock, neither of which exists here. This needs a user ruling (D1).
