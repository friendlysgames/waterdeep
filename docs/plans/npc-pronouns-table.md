## Disagreements between sources and the campaign

No source states a gender that the campaign contradicts. Two alignments differ and were left alone:

- Fenerus Stormcastle: WDH `:10656` prints "N male", the campaign says Neutral Evil (`06-fenerus-stormcastle.md:5`, `trades-ward.md:120`, `guilds.md:54`).
- Grinda Garloth: WDH `:7472` prints "CN female", the campaign says Neutral (`09-grinda-garloth.md:5`, `dock-ward.md:176`).

One title differs: the Cassalanters remix file calls Caladorn a "former Open Lord", while WDH `:18404` and `:22713` and the campaign say "former Masked Lord and hero of the North".

A grep of `campaign/` for every listed name followed by a stat line found the same pronouns everywhere (gazetteer wards, `governance-and-guilds/`, the faction events). No stat line disagreed, so no event, gazetteer or guide line was changed.
